# Teacher Autonomy And Creativity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Autonomy And Creativity Survey is designed to gather insights into teacher satisfaction with the school environment, specifically regarding autonomy and creativity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form carefully.
2. Answer each question truthfully based on your experiences as a teacher.
3. Use the options provided for each question to select your answer.
4. Make sure to fill in all required fields (although none are marked as required).
5. Once you have completed all questions, review your answers before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Administration (Page 1)**: Please select the level of autonomy you have in school administration (e.g., high, medium, low).
* **Decision Making (Page 2)**: Please select the level of decision-making freedom you have (e.g., high, medium, low).
* **Teaching Style (Page 3)**: Select all teaching styles that apply to your work (e.g., traditional, collaborative, inclusive, flexible, autonomous).
* **Innovation (Page 4)**: Please select the level of innovation encouraged in the school (e.g., high, low).
* **Support (Page 5)**: Please describe the level of support you receive from the school (e.g., always, rarely, sometimes, never).
* **Feedback (Page 6)**: Please select how often you receive feedback on your performance (e.g., always, rarely, sometimes, never).
* **Autonomy (Page 7)**: Please select the level of autonomy you have in decision-making (e.g., high, medium, low).
* **Creativity (Page 8)**: Please select the level of creativity encouraged in the school (e.g., high, low).

## Tips

- Be honest and truthful in your answers.
- Review your answers carefully before submission.
- If you need help, feel free to ask your school administrator for assistance.
