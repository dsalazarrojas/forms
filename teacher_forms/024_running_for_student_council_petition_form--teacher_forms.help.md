# running_for_student_council_petition_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The running for student council petition form is used to collect information and endorsements from students and teachers to support a student's candidacy for student council.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Petition Form Info** page with a brief description of the student's qualifications and why you are running for student council.
2. Select up to 5 **Student Candidates** from the list of available candidates.
3. Write a clear and concise **Reason for Support** for why you are supporting the student.
4. Add your **Student Signature** to confirm your support.
5. If you are a teacher, add your **Teacher Signature** to confirm your support.
6. If applicable, fill out the **Date Completed**, **Student Name**, **Teacher Name**, **Student Grade**, **Teacher Grade**, **Student Class**, **Teacher Class**, **Teacher Email**, **Teacher Phone**, and **Teacher Note** fields with the relevant information.
