# Preschool Graduation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a preschool graduation ceremony, including the category and type of event, a brief description, experience level of the person completing the form, the date of the graduation, and any additional comments or details. The form is not mandatory, but rather a tool to assist with the planning and organization of the preschool graduation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a category from the list provided.
2. Write a brief description of the graduation ceremony.
3. Rate your level of experience with the preschool (1-5, where 1 means "little to no experience" and 5 means "significant experience").
4. Choose a date for the graduation ceremony.
5. Provide any additional comments or details about the graduation ceremony.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **category** (`Category`, Select One, optional): Choose a category that best describes the graduation ceremony, such as "Teacher Forms" or "Parent Forms".
* **description** (`Description`, Text, optional): Write a brief description of the graduation ceremony.
* **preschool_experience** (`Preschool Experience`, Number, optional): Rate your level of experience with the preschool, with 1 being "little to no experience" and 5 being "significant experience".
* **graduation_day** (`Graduation Day`, Date, optional): Choose a date for the graduation ceremony.
* **graduation_ceremony** (`Graduation Ceremony`, Text, optional): Provide any additional comments or details about the graduation ceremony.
* **comments** (`Comments`, Note, optional): Add any additional comments or details about the graduation ceremony.
* **form_assigned_tool** (`Form Assigned Tool`, Select One, optional): Choose the tool assigned to the form, such as "chatjimmy" or "other".
* **output_file** (`Output File`, Text, optional): The output file generated by the form.

Note: This guide is not intended to be a comprehensive guide on how to plan a preschool graduation ceremony, but rather a user-friendly guide on how to complete the form. For specific questions or concerns, please reach out to the form administrators.
