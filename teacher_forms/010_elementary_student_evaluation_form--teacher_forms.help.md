# elementary_student_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "elementary_student_evaluation_form" is a tool used by teachers to track and document their observations, notes, and feedback for students' development. It is meant to be used during regular intervals to monitor students' progress and identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
3. Enter the student's current grade level in the "Student Grade" field.
4. Select the relevant subject area from the "Subject Area" field.
5. Write down the student's strengths and weaknesses in the "Strengths and Weaknesses" field.
6. Specify the student's development goals in the "Development Goals" field.
7. Add any additional comments or notes about the student's performance in the "Comments" field.
8. Record the date the form was submitted in the "Submitted Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student being evaluated. (text, required: false)
- **Teacher Name**: Enter your name as the teacher evaluating the student. (text, required: false)
- **Student Grade**: Enter the student's current grade level. (number, required: false)
- **Subject Area**: Select the relevant subject area the student is being evaluated for. (text, required: false)
- **Strengths and Weaknesses**: Describe the student's strengths and areas of improvement. (note, required: false)
- **Development Goals**: Outline specific goals for the student's development. (note, required: false)
- **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the student's performance. (text, required: false)
- **Submitted Date**: Record the date the form was submitted. (date, required: false)

## Tips
- Be specific and detailed when filling out the "Strengths and Weaknesses" and "Development Goals" fields.
- Use the "Comments" field to provide any additional context or information not covered in the other fields.
- Ensure that the "Submitted Date" field is accurate and up-to-date.
- Review and revise the form regularly to monitor students' progress and adjust their development goals accordingly.
- Keep the "Strengths and Weaknesses" and "Development Goals" fields concise and focused on the most critical areas of improvement.
- The "Subject Area" field can be used to select from a list of predefined subjects or categories.
- The "Student Grade" field should reflect the student's current grade level, not their previous or future grade level.
