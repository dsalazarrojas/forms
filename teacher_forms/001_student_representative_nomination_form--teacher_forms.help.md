# Student Representative Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect nominations for student representatives, allowing teachers to identify students who have demonstrated exemplary qualities such as leadership, communication, and teamwork skills. The purpose of this form is to help teachers select the most deserving students to represent their class or school in events and activities.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, select the relevant student class from the options provided.
2.  Enter the student's name.
3.  Enter the student's email address.
4.  Optionally, provide the student's phone number.
5.  Enter the name of the person nominating the student.
6.  Indicate the position or title of the person nominating the student (e.g., teacher, parent, etc.).
7.  Enter the contact information of the person nominating the student.
8.  Enter the email address of the person nominating the student.
9.  Provide a brief reason for selecting the student as a representative.
10.  Include a signature from the person nominating the student, if required.
11.  Enter a message from the person nominating the student, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Student Class** (`student_class_id`): Select the student's class to which they belong.
*   **Student Name** (`student_name`): Enter the student's name.
*   **Student Email** (`student_email`): Enter the student's email address.
*   **Student Phone** (`student_phone`): Optionally, enter the student's phone number.
*   **Nominator Name** (`nominator_name`): Enter the name of the person nominating the student.
*   **Nominator Position** (`nominator_position`): Indicate the position or title of the person nominating the student (e.g., teacher, parent, etc.).
*   **Nominator Contact** (`nominator_contact`): Enter the contact information of the person nominating the student.
*   **Nominator Email** (`nominator_email`): Enter the email address of the person nominating the student.
*   **Reason for Selection** (`reason_for_selection`): Provide a brief reason for selecting the student as a representative.
*   **Nominator Signature** (`nominator_signature`): Include a signature from the person nominating the student, if required.
*   **Nominator Message** (`nominator_message`): Enter a message from the person nominating the student, if required.
