# School Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Report Form is a tool designed to collect information from teachers about student progress, behavior, and other relevant details for school reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the school from the list provided in the "School ID" field.
2. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
3. Enter the teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
4. Describe the student's progress in the "Progress" field.
5. Note any notable behaviors or incidents in the "Behavior" field.
6. List any achievements or notable accomplishments of the student in the "Achievements" field.
7. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.
8. Select the relevant school category (e.g., School, Department, College) in the "School Category" field.
9. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School ID** (`School ID`, `select_one`, not required): Select the school from the list provided. This will help the administrator to easily identify the school to which the report belongs.
* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, `text`, not required): Enter the student's name as it appears in official records.
* **Teacher Name** (`Teacher Name`, `text`, not required): Enter your name as the teacher completing this form.
* **Progress** (`Progress`, `text`, not required): Describe the student's progress, including any notable events or observations.
* **Behavior** (`Behavior`, `text`, not required): Note any notable behaviors or incidents that occurred during this reporting period.
* **Achievements** (`Achievements`, `text`, not required): List any achievements or notable accomplishments of the student.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, not required): Provide any additional comments or feedback for the administrator's reference.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of the reporting period.
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, not required): Enter the time of the reporting period.
* **School Category** (`School Category`, `select_one`, not required): Select the relevant school category (e.g., School, Department, College).
* **Tool Version** (`Tool Version`, `text`, not required): Enter the version of the tool or system used to generate this form.
