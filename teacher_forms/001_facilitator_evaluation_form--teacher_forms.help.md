# Facilitator Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facilitator Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from participants after a session led by a facilitator. It aims to provide an evaluation of the facilitator's performance and improve future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Title** (text): Enter the title of the program or session you attended.
* **Overall Rating** (number): Rate the overall performance of the facilitator, with higher numbers indicating better performance.
* **Content** (select_one): Select whether the facilitator provided content that supported the session. Choose all that apply.
* **Presentation** (number): Rate the quality of the presentation.
* **Materials** (note): Provide any additional feedback or comments on the materials provided by the facilitator.
* **Facilitation** (select_multiple): Check the boxes of all facilitation skills you feel the facilitator provided, such as yes for "yes" and no for "no".
* **Organization** (text): Enter any feedback on the facilitator's organization and presentation style.
* **Feedback** (text): Provide any general feedback on the facilitator's performance.
* **Facilitator** (select_one): Rate the facilitator's performance as a whole. Choose "yes" or "no".
* **Name** (text): Enter the facilitator's name.
* **Title** (text): Enter the facilitator's title.
* **Organization** (text): Enter the facilitator's organization.
* **Rating** (number): Rate the facilitator's performance.
* **Comments** (text): Provide any additional comments on the facilitator's performance.

## Tips

* Please provide honest and constructive feedback to help facilitators improve their performance.
* Be specific and concrete with your comments and ratings.
* Complete the form for each facilitator per session.
