# Tutorial Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect requests from students for tutorials or support from teachers or educators. This form is designed to provide a structured way for students to submit their requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **Request Subject** in the "Request Subject" field. This is a brief description of your request.
2.  Provide a detailed description of your request in the "Description" field.
3.  If you have any additional comments or information, you can enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.
4.  Enter your **Student Name** in the "Student Name" field.
5.  Enter your **Student Email** in the "Student Email" field.
6.  Enter your **Teacher Name** in the "Teacher Name" field (this is for internal use only, not required for submission).
7.  Select the relevant **Subject Area** in the "Subject Area" field.
8.  Enter the date you submitted your request in the "Date Submitted" field.
9.  Enter the time you submitted your request in the "Time Submitted" field.
10. Select any additional information that may be relevant to your request in the "Additional Information" field.
11. If you have any additional notes or comments for the teacher, you can enter them in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Subject** (`request_subject`, text, required: false): A brief description of your request.
* **Description** (`request_description`, text, required: false): A detailed description of your request.
* **Additional Comments** (`request_additional_comments`, text, required: false): Any additional comments or information you have about your request.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Your name as a student.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required: false): Your email address.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required: false): The name of your teacher or educator (this is for internal use only, not required for submission).
* **Subject Area** (`subject_area`, text, required: false): The subject area relevant to your request.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, required: false): The date you submitted your request.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time, required: false): The time you submitted your request.
* **Additional Information** (`select_additional_info`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any additional information that may be relevant to your request.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Any additional notes or comments for the teacher.
