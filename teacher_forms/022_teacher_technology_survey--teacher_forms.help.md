# teacher_technology_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help teachers evaluate their technology needs and preferences for teaching, providing information that will support the development of technology-related initiatives and projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer all questions honestly.
2. Select the technologies that you currently use in your classroom, such as computers, smartphones, and tablets.
3. Enter the number of pieces of equipment you need, such as laptops and desktops.
4. Choose the category that best describes your role as a teacher (e.g. teacher, student, administrator).
5. Enter any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **technology_used** (`technology_used`, select_multiple, required): This field asks you to select the technologies you currently use in your classroom. Please select all that apply.
* **equipment_needed** (`equipment_needed`, number, required): This field asks you to enter the number of pieces of equipment you need to support your teaching activities.
* **knowledge_required** (`knowledge_required`, select_one, required): This field asks you to select the type of knowledge that is required to use the technology. Please select one that best describes your needs.
* **additional_comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required): Please enter any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the technology you use in your classroom.
* **category** (`category`, select_one, required): This field asks you to choose the category that best describes your role as a teacher (e.g. teacher, student, administrator).
* **technology_survey** (`technology_survey`, number, required): This field asks you to enter the number of pieces of technology you need, but this question is redundant with the equipment_needed field.
* **additional_equipment** (`additional_equipment`, select_multiple, required): This field asks you to select any additional equipment you may need beyond the standard equipment.
* **total_equipment_cost** (`total_equipment_cost`, number, required): This field asks you to enter the total cost of the equipment you need.
* **technology_survey_category** (`technology_survey_category`, select_one, required): This field asks you to choose the category that best describes the technology survey, such as classroom, school, or district. 

## Tips
* Please answer all fields honestly and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about a field, please enter a comment or ask for clarification.
* If you have any additional suggestions or comments, please enter them in the additional_comments field.
