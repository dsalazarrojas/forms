# Course Withdrawal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Withdrawal Form is used by students to officially withdraw from a course. This form is intended for course withdrawals that do not involve any fees or penalties, or when a student is withdrawing before the course has started.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information and submit it to your instructor or administrator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Code** (`course_code`, Select Multiple, Required: False): Select the code of the course you are withdrawing from.
* **Student Id** (`student_id`, Number, Required: False): Enter your student ID number.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, Text, Required: False): Enter your full name as it appears on your student record.
* **Date of Withdrawal** (`date_of_withdrawal`, Date, Required: False): Select the date you are withdrawing from the course.
* **Reason for Withdrawal** (`reason_for_withdrawal`, Note, Required: False): Briefly explain the reason for your course withdrawal.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information to avoid any processing delays.
* If you are unsure about the purpose or requirements of this form, ask your instructor or administrator for clarification.
* This form can only be used for course withdrawals that do not involve any fees or penalties, or when a student is withdrawing before the course has started.
