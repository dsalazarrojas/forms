# student_information_collection_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The `student_information_collection_form` is designed to collect essential information about students. This form is used to gather details such as name, age, date of birth, grade level, subject interest, phone number, email, and notes about a student. The form is typically used in educational institutions to keep track of student records and provide accurate information for administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Student Name` field with the student's full name.
2. Provide the student's `Age`, as indicated by their age in years.
3. Enter the `Date Of Birth` of the student in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Select the student's `Grade Level` from the available options (High or Low).
5. Choose the `Subject` of interest for the student from the available options (Yes or No).
6. Enter the student's `Phone` number.
7. Provide the student's `Email` address.
8. Add any additional `Notes` about the student.
9. Enter the student's `Address` and `City`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter the student's full name, as per the format First Name Last Name.
* **Age**: Enter the student's age in years.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter the student's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Grade Level**: Select the grade level that best describes the student's current academic position (High or Low).
* **Subject**: Select the subject that interests the student (Yes or No).
* **Phone**: Enter the student's phone number.
* **Email**: Enter the student's email address.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information or comments about the student.
* **Address**: Enter the student's address.
* **City**: Enter the student's city.

## Tips

* Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information about the student.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the designated authority for clarification.
