# Preschool Admission Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for parents or guardians to contact the preschool with their contact information and other relevant details to inquire about admission or express their interest in enrolling their child. Please fill out this form carefully to provide the necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, please fill in your **First Name** in the text field next to the "First Name" label.
2. Next, enter your **Last Name** in the corresponding text field.
3. Enter your **Email** address to ensure that we can contact you back.
4. Fill in your **Phone** number so that we can reach out to you for further information.
5. Enter the **School Name** where you have interest in enrolling your child.
6. Type in your child's **Student Name** if they are not already registered.
7. If you have a **Parent Number**, please enter it.
8. Specify the **Contact Reason** for your visit, e.g., "Enquiry for Admission", "Enquiry for Enrolment".
9. Finally, select the person you would like to be **Assigned To** from the dropdown list "chatjimmy" or "Other". Please select "Other" if you have a special request or require further attention.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: A field for you to input your first name.
* **Last Name**: A field for you to input your last name.
* **Email**: A text input field for you to provide your email address, where we can contact you.
* **Phone**: A field to enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **School Name**: A field where you can specify the school's name where you wish to enroll your child.
* **Student Name**: If your child is not registered, input their name.
* **Parent Number**: If you have an identification number or reference number as a parent, input it here.
* **Contact Reason**: Type the reason you are contacting the school.
* **Assigned To**: Select the name of the person you would like to be in charge of your visit or inquiry, either "chatjimmy" or "Other" for special request or need further attention.
