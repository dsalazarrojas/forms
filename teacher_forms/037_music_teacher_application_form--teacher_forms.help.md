# Music Teacher Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Teacher Application Form is designed to collect necessary information from potential music teachers who wish to be listed on our music school's website. This includes personal details, teaching experience, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please provide your details as instructed below.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required") as accurately as possible.
3. You may leave the optional fields (marked as "optional") blank if you are not able to provide information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (`First Name`, `text`, required)
Enter your first name.

### Last Name (`Last Name`, `text`, required)
Enter your last name.

### Email (`Email`, `email`, required)
Enter your valid email address. This will be used for communication with our school.

### Phone (`Phone`, `text`, required)
Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### Resume (`Resume`, `text`, required)
Attach your resume or a link to your online resume. This will help us verify your qualifications.

### Teaching Experience (`Teaching Experience`, `select_multiple`, required)
Select all applicable teaching experiences:
* Instrumental
* Vocal
* Music Theory
* Music Theory and History
* Other (please specify)

### Available Days (`Available Days`, `select_one`, required)
Select all the days you are available to teach:
* Monday
* Tuesday
* Wednesday
* Thursday
* Friday
* Saturday
* Sunday

### Music Instruments (`Music Instruments`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select all the music instruments you are proficient in:
* Piano
* Guitar
* Violin
* Other (please specify)

### Education (`Education`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select all the education qualifications you have:
* Bachelor
* Master
* Ph.D

## Tips
* Please ensure you have all necessary permissions or approvals before submitting your application.
* If you are having trouble with the form, do not hesitate to contact our support team.
* This form is for internal review purposes only; we appreciate your cooperation in keeping the information private.
