# Student Peer Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Peer Evaluation Form is a tool for students to evaluate their peers' performance in a group setting or workshop. It helps to assess a student's collaboration, communication, and overall performance. This form is typically used to gather feedback from students on their peers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields:
	* `Peer Attendance`: Please indicate whether you attended all meetings or events with your peer.
	* `Overall Rating`: Rate your peer's overall performance on a scale of 1-5.
	* `Peer Rating`: Select the top three ratings that best describe your peer's performance.
2. If applicable, fill out the optional fields:
	* `Peer Name`: If you have direct interaction with your peer, please provide their name.
	* `Communication Skills`: Describe your peer's communication skills.
	* `Collaboration`: Describe your peer's collaboration skills.
	* `Peer Comments`: Provide any additional comments about your peer's performance.
	* `Teacher Comments`: If you have any comments or suggestions for your teacher, please provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Peer Attendance**: (`Peer Attendance`, `select_one`, required): Indicates whether you attended all meetings or events with your peer. Please select "True" if you attended all meetings, "False" if you did not attend any, "Sometimes" if you attended some, or "Unable to determine" if you're unsure.
* **Overall Rating** (`Overall Rating`, `number`, required): Rate your peer's overall performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Peer Rating** (`Peer Rating`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the top three ratings that best describe your peer's performance.
* **Peer Name** (`Peer Name`, `text`, optional): If you have direct interaction with your peer, please provide their name.
* **Communication Skills** (`Communication Skills`, `text`, optional): Describe your peer's communication skills.
* **Collaboration** (`Collaboration`, `text`, optional): Describe your peer's collaboration skills.
* **Peer Comments** (`Peer Comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments about your peer's performance.
* **Teacher Comments** (`Teacher Comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any comments or suggestions for your teacher, please provide them.
* **Teacher Comments 2** (`Teacher Comments 2`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions for your teacher, please provide them. 

## Tips
* Be honest and objective when rating your peer's performance.
* Use specific examples or incidents to support your ratings and comments.
* Keep your comments concise and focused on your peer's performance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your teacher for guidance.
