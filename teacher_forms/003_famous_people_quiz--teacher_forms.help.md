# famous_people_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "famous_people_quiz" form is designed to collect information about historical figures. It is used to gather data on their birth dates, occupations, and notable periods of time in history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
- Start by filling out the "Name" field with the historical figure's name.
- Enter the birth date of the historical figure in the "Birth Date" field in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
- Check the appropriate box for the historical figure's occupation. If they have a different occupation, type it in the "Other Occupation" field.
- Determine if the historical figure was active during a specific "Period" of time. If they were active during a different time, type it in the "Other Period" field.
- Check the boxes for any notable "Fields" the historical figure was active in. If they were active in a different field, type it in the "Other Fields" field.
- Finally, enter the "Time of Day" the historical figure was active in the "Time" field in the format hh:mm.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the historical figure's name.
* **Birth Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the historical figure's birth date in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Occupation** (Select, Optional): Check the box for the historical figure's occupation.
* **Other Occupation** (Text, Optional): Enter the historical figure's occupation if it's different from the options provided.
* **Period** (Select, Optional): Check the box for the historical figure's period of activity.
* **Other Period** (Text, Optional): Enter the historical figure's period of activity if it's different from the options provided.
* **Fields** (Select, Optional): Check the boxes for the historical figure's active fields.
* **Other Fields** (Select, Optional): Enter the historical figure's active fields if they are different from the options provided.
* **Fields Others Other** (Text, Optional): Enter the historical figure's active fields if they are different from the options provided.
* **Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the historical figure's time of activity in the format hh:mm.
