# music_school_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students who are interested in signing up for music lessons at the school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with your information. Each field is optional, so you can choose to provide as much or as little information as you like.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter your name as it should be listed in our records.
* **Preferred Class Days**: Select which days you would prefer to have your classes. You can choose one or more of the available options.
* **Start Time**: Choose the start time that works best for you. You can select from the available options.
* **Instrument**: Select the instrument you would like to learn.
* **Student Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and information.
* **Teacher Name**: If you have a preferred teacher, you can enter their name here. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Preferred Class Time**: Select the time of day that you prefer for your classes. Please note that this field is not the same as start time.
* **Student Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any special requests or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
