# Special Education Progress Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track the progress of students in special education programs. It captures information about the student's ID, name, teacher's ID, name, and the date of assessment, providing a snapshot of the student's development at that point in time.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the student's ID to uniquely identify the student.
2. Enter the student's name to provide their full name.
3. Select the teacher's ID to identify the staff member responsible for the student's education.
4. Enter the teacher's name to provide their full name.
5. Choose the assessment date to specify the date when the progress report was made.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Id** (`student_id`, Number, Optional): Enter the unique ID number assigned to the student. This is used to track individual student progress over time.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, Text, Optional): Provide the student's full name for identification purposes.
* **Teacher Id** (`teacher_id`, Number, Optional): Enter the unique ID number assigned to the teacher who is working with the student.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, Text, Optional): Provide the teacher's full name for identification purposes.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, Date, Optional): Choose the date when the progress report was made, marking the point in time when the report was generated.
