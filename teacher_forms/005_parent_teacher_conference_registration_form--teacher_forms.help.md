# Parent Teacher Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Teacher Conference Registration Form is designed to streamline the process of scheduling parent-teacher conferences for students. This form is used to collect information from both parents and teachers to facilitate effective communication and organization of conferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your parent's name in the "Parent Name" field.
2. Enter your teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Parent Phone" field.
5. Select the preferred conference day from the "Conference Day" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the conference time in the "Conference Time" field.
7. Select the person assigned to attend the conference from the "Assigned To" dropdown menu.
8. Enter the school's email address in the "School Email" field.
9. Enter the school's phone number in the "School Phone" field.
10. Enter the school's address in the "School Address" field.
11. Enter your name as the teacher's signature in the "Teacher Signature" field.
12. Enter your name as the parent's signature in the "Parent Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the parent.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the teacher.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, text, optional): Enter the teacher's phone number.
* **Conference Day** (`conference_day`, select_one, required): Select the preferred conference day.
* **Conference Time** (`conference_time`, time, required): Enter the conference time.
* **Notes** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required): Select the person assigned to attend the conference.
* **Assigned By** (`assigned_by`, select_one, optional): Select the person assigning the conference.
* **Conference Date** (`parent_conference_date`, date, required): Enter the parent's conference date.
* **Conference Time** (`parent_conference_time`, time, optional): Enter the parent's conference time.
* **Teacher Conference Date** (`teacher_conference_date`, date, required): Enter the teacher's conference date.
* **Teacher Conference Time** (`teacher_conference_time`, time, required): Enter the teacher's conference time.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the school's name.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, required): Enter the school's email address.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, required): Enter the school's phone number.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, required): Enter the school's address.
* **Teacher Signature** (`teacher_signature`, text, required): Enter the teacher's signature.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, text, required): Enter the parent's signature.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Conference Location** (`conference_location`, text, optional): Enter the conference location.
