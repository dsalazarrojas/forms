# organization_awards_nomination_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for an award nomination from an organization, including the recipient, submission date and time, and contact information for the submitter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the award type (Awards Nomination or Other) in the "Award Planning" section.
2. Enter the nomination recipient's name and any additional details in the "Nomination Recipient" field.
3. Enter the recipient's email address in the "Email Notification" field.
4. Select a date for the submission in the "Nomination Date" field.
5. Enter a brief description of the nomination in the "Description" field.
6. Enter your name and contact information in the "Submitter" section.
7. Enter your email address in the "Submitter Email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Submitter Phone" field.
9. Enter your job title and department in the "Job Title" and "Department" fields.
10. Select your job role in the "Job Role" field.
11. Enter your work phone number and email address in the "Work Phone" and "Work Email" fields.
12. Enter your work extensions (1-7) in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Award Planning (1)** (`Do you plan to provide awards`, select_one): Select whether the award is Awards Nomination or Other.

* **Contact Name (6)** (`Contact Name`, text): Enter your name.
* **Contact Phone (7)** (`Contact Phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Email (8)** (`Contact Email`, email): Enter your email address.
* **Submit Date (9)** (`Submit Date`, date): Select a date for the submission.
* **Submit Time (10)** (`Submit Time`, time): Select a time for the submission.
* **Submitter Name (11)** (`Submitter Name`, text): Enter your name.
* **Submitter Email (12)** (`Submitter Email`, email): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone (13)** (`Submitter Phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Job Title (14)** (`Job Title`, text): Enter your job title.
* **Department (15)** (`Department`, text): Enter your department.
* **Job Role (16)** (`Job Role`, text): Select your job role.
* **Work Phone (17)** (`Work Phone`, text): Enter your work phone number.
* **Work Email (18)** (`Work Email`, email): Enter your work email address.
* **Work Extension 1 (19)** (`Work Extension 1`, number): Enter your work extension 1.
* **Work Extension 2 (20)** (`Work Extension 2`, number): Enter your work extension 2.
* **Work Extension 3 (21)** (`Work Extension 3`, number): Enter your work extension 3.

## Tips
* Make sure to check the accuracy of all fields before submission.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
