# course_completion_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and record course completion information for students. It helps teachers and administrators monitor student progress and completion status in their courses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Course Name** in the first field.
2. If applicable, provide your **Student ID**.
3. Enter your **Grade** obtained in the course (if applicable).
4. Select the **Course Progress** status (Completed or Incomplete).
5. Enter the **Date Completed** when the course was finished.
6. Add any **Submission Notes** if necessary.
7. Input your **Email** address (if applicable).
8. Enter your **Phone Number** (if applicable).
9. Select the **Course Completion Date** when the course was completed.
10. Choose the **Course Status** (Active, Inactive, or Completed).
11. Add any **Teacher Comments** or **Student Comments** if necessary.
12. Select who **Submitted By** (Self, Teacher, or Administrator).
13. Input the **Submitted At** time when the form was submitted.
14. Leave the **Created At** and **Updated At** fields for system use.
15. Enter the **Deleted At** time when the form was deleted (if applicable).
16. Choose who **Submitted** the form (User, Teacher, or Admin).
17. Enter the **Course Completion Form ID**, **Course ID**, and **User ID**, which are automatically generated fields for system use.
18. Select additional data from the **Form Data** field if necessary.
