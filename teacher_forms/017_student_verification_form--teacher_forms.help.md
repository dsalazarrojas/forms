# Student Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Verification Form is a tool used to verify student information and track their progress. It's designed to gather necessary data for reporting and administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the title of the form as per the school's or institution's requirement.
2. Choose the correct category for the student.
3. Enter a brief description of the student, if required.
4. Enter a Form ID, which is likely a unique identifier for the student.
5. Answer the questions about assigned tools, output file, and other submission details.
6. Enter the email address of the student or contact person.
7. Provide notes, if necessary.
8. Confirm the submission details, including the submitted-from and submitted-to information.
9. Review and submit the form.
