# Student Behavior Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to walk you through the process of completing the Student Behavior Survey form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and answer the questions as accurately as possible.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled in, but feel free to skip optional ones.
3. Enter your honest and thoughtful responses to provide helpful feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Behavior Survey** (`student_behavior_survey`): This is the main section of the form, where you can provide your thoughts and observations about your behavior as a student. Please use this space to share your ideas and insights freely.
- **Student suggestions** (`student_suggestions`): Share any suggestions you have for improving the school or educational setting. Be as specific and creative as you can be.
- **Teacher suggestions** (`teacher_suggestions`): If you have any suggestions for teachers or educators, this is the place to share them. Think about how they can be supported or improved.
- **Student concerns** (`student_concerns`): If you have any concerns or issues you'd like to raise, use this field to express them. Your feedback is valuable in helping the school understand your needs.
- **School suggestions** (`school_suggestions`): Suggest any improvements you think the school could make for better student outcomes or experiences.
- **Teacher suggestions 2** (`teacher_suggestions_2`): Another opportunity to share suggestions for teachers or educators.
- **Teacher behaviors** (`teacher_behaviors`): Describe any positive or negative behaviors you've observed from teachers or educators.
- **Student suggestions 2** (`student_suggestions_2`): More space for student suggestions.
- **School behaviors** (`school_behaviors`): Share any observations about the school's behavior or policies.
- **Teacher comments** (`teacher_comments`): If you have any comments or feedback for teachers, this is the place to share them.
- **Student comments** (`student_comments`): Your comments and feedback are valuable in helping the school understand your needs and experiences.
- **School comments** (`school_comments`): If you have any comments or feedback for the school administration or staff, use this field.
- **Form completion** (`form_completion`): Enter the date and time you completed the form. This helps keep track of when the feedback was provided.
- **Teacher suggestions 2** (`teacher_suggestions_2`): More suggestions for teachers or educators.
- **Student suggestions 3** (`student_suggestions_3`): Additional space for student suggestions.
- **Teacher suggestions 3** (`teacher_suggestions_3`): More space for teacher suggestions.
- **Student concerns 2** (`student_concerns_2`): If you have any further concerns or issues to raise, use this field.
- **Teacher suggestions 4** (`teacher_suggestions_4`): More suggestions for teachers or educators.
- **School behaviors 2** (`school_behaviors_2`): Share any observations about the school's behavior or policies.
- **Teacher suggestions 5** (`teacher_suggestions_5`): More space for teacher suggestions.
- **Student suggestions 5** (`student_suggestions_5`): Additional space for student suggestions.
- **School suggestions 2** (`school_suggestions_2`): More space for school suggestions.
- **Teacher comments 2** (`teacher_comments_2`): More space for teacher feedback.
- **School suggestions 3** (`school_suggestions_3`): Additional space for school suggestions.
- **Teacher suggestions 6** (`teacher_suggestions_6`): More space for teacher suggestions.

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible with your feedback.
- Be respectful and constructive in your comments.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to contact the school administration or staff.
- Your feedback is valuable in helping the school improve. Thank you for taking the time to complete this form.
