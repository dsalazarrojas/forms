# Sample Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Sample Course Evaluation Form is a tool used to gather feedback from teachers on their courses. This form helps the course creators and instructors understand the strengths and weaknesses of their courses, which can be used to improve future course development and instruction.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the `teacher_id` of the teacher who taught the course.
2. Enter the `course_id` of the course being evaluated.
3. Enter the date of evaluation.
4. Answer the questions on the course's content, usefulness, and overall rating.
5. Enter any additional comments about the course.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Id (1)**: Enter the ID number of the teacher who taught the course.
* **Course Id (2)**: Enter the ID number of the course being evaluated.
* **Course Name (3)**: Enter the name of the course being evaluated. (Note: This field is not required, but it can be useful for context)
* **Evaluation Date (4)**: Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **How would you rate the course? (5)**: Rate the course on a scale of Good or Fair.
* **Question1 Answer (6)**: Select all that apply for your response to Question 1.
* **What did you think of the course content? (7)**: Enter your thoughts about the course content.
* **Question2 Answer (8)**: Enter your answer to Question 2.
* **Did you find this course useful? (9)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to answer this question.
* **Question3 Answer (10)**: Select all that apply for your response to Question 3.

## Tips

* Please be honest and thorough in your responses.
* If you have any additional comments, please use the "What did you think of the course content?" field to provide more information.
* If you need help or clarification on any of the questions, please ask your instructor or course creator for assistance.
