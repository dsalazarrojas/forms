# Academic Honesty Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Honesty Form is a mandatory pledge that students must sign to confirm their understanding and commitment to maintaining academic honesty in all their coursework.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Fill in your full name and student ID in the respective fields.
3. Select the correct answer for each question regarding your understanding of academic dishonesty, plagiarism, citation practices, collaboration boundaries, and consequences of academic dishonesty.
4. Indicate your commitment to upholding academic honesty in all your coursework.
5. If you have any questions or concerns, type them in the "Questions or Concerns" field.
6. Finally, sign the form with your full name and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as per your identification documents.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, required): Enter your official student identification number.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the course for which this pledge applies.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your course instructor.
* **Academic Honesty Understanding** (`academic_honesty_understanding`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes, completely" if you understand what constitutes academic dishonesty.
* **Plagiarism Definition Knowledge** (`plagiarism_definition_knowledge`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes, completely" if you understand what plagiarism means.
* **Citation Practices Knowledge** (`citation_practices_knowledge`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes, completely" if you understand how to properly cite sources.
* **Collaboration Boundaries** (`collaboration_boundaries`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes, completely" if you understand when collaboration is allowed versus individual work.
* **Academic Honesty Commitment** (`academic_honesty_commitment`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes, I commit" to confirm your commitment to upholding academic honesty.
* **Consequences Acknowledgment** (`consequences_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes, I understand" to confirm your understanding of the consequences of academic dishonesty.
* **Resources Used Statement** (`resources_used_statement`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes, I certify" to confirm that you have properly cited all resources used in your work.
* **Original Work Statement** (`original_work_statement`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes, I certify" to confirm that the work you submit is your own original work.
* **Honor Code Familiarity** (`honor_code_familiarity`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes, very familiar" to confirm your familiarity with the institution's honor code or academic integrity policy.
* **Questions or Concerns** (`questions_or_concerns`, text, optional): Type any questions or concerns you may have about academic honesty expectations.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, text, required): Type your full name as your electronic signature.
* **Date of Signature** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you signed this pledge.
