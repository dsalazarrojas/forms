# Academic Integrity Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents of academic integrity violations, ensuring that all necessary information is collected to thoroughly investigate these incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in a clear and concise manner, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Ensure that all fields are completed, especially those marked as "required" to avoid submission errors.
3. For date fields, please use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. For fields where possible, provide supporting evidence or documents that can aid in the investigation.
5. If unsure about any field, please do not hesitate to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Academic Integrity Notice --** (`integrity_header`, `note`, required: false): This is a title section that serves as a reminder of the purpose and importance of the form.
* **Student name** (`student_name`, `text`, required: true): Please enter the full name of the student involved in the incident.
* **Student id** (`student_id`, `text`, required: true): Enter the institutional identifier of the student, such as their student ID number.
* **Date of incident** (`incident_date`, `date`, required: false): If the incident occurred on a specific date, please enter it in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Description of incident** (`description_of_incident`, `text`, required: true): Provide a factual account of the events leading up to and including the incident.
* **Evidence provided** (`evidence_provided`, `text`, required: false): If available, please list any documents or witnesses that can support the incident report.
* **Recommended action** (`recommended_action`, `select_one`, required: false): Suggest a possible course of action to take in response to the incident, such as warning, probation, suspension, or dismissal.
* **Name of reporter** (`reporter_name`, `text`, required: true): Please enter your name and title, as this is the information that will be associated with your submission.
* **Reporter contact email** (`reporter_contact`, `email`, required: false): If you would like to be contacted with further questions or updates, please enter your contact email address.
* **Date submitted** (`date_submitted`, `date`, required: true): This field will automatically populate with the date you submit the form, but please ensure it reflects the correct date of submission.
