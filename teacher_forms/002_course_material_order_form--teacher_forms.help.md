# Course Material Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and manage course material orders. It helps to collect important information about the course, customer, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields (indicated by the * required* label) with accurate information.
2. Enter the course name, price, and description in the respective fields.
3. Enter the customer's email, phone number, shipping and billing addresses.
4. Select the payment method and payment gateway ID.
5. Enter the quantity and shipping cost of the course material.
6. Confirm the order date and time.
7. Enter any customer comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Form**: This field is used to enter the course material title or name.
* **Course Info**: This field is used to enter the course information, such as name, price, and description.
* **Payment Information**: This field is used to enter the payment details.
* **Payment Confirmation**: This field is used to confirm the payment.
* **Order Confirmation**: This field is used to confirm the order.
* **Order Summary**: This field is used to summarize the order details.
* **Payment Gateway**: This field is used to select the payment gateway.
* **Payment Method**: This field is used to select the payment method.
* **Course Name**: This field is used to enter the course name.
* **Course Price**: This field is used to enter the course price.
* **Course Description**: This field is used to enter the course description.
* **Customer Email**: This field is used to enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone**: This field is used to enter the customer's phone number.
* **Shipping Address**: This field is used to enter the shipping address.
* **Billing Address**: This field is used to enter the billing address.
* **Tax ID**: This field is used to enter the tax ID.
* **Coupon**: This field is used to enter any coupon codes or discounts.
* **Quantity**: This field is used to enter the quantity of course materials ordered.
* **Shipping Cost**: This field is used to enter the shipping cost.
* **Order Date**: This field is used to enter the order date.
* **Order Time**: This field is used to enter the order time.
* **Customer Comments**: This field is used to enter any comments or feedback from the customer.
* **Order Status**: This field is used to update the order status.
* **Payment Gateway ID**: This field is used to enter the payment gateway ID.
