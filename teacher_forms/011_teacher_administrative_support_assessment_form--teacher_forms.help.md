# Teacher Administrative Support Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the administrative support provided to teachers. It helps administrators and school staff evaluate the quality and effectiveness of support services, identify areas of improvement, and make informed decisions about resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field to understand what information is required.
2. Provide honest and detailed feedback in the fields marked as "text" (e.g., general_comments, support_quality, administrative_support, feedback).
3. Select the correct options for fields that are "select_multiple" or "text" with a limited set of options (e.g., support_availability, category).
4. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Comments** (`general_comments`, text, required): Use this field to provide any additional comments or thoughts about the support provided to the teacher.
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, number, optional): Rate the effectiveness of communication from the teacher on a scale of 1-10.
* **Availability Of Support** (`support_availability`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Quality Of Support** (`support_quality`, text, optional): Assess the quality of support provided to the teacher.
* **Quality Of Communication** (`communication_quality`, text, optional): Assess the quality of communication from the teacher.
* **Timeliness Of Support** (`support_timeliness`, text, optional): Assess the timeliness of support provided to the teacher.
* **Administrative Support** (`administrative_support`, text, optional): Assess the level of administrative support provided to the teacher.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any feedback or suggestions to the teacher.
* **Administrative Feedback** (`administrative_feedback`, text, optional): Provide any feedback or suggestions to the administration team.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, text, optional): Assess your overall satisfaction with the support provided to the teacher.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Use this field to provide any additional comments or thoughts.
* **Category** (`category`, text, optional): Select a category relevant to the support provided to the teacher (e.g., IT, HR, etc.).
* **Subcategory** (`subcategory`, text, optional): Select a subcategory relevant to the support provided to the teacher (e.g., hardware, software, etc.).
* **Priority** (`priority`, text, optional): Select a priority level for the support provided to the teacher (e.g., high, medium, low).
* **Due Date** (`due_date`, text, optional): Enter the due date for the support provided to the teacher.
* **Status** (`status`, text, optional): Select the status of the support provided to the teacher (e.g., completed, pending, etc.).
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, optional): Select who was assigned to provide the support to the teacher (e.g., teacher's name).
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Select who submitted the support request (e.g., teacher's name).
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, text, optional): Enter the date the support request was submitted.

## Tips
- Be honest and objective when providing feedback.
- Use specific examples to support your comments.
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
