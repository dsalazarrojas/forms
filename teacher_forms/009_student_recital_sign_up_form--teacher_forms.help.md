# Student Recital Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Recital Sign Up Form is used by teachers to register their students for a recital. This form captures the teacher's name and email address for communication and administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the desired page number (currently only Page 1 is provided).
2. Enter your name in the field titled "teacher_name".
3. Enter your email address in the field titled "teacher_email".
4. Click the "submit" button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **student_recital_sign_up_form** (1:1): This is the main section of the form, which currently does not have any specific fields to fill out as the page structure is currently not defined further.
- **teacher_name** (3: teacher_name): This field is intended to capture the teacher's name associated with the student signing up for the recital. Enter your name in this field.
- **teacher_email** (3: teacher_email): Enter your email address here, which will be used by the system for communication and administrative purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled out your name and email address correctly to ensure that you can be reached and communicated with for recital details.
* If you encounter any issues with the form or have questions, contact your administrator or support team.
