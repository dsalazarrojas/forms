# Distance Learning Opt In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to track and record information on distance learning programs, including student numbers, modes of delivery, implementation dates and times, and opt-in information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete the form, start by selecting the "Number of students" field, where you can enter the total number of students participating in the program.
2. Next, choose the "Mode of delivery" options, selecting the relevant modes of delivery (face-to-face or online) that apply to your program.
3. Enter the "Implementation date" and "Implementation time" fields to record the scheduled start date and time of the program.
4. Enter the "Implementation duration" field to specify the length of the program.
5. Select the "Implementation time zone" and "Implementation frequency" fields to record the relevant time zone and frequency of the implementation.
6. Enter the "Opt-in date" and "Opt-in time" fields to record the date and time of the opt-in process.
7. Select the "Opt-in mode" options for the first and second modes of delivery.
8. Enter the "Opt-in duration" field to specify the length of the opt-in process.
9. Select the "Opt-in frequency" for the first and second opt-in modes.
10. Enter the "Opt-in note" fields for free-text comments on the opt-in process.
11. Select the "Implementation status" and "Opt-in status" fields to record the progress of the implementation and opt-in process.
12. Finally, enter any additional "Implementation note" or "Opt-in note" fields for free-text comments on the implementation and opt-in processes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Number of students**: Enter the total number of students participating in the program.
* **Mode of delivery**:
	+ Face-to-face: The program is delivered in-person.
	+ Online: The program is delivered online.
* **Implementation date**: Enter the date the program is scheduled to be implemented.
* **Implementation time**: Enter the time the program is scheduled to be implemented.
* **Implementation duration**: Enter the length of the program.
* **Implementation time zone**: Select the time zone relevant to the implementation.
* **Implementation frequency**: Select the frequency of the implementation (daily, weekly, or monthly).
* **Opt-in date**: Enter the date of the opt-in process.
* **Opt-in time**: Enter the time of the opt-in process.
* **Opt-in mode**:
	+ Face-to-face: The opt-in process is delivered in-person.
	+ Online: The opt-in process is delivered online.
* **Opt-in duration**: Enter the length of the opt-in process.
* **Opt-in frequency**:
	+ Daily: The opt-in process happens daily.
	+ Weekly: The opt-in process happens weekly.
	+ Monthly: The opt-in process happens monthly.
* **Opt-in note**: Enter free-text comments on the opt-in process.
* **Implementation status**: Select the status of the implementation (implemented, not implemented, or not known).
* **Opt-in status**:
	+ Accepted: The opt-in process has been accepted.
	+ Not accepted: The opt-in process has been rejected.
	+ Pending: The opt-in process is pending.
* **Implementation note**: Enter free-text comments on the implementation process.
* **Opt-in note**: Enter free-text comments on the opt-in process.

Note that some fields may not be applicable to your program or context, and you should only fill out fields that are relevant to your specific situation.
