# Adjunct Teaching Preference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for adjunct instructors to provide their teaching preferences when applying to teach at our institution. It will help us to better understand your availability, desired teaching formats, and course interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your instructor information, including your name and email address.
2. Select your preferred courses to teach.
3. Choose your preferred campus or online teaching locations.
4. Indicate which semesters you are available to teach (Fall, Spring, or Summer).
5. Specify your maximum course load per term.
6. Select your preferred teaching format (in person, hybrid, or online).
7. Add any additional notes or preferences.
8. Finally, sign and date the form to confirm your identity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Instructor name** (Required): Please enter your full professional name.
* **Email address** (Required): This is your official email address for communication and scheduling purposes.
* **Preferred courses to teach** (Required): List the course titles or codes you are interested in teaching.
* **Preferred campus or online** (Optional): Choose your preferred teaching location (Main campus, Satellite campus, or Online).
* **Semester availability** (Required): Select which semesters you are available to teach (Fall, Spring, or Summer).
* **Maximum course load** (Required): Specify the maximum number of courses you can teach per term.
* **Teaching format preference** (Required): Choose your preferred teaching format (In person, Hybrid, or Online).
* **Additional notes** (Optional): Provide any other preferences or constraints that may impact your teaching availability.
* **Signature name** (Required): Type your name to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
* **Additional notes are not a required field but are encouraged to be used for any other preferences or constraints you may have.
