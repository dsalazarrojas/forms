# Student Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a recommendation for a student's development and progress within the institution. This form will be used by staff members to evaluate student performance and provide feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the student's name as per your knowledge.
2. Select the student's grade from the provided options.
3. Enter the student's date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, Required): Enter the student's name as per your knowledge.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, number, Optional): Select the student's grade from the provided options. This will help us understand their current level of performance.
* **Student Date of Birth** (`student_dob`, date, Optional): Enter the student's date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`. This will help us understand their age and developmental stage.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any information, please check with the previous reviewer or student profile manager.
* Please submit the form once you have filled all the required fields.
