# education_session_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about education sessions, including session details, contact information, booking details, and any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Session Details** section (Page 1) with the necessary information about the session, such as the title, description, or any other relevant details.
2. In the **Contact Details** section (Page 2), enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant contact details.
3. In the **Booking Details** section (Page 3), provide the necessary booking information, such as dates, times, and numbers of attendees.
4. In the **Additional Notes** section (Page 4), add any extra comments or information about the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Details** (Page 1): This section is used to provide general information about the session, such as its title and description.
* **Contact Details** (Page 2): This section is used to provide your contact information, such as your name, email, and phone number.
* **Booking Details** (Page 3): This section is used to specify the details of the booking, including dates and times.
* **Additional Notes** (Page 4): This section is optional and can be used to provide any extra comments or information about the session.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the administrator or instructor for assistance.
