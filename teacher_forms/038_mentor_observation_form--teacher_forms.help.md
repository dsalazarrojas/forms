# Mentor Observation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mentor Observation Form is used to provide feedback and evaluation on new teachers, focusing on their strengths and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the new teacher from the list in the "New Teacher" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the date of the observation in the "Observation Date" field.
3. Provide specific feedback on the new teacher's performance in the "Feedback" field.
4. Summarize the observation in the "Observation Summary" field.
5. Offer any recommendations for the new teacher in the "Recommendations" field.
6. Select who observed the new teacher from the "Observed By" dropdown menu.
7. Enter the name of the person who observed the new teacher in the "Observer Name" field.
8. Sign the form as the mentor/observer in the "Mentor Signature" field.
9. Sign the form as the new teacher in the "New Teacher Signature" field.
10. Select the completion status of the form from the "Observation Form Completion" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **New Teacher**: Select the new teacher whose performance you are observing. This field is required.
  - Type: Select One
  - Required: Yes
- **Observation Date**: Enter the date of the observation. This field is required.
  - Type: Date
  - Required: Yes
- **Feedback**: Provide specific feedback on the new teacher's performance. This field is required.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes
- **Observation Summary**: Summarize your observation of the new teacher's performance.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes
- **Recommendations**: Offer any recommendations for the new teacher's growth and development.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes
- **Observed By**: Select who observed the new teacher.
  - Type: Select One
  - Required: Yes
- **Observer Name**: Enter the name of the person who observed the new teacher.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes
- **Mentor Signature**: Sign the form as the mentor/observer.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes
- **New Teacher Signature**: Sign the form as the new teacher.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes
- **Observation Form Completion**: Select the completion status of the form.
  - Type: Select One
  - Required: Yes
