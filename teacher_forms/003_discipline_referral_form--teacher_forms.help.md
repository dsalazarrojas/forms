# Discipline Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document incidents or referrals regarding student discipline. Please fill in the form accurately and thoroughly to provide a clear account of what happened.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the First Name and Last Name fields with the student's information.
2. Enter the Student ID number.
3. Select the Date and Time of the incident.
4. Describe the Location of the Incident.
5. Provide a detailed explanation of the Nature of the Incident.
6. Document the Actions Taken by staff or other relevant parties.
7. Enter the Date and Time of the actions taken.
8. Describe the Staff Involvement and Student Involvement.
9. If applicable, mention any Other Incidents related to the student.
10. Add any additional Notes or comments about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the student's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the student's last name.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required): Enter the student's ID number.
* **Date of Incident** (`date_of_incident`, date, required): Select the date of the incident.
* **Time of Incident** (`time_of_incident`, time, required): Select the time of the incident.
* **Location of Incident** (`location_of_incident`, text, required): Describe the location where the incident occurred.
* **Nature of Incident** (`nature_of_incident`, text, required): Explain the nature of the incident in detail.
* **Actions Taken** (`actions_taken`, text, required): Describe the actions taken by staff or other relevant parties.
* **Date of Action** (`date_of_action`, date, required): Select the date of the actions taken.
* **Time of Action** (`time_of_action`, time, required): Select the time of the actions taken.
* **Staff Involvement** (`staff_involvement`, text, required): Describe the involvement of staff in the incident.
* **Student Involvement** (`student_involvement`, text, required): Describe the student's involvement in the incident.
* **Other Incidents** (`other_incidents`, text, required): If applicable, mention any other incidents related to the student.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Add any additional comments or notes about the incident.

# Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Be clear and concise in your explanations.
* Use the correct format for dates and times (dd/mm/yyyy and HH:MM AM/PM).
* Proofread your answers before submitting the form.
