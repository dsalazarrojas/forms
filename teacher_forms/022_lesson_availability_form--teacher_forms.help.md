# Lesson Availability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lesson Availability Form is a tool for teachers to communicate their schedules, lesson plans, and contact information to school administration and other stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the Lesson Availability Form Class field with the class you are teaching.
2. Select the days you are available to teach in the Schedule field.
3. Enter your preferred time of availability in the Time field.
4. Select the date(s) you are available to teach in the Date field.
5. Enter any additional notes about your availability in the Note field.
6. Provide your email address in the Email field.
7. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
8. Confirm your email address status in the Email Confirmed field.
9. Confirm your phone number status in the Phone Confirmed field.
10. Enter any additional notes about your availability in the Additional Notes field.
11. Finally, provide a brief statement about your lesson availability in the Lesson Availability field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lesson Availability Form Class** (`lesson_availability_form_class`, text, required): This field is used to select the class you are teaching.
* **Lesson Availability Group Lessons** (`lesson_availability_group_lesson`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to indicate which group(s) of lessons you are available to teach. 
* **Schedule** (`lesson_availability_schedule`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select the specific day you are available to teach (e.g. Monday, Tuesday, etc.).
* **Time** (`lesson_availability_time`, time, optional): This field is used to enter your preferred time of availability.
* **Date** (`lesson_availability_date`, date, optional): This field is used to select the date(s) you are available to teach.
* **Note** (`lesson_availability_note`, note, optional): This field is used to enter any additional notes or information about your availability.
* **Email** (`lesson_availability_email`, email, optional): This field is used to enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`lesson_availability_phone`, text, optional): This field is used to enter your phone number.
* **Email Confirmed** (`lesson_availability_email_confirmed`, select_one, optional): This field is used to confirm whether your email address is confirmed or not.
* **Phone Confirmed** (`lesson_availability_phone_confirmed`, select_one, optional): This field is used to confirm whether your phone number is confirmed or not.
* **Additional Notes** (`lesson_availability_additional_notes`, note, optional): This field is used to enter any additional notes about your availability.
* **Lesson Availability** (`lesson_availability_lesson_availability`, text, optional): This field is used to provide a brief statement about your lesson availability.
