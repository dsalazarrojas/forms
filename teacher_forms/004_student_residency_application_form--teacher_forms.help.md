# Student Residency Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Residency Application Form is designed to collect relevant information from students who are applying to be a resident in a particular program or institution. This form will help the evaluation process by gathering essential details about the student.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Student ID, Occupation, and Address accurately and truthfully.
2. Select your Education Level from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter your Email and Phone Number in the correct format.
4. Fill in your Student Details, ensuring that you provide a clear and concise description.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, required): This is your unique student identifier.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, text, required): Please enter your current occupation or field of study.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your current address, including street, city, and state/region.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, required): Select your current level of education (High School, College/University, Doctoral/Ph.D.).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your email address in the format [name@example.com](mailto:name@example.com).
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number in the format (###) ###-#####.
* **Student Details** (`student_details`, text, required): Fill in a clear and concise description of your student qualifications and circumstances.

## Tips
- Please fill in all required fields accurately and truthfully.
- Use the correct format for Email and Phone Number.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
