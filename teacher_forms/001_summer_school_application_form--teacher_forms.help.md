# Summer School Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Summer School Application Form is designed to gather information from students who are interested in attending the summer school program. The form helps us to collect demographic data, payment information, and other relevant details to ensure a smooth and efficient application process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Step 1**: Please ensure that you have all the necessary information required in the form before starting to fill it out.
2. **Step 2**: Fill in the form with accurate and complete information.
3. **Step 3**: Review your answers for any errors before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, text, required): Enter the student's name as it appears on their ID or passport.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select_one, optional): Select a payment method from the options provided, e.g., "Square" or "PayPal".
* **Tuition Fees** (`Tuition Fees`, number, optional): Enter the amount of tuition fees you are willing to pay. Please ensure the format is as per the instructions provided.
* **Student Address** (`Student Address`, note, optional): Enter the student's address in a note field. This is not a regular text field, so please keep the information concise.
* **Guardian Name** (`Guardian Name`, text, optional): Enter the guardian's name as per the provided instructions.
* **Guardian Phone** (`Guardian Phone`, text, optional): Enter the guardian's phone number.
* **Student Email** (`Student Email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Application Date** (`Application Date`, date, optional): Enter the date of application. Please ensure the format is as per the instructions provided.
