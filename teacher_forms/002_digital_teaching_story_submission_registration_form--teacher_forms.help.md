# digital_teaching_story_submission_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect submissions for digital teaching stories from educators. It is meant to gather information about the user, including their name, email, and submission details such as subject, date, and time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the User Info section, which includes your name and any other relevant information about yourself as an educator.
2. Fill in your Email address to ensure we can contact you regarding your submission.
3. Select a Subject to describe the teaching story you are submitting.
4. Write a brief Teaching Story about your experience.
5. Enter the Submission Date and Submission Time when you are submitting your story.
6. Once you have filled out all the required and optional fields, submit the form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info**: Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself as an educator. 
* **User Name**: Enter your name as it should be displayed on the submission.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your submission. 
* **Subject**: Briefly describe the focus or theme of your teaching story.
* **Teaching Story**: Share your teaching story in this text field. This is an opportunity to express your experience and insights as an educator.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you are submitting this teaching story.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the exact time you are submitting this teaching story.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a successful submission.
* Use a clear and concise format for your teaching story.
* If you have any attachments or files to support your story, you can upload them separately after completing this form.
