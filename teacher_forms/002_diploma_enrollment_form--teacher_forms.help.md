# Diploma Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Diploma Enrollment Form is used to collect information from teachers for the purpose of their diploma enrollment. This form helps to streamline the enrollment process and ensures that all necessary details are collected in a structured and organized manner.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the relevant fields:
	* **User name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
	* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for any further communication.
	* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for any further communication.
2. Provide your diploma details:
	* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
	* **Enrollment date**: Enter the date you enrolled for the diploma program.
	* **Enrollment time**: Enter the time you enrolled for the diploma program.
	* **Diploma type**: Select the type of diploma you are enrolling for.
3. Select your payment method and status:
	* **Payment method**: Choose how you intend to pay for your diploma enrollment.
	* **Payment status**: Select the current status of your payment.
4. Add any additional notes:
	* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information you would like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for any further communication.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for any further communication.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Enrollment date**: Enter the date you enrolled for the diploma program.
* **Enrollment time**: Enter the time you enrolled for the diploma program.
* **Diploma type**: Select the type of diploma you are enrolling for.
* **Payment method**: Choose how you intend to pay for your diploma enrollment.
* **Payment status**: Select the current status of your payment.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information you would like to provide.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you need to add any additional comments or information, use the notes field.
* Review your entries carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
