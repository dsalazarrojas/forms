# k12_attendance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The k12_attendance_form is a tool for recording and monitoring student attendance in educational settings. It helps track student attendance, tardiness, and any notable comments or reasons for missing or late arrivals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the student's **name** in the "Student Name" field.
2. Select the **date** of the attendance entry.
3. Enter the **time** the student arrived.
4. Provide a brief explanation for any **reason for tardiness** in the "reason_tardy" field.
5. Indicate if the student was **absent** or **tardy** in the corresponding fields.
6. If applicable, provide a brief note or explanation for any **late or excused absence**.
7. Enter the student's **email address** and **phone number** for contact purposes.
8. Add any **comments** or additional notes about the student's attendance.
9. Leave a digital **signature** to confirm the accuracy of the data entered.
10. Indicate if the form was **submitted by** the school staff or other authorized personnel.
11. Choose the **date** the form was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student who attended the school on this date.
* **date**: Enter the date of the attendance entry (format: MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time Arrived**: Enter the time the student arrived at school.
* **reason_tardy**: Provide a brief explanation for any tardiness or reason for missing school.
* **absent**: Indicate if the student was absent on this date (Yes/No).
* **tardy**: Indicate if the student was tardy on this date (Yes/No).
* **excused**: Provide a brief explanation for any excused absence or tardiness.
* **late**: Indicate if the student was late on this date (Yes/No).
* **Note**: Add any comments or additional notes about the student's attendance.
* **email**: Enter the student's email address for contact purposes.
* **phone**: Enter the student's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the student's attendance.
* **Signature**: Leave a digital signature to confirm the accuracy of the data entered.
* **submitted_by**: Indicate who submitted the form (Yes/No).
* **submitted_date**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
