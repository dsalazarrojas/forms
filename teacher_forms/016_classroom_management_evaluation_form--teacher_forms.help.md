# Classroom Management Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Classroom Management Evaluation Form is designed to help teachers understand how their classroom management practices are perceived by their students. This form provides an opportunity for students to provide feedback on the teacher's ability to create a safe, respectful, and organized learning environment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form on your own device, using the options provided for each question.
* Answer each question honestly, considering your personal experience in the classroom.
* For questions with multiple-choice options, select the answer that best reflects your opinion.
* For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you feel is necessary to answer the question.
* Make sure to answer all required questions.
