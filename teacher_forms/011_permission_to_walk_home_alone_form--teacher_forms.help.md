# Permission To Walk Home Alone Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Permission To Walk Home Alone Form" is used to obtain parent or guardian permission for students to walk home from school or from after-school activities. This form is required to ensure that students have the necessary authorization to walk home alone and that parents or guardians are aware of the risks and responsibilities involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the school ID number to identify the school the student attends.
2. Select the activity ID number to indicate the reason for walking home from school or the after-school activity.
3. Enter the student ID number to identify the student.
4. Enter the parent or guardian ID number to ensure proper contact information is on file.
5. Enter the date of the event.
6. Choose the reason for walking home from school or after-school activities.
7. Add any additional comments or concerns related to the student's walk home.
8. Provide parent contact information in case of an emergency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **schoolid** (School ID, Number, Required): Enter the school ID number to identify the school the student attends.
* **activityid** (Activity ID, Number, Required): Select the activity ID number to indicate the reason for walking home from school or the after-school activity.
* **studentid** (Student ID, Number, Required): Enter the student ID number to identify the student.
* **parentid** (Parent ID, Number, Required): Enter the parent or guardian ID number to ensure proper contact information is on file.
* **dateid** (Date, Date, Required): Enter the date of the event.
* **timeid2** (Time, Time, Required): Enter the time the student will walk home.
* **reasonid** (Reason, Select One, Required): Choose the reason for walking home from school or after-school activities.
	+ Home from school
	+ Home from after school activity
	+ Home from school and after school activity
* **addcomments** (Additional Comments, Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or concerns related to the student's walk home.
* **parcontactinfo** (Parent Contact Info, Text, Optional): Provide parent contact information in case of an emergency.
