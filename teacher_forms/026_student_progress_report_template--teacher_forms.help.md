# Student Progress Report Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This template is designed to be used by teachers to track student progress in a subject.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "student_name" field.
2. Enter your name as the teacher in the "teacher_name" field.
3. Enter the subject the student is learning in the "subject" field.
4. Describe the student's current progress in the "student_progress" field.
5. Enter the student's current grade level in the "current_grade_level" field.
6. Add any comments or feedback you have about the student in the "student_comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **student_name**: Enter the student's name.
* **teacher_name**: Enter your name as the teacher.
* **subject**: Enter the subject the student is learning.
* **student_progress**: Describe the student's current progress, e.g., "Is meeting expectations" or "Needs extra support".
* **current_grade_level**: Enter the student's current grade level.
* **student_comments**: Add any comments or feedback you have about the student.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when filling out this form to ensure accurate and helpful progress reporting.
* Use this form to identify areas where a student may need extra support.
* Review and update progress reports regularly to track student progress over time.
* Consider discussing progress reports with the student to set goals and identify areas for improvement.
