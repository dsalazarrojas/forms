# wind_energy_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The wind_energy_quiz form is designed to collect information from users on wind energy-related topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by confirming you are ready to proceed with the quiz.
2. Answer the questions with the relevant options.
3. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "notes" field.
4. Submit your response by selecting the "submit" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Start (note)** (`start`, note, required: false): This is a placeholder for the user to acknowledge they are ready to start the quiz.
* **Questions (select_multiple)** (`questions`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all relevant options that apply to your wind energy experiences or knowledge. You can select one or more options.
* **Score (number)** (`score`, number, required: false): Enter your self-assigned score for the wind energy quiz.
* **Wind Energy Quiz (select_one)** (`wind_energy_quiz`, select_one, required: false): Select one option that best describes your wind energy response.
* **Wind Energy Quiz 2 (select_one)** (`wind_energy_quiz2`, select_one, required: false): Select one option that best describes your wind energy response.
* **Wind Energy Quiz 3 (select_one)** (`wind_energy_quiz3`, select_one, required: false): Select one option that best describes your wind energy response.
* **Notes (text)** (`notes`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or notes about your wind energy quiz experience.
* **Submit (select_one)** (`submit`, select_one, required: false): Choose "Yes" to submit your response and complete the quiz.

## Tips
* Review each question carefully to ensure you provide accurate information.
* Use the notes field to add any additional context or explanations.
* Make sure to submit your response to complete the quiz.
