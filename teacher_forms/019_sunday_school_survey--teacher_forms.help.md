# sunday_school_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students about their Sunday school experience, providing valuable insights for teachers and other stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and answer the questions based on your experience.
2. Make sure to fill out each field that applies to you.
3. If you're unsure about any question, try your best to provide a honest answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to our sunday school** (text): This is a brief introduction to let you know what to expect. Please don't worry about filling out this section, but feel free to add any comments or suggestions if you want.
* **How do you like the sunday school?** (select_one): Please choose one of the following options:
	+ True: You really enjoy the Sunday school.
	+ Somewhat: You have mixed feelings about the Sunday school.
	+ False: You don't like the Sunday school.
* **Comments** (text): This is an optional section where you can add any comments or suggestions about the Sunday school.
* **Favorite subject** (select_multiple): Please select all the subjects you enjoy learning about the most:
	+ Bible
	+ Sunday school
* **How would you rate your teacher?** (select_one): Please rate your teacher from 1 to 5:
	+ 1
	+ 2
	+ 3
	+ 4
	+ 5
* **How would you rate the sunday school?** (select_one): Please rate the Sunday school from 1 to 5:
	+ 1
	+ 2
	+ 3
	+ 4
	+ 5
* **Date** (date): Please select the date you attended the Sunday school.
* **Time** (time): Please select the time you attended the Sunday school.
* **Comments** (text): This is an optional section where you can add any comments or suggestions about the Sunday school.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you have any comments or suggestions, please add them to the "Comments" section.
* Don't worry if you're unsure about any question, just do your best to answer truthfully.
* This form is meant to be a quick and easy way to provide feedback, so don't feel overwhelmed by the number of questions.
