# visual_arts_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to evaluate and provide feedback on student-created artworks, ensuring quality and effectiveness in a structured and objective manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Artwork Title, a brief description of the artwork.
2. Input the Artist Name, the person who created the artwork.
3. Provide the Artwork Type, a description of the type of artwork (e.g., painting, sculpture, etc.).
4. Enter the date the artwork was completed, if applicable.
5. Enter the Artwork Grade, a numerical score out of 5 to assess the artwork's quality.
6. Provide Feedback on the artwork, if desired.
7. Enter your Name as the teacher or instructor.
8. Enter your Email, which will be used for communication and feedback exchange.
9. Enter your Phone Number, for easy contact.
10. Add any additional comments or feedback you'd like to include.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Artwork Title** (`text`, required/optional): Enter a brief title to describe the artwork.
* **Artist Name** (`text`, required/true): Enter the name of the artist who created the artwork.
* **Artwork Type** (`text`, required/optional): Describe the type of artwork (e.g., painting, sculpture, etc.).
* **Artwork Date** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date the artwork was completed, if applicable.
* **Artwork Grade** (`number`, required/true): Enter a numerical score, out of 5, to evaluate the artwork's quality.
* **Feedback** (`text`, required/optional): Provide your thoughts and feedback on the artwork.
* **Teacher Name** (`text`, required/true): Enter your name as the evaluating teacher or instructor.
* **Teacher Email** (`email`, required/true): Enter your email, for efficient communication and feedback exchange.
* **Teacher Phone** (`text`, required/true): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Comments** (`text`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or feedback you'd like to include.
* **Artwork File Upload** (`text`, required/optional): You can upload a file related to the artwork if necessary.
