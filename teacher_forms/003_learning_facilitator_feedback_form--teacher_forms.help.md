# Learning Facilitator Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Learning Facilitator Feedback Form is a tool for teachers to provide feedback on the performance of learning facilitators. This form helps to collect information about the facilitators' teaching style, communication skills, and ability to answer questions, as well as any comments or suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your name in the "What is your name" field.
2. Rate the facilitator's teaching style, communication skills, and ability to answer questions using the numbers 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions for the facilitator, enter them in the "Any comments" field.
4. If you are providing feedback about a specific facilitator, please enter their name and email address in the "Facilitators name" and "Facilitators email" fields respectively.
5. If you would like to provide a phone number for the facilitator, enter it in the "Facilitators phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name**: Enter your name, as the teacher who is providing feedback.
*   **How would you rate the facilitators teaching style**: Rate the facilitator's teaching style using numbers 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
*   **How would you rate the facilitators communication skills**: Rate the facilitator's communication skills using numbers 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
*   **How would you rate the facilitators ability to answer questions**: Rate the facilitator's ability to answer questions using numbers 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
*   **Any comments**: Enter any comments or suggestions you may have for the facilitator.
*   **Facilitators name**: Enter the name of the facilitator you are providing feedback about.
*   **Facilitators email**: Enter the email address of the facilitator you are providing feedback about.
*   **Facilitators phone**: Enter the phone number of the facilitator you are providing feedback about.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you have any comments or suggestions, try to be constructive and objective.
* If you are providing feedback about a specific facilitator, make sure to enter their name and contact information accurately.
