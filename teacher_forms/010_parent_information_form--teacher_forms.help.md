# parent_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect parent information for a student.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "student_name" field.
2. Provide the student's email address in the "student_email" field.
3. Enter the parent's name in the "parent_name" field.
4. Enter the student's grade level in the "student_grade" field.
5. Enter the parent's job in the "parent_job" field.
6. Enter the student's birthdate in the "student_birthdate" field.
7. Enter the parent's address in the "parent_address" field.
8. Enter the student's phone number in the "student_phone" field.
9. Enter the teacher's name in the "teacher_name" field.
10. Enter the parent's work phone number in the "parent_work_phone" field.
11. Enter the student's school name in the "student_school" field.
12. Enter the student's room number in the "student_room_number" field.
13. Enter the parent's school in the "parent_school" field.
14. Enter the student's grade level in the "student_grade_level" field.
15. Enter the student's class in the "student_class" field.
16. Enter any additional notes in the "parent_note" field.
17. Enter any additional notes about the student in the "student_notes" field.
18. Enter any additional notes for the teacher in the "teacher_note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (student_name)**: Enter the student's full name.
* **Student Email (student_email)**: Enter the student's email address.
* **Parent Name (parent_name)**: Enter the parent's full name.
* **Student Grade (student_grade)**: Enter the student's grade level (e.g., 1st, 2nd, 3rd, etc.).
* **Parent Job (parent_job)**: Enter the parent's job title or profession.
* **Student Birthdate (student_birthdate)**: Enter the student's birthdate in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Parent Address (parent_address)**: Enter the parent's home address.
* **Student Phone (student_phone)**: Enter the student's phone number.
* **Teacher Name (teacher_name)**: Enter the teacher's name.
* **Parent Work Phone (parent_work_phone)**: Enter the parent's work phone number.
* **Student School (student_school)**: Enter the student's school name.
* **Student Room Number (student_room_number)**: Enter the student's room number.
* **Parent School (parent_school)**: Enter the parent's school name.
* **Student Grade Level (student_grade_level)**: Enter the student's grade level (e.g., 1st, 2nd, 3rd, etc.).
* **Student Class (student_class)**: Enter the student's class name or section.
* **Parent Note (parent_note)**: Enter any additional notes about the parent.
* **Student Notes (student_notes)**: Enter any additional notes about the student.
* **Teacher Note (teacher_note)**: Enter any additional notes about the teacher.
