## Form Purpose
The "Field Trip Interest Form" is used to collect information from teachers about their students' interests and preferences for field trips. This helps us better understand the needs and expectations of our teachers and students, ensuring that our field trip programs are tailored to meet their needs and provide a valuable and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the trip in the "Trip Name" field.
2. Select the date you would like to schedule the trip in the "Trip Date" field.
3. Enter the location of the trip in the "Trip Location" field.
4. Choose the grade level(s) you would like to include on the trip in the "Grade Level" field (select one or more).
5. If you have a specific number of students in mind, enter it in the "Student Count" field.
6. Enter your name in the "Teacher's Name" field.
7. Enter your school name in the "School Name" field.
8. If you have a email address, please enter it in the "Teacher Email" field.
9. If you have a phone number, please enter it in the "Teacher's Phone" field.
10. In the "Trip Details" field, provide any additional information you think would be helpful for us to know about the trip.
11. In the "Comments" field, add any comments or notes that would be helpful for us to know about the trip.
12. In the "Trip Interests" field, select the subjects you would like to focus on during the trip (choose one or more).
13. In the "More Trip Interests" field, select any additional subjects that may be of interest (choose one or more).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trip Name** (`trip_name`, text, optional): Enter a name for the trip that will help us identify it.
* **Trip Date** (`trip_date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like to schedule the trip. Please use the standard date format (MM/DD/YY).
* **Trip Location** (`trip_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the trip.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, optional): Choose the grade level(s) you would like to include on the trip. Select one or more of the following: First Grade, Second Grade, Third Grade, Fourth Grade, Fifth Grade, Sixth Grade, Seventh Grade, Eighth Grade.
* **Student Count** (`student_count`, number, optional): If you have a specific number of students in mind, enter it here.
* **Teacher's Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the teacher leading the trip.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter your school name.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, optional): If you have a email address, please enter it.
* **Teacher's Phone** (`teacher_phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it.
* **Trip Details** (`trip_details`, note, optional): Provide any additional information that would be helpful for us to know about the trip.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or notes that would be helpful for us to know about the trip.
* **Trip Interests** (`trip_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select the subjects you would like to focus on during the trip. Choose one or more of the following: Arts, Sports, Science, History, Geography, Technology, Language, Culture, Music.
* **More Trip Interests** (`trip_interests_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional subjects that may be of interest. Choose one or more of the following: Math, English, French, Spanish, German, Chinese, Other.

**Tips:**

* Please make sure to select one or more subjects in the "Trip Interests" and "More Trip Interests" fields if you have any interests outside of the default list.
* If you have any additional comments or notes, please use the "Comments" field.
* If you have any specific requests or requirements, please let us know by providing us with your email address and phone number.
