# Music School Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for enrolling a student in a music school by providing necessary information about the student and their learning status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2.  Enter the student's email address in the "Student Email" field.
3.  Enter the student's phone number in the "Student Phone" field (optional).
4.  Enter the teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
5.  Enter the teacher's email address in the "Teacher Email" field.
6.  Select the student's instrument from the list provided in the "Student Instrument" field.
7.  Select the student's level of proficiency from the list provided in the "Student Level" field.
8.  Select the class time from the list provided in the "Class Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name in this field.
*   **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter the student's email address.
*   **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter the student's phone number (optional).
*   **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter the teacher's name.
*   **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, required): Enter the teacher's email address.
*   **Student Instrument** (`student_instrument`, text, required): Select the student's instrument from the list provided.
*   **Student Level** (`student_level`, select_one, required): Select the student's level of proficiency from the list provided.
*   **Class Time** (`class_time`, time, required): Select the class time that the student will attend.
