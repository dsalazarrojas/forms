# Course AddDrop Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by students to request to add or drop courses in their program. To submit a request, fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name: Enter your name in the "Name" field. This is a required field and should be filled out with your full name (e.g., John Doe).
2. Enter your email: Enter your university email address in the "Email" field. This is a required field and should be formatted as your university email (e.g., johndoe@example.uwaterloo.ca).
3. Enter your Student ID: Enter your student ID number in the "Student ID" field. This is a required field and should be formatted as a 9-digit number (e.g., 012345678).
4. Select your Program: You will be presented with a select menu for your program. Select "Yes" if you are requesting to add or drop a course in your program, or "No" if you are not requesting to make any changes to your program.
5. Select Add Course: If you are requesting to add a course, select "Yes" from the select menu. If you are not requesting to add a course, select "No".
6. Select Drop Course: If you are requesting to drop a course, select "Yes" from the select menu. If you are not requesting to drop a course, select "No".
7. Submit your request: Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to send your request to the administration team for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: This is the field where you enter your full name.
* **Email**: This is the field where you enter your university email address.
* **Student ID**: This is the field where you enter your student ID number.
* **Program**: This field is used to select whether you are requesting to add or drop a course in your program. Select "Yes" to make a request, or "No" to indicate no changes.
* **Add Course**: This field is used to select whether you are requesting to add a course. Select "Yes" to add a course, or "No" to indicate no additions.
* **Drop Course**: This field is used to select whether you are requesting to drop a course. Select "Yes" to drop a course, or "No" to indicate no drops.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your request is processed correctly.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact the administration team for assistance.
