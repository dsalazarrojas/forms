# Teaching Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teaching Survey is a form designed to collect demographic information from teachers to better understand their needs and provide more effective support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field. Please make sure to enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field, if you have one. This is optional.
4. If you have teaching experience, please enter the number of years you have been teaching in the "Teaching Experience" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we will use to contact you. Please note that we will not share your email address with anyone.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here. This is not required but can be helpful for us to contact you.
* **Teaching Experience** (Teaching Experience, number, required): If you have teaching experience, please enter the number of years you have been teaching. This will help us to better understand your needs and provide more effective support.
