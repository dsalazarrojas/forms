# Parent Guardian Attestation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Guardian Attestation form is used to gather information from parents or guardians about their relationship to the student and their contact information. This form is essential for the school administration to verify the identity and contact details of the individuals responsible for the student's well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Read the form carefully to understand what information is required and what options are available.
2. Select the correct relationship status from the "Relationship To Student" dropdown menu.
3. Fill in the student's name and other contact information as required.
4. Indicate if the student has COVID-19 symptoms or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Has Covid 19?** (`select_multiple`): Check this box if the student has COVID-19 symptoms, or leave it blank if the student does not have any symptoms.
* **Symptoms** (`note`): This field is not used in this form, but it is available for any additional comments or information from the parent or guardian.
* **Student Name** (`text`): Enter the student's name as it appears on their official documents.
* **Relationship To Student** (`select_one`): Select one of the available options to indicate your relationship to the student. Options are:
	+ Parent
	+ Grandparent
	+ Guardian
	+ Other
* **Contact Info** (`text`): Provide any additional contact information for the parent or guardian. This could include phone numbers, email addresses, etc.
* **Student ID** (`number`): Enter the student's ID number as it appears on their official documents.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the school can contact you effectively.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the school administration for clarification.
