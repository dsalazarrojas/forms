# High School Student Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The High School Student Evaluation Form is designed to help teachers and educational administrators gather relevant information about high school students. This form will help in understanding the students' progress, strengths, and areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  The form consists of 12 fields that require you to provide information about your name, school attendance, years attended, grade level, school experience, extracurricular activities, special talents, challenges, support systems, school policies, concerns, and suggestions.
2.  Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
3.  Make sure to provide accurate and truthful information in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required): This field asks for your name, so please enter your full name as you would like to be referred to in this evaluation.
*   **School** (`School`, `text`, required): This field is for your school name, where you are currently studying or have studied. Please enter your school's name as it appears on your official school documents.
*   **Years Attended** (`Years Attended`, `number`, required): This field asks for the number of years you have attended your current school. Please enter the total number of years you have been in school.
*   **Grade Level** (`Grade Level`, `select_one`, required): This field allows you to select your current grade level from the options provided. Choose the grade level that matches your current academic level.
*   **School Experience** (`School Experience`, `number`, required): This field asks you to rate your school experience on a scale of 1-10. Please select a number that reflects your experience at your school, taking into account the quality of education, facilities, and overall experience.
*   **Extracurricular Activities** (`Extracurricular Activities`, `text`, required): This field is for listing your involvement in extracurricular activities, such as sports, clubs, or other activities outside of regular classes. Please provide a brief description of your participation.
*   **Special Talents** (`Special Talents`, `text`, optional): This field allows you to share any special talents or skills you have, such as music, art, or languages. If you don't have any special talents, you can leave this field blank.
*   **Challenges** (`Challenges`, `text`, optional): This field is for listing any challenges you have faced while studying or attending your school. Please provide a brief description of the challenges you faced.
*   **Support Systems** (`Support Systems`, `text`, optional): This field is for mentioning any support systems you have in place, such as family members, teachers, or friends, that help you in your studies. Please list any support systems you rely on.
*   **School Policies** (`School Policies`, `text`, optional): This field allows you to share any school policies that you think are effective or ineffective. If you don't have any thoughts on school policies, you can leave this field blank.
*   **Concerns** (`Concerns`, `text`, optional): This field is for sharing any concerns you have about your school or educational environment. Please provide a brief description of your concerns.
*   **Suggestions** (`Suggestions`, `text`, optional): This field is for suggesting any improvements or changes you think could be made to your school or educational system. Please provide a brief description of your suggestions.
