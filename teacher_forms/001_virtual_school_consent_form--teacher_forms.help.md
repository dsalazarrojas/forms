# virtual_school_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from parents or guardians about their child's virtual school attendance and provide consent for their participation in the virtual school program. It is crucial to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth and successful virtual school experience for the child.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the **User Info** page with the following information:
	* Provide details about yourself, the parent or guardian.
2. Move on to the **Parent Info** page to input information about yourself, the parent or guardian.
3. Finally, proceed to the **Student Info** page and enter details about your child, the student.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info (1)**: *Required: No* (`text`, User Info)
	+ This field is for you to input your personal details as the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Info (2)**: *Required: No* (`text`, Parent Info)
	+ Enter your personal details as the parent or guardian.
* **Student Info (3)**: *Required: No* (`text`, Student Info)
	+ Provide information about your child, including their name, grade level, and any relevant health or learning requirements.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and consent from your child to access and input their information.
* Double-check for accuracy and completeness of the inputted information before submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to your virtual school administration for assistance.
* This form will be reviewed and verified for completion and accuracy before submission to ensure a successful virtual school experience for your child.
