# School Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students applying to a school for admission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start at the first page and fill out all the fields in sequence.
2. Be as complete and accurate as possible when filling out each field.
3. Use the options for select_multiple fields to select all relevant choices.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Application Form** (text, optional): This is a title field and can be left empty.
* **Teacher Info** (text, optional): This field is for any information from the teacher or school staff that is relevant to the student's application.
* **Student Info** (text, optional): This field is for any personal details about the student, such as contact information or other relevant details.
* **School Info** (text, optional): This field is for any information about the school the student is applying to.
* **Parent Info** (text, optional): This field is for any relevant information from the parent or guardian of the student.
* **Student Documentation** (text, optional): This field is for any relevant documents or attachments related to the student's application.
* **Student Accommodation** (text, optional): This field is for any accommodation or special needs the student may have.
* **Student Medical History** (text, optional): This field is for any medical history or conditions of the student.
* **Student Family History** (text, optional): This field is for any family history or circumstances that may be relevant to the student's application.
* **Student Education History** (text, optional): This field is for any prior education experience or achievements of the student.
* **Student Language History** (text, optional): This field is for any language proficiency or experience the student has.
* **Student Employment History** (text, optional): This field is for any work experience or employment history of the student.
* **Student Relevant Courses** (select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant courses or certifications that may be relevant to the student's application.
* **Other Info** (text, optional): This field is for any additional information not covered in other fields.
* **Additional Info** (text, optional): This field is for any further details that the student wants to share.
* **School Address** (text, optional): This field is for the address of the school the student is applying to.
* **School Contact** (text, optional): This field is for any contact information of the school.
* **Student Signature** (note, optional): This is a field for the student to sign their name and date.
* **Parent Signature** (note, optional): This is a field for the parent or guardian to sign their name and date.
* **School Signature** (note, optional): This is a field for the school representative to sign their name and date.
* **School Comments** (text, optional): This field is for any comments from the school about the student's application.
* **Student Comments** (text, optional): This field is for any comments from the student about their application.
* **Parent Comments** (text, optional): This field is for any comments from the parent or guardian about the student's application.
* **School Admin Comments** (text, optional): This field is for any comments from the school administration about the student's application.
* **Submission Status** (select_one, optional): This field is for the status of the student's application, which can be pending, completed, or cancelled.
