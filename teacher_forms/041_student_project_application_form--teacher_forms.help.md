# Student Project Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Project Application Form is a form designed to collect information about student projects. It is intended to be used by students to submit their project applications to their supervisors or project managers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the project title and description fields with relevant information about your project.
2. Select the expected start and end dates for your project.
3. Choose the project status and provide any additional status notes if necessary.
4. Select the categories and tags relevant to your project.
5. Select the project supervisor(s) and provide their email and phone number.
6. Enter your student name, email, phone number, and major.
7. Select your student supervisor(s) and provide their supervisor approval and signature.
8. Sign and date the form with your student signature and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Title** (`project_title`, text): Enter a brief title for your project.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text): Provide a detailed description of your project.
* **Expected Start Date** (`expected_start_date`, date): Enter the expected start date for your project.
* **Expected End Date** (`expected_end_date`, date): Enter the expected end date for your project.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one): Choose the status of your project (In Progress, In Development, Finished).
* **Project Status Notes** (`project_status_notes`, text): Provide any additional status notes for your project.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple): Select one or more categories relevant to your project (Category 1, Category 2, Category 3).
* **Tags** (`tags`, select_multiple): Select one or more tags relevant to your project (Tag 1, Tag 2, Tag 3).
* **Project Supervisor** (`project_sup_report_to`, select_multiple): Select the project supervisor(s) for your project (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Supervisor Email** (`project_sup_email`, email): Enter the supervisor's email address.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`project_sup_phone`, text): Enter the supervisor's phone number.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text): Enter your student name.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email): Enter your student email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text): Enter your student phone number.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, text): Enter your student grade.
* **Student Major** (`student_major`, text): Enter your student major.
* **Student Supervisor** (`student_supervisor`, select_one): Select your student supervisor (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Supervisor Approval** (`supervisor_approval`, text): Provide supervisor approval (if applicable).
* **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`, text): Provide supervisor signature (if applicable).
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, text): Provide your student signature.
* **Student Supervisors** (`student_supervisors`, select_multiple): Select one or more student supervisors (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Student Supervisor Signature** (`student_supervisor_signature`, text): Provide your student supervisor signature.
* **Project Report Date** (`project_sup_report_date`, date): Enter the project report date.
* **Project Report** (`project_sup_report`, text): Provide a project report (if applicable).
* **Supervisor Comment** (`project_sup_comment`, text): Provide any additional supervisor comment (if applicable).

Note: This form is for student project applications only. Please ensure that you have all necessary approvals and signatures before submitting your project.
