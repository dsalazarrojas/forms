# Substitute Teacher Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Substitute Teacher Feedback Form is a tool for substitute teachers to provide their feedback and input on their experience in the school. This form will help us understand your thoughts, suggestions, and concerns to improve our substitute teacher program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the teacher with whom you worked on this assignment.
2. Choose the subject(s) you taught during this assignment.
3. Provide any feedback or comments you have about your experience as a substitute teacher.
4. Add any additional comments or notes about your experience.
5. Enter the date you worked as a substitute teacher.
6. Choose your start time (if applicable).
7. Choose your end time (if applicable).
8. Leave a note to the school administration or teachers (optional).
9. Enter your email address (so we can get back to you) and phone number (in case we need to reach you urgently).
10. Select the teacher assigned to you for this assignment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Teacher**: Select the teacher you worked with during this assignment.
* **Subject(s)**: Choose the subject(s) you taught during this assignment.
* **Feedback**: Provide any feedback or comments about your experience as a substitute teacher. This could be positive or negative feedback, suggestions for improvement, or concerns you have about the school or your experience.
* **Class**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your experience, such as the class size, behavior, or materials used.
* **Date**: Enter the date on which you worked as a substitute teacher.
* **Start Time**: Choose your start time (if applicable).
* **End Time**: Choose your end time (if applicable).
* **Note**: Leave a note to the school administration or teachers about your experience. This is an optional field.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can get back to you and keep you updated.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in case we need to reach you urgently.
* **Phone**: Select the teacher assigned to you for this assignment.
