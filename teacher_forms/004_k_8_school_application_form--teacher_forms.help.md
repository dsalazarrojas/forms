# K 8 School Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The K 8 School Application Form is designed to collect information from students applying to attend K-8 school. This form should be completed with accuracy and thoroughness to ensure that your application is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student information on page 1 (`Student Information`).
2. Enter your contact details on page 2 (`Contact Information`).
3. Select the student's documents that you have (`Student Documents`).
4. Choose your school preference on page 3 (`School Preferences`).
5. Add any additional information on page 4 (`Additional Information`).
6. Provide emergency contact information on page 5 (`Emergency Contact`).
7. Review and select the certification statements on page 6 (`Submit`).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Student Information (Page 1)
* **Student Name** (`student_info`): Enter the student's name as it appears on their ID or passport.
* **Student ID/Passport Number** (`student_info`): Enter the student's ID or passport number.
* **Date of Birth** (`student_info`): Enter the student's date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Address** (`student_info`): Enter the student's home address.

### Contact Information (Page 2)
* **Contact Name** (`contact_info`): Enter the contact person's name as they would like to be reached.
* **Relationship** (`contact_info`): Select your relationship with the student (Parent, Caregiver, etc.).
* **Phone Number** (`contact_info`): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Email** (`contact_info`): Enter the contact person's email.

### Student Documents (Page 3)
* **Report Card** (`student_documents`): Select if you have a report card available.
* **Birth Certificate** (`student_documents`): Select if you have a birth certificate available.
* **Immunization Record** (`student_documents`): Select if you have an immunization record available.
* **Physical Health Record** (`student_documents`): Select if you have a physical health record available.

### School Preferences (Page 4)
* **School Preference** (`school_preferences`): Select the school you are applying to (K-8 School, Other).

### Additional Information (Page 5)
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`): Enter any additional information you would like to share with the school.

### Emergency Contact (Page 6)
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact`): Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact`): Select the relationship with the emergency contact (Parent, Guardian, etc.).
* **Phone Number** (`emergency_contact`): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Email** (`emergency_contact`): Enter the emergency contact's email.

### Submit (Page 7)
* **Certification 1** (`submit`): I certify that all the information I have provided is accurate.
* **Certification 2** (`submit`): I am aware of the school rules and regulations.
* Please review and sign both certifications before submitting the form.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly. If you have any doubts or questions, please contact the school for assistance.
