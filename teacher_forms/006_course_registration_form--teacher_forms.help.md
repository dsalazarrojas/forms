# Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Registration Form is used to collect information from users for course registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **Program Name** in the first field.
2. Provide your **Name** in the second field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the third field.
4. Optional: Enter your **Phone** number in the fourth field.
5. Select your **Date of Birth** in the fifth field.
6. Enter your **Address** in the sixth field.
7. Select one **Option** from the seventh field.
8. Select multiple **Options** from the eighth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the program you are registering for.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Select your date of birth.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your physical address.
* **Select Option** (`select_option`, select_one, optional): Select one option from the options listed.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options from the options listed.
