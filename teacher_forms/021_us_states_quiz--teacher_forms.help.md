# US States Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The US States Quiz form is designed to test knowledge of US state capitals. It consists of two pages with two questions and a result field. Fill in the blanks for each question, and the form will not track or store any user data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, type the name of the capital of California.
2. On the second page, type the name of the capital of New York.
3. On the third page, type your answers to the two questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is the capital of California?** (`question_1`, text, optional): Type in the name of the capital of California.
- **Answer 1** (`answer_1`, text, optional): Enter your answer to the first question.
- **What is the capital of New York?** (`question_2`, text, optional): Type in the name of the capital of New York.
- **Answer 2** (`answer_2`, text, optional): Enter your answer to the second question.
- **Result** (`result`, text, optional): Type in your answers to both questions. This is where you will enter your final result.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in both question answers correctly to get an accurate result.
- The form does not track or store any user data, so your answers will not be saved.
