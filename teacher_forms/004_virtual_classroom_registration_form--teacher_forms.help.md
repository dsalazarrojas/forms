# Virtual Classroom Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Classroom Registration Form is designed to collect information from teachers for the virtual classroom registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
2.  Ensure you have the permission of your institution or organization to fill out this form if it is not for personal use.
3.  Review the form for any errors or typos before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First name
**Teacher's First name** (text, required/optional): Fill in your first name as it appears on your official documents. Please use the name as it is written on your birth certificate or passport.

### Last name
**Teacher's Last name** (text, required/optional): Fill in your last name as it appears on your official documents. Please use the name as it is written on your birth certificate or passport.

### Email Address
**Email Address** (email, required/optional): Enter your personal email address associated with your official contact information. Ensure this is a valid email address that you regularly use.

### Date of Birth
**Date of Birth** (date, required/optional): Enter your Date of Birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

### Age
**Age** (number, required/optional): Enter your age as an integer.

### Contact Number
**Contact Number** (text, required/optional): Enter your contact number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX.
