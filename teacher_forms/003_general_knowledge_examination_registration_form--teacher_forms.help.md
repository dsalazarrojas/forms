# General Knowledge Examination Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The General Knowledge Examination Registration Form is a tool designed to collect information from teachers for a General Knowledge Examination. This form serves as a data collection mechanism to gather details about participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the text field provided.
2. Provide your **Email Address** in the format of [name@example.com](mailto:name@example.com).
3. Enter your school's name in the text field provided.
4. Select the examination date from the date picker.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **required**): Enter your personal name as per official records.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, **required**): Enter your registered email address.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of your school.
* **Examination Date** (`exam_date`, date, **required**): Select the date for which you are registering.

## Tips
* Double-check your email address for accuracy to ensure it's up to date.
* Ensure your school name is correctly spelled and formatted.
* Select the examination date carefully to avoid any confusion.
* Fill out this form accurately to ensure your registration is processed smoothly.
