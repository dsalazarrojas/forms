# Music Theory Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

This quiz is designed to gather information from music enthusiasts about their musical experience, preferences, and interests.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully, selecting the option that best fits your response.
2. For questions with a "Other" option, you can provide a brief description in the text field.
3. You can skip any questions that do not apply to you or do not fit your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your favorite instrument?** (`favorite_instrument`, `select_one`, required: false): This question is asking about your favorite instrument to play or listen to. You can choose from a list of common instruments or select "Other" and provide a description in the field below.
* **In what style of music do you typically practice?** (`practice_music_style`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question is asking about the type of music you usually practice or perform. You can choose from a list of common music styles, including Solo, Ensemble, and Accompaniment.
* **Do you have a favorite note value?** (`favorite_note_value`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question is asking about your favorite type of note value. You can choose from Sharp, Flat, and Natural, or select "Other" and provide a description in the field below.
* **What is your favorite music genre?** (`music_genre_description`, `text`, required: false): This question is asking for a brief description of your favorite music genre.
* **How many times do you practice per week?** (`how_many_times_per_week`, `number`, required: false): This question is asking about your practice frequency. Enter a number of times you practice per week.
* **What area of music theory do you find most interesting?** (`music_theory_formation`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question is asking about your interests in music theory. You can choose from Harmony, Counterpoint, Melody, and Rhythm, or select "Other" and provide a description in the field below.
* **How long have you been learning music?** (`how_long_have_you_been_learning`, `select_one`, required: false): This question is asking about your musical experience. Choose from the provided options or select "Other" and provide a description.
* **How many minutes do you typically practice per session?** (`how_many_minutes`, `number`, required: false): This question is asking about your practice duration. Enter a number of minutes you typically practice per session.
* **Favorite Note Value Other** (`favorite_note_value_other`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing a description of your favorite note value if it's not listed in the previous question.
* **Favorite Chord Progression** (`favorite_chord_progression`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question is asking about your favorite type of chord progression. You can choose from Major, Minor, Diminished, and Augmented, or select "Other" and provide a description in the field below.
* **How long is your typical practice session?** (`how_long_is_your_practice_session`, `number`, required: false): This question is asking about your practice duration. Enter a number of minutes or hours you typically practice per session.
* **Favorite Music Style Other** (`practice_music_style_other`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing a description of your favorite music style if it's not listed in the previous question.
* **In what style of music do you typically practice?** (`practice_music_style_other_description`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing a description of the style of music you typically practice.
* **What is your favorite instrument to play?** (`favorite_instrument_description`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing a description of your favorite instrument.
* **Music Theory Formation Other** (`music_theory_formation_other`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing a description of the area of music theory you find most interesting if it's not listed in the previous question.
* **What is your favorite music genre?** (`music_genre_description`, `text`, required: false): This question is asking for a brief description of your favorite music genre.

Please note that some of these fields are optional and you can skip them if they do not apply to you or your experience.
