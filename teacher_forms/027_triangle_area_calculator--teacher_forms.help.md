# Triangle Area Calculator - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Triangle Area Calculator is a tool used to calculate the area of a triangle. It is designed to provide a quick and easy way to compute the area of a triangle given its base and height.

## How To Complete This Form
To use the Triangle Area Calculator, simply fill in the base and height of the triangle in their respective fields, and press the "calculate" button to get the result.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Base** (`base`, `number`, required/optional: false): Enter the length of the base of the triangle. This is the distance between the two legs of the triangle.
* **Height** (`height`, `number`, required/optional: false): Enter the height of the triangle. This is the distance from the base to the opposite vertex.
* **Area** (`area`, `number`, required/optional: false): This field is automatically populated with the calculated area of the triangle based on the input values for base and height.
