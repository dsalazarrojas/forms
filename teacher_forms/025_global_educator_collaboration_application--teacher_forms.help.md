# Global Educator Collaboration Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Educator Collaboration Application is designed to collect information about educators for collaboration purposes. This form helps us gather essential data about you and your institution to facilitate effective collaboration and communication among educators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your `user_id` - a unique number assigned to you as an educator.
2.  Fill in your `name` - your full name as an educator.
3.  Enter your valid `email` - a professional email address.
4.  Provide your `phone` - a contact number for communication.
5.  Write a short `description` - a brief introduction about yourself.
6.  Mention your institution - where you are currently working.
7.  Choose your `country` - where you are located.
8.  Select your language proficiency - are you proficient in one or more languages.
9.  Select your collaboration type - what kind of collaboration are you open to.
10. Submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **user_id** (`user_id`, number, required): Enter a unique number assigned to you as an educator.
*   **name** (`name`, text, required): Fill in your full name.
*   **email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid professional email address.
*   **phone** (`phone`, text, required): Provide a contact number for communication.
*   **description** (`description`, text, required): Write a short introduction about yourself.
*   **institution** (`institution`, text, required): Mention your institution where you are currently working.
*   **country** (`country`, select_one, required): Choose your location.
*   **language** (`language`, select_multiple, required): Select languages in which you are proficient.
*   **collaboration_type** (`collaboration_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of collaboration you are open to.

## Tips
Make sure to fill out each field accurately and thoroughly, as this information will be used to facilitate collaboration and communication. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
