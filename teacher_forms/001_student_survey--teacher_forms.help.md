# Student Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students about their experience in a course. The survey aims to gather insights on various aspects of the course, including teacher resources, facilities, instructor quality, and overall course experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review each question carefully and choose the answer that best reflects your experience.
* Select one option for each question.
* Make sure to answer all questions, as some are required.
* Submit the form once you have completed all questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are the teacher's materials and resources useful for you?**
    * This question asks about the usefulness of the teacher's materials and resources provided in the course.
    * Choose 'True' if you found the materials and resources helpful.
    * Choose 'False' if you did not find them helpful.
