# School Audition Application Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Audition Application Form Template is designed to gather information from students who are auditioning for school events. This form aims to collect essential details about the student's talent, event type, audition date, location, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly with your information.
2. Review each field carefully before proceeding to ensure you are providing the correct information.
3. Ensure that you have answered all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **User Input Form** (1, text, optional)
This field is a text box where you can enter any additional information about your audition.

### **Student Name** (2, text, optional)
Enter your name as it appears on your official identification documents.

### **Event Type** (3, select_one, optional)
Select the type of event you are auditioning for from the provided options (Event 1, Event 2, Event 3).

### **Audition Date** (4, date, optional)
Enter the date of the audition event.

### **Audition Time** (5, time, optional)
Enter the time of the audition event.

### **Audition Location** (6, text, optional)
Enter the location of the audition event.

### **Talent Description** (7, text, optional)
Describe your talent, skills, or abilities that you want to showcase during the audition.

### **Student Statement** (8, text, optional)
Enter any additional comments or statements about the event.

### **Teacher Signature** (9, select_one, optional)
Select the teacher who has recommended you for this audition from the provided options (Teacher 1, Teacher 2, Teacher 3).

### **Student Signature** (10, select_one, optional)
Select your name from the provided options (Student 1, Student 2, Student 3).

### **School Signature** (11, select_one, optional)
Select the school name from the provided options (School 1, School 2, School 3).

### **School Name** (12, text, optional)
Enter your school name.

### **Contact Email** (13, email, optional)
Enter your contact email.

### **Contact Phone** (14, text, optional)
Enter your contact phone number.

### **School Name 2** (15, text, optional)
Enter your school name if different from the previous school name.

### **Event Description** (16, text, optional)
Describe the event you are auditioning for.

### **Teacher Name** (17, select_one, optional)
Select the teacher who will be conducting the audition from the provided options (Teacher 1, Teacher 2, Teacher 3).

### **Teacher Name 2** (18, select_one, optional)
Select another teacher who will be conducting the audition from the provided options (Teacher 1, Teacher 2, Teacher 3).

### **Student Talent** (19, text, optional)
Describe your talent.

### **Teacher Talent** (20, text, optional)
Describe the teacher's talent.

### **Student Skills** (21, text, optional)
List your skills.

### **Teacher Skills** (22, text, optional)
List the teacher's skills.

### **School Event** (23, select_one, optional)
Select the event where you will be performing from the provided options (Event 1, Event 2, Event 3).

### **Teacher Lesson** (24, text, optional)
Describe the teacher's lesson.

### **School Audition** (25, text, optional)
Describe the school event you are auditioning for.
