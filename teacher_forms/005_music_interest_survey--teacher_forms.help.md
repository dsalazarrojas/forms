# Music Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your interest in music, including your favorite genres, listening habits, musical experiences, and preferences for different aspects of music.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select all that apply from the options below.
2. Enter a number as instructed.
3. Choose your answer from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What genre of music do you enjoy listening to?** (`music_interest_survey_form_1`, `select_one`, required/optional)
	+ Select the genre of music that you enjoy listening to the most.
* **How often do you listen to music?** (`music_interest_survey_form_2`, `select_one`, required/optional)
	+ Indicate how often you listen to music, whether it's daily, occasionally, or never.
* **How many hours do you spend per day listening to music?** (`music_interest_survey_form_3`, `number`, required/optional)
	+ Enter the number of hours you spend per day listening to music.
* **What type of music instrument do you play?** (`music_interest_survey_form_4`, `select_multiple`, required/optional)
	+ Select all the instruments you play, or none if you don't play any.
* **Are you part of a music group?** (`music_interest_survey_form_5`, `select_one`, required/optional)
	+ Indicate if you are part of a music group or not.
* **Do you enjoy singing along to music?** (`music_interest_survey_form_6`, `select_one`, required/optional)
	+ Select if you enjoy singing along to music or not.
* **How would you rate your knowledge of music history?** (`music_interest_survey_form_7`, `select_multiple`, required/optional)
	+ Rate your knowledge of music history as good, fair, or poor.
* **Do you attend concerts?** (`music_interest_survey_form_8`, `select_one`, required/optional)
	+ Indicate if you attend concerts or not.
* **How often do you attend concerts?** (`music_interest_survey_form_9`, `number`, required/optional)
	+ Enter the number of concerts you attend per year.
* **Musician of choice?** (`music_interest_survey_form_10`, `select_one`, required/optional)
	+ Select your favorite musician or band.
* **Have you ever written a song?** (`music_interest_survey_form_11`, `select_one`, required/optional)
	+ Indicate if you have written a song or not.
* **How many music events do you attend per year?** (`music_interest_survey_form_12`, `number`, required/optional)
	+ Enter the number of music events you attend per year.
* **Do you enjoy music festivals?** (`music_interest_survey_form_13`, `select_one`, required/optional)
	+ Select if you enjoy music festivals or not.
* **How many years have you been enjoying music?** (`music_interest_survey_form_14`, `number`, required/optional)
	+ Enter the number of years you have been enjoying music.
* **Do you enjoy live music?** (`music_interest_survey_form_15`, `select_one`, required/optional)
	+ Select if you enjoy live music or not.
* **Do you enjoy music awards?** (`music_interest_survey_form_16`, `select_one`, required/optional)
	+ Select if you enjoy music awards or not.

## Tips

* Please answer each question as honestly as possible.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to reach out to us.
* This form should only be completed once, so please do not repeat it.
