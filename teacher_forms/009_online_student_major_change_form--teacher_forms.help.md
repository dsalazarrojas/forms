# online_student_major_change_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online_student_major_change_form is a tool for students to update their major in a convenient and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your new major from the list of options provided.
2. If you're changing your major due to a specific reason, provide a brief explanation in the text field.
3. Enter your current GPA (if applicable).
4. Choose your expected graduation date (if applicable).
5. Add any additional comments or remarks you'd like to include.
6. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Major** (`user_major`, `select_one`): Select your new major from the list of options provided.
* **Reason for Major Change** (`reason_for_major_change`, `text`): If you're changing your major due to a specific reason, provide a brief explanation in this field.
* **New Major** (`new_major`, `text`): Enter your new major (if it's not among the provided options).
* **Current GPA** (`current_gpa`, `number`): Enter your current GPA (if applicable).
* **Expected Graduation Date** (`expected_degree`, `date`): Choose your expected graduation date (if applicable).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`): Add any additional comments or remarks you'd like to include.
Note: Fields marked as optional are not required to be filled out.
