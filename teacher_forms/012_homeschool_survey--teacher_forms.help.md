# Homeschool Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Homeschool Survey form collects demographic information from homeschooling students, providing valuable data for the homeschool program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Enter your full name in the Student Name field.
2. Choose your student's grade level from the dropdown menu.
3. Select all subjects you are interested in learning more about.
4. Enter your email address in the Email field.
5. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, *): 
  This field is for your full name to be filled in, no other information is required here.
- **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, text, *): 
  This is where you select your student's current grade level from the dropdown menu. Make sure to choose the correct one to ensure accurate tracking.
- **Subject Interested** (`student_subjects`, select_multiple, *): 
  This field allows you to select all subjects you are interested in learning more about. Please pick all that apply from the options provided.
- **Email** (`parent_email`, email, ): 
  Enter your email address here.
- **Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, ): 
  Enter your phone number here. 

## Tips
- Please ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
- Make sure to be accurate when selecting your grade level.
- Double-check your email address for correct formatting.
- Enter a valid phone number for the best contactability.
