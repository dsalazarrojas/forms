# Kids Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from users about a quiz. It's meant to be completed by kids and is likely used for research or educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with your personal details and opinions.
2. For questions with a select_one or select_multiple option, choose one or multiple options from the list.
3. Enter your answers to the text fields.
4. For date fields, select a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Click submit to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (text, required/optional): This field is for you to enter any additional information about yourself.
* **What is your name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your birth certificate or ID.
* **What is your favorite color** (select_one, required): Choose one color from the list: red, green, blue, yellow, purple.
* **When is your birthday** (date, required): Select your birthday in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **What is your favorite food** (text, optional): Enter your favorite food.
* **Select a color** (select_multiple, optional): Choose one or multiple colors from the list: red, green, blue, yellow, purple.
* **What is your email** (email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **What is your phone number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Write a message** (note, optional): Write any message or comment about the quiz.
* **Quiz Submission** (date, optional): Select a date for when you completed the quiz.
* **How old are you** (number, optional): Enter your age.
* **What do you think about this form** (text, optional): Enter your thoughts or opinions about the form.
* **What do you think about this form** (text, optional): This is a duplicate question, please ignore.
* **How did you find this form** (text, optional): Enter how you found the form.
* **How did you learn from this form** (select_one, optional): Choose one from the list: a lot, a little, not at all.
* **What do you think about this quiz** (text, optional): Enter your thoughts or opinions about the quiz.
* **What is your favorite hobby** (text, optional): Enter your favorite hobby.
* **Would you take this quiz again** (select_one, optional): Choose one from the list: yes, no.
* **Would you recommend this quiz** (select_one, optional): Choose one from the list: yes, no.
* **What did you like most about this quiz** (text, optional): Enter what you liked most about the quiz.
* **What did you dislike most about this quiz** (text, optional): Enter what you disliked most about the quiz.
* **What would make this quiz better** (text, optional): Enter suggestions to improve the quiz.
* **What would make this quiz worse** (text, optional): Enter things that would worsen the quiz.
* **What would you add to this quiz** (text, optional): Enter ideas to add to the quiz.
* **What would you take away from this quiz** (text, optional): Enter things that you'd take away from the quiz.
