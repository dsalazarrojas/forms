## Purpose
This form is used to calculate the area of a trapezium shape. Enter the base length, length, and height of the shape to find its area.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the base length of the trapezium shape.
2. Enter the length of the trapezium shape.

4. Click "Submit" to calculate the area.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Base Length (`base_length`, Number, Optional):** Enter the base length of the trapezium shape, which is the length of the lower base.
* **Length (`length`, Number, Optional):** Enter the length of the trapezium shape, which is the length of the upper base.
* **Height (`height`, Number, Optional):** Enter the height of the trapezium shape, which is the distance between the two bases.
* **Result (`result`, Note, Not Required):** This field will display the calculated area of the trapezium shape after clicking "Submit".

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate values for the base length, length, and height.
* The formula used to calculate the area is: (base_length + length) × height / 2.
* The result will be displayed in square units (e.g., square inches, square meters, etc.).

Note: This form assumes a right trapezium shape. If you have an irregular trapezium shape, please adjust your values accordingly.
