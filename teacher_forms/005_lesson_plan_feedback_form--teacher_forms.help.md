# Lesson Plan Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lesson Plan Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from teachers on the effectiveness and content of lesson plans. This form is used to help teachers identify areas of improvement and provide constructive suggestions for future lesson plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the lesson plan number and make sure you are providing feedback on the correct lesson plan.
2. Answer question 1: What was the lesson plan about? This is a chance to describe the main topic or subject of the lesson.
3. Answer question 2: What did you like about the lesson plan? Provide specific examples or details about what worked well.
4. Answer question 3: What did you dislike about the lesson plan? If there were any issues or areas for improvement.
5. Answer question 4: How could the teacher have improved the lesson plan? Provide suggestions or ideas for future development.
6. Click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Lesson Plan Number (<name>: lesson_plan_number, <type>: number, <required>: false)
This field is to help us keep track of which lesson plan you are providing feedback on. Please ensure you are providing feedback on the correct lesson plan by entering the correct number.

### What was the lesson plan about? (<name>: question_1, <type>: text, <required>: false)
This field is an opportunity to describe the main topic or subject of the lesson. Try to be as specific as possible and include any relevant details.

### What did you like about the lesson plan? (<name>: question_2, <type>: text, <required>: false)
This field is for providing feedback on what worked well with the lesson plan. Be specific about what you liked and why.

### What did you dislike about the lesson plan? (<name>: question_3, <type>: text, <required>: false)
This field is for providing feedback on what didn't work as well as it could have. Try to be specific about the issues and offer suggestions for improvement.

### How could the teacher have improved the lesson plan? (<name>: question_4, <type>: text, <required>: false)
This field is for providing suggestions or ideas on how the teacher could improve the lesson plan. Be as specific as possible and try to offer actionable advice.

### Submit (<name>: submit, <type>: note, <required>: false)
This field is a final step to submit your feedback.
