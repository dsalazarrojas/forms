# Weekly College Bound Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Weekly College Bound Check In Form is designed to collect essential information about students who are bound for college. This form helps track their progress, meeting details, and follow-up activities. It's a crucial tool for advisors to stay updated on students' status and take necessary actions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for each field. If a field is marked as "required," you must provide an answer.
2. Enter the necessary details for each field according to the instructions provided.
3. Review your submission to ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (Student Name)** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's full name as it appears on their record.
* **Check In Date (Check In Date)** (`check_in_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date of the student's check-in session.
* **Advisor Comments (Advisor Comments)** (`advisor_comments`, text, required): Share any important comments or observations about the student's progress.
* **Student Goals (Student Goals)** (`student_goals`, select_multiple, required): Select the student's current goals (e.g., "Goals1," "Goals2," "Goals3").
* **Check In Notes (Check In Notes)** (`check_in_notes`, text, optional): If necessary, add any additional notes about the student's check-in session.
* **Student Contact Info (Student Contact Info)** (`student_contact_info`, text, required): Enter the student's contact information (e.g., phone number, email address).
* **Advisor Name (Advisor Name)** (`advisor_name`, text, required): Enter your own name as the advisor.
* **Meeting Details (Meeting Details)** (`meeting_details`, text, required): Describe the meeting details, including the date, time, and location.
* **Follow-up Action (Follow-up Action)** (`follow_up_action`, select_one, required): Choose the next action to be taken (e.g., "Action1," "Action2," "Action3").
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Next Check In Date (Next Check In Date)** (`next_check_in_date`, date, required): Set the next scheduled check-in date.
* **Meeting Time (Meeting Time)** (`meeting_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the meeting.
* **Student Status (Student Status)** (`student_status`, select_one, required): Select the student's current status (e.g., "Status 1," "Status 2," "Status 3").
* **Meeting Location (Meeting Location)** (`meeting_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the meeting.
* **Check In Method (Check In Method)** (`check_in_method`, text, required): Describe the method used for the check-in session.
* **Next Check In Method (Next Check In Method)** (`next_check_in_method`, text, required): Determine the next check-in method.
* **Check In Frequency (Check In Frequency)** (`check_in_frequency`, number, required): Set the frequency of the check-in sessions.
* **Check In Frequency Unit (Check In Frequency Unit)** (`check_in_frequency_unit`, text, required): Specify the unit of the check-in frequency (e.g., "sessions," "days," "weeks").
* **Notes (Notes)** (`notes`, text, required): Add any additional comments or notes about the student's progress.
* **Follow-up Frequency (Follow-up Frequency)** (`follow_up_frequency`, number, required): Set the frequency of follow-up activities.
* **Follow-up Frequency Unit (Follow-up Frequency Unit)** (`follow_up_frequency_unit`, text, required): Specify the unit of follow-up frequency (e.g., "sessions," "days," "weeks").
* **Check In Date Range (Check In Date Range)** (`check_in_date_range`, date, required): Set the range of check-in dates.
* **Check In Date Range Unit (Check In Date Range Unit)** (`check_in_date_range_unit`, text, required): Specify the unit of the check-in date range (e.g., "weeks," "months," "years").
* **Advisor Name Title (Advisor Name Title)** (`advisor_name_title`, text, required): Enter your title or position.
* **Advisor Signature (Advisor Signature)** (`advisor_signature`, text, required): Sign your name as the advisor.

Please review the form carefully and fill in the required information. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out.
