# School Trip Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Trip Permission Form is a required document that must be completed and submitted by parents or guardians before their child participates in a school trip. The form helps to obtain permission for the student to attend the trip and ensures that the school has informed consent from the parent/guardian.

## How To Complete This Form
This form consists of several fields, each designed to gather specific information from parents or guardians. Complete the form carefully to ensure accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Please enter the student's name as it appears on their school records.
* **Parent Name**: Please enter your name as a parent or guardian of the student.
* **Student Email**: Enter the student's email address for communication purposes.
* **Trip Details**: Provide a brief description of the trip, including the destination, date, and time.
* **Guardian Contact**: Enter a contact number or email address where you can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Permission**: Select one of the options: Parent, Teacher, or Both, indicating who has permission to grant.
* **Trip Date**: Enter the date of the trip in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Trip Time**: Enter the time of the trip in 24-hour format (e.g., 0900 for 9:00 AM).
* **Trip Duration**: Enter the length of the trip, in hours or days.
* **Trip Cost**: Enter the estimated cost of the trip.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information regarding the trip.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter any special equipment or tools that the student will be using during the trip.
* **Category**: Enter the category of the trip, e.g., field trip, sports, or arts.
* **Form ID**: This field is not applicable for end-users.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly.
* If the trip involves any special activities or equipment, be sure to list them under "Trip Details" and "Assigned Tool".
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact your teacher or school administration.
