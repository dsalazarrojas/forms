# parent_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The parent_feedback_form is designed to collect feedback from parents about their experience with a school or educational institution. This form helps the institution understand their students' and parents' needs and make informed decisions to improve the learning environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you're providing accurate and helpful feedback.
2. Provide your name and contact information to allow the institution to follow up with you.
3. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing any additional details that you think are relevant.
4. Check that all fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **parent_feedback** (`parent_feedback`, text, required/optional: no)
Please provide a brief description of your overall experience as a parent.

### **student_feedback** (`student_feedback`, text, required/optional: no)
Please provide feedback about your child's experience at the school or educational institution.

### **feedback_details** (`feedback_details`, text, required/optional: no)
This is an opportunity to provide any additional details or information that might be helpful for us to know.

### **comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional: no)
Please use this field to include any comments or suggestions you might have about the school or educational institution.

## Tips
* Be as specific and honest as possible when providing feedback.
* Remember that your input is valuable and will help us improve the learning environment.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
