# Interdisciplinary Teaching Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Interdisciplinary Teaching Perception Survey is a tool used to gather information about teachers' perceptions of interdisciplinary teaching practices in their school or university. This information will help us better understand how teachers perceive the effectiveness of these methods and the challenges they face in implementing them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields below, one by one. Make sure to answer each question as thoroughly and accurately as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`id: 1`): A brief introduction to the form, explaining its purpose and importance.
* **Benefits** (`id: 2`): Select all benefits you believe have resulted from interdisciplinary teaching practices in your school or university. Choose all that apply.
	+ Option 1: Improved student engagement
	+ Option 2: Enhanced critical thinking skills
	+ Option 3: Better student outcomes
* **Challenges** (`id: 3`): Select all challenges you face when implementing interdisciplinary teaching practices in your school or university. Choose all that apply.
	+ Yes: High administrative burden
	+ No: Other (please specify in the comments section below)
* **Effectiveness** (`id: 4`): A number between 1-10 indicating how effective you believe interdisciplinary teaching practices are in your school or university.
* **School Info** (`id: 5`): Enter your school's name and address.
* **University Info** (`id: 6`): Enter your university's name and address.
* **Department Info** (`id: 7`): Enter your department's name and any relevant details.
* **Teaching Approach** (`id: 8`): Describe your teaching approach, including any unique practices or methods you use.
* **Comments** (`id: 9`): Any additional comments or feedback you may have about interdisciplinary teaching practices in your school or university.
* **Email** (`id: 10`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`id: 11`): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`id: 12`): The date you are completing this form.
* **Time** (`id: 13`): The time you are completing this form.
* **Notes** (`id: 14`): Any additional notes about your teaching practices or experiences.
* **School Name** (`id: 17`): Enter your school's name.
* **School Address** (`id: 18`): Enter your school's address.
* **School Phone** (`id: 19`): Enter your school's phone number.
* **School Email** (`id: 20`): Enter your school's email address.
* **University Name** (`id: 21`): Enter your university's name.
* **University Address** (`id: 22`): Enter your university's address.
* **University Phone** (`id: 23`): Enter your university's phone number.
* **University Email** (`id: 24`): Enter your university's email address.
* **Department** (`id: 25`): Enter your department's name.
* **Submit** (`id: 15`): Click this button to submit your form.
* **Cancel** (`id: 16`): Click this button to cancel your form and start again later.
