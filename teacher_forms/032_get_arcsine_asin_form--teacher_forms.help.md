# Get Arcsine (asin) Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Get Arcsine (asin) Form is used to collect user information for some sort of calculation or filtering based on Asin values and categories. It is recommended to fill out this form to get the desired result.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, fill out the "User Input" field with any information you have. You may or may not need to fill out this field.
2. If you have an Asin value, fill it out in the "Asin Value" field. You may or may not need to fill out this field.
3. Select one or more categories that apply to your input in the "Category" field. You can select "Teacher Forms" or "Other".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input**: (`user_input`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information you have. You can fill it out if you need to provide more context.
* **Asin Value**: (`asin_value`, number, optional): Fill out this field if you have an Asin value related to your input. If you have no Asin value, you can leave this field blank.
* **Result**: (`result`, text, optional): This field will display the result of your calculation or filtering. You don't need to fill it out.
* **Category**: (`category`, select multiple, optional): Choose one or more categories that apply to your input. If you select "Teacher Forms", it likely relates to educational materials. If you select "Other", it may relate to other topics.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all relevant fields to get the desired result.
- If you're unsure what a field is for, try to use your best judgment based on the field label and options.
- If you need more help or clarification, contact your administrator.
