# High School Memory Book - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "High School Memory Book" form is designed to collect information about high school students. This form will help teachers or administrators gather comprehensive data about the students, including contact information, personal details, and high school memories.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your high school email address in the "Contact Information Email" field.
2. Enter your high school phone number in the "Contact Information Phone" field.
3. Enter your first name in the "Personal Details First Name" field.
4. Enter your last name in the "Personal Details Last Name" field.
5. Select your high school grade level in the "Personal Details Grade Level" field.
6. Enter your major in the "Personal Details Major" field.
7. Select your current employment status in the "Current Status Employed" field.
8. (Optional) Enter your student status in the "Current Status Student" field.
9. (Optional) Enter the number of years you've been employed in the "Current Status Employed Years" field.
10. Enter the name of your high school in the "High School Attended Name" field.
11. Enter the city where your high school is located in the "High School Attended City" field.
12. Select the state where your high school is located from the dropdown list in the "High School Attended State" field.
13. (Optional) Check if you were employed during your high school in the "High School Attended Employment" field.
14. Enter the year you graduated from high school in the "High School Year Graduated" field.
15. Enter a unique ID for your high school memory book in the "High School Memory Book ID" field.
16. (Optional) Enter the URL of the image for your high school memory book in the "High School Memory Book Image URL" field.
17. (Optional) Enter a description for your high school memory book image in the "High School Memory Book Image Description" field.
18. (Optional) Enter a caption for your high school memory book image in the "High School Memory Book Image Caption" field.
19. (Optional) Enter the width of your high school memory book image in the "High School Memory Book Image Width" field.
20. (Optional) Enter the height of your high school memory book image in the "High School Memory Book Image Height" field.
21. (Optional) Enter a caption for your high school memory book image in the "High School Memory Book Image Caption 1" field.
