# Student Responsibility Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Responsibility Agreement Form is a document that outlines the student's understanding of their responsibilities while participating in a certain program or activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand your responsibilities as a student.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and truthful information.
3. Sign and date the form to indicate your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Description
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, note, not required): This is a brief description of the program or activity you are participating in. Please provide a concise summary of the details.

### Student Name
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, not required): This field is for you to enter your name. Please ensure it is accurate and spelled correctly.

### Guardian Name
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, not required): If you are a student under 18, please enter the name of your parent or guardian who is responsible for you.

### Parent Signature
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, number, not required): If you are a student under 18, your parent or guardian must sign this field to indicate their agreement. Please have them sign and date this field.

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully before signing to ensure you understand your responsibilities.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the program administrators.
* Make sure to sign and date the form once you have reviewed and agreed to the terms.
