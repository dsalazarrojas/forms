# Grade Replacement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a grade replacement for a student. It collects information necessary to verify and replace the original grade.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Student ID of the student whose grade needs to be replaced.
2. Enter the name of the teacher who can verify the original grade.
3. Describe the reason for the grade replacement in the Reason for Replacement field.
4. Enter the original grade that needs to be replaced.
5. Enter the new grade to be assigned to the student.
6. Provide the semester and year the grade needs to be replaced for.
7. Finally, enter the student's name to verify that the correct student is being replaced.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Grade Replacement Request** (`Grade Replacement Request`, `text`, required/optional): This is the main field to describe the purpose of the form.
* **Student ID** (`Student ID`, `text`, required): Enter the Student ID of the student whose grade needs to be replaced.
* **Teacher** (`Teacher`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the teacher who can verify the original grade.
* **Reason for Replacement** (`Reason for Replacement`, `text`, required): Describe the reason why the grade needs to be replaced.
* **Original Grade** (`Original Grade`, `text`, required): Enter the original grade that needs to be replaced.
* **New Grade** (`New Grade`, `text`, required): Enter the new grade to be assigned to the student.
* **Semester/Year** (`Semester/Year`, `text`, required): Enter the semester and year the grade needs to be replaced for.
* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, `text`, required): Enter the student's name to verify that the correct student is being replaced.
* **Check Student Name** (`Check Student Name`, `time`, optional): This field is not clearly described in the form metadata. However, it's possible that this field is intended to be a validation field to confirm the student's name. If this is the case, it's not clearly explained in the form.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields for accurate grade replacement.
- If the student's name is not verified in the Check Student Name field, it's recommended to recheck the student's name to ensure accuracy.
- This form can be used by teachers to facilitate the grade replacement process and reduce manual verification processes.
