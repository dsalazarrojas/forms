# Academic Reference Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students to formally request an academic reference from a teacher, professor, or advisor for purposes such as graduate school applications, job or internship applications, scholarships, or transfers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the requested information.
2. Be prepared to submit the completed form with supporting documents if requested.
3. Send the form to your selected reference provider for completion and submission.
4. Follow up with your reference provider to ensure they have received the form and are prepared to provide the reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Academic Reference Request Form** (`title`, `required`: `false`):
	+ This is the title of the form that explains its purpose and importance.
* **First name** (`student_first_name`, `required`: `true`):
	+ Enter your first name as it is listed in your academic records.
* **Last name** (`student_last_name`, `required`: `true`):
	+ Enter your last name as it is listed in your academic records.
* **Student email address** (`student_email`, `required`: `true`):
	+ Enter your university email address or the address you have associated with your student account.
* **Phone number** (`student_phone`, `required`: `false`):
	+ Enter your phone number, which may be useful for the reference provider to contact you.
* **Student ID number** (`student_id`, `required`: `true`):
	+ Enter your student ID number as it is assigned by your university.
* **Program of study** (`program_of_study`, `required`: `true`):
	+ Select the academic program you are currently enrolled in or have completed.
* **Expected graduation date** (`expected_graduation`, `required`: `true`):
	+ Enter the expected date of your graduation.
* **Name of the teacher or professor you are requesting a reference from** (`reference_provider_name`, `required`: `true`):
	+ Enter the full name of the person you are requesting the reference from.
* **Reference provider email address** (`reference_provider_email`, `required`: `true`):
	+ Enter the email address of the person providing the reference.
* **Reference provider department** (`reference_provider_department`, `required`: `false`):
	+ If applicable, enter the department or area of study of the reference provider.
* **How do you know this reference provider** (`relationship_type`, `required`: `true`):
	+ Select how you know or are familiar with the reference provider.
* **Purpose of the reference** (`reference_purpose`, `required`: `true`):
	+ Select the reason or purpose of the reference you are requesting.
* **Name of the institution or employer the reference will be sent to** (`target_institution_or_employer`, `required`: `true`):
	+ Enter the name of the institution or employer the reference will be sent to.
* **Deadline for the reference submission** (`submission_deadline`, `required`: `true`):
	+ Enter the deadline for the reference provider to submit the reference.
* **How should the reference be submitted** (`submission_method`, `required`: `true`):
	+ Select how you would like the reference to be submitted.
* **Key strengths or accomplishments you would like highlighted** (`key_strengths`, `required`: `false`):
	+ Enter any key strengths or accomplishments you would like the reference provider to highlight.
* **Any additional information for the reference provider** (`additional_information`, `required`: `false`):
	+ Provide any additional information that may be helpful for the reference provider in writing the reference.
