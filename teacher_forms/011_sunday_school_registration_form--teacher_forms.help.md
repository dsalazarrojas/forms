# Sunday School Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sunday School Registration Form is used to collect information from students and their parents/guardians for Sunday School purposes. This form helps us understand the student's details, schedule, and preferences, ensuring they can attend and engage with the Sunday School program efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: 
   - First Name
   - Last Name (if applicable)
   - Email (email is required if you wish to receive updates)
   - Parent Name (if you are a parent/guardian)
   - Parent Phone (if you are a parent/guardian)
   - Student Grade Level (if applicable)
   - Sunday School Class (if applicable)
   - Sunday School Section (if applicable)

2. If applicable, fill in the following:
   - Date of Birth
   - Phone Number
   - Parent Name (if not already filled in)
   - Parent Phone (if not already filled in)
   - Emergency Contact Name
   - Emergency Contact Phone
   - Special Instructions (if needed)
   - Tutor Name (if applicable)
   - Tutor Phone (if applicable)
   - Class Teacher (if applicable)
   - Class Notes (if applicable)
   - Sunday School Location
   - Sunday School Time
   - Sunday School Day
   - Sunday School Duration
   - Sunday School Frequency

3. Select the correct options:
   - Sunday School Class: Yes/No
   - Sunday School Section: Yes/No
   - Sunday School Frequency: Yes/No

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required for updates): Enter your email address.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, optional): If you are a parent/guardian, enter your name.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, `text`, optional): If you are a parent/guardian, enter your phone number.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Student Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, `number`, optional): Enter your grade level.
* **Sunday School Class** (`sunday_school_class`, `select_one`, optional): Select Yes if you wish to attend Sunday School.
* **Sunday School Section** (`sunday_school_section`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select Yes if you wish to attend Sunday School.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `note`, optional): Enter any special instructions or information.
* **Tutor Name** (`tutor_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your tutor.
* **Tutor Phone** (`tutor_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of your tutor.
* **Class Teacher** (`class_teacher`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your class teacher.
* **Class Notes** (`class_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any notes about your class.
* **Sunday School Location** (`sunday_school_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of the Sunday School.
* **Sunday School Time** (`sunday_school_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the Sunday School.
* **Sunday School Day** (`sunday_school_day`, `date`, optional): Enter the day of the Sunday School.
* **Sunday School Duration** (`sunday_school_duration`, `number`, optional): Enter the duration of the Sunday School.
* **Sunday School Frequency** (`sunday_school_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Select Yes if you wish to attend Sunday School.
