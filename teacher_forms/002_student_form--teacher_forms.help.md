# Student Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Form is used for administrative and organizational purposes to collect essential details about students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Date of Birth** fields with the relevant information.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Phone** numbers.
3. Type your **Address** in the designated field.
4. Select your **Category** from the provided options.
5. Optionally, fill in the **Assigned Tool** field if you have one.
6. Provide a brief **Description** of yourself or your role.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`student_form_1`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`student_form_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`student_form_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Email** (`student_form_email`, email, required): Enter your email address that you use for official communication.
* **Phone** (`student_form_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number that you use for contact.
* **Address** (`student_form_address`, text, required): Enter your residential or office address.
* **Category** (`student_form_category`, select_one, required): Select the category that best describes your role or affiliation.
* **Assigned Tool** (`student_form_assigned_tool`, text, optional): If you have been assigned a tool, enter its name here.
* **Description** (`student_form_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of yourself or your role.

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about your category, consult with your supervisor or refer to your ID documents.
* Keep your description brief and concise.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact your administrative support team.
