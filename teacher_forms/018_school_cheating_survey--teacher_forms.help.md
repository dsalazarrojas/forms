# School Cheating Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from teachers on the prevalence and handling of cheating in schools.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions to access and complete this form.
2. Review each question carefully and answer honestly.
3. Select the correct options for each question.
4. Fill in any text fields as needed.
5. Click submit to submit your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Id**: Enter your teacher ID number.
* **Class Id**: Enter your class ID number.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your school.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter your name.
* **Class Name**: Enter the name of your class.
* **Do you think cheating is a serious problem in your school?**:
	+ Select one of the options: Yes, No, or Not sure.
* **What type of cheating do you think is most prevalent in your school?**:
	+ Select all that apply:
		- Cheating on tests and quizzes
		- Cheating on homework
		- Cheating on classwork
		- Other (please specify)
* **Have you seen cheating in your class?**:
	+ Select one of the options: True or False.
* **Class size**: Enter the number of students in your class.
* **Student count**: Enter the total number of students in your school.
* **Teacher support**: Enter the number of teachers who support you in handling cheating.
* **Class duration**: Enter the duration of your class in minutes.
* **School support**: Enter the number of school staff who support you in handling cheating.
* **Student attendance**: Enter the number of students who attend your class regularly.
* **How often do you think students cheat in class?**:
	+ Select one of the options: Daily, Weekly, Rarely, or Never.
* **How do you handle cheating in class?**:
	+ Select one of the options: Verbal warning, Written warning, Detention, or Other.
* **Teacher Id 2**: Enter your teacher ID number (this field is likely a duplicate, please ignore).
* **What do you do when you catch a student cheating?**:
	+ Enter a brief description of your actions when you catch a student cheating.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by teachers, and the information collected will be used to understand cheating behaviors in schools. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your school administration.
