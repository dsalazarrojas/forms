# Student Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a Student Satisfaction Survey, designed to gather feedback from students about their learning experience. It aims to gauge their satisfaction, assess their understanding of the material, and collect suggestions for improvement. Please complete the form to provide your insights and help us refine our educational content.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your thoughts and ratings.
2. Provide feedback on what you learned today.
3. Rate your overall experience and the instructor's performance.
4. Add any comments or suggestions you may have.
5. Enter the instructor's name and course URL (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What did you learn today?** (`student_survey`, text, optional): Share your thoughts on what you learned during today's session.
* **Rate your experience** (`rating`, number, optional): Choose a number between 1 and 10 to rate your overall experience.
* **Rate instructor** (`instructor_rating`, number, optional): Rate the instructor's performance on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Comments about instructor** (`instructor_comments`, text, optional): Provide any comments or feedback about the instructor.
* **Overall rating** (`overall_rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Instructor name** (`instructor_name`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you received instructor's feedback (Yes/No).
* **Course name** (`course_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the course you are taking.
* **Course URL** (`course_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of the course, if available.
* **Submission date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of submission. 

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when providing feedback.
* Your responses are invaluable for our improvement.
* If you have any questions, feel free to contact us.
