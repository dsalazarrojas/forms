# Online Summer School Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow teachers to submit applications for the online summer school program. This form will help us collect essential information about the teachers and students participating in the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Application Submission field with your thoughts and proposals for the online summer school program.
2. Provide the number of students that will participate in the summer classes.
3. Choose the start and end dates for the program.
4. Select the start and end times for the program.
5. Enter your name and contact information (email and phone number) for communication purposes.
6. List the names of the students participating in the program.
7. Enter the email and phone numbers for each student.
8. Indicate if each student will attend the summer school program (Yes/No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Submission** (application_submission, text, optional): Briefly describe your application for the online summer school program.
* **Number Of Students** (num_students, number, optional): Enter the number of students that will participate in the summer classes.
* **School Start Date** (school_start_date, date, optional): Choose the start date for the online summer school program.
* **School Start Time** (school_start_time, time, optional): Select the start time for the online summer school program.
* **School End Date** (school_end_date, date, optional): Choose the end date for the online summer school program.
* **School End Time** (school_end_time, time, optional): Select the end time for the online summer school program.
* **Teacher Name** (teacher_name, text, optional): Enter your name for communication purposes.
* **Teacher Email** (teacher_email, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Teacher Phone** (teacher_phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Student Names** (student_names, text, optional): List the names of the students participating in the program.
* **Student Emails** (student_emails, text, optional): Enter the email addresses of the students participating in the program.
* **Student Phones** (student_phones, text, optional): Enter the phone numbers of the students participating in the program.
* **Student Attendance** (student_attendance, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if each student will attend the summer school program (Yes/No).
