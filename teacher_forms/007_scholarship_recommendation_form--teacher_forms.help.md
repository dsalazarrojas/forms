# Scholarship Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for teachers to provide a recommendation for a student applying for a scholarship. Please provide as much information as possible about the student to help us evaluate their qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and the name of the student you are recommending.
2. Select your title and the years you have worked with the student.
3. Provide your contact information, including phone number and email.
4. Explain the reason for your recommendation.
5. Choose the qualities you would like us to consider when evaluating the student's scholarship application.
6. Sign and date the form.
7. If applicable, provide additional comments or recommendations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Teachers Name**: Please fill in your name, which will be used to authenticate your identity.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school where you have worked with the student.
* **Students Name**: Fill in the name of the student you are recommending.
* **Academic Title**: Choose the title that best describes your position at the school (e.g., Professor, Teacher, Instructor, Administrator, Counselor, or Other).
* **Years Worked**: Enter the number of years you have worked with the student.
* **Contact Number**: Provide your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Reason For Recommending**: Explain why you are recommending the student for a scholarship.
* **Students Qualities**: Choose the qualities you believe the student possesses (e.g., Strong Work Ethic, Excellent Attendance, Respectful, Responsible, Punctual, or Other).
* **Signature**: Sign your name to authenticate your identity.
* **Date Released**: Date when you submitted this form.
* **School Name2**: Enter the name of the school where you are recommending the student.
* **Teacher Signature**: Sign your name again to confirm your identity.
* **Date Submitted**: Date when you submitted this form.
* **Other Scholarships**: Check if this student has applied for other scholarships and provide details if applicable.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or recommendations for the student.
* **Teacher Name2**: Fill in your name again for authentication.
* **School District**: Enter the school district where you are recommending the student.
* **Teachers Address**: Enter your address.
* **Teacher Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Teacher Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Other Recommendations**: Check if you have other recommendations for the student and provide details if applicable.
* **Additional Comments2**: Add any additional comments or recommendations for the student.
* **School Name3**: Enter the name of the school where you are recommending the student.
* **Teachers Education**: Enter any additional educational details about yourself.
* **Teacher Name3**: Fill in your name again for authentication.
