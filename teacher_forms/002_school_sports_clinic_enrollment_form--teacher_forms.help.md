# School Sports Clinic Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to enroll students into school sports clinics. Please fill out this form carefully to provide us with the necessary information to process your enrollment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name
2. Fill in your Last Name
3. Select your Grade Level (6th, 7th, 8th, 9th, 10th, 11th, or 12th)
4. Fill in your Student Name
5. Fill in your Parent/Guardian Name
6. Enter your Email
7. Fill in your Phone number
8. Select the program you wish to enroll in (Basketball, Cheerleading, Dance, Football, Soccer, Track, or Volleyball)
9. Select your payment method (Cash, Check, or Credit Card)
10. Enter the payment amount
11. Enter the payment date
12. Select the payment time

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Your first name, which will be displayed in our records.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Your last name, which will be displayed in our records.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, optional): The grade level you are currently in. This is helpful for us to understand your eligibility for certain programs.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Your full name as it should appear in our records.
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): The name of your parent or guardian.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address, which we will use to contact you with important information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Your phone number, which we will use to contact you with important information.
* **Program** (`program`, select_one, optional): The program you wish to enroll in (Basketball, Cheerleading, Dance, Football, Soccer, Track, or Volleyball).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): The method you will use to make payment for the program (Cash, Check, or Credit Card).
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, optional): The amount you plan to pay for the program.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, optional): The date you plan to make payment.
* **Payment Time** (`payment_time`, time, optional): The time you plan to make payment.
