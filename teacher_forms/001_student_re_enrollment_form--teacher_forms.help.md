# Student Re Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect and store student information for re-enrollment purposes. Re-enrollment is the process of re-registering a student after they have been removed from the system or have not been active for a certain period. It is an important part of maintaining accurate student records and ensuring that students have access to the necessary resources and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's first name in the "First Name" field. This is the student's first name.
2. Enter the student's last name in the "Last Name" field. This is the student's last name.
3. Select the student's grade level from the "Grade Level" dropdown menu. Choose from "Freshman", "Sophmore", "Junior", or "Senior".
4. Enter the student's email address in the "Email" field. This will help us contact the student and keep them informed.
5. If the student is re-enrolling, enter their date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field. This will help us verify the student's identity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name (First Name)** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the student's first name.
  - **Last Name (Last Name)** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the student's last name.
  - **Grade Level (Grade Level)** (`grade_level`, `select_one`, optional): Select the student's grade level from "Freshman", "Sophmore", "Junior", or "Senior".
  - **Email (Email)** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter the student's email address. This will help us contact the student and keep them informed.
  - **Date of Birth (Date of Birth)** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): If the student is re-enrolling, enter their date of birth to verify their identity.
