# Tutor Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students seeking tutoring services to match them with suitable tutors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details in the "User Details" field, including your name and any relevant contact information.
2. Specify the subject you need help with in the "Subject" field.
3. Provide a brief description of your request in the "Request Details" field.
4. Select the tutors you are interested in from the "Available Tutors" dropdown menu. You can choose multiple tutors if desired.
5. If you have a preferred time for the tutoring session, enter it in the "Preferred Time" field.
6. If you have a specific date for the tutoring session, enter it in the "Preferred Date" field.
7. Once you have completed all the fields, click the "Submit" button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details (User Details)**: Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information in this field.
* **Subject (Subject)**: Specify the subject area you need help with, e.g., math, science, or language arts.
* **Request Details (Request Details)**: Briefly describe your request, including any specific areas of difficulty or goals you have for the tutoring session.
* **Available Tutors (Available Tutors)**: Select the tutors you are interested in working with. You can choose multiple tutors if you have a preference.
* **Preferred Time (Preferred Time)**: If you have a preferred time for the tutoring session, enter it here. This will help the tutor schedule accordingly.
* **Preferred Date (Preferred Date)**: If you have a specific date for the tutoring session, enter it here. This will help the tutor plan accordingly.
* **Submit (Submit)**: Once you have completed all the fields, click this button to send your request to the available tutors.
