# Hybrid Learning Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect opinions and feedback from teachers about hybrid learning, an educational approach that combines traditional face-to-face learning with online or digital components.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions about your level of familiarity with hybrid learning and your opinions on its effectiveness.
2. Share any suggestions or comments you may have about hybrid learning.
3. Enter your email address and phone number to provide a way for us to contact you with follow-up information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (Page 1): This is a free-text field where you can briefly introduce yourself and your experience with hybrid learning.
	* Please provide a short description of your background and experience with hybrid learning.
* **What is your experience with hybrid learning?** (Page 2):
	+ Choose one of the three options:
		- I am not sure: You don't have any experience with hybrid learning.
		- Somewhat familiar: You have some experience or knowledge about hybrid learning.
		- Very familiar: You have a good understanding of hybrid learning and its concepts.
* **What do you think about hybrid learning?** (Page 3):
	+ Choose one of the five options:
		- Strongly agree: You strongly agree with the effectiveness of hybrid learning.
		- Somewhat agree: You somewhat agree with the effectiveness of hybrid learning.
		- Neither agree nor disagree: You are neutral about hybrid learning.
		- Somewhat disagree: You somewhat disagree with the effectiveness of hybrid learning.
		- Strongly disagree: You strongly disagree with the effectiveness of hybrid learning.
* **Do you have any suggestions about hybrid learning?** (Page 4):
	+ This is a free-text field where you can share any suggestions or ideas you may have about hybrid learning.
* **Additional comments** (Page 5):
	+ This is a free-text field where you can provide any additional comments or thoughts about hybrid learning.
* **Email** (Page 6):
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you with follow-up information.
* **Phone** (Page 7):
	+ Enter your phone number so we can contact you with follow-up information.

## Tips
- Please be honest and thorough in your answers.
- Make sure to enter your correct email address and phone number.
- If you have any suggestions or comments, please provide as much detail as possible.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to ask.
