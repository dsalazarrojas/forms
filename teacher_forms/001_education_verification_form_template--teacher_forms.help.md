# Education Verification Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Education Verification Form Template is a form used to verify an employee's educational background, including their high school, college, and university education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter the employee's email address in the "Employee Email" field.
3. Provide the employee's phone number in the "Employee Phone" field.
4. Enter the employee's address in the "Employee Address" field.
5. Select the type of institution the employee attended from the "High School", "College", or "University" dropdown menu.
6. If applicable, enter the employee's high school name and location.
7. If applicable, select the degree obtained from the "High School Degree", "College Degree", or "University Degree" dropdown menu.
8. Enter the name of the institution where the employee earned their degree.
9. If applicable, select the type of degree obtained from the "Diploma" dropdown menu.
10. Enter any additional certification or qualifications the employee has.
11. Enter the certification number (if applicable).
12. Enter the details of who verified the employee's education.
13. Enter the date the education was verified.
14. Enter the time the education was verified (if applicable).
15. Add any verification notes.
16. Add any employee notes.
17. Sign the form with the employee's signature.
18. Sign the form with the employer's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (Employee Name, text, required): Enter the employee's full name.
* **Employee Email** (Employee Email, email, optional): Enter the employee's email address.
* **Employee Phone** (Employee Phone, text, optional): Provide the employee's phone number.
* **Employee Address** (Employee Address, text, optional): Enter the employee's address.
* **High School** (High School, select_one, optional): Select "High School" to verify the employee's high school education.
* **High School Degree** (High School Degree, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of degree obtained from high school.
* **High School Name** (High School Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the high school attended.
* **High School Location** (High School Location, text, optional): Enter the location of the high school.
* **College Name** (College Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the college attended.
* **College Degree** (College Degree, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of degree obtained from college.
* **College Location** (College Location, text, optional): Enter the location of the college.
* **University Name** (University Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the university attended.
* **University Degree** (University Degree, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of degree obtained from university.
* **University Location** (University Location, text, optional): Enter the location of the university.
* **Diploma** (Diploma, select_one, optional): Select the type of diploma obtained.
* **Certification** (Certification, text, optional): Enter any additional certification or qualifications.
* **Certification Number** (Certification Number, text, optional): Enter the certification number (if applicable).
* **Education Verified By** (Education Verified By, text, optional): Enter the details of who verified the employee's education.
* **Education Verified Date** (Education Verified Date, date, optional): Enter the date the education was verified.
* **Education Verified Time** (Education Verified Time, time, optional): Enter the time the education was verified (if applicable).
* **Verification Notes** (Verification Notes, note, optional): Add any verification notes.
* **Employee Notes** (Employee Notes, note, optional): Add any employee notes.
* **Employee Signature** (Employee Signature, note, optional): Sign the form with the employee's signature.
* **Employer Signature** (Employer Signature, note, optional): Sign the form with the employer's signature.
