# Graduation Apparel Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students or administrators for ordering graduation apparel for the school. The form is designed to be filled out by students or school staff to order graduation attire in an efficient and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **School ID**: Enter your school's unique identifier if required.
2. **Graduation Year**: Indicate the year of the graduation event.
3. **Graduation Day**: Specify the day of the graduation event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### School ID (school_id)
* The **school ID** field is used to identify the school affiliated with the graduation order. It is an optional field but can be required based on the school's internal policies.

### Graduation Year (graduation_year)
* The **graduation year** field is used to specify the year of the graduation event. This helps in organizing and preparing the graduation attire orders according to the year.

### Graduation Day (graduation_day)
* The **graduation day** field is used to specify the day of the graduation event. This is crucial in determining the exact date for preparation and delivery of the ordered attire.

## Tips
* Ensure that the fields are filled out accurately to avoid any confusion or delays in processing the order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant school staff or administrators.
* This form is designed to be used for a specific purpose; ensure that it is not used for any other purpose or industry.
