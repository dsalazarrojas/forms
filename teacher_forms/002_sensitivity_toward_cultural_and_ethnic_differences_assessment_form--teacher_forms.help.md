# sensitivity_toward_cultural_and_ethnic_differences_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess a teacher's sensitivity and awareness towards cultural and ethnic differences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reading the introduction section and understand the context of the form.
2. Select the relevant options for each question in the Questions section, which asks about your experience and approach to teaching students from different cultural backgrounds.
3. In the Self Reflection sections, provide detailed answers to the open-ended questions, describing your thoughts and feelings about cultural differences.
4. For the Sensitivity and Cultural Differences sections, select the most relevant option from the given choices.
