# Substitute Staff Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for Substitute Staff to complete an application for substitute teaching positions within the district. It is a required form for all substitute applicants. The form must not be submitted multiple times; each submission will be treated as a new application.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields: `teacher_form`, `contact_form`, `availability`, and `agreement`.
2. For fields that are optional, provide any additional information you wish to share with the district.
3. Upload your resume in the `upload_resume` field.
4. Ensure that you have selected "Yes" for all applicable `availability` fields.
5. Review your application form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **teacher_form** (text, required): Enter your full name as a teacher.
* **contact_form** (text, required): Provide your contact information (phone number and email).
* **work_history_form** (text, optional): Describe your work history, including any previous teaching experience.
* **availability** (select_multiple, required): Select all the days and times you are available to substitute teach. Choose from: "Yes" (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday).
* **job_preferences** (text, optional): Share any specific job preferences, such as specific schools or departments you would be interested in substituting in.
* **education** (text, optional): Share your educational background, including any relevant degrees or certifications.
* **work_experience** (text, optional): Describe your relevant work experience.
* **references** (text, optional): Provide the names and contact information of at least two professional references.
* **emergency_contact** (text, optional): Provide an emergency contact person's name and phone number.
* **upload_resume** (text, optional): Upload your resume in PDF or docx format.

## Tips
- Make sure to proofread your application for any errors or missing information before submitting it.
- Ensure that you have answered all required fields.
- If you are unsure about a field or its requirement, contact the district administrator before submitting the form.
