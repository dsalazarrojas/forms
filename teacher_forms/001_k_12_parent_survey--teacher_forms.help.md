# K 12 Parent Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The K 12 Parent Survey is designed to collect feedback and information from K-12 parents on their experiences with their child's education. This form is used to gather insights and suggestions from parents to improve the quality of education provided to their children.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact information: Enter your school's name, your contact email, and phone number.
2. Provide feedback: Answer the open-ended questions (school name, feedback questions, suggestions, and comments).
3. Optional: You can optionally provide your teacher's name and student's ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name (school_name)**: Enter the name of your child's school.
* **Contact Email (contact_email)**: Enter your email address so the school can contact you.
* **Contact Phone (contact_phone)**: Enter your phone number so the school can contact you.
* **Feedback Questions (feedback_questions)**: Answer the open-ended question about your child's educational experience.
* **Suggestions (suggestions)**: Provide any suggestions you have to improve the school or your child's education.
* **Comments (comments)**: Share any additional comments you have about your child's education.
* **Teacher Name (teacher_name)**: If you have a preferred teacher, enter their name.
* **Student ID (student_id)**: If you have your child's student ID, enter it (though this field is not required).
* **School Grade (school_grade)**: Enter the grade level of your child (e.g., 3rd, 4th, 5th, etc.).
* **Parent Survey Response (parent_survey_response)**: Share your general thoughts on your child's education.
* **Comments (comments_2, comments_3, comments_4, comments_5)**: Share any additional comments you have (though these fields are not required).

## Tips
- Be honest and open in your responses.
- Keep your answers concise and clear.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the school.
