# Parent Handbook Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about your child and your relationship with us. Please fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure we can provide the best possible support for your child.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer all the questions accurately and completely.
2. If a question is not applicable, please write "Not Applicable" in the space provided.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Information** (1, <name>, required/optional): This is the section for you to provide your contact information and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Contact Information** (2, <name>, required/optional): Please provide your contact details, including your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Emergency Contact** (3, <name>, required/optional): This is the section for you to provide information about your emergency contact, including your relationship with them and their contact details.
* **Medical Conditions** (4, <name>, required/optional): Please list any medical conditions or health issues that your child may have.
* **Allergies** (5, <name>, required/optional): Please list any allergies or sensitivities that your child may have.
* **Student Information** (6, <name>, required/optional): Please provide any other relevant information about your child, such as any special needs or requirements.
* **Emergency Medical** (7, <name>, required/optional): This is the section for you to provide information about any emergency medical procedures or conditions that may affect your child.
* **Medication** (8, <name>, required/optional): Please list any medications or treatments that your child is currently taking.
* **Special Needs** (9, <name>, required/optional): Please list any special needs or requirements that your child may have.
* **Medical Authorization** (10, <name>, required/optional): This is the section for you to provide medical authorization or consent information.
* **Consent** (11, <name>, required/optional): Please indicate if you give your consent for us to share your information with medical professionals or other relevant parties.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (12, <name>, required/optional): Please provide information about your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (13, <name>, required/optional): Please provide the address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (14, <name>, required/optional): Please provide the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (15, <name>, required/optional): This is the duplicate of the previous field and is likely an error in the form schema. Please ignore this field. 

Note: The last field is a duplicate and is likely an error in the form schema. Please fill out the previous field, Emergency Contact Relationship (12, <name>, required/optional).
