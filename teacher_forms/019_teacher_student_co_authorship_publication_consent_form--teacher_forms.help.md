# Teacher Student Co Authorship Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain the consent of both the student and the teacher for co-authorship publication. It is a critical document that ensures both parties are aware of and agree to the terms of co-authorship publication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your details as a student, including your name and email address.
2. Select the type of co-authorship you are involved in (Student, Teacher, Both).
3. Provide your consent for co-authorship publication.
4. Fill out your details as a teacher, including your name and department.
5. Provide your consent for co-authorship publication.
6. Add any additional comments if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, text, required): Please fill out your name as a student.
* **Student Email** (`Student Email`, email, required): Please fill out your email address as a student.
* **Teacher Name** (`Teacher Name`, text, required): Please fill out your name as a teacher.
* **Teacher Department** (`Teacher Department`, text, required): Please fill out your department as a teacher.
* **Publication Title** (`Publication Title`, text, required): Please fill out the title of the publication.
* **Publication Date** (`Publication Date`, date, required): Please fill out the date of publication.
* **Co-Authorship Type** (`Co-Authorship Type`, select_one, required): Select the type of co-authorship you are involved in (Student, Teacher, Both).
* **Student Consent** (`Student Consent`, select_multiple, required): Check if you agree to co-authorship publication.
* **Teacher Consent** (`Teacher Consent`, select_multiple, required): Check if you agree to co-authorship publication.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, note, required): Please add any additional comments.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* Ensure that you select the correct co-authorship type.
* Check the boxes for both student and teacher consent carefully.
* Add any additional comments if necessary.
