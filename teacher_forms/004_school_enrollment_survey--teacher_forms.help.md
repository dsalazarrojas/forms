# School Enrollment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Enrollment Survey is a form designed to collect information about students and their families. It is intended for use by schools or educational institutions to gather data for administrative or educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for each field, as indicated by the "required" label.
2. Leave fields blank if you are unable to provide information or if it is not applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student family** (`Student family`, text, required: false): This field is for collecting information about the student's family members, such as parents or guardians.
* **Student background** (`Student background`, text, required: false): This field is for collecting any relevant background information about the student.
* **Academic background** (`Academic background`, text, required: false): This field is for collecting information about the student's academic background, including any relevant education or coursework.
* **Student contact** (`Student contact`, text, required: false): This field is for collecting the student's contact information, such as email, phone number, or address.
* **School information** (`School information`, text, required: false): This field is for collecting information about the school or educational institution the student is associated with.
* **Parent info** (`Parent info`, text, required: false): This field is for collecting information about the student's parents or guardians.
* **Family info** (`Family info`, text, required: false): This field is for collecting information about the student's family members.
* **Student notes** (`Student notes`, text, required: false): This field is for collecting any additional notes or comments about the student.
* **Teacher notes** (`Teacher notes`, text, required: false): This field is for collecting any additional notes or comments from the teacher or school staff.
* **Family background** (`Family background`, text, required: false): This field is for collecting information about the student's family background, including any relevant socioeconomic data.
* **Student status** (`Student status`, text, required: false): This field is for collecting the student's current status, such as active, inactive, or pending.

## Tips
This form is designed to be flexible and adaptable to the specific needs of schools or educational institutions. Please feel free to modify or add fields as needed to suit your specific requirements. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
