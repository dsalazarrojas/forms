# Homeschool Co Op Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps homeschooling families register with a cooperative, providing basic contact information to enable communication and membership management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with your desired information.
2. Submit the form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`text`, optional): Enter your first name.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`text`, optional): Enter your last name.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter a valid email address to receive updates and communications from the cooperative.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact or communication purposes.

### Address
* **Address** (`text`, optional): Enter your address for record-keeping and logistical purposes.

## Tips
- You can fill out as many or as few fields as you need. 
- If you have any issues or questions while registering, contact us for assistance.
