# Online Slam Book Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Slam Book Form is designed to collect feedback, comments, or memories from users about a specific online event or occasion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter your User Name in the "User Name" field.
2. Provide your User Email in the "User Email" field.
3. Type your message or comment in the "Message" field.
4. Describe the event or occasion in the "Event" field.
5. Share your memory or reflection in the "Memory" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your name to associate your feedback with your identity.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, optional): Provide your email address to allow us to contact you, if needed.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Share your thoughts, comments, or feedback about the event.
* **Event** (`event`, text, optional): Describe the event or occasion that inspired your feedback.
* **Memory** (`memory`, text, optional): Record your memory or reflection about the event.
