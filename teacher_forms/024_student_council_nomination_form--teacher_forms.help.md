# Student Council Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from students who are interested in running for a position in the Student Council.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Introduce yourself and explain why you are interested in being on the Student Council.
2.  Fill out all the required fields, such as your name, role of interest, and availability.
3.  Choose your skills and strengths that make you a good fit for the position.
4.  Provide your contact information and any additional comments.
5.  Indicate whether you are available to serve and which student council positions you are interested in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (Introduction, text, required): Briefly introduce yourself and explain why you are interested in being on the Student Council.
* **Name** (Name, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your student ID or official records.
* **Role of Interest** (Role of Interest, select_one, required): Choose the role you are interested in, such as President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer, or Other.
* **Available to Serve** (Available to Serve, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you are available to serve on the Student Council. If you are not available, explain your reasons.
* **Skills and Strengths** (Skills and Strengths, text, optional): List your relevant skills and strengths that make you a good fit for the position.
* **Availability** (Availability, date, optional): Provide your availability for meetings and events, if applicable.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, optional): Enter your contact information, such as your email and phone number.
* **Position Desires** (Position Desires, text, optional): If you have a specific position in mind, describe it here.
* **Student Council Nominations** (Student Council Nominations, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you are interested in being nominated for the Student Council.
* **Student Council Committee** (Student Council Committee, select_multiple, optional): Choose whether you are interested in joining the Student Council Committee.
* **School Events** (School Events, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you have experience or interest in organizing school events.
* **Extracurricular Activities** (Extracurricular Activities, select_one, optional): Choose whether you are part of or interested in extracurricular activities.
* **Student Leadership** (Student Leadership, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you have leadership experience or interest in leadership roles.
* **Class Representative** (Class Representative, select_one, optional): Choose whether you are interested in being a Class Representative.
* **Position Ideas** (Position Ideas, text, optional): If you have specific ideas for positions or roles, describe them here.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your qualifications.
* **Name Title** (Name Title, text, optional): Enter your name title or preferred name format.
* **Position** (Position, text, optional): If you have a specific position in mind, describe it here.
* **Position Id 2** (Position Id 2, text, optional): This field is not used in the nomination process.
