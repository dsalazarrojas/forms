# School Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Absence Form is designed to inform the school administration about your child's absence from school. Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure that your child's absence is properly recorded and handled.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your child's **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Parent Name** in the respective fields.
2. Enter your **Contact Information** (email address) so we can reach you in case of any updates.
3. Select the **Dates of Absence** when your child will be absent.
4. Choose the **Dates of Return** when your child will be back to school.
5. Provide a brief reason for the absence in the **Reason for Absence** field.
6. Add any additional **Parent Contact Info** if you want to be contacted by the school.
7. Enter the **School Name** where your child attends classes.
8. Fill in the name of the **Teacher's Name** responsible for your child's absence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your child's first name.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your child's last name.

### Parent Name
* **Parent Name** (text, not required): Enter your name if you need to be contacted by the school.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (email, required): Enter your email address so we can reach you.

### Dates of Absence
* **Dates of Absence** (date, required): Select the dates when your child will be absent.

### Dates of Return
* **Dates of Return** (date, required): Choose the date when your child will return to school.

### Reason for Absence
* **Reason for Absence** (text, required): Give a brief reason for your child's absence.

### Parent Contact Info
* **Parent Contact Info** (text, not required): Add any additional contact information for the school to reach you.

### School Name
* **School Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the school your child attends.

### Teacher's Name
* **Teacher's Name** (text, required): Fill in the name of the teacher responsible for your child's absence.
