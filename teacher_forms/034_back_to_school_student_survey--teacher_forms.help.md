# Back To School Student Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your thoughts, feelings, and experiences at school. It's a helpful tool for teachers to get to know you better and identify areas where they can provide support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Read each question carefully** and select the answer that best fits your feelings or experiences.
2. **Fill in any text fields** (like "Your Name" and "Something Teacher Should Know") with your responses.
3. **Select all applicable options** for multiple-choice questions (like "Extracurricular Activities Interest").
4. **Don't worry if you're unsure** about an answer. Just choose the option that feels most accurate.
5. **Be honest** about your feelings and experiences. This form is designed to help teachers get to know you better!

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name**: Write your full first and last name here.
* **Grade Level**: Select your current grade level (e.g., Grade 1, Grade 2, etc.).
* **Teacher Name**: Write the name of your current teacher.
* **How Do You Feel About School**: Select how you feel about school (e.g., Very Excited, Neutral, etc.).
* **Favorite Subject**: Choose your favorite subject (e.g., Math, Reading or Language Arts, etc.).
* **Most Challenging Subject**: Select the subject that's hardest for you (e.g., Math, Reading or Language Arts, etc.).
* **How Do You Learn Best**: Choose how you prefer to learn (e.g., Reading and writing, Listening to lectures, etc.).
* **Homework Habits**: Select how you handle homework (e.g., Complete right away, Need reminders, etc.).
* **Do You Enjoy Reading**: Choose how much you like reading (e.g., Love it, Like it, etc.).
* **Books Read for Fun Last Year**: Select approximately how many books you've read (e.g., 0 books, 1 to 5 books, etc.).
* **Friend Group Size**: Choose how many close friends you have (e.g., No close friends, 1 to 2 friends, etc.).
* **Get Along with Classmates**: Select how well you get along with your classmates (e.g., Very Well, Okay, etc.).
* **Extracurricular Activities Interest**: Select any activities that interest you (e.g., Sports teams, Music or band, etc.).
* **Have You Been Bullied**: Select if you've experienced bullying (e.g., Yes, recently, No, never, etc.).
* **Feel Safe at School**: Choose how safe you feel at school (e.g., Always, Most of the time, etc.).
* **Teachers Are Supportive**: Select if you feel supported by your teachers (e.g., Strongly Agree, Disagree, etc.).
* **Your Goals This Year**: Write any goals you have for the upcoming year.
* **What Worries You About School**: Select any concerns you have about school (e.g., Grades, Bullying, etc.).
* **Favorite School Activity**: Write your favorite activity at school.
* **Something Teacher Should Know**: Write any important information about yourself that you'd like your teacher to know.

## Tips
* Take your time and be honest about your thoughts and feelings.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to ask your teacher.
* Remember, this form is designed to help your teacher get to know you better and provide support where needed!
