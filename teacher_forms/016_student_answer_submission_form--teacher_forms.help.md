# Student Answer Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for students to submit their answers to assignments, providing a structured and organized way to collect and review student responses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary information and answers ready before starting.
2. Fill in the required fields, such as `Submission Method` and `Submission Status`.
3. Enter your answers to each question, taking care to fill in all relevant fields.
4. Review your responses for accuracy and completeness.
5. Submit the form once you are satisfied with your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student ID**: Enter your unique student ID number.
* **Course Code**: Enter the code for the course related to the assignment.
* **Assignment Title**: Enter the title of the assignment you are submitting for.
* **Question**: Enter your answer to the question posed by the teacher.
* **Student Answer**: Enter your answer to the assignment.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the date and time you submitted the answer.
* **Submission Method**: Select how you submitted the answer (`Online`, `In-Person`, or `Other`).
* **Submitted By**: Select who submitted the answer (`Teacher`, `Staff`, or `Student`).
* **Submission Status**: Select the current status of your submission (`Pending`, `Approved`, `Rejected`, or `Archived`).
* **Grade**: Enter the grade you believe you deserve for the assignment.
* **Grade Level**: Enter your grade level (e.g., grade 1-12).
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or notes related to your submission.
* **Comments 2**: Enter any additional comments or notes related to your submission.
* **Student Name**: Enter your name.
* **Assignment Code**: Enter the code for the assignment.
* **Course Name**: Enter the name of the course related to the assignment.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of the teacher for the assignment.
* **School Year**: Enter the school year for the assignment.
* **Department**: Enter the department for the assignment.
* **Section**: Enter the section for the assignment.
* **Student Group**: Enter the student group for the assignment.
* **Submitted To**: Select who the submission was submitted to (`Teacher`, `Staff`, or `Student`).
* **Submitted By Teacher**: Select who submitted the answer (`Teacher`, `Staff`, or `Student`).
* **Submitted At 2**: Enter the date and time you submitted the answer.
