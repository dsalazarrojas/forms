# Certification Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Certification Application Form is a comprehensive application process designed to gather information from applicants for a certification program in education. This form is used to evaluate applicants' qualifications, experience, and background to determine their eligibility for the certification program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read and understand the form instructions and questions.
2. Provide accurate and truthful information about yourself.
3. Fill out the form in a sequential manner, one page at a time.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
5. Submit the completed form to the certification program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name of Applicant** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter your legal first, middle, and last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your birth date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, text, required): Enter your street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Current Employment Status** (`current_employment`, select_one, required): Select your current employment status:
	+ Employed Full-time
	+ Employed Part-time
	+ Self-Employed
	+ Unemployed
	+ Student
* **Current Position or Title** (`employment_position`, text, optional): Enter your current job title if employed.
* **Years of Relevant Experience** (`years_experience`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been working in the relevant field.
* **Certification Program Applying For** (`certification_program`, select_one, required): Select the certification program you are applying for:
	+ Early Childhood Education
	+ Elementary Education
	+ Secondary Education
	+ Special Education
	+ Administrative Leadership
	+ Instructional Technology
* **Highest Degree Earned** (`highest_degree`, select_one, required): Select your highest educational credential:
	+ High School Diploma
	+ Bachelor's Degree
	+ Master's Degree
	+ Doctoral Degree
* **Field of Study for Highest Degree** (`degree_field`, text, optional): Enter the major or subject area of your highest degree.
* **College or University Attended** (`college_attended`, text, optional): Enter the name of the institution where you earned your highest degree.
* **Grade Point Average** (`gpa_record`, number, optional): Enter the GPA of your highest degree.
* **Background Check Consent** (`background_check_consent`, select_one, required): Select whether you consent to a background check:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Any Criminal History** (`criminal_history`, select_one, required): Select whether you have ever been convicted of a crime:
	+ No
	+ Yes
	+ Prefer Not to Answer
* **If Yes, Provide Details** (`criminal_details`, text, optional): Enter a description of any conviction.
* **Three Professional References** (`professional_references`, text, required): Enter the names, titles, and contact information of three professional references.
* **Motivation for Seeking Certification** (`motivation_essay`, text, required): Enter a brief statement about why you are pursuing certification.
* **Professional Goals** (`professional_goals`, text, optional): Enter your objectives after earning certification.
* **Financial Assistance Needed** (`financial_need`, select_one, optional): Select whether you need financial aid:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **When Can You Start Program** (`availability_start`, date, optional): Enter your preferred program start date.
* **Agreement to Program Requirements** (`application_agreement`, select_one, required): Select whether you agree to meet all program requirements:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **I Certify Information is True and Complete** (`acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Select whether you certify that the information provided is true and complete:
	+ Yes, I Agree
	+ No

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when providing information.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.
- Ensure you have all necessary documents and references before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the certification program administrators.
