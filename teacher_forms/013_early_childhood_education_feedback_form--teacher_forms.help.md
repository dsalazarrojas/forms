# Early Childhood Education Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Early Childhood Education Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from parents or guardians about their child's educational experience in an early childhood education program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information (contact number and email) in the provided fields.
2. Select the teacher or teachers who have been assigned to your child's case.
3. Choose the education program your child is enrolled in.
4. Provide feedback about your child's progress and experience in the program.
5. Select the rating for your child's progress and experience.
6. Add any additional comments or feedback about your child's education.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Parent Name
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your name and the name of your child who is enrolled in the program.

### Teacher ID
* **Teacher ID** (`teacher_id`, number, required): Enter the ID number of the teacher who has been assigned to your child's case. If you're unsure, please contact the program administrator for assistance.

### Education Program
* **Education Program** (`education_program`, text, required): Select the education program your child is enrolled in.

### Feedback
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required): Provide detailed feedback about your child's progress and experience in the program. Include any concerns, praises, or suggestions you may have.

### Start Date
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the program.

### End Date
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the program.

### Rating
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, required): Select one of the following ratings:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about your child's education.

### Contact Number
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number so that the program administrators can reach you for follow-up discussions.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so that the program administrators can contact you for follow-up discussions.

### Assigned To
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_multiple, required): Choose the teacher or teachers who have been assigned to your child's case.

### Assigned By
* **Assigned By** (`assigned_by`, select_multiple, required): Choose the teacher or teachers who have assigned to your child's case.

Note: The "Assigned To" and "Assigned By" fields are meant to be filled with the same teacher names as in the "Teacher ID" and "Teacher 1, 2" fields, respectively.
