# Course Registration And Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Registration And Payment Form is used to collect information from individuals registering for a course, including their name, contact information, payment method, and payment amount. This form is an essential part of the course registration process, allowing course administrators to track attendance and manage course finances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the course you are registering for in the text field labeled "course_name".
2. Provide your name in the text field labeled "attendee_name".
3. Enter your email address in the text field labeled "email".
4. If necessary, enter your phone number in the text field labeled "phone".
5. Select your preferred payment method from the dropdown menu labeled "payment_method" (Cash, Card, or PayPal).
6. Enter the amount you wish to pay in the number field labeled "payment_amount".
7. Select the date you plan to attend the course in the date field labeled "date".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the course you are registering for.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the person registering for the course.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication and course updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact information.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select from the available payment options:
	+ Cash: Pay by cash at the time of course registration.
	+ Card: Pay by credit or debit card.
	+ PayPal: Pay using your PayPal account.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you wish to pay for the course.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date you plan to attend the course.

## Tips
* Please ensure you are registering for the correct course by double-checking the course name.
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any issues with course registration or payment processing.
* If you are having trouble with the form, contact the course administrator for assistance.
