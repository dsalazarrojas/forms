# Curriculum Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from teachers to help them better understand the curriculum and their own role in the educational process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions carefully and accurately.
2. Select the relevant options for each question.
3. Use the text fields to provide any additional information or comments.
4. Ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **main_page** (Text, Optional): This field is the main page of the form where you can provide any additional comments or information.
* **introduction** (Text, Optional): This field will serve as an introduction to the form, explaining the purpose and expectations.
* **what_is_your_role** (Select One, Optional): This question asks about your role or position in the educational institution.
	+ **role1**: This option is for teachers or instructors with a specific role or title.
	+ **role2**: This option is for administrators or staff with a specific role or title.
	+ **role3**: This option is for other roles or titles that are not specified.
* **what_is_your_level** (Select Multiple, Optional): This question asks about your level of expertise or experience.
	+ **Option 1**: This option is for beginner or entry-level teachers.
	+ **Option 2**: This option is for intermediate or experienced teachers.
	+ **Option 3**: This option is for advanced or expert-level teachers.
* **what_do_you_like** (Text, Optional): This field is for you to provide your favorite or preferred topics or subjects to teach.
* **what_do_you_dislike** (Text, Optional): This field is for you to provide any topics or subjects you are not comfortable teaching.
* **comments** (Note, Optional): This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you have.
* **email** (Email, Optional): This field is for your contact information, including your email address.
* **phone** (Text, Optional): This field is for your contact information, including your phone number.
* **submission** (Text, Optional): This field is for any additional submission or comments.
