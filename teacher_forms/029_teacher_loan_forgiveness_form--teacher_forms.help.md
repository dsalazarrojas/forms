# Teacher Loan Forgiveness Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Loan Forgiveness Form is designed to collect information about a teacher's loan forgiveness program. This form is used to process loan forgiveness applications and ensure that teachers with high loan balances can get their loans forgiven.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form accurately and effectively, follow these steps:

1.  Read the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and requirements.
2.  Complete each field according to the provided instructions.
3.  Review your answers for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Loan Forgiveness Program Application** (`student_loan_forgiveness_program_application`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter a brief description of the loan forgiveness program you are applying for. Please provide a clear and concise explanation of the program you are applying for.
*   **Teacher Loan Amount Borrowed** (`teacher_loan_amount_borrowed`, number, optional): This field is used to enter the total amount of money you borrowed for your teacher loan. Please enter the exact amount you borrowed.
*   **Loan Forgiveness Amount** (`loan_forgiveness_amount`, number, optional): This field is used to enter the amount of money you are applying for loan forgiveness. Please enter the exact amount you are applying for.
*   **Teacher Loan Interest Rate** (`teacher_loan_interest_rate`, number, optional): This field is used to enter the interest rate of your teacher loan. Please enter the exact interest rate.
*   **Teacher Loan Forgiveness Program** (`teacher_loan_forgiveness_program`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options provided:
    *   Public Service Loan Forgiveness (PSLF) Program
    *   Teacher Loan Forgiveness (TLF) Program
    *   Other (please specify)
*   **Date Loan Forgiveness Granted** (`date_loan_forgiveness_granted`, date, optional): This field is used to enter the date when your loan forgiveness was granted. Please enter the date in MM/DD/YYYY format.

## Tips

*   Be clear and concise in your answers.
*   Double-check your answers for accuracy.
*   If you're unsure about any field, contact your school administrator or HR department for assistance.
