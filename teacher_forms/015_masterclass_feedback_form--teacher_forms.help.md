# Masterclass Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Masterclass Feedback Form is a tool for students to share their thoughts, opinions, and experiences about their Masterclass. It helps us understand what you liked, what you didn't like, and what we can do better in future Masterclasses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name and feedback about their Masterclass experience on the first page.
2. On the second page, please enter the course name and any additional comments about the Masterclass.
3. On the third page, you can add any additional comments or suggestions.
4. Click submit to send your feedback to the instructor and program managers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter the student's name as it appears on their ID or in your records.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required): Share your thoughts and opinions about the Masterclass. What did you like or dislike about the experience? What did you learn or achieve during the Masterclass?
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): The date when the Masterclass took place.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): The time when the Masterclass occurred.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, `text`, required): The name of the Masterclass instructor.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, `email`, optional): The email address of the Masterclass instructor.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, `text`, optional): The phone number of the Masterclass instructor.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, `email`, optional): Your email address (not required for completion).
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, `text`, optional): Your phone number (not required for completion).
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, `text`, required): The name of the Masterclass course.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Any other thoughts or suggestions you have about the Masterclass.

## Tips
* Please be honest and sincere in your feedback. Your input is valuable to us in making future Masterclasses better.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improving the Masterclass, please share them with us.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to the instructor or program managers.
