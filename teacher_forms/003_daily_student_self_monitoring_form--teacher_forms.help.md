# Daily Student Self Monitoring Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Daily Student Self Monitoring Form is used by teachers to track student progress, behavior, and performance on a daily basis.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's ID number in the "Student ID" field. This is a required field.
2. Enter the teacher's ID number in the "Teacher ID" field. This is a required field.
3. Enter the date in the "Date" field. This is a required field.
4. Enter the subject being monitored in the "Subject" field. This is a required field.
5. Enter any relevant notes about the student's progress in the "Notes" field. This field is optional.
6. Enter any comments or feedback for the student in the "Comments" field. This field is optional.
7. Rate the student's behavior on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest, in the "Student Behavior Rating" field. This is a required field.
8. Rate the student's academic performance on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest, in the "Student Academic Rating" field. This is a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID (1)**: Enter the student's unique identifier. This is a required field.
* **Teacher ID (2)**: Enter the teacher's unique identifier. This is a required field.
* **Date (3)**: Enter the date in a format that can be recognized by your computer (e.g. mm/dd/yyyy). This is a required field.
* **Subject (4)**: Enter the subject being monitored. This is a required field.
* **Notes (5)**: Enter any relevant notes or comments about the student's progress. This field is optional.
* **Comments (6)**: Enter any comments or feedback for the student. This field is optional.
* **Student Behavior Rating (7)**: Rate the student's behavior on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest. This is a required field.
* **Student Academic Rating (8)**: Rate the student's academic performance on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest. This is a required field.
