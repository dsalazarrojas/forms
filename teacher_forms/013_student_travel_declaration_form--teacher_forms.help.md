# Student Travel Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Travel Declaration Form is used to document a student's travel history for academic purposes. It helps the university to keep track of students' movements and ensure that their absence is accounted for.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the travel history section with any previous travel details.
2. Provide the travel destination and type of travel.
3. Enter the travel dates, including the start and end dates.
4. Specify the method of travel used.
5. Give a reason for the travel.
6. Enter the contact details of a person to contact during the trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel History**: Describe any previous travel history, including dates and destinations.
* **Travel Destination**: Select the country or state you are traveling to. This is required for internal tracking purposes.
* **Travel Dates**: Enter the start and end dates of your trip. This will help us to schedule your absences and ensure that you are not missing important university events.
* **Travel Type**: Specify the type of travel, which can be International, Domestic, Out of Country, or Within Country.
* **Travel Method**: Select how you will be traveling (Airplane, Car, Train, Bus, or Boat).
* **Travel Reason**: Provide a brief reason for your travel, such as attending a conference or visiting a family member.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of a person that can be reached during the trip.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the person that can be reached during the trip.

Note: This form is for academic travel only, please make sure you are filling it for a legitimate reason.
