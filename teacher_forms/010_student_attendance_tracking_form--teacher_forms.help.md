# Student Attendance Tracking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Attendance Tracking Form is a tool used by teachers to record student attendance for a lesson or class.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the student's ID number.
2. Select the date of the lesson that the student attended.
3. Enter the time that the lesson started.
4. Enter the student's name as it appears on the class register.
5. Indicate the attendance status of the student (e.g., "present", "absent", "unexcused absence", etc.).
6. Enter any reason for the student's absence (if applicable).
7. Enter the teacher's name (if applicable).
8. Enter the total number of students present.
9. Enter the number of students present.
10. Enter the total number of students in the class.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID** (1, `number`, required): Enter the student's unique ID number as it appears on the class register.
* **Date** (2, `date`, required): Select the date of the lesson that the student attended.
* **Time In** (3, `time`, required): Enter the time that the lesson started.
* **Student Name** (4, `text`, required): Enter the student's full name as it appears on the class register.
* **Attendance Status** (5, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate the attendance status of the student (e.g., "present", "absent", etc.).
* **Reason** (6, `text`, not required): Enter any reason for the student's absence (if applicable).
* **Teacher Name** (7, `text`, not required): Enter your name as the teacher of the lesson.
* **Student Count** (8, `number`, required): Enter the total number of students in the lesson.
* **Student Attendance Count** (9, `number`, required): Enter the number of students present.
* **Total Count** (10, `number`, required): Enter the total number of students in the class.
