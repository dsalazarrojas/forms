# Adult Learning Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adult Learning Plan Form is a tool for teachers to create a comprehensive learning plan for their adult students. This form collects information about the student's learner profile, learning goals, and other relevant details to inform instruction and planning.
## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, fill in the **Learner Profile** section with a brief description of the student's background and characteristics.
2. In the **Learner Name** section, enter the student's legal name.
3. Describe the **Learning Goals** section by providing a brief overview of the student's short and long-term learning objectives.
4. In the **Prior Education or Certifications** section, list any relevant credentials the student may have.
5. Select the student's **Preferred Learning Style** by choosing from the options provided.
6. Estimate the number of **Available Study Time Per Week** hours in the space provided.
7. If applicable, describe any **Support or Accommodations Needed** in this section.
8. If desired, select a **Target Completion Date** for the student's learning goals.
9. Use the **Instructor Notes and Recommendations** section for any additional comments or recommendations from the teacher.
10. Finally, sign and date the form with the **Agreement Date**.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Learner Profile**: A brief description of the student's background and characteristics.
* **Learner Name**: Enter the student's legal name.
* **Learning Goals**: Describe the student's short and long-term learning objectives.
* **Prior Education or Certifications**: List any relevant credentials the student may have.
* **Preferred Learning Style**: Choose from the options provided: Visual, Auditory, Kinesthetic, or Blended.
* **Available Study Time Per Week**: Estimate the number of hours the student has available to study per week.
* **Support or Accommodations Needed**: Describe any accommodations the student may require to succeed in the learning process.
* **Target Completion Date**: Select a date by which the student hopes to complete their learning goals.
* **Instructor Notes and Recommendations**: Use this section for any additional comments or recommendations from the teacher.
* **Agreement Date**: Sign and date the form to confirm the plan's completion.
