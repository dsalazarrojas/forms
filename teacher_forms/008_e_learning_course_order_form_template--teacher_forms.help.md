# E Learning Course Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a template for customers to order e-learning courses. It is used to gather necessary information for course registration, payment, and submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the course name in the "Course Name" field.
2. Enter the course price in the "Course Price" field.
3. Provide a brief description of the course in the "Description" field (optional).
4. Enter your first name, last name, and email address in the corresponding fields.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Select your preferred payment method from the dropdown menu in the "Payment Method" field.
7. Enter the payment amount in the "Payment Amount" field.
8. Choose the date you would like to submit the course order in the "Submit Date" field.
9. Select who submitted the order, either "Instructor" or "Admin", in the "Submitted By" field.
10. If required, confirm your signature in the "Signature Confirmation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the course you are interested in.
* **Course Price** (`course_price`, number, required): Enter the price of the course.
* **Description** (`course_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the course (if required).
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method, either PayPal or Credit Card.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the payment amount.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, required): Choose the date you would like to submit the course order.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_multiple, required): Select who submitted the order, either "Instructor" or "Admin".
* **Signature Confirmation** (`signature_confirmation`, note, optional): If required, confirm your signature.
Note: The "Signature Confirmation" field is not required for most users, but is used in specific scenarios where a signature is necessary.
