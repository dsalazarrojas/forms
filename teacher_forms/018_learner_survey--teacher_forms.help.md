# Learner Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and suggestions from learners on the effectiveness and areas of improvement of a training or educational program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page of the form carefully to ensure you are answering the correct questions.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and provide as much detail as possible.
3. Select all applicable options for each question.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the text fields to provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Learner Survey Page 1** (`learner_survey_page_1`, text, not required): Please enter any additional comments or feedback on the program.
* **Learners Answers** (`learners_answers`, select_multiple, required): Select all options that apply to your experience with the program.
* **Learners Comments** (`learners_comments`, text, not required): Please provide any additional comments or feedback on the program.
* **Learners Suggestions** (`learners_suggestions`, select_one, not required): Suggest ways to improve the program.
* **Learner Survey Date** (`learner_survey_date`, date, not required): Enter the date you completed the program.
* **Learner Survey Time** (`learner_survey_time`, time, not required): Enter the time you completed the program.
* **Learners Email** (`learners_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Learners Phone** (`learners_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Learners Notes** (`learners_notes`, note, not required): Please provide any additional notes or comments.
* **Learners Feedback** (`learners_feedback`, select_one, required): Provide feedback on your overall experience with the program.
* **Learners Agreement** (`learners_agreement`, select_one, required): Please indicate whether you agree or disagree with the program's terms and conditions.
* **Survey Instructions** (`survey_instructions`, text, not required): Review the program's terms and conditions and provide any additional comments.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, not required): Review the program's terms and conditions.
* **Survey Conclusion** (`survey_conclusion`, text, not required): Please provide any additional comments or feedback on the survey.
* **Survey Thank You** (`survey_thank_you`, text, not required): A confirmation message to show appreciation for your feedback.
* **Survey Disclaimer** (`survey_disclaimer`, text, not required): Please review the survey's disclaimer.
* **Learner Survey Page 23** (`learner_survey_page_23`, text, not required): (Unused field)
* **Learner Survey Page 24** (`learner_survey_page_24`, text, not required): (Unused field)
* **Learner Survey Page 25** (`learner_survey_page_25`, text, not required): (Unused field)

Please note that some of the fields are not required and are intended for optional feedback or information. Ensure you review each field carefully and provide the requested information to help us improve the program.
