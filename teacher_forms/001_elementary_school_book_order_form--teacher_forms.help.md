# Elementary School Book Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for elementary school staff to place orders for books. It gathers essential information about the school and its staff to facilitate accurate ordering and communication.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your school's name in the "School Name" field.
2. Provide your name as a teacher or staff member in the "Teacher Name" field.
3. Select the grade level(s) relevant to your order.
4. Enter the number of students for your class or department.
5. Enter your school's contact email address.
6. Enter your school's phone number.
7. Provide the school's address.
8. Enter the city where your school is located.
9. Enter the state where your school is located.
10. Enter the zip code of your school's address.
11. Select the date for your order.
12. Enter your personal phone number.
13. Enter your personal email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (text, required): Enter your school's name to identify it accurately.
* **Teacher Name** (text, required): Enter your name as a teacher or staff member placing this order.
* **Grade Level** (select_one, required): Select the grade level(s) relevant to your order (High or Low).
* **Number of Students** (number, required): Enter the number of students for your class or department.
* **School Email** (email, required): Enter your school's contact email address.
* **School Phone** (text, optional): Provide your school's phone number for contact.
* **Address** (text, optional): Enter your school's address to ensure accurate delivery of materials.
* **City** (text, required): Enter the city where your school is located.
* **State** (text, required): Enter the state where your school is located.
* **Zip Code** (number, required): Enter your school's zip code for efficient shipping.
* **Order Date** (date, required): Select the date for your order.
* **Teacher Phone** (text, optional): Enter your personal phone number for communication.
* **Teacher Email** (email, optional): Enter your personal email address for communication.
* **Grade Level**: This field should be completed with the grade level(s) relevant to your order.
* **School Phone**: This field should be completed with your school's phone number.

Note: All fields are marked with their type (text, select_one, number, etc.) and whether they are required (yes/no).
