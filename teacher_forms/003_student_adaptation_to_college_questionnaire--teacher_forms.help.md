# Student Adaptation To College Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Adaptation To College Questionnaire is a tool used by teachers and counselors to assess a student's level of adaptation to college life. It helps to identify areas where students may need extra support and provides a basis for developing strategies to aid students in their transition to higher education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have authority to access the form, as it's intended for authorized personnel only.
2. Choose the relevant student information by selecting the student ID and entering their name.
3. Select the school year the student is currently in.
4. Evaluate each of the student's attributes:
	* Teacher name and rating (optional)
	* Adaptation level (select one or multiple)
	* School programs (select one or multiple)
	* Comments sections for teacher, parent, and student
	* Other attributes such as teacher support, involvement, participation, and adaptation to college

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student ID** (1): Enter the student's ID number to ensure that you are evaluating the correct student.
* **Student Name** (2): Enter the student's name to associate it with their ID.
* **Teacher Name** (3): Enter the name of the student's teacher, if applicable.
* **Student Grade** (4): Select the student's current grade level.
* **School Year** (5): Select the student's current school year.
* **Teacher Rating** (6): Rate the student's academic performance.
* **Adaptation Level** (7): Select one or multiple levels of the student's adaptation:
	+ None
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High
	+ Very High
* **School Program** (8): Select one or multiple school programs the student is in.
* **Teacher Comments** (9): Provide additional comments about the student's strengths and challenges.
* **Parent Comments** (10): Provide comments from the student's parent or guardian.
* **Student Comments** (11): Provide comments from the student themselves.
* **Adaptation Level** (12): Enter a brief text description of the student's adaptation level.
* **Teacher Support** (13): Rate the level of support the teacher provides to the student.
* **Teacher Involvement** (14): Rate the teacher's level of involvement with the student:
	+ None
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High
	+ Very High
* **Student Participation** (15): Rate the student's level of participation:
	+ None
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High
	+ Very High
* **Teacher Adaptation** (16): Select one or multiple levels of the teacher's adaptation:
	+ None
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High
	+ Very High
* **Adaptation to College** (17): Select one or multiple levels of the student's adaptation to college:
	+ None
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High
	+ Very High
* **Teacher Adaptation to College Comments** (18): Provide comments about the student's ability to adapt to college.
* **Adaptation to College** (19): Rate the student's ability to adapt to college.
* **School Program Comments** (20): Provide comments about the student's school program.

Note: The teacher and student comments sections are for providing additional context and insights about the student. The other fields are for rating or selecting specific attributes. Ensure to provide clear and concise answers that are relevant to the student's situation.
