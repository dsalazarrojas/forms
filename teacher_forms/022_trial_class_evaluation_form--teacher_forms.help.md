# Trial Class Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback and evaluation from teachers and students about their experience with the trial class. The form aims to collect information on the effectiveness of the trial class, overall satisfaction, communication, equipment, facility, and overall experience. This feedback will help us to improve and tailor our services to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields, marked as **Required**.
2. Provide your email address and phone number as **Email** and **Phone** fields are optional, but can be filled in if you would like us to contact you for follow-up questions or to provide additional information.
3. Use the **Comments** field to add any additional information or suggestions you'd like to share.
4. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher**: <em>Required</em> - This field is for the name of the teacher who conducted the trial class.
* **Trial Class**: <em>Required</em> - This field is for the name of the trial class you attended.
* **Evaluation Date**: <em>Required</em> - This field is for the date you evaluated the trial class.
* **Trial Class Effectiveness**: <em>Required</em> - Rate how effective you found the trial class to be, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Teacher Suggestion**: <em>Optional</em> - Share any suggestions you have for the teacher, if any.
* **Student Suggestion**: <em>Optional</em> - Share any suggestions you have for the students, if any.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: <em>Required</em> - Rate how satisfied you are with the trial class, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Will Attend**: <em>Required</em> - Indicate if you will attend another trial class in the future.
* **Will Recommend**: <em>Required</em> - Indicate if you will recommend the trial class to others.
* **Comments**: <em>Optional</em> - Add any additional comments or suggestions you have.
* **Email**: <em>Optional</em> - Provide your email address if you would like us to contact you for follow-up questions or to provide additional information.
* **Phone**: <em>Optional</em> - Provide your phone number if you would like us to contact you for follow-up questions or to provide additional information.
* **Teacher Comments**: <em>Optional</em> - Add any comments or feedback you have about the teacher.
* **Student Comments**: <em>Optional</em> - Add any comments or feedback you have about the students.
* **Follow-up Contact**: <em>Optional</em> - Provide your email address if you would like us to contact you for follow-up questions or to provide additional information.
* **Trial Class Details**: <em>Optional</em> - Add any additional details about the trial class you would like to share.
* **Teacher Satisfaction**: <em>Required</em> - Rate how satisfied you are with the teacher, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Student Satisfaction**: <em>Required</em> - Rate how satisfied you are with the students, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Communication**: <em>Required</em> - Indicate if you had good communication with the teacher or students during the trial class.
* **Equipment**: <em>Required</em> - Indicate if the trial class had sufficient equipment.
* **Facility**: <em>Required</em> - Indicate if the trial class took place in a suitable facility.
* **Overall Experience**: <em>Required</em> - Rate the overall experience of the trial class, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Will Rebook**: <em>Required</em> - Indicate if you will rebook another trial class in the future.
