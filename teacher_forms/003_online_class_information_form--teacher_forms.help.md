# Online Class Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for teachers to input details about their upcoming online classes. By filling out this form, you can provide students with the necessary information to get the most out of their online learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Online Class Information Form, follow these steps:

1. Select a course from the options provided.
2. Enter the start date of the course.
3. Enter the end date of the course.
4. Specify the duration of the course in hours.
5. Enter the name and title of the course instructor.
6. Enter any additional details about the course location.
7. Provide any additional course details.
8. Add any comments or notes about the course.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select A Course** (`course_selection`, select_multiple, required: false): This field asks you to select one or more courses from the provided options. Select all relevant courses to provide students with comprehensive information.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of the course, ensuring students know when the class begins.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of the course, so students are aware of the class duration.
* **Course Duration** (`course_duration`, number, required: false): Specify the length of the course in hours, helping students plan their schedule.
* **Course Instructor** (`course_instructor`, text, required: false): Enter the name and title of the course instructor, providing students with contact information.
* **Course Location** (`course_location`, text, required: false): Enter any additional details about the course location, such as the URL or physical address.
* **Course Details** (`course_details`, text, required: false): Provide any additional course details, like prerequisites or materials needed.
* **Comments** (`form_comments`, text, required: false): Add any comments or notes about the course, helping students understand its context.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields carefully.
* Use the provided options for select fields whenever possible.
* Double-check the start and end dates for accuracy.
* Be concise and clear with course details and instructor information.
* Use this guide to ensure a smooth and accurate filling-out process.
