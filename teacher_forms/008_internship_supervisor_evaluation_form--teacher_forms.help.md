# internship_supervisor_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The internship supervisor evaluation form is a tool used by supervisors to evaluate the performance of students during their internship. It allows supervisors to provide feedback on the student's work, skills, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing your expert opinion on the student's performance.
*   Make sure to sign the supervisor signature field to indicate that you have evaluated the student.
*   If you're unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to the relevant department or authority for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor name`, `text`, required: false): This field is for your name, please fill it out accurately.
*   **Student ID** (`student id`, `number`, required: false): This field is for the student's ID number, please fill it out accurately.
*   **Date of Submission** (`date of submission`, `date`, required: false): This field is for the date you're submitting the evaluation, please fill it out accurately.
*   **Date of Employment** (`date of employment`, `date`, required: false): This field is for the duration of the internship, please fill it out accurately.
*   **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation date`, `date`, required: false): This field is for the date of your evaluation, please fill it out accurately.
*   **Evaluation Period** (`evaluation period`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the options: Excellent, Satisfactory, or Needs Improvement.
*   **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation comments`, `text`, required: false): This field is for you to provide additional comments about the student's performance.
*   **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor signature`, `text`, required: false): This field is for your digital signature, please fill it out accurately.
