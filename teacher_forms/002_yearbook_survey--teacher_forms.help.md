# Yearbook Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students and teachers to create a yearbook for the school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your student ID, name, student number, email, and phone number.
2. Select your grade level from the dropdown list.
3. Select any clubs and activities you are involved in.
4. Provide any additional comments about your year.
5. If you are a teacher, fill out the teacher contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, required/optional: required): Enter your student ID number.
* **Name** (`name`, required/optional: required): Enter your name as it should appear in the yearbook.
* **Student Number** (`student_number`, required/optional: required): Enter your student number.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, required/optional: required): Enter your email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, required/optional: required): Enter your phone number.
* **Grade** (`student_grade`, required/optional: required): Select your current grade level from the dropdown list.
* **Subjects** (`student_subjects`, optional/optional: false): Select any subjects you are currently taking.
* **Clubs and Activities** (`student_clubs`, optional/optional: false): Select any clubs and activities you are involved in.
* **Comments** (`student_comments`, optional/optional: false): Provide any additional comments about your year.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, optional/optional: false): If you are a parent, enter your name.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, optional/optional: false): If you are a parent, enter your email address.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, optional/optional: false): If you are a parent, enter your phone number.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, optional/optional: false): If you are a teacher, enter your name.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, optional/optional: false): If you are a teacher, enter your email address.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, optional/optional: false): If you are a teacher, enter your phone number.
