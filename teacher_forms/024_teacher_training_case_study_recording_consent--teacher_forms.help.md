# Teacher Training Case Study Recording Consent - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a teacher's consent to record a training case study. The form is intended to gather details such as the form template name, provider name, date and time of the recorded session, session duration, and other relevant information that helps in understanding the case study recording consent.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Form Template Name** field with the name of the form template used for the teacher training case study recording.
2. Provide the name of the **Provider** who is offering the teacher training.
3. Enter the **Recorded Session Date** in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` (year-month-day).
4. Enter the **Recorded Session Time** in the format `HH:MM:SS` (hour-minute-second).
5. Select the **Recorded Session Duration** in the format of a numerical value.
6. Indicate whether **Consent Provided** was given by selecting 'True' or 'False'.
7. Add any additional **Notes** about the recording consent in the text area.
8. Enter the **Email** address of the teacher or provider.
9. Enter the **Phone** number of the teacher or provider.
10. Select the **Category** of the recording consent (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Template Name** (`teacher_training_case_study_recording_consent_form_template_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the form template used for the recording case study.
* **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the provider offering the teacher training.
* **Recorded Session Date** (`recorded_session_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the recorded session in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Recorded Session Time** (`recorded_session_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the recorded session in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
* **Recorded Session Duration** (`recorded_session_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the recorded session.
* **Consent Provided** (`consent_provided`, select_one, required): Select 'True' if consent was provided for recording, 'False' otherwise.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the recording consent.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the teacher or provider.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the teacher or provider.
* **Category** (`category`, text, optional): Select the category of the recording consent if required.
