# Teacher Empowerment Grant Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to support and empower teachers by providing a grant to help them in their professional development and teaching practices.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your school ID number.
2. Enter your teacher ID number.
3. Enter your name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., your passport).
4. Enter your email address.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Select the grade level you teach (elementary, middle school, or high school).
7. Enter your school's name.
8. Enter your school's address.
9. Enter your city.
10. Enter your state.
11. Enter your zip code.
12. If you teach outside the standard grade levels, please provide additional information in the "Other (if applicable)" field.
13. Enter your school district ID (if applicable).
14. Enter your school county ID (if applicable).
15. Enter your school city ID (if applicable).
16. Enter your name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., your passport).
17. Enter your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **school_id** (school id): Enter your school ID number.
* **teacher_id** (Teacher ID): Enter your teacher ID number.
* **name** (Name): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., your passport).
* **email** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (Phone): Enter your phone number.
* **grade_level** (Grade Level): Select the grade level you teach (elementary, middle school, or high school).
* **school_name** (School Name): Enter your school's name.
* **school_address** (School Address): Enter your school's address.
* **city** (City): Enter your city.
* **state** (State): Enter your state.
* **zip** (Zip): Enter your zip code.
* **grade_level_other** (Other (if applicable)): If you teach outside the standard grade levels, please provide additional information here.
* **school_district_id** (School District ID): Enter your school district ID (if applicable).
* **school_county_id** (School County ID): Enter your school county ID (if applicable).
* **school_city_id** (School City ID): Enter your school city ID (if applicable).
* **teacher_name** (Teacher Name): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., your passport).
* **teacher_email** (Teacher Email): Enter your email address.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure a successful submission.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We will review your submission and contact you with further instructions.
