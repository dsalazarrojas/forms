# Remote Learning Device Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for schools to submit requests for remote learning devices. It will be reviewed and processed by the administration to ensure timely allocation of devices to meet the needs of students and teachers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Enter the school name in the "School Name" field.
2. Select the grade level that the device will be used for in the "Grade Level" field.
3. Enter the number of devices needed in the "Number Of Students" field.
4. Choose the type of device that the school requires from the "Device Type" options.
5. Describe the reason for the device request in the "Device Request Reason" field.
6. Provide the name of the user who will be using the device in the "Assigned User" field.
7. Enter the school's email address in the "School Email" field.
8. Enter the school's phone number in the "School Phone" field.
9. Add any additional comments about the request in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of your school.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, optional): Select the grade level of the students who will be using the device. (Note: This field is optional, but it can help us better understand the purpose of the request.)
* **Number Of Students** (`number_of_students`, number, optional): Enter the number of devices needed for the students.
* **Device Type** (`device_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of device that your school requires (Laptop, Desktop, Tablet, or Smartphone).
* **Device Request Reason** (`device_request_reason`, text, optional): Explain the reason for your device request. This will help us understand the purpose of the request.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, optional): Provide the name of the user who will be using the device.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, required): Enter your school's email address.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, optional): Enter your school's phone number.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the request.

# Tips
Please ensure that you submit a complete and accurate request, as it will be reviewed and processed by the administration. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
