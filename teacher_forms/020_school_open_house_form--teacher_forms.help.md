# School Open House Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Open House Form is designed to gather information from teachers about the school open house event. This information will be used to facilitate communication and organization for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
3. Leave optional fields blank if not applicable.
4. Submit the form when you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`general_information`, text, optional): This field is for any general information about the event or your role in it. Please provide a brief description of your participation or involvement.
* **Parents Details** (`parents_details`, text, optional): If you have any relevant information about parents or guardians attending the event, you can provide it here.
* **Contact** (`contact`, text, optional): If you have any contact information or questions, you can include it in this field.
* **School Open House Details** (`school_open_house_details`, text, optional): This field is for any additional details about the event, such as its purpose or goals.
* **Teachers** (`teachers_attending`, text, optional): List any teachers who will be attending the event.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): If you have an assigned tool or resource for the event, you can provide its name or description.
