# parent_teacher_conference_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and document information for parent-teacher conferences, ensuring that both parents and teachers are on the same page about student progress and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the parent's name in the "Parent Name" field.
2. Provide the parent's contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
3. Enter the parent's email address in the "Parent Email" field.
4. Enter the teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
5. Select a conference date and time in the "Conference Date" and "Conference Time" fields.
6. Add any important notes about the conference in the "Notes" field.
7. Include any additional notes or comments from the parent or teacher in the "Additional Notes" field.
8. If the parent or teacher has any specific requests, enter them in the "Parent Request" and "Teacher Request" fields.
9. Specify the location of the conference in the "Conference Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the parent's name.
* **Contact Number** (text, required/optional): Enter the parent's contact number.
* **Parent Email** (email, required/optional): Enter the parent's email address.
* **Teacher Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the teacher's name.
* **Conference Date** (date, required/optional): Select a date for the parent-teacher conference.
* **Conference Time** (time, required/optional): Select the time of the parent-teacher conference.
* **Notes** (note, required/optional): Add any important notes about the conference.
* **Additional Notes** (note, required/optional): Include any additional comments or notes from the parent or teacher.
* **Parent Request** (text, required/optional): Enter any specific requests from the parent.
* **Teacher Request** (text, required/optional): Enter any specific requests from the teacher.
* **Conference Location** (text, required/optional): Specify the location of the conference.
* **Student Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the student's name.
