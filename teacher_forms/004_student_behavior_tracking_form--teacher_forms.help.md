# Student Behavior Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Behavior Tracking Form is a tool used to collect and track student behavior data in educational settings. This form helps monitor students' general behavior and specific hours spent on various activities, providing valuable insights for educators to make informed decisions.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and accurately.
3. Provide specific details for each field, as requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Behavior (student_behavior_tracking_1)**: Describe the student's general behavior, such as their attitude, engagement, or any notable incidents.
* **General Behavior (student_behavior_tracking_2)**: Another opportunity to provide additional information about the student's general behavior.
* **Specific Hours (specific_hours)**: Report the specific hours the student spends on various activities, such as homework, reading, or extracurricular activities.
* **Student Behaviors (student_behavior_tracking_4)**: Select all applicable options that describe the student's behavior, such as 'Yes' for positive behaviors or 'No' for negative behaviors.
* **Teacher Comments (teacher_comments)**: Share any observations, notes, or concerns about the student's behavior, providing additional context and insights.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- Provide specific examples and details to support your answers.
- If you are unsure about any field, ask your educator for guidance.
- Review the form carefully to ensure you have completed it accurately and thoroughly.
