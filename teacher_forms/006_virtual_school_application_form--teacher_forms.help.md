# Virtual School Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students who are interested in applying to a virtual school. The form is divided into different sections, each with specific fields that require completion. Please read through the form carefully to ensure you provide accurate and truthful information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name in the **name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **studentemail** field.
3. If you have any contact details for your parent/guardian, please provide them in the **contact_details** field.
4. In the **educationalbackground** field, select the option that best describes your educational background.
5. Select all programs of interest in the **programofinterest** field.
6. Please provide any additional comments or information about yourself in the **comments** field, if necessary.
7. In the **agreedterms** field, select the option that indicates your agreement to the terms and conditions.
8. Finally, sign your name in the **signature** field to confirm your identity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **name** (Text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **studentemail** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address that you regularly check.
- **contact_details** (Text, Optional): If you have any contact details for your parent/guardian, please provide them here.
- **educationalbackground** (Select One, Required): Choose an option that best describes your educational background.
  • Option 1: High School Diploma
  • Option 2: College Degree
  • Option 3: Other (please specify)
- **programofinterest** (Select Multiple, Required): Select all programs of interest to you. Multiple options can be selected.
  • Option 1: Option 1
  • Option 2: Option 2
  • Option 3: Option 3
  • Option 4: Option 4
- **comments** (Note, Optional): If you have any additional comments or information, please provide them here.
- **agreedterms** (Select One, Required): Choose an option that indicates your agreement to the terms and conditions.
  • Option 1: I have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
  • Option 2: I have not read the terms and conditions.
  • Option 3: Other (please specify)
- **signature** (Text, Required): Sign your name to confirm your identity.
