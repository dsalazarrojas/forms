# High School Teacher Maternity Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a maternity leave of absence as a high school teacher. It will collect information about your leave dates, reason for leave, and other relevant details to ensure that your request is processed fairly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete this form carefully and thoroughly.
- Enter your start date and end dates for your leave.
- Provide the reason for your leave, including any relevant medical information.
- If you have insurance coverage, please provide the necessary information.
- If you have a doctor's note or contact information, please include it in the relevant fields.
- Finally, review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Maternity Request** (`teacher_maternity_request`, text, optional): This is the main section where you can enter your request for maternity leave. Be as specific as possible about your reasons for requesting leave.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to start your maternity leave. This is a crucial field, as it will help administrators determine when your leave will take effect.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to return to work after your maternity leave. This field is also crucial for scheduling and planning purposes.
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, required): This field is for providing a clear and concise explanation of why you are requesting maternity leave. Please be sure to include any relevant medical information.
* **Doctor's Note** (`doctor_note`, note, optional): If you have a doctor's note or other medical documentation, please attach it here.
* **Doctor's Name** (`doctor_name`, text, optional): If you have a doctor's name and contact information, please enter it here.
* **Doctor's Phone** (`doctor_phone`, text, optional): Enter your doctor's phone number, if applicable.
* **Doctor's Email** (`doctor_email`, email, optional): Enter your doctor's email address, if applicable.
* **Insurance Information** (`insurance_info`, text, required): Please provide your insurance information, including any relevant policy numbers or provider details.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, required): Identify your insurance provider.
* **Doctor Phone Call** (`doctor_phone_call`, text, optional): If you have a doctor's phone number, you can enter it here.
* **Doctor Email Call** (`doctor_email_call`, email, optional): If you have a doctor's email address, you can enter it here.
* **Leave Status** (`leave_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your leave from the options provided (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): This is an optional field for any additional comments or information you may want to provide.
* **School Admin Notes** (`school_admin_notes`, text, required): Please provide any additional comments or information that would be helpful for school administrators to know.
* **Teacher Notes** (`teacher_notes`, text, required): Please enter any additional comments or information that would be helpful for school administrators to know.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your approval from the options provided (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
* **Reason for Denial** (`reason_for_denial`, text, optional): If your request is denied, please enter the reason why.
* **Teacher Signature** (`teacher_signature`, text, required): Please sign and date your form to confirm that you have completed it accurately.
* **School Signature** (`school_signature`, text, required): Please sign and date the form to acknowledge receipt and review of your request.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_multiple, required): Select the status of your submission from the options provided (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, select_multiple, required): Select the status of your review from the options provided (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
* **Date Reviewed** (`date_reviewed`, date, optional): If you have a specific date for review, enter it here.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): If you have a specific review date, enter it here.
* **School Admin Name** (`school_admin_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the school administrator who reviewed your form.

## Tips
- Be sure to review and proofread your form carefully before submitting it.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to your school's HR or administrative office.
