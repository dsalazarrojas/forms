# Student Sign In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Student Sign In Form is intended for students to sign in with their details for class attendance or enrollment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill in the following fields with your accurate information.
2.  Select your class from the Class dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Name (`student_name`, text)**: Enter your name.
*   **Email (`email`, email)**: Enter your email address.
*   **Student ID (`student_id`, number)**: Enter your student ID number.
*   **Class (`class_name`, select_multiple)**: Select your class from the menu.
*   **Date (`attendance_date`, date)**: Select the date of attendance or enrollment.
*   **Time (`time`, time)**: Select the time of attendance or enrollment.
*   **Note (`attendance_note`, text)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your attendance.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all required fields.
*   Select a valid class from the dropdown menu.
*   If you need to add any additional notes or comments, use the Note field.
