# teaching_session_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Teaching Session Feedback Form" is a tool used by students to provide feedback on a teaching session they have attended. The form is designed to gather information about the session, the student's experience, and their overall rating of the session.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields with the requested information, such as the session title, description, trainer name, date, and student's name, email, and phone number.
* Select the rating of the session from the dropdown menu.
* Provide feedback about the session in the "feedback" field.
* If you have additional comments, please provide them in the "additional comments" field.
* Submit the form to send the feedback to the trainer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Title** (`session_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the session you attended.
* **Session Description** (`session_description`, text, required): Briefly describe the session you attended.
* **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the trainer for the session.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the session you attended.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the session you attended.
* **Session Start Time** (`session_start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the session you attended.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, text, required): Briefly describe the session duration.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a student.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter your email address as a student.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number as a student.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Provide feedback about the session, including what you liked and what you didn't like.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, required): Select the rating of the session from the dropdown menu (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions for the trainer, please provide them here.
* **Additional Comments for the Student** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions for the student, please provide them here. 

Note: Fields with a "required" label are mandatory and must be filled in for the form to be submitted.
