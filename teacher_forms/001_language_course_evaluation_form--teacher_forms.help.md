# Language Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from students regarding their experience and evaluation of the language course. Please take a few minutes to answer the questions below to help us improve the course.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all the fields in the form as accurately as possible.
2. If a field is not applicable, please leave it blank.
3. If you have any comments or feedback, please provide them in the "comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Info**: Please enter your school information, such as the name and location of your school.
* **Evaluation Date**: Please select the date you attended the language course.
* **Teacher Rating**: On a scale from 1-5, rate the quality of your teacher's instruction.
* **Language Course**: Select the language course you attended (e.g., "Option 1: Language Course A", "Option 2: Language Course B", etc.).
* **Student Feedback**: Please provide any comments or feedback about your experience with the language course.
* **Teacher Feedback**: Please provide any comments or feedback about your teacher's performance.
* **Overall Experience**: On a scale from 1-5, rate your overall experience with the language course.
* **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them here.
* **Teacher Name**: If you are comfortable doing so, please provide the name of your teacher.
* **Department**: If you are comfortable doing so, please provide the name of your school department.

## Tips

* Please answer all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
* Be as specific and detailed as possible in your answers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
