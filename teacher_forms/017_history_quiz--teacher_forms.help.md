# History Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The History Quiz form is designed to evaluate a student's knowledge of historical events and figures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin with the introduction page, where you will have the opportunity to briefly describe your background and context.
2. On the time period page, select the period in which the event or figure you are answering about occurred.
3. On the historical figure page, choose the figure you are discussing.
4. On the description page, provide a brief description of the event or figure.
5. On the correct answer page, select the correct answer to the given question.
6. On the additional comments page, you can provide any additional information that supports your answer.
7. On the correct answer date page, enter the date of the correct answer.
8. On the correct answer time page, enter the time of the correct answer.
9. On the correct answer email page, enter the email address of the person or organization that provided the correct answer.
10. On the correct answer phone page, enter the phone number of the person or organization that provided the correct answer.
11. On the teacher comments page, the teacher may provide feedback or comments on your work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, required: false): A brief description of your background and context for the quiz.
* **Time Period** (`time_period`, select_one, required: false): Choose the time period in which the event or figure you are answering about occurred.
	+ Options: Ancient, Medieval, Modern
* **Historical Figure** (`historical_figure`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the historical figure you are discussing.
	+ Options: George Washington, Abraham Lincoln, Napoleon, Alexander the Great, Cleopatra, Other
* **Description** (`event_description`, select_one, required: false): Select a brief description of the event or figure.
	+ Options: The American Revolution, The French Revolution, The Civil War, The Battle of Gettysburg, The Battle of Iwo Jima
* **Correct Answer** (`correct_answer`, select_one, required: false): Select the correct answer to the given question.
	+ Options: True, False
* **Reason** (`reason_answer`, text, required: false): Provide a brief explanation for your answer.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or information you would like to provide.
* **Correct Answer Date** (`correct_answer_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the correct answer.
* **Correct Answer Time** (`correct_answer_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the correct answer.
* **Correct Answer Email** (`correct_answer_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the person or organization that provided the correct answer.
* **Correct Answer Phone** (`correct_answer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person or organization that provided the correct answer.
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, text, required: false): The teacher may provide feedback or comments on your work.
