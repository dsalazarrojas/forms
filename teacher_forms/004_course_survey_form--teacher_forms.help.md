# course_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students about a course they have attended, aiming to gauge their satisfaction and provide a platform for instructors to improve their teaching.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the survey in the "Enter Survey Name" field.
2. Provide feedback on the course by selecting one of the three options: Positive, Negative, or Neutral.
3. Enter the name of the instructor who taught the course.
4. Rate your satisfaction with the course by entering a number between 1 and 5 (or selecting Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
5. Select the date and time you attended the course.
6. If you want to provide additional feedback or suggestions, use the "Comments" field.
7. You can also add your contact information, including email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Name** (`Enter Survey Name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the survey to identify it easily.
* **Course Feedback** (`How do you feel about this course?`, select_one, Required): Provide feedback on the course by selecting one of the three options: Positive, Negative, or Neutral.
* **Instructor Name** (`Enter Instructor Name`, text, Required): Enter the name of the instructor who taught the course.
* **Course Satisfaction** (`How satisfied do you feel about this course?`, number, Required): Rate your satisfaction with the course by entering a number between 1 and 5 (or selecting Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Course Satisfaction Score** (`Course Satisfaction`, select_multiple, Required): Select one or more of the options to describe your level of satisfaction (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Date Attended** (`Date Attended`, date, Required): Select the date you attended the course.
* **Time Attended** (`Time Attended`, time, Required): Select the time you attended the course.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, note, Optional): Provide additional feedback or suggestions about the course.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for further feedback.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for further feedback.
