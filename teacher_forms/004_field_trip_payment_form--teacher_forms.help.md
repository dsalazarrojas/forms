# Field Trip Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Field Trip Payment Form is a document used to collect payment information and other relevant details related to field trips. This form is designed to facilitate the process of managing and tracking payments for field trips.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the payment information in the **Payment Information** page, which includes fields such as **Payment Method**, **Payment Amount**, and **Payment Date**.
2. Select the details of the trip in the **Trip Details** page.
3. Provide the student's and teacher's contact information in the relevant fields.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments in the **Note** field.
5. Confirm the form's status in the **Form Status** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Information** (`payment_form`, text, not required): Enter the payment details, including the amount and method of payment.
* **Trip Details** (`trip_details`, select_one, not required): Select the details of the trip, such as the location and duration.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, not required): Select the method of payment, such as credit card or bank transfer.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, not required): Enter the amount of payment.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the school.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the student.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, not required): Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, not required): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of payment.
* **Payment Time** (`payment_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of payment.
* **Note** (`note`, note, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the teacher.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, not required): Enter the teacher's email address.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, text, not required): Enter the teacher's phone number.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, not required): Select the status of the payment, such as paid or pending.
* **Payment Status Note** (`payment_status_note`, text, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments about the payment status.
* **Payment Status Date** (`payment_status_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the payment status update.
* **Payment Method Note** (`payment_method_note`, text, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments about the payment method.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, text, not required): Enter the student's signature.
* **Teacher Signature** (`teacher_signature`, text, not required): Enter the teacher's signature.
* **School ID** (`school_id`, text, not required): Enter the school ID.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, not required): Enter the student ID.
* **Form Submitted** (`form_submitted`, select_one, not required): Select the status of the form, such as submitted or declined.
* **Form Completed** (`form_completed`, select_one, not required): Select the status of the form, such as completed or incomplete.
* **Form Status** (`form_status`, select_one, not required): Select the overall status of the form, such as approved or rejected.
