# Field Trip Approval Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request approval for field trips from school principals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your school principal's name and email address.
2. Enter the date of the trip (if applicable).
3. Provide a brief description of the trip (trip details).
4. Enter the trip dates.
5. Enter the number of students participating in the trip.
6. Enter the sponsor company and contact information (name, phone number, and email).
7. Enter your name and email address as the teacher leading the trip.
8. Select the current status of the school principal's approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Principal Name** (`school_principal_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school principal who will be approving this trip.
* **School Principal Email** (`school_principal_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the school principal who will be approving this trip.
* **Date of Trip** (`date_of_trip`, date, optional): Enter the date of the trip (if applicable).
* **Trip Details** (`trip_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the trip, including any relevant details.
* **Trip Dates** (`trip_dates`, date, required): Enter the dates of the trip.
* **Number of Students** (`student_count`, number, required): Enter the number of students participating in the trip.
* **Sponsor Company** (`sponsor_company`, text, required): Enter the company or organization sponsoring the trip.
* **Sponsor Contact** (`sponsor_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person for the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Phone** (`sponsor_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the sponsor contact.
* **Sponsor Email** (`sponsor_email`, text, optional): Enter the email address of the sponsor contact.
* **School Principal Approval Status** (`school_principal_approval_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the school principal's approval.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the teacher leading the trip.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, required): Enter your email address as the teacher leading the trip.
