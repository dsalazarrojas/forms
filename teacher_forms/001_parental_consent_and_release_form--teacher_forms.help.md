# Parental Consent And Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather necessary information from parents or guardians about their child's abilities, medical conditions, and emergency contacts. It's essential to complete this form accurately to ensure the child's well-being and safety while participating in activities or programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer each question carefully and truthfully.
2. Ensure you understand the purpose of each field before providing information.
3. Complete all required fields (indicated by a red asterisk *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parental Consent** (`parent_consent`, text, required): Please provide a brief explanation or statement of your consent as a parent or guardian. This is an opportunity to express your agreement or disagreement with the terms and conditions of the activity or program your child will be participating in.
* **Release Abilities** (`release_abilities`, text, optional): If your child has any abilities or disabilities that you would like to inform the educators or staff about, please describe them in this field. This helps the educators to provide suitable support and accommodations.
* **Medical Authorization** (`medical_authorization`, text, optional): If your child has any medical conditions or allergies that require special attention, please provide details in this field. This ensures that the educators are aware of any potential risks or precautions.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Please list the names and contact information of individuals who can be reached in case of an emergency.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
- If you're unsure about any field, consult the activity or program administrators.
- Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
- If you need to make changes, please contact the administrators for assistance.
