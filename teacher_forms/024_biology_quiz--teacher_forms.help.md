# Biology Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Biology Quiz is designed to test your knowledge of various biology concepts. The quiz is divided into four sections: Cell Biology, Genetics, Ecology, and Human Body Systems.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the section you want to complete first (Cell Biology, Genetics, Ecology, or Human Body Systems).
2. Read each question carefully and select the best answer from the options provided.
3. For multiple choice questions, select one answer from the options.
4. For short answer questions, write your answer in the text box.
5. If you are unsure of any question, skip it and move on to the next section.
6. When you have completed all the questions, click the "Submit" button to save your answers.
