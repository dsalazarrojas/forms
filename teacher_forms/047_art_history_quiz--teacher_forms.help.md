# Art History Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art History Quiz form is designed to collect data about art history knowledge from users. It contains questions about famous artists, art movements, and mediums, which will help researchers and educators better understand the knowledge base of art history students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Student Name** (Field 1) - A text field asking for the user's full name.
2. Provide your **Student ID** (Field 2) - An optional text field for users who want to identify themselves with an ID number. This is not mandatory.
3. Answer **Who painted the Mona Lisa** (Field 3) - A select_one field offering options for the most famous artist associated with the painting.
4. Choose the art movement associated with **Picasso** (Field 4) - A select_one field providing options for the art movement Picasso is associated with.
5. Select the decade when **Modern Art began** (Field 5) - A select_one field offering options for when modern art started.
6. Name a famous **Dutch Golden Age painter** (Field 6) - An open-response text field asking for the name of a painter from the Dutch Golden Age.
7. Identify the medium used for **Fresco** (Field 7) - A select_one field offering options for the medium used for fresco.
8. (Optional) Enter a **Score** (Field 8) - A number field for teachers to record scores.
9. Provide **Feedback** (Field 9) - A text field for teachers to offer feedback and comments.
10. (Optional) Record the **Completed Date** (Field 10) - A date field for tracking when the quiz was completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (Field 1): Enter your full name as you want to be identified.
* **Student ID** (Field 2): Provide an ID number if you have one; not required.
* **Who painted the Mona Lisa** (Field 3): Choose the artist you think painted the famous painting.
* **What art movement is Picasso associated with** (Field 4): Select the art movement Picasso is best known for.
* **Which decade is considered modern art** (Field 5): Choose the decade when modern art began.
* **Name a famous Dutch Golden Age painter** (Field 6): Type the name of a famous painter from the Dutch Golden Age.
* **What medium is fresco** (Field 7): Select the medium used for fresco.
* **Score** (Field 8): (Optional) Enter your score; only for teachers.
* **Feedback** (Field 9): Leave comments or feedback for the teacher.
* **Completed Date** (Field 10): (Optional) Record the date the quiz was completed.
