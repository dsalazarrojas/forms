# Student Pre Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Pre Registration Form is designed to collect general information from students before the registration process. This form will help the administration to gather necessary details from students, prevent duplication of effort, and facilitate the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to enter your information in the fields marked as required.
3. Double-check your answers for any errors before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (Field 1)** (`general_info`, text, not required)
	+ This field is meant to capture any general information about the student that the administration may need to know.
* **Personal Details (Field 2)** (`personal_details`, text, not required)
	+ This field is used to collect personal details of the student.
* **Emergency Contact (Field 3)** (`emergency_contact`, text, not required)
	+ This field is for providing emergency contact information in case of emergency.
* **Contact Details (Field 4)** (`contact_details`, text, not required)
	+ This field is to provide your contact information for communication.
* **Student Info (Field 5)** (`student_info`, text, not required)
	+ This field is to provide information related to the student.
* **Parent Info (Field 6)** (`parent_info`, text, not required)
	+ This field is to provide information related to the parent or guardian.
* **Education (Field 7)** (`education`, text, not required)
	+ This field is used to capture education-related information.
* **Academic Info (Field 8)** (`academic_info`, text, not required)
	+ This field is to provide any other academic-related information.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Review your answers before submission for any errors.
- If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to the administration.
