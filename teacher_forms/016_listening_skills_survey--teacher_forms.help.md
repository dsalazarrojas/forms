# listening_skills_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The listening_skills_survey form is a tool to understand an individual's preferences and behaviors related to music and podcast listening. The data collected will help us create better experiences for users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Enter your first name in the **First name** field.
2. Provide your email address in the **Email** field.
3. Choose who you most often listen to from the **Who do you listen to** field.
4. Select how often you listen to music from the **How often do you listen to music** field.
5. Choose how long you typically listen to music from the **How long do you listen to music** field.
6. Select the best time of day to listen to music from the **Best time of day to listen** field.
7. Choose your favorite music streaming channels from the **Music streaming service** field.
8. Select how often you use each music streaming service from the **Music streaming service** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Who do you listen to**: Choose who you most often listen to, such as a favorite podcast, news, music, or sports.
* **How often do you listen to music**: Select how often you listen to music, such as every time you have time, sometimes, rarely, or never.
* **How long do you listen to music**: Enter the length of time you typically listen to music in minutes.
* **Best time of day to listen**: Select the best time of day to listen to music, such as morning, afternoon, evening, or any time.
* **Most popular music channel**: Choose your favorite music streaming channel, such as Apple Music, Spotify, Amazon Music, or Tidal.
* **Music streaming service**: Select all the music streaming services you use, such as Apple Music, Spotify, prophets, or Tidal.
 
## Tips
* Please answer all questions truthfully to provide accurate data.
* Make sure to select all applicable options in the **Music streaming service** field.
* You can choose multiple options for the **Who do you listen to** and **Music streaming service** fields.
* You can only select one option for the **Who do you listen to** field if the required flag is set to true.
