# music_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your music preferences, interests, and demographic details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please start by filling out the required fields:
	* **Age**: Please enter your age in numerical format.
	* **Email**: Enter your email address.
	* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
	* **Location**: Enter your location.
2. For the music genre section, select all the genres that apply to you:
	* **Music Genre**: Select 'Yes' if you enjoy each genre:
		+ **Yes**
3. For the favorite artist section, enter your favorite artist(s):
	* **Favorite Artist**: Enter the name of your favorite artist.
4. For the music format section, select or enter your preferred format:
	* **Music Format**: Select 'Yes' if you prefer each format:
		+ **Yes**
	* **Music Format Other**: If you have a specific music format preference not listed, enter it here.
5. If you don't have a favorite song, you can skip the next sections. Otherwise:
	* **Favorite Song**: Enter the name of your favorite song.
	* **Favorite Album**: Enter the name of your favorite album.
	* **Favorite Band**: Enter the name of your favorite band.
6. If you have a favorite artist or music format that is not listed, you can provide more details:
	* **Favorite Artist Other**: Enter the name of your favorite artist.
	* **Music Format Other**: Enter the name of your preferred music format.
7. Finally, provide any additional comments about your music preferences:
	* **Music Survey Comments**: Enter any comments or suggestions about your music preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Age**: Your age in years.
* **Email**: Your email address.
* **Phone**: Your phone number.
* **Location**: Your city, state, or zip code.
* **Music Genre**: Select all music genres you enjoy.
* **Music Format**: Select all your preferred music formats.
* **Favorite Artist**: Your favorite artist or band.
* **Favorite Song**: Your favorite song.
* **Favorite Album**: Your favorite album.
* **Favorite Band**: Your favorite band.
* **Favorite Artist Other**: If you have a different favorite artist, enter it here.
* **Music Format Other**: If you have a different music format preference, enter it here.
* **Music Format Other Details**: Provide more details about your music format preference.
* **Music Survey Comments**: Any comments or suggestions about your music preferences.
