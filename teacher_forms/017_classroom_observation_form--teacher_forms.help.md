# Classroom Observation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help observers gather data from a specific classroom observation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the required fields with your name and the name of the teacher being observed, the observation date, start and end time of the observation, and the subject and grade level taught.
2. For each section, carefully read and answer the questions based on your observation.
3. Choose the response that best matches your observation.
4. For the "Comments and Recommendations" section, describe any positive aspects of the lesson and suggest areas for professional development.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Name (`teacher_name`, text, required):** The name of the teacher being observed.
* **Observer Name (`observer_name`, text, required):** The name of the person conducting the observation.
* **Observation Date (`observation_date`, date, required):** The date of the classroom observation.
* **Observation Start Time (`observation_time_start`, time, required):** The time the observation began.
* **Observation End Time (`observation_time_end`, time, required):** The time the observation ended.
* **Subject and Grade Level (`subject_grade`, text, required):** The subject and grade level being taught.
* **Number of Students Present (`number_students_present`, number, required):** The number of students present during the observation.
* **Lesson Objectives (`lesson_objectives`, select_one, required):** How well were the lesson objectives clearly stated.
	+ **Strongly Agree:** The teacher clearly stated the objectives and the students were well aware of them.
	+ **Agree:** The teacher mostly clearly stated the objectives, but not all students were well aware of them.
	+ **Neutral:** The teacher stated some objectives, but not all students were aware of them.
	+ **Disagree:** The teacher did not clearly state the objectives or did not inform students about them.
	+ **Strongly Disagree:** The teacher did not state the objectives, and it was clear that students were not informed.
* **Content Accuracy (`content_accuracy`, select_one, required):** How accurate and well-organized was the content delivered.
	+ **Strongly Agree:** The content was highly accurate and well-organized.
	+ **Agree:** The content was mostly accurate and well-organized.
	+ **Neutral:** The content was somewhat accurate, but not well-organized.
	+ **Disagree:** The content was not accurate or not well-organized.
	+ **Strongly Disagree:** The content was highly inaccurate or not well-organized at all.
* **Pacing of Instruction (`instructional_pace`, select_one, required):** Was the pacing of instruction appropriate.
	+ **Strongly Agree:** The pacing was just right, students were not rushed or bored.
	+ **Agree:** The pacing was mostly just right, some students were a bit rushed or bored.
	+ **Neutral:** The pacing was a bit slow or fast, but not significantly so.
	+ **Disagree:** The pacing was too slow or too fast.
	+ **Strongly Disagree:** The pacing was highly slow or fast.
* **Effective Use of Technology (`use_of_technology`, select_one, required):** Was the use of technology effective?
	+ **Strongly Agree:** The teacher effectively used technology to enhance the lesson.
	+ **Agree:** The teacher mostly used technology to enhance the lesson.
	+ **Neutral:** The teacher somewhat used technology, but not very effectively.
	+ **Disagree:** The teacher did not use technology effectively.
	+ **Strongly Disagree:** The teacher did not use technology at all.
* **Students Were Actively Engaged (`student_engagement`, select_one, required):** Were the students actively engaged in learning?
	+ **Strongly Agree:** The students were highly engaged and interested.
	+ **Agree:** The students were mostly engaged and interested.
	+ **Neutral:** The students were somewhat engaged, but not all the time.
	+ **Disagree:** The students were not very engaged.
	+ **Strongly Disagree:** The students were not engaged at all.
* **Teacher Encouraged Student Participation (`participation_encouragement`, select_one, required):** Did the teacher encourage student participation?
	+ **Strongly Agree:** The teacher encouraged participation very effectively.
	+ **Agree:** The teacher mostly encouraged participation.
	+ **Neutral:** The teacher somewhat encouraged participation.
	+ **Disagree:** The teacher did not encourage participation.
	+ **Strongly Disagree:** The teacher discouraged participation.
* **Teacher Responds Effectively to Student Questions (`student_question_response`, select_one, required):** Does the teacher respond effectively to student questions?
	+ **Strongly Agree:** The teacher responded very effectively to student questions.
	+ **Agree:** The teacher mostly responded effectively.
	+ **Neutral:** The teacher somewhat responded to student questions.
	+ **Disagree:** The teacher did not respond effectively to student questions.
	+ **Strongly Disagree:** The teacher did not respond to student questions at all.
* **Teacher Manages Student Behavior (`behavior_management`, select_one, required):** Did the teacher manage student behavior effectively?
	+ **Strongly Agree:** The teacher managed student behavior very effectively.
	+ **Agree:** The teacher mostly managed student behavior.
	+ **Neutral:** The teacher somewhat managed student behavior.
	+ **Disagree:** The teacher did not manage student behavior well.
	+ **Strongly Disagree:** The teacher did not manage student behavior at all.
* **Classroom Environment is Safe and Conducive to Learning (`classroom_environment`, select_one, required):** Is the classroom environment safe and conducive to learning?
	+ **Strongly Agree:** The classroom environment was highly safe and conducive.
	+ **Agree:** The classroom environment was mostly safe and conducive.
	+ **Neutral:** The classroom environment was somewhat safe, but not very conducive.
	+ **Disagree:** The classroom environment was not safe or not conducive.
	+ **Strongly Disagree:** The classroom environment was not safe at all.
* **Teacher Has Positive Relationships with Students (`positive_relationships`, select_one, required):** Does the teacher have positive relationships with students?
	+ **Strongly Agree:** The teacher has very positive relationships with students.
	+ **Agree:** The teacher has mostly positive relationships with students.
	+ **Neutral:** The teacher has somewhat positive relationships with students.
	+ **Disagree:** The teacher has not very positive relationships with students.
	+ **Strongly Disagree:** The teacher has not positive relationships with students at all.
* **Teacher Addresses Diverse Learning Needs (`diverse_needs`, select_one, required):** Does the teacher address diverse learning needs?
	+ **Strongly Agree:** The teacher addresses diverse learning needs very effectively.
	+ **Agree:** The teacher mostly addresses diverse learning needs.
	+ **Neutral:** The teacher somewhat addresses diverse learning needs.
	+ **Disagree:** The teacher does not address diverse learning needs.
	+ **Strongly Disagree:** The teacher does not address diverse learning needs at all.
* **Teacher Provides Support for Special Needs (`special_needs_support`, select_one, required):** Does the teacher provide support for special needs students?
	+ **Strongly Agree:** The teacher provides support for special needs students very effectively.
	+ **Agree:** The teacher mostly provides support for special needs students.
	+ **Neutral:** The teacher somewhat provides support for special needs students.
	+ **Disagree:** The teacher does not provide support for special needs students.
	+ **Strongly Disagree:** The teacher does not provide support for special needs students at all.
* **Teacher Strengths Observed (`strengths`, text, required):** Describe positive aspects of the lesson.
* **Areas for Improvement (`areas_improvement`, text, optional):** Suggest areas for professional development.
* **Overall Observation Rating (`overall_rating`, select_one, required):** Overall rating of the observation.
	+ **Excellent:** The teacher's performance was excellent.
	+ **Very Good:** The teacher's performance was very good.
	+ **Good:** The teacher's performance was good.
	+ **Fair:** The teacher's performance was fair.
	+ **Needs Improvement:** The teacher's performance needs improvement.

## Tips
* Please complete all required fields for accurate and thorough analysis.
* Answer based on your observation, not secondary information.
* If you have any suggestions or comments for the teacher, please include them in the "Comments and Recommendations" section.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask the observer or the teacher for clarification.
