# Teacher Class Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Class Verification Form is used to record and track student attendance, including student name, ID, attendance date and time, student status, and any additional notes or comments from the teacher.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant student information in the "Student Information" section.
2. Enter the student's name and ID in the "Student Name" and "Student ID" fields, respectively.
3. Choose the attendance date and time from the calendar or clock in your device.
4. Select the student's status (Present, Absent, Late, or Unscheduled Absence) from the dropdown menu.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Additional Notes" field.
6. Record the date and time the form was submitted.
7. Optionally, enter the name of the person who submitted the form and the date and time it was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information** (`student_class_verification_form_1`, text, optional): This section contains the basic student information. Please select the relevant student details to be verified.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Please enter the student's name.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): Please enter the student's ID number.
* **Attendance Date** (`attendance_date`, date, optional): Choose the date of the student's attendance from the calendar.
* **Attendance Time** (`attendance_time`, time, optional): Choose the time of the student's attendance from the clock.
* **Student Status** (`student_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the student's status (Present, Absent, Late, or Unscheduled Absence).
* **Attendance Status** (`attendance_status`, select_one, optional): Select the attendance status (Present, Absent, Late, or Unscheduled Absence) for a different format.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the student's attendance.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Optionally, enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, text, optional): Choose the date and time the form was submitted.
* **Submitted On Date** (`submitted_on_date`, date, optional): Choose the date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted On Time** (`submitted_on_time`, time, optional): Choose the time the form was submitted.
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments or notes from the teacher.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, text, optional): Record the version of the form (not used in this case).
* **Form Last Modified** (`form_last_modified`, date, optional): Automatically updated with the last modification date and time of the form.
* **Form Last Modified By** (`form_last_modified_by`, text, optional): Automatically updated with the name of the person who last modified the form.
* **Form Last Modified On** (`form_last_modified_on`, date, optional): Automatically updated with the date and time the form was last modified.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, optional): Automatically updated with the name of the person who created the form.
* **Created On** (`created_on`, date, optional): Automatically updated with the date and time the form was created.
* **Created On Date** (`created_on_date`, date, optional): Automatically updated with the date the form was created.
* **Created On Time** (`created_on_time`, time, optional): Automatically updated with the time the form was created.
* **Last Updated By** (`last_updated_by`, text, optional): Automatically updated with the name of the person who last updated the form.
* **Last Updated On** (`last_updated_on`, date, optional): Automatically updated with the date and time the form was last updated.
* **Last Updated Time** (`last_updated_time`, time, optional): Automatically updated with the time the form was last updated.

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct student information to ensure accurate attendance records.
- Be concise when entering student notes or comments.
- Update the form regularly to reflect any changes in student status.
- Review and update the form submission date and time accurately.
