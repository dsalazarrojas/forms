# Teacher Interview Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect demographic and qualitative information from teachers about their experiences, opinions, and perceptions regarding their work environment and teaching practices. This data will aid administrators, researchers, and school officials in making informed decisions to improve the educational system.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each question carefully and answer according to your personal experiences and opinions as a teacher.
- Fill in your details accurately, and do not worry about the required fields. We encourage you to answer all the questions, even if they seem irrelevant or redundant. Your feedback is invaluable to us.
- If you have trouble understanding any question, please refer to the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: This is your personal name as a teacher. It will be used to identify you in our records.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No
- **Last Name**: This is your surname as a teacher. It will be used to identify you in our records.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No
- **Birth Date**: Please enter your birth date in the format of month/day/year (MM/DD/YYYY). This will help us understand your age group and experiences in the education system.
  * Type: date
  * Required: No
- **Email**: This is your official email address used for communication with the school or institution. Please make sure to enter it accurately.
  * Type: email
  * Required: No
- **School Name**: This is the name of the school where you work or have been working as a teacher. Please enter the name as it is officially known or used in the institution.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No

## Tips
- Take your time to answer each question carefully and honestly.
- You can use the hints provided in each field to help guide your responses.
- If you need further assistance or clarification, please reach out to [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
- Thank you for participating and contributing to this study. Your input is invaluable to us!
