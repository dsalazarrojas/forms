# Teacher Interview Publication Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Teacher Interview Publication Consent Form is designed to obtain a teacher's consent for the publication of their interview, and collect relevant details such as their name, school affiliation, and contact information. The form serves as a way to formalize the teacher's permission and provide a clear understanding of how their interview will be published.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form carefully, making sure to provide accurate information for each field.
2.  Enter your name in the **Teacher Name** field.
3.  Enter your school's name in the **School Name** field.
4.  Enter your email address in the **Teacher Email** field, following the format of `example@example.com`.
5.  If applicable, enter your school district in the **School District** field.
6.  Indicate your consent in the **Publication Consent** field by selecting either **Yes** or **No**.
7.  Enter the date of the interview in the **Interview Date** field.
8.  Enter the time of the interview in the **Interview Time** field.
9.  Select who will be credited as the publisher, either **Teacher** or **School**.
10.  If necessary, add any additional notes in the **Notes** field.
11.  If you have any further comments or details, include them in the **Additional Notes** field.
12.  Enter the URL of the publication in the **Publication URL** field.
13.  If the publication has already been published, enter the publication date in the **Published On** field.
14.  If you have a specific date for when the teacher's interview was published by, enter the date in the **Published By Date** field.
15.  Optionally, add any responses or comments as a teacher in the **Teacher Survey Response** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Teacher Name**: <small>Enter your full name as it should appear in the publication.</small>
*   **School Name**: <small>Enter the name of your school where you teach.</small>
*   **School District**: <small>Enter the name of your school district (if applicable).</small>
*   **Teacher Email**: <small>Enter a valid teacher email address in the format `example@example.com`.</small>
*   **Teacher Phone**: <small>Enter your contact phone number for further communication (optional).</small>
*   **Publication Consent**: <small>Select **Yes** if you consent to the publication of your interview, or **No** otherwise.</small>
*   **Interview Date**: <small>Enter the date of the interview you are referring to.</small>
*   **Interview Time**: <small>Enter the time of the interview, if applicable.</small>
*   **Published By**: <small>Select **Teacher** or **School** depending on who will be credited for publishing the interview.</small>
*   **Notes**: <small>Enter any additional comments or information about the interview.</small>
*   **Additional Notes**: <small>Enter any further comments or details about the interview, if needed.</small>
*   **Publication URL**: <small>Enter the URL of the publication where your interview will be published.</small>
*   **Published On**: <small>Enter the date when the publication was published (if already published).</small>
*   **Published By Date**: <small>Enter the date when the interview was published by the teacher or school.</small>
*   **Teacher Survey Response**: <small>Enter your responses or comments as a teacher (if applicable).</small>
