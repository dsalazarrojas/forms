# student_information_sheet_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for collecting basic information about students, likely for educational purposes such as registration, communication, or data management.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Start by entering the student's ID number.
*   Enter the student's name as it appears on their official records.
*   If applicable, select the student's high school name from the available options.
*   Enter the student's grade level.
*   List the student's academic interests (multiple selections allowed).
*   Enter the student's email address.
*   Enter the student's phone number.
*   Enter the parent's name.
*   Enter the parent's email address.
*   Enter the parent's phone number.
*   Enter the teacher's name.
*   Enter the teacher's email address.
*   Enter the teacher's phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student ID** (1): The student's unique identifier number, usually assigned by the institution.
*   **Name** (2): The student's full name, as it appears on official records.
*   **High School** (3): The name of the student's current or previous high school (if applicable).
*   **Grade Level** (4): The student's current grade level, e.g., 9th, 10th, 11th, 12th, etc.
*   **Academic Interests** (5): A list of the student's academic interests or subjects they are enrolled in.
*   **Email** (6): The student's email address.
*   **Phone** (7): The student's phone number.
*   **Parent Name** (8): The student's parent or guardian's name.
*   **Parent Email** (9): The parent or guardian's email address.
*   **Parent Phone** (10): The parent or guardian's phone number.
*   **Teacher Name** (11): The teacher's name, if assigned to the student.
*   **Teacher Email** (12): The teacher's email address.
*   **Teacher Phone** (13): The teacher's phone number.
