# office_hours_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help teachers schedule office hours appointments with students. It is intended to collect necessary information to facilitate a smooth and efficient appointment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Office Hours" page with any relevant details or comments.
2. Enter the student's information on the "Student Information" page.
3. Describe the appointment on the "Appointment" page.
4. Provide your information as the teacher on the "Teacher" page.
5. Add any additional details on the "Details" page.
6. Click "Submit" to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Office Hours** (text):
	+ A brief description of the office hours details or comments.
* **Student Information** (text):
	+ Enter the student's name, student ID, or any other relevant information.
* **Appointment** (text):
	+ Describe the meeting purpose, duration, or any specific details.
* **Teacher** (text):
	+ Provide your name, title, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Details** (text):
	+ Add any additional comments or information about the meeting.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out each page carefully and accurately to avoid any potential issues.
* Review the form before submitting it to ensure that it is complete and correct.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask for help.
