# Primary School Swimming Carnival Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Primary School Swimming Carnival Nomination Form is designed to gather information about each student nominated for the swimming carnival. This form aims to streamline the nomination process and ensure that all necessary details are captured accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter the student's last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Provide the student's email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select the student's grade level from the drop-down menu in the "Grade level" field.
5. Enter the teacher's name in the "Teacher name" field.
6. Enter the student's name in the "Student name" field.
7. Indicate if the student will participate in the swim event by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Swim event" field.
8. If applicable, provide any additional event details in the "Event details" field.
9. If applicable, provide any additional student details in the "Student details" field.
10. If applicable, provide any additional parent details in the "Parent details" field.
11. Enter the name and contact information of the student's emergency contact person in the "Emergency contact" field.
12. If applicable, provide any medical conditions that the student may have in the "Medical condition" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name (First name)**: Enter the student's first name.
* **Last name (Last name)**: Enter the student's last name.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter the student's email address.
* **Grade level (Grade level)**: Select the student's grade level from the drop-down menu.
* **Teacher name (Teacher name)**: Enter the teacher's name.
* **Student name (Student name)**: Enter the student's name.
* **Swim event (Swim event)**: Indicate if the student will participate in the swim event by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Event details (Event details)**: If the student is participating in the swim event, provide any additional event details here.
* **Student details (Student details)**: If applicable, provide any additional details about the student.
* **Parent details (Parent details)**: If applicable, provide any additional details about the parent.
* **Emergency contact (Emergency contact)**: Enter the name and contact information of the student's emergency contact person.
* **Medical condition (Medical condition)**: If the student has any medical conditions, enter them here.
