# Preschool Director Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The preschool director evaluation form is a tool for teachers to provide feedback on the director's performance, qualifications, and ability to create a safe and supportive environment for students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form thoroughly before starting to fill it out.
2. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
3. Be specific when providing feedback, using concrete examples or anecdotes to support your answers.
4. Complete all required fields (indicated by a *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Feedback* (text, required):**
Please provide your overall feedback on the director's performance and qualifications.
* **Director Qualifications** (select_one, optional):
This question allows you to provide feedback on one of three options:
	+ Option 1: The director has excellent qualifications.
	+ Option 2: The director has some qualifications, but room for improvement.
	+ Option 3: The director has limited qualifications.
* **Director Performance** (text, optional):
This question allows you to provide feedback on the director's performance, such as their leadership, communication style, or ability to create a safe and supportive environment.
* **Support** (number, optional): 
Rate the level of support the director provides to teachers and students.
* **Communication Skills** (text, optional):
This question allows you to provide feedback on the director's communication style and effectiveness.
* **Feedback** (note, optional):
This is a space for you to provide any additional feedback or comments on the director's performance.
* **Teacher Availability** (number, optional):
Rate the director's availability to answer questions or address concerns.
* **Director Safety** (select_multiple, optional):
Select all that apply:
	+ Option 1: The director is very effective in maintaining a safe environment.
	+ Option 2: The director is somewhat effective in maintaining a safe environment.
	+ Option 3: The director is not effective in maintaining a safe environment.
* **Safety Procedures** (select_multiple, optional):
Select all that apply:
	+ Option 1: The director has established clear and effective safety procedures.
	+ Option 2: The director has some safety procedures in place, but room for improvement.
	+ Option 3: The director has limited safety procedures in place.

## Tips
* Be honest and constructive in your feedback.
* Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your answers.
* If you're unsure about any question, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
