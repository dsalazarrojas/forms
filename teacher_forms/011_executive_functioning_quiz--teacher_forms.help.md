# Executive Functioning Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Executive Functioning Quiz is designed to assess a student's executive functioning abilities, such as working memory, planning, and problem-solving. This quiz helps teachers to identify areas of strength and weakness and provide accurate and individualized support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Fill out the intro_question field with a brief description of the reason you are completing this quiz.
2.  Enter your name in the teacher_info field.
3.  Enter the student's name in the student_info field.
4.  Enter the student's date of birth and age in the student_date_of_birth and student_age fields, respectively.
5.  Select all the cognitive skills that were assessed for the student in the cognitive_skills_assessed field.
6.  Provide any additional notes about the student's performance and cognitive strengths and weaknesses in the student_performance field.
7.  Select all the recommendations for supporting the student in the recommendations field.
8.  Summarize the student's performance and goals in the output_statement field.
9.  Summarize the output goals for the student in the output_goals field.
10. Enter the output goals for the student's therapist or support team in the output_therapist_goals field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **intro_question** (text, required/optional): A brief description of the reason this quiz is being completed.
* **teacher_info** (text, true): Your name and title.
* **student_info** (text, true): The student's name.
* **student_date_of_birth** (date, true): The student's date of birth.
* **student_age** (number, true): The student's age.
* **cognitive_skills_assessed** (select_multiple, true): Select all the cognitive skills that were assessed for the student.
* **student_performance** (select_multiple, true): Provide additional notes about the student's performance and cognitive strengths and weaknesses.
* **recommendations** (select_multiple, true): Select all the recommendations for supporting the student.
* **output_statement** (text, true): Summarize the student's performance and goals.
* **output_goals** (text, true): Summarize the student's output goals.
* **output_therapist_goals** (text, true): Enter the output goals for the student's therapist or support team.
