# Instructional Technology Needs Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help instructional technology staff assess the needs of teachers for instructional technology support and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the category that best describes the type of technology support you need.
2. Provide a brief description of your needs in the description field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Forms** (`1`, `text`, optional): This field is intended to be a brief introduction or title for your request. You can type whatever you like here, but it's not required.
* **Category** (`2`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the options provided to describe the type of technology support you need. This will help us better understand your request.
* **Description** (`3`, `text`, optional): This field is where you can provide more information about your technology needs. Please keep your description brief and to the point.
* **Description** (`4`, `text`, optional): This is another description field, intended to provide additional information about your needs if needed.
* **Description** (`5`, `text`, optional): This is yet another description field, which can be used to provide further details about your technology needs.

## Tips
* Be brief and concise in your description fields. We want to get a clear understanding of your needs quickly.
* If you're unsure about any of the categories, please select "Other" and we'll do our best to assist you.
