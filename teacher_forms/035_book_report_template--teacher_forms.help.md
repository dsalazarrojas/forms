# Book Report Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive guide for students to submit their book reports. It covers various aspects of the book, including details, plot and character analysis, and personal response.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student's name in the "Student Full Name" field.
2. Select your current grade level from the "Grade Level" dropdown menu.
3. Enter your teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
4. Enter the title of the book in the "Book Title" field.
5. Enter the author of the book in the "Book Author" field.
6. Select the genre of the book from the "Book Genre" dropdown menu.
7. Enter the publication year of the book in the "Publication Year" field (if available).
8. Describe the setting of the book in the "Describe the Setting" field.
9. List the main characters in the "Main Characters" field.
10. Identify the protagonist and antagonist of the book in the "Protagonist" and "Antagonist" fields respectively.
11. Summarize the plot in 5-7 sentences in the "Plot Summary" field.
12. Describe the main conflict of the book in the "Main Conflict" field.
13. Describe the climax of the book in the "Climax Description" field.
14. Describe how the conflict was resolved in the "Resolution" field.
15. Choose your favorite scene from the book and describe it in the "Favorite Scene" field.
16. Analyze the character development of the main character in the "Character Analysis" field.
17. Identify the theme of the book in the "Theme" field.
18. Explain how the book relates to your life in the "Personal Connection" field (if applicable).
19. Rate the book using the "Your Rating" dropdown menu.
20. Decide if you would recommend the book to classmates and select the corresponding option.
21. Identify who you think would enjoy this book in the "Who Would Enjoy This Book" field (if applicable).
22. Enter the due date for the report in the "Report Due Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Full Name (id: 1)**: Enter your first and last name.
* **Grade Level (id: 2)**: Select your current grade level from the dropdown menu.
* **Teacher Name (id: 3)**: Enter your teacher's name.
* **Book Title (id: 4)**: Enter the full title of the book.
* **Book Author (id: 5)**: Enter the author of the book.
* **Book Genre (id: 6)**: Select the genre of the book from the dropdown menu.
* **Publication Year (id: 7)**: Enter the publication year of the book (if available).
* **Describe the Setting (id: 8)**: Where and when does the story take place?
* **Main Characters (id: 9)**: List the main characters in the book.
* **Protagonist (id: 10)**: Identify the main character of the story.
* **Antagonist (id: 11)**: Identify the character opposing the protagonist.
* **Plot Summary (id: 12)**: Summarize the plot in 5-7 sentences.
* **Main Conflict (id: 13)**: Describe the central problem of the story.
* **Climax Description (id: 14)**: Describe the most exciting part of the story.
* **Resolution (id: 15)**: Describe how the conflict was resolved.
* **Favorite Scene (id: 16)**: Choose a favorite scene from the book and describe it.
* **Character Analysis (id: 17)**: Analyze how the main character developed throughout the book.
* **Theme (id: 18)**: Identify the underlying message or theme of the book.
* **Personal Connection (id: 19)**: Explain how the book relates to your life (if applicable).
* **Your Rating (id: 20)**: Rate the book using the dropdown menu.
* **Would You Recommend This Book (id: 21)**: Decide if you would recommend the book to classmates and select the corresponding option.
* **Who Would Enjoy This Book (id: 22)**: Identify who you think would enjoy this book (if applicable).
* **Report Due Date (id: 23)**: Enter the due date for the report.
* **Book Rating (id: 24)**: Rate the book using the dropdown menu.
* **Would Recommend (id: 25)**: Decide if you would recommend the book to classmates and select the corresponding option.
