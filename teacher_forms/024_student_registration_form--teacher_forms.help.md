# Student Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a student's personal details for registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
2. Enter your valid email address.
3. Enter your student ID number.
4. Select the program you are currently enrolled in.
5. Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
6. Select your current grade level.
7. Indicate if you are a student type (e.g., yes or no).
8. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
9. Enter the name of your parent or guardian.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Full Name): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (Email): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Student ID** (Student ID): Enter your unique student ID number for identification purposes.
* **Program** (Program): Select the program you are currently enrolled in.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 1999-07-12).
* **Grade Level** (Grade Level): Select your current grade level from the options provided.
* **Student Type** (Student Type): Indicate if you are a student type (e.g., yes or no).
* **Phone** (Phone): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Parent Name** (Parent Name): Enter the name of your parent or guardian.
