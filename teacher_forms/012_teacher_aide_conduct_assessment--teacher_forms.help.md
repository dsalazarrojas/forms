# Teacher Aide Conduct Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from teachers on the conduct of their teacher aides. This form helps to assess the effectiveness of the teacher aide's performance and identify areas for improvement. The form is used to rate and comment on the teacher aide's conduct, student engagement, teacher support, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the questions below and provide your honest feedback.
2. Answer the questions according to your observations and experiences with the teacher aide.
3. Provide specific examples or details when answering the questions.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by the "required" tag).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction (Required: false)
* **Introduction**: This section is a brief description of your interaction with the teacher aide. Please provide any relevant context or information that may be helpful for the teacher aide's supervisor.

### Rating (Required: false)
* **Rating**: This question asks you to rate the teacher aide's conduct on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "poor" and 5 is "excellent".

### Teacher Support (Required: false)
* **Teacher Support**: This question asks you to rate the level of support provided by the teacher aide to you during the lesson or task. Please select the option that best describes your experience.

### Student Engagement (Required: false)
* **Student Engagement**: This question asks you to indicate whether the teacher aide was able to engage the students effectively during the lesson or task.

### Teacher Aide Conduct (Required: false)
* **Teacher Aide Conduct**: This question asks you to describe any specific behaviors or actions of the teacher aide that were observed during the lesson or task.

### Areas For Improvement (Required: false)
* **Areas For Improvement**: This question asks you to select any areas where the teacher aide could improve their conduct or performance.

### Overall Performance (Required: false)
* **Overall Performance**: This question asks you to rate the teacher aide's overall performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "poor" and 5 is "excellent".
