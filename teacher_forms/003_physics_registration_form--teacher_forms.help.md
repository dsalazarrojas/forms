# Physics Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering students for a physics course. It requires information from both students and teachers, including student contact details, course specifics, and student course preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your student name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Provide your valid email address in the "Student Email" field.
3. Enter your teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
4. Enter your teacher's valid email address in the "Teacher Email" field.
5. Choose a suitable time for classes from the "Class Time" options.
6. Select all the days you will be attending classes from the "Class Days" options.
7. Enter the duration of each class in the "Class Duration" field.
8. Choose a course date.
9. Select the physics courses you wish to register for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (1)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Student Email (2)**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Teacher Name (3)**: Enter your teacher's full name as it appears on their official documents.
* **Teacher Email (4)**: Enter your teacher's valid email address that we can use to contact them.
* **Course Name (5)**: Enter the name of the course you are registering for (optional).
* **Course Id (6)**: Enter the unique ID of the course (optional).
* **Class Time (7)**: Choose the time of your class from the options 08:00, 10:00, or 12:00.
* **Class Days (8)**: Select all the days you will be attending classes. You can choose multiple days.
* **Class Duration (9)**: Enter the duration of each class in minutes.
* **Course Date (10)**: Choose a date for your course.
* **Course Time (11)**: Enter the time of your course (optional).
* **Note (12)**: Leave any additional comments or notes about your course (optional).
* **Physics 1 (13)**: Select the physics course you wish to register for.
* **Physics 2 (14)**: Select all the physics courses you wish to register for.
* **Physics 3 (15)**: Select all the physics courses you wish to register for.
* **Physics 4 (16)** - **Physics 13 (25)**: Enter the number of the physics course you wish to register for.

Note: This form is for registering students for a physics course. Please fill it out carefully and accurately to avoid any issues. If you have any difficulties or questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
