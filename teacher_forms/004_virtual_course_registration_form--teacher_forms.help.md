# Virtual Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide helps you complete the Virtual Course Registration Form, which allows you to create and manage virtual courses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details as prompted, including student name, email, phone, and other relevant information.
2. Add course information, such as course title, description, price, and category.
3. Specify course details like start and end dates, duration, and status.
4. Add course media, such as images, videos, and documents.
5. Select enrollment options and language level.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your name as a student or registrant.

### 2. **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false)
Enter your email address for communication purposes.

### 3. **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### 4. **Course Title** (`course_title`, `text`, required: false)
Enter a title for your course, e.g., "Introduction to Programming."

### 5. **Course Start Date** (`course_start_date`, `date`, required: false)
Enter the start date of your course.

### 6. **Course End Date** (`course_end_date`, `date`, required: false)
Enter the end date of your course.

### 7. **Course Description** (`course_description`, `text`, required: false)
Describe your course in a few words, e.g., "This course covers the basics of programming."

### 8. **Course Price** (`course_price`, `number`, required: false)
Enter the price of your course, e.g., 100.00.

### 9. **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the name of the teacher or instructor of your course.

### 10. **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, `email`, required: false)
Enter the email address of the teacher or instructor.

### 11. **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the phone number of the teacher or instructor.

### 12. **Course Category** (`course_category`, `select_one`, required: false)
Select a category for your course, e.g., "Programming," "Science," or "Arts."

### 13. **Course Tags** (`course_tags`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Select any additional tags that describe your course, e.g., "python," "java," "beginner-friendly."

### 14. **Course Start Time** (`course_start_time`, `time`, required: false)
Enter the start time of your course, e.g., "10:00 AM."

### 15. **Course End Time** (`course_end_time`, `time`, required: false)
Enter the end time of your course, e.g., "11:00 AM."

### 16. **Course Duration** (`course_duration`, `text`, required: false)
Specify the duration of your course, e.g., "1 hour," "2 hours," "half day."

### 17. **Course Status** (`course_status`, `select_one`, required: false)
Select the status of your course, e.g., "Active," "Inactive," or "Pending."

### 18. **Course Image** (`course_image`, `text`, required: false)
Upload an image for your course, e.g., a screenshot or logo.

### 19. **Course Videos** (`course_videos`, `text`, required: false)
Upload any videos related to your course, e.g., video lectures.

### 20. **Course Documents** (`course_documents`, `text`, required: false)
Upload any documents related to your course, e.g., reading materials.

### 21. **Course Comments** (`course_comments`, `text`, required: false)
Add any comments or feedback from students or registrants.

### 22. **Course Enrolment** (`course_enrolment`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Select any enrollment options, e.g., "Public," "Private," or "Limited."

### 23. **Course Language** (`course_language`, `select_one`, required: false)
Select the language of your course, e.g., "English," "Spanish," "French."

### 24. **Course Level** (`course_level`, `select_one`, required: false)
Select the level of your course, e.g., "Beginner," "Intermediate," "Advanced."

### 25. **Additional Course Tags** (`course_tags_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Select any additional tags that describe your course, e.g., "beginner-friendly," "python," "online course."
