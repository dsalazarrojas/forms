# Home School Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Home School Survey form is designed to collect information from students, parents, or guardians about a student's home schooling experience. This form will help us better understand your child's educational background and needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer all the required fields (indicated by a star *).
2. Fill in your responses in the corresponding fields.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home School Survey Form** (`home_school_survey_form`, text, optional): This is the main form title. Please type a brief description of the form purpose or any additional information you'd like to share.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, number, optional): Select the grade level of the student from the provided options (e.g., 1-12).
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Type the student's name as it appears on official records or documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email address (if applicable).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Type the student's phone number (if applicable).

## Tips

* Please answer each question as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can always contact us for clarification.
* You can save your progress and continue later if needed.
* Once you've completed the form, click the submit button to send it to us.
