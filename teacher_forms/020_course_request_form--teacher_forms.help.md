# Course Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Request Form is a tool used to gather information from students, educators, or administrators who wish to request a course for personal or professional development. The form helps to streamline the course request process, ensuring that all necessary information is collected and communicated to the relevant parties in a clear and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Student info" field with your name, student ID, or other relevant student information.
2. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email address" field.
4. Select the relevant "Contact Info" field that best describes your relationship with the course request.
5. Enter the number of course requests you are making in the "Number of course requests" field.
6. Enter the date you wish to start the course in the "Request start date" field.
7. Enter the date the course will end in the "Request end date" field.
8. Provide any additional course-related information in the "Course info" field.
9. Enter any other relevant information in the "Other info" field.
10. Click the "Submit" button to submit your course request form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student info** (student_info, text, required: false): Enter your name, student ID, or other relevant student information.
- **Contact Number** (contact_number, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
- **Email address** (email_addr, text, required: false): Enter your email address.
- **Contact Info** (contact_info, select_multiple, required: false):
  - Select '1' for 'Parent'.
  - Select '2' for 'Teacher'.
  - Select '3' for 'Staff'.
- **Number of course requests** (course_req_num, number, required: false): Enter the number of courses you are requesting.
- **Request start date** (start_date, text, required: false): Enter the date you wish to start the course.
- **Request end date** (end_date, text, required: false): Enter the date the course will end.
- **Course info** (course_info, text, required: false): Provide any additional course-related information.
- **Other info** (other_info, text, required: false): Enter any other relevant information.
- **Submit** (submit_button, note, required: false): Click the "Submit" button to submit your course request form.
- **Request form** (course_request_form, note, required: false): This field is not applicable, as it's a note field and not a field to fill in. It's there to indicate that this is a course request form.
