# Parents Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain permission from parents or guardians for their child's participation in school events, activities, or programs. It is a way for teachers to gather necessary information and consent from parents before allowing their child to take part.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  **Event Details**: Provide a brief description of the event, activity, or program for which you are seeking permission.
2.  **Event Organizer**: Enter the name of the person or organization responsible for organizing the event.
3.  **Parent Permission**: Choose either "Yes" or "No" to indicate your permission for your child's participation.
4.  **Contact Details**: Provide any relevant contact details you may have, such as phone numbers or emails, for the teacher or school staff to reach you.

## Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible to help the teacher or school staff understand your situation.
* If you are unable to complete the form in its entirety, please contact the teacher or school staff directly.
* Make sure to double-check the information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
