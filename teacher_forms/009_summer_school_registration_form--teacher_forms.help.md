# Summer School Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer School Registration Form is designed to collect information from students and teachers to register students for summer school programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out all required fields (indicated by <strong>required</strong>)
* Provide accurate information for each field
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below for guidance

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (student_name)**: Enter the student's name as they want it to appear on their summer school documents. 
* **Teacher Name (teacher_name)**: Enter the teacher's name who will be leading the summer school program. 
* **School Name (school_name)**: Enter the name of the school that the student will be attending. 
* **Summer School Experiences (summer_school_experiences)**: This is a note field for any relevant summer school experiences that the student has completed. 
* **Email (email)**: Enter the student's email address to ensure that they can receive important summer school updates and information. 
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter the student's phone number in case the school needs to contact them.

Note: If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the school administration for assistance.
