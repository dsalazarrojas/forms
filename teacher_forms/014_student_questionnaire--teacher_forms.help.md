# Student Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Questionnaire is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from students about their learning experience. This form helps teachers evaluate their teaching methods and make necessary adjustments to improve student satisfaction and learning outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with your honest and accurate feedback. You can provide as much or as little information as you'd like, but make sure to answer all required fields to ensure that your feedback is complete and actionable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Feedback** (student_feedback, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your overall experience in the class.
* **Class Performance** (class_performance, text, optional): Describe your performance in this class, including any areas of strength or improvement.
* **Class Environment** (class_environment, text, optional): Describe your environment of learning in this class, including any physical or emotional factors that may have impacted your learning.
* **Teacher Quality** (teacher_quality, text, optional): Rate your experience with the teacher, including their communication style, teaching methods, and overall effectiveness.
* **Classroom Facilities** (classroom_facilities, text, optional): Rate your experience with the classroom facilities, including the comfort, availability, and functionality of the facilities.
* **Teacher Response** (teacher_response, text, optional): Describe how the teacher responded to your questions or concerns, including their timeliness and helpfulness.
* **Student Suggestion** (student_suggestion, text, optional): Provide suggestions or ideas for improving your learning experience in the class or school.
* **School Facilities** (school_facilities, text, optional): Rate your experience with the school facilities, including the comfort, availability, and functionality of the facilities.
* **Student Expectations** (student_expectations, text, optional): Describe your expectations for the teacher, class, or school, including what you believe you need to succeed.
* **Teacher Support** (teacher_support, text, optional): Rate your experience with the teacher's support, including their availability, helpfulness, and response time.
* **School Environment** (school_environment, text, optional): Describe your environment of learning in the school, including any physical or emotional factors that may have impacted your learning.
* **Student Recommend** (student_recommend, text, optional): Recommend any changes or improvements you would like to see in the class or school.
* **Teacher Support System** (teacher_support_system, text, optional): Describe your experience with the teacher's support system, including its effectiveness and accessibility.
* **Student Satisfaction** (student_satisfaction, select_one, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the class or school, using the scale: 5 (very satisfied), 4 (somewhat satisfied), 3 (neutral), 2 (somewhat dissatisfied), 1 (very dissatisfied).
* **Student Satisfaction 2** (student_satisfaction2, select_one, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the class or school, using the same scale as above.
* **Class Time** (class_time, time, optional): Provide the time of your class in 24-hour format (e.g., 09:00).
* **Class Day** (class_day, time, optional): Provide the day of your class in 24-hour format (e.g., 01/02/2023).
* **Class Date** (class_date, date, optional): Provide the date of your class in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2023-02-01).
* **Class Time 2** (class_time2, time, optional): Provide the time of your class in 24-hour format (e.g., 10:00).
* **School Time** (school_time, time, optional): Provide the time of your school in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00).
* **Class Time 2** (class_time2, time, optional): Provide the time of your class in 24-hour format (e.g., 11:00).
* **Student Name** (student_name, text, optional): Provide your name as a student.
* **Teacher Name** (teacher_name, text, optional): Provide the name of your teacher.
* **School ID** (school_id, text, optional): Provide the ID of your school.
* **Assigned Tool** (assigned_tool, text, optional): Describe any tools or resources assigned to you during the class.

Please note that all fields are optional, but providing accurate and honest feedback is crucial to helping teachers improve their teaching methods and learning environment.
