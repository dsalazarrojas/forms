# Parent Involvement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to understand your preferred method of communication and involvement in your child's education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the questions below, providing your preferred answers to each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Header** (`survey_header`, `note`, required: false): This section provides some introductory information about the survey.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name, so we can address you personally.
* **How often do you want to be informed about your child's activities?** (`question_1`, `select_one`, required: false): Select how often you would like to receive updates about your child's activities, such as:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
* **What is the best way to contact you?** (`question_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose your preferred method of communication, such as:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Text Message
* **What time is best for you to be called?** (`question_3`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the time of day that suits you best for a call, such as:
	+ Morning
	+ Afternoon
	+ Evening
* **What time of day do you prefer to talk during the day?** (`question_4`, `time`, required: false): Specify a time that works best for you to talk.
* **What would you like to talk about?** (`question_5`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose topics you would like to discuss with your child, such as:
	+ Schoolwork
	+ Homework
	+ Personal matters
* **Are you interested in volunteering at school?** (`question_6`, `select_one`, required: false): Let us know if you are interested in helping out at school.
* **How can we reach you in an emergency?** (`question_7`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your contact information so we can reach you in case of an emergency.
* **How do you like to communicate with your child about their grades?** (`question_8`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your preferred method of communication, such as:
	+ Text
	+ Email
	+ In-person
* **How would you like to receive updates on your child's activities and progress?** (`question_9`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the frequency of updates you would like to receive, such as:
	+ Daily updates
	+ Weekly updates
	+ Monthly updates
* **Is there a specific time of day you would like to receive updates?** (`question_10`, `time`, required: false): Specify a time that works best for you to receive updates.
* **How often would you like to talk about your child's activities and progress?** (`question_11`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how often you would like to discuss your child's activities and progress, such as:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
* **How do you like to communicate about concerns?** (`question_12`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose your preferred method of communication, such as:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Text Message
* **How would you like to receive feedback on your child's assignments?** (`question_13`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose your preferred method of communication, such as:
	+ Text
	+ Email
	+ Parent portal
* **Are you available for a parent-teacher conference?** (`question_14`, `select_one`, required: false): Let us know if you are available for a conference.
* **How would you like to communicate about your child's behavior?** (`question_15`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose your preferred method of communication, such as:
	+ Email
	+ Text Message
	+ Parent portal
* **Is there something else you would like to discuss about your child?** (`question_16`, `text`, required: false): Please enter any additional comments or concerns you may have.
* **Would you like to be informed about school closures or other events?** (`question_17`, `select_one`, required: false): Let us know if you would like to be informed about school events.
* **How do you like to communicate about school events?** (`question_18`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose your preferred method of communication, such as:
	+ Email
	+ Text Message
	+ Parent portal
* **Do you have a preferred method of communication?** (`question_19`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your preferred method of communication, such as:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Text
* **Is there a time of day you are unavailable for a call?** (`question_20`, `time`, required: false): Specify a time that does not suit you for a call.
* **Is there a specific time of day you would like to be called?** (`question_21`, `time`, required: false): Choose a time that suits you for a call.
* **Do you have any other questions or concerns?** (`question_22`, `note`, required: false): Please enter any additional comments or concerns you may have.
