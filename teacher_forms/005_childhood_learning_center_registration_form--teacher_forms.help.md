# Childhood Learning Center Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and demographic information from parents or guardians of children who will be attending the Childhood Learning Center.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by providing personal information about the child, including their full name, preferred name, and birth date.
2. Enter the name of the parent or guardian who will be the primary contact for the child.
3. Provide the child's home address, including street address, city, state, and zip code.
4. Enter a phone number for the parent or guardian.
5. List any emergency contacts, including their name and phone number.
6. If applicable, provide any allergies or medical conditions that the child may have.
7. Select the preferred days of attendance for the child.
8. Finally, provide any additional notes or comments that you would like to share with the staff at the Childhood Learning Center.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Information
* **Child Full Name** (Text, Required): Enter the full name of the child.
* **Preferred Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name that the child prefers to be called.
* **Birth Date** (Date, Required): Enter the child's date of birth.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the parent or guardian who will be the primary contact for the child.
* **Home Address** (Text, Required): Enter the child's street address, city, state, and zip code.

### Additional Information
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter a phone number for the parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Text, Required): Enter the phone number for the emergency contact.
* **Allergies or Medical Conditions** (Text, Optional): If applicable, provide any allergies or medical conditions that the child may have.
* **Preferred Days of Attendance** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the days of the week that the child will attend the Childhood Learning Center. 
* **Additional Notes** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information you would like to share with the staff at the Childhood Learning Center.

## Tips
* Please make sure to complete all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for assistance.
* This form should only be completed once per child.
