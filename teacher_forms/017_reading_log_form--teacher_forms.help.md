# reading_log_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The reading log form is designed to help users track their progress in reading materials, allowing them to record and analyze their reading experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by navigating to the first page of the form, "reading_log_form_page_1".
2.  Review the page content and fill out the relevant fields as prompted.
3.  Proceed to the next page, "reading_log_form_page_2", and repeat the process.
4.  Continue this process until you reach the last page, "reading_log_form_page_6".
5.  On the "reading_log_form_page_4", select the level of your reading proficiency from the list of options provided.
6.  Fill out the remaining fields on the last page, "reading_log_form_page_6", as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Page 1: Reading Log Form**
    *   **Reading Log Form Label 1** (`note`, required/optional): This is the first field on the form where you can record your reading log notes.
    *   **Reading Log Form Label 2** (`note`, required/optional): This field is for additional notes about your reading experience.
*   **Page 2: Reading Log Form**
    *   **Reading Log Form Label 3** (`date`, required/optional): Record the date you completed the material you're logging.
*   **Page 3: Reading Log Form**
    *   **Reading Log Form Label 4** (`time`, required/optional): Record the amount of time spent reading the material.
*   **Page 4: Reading Log Form**
    *   **Reading Log Form Label 5** (`time`, required/optional): This field is for recording the amount of time spent reading the material again.
    *   **Reading Level** (`select_one`, optional): Select the level of your reading proficiency from the options provided:
        *   Reading Level: Choose from options like Intermediate, Advanced, Proficient, None.
*   **Page 5: Reading Log Form**
    *   **Reading Log Form Label 6** (`time`, required/optional): This field is similar to Reading Log Form Label 4, for recording the amount of time spent reading the material.
*   **Page 6: Reading Log Form**
    *   **Reading Log Form Label 7** (`date`, required/optional): Record the date you plan to complete or review the material again.

## Tips
*   Please fill out the form honestly and accurately.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form owner or administrator.
*   You can review and edit your form entries as needed.
