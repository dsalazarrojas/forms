# Language School Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Language School Application Form is a comprehensive form designed for students to apply to a language program. It collects personal, school, and language-related information to assess their eligibility and suitability for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in your personal details, such as first name, last name, email, and contact phone (if applicable).
2.  Select your school grade level and school program of interest.
3.  Provide information about your language skills, including language level, available languages, and preferred language.
4.  Specify your reason for attending the program and language interest.
5.  Enter your date of birth and date of arrival (if applicable).
6.  If you have attended any other language schools, provide the details.
7.  If you are currently enrolled in a language school, specify the name and details of your current school.
8.  Provide any additional comments or reasons you'd like to share.
9.  Specify whether you have a teacher recommendation.
10. Sign and date the form with your signature and provide your parent's signature and name (if applicable).
11. Enter your parent's phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name.
*   **Last name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name.
*   **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your email address.
*   **Contact phone** (4, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number (if you want to be contacted).
*   **Student name** (5, text, required): Enter your name.
*   **School grade level** (6, select_one, required): Select your current school grade level (A, B, C).
*   **School program of interest** (7, select_multiple, required): Choose your preferred language program (Language Program A, Language Program B, Other (please specify)).
*   **Other (please specify)** (8, text, optional): If you selected "Other" as your school program of interest, specify which one in this field.
*   **Language skills** (9, text, optional): Describe your language skills and proficiency levels.
*   **Language level** (10, select_one, optional): Select your language level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
*   **Available languages** (11, select_multiple, optional): Specify the languages you are proficient in.
*   **Preferred language** (12, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred language (English, Spanish, French, German).
*   **Language interest** (13, select_multiple, optional): Specify your language interest (Reading, Writing, Listening, Speaking).
*   **Date of birth** (14, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
*   **Date of arrival** (15, date, optional): Enter the date you would arrive at the language school (if applicable).
*   **Date of departure** (16, date, optional): Enter the date you would depart from the language school (if applicable).
*   **Language schools attended** (17, text, optional): If you have attended any language schools, provide the details.
*   **Current language school** (18, text, optional): If you are currently enrolled in a language school, specify the name and details of your current school.
*   **Reason for attending** (19, text, optional): Provide your reason for attending the language program.
*   **Language interest** (20, text, optional): Describe your language interest.
*   **Teacher recommendation** (21, select_one, optional): Specify whether you have a teacher recommendation ('True', 'False').
*   **Parent signature** (22, text, optional): Sign and date the form with your signature.
*   **Student signature** (23, text, optional): Sign and date the form with your signature.
*   **Parent name** (24, text, optional): Enter your parent's name (if applicable).
*   **Parent phone** (25, text, optional): Enter your parent's phone number (if applicable).
