# Case Study Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Case Study Submission Form is a tool for teachers to submit case studies to be reviewed and graded by instructors. This form captures essential details about students, courses, and the case study itself, aiding in the evaluation and assessment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Student Information section, providing your name, ID number, and email address.
2. Move on to the Course Information section, entering the course name and number.
3. In the Case Study Details section, provide a title, subject area or topic, context or background, and research methods used.
4. In the Methodology and Analysis section, describe how you analyzed the case and select relevant research methods used.
5. Summarize the key findings and conclusions in the Key Findings section, and discuss implications and recommendations in the Implications section.
6. Enter the submission details, including the approximate word count, page count, and file format.
7. Finally, add any additional comments or notes in the Additional Comments section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name (Student Information)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your student ID.
* **Student ID Number (Student Information)**: Enter your student ID number as assigned by the institution.
* **Student Email Address (Student Information)**: Enter your student email address.
* **Course Name (Course Information)**: Enter the name of the course you're submitting for.
* **Course Number (Course Information)**: Enter the course number assigned by the institution.
* **Instructor Name (Course Information)**: Enter the name of your instructor.
* **Semester (Course Information)**: Select the semester in which you're submitting the case study.
* **Submission Date (Course Information)**: Enter the date you're submitting the case study.
* **Case Study Title (Case Study Details)**: Enter a brief title of the case study.
* **Subject Area or Topic (Case Study Details)**: Enter the subject area or topic of the case study.
* **Context or Background (Case Study Details)**: Briefly describe the context or background of the case study.
* **Research Methods Used (Methodology and Analysis)**: Select all the research methods used in the case study.
* **Analysis Approach (Methodology and Analysis)**: Describe how you analyzed the case study.
* **Main Findings and Conclusions (Key Findings)**: Summarize the key findings and conclusions of the case study.
* **Implications and Recommendations (Key Findings)**: Discuss the implications of the case study and provide recommendations.
* **Approximate Word Count (Submission Details)**: Enter the approximate word count of the case study.
* **Number of Pages (Submission Details)**: Enter the number of pages in the case study.
* **File Format (Submission Details)**: Select the file format used for the case study submission.
* **Additional Comments or Notes (Submission Details)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the submission.
