# Back To School Parent Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Back To School Parent Survey is designed to gather your feedback as a parent or guardian regarding your child's learning experience during the pandemic, their views on school reopening, and their expectations for in-school learning. This survey will help us understand your concerns, needs, and expectations, which will enable us to provide better support to you and your child.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your information in the corresponding fields.
2. Select your answers for each question based on your experiences and views.
3. You may skip fields that are not relevant to your situation.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments or Suggestions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent/Guardian Name**: Please fill in your name as the parent or guardian of the child.
* **Child Name**: Enter your child's name to help us understand their learning experience.
* **Email Address**: Provide your email address to contact you for any further questions or discussions.
* **How Effective Was Home Learning for Your Child?**: Select one of the options to describe how effective you found the home learning experience for your child.
* **Main Challenges With Home Learning**: Select all that apply from the list of challenges you faced during home learning.
* **Level of Support You Provided With Learning**: Choose one of the options to describe your level of involvement with your child's learning.
* **Child's Motivation During Home Learning**: Select one of the options to describe your child's motivation level during home learning.
* **Comfort Level With School Reopening**: Choose one of the options to express your comfort level with school reopening.
* **Health and Safety Concerns**: If you have any health and safety concerns, please provide more information in this field.
* **Outlook on Child's Academic Progress**: Select one of the options to describe your outlook on your child's academic progress.
* **Does Your Child Need Additional Tutoring or Support?**: Select one of the options to answer whether your child needs additional support.
* **Areas Needing Support**: Select all that apply from the list of areas your child needs support in.
* **Special Accommodations or Services Needed**: If your child requires any special accommodations or services, please provide more information in this field.
* **Expectations for In-School Learning**: Describe your expectations for your child's in-school learning experience.
* **Preferred Method of Teacher Communication**: Select one of the options to describe your preferred method of communication with teachers.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

# Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when providing your feedback.
* If you're unsure about any of the options, choose the one that best describes your situation.
* You may skip fields that are not relevant to your situation.
* If you have any questions, don't hesitate to contact us.
