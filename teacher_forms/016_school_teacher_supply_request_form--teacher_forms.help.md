# School Teacher Supply Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Teacher Supply Request Form is used for teachers to request supplies for their classrooms or schools. This form collects information about the supply request, teacher and classroom details, submission details, and submitter details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Supplies Needed" to indicate if supplies are required.
2. If "Supplies Needed" is "Yes", select the "Teacher ID" and "Classroom ID" fields to identify the teacher and classroom requesting the supplies.
3. Provide additional information in the "Additional Information" field to elaborate on the supply request.
4. Enter the date and time of submission in the "Date Submitted" and "Time Submitted" fields, respectively.
5. Enter the "Form ID" and "Form Version" fields to uniquely identify the form and its version.
6. Select the "Status" field to indicate the current status of the supply request (Active or Inactive).
7. If submitting on behalf of someone else, enter the "Submitter Name", "Submitter Position", "Submitter Email", and "Submitter Phone" fields to identify the submitter.
8. Enter the "Submitter Comments" field to provide any additional comments or explanations.
9. Enter the "School", "Department", "Principal", and "Assistant" fields to provide school and departmental context.
10. Enter any additional comments in the "Form Comments" field.
11. Enter the "Submitter IP Address" and "Submitter User Agent" fields to track submission details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplies Needed**: Check if supplies are required for the classroom or school. If "Yes", proceed with the rest of the form.
* **Teacher ID**: Select the teacher ID to identify the teacher requesting the supplies.
* **Classroom ID**: Select the classroom ID to identify the classroom requesting the supplies.
* **Additional Information**: Provide any additional information about the supply request.
* **Date Submitted**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Time Submitted**: Enter the time of submission.
* **Form ID**: Uniquely identify the form.
* **Form Version**: Enter the version of the form.
* **Status**: Select the current status of the supply request (Active or Inactive).
* **Submitter Name**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form, if submitting on behalf of someone else.
* **Submitter Position**: Enter the position of the person submitting the form, if submitting on behalf of someone else.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter the email of the person submitting the form, if submitting on behalf of someone else.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form, if submitting on behalf of someone else.
* **Submitter Comments**: Enter any additional comments or explanations from the submitter.
* **School**: Enter the name of the school where the supply request is made.
* **Department**: Enter the department where the supply request is made.
* **Principal**: Enter the principal's name where the supply request is made.
* **Assistant**: Enter the assistant's name where the supply request is made.
* **Form Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the form.
* **Submitter IP Address**: Enter the IP address of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter User Agent**: Enter the user agent of the person submitting the form.
