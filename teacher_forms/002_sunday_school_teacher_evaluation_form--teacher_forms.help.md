# Sunday School Teacher Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sunday School Teacher Evaluation Form is a tool used to assess the effectiveness of Sunday School teachers. It gathers feedback on the teacher's teaching methods, materials, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer honestly based on your experience.
2. Select the most relevant option(s) from the multiple-choice questions (Questions 2 and 3).
3. For Question 1, the 'introduction' page, simply read the information provided, as there are no fields to fill out.
4. In Question 5 (teacher_comments), provide a detailed written response to the question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sunday School Teacher Evaluation Form 1 (introduction)** (`note`, no answer required): Read this section carefully as it contains general information about the form and its purpose.
* **How Well Did Your Students Listen?** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate if your students listened attentively or not during your lessons. Choose one or more of the options that best describe your experience.
* **How Effective Was Your Lesson?** (`select_one`, required): Choose the option that best describes how effective your lesson was.
* **What Subject Area** (`select_one`, required): Choose the subject area you taught.
* **Teacher Comments** (`text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about your lesson or teaching methods.

## Tips
* Answer the questions based on your honest experience and observation.
* Be as specific as possible when answering Question 5 (teacher_comments).
* If you're unsure about any of the options, choose the one that best fits your experience.
