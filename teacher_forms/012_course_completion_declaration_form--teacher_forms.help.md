# Course Completion Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Course Completion Declaration Form is used to declare the completion of a course for a student. This form is typically used by students to notify the training team that they have completed a course.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Review the form before submitting it to ensure that everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Identifier** (`course_identifier`, text, optional): This field is used to uniquely identify the course. If you are unsure of the course identifier, please refer to your course materials or speak with your instructor.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): This field is used to capture the student's name.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, optional): This field is used to record the start date of the program.
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, date, optional): This field is used to record the end date of the program.
* **Course Completion Date** (`course_completion_date`, date, optional): This field is used to record the date the course was completed.
* **Training Program Name** (`training_program_name`, text, optional): This field is used to capture the name of the training program.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): This field is used to capture the student's ID.
* **Course Title** (`course_title`, text, optional): This field is used to capture the title of the course.
* **Course Status** (`course_status`, select_multiple, true): This field is used to select the current status of the course.
	+ Choose **Active** if the course is currently active.
	+ Choose **In Progress** if the course is still in progress.
	+ Choose **Completed** if the course is complete.
	+ Choose **Withdrawn** if the course has been withdrawn.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, number, optional): This field is used to capture the student's grade in the course.
* **Hours Completed** (`hours_completed`, number, optional): This field is used to record the hours completed in the course.
* **Completion Date** (`completion_date`, date, optional): This field is used to record the date the course was completed.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, select_one, true): This field is used to select the location where the training took place.
* **Training Facilitator** (`training_facilitator`, select_one, true): This field is used to select the facilitator who led the training.
* **Course Notes** (`course_notes`, text, optional): This field is used to capture any additional notes about the course.
* **Certificate Number** (`certificate_number`, text, optional): This field is used to capture the certificate number associated with the course.

## Tips

* Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are filled out.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please refer to your course materials or speak with your instructor.
* Make sure to enter accurate dates in the date fields.
