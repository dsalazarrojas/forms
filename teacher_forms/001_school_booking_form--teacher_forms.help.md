# School Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Booking Form is a digital tool designed to collect information for school booking requests. Its primary purpose is to gather essential details from teachers, allowing the school administration to efficiently manage bookings and optimize facility utilization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, follow these steps:

1.  **Complete the Booking Details** (Field 1)
	* This section captures the fundamental details of the booking request.
	* Enter the required information, such as the booking purpose and date.
2.  **Enter Student Information** (Field 2)
	* Provide information about the students involved in the booking, including names and relevant details.
3.  **Select Facility** (Field 3)
	* Choose the facilities that you wish to book, indicating whether they should be included or not.
4.  **Enter Date and Time** (Field 4)
	* Provide the date and time for the booking request.
5.  **Enter Teacher's Name** (Field 5)
	* Enter the name of the teacher who submitted the form.
6.  **Message** (Field 6)
	* Add any additional information or comments related to the booking request.
7.  **Submit** (Field 7)
	* Confirm that the form is complete and submit it for review.
8.  **School Booking Form ID** (Field 8)
	* Review the generated form ID, which will be used for reference and tracking.
9.  **Assigned Tool** (Field 9)
	* Select whether a specific tool is assigned to the booking.
10. **Created By** (Field 10)
	* Displays the teacher's name who created the form.
