# Math Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Math Survey is a form designed to collect information about your mathematical abilities and interests. This information will help us better understand the strengths and weaknesses of our teaching staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Math Survey, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective text fields.
2. Select your Math Ability from the dropdown menu (Strong, Average, or Weak).
3. Choose your Math Interest(s) from the multiple-select list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is typically spelled.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is typically spelled.
* **Math Ability**: Select Strong if you are very confident in your mathematical abilities, Average if you are average, and Weak if you are not confident in your math skills.
* **Math Interest**: Select all the math subjects that interest you, such as Algebra, Geometry, or Calculus.
