# Tax Preparer Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Preparer Agreement Form is used for both tax preparers and clients to agree on the scope of services for tax preparation and other related activities. This form helps ensure a clear understanding of the services provided and accepted by the client.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the Service Agreement section to select the services you wish to perform for the client.
2. Enter your Tax Preparer Name and any additional comments.
3. Provide the client's Client Name and any additional comments.
4. Select the Tax Year for which the services are applicable.
5. If necessary, enter the client's Tax ID.
6. Choose the Agreement Status to indicate the form's current status.
7. Select the type of Tax Return services you will perform for the client (if applicable).
8. Finally, date and sign the form to complete it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (Client Information, text, optional):
	+ A field to enter information about the client.
* **Service Agreement** (Service Agreement, select multiple, optional):
	+ Select the services you will provide for the client, including Client Information, Tax Preparation, and Financial Planning.
* **Tax ID** (Tax ID, number, optional):
	+ Enter the client's tax identification number (if required).
* **Tax Year** (Tax Year, select one, optional):
	+ Select the tax year for which you will provide services to the client (e.g. '2018', '2019', '2020').
* **Tax Preparer Name** (Tax Preparer Name, text, optional):
	+ Enter your name as the tax preparer.
* **Client Name** (Client Name, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the client.
* **Signature Date** (Signature Date, date, optional):
	+ Date and time you sign the agreement.
* **Signature Time** (Signature Time, time, optional):
	+ Time you sign the agreement.
* **Preparer Comments** (Preparer Comments, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional comments from the tax preparer.
* **Client Comments** (Client Comments, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional comments from the client.
* **Agreement Status** (Agreement Status, select one, optional):
	+ Choose the status of the agreement, including Signed and Executed, In Progress, and Expired.
* **Tax Return** (Tax Return, select multiple, optional):
	+ Select the type of tax return services you will perform for the client, including Income Statement, Tax Declaration, and Other.
* **Agreement Status** is used to reflect the current status of the agreement.
* **Preparer Comments** and **Client Comments** can be used to enter any additional information or feedback.
* **Tax Year** is used to determine the scope of services for the selected tax year.
* **Tax Preparer Name** and **Client Name** are used to identify the individuals involved.
* **Tax ID** is used to identify the client's tax identification number (if required). 
* **Signature Date** and **Signature Time** are used to document the date and time of signing the agreement. 
* **Service Agreement** is used to select the services provided to the client.
