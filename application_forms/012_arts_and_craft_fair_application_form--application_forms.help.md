# Arts And Craft Fair Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Arts And Craft Fair Application Form is for vendors who want to participate in the fair, providing their contact and product information for consideration. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to increase your chances of being accepted.
## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer each question carefully, as it will be used to evaluate your application.
- Make sure to provide a valid email address and phone number.
- For the "List of Items for Sale" field, list each item separately, e.g., "item1, item2, item3".
- For the "Average Price Range of Items" field, enter a range, e.g., "10 to 50 USD".
- For the "Booth Size" field, select the size that best fits your needs.
- Review and proofread your application before submitting it.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Primary Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information for the person responsible for your application.
* **Mailing Address**: Enter your business or mailing address, including street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Phone Number**: Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **List of Items for Sale**: Enter a list of items you plan to sell, separated by commas.
* **Average Price Range of Items**: Enter the price range of your items, e.g., "10 to 50 USD".
* **Are all items 100 percent handmade by you**: Select "True" if all items are handmade by you, or "False" otherwise.
* **List any previous fairs you have participated in**: If you have participated in previous fairs, list them here.
* **Requested Booth Size**: Select the size of booth you need.
* **I release the organizers from any liability for loss or damage**: Select "I Accept" if you agree to this statement.
Note: This form is for application purposes only. Please do not send any payment information or other sensitive data. Review and agree to the fair's rules and regulations before submitting your application.
