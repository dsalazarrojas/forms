# CV Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for applicants to submit their application for a job or position within an organization. It collects relevant information about the applicant, including their contact details, work experience, education, and skills, to help the organization assess their qualifications and suitability for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your full legal name in the "Full Legal Name" field.
* Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field.
* Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
* If you have a LinkedIn profile, provide the URL in the "LinkedIn Profile URL" field (optional).
* Select the position you are applying for from the "Position Applied For" field.
* Choose how you found about this position from the "How did you hear about this position" field.
* Enter the number of years of work experience you have in the "Total Years of Work Experience" field.
* Select your highest degree earned from the "Highest Degree Earned" field.
* List your top 3 primary skills or expertise in the "Primary Skillset" field.
* Specify the software and tools you are proficient in the "Software and Tools Proficiency" field.
* Upload a CV/Resume document prepared for submission in the "CV/Resume document prepared for submission" field.
* Write a brief summary of why you are the best fit for the position in the "Cover Letter Summary" field.
* Enter your earliest available start date in the "Earliest Available Start Date" field.
* Finally, confirm that the information you have provided is true and accurate by selecting "True" in the "I certify that the information provided is true and accurate" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Legal Name** (`full_name`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Contact Phone** (`phone_number`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **LinkedIn Profile URL** (`linkedin_profile`, optional): If you have a LinkedIn profile, provide the URL.
* **Position Applied For** (`applied_position`, required): Select the position you are applying for.
* **How did you hear about this position** (`referral_source`, required): Choose how you found about this position.
* **Total Years of Work Experience** (`work_experience_years`, required): Enter the number of years of work experience you have.
* **Highest Degree Earned** (`highest_degree`, required): Select your highest degree earned.
* **Primary Skillset** (`primary_skillset`, required): List your top 3 primary skills or expertise.
* **Software and Tools Proficiency** (`software_proficiency`, required): Specify the software and tools you are proficient in.
* **CV/Resume document prepared for submission** (`cv_upload_confirmed`, required): Upload a CV/Resume document prepared for submission.
* **Cover Letter Summary** (`cover_letter_text`, required): Write a brief summary of why you are the best fit for the position.
* **Earliest Available Start Date** (`available_start_date`, required): Enter your earliest available start date.
* **I certify that the information provided is true and accurate** (`applicant_confirmation`, required): Confirm that the information you have provided is true and accurate.
