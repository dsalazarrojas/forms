# Auto Sales Customer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Auto Sales Customer Application Form is designed to collect information from potential customers about their vehicle needs and interests. This information will help us better understand their requirements and preferences, allowing us to provide personalized sales experiences. To avoid duplicated or irrelevant submissions, please note that if you have already been pre-approved for a loan, some sections of the form may be skipped.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name, email address, and phone number in the required fields.
2.  Select your preferred contact time and interested vehicle make and model.
3.  Choose your preferred vehicle condition (New, Used, or Any).
4.  Indicate whether you have a vehicle to trade-in and provide details about your current car.
5.  Enter your estimated total budget for the vehicle.
6.  Indicate whether you have already secured financing or are pre-approved for a loan.
7.  Select your credit score range.
8.  Enter any additional sales notes or preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Lead Qualification** (`lead_qualification_header`): This section serves as a header for the form.
*   **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`): Enter your full name to identify yourself.
*   **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your email address to receive vehicle information and quotes.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your best contact number for follow-up.
*   **Preferred Contact Time** (`preferred_contact_time`): Choose a time when you are available to be contacted.
*   **Vehicle Interest** (`vehicle_interest_header`): This section is a header for the next page.
*   **Interested Vehicle Make and Model** (`interested_vehicle`): Describe the type of vehicle you are looking for.
*   **Condition Preference** (`condition_preference`): Select your preferred vehicle state (New, Used, or Any).
*   **Trade-In Analysis** (`trade_in_analysis_header`): This section is a header for the next page.
*   **Do you plan to trade-in a vehicle?** (`plan_to_trade_in`): Select yes if you have a car to trade.
*   **Trade-In Details** (`trade_in_desc`): Enter details about your current car if you plan to trade-in.
*   **Trade-In Mileage** (`trade_in_mileage`): Enter the estimated mileage of your current car.
*   **Financing** (`financing_header`): This section is a header for the next page.
*   **Pre-approved for a loan?** (`pre_approved`): Indicate whether you already secured financing or are pre-approved.
*   **Credit Score Range** (`credit_score_range`): Select your credit score range for preliminary financing estimates.
*   **Additional Sales Notes** (`sales_notes`): Enter any additional requirements or preferences.
*   **Maximum Budget** (`maximum_budget`): Enter your estimated total budget for the vehicle.
