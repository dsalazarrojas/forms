# BossyBabe Hair And Lash Co Brand Ambassador Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The BossyBabe Hair And Lash Co Brand Ambassador Application form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in becoming brand ambassadors for our company. This form helps us assess their qualifications and suitability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your personal details, including your full name, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
2.  Share your Instagram and TikTok handles, if you have them, to help us connect with you better.
3.  Answer questions about your brand alignment, including your favorite BossyBabe products and your interest in sharing content.
4.  Select the frequency at which you can post brand content on your social media platforms.
5.  Briefly share an idea for a BossyBabe promotion.
6.  Agree to the Brand Ambassador Terms and Conditions.
7.  Finally, select a date for the application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`ambassador_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`ambassador_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Phone Number** (`ambassador_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Instagram Handle** (`ig_handle`, text, required): Enter your Instagram handle if you have one. Use the format `e.g. @yourname`.
* **TikTok Handle** (`tt_handle`, text, optional): Enter your TikTok handle if you have one.
* **Brand Alignment - Are you a current BossyBabe customer?** (`customer_status`, select_one, required): Select 'True' if you are currently a BossyBabe customer or 'False' otherwise.
* **Brand Alignment - Why do you want to be a BossyBabe Brand Ambassador?** (`reason_why`, text, required): Briefly explain why you want to represent our brand.
* **Favorite BossyBabe Products** (`fav_products`, select_multiple, required): Select all the BossyBabe products you enjoy using.
* **Content Creation - How often can you post brand content?** (`post_freq`, select_one, required): Choose the frequency at which you can post brand content on your social media platforms.
* **Content Creation - Briefly share an idea for a BossyBabe promotion** (`promo_idea`, text, required): Share a creative idea for promoting our brand.
* **Agreement - I agree to the Brand Ambassador Terms and Conditions** (`t_and_c_agree`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes - I agree' to confirm your understanding of the terms and conditions.
* **Date of Application** (`app_date`, date, required): Select the date you are submitting this application.
