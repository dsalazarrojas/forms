# Bookkeeping Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bookkeeping Client Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from bookkeeping clients to provide personalized bookkeeping services tailored to their specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields (marked as `true`) to ensure our team can effectively understand your business operations and needs.
2. For optional fields (marked as `false`), you can choose to provide additional information if you feel it's relevant to your bookkeeping requirements.
3. Use your best judgment when selecting options for fields like `business_structure` and `current_accounting_method` as they might not be exhaustive, and you can always add more details in the `additional_notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Business Name** (1)
* Label: Business Name
* Type: text
* Required: true
* Explanation: Enter the legal name of your business as it appears on your business registration or tax documents.

### **Contact Person Name** (2)
* Label: Contact Person Name
* Type: text
* Required: true
* Explanation: Enter your full name to contact you directly for any inquiries or updates.

### **Email Address** (3)
* Label: Email Address
* Type: email
* Required: true
* Explanation: Enter your business or personal email address where you can be reached for updates or notifications.

### **Phone Number** (4)
* Label: Phone Number
* Type: text
* Required: true
* Explanation: Provide your best contact number where our team can reach you for any urgent matters.

### **Business Structure** (5)
* Label: Business Structure
* Type: select_one
* Required: true
* Explanation: Select the type of business entity you operate (e.g., Sole Proprietship, Partnership, LLC, etc.).

### **Years in Business** (6)
* Label: Years in Business
* Type: number
* Required: true
* Explanation: Indicate how long your business has been operational.

### **Monthly Transaction Volume** (7)
* Label: Monthly Transaction Volume
* Type: select_one
* Required: true
* Explanation: Approximate the number of transactions your business handles in a month.

### **Current Accounting Method** (8)
* Label: Current Accounting Method
* Type: select_one
* Required: true
* Explanation: Describe how you currently track your finances (e.g., manual spreadsheets, accounting software, etc.).

### **Accounting Software Used** (9)
* Label: Accounting Software Used
* Type: select_one
* Required: false
* Explanation: If applicable, specify the accounting software you're currently using for bookkeeping.

### **Current Financial Challenges** (10)
* Label: Current Financial Challenges
* Type: select_multiple
* Required: true
* Explanation: Select any challenges you're currently facing with your finances (e.g., disorganized records, cash flow issues, etc.).

### **Specific Bookkeeping Needs** (11)
* Label: Specific Bookkeeping Needs
* Type: select_multiple
* Required: true
* Explanation: Indicate which bookkeeping services you require (e.g., general bookkeeping, payroll processing, etc.).

### **Monthly Budget Range** (12)
* Label: Monthly Budget Range
* Type: select_one
* Required: false
* Explanation: Specify your approximate monthly budget allocation.

### **Number of Employees** (13)
* Label: Number of Employees
* Type: number
* Required: false
* Explanation: If applicable, provide the number of people working for your business.

### **Next Tax Deadline** (14)
* Label: Next Tax Deadline
* Type: date
* Required: false
* Explanation: If applicable, indicate the date of your next tax filing deadline.

### **Additional Information** (15)
* Label: Additional Information
* Type: text
* Required: false
* Explanation: Provide any other relevant information our team should know about your bookkeeping requirements.
