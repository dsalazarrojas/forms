# Webinar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees for a webinar. It's a simple, AI-generated form that helps us understand who's attending and what they're interested in learning about.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the webinar name in the "Webinar Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "email" field. Make sure to use a valid email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Webinar Name** (`webinar_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the webinar you're interested in attending.
  This will help us keep track of which webinars are being attended and by whom.
- **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address.
  This is required for our records and so we can follow up with you after the webinar.
  Please make sure to enter a valid email address that you have access to.
- **attendee_name** (`attendee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
  This will allow us to address you properly during the webinar and after.
  Please provide your full name as you would like to be addressed.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter a valid email address for communication and follow-up purposes.
- You can always contact us if you have any questions or need further assistance.
- If you're unable to attend the webinar, please let us know so we can update our records.
- We'll be using the information you provide for internal purposes only and will not share it with third parties.
