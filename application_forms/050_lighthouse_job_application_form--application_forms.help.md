# Lighthouse Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lighthouse Job Application Form is a tool used to gather essential information from job applicants. It helps the Lighthouse team assess their qualifications, skills, and experience to determine the best fit for potential roles within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Lighthouse Job Application Form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the form on your computer or mobile device.
2. Enter your contact information in the relevant fields (Contact Email, Contact Phone).
3. Provide your current job status (Available for hire, Onboarding Process, On leave).
4. List your relevant job skills.
5. Share your relevant work and Lighthouse experience.
6. Submit your application by clicking the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Job Location
* **Job Location**: Enter the location where you are applying for a job, e.g., a specific city or region.

### 2. Job Title
* **Job Title**: Enter the title of the job you are applying for, e.g., "Software Engineer" or "Marketing Manager".

### 3. Contact Email
* **Contact Email**: Enter your professional email address where you can be reached for further communication.

### 4. Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached for further communication.

### 5. Job Status
* **Job Status**: Select one or more of the following options:
	+ **Available for hire**: You are available to take on new roles.
	+ **Onboarding Process**: You are currently in the onboarding process for another role.
	+ **On leave**: You are currently not available for new roles due to prior commitments or leave.

### 6. Job Skills
* **Job Skills**: List any relevant skills you possess that are related to the job you are applying for, e.g., programming languages, data analysis, or marketing strategies.

### 7. Lighthouse Experience
* **Lighthouse Experience**: Share any relevant experience you have gained while working with Lighthouse or in a similar field.

### 8. Work Experience
* **Work Experience**: List any relevant work experience you have gained in the past, including job titles, industries, and job responsibilities.

### 9. Submit
* **Submit**: Click the submit button to send your application for review.
