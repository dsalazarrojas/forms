# Cat Adoption Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from applicants who are interested in adopting a cat from our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, making sure to provide accurate and honest answers.
2. Answer each question based on your personal experiences and lifestyle.
3. Take your time to complete the form, as it is essential for us to have accurate information about you and your household.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Your full name as it appears on your official documents, such as a driver's license or passport.
* **Email Address**: Your personal email address where we can contact you about your adoption application and any updates about your potential new cat.
* **Phone Number**: Your primary phone number where we can reach you about your adoption application.
* **Residential Address**: Your current street address, city, state, and zip code, including apartment or house number.
* **Do you own or rent your home?**: Choose "Own" if you own your home, "Rent" if you rent, or "Live with parents" if you live with family members.
* **If renting, do you have landlord permission for a cat?**: If you are a renter, indicate if you have permission from your landlord to have a cat.
* **Number of people in the household**: Enter the total number of people living in your household.
* **Are there children in the home?**: Indicate if there are children living in your household.
* **Do you currently have other pets?**: If yes, list the species and ages of the pets you have.
* **Have you owned cats before?**: Choose if you have previous experience with cat ownership.
* **Current or Future Veterinarian**: If you have a current or future veterinarian, please list their clinic name and phone number.
* **Name of the cat you are interested in**: If you have a specific cat in mind, please enter its name. Otherwise, you can enter "General" if you are not sure.
* **Why do you want to adopt a cat at this time?**: Share your reasons for wanting to adopt a cat.
* **I understand that a cat is a long-term commitment (15-20 years)**: Please confirm that you understand the commitment involved in adopting a cat and are willing to care for it for its entire lifespan.
