# Discord Staff Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Discord Staff Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants interested in joining the Discord team. It's the first step in the application process for Discord staff positions. This form helps the staff team to determine your suitability for the role and gather necessary details to assess your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, please carefully review each field and follow the instructions provided. Here's a step-by-step guide:

1. Answer the "What type of server are you applying for?" question with one of the options provided. 
   - If you're applying for a specific role or position, select the corresponding server type.

2. Enter your server name in the "Server Name" field if you're applying for a specific server or have a preferred server name.

3. Choose a role you're applying for by selecting one of the options in the "What role are you applying for?" field.

4. Select the level of experience you have with Discord from the options in the "What level of experience do you have with Discord?" field.

5. Enter your contact email in the "Contact Email" field for us to reach out to you if necessary.

6. Provide your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field, if available.

7. Indicate your availability for Discord by checking the relevant options in the "What is your availability for Discord?" field.

8. Describe your availability in more detail in the "Availability Details" field if needed.

9. Enter your Discord username in the "Discord Name" field if you have one.

10. Enter your Discord tag in the "Discord Tag" field if you have one.

11. Write a brief explanation in the "Why do you want to join Discord?" field to help us understand your interest in joining the team.

12. Provide further details about why you want to join Discord in the "Why do you want to join Discord Details" field if necessary.

13. Check the "References" box if you have relevant references.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What type of server are you applying for?** (Select one of the options):
  - This question helps us determine for which server type you're applying. Please select the option that best matches your application.
* **Server Name** (Text field):
  - Enter the name of the server you're applying for or the name of the server you're interested in joining.
* **What role are you applying for?** (Select one):
  - Choose the role you're applying for from the provided options.
* **What level of experience do you have with Discord?** (Select one):
  - Choose the level of experience you have with Discord.
* **Contact Email** (Text field):
  - Enter your email address so we can contact you for further information.
* **Contact Phone** (Text field):
  - Provide your phone number for potential further communication.
* **What is your availability for Discord?** (Select multiple):
  - Indicate your availability for Discord by selecting the relevant options.
* **Availability Details** (Text field):
  - Provide further details about your availability if necessary.
* **Discord Name** (Text field):
  - Enter your Discord username if you have one.
* **Discord Tag** (Text field):
  - Enter your Discord tag if you have one.
* **Why do you want to join Discord?** (Text field):
  - Write a brief explanation of why you want to join Discord.
* **Why do you want to join Discord Details** (Text field):
  - Provide further details about why you want to join Discord.
* **References** (Select multiple):
  - Check this box if you have relevant references.

By following this guide, you'll be able to submit a comprehensive and accurate application for a Discord staff position. Please don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or concerns about the application process.
