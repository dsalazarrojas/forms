# Discord Staff Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for staff members to submit their application for joining the Discord community's staff team. Please note that this form is not a substitute for an in-person interview or phone call, and we reserve the right to request additional information or verification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: "Discord Username", "Email", and "Phone Number".
2. Optional fields are "New Staff Member", "Discord Tag", and "Email". You can fill them out if you have the necessary information.
3. Submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.
4. If you need help or have any issues, contact us and we'll be happy to assist you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **New Staff Member**: This field is an optional description of your role or responsibilities. Feel free to provide any additional information about yourself, such as previous experience or skills.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
  - Hint: null
- **Discord Username**: This field is the username you use to identify yourself on Discord. Please enter your real username.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true
  - Hint: Your actual Discord username
- **Discord Tag**: This field is an optional tag you would like to display next to your username on Discord. Please be brief and concise.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
  - Hint: A short nickname or abbreviation
- **Email**: This field is your email address where we can contact you. Please ensure it is up-to-date and correct.
  - Type: email
  - Required: true
  - Hint: Your email address (example: john.doe@example.com)
- **Phone Number**: This field is your phone number where we can contact you. Please provide a valid number.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true
  - Hint: Your phone number (example: 123-456-7890)
