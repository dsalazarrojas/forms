# W9 Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The W9 Form is a tax form used to report certain types of income, such as non-employee compensation. It is used by the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) to collect personal information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the W9 Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the first name field, which is required.
2. Enter your surname in the last name field, which is required.
3. Enter your email address in the email field, which is optional.
4. Enter your residential or business address in the address field, which is optional.
5. Enter the city where you live or work in the city field, which is optional.
6. Select your state of residence or business location from the state field, which is optional.
7. Enter your zip code in the zip field, which is optional.
8. Enter the date of the W9 Form in the date field, which is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (first_name, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (last_name, text, required): Enter your surname as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Address** (address, text, optional): Enter your residential or business address.
* **City** (city, text, optional): Enter the city where you live or work.
* **State** (state, select_one, optional): Select your state of residence or business location.
* **Zip** (zip, number, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Date** (w9_date, date, optional): Enter the date of the W9 Form.
