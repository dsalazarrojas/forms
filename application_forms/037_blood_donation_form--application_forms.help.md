# Blood Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to register as blood donors, providing personal information and medical history to determine eligibility for donation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form by providing your personal information, including first and last name, email address, phone number, and street address.
2. Enter your date of birth to confirm you are at least 17 years old.
3. Select your gender for medical records.
4. Enter your weight in pounds and height in feet and inches.
5. If you have any prior donation history, select "Yes" and provide the date of your last donation.
6. If applicable, select your blood type and any chronic medical conditions, current medications, and known allergies.
7. Indicate whether you have been recently ill, traveled outside the country, or have recent tattoos or piercings.
8. Select your preferred donation location and availability.
9. Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact person.
10. Finally, indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions.
