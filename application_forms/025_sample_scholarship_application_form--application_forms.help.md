# Sample Scholarship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sample Scholarship Application Form is a tool for students and individuals to provide detailed information about themselves for scholarship applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the applicant information section (page 1) with your personal details.
2. Provide contact information on the contact page (page 2).
3. Enter your academic background on the academic info page (page 3).
4. Choose from the scholarship programs you are applying for (page 4).
5. Select the type of scholarship you are applying for (page 5).
6. Choose the status of your application (page 6).
7. Enter any information about the scholarship committee (page 7).
8. Enter the contact information for the committee (page 8).
9. Select the date you submitted your application (page 9).
10. Choose the time of submission (page 10).
11. Add any additional notes about your application (page 11).
12. Sign the form with your signature (page 12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Info** (Page 1, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for your personal details such as name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Contact Info** (Page 2, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Academic Info** (Page 3, text, required: false)
	+ Provide your academic background, including relevant courses, degrees, and institutions.
* **Scholarship Program Info** (Page 4, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Choose the scholarship programs you are applying for.
* **Scholarship Type** (Page 5, select_one, required: false)
	+ Select the type of scholarship you are applying for, such as need-based or merit-based.
* **Application Status** (Page 6, select_one, required: false)
	+ Choose the status of your application, such as new, updated, or approved.
* **Scholarship Committee** (Page 7, text, required: false)
	+ Enter any information about the scholarship committee, including their name and contact details.
* **Committee Contact** (Page 8, email, required: false)
	+ Enter the contact information for the committee.
* **Application Date** (Page 9, date, required: false)
	+ Select the date you submitted your application.
* **Application Time** (Page 10, time, required: false)
	+ Choose the time you submitted your application.
* **Note** (Page 11, note, required: false)
	+ Add any additional notes about your application.
* **Applicant Signature** (Page 12, text, required: false)
	+ Sign the form with your signature.

## Tips

* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Make sure to choose the correct scholarship programs and type for your application.
* Enter all required information for the committee.
* Proofread your application for any mistakes before submitting it.
* Keep a record of your application ID for future reference.
