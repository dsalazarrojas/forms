# Home Loan Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to provide a detailed and accurate view of the loan applicant's information. It's a comprehensive tool for the lender to assess the applicant's creditworthiness and determine the suitability of the loan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your borrower information, including your name, date of birth, and other relevant details.
2. Next, provide your employment information, including your employment start date and credit score.
3. Fill in the loan details, including the loan amount, term, and interest rate.
4. Specify the type of property you wish to purchase or refinance.
5. Finally, review your information and ensure that all fields are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Borrower Info (1)** (text, optional): This field is used to collect your name and other identifying information. Please provide your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Loan Details (2)** (text, optional): This field is used to capture the specifics of the loan, including the amount, term, and interest rate.
* **Employment Info (3)** (text, optional): This field is used to gather information about your employment history, including your employment start date.
* **Income Info (4)** (text, optional): This field is used to collect information about your income, although it's currently not being used for any calculations.
* **Loan Amount (5)** (number, optional): This field is used to specify the amount you wish to borrow.
* **Loan Term (6)** (number, optional): This field is used to select the loan term in years.
* **Property Value (7)** (number, optional): This field is used to specify the value of the property you wish to purchase or refinance.
* **Loan Type (8)** (select one, optional): This field is used to select the type of loan, which can be Fixed, Variable, or Adjustable.
* **Loan Term in Years (9)** (select multiple, optional): This field is used to select the loan term in years for the chosen loan type.
* **Loan Interest Rate (10)** (select one, optional): This field is used to select the interest rate for the loan.
* **Property Type (11)** (select one, optional): This field is used to select the type of property you wish to purchase or refinance.
* **Date of Birth (12)** (date, optional): This field is used to collect your date of birth.
* **Loan Date (13)** (date, optional): This field is used to collect the date of the loan.
* **Employment Start Date (14)** (date, optional): This field is used to collect your employment start date.
* **Credit Score (15)** (number, optional): This field is used to collect your credit score.
* **Property Type 2 (16)** (select one, optional): This field is used to select the type of property you wish to purchase or refinance.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are accurate and complete.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the lender for guidance.
* Filling out this form will help the lender assess your creditworthiness and determine the suitability of the loan.
