# Volunteer Application Form For Non Profit - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from individuals interested in volunteering for a non-profit organization, enabling the organization to understand their qualifications and preferences, and matching them with suitable volunteer roles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and email address.
2. Enter your phone number and address.
3. Indicate your availability for volunteering and preferred volunteer role.
4. Select your preferred volunteer shift.
5. Specify how many hours you are willing to volunteer per week.
6. Confirm your agreement to volunteer.
7. Optionally, provide any additional comments or explanations in the "Application Submission" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Non Profit Organization** (Required: false, Type: text, Label: Non Profit Organization): This field is a text field where you can enter the name of the non-profit organization you are applying to.
  * No explanation is required for this field as it is optional.
- **Name** (Required: false, Type: text, Label: Name): This field is for your name. 
  * Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
- **Email** (Required: false, Type: email, Label: Email): This field is for your email address.
  * Please use a valid email address that is checked regularly for any updates or notifications.
- **Phone** (Required: false, Type: text, Label: Phone): This field is for your phone number.
  * Please enter a valid phone number that we can use to contact you.
- **Date of Birth** (Required: false, Type: date, Label: Date of Birth): This field is for your date of birth.
  * Please enter your date of birth in the format of month/day/year (MM/DD/YYYY).
- **Address** (Required: false, Type: text, Label: Address): This field is for your address.
  * Please enter a valid address where you can be reached for volunteering purposes.
- **Volunteer Role** (Required: false, Type: select_one, Label: Volunteer Role): This field is for selecting your preferred volunteer role.
  * Please choose the role that best fits your skills and preferences.
- **Volunteer Shift** (Required: false, Type: time, Label: Volunteer Shift): This field is for selecting your preferred volunteer shift.
  * Please choose the shift that best fits your schedule and preferences.
- **Volunteer Hours per Week** (Required: false, Type: number, Label: Volunteer Hours per Week): This field is for specifying how many hours you are willing to volunteer per week.
  * Please enter a number that best represents your actual availability.
- **Agree** (Required: false, Type: select_multiple, Label: Agree): This field is for agreeing to volunteer.
  * Please select the option that indicates your willingness to volunteer.
- **Our Non Profit** (Required: false, Type: text, Label: Our Non Profit): This field is optional and for non-profit specific information.
  * You can use this field to provide additional information specific to the non-profit organization.
- **Volunteer Start Date** (Required: false, Type: date, Label: Volunteer Start Date): This field is for specifying the start date of your volunteering.
  * Please enter a date in the format of month/day/year (MM/DD/YYYY).
- **Application Submission** (Required: false, Type: note, Label: Application Submission): This field is optional and for any additional comments or explanations.
  * Please use this field to provide any additional information that may be helpful in processing your application.
- **Agreement** (Required: false, Type: select_one, Label: Agreement): This field is for agreeing to the terms of volunteering.
  * Please select the option that indicates your agreement.
