# Training Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals who want to apply for training programs. Please ensure that you have not already submitted an application and that you have a valid reason for applying for the training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Provide your participant name and contact email address in the respective fields.
2. Enter a valid email address for the participant in the "Participant Email" field.
3. Choose the type of training you are applying for by selecting the appropriate option in the "Training Type" field.
4. Enter the date you are available to attend the training in the "Training Date" field.
5. Enter the time you are available to attend the training in the "Training Time" field.
6. Enter your participant address in the "Participant Address" field.
7. Add any additional notes about your application in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it is written on your official government-issued identification.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further information.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter a valid phone number for the participant.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address for the participant.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter a valid phone number for the participant.
* **Training Type** (`training_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of training you are applying for (Course, Workshop, Other).
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you are available to attend the training.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you are available to attend the training.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your participant's address.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your application.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the email addresses you enter are valid and up-to-date.
* Make sure to select the correct training type that applies to your needs.
* Be accurate when entering your address and participant phone number.
