# Rental Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from potential tenants to assess their qualifications and preferences for a rental property. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to provide the best possible representation of yourself as a tenant.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form with your basic contact information, including your name and email address.
2. Enter your phone number so that the landlord or property manager can contact you for further information.
3. Provide your current address to help the landlord or property manager assess your rental history.
4. Indicate the date you would like to rent the property, as well as the start and end dates of the intended lease.
5. If you have a preferred move-in date, enter it. If not, you can leave this field blank.
6. If you have a preferred move-out date, enter it. If not, you can leave this field blank.
7. Enter the duration of your stay in the property, in days.
8. Finally, enter the amount of rent you are willing to pay.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your personal email address that you regularly use.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your current address to provide context for your rental history.
* **Rent Date** (`rent_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to start renting the property.
* **Rent Start Date** (`rent_start_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to start your rental agreement.
* **Rent End Date** (`rent_end_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to end your rental agreement.
* **Move In Date** (`move_in_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to move into the property.
* **Move Out Date** (`move_out_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to move out of the property.
* **Move In Duration** (`move_in_duration`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of days you plan to stay in the property.
* **Move Out Duration** (`move_out_duration`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of days you plan to stay in the property.
* **Rent Amount** (`rent_amount`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the amount of rent you are willing to pay.
