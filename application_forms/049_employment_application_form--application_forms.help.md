# Employment Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for employment applications, where you can provide your personal details, contact information, skills, education, experience, and other relevant information to a potential employer or recruiter.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your personal details such as name, email, and any other relevant personal information.
2. Provide your contact information, including address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.
3. Share your availability and any specific dates or times you are available for interviews or meetings.
4. Choose any relevant skills that you possess from the dropdown list.
5. Briefly describe your education and any relevant qualifications.
6. Share your work experience and any notable achievements or projects you've completed.
7. List your referees' names and contact information, if applicable.
8. Attach any relevant documents or files that support your application.
9. Enter the name of the tool or platform that is assigned for this application (if applicable).
10. Verify your full name as it will appear on the submitted form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details** (text): Enter your personal name.
* **Contact Information** (text): Provide your address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Availability** (text): Share your availability and any specific dates or times you are available for interviews or meetings.
* **Skills** (select multiple): Choose any relevant skills that you possess from the dropdown list.
* **Education** (text): Briefly describe your education and any relevant qualifications.
* **Experience** (text): Share your work experience and any notable achievements or projects.
* **Referees** (text): List your referees' names and contact information, if applicable.
* **Uploaded Documents** (text): Attach any relevant documents or files that support your application.
* **Submitted By** (text): This field is for internal use only.
* **Submitted On** (date): This field will automatically be populated with the date and time you submit this form.
* **Assigned Tool** (text): Enter the name of the tool or platform assigned for this application (if applicable).
* **Submitted By Full name** (text): Verify your full name as it will appear on the submitted form.
