# Illuminati Join Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Illuminati Join Application Form is a crucial tool for potential members to submit their applications and for our team to verify their eligibility. This form gathers essential information about you, such as your name, contact details, and preferred role within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Provide your Nickname (optional).
4. Enter your Email address (ensure it's valid).
5. Enter your Phone number.
6. Enter your Discord Tag.
7. Enter your Discord ID (numbers only).
8. Explain your reason for joining the Illuminati.
9. Select your preferred role within the Illuminati (choose from the provided options or specify "Other" if not listed).
10. If "Other" is selected for your role, provide a brief description in the optional "Other (please specify)" field.
11. Read and agree to the terms of service (select all applicable options).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Required)**: Enter your real name, as it appears on your ID.
* **Last Name (Required)**: Enter your surname, as it appears on your ID.
* **Nickname (Optional)**: A nickname you'd like to be known by in the Illuminati.
* **Email (Required)**: Enter a valid email address to which we can contact you.
* **Phone (Required)**: Enter a valid phone number to which we can contact you.
* **Discord Tag (Required)**: Enter your Discord username, including the tag.
* **Discord ID (Required)**: Enter your unique Discord ID number.
* **Reason for Joining Illuminati (Required)**: Describe your motivation for applying to join the Illuminati.
* **Role in Illuminati (Required)**: Choose your preferred role within the organization from the provided options or select "Other" and provide a description in the next field.
* **Other (please specify) (Optional)**: If you selected "Other" for your role, provide a brief description here.
* **Agree to Terms (Required)**: Select all applicable options to demonstrate your understanding of, and agreement to, the terms of service.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled in accurately to avoid delays in processing your application.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to us via our support channels.
* Our team reviews applications carefully; we appreciate your honesty and attention to detail in completing this form.
