# Staff Leave Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Leave Application Form is designed to collect leave application information from staff members. This form is used by HR or managers to process leave requests and ensure smooth workflow continuity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the form instructions carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information required.
2. Fill in the form fields accurately and completely.
3. Submit your completed form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Leave Application** (`1`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of your leave request.
* **Leave Start Date** (`2`, `date`, optional): Enter the start date of your leave.
* **Leave End Date** (`3`, `date`, optional): Enter the end date of your leave.
* **Leave Duration** (`4`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of days you will be away.
* **Leave Reason** (`5`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief reason for your leave.
* **Employee ID** (`6`, `number`, optional): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Leave Status** (`7`, `select_one`, required): Select the current status of your leave application:
	+ **Pending**: Your leave application is being reviewed.
	+ **Approved**: Your leave has been approved.
	+ **Rejected**: Your leave has been rejected.
	+ **On Leave**: You are currently on leave.
* **Leave Type** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the type of leave you are applying for:
	+ **Sick Leave**
	+ **Vacation Leave**
	+ **Emergency Leave**
	+ **Other**
* **Comments** (`9`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes regarding your leave request.
* **File Upload 1** (`10`, `text`, optional): Upload any supporting files or documents related to your leave request.
* **File Upload 2** (`11`, `text`, optional): Upload any additional files or documents related to your leave request.
* **File Upload 3** (`12`, `text`, optional): Upload any more files or documents related to your leave request.
* **Submit** (`13`, `text`, optional): Click to submit your completed form for review and approval.
* **Cancel** (`14`, `text`, optional): Click to cancel your leave application form.
* **Back** (`15`, `text`, optional): Click to go back to the previous page.
* **Next** (`16`, `text`, optional): Click to go to the next page.
* **Previous** (`17`, `text`, optional): Click to go back to the previous page.
* **Page 1** (`18`, `text`, optional): This field is currently not used, please do not fill it in.
 
**Tips**: Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely to avoid delays in processing your leave application.
