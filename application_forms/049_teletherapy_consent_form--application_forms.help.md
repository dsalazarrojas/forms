# teletherapy_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The teletherapy_consent_form is used to gather information from patients for teletherapy services. It collects demographic information and confirms the patient's understanding of the teletherapy process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling in your first name and last name.
- Enter your email address and confirm it by typing it in the "Confirm Email" field.
- If you have any medical condition, provide a brief description in the "Medical Condition" field.
- Specify your therapy goals in the "Therapy Goals" field.
- Read and confirm your understanding of the teletherapy process in the "Informed Consent" field.
- Sign to confirm that you have read and understood the teletherapy process.
- Optionally, enter the date and time you are completing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Confirm Email** (email, required): Confirm your email address to ensure accuracy.
* **Medical Condition** (text, optional): If you have any medical condition, provide a brief description.
* **Therapy Goals** (text, optional): Specify your therapy goals.
* **Informed Consent** (select_one, required): Confirm your understanding of the teletherapy process.
* **Signed** (select_one, required): Sign to confirm that you have read and understood the teletherapy process.
* **Date** (date, optional): Enter the date you are completing the form.
* **Time** (time, optional): Enter the time you are completing the form.
