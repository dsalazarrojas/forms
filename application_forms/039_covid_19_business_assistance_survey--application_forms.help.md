# COVID 19 Business Assistance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The COVID 19 Business Assistance Survey is a questionnaire designed to gather information about businesses that have been impacted by the COVID-19 pandemic. The survey aims to identify areas where businesses need assistance and support to recover from the pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete the survey, follow the instructions below:
   - Answer each question carefully and accurately.
   - Select the most relevant options for each question.
   - Make sure to submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Enter your business's legal name.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the category of your business, such as Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, or Corporation.
* **Industry** (`industry`, `select_one`, required): Choose the primary industry of your business, such as Retail, Restaurant, or Healthcare.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, `text`, required): Enter your business's complete address.
* **City** (`business_city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where your business is located.
* **State** (`business_state`, `text`, required): Enter the state or province where your business is located.
* **ZIP Code** (`business_zip`, `text`, required): Enter your business's postal code.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the contact person.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, `text`, required): Enter the job title of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the business email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the business phone number.
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, `select_one`, required): Select how long your business has been in operation.
* **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, `select_one`, required): Select the total number of employees your business has.
* **Revenue Impact from COVID-19** (`revenue_impact`, `select_one`, required): Choose how your business's revenue has been impacted by the pandemic.
* **Revenue Decline Percentage** (`revenue_decline_percentage`, `select_one`, optional): If your revenue declined, select the percentage of decline.
* **Operational Changes Made** (`operational_changes`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the operational changes your business has made due to the pandemic, such as implementing remote work or reducing hours.
* **Employee Impact** (`employee_impact`, `select_one`, required): Choose how your business has been impacted by the pandemic in terms of staff.
* **Supply Chain Impact** (`supply_chain_impact`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of disruption to your business's supply chain.
* **Assistance Received** (`assistance_received`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the types of assistance your business has received, such as PPP loans or state grants.
* **Assistance Still Needed** (`assistance_needed`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the types of assistance your business still needs, such as financial grants or tax relief.
* **Expected Recovery Timeline** (`recovery_timeline`, `select_one`, required): Choose when you expect your business to recover from the pandemic.
* **Biggest Challenge** (`biggest_challenge`, `select_one`, required): Select the biggest challenge your business is facing due to the pandemic.
* **Challenge Details** (`challenge_details`, `text`, optional): Enter more details about the biggest challenge facing your business.
* **Technology Adoption** (`technology_adoption`, `select_one`, optional): Select any new technologies your business has adopted due to the pandemic, such as e-commerce platforms.
* **Future Business Outlook** (`future_outlook`, `select_one`, required): Choose your confidence level in your business's future.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information about your business.
* **Willing to be Contacted** (`willing_to_be_contacted`, `select_one`, required): Select if you are willing to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, `date`, required): Enter the current date.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer each question carefully and accurately.
* Select the most relevant options for each question.
* Enter the most complete and correct information for your business.
* Submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

Note: The survey is intended for businesses that have been in operation for at least 1 year and are willing to be contacted for follow-up. It is not intended for non-businesses or businesses that have not been impacted by the pandemic.
