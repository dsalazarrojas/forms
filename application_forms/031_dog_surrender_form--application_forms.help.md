# dog_surrender_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "dog_surrender_form" is a form designed to aid animal shelters or rescue organizations in the process of dog surrender. It is meant to collect essential information about the dog and its owner to ensure a smooth and efficient transfer of the pet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your name and email.
2. Enter the dog's name, breed, age, and weight.
3. Specify the dog's gender.
4. Explain the reason for surrendering the dog.
5. Enter your address, if applicable.
6. Share any relevant notes about the dog.
7. Select the responsible party (Owner, Rescue, or Shelter) who will be surrendering the dog.
8. Provide the date and time the dog is available for pick-up.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure the shelter or rescue organization has the necessary information to care for the dog.
* If you are surrendering the dog due to medical reasons, please provide veterinary records or information, if possible.
