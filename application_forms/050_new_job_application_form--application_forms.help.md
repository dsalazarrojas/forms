# New Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Job Application Form is designed for users to apply for job openings. It is intended to gather information from applicants regarding their job preferences, skills, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose your job application status by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown list.
2. Fill out your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
3. Enter your address for communication purposes.
4. Provide details about the job you are applying for, including the job title, job description, and expected salary.
5. Select the type of job you are looking for (e.g., full-time, part-time, internship).
6. Choose the status of your job application (e.g., pending, approved, rejected).
7. Select the date and time for the interview (if applicable).
8. Choose the status of your job application (e.g., pending, approved, rejected).
9. Select the category of the job you are applying for (e.g., software development, marketing, sales).
10. Choose the type of job you are looking for (e.g., management, non-management).
11. Select the level of the job you are applying for (e.g., entry-level, mid-level, senior-level).
12. Choose the industry of the job you are applying for (e.g., tech, finance, healthcare).
13. Select the function of the job you are applying for (e.g., programming, writing, designing).
14. Enter the name of the company you are applying to.
15. Enter the position title of the job you are applying for.
16. Enter a brief description of the job position.
17. Enter the location of the job position.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Job Application Form** (`job_application_form`, select_multiple, not required): Please select all that apply to indicate that you are applying for multiple job openings.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, not required): Enter your name, phone number, and email address to ensure we can contact you regarding your application.
- **Address** (`address`, text, not required): Provide your address for communication purposes.
- **Job Details** (`job_details`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the job you are applying for, including the job title and expected salary.
- **Resume** (`resume`, text, not required): Upload your resume to provide us with more information about your qualifications.
- **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, not required): Choose the type of job you are looking for (e.g., full-time, part-time, internship).
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, not required): Enter the title of the job you are applying for.
- **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the job position.
- **Job Salary** (`job_salary`, number, not required): Enter your expected salary for the job.
- **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, not required): Choose the status of your job application (e.g., pending, approved, rejected).
- **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, not required): Enter the date you submitted your application.
- **Interview Date** (`interview_date`, time, not required): If applicable, enter the date and time of your scheduled interview.
- **Application Status** (`application_status`, select_one, not required): Choose the status of your job application (e.g., pending, approved, rejected).
- **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, not required): Choose the status of your job (e.g., full-time, part-time, internship).
- **Job Category** (`job_category`, select_one, not required): Choose the category of the job you are applying for (e.g., software development, marketing, sales).
- **Job Type 2** (`job_type2`, select_one, not required): Choose the type of job you are looking for (e.g., management, non-management).
- **Job Level** (`job_level`, select_one, not required): Choose the level of the job you are applying for (e.g., entry-level, mid-level, senior-level).
- **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, not required): Enter the location of the job position.
- **Job Industry** (`job_industry`, select_one, not required): Choose the industry of the job you are applying for (e.g., tech, finance, healthcare).
- **Job Function** (`job_function`, select_multiple, not required): Choose the function of the job you are applying for (e.g., programming, writing, designing).
- **Job Position** (`job_position`, text, not required): Enter the name of the job position.
- **Job Company** (`job_company`, text, not required): Enter the name of the company you are applying to.
- **Job Position Title** (`job_position_title`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the job position title.
- **Job Position Description** (`job_position_description`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the job position.
- **Job Position Location** (`job_position_location`, text, not required): Enter the location of the job position.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure that your application is complete and accurate.
- Double-check your information before submitting your application.
- If you have any supporting materials, such as a resume or references, please attach them to your application.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
