# online-doctor-appointment-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Doctor Appointment Form is designed for patients to schedule appointments with healthcare providers at a practice. It is used to collect necessary information for assigning an available doctor to the patient based on their preference for a specific date and time.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the respective fields. These are required fields and must be completed before proceeding.
2. Select a date for your appointment. Please note that the date should be in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
3. Select a time for your appointment. Please note that the time should be in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM).
4. Enter the name of your preferred practice.
5. Specify the purpose of your appointment.
6. Select whether you have been assigned a practice or doctor.
7. If you have been assigned a practice, select whether you have access to the assigned practice's available doctors.
8. Select your preferred doctor from the list of available doctors.
9. If you have selected a doctor, confirm the date and time of your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address to be contacted for your appointment.
* **Date** (Date, date, required): Select the date for your appointment in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time** (Time, time, required): Select the time for your appointment in 24-hour format.
* **Practice Name** (Practice Name, text, optional): Enter the name of your preferred practice (if you have been assigned a practice).
* **Purpose of Appointment** (Purpose of Appointment, text, required): Specify the reason for your appointment.
* **Assigned Practice** (Assigned Practice, select_one, required): Select whether you have been assigned a practice or not.
* **Assigned Doctor** (Assigned Doctor, select_one, required): Select whether you have been assigned a doctor or not.
* **Available Doctors** (Available Doctors, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you have access to the assigned practice's available doctors.
* **Select Doctor** (Select Doctor, select_one, optional): Select your preferred doctor from the list of available doctors.
* **Selected Date** (Selected Date, date, optional): Confirm the date of your appointment.
* **Selected Time** (Selected Time, time, optional): Confirm the time of your appointment.
