# Camp Staff Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Camp Staff Application Form is designed to collect your personal and professional information as a potential camp staff member. This form will help us get to know you better and understand your qualifications for working at our camp.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
3. Review our camp's policies and procedures on our website to ensure you're a good fit.
4. Submit the form when you're ready for us to review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Current Mailing Address**: Enter your current home address, including street address and postal code.
* **Background and Experience**: This section is to describe your background and any relevant experience you have. This can include previous work or volunteer experience, or any certifications you hold.
* **Preferred Position - Role**: Select the role you're interested in working as at our camp.
* **Highest Education Level Completed**: Select your highest level of education completed.
* **Previous Work or Volunteer History**: Describe your previous work or volunteer experience, focusing on roles and responsibilities.
* **Experience Working with Children**: Describe your previous experience working with children, including any supervisory roles.
* **Current Certifications Held**: Select any certifications you hold that may be relevant to working at our camp.
* **Why are you interested in working at our camp?**: Describe why you're interested in working at our camp and what you can bring to our team.
* **Seasonal Availability**: Select the sessions you're available to work during the camp season.
* **Professional References (Name and Contact)**: List at least two professional references with their name and contact information.
* **Applicant Electronic Signature**: Sign this section digitally, indicating that all information is true and accurate.
