# Online Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Job Application Form is designed for users to submit their online job applications to a job posting. This form gathers information about the applicant, including contact details, resume, job preferences, and work experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a valid email address, as you will need to provide it to complete the form.
2. Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
3. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
4. Upload a resume as a PDF or .docx file in the **Resume** field, which will be used to evaluate your qualifications.
5. Enter your job preferences in the **Job Reference** field, if you have any.
6. Enter the **Job Title** of the position you are applying for.
7. Provide a brief description of your experience and qualifications for the **Job Description** field.
8. Describe your work experience in the **Work Experience** field.
9. Enter the location where you would like to work in the **Job Location** field.
10. Select the type of job you are applying for from the **Job Type** dropdown menu, which includes **Full-time**, **Part-time**, **Freelance**, and **Internship**.
11. Enter any other relevant details about the job level you are applying for in the **Job Level** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can contact you with.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, optional): Upload a resume as a PDF or .docx file to showcase your qualifications.
* **Job Reference** (`job_reference`, text, optional): If you have any relevant job references, enter them here.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the job title you are applying for.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your experience and qualifications for the job.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): Describe your relevant work experience.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you would like to work.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of job you are applying for from the dropdown menu:
	+ **Full-time**
	+ **Part-time**
	+ **Freelance**
	+ **Internship**
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, text, optional): Enter any other relevant details about the job level you are applying for.
