# Account Opening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and professional information from users to open a new account with the financial institution.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
- Review each section and field carefully before proceeding.
- Don't hesitate to contact our support team if you have any questions or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Full Legal Name (`full_name`, text, required):** Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
- **Date of Birth (`date_of_birth`, date, required):** Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Gender (`gender`, select_one, optional):** Select your gender from the options provided.
- **Nationality (`nationality`, text, required):** Enter your nationality.
- **Identification Document Type (`id_type`, select_one, required):** Select the type of identification document you possess.
- **Identification Document Number (`id_number`, text, required):** Enter the number on your identification document.
- **Residential Address (`residential_address`, text, required):** Enter your full street address including city, state, and ZIP code.
- **Mailing Address (if different) (`mailing_address`, text, optional):** Enter your mailing address if different from residential address.
- **Email Address (`email`, email, required):** Enter your email address.
- **Phone Number (`phone_number`, text, required):** Enter your phone number.
- **Employment Status (`employment_status`, select_one, required):** Select your employment status from the options provided.
- **Employer Name (if applicable) (`employer_name`, text, optional):** Enter your employer's name if applicable.
- **Occupation (`occupation`, text, required):** Enter your occupation.
- **Approximate Annual Income (`annual_income`, select_one, required):** Select the approximate annual income from the options provided.
- **Type of Account (`account_type`, select_one, required):** Select the type of account you wish to open.
- **Initial Deposit Amount (`initial_deposit`, number, required):** Enter the initial deposit amount.
- **Source of Funds (`source_of_funds`, select_one, required):** Select the source of funds from the options provided.
- **Purpose of Account (`purpose_of_account`, select_one, required):** Select the purpose of the account from the options provided.
- **Would You Like to Enroll in Online Banking (`online_banking`, select_one, required):** Select whether you want to enroll in online banking.
- **I Agree to the Terms and Conditions (`terms_agreement`, select_one, required):** Select "Yes, I agree" to agree to the terms and conditions.
