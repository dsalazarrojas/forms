# Generic Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a generic job application form designed to collect basic information about a job seeker's background, experience, and contact details. It can be used in various contexts, such as when applying for a job, internship, or other career opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as follows:

1. Fill in your name and contact details in the "Contact Details" field.
2. Describe your job history in the "Job History" field.
3. Provide your job title and the name of the company you worked for in the "Job Title" and "Job Title Employer" fields.
4. Enter the start and end dates of your job in the "Job Title Start Date" and "Job Title End Date" fields.
5. Enter a brief description of your role in the "Job Description" field.
6. If you have experience in a specific industry, select it in the "Job Industry" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Fill in your name and contact information, including phone number and email address.
### 2. **Job Application** (`job_application`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of why you are applying for the job.
### 3. **Job History** (`job_history`, text, optional): Describe your previous work experience, including job titles and dates of employment.
### 4. **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your current or previous job title.
### 5. **Job Title Employer** (`job_title_employer`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company you worked for.
### 6. **Job Title Start Date** (`job_title_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your job.
### 7. **Job Title End Date** (`job_title_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your job.
### 8. **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your role and responsibilities.
### 9. **Job Industry** (`job_industry`, text, optional): If you have experience in a specific industry, select it to indicate your expertise.
