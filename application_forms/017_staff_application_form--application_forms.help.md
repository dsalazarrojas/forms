# Staff Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about applicants for staff positions within your organization. It will help us assess your qualifications and suitability for the role. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Staff Application Form" option from the menu.
2. Choose the correct page (personal details) and begin filling out the form.
3. Complete each field according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Personal Info
* **Personal Info (text)**: Provide a brief description of yourself, including your name and a short introduction.
### On Call Positions
* **On Call Positions (select one)**: Choose the position you are most interested in applying for: Front Desk or Back Office.
### Resume (text)
* **Resume (text)**: Upload or paste a brief summary of your work experience, skills, and qualifications.
### Contact Info (text)
* **Contact Info (text)**: Enter your contact details, including phone number and email address.
### Job Applicant (text)
* **Job Applicant (text)**: Enter your name as it should appear on any official documents or correspondence.
### Business Interest (text)
* **Business Interest (text)**: Briefly describe your business interests and how they relate to the role you are applying for.
### Work Experience (text)
* **Work Experience (text)**: Provide a detailed account of your relevant work experience, including job title, company, and duration.
### Education (text)
* **Education (text)**: List your educational background, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
### Skills (text)
* **Skills (text)**: List the skills you possess that are relevant to the role, and be prepared to provide examples of how you have applied them.
### Work Experience Years (number)
* **Work Experience Years (number)**: Enter the number of years you have spent in your current or most recent role.

Note: Please ensure that you provide as much detail as possible in each field to give us a complete understanding of your qualifications. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
