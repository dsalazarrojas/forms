# Emotional Support Animal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an emotional support animal accommodation from a landlord or housing provider. It is meant to be completed by a tenant or resident who wishes to have an emotional support animal in their living space.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of mental disability you have from the list.
2. Select the type of animal you have.
3. Provide information about the animal's weight and how long you have had it.
4. Choose the species of your animal.
5. Answer questions about your relationship with the animal and how long you have it.
6. Indicate if you have a doctor's note for your mental disability.
7. Explain why you need this accommodation.
8. Confirm if this is an emergency situation.
9. List any other pets you have.
10. Confirm if your landlord is willing to work with you.
11. Provide your address and contact information.
12. Indicate if you live with roommates.
13. Confirm if you have a copy of your lease.
14. Specify how many people live with you.
15. Provide the start and end dates of your lease.
16. Confirm if you have a roommate agreement.
17. Indicate if you can attach a copy of the agreement.
18. Provide the name of your landlord.
19. Confirm if you have a pet deposit.
20. List any other pets you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What type of mental disability do you have**: Choose the type of mental disability you have from the list. This will help us understand your situation better.
* **What animal do you have**: Select the type of animal you have. This will help us understand your relationship with your animal.
* **What is the animal's weight**: Please provide the weight of your animal in pounds.
* **What is the animal's species**: Choose the species of your animal from the list.
* **How long have you had the animal**: Please select how long you have had the animal.
* **How long do you have the animal**: Please select how long you have the animal in months.
* **What is your relationship to the animal**: Please describe your relationship with the animal.
* **Do you have a doctor's note**: Check if you have a doctor's note for your mental disability.
* **Why do you need this accommodation**: Please explain why you need this accommodation.
* **Is this an emergency**: Confirm if this is an emergency situation.
* **Do you have any other pets**: Please list any other pets you have.
* **Do you have any other pets with similar needs**: Confirm if you have any other pets with similar needs.
* **Is your landlord willing to work with you**: Confirm if your landlord is willing to work with you.
* **What is your address**: Please provide your address.
* **What is your email**: Please provide your email address.
* **What is your phone number**: Please provide your phone number.
* **Do you live with roommates**: Confirm if you live with roommates.
* **Do you have a copy of your lease**: Check if you have a copy of your lease.
* **How many people live with you**: Please select how many people live with you.
* **What date is your lease start date**: Please provide the start date of your lease.
* **What date is your lease end date**: Please provide the end date of your lease.
* **Do you have a roommate agreement**: Confirm if you have a roommate agreement.
* **Can you attach a copy of the agreement**: Check if you can attach a copy of the agreement.
* **What is your landlord's name**: Please provide the name of your landlord.
* **Do you have a pet deposit**: Confirm if you have a pet deposit.

Note: This form is for informational purposes only and is subject to change. The actual fields and options may vary depending on the landlord or housing provider's requirements.
