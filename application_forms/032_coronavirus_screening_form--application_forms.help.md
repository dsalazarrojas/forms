# coronavirus_screening_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects patient information and screening data for the coronavirus to ensure proper assessment and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter patient information on the **Patient Information** page, which includes fields to provide details such as name, contact information, and any other relevant patient data.
2. In the **Symptoms** page, describe the patient's symptoms related to the coronavirus, if any.
3. On the **Health Status** page, select whether the patient is currently **Active** or **Inactive** in terms of their health status.
4. In the **Travel History** page, provide details about any recent travel history that may be relevant to the patient's condition.
5. In the **Contact History** page, provide details about any known contacts with potentially infected individuals.
6. On the **Test Date** page, enter the date of the patient's test for the coronavirus.
7. Finally, on the **Test Result** page, select whether the patient tested positive or negative for the coronavirus.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Symptoms**: Describe any symptoms the patient is experiencing, such as fever, cough, or shortness of breath.
* **Health Status**: Select whether the patient is currently **Active** or **Inactive** in terms of their health status. Active means the patient is experiencing symptoms or is suspected to be infected, while Inactive means the patient is not experiencing symptoms and is not suspected to be infected.
* **Travel History**: Provide details about any recent travel history that may be relevant to the patient's condition.
* **Contact History**: Provide details about any known contacts with potentially infected individuals.
* **Test Date**: Enter the date of the patient's test for the coronavirus.
* **Test Result**: Select whether the patient tested positive or negative for the coronavirus.
