# Loan Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Loan Application Form is a document used to collect information from individuals applying for a loan. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth loan application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide the co-applicant's information if applicable.
3. Select the loan term type and interest rate type.
4. Choose the loan amount and term length.
5. Enter the co-applicant's employment details if applicable.
6. Review and submit the form once you have filled in all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Loan Application** (`1`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the loan application.
* **Loan Amount** (`2`, `number`, optional): Enter the loan amount you are applying for.
* **Interest Rate** (`3`, `number`, optional): Enter the interest rate for the loan.
* **Term Length** (`4`, `select_one`, required): Select the term length of the loan (6, 12, or 24 months).
* **Monthly Payment** (`5`, `number`, optional): Enter the monthly payment amount.
* **Loan Term Type** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the loan term type (Fixed, Variable, or Adjustable).
* **Interest Rate Type** (`7`, `select_one`, optional): Select the interest rate type (Fixed or Floating).
* **Loan Amount Range** (`8`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the loan amount range (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Credit Score** (`9`, `number`, optional): Enter the credit score.
* **Loan Status** (`10`, `select_one`, required): Select the loan status (Active or Inactive).
* **Notes** (`11`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the loan application.
* **Applicant Email** (`12`, `email`, optional): Enter the applicant's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`13`, `text`, optional): Enter the applicant's phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`14`, `date`, optional): Enter the applicant's date of birth.
* **Social Security Number** (`15`, `text`, optional): Enter the applicant's social security number.
* **Address** (`16`, `text`, optional): Enter the applicant's address.
* **Loan Officer** (`17`, `text`, optional): Enter the loan officer's name.
* **Applicant Name** (`18`, `text`, optional): Enter the applicant's name.
* **Co-Applicant Name** (`19`, `text`, optional): Enter the co-applicant's name.
* **Co-Applicant Relationship** (`20`, `text`, optional): Enter the co-applicant's relationship to the applicant.
* **Co-Applicant Income** (`21`, `number`, optional): Enter the co-applicant's income.
* **Co-Applicant Employment Status** (`22`, `select_one`, optional): Select the co-applicant's employment status (Employed or Unemployed).
* **Co-Applicant Employment Years** (`23`, `number`, optional): Enter the co-applicant's employment years.
* **Co-Applicant Employment Income** (`24`, `number`, optional): Enter the co-applicant's employment income.
* **Co-Applicant Employment Address** (`25`, `text`, optional): Enter the co-applicant's employment address.
