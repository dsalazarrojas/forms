# Identity Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Identity Verification Form is a document designed to collect and verify personal information from users. This form is intended for high-security contexts where accurate identity verification is essential.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To fill out the form, follow the instructions below for each field.
2.  Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure successful verification.
3.  Review each field carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Personal Info** (`personal_info`, text, optional): This field is where you enter your personal details, such as your name or any other relevant information.
*   **Verification Questions** (`verification_questions`, select_multiple, optional): Choose from the options provided to answer questions related to your identity. Select all that apply to ensure accurate verification.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the required format (MM/DD/YYYY).
*   **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, number, optional): Enter your Social Security Number exactly as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, which will be used for verification purposes.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out the form correctly and accurately to avoid any potential issues with verification.
*   If you're unsure about any of the fields, consult the provided help guide or ask the form administrator for assistance.
*   Double-check the form before submitting it to ensure you haven't missed any required fields.
