# Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Application Form is a general application form used to collect contact information from individuals who are interested in volunteering for various roles or events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your name, email, phone number, and address.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out, as indicated by the * symbol.
3. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Application Form** (`volunteer_application_form`, text, required): This is the title of the form.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required): Please enter your name in this field.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, text, required): Enter your email address to allow us to communicate with you.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you.
* **Address** (`contact_address`, text, required): Enter your physical address to allow us to mail any necessary documents.
* **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1`, text, optional): Enter any additional address line information.
* **Address Line 2** (`address_line_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional address line information.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where you reside.
* **Tips**

Note: The information collected from this form will only be used for the purpose of volunteering and will not be shared with any third party. If you have any concerns, please reach out to us directly.
