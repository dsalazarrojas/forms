# Online Internship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals who are interested in applying for an internship in the field of [specific field of study or area of interest]. The purpose of this form is to gather essential information about your background, skills, and experience that will be used to assess your eligibility for the internship program.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the User Information page, which includes fields for your name, contact information, and other basic details.
2.  Move on to the Education Background page, where you will be asked about your educational background, including degree, university, and graduation date.
3.  The Work Experience page is where you will provide information about your previous work experience, including job title, workplace, and duration.
4.  On the Skills List page, select the skills you possess from the provided list. You can select multiple skills if applicable.
5.  The Language Proficiency page is where you will be asked about your language proficiency, including language and level.
