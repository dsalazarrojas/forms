# Electric Motor Nameplate Data Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture detailed information about electric motor nameplates, including specifications, ratings, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure that you are filling it out correctly.
2. Complete each field accurately and completely, paying attention to any required fields.
3. If you are unsure about a particular field, refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Motor Nameplate Spec** (`motor_nameplate_spec`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the motor's nameplate, including any notable features or specifications.
* **Motor Model Number** (`motor_model_number`, text, optional): Enter the model number of the motor, if applicable.
* **Motor Rating (Rating)** (`motor_rating_rating`, number, optional): Enter the motor's rating (e.g., 5 kW) or leave blank if not applicable.
* **Motor Rating Units** (`motor_rating_units`, select_one, optional): Select the unit of measurement for the rating, typically "kW" or "HP".

* **Motor Vibration Level** (`motor_vibration_level`, select_multiple, optional): Select the level of vibration produced by the motor, such as "Low" or "High".
