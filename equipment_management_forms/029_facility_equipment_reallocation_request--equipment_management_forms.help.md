# Facility Equipment Reallocation Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facility Equipment Reallocation Request form is used to submit a request to relocate facility equipment to a different location within the facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the facility where the equipment is currently located and will be relocated to.
2. Enter the ID of the equipment you want to relocate.
3. Specify the new location of the equipment in the text field.
4. Provide a brief reason for relocating the equipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Location ID** (`facility_location_id`): Enter the ID of the facility where the equipment is currently located and will be relocated to.
* **Equipment ID** (`equipment_id`): Enter the ID of the equipment you want to relocate.
* **New Location** (`new_location`): Specify the new location of the equipment in the text field. Please provide a clear description of the location.
* **Reason for Relocation** (`reason_for_relocation`): Provide a brief reason for relocating the equipment. This will help the facility manager to understand the justification for the relocation request.
