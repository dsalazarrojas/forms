# Material Handling Equipment Maintenance Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Material Handling Equipment Maintenance Log is used to track and record maintenance activities related to Material Handling Equipment. It helps to keep accurate records of the equipment status and maintenance history.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the following fields:
	* `equipment_id`: The ID number of the equipment being maintained.
	* `description`: A brief description of the maintenance activity.
	* `equipment_status`: The current status of the equipment (Running, Non-operational, Offline).
	* `maintenance_type`: The type of maintenance activity (Scheduled, Unscheduled, Emergency).
	* `notes`: Any additional comments or notes related to the maintenance activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <u>equipment_id</u>
*   `equipment_id` (Number, Required): This field is used to identify the Material Handling Equipment being maintained. Enter the equipment's ID number.

### <u>description</u>
*   `description` (Text, Optional): A brief description of the maintenance activity being performed.

### <u>equipment_status</u>
*   `equipment_status` (Select One, Required): The current status of the equipment (Running, Non-operational, Offline).

### <u>maintenance_type</u>
*   `maintenance_type` (Select One, Required): The type of maintenance activity (Scheduled, Unscheduled, Emergency).

### <u>notes</u>
*   `notes` (Text, Optional): Any additional comments or notes related to the maintenance activity.

## Tips
*   Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all fields are accurately filled in.
*   Only use this form for recording maintenance activities related to Material Handling Equipment.
*   If you are unsure about any field or value, seek clarification from the relevant authorities.
