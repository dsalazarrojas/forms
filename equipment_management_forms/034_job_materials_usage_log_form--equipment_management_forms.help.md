# Job Materials Usage Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to log the usage of job materials, including site usage, material type, quantity, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form for each job site where you have used materials.
2. Provide accurate information about the materials used, including their type, quantity, and unit price.
3. Enter the total cost of the materials used.
4. Enter the date of the material usage.
5. Provide your name and the name of the team you are working with.
6. Enter any remarks or comments about the material usage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Job Site Usage (1)
* **Job Site Usage** (`job_site_usage`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the job site where the materials were used.

### Material Type (2)
* **Material Type** (`material_type`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if the material type is applicable, 'No' otherwise.

### Material Name (3)
* **Material Name** (`material_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the material used.

### Quantity (4)
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required: false): Enter the quantity of the material used.

### Unit Price (5)
* **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, number, required: false): Enter the unit price of the material used.

### Total Cost (6)
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, number, required: false): Enter the total cost of the materials used.

### Date (7)
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the material usage.

### User Name (8)
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name.

### Team Name (9)
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the team you are working with.

### Location (10)
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the materials were used.

### Remarks (11)
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, text, required: false): Enter any remarks or comments about the material usage.

### Comments (12)
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments about the material usage.

### Job Site Usage 2 (13)
* **Job Site Usage 2** (`job_site_usage_2`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the second job site where the materials were used.

### Equipment ID (14)
* **Equipment ID** (`equipment_id`, text, required: false): Enter the equipment ID.

### Material Type 2 (15)
* **Material Type 2** (`material_type_2`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if the material type is applicable, 'No' otherwise.

### Material Name 2 (16)
* **Material Name 2** (`material_name_2`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the material used.

### Quantity 2 (17)
* **Quantity 2** (`quantity_2`, number, required: false): Enter the quantity of the material used.

### Remarks 2 (18)
* **Remarks 2** (`remarks_2`, text, required: false): Enter any remarks or comments about the material usage.

Note: This form is designed to be filled out for new job sites or significant changes to material usage. If you are unsure about the form's purpose or usage, please reach out to the relevant team or department for clarification.
