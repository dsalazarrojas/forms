# Equipment Decommissioning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to document the decommissioning of equipment, ensuring a smooth and secure process. It captures essential details to keep track of the equipment's status and history.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and select the relevant information for the equipment being decommissioned.
2. Fill in the required fields, starting with "equipment_type" and including "last_in_use_date", "disposal_date", "warranty_status", and "asset_status".
3. Choose "decommissioned_by" and "warranty_status" according to the actual circumstances.
4. For "warranty_status", select "In Warranty", "Expired", or "Unknown", depending on the equipment's warranty status.
5. If the equipment is being disposed of, select "Recycle", "Trade-in", or "Scrap" for "disposal_method".
6. Fill in "purchase_date" and "last_calibration_date" if applicable.
7. Complete "data_wipe_completed" to indicate if data was wiped from the equipment.
8. Sign and date "signature" and "witness_signature" to confirm the decommissioning.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **equipment_type**: Select the type of equipment being decommissioned, such as "Computer".
* **asset_number**: Enter the asset number assigned to the equipment.
* **last_in_use_date**: Fill in the date when the equipment was last in use.
* **disposal_date**: Enter the date when the equipment was disposed of or will be disposed of.
* **decommissioned_by**: Select who decommissioned the equipment, e.g., "John".
* **data_wipe_completed**: Choose if data was successfully wiped from the equipment.
* **disposal_method**: If disposing of the equipment, select how it will be disposed of, such as "Recycle", "Trade-in", or "Scrap".
* **disposal_location**: Enter the location where the equipment was disposed of.
* **notes**: Add any additional comments or observations regarding the decommissioning process.
* **signature**: Sign and date to confirm that you've completed the form.
* **witness_signature**: Ask a witness to sign and date to confirm that you've completed the form.
* **asset_status**: Select the current status of the equipment, such as "Decommissioned", "Available", or "Reserved".
* **remarks**: Add any additional comments or observations regarding the equipment.
* **equipment_location**: Enter the location where the equipment was last in use.
* **asset_tag**: Enter the asset tag assigned to the equipment.
* **vendor**: Enter the vendor or manufacturer of the equipment.
* **model_number**: Enter the equipment's model number.
* **serial_number**: Enter the equipment's serial number.
* **purchase_date**: Enter the date the equipment was purchased.
* **warranty_status**: Select the warranty status of the equipment.
* **last_calibration_date**: Enter the date the equipment was last calibrated.
* **last_servicing_date**: Enter the date the equipment was last serviced.
* **notes_2**: Add any additional comments or observations regarding the equipment.
* **remarks_2**: Add any additional comments or observations regarding the equipment.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If unsure about any field, refer to the equipment's documentation or consult with the equipment's owner.
* This form is for internal use only, please handle sensitive information with care.
* Keep a record of decommissioned equipment for future reference.
* Update this form as necessary to reflect the current status of the equipment.
