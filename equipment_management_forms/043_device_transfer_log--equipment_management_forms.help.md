# Device Transfer Log - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Device Transfer Log form is designed to record the transfer of devices between departments or locations within an organization. This form helps track the movement of equipment, ensuring that all necessary information is captured.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete the Device Transfer Log form, start by selecting the appropriate page (device_transfer_log_form or equipment_details_form or ownership_details_form). Each page is designed to capture specific information about the transfer.
2.  For each page, provide accurate and relevant information as requested in the form fields.
3.  Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly to avoid any issues during review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Device Transfer Log Form (page 1)**:
    *   <label>Device Transfer Log Form</label> (id: 1, <type>text, required/optional: false)
        This page allows you to document the basic details of the device transfer. Please enter a brief description of the transfer, including the device name, transfer date, and transfer reason.
*  **Equipment Details (page 2)**:
    *   <label>Equipment Details</label> (id: 2, <type>text, required/optional: false)
        In this field, enter the essential details about the equipment being transferred, such as its model, serial number, and condition.
*  **Ownership Details (page 3)**:
    *   <label>Ownership Details</label> (id: 3, <type>text, required/optional: false)
        This field is used to capture information about the previous and current owners of the device. Please enter the names and contact information for both parties involved in the transfer.

## Tips

*   Always ensure that each field is filled out accurately and completely.
*   Double-check the form for any errors or missing information before submitting.
*   If you're unsure about any field or information, consult with the relevant department or contact person for clarification.
