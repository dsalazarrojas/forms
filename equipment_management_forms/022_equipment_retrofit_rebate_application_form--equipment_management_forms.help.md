# Equipment Retrofit Rebate Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Retrofit Rebate Application Form is used to collect information from applicants regarding equipment retrofitting. It is essential to provide accurate and complete details to ensure a smooth review and processing of the application.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your information and project details.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out, but note that some fields are not mandatory.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Information**: Enter your name, company, and other relevant details as an applicant.
* **Contact Information**: Provide your contact details, including name, email, and phone number.
* **Project Information**: Describe your project in a few sentences, including its name, location, and any other relevant details.
* **Project Location**: Enter the location where the project will take place.
* **Retrofit Equipment**: Specify the equipment being retrofitted, including its type and model.
* **Retrofit Project Details**: Describe the retrofit project in detail, including any relevant dates and timelines.
* **Project Status**: Select the current status of your project, whether it is Active or Inactive.
* **Upload File**: If required, upload any relevant files or documents related to your project.
* **Agreement**: Indicate if you have agreed to the terms and conditions of the rebate program.
* **Review Status**: Select the status of your review, whether it is Active or Inactive.
* **Submit**: Review and submit your application carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult the relevant documentation or contact the team handling the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the team for assistance.
