# fiberglass_extension_ladder_inventory_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fiberglass_extension_ladder_inventory_form is designed to track the inventory and status of fiberglass extension ladders within your organization. It helps in ensuring the efficient management of these critical equipment by collecting essential data on their location, inspection history, and assigned personnel.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "location" field, which is a required field indicating the current location of the ladder. Choose the correct location from the provided options.
2. Select all applicable ladders for the "ladder_inventory" field. You must select all ladders that are currently in inventory.
3. Enter the "inspection_date" of the most recent inspection in the corresponding field.
4. Select the correct location where the inspection took place for the "inspection_location" field.
5. Indicate the current status of the ladder as available, not available, or in maintenance for the "equipment_status" field.
6. Choose the person responsible for the ladder from the "assigned_to" field.
7. Enter the date the ladder was assigned to the selected person for the "assigned_to_date" field.
8. Optionally, add any inspection notes for the "inspection_notes" field.
9. Set the "next_inspection" date for future reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a text field providing a background to the form and its purpose.
* **Location**: Enter the current location of the fiberglass extension ladder. This will help track the ladder's status.
* **Ladder inventory**: Select all the ladders that are currently in inventory. This is a required field to ensure accurate tracking.
* **Inspection date**: Enter the date of the last inspection. This helps in scheduling future inspections and maintenance.
* **Inspection location**: Choose the location where the inspection took place. This provides valuable information for future reference.
* **Equipment status**: Select the current status of the ladder as available, not available, or in maintenance. This helps in prioritizing maintenance and availability.
* **Assigned to**: Choose the person currently handling the ladder. This ensures accountability and responsibility.
* **Assigned to date**: Enter the date the person was assigned to handle the ladder. This keeps track of assignment history.
* **Inspection notes**: Add any notes from the inspection. This provides valuable context and history.
* **Next inspection**: Schedule the next inspection date. This helps in maintaining regular maintenance.

## Tips

* Always ensure that you select all applicable ladders for the "ladder_inventory" field.
* Be accurate when selecting the "equipment_status" to reflect the current condition of the ladder.
* Enter the correct "inspection_date" and "assigned_to_date" for accurate tracking.
* Use the "inspection_notes" field for any additional context or notes from the inspection.
* Set the "next_inspection" date to ensure timely maintenance and follow-up.
