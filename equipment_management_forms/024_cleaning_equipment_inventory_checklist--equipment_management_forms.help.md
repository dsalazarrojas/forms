# Cleaning Equipment Inventory Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Cleaning Equipment Inventory Checklist" is a tool designed to help facilities and cleaning staff maintain an up-to-date inventory of cleaning equipment. This form helps ensure that all equipment is accounted for, and that any issues or concerns are documented and addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the location information section, which includes:
	* Location Name: Enter the name of the location or facility being inventoried.
	* Location Address: Enter the full address of the location or facility being inventoried.
	* Inventory Date: Enter the date of the inventory check.
	* Inventory Conducted By: Enter the name of the person conducting the inventory.
2. In the Equipment Information section, fill out the following fields:
	* -- Vacuum Cleaners --
		+ Upright Vacuum Cleaners - Quantity: Enter the number of upright vacuums in the inventory.
		+ Upright Vacuum Cleaners - Condition: Select the overall condition of the upright vacuums using the options provided.
	* -- Floor Care Equipment --
		+ Floor Buffers or Polishers - Quantity: Enter the number of floor buffers or polishers in the inventory.
		+ Floor Buffers or Polishers - Condition: Select the overall condition of the floor buffers or polishers using the options provided.
		+ Carpet Cleaners or Extractors - Quantity: Enter the number of carpet cleaners or extractors in the inventory.
		+ Carpet Cleaners or Extractors - Condition: Select the overall condition of the carpet cleaners or extractors using the options provided.
		+ Floor Scrubbers - Quantity: Enter the number of floor scrubbers in the inventory.
		+ Floor Scrubbers - Condition: Select the overall condition of the floor scrubbers using the options provided.
		+ Steam Cleaners - Quantity: Enter the number of steam cleaners in the inventory.
		+ Steam Cleaners - Condition: Select the overall condition of the steam cleaners using the options provided.
3. In the Cleaning Tools and Supplies section, fill out the following fields:
	* Mop and Bucket Sets - Quantity: Enter the number of mop and bucket sets in the inventory.
	* Mop and Bucket Sets - Condition: Select the overall condition of the mop and bucket sets using the options provided.
	* Brooms - Quantity: Enter the number of brooms in the inventory.
	* Dustpans - Quantity: Enter the number of dustpans in the inventory.
	* Scrub Brushes - Quantity: Enter the number of scrub brushes in the inventory.
	* Squeegees - Quantity: Enter the number of squeegees in the inventory.
	* Extension Poles - Quantity: Enter the number of extension poles in the inventory.
4. In the Safety Equipment section, fill out the following fields:
	* Safety Goggles - Quantity: Enter the number of pairs of safety goggles in the inventory.
	* Cleaning Gloves - Quantity: Enter the number of pairs of cleaning gloves in the inventory.
	* Face Masks - Quantity: Enter the number of face masks in the inventory.
	* First Aid Kits - Quantity: Enter the number of first aid kits in the inventory.
	* Wet Floor Signs - Quantity: Enter the number of wet floor signs in the inventory.
5. In the Equipment Issues and Notes section, fill out the following fields:
	* Equipment Needing Repair: List any equipment that is in need of repair.
	* Equipment Needing Replacement: List any equipment that is in need of replacement.
	* Missing Equipment: List any equipment that is missing from the inventory.
	* New Equipment Needed: List any new equipment that should be purchased.
	* Additional Notes: Enter any other observations or comments.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form regularly to ensure the inventory is up-to-date and accurate.
* Use the equipment condition options provided for an overall condition assessment.
* Use the quantity fields for counting and listing the number of each type of equipment.
* Be specific and detailed when listing equipment that is in need of repair, replacement, or is missing.
* Make sure to include any new equipment that should be purchased.
* Use the additional notes field for any observations or comments.
* Ensure that the supervisor review section is completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the equipment issues and notes section regularly to ensure that issues are addressed and resolved.
