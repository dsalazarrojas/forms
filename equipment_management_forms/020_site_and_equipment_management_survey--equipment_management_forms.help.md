# Site And Equipment Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a site and its associated equipment for management purposes. It will help to record and track the status of the equipment, maintenance needs, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the site information section (Site Info) with the required details.
2. Provide your contact details, including your name and phone number, in the Contact Details section.
3. Add any relevant equipment information in the Equipment Info section.
4. Indicate the current maintenance status of the equipment using the drop-down menu in the Maintenance Status section.
5. Add any comments or observations in the Comments 1, Additional Comments, Comments 3, and Comments 4 sections.
6. If you have any photos or attachments related to the site or equipment, upload them in the Photos section.
7. Finally, fill in the Contact Phone number and any additional comments in the Comments 2 section, and assign the site to a team member or individual in the Assigned To section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Site Info
* **Site Info** (Site Info, text, required): This section is where you should provide details about the site, including its name, location, and other relevant information.

### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (Contact Details, text, required): Provide your name and contact information, such as your phone number, email, or other contact method.

### Equipment Info
* **Equipment Info** (Equipment Info, text, required): Enter any relevant equipment information, such as model number, serial number, or manufacturer details.

### Maintenance Status
* **Maintenance Status** (Maintenance Status, select_multiple, required): Choose from the options to indicate the current status of the equipment, which can be Working, Not working, Maintenance needed, or Other (please specify).

### Comments 1
* **Comments** (Comments 1, text, optional): Add any comments or observations about the site or equipment.

### Comments 2
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or details that are not already mentioned in the Comments 1 section.

### Comments 3
* **Comments 3** (Comments 3, text, optional): Enter any further comments or observations about the site or equipment.

### Comments 4
* **Comments 4** (Comments 4, text, optional): This is the last comments section, enter any final comments or details.

### Site Inspection
* **Site Inspection** (Site Inspection, date, optional): Record the date of the site inspection, if applicable.

### Photos
* **Photos** (Photos, text, optional): Upload or enter any photos or attachments related to the site or equipment, if applicable.

### Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the person completing this form.

### Assigned To
* **Assigned To** (Assigned To, text, optional): Identify the team member or individual assigned to manage or handle the site or equipment.
