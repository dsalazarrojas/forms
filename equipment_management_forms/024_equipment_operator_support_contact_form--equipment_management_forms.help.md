# equipment_operator_support_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect operator contact information and equipment details for support purposes. Fill out this form to request support from the equipment management team.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the operator's information, including their contact name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide details about the equipment, including its type, location, and condition.
3. Specify the level of urgency for the support request.
4. Select the type of support requested (phone, text, email, or other).
5. Attach any relevant files or documents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Operator Info**: Enter the operator's name, email, and phone number to ensure we can contact them about the support request.
* **Equipment Info**: Enter details about the equipment, including its type, location, and condition, to help the support team understand the context of the request.
* **Support Type**: Choose how you would like to be contacted (phone, text, email, or other).
* **Urgency Level**: Indicate the level of urgency for the support request (High, Low).
* **File Upload 1-5**: Attach any relevant files or documents to support your request.
* **Support Type Options**: Select multiple methods for how you would like to be contacted (phone, text, email, or other).
* **Equipment ID**: Enter the ID number of the equipment, if applicable.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the phone number to contact you at.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name for the support team to address you by.
* **Support Details**: Enter any additional details about the support request.
* **File Upload 3-5**: Attach any additional files or documents to support your request.
* **Support Details 2**: Enter any additional details about the support request.
* **Operator Name**: Enter your name or the name of the operator requesting support.
* **Operator Contact**: Enter your phone number or email where you can be reached.
* **Equipment Name**: Enter the name of the equipment, if applicable.
* **Equipment Location**: Enter the location of the equipment, if applicable.
* **Equipment Type**: Enter the type of equipment, if applicable.
* **Support Type 2**: Enter the type of support requested, if different from the initial selection.
* **Support Description**: Enter a brief description of the support request.
* **Equipment Condition**: Enter the condition of the equipment, if applicable.
* **Support Details 3**: Enter any additional details about the support request.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing as much information as possible will help the support team better assist you with your request.
