# Volunteer Fire Department Equipment Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Fire Department Equipment Donation Form is a tool used to collect information from volunteers about equipment they are willing to donate to the Volunteer Fire Department. This form helps the department to identify potential equipment donations, categorize them, and ensure a smooth process for the volunteers and department staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact information in the **Contact Info** field.
2. Describe the equipment you are willing to donate in the **Equipment Details** field.
3. Indicate whether the equipment will be delivered to the department or if you will arrange for delivery/pickup yourself in the **Delivery Method** field.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Info (Contact Info, text, required: false):** This field is where you provide your name and contact information so the Volunteer Fire Department can get in touch with you to discuss further details about the equipment donation.
* **Equipment Details (Equipment Details, text, required: false):** Here, please describe the equipment you are willing to donate, including its condition, any existing maintenance or repair needs, and its intended use in the Volunteer Fire Department.
* **Delivery Method (Delivery Method, select multiple, required: false):** In this field, you can choose whether you will deliver the equipment directly to the department or if you will arrange for pickup or other arrangements. Please select all methods that apply.
* **Volunteer Fire Department Equipment Donation Form Page 2 (Volunteer Fire Department Equipment Donation Form Page 2, text, required: false):** This field is currently not required and appears to be intended for further information or additional details not covered in previous fields. Please only fill it out if you have any additional comments or details.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate contact information so the department can contact you about the equipment donation.
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the equipment to help the department assess its suitability.
* Select all applicable delivery methods to ensure the department has all possible options.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid any errors or omissions.
