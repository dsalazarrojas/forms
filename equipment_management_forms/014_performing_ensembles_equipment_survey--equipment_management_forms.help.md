# Performing Ensembles Equipment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performing Ensembles Equipment Survey is a form used to collect information about the equipment used by performing ensembles. This information will help the ensemble management team make informed decisions and ensure the equipment is properly maintained.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Name (contact_name) (Required)
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the equipment.

### Contact Email (contact_email) (Required)
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person responsible for the equipment.

### Instrument Make (instrument_make) (Required)
* **Instrument Make**: Enter the make of the instrument, e.g., Yamaha, Gibson, etc.

### Instrument Model (instrument_model) (Required)
* **Instrument Model**: Enter the model of the instrument, e.g., Yamaha 300, Gibson Les Paul, etc.

### Instrument Year (instrument_year) (Required)
* **Instrument Year**: Enter the year the instrument was purchased or manufactured.

### Instrument Type (instrument_type) (Required)
* **Instrument Type**: Select 'Yes' if the instrument is active and in use, or 'No' if it's not.

### Condition (condition) (Required)
* **Condition**: Describe the condition of the instrument, e.g., good, fair, poor, etc.

### Maintenance Status (maintenance_status) (Required)
* **Maintenance Status**: Enter the current maintenance status of the instrument, e.g., in maintenance, needs maintenance, etc.

### Purchase Date (purchase_date) (Required)
* **Purchase Date**: Enter the date the instrument was purchased.

### Last Inspection Date (last_inspection_date) (Required)
* **Last Inspection Date**: Enter the date the instrument was last inspected.

### Next Inspection Date (next_inspection_date) (Required)
* **Next Inspection Date**: Enter the date the instrument is due for its next inspection.

### Maintenance Cost (maintenance_cost) (Required)
* **Maintenance Cost**: Enter the estimated or actual cost of maintenance for the instrument.

### Replacement Status (replacement_status) (Required)
* **Replacement Status**: Select 'Active' if the instrument is still in use, or 'Inactive' if it's not.

### Comments (comments) (Optional)
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the instrument.
