# Battery Discharge Test Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Battery Discharge Test Log form is used to record the results of a battery discharge test. This form gathers information about the battery's performance, including its nominal voltage, test parameters, and final test results. The form helps to monitor the health of the battery and make informed decisions regarding its maintenance and replacement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information:
	* **Technician's Name**: Enter the name of the technician performing the test.
	* **Battery ID or Serial Number**: Enter the identification number or serial number of the battery being tested.
	* **Battery Chemistry Type**: Select the type of battery being tested (e.g., Lead-Acid, Lithium-Ion, etc.).
	* **Nominal Voltage (V)**: Enter the nominal voltage of the battery.
	* **Date of Test**: Enter the date of the test.
	* **Starting Voltage (V)**: Enter the measured starting voltage of the battery.
	* **Constant Discharge Current (Amps)**: Enter the current used for the test.
	* **Target Cutoff Voltage (V)**: Enter the target cutoff voltage for the test.
	* **Ambient Temperature (Celsius)**: Enter the temperature during the test.
	* **Total Discharge Duration (Minutes)**: Enter the total time the test lasted.
	* **Calculated Capacity (Ah)**: Calculate the capacity of the battery using the formula: (Current x Time) / 60.
	* **Percentage of Rated Capacity (%)**: Enter the percentage of the rated capacity of the battery.
	* **Test Disposition**: Select the test disposition from the options provided.
	* **Technical Observations and Notes**: Enter any technical observations or notes about the test.
	* **Date Log Entry Completed**: Enter the date and time the test was completed.
