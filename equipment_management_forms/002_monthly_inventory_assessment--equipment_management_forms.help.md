# Monthly Inventory Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the current stock levels and locations of various pieces of equipment across the company.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select all relevant options from the "Stock Counts" section that apply to the current status of your equipment stock.
* Select all relevant options from the "Locations" section that apply to the current locations of your equipment.
* Select all relevant options from the "Issues" section that apply to any problems or issues that have occurred during the month.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stock Counts (Select All That Apply)** (`stock_counts`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
This field asks you to select all the stock counts that apply to the equipment in your possession. This could include:
	+ Yes, all stock is accounted for and accurate
	+ No, there is some discrepancy or issue with stock levels
* **Locations (Select All That Apply)** (`locations`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
This field asks you to select all locations where the equipment is currently stored or located. This could be:
	+ Yes, all equipment is stored at the main office
	+ No, some equipment is stored at the warehouse
* **Issues (Select All That Apply)** (`issues`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
This field asks you to select all issues or problems that have occurred during the month. This could be:
	+ Yes, there was an issue with inventory tracking
	+ No, there were no issues with inventory tracking

## Tips

* Make sure to answer honestly and thoroughly to ensure accurate reporting.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, please consult with your supervisor or manager for guidance.
* Remember to keep your answers up-to-date and accurate throughout the month to ensure that the inventory is always current and accurate.
