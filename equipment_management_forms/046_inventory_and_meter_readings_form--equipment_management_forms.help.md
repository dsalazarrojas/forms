# Inventory And Meter Readings Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inventory And Meter Readings Form is used to record and track the readings and status of equipment in a location. This form helps to ensure that the inventory is up-to-date and accurate for maintenance, maintenance scheduling, and other related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the location where the equipment is located.
2. Enter the current reading of the meter for the equipment.
3. Report any issues with the equipment, if applicable.
4. Update the inventory count of the equipment.
5. If the equipment has been replaced recently, provide the date of the last replacement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **location**: Enter the location where the equipment is currently situated. This is a text field where you can enter a short description or code to identify the location.
* **meter_value**: This field is used to input the current reading of the meter for the equipment. It's a numeric field where you can enter a whole number.
* **issue**: If the equipment is experiencing any issues, enter a brief description here. This field is not required, but it's a good opportunity to report any problems or concerns.
* **inventory_count**: Update the inventory count of the equipment in this field. This is another numeric field where you can enter a whole number.
* **last_replaced**: If the equipment has been replaced recently, enter the date of the last replacement. This field is optional and not required for most cases, but it helps with tracking replacement records.
