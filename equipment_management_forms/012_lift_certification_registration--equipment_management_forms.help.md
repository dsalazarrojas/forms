# Lift Certification Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lift Certification Registration form is designed to collect information necessary to ensure that lifts are properly inspected and certified to prevent accidents and ensure compliance with safety regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the required information for the "Company Name", "Lift Type", "Lift Location", "Safety Manager", and "Service Provider" fields. These fields are marked as "required".
2. For the "Preferred Date" field, select the date you would like to complete the certification process.
3. In the "Lift Inspection Requests" field, select all the inspections that are necessary for your lift.
4. Enter any additional information in the "Notes" field, if necessary.
5. Enter the "Email" and "Phone" fields with the contact information for the person responsible for this certification.
6. Finally, select a "Certification Date" for when the certification will be completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Lift Type**: Enter the type of lift you are certifying.
* **Lift Location**: Enter the location of the lift.
* **Preferred Date**: Select the date you would like to complete the certification process.
* **Safety Manager**: Enter the name of the person responsible for ensuring the lift's safety.
* **Service Provider**: Enter the name of the service provider responsible for the certification.
* **Lift Inspection Requests**: Select all the inspections required for your lift.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information about the certification process.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the person responsible for this certification.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person responsible for this certification.
* **Certification Date**: Select the date when the certification will be completed.
* **Lift Inspection Requests** (Select Multiple): This field allows you to select multiple inspections that are required for your lift.
