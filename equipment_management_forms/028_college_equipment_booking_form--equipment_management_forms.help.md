# College Equipment Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The College Equipment Booking Form is designed to collect information about booking requests for college equipment. To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the date you are submitting your booking request.
2. Enter your full name as the person making the request.
3. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Select your role or status (Student, Faculty, Staff, Department, External).
6. Enter your department or organization affiliation.
7. Choose the type of equipment you need (e.g., Projector, Microphone and Speaker System, etc.).
8. Enter the number of items you need.
9. If you know the specific model of the equipment, enter it here.
10. Select the purpose of the equipment use (e.g., Class or Lecture, Research Project, etc.).
11. Enter the name of the event or course where the equipment will be used.
12. Select the date when you need the equipment.
13. Select the date when you will return the equipment.
14. Enter the expected number of users for the equipment.
15. Enter the location where the equipment will be used (e.g., building, room number).
16. Select if you need technical support for setting up the equipment.
17. Select if you need training or orientation before using the equipment.
18. Select if you need transportation services for moving the equipment.
19. Select if you need insurance or liability coverage for the equipment.
20. Enter any special requirements or constraints for the equipment.
21. Enter alternative dates if your primary date is not available.
22. Enter any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Booking Request Date**: When you are submitting this request.
- **Requestor Name**: Full name of the person making the request.
- **Email Address**: Contact email for the person making the request.
- **Phone Number**: Contact phone number for the person making the request.
- **Role or Status**: Select your role or status (Student, Faculty, Staff, Department, External).
- **Department or Organization**: Enter your department or organization affiliation.
- **Equipment Type Needed**: Choose the type of equipment you need (e.g., Projector, Microphone and Speaker System, etc.).
- **Quantity Needed**: Enter the number of items you need.
- **Specific Equipment Model or Model Number**: If you know the specific model of the equipment, enter it here.
- **Purpose of Equipment Use**: Select the purpose of the equipment use (e.g., Class or Lecture, Research Project, etc.).
- **Event or Course Name**: Enter the name of the event or course where the equipment will be used.
- **Equipment Needed Start Date**: When you need the equipment (date).
- **Equipment Needed End Date**: When you will return the equipment (date).
- **Expected Number of Users**: Enter the expected number of users for the equipment.
- **Where Will Equipment Be Used**: Enter the location where the equipment will be used (e.g., building, room number).
- **Technical Support Needed**: Select if you need help setting up the equipment.
- **Training or Orientation Required**: Select if you need training or orientation before using the equipment.
- **Transportation Needed**: Select if you need transportation services for moving the equipment.
- **Insurance or Liability Required**: Select if you need insurance or liability coverage for the equipment.
- **Special Requirements or Constraints**: Enter any specific needs or restrictions for the equipment.
- **Alternative Dates if Primary Date Unavailable**: Enter alternative dates if your primary date is not available.
- **Additional Comments or Notes**: Enter any other information or comments.
