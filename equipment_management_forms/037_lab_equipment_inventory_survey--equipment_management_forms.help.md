# Lab Equipment Inventory Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information about lab equipment, enabling accurate inventory management and maintenance planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the lab equipment in the "equipment_details" field.
2. Specify the location where the equipment is housed in the "location" field.
3. Assess the condition of the equipment (e.g., good, fair, poor) in the "condition" field.
4. Select the type of equipment from the options provided in the "equipment_type" field.
5. Enter the current quantity of the equipment in the "quantity" field.
6. Identify the person responsible for maintaining the equipment in the "responsible_person" field.
7. If applicable, enter the maintenance date for the equipment in the "maintenance_date" field.
8. Select the frequency of maintenance required (daily, weekly, monthly, etc.) in the "maintenance_frequency" field.
9. Add any additional comments or notes about the equipment in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Equipment Details** (`equipment_details`): Enter a brief description of the equipment, including its model, serial number, or any other relevant details.
- **Location** (`location`): Specify the room, shelf, or location where the equipment is currently housed.
- **Condition** (`condition`): Assess the overall condition of the equipment, e.g., good, fair, or poor.
- **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`): Choose a category or type of equipment from the provided options.
- **Quantity** (`quantity`): Enter the number of units or pieces of equipment.
- **Responsible Person** (`responsible_person`): Identify the person responsible for maintaining the equipment.
- **Maintenance Date** (`maintenance_date`): If applicable, enter the date of the last maintenance or inspection.
- **Maintenance Frequency** (`maintenance_frequency`): Choose the frequency of maintenance required (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
- **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional comments or notes about the equipment, its maintenance, or other relevant information.

## Tips
- Be as accurate as possible when entering equipment details and quantities.
- If you are unsure about the maintenance frequency, consult with the equipment manufacturer or the person responsible for maintenance.
- Keep the notes field for any future reference or communication about the equipment.
