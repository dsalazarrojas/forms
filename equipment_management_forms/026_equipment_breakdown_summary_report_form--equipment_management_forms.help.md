# equipment_breakdown_summary_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture comprehensive information about equipment breakdowns, including incident history, repair status, failure cause, and maintenance schedules. It helps in tracking and analyzing equipment performance and identifying areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you are filling it out for a legitimate equipment breakdown or maintenance schedule.
2. Enter the Equipment Breakdown Summary Form (page 1) with a brief description of the issue or breakdown.
3. Complete the Equipment Details (page 2) section, including equipment type, manufacturer, model number, and other relevant details.
4. Document the Incident History (page 3) and Repair History (page 4) sections, detailing past incidents or repairs.
5. Update the Repair Status (page 5) field according to the current status of the repair.
6. Identify the Failure Cause (page 5) and enter it in the corresponding field.
7. Record the Repair Cost (page 5) associated with the current repair.
8. Add any Comments (page 5) or notes related to the equipment.
9. Designate a Contact Person (page 5) to handle the issue, if necessary.
10. Update the Maintenance Status (page 6) and schedule the Next Scheduled Maintenance Date (page 6) according to the equipment's maintenance schedule.
11. Enter the Last Maintenance Date (page 6) and Last Inspection Date (page 6) if applicable.
12. Provide a detailed description of the Equipment Condition (page 7) and Equipment Description (page 8).
13. Add any additional Notes (page 8) or comments.
14. Upload any Photos (page 9) related to the equipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Breakdown Summary Form** (Page 1)
	+ A brief description of the issue or breakdown.
* **Equipment Details** (Page 2)
	+ Enter the equipment type.
	+ Specify the manufacturer.
	+ Enter the model number.
	+ Enter any other relevant details.
* **Incident History** (Page 3)
	+ Document past incidents or issues.
* **Repair History** (Page 4)
	+ Record previous repair attempts or outcomes.
* **Repair Status** (Page 5)
	+ Update according to the current status of the repair (Pending, Completed, Cancelled).
* **Failure Cause** (Page 5)
	+ Enter the underlying reason for the equipment breakdown.
* **Repair Cost** (Page 5)
	+ Track the cost associated with the repair.
* **Comments** (Page 5)
	+ Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Contact Person** (Page 5)
	+ Designate a person to handle the issue, if necessary.
* **Equipment Type** (Page 6)
	+ Categorize the equipment into A, B, C, or D types.
* **Location** (Page 6)
	+ Specify where the equipment is located.
* **Serial Number** (Page 6)
	+ Enter the equipment's serial number.
* **Asset Tag** (Page 6)
	+ Enter the asset tag number.
* **Purchase Date** (Page 6)
	+ Track the date the equipment was purchased.
* **Manufacturer** (Page 6)
	+ Specify the equipment's manufacturer.
* **Model Number** (Page 6)
	+ Enter the equipment's model number.
* **Maintenance Status** (Page 7)
	+ Update according to the equipment's maintenance status (Pending, Completed, In Progress).
* **Last Maintenance Date** (Page 7)
	+ Record the date of the last maintenance.
* **Last Inspection Date** (Page 7)
	+ Track the date of the last inspection.
* **Next Scheduled Maintenance Date** (Page 7)
	+ Schedule the next maintenance date.
* **Equipment Condition** (Page 8)
	+ Describe the current condition of the equipment.
* **Notes** (Page 8)
	+ Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Photos** (Page 9)
	+ Upload any photos related to the equipment.
* **Equipment Location** (Page 9)
	+ Track the current location of the equipment.
* **Equipment Description** (Page 10)
	+ Provide a detailed description of the equipment.
