# outdoor_equipment_check_list - Help Guide
## Purpose
The outdoor equipment check list form is used to verify that all equipment is properly checked and accounted for before use in outdoor activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant details, including any relevant information about the participant, such as their contact details.
2. Select the contact method(s) that the participant will be using to contact them.
3. Check the gear readiness by selecting the status of the equipment that will be used.
4. Enter the date of the hike or outdoor activity.
5. Choose the type of activity that will be conducted.
6. Add any participant notes that are relevant to the activity.
7. Select the location of the activity.
8. Choose the current weather conditions.
9. Enter the number of participants that will be joining the activity.
10. Check if there will be a guide present during the activity.
11. Select the status of the equipment.
12. Check the emergency procedures that will be followed in case of an emergency.
13. Review and agree to the participant agreement.
14. Add any notes that are relevant to the activity.
15. Select the status of the equipment for the activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant details**: Enter the participant's details, including their name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Info**: Select the contact method(s) that the participant will be using to contact them.
	+ **Email**: The participant's email address.
	+ **Phone**: The participant's phone number.
	+ **Address**: The participant's physical address.
* **Gear Readiness**: Check the status of the equipment that will be used for the activity.
* **Hike Date**: Enter the date of the hike or outdoor activity.
* **Activity Type**: Choose the type of activity that will be conducted.
	+ **Hike**: A hike in a wilderness area.
	+ **Camp**: A camping trip.
	+ **Other**: Other activities, such as rock climbing, kayaking, etc.
* **Participant Notes**: Add any notes that are relevant to the participant, such as medical conditions or special requirements.
* **Activity Location**: Select the location of the activity.
	+ **Mountain**: A mountainous terrain.
	+ **Forest**: A forested area.
	+ **Park**: A park or protected area.
* **Weather Condition**: Choose the current weather conditions.
	+ **Sunny**: Clear skies and warm temperatures.
	+ **Cloudy**: Overcast skies.
	+ **Rainy**: Rain or precipitation.
* **Participant Count**: Enter the number of participants that will be joining the activity.
* **Guide Info**: Check if there will be a guide present during the activity.
* **Equipment Checklist**: Add any notes that are relevant to the equipment that will be used.
* **Emergency Procedure**: Check the emergency procedures that will be followed in case of an emergency.
	+ **First Aid**: Basic first aid procedures will be followed in case of an injury.
	+ **Fire Safety**: Fire safety procedures will be followed in case of a fire.
	+ **Weather Alert**: Weather-related emergency procedures will be followed.
* **Participant Agreement**: Review and agree to the participant agreement, which outlines the terms and conditions of the activity.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes that are relevant to the activity.
* **Equipment Status**: Select the status of the equipment.
	+ **Available**: The equipment is available for use.
	+ **Not Available**: The equipment is not available for use.
	+ **In Use**: The equipment is currently in use.
