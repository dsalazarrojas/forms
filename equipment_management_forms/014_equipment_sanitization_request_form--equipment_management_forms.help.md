# equipment_sanitization_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request equipment sanitization, which is the process of cleaning and disinfecting equipment to prevent the spread of germs and contamination.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Request Form page with the required information.
2. Provide detailed information about the equipment in the Equipment Details field.
3. Select a sanitization schedule that works for you in the Sanitization Schedule field.
4. Enter the contact person's name in the Contact Person field.
5. Enter the contact person's email in the Contact Email field.
6. Enter the contact person's phone number in the Contact Phone field.
7. Choose the sanitization frequency in the Sanitization Frequency field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Form**: This field is used to request equipment sanitization.
	+ **Equipment Details**: Provide detailed information about the equipment that needs sanitization.
	+ **Sanitization Schedule**: Select a date for the sanitization to occur.
	+ **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person who should be contacted for further information.
	+ **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
	+ **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
	+ **Sanitization Frequency**: Choose the frequency of the sanitization (e.g., weekly, monthly, etc.).
