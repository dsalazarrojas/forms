# equipment_lookup_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The equipment lookup form allows users to search for equipment information by various attributes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the mandatory fields: equipment category, equipment details, and equipment status.
2. Select the required option for equipment category.
3. Optionally, fill in the other fields to narrow down your search results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **equipment_details** (text, required): Enter a description of the equipment you are searching for.
* **assigned_tool** (select_one, optional): Select the assigned tool for the equipment.
* **equipment_category** (select_multiple, required): Select the category of the equipment you are searching for.
* **equipment_status** (select_one, optional): Select the status of the equipment.
* **equipment_type** (select_multiple, optional): Select the type of the equipment.
* **equipment_description** (text, optional): Enter a description of the equipment you are searching for.
* **equipment_serial_number** (text, optional): Enter the serial number of the equipment.
* **equipment_asset_tag** (text, optional): Enter the asset tag of the equipment.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill in the required fields to get accurate results.
* Select relevant options to narrow down your search results.
* If you are unsure about a particular attribute, you can leave it blank to include all possibilities.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact your administrator or support team.
