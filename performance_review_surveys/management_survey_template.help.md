# management_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to collect feedback on management performance and effectiveness. It's a tool to help managers grow and improve their skills, leading to a more positive work environment and better outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly, based on your personal experience and observations.
2. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
3. Be specific when providing feedback and suggestions for areas of improvement.
4. Use the text fields to add any additional comments or insights.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with your manager's overall performance?** (`Very satisfied`, `Somewhat satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`): Rate your satisfaction level with your manager's overall performance.
* **How often do you receive constructive feedback from your manager?** (`Daily`, `Weekly`, `Monthly`, `Rarely`, `Never`): Indicate how frequently you receive feedback from your manager.
* **Which leadership qualities does your manager consistently demonstrate?** (`Clear communication`, `Supports growth`, `Empowers team`, `Decisive`, `Approachable`): Select all the leadership qualities that your manager consistently demonstrates.
* **What specific areas could your manager improve upon?**: Provide specific suggestions on areas where your manager can improve.
* **Do you have any other comments or suggestions regarding management?**: Add any additional insights or suggestions you have for your manager's growth and improvement.
