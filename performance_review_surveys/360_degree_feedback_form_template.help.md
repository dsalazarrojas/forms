# 360_degree_feedback_form_template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather comprehensive feedback on an individual's performance and identify areas for development. It is meant for peer, direct report, and manager reviews, providing a 360-degree view of an employee's strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "How often do you interact with the employee being reviewed?" by selecting one of the provided options: Daily, Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Rarely.
2. Rate the employee's "Overall Performance" by selecting one of the provided categories: Outstanding, Exceeds expectations, Meets expectations, Needs development, or Below expectations.
3. Choose one or more strengths that best describe the employee from the list of provided strengths (Communication skills, Teamwork & collaboration, Problem-solving, Adaptability, Leadership potential, Proactiveness, Technical expertise, Customer focus).
4. Rate the employee's contribution to team goals and objectives as "Very effectively", "Effectively", "Moderately effectively", "Needs improvement", or "Not applicable".
5. Provide one area where the employee could further develop or improve.
6. Share a specific example of when the employee demonstrated excellence or positive impact.
7. Add any additional feedback or comments about the employee's performance or potential.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you interact with the employee being reviewed?** (`daily_frequency`, `select`, required)
  This question helps to gauge the frequency of interactions between the reviewer and the employee. Choose the option that best reflects your interaction frequency.
* **Overall performance** (`overall_performance`, `select`, required)
  This question helps to evaluate the employee's overall performance. Select the category that best represents their performance level.
* **Strengths** (`strengths`, `multiple`, required)
  Choose one or more strengths that best describe the employee's abilities and qualities.
* **Contribution to team goals and objectives** (`contribution`, `select`, required)
  Rate the employee's contribution to team goals and objectives as "Very effectively", "Effectively", "Moderately effectively", "Needs improvement", or "Not applicable".
* **Area for development** (`development_area`, `text`, required)
  Provide one area where the employee could further develop or improve.
* **Example of excellence or positive impact** (`example`, `text`, required)
  Share a specific example of when the employee demonstrated excellence or positive impact.
* **Additional feedback or comments** (`feedback`, `text`, optional)
  Add any additional feedback or comments about the employee's performance or potential.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide specific examples and concrete feedback whenever possible.
* Consider the employee's role and responsibilities when providing feedback.
* Be honest and constructive in your feedback to help the employee grow and develop.
