# employee_performance_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather honest feedback from employees on their performance, satisfaction, and development within the organization. This will help management understand the needs and challenges of employees and make data-driven decisions to support employee growth.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please take a few minutes to read each question carefully and answer honestly.
- Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
- Provide detailed responses for text-based questions.
- Make sure to answer all required questions, and you can skip optional questions if you prefer.
- Submit the form to provide your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Overall Satisfaction
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`Overall Satisfaction`, `single`, required):
	+ This question asks how satisfied you are with your role at the organization, on a scale of satisfaction.
* **Communication from Management** (`How would you rate communication from management?`, `single`, required):
	+ This question rates how effectively management communicates with employees on a scale of good, fair, or poor communication.
* **Professional Development Support** (`Do you feel supported in your professional development?`, `single`, required):
	+ This question checks if employees feel supported in their professional growth and development.
* **Engaging Activities** (`Which aspects of your role do you find most engaging?`, `multiple`, optional):
	+ Select all that apply from a list of engaging activities in your role, such as project work, team collaboration, learning new skills, client interaction, or problem-solving.
* **Recognition** (`Do you feel recognized for your contributions?`, `single`, required):
	+ This question assesses whether employees feel valued and recognized for their contributions.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions** (`What could improve your overall experience at work?`, `text`, required):
	+ Please provide specific comments or suggestions to improve employee experience.
* **Open-Ended Comments or Suggestions** (`Do you have any additional comments or suggestions?`, `text`, optional):
	+ Provide any other comments, suggestions, or feedback you may have that were not covered in previous questions.
