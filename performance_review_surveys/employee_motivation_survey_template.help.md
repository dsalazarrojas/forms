# employee_motivation_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees on their motivation and overall work experience. The survey aims to understand what factors motivate employees in their current roles and identify areas for improvement to enhance employee motivation and job satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly, using the options provided for each question.
2. Select all that apply for questions with multiple options.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the text field at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Overall, how satisfied are you with your role at the organization?
* **Overall, how satisfied are you with your role at the organization?** (Single): Rate your overall satisfaction with your role using the provided scale:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied

### How would you rate communication from management?
* **How would you rate communication from management?** (Single): Rate your experience with communication from management using the provided scale:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

### Do you feel supported in your professional development?
* **Do you feel supported in your professional development?** (Single): Answer this question with the provided options:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Partially

### What factors most motivate you in your current role? (Select all that apply)
* **What factors most motivate you in your current role?** (Multiple): Select all the factors that motivate you the most:
	+ Recognition
	+ Compensation
	+ Work-life balance
	+ Growth opportunities
	+ Challenging tasks
	+ Positive team culture

### What could be improved to enhance your daily work experience?
* **What could be improved to enhance your daily work experience?** (Text): Provide any suggestions or comments on how to improve your daily work experience.

### Do you feel your contributions are valued by your team and the organization?
* **Do you feel your contributions are valued by your team and the organization?** (Single): Rate your agreement with this statement:
	+ Strongly agree
	+ Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Disagree
	+ Strongly disagree

### Any additional comments or suggestions?
* **Any additional comments or suggestions?** (Text): Provide any additional feedback or suggestions to help improve employee motivation and job satisfaction.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Select all that apply for questions with multiple options.
* Provide clear and concise answers for text fields.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to reach out to us at [contact email].
