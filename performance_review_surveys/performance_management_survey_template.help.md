# performance_management_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The performance management survey is designed to gather feedback on the current performance management process and tools, helping to identify areas for improvement and inform data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Read each question carefully and select the most accurate response for your experience and observations.
2.  Select all that apply for the question "What aspects of the performance review process do you find most effective?" 
3.  Provide clear and concise answers in the text fields.
4.  If you have any additional comments or feedback, please share them in the text box at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### How satisfied are you with the current performance management process overall?
* **<label> How satisfied are you with the current performance management process overall?** (`question_1`, `single`, required)
	+ This question asks for your overall satisfaction with the current performance management process. Please select the response that best reflects your experience.
* **How often do you engage in formal performance discussions or reviews with your manager/team lead?** (`question_2`, `single`, required)
	+ This question is about how frequently you participate in formal performance reviews with your manager or team lead. Please select the option that best describes your experience.
* **What is the clarity of performance goals and expectations?** (`question_3`, `single`, required)
	+ This question assesses the clarity of performance goals and expectations. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **What aspects of the performance review process do you find most effective?** (`question_4`, `multiple`, required)
	+ This question is about identifying the most effective aspects of the performance review process. Please select all that apply.
* **What specific improvements would you suggest for our performance management process or tools?** (`question_5`, `text`, optional)
	+ This is an open-ended question for you to provide any specific improvements or suggestions for the performance management process or tools.
* **Do you feel the performance management process contributes to your professional growth and development?** (`question_6`, `single`, required)
	+ This question is about your personal growth and development. Select the response that best reflects your feelings about the performance management process.
* **Please share any additional comments or feedback regarding performance management.** (`question_7`, `text`, optional)
	+ This text box is for any additional comments or feedback you have regarding the performance management process or tools.

## Tips
* Please be honest and sincere in your responses.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, please provide them in the text fields.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please share them in the text box at the end.
* If you are not sure about any question, please select the option that best reflects your experience.
