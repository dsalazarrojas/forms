# Self Evaluation Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Self Evaluation Form is a tool for users to assess their performance and development throughout a review period. It's designed to guide users in evaluating their strengths, weaknesses, and areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer truthfully.
2. Choose the best response for each question based on your experience and performance.
3. Be specific when providing feedback and examples.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with your overall performance this review period?**
  • **label:** How satisfied are you with your overall performance this review period?  
  • **type:** single 
  • **options:** Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied

* **How would you rate your contribution to team or company goals?**
  • **label:** How would you rate your contribution to team or company goals?  
  • **type:** single 
  • **options:** Exceeded expectations, Met all expectations, Met most expectations, Partially met expectations, Did not meet expectations

* **Which of the following areas represent your key achievements this period? (Select all that apply)**
  • **label:** Which of the following areas represent your key achievements this period?  
  • **type:** multiple 
  • **options:** Project completion, Process improvement, Skill development, Team collaboration, Problem solving, Client satisfaction

* **What challenges did you face, and how did you address them?**
  • **label:** What challenges did you face, and how did you address them?  
  • **type:** text 
  • **description:** Please describe the challenges you faced and the actions you took to address them.

* **What are your primary development goals for the next period?**
  • **label:** What are your primary development goals for the next period?  
  • **type:** text 
  • **description:** Please specify your main goals and objectives for the next review period.

* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback regarding your self-evaluation?**
  • **label:** Do you have any additional comments or feedback regarding your self-evaluation?  
  • **type:** text 
  • **description:** Please provide any additional insights or suggestions you think may be helpful for future performance and development.
