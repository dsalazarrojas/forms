## Purpose
The Gadget Prototype Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback on gadget prototypes, helping us understand user opinions and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **User Information**: Enter your personal details, including your name and email address. This information will help us contact you if we need to follow up on your feedback.
2.  **Gadget Type**: Choose the type of gadget that you have evaluated. This can be a desktop or laptop, or select "other" if the gadget is of a different type.
3.  **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the gadget. This will help us understand your general impression.
4.  **User Interface**: Describe your experience with the user interface of the gadget. This can include your impressions on its usability and ease of use.
5.  **Performance**: Rate the performance of the gadget, choosing from "fast", "slow", or "average".
6.  **Easiness**: Rate how easy or difficult it was to use the gadget, on a numerical scale.
7.  **Recommendation**: Indicate whether you would recommend the gadget or not, and if so, with what level of enthusiasm.
8.  **Feedback**: Provide any additional feedback or comments about the gadget.
9.  **Features**: If applicable, describe any additional features of the gadget that you found useful or otherwise.
10. **Comparison**: If you have evaluated similar gadgets, describe how they compare to this one.
11.  **Frequency**: Indicate how often you use the gadget.
12.  **Usability**: Check the usability of the gadget, choosing between two options.
13.  **Features Usefulness**: Describe any features of the gadget that you found useful or not.
14.  **Market Usefulness**: Describe how the gadget's features meet your needs in the market.
15.  **Similarity**: Describe how similar the gadget is to others you have used.
16.  **Usage**: Describe how you used the gadget.
17.  **Previous Experience**: Indicate whether you have had previous experience with similar gadgets.
18.  **Similar Product**: Indicate whether the gadget is similar to others you have used.
