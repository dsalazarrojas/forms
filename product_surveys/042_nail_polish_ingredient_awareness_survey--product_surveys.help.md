# Nail Polish Ingredient Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nail Polish Ingredient Awareness Survey is designed to gather information from users about their awareness and concerns regarding nail polish ingredients. This survey aims to better understand users' needs and preferences, which will be used to inform product development decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the ingredients you are concerned about from the list.
2. Choose how familiar you are with the ingredients.
3. Write down your concerns about the ingredients you selected.
4. Choose how you would like to be contacted for follow-up information.
5. Confirm your preferred contact method and follow-up time.
6. Provide any additional comments or feedback you would like to share.
7. Confirm your preferred follow-up method and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What ingredients are you concerned about?** (`ingredients`, `select_multiple`): This field allows you to select the ingredients that you are concerned about. Please choose from the list of options provided.
* **How familiar are you with the ingredients?** (`ingredient_knowledge`, `select_one`): This field asks about your level of familiarity with the ingredients you selected. Choose from the options: Very Familiar, Somewhat Familiar, or Not Familiar.
* **What concerns do you have about these ingredients?** (`concerns`, `text`): This field is for you to write down any concerns you have about the ingredients you selected.
* **How would you like to be contacted?** (`contact_info`, `select_one`): This field asks about your preferred contact method. Choose from the options: Email, Phone, or Not Contacted.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`): This field is for you to enter your email address if you would like to be contacted via email.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`): This field is for you to enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted via phone.
* **Any additional comments?** (`comments`, `text`): This field is for you to provide any additional comments or feedback you would like to share.
* **How would you like to be contacted for follow up?** (`follow_up`, `select_one`): This field asks about your preferred contact method for follow-up information. Choose from the options: Email, Phone, or Not Contacted.
* **Confirm Follow-Up?** (`confirm_follow_up`, `select_one`): This field asks you to confirm whether you would like to be contacted for follow-up information.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, `select_one`): This field asks you to confirm whether you would like to be contacted via email.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, `select_one`): This field asks you to confirm whether you would like to be contacted via phone.
* **Confirm Follow-Up Method** (`confirm_follow_up_method`, `select_one`): This field asks you to confirm your preferred contact method for follow-up information.
* **Confirm Follow-Up Time** (`confirm_follow_up_time`, `select_one`): This field asks you to confirm your preferred follow-up time.
* **Follow-Up Time** (`follow_up_time`, `time`): This field is for you to select the time of follow-up.
* **Follow-up Comments** (`comments`, `text`): This field is for you to provide any additional comments or feedback about the follow-up information.
