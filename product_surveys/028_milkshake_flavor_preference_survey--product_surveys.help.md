# Milkshake Flavor Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Milkshake Flavor Preference Survey is designed to collect feedback on milkshake flavors, helping us understand your preferences, satisfaction, and interest in new flavors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select all the flavors you like from the options on page 1.
2. Choose how satisfied you are with our current milkshake flavors on page 2.
3. Select the flavors you are interested in trying on page 3.
4. Rate your satisfaction with the flavor of your favorite milkshake on page 4.
5. Indicate how much you like our milkshake flavors on page 5.
6. Choose your favorite milkshake flavor from the options on page 6.
7. Select how satisfied you are with our milkshake flavors on page 7.
8. Indicate how much you like our milkshake flavors on page 8.
9. (Optional pages 9-18 are identical, please only answer if you have something to say, otherwise, you can skip them.)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What flavors do you like?** (`Vanilla, Chocolate, Strawberry`, select_multiple): Choose all the flavors you like from the options provided.
* **How satisfied are you with our current milkshake flavors?** (`Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied`, select_one): Choose one option that best describes your level of satisfaction.
* **What flavors are you interested in trying?** (`Vanilla, Chocolate, Strawberry`, select_multiple): Choose all the flavors you are interested in trying from the options provided.
* **How satisfied are you with the flavor of your favorite milkshake?** (`Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied`, select_one): Choose one option that best describes your level of satisfaction.
* **How interested are you in trying new milkshake flavors?** (`Very Interested, Somewhat Interested, Not Interested`, select_multiple): Choose all the options that apply to you.
* **How much do you like the current flavor of our milkshake?** (`High, Medium, Low`, select_multiple): Choose all the options that best describe your level of like.
* **What is your favorite milkshake flavor?** (`Vanilla, Chocolate, Strawberry`, select_one): Choose one option that is your favorite milkshake flavor.
* **How satisfied are you with our milkshake flavors?** (`Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied`, select_multiple): Choose all the options that apply to you.
* **How much do you like our milkshake flavors?** (`High, Medium, Low`, select_multiple): Choose all the options that apply to you.

## Tips
- Take your time to read each question carefully.
- Choose the options that best describe your thoughts.
- If you have any other comments or suggestions, please let us know.
