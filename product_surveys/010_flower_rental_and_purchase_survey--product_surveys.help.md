# Flower Rental And Purchase Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about flower rental or purchase requests. It's intended to help the user provide the necessary details about their requirements, allowing the service provider to offer the best-suited flower arrangements for their occasion.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the occasion for which you are renting or purchasing flowers.
2. Choose the quantity of flowers you require.
3. Select the color of flowers you prefer.
4. Choose the type of flowers you like (e.g., roses, lilies, etc.).
5. Indicate if you need delivery or if you prefer to pick up the flowers.
6. If applicable, choose a delivery date for the flowers.
7. Indicate if you require any special payment terms.
8. Provide your contact information (email and phone number).
9. If applicable, select any event planners you are working with.
10. Select the type of events you are attending or hosting (e.g., wedding, corporate, etc.).
11. If you have any other events, provide them in the "Other" field.
12. Choose the type of flower arrangement you prefer (e.g., single, multiple, bouquet, etc.).
13. Indicate if you want the flowers to be of a specific size.
14. Select the color of flowers you want.
15. If you have other colors in mind, provide them in the "Other" field.
16. If you want to pay for the flowers in instalments, indicate this in the "Flower Price" field.
17. You can add any additional comments or notes about your flower rental or purchase in the "Notes" field.
