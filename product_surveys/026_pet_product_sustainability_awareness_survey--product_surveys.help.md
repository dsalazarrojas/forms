# Pet Product Sustainability Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on pet owners' perceptions and behaviors related to pet sustainability, with the goal of understanding the current state of sustainability in the pet industry and identifying areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Introduction**: This page provides a brief introduction to the survey and explains its purpose.
2. **Pet Type**: Please select the type of pet you own (Cat, Dog, or Other).
3. **Pet Ownership Status**: Please select your current pet ownership status (Own a Pet, Renting a Pet, or Other).
4. **Motivation for Eco-Friendly Pet Products**: Please select all reasons why you choose eco-friendly pet products (Yes, No).
5. **Sustainability Goals**: Please select the goal that is most important to you (Reducing plastic, Reducing carbon footprint, Cost savings).
6. **Importance of Sustainability**: Please select how important you think sustainability is to you (Very important, Somewhat important, Not important at all).
7. **Product Purchase Frequency**: Please select how often you purchase pet products (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Rarely).
8. **Eco-Friendly Features**: Please select all features that are important to you when purchasing pet products (Biodegradable, Eco-friendly packaging, Cruelty-free, Non-GMO).
9. **Eco-Friendly Product Purchasing**: Please select how often you purchase eco-friendly pet products (Always, Sometimes, Rarely).
10. **Awareness of Pet Sustainability**: Please select how much you think pet owners should care about pet sustainability (A lot, Somewhat, Not at all).
11. **Open-Ended Question**: Please share any additional thoughts or comments about pet sustainability.
12. **Demographic Awareness**: Please select how much you think the pet industry is doing to promote pet sustainability (A lot, Somewhat, Not at all).
13. **Importance of Pet Sustainability**: Please select how much you think pet sustainability is important (Very important, Somewhat important, Not at all important).
14. **Additional Comments**: Please provide any additional comments about pet sustainability.
15. **Email**: Please enter your email address.
16. **Phone Number**: Please enter your phone number.
17. **Age**: Please enter your age.
18. **Zip Code**: Please enter your zip code.
19. **Comments**: Please provide any additional comments.
20. **Survey Completion Date**: This will be automatically generated when you complete the survey.
21. **Submission Time**: This will be automatically generated when you submit the survey.
