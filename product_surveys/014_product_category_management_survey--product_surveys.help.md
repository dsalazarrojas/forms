# Product Category Management Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for product category management, allowing users to categorize and organize products in an inventory or catalog.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one or more categories that the product belongs to in the Category Selection field.
2. Provide a brief description of the category in the Category Description field.
3. Enter the name of the category in the Category Name field.
4. Add any relevant keywords for the category in the Category Keywords field.
5. Optionally, enter any additional product preferences in the Product Preferences field.

* **Category Name** (`Category Name`, optional): Enter the name of the category.
* **Category Keywords** (`Category Keywords`, optional): Enter any relevant keywords for the category.
