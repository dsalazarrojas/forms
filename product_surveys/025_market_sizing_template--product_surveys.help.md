# Market Sizing Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Market Sizing Template is a tool to help estimate the potential revenue for a product or service in a specific market. This template guides you through a series of questions to gather essential information about your market, competition, and revenue projections.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, navigate through its pages and fill out each field according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, Optional): Enter your company's name as it would be displayed on a business card or marketing materials.
  * This will help in identifying your company for our records and any follow-up correspondence.

- **Revenue Estimate** (`revenue_estimate`, number, Optional): Estimate your revenue for the target market geography.
  * If you have a specific revenue figure in mind, provide it; otherwise, provide an estimate based on your best understanding of the market.

- **Target Market Geography** (`target_market_geography`, select_one, Optional): Select the geographic area you are targeting with your product or service.
  * Choose from the options provided, and be as specific as possible (e.g., country, region, city).

- **Target Market Segment** (`target_market_segment`, select_one, Optional): Select the specific segment of the market you are targeting.
  * Choose from the options provided, which could be industry, demographics, or other relevant categories.

- **Competition Analysis** (`competition_analysis`, text, Optional): Describe the competitive landscape for your product or service in the target market.
  * Provide a brief analysis of your competitors, their strengths, and weaknesses.

- **Revenue Share** (`revenue_share`, number, Optional): If you have an established revenue stream in the target market, estimate the percentage of the market that you own.
  * This will help us understand your market presence.

- **Revenue Growth Rate** (`revenue_estimate`, number, Optional): Estimate the expected growth rate of your revenue in the target market over a specific period.
  * Provide a rate (e.g., 5%, 10%, 20%) and the unit of time (e.g., quarter, year, month).

- **Revenue Growth Rate Unit** (`revenue_growth_rate_unit`, select_one, Optional): Choose the unit of time for your revenue growth rate estimate.
  * Select from the options provided, such as quarter, year, or month.

- **Total Revenue Potential** (`total_revenue_potential`, number, Optional): If you have a revenue estimate, provide the total revenue potential for the target market.
  * This is the sum of the revenue estimate and the revenue share.
