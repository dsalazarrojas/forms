# Sensitive Skin Product Testing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about sensitive skin products. It helps the company understand how its products perform in real-world usage and to identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the product you are testing from the options provided.
2. Choose your skin condition from the options provided.
3. Enter the name of the product you used.
4. Describe any skin irritation you experienced.
5. Provide your overall feedback about the product.
6. Rate the product out of 10.
7. Decide if you would recommend the product.
8. If you would recommend it, explain why.
9. Add any additional comments you have about the product.
10. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
11. Enter the date and time you used the product.
12. Rate the product out of 10 (optional).
13. Enter the name of the product.
14. Enter the company name of the product.
15. Enter the type of product.
16. Enter the category of the product.
17. Enter the subcategory of the product.
18. Enter the price range of the product.
19. Enter the country of origin of the product.
20. Enter the ingredients of the product.
21. Enter any allergic reactions you experienced.
22. Enter any side effects you experienced.
23. Enter any additional comments about the product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What product are you testing** (Page 1, Required): Enter the name of the product you are testing.
* **Skin condition** (Page 2, Required): Choose the skin condition you are experiencing.
* **Name of the product used** (Page 2, Required): Enter the name of the product you used.
* **Skin irritation** (Page 3, Optional): Check any skin irritation you experienced, such as redness, burning sensation, itching, or stinging sensation.
* **Overall feedback** (Page 5, Required): Provide your overall feedback about the product, such as its effectiveness, ease of use, or any issues you encountered.
* **Rating** (Page 5, Required): Rate the product out of 10, with 10 being excellent and 1 being poor.
* **Would recommend** (Page 6, Required): Decide if you would recommend the product and why.
* **Why would you recommend it** (Page 7, Optional): If you would recommend the product, provide an explanation.
* **Comments** (Page 8, Optional): Add any additional comments about the product.
* **Email** (Page 9, Optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone number** (Page 9, Optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date** (Page 10, Optional): Enter the date you used the product.
* **Time** (Page 10, Optional): Enter the time you used the product.
* **Product rating** (Page 11, Optional): Rate the product out of 10 (optional).
* **Product name** (Page 12, Required): Enter the name of the product.
* **Company** (Page 13, Required): Enter the company name of the product.
* **Type** (Page 13, Required): Enter the type of product.
* **Category** (Page 14, Required): Enter the category of the product.
* **Subcategory** (Page 14, Required): Enter the subcategory of the product.
* **Price range** (Page 15, Required): Enter the price range of the product.
* **Country of origin** (Page 15, Required): Enter the country of origin of the product.
* **Ingredients** (Page 16, Required): Enter the ingredients of the product.
* **Allergic reactions** (Page 17, Required): Enter any allergic reactions you experienced.
* **Side effects** (Page 18, Required): Enter any side effects you experienced.
* **Other comments** (Page 19, Optional): Enter any additional comments about the product.
