# Productivity App Recommendation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to collect information about your preferences and requirements for a productivity app. Your responses will help us understand your needs and improve the app to provide a better experience for you and other users.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Start" button to begin the survey.
2. Select the options that apply to you for each question.
3. If you have any trouble or questions, feel free to contact us.
4. Click "Submit" to submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (user_information, text, optional)**: Please enter a brief introduction about yourself, so we can better understand your profile.
* **What are your preferred tools and devices? (tool_preferences, select_multiple, optional)**: Choose all the tools and devices you are familiar with or use regularly. For example, Microsoft Office, Google Apps, or Other.
* **Which features are you looking for in a productivity app? (app_features, select_one, optional)**: Select the feature that matters most to you. For example, Task management, Project management, Time tracking, or Organization and focus.
* **What are your app preferences? (app_preferences, select_multiple, optional)**: Select your preferred app type. Choose from Android, iOS, Web, or Desktop.
* **What devices do you use? (device_preferences, select_multiple, optional)**: Indicate if you use devices such as Android, iOS, or Desktop computers. You may choose more than one option.
* **What hours do you work? (work_hours, time, optional)**: Enter the hours you typically work. Please use the format "HH:MM" (e.g., 09:00 for 9:00 AM, 15:00 for 3:00 PM).
* **Note:** If you have any other comments or suggestions about your preferences or needs, feel free to add them in the text box at the end of the form.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when selecting your options.
* If you're unsure about a feature or app preference, choose the one that best describes your current situation.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey! Your feedback is valuable to us, and we'll use it to improve the app for your benefit.
