# nail_polish_purchase_behavior_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about customers' behavior and preferences when it comes to nail polish purchases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions honestly and select all that apply. You can choose from the options provided in each question, and you can select as many or as few answers as you think are relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How often do you buy nail polish?** (`how_often_do_you_buy_nail_polish`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks how often you buy nail polish. You can choose from the options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, or Never.
- **Which shopping channel?** (`which_shopping_channel`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks where you usually buy nail polish. You can choose from the options: Online, In-store, Both, or Other.
- **Favorite brand?** (`favorite_brand`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks about your favorite brand of nail polish. You can choose from the options: OPI, Sally Hansen, Revlon, or Other.
- **Preferred payment method?** (`preferred_payment_method`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks how you usually pay for nail polish. You can choose from the options: Cash, Credit card, Mobile payment, or Bank transfer.
- **Age group?** (`age_group`, `text`, required: false): This question asks about your age group. Please enter your age group (e.g., 18-24, 25-34, etc.).
- **How much do you spend?** (`how_much_do_you_spend`, `number`, required: false): This question asks how much you usually spend on nail polish. Please enter a numerical value.
- **How often do you get nail services?** (`how_often_do_you_get_nail_services`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks how often you get nail services (e.g., salon treatments). You can choose from the options: Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Rarely, or Never.
- **Preferred location?** (`preferred_location`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks where you prefer to get nail services. You can choose from the options: Salon, Spa, At home, or Other.
- **How often do you buy from online stores?** (`how_often_do_you_buy_from_online_stores`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks how often you buy nail polish from online stores. You can choose from the options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, or Never.

You can choose from the options: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.

- **Have you had a recent purchasing experience?** (`have_you_had_a_recent_purchasing_experience`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks if you have had a recent purchasing experience with nail polish. You can choose from the options: Yes, No.
- **Why didn't you buy?** (`why_didnt_you_buy`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks why you might not have purchased nail polish in the past. You can choose from the options: Price, Product unavailability, or Other.

- **What influenced your purchases?** (`what_influenced_your_purchases`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks what influenced your decisions to buy nail polish. You can choose from the options: Price, Reviews, Recommendation, Product features, or Other.
