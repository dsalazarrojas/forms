# Feature Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Feature Request Form is a tool used to gather feedback and suggestions from customers on product features and requirements. It helps us understand what customers are looking for in a product and improve our product development process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the "Customer Suggestions" question by selecting "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have any suggestions or ideas for the product.
2. Answer the "Team Suggestions" question by selecting "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have any suggestions or ideas for the product team.
3. Provide a description of your feature request in the "Description" field. Be as specific as possible about what you are looking for.
4. Choose whether your feature request is for "Customer" or "Team" by selecting the corresponding option in the "Suggestions" dropdown.
5. Select the category of the feature request by choosing one of the options in the "Form Category" dropdown.
6. Provide a brief description of the feature request in the "Form Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Title** (`form_title`, `text`, required: false): A title for the feature request. Please make it clear and concise to help us understand your idea.
- **Customer Suggestions** (`customer_suggestions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Answer "Yes" if you have any suggestions or ideas for the product, or "No" otherwise.
- **Team Suggestions** (`team_suggestions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Answer "Yes" if you have any suggestions or ideas for the product team, or "No" otherwise.
- **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief and clear description of your feature request. Be as specific as possible about what you are looking for.
- **Fully Customizable** (`fully_customizable`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select "Yes" if the feature you are requesting is fully customizable, or "No" otherwise.
- **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose whether your feature request is for "Customer" or "Team" by selecting the corresponding option.
- **Form Category** (`form_category`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the category of the feature request from the options provided.
- **Form Description** (`form_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the feature request, explaining what the feature is about and why you think it would be beneficial.
