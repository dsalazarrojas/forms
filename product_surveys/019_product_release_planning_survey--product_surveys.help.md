# product_release_planning_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information and feedback from various stakeholders involved in product releases, ensuring that all stakeholders are aligned and that releases are properly planned and executed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and understand the questions and fields that apply to your role and involvement in the product release process.
2. Answer each question or input field truthfully and accurately, providing specific examples or explanations when necessary.
3. Ensure that your response is not a duplicate of another stakeholder's submission.
4. If you're unsure about any question or field, reach out to the product development team for clarification.
