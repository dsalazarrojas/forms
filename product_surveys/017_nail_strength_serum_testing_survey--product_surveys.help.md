# Nail Strength Serum Testing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect user feedback and experiences with nail strength serums. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability to provide valuable insights for our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. For each question, provide your honest answer based on your experience with the nail strength serum.
2. Please select the most suitable option from the provided choices.
3. For questions with no options, provide your answer in the text field.
4. Make sure to fill in all required fields to submit the form successfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Usage Experience** (`Usage Experience`, text, optional): Please describe your usage experience with the nail strength serum, including any relevant details such as frequency and duration of use.
* **Nail Strength** (`Nail Strength`, number, optional): Rate your nail strength, using a numerical value of your choice.
* **Usage Frequency** (`Usage Frequency`, text, optional): Describe how often you use the nail strength serum.
* **Overall Effectiveness** (`Overall Effectiveness`, select multiple, optional): Select all the options that best describe your overall experience with the nail strength serum:
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
	+ Not Sure
* **Recommendation** (`Recommendation`, text, optional): Share your recommendation or suggestion for the nail strength serum.
* **Product Recommender** (`Product Recommender`, select one, optional): Select whether you would recommend the product to others:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Maybe
* **Nail Condition** (`Nail Condition`, select multiple, optional): Select all the options that best describe your current nail condition:
	+ Brittle
	+ Soft
	+ Weak
	+ Healthy
* **Product Recommendation** (`Product Recommendation`, select multiple, optional): Select all the options that best describe your product recommendation:
	+ Recommend to others
	+ Not Recommend
* **Time Spent Testing** (`Time Spent Testing`, time, optional): Describe the amount of time you spent testing the nail strength serum.
* **Testing Period** (`Testing Period`, select one, optional): Select the duration of time spent testing the product:
	+ 1-2 weeks
	+ 3-4 weeks
	+ 5-6 weeks
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, text, optional): Share any additional feedback or comments about the nail strength serum.
* **Rating** (`Rating`, number, optional): Rate your experience with the nail strength serum using a numerical value.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Name** (`Name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Company** (`Company`, text, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Job Function** (`Job Function`, text, optional): Enter your job function.
* **Contact** (`Contact`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information.
