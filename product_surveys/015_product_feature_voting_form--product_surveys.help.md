# Product Feature Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Feature Voting Form is designed to collect user feedback and opinions on various product features. It helps us understand what features you like or dislike, and what you would like to see added or removed from the product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the features you're interested in from the list on page 1.
2. Choose the voting options for each feature on page 2.
3. Enter your voting scores for each feature on page 3.
4. Provide a brief description of the feature you're voting on on page 4 (optional).
5. Enter your email address on page 5.
6. Enter your phone number on page 6 (optional).
7. Select a date and time for your feedback on page 7 (optional).
8. Select a time for your feedback on page 8 (optional).
9. Select the features you're interested in on page 9 (optional).
10. Select the features you're interested in on page 10 (optional).
11. Enter your score for each feature on page 11 (required).
12. Provide any additional comments on page 12 (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Features** (page 1, select_one): Choose the features you're interested in voting on from the list.
* **Select Voting Options** (page 2, select_multiple): Choose the voting options for each feature you selected on page 1.
* **Enter Voting Scores** (page 3, number): Enter a score between 1 and 10 for each feature to indicate how much you like or dislike it.
* **Feature Description** (page 4, note): Provide a brief description of the feature you're voting on (optional).
* **Email** (page 5, email): Enter your email address to associate with your feedback.
* **Phone** (page 6, text): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Date and Time** (page 7, date): Select a date and time for your feedback (optional).
* **Time** (page 8, time): Select a time for your feedback (optional).
* **Page Nine** (page 9, select_one): Select the features you're interested in (optional).
* **Page Ten** (page 10, select_multiple): Select the features you're interested in (optional).
* **Page Eleven** (page 11, number): Enter your score for each feature (required).
* **Page Twelve** (page 12, note): Provide any additional comments on the feature you're voting on (optional).

## Tips

* Please provide honest and constructive feedback on the features you select.
* Use the provided options for each feature to guide your selection.
* Enter your score between 1 and 10 for each feature to ensure accurate data collection.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use page 12 to provide them.
