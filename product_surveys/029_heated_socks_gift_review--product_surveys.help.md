# Heated Socks Gift Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit reviews and feedback on gifts.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out each field completely and honestly.
2.  Enter your gift information in the provided fields.
3.  Select the type of gift you received (Gift, Gift Card, Other).
4.  Choose whether you're providing the gift type as Gift, Gift Card, or another option.
5.  Enter the date you received the gift.
6.  Enter the name and gender of the gift recipient.
7.  Select the currency of the gift's price (USD, USD$, EUR, Other).
8.  Choose how you rated the gift recipient's feedback.
9.  Rate how you liked the gift as a seller.
10.  Rate your overall satisfaction with the gift.
11.  Write a review of the gift.
12.  Choose whether the review is public or not.
13.  Enter the date you completed the review.
14.  Enter the store where you purchased the gift.
15.  Enter the order number and date.
16.  Enter the gift ID.
17.  Enter the product name and description.
18.  Enter the product URL.
19.  Enter your email address.
20.  Enter your phone number.
21.  Enter the store URL.
22.  Enter any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Heated Socks Gift Review**: This is the title of the form.
* **Gift Type**: Choose the type of gift you received (Gift, Gift Card, Other).
* **Received At**: Enter the date you received the gift.
* **Recipient Name**: Enter the name of the person who received the gift.
* **Recipient Gender**: Choose the gender of the gift recipient (Male, Female, Other).
* **Gift Price**: Enter the price of the gift.
* **Gift Price Currency**: Choose the currency of the gift's price (USD, USD$, EUR, Other).
* **Recipient Feedback**: Choose how you liked the gift recipient's feedback (Good, Bad, Neutral).
* **Gift Seller Feedback**: Rate how you liked the gift as a seller (Good, Bad, Neutral).
* **Gift Seller Rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the gift.
* **Gift Recipient Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the gift.
* **Review**: Write a review of the gift.
* **Review Public**: Choose whether the review is public or not.
* **Review At**: Enter the date you completed the review.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Store URL**: Enter the store URL.
* **Order Number**: Enter the order number.
* **Order Date**: Enter the order date.
* **Gift ID**: Enter the gift ID.
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product.
* **Product Description**: Enter a brief description of the product.
* **Product URL**: Enter the product URL.
* **Form Completed At**: Enter the date you completed the form.
* **Store**: Enter the store where you purchased the gift.

Note: Please fill out all the fields completely and honestly to ensure that your feedback is accurate and helpful. The information will be used to improve the gift-giving process.
