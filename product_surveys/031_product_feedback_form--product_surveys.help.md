# Product Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The product feedback form is designed to gather customer opinions on the quality, design, and overall experience of our product. Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time in providing it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "What do you think of this product?" in the text field.
2. Select your opinion on the quality of the product from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose your opinion on the product design from the dropdown menu.
4. Rate the product by entering a number from 1 to 5.
5. If you'd like to be contacted, please provide your contact number.
6. If you'd like to be contacted, please provide your email address.
7. If you've used the product recently, please select the date and time of your experience.
8. Provide any additional comments about your experience with the product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What do you think of this product?**: This field is a text field where you can provide your opinion on the product. Please answer in your own words.
* **Quality of product**: This field is a dropdown menu with three options: Very good, Average, and Bad. Select your honest opinion on the quality of the product.
* **Product design**: This field is a dropdown menu with three options: Good, Not good, and Average. Select your honest opinion on the product design.
* **Rating**: This field is a numeric field where you can rate the product from 1 to 5.
* **Contact number**: This field is a text field where you can provide your contact number if you'd like to be contacted by our team.
* **Email**: This field is an email field where you can provide your email address if you'd like to be contacted by our team.
* **Date**: This field is a date field where you can select the date you've used the product.
* **Time**: This field is a time field where you can select the time you've used the product.
* **Comments**: This field is a text field where you can provide any additional comments about your experience with the product.
