# Charger Market Research Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Charger Market Research Survey is a short survey designed to capture consumer preferences for chargers. It aims to gather information about your charger usage habits, preferred features, and pain points, as well as your likelihood to purchase new chargers in the next 6 months.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Select the appropriate answer options for each question, considering your current charger usage and preferences.
2. Provide accurate and honest answers to help us better understand your needs.
3. Fill out all required fields (*marked with *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Charger Market Research --** (Note): This is an introductory section that briefly explains the purpose of the survey.
* **respondent age_group** (Age Group, optional): Select the correct age range that best describes your age.
	+ Under 18: You are under 18 years old.
	+ 18 to 34: You are between 18 and 34 years old.
	+ 35 to 54: You are between 35 and 54 years old.
	+ 55 and over: You are 55 years old or older.
* **current_charger_type** (Current Charger Type, required): Select the type of charger you currently use the most.
	+ Wired: Wired chargers.
	+ Wireless: Wireless chargers.
	+ Both: Both wired and wireless chargers.
	+ None: You don't own a charger or haven't used one recently.
* **number_of_chargers** (Number of Chargers Owned, optional): Approximately how many chargers you own.
* **purchase_interest** (Purchase Interest, required): Select your likelihood to purchase a new charger in the next 6 months.
	+ Very unlikely: You are very unlikely to purchase a new charger.
	+ Unlikely: You are unlikely to purchase a new charger.
	+ Neutral: You are neutral about purchasing a new charger.
	+ Likely: You are likely to purchase a new charger.
	+ Very likely: You are very likely to purchase a new charger.
* **preferred_features** (Preferred Features, optional): Select the features that are most important to you when buying a charger.
	+ Fast charging: Fast charging speed.
	+ Portability: Portable charger.
	+ Multiple ports: Multiple USB ports.
	+ Brand reputation: Brand reputation.
	+ Price: Price of the charger.
* **average_spend** (Average Spend, optional): Approximately how much you are willing to pay for a charger in your local currency.
* **pain_points** (Pain Points, optional): Describe any common problems you experience with chargers.
* **brand_loyalty** (Brand Loyalty, optional): Do you prefer a single brand for chargers?
	+ True: Yes, you prefer a single brand.
	+ False: No, you don't prefer a single brand.
	+ Sometimes: You prefer a single brand sometimes.
* **additional_comments** (Additional Comments, optional): Any other feedback or comments for product teams.

Note: If you have any other questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact information].
