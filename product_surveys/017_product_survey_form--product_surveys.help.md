# Product Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Survey Form is a tool used to gather insights and feedback from customers on our products and services. This form is used to understand customer experiences, satisfaction levels, and preferences to improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Product Survey Form, follow these steps:

1.  Read each question carefully and answer the questions based on your true experience.
2.  For select and multiple select questions, choose one or multiple answers from the options provided.
3.  For numerical questions, enter the number that corresponds to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **How long have you been using our products/services?** (`product_survey_form`, number, optional)
    Please answer this question based on how long you have been using our products or services. For example, if you have been using our products for more than 6 months, select 6 or higher.
*   **Impressions on how we compete with other competitors** (`product_survey_form_impression`, text, optional)
    Share your thoughts about how our company competes with other competitors.
*   **Satisfaction with our products/services** (`product_survey_form_satisfaction`, select_one, optional)
    Rate your satisfaction with our products or services. Choose from Very good, Neutral, or Very bad.
*   **How would you rate our customer service?** (`product_survey_form_impression`, select_multiple, optional)
    Rate your experience with our customer service. Choose from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Bad.
*   **What was the main reason for your last purchase with us?** (`product_survey_form_impression`, select_one, optional)
    Select the main reason for your last purchase. Choose from To replace old products, To get a product recommendation, To purchase a gift, or Other.
*   **Do you have a suggestion for us?** (`product_survey_form_suggestion`, text, optional)
    Share any suggestions or ideas for our products or services.
*   **Overall experience with our products/services** (`product_survey_form_experience`, select_multiple, optional)
    Rate your overall experience with our products or services. Choose from Very good, Neutral, or Very bad.
*   **How often do you purchase from us?** (`product_survey_form_purchased`, number, optional)
    Enter the frequency of your purchases with us. For example, if you buy from us once a month, enter 1.
*   **Are you loyal to our products/services?** (`product_survey_form_loyalty`, select_one, optional)
    Answer this question based on your loyalty to our products or services. Choose True or False.
