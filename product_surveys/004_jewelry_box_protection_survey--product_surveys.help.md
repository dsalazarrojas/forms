# Jewelry Box Protection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Jewelry Box Protection Survey is a tool to gather your feedback on how you store and protect your jewelry. Your input will help us create a better experience for you and our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select one of the options in each section.
2. Enter your response to each question in the text field.
3. Make sure to answer all questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Do you protect your jewelry boxes with a cloth or pouch? (jewelry_box_protection_survey_page1)**: Choose 'Yes' if you cover your jewelry box to prevent scratches or damage, and 'No' if you don't use any protective covering. 
* **Please describe your storage habits for your jewelry. (storage_habits)**: Enter a clear and concise description of how you store your jewelry to help us understand your habits and preferences.
* **Do you think the jewelry box has any protection features? (protection_features)**: Choose 'Yes' if you believe the jewelry box has features such as padding or cushioning to protect your jewelry, and 'No' if you don't think it does.
* **Rate your satisfaction with the jewelry box protection. (satisfaction)**: Enter a numerical value between 1 and 5 to express your level of satisfaction with the protection of your jewelry box. 

## Tips
* Be as thorough as possible when describing your storage habits.
* Answer each question as accurately as you can based on your personal experience.
* Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve our product and service.
