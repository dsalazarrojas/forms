# Seasonal Product Launch Analysis Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The seasonal product launch analysis report is designed to collect data about the launch of new products, providing insights into sales channels, regions, and other relevant metrics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the product name in the "product name" field.
* Provide a brief description of the product in the "product description" field.
* Enter the launch date of the product in the "launch date" field.
* Enter the product price, including any sales tax or discounts, in the "product price" field.
* Select the sales channels for the product from the options provided in the "sales channels" field.
* Choose the regions where the product will be sold from the options provided in the "sales region" field.
* Select the countries where the product will be sold from the options provided in the "sales countries" field.
* Enter the sales profits for the product in the "sales profits" field.
* Enter the sales revenue for the product in the "sales revenue" field.
* Enter the sales costs for the product, including all expenses, in the "sales costs" field.
* Enter the product rating, reported by customers, in the "product rating" field.
* Enter the customer feedback about the product in the "customer feedback" field.
* Enter the launch time of the product in the "launch time" field.
* Select the sales season for the product from the options provided in the "sales season" field.
* Choose the seasonal status of the product from the options provided in the "product seasonal status" field.
* Enter the seasonal discount for the product in the "seasonal discount" field.
* Enter the product return rate in the "product return rate" field.
* Enter the customer demographics for the product in the "customer demographics" field.
* Enter the sales trends for the product in the "sales trends" field.
* Enter the sales forecasts for the product in the "sales forecasts" field.
* Enter the seasonality factor for the product in the "seasonality factor" field.
* Enter the sales trend for the product in the "sales trend" field.
* Select the season for the product from the options provided in the "season" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seasonal Product Launch** (1): Enter the name of the product being launched.
* **Product Name** (2): Enter a brief name for the product.
* **Product Description** (3): Provide a short description of the product.
* **Launch Date** (4): Enter the date the product will be launched.
* **Product Image** (5): Enter the image URL or link to the product image.
* **Product Price** (6): Enter the price of the product, including any sales tax or discounts.
* **Sales Channels** (7): Select where you will sell this product (Online Store, Offline Store, Both).
* **Sales Region** (8): Choose the region where you will sell this product (North America, Europe, Asia, Africa, Australia, South America).
* **Sales Countries** (9): Select the countries where you will sell this product (Yes, No).
* **Sales Profits** (10): Enter the sales profits for the product.
* **Sales Revenue** (11): Enter the sales revenue for the product.
* **Sales Costs** (12): Enter the sales costs for the product, including all expenses.
* **Product Rating** (13): Enter the product rating, reported by customers, from 0-5 stars.
* **Customer Feedback** (14): Enter customer feedback about the product.
* **Launch Time** (15): Enter the launch time of the product.
* **Sales Season** (16): Select the season for the product (Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter).
* **Product Seasonal Status** (17): Choose the seasonal status of the product (On Sale, Off Sale, Limited Time Offer).
* **Seasonal Discount** (18): Enter the seasonal discount for the product.
* **Product Return Rate** (19): Enter the product return rate.
* **Customer Demographics** (20): Enter customer demographics for the product.
* **Sales Trends** (21): Enter sales trends for the product.
* **Sales Forecasts** (22): Enter sales forecasts for the product.
* **Seasonality Factor** (23): Enter the seasonality factor for the product.
* **Sales Trend** (24): Enter the sales trend for the product.
* **Season** (25): Select the season for the product (Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter).
