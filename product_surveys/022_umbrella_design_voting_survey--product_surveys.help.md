# umbrella_design_voting_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Umbrella Design Voting Survey is a form used to collect feedback and ratings on various aspects of a product's design. This guide will walk you through each field to help you complete the form accurately and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each field carefully and provide your honest feedback and ratings.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
3. Use the provided options for select fields, as they are specific to each question.
4. If a field is of type "text", provide a brief and clear explanation.
5. For numerical fields, enter a value between the minimum and maximum range (if specified).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Design Concept** (`design_concept`, `text`, required): Describe your understanding of the design concept, including your thoughts on its effectiveness, creativity, and overall impression.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the design, using a numerical value from 1 to 5 (assuming the range is not specified).
* **Vote** (`vote`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the provided options (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3) to express your opinion on the design.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required): Share your thoughts, ideas, or concerns about the design.
* **Color Rating** (`color_rating`, `number`, required): Rate the color scheme of the design, using a numerical value.
* **Size Rating** (`size_rating`, `number`, required): Rate the size of the design, using a numerical value.
* **Design Team Feedback** (`design_team_feedback`, `text`, required): Provide feedback for the design team, including suggestions or constructive criticism.
* **Retailer Feedback** (`retailer_feedback`, `text`, required): Offer feedback to the retailer, sharing your experience and thoughts.
* **Product Team Feedback** (`product_team_feedback`, `text`, required): Provide feedback to the product team, including suggestions or ideas.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Share any additional comments or thoughts about the design.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Choose the date you completed the survey.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Select the time you completed the survey.
* **Design Concept Rating** (`design_concept_rating`, `number`, required): Rate the design concept, using a numerical value.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more of the provided scales (Scale 1, Scale 2, or Scale 3) to express your opinion on the rating.
* **Design Concept Liking** (`design_concept_liking`, `number`, required): Rate how much you like the design concept, using a numerical value.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the provided options (Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neither Satisfied nor Dissatisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied) to express your overall satisfaction.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction, using a numerical value.
* **Color Scheme** (`color_scheme`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the provided options (Scheme 1, Scheme 2, or Scheme 3) to select the color scheme.
* **Design Concept Liking Scale** (`design_concept_liking_scale`, `number`, required): Rate the design concept liking, using a numerical value.
* **Color Rating Scale** (`color_rating_scale`, `number`, required): Rate the color scheme, using a numerical value.
* **Design Concept Liking Score** (`design_concept_liking_score`, `number`, required): Rate the design concept liking, using a numerical value.
* **Color Liking** (`color_liking`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the provided options (Liking 1, Liking 2, or Liking 3) to select the color liking.
* **Rating Score** (`rating_score`, `number`, required): Enter a numerical value for the rating score.
* **Design Concept Rating Score** (`design_concept_rating_score`, `number`, required): Rate the design concept, using a numerical value.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): This field is not present in the original form data but was included in the help guide due to its presence in the options and to maintain consistency. Please ignore it.
* **Color Liking** (`color_liking`, `select_one`, required): This field is not present in the original form data but was included in the help guide due to its presence in the options and to maintain consistency. Please ignore it.
