# Audio Product Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with an audio product. It aims to gather information about the product's performance, usability, and features, as well as any defects or issues encountered. The collected data will help us improve future audio product developments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in a quiet and private space, free from distractions.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, taking into account your actual experience with the product.
3. Make sure to select only one option for multiple-choice questions.
4. Provide detailed descriptions for any issues or suggestions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Product Model/Name
**Product Model/Name** (`product_model_name`, text, required): Please enter the name or model number of the audio product you are providing feedback on.

### Date of Purchase
**Date of Purchase** (`purchase_date`, date, optional): If you're unsure about the date, please enter the best estimate or leave this field blank.

### Ease of Initial Setup
**Ease of Initial Setup** (`setup_ease`, select_one, required): Choose the level of difficulty you experienced while setting up the product for the first time. 
* Extremely Easy
* Easy
* Moderate
* Difficult

### Overall Sound Quality
**Overall Sound Quality** (`sound_quality_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the clarity, balance, and depth of the sound produced by the product. 
* 5 - Outstanding
* 4 - Good
* 3 - Average
* 2 - Poor
* 1 - Unacceptable

### Build Quality and Durability
**Build Quality and Durability** (`build_quality_rating`, select_one, required): Rate how robust and sturdy the product feels. 
* Premium Feel
* Sturdy
* Average
* Feels Flimsy

### Satisfaction with Included Features
**Satisfaction with Included Features** (`feature_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with the features offered by the product, such as connectivity, app control, and EQ. 
* Very Satisfied
* Satisfied
* Dissatisfied

### Primary Use Case
**Primary Use Case** (`primary_use_case`, select_one, required): Select the primary purpose or use you have for the product. 
* Music Production/Studio
* Live Performance
* Casual Listening
* Gaming
* Content Creation (Podcast/Video)

### Have you encountered any defects or technical issues?
**Have you encountered any defects or technical issues?** (`defect_report`, select_one, required): Indicate if you've experienced any issues with the product. 
* No issues
* Minor software bug
* Hardware defect
* Connectivity issues

### Description of Issue (if any)
**Description of Issue (if any)** (`issue_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of any issues you've encountered, for further investigation.

### How does this compare to other brands you have used?
**How does this compare to other brands you have used?** (`competitor_comparison`, text, optional): Share your thoughts on how this product compares to others you've used.

### Likelihood to Recommend
**Likelihood to Recommend** (`recommendation_score`, select_one, required): Rate how likely you are to recommend this product to others. 
* Definitely Recommend
* Likely Recommend
* Neutral
* Unlikely

### Any Suggestions for product improvement?
**Any Suggestions for product improvement?** (`general_suggestions`, text, optional): Share any suggestions or ideas for improving the product.

### Email Address
**Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, optional): Provide your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up if an issue is reported.

Note: Please answer all required fields to provide a complete and accurate feedback.
