# Scented Sachet Opinion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect opinions from customers on their experiences with scented sachets. It will help us understand what customers like and dislike about the product, and what we can do to improve it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the introductory page to understand the purpose of the form.
2. Answer the questions on the following pages, one page at a time.
3. For each question, select the answer that best describes your opinion.
4. If you have any comments or feedback, please enter them in the comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Scent preferences** (`Scent preferences`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select all the scented options that you like or dislike. You can select one or more options.
* **Usage habits** (`Usage habits`, `select_one`, optional): Please select how often you use scented sachets.
* **Satisfaction** (`Satisfaction`, `number`, required): Please rate your overall satisfaction with the product.
* **Recommendations** (`Recommendations`, `text`, required): Please enter any recommendations you have for our product.
* **Follow-up contact** (`Follow-up contact`, `select_one`, optional): If you would like to be contacted for follow-up, please select your preferred method.
* **How would you like to contact you?** (`How would you like to contact you?`, `select_one`, optional): Please select the method you would like to be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `number`, optional): If you selected phone as your preferred contact method, please enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional): If you selected email as your preferred contact method, please enter your email address.
* **Follow-up message** (`Follow-up message`, `text`, optional): Please enter any additional comments or feedback you have for us.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, optional): Please enter any additional comments or feedback you have for us.
