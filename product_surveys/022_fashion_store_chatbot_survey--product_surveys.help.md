# Fashion Store Chatbot Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fashion Store Chatbot Survey is designed to collect information about customers' preferences, demographics, and shopping habits to improve the fashion store's customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and enter the information that best describes your preferences and demographics.
2. Enter your responses for each field, and make sure to save your progress if you need to take breaks.
3. When you have completed all fields, review your responses for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Preferences
**Customer Preferences (id: 1)**: Briefly describe your preferences, such as favorite styles or colors.

### Budget
**Budget (id: 2)**: Enter the amount you are willing to spend on a product or service. This helps the fashion store understand your price range.

### Feedback
**Feedback (id: 3)**: Share any comments, suggestions, or concerns you have about the fashion store. This helps us improve our services.

### Product Type
**Product Type (id: 4)**: Select the type of product you are interested in, such as clothing, accessories, shoes, or bags.

### Price Range
**Price Range (id: 5)**: Choose the price range you are willing to pay for a product, such as Low, Medium, or High.

### Preferred Color
**Preferred Color (id: 6)**: Select your favorite colors, such as Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, or Purple.

### Age
**Age (id: 7)**: Enter your age to help us understand your demographics.

### Income Range
**Income Range (id: 8)**: Enter the range of your annual income to help us understand your financial situation.

### Marital Status
**Marital Status (id: 9)**: Select your current marital status to help us understand your personal situation.

### Education Level
**Education Level (id: 10)**: Select your highest level of education completed.

### Employment Status
**Employment Status (id: 11)**: Select your current employment status to help us understand your work situation.

### Gender
**Gender (id: 12)**: Select your gender to help us understand your demographics.

### Occupation
**Occupation (id: 13)**: Briefly describe your occupation or job title.

### Preferred Store Type
**Preferred Store Type (id: 14)**: Select the type of store you prefer, such as Online Store, Retail Store, or Outlet Store.

### Visit Frequency
**Visit Frequency (id: 15)**: Choose how often you visit the fashion store, such as rarely, occasionally, often, or daily.

### Social Media
**Social Media (id: 16)**: Select the social media platforms you use, such as Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, or Snapchat.

### Email
**Email (id: 17)**: Enter your email address to receive updates and promotions from the fashion store.

### Phone
**Phone (id: 18)**: Enter your phone number to receive calls or text messages from the fashion store.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and honest information to help us better serve you.
* You can save your progress and complete the form at your own pace.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
