# Religious Parenting App Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about your interests, app usage, and demographic details to help us better understand our target audience and provide more relevant content in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select the relevant options for each field, as indicated by the label and hint text.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields to complete the form successfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Interests** (`parent_interests`, text, optional): This field is designed to collect any information about your interests that you would like to share with us.
* **App Features** (`app_features`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the features you find most useful in our app, such as Parental Control, Parenting Community, Prayer Reminders, and Bible Studies.
* **Prayer Practices** (`prayer_practices`, date, optional): If you practice prayer, please select the date when you last had a prayer practice.
* **App Usage** (`app_usage`, select_multiple, required): Indicate how often you use our app, which could be Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Rarely.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any additional feedback or thoughts about our app that you would like to share.
* **Religious Background** (`religious_background`, select_one, required): Please select your religious background from Christian, Catholic, or Other.
* **Survey Preferences** (`survey_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Let us know if you would like to receive more survey questions like this in the future, either True or False.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for further communication or updates.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Please provide your name for contact purposes.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): If you would like to be contacted by email, please provide your email address.
* **Age Range** (`age_range`, number, optional): Indicate your age range.
* **Children Count** (`children_count`, number, optional): If you have children, please provide their count.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_multiple, required): Please select your gender, which could be Male, Female, or Prefer Not to Answer.
* **Ethnicity** (`ethnicity`, text, optional): Share your ethnicity.
* **Education** (`education`, select_multiple, optional): Select your education level from Less Than HS, HS Grad, Some College, Some College Grad, Bachelor, Some Grad School, Grad School, or Master.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose your employment status from Working Full-time, Working Part-time, Unemployed, Retired, or Stay-At-Home Parent.
* **Marital Status** (`marital_status`, select_multiple, required): Select your marital status from Single, Married, Separated, Divorced, or Widowed.
* **Income Range** (`income_range`, number, optional): Indicate your income range.
* **Family Income** (`family_income`, number, optional): If you're part of a family, please provide their income range.
* **Survey Completed** (`survey_completed`, date, optional): If you have completed this survey before, please provide the date.
* **Contact Street** (`contact_street`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted in person, please provide your street address.
* **Contact City** (`contact_city`, text, optional): Please provide your city name.
* **Contact State** (`contact_state`, text, optional): Please provide your state name.

Please note that you must fill out all required fields to complete the form successfully. If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us.
