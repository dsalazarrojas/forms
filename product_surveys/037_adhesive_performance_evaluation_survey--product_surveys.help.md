# Adhesive Performance Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adhesive Performance Evaluation Survey is a tool for experts and technicians to provide feedback on adhesives' performance. It helps us understand and improve the properties and behavior of our adhesives.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with as much detail as possible.
2. Answer all required fields (marked with an asterisk \*).
3. Choose the correct options for each field as best as your knowledge and experience suggest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Tester information
\* Tester name: Enter your name for internal tracking purposes.
### Tester name
\* Enter your name for reference and tracking.
### Adhesive sample id
\* Enter the unique identifier for the tested adhesive sample.
### Substrate material
\* Select the material of the substrate used during the test (e.g., Aluminum, Plastic, Wood, Composite).
### Bond strength measured newtons
\* Enter the measured force value for the bond strength of the adhesive.
### Peel performance rating
\* Rate the peel performance of the adhesive from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
### Environmental conditions during test
\* Enter the conditions during the test (e.g., Temperature, Humidity, Exposure).
### Failure mode observed
\* Select the failure mode observed during the test (e.g., Cohesive failure, Adhesive failure, Substrate failure, Mixed).
### Recommended improvements
\* Enter any suggestions for formulation or process improvements.
### Additional comments
\* Add any other observations or comments about the adhesive performance.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when completing this form.
* Use industry-standard terminology for adhesive-related terms.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact our team for guidance.
