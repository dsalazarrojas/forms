# Product Usage Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from end-users about their product usage and experiences. It helps us understand how you use our product, what features you enjoy, and any issues you've encountered.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Select your response from the available options for questions with options.
3. For text fields, provide a brief description or example that best represents your experience with the product.
4. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
5. Once you've completed the form, click submit to send your response to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Product Usage
*   **Product Usage** (`product_usage_survey`, text, required): Describe how often you use our product. This could be daily, weekly, monthly, or rarely.

### 2. Product Name (`product_name`, text, required)
*   **Product Name**: Please enter the name of the product you're using.

### 3. Product Version (`product_version`, text, required)
*   **Product Version**: Enter the current version of the product you're using.

### 4. Company (`company`, text, required)
*   **Company**: Enter the name of your company or organization.

### 5. Usage Frequency (`usage_frequency`, select_one, required)
*   **Usage Frequency**: Select how often you use our product. Options are:
    + Daily
    + Weekly
    + Monthly
    + Rarely

### 6. Product Purpose (`product_purpose`, text, required)
*   **Product Purpose**: Describe the main reason you use our product.

### 7. Product Features (`product_features`, text, required)
*   **Product Features**: List the features you use most frequently.

### 8. Product Value (`product_value`, text, required)
*   **Product Value**: Rate how valuable you find our product (e.g., helpful, time-saving, etc.).

### 9. Product Improvement (`product_improvement`, text, required)
*   **Product Improvement**: Suggest changes or improvements you'd like to see in our product.

### 10. Product Issues (`product_issues`, text, required)
*   **Product Issues**: Describe any issues or difficulties you've encountered while using our product.

### 11. Survey Participation (`survey_participation`, text, required)
*   **Survey Participation**: Thank you for participating in this survey. Your feedback is invaluable to us.

### 12. Email (`email`, email, required)
*   **Email**: Enter your email address.

### 13. Phone (`phone`, text, required)
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number.

Please note that all fields marked "required" are mandatory and must be filled out to complete the form. Ensure you've answered all required fields before submitting your response.
