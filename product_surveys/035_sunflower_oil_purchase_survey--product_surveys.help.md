# Sunflower Oil Purchase Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for customers who have purchased sunflower oil to share their experiences, opinions, and feedback about the product. Your input will help us understand your preferences and usage habits, which will be used to improve the product and better serve your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in each field below, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
4. When prompted to select multiple options, choose all that apply.
