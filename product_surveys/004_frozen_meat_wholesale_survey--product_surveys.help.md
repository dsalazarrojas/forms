# Frozen Meat Wholesale Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from frozen meat buyers and sellers about their role in the meat industry, their buying or selling preferences, and their satisfaction with current suppliers. This data will help us better understand the market needs and preferences of frozen meat professionals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Read through each page of the survey, which is divided into sections to help guide you through the questions.
2. Choose your answers from the provided options for each question.
3. When selecting multiple options, you can choose one or more of the provided answers.
4. Enter your information in the text fields as prompted.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Introduction
* **Frozen Meat Wholesale Survey**: This is the title of the survey, which is just a heading to let you know what this form is about.
* **Introduction**: A brief welcome message to get you started on the survey.

### Page 2: Introduction
* **What is your role in the meat industry?**: This question helps us understand your position in the meat industry. Please choose one of the four options: Frozen Meat Buyer, Frozen Meat Seller, Frozen Meat Producer, or Other.

### Page 3: Introduction
* **What is your role in the meat industry?**: This question is the same as the one above, but with a slight difference in wording. Please choose the same option as before.
* **What is your role in the meat industry?:** This question is intended to confirm your role in the meat industry, likely for validation or consistency checks.

### Page 4: Frozen Meat Products
* **What type of frozen meat do you buy or sell?**: This question asks about the type of frozen meat you are involved with. Please separate your answers with commas, and choose as many as apply. The options include: Beef, Lamb, Pork, Chicken, and Turkey.

### Page 5: Frozen Meat Products
* **What type of frozen meat do you sell?**: This question is similar to the one above, but for sellers. Please choose the frozen meats you sell, separated with commas.

### Page 6: Satisfaction and Priorities
* **How satisfied are you with your current suppliers?**: This question measures your satisfaction level with your current suppliers. Choose one of the five options: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
* **What is your top priority when purchasing frozen meat?**: This question is a multiple-choice question where you can choose one or more of the provided options. Please choose your top priority among: Price, Quality, Service, Availability.
* **What is your second priority when purchasing frozen meat?**: This is another multiple-choice question where you can choose one or more of the provided options. Please choose your second priority among: Quality, Service, Availability.

### Page 7: Additional Information
* **What is the name of the company or organization you work for?**: This question asks for the name of your company or organization. Please enter it in the text field.
* **Email**: This question asks for your email address. Please enter it in the text field.
* **Contact**: This question asks for your contact number or email. Please enter it in the text field.

### Page 8: Conclusion
* **Thank you**: This is a final message to thank you for completing the survey.
