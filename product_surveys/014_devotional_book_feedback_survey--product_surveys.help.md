# Devotional Book Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Devotional Book Feedback Survey is a tool for readers to provide their honest feedback on a devotional book. Your input will help us create more valuable content for our readers in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields as prompted.
2. Select your preferred likers for the "Likers" field.
3. Provide your rating and comments for the book.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reader Name** (`reader_name`, text, required): This field is for you to provide your name so we can address you in our response. Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): This field is for you to rate the book on a scale of 1-5. A higher rating indicates a more positive experience.
* **Rating Comments** (`rating_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments on your rating, e.g., what you liked or disliked.
* **Likers** (`rating_likers`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the likers you appreciated in the book. You can choose from the options provided.
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, text, required): Share with us if you would recommend this book to others.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments on your overall experience with the book.

## Tips
- Please be honest and objective in your feedback.
- Keep your comments concise and relevant to the book.
- If you have any other suggestions or questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
- We value your feedback and will use it to improve our content and services.
