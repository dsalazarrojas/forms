# Beta Product Early Adopter Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from early adopters of a beta product to gather information on their experience, satisfaction, and opinions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required") with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the relevant options for each question, taking into account the hints provided.
3. Make sure to review your answers before submitting, as you will not be able to edit or change your submission once it is submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (`full_name`, Text, Required): Enter your legal full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Include your country code if you're outside of our local region.
* **Date** (`reference_date`, Date, Required): Select the relevant date for your feedback.
* **Primary Selection** (`primary_selection`, Select One, Required): Choose the option that best applies to your experience with the beta product.
* **Quantity or Rating** (`quantity_or_rating`, Number, Optional): Provide a whole number for quantity or rating if applicable.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, Select Multiple, Required): Select any that apply from the options provided.
* **Estimated Value** (`estimated_value`, Number, Optional): Enter a decimal value if applicable.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, Time, Optional): Enter a time if scheduling is required.
* **Final Comments or Notes** (`final_comments`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes you may have.

Note: The form is not intended for general public access, and users who have already submitted feedback will be prevented from resubmitting it.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in the form with accurate and complete information to help us improve the beta product.
* Be as specific as possible when answering questions to provide valuable feedback.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the final comment section to provide them.
