# Retail Skincare Product Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about retail skincare products. The goal is to gather information about the product's quality, effectiveness, and overall customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the product in question.
2. Select the correct **product id** for the product you are providing feedback on.
3. Choose your rating for the product from **rating**: very good, good, neutral, bad, or very bad.
4. Type the actual **product name** of the product you are providing feedback on.
5. Enter the **price** you paid for the product.
6. Select the sales channel where you purchased the product from: in store, online, or both.
7. Choose whether you would **recommend** the product to others: yes, no, or maybe.
8. Enter any additional **comments** about your experience with the product.
9. If you'd like to contact us about your feedback, you can enter your **contact email** address. (This field is optional.)
10. If you'd like to share your phone number, you can enter your **contact phone**. (This field is optional.)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **product id** (Required): Enter the unique product code provided by the manufacturer or retailer.
* **rating** (Required): Rate your overall experience with the product.
	+ Select "very good" if the product met your expectations and you are very satisfied.
	+ Select "good" if the product met your expectations and you are somewhat satisfied.
	+ Select "neutral" if the product met your expectations, but you are not strongly satisfied or dissatisfied.
	+ Select "bad" if the product did not meet your expectations and you are somewhat dissatisfied.
	+ Select "very bad" if the product did not meet your expectations and you are very dissatisfied.
* **product name** (Required): Enter the actual name of the product you are providing feedback on.
* **price** (Required): Enter the price you paid for the product.
* **sales channel** (Required): Select the sales channel where you purchased the product from.
	+ Select "in store" if you purchased the product in a physical store.
	+ Select "online" if you purchased the product on a website or online store.
	+ Select "both" if you purchased the product in a physical store and an online store.
* **recommendations** (Required): Choose whether you would recommend the product to others.
	+ Select "recommend" if you would recommend the product to others.
	+ Select "do not recommend" if you would not recommend the product to others.
	+ Select "maybe" if you are unsure if you would recommend the product to others.
* **comments** (Required): Enter any additional comments about your experience with the product.
* **contact email** (Optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to contact us about your feedback.
* **contact phone** (Optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to contact us about your feedback.

Note: This form is for customer feedback purposes only and is not intended for commercial use.
