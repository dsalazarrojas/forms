# Flip Sample Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with a product. It includes questions about their name, email, product experience, and category preferences, as well as space for free-text feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your **First name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Last name** in the second field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the third field.
4. Provide your feedback in the **Feedback** field, describing your thoughts and opinions about the product.
5. Select the level of **Product experience** you have with the product (Not satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Satisfied, Very satisfied).
6. Choose all the categories that apply to your product experience (Category 1, Category 2, Category 3).
7. Select the date you used the product.
8. Select the time you used the product.
9. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
- **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
- **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of your experience with the product.
- **Product experience** (`product_experience`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the following: Not satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Satisfied, Very satisfied.
- **Category** (`category`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the categories that apply to your product experience.
  - Category 1
  - Category 2
  - Category 3
- **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date you used the product.
- **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Select the time you used the product.

## Tips
- Please be honest and constructive with your feedback.
- Make sure to enter the correct email address so that we can contact you if needed.
- Select all categories that apply to your product experience.
- Use the feedback field to provide specific details about your experience.
- Select the correct date and time of your product use.
