# Acrylic Signage Solutions Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback and insights from individuals related to acrylic signage to improve our product line and customer service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your primary role related to signage from the options provided in the "Your Role" field.
2. Choose the type of organization you represent from the options provided in the "Type of Organization" field.
3. Estimate how often you purchase acrylic signage from the options provided in the "How Often Do You Purchase Acrylic Signage" field.
4. Select the main use for your acrylic signage from the options provided in the "Primary Application for Acrylic Signage" field.
5. Rate your satisfaction with the quality of acrylic signage from the options provided in the "Satisfaction with Acrylic Sign Quality" field.
6. Rate the durability of acrylic signage from the options provided in the "Durability Rating of Acrylic Signs" field.
7. Indicate your satisfaction with the design options available for acrylic signage from the options provided in the "Satisfaction with Design Options" field.
8. Rate your satisfaction with the pricing of acrylic signage from the options provided in the "Satisfaction with Pricing" field.
9. Assess the ease of installation of acrylic signage from the options provided in the "Ease of Installation" field.
10. Select the features that are most important to you from the options provided in the "Most Important Features in Acrylic Signage" field. (Optional)
11. Identify areas for improvement of our acrylic signage solutions from the options provided in the "Areas for Improvement" field. (Optional)
12. Rate how likely you are to recommend acrylic signage to others on a scale of 0-10 from the "How Likely Are You to Recommend Acrylic Signage" field.
13. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional Feedback or Suggestions" field. (Optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Role (Business Owner, Marketing Manager, Facilities Manager, Sign Designer, Procurement Specialist, Other)**: Please select your primary role related to signage.
* **Type of Organization (Retail Store, Restaurant, Corporate Office, Healthcare Facility, Educational Institution, Other Business)**: Choose the type of organization you represent.
* **How Often Do You Purchase Acrylic Signage (Monthly, Quarterly, Twice a year, Once a year, As needed, First time buyer)**: Estimate how often you purchase acrylic signage.
* **Primary Application for Acrylic Signage (Interior signs, Exterior signs, Point of sale displays, Directional signage, Branding elements, Trade show displays)**: Select the main use for your acrylic signage.
* **Satisfaction with Acrylic Sign Quality (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied)**: Rate your satisfaction with the quality of acrylic signage.
* **Durability Rating of Acrylic Signs (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Very poor)**: Rate the durability of acrylic signage.
* **Satisfaction with Design Options (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied)**: Indicate your satisfaction with the design options available for acrylic signage.
* **Satisfaction with Pricing (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied)**: Rate your satisfaction with the pricing of acrylic signage.
* **Ease of Installation (Very easy, Easy, Neutral, Difficult, Very difficult)**: Assess the ease of installation of acrylic signage.
* **Most Important Features in Acrylic Signage (Durable, Visually appealing, Customizable, Weather resistant, Cost-effective, Lightweight, Easy to clean)**: Select the features that are most important to you. (Optional)
* **Areas for Improvement (Quality consistency, Price point, Design options, Installation process, Delivery time, Customer service, Product range)**: Identify areas for improvement of our acrylic signage solutions. (Optional)
* **How Likely Are You to Recommend Acrylic Signage (Scale 0-10)**: Rate how likely you are to recommend acrylic signage to others.
* **Additional Feedback or Suggestions**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions about acrylic signage solutions. (Optional)
