# Buyer Wishlist Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Buyer Wishlist Survey is a tool that helps us understand what you, as a customer or potential customer, are looking for in our products or services. Your responses will help us better understand your needs and preferences, allowing us to improve and offer more relevant products that meet your expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. If applicable, provide your phone number.
4. Indicate your current customer status (e.g., current customer, past customer, or prospective customer).
5. Select the categories of products that interest you.
6. Specify the top three products you would like to see from us.
7. Choose your ideal price range for new products.
8. Select the features or qualities that matter most to you when making a purchase.
9. Indicate how often you shop with us.
10. Indicate your level of loyalty to our brand.
11. Choose the factors that influence your purchase decisions.
12. Provide suggestions on how we can improve our products.
13. Suggest any new products you would like to see us offer.
14. Share any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone Number** (`respondent_phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it for us to contact you more effectively.
* **Customer Status** (`customer_status`, select_one, required): Please select one of the options to indicate your current customer status:
	+ Current Customer: You are a current customer of ours.
	+ Past Customer: You were a customer in the past but no longer are.
	+ Prospective Customer: You are interested in becoming a customer but haven't made a purchase yet.
	+ Other: If none of the above apply.
* **Product Categories of Interest** (`product_interests`, select_multiple, required): Select all the product categories that interest you:
	+ Electronics
	+ Home and Garden
	+ Clothing and Fashion
	+ Sports and Outdoors
	+ Health and Beauty
	+ Tools and Hardware
	+ Books and Media
	+ Other
* **Top Wishlist Items** (`top_wishlist_items`, text, required): Please specify the top three products you would like to see from us.
* **Ideal Price Range for Products** (`ideal_price_range`, select_one, required): Choose your ideal price range for new products:
	+ Under 25
	+ 25 - 50
	+ 50 - 100
	+ 100 - 250
	+ Over 250
* **Preferred Features or Qualities** (`preferred_features`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the features or qualities that matter most to you when making a purchase:
	+ Eco-friendly
	+ Affordable
	+ Premium Quality
	+ Durable
	+ Easy to Use
	+ Innovative
	+ Sustainable
	+ Other
* **Shopping Frequency** (`shopping_frequency`, select_one, required): Indicate how often you shop with us:
	+ Weekly
	+ Bi-weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Annually
	+ As needed
* **Brand Loyalty** (`brand_loyalty`, select_one, required): Choose your level of loyalty to our brand:
	+ Very Loyal
	+ Somewhat Loyal
	+ Not Loyal
	+ Depends on Product
* **Factors That Influence Your Purchase** (`influencing_factors`, select_multiple, required): Select all the factors that influence your purchase decisions:
	+ Price
	+ Quality
	+ Brand Reputation
	+ Recommendations
	+ Online Reviews
	+ Availability
	+ Sustainability
* **How We Can Improve Our Products** (`product_improvements`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions on how we can improve our products.
* **Suggestions for New Products** (`new_product_suggestions`, text, required): Suggest any new products you would like to see us offer.
* **Additional Comments or Feedback** (`feedback_comments`, text, optional): Share any other thoughts or feedback about our products or services.
