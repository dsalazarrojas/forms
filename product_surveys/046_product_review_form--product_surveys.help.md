# Product Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Review Form is designed to collect reviews from customers about the products they have used or purchased.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have already provided a review for the product being reviewed.
2. Fill out the form with your honest feedback about the product.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Would recommend this product" field if applicable.
4. Enter your email address (if desired).
5. Enter your phone number (if desired).
6. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Review Prophet" field if you're a regular reviewer.
7. Choose the date and time of your last review (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **product name**: Enter the name of the product you are reviewing.
* **Rating**: Rate your experience with the product from 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Review**: Provide a written review of your experience with the product.
* **Would recommend this product**: If you would recommend this product to others, select "Yes", otherwise select "No".
* **Email**: Enter your email address (if you want to be contacted about the review).
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (if you want to be contacted about the product).
* **Review Prophet**: If you're a regular reviewer (someone who frequently reviews products), select "Yes", otherwise select "No".
* **Date of Last Review**: Enter the date of your last review (if applicable).
* **Time of Last Review**: Enter the time of your last review (if applicable).

## Tips
* Please only fill out the form for products you have already reviewed.
* Be honest and thorough in your review to help us improve our products.
* If you're a regular reviewer, please make sure to select "Yes" in the "Review Prophet" field.
