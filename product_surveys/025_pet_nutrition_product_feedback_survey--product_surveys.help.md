# Pet Nutrition Product Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback about our pet nutrition products from users who have interacted with them. Your input helps us understand how to improve and provide better products for pets.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide valuable feedback, follow these steps:

1. **Rate your experience with the "Rate" field**: Choose one of the three rating options: Pet Health, Nutrition, or Quality.
2. **Assess the product's performance**: Enter a numerical rating (1-5) in the "How would you rate this product" field to express your level of satisfaction.
3. **Share your thoughts**: Type any additional comments about the product in the "Product Comment" field.
4. **Recommendation**: Choose whether you would recommend this product in the "Would you recommend this product" field (Yes, highly recommend; False; or No, not at all).
5. **Leave your contact information**: Enter your email address or phone number for us to reach out to you for further discussion (optional, but helpful if you're willing to receive follow-up questions).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Nutrition Product**: Enter the name or description of the pet nutrition product you're providing feedback about. This helps us understand which product specifically you're commenting on.
* **Rate**: Select one of the three options that best represent your experience with the product: Pet Health, Nutrition, or Quality. This gives us insight into your overall impression of the product.
* **How would you rate this product**: Enter a numerical rating between 1 and 5 to express your level of satisfaction with the product. A higher number indicates a more positive experience.
* **Product Comment**: Share any additional thoughts, concerns, or suggestions you have about the product. This helps us understand your specific experiences and areas for improvement.
* **Would you recommend this product**: Choose whether you would highly recommend this product, are not opposed to recommending it, or wouldn't recommend it at all. This helps us gauge the product's value to others.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address for us to contact you for follow-up questions or discussion.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for us to reach out to you for further discussion (optional).
