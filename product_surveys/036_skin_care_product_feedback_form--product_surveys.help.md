# Skin Care Product Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their skin care product experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  The form is divided into multiple sections, each with a specific question or prompt.
2.  Please fill out each section thoroughly and accurately to help us better understand your experience with the skin care product.
3.  If a question or prompt is not relevant to your experience, please feel free to skip it or mention it in the "Additional Comments" section.
4.  Make sure to answer all required questions, as indicated by the "Required" label.
5.  For the "Email" and "Phone Number" fields, please enter your actual email address and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Form**: This is the main section of the form where you can enter any additional comments or feedback about the skin care product.
*   **How often do you use the product?** (`page2`, `number`, required): This question aims to understand how frequently you use the skin care product. Please answer with the number of times you use the product per week, month, etc.
*   **How would you rate the product?** (`page3`, `select_one`, required): This question asks for your overall satisfaction rating of the product. Choose one of the options: Good, Fair.
*   **What features would you like to see improved?** (`page4`, `text`, required): Here, please list any features of the product that you would like to see changed or improved.
*   **Any Additional Feedback?** (`page5`, `text`, optional): This is an open-ended question where you can provide any additional thoughts, comments, or suggestions about the skin care product.
*   **Email**: (`page6`, `email`, required): Please enter your actual email address to ensure we can contact you with any further questions or follow-up regarding your feedback.
*   **Phone Number**: (`page7`, `text`, required): Enter your actual phone number to allow us to contact you for further clarification or follow-up.
*   **Additional Comments**: (`page8`, `note`, optional): This is an optional section where you can provide any further comments or thoughts about your experience with the skin care product.
