# Self Defense Product Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Self Defense Product Feedback form is designed to collect customer insights and feedback about our self-defense products, helping us understand customer experiences and improve our products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Customer Details** field with your name, company name, and contact information.
2. Rate your **Product Experience** on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
3. Select your level of **Would You Recommend** from the options provided (Very Likely, Somewhat Likely, Not Very Likely, Not At All Likely).
4. Provide any additional **Additional Feedback** you have about your experience with our product in the text field.
5. Share any **Safety Insights** you have about our product in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, `text`, required): Please fill out this field with your name, company name, and contact information for our records.
* **Product Experience** (`product_experience`, `number`, required): Rate your experience with our product on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
* **How Satisfied Are You** (`product_satisfaction`, `number`, required): This field is similar to **Product Experience**, but you can also use it to rate your overall satisfaction with our product. Please fill it out if you haven't filled **Product Experience**.
* **Would You Recommend** (`recommendation`, `select_one`, required): Select your level of recommendation for our product from the options provided (Very Likely, Somewhat Likely, Not Very Likely, Not At All Likely).
* **Additional Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience with our product in this field.
* **Safety Insight** (`safety_insight`, `text`, required): Share any safety insights or concerns you have about our product in this field.
