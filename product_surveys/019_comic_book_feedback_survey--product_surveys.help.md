# Comic Book Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from readers about their experience with a comic book issue. Please take a few minutes to answer the questions to help us better understand your thoughts and opinions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your name and email address (optional) in sections 1 and 2.
2. In sections 3-5, select the title of the comic book you are reviewing, the issue number, and the date you purchased or read it.
3. In sections 6-13, rate the story and art quality, using the provided scales.
4. In sections 14-19, answer the questions about your overall experience with the comic book.
5. In sections 20-28, provide more detailed feedback about your favorite and least favorite aspects of the comic book.
6. Finally, add any additional comments or feedback you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reader Name** (`reader_name`, text, optional): Enter your name, if you'd like to be credited for your feedback.
* **Email Address** (`reader_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, if you'd like us to contact you for follow-up questions or discussions.
* **Comic Book Title** (`comic_title`, text, required): Select the title of the comic book you are reviewing.
* **Issue Number** (`issue_number`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the issue number of the comic book you are reviewing.
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, required): Enter the date you purchased or read the comic book.
* **Story Rating** (`story_rating`, note, optional): Rate the overall story quality using the provided scale.
* **Plot Engagement** (`plot_engagement`, select_one, required): Rate how engaging the plot was.
* **Character Development** (`character_development`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of character development.
* **Dialogue Quality** (`dialogue_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of dialogue.
* **Story Pacing** (`pacing`, select_one, required): Rate the story pacing.
* **Art Rating** (`art_rating`, note, optional): Rate the overall art quality using the provided scale.
* **Cover Art** (`cover_art`, select_one, required): Rate the cover art.
* **Color Quality** (`color_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the coloring.
* **Panel Layout** (`panel_layout`, select_one, required): Rate the panel composition.
* **Overall Impression** (`overall`, note, optional): Provide a brief statement about your overall impression of the comic book.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the comic book.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, select_one, required): Rate how worth the price was.
* **Would You Recommend** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Say whether you would recommend the comic book to others.
* **Likelihood to Buy Next Issue** (`repurchase_likelihood`, select_one, required): Rate how likely you are to buy the next issue.
* **Favorite Aspect** (`favorite_aspect`, select_one, required): Select the aspect that you enjoyed the most.
* **Least Favorite Aspect** (`least_favorite_aspect`, select_one, optional): Select the aspect that you enjoyed the least.
* **Favorite Character** (`favorite_character`, text, optional): If you have a favorite character, enter their name.
* **Story Improvements** (`story_improvements`, text, optional): Provide suggestions for improving the story.
* **Art Improvements** (`art_improvements`, text, optional): Provide suggestions for improving the art.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any other feedback or comments you have.

## Tips
* Please be honest and sincere in your answers. Your feedback is valuable to us.
* You can select multiple options for some fields, but please only choose one that best describes your experience.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us at <form@gic.mx>.
