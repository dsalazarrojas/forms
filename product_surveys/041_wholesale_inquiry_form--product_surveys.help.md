# Wholesale Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wholesale Inquiry Form is a tool for businesses to provide information about themselves and their interests in purchasing products from our company. It helps us understand your business needs and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Name" and "email" fields.
2. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
3. Choose one or multiple product interests that apply to your business in the "product_interest" field.
4. Indicate the type of inquiry you would like to make (Informational or Order-related) in the "inquiry_type" field.
5. Enter your company's name, address, and contact person in the "company_name", "company_address", and "contact_person" fields respectively.
6. Add your company's email address in the "contact_email" field.
7. Enter your company's phone number in the "contact_phone" field.
8. Add your company's website in the "company_website" field (if applicable).
9. Provide any additional notes in the "note" field (if necessary).
10. Choose the product line(s) you are interested in (Branding Products, Cable Management, or Other) in the "product_line" field.
11. Enter the minimum and maximum order quantities you are interested in for a wholesale order in the "min_order_quantity" and "max_order_quantity" fields.
12. Enter the wholesale price you would like to pay for the product(s) in the "wholesale_price" field.
13. Enter the shipping cost you are willing to accept in the "shipping_cost" field.
14. Indicate the lead time (time before delivery) you require for the order in the "lead_time" field.
15. Choose your preferred payment terms (Net 30, Net 45, or Net 60 days) in the "wholesale_terms" field.
16. Select the payment schedule you would like to follow (Pay on receipt, Pay 30 days, Pay 45 days, or Pay 60 days) in the "wholesale_payment_terms" field.
17. Provide a brief description of the product(s) you are interested in, and any other relevant details, in the "product_description" field.
18. Add any product image or image URL that can be used to showcase the product in the "product_image" and "product_image_url" fields.
19. Enter any product specifications that you would like to know more about in the "product_specification" field.
