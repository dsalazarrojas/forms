# sugar-industry-challenges-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on your experiences and challenges in the sugar industry. Your input will help us better understand the pain points and priorities of the industry, which will aid us in providing more effective support and services. Please provide your honest feedback to help us improve our offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully, as each section is designed to gather specific information related to the sugar industry.
2. Answer the questions to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as possible.
3. If you have any additional comments or questions, feel free to add them in the designated field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`sugar_industry_challenges`): A brief introduction to the survey, providing context and background information.
* **What are your top 3 sugar industry pain points?** (`sugar_pain_points`): Select up to three pain points you are currently facing in the sugar industry.
* **What are your top 3 sugar industry priorities?** (`sugar_priorities`): Choose up to three priorities for the sugar industry.
* **What are you hoping to achieve with this survey?** (`sugar_expected_outcomes`): Briefly describe your expected outcomes or goals with this survey.
* **What region are you from?** (`sugar_region`): Select your region of origin.
* **What is your role in the sugar industry?** (`sugar_role`): Choose your role in the sugar industry.
* **What is your preferred method of communication?** (`sugar_communication_preference`): Select your preferred method of communication (Email, Phone, or Web).
* **Do you have any additional questions?** (`sugar_questions`): Feel free to ask any questions or provide additional feedback.
* **Email** (`sugar_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`sugar_phone`): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Comment** (`sugar_comment`): Leave any additional comments or feedback.
* **Date** (`sugar_date`): Select the date (optional).
* **Time** (`sugar_time`): Select the time (optional).
* **Name** (`sugar_name`): Enter your name.
* **Website** (`sugar_website`): Enter your website (optional).
* **File** (`sugar_file`): Attach any relevant files (optional).
* **Terms and Conditions** (`sugar_terms`): Read and agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Privacy Policy** (`sugar_privacy`): Read and agree to our privacy policy.
* **Submit** (`sugar_submit`): Once you have completed the form, click submit to proceed.
* **Cancel** (`sugar_cancel`): If you wish to cancel your responses, click cancel.
* **Back** (`sugar_back`): Review your previous responses or navigate back to a previous page.
* **Next** (`sugar_next`): Proceed to the next page.
* **Done** (`sugar_done`): Confirm you have completed the form.

Note: The fields marked as "optional" can be skipped if not applicable.
