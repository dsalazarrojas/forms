# Powdered Juice Consumption Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on powdered juice consumption, including preferred products, purchase frequency, satisfaction, and buying habits. This information will help us refine our product offerings and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, please fill in your name (field 2) and email address (field 3) if you wish to be contacted for further questions or to receive survey results.
2. Select your preferred powdered juice product from the list provided (field 4).
3. Indicate your purchase frequency (field 5) on a scale.
4. Express your level of satisfaction with our powdered juice products (field 6).
5. If you would recommend our powdered juice to friends, select all applicable options (field 7).
6. Choose all channels through which you purchase powdered juice (field 8).
7. Provide any additional comments about our powdered juice products, if you'd like (field 10).
8. Finally, indicate your purchase intent for our powdered juice products and the channel through which you'd typically buy (field 9).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (field 2, text, required): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (field 3, email, optional): Enter your email address if you want us to contact you regarding this survey or to send you the results.
* **Preferred Powdered Juice Product** (field 4, select_one, optional): Select the powdered juice product you prefer. This will help us understand which product is most popular or in demand.
* **Purchase Frequency** (field 5, number, optional): Indicate how often you purchase powdered juice. This can be a daily, weekly, or monthly basis.
* **Satisfaction** (field 6, select_one, optional): Express your satisfaction level with our powdered juice. Choose from very satisfied, somewhat satisfied, neutral, somewhat dissatisfied, or very dissatisfied.
* **Recommended to Friends** (field 7, select_multiple, optional): If you would recommend our powdered juice to friends, select all applicable options.
* **Purchase Intent** (field 8, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you intend to purchase our powdered juice and the channel through which you would typically buy.
* **Purchase Channel** (field 8, select_multiple, optional): This field is part of "Purchase Intent" field above.
* **Comments** (field 10, note, optional): Feel free to share any thoughts or experiences about our powdered juice products. This helps us understand your perspective and make improvements.
* **Survey Footer** (field 11, note, required): This is the last section, and it is not required to be filled but serves as a confirmation of completion of the survey.
