# Women Golf Shorts Shopping Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on your opinions and preferences regarding women's golf shorts. The data collected will help us understand what you like and dislike about golf shorts, and will be used to inform product development and marketing strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred category for the golf shorts (Golf, Fitness, or Lifestyle).
2. Choose the shopping channel(s) where you have purchased or would like to purchase golf shorts (Amazon, Walmart, eBay, or Other).
3. Select the price range you are willing to pay for golf shorts (Under 50, 50-100, 101-200, or More than 200).
4. Enter the length of the golf shorts (number of inches).
5. Select your preferred waist size.
6. Choose the material type(s) of the golf shorts you prefer (Polyester, Cotton, Nylon, or Others).
7. Select the sleeve length(s) of the golf shorts you prefer (Shorts, Capris, Pants, or Leggings).
8. Choose the pocket type(s) of the golf shorts you prefer (Pocket, No pocket, Multiple pockets).
9. Select the color(s) of the golf shorts you prefer (Black, White, Red, Blue).
10. Enter the size of the golf shorts you prefer (number of inches).
11. Select your level of satisfaction with your current golf shorts (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Satisfied).
12. Indicate whether you would buy the golf shorts again (True or False).
13. Indicate whether you would recommend the golf shorts to others (True or False).
14. Choose the location where you purchased the golf shorts (Amazon, Walmart, eBay, Online Store, or Offline Store).
15. Enter the date you purchased the golf shorts (format: mm/dd/yyyy).
