# Eco Friendly Product Preference Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your attitudes towards eco-friendly products to better understand consumer preferences and behaviors.

## How To Complete This Form

### To complete this survey:

- Please fill out the form completely and truthfully.
- You can skip any questions that are not relevant to your preferences.
- If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the Survey**: This is a welcome note to inform you that you are about to start the survey.
* **How Don't You Like Paying**: This question asks you about your sensitivity to price when it comes to eco-friendly products. Please choose a number that best represents your feelings.
* **What Do You Do to Reduce Your Eco Footprint**: This question inquires about your daily habits that help reduce your environmental impact. Choose all the options that apply to you.
* **What Drives Your Pricing Decisions**: This question is about the factors that influence your purchasing decisions when it comes to eco-friendly products. Choose the option that best represents your decision-making process.
* **Thank You for Complting the Survey**: This is a note of appreciation for taking the time to complete the survey.
* **What Do You Prefer**: This question is about your preferences when it comes to eco-friendly products. Choose all the options that apply to you.
* **Demographic Information**: Please select the option that best describes your demographic information such as age, occupation, etc.
* **Contact Information**: This question asks for your contact information. Please choose all the options that apply.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any further comments or concerns, please use this field.
* **Email**: Please enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: You can enter your phone number for further contact or follow-up.

Note: Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields to get the full benefit of this survey.
