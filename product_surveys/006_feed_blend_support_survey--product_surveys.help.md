# feedblend_support_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The feedblend_support_survey form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experiences with the FeedBlend product, particularly regarding issues they encounter and their satisfaction with the product's support channels. This form will help us improve our support processes and provide better assistance in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions on this page. The form is divided into sections with different topics, so make sure to answer all the questions in each section.
2. Select the relevant options for each question using the dropdown menus.
3. Submit your feedback when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Introduction** (Optional)
* **Introduction** (`title`, `text`, Optional): This is a brief introduction to your feedback. You can write a few sentences to explain your experience with the FeedBlend product and the reason you're taking the time to fill out this form.

### 2. **Issue Frequencies** (Optional)
* **Issue Frequencies** (`issue_frequency`, `select_one`, Optional): This question asks about the common issues you experience with the FeedBlend product. Please select the option that best describes how often you encounter these issues.
	+ **Rarely**: You hardly ever experience these issues.
	+ **Sometime**: You occasionally experience these issues.
	+ **Often**: You frequently experience these issues.

### 3. **Issue Description** (Optional)
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, `text`, Optional): This is an open-ended question where you can describe the issues you experience with the FeedBlend product in more detail.

### 4. **Support Channels** (Optional)
* **Support Channels** (`support_channels`, `select_multiple`, Optional): This question asks about the support channels you've used to contact us. Please select all the channels that apply.
	+ **Email**: You've sent us an email for support.
	+ **Call**: You've called us for support.
	+ **Message**: You've sent us a message, e.g., via our chat or messaging system.
	+ **Chat**: You've chatted with us for support.
	+ **Support Ticket**: You've submitted a support ticket for your issue.

### 5. **Satisfaction** (Optional)
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, Optional): This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the FeedBlend product's support channels. Please select the option that best describes your satisfaction level.
	+ **Very Satisfied**: You're extremely satisfied with our support.
	+ **Somewhat Satisfied**: You're fairly satisfied with our support.
	+ **Neutral**: You're neutral, it's okay, but not outstanding.
	+ **Somewhat Dissatisfied**: You're fairly dissatisfied with our support.
	+ **Very Dissatisfied**: You're extremely dissatisfied with our support.
