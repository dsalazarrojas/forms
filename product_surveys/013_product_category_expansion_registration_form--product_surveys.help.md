# Product Category Expansion Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect details for product category expansion registration.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the status of the product category from the dropdown menu.
2. Fill in the product details and description.
3. Enter the review status and reviewer information.
4. Confirm the review status and review details.
5. Review the form for any errors or missing information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Category Expansion Details** (`category_expansion_registration_form_page_1`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the active or inactive status of the product category.
* **Team Details** (`product_category_expansion_details`, `text`, optional): Enter the team details for the product category.
* **Product Description** (`product_description_registration_form_page_3`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the product.
* **Product Details** (`product_registration_form_page_4`, `text`, required): Enter the product details.
* **Review Status** (`status_registration_form_page_5`, `select_one`, required): Select the review status for the product.
* **Product Status** (`product_status_details`, `text`, optional): Enter the product status.
* **Reviewer Information** (`reviewer_details`, `text`, optional): Enter the reviewer information.
* **Review Status** (`review_status_registration_form_page_8`, `select_one`, required): Select the review status.
* **Review Details** (`review_details`, `text`, optional): Enter the review details.
* **Reviewers Info** (`reviewers_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information about the reviewers.
* **Review Status** (`review_status_registration_form_page_13`, `select_one`, optional): Select the review status.
* **Review Details Registration Form Page 14** (`review_details_registration_form_page_14`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional review details.

Note: Fields marked as optional should only be filled in if required fields are complete.
