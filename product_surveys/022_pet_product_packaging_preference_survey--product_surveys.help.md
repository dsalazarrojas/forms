# Pet Product Packaging Preference Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a tool to gather customer feedback about pet product packaging. We want to understand what customers are looking for in their pet packaging and where we can improve to meet their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply work through each page of questions, following the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What type of materials do you use in your pet packaging?** (`pet_packaging_materials`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question asks about your current usage of materials such as paper, cardboard, and plastic in your pet packaging.
- **What matters most when choosing eco-friendly materials?** (`eco_priority`, `select_one`, required): This question is about what matters most to you when choosing eco-friendly materials. Options include sustainability, cost, and brand reputation.
- **How would you rate your satisfaction with our current packaging?** (`satisfaction_score`, `number`, required): This question asks you to rate your satisfaction on a scale, where you can provide a numerical value.
- **Which of the following materials do you prefer in your pet packaging?** (`material_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question is about your preferences for materials in pet packaging, such as bioplastic or bioplastic-free, paper, or cardboard.
- **What are your top 3 pet pain points with our current packaging?** (`pet_pain_points`, `note`, required): This is an open-ended question where you can list up to three pain points you have with our current packaging.
- **Do you have any open ended comments about our current packaging?** (`open_ended_response`, `text`, required): This is an open-ended question where you can provide any feedback or comments you have about our current packaging.
- **How likely are you to recommend us to friends and family?** (`email`, `email`, required): This question is about your willingness to recommend us to others, and you should provide an email address.
- **How likely are you to recommend us to friends and family?** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): This is similar to the previous question, but you should provide a phone number.
- **Which time of day do you typically shop for pet supplies?** (`time_of_day`, `time`, required/optional): This question is about your shopping habits, and you should provide a time of day in 24-hour format.
- **What size of packaging do you prefer?** (`packaging_size`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question is about your preference for packaging size, with options for regular, compact, or other.
- **What type of packaging do you prefer?** (`packaging_type`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question is about your preference for single or multi-packaging.
- **What is your priority when choosing a pet product with packaging?** (`packaging_priority`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question is about your priorities when choosing pet products, with options for price, brand name, and packaging.
