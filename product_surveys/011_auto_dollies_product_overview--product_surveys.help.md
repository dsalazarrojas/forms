# Auto Dollies Product Overview - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your product needs and preferences. Please fill out the form to provide your prospective buyer information, product interest, and sales conversation details.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Provide Your Contact Information
* **Full Name**: Enter your complete name
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address, which will be used for product information and quotes
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, which will be used to discuss your requirements
* **Company Name** (optional): Leave blank if you are a personal buyer

### Step 2: Select Your Interested Dollie Models
* **Interested Dollie Models**: Select the products you are interested in learning more about from the options below:
	+ Standard Duty
	+ Heavy Duty
	+ Car Casters
	+ Tire Skates
	+ Hydraulic Dollies

### Step 3: Specify Your Purchase Details
* **Primary Use Case**: Choose how you plan to use these dollies:
	+ Personal Garage
	+ Professional Workshop
	+ Showroom
	+ Storage Facility
* **Quantity Interested**: Estimate the number of units you are interested in purchasing
* **Timeframe to Purchase**: Choose when you are looking to acquire these:
	+ Within 30 days
	+ 1-3 months
	+ 6+ months
	+ Just researching

### Step 4: Choose Your Contact Preferences
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select how you would like to be contacted:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ SMS

### Step 5: Request a Demo or Ask Questions
* **Request a Demo**: Would you like to see a product demonstration?
* **Specific Questions or Requirements**: Note any special needs or custom requests you have

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately to ensure we can better understand your product needs and preferences.
* If you have any questions or would like to request additional information, please don't hesitate to contact us.

This guide explains the form in a concrete and practical way to help you complete it effectively. If you need further assistance, please do not hesitate to contact us.
