# Beverage New Drink Satisfaction Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on a new drink, including demographics, taste, and purchase intentions. The feedback will help manufacturers and producers understand consumer preferences and satisfaction levels with their products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Your Name" and "Email Address" fields.
2. Select your age group from the "Age Group" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the location where you tasted the drink in the "Where Are You Tasting This Beverage" field.
4. Provide the date of your drink tasting experience in the "Date of Tasting" field.
5. Enter the name of the drink you're providing feedback on in the "Beverage Name" field.
6. Choose the category of the drink from the "Beverage Category" dropdown menu.
7. Describe the color of the drink in the "Color of Beverage" field.
8. Answer the questions about your first impression of the drink's appearance.
9. Describe the drink's aroma in the "What Did the Drink Smell Like" field.
10. Answer the questions about the appeal of the drink's smell.
11. Rate your overall taste impression of the drink and describe its flavor profile in the "Overall Taste Impression" and "Describe the Flavor Profile" fields.
12. Answer the questions about the drink's sweetness, tartness, and aftertaste quality.
13. Choose whether the flavor is authentic or not.
14. If you've tried similar products, enter them in the "What Existing Products is This Similar To" field.
15. Rate how the new drink compares to similar products in the "Compared to Similar Products" field.
16. If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them in the "What Could Be Improved About This Drink" field.
17. Rate your willingness to repurchase the drink and whether you'd recommend it to friends.
18. If you could pay a fair price for the drink, enter your answer in the "What Would Be a Fair Price for This Drink" field.
19. Provide any other comments or feedback in the "Any Other Comments or Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, required): Select your age group from 18-25, 26-35, 36-45, 46-55, 56-65, or 65+.
* **Where Are You Tasting This Beverage** (`tasting_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you tasted the drink.
* **Date of Tasting** (`tasting_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you tried this drink.
* **Beverage Name** (`drink_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the drink.
* **Beverage Category** (`drink_category`, select_one, required): Select the category of the drink: Soft drink, Energy drink, Juice or smoothie, Sports drink, Coffee beverage, Tea beverage, Flavored water, or Other.
* **Color of Beverage** (`drink_color`, text, optional): Describe the color of the drink.
* **Was the Appearance Appealing** (`appearance_appeal`, select_one, required): Rate the drink's appearance as Very appealing, Appealing, Neutral, Not very appealing, or Not appealing.
* **What Did the Drink Smell Like** (`aroma`, text, optional): Describe the aroma of the drink.
* **Was the Smell Appealing** (`aroma_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate the drink's smell as Very appealing, Appealing, Neutral, Not appealing, or Not appealing at all.
* **Overall Taste Impression** (`overall_taste`, select_one, required): Rate the drink's taste as Excellent, Good, Okay, Not good, or Poor.
* **Describe the Flavor Profile** (`flavor_profile`, text, required): Describe the flavor profile of the drink.
* **Sweetness Level** (`sweetness`, select_one, required): Rate the drink's sweetness level as Much too sweet, Too sweet, Just right, Not sweet enough, or Not sweet at all.
* **Is There an Appropriate Level of Tartness** (`tartness`, select_one, required): Rate the drink's tartness level as Much too tart, Too tart, Just right, Not tart enough, or Not tart.
* **Aftertaste Quality** (`aftertaste_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the drink's aftertaste quality as Pleasant aftertaste, Neutral aftertaste, Unpleasant aftertaste, or No aftertaste.
* **How Authentic is the Flavor** (`flavor_authenticity`, select_one, required): Rate the drink's flavor as Very authentic, Mostly authentic, Somewhat authentic, Not authentic, or Completely artificial.
* **What Existing Products is This Similar To** (`similar_products`, text, optional): Enter any similar products or brands you've tried.
* **Compared to Similar Products** (`vs_competitors`, select_one, required): Rate the new drink as Much better, Better, About the same, Worse, or Much worse.
* **What Makes This Drink Unique or Special** (`unique_aspect`, text, optional): Describe what makes this drink unique or special.
* **Would You Buy This Drink Again** (`repurchase_likelihood`, select_one, required): Rate your willingness to repurchase the drink as Definitely would buy, Probably would buy, Might buy, Probably would not buy, or Would not buy.
* **Would You Recommend This to Friends** (`recommend_likelihood`, select_one, required): Rate your willingness to recommend the drink to friends as Definitely would recommend, Probably would recommend, Might recommend, Probably would not recommend, or Would not recommend.
* **What Would Be a Fair Price for This Drink** (`price_acceptable`, text, optional): Enter your perceived fair price for the drink.
* **What Could Be Improved About This Drink** (`improvements`, text, optional): Suggest any improvements.
* **When Would You Most Likely Buy This Drink** (`purchase_occasion`, text, optional): Describe when you would most likely buy the drink (e.g., morning, afternoon, workout).
* **Any Other Comments or Feedback** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any other feedback about the drink.

## Tips
* Please answer questions as honestly and accurately as possible.
* Take as much time as you need to complete the form.
* If you have any questions, do not hesitate to ask.
* Thank you for taking the time to help us improve the new drink!
