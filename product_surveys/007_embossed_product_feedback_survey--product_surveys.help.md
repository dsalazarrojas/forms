# embossed_product_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about a specific product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions and select your rating and recommendation.
2. Provide any additional feedback you would like to share.
3. Enter your email address to receive any follow-up communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What do you think about this product? (`product_feedback`, text, required/optional)
* This field is a free-text area where you can share your thoughts, opinions, and experiences about the product.
### 2. Rate this product (`product_rating`, select_one, required/optional)
* This field allows you to rate the product from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
### 3. Would you recommend this product to a friend? (`product_recommend`, select_one, required/optional)
* Select "True" if you would recommend the product, "False" otherwise.
### 4. Additional feedback about this product (`additional_feedback`, note, required/optional)
* This field is a free-text area where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions about the product.
### 5. Email us at (`email`, email, required/optional)
* Enter your email address to receive any follow-up communication from the company.
### 6. What type of product is this? (`product_type`, select_one, required/optional)
* Select the type of product you are providing feedback about.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide specific feedback about your experience with the product.
* Use the additional feedback field to provide any specific details or suggestions that you want to share.
* Enter a valid email address if you would like to receive follow-up communication from the company.
