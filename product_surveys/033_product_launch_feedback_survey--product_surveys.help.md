# Product Launch Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects feedback from customers on a newly launched product, allowing us to gauge their satisfaction and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide feedback, follow these steps:

1.  Answer the questions about the category the product belongs to.
2.  Select how satisfied you are with the product.
3.  Provide suggestions for improvement, if any.
4.  Enter your contact information (email, phone number, and name) if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion.
5.  Enter any additional comments about the product.
6.  Finally, click on "Submit" to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Product Launch Feedback** (`product_launch_feedback`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you can provide your overall feedback on the product. Please feel free to share your thoughts and suggestions on how to improve the product.
*   **What category does this product belong to?** (`product_launch_category`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the category that best describes the type of product you are giving feedback on. Options are Electronics, Home, Clothing, and Others.
*   **How satisfied are you with this product?** (`product_launch_satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Let us know how satisfied you are with the product by selecting from Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
*   **What could we improve?** (`product_launch_improvement`, `text`, required: false): Here, you can specify what we could do better. If you have an idea, share it with us and we'll use it as a starting point for our next iteration of the product.
*   **Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Provide your email address if you would like to be contacted for further discussion or feedback on your suggestions.
*   **Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for further contact.
*   **Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name for us to know who you are.
*   **Additional comments** (`product_launch_comments`, `note`, required: false): This field is where you can provide any other comments or suggestions that you might want to share about the product.
*   **Submit** (`form_submit`, `text`, required: false): Once you have completed all the fields, click on the "Submit" button to send your feedback to us.

Note that the form fields are optional, but providing feedback is encouraged as it helps us make the product more user-friendly and competitive in the market.
