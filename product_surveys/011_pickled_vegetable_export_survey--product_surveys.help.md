# pickled_vegetable_export_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "pickled_vegetable_export_survey" form is designed to collect user information about their pickled vegetable export practices.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, you will be asked for information about your pickled vegetable export activities.
- Please provide the required information for each field to ensure accurate data collection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **survey_details** (Page 1, Text): This field allows you to enter a brief description of your pickled vegetable export practices.
  - Please enter a short summary of your export activities.
- **pickled_vegetables** (Page 1, Select Multiple): Choose all that apply to indicate which types of pickled vegetables you export.
  - Select "Yes" for each type of pickled vegetable you export.
- **export_destinations** (Page 1, Select One): Choose the location where you export your pickled vegetables.
  - Select the location from the provided options.
- **operational_challenges** (Page 1, Text): If you have faced any operational challenges with your pickled vegetable export, describe them in this field.
  - Please provide a clear and concise description of any challenges you have faced.
- **reporting_and_analysis** (Page 1, Date): Provide the date when you last reviewed your pickled vegetable export data.
  - Enter the date in the correct format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
- **operational_challenges_2** (Page 1, Text): If you have faced any operational challenges with your pickled vegetable export, describe them in this field.
  - Please provide a clear and concise description of any additional challenges you have faced.
- **reporting_and_analysis_2** (Page 1, Date): Provide the date when you last reviewed your pickled vegetable export data.
  - Enter the date in the correct format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
- **export_destinations_2** (Page 1, Select One): Choose the location where you export your pickled vegetables.
  - Select the location from the provided options.
- **reporting_and_analysis_3** (Page 1, Text): If you have any additional insights or observations about your pickled vegetable export, provide them here.
  - Please provide a clear and concise description of any insights you have gained.
- **export_destinations_3** (Page 1, Select One): Choose the location where you export your pickled vegetables.
  - Select the location from the provided options.
- **pickled_vegetables_2** (Page 1, Select Multiple): Choose all that apply to indicate which types of pickled vegetables you export.
  - Select "Yes" for each type of pickled vegetable you export.

## Tips
- Be as accurate as possible when filling out this form.
- If unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or support team.
- Double-check your responses before submitting the form.
