# Animal Image Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Image Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from users on a set of images. This feedback will help us understand how the images are perceived and will inform the development of future image sets. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button at the end of the form to save your feedback.
2. Fill out each section carefully, as they are all relevant to understanding your reaction to the images.
3. If you have trouble with a particular question, feel free to ask for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your name, if you're comfortable sharing it. This will help us associate your feedback with your identity.
* **Email Address (Optional)**: Enter your email address, if you'd like to be contacted with follow-up questions or to receive a summary of your feedback.
* **Animal Image Feedback Survey**: Read the introduction, which explains the purpose of the survey.
* **What Best Describes You**: Choose a category that best describes you from the list provided. This helps us understand your perspective.
* **Age Group (Optional)**: Select an age range that you identify with.
* **Image Set**: Select which set of images you're viewing.
* **Image Category**: Choose the category that best describes the images you're viewing.
* **Overall Impression**: Select how you feel about the images in general.
* **Image Quality**: Rate the quality of the images.
* **Emotional Response**: Choose the emotions the images evoke for you.
* **Clarity**: Rate how clear the images are.
* **Appropriateness**: Rate how well the images fit their intended purpose.
* **Favorite Image**: If you have a favorite image, select it. 
* **Reason for Favorite**: If you selected a favorite image, explain why.
* **Least Favorite Image**: If you have a least favorite image, select it. 
* **Reason for Least Favorite**: If you selected a least favorite image, explain why.
* **Authenticity**: Rate how authentic the images appear.
* **Diversity**: Rate the variety of animals in the images.
* **Engagement**: Rate how engaging the images are.
* **Memorability**: Rate how well you'll remember the images.
* **Intended Use**: Select where you'd best use the images.
* **Improvements**: Provide any suggestions for improvement.
* **Additional Images**: Suggest additional types of images that would be helpful.
* **Usage Permission**: Select whether you're okay with us using your feedback for analysis.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional feedback or comments about the images.

Please note that some fields are optional, but all are relevant to understanding your reaction to the images.
