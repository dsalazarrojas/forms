# Eco Conscious Makeup Brand Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eco Conscious Makeup Brand Survey aims to gather information about your purchasing habits and preferences for eco-conscious makeup brands. Your responses will help us better understand our customers' needs and improve our brand offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer according to your true preferences and habits.
2. Select the options that best match your answers.
3. Fill in your email and phone number (optional) if you'd like to be contacted back.
4. Submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`intro_page`): This page provides a brief introduction to the survey.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Please provide your email and/or phone number so we can reach you back with any further questions or to follow up on your survey responses.
	+ **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
	+ **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **What type of eco-conscious products have you purchased from** (`product_purchased`): Select the type of eco-friendly makeup products you've purchased from our brand or similar brands.
	+ Options: Eco-friendly makeup, Non-ecofriendly makeup
* **Which eco-conscious brand do you like most?** (`eco_conscious_brand`): Choose the brand you prefer among the listed options or select "Other" if you have a different brand in mind.
	+ Options: Pat McGrath, Huda Beauty, Fenty Beauty, Other
* **How old are you?** (`age_range`): Enter your age range.
* **What area of the world do you live in?** (`location`): Select the region where you reside.
	+ Options: North America, Europe, Asia, Africa, South America, Other
* **How often do you do makeup?** (`how_often_makeup`): Choose how frequently you use makeup.
	+ Options: Daily, 3-4 times a week, 1-2 times a week, Less than once a month
* **What's your social media preference?** (`social_media`): Select your preferred social media platform.
	+ Options: Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, Other
* **How much money do you spend on makeup?** (`how_much_money`): Choose your average expenditure on makeup products.
	+ Options: Less than $10, $10-20, $21-50, $51 or more
* **How much do you know about eco-friendly products?** (`eco_awareness`): Choose how much you know about eco-friendly products.
	+ Options: A lot, A little, None
* **Would you recommend this eco-friendly brand?** (`recommend_brand`): Select whether you would recommend our brand to others.
	+ Options: True, False

Note: All responses will be kept anonymous. We value your time and appreciate your participation in this survey.
