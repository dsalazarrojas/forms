# Neighborhood Price Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool to gather information about a household's expenses, financial situation, and other relevant factors that impact their well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly. You will need to provide information about your household's income, household size, neighborhood, transportation costs, housing, health insurance, food expenses, entertainment, community resources, housing rent, and housing ownership status. Make sure to review each question carefully and provide as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Household Income 1**: Enter the total monthly income for your household. This can include income from employment, investments, or government benefits.
* **Household Size 1**: Enter the number of people living in your household.
* **Neighborhood 1**: Enter the name of your neighborhood or the name of the area you reside in.
* **Neighborhood 2**: Enter any additional information about your neighborhood, such as the name of the city or town.
* **Grocery Shopping Frequency 1**: Check if your household shops for groceries weekly or daily. This question helps us understand your shopping frequency.
* **Grocery Shopping Frequency 2**: Check if your household shops for groceries more than once a week or has a specific schedule.
* **Transportation Cost 1**: Enter the estimated monthly cost of your household's transportation expenses.
* **Transportation Cost 2**: Enter any additional information about your transportation expenses, such as fuel costs or parking fees.
* **Housing Cost 1**: Enter the estimated monthly cost of your household's housing expenses.
* **Housing Cost 2**: Enter any additional information about your housing expenses, such as rent or mortgage payments.
* **Health Insurance 1**: Check if your household has health insurance coverage. This can include private insurance, government programs, or other forms of coverage.
* **Health Insurance 2**: Check if your household's health insurance is employer-sponsored, private, or other.
* **Food Cost 1**: Enter the estimated monthly cost of your household's food expenses.
* **Food Cost 2**: Enter any additional information about your food expenses, such as dietary restrictions or food assistance programs.
* **Entertainment 1**: Check if your household engages in various forms of entertainment, such as movies, concerts, or sports.
* **Entertainment 2**: Check if your household has a preferred form of entertainment or a specific schedule.
* **Community Resources 1**: Check if your household uses community resources, such as parks, libraries, or community centers.
* **Community Resources 2**: Check if your household has a favorite community resource or a specific schedule.
* **Housing Rent 1**: Enter the estimated monthly rent cost of your household's housing expenses.
* **Housing Rent 2**: Enter any additional information about your housing rent, such as lease terms or landlord requirements.
* **Housing Own 1**: Check if your household owns or rents their housing.
* **Housing Own 2**: Check if your household has a preferred form of housing ownership.
* **Housing Mortgage 1**: Enter the estimated monthly mortgage cost of your household's housing expenses.
* **Housing Mortgage 2**: Enter any additional information about your housing mortgage, such as interest rates or loan terms.
* **Comments 1**: Please enter any additional comments or information about your household's financial situation.
