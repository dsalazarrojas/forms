# Digital Product Feedback Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to provide feedback about digital products, which includes reporting bugs, suggesting new features, or sharing their general thoughts and comments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of feedback you would like to provide: "Feedback Type" is a dropdown menu that allows you to choose how you want to submit your feedback. Please select "Feedback Type" to start.
2. Enter your thoughts: In the "Feedback" text field, type out your comment or concern. Be as detailed as possible so we can understand your issue.
3. Identify the product: Enter the name of the product you are providing feedback about in the "Product Name" field.
4. Provide your contact information: You can submit your "Email Address" and "Phone Number" if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions.
5. Add context: If relevant, use "Date" and "Time" fields to provide a specific timeline for your feedback.
6. Attach additional information: You can "Upload Attachment" or a "Document" or "Image" related to your feedback.
7. Submit your feedback: Click submit to send your feedback to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Type** (`select_one`):
	+ This is a dropdown menu that allows you to choose the type of feedback you'd like to provide: "Feedback Type", "Bug Report", or "Feature Request".
* **Feedback** (`text`):
	+ This is a text field where you can type out your detailed comment or concern.
* **Product Name** (`text`):
	+ Enter the name of the product you are providing feedback about.
* **Email Address** (`email`):
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions.
* **Phone Number** (`text`):
	+ Enter your phone number so we can contact you for follow-up questions.
* **Date** (`date`):
	+ If you're reporting a bug or issue, use this field to provide the date you experienced it.
* **Time** (`time`):
	+ If you're reporting a bug or issue, use this field to provide the time you experienced it.
* **Comments** (`note`):
	+ This is a text area where you can add additional comments or details about your feedback.
* **Upload Attachment** (`text`):
	+ This field allows you to upload files related to your feedback.
* **Upload Document** (`text`):
	+ This field allows you to upload documents related to your feedback.
* **Upload Image** (`text`):
	+ This field allows you to upload images related to your feedback.

## Tips

* Please be specific when providing feedback, especially if reporting a bug or requesting a feature.
* Make sure to provide clear and concise comments so we can understand your issue.
* If you're reporting a bug, please provide as much detail as possible so we can reproduce and fix the issue.
* If you're requesting a feature, please explain why it would be useful and how it would improve the product.
* Duplicate submissions will be deleted.
