# Business Notepad Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Business Notepad Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from business users on their experience with our product. Your input will help us understand your satisfaction, pain points, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select the form sections you'd like to fill out**: You can navigate through the form's pages using the "Next" and "Previous" buttons.
2. **Fill out each field according to the instructions**: Each field has a label, hint, and type. Please make sure to follow these guidelines to ensure accurate and complete feedback.
3. **Submit your feedback**: Once you've filled out all required fields, click "Submit" to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Date (1)** (`feedback_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are providing feedback.
* **Your Name (2)** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the feedback provider.
* **Email Address (3)** (`user_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Company Name (4)** (`user_company`, text, optional): If you're providing feedback from your company, enter your organization's name.
* **How Long Have You Used the Product? (5)** (`product_experience_duration`, select_one, required): Select how long you've used the product from the given options.
* **Overall Product Satisfaction (6)** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the product (1-10).
* **Ease of Use (7)** (`ease_of_use`, number, required): Rate how easy it is to use the product (1-10).
* **Feature Completeness (8)** (`feature_completeness`, number, required): Rate if features meet your needs (1-10).
* **Value for Money (9)** (`value_for_money`, number, required): Rate if the product is worth the price (1-10).
* **Customer Support Quality (10)** (`customer_support_quality`, number, optional): Rate the quality of customer support (1-10).
* **What Are Your Favorite Features? (11)** (`best_features`, text, required): Describe the best aspects of the product.
* **Areas for Improvement (12)** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, required): Suggest areas where the product can improve.
* **Missing Features or Functionality (13)** (`missing_features`, text, optional): List any features or functionality you're missing.
* **Biggest Issue or Frustration (14)** (`biggest_issue`, text, optional): Describe what frustrated you most.
* **Would You Recommend to Others? (15)** (`recommend_to_others`, select_one, required): Select how likely you are to recommend the product to others.
* **Likelihood to Recommend Score (16)** (`likelihood_score`, number, required): Rate your likelihood to recommend the product (0-10).
* **How Does It Compare to Competitors? (17)** (`comparison_with_competitors`, text, optional): Provide a comparison with competitors.
* **Additional Comments (18)** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any other feedback or suggestions.
* **May We Contact You for More Details? (19)** (`permission_to_contact`, select_one, optional): Select if you're okay with us following up on your feedback.
