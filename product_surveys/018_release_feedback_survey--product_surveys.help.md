# Release Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from end users about a specific release or product launch. It helps us understand what you think about the release and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of feedback you want to provide by selecting one or more options in the "What type of feedback?" field.
2. Type in your thoughts on what could be improved in the "What do you think could be improved?" field.
3. If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them in the "Suggest an improvement" field.
4. Enter your email address and phone number in the respective fields if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
5. Choose a date and time when the release was experienced.
6. Add any additional comments about the release.
7. Select the release from the "Release Survey" dropdown menu.
8. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Release Feedback Survey** (`intro_page`, text, required): This is the title of the form, which you won't need to fill in.
* **What type of feedback?** (`feedback_type`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more options to indicate the type of feedback you're providing.
* **What do you think could be improved?** (`feedback_question`, text, required): Describe what you think could be improved.
* **Suggest an improvement** (`suggest_improvement`, text, required): Share your suggestions for improvement.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your feedback.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Choose the date when the release was experienced.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Choose the time when the release was experienced.
* **Additional Comments** (`note`, note, required): Add any additional comments or observations about the release.
* **Release Survey** (`release_survey`, select_one, required): Select the release from the dropdown menu that corresponds to your feedback. 
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, note, required): Click this button to send your feedback. 

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback.
* Make sure to provide specific examples or details about the release.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact us at your email address.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide us with your feedback!
