# Product Stocking Retailer Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from retailers about their experiences with our product stocking and delivery processes. The goal is to identify areas of improvement and provide a platform for retailers to share their thoughts on how we can better meet their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions in the form according to your experiences with our product stocking and delivery processes.
2. Choose the options that best describe your answers.
3. Fill in any additional comments or information you'd like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is the main reason you're experiencing stocking issues with our products?**: Describe the main reason you're experiencing stocking issues with our products.
- **Delivery issue**: Please select all the delivery issues you're experiencing, such as "Delivery issue", "Late delivery", "Incorrect delivery", or "No delivery information provided".
- **How satisfied are you with the delivery experience?**: Rate your satisfaction with the delivery experience using the options "Very satisfied", "Somewhat satisfied", "Neutral", "Somewhat dissatisfied", or "Very dissatisfied".
- **What type of information would you like to see about the delivery status in our system?**: Select all the types of information you'd like to see, such as "Estimated delivery date", "Actual delivery date", "Order status", "Estimated delivery time", or "Other".
- **What is your overall ordering experience with our system?**: Describe your overall experience with placing orders through our system.
- **Ordering process**: Rate your experience with the ordering process using the options "Easy", "Difficult", "Very easy", "Neutral", or "Neutral".
- **Order fulfillment**: Rate your experience with order fulfillment using the options "Fast", "Slow", "Very fast", "Neutral", or "Neutral".
- **Price and availability**: Rate your experience with price and availability using the options "Good", "Bad", "Neutral".
- **How often do you receive promotional emails from us?**: Select how often you receive promotional emails from us, such as "Daily", "Weekly", "Monthly", or "Rarely".
- **What type of products would you like to see more promotions on?**: Select all the types of products you'd like to see more promotions on.
- **How often do you open emails from us?**: Select how often you open emails from us, such as "Daily", "Weekly", "Monthly", or "Rarely".
- **What type of promotional content do you find most effective?**: Select the types of promotional content you find most effective, such as "Video", "Image", "Text", or "Offer".
- **How often do you engage with our promotional content?**: Select how often you engage with our promotional content, such as "Almost all", "Some", "Rarely", or "Never".
- **How satisfied are you with our promotional content?**: Rate your satisfaction with our promotional content using the options "Very satisfied", "Somewhat satisfied", "Neutral", "Somewhat dissatisfied", or "Very dissatisfied".
- **How much would you pay for a promotional service?**: Select the range you'd be willing to pay for a promotional service, such as "$0-$10", "$10-$50", "More than $50", or "I do not want to pay".
- **What do you want to see included in our promotional service?**: Select all the features you'd like to see included in our promotional service, such as "Delivery tracking", "Order tracking", "Product tracking", or "Other".
- **How often do you want to see our promotional service available?**: Select how often you'd like to see our promotional service available, such as "Every 1 week", "Every 2 weeks", "Every 4 weeks", or "Every month".
- **Do you want to be contacted for further feedback or discussion?**: Select whether you'd like to be contacted for further feedback or discussion, such as "Yes", "No", or "Maybe".
- **How do you want to be contacted?**: Select the method you'd prefer to be contacted, such as "Email", "Phone", "In-app message", or "Other".
