# Lash Product Packaging Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from users about the Lash product packaging. Your input will help us to improve the packaging design and user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each page of the form carefully.
2. Answer the questions and provide any necessary information in the spaces provided.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intoduction** (`intro`, text, required: false): This is the introduction to the survey.
* **Lash Product** (`lash_product`, text, required: false): Please enter the name of the Lash product you are providing feedback on.
* **Packaging** (`packaging`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Choose "Yes" if you have received the product in its correct packaging.
	+ Choose "No" if you have not received the product in its correct packaging.
* **Packaging Rating** (`packaging_rating`, select_one, required: false):
	+ 1/5: The packaging was very bad.
	+ 2/5: The packaging was somewhat bad.
	+ 3/5: The packaging was neutral.
	+ 4/5: The packaging was somewhat good.
	+ 5/5: The packaging was very good.
* **Comments** (`packaging_comments`, text, required: false): Please provide any comments you have about the packaging.
* **Suggestions** (`packaging_suggestions`, text, required: false): If you have any suggestions for improving the packaging, please enter them here.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, email, required: false): Please enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Please enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Please enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Please enter the time you submitted this form.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Please enter any additional comments you have about the product.
* **Agree** (`agree`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Strongly Agree: I completely agree with the feedback I provided.
	+ Somewhat Agree: I somewhat agree with the feedback I provided.
	+ Neither: I do not agree or disagree with the feedback I provided.
	+ Somewhat Disagree: I somewhat disagree with the feedback I provided.
	+ Strongly Disagree: I completely disagree with the feedback I provided.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, required: false): If you have any additional suggestions, please enter them here.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required: false): Please enter your signature.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): Please enter the name of your company.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): Please enter the name of your department.
* **Position** (`position`, text, required: false): Please enter your position.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Please enter your job title.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Please enter the name of the contact person.
* **Email Address** (`email_addr`, text, required: false): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_no`, text, required: false): Please enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Please enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Please enter the time you submitted this form.
