# Stock Market Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your investment strategy, market preferences, and risk tolerance. Your responses will help us better understand your needs and provide more relevant information to help you make informed investment decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
2. Select only the options that apply to your current situation.
3. If you have any other investment strategies, risks, or preferences not listed, please include them in the "Note" field.
4. You can leave the "Email" and "Phone" fields empty if you don't want to provide this information.
5. Finally, provide any additional comments or questions you have in the "Note" field.

- **Stock Market Analysis Survey** (`stock_market_analysis_survey`, `text`): This is the title of the form. Please ignore this field.
- **What is your current investment strategy?** (`stock_market_analysis_survey_what`, `select_one`): Select one of the options that best describes your current investment strategy.

- **What is your risk tolerance?** (`stock_market_analysis_survey_what_is`, `select_multiple`): Select all the options that apply to your risk tolerance.
- **What is your favorite stock?** (`stock_market_analysis_survey_what_is_2`, `select_multiple`): Select all the stocks that you like or have an interest in.
