The Wallpaper Change Interval Survey form is designed to gather information from users about their habits and preferences related to changing their wallpaper. This form aims to understand how often users change their wallpaper, what influences their decisions, and their preferred interval for changing it. The form is used by the product team to inform future development decisions and product enhancements.

# Wallpaper Change Interval Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wallpaper Change Interval Survey is designed to collect user feedback and preferences to inform product development decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply navigate through the pages below and provide your answers according to the instructions for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How Frequently Do You Change Your Wallpaper?**
	+ (`user_frequent_change`, select_one, Optional)
	+ This question asks about the frequency of your wallpaper changes. Select the option that best describes your habits:
		- Frequent: You change your wallpaper often.
		- Occasionally: You change your wallpaper from time to time.
		- Rarely: You change your wallpaper very seldom.
		- Never: You never change your wallpaper.
* **What Influences Your Wallpaper Change Decisions?**
	+ (`user_reason_for_change`, select_multiple, Optional)
	+ This question asks about the factors that influence your wallpaper change decisions. Select all that apply:
		- Boredom: You change your wallpaper due to boredom.
		- Aesthetics: You change your wallpaper for aesthetic reasons.
		- Functionality: You change your wallpaper for functional reasons (e.g., for better performance).
		- Other: Other factors influence your wallpaper change decisions.
* **What Is Your Preferred Interval for Changing Your Wallpaper?**
	+ (`user_interval`, select_one, Optional)
	+ This question asks about your preferred interval for changing your wallpaper. Select the option that best suits your needs:
		- Monthly: You prefer to change your wallpaper every month.
		- Quarterly: You prefer to change your wallpaper every quarter.
		- Semi-annually: You prefer to change your wallpaper twice a year.
		- Annually: You prefer to change your wallpaper once a year.
* **Do You Have a Contact Email Address for Wallpaper Inquiries?**
	+ (`user_email`, email, Optional)
	+ Enter your contact email address if you'd like to be contacted about new wallpaper features or changes.
* **Do You Have a Contact Phone Number?**
	+ (`user_phone`, text, Optional)
	+ Enter your contact phone number if you'd like to be contacted about new wallpaper features or changes.
* **Any Additional Notes or Comments?**
	+ (`user_additional_notes`, note, Optional)
	+ Provide any additional information or comments about your wallpaper preferences or habits.
