# Bear Trap Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather information about the use of bear traps by individuals involved in wildlife management. The survey aims to collect data on various aspects of bear trap usage, including the frequency of use, purpose, and effectiveness of traps. This information will help improve the understanding of bear trap usage and contribute to more effective and humane wildlife management practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
2. Select the name of your organization or agency from the list.
3. Choose your job title or role from the provided options.
4. Select the number of years you have experience using bear traps.
5. Indicate how often you use bear traps.
6. Choose the primary purpose for using bear traps.
7. Select the type of trap you commonly use.
8. Confirm whether you have received safety training on bear trap use.
9. Report any safety incidents you have experienced while using bear traps.
10. Indicate how often non-target species are caught with bear traps.
11. Rate the effectiveness of bear traps in achieving your goals.
12. Express your level of concern for the humane treatment of animals caught in bear traps.
13. Indicate how often you follow regulatory requirements for bear trap use.
14. Express your level of interest in alternative methods for managing wildlife.
15. Share your opinion on the current regulations for bear trap use.
16. Add any additional comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official identification. This is an optional field, but it helps us get in touch with you for further clarification or follow-up questions.
* **Organization**: Select the name of your organization or agency from the list. This helps us identify your affiliation and understand your role in wildlife management.
* **Your Role**: Choose your job title or role from the provided options. This helps us understand your responsibilities and expertise in wildlife management.
* **Years of Experience**: Select the number of years you have experience using bear traps. This helps us understand your level of familiarity with bear traps and their usage.
* **Frequency of Bear Trap Usage**: Indicate how often you use bear traps. This helps us understand your usage patterns and frequency of trap deployment.
* **Primary Purpose**: Choose the primary purpose for using bear traps. This helps us understand your goals and objectives when using bear traps.
* **Type of Trap Used**: Select the type of trap you commonly use. This helps us understand your preferred trap type and usage.
* **Safety Training Completed**: Confirm whether you have received safety training on bear trap use. This helps us assess your level of preparedness for safe trap handling and usage.
* **Safety Incidents Experienced**: Report any safety incidents you have experienced while using bear traps. This helps us identify potential hazards and risks associated with bear trap use.
* **Non-Target Captures**: Indicate how often non-target species are caught with bear traps. This helps us understand the species caught and the effectiveness of traps.
* **Effectiveness Rating**: Rate the effectiveness of bear traps in achieving your goals. This helps us understand the performance of bear traps in your specific context.
* **Humane Concerns**: Express your level of concern for the humane treatment of animals caught in bear traps. This helps us understand your values and attitudes towards animal welfare.
* **Regulatory Compliance**: Indicate how often you follow regulatory requirements for bear trap use. This helps us assess your level of adherence to rules and regulations.
* **Interest in Alternative Methods**: Express your level of interest in alternative methods for managing wildlife. This helps us understand your openness to exploring new approaches.
* **Opinion on Bear Trap Regulations**: Share your opinion on the current regulations for bear trap use. This helps us understand your views on regulatory frameworks and their effectiveness.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback you may have on bear trap usage. This helps us gather more detailed information and insights from your experiences.
