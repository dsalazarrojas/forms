# Appliance And Furniture Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Appliance And Furniture Feedback form, which is designed to collect feedback from customers on their experience with our products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your personal details such as your name and email address.
2. Provide details about the product you are submitting feedback on, including the product category and type.
3. Rate your satisfaction and experience with the product, including its quality, functionality, and customer service experience.
4. Describe any issues you experienced with the product or its delivery.
5. Share any photos of the product if you have some.
6. Let us know if you would recommend this product to others and if you would buy it again.
7. Finally, submit your feedback and we will review it and use it to improve our products and services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (1)**: Full name of the person submitting feedback.
* **Email Address (2)**: The email address of the person submitting feedback.
* **Phone Number (3)**: The phone number of the person submitting feedback (optional).
* **Feedback Date (4)**: The date of submission of the feedback.
* **Product Category (5)**: Select the category of the product you are providing feedback on (e.g. Kitchen appliances, Laundry appliances, etc.).
* **Product Type (6)**: The specific model or type of product (e.g. Washing machine, sofa, etc.).
* **Brand Name (7)**: The name of the brand of the product.
* **Model Number (8)**: The model number of the product (if known).
* **Purchase Date (9)**: The date you purchased the product.
* **Purchase Location (10)**: Where did you purchase the product (e.g. In-store, Online, etc.).
* **Store Location (11)**: The location of the store where you purchased the product (if in-store, optional).
* **Price Paid (12)**: The price you paid for the product.
* **Ownership Duration (13)**: How long you have owned the product (e.g. Less than 1 month, 6 months to 1 year, etc.).
* **Overall Satisfaction (14)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the product.
* **Quality Rating (15)**: Rate the quality of the product.
* **Value for Money (16)**: Rate how well the product met your expectations in terms of value.
* **Appearance Rating (17)**: Rate the appearance of the product.
* **Functionality Rating (18)**: Rate how well the product functions.
* **Durability Rating (19)**: Rate how well the product has held up over time.
* **Ease of Use (20)**: Rate how easy it is to use the product.
* **Features Rating (21)**: Rate the features of the product.
* **Favorite Features (22)**: The features you liked most about the product.
* **Least Favorite Features (23)**: The features you liked least about the product.
* **Issues Experienced (24)**: Select all issues you experienced with the product (e.g. Does not work as expected, Broke or malfunctioned, etc.).
* **Issues Description (25)**: Describe any issues you experienced with the product.
* **Customer Service Experience (26)**: Rate your experience with our customer service.
* **Delivery Experience (27)**: Rate your experience with the delivery of the product.
* **Assembly Experience (28)**: Rate your experience with assembling the product (if applicable).
* **Would Recommend (29)**: Would you recommend this product to others.
* **Would Buy Again (30)**: Would you buy this product again.
* **Comparison to Competitors (31)**: How does this product compare to others in the market.
* **Photos Attached (32)**: Do you have photos of the product you can attach.
* **Additional Comments (33)**: Any additional comments or feedback you have about the product.
* **Follow-up Contact (34)**: Would you be willing to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussion.
