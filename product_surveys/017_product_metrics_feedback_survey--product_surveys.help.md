# product_metrics_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The product metrics feedback survey is an optional form designed to collect feedback from customers on their satisfaction and experience with a product. This feedback helps the product development team understand customer needs and improve the product and its features.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Intro**: This is a note field where you can provide any additional context or information about your experience with the product.
2. **Satisfaction**: Please select a number that best represents your level of satisfaction with the product. This is a subjective measure of how satisfied you are with the product's performance and features.
3. **Feature Usage**: Describe how you use the product's features, including any specific features you use frequently or struggle with. This will help us understand how you interact with the product.
4. **Improvement Ideas**: Select all ideas you have for improving the product. These could be related to its performance, usability, or features. Think about how we can make the product more useful and enjoyable for you and others.
5. **Satisfaction Scale**: Select a scale that best represents your level of satisfaction with the product's performance and features. This scale is subjective and should reflect your real feelings.
6. **Email**: If you're comfortable sharing your email address, please enter it to allow us to follow up on your feedback and discuss any further questions or issues you may have.
7. **Phone Number**: This is an optional field where you can enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted directly for further questions or clarification.
8. **Comments**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the product and its features.
