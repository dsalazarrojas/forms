# 3D Wall Art Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers on their experience with 3D wall art products, with the goal of improving product quality and user experience.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Submit Feedback" button.
2.  Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
3.  Choose the answers for each question carefully, as they will help us understand your experience and satisfaction with our products.
4.  Don't hesitate to add any additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first and last name to help us identify you.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): We'll use this to follow up on your feedback and may contact you if needed.
*   **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, `date`, optional): If you'd like to share when you purchased your 3D wall art, please select the date.
*   **Product Line** (`product_line`, `select_one`, required): Select the product line you purchased, such as Geometric Patterns, Nature Landscapes, Abstract Art, Modern Minimalist, Vintage Collection, or Custom Design.
*   **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Choose how satisfied you are with your 3D wall art, from Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
*   **Installation Experience** (`installation_experience`, `select_one`, required): Rate how easy or difficult the installation process was, from Very Easy to Very Difficult.
*   **Quality Rating** (`quality_rating`, `integer`, required): Rate the quality of your 3D wall art, from 1 (Poor quality) to 10 (Excellent quality).
*   **Key Features** (`key_features`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all aspects that influenced your satisfaction, such as Visual appeal, Durability, Installation ease, Value for money, Color accuracy, Depth effect, Size proportions.
*   **Likelihood to Recommend** (`likelihood_to_recommend`, `integer`, required): Rate how likely you are to recommend our 3D wall art to others, from 0 (Not at all) to 10 (Extremely likely).
*   **Price Perception** (`price_perception`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you perceive the pricing of our 3D wall art, from Great value to Too expensive.
*   **Room Placement** (`room_placement`, `select_one`, optional): Select where you installed the 3D wall art, from Living room to Other (please specify).
*   **Light Condition** (`light_condition`, `select_one`, optional): Rate how lighting affects the appearance of the 3D effect, from Excellent in all lighting to Poor in all lighting.
*   **Improvement Suggestion** (`improvement_suggestion`, `text`, optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions you have for our 3D wall art products.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any other thoughts or suggestions you have.
*   **Contact Permission** (`contact_permission`, `select_one`, optional): Choose if you'd like us to contact you for further feedback or product updates, from True to False.
