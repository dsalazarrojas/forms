# Customer Feedback On Pricing - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers on the pricing of a product or service, aiming to gather information on customer understanding, pricing clarity, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions based on your current or previous experience with the product or service.
2. Provide specific examples or details when answering questions.
3. Answer all questions that apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Feedback On Pricing**: This is the main question where you can provide overall feedback on the pricing.
	+ Type: `note`
	+ This field allows you to give a general comment on the pricing.
* **Price Clarity**: This question helps us understand how clear the price is to you.
	+ Type: `select_one`
	+ Answer based on how clear the price is to you:
		- `clear`: The price is clear and easy to understand.
		- `not clear`: The price is not clear, and you need more information.
		- `unclear`: You are unsure about the price.
* **Value For Money**: This question rates your perception of the value of the product or service for the price paid.
	+ Type: `select_multiple`
	+ Answer based on your experience:
		- `good`: You think the product or service is worth the price paid.
		- `bad`: You do not think the product or service is worth the price paid.
		- `neutral`: You are neutral about the value for money.
* **Suggestions**: This is a text field where you can provide specific suggestions for improvement.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Provide concrete and constructive feedback.
* **Loyalty**: This question measures how much you are likely to be loyal to the product or service based on its pricing.
	+ Type: `number`
	+ Answer based on your likelihood of loyalty:
		- Higher numbers indicate greater likelihood of loyalty.
* **Pricing Clarity Important**: This question rates how important pricing clarity is to you.
	+ Type: `select_one`
	+ Answer based on how important pricing clarity is:
		- `very important`: Pricing clarity is very important to you.
		- `somewhat important`: Pricing clarity is somewhat important to you.
		- `not important`: Pricing clarity is not important to you.
* **Price Range**: This question asks about your perception of the price range of the product or service.
	+ Type: `number`
	+ Answer based on your understanding:
		- Higher numbers indicate a higher price range.
* **Price Comparisons**: This question asks if you have compared the price of the product or service with competitors.
	+ Type: `select_multiple`
	+ Answer based on your experience:
		- `True`: You have compared prices with competitors.
		- `False`: You have not compared prices with competitors.
		- `maybe`: You are unsure about price comparisons.

## Tips
* Be honest and specific when answering the questions.
* Provide concrete examples when giving feedback.
* Answer all questions that apply to you.
