# TV Show Design Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your proposed TV show ideas. Please fill out this form to provide as much detail as possible about your idea.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Fill out each page of the form, working from top to bottom.
3. Answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
4. If a field is marked as "required", make sure to fill it out accordingly.
5. If you have any issues or questions, please contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **TV Show Design Survey Page 1**: This page is meant to capture a brief overview of your proposed TV show.
	+ **TV Show Design Survey Page 1**: This is the title of the form.
	+ **Demographics**: Please select the demographic details that best fit your proposed TV show.
	+ **Proposed Series Name**: Enter the title of your proposed TV show.
* **TV Show Design Survey Page 2**: This page collects information about your proposed TV show.
	+ **Series Type**: Please select the type of series you are proposing (e.g. Drama, Comedy, etc.).
	+ **Target Audience Age Group**: Select the age group that would be interested in your proposed TV show.
	+ **Proposed Series Genres**: Please select the genres that best fit your proposed TV show.
	+ **Proposed Series Plot**: Select the plot details that best fit your proposed TV show.
	+ **Proposed Series Characters**: Select the characters that will appear in your proposed TV show.
* **TV Show Design Survey Page 3**: This page collects information about your proposed TV show's production details.
	+ **Proposed Series Episodes**: Enter the number of episodes you propose for your TV show.
	+ **Proposed Series Length**: Enter the length of your proposed TV show in minutes.
	+ **Network**: Select the network where your proposed TV show would be airing.
* **TV Show Design Survey Page 4**: This page collects information about your proposed TV show's production details.
	+ **Proposed Series Budget**: Enter the proposed budget for your TV show.
	+ **Production Company**: Select the production company that will produce your proposed TV show.
	+ **Distribution Channels**: Select the channels through which your proposed TV show will be distributed.
	+ **Marketing Strategy**: Select the marketing strategy you propose for your TV show.
* **TV Show Design Survey Page 5**: This page collects information about your proposed TV show's content delivery details.
	+ **Content Delivery Date**: Enter the date when you propose to deliver your TV show's content.
	+ **Content Delivery Time**: Enter the time when you propose to deliver your TV show's content.
* **TV Show Design Survey Page 6**: This page collects information about your proposed TV show's target audience.
	+ **Target Audience**: Select the target audience for your proposed TV show.
	+ **Target Audience Location**: Select the location of your target audience.
	+ **Target Audience Education**: Select the educational level of your target audience.
	+ **Target Audience Income**: Select the income level of your target audience.
	+ **Target Audience Employment**: Select the employment status of your target audience.
	+ **Target Audience Interests**: Select the interests of your target audience.
+ **Proposed Series Budget**: Enter the proposed budget for your TV show.
* **Additional Details**: This is a text field for any additional information you would like to provide about your proposed TV show.
