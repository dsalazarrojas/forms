# Salad Kit Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers who have purchased a salad kit.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide feedback, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the required fields.
2. Enter your email address, which will be used to contact you in case we need further information.
3. Choose the type of salad kit you purchased (Fresh, Organic, Regular, or Vegan).
4. Rate your satisfaction with the product (1-5 stars).
5. Provide any comments about your experience with the product.
6. Submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or driver's license.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or driver's license.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you if we need further information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for further information.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): If you're a business customer, please enter your company name.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Please enter the date you purchased the salad kit.
* **Kit Type** (`kit_type`, select_one, required): Please choose the type of salad kit you purchased (Fresh, Organic, Regular, Vegan).
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, number, required): Please rate your satisfaction with the product on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Please provide any comments or suggestions about your experience with the product.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, required): Please choose a rating for the product (1-5 stars).

## Tips
* Please fill out the form in its entirety before submitting it. If you're unsure about a field, please refer to this help guide for clarification.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact email or phone number].
* Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your time and effort in helping us improve our product.
