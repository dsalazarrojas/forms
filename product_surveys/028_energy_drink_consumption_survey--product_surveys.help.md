# Energy Drink Consumption Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about energy drink consumption habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. **Energy Drink Consumption** (`energy_drink_consumption_survey`, `select_one`): Select one option from the list to indicate how often you consume energy drinks. The options are:
	* Never Consumed
	* Less than once a month
	* 1-2 times a month
	* 3-4 times a month
	* Daily
2. **Other** (`consumption_frequency_other`, `text`): If you consume energy drinks less than once a month, please specify the frequency in the blank field.
3. **Reasons for Consumption** (`reasons_for_consumption`, `select_multiple`): Select all that apply for reasons why you consume energy drinks. The options are:
	* Energy and alertness
	* Taste
	* Social events
	* To relax or relieve stress
4. **Reasons For Consumption Other** (`reasons_for_consumption_other`, `text`): If other, please specify your reason for consumption.
5. **Side Effects** (`side_effects`, `select_multiple`): Select all that apply for any side effects you experience after consuming energy drinks. The options are:
	* Headaches
	* Insomnia
	* Anxiety
	* High blood pressure
6. **Side Effects Other** (`side_effects_other`, `text`): If you experience any other side effects, please specify them in the blank field.
7. **Consumption Amount** (`consumption_amount`, `select_multiple`): Select one option from the list to indicate how much energy drink you typically consume. The options are:
	* Less than 500ml
	* 500ml-1000ml
	* 1-2L
	* 2-4L
	* 4L or more
8. **Consumption Amount Other** (`consumption_amount_other`, `text`): If other, please specify the amount you consume.
9. **Duration of Consumption** (`duration_of_consumption`, `date`): Enter the date you started consuming energy drinks.
10. **Daily Routine** (`daily_routine`, `text`): If you want to provide additional information about your daily routine, please enter it in this field.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and accurately.
* If you have any other information about your energy drink consumption habits, please provide it in the "Daily Routine" field.

Note: This form is for gathering information only, and your responses will be kept confidential.
