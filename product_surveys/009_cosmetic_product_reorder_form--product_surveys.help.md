# Cosmetic Product Reorder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers who have previously ordered from us and want to reorder their cosmetic products. It's used to collect information for a new order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your product name to let us know what you are reordering.
2. Enter your name as a customer.
3. Provide the date you'd like to receive your reordered product.
4. Specify the product category of the item you are reordering.
5. Choose how frequently you'd like to receive your product (e.g., daily, weekly, bi-weekly).
6. Select the status of your reorder request (e.g., pending, approved, rejected).
7. Add any additional notes about your reorder request.
8. Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
9. Provide your phone number for any further contact or follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product you are reordering.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as the customer.
* **Reorder Request Date**: Provide the date you'd like to receive your reordered product.
* **Product Category**: Enter the category of the product you are reordering (e.g., skincare, haircare, etc.).
* **Reorder Request Frequency**: Choose how frequently you'd like to receive your product (e.g., daily, weekly, bi-weekly).
* **Reorder Request Quantity**: This field is not used in the current implementation, but it's here for future reference.
* **Reorder Request Reason**: Explain why you are reordering the product.
* **Reorder Frequency Type**: Choose how you'd like to receive your product (e.g., daily, weekly, bi-weekly).
* **Reorder Status**: Select the status of your reorder request (e.g., pending, approved, rejected).
* **Notes**: Add any additional information about your reorder request.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number for any further contact or follow-up.
