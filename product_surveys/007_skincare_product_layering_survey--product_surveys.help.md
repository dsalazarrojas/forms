# Skincare Product Layering Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data about skincare routines, layering frequencies, and product amounts used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your skincare routine from the options provided in the "Skincare Routine 1" field.
2. Choose the products you use in your skincare routine from the options in the "Routine Products" field. Select all applicable products.
3. For each product, enter the amount used in the corresponding "Product Amount" field.
4. Select the layering frequency for each product from the options provided in the "Layering Frequency" field.
5. Choose the order in which you apply each product in the "Layering Order" field.
6. Repeat steps 2-5 for each product used in your skincare routine.
7. Finally, submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Skincare Routine 1**: Select one of the provided options to indicate your current skincare routine.
* **Routine Products**: Select all the products you use in your skincare routine. You can choose multiple products.
* **Product Amount**: Enter the amount of each product used. This field is only required for each product selected in the "Routine Products" field.
* **Layering Frequency**: Select the frequency at which you apply each product. This field is only required for each product selected.
* **Layering Order**: Select the order in which you apply each product. This field is only required for each product selected.
* **Product Amount 1**, **Product Amount 2**, **Product Amount 3**, **Product Amount 4**: Enter the amount of each product used in each step of your skincare routine.
* **Layering Frequency 1**, **Layering Frequency 2**, **Layering Frequency 3**, **Layering Frequency 4**: Select the frequency at which you apply each product in each step of your skincare routine.
* **Layering Order 1**, **Layering Order 2**, **Layering Order 3**, **Layering Order 4**: Select the order in which you apply each product in each step of your skincare routine.
* **Skincare Products Used 1**, **Skincare Products Used 2**, **Skincare Products Used 3**, **Skincare Products Used 4**: Select all the products used in each step of your skincare routine.
* **Submission**: A text field to provide any additional comments or explanations about your answers.
