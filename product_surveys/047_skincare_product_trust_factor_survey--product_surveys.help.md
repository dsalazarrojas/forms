# Skincare Product Trust Factor Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skincare Product Trust Factor Survey is a tool used to gauge the level of trust customers have in skincare products. This form is designed to gather information on various aspects of skincare products, including loyalty level, specific trust factors, and overall product trust levels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select all that apply from the list of skincare product trust factors.
2. Choose your answer for the product loyalty level and overall product trust level.
3. Fill in any additional information in the 'Trust factors' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Skincare product trust factors**: Choose all that apply from the list of options provided. This question is asking about various aspects of the skincare product, such as whether it has a positive or negative impact on skin health, if it is effective, or if it is a good value for money.
* **Product loyalty level**: This question asks about your level of loyalty towards the product. Please select one of the four options:
	+ Not sure
	+ Somewhat not sure
	+ Somewhat
	+ Very
* **Trust factors**: This field is for any additional comments or thoughts about why you do or do not trust the product. Please provide any relevant information in a concise and respectful manner.
* **Product trust level**: This question asks about your overall level of trust in the product. Please select one of the four options:
	+ Not sure
	+ Somewhat not sure
	+ Somewhat
	+ Very
* **Skincare product trust factors 2-5**: These questions are similar to the first 'skincare product trust factors' question, but with different aspects of the product. Please select all that apply from the list of options provided. 

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when completing this form.
- Select all that apply from the list of options provided.
- If you have any additional comments or concerns, please provide them in the 'Trust factors' field.
