# Apparel Shopping Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on your preferences and habits when shopping for apparel. Your responses will help us better understand your needs and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your age group from the list provided.
2. Choose your primary shopping channel (online, in-store, or equal mix of both).
3. Indicate how often you purchase new clothing.
4. Select all that apply for the factors that influence your purchase decisions.
5. Choose the style that best describes you.
6. If you have a preferred color palette, describe it in the text field.
7. Estimate your annual clothing budget.
8. Rate the importance of sales and discounts (1-5 scale).
9. Choose where you usually discover new fashion trends.
10. Indicate how important you consider sustainable manufacturing to be.
11. Provide any feedback on how brands can improve your shopping experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your age group?** (`age_group`, `select_one`, `required`): Select your age group from the options provided.
* **Where do you primarily shop for apparel?** (`primary_shopping_channel`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose your primary shopping channel from the options provided.
* **How often do you purchase new clothing?** (`shopping_frequency`, `select_one`, `required`): Indicate how often you purchase new clothing from the options provided.
* **What factors influence your purchase decisions?** (`purchase_drivers`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select all that apply for the factors that influence your purchase decisions.
* **Which style best describes you?** (`preferred_style`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the style that best describes you from the options provided.
* **Your favorite colors to wear** (`color_preferences`, `text`, `optional`): Describe your preferred color palette.
* **Estimated annual clothing budget** (`annual_budget`, `select_one`, `optional`): Estimate your annual clothing budget from the options provided.
* **Importance of sales and discounts** (`sale_importance`, `number`, `required`): Rate the importance of sales and discounts on a 1-5 scale.
* **Where do you discover new fashion trends?** (`fashion_discovery_source`, `select_one`, `optional`): Choose where you usually discover new fashion trends.
* **How important is sustainable manufacturing?** (`sustainability_priority`, `select_one`, `required`): Indicate how important you consider sustainable manufacturing to be.
* **What can brands do to improve your shopping experience?** (`feedback_to_brands`, `text`, `optional`): Provide any feedback on how brands can improve your shopping experience.
