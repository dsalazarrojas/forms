# Product Evaluation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Evaluation Checklist is a form designed to collect feedback and evaluation of a product or service from users. It is used by companies to gather insights from customers to improve their products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the required questions carefully and honestly.
2. Select only one option for select_one fields (e.g., Overall Satisfaction, Quality of Design, Ease of Use, Product Type, Purchase Price, Overall Rating, Recommendation, Would Recommend, Would Recommend to a Friend).
3. Select multiple options for select_multiple fields (e.g., Ease of Use, Product Evaluation Checklist Review, Features and Specifications).
4. Enter your actual phone number and email address in the respective fields (Contact Information and Product Evaluation Checklist Submission).
5. Enter the date and time you purchased the product (Purchase Date).
6. Enter the number of stars you would rate the product (Rating and Overall Rating).
7. Enter your comments in the text fields (Additional Comments and Comments).
8. Click "Submit" to submit your evaluation.

* **Overall Satisfaction** (`product_evaluation_checklist_1`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the product.

* **Price** (`product_evaluation_checklist_5`, number, required): Enter the actual price you paid for the product.
