# Online Platform User Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather user feedback on their evaluation of the online platform, which will help us improve the platform's usability, user experience, and overall quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please provide your User ID, which is a unique identifier for you.
2.  Enter your Platform Name, which is the name of the platform you are evaluating.
3.  Select the method by which you evaluated the platform (e.g., Platform, Mobile, Desktop, Web).
4.  Rate your satisfaction with the platform using the number scale provided.
5.  Rate your overall experience using the number scale provided.
6.  If you have any issues, please describe them in the text field.
7.  Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
8.  Enter your email address (optional).
9.  Enter your phone number (optional).
10. Enter your Platform ID, which is a unique identifier for the platform you are evaluating.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User ID** (`user_id`, number, required: false): Your unique identifier to help us track your responses.
* **Platform Name** (`platform_name`, text, required: false): The name of the platform you are evaluating.
* **Evaluation Method** (`evaluation_method`, select_one, required: false): The method by which you evaluated the platform (e.g., Platform, Mobile, Desktop, Web).
* **User Satisfaction** (`user_satisfaction`, number, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the platform using the number scale provided.
* **User Experience** (`user_experience`, number, required: false): Rate your overall experience with the platform using the number scale provided.
* **User Issues** (`user_issues`, text, required: false): Describe any issues you experienced while using the platform.
* **Feedback Comments** (`feedback_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address (optional).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Platform ID** (`platform_id`, number, required: false): A unique identifier for the platform you are evaluating.
