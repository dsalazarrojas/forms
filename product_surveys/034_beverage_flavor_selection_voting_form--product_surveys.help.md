# Beverage Flavor Selection Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on their preferences for different beverage flavors. It aims to gather information on their favorite flavors, flavor combinations, and other related preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your personal details: Enter your name and email address to be included in the survey results.
2. Select your age group: Choose from the available options to indicate your age range.
3. Choose a beverage category: Select the type of beverage you are voting for (e.g. soda, juice, tea, etc.).
4. Select your favorite flavor: Pick your top favorite flavor from the list.
5. Select second and third favorite flavors (optional): If you have a second or third favorite flavor, choose them from the available options.
6. Choose flavor combinations: Select multiple flavor combinations you enjoy.
7. Answer about trying new flavors: Indicate your willingness to try new or unique flavors.
8. Interest in seasonal flavors: Select your interest in buying limited edition or seasonal flavors.
9. Flavor intensity preference: Choose how strong you like your flavors.
10. Natural vs artificial preference: Indicate your preference for natural or artificial flavors.
11. Purchase frequency: Select how often you buy flavored beverages.
12. Brand preference: Choose whether you always choose brands based on flavor options.
13. Flavor suggestions: Provide any new flavor suggestions you might have.
14. Additional comments: Share any other thoughts or feedback about beverage flavors.

* **Your Name (1)**: Enter your name to be included in the survey results.
* **Email Address (2)**: Enter your email address to receive the survey results.
* **Age Range (3)**: Select your age group from the available options.
* **Beverage Category (4)**: Choose the type of beverage you are voting for.
* **Favorite Flavor (5)**: Select your top favorite flavor from the list.
* **Second Favorite Flavor (6)**: If you have a second favorite flavor, choose it from the list. (Optional)
* **Third Favorite Flavor (7)**: If you have a third favorite flavor, choose it from the list. (Optional)
* **Favorite Flavor Combinations (8)**: Select multiple flavor combinations you enjoy.
* **New Flavors (9)**: Indicate your willingness to try new or unique flavors.
* **Seasonal Flavors (10)**: Select your interest in buying limited edition or seasonal flavors.
* **Flavor Intensity Preference (11)**: Choose how strong you like your flavors.
* **Natural vs Artificial (12)**: Indicate your preference for natural or artificial flavors.
* **Purchase Frequency (13)**: Select how often you buy flavored beverages.
* **Brand Preference (14)**: Choose whether you always choose brands based on flavor options.
* **Flavor Suggestions (15)**: Provide any new flavor suggestions you might have.
* **Additional Comments (16)**: Share any other thoughts or feedback about beverage flavors.

Note: This form is for collecting feedback and suggestions. Please answer honestly and provide any additional comments or suggestions you might have.
