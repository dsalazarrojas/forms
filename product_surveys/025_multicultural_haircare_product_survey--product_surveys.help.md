# Multicultural Haircare Product Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Multicultural Haircare Product Survey is designed to gather information about your haircare habits, preferences, and experiences. This survey aims to understand your needs and preferences related to haircare products, including your hair type, texture, length, and condition, as well as your usage and purchase behavior of haircare products. The survey will help us to create better haircare products that cater to your specific needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the options that best describe your hair type, texture, length, thickness, and color from the respective dropdown menus.
2. Indicate the problems you are experiencing with your hair, such as hair loss, dandruff, or split ends.
3. Select the haircare products you use regularly, including shampoos, conditioners, and serums.
4. Choose the social media platforms you use to connect with us.
5. Confirm your submission by selecting the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hair Type** (1): Choose the type of hair you have, such as curly, straight, or wavy.
* **Hair Texture** (2): Select the texture of your hair, like smooth, rough, or coarse.
* **Hair Length** (3): Indicate the length of your hair, such as short, medium, or long.
* **Hair Thickness** (4): Choose the thickness of your hair, like thin, medium, or thick.
* **Hair Color** (5): Select the color of your hair, such as black, brown, blonde, or red.
* **Hair Density** (6): Choose the density of your hair, like fine, medium, or thick.
* **Hair Condition** (7): Indicate the condition of your hair, such as dry, oily, or normal.
* **Hair Problems** (8): Select the problems you experience with your hair, such as hair loss, dandruff, or split ends.
* **Hair Routine** (9): Choose your regular hair routine, such as regular washing, conditioning, or using a serum.
* **Haircare Products Used** (10): Select the haircare products you use, like shampoos, conditioners, or serums.
* **Salon Experience** (11): Indicate your experience with salons, such as having regular haircuts, or color treatments.
* **Survey Type** (12): Choose the type of survey you are taking, such as online or in-person.
* **Survey Purpose** (13): Select the purpose of the survey, such as product testing or market research.
* **Survey Frequency** (14): Indicate how often you take surveys, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Hair Products Used** (15): Select the hair products you use, such as shampoos, conditioners, or serums.
* **Hair Products Purchased** (16): Indicate the hair products you have purchased, such as shampoos, conditioners, or serums.
* **Recommend Products** (17): Choose the products you would recommend, such as shampoos, conditioners, or serums.

* **Submission Confirmation** (24): Confirm your submission by selecting the "Submit" button.

Note: Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to provide us with valuable information to create better haircare products for you.
