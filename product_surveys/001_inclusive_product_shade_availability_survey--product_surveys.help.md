# inclusive_product_shade_availability_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on the availability of shades for different products. It will help product managers and teams to plan and manage product inventory more effectively.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form pages and fields to understand what information is being collected.
2. Answer each field according to the product information you are providing.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (note): This page provides an introduction to the form and its purpose.
- **Product Info** (select multiple, optional): Select the available shade options for the product.
- **Shade** (select one, optional): Choose the specific shade of the product.
- **Shade Availability** (select multiple, optional): Select the shade availability for the product.
- **Shade Availability 0-4** (number, optional): Enter the quantity of shade 0-4 available for the product.
## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to the product documentation or contact the product manager for clarification.
