# Cabinet Color Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your cabinet color preferences. It will help us understand what types of colors you like and dislike, and what factors influence your decisions when selecting cabinet colors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address (optional) for follow-up questions or survey results.
2. Select your occupation or profession from the options listed.
3. Choose the type of project you're working on (e.g., new construction, kitchen remodel, etc.).
4. Pick your preferred color family (e.g., white and off-whites, gray and greige, etc.).
5. If you've chosen white or gray, select your preferred shade.
6. Choose your preferred wood tone (if applicable).
7. Select your preferred finish (e.g., matte, satin, semi-gloss, etc.).
8. Choose the finish of your hardware (e.g., brushed nickel, polished chrome, etc.).
9. Select your preferred cabinet style (e.g., shaker, flat panel, etc.).
10. Indicate your interest in two-tone cabinets.
11. Rate your confidence level in your color choice (e.g., very confident, somewhat confident, need more samples, etc.).
12. Select an approximate budget range for your cabinet project.
13. Choose when you plan to purchase or implement your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Enter your name for follow-up questions or survey results (optional).
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for follow-up questions or survey results (optional).
* **I Am A**: Select your occupation or profession from the options listed (e.g., homeowner, interior designer, etc.).
* **Project Type**: Choose the type of project you're working on (e.g., new construction, kitchen remodel, etc.).
* **Preferred Color Family**: Select your preferred color family from the options listed (e.g., white and off-whites, gray and greige, etc.).
* **Specific White Preference**: If you've chosen white, select your preferred shade (e.g., pure white, warm white, cool white, etc.).
* **Specific Gray Preference**: If you've chosen gray, select your preferred shade (e.g., light gray, medium gray, dark charcoal, etc.).
* **Wood Tone Preference**: If you've chosen wood, select your preferred tone (e.g., light natural, honey oak, etc.).
* **Finish Preference**: Select your preferred finish (e.g., matte, satin, semi-gloss, etc.).
* **Hardware Finish**: Select the finish of your hardware (e.g., brushed nickel, polished chrome, etc.).
* **Cabinet Style**: Select your preferred cabinet style (e.g., shaker, flat panel, etc.).
* **Two-Tone Cabinet Interest**: Indicate your interest in two-tone cabinets (e.g., yes, maybe, no, etc.).
* **Color Decision Confidence**: Rate your confidence level in your color choice (e.g., very confident, somewhat confident, need more samples, etc.).
* **Budget Range for Cabinets**: Select an approximate budget range for your cabinet project.
* **Project Timeline**: Choose when you plan to purchase or implement your project (e.g., immediately, within a month, etc.).
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or questions about your color preferences.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when selecting your preferences.
* If you're unsure about a particular option, choose the one that best represents your feelings.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, use the "Additional Comments" field to share them.
* Thank you for participating in this survey! Your input will help us better understand your cabinet color preferences.
