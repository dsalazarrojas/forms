# Product Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and opinions from users about the quality of our products. This survey aims to understand how well our products meet your expectations and how we can improve them in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Read the survey carefully and answer each question truthfully.
2. Select the product you are evaluating from the list.
3. Choose the characteristics that best describe the product's quality.
4. Rate your overall experience and satisfaction with the product.
5. Decide if you would recommend the product to others.
6. Add any additional comments about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **product_name**: Select the name of the product you are evaluating from the list.
* **quality_characteristics**: Choose the characteristics that best describe the product's quality. This can include factors such as its price, performance, features, and aesthetics.
* **experience**: Rate your overall experience with the product on a numerical scale. This will help us understand how well the product met your expectations.
* **satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with the product on a numerical scale. This will help us understand how satisfied you are with the product.
* **recommendation**: Decide if you would recommend the product to others. This will help us understand how likely you are to suggest the product to friends and family.
* **comments**: Share any additional comments or thoughts about your experience with the product. This is an opportunity to provide more detailed feedback.
