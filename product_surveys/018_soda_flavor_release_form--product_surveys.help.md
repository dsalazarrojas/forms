# Soda Flavor Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect user feedback on new flavor submissions for the product development team. Please fill it out carefully to provide accurate and helpful information to the team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "User Input" page with your name, and any other relevant information.
2. Select the correct flavor submission on the "Flavor Input" page.
3. Provide a detailed description of your flavor submission on the "Description" page.
4. Enter your email address and phone number on the "Email" and "Phone" pages.
5. Choose the correct date and time of entry on the "Date of Entry" and "Time of Entry" pages.
6. Add any additional notes on the "Notes" page.
7. On the "Submitter Permission" page, select if you have submitted a flavor previously or not.
8. Enter your name, email address, and phone number on the "Submitter Name", "Submitter Email", and "Submitter Phone" pages.
9. Select the correct time of submission and submit your notes on the "Submitter Notes" page.
10. Choose whether you agree to share your submission with the product team on the "Submitter Permission Agreement" page.
11. On the "Product Development Team" and other "Team Member" pages, select who will be involved in the project.
12. Review your submission and submit the form.

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, required/optional): Enter your name for identification purposes.
