# Innovative Product Design Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings for innovative product designs. It is intended to gauge users' opinions on various aspects of the product, including its design, functionality, and overall appeal. The form is used to gather data for product development and improvement purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the best option for each design concept (First Concept, Second Concept, Third Concept, Fourth Concept).
2. Enter the product name, description, and designer name.
3. Provide a URL for the product image.
4. Enter your email address and phone number.
5. Select multiple URLs for the product image (if applicable).
6. Rate the product from 1 to 5.
7. Choose a category for the product.
8. Select multiple tags for the product.
9. Enter the date and time of submission.
10. Enter any additional comments or notes about the product.
11. Rate each design concept from 1 to 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Concept** (Select One, required): Choose the best option for this design concept.
* **Second Concept** (Select One, required): Choose the best option for this design concept.
* **Third Concept** (Select One, required): Choose the best option for this design concept.
* **Fourth Concept** (Select One, required): Choose the best option for this design concept.
* **Product Name** (Text, required): Enter the name of the product.
* **Description** (Text, required): Enter a brief description of the product.
* **Designer** (Text, required): Enter the name of the product designer.
* **Submit** (Date, optional): Enter the date and time of submission (if applicable).
* **Email** (Email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (Text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Product Image URL** (Select Multiple, required): Select multiple URLs for the product image (if applicable).
* **Rating** (Number, required): Rate the product from 1 to 5.
* **Price** (Number, required): Enter the price of the product.
* **Category** (Select One, required): Choose a category for the product.
* **Tag** (Select Multiple, required): Select multiple tags for the product.
* **Date** (Date, required): Enter the date of submission.
* **Time** (Time, required): Enter the time of submission.
* **Note** (Note, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the product.
* **Comment** (Text, required): Enter any additional comments about the product.
* **Rating 1** (Number, required): Rate the first design concept from 1 to 5.
* **Rating 2** (Number, required): Rate the second design concept from 1 to 5.
* **Rating 3** (Number, required): Rate the third design concept from 1 to 5.
* **Rating 4** (Number, required): Rate the fourth design concept from 1 to 5.
* **Rating 5** (Number, required): Rate the fifth design concept from 1 to 5.
* **Form Completion** (Select Multiple, required): Choose whether the form is completed, not completed, or partially completed.
