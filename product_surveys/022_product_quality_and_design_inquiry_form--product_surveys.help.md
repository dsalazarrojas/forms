# Product Quality And Design Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and inquiries about product quality and design from customers. Please fill out this form to provide your input and help us improve our products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the product quality and design rating for the product you are inquiring about.
2. Enter the date of your overall experience with the product.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback about the product.
4. Enter the product name and image URL (if applicable).
5. Select the product category.
6. Enter any product tag or description.
7. Enter the product price.
8. Describe any support request you have for the product.
9. Enter any additional product feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Quality** (`product_quality_and_design_inquiry_form_2`): Rate the product's quality as Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Product Design** (`product_quality_and_design_inquiry_form_3`): Rate the product's design as Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Overall Experience** (`product_quality_and_design_inquiry_form_4`): Enter the date of your overall experience with the product (e.g., "YYYY-MM-DD").
* **Comments** (`product_quality_and_design_inquiry_form_5`): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the product.
* **Product Name** (`product_quality_and_design_inquiry_form_6`): Enter the name of the product.
* **Product Image** (`product_quality_and_design_inquiry_form_7`): Enter the URL of the product image (if applicable).
* **Product Category** (`product_quality_and_design_inquiry_form_9`): Select the category of the product.
* **Product Price** (`product_quality_and_design_inquiry_form_11`): Enter the price of the product.
* **Support Request** (`product_quality_and_design_inquiry_form_12`): Describe any support requests you have for the product.
* **Product Feedback** (`product_quality_and_design_inquiry_form_13`): Enter any additional feedback about the product.
* **Submit** (`product_quality_and_design_inquiry_form_14`): Select "Submit" to send your inquiry, or "Do Not Submit" to cancel.
