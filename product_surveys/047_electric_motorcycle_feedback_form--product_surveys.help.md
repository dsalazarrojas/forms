# Electric Motorcycle Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from electric motorcycle users about their experience, favorite and least favorite features, and overall satisfaction with their vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field.
3. In the "Feedback" field, write your thoughts and comments about your experience with your electric motorcycle.
4. In the "Motorcycle Model" field, select the model of your electric motorcycle.
5. If you ride your motorcycle frequently, answer the "Ride Frequency" question, otherwise, you can skip this field.
6. In the "Favorite Feature" field, select the feature you enjoy most about your electric motorcycle.
7. In the "Least Favorite Feature" field, select the feature you enjoy least about your electric motorcycle.
8. In the "Overall Experience" field, rate your overall experience with your electric motorcycle, using the provided time options.
9. If you are ready, click "Submit" to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Your first name.
- **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Your last name.
- **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter a valid email address to receive responses to your feedback.
- **Feedback** (Feedback, text, required): Share your thoughts and comments about your experience with your electric motorcycle.
- **Motorcycle Model** (Motorcycle Model, text, required): Select the model of your electric motorcycle.
- **Ride Frequency** (Ride Frequency, text, optional): If you ride frequently, answer how often you ride.
- **Favorite Feature** (Favorite Feature, select_one, optional): Select the feature you enjoy most about your electric motorcycle.
- **Least Favorite Feature** (Least Favorite Feature, select_one, optional): Select the feature you enjoy least about your electric motorcycle.
- **Overall Experience** (Overall Experience, time, required): Rate your overall experience with your electric motorcycle.
- **Submitted** (Submitted, date, optional): Not required for submission, this field is not visible in the form output.
 
## Tips
- Make sure to enter a valid email address to receive feedback responses.
- Be honest about your experience and features.
- Select the correct motorcycle model.
- You can skip the "Ride Frequency" field if you don't ride frequently.
- If you answer the "Ride Frequency" field, please provide a specific answer, e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, etc.
- If you choose the "Overall Experience" field, select the correct time option, e.g., excellent, good, fair, poor.
