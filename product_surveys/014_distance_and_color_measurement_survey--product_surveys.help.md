# Distance And Color Measurement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your habits of measuring distance and color.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. Enter numerical answers (e.g., distance accuracy) in the appropriate units (e.g., centimeters, meters, kilometers).
4. Answer all questions, even if you have not measured distance or color recently.
