# Shampoo Size Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from customers about their shampoo size preferences and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select your current shampoo bottle size from the options provided.
2.  Choose the size of shampoo you currently use.
3.  Indicate your preferred shampoo size.
4.  If you don't see your current product listed, select "Other".
5.  Provide any additional thoughts or comments about your experience with the product.
6.  Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
7.  Optionally, enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted.
8.  Click "Submit" to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your current shampoo bottle size?** (`shampoo_size_id`, select_one, required/optional): This field asks you to select your current shampoo bottle size.
* **What size do you use?** (`what_do_you_use`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose from the options the size of shampoo you currently use.
* **Which size do you prefer?** (`size_preferences`, select_one, required/optional): Select the size of shampoo you prefer.
* **What is your current product?** (`current_product`, select_one, required/optional): If you've used a different product, select "Other" and enter the name of the product.
* **What are your thoughts?** (`feedback`, note, required/optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments about your experience with the product.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): If you'd like to be contacted, enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): If you'd like to be contacted, enter your phone number.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): Click to submit your feedback.
