# non_toxic_product_swap_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user feedback and data about non-toxic products to help us tailor our products and services to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your non-toxic product name and any other relevant information about the product.
2. Select your category, which will help us understand where your product fits into our product offerings.
3. Indicate how long you've used the product, which will help us understand your experience with our product.
4. Tell us why you are swapping this product if applicable.
5. Let us know if you meet our needs and what's most important for you about our products.
6. Rate your satisfaction with our product, and let us know the price of the non-toxic product if you've purchased it from us.
7. Please enter the URL of any product image you have.
8. Provide any other comments or feedback about the product.
9. Enter your email address and phone number for us to contact you if needed.
10. Let us know when you submitted this form.
11. Provide any other notes about your experience with our product.
12. Choose how well we meet your needs, which will help us improve our products.
13. Lastly, choose how satisfied you are with our products. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* Non Toxic Product Name (1): This is the name of the non-toxic product you are providing feedback on. 
* Category (2): Select the category that best fits your non-toxic product.
* How long have you used (3): Select the time frame in which you've used the product.
* How long planning to swap (4): If you are swapping the product, select the time frame in which you plan to do so.
* Why swap (5): If you are swapping, tell us why you are swapping the product.
* How well do we meet your needs (6): Rate how well we meet your needs with our product.
* What is most important (7): Identify what's most important for you about our product.
* Product Rating (8): Rate your satisfaction with our product.
* Non Toxic Product Price (9): If you purchased the product from us, enter the price you paid.
* Non Toxic Product Link (10): If applicable, enter the URL for the product.
* Non Toxic Product Image (11): If applicable, enter the URL for the product image.
* User Comments (12): Provide any comments or feedback about the product.
* Email (13): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* Phone Number (14): Enter your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
* Date Submitted (15): Let us know when you submitted this form.
* Time Submitted (16): Enter the time you submitted this form.
* Notes (17): Provide any other notes about your experience with our product.
* Form Submitted (18): Let us know when you submitted this form.
* Form Submission (19): This is a note field for any other information.
* User Surveied (20): This is a text field for you to provide any additional information.
* Product Type (21): If applicable, select the type of product.
* Product Description (22): If applicable, enter a description of the product.
* Product Image URL (23): If applicable, enter the URL for the product image.
* Non Toxic Product Rating 2 (24): Rate your satisfaction with our product on a scale of 1-5.
* Non Toxic Product Rating 3 (25): Rate your satisfaction with our product on a scale of 1-5.
