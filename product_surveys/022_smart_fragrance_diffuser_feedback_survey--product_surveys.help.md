# Smart Fragrance Diffuser Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Fragrance Diffuser Feedback Survey is a tool designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with the Smart Fragrance Diffuser product. This form will help us understand your opinions and suggestions, enabling us to improve the product and provide better service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the spaces provided.
2. Select the correct option for each question from the provided list.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please type them in the designated fields.
4. Once you have completed all the fields, click submit to send us your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Which type of product is this?
* **Which type of product is this?** (`product_type`, select_one): This field is to help us understand which type of product you are providing feedback on.
	+ Select the type of product from the provided options: AirPurifier, Fan, or Light.
### 2. How often do you use this product?
* **How often do you use this product?** (`usage_frequency`, select_one): This field helps us understand how frequently you use the product.
	+ Select the frequency at which you use the product: Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
### 3. What is your satisfaction with this product?
* **What is your satisfaction with this product?** (`satisfaction`, select_one): This field is to gauge your level of satisfaction with the product.
	+ Select the level of satisfaction that best describes your experience: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, or Not Satisfied.
### 4. What is the name of the product you own?
* **What is the name of the product you own?** (`product_name`, text): Please enter the name of the product you are providing feedback on.
### 5. What is your name?
* **What is your name?** (`contact_name`, text): Please enter your name so we can contact you if needed.
### 6. Email
* **Email** (`email`, email): If you have any questions or need to be contacted, please enter your email address.
### 7. Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): If you prefer to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number.
### 8. What features or improvements would you suggest?
* **What features or improvements would you suggest?** (`improvement_ideas`, text): This field is for any suggestions or ideas you may have to improve the product.
### 9. Do you have any comments or suggestions?
* **Do you have any comments or suggestions?** (`comments`, text): Please share any additional comments or suggestions you have.
### 10. Is there something we can contact you about?
* **Is there something we can contact you about?** (`contact_request`, select_one): If there is something specific you'd like to be contacted about, please select this option.
### 11. Product ID?
* **Product ID?** (`product_id`, text): If you know the product ID, please enter it.
