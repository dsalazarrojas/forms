# techGadgetUsabilitySurvey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **techGadgetUsabilitySurvey** form is used to gather feedback and usability insights from product team members on the usability of our tech gadgets.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions honestly and accurately based on your experience with our tech gadgets.
2. Select the relevant options for each question.
3. Provide detailed feedback in the text fields.
4. Fill out all required fields (indicated by *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **productTeam** (Select Multiple, productTeam, required: false): Choose all the teams that you work with our tech gadgets.
- **deviceType** (Select One, devicetype, required: false): Select the type of tech gadget you use (e.g., phone, laptop, etc.).
- **userFeedback** (Text, userfeedback, required: false): Provide any feedback or comments on your experience with our tech gadgets. Try to be as specific as possible.
- **satisfaction** (Number, satisfaction, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with our tech gadgets on a scale of 0-10.
- **improvementOpportunities** (Note, improvementopportunities, required: false): List any areas where you think we can improve our tech gadgets.
- **improvementDescription** (Text, improvementDescription, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the improvements you mentioned in the previous field.
- **rating** (Number, rating, required: false): Rate our tech gadgets on a scale of 1-5.
- **contactInformation** (Email, contactInformation, required: false): If you have any further comments or questions, please provide your email address so we can get in touch with you.

## Tips

* Answering this form honestly will help us better understand the usability of our tech gadgets and make informed decisions for future product development.
* Please provide clear and concise answers to the questions.
* You can attach any relevant files or images to the text fields if needed.
* If you are having trouble completing any of the questions, please don't hesitate to ask for help.
