# platform_user_experience_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather user feedback and experience with our platform. It aims to understand how users interact with the platform, identify areas for improvement, and gather suggestions for future development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide an honest account of your experience with our platform.
2. Answer each question carefully and thoroughly.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **1. What is your experience with our platform?** (Introduction)
* A brief note field where you can describe your experience with the platform.
* Required: No

### **2. How satisfied are you with the platform?** (User Satisfaction)
* A scale with three options:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Not Satisfied

### **3. What features would you like to see added or improved?** (Product Improvement)
* A text field where you can suggest new features or improvements.

### **4. Describe any issues you've encountered with the platform** (Issue Description)
* A text field to provide detailed description of any issues you've experienced.

### **5. Do you have any feedback or suggestions for the platform?** (Feedback)
* A text field for general feedback or suggestions.

### **6. What is your email address and phone number?** (Contact Info)
* This field is for us to contact you back, please provide a valid email address and phone number.

### **7. Do you prefer to communicate through email, in-app, phone, or other?** (Preferred Channel)
* A select one field with four options:
	+ Email
	+ In-App
	+ Phone
	+ Other

### **8. Do you have any other comments or details to share?** (Other Info)
* A text field for any additional comments or details.

### **9. Is there anything else you'd like to add or comment on?** (Additional Comments)
* A text field for any final thoughts or comments.

### **10. What are your final thoughts on the platform?** (Conclusion)
* A note field for final thoughts or comments.

### **11. Have you read and understood the terms and conditions?** (Terms and Conditions)
* A select one field with two options:
	+ True
	+ False

### **12. What date did you complete this survey?** (Date Completed)
* A date field to record the date you completed the survey.

### **13. What time did you complete this survey?** (Time Completed)
* A time field to record the time you completed the survey.

### **14. How would you rate the platform overall?** (Rating)
* A number field with a scale.

### **15. Describe your overall experience with the platform?** (Platform Experience)
* A text field for a detailed description of your overall experience.

### **16. What is your preferred method of contact (email, in-app, phone, etc.)?** (Platform Contact)
* A select multiple field with four options:
	+ Email
	+ In-App
	+ Phone
	+ Other

### **17. Do you have any additional comments or thoughts?** (Additional Comments)
* A text field for any final thoughts or comments.

### **18. Are there any final comments or thoughts you'd like to add?** (Final Comments)
* A text field for any final comments or thoughts.
