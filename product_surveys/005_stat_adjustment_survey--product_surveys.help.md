# Stat Adjustment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stat Adjustment Survey is used to collect feedback from users on the effectiveness and usefulness of adjustments made to their player data. This form helps us identify areas where we can improve our services and make adjustments that better meet the needs of our users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Select all options that apply to your feedback
2. Rate your overall satisfaction with the adjustments made to your data
3. Provide additional comments or suggestions
4. Answer questions about your experience and satisfaction with the adjustments

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Feedback** (`stat_adjustment_survey_page`, select_multiple, required: false): This field allows you to select all options that apply to your feedback. You can choose one or more of the options to express your thoughts on the adjustments made to your data.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`rating`, number, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the adjustments made to your data. Please rate between 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **What adjustment do you suggest?** (`adjustment_suggestion`, text, required: false): Provide a specific suggestion or idea for improvement.
* **Why do you suggest this adjustment?** (`adjustment_reason`, text, required: false): Explain the reasoning behind your suggestion.
* **Is this adjustment important?** (`stat_adjustment_important`, select_one, required: false): If you think the adjustment is important, select "True". Otherwise, select "False".
* **Why is this adjustment important?** (`stat_adjustment_important_reason`, text, required: false): Explain why you think the adjustment is important.
* **Additional comments** (`stat_adjustment_comment`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or thoughts you have about the adjustments.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required: false): Select the date when you submitted your feedback.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required: false): Select the time when you submitted your feedback.
* **Overall Rating** (`user_rating`, number, required: false): Rate your overall experience with a number between 1-5.
* **Why did you rate your overall experience this way?** (`user_rating_reason`, text, required: false): Explain the reasoning behind your overall rating.
* **How satisfied are you with our stat adjustments?** (`stat_adjustment_user_satisfaction`, number, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with our stat adjustments on a scale of 1-5.
* **Why did you rate your satisfaction this way?** (`stat_adjustment_user_satisfaction_reason`, text, required: false): Explain the reasoning behind your satisfaction rating.
* **Additional comments** (`submission_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or thoughts you have about your experience.
* **Email** (`user_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`user_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Name** (`user_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Team Feedback** (`team_feedback`, text, required: false): Provide any feedback or comments about the team.
* **Satisfaction Score** (`stat_adjustment_satisfaction_score`, number, required: false): Rate your satisfaction score on a scale of 1-5.
* **Why is this adjustment satisfying for you?** (`stat_adjustment_satisfaction_description`, text, required: false): Explain why the adjustment is satisfying for you.
* **Stat adjustment improvement idea** (`stat_adjustment_improvement_idea`, text, required: false): Provide an idea for improvement.
* **Stat adjustment improvement plan** (`stat_adjustment_improvement_plan`, text, required: false): Describe a plan for improvement.
* **Improvement status** (`stat_adjustment_improvement_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of the improvement.
* **Description** (`stat_adjustment_improvement_description`, text, required: false): Describe the improvement.
