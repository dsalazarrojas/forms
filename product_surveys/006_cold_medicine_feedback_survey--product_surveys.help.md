# Cold Medicine Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with a specific cold medicine product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Full Name** (optional) in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your **Email Address** (optional) in the "Email Address" field, which will be used for survey completion confirmation.
3. Select your **Age Group** from the drop-down menu in the "Age Group" field.
4. Select your **Gender** from the drop-down menu in the "Gender" field (optional).
5. Enter the **Cold Medicine Product Name** in the "Cold Medicine Product Name" field.
6. Select the **Type of Cold Medicine** from the drop-down menu in the "Type of Cold Medicine" field.
7. Select the location where you **Purchased** the product from the drop-down menu in the "Where Did You Purchase" field.
8. Enter the **Date or Timeframe** when you used the product in the "When Did You Use This Product" field.
9. Select all the **Symptoms You Were Treating** from the drop-down menu in the "Symptoms You Were Treating" field.
10. Select whether you **Followed Recommended Dosage** from the drop-down menu in the "Did You Follow Recommended Dosage" field.
11. Select your **Overall Effectiveness Rating** from the drop-down menu in the "Overall Effectiveness Rating" field.
12. Select all the **Symptoms That Improved** from the drop-down menu in the "Which Symptoms Were Relieved" field.
13. Select how **Quickly Did It Start Working** from the drop-down menu in the "How Quickly Did It Start Working" field.
14. Select how **Long Did Relief Last** from the drop-down menu in the "How Long Did Relief Last" field.
15. Select whether you **Experienced Side Effects** from the drop-down menu in the "Did You Experience Side Effects" field.
16. Select all the **Side Effects Experienced** from the drop-down menu in the "Side Effects Experienced" field (optional).
17. Select the **Severity of Side Effects** from the drop-down menu in the "Side Effects Severity" field (optional).
18. Select your **Taste Rating** from the drop-down menu in the "Taste Rating" field (if applicable).
19. Select how **Easy Was It to Use** from the drop-down menu in the "Ease of Use" field.
20. Select the **Value for Money** from the drop-down menu in the "Value for Money" field.
21. Select whether you **Would Recommend This Product** to friends or family from the drop-down menu in the "Would You Recommend This Product" field.
22. Select whether you **Would Purchase Again** from the drop-down menu in the "Would You Purchase Again" field.
23. Select how **Does This Compare to Other Cold Medicines** from the drop-down menu in the "How Does This Compare to Other Cold Medicines" field.
24. Enter any **Additional Comments or Feedback** in the "Additional Comments or Feedback" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your full name for follow-up purposes.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for survey completion confirmation.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, `select_one`, required): Select your age range.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, optional): Select your gender.
* **Cold Medicine Product Name** (`medicine_name`, `text`, required): Enter the brand and product name of the cold medicine you used.
* **Type of Cold Medicine** (`medicine_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of cold medicine.
* **Where Did You Purchase** (`purchase_location`, `select_one`, required): Select the location where you purchased the product.
* **When Did You Use This Product** (`usage_date`, `text`, required): Enter the date or timeframe when you used the product.
* **Symptoms You Were Treating** (`symptoms_treated`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the symptoms you were treating.
* **Did You Follow Recommended Dosage** (`dosage_followed`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you followed the recommended dosage.
* **Overall Effectiveness Rating** (`effectiveness_rating`, `select_one`, required): Select your overall effectiveness rating.
* **Which Symptoms Were Relieved** (`symptom_relief`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the symptoms that improved.
* **How Quickly Did It Start Working** (`onset_time`, `select_one`, required): Select how quickly the product started working.
* **How Long Did Relief Last** (`duration_of_relief`, `select_one`, required): Select how long the relief lasted.
* **Did You Experience Side Effects** (`side_effects_experienced`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you experienced side effects.
* **Side Effects Experienced** (`side_effects_list`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the side effects you experienced (optional).
* **Severity of Side Effects** (`side_effects_severity`, `select_one`, optional): Select the severity of side effects (optional).
* **Taste Rating** (`taste_rating`, `select_one`, required): Select your taste rating (if applicable).
* **Ease of Use** (`ease_of_use`, `select_one`, required): Select how easy it was to use the product.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you felt the product was worth the price.
* **Would You Recommend This Product** (`would_recommend`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you would recommend the product to friends or family.
* **Would You Purchase Again** (`would_purchase_again`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you would purchase the product again.
* **How Does This Compare to Other Cold Medicines** (`comparison_to_other_products`, `select_one`, required): Select how the product compares to other cold medicines.
* **Additional Comments or Feedback** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or doubts about a particular question, please do not hesitate to ask.
* Your feedback is valuable to us and will help us improve the product.
