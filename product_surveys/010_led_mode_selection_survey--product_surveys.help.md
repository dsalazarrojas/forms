# LED Mode Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect your input and preferences for the LED mode selection. Please answer the questions honestly to help us understand your needs better.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the answers for each question based on your genuine response.
2. For "Home Owner", choose "Yes" if you own the home, "No" otherwise.
3. For "Facilities", select all the facilities you are interested in (e.g., smart lighting, energy efficiency, etc.).
4. For "Smart Lighting Users", select all users who will be using smart lighting in your home.
5. For "LED Usage", describe how you currently use LED lighting in your home or office.
6. For "Lighting Satisfaction", rate your satisfaction with the current lighting system on a scale of 1-10.
7. For "Comments", provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Owner** (Yes, No): Please select "Yes" if you are the owner of the home, "No" otherwise.
* **Facilities** (Select all): This option allows you to select multiple facilities that are relevant to your needs, such as smart lighting, energy efficiency, or others. Please choose "Yes" for each facility that applies to you.
* **Smart Lighting Users** (Select all): This option allows you to select all users who will be using smart lighting in your home, such as yourself, family members, or guests.
* **LED Usage** (Text): Describe how you currently use LED lighting in your home or office. For example, you can describe your usage habits, frequency, or any specific benefits you've noticed.
* **Lighting Satisfaction** (Number): Rate your satisfaction with the current lighting system on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 10 is very satisfied.
* **Comments** (Text): Please provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the current lighting system or your experience with it.
