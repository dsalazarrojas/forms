# lash_growth_product_effectiveness_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about the effectiveness of the Lash Growth product line.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your results and satisfaction level with the product.
2. Choose the product you used from the provided options.
3. Describe how you applied the product.
4. Report any side effects you experienced while using the product.
5. Indicate how often you used the product.
6. Specify whether you recommend the product to others.
7. Optionally, describe any side effects you experienced or how long you've used the product.
8. Enter your contact information to facilitate further communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **results_and_satisfaction** (Satisfied/Dissatisfied): Describe your overall results and satisfaction level with the product.
* **side_effects** (text): Report any side effects you experienced while using the product.
* **satisfaction** (number): This field is not currently used in the form. Please provide your overall satisfaction level with the product.
* **product_use** (select one): Choose the type of Lash Growth product you used.
* **application_instructions** (text): Describe how you applied the product.
* **results_improvement** (select one): Indicate how much improvement you experienced while using the product.
* **satisfaction_improvement** (select one): Indicate how much improvement you experienced while using the product.
* **side_effects_frequency** (select multiple): Report how often you experienced any side effects.
* **side_effects_severity** (select multiple): Report the severity of any side effects you experienced.
* **other_side_effects** (text): Report any other side effects you experienced.
* **how_long_used_product** (select one): Specify how long you've used the product.
* **how_long_used_product_other** (text): Optionally, describe how long you've used the product (if it's not listed).
* **how_often_used_product** (select one): Specify how often you used the product.
* **how_often_used_product_other** (text): Optionally, describe how often you used the product (if it's not listed).
* **recommend_product** (select one): Indicate whether you recommend the product to others.
* **recommend_to_friends** (select one): Indicate whether you recommend the product to friends and family.
* **comments** (text): Leave any additional comments about the product.
* **product_recommendation** (text): Optionally, provide additional product recommendations.
* **product_liked_features** (text): Describe any liked features of the product.
* **product_disliked_features** (text): Describe any disliked features of the product.
* **rating** (number): Provide a rating for the product (not currently required).
* **open_ended_comment** (text): Leave any additional open-ended comments about the product.
* **email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
