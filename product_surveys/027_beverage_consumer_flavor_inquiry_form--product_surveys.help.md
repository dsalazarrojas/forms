# Beverage Consumer Flavor Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather information about your preferences and behavior as a consumer of different types of beverages. Your input will help us better understand your tastes and preferences to develop more appealing products.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name, email address (for follow-up purposes), and age range.
2. Select your gender and choose your preferred beverage category.
3. Indicate your preferred flavors, sweet level, and carbonation preference.
4. Let us know how often you buy flavored beverages and where you usually purchase them.
5. Choose your typical price range and level of brand loyalty.
6. Rate the importance of health factors in your purchasing decisions.
7. Select the factors that influence your purchase decisions.
8. Indicate your willingness to try new flavors and suggest new flavors you'd like to see.
9. Provide any additional comments or feedback you might have.
