# retail_home_improvement_preferences_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their shopping habits, satisfaction levels, and preferences regarding home improvement products. This data will help us understand our customers' needs and preferences, enabling us to provide better services and products.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all questions honestly and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For fields with options, select the most relevant choice.
3. If you are unsure about a question, please provide a brief explanation in the field marked "Additional Comments" (not shown in this version).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shopping Habits** (`shopping_habits`, text, optional): Briefly describe your shopping habits, including how often you shop, what products you typically purchase, and any other relevant information.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your level of satisfaction with our home improvement products and services. Select all that apply:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Product Interest** (`product_interest`, select_one, optional): Indicate your level of interest in our home improvement products:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Demographic Information** (`demographic_info`, text, optional): Provide your demographic information, including your age, occupation, income level, etc.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your contact information, including your name, email, phone number, etc.
* **Home Improvement** (`home_improvement`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your interest in home improvement products:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Satisfaction Scale** (`satisfaction_scale`, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction level on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not satisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
* **Store Visits** (`store_visits`, number, optional): Indicate how often you visit our stores:
* **Purchase Frequency** (`purchase_frequency`, select_one, optional): Indicate how often you purchase home improvement products:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Purchase Amount** (`purchase_amount`, number, optional): Indicate the average amount you spend on home improvement products per month.
* **Purchase Frequency Scale** (`purchase_frequency_scale`, select_one, optional): Rate how often you purchase home improvement products on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is never and 5 is frequently.
* **Satisfaction Frequency** (`satisfaction_frequency`, select_one, optional): Indicate how often you are satisfied with our home improvement products:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Product Relevance** (`product_relevance`, number, optional): Rate the relevance of our products to your needs on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not relevant and 5 is very relevant.
* **Product Importance** (`product_importance`, number, optional): Rate the importance of our products to you on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not important and 5 is very important.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional): Indicate your preferred contact method:
	+ Yes
	+ No
