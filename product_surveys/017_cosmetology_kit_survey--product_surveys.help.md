# cosmetology_kit_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cosmetology_kit_survey is designed to collect feedback and data about products used by cosmetologists in their daily practice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Make sure to proofread your answers for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, optional): Please provide a brief introduction to your answer, including your name, location, and any other relevant information.
- **Product Used** (`product_used`, text, required): Enter the name of the product you are using for this survey.
- **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the official name of the product you are using.
- **Product Price** (`product_price`, number, required): Enter the price of the product you are using.
- **Product Condition** (`product_condition`, select_one, required): Choose the condition of the product you are using (new, used, or refurbished).
- **Product Recommendation** (`product_recommendation`, text, optional): Provide any recommendations or comments you have about the product.
- **User Experience** (`user_experience`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your level of experience with the product (beginner, intermediate, or advanced).
- **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred method of contact (email, phone, or mail).
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person to contact for more information.
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about the product.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact and follow-up information.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact and follow-up information.
- **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your physical address for product delivery or other purposes.
- **Website** (`website`, text, optional): Enter your website or social media handle for more information.
- **Social Media** (`social_media`, text, optional): Enter your social media handle for more information.
- **Product Image** (`product_image`, select_multiple, optional): Choose any images you would like to attach to this survey.
- **Product Video** (`product_video`, select_multiple, optional): Choose any videos you would like to attach to this survey.
- **Survey Response** (`survey_response`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your response to this survey (satisfied, not satisfied, or neutral).
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about the product.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date you are completing this survey.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time you are completing this survey.
- **Timezone** (`timezone`, text, optional): Enter your timezone for time zone information.
- **Language** (`language`, text, optional): Enter the language you are most comfortable with.
- **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter your physical location for product distribution or other purposes.
