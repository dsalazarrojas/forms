# Gym Equipment Usability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback and opinions from users on the usability of gym equipment. The collected data will help the gym administration to make informed decisions to improve the overall user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form thoroughly and accurately to provide valuable insights to the gym administration.
2. Start with the first page, which is labeled "first_page". You can find this page at the beginning of the form.
3. Answer the questions as they appear, and make sure to complete all required fields.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "additional_comments" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Page** (1, text, false):
	+ This is the introductory page of the survey, and it serves as a starting point for your feedback.
* **Equipment Availability** (2, select_one, false):
	+ Please select how often you find the gym equipment available.
		- Always available: You can always find the equipment you need when you want it.
		- Most of the time: You usually find the equipment available, but occasionally it's not.
		- Rarely available: The equipment is often not available when you need it.
		- Never: You never find the equipment available.
* **Usability** (3, select_multiple, false):
	+ Select the equipment that you find easiest or most difficult to use.
		- Very easy to use: You find it very easy to use and operate.
		- Somewhat easy to use: You find it easy to use, but with some effort.
		- Neutral: You don't have strong feelings about its usability.
		- Somewhat difficult to use: You find it challenging to use, but manageable.
		- Very difficult to use: You find it very difficult to use and require assistance.
* **Facility Improvements** (4, note, false):
	+ If you have any suggestions or comments about the equipment, please list them here.
* **Date** (5, date, false):
	+ This field is not currently used in the form, but it's included in case you have a specific date you'd like to associate with your feedback.
* **Time** (6, time, false):
	+ This field is also not currently used, but it's available in case you need to specify a time of day for your feedback.
* **Contact Email** (7, email, false):
	+ Please enter your email address so the gym administration can contact you for further clarification or follow-up.
* **Contact Phone** (8, text, false):
	+ This is your phone number where the gym administration can reach out to you.
* **Additional Comments** (9, text, false):
	+ If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
