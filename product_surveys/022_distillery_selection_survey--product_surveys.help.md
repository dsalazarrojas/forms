# Distillery Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Distillery Selection Survey is a tool to gather information about your preferences and experiences related to distilleries. Please take a few minutes to answer the questions below to help us understand what you enjoy most at a distillery and what you usually do during a visit.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each question carefully and make sure you understand what is being asked.
2. Select one or more options for questions that ask for multiple selections.
3. Fill in the text fields with your thoughts and experiences.
4. Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted by the distillery.
5. Make sure to complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is a welcoming message to get you started with the survey.
* **Distillery Preferences** (`Distillery Preferences`, select_one, required): What do you enjoy most at a distillery? Please select one or more options from the list (Atmosphere, Ambience, Food and Drink).
	+ Select one or more from the list to indicate what you enjoy most at a distillery.
* **Distillery Experience** (`Distillery Experience`, select_multiple, required): What do you usually do at a distillery? Please select one or more options from the list (Tour, Event, Meet and Greet).
	+ Select one or more from the list to indicate what you usually do at a distillery.
* **Distillery Visits** (`Distillery Visits`, select_one, required): How often do you visit distilleries? Please select one option from the list (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Never).
	+ Select one option from the list to indicate how often you visit distilleries.
* **Distillery Preferences** (`Distillery Preferences`, text, optional): What are you looking for in a distillery? Please provide any additional thoughts or details.
	+ Type in your thoughts and experiences.
* **Distillery Rating** (`Distillery Rating`, number, optional): How would you rate this distillery? Please enter a number to indicate your rating.
	+ Enter a number from 1 to 5 to indicate your rating.
* **Distillery Comments** (`Distillery Comments`, text, optional): Any additional comments about this distillery? Please provide any thoughts or suggestions.
	+ Type in your thoughts and experiences.
* **Distillery Email** (`Distillery Email`, email, optional): Would you like to be contacted by the distillery? If so, enter your email address.
	+ Type in your email address if you'd like to be contacted.
