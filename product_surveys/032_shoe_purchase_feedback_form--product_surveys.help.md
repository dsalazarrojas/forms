# Shoe Purchase Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with purchasing shoes. Your input will help us understand your satisfaction and pain points, allowing us to improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the fields with your thoughts and opinions. If a field is optional, you can skip it if you're unsure or don't have an answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. How would you rate the quality of the shoes?
* **Shoe Purchase Feedback Form 1**: Rate your experience with the quality of the shoes on a scale of 1-10.

### 2. How would you rate the fit of the shoes?
* **Shoe Purchase Feedback Form 2**: Rate your experience with the fit of the shoes on a scale of 1-10.

### 3. Which of the following sizes did you purchase?
* **Shoe Purchase Feedback Form 3**: Choose all the sizes that apply to the shoes you purchased.

### 4. How did you hear about us?
* **Shoe Purchase Feedback Form 4**: Select how you heard about us, or choose "Other" if it was through a different means.

### 5. What was the reason for your purchase?
* **Shoe Purchase Feedback Form 5**: Select all the reasons that apply to your purchase.

### 6. How likely are you to recommend us?
* **Shoe Purchase Feedback Form 6**: Rate your likelihood of recommending us on a scale of 1-10.

### 7. What is your feedback about the website?
* **Shoe Purchase Feedback Form 7**: This is a free-text field for any additional comments or feedback you may have about the website.

### 8. Would you like to be contacted by us?
* **Shoe Purchase Feedback Form 8**: Select "True" if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or feedback, or "False" if not.

### 9. Additional Comments
* **Shoe Purchase Feedback Form 9**: This is a free-text field for any additional comments or feedback you may have.
