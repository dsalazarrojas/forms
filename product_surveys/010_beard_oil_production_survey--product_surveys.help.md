# Beard Oil Production Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from beard oil users about their experience with our product. The goal is to understand their satisfaction with the product's quality, effectiveness, and overall usage. This information will help us identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions about future product development.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Answer the questions about your demographics and beard characteristics (Age Group, Current Beard Length, Beard Type).
4. Rate your satisfaction with the product's quality, scent, texture, and effectiveness (Overall Product Quality Rating, Satisfaction with Scent, Satisfaction with Texture, Moisturizing Effectiveness, Shine and Appearance Improvement, Relief from Itching or Irritation).
5. Provide any suggestions for improvement in the "Suggested Formula Improvements" and "Packaging Feedback" fields.
6. Choose whether you would recommend the product to friends in the "Would You Recommend to Friends" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for any follow-up communications.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, required): Select the age group that best describes you.
* **Geographic Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter your geographic location, if desired.
* **How Often Do You Use Beard Oil** (`usage_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you use beard oil.
* **How Long Have You Used Beard Oil** (`time_as_customer`, select_one, required): Select how long you have used beard oil.
* **Current Beard Length** (`beard_length`, select_one, required): Select the length of your current beard.
* **Beard Type** (`beard_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of your beard (Straight, Wavy, Curly, Coarse, Fine).
* **Overall Product Quality Rating** (`product_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the overall quality of the product.
* **Satisfaction with Scent** (`scent_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with the scent of the product.
* **Satisfaction with Texture** (`texture_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with the texture of the product.
* **How Quickly Does Product Absorb** (`absorption_rate`, select_one, required): Select how quickly the product absorbs into your skin.
* **Moisturizing Effectiveness** (`moisture_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Rate the moisturizing effectiveness of the product.
* **Shine and Appearance Improvement** (`shine_appearance`, select_one, required): Rate the improvement in shine and appearance of your skin.
* **Relief from Itching or Irritation** (`itching_relief`, select_one, required): Rate the relief from itching or irritation provided by the product.
* **Suggested Formula Improvements** (`formula_improvements`, text, optional): Share any suggestions for product formula improvements.
* **Packaging Feedback** (`packaging_feedback`, text, optional): Share any feedback about the packaging.
* **Preferred Scent Profiles** (`scent_preferences`, text, optional): Share your preferred scent profiles.
* **Would You Recommend to Friends** (`recommend_friends`, select_one, required): Choose whether you would recommend the product to friends.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Suggested Formula Improvements" and "Packaging Feedback" fields.
