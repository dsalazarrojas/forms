# Preference Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preference Selection Survey is a form designed to collect information about your personal preferences, allowing us to better understand your needs and tailor our interactions to your liking.

## How To Complete This Form
Please complete the form by following these steps:

1. Select your preferred product category from the provided options.
2. Choose your preferred product(s) from the options listed.
3. Select your preferred communication channels from the list of options provided.
4. Provide a satisfaction rating (optional).
5. Enter your email address (optional).
6. Enter your phone number (optional).
7. Enter your age (optional).
8. Choose your gender (optional).
9. Enter your occupation (optional).
10. Choose your preferred day of the week (optional).
11. Select your preferred time slot(s) (optional).
12. Provide any comments or feedback (optional).
13. Give your consent (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Category**: Select the product category that best suits your needs.
* **Preferred Product**: Choose the product(s) you prefer from the list.
* **Preferred Communication Channels**: Select the channels you would like to use for communication, such as Email, Phone, SMS, or Social Media.
* **Satisfaction Rating**: Provide a rating of your satisfaction, from 1 to 10 (optional).
* **Email**: Enter your email address (if you wish to be contacted via email, optional).
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (if you wish to be contacted via phone, optional).
* **Age**: Enter your age (if you wish to share your age, optional).
* **Gender**: Choose your gender (if you wish to share it, optional).
* **Occupation**: Enter your occupation (if you wish to share it, optional).
* **Preferred Day**: Choose your preferred day of the week (if you wish to schedule interactions, optional).
* **Preferred Time Slot**: Select your preferred time slot(s) for interactions (if you wish to schedule specific times, optional).
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback (if you wish to share your thoughts, optional).
* **Consent**: Give your consent to share your preferences with us (if you wish to participate in future interactions, optional).
