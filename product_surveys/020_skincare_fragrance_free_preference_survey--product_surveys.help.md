# Skincare Fragrance Free Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skincare Fragrance Free Preference Survey is a tool designed to gather information about your preferences and habits related to skincare and fragrances. This survey will help us understand your needs and concerns, allowing us to provide more tailored advice and recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that accurately reflect your preferences and habits.
3. If a question does not apply to you, select "Not sure" or leave it blank.
4. Provide your email address to receive any follow-up communications or offers from us.
5. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please provide them in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Skincare Fragrance Free Preference Survey Page 1**: This is the introduction page of the survey.
* **Product Category**: Select the category that best describes the type of products you are interested in or currently use.
  * Skincare: Products for skin care, such as creams, lotions, and serums.
  * Fragrance: Perfumes, colognes, or other scented products.
  * Haircare: Products for hair care, such as shampoos, conditioners, and hair masks.
  * Makeup: Products for applying makeup, such as foundations, mascaras, and eyeshadows.
  * Bodycare: Products for body care, such as body washes, lotions, and body scrubs.
* **Skin Type**: Select your skin type from the following options:
  * Normal: Your skin is generally healthy and well-maintained.
  * Sensitive: Your skin is sensitive, and you may experience irritation or allergic reactions.
  * Oily: Your skin tends to be oily or produces excess sebum.
  * Dry: Your skin tends to be dry or flaky.
  * Combination: Your skin is a combination of two or more of the above skin types.
* **Fragrance Preferred**: Select your preference for fragrances:
  * True: You prefer fragrances.
  * False: You do not prefer fragrances.
  * Not sure: You are unsure or do not have a preference.
* **Skin Condition**: Please describe your current skin condition or concerns, if any (optional).
* **Skincare Concerns**: Please list any specific skincare concerns you have, such as acne, aging, or hyperpigmentation (optional).
* **Skincare Products Used**: Please list any skincare products you currently use (optional).
* **Skincare Goals**: Please describe your skincare goals, such as reducing acne or improving skin elasticity (optional).
* **Current Fragrance Use**: Select your current fragrance use:
  * True: You currently use fragrances.
  * False: You do not currently use fragrances.
  * Not sure: You are unsure or do not have a preference.
  * Never: You have never used fragrances.
* **Fragrance Preferred**: Select your preferred type of fragrance:
  * Fragrance free: You prefer fragrance-free products.
  * Fragrance: You prefer products with fragrances.
* **Natural Ingredients**: Select your preference for natural ingredients:
  * True: You prefer products with natural ingredients.
  * False: You do not prefer products with natural ingredients.
  * Not sure: You are unsure or do not have a preference.
* **Skin Sensitivities**: Select your skin sensitivities:
  * True: You experience skin sensitivities.
  * False: You do not experience skin sensitivities.
  * Not sure: You are unsure or do not have a preference.
* **Allergies**: Select any allergies you may have, such as fragrance, dye, or sulfite allergies (optional).
* **Age**: Please enter your age (optional).
* **Email**: Enter your email address to receive any follow-up communications or offers from us.
* **Phone**: Please enter your phone number (optional).
* **Note**: Any additional comments or concerns you may have (optional).
