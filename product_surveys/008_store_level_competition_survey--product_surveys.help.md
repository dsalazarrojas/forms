# store_level_competition_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The store_level_competition_survey form is used to collect data about store level competition, allowing you to compare and analyze different stores and their competitors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and select the relevant information for each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **store_level_competition_survey**:
  - **store_level_competition_survey** (`select_multiple`): Select multiple store level competition (High or Low).
- **store_name** (`text`): Enter the name of the store.
- **store_type** (`select_one`): Select the type of store (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
- **competitor_name** (`text`): Enter the name of the competitor.
- **location** (`text`): Enter the location of the store.
- **competitor_location** (`text`): Enter the location of the competitor.
- **competitor_price** (`number`): Enter the competitor's price.
- **store_hours** (`time`): Enter the store's hours of operation.
- **competitor_hours** (`time`): Enter the competitor's hours of operation.
- **store_rating** (`select_one`): Select the store's rating (Option 10, Option 11, or Option 12).
- **competitor_rating** (`select_one`): Select the competitor's rating (Option 13, Option 14, or Option 15).
- **store_level** (`select_one`): Select the store's level (Option 16, Option 17, or Option 18).
- **competitor_level** (`select_one`): Select the competitor's level (Option 19, Option 20, or Option 21).
- **competitive_analysis** (`text`): Enter your analysis of the competitive store.
- **competitor_analysis** (`text`): Enter your analysis of the competitor store.
- **store_insights** (`text`): Enter any insights or notes about the store.
- **competitor_insights** (`text`): Enter any insights or notes about the competitor store.
- **store_comments** (`text`): Enter any comments about the store.
- **competitor_comments** (`text`): Enter any comments about the competitor store.
- **competitive_comments** (`text`): Enter any comments about the competition between the two stores.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (none are marked as required in this form).
- Select relevant options for each field based on your analysis.
- Keep your answers clear and concise for easier analysis.
