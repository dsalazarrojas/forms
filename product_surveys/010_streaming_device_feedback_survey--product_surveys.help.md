# Streaming Device Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and improvement suggestions from users related to their streaming devices. Please answer the questions accurately and thoroughly to help us identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your answer to each question carefully.
2. If you have additional comments or suggestions, please type them in the "Additional Comments" field.
3. Please ensure that your email and phone number are correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input**: This question is to gather your initial response regarding your streaming device experience.
* **Streaming Device Model**: Choose the model of your streaming device from the options listed.
* **Feedback**: If you have any positive or negative feedback about your streaming device, please write it here.
* **Streaming Device Type**: This question is to determine the type of streaming device you are using. Choose from the options listed.
* **Operating System**: Select the operating system you are using on your streaming device.
* **Streaming Service Used**: This question is to determine which streaming services you are using with your device.
* **Streaming Quality**: Choose your experience with the streaming quality of your device.
* **Technical Issues**: If you are experiencing any technical issues, select all that apply.
* **Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with your streaming device experience.
* **Improvement Suggestions**: This is your chance to suggest ways to improve the device.
* **Additional Comments**: Any additional comments or suggestions can be placed here.
* **Email**: Please enter your email address so we can contact you for further clarification.
* **Phone Number**: If you would like to be contacted for further discussion, please enter your phone number.
* **Operating System Version**: If you are aware of your operating system version, please enter it here.
* **Streaming Device Serial Number**: If you know your device's serial number, please enter it here.
* **Streaming Device Improve**: This date and time field is to record when you would like to improve your device (for testing purposes only).
* **Streaming Device Improve**: This date field is to record when you would like to improve your device (for testing purposes only).
* **Streaming Device Improve**: This time field is to record when you would like to improve your device (for testing purposes only).
