# Form Field Structure Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Form Field Structure Survey is a collection of questions that help us understand your personal and professional details. The information gathered through this form will be used for internal purposes and will help us better serve you and our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully before filling out the form.
2. Enter your information accurately and completely.
3. If a field is not applicable to you, simply leave it blank.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`2`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your official email address.
* **Job Title** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your job title within the company.
* **Company Type** (`4`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of company you work for (Small Business, Medium Business, Large Business, Enterprise).
* **Department** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the department(s) you work in (Marketing, Finance, Operations, Human Resources).
* **Role in Company** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your role within the company.
* **Job Level** (`7`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your job level (Entry Level, Mid Level, Senior Level).
* **Form Filling Experience** (`8`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your experience with forms (Never, Less than a year, 1-2 years, More than 2 years).
* **Most Frequent Form Type** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the most frequent type of form you fill out (Single Field, Multi Field, Other).
* **Time Spent in Form** (`10`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time you spend filling out forms.
* **Date of Last Form Submission** (`11`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of your last form submission.
* **Time of Last Form Submission** (`12`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of your last form submission.
* **Note** (`13`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your form filling experience.
* **Email 2** (`14`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your secondary email address.
* **Phone** (`15`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Company** (`16`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your company name.
* **Address** (`17`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your address.
* **Job Title 2** (`18`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your secondary job title.
* **Department 2** (`19`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your secondary department.
* **Role** (`20`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your role in the company.
* **Job Level 2** (`21`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your job level in the company.
* **Form Filling Experience 2** (`22`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your experience with forms.
* **Most Frequent Form Type 2** (`23`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the most frequent type of form you fill out.
* **Time Spent In Form 2** (`24`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the time you spend filling out forms.
* **Date Of Last Form Submission 2** (`25`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the date of your last form submission.
