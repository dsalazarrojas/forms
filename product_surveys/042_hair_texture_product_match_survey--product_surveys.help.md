# Hair Texture Product Match Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your hair characteristics to match you with a product that suits your hair type and preferences. However, please note that the form's purpose may be for research or data collection purposes, and its primary goal is to provide a product recommendation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that best describe your hair characteristics. For the fields with multiple options, select as many as apply. For the fields with single options, select only one option. You can enter any additional information in the "Product Match" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hair Texture**: Select one or more of the following hair textures that apply to you:
	+ Fine
	+ Coarse
	+ Wavy
	+ Straight
	+ Curly
	+ Very Curly
	+ Bouncy
	+ Dry
* **Hair Type**: Select the type of hair you have:
	+ Natural
	+ Chemical-processed
	+ Synthetic
* **Length**: Enter the length of your hair in a numerical value.
* **Hair Color**: Select one or more of the following hair colors that apply to you:
	+ Black
	+ Blond
	+ Brown
	+ Red
	+ Grey
	+ Other
* **Hair Porosity**: Select the porosity of your hair:
	+ Porous
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High
* **Hair Density**: Select the density of your hair:
	+ Fine
	+ Medium
	+ Thick
	+ Very Thick
* **Product Match**: Enter any additional information or product match suggestions you may have.
* **Salon Preferences**: Enter any additional information or preferences you may have for a salon.
