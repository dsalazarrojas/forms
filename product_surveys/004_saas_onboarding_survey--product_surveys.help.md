# saas_onboarding_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about new accounts and their settings to facilitate smooth onboarding and account management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "new_account" field with the account name or description.
2. Enter the email address associated with the account in the "email" field.
3. Describe the software used in the "software_used" field.
4. Choose the correct license type from the options provided in the "license_type" field.
5. Enter the time taken to complete the onboarding process in the "time_to_go" field.
6. Provide a brief description of the software subscriptions in the "saas_subscriptions" field.
7. Enter any additional information about the software license in the "saas_license" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **new_account** (new_account, text, required: false)
Enter the name or description of the new account.
### 2. **email** (email, email, required: false)
Enter the email address associated with the account.
### 3. **software_used** (software_used, text, required: false)
Describe the software used by the account.
### 4. **license_type** (license_type, select_one, required: false)
Choose the type of license used:
* Basic
* Business
* Enterprise
### 5. **time_to_go** (time_to_go, text, required: false)
Enter the time taken to complete the onboarding process.
### 6. **saas_subscriptions** (saas_subscriptions, text, required: false)
Describe the software subscriptions for this account.
### 7. **saas_license** (saas_license, text, required: false)
Enter any additional information about the software license.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information to ensure that the account is set up correctly.
* Double-check your entries for any errors or inconsistencies.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the organization's specific guidelines or ask your supervisor for clarification.
