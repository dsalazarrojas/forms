# New Beverage Flavor Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Beverage Flavor Feedback Survey is a survey designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with a new beverage flavor. It is intended for customers who have purchased or tried the new flavor.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you are a customer who has purchased or tried the new flavor.
2. Fill out the form with your honest feedback and opinions.
3. Mark all required fields with an asterisk (*).
4. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Drink name** (`drink_name`): Enter the name of the drink you are providing feedback on. This should be the name of the drink as it appears on the packaging or label.
* **Flavor profile** (`flavor_profile`): Describe the flavor profile of the drink, including any notes or characteristics you noticed.
* **Overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`): Select one of the following options:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Would you recommend?** (`would_recommend`): Select all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Additional comments** (`feedback`): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the drink.
* **Have you purchased this drink?** (`drink_purchase`): Select one of the following options:
	+ True
	+ False
* **How often do you buy this drink?** (`purchase_frequency`): Enter the frequency of your purchases (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, never).
* **Where do you usually buy this drink?** (`purchase_location`): Select all that apply:
	+ Local store
	+ Online
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Rating** (`rating`): Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Is your favorite drink?** (`favorite`): Select one of the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **What is the price range you're willing to pay?** (`price`): Select all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **What is the price range you're willing to pay?** (`price_range`): Enter the price range you are willing to pay (e.g., $0-$5, $5-$10, etc.).
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name.
* **Age** (`age`): Enter your age.
* **Time of day** (`time`): Enter the time of day in the format 12:00 AM or 12:00 PM.
* **Date of visit** (`date_of_visit`): Enter the date of visit in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of visit** (`time_of_visit`): Enter the time of visit in the format 12:00 AM or 12:00 PM.
* **Other (please specify)** (`purchase_location_other`): If you selected "Other" for where you usually buy this drink, please specify the location.
* **Describe where you usually buy this drink?** (`purchase_location_other_description`): If you selected "Other" for where you usually buy this drink, please provide a brief description of the location.
* **Assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`): Select one of the following options:
	+ JotForm
	+ Other
	+ None
