# Product Catalog - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Product Catalog" form is used to collect and manage product information in the catalog.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the product in the "Product Title" field.
2. If necessary, upload a product image in the "Product Image" field.
3. Enter a description of the product in the "Product Description" field.
4. Set the product price in the "Product Price" field.
5. Select relevant tags from the list in the "Product Tags" field (this field is required).
6. Enter a review of the product in the "Customer Review" field (this field is optional).
7. Enter a URL for the product image in the "Image Url" field (this field is optional).
8. Choose the product's category from the list in the "Product Category" field (this field is required).
9. Set the product's status to either "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Product Status" field.
10. If applicable, select a rating for the product in the "Product Rating" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Tags** (`Product Tags`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable tags for the product from the provided options.
* **Product Category** (`Product Category`, select_one, required): Choose the product category that best describes the product.
* **Product Status** (`Product Status`, select_one, required): Set the product status to either "Active" or "Inactive" to reflect its availability.
* **Product Rating** (`Product Rating`, number, optional): Enter a rating for the product, if applicable.
* **Product Image** (`Product Image`, text, optional): Enter a URL for the product image, if applicable.
* **Product Description** (`Product Description`, text, optional): Enter a description of the product.
* **Product Price** (`Product Price`, number, optional): Enter the price of the product.
* **Customer Review** (`Customer Review`, text, optional): Enter a review of the product, if applicable.
* **Image Url** (`Image Url`, text, optional): Enter a URL for the product image, if applicable.
* **Product Title** (`Product Title`, text, required): Enter the title of the product.
* **Active/Inactive** (`Product Status`, select_one): Set the product status to either "Active" or "Inactive". 

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* Selecting the correct tags, category, and status are crucial for accurate product management.
* If you need to update an existing product, use the correct form for editing, not this form for creating a new product.
