# e_pal_user_experience_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e Pal User Experience Survey is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with E Pal, a platform that offers various features and services across different categories.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your First Name.
2. Provide your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address (this will be used to contact you if needed).
4. Enter your Phone number (if you'd like to be contacted).
5. Rate your satisfaction with E Pal on a scale of 1-5.
6. Choose how often you use E Pal features (e.g., E Pal Desktop, E Pal Web, E Pal Mobile, Other).
7. Provide any features you would like us to improve.
8. Enter any other comments or suggestions you have.
9. Select the category that best describes your E Pal experience (Finance, Health, Technology, Other).
10. Provide a brief description of your category choice (if you selected "Other" in the previous step).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., driver's license).
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., driver's license).
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you check regularly. This will be used to contact you if needed.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or to provide more information.
* **How satisfied are you with E Pal?**: Rate your satisfaction with E Pal on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **How often do you use E Pal features?**: Choose how often you use E Pal features, such as E Pal Desktop, E Pal Web, E Pal Mobile, or Other.
* **Any features you would like us to improve?**: Provide specific details about features you'd like to see improved or added.
* **Any other comments or suggestions?**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have about E Pal.
* **Category**: Select the category that best describes your E Pal experience (e.g., Finance, Health, Technology). If you selected "Other" in the previous step, provide a brief description.
* **Category (if "Other")**: If you selected "Other" as your category, provide a brief description of your E Pal experience that doesn't fit into the other categories.
