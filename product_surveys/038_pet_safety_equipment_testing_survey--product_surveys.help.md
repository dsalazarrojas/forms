# Pet Safety Equipment Testing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Safety Equipment Testing Survey is designed to gather information on pet safety equipment testing from end users. This survey aims to improve the design and functionality of pet safety equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide a brief introduction in the text field (Field 1: Introduction). This section is optional, but it will help us understand your context.
2. Enter the name of the product you are providing feedback on (Field 2: product_name).
3. Select the category of the product (Field 3: product_type).
4. Indicate if the product was tested at a specific location (Field 4: testing_location).
5. Choose the method used for testing the product (Field 5: testing_method).
6. Specify how frequently the product was tested (Field 6: testing_frequency).
7. Enter the duration of the testing period (Field 7: testing_duration).
8. Share any results or outcomes from the testing (Field 8: results).
9. Provide any additional comments or feedback on the testing experience (Field 9: comments).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: A brief text field for you to provide context about your experience with pet safety equipment testing.
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product you are providing feedback on.
* **Product Type**: Select the category of the product (e.g., Safety Harnesses, Collars, Leashes).
* **Testing Location**: Indicate if the product was tested at a specific location (e.g., Yes or No).
* **Testing Method**: Choose the method used for testing the product (e.g., In-house, In-field, Laboratory).
* **Testing Frequency**: Specify how frequently the product was tested (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Quarterly).
* **Testing Duration**: Enter the duration of the testing period.
* **Results**: Share any results or outcomes from the testing.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback on the testing experience.

## Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible in each field.
* If a field is optional, feel free to skip it if it's not relevant to your experience.
