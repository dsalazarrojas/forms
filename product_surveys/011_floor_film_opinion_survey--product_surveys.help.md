# Floor Film Opinion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather your opinions and feedback on your experience with the floor film.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the questions and fields below and complete the form accordingly.
2.  Fill out the required fields with the requested information.
3.  Provide as much detail as possible in the optional fields to help us better understand your thoughts and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Usage Patterns** (`usage_patterns`, `select_one`, required): This field asks about how you typically use the floor film. Please choose one of the options provided, as it best reflects your usage patterns.
    *   Option 1: This option is intended for users who use the floor film for general information or entertainment.
    *   Option 2: This option is intended for users who use the floor film for specific tasks or workflows.
    *   Option 3: This option is intended for users who use the floor film for other purposes not listed above.
*   **Key Preferences** (`key_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required): This field asks about your key preferences when using the floor film. Please select all that apply.
    *   Option 1: This option is intended for users who prefer a specific feature or aspect of the floor film.
    *   Option 2: This option is intended for users who prefer another specific feature or aspect of the floor film.
    *   Option 3: This option is intended for users who prefer a different feature or aspect of the floor film.
*   **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `number`, required): This field asks about your overall satisfaction with the floor film. Please rate your satisfaction on a scale from 1 to 10.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): This field is for any additional comments or thoughts you may have about the floor film.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): If you want to be contacted for follow-up or additional information, please provide your email address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you want to be contacted for follow-up or additional information, please provide your phone number.
*   **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you've used other tools or software related to the floor film, please select all that apply.
*   **Output File** (`output_file`, `text`, optional): If you would like to save your input as a file, please enter your desired file path or name.
*   **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, optional): Please select a category that best describes the purpose of the floor film you used.
*   **Description** (`description`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide a brief description of your experience with the floor film, please enter your thoughts here.
*   **Form ID** (`form_ids`, `select_one`, optional): Please select a Form ID that best describes the form you're completing.
*   **Title** (`title`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide a title for your response, please enter it here.
*   **Description** (`description`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide more details about your response, please enter them here.
