# Liquid Acceptance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Liquid Acceptance Survey is a form designed to gather information about products, specifically their acceptance levels, factors that influence customer preferences, and contact details. The survey is meant to be completed by customers or users to provide insight into their acceptance and preferences for different products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of product you are providing feedback on from the drop-down menu.
2. Choose the acceptance level of the product (high, medium, or low).
3. Select all the factors that influence your acceptance of the product (price, taste, convenience, etc.).
4. Enter any comments about the product in the text area.
5. If you have contact information (email or phone number), please enter it.
6. If you have a date and time when you last interacted with the product, please enter it.
7. Choose the category of the product (liquid or non-liquid).
8. Enter the name of the product.
9. Enter any other relevant information about the product.
10. Submit the form to complete it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Type** (`product_type`, `select_one`): Select the type of product you are providing feedback on (liquid or non-liquid).
* **Acceptance Level** (`acceptance_level`, `select_one`): Choose how much you accept the product (high, medium, or low).
* **Influences Factors** (`influences_factors`, `select_multiple`): Select all the factors that influence your acceptance of the product (e.g. price, taste, convenience, etc.).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`): Enter any comments or thoughts about the product.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `email`): If you have an email address, enter it here.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`): If you have a phone number, enter it here.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`): Enter the date when you last interacted with the product.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`): Enter the time when you last interacted with the product.
* **Product Category** (`product_category`, `select_one`): Choose the category of the product (liquid or non-liquid).
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`): Enter the name of the product.
* **Brand** (`brand`, `text`): Enter the brand of the product.
* **Lab** (`lab`, `text`): Enter any relevant information about the product from a lab or testing perspective.
* **Product ID** (`product_id`, `text`): Enter the ID of the product.
* **Lab Code** (`lab_code`, `text`): Enter any lab or testing code related to the product.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
* Make sure to provide enough information to help us understand your acceptance and preferences.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to ask.
