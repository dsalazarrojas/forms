# Electric Vehicle Owners Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information from electric vehicle owners about their experiences and preferences. The data collected will be used to improve the design and functionality of electric vehicles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
* You can answer as many questions as you feel comfortable.
* You can skip any questions that you do not feel comfortable answering.
* If you have any comments or feedback, please use the "comments" field to provide your thoughts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Electric Vehicle Owners Survey** (`electric_vehicle_owners_survey`, note, optional): This is an introduction to the survey.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all categories that apply to you. 
  - Yes: You own an electric vehicle.
  - No: You do not own an electric vehicle.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_one, optional): Please select the type of electric vehicle you own.
  - Electric Vehicle: A battery electric vehicle, such as a Tesla.
  - Plug-In Hybrid: A hybrid vehicle with an electric engine and a gasoline engine.
* **Experience 1** (`experience_1`, number, optional): Please rate your level of experience with your electric vehicle from 1-10.
* **Experience 2** (`experience_2`, number, optional): Please rate your level of experience with your electric vehicle from 1-10.
* **Experience 3** (`experience_3`, number, optional): Please rate your level of experience with your electric vehicle from 1-10.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any comments or feedback, please provide them here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address (if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number (if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions).
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Please enter the date you acquired your electric vehicle.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Please enter the time you acquired your electric vehicle.

Note: All fields are optional, and you can skip any question that you do not feel comfortable answering.
