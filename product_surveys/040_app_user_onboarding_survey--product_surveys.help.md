# App User Onboarding Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This survey aims to gather information from new users about their experience with our app during the onboarding process. Your feedback is valuable in helping us identify areas for improvement and ensure a smooth experience for all users.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly to provide us with accurate and helpful feedback.
2. Select the options that best fit your experience with our app.
3. Be as detailed as possible in your answers to provide us with a clear understanding of your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Getting Started --** (signup_experience_header, note, false): This section is a heading and serves as an introduction to the onboarding process. Please proceed to the next section for the questions.
* **How easy was it to create your account?** (account_creation_ease, number, true): Rate your experience on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is "very difficult" and 5 is "very easy".
* **Clarity of the initial walkthrough or tutorial** (tutorial_clarity, select_one, true): Select the option that best describes your experience with our initial walkthrough or tutorial.
	+ Very Clear
	+ Helpful
	+ Neutral
	+ Confusing
	+ Skipped it
* **Time taken to complete the setup process** (onboarding_time, select_one, true): Select the option that best describes the time taken to complete the setup process.
	+ Less than 1 minute
	+ 1-3 minutes
	+ 3-5 minutes
	+ Over 5 minutes
* **Which part of the onboarding was most helpful?** (helpful_step, text, false): Share with us the part of the onboarding process that was most helpful to you.
* **Which part of the onboarding was most confusing?** (confusing_step, text, false): Share with us the part of the onboarding process that was most confusing to you.
* **Initial impression of the user interface** (ui_first_impression, select_one, true): Select the option that best describes your first impression of our user interface.
	+ Modern and Clean
	+ Functional but dated
	+ Overwhelming
	+ Simple and Intuitive
* **Is it clear what value this app provides?** (value_prop_understanding, select_one, true): Select the option that best describes your understanding of the value our app provides.
	+ Yes, very clear
	+ Somewhat clear
	+ No, not yet
* **What is your primary goal for using this app?** (primary_goal, text, true): Share with us your primary goal for using our app.
* **Did you invite anyone else to join during signup?** (referral_intent, select_one, false): Select the option that best describes your intention to invite others to join our app.
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Plan to later
* **What could we do to improve the first 5 minutes of your experience?** (onboarding_improvement, text, false): Share with us your suggestions on how we can improve the onboarding process.
* **Where did you first hear about us?** (signup_source, text, false): Share with us the source where you first heard about our app.
