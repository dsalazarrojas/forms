# Trade Show Product Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trade Show Product Evaluation Form is a basic form to collect information about products displayed at a trade show. You can use this form to evaluate and provide feedback on products you've seen at the show.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Product Name" field to enter the product name.
2. Enter the "Product Image" field to describe the product image you saw.
3. Use the "Rating" field to give your evaluation (e.g., 1-5 stars).
4. In the "Product URL" field, enter the product's website or landing page.
5. Enter the company name in the "Company" field.
6. Provide the contact person's details in the "Contact" field.
7. Enter any additional comments about the product in the "Comments" field.
8. If you'd like to be notified when your evaluation is received, enter your email address in the "Email" field.
9. Finally, click "Submit" to send your evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product you want to evaluate.
* **Product Image**: Describe the product image you saw at the trade show.
* **Rating**: Evaluate the product with a 1-5 star rating.
* **Product URL**: Enter the product's website or landing page (if available).
* **Company**: Enter the company that displayed the product at the trade show.
* **Contact**: Provide the name and details of the contact person at the company.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about the product.
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you'd like to receive a confirmation of receipt of your evaluation.
* **Phone**: Enter any phone number where you can be reached for follow-up questions.
* **Submit**: Click to send your evaluation. 

## Tips
* Please fill in the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your evaluation is useful.
* You can fill this form once for each product you want to evaluate.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the trade show organizers.
