# new_product_launch_consumer_opinion_poll - Help Guide
## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll through each section and provide your honest opinions and answers to the best of your ability.
2. If you're unsure about any question, please select the "unsure" option.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields (indicated by a *), but you can leave the optional fields empty if you prefer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Launch Details** (`product_launch_details`, `text`, required): Describe the product being launched, including its features, benefits, and any notable characteristics.
* **Product Launch Details Rating** (`product_launch_details_rating`, `number`, required): Rate the product on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "not at all" and 5 being "very likely".
* **Product Launch Details Importance** (`product_launch_details_importance`, `number`, required): Rate the importance of the product on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "not at all" and 5 being "very important".
* **Product Launch Details Confidence** (`product_launch_details_confidence`, `number`, required): Rate how confident you are in your answer on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "not at all" and 5 being "very confident".
* **Demographic Details** (`demographic_details`, `text`, required): Provide your demographic information, including your age, gender, and contact preferences.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required): Enter your age.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Contact Preference** (`contact_preference`, `select_multiple`, required): Select how you would like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone, none).
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address (if you selected "email" as a contact preference).
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number (if you selected "phone" as a contact preference).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the product.
* **Purchase Intent** (`purchase_intent`, `select_one`, optional): Select how likely you are to purchase the product (e.g., very likely, somewhat likely, not likely, not at all).
* **Purchase Frequency** (`purchase_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Select how often you would purchase the product (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, rarely, never).
* **Follow-up Method** (`follow_up`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select how you would like to be followed up (e.g., email, phone, social media, none).
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Select how often you would like to be followed up (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, rarely, never).
* **Follow-up Method Frequency** (`follow_up_method_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Select how often you would like to be followed up (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, rarely, never).
* **Product Launch Details Frequency** (`product_launch_details_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Select how often you would use the product (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, rarely, never).
* **Product Launch Details Purchase** (`product_launch_details_purchase`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you would purchase the product (e.g., purchase, not please, unsure).
* **Product Launch Details Satisfaction** (`product_launch_details_satisfaction`, `number`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the product on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "very dissatisfied" and 5 being "very satisfied".
* **Product Launch Details Willingness to Recommend** (`product_launch_details_willingness_to_recommend`, `number`, optional): Rate how likely you are to recommend the product on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "not at all" and 5 being "very likely".
* **Product Launch Details Price Range** (`product_launch_details_price_range`, `select_one`, optional): Select the price range you would be willing to pay for the product (e.g., very likely, somewhat likely, not likely, not at all).
* **Product Launch Details Satisfaction Frequency** (`product_launch_details_satisfaction_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Select how often you would use the product (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, rarely, never).
* **Product Launch Details Satisfaction Level** (`product_launch_details_satisfaction_level`, `select_one`, optional): Select your level of satisfaction with the product (e.g., very satisfied, somewhat satisfied, neutral, somewhat dissatisfied, very dissatisfied).

## Tips

* Be honest and provide genuine feedback.
* If you're unsure about any question, please select the "unsure" option.
* Make sure to complete all required fields, but feel free to leave optional fields empty if you prefer.
