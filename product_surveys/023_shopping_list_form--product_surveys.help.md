# Shopping List Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shopping List Form is designed to collect information from users about their shopping preferences and habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Fill in the items listed on the first page of the form, entering each item in the corresponding text field.
3. Make sure to fill in all required fields (marked as "required") to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_page_item_1** (Text, Required): Enter the first item on your shopping list.
* **first_page_item_2** (Text, Required): Enter the second item on your shopping list.
* **first_page_item_3** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information about the third item on your shopping list.
* **first_page_item_4** to **first_page_item_24** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional items on your shopping list.
*   These fields are for listing additional items and are not required. You can fill in as many or as few as you need.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (marked as "required") to complete the form.
* You can fill in as many or as few items as you need, depending on your shopping list.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, contact form@gic.mx.
