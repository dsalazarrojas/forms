# Product Market Fit Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Market Fit Confirmation form is a validation tool to ensure that our products or services meet the market requirements and are positioned correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Go through each page of the form carefully and answer the questions based on your product or service.
2. Fill in the product name and description on page 1.
3. Identify the target customer segment on page 2.
4. Describe the value proposition and competitive differentiators on page 3.
5. List the main competitors on page 4.
6. Define the unique value proposition on page 5.
7. Choose the product life cycle stage on page 6.
8. Select the market fit confirmation pages on page 7.
9. Identify the product category on page 8.
10. Choose the target geography on page 9.
11. Answer the target revenue stage on page 10.
12. Select the product pricing on page 11.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 2: What is the product or service name?
 * **What is the product or service name?** (`product_market_fit_confirmation_page_2`, text, **Required**): Enter a clear and concise name of your product or service.
 * **What is the product or service description?** (`product_market_fit_confirmation_page_14`, text, **Required**): Provide a brief description of your product or service.

### Page 3: What is the target customer segment?
 * **What is the target customer segment?** (`product_market_fit_confirmation_page_3`, text, **Required**): Identify the specific group of customers you're targeting with your product or service.

### Page 4: What is the value proposition?
 * **What is the value proposition?** (`product_market_fit_confirmation_page_4`, text, **Required**): Describe the unique benefits and value your product or service provides to customers.
 * **What are the main competitors?** (`product_market_fit_confirmation_page_5`, text, **Required**): List the main competitors in the market.

### Page 5: What is the unique value proposition?
 * **What is the unique value proposition?** (`product_market_fit_confirmation_page_6`, text, **Required**): Define the unique benefits and value that differentiate your product or service from the competition.

### Page 6: What is the product life cycle stage?
 * **What is the product life cycle stage?** (`product_market_fit_confirmation_page_7`, select_one, **Optional**): Choose the current life cycle stage of your product or service:
  - **launch**: The product has just been launched and is still in the initial phase.
  - **growth**: The product is growing in popularity and expanding its customer base.
  - **maturity**: The product has reached a stable and established customer base.
  - **decline**: The product is no longer growing and may need significant changes to survive.
 * **What is the market fit confirmation pages?** (`product_market_fit_confirmation_page_8`, select_multiple, **Optional**): Select the market fit confirmation pages that apply to your product or service:
  - **launch**: The product has just been launched and is still in the initial phase.
  - **growth**: The product is growing in popularity and expanding its customer base.
  - **maturity**: The product has reached a stable and established customer base.
  - **decline**: The product is no longer growing and may need significant changes to survive.

### Page 8: What is the product category?
 * **What is the product category?** (`product_market_fit_confirmation_page_9`, select_one, **Optional**): Choose the category your product or service belongs to:
  - **product**: A physical or tangible product.
  - **software**: A digital or intangible software product.
  - **service**: An intangible service.
  - **other**: Other (please specify).

### Page 9: What is the target geography?
 * **What is the target geography?** (`product_market_fit_confirmation_page_10`, select_one, **Optional**): Identify the specific region or country you're targeting with your product or service:
  - **US**: The United States.
  - **Canada**: Canada.
  - **Mexico**: Mexico.
  - **other**: Other (please specify).

### Page 10: What is the target revenue stage?
 * **What is the target revenue stage?** (`product_market_fit_confirmation_page_10`, text, **Optional**): Describe the current revenue stage of your product or service.

### Page 11: What is the product pricing?
 * **What is the product pricing?** (`product_market_fit_confirmation_page_11`, select_one, **Optional**): Choose the pricing model of your product or service:
  - **free**: The product or service is offered for free.
  - **subscription**: The product or service is subscription-based.
  - **one time**: The product or service is sold once.
  - **recurring**: The product or service is sold with recurring payments.

## Tips

* Be clear and concise in your answers.
* Focus on specific and real-world examples.
* Use data and statistics to support your answers.
* Keep the answers brief and to the point.
* Don't overthink the questions; just answer from the perspective of your product or service.
