# Leather Sofa Care Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather information from customers about their experiences with their leather sofa. We're interested in understanding your cleaning habits, maintenance practices, and overall satisfaction with your sofa's performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To get started, simply fill out each section as prompted.
2. Answer each question based on your actual experiences with your leather sofa.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to share them in the "Any additional comments or suggestions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (first_page): Please enter your name, email address, and phone number so we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Sofa Details** (sofa_details): Describe your leather sofa, including its brand, model, and any specific features or characteristics.
* **How often do you clean your leather sofa?** (care_frequency): Choose the frequency with which you clean your sofa, from "Daily" to "Rarely".
* **Have you ever purchased any cleaning products for your leather sofa?** (recommended_products): Select all products you've purchased for cleaning your sofa, including "Yes" or "No".
* **What cleaning method do you use?** (cleaning_method): Choose the method you use to clean your sofa, from "Vacuum", "Soap and water", or "Specialized cleaning solution".
* **Have you used any professional maintenance services for your leather sofa?** (maintenance_services): Select all services you've used, including "Yes" or "No".
* **Please share any positive or negative experiences with your leather sofa's maintenance?** (customer_experience): Describe any notable experiences you've had with your sofa's maintenance, such as successes or difficulties.
* **Any additional comments or suggestions?** (additional_comments): Share any additional thoughts or ideas you have about your leather sofa's care and maintenance.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Phone** (phone): Enter your phone number for follow-up contact if necessary.
