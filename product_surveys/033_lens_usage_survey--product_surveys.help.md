# Lens Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather information about your interactions with Lens.

## How To Complete This Form

This form has 12 fields. Please fill out all the fields that apply to you. You can select one or multiple options for some fields.

1. First Name: Enter your first name.
2. Last Name: Enter your last name.
3. Email: Enter your work email address.
4. Organization: Enter your organization or company name.
5. Department: Enter your department or team name.
6. Job Title: Enter your job title or position.
7. How many days do you use Lens?: Enter the number of days you use Lens in a week.
8. Average minutes per day: Enter the average number of minutes you spend using Lens in a day.
9. What is your preferred lens?: Select whether you prefer to use a lens.
10. How often do you use Lens?: Select all the ways you use Lens (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
11. Why do you use Lens?: Enter a short description of why you use Lens.
12. Do you have any suggestions for Lens?: Enter any suggestions or comments about Lens.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required): Enter your organization or company name.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter your department or team name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **How many days do you use Lens?** (`lens_usage_days`, number, required): Enter the number of days you use Lens in a week.
* **Average minutes per day** (`lens_usage_minutes`, number, required): Enter the average number of minutes you spend using Lens in a day.
* **What is your preferred lens?** (`preferred_lenses`, select_one, required): Select whether you prefer to use a lens.
*   - Select 'Yes' if you prefer to use a lens.
* **How often do you use Lens?** (`lens_usage_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Select all the ways you use Lens (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
*   - Select all the ways you use Lens.
*   - Select 'Yes' if you use Lens for each option.
* **Why do you use Lens?** (`why_lens`, text, optional): Enter a short description of why you use Lens.
*   - This field is optional.
* **Do you have any suggestions for Lens?** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Enter any suggestions or comments about Lens.
*   - This field is optional.
