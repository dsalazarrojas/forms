# Pharmaceutical New Drug Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from healthcare professionals and patients about their experiences with new pharmaceutical drugs. The purpose of this form is to gather information on dosages, side effects, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Start by providing a brief overview of the new medicine in the "New medicine name" field.
*   Select the appropriate dosage and frequency of the new medicine from the "Dosage And Frequency" field.
*   Provide any additional new medicine name information in the "New medicine name 2" field.
*   Enter the name of the healthcare professional who prescribed the new medicine in the "Healthcare Professional Name" field.
*   Enter the name of the patient who received the new medicine in the "Patient Name" field.
*   List any medical conditions related to the new medicine in the "Medical Conditions" field.
*   List any additional medical conditions related to the new medicine in the "Medical Conditions 2" field.
*   Select the option that best describes any side effects experienced with the new medicine from the "Side Effects" field.
*   Rate your overall satisfaction with the new medicine on a scale from 1 to 10 in the "Overall Satisfaction" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **New medicine name** (`new_medicine_name`, text, optional):
    This field is used to provide a brief overview of the new medicine.
*   **Dosage And Frequency** (`dosage_and_frequency`, select_multiple, optional):
    This field allows you to select the appropriate dosage and frequency of the new medicine.
*   **New medicine name 2** (`new_medicine_name_2`, text, optional):
    This field is for any additional information about the new medicine.
*   **Healthcare Professional Name** (`healthcare_professional_name`, text, optional):
    Enter the name of the healthcare professional who prescribed the new medicine.
*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional):
    Enter the name of the patient who received the new medicine.
*   **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional):
    List any medical conditions related to the new medicine.
*   **Medical Conditions 2** (`medical_conditions_2`, text, optional):
    List any additional medical conditions related to the new medicine.
*   **Side Effects** (`side_effects`, select_one, optional):
    Select the option that best describes any side effects experienced with the new medicine.
*   **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, optional):
    Rate your overall satisfaction with the new medicine on a scale from 1 to 10.
