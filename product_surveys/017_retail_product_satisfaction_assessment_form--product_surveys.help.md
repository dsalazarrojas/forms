# Retail Product Satisfaction Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather customer feedback about a retail product, focusing on aspects such as product quality, customer service, and overall satisfaction. The information collected will help us understand customer experience and improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your product rating and satisfaction level in the respective fields.
2. Choose how you would recommend the product from the options provided.
3. Enter any additional comments or product description if applicable.
4. Provide product image (optional) for reference.
5. Submit the form when completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Rating** (1): Rate your overall experience with the product from 1 to 5.
* **Overall Satisfaction Level** (2): Select your level of satisfaction with the product from the options provided.
* **Would you recommend this product?** (3): Choose how you would recommend the product from the options provided.
* **Product Purpose** (4): Enter the primary purpose or use of the product.
* **Purchase Date** (5): Enter the date you purchased the product.
* **Contact Information** (6): Enter your contact information (name, email, phone number) for follow-up.
* **Product Name** (7): Enter the name of the product you purchased.
* **Product Price** (8): Enter the price you paid for the product.
* **Customer Service** (9): Select the level of customer service you received from our sales representative.
* **Comments** (10): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Product Quality** (11): Select the quality of the product from the options provided.
* **Purchase Location** (12): Select where you purchased the product from the options provided.
* **Sales Channel** (13): Select the sales channel you used to purchase the product from the options provided.
* **Sales Person** (14): Select the sales person who assisted you in the purchase.
* **Store Name** (15): Enter the name of the store where you made the purchase.
* **Contact Name** (16): Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Contact Email** (17): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (18): Enter your phone number.
* **Store Address** (19): Enter the store address.
* **Store Phone** (20): Enter the store phone number.
* **Store Email** (21): Enter the store email.
* **Product Description** (22): Enter a brief description of the product.
* **Product Image** (23): Upload an image of the product (optional).
* **Customer Satisfaction** (24): Select your level of satisfaction with the product and sales experience from the options provided.

Note: Fields marked as `required` should be filled in as they are crucial for us to understand your experience.
