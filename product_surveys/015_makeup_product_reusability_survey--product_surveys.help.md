# Makeup Product Reusability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from end-users on their product usage and concerns. The data collected will help us better understand how our products are used and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the relevant answers for each question and provide any comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Product Usage (1) <small>product_usage</small>
* This question asks whether you have used the product before.
* Please select either "Yes" or "No" to indicate your product usage.

### Reasons for Keeping (2) <small>reasons_for_keeping</small>
* This question asks why you keep the product, if you answered "Yes" to the previous question.
* Please select all the reasons that apply.

### Safety Concerns (3) <small>safety_concerns</small>
* This question asks about any safety concerns you have regarding the product.
* Please select all the concerns that apply.

### Sustainability Concerns (4) <small>sustainability_concerns</small>
* This question asks about any sustainability concerns you have regarding the product.
* Please select all the concerns that apply.

### Reusability (5) <small>reusability</small>
* This question asks for your rating of the product's reusability.
* Please enter a number between 1 (very low) and 10 (very high).

### Product Reusability (6) <small>product_reusability</small>
* This question asks if you consider the product to be reusable.
* Please select either "Yes" or "No".

### Concerns (7) <small>concerns</small>
* This question asks about any concerns you have regarding the product.
* Please select all the concerns that apply.

### Related Products (8) <small>related_products</small>
* This question asks if you have used similar products in the past.
* Please select all the products that apply.

### Other Concerns (9) <small>other_concerns</small>
* This question is for any other concerns you may have about the product.
* Please enter your answer in the text field provided.

### Comments (10) <small>comments</small>
* This question is for any additional comments or feedback you may have about the product.
* Please enter your answer in the text field provided.
