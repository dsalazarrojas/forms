# Wellness Product Subscription Interest Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather your interest and preferences for wellness products or services. Please answer the questions honestly, and feel free to provide any additional notes or comments you have.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select all applicable product types that interest you.
2. Choose your preferred frequency of use.
3. Enter your preferred price for the product.
4. Provide your name, and confirm it if you wish.
5. Enter your email address, and confirm it if you wish.
6. Enter your preferred quantity of the product.
7. Select your preferred subscription preference.
8. Choose your subscription plan.
9. Select your preferred subscription duration.
10. Add any additional notes or comments about your preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Type**: Please select all the types of wellness products or services that you are interested in (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3").
* **Frequency**: Choose how often you would like to use or interact with the product (e.g., "Daily", "Weekly", "Monthly").
* **Price**: Enter the maximum price you are willing to pay for the product (e.g., a numerical value).
* **Update Preferences**: Enter any specific preferences or instructions on how you would like to be updated about new products or services (e.g., email, phone, etc.).
* **Product Name**: If you have a specific product in mind, please enter its name here.
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the product you are interested in (e.g., a numerical value).
* **Price Preference**: Select your preferred price level for the product (e.g., "Low", "Medium", "High").
* **Subscription Preference**: Select all the subscription preferences that apply to you (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3").
* **Subscription Plan**: Choose the subscription plan that best fits your needs (e.g., "Plan 1", "Plan 2", "Plan 3").
* **Subscription Duration**: Select the duration of your desired subscription (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3").
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your preferences.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about new products or services.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about new products or services.
* **Confirm Email**: Confirm your email address to ensure accuracy.
* **Confirm Phone**: Confirm your phone number to ensure accuracy.
* **Confirm Name**: Confirm your name to ensure accuracy.
* **Confirm Product Name**: Confirm the name of the product you are interested in, if applicable.
* **Confirm Quantity**: Confirm the quantity of the product you are interested in.
* **Confirm Subscription Preference**: Confirm your subscription preference selections.
* **Confirm Subscription Plan**: Confirm your subscription plan selection.
* **Confirm Subscription Duration**: Confirm your subscription duration selection.
* **Confirm Price Preference**: Confirm your preferred price level.
* **Confirm Update Preferences**: Confirm your update preferences.
* **Confirm Notes**: Confirm any additional comments or notes you have.

Please answer these questions honestly, and feel free to provide any additional comments or notes you have. This information will help us better understand your interests and preferences for wellness products or services.
