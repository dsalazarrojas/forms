# Digital Tracking Features Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on their preferences and concerns regarding digital tracking features. The goal is to gather information that will help improve the development and design of digital tracking features.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select your preferred options for each field. You can choose as many options as apply to you. If you have any additional concerns or preferences not listed, please use the "Other" option or provide a detailed answer in the corresponding text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What features do you like about digital tracking?** (`tracking_features`, select_multiple): Select all the features that you find useful or like about digital tracking. This includes, but is not limited to, features such as location tracking, activity tracking, or notification reminders.
* **What are your general privacy preferences regarding digital tracking?** (`tracking_preferences`, select_one): Please select one option that best describes your general approach to privacy when it comes to digital tracking. This includes options like "I always opt-out", "I'm neutral", or "I'm not sure".
* **What are your acceptable use cases for digital tracking?** (`tracking_usage`, select_multiple): Choose all the scenarios where you would be okay with digital tracking. This includes, but is not limited to, scenarios like online shopping, social media, or location-based tracking.
* **What concerns do you have about digital tracking?** (`tracking_concerns`, text): If you have any specific concerns about digital tracking, please provide a detailed answer in this text field.
* **Are there any other concerns not mentioned?** (`tracking_concerns_other`, text): If you think there are any other concerns that are not listed, please provide a detailed answer in this text field.
* **What features are most important to you in a digital tracking system?** (`tracking_features_important`, select_multiple): Select all the features that are most important to you when it comes to digital tracking. This includes, but is not limited to, features like data accuracy, user interface, or feature customization.
* **How do you protect your privacy when using digital products?** (`tracking_privacy`, select_multiple): Choose all the methods you use to protect your privacy when using digital products. This includes, but is not limited to, using strong passwords, two-factor authentication, or avoiding tracking cookies.
* **Why do you use digital products?** (`tracking_why`, select_multiple): Select all the reasons why you use digital products. This includes, but is not limited to, convenience, entertainment, or staying connected with others.
