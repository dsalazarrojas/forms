# Kandi Product Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kandi Product Feedback Survey is designed to gather your thoughts, experiences, and insights about the Kandi Product. Your feedback will help us understand how to improve the product's functionality, usability, and overall user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and fill out the fields as required. You can skip pages that are not applicable to your experience with the Kandi Product. Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and comprehensively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### What do you think about Kandi Product? 
* **What do you think about Kandi Product?** (`product_feedback`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is a text input where you can express your thoughts, opinions, and feelings about the Kandi Product. Be as detailed as possible to help us understand your perspective.

### Satisfaction Level
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, `number`, required: false)
	+ Rate your satisfaction level with the Kandi Product on a scale from 1 to 5. This will help us understand your overall satisfaction with the product.

### Improvement Ideas
* **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Please select one or more options on how we can improve the Kandi Product. This is your chance to suggest features, functionalities, or any other ideas you think could enhance the product.

### Issue Reports
* **Issue Reports** (`issue_reports`, `text`, required: false)
	+ If you've encountered any issues while using the Kandi Product, describe them in detail here. This will help us identify and fix problems that need addressing.

### Customer Service
* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, `select_one`, required: true)
	+ Please select how you experienced the customer service provided by our team. This is a crucial feedback point for us to improve our support quality.

### How useful is Kandi Product? 
* **How useful do you think Kandi Product is?** (`product_usefulness`, `number`, required: false)
	+ Rate how useful you find the Kandi Product on a scale from 1 to 5. This helps us gauge the product's usefulness and impact.

### Time Spent 
* **How long did you spend using Kandi Product?** (`time_spent`, `time`, required: false)
	+ Please provide an estimate of the time you spent using the Kandi Product. This will help us understand your engagement with the product.

### Contact Preferences
* **What are your contact preferences?** (`contact_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: true)
	+ Please select your contact preferences for future communication with us. This helps us tailor our approach to best suit your needs.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false)
	+ Enter your email address for us to contact you regarding your feedback and any follow-up actions.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Enter your phone number for us to contact you via phone call.

### Date 
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false)
	+ Enter the date you started using or have experience with the Kandi Product.

### Time 
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false)
	+ Enter the time you spent using the Kandi Product.

### Note 
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false)
	+ If you have any additional comments or thoughts about the Kandi Product, share them here.

### Email (again)
* **Email** (`email_2`, `email`, required: false)
	+ Repeat your email address for our records.

### Phone (again)
* **Phone** (`phone_2`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Repeat your phone number for our records.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* You can skip pages that are not applicable to your experience.
* If you have any additional comments or thoughts, share them in the "Note" field.
* Ensure all required fields are filled in accurately.
* If you need help or clarification on any field, feel free to ask us.
