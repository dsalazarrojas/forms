# Beverage Flavor Satisfaction Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback on a specific flavor of a beverage product. We aim to understand your experience, likes, and dislikes to improve the taste and quality of our products.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the "Your Name" field and enter your name.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Select your age group from the options provided in the "Age Range" field.
4. Enter the name of the beverage that you tried in the "Beverage Tested" field.
5. Select the flavor name of the beverage from the options provided in the "Flavor Name" field.
6. Enter the date you tried the beverage in the "Tasting Date" field.
7. Rate your overall satisfaction with the flavor in the "Overall Satisfaction" field.
8. Rate the intensity of the flavor in the "Flavor Intensity" field.
9. Rate the sweetness level of the flavor in the "Sweetness Level" field.
10. Rate the tartness level of the flavor (if applicable).
11. Describe the aftertaste of the flavor in the "Aftertaste" field.
12. Rate the texture and mouthfeel of the flavor in the "Texture Rating" field.
13. Rate the visual appearance of the flavor in the "Appearance Rating" field.
14. Rate the scent or aroma of the flavor in the "Aroma Rating" field.
15. Rate how authentic the flavor tastes in the "Flavor Authenticity" field.
16. Indicate whether you would purchase this product in the "Would You Purchase This Product" field.
17. Indicate whether you would recommend this product to others in the "Would You Recommend to Others" field.
18. Select your favorite aspect of the flavor in the "What Did You Like Most" field.
19. Provide any suggestions on how to improve the flavor in the "What Could Be Improved" field.
20. Add any additional comments or feedback about the flavor in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, Text, Required): Enter your name to identify yourself in our records.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address for follow-up communications.
* **Age Range** (`age_range`, Select One, Required): Select your age group to ensure accurate representation of our demographic data.
* **Beverage Tested** (`beverage_tested`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the beverage you tried.
* **Flavor Name** (`flavor_name`, Text, Required): Select the flavor name of the beverage.
* **Tasting Date** (`tasting_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date you tried the beverage.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, Select One, Required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the flavor, from Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
* **Flavor Intensity** (`flavor_intensity`, Select One, Required): Rate the intensity of the flavor.
* **Sweetness Level** (`sweetness_level`, Select One, Required): Rate the level of sweetness in the flavor.
* **Tartness Level** (`tartness_level`, Select One, Optional): Rate the level of tartness in the flavor (if applicable).
* **Aftertaste** (`aftertaste`, Select One, Required): Describe the aftertaste of the flavor.
* **Texture Rating** (`texture_rating`, Select One, Required): Rate the texture and mouthfeel of the flavor.
* **Appearance Rating** (`appearance_rating`, Select One, Required): Rate the visual appearance of the flavor.
* **Aroma Rating** (`aroma_rating`, Select One, Required): Rate the scent or aroma of the flavor.
* **Flavor Authenticity** (`flavor_authenticity`, Select One, Required): Rate how authentic the flavor tastes.
* **Would You Purchase This Product** (`would_purchase`, Select One, Required): Indicate whether you would purchase this product.
* **Would You Recommend to Others** (`would_recommend`, Select One, Required): Indicate whether you would recommend this product to others.
* **What Did You Like Most** (`favorite_aspect`, Text, Required): Select your favorite aspect of the flavor.
* **What Could Be Improved** (`improvement_suggestions`, Text, Required): Provide any suggestions on how to improve the flavor.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about the flavor.
