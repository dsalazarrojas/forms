# Fitness App Workout Program Effectiveness Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback on the effectiveness of the fitness app's workout program. It will help us understand how well the program has met your goals, motivation, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Start by reading the introduction page.
2. Answer the questions on each page, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Make sure to submit the form when you have completed all the pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This page provides a brief introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, required: true): Rate your overall satisfaction with the program on a scale of 0-10.
* **Motivation** (`motivation`, select_one, required: true): Choose how motivated you feel about your workout program:
	+ Very High
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
	+ Very Low
* **Goals Attained** (`goals_attained`, select_multiple, required: true): Select the goals you have achieved through the program:
	+ Goal1
	+ Goal2
	+ Goal3
	+ Goal4
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, number, required: true): Rate your satisfaction with the program on a scale of 0-10.
* **Outcomes** (`outcomes`, date, required: true): Please enter the date you have reached your goals or completed the program.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the program.

## Tips
- Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
- Make sure to complete all the pages before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
