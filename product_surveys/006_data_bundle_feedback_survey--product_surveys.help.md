# data_bundle_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "data_bundle_feedback_survey" form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experiences with data bundles. This form is meant to help us understand what customers think of our data bundles, what they like or dislike, and how we can improve them.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you have had a recent experience with data bundles.
- Carefully read and fill out each field accurately.
- Provide as much detail as possible, but only about your personal experiences.
- If unsure about a question, please try to answer based on your best understanding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Customer Name
 **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.

### 2. Email
 **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.

### 3. Feedback
 **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required): Share your thoughts about your experience with data bundles. Be as detailed as possible.

### 4. How satisfied are you with data bundles?
 **How satisfied are you with data bundles?** (`satisfaction`, number, required): Rate your satisfaction level from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

### 5. Prophets
 **Prophets** (`prophets_who_is_satisfied`, select_one, required): Choose which prophet you are satisfied with. Please select from the options provided.

### 6. Additional Prophets
 **Additional Prophets** (`additional_prophet`, select_multiple, required): Choose other prophets who you are satisfied with. Please select all that apply.

### 7. Do you have a reason for being satisfied?
 **reason** (`reason`, select_one, required): Answer yes or no if you have a reason for being satisfied.

### 8. Other reason for being satisfied
 **Other reason for being satisfied** (`other_reason`, text, required): If you answered yes in the previous question, please provide a brief explanation.

### 9. Prophets rating
 **Prophets rating** (`rating`, number, required): Rate the quality of the prophets from 1 to 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.

### 10. Comments
 **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Share any additional comments or thoughts about your experience with data bundles.

### 11. Recommended
 **Recommended** (`recommended`, select_one, required): Choose if you would recommend the data bundle. Please select from the options provided.

### 12. Other recommended
 **Other recommended** (`recommended_other`, text, required): If you answered yes in the previous question, please provide a brief explanation.

### 13. Contact Info
 **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter any contact information you would like to share for follow-up.

### 14. Email for follow-up
 **Email for follow-up** (`email_follow_up`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.

### 15. Additional Info
 **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, required): Share any additional information about your experience with data bundles.

### 16. Rating scale
 **Rating scale** (`rating_scale`, select_one, required): Choose the rating scale you prefer.

### 17. Rating value
 **Rating value** (`rating_value`, number, required): Enter the numerical value of your rating.

### 18. Customer message
 **Customer message** (`customer_message`, text, required): Leave a message for us.

### 19. Date
 **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date you experienced the data bundle.

### 20. Time
 **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time you experienced the data bundle.

### 21. Time zone
 **Time zone** (`time_zone`, select_one, required): Choose your time zone.

### 22. Comments
 **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Share any additional comments or thoughts about your experience with data bundles.

### 23. Contact
 **Contact** (`contact`, text, required): Enter any contact information you would like to share.

### 24. Other
 **Other** (`other`, text, required): Share any other thoughts or comments.

## Tips
- Ensure you have filled out each field accurately and completely.
- If unsure about a question, please answer based on your best understanding.
- Please provide as much detail as possible.
- If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us.
