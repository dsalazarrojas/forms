# Digital Product Development Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your digital product, including its concept, target audience, key features, benefits, development process, launch plan, and other relevant details. This will help us better understand your digital product development needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2.  Start by describing your digital product concept in the text field under "Product Concept".
3.  Select your target audience from the options provided under "Target Audience".
4.  List the key features of your product in the text field under "Product Features".
5.  Describe the benefits of your product in the text field under "Product Benefits" (if applicable).
6.  Choose your preferred product development process from the options provided under "Product Development Process".
7.  Enter the date for the product launch under "Product Launch Date".
8.  You can optionally provide additional feedback, review, or product improvement suggestions in the text fields under "Product Feedback", "Product Review", and "Product Improvement".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   *Product Concept* (`product_concept`, `text`, required): Describe your digital product concept, including its idea, purpose, and main components.
*   *Target Audience* (`target_audience`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the group of people your digital product is intended for.
*   *Product Features* (`product_features`, `text`, required): List the main features and functionalities of your digital product.
*   *Product Benefits* (`product_benefits`, `text`, optional): Describe the benefits and value that your digital product provides (if applicable).
*   *Product Development Process* (`product_development_process`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you want to develop your digital product.
*   *Product Launch Plan* (`product_launch_plan`, `date`, required): Enter the date when you plan to launch your digital product.
*   *Product Review* (`product_review`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional feedback or insights about your digital product.
*   *Product Feedback* (`product_feedback`, `text`, optional): Share any feedback or suggestions you have about your digital product development process or the form itself.
*   *Product Improvement* (`product_improvement`, `text`, optional): Suggest any improvements to the form or your digital product development process.
*   *Product Launch Date* (`product_launch_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when you plan to launch your digital product.
*   *Form Footer* (`form_footer`, `note`, optional): This is a placeholder for any additional notes or comments about the form or your digital product development process.
