# product_feedback_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to gather feedback on the provided product. It is meant to be a concise way to collect user opinions and ratings about the product's quality, features, and overall satisfaction. Please answer the questions honestly to help us improve the product.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer the question "What do you think about this product?" in the space provided. This is an open-ended question, so feel free to share your thoughts and opinions about the product.
2.  In the "Feedback" field, describe what you like most about the product. Try to be as specific as possible so we can understand your point of view.
3.  Rate the product from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest. This will help us gauge your overall satisfaction with the product.
4.  Choose how likely you are to recommend the product. Select "1 - highly likely", "2 - maybe", or "3 - not likely" depending on your confidence in recommending the product.
5.  If you have any additional feedback or comments about the product, you can type them in the "Additional Feedback" field. This is optional, but it will help us better understand your thoughts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you think about this product?** (Text, Required): This is an open-ended question where you can share your thoughts and opinions about the product.
* **Feedback** (Text, Required): Describe what you like most about the product.
* **How would you rate this product?** (Number, Required): Rate the product from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Would you recommend this product?** (Select One, Required): Choose how likely you are to recommend the product. Select "1 - highly likely", "2 - maybe", or "3 - not likely".
* **Additional Feedback** (Text, Optional): If you have any additional thoughts or comments, type them in this field.
