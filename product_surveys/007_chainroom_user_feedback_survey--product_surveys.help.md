# Chainroom User Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chainroom User Feedback Survey is designed to collect your thoughts, opinions, and experiences with the Chainroom product. Your feedback is crucial in helping us understand your needs and improve the product to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and email address (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up purposes).
2. Choose how long you have used Chainroom and how often you use it.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with Chainroom and how easy it is to use.
4. Choose whether Chainroom meets your needs and rate its performance.
5. Provide feedback on the customer support quality, missing features, and main frustrations.
6. Answer whether you'd recommend Chainroom to others and how it compares to alternative products.
7. Rate the price and value of Chainroom and its integration with other tools.
8. Express your confidence in data security and likelihood to continue using Chainroom.
9. Add any additional feedback or comments about your experience with Chainroom.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name for follow-up purposes, if desired.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required: false): Provide your email address for potential follow-up or updates.
* **How Long Have You Used Chainroom** (`user_since`, select_one, required: true): Choose how long you have used Chainroom from the options: "Less than 1 month", "1-3 months", "3-6 months", "6-12 months", "Over 1 year".
* **Frequency of Use** (`usage_frequency`, select_one, required: true): Select how often you use Chainroom: "Daily", "Multiple times per week", "Weekly", "Multiple times per month", "Monthly or less".
* **Primary Use Case** (`primary_use_case`, text, required: false): Describe what you mainly use Chainroom for.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required: true): Rate your overall satisfaction with Chainroom: "Very Satisfied", "Satisfied", "Neutral", "Dissatisfied", "Very Dissatisfied".
* **Ease of Use** (`ease_of_use`, select_one, required: true): Rate how easy Chainroom is to use: "Very Easy", "Easy", "Neutral", "Difficult", "Very Difficult".
* **Does Chainroom Meet Your Needs** (`feature_completeness`, select_one, required: true): Choose whether Chainroom meets your needs: "Completely", "Mostly", "Partially", "Minimally", "Not at All".
* **Application Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, select_one, required: true): Rate Chainroom's performance: "Excellent", "Good", "Average", "Poor", "Very Poor".
* **Customer Support Quality** (`customer_support`, select_one, required: false): Rate the helpfulness of customer support: "Excellent", "Good", "Average", "Poor", "Have not contacted support".
* **Missing Features or Improvements** (`missing_features`, text, required: false): Suggest any features you'd like to see added.
* **Main Frustrations or Issues** (`main_frustrations`, text, required: false): Describe any problems you have encountered.
* **Likelihood to Recommend Chainroom** (`likelihood_recommend`, select_one, required: true): Choose how likely you are to recommend Chainroom: "Very Likely", "Likely", "Neutral", "Unlikely", "Very Unlikely".
* **How Does Chainroom Compare to Alternatives** (`comparison_alternatives`, select_one, required: false): Choose how Chainroom compares to alternative products: "Much Better", "Better", "About the Same", "Worse", "Much Worse", "Not sure".
* **Does Price Reflect Value** (`price_value`, select_one, required: true): Rate whether the price of Chainroom reflects its value: "Excellent Value", "Good Value", "Fair Value", "Overpriced", "Very Overpriced".
* **Integration with Other Tools** (`integration_quality`, select_one, required: false): Rate how well Chainroom integrates with other tools: "Excellent", "Good", "Average", "Poor", "Don't use integrations".
* **Confidence in Data Security** (`data_security_confidence`, select_one, required: true): Rate your confidence in Chainroom's data security: "Very Confident", "Confident", "Neutral", "Concerned", "Very Concerned".
* **Likelihood to Continue Using Chainroom** (`likelihood_continue`, select_one, required: true): Choose your likelihood to continue using Chainroom: "Very Likely", "Likely", "Undecided", "Unlikely", "Very Unlikely".
* **Additional Feedback or Comments** (`additional_feedback`, text, required: false): Add any other thoughts or comments about Chainroom.
