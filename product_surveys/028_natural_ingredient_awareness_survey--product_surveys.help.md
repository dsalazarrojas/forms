# naturalIngredientAwarenessSurvey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to measure consumer awareness and perceptions of natural ingredients used in products.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please answer the questions as accurately as possible based on your current knowledge and perceptions.
2.  For each question, select all the options that apply to you.
3.  If you are unsure or have no knowledge about a particular question, choose the "I do not know" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Survey** (`productsurvey`, text, required): This field asks about the product you are referring to.
* **Natural Ingredients** (`naturalingredients`, text, required): In this field, please enter the natural ingredients you are aware of in the product mentioned above.
* **Awareness Score** (`awarenessscore`, text, required): This question asks about your level of awareness of natural ingredients in the product. Choose a number from 1 (not aware) to 10 (very aware).
* **Perceived Importance** (`perceivedimportance`, text, required): Please rate the importance of natural ingredients in the product, also on a scale from 1 (not important) to 10 (very important).
* **Product Labeling** (`productlabeling`, text, required): In this field, indicate what label or certification you are familiar with regarding the product's natural ingredients.
* **Perceived Risks** (`perceivedrisks`, text, required): Select all risks you are aware of associated with natural ingredients.
* **Label Claims** (`labelclaims`, text, required): Please choose all claims you are familiar with on the product's label.
* **Perceived Sustainability** (`perceivedsustainability`, text, required): Rate your perception of the product's environmental sustainability, also on a scale from 1 (not sustainable) to 10 (very sustainable).
* **Perceived Value** (`perceivedvalue`, text, required): This question asks about your perceived value of the product's natural ingredients, also on a scale from 1 (no value) to 10 (very valuable).
* **Perceived Effectiveness** (`perceivedeffectiveness`, text, required): Rate the effectiveness of the product's natural ingredients, also on a scale from 1 (not effective) to 10 (very effective).
* **Perceived Cost** (`perceivedcost`, text, required): Choose your perceived cost of the product with natural ingredients, also on a scale from 1 (very expensive) to 10 (very cheap).
* **Perceived Safety** (`perceivedsafety`, text, required): Rate your perception of the product's natural ingredients as safe, also on a scale from 1 (not safe) to 10 (very safe).
* **Perceived Quality** (`perceivedquality`, text, required): Rate the quality of the product's natural ingredients, also on a scale from 1 (low quality) to 10 (high quality).
* **Perceived Health** (`perceivedhealth`, text, required): Rate your perception of the product's natural ingredients as beneficial to health, also on a scale from 1 (no health benefits) to 10 (very healthy).
* **Perceived Environmental Impact** (`perceivedenvironmentalimpact`, text, required): Rate the product's natural ingredients' impact on the environment, also on a scale from 1 (high negative impact) to 10 (very positive impact).
* **Perceived Social Impact** (`perceivedsocialimpact`, text, required): Rate your perception of the product's natural ingredients' social impact, also on a scale from 1 (low social impact) to 10 (very positive social impact).
* **Perceived Animal Welfare** (`perceivedanimalwelfare`, text, required): Rate your perception of the product's natural ingredients' impact on animal welfare, also on a scale from 1 (low animal welfare) to 10 (very high animal welfare).
* **Perceived Economic Impact** (`perceivedeconomicimpact`, text, required): Rate your perception of the product's natural ingredients' economic impact, also on a scale from 1 (low economic impact) to 10 (very high economic impact).
* **Label Claims Impact** (`labelclaimsimpact`, text, required): Please choose all impacts you are familiar with on the product's label claims.
* **Perceived Impact** (`perceivedimpact`, text, required): This question asks about your overall perception of the product's natural ingredients' impact, also on a scale from 1 (low impact) to 10 (very high impact).
* **Perceived Certification** (`perceivedcertification`, text, required): In this field, indicate what certification you are familiar with regarding the product's natural ingredients.
* **Perceived Certification Level** (`perceivedcertificationlevel`, text, required): Rate the level of certification you are familiar with, also on a scale from 1 (low level) to 10 (very high level).
* **Perceived Certified Claims** (`perceivedcertifiedclaims`, text, required): Please choose all certified claims you are familiar with on the product's label.
* **Perceived Certification** (`perceivedcertification`, text, required): This question asks about your understanding of the product's natural ingredients' certification, also on a scale from 1 (not certified) to 10 (very certified).
* **Perceived Certification Level** (`perceivedcertificationlevel`, text, required): Rate your perception of the level of certification, also on a scale from 1 (low level) to 10 (very high level).
* **Natural Ingredient Awareness Survey** (`naturalingredientawarenesssurvey`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all that apply to your level of awareness of natural ingredients in products.

**Tips**

* Answer questions based on your current knowledge and perception.
* If unsure, choose "I do not know" for questions that do not apply.
* Select all that apply for questions with multiple options.
* Rate questions on the provided scale to indicate your level of agreement.
