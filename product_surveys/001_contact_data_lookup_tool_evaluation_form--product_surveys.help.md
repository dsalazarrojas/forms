# Contact Data Lookup Tool Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contact Data Lookup Tool Evaluation Form is a tool designed to gather feedback and evaluations from users on the effectiveness of contact data lookup tools. This form helps vendors and internal teams to understand the tool's performance, usability, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as the evaluator.
2. Provide your business email address for follow-up purposes.
3. Enter the name of the organization or team you are evaluating the tool for.
4. Describe your role in relation to data or operations.
5. Enter the date of evaluation.
6. Briefly describe the primary use case for the lookup tool.
7. Rate the accuracy of returned contact data.
8. Estimate the percentage of successful contact matches.
9. Select the regions you tested the tool for geographic coverage.
10. Rate your satisfaction with the turnaround time of the tool.
11. Select how easy it was to connect the tool to existing systems.
12. Rate the quality of API documentation and examples.
13. Note any privacy and compliance concerns.
14. Provide a brief assessment of the tool's cost versus value.
15. State your overall recommendation for the tool.
16. Add any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Evaluator full name
* **Evaluator full name** (`evaluator_name`, text, required): Enter your full name for follow-up purposes.
### Evaluator email address
* **Evaluator email address** (`evaluator_email`, email, required): Provide a business email address for follow-up purposes.
### Organization name
* **Organization name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company or team using the lookup tool.
### Role or job title
* **Role or job title** (`role`, text, optional): Describe your role in relation to data or operations.
### Evaluation date
* **Evaluation date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of evaluation.
### Primary use case for the lookup tool
* **Primary use case** (`primary_use_case`, text, required): Briefly describe why you evaluated this tool.
### Data accuracy rating
* **Data accuracy rating** (`data_accuracy_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the accuracy of returned contact data.
### Typical match rate percentage
* **Typical match rate percentage** (`match_rate_percentage`, number, optional): Estimate the percentage of successful contact matches.
### Geographic coverage relevance
* **Geographic coverage relevance** (`coverage_scope`, select_multiple, optional): Select the regions you tested the tool for geographic coverage.
### Turnaround time satisfaction
* **Turnaround time satisfaction** (`turnaround_time_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with the turnaround time of the tool.
### Ease of integration
* **Ease of integration** (`ease_of_integration`, select_one, required): Select how easy it was to connect the tool to existing systems.
### API documentation quality
* **API documentation quality** (`api_documentation_quality`, select_one, optional): Rate the quality of API documentation and examples.
### Privacy and compliance concerns
* **Privacy and compliance concerns** (`privacy_and_compliance`, text, optional): Note any regulatory or compliance issues observed.
### Cost versus value assessment
* **Cost versus value assessment** (`cost_value_assessment`, text, optional): Provide a brief assessment of the tool's cost versus value.
### Overall recommendation
* **Overall recommendation** (`overall_recommendation`, select_one, required): State your overall recommendation for the tool.
### Additional comments or suggestions
* **Additional comments or suggestions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any other notes for vendors or internal teams.
