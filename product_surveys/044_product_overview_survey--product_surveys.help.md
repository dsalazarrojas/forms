# Product Overview Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user feedback and information about products or services to help improve them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select whether the product or service is a "Product", "Service", or "Both" to determine the type of feedback and information required.
2.  Fill out the form with the relevant sections based on your selection.
