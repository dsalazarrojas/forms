# Skincare Product Comparison Behavior Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect data on your behavior when comparing skincare products. This will help us understand how you compare and evaluate different products. You will be asked about your preferences and behaviors when choosing between two skincare products.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully.
2. Answer the questions honestly and accurately.
3. Enter the required information and select the relevant options for each question.
4. Submit the form when you're finished.
