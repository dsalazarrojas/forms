# Skincare Product Preference Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your skincare habits and preferences. It will help us understand how often you use skincare products, what types of products you use, and what factors influence your purchasing decisions. Your responses will be anonymous and used to improve our understanding of skincare habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Read the form carefully to understand the questions and options.
2. Select your answers from the provided options for each question.
3. Make sure to select all applicable answers for each question.
4. Submit your completed form to share your preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Introduction** (`form_intro`, `note`, required: false): This is an introduction to the form. It's a brief overview of what the form is about.
* **How often do you use skincare products?** (`skincare_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Answer this question by selecting one or more options that describe how often you use skincare products. Options are:
	+ Daily
	+ Several times a week
	+ Less than once a week
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **Which types of skincare products do you use?** (`product_types`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the types of skincare products you use. Options are:
	+ Moisturizing products
	+ Exfoliating products
	+ Eye care products
	+ Sunscreen
	+ Other (please specify)
* **What influences your skincare product purchase decisions?** (`product_purchase_behavior`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Answer this question by selecting one or more options that influence your purchasing decisions. Options are:
	+ Price
	+ Brand
	+ Ingredient
	+ Friend/celebrity endorsement
	+ Other
* **How do you typically apply your skincare routine?** (`routine`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the time of day you typically apply your skincare routine. Options are:
	+ Morning
	+ Nighttime
	+ Combination (morning and night)
	+ Other (please specify)
* **What factors influence your skincare product decision?** (`product_decision_factors`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Answer this question by selecting one or more options that influence your skincare product decision. Options are:
	+ Price
	+ Brand
	+ Ingredient
	+ Friend/celebrity endorsement
	+ Online reviews
	+ Other
* **What are your favorite skincare products?** (`favorite_products`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the types of skincare products you like. Options are:
	+ Moisturizing products
	+ Exfoliating products
	+ Eye care products
	+ Sunscreen
	+ Other (please specify)
* **What are your skincare goals?** (`skincare_goals`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the goals you have for your skincare routine. Options are:
	+ Anti-aging
	+ Hydration
	+ Brightening
	+ Acne treatment
	+ Other (please specify)

## Tips
* Take your time to carefully read each question and select the most accurate options.
* Make sure to select all applicable answers for each question.
* If you're unsure about any question, ask yourself what you would typically do in that situation.
* Submit your completed form to help us better understand your skincare habits.
