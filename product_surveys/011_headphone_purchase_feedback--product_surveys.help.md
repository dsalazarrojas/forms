# Headphone Purchase Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers who have purchased our headphones.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the brand of headphones you used during your purchase.
2. Choose where you purchased the headphones (online, offline, or other).
3. Enter the date of purchase.
4. Rate your satisfaction level with the purchase.
5. Provide any suggestions for improvement.
6. Check if the headphones come with a warranty.
7. Enter the price you paid for the headphones.
8. Specify the channel through which you made the purchase (retailer, direct, or online ad).
9. Choose the type of headphones you purchased (in-ears, on-ears, or earbuds).
10. Provide your feedback and ratings for the product.
11. Specify any pros and cons you experienced with the product.
12. Offer any improvement suggestions.
13. Provide your contact information.
14. Enter your email.
15. Upload a product image (if applicable).
16. Enter any proof of purchase (if applicable).
17. (Optional) Provide any additional comments.
18. (Optional) Enter any additional purchase information (if applicable).
