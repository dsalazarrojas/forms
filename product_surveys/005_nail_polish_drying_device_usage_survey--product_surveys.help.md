# Nail Polish Drying Device Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect your feedback on your experience with nail polish drying devices. Your input will help us improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the relevant answers for each question from the provided options.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the "Comments" field to provide them.
4. If you have a phone number, please enter it in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Which device do you use** (`device_type`, select_one, required: false):
    + This question asks about the type of nail polish drying device you use. Please select the option that best describes your device.
* **How satisfied are you** (`satisfaction`, number, required: false):
    + This question asks about your level of satisfaction with the device. Please rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1-10.
* **Have you experienced any issues** (`issues`, select_multiple, required: false):
    + This question asks if you have encountered any problems with the device. Please select all the issues that apply.
* **How often do you use it** (`frequency_of_use`, select_one, required: false):
    + This question asks about how frequently you use the device. Please select the option that best describes your use frequency.
* **How long do you use it** (`duration_of_use`, time, required: false):
    + This question asks about the duration of your use of the device. Please enter the time in the format "HH:MM".
* **How would you describe your experience** (`user_experience`, text, required: false):
    + This question asks for your overall experience with the device. Please provide a brief description.
* **Would you recommend it to others** (`recommended_to_others`, number, required: false):
    + This question asks about your willingness to recommend the device to others. Please rate your recommendation on a scale of 1-10.
* **Why not recommended** (`why_not_recommended`, text, required: false):
    + This question is meant to provide a brief explanation for why you would not recommend the device.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false):
    + This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you might have about the device.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false):
    + This field is for your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false):
    + This field is for your phone number.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required: false):
    + This question asks if you belong to any of the provided categories. Please select the one that applies to you.
    Please note that the provided categories are "Yes" and "No".
