# Fashion Design Preference Poll Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects information about your fashion design preferences to help us understand your opinions and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all the required fields marked as required.
2. For numerical fields like "What is your age?", enter a whole number that represents your age.
3. For select fields like "What is your favorite color?", choose one of the available options.
4. For text fields, provide a clear and concise answer to the questions asked.
