# product_durability_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the durability of a product. It's meant to help our team understand how well the product can withstand normal usage and potential external factors. Please provide your honest assessment to help us improve our products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question about your experience with the product's durability (page 1).
2. Rate the product's durability on a scale of 0-10 (page 2).
3. Describe your experience with the product (page 3).
4. Select the category that best describes your perception of the product's durability (page 4).
5. Choose up to two subcategories that are relevant to your experience (page 5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How would you rate the product's durability?** (`product_durability_feedback`, text, required): Describe your experience with the product's durability. Please answer this question in a few sentences or bullet points.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Rate the product's durability on a scale of 0 to 10. The higher the score, the more durable you perceive the product to be.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Write a brief description of your experience with the product, including any notable features or issues that may impact your assessment of its durability.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select the category that best describes your perception of the product's durability. You can choose from:
	+ Durable
	+ Non-durable
	+ Not sure
* **Subcategory** (`subcategory`, select_multiple, required): Choose up to two subcategories that are relevant to your experience with the product. You can select from:
	+ Material
	+ Design
	+ Price
Please answer these questions honestly, and provide as much detail as possible to help us improve our products.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when describing your experience with the product.
* If you're unsure about a particular aspect of the product's durability, feel free to choose "Not sure" for the category and subcategory fields.
* If you have any additional feedback or comments, please include them in the description field.
