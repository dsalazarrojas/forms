# Seasonal Collection Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seasonal Collection Evaluation Form is a tool used by product managers and team members to assess the performance of seasonal product collections. It helps track the collection's status, product ratings, and any relevant feedback or comments.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review the provided fields and select the relevant information about the seasonal collection.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are completed, if any.
3.  Fill in the form with accurate and relevant information.
4.  Review and submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Name (`product_name`, text, optional)**: Enter the name of the product being evaluated.
* **Product Rating (`product_rating`, select_one, optional)**: Choose a rating for the product from the given options (Very Good, Average, Poor, Very Poor, N/A).
* **Recommendation (`recommendation`, text, optional)**: Provide any recommendation or suggestion for the product.
* **Notes (`notes`, text, optional)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the product.
* **Email (`email`, email, optional)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (`phone`, text, optional)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Category (`category`, text, optional)**: Enter the category of the product.
* **Subcategory (`subcategory`, text, optional)**: Enter the subcategory of the product.
* **Collection Date (`collection_date`, date, optional)**: Enter the date the collection was released.
* **Collection Status (`collection_status`, select_one, optional)**: Choose the status of the collection from the given options (In Progress, Completed, N/A).
* **Team Members Involved (`team_members_involved`, text, optional)**: List the team members involved in the product's development.
* **Ratings and Comments (`ratings_and_comments`, text, optional)**: Enter any relevant feedback or comments about the product.
