# orthopaedic_shoe_market_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers about their demographic information, purchase behavior, purchasing history, product preferences, purchase decision, other relevant information, and their reasons for purchase and satisfaction with the product. This data will help us better understand our customers' needs and preferences, and improve our orthopaedic shoe offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question to the best of your ability. Fill in the information as prompted, and submit the form when you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **demographic info (1)** (demographic info, text, required: false): Provide us with your demographic information, such as your age, gender, occupation, or any other relevant details.
* **purchase behaviour (2)** (purchase behaviour, text, required: false): Describe your purchasing behavior, such as how often you buy orthopaedic shoes, your preferred payment methods, or any other relevant habits.
* **purchasing history (3)** (purchasing history, text, required: false): Share with us your previous purchasing history, including your experiences with similar products, or any other relevant information.
* **product preferences (4)** (product preferences, text, required: false): Tell us about your preferences for orthopaedic shoes, such as color, material, style, or any other features that matter to you.
* **purchase decision (5)** (purchase decision, text, required: false): Explain the process you followed to make your purchase decision, including any factors that influenced your choice.
* **other (6)** (other, text, required: false): If there's anything else you'd like to share with us, feel free to write it here.
* **purchase reason (7)** (purchase reason, text, required: false): Describe the reason(s) why you made the purchase, such as for health reasons or as a gift.
* **satisfaction (8)** (satisfaction, text, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the product, from 1 to 5, or describe your level of satisfaction in a few words.

## Tips

* Be as accurate and honest as possible when providing your answers.
* If you have trouble with a question, feel free to ask for help.
* Your input is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your participation in our survey!
