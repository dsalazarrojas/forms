# product_testing_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The product_testing_survey form is designed to collect feedback and survey data from customers on products in the testing phase. This form is intended for customers who have tested products and want to share their experiences with the development team to help improve the product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all applicable products that you have tested (page 1).
2. Enter the product name (page 2).
3. Enter your customer name (page 3).
4. Enter your email address (page 4).
5. Enter your phone number (page 5).
6. Enter any additional notes or comments (page 6).
7. Provide feedback on the product (page 7).
8. Rate the product (page 8).
9. Enter your product comment (page 9).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Survey** (`product_survey`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable products that you have tested. This field allows you to choose multiple products.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the product you are providing feedback on.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as a customer.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to contact you for further questions or clarification.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes or comments about your experience with the product.
* **Customer Feedback** (`customer_feedback`, `text`, required): Provide feedback on the product, including any suggestions or concerns.
* **Product Rating** (`product_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the product as either "Good" or "Fair".
* **Product Comment** (`product_comment`, `text`, required): Enter any additional comments about the product.
