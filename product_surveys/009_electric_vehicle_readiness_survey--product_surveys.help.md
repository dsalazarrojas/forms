# Electric Vehicle Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Electric Vehicle Readiness Survey is designed to assess your readiness to adopt an electric vehicle. The survey will gather information about your contact details, vehicle preferences, and needs for outreach and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select all the options that apply to your electric vehicle needs.
2. For each section, select one or more options from the given choices.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Contact Info (required)
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `select_multiple`, required): This field asks for your contact information to be reached out by our team about your electric vehicle needs. You can select any or all of the following: Name, Email, and Phone.

### 2. Survey Goals (required)
* **Survey Goals** (`Survey Goals`, `select_multiple`, required): In this field, select the goals you have for adopting an electric vehicle. You can choose any or all of the following: Interested in EV, Charging Feasibility, and Outreach Needs.

### 3. Outreach Plan (required)
* **Outreach Plan** (`Outreach Plan`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the types of outreach and support you need for your electric vehicle adoption. You can choose any or all of the following: Community Support, Marketing Materials, and Training Programs.

### 4. Vehicle Info (required)
* **Vehicle Info** (`Vehicle Info`, `select_multiple`, required): In this field, select your vehicle information. You can choose any or all of the following: Vehicle Type, Vehicle Model, and Vehicle Year.

### 5. Charging Info (required)
* **Charging Info** (`Charging Info`, `select_multiple`, required): Here, you can select your charging needs. You can choose any or all of the following: Charging Frequency, Charging Duration, and Charging Location.

### 6. Charging Hardware (required)
* **Charging Hardware** (`Charging Hardware`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the types of charging hardware you need for your electric vehicle. You can choose any or all of the following: Charger Type, Charger Model, and Charger Location.

### 7. Data Collection (required)
* **Data Collection** (`Data Collection`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the types of data you need to collect for your electric vehicle adoption. You can choose any or all of the following: Data Frequency, Data Duration, and Data Location.

### 8. Outreach Methods (required)
* **Outreach Methods** (`Outreach Methods`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the methods you prefer for our outreach and communication. You can choose any or all of the following: Social Media, Email Marketing, and Online Events.

### 9. Additional Comments (optional)
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, `note`, optional): You can provide any additional comments or feedback about your electric vehicle adoption needs.

### 10. Contact Person (optional)
* **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, `text`, optional): If you have any contact person, you can provide their name here.
