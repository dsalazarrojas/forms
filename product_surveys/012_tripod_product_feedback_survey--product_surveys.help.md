# Tripod Product Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tripod Product Feedback Survey form is designed to collect customer feedback about their experience with a Tripod product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and understand what information is being asked for.
2. Answer each question thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Submit the form when you are ready.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Introduction**
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, not required): This is a note field where you can provide a brief introduction to your feedback. You can explain why you are providing feedback, what product you are referring to, and any other relevant context.

### **Product ID**
* **Product ID** (`product_id`, `number`, not required): Enter the product ID number if you have it. This helps us to identify the specific product you are referring to.

### **Email**
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): This is where you enter your email address. We may contact you for further questions or to follow up on your feedback.

### **Phone**
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number if you would like us to contact you by phone.

### **Rating**
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, not required): Rate your experience with the product on a scale from 1 to 5.

### **Suggestion**
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, `text`, not required): Provide any suggestions or ideas you may have for how we can improve the product.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your feedback.
- Try to provide concrete examples or details about your experience.
- If you have any questions, you can contact us at [contact information].
