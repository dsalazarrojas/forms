# pet_care_product_feedback_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pet_care_product_feedback_request_form is designed to collect feedback from pet owners regarding pet care products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the pet care product you are providing feedback on in the "Product Name" field.
2. Select the type of product from the options provided in the "Product Type" field.
3. If you have an opinion, rate the product by entering a number in the "Product Rating" field.
4. Share your thoughts and experiences with the product in the "Feedback Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the pet care product you are providing feedback on.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of product from the options provided. The options are:
	+ Pet Food
	+ Pet Accessories
	+ Pet Toys
	+ Pet Treats
* **Product Rating** (`product_rating`, number, optional): Enter a number to rate your opinion of the product. This rating is used to give us an idea of your overall satisfaction with the product.
* **Feedback Message** (`feedback_message`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and experiences with the product. This is a place to provide personal feedback and suggestions for improvement.
