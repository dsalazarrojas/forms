# New Product Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a new product, including its launching context, target audience, and other relevant details. The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and evaluation data from users, which can be used to improve the product and better serve the target audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the correct "Product Launching As" option, which describes how the product is being launched (As a company or As a teacher).
2. Enter the name of the product in the "Product Name" field.
3. Provide a brief description of the product in the "Product Description" field.
4. Enter any feedback about the product in the "Feedback" field.
5. Select the target audience(s) for the product (Customers, Employees, Students, Teachers, or Other).
6. If Other is selected, enter the target audience in the "Other" field.
7. Choose the age group(s) of the target audience (18-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55-64, 65+).
8. If Other is selected, enter the age group in the "Age Group Other" field.
9. Select the gender of the target audience (Male, Female, Other).
10. Choose the education level of the target audience (High school, Some college, College, University, Doctoral, Postgraduate).
11. Enter a product rating (1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest).
12. Select whether the product is recommended or not (True or False).
13. Enter any comments about the product.
14. Enter contact information (e.g., email, phone number).
15. Enter the company and job title of the person filling out the form.
16. Enter the department and location of the company.
17. Enter the job function of the person filling out the form.
18. Select whether the product is launched as a company or a teacher (As a company or As a teacher).
19. Enter the name of the product (if different from the original product).
20. Enter a description of the product (if different from the original).
21. Enter feedback about the product (if any).
22. Select the target audience for the product (Customers, Employees, Students, Teachers).
23. Enter any additional contact information (e.g., email, phone number).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Launching As** (id 1, select_one): This field describes how the product is being launched. Select As a company or As a teacher to indicate the context in which the product is being launched.
* **Product Name** (id 2, text): Enter the name of the product, which should be a clear and concise description of the product.
* **Product Description** (id 3, text): Provide a brief description of the product, including its features and benefits.
* **Feedback** (id 4, text): Share any feedback or comments about the product, including both positive and negative experiences.
* **Category** (id 5, select_multiple): Select the category in which the product belongs (Product, Service, Other).
* **Target Audience** (id 6, select_one): Choose the target audience for the product (Customers, Employees, Students, Teachers).
* **Other** (id 7, text): If Other is selected as the target audience, enter the specific audience in this field.
* **Age Group** (id 8, select_multiple): Select the age group of the target audience (18-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55-64, 65+).
* **Age Group Other** (id 9, text): If Other is selected as the age group, enter the specific age group in this field.
* **Gender** (id 10, select_one): Select the gender of the target audience (Male, Female, Other).
* **Education Level** (id 11, select_multiple): Choose the education level of the target audience (High school, Some college, College, University, Doctoral, Postgraduate).
* **Product Rating** (id 12, number): Enter a rating of the product, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **Recommended** (id 13, select_one): Select whether the product is recommended or not (True or False).
* **Comments** (id 14, text): Enter any comments or additional feedback about the product.
* **Contact Information** (id 15, text): Enter any contact information (e.g., email, phone number).
* **Email** (id 16, email): Enter the email address of the person filling out the form.
* **Company** (id 17, text): Enter the name of the company.
* **Job Title** (id 18, text): Enter the job title of the person filling out the form.
* **Department** (id 19, text): Enter the department of the company.
* **Location** (id 20, text): Enter the location of the company.
* **Job Function** (id 21, text): Enter the job function of the person filling out the form.
* **Product Launching As 2** (id 22, select_one): Select whether the product is launched as a company or a teacher.
* **Product Name 2** (id 23, text): Enter the name of the second product (if different from the original).
* **Product Description 2** (id 24, text): Enter a description of the second product (if different from the original).
* **Feedback 2** (id 25, text): Enter any feedback about the second product.
* **Target Audience 2** (id 26, select_one): Select the target audience for the second product.

## Tips

* Be as accurate and honest as possible when filling out the form.
* If unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification or assistance.
* This form is designed to help gather feedback and improve the product, so your input is greatly appreciated!
