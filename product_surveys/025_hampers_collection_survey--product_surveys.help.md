# Hampers Collection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hampers Collection Survey form is designed to collect information about hampers, a type of container used to carry or store items, from customers or clients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question thoroughly and accurately to provide complete and relevant information.
2. Select the correct options for each field as per your preference.
3. For fields with dropdown options, select the one that best matches your choice.
4. For text fields, type in your response clearly and concisely.
5. Review your responses before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
