# Product Quality Score Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Quality Score Request Form is designed to collect product quality scores from customers to provide valuable feedback for product improvement and development.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Select the Product Type
- **Product Type**: This field allows you to select the type of product you are rating. Select all relevant categories that apply to your product. You can choose one or more of the following options:
    - Mobile
    - Laptops
    - Tablets
    - Desktop
    - Wearable
    - Gaming Console

### Step 2: Choose Your Rating
- **Product Rating**: This field asks you to rate your experience with the product on a scale from Excellent to Bad. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
    - Excellent
    - Good
    - Fair
    - Poor
    - Bad

### Step 3: Enter Your Details
- **Name**: Please provide your name so we can contact you if needed.
- **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your feedback.
- **Contact Number**: This is an optional field for you to provide your contact number.

### Step 4: Provide Additional Feedback
- **Product Comment**: If you have any additional comments or suggestions about the product, please provide them here.
- **Rating Frequency**: If you would like to provide more feedback about your experience with the product, please let us know how often you rate products.

### Step 5: Select Your Operating System
- **Operating System**: Please select the operating system you are using the product with.
    - Windows
    - Mac
    - Android

### Step 6: Confirm Your Purchase Date (Optional)
- **Purchase Date**: If you are providing feedback for a specific product purchase, please enter the date you made the purchase.

### Step 7: Submit Your Form
Once you have completed all the above steps, click the submit button to send us your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Quality Score**: This field is not visible as it is a hidden field.
* **Product Rating**: This field is used to rate your experience with the product.
* **Name**: This field is required to gather your name for contact purposes.
* **Email**: This field is required to gather your email address for contact purposes.
* **Company**: This is an optional field for you to provide your company information.
* **Contact Number**: This is an optional field for you to provide your contact number.
* **Product Type**: This field is used to select the type of product you are rating.
* **Operating System**: This field is used to select the operating system you are using the product with.
* **Purchase Date**: This is an optional field for you to provide the date you made the purchase.
* **Rating Frequency**: This field is used to let us know how often you rate products.
* **Comment**: This field is used to gather your comments and suggestions about the product.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Be as specific and honest as possible when providing your feedback.
* If you are not sure about an option, choose the one that best applies to your experience.
