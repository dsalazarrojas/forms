# standing_desk_market_research_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather market research data on standing desks, including adoption rates, market trends, and user demographics. Your input will help us better understand the market and inform our product development and marketing strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and answer all questions accurately.
2. Select the most relevant options for each question.
3. Fill out all required and optional fields as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction**: This section provides an optional note-taking space for you to briefly introduce yourself and explain any context or background information that may be relevant to your answers.
- **Standing Desk Adopters**: Select all that apply to indicate whether you adopt or plan to adopt a standing desk. This includes "Yes" if you currently use a standing desk, "No" if you don't, and "Plan" if you plan to adopt one in the future.
- **Preferred Height**: Enter your preferred height for a standing desk, measured in inches. This will help us understand your ergonomic needs.
- **Preferred Height Units**: Select "Yes" if you prefer your standing desk height to be measured in units other than the standard metric (e.g., feet and inches, etc.).
- **Buying Intent**: Indicate whether you plan to buy a standing desk. Select "Yes" if you plan to purchase a standing desk, "No" if you don't, or "Other" if you have other plans.
- **Buying Intent Adopters**: Select all that apply to indicate whether you have adopted or plan to adopt a standing desk for yourself or others.
- **Work from Home**: Enter the number of hours you typically work from home. This will help us understand how you use your standing desk in your work environment.
- **Standing Desk Purpose**: Select "Yes" if you use your standing desk for a specific purpose, such as improving your posture or increasing productivity.
- **Standing Desk Features**: Select all that apply to indicate which features you consider important in a standing desk, such as adjustable height, ergonomic design, or built-in monitor risers.
- **Standing Desk Ergonomics**: Select all that apply to indicate the ergonomic priorities you consider when choosing a standing desk, such as comfort, ease of use, or adjustability.
- **Standing Desk Ergonomic Priorities**: Select all that apply to indicate the priorities you consider when designing an ergonomic standing desk, such as comfort, adjustability, or user experience.
- **Standing Desk Adoption Rate**: Enter the number of users you estimate have adopted standing desks in your organization or community.
- **Recommended Height**: Enter your recommended height for a standing desk based on your experience and expertise.
- **Standing Desk Market Trends**: This section is for optional notes and observations on market trends related to standing desks.
- **Survey Completion Rate**: Select "Good" if you found the survey easy to complete, "Fair" if it was somewhat challenging, or "Hard" if it was very challenging.
- **Time Spent on Survey**: Enter the amount of time you spent completing the survey.
- **Survey Completion Frequency**: Select "Yes" if you plan to complete this survey again in the future.
- **Survey Length**: Enter the length of time you estimate it took to complete the survey.
- **Survey Purpose**: Select "Yes" if you took the survey to inform a specific decision or purpose.
- **Demographics**: Select all that apply to indicate your demographics, such as age group, income group, or location.
- **Age Group**: Enter your age group (e.g., 20-30, 40-50, etc.).
- **Income Group**: Enter your income group (e.g., 0-20k, 50k-100k, etc.).
- **Location**: Enter your current location or where you work from home.
- **Industry**: Enter your industry or field of work.
- **Company**: Enter the name and size of your company.
- **Contact Information**: Enter any additional contact information you'd like to share.
