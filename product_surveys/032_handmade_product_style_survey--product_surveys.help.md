# Handmade Product Style Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the style and characteristics of handmade products to ensure accurate labeling, categorization, and customer communication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of product that best describes your handmade item: Handmade Jewelry, Handmade Home Decor, Handmade Clothing, Handmade Shoes, or Handmade Accessories.
2. Choose the materials used to create your product: Natural Fiber, Synthetic Fiber, Recycled Materials, or Recycled Paper and Glass.
3. Select the colors associated with your product: Black, White, Red, Blue, or Green.
4. Choose the product collection it belongs to from the list: Collection 1, Collection 2, or Collection 3.
5. Enter the collection date (mm/dd/yyyy) and collection time (12:00 am) for the product.
6. Provide a brief description of the product in the text field.
7. Enter a unique email address associated with the product.
8. Enter a unique name or code for the product's collection.
9. Enter a unique number for the product's collection.
10. Enter a brief description of the product's collection.
11. Enter a brief description of the product's collection.
12. Enter a brief description of the product's collection.
