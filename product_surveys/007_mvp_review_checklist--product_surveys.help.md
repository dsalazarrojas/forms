# mvp_review_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The MVP Review Checklist is a form designed to gather user feedback and reviews on the product or service. The purpose of this form is to help the product team make data-driven decisions and improve the product's usability, feature fit, and overall user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section of the form carefully and answer the questions according to your honest opinions.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all the options that apply to you.
3. For written feedback, provide clear and concise answers.
4. For product experience questions, rate the product experience from 1 to 5.
