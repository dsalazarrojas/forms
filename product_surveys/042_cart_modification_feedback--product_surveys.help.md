# Cart Modification Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather your feedback on your experience with our cart modification feature. Your input will help us understand how you use the cart and identify areas where we can improve to make your shopping experience more convenient and enjoyable.

## How To Complete This Form
Please answer the questions in the form, providing as much detail as you need to answer each question. You can use the space provided for any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Role**: Please select the best description of your role or perspective when interacting with our cart modification feature.
	+ Individual Shopper: You are a typical customer using our platform for personal shopping.
	+ Fleet Manager: You are a business user managing a fleet of users or orders.
	+ Event Coordinator: You are an event planner or organizer using our platform for event-related activities.
	+ Other: Please describe your role in the comments section.
* **How often do you modify your cart?**: Please select how often you modify your cart during a typical shopping session.
	+ Daily: You frequently add or remove items from your cart.
	+ Weekly: You modify your cart on a weekly basis.
	+ Monthly: You rarely modify your cart.
	+ Rarely: You almost never modify your cart.
* **Primary device used for modification**: Please select the platform or device you primarily use to interact with our cart modification feature.
	+ Desktop: You use a computer for cart modifications.
	+ Tablet: You use a tablet for cart modifications.
	+ Mobile App: You use our mobile app for cart modifications.
	+ Mobile Browser: You use a mobile browser for cart modifications.
