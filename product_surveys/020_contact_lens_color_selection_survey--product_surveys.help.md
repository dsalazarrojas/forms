# Contact Lens Color Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information on your preferences and demographics to help us better understand your needs and interests in contact lens colors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select all that apply for questions that have multiple options.
3. Be honest and provide as much detail as you're comfortable sharing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text Box, Optional): Your full name.
* **Age Group** (Select One, Required): Select your age range.
	+ Under 18: You are under 18 years old.
	+ 18-24: You are between 18-24 years old.
	+ 25-34: You are between 25-34 years old.
	+ 35-44: You are between 35-44 years old.
	+ 45-54: You are between 45-54 years old.
	+ 55-64: You are between 55-64 years old.
	+ 65 or older: You are 65 years old or older.
* **Gender** (Select One, Optional): Select your gender.
	+ Male: You are male.
	+ Female: You are female.
	+ Non-binary: You identify as non-binary.
	+ Prefer not to say: You do not want to share your gender.
* **Do You Currently Wear Contact Lenses?** (Select One, Required): Select your current lens usage.
	+ Yes - Daily: You wear contact lenses daily.
	+ Yes - Occasionally: You wear contact lenses occasionally.
	+ No - But Interested: You are interested in wearing contact lenses but do not currently.
	+ No - Not Interested: You do not want to wear contact lenses.
* **Type of Lenses You Use** (Select One, Optional): If applicable, select the type of contact lenses you currently use.
	+ Daily Disposable: You use daily disposable contact lenses.
	+ Bi-weekly: You use bi-weekly contact lenses.
	+ Monthly: You use monthly contact lenses.
	+ Yearly: You use yearly contact lenses.
	+ Not Applicable: You do not currently use contact lenses.
* **Preferred Lens Color Category** (Select One, Required): Select the category of colored lens colors you prefer.
	+ Natural Colors: You prefer natural-looking colors.
	+ Enhancement Tints: You prefer colors that enhance your natural eye color.
	+ Vibrant Colors: You prefer bold, vibrant colors.
	+ Special Effects: You prefer unique, special-effect colors.
	+ No Preference: You do not have a preference.
* **Specific Colors of Interest** (Select Multiple, Required): Select all the specific colored lens colors that interest you.
	+ Blue: You are interested in blue-colored lenses.
	+ Green: You are interested in green-colored lenses.
	+ Hazel: You are interested in hazel-colored lenses.
	+ Gray: You are interested in gray-colored lenses.
	+ Brown: You are interested in brown-colored lenses.
	+ Violet: You are interested in violet-colored lenses.
	+ Turquoise: You are interested in turquoise-colored lenses.
	+ Honey: You are interested in honey-colored lenses.
* **Your Natural Eye Color** (Select One, Required): Select your natural eye color.
	+ Brown: You have brown eyes.
	+ Blue: You have blue eyes.
	+ Green: You have green eyes.
	+ Hazel: You have hazel eyes.
	+ Gray: You have gray eyes.
	+ Other: You have a different eye color.
* **Reason for Wanting Color Change** (Select Multiple, Required): Select all the reasons you want to change your eye color.
	+ Enhance Natural Color: You want to enhance your natural eye color.
	+ Dramatic Change: You want a bold, eye-catching change.
	+ Special Occasion: You want to change your eye color for a special occasion.
	+ Fashion Statement: You want to make a fashion statement with your eye color.
	+ Curiosity: You are curious about colored lenses and want to try something new.
	+ Costume or Cosplay: You want to wear colored lenses for a costume or cosplay event.
* **How Often Would You Purchase** (Select One, Optional): Select how often you would purchase colored lenses.
	+ Monthly: You would purchase contact lenses monthly.
	+ Quarterly: You would purchase contact lenses quarterly.
	+ Twice a Year: You would purchase contact lenses twice a year.
	+ Once a Year: You would purchase contact lenses once a year.
	+ Only for Special Occasions: You would only purchase contact lenses for special occasions.
* **Price Range Willing to Pay** (Select One, Required): Select the price range you are willing to pay per box of lenses.
	+ Under $30: You are willing to pay under $30 per box of lenses.
	+ $30-$50: You are willing to pay between $30-$50 per box of lenses.
	+ $51-$75: You are willing to pay between $51-$75 per box of lenses.
	+ $76-$100: You are willing to pay between $76-$100 per box of lenses.
	+ Over $100: You are willing to pay over $100 per box of lenses.
* **How Important Is Brand Name** (Select One, Required): Select how important brand name is to you.
	+ Very Important: Brand name is very important to you.
	+ Somewhat Important: Brand name is somewhat important to you.
	+ Neutral: Brand name is not very important to you.
	+ Not Very Important: Brand name is not very important to you.
	+ Not Important: Brand name is not important to you at all.
* **Where Would You Purchase** (Select Multiple, Required): Select all the places where you would purchase colored lenses.
	+ Eye Doctor Office: You would purchase contact lenses from an eye doctor's office.
	+ Online Retailer: You would purchase contact lenses from an online retailer.
	+ Optical Store: You would purchase contact lenses from an optical store.
	+ Pharmacy: You would purchase contact lenses from a pharmacy.
	+ Department Store: You would purchase contact lenses from a department store.
* **Any Concerns About Colored Lenses** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all concerns you have about colored lenses.
	+ Eye Health: You are concerned about the impact on eye health.
	+ Comfort: You are concerned about the comfort of colored lenses.
	+ Natural Appearance: You are concerned about how natural-colored lenses look like your natural eye color.
	+ Cost: You are concerned about the cost of colored lenses.
	+ Maintenance: You are concerned about the maintenance and upkeep of colored lenses.
	+ None: You do not have any concerns.
* **Additional Comments** (Text Box, Optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments about colored lenses.
