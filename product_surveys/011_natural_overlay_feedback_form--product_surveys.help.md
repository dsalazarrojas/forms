# Natural Overlay Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from users about the natural overlay feature. The natural overlay feature is used to display information and provide functionality on top of other apps. This form is intended to help us understand your experience and improve the natural overlay for a better user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in your rating of the natural overlay feature (page 1).
*   Select the product you were using the natural overlay on (page 2).
*   Choose the device you were using when you encountered an issue with the natural overlay (page 3).
*   Select any issues you encountered with the natural overlay (page 4).
*   Provide any additional comments or feedback (page 5).
*   Enter your email address (page 6).
*   Provide your phone number (page 7).
*   Rate your satisfaction with the natural overlay (page 8).
*   Have any suggestions to improve the natural overlay (page 9).
*   If you experienced an issue, provide more details about it (page 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. How would you rate the natural overlay feature? (required)
*   Rate the natural overlay feature on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.

### 2. What product was the natural overlay used on? (required)
*   Please select the product you were using the natural overlay on.

### 3. What device were you on when using the natural overlay? (optional)
*   Choose the device you were using when you encountered an issue with the natural overlay.
    *   Desktop
    *   Mobile
    *   Web

### 4. What issue did you encounter with the natural overlay? (required)
*   Select any issues you experienced with the natural overlay.
    *   It was difficult to find where to click
    *   It was difficult to understand the natural overlay
    *   It was a minor issue

### 5. Additional comments or feedback (optional)
*   Provide any additional comments or feedback about the natural overlay.

### 6. Email (required)
*   Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.

### 7. Phone (optional)
*   Please provide your phone number if you'd like us to contact you.

### 8. How satisfied are you with the natural overlay? (required)
*   Rate your satisfaction with the natural overlay on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.

### 9. Have any suggestions to improve the natural overlay? (optional)
*   Choose any suggestions you have for improving the natural overlay.
    *   Increase the font size
    *   Change the natural overlay to always be on top of other apps

### 10. Provide more details about the issue (optional)
*   If you experienced an issue, please provide more details about it.
