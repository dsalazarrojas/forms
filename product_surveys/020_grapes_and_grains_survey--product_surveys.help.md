# Grapes And Grains Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grapes And Grains Survey is a collection of questions designed to help us understand your opinions and preferences on different types of beverages, specifically grapes and grains. Your input will help us better serve you and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the options that best fit your preferences for each field.
2. You can skip any fields that you do not feel comfortable answering.
3. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "how_do_you_like_your_beverage" and "how_do_you_pronounce_your_fav_beverage" fields if you would like to provide more detailed feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer_preferences** (`customer_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all that apply to your preferences.
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **favorite_beverage** (`favorite_beverage`, select_one, optional): Choose your favorite type of beverage.
	+ Grapes
	+ Grains
* **wine_type** (`wine_type`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred type of wine.
	+ White
	+ Red
	+ Rosé
	+ Sparkling
	+ Fortified
* **beer_type** (`beer_type`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred type of beer.
	+ Lager
	+ Ale
	+ IPA
	+ Stout
	+ Sour
	+ Wheat
	+ Seltzer
* **favorite_wine_color** (`favorite_wine_color`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred color of wine.
	+ Light
	+ Dark
	+ Red
	+ White
	+ Pink
* **favorite_beer_color** (`favorite_beer_color`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred color of beer.
	+ Light
	+ Dark
	+ Amber
	+ Gold
* **favorite_seltzer_color** (`favorite_seltzer_color`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred color of seltzer.
	+ Clear
	+ Light
	+ Dark
* **how_do_you_prefer_your_beverage** (`how_do_you_prefer_your_beverage`, note, optional): If you have any comments or preferences, please enter them here.
* **how_do_you_like_your_beverage** (`how_do_you_like_your_beverage`, text, optional): Enter your preferred way to enjoy your beverage.
* **how_do_you_pronounce_your_fav_beverage** (`how_do_you_pronounce_your_fav_beverage`, text, optional): Enter your preferred way to pronounce your favorite beverage.
