# Motorcycle User Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from motorcycle users, aiming to understand their experience and satisfaction with their motorcycle. The data collected will help the motorcycle manufacturer to improve their products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select "What's your relationship with the motorcycle?" from the provided options.
2. Rate your satisfaction with the motorcycle by selecting one of the options provided.
3. Answer "What model of motorcycle do you own?" by selecting all applicable options.
4. Enter the year of your motorcycle.
5. Select your riding frequency by choosing all applicable options.
6. Enter the number of kilometers you ride per month.
7. Select the number of hours you ride per week.
8. Choose where you ride most often.
9. Select all applicable features you value on your motorcycle.
10. Select the color of your motorcycle.
11. Choose if you are part of a riding group.
12. Answer if you have visited a dealership.
13. Rate the customer service at a dealership by selecting all applicable options.
14. Rate the parts and service quality at a dealership by selecting all applicable options.
15. Answer "Riding experience with your motorcycle?" in as much detail as possible.
16. Answer "Overall satisfaction with your motorcycle?" in as much detail as possible.
17. Select if you have recommended this motorcycle to anyone.
18. Answer "What do you think is the most expensive part of the motorcycle?" in as much detail as possible.
19. Answer "What is your perception of the motorcycle's reliability?" in as much detail as possible.
20. Answer "How much fun do you have riding this motorcycle?" in as much detail as possible.
21. Answer "How comfortable is this motorcycle?" in as much detail as possible.
22. Select the style of bike you like.
23. Rate the engine performance of your motorcycle by selecting one of the options.
24. Rate the suspension performance of your motorcycle by selecting one of the options.
25. Select "How satisfied are you with your motorcycle overall?" by choosing one of the options.
