# Luxury Watch Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Luxury Watch Order Form is designed to collect customer information and order details for luxury watches. This form helps in gathering crucial data for order processing, ensuring accurate billing and delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields (marked as required) with accurate and complete information.
2. For non-required fields, you can leave them blank or fill in if relevant.
3. Review and submit the form to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information (#1)**: Enter your name, email, and phone number for our records.
* **Watch Model (#2)**: Select the model of the luxury watch you'd like to purchase.
* **Case Type (#3)**: Choose the type of watch case you prefer.
* **Band Material (#4)**: Select the material for the watch band (e.g., leather, steel, or gold).
* **Band Type (#5)**: Choose the style of the watch band (e.g., mesh, leather, or rubber).
* **Strap Color (#6)**: Select the color of the watch strap.
* **Strap Pattern (#7)**: Choose the pattern of the watch strap (e.g., solid, patterned, or perforated).
* **Crystal Type (#8)**: Select the type of crystal or glass used on the watch face.
* **Water Resistance (#9)**: Choose the level of water resistance for the watch (e.g., splash, water, or diving).
* **Case Size (#10)**: Select the size of the watch case.
* **Watch Price (#11)**: Enter the price you're willing to pay for the watch.
* **Customer Phone Number (#12)**: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Order Comments (#13)**: Add any additional comments or requests you have for the order.
* **Order Total (#14)**: This field is for internal use; you don't need to fill it.
* **Order Date (#15)**: Enter the date you'd like to receive your order.
* **Order Time (#16)**: Enter the time you'd like to receive your order.
* **Customer Email (#17)**: Enter your email address for our records and updates.
* **Customer Notes (#18)**: Add any additional notes or information for the order.
* **Customer Address (#19)**: Enter your address for shipping purposes.
* **Customer City (#20)**: Enter the city where you'd like to receive the order.
* **Customer Country (#21)**: Select the country where you'd like to receive the order.
* **Customer State (#22)**: Enter the state or province where you'd like to receive the order.
* **Customer Postal Code (#23)**: Enter your postal or zip code for shipping purposes.
* **Customer State (#24)**: Another field for state or province (this field seems to be a duplicate).
* **Order Notes (#25)**: Add any additional notes or information for the order.

Please note that field #24 seems to be a duplicate of #22. If you meant to include a different field, please let us know for correction.
