# smart_home_feature_trust_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects user feedback on their trust and usage of smart home features, aiming to understand user concerns and preferences in relation to smart home devices.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the type of smart device you are using from the options provided.
2. Indicate your level of trust in the smart device's data privacy and security.
3. Select any concerns you have regarding data privacy and security.
4. Identify which features you are currently using on your smart home device.
5. (Optional) Leave any additional feedback or comments about your smart home experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user_id (Number, Required):** This field is used to identify the user submitting the form.
* **smart_device_type (Select, Required):** Choose the type of smart device you are currently using (e.g. Smart Speaker, Smart TV, etc.).
* **trust_level (Number, Required):** Indicate your level of trust in the smart device's data privacy and security (e.g. No worries, Worry a little, Worry some, Worry very much).
* **privacy_concerns (Select Multiple, Required):** Select any concerns you have regarding data privacy and security.
* **features_used (Select Multiple, Required):** Identify which smart home features you are currently using.
* **feedback_device (Note, Optional):** Leave any additional feedback or comments about your smart home experience.
* **additional_feedback (Text, Optional):** Enter any further thoughts or questions you'd like to share about your smart home features.
