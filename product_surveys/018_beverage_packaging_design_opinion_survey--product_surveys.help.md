# Beverage Packaging Design Opinion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather opinions and feedback from respondents on beverage packaging design, materials, and sustainability. Your input will help us better understand consumer preferences and behaviors, informing future product development and packaging design decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best represents your opinion.
3. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you like.
4. You can review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Your Name** (`name`, text, **required**): Please enter your full name.
  
- **Email Address** (`email`, email, **required**): Please enter your email address. This is used for communication purposes only.

- **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, **required**): Select your age group:
  - 18-25
  - 26-35
  - 36-45
  - 46-55
  - 56-65
  - 65+

- **How Often Do You Purchase Beverages** (`shopping_frequency`, select_one, **required**): Select how often you purchase beverages:
  - Daily
  - 3-4 times per week
  - 1-2 times per week
  - 2-4 times per month
  - Once a month or less

- **How Much Do You Like Current Beverage Packaging Designs** (`current_design_appeal`, select_one, **required**): Rate your liking for current beverage packaging designs:
  - Very much
  - Somewhat
  - Neutral
  - Not very much
  - Not at all

- **What Elements Make Packaging Appealing (Select all that apply)** (`current_packaging_elements`, select_multiple, **required**): Select all elements that make packaging appealing:
  - Color
  - Shape/design
  - Typography/font
  - Sustainability messaging
  - Brand logo
  - Product information clarity
  - Uniqueness
  - Premium feel

- **Which Beverage Packaging Design Do You Find Most Appealing** (`current_brand_preference`, text, **optional**): Enter the name of your favorite brand or design.

- **Visual Appeal of New Design** (`visual_appeal`, select_one, **required**): Rate the visual appeal of the new design:
  - Very appealing
  - Appealing
  - Neutral
  - Not very appealing
  - Not appealing at all

- **Opinion on Color Scheme** (`color_scheme`, select_one, **required**): Rate the color scheme:
  - Excellent choice
  - Good choice
  - Acceptable
  - Could be better
  - Poor choice

- **Typography and Text Readability** (`typography`, select_one, **required**): Rate the typography and text readability:
  - Very readable
  - Readable
  - Adequate
  - Difficult to read
  - Very difficult to read

- **Does This Design Stand Out From Competitors** (`uniqueness`, select_one, **required**): Rate how distinctive the design is:
  - Yes, very distinctive
  - Yes, somewhat distinctive
  - Similar to others
  - Less distinctive
  - Not distinctive

- **Does the Design Feel Modern or Updated** (`modern_perception`, select_one, **required**): Rate how modern the design feels:
  - Very modern
  - Modern
  - Neutral
  - Outdated
  - Very outdated

- **Preferred Packaging Material** (`material_preference`, select_one, **required**): Select your preferred packaging material:
  - Plastic bottle
  - Glass bottle
  - Aluminum can
  - Carton/box
  - Sustainable/eco-friendly
  - No preference

- **How Important is Eco-Friendly Packaging** (`sustainability_important`, select_one, **required**): Rate the importance of eco-friendly packaging:
  - Very important
  - Important
  - Somewhat important
  - Not very important
  - Not important

- **Is the Packaging Perceived as Recyclable** (`recyclable_perception`, select_one, **required**): Rate how recyclable the packaging is perceived:
  - Yes, very much
  - Yes, probably
  - Uncertain
  - Probably not
  - Not at all

- **Would This Packaging Influence Your Purchase Decision** (`purchase_likelihood`, select_one, **required**): Rate the influence of packaging on purchase decision:
  - Definitely yes
  - Probably yes
  - Maybe
  - Probably not
  - Definitely not

- **Does This Packaging Suggest Premium Quality** (`price_perception`, select_one, **required**): Rate the premium quality perception:
  - Very much
  - Somewhat
  - Neutral
  - Not really
  - Not at all

- **Does Packaging Represent Good Value for Money** (`value_perception`, select_one, **required**): Rate the value for money perception:
  - Yes, great value
  - Good value
  - Fair value
  - Poor value
  - Very poor value

- **What Are the Strengths of This Design** (`strengths`, text, **required**): Enter the strengths of this design.

- **What Could Be Improved** (`weaknesses`, text, **required**): Enter areas for improvement.

- **What Specific Changes Would You Recommend** (`specific_changes`, text, **optional**): Enter concrete suggestions for improvement.

- **Additional Comments or Observations** (`additional_comments`, text, **optional**): Enter any other thoughts or comments.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
- For select-all-that-apply questions, select all applicable items.
- You can review your answers before submitting the form.
