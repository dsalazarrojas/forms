# food_delivery_app_usability_inquiry_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The food_delivery_app_usability_inquiry_form is designed to collect user feedback and improve the usability of our food delivery app. Please take a few minutes to complete the form, and your input will help us make our app more convenient and enjoyable for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your general information in the first field.
2. Select all that apply in the second field to provide feedback on the app's usability.
3. Use the third field to add any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
4. Enter your email address in the fourth field so we can get in touch with you.
5. Enter your phone number in the fifth field.
6. Select the category that best describes your experience with the app in the sixth field.
7. Rate the app's usability on a scale of 1-5 in the seventh field.
8. Finally, use the last field to add any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information (Page 1)** (`general_info`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide any general information about yourself, such as your name, contact details, or any other relevant context for your feedback.
* **App Usability (Page 2)** (`app_usability`, select_multiple, required: false): This field allows you to select all that apply from the list of options provided. Please choose the options that best describe your experience with the app.
* **Feedback (Page 3)** (`feedback`, note, required: false): This field is for you to add any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the app. Use this space to provide more detailed feedback about your experience.
* **Email (Page 4)** (`email`, email, required: false): This field is for you to enter your email address so we can get in touch with you about your feedback.
* **Phone Number (Page 5)** (`phone`, text, required: false): This field is for you to enter your phone number, in case we need to contact you to follow up on your feedback.
* **Category (Page 6)** (`category`, select_one, required: false): This field is for you to select the category that best describes your experience with the app.
* **Rating (Page 7)** (`rating`, number, required: false): This field is for you to rate the app's usability on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments (Page 8)** (`comments`, note, required: false): This field is for you to add any additional comments or feedback you may have about the app.
