# Menu Item Preference Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form, "Menu Item Preference Survey," is used to gather information about customers' preferences and opinions on menu items. It helps us understand their preferences on various aspects of menu items, such as their frequency of consumption, preference levels, and price ranges, which will be used to enhance our menu offerings and customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select all that apply from the options provided for each field.
* For fields with a dropdown menu, choose the options that best match your preferences.
* For fields without a dropdown menu, enter your answer in the text field.
* Make sure to fill out all fields, as they are all optional (except for none, as per the form's configuration).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Menu Item Preferred** (`Menu Item Preferred`, select_multiple, required/optional: optional)
  This field asks about your preferred menu item. Select all the options that you like.
* **Item Type** (`Item Type`, select_one, required/optional: optional)
  Please choose the type of menu item you are answering questions about.
* **Frequency Eaten** (`Frequency Eaten`, number, required/optional: false)
  This field is currently not being used (as it's not defined in the options).
* **Preference** (`Preference`, number, required/optional: false)
  This field is also not being used (as it's not defined in the options).
* **Rating** (`Rating`, number, required/optional: false)
  You can rate your preference for the menu item on a scale of 1-10.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, required/optional: false)
  Enter any additional comments about the menu item.
* **Menu Item ID** (`Menu Item ID`, text, required/optional: false)
  Enter the menu item ID as it appears on our menu.
* **Category** (`Category`, select_multiple, required/optional: false)
  Select all categories that apply.
* **Price Range** (`Price Range`, number, required/optional: false)
  This field is not clearly defined in the options, so assume it's asking about a specific price range.
* **Customer Feedback** (`Customer Feedback`, text, required/optional: false)
  Share your thoughts about your experience with the menu item.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* Try to answer all fields, even if you're not certain or unsure.
* If you have any questions, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* We appreciate your feedback and help in improving our menu offerings!
