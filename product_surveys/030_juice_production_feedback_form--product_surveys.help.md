# Juice Production Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from end users on the juice production process. Your input will help us improve the quality and production of our juice products.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Read through each question carefully** to ensure you answer all required fields.
2. **Fill out the form with your feedback** and rating for the selected product.
3. **Submit your feedback** and contact information at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name (<name>):** The name of the juice product you are providing feedback on.
	+ Please type the name of the product as it appears on the label or packaging.
* **Product Information (<name>):** Please provide additional information about the product you are rating.
	+ This could include details such as the specific variant, flavor, or batch number.
* **Product Rating (<name>):** Rate your overall satisfaction with the product.
	+ Use the scale provided in the **Production Quality** field to guide your rating.
* **Production Quality (<name>):**
	+ Select the quality level that best describes your experience with the product:
		- Good: The product meets or exceeds your expectations.
		- Fair: The product is average or mediocre.
		- Poor: The product falls short of your expectations.
* **Feedback (<name>):** Share any additional comments or suggestions about the product.
	+ This is your chance to provide detailed feedback on the product's taste, texture, or any other aspect you'd like to comment on.
* **Contact Information (<name>):** Enter your contact details so we can follow up with you.
	+ Please enter your email address or other contact information to help us reach out to you for further discussion or clarification on your feedback.
