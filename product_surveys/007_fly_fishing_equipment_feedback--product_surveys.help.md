# Fly Fishing Equipment Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their fly fishing equipment experiences. The goal is to gather information about their satisfaction, the condition and type of gear used, and any issues they encountered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the brand of the fly fishing equipment you are providing feedback on.
2. Choose the specific product you are providing feedback on.
3. Select the type of gear you used (if you used multiple types of gear, select all that apply).
4. Choose the condition of the gear you used (Very Good, Good, Fair, or Bad).
5. Indicate if you encountered any issues with the gear (Yes, No, or Unknown).
6. Provide any additional comments about your experience (optional).
7. Rate your satisfaction with the product (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Brand** (Select One, Required): Choose the brand of the fly fishing equipment you are providing feedback on. The options are:
	+ Orvis
	+ Simms
	+ Other
* **Product** (Select One, Required): Choose the specific product you are providing feedback on. The options are:
	+ Rod
	+ Reel
	+ Fishing Waders
	+ Other
* **Gear Type** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the types of gear you used during your experience. You may select multiple options. The options are:
	+ Rod
	+ Reel
	+ Fishing Waders
* **Gear Condition** (Select One, Required): Choose the condition of the gear you used. The options are:
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Bad
	+ Unknown
* **Issue** (Select One, Required): Indicate if you encountered any issues with the gear during your experience. The options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Unknown
* **Comment** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments or details about your experience.
* **How Satisfied Are You?** (Select One, Required): Rate your satisfaction with the product. The options are:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
