# Wheel Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and opinions from users about a product. It's meant to collect information on the product's overall impression, quality, price, and features, as well as any suggestions or additional feedback the user may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the most relevant options for each field, based on your experience with the product.
3. Fill out all required fields, denoted by an asterisk (\*) next to the field label.
4. Leave optional fields blank if you're unsure or have no opinion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Impression** (`page_1`, `note`, required: false): This field is a free text area where you can describe your overall impression of the product. It's not required, but feel free to share your thoughts if you have any.
* **Product Quality** (`page_2`, `select_one`, required: true): Select one option from the dropdown menu to rate the product's quality. Choose from Excellent, Good, or another option that best describes your opinion.
* **Satisfaction** (`page_3`, `select_one`, required: false): This field allows you to rate your overall satisfaction with the product. Select one of the options: Excellent, Good, or Neutral.
* **Price** (`page_4`, `number`, required: true): Please enter a numerical value to indicate your opinion about the product's price. This could be an estimate or a comparison to similar products.
* **Helpful Features** (`page_5`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field allows you to select all features from the options that you found helpful during your interaction with the product. Select as many or as few as you like.
* **Page 6** (`page_6`, `text`, required: false): This is a free text area for any additional comments or feedback you may have about the product.
* **Unhelpful Features** (`page_7`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Similar to Helpful Features, select all features from the options that you found unhelpful during your interaction with the product.
* **Suggestions** (`page_8`, `note`, required: false): Share any suggestions or ideas you have for improving the product in this free text area.
* **Repurchase** (`page_9`, `select_one`, required: true): Select one option from the dropdown menu to indicate your likelihood of repurchasing the product. Choose from Excellent, Good, or another option that best describes your opinion.
* **Additional Feedback** (`page_10`, `text`, required: false): This field is for any additional thoughts or comments you may have about the product. It's not required, but feel free to share your thoughts.
