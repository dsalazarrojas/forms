# Digital Campaign Purchase Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information about customer purchases for the purpose of improving our digital marketing campaigns. Your responses will help us understand how our customers are making purchases and what we can do to better serve them.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the purchase channel(s) that applied to your recent purchase.
2. Choose how satisfied you were with your purchase.
3. If you made your purchase online, please enter the date and time of the purchase.
4. Enter the amount you spent on the purchase.
5. Choose how much you spent on the purchase (Low, Medium, or High).
6. Share any feedback you have about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Purchase Channel**: Select the channel(s) through which you made your purchase (Online, In-store, or Other. If you selected "Other", please specify in the "Feedback" field below).
* **Satisfaction**: Rate your level of satisfaction with the purchase (Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied).
* **Purchase Date**: If you made your purchase online, enter the date of the purchase in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This field is only required if you selected "Online" as the purchase channel.
* **Purchase Time**: If you made your purchase online, enter the time of the purchase in the format HH:MM AM/PM. This field is only required if you selected "Online" as the purchase channel.
* **Purchase Amount**: Enter the total amount you spent on the purchase.
* **Spend**: Choose how much you spent on the purchase (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Feedback**: Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience. If you selected "Other" as the purchase channel, please provide more details here.
