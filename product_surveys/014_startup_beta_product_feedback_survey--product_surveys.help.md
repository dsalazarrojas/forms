# Startup Beta Product Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather your feedback about the startup beta product you've tested. Your input will help us make the product more user-friendly, efficient, and reliable. Please take a few minutes to answer these questions to help us understand your experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide your feedback, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in each section, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For the "category" field, choose the most relevant category that best describes your product feedback.
3. If you're providing feedback about a specific tool or feature, type its name in the "assigned tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Title (1)
* **Startup Beta Product Feedback Survey** (`form_title`, text, not required): This is the title of the survey, which is already filled in for your convenience.

### Category (2)
* **Category** (`form_category`, select_one, not required): Choose the category that best describes your feedback. Options are:
	+ Product Surveys
	+ Other

### Description (3)
* **Description** (`form_description`, text, not required): Share your thoughts about the product, including any concerns or suggestions you have.

### Category (4)
* **Category** (`form_category`, select_multiple, not required): Similar to the first category field, choose the categories that best describe your feedback. Select all that apply.
+ Product Surveys
+ Other

### Output File (5)
* **Output File** (`form_output_file`, text, not required): If you'd like to provide an output file related to your feedback, such as a screenshot or a document, upload it here.

### Assigned Tool (6)
* **Assigned Tool** (`form_assigned_tool`, text, not required): If you're providing feedback about a specific tool or feature, type its name in this field.

### Required (7)
* **Required** (`form_required`, select_one, not required): Indicate whether the product is required for your work. Options are:
	+ true
	+ false

### Description 1 (8)
* **Description 1** (`form_description_1`, note, not required): This is a note field where you can provide any additional details about the product.

### Satisfaction (9)
* **Satisfaction** (`form_satisfaction`, select_one, not required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the product. Options are:
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Bad

## Tips
* Please provide clear and concise answers to help us better understand your feedback.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, feel free to add them in the "Description" field.
* Your input is valuable, and we appreciate your participation in helping us improve the product.
