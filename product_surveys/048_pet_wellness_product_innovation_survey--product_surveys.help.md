# Pet Wellness Product Innovation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ideas from pet owners on potential product innovations for pet wellness products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide basic information about yourself as a pet owner.
2. Answer questions about your current experiences with pet wellness products.
3. Share your thoughts on what features you would like to see in future pet wellness products.
4. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
5. Enter your contact information so we can follow up with you.
6. Answer questions about your involvement with veterinarians for pet care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **pet_owner_info** (`pet_owner_info`, `text`, optional): This field is intended for you to provide basic information about yourself as a pet owner, such as your name, address, and other contact details.
* **current_product_features** (`current_product_features`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select all the features that you currently use in your pet wellness products. This will help us understand what features you value most in pet wellness products.
* **desired_features** (`desired_features`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the features that you would like to see in future pet wellness products.
* **innovation_interest** (`innovation_interest`, `select_one`, optional): Please indicate whether you are interested in innovative features for pet wellness products.
* **open_ended** (`open_ended`, `text`, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about pet wellness products.
* **additional_comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you may have about pet wellness products.
* **contact_info** (`contact_info`, `select_one`, optional): Please select whether you would like to be contacted for follow-up regarding your feedback.
* **product_rating** (`product_rating`, `number`, optional): Rate your current pet wellness product experience on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number so we can follow up with you if needed.
* **veterinarian_involvement** (`veterinarian_involvement`, `select_one`, optional): Please indicate whether you involve your veterinarian in your pet care decisions.
