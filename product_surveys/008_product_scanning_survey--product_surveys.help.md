# Product Scanning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your feedback and insights on our product scanning services. Your input will help us improve our services to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the options that best match your experience with our product scanning services.
2. Provide detailed answers for any text fields, such as "Additional Comments".
3. You can skip any fields that do not apply to you.
