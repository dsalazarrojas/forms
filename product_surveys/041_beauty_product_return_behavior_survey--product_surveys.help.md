## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their return experiences with our beauty products. By providing accurate and detailed information, you will help us understand your concerns and improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide the required information for each field.
2. Answer the questions based on your actual experience with our product.
3. If you are unsure about a question, please answer based on your best recollection.
4. Do not provide any personal contact information unless explicitly requested.
5. Ensure that you save your work as you fill out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer ID or Order Number** (`customer_id`, text, optional): Enter your customer ID or order number associated with the returned product.
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, required): Enter the date you purchased the product.
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, required): Enter the date you returned the product.
* **Product Category** (`product_category`, select_one, required): Select the category of the product that was returned. Options include:
	+ Skincare
	+ Makeup
	+ Haircare
	+ Fragrance
	+ Nail Care
	+ Other
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the product that was returned.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, number, required): Enter the price of the product at the time of purchase.
* **Was This Your First Time Purchasing This Product** (`first_time_purchase`, select_one, required): Answer whether this was your first time purchasing this product. Options include:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Primary Reason for Return** (`primary_reason`, select_one, required): Select the primary reason for returning the product. Options include:
	+ Not as Described
	+ Quality Issues
	+ Allergic Reaction
	+ Ineffective
	+ Damaged Product
	+ Changed Mind
	+ Wrong Item
* **Detailed Reason for Return** (`reason_details`, text, required): Provide a detailed explanation for why you returned the product.
* **How Long Did You Use the Product** (`usage_duration`, select_one, required): Select how long you used the product. Options include:
	+ Never Opened
	+ Less than 1 Week
	+ 1-2 Weeks
	+ 2-4 Weeks
	+ Over 1 Month
* **How Did Product Compare to Expectations** (`expectation_vs_reality`, select_one, required): Answer how the product compared to your expectations. Options include:
	+ Much Worse
	+ Worse
	+ As Expected
	+ Better
	+ Much Better
* **Did Marketing Match the Product** (`marketing_alignment`, select_one, required): Select whether the marketing material accurately represented the product. Options include:
	+ Significantly Misrepresented
	+ Somewhat Misrepresented
	+ Accurate
	+ Conservative
	+ Exceeded
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Select your overall satisfaction with the product. Options include:
	+ Very Dissatisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Satisfied
	+ Very Satisfied
* **Product Quality Rating** (`quality_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the product quality. Options include:
	+ Very Poor
	+ Poor
	+ Average
	+ Good
	+ Excellent
* **Value for Money Rating** (`value_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the value for money. Options include:
	+ Very Poor
	+ Poor
	+ Average
	+ Good
	+ Excellent
* **Would You Recommend This Product** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Answer whether you would recommend this product. Options include:
	+ Definitely Not
	+ Probably Not
	+ Unsure
	+ Probably Yes
	+ Definitely Yes
* **Ease of Return Process** (`return_ease`, select_one, required): Select how easy or difficult the return process was. Options include:
	+ Very Difficult
	+ Difficult
	+ Average
	+ Easy
	+ Very Easy
* **Customer Service Quality During Return** (`customer_service_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of customer service during the return process. Options include:
	+ Very Poor
	+ Poor
	+ Average
	+ Good
	+ Excellent
* **Will You Shop with Us Again** (`future_behavior`, select_one, required): Answer whether you would shop with us again. Options include:
	+ Definitely Not
	+ Probably Not
	+ Unsure
	+ Probably Yes
	+ Definitely Yes
