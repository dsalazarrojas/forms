# Software Usability Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Software Usability Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback from users about the usability of a product. This form helps developers understand how users interact with the product, what they like or dislike about it, and how they use it.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read through each section carefully and complete the form according to the guidelines provided.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields to ensure your feedback is considered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Product Feedback

* **Product Feedback** (`Product Feedback`, Select Multiple, Required: False): Answer this question to provide an overall assessment of the product. You can select multiple options that apply to your experience.

* **User Information** (`User Information`, Text, Required: False): This field is an open text field where you can provide more details about yourself, such as name, email, or other relevant information.

* **Rating** (`Rating`, Number, Required: False): Provide a numerical rating of the product, with a range of 1-10.

* **Rating Comment** (`Rating Comment`, Text, Required: False): Elaborate on your rating in this open text field.

* **Suggest Improvement** (`Suggest Improvement`, Text, Required: False): Suggest improvements to the product in this open text field.

* **Product Use** (`Product Use`, Text, Required: False): Describe how you use the product in this open text field.

* **Frequency of Use** (`Frequency of Use`, Select One, Required: False): Indicate how often you use the product, with options for daily, weekly, or rarely.

* **How Satisfied** (`How Satisfied`, Select One, Required: False): Indicate your level of satisfaction with the product, with options for yes or no.

* **Likely to Recommend** (`Likely to Recommend`, Select One, Required: False): Indicate whether you would recommend the product, with options for yes or no.

* **Email** (`Email`, Email, Required: False): Provide your email address in this field.

* **Contact Method** (`Contact Method`, Select One, Required: False): Indicate how you would like to be contacted, with options for yes or no.

* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, Text, Required: False): Provide additional contact information in this open text field.

* **Submit** (`Submit`, Note, Required: False): Leave any additional comments or feedback in this note field.

* **Form Id** (`Form Id`, Text, Required: False): Provide a unique identifier for the form.

* **Form Version** (`Form Version`, Text, Required: False): Provide the version of the form you are filling out.

* **User Agent** (`User Agent`, Text, Required: False): Provide information about your browser or device.

* **Created At** (`Created At`, Text, Required: False): Provide the date and time you started filling out the form.

* **Updated At** (`Updated At`, Text, Required: False): Provide the date and time you completed the form.

* **User Id** (`User Id`, Text, Required: False): Provide a unique identifier for yourself.

* **Submitted At** (`Submitted At`, Text, Required: False): Provide the date and time you submitted the form.

* **Assigned User** (`Assigned User`, Text, Required: False): Provide the name of the user assigned to you.

* **Form Type** (`Form Type`, Text, Required: False): Provide the type of form you are filling out.
