# Information Systems Concepts Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide provides a step-by-step explanation of the "Information Systems Concepts Quiz" form. It will help you understand the purpose of each field and how to complete the form correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the quiz in the "Quiz Title" field.
2. Select the category of the quiz from the "Category" dropdown menu.
3. Enter a brief description of the quiz in the "Description" field.
4. Provide your student's name in the "Student Name" field.
5. Enter the date of the quiz in the "Date" field.
6. Enter the time of the quiz in the "Time" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Quiz Title (1)
* **Quiz Title (quiz_title)**: Enter a brief and descriptive title of the quiz. This field is not required, but it's recommended to give the quiz a title that reflects its content.

### Category (2)
* **Category (category)**: Select the category of the quiz from the dropdown menu. The category is not required, but selecting it helps to categorize the quiz for easier searching and organization.

### Description (3)
* **Description (description)**: Enter a brief description of the quiz. This field is not required, but it provides additional information about the quiz.

### Student Name (4)
* **Student Name (student_name)**: Enter your name as a student taking the quiz. This field is not required, but it's recommended to provide your name for identification purposes.

### Date (5)
* **Date (date)**: Enter the date on which the quiz was taken or is scheduled to be taken. Use the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

### Time (6)
* **Time (time)**: Enter the time of the quiz. Use 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).

### Email (7)
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address. This field is not required, but it's recommended to receive notifications or updates about the quiz.

### Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, if any.
* Use the correct format for date and time fields.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can ask your instructor or administrator for clarification.
