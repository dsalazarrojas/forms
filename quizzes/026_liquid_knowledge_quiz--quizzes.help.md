# Liquid Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Liquid Knowledge Quiz form is designed to allow you to create a quiz that can be used in a variety of settings, including educational, corporate, and other environments. By filling out this form, you can provide details such as category, title, description, and more to customize your quiz and make it more engaging for your audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the category that best fits your quiz.
2. Enter a brief title for your quiz.
3. Provide a short description of your quiz.
4. Select the type of quiz you want to create (Multiple Choice, True/False, Hotspot, or Hotspot Drag and Drop).
5. Select the language of your quiz (English, Spanish, Chinese (Simplified), or French).
6. Choose the target audience for your quiz (Educators, Trainers, or Marketers).
7. Select the method of training (Online or Offline).
8. Enter the expected duration of your quiz.
9. Enter the minimum and maximum number of participants.
10. Enter the date and time you want the quiz to be available.
11. Enter the email address of the person submitting the quiz.
12. Enter the phone number of the person submitting the quiz.
13. Enter any additional notes or information about the submitter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **quiz title** (`quiz_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief and descriptive title for your quiz that accurately represents the content.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the category that best fits your quiz. This will help determine where your quiz will be hosted and how it will be promoted.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of your quiz to help users understand what it's about.
* **quiz_type** (`quiz_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of quiz you want to create. This can be Multiple Choice, True/False, Hotspot, or Hotspot Drag and Drop.
* **question_type** (`question_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of questions you want to include in your quiz. This can be Multiple Choice or Hotspot Drag and Drop.
* **quiz_language** (`quiz_language`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the language of your quiz. This will determine the language in which the quiz will be displayed.
* **target_audience** (`target_audience`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the target audience for your quiz. This can be Educators, Trainers, or Marketers.
* **training_method** (`training_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the method of training you want to use for your quiz. This can be Online or Offline.
* **training_length** (`training_length`, `number`, required: false): Enter the expected duration of your quiz in minutes.
* **min_participants** (`min_participants`, `number`, required: false): Enter the minimum number of participants required for the quiz.
* **max_participants** (`max_participants`, `number`, required: false): Enter the maximum number of participants allowed for the quiz.
* **duration** (`duration`, `time`, required: false): Enter the duration of your quiz in minutes.
* **date_time** (`date_time`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date and time you want the quiz to be available.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the person submitting the quiz.
* **phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the quiz.
* **submitter** (`submitter`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about the person submitting the quiz.
* **submitter_company** (`submitter_company`, `text`, required: false): Enter the company or institution of the person submitting the quiz.
* **submitter_position** (`submitter_position`, `text`, required: false): Enter the position or job title of the person submitting the quiz.
* **submitter_email** (`submitter_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the person submitting the quiz.
* **submitter_phone** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the quiz.
* **submitter_notes** (`submitter_notes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or information about the person submitting the quiz.
* **submitter_website** (`submitter_website`, `text`, required: false): Enter the website of the person or institution submitting the quiz.
* **submitter_job_title** (`submitter_job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter the job title of the person submitting the quiz.
* **submitter_department** (`submitter_department`, `text`, required: false): Enter the department of the person submitting the quiz.
