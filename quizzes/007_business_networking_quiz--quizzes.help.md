# Business Networking Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your business networking skills and behaviors. It consists of 7 questions that aim to gather information about your networking frequency, comfort level with strangers, meaningful relationships built, primary networking goals, common challenges, follow-up methods, and areas for improvement. By completing this form, you will receive personalized feedback based on your answers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the most accurate response for each question.
2. Rate your comfort level with approaching strangers on the scale provided.
3. Choose the closest range for the number of meaningful relationships built.
4. Choose your primary networking goal from the options provided.
5. Select all challenges that apply to you.
6. Choose your usual method of follow-up after meeting new contacts.
7. Enter your email address to receive personalized feedback.
8. Enter your name for personalization purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How often do you attend networking events**: This question aims to gauge your frequency of attendance at networking events. Select the most accurate option that reflects your habits.
* **What is your comfort level with approaching strangers at events**: This question assesses your comfort level with initiating conversations with people you don't know. Rate your comfort level on the provided scale.
* **How many meaningful business relationships have you built through networking in the past year**: This question measures the number of meaningful relationships you've established through networking. Select the closest range that reflects your experience.
* **What is your primary networking goal**: This question inquires about your main focus or aim when networking. Choose the option that best describes your primary objective.
* **Which networking challenges apply to you**: This question identifies common challenges you face in your networking endeavors. Select all that are relevant to you.
* **How do you typically follow up with new connections**: This question explores your usual method of staying in touch with new contacts. Choose the option that reflects your usual practice.
* **What would most improve your networking effectiveness**: This question helps identify areas for improvement in your networking skills. Choose the one primary need that would enhance your networking abilities.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address to receive personalized feedback based on your answers.
* **Your Name**: Enter your name for personalization purposes in your feedback report.
