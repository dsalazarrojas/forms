# Bestie!!! - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to submit a friend request, providing information about themselves and their preferences to gauge compatibility with another person.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, age, and favorite music artist in the respective text fields.
2. Select your favorite food, gaming, travel, and personality traits from the available options.
3. Choose whether you like to hang out frequently, and if so, how often.
4. Indicate your favorite movie and what you would do with a lottery win in the text fields.
5. Select whether you can handle the other person's personality and how you recharge (introvert or extrovert).
6. Choose a personality trait that's important to you in a friend.
7. If you won the lottery, what would you buy first?
8. Indicate how often you like to hang out.
9. Finally, select whether you want to be friends with the other person and submit your social media handle (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`your_name_text`): Enter your name so we can call you.
* **Your Age** (`your_age_number`): Enter your age.
* **Favorite Music Artist** (`music_artist_fav`): What music artist do you like the most?
* **Favorite Food** (`food_fav_input`): What's your favorite food to eat when we hang out?
* **Do you like gaming** (`gaming_interest_choice`): Do you enjoy playing games?
* **Do you like out-of-town trips** (`travel_interest_choice`): Are you an adventure-seeker?
* **Are you an introvert or extrovert** (`personality_type_select`): How do you recharge, do you like being alone or around people?
* **Favorite Movie** (`movie_fav_input`): What's your favorite movie?
* **If you won the lottery, what would you buy?** (`lottery_win_purchase`): If you won the lottery, what would be the first thing you buy?
* **Can you handle my personality?** (`crazy_tolerance_check`): Can you handle my quirks?
* **How often do you like to hang out** (`hanging_out_frequency`): How often do you like to socialize?
* **Best personality trait in a friend** (`personality_trait_priority`): What's the most important trait you look for in a friend?
* **If you won the lottery, what is the first thing you would buy** (`lottery_win_purchase`): Repeat of the above question for consistency.
* **Do you want to be my bestie?** (`bestie_status_intent`): Are you ready to be friends?
* **Instagram or Social Handle** (`social_handle_entry`): Your social media handle for easy finding. (Optional)
* **Today's Date** (`submission_date_entry`): This is automatically populated, but please verify that it's correct.

Note: Some fields are required, while others are optional. Make sure to fill in all required fields to complete the form.
