# Programming Language Debug Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your familiarity with programming languages and debugging skills. The quiz is used for educational or research purposes and is not a high-stakes assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose the programming language you are most familiar with from the dropdown menu.
2. List the programming languages you are familiar with.
3. Describe your debugging skills and experience.
4. Enter the number of years you have been programming.
5. Choose your level of education related to programming.
6. Describe your work experience with programming.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Which programming language are you most familiar with?** (`first_language`, `select_one`, required: false): This field asks you to select the programming language you are most familiar with from the dropdown menu. You can choose one of the options: JavaScript, Python, C++, or C.
* **List the programming languages you know** (`programming_languages`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field asks you to list the programming languages you are familiar with. You can select multiple languages from the options: JavaScript, C++, C, or Java.
* **What debugging skills do you have?** (`debug_skills`, `text`, required: false): This field is for you to describe your debugging skills and experience. This can include any relevant training, certifications, or experience you have with debugging programming languages.
* **Years of experience with programming** (`years_of_experience`, `number`, required: false): This field asks you to enter the number of years you have been programming. Please enter a numerical value.
* **What level of education have you achieved?** (`education`, `select_one`, required: false): This field asks you to choose your level of education related to programming. You can select from the options: High School, Bachelor's Degree, or Master's Degree.
* **Work experience with programming** (`work_experience`, `text`, required: false): This field is for you to describe your work experience with programming. This can include any relevant projects, roles, or experience you have with programming.
