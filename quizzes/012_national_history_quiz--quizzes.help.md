# National History Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The National History Quiz form is used to collect personal information about a user's national history, including their name, age, country of birth, and date of birth.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Age**: Enter your current age.
3. **Country of Birth**: Enter the country where you were born.
4. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (text, optional): Please provide your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Age** (number, optional): Enter your current age.
* **Country of Birth** (text, optional): Enter the country where you were born.
* **Date of Birth** (date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields if you want to submit the form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact our support team for assistance.
