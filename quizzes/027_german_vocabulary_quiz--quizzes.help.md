# German Vocabulary Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The German Vocabulary Quiz is designed to test your knowledge of German words and phrases. It will present you with a series of questions and prompts, and you will be asked to choose the correct options to answer them. Please take your time and answer each question carefully.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Quiz Start` field with your name or a reference number (if applicable).
2. Proceed to the next page, where you will be presented with a question.
3. Select the correct answer from the options provided in the `question` field.
4. In the `answer` field, type in your response to the question.
5. If you need to go to the next question, select the `next question` option in the `next_question` field.
6. When you are ready to submit your answers, select the `Submit` option in the `submit_button` field.
7. Finally, type in your completion status in the `Quiz Complete` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quiz Start** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your name or a reference number to identify yourself.
* **question** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose the correct answer from the options provided.
* **answer** (`text`, required/optional): Type in your response to the question.
* **next question** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the next question you want to answer.
* **Submit** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose to submit your answers or cancel the form.
* **Quiz Complete** (`text`, required/optional): Type in your completion status after finishing the quiz.

Note: Some fields are optional, but please fill them out as needed to provide accurate information.
