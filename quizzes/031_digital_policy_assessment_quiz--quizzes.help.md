Before proceeding, please ensure that you are not taking this quiz as part of a duplicate or redundant process, and that you have not completed it previously. Completing this form multiple times may lead to inconsistent results.

### Digital Policy Assessment Quiz - Help Guide
### How To Complete This Form
To complete the Digital Policy Assessment Quiz, follow these steps:

1.  Select the "Start" button to begin the quiz. You will be presented with a series of questions across multiple pages.
2.  Answer the questions by selecting the appropriate options or entering the required information.
3.  Review your answers carefully before submitting.

### Field-by-Field Explanation
#### Page 1: What is your name
*   **What is your name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
#### Page 2: Which of the following best describes you?
*   **Which of the following best describes you?** (`name`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more of the following options to describe yourself:
    *   Apple
    *   Android
    *   Other
#### Page 3: What is your age
*   **What is your age** (`age`, `number`, required: false): Enter your age as a numerical value.
#### Page 4: What is your phone number
*   **What is your phone number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number without the country code.
#### Page 5: What is your email
*   **What is your email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address associated with your identity.
#### Page 6: How many years of experience do you have in this area?
*   **How many years of experience do you have in this area?** (`experience`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of years you have experience in this area.
