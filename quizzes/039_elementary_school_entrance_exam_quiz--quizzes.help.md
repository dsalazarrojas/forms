# Elementary School Entrance Exam Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Elementary School Entrance Exam Quiz is a form designed to collect information from applicants for elementary school entrance. The form is used to gather details about the applicant, their parents, and their educational background to determine their eligibility for admission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the Applicant Information section with your name, your parent's name, and a contact email and phone number.
2. Select the level of the school you are interested in applying for from the School Level dropdown list.
3. Enter your grade level, which will be used to assess your eligibility.
4. Answer the questions in Section 1, which will have a time limit.
5. Choose your response to Section 2, which is a multiple-choice question.
6. Add any additional comments or notes in the Note section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Applicant Information (Page 1)
- **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your parent's name.
- **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.

### School Information (Page 2)
- **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school you are applying to.
- **School Level** (`school_level`, select_one, required): Select the level of school you are interested in applying for: Primary, Secondary, High School, College, or University.

### Section 1 (Page 3)
- **Section 1** (`quiz_section_1`, time, required): Answer the question within the time limit.

### Section 2 (Page 4)
- **Section 2** (`quiz_section_2`, select_multiple, required): Choose your response to this multiple-choice question.

### Note (Page 5)
- **Note** (`note`, note, required): Add any additional comments or notes about your application.
