# Synthetic Food Ingredients Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Synthetic Food Ingredients Quiz is designed to test your knowledge and opinions on various aspects of synthetic food ingredients, including general knowledge, safety concerns, health effects, regulatory guidelines, and more. This quiz will help us understand your perceptions and understanding of these topics.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please go through each page and answer the questions according to the instructions and options provided. The form is divided into 11 sections, each with different types of questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Knowledge** (`general_knowledge`, text, optional): This section is open-ended and asks you to provide any general knowledge you have about synthetic food ingredients.
* **Synthetic Additives** (`synthetic_additives`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the options that apply to your understanding of synthetic additives. You can select multiple options.
* **Safety Concerns** (`safety_concerns`, select_one, optional): Choose the option that best represents your level of concern about safety aspects of synthetic food ingredients.
* **Health Effects** (`health_effects`, note, optional): Please provide any information about potential health effects of synthetic food ingredients you are aware of.
* **Regulatory Guidelines** (`regulatory_guidelines`, text, optional): Share any knowledge or opinions you have on regulatory guidelines related to synthetic food ingredients.
* **Education Impact** (`education_impact`, text, optional): Discuss how you think synthetic food ingredients should be taught or learned about in schools or educational institutions.
* **Assessment Strategy** (`assessment_strategy`, select_one, optional): Choose the option that best describes how you assess the safety of synthetic food ingredients.
* **Evaluation and Reporting** (`evaluation_and_reporting`, select_multiple, optional): Select all options that apply to how you evaluate and report on the safety of synthetic food ingredients.
* **Additional Questions** (`additional_questions`, text, optional): If you have any additional questions or concerns that are not covered by the other sections, please share them here.
* **Review and Appeal** (`review_and_appeal`, select_one, optional): Choose the option that best describes how you would like to review and appeal any feedback or evaluation.
* **Feedback and Comments** (`feedback_and_comments`, text, optional): Share any final feedback or comments you have about the quiz or synthetic food ingredients.

Please complete each section according to your knowledge and opinions, and do not hesitate to ask if you need further clarification on any question.
