# Strategic Tools Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Strategic Tools Knowledge Quiz is designed to test your knowledge and expertise in specific tools and frameworks used in strategic planning and decision-making. The quiz will ask you to provide information about your experience, skills, and knowledge in these areas. Your responses will help us understand your current level of proficiency and identify areas where you may need additional training or support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the quiz, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question on the form with as much detail as possible.
2. Make sure to answer all questions, but feel free to skip any question that does not apply to your experience.
3. Select the options that best describe your situation.
4. Enter your results and comments in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Page
* **first_page**: (text) Enter your answer in the space provided.

### Tool Name
* **tool_name**: (text) Enter the name of a specific tool you have experience with. For example, "Microsoft Excel" or "Google Sheets".

### Framework Name
* **framework_name**: (text) Enter the name of a specific framework you have experience with. For example, "Agile" or "Waterfall".

### Knowledge Level
* **knowledge_level**: (text) Select the level of knowledge you have in the tool or framework. For example, "Basic", "Intermediate", or "Advanced".

### Tool Experience
* **tool_experience**: (text) Describe your experience with the tool, including how long you have been using it and what type of projects you have worked on with it.

### Team Size
* **team_size**: (number) Enter the size of the team you typically work with. For example, "1-5" or "6-10".

### Team Distributed
* **team_distributed**: (select multiple) Select all that apply:
	+ Option 1: Yes, I work in a team.
	+ Option 2: I work individually.
	+ Option 3: Other (please describe)

### Tool Usefulness
* **tool_usefulness**: (select one) Select one that best describes your experience:
	+ Option 1: Very useful.
	+ Option 2: Somewhat useful.
	+ Option 3: Not very useful.

### Results
* **results**: (text) Enter any additional comments or results you have achieved using the tool or framework.
