# Skincare Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skincare Quiz helps us understand your preferences and needs regarding skincare. Answer the following questions to get personalized recommendations for your skin type and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions at your own pace, there are no required fields.
2. Please select all that apply from the options provided for each question.
3. Fill out all the questions before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`intro`, note, optional): This is a brief introduction to the quiz. You can type any information you'd like about your skin concerns or goals.
* **Skin Type** (`skin_type`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all that apply from the options 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your skin type. If you have a combination of skin types, feel free to select both options.
* **Concerns** (`concerns`, select_multiple, optional): Similar to **Skin Type**, please select all that apply from the options 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your skin concerns. This will help us understand the specific issues you're experiencing.
* **Recommended products** (`recommended_products`, text, optional): Describe any specific products you're interested in or have already tried. This will help us provide more tailored recommendations.
