# Guess The Value Challenge - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Guess The Value Challenge" form is a tool designed to test or gather feedback on numeric value guessing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, enter your numeric guess for the value. This is a required field.
2. Optionally, provide a reason for your guess. This is not a required field.
3. Enter your name. This is not a required field.
4. Enter your phone number. This is not a required field.
5. Enter your email address. This is not a required field.
6. Optionally, provide an additional reason for your guess. This is not a required field.
7. Click the "Submit" button to submit your guess.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guess The Value Challenge** (`title`, `text`, required): A short description of the form purpose.
* **guess_1** (`numeric_guess_1`, `number`, required): Enter a numeric value for the challenge.
* **reason_1** (`reason_1`, `text`, optional): Provide a reason for your guess, if you want to explain your answer.
* **name** (`contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name, if you want to be credited for your guess.
* **phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number, if you want to be contacted for further feedback.
* **email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address, if you want to be contacted for further feedback.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, `text`, optional): Click this button to submit your guess and get further instructions or feedback.
