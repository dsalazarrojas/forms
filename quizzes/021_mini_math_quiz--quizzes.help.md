# Mini Math Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mini Math Quiz is a simple math quiz that consists of a few questions to test your math skills. The quiz is meant to be completed in a few minutes, and it's a great way to assess your knowledge on specific math topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the math questions on pages 1-2.
2. Enter your answers in the corresponding fields on page 3.
3. Click on the "Submit" button to submit your quiz.
4. Review your score on page 5.
5. If you'd like, add comments about your quiz experience on page 7.
6. You can also view your submission date and time on page 8.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **question_one (1)**: This is the first question of the math quiz.
* **question_two (2)**: This is the second question of the math quiz.
* **answer_form (3)**: Enter your answers to the math questions here.
* **score (4)**: This field displays your score after you submit the quiz.
* **grade (5)**: This field is currently not being used and is here for future development.
* **comments (6)**: Add any comments or feedback about your quiz experience here.
* **submitted (7)**: This field shows the date and time you submitted the quiz.
* **(8)**: Currently not used.
