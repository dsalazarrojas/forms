# First Aid Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your knowledge and awareness about First Aid procedures. It contains questions related to various injuries and medical emergencies, such as cardiac issues, respiratory problems, bleeding, and more. This form helps users understand what actions to take in case of different types of injuries, and is intended for educational purposes only.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each question carefully and select the most appropriate response.
- Choose one or multiple options for each question, depending on the question type.
- Make sure to answer all questions to get a comprehensive assessment of your knowledge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **quiz_question_1**: What is the first step you would take when someone is injured? 
  (Select one option)
  - This question assesses your ability to recognize the initial response to an injury.
  - Options: 
    - CPR: Cardiopulmonary resuscitation, a lifesaving technique used to restore breathing and circulation.
    - First Aid Responder: A person trained to provide medical assistance in emergency situations.
    - Call 911: Calling emergency services for professional help.
    - None of the above: This is not a correct response.
* **quiz_question_2**: How would you check a person's airway, breathing, and circulation?
  (Select one or multiple options)
  - This question checks your knowledge of the primary survey technique in First Aid.
  - Options: 
    - Airway: Check the person's airway for blockages or obstructions.
    - Breathing: Check the person's breathing rate and quality.
    - Circulation: Check the person's pulse and blood pressure.
    - None of the above: This is not a correct response.
* **quiz_question_3**: What are the steps to take when someone is having a heart attack?
  (Select one or multiple options)
  - This question assesses your knowledge of heart attack symptoms and response.
  - Options: 
    - Chest pain: A common symptom of a heart attack.
    - Difficulty breathing: Another symptom of a heart attack.
    - Sweating: A symptom of a heart attack.
    - Nausea: A symptom of a heart attack.
    - All of the above: This is the correct response, as all these symptoms can be present.
* **quiz_question_4**: How would you help someone who is bleeding?
  (Select one or multiple options)
  - This question checks your knowledge of bleeding response.
  - Options: 
    - Apply pressure: To stop the bleeding.
    - Elevate the affected area: To reduce swelling and promote healing.
    - Apply a tourniquet: To reduce blood loss.
    - Apply cold water: Not a recommended response.
    - None of the above: This is not a correct response.
* **quiz_question_5**: What is the primary goal of First Aid?
  (Select one option)
  - This question assesses your understanding of First Aid's purpose.
  - Options: 
    - Prevent further injury: While prevention is part of First Aid.
    - Relieve pain: A secondary goal.
    - Stop bleeding: A specific action, but not the primary goal.
    - Preserve life: The primary goal of First Aid.
    - None of the above: This is not a correct response.
* **quiz_question_6**: What should you do if someone is not breathing?
  (Select one or multiple options)
  - This question checks your knowledge of CPR and breathing response.
  - Options: 
    - Call 911: To call emergency services.
    - CPR: To restore breathing.
    - Use a mask: To provide oxygen if possible.
    - None of the above: This is not a correct response.
* **quiz_question_7**: What should you do if someone has a severe allergic reaction?
  (Select one or multiple options)
  - This question assesses your knowledge of allergic reactions.
  - Options: 
    - Call 911: To call emergency services.
    - Administer an EpiPEN: To counteract the allergic reaction.
    - Apply a cold compress: Not a recommended response.
    - None of the above: This is not a correct response.

  (Select one or multiple options)
  - This question checks your knowledge of injury causes.
  - Options: 
    - Head: Common areas for injury.
    - Neck: Common area for injury.
    - Face: Common area for injury.
    - Back: Common area for injury.
    - Abdomen: Common area for injury.
    - All of the above: This is the correct response.

  (Select one or multiple options)
  - This question assesses your knowledge of concussions.
  - Options: 
    - Confusion and disorientation: Symptoms of a concussion.
    - Loss of consciousness: A symptom of a concussion.
    - Nausea or vomiting: Symptoms of a concussion.
    - Difficulty speaking: A symptom of a concussion.
