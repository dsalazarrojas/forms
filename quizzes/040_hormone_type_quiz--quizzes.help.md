# Hormone Type Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users identify potential hormone imbalances by asking about their symptoms. It consists of a series of questions about symptoms and a final quiz completion confirmation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions about symptoms on pages 2, 3, and 4.
2. Choose all the symptoms that apply to you from the select multiple options.
3. On page 4, create a hormonal profile (optional).
4. On pages 5, 6, and 8, confirm that you have completed the quiz.
5. On page 9, enter your email address (optional).
6. On page 10, review and confirm your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Symptoms of Hormonal Imbalance** (`symptoms_page`): This question asks about potential symptoms of hormonal imbalances. Select all the symptoms that you are experiencing.
* **Symptoms of Hormonal Imbalance 2** (`symptoms_page_2`): Another question asking about symptoms, similar to the previous one.
* **Create Hormonal Profile** (`hormonal_profile_page`): This is an optional section where you can provide more information about your hormonal profile (note: this field is not explained in the provided YAML data, so we'll just leave it blank).
* **Finish Quiz** (`finish_quiz`): Confirm that you have completed the quiz.
* **Finish Quiz 2** (`finish_quiz_2`): Another confirmation question for the quiz completion.
* **Provide Notes** (`notes_page`): Leave any additional comments or notes about your experience.
* **Finish the Quiz** (`finish_quiz`): Confirm that you have completed the quiz again.
* **Enter Email** (`email_page`): Optionally, enter your email address.
* **Email Confirmation** (`email_page_2`): Review and confirm your email address.
* **True/False Confirmation** (`quiz_complete`, `finish_quiz`, `finish_quiz_2`): Confirm that you have completed the quiz or not.
	+ Select "True" if you have completed the quiz.
	+ Select "False" if you have not completed the quiz.
* **Email Address** (`email_page`): If you provide your email address, it will be stored with your submission.
