# Monat Beauty Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Monat Beauty Quiz is a simple online quiz designed to help users understand their beauty and skincare needs. It's intended to be a fun and educational experience, and not a formal assessment or survey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Monat Beauty Quiz, follow these steps:

1. Select the options that best describe your hair and skin type in the "hair and skin" field.
2. Answer the quiz question by entering a numerical value.
3. Choose your answer to the quiz question by selecting one or multiple options.
4. Enter your email address if you'd like to receive further recommendations or updates.
5. Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for further assistance.
6. Enter a date if you'd like to be contacted on a specific date.
7. Choose a time if you'd like to be contacted at a specific time.
8. Enter any additional notes or comments you'd like to share.
9. Select one of the options for "select-one".
10. Select multiple options from the list for "select-multiple".
11. Enter a short text response in the "text" field if you'd like to share additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **hair and skin** (select_one): Select one option that best describes your hair and skin type.
- **quiz-question** (number): Answer the question by entering a numerical value.
- **answer** (select_multiple): Choose one or multiple answers that best fit your preferences.
- **email** (email): Enter a valid email address if you'd like to receive further recommendations or updates.
- **phone-number** (text): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for further assistance.
- **date** (date): Enter a specific date if you'd like to be contacted on that day.
- **time** (time): Enter a specific time if you'd like to be contacted at that time.
- **note** (note): Enter any additional notes or comments you'd like to share.
- **select-one** (select_one): Select one of the options provided.
- **select-multiple** (select_multiple): Select multiple options that best fit your preferences.
- **text** (text): Enter a short text response to share additional information.
