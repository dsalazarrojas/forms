# Quiz Leaderboard Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quiz Leaderboard Submission Form is used to submit participant information for a quiz competition. This form helps track participant performance, such as name, email, competition details, and result information. Please complete this form to submit your participation details accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Participant Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Participant Email** in the second field.
3. Provide the name of the **Competition** you're participating in.
4. Enter your **Result Position** (e.g., 1st, 2nd, 3rd, etc.).
5. Enter your **Score** achieved in the competition.
6. Select the **Submission Time** when you submitted your results.
7. Enter the **Competition ID** (if provided).
8. Enter your **Participant ID** (if provided).
9. Enter the **Quiz Name** (if applicable).
10. Provide any additional **Result Details** about your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Your name, as it should be spelled correctly.
* **Participant Email**: A valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Competition Name**: The name of the quiz competition you're participating in.
* **Result Position**: The position you achieved in the competition (e.g., 1st, 2nd, 3rd).
* **Score**: The points or score you achieved in the competition.
* **Submission Time**: The date and time when you submitted your results.
* **Competition ID**: A unique identifier for the competition you're participating in (if provided).
* **Participant ID**: A unique identifier for you as a participant (if provided).
* **Quiz Name**: The name of the quiz you're participating in (if applicable).
* **Result Details**: Any additional information about your participation in the competition.
