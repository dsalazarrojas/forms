# Short Story Elements Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your understanding of the fundamental elements of a short story. It is intended for users who have completed a reading comprehension course and are familiar with the basic concepts of fiction writing. The quiz tests your ability to identify and describe the core components of a short story, including the plot, character development, and theme. Please note that you should only attempt this quiz if you have a basic understanding of these concepts, as it is not intended for those without prior knowledge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the designated field.
2. For questions with a "select_one" type, choose only one option that best describes your understanding.
3. For questions with a "select_multiple" type, choose all applicable options.
4. Enter your answers for each section, and submit your form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. intro (page 1)
* **intro** (`intro`, `note`, required): This section is designed to introduce you to the quiz and provide a brief overview of the questions you will be asked. Please take a moment to read through each question carefully before proceeding.

### 2. plot (page 1)
* **plot** (`plot`, `select_one`, required): For this question, choose the option that best describes the plot of a short story. This is the sequence of events that makes up the story's narrative.

### 3. character (page 1)
* **character** (`character`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the types of characters that are typically found in a short story. This includes main characters, supporting characters, and potentially even minor characters.

### 4. theme (page 1)
* **theme** (`theme`, `select_one`, required): For this question, choose the option that best describes the theme of a short story. This is the underlying idea or message conveyed by the story.

### 5. plot hint (page 2)
* **plot hint** (`plot_hint`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional information or insights about the plot, feel free to enter them in this text field. This is an opportunity to provide more context or clarity about your understanding of the plot.

### 6. main character (page 2)
* **main character** (`main_character`, `select_one`, required): For this question, choose the option that best describes the main character of a short story. Typically, this is the character around whom the plot revolves.

### 7. supporting character 1 (page 2)
* **supporting character 1** (`supporting_character_1`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the types of supporting characters that may be found in a short story. These characters support the main character and contribute to the plot.

### 8. supporting character 2 (page 3)
* **supporting character 2** (`supporting_character_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Similar to the previous question, select all the types of supporting characters that may be found in a short story. These characters may also support the main character and advance the plot.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* Take your time and consider each question carefully before providing your answer.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact our support team for assistance.
