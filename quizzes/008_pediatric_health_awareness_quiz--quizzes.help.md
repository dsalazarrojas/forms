# Pediatric Health Awareness Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pediatric Health Awareness Quiz is a tool designed to provide pediatric healthcare professionals with information about the individual's health status. It consists of a set of questions that help assess the individual's awareness of their health and identify potential risks or concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "What is your name?" field.
2. Enter your age in the "Age" field.
3. Enter your birth date in the "Birth Date" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "E-mail" field.
6. Answer the health-related questions in the "Do you have any health issues?", "Do you have any allergies?", "Do you take any medications?", "Do you have any medical conditions?", "Do you have any chronic conditions?", and "Do you have any mental health conditions?" fields.
7. Answer the "Do you have any other health concerns?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name?** (`quiz_question_1`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name in this field.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required/optional): Enter your age in this field.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, required/optional): Enter your birth date in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number in this field.
* **E-mail** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address in this field.
* **Do you have any health issues?** (`quiz_question_2`, text, required/optional): Answer this question with a brief description of any current health issues you are experiencing.
* **Do you have any allergies?** (`quiz_question_3`, text, required/optional): Answer this question with a list of any known allergies you have.
* **Do you take any medications?** (`quiz_question_4`, text, required/optional): Answer this question with a list of any medications you are currently taking.
* **Do you have any medical conditions?** (`quiz_question_5`, text, required/optional): Answer this question with a list of any medical conditions you have been diagnosed with.
* **Do you have any chronic conditions?** (`quiz_question_6`, text, required/optional): Answer this question with a list of any chronic conditions you have.
* **Do you have any mental health conditions?** (`quiz_question_7`, text, required/optional): Answer this question with a list of any mental health conditions you have.
* **Do you have any other health concerns?** (`quiz_question_8`, text, required/optional): Answer this question with any other health concerns you have.
