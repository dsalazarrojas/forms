# Procurement Skills Assessment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a Procurement Skills Assessment Quiz aimed at evaluating the skills and knowledge of the user in the field of procurement. The quiz consists of several questions and fields designed to assess the user's proficiency in various procurement skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct options for each question and field as per your understanding and knowledge.
2. Ensure you answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
3. Enter your details in the required fields.

* **Procurement Category** (`procurement_category`): This field is used to select the category of procurement skills you possess. Please choose all the categories that apply to you from the available options.
* **First Question** (`first_question`): This is a multiple-choice question asking whether you have the knowledge or experience in the first procurement skill.
* **First Answer** (`first_answer`): This is the corresponding answer to the first question, which helps to evaluate your knowledge or experience in the first procurement skill.
