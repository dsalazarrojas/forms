# Mobile Computing Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your experiences, behaviors, and preferences related to mobile computing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer the multiple-choice questions by selecting one or multiple options for each question.
2.  Provide your responses to the free-text questions (first\_page, time, date, note, phone, select\_multiple\_dates, and select\_multiple\_durations).
3.  Fill in the required fields, indicated by the \***required* label.
4.  You can save your progress and continue later by clicking the "Submit" button, which will save your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Multiple Choice**: You will be asked to choose one or multiple options for each question. Select the options that apply to your experiences or behaviors.
*   **Select One**: You will be asked to choose one option for this question. Select the option that best describes your answer.
*   **First Page**: This is a free-text field where you can provide a description or answer to the question.
*   **Time**: Provide the correct time in the format "HH:MM:SS" for this question.
*   **Date**: Enter the correct date in the format "MM/DD/YYYY" for this question.
*   **Note**: This is a free-text field where you can provide any additional notes or comments.
*   **Phone**: Enter your telephone number in the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX" for this question.
*   **Select Multiple Dates**: You will be asked to choose one or multiple dates for this question.
*   **Select Multiple Durations**: You will be asked to choose one or multiple durations for this question.
