# Cone Volume Math Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help students demonstrate their understanding of the math concept of cone volume.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Student name" field.
2. Enter the name of your class or course in the "Class or course" field.
3. For each problem, enter the radius and height of the cone in centimeters.
4. Calculate the volume of the cone using the formula `V = 1/3 \* π \* r^2 \* h`, where `V` is the volume, `r` is the radius, and `h` is the height.
5. Enter your calculated volume in the corresponding "Problem one calculated volume cm³" or "Problem two calculated volume cm³" field.
6. If you'd like to describe your calculation steps, you can type them in the "Method notes" field.
7. Finally, if you have any feedback or comments for the instructor, please type it in the "Instructor comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Problem one

* **Student name**: 
  (`student_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name to identify yourself as the student.
  * **Class or course**: 
    (`class`, text, optional): Enter the name of your class or course.
  * **Problem one radius cm**: 
    (`problem_one_radius`, number, required): Enter the radius of the cone in centimeters.
  * **Problem one height cm**: 
    (`problem_one_height`, number, required): Enter the height of the cone in centimeters.
  * **Problem one calculated volume cm³**: 
    (`problem_one_answer`, number, required): Use the formula `V = 1/3 * π * r^2 * h` to calculate the volume and enter your answer here.
  * **Method notes**: 
    (`method_notes`, text, optional): Describe your calculation steps if you'd like.

* **Problem two**

  + **Problem two radius cm**:

  + **Problem two height cm**:
