# Candy Jar Guessing Game Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Candy Jar Guessing Game Template is designed to collect information from participants in a game where they guess the total number of candies in a jar. The game is intended to be a fun and engaging activity for participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field. This will be used to notify you if you win.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field (optional).
4. Choose your group or department from the "Group or Department" field (optional).
5. Enter your guess for the total number of candies in the jar in the "What is your guess for the total number of candies in the jar" field.
6. Choose the main type of candy you observe in the "What is the main type of candy you see" field.
7. Answer the bonus question "What is the total weight of the jar" (optional).
8. If you win, specify who you would share the candy with in the "If you win, who would you share the candy with" field (optional).
9. Provide feedback on the event's organization in the "How would you rate this event's organization" field.
10. Let us know if you'd like to see more guessing games in the future in the "Would you like to see more guessing games in the future" field.
11. Confirm that you understand that only the closest guess wins in the "I understand that only the closest guess wins" field.
12. Confirm that you agree to the game rules in the "I agree to the game rules" field.
13. Finally, enter the date of submission in the "Submission Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name_guess`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email_guess`, email, required): Enter a valid email address to which we can notify you if you win.
* **Phone Number** (`participant_phone_guess`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact.
* **Group or Department** (`participant_group_dept`, text, optional): Choose the group or department you are representing.
* **What is your guess for the total number of candies in the jar** (`candy_count_guess`, number, required): Enter a whole number for your guess.
* **What is the main type of candy you see** (`main_candy_type_observation`, select_one, required): Choose the type of candy you observe as the primary contents.
* **What is the total weight of the jar** (`jar_weight_guess`, number, optional): Enter the weight of the jar (optional).
* **If you win, who would you share the candy with** (`winner_sharing_plan`, text, optional): Specify who you would share the candy with if you win.
* **How would you rate this event's organization** (`event_organization_rating`, number, required): Rate the organization of the event from 1 to 5 stars.
* **Would you like to see more guessing games in the future** (`more_guessing_games_pref`, select_one, required): Choose whether you'd like to see more guessing games in the future.
* **I understand that only the closest guess wins** (`winner_rule_understanding`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand that only the closest guess wins.
* **I agree to the game rules** (`game_rules_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to the game rules.
* **Submission Date** (`guess_submission_date_field`, date, required): Enter the date of submission.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter a whole number for your guess.
* Choose the correct type of candy as the primary content.
* Enter the correct weight of the jar if you choose to answer the bonus question.
* Specify who you would share the candy with if you win.
* Rate the event's organization honestly.
* Confirm that you understand the game rules and agree to them.
* Enter the correct date of submission.
