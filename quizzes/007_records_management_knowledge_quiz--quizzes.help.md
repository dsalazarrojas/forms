# Records Management Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the user's knowledge on records management, specifically to evaluate their understanding of the records management process, its benefits, and common practices. The quiz aims to gauge the user's grasp of key concepts related to records management, such as data handling, data protection, and compliance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Introduction**: This is a text box that provides a general overview of the form and its purpose. Please feel free to read and understand the content provided.
2. **Quiz Questions**: This section presents a series of multiple-choice questions to test your knowledge on records management. Select one of the options for each question.
3. **Quiz Answer**: Here, you will be asked to select all that apply for each question. Be sure to choose the relevant options that reflect your understanding of the records management principles.
4. **Feedback**: This text box provides an opportunity for you to provide any comments or observations you may have about the form or the questions.
5. **Name**: Please enter your name as it should appear on your score report.
6. **Email**: Enter your email address if you wish to receive your score report.
7. **Score**: Your score will be calculated based on your answers to the questions.
8. **Results**: This section will display your final score and any additional feedback or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quiz Questions** (`id: 2`, `type: select_one`): A series of multiple-choice questions that test your knowledge on records management principles. Select the option that best answers each question.
* **Quiz Answer** (`id: 3`, `type: select_multiple`): For each question, select all the options that apply to your understanding of the records management principle.
* **Feedback** (`id: 4`, `type: text`): Share any comments or observations you may have about the form or the questions.
* **Name** (`id: 5`, `type: text`): Enter your name as it should appear on your score report.
* **Email** (`id: 6`, `type: email`): Enter your email address if you wish to receive your score report.
* **Score** (`id: 7`, `type: number`): This field displays your final score.
* **Results** (`id: 8`, `type: text`): This section provides your final score and any additional feedback or comments.

## Tips

* Please answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
