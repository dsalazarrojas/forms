# tmj_awareness_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a custom quiz project. The quiz will help raise awareness about a specific topic, and this form will guide you through the process of setting up the quiz.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure that you have all the necessary information before submitting.
2. Fill out each field carefully, as they are all important for creating a well-rounded quiz.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction (Page 1)
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, optional): This field is a brief introduction to the quiz. It will be displayed at the beginning of the quiz to help set the context. Please enter a short description of the quiz.

### Symptoms (Page 2)
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is to ask users about their symptoms. Please select all the symptoms that apply to your experience. Yes/No options are provided for each symptom.

### Knowledge (Page 3)
* **Knowledge** (`knowledge`, `select_one`, optional): This field is to gauge your current level of knowledge about the topic. Please select one option: Yes/No.

### Discomfort (Page 4)
* **Discomfort** (`discomfort`, `number`, optional): This field is to ask users about their level of discomfort related to the topic. Enter a number between 0 and 10 to represent your level of discomfort.

### Questions (Page 5)
* **Questions** (`questions`, `text`, optional): This field is to submit any additional questions or concerns you have about the quiz. Please enter any relevant questions or comments.

### Email (Page 6)
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): This field is to provide your email address. Enter your email address if you want us to contact you regarding the quiz.

### Phone (Page 7)
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): This field is to provide your phone number. Enter your phone number if you want us to contact you regarding the quiz.

### Category (Page 8)
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, optional): This field is to select the category of the quiz. Please select Quizzes.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out all the necessary fields to avoid delays in the quiz creation process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We will review your submission and contact you to discuss further details about the quiz.
