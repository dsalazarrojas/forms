# hospitality_knowledge_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hospitality_knowledge_assessment form is designed to evaluate the knowledge and proficiency of hospitality industry professionals in various categories.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your category of work from the dropdown menu.
2. Describe your role in the organization.
3. If applicable, select the tools you have been assigned to use.
4. Enter your current score.
5. Provide any comments or suggestions you may have.
6. Confirm if you have submitted the form previously.
7. Enter your unique form ID.
8. Specify where you can access your form output.
9. Describe your form in your own words.
10. Select who assigned this form to you.
11. Provide the current status of your form.
12. Specify the location of your form.
13. Identify who created this form.
14. Specify when the form was last updated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category (1)** (`What is your category of work?`, required/optional): Please select your category from the dropdown menu. This could be Front Desk, Housekeeping, F&B, or Engineering.
* **Role (2)** (`Can you describe your role in our organization?`, required): Please provide a brief description of your role in the organization.
* **Assigned Tool (3)** (`What tools have you been assigned to use?`, optional): Select the tools you have been assigned to use. You may choose multiple tools.
* **Score (4)** (`What is your current score?`, required): Please enter your current score.
* **Comments (5)** (`Do you have any comments or suggestions?`, optional): You may provide any comments or suggestions you have.
* **Submitted (6)** (`Have you submitted the form previously?`, optional): Confirm if you have submitted the form previously.
* **Form ID (7)** (`What is your form ID?`, required): Please enter your unique form ID.
* **Form URL (8)** (`Where can you access your form output?`, optional): Specify where you can access your form output.
* **Form Description (9)** (`Can you describe your form?`, optional): Please describe your form in your own words.
* **Assigned by (10)** (`Who assigned this form to you?`, optional): Select who assigned this form to you.
* **Form Status (11)** (`What is the status of your form?`, optional): Please specify the current status of your form.
* **Form Location (12)** (`Where is your form located?`, optional): Specify the location of your form.
* **Created by (13)** (`Who created this form?`, optional): Identify who created this form.
* **Updated at (14)** (`When was the form updated?`, optional): Specify when the form was last updated.
