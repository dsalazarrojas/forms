# Who Knows Me Best Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Who Knows Me Best Quiz" form is a fun and interactive tool designed to help you assess your relationships with your friends. It presents a series of questions that ask about your friendships, and provides you with a lighthearted way to explore and discuss your relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow the instructions below.

1. Begin by filling out the form as instructed.
2. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
3. Select the correct options for each field.
4. Submit your answers to see the results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **name** (text, required/optional)
Enter your name to identify yourself for the quiz.

### 2. **friend1** (select_one, required/optional)
Indicate if you consider your friend1 to know you best.

### 3. **Friend1 Name** (text, required/optional)
If you selected friend1 in the previous question, enter their name here.

### 4. **friend2** (select_one, required/optional)
Indicate if you consider your friend2 to know you best.

### 5. **Friend2 Name** (text, required/optional)
If you selected friend2 in the previous question, enter their name here.

### 6. **friend3** (select_one, required/optional)
Indicate if you consider your friend3 to know you best.

### 7. **Friend3 Name** (text, required/optional)
If you selected friend3 in the previous question, enter their name here.

### 8. **friend4** (select_one, required/optional)
Indicate if you consider your friend4 to know you best.

### 9. **Friend4 Name** (text, required/optional)
If you selected friend4 in the previous question, enter their name here.

### 10. **submit** (select_one, required/optional)
Select "Yes" to submit your answers and see the results.

### 11. **Submit Text** (text, required/optional)
If you selected "Yes" in the previous question, enter any additional comments or thoughts here.
