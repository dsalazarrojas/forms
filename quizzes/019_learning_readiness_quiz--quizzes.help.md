# Learning Readiness Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess a user's readiness to learn and progress through a course or program. It is designed to help instructors and administrators gauge a learner's preparedness and identify potential areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and familiarize yourself with the questions and options presented.
2. Select the answers that best describe your current state or experience.
3. Complete each page of the form, which consists of multiple-choice questions and text-entry fields.
4. Submit the form when you have completed all pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1** (`select_one`, required: false): Select "Yes" if you feel you are ready to start the course, and "No" if you are not confident in your current level of knowledge or preparation.
* **Page 2** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the options that best describe your current skills or experience related to the course subject matter. This will help the instructor or administrator get a better understanding of your background and abilities.
* **Page 3** (`text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of any relevant personal or professional experience you have that is not listed on the previous page.
* **Page 4** (`time`, required: false): Record the amount of time you are able to dedicate to the course, in hours or minutes per week.

Note: The options for each page are not exhaustive, and you can select all or only some of the options that apply to your situation.
