# Family Size Impact Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this quiz is to gather data on the impact of family size on various aspects of an individual's or household's life.

## How To Complete This Form

This form consists of multiple sections, each focusing on different aspects of family life. Please go through the form carefully and answer each question truthfully. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **House Size** (house_size): This field asks for the number of people living in your household.
* **Living Arrangement** (living_arrangement): This field asks for the type of living arrangement, such as apartment, house, condo, or other.
* **Household Income** (income_range): This field asks for the average household income, categorized as low, medium, high, or very high.
* **Financial Impact** (financial_impact): This field is not applicable in this form.
* **Energy Consumption** (energy_consumption): This field asks for the average monthly energy consumption in your household.
* **Transportation Method** (transportation_method): This field asks for the primary type of fuel used for transportation, such as gasoline, diesel, electric, or other.
* **Household Size** (household_size): This field is a duplicate of "House Size" and is not applicable.
* **Financial Support** (financial_support): This field asks if you receive financial support from household members, and if so, to what extent (true, false, partially).
* **House Type** (house_type): This field asks for the type of housing you live in, such as single-family house, multi-family house, or other.
* **Family Status** (family_status): This field asks for your marital status, categorized as married, single, divorced, or separated.
* **Family Members** (family_members): This field asks for the number of family members living with you.
* **Family Age** (family_age): This field asks for the age of the youngest household member.
* **Family Education** (family_education): This field asks for the average education level of household members, categorized as elementary school, high school, bachelor, master, or PhD.
* **Family Income** (family_income): This field asks for the average annual household income, categorized as low, medium, high, or very high.
* **Environmental Footprint** (family_environmental_footprint): This field asks for the average environmental footprint of your household.
* **Financial Contribution** (family_financial_contribution): This field asks for the average financial contribution of your household members, categorized as high, medium, or low.
* **Financial Support** (financial_support): This field is a duplicate of "Financial Support" and is not applicable.
* **Environmental Contribution** (family_environmental_contribution): This field asks for the average environmental contribution of your household members, categorized as high, medium, or low.
* **Transportation** (family_transportation): This field asks for the main mode of transportation used.
* **Financial Savings** (family_financial_savings): This field asks for the number of months you have saved money in your household.
* **Environmental Savings** (family_environmental_savings): This field asks for the number of months you have saved money for the environment.
* **Housing Type** (family_housing_type): This field asks for the type of housing you live in, categorized as single-family house, multi-family house, townhouse, or other.
* **Housing Size** (family_housing_size): This field asks for the size of the house you live in, categorized as small, medium, or big.
* **Financial Security** (family_financial_security): This field asks for how secure you feel about your financial situation, categorized as high, medium, or low.
* **Financial Stress** (family_financial_stress): This field asks for how stressed you feel about your financial situation, categorized as high, medium, or low.
