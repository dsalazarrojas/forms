# Motion And Chemistry Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Motion And Chemistry Assessment form is designed to evaluate students' backgrounds and interests in motion and chemistry. It is used to gather information about students' knowledge and experience in these subjects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Student ID number in the field labeled "Student ID".
2. Provide your name in the field labeled "Student Name".
3. Enter your age in the field labeled "Age".
4. Select your chemistry background from the options listed in the field labeled "Chemistry Background".
5. Select your motion background from the options listed in the field labeled "Motion Background".
6. Indicate whether you have a science classroom setting for your lessons from the options listed in the field labeled "Science Classroom".
7. Describe any motion-related interests you have in the field labeled "Motion Interests".
8. Describe any chemistry-related interests you have in the field labeled "Chemistry Interests".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student ID (1)**: Enter your student ID number to identify yourself as a student.
* **Student Name (2)**: Provide your name as it appears on your student ID or other official documentation.
* **Age (3)**: Enter your age to help us understand your experience level.
* **Chemistry Background (4)**: Select all that apply from the options provided to describe your background in chemistry.
	+ Chemistry 101: You have a basic understanding of chemistry.
	+ Chemistry 202: You have an intermediate understanding of chemistry.
	+ Chemistry 203: You have an advanced understanding of chemistry.
	+ Other: Please specify your background in chemistry.
* **Motion Background (5)**: Select all that apply from the options provided to describe your background in motion.
	+ Motion 101: You have a basic understanding of motion.
	+ Motion 202: You have an intermediate understanding of motion.
	+ Motion 203: You have an advanced understanding of motion.
	+ Other: Please specify your background in motion.
* **Science Classroom (6)**: Indicate whether you have a science classroom setting for your lessons.
	+ True: You have a science classroom setting.
	+ False: You do not have a science classroom setting.
	+ Unsure: You are unsure or do not know.
* **Motion Interests (7)**: Describe any interests you have in motion, including topics or activities.
* **Chemistry Interests (8)**: Describe any interests you have in chemistry, including topics or activities.
