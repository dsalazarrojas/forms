# music_trivia_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to test users' knowledge of music genres, styles, and trivia. It contains a series of questions to determine users' preferences and familiarity with different music styles and genres.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question according to your preferences and knowledge.
2. For the select-one fields (music genre, correct answer, music style, music influence, and favorite band), select one option that best suits your answer.
3. For the select-multiple fields (favorite bands and music influence), select all options that apply.
4. For the text fields (music genre, favorite band, and answer), enter your answer in the corresponding field.
5. For the number field (music year), enter the year in which you think the music style or genre emerged.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Music Genre** (`music_genre`, text, optional): Please enter your favorite music genre (e.g., rock, pop, jazz, classical).
* **Favorite Bands** (`favorite_bands`, select_multiple, optional): Select all your favorite bands from the available options.
* **Correct Answer** (`correct_answer`, select_one, optional): Choose the correct answer to the question (correct or incorrect).
* **Music Style** (`music_style`, select_one, optional): Select the music style that best describes your taste in music (rock, pop, jazz, classical).
* **Music Influence** (`music_influence`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the music styles that influence your taste in music.
* **Music Year** (`music_year`, number, optional): Enter the year in which you think the music style or genre emerged.
* **Favorite Band** (`favorite_band`, text, optional): Enter your favorite band, if you have one.
* **Answer** (`answer`, text, optional): Enter any additional answer or comment about your favorite music.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer each question honestly, and have fun!
