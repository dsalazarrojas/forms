# foundation_shade_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The foundation_shade_quiz form is used to determine the best foundation shade for a person's skin tone and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each question and select the answer that best matches your skin characteristics. All questions are optional, and you can skip any questions that do not apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your natural skin tone?** (Light, Medium, Dark): This question helps determine your natural skin tone, which is how light or dark your skin is naturally.
* **What is your undertone?** (Cool, Warm): This question helps determine the undertone of your skin, which is how your skin looks under the surface.
* **What is your skin type?** (Normal, Oily, Dry): This question helps determine your skin type, which is how your skin feels and looks.
* **Are you looking for a foundation shade that matches your skin tone?** (Yes, No): This question helps determine if you want a foundation that matches your natural skin tone.
