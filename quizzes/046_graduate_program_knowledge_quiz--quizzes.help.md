The purpose of this form is to collect information from students about their knowledge and understanding of the graduate program they are interested in. The form asks for student identification (name and email) and then a series of multiple-choice questions, some of which are about yes/no answers and some about selecting between multiple options. Finally, the form ends with a submit button to allow students to submit their answers.

## Purpose
The Graduate Program Knowledge Quiz is designed to help us understand your background, interests, and knowledge about our graduate programs. By answering the questions, you will be able to demonstrate your understanding of the graduate programs we offer and help us better understand what you're looking for in a graduate program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your Student Name and email address.
2. Answer each of the multiple-choice questions by selecting the options that best fit your experience or knowledge.
3. For yes/no questions, simply select "Yes" or "No".
4. For questions that ask you to select between multiple options, choose the one that best fits your understanding or experience.
5. Once you've finished answering all the questions, click the "Submit" button to send your answers to us.
