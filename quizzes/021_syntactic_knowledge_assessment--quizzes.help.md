# Syntactic Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an individual's understanding of syntactic knowledge, specifically the structure of sentences, by presenting them with a series of questions. The results will help evaluate their ability to identify the correct order of words and the correct use of verb tenses in sentences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and choose the correct answer from the available options.
2. Type your answers to the open-ended questions (User Input, My answer 1, My answer 2) in the text fields provided.
3. Provide additional comments if you have any further insights or explanations.
4. Enter your email and phone number for contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, `text`, optional): This field is for any additional information you'd like to provide that doesn't fit into the other categories. You can type anything you think is relevant to the assessment.
* **What is the correct order of words in a sentence?** (`question_1`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one of the options: Correct, Incorrect, or Unsure.
* **What is the correct use of verb tenses in the sentence?** (`question_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply (Correct, Incorrect, Unsure).
* **My answer 1** (`user_answer_1`, `text`, optional): Please provide your answer to the first question in this field.
* **My answer 2** (`user_answer_2`, `text`, optional): Please provide your answer to the second question in this field.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or explanations you have about your answers.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further information.

## Tips
* Make sure to review each question carefully before answering.
* Take your time to provide thorough answers.
* If you're unsure about any question, choose the "Unsure" option.
