# Hair Consultation Quiz Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair Consultation Quiz Template is a tool designed to provide personalized hair consultation results based on individual's hair characteristics and goals. This form will help users find the best hair style, color, and cut that suits them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start with the intro page, where you'll find a welcome message.
2. On the "hair_details" page, select your hair type from the options (blonde, brunette, redhead).
3. On the "hair_goals" page, select all the hair goals that apply to you (color, cut, style).
4. On the "hair_type" page, enter your hair type (if it's not already selected).
5. On the "hair_color" page, enter your hair color.
6. On the "hair_length" page, enter your hair length.
7. On the "hair_style" page, select your hair style (curly, wavy, straight, curly wavy, or curly straight).
8. On the "hair_texture" page, select your hair texture (fine, medium, or coarse).
9. On the "hair_damage" page, enter your hair damage level (if any).
10. On the "hair_goals_other" page, select all the hair goals that apply to you (color, cut, style, length).
11. On the "quiz_questions" page, write any additional information you'd like to provide.
12. On the "additional_info" page, provide any additional information you'd like to share.
13. On the "quiz_result" page, the form will provide your personalized hair consultation result.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Hair Type** (`hair_details`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is where you select your hair type. Choose from the options: blonde, brunette, or redhead.
- **Hair Goals** (`hair_goals`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field allows you to select all the hair goals that apply to you (color, cut, style).
- **Hair Type** (`hair_type`, `text`, required: false): Enter your hair type here.
- **Hair Color** (`hair_color`, `text`, required: false): Enter your hair color.
- **Hair Length** (`hair_length`, `number`, required: false): Enter your hair length.
- **Hair Style** (`hair_style`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your hair style from the options: curly, wavy, straight, curly wavy, or curly straight.
- **Hair Texture** (`hair_texture`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your hair texture from the options: fine, medium, or coarse.
- **Hair Damage** (`hair_damage`, `number`, required: false): If you have any hair damage, enter it here.
- **Hair Goals Other** (`hair_goals_other`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the hair goals that apply to you (color, cut, style, length).
- **Quiz Questions** (`quiz_questions`, `text`, required: false): Write any additional information you'd like to provide.
- **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional information you'd like to share.
- **Quiz Result** (`quiz_result`, `text`, required: false): This is where the form will provide your personalized hair consultation result.
