# Basic English Exam - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the English language proficiency of a student by evaluating their reading, vocabulary, grammar, and writing skills.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly to get an accurate assessment of your English skills.
2. Provide your Student name, ID, and Test date as requested on the first page.
3. Answer each question accurately according to your ability.
4. For the "What is a synonym for happy?" question, select a word that means the same as happy.
5. For the "What does the word persistent mean?" question, choose the definition that best fits the word.
6. In the "Fill in the blank" question, choose the correct word that best completes the sentence.
7. In the "Choose the correct sentence" question, select the sentence that uses the correct article (a, an, the).
8. In the "Select the correct verb tense" question, choose the correct verb that matches the sentence.
9. In the "Which sentence uses the correct article?" question, select the sentence that uses the correct article.
10. In the "Write a short paragraph about your favorite place" question, write a minimum of 50 words about a place you enjoy.
11. In the "Correct this sentence" question, correct the sentence by replacing "She dont" with the correct subject-verb agreement.
12. In the "Listening Comprehension (Description Based)" section, read the conversation carefully and answer the questions accurately.
