# Examination Preparation Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your understanding of a specific topic or subject, helping you prepare for an examination.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each page carefully.
2. On the "Introduction" page, you will find a general description of the examination. Please read it carefully and make sure you understand what the examination covers.
3. On the "questions" page, select all answers that apply to you. If you are unsure, choose "No" and provide a brief explanation in the answers page.
4. On the "answers" page, select one answer that best describes your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`text`, `text`, optional): Please read and understand the examination description.
* **Questions** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all options that apply to you. If you are unsure, choose "No" and provide a brief explanation in the next field.
* **Answers** (`select_one`, optional): Select one answer that best describes your situation.
 
## Tips
* Please answer truthfully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about an answer, choose "No" and provide a clear explanation.
* Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form.
