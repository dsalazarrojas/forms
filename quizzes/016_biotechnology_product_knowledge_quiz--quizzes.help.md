# Biotechnology Product Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This quiz is designed to assess your knowledge and understanding of biotechnology concepts, applications, and related terminologies. It will guide you through a series of questions, covering various aspects of biotechnology, from DNA sequencing to genetic engineering.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your participant information:
	* **Full Name**: Provide your full name.
	* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
	* **Your Role**: Describe your role or department.
2. Answer the following questions:
	* **Question 1**: Select the best answer to describe the primary function of biotechnology in drug development.
	* **Question 2**: Choose the technique used to identify specific DNA sequences.
	* **Question 3**: Select the best answer to describe what CRISPR-Cas9 technology allows scientists to do.
	* **Question 4**: Select the best answer to describe an example of a monoclonal antibody therapeutic.
	* **Question 5**: Choose the purpose of a clinical trial Phase 1 study.
	* **Question 6**: Select the term that refers to the insertion of genetic material into a cell.
	* **Question 7**: Select the main advantage of producing recombinant proteins using biotechnology.
	* **Question 8**: Choose the regulatory agency that approves new biotech products in the United States.
	* **Question 9**: Select the best answer to describe what bioprinting is primarily used for in biotechnology.
	* **Question 10**: Choose the description that best fits personalized medicine in biotech.
3. Reflect on your knowledge:
	* **Question 11**: Provide a brief explanation of synthetic biology to a non-scientist.
	* **Question 12**: Describe a real-world application of biotechnology that interests you most.
	* **Question 13**: Provide your perspective on the ethical considerations in genetic engineering.
4. Self-Assessment:
	* **Your Overall Confidence in Biotechnology Knowledge**: Rate your confidence level from 1-10.
	* **Has this Training Improved your Biotech Knowledge**: Select the level of improvement.
	* **Additional Comments or Feedback**: Share any thoughts or feedback about the quiz.
