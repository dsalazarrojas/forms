# Orientation Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Orientation Quiz is a form designed to help users understand their orientation in a given environment or situation. This quiz is intended to gauge a user's familiarity with the surroundings, their ability to navigate, and their overall comfort level in the given context.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Orientation Quiz, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `name` field.
2. Provide your email address in the `email` field.
3. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field.
4. Select one option from the `select one` field.
5. Select multiple options from the `select multiple` field.
6. Enter any additional notes or comments in the `note` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name (Text, Required)**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents or identification.
* **email (Email, Required)**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **phone (Text, Optional)**: Enter your phone number if you want us to reach out to you.
* **select one (Select One, Optional)**: Choose one option from the available list to help us understand your orientation.
* **select multiple (Select Multiple, Optional)**: Choose multiple options from the available list to help us understand your orientation.
* **note (Note, Optional)**: Enter any additional comments or notes you would like to share with us.
* **note 1-16 (Note, Optional)**: Enter any additional comments or notes you would like to share with us, with a specific focus on a particular aspect of your orientation.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when providing information.
- Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
