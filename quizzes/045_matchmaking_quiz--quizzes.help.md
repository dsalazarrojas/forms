# Matchmaking Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Matchmaking Quiz is a form designed to help users match with potential partners based on their preferences, goals, and values. This form collects information about the user's age, relationship status, match goals, attributes they are looking for in a partner, their values, location, social media presence, email and phone number, education, income, personality, and interests. The goal of this form is to provide a comprehensive profile of the user and help them find potential matches.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start the quiz, click on "Complete Quiz" button.
2. You will be directed to a series of pages that ask for different information about yourself.
3. For each page, you will see a "Skip Quiz" button which will allow you to skip the current page and continue with the quiz.
4. You can also reset the quiz at any time using the "Reset Quiz" button.
5. If you need help, you can click on the "Help" button for guidance.
6. Once you have completed all the pages, click on "Complete Quiz" to submit your information and receive your match results.
