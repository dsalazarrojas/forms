# optical_illusion_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to provide feedback and data through a series of questions and a text input field. The form is intended to be user-friendly and accessible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start on the "Introduction" page, where you can read the form's introduction.
2. On the "Questions" pages, select one of the available options for each question. You can choose from A, B, C, or D.
3. On the "Feedback" page, type any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
4. Finally, submit your form by clicking on the "Submit" button, and enter your name and email address if you would like to be contacted for further correspondence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is the first page of the form, which explains the purpose and guidelines of the form.
* **Question 1**: Select one of the options (A, B, C, or D) that best answers the question.
* **Question 2**: Select one of the options (A, B, C, or D) that best answers the question.
* **Question 3**: Select all the options (A, B, C, or D) that apply to your answer.
* **Feedback**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your form and review your answers.
* **Name**: Enter your name to be contacted for further correspondence.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to be contacted for further correspondence.
