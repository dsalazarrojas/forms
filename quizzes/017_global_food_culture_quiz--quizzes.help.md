# Global Food Culture Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your global food culture preferences, including your views on the country of origin, cuisine type, and your interaction with specific food items.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully, making sure to choose the most accurate response based on your genuine opinions and experiences.
2. Select the options that best fit your views on the global food culture questions.
3. For the "Food Item" questions, select the items that you have interacted with or have opinions about.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Country Of Origin** (Yes/No, required: false): Please indicate whether you believe the food you interacted with has a strong connection to a specific country or region where it originated. Choose "Yes" if you associate the food with its country of origin.
* **Cuisine Type** (Yes/No, required: false): Please select the type of cuisine you think best describes the food you interacted with. This can be based on your personal experience or general knowledge.
* **Global Food Culture** (Yes/No, required: false): Choose "Yes" if you believe the food you interacted with has a significant impact on or reflects the global food culture.
* **Food Item** (Yes/No, required: false): Select the food item that best represents your global food culture preferences. Please choose items that you have interacted with or have strong opinions about.
* **Food Item 1** (Yes/No, required: false) and **Food Item 2** (Yes/No, required: false): Similar to the "Food Item" question, select the specific items that best represent your global food culture preferences.
* **Quiz Questions** (Yes/No, required: false): Please choose whether you find the quiz questions helpful in evaluating global food culture.

Note: This form is designed for research purposes, and your responses will be kept anonymous. Please answer honestly, and you will not be contacted with follow-up questions.

## Tips
* Please take your time to answer each question carefully, and avoid selecting options that you're unsure about.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, feel free to ask, and we'll be happy to clarify.
* This form should take approximately 5-7 minutes to complete.
