# Halloween Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user responses to various Halloween-themed questions, including favorite Halloween movies, scores, actors, games, comments, and other related topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select your favorite Halloween movie(s) from the list.
2. Enter your best Halloween score, if applicable.
3. Choose your favorite Halloween actor(s) from the list.
4. Select your favorite Halloween game(s) from the list.
5. Enter any additional comments or thoughts about Halloween.
6. Optionally, enter a score for the Halloween experience.
7. If you have any other favorite Halloween movie or game, enter it in the designated field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Favorite Halloween Movie** (`halloween_movies`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or multiple favorite Halloween movies from the list. This field is used to gather information about your favorite Halloween movies.
* **Best Halloween Score** (`best_halloween_scores`, `number`, required/optional): If you have achieved a score in a Halloween-related activity or game, enter it here. This field is used to track your performance in Halloween-themed games or activities.
* **Best Halloween Actors** (`best_halloween_actors`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose one or multiple favorite Halloween actors from the list. This field helps us understand who you think are the best in Halloween-themed movies or games.
* **Favorite Halloween Game** (`halloween_games`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or multiple favorite Halloween games from the list. This field gathers information about your favorite Halloween games.
* **Comments** (`halloween_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or thoughts about Halloween. This field is for any miscellaneous information you'd like to share about Halloween.
* **Score** (`halloween_score_1`, `number`, required/optional): Enter a score, if applicable, to track your performance in a Halloween-themed game or activity.
* **Other Halloween Movie** (`halloween_movies_2`, `text`, required/optional): If you have a favorite Halloween movie that is not on the list, enter it here. This field allows you to specify any other favorite movie related to Halloween.
* **Other Halloween Game** (`halloween_games_2`, `text`, required/optional): If you have a favorite Halloween game that is not on the list, enter it here. This field enables you to specify any other favorite game related to Halloween.
* **Halloween Comments 2** (`halloween_comments_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or thoughts about Halloween. This field is for any miscellaneous information you'd like to share about Halloween.
