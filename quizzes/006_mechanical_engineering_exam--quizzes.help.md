# Mechanical Engineering Exam - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mechanical Engineering Exam form is designed to collect information about a student's knowledge in mechanical engineering. This form aims to assess a student's understanding of various topics, including mechanical systems, components, problem-solving, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the correct exam paper ID, which is a unique identifier for the exam.
2. Enter the date and time of the exam, including the date and time you took the exam.
3. Provide information about the exam location where you took the exam.
4. Answer any technical questions related to the exam.
5. Add any notes about the exam, such as any difficulties or areas of improvement.
6. Enter your results after completing the exam.
7. Provide answers to any technical questions asked.
8. Add any comments about the exam, such as feedback or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Placement Exam** (`placement_exam`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about the placement exam you took.
* **Exam Paper Id** (`exam_paper_id`, text, optional): Enter the ID of the exam paper you took.
* **Mechanical Systems** (`mechanical_systems`, text, optional): Describe the mechanical systems you studied or worked with.
* **Mechanical Components** (`mechanical_components`, text, optional): List any mechanical components you used or studied.
* **Problem Solving** (`problem_solving`, text, optional): Describe any problems you encountered or overcame.
* **Technical Questions** (`technical_questions`, text, optional): Provide answers to any technical questions asked.
* **Exam Duration** (`exam_duration`, text, optional): Enter the duration of the exam.
* **Exam Date** (`exam_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you took the exam.
* **Exam Time** (`exam_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you took the exam.
* **Exam Location** (`exam_location`, text, optional): Describe the location where you took the exam.
* **Mechanical Engineering Exam** (`mechanical_engineering_exam`, text, optional): This field is not clearly defined. It could be an optional field for notes or comments.
* **Mechanical Engineering Exam Notes** (`mechanical_engineering_exam_notes`, text, optional): This field seems to be redundant with the previous one. It could be used for additional notes or comments.
* **Exam Results** (`exam_results`, text, optional): Enter your results after completing the exam.
* **Technical Questions Answers** (`technical_questions_answers`, text, optional): Provide answers to any technical questions asked.
* **Exam Comments** (`exam_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or feedback about the exam.

Note: Some fields seem to be redundant or not clearly defined. To avoid confusion, it's recommended to focus on the essential fields like placement exam, exam date, exam location, exam results, and technical questions answers.
