# Character Study Reflection - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Character Study Reflection form is used to analyze and reflect on a literary character, exploring their motivations, turning points, and impact on the student.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

### Step 1: Header
* -- Character Study Reflection --

### Step 2: Student Name
* **Student name** (optional for anonymous submission)

### Step 3: Character Name
* **Character name** (required)

### Step 4: Key Motivations
* **Key Motivations** (required)

### Step 5: Turning Points
* **Turning points** (optional)

### Step 6: Personal Response
* **Personal Response** (required)

### Step 7: Evidence from Text
* **Evidence from text** (required)

### Step 8: Lessons Learned
* **Lessons Learned** (optional)

### Step 9: Instructor Comments
* **Instructor comments** (optional)

### Step 10: Submission Date
* **Submission date** (required)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Character Study Reflection**: 
  * This form is used to analyze and reflect on a literary character.
* **Student name** (`student_name`):
  * Enter your name for reference, but you can also submit anonymously.
  * This field is not required but can help the instructor know who submitted the reflection.
* **Character name** (`character_name`):
  * Type in the name of the character being studied.
  * This field is required to ensure you're analyzing a specific character.
* **Key Motivations** (`key_motivations`):
  * Describe what drives the character.
  * This field is required to understand the character's motivations.
* **Turning points** (`turning_points`):
  * Describe important moments that changed the character.
  * This field is optional but can add depth to your analysis.
* **Personal Response** (`personal_response`):
  * Explain how the character made you feel and why.
  * This field is required to understand your personal connection to the character.
* **Evidence from text** (`evidence_from_text`):
  * Provide quotes or scene references that support your analysis.
  * This field is required to support your claims with concrete evidence.
* **Lessons Learned** (`lessons_learned`):
  * Reflect on what you learned from the character.
  * This field is optional but can help you reflect on your understanding.
* **Instructor comments** (`instructor_comments`):
  * This is a space for teacher feedback.
  * This field is optional and can be used by the instructor for guidance.
* **Submission date** (`submission_date`):
  * Enter the date you submitted this reflection.
  * This field is required to track submission dates.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form.
* Use quotes or scene references to support your analysis.
* Reflect on how the character made you feel and why.
* Use this form as a tool to develop your critical thinking and writing skills.
