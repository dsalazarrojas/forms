# Surfboard Recommendation Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to help recommend a surfboard to a user based on their preferences and skills.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully, considering your surfing style, level, and preferences.
2. Select the options that best fit your surfing style and preferences.
3. Fill out all required fields (indicated by a red asterisk (*)).
4. Once you have completed all the fields, submit the form to get your personalized surfboard recommendation.
