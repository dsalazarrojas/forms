# Women In Sports History Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information and record their progress in a quiz about women's sports history. The quiz is meant to be a fun and educational experience that tests the participant's knowledge on the subject.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Start Quiz" button to begin the quiz.
2. Answer the quiz questions on the first page.
3. If you want to save your progress, click on the "Save Progress" button. Otherwise, submit your answers and proceed to the next page.
4. Fill out the participant information on the following pages.
5. Once you have completed all the questions, click on the "Submit" button to finalize your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Quiz Questions** (<code>quiz_questions</code>, required: false): This page contains the quiz questions. Answer each question carefully and accurately to the best of your knowledge.
* **Participant Name** (<code>participant_name</code>, required: false): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the quiz results.
* **Email** (<code>participant_email</code>, required: false): Enter your email address if you want to receive any notifications or updates about the quiz results.
* **Phone** (<code>participant_phone</code>, required: false): Enter your phone number if you want to receive any notifications or updates about the quiz results.
* **Quiz Completed** (<code>quiz_completed</code>, required: false): This field is not applicable as this is a text input and does not seem to have any relation to the actual quiz completion time. Please disregard this field.

Note: This field-by-field explanation does not include the actual field labels but uses the field names as they appear in the YAML inputSchema. 

## Tips
* Please answer the questions carefully and accurately to the best of your knowledge.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the quiz administrators for assistance.
* Make sure to provide a valid email and phone number if you want to receive any notifications or updates about the quiz results.
