# ACD Knowledge Check - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "ACD Knowledge Check" form is designed to evaluate the knowledge and skills of employees who have completed a specific type of training. This form is intended for trainees to assess their understanding of the ACD system's features, usage, and effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in your complete name, employee ID, and email address.
2. Select your primary department from the list provided.
3. Enter the date of the assessment.
4. Choose your level of completion for previous ACD training modules.
5. Rate your confidence level with ACD systems on a scale of 1-10.
6. Enter your average call handling time in decimal format.
7. Rate your satisfaction level with ACD Knowledge Check.
8. Select all the features of the ACD system that you regularly use.
9. Choose the areas that you find most challenging with ACD.
10. Rate your overall satisfaction with the ACD system on a scale of 1-10.
11. Select all the aspects that you found helpful to your experience with ACD Knowledge Check.
12. Rate the effectiveness of the training in preparing you for real scenarios.
13. Choose the additional support that would help you with ACD systems.
14. Add any additional comments or feedback about the ACD system or training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, **required**): Please enter your complete name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Email address** (`email_address`, `email`, **required**): We'll use this to send your results.
*   **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `text`, **required**): Enter your 6-digit employee identification number.
*   **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your primary department from the list provided.
*   **Date of assessment** (`date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date of the assessment.
*   **Have you completed previous ACD training modules?** (`previous_training`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose your level of completion for previous ACD training modules.
*   **Rate your confidence level with ACD systems** (`confidence_level`, `integer`, **required**): Rate your confidence level with ACD systems on a scale of 1-10.
*   **Average call handling time** (`call_handling_time`, `decimal`, **required**): Enter your average call handling time in decimal format.
*   **How satisfied are you with ACD Knowledge Check?** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, **required**): Rate your satisfaction level with ACD Knowledge Check on a scale from Very satisfied to Very dissatisfied.
*   **Which ACD features do you regularly use?** (`system_features_used`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select all the features of the ACD system that you regularly use.
*   **Which areas do you find most challenging?** (`difficulty_areas`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Choose the areas that you find most challenging with ACD.
*   **Overall rating** (`overall_rating`, `integer`, **required**): Rate your overall satisfaction with the ACD system on a scale of 1-10.
*   **Which aspects applied to your experience with ACD Knowledge Check?** (`experience_aspects`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select all the aspects that you found helpful to your experience with ACD Knowledge Check.
*   **How effective was the training in preparing you for real scenarios?** (`training_effectiveness`, `select_one`, **required**): Rate the effectiveness of the training in preparing you for real scenarios.
*   **What additional support would help you with ACD systems?** (`support_needed`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select all the additional support that would help you with ACD systems.
*   **What would you improve or change about ACD Knowledge Check?** (`open_feedback`, `text`, **required**): Please be specific about your suggestions.
*   **Additional comments or notes** (`additional_comments`, `text`, **required**): Add any other feedback about the ACD system or training.

## Tips

*   Please fill in the form carefully and truthfully.
*   Review each field before answering to ensure you are answering accurately.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please ask your trainer or manager for assistance.
*   This form will help in identifying areas that require further training or support.
*   It will aid in improving the ACD system and training process as a whole.
