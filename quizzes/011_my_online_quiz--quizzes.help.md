This help guide is to be used in conjunction with the "my_online_quiz" form. Please note that the form's purpose and fields may change over time. This guide provides the most up-to-date information as of its creation.

## Purpose
The "my_online_quiz" form is intended for users to provide general information, questions, options, and other relevant details about a quiz. This form allows users to create and customize a quiz according to their preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the required fields to the best of your ability.
2. Fill in the remaining fields as needed, based on your specific quiz requirements.
3. Review your input carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (1)**: Enter a brief description of your quiz.
* **question (2)**: Type in the main question for your quiz.
* **options (3)**: Select one or multiple options for your quiz. You can choose from the available options provided.
* **submit (4)**: Choose whether to submit or cancel your quiz. Please note that submitting this form is required.
* **date (5)**: Enter the date for your quiz, if applicable.
* **time (6)**: Enter the time for your quiz, if applicable.
* **email (7)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes, if applicable.
* **phone (8)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes, if applicable.
* **description (9)**: Provide a detailed description of your quiz.
* **answer (10)**: Enter the expected answer for your quiz.
* **category (11)**: Enter the category for your quiz.
* **quiz_type (12)**: Enter the type of quiz.
* **score (13)**: Enter the score for your quiz, if applicable.
* **duration (14)**: Enter the duration of your quiz, if applicable.
* **status (15)**: Enter the status of your quiz, if applicable.

Please review the form carefully and ensure that all required fields are filled before submission. If you have any questions or need further assistance, feel free to ask.
