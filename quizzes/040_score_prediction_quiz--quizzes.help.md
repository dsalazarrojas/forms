# score-prediction-quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect score predictions from users for sports events. It is meant to be completed by users who want to provide their forecasts for the outcome of a game or match.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields: Submitter Email, Submitter Name, and Submitter Organization.
2. If you want to provide additional details, fill in any of the other fields as needed.
3. Select your score predictions from the "Score Predictions" field.
4. Confirm your submission by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter Email** (`id: 9`, `type: email`, `required: true`): This is the email address of the person submitting the form. Enter a valid email address.
* **Submitter Name** (`id: 11`, `type: text`, `required: true`): This is the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Organization** (`id: 21`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This is the organization or company of the person submitting the form. This field is optional.
* **Score Predictions** (`id: 3`, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`): This field allows you to select your score predictions for the game or match. Choose one of the available options, such as "Team A Wins" or "Team B Wins".
* **Team A Goals** (`id: 4`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This field is for entering the number of goals you think Team A will score.
* **Team B Goals** (`id: 5`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This field is for entering the number of goals you think Team B will score.
* **Submitter Location** (`id: 20`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This field is for the location where the person submitting the form is from or based. This field is optional.
* **I Agree** (`id: 17`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This field is for confirming that you have read and agreed to the terms of submission. This field is required for submission.
* **Submitter Date** (`id: 18`, `type: date`, `required: false`): This field is for the date of submission.
* **Submitter Time** (`id: 19`, `type: time`, `required: false`): This field is for the time of submission.
* **Submitter Job Title** (`id: 22`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This field is for entering the job title of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Job Function** (`id: 23`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This field is for entering the job function of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Job Title 2** (`id: 24`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This field is for entering the job title of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Job Function 2** (`id: 25`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This field is for entering the job function of the person submitting the form.
