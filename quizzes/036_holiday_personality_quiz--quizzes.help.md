# Holiday Personality Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you determine your holiday personality by asking a series of questions about your holiday habits and preferences. Your responses will help us better understand how you celebrate holidays and what kind of personality type you have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Go through each page of questions, reading each one carefully.
2. For the "Holiday Personality Quiz Category" and "Holiday Personality Quiz Subcategory" fields, select the options that best describe you.
3. For the "Holiday Personality Quiz Question" and "Holiday Personality Quiz Answer" fields, type in your response to each question.
4. Review your answers and submit the form when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Holiday Personality Quiz Questions**: This is the first set of questions about your holiday habits and preferences.
* **Holiday Personality Quiz Category**: Choose the category that best fits your holiday personality:
	+ Yes: You identify with this personality type.
	+ No: You do not identify with this personality type.
* **Holiday Personality Quiz Subcategory**: Select all categories that apply to your holiday personality:
	+ Yes: You identify with this subcategory.
	+ No: You do not identify with this subcategory.
* **Holiday Personality Quiz Question**: Answer this question as honestly and thoroughly as possible.
* **Holiday Personality Quiz Answer**: Type your response to the question asked.
* **Holiday Personality Quiz Results**: This field will display your holiday personality quiz results after submitting the form.
