# Math Practice Worksheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to create a customized math practice worksheet for students to assess their skills and understanding in different math topics and grade levels.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name.
2. **Grade** (`grade_level`, text, required): Select the student's grade level from the options provided.
3. **Math Topic** (`math_topic`, select_one, optional): Choose a math topic from the dropdown menu, such as Algebra, Geometry, or Calculus.
4. **Written Work** (`written_work`, text, optional): Upload any written work related to the math topic, such as solutions or notes.
5. **Attachments** (`file_uploads`, note, optional): Attach any files relevant to the math topic, such as diagrams or charts.
6. **Confidence Rating** (`confidence_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate your confidence level in solving math problems related to the selected topic, with options High, Medium, or Low.
7. **Attachments 2** (`file_uploads_2`, note, optional): Attach any additional files relevant to the math topic.
8. **Student Name 2** (`student_name_2`, text, optional): Enter an additional student's name, if necessary.
9. **Attachments 3** (`file_uploads_3`, note, optional): Attach any more files relevant to the math topic.
10. **Student Name 3** (`student_name_3`, text, optional): Enter another student's name, if needed.
11. **Attachments 4** (`file_uploads_4`, note, optional): Attach further files relevant to the math topic.
12. **Grade** (`grade_level_2`, select_one, optional): Select the grade level for another student, from the options provided.
13. **Math Topic 2** (`math_topic_2`, select_one, optional): Choose another math topic for the student, from the dropdown menu.
14. **Attachments 5** (`file_uploads_5`, note, optional): Attach more files related to the math topic.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name to associate it with their practice worksheet.
* **Grade**: Select the student's grade level to tailor the worksheet to their skill level.
* **Math Topic**: Choose a specific math topic to focus on the student's understanding and confidence in that area.
* **Written Work**: Upload written work related to the math topic, such as problem solutions or notes, to help with self-assessment.
* **Attachments**: Attach relevant files, like diagrams or charts, to support the student's understanding.
* **Confidence Rating**: Rate your confidence level in solving math problems related to the selected topic.
* **Attachments 2**: Attach additional files relevant to the math topic.
* **Student Name 2**: Enter another student's name, if needed.
* **Attachments 3**: Attach more files related to the math topic.
* **Student Name 3**: Enter a third student's name, if necessary.
* **Attachments 4**: Attach further files relevant to the math topic.
* **Grade 2**: Select another student's grade level, from the options provided.
* **Math Topic 2**: Choose another math topic for the student, from the dropdown menu.
* **Attachments 5**: Attach more files related to the math topic.
