# Shorthand Skills Assessment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Shorthand Skills Assessment Quiz is designed to evaluate an individual's shorthand skills, including their ability to transcribe written text quickly and accurately, their proficiency in shorthand writing, and their overall transcription speed and accuracy. This quiz is meant to provide a standardized assessment of an individual's shorthand skills.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question according to your knowledge and experience.
2. Select the correct options for each question from the provided list.
3. Enter the required information in the designated fields.
4. Fill out the form completely and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (page 1, id: 1):
  • A brief description of the purpose and instructions of the quiz.
* **Shorthand Proficiency** (page 2, id: 2):
  • Select one or more options that describe your level of proficiency in shorthand:
    + Option 1: Basic knowledge of shorthand symbols and rules.
    + Option 2: Intermediate knowledge of shorthand writing and transcription.
    + Option 3: Advanced knowledge of shorthand writing and transcription.
* **Transcription Ability** (page 3, id: 3):
  • Enter the date of your last transcription experience.
* **Real-World Usage** (page 4, id: 4):
  • Select the number of hours per week you use shorthand in real-world situations.
* **Shorthand Practice** (page 5, id: 5):
  • Select one option that describes your practice schedule for shorthand:
    + Option 1: Daily practice.
    + Option 2: Weekly practice.
    + Option 3: Less than once a week.
