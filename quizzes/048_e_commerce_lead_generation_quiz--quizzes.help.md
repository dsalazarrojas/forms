# e_commerce_lead_generation_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e_commerce_lead_generation_quiz form is designed to help e-commerce businesses collect lead information from customers, enabling them to improve their sales and marketing strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in the required and optional fields as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First page** (First page, text, required/optional): The first page of the form is where you will enter the general information about your e-commerce lead generation.
- **Contact Information** (contact_information, select multiple, required/optional): Select the contact information you want to capture from the customers, such as email, phone, or address.
- **Lead Generation** (lead_generation, text, required/optional): Enter the information about the lead generation, such as the sales strategy or approach used.
- **Sales Person** (sales_person, select multiple, required/optional): Select the sales person(s) involved in the lead generation process.
- **Lead Capture** (lead_capture, text, required/optional): Enter the information about the lead capture, such as the methods used to capture the lead.
- **Lead Capture Methods** (lead_capture_methods, select multiple, required/optional): Select the methods used to capture the lead, such as email, phone, or mail.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to get the most out of the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the form title and description for more information.
- This form was created to simplify the lead generation process for e-commerce businesses, so feel free to modify it as needed to fit your specific use case.
