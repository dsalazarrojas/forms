# Shareholder Rights Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your knowledge about the rights and responsibilities of shareholders in a company. It will guide you through several questions that cover key aspects of shareholder rights, helping you evaluate your understanding of this domain.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply read each question carefully and answer accordingly. You do not need to provide any additional information or justification for your answers. If a field is marked as "required", please fill it out. If a field is not marked as "required", you can choose to fill it out or skip it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **What is a company?** (text) 
* This question is asking for a basic definition of a company. Please provide a brief description of what a company is and what it represents.

### 2. **Board meeting** (select_one)
* This question is asking about the involvement of shareholders in the decision-making process of a company. Please choose whether the company has a board meeting or not.

### 3. **What are the rights of a shareholder?** (text) 
* This question is asking about the privileges or advantages that a shareholder possesses. Please provide a brief description of what rights shareholders have.

### 4. **Dividend payout** (number) 
* This question is asking about the payment of dividends to shareholders. Please provide the amount or percentage of dividend paid out, if applicable.

### 5. **Shareholding Proportions** (select_one)
* This question is asking about the distribution of shares among shareholders. Please choose the correct proportion of shareholding among shareholders.

### 6. **Investor information** (text) 
* This question is asking for any other relevant information about the investor or shareholder. Please provide any additional details you think might be helpful.

### 7. **Voting rights** (select_one)
* This question is asking about the ability to participate in decision-making processes within the company. Please choose whether the shareholder has voting rights or not.

### 8. **Stock split** (number) 
* This question is asking about the event of stock split. Please provide the ratio or number of shares split, if applicable.

### 9. **Shareholder meeting** (select_one)
* This question is asking about the opportunity for shareholders to meet and discuss company matters. Please choose whether the company holds a shareholder meeting or not.

### 10. **Financial statements** (select_multiple)
* This question is asking about access to financial information about the company. Please choose which financial statements are available or provided to shareholders.
