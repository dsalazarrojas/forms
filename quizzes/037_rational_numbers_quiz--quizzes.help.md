# Rational Numbers Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rational Numbers Quiz is designed to test your understanding of rational numbers. Please answer the questions to the best of your ability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field according to the instructions provided for each field.
2. Enter your user name, student number (if applicable), and time taken (in minutes) on the respective fields.
3. Enter each rational number (e.g., 1/2) in the corresponding field, following the format "numerator/denominator".
4. If you have any additional comments or questions, please provide them in the "Note" field.
