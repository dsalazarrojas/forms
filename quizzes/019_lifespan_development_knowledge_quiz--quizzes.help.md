# Lifespan Development Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form, the Lifespan Development Knowledge Quiz, is designed to test your knowledge about the different stages of human development across a person's lifespan. The quiz covers various stages from infancy to adulthood, asking you to identify the correct age ranges associated with each stage. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the options provided for each question carefully.
2. Select the correct answer for each question.
3. Ensure you complete all required questions (indicated by a required label).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the first three years of life called?** (`first_three_years`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question asks about the first stage of human development. Choose one of the options: 
    * Infancy: This stage typically begins at birth and ends at 3 months.
    * Toddlerhood: This stage is from 3 to 4 years.
* **What is prenatal?** (`prenatal_pregnancy`, `text`, optional): This question asks about the time before birth.
* **Infancy** (`infancy`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks about the first stage of human development. Choose one of the options: 
    * Birth to 3 months
    * 3 to 6 months
    * 6 to 12 months
* **Toddlershood** (`toddlerhood`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks about the second stage of human development. Choose one of the options: 
    * 1 to 2 years
    * 2 to 3 years
    * 3 to 4 years
* **Childhood** (`childhood`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks about the third stage of human development. Choose one or more of the options: 
    * 2 to 6 years
    * 6 to 12 years
    * 12 to 18 years
* **Adolescence** (`adolescence`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks about the fourth stage of human development. Choose one of the options: 
    * 10 to 14 years
    * 14 to 18 years
* **Young Adulthood** (`young_adulthood`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks about the fifth stage of human development. Choose one of the options: 
    * 18 to 25 years
    * Other
* **Adulthood** (`adulthood`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks about the final stage of human development. Choose one of the options: 
    * 25 years and above
    * Other
* **What stage is the most important?** (`lifespan_stage_important`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks you to select the most important stage of human development from the provided options. Choose one of the options: 
    * None of the above
    * Infancy
    * Toddlerhood
    * Childhood
    * Adolescence
    * Young Adulthood
    * Adulthood
