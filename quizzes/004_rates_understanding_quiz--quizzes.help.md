# rates_understanding_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to test your understanding of rates and units. Please provide answers to the questions in the form to assess your knowledge.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer it to the best of your ability.
2. Fill in the blanks with your answers.
3. Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy.
4. Submit the form when you're ready.

* **Rates concept** (`rates_concept`, text, required): This question is about understanding the concept of rates. Describe your understanding of the term "rate" in the context of various fields such as chemistry, physics, or economics.
* **Quiz questions** (`quiz_questions`, text, required): This is where you'll provide the questions that will be used to test your understanding of rates. Write a few questions that relate to rates and units.
* **Quiz questions answer** (`quiz_questions_answer`, text, required): Provide the answers to the questions you listed in the previous field.
* **Unit rates concept** (`unit_rates_concept`, text, required): Describe your understanding of unit rates, a concept closely related to rates. 
* **Unit rates conversion** (`unit_rates_conversion`, text, required): Explain the process of converting between unit rates. 
* **Conversion factors** (`conversion_factors`, text, optional): If you want, provide any conversion factors that might be useful for converting between unit rates. 
* **Conversion factors 1** (`conversion_factors_1`, text, optional): Another option for providing conversion factors. 
* **Rates and unit rates** (`rates_and_unit_rates`, text, required): Describe the relationship between rates and unit rates. 
* **Rates and unit rates 1** (`rates_and_unit_rates_1`, text, optional): Provide another aspect of your understanding of rates and unit rates. 
* **Rates and unit rates 2** (`rates_and_unit_rates_2`, text, optional): More details about rates and unit rates, if you'd like. 
* **Rates and unit rates 3** (`rates_and_unit_rates_3`, text, optional): Additional information about rates and unit rates. 
* **Rates and unit rates 4** (`rates_and_unit_rates_4`, select_one, required): Select one of the options to demonstrate your understanding of rates and unit rates.
