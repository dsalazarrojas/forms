# Patriotism And National Identity Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge your thoughts and opinions on patriotism and national identity. It will ask you to provide responses to a series of questions, and you will be presented with options for select questions. Your feedback will help us better understand the perspectives on these topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Fill in your response to the introduction question on the first page.
* For questions 1 and 3, select all that apply from the options provided.
* For question 2, select only one option from the list.
* You can skip any question if you prefer, but please note that it may affect the overall accuracy of the results.
* Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **intro_question** (`intro_question`, text, optional): This is a text-based question where you can provide any thoughts or opinions you have on patriotism and national identity.
* **question1** (`question1`, select_one, optional): Choose the option that best aligns with your perspective on this topic.
* **question2** (`question2`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the options that apply to your views on this topic.
* **question3** (`question3`, text, optional): This is a text-based question where you can provide any thoughts or opinions you have on patriotism and national identity.

## Tips
* Be as honest and thoughtful in your responses as possible.
* Please note that your feedback is voluntary and will be kept confidential.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
* You can submit the form multiple times if you wish, but each submission will be treated as a separate response.
