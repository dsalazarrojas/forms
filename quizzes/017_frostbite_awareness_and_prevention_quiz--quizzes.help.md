# frostbite-awareness-and-prevention-quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The frostbite-awareness-and-prevention-quiz is designed to help users assess their knowledge and prevention methods for frostbite. This form will provide an overview of their understanding of frostbite and suggest areas where they need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
2. Answer the questions with the options provided, or type your own answer in the text fields.
3. Make sure to provide your contact information so we can get back to you with results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Frostbite Knowledge** (1): This question assesses your understanding of frostbite. Select a number from 0 to 100 to indicate how much you think you know about frostbite.
* **Frostbite Prevention** (2): Describe your current prevention methods for frostbite.
* **Frostbite Risk Level** (3): Rate your risk level for frostbite, where 1 is lowest and 100 is highest.
* **Frostbite Training Needs** (4): Select all areas where you would like additional training on frostbite awareness and prevention.
* **Frostbite Knowledge Confidence** (5): Select all areas where you feel confident with your knowledge of frostbite.
* **Frostbite Training Needs 2** (6): Describe the specific topics where you need additional training on frostbite awareness and prevention.
* **Frostbite Prevention Methods** (7): Select all prevention methods you are using for frostbite.
* **Frostbite Quiz Complete** (8): Date you completed this quiz, in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Frostbite Comments** (9): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the quiz.
* **Frostbite Email** (10): Enter your email address where we can get in touch with you.
* **Frostbite Phone** (11): Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Frostbite Name** (12): Enter your name.
* **Frostbite Age** (13): Enter your age.
* **Frostbite Location** (14): Enter your location.
* **Frostbite Organization** (15): Enter your organization name.

Note: Please fill out all required fields to get a complete assessment of your frostbite awareness and prevention methods.
