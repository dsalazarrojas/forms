# Who Is More Likely Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Who Is More Likely Quiz" is designed to help you decide who is more likely to achieve a specific outcome or scenario. The form consists of multiple questions that allow you to choose the most probable option.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct option from the "who" field for each question.
2. Enter the size of the group (if applicable) in the "group size" field.
3. Enter the names of two individuals (if applicable) in the "name 1" and "name 2" fields.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "note 1" field.
5. Finally, select the correct category from the "category" field.
6. Enter your email address (if applicable) and phone number (if applicable) in the respective fields.
7. Once you have filled out all the fields, click the "submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **who** (whos_more_likely): This field requires you to select who is more likely to achieve a specific outcome or scenario. You can choose one or multiple options from the list provided.
* **group_size** (group_size): If applicable, enter the size of the group you are considering. This field is optional, but it may help provide more context to your answer.
* **name1** (name1): Enter the name of the first individual you are considering.
* **name2** (name2): Enter the name of the second individual you are considering.
* **category** (category): Select the correct category from the list provided.
* **email** (email): If applicable, enter your email address for further communication.
* **phone** (phone): If applicable, enter your phone number for further communication.
* **note_1** (note_1): Enter any additional notes or comments about your answer.
* **submit** (submit): Click this button to submit your completed form.
