# Fruit Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fruit Knowledge Quiz form is designed to test your knowledge about fruits. It presents a series of questions about fruits and asks you to respond to each one.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each question and select the answer that best fits your response. You can find the questions and answer options below.

* **introduction** (note, optional): This field is a note that serves as an introduction to the quiz.
* **fruit-questions-1** (select one, optional): Choose "Yes" if you know the answer to this question, or "No" if you don't.
* **fruit-questions-2** (select one, optional): Choose "Yes" if you know the answer to this question, or "No" if you don't.

## Tips
* Please answer each question to the best of your knowledge.
* You can skip questions if you don't know the answer or are unsure.
* This form is for testing your knowledge about fruits, so feel free to take it as many times as you want.
