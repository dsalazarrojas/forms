# venture_capital_knowledge_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Venture Capital Knowledge Quiz is designed to assess users' understanding of venture capital and its various aspects. It helps to evaluate and improve knowledge internally, and the data collected is kept confidential.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions accurately and truthfully.
2. Review each field carefully before proceeding to ensure you understand what information is required.
3. Take as much time as you need to complete the form, as it is not a timed assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **venture capital knowledge quiz** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): This field is not actually a field but a section header.
* **Which of the following is a type of private equity firm?** (`quiz questions`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select one option from the list that best answers the question.
	+ *Venture Capital*: Private equity firms that invest in startups and early-stage companies.
	+ *Private Equity*: Private equity firms that invest in established companies.
	+ *Hedge Funds*: Investment funds that pool money from investors to invest in a variety of assets.
	+ *Family Offices*: Wealth management firms that handle investment and financial planning for high net worth individuals.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name or a pseudonym you prefer to be known by.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted for further information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further information.

## Tips
Please take your time to review each question carefully and answer accurately. If you are unsure about any question, please refer to external resources or seek guidance from experts in the field.
