# English Grammar And Vocabulary Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the student's English language skills and vocabulary knowledge.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions as accurately as possible.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all the options that apply.
3. Fill in the text fields with the required information.
4. Ensure that all fields are filled correctly before submitting.
5. If you need to provide any additional comments or notes, use the provided fields for submission status and notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: `false`): This is a general introduction to the form.
*  **Test Part 1** (`2`, `select_multiple`, `false`): This section presents multiple-choice options. Select all the options that apply to your situation.
* **Test Part 2** (`3`, `text`, `false`): In this section, please provide a short description of your answer to Test Part 1.
* **Answer Key** (`4`, `select_one`, `false`): Select the correct option for each question from Test Part 1.
* **Submission Status** (`5`, `select_one`, `false`): Choose the status of your submission: submitted, pending, or cancelled.
* **Learner Name** (`6`, `text`, `false`): Please enter your name as it appears on your school records.
* **School Name** (`7`, `text`, `false`): Enter the name of your school.
* **Submission Date** (`8`, `date`, `false`): Select the date of submission.
* **Submission Time** (`9`, `time`, `false`): Select the time of submission.
* **School ID** (`10`, `text`, `false`): Enter your school ID if applicable.
* **Email** (`11`, `email`, `false`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`12`, `text`, `false`): Enter your phone number.
* **Submission Notes** (`13`, `note`, `false`): If you have any additional comments, use this field.
* **Submission Status Notes** (`14`, `note`, `false`): Enter any status updates or comments related to your submission.
