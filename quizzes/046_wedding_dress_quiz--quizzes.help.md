# wedding dress quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help you find your perfect wedding dress style based on your preferences and style.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select your preferred wedding style from the options provided.
2. Choose your preferred neck line for your wedding dress.
3. Select your preferred neckline shape.
4. Choose your preferred sleeve type.
5. Select your preferred wedding length.
6. Choose your preferred wedding color.
7. Select your favorite features for your wedding dress.
8. Choose how you feel when you see a wedding dress.
9. Rate your comfort level in your wedding dress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Style** (`wedding_style`, select_one): Choose the style that best describes your wedding dress style, such as ball gown, mermaid, or tea party.
* **Neck Line** (`neck_line`, select_one): Select the neck line that you prefer for your wedding dress, such as none, sweep, or strapless.
* **Neckline Shape** (`neckline_shape`, select_one): Choose the neckline shape that you prefer, such as round, square, or sweetheart.
* **Sleeve Type** (`sleeve_type`, select_one): Select the sleeve type that you prefer, such as none, long, cap, or bell.
* **Wedding Length** (`wedding_length`, select_one): Choose the length of your wedding dress, such as mini, tea party, or floor length.
* **Wedding Color** (`wedding_color`, select_multiple): Select the colors you prefer for your wedding dress from the options provided.
* **Favorite Feature** (`favorite_feature`, select_multiple): Select any features that you would like to see on your wedding dress, such as beaded or appliques.
* **Overall Feeling** (`overall_feeling`, select_one): Choose how you feel when you see a wedding dress, such as romantic, classy, or strong.
* **Comfort Level** (`comfort_level`, select_one): Rate your comfort level in your wedding dress, such as comfortable or uncomfortable.

Tips: Please select the options that best fit your style and preferences. You can choose as many or as few options as you like for each field. The more information you provide, the more accurate your recommendations will be.
