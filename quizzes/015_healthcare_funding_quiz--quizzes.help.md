# Healthcare Funding Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide helps you complete the Healthcare Funding Quiz form, which is designed to understand your options and resources for healthcare funding.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select all the types of care you are looking for from the list below:
	* Primary care
	* Specialist care
	* Hospital care
2. Choose all the healthcare funding sources that apply to you:
	* Government programs
	* Private insurance
	* Self pay
	* Other
3. Select how you typically pay for care:
	* Out of pocket
	* Through insurance
	* Through a charity
	* Other
4. If you have trouble affording care, select the option that best describes your situation:
	* True
	* False
5. Enter your monthly income.
6. Choose the ways you currently contact your healthcare provider:
	* Phone
	* Email
	* In person
7. Enter any additional comments about paying for healthcare.
8. Enter your name, phone number, email, address, and zip code.
9. Enter your city and state.
10. Enter your country of residence.
11. Enter any additional comments.
