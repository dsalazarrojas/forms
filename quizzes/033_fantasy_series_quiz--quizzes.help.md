# Fantasy Series Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a quiz designed to gauge your familiarity or interest in various fantasy series. It is meant to be a quick and fun way to test your knowledge and opinions on these popular series.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question about your familiarity or interest in the fantasy series listed.
2.  For each series, select 'Yes' if you have watched or read it, or 'No' if you haven't.
3.  Fill out the form in its entirety before submitting.
