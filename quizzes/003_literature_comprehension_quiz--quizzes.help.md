# Literature Comprehension Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to test your understanding of literature. It will ask you to answer a series of questions related to reading, comprehension, and background in literature. Please answer each question with either "Yes" or "No" depending on your understanding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply read each question carefully and answer "Yes" if you think it is relevant, or "No" if you do not think it is relevant. You can select multiple answers for questions that allow multiple choices, and only one answer for questions that require only one choice.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reading Passage** (page_1, select_multiple, required: false): Please choose the passages that you have read and understood. Select as many passages as you think are relevant to your understanding of literature.
* **Reading Comprehension** (page_2, select_one, required: false): Choose the level of comprehension you have for reading. This question asks about how well you can understand a reading passage.
* **Literature Background** (page_3, select_multiple, required: false): Select the background knowledge that helps you understand literature. Think about your experience, education, or other relevant information that contributes to your understanding of literature.
