# Ancient Egypt Architecture Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information and feedback on the Ancient Egypt Architecture Quiz.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Enter your full name**: Type your full name in the "Participant name" field.
2. **Answer the first question**: Choose one of the options for "What is the primary purpose of pyramids" from the dropdown menu.
3. **Answer the second question**: Type a short answer to "Name a famous ancient Egyptian monument".
4. **Rate your confidence (optional)**: If you'd like, rate your confidence level on a scale from 1 to 5 in the "Confidence level" field.
5. **Provide feedback (optional)**: You can add any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Quiz Instructions --**: This is a note field that provides instructions for the quiz.
* **Participant name**: Enter your full name here.
* **What is the primary purpose of pyramids**: Choose one of the options from the dropdown menu:
	+ Tomb
	+ Fortress
	+ Temple
* **Name a famous ancient Egyptian monument**: Type a short answer here. For example, the Great Pyramid of Giza or the Sphinx.
* **Confidence level**: Rate your confidence level on a scale from 1 to 5. 1 means you're not sure, and 5 means you're very confident.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback here. This is optional, but helps us improve the quiz.
