# Symbolic Sequence Analysis Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data for symbolic sequence analysis, a research methodology used to study and understand complex symbolic sequences. The primary goal of this form is to gather information from participants about their experiences with a specific symbolic sequence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the introductory page and ensure you understand the purpose and instructions of the symbolic sequence analysis.
2. Fill in your participant information (name, email, and phone number, if provided) on the participant info page.
3. Enter the sequence length, start date, and end date on the sequence length page.
4. Select the sequence questions that apply to your experience on the sequence questions page.
5. Choose your answers to the sequence questions on the sequence answers page.
6. Review and submit your answers on the submission page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, text, required): Fill in your name and email. If you have a phone number, you can provide it as well.
* **Sequence Length** (`sequence_length`, number, required): Enter the number of steps in the symbolic sequence you have analyzed or are analyzing.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you started your analysis of the symbolic sequence.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you completed your analysis of the symbolic sequence.
* **Sequence Questions** (`sequence_questions`, select_multiple, required): Select all the questions that apply to your experience with the symbolic sequence.
* **Sequence Answers** (`sequence_answers`, select_one, required): Select the answer that best describes your experience with the symbolic sequence.
* **Submission** (`submission`, note, required): Review and submit your answers to complete the form.

## Tips
- Ensure that you answer all required fields to ensure accurate data collection.
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
