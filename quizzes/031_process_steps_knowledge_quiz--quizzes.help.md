# process_steps_knowledge_quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help you assess your knowledge about process steps in various scenarios. It will ask you a series of questions to test your understanding of process steps and their relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

* Answer the questions on each page by selecting the options that apply to your role in the process.
* For the "process_steps" field, type in the correct process steps in the text box.
* For the "correct_answers" and "incorrect_answers" fields, select the correct options from the dropdown menus.
* Make sure to complete all the required fields (indicated by *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **what_is_your_role** (`what_is_your_role`, select_one, optional): This field asks you to select whether or not you have a specific role in the process.
* **process_step** (`role`, number, optional): This field asks you to enter the number of the process step you are currently on.
* **process_steps** (`process_steps`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter the process steps in the text box.
* **which_step_came_next** (`correct_answers`, select_one, optional): This field asks you to select whether or not the next step in the process came next.
* **correct_answer** (`correct_answer_ids`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter the correct answer(s) in the text box.
* **which_step_came_previous** (`incorrect_answers`, select_one, optional): This field asks you to select whether or not the previous step in the process came previous.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer the questions based on your actual experience or knowledge about the process.
* If you are unsure about a process step, you can always ask for help from a process expert.
* Take your time to ensure you answer the questions accurately.
