# Structural Fitter Skills Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Structural Fitter Skills Quiz is a comprehensive form designed to evaluate the skills and experience of structural fitters in various aspects of their profession. This form helps to assess their knowledge, blueprint reading, safety awareness, and other relevant skills.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Structural Fitter Knowledge section (Field 1) to answer questions related to structural fitter's theoretical knowledge.
2. Select the correct options for Blueprint Reading (Field 2) and describe your skills and experience in this area.
3. Check the appropriate boxes for Safety Awareness (Field 3) to indicate your level of awareness in this area.
4. Select the correct teams for Hiring Teams (Field 4) that you have experience in.
5. Select the relevant skills for Training Coordinators (Field 5) that you have experience in.
6. Select the relevant skills for Construction Supervisors (Field 6) that you have experience in.
7. Enter your Years of Experience (Field 7) to indicate the number of years you have been working as a structural fitter.
8. Describe your Blueprint Reading Skills (Field 8) in the text box provided.
9. Mention the Safety Equipment (Field 9) you are familiar with.
10. Describe the Safety Procedure (Field 10) you follow.
11. Provide the Date (Field 11) you are filling this form.
12. Provide the Time (Field 12) you are filling this form.
13. Enter your Email (Field 13) contact information.
14. Enter your Phone Number (Field 14) contact information.
15-24. Fill out the Note fields (Fields 15-24) with any additional information you want to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Structural Fitter Knowledge** (Field 1): This field is used to answer questions related to structural fitter's theoretical knowledge.
* **Blueprint Reading** (Field 2): This field is used to describe your skills and experience in blueprint reading.
* **Safety Awareness** (Field 3): This field is used to check your level of awareness in safety areas.
* **Hiring Teams** (Field 4): This field is used to select the teams you have experience in, such as Construction Supervisor or Training Coordinator.
* **Training Coordinators** (Field 5): This field is used to select the relevant skills you have experience in as a training coordinator.
* **Construction Supervisors** (Field 6): This field is used to select the relevant skills you have experience in as a construction supervisor.
* **Years of Experience** (Field 7): This field is used to enter the number of years you have been working as a structural fitter.
* **Blueprint Reading Skills** (Field 8): This field is used to describe your skills and experience in blueprint reading.
* **Safety Equipment** (Field 9): This field is used to mention the safety equipment you are familiar with.
* **Safety Procedure** (Field 10): This field is used to describe the safety procedure you follow.
* **Date** (Field 11): This field is used to provide the date you are filling this form.
* **Time** (Field 12): This field is used to provide the time you are filling this form.
* **Email** (Field 13): This field is used to enter your email contact information.
* **Phone** (Field 14): This field is used to enter your phone number contact information.
* **Note 1-11** (Fields 15-25): These fields are used to fill out any additional information you want to provide.
