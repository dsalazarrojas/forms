# AI Reading Comprehension Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess users' ability to read and analyze written texts, specifically their ability to identify main ideas, supporting details, and author tone. It helps users develop their reading comprehension skills and provide feedback for the quiz creator.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the provided passage carefully and understand the content.
2. Choose the best answer to the three questions: Main Idea, Supporting Detail, and Author Tone.
3. Provide a short explanation of your choice for the Main Idea question.
4. Estimate the time you spent on the quiz (optional).
5. Provide any additional feedback about the quiz.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **-- Quiz Instructions --** (`--quiz_instructions--`, note, required: false): This page is a brief introduction to the quiz. Read it carefully before proceeding.
- **Student name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): This is an optional field where you can provide your name as a student.
- **Question 1 - Main idea** (`question_1`, select_one, required: true): Choose the best answer to the question "What is the main idea of the passage?"
  - The passage explains a problem and solutions
  - The passage is a personal narrative
  - The passage is a technical manual
  - The passage is an advertisement
- **Question 2 - Supporting detail** (`question_2`, select_one, required: true): Choose the best answer to the question "What is a supporting detail in the passage?"
  - A specific example
  - A statistical claim
  - An unrelated anecdote
  - A fictional event
- **Question 3 - Author tone** (`question_3`, select_one, required: true): Choose the best description of the author's tone in the passage.
  - Neutral and factual
  - Humorous and ironic
  - Angry and accusatory
  - Sarcastic and biting
- **Short answer explanation** (`short_answer`, text, required: true): Provide a brief explanation of your choice for Question 1.
- **Time taken in minutes** (`time_taken_minutes`, number, required: false): If you want to estimate the time you spent on the quiz.
- **Additional feedback about the quiz** (`additional_feedback`, text, required: false): This is an optional field where you can provide any additional feedback about the quiz.
