# Sofas Recommendation Quiz Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sofas Recommendation Quiz Template is a form designed to help our sales team understand the customer's preferences and needs, allowing us to provide personalized recommendations for the right sofa. By answering this quiz, the customer will be able to choose from a variety of options to match their desired size, material, color, style, pattern, comfort level, fabric type, and additional preferences, helping us to narrow down the most suitable sofa recommendations for them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that best match your preferences for each field. Please make sure to answer all required fields to ensure we get a clear understanding of your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the customer's preferred size for the sofa?** (Required)
  * Select the size that best matches your desired space and comfort level.
  * Options: Small, Medium, Large, Extra Large

* **What material does the customer prefer for the sofa?** (Required)
  * Choose a material that aligns with your lifestyle and preferences.
  * Options: Leather, Fabric, Faux Leather, Velvet

* **What color does the customer prefer for the sofa?** (Required)
  * Select a color that resonates with your personal taste.
  * Options: Red, Blue, Green, Yellow

* **What style does the customer prefer for the sofa?** (Required)
  * Choose a style that fits with your desired aesthetic.
  * Options: Modern, Classic, Rustic, Contemporary

* **What pattern does the customer prefer for the sofa?** (Required)
  * Select a pattern that suits your taste in home decor.
  * Options: Small Pattern, Large Pattern, No Pattern, Random Pattern

* **What comfort level is the customer expecting?** (Required)
  * Choose the comfort level that aligns with your priorities.
  * Options: High, Medium, Low

* **What type of fabric does the customer prefer?** (Required)
  * Select up to three fabric types that you enjoy.
  * Options: Cotton, Polyester, Linen, Silk

* **What other type of fabric does the customer prefer?** (Required)
  * Select up to three additional fabric types.
  * Options: Microfiber, Faux Fur, Velvet, Leather

* **What is another type of fabric the customer prefers?** (Required)
  * Select up to three more fabric types.
  * Options: Denim, Chenille, Rayon, Lace

## Tips
- Try to be as specific as possible when answering each field to ensure we get the most accurate picture of the customer's preferences.
