# Accounting Information Systems Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This quiz is designed to assess your knowledge and understanding of Accounting Information Systems (AIS). It will help you evaluate your understanding of various aspects of AIS, including its purpose, components, and best practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this quiz, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as the student taking the quiz in the "Student Name" field.
2. Provide your unique student ID in the "Student ID" field.
3. Enter the name of the course this quiz is for in the "Course Name" field.
4. Enter the date when the quiz is taken in the "Quiz Date" field.
5. Select the correct answers to each of the multiple-choice questions (questions 1-8).
6. For question 9, select the correct example of source data automation.
7. For question 10, provide a concise explanation of the difference between batch processing and real-time processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the student taking the quiz.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, required): Provide your unique student ID.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the course this quiz is for.
* **Quiz Date** (`quiz_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the quiz is taken.
* **Question 1** (What is the primary purpose of an Accounting Information System (AIS), select_one, required): Select the best answer from the options: "To store financial data securely", "To collect, store, and process financial and accounting data", "To generate reports for management", or "To comply with regulatory requirements".
* **Question 2** (Which of the following is NOT a component of an AIS, select_one, required): Select the incorrect option from the choices: "People", "Procedures", "Data", "Marketing strategies".
* **Question 3** (What does ERP stand for in the context of accounting systems, select_one, required): Select the correct acronym from the options: "Enterprise Resource Planning", "Electronic Record Processing", "Efficient Reporting Platform", or "Enterprise Risk Prevention".
* **Question 4** (Which internal control principle involves dividing responsibility for a transaction between two or more people, select_one, required): Select the correct principle from the options: "Documentation procedures", "Establishment of responsibility", "Segregation of duties", or "Independent internal verification".
* **Question 5** (What is the main advantage of cloud-based accounting systems, select_one, required): Select the best answer from the options: "Lower initial cost", "Accessibility from anywhere", "Automatic updates", or "All of the above".
* **Question 6** (Which of the following is a common risk in accounting information systems, select_one, required): Select the correct risk from the options: "Data breaches", "System failures", "Unauthorized access", or "All of the above".
* **Question 7** (What is the purpose of audit trails in an AIS, select_one, required): Select the best answer from the options: "To improve system performance", "To track who accessed the system and what changes were made", "To reduce storage costs", or "To automate financial reporting".
* **Question 8** (True or False - Real-time processing means transactions are processed immediately as they occur, select_one, required): Select "True" or "False".
* **Question 9** (Which of the following is an example of source data automation, select_one, required): Select the best example from the options: "Bar code scanners", "Manual data entry", "Paper invoices", or "Spreadsheet calculations".
* **Question 10** (Briefly explain the difference between batch processing and real-time processing, text, optional): Provide a concise explanation (less than 250 words).
