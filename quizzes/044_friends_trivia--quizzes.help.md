# Friends Trivia - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Friends Trivia form is a survey designed to gather information about your favorite TV show "Friends". Your participation will help us understand more about your interests and preferences related to the show.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each page of the form and select the corresponding answers to the questions.
2. Fill out each page completely, even if you're not sure about an answer.
3. When prompted to select multiple options, choose the ones that apply to you.
4. Click submit once you've answered all questions on each page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What's your favorite Friends character?** (`friend_1`, text, optional): This question allows you to choose your favorite character from the show.
* **Friends' favorite food?** (`favorite_friend_food`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the foods that you enjoy eating at Central Perk or think are popular with the gang.
* **What's the name of your favorite TV show?** (`favorite_tv_show`, text, optional): This is your chance to share your favorite show, even if it's not "Friends".
* **Friends' favorite quote?** (`favorite_friend_quote`, text, optional): Enter a quote that you find memorable or funny from the show.
* **How many Friends have you seen?** (`friend_5`, number, optional): Estimate the number of times you've watched or seen the show.
* **What do you do when someone is funny?** (`reaction_to_funny`, select_one, optional): Choose one way you react when someone is trying to be funny.
	+ **Laugh**: You can't help but laugh at their humor.
	+ **Smile**: You find their humor amusing and smile.
	+ **Nod**: You acknowledge their attempt at humor without fully engaging.
	+ **Ignore**: You're not amused and move on.
* **Favorite Friends season?** (`friend_7`, number, optional): Choose your favorite season of the show.
* **Favorite Friends episode?** (`friend_8`, number, optional): Select the episode that you think is the most memorable or enjoyable.
* **Favorite quote from Friends 2?** (`friend_9`, text, optional): Share another quote from the show that resonates with you.

## Tips
* Be honest and have fun with your answers.
* You can always skip questions that don't apply to you.
* If you're stuck, just submit what you know and enjoy the rest of the quiz!
