# Combat Techniques Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Combat Techniques Knowledge Quiz is a form designed to assess your knowledge of combat techniques. This quiz is intended to help you evaluate your understanding of various combat disciplines, including boxing, Muay Thai, and Jiu Jitsu. It's a tool for martial arts enthusiasts, athletes, and practitioners to gauge their knowledge and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Combat Techniques Knowledge Quiz, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Question 1 - Primary striking targets: Select all that apply (Head, Torso, Legs, Arms)
	* Question 2 - Basic defensive technique: Choose the correct technique (Blocking, Grappling, Kicking)
	* Question 3 - Safe training practice: Select the best practice (Warm up, Skip rest, Overload training)
2. Provide optional information:
	* Participant name (Optional)
	* Training hours per week (numeric hours)
	* Martial art styles trained (Select all that apply)
	* Safety equipment used (List protective gear)
3. Estimate your score and provide feedback:
	* Estimated score (self-assessed result)
	* Feedback on quiz (Suggestions or corrections)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name** (participant_name, text, Optional): Enter your name, if you want to be identified as a participant.
* **Question 1 - Primary striking targets** (question_1, select_multiple, required): Select all the primary striking targets from the options provided (Head, Torso, Legs, Arms). This question assesses your understanding of basic striking techniques.
* **Question 2 - Basic defensive technique** (question_2, select_one, required): Choose the correct basic defensive technique from the options provided (Blocking, Grappling, Kicking). This question evaluates your knowledge of defensive strategies.
* **Question 3 - Safe training practice** (question_3, select_one, required): Select the best safe training practice from the options provided (Warm up, Skip rest, Overload training). This question tests your understanding of training safety.
* **Training hours per week** (training_hours_per_week, number, Optional): If you want to provide additional information on your training schedule, enter the numeric hours you train per week.
* **Martial art styles trained** (martial_art_styles, select_multiple, Optional): Select all the martial art styles you have trained in from the options provided (Boxing, Muay Thai, Wrestling, Jiu Jitsu).
* **Safety equipment used** (safety_equipment_used, text, Optional): List any protective gear you use during training.
* **Estimated score** (score_estimate, number, Optional): Self-assess your score based on the questions.
* **Feedback on quiz** (feedback, text, Optional): Provide any suggestions or corrections you have on the quiz.

Note: The form is designed to be completed by martial arts practitioners to assess their knowledge and understanding of combat techniques. If you have any feedback or suggestions, please provide them in the "Feedback on quiz" field.
