# 3D Printer Product Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This quiz is designed to gather feedback from users about their experiences with 3D printing products. Your input will help us better understand your needs and preferences, which will inform our product development and improvement efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name, email address, and phone number (optional).
2. Choose your level of experience with 3D printing:
	* Beginner: You have just started exploring 3D printing.
	* Hobbyist: You have some experience with 3D printing, but it's not a significant part of your work.
	* Enthusiast: You regularly use 3D printing for various projects.
	* Professional: 3D printing is a significant part of your work.
	* Industrial: You use 3D printing for industrial applications.
3. Select all your primary use cases for 3D printing:
	* Prototyping
	* End-use parts
	* Education
	* Hobby projects
	* Art & design
	* Research & development
4. Choose how often you operate a 3D printer:
	* Daily
	* Several times a week
	* Weekly
	* Monthly
	* Rarely
5. Enter your typical maximum build volume (liters) if you have a 3D printer, or leave this field blank if you don't.
6. Select all features that influence your purchasing decision:
	* Print quality
	* Speed
	* Reliability
	* Price
	* Ease of use
	* Manufacturer support
	* Material compatibility
7. Choose your current material requirements:
	* PLA only
	* PLA and PETG
	* PLA/PETG/ABS
	* Composite and flexible materials
	* Engineering/industrial materials (Nylon, PEEK)
8. Rate your overall satisfaction with the product selection guidance:
	* Very satisfied
	* Satisfied
	* Neutral
	* Dissatisfied
	* Very dissatisfied
9. Rate the print quality of the 3D printer:
	* 1-10
10. Rate how likely you are to recommend this printer to others:
	* 0-10
11. Compare this 3D printer to alternatives:
	* Much better
	* Somewhat better
	* About the same
	* Somewhat worse
	* Much worse
12. Rate how you perceive the pricing of the 3D printer:
	* Great value
	* Fair price
	* Slightly expensive
	* Too expensive
	* Premium but justified
13. If you could choose one improvement to increase your satisfaction, please provide a specific feature or aspect you'd like to see changed or added:
14. Finally, include any additional comments or notes about your experience with 3D printing or this specific product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter the email address we'll use to send you the results of this quiz and follow-up questions.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, phone, optional): If you'd like to provide your phone number for further contact.
* **Experience level with 3D printing** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Select the option that best describes your level of experience with 3D printing.
* **Primary use cases** (`primary_use_cases`, select_multiple, required): Select all the use cases that apply to your typical work with 3D printing.
* **Frequency of use** (`frequency_of_use`, select_one, required): Choose the frequency with which you operate a 3D printer.
* **Typical build volume** (`typical_build_volume_liters`, decimal, optional): Enter your approximate build volume in liters.
* **Features influencing purchasing decision** (`importance_of_features`, select_multiple, required): Select all the features that affect your purchasing decision.
* **Current material requirements** (`material_compatibility`, select_one, optional): Choose the materials you currently use or require for 3D printing.
* **Overall satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the product selection guidance.
* **Print quality rating** (`print_quality_rating`, integer, required): Rate the print quality of the 3D printer on a scale of 1-10.
* **Likelihood to recommend** (`likelihood_to_recommend`, integer, required): Rate how likely you are to recommend this 3D printer to others.
* **Comparison to alternatives** (`competitor_comparison`, select_one, required): Compare this 3D printer to alternative products.
* **Pricing perception** (`price_perception`, select_one, required): Rate how you perceive the pricing of the 3D printer.
* **Desired improvement** (`desired_improvements`, text, optional): If you'd like to see a specific improvement, please provide a feature or aspect you'd like to see changed or added.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Include any additional comments or notes about your experience with 3D printing or this specific product.
