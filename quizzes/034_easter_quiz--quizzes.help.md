# Easter Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form allows you to create a customized Easter-themed quiz for you and your audience. It's a fun way to test your knowledge and creativity about Easter and springtime.

## How To Complete This Form
To create a quiz, follow these steps:

1. Answer the "What do you love about Easter" question with a brief description of your favorite Easter traditions or activities.
2. Enter the name of the Easter Bunny, if you have one.
3. Choose up to three items you typically find in an Easter basket.
4. Select the color of the Easter egg you envision.
5. Choose the shape of the Easter egg.
6. Select the type of quiz you want to create (True or False, Multiple Choice, Short Answer, or Essay).
7. Decide how many questions you want your quiz to have.
8. Enter the date and time of Easter (optional).
9. Finally, enter a brief description of your goals for creating this quiz.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you love about Easter** (`easter_quiz`): This is a text field where you can describe your favorite Easter traditions or activities.
* **What is the Easter Bunny's name** (`easter_bunny_name`): If you have a special name for the Easter Bunny, enter it here.
* **What items do you typically find in an Easter basket** (`easter_basket_items`): Select up to three items from the list (Chocolate, Marshmallow Peeps, Small toy, Book, Stuffed animal) that are commonly found in Easter baskets.
* **What is the Easter egg color** (`easter_egg_color`): Choose the color of the Easter egg you have in mind.
* **What is the Easter egg shape** (`easter_egg_shape`): Choose the shape of the Easter egg you envision.
* **What type of quiz do you want to create** (`easter_quiz_type`): Select the type of quiz you want to create (True or False, Multiple Choice, Short Answer, or Essay).
* **How many questions will your quiz have** (`quiz_length`): Enter the number of questions you want in your quiz.
* **What date is Easter** (`easter_date`): If you want to include the date of Easter, enter it here (optional).
* **What time is Easter** (`easter_time`): If you want to include the time of Easter, enter it here (optional).
* **Why do you want to create this quiz** (`note_goals`): Enter a brief description of your goals for creating this quiz.
* **Enter email** (`easter_quiz_email`): Enter your email address (optional).
* **Enter phone** (`easter_quiz_phone`): Enter your phone number (optional).

## Tips

* Use this form to create a fun and interactive quiz for you and your audience.
* Be creative and think about the types of questions and activities you can include in your quiz.
* Make sure to enter your email address and phone number if you want to receive any updates or notifications about your quiz.
