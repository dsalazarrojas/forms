# Riddle Creation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help us create and store new riddles for our quiz system. It's designed to collect relevant information about the riddle, its solution, and additional details from the creator. Your input will help us better understand what kind of content we should create and provide to our users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2. Leave the non-required fields (marked as "optional") blank or fill them in if you have additional information.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Title** (id: 1)
Title: `<title>` (text, required)
Enter a short and descriptive title for the riddle.

### 2. **Riddle Question** (id: 2)
Riddle Question: `<riddle_question>` (text, required)
Describe the riddle in a concise and clear way.

### 3. **Riddle Answer** (id: 3)
Riddle Answer: `<riddle_answer>` (text, required)
Enter the correct solution to the riddle.

### 4. **Riddle Explanation** (id: 4)
Riddle Explanation: `<riddle_explanation>` (text, required)
Provide a brief explanation of the riddle and its solution.

### 5. **Solution** (id: 5)
Solution: Select a solution type from the list (select_multiple, optional)
Choose the type of solution for the riddle (e.g., '1', '2', or '3').

### 6. **Creator** (id: 6)
Creator: `<creator>` (text, optional)
Enter your name or alias as the creator of the riddle.

### 7. **Created By** (id: 7)
Created By: `<created_by>` (text, optional)
Enter your name or alias as the person who created the form.

### 8. **Email** (id: 8)
Email: `<email>` (email, optional)
Enter your email address if you want to be contacted about your riddle.

### 9. **Phone** (id: 9)
Phone: `<phone>` (text, optional)
Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted about your riddle.

### 10. **Additional Notes** (id: 10)
Additional Notes: <additional_notes> (note, optional)
Enter any additional comments or notes about the riddle.

### 11. **Main 2** (id: 11)
Main 2: `<main_2>` (text, optional)
Enter additional details about the riddle (if needed).

Remember, you can always ask for help or clarification about any field if you're unsure about what to fill in.
