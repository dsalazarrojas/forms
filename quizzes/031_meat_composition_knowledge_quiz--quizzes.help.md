# Meat Composition Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meat Composition Knowledge Quiz is designed to test your knowledge about meat composition, including type, nutrient composition, and other relevant details. The quiz consists of various sections, and each question is carefully crafted to assess your understanding of these topics.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2.  Make sure to complete all required fields.
3.  You can submit your answers after filling out all questions.
4.  Please note that each section has its own rules and guidelines, so please take your time to understand each question before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Participant Details**
    Participant Details is a text field where you can provide any additional information about yourself.
*   **Age**
    Age is a numeric field where you can input your age.
*   **Gender**
    Gender is a select-one field where you can choose your gender (Male or Female).
*   **Type Of Meat**
    Type Of Meat is a select-multiple field where you can choose the type of meat you are familiar with (Beef, Poultry, Fish).
*   **Quiz Part**
    Quiz Part is a select-one field where you can choose which part of the quiz to focus on (Part 1 or Part 2).
*   **Meat Nutrient**
    Meat Nutrient is a select-multiple field where you can choose the nutrient you are familiar with related to meat (Energy, Protein, Fat).
*   **Participants Nutrient Level**
    Participants Nutrient Level is a select-multiple field where you can input your level of knowledge about the nutrients in meat (High, Low).
*   **Score**
    Score is a numeric field where you can input your score for the quiz.
*   **Comments**
    Comments is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or explanations for your answers.
*   **Email**
    Email is an email field where you can input your email address.
*   **Phone**
    Phone is a text field where you can input your phone number.
*   **Date**
    Date is a date field where you can input the date.

Please note that this form is for educational purposes only, and the scores will not be used for any other purpose than to gauge your knowledge in this area. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
