# Legislature Vs Executive Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This quiz is designed to educate users about the role and powers of the legislative and executive branches in government. It is a self-assessment tool to help users test their knowledge and understanding of the subject matter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply scroll through each page and answer the questions according to your knowledge. Each page will present a question, and you can type your answer in the corresponding text field. You can skip any question that you are not sure about or do not know the answer to. When you reach the end of the quiz, you can select the "Submit" option to review your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the role of the legislative branch?** (`intro`): A brief text field where you can explain your understanding of the role of the legislative branch. Please answer in a few sentences.
* **What is the role of the executive branch?** (`executive_branch`): A brief text field where you can explain your understanding of the role of the executive branch. Please answer in a few sentences.
* **What powers does the legislative branch have?** (`legislative_role`): A brief text field where you can explain your understanding of the powers of the legislative branch. Please answer in a few sentences.
* **What powers does the executive branch have?** (`executive_role`): A brief text field where you can explain your understanding of the powers of the executive branch. Please answer in a few sentences.
* **What are the main differences between the legislative and executive branches?** (`differences`): A brief text field where you can explain your understanding of the differences between the two branches. Please answer in a few sentences.
* **What are the main functions of the legislative branch?** (`key_legislative_terms`): A brief text field where you can explain your understanding of the main functions of the legislative branch. Please answer in a few sentences.
* **What are the main functions of the executive branch?** (`key_executive_terms`): A brief text field where you can explain your understanding of the main functions of the executive branch. Please answer in a few sentences.
* **Submit Quiz** (`submit`): A dropdown menu to select how you would complete the quiz:
	+ **Complete**: You have answered all the questions correctly.
	+ **Incomplete**: You have not answered all the questions or answered some questions incorrectly.
* **Email** (`email`): This is a text field to input your email address for future communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`): This is a text field to input your phone number for future communication.

## Tips
* Take your time to read each question carefully before answering.
* If you are unsure about a question, simply move on to the next one.
* You can always come back to any question if you need more time to think about it.
* Make sure to input your correct email address and phone number so that you can receive any updates or follow-ups about the quiz.
