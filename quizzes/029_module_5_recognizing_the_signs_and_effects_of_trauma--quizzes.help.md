# Module 5 Recognizing The Signs And Effects Of Trauma - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you identify and recognize the signs and effects of trauma. It's a tool to support you in assessing your mental health and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question with the best of your ability. You can use this guide to help you understand what each question means and what you should do with the answers you provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Recognizing the Signs of Trauma
**Recognizing the Signs of Trauma** (text)
You will be asked to provide a description of your experiences related to recognizing the signs of trauma. This could include any physical, emotional, or psychological signs you've noticed in yourself or others.

### 2. Trauma's Effects on Mental Health (Number)
**Trauma's Effects on Mental Health** (number)
Please select all the ways in which you believe trauma has affected your mental health. You can choose multiple options.

### 3. Last Exposure to Trauma
**Last Exposure to Trauma** (date)
Please provide the date of your last exposure to trauma. If you are unsure or do not know the date, please provide an approximate date or a description of the event.

### 4. Trauma Severity
**Trauma Severity** (select)
Please select one of the options to describe the severity of your traumatic experience: 
- Mild
- Moderate
- Severe
- Extreme

### 5. Support System
**Support System** (text)
Describe your support system, such as friends, family, or a mental health professional.

### 6. Trauma Symptoms
**Trauma Symptoms** (checkbox)
Please select all the symptoms you have experienced due to trauma:
- Nightmares
- Flashbacks
- Dissociation
- Hypervigilance
- Emotional numbing
- Anhedonia
- Self-destructive behavior
- Suicidal thoughts
- Self-harm

### 7. Mental Health Professional
**Mental Health Professional** (select)
Please select all mental health professionals you have consulted:
- Psychologist
- Therapist
- Psychiatrist
- Social worker

### 8. Self-Help Resources
**Self-Help Resources** (text)
Describe any self-help resources you have used or currently use, such as books, online forums, or support groups.

### 9. Impact on Daily Life
**Impact on Daily Life** (select)
Please select all areas that have been affected by trauma:
- Work or school
- Relationships
- Hobbies and interests
- Social activities

### 10. Coping Mechanisms
**Coping Mechanisms** (text)
Describe your coping mechanisms, such as exercise, meditation, or creative activities.

### 11. Self-Identified Trauma
**Self-Identified Trauma** (select)
Please select the type of trauma you have experienced:
- Emotional
- Physical
- Psychological

### 12. Follow-up Question
**Follow-up Question** (text)
Please answer the follow-up question in detail, if any.

## Tips
- Be honest and detailed when answering each question.
- If you are unsure or unsure about any question, please provide the best answer you can.
- Remember, this form is here to support your mental health and well-being. Do not hesitate to seek professional help if you need it.
