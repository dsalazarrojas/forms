# Agile Methodology Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agile Methodology Knowledge Quiz is designed to assess your understanding of Agile principles and practices. The quiz covers various aspects of Agile, including its values, roles, and processes. Completing this quiz will help you evaluate your knowledge in this area and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Enter your full name**: Type your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
2. **Enter your email address**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached in case of any updates or notifications.
3. **Select the correct dates**: Enter the date you are taking this quiz.
4. **Set the timer**: You have 45 minutes to complete the quiz.
5. **Answer the questions**: For each question, select the correct option from the available choices.
6. **Submit the quiz**: Once you have answered all the questions, click the submit button to get your quiz score.
