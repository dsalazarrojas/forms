# Rain Coat Recommendation Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This help guide will explain each field in the rain coat recommendation form to assist users in completing it accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly to get accurate recommendations.
2. For multiple-choice fields, select all that apply.
3. Enter numbers in the required fields (temperature, wind tolerance, and budget).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What kind of activities do you usually do in the rain?**: This field is asking about your common activities or hobbies you do during rainy days. Please type a few words to describe your usual activities.
* **How much time do you spend outdoors during rainy activities?**: This field asks about your time spent in activities outside during rainy days. You can enter a numerical value to indicate the amount of time.
* **What's your average body temperature?**: This field requires you to estimate your average body temperature. Please be honest as it will help in recommending a rain coat that suits you.
* **What's your comfort level with heavy rain?**: This field is about your preference for heavy rain. Please type in your comfort level.
* **What's your tolerance for wind?**: This field asks about your tolerance for wind when it's rainy. Please enter a numerical value.
* **What's your preference for water resistance?**: This field is asking about your preference for water resistance in a rain coat. Please type in your preference.
* **How important is weight to you?**: This field asks about your importance level for weight when choosing a rain coat. You can enter a numerical value.
* **What style of raincoat are you looking for?** (Select one): This field asks you to select a style of raincoat that you prefer. You can choose from the available options.
* **What color is your favorite?** (Select multiple): This field asks you to choose one or more color(s) that you like. You can select multiple options.
* **What size are you?** (Select multiple): This field asks you to choose one or more sizes that fit you. You can select multiple options.
* **How much are you willing to spend?**: This field requires you to enter a numerical value that represents your budget for a rain coat.

## Tips
* Please answer all required fields to get accurate recommendations.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for help.
* Remember, the more accurate your answers, the better the recommendations will be.
