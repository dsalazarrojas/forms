# Contact Lens Recommendation Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you find the perfect contact lenses for your needs by gathering information about your personal preferences and characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions honestly and carefully, as the information provided will be used to make personalized recommendations.
2. Choose the most accurate options for each question.
3. Enter any additional notes or concerns you may have in the "Additional Notes" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)** (`text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address (2)** (`email`, required): Enter a valid email address for results delivery and follow-up.
* **Age (3)** (`number`, required): Enter your age in years.
* **How Do You Currently Correct Your Vision (4)** (`select_one`): Choose how you currently correct your vision:
	+ Glasses Only
	+ Contact Lenses
	+ Both Glasses and Contacts
	+ No Correction Currently
* **Contact Lens Experience (5)** (`select_one`): Select your level of experience with contact lenses:
	+ Never Worn
	+ Beginner - Less Than 1 Year
	+ Intermediate - 1 to 5 Years
	+ Experienced - Over 5 Years
* **How Often Do You Want to Wear Lenses (6)** (`select_one`): Choose how often you plan to wear lenses:
	+ Every Day
	+ Most Days
	+ Weekends Only
	+ Special Occasions Only
* **How Many Hours Per Day Do You Plan to Wear Lenses (7)** (`select_one`): Select your intended wear time:
	+ Less than 8 hours
	+ 8 to 12 hours
	+ 12 to 16 hours
	+ Extended Wear - Overnight
* **What Vision Correction Do You Need (8)** (`select_one`): Choose your prescription type:
	+ Nearsighted Only
	+ Farsighted Only
	+ Astigmatism
	+ Presbyopia - Reading Vision
	+ Combination
	+ Not Sure
* **Do You Have Sensitive Eyes (9)** (`select_one`): Select your sensitivity level:
	+ Yes - Very Sensitive
	+ Yes - Somewhat Sensitive
	+ No - Normal Sensitivity
	+ Not Sure
* **Do You Experience Dry Eyes (10)** (`select_one`): Select your dry eye frequency:
	+ Frequently
	+ Occasionally
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **Do You Have Seasonal Allergies (11)** (`select_one`): Select your allergy status:
	+ Yes - Severe
	+ Yes - Moderate
	+ Yes - Mild
	+ False
* **Maintenance Preference (12)** (`select_one`): Choose your maintenance preference:
	+ Minimal - Daily Disposables
	+ Low - Bi-weekly
	+ Moderate - Monthly
	+ Traditional - Yearly
* **Monthly Budget for Lenses (13)** (`select_one`): Select your budget range:
	+ Under $30
	+ $30 to $50
	+ $51 to $75
	+ $76 to $100
	+ Over $100
* **Interested in Colored Lenses (14)** (`select_one`): Select your interest in colored lenses:
	+ Yes - Want Color Change
	+ Maybe - Open to Options
	+ No - Clear Only
* **Do You Play Sports or Exercise Regularly (15)** (`select_one`): Select your activity level:
	+ Yes - Very Active
	+ Yes - Moderately Active
	+ No - Sedentary Lifestyle
* **Daily Computer Screen Time (16)** (`select_one`): Select your daily screen time:
	+ Less than 2 hours
	+ 2 to 4 hours
	+ 4 to 8 hours
	+ Over 8 hours
* **Top Priority (17)** (`select_one`): Select your main concern:
	+ Comfort
	+ Convenience
	+ Cost
	+ Vision Quality
	+ Eye Health
* **Would You Like a Professional Consultation (18)** (`select_one`): Select your follow-up interest:
	+ Yes - Schedule Appointment
	+ Yes - But Not Yet
	+ No - Just Browsing
* **Additional Notes (19)** (`text`, optional): Enter any specific requirements or concerns you may have.

# Additional Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any existing eye conditions or concerns, please consult an eye care professional before using this form.
* The recommended contact lenses will be based on your preferences and characteristics.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
