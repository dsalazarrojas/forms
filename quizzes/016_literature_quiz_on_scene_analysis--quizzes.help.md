# Literature Quiz On Scene Analysis - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect scene analysis information from literature students or enthusiasts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Read the provided instructions and guidelines.
2. Select the scene description that best describes the scene you would like to analyze.
3. Enter the character name(s) involved in the scene.
4. Choose the scene number or location from the available options.
5. Select the era or time period in which the scene takes place.
6. Choose the actor(s) involved in the scene from the available options.
7. Enter the date of the scene.
8. Enter the time of day for the scene.
9. Enter any additional notes about the scene.
10. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **The Scene** (`scene_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the scene you would like to analyze.
* **Character Name** (`character_name`, text, optional): Enter the name(s) of the character(s) involved in the scene.
* **Scene Number** (`scene_number`, number, optional): Enter the scene number from the available options.
* **Scene Location** (`scene_location`, select_one, optional): Choose the location of the scene from the available options.
* **Scene Era** (`scene_era`, select_one, optional): Select the era or time period in which the scene takes place.
* **Scene Actors** (`scene_actors`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the actor(s) involved in the scene from the available options.
* **Date of Scene** (`date_of_scene`, date, optional): Enter the date of the scene.
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, time, optional): Enter the time of day for the scene.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the scene.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
