## Cuban Missile Crisis Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cuban Missile Crisis Quiz is a form designed to assess knowledge about the Cuban Missile Crisis. It asks questions about the key events and players involved in this critical moment in history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, users should:

1. Read through each field carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
2. Be aware that some questions are optional, while others are required.
3. Make sure to choose the correct options for each question, based on their knowledge about the Cuban Missile Crisis.

	+ Other
