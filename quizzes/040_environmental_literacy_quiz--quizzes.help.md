# Environmental Literacy Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Environmental Literacy Quiz aims to gauge your level of understanding about environmental topics, such as sustainability practices, ecological concepts, climate change, conservation efforts, eco-friendly practices, and green space.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page of the quiz, and answer all the questions truthfully based on your knowledge.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select one or multiple options that best match your answer.
3. Ensure you've answered every question, as each one is crucial for an accurate assessment.
4. Once you've completed the form, submit your responses to see your results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sustainability Practices** (`sustainability_practices`, select multiple, required: false): Select all the practices that you're familiar with or regularly implement in your daily life, such as recycling, reducing plastic use, or conserving water.
* **Ecological Concepts** (`ecological_concepts`, select one, required: false): Choose the concept that best describes your understanding of the natural world, such as 'Yes' for a basic understanding or 'No' for a lack of understanding.
* **Environmental Issues** (`environmental_issues`, select multiple, required: false): Select all the issues that you're aware of, such as deforestation, pollution, or climate change.
* **Climate Change** (`climate_change`, select one, required: false): Choose whether you're familiar with or concerned about climate change, selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Conservation Efforts** (`conservation_efforts`, select multiple, required: false): Select all the efforts you're involved in or support, such as reducing, reusing, or recycling.
* **Eco-Friendly Practices** (`eco_friendly_practices`, select multiple, required: false): Choose all the eco-friendly practices you regularly implement, such as using public transport, reducing energy consumption, or avoiding single-use plastics.
* **Green Space** (`green_space`, select one, required: false): Select whether you have a green space or area in your daily life, such as a park or nature reserve.
