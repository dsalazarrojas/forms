# City Representations Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The City Representations Quiz is a fun and educational quiz designed to test your knowledge about cities and landmarks from around the world. This quiz will ask you a series of multiple-choice questions about the location of famous cities and landmarks.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by providing your name and email address** in the "Your Name" and "Your Email" fields, respectively. This information is required to submit your quiz score.
2. **Answer each of the multiple-choice questions**, selecting the correct city or location from the options provided.
3. **Select the difficulty level of the quiz** by choosing "Very Easy", "Easy", "Moderate", "Difficult", or "Expert" in the "How would you rate the difficulty of this quiz?" field. This is optional.
4. **Indicate whether you want to receive your quiz score via email** by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Send me my score via email" field. This is required.
5. **Complete the quiz by answering all questions** and clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to appear in the quiz results.
* **Your Email**: Enter your email address where you would like your quiz score to be sent.
* **In which city is the Eiffel Tower located?**: Select the correct city from the options "London", "Paris", "Berlin", or "Rome".
* **Which city hosts the Statue of Liberty?**: Select the correct city from the options "New York", "Los Angeles", "Chicago", or "Miami".
* **Tokyo is the capital of which country?**: Select the correct country from the options "China", "South Korea", "Japan", or "Thailand".
* **Where can you find the ancient Colosseum?**: Select the correct city from the options "Athens", "Rome", "Cairo", or "Istanbul".
* **The Opera House is a famous landmark in which Australian city?**: Select the correct city from the options "Melbourne", "Brisbane", "Sydney", or "Perth".
* **The Christ the Redeemer statue overlooks which city?**: Select the correct city from the options "Sao Paulo", "Rio de Janeiro", "Buenos Aires", or "Lima".
* **Which river flows through London?**: Select the correct river from the options "Seine", "Danube", "Thames", or "Rhine".
* **How would you rate the difficulty of this quiz?**: Select the difficulty level from the options "Very Easy", "Easy", "Moderate", "Difficult", or "Expert".
* **Do you have any suggestions for future quiz topics?**: Enter any comments or suggestions you may have for future quiz topics.
* **Send me my score via email**: Select "True" to receive your quiz score via email, or "False" to keep your score private.
* **Date Completed**: The date and time you completed the quiz will be recorded automatically.

Note: All fields with an asterisk (\*) are required.
