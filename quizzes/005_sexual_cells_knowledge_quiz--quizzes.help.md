	+ Meiosis: A type of cell division that occurs in reproductive cells and results in four non-identical daughter cells.
	+ Somatic: Not applicable to cells, but rather a term referring to non-reproductive cells.

	+ None: No cell division has occurred.

* **cell_structure**: What is the primary structure of cells?
	+ Plasma: The fluid that makes up the cell's interior.
	+ Cytoplasm: The jelly-like substance that fills the cell.
	+ Nucleus: The control center of the cell.
	+ None: The cell has no structure.
* **cell_transport**: How do cells transport materials?
	+ Diffusion: The process of movement through a medium without the need for energy.
	+ Osmosis: The movement of water through a membrane.

	+ Exocytosis: The expulsion of substances from the cell.
	+ None: No transport has occurred.
