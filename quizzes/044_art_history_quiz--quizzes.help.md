# Art History Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a quiz designed to test your knowledge of art history, covering various styles and periods from the 19th and 20th centuries. The form is divided into three sections, each dedicated to a specific period or style.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the required fields for your name and date.
2. Choose the correct answers to each question, making sure to select only one option per question.
3. Write a brief essay (100-200 words) on the importance of the Renaissance period in the development of Western art.
4. Finally, pledge to have completed the quiz independently without external aid.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name to identify yourself for the quiz results.
* **Date** (`quiz_date`, date, required): Select the date you completed the quiz to track your progress.
* **Who painted the Mona Lisa?** (`mona_lisa_artist`, select_one, required): Choose the correct artist who painted the famous portrait, which is a key work of the High Renaissance.
* **In which city is the Parthenon located?** (`parthenon_location`, select_one, required): Select the correct city where the ancient Greek temple of the Parthenon is found, a symbol of ancient Greek culture.
* **Which artist painted the ceiling of the Sistine Chapel?** (`sistine_chapel_ceiling_artist`, select_one, required): Choose the correct artist who created the famous frescoes on the Sistine Chapel's ceiling, a masterpiece of the High Renaissance.
* **Which artist is credited with naming the Impressionist movement with his painting - Impression, Sunrise?** (`impression_sunrise_artist`, select_one, required): Select the correct artist who started the Impressionist movement with his iconic painting.
* **Vincent van Gogh is a primary figure in which artistic movement?** (`van_gogh_style`, select_one, required): Choose the correct movement that van Gogh was associated with, known for his bold and expressive style.
* **In which museum is van Gogh's - The Starry Night - currently housed?** (`starry_night_location`, select_one, required): Select the correct museum where the famous painting is displayed.
* **Andy Warhol was a leading figure in which visual art movement?** (`andy_warhol_movement`, select_one, required): Choose the correct movement that Warhol was associated with, known for his pop art and consumerist themes.
* **What was the nationality of the artist Frida Kahlo?** (`frida_kahlo_nationality`, select_one, required): Select the correct nationality of the famous Mexican artist.
* **Pablo Picasso is famous for co-founding which movement?** (`picasso_cubism_pioneer`, select_one, required): Choose the correct movement that Picasso co-founded, known for its revolutionary approach to art.
* **Briefly explain the importance of the Renaissance period in the development of Western art** (`short_essay_question`, text, required): Write a short essay (100-200 words) on the significance of the Renaissance period, which marked a significant shift in art and culture.
* **I certify that I have completed this quiz independently without external aid** (`honor_pledge`, select_one, required): Pledge your honesty and confirm that you completed the quiz without any external help.
