# Perimenopause Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to assist healthcare providers in identifying potential perimenopause symptoms and stages in their female patients. The form is intended for patients who are experiencing symptoms that may be related to perimenopause, a transitional period before menopause. The questions in this form will help healthcare providers assess the severity of symptoms and stages of perimenopause, and provide a basis for further evaluation and potential treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the appropriate option for each question based on your current symptoms or experiences.
2. Answer all questions thoroughly to ensure accurate assessment.
3. If you are unsure of any question, please select the "Don't Know" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Perimenopause Quiz Form** (`perimenopause_quiz_form`): This is the form title.
* **Age** (`age`): Enter your age to help healthcare providers assess your perimenopause symptoms and stages.
* **Menopause Year** (`menopause_year`): Select 'Yes' if you are experiencing perimenopause or menopause symptoms, or 'No' if you are not.
* **Birthdate** (`birthdate`): Enter your birthdate to help healthcare providers assess your perimenopause symptoms and stages.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Select all that apply if you have any medical conditions or allergies that may be related to perimenopause.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`): Select all that apply if you are experiencing any perimenopause symptoms.
* **Perimenopause Stage** (`perimenopause_stage`): Select the stage you are experiencing perimenopause:
	+ 'Yes' indicates you are experiencing perimenopause symptoms.
	+ 'No' indicates you are not experiencing perimenopause symptoms.
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (`healthcare_provider_name`): Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare Provider Phone** (`healthcare_provider_phone`): Enter the phone number of your healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare Provider Email** (`healthcare_provider_email`): Enter the email of your healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare Provider Relationship** (`healthcare_provider_relationship`): Select 'Yes' if you have a healthcare provider for perimenopause-related issues, or 'No' if you do not.
* **Other Providers** (`other_providers`): Select 'Yes' if you are seeing other healthcare providers for perimenopause-related issues, or 'No' if you are not.
* **Perimenopause Quiz Completed** (`perimenopause_quiz_completed`): Enter your comments about the quiz completion.
* **Follow Up Plan** (`follow_up_plan`): Select 'Yes' if you have a follow-up plan with your healthcare provider, or 'No' if you do not.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions thoroughly and honestly to ensure accurate assessment.
* If you are unsure of any question, please select the "Don't Know" option.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please discuss with your healthcare provider.
* This form is for informational purposes only, and should not be used as a diagnostic tool without consulting a healthcare provider.
