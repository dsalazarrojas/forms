# MCQ - Help Guide
## Purpose
The MCQ form is designed to gather user preferences and demographic information for research or feedback purposes. Please fill out the form carefully to provide us with your input.

## How to Complete This Form
1. **Page 1: Question Text** (1): This is the first question in the form. Please type your response to the question "What is your favorite color?"
2. **Answer Choices** (2): Select multiple options that apply to you. You can choose from "Yes" or "No" for each option.
3. **Answer** (3): Choose the answer that best fits your response to the previous question.
4. **Submit** (4): Click this button to submit your responses. Please ensure you fill out all required fields.
5. **Back** (5): If you need to go back to the previous page, click this button.
6. **Note** (6): This field is optional and allows you to leave any additional comments or information.
7. **Email** (7): If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up or further information, please provide your email address here. This field is optional.
8. **Phone Number** (8): If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number here. This field is optional.
