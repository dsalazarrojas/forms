# Coach Onboarding Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This quiz is part of the onboarding process for coaches to ensure they have the necessary knowledge and understanding of coaching principles, techniques, and policies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Select the best answer to each question or scenario.
3. Rate your confidence level with regard to company policies.
4. Optionally, select areas where you need more training.
5. Add any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coach Full Name** (`coach_name`, text, *): Enter your full name as per your legal documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, *): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, *): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, *): When did you start or will start coaching?
* **Coaching Specialty** (`coaching_specialty`, select_one, *): Select your primary coaching focus, e.g., Health Coaching, Wellness Coaching, etc.
* **Years of Coaching Experience** (`years_experience`, select_one, *): How long have you been coaching?
* **What Is the Primary Goal of Coaching** (`question_1`, select_one, *): This question aims to determine your understanding of a coach's primary goal.
* **Which Communication Technique Is Most Important in Coaching** (`question_2`, select_one, *): This question assesses your knowledge of effective communication techniques in coaching.
* **What Should You Do If a Client Discloses a Medical Emergency** (`question_3`, select_one, *): This question evaluates your ability to handle sensitive situations.
* **Confidentiality Can Be Broken When** (`question_4`, select_one, *): This question tests your understanding of confidentiality guidelines.
* **What Is the GROW Model Used For** (`question_5`, select_one, *): This question assesses your knowledge of the GROW model.
* **A Coach Should Maintain Professional Boundaries By** (`question_6`, select_one, *): This question evaluates your understanding of professional boundaries.
* **What Is Motivational Interviewing** (`question_7`, select_one, *): This question assesses your understanding of Motivational Interviewing.
* **When Should Documentation Be Completed** (`question_8`, select_one, *): This question evaluates your understanding of documentation requirements.
* **What Does SMART Goals Stand For** (`question_9`, select_one, *): This question tests your knowledge of SMART goals.
* **If a Client Is Not Making Progress You Should** (`question_10`, select_one, *): This question assesses your approach to handling clients who are not making progress.
* **Cultural Competence in Coaching Means** (`question_11`, select_one, *): This question evaluates your understanding of cultural competence.
* **What Is the Role of Accountability in Coaching** (`question_12`, select_one, *): This question assesses your understanding of accountability in coaching.
* **Scenario - A Client Wants to Skip Sessions Due to Cost** (`scenario_1`, select_one, *): This question evaluates your ability to handle client cancellations.
* **Scenario - A Client Asks for Medical Advice** (`scenario_2`, select_one, *): This question tests your understanding of handling sensitive situations.
* **Scenario - You Notice a Client Seems Depressed** (`scenario_3`, select_one, *): This question assesses your approach to handling client mental health.
* **Our Company Requires Session Notes Within** (`company_policy_1`, select_one, *): This question evaluates your understanding of company documentation policies.
* **Client Cancellations Must Be Reported To** (`company_policy_2`, select_one, *): This question tests your knowledge of client cancellation procedures.
* **How Confident Do You Feel About Company Policies** (`self_assessment`, select_one, *): This question rates your self-assessment of your understanding of company policies.
* **Areas Where You Need More Training** (`additional_training_needed`, select_multiple, false): Select all areas where you feel you need additional training.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`quiz_comments`, text, false): Enter any additional comments or questions you may have.
