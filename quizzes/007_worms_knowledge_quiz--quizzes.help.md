# Worms Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic information from users, including their contact details, quiz questions, and answers, as well as their score and submitted progress. It is intended for users to gauge their understanding of a particular subject area, likely "worms".

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the User Info section with your details, if required.
2. Select the correct answer(s) for the Quiz Questions from the provided options.
3. Input your score, if applicable.
4. Enter your email and phone number, if required.
5. Add any notes or comments in the Note field.
6. Submit the form once you have completed all required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required/optional): This section is where you enter your basic contact information, such as your name and other identifying details.
* **Quiz Questions** (`quiz_questions`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the correct answer(s) for the provided quiz question(s) from the available options.
* **Quiz Answers** (`quiz_answers`, text, required/optional): This field is not applicable as there are no options provided in the YAML.
* **Score** (`score`, number, required/optional): Enter your score, if applicable, to gauge your progress.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for future contact or feedback purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number, if required, for additional contact information.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your submission, if needed.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required/optional): Enter your name or username, if required, to identify who submitted the form.
* **Quiz Progress** (`quiz_progress`, text, required/optional): This field is not applicable as there are no options provided in the YAML.
