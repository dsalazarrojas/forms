# Limit Lines Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Limit Lines Quiz is designed to collect information from respondents about their understanding or proficiency in various math concepts.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name.
3. Provide your Email (if you have one).
4. Type in your answers to the various math concepts.
