# Functions Math Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Functions Math Quiz form is designed to assess students' understanding of mathematical functions, providing teachers with a tool to monitor student progress and identify areas that require extra attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:
1. Fill in your student name in the text field "student_name".
2. Provide your email address in the email field. This information will be used for contacting you in case we need to discuss your progress.
3. Answer the math-related questions, following the instructions for each question:
   - For "question_1" and "question_7" (select_one), select a single option that best fits the question.
   - For "question_2" and "question_8" (select_multiple), select all that apply.
   - For "question_3", enter the time in 24-hour format (HH:MM:SS).
   - For "question_4", enter the date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
   - For "question_5" and "question_11", enter a number.
   - For "question_6", "question_10", "question_16", "question_17", and "question_22", enter the date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
   - For "question_9", "question_14", and "question_21", enter the time in 24-hour format (HH:MM:SS).
   - For "question_12", "question_18", and "question_23", enter a number.
   - For "question_13" and "question_19", enter a text description.
   - For "question_15" and "question_20", select all that apply.
   - For "question_20" and "question_20" is actually not a part of this form
