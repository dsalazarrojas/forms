# iso_security_exam_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The iso_security_exam_quiz form is designed to assess the security knowledge and awareness of the user. It is a comprehensive quiz that covers various aspects of security, and its purpose is to collect information from users to gauge their understanding of security concepts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
5. Select your company from the options provided in the "company" field (if applicable).
6. Choose an answer for the security questions, following the instructions and options provided.
