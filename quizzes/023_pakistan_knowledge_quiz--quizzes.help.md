# Pakistan Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data from participants of the Pakistan Knowledge Quiz, and the information collected will be used for tracking and analysis purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the First Name field with your name as it appears on your official documents.
2. Fill out the Last Name field with your surname.
3. Enter your participant email address in the Participant Email field. This will be used for communication and verification purposes.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it in the Participant Phone field. This is optional but recommended for future contact purposes.
5. Select the Quiz Category that best fits the quiz you participated in from the dropdown menu.
6. Choose your level of expertise from the Quiz Level dropdown menu.
7. Enter the duration of the quiz you participated in using the date and time fields.
8. Enter the start and end time of the quiz using the time fields.
9. Optionally, enter any additional comments about your quiz experience in the Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your given name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname.
* **Participant Email**: Enter a valid email address that will be used for communication and verification purposes.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter a phone number for future contact purposes (optional).
* **Quiz Category**: Select the category that best fits the quiz you participated in (e.g., Geography, History, etc.).
* **Quiz Level**: Choose your level of expertise from Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Quiz Duration**: Enter the duration of the quiz you participated in using the date format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Quiz Start Time**: Enter the start time of the quiz using the 24-hour time format (e.g., HH:MM).
* **Quiz End Time**: Enter the end time of the quiz using the 24-hour time format (e.g., HH:MM).
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about your quiz experience.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are filling out the form for the correct purpose and that you are not duplicating an existing participant's entry.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please refer back to this help guide for clarification.
* Please ensure that you are entering accurate and truthful information in the form.
