# Conditional Logic Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess participants' ability to apply conditional logic in decision-making. The quiz consists of several questions that require participants to choose the most appropriate action or response based on the scenario presented. The final result will indicate whether the participant has passed or failed the quiz.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Enter your name**: In this field, please type your name as you would like it to be displayed on the final result page. This is a required field.
2. **Select the date**: Choose the date on which you took the quiz. This is a required field.
3. **Choose the correct action for Scenario One**: Select the most appropriate action from the options provided in response to the scenario. This is a required field.
4. **Choose the correct action for Scenario Two**: Select the most appropriate action from the options provided in response to the scenario, taking into account your previous answer to Scenario One.
5. **Select all applicable options for Multiple Choice**: Choose all options that apply to the scenario, as many or as few as you need. This field is optional.
6. **Provide a short written response**: Type a short answer to the short answer question. This field is optional.
7. **Read the passing score threshold**: This field is not fillable and is for informational purposes only.
8. **Enter the completion time**: Enter the time it took you to complete the quiz. This field is optional.
9. **Add reviewer comments (if applicable)**: If you would like to provide feedback or comments, please enter them here. This field is optional.
10. **Final Result**: The system will automatically determine your final result based on your answers. This field is not editable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on the final result page. (Required)
* **Quiz date**: Choose the date on which you took the quiz. (Required)
* **Scenario One**: Select the most appropriate action from the options provided. (Required)
* **Scenario Two**: Select the most appropriate action from the options provided, taking into account your previous answer to Scenario One. (Required)
* **Multiple Choice**: Choose all options that apply to the scenario. (Optional)
* **Short answer question**: Type a short answer to the question. (Optional)
* **Passing score threshold**: This field is for informational purposes only and cannot be edited.
* **Completion time**: Enter the time it took you to complete the quiz. (Optional)
* **Reviewer comments**: If you would like to provide feedback or comments, please enter them here. (Optional)
* **Final result**: This field will be automatically populated based on your answers.
