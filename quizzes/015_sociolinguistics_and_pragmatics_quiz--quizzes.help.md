# Sociolinguistics And Pragmatics Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information from educators about their sociolinguistics and pragmatics quiz. The collected data will be used for creating and managing quizzes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the educator in the "Educator Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Educator Email" field. This field should be a valid email that can be used to contact you.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Educator Phone" field.
4. Describe your institution in the "Institution" field. This can be any name or description of your institution.
5. Select your quiz type from the "Quiz Type" dropdown menu.
6. Provide a response to the question in the "Quiz Response" field.
7. If applicable, enter the URL for your institution in the "Institution URL" field.
8. If necessary, provide any additional notes about your institution in the "Institution Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Educator Name** (`educator_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the educator.
  - This field is used to identify you as the educator submitting the form.
- **Educator Email** (`educator_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
  - This field is used to contact you regarding your quiz.
- **Educator Phone** (`educator_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
  - This field is used to contact you in case of any further queries.
- **Institution** (`institution`, text, required/optional): Describe your institution.
  - This field helps identify your institution and its connection to the quiz.
- **Quiz Type** (`quiz_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your quiz type.
  - Select one of the options provided, which are "Yes" and "No", or "Other" (if available).
- **Quiz Response** (`quiz_response`, text, required/optional): Provide a response to the question.
  - This field is used to collect your thoughts and opinions about the quiz.
- **Institution URL** (`institution_url`, text, required/optional): Enter the URL of your institution.
  - If applicable, enter the URL for your institution.
- **Institution Notes** (`institution_notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about your institution.
  - Use this field for any further information about your institution.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and truthful information.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us at [insert contact information].
