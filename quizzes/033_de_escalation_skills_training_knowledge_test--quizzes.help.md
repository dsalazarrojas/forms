# De Escalation Skills Training Knowledge Test - Help Guide
## Purpose
The De Escalation Skills Training Knowledge Test is designed to evaluate your de-escalation skills and knowledge after completing the training program. This form will assess your ability to apply the learned skills in real-world scenarios.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Select the correct options for each question, taking into account the training content.
3. Fill in all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*).
4. Double-check your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`De Escalation Skills Training Knowledge Test`): This is the title of the form, which is displayed at the top of the page. It does not require a specific answer.
* **Training Outcome 1** (`training_outcome_1`): This question is a multiple-choice question that asks about your understanding of the training content. Select the correct option that best reflects your knowledge.
* **Training Outcome 2** (`training_outcome_2`): This question is also a multiple-choice question that asks about your understanding of the training content. Select all correct options that apply to your knowledge.
* **Training Outcome 3** (`training_outcome_3`): This question is a text field where you can provide a brief answer about your understanding of the training content.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date when you completed the training program or when you last used your de-escalation skills.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time when you completed the training program or when you last used your de-escalation skills.
* **Note** (`note`): This field is for any additional comments or observations you would like to make about the training program or your de-escalation skills.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address, but this field is not required for the form submission.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number, but this field is not required for the form submission.

## Tips
- Ensure your answers are honest and accurate.
- Take your time to answer each question carefully.
- If you're unsure about any question, ask your trainer or supervisor for clarification.
- Double-check your answers before submitting.
