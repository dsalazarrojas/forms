# Media Literacy Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Media Literacy Quiz is a tool designed to gauge your understanding of media literacy concepts, skills, and practices. It is a self-contained quiz that assesses your critical thinking and reflection on media interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the quiz, please follow the instructions below:

1. Read each question carefully and think about the answer before selecting an option.
2. Select an answer from the options provided for each question.
3. You can move forward or backward through the quiz pages as needed.
4. The quiz has ten questions in total.
5. When you finish, click the "Quiz Completed" button to submit your answers.
