# Workshop Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workshop Knowledge Quiz form is used to gather data about a participant's experience after a workshop session. It's meant to be filled out once per participant after the session has ended.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the Session Name field with the name of the workshop session.
2.  Enter your Participant Name and email address (if you've completed the session).
3.  Fill out the Completed field with a "yes" or "no" indicating whether you've completed the workshop session.
4.  For each question score, fill out the corresponding score from 1 to 10.
5.  Finally, provide your feedback in the Feedback field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Name** (`session_name`, text, optional): This field is meant for the name of the workshop session you participated in.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): This field is meant for your name as a participant.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you've completed the session, please enter your email address.
* **Completed** (`Completed`, text, optional): Check "yes" if you've completed the workshop session, "no" otherwise.
* **Score** (`Session Score`, number, optional): Enter your score from 1 to 10 for the session.
* **Question 1 Score** (`question_1_score`, number, optional): Enter your score from 1 to 10 for the first question.
* **Question 2 Score** (`question_2_score`, number, optional): Enter your score from 1 to 10 for the second question.
* **Question 3 Score** (`question_3_score`, number, optional): Enter your score from 1 to 10 for the third question.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share your thoughts about the workshop session.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form only once per participant.
* Make sure to enter your email address only if you've completed the session.
* If you have any feedback, please provide it in the Feedback field.
* Enter scores from 1 to 10 for each question.
