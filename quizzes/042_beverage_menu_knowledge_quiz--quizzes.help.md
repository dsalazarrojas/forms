# Beverage Menu Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your knowledge of beverages, specifically cocktails, wine, beer, coffee, and other drinks. The quiz is intended to help identify areas where you may need more training in the hospitality industry.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out your name and email address in the first two fields.
2. Select your current position in the third field.
3. Enter the location where you work.
4. Answer each question with the correct information.
5. Rate your confidence level in your answers.
6. Select any areas where you need more training.
7. Add any additional comments or feedback.
