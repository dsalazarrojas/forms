The purpose of this form is to gather information from medical professionals for the Rapid Sequence Intubation (RSI) Quiz, which is used to assess their knowledge and skills in performing RSI. The form collects demographic information (First Name, Email Address, and Phone Number), RSI knowledge, simulation experience, and course completion status.

To complete this form, you will need to provide the following information:

### How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your First Name, Email Address, and Phone Number (if available).
2. Select your RSI knowledge level from the "RSI Knowledge" dropdown menu.
3. Choose which simulations you have completed from the "Simulation" dropdown menu.
4. Enter your Course Number.
5. Select whether you have completed training or not from the "Training Completed" and "Course Completed" dropdown menus.
6. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments" and "Feedback" text boxes.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number (if available).
* **RSI Knowledge** (`rsi_knowledge`, `select_one`, required): Select your level of RSI knowledge.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth (if available).
* **Medical School** (`medical_school`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your medical school (if applicable).
* **Simulation** (`simulation`, `select_multiple`, required): Select which simulations you have completed.
* **Course Number** (`course_number`, `number`, required): Enter your course number.
* **RSI Rating** (`rsi_rating`, `select_one`, optional): Select your RSI rating (if available).
* **RSI Training Completed** (`training_completed`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether you have completed RSI training.
* **Training Completed** (`training_completed`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether you have completed training (if not already selected).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments.
* **Medical License** (`medical_license`, `text`, optional): Enter your medical license number (if available).
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Number** (`emergency_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Course Completed** (`course_completed`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether you have completed your course.
* **RSI Rating Scale** (`rsi_rating_scale`, `select_one`, optional): Select your RSI rating scale (if available).
* **RSI Score** (`rsi_score`, `number`, optional): Enter your RSI score (if available).
* **RSI Comment** (`rsi_comment`, `text`, optional): Enter a comment about your RSI experience.
* **RSI Recommendation** (`rsi_recommendation`, `text`, optional): Enter a comment or recommendation about RSI.
* **RSI Onsite Training** (`rsi_onsite_training`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of your RSI onsite training.
* **RSI Evaluation** (`rsi_evaluation`, `text`, optional): Enter your evaluation of the RSI course or training.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): Enter any feedback about the RSI quiz or training.
* **RSI Additional Info** (`rsi_additional_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information about RSI.
