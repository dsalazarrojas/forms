# Music Theory Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Theory Quiz form is used to test your knowledge of music theory basics. This form will help us assess your understanding of music fundamentals and related concepts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question or select the relevant options for the corresponding field. You can skip any questions that don't apply to your situation. For most fields, you can type in your answer directly, while for fields with options, select the correct answer from the list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **music_theory_quiz** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This is the title of the quiz, which is not editable and is used for internal purposes.
* **what_is_music_theory** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Describe what you think music theory is. This question is meant to be answered in a few sentences and can be as brief or detailed as you like.
* **what_are_the_main_components_of_music** (`3`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose one of the following options for the main components of music: `12`, `34`, `56`. Select the one that best describes your understanding.
* **music_theory_quiz_4** (`4`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of notes that make up a scale.
* **music_theory_quiz_5** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all that apply: `12`, `34`, `56`, `78`. This question requires you to choose multiple answers.
* **music_theory_quiz_6** (`6`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of keys on a piano keyboard.
* **music_theory_quiz_7** (`7`, `date`, required/optional): If you're not familiar with music theory, when did you first learn about it? (Leave blank if you're not sure)
* **music_theory_quiz_8** (`8`, `time`, required/optional): What time of day do you usually practice your music? (Leave blank if you don't practice music)
* **music_theory_quiz_9** (`9`, `note`, required/optional): Write any additional comments or notes about music theory that you'd like to share.
* **music_theory_quiz_10** (`10`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further instructions.
* **music_theory_quiz_11** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): What is your favorite music theory concept or idea?
* **music_theory_quiz_12** (`12`, `text`, required/optional): If you have any other music theory-related questions or comments, please share them with us.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
