# Opera Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Opera Knowledge Quiz is designed to evaluate your knowledge of opera-related concepts, covering starting points, musical styles, periods, and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Choose the starting points that are relevant to your knowledge (Select all that apply).
2. Select the musical style that best describes your area of interest.
3. Indicate your level of knowledge of musical periods (Select all that apply, if desired).
4. Answer the text field with any additional knowledge questions or comments.
5. Choose your interests from the list (Select all that apply).
6. Select your familiarity level with the subject matter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Starting Points**: This field asks you to select all the opera companies or artists that are relevant to your knowledge. Examples include:
	+ Verdi, Rossini, and Puccini
	+ Bellini
	+ Puccini
	+ Mozart (Note: Be cautious not to select Mozart as Bellini is a composer from a different art form, while Puccini is a well-known opera composer. Selecting both might be correct, but the intent is to select opera composers.)
* **Musical Styles**: This field is used to determine which genre of opera you are interested in. Options include:
	+ Serious (e.g., Verdi's Rigoletto)
	+ Comic (e.g., Offenbach's Orpheus)
	+ Lyric (e.g., Puccini's La Boheme)
	+ Tragic (e.g., Mozart's Requiem)
	+ Melodrama (e.g., Massenet's Herodiade)
	+ Serious and Comic (e.g., Puccini's Tosca)
	+ Serious and Tragic (e.g., Verdi's Otello)
	+ Melodrama and Comic (e.g., Puccini and Offenbach)
* **Musical Periods**: This field allows you to specify your knowledge of different musical periods. Select all that apply:
	+ Baroque
	+ Classical
	+ Romantic
	+ Impressionist
	+ Modern
	+ Contemporary
* **Knowledge Questions**: This field is for any additional information or knowledge you'd like to share about opera.
* **Interests**: This field is used to select all the opera-related interests that you have:
	+ Verdi
	+ Mozart
	+ Puccini
	+ Puccini and Offenbach
	+ Operatic
	+ Offenbach and Strauss
	+ Offenbach and Massenet
* **Familiarity**: This field is used to indicate your level of familiarity with the subject matter:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low

## Tips

* Be honest about your level of familiarity to ensure accurate assessment.
* If unsure, select the highest level that reflects your understanding.
* Take your time to carefully review and fill in the form accurately.
