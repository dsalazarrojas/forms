# waiting_room_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about patients' experiences in the waiting room. It aims to collect data on various aspects of their visit, including wait time, activities during the wait, mode of information, satisfaction, and demographics. The responses will help the healthcare provider to understand patient expectations, identify areas for improvement, and enhance the overall waiting room experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly. You will be asked about your visit, including how long you waited in the waiting room, what you did during the wait, how you were informed about your wait time, and your overall satisfaction with the visit. You will also be asked about your demographics, such as age, gender, ethnicity, marital status, education level, and income range. The form is divided into several sections, and each section is designed to gather specific information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How long do you usually wait in the waiting room?** (`select_one`, 0-10 minutes to More than 40 minutes) - Select how long you normally wait in the waiting room from the provided options.
* **What were you doing while you waited?** (`select_multiple`, Watching TV or playing games, Looking at books or magazines, Reading on phone/tablet, Thinking about your visit, Other) - Choose all that apply from the given options.
* **How were you informed about your wait time?** (`select_one`, Through the receptionist or nurse, On the digital display, Through an app, Through a staff member, Other) - Select how you were informed about your wait time.
* **What did you think about your waiting room experience?** (`select_multiple`, Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor) - Choose all that apply from the provided options.
* **How satisfied were you with your visit?** (`select_multiple`, Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied) - Choose all that apply from the given options.
* **Any additional feedback?** (`note`, free-text field) - Share any additional comments or suggestions about your waiting room experience.
* **Patient Name** (`text`, free-text field) - Enter your name as it appears on your medical record.
* **Visit Date** (`date`, in YYYY-MM-DD format) - Enter the date of your visit in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-02-20).
* **Visit Time** (`time`, in HH:MM format) - Enter the time of your visit in the HH:MM format (e.g., 13:45).
* **Age** (`text`, free-text field) - Enter your age.
* **Email** (`email`, email address) - Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`text`, free-text field) - Enter your phone number.
* **Gender** (`select_one`, Male, Female, Other) - Select your gender.
* **Ethnicity** (`select_one`, White, Black or African American, Asian, Hispanic or Latino, Other) - Select your ethnicity.
* **Marital Status** (`select_one`, Married, Single, Divorced, Separated) - Select your marital status.
* **Education Level** (`select_one`, Less than high school graduate, High school graduate, Some college, Bachelor's degree, Master's degree, Doctoral degree) - Select your education level.
* **Income Range** (`select_one`, Less than $25,000, $25,000-$49,999, $50,000-$74,999, $75,000-$99,999, $100,000 or more) - Select your income range.
* **Insurance Status** (`select_one`, No insurance, Private insurance, Medicare, Medicaid, Other) - Select your insurance status.
* **How long was your visit?** (`number`, in minutes) - Enter the length of your visit in minutes.
* **Number of visits** (`number`, free-text field) - Enter the number of visits you made.
* **Age group** (`select_one`, 18 or younger, 19-29, 30-39, 40-49, 50 or older) - Select your age group.
* **What is your health condition?** (`select_multiple`, None of the above, Allergies, Arthritis, Cancer, Diabetes, Heart condition, High blood pressure, Other) - Choose all that apply from the given options.
* **How many people visited with you?** (`select_one`, 1, 2, 3, 4, More than 4) - Select how many people visited with you.
* **What type of visit?** (`select_multiple`, Routine Visit, Specialist Visit, Emergency, New patient visit, Other) - Choose all that apply from the given options.
* **How many times have you visited this provider?** (`select_one`, Once, 2-5 times, 6-10 times, 11-20 times, 21 or more times) - Select how many times you have visited this provider.
