# HIV Awareness Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HIV Awareness Quiz is a form designed to collect information about an individual's HIV status, medical history, and other relevant factors. This form is intended to educate individuals about HIV and its risks, and to help them assess their own risk factors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, answering all the questions truthfully.
2. Be honest about your medical history, HIV status, and other risk factors.
3. Use the options provided to select the best answer for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address for correspondence purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`birthdate`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required): Enter your current age.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Sexual Orientation** (`sexualorientation`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your sexual orientation from the options provided.
* **HIV Status** (`hiv_status`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your HIV status from the options provided.
* **HIV Diagnose** (`hiv_diagnosis`, `date`, required): Enter your HIV diagnosis date.
* **Last HIV Test Date** (`hiv_last_test_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your last HIV test.
* **Last HIV Test Result** (`hiv_last_test_result`, `select_one`, required): Select the result of your last HIV test.
* **Sexual Partner** (`sexual_partner`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether you have a sexual partner from the options provided.
* **Sexual Risk Factors** (`sexual_risk_factors`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your sexual risk factors from the options provided.
* **Drug Use** (`drug_use`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether you use drugs from the options provided.
* **Alcholol Use** (`alcohol_use`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether you use alcohol from the options provided.
* **Blood Sharing** (`blood_sharing`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether you share blood from the options provided.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether you have a medical history from the options provided.
* **Medical History HIV** (`medical_history_hiv`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether you have a medical history related to HIV from the options provided.
* **Test Frequency** (`test_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required): Select how often you get tested for HIV.
* **Test Method** (`test_method`, `select_multiple`, required): Select how you get tested for HIV.
* **Test Location** (`test_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of your HIV test.
* **Test Result** (`test_result`, `select_one`, required): Select the result of your HIV test.

## Tips
* Be honest about your medical history and HIV status.
* Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about a question, select the option "Unknown" or "Not Applicable".
