# Best Friend Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to answer questions about their best friend, to assess the user's knowledge about their friend's preferences, habits, and characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question with the best information you have about your best friend.
2. Enter your name and your friend's name in the required fields.
3. Answer the questions in the subsequent sections according to the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`taker_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Friend Name** (`target_friend_name`, text, required): Enter your friend's name.
* **When is your friend's birthday** (`friend_birthday`, date, required): Enter your friend's birthday in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **What is your friend's middle name** (`friend_middle_name`, text, optional): If your friend has a middle name, enter it here.
* **What is your friend's favorite color** (`friend_fav_color`, text, required): Select your friend's favorite color.
* **What is your friend's favorite food** (`friend_fav_food`, text, required): Select your friend's favorite food.
* **Coffee or Tea** (`beverage_preference`, select_one, required): Select which beverage your friend drinks more often.
* **Preferred Pizza Topping** (`pizza_topping_choice`, select_one, required): Select your friend's preferred pizza topping.
* **Cats or Dogs** (`pet_preference`, select_one, required): Select your friend's favorite pet.
* **Where did you first meet** (`meeting_place_desc`, text, required): Describe the location or event where you first met your friend.
* **What was your first impression of each other** (`first_impression_text`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your first impression of each other.
* **What is the funniest memory you share** (`funniest_memory_desc`, text, required): Enter a description of the funniest memory you share with your friend.
* **What is your friend's biggest fear** (`friend_biggest_fear`, text, required): Enter your friend's biggest fear.
* **What is your friend's dream job** (`friend_dream_job`, text, required): Enter your friend's dream job.
* **What is your friend's proud moment** (`friend_proud_moment`, text, required): Enter one thing your friend is really proud of.
* **How many questions do you think you got right** (`estimated_score`, number, required): Estimate how many questions you think you got right, out of the specific facts asked.
* **Today's Date** (`quiz_date_stamp`, date, required): The current date will be automatically filled in.
