# Urine Color Analysis Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your urine color and other related details for analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Answer the Urine Color question with the current color of your urine.
2. Select your hydration frequency from the provided options (Daily, Every Other Day, Every Few Days, Never).
3. Select all that apply for the Color of Urine question (Clear, Dark Yellow/Amber, Light Yellow, Pink, Red/Brown, Tan/Brown, White/Cloudy, Watermelon).
4. Provide any additional specifics about your urine in the Urine Specifics field.
5. Add any comments about your situation in the Comments field.
6. (Optional) Enter your email address and phone number for follow-up purposes.
7. Schedule your next submission date and time in the Next Submission Date and Time fields.
8. Add any additional notes for the next submission in the Next Submission Note field.
9. Add any further comments in the Comments 2 field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Urine Color**: Please enter the current color of your urine.
* **Hydration Frequency**: Please select how often you urinate (Daily, Every Other Day, Every Few Days, Never).
* **Color of Urine**: Select all that apply for the following colors:
	+ Clear
	+ Dark Yellow/Amber
	+ Light Yellow
	+ Pink
	+ Red/Brown
	+ Tan/Brown
	+ White/Cloudy
	+ Watermelon
* **Urine Specifics**: Please provide any additional details about your urine.
* **Comments**: Please add any comments or concerns you have about your situation.
* **Email**: (Optional) Please enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Phone**: (Optional) Please enter your phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Next Submission Date**: (Optional) Please enter your next submission date.
* **Next Submission Time**: (Optional) Please enter your next submission time.
* **Next Submission Note**: (Optional) Please add any additional notes for the next submission.
* **Comments 2**: (Optional) Please add any further comments.
Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required.
