# Natural Resources Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Natural Resources Quiz is designed to gauge your understanding of natural resources, conservation practices, and sustainable management methods. This quiz aims to assess your knowledge in these critical areas to help identify potential knowledge gaps and provide insights for further learning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by selecting the "General Knowledge" page. You will be presented with a text-based question where you can provide your answer.
2. Move on to the "Natural Resources Importance" page, where you will be asked to rate the importance of natural resources. Select a number to indicate your level of agreement.
3. On the "Conservation Practices" page, choose whether you have adopted conservation practices in your daily life.
4. The "Conservation Management" page is a text-based question where you can provide any relevant information about your conservation management strategies.
5. In the "Sustainable Management" page, you will have the opportunity to provide any additional notes about your sustainable management practices.
6. Finally, on the "Quiz Result" page, you will be presented with multiple choice options to rate your agreement with the statements provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Knowledge** (general_knowledge, text, optional): This is an open-ended question where you can provide any information related to natural resources.
* **Natural Resources Importance** (natural_resouces_importance, number, required): Rate the importance of natural resources on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being least important and 10 being most important.
* **Conservation Practices** (conservation_practices, select_one, required): Choose whether you have adopted conservation practices in your daily life by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Conservation Management** (conservation_management, text, optional): Provide any relevant information about your conservation management strategies.
* **Sustainable Management** (sustainable_management, note, optional): This is a text field for you to provide any additional information about your sustainable management practices.
* **Quiz Result** (quiz_result, select_multiple, optional): Rate your agreement with the statements provided by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the multiple options.
