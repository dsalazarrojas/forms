# Digital Escape Room Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a digital escape room experience by inputting the title, description, and background story of the game.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of your digital escape room in the "Game Title" field.
2. Add a brief description of your game in the "Game Description" field.
3. Enter a brief background story of your game in the "Game Background" field.
4. Select a category for your game from the "Quiz Category" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Game Title** (`game_title`, text, required): Enter a brief and catchy title for your digital escape room.
* **Game Description** (`game_description`, text, required): Add a short paragraph describing your game, including its main objective and challenges.
* **Game Background** (`game_background`, text, required): Enter a brief background story of your game, including any setting or plot.
* **Quiz Category** (`quiz_category`, select_one, optional): Select a category for your game, helping it get found by relevant players.
* **Game Title Hint** (`game_title`, text, optional): Enter a hint about the game title, not always necessary but can be useful for search purposes.
* **Game Description Hint** (`game_description`, text, optional): Enter a hint about the game description, not always necessary but can be useful for search purposes.
* **Game Background Hint** (`game_background`, text, optional): Enter a hint about the game background, not always necessary but can be useful for search purposes.
