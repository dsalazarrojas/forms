# Herons Formula Math Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Herons Formula Math Quiz is a tool to help users understand and apply Heron's Formula, a mathematical concept used to calculate the area of a triangle when all three sides are known. This quiz is designed to walk you through a series of questions and calculations to solidify your understanding of the formula.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions on each page and fill in the required information.

1. **First Page**: This is the introductory page where you will begin your calculations.
2. **First Question**: Choose one of the available options (A, B, C, or D) that best describes the shape of the triangle.
3. **Side A Length**: Enter the length of side A of the triangle.
4. **Side B Length**: Enter the length of side B of the triangle.
5. **Side C Length**: Enter the length of side C of the triangle.
6. **Height**: Enter the height of the triangle.
7. **Answer**: Enter your calculated answer using Heron's Formula.
8. **First Note**: This is a note for you to record any important information or calculations related to your first question.
9. **Second Note**: This is a note for you to record any important information or calculations related to your second question.

* **First Question (First Question, select_one, required):** Select the shape of the triangle by choosing one of the available options (A, B, C, or D).
* **Side A Length (Side A Length, number, required):** Enter the length of side A of the triangle. This field is used in Heron's Formula to calculate the area of the triangle.
* **Side B Length (Side B Length, number, required):** Enter the length of side B of the triangle. This field is used in Heron's Formula to calculate the area of the triangle.

* **Height (Height, number, required):** Enter the height of the triangle. This field is used in Heron's Formula to calculate the semi-perimeter of the triangle.
* **Answer (Answer, number, required):** Enter your calculated answer using Heron's Formula. The formula is: `area = sqrt(s - (a + b + c) * sqrt(s - a) * sqrt(s - b) * sqrt(s - c))` where `s` is the semi-perimeter and `s = (a + b + c) / 2`.
