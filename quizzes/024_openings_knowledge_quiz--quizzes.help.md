# Openings Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Openings Knowledge Quiz form is designed to collect user information, including their name, contact details, and responses to a few questions. The form is not intended for any other purpose and is only for collecting user input for knowledge quiz purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Openings Knowledge Quiz form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field. Please use a valid email format.
3. Answer the question "question-one" in the "answer" field. You can choose one or multiple options.
4. Select one option for "select-one" field from the provided choices.
5. Optionally, provide your date of birth in the "date" field. If you choose to provide a date, please use the format of MM/DD/YY.
6. Optionally, choose your time in the "time" field.
7. Optionally, enter your phone number in the "phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`user_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (`user_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **question-one** (`user_question`, `note`, required/optional): Answer the question provided.
* **answer** (`user_answer`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose one or multiple answers from the provided options.
* **select-one** (`user_select_one`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose one option from the provided choices.
* **date** (`user_date`, `date`, required/optional): Optionally, enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YY.
* **time** (`user_time`, `time`, required/optional): Optionally, choose your time.
* **Phone** (`user_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Optionally, enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Please ensure you provide valid input for all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field label or description above.
* If you need assistance with the form, contact our support team.
* Remember, the form is designed to collect user input only for knowledge quiz purposes.
