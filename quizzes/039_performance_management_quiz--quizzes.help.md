# performance_management_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information related to performance management quizzes from end-users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by selecting the relevant section for your information.
2. If you are filling out Section One, please provide a brief note about your general information.
3. If you are filling out Section Two, choose a date from the provided options.
4. For Section Three, select all the options that apply to your performance management quiz.

* **Section One (Section One)**: This section is for general information. Please provide a brief note about yourself.
