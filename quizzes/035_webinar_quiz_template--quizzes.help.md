# Webinar Quiz Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to create a quiz for a webinar or training session. It allows you to define the structure and questions of your quiz, including multiple-choice and date/time questions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Webinar Quiz Template** field with the title of your quiz.
2. Select the answers to each question in the format of **Start**, **Question 1**, **Question 1 Answer 1**, **Question 1 Answer 2**, **Question 2**, **Question 2 Answer 1**, etc.

* **Webinar Quiz Template** (`webinar_quiz_template_start_page`): Fill in the title of your quiz.
