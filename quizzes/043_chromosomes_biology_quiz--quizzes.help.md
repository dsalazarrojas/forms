# Chromosomes Biology Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a quiz designed to test your knowledge on chromosomes. Please answer the questions carefully and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student information: First Name, Last Name, Student ID, and Email Address.
2. Answer each of the 8 questions carefully, selecting the correct answer from the options provided.
3. If you have any additional comments or questions, type them in the "Additional Comments or Questions" field.
4. Finally, select the quiz date and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Student ID** (text, optional): Enter your student ID number (if applicable).
* **Email Address** (email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Class Section** (text, optional): Enter your class section.
* **-- Quiz Instructions --** (note, required): Read through the instructions for this quiz before proceeding.
* **What are chromosomes primarily made of?** (select_one, required): Choose from the options: Proteins only, DNA and proteins (histones), RNA only, or Lipids and carbohydrates.
* **How many chromosomes do humans have in each cell?** (select_one, required): Choose from the options: 23, 46, 92, or 64.
* **Which type of chromosome abnormality involves having three copies?** (select_one, required): Choose from the options: Monosomy, Trisomy, Tetrasomy, or Nullisomy.
* **What is the term for the observable structure of chromosomes?** (select_one, required): Choose from the options: Genotype, Karyotype, Phenotype, or Biotype.
* **Mitochondria contain which type of chromosome?** (select_one, required): Choose from the options: Linear chromosomes, Circular chromosomes, Paired chromosomes, or Sex chromosomes.
* **What is the primary role of the centromere?** (select_one, required): Choose from the options: DNA replication, Protein synthesis, Chromosome segregation during cell division, or Gene expression.
* **Which chromosome pair determines sex in humans?** (select_one, required): Choose from the options: First pair, 22nd pair, 23rd pair (X and Y), or Last pair.
* **What is a telomere?** (select_one, required): Choose from the options: The middle region of a chromosome, Protective caps at the ends of chromosomes, The point where chromosomes attach during mitosis, or DNA strand segments.
* **Down syndrome is caused by a trisomy in which chromosome?** (select_one, required): Choose from the options: Chromosome 13, Chromosome 18, Chromosome 21, or Chromosome 22.
* **During which phase of meiosis do homologous chromosomes separate?** (select_one, required): Choose from the options: Prophase I, Metaphase I, Anaphase I, or Telophase I.
