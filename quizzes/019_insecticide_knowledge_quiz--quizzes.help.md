# Insecticide Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your knowledge about insecticides. It consists of a series of questions and fields that help us evaluate your understanding of insecticides, their usage, safety precautions, and other related information. Please fill out the form carefully to provide accurate and complete responses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer all the questions truthfully to the best of your ability.
2. Select the correct options for each field, paying attention to the label and options provided.
3. Fill out all required fields, as indicated by the required label.
4. Submit your completed form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`General Information`, text, optional): This field is a free-text area where you can provide any additional information you think is relevant to your knowledge about insecticides.
* **List of Insecticides** (`List of Insecticides`, select multiple, optional): Please select all the insecticides that you are familiar with.
* **Usage Frequency** (`Usage Frequency`, number, optional): If applicable, indicate how often you use insecticides. For example, "daily", "weekly", "monthly", etc.
* **Safety Precautions** (`Safety Precautions`, text, optional): Describe any safety precautions you follow when handling insecticides.
* **Storage and Disposal** (`Storage and Disposal`, text, optional): Explain how you store and dispose of insecticides, if applicable.
* **Emergency Procedures** (`Emergency Procedures`, text, optional): Describe the emergency procedures you follow in case of an insecticide-related incident.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information you think is relevant to your knowledge about insecticides.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for further information or to receive your results.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by phone for further information or to receive your results.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, optional): If applicable, indicate the date you completed this quiz.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, optional): If applicable, indicate the time you completed this quiz.
* **Select One Insecticide** (`Select One Insecticide`, select one, optional): Please select one insecticide from the options provided.
* **Select Multiple Insecticides** (`Select Multiple Insecticides`, select multiple, optional): Please select all the insecticides that you are familiar with.
* **Date & Time** (`Date & Time`, date, optional): If applicable, indicate the date and time you completed this quiz.
* **Date Integer** (`Date`, number, optional): If applicable, enter a date integer.
* **Time Integer** (`Time`, number, optional): If applicable, enter a time integer.
* **Select Integer** (`Select Integer`, select one, optional): Please select one of the options provided.
* **Select Multiple Integer** (`Select Multiple Integer`, select multiple, optional): Please select all the options that apply.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your completed form.
