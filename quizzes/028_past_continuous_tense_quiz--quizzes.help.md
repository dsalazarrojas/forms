# Past Continuous Tense Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your proficiency in using the past continuous tense in English language. It consists of questions that will test your ability to express ongoing actions and events in the past.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question as it appears on the form.
2. Type your responses in the text fields provided.
3. If you need to choose a specific time or date, use the format "HH:MM" for times and "YYYY" for years.
4. Complete all fields if possible, but feel free to skip questions if you're unsure or unable to answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Please enter your name.
* **what_was_your_favorite_food** (`question_1`, text, optional): What was your favorite food in the past?
* **what_is_your_favorite_color** (`question_2`, text, optional): What is your favorite color?
* **when_do_you_like_to_wake_up** (`question_3`, time, optional): At what time do you like to wake up?
* **what_time_do_you_sleep** (`question_4`, time, optional): At what time do you sleep?
* **how_long_do_you_study** (`question_5`, time, optional): How long do you study?
* **what_were_you_doing_last_night** (`question_6`, text, optional): What were you doing last night?
* **what_were_your_parents_names** (`question_7`, text, optional): What were your parents' names?
* **what_time_were_your_parents_married** (`question_8`, time, optional): At what time did your parents get married?
* **what_year_was_it** (`question_9`, date, optional): What year was it (an event, not a date of birth)?
