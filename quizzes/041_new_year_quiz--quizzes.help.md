# New Year Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a New Year's quiz that tests your knowledge on various topics such as geography, chemistry, and more. It has multiple pages with a mix of single and multiple-select questions, as well as text, number, and date fields.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by answering the questions on the first page, which asks about your New Year's resolutions.
2. On the subsequent pages, answer the questions that apply to you.
3. For each question, select the correct answer from the provided options or type in your answer.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields, as indicated by the red asterisk (*) next to the label.
5. You can skip optional questions, but be aware that they might affect the overall outcome or analysis of your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What are your New Year's resolutions?**: This is a text field where you can type your New Year's resolutions. Make it as specific and clear as possible.
- **What is the coldest recorded temperature in Alaska in December?**: Answer this question with a number, indicating the temperature in degrees.
- **What is the chemical symbol for gold?**: Select the correct symbol for gold from the options provided.
- **What is the time now?**: Type in the current time, in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30 for 2:30 PM).
- **What is your email address?**: Type in your valid email address.
- **What is your phone number?**: Type in your phone number.
- **What is your favorite hobby?**: This is a note field where you can type in your favorite hobby.
- **What is your favorite color?**: Select one or multiple colors that you prefer.
- **What is the average airspeed velocity of an unladen swallow?**: Answer this question with a number, indicating the speed in meters per second.
- **What is your favorite food for the new year?**: Select one or multiple favorite foods.
- **What is your date of birth?**: Type in your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
- **What is your favorite movie genre?**: Select one or multiple genres that you enjoy.
- **How many hours in a day?**: Answer this question with a number, indicating the number of hours.
- **What is your favorite animal?**: Select one or multiple animals that you like.
- **What is the highest mountain peak in the world?**: Answer this question with a text description.
- **What is your favorite sport?**: Select one or multiple sports that you enjoy.
- **What is the average human lifespan?**: Answer this question with a number, indicating the years.
- **What is your favorite time of day?**: Type in your favorite time of day.
- **What is the largest planet in our solar system?**: Answer this question with a text description.
