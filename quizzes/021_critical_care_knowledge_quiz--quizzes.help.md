# Critical Care Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Critical Care Knowledge Quiz is designed to test your critical care knowledge and skills, specifically focusing on identifying patients who are at risk of sepsis and recognizing signs of critical care conditions such as ventilation and critical care skills. This quiz helps assess your ability to make timely and accurate assessments in high-pressure situations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review each question carefully and choose the most appropriate answer based on your clinical knowledge and experience.
2. Select the relevant options for the "critical_care_questions" section as they apply to your clinical practice.
3. If you are unsure about any question, use your clinical judgment to make an informed decision.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **sepsis_questions (id 1)** (`sepsis_1`, `sepsis_2`, `sepsis_3`, optional): This section asks about identifying patients who are at risk of sepsis, a life-threatening medical condition that requires prompt attention.
* **ventilation_questions (id 2)** (`ventilation_1`, `ventilation_2`, `ventilation_3`, optional): This section tests your knowledge of ventilation conditions, which is a critical care skill that requires timely and accurate assessments.
* **critical_care_questions (id 3)** (`critical_care_1`, `critical_care_2`, `critical_care_3`, required): In this section, select the critical care skills that apply to your clinical practice. This is a required field, so please make sure to answer all questions.

## Tips
* Please answer each question based on your clinical knowledge and experience.
* Use your best judgment when unsure about any question.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or administrator.
* This quiz is a tool to assess your critical care knowledge and skills, not a diagnostic tool. If you suspect a patient has a critical condition, please seek immediate medical attention.
