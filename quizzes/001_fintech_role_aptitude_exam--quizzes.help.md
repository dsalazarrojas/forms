# Fintech Role Aptitude Exam - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fintech Role Aptitude Exam is a self-assessment tool designed to evaluate the knowledge and skills of individuals in the fintech industry. It covers various aspects of fintech, including concepts, skills, and best practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions carefully and answer the questions on each page.
2. On the "questions" page, select all the options that apply to your experience and knowledge in the fintech industry.
3. On the "name" page, enter your name as it appears on your government-issued identification.
4. On the "email" page, enter your email address.
5. Finally, click the "submit" button to complete the exam.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **intro (Note)**: This page is a introduction to the exam. Please read it carefully to understand the exam's purpose and instructions.
* **questions (Select Multiple)**: This page contains multiple-choice questions related to the fintech industry. Select all the options that apply to your experience and knowledge.
* **name (Text)**: Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued identification.
* **email (Email)**: Enter your email address.
* **submit (Text)**: Click this button to complete and submit your exam.
