# numeric_input_practice_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The `numeric_input_practice_form` is a form designed to help users practice entering numbers in different contexts. It is meant to assist learners or users who need to develop their skills in inputting numbers accurately and quickly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Start by selecting the page you want to practice, which is the "math_practice" page.
* On this page, you will be prompted to enter a number. Please enter the number accurately and quickly.
* Once you've completed the task, click the "Next" button to proceed.
* Repeat this process for each page, which covers different contexts, such as "number_sequence" and "time_practice".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **math_practice (Number)** (`math_practice`, number, required): This is a math-related input field where you need to enter a specific number as per the task description.
* **number_sequence (Number)** (`number_sequence`, number, required): In this field, you are asked to enter a series of numbers that meet specific conditions or patterns.
* **time_practice (Number)** (`time_practice`, number, required): This is a time-related input field where you need to enter a specific number to match the task requirements.

## Tips
* Make sure to read the task description carefully before entering the number.
* Take your time to double-check your entries for accuracy.
* Practice regularly to improve your skills in inputting numbers accurately and quickly.
