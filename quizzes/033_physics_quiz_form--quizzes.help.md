# Physics Quiz Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physics Quiz Form aims to evaluate users' grasp of fundamental physics concepts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Begin by reading the introduction, which provides a brief overview of the physics concepts being assessed.
2. Answer the question, "What is the speed of light?" in meters per second.
3. Provide your response to the question in the "Answer" field.
4. Rate your understanding of the concept from 0 to 10 in the "Scoring" field.
5. Optionally, provide the date when you last studied physics.
6. Choose whether you believe Einstein's theory of relativity is correct or not.
7. Select which of the following statements about quantum mechanics are true.
8. If required, provide your contact phone number.
9. Answer whether you think black holes exist.
10. Select which of the following are types of waves.
11. If required, provide your email address.
12. Enter another phone number (if applicable).
