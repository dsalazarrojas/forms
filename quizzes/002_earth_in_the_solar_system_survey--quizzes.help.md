# Earth In The Solar System Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect your knowledge and opinions about the Earth in the Solar System. It is designed to gather your responses to a series of questions and demographic information to help us better understand your views and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the correct answer for each question on the "solar-system-questions" page.
2. Enter your date and time of completion on the "date" page.
3. Enter your email address on the "email" page.
4. Optionally, enter any additional comments or notes on the "note" page.
5. Review your responses before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **solar-system-questions** (`select_one`, required): Choose one of the options provided to answer the question about the Solar System.
	+ This question is designed to gauge your understanding of the Solar System.
* **date** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of completion in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
	+ This field is used to record the date of when you completed the form.
* **time** (`time`, optional): Enter the time of completion in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
	+ This field is used to record the time of when you completed the form.
* **note** (`note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the Solar System.
	+ This field is used to collect any additional thoughts or opinions.
* **email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address.
	+ This field is used to collect your contact information.
* **phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
	+ This field is used to collect your demographic information.
* **earth-science-questions** (`text`, optional): Enter any additional information or answers to other questions not listed.
	+ This field is used to collect any additional comments or information.
