# Hamlet Quiz Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help you create interactive, online quizzes based on the story of Hamlet. The form is a text-based template that will guide you through the process of crafting a quiz about Hamlet, one of Shakespeare's most famous characters. This template is intended for personal or professional use, and you can modify it according to your preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling in each field (1-5) with your answers. Each field is a separate question for your quiz.
2.  Use a descriptive title for your quiz that reflects the theme and content of your quiz.
3.  Review and modify the form as needed to best suit your goals and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **hamlet_1_label** (Required: False, Type: text): This is the first question of your quiz. Use this field to create a question about the story, themes, or characters of Hamlet. Write it in a descriptive and engaging way to capture the reader's interest.
* **hamlet_2_label** (Required: False, Type: text): This is the second question of your quiz. It should be related to the first question and continue the story or themes of Hamlet. Make sure it's clear and concise for easy understanding.
* **hamlet_3_label** (Required: False, Type: text): This is the third question of your quiz. It should be related to the first two questions and add more depth to the story or themes of Hamlet. Use your creativity to create an interesting question.
* **hamlet_4_label** (Required: False, Type: text): This is the fourth question of your quiz. It should be related to the first three questions and continue the story or themes of Hamlet. Be creative and engaging in your writing.
* **hamlet_5_label** (Required: False, Type: text): This is the fifth question of your quiz. It should be related to the first four questions and conclude the story or themes of Hamlet. Summarize your questions in a clear and concise manner.
