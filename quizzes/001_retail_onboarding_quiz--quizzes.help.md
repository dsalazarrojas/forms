## Purpose
This form, "retail_onboarding_quiz," is designed to provide a new retail employee with a comprehensive onboarding process. The form guides the employee through various topics to ensure they understand the retail environment, team collaboration, onboarding process, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start at the first page and carefully read each question.
2.  For select\_one or select\_multiple questions, choose the most appropriate option from the given choices.
3.  For select\_one questions, pick one option, while for select\_multiple, pick one or more options, depending on your understanding.
4.  Be precise and accurate in your responses, as this will provide a clear picture of your onboarding progress.
5.  Complete each page until you reach the final page, "Page 16."
6.  Review your answers before proceeding to the next page to ensure coherence and accuracy.
7.  Use the form as a guide, and if unsure about any question, consult with a colleague or manager for guidance.
