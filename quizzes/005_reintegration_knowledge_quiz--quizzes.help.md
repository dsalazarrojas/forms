# Reintegration Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reintegration Knowledge Quiz is a form designed to assess a participant's knowledge about reintegration, a process of reuniting with a company or organization after a separation or merger.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the quiz questions on **Quiz Questions** page.
2. Select the **Correct Answer** from the provided options on **Correct Answer** page.
3. Fill in your **Participant Name** on **Participant Name** page.
4. Enter your **Participant Email** on **Participant Email** page.
5. Fill in your **Participant Phone** on **Participant Phone** page (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Quiz Questions** (`id: 1`, `type: number`): Answer the questions based on your knowledge and experiences.
* **Correct Answer** (`id: 2`, `type: select_one`): Select the correct answer from the options provided.
* **Participant Name** (`id: 3`, `type: text`): Enter your name accurately (optional).
* **Participant Email** (`id: 4`, `type: email`): Enter your email address accurately (required for communication purposes).
* **Participant Phone** (`id: 5`, `type: text`): Enter your phone number accurately (optional).
