The In Class Quiz form is used to record information about a quiz conducted in class, including whether the quiz was completed, the student's score, and who submitted the quiz.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options for "Completed in Class", "Student Score", "Submitted by", and "Submitter". You can choose one or more options for "Completed in Class" and "Submitted by" as they are multiple-choice fields.
