# Monat Hair Quiz UBB - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Monat Hair Quiz UBB form is designed to collect user information related to their hair. This form is likely used by a hair-related service or product provider to understand users' hair preferences, needs, or concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "quiz_template" field, indicating your interest in taking the quiz.
2. Select "Yes", "No", or "Other" for the "category" field, specifying how you would like to categorize your hair.
3. Select "Yes", "No", or "Other" for the "quizzes" field, indicating your interest in participating in future quizzes or surveys.
4. For the "description" field, provide a brief description of your hair-related needs or concerns.
5. In the "assigned_tool" field, select "chatjimmy" if you would like to use a specific tool or service related to hair.
6. Enter your response in the "form_id" field, which is a text field for you to provide any additional information.
7. Finally, submit the form to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **quiz_template** (`page2`, number, optional): Select "1" to indicate your interest in taking the quiz.
* **category** (`page3`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to categorize your hair ("Yes", "No", "Other").
* **quizzes** (`page4`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" to participate in future quizzes or surveys, or "No" if you are not interested.
* **description** (`page5`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your hair-related needs or concerns.
* **assigned_tool** (`page7`, select_one, optional): Choose "chatjimmy" if you would like to use a specific tool or service related to hair.
* **form_id** (`page8`, text, optional): Enter any additional information related to your hair.

Please note that some fields are marked as optional, which means you can skip them if you don't have information to provide. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
