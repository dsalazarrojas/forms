# Mini Exam Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Mini Exam Quiz" form is a tool for assessing knowledge or opinions on a specific topic. It asks users to provide input in the form of free text responses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the "Mini Exam Quiz" form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the category from the drop-down menu to specify the topic of your response.
2.  Enter your answer to each of the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the category for your quiz.
  - This field is used to determine the topic of your response.
  - Select a category from the drop-down menu.
- **Description** (`description`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your response.
  - This field is used to collect your response to the question.
  - Enter your answer in the text box.
  - Repeat this process for each of the 23 description fields.
  - Each of these fields is used to collect additional information for your response.
  - Enter your answer in the text box.
- **Description 2** (`description_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your response.
  - This field is used to collect additional information for your response.
  - Enter your answer in the text box.
  - Repeat this process for each of the remaining description fields.
  - Each of these fields is used to collect additional information for your response.
  - Enter your answer in the text box.

## Tips
- Please choose the correct category for your response.
- Enter a clear and concise answer for each description field.
- Repeat the process until you have completed all the fields.
