# scientific_writing_skills_quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to test your skills in scientific writing, specifically in creating and categorizing quizzes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information:
	* **Form Title**: Enter a brief title for your quiz.
	* **Form Category**: Select the category for your quiz from the provided options.
	* **Assigned Tool**: Choose the tool you used for this quiz.
	* **Form IDs**: Enter a numeric value representing your quiz's unique ID.
2. (Optional)
	* **Form Description**: This field is not required, but it's a good place to add additional context or details about your quiz.
	* **Output File**: If you've generated an output file, you can provide its name or URL.
3. Click "Submit" to complete the form and submit your quiz for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (1): Enter a title that accurately represents your quiz, such as "Scientific Writing Skills Quiz 1".
* **Form Description** (2): This field is for providing additional context or details about your quiz. If you don't have anything to add, you can leave it blank.
* **Form Category** (3): Select the category for your quiz from the options provided. This helps us understand where your quiz fits into our collection.
* **Assigned Tool** (4): Choose the tool you used to create your quiz from the options provided. This helps us understand your process and workflow.
* **Output File** (5): If you've generated an output file for your quiz, provide its name or URL here. This is optional but helps us review your work.
* **Form IDs** (6): Enter a unique numeric value for your quiz. This helps us keep track of your quiz's identity.
* **Description** (7): This field is not required, but if you have additional comments or feedback, you can add them here.
* **Category** (8): Select the category for your quiz from the options provided. This should match the category you selected in step 3.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct category and assigned tool for your quiz.
* Enter a clear and descriptive title and ID for your quiz.
* If you've generated an output file, make sure to provide its name or URL.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
