# quiz_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The quiz_form is a survey used to collect personal and demographic information from users.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, click on the link to access the form.
2. Fill out the form with your information, one question at a time.
3. Make sure to provide accurate answers, especially for required fields.
4. Once you've completed all questions, review your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **How old are you** (Number, Optional): If applicable, enter your age in whole numbers.
* **How old are you** (Text, Optional): This field is identical to the previous question, possibly for testing purposes.
* **Select an option** (Select One, Optional): Choose one of the three options provided.
* **Select multiple options** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose one or more options from the list.
* **Date of birth** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you were born in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Time of birth** (Time, Optional): Enter the time you were born, not applicable for most users.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone number** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and consistently.
- If you've already filled out this form, ensure it's for a different purpose or user.
