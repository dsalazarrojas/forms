# Laboratory Management Trends Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about laboratory management trends and best practices from laboratory professionals. The form will help gather insights into current laboratory roles, key trends, and challenges in laboratory management, as well as hospital size, type, technology adoption, and other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review each page of the form carefully and answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Select the relevant options for each question, or type in your response for text fields.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk (*)).
* If you are unsure about any question or field, contact the form administrators for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Current Laboratory Roles (Page 1)
* **Current Laboratory Roles** (`current_laboratory_roles`, select_multiple, optional)
  Describe the current state of laboratory roles in your hospital, selecting all that apply:
  - Clinical Laboratory
  - Research Laboratory
  - Testing and Validation Laboratory
  - Other (Please Specify)
