# Discover Your Character Personality - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you discover your character personality by collecting various pieces of information that will allow us to generate a personalized report based on your characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields:
	* First name
	* Last name
	* Email address
	* Date of birth (in the format YYYY)
	* Age
	* Year of birth
	* Month of birth
	* Day of birth
	* Select one type (from the dropdown list)
	* Select multiple types (from the dropdown list)
	* Select one unit (from the dropdown list)
	* Note (optional)
	* Select one time (from the dropdown list)
2. You can leave the non-required fields empty, but please note that they will not affect the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Please fill in your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Last name**: Please fill in your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email address**: Please enter your email address where you would like to receive the report.
* **Phone number**: Please fill in your phone number where you would like to be contacted if needed.
* **Email address**: Please enter your email address where you would like to receive the report.
* **Date of birth**: Please fill in your date of birth in the format YYYY.
* **Select one type**: Please select one type from the list that best describes you.
* **Select multiple types**: Please select all that apply from the list of types that best describe you.
* **Select one unit**: Please select the unit that best represents the height/weight you will input later.
* **Note**: You can write any additional information that would help us generate a more accurate report.
* **Select one time**: Please select the time of the day that best describes your morning routine.
* **Select multiple time**: Please select all the times that best describe your day.
* **Age**: Please fill in your age.
* **Height**: Please fill in your height.
* **Weight**: Please fill in your weight.
* **Height unit**: Please select the unit of your height (cm, in, or ft).
* **Weight unit**: Please select the unit of your weight (kg or lb).
* **Time of birth**: Please select the time of the day you were born.
* **Year of birth**: Please fill in the year you were born.
* **Month of birth**: Please select the month you were born.
* **Day of birth**: Please select the day you were born.
* **Select multiple units**: Please select all the units that apply.
* **Select one unit**: Please select one unit from the list.
* **Note 2**: You can write any additional information that would help us generate a more accurate report.
* **Date**: You can leave this field empty.
* **Date time**: You can leave this field empty.
* **Select one time**: Please select one time from the list.
* **Select multiple time**: You can leave this field empty.

Note: This form will not work properly with incorrect or invalid input. Please make sure to follow the instructions carefully.
