# Physical Quantities Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for collecting basic user information, including personal details and possibly quantities or measurements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. On **Page 1**, enter your **First Name** in the text field provided.
2. On **Page 2**, enter your **Surname** in the text field provided.
3. On **Page 3**, enter your email address in the email field provided. Please ensure the format is in the standard email format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
4. (Optional) On **Page 4**, if relevant, enter any quantity or measurement you wish to share. This field is not required and is for your discretion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, not required): Enter your first name in this field.
- **Surname** (`surname`, text, not required): Enter your surname in this field.
- **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address in this standard format.
- **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, not required): This field is optional and can be used for any quantity or measurement you want to share.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter your email address correctly to avoid submission errors.
- The **Quantity** field is not required, so you can skip it if not applicable.
