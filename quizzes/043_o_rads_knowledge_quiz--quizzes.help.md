# o_rads_knowledge_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information in the "quizzes" category using a simple 5-field form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Read the introduction to understand the context or topic.
2. Select one or more categories (if applicable).
3. Type in the question.
4. Provide an answer.
5. Click the submit button to finalize your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, optional): A short note field for providing context or introduction to the quiz.
* **Category** (`2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more categories from the available options to categorize your quiz.
* **Question** (`3`, `text`, optional): Type in the question to be asked or explored.
* **Answer** (`4`, `text`, optional): Provide your answer or solution to the question.
* **Submit** (`5`, `text`, optional): Click the submit button to finalize and submit your form.

## Tips
Remember, this form is optional and designed for general informational purposes. You can fill it out without any restrictions.
