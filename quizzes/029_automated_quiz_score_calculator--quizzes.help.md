## Purpose
This form is designed to allow participants to complete a quiz and receive their score automatically. It is used for assessing knowledge in various fields such as general knowledge, science, and mathematics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Complete the participant information section**: Fill in your name, last name, email address, and participant ID if applicable.
