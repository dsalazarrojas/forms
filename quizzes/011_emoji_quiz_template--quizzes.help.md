# Emoji Quiz Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you create an Emoji Quiz Template, a fun and interactive way to share your knowledge or create a quiz for your audience. You can add your own emojis, customize the appearance, and share it with others.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, fill out the required fields and select your preferred options from the dropdown menus. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **create an emoji round** (text, required): Enter a title for your emoji round.
* **add your own emojis** (select multiple, optional): Choose up to 3 emojis to add to your quiz.
* **customize** (text, required): Enter a custom title or description for your quiz.
* **description** (text, required): Add a description for your quiz.
* **round name** (text, required): Enter a name for your round.
* **Share on** (select one, optional): Choose whether to share your quiz on email or social media.
* **share with** (select one, optional): Decide who to share your quiz with, either friends or public.
* **share link** (text, optional): Enter a URL to share your quiz.
* **quiz title** (text, required): Set a title for your quiz.
* **description (2)** (text, optional): Add another description for your quiz.
* **categories** (select multiple, optional): Select categories for your quiz, if applicable.
* **tags** (text, optional): Enter tags for your quiz, if applicable.
* **custom fields** (text, optional): Add custom fields for your quiz, if applicable.
* **custom links** (text, optional): Add custom links for your quiz, if applicable.
* **Share With 2** (select one, optional): Choose whether to share your quiz with friends or public.
* **Share Link 2** (text, optional): Enter a URL to share your quiz.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter an email address.
* **password** (text, optional): Enter a password.
* **phone** (text, optional): Enter a phone number.
* **date and time** (date, required): Select a date and time for your quiz.
* **time zone** (select one, optional): Choose the time zone for your quiz.
* **location** (text, optional): Enter a location for your quiz.
* **time** (time, optional): Enter a time for your quiz.
* **note** (note, optional): Add a note for your quiz.
* **note 2** (note, optional): Add another note for your quiz.

Note: Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
