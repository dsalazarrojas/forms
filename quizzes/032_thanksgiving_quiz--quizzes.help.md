# thanksgiving_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect users' preferences and experiences on Thanksgiving.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question according to your personal preferences and experiences.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select one of the options that best fits your answer.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, but note that most fields are optional.
4. Once you've completed the form, click the submit button to submit your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What's your favorite part of Thanksgiving?** (`favorite_part`, text, optional): This question asks about your favorite part of the holiday. Feel free to express your love for family, friends, food, or any other aspect of the holiday.
* **How much time do you spend with family on Thanksgiving?** (`time_spent_with_family`, number, optional): Answer in hours, minutes, or a general idea of how much quality time you spend with loved ones.
* **What's your favorite food to eat on Thanksgiving?** (`favorite_food`, text, optional): Share your go-to dish or dessert that you look forward to on this special day.
* **How old were you on your last Thanksgiving?** (`age_on_last_thanksgiving`, number, optional): Reflect on how the years have passed since your last Thanksgiving celebration.
* **What's your favorite holiday song?** (`favorite_holiday_song`, text, optional): Sing your favorite tune that gets you in the holiday spirit.
* **What's your favorite movie to watch on Thanksgiving?** (`favorite_movie`, text, optional): Share your favorite film to cozy up with on this holiday.
* **Do you celebrate Thanksgiving with a big holiday dinner?** (`big_dinner`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you enjoy a grand feast or keep things simple.
* **What's your favorite thing to do on Thanksgiving morning?** (`morning_activity`, text, optional): Get into the holiday mood with this question.
* **How much money do you typically spend on Thanksgiving?** (`money_spent`, number, optional): Reflect on your spending habits during the holiday.
* **What's your favorite holiday activity?** (`holiday_activity`, text, optional): Share your favorite activity that brings you joy during this time of year.
* **What's a memorable experience you've had on Thanksgiving?** (`something_made`, text, optional): Think back to a special moment that stands out.
* **What's your favorite holiday movie?** (`favorite_movie_2`, text, optional): Another chance to recommend a film that's a holiday classic.

## Tips

* Take your time to think about your answers before submitting the form.
* Feel free to share as much or as little as you'd like about your preferences.
* If you have any questions or concerns, you can contact us for assistance.

Note: This help guide is a general guide and may be subject to minor updates based on form changes.
