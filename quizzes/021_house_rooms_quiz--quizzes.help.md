# House Rooms Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The House Rooms Quiz form is designed to collect specific information about a house room, including its size, shape, orientation, and features.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Answer each question accurately and truthfully.
3. Complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name.
* **What type of room** (`room_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of room from the list: Living room, Bedroom, Kitchen, Bathroom, or Hallway.
* **Number of rooms** (`number_of_rooms`, `number`, required): Enter the number of rooms.
* **Room layout** (`room_layout`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the room's layout from the list: Open, Closed, L-shaped, or U-shaped.
* **What features does the room have?** (`room_features`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the features from the list: Window, Door, TV, Bed, or Desk.
* **How do you use the room?** (`room_use`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the room's purpose from the list: Living room, Sleeping area, Storage area, or Other.
* **How big is the room?** (`room_size`, `number`, required): Enter the room's size.
* **Room shape** (`room_shape`, `select_one`, required): Choose the room's shape from the list: Square, Rectangle, L-shape, or Other.
* **How tall is the room?** (`room_height`, `number`, required): Enter the room's height.
* **How wide is the room?** (`room_width`, `number`, required): Enter the room's width.
* **How long is the room?** (`room_length`, `number`, required): Enter the room's length.
* **How deep is the room?** (`room_depth`, `number`, required): Enter the room's depth.
* **Room height unit** (`room_height_units`, `select_one`, required): Choose the unit of measurement for the room's height from the list: Feet, Meters, or Inches.
* **Room width unit** (`room_width_units`, `select_one`, required): Choose the unit of measurement for the room's width from the list: Feet, Meters, or Inches.
* **Room length unit** (`room_length_units`, `select_one`, required): Choose the unit of measurement for the room's length from the list: Feet, Meters, or Inches.
* **Room depth unit** (`room_depth_units`, `select_one`, required): Choose the unit of measurement for the room's depth from the list: Feet, Meters, or Inches.
* **Room orientation** (`room_orientation`, `select_one`, required): Choose the room's orientation from the list: North-facing, South-facing, East-facing, or West-facing.
* **Room view** (`room_view`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the room's view from the list: View, No View, or Partial View.
* **Room illumination** (`room_illum`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the room's illumination from the list: Light, Dark, or Dim.
* **Room smell** (`room_smell`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the room's smell from the list: Pleasant, Unpleasant, or Neutral.
* **Room taste** (`room_taste`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the room's taste from the list: Pleasant, Unpleasant, or Neutral.
* **Room touch** (`room_touch`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the room's touch from the list: Pleasant, Unpleasant, or Neutral.
* **Room sound** (`room_sound`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the room's sound from the list: Pleasant, Unpleasant, or Neutral.
* **Room feel** (`room_feel`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the room's feel from the list: Pleasant, Unpleasant, or Neutral.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `note`, optional): Click the submit button to complete and submit the form.

## Tips
* Ensure you provide accurate and truthful information to ensure the form's data is reliable.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please do not hesitate to seek additional information or clarification.
* If you complete the form, please review it carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are accurate and complete.
* If you need help or have questions, do not hesitate to contact us.
