# product_recommendation_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about users' preferences and demographics to provide personalized product recommendations in the context of beauty products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each page and answer the questions accordingly.
2. For pages with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. For pages with single options, select the most accurate answer.
4. Make sure to review and correct any errors before proceeding to the next page.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (text, required: false): This is the first page of the form. Please introduce yourself and provide any additional information that may be helpful for our recommendations.
* **Age** (number, required: false): Please enter your age to help us understand your demographic.
* **Interests** (select_one, required: false): Do you have any interests? (Yes/No)
* **Favorite Beauty Products** (select_multiple, required: false): What are your favorite beauty products? (eyeshadow, mascara, foundation, lip gloss, serum)
* **What Products Do You Like** (select_multiple, required: false): What type of products do you like? (skincare, haircare, makeup, fragrance)
* **Skin Tone** (select_one, required: false): What is your skin tone? (fair, medium, olive, tanned, very dark, light)
* **Hair Color** (select_one, required: false): What is your hair color? (blonde, brown, black, red, gray)
* **Quiz Preferences** (note, required: false): Please note any additional information or preferences that may be helpful for our recommendations.
* **Results** (text, required: false): This is where you will find your personalized product recommendations.
* **Recommendations** (select_one, required: false): Do you want to receive recommendations? (Yes/No)

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, please select "No" or "Unknown".
* Review and correct any errors before proceeding to the next page.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact us for assistance.
