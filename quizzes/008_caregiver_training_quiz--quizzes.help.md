# Caregiver Training Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the knowledge and understanding of caregivers on key concepts such as patient safety, medication management, ethics, and documentation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions honestly and accurately based on your understanding of the concepts.
2. Select the best answer for each question from the provided options.
3. For open-ended questions, provide clear and concise answers in the space provided.
4. Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Enter your employee ID if applicable.
* **Date of Quiz** (`date_of_quiz`, date, required): Select the date you completed this quiz.
* **What is the first step in fall prevention for a new client?** (`quiz_q1`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer from the options provided.
* **How often should a bed-bound patient be repositioned?** (`quiz_q2`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer from the options provided.
* **Which of these is a sign of infection to report immediately?** (`quiz_q3`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer from the options provided.
* **What must be checked before administering medication?** (`quiz_q4`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer from the options provided.
* **True or False - Caregivers can administer non-prescribed OTC medication without approval?** (`quiz_q5`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer from the options provided.
* **When should an incident report be completed?** (`quiz_q6`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer from the options provided.
* **Which of these is a violation of patient confidentiality?** (`quiz_q7`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer from the options provided.
* **Define Person-Centered Care** (`quiz_q8`, text, required): Provide a clear and concise definition of Person-Centered Care in your own words.
* **How would you rate the clarity of the training material?** (`quiz_feedback`, number, required): Rate the clarity of the training material on a scale of 1-5.
* **What topics do you need more training on?** (`training_suggestions`, text, optional): Provide suggestions for future educational needs.
* **I certify that I completed this quiz independently** (`participant_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer from the options provided.
* **Electronic Signature** (`signature_confirmation`, text, required): Type your full name as an electronic signature.

Please take the quiz seriously and honestly, and make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
