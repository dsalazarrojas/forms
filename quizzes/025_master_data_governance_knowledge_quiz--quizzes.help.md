# master_data_governance_knowledge_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The master_data_governance_knowledge_quiz form is designed for knowledge assessments of data governance practices within an organization. It helps evaluate an individual's understanding of data governance principles, challenges, and best practices. This form is typically used during onboarding, training, or internal knowledge checks.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Begin by answering the questions and selecting the best options for each field.
2. Ensure you fill in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. For fields with multiple options, select all that apply.
4. If there are any additional comments, use the "Additional Comments" field to provide further context.
5. Complete the form by entering the submission details: email address and phone number.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `text`, required): This field is meant to be a brief summary of your understanding of data governance practices within the organization.
### question_1
* **What Is Data Governance** (`What Is Data Governance`, `text`, optional): This field is intended to gather your understanding of data governance principles. Please provide a concise answer.
### governance_challenges
* **Organizational Challenges** (`Organizational Challenges`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the options that best describes the main challenges your organization faces in implementing data governance practices.
	+ Option 1: Data governance is not clearly defined or understood within the organization.
	+ Option 2: Data governance is not well supported by leadership.
	+ Option 3: Data governance is not well communicated to stakeholders.
### training_support
* **Training or Internal Reviews** (`Training or Internal Reviews`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all options that apply to how your organization supports data governance practices.
	+ Option 1: Regular training sessions are provided.
	+ Option 2: Internal reviews are conducted.
	+ Option 3: Online resources are available.
	+ Option 4: Other (please specify in the "Additional Comments" field).
### submission_tracking
* **Centralized Form Submission Tracking** (`Centralized Form Submission Tracking`, `text`, optional): This field is intended to gather information about how your organization tracks submissions. Please provide a brief answer.
### additional_comments
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any further comments or suggestions about data governance practices within your organization, please use this field to provide them.
### submission_date
* **Submission Date** (`Submission Date`, `date`, optional): If you need to provide a submission date for the assessment, you can enter it in this field. This will be used for tracking purposes.
### submission_time
* **Submission Time** (`Submission Time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of submission if you need to provide it.
### submission_email
* **Submission Email** (`Submission Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you about the submission.
### submission_phone
* **Submission Phone** (`Submission Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number to enable us to contact you about the submission.
### submission_notes
* **Submission Notes** (`Submission Notes`, `note`, optional): Use this field to include any additional information about your submission.
## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us for assistance.
