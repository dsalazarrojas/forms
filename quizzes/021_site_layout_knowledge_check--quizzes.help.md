# Site Layout Knowledge Check - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Site Layout Knowledge Check form is designed to evaluate your understanding of web layout principles, ensuring that you can create websites that are user-friendly, accessible, and visually appealing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and consider your knowledge of web layout design principles.
2. Select the correct answers based on your understanding of the topics.
3. Fill in all the required fields (indicated as "required" in the form).
4. For multiple-select fields, choose all applicable options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page** (`first_page`, `text`, optional): This field is where you get to start your website's visual journey. Think of the main entrance of your website, and what you want users to see first.
* **Second Page** (`second_page`, `text`, optional): Consider the next logical step in your website's composition. How do you continue the flow of information from the first page?
* **Layout Elements** (`layout_elements`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all the layout elements that you would typically find in a standard website, such as a header, footer, and content area.
* **Navigation** (`navigation`, `select_one`, optional): Determine which type of navigation best suits your website's structure: top, left, right, or bottom.
* **Layout Types** (`layout_types`, `select_multiple`, optional): Decide which layout types are suitable for your website: fixed, fluid, responsive, or grid.
* **Page Size** (`page_size`, `number`, optional): Consider the size of the content area, where you can select a numerical value for its width.
* **Content Width** (`content_width`, `number`, optional): Similarly, decide on the width of the content area, again selecting a numerical value.

## Tips
* Be honest about your knowledge. This form is not a test, but rather an opportunity for you to learn and showcase your understanding.
* If you're unsure about a particular question, use your best judgment based on your prior knowledge or experience.
* Take your time, and don't rush through the form.
