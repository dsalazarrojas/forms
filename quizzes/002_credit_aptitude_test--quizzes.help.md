# Credit Aptitude Test - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Credit Aptitude Test is designed to evaluate your knowledge on various aspects of credit, including credit instruments, risk, scoring, and the purpose of scoring.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and choose the correct option for each select_one or select_multiple question.
2. For text fields, enter a clear and concise answer to the question.
3. Make sure to answer all questions that are marked as required.
4. If you're unsure or don't know the answer to a question, you can leave it blank, but be aware that it may affect the overall score.

* **What is the primary function of a credit instrument?** (`question1`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question asks about the main purpose of a credit instrument. Choose the option that best describes its primary function.
