# Spain Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spain Knowledge Quiz is a series of interactive questions designed to test your knowledge about Spain, covering its geography, culture, history, and more. This form is part of an educational exercise aimed at assessing your understanding of various aspects of Spain.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the options that best answer each question.
2. Choose all that apply for multi-select questions (e.g., select_multiple).
3. For text fields, provide a brief answer to the question.
4. Ensure you complete all required fields, indicated by a * in the label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Spain Map Quiz 1** (select_multiple, required: false): This question tests your knowledge of Spain's geographical regions. Select all the cities that you believe are in Spain.
  * Madrid
  * Barcelona
  * Valencia
  * Seville
* **Spanish Culture Quiz 1** (text, required: false): What is an essential part of Spanish culture?
  * Please provide a brief answer, e.g., "Flamenco Dance" or "Tapas".
* **Spanish Tradition 1** (select_one, required: false): What is a significant Spanish tradition?
  * Choose one of the options: Flamenco Dance, Tapas, or Football.
* **Spanish History 1** (number, required: false): What is the approximate year of a significant event in Spanish history?
  * Enter a numerical answer.
* **Spanish Language 1** (select_multiple, required: false): Which languages are spoken in Spain?
  * Select all that apply: Spanish, Basque, or Catalan.
* **Spanish Food 1** (select_multiple, required: false): What are some essential Spanish dishes or cuisines?
  * Select all that apply: Tapas, Paella, or Gazpacho.
* **Spanish Tradition 2** (select_one, required: false): What is another significant Spanish tradition?
  * Choose one of the options: Flamenco Dance, Tapas, or Football.
* **Spanish Culture 2** (select_multiple, required: false): What is an essential part of Spanish culture?
  * Select all that apply: Fiestas, Salsa, or Castillianos.
* **Spanish Culture 3** (text, required: false): What is another important aspect of Spanish culture?
  * Please provide a brief answer.
* **Spanish Language 2** (select_multiple, required: false): Which languages are spoken in Spain?
  * Select all that apply: Spanish, Basque.

## Tips
- Take your time, as there is no time limit.
- If you're unsure about any question, you can research or seek assistance.
- This form is designed to be educational, so don't worry about making mistakes – it's about learning.
