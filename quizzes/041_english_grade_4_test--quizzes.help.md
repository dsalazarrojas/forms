# English Grade 4 Test - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from students for an English Grade 4 test. The test assesses students' language skills and knowledge of general knowledge questions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct option for each question.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and honest information.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting.
4. Submit your form once you have completed all required fields.
