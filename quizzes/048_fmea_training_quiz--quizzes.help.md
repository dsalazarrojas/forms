# FMEA Training Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The FMEA Training Quiz is designed to test your knowledge of FMEA principles and procedures. This quiz will help you gauge your understanding of effective ways to identify potential hazards and assess your performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each question carefully to determine the correct answers.
2. Select the correct answers for each question.
3. Ensure you have filled out all required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
4. Submit your answers to receive feedback on your performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **FMEA Session** (`fmea_session`, text, required: false): This field allows you to describe the FMEA session you are participating in, including the date and time it took place.
* **Which of the following is an effective way to identify a potential hazard** (`questions`, select_multiple, required: false): This field presents multiple-choice options for identifying potential hazards. Read each option carefully and select all the correct answers.
* **Answer 1** (`correct_answer_1`, select_one, required: false): Choose the correct answer from the options provided. 
* **Answer 2** (`correct_answer_2`, select_one, required: false): Choose the correct answer from the options provided.
* **Answer 3** (`correct_answer_3`, select_one, required: false): Choose the correct answer from the options provided.
* **Date of FMEA Session** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the FMEA session you participated in.
* **Time of FMEA Session** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the FMEA session you participated in. 

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly to receive an accurate assessment of your knowledge.
* Review the FMEA concepts and terminology before attempting the quiz to maximize your understanding.
* Ensure you fill out all required fields to avoid incomplete submissions.
