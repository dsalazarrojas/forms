# Electrochemistry Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Electrochemistry Knowledge Quiz is designed to assess your knowledge and understanding of electrochemical processes and their applications. It is intended to help you evaluate your comprehension of the subject.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant details section with your name, email, and phone number.
2. Answer the three multiple-choice questions about electrochemistry.
   - Select the correct answer for "What is the primary process that occurs in electrochemistry?" from the options provided.
   - Choose all applicable answers for "How is electrochemistry applied in the manufacture of steel?"
   - Note your answer for "What is the most important aspect of electrochemistry?"
3. Fill in the submission date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Information (1)**: This is the title of the form, which is not required to be filled in.
* **participant_details (2)**: Enter your name, email, and phone number in this section.
  - **Name (6)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
  - **Email (7)**: Enter a valid email address.
  - **Phone (8)**: Enter your phone number.
* **What is the primary process that occurs in electrochemistry? (3)**: Choose one of the four options to answer this question.
* **How is electrochemistry applied in the manufacture of steel? (4)**: Choose all applicable answers from the four options provided.
* **What is the most important aspect of electrochemistry? (5)**: This is a note field where you can provide a brief answer to this question.
* **Submission Date (9)**: Enter the date on which you are submitting the form.
* **Submission Time (10)**: Enter the time at which you are submitting the form.
