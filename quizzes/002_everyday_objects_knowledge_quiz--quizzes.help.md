# everyday_objects_knowledge_quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The everyday objects knowledge quiz form is designed to assess users' knowledge about everyday objects. It is used to collect information on various aspects related to objects, such as their properties, characteristics, and usage. This form helps in gathering data to improve the understanding of everyday objects and their impact on our lives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select all the necessary questions and answers on each page according to your knowledge about the everyday objects.
2. Fill in the answers for each question, making sure to provide detailed and accurate information.
3. Enter the correct category and tag for each object, if applicable.
4. Provide a score for each object, if relevant.
5. Enter your name and email address for identification purposes.
6. Optionally, you can add a note or comments about each object.
7. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the questions.
