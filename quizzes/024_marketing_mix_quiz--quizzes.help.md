# Marketing Mix Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Mix Quiz is a tool to evaluate and improve your marketing strategy by identifying areas of strength and weakness in your marketing efforts. The quiz assesses your company's marketing activities across 7 different categories: Product, Price, Place, Promotion, People, and Process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the options that best describe your company's current marketing mix.
2. Answer each question truthfully, without overthinking or second-guessing your responses.
3. Submit your answers and review the results to identify areas where you can improve your marketing strategy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product (Product)** (`product`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of emphasis placed on the quality and features of your product or service. This includes factors such as product design, functionality, and quality control.
* **Price (Price)** (`price`, `number`, required): Indicate the price level of your product or service, with higher values indicating higher prices.
* **Place (Place)** (`place`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you sell and distribute your product or service, with options such as online, retail, or direct-to-consumer.
* **Promotion (Promotional)** (`promotion`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of promotional efforts you use to reach your customers, such as social media, advertising, or trade shows.
* **People (Customer Service)** (`customer_service`, `select_one`, required): Choose the level of customer service you provide, with options such as personalized support, automated support, or self-service options.
* **Customer Service Level (Customer Service Level)** (`customer_service_level`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate the level of service you aim to provide, with options such as high, medium, or low.
* **Service (Service)** (`service`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of focus on after-sales services, with options such as support, maintenance, or upgrades.
* **Customer Communication (Customer Communication)** (`customer_communication`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the types of communication you use with your customers, such as email, phone, or text message.
* **Product Communication (Product Communication)** (`product_communication`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the ways you communicate the features and benefits of your product, with options such as brochures, videos, or social media.
* **Customer Experience (Customer Experience)** (`customer_experience`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate the level of focus on customer experience, with options such as ease of use, enjoyment, or satisfaction.
* **Product Experience (Product Experience)** (`product_experience`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the level of focus on product experience, with options such as quality, functionality, or aesthetics.
* **Product Development (Product Development)** (`product_development`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate the level of focus on product development, with options such as innovation, quality, or cost-effectiveness.
* **Sales (Sales)** (`sales`, `select_one`, required): Choose the level of focus on sales channels, with options such as online, retail, or direct-to-consumer.
* **Place of Purchase (Place of Purchase)** (`place_of_purchase`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the places where you sell your product or service, with options such as online, retail, or in-store.
* **Sales Channel (Sales Channel)** (`sales_channel`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate the level of focus on sales channels, with options such as online, retail, or direct-to-consumer.
* **Customer Satisfaction (Customer Satisfaction)** (`customer_satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate the level of customer satisfaction, with options such as Satisfied, Dissatisfied, or Neutral.
* **Customer Commitment (Customer Commitment)** (`customer_commitment`, `select_one`, required): Choose the level of customer commitment, with options such as Yes or No.
