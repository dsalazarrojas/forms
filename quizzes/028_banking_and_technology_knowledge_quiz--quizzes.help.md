# Banking And Technology Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate your knowledge on various banking and technology-related topics. It contains 15 questions that cover different aspects of banking technology, from APIs and open banking to mobile banking and real-time payments. The quiz is intended to be a helpful tool for participants to gauge their understanding of these concepts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your participant name and email address on page 1. These are required fields and will be used to store your information for the purpose of the quiz.
2. Answer each question with the best option from the provided choices. For multiple-choice questions, select only one option.
3. For questions with multiple answers, select all that apply.
4. Once you have completed the form, review your answers and provide any additional comments or feedback you might have.
