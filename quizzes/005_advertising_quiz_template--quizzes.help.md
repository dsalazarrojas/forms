# Advertising Quiz Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This template is designed to assess an individual's knowledge on advertising-related concepts.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in your name and email address in the first section of the form.
2. Select the correct answers for the advertising-related questions that follow.
3. Choose how you would rate your advertising knowledge and select the topics you're interested in learning more about.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Participant Info --**
	+ This is a section for providing your basic contact information.
	+ You will need to enter your full name and email address to receive your quiz results.
* **Your Name** (Required)
	+ This field is for your personal name.
* **Email Address** (Required)
	+ This field is where you'll enter your email address to receive your quiz results.
* **-- Advertising Knowledge Quiz --**
	+ This is a section that tests your knowledge on various advertising-related concepts.
* **What does CTR stand for?** (Required)
	+ This question tests your understanding of a key advertising metric.
* **Which of the following is a form of paid media?** (Required)
	+ This question checks your knowledge on different types of paid advertising.
* **What is A/B testing?** (Required)
	+ This question evaluates your understanding of a crucial advertising strategy.
* **What metric best measures brand awareness?** (Required)
	+ This question assesses your knowledge on how to measure advertising success.
* **Which platform is best known for B2B advertising?** (Required)
	+ This question tests your understanding of different advertising platforms.
* **-- Additional Information --**
	+ This section is optional and allows you to self-assess your advertising knowledge and indicate your interests for further learning.
* **How would you rate your advertising knowledge?** (Optional)
	+ This question allows you to self-assess your level of knowledge on advertising.
* **What topics do you want to learn more about?** (Optional)
	+ Select all the topics you're interested in learning more about, such as social media ads, Google ads, or programmatic advertising.
