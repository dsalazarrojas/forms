# Video Game Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Video Game Quiz" form is designed to gather information about your gaming preferences and habits. Please answer the following questions to help us better understand what you like about video games.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Submit" button when you have completed all the fields.
2. Fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.
3. If you need help with any field, you can use the hint or ask for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Game Name** (`game_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your favorite video game or a game that interests you. This can be a recent game you've played or an old classic.
* **Game Platform** (`game_platform`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you played the game on a:
	+ Yes: Yes
	+ No: No
* **Game Genre** (`game_genre`, select_multiple, optional): Select the genre(s) of your favorite game(s). Choose from:
	+ Adventure
	+ Action
	+ Sports
	+ Puzzle
	+ Role-Playing
* **Game Release Date** (`game_release_date`, date, optional): Enter the release date of your favorite game or the date you first played it.
* **Game Price** (`game_price`, number, optional): If you remember the price of your favorite game, please enter it. This can help us understand the value of the game to you.
* **Player Gender** (`player_gender`, select_one, optional): Indicate your gender:
	+ Male
	+ Female
* **Player Age** (`player_age`, number, optional): Enter your age to help us better understand your demographics.
* **Player Interest** (`player_interest`, select_multiple, optional): Select your interests from:
	+ Sports
	+ Music
	+ Technology
	+ Arts
	+ Hobbies
* **Player Location** (`player_location`, select_one, optional): Indicate your location:
	+ Urban
	+ Rural
	+ Suburban
* **Favorite Game** (`player_favorite_game`, text, optional): Enter the name of your all-time favorite game.
* **Favorite Console** (`player_favorite_console`, text, optional): If you have a favorite gaming console, enter its name.
* **Favorite Genre** (`player_favorite_genre`, text, optional): If you have a favorite genre, enter its name.
