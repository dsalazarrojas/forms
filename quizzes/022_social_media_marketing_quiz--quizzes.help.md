# Social Media Marketing Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Social Media Marketing Quiz is designed to assess your knowledge and experience in social media marketing. This quiz is intended to help evaluate your skills and knowledge in various aspects of social media marketing, including marketing channels, platforms, and budget management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the quiz, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the respective fields.
2. Select your preferred Social Media Platforms that you are familiar with.
3. Enter your Years Experience in social media marketing.
4. Choose your Job Title.
5. Select your Industry (e.g., Technology, Marketing, Finance, etc.).
6. Enter your Company Name (if applicable).
7. Provide your Location.
8. Select your Timezone Offset (if applicable).
9. Select your Marketing Channels (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, etc.).
10. Enter your Marketing Budget (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address that will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Birthday** (date, optional): Enter your birthday (optional).
* **Social Media Platforms** (select_multiple, required): Select all the social media platforms you are familiar with (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, etc.).
* **Years Experience** (number, optional): Enter your years of experience in social media marketing (optional).
* **Job Title** (text, optional): Enter your job title (optional).
* **Industry** (select_one, required): Select your industry (e.g., Technology, Marketing, Finance, etc.).
* **Company Name** (text, optional): Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Location** (text, optional): Enter your location.
* **Timezone** (text, optional): Enter your timezone.
* **Timezone Offset** (time, optional): Enter your timezone offset (if applicable).
* **Marketing Channels** (select_multiple, required): Select all the marketing channels you are familiar with (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, etc.).
* **Marketing Budget** (number, optional): Enter your marketing budget (if applicable).
