# sensitivity_toward_cultural_and_ethnic_differences_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess an individual's sensitivity toward cultural and ethnic differences. It is a self-assessment tool that helps gauge their awareness, understanding, and skills in this area.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each section honestly and thoroughly. You can select from the provided options or provide a brief explanation for each question. Do not leave any sections blank unless explicitly allowed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, note, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please read through it to understand the purpose and context of the assessment.
* **Demographic Information** (`demographic_information`, number, required: false): This section collects demographic information about you. Please select or enter your relevant demographic data.
* **Cultural Background** (`cultural_background`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all the cultural backgrounds that apply to you.
* **Ethnic Groups Awareness** (`ethnic_groups_awareness`, number, required: false): This section asks about your awareness of different ethnic groups. Please select or enter your relevant experiences or knowledge.
* **Cultural Fusion** (`cultural_fusion`, text, required: false): This is an open-ended section where you can describe your experiences or thoughts about cultural fusion.
* **Cultural Values** (`cultural_values`, select_one, required: false): Please select which cultural values are important to you.
* **Cultural Contextualization** (`cultural_contextualization`, select_multiple, required: false): This section asks about your ability to understand cultural differences in context. Please select all the options that apply.
* **Diversity and Inclusion** (`diversity_and_inclusion`, number, required: false): This section assesses your understanding of diversity and inclusion. Please select or enter your relevant experiences or knowledge.
* **Sensitivity Training** (`sensitivity_training`, select_multiple, required: false): This section asks about your experiences with sensitivity training. Please select all the options that apply.

## Tips
- Answer honestly and thoroughly.
- Select all applicable options for multiple-choice questions.
- Enter relevant demographic data.
- Be as detailed as possible in open-ended sections.
- This form is not a definitive assessment tool; results should be interpreted with caution.
