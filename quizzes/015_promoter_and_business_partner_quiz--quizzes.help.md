# promoter_and_business_partner_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to educate users about the concept of a promoter and a business partner, including how they earn money, the promotion process, and the benefits of being a promoter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Read through each section carefully, and answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Use the space provided on each page to write your answers in as much detail as possible.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the Quiz**: This page provides an introduction to the topic of promoters and business partners.
* **What is a promoter?** (`promoter_description`): This field is for you to describe what you think a promoter is.
* **How do promoters earn money?** (`earn_money`): In this field, write how promoters earn money.
* **What is a business partner?** (`business_partner`): Write your understanding of what a business partner is.
* **What is the promotion process?** (`promotion_process`): Explain the process of promotion.
* **How do you earn money as a promoter?** (`earning_process`): Describe how you, as a promoter, earn money.
* **What are the benefits of being a promoter?** (`benefits`): List the benefits of being a promoter.
* **What are the requirements to be a promoter?** (`requirements`): Write the requirements to be a promoter.
* **Conclusion** (`conclusion`): Summarize your understanding of the topic.
* **Do you have any questions?** (`questions`): If you have any questions, write them here.
* **Thanks for taking the Quiz** (`thanks`): This field is a confirmation that you have completed the form.
* **Disclaimer** (`disclaimer`): This field is for any additional comments or information you wish to provide.

## Tips
* Please answer each question in as much detail as possible.
* Use your best effort to provide accurate and clear answers.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask.
