# Mineral Science Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your knowledge and practices in the field of mineral science. It covers various aspects, including your background, approaches to teaching, and engagement with students and the community.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Use the available options provided for select_multiple questions.
3. Do not skip any questions, as they are all required.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, use the "additional_comments" field at the end of the form.
