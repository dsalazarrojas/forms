# Exercise Metabolism Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps you estimate your basal metabolic rate (BMR) and understand your energy expenditure based on your exercise habits, fitness goals, and other relevant health and lifestyle factors.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select your primary exercise type from the list: Endurance, HIIT (High-Intensity Interval Training), Strength, Flexibility, or Balance.
2. Enter the average number of days you exercise per week.
3. Choose your age group: Adult, Child, or Elderly.
4. Select your fitness goal: Weight loss, Weight gain, Strength, Endurance, Flexibility, Balance, or Other.
5. (Optional) Enter your body fat percentage range (note that this field is optional).
6. If you have any of the listed health conditions, select them. (You can select multiple conditions if applicable).
7. Enter your resting heart rate.
8. Indicate whether you smoke or do not.
9. Choose your current activity level from Low, Medium, or High.
10. (Optional) Enter the average number of hours you exercise per week (note that this field is optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your primary exercise type?** (Endurance, HIIT, Strength, Flexibility, Balance)
	+ This question helps us understand your exercise routine and intensity.
* **On average, how many days do you exercise per week?**
	+ Please enter a whole number (e.g., 2, 3, 4, etc.).
* **What is your age group?** (Adult, Child, Elderly)
	+ Select the age group that best describes you.
* **What is your fitness goal?** (Weight loss, Weight gain, Strength, Endurance, Flexibility, Balance, Other)
	+ Choose the goal that best aligns with your objectives.
* **What is your body fat percentage range?** (Optional)
	+ If you know your body fat percentage, please enter it. Note that this field is optional and may not be applicable for everyone.
* **Do you have any health conditions?** (Diabetes, Hypertension, Heart condition, Respiratory condition, Other)
	+ Select all the health conditions that apply to you.
* **What is your resting heart rate?** (Please enter in beats per minute, e.g., 60-80 bpm)
	+ This will help us estimate your energy expenditure.
* **Do you smoke?** (True, False)
	+ Indicate whether you smoke or not.
* **How would you describe your current activity level?** (Low, Medium, High)
	+ Choose the activity level that best describes your daily routine.
* **On average, how many hours do you exercise per week?** (Optional)
	+ If you track your exercise hours, please enter them. Note that this field is optional and may not be applicable for everyone.
