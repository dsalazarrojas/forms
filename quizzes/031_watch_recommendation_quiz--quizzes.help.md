# Watch Recommendation Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you find the perfect watch that suits your style and needs. Please answer the following questions, and we'll provide you with personalized watch recommendations based on your preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and select the option that best describes your preferences.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).
3. You can select multiple options for questions that allow it.
4. If you have any additional features in mind, you can select them in the "Additional Features" field.
5. Once you've completed the form, click the "Submit" button to receive your watch recommendations.

* **What is your budget range?** (`budget`, select_one): *Select your budget range to help us narrow down the watch options within your budget.*

* **For which occasion do you want to wear the watch?** (`occasion`, select_one): *Please select the occasion where you'll be wearing the watch most often: Formal, Casual, or Special.*

* **Which watch brand do you like?** (`brand`, select_multiple): *Choose the watch brands you're interested in, such as Rolex, Omega, or Patek.*
