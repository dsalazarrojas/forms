# LLM Hallucinations Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The LLM Hallucinations Quiz is designed to gauge your level of awareness and understanding of LLM hallucinations. This quiz will guide you through a series of questions and prompts to assess your ability to recognize and identify LLM errors or inaccuracies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this quiz, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first name.
2. Fill in your last name.
3. Enter your email address (if you have one).
4. Choose one of the options for "answer_one" and select the best answer.
5. Choose one of the options for "answer_two".
6. Select multiple options for "answer_three" (up to 3 options).
7. Enter the date of last occurrence (not required).
8. Enter the time of last occurrence (not required).
9. Write any additional notes or comments (not required).
10. Enter your phone number (not required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`user_input_1`, text, optional): This field is for your first name. Please enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Last name** (`user_input_2`, text, optional): This field is for your last name. Please enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email** (`email_input`, email, optional): This field is for your email address. Please enter a valid email address if you have one.
* **Score One** (`score_1`, number, optional): This field is not clearly labeled, but it appears to be a field for scoring or rating. Please enter a number as you see fit.
* **Score Two** (`score_2`, number, optional): Another scoring field similar to "Score One". Please enter a number as you see fit.
* **Answer One** (`select_one_1`, select one, optional): This field allows you to choose one option from the provided list.
* **Answer Two** (`select_one_2`, select one, optional): This field also allows you to choose one option from the provided list.
* **Answer Three** (`select_multiple_1`, select multiple, optional): This field allows you to choose multiple options from the provided list.
* **Date** (`date_input`, date, optional): This field is for the date of last occurrence. Please enter a date in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Time** (`time_input`, time, optional): This field is for the time of last occurrence. Please enter a time in the correct format (HH:MM).
* **Note** (`note_input`, note, optional): This field is for any additional comments or notes you may have. Please feel free to add any information you think is relevant.
* **Phone** (`phone_input`, text, optional): This field is for your phone number. Please enter your phone number as you would like it to be displayed.
