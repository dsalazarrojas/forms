# digital_marketing_quiz_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "digital_marketing_quiz_template" form is designed to collect information about your role and experience in digital marketing. This will help us understand your background and provide relevant educational content.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by answering the questions on each page.
2. For each question, enter your response in the designated field.
3. You can leave optional fields blank if you don't want to provide the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your role in digital marketing?** (`intro`, `text`, `false`): This field is an introduction to your role in digital marketing. Please provide a brief description of your job title or position.
* **How many years of experience do you have in digital marketing?** (`experience`, `number`, `false`): Enter the number of years you have worked in the digital marketing field.
* **What is your email address?** (`email`, `email`, `false`): Provide your professional email address for communication purposes. This information will be kept secure and confidential.

## Tips
* Ensure that you're not completing the form multiple times on your platform.
* Double-check that your email address is correct before submitting the form.
