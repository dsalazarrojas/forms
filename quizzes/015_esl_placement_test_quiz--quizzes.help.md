# esl_placement_test_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The esl_placement_test_quiz form is designed to gather information about a user's proficiency levels in various categories for educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and select the most accurate answer from the provided options.
2. For select_one and select_multiple questions, choose the options that best match your proficiency level.
