# Monat Hair Consultation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Monat Hair Consultation form is a tool designed to collect information from users regarding their hair and hair care needs, allowing hair care professionals to gather essential details for scheduling appointments and providing better service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form by providing information as instructed on each page.
2. Start by filling out the "Personal Information" page, which includes your:
	* Personal details, as requested in the "Personal Information" page.
3. Next, proceed to the "Contact Information" page to provide your:
	* Contact details, as requested in the "Contact Information" page.
4. Continue on to the "Hair Details" page to provide:
	* Information about your hair, as requested in the "Hair Details" page.
5. After that, select your preferred:
	* Hair style on the "Hair Style" page.
6. If you have any concerns or issues with your hair, please mention them on the "Hair Concerns" page.
7. Confirm the next steps on the "Next Steps" page.
8. Provide your monat information on the "Monat Information" page.
9. Schedule the monat appointment on the "Monat Schedule" page.
10. Finally, follow up on the appointment details on the "Follow Up" page.
11. If you have any additional information to share, please do so on the "Additional Information" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Information (Personal Information page)** (`Personal details`, text, optional): Please provide your personal details, such as name, contact number, and email.
* **Contact Information (Contact Information page)** (`Contact details`, text, optional): Please provide your contact details, such as email, address, and phone number.
* **Hair Details (Hair Details page)** (`Hair information`, text, optional): Please provide information about your hair, such as type, length, and condition.
* **Hair Style (Hair Style page)** (`Hair style`, text, optional): Please choose your preferred hair style.
* **Hair Concerns (Hair Concerns page)** (`Hair concerns`, text, optional): If you have any concerns or issues with your hair, please mention them.
* **Next Steps (Next Steps page)** (`Next Steps`, text, optional): Please confirm the next steps, such as scheduling a monat appointment.
* **Monat Information (Monat Information page)** (`Monat information`, text, optional): Please provide your monat information.
* **Monat Schedule (Monat Schedule page)** (`Monat schedule`, text, optional): Please schedule the monat appointment.
* **Follow Up (Follow Up page)** (`Follow Up`, text, optional): Please follow up on the appointment details.
* **Additional Information (Additional Information page)** (`Additional information`, text, optional): If you have any additional information to share, please do so.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form as thoroughly as possible to ensure that the hair care professional has all the necessary information to provide the best service.
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please reach out to the hair care professional for further assistance.
