# Math Multiplication Skills Assessment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Math Multiplication Skills Assessment Quiz is a form used to evaluate a student's math multiplication skills. The form captures information about the student, including their name, email, phone number, grade, age, and attendance, as well as the date of the quiz. The teacher can provide feedback and ratings for the student, and the student can also provide feedback and comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter the student's name, grade, and age in the corresponding fields.
3. Choose the correct options for the student's attendance and teacher feedback.
4. Enter the date of the quiz.
5. Leave the student's email and phone number fields blank if not applicable.
6. Select the correct options for the teacher's rating and reasons.
7. Provide any additional comments for the student and teacher.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Practice Quiz** (id: 1, text, required): This is the main section of the form where the student answers the math multiplication quiz questions.
* **Teacher Feedback** (id: 2, note, required): The teacher can provide feedback and comments for the student's performance.
* **Student Feedback** (id: 3, select multiple, optional): The student can provide feedback and comments for their own performance.
* **Teacher Name** (id: 4, text, required): Enter the teacher's name who administered the quiz.
* **Student Name** (id: 5, text, required): Enter the student's name who took the quiz.
* **Quiz Date** (id: 6, date, required): Enter the date of the quiz.
* **Student Email** (id: 7, email, optional): Leave this field blank if not applicable.
* **Student Phone** (id: 8, text, optional): Leave this field blank if not applicable.
* **Student Age** (id: 9, number, required): Enter the student's age.
* **Student Grade** (id: 10, text, required): Enter the student's grade level.
* **Teacher Rating** (id: 11, number, optional): Enter the teacher's rating for the student's performance.
* **Comments** (id: 12, note, optional): Leave this field blank if not applicable.
* **Teacher Rating Reason** (id: 13, text, optional): Enter a reason for the teacher's rating.
* **Student Attendance** (id: 14, number, required): Enter the student's attendance status.
* **Teacher Comments** (id: 15, note, optional): Leave this field blank if not applicable.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
* Double-check the student's name, grade, and age for accuracy.
* Select the correct options for the teacher's rating and reasons.
* Enter the correct date of the quiz.
