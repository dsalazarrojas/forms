# Nutrition Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your nutrition and health goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with your relevant information. Make sure to answer each question truthfully and accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required): Please fill out your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required): Please fill out your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address that you would like us to use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Height** (`Height`, `number`, required): Enter your height in centimeters.
* **Weight** (`Weight`, `number`, required): Enter your weight in kilograms.
* **Blood Pressure** (`Blood Pressure`, `text`, optional): Enter your current blood pressure reading.
* **Medical Notes** (`Medical Notes`, `note`, optional): If you have any medical notes or concerns, please enter them here.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`Dietary Restrictions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any dietary restrictions you may have (e.g. vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free).
* **Health Goals** (`Health Goals`, `select_one`, required): Select your current health goals (e.g. weight loss, gain muscle, improve overall health).
* **Dietary Habits** (`Dietary Habits`, `text`, optional): Describe your current dietary habits (e.g. meat-based, plant-based, flexitarian).
* **Exercise Frequency** (`Exercise Frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select how often you exercise (e.g. daily, weekly, monthly).
* **Water Intake** (`Water Intake`, `select_one`, optional): Select your current water intake level (e.g. low, moderate, high).
* **Sleep Habits** (`Sleep Habits`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your current sleep habits (e.g. early, late, varied).
* **Medical Conditions** (`Medical Conditions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any medical conditions you may have (e.g. diabetes, hypertension, asthma).
* **Medications** (`Medications`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any allergies you may have (e.g. food, environmental).
* **Medical Tests** (`Medical Tests`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any medical tests you have undergone.
* **Dietician/Dietitian** (`Dietician/Dietitian`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your dietician or dietitian.
* **Doctor** (`Doctor`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your doctor.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`Emergency Contact Email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.

Note: Please fill out each field as accurately and truthfully as possible, and don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or concerns.
