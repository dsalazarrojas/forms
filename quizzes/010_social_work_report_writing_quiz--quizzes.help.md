# Social Work Report Writing Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Work Report Writing Quiz is designed to assess your ability to write a social work report based on given scenarios and questions. It helps you develop your skills and knowledge in social work report writing by evaluating your understanding of key concepts and principles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the quiz, follow these steps:

1. Read each scenario carefully and choose the best answer based on your understanding of social work report writing principles.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, and make sure to read the explanations and feedback carefully.
3. For select_one questions, choose only one answer from the options provided.
4. For select_multiple questions, choose all applicable answers from the options provided.
5. For text fields, write your answer in the space provided.
6. If you are unsure about any question, you can skip it and come back to it later.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **introduction**: This section is an optional note-taking area where you can jot down any important notes or references that you want to include in your social work report.
* **scenario**: Choose the answer that best describes the situation. This question will test your ability to analyze and understand the scenario presented.
* **knowledge**: This is a text field where you can provide your thoughts on the principles of social work report writing that are relevant to the scenario.
* **skills**: Choose all the skills that are relevant to the scenario. This question will test your ability to apply your knowledge of social work report writing principles.
* **confidence**: Rate your confidence level (1-5) in your answer to this question. This will help you reflect on your understanding of the principles of social work report writing.
* **scenario 2**: Choose the answer that best describes the second situation. This question will test your ability to analyze and understand the second scenario presented.
* **knowledge 2**: This is a text field where you can provide your thoughts on the principles of social work report writing that are relevant to the second scenario.
* **skills 2**: Choose all the skills that are relevant to the second scenario. This question will test your ability to apply your knowledge of social work report writing principles.
* **confidence 2**: Rate your confidence level (1-5) in your answer to this question. This will help you reflect on your understanding of the principles of social work report writing.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer each question to the best of your ability.
* Take your time to read each question carefully and reflect on your thoughts.
