# Syrups And Elixirs Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about syrups and elixirs, with a focus on liquid dosage forms. It is intended to be used by individuals who need to categorize and note relevant details about syrups and elixirs. The form can be used for personal or professional purposes, such as in medical settings or in research studies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter a title for the form**: In this field, please enter a brief title for your quiz or survey.
2. **Provide a description of the form**: If you would like to, enter a description of the form to provide more context and background information.
3. **Select a category**: From the dropdown menu, select the category you are using the form for (e.g., medical, research, etc.).
4. **Enter an email address**: If you would like to be contacted, enter your email address.
5. **Select liquid dosage forms**: Choose the relevant liquid dosage forms for your quiz or survey.
6. **Enter notes**: If you need to, enter any additional notes or comments related to the form.
7. **Submit the form**: Click the "Submit" button to save your form.
