## Profit And Loss Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to test your knowledge about profit and loss, a fundamental concept in finance. It will ask you three questions to evaluate your understanding of the relationship between income and expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the three questions one by one.
2. For each question, provide a numerical value in the box, even if it's a decimal number.
3. The form is not meant to be time-sensitive, so take your time to answer each question carefully.
4. You can go back and forth between questions to review your answers if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your current income** (`profit_and_loss_knowledge_quiz_question_1`, Number, Optional): Enter your current monthly income. This is the amount of money you receive as pay or salary.
* **What is your current expenses** (`profit_and_loss_knowledge_quiz_question_2`, Number, Optional): Enter your current monthly expenses, such as rent, utilities, and other regular costs.
* **What is your profit and loss** (`profit_and_loss_knowledge_quiz_question_3`, Number, Optional): Enter the difference between your current income and expenses. This will give us an idea of your financial situation.

## Tips
* Please answer each question carefully, as your responses will be used to evaluate your understanding of profit and loss.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, feel free to review them again before submitting your answers.
* This form is a knowledge quiz, not a real-life financial assessment, so don't worry about providing actual numbers - just use hypothetical values if you're not sure what to enter.
