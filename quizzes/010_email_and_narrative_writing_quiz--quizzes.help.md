# Email and Narrative Writing Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the quality of email and narrative writing, providing feedback on the tone, style, and purpose of the writing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Evaluate the email and narrative writing samples provided.
2. Choose the appropriate options for each section, considering the tone, style, and purpose of the writing.
3. Select the correct tone, style, and purpose for each section.
4. Provide specific feedback on the writing, taking into account the selected options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, **required**): Enter the email address of the person who wrote the email and narrative.
* **Subject** (`subject`, text, **optional**): Enter a brief subject line for the email and narrative.
* **Greeting** (`greeting`, text, **optional**): Enter a greeting for the email and narrative.
* **Body** (`body`, text, **optional**): Enter the main content of the email and narrative.
* **Closing** (`closing`, text, **optional**): Enter a closing for the email and narrative.
* **CC Recipients** (`cc_recipients`, text, **optional**): Enter any CC recipients for the email and narrative.
* **BCC Recipients** (`bcc_recipients`, text, **optional**): Enter any BCC recipients for the email and narrative.
* **Attachment** (`attachment`, text, **optional**): Enter any attachment for the email and narrative.
* **Tone** (`tone`, text, **optional**): Enter the tone of the email and narrative writing.
* **Narrative** (`narrative`, text, **optional**): Enter a brief description of the narrative writing.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, **optional**): Enter any feedback on the email and narrative writing.
* **Email Format** (`email_format`, select_one, **required**): Choose the format of the email and narrative writing.
* **Writing Style** (`writing_style`, select_multiple, **required**): Choose the writing styles for the email and narrative writing.
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, select_multiple, **required**): Choose the purpose of the email and narrative writing.
* **Audience** (`audience`, select_multiple, **required**): Choose the audience for the email and narrative writing.
* **Tone Level** (`tone_level`, select_one, **required**): Choose the tone level of the email and narrative writing.
* **Narrative Writing** (`narrative_writing`, select_one, **required**): Choose the narrative writing style.
* **Feedback Writing** (`feedback_writing`, select_one, **required**): Choose the feedback writing style.
* **Email and Narrative Score** (`email_and_narrative_score`, select_one, **required**): Choose the score for the email and narrative writing.
* **Email and Narrative Comments** (`email_and_narrative_comments`, text, **required**): Enter any comments on the email and narrative writing.
* **Email and Narrative Attachment** (`email_and_narrative_attachment`, text, **optional**): Enter any attachment for the email and narrative writing.
* **Email and Narrative Additional Comments** (`email_and_narrative_additional_comments`, text, **optional**): Enter any additional comments on the email and narrative writing.
* **Email and Narrative Additional Attachment** (`email_and_narrative_additional_attachment`, text, **optional**): Enter any additional attachment for the email and narrative writing.
* **Email and Narrative Additional Narrative** (`email_and_narrative_additional_narrative`, text, **optional**): Enter any additional narrative information on the email and narrative writing.

## Tips

* Be thorough and specific when providing feedback on the email and narrative writing.
* Choose the correct options for each section, taking into account the tone, style, and purpose of the writing.
* Enter any additional comments or attachments if necessary.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
