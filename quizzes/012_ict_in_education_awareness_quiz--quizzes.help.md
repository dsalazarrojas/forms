# ICT In Education Awareness Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from teachers and educators about their awareness and usage of Information and Communication Technology (ICT) in education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and select the most appropriate answer based on your understanding and experience.
2. For questions that require multiple selections, choose all the options that apply.
3. For questions that ask for a specific time or date, provide the correct time or date according to your usual practice.
