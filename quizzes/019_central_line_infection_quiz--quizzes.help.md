# Central Line Infection Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to educate and inform healthcare professionals about the best practices for preventing central line infections. It assesses a user's knowledge and understanding of central line infection prevention and management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your name and email address.
2. Select your job role from the provided options.
3. Choose the most common cause of central line infections.
4. Select the recommended hand hygiene practices for line insertion.
5. Choose the preferred site for adult central line placement.
6. Select all the components of the central line bundle.
7. Identify the most indicative symptom of catheter-related bloodstream infection (CRBSI).
8. Select the dressing change frequency for transparent dressings.
9. Certify that you have completed the quiz independently.
10. Enter the date of completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`quiz_taker_name`, `text`, required): This is the field where you should enter your name.
* **Email Address** (`quiz_taker_email`, `email`, required): This is the field where you should enter your email address. This will be used to receive your quiz results.
* **Job Role** (`quiz_taker_role`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to determine your role in the healthcare industry. Select the option that best describes your position.
* **Most Common Cause of Central Line Infections** (`common_infection_cause`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to gauge your understanding of the most common causes of central line infections.
* **Recommended Hand Hygiene for Line Insertion** (`recommended_hand_hygiene`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is used to test your knowledge of hand hygiene best practices.
* **Preferred Site for Adult Central Line Placement** (`preferred_insertion_site`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to determine the best site for central line placement.
* **Components of the Central Line Bundle** (`central_line_bundle_components`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is used to test your knowledge of the central line bundle.
* **Most Indicative Symptom of CRBSI** (`crbsi_symptom_indicator`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to identify the most indicative symptom of catheter-related bloodstream infection (CRBSI).
* **Dressing Change Frequency for Transparent Dressings** (`dressing_change_frequency`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to determine the recommended dressing change frequency for transparent dressings.
* **Independence Certification** (`independence_certification`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to certify that you have completed the quiz independently.
* **Date of Quiz** (`quiz_completion_date`, `date`, required): This is the field where you should enter the date of completion.
