# open_ended_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "open_ended_quiz" form is designed to collect open-ended responses to questions, likely for a survey or research context. It is created to gather qualitative feedback or answers that require more in-depth explanations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Read the intro label carefully, as it provides context and background information for the questions that follow.
2. Answer each question with a brief or detailed response, depending on your thoughts and feelings about the topic.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields (although they are currently all optional, this may change in the future).
4. Review your responses for completeness and accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **intro_label**: This is a note field that provides background information about the questions that follow. It's a one-time read-only field that doesn't require a specific answer, but it helps set the context for the rest of the form.
* **open_ended_question_1**: This is a text field that asks for your thoughts on a specific topic. Type your response in the provided field, and be as brief or detailed as you feel necessary.
* **open_ended_question_2**: Similar to the previous question, this text field asks for your thoughts on another topic. Respond accordingly.

## Tips
* Be honest and sincere in your responses, as they will help in creating a comprehensive understanding of the topic.
* If you need more information or clarification on any of the questions, feel free to ask the form creator or contact us at form@gic.mx.
* Remember to review your responses for completeness and accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any feedback or suggestions on the form, we would love to hear from you.
