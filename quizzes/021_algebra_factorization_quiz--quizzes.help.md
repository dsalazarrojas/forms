# Algebra Factorization Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Algebra Factorization Quiz is designed to test your skills in factoring algebraic expressions. The quiz consists of 20 questions that will challenge your ability to factor quadratic and cubic expressions. By answering these questions, you will assess your understanding of the different factoring techniques used in algebra.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the demographic information: 
   - Enter your full name in the "Student Name" field.
   - Enter your ID number in the "Student ID" field.
   - Choose your class period in the "Class Period" field.
2. Answer each question by typing the factored form of the given expression in the "Factored Form" field. 
   - Be sure to follow the format for factoring expressions correctly.
3. Select your confidence level after completing the quiz in the "Confidence Level" field.
4. Record the time you spent on the quiz in the "Time Spent" field.
