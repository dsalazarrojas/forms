# Weekly Concepts Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Concepts Quiz is a tool used to gauge your understanding of weekly concepts relevant to your role or job function. Completing this form will help you demonstrate your knowledge and application of these concepts in a practical setting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the questions and concepts presented in each page.
2. For each page, fill in the required information based on your understanding of the concepts.
3. Use the "Submit" button to save your progress and move to the next page.
4. Continue completing each page until you have filled out all the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Concept 1** (`concept_1_label`, text, required/optional): This is the first concept you are being asked to understand. Please provide a brief description or explanation of this concept.
* **Concept 2** (`concept_2_label`, text, required/optional): This is the second concept you are being asked to understand. Please provide a brief description or explanation of this concept.
* **Concept 3** (`concept_3_label`, text, required/optional): This is the third concept you are being asked to understand. Please provide a brief description or explanation of this concept.
* **Question 1** (`question_1_label`, text, required/optional): This is a question related to the concepts you have been presented. Please provide a response based on your understanding of the concepts.
* **Answer 1** (`answer_1_label`, select_one, required/optional): This question requires a select-one response. Choose the most accurate answer from the provided options.
 

## Tips
* Make sure to answer each question based on your understanding of the concepts.
* If you are unsure of a concept, please ask your supervisor or colleague for clarification.
* Review your progress regularly to ensure you have completed all the required information.
