# Demography Training Assessment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess users' understanding and opinions on demographic changes and trends. It helps us evaluate your knowledge and perceptions on these topics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and select the most accurate answer.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. Write your feedback and insights in the "Feedback" field.
4. Enter your email address and phone number (if applicable).
5. Select your category of interest from the dropdown list.
6. Enter your name and date (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is a brief text-based section where you can introduce yourself and set the context for your answers.
* **How do you feel about demographic changes in the world**: Select all options that apply to you.
* **answer1**: Select one option that best describes your perspective.
* **What do you think about the current demographic trends**: Select all options that apply to you.
* **Feedback**: Share your thoughts, insights, or interesting findings about demography in this field.
* **Results**: Select one option that best describes your assessment results.
* **email**: Enter your email address (e.g., john.doe@example.com).
* **phone**: Enter your phone number (e.g., +1-555-1234).
* **Date**: Enter the current date (e.g., 2022-07-25).
* **Time**: Enter the current time (e.g., 14:30).
* **Category**: Select one option that best describes your area of interest.
* **Name**: Enter your name (e.g., John Doe).

Note: The form is designed to be completed quickly, so please do not spend more than 10-15 minutes on it.
