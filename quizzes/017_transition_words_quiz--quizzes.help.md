# Transition Words Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This quiz is designed to assess your understanding of transition words, which are words that connect ideas, sentences, and paragraphs in writing. You'll be tested on your ability to identify, use, and describe transition words effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and choose the correct answer based on your knowledge of transition words.
2. For select_one and select_multiple questions, select the most appropriate option from the list.
3. For text questions, provide a detailed answer in the designated space.
4. Complete all questions, and you'll have a good grasp of transition words and their importance in writing!
