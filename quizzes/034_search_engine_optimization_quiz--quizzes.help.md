# search_engine_optimization_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users evaluate their website's search engine optimization (SEO) strategy. Fill out this form to gather information on how well your website is optimized for search engines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field as described below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Search Engine Optimization** (`search_engine_optimization`): This field is a text input for you to briefly describe your website's current search engine optimization strategy. Please enter a short description of your current efforts.
* **Search Engine Keywords** (`search_engine_keywords`): Enter a list of the most important keywords related to your website and the topics it covers.
* **Search Engine Description** (`search_engine_description`): Briefly describe your website's purpose and what users can expect to find when visiting your website.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`): Identify the group of people your website is intended for (e.g., age group, location, etc.).
* **Keyword Clustering** (`keyword_clustering`): Describe how you cluster or group related keywords together (e.g., using categories, subcategories, etc.).
* **Search Engine Keywords (2)** (`search_engine_keywords_2`): Enter another list of important keywords related to your website and the topics it covers.
* **Meta Description** (`meta_description`): Briefly summarize your website's main topic or purpose in a text input.
* **Keyword Density** (`keyword_density`): Describe your keyword density strategy (e.g., the number of times keywords are used on a page).
* **Meta Description** (`meta_description`): This field is not used in this form as it seems to be a duplicate of the previous field.
