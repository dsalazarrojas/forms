# kitchen_task_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The kitchen_task_assessment_form is used to assess the performance of kitchen staff in various aspects of their work. It helps kitchen managers or supervisors evaluate employee performance and provide feedback for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of assessment you are performing (Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced).
2. Choose the date you assessed the staff member's performance.
3. Select who assessed the staff member's performance (Chef, Cook, or Restaurateur).
4. Enter a score out of 5 for the staff member's performance.
5. Optionally, provide comments about the staff member's performance.
6. Select the relevant training areas the staff member needs to improve in (e.g., knife skills, knife safety, knife handling).
7. Check the areas where the staff member demonstrates good kitchen skills, such as prep, cooking, food safety.
8. Check the kitchen equipment the staff member needs to improve in (e.g., knives, pots and pans, appliances).
9. Select the kitchen layout and organization the staff member demonstrates (e.g., layout, organization, efficiency).
10. Choose the aspects of food quality the staff member achieves (e.g., presentation, taste, consistency).
11. Select the areas where the staff member shows good team work skills (e.g., communication, cooperation, leadership).
12. Select the areas where the staff member demonstrates good time management skills (e.g., scheduling, prioritization, pacing).
