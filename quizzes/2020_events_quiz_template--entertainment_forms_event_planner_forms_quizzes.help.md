# 2020 Events Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 2020 Events Quiz is a survey designed to test your knowledge of significant events that occurred in 2020. The quiz aims to assess your understanding of global news, politics, and social changes that took place during that year.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and select the most accurate answer from the provided options.
2. If a question has multiple correct answers, choose all applicable options.
3. Fill out the form completely and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you'd like it displayed.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, email, required): Used to send quiz results and updates.
* **Date you took the quiz** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date you are completing this quiz.
* **How did you access this quiz?** (`attendance_type`, select_one, optional): Choose how you accessed this quiz, whether it's in person, virtually, or in a hybrid mode.
* **Which global event led to widespread lockdowns in 2020?** (`q_lockdown_cause`, select_one, required): Pick the most widely recognized cause of lockdowns.
* **Which company announced a permanent work-from-home option for many employees in 2020?** (`q_company_wfh`, select_one, required): Choose the most notable early adopter company.
* **Which streaming service released "Tiger King" in 2020?** (`q_tiger_king`, select_one, required): Select the streaming service that released "Tiger King".
* **Which major sports event was postponed from 2020 to 2021?** (`q_tokyo_olympics`, select_one, required): Choose the sports event that was postponed.
* **Which U.S. city was the center of large protests after George Floyd's death?** (`q_george_floyd_protests`, select_one, required): Select the city where the incident occurred.
* **Which of the following events occurred in 2020? (Select all that apply)** (`q_multiple_events_2020`, select_multiple, optional): Choose every option that applies to the events listed.
* **Which national leader tested positive for COVID-19 in 2020?** (`q_leader_covid`, select_one, required): Select the national leader who tested positive.
* **Rate your knowledge of major 2020 events (1-10)** (`q_self_rating`, integer, required): Rate your knowledge from 1 (novice) to 10 (expert).
* **Would you like to receive more quizzes like this?** (`would_like_more_quizzes`, select_one, optional): Choose your preference.
* **May we email your quiz results?** (`consent_receive_results`, select_one, required): Allow us to send you your quiz results.
* **Additional comments or corrections about the quiz** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Share any feedback or note any incorrect items.
