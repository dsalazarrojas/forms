# Synonym Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you assess your understanding of synonyms and test your knowledge in identifying alternative words or phrases. By completing this quiz, you'll have the opportunity to showcase your vocabulary skills and provide feedback on the questions asked.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question by selecting one or more options from the provided list.
2. For each question, choose the correct synonym from the available options.
3. Once you've answered all questions, click the "Submit" button to submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **What is another word for** (`synonym_question`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to identify synonyms for a given word or phrase. Select one or more options that you believe are correct synonyms.
### 2. **Answer** (`answer`, note, required): This field is for you to type your answer.
### 3. **Synonym Response** (`synonym_response`, select_multiple, required): This question asks for your response regarding the synonym provided. Select the correct response from the options.
### 4. **Feedback** (`synonym_feedback`, select_multiple, optional): This question allows you to provide feedback on the question asked. Select from the options if you have any feedback.
### 5. **Score** (`synonym_score`, number, optional): If you have a score or rating for the question, enter it here. This field is optional.
### 6. **Time** (`synonym_time`, time, required): Enter the time taken to complete the question. This field is required for accurate assessment.
### 7. **Date** (`synonym_date`, date, optional): This field is for you to enter the date of completion, if needed.
### 8. **Score out of 10** (`synonym_score_out_of_10`, number, required): If you have a score or rating out of 10 for the question, enter it here. This field is required.
### 9. **Feedback out of 10** (`synonym_feedback_out_of_10`, select_multiple, optional): This question allows you to provide feedback on the question asked, with an option to score it out of 10. Select from the options if you have any feedback.
### 10. **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, required): Click the "Submit" button to submit your answers and complete the quiz.

## Tips
- Take your time to carefully read and understand each question before answering.
- Ensure you select the correct options for each question.
- Provide clear and concise feedback for each question.
- Enter accurate scores and times for each question.
- Review your answers before submission to ensure accuracy.
