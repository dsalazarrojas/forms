# Oviparous Animals Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Oviparous Animals Survey is designed to collect information about oviparous animals, which lay eggs as a method of reproduction. This form will help us gather data about the species, frequency, and habits of these animals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the species of the oviparous animal you are providing information about. You can choose from the provided options or select "Other" if the species is not listed.
2. If you selected "Other", provide the name of the species in the "Other Species" field.
3. Answer the questions about how often you lay eggs, how many eggs you lay at a time, and when you lay eggs.
4. Choose where you lay eggs (water, land, or both).
5. Provide your contact information, including your email address and any additional notes about the species.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Species** (`Select Species`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the species of the oviparous animal you are providing information about. Options: Birds, Reptiles, Insects.
* **Other Species** (`Other Species`, text, required/optional): If you selected "Other" in the "Select Species" field, provide the name of the species in this field.
* **How often do you lay eggs?** (`How often do you lay eggs?`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select how often you lay eggs (daily, weekly, monthly).
* **How many eggs do you lay at a time?** (`How many eggs do you lay at a time?`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of eggs you lay at a time.
* **When do you lay eggs?** (`When do you lay eggs?`, date, required/optional): Select the date you lay eggs.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, required/optional): Select the time of day you lay eggs.
* **Where do you lay eggs?** (`Where do you lay eggs?`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose where you lay eggs (water, land, or both).
* **Contact** (`Contact`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information about yourself or the species.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, text, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the species.
