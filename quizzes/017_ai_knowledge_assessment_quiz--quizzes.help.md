# AI Knowledge Assessment Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The AI Knowledge Assessment Quiz is designed to evaluate a person's knowledge and understanding of AI-related concepts and theories. This quiz aims to assess an individual's knowledge in various areas of AI, from machine learning to model evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Read the quiz instructions provided on the first page.
2. Enter your name and email (if you want to be notified with your results).
3. Answer the questions by choosing the correct response or providing a short answer.
4. Rate your self-assessment of AI knowledge on the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Participant email** (`email`, email, optional): If you want to be notified with your results, provide your email address.
* **What is machine learning** (`q1_understanding`, text, required): In 1-2 sentences, explain what machine learning is.
* **Which scenario is supervised learning** (`q2_supervised`, select_one, required): Choose the best option among: Labeled data training, Unlabeled data clustering, or Reinforcement agent learning.
* **Best metric for imbalanced classification** (`q3_model_eval`, select_one, required): Choose the best metric among: Accuracy, Precision recall, or AUC ROC.
* **How to mitigate dataset bias** (`q4_bias`, text, optional): In 1-2 sentences, describe a method to mitigate dataset bias.
* **Key compliance consideration** (`q5_regulations`, text, optional): In 1-2 sentences, mention a key consideration for AI-related regulations.
* **Self assessment of AI knowledge** (`overall_score`, select_one, required): Rate your self-assessment of AI knowledge from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
