# English Grammar Error Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The English Grammar Error Quiz is a tool designed to assess the user's knowledge of English Grammar by providing a quiz with different sections. The form is meant to be a simple and straightforward way to gather information about the user's progress in the English Grammar quiz.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields. The form has several sections, each representing a different part of the quiz. You will be asked to enter your first name, email, and other details as specified in each section. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. First Name
* **first_name** (Text, Optional): Enter your first name as you would like to be referred to in the quiz.

### 2. Email
* **email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address for identification purposes.

### 3. Quiz Score
* **quiz_score** (Number, Optional): Record your self-assessed score for the current quiz round.

### 4. Section 1 Score
* **section_1** (Number, Optional): Record your self-assessed score for Section 1 of the quiz.

### 5. Section 2 Score
* **section_2** (Number, Optional): Record your self-assessed score for Section 2 of the quiz.

### 6. Section 3 Score
* **section_3** (Number, Optional): Record your self-assessed score for Section 3 of the quiz.

### 7. Section 4 Score
* **section_4** (Number, Optional): Record your self-assessed score for Section 4 of the quiz.

### 8. Final Score
* **final_score** (Number, Optional): Record your final self-assessed score for the entire quiz.

### 9. Feedback
* **feedback** (Note, Optional): Any feedback you'd like to provide about your experience taking this quiz.

### 10. Submission Time
* **submission_time** (Time, Optional): Record the time you spent taking this quiz.

Please fill out each field as accurately as possible, and submit your form to get your progress in the quiz.
