# Hydrophilic Vs Hydrophobic Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hydrophilic Vs Hydrophobic Quiz is designed to help you understand the difference between hydrophilic and hydrophobic materials. Hydrophilic materials are those that attract water, while hydrophobic materials are those that repel water. This quiz will guide you through examples and questions to help you determine which category a material falls under.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the correct answer for each question.
2. Answer the open-ended questions with a brief description of the material's properties.
3. You can skip the open-ended questions if you're short on time or don't have a clear answer.
4. Submit the form to view your results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Intro** (`intro`, text, required: false): A brief introduction to the quiz.
- **What is hydrophilic?** (`intro_question`, select_one, required: true): This question asks you to choose between Hydrophilic, Hydrophobic, and Amphiphilic. Choose the one that best describes materials that attract water.
  - Hydrophilic: Materials that attract water.
  - Hydrophobic: Materials that repel water.
  - Amphiphilic: Materials that have both hydrophilic and hydrophobic properties.
- **What is hydrophobic?** (`hydrophobic_question`, select_one, required: true): This question asks you to choose between Hydrophilic, Hydrophobic, and Amphiphilic. Choose the one that best describes materials that repel water.
  - Hydrophilic: Materials that attract water.
  - Hydrophobic: Materials that repel water.
  - Amphiphilic: Materials that have both hydrophilic and hydrophobic properties.
- **What is hydrophobic?** (`hydrophobic_properties`, select_multiple, required: true): This question asks you to choose one or more of the following options:
  - Does not attract water
  - Does not repel water
  - Does repel water
  - Choose the options that best describe materials that repel water.
- **What is hydrophilic?** (`hydrophilic_properties`, select_multiple, required: true): This question asks you to choose one or more of the following options:
  - Attracts water
  - Repels water
  - Does both
  - Choose the options that best describe materials that attract water.
- **Examples:** (`hydrophilic_example`, `hydrophobic_example`, `hydrophilic_example_2`, etc., text, optional): These questions allow you to describe examples of hydrophilic and hydrophobic materials. You can answer as many or as few as you like.
- **Result** (`result`, note, required: false): This is the final question that will display your results after you've completed the quiz.

## Tips
- Take your time and read each question carefully before selecting your answer.
- Think about the properties of each material as you answer the questions.
- Don't hesitate to skip a question if you're unsure, but make sure to answer all required questions.
