# Additive Manufacturing Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to test your knowledge on additive manufacturing, a type of 3D printing technology.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the multiple-choice questions carefully, selecting the best answer for each question.
2. For question 4, select one of the available materials used in FDM.
3. For question 5, choose the process that uses powder and laser.
4. For question 6, select the approximate dimensional tolerance range for consumer printers.
5. For question 7, choose all the common post-processing steps that apply to you.
6. For question 8, select the primary safety concern for powder processes.
7. If you want to receive results and feedback, enter your email address in the "Email" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Participant -- (Note)**: This is a note field for your reference and identification.
* **Participant name**: Enter your name to record your score and provide feedback.
* **Email (Optional)**: If you want to receive results and feedback, enter your email address here. Leave blank if not applicable.
* **Q1 Which materials are commonly used in FDM**: Choose one of the four options:
	+ PLA
	+ Nylon
	+ Stainless steel
	+ Epoxy resin
* **Q2 Which process uses powder and laser**: Choose one of the four options:
	+ SLA
	+ SLS
	+ FDM
	+ Binder jetting
* **Q3 Typical dimensional tolerance for consumer printers**: Select one of the four options:
	+ 0.1 mm
	+ 0.5 mm
	+ 1 mm
	+ 5 mm
* **Q4 Common post-processing steps**: Select all that apply:
	+ Support removal
	+ Sanding
	+ Sintering
	+ Electroplating
* **Q5 Primary safety concern for powder processes**: Choose one of the four options:
	+ Inhalation
	+ Noise
	+ Heat
	+ Electrocution
* **Total score (Admin only)**: This field is for recording your final score, not editable by participants.
* **Feedback on quiz**: Optional comments about the quiz content, help us improve it.
