# neuroscience_and_consciousness_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "neuroscience_and_consciousness_quiz" form is designed to gather participant information and follow-up question answers for research purposes. It is intended to collect data on learners' confidence levels, study group names, types, and follow-up questions and answers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2. Select the relevant options for each multiple-choice question.
3. Enter your answers for each text field.
4. Ensure you submit the form after completing all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Follow Up Question** (`follow_up_question`: text, optional): This field is a text entry for follow-up questions.
* **2. Participant Information** (`participant_information`: note, required): This field is a note where you can provide any additional information about the participant.
* **3. Email** (`email`: email, required): Enter your email address.
* **4. Phone** (`phone`: text, optional): This field is for your phone number.
* **5. Study Group Name** (`study_group_name`: select_multiple, required): Select one or more study group names from the available options.
* **6. Study Group Type** (`study_group_type`: select_one, required): Choose the type of study group from the available options.
* **7. Follow Up Question Date** (`follow_up_question_date`: date, required): Enter the date for the follow-up question.
* **8. Follow Up Question Time** (`follow_up_question_time`: time, required): Enter the time for the follow-up question.
* **9. Follow Up Question Note** (`follow_up_question_note`: note, optional): This field is for additional notes on the follow-up question.
* **10-20. Follow Up Questions 2-7** (`follow_up_question_2`, `follow_up_question_3`, `follow_up_question_4`, `follow_up_question_5`, `follow_up_question_6`, `follow_up_question_7`: select_one/multiple, optional): These fields are for additional follow-up questions and answers.
* **11-21. Follow Up Question 2-7 Answers** (`follow_up_question_2_answer`, `follow_up_question_3_answer`, `follow_up_question_4_answer`, `follow_up_question_5_answer`, `follow_up_question_6_answer`, `follow_up_question_7_answer`: text, optional): Enter your answers for the corresponding follow-up questions.

## Tips
* Ensure you complete all required fields before submitting the form.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to ask.
