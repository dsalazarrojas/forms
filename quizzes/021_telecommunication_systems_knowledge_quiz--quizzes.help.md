## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on a page to start creating or editing a quiz.
2. Select the status of the quiz (Active or Inactive) to indicate if it is currently available or not.
3. Set the start and end time of the quiz.
4. Choose the date for the quiz.
5. Enter the title of the quiz.
6. Choose the duration of the quiz.
7. Select the options for the questions (Yes or No) and choose whether the user can submit multiple answers (select multiple) or only one correct answer (select one).
8. Enter the expected instructor feedback and any other comments.
9. Calculate or enter the final score of the quiz.
10. Finally, click on the submit button to finish creating or editing the quiz.
