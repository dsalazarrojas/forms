# Analog Color TV Standards Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This quiz is designed to test your knowledge about different analog color TV standards. It will assess your understanding of various technical aspects related to analog color TV standards, specifically regarding standards such as NTSC, PAL, and SECAM.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Student Full Name and Student Email Address in the respective fields.
2. Answer each question in the designated sections, selecting the correct options for each question.
3. At the end, choose whether you would like a copy of your results sent to your email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Student Email Address** (`student_email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address where you can receive notifications and results.
* **Which countries primarily used the NTSC standard?** (`ntsc_standard_countries`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the options USA and Japan, UK and Germany, France and Russia, or Brazil. This question is about the countries where NTSC (National Television System Committee) was primarily used.
* **What is the standard frame rate for PAL signals?** (`pal_frame_rate`, `select_one`, required): Select 25 fps from the options 25 fps, 30 fps, 24 fps, or 60 fps. This question is about the standard frame rate for PAL (Phase Alternating Line) signals.
* **In which country was the SECAM standard developed?** (`secam_origin_country`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the options France, USA, Germany, or Japan. This question is about the country where SECAM (Système Électronique pour un Canal Analogique de Mesure) was developed.
* **Which of these are color encoding methods used in analog TV?** (`color_encoding_methods`, `select_multiple`, required): Select QAM (Quadrature Amplitude Modulation), FM (Frequency Modulation), Phase Modulation, or Amplitude Modulation from the options. This question is about the color encoding methods used in analog TV.
* **How many total lines are in a standard NTSC signal?** (`total_lines_in_standard_ntsc_signal`, `number`, required): Enter the number of lines as it is a numerical value. This question is about the total number of lines in a standard NTSC signal.
* **What is the typical color subcarrier frequency for PAL?** (`subcarrier_frequency_for_pal`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the options 4.43 MHz, 3.58 MHz, 4.2 MHz, or 5.5 MHz. This question is about the typical color subcarrier frequency for PAL signals.
* **Briefly explain one major difference between PAL and NTSC** (`explain_difference_between_pal_and_ntsc`, `text`, required): Write a short description of the difference between PAL and NTSC. This question is about understanding the fundamental difference between these two standards.
* **Would you like a copy of your results sent to your email?** (`request_copy_of_results`, `select_one`, required): Select True or False to receive or not receive a copy of your results via email.
