# Primary Care Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Primary Care Knowledge Quiz" form is designed to assess users' knowledge and understanding of primary care topics. It is a comprehensive tool for evaluating your knowledge in this area, helping you gauge your proficiency and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand what is required and what information is being requested.
2. For text fields, enter your response in the provided text box.
3. For select options fields, choose the correct option from the list.
4. For date fields, enter the date in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
5. For time fields, enter the time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
6. For note fields, enter any relevant notes or comments in the provided text box.
7. For email fields, enter your email address.
8. Review your responses carefully before submitting the form.
