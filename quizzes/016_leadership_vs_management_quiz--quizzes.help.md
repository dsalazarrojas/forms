# Leadership Vs Management Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals understand the difference between leadership and management. It consists of six questions that assess your knowledge of leadership and management concepts, and allows you to calculate your score based on your answers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and select all that apply from the options provided.
2. For each question, select all the correct options that best describe the concept.
3. Once you have completed all questions, click "Submit" to calculate your score.
