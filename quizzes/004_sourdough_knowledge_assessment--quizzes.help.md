# Sourdough Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sourdough Knowledge Assessment is a form designed to evaluate your knowledge and skills related to sourdough bread baking. It's intended to help identify areas where you'd like to learn more about sourdough, and provide a starting point for further education and training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Sourdough Knowledge Assessment form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and answer them truthfully to the best of your ability.
2. For each question, choose the most accurate response from the available options.
3. Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How do you feel about sourdough?**: This question is an introduction to the assessment and aims to gauge your initial thoughts and feelings about sourdough bread baking. Answer this question in as much detail as you like.
* **Years of baking experience?**: This question is asking about your overall baking experience, not specifically with sourdough. Please enter the number of years you've been baking.
* **Sourdough bread baked**: This question is asking if you have experience baking sourdough bread. Please answer "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
* **Sourdough kneading**: This question is asking about your experience with kneading sourdough. Answer "Yes" or "No" based on your experience.
* **Sourdough bread tasting**: This question is asking if you enjoy the taste of sourdough bread. Answer "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
* **What do you want to learn about sourdough?**: This question is asking about your learning goals for sourdough. Choose "Yes" if you're interested in learning, "No" if not, or "Other" if you have other goals.
* **Baking frequency**: This question is asking how often you bake bread. Choose the options that apply to you:
	+ "Yes" for baking frequently (e.g., every day)
	+ "No" for baking occasionally (e.g., once a week)
	+ "Other" for baking infrequently or not at all
* **Baking time**: This question is asking about the time you spend baking. Enter the time in the format "HH:MM" (e.g., 02:30 for 2:30 AM)
* **Baking frequency days a week**: This question is asking how many days a week you bake. Choose the options that apply to you:
	+ "Yes" for baking on most days of the week
	+ "No" for baking on a few days of the week
	+ "Other" for baking on a custom schedule
* **Sourdough kneading time**: This question is asking about the time spent kneading sourdough. Enter the time in the format "HH:MM" (e.g., 02:30 for 2:30 AM)
* **Sourdough bread shape**: This question is asking about the shape of sourdough bread you bake. Choose the options that apply to you:
	+ "Yes" for baking round bread
	+ "No" for not baking round bread
	+ "Other" for baking other shapes (e.g., boule, batard)
* **Baking environment**: This question is asking about your baking environment. Choose the options that apply to you:
	+ "Yes" for baking in a home kitchen
	+ "No" for baking in a commercial environment
	+ "Other" for baking in other locations (e.g., outdoors, community oven)
* **Sourdough bread temperature**: This question is asking about the temperature of your sourdough bread. Choose the options that apply to you:
	+ "Yes" for baking at low temperature (e.g., 150°F-200°F)
	+ "No" for baking at high temperature (e.g., 210°F-220°F)
	+ "Other" for baking at other temperatures (e.g., proofing temperature, refrigeration)
* **Learning Needs**: This question is asking about your learning needs for sourdough. Choose the options that apply to you:
	+ "Yes" for wanting to learn more about sourdough
	+ "No" for not needing to learn more about sourdough
	+ "Other" for having other learning needs (e.g., specific techniques, ingredients)
