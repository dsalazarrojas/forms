# Oedipus Rex Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Oedipus Rex Knowledge Quiz form is designed to collect information from users about their knowledge of the Oedipus Rex subject. The form is likely used by educators or researchers to evaluate user's knowledge level and identify areas where they need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields: Correct Answer, Question Type, Question Number, Question Type, and User Name.
2. Fill in the optional fields: Character 1, Character 2, Character 3, Character 4, Character 5, User Email, User Phone, User Location, User Organisation, User Notes, and Time Taken.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Character 1 (Text)**: Enter the name of the first character in the Oedipus Rex story.
  * Optional field.
* **Character 2 (Text)**: Enter the name of the second character in the Oedipus Rex story.
  * Optional field.
* **Character 3 (Text)**: Enter the name of the third character in the Oedipus Rex story.
  * Optional field.
* **Character 4 (Text)**: Enter the name of the fourth character in the Oedipus Rex story.
  * Optional field.
* **Character 5 (Text)**: Enter the name of the fifth character in the Oedipus Rex story.
  * Optional field.
* **Correct Answer (Text)**: Enter the correct answer to the question.
  * Required field.
* **Incorrect Answer 1 (Text)**: Enter an incorrect answer 1 to the question.
  * Optional field.
* **Incorrect Answer 2 (Text)**: Enter an incorrect answer 2 to the question.
  * Optional field.
* **Incorrect Answer 3 (Text)**: Enter an incorrect answer 3 to the question.
  * Optional field.
* **Question Type (Select One)**: Select the type of question you are answering, which could be A, B, C, or D.
  * Required field.
* **Question Number (Number)**: Enter the question number.
  * Optional field.
* **Quiz Type (Select One)**: Select the type of quiz, which could be Oedipus Rex or Quizzes.
  * Required field.
* **User Email (Text)**: Enter your email address.
  * Optional field.
* **User Name (Text)**: Enter your name.
  * Required field.
* **User Notes (Text)**: Enter any additional notes about your experience with the quiz.
  * Optional field.
* **User Organisation (Text)**: Enter the name of your organisation.
  * Optional field.
* **Time Taken (Time)**: Enter the time it took you to complete the quiz.
  * Optional field.
* **User Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number.
  * Optional field.
* **User Location (Text)**: Enter your location.
  * Optional field.
* **User Comment (Text)**: Enter any additional comments about your experience with the quiz.
  * Optional field.
