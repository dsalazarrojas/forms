# Classmates Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Classmates Knowledge Quiz is designed to assess your knowledge on various subjects, including history, geography, science, and literature. It's a 15-question quiz that tests your understanding of different subjects, and you will be required to answer all questions to the best of your knowledge.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields.
2. Answer each question carefully, using the provided hints as a guide.
3. Make sure to choose the correct options for select-one fields.
4. Enter your confidence level and the difficulty of the quiz at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name to identify yourself.**
* **Student ID (`student_id`, text, optional): Enter your student identification number, if applicable.**
* **Class Period (`class_period`, select_one, required): Choose your class period from the provided options.**
* **Quiz Date (`quiz_date`, date, required): This field is automatically populated with the current date.**
* **Instructions (`section_header_instructions`, note, optional): A reminder to answer all questions carefully.**
* **Question 1 - What year was the United States founded? (`question_1`, number, required): Enter the year the United States was founded.**
* **Question 2 - Who was the first President of the United States? (`question_2`, text, required): Enter the full name of the first President of the United States.**
* **Question 3 - Which continent is the largest by area? (`question_3`, select_one, required): Choose the continent with the largest area from the provided options.**
* **Question 4 - What is the chemical symbol for gold? (`question_4`, text, required): Enter the two-letter symbol for gold.**
* **Question 5 - In what year did World War II end? (`question_5`, number, required): Enter the year World War II ended.**
* **Question 6 - How many bones are in the adult human body? (`question_6`, number, required): Approximate the number of bones in the adult human body.**
* **Question 7 - Which planet is closest to the sun? (`question_7`, select_one, required): Choose the planet closest to the sun from the provided options.**
* **Question 8 - What is the capital of France? (`question_8`, text, required): Enter the name of the capital of France.**
* **Question 9 - Who wrote Romeo and Juliet? (`question_9`, text, required): Enter the full name of the author of Romeo and Juliet.**
* **Question 10 - What is the smallest prime number? (`question_10`, number, required): Enter the smallest prime number.**
* **Question 11 - Which ocean is the largest? (`question_11`, select_one, required): Choose the largest ocean from the provided options.**
* **Question 12 - In what year did the Titanic sink? (`question_12`, number, required): Enter the year the Titanic sank.**
* **Question 13 - What is the speed of light? (`question_13`, number, required): Enter the speed of light in meters per second (approximately).**
* **Question 14 - Which country is home to the Great Wall? (`question_14`, text, required): Enter the name of the country with the Great Wall.**
* **Question 15 - How many continents are there? (`question_15`, number, required): Enter the number of continents.**
* **Confidence Level (`confidence_level`, select_one, optional): Choose how confident you are in your answers.**
* **Quiz Difficulty (`quiz_difficulty`, select_one, optional): Choose how difficult you found this quiz.**
