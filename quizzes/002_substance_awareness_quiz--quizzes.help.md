# substance awareness quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess a user's knowledge and attitudes towards substance use and awareness. It is intended to help users understand their level of awareness and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer them to the best of your ability.
2. Fill out all the required fields, marked with a (*).
3. Select the correct answer for each multiple-choice question.
4. If you are unsure or have questions, you can ask for help.
