# Physics Formula Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physics Formula Calculator is a tool designed to help you calculate various physical quantities using simple formulas. This form allows you to input values and formulas to get accurate results.

## How To Complete This Form
To use the Physics Formula Calculator, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of formula you want to calculate from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the necessary values for the input fields.
3. Fill out any additional input fields as required.
4. Submit the form to get your result.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_input** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information or context about your calculation.
* **formula_type** (Select, Required): Choose the type of formula you want to calculate, such as kinetic energy or potential energy.
* **input_1** (Number, Optional): Enter a value to use in your calculation (e.g., mass or velocity).
* **input_2** (Number, Optional): Enter another value to use in your calculation (e.g., acceleration or time).
* **result** (Note, Optional): This field displays the calculated result.
* **email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address to receive the calculation result.
* **phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number to receive a call or text with the calculation result.
* **date** (Date, Optional): Select a date to use in your calculation (e.g., for time-dependent calculations).
* **time** (Time, Optional): Select a time to use in your calculation.
* **user_input_2** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional context or information about your calculation.
* **select_1** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose additional options relevant to your calculation (e.g., units or systems).
* **select_2** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose more options relevant to your calculation (e.g., formulas or constants).
* **user_input_1** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information or context about your calculation.
* **user_input_3** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional context or information about your calculation.

Note: The exact fields and options available will depend on the selected formula type. Some fields may be required or optional based on the chosen formula.
