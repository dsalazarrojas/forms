# glycolysis_knowledge_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "glycolysis_knowledge_assessment" form is designed to evaluate students' knowledge about glycolysis, a process of cellular respiration. The form collects information about various aspects of glycolysis, including its steps, enzymes, ATP yield, and related equipment.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, select each field to provide the required information. You can select one or more options for each field, depending on your understanding of glycolysis.
*   Be sure to fill out each field carefully, as it will be used to assess your knowledge about glycolysis.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Glycolysis steps** (`glycolysis_steps`, Select Multiple, Optional): This field asks you to select the steps involved in glycolysis. You can choose one or more of the following options:
    *   Yes
*   **Glycolysis enzymes** (`glycolysis_enzymes`, Select One, Optional): This field asks you to select one enzyme involved in glycolysis. You can choose one of the following options:
    *   Option 1
    *   Option 2
    *   Option 3
*   **ATP yield** (`atp_yield`, Number, Optional): This field asks you to enter the amount of ATP yield in glycolysis.
*   **Glycolysis process** (`glycolysis_process`, Text, Optional): This field is for any additional information about the glycolysis process.
*   **Quiz type** (`quiz_type`, Select One, Optional): This field asks you to select the type of quiz you are taking. You can choose one of the following options:
    *   Option 1
    *   Option 2
    *   Option 3
*   **Glycolysis equipment** (`glycolysis_equipment`, Select One, Optional): This field asks you to select one piece of equipment related to glycolysis. You can choose one of the following options:
    *   Option 1
    *   Option 2
*   **Location** (`glycolysis_location`, Text, Optional): This field is for any additional location-related information about glycolysis.
*   **Instructor** (`glycolysis_instructor`, Text, Optional): This field is for the name of the instructor who taught the glycolysis-related content.
*   **Duration** (`glycolysis_duration`, Time, Optional): This field asks you to enter the duration of the glycolysis-related content.
*   **Frequency** (`glycolysis_frequency`, Number, Optional): This field asks you to enter the frequency of glycolysis-related content.
*   **Notes** (`glycolysis_notes`, Note, Optional): This field is for any additional notes about glycolysis.
*   **Email** (`glycolysis_email`, Email, Optional): This field is for the email address of the person who completed the form.

*   **Additional** (`glycolysis_additional`, Text, Optional): This field is for any additional information about glycolysis.
*   **Additional 2** (`glycolysis_additional2`, Text, Optional): This field is for any additional information about glycolysis.
