# Liver Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Liver Quiz is a tool used by healthcare professionals to assess a patient's liver health by asking a series of questions and selecting the most appropriate options. It is used to gather information about a patient's liver symptoms, conditions, and test results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. For the "Liver Symptoms" section, select all applicable symptoms that you are experiencing.
3. For the "Liver Conditions" section, select all applicable conditions that you are experiencing.
4. For the "Blood Tests" section, select one of the listed options as the result of your most recent blood test.
5. For the "Diagnosis" section, select the option that best describes your current liver diagnosis.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Liver Symptoms** (`liver_quiz_symptoms`, `text`, required/optional): List all applicable liver symptoms you are experiencing. 
* **Liver Conditions** (`liver_quiz_conditions`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all applicable liver conditions you are experiencing.
* **Blood Tests** (`liver_quiz_blood_tests`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the result of your most recent blood test.
* **Diagnosis** (`liver_quiz_diagnosis`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the option that best describes your current liver diagnosis.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any section, do not hesitate to ask for assistance from a medical professional.
* This form is for informational purposes only and should not be used as a substitute for a proper medical examination.
