# Metallurgy Heat Treatment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the user's knowledge of metallurgy heat treatment principles.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct answer to each question in the "quiz_questions" field.
2. Fill out the remaining fields as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Heat Treatment Processes** (`metallurgy_heat_treatment_quiz_form`): This is the title of the form.
* **What is heat treatment?** (`heat_treatment_processes`): A brief description of what heat treatment is.
* **What are the main steps of heat treatment?** (`heat_treatment_process_steps`): A list of the main steps of heat treatment.
* **What are some common heat treatment types?** (`metallurgy_heat_treatment_types`): A list of some common heat treatment types.
* **Thermodynamics of heat treatment** (`thermodynamics_of_heat_treatment`): A description of the thermodynamics of heat treatment.
* **Kinetics of heat treatment** (`kinetics_of_heat_treatment`): A description of the kinetics of heat treatment.
* **Heat transfer processes** (`heat_transfer_processes`): A description of heat transfer processes.
* **Mechanical properties of heat treated materials** (`mechanical_properties_of_heat_treated_materials`): A description of the mechanical properties of heat treated materials.
* **Surface finite element modeling** (`surface_finite_element_modeling`): A description of surface finite element modeling.
* **Microstructure evolution** (`microstructure_evolution`): A description of microstructure evolution.
* **Heat treatment equipment** (`heat_treatment_equipment`): A description of heat treatment equipment.
* **Applications of heat treatment** (`applications_of_heat_treatment`): A description of applications of heat treatment.
* **Heat treatment industry standards** (`heat_treatment_industry_standards`): A description of heat treatment industry standards.
* **Metallurgy Heat Treatment Quiz assessment** (`metallurgy_heat_treatment_quiz_assessment`): This is the assessment score out of.
* **Quiz questions** (`quiz_questions`): Select the correct answer to each question.
* **Assessment score** (`assessment_score`): This field is automatically calculated.
* **Quiz completion date** (`quiz_completion_date`): The date and time the quiz was completed.
* **Quiz completion time** (`quiz_completion_time`): The time taken to complete the quiz.
* **User comments** (`user_comments`): Any additional comments or feedback.
* **Submission status** (`submission_status`): The status of the submission.
* **Submission time** (`submission_time`): The time of submission.
* **Submission date** (`submission_date`): The date of submission.
* **Form completion status** (`form_completion_status`): The status of the form completion.
* **Assessment score out of** (`assessment_score_out_of`): The maximum score possible.
* **Submission comments** (`submission_comments`): Any additional comments or feedback.
* **Yes/No** (`quiz_questions`): Select yes or no for each question.
* **Active/Inactive** (`submission_status`/`form_completion_status`): Select active or inactive for each field.
* **Time** (`submission_time`/`quiz_completion_time`): Enter the time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
