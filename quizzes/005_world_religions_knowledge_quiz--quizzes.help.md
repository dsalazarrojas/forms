# World Religions Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create and manage educational quizzes, surveys, and programs related to world religions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Title** of the quiz in the "world_religions_knowledge_quiz" field.
2. Select the **Category** for the quiz from the options "Quizzes", "Surveys", "Exams".
3. Enter a **Description** of the quiz in the "quiz_description" field.
4. Enter the **Teacher's Email** and **Phone Number** for contact information.
5. Choose the **Lessons**, **Workshops**, and **Programs** that are part of the quiz, as applicable.
6. Enter the **Topics** related to the quiz, lessons, and programs.
7. Specify the **Submission Time** and **Submission Date**.
8. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **world_religions_knowledge_quiz** (text): Enter a title for the quiz.
* **quiz_category** (select_one): Select the category for the quiz from the options "Quizzes", "Surveys", "Exams".
* **quiz_description** (text): Enter a description for the quiz.
* **teacher_email** (email): Enter the email address of the teacher or administrator.
* **teacher_phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the teacher or administrator.
* **form_submitted** (date): Enter the date when the quiz was submitted.
* **submission_time** (time): Enter the time when the quiz was submitted.
* **lessons** (select_multiple): Choose the lessons related to the quiz.
* **workshops** (select_multiple): Choose the workshops related to the quiz.
* **programs** (select_multiple): Choose the programs related to the quiz.
* **lesson_topics** (text): Enter topics related to the lessons.
* **program_topics** (text): Enter topics related to the programs.
* **lesson_program_topics** (text): Specify topics related to lessons and programs.
* **assigned_tool** (text): Specify any tools assigned to the quiz.
* **lessons_workshops** (select_multiple): Choose the lessons and workshops related to the quiz.
* **lessons_workshops_interfaith** (select_multiple): Choose the lessons and workshops related to interfaith programs.
* **lessons_workshops_topics** (text): Specify topics related to lessons and workshops.
* **lesson_topics_workshops** (text): Specify topics related to lessons and workshops.
* **program_topics_interfaith** (text): Specify topics related to interfaith programs.
* **programs_workshops_interfaith** (text): Specify topics related to interfaith programs and workshops.
* **lesson_topics_interfaith** (text): Specify topics related to lessons and interfaith programs.
* **program_topics_lesson** (text): Specify topics related to programs and lessons.
* **program_topics_lesson_topics** (text): Specify topics related to programs and lessons.
* **program_topics_workshops** (text): Specify topics related to programs and workshops.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
* Review the form carefully before submission.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact the teacher or administrator.
