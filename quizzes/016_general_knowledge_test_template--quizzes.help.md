# General Knowledge Test Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to create a general knowledge test with 12 questions and answers. It can be used for educational purposes, such as creating quizzes for students, or for personal use, such as testing one's knowledge on a specific topic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for your quiz in the "Quiz Title" field.
2. For each question, enter the question text in the corresponding "Question" field.
3. For each answer, enter the correct answer in the corresponding "Answer" field.
4. Repeat steps 2 and 3 for all 12 questions.
5. Submit the form to generate your quiz.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Quiz Title**: A text field where you can enter a title for your quiz. This title will be displayed at the top of the quiz.
* **Question 1**: A text field where you can enter the first question of your quiz. 
* **Answer 1**: A text field where you can enter the correct answer for the first question.
* **Question 2**: A text field where you can enter the second question of your quiz.
* **Answer 2**: A text field where you can enter the correct answer for the second question.
* ... (Repeat the pattern for questions 3-12)

Note: All questions and answers are required fields. This means that you must fill in all 12 questions and answers to complete the form.
