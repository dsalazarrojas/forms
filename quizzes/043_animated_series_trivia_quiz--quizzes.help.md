# Animated Series Trivia Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animated Series Trivia Quiz is designed to test your knowledge on various animated TV shows. The form is divided into multiple sections, each focusing on a different series or concept.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with your answers.
2. Select the correct option from the provided list for each question.
3. If you have a favorite animated series, enter your answer in the "Favorite Animated Series of all time" field.
4. Submit the form to calculate your total score.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: This is a text field where you should enter your name, which will be used to identify you as a quiz taker. (Label: Participant Name, Type: text, Required: true)
* **Who lives in a pineapple under the sea?**: Select the correct character who lives in a pineapple under the sea. (Label: Who lives in a pineapple under the sea?, Type: select_one, Required: true)
* **What is the name of the dog in Family Guy?**: Choose the correct name of the dog in Family Guy. (Label: What is the name of the dog in Family Guy?, Type: select_one, Required: true)
* **Which animated series features a portal gun?**: Select the series that features a portal gun. (Label: Which animated series features a portal gun?, Type: select_one, Required: true)
* **The mystery solving gang travels in which vehicle?**: Choose the correct vehicle used by the mystery solving gang. (Label: The mystery solving gang travels in which vehicle?, Type: select_one, Required: true)
* **Name the primary avatar from the Last Airbender**: Select the main character from the Last Airbender series. (Label: Name the primary avatar from the Last Airbender, Type: select_one, Required: true)
* **What is the name of the futuristic city in The Jetsons?**: Enter the correct name of the city in The Jetsons. (Label: What is the name of the futuristic city in The Jetsons?, Type: text, Required: true)
* **Who is the main character of the Pokemon series?**: Enter the correct name of the main character from the Pokemon series. (Label: Who is the main character of the Pokemon series?, Type: text, Required: true)
* **Favorite Animated Series of all time**: If you have a favorite animated series, enter its name here. (Label: Favorite Animated Series of all time, Type: text, Required: false)
* **Total Score**: This is a calculated score after submitting the form. (Label: Total Score, Type: number, Required: false)
