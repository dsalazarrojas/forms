# Business English Placement Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess your English language skills in a business context. It includes questions on reading, writing, and speaking abilities, as well as your background and learning goals. The quiz is designed to help you identify areas where you need improvement and provide a basis for further language training or assessment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out each field carefully, taking about 5-7 minutes to complete.
2. Answer questions based on your actual experience and abilities in a business setting.
3. Select the best option for each question, using the scale provided (e.g., Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, Strongly Disagree).
4. You can select multiple options for questions that allow it (e.g., What are your main English learning goals?).
5. Type in your answer for questions that require text input (e.g., Areas needing most improvement).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Information
*   **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Your professional email address associated with your work or institution.
*   **Company or Organization** (`company_name`, `text`, **optional**): If you're an employee or associated with an organization, enter your company name.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your current job title or position.
*   **Is English Your Native Language?** (`native_language`, `select_one`, **required**): Select "Yes" if English is your native language; otherwise, select "No".
*   **How Long Have You Studied English?** (`english_study_duration`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the time frame that best describes your English study duration (Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 4-7 years, 8+ years, or Native speaker).
*   **How Often Do You Use English at Work?** (`english_use_frequency`, `select_one`, **required**): Select how frequently you use English in your work (Rarely, Sometimes, Frequently, Very Frequently, Always).
