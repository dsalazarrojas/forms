This form is designed to assess an individual's level of consciousness awareness, a state of being fully present and aware of one's thoughts, emotions, and physical sensations in the present moment. The quiz is a self-reporting assessment that asks participants to answer a series of questions regarding their experiences, habits, and tendencies related to awareness and mindfulness.

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

## Purpose

This form is an assessment tool to gauge your level of consciousness awareness. It is meant to be completed individually and will take approximately 10-15 minutes to complete.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each question carefully and select the response that best describes your typical experience or behavior.
2. For questions with a date field (e.g., "Quiz Date"), enter the date you complete this form.
3. For questions with a select_one field, select only one option that best fits your experience.
4. For questions with a select_multiple field (e.g., "Current Awareness Practices"), select all that apply.
5. For questions with a text field (e.g., "Insights Gained" and "Additional Comments"), provide a brief description or reflection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: This field is optional and allows you to provide your name for results delivery if desired.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address if you'd like to receive results or follow-up communications.
* **Quiz Date**: Enter the date you complete this form to help track your progress over time.
* **Question 1 - How often do you practice mindful awareness**: Select the response that best fits your experience, including how often you practice meditation, mindfulness exercises, or other activities that promote mindfulness.
* **Question 2 - When experiencing strong emotions**: Select your usual response when faced with strong emotions, including how you typically react or manage them.
* **Question 3 - Awareness of bodily sensations**: Select the level of awareness you experience regarding bodily sensations throughout the day.
* **Question 4 - Conversational habits**: Choose how you usually behave during conversations, including whether you tend to listen fully or get distracted.
* **Question 5 - Awareness of thoughts as mental events**: Select how often you notice your thoughts as separate from reality.
* **Question 6 - Awareness while moving**: Choose your typical level of awareness when walking or engaging in physical activities.
* **Question 7 - Moments of pure presence**: Select how often you experience moments of pure, unadulterated awareness.
* **Question 8 - Eating habits**: Choose your typical behavior while eating, including whether you tend to savor each bite or get distracted.
* **Question 9 - Awareness of present moment**: Select the best description of your awareness of the present moment.
* **Question 10 - Relationship with inner experience**: Choose how you typically relate to your thoughts, feelings, and experiences.
* **Question 11 - Stressful situations**: Select your typical response to stressful situations, including whether you tend to remain centered or become reactive.
* **Question 12 - Awareness of breathing**: Choose your level of awareness regarding your breath throughout the day.
* **Question 13 - Observer of experience**: Select the best description of your experience as the observer of your experiences.
* **Question 14 - Automatic patterns**: Choose how often you notice yourself engaging in automatic, habitual patterns.
* **Question 15 - Gap between stimulus and response**: Select the best description of your experience regarding the gap between stimulus and response.
* **Self-Rated Awareness Level**: Rate your overall awareness on a scale of 1-10.
* **Interest in Developing Awareness**: Select your level of interest in deepening your awareness.
* **Current Awareness Practices**: Select all practices you currently engage in to promote awareness.
* **Insights Gained**: Provide any reflections or insights you gained from completing this quiz.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional thoughts or comments you'd like to share.

By completing this form, you will have a better understanding of your current level of consciousness awareness and can reflect on areas for growth and development.
