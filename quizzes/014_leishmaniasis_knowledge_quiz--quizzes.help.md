# leishmaniasis_knowledge_quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form, "leishmaniasis_knowledge_quiz", is designed to assess users' knowledge about Leishmaniasis, a disease caused by the Leishmania parasite. The form aims to gather information about users' understanding of the disease's characteristics and symptoms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "start_quiz" page, which serves as an introduction to the form.
2. On the next page, select the correct answer to the question "Leishmaniasis is" by choosing between "True" and "False".
3. On the next page, choose all the symptoms of Leishmaniasis that apply to you from the list: "skin lesions", "skin fever", or "other".
4. On the final page, fill in the correct prevalence of Leishmaniasis, if applicable.
5. End the form by filling out the "end_quiz" page, which serves as a completion note.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **start_quiz (Note)**: This is the starting page of the form, and it serves as an introduction to the form's questions.
- **Leishmaniasis is (Select One)**: Choose whether Leishmaniasis is a true or false condition.
  - If you select "True", it means you think Leishmaniasis is a real disease with the described symptoms.
  - If you select "False", it means you are unsure or disagree with the existence of the disease.
- **Symptoms of Leishmaniasis (Select Multiple)**: Choose all the symptoms you have experienced or are familiar with.
  - **Skin lesions**: Skin lesions are a common symptom of Leishmaniasis.
  - **Skin fever**: Skin fever is also a symptom of Leishmaniasis.
  - **Other**: If you have experienced other symptoms not listed, choose this option.
- **Leishmaniasis Prevalence (Number)**: If you know the prevalence of Leishmaniasis, fill in the correct number. If you do not know, you can leave this field blank.
- **end_quiz (Note)**: This is the completion page of the form, where you can add any additional comments or remarks about your experience with Leishmaniasis.
