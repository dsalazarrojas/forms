# Continuous Improvement Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your knowledge and understanding of Lean and Kaizen concepts, analysis tools, and improvement techniques. It is intended to help individuals identify areas of improvement and provide feedback on training materials.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled in.
2. Select the correct answers to each question based on your understanding of the concepts.
3. For each field, follow the specific instructions for selecting an answer from the options provided.
4. Ensure you complete all required fields, as indicated by the "required" label for each field.
5. Once you have completed all fields, submit your responses for review and feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Assessment Participant --** (`participant_header`, `note`, required: false): This field is a title for the participant section of the form, indicating that the following questions are related to the participant's assessment.
* **Full Name** (`participant_full_name`, `text`, required: true): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Department** (`participant_department`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the department you belong to from the provided options: Quality Assurance, Operations/Production, Human Resources, Information Technology, or Supply Chain.
* **-- Part 1 - Lean and Kaizen Concepts --** (`section_1_concepts`, `note`, required: false): This section provides an introduction to the next set of questions, which cover Lean and Kaizen concepts.
* **What does the PDCA cycle stand for** (`question_pdca_cycle`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the correct answer from the options: Plan - Do - Check - Act, Process - Design - Control - Analyze, Perform - Deliver - Create - Assess, or Prepare - Deploy - Change - Audit.
* **What is the literal translation of 'Kaizen'** (`question_kaizen_meaning`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the correct answer from the options: Sudden Change, Good Change (Continuous Improvement), Quality Control, or Error Proofing.
* **Which of these is NOT one of the 'Seven Wastes' (TIMWOOD)** (`question_seven_wastes`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the option that does not belong to the 'Seven Wastes' (TIMWOOD): Transportation, Inventory, Innovation, or Overprocessing.
* **-- Part 2 - Analysis Tools --** (`section_2_analysis`, `note`, required: false): This section introduces the next set of questions, which cover analysis tools.
* **Which tool is most effective for identifying a root cause** (`question_root_cause_tool`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the best answer from the options: Gantt Chart, The 5 Whys, Pareto Chart (80/20 Rule), or SWOT Analysis.
* **A Fishbone Diagram is also known as a** (`question_fishbone_diagram`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the correct answer from the options: Flowchart, Ishikawa Diagram, Control Chart, or Histogram.
* **-- Training Feedback --** (`section_3_feedback`, `note`, required: false): This section is for providing feedback on the improvement training material.
* **How clear was the improvement training material** (`training_clarity_rating`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate the clarity of the improvement training material on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Date of Completion** (`quiz_completion_date`, `date`, required: true): Enter the date you completed this quiz in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Internal Quiz Reference Code** (`quiz_id_reference`, `text`, required: false): This field is for internal reference and tracking purposes only.

## Tips

* Ensure you answer each question to the best of your knowledge and understanding.
* If you are unsure or unsure of the answer, please choose the best option provided.
* If you need further clarification on any question, please consult the provided resources or ask your supervisor for guidance.
* Once completed, submit your responses for review and feedback.
