# flamenco_basics_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The flamenco_basics_quiz form is designed to collect basic information about a student's or teacher's experience with Flamenco, a traditional dance style from Spain.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the introductory questions in the "intro_questions" section.
2. Provide your name, student's name, and other required demographic information.
3. Answer the quiz questions, selecting "Yes" or "No" for each question.
4. Submit your answers to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **intro_questions** (text, required): Answer the introduction questions to get started with the quiz.
* **name** (text, required): Enter your name or the student's name.
* **student_info** (text, required): Enter information about the student, such as their grade level or age.
* **student_phone** (text, required): Enter the student's phone number.
* **teacher_info** (text, required): Enter information about the teacher, such as their years of experience or qualifications.
* **school_info** (text, required): Enter information about the school or institution where Flamenco is being taught.
* **school_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the school or institution.
* **teacher_name** (text, required): Enter the teacher's name.
* **student_level** (text, required): Select the student's level of study, such as beginner or advanced.
* **score** (number, required): Enter a score or rating for your answer to the previous questions.
* **submit** (select multiple, required): Select "Yes" if you have experience with Flamenco, or "No" otherwise.
* **teacher_experience** (select one, required): Select "True" if the teacher has experience with Flamenco, or "False" otherwise.
