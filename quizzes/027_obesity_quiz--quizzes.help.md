# Obesity Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a person's general health, medical history, measurements, health outcomes, and assessment related to obesity. It is intended to be used for educational purposes or as a starting point for further medical consultation. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following sections:

### General Information
* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, required: false): This section is for providing any general information about yourself that you feel is relevant to your health. You can write a brief description of your condition, any medications you're currently taking, or other relevant details.

### Medical History
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): In this section, you can describe your medical history, including any previous surgeries, medical conditions, or allergies.

### Measurements
* **Measurements** (`measurements`, text, required: false): This section is for recording any measurements that are relevant to your health, such as height, weight, or body mass index (BMI).

### Health Outcomes
* **Health Outcomes** (`health_outcomes`, text, required: false): Describe any health outcomes or results you've experienced due to your medical condition or treatment.

### Assessment
* **Assessment** (`assessment`, text, required: false): In this section, you can provide your assessment or evaluation of your current health condition, including any concerns or worries you may have.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed and honest as possible when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about any information, you can always ask for help or clarification.
* This form is for educational purposes only and should not be used for medical diagnosis or treatment. If you're experiencing any health issues, please consult a medical professional.
