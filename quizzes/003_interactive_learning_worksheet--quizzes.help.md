# Interactive Learning Worksheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Interactive Learning Worksheet is a tool designed for students to interact with educational content in a structured format. It allows teachers to create a worksheet with lesson title, description, quiz questions, and space for students to submit their answers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the Category: Choose the category of the lesson, such as Quizzes, Essays, or Projects.
2. Enter the Lesson Title: Type in the title of the lesson.
3. Enter your Name (Teacher) and the Student Name: These fields are meant to track who is creating and interacting with the worksheet.
4. Upload a Lesson Image (optional): If you want to add a visual element to the worksheet, upload an image related to the lesson.
5. Add a Lesson Link (optional): Share a link to a relevant resource or website related to the lesson.
6. Enter Student Responses: This field is meant for students to submit their answers or responses to the quiz questions.
7. Enter Quiz Questions: Type in the quiz questions that students will be answering.
8. Enter Quiz Options (optional): This field is meant for additional information about the quiz, such as the number of points or time limit.
9. Enter Student Answer: This field is meant for students to submit their answers to the quiz questions.
