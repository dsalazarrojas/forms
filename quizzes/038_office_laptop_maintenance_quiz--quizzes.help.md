# Office Laptop Maintenance Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Office Laptop Maintenance Quiz is designed to test your knowledge on the correct practices for maintaining office laptops. This quiz has multiple pages with a mix of multiple-choice and single-choice questions, and each question is crucial in keeping your office equipment in good working order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the correct option for each question.
2. Make sure to review each answer before proceeding to the next page.
3. Review the results page for a final confirmation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Page** (`first_page`, `note`, optional): This is the introduction page. It is not required to fill out this page.
* **Laptop Maintenance** (`second_page`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all correct maintenance practices for office laptops.
* **Maintenance Quiz** (`third_page`, `select_one`, required): Answer "correct" if you are certain about the maintenance practice.
* **Fourth Page** (`fourth_page`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct maintenance interval for office laptops.
* **Results** (`fifth_page`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments about your experience with office laptop maintenance.
* **Results Page** (`results_page`, `select_one`, optional): Select "correct" if you have confidence in your maintenance practices.
* **Submit** (`submit_section`, `text`, optional): Enter your name or a message for the form submitter.
* **Submit Label** (`submit_label`, `text`, optional): Enter a label for the submit button.
* **Submit Button** (`submit_button`, `text`, optional): Enter the text for the submit button.
* **Submission** (`submit_section_2`, `note`, optional): This is the end of the form. Please review your answers before submitting.
* **Submit 2** (`submit_2_section`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have reviewed all answers before submitting.
* **End of Form** (`end_of_form`, `note`, optional): This is the final page of the form.
