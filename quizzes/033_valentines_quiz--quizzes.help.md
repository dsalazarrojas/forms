# Valentines Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a fun and informative quiz about Valentine's Day, covering its history, trivia, and modern-day traditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and choose the answer that best fits the description.
2. Select one answer from the list of options provided for each question.
3. You can skip questions that you're not sure or uncomfortable answering.
4. Click the submit button to review and submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (Text Field): A brief introduction to get you started on the quiz.
* **Valentines History** (Text Field): Share your thoughts on the history of Valentine's Day.
* **Valentines Trivia** (Select One Question):
	+ **What is the origin of Valentine's Day?**: Choose one of the provided options that best describes the origin of Valentine's Day.
	+ **Who is often credited with inventing the first Valentine's Day card?**: Select one of the provided options that best answers this question.
	+ **Which of the following is NOT a common gift associated with Valentine's Day?**: Choose one of the provided options that best answers this question.
* **Valentines Dating** (Select One Question):
	+ **Before the 1950s, what was the most common way for couples to celebrate Valentine's Day?**: Select one of the provided options that best answers this question.
	+ **In the early 20th century, what was the name given to a woman who was considered a good catch?**: Choose one of the provided options that best answers this question.
