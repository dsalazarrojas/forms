# Accounting And VAT Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to test your knowledge on accounting and VAT concepts, including double-entry bookkeeping, financial statements, VAT definitions, and more.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, which are marked as `required`.
2. Answer all multiple-choice questions by selecting the correct option from the list.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (`participant_name`, `text`, `required`): This is the field where you will enter your full name.
* **Email Address (`email`, `email`, `required`): This is the field where you will enter your email address.
* **Your Role (`role`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose your role from the list: Student, Accountant, Business owner, Finance professional, Tax specialist, or Other.
* **In double-entry bookkeeping, every transaction affects at least (`q1_double_entry`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the correct answer: One account, Two accounts, Three accounts, or Four accounts.
* **A debit increases which of the following account types (`q2_debit_increase`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the correct account type: Revenue, Liabilities, Assets, or Equity.
* **Which financial statement shows revenue and expenses over a period (`q3_income_statement`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the correct financial statement: Balance sheet, Income statement, Cash flow statement, or Statement of equity.
* **Accounts payable is classified as a (`q4_accounts_payable`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the correct classification: Current asset, Current liability, Long-term liability, or Equity.
* **What is the purpose of a trial balance (`q5_trial_balance`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the correct purpose: To calculate profit, To verify debits equal credits, To file taxes, or To determine cash flow.
* **What does VAT stand for (`q6_vat_stands_for`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the correct abbreviation: Value Added Tax, Variable Assessment Tax, Vendor Applied Tariff, or Volume Adjusted Tax.
* **VAT is classified as what type of tax (`q7_vat_type`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the correct tax type: Direct tax, Indirect tax, Capital gains tax, or Property tax.
* **Input VAT refers to (`q8_input_vat`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the correct meaning: Tax charged on sales, Tax paid on purchases, Tax refund from the government, or Tax on employee wages.
* **A business VAT liability is calculated as (`q9_vat_liability`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the correct calculation: Total sales multiplied by VAT rate, Output VAT minus input VAT, Input VAT minus output VAT, or Revenue minus expenses.
* **Zero-rated supplies mean (`q10_zero_rated`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the correct meaning: No VAT is charged and no input VAT can be reclaimed, VAT is charged at 0% but input VAT can be reclaimed, The item is exempt from all taxes, or The seller absorbs the VAT cost.
* **How Would You Rate the Difficulty of This Quiz (`difficulty_rating`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the difficulty level: Very easy, Easy, Moderate, Difficult, Very difficult.
* **Additional Comments or Feedback (`additional_comments`, `text`, `optional`): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have.
