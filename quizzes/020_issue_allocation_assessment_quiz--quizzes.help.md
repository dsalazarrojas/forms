# issue-allocation-assessment-quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The issue-allocation-assessment-quiz form is used to assess the skills and expertise of team members in various areas and scenarios. This quiz helps identify individual and collective strengths and weaknesses, aiding in the development and implementation of team projects and resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all team members who are being assessed.
2. For each selected team member, choose the type of issue they are best suited for.
3. Provide a score from 0 to 100 for the team member's confidence in resolving the issue.
4. Choose a level of confidence (low, medium, high) for the team member's confidence.
5. Provide a text description of the allocation scenario for the selected team member.
6. Enter the correct allocation (a numerical value) for the team member.
7. Enter the actual allocation (a numerical value) for the team member.
8. Choose a high or low confidence score for how confident the team member is about the correct allocation.
9. If needed, add comments about the selected team member's performance.
10. Add any additional comments about the team member's performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select multiple, required): Select the team members being assessed. You can choose one or multiple team members, as needed.
* **Issue Type** (`issue_type`, select one, required): Choose the type of issue the team member is best suited for. Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
* **Confidence Score** (`confidence_score`, text, required): Enter a score from 0 to 100 representing the team member's confidence in resolving the issue.
* **Confidence Level** (`confidence_level`, note, required): Choose a level of confidence (low, medium, high) for the team member's confidence.
* **Allocation Scenario** (`allocation-scenario`, text, required): Provide a text description of the allocation scenario for the selected team member.
* **Correct Allocation** (`correct_allocation`, number, required): Enter the correct allocation (a numerical value) for the team member.
* **Actual Allocation** (`actual_allocation`, number, required): Enter the actual allocation (a numerical value) for the team member.
* **Confidence in Correct Allocation** (`confidence_score_again`, select one, required): Choose a high or low confidence score for how confident the team member is about the correct allocation.
* **Correct Allocation Comments** (`correct_allocation_again_comments`, text, required): Add comments about the selected team member's performance.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required): Add any additional comments about the team member's performance.
