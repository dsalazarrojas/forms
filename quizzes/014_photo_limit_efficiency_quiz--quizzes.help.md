# Photo Limit Efficiency Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you assess your photo limit efficiency by asking a series of questions about photo quality, storage efficiency, and system settings.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply go through each page and answer the questions to the best of your ability. You can skip questions that do not apply to your situation or are not relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photo Quality** (`photo_quality`, select_multiple, optional): This question asks about the quality of the photos you typically upload. Please choose the options that best describe your photo quality.
  * High: High-quality photos with good resolution and color depth.
  * Medium: Average quality photos with some resolution and color depth.
  * Low: Low-quality photos with poor resolution and color depth.
* **Storage Efficiency** (`storage_efficiency`, select_one, optional): This question asks about how efficiently you store photos on your system. Please choose the option that best describes your storage efficiency.
  * Very Efficient: You store photos very efficiently, with minimal space used.
  * Somewhat Efficient: You store photos somewhat efficiently, but with some wasted space.
  * Not Very Efficient: You store photos inefficiently, with a lot of wasted space.
* **Balance** (`balance`, text, optional): This question asks for your personal balance between photo quality and storage efficiency. Please enter a number that best describes your balance.
* **Balance Note** (`balance_note`, note, optional): This question allows you to add any additional information about your balance.
* **How many photos** (`photo_count`, number, optional): This question asks about the number of photos you typically upload.
* **Storage Limit** (`storage_limit`, number, optional): This question asks about the storage limit of your system.
* **Upload Speed** (`upload_speed`, number, optional): This question asks about the upload speed of your system.
* **Resolution** (`resolution`, select_one, optional): This question asks about the resolution of the photos you typically upload. Please choose the option that best describes your resolution.
  * High: High-resolution photos (e.g., 4K or higher).
  * Medium: Medium-resolution photos (e.g., 2K or lower).
  * Low: Low-resolution photos (e.g., 1K or lower).
* **Aspect Ratio** (`aspect_ratio`, select_one, optional): This question asks about the aspect ratio of the photos you typically upload. Please choose the option that best describes your aspect ratio.
  * 61: 16:9 (Widescreen).
  * 121: 9:16 (Phone or Tablet).
  * 241: 1:1 (Square).
* **Color Depth** (`color_depth`, select_one, optional): This question asks about the color depth of the photos you typically upload. Please choose the option that best describes your color depth.
  * True Color: Photos with a full color range (e.g., 24-bit or higher).
  * Color: Photos with a reduced color range (e.g., 8-bit or lower).
  * Black & White: Monochrome photos.

* **Storage Space** (`storage_space`, number, optional): This question asks about the storage space available on your system.
