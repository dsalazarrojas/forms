# Halloween Movie Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your favorite Halloween movies, allowing you to test your knowledge and score your favorite films in a few key areas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering the title of your favorite Halloween movie in the `movie-title` field.
2. Next, select the release year of your favorite movie from the list of provided options in the `release-year` field.
3. The `genre` field allows you to choose multiple genres that your favorite movie belongs to. Select all that apply.
4. In the `director` field, select the director of your favorite movie from the list of options provided.
5. You can add a screenshot or a description of your favorite movie in the `movie-screenshot` field.
6. Score your favorite movie in the following categories: 
    * score-1
    * score-2
    * score-3
7. Finally, select all scores that you agree with for your favorite movie in the `movie-scores` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### movie-title (<name>: Movie Title)
* **movie-title (text)**: Enter the title of your favorite movie.

### release-year (<name>: Release Year)
* **release-year (number)**: Select the release year of your favorite movie.

### genre (<name>: Genre)
* **genre (select_multiple)**: Select all genres that apply to your favorite movie.

### director (<name>: Director)
* **director (select_one)**: Select the director of your favorite movie.

### movie-screenshot (<name>: Movie Screenshot)
* **movie-screenshot (text)**: Add a screenshot or a description of your favorite movie.

### score-1 (<name>: Score 1)
* **score-1 (number)**: Score your favorite movie in the first category.

### score-2 (<name>: Score 2)
* **score-2 (number)**: Score your favorite movie in the second category.

### score-3 (<name>: Score 3)
* **score-3 (number)**: Score your favorite movie in the third category.

### movie-scores (<name>: Movie Scores)
* **movie-scores (select_multiple)**: Select all scores that you agree with for your favorite movie.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and have fun while completing this form.
* Ensure that you select all scores that accurately reflect your opinion.
* You can add a screenshot or a description of your favorite movie in the `movie-screenshot` field to help you remember the movie. 
* This form is designed to be fun and engaging, so don't hesitate to ask if you have any questions or need help. 
* Make sure to review your answers before submitting to ensure you are accurate.
