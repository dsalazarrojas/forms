# WhatsYourLoveLanguageQuiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you discover your love language and that of your partner. By answering the questions, you will gain insight into how you and your partner express and receive love.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your love language from the options provided in the "whats_your_love_language" field.
2. Answer the questions about how much you use giving, receiving, quality time, words of affirmation, and physical touch to show love in the corresponding fields.
3. For each field, indicate how much you spend on average per month (in a scale from 0-100) on your partner and how much your partner spends on you.

	+ **Giving**: I like to do things for my partner.
	+ **Receiving**: I like to receive thoughtful gestures from my partner.
	+ **QualityTime**: I value spending time with my partner.
	+ **WordsOfAffirmation**: I like to give kind words to my partner.
	+ **PhysicalTouch**: I like to show love physically.
	+ **Gifts**: I like to give gifts to my partner.
* **HowMuchDoesYourPartnerUseGivingToShowLove**: How much does your partner spend on you to show love (in 0-100 scale)?
* **HowMuchDoYouUseGivingToShowLove**: How much do you spend on your partner to show love (in 0-100 scale)?
* **HowMuchTimeDoYouSpendWithYourPartnerInQualityTime**: How much time do you spend with your partner in quality time (in 0-100 scale)?
* **HowMuchDoesYourPartnerUseWordsOfAffirmationToShowLove**: How much does your partner use words of affirmation to show love (in 0-100 scale)?
