# Clothing Vocabulary Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This quiz is designed to test your knowledge of clothing vocabulary. Answer each question to the best of your ability, and you'll be asked about your confidence level at the end.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Your Name" field.
2. Enter the date and time of completion in the "Quiz Date" field.
3. Answer each question according to the options provided.
4. At the end, select your confidence level about clothing vocabulary.

* **Class Period - Grade** (`class_period`, text, optional): If you're using this form in a class setting, enter your class period and grade level.
* **Quiz Date** (`quiz_date`, date, required): Enter the date and time of completion.
