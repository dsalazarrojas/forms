# Food Hygiene Course Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge your understanding and practices related to food hygiene, food safety, and risk control measures. It will help assessors evaluate your knowledge and adherence to food safety standards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully.
2. Answer every question that applies to you, but do not answer questions that do not apply.
3. Select all applicable answers for multiple-choice questions.
4. Enter your responses in the text fields.
5. Make sure to answer all questions marked as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Food Hygiene Course Assessment Form

* **food_hygiene_course_assessment_form** (1, text, optional): This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or information about your food hygiene course experience.
* **food_safety_practice** (2, select_one, optional): Please select "Yes" if you consistently follow good food safety practices, "No" otherwise.
* **understanding_food_safety** (3, select_multiple, optional): Select all "Yes" if you have a good understanding of food safety principles and practices.
* **risk_control_measures** (4, text, optional): Describe the risk control measures you take to ensure food safety.
* **food_hygiene_practice** (5, select_one, optional): Please select "Yes" if you follow good food hygiene practices, "No" otherwise.
* **food_safety_practice** (6, text, optional): Describe your approach to food safety practices.
* **high_hygiene_standards** (7, select_multiple, optional): Select all "Yes" if you maintain high standards of food hygiene.
* **assessment_form** (8, text, optional): This is a text field for any additional comments or observations you have about your food hygiene course assessment.
