# Workplace Culture Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about employee perception of their workplace culture.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
4. Enter your department in the "Department" field.
5. Enter your years of service in the "Years of Service" field.
6. Enter your manager's name in the "Manager Name" field.
7. Enter your supervisor's name in the "Supervisor Name" field.
8. Enter your supervisor's phone number in the "Supervisor Phone" field.
9. Enter your supervisor's email address in the "Supervisor Email" field.
10. Enter your supervisor's department in the "Supervisor Department" field.
11. Enter any feedback about your workplace culture in the "Feedback" field.
12. Select your manager's approval in the "Manager Approve" field.
13. Assess your team's leadership, communication, trust, decision-making, time management, problem-solving, adaptability, work-life balance, innovation, results ownership, collaboration, and overall performance in the "Team Leadership", "Communication", "Trust", "Decision Making", "Time Management", "Problem Solving", "Adaptability", "Work Life Balance", "Innovation", "Results Ownership", "Collaboration", and "Overall Performance" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, Optional): This is the field to enter your name as an employee.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): This is the field to enter your email address.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, Optional): This is the field to enter your job title.
* **Department** (`department`, text, Optional): This is the field to enter your department.
* **Years of Service** (`years_of_service`, number, Optional): This is the field to enter your years of service.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, Optional): This is the field to enter your manager's name.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, Optional): This is the field to enter your supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, text, Optional): This is the field to enter your supervisor's phone number.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, Optional): This is the field to enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Supervisor Department** (`supervisor_department`, text, Optional): This is the field to enter your supervisor's department.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, Optional): This is the field for any feedback about your workplace culture.
* **Manager Approve** (`manager_approve`, select_one, Optional): This is the field for your manager's approval of your performance.
* **Team Leadership** (`team_leadership`, select_multiple, Optional): This assesses your team's leadership.
* **Communication** (`communication`, select_multiple, Optional): This assesses your team's communication.
* **Trust** (`trust`, select_multiple, Optional): This assesses your team's trust.
* **Decision Making** (`decision_making`, select_multiple, Optional): This assesses your team's decision-making.
* **Time Management** (`time_management`, select_multiple, Optional): This assesses your team's time management.
* **Problem Solving** (`problem_solving`, select_multiple, Optional): This assesses your team's problem-solving.
* **Adaptability** (`adaptability`, select_multiple, Optional): This assesses your team's adaptability.
* **Work Life Balance** (`work_life_balance`, select_multiple, Optional): This assesses your team's work-life balance.
* **Innovation** (`innovation`, select_multiple, Optional): This assesses your team's innovation.
* **Results Ownership** (`results_ownership`, select_multiple, Optional): This assesses your team's results ownership.
* **Collaboration** (`collaboration`, select_multiple, Optional): This assesses your team's collaboration.
* **Overall Performance** (`overall_performance`, select_multiple, Optional): This assesses your team's overall performance.
