# Energy Saving Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help homeowners assess their energy consumption and identify potential areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions to the best of your ability. You can refer to your energy bills and records to provide accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What does your home's energy consumption look like right now?** (`home_energy_consumption`, text, optional): This question helps us understand your current energy usage.
* **How much electricity do you currently use at home?** (`electricity_consumption`, number, optional): Please provide your current electricity usage in units of kilowatt-hours (kWh) or kilowatt-hours per day (kWh/d).
* **How much natural gas do you currently use at home?** (`gas_consumption`, number, optional): Please provide your current natural gas usage in units of cubic meters (m^3) or therms.
* **What is the cost of your electricity bill?** (`electricity_cost`, number, optional): Please provide the total cost of your electricity bill in your local currency.
* **What is the cost of your gas bill?** (`gas_cost`, number, optional): Please provide the total cost of your gas bill in your local currency.
* **What type of heating system do you have in your home?** (`heating_system`, select_one, optional): This question helps us understand your heating system.
* **What type of cooling system do you have in your home?** (`cooling_system`, select_one, optional): This question helps us understand your cooling system.
* **How well insulated is your home?** (`insulation`, select_one, optional): This question helps us understand your home's insulation levels.
* **Do you have double glazing in your home?** (`double_glass`, select_multiple, optional): This question helps us understand if you have double glazing.
* **Do you use smart home devices in your home?** (`smart_home_devices`, select_multiple, optional): This question helps us understand if you use smart home devices.
* **Do you have solar panels installed on your home?** (`solar_panels`, select_multiple, optional): This question helps us understand if you have solar panels installed.
* **Do you use renewable energy sources in your home?** (`renewable_energy_sources`, select_multiple, optional): This question helps us understand if you use renewable energy sources.
* **Are you offsetting your carbon footprint?** (`carbon_offsetting`, select_multiple, optional): This question helps us understand if you are offsetting your carbon footprint.
* **Do you have an Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) for your home?** (`eco_certification`, select_one, optional): This question helps us understand if you have an EPC for your home.
* **Have you taken energy efficiency measures for your home?** (`energy_efficiency_measures`, select_multiple, optional): This question helps us understand if you have taken energy efficiency measures.
* **Have you implemented the energy efficiency measures?** (`energy_efficiency_measures_implement`, select_multiple, optional): This question helps us understand if you have implemented the energy efficiency measures.
* **Do you have an energy-saving plan for your home?** (`energy_saving_plan`, select_one, optional): This question helps us understand if you have an energy-saving plan for your home.
* **What is your energy-saving goal?** (`energy_savings_goal`, select_one, optional): This question helps us understand your energy-saving goal.
* **What is your energy-saving timeframe?** (`energy_savings_timeframe`, select_one, optional): This question helps us understand when you plan to achieve your energy-saving goal.
* **What is your target energy saving percentage?** (`energy_savings_target`, text, optional): This question helps us understand your target energy saving percentage.
* **What energy-saving measurements do you plan to take?** (`energy_saving_measurements`, text, optional): This question helps us understand your energy-saving measurements.
* **What are your next steps?** (`next_steps`, text, optional): This question helps us understand your next steps.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): This question allows you to provide additional comments.
* **What measures do you recommend to reduce energy consumption?** (`recommended_measures`, text, optional): This question allows you to provide measures to reduce energy consumption.
* **Submission status** (`submission_status`, select_one, optional): This question allows you to confirm the submission status of your form.
