# Online MCQ Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Online MCQ Assessment form is designed to test a user's knowledge and understanding of a subject by presenting multiple-choice questions. This form is intended for individuals who are taking an assessment or test, and it is not meant for multiple selections or open-ended answers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the text question on **Question 1**. This question is a basic question that requires a brief answer.
2. Choose only one answer for the **Select One** question on **Select One**. Select one option that best fits your response.
3. Choose multiple answers for the **Select Multiple** question on **Select Multiple**. Select as many options as applicable to your response.
4. Enter a date in the format `mm/dd/yyyy` for the **Date** question on **Date**.
5. Enter a time of day in the format `HH:MM AM/PM` for the **Time** question on **Time**.
6. Enter your email address in the format `username@domain.com` for the **Email** question on **Email**.
7. Enter your phone number without any special characters or symbols for the **Phone** question on **Phone**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Question 1** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter your answer to the question.
* **Select One** (Select One, Required/Optional): Choose only one option that best fits your response.
* **Select Multiple** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose multiple options that apply to your response.
* **Date** (Date, Required/Optional): Enter a date in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Time** (Time, Required/Optional): Enter a time of day in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Email** (Email, Required/Optional): Enter your email address in the format `username@domain.com`.
* **Phone** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter your phone number without any special characters or symbols.
