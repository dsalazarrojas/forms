# spanish_verb_conjugation_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The spanish_verb_conjugation_quiz is a tool designed to evaluate your understanding of Spanish verb conjugation rules. It consists of three questions, each with multiple-choice options, that assess your knowledge of the correct answers to different verb forms.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete the quiz, start by reading each question carefully and selecting the correct answer from the options provided.
2. For each question, select one correct answer (for question types: select_one) or multiple correct answers (for question types: select_multiple) from the options.
3. Once you have answered all questions, click the "Submit" field to submit your answers.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure you have answered each question correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **question_1** (Select One: yes/no): This question tests your understanding of a specific aspect of Spanish verb conjugation. Please answer with the correct response based on your knowledge.
* **answer_1_label** (Select Multiple: yes/no): This field is not a user-facing field and is used to store the correct answer for the quiz.
* **question_2** (Select One: yes/no): This question assesses your understanding of another aspect of Spanish verb conjugation. Please answer with the correct response based on your knowledge.
* **answer_2_label** (Select Multiple: yes/no): This field is not a user-facing field and is used to store the correct answer for the quiz.
* **question_3** (Select One: yes/no): This question evaluates your understanding of a third aspect of Spanish verb conjugation. Please answer with the correct response based on your knowledge.
* **answer_3_label** (Select Multiple: yes/no): This field is not a user-facing field and is used to store the correct answer for the quiz.
* **submission** (Text): This is the submission field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about your answers. Please use this field to provide any additional context or explanations for your answers.
* **welcome_to_the_quiz** (Text): This field is a welcome message and does not require a response.
