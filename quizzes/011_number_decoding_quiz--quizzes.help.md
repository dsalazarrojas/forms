# Number Decoding Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess a student's number decoding skills. It is intended for educational purposes only.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose the correct starting point for the quiz from the options provided.
2. Provide answers to the questions or perform the task related to number decoding.
3. Choose the end point of the quiz from the options provided.
4. Enter any scores or results from the quiz (if applicable).
5. Enter student information, including name, class, school, and teacher.
6. Select the type of quiz and level of difficulty.
7. Choose who submitted the quiz (usually the student themselves).
8. Review and submit the form for processing.
