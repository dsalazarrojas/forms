# Food Delivery Agent Quiz Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from food delivery agents about their experiences and preferences to help improve our food delivery service.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, follow the instructions below for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **About the food delivery service**: This field is for sharing a brief story about a positive experience you've had with our food delivery service. Please keep it concise and descriptive.
* **Have you ever had an issue with an order?**: If yes, describe what happened. If not, select "No" or write "None" in the field.
* **How would you rate the quality of our food?**: Please select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Neutral, or Bad, based on your overall experience with the quality of food from our service.
* **How would you rate your experience with our customer service?**: Select a number from 1 to 5 (1 is the lowest, 5 is the highest), where 1 means "Very Poor" and 5 means "Excellent".
* **What is the most important factor for you when choosing a food delivery service?**: Select all that apply from: Price, Speed of delivery, Quality of food. If there's another important factor for you, choose "Other" and provide a brief explanation.
* **Do you use our food delivery service?**: Select "True" if you have used our service at least once, "False" otherwise.
* **How often do you use our food delivery service?**: Select the frequency you use our service: Daily, Several times a week, Occasionally, Rarely.
* **Form Footer**: This field is not for your response, but a message to acknowledge that you have read and completed the form.
