# public_law_knowledge_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Law Knowledge Quiz is a form designed to test the general knowledge of individuals in the field of public law. This form is not intended to be used in real-world legal settings, but rather as a educational tool to gauge the understanding of public law concepts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the different pages of the form, filling in the required information for each page. The form consists of 8 different pages, each with its own set of fields to fill in. You can complete the form at your own pace, and save your progress as you go along.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Knowledge** (`general_knowledge`, text, optional): This field is a free-text field where you can provide any information that you think is relevant to your knowledge of public law.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): This field is for entering your date of birth, which is a common piece of information required in many legal contexts.
- **Select Option 1** (`select_option_1`, select_one, optional): This field is a multiple-choice question where you can select one of the options provided. Please choose the option that best reflects your knowledge or understanding of public law.
- **Select Option 2** (`select_option_2`, select_multiple, optional): This field is also a multiple-choice question, but you can select multiple options from the list.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): This field is for entering your email address, which is an important piece of contact information.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): This field is for entering your phone number, which is another way to contact you.
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional): This field is a free-text field where you can provide any additional notes or comments about your answers.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): This field is not currently applicable, but it's likely intended for a time-based question or constraint. Please disregard this field for now.

## Tips
If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to contact us for clarification. Remember to fill in all required fields, as this will help us get a more accurate assessment of your knowledge. You can save your progress at any time, and come back to the form later to complete it. Have fun!
