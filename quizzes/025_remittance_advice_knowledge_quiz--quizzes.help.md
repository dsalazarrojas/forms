# Remittance Advice Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remittance Advice Knowledge Quiz is a tool designed to test your understanding of remittance advice concepts. This quiz will assess your knowledge of remittance advice, its types, and its application in financial transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and select the correct answer from the provided options.
2. Enter your name and email address in the designated fields.
3. Submit your answers for scoring.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **staff_name**: This field is for your name. Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **email**: Enter your email address. This will be used for communication purposes if your quiz results are shared with you.
* **select_question1**: Which of the following is an example of remittance advice?
	+ Option 1: Payment instructions from the seller to the buyer.
	+ Option 2: Invoice with payment terms.
	+ Option 3: Bank draft.
* **select_question2**: Which of the following is another example of remittance advice?
	+ Option 1: Payment terms on an invoice.
	+ Option 2: Payment schedule.
	+ Option 3: Bank statement.
* **answer1**: Enter your answer to the first question.
* **answer2**: Enter your answer to the second question.
* **quiz_results**: Select the correct outcome of your quiz.
	+ Option 1: Passed.
	+ Option 2: Failed.
* **score**: Your score will be automatically calculated based on your answers.
* **email2**: This field is not used in this form. Please ignore it.
* **submit**: Click this button to submit your quiz results for scoring.
