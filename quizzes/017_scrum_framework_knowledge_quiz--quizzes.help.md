# scrum_framework_knowledge_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The scrum_framework_knowledge_quiz form is designed to evaluate your knowledge of the Scrum framework, a popular agile project management methodology. It covers various aspects of Scrum, including its core values, team roles, phases, and framework roles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for each field based on your knowledge of the Scrum framework.
2. For fields with multiple options, select all that apply to you.
3. For fields with single-select options, choose the one that best represents your understanding.
4. Enter any additional comments or observations you have at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false)
This is an introductory question that allows you to set the context for your answers.

### 2. **Scrum Values** (`scrum_values`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Scrum is built around a set of values that include:
* Focus on customer satisfaction
* Agile principles
* Continuous improvement
* Self-organization

Choose all that apply to your knowledge of Scrum values.

### 3. **Team Roles** (`team_roles`, `select_one`, required: false)
Select the role that best describes your understanding of team roles in Scrum:
* Product Owner
* Scrum Master
* Development Team Member
* Team Member

### 4. **Team Structure** (`team_structure`, `select_one`, required: false)
Choose the team structure that aligns with your understanding of Scrum:
* Team with a single Product Owner
* Team with multiple Product Owners
* Team with a separate Scrum Master
* Other (please specify)

### 5. **Scrum Phases** (`scrum_phases`, `select_one`, required: false)
Select the phase that you are most familiar with:
* Sprint Planning
* Daily Scrum
* Sprint Review
* Other (please specify)

### 6. **Scrum Framework Roles** (`scrum_framework_roles`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Choose all roles that you are familiar with:
* Product Owner
* Scrum Master
* Development Team Member
* Other (please specify)

### 7. **Agile Training Outcomes** (`agile_training_outcomes`, `select_one`, required: false)
Select the outcome that you are most familiar with:
* Improved team collaboration
* Better time estimation
* Increased customer satisfaction
* Other (please specify)

### 8. **Form Completion** (`form_completion`, `number`, required: false)
Enter the amount of time it took you to complete this form.

### 9. **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false)
Enter any additional comments or observations you have about the form or the Scrum framework.
