# Health And Wellbeing Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health And Wellbeing Quiz is a short, self-assessment tool designed to provide an initial indication of an individual's overall wellbeing. It helps identify potential areas that may benefit from attention or support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all that apply for each question.
2. Choose one category for your wellbeing score.
3. Answer the wellbeing question questions honestly.

* **Wellbeing Quiz Questions** (`wellbeing_quiz_questions`, select_multiple, not required):

* **Name** (`name`, text, not required):

* **Wellbeing Score** (`wellbeing_score`, number, not required):
