# rick and morty trivia - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to test your knowledge about the popular animated series "Rick and Morty". It will ask you a series of questions about your favorite characters, the show's history, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your favorite character from the options provided in question 1.
2. Answer question 2 with a brief description of your favorite character.
3. Provide a brief answer to question 3, specifying the year the series was first released.
4. Choose the main villain from the options provided in question 4.
5. Answer question 5 with a brief description of your favorite episode.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your favorite character** (`Rick`, `select_one`, **required**):
	+ This question asks you to choose your favorite character from the show "Rick and Morty". Select the character that you think is the most interesting or relatable to you.
* **Question 1** (`question 1`, `text`, **required**):
	+ This is a text field where you can answer any question about the show that comes to mind. Be as creative as you can be!
* **Answer 1** (`answer 1`, `text`, **required**):
	+ This field is similar to question 1, where you can provide another answer about the show.
* **What is Rick and Morty** (`rick and morty`, `select_one`, **required**):
	+ This question asks you to confirm the title of the show "Rick and Morty" itself. Select the correct answer to proceed.
* **What year was the series first released** (`question 3`, `date`, **required**):
	+ This question asks for the year when the show "Rick and Morty" first aired. Enter the year you think it was released.
* **What is the main villain** (`Mr. Hate`, `select_one`, **required**):
	+ This question asks you to choose the main villain from the options provided. Select the one you think is the most iconic or terrifying.
* **What is your favorite episode** (`question 5`, `text`, **required**):
	+ In this text field, you can describe your favorite episode of "Rick and Morty". Be as specific as you can be, and have fun with it!
