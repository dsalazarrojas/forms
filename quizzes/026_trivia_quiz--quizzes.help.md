# Trivia Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to test your knowledge of trivia questions and provide a score at the end.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Trivia Quiz form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each trivia question to the best of your ability.
2. Enter your answers for each question in the corresponding text fields.
3. Review your answers and submit the form to receive your score at the end.
