# Community Project Evaluation Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Community Project Evaluation Quiz, a form designed to test your knowledge about community project evaluation. Please review the form carefully before starting, as it contains 15 questions that assess your understanding of various concepts related to project evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and email address in the corresponding fields, as these are required for results delivery.
2. Select your role and experience level with project evaluation from the provided options.
3. Answer each question by selecting the most appropriate option from the choices provided.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting, as they will be used to score your quiz.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Your Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
- **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to receive results delivery.
- **Your Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required): Choose your role from the options: Project Manager, Program Coordinator, Volunteer, Community Member, Student, or Other.
- **Experience with Project Evaluation** (`experience_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose your level of experience with project evaluation from the options: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert.
- **Question 1 - What is the primary purpose of project evaluation** (`question_1`, `select_one`, required): Choose the best answer from the options: To justify funding, To assess effectiveness and improve outcomes, To find faults, or To complete paperwork.
- **Question 2 - Which of the following is a quantitative data collection method** (`question_2`, `select_one`, required): Choose one from the options: Focus groups, Surveys with rating scales, Open-ended interviews, or Case studies.
- **Question 3 - What does SMART stand for in goal setting** (`question_3`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct definition from the options: Simple, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound, Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound, Specific, Meaningful, Actionable, Realistic, Timely, or Strategic, Measurable, Attainable, Relevant, Trackable.
- **Question 4 - When should evaluation planning begin** (`question_4`, `select_one`, required): Choose the best answer from the options: At the end of the project, During project implementation, At the beginning of project planning, or After funding is secured.
- **Question 5 - What is a logic model** (`question_5`, `select_one`, required): Choose the best description from the options: A computer program for evaluation, A visual representation of how a program works, A type of survey, or A budget template.
- **Question 6 - Which is an example of an outcome measure** (`question_6`, `select_one`, required): Choose one from the options: Number of workshops held, Number of participants attending, Improved knowledge or behavior change, or Hours of staff time.
- **Question 7 - What is formative evaluation** (`question_7`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct definition from the options: Evaluation at the end of a project, Evaluation conducted during implementation to improve, Evaluation of financial records, or Evaluation by external reviewers.
- **Question 8 - What is summative evaluation** (`question_8`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct definition from the options: Ongoing assessment during the project, Assessment of overall impact at project end, Budget review, or Staff performance evaluation.
- **Question 9 - Why is stakeholder engagement important in evaluation** (`question_9`, `select_one`, required): Choose the best answer from the options: It is required by funders, It ensures evaluation addresses relevant questions and increases buy-in, It makes the report longer, or It reduces costs.
- **Question 10 - What is a baseline measurement** (`question_10`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct definition from the options: The final measurement, The initial measurement before intervention, The average of all measurements, or The minimum acceptable score.
- **Question 11 - Which sampling method gives every member an equal chance of selection** (`question_11`, `select_one`, required): Choose one from the options: Convenience sampling, Random sampling, Purposive sampling, or Snowball sampling.
- **Question 12 - What is triangulation in evaluation** (`question_12`, `select_one`, required): Choose the best definition from the options: Using three evaluators, Using multiple methods or data sources, Creating three reports, or Measuring three outcomes.
- **Question 13 - What is response rate** (`question_13`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct definition from the options: How quickly surveys are returned, The percentage of people who respond to a survey, The number of questions answered, or The speed of data analysis.
- **Question 14 - Which is a qualitative data analysis method** (`question_14`, `select_one`, required): Choose one from the options: Statistical regression, Thematic analysis, Mean calculation, or Correlation analysis.
- **Question 15 - What should an evaluation report include** (`question_15`, `select_one`, required): Choose the most comprehensive answer from the options: Only positive findings, Executive summary, methodology, findings, conclusions, and recommendations, Just the budget, or Only participant quotes.
- **Are You Ready to Submit** (`submit_quiz`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you are ready to submit your quiz, Yes - Submit My Quiz, or No - I Need to Review.
- **Would You Like Detailed Feedback** (`feedback_request`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you would like detailed feedback, Yes - Send Me Feedback, or No - Just My Score.
- **Comments About This Quiz** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any comments about the quiz, please enter them here.
