# Volunteer Award Category Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to categorize volunteers based on their award category. The fields in this form are meant to gather specific information about the volunteer's experience and help determine the most suitable category for their award.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose a category from the options under "Choose a category" that best describes your volunteer work.
2. Select a recognition group from the options under "Choose a recognition group" that aligns with your volunteer work.
3. For "Why did you volunteer?", select the reason(s) that best fit your experience.
4. If you have volunteered at any point in time, select the date of your volunteer work.
5. If you have volunteered for a specific time period, enter the time of your volunteer work.
6. Enter the number of hours you volunteered.
7. Enter the amount of time you volunteered.
8. Select how often you typically volunteer.
9. Enter any additional comments or notes about your volunteer experience.
10. Enter your email address (if you wish to be contacted about your volunteer work).
11. Enter your phone number (if you wish to be contacted about your volunteer work).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Choose a category**: Select the category that best describes your volunteer work. This will help us determine which award category you are eligible for.
* **Choose a recognition group**: Select the recognition group that aligns with your volunteer work. This will help us determine which award category you are eligible for.
* **Why did you volunteer?**: Select all the reasons why you volunteered. This will help us determine which category your award belongs in.
* **When did you volunteer?**: If you have volunteered at any point in time, select the date of your volunteer work. This will help us determine when your volunteer work took place.
* **What time did you volunteer?**: If you have volunteered for a specific time period, enter the time of your volunteer work. This will help us determine the specific time of your volunteer work.
* **How many hours did you volunteer?**: Enter the number of hours you volunteered. This will help us determine the amount of time you committed to your volunteer work.
* **How much time did you volunteer?**: Enter the amount of time you volunteered. This will help us determine the amount of time you committed to your volunteer work.
* **How often do you volunteer?**: Select how often you typically volunteer. This will help us determine your level of commitment to volunteer work.
* **Additional comments or notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your volunteer experience. This will help us determine any other relevant information about your volunteer work.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address (if you wish to be contacted about your volunteer work). This will allow us to contact you about your award.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number (if you wish to be contacted about your volunteer work). This will allow us to contact you about your award.
* **Other notes**: This field is optional, and you can add any additional notes or comments about your volunteer experience.
