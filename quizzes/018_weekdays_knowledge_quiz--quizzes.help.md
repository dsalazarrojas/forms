# Weekdays Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about weekdays knowledge quizzes. The form allows users to select the best weekday and enter scores for each day of the week, along with any comments and the person who submitted the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the best weekday from the drop-down menu.
2. Enter a score for each day of the week (Monday to Friday).
3. Enter any comments about the week.
4. Select the person who submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Best Weekday**: Select the best weekday from the available options (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
* **Score for Monday** (`score_monday_1`): Enter a numerical score for Monday, indicating its importance or value.
* **Score for Tuesday** (`score_tuesday_1`): Enter a numerical score for Tuesday, indicating its importance or value.
* **Score for Wednesday** (`score_wednesday_1`): Enter a numerical score for Wednesday, indicating its importance or value.
* **Score for Thursday** (`score_thursday_1`): Enter a numerical score for Thursday, indicating its importance or value.
* **Score for Friday** (`score_friday_1`): Enter a numerical score for Friday, indicating its importance or value.
* **Comments** (`comments_1`): Enter any comments or notes about the week, including any observations or thoughts.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by_1`): Select the person who submitted the form, from the provided options (John, Jane, Jimmy).
