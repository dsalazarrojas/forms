# Pessimism And Transformation Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals assess their current level of pessimism and transformation, as well as set goals for future personal growth. The form's results can be used by coaches, therapists, or counselors to identify areas where the individual may need support and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the following questions and select your choices from the drop-down menu as needed:

1. Introduction - This is a text field for any information you'd like to share about your background, goals, or context that might be relevant to the rest of the form.
2. Current Pessimism Level - Please select a number from 0-10 to indicate your current level of pessimism.
3. Self Reflection - Describe your current state of mind and how you feel about your life.
4. Current Outlook - Describe your current outlook on life, including any challenges or difficulties you're facing.
5. Transformation Goals - Describe your goals for personal growth and transformation.
6. Current Motivation - Describe your current motivation and drive.
7. Future Pessimism Level - Please select a number from 0-10 to indicate your expected level of pessimism in the future.
8. Future Outlook - Describe your expected outlook on life, including any challenges or difficulties you might face.
9. Future Motivation - Describe your expected motivation and drive.
10. Follow-up Resources - Select all the resources you would like to receive follow-up on, such as Coaching Sessions, Resources, or Other.
11. Follow-up Resources Other - If you selected "Other" in the previous field, please specify what you would like to receive follow-up on.
12. Notes - Leave any additional comments or notes about your answers in this field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Text): This field is for you to provide any context or background information about yourself.
* **Current Pessimism Level** (Number): This field is for you to select your current level of pessimism on a scale from 0-10, where 0 is minimal pessimism and 10 is extreme pessimism.
* **Self Reflection** (Text): This field is for you to describe your current state of mind and how you feel about your life.
* **Current Outlook** (Text): This field is for you to describe your current outlook on life, including any challenges or difficulties you're facing.
* **Transformation Goals** (Text): This field is for you to describe your goals for personal growth and transformation.
* **Current Motivation** (Text): This field is for you to describe your current motivation and drive.
* **Future Pessimism Level** (Number): This field is for you to select your expected level of pessimism on a scale from 0-10, where 0 is minimal pessimism and 10 is extreme pessimism.
* **Future Outlook** (Text): This field is for you to describe your expected outlook on life, including any challenges or difficulties you might face.
* **Future Motivation** (Text): This field is for you to describe your expected motivation and drive.
* **Follow-up Resources** (Select Multiple): This field is for you to select all the resources you would like to receive follow-up on, such as Coaching Sessions, Resources, or Other.
* **Follow-up Resources Other** (Text): This field is for you to specify what you would like to receive follow-up on if you selected "Other" in the previous field.
* **Notes** (Note): This field is for you to leave any additional comments or notes about your answers.
