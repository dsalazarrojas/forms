# Materials Accounting Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information about a user's materials accounting knowledge by asking for their first name, last name, email, phone number, category of interest, date completed, time completed, and any additional notes. This form is not intended for sensitive or personal data.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select the category that best describes your interest from the "Category" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the date you completed the quiz in the "Date Completed" field.
7. Enter the time you completed the quiz in the "Time Completed" field.
8. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Note" field.
9. Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the "Submit" button to submit your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name here. (First Name, text, required: false)
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name here. (Last Name, text, required: false)
* **Email**: Enter your email address here. This will help us contact you if we need more information. (Email, email, required: false)
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number here. This will help us contact you if we need more information. (Phone Number, text, required: false)
* **Category**: Select the category that best describes your interest from the dropdown menu. (Category, select_one, required: false)
* **Date Completed**: Enter the date you completed the quiz. (Date Completed, date, required: false)
* **Time Completed**: Enter the time you completed the quiz. (Time Completed, time, required: false)
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes here. (Note, note, required: false)
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your information. (Submit, text, required: false)

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure that your information is accurately recorded.
* Use the "Category" dropdown menu to select the category that best describes your interest.
* Be as specific as possible when entering your first name, last name, email, and phone number.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, please add them in the "Note" field.
