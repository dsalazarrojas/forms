# Symbiotic Relationships Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This quiz is designed to test your understanding of symbiotic relationships between living organisms in different ecosystems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of relationship between the organisms (mutualism, commensalism, or parasitism) from the dropdown menu for "relationship_type".
2. Choose all the ecosystems where the organisms interact (terrestrial, freshwater, or oceanic).
3. Indicate if the first organism has a symbiont (yes or no).
4. Indicate if the second organism has a symbiont (yes or no).
5. Answer yes or no to whether a relationship exists between the organisms.
6. Answer yes or no to whether the first organism has an impact on the second organism.
7. Answer yes or no to whether the second organism has an impact on the first organism.
8. Answer each of the open-ended questions (question1, question2, question3, question4, question5, question6, question7, question8) in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **relationship_type**: Select the type of relationship between the organisms (mutualism, commensalism, or parasitism).
* **ecosystem**: Choose all the ecosystems where the organisms interact (terrestrial, freshwater, or oceanic).
* **symbiont1**: Indicate if the first organism has a symbiont (yes or no).
* **symbiont2**: Indicate if the second organism has a symbiont (yes or no).
* **relationship**: Answer yes or no to whether a relationship exists between the organisms.
* **impact**: Answer yes or no to whether the first organism has an impact on the second organism.
* **symbiont1_impact**: Answer yes or no to whether the second organism has an impact on the first organism.
* **question1**, **question2**, **question3**, **question4**, **question5**, **question6**, **question7**, **question8**: Answer each of these open-ended questions in the space provided.
