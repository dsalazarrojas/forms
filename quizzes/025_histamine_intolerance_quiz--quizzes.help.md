# Histamine Intolerance Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps users identify whether they might be experiencing histamine intolerance, a condition characterized by symptoms like hives, stomach cramps, and fatigue after consuming certain foods.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and choose the answer that best describes your experience.
2. Answer all questions, even if you don't think your symptoms are related to histamine intolerance.
3. Submit your responses to get an assessment of your symptoms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `text`, optional): This is a brief introduction to the quiz. Please take a few minutes to read it carefully before answering the questions. 
* **Do you get hives or itching after eating** (`Very frequent`, `Some of the time`, `Never`, optional): This question asks about your experience of hives or itching after eating. If you never get these symptoms, select "Never".
* **Do you get stomach cramps or diarrhea after eating** (`Very frequent`, `Some of the time`, `Never`, optional): This question asks about your experience of stomach cramps or diarrhea after eating. If you never get these symptoms, select "Never".
* **Do you experience bloating and gas after eating** (`Very frequent`, `Some of the time`, `Never`, optional): This question asks about your experience of bloating and gas after eating. If you never get these symptoms, select "Never".
* **Do you get fatigue after eating** (`Very frequent`, `Some of the time`, `Never`, optional): This question asks about your experience of fatigue after eating. If you never get these symptoms, select "Never".

## Tips
* Please answer all questions, even if you don't think your symptoms are related to histamine intolerance.
* If you have already been diagnosed with histamine intolerance, do not fill out this form.
* If you have a doctor's diagnosis, do not fill out this form.
