# Body Language Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Body Language Quiz is designed to test your understanding and familiarity with various body language cues. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your participant name.
2. Select your level of familiarity with body language (Beginner to Expert).
3. Answer each multiple-choice question with the option that best matches your understanding of the body language cue.
4. You may leave additional comments or insights in the "Additional Comments" field.
