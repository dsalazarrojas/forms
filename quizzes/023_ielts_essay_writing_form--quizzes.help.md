# IELTS Essay Writing Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The IELTS Essay Writing Form is used to assess and improve English writing skills by providing a platform for users to submit essays on a given question. The form is designed to be completed by individuals who want to evaluate their writing skills, identify areas of improvement, and get feedback on their essays. The information collected from this form will help users and instructors to assess the quality of their essays and suggest areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read the provided essay question carefully and think about your response.
2.  Type your answer to the essay question in the "Essay Answer" field.
3.  If you want to provide feedback on your essay, you can do so in the "Comments" field.
4.  To provide feedback on areas where you need improvement, choose from the options listed in the "Improvement Areas" field or type your own comments in the "Other Areas" field.
5.  Finally, rate your essay score out of 100 (if you want to) and provide any comments in the "Score Comment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Essay Question** (`essay_question`, text, required): This field is where you will find the essay question that you need to answer. Read it carefully and think about your response.
*   **Essay Answer** (`essay_answer`, text, required): Type your answer to the essay question in this field.
*   **Score** (`essay_score`, number, optional): If you want to rate your essay score out of 100, you can do so in this field. This is optional.
*   **Improvement Areas** (`essay_improvement_areas`, select_multiple, required): This field provides you with options to select areas where you think you need improvement. You can choose from:
	+ Yes
	+ No
*   **Other Areas** (`essay_improvement_areas_other`, text, optional): If there are areas of improvement not listed, you can type them in this field.
*   **Comments** (`essay_comments`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide feedback or comments on your essay.
*   **Score Comment** (`essay_score_comment`, text, optional): If you provided a score for your essay, you can add comments to explain your score in this field.
*   **Score Out of** (`essay_score_out_of`, number, optional): This field is used to specify the maximum score for your essay.
*   **Total Score** (`essay_total_score`, number, optional): This field will display the total score of your essay based on your input.
*   **Improvement Areas List** (`essay_improvement_areas_list`, text, optional): This field is used to provide a list of areas where you need improvement.
