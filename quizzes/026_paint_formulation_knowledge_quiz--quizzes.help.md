# paint_formulation_knowledge_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a knowledge quiz for paint formulators to test their knowledge of paint formulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, select the correct answers to each question based on your knowledge of paint formulations. Be sure to read each question carefully and choose the best answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, not required): A brief introduction to the quiz.
* **paint_type** (`paint_type`, `select_one`, not required): Select the type of paint you are familiar with. This may include options like Waterborne, Solventborne, Solventless, High-Pressure, Powder.
* **binder_selection** (`binder_selection`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select one or more binders you have experience with. Options include Resin, Latex, Alkyd, Epoxy, Acrylic, Polyester, Urethane, Urethane/Alkyd, Polyurethane, Other.
* **solvent_selection** (`solvent_selection`, `select_one`, not required): Select the solvent you are most familiar with. This may include options like Solvent, Water, Organic.
* **filler_selection** (`filler_selection`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select one or more fillers you have experience with. Options include Silica, Calcium Carbonate, Talc, Silica/Silica, Other.
* **colorants_selection** (`colorants`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select one or more colorants you have experience with. Options include Pigments, Dyes, Interlayers, Other, Pint.
* **finish_selection** (`finish`, `select_one`, not required): Select the finish type you are most familiar with. This may include options like Matte, Gloss, Semi Gloss.
* **pigment_selection** (`pigment`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select one or more pigments you have experience with. Options include Titanium Dioxide, Iron Oxide, Carbon Black, Other.
* **primer_selection** (`primer`, `select_one`, not required): Select the type of primer you are most familiar with. This may include options like True, False, Not Applicable.
* **filler_ratio** (`filler ratio`, `number`, not required): Enter the ratio of filler you are most familiar with.
* **colorants_ratio** (`colorants ratio`, `number`, not required): Enter the ratio of colorants you are most familiar with.
* **color_value** (`color value`, `number`, not required): Enter the color value you are most familiar with.
* **filler_value** (`filler value`, `number`, not required): Enter the filler value you are most familiar with.
* **gloss_level** (`gloss`, `number`, not required): Enter the gloss level you are most familiar with.
* **thickness** (`thickness`, `number`, not required): Enter the thickness you are most familiar with.
* **temperature** (`temperature`, `number`, not required): Enter the temperature you are most familiar with.
* **finish_type** (`finish type`, `select_one`, not required): Select the finish type you are most familiar with. This may include options like Matt, Semi-gloss, High-Gloss.
* **paint_type_2** (`Paint Type 2`, `select_one`, not required): Select the type of paint you are most familiar with. This may include options like Waterborne, Solventborne, Solventless, High-Pressure, Powder.
* **finish** (`finish (2)`, `select_one`, not required): Select the finish type you are most familiar with. This may include options like Matte, Gloss, Semi Gloss.
* **colorants** (`colorants (2)`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select one or more colorants you have experience with. Options include Pigments, Dyes, Interlayers, Other.
* **other_info** (`other info`, `text`, not required): Enter any other relevant information.
* **submission** (`submission`, `select_one`, not required): Select whether this is a submission or not.
* **submitter** (`submitter`, `text`, not required): Enter the submitter's name.
* **submitter_info** (`submitter info`, `text`, not required): Enter any other relevant information about the submitter.
* **submitter_name** (`submitter name`, `text`, not required): Enter the submitter's name.

## Tips
Please answer each question carefully and honestly based on your experience and knowledge of paint formulations. This quiz is designed to assess your knowledge, not to test your ability to complete a form. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
