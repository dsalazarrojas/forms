# Basic Concepts Of Calculus Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to assess your understanding of basic calculus concepts, specifically limits, derivatives, integrals, and their applications. It is divided into six sections, each focusing on a specific aspect of calculus.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form in its entirety, answering all questions in each section.
2. For questions that require a numerical answer, enter your answer in the format specified (e.g., x^2 + 3x + 2).
3. For questions that require a descriptive answer, provide a clear and concise explanation in the provided text box.
4. Review your answers carefully to ensure they are accurate and complete.
5. If you need help or clarification on any question, feel free to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, `text`, optional): If applicable, please enter your student ID number.
* **Course name** (`course_name`, `text`, optional): If taking a calculus course, please enter the name of your course.
* **Quiz date** (`quiz_date`, `date`, required): The date you are taking this quiz.
* **What is the limit of (x+2) as x approaches 3?** (`limit_concept`, `integer`, required): Enter the limit value as x approaches 3.
* **Choose the best definition of a limit** (`limit_definition`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct definition of a limit from the provided options.
* **What does a derivative represent?** (`derivative_meaning`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct meaning of a derivative from the provided options.
* **Which notation represents a derivative?** (`derivative_notation`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct notation for a derivative from the provided options.
* **What is the derivative of x^3?** (`power_rule`, `text`, required): Enter the derivative of x^3.
* **The product rule states d/dx[f(x)g(x)] equals:** (`product_rule`, `text`, optional): Enter the derivative of the product of two functions f(x) and g(x) using the product rule.
* **What does an integral represent?** (`integral_meaning`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct meaning of an integral from the provided options.
* **What is the difference between definite and indefinite integrals?** (`integral_types`, `text`, optional): Enter the difference between definite and indefinite integrals.
* **What is the integral of 2x?** (`simple_integral`, `text`, required): Enter the integral of 2x.
* **For a function to be continuous at point c, it must:** (`continuity_definition`, `text`, optional): Enter a condition for a function to be continuous at a point.
* **Which is NOT a type of discontinuity?** (`discontinuity_types`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of discontinuity that is NOT a correct answer.
* **Use calculus concepts to find critical points** (`optimization`, `text`, optional): Enter how you would apply calculus concepts to find critical points in a problem.
* **If position is s(t) = 3t^2 - 2t, find velocity at t=2** (`rate_problem`, `text`, optional): Enter the velocity at t=2 using the given position function.
* **How would you rate this quiz difficulty?** (`overall_difficulty`, `select_one`, optional): Select the difficulty level of this quiz.
* **Confidence in your answers** (`confidence_level`, `select_one`, optional): Select your level of confidence in your answers.
