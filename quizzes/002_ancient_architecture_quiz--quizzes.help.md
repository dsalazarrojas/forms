# Ancient Architecture Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your knowledge of ancient architecture from around the world. It consists of ten questions that cover various structures, styles, and techniques used in the past. The goal is to test your understanding of the historical context, materials, and features of ancient architectural wonders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Participant Email" field for results delivery.
3. Optionally, you can mention your institution or organization you represent, but it's not required.
4. Read the introduction, "Welcome to the Ancient Architecture Quiz," and understand that you'll be selecting the best answer for each question.
5. Answer each question by selecting the correct option from the provided choices.
6. For questions with multiple correct answers (e.g., "Which architectural feature is characteristic of Gothic cathedrals"), select all that apply.
7. When you've completed all questions, rate the difficulty of the quiz as "Very Easy," "Easy," "Moderate," "Difficult," or "Very Difficult" to help us improve.

* **The Pantheon in Rome is famous for its** (10, select_one, required): Choose the correct answer.
