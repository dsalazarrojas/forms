# plant_and_human_transport_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data on the relationship between plant and human transportation, including whether transportation is used in plants and human transportation for education, tutoring, or homeschooling purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the questions carefully and answer them accurately.
2. Select all that apply from the options provided.
3. Fill in all the required fields.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **plant_transport** (select multiple): This field asks whether plants use transportation. Select all the options that apply.
* **human_transport** (select one): This field asks whether humans use transportation. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **transport_in_plant_and_human** (select one): This field asks whether transportation is used in both plants and human transportation. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **science_classes_tutoring_and_homeschool_evaluation** (select multiple): This field asks whether transportation is used in science classes, tutoring, or homeschooling. Select all the options that apply.
* **fast_online_data_collection** (select multiple): This field asks about the speed of data collection. Select all the options that apply.

## Tips
* Please be accurate when answering the questions, as the data collected will be used to understand the relationship between plant and human transportation.
* If you are unsure about any question, select the option that best describes your experience or lack thereof.
* Please fill in all the required fields to ensure that your answers are complete and accurate.
