# Historical Resistance Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help users complete the Historical Resistance Quiz, which aims to gather information about historical resistance movements. It helps to categorize and provide details about these movements in a structured and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the historical resistance movement in the `historical_resistance_quiz` field.
2. Choose the category of the movement from the `quiz_category` dropdown menu.
3. Provide a brief description of the movement in the `movement_description` field.
4. Enter the date range associated with the movement in the `date_range` field (in the format of a date, e.g., `1850-1900`).
5. Answer the quiz questions in the `quiz_question`, `quiz_question_1`, and `quiz_question_2` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **historical_resistance_quiz** (`historical_resistance_quiz`, text, required): Enter the title of the historical resistance movement.
* **quiz_category** (`quiz_category`, select_one, required): Select the category of the movement from the dropdown menu.
	+ This field helps to narrow down the type of movement (e.g., resistance movements, other).
* **movement_description** (`movement_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the movement.
* **date_range** (`date_range`, date, optional): Enter the date range associated with the movement (in the format of a date, e.g., `1850-1900`).
* **quiz_question** (`quiz_question`, text, optional): Answer the first quiz question.
* **quiz_question_1** (`quiz_question_1`, text, optional): Answer the second quiz question.
* **quiz_question_2** (`quiz_question_2`, text, optional): Answer the third quiz question.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form carefully, as the information will be used to categorize and provide details about historical resistance movements.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, feel free to contact us.
