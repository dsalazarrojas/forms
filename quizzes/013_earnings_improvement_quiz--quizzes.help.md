# Earnings Improvement Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Earnings Improvement Quiz is designed to help users assess their current earnings situation, identify areas for improvement, and set realistic goals for achieving their desired income level.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly, considering your current earnings situation.
2. Choose the most accurate option for each question, based on your understanding.
3. Review and adjust your answers as needed, ensuring that they reflect your actual earnings circumstances.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Income Goals** (`income_goals`, number, optional): Enter your desired annual income level.
* **Financial Readiness** (`financial_readiness`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose all options that apply to your current financial situation:
		- Yes (you have an emergency fund)
		- No (you do not have an emergency fund)
		- Somewhat (you have some savings, but not enough for emergencies)
* **Income Level** (`income_level`, select_one, optional):
	+ Low (your annual income is below $20,000)
	+ Medium (your annual income is between $20,000-$50,000)
	+ High (your annual income is above $50,000)
* **Income Growth** (`income_growth`, select_one, optional):
	+ Less than 10% per year
	+ 10-20%
	+ 21-30%
	+ More than 30%
* **Income Frequency** (`income_frequency`, select_one, optional):
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ 6 months
	+ Yearly
* **Income Stable** (`income_stable`, select_one, optional):
	+ Stable
	+ Somewhat stable
	+ Not stable at all
* **Income Savings** (`income_savings`, select_one, optional):
	+ Savings
	+ Spending
	+ Both
* **Income Guaranteed** (`income_guaranteed`, select_one, optional):
	+ True (you have a stable income)
	+ False (you do not have a stable income)
* **Income Investments** (`income_investments`, select_one, optional):
	+ True (you have investments)
	+ False (you do not have investments)
* **Income Education** (`income_education`, select_one, optional):
	+ True (you have an education or training that contributes to your income)
	+ False (you do not have an education or training that contributes to your income)
* **Income Family Support** (`income_family_support`, select_one, optional):
	+ True (you receive family support for income)
	+ False (you do not receive family support for income)
* **Income Other** (`income_other`, text, optional): Enter any other sources of income not listed above.
* **Income Other Details** (`income_other_details`, text, optional): Provide additional information about other sources of income.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
* Take your time to review and adjust your answers as needed.
* Use this quiz as a tool to identify areas for improvement and set realistic goals for achieving your desired income level.
