# Pituitary Adenoma Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to test your knowledge about pituitary adenoma. It consists of six questions that cover symptoms, risk factors, diagnosis, treatment, and follow-up options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply read each question carefully and select the correct answer from the options provided. You can choose from the options on each select_multiple or select_one field. For the text fields, type your answers in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, optional): Please type a brief introduction about pituitary adenoma.
- **Pituitary Adenoma Symptoms** (`pituitary_adenoma_symptoms`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the symptoms that you associate with pituitary adenoma.
  - Options: 'Yes', 'No'
- **Pituitary Adenoma Risk Factors** (`pituitary_adenoma_risk_factors`, select_one, optional): Select one risk factor that you associate with pituitary adenoma.
  - Options: 'Yes', 'No'
- **Diagnosis** (`diagnosis`, text, optional): Please provide a diagnosis for pituitary adenoma.
- **Treatment** (`treatment`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the treatments that you would recommend for pituitary adenoma.
  - Options: 'Yes', 'No'
- **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, date, optional): Please provide a date for follow-up care.
- **_** (no label): This field is not used in this form.

## Tips
- Please answer each question carefully, as it is designed to test your knowledge about pituitary adenoma.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, please refer to medical resources or seek guidance from a healthcare professional.
