# Snake Awareness Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Snake Awareness Quiz is a survey designed to test your knowledge about snakes. It consists of multiple-choice questions about various aspects of snakes, including their behavior, safety, and habitat. The quiz is intended to educate and raise awareness about snakes and their role in the ecosystem.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Snake Awareness Quiz, follow these steps:

1. Answer the multiple-choice questions about snake behavior, safety, and habitat in the **questions_1_10**, **questions_12_16**, **questions_17**, and **questions_18_19** sections.
2. Provide your answers to the open-ended questions in the **questions_20_24** and **questions_25_30** sections.
3. Enter the completion date and time in the **submission_info** section at the end of the form.
