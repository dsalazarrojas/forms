# Web Content Management Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on users' roles and preferences regarding web content management. By filling out this form, you will help us better understand how users interact with web content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the first field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the second field.
3. Enter a valid email address in the third field.
4. Enter your phone number in the fourth field.
5. Select your role in the fifth field. You can choose from the options: Administrator, Editor, Author, Reviewer, or Commenter.
6. Select all the platforms you use for your work in the sixth field. You can choose from the options A, B, C, or D.
7. Enter the date of your last update in the seventh field.
8. Enter the time of your last update in the eighth field.
9. Add any additional notes or comments about your role and preferences in the ninth field.
10. Select one of the options in the tenth field.
11. Select multiple options in the eleventh field. You can choose from the options A, B, or C.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`user_input_1`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`user_input_2`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`user_input_3`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`user_input_4`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **What is your role?** (`user_input_5`, select_one, optional): Select your role. You can choose from the options: Administrator, Editor, Author, Reviewer, or Commenter.
* **Which platform do you use?** (`user_input_6`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the platforms you use for your work. You can choose from the options A, B, C, or D.
* **Last updated date** (`user_input_7`, date, optional): Enter the date of your last update.
* **Time** (`user_input_8`, time, optional): Enter the time of your last update.
* **Note** (`user_input_9`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your role and preferences.
* **Select one** (`user_input_10`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options.
* **Select multiple** (`user_input_11`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options. You can choose from the options A, B, or C.
