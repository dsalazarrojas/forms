# Neck Pain Assessment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Neck Pain Assessment Quiz is designed to help individuals assess their neck pain symptoms and history. It's a helpful tool for anyone experiencing neck pain or discomfort.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Neck Pain Assessment Quiz, simply follow these steps:

1. Start on the "Main Page" section, which serves as an introduction to the quiz.
2. Review the list of symptoms on the "Symptoms" section and select all the symptoms that you are experiencing. This can include:
	* Cervical spine pain
	* Thoracic spine pain
	* Other (please specify)
	* Headache
	* Shoulder pain
	* Arm pain
	* Elbow pain
	* Back pain
	* Hip pain
	* Leg pain
3. Answer the "History" section by selecting how long you have experienced neck pain. You can choose from:
	* Yes, less than 1 year
	* Yes, more than 1 year
	* No, never

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Symptoms** (`Symptoms`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the symptoms you are experiencing. This will help us understand the extent of your neck pain.
* **History** (`History`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose how long you have experienced neck pain. This will help us understand the duration of your symptoms.

## Tips
* Please be honest when answering the questions.
* Select all relevant symptoms, even if they are not listed.
* If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
* This quiz is for self-assessment purposes only and should not be used for diagnostic purposes. If you're experiencing severe or persistent neck pain, please consult a medical professional.
