# phone_vocabulary_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The phone_vocabulary_assessment form is designed to evaluate a user's vocabulary skills, specifically their ability to recognize and use correct phone terminology.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Phone Number** to ensure accurate submission.
2. Select the correct **Terminology** for each prompt, paying attention to the options provided.
3. Choose one or multiple correct **Terminology** from the options.
4. If required, select **Email** confirmation to ensure we can contact you.
5. If required, provide a brief **Note** about your submission.
6. Select one correct **Terminology** for the validation prompt.
7. Finally, confirm your **Email** address and **Phone Number** for validation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Your first name will be used to identify you in the submission.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Your last name will be used to identify you in the submission.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Your email address will be used for communication and validation.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Your phone number will be used for validation.
* **Select Terminology** (`select_terminology`, select_multiple, required): Select the correct terminology for each prompt.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Not applicable in this form.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Not applicable in this form.
* **Select Multiple Terminology** (`select_multiple_terminology`, select_multiple, required): Select multiple correct terminologies for each prompt.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Provide a brief note about your submission.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, date, optional): Not applicable in this form.
* **Select One Terminology** (`select_one_terminology`, select_one, required): Select one correct terminology.
* **Email Confirmation** (`email_confirmation`, email, optional): Confirm your email address.
* **Phone Number Validation** (`phone_number_validation`, text, optional): Not applicable in this form.
* **Select Terminology Validation** (`select_terminology_validation`, select_multiple, optional): Select the correct terminology for validation.
* **Select One Terminology Validation** (`select_one_terminology_validation`, select_one, optional): Select one correct terminology for validation.
