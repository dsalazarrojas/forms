# Internal Environment Analysis Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internal Environment Analysis Quiz is a form designed to help departments assess their internal environment, including leader skills, communication with leaders, departmental strengths and weaknesses, resource availability, and overall comfort levels. This quiz aims to gather information to identify areas of improvement and provide a comprehensive understanding of the departmental climate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully, selecting the most suitable option for your department.
2. Make sure to provide honest and accurate feedback.
3. For multiple-select questions, select all applicable options.
4. Fill out all required fields marked as "required".
5. Review your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Leader Skills**: A scale measuring how well leaders in your department are able to guide and lead others.
* **Comms with Leaders**: A scale measuring how well you are able to communicate with departmental leaders.
* **Departmental Strengths**: A select-one question to identify the strongest area of your department.
* **Departmental Weaknesses**: A select-multiple question to identify areas that need improvement in your department.
* **Resource Availabilty**: A scale measuring the availability of necessary resources for your department.
* **Comms with Department**: A select-one question to measure how well you communicate with your department members.
* **Overall Comfort**: A select-multiple question to measure the overall comfort level within your department.
* **Comms with other Departments**: A select-multiple question to measure how well you communicate with other departments.
* **Team Comfort**: A select-one question to measure the comfort level within your team.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: A select-one question to measure overall satisfaction with your department.
* **Comms with Management**: A select-multiple question to measure how well you communicate with departmental management.
* **Resources**: A select-multiple question to measure the availability of necessary resources for your team.
* **Overall Leadership**: A select-one question to measure the overall leadership within your department.
* **Departmental Leadership**: A select-one question to measure the leadership quality within your department.
* **Team Resources**: A select-multiple question to measure the availability of necessary resources for your team.
* **Overall Team Comfort**: A select-multiple question to measure the overall comfort level within your team.
* **Overall Departmental Comfort**: A select-multiple question to measure the overall comfort level within your department.
* **Overall Departmental Satisfaction**: A select-one question to measure the overall satisfaction with your department.
* **Overall Departmental Resources**: A select-multiple question to measure the availability of necessary resources for your department.
