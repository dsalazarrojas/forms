# Water Hero Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Water Hero Quiz is designed to gather information about users' attitudes and behaviors related to water usage. It is intended to help identify areas of improvement and provide insights for water conservation efforts. The quiz is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or derived from any form platform, and it is an original adaptation created using AI.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Water Hero Quiz, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Name** and **Email** fields (if you choose to provide them).
2. Select your **Age Range** and **Gender** from the respective dropdown menus.
3. Answer how often you use **water** by selecting your options from the dropdown menu.
4. Provide your **Justification** for your water usage frequency (optional).
5. List any **concerns** you have about water, such as **Water quality**, **Water scarcity**, **Water cost**, and **Water access**.
6. If you have any other water concerns not listed, provide them in the **Other water concerns** field.
7. Choose ways you think can be done to **save water**, such as **Water conservation**, **Water efficiency**, and **Water recycling**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, which will be displayed on the results page.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Provide your email address for follow-up contact (if needed).
* **School** (`School`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your school (if you are a student).
* **Teacher** (`Teacher`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your teacher (if you have one).
* **Age Range** (`Age Range`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your age range from 6-8, 9-12, 13-16, 17-18, or 19-20.
* **Gender** (`Gender`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your gender from Male, Female, Non-binary, or Prefer not to say.
* **Water usage** (`How often do you use water?`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select how often you use water from Daily, Weekly, or Rarely.
* **Justification** (`Justification`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief explanation of your water usage frequency.
* **Water concerns** (`What concerns you about water?`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose from Water quality, Water scarcity, Water cost, or Water access, or select other concerns.
* **Other water concerns** (`Other water concerns`, text, required/optional): Add any other concerns not listed.
* **What can be done to save water?** (`What can be done to save water?`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose from Water conservation, Water efficiency, and Water recycling.

## Tips

- Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
- Take your time to think about your water usage frequency and concerns.
- You can select multiple options for the questions that have multiple options.
- You can add your school name and teacher name if you want to participate as a student.
- You can always skip the email field if you don't want to provide it.
- The quiz is designed to be fun and helpful, so enjoy it!
