# Reaction Time Score Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for participants to submit their reaction time scores for evaluation or research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your reaction time score in the **Reaction Time Score** field. This is the core of this form.
2. In the **Submission Form**, provide a brief description or explanation of your reaction time. This field is optional but recommended for context.
3. Fill in your **Participant Username**, **Participant Name**, **Participant Email**, **Participant Organization**, **Participant Division**, **Participant Team**, **Participant Position**, and **Participant Division/Team** fields with your relevant information. These fields are optional but help us understand who you are and your affiliation.
4. Review and submit the form once you have filled in all the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submission Form**: A text field for a brief description or explanation of your reaction time score. Please provide any relevant context or details about your score. 
* **Reaction Time Score**: A numerical field to record your reaction time. Enter your score here.
* **Participant Username**: A field to enter your username. This helps us identify you within our system.
* **Participant Name**: A field to enter your name. This will help us understand who you are.
* **Participant Email**: A field to enter your email address. This will allow us to contact you if needed.
* **Participant Organization**: A field to enter your organization name or affiliation. This helps us understand your background.
* **Participant Division**: A field to enter your division or department name. This provides more information about your position within your organization.
* **Participant Team**: A field to enter your team name. This helps us understand your team structure.
* **Participant Position**: A field to enter your job title or position. This provides more information about your role.
* **Participant Division/Team**: A field to enter your division or team name. This seems redundant with other fields but is here for your convenience to fill in if you have multiple teams or divisions.
