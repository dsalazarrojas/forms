# Geometric Design Patterns Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the understanding of geometric design patterns. It is used to evaluate your knowledge and skills in identifying and applying geometric design patterns. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions in each page as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Select the correct option for the "Geometric Pattern" question.
3. For the "What is the name of the geometric pattern?" question, type in the correct name of the pattern you have selected.
4. You can skip the "Submit" field unless you need to submit your answers.
5. The "Time Spent" field is for you to record how much time you spent on the quiz.
6. The "Date Submitted" field is for you to record the date and time you submitted your answers.
7. The "Email" field is for you to enter your email address if you want to receive your results.
8. The "Phone" field is for you to enter your phone number if you want to be contacted.
9. The "Description" field is for you to enter any additional comments or explanations.
10. The "Name" field is for you to enter your name.
11. The "Grade" field is for you to enter your grade level.
12. The "Student ID" field is for you to enter your student ID.
13. The "Teacher" field is for you to enter your teacher's name.
14. The "Class Name" field is for you to enter the name of your class.
15. The "Subject" field is for you to enter the subject area you are taking.
16. The "School Name" field is for you to enter the name of your school.
17. The "Student Number" field is for you to enter your student number.
18. The "Score" field is for you to enter your score.
19. The "Time Spent 2" field is for you to record how much time you spent on the quiz.
20. The "Date Submitted 2" field is for you to record the date and time you submitted your answers.
21. The "Student" field is for you to enter your student name.
22. The "Teacher Email" field is for you to enter your teacher's email address.
23. The "Class Name 2" field is for you to enter the name of your class.
24. The "Subject Area" field is for you to enter the subject area you are taking.
