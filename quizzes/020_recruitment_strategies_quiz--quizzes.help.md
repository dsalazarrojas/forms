# Recruitment Strategies Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help job seekers apply for a job opening by collecting relevant information about their qualifications, experience, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the job posting carefully and understand what the job opening is looking for.
2. Select the options that best fit your qualifications, experience, and preferences.
3. Fill out all required fields.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Opening**: Select whether the job opening is internal to the company or an external job opportunity. Please choose the option that best represents your employment status.
* **Job Description**: Please provide a brief description of the job and what it entails.
* **Interview Process**: Select the interview process that best suits your availability and preferences. Please choose one or more options that you are comfortable with.
* **Job Location**: Select the location where you are willing to work. Please choose from Office, Remote, or Hybrid options.
* **Job Type**: Select the type of job that best suits your career goals. Please choose from Full-time, Part-time, or Contract options.
* **Job Title**: Please provide the job title you are applying for.
* **Salary Range**: Provide your expected salary range.
* **Work Experience**: Please enter the number of years of work experience you have.
* **Education Background**: Select the level of education you have completed. Please choose from Bachelor's degree, Master's degree, or PhD options.
* **Language**: Select the languages you are proficient in. Please choose from English, French, or Spanish options.
* **Notice Period**: Please enter the date you would like to start the job.
* **Application Deadline**: Please enter the date by which you would like to submit your application.
* **Application Process**: Select the method by which you would like to apply for the job. Please choose from Email, Online form, Phone call, or In-person options.
* **Contact Information**: Please provide your contact information.
* **Submission Guidelines**: Please provide any additional submission guidelines you would like to mention.
* **Submission Requirements**: Please provide any additional submission requirements you would like to mention.
* **Contact Person**: Select the contact person responsible for your application.
* **Contact Email**: Provide your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone**: Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Contact Address**: Provide your physical address for contact purposes.
* **Job Description**: Please provide a brief description of the job and what it entails.
* **Job Requirements**: Please provide a list of your job requirements.
* **Job Responsibilities**: Please provide a list of your job responsibilities.
* **Job Benefits**: Please provide any job benefits you are looking for.
* **Job Eligibility**: Please provide any job eligibility criteria you have.
