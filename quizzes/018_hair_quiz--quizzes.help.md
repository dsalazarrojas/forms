# Hair Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair Quiz form is a tool for healthcare professionals to collect information about a patient's hair condition, styling habits, and treatment history. It's intended to be used as a starting point for further evaluation and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your patient's information in the "Patient info" field.
2. Select the condition that best describes your hair from the list of options.
3. Choose any concerns you have about your hair from the "Client concerns" list.
4. Indicate the length of your hair from the "Hair length" dropdown.
5. Rate the dryness of your hair from the "How dry is your hair?" dropdown.
6. Estimate the time you spend on your hair's fringe from the "How much time do you spend on your hair's fringe?" dropdown.
7. Select any hair care products you currently use from the "Which of the products do you use on your hair?" list.
8. Answer whether you've ever had hair coloring from the "Have you ever had hair coloring?" dropdown.
9. Indicate whether you've recently had a hair treatment from the "Have you had any hair treatment recently?" dropdown.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient info**: Please enter your name, email, or other identifying information to help us keep track of your submission.
* **Hair condition**: Select the condition that best describes your hair. Options include:
	+ Balding
	+ Dull
	+ Dry
	+ Fine
	+ Greying
	+ Thinning
	+ Thinning on top
	+ Volume loss
	+ Normal
* **Client concerns**: Choose any concerns you have about your hair from this list:
	+ Dullness
	+ Thinning
	+ Colour loss
	+ Greys
	+ Itchiness
	+ Dryness
	+ Frizziness
	+ Breakage
	+ Other
* **Hair length**: Choose the length of your hair from the dropdown options:
	+ Short
	+ Medium
	+ Long
* **How dry is your hair?**: Rate the dryness of your hair from the dropdown options:
	+ Very dry
	+ Somewhat dry
	+ Not dry at all
* **How much time do you spend on your hair's fringe?**: Estimate the time you spend on your hair's fringe from the dropdown options:
	+ 0-5 minutes
	+ 5-15 minutes
	+ 15-30 minutes
* **Which of the products do you use on your hair?**: Select any of the following products:
	+ Conditioner
	+ Shampoo
	+ Serum
	+ Oil
	+ Mask
	+ Other
* **Have you ever had hair coloring?**: Answer whether you've ever had hair coloring:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Have you had any hair treatment recently?**: Answer whether you've had any hair treatment recently:
	+ True
	+ False

## Tips
- Please answer each question honestly and accurately to ensure the assessment is reliable.
- If you're unsure or don't know how to answer a question, don't hesitate to ask your healthcare professional for guidance.
- This form is for your convenience, and you're free to modify or stop using it at any time.
