# Education Reform Assessment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your level of experience and comfort with various aspects of education reform. It is a self-assessment tool to help educators and stakeholders better understand their knowledge and skills in this field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose your role in the education sector from the options provided.
2. Select all the types of schools you have worked in.
3. Indicate your level of experience with education reform initiatives.
4. Choose your preferred method of receiving information.
5. Select your preferred method of communication.
6. Indicate your comfort with online data collection.
7. Choose your preferred method of providing feedback.
8. Select your preferred method of receiving feedback.
9. Choose your preferred method of communicating sensitive topics.
10. Indicate your concerns about online data collection.
11. Indicate your concerns about data privacy.
12. Indicate your experience with online forms.
13. Choose the number of schools you have worked with.
14. Select your comfort with large school districts.
15. Choose your level of experience with data collection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role?** (Teacher, Student, Parent): Select the role you hold in the education sector. This will help us assess your level of involvement in education reform.
* **What is your school type?** (Select all that apply): Choose all the types of schools you have worked in, such as high school, middle school, university, vocational school, etc.
* **What is your role in the school?** (Select all that apply): Indicate your role in the school, such as school administrator, teacher, school counselor, etc.
* **How long have you been working in education?** (Select all that apply): Choose the length of time you have been working in education.
* **How many schools have you worked at?** (Yes, No, Other): Indicate if you have worked at one or more schools. If other, please specify.
* **What is the size of the school district you work in?** (Select all that apply): Choose the size of the school district you work in.
* **What is your level of experience with education reform initiatives?** (Some experience, Moderate experience, Significant experience, Expert level): Indicate your level of experience with education reform initiatives.
* **How do you prefer to receive information?** (Select all that apply): Choose how you would like to receive information, such as email, phone call, text message, etc.
* **How do you prefer to communicate?** (Select all that apply): Choose how you would like to communicate, such as formal email, formal letter, text message, etc.
* **What is your preferred method of communication?** (Select all that apply): Choose your preferred method of communication.
* **Do you have experience with online forms?** (Yes, No): Indicate if you have experience with online forms.
* **Do you have experience with online surveys?** (Yes, No): Indicate if you have experience with online surveys.
* **Have you ever used a drag-and-drop interface?** (Yes, No): Indicate if you have experience with drag-and-drop interfaces.
* **What is your experience with data collection?** (Select all that apply): Choose your level of experience with data collection.
* **How do you prefer to provide feedback?** (Select all that apply): Choose how you would like to provide feedback, such as formal email, formal letter, etc.
* **How do you prefer to receive feedback?** (Select all that apply): Choose how you would like to receive feedback.
* **How do you prefer to communicate on sensitive topics?** (Select all that apply): Choose how you would like to communicate sensitive topics.
* **Do you have any concerns about online data collection?** (Yes, No): Indicate if you have concerns about online data collection.
* **Do you have concerns about data privacy?** (Yes, No): Indicate if you have concerns about data privacy.
* **Have you ever used a no-code form builder?** (Yes, No): Indicate if you have experience with no-code form builders.
* **How many schools have you worked with?** (Select one): Choose the number of schools you have worked with.
* **What type of feedback do you prefer to receive?** (Select all that apply): Choose how you would like to receive feedback.

## Tips
- Be honest with your answers, as this form is for your personal assessment.
- If you are unsure about any of the options, choose the one that best fits your experience or comfort level.
- Take your time and answer each question carefully to ensure accurate results.
