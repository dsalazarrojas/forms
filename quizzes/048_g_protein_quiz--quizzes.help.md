# G Protein Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The G Protein Quiz form is designed to test your knowledge about G Proteins, a crucial type of protein in cellular signaling. This form will guide you through a series of questions to assess your understanding of G Proteins, their signaling mechanisms, and related concepts. Please answer the questions to the best of your abilities, and provide your contact information to receive a final assessment of your knowledge.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and choose the correct answer.
2. For select-one fields (e.g., "what-is-a-g-protein"), select only one option that best answers the question.
3. For select_multiple fields (e.g., "how-does-a-g-protein-signal"), select all options that apply.
4. For text fields (e.g., "conclusion"), write your answer in the space provided.
5. Enter your name, email, phone number, and institution to provide additional context to your quiz results.
6. Click the "submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (text): This is the first question of the quiz, which is a general introduction to the topic of G Proteins.
* **What-is-a-G-Protein** (select_one): Choose the answer that best describes what is a G Protein. For example, "Yes" if you think it is a receptor, or "No" if you think it is a type of enzyme.
* **How-does-a-G-Protein-Signal** (select_multiple): Choose all the ways in which a G Protein signals. For example, "Yes" if G Proteins signal through GTP hydrolysis, "Yes" if G Proteins signal through GTP binding, and "No" if G Proteins do not signal through any of the above options.
* **What-is-G-Protein-Linked-Receptor** (select_one): Choose the answer that best describes what is a G Protein-linked receptor. For example, "Yes" if it is a receptor that is linked to a G Protein, or "No" if it is not.
* **What-is-G-Protein-Linked-Receptor-Kinase** (select_multiple): Choose all the ways in which a G Protein-linked receptor kinase signals. For example, "Yes" if it is through GTPase activity, "Yes" if it is through receptor phosphorylation, and "No" if it is not through any of the above options.
* **What-is-the-G-Protein-Effector-Enzyme** (select_multiple): Choose all the ways in which a G Protein effector enzyme signals. For example, "Yes" if it is through GTP hydrolysis, "Yes" if it is through enzyme activation, and "No" if it is not through any of the above options.
* **What-is-the-G-Protein-Effector-Enzyme** (note): This is not a question, but a field to provide any additional information or thoughts you may have. Please use it to provide any relevant context or insights about G Proteins.
* **Conclusion** (text): Provide a final thought or reflection about what you have learned from this quiz.
* **Student Name** (text): Enter your name as you would like it to appear on your quiz results.
* **Student Email** (email): Enter your email address to receive your quiz results.
* **Student Phone** (text): Enter your phone number to receive your quiz results.
* **Student Institution** (text): Enter the name of your institution to receive your quiz results.
* **Student Course** (select_one): Choose the course you are currently enrolled in.
* **Submit** (text): Click this button to submit your quiz results.
