# which_raptor_suits_you - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is a survey meant to help us learn more about what type of raptor you're interested in, based on your preferences and characteristics.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below.

1. Enter your name in the "What is your name" field. This is a required field for identification purposes.
2. Select the raptor that suits you the most from the "Which raptor" dropdown. We have Raptor Alpha, Raptor Beta, Raptor Gamma, and Raptor Delta options.
3. Choose the colors you like from the "Which color" multiple-select field. This is a required field.
4. Type in your thoughts on the "What do you think?" field. This is an optional field for further explanation.
5. Select your favorite animal from the "What is your favorite animal" dropdown. This is an optional field.
6. Enter a number in the "What number" field. This is an optional field.
7. Enter a decimal number in the "What decimal" field. This is an optional field.
8. Enter a date in the "Date" field. This is an optional field.
9. Enter a time in the "Time" field. This is an optional field.
10. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is an optional field.
11. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This is an optional field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name** (`title`, `text`, required): Enter your full name for identification purposes.
* **Which raptor** (`title`, `select_one`, required): Choose the raptor that suits your personality or interests. Options are Raptor Alpha, Raptor Beta, Raptor Gamma, and Raptor Delta.
* **Which color** (`title`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more colors that you like. Options are Red, Green, Yellow, and Blue.
* **What do you think?** (`title`, `text`, optional): Share your thoughts or comments about the raptors or the survey.
* **What is your favorite animal** (`title`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your favorite animal. Options are Dog, Cat, and Bird.
* **What number** (`title`, `number`, optional): Enter a number that represents your favorite number or a significant number in your life.
* **What decimal** (`title`, `number`, optional): Enter a decimal number that represents a significant number or a calculation in your life.
* **Date** (`title`, `date`, optional): Enter a date that is significant to you, such as your birthday or a special occasion.
* **Time** (`title`, `time`, optional): Enter a time that is significant to you, such as your favorite time of day or a regular schedule.
* **Email** (`title`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Phone** (`title`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* You can select multiple colors for "Which color".
* You can only select one raptor for "Which raptor".
* You can enter a single number or a range for "What number".
* You can enter a single decimal number or a range for "What decimal".
* You can enter a specific date or a date range for "Date".
* You can enter a specific time or a time range for "Time".
* You can enter your email address with the format `example@example.com`.
* You can enter your phone number with the format `(123) 456-7890`.
