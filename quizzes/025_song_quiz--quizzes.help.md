# Song Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Song Quiz form is a tool used to test your knowledge about songs in the music industry. It is designed to be completed once by each user and is likely used as a test or assessment tool.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the genre of the song from the Music Trivia dropdown menu.
2. If you know the year the song was released, enter it in the Year of Release field. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
3. Enter the name of the artist who performed the song in the Artist field.
4. If you know multiple genres of music that the song belongs to, select them from the Multiple Genres dropdown menu.
5. If you know the release date of the song, enter it in the Release Date field. Otherwise, leave this field blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Music Trivia** (select_one): Select the genre of the song from the dropdown menu.
* **Year of Release** (number): Enter the year the song was released (optional).
* **Artist** (text): Enter the name of the artist who performed the song.
* **Multiple Genres** (select_multiple): Select all genres that the song belongs to.
* **Release Date** (date): Enter the release date of the song (optional).
