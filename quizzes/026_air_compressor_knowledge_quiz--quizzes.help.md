# Air Compressor Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the completion of the Air Compressor Knowledge Quiz form, providing an explanation of each field to help you understand what to expect and how to complete it accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name (optional) in the Participant name field.
2. Answer the questions regarding air compressor maintenance, pressure relief valves, shutdown procedures, and signs of air leaks.
3. Indicate whether you require further training.
4. Self-assess your score.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name for reference purposes only. This field is not required.
- **What is regular maintenance for compressors** (`question_1`, select_one, required): Choose the best answer from the provided options to describe regular maintenance for compressors.
  - Lubrication and filter changes: Regular maintenance for compressors typically includes lubrication and filter changes to ensure proper operation and extend the lifespan of the compressor.
  - Never perform maintenance: This is incorrect, as regular maintenance is essential for the proper functioning of compressors.
  - Replace entire unit yearly: This is an oversimplification and not accurate.
  - Only check when broken: This is not an effective maintenance strategy.
- **Purpose of pressure relief valve** (`question_2`, select_one, required): Select the correct purpose of pressure relief valves.
  - Prevent overpressure: Pressure relief valves are designed to prevent overpressure and ensure safe operation.
  - Increase pressure: This is the opposite of the intended purpose.
  - Reduce airflow: This is not the primary function of pressure relief valves.
  - Measure temperature: This is not the primary function of pressure relief valves.
- **Correct compressor shutdown procedure** (`question_3`, select_one, required): Choose the correct shutdown procedure for compressors.
  - Depressurize and isolate: This is the recommended procedure to safely shut down a compressor.
  - Turn off then leave running: This is not a safe or recommended procedure.
  - Remove safety guards: This can be dangerous and not a recommended procedure.
  - None: This is not the correct procedure.
- **Signs of air leak** (`question_4`, select_one, required): Choose the most indicative sign of an air leak.
  - Hissing sounds and pressure drop: These are common signs of an air leak.
  - Increased heat only: This can be a sign of an issue, but not the only sign of an air leak.
  - No noise: This is not a reliable indicator of an air leak.
  - Faster operation: This is not typically indicative of an air leak.
- **Do you require further training** (`training_needed`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you feel you require further training on air compressor knowledge.
  - True: You may need additional knowledge or clarification on air compressor topics.
  - False: You feel confident in your current knowledge.
- **Estimated score** (`score_estimate`, number, optional): Self-assess your score based on your understanding of the air compressor topics.
