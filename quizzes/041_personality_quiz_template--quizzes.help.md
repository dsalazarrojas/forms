# personality_quiz_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your personality traits and preferences. It is a series of questions that will help you discover more about yourself, your interests, and your tendencies. Please answer the questions honestly, and don't worry about being too personal. This form is meant to be fun and enlightening, not intrusive or judgmental.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer the options that best fit your thoughts and feelings.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. If a question doesn't fit your experience, answer with "Not applicable" or "Not relevant".
4. Take your time, and don't rush. You can save your progress and come back later if needed.
5. When you're finished, click "Submit" to review your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`question1`, text, optional):
  This is an introduction page where you can provide any context or background information about yourself.
  You can write as little or as much as you like, but keep in mind that it will not be scored or used for analysis.
* **What is your favorite color** (`question2`, text, optional):
  We're curious about what colors you like and why they're your favorite. Don't worry if you can't choose just one – you can list multiple colors.
* **Do you like pineapple on pizza?** (`question3`, select_multiple, optional):
  This question might seem silly, but it's actually a great indicator of your personality. Choose one, more, or all of the options that you think apply to you.
* **How much time do you spend on gaming** (`question4`, select_one, optional):
  Think about how many hours per week you dedicate to gaming, whether it's solo or with friends.
* **What is your favorite hobby** (`question5`, text, optional):
  We're interested in activities that bring you joy and relaxation. Think about sports, art, reading, or anything else that suits your taste.
* **Which of the following is your favorite food** (`question6`, select_multiple, optional):
  Choose all the foods that you enjoy eating the most. Don't worry if you can't choose just one – we want to see what you like!
* **How much time do you spend on social media** (`question7`, select_one, optional):
  Consider how many hours per week you dedicate to social media, whether it's browsing, scrolling, or engaging with others.
* **What is your favorite type of music** (`question8`, select_one, optional):
  Think about the genre or style of music that you enjoy listening to or playing.
* **How many friends do you have on social media** (`question9`, number, optional):

* **How many siblings do you have** (`question10`, number, optional):

* **What is your current level of education** (`question11`, select_one, optional):
