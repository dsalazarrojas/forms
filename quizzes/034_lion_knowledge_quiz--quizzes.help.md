# Lion Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lion Knowledge Quiz form is designed to collect user demographics and quiz results. Please complete this form to provide accurate information about your lion knowledge and quiz experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Choose the correct options for each select-type field.
3. Ensure that the date and time fields are formatted correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, optional): Enter your age in numbers.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Score** (`score`, `number`, optional): Enter the score you achieved on the quiz.
* **Quiz Type** (`quiz_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of quiz you took (e.g., online, offline, etc.).
* **Quiz Score** (`quiz_score`, `number`, optional): Enter the score you achieved on the quiz.
* **Quiz Date** (`quiz_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you took the quiz.
* **Quiz Time** (`quiz_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you took the quiz.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your quiz experience.
* **Quiz Category** (`quiz_category`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the categories that apply to your quiz (e.g., type, location, etc.).
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, optional): Choose who submitted the quiz (e.g., you, an administrator, etc.).
* **School or Program** (`school_or_program`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the school or program you are associated with.
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the type of program you are associated with (e.g., academic, research, etc.).
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, `date`, optional): Enter the date for any follow-up activities.
* **Follow-up By** (`follow_up_by`, `select_one`, optional): Choose who will follow-up with you (e.g., you, an administrator, etc.).
* **School or Program Location** (`school_or_program_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of the school or program you are associated with.
* **User Location** (`user_location`, `text`, optional): Enter your location.
* **Follow-up By Location** (`follow_up_by_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of the person following up with you.
* **Submitted** (`submitted`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you submitted the quiz.
* **Submitted By Location** (`submitted_by_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location where you submitted the quiz.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you submitted the quiz.
* **Quiz Score Range** (`quiz_score_range`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the score range you fell within (e.g., low, medium, high, etc.).
* **Quiz Score Range Value** (`quiz_score_range_value`, `number`, optional): Enter the value of the score range you chose.
* **School or Program ID** (`school_or_program_id`, `text`, optional): Enter the ID of the school or program you are associated with.

Note: This help guide is based on the assumption that the form is for user completion. If there are any further clarifications or instructions for administrators or other users, please add those as necessary.
