# Regional Integration Challenges Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Regional Integration Challenges Quiz is a comprehensive form designed to help regions identify and document integration challenges. This quiz is a critical component of the regional integration process, allowing regions to gain a deeper understanding of their strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section and field carefully, ensuring you understand the purpose of each field.
2. For fields with options, select the most relevant option that accurately reflects your response.
3. For text fields, provide clear and concise answers.
4. Ensure you complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your name for identification purposes.
  - This field is required to ensure we accurately address you in our response.
- **Regional Integration Challenges** (`regional_integration_challenges`, text, required): Describe the integration challenges you are facing.
  - Please provide a clear and concise description of the challenges you are experiencing.
- **Obstacles** (`obstacles`, select_one, required): Identify any obstacles you are currently facing.
  - Select 'True' if you are currently experiencing obstacles, 'False' if not, or 'Other' if you have additional information.
- **Training Programs** (`training_programs`, select_multiple, required): List the training programs you have used to address integration challenges.
  - Select all applicable options that describe the training programs you have utilized.
- **Obstacles' Solutions** (`obstacles_solutions`, text, optional): Describe any solutions you have implemented to address obstacles.
  - This field is optional, but providing additional information will help us better understand your challenges.
- **Regional Integration Obstacles** (`regional_integration_obstacles`, select_one, optional): Identify any regional integration obstacles you are experiencing.
  - Select 'Yes' if you are experiencing regional integration obstacles, 'No' if not, or 'Other' if you have additional information.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
  - This field is required to ensure we can contact you regarding your response.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact information.
  - This field is optional, but providing your phone number will allow us to contact you more easily.
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your regional integration challenges.
  - This field is optional, but providing the date will help us track your integration progress.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your regional integration challenges.
  - This field is optional, but providing the time will help us track your integration progress.
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your integration challenges.
  - This field is optional, but providing additional context will help us better understand your situation.
- **Obstacles Solutions Note** (`obstacles_solutions_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about the solutions you have implemented.
  - This field is optional, but providing additional context will help us understand your progress.
- **Regional Integration Obstacles Note** (`regional_integration_obstacles_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about regional integration obstacles.
  - This field is optional, but providing additional context will help us understand your situation.
- **Additional Information 1** (`additional_info_1`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your integration challenges.
  - This field is optional, but providing additional context will help us better understand your situation.
- **Additional Information 2** (`additional_info_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your integration challenges.
  - This field is optional, but providing additional context will help us better understand your situation.
- **Additional Information 3** (`additional_info_3`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your integration challenges.
  - This field is optional, but providing additional context will help us better understand your situation.
- **Regional Integration Challenges Quiz Date** (`regional_integration_challenges_quiz_date`, date, required): Enter the date you completed this form.
  - This field is required to track the completion date.
- **Regional Integration Challenges Quiz Time** (`regional_integration_challenges_quiz_time`, time, required): Enter the time you completed this form.
  - This field is required to track the completion time.
