# Carl Rogers Theory Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Carl Rogers Theory Quiz is designed to test your understanding of Carl Rogers' theory of person-centered therapy. The quiz will assess your knowledge of key concepts, core principles, and applications of this humanistic approach to psychology.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by providing your **Participant Identification**:
    -   **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
    -   **Student ID**: Enter your student ID if applicable. This is optional but recommended for students.
    -   **Academic Level**: Choose your current level of study from the provided options (Undergraduate, Graduate, Doctorate, Professional, or Self-study).
2.  Answer the **Quiz Questions**:
    -   **Quiz Q1**: Identify the core condition that is NOT a fundamental aspect of therapy according to Carl Rogers.
    -   **Quiz Q2**: Define congruence in the context of Rogers' theory, focusing on the relationship between self-concept and experience.
    -   **Quiz Q3**: Determine the primary category of psychology that Rogers' theory falls under.
    -   **Quiz Q4**: Identify the term used by Rogers to describe a person whose self-concept and experience are aligned.
    -   **Quiz Q5**: Determine who is the expert in the therapeutic relationship, according to Rogers.
    -   **Quiz Q6**: Answer True or False: Rogers believed that unconditional positive regard means the therapist must agree with everything the client does.
    -   **Quiz Q7**: Identify the primary role of the therapist in Person-Centered Therapy.
    -   **Quiz Q8**: Identify the inherent tendency to develop all capacities as described by Rogers.
    -   **Quiz Q9**: Explain how an individual evaluates experiences according to the Organismic Valuing Process.
3.  Provide **Final Declaration**:
    -   Certify that you completed the quiz without external assistance.
    -   Rate the difficulty of the quiz (1-5) and indicate whether the questions were clear and understandable.
