# judicial_cases_knowledge_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The judicial_cases_knowledge_quiz form is designed to collect and record information about a single judicial case. This form should not be used to submit multiple cases; each submission should be a single case.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of case from the "Select case type" dropdown menu.
2.  Choose the court process that applies to the case.
3.  Read and enter the case scenario.
4.  Enter the legal issue related to the case.
5.  Enter the court ruling.
6.  Enter the court outcome.
7.  Enter the name of the educator or trainer who submitted the case.
8.  Enter the program information.
9.  Enter the class type.
10. Enter the program length.
11. Enter the class length.
12. Enter the student count.
13. Enter the quiz date.
14. Enter the quiz time.
15. Enter the submitted by name.
16. Enter the name of the reviewer.
17. Enter the review date.
18. Enter the review time.
19. Enter any comments related to the case.
20. Enter the submitted by name for a second person, if applicable.
21. Enter the name of the second reviewer, if applicable.
22. Enter the review date for the second reviewer, if applicable.
23. Enter the review time for the second reviewer, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Select case type**: Select the type of case from the dropdown menu. This could be Civil, Criminal, Family, or Other.
*   **Which court process**: Choose the court process that applies to the case, such as Trial, Appeal, Dismissal, or Other.
*   **Read the case**: Enter a detailed description of the case.
*   **Legal issue**: Enter the legal issue related to the case.
*   **Court ruling**: Enter the ruling made by the court.
*   **Court outcome**: Enter the outcome of the court case.
*   **Educator**: Enter the name of the educator or trainer who submitted the case.
*   **Trainer**: Enter the name of the trainer.
*   **Program**: Enter information about the program related to the case.
*   **Class type**: Enter the type of class or program related to the case.
*   **Program length**: Enter the length of the program.
*   **Class length**: Enter the length of the class.
*   **Student count**: Enter the number of students involved in the case.
*   **Quiz date**: Enter the date when the quiz took place.
*   **Quiz time**: Enter the time when the quiz took place.
*   **Submitted by**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the case.
*   **Reviewer**: Enter the name of the person who reviewed the case.
*   **Review date**: Enter the date when the case was reviewed.
*   **Review time**: Enter the time when the case was reviewed.
*   **Comments**: Enter any comments related to the case.
*   **Submitted By 2**: Enter the name of the second person who submitted the case, if applicable.
*   **Reviewer 2**: Enter the name of the second reviewer, if applicable.
*   **Review Date 2**: Enter the review date for the second reviewer, if applicable.
*   **Review Time 2**: Enter the review time for the second reviewer, if applicable.
*   **Submitted By 3**: Enter the name of the third person who submitted the case, if applicable.
