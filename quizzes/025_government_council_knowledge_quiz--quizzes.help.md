# Government Council Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Government Council Knowledge Quiz form is a tool for tracking student completion of quizzes. It captures essential information about the quiz, including start and end times, duration, and scores. The form helps educators monitor student progress and quiz performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the quiz start time by choosing the start date and time from the calendar.
2. Enter the student's name and select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if they started the quiz.
3. Choose the correct type of quiz being taken (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
4. Enter the duration of the quiz and the number of correct answers.
5. Enter the quiz score.
6. Select the quiz result ("Yes" or "No").
7. Enter the submission time and date.
8. Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the quiz was completed.
9. Select the submission status ("Active" or "Inactive").
10. Select the status of the quiz completion ("Active" or "Inactive").
11. Indicate if the quiz was submitted by the student ("Yes" or "No").
12. Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the student attended the quiz.
13. (Optional) Enter the submission source, device, platform, IP address, user agent, and referrer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quiz Start** (`id: 1`): Select the start time of the quiz from the calendar.
* **Student Name** (`id: 2`): Enter the student's name for the quiz.
* **Quiz Type** (`id: 3`): Choose the type of quiz being taken (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Quiz Start Time** (`id: 4`): Enter the start time of the quiz.
* **Quiz End Time** (`id: 5`): Enter the end time of the quiz. (Optional)
* **Quiz Duration** (`id: 6`): Enter the duration of the quiz in minutes.
* **Correct Answers** (`id: 7`): Enter the number of correct answers.
* **Correct Answers** (`id: 8`): (Optional) Enter any additional comments about correct answers.
* **Quiz Score** (`id: 9`): Enter the score achieved in the quiz.
* **Quiz Result** (`id: 10`): Select the quiz result ("Yes" or "No").
* **Notes** (`id: 11`): (Optional) Enter any additional comments about the quiz.
* **Submitted By** (`id: 12`): Enter the user's email address who submitted the quiz.
* **Submission Time** (`id: 13`): Enter the time of submission.
* **Submission Date** (`id: 14`): Enter the date of submission. (Optional)
* **Quiz Completed** (`id: 15`): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the quiz was completed.
* **Submission Status** (`id: 16`): Select the submission status ("Active" or "Inactive").
* **Quiz Completed Status** (`id: 17`): Select the status of the quiz completion ("Active" or "Inactive").
* **Submitted By** (`id: 18`): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the quiz was submitted by the student.
* **Student Attended** (`id: 19`): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the student attended the quiz.
* **Submission Source** (`id: 20`): (Optional) Enter the source of submission.
* **Submission Device** (`id: 21`): (Optional) Enter the device used for submission.
* **Submission Platform** (`id: 22`): (Optional) Enter the platform used for submission.
* **Submission IP Address** (`id: 23`): (Optional) Enter the IP address used for submission.
* **Submission User Agent** (`id: 24`): (Optional) Enter the user agent used for submission.
* **Submission Referrer** (`id: 25`): (Optional) Enter the referrer used for submission.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information in each field.
* Review and verify the data entered before submitting.
* Use the form only for its intended purpose, to track student completion of quizzes.
* If you encounter any issues or have questions, contact your administrator for assistance.
