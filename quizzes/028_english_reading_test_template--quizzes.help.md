# english_reading_test_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The English reading test template is designed to assess an individual's English reading comprehension skills. It consists of multiple questions that evaluate their ability to understand and interpret text-based materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Start" button to begin the English reading test.
2. Answer the comprehension questions one by one, providing your responses in the text boxes provided.
3. Make sure to read each passage carefully before answering the corresponding questions.
4. Submit your answers when you have completed all the questions.
