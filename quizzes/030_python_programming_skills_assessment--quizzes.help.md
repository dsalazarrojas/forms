# python_programming_skills_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Python Programming Skills Assessment form is designed to evaluate your skills and knowledge in Python programming. It will help you identify areas where you need improvement and provide a baseline for future progress checks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Fill out each section of the form with your responses to the questions.
* Make sure to answer each question to the best of your ability, being honest about your level of knowledge and experience.
* You can skip questions that are not applicable or relevant to your skills.
* Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **python_knowledge** (`python_knowledge`, text, required/optional): This section assesses your basic knowledge of Python syntax, concepts, and features.
* **python_knowledge_2** (`python_knowledge_2`, text, required/optional): This section is similar to python_knowledge, but it's a separate question that may cover additional topics or nuances in Python knowledge.
* **knowledge_of_python** (`knowledge of python`, text, required/optional): This section evaluates your understanding of the Python ecosystem, including its applications, libraries, and best practices.
* **programming skills** (`programming skills`, text, required/optional): This section asks you to rate your proficiency in programming skills such as debugging, testing, and problem-solving.
* **python_experience** (`python experience`, text, required/optional): This section asks you to describe your experience with Python projects, including the duration and type of projects you've completed.
* **python_proficiency** (`python proficiency`, number, required/optional): This section asks you to rate your proficiency in Python programming, including your ability to write efficient and effective code, debug, and optimize it.
