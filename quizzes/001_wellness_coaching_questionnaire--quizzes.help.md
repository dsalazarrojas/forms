# Wellness Coaching Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wellness Coaching Questionnaire is a tool designed to gather information about an individual's general well-being, goals, and health status. It is meant to be used by wellness coaches or healthcare professionals to assess a client's overall health and wellness, providing them with a comprehensive understanding of their needs and priorities. This form helps coaches and professionals to identify areas that require attention and guidance, facilitating more effective coaching and treatment plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Start by filling out the General Information page**, which includes basic details such as name, contact information, and any relevant medical history.
2. **On the Goals page**, describe your goals and objectives related to your well-being, such as improving physical health, increasing mental clarity, or enhancing relationships.
3. **On the Health Information page**, provide details about your current health status, including any medical conditions, medications, or health concerns you are currently experiencing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **General Information (page 1)**
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Information**: Provide your email address, phone number, or any other relevant contact details.
* **Medical History**: Describe any medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies that may be relevant to your health.

### **Goals (page 2)**
* **Goals**: Describe your short-term and long-term goals related to your well-being, such as improving physical health, increasing mental clarity, or enhancing relationships.

### **Health Information (page 3)**
* **Health Status**: Describe your current health status, including any medical conditions, medications, or health concerns you are currently experiencing.

**Tips**

* Please be as honest and thorough as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact us at form@gic.mx.
* This form is for your personal use and confidentiality is ensured.
