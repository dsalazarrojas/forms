# internet-governance-knowledge-quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to test your knowledge on internet governance-related topics, specifically DNS and net neutrality.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. For each question, provide a clear and concise response in the text box provided.
3. Make sure to provide a response for every question, as they are all required to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is DNS?** (`dns_question`, text, required): This is the first question that asks you to describe what you understand DNS (Domain Name System) to be.
* **What is DNS? (`dns_answer`, text, required)**: This question is a second opportunity to confirm your understanding of DNS. Please make sure to answer correctly based on the previous question's response.
* **What is net neutrality? (`net_neutrality_question`, text, required)**: This is the first question that asks you to describe what you understand net neutrality to be.
* **What is net neutrality? (`net_neutrality_answer`, text, required)**: This is a second chance to confirm your understanding of net neutrality.
* **What is a fundamental governance principle? (`governance_principle_question`, text, required)**: This question assesses your understanding of the fundamental principles that govern internet governance.

## Tips
* Please answer each question to the best of your ability, based on your current knowledge.
* If you are unsure of any question, try to make an educated guess.
* Make sure to proofread your responses for clarity and accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact "form@gic.mx".
