# Cicerone Discourse Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to evaluate users' knowledge of various beer-related topics, including brewing processes, styles, and terminology.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Your Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
2. **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address where you would like to receive the results of this quiz.
3. **Beer Knowledge Experience Level**: Choose your level of experience with beer, from Beginner to Cicerone certified.
4. **Question 1: Bitter Flavor**: Select the primary ingredient that gives beer its bitter flavor.
5. **Question 2: Fruity Flavor**: Choose the beer style known for its high ABV and fruity esters.
6. **Question 3: IBU Definition**: Select the definition of IBU in beer terminology.
7. **Fermentation Temperature**: Determine the temperature range at which lager yeast ferments.
8. **Pilsner Origin**: Choose the country most known for Pilsner beer.
9. **Hopping Process**: Select the hopping process that occurs at the end of the boil.
10. **British Beer Style**: Choose a British beer style.
11. **Reinheitsgebot**: Select the German Beer Purity Law.
12. **Fermentation Time**: Determine the fermentation time for a typical lager.
13. **Alcohol Content**: Select the typical alcohol content of an Imperial Stout.
14. **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the quiz.
