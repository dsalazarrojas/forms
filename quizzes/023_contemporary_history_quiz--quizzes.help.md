# Contemporary History Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contemporary History Quiz is designed to evaluate your knowledge of significant historical events that occurred in the 20th century. It will assess your ability to identify key figures, dates, and events that shaped the world in that time period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the quiz, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student details, including your full name and identification number. These fields are required.
2. Select the correct date for the Quiz Date.
3. Choose the correct answers for the five history questions. Each question has four options.
4. Confirm that you have completed the quiz independently.
5. Optionally, provide any comments or questions you may have.
6. Confirm your quiz submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Full Name** (Student Name, text, required): This field should be filled with your full name as recorded in school records.
* **Student Identification Number** (Student ID, text, required): Please enter your school-issued ID number.
* **Quiz Date** (Quiz Date, date, required): Enter the date you took the quiz.
* **Who was the first person to walk on the moon?** (Question 1, select one, required): Choose the correct historical figure who was the first person to walk on the moon.
* **In what year did the Berlin Wall fall?** (Question 2, select one, required): Select the correct year the Berlin Wall fell.
* **Who was the leader of the Soviet Union during the Cuban Missile Crisis?** (Question 3, select one, required): Choose the correct leader of the Soviet Union during the Cuban Missile Crisis.
* **What was the primary reason for the start of World War I?** (Question 4, select one, required): Select the primary reason for the start of World War I.
* **In what year did the United Nations form?** (Question 5, select one, required): Choose the correct year the United Nations formed.

## Tips
* Please ensure you complete the form independently.
* Answer each question carefully, as the correct answers are based on historical facts.
* Review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or comments, you can provide them in the additional comments section.
