# post_test_on_safety_and_performance - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on the safety and performance of a specific test. It is intended for use by individuals who need to evaluate their knowledge, confidence, and actions related to safety and performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the form's pages and fields.
2. For each field, provide the requested information as accurately and thoroughly as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Post Test on Safety and Performance** (`post_test_on_safety_and_performance`, `text`, **required**): Enter a brief title or description for the test.
- **Safety Knowledge** (`safety_knowledge`, `select_one`, **optional**): Select one of the options to indicate your level of safety knowledge.
- **Safety Confidence** (`safety_confidence`, `text`, **optional**): Describe your level of safety confidence for the test.
- **Correct Actions** (`correct_actions`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Select all the actions that you took during the test.
- **Correct Actions (Others)** (`correct_actions_others`, `text`, **optional**): If there are any other actions you took during the test that are not listed, describe them here.
- **Correct Actions (Others) How** (`correct_actions_others_how`, `text`, **optional**): If you described any other actions in the previous field, describe how you took those actions.
- **Correct Actions (Others) Where** (`correct_actions_others_where`, `text`, **optional**): If you described any other actions in the previous field, describe where you took those actions.
- **Correct Actions (Others) How Many** (`correct_actions_others_how_many`, `text`, **optional**): If you described any other actions in the previous field, describe how many actions you took.
- **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, `text`, **optional**): Describe the safety equipment you used during the test.
- **Safety Equipment (Others)** (`safety_equipment_others`, `text`, **optional**): If you used any other safety equipment during the test, describe it here.
- **Safety Equipment (Others) How** (`safety_equipment_others_how`, `text`, **optional**): If you described any other safety equipment in the previous field, describe how you used it.
- **Safety Equipment (Others) Where** (`safety_equipment_others_where`, `text`, **optional**): If you described any other safety equipment in the previous field, describe where you used it.
- **Safety Equipment (Others) How Many** (`safety_equipment_others_how_many`, `text`, **optional**): If you described any other safety equipment in the previous field, describe how many of it you used.
- **Safety Equipment (Others) Type** (`safety_equipment_others_type`, `text`, **optional**): If you described any other safety equipment in the previous field, describe its type.
- **Supervisor** (`supervisor`, `text`, **optional**): Describe your supervisor's role or involvement during the test.
- **Supervisor (Others)** (`supervisor_others`, `text`, **optional**): If your supervisor was involved in the test, describe them here.
- **Supervisor (Others) How** (`supervisor_others_how`, `text`, **optional**): If you described your supervisor in the previous field, describe how they were involved.
- **Supervisor (Others) Where** (`supervisor_others_where`, `text`, **optional**): If you described your supervisor in the previous field, describe where they were involved.
- **Supervisor (Others) How Many** (`supervisor_others_how_many`, `text`, **optional**): If you described your supervisor in the previous field, describe how many were involved.
- **Safety Training** (`safety_training`, `text`, **optional**): Describe any safety training you received prior to the test.
- **Safety Training (Others)** (`safety_training_others`, `text`, **optional**): If you received any other safety training prior to the test, describe it here.
- **Safety Training (Others) How** (`safety_training_others_how`, `text`, **optional**): If you described any other safety training in the previous field, describe how it was provided.
- **Safety Training (Others) Where** (`safety_training_others_where`, `text`, **optional**): If you described any other safety training in the previous field, describe where it was provided.
- **Safety Training (Others) How Many** (`safety_training_others_how_many`, `text`, **optional**): If you described any other safety training in the previous field, describe how many sessions you received.
- **Submission** (`submission`, `note`, **optional**): This field is for any additional comments or notes about the test.

Please note that the fields marked as **optional** can be left blank if you do not have information to provide for them.
