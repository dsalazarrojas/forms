# Stereochemistry Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your knowledge in the field of stereochemistry. Please answer the questions to the best of your ability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and email address in the first two fields.
2. Select the category that best matches your field of study or expertise.
3. Enter your score out of a certain amount in the third field.
4. Optionally, add any additional comments or information in the fourth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`text`, required/optional): Please enter your name for identification purposes. This field is not required.
* **Email** (`email`, required/optional): Please enter your email address for contact purposes. This field is not required.
* **Category** (`select_one`, required/optional): Please select the category that best matches your field of study or expertise. You can choose from Chemistry, Physics, or Biology.
* **Score** (`number`, required/optional): Please enter your score out of a certain amount. The exact amount will be provided in the form instructions.
* **Note** (`note`, required/optional): Please add any additional comments or information you think is relevant to your assessment. This field is optional.
