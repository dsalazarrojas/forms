# loungewear_recommendation_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The loungewear recommendation quiz is designed to suggest suitable loungewear based on your preferences and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Scroll through the form pages and click on the "Complete" button at the end of each page to navigate to the next page.
2. Answer all questions to the best of your ability, and provide honest responses.
3. Use the options provided for questions with multiple options.
4. Enter your email address and phone number (if applicable) to receive your recommendations.
