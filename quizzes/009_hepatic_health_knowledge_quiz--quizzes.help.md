# hepatic_health_knowledge_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

This help guide is for the <b>hepatic_health_knowledge_quiz</b> form. This form is designed to assess your knowledge on hepatic health, a crucial aspect of overall bodily functions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully, and provide a brief response to each question.
2. Choose the correct options from the dropdown menu when you see one.
3. Enter your answers in the provided text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hepatic Health Knowledge** (`hepatic_health_knowledge`): Select all the options that apply to your understanding of the liver's function.
* **What is the function of the hepatic bile?** (`question_1`): Please provide a brief description of the bile's function in the liver.
* **Answer** (`answer_1`): Enter your answer to the question above.
* **What is the role of the hepatic bile?** (`question_2`): Select one of the options to indicate your understanding of the liver's bile role.
* **Answer** (`answer_2`): Enter your answer to the question above.
* **What are the risks of liver disease?** (`question_3`): This field is for you to note any risks associated with liver disease.
* **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors`): Enter any risk factors that you are aware of related to liver disease.
* **How does liver disease manifest?** (`question_4`): Select one of the options to indicate your understanding of how liver disease manifests.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`): Enter any symptoms you have experienced or are aware of related to liver disease.
* **What is the importance of healthy living?** (`question_5`): Select one of the options to indicate your understanding of the importance of healthy living.
* **Answer** (`answer_5`): Enter your answer to the question above.
* **What are the key takeaways?** (`question_6`): Select all the options that apply to your understanding of the key takeaways.
* **Key Takeaways** (`key_takeaways`): Enter your key takeaways from the knowledge gained.
* **Summary of Knowledge** (`question_7`): This field is for you to note a summary of your understanding.
* **Summary** (`summary`): Enter a brief summary of your knowledge.
* **Additional Comments** (`question_8`): Add any additional comments or thoughts you'd like to share.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any further comments or feedback you'd like to provide.
