# Potato Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess your knowledge about potatoes. It is a series of questions designed to gauge your understanding of various aspects of potatoes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:
1. Answer the "Form Questions" page, which contains the main questions about potatoes.
2. Provide honest and accurate answers to each question.
3. Once you have completed all the questions, click on the "Submit Form" button to submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Questions**: This page contains the main questions about potatoes. You will be asked about your favorite food type, what you like about the food, how many times you have eaten the food, if you have any food allergies, and when you last ate the food.
* **What type of food do you like**: Select the correct type of food that you like (Yes or No).
* **What's your favorite food type**: Choose all the types of food that you like (Yes, No, or multiple options).
* **What do you like about this food**: Answer in text, describing what you like about the food.
* **How many times have you had the food**: Enter the number of times you have eaten the food.
* **Do you have any food allergies**: Choose all the allergies you have (Yes or No).
* **When did you eat the food last time**: Enter the date you last ate the food.
* **How many people did you eat the food with**: Answer in text, describing how many people you ate the food with.
* **Submit Form**: Click this button to submit your answers and complete the quiz.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
- Make sure to complete all the questions before submitting the form.
