# Salad Ingredients Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Salad Ingredients Knowledge Quiz" is a form designed to assess your knowledge of salad ingredients. It asks you to select some suggested salad ingredients and choose a salad dressing to go with them. You will also have the option to provide additional information such as the date and time of the quiz, a note about your experience, your email, phone number, and the name and contact information of your trainer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select Suggested Salad Ingredients**: Choose some suggested salad ingredients from the list. You can select multiple ingredients.
2. Choose a **Salad Dressing**: Select a salad dressing that you think pairs well with the salad ingredients you have chosen.
3. (Optional) Enter a **Date**: If you have taken the quiz on a specific date, you can enter it here.
4. (Optional) Enter a **Time**: If you took the quiz at a specific time, you can enter it here.
5. (Optional) Add a **Note**: If you want to share something about your experience, you can add a note.
6. (Optional) Enter your **Email**: If you want to be contacted with the results, you can enter your email address here.
7. (Optional) Enter your **Phone Number**: If you prefer to be contacted by phone, you can enter your phone number here.
8. (Optional) Enter your **Trainer Name**: If you have a trainer, you can enter their name here.
9. (Optional) Enter your **Trainer Email**: If you have a trainer and want to be contacted by them, you can enter their email address here.
10. (Optional) Enter your **Trainer Phone Number**: If you have a trainer and want to be contacted by them, you can enter their phone number here.
