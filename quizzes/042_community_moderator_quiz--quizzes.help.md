# Community Moderator Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from community moderators to assess their suitability for moderation duties. The questions and fields in this form are designed to evaluate their experience, approach, and qualifications for moderation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the fields in the order they appear on the page. You can navigate between pages using the "Next" and "Previous" buttons.
2.  For each field, provide a clear and concise answer to the question.
3.  Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
4.  Click "Submit" when you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Candidate name** (`candidate_name`, text, required)
Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.

### 2. **Email address** (`email`, email, required)
Provide your email address for follow-up and test results.

### 3. **Scenario 1 response** (`scenario_response_1`, text, required)
Describe how you would handle a situation where a member is posting disruptive comments.

### 4. **Scenario 2 response** (`scenario_response_2`, text, required)
Explain your approach to resolving conflicts between members.

### 5. **Years of moderation experience** (`moderation_experience_years`, number, optional)
If you have experience moderating communities, enter the number of years.

### 6. **Availability for moderation duties** (`availability_for_moderation`, text, optional)
Enter the days or hours you are available for moderation duties.

### 7. **Familiarity with policies** (`familiarity_with_policies`, select_one, required)
Rate your level of familiarity with community policies: Very familiar, Somewhat familiar, or Not familiar.

### 8. **References** (`references`, text, optional)
Provide any relevant references or past moderation examples.

### 9. **Consent to background check** (`consent_background_check`, select_one, optional)
Indicate your willingness to consent to a background check: True or False.

### 10. **Consent background check** is not visible in the help guide as it is not a "visible" field. 
Please note that the **Consent to background check** field is optional and only appears if required in the form configuration.
