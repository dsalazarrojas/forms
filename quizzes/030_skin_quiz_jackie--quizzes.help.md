# skin_quiz_jackie - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you create and configure a quiz in the "quizzes" category.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Quiz Name` field with the name of your quiz.
2. Provide a brief `Description` of your quiz.
3. If your quiz has a start time, enter it in the `Start Time` field. Similarly, if your quiz has an end time, enter it in the `End Time` field.
4. Enter the `Location` of your quiz, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quiz Name** (`quiz_name`, **text**, required/optional): Enter the name of your quiz. This will be the title that identifies your quiz.
* **Description** (`description`, **text**, required/optional): Provide a short description of your quiz. This will help users understand what to expect.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, **time**, required/optional): If your quiz has a start time, enter the date and time when the quiz will begin. You can format the time as `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM`.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, **time**, required/optional): If your quiz has an end time, enter the date and time when the quiz will end. You can format the time as `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM`.
* **Location** (`location`, **text, required/optional): Enter the location where your quiz will take place, if applicable.
