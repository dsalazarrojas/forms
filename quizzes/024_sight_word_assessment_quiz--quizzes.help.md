# Sight Word Assessment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool to help assess students' performance with sight words. It collects information on the student's name, grade level, experience with sight words, and time spent reading sight words. This data will help identify areas of improvement for reading skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "What is your name?" field.
2. Enter your grade level in the "What is your grade level?" field.
3. Answer the questions about your experience with sight words and time spent reading sight words.
4. Select the sight words you have read correctly or incorrectly.
5. Enter the total time spent reading and total sight words read.
6. Answer questions about which sight words you have missed.
7. Enter the time spent reading the second set of questions.
8. Select which correct sight words you have read.
9. Select which sight words you have read.
10. Enter your student ID in the "Your ID?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name?** (student_name, text, required): Enter your name.
* **What is your grade level?** (grade_level, number, required): Enter your grade level in numbers.
* **Have you ever seen this kind of words?** (experience_with_sight_words, select_one, required): Indicate your experience with sight words, either True if you have or False if you haven't.
* **Which sight words have you read correctly?** (correct_sight_words_read, select_multiple, required): Select all the sight words you have read correctly.
* **How many minutes have you spent reading these words?** (time_spent_reading_sight_words, number, required): Enter the total time spent reading sight words.
* **Total time spent reading (minutes)?** (total_time_spent_reading, number, required): Enter the total time spent reading.
* **Total words read?** (total_words_read, number, required): Enter the total number of words read.
* **Which sight words have you missed?** (sight_words_missed, select_multiple, required): Select all the sight words you have missed.
* **Time Spent Reading Total** (time_spent_reading_total, number, required): Enter the total time spent reading.
* **Total sight words?** (total_sight_words, number, required): Enter the total number of sight words.
* **Which correct sight words have you read?** (correct_sight_words_read_2, select_one, required): Select all the correct sight words you have read.
* **Time Spent Reading Sight Words 2** (time_spent_reading_sight_words_2, number, required): Enter the total time spent reading the second set of questions.
* **Which sight words have you read?** (sight_words_read, select_multiple, required): Select all the sight words you have read.
* **Your ID?** (student_id, text, required): Enter your student ID.

## Tips
- Please answer questions honestly and accurately.
- Make sure to select all relevant options for each question.
- Enter numerical values in the correct format (e.g., 30 minutes for 30 minutes).
- If you need help or clarification, ask your teacher or instructor for assistance.
