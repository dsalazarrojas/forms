# Trapping Techniques Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about trapping techniques, specifically for a quiz.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions as they appear on each page. The form has 7 pages, each with a unique question.
2. Each question is a text field where you can provide your response.
3. You can skip questions that are not required or provide any information you feel is relevant to the topic.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (page 1): This page is an introduction to the form.
* **Trapping Practices** (page 2): Please provide your experience with trapping practices.
* **Animal Hazards** (page 3): Describe any hazards you've encountered while trapping animals.
* **Trapping Locations** (page 4): Share your experience with where you trap animals.
* **Animal Handling** (page 5): Share your experience with handling animals.
* **Trap Selection** (page 6): Describe your approach to selecting traps.
* **Quiz Conclusion** (page 7): This page is a final review of your answers.

## Tips
- Be as detailed and honest as possible in your responses.
- You are not required to complete all questions, but it's helpful to answer as many as you can.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact form@gic.mx.
