# Wallets Recommendation Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wallets Recommendation Quiz is designed to help you find the perfect wallet based on your preferences and needs. It's a 10-question form that will guide you through different aspects of a wallet, such as size, material, color, price, style, purpose, features, budget, and submission of your preferred wallet type. Answer the questions honestly, and our quiz will recommend a set of wallets that best match your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by selecting an option from the "What type of wallet do you prefer?" question.
2.  Select multiple options for "What size is your wallet?" and "What material do you prefer for your wallet?"
3.  Choose multiple options for "What is your favorite color?" and "What features do you want in your wallet?"
4.  Choose a price range from "What is your price range?"
5.  Select multiple options for "What is your favorite style?"
6.  Select a purpose for "What is your wallet purpose?"
7.  Choose a combination of features that you'd like to have in your wallet.
8.  Select a budget level for "What is your budget?"
9.  Finally, provide a brief submission of your preferred wallet type (if any).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of wallet do you prefer?** (`start`, `select_one`): This is the starting point of the quiz. Choose from the options: Wallets, Purse, Pockets, Handbag, Backpack, Briefcase, Bag, or Other.
* **What size is your wallet?** (`size`, `select_multiple`): Select all the sizes that apply to you: 3x5, 4x6, 5x7, 6x8, 7x9, 8x10, 9x11, 10x12.
* **What material do you prefer for your wallet?** (`material`, `select_one`): Choose from the options: Leather, Fabric, Metal, Plastic, Wood, Card, or Other.
* **What is your favorite color?** (`color`, `select_multiple`): Select all the colors that you like: Black, Brown, Gray, White, Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, Purple.
* **What is your price range?** (`price`, `select_one`): Choose a price range from the options: Under 20, 20-50, 50-100, 100-200, 200-500, Over 500.
* **What is your favorite style?** (`style`, `select_multiple`): Select all the styles that you prefer: Classic, Modern, Slim, Large, Minimalist, Oversized, Retro, Vintage.
* **What is your wallet purpose?** (`purpose`, `select_one`): Choose a purpose for your wallet from the options: Daily, Travel, Formal, Semi-Formal, Casual.
* **What features do you want in your wallet?** (`features`, `select_multiple`): Select all the features that you would like to have in your wallet: Zip, Magnetic, RFID, RFID+RFID, Camera, GPS, NFC, Card, Coin.
* **What is your budget?** (`budget`, `select_one`): Choose a budget level from the options: Low, Medium, High, Very High.
* **Recommended Wallets** (`submit`, `text`): Enter a brief description of the type of wallet you prefer, and we'll do the rest to recommend the perfect wallet for you.
