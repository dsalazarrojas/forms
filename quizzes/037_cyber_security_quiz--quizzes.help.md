The cyber security quiz is a form designed to evaluate a user's knowledge of security fundamentals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and choose the correct answer from the options provided.

3. Once you have answered all the questions, click on the "Submit" button to submit your quiz.
