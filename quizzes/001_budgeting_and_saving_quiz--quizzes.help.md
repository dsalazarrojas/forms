# Budgeting And Saving Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This quiz is designed to assess your current financial habits and knowledge, and provide you with personalized feedback on areas where you can improve your financial well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly, choosing the best response that reflects your current financial situation and habits.
2. Select the correct option for each question, taking into account your personal experiences and financial goals.
3. Provide your email address if you would like to receive personalized financial guidance after completing the quiz.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you track your monthly budget?** (`monthly_budget_tracking`): This question helps us understand how often you review your income and expenses. Choose your response based on your usual practice:
	+ Daily: You regularly track your expenses and income daily.
	+ Weekly: You review your budget on a weekly basis.
	+ Monthly: You review your budget once a month.
	+ Never: You don't track your budget regularly.
* **What percentage of your income do you typically save each month?** (`savings_rate_percentage`): Estimate the percentage of your income that you usually save each month.
* **Do you have an emergency fund with 3-6 months of expenses?** (`emergency_fund_status`): Choose this option if you have a fund to cover 3-6 months of essential expenses.
* **How do you manage your debt payments?** (`debt_management_approach`): Select how you usually approach debt repayment:
	+ Pay minimum only: You only pay the minimum payment required.
	+ Pay more than minimum: You pay more than the minimum payment.
	+ Debt consolidation plan: You have a plan to consolidate your debt.
	+ No current debt: You don't have any debt.
* **Do you have specific savings goals for the next 12 months?** (`savings_goal_awareness`): Choose this option if you have a clear plan for your savings.
* **Rate your confidence in achieving your financial goals** (`financial_confidence`): Assess your confidence level in reaching your financial goals:
	+ Very confident
	+ Somewhat confident
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat uncertain
	+ Very uncertain
* **How comfortable are you creating a personal budget?** (`budgeting_knowledge`): Select how comfortable you are with creating a personal budget:
	+ Very comfortable
	+ Somewhat comfortable
	+ Neutral
	+ Could use help
	+ Very unfamiliar
* **Have you invested in stocks, bonds, or retirement accounts?** (`investment_experience`): Choose your level of investment experience:
	+ Extensive experience
	+ Some experience
	+ Minimal experience
	+ No experience
* **Interest areas for financial education** (`financial_education_interest`): Select all areas that interest you for further education:
	+ Budgeting techniques
	+ Debt elimination
	+ Investment strategies
	+ Tax planning
	+ Retirement planning
	+ Saving for education
* **What are your biggest financial challenges?** (`current_challenges`): Choose all that apply:
	+ Overspending
	+ Low savings rate
	+ High debt
	+ Irregular income
	+ Lack of knowledge
	+ No clear goals
* **How do you prefer to learn about finances?** (`preferred_learning_method`): Select your preferred learning method:
	+ Online courses
	+ In-person workshops
	+ One-on-one coaching
	+ Books and articles
	+ Videos and webinars
* **Will you implement at least one money-saving strategy this month?** (`action_steps_commitment`): Confirm your commitment to implementing a money-saving strategy.
* **May we contact you with personalized financial guidance?** (`contact_follow_up`): Choose if you'd like to receive follow-up communication:
	+ Yes, email
	+ Yes, phone
	+ Yes, either
	+ No thanks
* **Your email address** (`respondent_email`): Enter your email address if you'd like to receive follow-up communication.
