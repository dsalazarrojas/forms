# Complex Text Comprehension Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess your reading comprehension skills by evaluating your ability to understand advanced texts. It is designed to test your ability to recall information, make inferences, analyze and interpret text, and provide written responses to comprehension questions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the provided text carefully and answer the questions based on your understanding.
2. For each question, select the answer that best matches your understanding of the text.
3. For written response questions, provide a concise and clear answer in 2-3 sentences.
4. Rate your confidence level and difficulty in completing the text.
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback about the survey.
