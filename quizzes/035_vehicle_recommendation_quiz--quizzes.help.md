# vehicle_recommendation_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vehicle recommendation quiz is designed to help us understand your vehicle preferences and needs. Your responses will help us tailor our recommendations to provide you with the best possible solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully, selecting the correct options for each field.
2. For fields with text input, provide a brief description or answer.
3. Do not duplicate your responses if you have multiple vehicles.
4. If you have any issues or concerns, contact us at your email address on file.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What type of vehicle do you drive?** (Select one: SUV, Truck, Van)
	+ Select the type of vehicle that best describes your current vehicle.
* **What year is your vehicle?** (Select one: 2015, 2016, 2017)
	+ Select the year of your current vehicle.
* **What model is your vehicle?** (Text input)
	+ Enter the make and model of your vehicle, e.g., Toyota Camry.
* **What color is your vehicle?** (Select one: Red, Blue, Green)
	+ Select the color of your vehicle.
* **What is the vehicle's condition?** (Select multiple: New, Used, Certified Pre-Owned)
	+ Select all the conditions that apply to your vehicle.
* **How many miles is your vehicle?** (Number input)
	+ Enter the exact mileage of your vehicle.
* **What type of engine does your vehicle have?** (Select one: Gasoline, Diesel, Electric)
	+ Select the type of engine in your vehicle.
* **What is the primary purpose of your vehicle?** (Select one: Commute, Road Trip, Off-Road)
	+ Select the primary purpose of your vehicle, e.g., daily driving or long trips.
* **Other (please describe)** (Text input)
	+ If none of the above options describe your vehicle's purpose, provide a brief explanation.
* **Please describe** (Text input)
	+ Enter any additional information about your vehicle's purpose.
* **Email** (Email input)
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (Text input)
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (Text input)
	+ Enter your address.
* **Name** (Text input)
	+ Enter your name.
* **City** (Text input)
	+ Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (Text input)
	+ Enter the state where you reside.
* **Zip** (Text input)
	+ Enter your zip code.

## Tips
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at your email address on file.
* If you've already filled out this form, you may have duplicated your response. Please let us know and we will ensure to correct it.
* Please ensure that your responses are accurate and complete to help us provide the best possible recommendations.
