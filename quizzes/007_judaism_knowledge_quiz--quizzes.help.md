# Judaism Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a quiz related to Judaism, specifically for tracking the performance of students in a class. It is not intended for any other purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Class Type" field by selecting one of the available options.
2. Provide the "Class Name" in the text field.
3. Optionally, enter the date when the quiz was taken.
4. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
5. Enter the student's grade in the "Student Grade" field.
6. Enter the teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
7. Enter the teacher's email in the "Teacher Email" field.
8. Optionally, enter the quiz score.
9. Enter any additional comments about the quiz in the "Comments" field.
10. Select whether the quiz was submitted by the student or not.
11. Select whether the assigned tool was used or not.
12. Enter the form ID.
13. Optionally, enter the category and description of the quiz.
14. If desired, select additional form IDs.
15. Optionally, select additional submitted by and assigned tool options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Class Type** (`Yes/No`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to indicate whether the class is a yes or no answer type.
* **Class Name** (``, text, required: false): Enter the name of the class where the quiz was taken.
* **Quiz Date** (``, date, required: false): Enter the date when the quiz was taken.
* **Class Location** (``, text, required: false): Enter the location where the class took place.
* **Student Name** (``, text, required: false): Enter the name of the student who took the quiz.
* **Student Grade** (``, number, required: false): Enter the grade of the student who took the quiz.
* **Teacher Name** (``, text, required: false): Enter the name of the teacher who gave the quiz.
* **Teacher Email** (``, email, required: false): Enter the email of the teacher who gave the quiz.

* **Comments** (``, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments about the quiz.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* If a field is optional, only fill it out if it's relevant to the quiz.
* Review your answers for accuracy before submitting.
