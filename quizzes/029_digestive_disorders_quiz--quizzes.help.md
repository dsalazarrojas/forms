# Digestive Disorders Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals identify potential digestive disorders based on their symptoms and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the introduction section and read about the possible digestive disorders.
2. Select the options that best describe your symptoms in the "symptoms" section.
3. Answer each question in the "questions" section by selecting one or multiple options.
4. Once you have filled out all the sections, submit the form to generate your results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Introduction (Page 1)
* **Introduction**: This is an informational page that provides background information about the possible digestive disorders. Read this section carefully to understand the potential issues you may be experiencing.

### 2. Digestive Disorders (Page 2)
* **Digestive Disorders**: This is a list of potential digestive disorders. Select the option that best describes your condition.
	+ **Crohn's disease**: A type of inflammatory bowel disease that affects the small intestine.
	+ **Ulcerative colitis**: A type of inflammatory bowel disease that affects the colon.
	+ **Celiac disease**: An autoimmune disorder that causes the small intestine to react to gluten.
	+ **Irritable bowel syndrome (IBS)**: A condition that causes abdominal pain and changes in bowel habits.
	+ **Other**: If your symptoms don't match any of the above options, select "Other" to provide more information about your condition.

### 3. Symptoms (Page 3)
* **Symptoms**: Select all the symptoms that you are experiencing.
	+ **Nausea and vomiting**: Feeling sick to your stomach and vomiting.
	+ **Abdominal pain**: Pain in the abdominal area.
	+ **Blood in stool**: Blood in your stool.
	+ **Diarrhea**: Loose, watery stools.
	+ **Abdominal distension**: Feeling bloated or swollen in the abdominal area.
	+ **Weight loss**: Losing weight unintentionally.
	+ **Other symptoms**: If your symptoms don't match any of the above options, select "Other" to describe your symptoms.
