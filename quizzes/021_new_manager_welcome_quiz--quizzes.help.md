# New Manager Welcome Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Manager Welcome Quiz is a tool to help onboard new managers by gathering crucial information to ensure a smooth transition and a successful tenure in the team.

## How To Complete This Form
* Review each field carefully and provide accurate answers to all questions.
* Select the correct options from dropdowns and list selections.
* Enter all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Manager Info**: Enter the name of the new manager you are filling out this form for.
* **Reporting Lines**: This question determines who the new manager reports directly to. Select from the options: Line Manager, Team Lead, or Other.
* **Manager Role**: Choose the role of the new manager. Select from the options: HR, IT, or Admin.
* **Years of Experience**: Enter the number of years of direct management experience the new manager has.
* **Manager Experience**: Enter the number of years of management experience in your company.
* **Department**: Enter the department this new manager will be working in.
* **Team Members**: Select the number of team members this new manager will be supervising. Choose from the options: 1-5, 6-10, 11-15, or More than 15.
* **Reporting Lines Manager Title**: Enter the title of the new manager's reporting lines manager.
* **Reporting Lines Manager Name**: Enter the name of the new manager's reporting lines manager.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of the new manager.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the new manager.
* **Reporting Lines Name**: Enter the name of the new manager's reporting lines manager.
* **Reporting Lines Email**: Enter the email address of the new manager's reporting lines manager.

Note: If you're unsure of any information, please contact the relevant department or manager to ensure accuracy.
