# Saint Biography Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Saint Biography Quiz is a comprehensive form designed to collect biographical information about a saint. It aims to gather detailed data about the saint's life, including their personal attributes and characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" in each section.
2. For fields that are not marked as required, provide information if you have it available.
3. Make sure to review each field carefully and ensure you are providing accurate information.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the saint's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the saint's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the saint's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the saint's phone number (if available).
* **Saint's Birthday** (`saint_birthday`, date, optional): Enter the saint's birth date (if known).
* **Saint's Belongings** (`saint_belongings`, select_multiple, optional): Select any attributes that apply to the saint, such as "Attribute 1", "Attribute 2", etc.
* **Saint's Belief** (`saint_belief`, text, optional): Enter any relevant belief or attributes that define the saint.
* **Saint's Place of Rest** (`saint_placeofrest`, text, optional): Enter the saint's place of rest or burial (if known).
* **Biography** (`saint_biography`, text, optional): Provide a brief biography of the saint.
* **Occupation** (`saint_occupation`, text, optional): Enter the saint's occupation or profession.
* **Achievement** (`saint_achievement`, text, optional): Enter any notable achievements or accomplishments of the saint.
* **Second Achievement** (`saint_achievement_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional notable achievements or accomplishments of the saint.
* **Second Place of Rest** (`saint_placeofrest_2`, text, optional): Enter the saint's second place of rest or burial (if known).
* **Second Belongings** (`saint_belongings_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional attributes that apply to the saint, such as "Attribute 1", "Attribute 2", etc.
