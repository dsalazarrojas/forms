# Physics Practice Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physics Practice Quiz is a form designed to collect information from students about their physics practice quiz submissions. This includes details about the student's name, quiz topic, question, and answer, as well as submission information such as date, time, location, and status. The form helps instructors to keep track of student submissions and gather data for assessment and evaluation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the corresponding information and submit. Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and approvals before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Topic** (`topic`, select_one, required): Choose the topic of the quiz from the options provided. Options are: 'Yes', 'No'.
* **Quiz Question** (`quiz_question`, text, required): Describe the quiz question you were asked, or the problem you were given.
* **Quiz Answer** (`quiz_answer`, text, required): Enter your answer to the quiz question.
* **Study Group** (`study_group`, text, optional): If you were in a study group, enter the name of your group here.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required): Enter the time of your submission.
* **Submission Location** (`submission_location`, text, optional): If you submitted your work from a specific location, enter that location here.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or notes about your submission here.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your instructor.
* **Instructor Email** (`instructor_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of your instructor.
* **Instructor Phone** (`instructor_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your instructor.
* **Quiz Topics** (`quiz_topics`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable topics from the options provided. Options are: Physics, Chemistry, Biology.
* **Topic Frequency** (`topic_frequency`, text, optional): If you would like to report how often you practice this topic, enter that frequency here.
* **Quiz Duration** (`quiz_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the quiz.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required): Choose who submitted the quiz from the options provided. Options are: chatjim, chatjimmy.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your submission. Options are: Completed, Incomplete.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about your submission here.
* **Submission Notes** (`submission_notes`, note, optional): Add any notes or comments about your submission here.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, date, required): Enter the date you submitted the quiz.
* **Submitted At Time** (`submitted_at_time`, time, required): Enter the time you submitted the quiz.
* **Submission Date Time** (`submission_date_time`, date, required): Enter the date and time you submitted the quiz.
* **Submission Date Time** (`submission_date_time_time`, time, required): Enter the time of your submission.
