# Yes Or No Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Yes Or No Survey Template is a simple form designed to collect binary responses from end users. It consists of 8 yes or no questions, each asking a single question.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question with a simple "yes" or "no" to the question asked.
2. Make sure to answer all questions, as there is no need to skip any of them.
3. Double-check your answers to ensure you have answered all questions correctly.
4. Once you have completed all questions, submit the form.
