# Lunar New Year Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lunar New Year Quiz is a form designed to gather information about the Lunar New Year traditions and practices of individuals. This form will help us better understand how people around the world celebrate this important cultural event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question honestly to the best of your ability.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required") with your information.
3. For optional fields, choose the options that best fit your experiences and traditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Years Lived** (2): This field asks how many years you have lived during the Lunar New Year. Please enter a number.
* **Birth Date** (3): Enter your birth date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Favourite Holiday** (4): Choose one or more of the following options for your favourite Lunar New Year holiday:
	+ Chinese New Year
	+ Japanese New Year
	+ Korean New Year
	+ Other (please specify in the "Other" field below)
* **Holiday** (5): Choose the one holiday that you celebrate the most during the Lunar New Year:
	+ Chinese New Year
	+ Japanese New Year
	+ Korean New Year
	+ Other (please specify in the "Other" field below)
* **Family** (6): Do you celebrate the Lunar New Year with your family? (Yes, No, Other)
* **Friends** (7): Do you celebrate the Lunar New Year with your friends? (Yes, No, Other)
* **Food** (8): What foods do you enjoy during the Lunar New Year? (Select one or more of the following options)
	+ Kimchi
	+ Sushi
	+ Tangyuan
	+ Other (please specify in the "Other" field below)
* **Decor** (9): What types of decorations do you have during the Lunar New Year? (Select one or more of the following options)
	+ Red envelopes
	+ Chinese couplets
	+ Lanterns
	+ Other (please specify in the "Other" field below)
* **Money** (10): What types of gifts do you receive or give during the Lunar New Year? (Select one or more of the following options)
	+ Red envelopes
	+ Gifts
	+ Other (please specify in the "Other" field below)
* **Greeting** (11): Write a brief greeting for the Lunar New Year.
* **Message** (12): Write a message for the Lunar New Year.
* **Essay** (13): Write a short essay about your experiences and traditions during the Lunar New Year.
* **Video** (14): Upload a video related to the Lunar New Year.
* **Audio** (15): Upload an audio file related to the Lunar New Year.
* **File** (16): Upload a file related to the Lunar New Year.
* **Email** (17): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (18): Enter your phone number.
* **English** (19-24): Write your thoughts on the Lunar New Year in English.
* **Other English Fields** (25): Write anything else you want to say about the Lunar New Year in English.
