# Plant Preference Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Plant Preference Quiz is designed to help users determine their preferences for plants, including their preferred plant type, size, color, and frequency of use. It will also collect user information such as email, phone number, and other details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select your preferred category (e.g., Apple, Flower, Succulent) from the drop-down menu.
2.  Choose the type of plants you prefer to have indoors or outdoors.
3.  Select the size of the plants you prefer.
4.  Choose the color of the plants you like.
5.  Select the frequency at which you like to plant (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
6.  Choose how you would like to communicate with us about plants (e.g., Email, Phone, Text, Social Media).
7.  If applicable, select any other preferred plants.
8.  Select the type of house you live in (e.g., Condo, Apartment, House).
9.  Choose the purpose of having plants (e.g., Decoration, Meditation, Health, Hobby).
10.  If applicable, select any recommended plants.
11.  Finally, enter your email, phone number, first name, last name, and any additional information you'd like to provide.
