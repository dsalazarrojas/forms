# Student Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Quiz form is designed to collect data from students for assessment and feedback purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student name in the **Student Name** field.
2. Select your **Grade Level** from the drop-down menu.
3. Type your **Subject** (e.g., math, reading, science, etc.).
4. Select the **Question Type** (e.g., multiple choice, essay, etc.).
5. Choose a date for the quiz using the calendar icon in the **Quiz Date** field.
6. Enter the time the quiz was taken in the **Quiz Time** field.
7. Type your response to the question in the **Student Answer** field.
8. Enter the correct answer to the question in the **Correct Answer** field.
9. Enter your score for the quiz in the **Score** field.
10. Provide any additional **Quiz Comments** (e.g., feedback, notes, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official records.
* **Grade Level**: Select your current grade level (e.g., Grade 1, Grade 2, Grade 3, Grade 4, Grade 5, Grade 6).
* **Subject**: Type the name of the subject you were assessed on (e.g., math, reading, science, etc.).
* **Subject**: This field is not actually used in the form. I've left it here for the sake of completeness and to reflect the original intent of the form.
* **Question Type**: Select the type of question you were asked to answer (e.g., multiple choice, essay, etc.).
* **Quiz Date**: Choose a date for the quiz using the calendar icon.
* **Quiz Time**: Enter the time the quiz was taken.
* **Student Answer**: Type your answer to the question.
* **Correct Answer**: Enter the correct answer to the question.
* **Score**: Enter the score you received on the quiz.
* **Quiz Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback from the teacher or instructor.
