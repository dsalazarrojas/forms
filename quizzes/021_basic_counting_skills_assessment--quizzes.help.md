# Basic Counting Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess a student's basic counting skills, including counting from 1 to 5, 1 to 10, counting objects, and recognizing numerals. The form provides a comprehensive evaluation of the student's abilities and identifies areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the student's name, age, grade level, and teacher's name.
2. Answer the counting questions in each section, indicating whether the student can count from 1 to 5, 1 to 10, count objects, and recognize numerals.
3. Provide feedback on the student's strengths and areas for development.
4. Select the overall counting skill level and recommendations for practice.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student name** (`student_name`, text, required): Please enter the student's name as it appears on their official identification documents.
* **Student age** (`student_age`, number, optional): Please enter the student's age in years.
* **Grade level** (`grade_level`, text, optional): Please enter the student's grade level (e.g., Pre-K, Grade 1, etc.).
* **Assessment date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Please enter the date of the assessment.
* **Teacher name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as the teacher conducting the assessment.
* **Can the student count from 1 to 5?** (`count_1_5`, select_one, required): Check the box indicating whether the student can count from 1 to 5 correctly.
* **Are the numbers in correct order?** (`count_1_5_order`, select_one, optional): Check the box indicating whether the numbers are in correct order for the student.
* **Can the student count from 1 to 10?** (`count_1_10`, select_one, required): Check the box indicating whether the student can count from 1 to 10 correctly.
* **Counting accuracy for 1-10** (`count_1_10_accuracy`, select_one, optional): Select the level of counting accuracy for the student.
* **Can the student count 5 objects correctly?** (`count_objects_5`, select_one, required): Check the box indicating whether the student can count 5 objects correctly.
* **Can the student count 10 objects correctly?** (`count_objects_10`, select_one, required): Check the box indicating whether the student can count 10 objects correctly.
* **Counting method used** (`counting_method`, select_one, optional): Select the counting method used by the student (One-to-One Correspondence, Counting Aloud, Pointing, or Other).
* **Recognizes numerals 1-5?** (`recognize_numbers_1_5`, select_one, required): Check the box indicating whether the student recognizes numerals 1-5 correctly.
* **Recognizes numerals 1-10?** (`recognize_numbers_1_10`, select_one, required): Check the box indicating whether the student recognizes numerals 1-10 correctly.
* **Student strengths in counting** (`strengths`, text, optional): Provide feedback on the student's strengths in counting.
* **Areas for development** (`areas_development`, text, optional): Identify areas where the student needs improvement in counting.
* **Overall counting skill level** (`overall_level`, select_one, required): Select the overall counting skill level for the student (Above Grade Level, At Grade Level, Below Grade Level, or Needs Intervention).
* **Recommendations for practice** (`recommendations`, text, optional): Provide recommendations for practice to improve the student's counting skills.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when assessing the student's counting skills.
* Take note of any areas where the student shows improvement or struggle.
* Use the form to provide constructive feedback and recommendations for practice to help the student improve their counting skills.
