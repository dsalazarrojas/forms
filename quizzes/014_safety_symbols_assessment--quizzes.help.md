# Safety Symbols Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Symbols Assessment form is used to evaluate a user's understanding and perception of safety symbols or icons used to warn or caution users about potential hazards or dangers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reading and understanding the safety symbols provided in the introduction section.
2. Select the confidence level of your understanding on a scale of 1-5.
3. Identify and select the safety symbols that are most relevant to the hazards you encounter.
4. Provide any additional feedback or comments you have about the safety symbols.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (1, text, required/optional): This is where you'll start your assessment by reading and understanding the safety symbols provided. It's a text box where you can take notes or reference the safety symbols as you complete the form.
* **Confidence Level** (2, number, required/optional): This is a scale of 1-5 where you rate your confidence level in understanding the safety symbols. A higher number indicates a higher confidence.
* **Hazard Icons** (3, select_multiple, required): This is where you select the safety symbols that are most relevant to the hazards you encounter. Select all the symbols that apply to you, using the letters A, B, C, or D.
* **Feedback** (4, text, required/optional): This is an open text box where you can provide any additional feedback or comments about the safety symbols.
