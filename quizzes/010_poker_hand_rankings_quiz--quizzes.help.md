# Poker Hand Rankings Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Poker Hand Rankings Quiz is designed to test your knowledge of poker hand rankings. It will ask you a series of questions to determine the ranking of a given poker hand.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read each question carefully and choose the correct answer from the options provided.
* Select all applicable options for each question.
* Answer all questions to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Poker Hand Ranking** (`poker_hand_ranking`, number, required): This field asks you to select a ranking for the entire hand. Choose 'Yes' if the hand is a high-ranking hand, and 'No' if it's not.
* **2. Hand Ranking** (`hand_ranking`, select_multiple, optional): Select all rankings that apply to the hand from the options below.
* **3. Royal Flush** (`royal_flush`, select_one, optional): This refers to a hand that contains the Ace, King, Queen, Jack, and Jack of the same suit.
*   Options: Royal Flush, Straight Flush, Four of a Kind, Full House, Three of a Kind, Two Pairs, One Pair, No Hand
* **4. Straight Flush** (`straight_flush`, select_one, optional): This refers to a hand that consists of five cards in sequential order (e.g., 1-2-3-4-5) and all cards of the same suit.
*   Options: Four of a Kind, Full House, Three of a Kind, Two Pairs, One Pair, No Hand
* **5. Four of a Kind** (`four_of_a_kind`, select_one, optional): This refers to a hand that contains four cards of the same rank (e.g., four 7's).
*   Options: Full House, Three of a Kind, Two Pairs, One Pair, No Hand
* **6. Full House** (`full_house`, select_one, optional): This refers to a hand that contains three cards of one rank and two cards of another rank.
*   Options: Three of a Kind, Two Pairs, One Pair, No Hand
* **7. Three of a Kind** (`three_of_a_kind`, select_one, optional): This refers to a hand that contains three cards of the same rank.
*   Options: Two Pairs, One Pair, No Hand
* **8. Two Pairs** (`two_pairs`, select_one, optional): This refers to a hand that contains two pairs (two sets of two cards of the same rank).
*   Options: One Pair, No Hand
* **9. One Pair** (`one_pair`, select_one, optional): This refers to a hand that contains two cards of the same rank.
*   Options: No Hand
* **10. No Hand** (`no_hand`, select_one, optional): This refers to a hand that does not meet the criteria for any of the above hand rankings.

## Tips
* Be sure to select all applicable hand rankings for each question.
* If a hand does not meet the criteria for any of the hand rankings, select 'No Hand'.
