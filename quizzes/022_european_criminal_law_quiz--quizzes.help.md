# European Criminal Law Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a knowledge assessment tool designed to gauge your understanding of European criminal law. It is used for educational purposes only and does not constitute legal advice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review each question carefully.
2. Select the correct answer for each question from the provided options.
3. Submit your responses when you have completed all questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Quiz Start** (`id: 1`, `type: note`): This field is a placeholder to mark the start of the quiz. You can ignore this field, as it is not a question.
* **criminal_law_questions** (`id: 2`, `type: select_multiple`): This question asks whether you think the following statements are true or false about European criminal law. Please select all the statements that you believe to be true.
	+ 'Yes': This option is used to indicate that you agree with the statement.
	+ 'No': This option is used to indicate that you do not agree with the statement.
* **answers** (`id: 3`, `type: select_multiple`): This question asks you to confirm your understanding of the answers to the previous question. Please select all the answers that you believe are correct.
	+ 'Yes': This option is used to indicate that you understand the answer.
	+ 'No': This option is used to indicate that you do not understand the answer.
* **submit** (`id: 4`, `type: select_one`): This question asks you to confirm your submission of the quiz. Please select 'Yes' if you are ready to submit your responses.
	+ 'Yes': This option is used to confirm your submission.
	+ 'No': This option is used to indicate that you are not ready to submit.
* **confirmation** (`id: 5`, `type: select_one`): This question asks you to confirm your understanding of the submission. Please select 'Yes' if you are confident in your submission.
	+ 'Yes': This option is used to confirm your understanding.
	+ 'No': This option is used to indicate that you are not confident in your submission.
