# Identify Words Starting With M - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help students identify words that start with the letter M as part of a learning activity. It guides them to select multiple words that begin with M, provide their answers, confirm their answers, and finally, review the results with their teacher.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Browse through the list of words on page one, "word_list", and select all the words that start with the letter M.
2. On page two, "answer_sheet", type your answers to the question in the text field.
3. On page three, "student_answers", select your confirmation of the answers you provided.
4. On page four, "results_table", review the results and confirm or select the correct answers.
5. On page five, "teacher_comments", your teacher will leave feedback or comments on your performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **word_list** (Select multiple): Choose all the words from the list that start with the letter M.
* **answer_sheet** (Text): Type your answers to the question in this field.
* **student_answers** (Select multiple): Confirm your answers provided on page two.
* **results_table** (Select one): Review and confirm the results of your answers.
* **teacher_comments** (Text): Your teacher will leave feedback or comments on your performance.
