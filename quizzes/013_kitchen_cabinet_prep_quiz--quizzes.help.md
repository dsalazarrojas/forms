# Kitchen Cabinet Prep Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kitchen Cabinet Prep Quiz is a tool used to determine a client's readiness and confidence when it comes to kitchen cabinet prep. It helps users assess the client's needs and preferences, ensuring a smooth experience for both the user and the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read through each page carefully, and ensure you understand the questions and options provided.
2. For each field, select the relevant options or enter your answer as instructed.
3. Make sure to provide accurate and honest answers, as they will be used to determine the client's readiness and confidence.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **intro**
`intro` (`intro`, null, required: false)
No explanation necessary, as this is just a note.

### 2. **client_info**
`client_info` (`client_info`, text, required: false)
Please enter client information as you would in your own work order.

### 3. **safety_precautions**
`safety_precautions` (`safety_precautions`, text, required: false)
This field is for you to enter any safety precautions or concerns you have regarding the client's safety during the cabinet prep process.

### 4. **cabinet_prep**
`cabinet_prep` (`cabinet_prep`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select all that apply from the following options:

* Standard Prep
* Full Prep
* Partial Prep
* Other (please specify)

### 5. **safety_equipment**
`safety_equipment` (`safety_equipment`, select_one, required: false)
Select the safety equipment you have access to from the following options:

* Full-face respirator
* Safety glasses
* Steel-toed boots
* Other (please specify)

### 6. **client_readiness**
`client_readiness` (`client_readiness`, number, required: false)
No explanation necessary, as this is a numerical field.

### 7. **client_confidence**
`client_confidence` (`client_confidence`, text, required: false)
Please enter your assessment of the client's confidence level, e.g., "High", "Medium", "Low".

## Tips
* Make sure to answer honestly and accurately, as the results will be used to determine the client's readiness and confidence.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, refer back to the form instructions for guidance.
* Remember to select all relevant options for cabinet prep and safety equipment to ensure a comprehensive assessment.
