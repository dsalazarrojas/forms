# Relationship Quality Test Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Relationship Quality Test Form Template is designed to assess the quality of relationships, helping users evaluate partner satisfaction, communication, and conflict resolution. It is a standardized template with multiple sections, each focusing on a specific aspect of relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the "Relationship Quality Test Form" section, which will guide you through the different sections of the form.
2. Answer the questions with the corresponding options provided for each section.
3. Once you have completed all the sections, you can review your answers before proceeding to the final "Test End" section.
4. The last section serves as a final note, indicating that you have completed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Relationship Status** (`relationship_status`, select_one, required/optional):
	* Select the status of your current relationship using the following options: Very good, Good, Fair, or Bad.
* **Partner Satisfaction** (`partner_satisfaction`, number, required/optional):
	* Rate your partner's satisfaction on a scale, but note that the scale is not explicitly defined.
* **Communication Level** (`communication_level`, select_multiple, required/optional):
	* Choose the level of communication in your relationship using the following options: Very good, Good, Fair, or Bad.
* **Trust Level** (`trust_level`, select_one, required/optional):
	* Select the level of trust in your relationship using the following options: Very high, High, Medium, or Low.
* **Partner Conflict** (`partner_conflict`, select_multiple, required/optional):
	* Indicate how often conflicts arise in your relationship using the following options: Rarely, Sometimes, Often, or Almost always.
* **Conflict Resolution** (`conflict_resolution`, select_one, required/optional):
	* Rate your ability to resolve conflicts in your relationship using the following options: Very good, Good, Fair, or Bad.
* **Relationship Growth** (`relationship_growth`, select_one, required/optional):
	* Select the rate of growth in your relationship using the following options: Very good, Good, Fair, or Bad.
* **Relationship Quality** (`relationship_quality`, select_one, required/optional):
	* Evaluate the overall quality of your relationship using the following options: Very high, High, Medium, or Low.
* **Test End** (`test_end`, note, required/optional):
	* Leave any final comments or observations about your relationship.
