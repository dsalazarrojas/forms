# vestibular_and_postural_assessment_quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects information on vestibular and postural assessment to aid in the diagnosis and treatment of patients with balance and dizziness issues.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure you have the necessary information and permissions to complete this form.
2. Complete each section as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3. If you're unsure about any question, please ask for clarification or consult with a healthcare professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Screening Information** (`screening_info`, `text`, required):
	+ This section is meant to capture any relevant information about the patient's visit, such as their medical history or any previous tests or treatments related to dizziness or balance.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required):
	+ Please enter the patient's full name, as it appears on their medical records.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, `date`, required):
	+ Enter the date of the patient's visit or assessment.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, `time`, required):
	+ Enter the time of the patient's visit or assessment.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `text`, required):
	+ Describe any symptoms the patient is experiencing, such as dizziness, lightheadedness, or falls.
* **Do you experience dizziness?** (`dizzy_ever`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select one of the options: Dizziness, No Dizziness, Unsure, Rarely, Frequently, or Never.
* **How often do you experience dizziness?** (`dizzy_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Select all options that apply: Rarely, Frequently, Never, Sometimes.
* **How would you describe the severity of your dizziness?** (`dizzy_severity`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select one of the options: Mild, Moderate, Severe, None.
