# Insulin Feedback Mechanism Worksheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on insulin regulation mechanisms from users. It helps us understand how individuals regulate their insulin, which is essential for improving the feedback mechanism.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the form sections as prompted.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Insulin Regulation** (`insulin_regulation`, text, required: false)
Explain how you regulate your insulin.

### 2. **Insulin Mechanism** (`mechanism_of_insulin`, text, required: false)
Describe the mechanism you use to regulate your insulin.

### 3. **Effects of Insulin** (`effects_of_insulin`, text, required: false)
List any effects you experience due to insulin regulation.

### 4. **Insulin Sensitivity** (`insulin_sensitivity`, number, required: false)
Enter your sensitivity to insulin.

### 5. **Blood Glucose Level** (`blood_glucose_level`, number, required: false)
Enter your blood glucose level.

### 6. **Insulin Dosage** (`insulin_dosage`, number, required: false)
Enter your insulin dosage.

### 7. **Meal Frequency** (`meal_frequency`, number, required: false)
Enter your meal frequency.

### 8. **Insulin Regulation Adherence** (`insulin_regulation_adherence`, select_multiple, required: true)
Choose how well you adhere to your insulin regulation:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
	+ Never

### 9. **Why Regulate Insulin** (`insulin_regulation_adherence_reason`, text, required: false)
Explain why you regulate your insulin.

### 10. **How Often** (`insulin_regulation_adherence_frequency`, text, required: false)
Describe how often you regulate your insulin.

### 11. **What Effect** (`insulin_regulation_adherence_effect`, text, required: false)
Explain the effect of regulating your insulin.

### 12. **Time** (`insulin_regulation_adherence_time`, time, required: false)
Enter the time you regulate your insulin.

### 13. **Place** (`insulin_regulation_adherence_place`, text, required: false)
Describe the place you regulate your insulin.

### 14. **Duration** (`insulin_regulation_adherence_duration`, text, required: false)
Explain the duration of regulating your insulin.

### 15. **How Often 2** (`insulin_regulation_adherence_frequency_2`, text, required: false)
Describe how often you regulate your insulin (again, this is the same question as #10).

Please answer these questions to help us understand your insulin regulation feedback. 

Note: This help guide is a simplified version of the original YAML, and some fields may have been merged or rearranged for better clarity and usability.
