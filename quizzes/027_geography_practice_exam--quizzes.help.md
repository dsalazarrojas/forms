# Geography Practice Exam - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Geography Practice Exam is a tool designed to help you test your knowledge of geography-related concepts and facts. This exam is intended to be a fun and interactive way to learn and assess your understanding of geography.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and choose the correct answer from the options provided.
2. Select all the correct answers for the multiple-choice questions.
3. Fill in your student name and email (if you want to save your progress and results).
4. Click "Submit" to submit your answers and review your results.
