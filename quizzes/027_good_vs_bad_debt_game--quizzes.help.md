# Good Vs Bad Debt Game - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Good Vs Bad Debt Game" form is designed to help users analyze debt by categorizing it as "good" or "bad" debt. This game-like approach makes it engaging and fun while providing valuable insights into debt types and management.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Carefully read each field and its description.
* Fill out the fields in order, starting from introduction and moving to the next field.
* Provide accurate and thoughtful responses for each field.
* Review your answers before submitting to ensure you have provided a thorough analysis.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a text field where you can introduce the topic of your debt analysis. It's not required, but it helps to provide context for the rest of the form.
* **Scenario1**: This field is for presenting a specific scenario related to debt. Describe the scenario in detail, and try to be as specific as possible.
* **Reflection1**: This field is for reflecting on the scenario you presented. What does it mean to you? How does it relate to your debt analysis?
* **Reflection2**: Another reflection field, this time for the second scenario.
* **Scenario2**: Yet another scenario to analyze. Be as specific as possible in your description.
* **Scenario2 Reflection**: Reflect on the second scenario, just like in Reflection1.
* **Scenario3**: Analyze this third scenario, and be specific about your thoughts.
* **Scenario3 Reflection**: Reflect on the third scenario, just like the previous reflections.
* **Quiz**: This field is a multiple-choice question. Choose the option that best describes your debt analysis.
* **Scenario4**: Analyze this fourth scenario, and be specific about your thoughts.
* **Scenario4 Reflection**: Reflect on the fourth scenario, just like the previous reflections.
* **Scenario5**: Analyze this fifth scenario, and be specific about your thoughts.
* **Scenario5 Reflection**: Reflect on the fifth scenario, just like the previous reflections.
* **Quiz2**: Another multiple-choice question. Choose the option that best describes your debt analysis.
* **Scenario6**: Analyze this sixth scenario, and be specific about your thoughts.
* **Scenario6 Reflection**: Reflect on the sixth scenario, just like the previous reflections.
* **Quiz3**: A third multiple-choice question. Choose the option that best describes your debt analysis.
* **Conclusion**: Summarize your debt analysis in this field. Review the form before submitting.
* **Feedback**: Provide any feedback you have about the form, or any suggestions for improvements.

Note: Some fields are required, but you can skip others if you're unsure or want to focus on the required fields.
