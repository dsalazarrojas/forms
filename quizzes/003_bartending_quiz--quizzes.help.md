# Bartending Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bartending Quiz is designed to test your knowledge of mixology and bartending concepts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and select the correct answer or answer accordingly.
2. For questions that require multiple choices, select all relevant options.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed to submit the quiz.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Examinee Name** (`examinee_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **-- Mixology and Bar Knowledge Quiz --** (`quiz_header`, note, required): This is a brief introduction to the quiz.
* **What is the primary base spirit in a classic Margarita?** (`q_base_spirit_margarita`, select_one, required): Choose one of the given options.
	+ Vodka
	+ Gin
	+ Tequila
	+ Rum
* **What is the standard volume of a single shot (jigger) in ounces?** (`q_standard_pour`, number, required): Enter a numerical value.
* **Which of these cocktails should typically be stirred rather than shaken?** (`q_stirred_vs_shaken`, select_one, required): Choose one of the given options.
	+ Whiskey Sour
	+ Manhattan
	+ Daiquiri
	+ Cosmopolitan
* **Select all common garnishes used for a Martini.** (`q_garnishes`, select_multiple, required): Choose all relevant options.
	+ Olive
	+ Lemon Twist
	+ Cherry
	+ Lime Wedge
	+ Onion (Gibson)
* **Which of the following has the highest typical alcohol by volume (ABV)?** (`q_alcohol_content`, select_one, required): Choose one of the given options.
	+ Beer
	+ Wine
	+ Fortified Wine
	+ Distilled Spirits
* **What is the legal drinking age in the United States?** (`q_legal_age`, number, required): Enter a numerical value.
* **What tool is used to crush fruit or herbs in the bottom of a glass?** (`q_muddling_tool`, text, required): Enter a single word or brief description.
* **On a scale of 1-10, how important is bar cleanliness for safety?** (`q_cleanliness_importance`, number, required): Enter a numerical value between 1 and 10.
* **Briefly define what bitters are in mixology.** (`q_bitters_definition`, text, optional): Enter a brief description (max 100 words).
* **Quiz Completion Date** (`completion_date`, date, required): Enter the date of completion in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Bartending Quiz** (`title`, text, title): This is the title of the quiz.
