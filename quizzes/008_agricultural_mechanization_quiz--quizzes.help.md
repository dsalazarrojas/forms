# Agricultural Mechanization Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This quiz is designed to evaluate your knowledge and experience in agricultural mechanization, specifically in terms of best practices for tractor maintenance, proper tire pressure, safe procedures before repair, and fuel storage.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your participant name (optional).
2. Choose your organization or school (if applicable).
3. Select your level of experience with mechanization (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
4. Answer the four questions about best practices for tractor maintenance, proper tire pressure, safe procedures before repair, and fuel storage.
5. (Optional) Estimate your score out of 10.
6. Indicate your interest in further training on mechanization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, text, Optional): Enter your name for identification purposes. This is not required but recommended for anonymous quizzes.
* **Organization or school** (`organization`, text, Optional): Enter your institution or employer. This is not required but recommended for tracking purposes.
* **Mechanization experience level** (`experience_level`, select_one, True): Choose your level of experience with agricultural mechanization.
	+ Beginner: You have basic knowledge and little experience with mechanization.
	+ Intermediate: You have some knowledge and experience with mechanization.
	+ Advanced: You have extensive knowledge and experience with mechanization.
* **Best practice for tractor maintenance** (`question_1`, select_one, True): Choose the most appropriate answer for best tractor maintenance practice.
	+ Regular lubrication
	+ Ignore minor noises
	+ Delay maintenance
	+ Only replace parts annually
* **Proper tire pressure importance** (`question_2`, select_one, True): Select the best explanation for proper tire pressure.
	+ Improves fuel efficiency
	+ Has no effect
	+ Only affects speed
	+ Affects pesticide application
* **Safe procedure before repair** (`question_3`, select_one, True): Choose the correct safety step before repair.
	+ Disconnect power and chock wheels
	+ Start engine
	+ Remove guards while running
	+ None of the above
* **Fuel storage best practice** (`question_4`, select_one, Optional): Choose the correct fuel storage practice.
	+ Store in labeled containers
	+ Keep any container
	+ Store near ignition sources
	+ Mix fuels
* **Estimated score** (`score_estimate`, number, Optional): Estimate your score out of 10, based on your answers.
* **Interest in further training** (`training_interest`, select_one, Optional): Indicate your interest in further training on mechanization.
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Maybe
