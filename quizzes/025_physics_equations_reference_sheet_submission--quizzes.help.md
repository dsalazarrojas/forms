# Physics Equations Reference Sheet Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect physics equations from users for our reference sheets. Your submissions will help us improve the accuracy and completeness of our resource.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully fill out all required fields.
- Make sure to provide accurate information about the equation type and user type.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for guidance.
- Click 'Submit' to complete the form and send it to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equation Type**: Choose 'Yes' if the equation is relevant to physics problems, 'No' otherwise.
* **Equation Description**: Briefly describe the equation and its relevance to physics problems.
* **Equation Itself**: Write the actual equation you want to include in our reference sheet.
* **Source**: Tell us where you found the equation (book, website, class, etc.).
* **User Type**: Choose 'Yes' if you're a student, 'No' if you're a teacher or have a different affiliation.
* **User Name**: Your name will be associated with the submission, so please use your actual name.
* **User Affiliation**: If you're a teacher, specify your school or institution.
* **User Email**: Enter a valid email address (e.g., `[yourname]@email.com`).
* **Equation Type**: 
  * **Yes**: The equation is relevant to physics problems.
  * **No**: The equation is not relevant to physics problems.
* **Equation Source**: Where you found the equation (optional, text field).
* **User Type**: 
  + **Yes**: You're a student.
  + **No**: You're a teacher or have a different affiliation.
* **User Email**: Enter a valid email address (e.g., `[yourname]@email.com`).
