# Healthy Relationship Skills Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthy Relationship Skills Quiz is designed to help you assess your relationship skills, identify areas of improvement, and gain a deeper understanding of your relationship dynamics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select the option that best describes your relationship goals and behaviors.
3. Be as detailed as possible in your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Relationship Skills** (`relationship_skills`, `text`, required): This section assesses your general relationship skills, such as conflict resolution, effective communication, and healthy relationship patterns. Please provide a brief description of your relationship skills, including any strengths and weaknesses.
* **Relationship Goals** (`relationship_goals`, `text`, required): Here, you'll describe your relationship goals and expectations. Please be specific about what you hope to achieve in your relationship.
* **Conflict Resolution** (`conflict_resolution`, `text`, required): In this section, you'll describe how you handle conflicts in your relationship. Please provide examples or details about your conflict resolution strategies and techniques.
* **Conflict Resolution Strategies** (`conflict_resolution_strategies`, `text`, required): Describe the specific strategies you use to resolve conflicts in your relationship. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Effective Communication** (`effective_communication`, `text`, required): This section assesses your communication style in your relationship. Describe how you communicate with your partner, including any verbal or non-verbal cues.
* **Relationship Expectations** (`relationship_expectations`, `text`, required): Here, you'll describe your relationship expectations, including any boundaries, needs, and desires.
* **Healthy Relationship Patterns** (`healthy_relationship_patterns`, `text`, required): In this section, you'll describe the patterns you've observed in your relationship, including any strengths and weaknesses.
* **Conflict Resolution Patterns** (`conflict_resolution_patterns`, `text`, required): Describe the patterns you've observed in your relationship regarding conflict resolution. Be as specific as possible.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* If you're unsure about any question, you can contact the form administrator (form@gic.mx) for guidance.
* The quiz results will provide you with a better understanding of your relationship dynamics and suggest areas for improvement.
