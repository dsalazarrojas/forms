# Five Pillars Of Islam Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Five Pillars of Islam Quiz is designed to educate users about the fundamental principles of the Islamic faith. The quiz assesses users' knowledge and understanding of the pillars and collects feedback from users.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions about the Five Pillars of Islam with a "Yes" or "No" response.
2. Fill in the demographic data and contact information.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the comments section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pillar of Faith** (`pillar_of_faith`, select_one, required): Answer with "Yes" if you have faith in Islam, and "No" otherwise.
* **Pillar of Prayer** (`pillar_of_prayer`, select_one, required): Answer with "Yes" if you pray five times a day, and "No" otherwise.
* **Pillar of Charity** (`pillar_of_charity`, select_multiple, required): Answer with "Yes" if you engage in charity work, and "No" otherwise. You can select multiple options.
* **Pillar of Purification** (`pillar_of_purification`, select_one, required): Answer with "Yes" if you fast during Ramadan or engage in other acts of purification, and "No" otherwise.
* **Pillar of Community** (`pillar_of_community`, select_multiple, required): Answer with "Yes" if you engage in community service, and "No" otherwise. You can select multiple options.
* **Pillar of Pilgrimage** (`pillar_of_pilgrimage`, select_one, required): Answer with "Yes" if you have performed the Hajj or Umrah, and "No" otherwise.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, required): Fill in your name to submit your response.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name to submit your response.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to submit your response.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number to submit your response.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth if you want to provide additional information.
* **Date of Death** (`date_of_death`, date, optional): Enter your date of death if you want to provide additional information.
* **Time of Completion** (`time_of_completion`, time, optional): Enter the time you completed the form, including the timezone.
* **Timezone** (`timezone`, select_one, optional): Select the time zone you are in.
* **Country** (`country`, select_one, required): Answer with "Yes" if you are from a particular country, and "No" otherwise.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Answer with "Yes" if you are a particular gender, and "No" otherwise.
* **Language** (`language`, select_one, required): Answer with "Yes" if you speak a particular language, and "No" otherwise.
* **Country of Residence** (`country_of_residence`, text, required): Enter the country where you reside.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state where you reside.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, optional): Enter the zip code where you reside.
* **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1`, text, optional): Enter the address line 1 where you reside.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
