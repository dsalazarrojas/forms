# Korean Language Beginner Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Korean Language Beginner Quiz is a form designed to collect information from users who take the quiz. It helps us understand the performance and progress of our learners in the Korean language.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your User Name, which is the name of the person taking the quiz.
2. Enter your Email address for contact purposes.
3. Provide your Teacher Name (name of the teacher or instructor who administered the quiz).
4. Enter your Teacher ID (unique identifier for the teacher).
5. Select your Level (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced) to indicate your proficiency in the Korean language.
6. Enter your Score, which is the number of points you achieved in the quiz.
7. Choose the Quiz Type (Multiple Choice, True/False, or Short Answer) that matches the type of quiz you took.
8. Select the Question Type (Grammar, Vocabulary, or Reading) that corresponds to the type of questions in the quiz.
9. Choose the Score Range (select one or more from 1-5, 6-10, 11-15, 16-20, 21-25) that indicates the range of scores you received in the quiz.
10. If required, enter the Submission Date and Submission Time when you took the quiz.
11. Add any additional notes or comments in the Submission Note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, Text, optional): Enter your name as you would like to be known in the quiz results.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, optional): Your email address for contact purposes.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, Text, optional): Name of the teacher or instructor who administered the quiz.
* **Teacher ID** (`teacher_id`, Number, optional): Unique identifier for the teacher.
* **Level** (`level`, Select One, optional): Choose your proficiency level in Korean language (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Score** (`score`, Number, optional): The number of points you achieved in the quiz.
* **Quiz Type** (`quiz_type`, Select One, optional): Type of quiz you took (Multiple Choice, True/False, Short Answer).
* **Question Type** (`question_type`, Select One, optional): Type of questions in the quiz (Grammar, Vocabulary, Reading).
* **Score Range** (`score_range`, Select Multiple, required): Choose the range of scores you received in the quiz.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, Date, optional): Date when you took the quiz.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, Time, optional): Time when you took the quiz.
* **Submission Note** (`submission_note`, Note, optional): Add any notes or comments about the quiz.

Note: The submission date and time fields are only required if you choose to submit your quiz.
