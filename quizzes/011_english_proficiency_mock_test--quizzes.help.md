# english_proficiency_mock_test - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the English proficiency of users by evaluating their performance on various sections related to language usage and comprehension. The form is meant to be completed after an English language test or assessment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each section according to your performance in the corresponding section of the test.
2. Choose the most accurate option(s) that describe your experience.
3. For the sections with date and time fields, enter the actual date and time it took you to complete the test.
4. Enter a score or feedback as per the question's requirement.
