# Polynomial Factoring Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Polynomial Factoring Quiz is designed to test your knowledge on factoring polynomials. This quiz will guide you through a series of questions to assess your ability to factor polynomials and identify their properties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your answer to the polynomial factoring question in the text field at the top of the page.
2. Select the correct answer for each of the multiple-select questions below.
3. Enter your answer to each of the subsequent text fields.
4. Verify that your answers match the original polynomial and properties.
