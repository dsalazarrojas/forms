# Addiction Recovery Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help individuals assess their habits and behaviors related to substance use. It's a tool for self-reflection and is not a clinical diagnosis. By completing this form, you'll be providing information to help identify potential areas where support may be needed.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Choose the response that best matches your experiences and feelings.
3. Ensure you complete all required fields (indicated by *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Understanding Your Relationship with Substances --**: This section is an introduction to the form and explains its purpose.
* **How often do you find yourself using substances more than you intended?**: This question helps us understand your patterns of substance use.
	+ Never: You rarely or never use substances more than you intended.
	+ Rarely: You use substances more than you intended occasionally.
	+ Sometimes: You use substances more than you intended sometimes.
	+ Frequently: You use substances more than you intended often.
	+ Almost always: You almost always use substances more than you intended.
* **Have you ever tried to cut down or stop but found it difficult?**: This question assesses your ability to control your substance use.
	+ No, never: You have never tried to cut down or stop substances.
	+ I have thought about it but haven't tried: You have considered cutting down or stopping but haven't taken action.
	+ Yes, I have tried once or twice: You have tried to cut down or stop substances once or twice.
	+ Yes, I have tried many times: You have repeatedly tried to cut down or stop substances.
* **Has your substance use ever interfered with your work, school, or home life?**: This question evaluates the impact of substance use on your daily life.
	+ No impact: Your substance use has not affected your work, school, or home life.
	+ Occasional minor impact: Your substance use has occasionally affected your work, school, or home life.
	+ Significant recurring impact: Your substance use has regularly affected your work, school, or home life.
	+ Severe impact on all areas: Your substance use has significantly affected all areas of your life.
* **Do you find yourself avoiding social activities or hobbies to use substances?**: This question helps us understand how substance use affects your social and leisure activities.
	+ Never: You rarely or never use substances to avoid social activities or hobbies.
	+ Occasionally: You use substances occasionally to avoid social activities or hobbies.
	+ Often: You use substances often to avoid social activities or hobbies.
	+ Most of the time: You use substances most of the time to avoid social activities or hobbies.
* **Have you noticed that you need more of the substance to get the same effect?**: This question assesses your tolerance to substances.
	+ No change noticed: You haven't noticed any change in your tolerance.
	+ Slight increase: You've noticed a slight increase in your tolerance.
	+ Significant increase (Tolerance): You've noticed a significant increase in your tolerance.
* **Do you experience physical or emotional discomfort when you stop using?**: This question helps us understand withdrawal symptoms.
	+ Never: You rarely or never experience withdrawal symptoms.
	+ Rarely: You experience withdrawal symptoms rarely.
	+ Sometimes: You experience withdrawal symptoms sometimes.
	+ Frequently / Always: You experience withdrawal symptoms often or always.
* **Have you continued to use despite knowing it's causing physical or mental health problems?**: This question assesses your awareness of the risks associated with substance use.
	+ False: You have not continued to use despite knowing the risks.
	+ True: You have continued to use despite knowing the risks.
* **How ready do you feel to talk to a professional about your recovery?**: This question helps us understand your willingness to seek help.
	+ Not ready at all: You are not ready to seek help.
	+ Thinking about it: You are considering seeking help.
	+ Ready to take the first step: You are ready to start the process.
	+ Already seeking help: You are already engaging in recovery efforts.
* **-- Request Follow-up --**: This section is for users who want to request follow-up contact from a professional.
* **Preferred Name**: This field is for users to provide their preferred name to be contacted by a professional.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: This field allows users to choose how they'd like to be contacted (email, phone call, text message, or no contact).
* **Email Address**: This field is for users to provide their email address to be contacted by a professional.
* **I understand that my responses are confidential and used to provide support**: This question ensures users understand the confidentiality of their responses.
