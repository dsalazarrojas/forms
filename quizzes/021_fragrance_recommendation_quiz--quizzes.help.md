# Fragrance Recommendation Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your personal fragrance preferences. We use the information you provide to offer you tailored scent suggestions that fit your tastes and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select your preferred options for each question and provide any additional information you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What type of scent do you typically enjoy?**
  * Choose from Floral, Oriental, Woody, Fruity, or Citrus.
* **What size of fragrance family do you usually choose?** (Select all applicable options)
  * Choose from Top, Middle, Base.
* **How concentrated would you like your fragrances to be?** (Select all applicable options)
  * Choose from High, Medium, or Low.
* **How long do you usually wear your fragrance?**
  * Choose from Less than 2 hours, 2-4 hours, 4-6 hours, 6-8 hours, or More than 8 hours.
* **When do you usually wear your fragrance after?** (Select all applicable options)
  * Choose from After showering, After exercising, After eating, After smoking, or After drinking.
* **What will you be doing with the fragrance?** (Select all applicable options)
  * Choose from Wearing, Giving as a gift, Using for a special occasion, Using for everyday.
* **What is the purpose of the fragrance for you?**
  * Choose from Personal use, Business use.
* **How would you like to receive fragrance recommendations?**
  * Choose from Email, Phone, or Text message.
* **Do you have any other preferences or information about your fragrance preferences?**
  * This is an optional field for any additional comments or notes.
* **If you're planning to use the fragrance for a special occasion, what is it?**
  * This is an optional field for any additional information about the occasion.
* **If you're giving the fragrance as a gift, who is the recipient?**
  * This is an optional field for any additional information about the recipient.
* **Is there anything else you'd like to share about your preferences?**
  * This is an optional field for any additional comments or notes.

## Tips
* Take your time to carefully consider your preferences before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any of the options, please select the one that best fits your usual behavior.
* Please provide any additional information that may help us better understand your preferences.
