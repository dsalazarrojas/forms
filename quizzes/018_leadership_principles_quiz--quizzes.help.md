# Leadership Principles Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leadership Principles Quiz is a form designed to evaluate an individual's understanding and application of leadership principles. It is likely used for personal or professional development, HR, or organizational development purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply fill in the required information for each field. Most fields are optional, but some fields like "form_id" and "category" should be completed as they are used for form tracking and organization. The form is divided into several sections, and the user can navigate through them by filling out the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Leader (id: 3, label: leader)**: This field is used to identify the leader or individual being assessed.
* **Development Programs (id: 4, label: development_programs)**: Select all applicable development programs that the leader or individual has participated in.
* **Results Tracking (id: 5, label: results_tracking)**: Choose how results are tracked for the leadership principles.
* **Easy Participation (id: 6, label: easy_participation)**: This field is not used in the form and can be ignored.
* **Collect Responses Online (id: 7, label: collect_responses_online)**: Select how responses are collected, either online or offline.
* **Review Results (id: 8, label: review_results)**: Choose how results are reviewed, either to track or to review.
* **Leadership Understanding (id: 9, label: leadership_understanding)**: This field is used to describe the individual's understanding of leadership principles.
* **Quiz Questions (id: 10, label: quiz_questions)**: This field is not used in the form and can be ignored.
* **Training Development Programs (id: 11, label: training_development_programs)**: Select all applicable training programs that the leader or individual has participated in.
* **Leadership Principles Quiz Comments (id: 12, label: leadership_principles_quiz_comments)**: This field is used to provide additional information about the quiz.
* **Assigned Tool (id: 13, label: assigned_tool)**: This field is not used in the form and can be ignored.
* **Form ID (id: 14, label: form_id)**: Fill in the form ID for tracking purposes.
* **Category (id: 15, label: category)**: Fill in the category for organization purposes.
* **Description (id: 16, label: description)**: This field is not used in the form and can be ignored.
* **Quiz Questions (id: 1, label: leadership_principles_quiz)**: Provide a short description or answer to the leadership principles quiz.
* **Leadership Principles Quiz Questions (id: 2, label: leadership_principles_quiz_questions)**: This field is used to provide additional information about the quiz questions.
* **Form (id: 16, label: form_id)**: Fill in the form ID for tracking purposes.
