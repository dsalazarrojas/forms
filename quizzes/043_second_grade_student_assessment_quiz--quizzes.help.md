# Second Grade Student Assessment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Second Grade Student Assessment Quiz is designed to collect and record data about second-grade students' assessments, specifically focusing on math, reading, science, and other related fields.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, click on "Submit" and fill out the form accordingly.
2. Enter the student's first name in the text field labeled "first_name".
3. Enter the student's last name in the text field labeled "last_name".
4. Enter the student's ID in the text field labeled "student_id".
5. Select the grade level of the student from the options provided for "grade_level".
6. Enter the student's math score in the number field labeled "math_score".
7. Enter the student's reading score in the number field labeled "reading_score".
8. Enter the student's science score in the number field labeled "science_score".
9. Enter the teacher's name in the text field labeled "teacher_name".
10. Enter the classroom name in the text field labeled "classroom".
11. Enter the school name in the text field labeled "school".
12. Enter the student's email in the email field labeled "email".
13. Enter the student's phone number in the text field labeled "phone".
14. Write any additional comments about the student in the note field labeled "comments".
15. Enter the date of the assessment in the date field labeled "date".
16. Enter the time of the assessment in the time field labeled "time".
17. Select the subject of the assessment from the options provided for "subject".
17. Enter the school's email in the email field labeled "school_email".
