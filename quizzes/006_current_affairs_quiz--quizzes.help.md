# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for taking users' current affairs quiz. It will help us get some basic information about the users regarding their current affairs quiz.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **<string>** field with your name or any other relevant title.
2. Go through each page of the form and fill out the required and optional fields accordingly:
   - Page 1: Fill out the first field with your answer to the current affairs quiz question.
   - Page 2: You will be presented with a question, and you should choose the answer that best fits your current affairs from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **<label>** (name, text, required/optional)
This is the title field. You can enter any name or title that you think is relevant to your current affairs quiz.

### **current-affairs-quiz-question** (string, select_one, required/optional)
This field asks you about the current affairs quiz question. Choose the answer that best suits your current affairs.

### **current-affairs-quiz-date** (string, date, required/optional)
This field is for you to provide the date related to your current affairs quiz. 

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
