# Dress Recommendation Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This is a quiz designed to help you discover the perfect dress for you. Please answer the questions to provide more information about your preferences, and we'll suggest a dress that fits your style.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all the options that apply to each question. You can select multiple options for each question.
2. Answer the questions in a way that best describes your dress preferences.
3. Click submit to get your dress recommendations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of dress are you looking for?** (`dress_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Choose all the types of dresses that appeal to you, such as formal, casual, evening, or wedding.
* **Occasion** (`occasion`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the occasion for which you're looking for a dress, like a party, wedding, or everyday wear.
* **Budget** (`budget`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Choose how much you're willing to spend on a dress, from under $20 to over $100.
* **Dress length** (`dress_length`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Pick the length of the dress you prefer, such as knee-length, floor-length, or body-hugging.
* **Sleeve length** (`sleeve_length`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the sleeve length you like, like short, long, or none.
* **Neck style** (`neck_style`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Choose the type of neck style you prefer, such as V-neck, round, or scoop.
* **Collar style** (`collar_style`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the style of the collar you like, like button-down, crew-neck, or scoop.
* **Collar height** (`collar_height`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Pick the height of the collar you prefer, such as high, low, or none.
* **Dress color** (`dress_color`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Choose the colors of the dress you like, such as red, blue, or black.
* **Dress pattern** (`dress_pattern`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the pattern of the dress you prefer, like stripes, dots, or polka dots.
* **Dress texture** (`dress_texture`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Choose the texture of the dress you like, such as smooth, velvety, or rough.
* **Closing style** (`dress_closing`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Select the type of closing style you prefer, like zip, button, or tie.
* **Type of clothing** (`dress_type_of_clothing`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Pick the category of clothing you're looking for, like tops, bottoms, or outerwear.
* **Clothing group** (`dress_clothing_group`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Choose the group of clothing you prefer, such as casual, formal, or streetwear.
* **Dress size** (`dress_size`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the size of the dress you prefer, like XS, S, M, or XL.
* **Sleeve type** (`sleeve_type`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Pick the type of sleeve you like, like long, short, or cap.
* **Clothing type** (`dress_clothing_type`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Choose the type of clothing you're looking for, like tops, bottoms, or outerwear.

* **Dress closing style** (`dress_closing_style`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the type of closing style you prefer, like zip, button, or snap.
* **Other clothing type description** (`dress_clothing_type_other_description`, `text`, required: false)
