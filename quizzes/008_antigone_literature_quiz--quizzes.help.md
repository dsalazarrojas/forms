# Antigone Literature Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to test your knowledge of the play Antigone by Sophocles. It consists of 12 multiple-choice questions that cover various aspects of the play, including its characters, plot, themes, and literary analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Student name" field.
2. Provide your student ID in the "Student ID" field (if applicable).
3. Specify the class section or period you are taking the quiz for.
4. Select the correct date you completed the quiz.
5. Answer the multiple-choice questions about Antigone, one at a time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student name**: Enter your full name as it is on your official records.
* **Student ID**: Enter your student ID, if applicable, to help us track your progress.
* **Class section/Period**: Specify the class section or period you are taking the quiz for.
* **Quiz date**: Select the date you completed the quiz.
* **Who is the author of Antigone?**: Choose one of the options, Sophocles, Euripides, Aeschylus, Aristophanes, or Seneca.
* **Where does Antigone take place?**: Select one of the options, Thebes, Athens, Corinth, Sparta, or Troy.
* **What is the central conflict in Antigone?**: Choose one of the options, Antigone vs Ismene, Antigone vs Creon over burial rights, Antigone vs her family curse, Ismene vs Creon, Haemon vs his father, or none of the above.
* **What decree does Creon issue at the beginning?**: Select one of the options, No one can mourn the dead, Citizens must worship Creon, All must pay heavy taxes, No burial for Polyneices, or Everyone must join the military.
* **Why does Antigone defy Creon's edict?**: Choose one of the options, Family loyalty and religious duty, Desire for fame, Love for Haemon, To help Ismene escape, or To start a rebellion.
* **What is Ismene's role in the play?**: Select one of the options, She refuses to help and represents caution, She helps Antigone bury Polyneices, She is Creon's daughter, She leads a rebellion, or She is a messenger from the gods.
* **What is Haemon's relationship to Antigone?**: Choose one of the options, He is her brother, He is her fiancé, He is her cousin, He is her uncle, or He is a family friend.
* **How is Creon characterized?**: Select one of the options, As a wise and just ruler, As a stubborn and prideful tyrant, As a weak and indecisive leader, As a cowardly man, or As a compassionate father.
* **What role does the blind prophet Teiresias play?**: Choose one of the options, He encourages Creon to defy the gods, He warns Creon that the gods are angry, He supports Antigone publicly, He serves as a messenger for Ismene, or He has no significant role.
* **What is Creon's tragic flaw?**: Select one of the options, His pride and refusal to listen, His love for Antigone, His weakness as a leader, His lack of intelligence, or His compassion for others.
* **What is the primary function of the Chorus?**: Choose one of the options, To provide comic relief, To comment on action and represent citizens, To predict the future, To fight alongside characters, or To resolve conflicts.
* **How does the play end?**: Select one of the options, Antigone and Creon reconcile, Antigone escapes and lives happily, Antigone dies, Creon loses everything, Ismene takes over the throne, or The gods strike down Creon.
* **Which theme is NOT central to Antigone?**: Choose one of the options, Loyalty to family vs state, Pride and hubris, Ancient prophecies, Commercial trading practices, or Divine law vs human law.
* **How would you rate your performance on this quiz?**: Select one of the options, Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Very poor.
* **How much time did you spend studying for this quiz?**: Select one of the options, More than 3 hours, 2-3 hours, 1-2 hours, Less than 1 hour, or Did not study.
