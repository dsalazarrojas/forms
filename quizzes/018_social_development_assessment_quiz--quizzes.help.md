# Social Development Assessment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Development Assessment Quiz is a tool designed to help social services or support staff assess an individual's social development. This form is used to gather information that can aid in providing support and guidance to those who need it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name (if you want to) or leave it blank.
2. Choose one category from the available options to categorize your social development.
3. Select relevant options for each section from the provided options.
4. Add any notes or comments about your social development.
5. Enter your email address or leave it blank.
6. Enter your phone number or leave it blank.
7. Choose one or more follow-up options to help us get in touch with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Quiz Title** (quiz-title, text, optional)
This field is for you to enter a title for your social development assessment, if you'd like.

### 2. **Category** (category, select_one, optional)
Choose one category from the options provided to best describe your social development.

### 3. **Section 1** (section-1, select_multiple, optional)
Select all relevant options for section 1 from the list. This section appears to be related to your personal and professional life.

### 4. **Section 2** (section-2, select_one, optional)
Choose one option for section 2 from the list. This section seems to be related to your relationships or social connections.

### 5. **Section 3** (section-3, select_multiple, optional)
Select all relevant options for section 3 from the list. This section could be related to your goals or aspirations.

### 6. **Section 4** (section-4, select_one, optional)
Choose one option for section 4 from the list. This field seems to be for indicating "other" options, possibly for section 4 that isn't explicitly listed.

### 7. **Notes** (notes, note, optional)
Write any additional comments or notes about your social development that you'd like to share.

### 8. **Email** (email, email, optional)
Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted.

### 9. **Phone** (phone, text, optional)
Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted.

### 10. **Follow-up** (follow-up, select_multiple, optional)
Choose one or more follow-up options to let us know how you'd like to be contacted.
