# music_school_enrollment_extension_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to extend enrollment information for students at the music school. It is intended to collect additional details for students who are already enrolled or have previously been enrolled in the school. This form helps to update existing student records and prepare for future classes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the School Name field with the name of the school your child is currently enrolled in or interested in enrolling in.
2. Enter your child's Student Name in the Student Name field.
3. Provide your child's Student Email Address in the Student Email field.
4. Enter your child's contact number in the Student Phone field.
5. Fill in your child's Date of Birth in the Birth Date field.
6. Select the instrument your child would like to learn or is learning in the Instrument field.
7. Enter your child's Parent's Name in the Parent Name field.
8. Provide your child's Parent's Email Address in the Parent Email field.
9. Enter your child's Parent's contact number in the Parent Phone field.
10. If your child is interested in enrolling in a specific program, please select it from the Program field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name** (`school_name`, required: false): Enter the name of the school your child is enrolled in or interested in enrolling in.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, required: false): Enter your child's name as it appears on their government-issued ID or passport.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, required: false): Enter your child's email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, required: false): Enter your child's contact number.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, required: false): Fill in your child's Date of Birth in the standard date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Instrument** (`instrument`, required: false): Select the instrument your child would like to learn or is learning.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, required: false): Enter your child's Parent's Name.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, required: false): Enter your child's Parent's Email Address.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, required: false): Enter your child's Parent's contact number.
* **Program** (`program`, required: false): Select a program your child is interested in enrolling in.
* **Class Time** (`class_time`, required: false): Enter your preferred class time (e.g., 9:00 AM, 12:00 PM, 3:00 PM).
* **Class Date** (`class_date`, required: false): Enter your preferred class date (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday).
