# New Zealand School Enrolment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Zealand School Enrolment Form is designed to collect essential information from students and parents for the purpose of school enrollment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your student's name in the "student_name" field.
2. Provide your name and contact information in the "parent_name" field.
3. Enter your student's date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
4. Enter your contact address in the "contact_address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **student_name**: The name of the student as it appears on their official document (e.g., birth certificate).
* **parent_name**: The name of the parent or guardian who is enrolling the student.
* **date_of_birth**: The date of birth of the student, in the format of mm/dd/yyyy (e.g., 01/01/2000).
* **contact_address**: The address where you can be reached for further communication regarding the student's enrollment.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you need to make any changes to the form or have questions, please contact form@gic.mx.
