# Enrollment Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Enrollment Verification Form is used to gather and verify student enrollment information for school administration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the entire form carefully before starting to fill it out.
- Complete each field accurately and thoroughly.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to this guide for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name** (id: 1, text, required: false): Your first name as a student or individual associated with the school.
- **Last Name** (id: 2, text, required: false): Your last name as a student or individual associated with the school.
- **Enrollment Date** (id: 3, date, required: false): The date you were enrolled in the school.
- **School Name** (id: 4, text, required: false): The name of the school you're affiliated with.
- **School ID** (id: 5, number, required: false): A unique ID assigned to the school.
- **Student ID** (id: 6, number, required: false): A unique ID assigned to the student.
- **Email** (id: 7, email, required: false): Your email address associated with the school.
- **Phone** (id: 8, text, required: false): Your phone number associated with the school.
- **Verification Date** (id: 9, date, required: false): The date of the verification process.
- **Verification Status** (id: 10, select_one, required: false): Choose from Verified, Not Verified, or Pending to indicate the status of your verification.
- **School Address** (id: 11, note, required: false): Additional notes about the school address. This field is primarily for internal use by school administrators.
- **Student Address** (id: 12, note, required: false): Additional notes about the student's address. This field is primarily for internal use by school administrators.
- **School Phone** (id: 13, text, required: false): The school's phone number.
- **Enrollment Type** (id: 14, select_one, required: false): Choose from Full-time, Part-time, or Other to indicate the type of enrollment.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure all required fields are filled correctly.
- If unsure about any field, ask a school administrator for assistance.
