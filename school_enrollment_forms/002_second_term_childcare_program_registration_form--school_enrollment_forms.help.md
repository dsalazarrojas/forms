# Second Term Childcare Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register students for the second term of the Childcare Program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the required fields (marked as "Required") accurately and completely.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all information is correct.
3. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please contact the relevant authorities or the school for guidance.
