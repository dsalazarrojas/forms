# School Enrollment Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Enrollment Verification Form is a document used by school administrators to verify and update student enrollment information. This form helps to ensure accuracy and consistency of student records.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter student information on page 2.
2. Select the correct enrollment status of the student on page 3.
3. Enter the reason for withdrawal (if applicable) on page 4.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm verification.
5. Enter school information and student grades on page 6.
6. Add any comments or notes on page 8.
7. Enter school grades on page 7.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Info (Page 2)** (`student_info`, text, required): Enter the student's name, student ID, or other identifying information.
* **Enrollment Status (Page 3)** (`enrollment_status`, select_one, required): Select 'True' if the student is currently enrolled, 'False' if not.
* **Reason for Withdrawal (Page 4)** (`reason_for_withdrawal`, text, optional): Enter a brief explanation if the student is withdrawing from the school.
* **Signature (Page 5)** (`signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form to confirm verification.
* **School Info (Page 6)** (`school_info`, text, required): Enter school name, address, or other identifying information.
* **Student Grades (Page 7)** (`student_grades`, text, required): Enter student grades or progress updates.
* **Comments (Page 8)** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or notes about the student's enrollment status.
* **School Grades (Page 9)** (`school_grades`, text, required): Enter school grades or progress updates.

## Tips
* Always sign and date the form to confirm verification.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid errors.
* Use the correct format for entering student grades.
* If a student is withdrawing, provide a clear reason for withdrawal.
