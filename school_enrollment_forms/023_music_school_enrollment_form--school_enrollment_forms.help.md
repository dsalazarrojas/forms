# music_school_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The music school enrollment form is designed to collect essential information from students and parents for enrollment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that all required fields are filled.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (Student Name, text, required): Enter the student's full name as it appears on their official documents.
* **Student Email** (Student Email, email, required): Enter the student's email address for communication purposes.
* **Student Phone** (Student Phone, text, required): Enter the student's phone number for contact.
* **Parent Name** (Parent Name, text, required): Enter the parent's/guardian's full name as it appears on their official documents.
* **Parent Email** (Parent Email, email, optional): Enter the parent's/guardian's email address for communication purposes (optional).
* **Parent Phone** (Parent Phone, text, required): Enter the parent's/guardian's phone number for contact.
* **Instrument** (Instrument, select_one, required): Select the preferred instrument for the student.
* **Practice Time** (Practice Time, text, required): Enter the student's available practice time per week.
* **Submission** (Submission, text, optional): Add any additional information or comments about the student's interest in the music school.
