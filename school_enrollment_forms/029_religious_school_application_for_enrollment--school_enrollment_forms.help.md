# Religious School Application For Enrollment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from students or their parents/guardians for the purpose of enrolling a student in the religious school.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out your name (First name and Last name) in the first two fields.
2. Enter your Date of Birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
3. Enter your email address and phone number in the respective fields.
4. Enter your address where you can be contacted.
5. Enter your relationship to the student (e.g., Student, Parent, Guardian).
6. Enter your name as the student's parent/guardian.
7. Enter the student's class (e.g., Kindergarten, Grade 1, etc.).
8. Enter the student's School ID number, if provided by the school.
9. Submit the form once you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): This is the first name of the student or the parent/guardian filling out the form.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): This is the last name of the student or the parent/guardian filling out the form.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): This is the date of birth of the student in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This is the email address of the student or the parent/guardian filling out the form.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): This is the phone number of the student or the parent/guardian filling out the form.
* **School ID** (`school_id`, number, required): This is the ID number assigned to the student by the school.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): This is the name of the parent/guardian, if applicable.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, optional): This is the name of the guardian, if applicable.
* **Student Class** (`student_class`, text, optional): This is the class of the student that the parent/guardian is enrolling.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): This is the address of the student or the parent/guardian where they can be contacted.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you are the parent/guardian, please provide your name for both yourself and your child.
* If you are enrolling a child, please ensure that you have the permission of the student to do so.
* If you have any questions or issues while filling out this form, please contact the school's administration.
