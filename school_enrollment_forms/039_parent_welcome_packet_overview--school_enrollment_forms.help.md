# Parent Welcome Packet Overview - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Welcome Packet Overview form is used to collect information from parents or guardians to welcome them into the school community. It helps admissions or enrollment teams to understand the parent's needs and preferences, ensuring a smooth and personalized experience for their child.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1.  Provide a short overview of yourself or your family in the text field titled "Parent Welcome Packet Overview".
2.  In the "Welcome Materials Reviewed" field, indicate which school materials you have reviewed, such as school calendars or policies.
3.  Choose your preferred contact method in the "Contact Preferences" field.
4.  In the "Questions and Comments" field, ask any questions or provide comments about the school or the enrollment process.
5.  Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
6.  Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
7.  Enter the date of your child's enrollment in the "Date of Enrollment" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Parent Welcome Packet Overview**: A text field for the parent or guardian to write a short overview of themselves or their family.
*   **Welcome Materials Reviewed**: A text field where the parent or guardian indicates what school materials they have reviewed.
*   **Contact Preferences**: A selection field with the options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3. This field is for the parent or guardian to choose their preferred method of contact.
*   **Questions and Comments**: A free text field for the parent or guardian to ask questions or provide comments.
*   **Email Address**: A field to collect the parent or guardian's email address.
*   **Phone Number**: A field to collect the parent or guardian's phone number.
*   **Date of Enrollment**: A date field to record the date of the parent's child's enrollment in the school.
