# primary_school_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The primary school enrollment form is used to collect information from new students and their parents to facilitate the enrollment process. This form helps the school gather accurate and complete data for each student.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Provide the parent's name in the "Parent Name" field.
3. Select the student's grade level from the "Grade Level" dropdown list.
4. Enter the student's enrollment date, if applicable.
5. Add the student's phone number in the "Student Phone" field, if applicable.
6. Enter the parent's phone number in the "Parent Phone" field, if applicable.
7. Enter the student's email address in the "Email" field, if applicable.
8. Enter the student's address in the "Student Address" field, if applicable.
9. Enter the parent's address in the "Parent Address" field, if applicable.
10. Provide the student's date of birth (DOB) in the "Student DOB" field, if applicable.
11. Select the student's gender from the "Student Gender" dropdown list.
12. Provide the parent's date of birth (DOB) in the "Parent DOB" field, if applicable.
13. Enter the emergency contact's name in the "Emergency Contact Name" field, if applicable.
14. Enter the emergency contact's phone number in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field, if applicable.
15. Select any allergies the student may have from the "Allergies" dropdown list, if applicable.
16. Select any medical conditions the student may have from the "Medical Conditions" dropdown list, if applicable.
17. Enter any other medical conditions or disabilities the student may have in the "Other Medical Conditions" field, if applicable.
18. Select any medical devices the student may use from the "Medical Devices" dropdown list, if applicable.
19. Describe the relationship between the emergency contact and the student in the "Relationship to Student" field, if applicable.
20. Upload any supporting images or documents for the student, if applicable, by clicking on the "Student Image," "Parent Image," or "Student Document" fields.
21. Provide any additional information about the student, if necessary, in the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (Student Name, text, required): Enter the student's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Parent Name** (Parent Name, text, required): Enter the parent's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Grade Level** (Grade Level, select_one, required): Select the student's grade level from the dropdown list.
* **Enrollment Date** (Enrollment Date, date, optional): Enter the date the student is enrolling in the school.
* **Student Phone** (Student Phone, text, optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Parent Phone** (Parent Phone, text, optional): Enter the parent's phone number.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Address** (Student Address, text, optional): Enter the student's home address.
* **Parent Address** (Parent Address, text, optional): Enter the parent's home address.
* **Student DOB** (Student DOB, date, optional): Enter the student's date of birth.
* **Student Gender** (Student Gender, select_multiple, optional): Select the student's gender.
* **Parent DOB** (Parent DOB, date, optional): Enter the parent's date of birth.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Emergency Contact Name, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Emergency Contact Phone, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Allergies** (Allergies, select_multiple, optional): Select any allergies the student may have.
* **Medical Conditions** (Medical Conditions, select_multiple, optional): Select any medical conditions the student may have.
* **Other Medical Conditions** (Other Medical Conditions, text, optional): Enter any other medical conditions or disabilities the student may have.
* **Medical Devices** (Medical Devices, select_multiple, optional): Select any medical devices the student may use.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Relationship to Student, text, optional): Describe the relationship between the emergency contact and the student.
* **Student Image** (Student Image, note, optional): Upload a supporting image for the student, if necessary.
* **Parent Image** (Parent Image, note, optional): Upload a supporting image for the parent, if necessary.
* **Student Document** (Student Document, note, optional): Upload any supporting documents for the student, if necessary.
* **Additional Information** (Additional Information, note, optional): Provide any additional information about the student, if necessary.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the student.
* **Confirmation** (Confirmation, text, required): Enter your confirmation that you have read and understood the form information.
