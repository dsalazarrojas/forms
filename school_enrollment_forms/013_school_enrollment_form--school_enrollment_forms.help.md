# School Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students or their parents/guardians for school enrollment purposes. It should only be used for this specific purpose and not for any other reason.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **school-enrollment-form** (`school-enrollment-form`, select_one, not required): This field is used to select whether the student is being enrolled in school or not.
* **parent-name** (`parent_name`, text, not required): Please enter the name of the parent or guardian who is providing the information.
* **student-name** (`student_name`, text, not required): Please enter the name of the student being enrolled.
* **date-of-birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, not required): Enter the date of birth of the student in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **contact-number** (`contact_number`, text, not required): Enter a contact number where the parent or guardian can be reached for any further information.
* **email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
* **student-enrollment-status** (`student_enrollment_status`, select_one, not required): Select whether the student is Active or Inactive.
* **school-program** (`school_program`, select_one, not required): Select whether the student is enrolled in a school program or not.
* **class-name** (`class_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the class or course the student is enrolled in.
* **student-attendance-status** (`student_attendance_status`, select_one, not required): Select whether the student's attendance is Active or Inactive.
* **additional-comments** (`additional_comments`, text, not required): Enter any additional comments or information about the student.
* **parent-consent** (`parent_consent`, select_one, not required): Select whether the parent or guardian has given consent for the student to be enrolled in the school.
* **student-consent** (`student_consent`, select_one, not required): Select whether the student has given consent for their own enrollment in the school.
* **school-consent** (`school_consent`, select_one, not required): Select whether the school administration has given consent for the student to be enrolled.
* **teacher-consent** (`teacher_consent`, select_one, not required): Select whether the teacher has given consent for the student to be enrolled.
* **student-siblings** (`student_siblings`, text, not required): Enter any information about the student's siblings who may also be enrolled in the school.
* **student-ethnicity** (`student_ethnicity`, select_one, not required): Select whether the student identifies with a particular ethnicity.
* **student-country-of-origin** (`student_country_of_origin`, text, not required): Enter the country of origin of the student.
* **student-language** (`student_language`, select_one, not required): Select whether the student has a dominant language at home.
* **student-special-needs** (`student_special_needs`, select_one, not required): Select whether the student has any special needs.
* **student-dietary** (`student_dietary`, select_one, not required): Select whether the student has any dietary restrictions or preferences.
* **student-medical-conditions** (`student_medical_conditions`, text, not required): Enter any medical conditions the student may have.
* **student-emergency-contact** (`student_emergency_contact`, select_one, not required): Select whether the school has an emergency contact for the student.
* **student-parents-guardians** (`student_parents_guardians`, text, not required): Enter the name(s) of the student's parent or guardian.
* **student-parents-email** (`student_parents_email`, text, not required): Enter the email address of the student's parent or guardian.

Note: All fields marked as required are not actually required for this specific form. The purpose of this form is to gather information for school enrollment purposes, and not all fields are necessary for this process.
