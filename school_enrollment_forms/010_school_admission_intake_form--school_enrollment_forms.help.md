# School Admission Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The School Admission Intake Form is used to gather necessary information from students and their parents/guardians for the admission process. This form helps the school administration to better understand each student's needs, background, and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields accurately and thoroughly.
2. For any field that requires a specific format, such as the Student DOB or Parent Email fields, ensure the entered data is in the correct format.
3. For optional fields, provide as much relevant information as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name as it appears on their official documents, typically with their first and last name.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter the parent or guardian's name as it appears on their official documents.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school the student is applying to.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, number, required): Enter the student's current grade level or age (if applicable).
* **Student DOB** (`student_dob`, date, required): Enter the student's date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` (e.g., 1999-12-01).
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required): Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, optional): Enter a contact number for the parent or guardian.
* **Guardian Relationship** (`guardian_relationship`, select_one, optional): Select the relationship of the parent or guardian to the student (Student, Parent, or Guardian).
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, optional): Enter the student's permanent address.
* **Student Parent Contact** (`student_parent_contact`, text, optional): Enter the preferred contact method for the parent or guardian.
* **School Location** (`school_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the school the student is applying to.
* **Student Language** (`student_language`, text, optional): Enter the student's native language.
* **Student Notes** (`student_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional relevant information about the student.

## Tips

- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
- Double-check the format for fields that require specific formats (e.g., date, phone number).
- If you're unsure about any field, contact the school administration for clarification.
