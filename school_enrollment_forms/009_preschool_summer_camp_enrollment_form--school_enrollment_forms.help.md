# Preschool Summer Camp Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preschool Summer Camp Enrollment Form is a necessary document for the enrollment process for students in the preschool summer camp. The form helps us to collect essential information about your child, ensuring a safe and enjoyable experience for them during the camp.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please fill out the form with the required information about your child.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3.  Double-check the accuracy of your submitted information.
4.  Once you have filled out the form, click the submit button to send it to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): This field is where you will enter your child's name. Please enter their name as it is written on their birth certificate or identification card.
*   **Student Age** (`student_age`, number, required): This is your child's age. Please enter the age as of the current year.
*   **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, select_one, required): Please select the grade your child is currently in from the options provided.
*   **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): If you have an email address for your child, please enter it here. This will help us stay in touch with you and keep you updated about your child's activities during the camp.
*   **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): If you prefer to be contacted via phone, please enter your number here. This will help us get in touch with you quickly in case of any urgent matter.
*   **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): This is where you enter your name, the parent/guardian responsible for your child's enrollment.
*   **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required): Please enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you about your child's participation.
*   **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, required): Enter the best number to contact you with, especially in case of emergencies.
*   **Camp Address** (`camp_address`, text, required): Please enter the address where the summer camp will take place.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Please enter your name and contact information for the person we can contact in case of emergencies.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the preferred method of payment from the options provided.
*   **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, optional): If you have any specific payment instructions or details, please enter them here.
*   **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, note, optional): If your child has any medical information that we should be aware of, please enter it here.
*   **Allergy Info** (`allergy_info`, note, optional): If your child has any allergies that we need to know about, please enter the information here.
*   **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, note, optional): If your child has any medical conditions that we should be aware of, please enter the information here.
*   **Medication Info** (`medication_info`, note, optional): If your child takes any medication that we should be aware of, please enter the information here.
*   **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, note, optional): If your child requires any special needs or accommodations, please enter the information here.
*   **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, required): Please select any dietary restrictions your child may have.
*   **Special Equipment** (`special_equipment`, select_multiple, required): Please select any special equipment your child may require during the camp.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form to ensure accurate and successful enrollment.
