# Online Enrolment Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect online student enrollment information. It is intended to be used by schools to collect relevant details from students and their parents, including student identification, contact information, and supporting documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing basic student information, such as name, on the first page, "Student Information".
2. Move on to the second page, "Parent Information", to enter details about the student's parent or guardian.
3. On the third page, "Student Registration", fill in the student's registration details.
4. Upload required documents, such as birth certificate, transcript, or identification card, on the fourth page, "Upload Documents".
5. Review and confirm the registration details on the final page, "Confirm Registration".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information** (`Student Information`, text, required): This page is where you will enter basic student details, including name and other relevant information.
* **Parent Information** (`Parent Information`, text, required): Provide details about the student's parent or guardian.
* **Student Registration** (`Student Registration`, text, required): Enter the student's registration information.
* **Upload Documents** (`Upload Documents`, select_multiple, required): Select up to 4 documents to upload, such as birth certificate, transcript, or identification card.
	+ Select "Birth Certificate" to upload a scanned copy of the birth certificate.
	+ Select "Transcript" to upload a scanned copy of the transcript.
	+ Select "Identification Card" to upload a scanned copy of the identification card.
* **Confirm Registration** (`Confirm Registration`, note, required): This is a review page to confirm the student's registration details.
