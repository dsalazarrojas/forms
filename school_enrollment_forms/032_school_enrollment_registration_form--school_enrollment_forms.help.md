# School Enrollment Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Enrollment Registration Form is designed to collect personal and contact information from students and their guardians as part of the school's enrollment process. This form helps the school to gather essential details to facilitate a smooth and efficient enrollment experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your basic personal information, including First Name, Last Name, and Date of Birth.
2. Enter your contact information, such as Email and Phone Number.
3. Enter your Student ID Number and School ID (if applicable).
4. If you are a guardian, provide your personal information and relationship to the student.
5. Indicate how you heard about the school.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address to enable us to communicate with you.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id_number`, number, required): If you are a student, enter your student ID number.
* **School ID** (`school_id`, number, required): If you have an existing school ID, enter it here.
* **Guardian First Name** (`guardian_first_name`, text, required): If you are a guardian, enter your first name.
* **Guardian Last Name** (`guardian_last_name`, text, required): If you are a guardian, enter your last name.
* **Relationship to Student** (`relationship_to_student`, select_one, required): Select how you are related to the student (Parent, Guardian, or Other).
* **How did you hear about us?** (`how_did_you_hear_about_us`, text, optional): Share with us how you found out about our school.

## Tips
* Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Please double-check that all required fields are filled in correctly to avoid delays in the enrollment process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
