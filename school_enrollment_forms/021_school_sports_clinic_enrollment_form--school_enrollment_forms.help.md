# School Sports Clinic Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for enrolling students in a school sports clinic. It is designed for students and their parents or guardians to provide details such as personal information, medical information, and payment status.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete the form, please fill out all required fields.
- If a field is not required, you can still fill it out if you have relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Your email address for contact purposes.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required): Please select your grade level (High or Low).
* **Sport** (`sport`, select_multiple, required): Select all sports that you are interested in participating in.
* **Parent Permission** (`parent_permission`, select_one, required): Please give your parent/guardian permission for your participation (Yes or No).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): How do you plan to pay for the clinic (Yes or No).
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): The amount you plan to pay for the clinic.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required): The status of your payment (Active or Inactive).
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): The date you want to enroll for the clinic.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): The time you want to enroll for the clinic.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required): Any additional notes about your enrollment.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required): The date of your payment for the clinic.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
- If you have any issues while filling out the form, contact the school or sports clinic management.
