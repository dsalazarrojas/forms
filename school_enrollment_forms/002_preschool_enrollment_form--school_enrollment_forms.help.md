# Preschool Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information from parents or guardians about their child's enrollment in our preschool program. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth and successful enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Parent Name** field with your name as the parent or guardian of the child.
2. Enter your **Email** in the format of `username@domain.com`.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** in the format of `XXX-XXX-XXXX`.
4. Enter the **Name** of the child you are enrolling.
5. Select the **Birthdate** of the child using the calendar tool.
6. Enter the **Age** of the child in years.
7. Choose your preferred **Payment Method** from the available options (credit card, bank transfer, or check).
8. Select your **Payment Schedule** (one time, recurring, or monthly).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name**: A field to collect your name as the parent or guardian of the child. Please enter your name accurately.
* **Email**: A field to collect your email address. Please enter your email in the format of `username@domain.com`.
* **Phone Number**: A field to collect your phone number. Please enter your phone number in the format of `XXX-XXX-XXXX`.
* **Name**: A field to collect the name of the child you are enrolling. Please enter the child's full name.
* **Birthdate**: A field to collect the birthdate of the child. Please select the date using the calendar tool.
* **Age**: A field to collect the age of the child. Please enter the child's age in years.
* **Payment Method**: A field to select your preferred payment method (credit card, bank transfer, or check).
* **Payment Schedule**: A field to select your payment schedule (one time, recurring, or monthly).

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact our preschool staff.
* We will contact you to confirm your enrollment and provide further instructions.
