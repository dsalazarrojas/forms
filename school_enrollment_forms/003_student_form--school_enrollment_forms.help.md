# Student Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Form is designed to collect essential data about students, including their name, academic year, contact information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Student Form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the First Name field**: Enter the student's first name as it appears on their identification.
2. **Fill in the Last Name field**: Enter the student's last name as it appears on their identification.
3. **Select the School Year field**: Choose the student's current school year from the options: Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, or Senior.
4. **Enter a valid Email field**: Provide a correct email address associated with the student.
5. **Enter a valid Phone field**: Provide a correct phone number associated with the student.
6. **Enter a valid Date of Birth field**: Enter the student's date of birth in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
7. **Enter a valid Time of Birth field**: Enter the student's time of birth in the correct format (e.g., 12:00 AM).
8. **Enter any additional Note field**: If you have any other relevant information, use the Note field to provide additional details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`student_form`, text, required: false): Enter the student's first name as it appears on their identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter the student's last name as it appears on their identification.
* **School Year** (`school_year`, select_one, required: false): Choose the student's current school year from the options: Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, or Senior.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a correct email address associated with the student.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter a correct phone number associated with the student.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter the student's date of birth in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, required: false): Enter the student's time of birth in the correct format (e.g., 12:00 AM).
* **Note** (`note`, text, required: false): If you have any other relevant information, use this field to provide additional details.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required: false): Choose the assigned tool for the student from the options: chatjimmy or Other.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
* Double-check the school year to ensure it matches the student's current academic level.
* Validate email and phone number formats before submitting.
* Use the Note field to provide any additional relevant information.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accurate and complete data.
