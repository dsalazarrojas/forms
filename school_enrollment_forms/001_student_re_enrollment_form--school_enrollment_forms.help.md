# Student Re Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Re Enrollment Form is used to collect information from students and their guardians for re-enrollment in the educational institution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the student's name in the `Student Name` field.
2. Provide the student's ID in the `Student ID` field.
3. Select the student's grade level from the `Student Grade` dropdown menu.
4. Add the guardian's name in the `Guardian Name` field (optional).
5. Enter the guardian's phone number in the `Guardian Phone` field (optional).
6. Enter the student's email address in the `Student Email` field (optional).
7. Enter the student's address in the `Student Address` field (optional).
8. Explain the reason for re-enrollment in the `Reason for Re-Enrollment` field.
9. If needed, provide comments in the `Comments` field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's full name as per their identification documents.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required): Enter the unique student ID number.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, select_one, required): Select the student's current grade level.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, optional): Enter the guardian's or parent's name.
* **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, text, optional): Enter the guardian's or parent's phone number.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, optional): Enter the student's current address.
* **Reason for Re-Enrollment** (`reason_for_re_enrollment`, text, required): Enter a brief explanation for the re-enrollment.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or information regarding the re-enrollment.
