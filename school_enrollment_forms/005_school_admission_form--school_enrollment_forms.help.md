# School Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from prospective students to be admitted to a school.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form with accurate and truthful information. Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting.
- Complete all required fields, marked as required, to ensure your application is processed correctly.
- Leave optional fields blank if not relevant or necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`school_admission_form_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`school_admission_form_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`school_admission_form_date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in a standard date format (MM/DD/YY or YYYY).
* **Email** (`school_admission_form_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`school_admission_form_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Grade Level** (`school_admission_form_grade_level`, select_one, optional): Choose your current grade level (High/Low).
* **Program Interest** (`school_admission_form_program_interest`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your interest in various programs (Yes/No).
* **Additional Comments** (`school_admission_form_additional_comments`, note, optional): Add any additional information about your application.
* **Why were you admitted to this school?** (`school_admission_form_why_admitted`, text, optional): Briefly explain why you want to be admitted to this school.
* **What do you expect from our program?** (`school_admission_form_what_do_you_expect_from_our_program`, text, optional): Explain your expectations from the program.
* **Additional Comments 2** (`school_admission_form_additional_comments_2`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or concerns.
* **References** (`school_admission_form_references`, text, optional): Provide any relevant references or supporting documents.

Note: The school's admissions team will review and process your application based on the information provided in this form. Please ensure that the details you enter are accurate and complete to avoid any delays or issues during the admissions process.
