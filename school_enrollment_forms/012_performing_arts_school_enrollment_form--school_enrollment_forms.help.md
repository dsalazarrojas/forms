# Performing Arts School Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Performing Arts School Enrollment Form is designed to collect essential information from students and parents to facilitate a smooth enrollment process into the performing arts school.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form carefully**: Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
2. **Enter your first name**: In the "First Name" field, enter your student's first name.
3. **Enter your last name**: In the "Last Name" field, enter your student's last name.
4. **Enter student email address**: In the "Student Email" field, enter your student's email address.
5. **Enter student phone number**: In the "Student Phone" field, enter your student's phone number.
6. **Enter date of birth**: In the "Date of Birth" field, select your student's date of birth using the calendar picker or type it in the required format.
7. **Select grade level**: In the "Grade Level" field, select the current grade level of your student from the available options.
8. **Enter instrument details**: In the "Instrument" field, enter the instrument your student plays.
9. **Enter parent's name**: In the "Parent's Name" field, enter your name as the parent or guardian.
10. **Enter parent's email address**: In the "Parent's Email" field, enter your email address.
11. **Enter parent's phone number**: In the "Parent's Phone" field, enter your phone number.
12. **Emergency contact details**: In the "Emergency Contact" field, enter the name of the emergency contact person.
13. **Relationship with emergency contact**: In the "Relationship" field, select the relationship between your student and the emergency contact person from the available options.
14. **Enter emergency contact phone number**: In the "Emergency Contact Phone" field, enter the emergency contact's phone number.
15. **Enter student address**: In the "Student Address" field, enter your student's address.
16. **Enter student city**: In the "Student City" field, enter your student's city.
17. **Enter student state**: In the "Student State" field, enter your student's state or province.
18. **Enter student zip code**: In the "Student Zip" field, enter your student's zip code.
19. **Enter school name**: In the "School Name" field, enter the name of the school your student is currently attending.
20. **Enter school address**: In the "School Address" field, enter the address of the school your student is currently attending.
21. **Enter school city**: In the "School City" field, enter the city of the school your student is currently attending.
22. **Enter school state**: In the "School State" field, enter the state or province of the school your student is currently attending.
23. **Enter school zip code**: In the "School Zip" field, enter the zip code of the school your student is currently attending.
24. **Enter student grade level**: In the "Student Grade Level" field, enter your student's grade level (if different from the selected grade level in step 6).
25. **Enter instrument level**: In the "Instrument Level" field, enter the level of your student's instrument.
26. **Special needs**: In the "Special Needs" field, enter any special needs or requirements your student may have.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Double-check your answers for errors before submitting.
