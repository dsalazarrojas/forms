# Flower Seeds Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to order flower seeds. It collects their contact information, seed type, and order details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the fields with your contact information: **Customer Name**, **Email**, and **Phone**.
* Select the type of seed you want to order: **Seed Type**.
* Enter the quantity of seeds you need.
* If you have any special instructions for your order, enter them in the **Special Instructions** field.
* If you have any additional comments, select them from the **Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Information**: Enter your name and contact details so we can get in touch with you.
  - **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as you would like to be known.
  - **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
  - **Phone**: Enter your phone number in case we need to reach you.
- **Seed Type**: Select the type of seed you would like to order.
- **Quantity**: Enter the number of seeds you need.
- **Special Instructions**: If you have any specific instructions for your order, enter them here.
- **Comments**: Select any additional comments or notes you would like to include with your order.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact us at your email address or phone number.
