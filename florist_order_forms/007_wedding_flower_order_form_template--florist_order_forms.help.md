# Wedding Flower Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to place a wedding flower order. It will collect the necessary details about the customer, ceremony preferences, flower choices, and contact information to facilitate a successful order.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*). You can skip the optional fields, but providing them will allow us to better understand your needs and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Customer Info (Customer Info, required: false)**
  • **Name (Customer Info Name, required: false):** Enter the name of the person placing the order.
  • **Phone (Customer Info Phone, required: false):** Enter the contact phone number of the person placing the order.
  • **Email (Customer Info Email 2, required: false):** Enter the email address of the person placing the order.
  • **Address (Customer Info Address 2, required: false):** Enter the physical address of the person placing the order.

### 2. **Ceremony Details (Ceremony Details, required: false)**
  • **Ceremony Date (Ceremony Date, required: false):** Enter the date of the wedding ceremony.
  • **Ceremony Time (Ceremony Time, required: false):** Enter the time of the wedding ceremony.

### 3. **Style Preferences (Style Preferences, required: false)**
  • **Florettes (Florettes, required: false):** Choose if you want to have flowerettes.
  • **Centerpiece (Centerpiece, required: false):** Choose if you want a centerpiece.
  • **Florets (Florets, required: false):** Choose if you want to have florets.
  • **Florettes Color (Florettes Color, required: false):** Choose the color of the flowerettes.
  • **Florettes Shape (Florettes Shape, required: false):** Choose the shape of the flowerettes.

### 4. **Flower Choices (Flower Choices, required: false)**
  • **Florets (Florets, required: false):** Choose if you want to have florets.
  • **Florettes (Flower Choices Florettes, required: false):** Choose if you want to have flowerettes.
  • **Florettes Color (Flower Choices Florettes Color, required: false):** Choose the color of the flowerettes.
  • **Florettes Shape (Flower Choices Florettes Shape, required: false):** Choose the shape of the flowerettes.

### 5. **Contact Info (Contact Info, required: false)**
  • **Message (Message, required: false):** Enter any additional message to the vendor.
  • **Contact Info Message 2 (Contact Info Message 2, required: false):** Enter a message for the vendor to contact you.
  • **Customer Info Name 2 (Customer Info Name 2, required: false):** Enter any other name related to the order.
  • **Customer Info Address 2 (Customer Info Address 2, required: false):** Enter any other address related to the order.
  • **Contact Info Phone (Contact Info Phone, required: false):** Enter the phone number of the person to contact.
  • **Customer Info Email 2 (Customer Info Email 2, required: false):** Enter the email address of the person to contact.
  • **Contact Info Message 3 (Contact Info Message 3, required: false):** Enter any other message for the contact.

Please note that this form is designed to collect a wide range of information. If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to contact us for clarification.
