# Flower Shop Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flower Shop Order Form is used by customers to place an order for flowers or other products from a flower shop. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form to ensure your order is completed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and fill out each field according to your needs.
2. Make sure to provide accurate information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used for providing details about your order, such as the type or quantity of flowers.
* **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date you would like to pick up your order.
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the time you would like to pick up your order.
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, optional):
	+ Please provide any additional information about your delivery, such as special instructions or contact method.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your address for delivery or pickup purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Message to Store** (`message_to_store`, text, optional):
	+ Any additional comments or messages for the store.
