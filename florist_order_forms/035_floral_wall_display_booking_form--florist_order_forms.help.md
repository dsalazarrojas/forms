# Floral Wall Display Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Floral Wall Display Booking Form is a tool used by event planners or coordinators to book and manage floral wall displays for events. This form helps to gather information about the event, the user, and the terms of the booking, including payment and cancellation policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your information as a user in the "User Info" section.
2.  Provide event details in the "Event Details" section.
3.  Read and agree to the "Terms and Conditions" section.
4.  Choose your preferred options in the "Payment Terms" section.
5.  Confirm your reservation and payment details.
6.  Add any additional notes for the florist.
7.  Confirm your booking and payment.
8.  Provide any additional notes for the event planner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Info (1)
*   **User Info** (`User Info`, text, optional): Fill in your name and contact information as a user.
### Event Details (2)
*   **Event Details** (`Event Details`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the event and any necessary details.
### Terms and Conditions (3)
*   **Terms and Conditions** (`Terms and Conditions`, text, optional): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the booking.
### Floral Wall Reservation (4)
*   **Floral Wall Reservation** (`Floral Wall Reservation`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your desired floral wall display.
### Confirm Terms (5)
*   **Confirm Terms** (`Confirm Terms`, select multiple, required): Choose one or more options to confirm you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
### Payment Terms (6)
*   **Payment Terms** (`Payment Terms`, select one, required): Choose how you intend to pay for the floral wall display.
### Notes (7)
*   **Notes** (`Notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes for the florist about the event or booking.
### Confirm Date (8)
*   **Confirm Date** (`Confirm Date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
### Confirm Time (9)
*   **Confirm Time** (`Confirm Time`, time, required): Enter the time of the event.
### Payment Method (10)
*   **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select one, required): Choose the method of payment for the floral wall display.
### Total Cost (11)
*   **Total Cost** (`Total Cost`, number, required): Enter the total cost of the booking.
### Confirm Reservation (12)
*   **Confirm Reservation** (`Confirm Reservation`, select one, required): Choose to confirm the booking.
### Notes for Florist (13)
*   **Notes for Florist** (`Notes for Florist`, note, optional): Add any additional notes for the florist about the event or booking.
### Confirm Booking (14)
*   **Confirm Booking** (`Confirm Booking`, select one, required): Choose to confirm the booking.
### Confirm Payment (15)
*   **Confirm Payment** (`Confirm Payment`, select one, required): Choose to confirm the payment.
### Notes for Event Planner (16)
*   **Notes for Event Planner** (`Notes for Event Planner`, note, optional): Add any additional notes for the event planner about the event or booking.
### Confirm Cancellation (17)
*   **Confirm Cancellation** (`Confirm Cancellation`, select one, required): Choose to confirm the cancellation.
### Reason for Cancellation (18)
*   **Reason for Cancellation** (`Reason for Cancellation`, text, required): Enter the reason for the cancellation.
### Cancellation Fees (19)
*   **Cancellation Fees** (`Cancellation Fees`, number, required): Enter the cancellation fees.
### Cancellation Fees Breakdown (20)
*   **Cancellation Fees Breakdown** (`Cancellation Fees Breakdown`, select multiple, required): Choose the breakdown of the cancellation fees.
### Notes for Cancellation (21)
*   **Notes for Cancellation** (`Notes for Cancellation`, note, optional): Add any additional notes for the cancellation.
### Confirm Refund (22)
*   **Confirm Refund** (`Confirm Refund`, select one, required): Choose to confirm the refund.
### Refund Method (23)
*   **Refund Method** (`Refund Method`, select one, required): Choose the method of refund for the booking.
### Refund Fees (24)
*   **Refund Fees** (`Refund Fees`, number, required): Enter the refund fees.
### Confirm Fulfillment (25)
*   **Confirm Fulfillment** (`Confirm Fulfillment`, select one, required): Choose to confirm the fulfillment of the booking.
