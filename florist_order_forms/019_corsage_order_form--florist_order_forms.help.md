# Corsage Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corsage Order Form is a form used to gather information from customers for corsage orders, allowing the florist to provide accurate and timely service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name** in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select the type of corsage you'd like to order (Casket, Bouquet, or Arrangement) in the "Corsage Type" dropdown menu.
5. If desired, include a **Corsage Message** in the "Corsage Message" field.
6. Choose a **Delivery Date** and **Delivery Time** for your order.
7. Select your preferred **Payment Method** (Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal).
8. Any additional comments or instructions can be provided in the **Comments** field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name (Customer Name)** (`text`, required): Enter the name of the customer placing the order.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone (Phone)** (`text`, required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Corsage Type (Corsage Type)** (`select_one`, required): Select one of the available corsage types (Casket, Bouquet, or Arrangement).
* **Corsage Message (Corsage Message)** (`text`, optional): If desired, enter a personal message to accompany the corsage order.
* **Order Date (Order Date)** (`date`, required): Choose a date for the order to be fulfilled.
* **Delivery Date (Delivery Date)** (`date`, required): Select a date for the delivery of the corsage.
* **Delivery Time (Delivery Time)** (`time`, required): Choose a time for the delivery of the corsage.
* **Payment Method (Payment Method)** (`select_one`, required): Select the payment method to use for the order (Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal).
* **Comments (Comments)** (`note`, optional): Include any additional comments or instructions regarding the order.
