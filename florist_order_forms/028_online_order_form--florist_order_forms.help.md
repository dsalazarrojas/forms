# Online Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Order Form is used to order and send flowers to someone. Fill out the form with your information and the recipient's information, choose the type of flowers and delivery date, and add any message you'd like to include.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your information in the **Sender Information** section, including your **Sender Email** and **Sender Phone**.
2. Enter the **Receiver Information** section with the recipient's details, including their **Receiver Name**, **Receiver Email**, and **Receiver Phone**.
3. In the **Delivery Address** field, enter the address to which the flowers will be delivered.
4. Choose the **Flowers** you'd like to send by selecting the variety from the options provided.
5. Enter the **Delivery Date** for when you'd like to send the flowers.
6. Finally, add any **Message** you'd like to include with the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sender Information**: Enter your name, contact information, and any other details you'd like to provide.
* **Sender Email**: Enter the email address to which you'll receive notifications and updates about your order.
* **Sender Phone**: Enter your phone number to receive phone updates about your order.
* **Receiver Information**: Enter the recipient's name, contact information, and any other details you'd like to share.
* **Receiver Name**: Enter the name of the person to whom you're sending the flowers.
* **Receiver Email**: Enter the recipient's email address to receive the order updates and notifications.
* **Receiver Phone**: Enter the recipient's phone number to receive phone updates about the order.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter the address where the flowers will be delivered.
* **Flowers**: Select one or more of the available flower options to send.
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date you'd like to have the flowers delivered.
* **Message**: Add any personal message or note you'd like to include with the order.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the recipient's information and delivery address to ensure accurate delivery.
* Choose the correct flower options that match the occasion and the recipient's preferences.
* Enter a valid delivery date to ensure timely delivery.
* Add any personal message to make the order more special.
* Review your order carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
