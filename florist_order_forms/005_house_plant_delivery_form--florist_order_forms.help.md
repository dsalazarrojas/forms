# House Plant Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The House Plant Delivery Form is designed for customers to place orders for house plants. This form allows customers to input their order details, including delivery date, time, and address, as well as payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer information in the fields provided:
	* **House Plant Order**: Type in a brief description of the plants you wish to order.
	* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
	* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
	* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
2. Select the delivery details:
	* **Delivery Date**: Choose the date you would like your plants delivered.
	* **Delivery Time**: Select the time of day you would like your plants delivered.
3. Add any additional details:
	* **House Plant Details**: Enter any additional information about the plants you are ordering, such as plant size or type.
4. Specify the order:
	* **Plant Quantity**: Enter the number of plants you are ordering.
	* **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to pay for your order, either Cash, Card, or Paypal.
	* **Payment Details**: Enter any additional payment details, such as payment amount or invoice number.
5. Provide delivery address:
	* **Delivery Address**: Enter the address where you would like your plants delivered.
6. Add any order notes:
	* **Order Notes**: Enter any additional information about your order, such as special instructions or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **House Plant Order**: (`house_plant_order`, text, optional)
Enter a brief description of the plants you wish to order.
* **Customer Name**: (`customer_name`, text, optional)
Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: (`email`, text, optional)
Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: (`phone`, text, optional)
Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Delivery Date**: (`delivery_date`, date, optional)
Choose the date you would like your plants delivered.
* **Delivery Time**: (`delivery_time`, time, optional)
Select the time of day you would like your plants delivered.
* **House Plant Details**: (`house_plant_details`, text, optional)
Enter any additional information about the plants you are ordering, such as plant size or type.
* **Plant Quantity**: (`plant_quantity`, number, optional)
Enter the number of plants you are ordering.
* **Payment Method**: (`payment_method`, select_one, optional)
Choose how you would like to pay for your order, either Cash, Card, or Paypal.
* **Payment Details**: (`payment_details`, text, optional)
Enter any additional payment details, such as payment amount or invoice number.
* **Delivery Address**: (`delivery_address`, text, optional)
Enter the address where you would like your plants delivered.
* **Order Notes**: (`order_notes`, text, optional)
Enter any additional information about your order, such as special instructions or requests.
* **House Plant Price**: (`house_plant_price`, number, optional)
Enter the price of the plants you are ordering.
* **House Plant Discount**: (`house_plant_discount`, number, optional)
Enter any applicable discount for the plants.
* **House Plant Tax**: (`house_plant_tax`, number, optional)
Enter any applicable tax for the plants.
* **Total Cost**: (`total_cost`, number, optional)
Enter the total cost of the plants.
* **Payment Gateway**: (`payment_gateway`, select_one, optional)
Choose which payment gateway to use, either Stripe or Paypal.

## Tips
- Ensure you enter accurate and clear information to avoid errors in processing your order.
- Double-check your delivery date and time to ensure your plants arrive on time.
- If you have any special requests or instructions, enter them in the **Order Notes** field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact our customer support team.
