# Live Plant Delivery Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Live Plant Delivery Order Form" is used to collect information from customers for live plant delivery orders. This form helps us understand what plants the customer wants, how they want to receive them, and any other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information for the form.
2. Enter your delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field.
3. Provide any additional details for your order in the "Order Details" field.
4. Select the plants you want to order (up to three) from the "Shipping Options" field.
5. Choose your preferred payment method from the "Payment Info" field.
6. Enter your customer information, including your name and any contact details.
7. Select the date you want your order delivered from the "Order Date" field.
8. Choose your preferred shipping method from the "Shipping Method" field.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Address**: Enter your delivery address where the plants will be delivered.
* **Order Details**: Provide any additional details about your order, such as specific plant varieties or quantities.
* **Shipping Options**: Select up to three plants to be delivered from the available options.
* **Payment Info**: Choose from the available payment options to complete your order.
* **Customer Info**: Enter your name and any additional contact details.
* **Order Date**: Select the date you want your order delivered.
* **Shipping Method**: Choose your preferred shipping method.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
