# Funeral Flower Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Funeral Flower Order Form is designed to collect information from customers who wish to place an order for funeral flowers. This form gathers the necessary details to process the order efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide your Customer Info: Enter your name and contact details in the "Customer Info" section.
2. Specify Order Details: Enter the order's specific details, such as the type of flowers and any special requests, in the "Order Details" section.
3. Select Delivery Info: Enter the delivery information for the order, including the date and time of the funeral, in the "Delivery Info" section.
4. Enter Payment Details: Enter the payment information for the order in the "Payment Details" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info**: Please enter your name and contact details in this section.
* **Order Details**: Enter the specific details of the order, such as the type of flowers, number of arrangements, and any special requests. 
* **Delivery Info**: Enter the delivery date, time, and any specific delivery instructions in this section.
* **Payment Details**: Enter the payment method and amount for the order in this section.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have any special requests or instructions, please include them in the "Order Details" section.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
