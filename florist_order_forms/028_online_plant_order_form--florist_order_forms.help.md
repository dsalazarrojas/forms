# Online Plant Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Plant Order Form is used to collect information for the ordering and purchasing of plants. This form is a crucial tool for customers to provide their details for successful plant delivery and payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the payment information to secure the order.
2.  Fill in the plant details including the name, description, and quantity of the plant you wish to purchase.
3.  Provide your shipping address, including your city, state, and country.
4.  Choose your preferred payment gateway method.
5.  Enter any additional notes or remarks for the order, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Online Plant Order** (`plant_order_form`, text, required/optional): This field is the main title of the form. It does not need to be filled unless required by the form submission system.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, required/optional): Please enter your payment details to complete the order securely.
* **Plant Details** (`plant_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the name, description, and quantity of the plant you wish to purchase.
* **Shipping Info** (`shipping_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your shipping address, including your city, state, and country.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your shipping address to ensure the plant reaches your location successfully.
* **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city of your shipping address.
* **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`, text, required/optional): Enter the state of your shipping address.
* **Shipping Zipcode** (`shipping_zipcode`, text, required/optional): Enter the zip code of your shipping address.
* **Shipping Country** (`shipping_country`, text, required/optional): Enter the country of your shipping address.
* **Payment Gateway** (`payment_gateway`, select_one, required/optional): Select your preferred payment method to complete the order.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for order confirmation and contact.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for order updates and contact.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your address for order delivery.
* **Plant Name** (`plant_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the plant you wish to purchase.
* **Plant Description** (`plant_description`, text, required/optional): Enter the description of the plant you wish to purchase.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required/optional): Enter the quantity of the plant you wish to purchase.
* **Price** (`price`, number, required/optional): Enter the price of the plant you wish to purchase.
* **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city of your shipping address.
* **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`, text, required/optional): Enter the state of your shipping address.
* **Shipping Zipcode** (`shipping_zipcode`, text, required/optional): Enter the zip code of your shipping address.
* **Shipping Country** (`shipping_country`, text, required/optional): Enter the country of your shipping address.
* **Shipping Info 2** (`shipping_info_2`, text, required/optional): Enter additional shipping information.
* **Plant Image** (`plant_image`, text, required/optional): Enter a photo or image of the plant.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date for the order.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time for the order.
* **Note** (`note`, text, required/optional): Enter any notes or remarks for the order.
* **Email Note** (`email_note`, text, required/optional): Enter any email-related notes or remarks.
* **Email 2** (`email_2`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address for additional contact.
* **Phone 2** (`phone_2`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact.
* **Address 2** (`address_2`, text, required/optional): Enter your address for additional contact.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required/optional): Enter the zip code of your address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city of your address.
* **State** (`state`, text, required/optional): Enter the state of your address.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required/optional): Enter the country of your address.

Note that some fields are required/optional, but the required fields are not specified clearly. To complete the form, fill in as much information as you can and proceed with the required fields. 

Note: Please ensure that the fields are filled correctly and accurately, as incorrect or missing information may lead to delays or cancellations of orders. 

This guide is for general understanding and is subject to updates. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact our support team.
