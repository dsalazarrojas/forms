# Holiday Wreath Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place orders for holiday wreaths. Please fill out the form to provide your shipping address, payment method, and any additional order comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "Order Wreath" page with your order details.
2. Enter your shipping address on the "Shipping Address" page.
3. Select your payment method on the "Payment Method" page.
4. Add any additional comments on the "Order Comments" page.
5. Review your order and click "Submit" to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Wreath**: This is the main section where you enter your holiday wreath order details. Please fill out this section carefully to ensure that your order is processed correctly.
* **Shipping Address**: Please enter your shipping address, including your street address and zip code.
* **City**: Enter the city where you would like your order shipped to.
* **State**: Select your state from the list. This will help us determine the shipping destination.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code to ensure that your order is delivered to the correct location.
* **Payment Method**: You can choose to pay with either a credit card or PayPal. Please select the payment method that is most convenient for you.
* **Order Comments**: If you have any special requests or comments about your order, you can enter them here.
* **Submit**: Review your order carefully before clicking "Submit". This will complete your order and we will process it accordingly.
* **Cancel**: If you need to cancel or make changes to your order, click "Cancel".
* **Save Changes - Preview**: If you want to save your changes and preview your order before submitting it, click "Save Changes - Preview". 

**Tips**
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your order is processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or issues with your order, please contact us for assistance.
