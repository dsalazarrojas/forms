# Pakistan Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant information and quiz scores for the "Pakistan Knowledge Quiz". Filling out this form is optional and helps us understand more about the participants and the quiz performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your participant details such as Name and Email.
2. Provide your Phone Number (optional).
3. Enter your Quiz Score.
4. Select the relevant Quiz Category(ies) that the question belonged to.
5. Select the Question Number that you answered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details** (Participant Details, text, optional): Enter your participant information such as name and any other details you want to share.
* **Name** (Name, text, optional): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Quiz Score** (Quiz Score, number, optional): Enter your quiz score.
* **Quiz Category** (Quiz Category, select multiple, optional): Select the relevant category(ies) that the question belonged to.
* **Question Number** (Question Number, number, optional): Enter the number of the question you answered.
