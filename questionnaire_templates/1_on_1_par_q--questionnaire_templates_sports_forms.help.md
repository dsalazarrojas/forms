## 1 On 1 Physical Activity Readiness Questionnaire (PAR-Q) - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare professionals or fitness professionals assess an individual's readiness for a 1-on-1 physical activity program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully and truthfully.
2. Select all options that apply for multiple-choice questions.
3. Provide as much detail as possible for text questions.
4. Review and agree to the questions above by selecting "Yes, I agree" at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/dd/yyyy.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your primary contact email address for communication and booking confirmations.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `phone`, required): Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, optional): Select your gender for demographic purposes and tailored program design.
* **Has a doctor ever said you have a heart condition or heart disease?** (`heart_condition`, `select_one`, required): Answer truthfully if a doctor has ever said you have a heart condition or heart disease.
* **Do you feel pain in your chest when you do physical activity?** (`chest_pain_activity`, `select_one`, required): Answer truthfully if you experience pain in your chest during physical activity.
* **Do you ever lose your balance because of dizziness or do you ever lose consciousness?** (`dizziness_fainting`, `select_one`, required): Answer truthfully if you experience dizziness or lose consciousness during physical activity.
* **Do you have a bone or joint problem that could be made worse by a change in your physical activity?** (`bone_joint_problem`, `select_one`, required): Answer truthfully if you have a bone or joint problem that could be made worse by physical activity.
* **Are you currently taking any prescription medication for blood pressure or a heart condition?** (`blood_pressure_meds`, `select_one`, required): Answer truthfully if you are taking prescription medication for blood pressure or a heart condition.
* **Do you have any other medical conditions or health concerns not listed above?** (`medical_conditions_others`, `text`, optional): Include recent surgeries, chronic illnesses, or disabilities not listed above.
* **What is your current physical activity level?** (`current_fitness_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your current physical activity level from the options provided.
* **What are your primary fitness goals?** (`fitness_goals`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply from the fitness goals provided.
* **How many 1-on-1 workout sessions per week are you aiming for?** (`weekly_sessions_target`, `integer`, required): Enter the number of 1-on-1 workout sessions you aim to have per week.
* **Briefly describe your previous exercise experience or preferred activities.** (`previous_exercise_experience`, `text`, optional): Provide as much detail as possible about your previous exercise experience or preferred activities.
* **Do you have any current injuries, chronic pain, or physical limitations?** (`current_injuries_pain`, `text`, optional): Provide detail about any current injuries, chronic pain, or physical limitations you have.
* **What is your preferred time of day for workouts?** (`preferred_workout_time`, `time`, optional): Select your preferred time of day for workouts.
* **On which days of the week are you generally available for sessions?** (`availability_days`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all days of the week you are generally available for sessions.
* **I confirm that I have read and understood the questions above, and I have answered them truthfully.** (`consent_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes, I agree" to confirm you have read and understood the questions and answered them truthfully.
* **Any additional comments or specific requests?** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): E.g., "Focus on core strength," "Avoid jumping," "Prefer morning sessions."
