# Personalized Activity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personalized Activity Survey form is designed to provide a better experience for users by understanding their preferences and availability. This form helps in reducing the likelihood of non-response and improving engagement.


## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start with the **Welcome to the survey** page.
2. In the **What activities do you like?** page, select all activities that you enjoy doing.
3. In the **How often do you have time for activities?** page, select the day(s) of the week that you are available for activities.
4. In the **What time of day do you have for activities?** page, select your preferred time slot for activities.
5. In the **How long do activities last?** page, enter the duration of activities you prefer.
6. In the **What days of the week do you have availability?** page, select all days of the week that you are available for activities.
7. In the **What time slots do you have availability?** page, select all time slots that you are available for activities.
8. Finally, indicate if there are any **Exceptions to your availability?**.


## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Welcome to the survey** (`1`, `note`, optional): This is a welcoming message to introduce the survey.
* **What activities do you like?** (`2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all activities that you enjoy doing. For example, sports, reading, hiking, or team-building exercises.
* **How often do you have time for activities?** (`3`, `select_one`, optional): Select the day(s) of the week that you are available for activities. You can choose more than one day if needed.
* **What time of day do you have for activities?** (`4`, `date`, optional): Enter the time you prefer for activities.
* **How long do activities last?** (`6`, `number`, optional): Enter the length of activities you prefer.
* **What days of the week do you have availability?** (`7`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all days of the week that you are available for activities.
* **What time slots do you have availability?** (`8`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all time slots that you are available for activities.
* **Exceptions to your availability?** (`9`, `select_multiple`, optional): If there's any exceptional situation that affects your availability, indicate here.

Note: This form is designed to be completed in a way that makes the user feel comfortable and engaged. If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask your administrator for help!
