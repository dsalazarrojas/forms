# Loan Program Self Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Loan Program Self Assessment form is used to collect and process loan program information for users. This form helps users evaluate their loan program status, providing valuable insights into their loan application process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the loan program status you are currently in (Yes/No).
2. Enter your contact information (name, contact number, email).
3. Enter your loan details (loan amount, loan type, purpose of loan).
4. Provide your financial information (income, employment status, employment length, employment type).
5. Enter your address and contact information (city, state, zip code).
6. Enter your demographic information (social security number, date of birth, time of birth, marital status).
7. Evaluate your education level (High/Low).
8. Check your employment termination status (Yes/No).
9. Review and confirm all the information provided.


## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **1. loan_program_self_assessment**: This field is used to select the loan program status. Please choose "Yes" or "No" based on your current loan program status.
  * Type: text
  * Required: False

- **2. name**: Enter your full name for contact purposes.
  * Type: text
  * Required: False

- **3. contact_number**: Enter your contact number for loan program communications.
  * Type: email
  * Required: False

- **4. email**: Enter your email address for loan program communications.
  * Type: email
  * Required: False

- **5. contact_number**: Enter your secondary contact number for loan program communications.
  * Type: text
  * Required: False

- **6. loan_amount**: Enter the loan amount you are requesting.
  * Type: number
  * Required: False

- **7. loan_type**: Select the type of loan you are interested in.

- **8. purpose_of_loan**: Select the purpose of your loan.

  * Required: False

- **9. income**: Enter your annual income.

- **10. employment_status**: Select your employment status.
