# Human Rights Literacy Level Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is intended to assess your level of understanding and knowledge of various human rights concepts. Your participation will help us identify areas where we can improve our education and awareness materials on human rights.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please answer all questions honestly, selecting the response that best matches your current understanding.

3.  Please do not skip any questions. If you are unsure of an answer, select "No" or the option that best describes your level of awareness.
4.  Take your time to review each question and answer carefully, and if needed, ask for help or clarification before proceeding.
