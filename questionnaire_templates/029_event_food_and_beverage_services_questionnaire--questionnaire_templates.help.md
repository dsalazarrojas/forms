# Event Food And Beverage Services Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Food And Beverage Services Questionnaire is designed to gather feedback from event attendees on the quality and experience of food and beverage services at an event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Attendee Name**: Please enter the name of the attendee who is completing this form.

2. **Email**: If you would like to be contacted with a follow-up question or to have your response included in future event planning, please enter your email address.

3. **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or feedback that you would like to share, please use this text box. You can write as much or as little as you like.

4. **Overall Experience**: Rate your overall experience with the food and beverage services at the event.

5. **Food Quality**: Rate the quality of the food served at the event.

6. **Bverage Quality**: Rate the quality of the beverages served at the event.

7. **Venue Experience**: Rate the experience at the venue where the event took place.

8. **Food Services**: Rate the level of service for food provided at the event.

9. **Catering Services**: Rate the level of catering services provided at the event.

10. **Food and Beverage Services**: If applicable, please enter the date that the food and beverage services were provided.

11. **Catering Cost**: If you were charged for catering services, please enter the cost.

12. **Food Cost**: If you were charged for food, please enter the cost.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your registration badge or ID badge. 
* **Email**: Your email address will only be used if you are contacted for follow-up questions or to have your responses included in future event planning. 
* **Comments**: This is your chance to provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience. 
* **Overall Experience**: Rate your overall experience with the food and beverage services. 
* **Food Quality**: Rate the quality of the food served. 
* **Bverage Quality**: Rate the quality of the beverages served. 
* **Venue Experience**: Rate the experience at the venue where the event took place. 
* **Food Services**: Rate the level of service for food provided. 
* **Catering Services**: Rate the level of catering services provided. 
* **Food and Beverage Services**: If you were provided with food and beverage services, please enter the date they were provided. 
* **Catering Cost**: If you were charged for catering services, please enter the cost. 
* **Food Cost**: If you were charged for food, please enter the cost. 

## Tips
* Please answer all questions as thoroughly and honestly as possible. 
* If you were not provided with food and beverage services, please select "Not Applicable" for the relevant fields. 
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Comments" field. 
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask for clarification or assistance.
