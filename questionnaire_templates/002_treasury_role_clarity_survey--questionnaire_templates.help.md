# Treasury Role Clarity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treasury Role Clarity Survey is designed to gather information about your role within the treasury department, including your responsibilities, reporting lines, and contact details. This information will help to improve internal communication and facilitate decision-making within the department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer all questions accurately.
2. Select the relevant options for each question.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Treasury Responsibilities
* **Treasury Responsibilities** (`treasury_responsibilities`, `select_one`, required): Please select 'Yes' if you are responsible for treasury-related activities, such as financial planning, budgeting, or investment management. Select 'No' otherwise.

### 2. Roles
* **Roles** (`roles`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the roles that apply to your position within the treasury department. This may include positions such as Financial Analyst, Portfolio Manager, or Operations Manager.

### 3. Reporting Line
* **Reporting Line** (`reporting_line`, `select_one`, required): Please select 'Yes' if you report directly to the treasury department head, 'No' otherwise.

### 4. Feedback
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your role within the treasury department.

### 5. Comments
* **Comments** (`comment`, `time`, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them here.

### 6. Email Address
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your work email address.

### 7. Name
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.

### 8. Department
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the department you belong to.

### 9. Reporting Line Title
* **Reporting Line Title** (`reporting_line_title`, `text`, optional): If you have a title or position within your reporting line, please enter it here.

### 10. Reporting Line Email
* **Reporting Line Email** (`reporting_line_email`, `email`, optional): If you have an email address for your reporting line, please enter it here.

### 11. Reporting Line Phone
* **Reporting Line Phone** (`reporting_line_phone`, `text`, optional): If you have a phone number for your reporting line, please enter it here.

### 12. Reporting Line (again)
* **Reporting Line** (`reporting_line`, `text`, optional): This is a duplicate field, please ignore it and do not fill it out.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all questions accurately and honestly.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or department head.
