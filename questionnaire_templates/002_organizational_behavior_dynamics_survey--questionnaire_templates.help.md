# Organizational Behavior Dynamics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on an individual's perceptions of their organizational behavior and dynamics.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, select one or more responses for each question. Multiple-choice questions will have a list of options, while single-choice questions will have only one option available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Culture Assessment (id: 1)
* **Culture Assessment** (`culture_assessment`, select_multiple): This question asks about the current state of the culture in your organization. You can select one or more responses that best describe your perception of the culture.

### Leadership Style (id: 2)
* **Leadership Style** (`leadership_style`, select_one): This question asks you to rate your supervisor's leadership style based on your experience.

### Communication Effectiveness (id: 3)
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, number): This question asks you to rate the effectiveness of communication within your team or department. Please enter a numerical value between 1 and 10.

### Collaboration Assessment (id: 4)
* **Collaboration Assessment** (`collaboration_assessment`, select_multiple): This question asks about the level of collaboration within your team or department. You can select one or more responses that best describe your perception of collaboration.

### Job Satisfaction (id: 5)
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, number): This question asks you to rate your overall job satisfaction. Please enter a numerical value between 1 and 10.

### Organizational Climate (id: 6)
* **Organizational Climate** (`organizational_climate`, select_one): This question asks you to rate the overall climate of your organization. Please select one response that best describes your perception.

### Leadership Effectiveness (id: 7)
* **Leadership Effectiveness** (`leadership_effectiveness`, select_one): This question asks you to rate the effectiveness of leadership within your organization. Please select one response that best describes your perception.

### Team Performance (id: 8)
* **Team Performance** (`team_performance`, select_one): This question asks you to rate the overall performance of your team. Please select one response that best describes your perception.

### Leadership Style Score (id: 9)
* **Leadership Style Score** (`leadership_style_score`, number): This question asks you to rate the overall leadership style within your organization. Please enter a numerical value.

### Team Satisfaction (id: 10)
* **Team Satisfaction** (`team_satisfaction`, select_one): This question asks you to rate the overall satisfaction of your team. Please select one response that best describes your perception.

## Tips
- Please be honest and sincere when completing this form.
- Select the response that best describes your perception, even if it's not what you would prefer.
- If you're unsure about any question, please select the response that best approximates your perception.
