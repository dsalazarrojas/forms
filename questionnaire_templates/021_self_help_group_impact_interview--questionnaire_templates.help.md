# Self-Help Group Impact Interview - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and insights from participants in a self-help group program. It's meant to be completed by both facilitators and participants to understand the impact and effectiveness of the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information:
	* **Participant Name**: The name of the participant who has attended the self-help group program.
	* **Facilitator Name**: The name of the facilitator who led the self-help group program.
	* **Group ID**: The ID of the self-help group program.
	* **Program Start Date**: The start date of the self-help group program.
	* **Program End Date**: The end date of the self-help group program.
2. Answer the following questions:
	* **Group Duration**: The duration of the program (e.g., number of weeks or sessions).
	* **Group Goals**: The goals set by the group or program.
	* **Participant Engagement**: A brief description of the participant's engagement with the program.
	* **Group Outcomes**: A brief description of the outcomes or results achieved by the group.
	* **Participant Satisfaction**: A rating of the participant's satisfaction with the program.
	* **Challenges Faced**: A list of challenges faced by the participant during the program.
	* **Support Needed**: A description of the support needed by the participant during the program.
	* **Participant Feedback**: Additional feedback from the participant.
3. Review and complete the rating sections:
	* **Participant Rating**: A rating of the participant's satisfaction with the program.
	* **Facilitator Rating**: A rating of the facilitator's performance.
	* **Participant Recommendation**: A recommendation from the participant for future program improvements.
	* **Facilitator Recommendation**: A recommendation from the facilitator for future program improvements.
4. Provide follow-up information:
	* **Follow Up Plan**: A plan for follow-up meetings or actions.
	* **Follow Up Actions**: A list of specific actions to be taken for follow-up.
	* **Follow Up Notes**: Additional notes for the follow-up meeting.
