# Children's Health Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a child's health, including demographic and medical history, lifestyle habits, and mental and emotional well-being.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in order from Page 1 to Page 2.
2. Start with the "Child Information" page, where you will find fields for "Child Full Name", "Date of Birth", "Age", and "Child's Gender".
3. Move on to the "Parent or Guardian Information" page, where you will find fields for "Parent or Guardian Name", "Email", and "Parent's Phone Number".
4. In the "Medical History" page, select all the chronic conditions that apply to the child from the drop-down menu.
5. On the "Health Habits" page, rate the child's eating habits, average daily physical activity in hours, and average hours of sleep per night.
6. In the "Mental and Emotional Health" page, select all the mental health concerns that apply to the child.
7. Finally, add any additional health notes or concerns in the "Additional Health Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Child Information
* **Child Full Name** (Child Name, text, required): Enter the child's full name.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Enter the child's date of birth.
* **Age** (Age, number, required): Enter the child's current age in years.
* **Gender** (Gender, select_one, optional): Select the child's gender from the drop-down menu.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (Parent or Guardian Name, text, required): Enter the parent or guardian's name.
* **Parent Email** (Parent Email, email, required): Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
* **Parent Phone** (Parent Phone, text, required): Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.

### Page 2: Medical History
* **Does the child have any chronic conditions** (Chronic Conditions, select_multiple, required): Select all the chronic conditions that apply to the child.
* **List all medications the child takes** (Medications, text, optional): List all the medications the child is currently taking.
* **List food, medicine, and environmental allergies** (Allergies, text, optional): List all the food, medicine, and environmental allergies the child has.
* **Recent illnesses or infections in the last 3 months** (Recent Illnesses, text, optional): List any recent illnesses or infections the child has had in the last 3 months.

### Page 3: Health Habits
* **Eating habits** (Eating Habits, select_one, required): Rate the child's eating habits as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Average daily exercise or physical activity in hours** (Exercise Level, number, optional): Enter the average number of hours the child engages in physical activity per day.
* **Average hours of sleep per night** (Sleep Hours, number, required): Enter the average number of hours the child sleeps per night.
* **Height (inches)** (Height, number, optional): Enter the child's height in inches.
* **Weight (pounds)** (Weight, number, optional): Enter the child's weight in pounds.

### Page 4: Mental and Emotional Health
* **Does the child have any mental health concerns** (Mental Concerns, select_multiple, required): Select all the mental health concerns that apply to the child.
* **Any mental health concerns** (Mental Concerns, text, optional): Add any additional mental health concerns not listed in the previous question.
* **Additional health notes or concerns** (Additional Notes, text, optional): Add any additional health notes or concerns about the child.

### Page 5: Vaccinations
* **Are vaccinations up to date** (Up to Date, select_one, required): Select whether the child's vaccinations are up to date.
* **Any missing or delayed vaccinations** (Missing Vaccines, text, optional): List any missing or delayed vaccinations the child has had.

### Page 6: Physical Health
* **List any known medical conditions** (Physical Health, text, optional): List any known medical conditions the child has.

### Page 7: Additional Health Information
* **Average daily exercise or physical activity in hours** (Exercise Level, number, optional): Enter the average number of hours the child engages in physical activity per day.
* **Average hours of sleep per night** (Sleep Hours, number, optional): Enter the average number of hours the child sleeps per night.
