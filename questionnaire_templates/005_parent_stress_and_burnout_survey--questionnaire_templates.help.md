# Parent Stress And Burnout Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Stress And Burnout Survey is designed to measure the stress and burnout experiences of parents and caregivers. It is intended to gather information about their current stress levels, coping strategies, sources of stress, and experiences with burnout.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each question carefully and respond to each section as accurately as possible.
- Please answer all required questions (marked as "required" in the form).
- Select all applicable answers for multiple-choice questions.
- Enter text or date responses in the designated fields.
- If you have any other comments or questions, you can use the "Do you have any other comments?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Parent or Caregiver Role** (`parent_caregiver_role`): Please enter your role as a parent or caregiver (e.g., mother, father, guardian, etc.).
- **How would you rate your current level of stress?** (`stress_level`): Rate your current stress level on a scale from 0 to 10.
- **What coping strategies do you use to manage your stress?** (`coping_strategies`): Select all the coping strategies you use to manage your stress (e.g., Parental self-care, Exercise, Mindfulness, etc.).
- **What are the main sources of stress in your life?** (`stress_sources`): Select all the sources of stress you are currently experiencing (e.g., Financial worries, Relationship issues, Career or job pressures, etc.).
- **How long have you been experiencing stress?** (`experience_with_stress`): Enter the date you started experiencing stress.
- **Can you please describe your experiences with stress?** (`experience_description`): Please provide a detailed description of your stress experiences.
- **How do you typically react when experiencing stress?** (`emotional_reactions`): Please describe your typical reaction when experiencing stress.
- **Have you sought support from any services or professionals?** (`support_services`): Select all the support services or professionals you have sought or would like to seek (e.g., Therapist, Counselor, Support group, etc.).
- **What burnout experiences have you had?** (`burnout_experiences`): Select all the burnout experiences you have had (e.g., Emotional exhaustion, Cognitive impairments, Reduced motivation, etc.).
- **How often do you feel burned out?** (`burnout_frequency`): Select how often you feel burned out (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Never).
- **How has burnout affected your health?** (`impact_on_health`): Select all the ways burnout has affected your health (e.g., Mental health, Physical health, Relationship issues, etc.).
- **How long have you been feeling burned out?** (`burnout_impact_duration`): Enter the date you started feeling burned out.
- **How severe has burnout been for you?** (`burnout_severity`): Rate the severity of your burnout on a scale from 0 to 10.
- **What consequences have you experienced due to burnout?** (`burnout_consequences`): Select all the consequences you have experienced due to burnout (e.g., Increased anxiety, Mood changes, Reduced productivity, etc.).
- **What prevents you from feeling more burned out?** (`burnout_prevention`): Please describe what prevents you from feeling more burned out.
- **What remedies or treatments have you tried to address burnout?** (`burnout_remediation`): Select all the remedies or treatments you have tried to address burnout (e.g., Self-care, Mindfulness, Exercise, etc.).
- **Which services have you sought or would you like to seek for burnout support?** (`burnout_support_services`): Select all the services you have sought or would like to seek for burnout support (e.g., Therapist, Counselor, Support group, etc.).
- **What measures would you suggest to prevent burnout?** (`burnout_preventive_measures`): Please describe measures you would suggest to prevent burnout.
- **Do you have any other comments about burnout?** (`burnout_additional_comments`): Please enter any other comments about burnout.
- **Can we share your responses with your school or organization?** (`burnout_agreed_to_share`): Check if you agree to share your responses with your school or organization.
- **Do you have any other comments?** (`burnout_additional_comments`): Please enter any other comments.
- **Can we follow up with you?** (`burnout_agreed_to_follow_up`): Check if you agree to follow up with you.
- **Can we share your responses with your school or organization?** (`burnout_agreed_to_share`): Check if you agree to share your responses with your school or organization.
- **Can we contact you for more information?** (`burnout_agreed_to_contact`): Check if you agree to be contacted for more information.
- **Do you have any other comments?** (`burnout_additional_comments`): Please enter any other comments.
