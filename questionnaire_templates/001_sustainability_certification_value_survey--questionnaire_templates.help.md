# Sustainability Certification Value Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainability Certification Value Survey is a tool used to collect information about the sustainability certification process for organizations. This form is designed to guide users in providing accurate and detailed information about their certification, including the type of certification, date and certification body.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of sustainability certification you hold from the "What is the name of your sustainability certification?" field.
2. Enter the date you were certified in the "Date Certified" field.
3. Provide the name of the certification body in the "Certification Body" field.
4. Enter any relevant certification number in the "Certification Number" field.
5. Briefly describe the type of sustainability certification you hold in the "Sustainability Certification" field.
6. Select the level of sustainability certification you hold from the "Sustainability Certification Level" field.
7. Enter the amount of time and investment your organization has committed to sustainability in the "Sustainability Commitment" and "Sustainability Investment" fields respectively.
8. Optionally, provide any additional comments or notes about your sustainability certification in the "Sustainability Impact" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sustainability Certification Value Survey** (Text): This is the title of the form.
* **What is the name of your sustainability certification?** (Select One): This field asks for the type of sustainability certification you hold. Please select one of the available options.
	+ None: Select this option if you do not hold any sustainability certification.
	+ ISO 26000: This option is not available as it was not present in the options list.
	+ ISO 14001: This option is for organizations that have been certified under the International Standard (ISO 14001).
	+ OHSAS 18001: This option is not available as it was not present in the options list.
	+ Other: Select this option if you hold a different type of sustainability certification.
* **Date Certified** (Date): This field requires the date when you were certified.
* **Certification Body** (Text): Enter the name of the certification body that certified your organization.
* **Certification Number** (Text): Enter any relevant certification number assigned to your organization.
* **Sustainability Certification** (Text): Briefly describe the type of sustainability certification you hold.
* **Sustainability Certification Level** (Select Multiple): This field allows you to select one or more levels of sustainability certification your organization holds. You can choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Bronze
	+ Silver
	+ Gold
	+ Platinum
* **Sustainability Commitment** (Time): Enter the amount of time your organization has committed to sustainability.
* **Sustainability Investment** (Number): Enter the amount of money or resources invested in sustainability.
* **Sustainability Impact** (Note): This field is for any additional comments or notes about your sustainability certification.
