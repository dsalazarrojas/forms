# Item Definition For Market Analysis - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather detailed information about a product and its competitor in the market analysis process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Product Name** of the product you are analyzing in the "Product Name" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the product in the **Product Description** field.
3. Enter the price of the product in the **Product Price** field.
4. Enter the name of the competitor product in the **Competitor Name** field.
5. Provide a brief description of the competitor product in the **Competitor Description** field.
6. Enter the price of the competitor product in the **Competitor Price** field.
7. Set the **Opportunity Priority** level for the product and competitor.
8. Enter the **Product Category** of the product.
9. List the unique features of the product in the **Product Features** field.
10. Identify the target market for the product in the **Product Target Market** field.
11. Set the **Product Competitor Priority** level for the product.
12. Calculate the **Opportunity Score** for the product.
13. Calculate the **Product Score** for the product.
14. Finally, select the date of form submission from the calendar in the **Form Submission Date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the product you are analyzing.
* **Product Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the product.
* **Product Price** (`price`, number, required: false): Enter the price of the product.
* **Competitor Name** (`competitor_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the competitor product.
* **Competitor Description** (`competitor_description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the competitor product.
* **Competitor Price** (`competitor_price`, number, required: false): Enter the price of the competitor product.
* **Opportunity Priority** (`opportunity_priority`, number, required: false): Set the opportunity priority level for the product and competitor.
* **Product Category** (`product_category`, text, required: false): Enter the category of the product.
* **Product Features** (`product_features`, text, required: false): List the unique features of the product.
* **Product Target Market** (`product_target_market`, text, required: false): Identify the target market for the product.
* **Product Competitor Priority** (`product_competitor_priority`, number, required: false): Set the competitor priority level for the product.
* **Opportunity Score** (`opportunity_score`, number, required: false): Calculate the opportunity score for the product.
* **Product Score** (`product_score`, number, required: false): Calculate the product score for the product.
* **Form Submission Date** (`form_submission_date`, date, required: false): Select the date of form submission from the calendar. 

Note: All fields are optional unless otherwise specified in the field label.
