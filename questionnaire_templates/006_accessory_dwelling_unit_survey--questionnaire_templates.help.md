# Accessory Dwelling Unit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This is a survey designed to gather information about your experience building or converting an Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) on your property. Your input will help us better understand the ADU development process and provide insights for future projects.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure you have all necessary information about your ADU handy, such as its location, type, size, and usage.
2. Read each field carefully and fill it in accordingly.
3. If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this guide for clarification.
4. Answer all fields truthfully, as they are all required unless otherwise indicated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Address** (`property_address`, `text`, required): Enter the full street address where the ADU is located.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where the ADU is located.
* **State or Province** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter the state or province where the ADU is located.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter the ZIP or postal code of the ADU's location.
* **Primary Property Type** (`property_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of primary property on which the ADU is located from the options: Single-family home, Duplex, Townhouse, Multi-family property, or Other.
* **Type of Accessory Dwelling Unit** (`adu_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of ADU from the options: Detached (separate structure), Attached (addition to main home), Converted garage, Basement conversion, Above-garage unit, or Internal conversion.
* **Approximate Square Footage of ADU** (`adu_square_footage`, `number`, required): Enter the approximate square footage of the ADU in square feet.
* **Number of Bedrooms in ADU** (`number_of_bedrooms`, `number`, required): Enter the number of bedrooms in the ADU.
* **Year ADU Was Built or Converted** (`year_built_or_converted`, `number`, optional): Enter the year the ADU was built or converted, if applicable.
* **Current Use of ADU** (`adu_current_use`, `select_one`, required): Choose the current use of the ADU from the options: Long-term rental, Short-term rental (Airbnb, VRBO, etc.), Family member housing, Home office or studio, Vacant or unused, or Other.
* **Monthly Rental Income (if applicable)** (`monthly_rental_income`, `number`, optional): Enter the monthly rental income, if applicable. If not rented, enter 0.
* **Challenges and Feedback** (`section_challenges`, `note`, optional): Share any challenges you faced during the ADU development process and provide any additional feedback.
* **How Difficult Was the Permitting Process** (`permitting_difficulty`, `select_one`, required): Choose how difficult the permitting process was from the options: Very easy, Somewhat easy, Neutral, Somewhat difficult, Very difficult, Did not go through permitting.
* **Challenges Faced During ADU Development** (`challenges_faced`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all the challenges you faced during the ADU development process from the options: Zoning restrictions, High construction costs, Financing difficulties, Permitting delays, Neighbor opposition, Utility connection issues, or None.
* **Approximate Total Construction or Conversion Cost** (`total_construction_cost`, `number`, optional): Enter the approximate total construction or conversion cost in dollars.
* **Would You Build or Convert an ADU Again** (`would_build_again`, `select_one`, required): Choose your willingness to build or convert an ADU again from the options: Definitely yes, Probably yes, Not sure, Probably not, Definitely not.
* **Additional Comments About Your ADU Experience** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional insights or feedback about your ADU experience.

## Tips

* Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for clarification.
* Your input will help shape future ADU development efforts and improve the lives of others who may follow in your footsteps.
* Thank you for participating in this survey!
