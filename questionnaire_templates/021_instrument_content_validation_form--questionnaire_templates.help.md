By filling out this form, you will be able to review and validate instruments, which is crucial for maintaining the quality of our database.

## Purpose
The Instrument Content Validation Form is used to validate and document the content of instruments in our system. This form helps to ensure consistency and accuracy in the rating and validation processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the instrument in the "instrument_name" field.
2. Choose the rating type from the "rating_type" select field. This field is not required, but if you choose to rate, this will determine the type of rating you are using. Options are:
	* Option 1
	* Expert
	* Researcher
	* Other
3. Select any instrument types that apply to the instrument from the "instrument_type" field. You can select multiple types if necessary.
	* Survey
	* Test
	* Scale
4. If you want to specify any scale type for the instrument, enter the details in the "scale_type" field.
5. If you want to rate the instrument, enter the "rating_score" value.
6. If you've chosen "Other" as the rating type and need to specify a custom instrument name, enter it in the "instrument_name_other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **instrument_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the instrument you are validating.
* **Rating Type** (select_one, optional): Choose the type of rating you are using. This will help us understand the context of the rating.
* **Instrument Type** (select_multiple, optional): Select any type of instrument that applies to the instrument you are validating. You can choose multiple types if necessary.
* **Scale Type** (text, optional): Enter any scale type for the instrument if applicable.
* **Rating Score** (number, optional): Enter the rating score for the instrument if you are rating.
* **Instrument Name Other** (text, optional): Enter a custom name for the instrument if you chose "Other" as the rating type.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and consistent information for the instrument in all fields.
* Ensure that the rating type you choose matches the type of rating you are using.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can contact our support team for assistance.
