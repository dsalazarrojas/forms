# AI Diagnostic Reliability Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the reliability and performance of AI diagnostic models in a clinical setting. It collects information from patients, the AI model, the clinician's assessment, and metrics on the AI's output.
## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the patient's full name in the **Full name** field.
2.  Enter the patient's date of birth in the **Date of birth** field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3.  Enter the patient's age in years in the **Age** field.
4.  Enter the patient's phone number in the **Phone number** field, including the country code if not from the same region.
5.  Enter the patient's email address in the **Email address** field, using a format that allows for easy contact (e.g., hospital or research contact).
6.  Enter the date of the visit in the **Date of visit** field.
7.  Enter the AI model name and version or commit tag in the **AI model name / version** field.
8.  Select the **Input modality** used by the model from the options provided.
9.  Enter the AI's suggested diagnosis in the **AI-suggested diagnosis (short)** field.
10.  Enter the AI's confidence score as a value between 0.0 and 1.0 in the **AI confidence score (0.0-1.0)** field.
11.  List any automated flags or warnings generated by the model in the **Automated flags or warnings** field, leaving blank if none.
12.  Select whether the clinician agrees with the AI's suggested diagnosis from the options provided.
13.  Enter the clinician's confidence in the final decision as a value between 0 and 10 in the **Clinician confidence in final decision (0-10)** field.
14.  Select the recommended next steps from the options provided.
15.  Indicate whether a confirmatory test was performed in the **Confirmatory test performed?** field.
16.  Enter the outcome within 30 days as selected from the options provided.
17.  Enter the time from data submission to AI result in seconds in the **Time from data submission to AI result (seconds)** field.
18.  Describe any discrepancies between the AI and clinician in the **Describe any discrepancy between AI and clinician** field.
19.  Select the overall reliability of the AI diagnostic model from the options provided.
20.  Enter any additional comments or notes in the **Additional comments or notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Enter the patient's full name for identification purposes.
* **Date of birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format to provide accurate demographic information.
* **Age**: Enter the patient's age in years for demographic analysis.
* **Phone number**: Enter the patient's phone number, including the country code if outside the region, for effective communication.
* **Email address**: Enter the patient's email address using a format that allows for easy contact by hospital or research staff.
* **Date of visit**: Enter the date of the visit when the diagnosis was made.
* **AI model name / version**: Enter the name and version or commit tag of the AI model used to make the diagnosis.
* **Input modality**: Select the primary data type used by the model from the options provided (e.g., radiology images, clinical notes, lab results, etc.).
* **AI-suggested diagnosis (short)**: Enter the AI's suggested diagnosis in concise clinical terms.
* **AI confidence score (0.0-1.0)**: Enter the AI's confidence score as a value between 0.0 and 1.0.
* **Automated flags or warnings**: List any automated flags or warnings generated by the model, leaving blank if none.
* **Clinician agrees with AI-suggested diagnosis**: Select whether the clinician agrees with the AI's suggested diagnosis from the options provided.
* **Clinician confidence in final decision (0-10)**: Enter the clinician's confidence in the final decision as a value between 0 and 10.
* **Recommended next steps**: Select the recommended next steps from the options provided.
* **Confirmatory test performed**: Indicate whether a confirmatory test was performed in relation to the diagnosis.
* **Outcome within 30 days**: Select the outcome within 30 days as selected from the options provided.
* **Time from data submission to AI result (seconds)**: Enter the time from data submission to AI result in seconds for performance analysis.
* **Describe any discrepancy between AI and clinician**: Describe any discrepancies between the AI and clinician in this field.
* **Overall AI diagnostic reliability**: Select the overall reliability of the AI diagnostic model from the options provided.

## Tips

* Be as accurate as possible when entering information.
* If unsure about any field, please consult with the relevant clinician or researcher.
* This form is designed to help improve the reliability and performance of AI diagnostic models; your input is valuable for this purpose.
