# # Karnes-County Grand Jury District Court Juror Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic and availability information from potential jurors in the Karnes County Grand Jury District Court. The information gathered will help the court select suitable jurors for trials.

## How to Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Start with the introduction page, which provides an overview of the questionnaire and its purpose.
2. Proceed to the contact information page, where you can enter your contact details.
3. Move on to the personal information page, where you can select your personal attributes.
4. Next, you will answer questions about your family and employment.
5. On the juror expertise page, indicate your areas of expertise.
6. Select your juror status and availability.
7. Answer the juror questions, which will help the court assess your suitability for trials.
8. Finally, review and submit the form.
