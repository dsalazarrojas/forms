# Doctor Feedback New Medication Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for healthcare professionals to provide feedback on a new medication. It helps to gather information on the medication's efficacy, dosing frequency, route of administration, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide the name of the new medication in the "Medication" field.
4. Specify the dosing frequency in the "Dosing Frequency" field.
5. Select the route of administration for the medication in the "Dosing Route" field.
6. Enter the date the medication was started and stopped (if applicable) in the "Dosing Start Date" and "Dosing Stop Date" fields, respectively.
7. Select the type(s) of feedback for the medication in the "Feedback" field.
8. Indicate whether follow-up is required in the "Followup Required" field.
9. Select the follow-up schedule(s) in the "Followup Schedules" field.
10. Enter the date and time for the follow-up appointment in the "Followup Date" and "Followup Time" fields, respectively.
11. Specify the follow-up location in the "Followup Location" field.
12. Select the outcomes(s) of the medication in the "Outcomes" field.
13. Choose the method of tracking outcomes in the "Outcomes Tracking" field.
14. Select the comparative satisfaction level(s) for the medication in the "Comparative Satisfaction" field.
15. Choose the method of tracking comparative satisfaction in the "Comparative Satisfaction Tracking" field.
16. Select the comparative outcomes(s) of the medication in the "Comparative Outcomes" field.
17. Choose the method of tracking comparative outcomes in the "Comparative Outcomes Tracking" field.
18. Select the comparative outcomes frequency in the "Comparative Outcomes Frequency" field.
19. Enter the duration and stop date for the comparative outcomes in the "Comparative Outcomes Duration" and "Comparative Outcomes Stop Date" fields, respectively.
20. Select the follow-up frequency in the "Followup Frequency" field.
21. Choose the follow-up status in the "Followup Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, required: false): Enter your first name to help us identify you.
* **Email** (Email, email, required: false): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Medication** (Medication, text, required: false): Provide the name of the new medication.
* **Dosing Frequency** (Dosing Frequency, text, required: false): Describe the dosing frequency of the medication.
* **Dosing Route** (Dosing Route, select_one, required: false): Select the route of administration for the medication.
* **Dosing Start Date** (Dosing Start Date, date, required: false): Enter the date the medication was started.
* **Dosing Stop Date** (Dosing Stop Date, date, required: false): Enter the date the medication was stopped (if applicable).
* **Feedback** (Feedback, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type(s) of feedback for the medication.
* **Followup Required** (Followup Required, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether follow-up is required.
* **Followup Schedules** (Followup Schedules, select_multiple, required: false): Select the follow-up schedule(s) for the medication.
* **Followup Date** (Followup Date, date, required: false): Enter the date for the follow-up appointment.
* **Followup Time** (Followup Time, time, required: false): Enter the time for the follow-up appointment.
* **Followup Location** (Followup Location, text, required: false): Specify the location for the follow-up appointment.
* **Outcomes** (Outcomes, select_multiple, required: false): Select the outcome(s) of the medication.
* **Outcomes Tracking** (Outcomes Tracking, select_one, required: false): Choose the method of tracking outcomes.
* **Comparative Satisfaction** (Comparative Satisfaction, select_multiple, required: false): Select the comparative satisfaction level(s) for the medication.
* **Comparative Satisfaction Tracking** (Comparative Satisfaction Tracking, select_one, required: false): Choose the method of tracking comparative satisfaction.
* **Comparative Outcomes** (Comparative Outcomes, select_multiple, required: false): Select the comparative outcome(s) of the medication.
* **Comparative Outcomes Tracking** (Comparative Outcomes Tracking, select_one, required: false): Choose the method of tracking comparative outcomes.
* **Comparative Outcomes Frequency** (Comparative Outcomes Frequency, select_multiple, required: false): Select the comparative outcomes frequency.
* **Comparative Outcomes Duration** (Comparative Outcomes Duration, number, required: false): Enter the duration for the comparative outcomes.
* **Comparative Outcomes Stop Date** (Comparative Outcomes Stop Date, date, required: false): Enter the stop date for the comparative outcomes.
* **Followup Frequency** (Followup Frequency, number, required: false): Select the follow-up frequency.
* **Followup Status** (Followup Status, select_one, required: false): Choose the follow-up status.

**Tips**
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Use the options provided in each field to ensure accurate and complete information.
- If you are unsure about any field, ask your healthcare professional for guidance.
- This form is intended to gather information for the benefit of your healthcare. Please provide clear and accurate answers.
