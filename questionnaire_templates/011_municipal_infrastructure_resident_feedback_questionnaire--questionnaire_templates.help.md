# municipal_infrastructure_resident_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from residents on the condition and quality of municipal infrastructure in their neighborhood, including services provided, accessibility, and overall satisfaction. It is meant to help city planners and decision-makers understand resident perceptions and opinions on the state of their community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Read through the form carefully and answer each question truthfully to the best of your abilities.
2.  Answer all questions that apply to you. If a question does not apply, mark the "Not Applicable" option or leave it blank.
3.  Use the options provided for each question or write your own answer in the "Other" field if necessary.
4.  Be as detailed as possible in your answers to provide clear and accurate feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction (Page 1)
*   **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This is a note field to provide a brief introduction to the form and its purpose. Please do not answer this question as it is a placeholder.

### Infrastructure Condition (Page 1)
*   **Infrastructure Condition** (`infrastructure_condition`, `text`, required: false): Describe the overall condition of the infrastructure in your neighborhood, such as the state of roads, parks, and public buildings.

### Services Accessibility (Page 1)
*   **Services Accessibility** (`services_accessibility`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all services that are accessible to you in your neighborhood. Options are:
    *   Yes
    *   No

### Neighborhood Quality (Page 1)
*   **Neighborhood Quality** (`neighborhood_quality`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the overall quality of your neighborhood. Options are:
    *   Good
    *   Poor

### Resident Satisfaction (Page 1)
*   **Resident Satisfaction** (`resident_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): How satisfied are you with the services and infrastructure in your neighborhood? Options are:
    *   Satisfied
    *   Dissatisfied

### Services Quality (Page 1)
*   **Services Quality** (`services_quality`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all services that are of good quality to you. Options are:
    *   Good
    *   Poor

### Neighborhood Safety (Page 1)
*   **Neighborhood Safety** (`neighborhood_safety`, `select_one`, required: false): Do you feel safe in your neighborhood? Options are:
    *   Yes
    *   No

### Services Availability (Page 1)
*   **Services Availability** (`services_availability`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all services that are available to you in your neighborhood. Options are:
    *   Yes
    *   No

### Resident Satisfaction Awareness (Page 1)
*   **Resident Satisfaction Awareness** (`resident_satisfaction_awareness`, `select_one`, required: false): How aware are you of the services and infrastructure in your neighborhood that affect your satisfaction? Options are:
    *   Satisfied
    *   Dissatisfied

### Infrastructure Services Priority (Page 2)
*   **Infrastructure Services Priority** (`infrastructure_services_priority`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all infrastructure services that have the highest priority to you. Options are:
    *   Yes
    *   No

### Resident Satisfaction Impact (Page 2)
*   **Resident Satisfaction Impact** (`resident_satisfaction_impact`, `select_one`, required: false): How has your satisfaction with the services and infrastructure in your neighborhood impacted your daily life? Options are:
    *   Satisfied
    *   Dissatisfied

## Tips
*   Please answer all questions that apply to you and be as detailed as possible in your answers.
*   If a question does not apply, mark the "Not Applicable" option or leave it blank.
*   Use the "Other" field to provide additional information if necessary.
