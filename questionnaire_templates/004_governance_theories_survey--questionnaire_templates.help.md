# Governance Theories Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Governance Theories Survey is a tool used to understand the application and impact of governance theories in a given context. This form helps users evaluate the effectiveness and relevance of various governance theories in their organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and select the best response based on your understanding of the governance theories.
2. For multiple-choice questions, choose one of the available options that best reflects your answer.
3. For open-ended questions, provide a brief description of your thoughts and experiences.
4. Indicate the completion time for the survey by providing a number of minutes taken to complete it.
5. Finally, submit the survey to provide your input.
