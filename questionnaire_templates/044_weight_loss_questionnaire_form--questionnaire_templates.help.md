# weight_loss_questionnaire_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from patients about their weight loss goals and medical history. The form helps healthcare providers understand the patient's motivation and expectations for weight loss, as well as any potential medical conditions that may affect their weight loss journey.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter patient information in the "Patient Info" section.
2. Answer the questions in the "Medical History" section about your medical conditions and medications.
3. Share your weight loss goals and expectations in the "Weight Loss Goals" section.
4. Record your height and weight in the "Height" and "Weight" sections.
5. Provide details about your weight loss goals and expectations in the "Weight Loss Goals Details" section.
6. Report any relevant medical conditions you have in the "Medical Conditions" section.
7. List any medications you are currently taking in the "Medications" section.
8. Describe your diet and exercise habits in the "Diet" and "Exercise" sections.
9. Share any supplement information in the "Supplements" section.
10. Indicate whether you are under medical supervision for weight loss in the "Medical Supervision" section.
11. List any weight loss programs you are currently enrolled in in the "Weight Loss Programs" section.
12. Describe your expectations for weight loss in the "Weight Loss Expectations" section.
13. Report your current weight loss progress in the "Weight Loss Progress" section.
14. Indicate if you have received any weight loss results in the "Weight Loss Results" section.
15. Add any comments from your doctor in the "Doctor Comments" section.
16. Share any comments you have as a patient in the "Patient Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info**: Enter your name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Medical History**: Check yes or no to indicate if you have any medical conditions that may affect your weight loss journey.
* **Weight Loss Goals**: Describe your motivation for weight loss and what you hope to achieve.
* **Height**: Record your current height.
* **Weight**: Record your current weight.
* **Weight Loss Goals Details**: Provide more information about your weight loss goals and expectations.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions that may impact your weight loss journey.
* **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Diet**: Describe your typical diet.
* **Exercise**: Describe your exercise habits.
* **Supplements**: List any supplements you are currently taking.
* **Medical Supervision**: Indicate if you are under medical supervision for weight loss.
* **Weight Loss Programs**: List any weight loss programs you are enrolled in.
* **Weight Loss Expectations**: Describe your expectations for weight loss.
* **Weight Loss Progress**: Report your current weight loss progress.
* **Weight Loss Results**: Indicate if you have received any weight loss results.
* **Doctor Comments**: Add any comments from your doctor.
* **Patient Comments**: Share any comments you have as a patient.
