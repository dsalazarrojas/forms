# Bridesmaid Measurement Collection - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Bridesmaid Measurement Collection" form is designed to collect vital measurements and information from bridesmaids to ensure a perfect fit for their wedding dresses. This form helps bridesmaids provide accurate measurements, preferences, and contact information to aid in dress design and fitting processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, email address, phone number, and the bride's name.
2. Select your role in the bridal party (Maid of Honor, Bridesmaid, Junior Bridesmaid, Matron of Honor, or Other).
3. Provide your standard dress size and measurements (Bust, Waist, Hip, Shoulder Width, Sleeve Length, Inseam Length, Height, Shoe Size).
4. Specify your height and shoe size if applicable.
5. Choose your dress length preference (Floor length, Tea length, Knee length, Mini, As designed).
6. Select your fit preference (Very Fitted, Fitted, Semi-fitted, Loose and comfortable, Flexible based on design).
7. Answer recent weight changes questions.
8. Optionally provide any fitting concerns or notes.
9. Choose a date when these measurements were taken.
10. Indicate who took these measurements.
11. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Bridesmaid Name** (1): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (2): Enter your email address, which will be used for dress delivery confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (3): Enter your contact number for any dress-related communication.
* **Bride Name** (4): Enter the bride's name whose wedding you are attending.
* **Role in Bridal Party** (5): Choose your role in the bridal party (Maid of honor, Bridesmaid, Junior Bridesmaid, Matron of honor, or Other).
* **Measurements Instructions** (6): This is an explanatory note about how to take your measurements accurately.
* **Standard Dress Size** (7): Select your typical dress size.
* **Bust Measurement** (8): Measure around your bust in inches.
* **Waist Measurement** (9): Measure around your natural waistline in inches.
* **Hip Measurement** (10): Measure around your hips in inches.
* **Shoulder Width** (11): Measure from shoulder to shoulder in inches (optional).
* **Sleeve Length** (12): Measure from shoulder to wrist in inches (optional).
* **Inseam Length** (13): Specify the desired dress length in inches (optional).
* **Height** (14): Enter your height in feet and inches.
* **Shoe Size** (15): Enter your shoe size.
* **Arm Length** (16): Measure from shoulder to fingertips for sleeve fit (optional).
* **Torso Length** (17): Measure from shoulder to natural waistline for torso fit (optional).
* **Dress Length Preference** (18): Choose your dress length preference (Floor length, Tea length, Knee length, Mini, As designed).
* **Fit Preference** (19): Select your fit preference (Very fitted, Fitted, Semi-fitted, Loose and comfortable, Flexible based on design).
* **Recent Weight Changes** (20): Answer the question about recent weight changes.
* **Expected Weight Changes** (21): Anticipate any weight changes before the wedding.
* **Alteration Notes** (22): Provide any alteration concerns or notes.
* **Measurement Date** (23): Enter the date when these measurements were taken.
* **Who Took Measurements** (24): Indicate who took these measurements.

Please submit the form accurately and completely to ensure the best possible fit for your wedding dress.
