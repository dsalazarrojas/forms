# Language School Curriculum Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from users interested in enrolling in a language school. The goal is to collect relevant data to provide users with the best possible language school experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the User Input page with any relevant details about your language school inquiry.
* Select the language you are interested in learning from the dropdown menu.
* Choose your current level of proficiency in the selected language.
* Select your preferred method of contact, such as call, email, or text.
* Provide any additional notes or comments in the text box, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, `note`, not required): This is the main section of the form where you can provide any additional information about your language school inquiry.
* **What Language School** (`language_school`, `select_one`, not required): This field allows you to select the language you are interested in learning. Please choose from the dropdown menu: Spanish, English, French, German, or Chinese.
* **Curriculum Level** (`curriculum_level`, `select_one`, not required): Select your current level of proficiency in the language you have chosen. Options are: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose your preferred method of contact, such as call, email, or text.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, not required): Provide any additional information or comments about your language school inquiry.
