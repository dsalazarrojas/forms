# Learning And Memory Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form, called "Learning And Memory Assessment," is designed to collect information from individuals for the purpose of assessing their learning and memory abilities. It is used in a clinical or research setting to gather data on cognitive functions such as memory, learning, and other related aspects. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the background information section, if applicable.
2. Rate your self-assessment scale (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor) and provide a brief description of your self-assessment in the text field.
3. Provide your consent, indicating that you understand the purpose and usage of the collected data.
4. Fill out the demographic information:
   - Date of birth.
   - Time of birth.
   - Phone number.
   - Email.
   - Address.
   - Age.
   - Gender.
   - Height.
   - Weight.
5. If necessary, provide any medical history, emergency contact information, medical provider information, and any medical conditions or medications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Background Information** (`background_information`, `note`, required: false): This section is for you to provide any additional information that you think is relevant to your assessment. 
* **Self Rating Scale** (`self_rating_scale`, `select_multiple`, required: true): This is a self-assessment scale where you rate yourself as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor in terms of your memory and learning abilities.
* **Self Rating Description** (`self_rating_description`, `note`, required: false): Provide a brief description of why you chose your self-assessment rating.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `note`, required: true): This is your confirmation that you understand the purpose and usage of the collected data.
* **Background Information 2** (`background_information2`, `note`, required: false): Similar to the background information section, but you can add more details if needed.
* **Self Rating Scale 2** (`self_rating_scale2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Another self-assessment scale, just like the first one.
* **Self Rating Description 2** (`self_rating_description2`, `note`, required: false): Another description for your self-assessment rating.
* **Consent 2** (`consent2`, `note`, required: false): A duplicate of the consent section, to confirm your understanding.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: true): Please enter your date of birth in the format of month, day, year.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, `time`, required: true): Enter your time of birth.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Provide your phone number, if you wish to do so.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: true): Enter your email address.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: true): Enter your current address.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required: true): Enter your age.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose your gender as Male, Female, or Other.
* **Height** (`height`, `number`, required: true): Enter your height in inches.
* **Weight** (`weight`, `number`, required: true): Enter your weight in pounds.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): If you have any medical conditions or allergies, provide details in this section.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false): If you have an emergency contact person, provide their name.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required: false): The name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): The phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, `text`, required: false): The name of your medical provider.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, required: false): If you have any medical condition, provide details in this section.
* **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, required: false): If you take any medications, provide details in this section.
* **Medical Provider Phone** (`medical_provider_phone`, `text`, required: false): The phone number of your medical provider.
