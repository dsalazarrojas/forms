# Personal Clarity Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help you assess your personal clarity on various aspects of your life. It's a self-explanatory form that will guide you through a series of questions to help you gain a better understanding of your priorities and values.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question in the space provided.
2. Answer questions based on your current state of mind and circumstances.
3. You can skip questions that don't apply to you.
4. Please provide clear and concise answers for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is just a space to let you take a moment to understand the purpose of this assessment.
* **Current Values**: Describe your current values and what's important to you.
* **Life Direction**: On a scale of 1-10, rate how clear you feel about your life direction.
* **Life Obstacles**: Check all that apply:
	+ **Yes** if you currently have obstacles in your life.
	+ **No** if you don't currently have obstacles.
* **Obstacles Description**: Describe any obstacles you're currently facing.
* **Goals**: Check all that apply:
	+ **Yes** if you have goals in your life.
	+ **No** if you don't have goals.
* **Values Description**: Describe what's most important to you.
* **Obstacles Frequency**: On a scale of 1-10, rate how often you encounter obstacles in your life.
* **Goals Description**: Describe your goals.
* **Current Life Satisfaction**: On a scale of 1-10, rate how satisfied you are with your life right now.
* **Self-Reflection**: Write a few sentences about your thoughts on your current situation.
* **Obstacles Impact**: On a scale of 1-10, rate how impactful obstacles are in your life.
* **Goals Importance**: On a scale of 1-10, rate how important your goals are to you.
* **Obstacles Priority**: On a scale of 1-10, rate how high a priority obstacles are to you.
* **Goals Status**: Check the status of your goals:
	+ **Active** if they're currently being worked on.
	+ **Inactive** if they're not currently being worked on.
* **Obstacles Status**: Check the status of your obstacles:
	+ **Active** if they're currently being worked on.
	+ **Inactive** if they're not currently being worked on.
* **Life Focus**: On a scale of 1-10, rate how focused you are on your life goals.
* **Life Balance**: On a scale of 1-10, rate how balanced you feel in your life.

This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Personal Clarity Assessment form. Please let me know if you have any questions or need further clarification on any of the questions.
