# ICG 1 1 Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ICG 1 1 Form is a questionnaire designed to collect information about one-to-one meetings, calls, or meetings with calls. This form will help users provide essential details about the type of meeting, location, participants, and duration to facilitate smooth and effective communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **name** of the person involved in the meeting, call, or meeting with call.
2. Provide your **email** address to facilitate communication.
3. Enter your **phone number** for easy contact.
4. Select the **type** of meeting or call from the options provided (Meeting, Call, or Meeting with Call).
5. If applicable, choose the **location** of the meeting (Online or Offline).
6. Set the **start time** and **end time** of the meeting or call. Note that these fields are optional.
7. Specify the **duration** of the meeting or call in minutes.
8. Select all participants involved in the meeting, including yourself (User 1, User 2, etc.).
9. If applicable, choose the **meeting location** (Location 1, Location 2, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter the name of the person involved in the meeting, call, or meeting with call. This field is required.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to facilitate communication. This field is required and helps us reach you efficiently.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for easy contact. This field is required.
* **One-to-One Meeting Type**: Select the type of meeting or call (Meeting, Call, or Meeting with Call). This field is required and helps us understand the nature of the meeting or call.
* **Location**: Choose the location of the meeting (Online or Offline). Note that this field is optional.
* **Start Time**: If applicable, enter the start time of the meeting or call. Note that this field is optional.
* **End Time**: If applicable, enter the end time of the meeting or call. Note that this field is optional.
* **Duration**: Specify the duration of the meeting or call in minutes. This field is required.
* **Meeting Participants**: Select all participants involved in the meeting, including yourself (User 1, User 2, etc.). This field is required.
* **Meeting Location**: Choose the meeting location (Location 1, Location 2, etc.). Note that this field is optional.
