# Ebook Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ebook Knowledge Quiz form is designed to assess your knowledge on various ebook-related topics. You will be presented with questions and asked to select the relevant topic and provide your rating and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill in your name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
2. **Enter your email**: Please provide a valid email address that you have access to.
3. **Select the relevant topic**: Choose the topic that best applies to your quiz.
4. **Rate your experience**: Rate your experience with the topic, on a scale that will be provided (e.g., 1-5).
5. **Provide feedback**: Share any comments or suggestions you have about the quiz.
6. **Submit your response**: Select a date and time to submit your response for tracking purposes.
7. **Optional fields**:
   - **Reader Email**: If you'd like to receive email updates or be contacted about your responses, please enter your email address.
   - **Reader Phone**: If you'd like to be contacted via phone, please enter your phone number.
   - **Reader Comment**: Share any comments or additional information you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reader Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official identification. (Required)
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you have access to. (Required)
* **Topic Selection**: Choose the topic that best applies to the quiz question. (Required)
* **Rating**: Rate your experience with the topic, on the provided scale. (Required)
* **Feedback**: Share any comments or suggestions you have about the quiz. (Optional)
* **Submit**: Select a date and time to submit your response for tracking purposes. (Optional)
* **Reader Email**: If you'd like to be contacted via email, please enter your email address. (Optional)
* **Reader Phone**: If you'd like to be contacted via phone, please enter your phone number. (Optional)
* **Reader Comment**: Share any additional comments or information about the quiz. (Optional)
* **Topic 1**: Choose the topic that best applies to the quiz question. (Optional)
* **Submit Time**: Select the time you'd like to submit your response. (Optional)
* **Submit Date**: Select the date you'd like to submit your response. (Optional)
