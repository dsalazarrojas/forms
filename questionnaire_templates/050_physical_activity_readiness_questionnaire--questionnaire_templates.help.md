# Physical Activity Readiness Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physical Activity Readiness Questionnaire is a form designed to gather information about an individual's physical activity level, available time, and preferred contact method. This information helps schedule appointments, consultations, or coaching sessions that are tailored to the individual's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your contact details, including your name and preferred contact method (phone, email, chat, or in-person).
2.  Select the highest level of physical activity you are currently engaging in (walking, running, swimming, etc.).
3.  Indicate the number of days per week you are available to schedule sessions.
4.  Choose the preferred time of day for these sessions (morning, afternoon, evening).
5.  Choose your preferred frequency of contact (daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, or annually).
6.  Provide any additional notes that may be helpful for planning your sessions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Details** (Contact Details, text, required: false): Enter your name and other contact information.
- **Activity Level** (Activity Level, select_one, required: false): Select the highest level of physical activity you are currently engaging in (e.g., walking, running, swimming, etc.).
- **Available Days** (Available Days, number, required: false): Indicate the number of days per week you are available to schedule sessions.
- **Available Time** (Available Time, number, required: false): Input the number of hours per day you are available for sessions.
- **Preferred Time** (Preferred Time, select_multiple, required: false): Choose one or more time slots that you prefer for sessions (morning, afternoon, evening).
- **Available Days Per Week** (Available Days Per Week, number, required: false): Enter the number of days per week you can dedicate to sessions.
- **Preferred Day** (Preferred Day, select_one, required: false): Select a specific day of the week when you prefer to schedule sessions.
- **Contact Method** (Contact Method, select_one, required: false): Choose how you prefer to be contacted (phone, email, chat, in-person).
- **Contact Frequency** (Contact Frequency, select_one, required: false): Select how often you would like to be contacted (daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, annually).
- **Notes** (Notes, note, required: false): Provide any additional information that may be helpful for planning your sessions.
