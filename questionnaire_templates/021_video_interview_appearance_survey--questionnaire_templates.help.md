# Video Interview Appearance Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on the appearance of the video interview. It is intended to assess how well the interviewer and the environment were presented, including factors such as confidence, communication, and professionalism. The form aims to gather information on the candidate's overall experience with the video interview.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the instructions on this page and ensure you have a clear understanding of the purpose and requirements of this form.
2. Select the appropriate answer choices for each field, taking into account the candidate's actual experience with the video interview.
3. Fill out the form thoroughly and accurately, providing as much information as possible.
4. Submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: Provide a brief introduction to the form. This section should give an overview of the form and explain its purpose.
* **Evaluator Rating**: Rate the candidate's appearance using the provided scale (1-5). Choose the number that best represents your opinion of their appearance.
* **Appearance**: Choose all that apply from the provided options to describe the candidate's appearance during the video interview.
* **Confidence**: Rate the candidate's confidence during the video interview using the provided scale (1-5). Choose the number that best represents your opinion of their confidence.
* **Communication**: Provide a brief description of the candidate's communication skills during the video interview. This can include their ability to articulate their thoughts and ideas.
* **Professionalism**: Rate the candidate's professionalism during the video interview using the provided scale (High, Average, Low). Choose the option that best represents your opinion of their professionalism.
* **Overall Rating**: Rate the candidate's overall appearance using the provided scale (1-5). Choose the number that best represents your opinion of their overall appearance.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the candidate's appearance.
* **Email**: This field is not intended for candidate feedback, but rather for contact information for follow-up communication.
* **Phone**: This field is not intended for candidate feedback, but rather for follow-up communication.
* **Follow Up**: Check "True" if you want to follow up with the candidate at a later time. This is not intended for rating or feedback.
* **Submission Date/Time**: Enter the date and time you submitted this form.
* **Follow Up Date/Time**: Enter the date and time you plan to follow up with the candidate.
* **Follow Up Status**: Check "Done" if you have completed the follow-up with the candidate. If not, choose "In Progress" or "Not Started".
* **Follow Up Next Call**: Enter the date you plan to follow up with the candidate next.
* **Follow Up Next Call Time**: Enter the time you plan to follow up with the candidate next.
* **Follow Up Notes**: Provide any additional notes or information about the follow-up with the candidate.

## Tips

* Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Use the provided scales and options to rate and describe the candidate's appearance.
* Ensure you have all necessary contact information (email and phone) to follow up with the candidate.
* Be sure to follow up with the candidate at the planned date and time.
