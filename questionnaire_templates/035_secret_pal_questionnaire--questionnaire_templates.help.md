# Secret Pal Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Secret Pal Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information from participants in a gift-giving context. It gathers details about the participant, their gift preferences, and other relevant information to help the gift-giver (Secret Pal) make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide participant information, including their name, email, and phone number in the respective fields.
2. Answer the questions regarding gift information, such as gift details, possibilities, and choices.
3. Share any additional information or message with the gift-giver.
4. Optionally, provide a gift budget and value to guide the gift-giver's selection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**:
	+ Participant Information: Enter the participant's name.
	+ Participant Information Form: Enter any additional information about the participant.
* **Gift Information**:
	+ Gift Details: Enter details about the gift, such as its description.
	+ Gift Possibilities: Enter any possible gift ideas.
	+ Gift Choices: Enter your preferred gift options.
* **Secret Pal Information**:
	+ Secret Pal Information: Enter any information about the Secret Pal.
* **Communication**:
	+ Message: Add any additional message for the gift-giver.
* **Additional Contact Information**:
	+ Participant Email: Enter the participant's email address.
	+ Participant Phone: Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Budget and Value**:
	+ Gift Budget: Enter a budget for the gift, e.g., 10-20 dollars.
	+ Gift Value: Enter the value of the gift.
* **Preferences**:
	+ Gift Wishes: Enter any specific gift wishes or preferences.

Note: Some fields are not required, but providing additional information will help the gift-giver make a more informed decision.
