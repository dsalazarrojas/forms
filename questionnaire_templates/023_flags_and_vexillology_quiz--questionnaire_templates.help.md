# flags_and_vexillology_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about flags and vexillology from participants for research or educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your flag question.
2. Answer the vexillology quiz questions.
3. Indicate whether you participated in a Class, Club, or Trivia Night event.
4. Enter the number of participants in your event.
5. Specify your age range by entering the start and end years of your age range.
6. Select your country of origin.
7. Describe the shape of the flag that interests you.
8. Describe the symbol of the flag that interests you.
9. Describe the position of the flag that interests you.
10. Select the type of vexillology quiz you are interested in.
11. Provide additional information about the quiz.
12. Select the country or countries whose flag(s) interest you.
13. If you selected a country in the previous question, enter additional details about the flag of interest.
14. If you have any additional flags of interest, enter their descriptions here.
15. Enter your gender.
16. Enter your participant name.
17. Enter your organisation (if applicable).
18. Enter your email address.
19. Enter your phone number.
20. Enter the name of the organisation that conducted the quiz.
21. If you have any additional information about the quiz, enter it here.
