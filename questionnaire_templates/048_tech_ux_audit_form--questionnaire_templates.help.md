# Tech UX Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tech UX Audit Form is a questionnaire designed to evaluate your experience with a specific product or service. Your input will help us identify areas where we can improve the product's user experience, ensuring it meets industry standards and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide us with actionable feedback, follow these steps:

1. Please answer all the fields, as indicated by the "required" label in each section.
2. Ensure you're rating the overall experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
3. Be as specific as possible with your feedback and ratings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tech UX Audit Form** (`title`, `note`, required: false): This is the title of the form, which is informational only. You can leave this field blank.
* **Tech UX Audit Form** (`name`, `email`, required: false): This field is a contact information field that allows you to provide your e-mail address for any follow-up questions.
* **Overall Rating (1-5)** (`rating_1`, `type: number`, required: false): This field will ask you to rate your overall experience with the product on a scale of 1-5.
* **Overall Rating (1-5)** (`rating_2`, `type: number`, required: false): This field will ask you to rate a specific aspect of the product on the same scale.
* **Overall Rating (1-5)** (`rating_3`, `type: number`, required: false): This field will ask you to rate another specific aspect of the product on the same scale.

## Tips
* Be honest and specific with your feedback. Your input is invaluable in helping us improve the product.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Contact Information" field.
* If you've already completed a similar audit, please let us know so we can avoid duplicating effort.
