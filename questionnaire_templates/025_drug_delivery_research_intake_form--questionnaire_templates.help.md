# drug_delivery_research_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **drug delivery research intake form** is a collection of questions aimed at gathering information from researchers about their drug delivery research projects. It's essential to fill this form accurately and completely to ensure that your research can be properly processed and followed up on.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the fields as follows:

1. **name**: Enter your full name as you want it to appear on the form.
2. **department**: Select the department where your research will take place.
3. **contact_number**: Provide your contact number so we can reach out to you for further information.
4. **email**: Enter your email address where we can send updates and requests for more information.
5. **research_question**: Describe the question you're trying to answer through this research.
6. **research_area_of_interest**: Choose the area of research your project falls under.
7. **research_location**: Select all the locations where your research will take place (e.g., lab, university).
8. **follow_up**: Choose a date and time for us to follow up with you.
9. **follow_up_time**: Specify a time frame for the follow-up discussion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name (2)**: Your full name as you want it to appear on the form.
* **department (3)**: The department where your research will take place.
* **contact_number (4)**: A valid contact number where we can reach out to you for further information.
* **email (5)**: A valid email address where we can send updates and requests for more information.
* **research_question (6)**: A brief description of the question you're trying to answer through this research.
* **research_area_of_interest (7)**: The area of research your project falls under (e.g., drug delivery, drug discovery, drug development).
* **research_location (8)**: All the locations where your research will take place (e.g., lab, university).
* **follow_up (9)**: A date for us to follow up with you on your research progress.
* **follow_up_time (10)**: A time frame for the follow-up discussion.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your email address and contact number for correct formatting.
* If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to your local research coordinator for guidance.
* This form is intended for internal use within a research institution; please do not share it externally.
