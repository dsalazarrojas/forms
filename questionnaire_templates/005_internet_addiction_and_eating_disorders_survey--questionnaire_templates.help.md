# Internet Addiction And Eating Disorders Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This guide is designed to help users complete the Internet Addiction And Eating Disorders Survey form efficiently and accurately. The form is a questionnaire intended to gather responses from individuals regarding their internet usage habits and potential presence of eating disorders. It is crucial to answer questions truthfully to help researchers or healthcare professionals understand these issues better.


## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start on the first page where you will be asked about what you consider internet addiction, its frequency, and whether you have an eating disorder.
2. For each question, answer according to your true feelings or experiences, as this information is crucial for the research or assessment.
3. Move on to the demographic page to input your relevant data such as email, date of birth, or time of birth.
4.  If you have any additional comments, please make use of the note field.
5.  On the second page, you will be asked about further details and possibly more specific pages depending on your previous answers.


## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **what_is_internet_addiction** (text):
    -   This is a text field where you will be asked about what you consider internet addiction. It's best to provide a clear and concise answer to help with the assessment.
*   **internet_use** (select one):
    -   Answer honestly about how often you use the internet. The options given are: Never, Rarely, Sometimes, Often, Almost always.
*   **do_you_have_eating_disorder** (select multiple):
    -   Choose all that apply if you do have an eating disorder.
*   **demographic_data** (select multiple):
    -   Select yes if you wish to provide demographic information about yourself, which might include email, date of birth, or time of birth.
*   **email_address** (email):
    -   This is a field for your email address. Please be sure to enter a valid email address.
*   **phone_number** (text):
    -   Enter your phone number here, if you're willing to share it.
*   **date_of_birth** (date):
    -   If you're comfortable with sharing your date of birth, select the correct date.
*   **time_of_birth** (time):
    -   If you want to share your time of birth, select the appropriate time.
*   **additional_comments** (note):
    -   Use this field to add any further comments or details that are relevant to your responses.
*   **page2** (select one):
    -   Choose whether you are ready to proceed to the next page (Page 2) or not.
*   **page3** (select multiple):
    -   Depending on your previous answers, you may be directed to more pages for further information.
*   **Page 3** or **Page 4** (select multiple):
    -   If directed, select all that apply if you're willing to proceed with further questioning or have specific responses that apply to you. 

Note that some fields might not be applicable depending on your answers, making sure to only answer questions that are relevant to you. 

This guide is provided to help users understand and correctly fill out the form, ensuring accurate and valuable responses for the intended purpose, whether it's a research project or a health assessment.
