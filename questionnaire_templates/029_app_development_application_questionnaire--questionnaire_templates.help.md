## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter the working title of your app.
### 2. **Primary Contact Name** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter your name as the primary contact for this project.
### 3. **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Provide your email address for us to contact you.
### 4. **Targeted Platforms** (`platform_preference`, select_multiple, required): Choose the platforms your app will be deployed on (iOS, Android, Web, or Desktop App).
### 5. **App Category** (`app_category`, select_one, required): Select a category that best describes your app (e.g., Social Media, E-Commerce, Education, etc.).
### 6. **Describe Your Target Audience** (`target_audience_desc`, text, required): Describe the characteristics and demographics of your target audience.
### 7. **Key Features and Functions** (`core_functionality`, text, required): List the main features and functions of your app.
### 8. **Preferred Design Aesthetic** (`design_style`, select_one, optional): Select a design style that suits your app's aesthetic (e.g., Minimalist and Clean, Colorful and Vibrant, etc.).
### 9. **Required Third-Party Integrations** (`third_party_integrations`, text, optional): Specify any third-party integrations needed for your app.
### 10. **Estimated Budget Range** (`budget_estimate`, select_one, required): Choose an estimated budget range for your project.
### 11. **Desired Launch Date or Deadline** (`deadline_date`, date, optional): Provide the desired launch date or deadline for your app.
### 12. **Competitor Apps or Inspirations** (`competitor_analysis`, text, optional): Mention any competitor apps or inspirations.
### 13. **Monetization Strategy** (`monetization_plan`, select_one, required): Select a monetization strategy for your app (e.g., Free with Ads, Paid Download, etc.).
