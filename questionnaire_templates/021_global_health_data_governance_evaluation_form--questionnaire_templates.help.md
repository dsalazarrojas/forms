# global_health_data_governance_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The global health data governance evaluation form is designed to assess the current state of data governance practices and policies within an organization. This form will help identify gaps and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and ensure you understand the questions and the purpose of the form.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Provide detailed explanations for each question where necessary.
4. Complete the form thoroughly, but feel free to skip questions that do not apply to your situation.
