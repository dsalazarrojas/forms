# Consultancy Project Management Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from clients on their experience with our consultancy. We value your input and would like to hear about your experiences, successes, and challenges during the project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have finished the project with our consultancy before completing this form.
2. Fill out each section thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
4. Review your responses carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Organization Name (18)**: Please enter the name of the client organization you worked with.
* **Contact Person and Title (20)**: Please provide the name and title of the contact person at the client organization.
* **Name of Consulting Project (22)**: Enter the name of the project you worked on with our consultancy.
* **Project Completion Date (24)**: Enter the date the project was completed.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Consulting Delivery (7)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the delivery of our consultancy services on a scale of 1-10.
* **Scope Fulfillment Rating (8)**: Rate to what extent we fulfilled the original project scope.
* **Objective Achievement Rating (9)**: Rate to what extent the project's primary objectives were met.
* **Satisfaction with Quality of Final Deliverables (10)**: Rate your satisfaction with the quality of the final deliverables on a scale of 1-5.
* **Communication and Responsiveness Rating (13)**: Rate your experience with our communication and responsiveness during the project on a scale of 1-5.
* **Schedule Adherence Rating (14)**: Rate our adherence to project milestones and schedule.
* **Subject Matter Expertise Demonstration (15)**: Rate our demonstration of subject matter expertise during the project on a scale of 1-5.
* **Effectiveness in Addressing Challenges and Risks (16)**: Rate our effectiveness in addressing challenges and risks on a scale of 1-5.
* **Strongest Aspect of our Consulting Delivery (17)**: Describe the strongest aspect of our consulting delivery to you.
* **Area for Improvement (18)**: Identify one area where we could improve our project management.
* **Likelihood to Hire Again (19)**: Rate your likelihood to hire our consultancy again on a scale of 1-5.
* **Likelihood to Refer (20)**: Rate your likelihood to refer us to a colleague on a scale of 1-5.
* **Additional Comments (22)**: Provide any additional comments or observations about your experience with our consultancy.
* **Permission to Use Testimonial (23)**: Indicate whether you grant permission to use your feedback as a professional testimonial.
