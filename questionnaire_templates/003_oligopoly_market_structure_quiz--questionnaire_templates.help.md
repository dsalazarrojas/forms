# oligopoly_market_structure_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The oligopoly_market_structure_quiz form is designed to help you understand the market structure of a company or industry, specifically assessing the characteristics of an oligopoly market structure.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully, considering the options provided for each field.
2. Review the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section for detailed explanations of each field.
3. Use the "Tips" section for additional guidance, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Market structure types** (`oligopoly_market_structure_quiz_page_1`, select_one, required: false): This question asks whether the market is an oligopoly, which is a market structure characterized by a small number of firms that have significant market power.
	+ Select "Yes" if the market is an oligopoly, meaning there are a few firms with significant market power.
	+ Select "No" if the market is not an oligopoly.
* **Number of firms** (`oligopoly_market_structure_quiz_page_2`, number, required: false): This question asks about the actual number of firms in the market.
	+ Enter a numerical value, indicating the total number of firms in the market.
* **Market structure types explanation** (`oligopoly_market_structure_quiz_page_3`, note, required: false): This is a text field for additional information about the market structure types. You may provide a brief explanation or note about the market structure.
* **Market demand and supply** (`oligopoly_market_structure_quiz_page_4`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about the market's demand and supply characteristics.
	+ Select "Yes" for each of the following characteristics:
		- High demand
		- Low supply
	+ Select "No" for characteristics that are not present.
* **Number of firms explanation** (`oligopoly_market_structure_quiz_page_5`, note, required: false): This is a text field for additional information about the number of firms. You may provide a brief explanation or note about the number of firms.
* **Number of firms type** (`oligopoly_market_structure_quiz_page_6`, select_one, required: false): This question asks about the type of the number of firms.
	+ Select "Yes" if the number of firms is a relevant characteristic of the market structure.
	+ Select "No" if the number of firms is not a relevant characteristic.
* **Market demand and supply explanation** (`oligopoly_market_structure_quiz_page_7`, note, required: false): This is a text field for additional information about the market demand and supply. You may provide a brief explanation or note about the market demand and supply.
* **Monopolistic market structure** (`oligopoly_market_structure_quiz_page_8`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about the presence of a monopolistic market structure.
	+ Select "Yes" if the market has a monopolistic structure.
	+ Select "No" if the market does not have a monopolistic structure.
