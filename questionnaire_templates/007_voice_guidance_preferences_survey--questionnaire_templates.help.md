# Voice Guidance Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experiences and preferences with voice guidance. The data collected will help us improve the design and functionality of voice guidance in our product.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form section by section.
2. For each field, select the option that best represents your answer.
3. For fields with multiple options, select all that apply.
4. Enter your email address and phone number if you wish to be contacted for further discussion.
5. Leave a note if you have any additional thoughts or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you use voice guidance?** (`voice_guidance_usage`, text, required):
    This question asks about your frequency of using voice guidance. Select the option that best describes how often you use it.
* **How satisfied are you with voice guidance?** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, false):
    This question measures your level of satisfaction with voice guidance. Select the option that best represents your satisfaction.
* **Which accessibility preferences do you have?** (`accessibility_preferences`, select_multiple, false):
    This question asks about any accessibility preferences you have. Select all that apply.
* **Which feature priorities do you have?** (`feature_priorities`, select_multiple, false):
    This question asks about your priorities for features in our product. Select all that apply.
* **Additional notes** (`note`, note, false):
    Leave a note if you have any additional thoughts or comments about your experience with voice guidance.
* **Email** (`email`, email, false):
    Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted for further discussion.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, false):
    Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted for further discussion.

## Tips
- Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
- Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey!
