# Golf Trip Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your preferences for a golf trip, allowing us to tailor the trip to your specifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your trip preferences, such as location and time of day.
2. Choose your travel dates and time of day.
3. Provide your contact information, including email and phone number.
4. Add any additional comments or special requests you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trip Preferences** (`trip_preferences_form_2`): Select one of the provided options to indicate your preferences for the golf trip.
	+ Select a preference for the golf trip
	+ Other (Please specify in the special requests field)
* **When would you like to travel?** (`travel_dates`): Choose the travel dates from the provided options or select "Other" and specify a different date.
	+ 1st week of April
	+ 2nd week of April
	+ 3rd week of April
	+ Other
* **At what time of day would you like to travel?** (`travel_time`): Choose the time of day from the provided options or select "Other" and specify a different time.
	+ Morning
	+ Afternoon
	+ Evening
	+ Other
* **What is your budget for the golf trip?** (`budget_range`): Enter a whole number representing your budget for the trip.
* **Number of players?** (`number_of_players`): Enter a whole number indicating how many players will be attending the trip.
* **What golf clubs would you like to play at?** (`golf_club_preferences`): Select one or more of the provided golf clubs to indicate your preferences.
	+ Gulf Club 1, Gulf Club 2, Golf Club 3
	+ Other
* **Is there anything special we should know about your trip?** (`special_request`): Provide any additional information or requests that would help us tailor your trip.
* **What is your preferred location for the golf trip?** (`travel_location`): Select one or more of the provided locations to indicate your preferences.
	+ Mountain
	+ Ocean
	+ City
	+ Other
* **What is your budget for the golf trip?** (`budget_range`): Enter a whole number representing your budget for the trip.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`): Provide any additional comments or information that you would like to share about your trip.
* **Contact Information** (`email` and `phone`): Enter your email and phone number so we can contact you about your trip.
	+ Email
	+ Phone
