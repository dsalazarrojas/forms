# Mission Statement Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mission Statement Collection Form is designed to collect mission statements from users. Mission statements are short, concise statements that describe the purpose and goals of an organization, project, or individual.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start collecting mission statements.
2. Each question is a text field, where you can type your mission statement.
3. Answer each question as accurately and completely as possible.
4. You can save your progress and come back to the form later if needed.
5. When you have finished, click "Submit" to send the form.
