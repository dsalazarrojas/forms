# Writing Group Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential members of a writing group, gathering their interests and preferences to facilitate a collaborative and effective writing group experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through the form carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. For each question, select the most relevant option from the provided list.
3. Provide a brief description if a field requires it (e.g., "about_me" and "additional_comments").
4. You can skip fields that are not applicable or not relevant to your situation.
5. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is a brief welcome message.
* **What is your primary role in the group?**: Please select one of the four options:
	+ I am a writer/author
	+ I am an editor/publisher
	+ I am a reader/fan
	+ Other (please specify)
* **What brings you here?**: Provide a brief explanation of why you are interested in joining the writing group.
* **What type of writing group are you interested in?**: Select all that apply:
	+ Creative Writing Group
	+ Non-Fiction Writing Group
	+ Poetry Group
	+ Playwriting Group
* **What type of writing group settings would you like?**: Select all that apply:
	+ Online
	+ In-person
	+ Hybrid
	+ Flexible/remote
* **What schedule would you like for your writing group?**: Select all that apply:
	+ Weekly
	+ Bi-weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Flexible
* **About You**: Please provide a brief description of yourself, including your writing experience and interests.
* **What would you like to achieve or gain in the group?**: Select all that apply:
	+ Feedback
	+ Networking
	+ Community
	+ Professional growth
* **Additional Comments**: Please provide any other comments or thoughts that you feel are relevant to your interest in joining the writing group.
* **Thank You**: We value your time and participation.

Note: This form is designed to be filled out only once, unless you have significantly changed your interests or circumstances since the last time you filled it out. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
