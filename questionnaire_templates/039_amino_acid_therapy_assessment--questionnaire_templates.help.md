# Amino Acid Therapy Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from you for an amino acid therapy assessment. Please fill out this form to provide us with details about your medical history, lifestyle, and treatment goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name**.
2. Enter your **Date of Birth** in MM/DD/YYYY format.
3. Provide your **Age**.
4. Select your **Gender** from the list.
5. Enter your **Phone Number**.
6. Enter your **Email Address**.
7. List your **Primary Health Concerns** and describe your **Health Concerns**.
8. List your **Current Medications**, **Supplements**, and **Diagnosed Medical Conditions**.
9. Answer questions about your **Lifestyle Factors**, such as **Sleep Quality**, **Sleep Hours**, **Stress Level**, **Exercise Frequency**, and **Diet Quality**.
10. Report your **Mental Health** symptoms and concerns, such as **Anxiety Frequency**, **Depression Frequency**, and **Concentration Issues**.
11. Answer questions about your **Previous Treatment Experience**, including **Amino Acid Therapy** and other **Treatments**.
12. State your **Primary Goal** for treatment.
13. Select **Specific Symptoms** you want to address through treatment.
14. Enter your **Timeline Expectations** for seeing results.
15. Confirm the **Accuracy of Information** you've provided.
16. Confirm that all your **Questions Have Been Addressed**.
17. **Consent to Treatment** by selecting 'Yes - I consent to assessment and treatment'.
18. Enter **Additional Comments or Concerns** if you have any.
19. Select how you **Hear About This Therapy**.
20. Enter the **Assessment Date**.
21. Enter the **Practitioner Name**.
22. Review the **Recommended Treatment Plan** (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Required): This is your given name.
* **Last Name** (Required): This is your surname.
* **Date of Birth** (Required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Age** (Required): Enter your age.
* **Gender** (Optional): Select your gender from the list.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter your email address.
* **What Are Your Primary Health Concerns** (Required): List your primary health concerns.
* **Describe Your Health Concerns** (Optional): Describe your health concerns in more detail.
* **Current Medications** (Required): List all medications and dosages.
* **Supplements** (Optional): List all supplements.
* **Diagnosed Medical Conditions** (Optional): List all diagnosed conditions.
* **Lifestyle Factors**
	+ **Sleep Quality** (Required): Select your sleep quality.
	+ **Sleep Hours** (Optional): Enter average hours of sleep per night.
	+ **Stress Level** (Required): Enter stress level on a scale of 1-10.
	+ **Exercise Frequency** (Required): Select exercise frequency.
	+ **Diet Quality** (Required): Select your diet quality.
	+ **Water Intake** (Optional): Enter glasses of water per day.
	+ **Caffeine Intake** (Optional): Enter cups of coffee or tea per day.
	+ **Alcohol Consumption** (Required): Select alcohol consumption frequency.
	+ **Tobacco/Nicotine Use** (Required): Select tobacco/nicotine use frequency.
* **Mental Health** symptoms and concerns:
	+ **Anxiety Frequency** (Required): Select anxiety frequency.
	+ **Depression Frequency** (Required): Select depression frequency.
	+ **Concentration Issues** (Required): Select concentration issues.
	+ **Memory Issues** (Required): Select memory issues.
* **Previous Treatment Experience**:
	+ **Have You Had Amino Acid Therapy Before** (Optional): Select if you've had amino acid therapy before.
	+ **What Were the Results** (Optional): Select the results of your previous therapy.
	+ **What Other Treatments Have You Tried** (Optional): List other treatments you've tried.
* **Primary Goal** (Required): Enter your primary goal for treatment.
* **Specific Symptoms** (Required): Select specific symptoms you want to address through treatment.
* **Timeline Expectations** (Optional): Enter your expectations for seeing results.
* **Accuracy of Information** (Required): Confirm the accuracy of the information you've provided.
* **Have All Your Questions Been Addressed** (Required): Confirm if all your questions have been addressed.
* **Consent to Treatment** (Required): Select 'Yes - I consent to assessment and treatment'.
* **Additional Comments or Concerns** (Optional): Enter any additional comments or concerns.
* **How Did You Hear About This Therapy** (Optional): Select how you heard about this therapy.
* **Assessment Date** (Required): Enter the date of assessment.
* **Practitioner Name** (Optional): Enter the name of the practitioner.
* **Recommended Treatment Plan** (Optional): Enter the recommended treatment plan.
* **Additional Comments** (Optional): Enter any additional comments.
