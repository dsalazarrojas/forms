# Secret Santa Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the Secret Santa gift exchange game. It will help the gift-giver and recipient to communicate their preferences and limits for the gift selection process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your details and preferences.
2. Ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
3. Save the form and submit it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user (id: 1)**: Enter your name.
* **gift_limit (id: 2)**: Select the maximum number of gifts you are willing to give or receive (1, 2, or 3).
* **start_date (id: 3)**: Enter the start date of the gift selection process (in format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **end_date (id: 4)**: Enter the end date of the gift selection process (in format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **max_budget (id: 5)**: Enter the maximum budget for the gift (in currency unit, e.g., dollars or euros).
* **min_budget (id: 6)**: Enter the minimum budget for the gift (in currency unit, e.g., dollars or euros).
* **max_value (id: 7)**: Enter the maximum value of the gift (in currency unit, e.g., dollars or euros).
* **min_value (id: 8)**: Enter the minimum value of the gift (in currency unit, e.g., dollars or euros).
* **max_price (id: 9)**: Enter the maximum price of the gift (in currency unit, e.g., dollars or euros).
* **min_price (id: 10)**: Enter the minimum price of the gift (in currency unit, e.g., dollars or euros).
* **max_price_range (id: 11)**: Enter the maximum price range for the gift (e.g., 20-50, 50-100, etc.).
* **min_price_range (id: 12)**: Enter the minimum price range for the gift (e.g., 20-50, 50-100, etc.).
* **price_range (id: 13)**: Select the price range of the gift you are willing to give or receive (under 20, 20-50, 50-100, 100-200).
* **gift_recipient (id: 14)**: Enter the name of the gift recipient.
* **gift_sender (id: 15)**: Enter the name of the gift sender.
* **secret_santa_group (id: 16)**: Enter the name of the Secret Santa group.
* **message (id: 17)**: Enter a message for the gift recipient.
* **sender_message (id: 18)**: Enter a message from the gift sender.
* **recipient_message (id: 19)**: Enter a message for the recipient.
* **senders_message (id: 20)**: Enter a message from the sender.
* **recipient_messages (id: 21)**: Enter messages for the recipient.
* **senders_messages (id: 22)**: Enter messages from the sender.
* **message_from (id: 23)**: Enter the message from.
* **message_to (id: 24)**: Enter the message to.
* **notes (id: 25)**: Enter any additional notes.

Please note that some fields are optional, and you should only fill out the fields that are relevant to you.
