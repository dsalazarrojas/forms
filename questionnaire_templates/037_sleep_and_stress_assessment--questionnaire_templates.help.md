# Sleep And Stress Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form aims to assess an individual's sleep and stress levels. It is designed to be completed by a wide range of users, including individuals dealing with anxiety, insomnia, or other sleep-related issues. This form will help users understand their sleep patterns, stress levels, and overall well-being.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Go through each question carefully and provide honest answers.
2.  For questions with multiple options, choose the one that best describes your situation.
3.  For open-ended questions, please provide as much detail as possible.
4.  Please note that all answers will be kept confidential and will not affect your treatment or services received.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (`general_info`, text, optional): This is a field for you to provide any additional information that you feel is relevant to your sleep and stress levels.
* **Sleep Patterns** (`sleep_patterns`, select_multiple, optional): 
  + 'Yes' - You generally sleep well.
  + 'No' - You do not generally sleep well.
  + Other - Please specify other details.
* **Stress Levels** (`stress_levels`, number, optional): Rate your stress levels from 1 (low) to 10 (high).
* **Bedtime** (`bedtime`, time, optional): Please enter your bedtime.
* **Waketime** (`waketime`, time, optional): Please enter your wake-up time.
* **Overall Stress** (`overall_stress`, number, optional): Rate your overall stress levels from 1 (low) to 10 (high).
* **Anxiety** (`anxiety`, text, optional): Please describe any feelings of anxiety you may have.
* **Relaxation** (`relaxation`, text, optional): Describe any relaxation techniques you use.
* **Exercise** (`exercise`, text, optional): Describe your exercise frequency.
* **Diet** (`diet`, text, optional): Describe your diet quality.
* **Work Life Balance** (`work_life_balance`, select_one, optional): 
  + 'Yes' - You generally maintain a good balance between work and life.
  + 'No' - You do not generally maintain a good balance between work and life.
  + Other - Please specify other details.
* **Relationship Status** (`relationship_status`, select_one, optional): 
  + 'Yes' - You are in a relationship.
  + 'No' - You are not in a relationship.
  + Other - Please specify other details.
* **Sleep Quality** (`sleep_quality`, number, optional): Rate your sleep quality from 1 (poor) to 10 (good).
* **Sleep Duration** (`sleep_duration`, number, optional): Rate your sleep duration from 1 (short) to 10 (long).
* **Wake Up Time** (`wake_up_time`, time, optional): Please enter your wake-up time.
* **Bedtime Time** (`bedtime_time`, time, optional): Please enter your bedtime.
* **Stressors** (`stresses`, text, optional): List any stressors you may have.
* **Relaxation Techniques** (`relaxation_techniques`, text, optional): Describe any relaxation techniques you use.
* **Exercise Frequency** (`exercise_frequency`, text, optional): Describe your exercise frequency.
* **Diet Quality** (`diet_quality`, text, optional): Describe your diet quality.
* **Work Life Balance Frequency** (`work_life_balance_frequency`, text, optional): Describe how often you balance your work and life.
* **Relationship Status Frequency** (`relationship_status_frequency`, text, optional): Describe how often you have relationships.
* **Sleep Quality Frequency** (`sleep_quality_frequency`, text, optional): Describe how often you maintain good sleep quality.
* **Sleep Duration Frequency** (`sleep_duration_frequency`, text, optional): Describe how often you get enough sleep.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Please add any additional comments you may have.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by a wide range of users. Please be honest with your answers and do not hesitate to ask for clarification if you need further details.
