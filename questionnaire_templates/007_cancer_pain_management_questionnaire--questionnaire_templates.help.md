# Cancer Pain Management Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare professionals assess and manage cancer pain effectively. It is a comprehensive questionnaire that covers various aspects of cancer pain, including location, intensity, characteristics, frequency, triggers, and impact on daily activities and relationships. The form is used to collect detailed information about the patient's pain experience, which can inform treatment decisions and improve pain management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Answer all questions**: Carefully read each question and provide accurate information about your pain experience.
2. **Be honest and detailed**: Answer questions as accurately and completely as possible, without leaving out any information.
3. **Select all that apply**: Choose all options that apply to your situation.
4. **Rate your answers**: Rate your answers using numerical values where indicated (e.g., pain intensity 0-10).
5. **Submit your form**: Once you have completed all questions, click the "Submit" button to save your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name**: Your legal name as it appears on your identification documents. (Required)
* **Patient ID or Medical Record Number**: Your hospital or clinic ID number or medical record number. (Required)
* **Patient Age**: Your age in years. (Required)
* **Gender**: Select your gender identity. (Optional)
* **Type of Cancer**: Select the type of cancer you are diagnosed with. (Required)
* **Cancer Stage**: Select the current stage of your cancer if known. (Optional)
* **Current Treatment Type**: Select all types of treatments you are currently receiving. (Required)
* **Where Do You Feel Pain**: Select all locations where you feel pain. (Required)
* **Current Pain Level**: Rate your current pain level from 0 (no pain) to 10 (worst pain ever). (Required)
* **Worst Pain in Last 24 Hours**: Rate the highest pain level you experienced in the past 24 hours. (Required)
* **Least Pain in Last 24 Hours**: Rate the lowest pain level you experienced in the past 24 hours. (Required)
* **Average Pain Level**: Rate your average pain level over the past 24 hours. (Required)
* **Describe Your Pain**: Select all types of pain you experience. (Required)
* **How Often Do You Experience Pain**: Select how often you experience pain. (Required)
* **What Makes Pain Worse**: Select all factors that make your pain worse. (Optional)
* **What Helps Relieve Pain**: Select all methods that provide pain relief. (Optional)
* **Current Pain Medications**: List all medications you take for pain management. (Required)
* **How Well Do Medications Work**: Rate the effectiveness of your pain medications. (Required)
* **Medication Side Effects**: Select all side effects you experience from your pain medications. (Optional)
* **Pain Impact on Sleep**: Select how pain impacts your sleep quality. (Required)
* **Pain Impact on Daily Activities**: Select how pain affects your daily activities. (Required)
* **Pain Impact on Mood**: Select how pain affects your emotional state. (Required)
* **Pain Impact on Relationships**: Select how pain affects your relationships. (Optional)
* **Interest in Non-Medication Approaches**: Select your interest in alternative pain relief methods. (Optional)
* **Additional Concerns**: Share any other information related to your pain experience. (Optional)
* **Questionnaire Completion Date**: Enter the date you completed this form. (Required)

By following these steps and completing this form accurately, you will help healthcare professionals better understand your pain experience and provide more effective care and management.
