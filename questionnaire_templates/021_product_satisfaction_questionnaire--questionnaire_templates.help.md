# Product Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on products, gathering information about customer satisfaction, product experience, and potential issues.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with information about the product you are evaluating.
2. Choose the product type that best describes the product you're evaluating.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with the product.
4. Provide any comments about your experience with the product.
5. Enter your email address for any follow-up communication.
6. If you have a phone number, enter it for any further contact.
7. Select the relevant options for the product type.
8. Indicate your satisfaction level with the product.
9. Select the relevant options for the time of purchase.
10. Select the relevant options for the time of the purchase.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Id** (`product_id`, number, optional): Enter the product's unique identifier.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the product you are evaluating.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the product (1-5).
* **Comment** (`comment`, note, optional): Provide any comments or feedback about your experience with the product.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for any follow-up communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any further contact.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of product you are evaluating (Option A, Option B, Option C, Option D, Option E).
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your satisfaction level with the product (Highly Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Satisfied).
* **Date of Purchase** (`date_of_purchase`, date, optional): Enter the date you purchased the product.
* **Time of Purchase** (`time_of_purchase`, time, optional): Enter the time you purchased the product.
* **Date of Purchase Time** (`date_of_purchase_time`, date, optional): Enter the time you purchased the product.
* **Time of Purchase Time** (`time_of_purchase_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you purchased the product.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing accurate and relevant information will help us improve the product and provide better customer support.
