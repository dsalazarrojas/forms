# Impact Of Isolation On Life Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your experience with isolation. This includes how you feel, how you cope with it, and how it affects your life. Please answer questions to the best of your ability and be honest with your responses.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete each page carefully and submit when finished.
- For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
- Use the provided options as a guideline, but feel free to answer in your own words if the options don't fit your experience.
- If you have trouble answering a question or need clarification, ask for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How Do You Feel** (`survey_start`, `select_multiple`, required): This question asks about your emotional state. Please choose one or more of the following options that best describes how you feel: 
  - Lonely
  - Neutral
  - Happy

* **What Changes** (`changes_in_life`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about significant changes that have occurred in your life due to isolation. Select one of the following options that best describes your experience:
  - Loss of a loved one
  - Moving to a new city
  - Job change
  - Other (Specify)

* **How Do You Cope** (`coping_mechans`, `select_multiple`, required): This question asks about the ways you cope with isolation. Select all that apply:
  - Talk to a therapist
  - Talk to friends
  - Talk to a family member
  - Call a support hotline
  - Other (Specify)

* **How Would You Rate** (`wellbeing_scales`, `number`, required): This question asks you to rate your overall well-being on a scale. Please enter a number between 1 and 10, where 1 indicates a very poor well-being and 10 indicates excellent well-being.

* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, optional): This field is for any additional thoughts or comments you'd like to share about your experience with isolation. Please keep your response concise and focused on the question at hand.

* **Contact Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so that we can contact you with any follow-up questions or concerns.

* **Contact Phone** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you with any follow-up questions or concerns. This field is optional, but recommended for easier communication.
