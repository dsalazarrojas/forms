# Interdisciplinary Learning Barriers Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Interdisciplinary Learning Barriers Survey is designed to gather information on obstacles that learners face when engaging in interdisciplinary learning activities. This survey aims to understand the difficulties that arise when integrating multiple subjects or disciplines into a single learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question on the pages provided, and submit your responses at the end.
2. You can select one or multiple options for questions that allow multiple selections.
3. For questions with no selections, simply type your answer in the text box provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intro** (`intro_page`, `note`, not required): This is a brief introduction to the survey. It's meant to provide context and background information on the purpose and goals of the survey.
* **How often do you experience difficulties with** (`page2`, `select_one`, not required): This question asks about the frequency of difficulties you encounter when engaging in interdisciplinary learning activities. Please select one of the options provided: Always, Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never.
* **What kind of difficulties do you experience when learning** (`page3`, `select_multiple`, not required): This question asks about the types of difficulties you face in interdisciplinary learning. Please select all that apply from the provided options: Lack of clear goals, Inadequate resources, Lack of support, Insufficient time, Difficulty with team members, or Other (please specify).
* **How would you rate your confidence in** (`page4`, `select_one`, not required): This question asks about your self-assessment of your confidence level in your abilities to engage in interdisciplinary learning. Please select one of the options provided: Low, Medium, or High.
* **What is your age** (`page5`, `number`, not required): This question asks about your age. Please enter a numerical value for your age.
* **What is your gender** (`page6`, `select_one`, not required): This question asks about your gender. Please select one of the provided options: Male, Female, or Other.
* **What is your department** (`page7`, `select_one`, not required): This question asks about your academic department. Please select one of the provided options: Biology, Chemistry, Physics, or Other.
* **Which of the following** (`page8`, `select_one`, not required): This question asks about a specific option. Please select one of the options provided: 1, 2, or 3.
* **Additional** (`page9`, `note`, not required): This is an open-ended text box for you to provide any additional comments or thoughts.
* **Thank you** (`page10`, `note`, not required): This is a thank-you message from the survey creators.
* **Additional comments** (`page11`, `note`, not required): This is another open-ended text box for you to provide any additional comments or thoughts.
* **Other comments** (`page12`, `note`, not required): This is yet another open-ended text box for you to provide any additional comments or thoughts.
* **None** (`page13`, `select_one`, not required): This question asks about the likelihood of experiencing difficulties. Please select one of the options provided: Very likely, Somewhat likely, Not likely, or Very unlikely.
* **What do you think will happen** (`page14`, `select_multiple`, not required): This question asks about your expectations. Please select all that apply from the provided options: A lot, A moderate amount, A small amount, None, or Other (please specify).
* **What would you recommend** (`page15`, `select_one`, not required): This question asks about your recommendations. Please select one of the options provided: Highly, Somewhat, or Not at all.
* **How important is it** (`page16`, `select_one`, not required): This question asks about the importance of a particular aspect. Please select one of the options provided: Very, Somewhat, Not very, or Not at all.
* **What do you think we should do** (`page17`, `select_multiple`, not required): This question asks about your suggestions. Please select all that apply from the provided options: A lot, A moderate amount, A small amount, None, or Other (please specify).
* **Other comments** (`page18`, `note`, not required): This is another open-ended text box for you to provide any additional comments or thoughts.
* **Additional comments** (`page19`, `note`, not required): This is yet another open-ended text box for you to provide any additional comments or thoughts.
* **Other comments** (`page20`, `note`, not required): This is the final open-ended text box for you to provide any additional comments or thoughts.
