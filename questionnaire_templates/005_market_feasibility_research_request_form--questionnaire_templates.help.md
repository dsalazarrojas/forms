# Market Feasibility Research Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for market feasibility research requests. It is intended to collect details about the project you wish to have researched, allowing us to understand your needs and provide valuable insights to inform your decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a title for your market feasibility research request.
2. Add a brief description of the project (if necessary).
3. Specify the number of people involved in the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Market Feasibility Research Request** (`market_feasibility_research_request_form`, text, optional): This is the main field where you can describe your market feasibility research request. You can provide as much detail as you need to help us understand your requirements.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, note, optional): This field allows you to add a more detailed description of your project, if needed. It's an open-ended text area where you can share any relevant information about your project.
* **Research Brief** (`research_brief`, number, optional): This field is intended for you to specify the number of people involved in the project. Please enter a numeric value for the number of people required for the research.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure we have a clear understanding of your requirements.
- If you have any additional information or questions, feel free to contact us.
- If you have already submitted a similar request, please let us know so we can direct you to the relevant information or avoid duplication of effort.
