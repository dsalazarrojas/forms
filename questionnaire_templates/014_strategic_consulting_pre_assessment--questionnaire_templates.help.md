**Purpose**
The Strategic Consulting Pre-Assessment form is a tool used to gather essential information from both consultants and clients about their readiness, goals, and expectations for the consulting engagement. This form helps consultants and clients establish a clear understanding of each other's expectations, ensuring a successful and effective partnership.

**How To Complete This Form**
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. As a consultant, provide detailed information about your expertise, skills, and experience in the `consultant_details` field.
2. Describe the client's challenges and goals in the `client_challenges` and `client_goals` fields, respectively.
3. Assess the client's readiness for the consulting engagement using the `engagement_readiness` field.
4. In the `client_details` field, provide any additional information about the client that may be relevant to the engagement.
5. If this is a valid lead, select "Yes" in the `lead_qualification` field.
6. List your relevant skills and qualifications in the `consultant_skills` field.
7. Outline your proposed engagement strategy in the `engagement_strategy` field.
8. Describe the next steps you plan to take in the `next_steps` field.
9. Outline the frequency and method of your follow-up meetings with the client in the `client_followup_frequency` and `consultant_followup_method` fields, respectively.
10. Provide any additional questions or comments in the `additional_questions` field.
11. Leave any comments or notes for the consultant or client in the `consultant_comments` and `client_comments` fields, respectively.
12. If the consultant and client have agreed on the terms, select "Yes" in the `consultant_agreed` and `client_agreed` fields.
13. If applicable, select the date and time of the agreed-upon next steps in the `client_agreed_date` and `client_agreed_time` fields.
14. Outline the next steps the consultant and client will take in the `consultant_next_steps` and `client_next_steps` fields.
15. If the consultant and client have agreed on the terms, provide the agreed-upon status in the `consultant_agreed_status` and `client_agreed_status` fields.
16. Finally, select the date of the next steps in the `consultant_next_steps_date` field.

**Field-by-Field Explanation**

* **Consultant Details (consultant_details)**: A text field where you can provide information about your expertise, skills, and experience.
* **Client Challenges (client_challenges)**: A text field where you can describe the client's challenges and issues.
* **Client Goals (client_goals)**: A text field where you can outline the client's objectives and aspirations.
* **Engagement Readiness (engagement_readiness)**: A numerical field where you can assess the client's readiness for the consulting engagement, with higher values indicating greater readiness.
* **Client Details (client_details)**: A text field where you can provide additional information about the client.
* **Lead Qualification (lead_qualification)**: A select-one field where you can determine if this is a valid lead.
* **Consultant Skills (consultant_skills)**: A text field where you can list your relevant skills and qualifications.
* **Engagement Strategy (engagement_strategy)**: A text field where you can outline your proposed engagement strategy.
* **Next Steps (next_steps)**: A text field where you can describe the next steps you plan to take.
* **Client Follow-up Frequency (client_followup_frequency)**: A numerical field where you can determine the frequency of your follow-up meetings with the client.
* **Consultant Follow-up Method (consultant_followup_method)**: A text field where you can outline the method of your follow-up meetings with the client.
* **Client Next Steps (client_next_steps)**: A text field where you can describe the next steps the client will take.
* **Consultant Agreed Status (consultant_agreed_status)**: A select-one field where you can select "Yes" or "No" indicating agreement with the terms.
* **Client Agreed Status (client_agreed_status)**: A select-one field where you can select "Yes" or "No" indicating agreement with the terms.
* **Next Steps Date (consultant_next_steps_date)**: A date field where you can select the date of the agreed-upon next steps.
* **Client Agreed Date (client_agreed_date)**: A date field where you can select the date of the agreed-upon next steps.
* **Client Agreed Time (client_agreed_time)**: A time field where you can select the time of the agreed-upon next steps.
* **Consultant Next Steps (consultant_next_steps)**: A text field where you can describe the next steps the consultant will take.
* **Consultant Agreed Status (consultant_agreed_status)**: A select-one field where you can select "Yes" or "No" indicating agreement with the terms.
* **Client Agreed Status (client_agreed_status)**: A select-one field where you can select "Yes" or "No" indicating agreement with the terms.
* **Additional Questions (additional_questions)**: A text field where you can provide any additional questions or comments.
* **Consultant Comments (consultant_comments)**: A text field where you can leave comments or notes for the consultant.
* **Client Comments (client_comments)**: A text field where you can leave comments or notes for the client.
* **Consultant Agreed (consultant_agreed)**: A text field where you can select "Yes" or "No" indicating agreement with the terms.
* **Client Agreed (client_agreed)**: A text field where you can select "Yes" or "No" indicating agreement with the terms.

**Tips**
* Make sure to fill out each field accurately and completely.
* Review the form before submission to ensure you haven't missed any important information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the consultant or client for clarification.
* Be sure to keep sensitive information confidential.
* Store this form for reference and follow-up purposes.
