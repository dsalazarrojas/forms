# Digital Skin Analysis Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Digital Skin Analysis Survey is designed to collect information from individuals to provide personalized services and recommendations for their skin health.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your last name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
5. Select your skin type from the Skin Type dropdown menu.
6. Enter any medical conditions you have in the Medical Condition field, if applicable.
7. Choose your preferred language in the Preferred Language dropdown menu.
8. Select if you have a medical condition in the Medical Condition field.
9. Enter your skin analysis method in the Skin Analysis Method field.
10. Select your skin color in the Skin Color field.
11. Enter your hair color in the Hair Color field.
12. Enter the name of your salon guest in the Salon Guest field.
13. Select if you have a salon in the Salon field.
14. Enter any skincare products you are using in the Skincare Products field.
15. Check if you will be following up with the salon in the Follow Up field.
16. Select the frequency of your follow-up in the Follow Up Frequency field.
17. Choose the method of your follow-up in the Follow Up Method field.
18. Enter the date of your follow-up in the Follow Up Date field.
19. Enter your skin treatment pros in the Skin Treatment Pros field.
20. Enter your follow-up pros in the Follow Up Pros field.
21. Select if you have a salon in the Salon field.
22. Enter any pros of your skincare products in the Skincare Products Pros field.
23. Enter your salon guest in the Salon Guest field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Skin Type** (Skin Type, select_one, required): Select your skin type from the dropdown menu.
* **Skin** (Skin, note, required): Enter any additional information about your skin.
* **Medical Condition** (Medical Condition, text, required): Enter any medical conditions you have, if applicable.
* **Medical History** (Medical History, text, optional): Enter any relevant medical history.
* **Skin Analysis Method** (Skin Analysis Method, select_multiple, optional): Select the methods you used for skin analysis.
* **Preferred Language** (Preferred Language, select_one, required): Select your preferred language.
* **Skin Color** (Skin Color, text, optional): Enter your skin color.
* **Hair Color** (Hair Color, text, optional): Enter your hair color.
* **Salon Guest** (Salon Guest, text, optional): Enter your salon guest name.
* **Salon** (Salon, select_one, required): Select if you have a salon.
* **Skincare Products** (Skincare Products, text, optional): Enter any skincare products you are using.
* **Follow Up** (Follow Up, select_one, required): Check if you will follow up with the salon.
* **Follow Up Frequency** (Follow Up Frequency, select_one, optional): Select the frequency of your follow-up.
* **Follow Up Method** (Follow Up Method, select_multiple, optional): Select the methods you will use for follow-up.
* **Follow Up Date** (Follow Up Date, date, optional): Enter the date of your follow-up.
* **Skin Treatment Pros** (Skin Treatment Pros, text, optional): Enter any pros of your skin treatment.
* **Follow Up Pros** (Follow Up Pros, text, optional): Enter any pros of your follow-up.
* **Salons** (Salons, select_one, required): Select if you have a salon.
* **Skincare Products Pros** (Skincare Products Pros, text, optional): Enter any pros of your skincare products.
* **Salon Guest** (Salon Guest, text, optional): Enter your salon guest name.

Note: The form requires some required and optional fields. Please fill in all required fields accurately to ensure the best possible outcome.
