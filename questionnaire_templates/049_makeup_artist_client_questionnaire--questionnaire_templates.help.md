# Makeup Artist Client Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients for a makeup artist service. It will help the makeup artist to understand the client's preferences and tailor their service accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each field carefully and ensure you provide accurate information.
2. Fill out each field according to your preferences and needs.
3. Review and submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you do?** (`intro`, text, not required): A brief description of what you do or what you are interested in. This field is not required but will help the makeup artist get a better understanding of your work or interests.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, not required): Your name as a client.
* **How do you prefer to be contacted?** (`how_do_you_prefer_to_be_contacted`, select_one, not required): Select one of the options: Email, Phone, or None.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, not required): Your address.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, text, not required): A brief description of your occupation.
* **Education** (`education`, text, not required): A brief description of your education.
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, not required): The date and time you are available for the service.
* **How many times per week are you available?** (`availability_2`, select_multiple, not required): Select multiple times per week you are available.
* **What days per week are you available?** (`availability_3`, select_multiple, not required): Select multiple days per week you are available.
* **Rate** (`rate`, number, not required): A rate or amount you are willing to pay for the service.
* **Services** (`services`, select_multiple, not required): Select multiple services you are interested in, such as makeup, hair, nails, waxing, tanning, or lash.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): Any additional notes or comments you would like to include.

## Tips
- Please fill out each field accurately and truthfully.
- Make sure to select all relevant services you are interested in.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask the makeup artist.

Please review and submit the form once you have completed all required fields.
