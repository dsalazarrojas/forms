# Commercial Mortgage Qualification Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from commercial property owners or businesses to assess their creditworthiness and determine the feasibility of a commercial loan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide accurate and complete information about your business and the commercial property. Ensure that you are authorized to submit this information on behalf of the business. The form is divided into several sections, each with specific fields that require your attention. Please read each section carefully and answer all questions truthfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Provide the legal name of the business as it appears on official documents.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of business entity (Corporation, LLC, Partnership, Sole Proprietorship, Non-Profit).
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, number, required): Enter the number of years the business has been operating.
* **Annual Revenue** (`annual_revenue`, number, required): Enter the gross annual revenue of the business in USD.
* **Credit Score Range** (`credit_score_range`, select_one, required): Select the primary borrower credit score range (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Below 600).
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of commercial property (Office Building, Retail Space, Industrial/Warehouse, Mixed-Use, Multi-Family, Hotel/Motel, Medical Facility).
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the full street address of the property.
* **Property Square Footage** (`property_square_footage`, number, required): Enter the total square footage of the property.
* **Year Built** (`year_built`, number, optional): Enter the year the property was constructed.
* **Loan Details** (`loan_details`, note, optional): Provide any additional information about the loan (e.g., loan purpose, collateral).
* **Loan Amount Requested** (`loan_amount_requested`, number, required): Enter the total loan amount needed in USD.
* **Loan Purpose** (`loan_purpose`, select_one, required): Select the primary purpose of the loan (Purchase, Refinance, Construction, Renovation/Improvement, Working Capital).
* **Desired Loan Term** (`desired_loan_term`, select_one, required): Select the preferred loan term in years (5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30).
* **Down Payment Available** (`down_payment_available`, number, required): Enter the amount available for down payment in USD.
* **Existing Debt Obligations** (`existing_debt_obligations`, number, optional): Enter the total existing business debt in USD.
* **Additional Borrowers** (`additional_borrowers`, select_one, required): Select whether there will be additional borrowers on this loan (Yes, No).
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the primary contact email for loan communications.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the primary contact phone number.

Please note that the form is designed to be completed by an authorized representative of the business. If you are unsure about any of the fields or have questions, please reach out to us for clarification.
