# Risk Tolerance Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Risk Tolerance Questionnaire Form is designed to help you understand your risk tolerance and financial situation for investment purposes. It will ask you questions about your investment preferences, risk capacity, time horizon, risk tolerance, investment experience, financial goal, investment assets, financial net worth, annual income, and investment expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. For each question, select the option that best describes your current situation or preference.
2. If you have no preference for a particular question, select "No preference" or "Not applicable" if available.
3. Fill in any numerical fields (e.g., investment assets, financial net worth, annual income) with accurate values.
4. Make sure to answer all questions, even if you're unsure or have no information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Investment Preferences (1)
Investment Preferences: **Conservative, Moderate, Aggressive** (`select_one`): 
Choose your investment style: Conservative (low risk), Moderate (balanced), or Aggressive (high risk).

### Risk Capacity (2)
Risk Capacity: **High, Medium, Low** (`select_one`): 
Choose the level of risk you're comfortable with: High (able to take on high risks), Medium (balanced), or Low (averse to high risks).

### Time Horizon (3)
Time Horizon: **Short term, Medium term, Long term** (`select_one`): 
Choose the time period you're investing for: Short term (less than 5 years), Medium term (5-10 years), or Long term (more than 10 years).

### Risk Tolerance (4)
Risk Tolerance: **High, Medium, Low** (`select_one`): 
Choose the level of risk you're willing to take: High, Medium, or Low.

### Investment Experience (5)
Investment Experience: **1-2 years, 2-5 years, 5-10 years, More than 10 years** (`select_multiple`): 
Choose your level of investment experience: 1-2 years, 2-5 years, 5-10 years, or More than 10 years.

### Financial Goal (6)
Financial Goal: Enter your financial goal (e.g., retirement, education, down payment on a house).

### Investment Assets (7)
Investment Assets: Enter the value of your investment assets.

### Financial Net Worth (8)
Financial Net Worth: Enter your current financial net worth.

### Annual Income (9)
Annual Income: Enter your annual income.

### Investment Expenses (10)
Investment Expenses: Enter your average investment expenses per year.
