# Chinese Language Proficiency Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess a person's proficiency level in the Chinese language. It collects information to determine a candidate's language abilities and recommend an appropriate placement level for language courses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Candidate Name** (field 1) with the examinee's full name.
2. Enter the **Date of Birth** (field 2) if applicable. This is optional for placement records.
3. Enter a valid **Contact Email** (field 3) to receive results and communication.
4. Select the **Proficiency Goal** (field 4) that best matches the examinee's language proficiency level:
	* Conversational: for everyday conversations
	* Academic: for academic purposes (e.g., university studies, research)
	* Professional: for business or professional settings
	* Examination Preparation: for test-taking or exam preparation purposes
5. If an audio sample was provided, indicate whether it was used for the assessment (field 5).
6. Enter a short **Reading Level Assessment** (field 6) of the examinee's reading comprehension.
7. Rate the examinee's **Speaking Rating** (field 7) with one of the following options:
	* Beginner: for those who struggle to communicate
	* Intermediate: for those who can hold basic conversations
	* Upper Intermediate: for those who can hold more complex conversations
	* Advanced: for those with near-native level proficiency
8. Enter **Writing Sample Notes** (field 8) with a brief evaluation of the examinee's writing skills.
9. Select the **Recommended Placement Level** (field 9) based on the assessment results. This will help determine the most suitable course or level for further language development.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name (field 1)**: Full name of the examinee.
* **Date of Birth (field 2)**: Optional field for placement records, if applicable.
* **Contact Email (field 3)**: Email address for results and communication with the candidate.
* **Proficiency Goal (field 4)**: Select the purpose for which the candidate wants to use Chinese language skills.
* **Listening Sample Provided (field 5)**: Check if an audio sample was used for the assessment.
* **Reading Level Assessment (field 6)**: Briefly describe the examinee's reading comprehension skills.
* **Speaking Rating (field 7)**: Rate the examinee's speaking skills with one of the four options.
* **Writing Sample Notes (field 8)**: Provide a brief evaluation of the examinee's writing skills.
* **Recommended Placement Level (field 9)**: Select the most suitable course or level for further language development based on the assessment results.
