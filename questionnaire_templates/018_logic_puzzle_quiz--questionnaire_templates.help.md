# Logic Puzzle Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a questionnaire that gathers some basic information from users about their interests and preferences.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Fill out each field as instructed.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **What do you want to do today** (`What do you want to do today`, text, optional)
This field is where you can write what you want to do today. Please enter a short message to describe your plans.

### 2. **What is your favorite color** (`What is your favorite color`, select one, optional)
Select your favorite color from the options provided.

### 3. **What is your favorite animal** (`What is your favorite animal`, select multiple, optional)
Select all of your favorite animals from the options provided.

### 4. **Date** (`date`, date, optional)
Enter your date of birth or a special date that is relevant to you.

### 5. **Time** (`time`, time, optional)
Enter your preferred time of day.

### 6. **Additional notes** (`Additional notes`, note, optional)
Write any additional information you would like to share with us.

### 7. **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
Enter your email address for us to contact you.

### 8. **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional)
Enter your phone number for us to contact you.

Note: This form is intended for personal use only. Please do not use it in a commercial setting.
