# Bar Event Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from attendees on their experience at a bar event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the questionnaire.
2. Fill out each field according to your experience at the bar event.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields (*Required*).
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting.
5. Click "Submit" to send your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Response Date** (`response_date`, `date`, *Required*): Enter the current date as the date you attended the event.
*   Please use the date format MM/DD/YYYY, e.g. 12/25/2020.
*   
- **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, *Required*): Enter the name of the event you attended.
*   This is a required field, please enter the actual name of the event.
*   
- **Your Name** (`attendee_name`, `text`, *Required*): Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
*   Please make sure to enter your real name.
*   
- **Email Address** (`attendee_email`, `email`, *Not Required*): If you have a valid email address, please enter it.
*   This field is not required but helps us get in touch with you.
*   
- **Phone Number** (`attendee_phone`, `text`, *Not Required*): If you have a valid phone number, please enter it.
*   This field is not required.
*   
- **Event Date Attended** (`event_date`, `date`, *Required*): Enter the date you attended the event.
*   Please use the date format MM/DD/YYYY, e.g. 12/25/2020.
*   
- **Was This Your First Time at This Event?** (`first_time_event`, `select_one`, *Required*): Choose an answer from the dropdown menu to indicate if this was your first time at the event.
*   Select 'Yes' if this was your first time, 'No' if you've attended before.
*   
- **Size of Your Group** (`group_size`, `number`, *Not Required*): Enter the number of people in your group.
*   This field is not required.
*   
- **How Did You Hear About This Event?** (`how_heard_about_event`, `select_one`, *Required*): Choose an answer from the dropdown menu to indicate how you heard about the event.
*   Select the source from the options provided.
*   
- **Event Atmosphere and Setup** (`event_atmosphere_rating`, `select_one`, *Required*): Rate the event's atmosphere and setup.
*   Choose a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is Poor and 5 is Excellent.
*   
- **Entertainment Quality** (`entertainment_quality`, `select_one`, *Not Required*): Rate the entertainment quality of the event.
*   Choose a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is Poor and 5 is Excellent.
*   
- **Music and Sound Quality** (`music_experience`, `select_one`, *Not Required*): Rate the music quality at the event.
*   Choose a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is Poor and 5 is Excellent.
*   
- **Event Organization and Flow** (`event_organization`, `select_one`, *Required*): Rate the event's organization and flow.
*   Choose a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is Poor and 5 is Excellent.
*   
- **Staff Service and Responsiveness** (`staff_service_rating`, `select_one`, *Required*): Rate the staff's service and responsiveness.
*   Choose a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is Poor and 5 is Excellent.
*   
- **Beverage Pricing** (`beverage_pricing`, `select_one`, *Not Required*): Rate the beverage pricing.
*   Choose a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is Too High and 5 is Great Value.
*   
- **Food Options Quality (if applicable)** (`food_options`, `select_one`, *Not Required*): Rate the food options quality (if you ate there).
*   Choose a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is Poor and 5 is Excellent.
*   Select 'N/A' if you did not eat food or drinks.
*   
- **Would You Attend This Event Again?** (`would_attend_again`, `select_one`, *Required*): Choose an answer to indicate if you would attend the event again.
*   Select 'Definitely Would', 'Probably Would', 'Unsure', 'Probably Not', or 'Would Not'.
*   
- **Overall Event Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, *Required*): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event.
*   Choose a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is Very Dissatisfied and 5 is Very Satisfied.
*   
- **Would You Recommend This Event to Others?** (`recommendation_likelihood`, `select_one`, *Required*): Choose an answer to indicate if you would recommend the event to others.
*   Select 'Definitely Would', 'Probably Would', 'Unsure', 'Probably Not', or 'Would Not'.
*   
- **What Was the Best Part of the Event?** (`best_aspect`, `text`, *Not Required*): Enter a brief description of what you enjoyed most about the event.
*   This field is not required but helps us understand what made the event special.
*   
- **How Could This Event Be Improved?** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, *Not Required*): Enter suggestions on how the event could be improved.
*   This field is not required but helps us identify areas for improvement.
*   
- **Suggestions for Future Events** (`future_suggestions`, `text`, *Not Required*): Enter ideas for future events.
*   This field is not required but helps us plan better events.
*
