# Conference Dietary Preference Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect dietary preference information from conference attendees to ensure we can cater to their needs and preferences during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the following steps:

1. Fill in your **Attendee Name**, **Email Address**, and **Registration ID** (if applicable).
2. Select your preferred **Meal Plan** (All Meals Included, Breakfast Only, Lunch Only, Dinner Only, Coffee Breaks Only).
3. Choose your **Dietary Classification** (No Restrictions, Vegetarian, Vegan, Pescatarian, Kosher, Halal, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut Allergy, Other Allergy).
4. Specify any **Dietary Details** if your choice of dietary classification doesn't match your requirements.
5. List any **Food Allergies** and their **Severity** (Mild Preference, Moderate Avoidance, Severe Must Avoid, Life Threatening Cross-Contamination Risk).
6. Provide any **Faith-Based Dietary Requirements**.
7. Specify any **Doctor-Recommended Dietary Requirements**.
8. Choose your **Protein Preference** (Beef, Pork, Chicken, Fish, Tofu, Legumes, Eggs).
9. List any **Foods to Avoid** or **Dislikes**.
10. Specify your **Breakfast Needs**, **Lunch Needs**, and **Dinner Needs**.
11. Select any **Snack Preferences** (Fresh Fruit, Nuts and Seeds, Granola Bars, Cheese and Crackers, Vegetables and Hummus, No Snacks Needed).
12. Specify your **Beverage Needs**.
13. Provide an **Emergency Contact** (in case of severe allergies).
14. Add any **Additional Notes** about your dietary preferences or needs.
