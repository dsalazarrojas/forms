# Automatic Thought Observation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help users track and reflect on their automatic thoughts, emotions, and behaviors when faced with challenging situations. By filling out this form, you can gain valuable insights into your thought patterns, identify potential distortions, and develop strategies for managing your emotions and behaviors.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each question carefully and answer truthfully.
2. For each section, provide as much detail as possible.
3. Use the provided options for select-type questions or describe your thoughts and feelings in the free-text fields.
4. Be honest and accurate in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: false): This is the initial page that welcomes you to the form. Please take a moment to review the form's purpose and instructions.
* **Participant Information** (`2`, `note`, required: false): Provide your details, such as your name and any relevant contact information.
* **First Name** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Date** (`5`, `date`, required: true): Enter the current date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time** (`6`, `time`, required: false): Enter the current time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Situation** (`7`, `text`, required: true): Describe the situation that led to your automatic thought.
* **Mood Before** (`8`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose how you were feeling before the thought occurred:
	+ Happy
	+ Calm
	+ Neutral
	+ Anxious
	+ Sad
	+ Angry
	+ Frustrated
	+ Overwhelmed
	+ Other
* **Mood Intensity** (`9`, `number`, required: true): Rate your mood intensity from 1 to 10.
* **Automatic Thought** (`10`, `text`, required: true): Describe the automatic thought you experienced.
* **Thought Category** (`11`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the category of thought:
	+ All or Nothing Thinking
	+ Overgeneralization
	+ Mental Filter
	+ Disqualifying the Positive
	+ Jumping to Conclusions
	+ Mind Reading
	+ Fortune Telling
	+ Magnification or Minimization
	+ Emotional Reasoning
	+ Should Statements
	+ Labeling
	+ Personalization
	+ Other
* **Emotions Felt** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Check which emotions you experienced:
	+ Anxiety
	+ Sadness
	+ Anger
	+ Guilt
	+ Shame
	+ Fear
	+ Frustration
	+ Disappointment
	+ Loneliness
	+ Hopelessness
	+ Other
* **Emotion Intensity** (`13`, `number`, required: true): Rate your emotion intensity from 1 to 10.
* **Physical Sensations** (`14`, `text`, required: false): Describe any physical sensations you noticed.
* **Behavioral Response** (`15`, `text`, required: false): Describe your behavioral response to the thought.
* **Evidence Supporting the Thought** (`16`, `text`, required: false): List any evidence that supports the thought.
* **Evidence Against the Thought** (`17`, `text`, required: false): List any evidence that contradicts the thought.
* **Alternative or Balanced Thought** (`18`, `text`, required: true): Describe a more balanced perspective on the thought.
* **Alternative Belief** (`19`, `number`, required: false): Rate how much you believe the alternative thought from 1 to 100%.
* **Emotion After Reframing** (`20`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how you feel after reframing the thought:
	+ Much Better
	+ Somewhat Better
	+ Same
	+ Somewhat Worse
	+ Much Worse
* **Reframed Intensity** (`21`, `number`, required: false): Rate the intensity of your emotion after reframing from 1 to 10.
* **Cognitive Distortions** (`22`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Identify which cognitive distortions were present:
	+ Catastrophizing
	+ Black and White Thinking
	+ Personalization
	+ Control Fallacies
	+ Fairness Fallacy
	+ Blaming
	+ Always Being Right
	+ Heaven Reward Fallacy
	+ None Identified
* **Potential Triggers** (`23`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose potential triggers:
	+ Work Stress
	+ Relationship Issue
	+ Financial Concern
	+ Health Worry
	+ Past Trauma Reminder
	+ Fatigue
	+ Environmental Factor
	+ Other
* **Coping Strategies** (`24`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose coping strategies used:
	+ Deep Breathing
	+ Mindfulness
	+ Talking to Someone
	+ Journaling
	+ Exercise
	+ Meditation
	+ Distraction
	+ Problem Solving
	+ None
* **Lesson Learned** (`25`, `text`, required: false): Describe any lessons learned from the experience.
* **Action Plan** (`26`, `text`, required: false): Describe any action plan for future situations.
* **Therapist Notes** (`27`, `text`, required: false): For therapist use only.
* **Follow-Up Needed** (`28`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate if follow-up is required:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Maybe
* **Session Number** (`29`, `number`, required: false): If part of therapy, enter the session number.
* **Overall Insight** (`30`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional insights or observations.

## Tips

* Be truthful and honest in your responses.
* Take your time to answer each question thoroughly.
* Use the available space to fully describe your thoughts and feelings.
* If unsure or need help, ask your therapist or mental health professional for guidance.
