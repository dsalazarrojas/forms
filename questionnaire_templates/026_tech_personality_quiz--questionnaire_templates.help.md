# Tech Personality Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tech Personality Quiz is a tool designed to help users understand their unique tech personality type. It's not a formal psychological assessment, but rather a fun and interactive way to gain insight into what drives you, what motivates you, and what makes you tick in the tech industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Tech Personality Quiz, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page, and don't worry about the order of the questions.
2. Take your time, and be honest with yourself.
3. Choose the options that best fit your personality and work style.
4. Review your answers carefully, as they will help determine your tech personality type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: What Is Your Relationship With Tech?
* **What-is-your-relationship-with-tech** (`intro`, note, not required): This is the intro page to the quiz, and it's just to get you started. Please take a moment to read through it carefully.
 
### Page 2: How Much Experience With Tech?
* **how-much-experience-with-tech** (`experience`, select_one, not required): This question helps us understand your level of experience with technology. Choose the option that best describes your level of experience.
	+ Option 1: 0-2 years
	+ Option 2: 2-5 years
	+ Option 3: 5-10 years
	+ Option 4: 10+ years

### Page 3: What Type Of Tech Interests You?
* **what-type-of-tech-interests-you** (`interest`, select_multiple, not required): This question helps us understand what areas of tech interest you. Choose all that apply.
	+ Option 1: Artificial Intelligence
	+ Option 2: Cybersecurity
	+ Option 3: Data Science
	+ Option 4: Other (please specify)

### Page 4: What Tech Stacks Do You Know?
* **what-tech-stacks-you-know** (`tech_stack`, select_multiple, not required): This question helps us understand your technical skills. Choose all that apply.
	+ Option 1: Front-end Development
	+ Option 2: Back-end Development
	+ Option 3: Cloud Computing
	+ Option 4: Other (please specify)

### Page 5: What Motivates You?
* **what-motivates-you** (`motivation`, select_one, not required): This question helps us understand what motivates you. Choose the option that best describes your motivation.
	+ Option 1: Learning and growth
	+ Option 2: Creativity and innovation
	+ Option 3: Making a difference

### Page 6: How Do You Work?
* **how-do-you-work** (`work_style`, select_one, not required): This question helps us understand your work style. Choose the option that best describes your work style.
	+ Option 1: Autonomously
	+ Option 2: Collaboratively
	+ Option 3: Proactively

### Page 7: Prophets Or Practitioners?
* **prophets-or-practioners** (`collaboration`, select_one, not required): This question helps us understand your preferred role in a team. Choose the option that best describes your role.
	+ Option 1: Prophets (lead and innovate)
	+ Option 2: Practitioners (execute and improve)

### Page 8: What Makes You Tick?
* **what-makes-you-tick** (`personality`, select_one, not required): This question helps us understand your personality traits. Choose the option that best describes your personality.
	+ Option 1: Analytical
	+ Option 2: Artistic
	+ Option 3: Ambitious

### Page 9: What Are Your Tech Personality Traits?
* **what-are-your-tech-personality-traits** (`summary`, note, not required): This is the final page, where you'll get to see your tech personality type classification and a brief summary of your strengths and weaknesses in the tech industry.
