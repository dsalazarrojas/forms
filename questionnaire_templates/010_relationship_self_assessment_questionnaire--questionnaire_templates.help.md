# Relationship Self Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you evaluate your current relationship and identify areas for improvement. It will ask you about your relationship status, goals, conflicts, and feelings of support and dynamics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. For relationship type, select the type of relationship you currently have (romantic, platonic, or family).
3. For relationship goals, select all the goals that apply to your relationship.
4. For relationship support, select whether you feel supported or not by your partner.
5. For relationship dynamics, provide a description of the dynamics in your relationship.
6. For any additional comments, add any other thoughts or feelings you have about your relationship.

* **What is your current relationship status with this person**: This question asks for a brief description of your current relationship status with this specific person.
* **How long have you been in a relationship with this person**: This question asks for the length of time you have been in a relationship with this person.

* **What are some common conflicts in your relationship**: This question asks for a description of common conflicts in your relationship.
