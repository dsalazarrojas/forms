# Character Search And Filter Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users search and filter characters based on various criteria.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the search criteria you would like to apply to your search, such as character role, status, alignment, traits, or story arc appearance. Note that not all fields are required.
2. Enter the specific details for each selected criteria, such as the character's name or a keyword.
3. Confirm your search parameters in the "Confirmation of Search Criteria" field.
4. Optionally, specify any additional search comments or requirements.
5. Select your preferred method for sorting the results.
6. Choose whether to be notified of new matching characters.
7. Enter the date for the search log entry.
8. Finally, click the "Submit" button to execute the search.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Search by Character Name or Keyword**: <code>Enter the name or a part of the character's name to search for.</code>
* **Filter by Character Role**: <code>Select the role(s) you are looking for (Protagonist, Antagonist, Supporting Character, Mentor, Foil, etc.).</code>
* **Filter by Current Status**: <code>Select the character's status in the story (Active, Inactive, Deceased, Unknown).</code>
* **Filter by Moral Alignment**: <code>Select the character's moral stance (Lawful Good, Neutral Good, Chaotic Good, etc.).</code>
* **Filter by Primary Trait**: <code>Select a core characteristic to filter by (Brave and Courageous, Intelligent and Analytical, etc.).</code>
* **Filter by Power Level Range**: <code>Select the approximate power level (Low, Medium, High, God-Like).</code>
* **Filter by Place of Origin or Homeland**: <code>Enter the location from which the character originates.</code>
* **Filter by Story Arc Appearance**: <code>Select the story arc(s) where the character appears (Arc 1 - The Beginning, Arc 2 - The Conflict, etc.).</code>
* **Results Sorting Preference**: <code>Select how you would like the results to be ordered (Alphabetical, Most Recent Update, By Power Level, etc.).</code>
* **Confirmation of Search Criteria**: <code>Review and confirm your search parameters before executing the search.</code>
* **Additional Search Comments**: <code>Enter any other specific requirements or details for your search.</code>
* **Notification Request New Matches**: <code>Choose whether to be notified of new matching characters.</code>
* **Date of Search Log Entry**: <code>Enter the current date for the search log entry.</code>
* **Additional Search Comments**: <code>Enter any other specific requirements or details for your search.</code>

## Tips

* Use this form to search for characters based on specific criteria.
* Review and adjust your search parameters as needed before confirming and executing the search.
* Be sure to confirm your search parameters before submitting the form to avoid unintended searches.
* Use the "Additional Search Comments" field to enter any specific requirements or details for your search.
* Choose your preferred results sorting order for an efficient and organized search.
