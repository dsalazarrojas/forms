# Wedding Ceremony Script Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wedding Ceremony Script Questionnaire is a form used to collect essential information from couples getting married to create a personalized, heartfelt, and memorable script for their special day. This form guides you through the steps to capture the details you'd like to include in your ceremony script, making it a treasured part of your wedding celebration.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the form on your computer or mobile device.
2. Select each page of the form by clicking the navigation buttons or links.
3. Fill out the fields for each page, following the guidelines and explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, not required): Please enter your first name as you'd like it to be displayed in the ceremony script.
	+ Note: You can enter your name as you prefer it to be pronounced or spelled.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, not required): Enter your last name as you'd like it to be displayed in the ceremony script.
	+ Note: You can enter your name as you prefer it to be pronounced or spelled.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address to receive the completed ceremony script and any updates.
	+ Note: We'll use this email to keep you updated on your ceremony script progress.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, not required): Enter your phone number to receive any urgent updates or reminders.
	+ Note: We'll use this number to keep you informed about your ceremony script.
