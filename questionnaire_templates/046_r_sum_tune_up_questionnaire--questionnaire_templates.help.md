# Resume 'tune up' questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of filling out the "Resume 'tune up' questionnaire" form.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the corresponding text field as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
2. For fields with no options provided, you can enter any information you feel is relevant.
3. For fields with required labels, please ensure you fill in the information.
4. After filling out all the questions, you can review your answers before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Info**: Please enter your personal details, such as your name, contact information, and any other relevant information you'd like to include.
* **Work Experience**: List your previous work experience, including job titles, company names, dates of employment, and a brief description of your job responsibilities.
* **Education**: Provide a list of your educational background, including degrees earned, institutions attended, and dates of attendance.
* **Job Objectives**: Describe your career goals and what you aim to achieve in your next role.
* **Skills**: List any relevant skills you possess, such as programming languages, tools, or certifications.
* **References**: Provide the names and contact information of your professional references.
* **Resume Attachment**: Upload your resume for us to review.

## Tips

* Make sure to proofread your answers for spelling, grammar, and formatting errors.
* Be as thorough as possible when filling out each field.
* If you need to add more information or clarify any of your answers, you can attach additional documents or notes as needed.
