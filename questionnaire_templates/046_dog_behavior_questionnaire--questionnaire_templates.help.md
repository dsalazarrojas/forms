# Dog Behavior Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about a dog's behavior and medical history to provide the best possible care for your pet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the instructions and ensuring that this form is not a duplicate of any other form in our system.
2. Fill out the form in order from top to bottom.
3. Provide accurate information about your dog in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dog Name** (`pet_name`, text, optional): Please provide the name of your dog.
* **Dog Age** (`pet_age`, number, optional): Enter your dog's age in years.
* **Dog Breed** (`pet_breed`, text, optional): Specify your dog's breed (e.g. Labrador, Poodle, etc.).
* **Owner's Phone Number** (`owner_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Owner's Email** (`owner_email`, email, optional): Provide your email address for further communication.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Describe any medical conditions or health issues your dog has had in the past.
* **Behavioral Issues** (`behavioral_issues`, select multiple, optional):
	+ **Separation anxiety**: Select if your dog experiences anxiety when you're away.
	+ **Fear of strangers**: Select if your dog is fearful of people they don't know.
	+ **Fear of noise**: Select if your dog is startled by loud noises.
	+ **Fear of water**: Select if your dog is uneasy around water.
	+ **Other**: Select if your dog has any other behavioral issues not listed above.

## Tips
* Please be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask for help or clarification.
* This form is only for gathering information about your dog, and it will help us provide better care and services.
