Below is a comprehensive help guide to assist end-users in completing the form accurately and effectively.

# Real Estate Marketing Strategy Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect detailed information about your real estate marketing strategies. This information will help us better understand your current approaches and provide you with personalized guidance on how to improve them.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and thoroughly.
2. Select the relevant options for select one and select multiple fields.
3. Enter accurate dates and times if applicable.
4. Fill in the required fields (if any).
5. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

* **More options** (9): Choose one or more of the provided options (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).

* **Second decimal** (13): Enter a decimal number with up to two decimal places.
* **Date range** (14): Select a start and end date from the calendar or enter them in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

**Tips**
