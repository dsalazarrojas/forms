# Chronic Illness Lifestyle Support Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Chronic Illness Lifestyle Support Evaluation Form is designed to gather information from individuals living with chronic illnesses. It is used to assess your lifestyle habits, support system, and self-management practices.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Full Name**: Enter your full name, if you wish to do so. If you do not wish to share your name, you can leave it blank.
2. **Email Address**: Provide your email address for follow-up communication.
3. **Age Group**: Select your age range.
4. **Gender**: Choose your gender.
5. **Chronic Condition**: Select your primary chronic condition.
6. **How Long Diagnosed**: Select the time since your condition's diagnosis.
7. **Current Treatment**: Choose the type of treatment you are receiving.
8. **Diet Quality**: Rate how you would rate your diet.
9. **Physical Activity**: Select how active you are weekly.
10. **Sleep Quality**: Rate your sleep quality.
11. **Stress Level**: Select your typical stress level.
12. **Stress Management**: Select all the ways you manage stress.
13. **Social Connections**: Choose how connected you feel.
14. **Support System**: Select if you have a support system.
15. **Support Type Needed**: Choose the type of support you need.
16. **Health Literacy**: Rate how well you understand your condition.
17. **Self-Efficacy**: Select how confident you are in managing your condition.
18. **Motivation Level**: Choose how motivated you are.
19. **Barriers to Healthy Living**: Select all the barriers you face.
20. **Goals for Improvement**: Choose all the areas you want to improve.
21. **Program Interest**: Select how interested you are in support programs.
22. **Preferred Support Format**: Choose how you would like to receive support.
23. **Quality of Life Rating**: Rate your current quality of life.
24. **Biggest Challenge**: Describe your biggest daily challenge.
25. **Additional Support Needed**: Describe any additional support you need.
26. **Additional Comments**: Provide any other feedback.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and thoroughly.
* Ensure you have the necessary time and privacy to complete this form.
* If you are unsure or have any questions, please contact form@gic.mx.
