# health_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The health_survey form is designed to collect health-related information from patients to facilitate accurate and informed care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the First Name field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the Last Name field. This is a required field.
3. Enter your valid **Email** in the Email field. This is a required field.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the Phone field.
5. Describe your **Medical History** in the Medical History field.
6. List any **Symptoms** you are experiencing in the Symptoms field.
7. Indicate your current **Diagnosis** in the Diagnosis field.
8. Provide your **Treatment Plan** if applicable.
9. Select any relevant **Medical Conditions** you have. This field allows you to choose multiple conditions.
10. If you take **Medication**, enter the name of your medication and dosage information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name for proper identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name for proper identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address to facilitate communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Describe any previous medical experiences or conditions.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, optional): List any current symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Diagnosis** (`diagnosis`, text, optional): Indicate your current diagnosis or medical condition.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, text, optional): Provide any relevant treatment plans or schedules.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, optional): Select any relevant medical conditions you have.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): Enter the name of your medication and dosage information if applicable.

## Tips
* Ensure accuracy and completeness in filling out the form to provide the best possible care.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your healthcare provider for clarification.
* This form is for informational purposes only and should not be used for other uses without permission.
