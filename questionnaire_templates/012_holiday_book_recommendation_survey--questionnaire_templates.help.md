# Holiday Book Recommendation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information on your preferred holiday and your thoughts on prophets and heroes related to that holiday. This data will help authors or publishers get insights on what type of holiday-themed books to write or promote next.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of this form in the "Holiday Book Recommendation Survey Form Title" field.
2. Select your preferred holiday from the list of options provided in the "Preferred Holiday" field.
3. In the "Prophets and Heroes" field, type your thoughts on the prophets and heroes associated with your preferred holiday.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Holiday Book Recommendation Survey Form Title** (`holiday_book_recommendation_survey_form_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title for this form.
* **Preferred Holiday** (`preferred_holiday`, select_one, required): Select the holiday that you prefer from the list of options.
* **Prophets and Heroes** (`prophets_and_heroes`, text, required): Share your thoughts on the prophets and heroes associated with your preferred holiday.
