# Monat Hair Consultation Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Monat Hair Consultation Quiz is designed to help us better understand your hair characteristics and preferences to provide personalized recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing detailed and accurate information about your hair.
2.  Take your time, as this form may take a few minutes to complete.
3.  Don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or need further guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Hair Problems** (`hair_problems`, text, optional): Describe any issues you are experiencing with your hair, such as frizzy hair.
*   **Hair Type** (`hair_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of hair you have, including curly, wavy, or straight.
*   **Hair Color** (`hair_color`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the color(s) of your hair, including blonde, brunette, redhead, gray, or other.
*   **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Select your age group to help us understand your lifestyle and hair growth rate.
*   **Hair Concerns** (`hair_concerns`, text, optional): Describe any specific hair concerns you have, such as dryness, brittleness, or oiliness.
*   **Previous Products** (`previous_products`, select_multiple, optional): Choose any hair care products you have previously used, including Monat tone, Monat shine, Monat hair, Monat pro, Monat fusion, or Monat dry shampoo.
*   **Have Used Monat** (`have_used_monat`, select_one, optional): Answer yes or no if you have used Monat products before.
*   **Have Shared Monat** (`have_shared_monat`, select_multiple, optional): Answer yes or no if you have shared Monat products with others.
*   **Hair Type 1** (`hair_type_1`, select_one, optional): Select the type of hair you have for each subsequent section of the form, if applicable.
*   **Hair Color 1** (`hair_color_1`, select_one, optional): Select the color of your hair for each subsequent section of the form, if applicable.
*   **Natural Ingredients** (`natural_ingredients`, select_one, optional): Answer yes or no if you are looking for natural ingredients in your hair care products.
*   **Hair Growth Rate** (`hair_growth_rate`, select_one, optional): Select the rate at which your hair grows.
*   **Previous Products 1** (`previous_products_1`, select_multiple, optional): Choose any hair care products you have previously used for each subsequent section of the form, if applicable.
*   **Monat Products 1** (`monat_products_1`, select_multiple, optional): Choose Monat products you have used for each subsequent section of the form, if applicable.
*   **Monat Fusion** (`monat_fusion`, text, optional): Describe your experience with Monat fusion, if applicable.
*   **Hair Problems 1** (`hair_problems_1`, select_one, optional): Select any hair problems you are experiencing for each subsequent section of the form, if applicable.
*   **Have Used Monat Fusion** (`have_used_monat_fusion`, select_one, optional): Answer yes or no if you have used Monat fusion before.
*   **Hair Problems 2** (`hair_problems_2`, select_one, optional): Select any hair problems you are experiencing for each subsequent section of the form, if applicable.
*   **Previous Monat Products 1** (`previous_monat_products_1`, select_multiple, optional): Choose Monat products you have previously used for each subsequent section of the form, if applicable.
