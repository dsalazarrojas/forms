# New Mentee Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from new mentees participating in a mentorship program. The purpose of this form is to gather demographic and interest-based information to better understand their background, goals, and preferences. This information will be used to pair mentees with suitable mentors and program resources.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in your first and last name.
*   Provide your email address.
*   Enter your phone number.
*   Select your preferred method of communication (SMS, Call, or Email).
*   Choose your preferred language (English, Spanish, French, etc.).
*   Enter your start and end dates for the program.
*   Select the mentorship programs you are interested in.
*   Choose your goals (Personal, Professional, Educational, etc.).
*   Select your interests (Art, Music, Sports, etc.).
*   Enter your availability (start and end dates for your work schedule).
*   Choose your preferred work schedule (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, etc.).
*   Select the days you are available to work (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, etc.).
*   Choose your time zone.
*   Enter your preferred work hours.
*   Select your preferred days to work (Monday, Tuesday, etc.).
*   Enter your preferred end hours for work.
*   Upload your profile picture.
*   Upload your resume.
*   Provide a reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your given first name.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your given last name.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
*   **Preferred Communication Method** (`preferred_communication_method`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose how you prefer to communicate (SMS, Call, or Email).
*   **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your preferred language (English, Spanish, French, etc.).
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your expected start date.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your expected end date.
*   **Mentorship Programs** (`mentorship_programs`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the mentorship programs you are interested in.
*   **Goals** (`goals`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose your personal, professional, educational, or social goals.
*   **Mentee Interests** (`mentee_interests`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose your interests (Art, Music, Sports, etc.).
*   **Availability** (`availability`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your availability (start and end dates).
*   **Preferred Work Schedule** (`preferred_work_schedule`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your preferred work schedule (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, etc.).
*   **Preferred Work Days** (`preferred_work_days`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the days you are available to work (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, etc.).
*   **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your time zone.
*   **Availability Time Zone** (`availability_time_zone`, `time`, required/optional): Enter your time zone.
*   **Availability Start** (`availability_start`, `time`, required/optional): Enter your start availability time.
*   **Availability End** (`availability_end`, `time`, required/optional): Enter your end availability time.
*   **Availability Notes** (`availability_notes`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any notes about your availability.
*   **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose your preferred work hours.
*   **Work Days** (`work_days`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the days you are available to work.
*   **Work Hours End** (`work_hours_end`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose your preferred end work hours.
*   **Mentee Profile Picture** (`mentee_profile_picture`, `text`, required/optional): Upload your profile picture.
*   **Mentee Resume** (`mentee_resume`, `text`, required/optional): Upload your resume.
*   **Reference** (`mentee_reference`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a reference.

## Tips

*   Make sure to complete all the required fields for a successful submission.
*   Be accurate when selecting your preferred language to ensure you receive the correct materials.
*   Clearly choose your availability to ensure you are matched with suitable mentors.
*   Enter your preferred work schedule and hours to ensure you receive accurate work assignments.
*   Choose your preferred days of work to ensure you are matched with suitable mentors.
*   Enter your preferred end availability time to ensure you receive accurate work assignments.
*   Provide clear and accurate contact information.
*   Upload a recent and clear profile picture.
