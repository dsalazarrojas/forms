# Personal Training Registration Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users who are interested in taking part in a personal training program. The collected data will be used to create a personalized training plan tailored to the user's needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out this form with the required contact information (First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone).
2. Provide your health history, including any medical conditions you may have.
3. Specify your current and ideal fitness levels.
4. Select the activities you currently enjoy.
5. Enter your personal data, including date of birth and height.
6. Enter your weight.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`id: 1`, `label: Contact Information`): This field is used to collect your contact details, including First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* **First Name** (`id: 2`, `label: First Name`): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`id: 3`, `label: Last Name`): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`id: 4`, `label: Email`): Enter your email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone** (`id: 5`, `label: Phone`): Enter your phone number that you would like to be contacted on.
* **Health History** (`id: 6`, `label: Health History`): Describe any medical conditions you have, e.g., high blood pressure, diabetes, etc.
* **Health History 1** (`id: 7`, `label: Health History 1`): Describe any medical conditions you have, e.g., high blood pressure, diabetes, etc.
* **Medical Conditions** (`id: 8`, `label: Medical Conditions`): Describe any medical conditions you have, e.g., high blood pressure, diabetes, etc.
* **Current Medications** (`id: 9`, `label: Current Medications`): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Fitness Goals** (`id: 10`, `label: Fitness Goals`): Describe your overall fitness goals, e.g., lose weight, run a marathon, etc.
* **What is your current fitness level?** (`id: 11`, `label: What is your current fitness level?`): Select your current fitness level from High, Medium, or Low.
* **What is your ideal fitness level?** (`id: 12`, `label: What is your ideal fitness level?`): Select your ideal fitness level from High, Medium, or Low.
* **What activities do you currently enjoy?** (`id: 13`, `label: What activities do you currently enjoy?`): Select all activities you currently enjoy, e.g., Running, Yoga, Cycling, Hiking, Gym.
* **Personal Data** (`id: 14`, `label: Personal Data`): This field is for any additional personal information you would like to share.
* **Date of Birth** (`id: 15`, `label: Date of Birth`): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Height** (`id: 16`, `label: Height`): Enter your height in feet and inches.
* **Weight** (`id: 17`, `label: Weight`): Enter your weight in pounds.

Please note that this form is for internal use only, and the data collected will be used solely for the purpose of providing training services.
