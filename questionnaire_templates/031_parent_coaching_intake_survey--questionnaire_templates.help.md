# Parent Coaching Intake Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Coaching Intake Survey is a comprehensive form designed to gather information about your parenting goals, support systems, and strategies. By filling out this form, you'll be able to identify areas of improvement and develop a tailored plan to achieve your goals for your child's development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Be as specific as possible when describing your goals and strategies.
3. Don't hesitate to elaborate on any question if you need more space.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What are your parenting goals for your child?
* **What are your parenting goals for your child?** (`What are your parenting goals for your child?`, text, required/optional)
This question helps us understand your goals and objectives for your child's development. Please describe what you hope to achieve through our coaching sessions.
