# Categorical Variable Relationship Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather data on the relationship between two variables, A and B. It is intended for survey respondents to provide their perceptions and opinions on this relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Respondent ID (optional).
2. Select your Age Group.
3. Choose your Gender.
4. Select your Education Level.
5. Choose your Employment Status.
6. Select your Income Bracket (if applicable).
7. Choose where you live (Location Type).
8. Select your Geographic Region.
9. Answer questions about your perception of Variable A and Variable B.
10. Indicate your perception of the relationship between Variable A and Variable B.
11. Choose how strong you see the relationship between Variable A and Variable B (if applicable).
12. Select how confident you are in your assessment.
13. Choose how familiar you are with the topic (Prior Knowledge).
14. If you want to specify, choose factors that influence your view (Factors Influencing Your View).
15. If you wish, provide an explanation for your answers to Variable A and Variable B.
16. If you have additional insights or suggestions, provide other variables to consider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent ID** (`respondent_id`, text, optional): This is an optional field where you can enter your unique identifier. Please note that this information will not be used for anything other than to help us understand which survey responses came from you.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, required): Select your age range to provide contextual information about your answers.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Choose your gender identity to help us understand your demographics.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, required): Select your highest level of education completed to give context to your answers.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required): Choose your current employment situation.
* **Income Bracket** (`income_bracket`, select_one, optional): If applicable, select your annual household income bracket.
* **Location Type** (`location_type`, select_one, required): Choose where you live.
* **Geographic Region** (`region`, select_one, required): Select your geographic region.
* **Variable A Perception** (`variable_a`, select_one, required): Choose your view on Variable A.
* **Variable B Perception** (`variable_b`, select_one, required): Choose your view on Variable B.
* **Perceived Relationship** (`perceived_relationship`, select_one, required): Select whether you see a relationship between Variables A and B.
* **Relationship Strength** (`relationship_strength`, select_one, optional): If applicable, choose how strong you see the relationship between Variables A and B.
* **Confidence Level** (`confidence_level`, select_one, required): Select how confident you are in your assessment.
* **Prior Knowledge** (`prior_knowledge`, select_one, required): Choose how familiar you are with the topic.
* **Factors Influencing Your View** (`factors_influencing`, select_multiple, optional): If you want to specify, choose factors that influence your view.
* **Explanation of Your View** (`explanation`, text, optional): If you wish, provide an explanation for your answers to Variable A and Variable B.
* **Other Variables to Consider** (`additional_variables`, text, optional): If you have additional insights or suggestions, provide other variables to consider.
* **Survey Feedback** (`survey_feedback`, text, optional): If you have any comments on this survey, please share them.
