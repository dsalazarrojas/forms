# Virtual Portfolio Preparation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information about your virtual portfolio. Please fill out this form to provide details about your portfolio, which will help us better understand your work and prepare your portfolio for further evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with accurate and relevant information.
2. Make sure to answer all the required fields (if any).
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Information (1)
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide your name and any other relevant information about yourself as a participant.

### Portfolio Type (2)
* **Portfolio Type** (`portfolio_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of portfolio you have prepared for this survey. You can select one of the options: Type A, Type B, or Type C.

### Portfolio URL (3)
* **Portfolio URL** (`portfolio_url`, text, optional): If you have a URL for your portfolio, enter it here. This will allow us to access your portfolio directly.

### Description (4)
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your portfolio, explaining its content and relevance to this survey.

### Date Last Updated (5)
* **Date Last Updated** (`date_last_updated`, date, optional): Enter the date when you last updated your portfolio. This will help us track the evolution of your work over time.

### Date Submitted (6)
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, optional): Enter the date when you submitted your portfolio for evaluation.

### Contact Info (7)
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide any contact information you would like us to use to get in touch with you regarding your portfolio.

### Portfolio Comments (8)
* **Portfolio Comments** (`portfolio_comments`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about your portfolio.
