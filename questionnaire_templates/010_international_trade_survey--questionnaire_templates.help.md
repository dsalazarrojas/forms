# International Trade Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about international trade activities. The purpose of this form is to gather data on the markets served, trade volumes, challenges faced, and other relevant details from international trade participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all the markets you serve from the provided options.
2. Enter the trade volumes for each market you have selected.
3. Describe any challenges you have faced in your international trade activities.
4. (Optional) Add any additional notes or comments about your experiences.
5. Enter your email address.
6. (Optional) Enter your phone number.
7. Select the date you completed this survey.
8. (Optional) Select the time you completed this survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **market_served** (Select, required): This field asks you to select all the markets you serve. Please choose the relevant countries from the provided options.
* **trade_volumes** (Number, required): Enter the trade volumes for each market you have selected. This is a numerical field where you can enter values such as quantities or amounts.
* **challenges** (Text, required): Describe any challenges you have faced in your international trade activities. This is a text field where you can provide detailed information about your experiences.
* **note** (Note, optional): This field is for any additional notes or comments you would like to add. This is not a required field, so feel free to use it if you have any extra information to share.
* **email** (Email, required): Enter your email address. This will be used to contact you about your survey responses.
* **phone** (Text, optional): If you would like to provide your phone number, please enter it here. This is not required, but it will help us contact you if needed.
* **date** (Date, required): Select the date you completed this survey. Please use the date picker to choose the correct date.
* **time** (Time, optional): If you would like to provide the time you completed this survey, please select it from the provided options. This is not required, but it will help us understand when you responded.
