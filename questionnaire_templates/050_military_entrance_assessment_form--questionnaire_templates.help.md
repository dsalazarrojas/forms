# Military Entrance Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals seeking to join the military or assess their readiness for military service. It aims to gather relevant details and insights to facilitate a smooth and accurate assessment process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form by providing accurate and honest information about yourself.
2. Answer all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the field label.
3. You can skip optional fields if you don't have the information or if they don't apply to your situation.
4. Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Details**: (`applicant_details`, text, required): This is the main section where you'll provide general information about yourself, such as your name, age, and contact details. Be sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Readiness Insights** (`readiness_insights`, text, optional): If you have any relevant insights or information that might impact your assessment, please share them here. This could include medical conditions, skills, or experiences that might affect your ability to serve.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): If you have any additional information or context that you think is relevant to your assessment, please provide it here. This could include your motivations for joining the military, any questions or concerns you have, or any other relevant details.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out the form in a quiet and private space, free from distractions.
* If you need help or have questions, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.
* Remember to review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* This form is for informational purposes only and will not be used for commercial purposes.
