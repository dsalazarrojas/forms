* **What is your risk tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`):

* **Investment Horizon** (`investment_horizon`):
	* This field asks you to select your investment horizon, which is the time frame in which you plan to hold onto your investment. Consider your time horizon and how long you are willing to wait for returns.
* **Financial Goals** (`financial_goals`):
	* This field asks you to select your financial goals, which can include:
		+ Option 1
		+ Option 2
		+ Option 3
* **Risk Appetite Score** (`risk_appetite_score`):
	* This field asks you to enter a number between 0 and 100, where 0 is very low and 100 is very high, to rate your risk appetite.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`):
	* This field asks you to enter your full name.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`):
	* This field asks you to enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`):
	* This field asks you to enter your phone number.
* **Additional Comments** (`risk_appetite_comment`):
	* This field is for any additional comments or information you'd like to share.
