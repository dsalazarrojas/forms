# cricket_knowledge_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

The 'cricket_knowledge_quiz' form is designed to collect participant details and assess their knowledge about the 2021-2022 ICL season. This form is used for internal evaluation and educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the 'Participant Details' page with your name and any other relevant contact information.
2. On the 'Quiz Questions' page, select your answer to the question about the 2021-2022 ICL season.
3. Enter your score if you think you scored well in the quiz.
4. Optionally, provide your email address and phone number for further contact.
5. Add any additional notes if you have any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (Participant Details, text, optional):
    This page is used to collect your name and contact information. Enter your name, and optionally, your email address and phone number.
* **Quiz Questions** (Quiz Questions, select multiple, optional):
    Select 'Yes' if you think you scored well in the quiz, or 'No' if not.
* **Participant Score** (Participant Score, number, optional):
    If you answered the quiz questions well, enter your score here. This will help us understand your knowledge level.
* **What is the current season of the 2021-2022 ICL?** (Participant Score Hint, select one, optional):
    Select the correct season from the options provided. You can choose from '2021-2022', '2020-2021', or '2019-2020'.
* **Score** (Participant Score Hint, text, optional):
    Add a brief description if you want to provide more information about your score.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional):
    Enter your email address for further contact if you wish.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional):
    Enter your phone number for further contact if you wish.
* **Note** (Note, note, optional):
    Add any additional notes if you want to provide more context.
