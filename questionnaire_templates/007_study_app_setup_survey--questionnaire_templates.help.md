# Study App Setup Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic contact information and preferences for setting up a study app.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Please provide your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Please provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Please provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select your study plan from the "Study Plan" field.
6. Select your reminder preferences from the "Reminder Preferences" field.
7. Select any accessibility needs from the "Accessibility Needs" field.
8. Provide any additional needs or comments in the "Other Needs" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address. This field will be used for communication.
* **Phone Number** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Study Plan** (select_one): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Plan A
	+ Plan B
	+ Plan C
* **Reminder Preferences** (select_multiple): Choose multiple options that apply:
	+ pref A
	+ pref B
	+ pref C
	+ pref D
* **Accessibility Needs** (select_multiple): Choose multiple options that apply:
	+ access A
	+ access B
	+ access C
	+ access D
* **Other Needs** (text): Provide any additional needs or comments.
