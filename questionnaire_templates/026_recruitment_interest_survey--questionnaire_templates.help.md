# Recruitment Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Recruitment Interest Survey is a form designed to help us understand your background, preferences, and interests to match you with suitable job openings. This information will help us get to know you better and improve our recruitment process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with your true and accurate information.
2. For fields that have multiple options, select all the options that apply to you.
3. If you need help with any field, use the hints provided for guidance.
4. Once you've completed all the fields, submit the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name**: Enter your full name, as we would like to get to know you by your name.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your work or personal email address where we can reach you.
* **Job Title**: Describe the kind of job you are interested in or have experience in. This could be a specific job title or an industry you are interested in.
* **Preferred Industry**: Select the industries you are interested in working in, from the options provided. 
  - **Advertising**
  - **Consulting**
  - **Finance**
  - **Healthcare**
  - **Information Technology**
  - **Manufacturing**
  - **Marketing**
  - **Retail**
  - **Software**
  - **Other** (please specify)
* **Education Level**: Select all the educational levels you have completed or are interested in pursuing.
  - **Bachelor's**
  - **Master's**
  - **Ph.D.**
  - **Certificate**
* **Work Experience**: Select the range of years you have worked.
  - **0-2 years**
  - **2-5 years**
  - **5-10 years**
  - **More than 10 years**
* **Job Status**: Select your current job status.
  - **Part-time**
  - **Full-time**
  - **Intern**
  - **Contract**
  - **Other**
* **Salary**: Enter your expected salary range. Please use numbers only.
* **Location**: Enter the location you are interested in or willing to work in.
* **Availability**: Select the time you are available to work.
  - (Please use the available time options)
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your work or personal phone number where we can reach you.
* **Preferred Skills**: Select all the skills you have or are interested in developing.
  - **Communication**
  - **Leadership**
  - **Problem-solving**
  - **Time management**
  - **Team management**
  - **Project management**
  - **Analytical skills**
  - **Other** (please specify)
* **Work Schedule**: Select your preferred work schedule.
  - **Mon-Fri**
  - **Tue-Thu**
  - **Mon-Thu**
  - **Mon-Sun**
  - **Fri-Sun**
  - **Other** (please specify)

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us.
* This form is for your information and will not be shared with anyone outside of our organization.
