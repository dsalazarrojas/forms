# Identify Your Core Emotional Pain - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you better understand your emotional pain and its impact on your life. It will guide you through a series of questions to identify the frequency, intensity, and areas affected by your emotional pain. The goal is to help you develop a plan to address it and improve your overall well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly, and take your time to provide detailed descriptions where needed.
2. Select the options that best match your experiences and feelings.
3. Be as specific as possible when describing your emotional pain and its effects on your life.
4. If you need help with a particular question or area, feel free to skip or come back to it later.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Core Emotional Pain** (`core_emotional_pain`): Describe your current emotional pain in a few sentences. Try to be as specific as possible about what you're feeling and why.
* **Describe your current emotional pain** (`core_emotional_pain_description`): Please provide a detailed description of your emotional pain, including any triggers, patterns, or circumstances that contribute to it.
* **How often does it occur?** (`emotional_pain_frequency`): Choose a frequency that best describes how often you experience your emotional pain: Always, Frequently, Rarely, or Never.
* **What is the intensity of your emotional pain?** (`emotional_pain_intensity`): Select an intensity level that best matches your experiences: Mild, Moderate, Severe, or Not at all.
* **Which areas of pain are you experiencing?** (`pain_areas`): Choose all areas affected by your emotional pain: Physical, Emotional, Mental, Spiritual, Financial, Relationship, Work, or Other (please specify).
* **Why are you experiencing this emotional pain?** (`pain_reason`): Describe the reasons behind your emotional pain, including any underlying causes or contributing factors.
* **How do you plan to address it?** (`self_reflection`): Share your thoughts on how you plan to address your emotional pain, including any steps you've taken or plans you have in place.
* **How has it affected your life?** (`pain_reduction`): Select the impact your emotional pain has had on your life: Significantly, Moderately, Mildly, or Not at all.
* **Which areas of life have been affected?** (`life_areas`): Choose all areas affected by your emotional pain: Work, Relationships, Finances, Health, Hobbies, Leisure, Travel, Education, Career, Personal growth, or Other (please specify).
* **How do you think the pain will affect you in the next 6 months?** (`pain_assessment`): Select how you anticipate your emotional pain will affect you in the next 6 months: Much worse, A bit worse, The same, A bit better, or Much better.
* **What impact does your emotional pain have on your relationships?** (`emotional_pain_impact`): Describe how your emotional pain affects your relationships, including any challenges or difficulties you've faced.
* **Do you have support from others?** (`emotional_pain_support`): Select whether you have support from others: Yes or No.
* **What plan do you have to address it?** (`emotional_pain_plan`): Share your thoughts on how you plan to address your emotional pain, including any steps you've taken or plans you have in place.
* **What would you like to achieve?** (`pain_reduction_goal`): Describe your goals for addressing your emotional pain, including any desired outcomes or improvements.
* **On a scale of 1-10, how bad does it feel?** (`core_emotional_pain_score`): Rate the intensity of your emotional pain on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **How often do you reflect on it?** (`self_reflection_frequency`): Select how often you reflect on your emotional pain: Often, Rarely, or Never.
