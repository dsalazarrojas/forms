# Supervisor Leadership Skills Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the leadership skills of a supervisor or manager.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Supervisor Title in the "Supervisor Title" field.
5. Rate your feedback frequency in the "Feedback Frequency" field.
6. Enter the date of the last time you received feedback in the "Feedback Date" field.
7. Choose the time of the last time you received feedback in the "Feedback Time" field.
8. Select the type of feedback you typically receive in the "Feedback Type" field.
9. Enter any comments or notes about feedback you've received in the "Comments" field.
10. Enter any additional comments or thoughts in the "Additional Comments" field.
11. Rate your overall leadership skills using the rating scale in the "Rating Scale" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required/Optional): This field is for your first name. Please enter your full first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required/Optional): This field is for your last name. Please enter your full last name.
* **Email** (Email, Required/Optional): This field is for your work email address. Please enter your work email address.
* **Supervisor Title** (Text, Required/Optional): This field is for your supervisor title or position. Please enter your title.
* **Feedback Frequency** (Text, Required/Optional): This field is for your feedback frequency. Please rate how often you receive feedback, e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, etc.
* **Feedback Date** (Date, Required/Optional): This field is for the date of the last time you received feedback. Please select a date.
* **Feedback Time** (Time, Required/Optional): This field is for the time of the last time you received feedback. Please select a time of day.
* **Feedback Type** (Text, Required/Optional): This field is for the type of feedback you typically receive. Please select the type of feedback you usually get, e.g., from manager, colleague, or self.
* **Comments** (Text, Required/Optional): This field is for any comments or notes about feedback you've received. Please enter any relevant comments.
* **Additional Comments** (Text, Required/Optional): This field is for any additional comments or thoughts you have. Please enter any extra information that you think is relevant.
* **Overall Rating** (Number, Required/Optional): This field is for your overall rating of your leadership skills. Please rate your skills using the provided scale.
* **Rating Scale** (Number, Required/Optional): This field is for the provided rating scale. Please refer to the scale to make your rating.
* **Feedback Date** (Date, Required/Optional): This field is for the date of the last time you received feedback. This field is likely to be used to track the date of your last feedback session.
* **Feedback Type** (Text, Required/Optional): This field is for the type of feedback you typically receive. This field is likely to be used to track the type of feedback you usually get.
* **Overall Rating** (Number, Required/Optional): This field is for your overall rating of your leadership skills. This field is likely to be used to track your progress and areas for improvement.
