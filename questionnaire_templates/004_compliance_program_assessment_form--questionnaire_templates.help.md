# Compliance Program Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Compliance Program Assessment Form is designed to evaluate the compliance program of an organization, focusing on its key aspects such as risk assessment, monitoring, reporting, staff training, and overall performance. This form helps program assessors provide a comprehensive and structured evaluation of the program's strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by providing general program details in the "Program Overview" section.
2. Enter your name and title as the assessor in the "Assessor name" field.
3. Enter the name of the compliance program in the "Program name" field.
4. In the "Scope and objectives" section, provide a brief description of the program's scope.
5. Evaluate the clarity of the program's governance structures in the "Governance and ownership" section.
6. Assess the effectiveness of the program's risk assessment practices in the "Risk assessment practices" section.
7. Evaluate the frequency and quality of monitoring and reporting in the "Monitoring and reporting" section.
8. Rate the adequacy of staff training efforts in the "Training and communication" section.
9. (Optional) Outline suggested corrective actions for improvement in the "Improvement actions" section.
10. Finally, provide a summary rating for the program in the "Overall assessment score" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Assessor name** (`assessor_name`, text, required): Please enter your name and title as the person completing the assessment.
- **Program name** (`program_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the compliance program being assessed.
- **Program Overview** (`section_overview`, note, optional): This section is a brief description of the program's overview.
- **Governance and ownership** (`governance_and_ownership`, select_one, required): Rate the clarity of the program's governance structures as Clear, Moderate, or Unclear.
- **Risk assessment practices** (`risk_assessment_practices`, select_one, required): Evaluate the effectiveness of the program's risk assessment practices as Effective, Partially effective, or Ineffective.
- **Monitoring and reporting** (`monitoring_and_reporting`, select_one, required): Rate the frequency and quality of monitoring and reporting as Regular, Sporadic, or None.
- **Training and communication** (`training_and_communication`, select_one, required): Rate the adequacy of staff training efforts as Sufficient, Needs improvement, or Insufficient.
- **Improvement actions** (`improvement_actions`, text, optional): If necessary, provide a brief description of suggested corrective actions for improvement.
- **Overall assessment score** (`overall_assessment_score`, select_one, required): Provide a summary rating for the program's overall performance as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.

## Tips
- Please be specific and objective when evaluating the program's aspects.
- Use concrete examples or anecdotes to support your assessments.
- Keep your answers brief and focused on key points.
- This form is a tool for constructive feedback and improvement; please provide actionable suggestions for enhancement.
