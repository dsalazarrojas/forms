# Creative Writing Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative Writing Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from users on their experiences with a particular creative writing prompt or exercise.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please make sure to use a valid email address to ensure you can receive any follow-up communications.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This is optional, but it can be helpful for the form owner to contact you if they need more information.
5. Enter your feedback in the "Feedback" field. Please be specific about your thoughts and opinions on the prompt.
6. Rate your experience with the prompt using the "Rate" field.
7. Choose one option from the list to answer the question in the "Agree 1" field.
8. Select multiple options from the list to answer the question in the "Agree 2" and "Agree 7" fields.
9. You can optionally enter a specific date and time in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
10. If you have any additional notes or comments, please enter them in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your full last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you can use to receive any follow-up communications.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, if you'd like the form owner to contact you.
* **Feedback**: Enter your thoughts, feelings, and opinions on the prompt. Be specific and detailed.
* **Rate**: Rate your experience with the prompt, from 1 to 10.
* **Agree 1**: Choose one option from the list that best answers the question.
* **Agree 2**: Select all applicable options from the list to answer the question.
