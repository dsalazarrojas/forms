# Cybersecurity Client Security Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cybersecurity Client Security Inquiry Form is designed to help us understand the client's security concerns and identify potential risks. It is a simple and straightforward form that asks for basic information about the client and their security inquiry. Please take a few minutes to fill it out and let us know how we can assist you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your company's name in the "Company" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Select the security risks you are concerned about in the "Security Risks" field.
7. Select the recommended actions you would like us to take in the "Recommended Actions" field.
8. If none of the recommended actions meet your needs, you can specify "Other" actions in the "Other Actions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Security Inquiry**: This is a text field where you can describe your security concerns and questions.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter the email address associated with your company or organization.
* **Company**: Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Address**: Enter your company's or organization's address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Security Risks**: Please select all the security risks that you are concerned about. You can choose from:
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High
* **Recommended Actions**: Please select all the recommended actions that you would like us to take. You can choose from:
	+ Perform a risk assessment
	+ Implement security measures
	+ Review and adjust
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Other Actions**: If you selected "Other" in the "Recommended Actions" field, please specify the actions you would like us to take in this field.
