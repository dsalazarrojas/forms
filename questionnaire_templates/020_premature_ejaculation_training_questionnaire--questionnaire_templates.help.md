# Premature Ejaculation Training Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients on their training plan and progress, to help identify areas of focus for coaching and support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the appropriate options for each field based on your training plan and progress.
2. Enter your contact number, email, and date of birth if applicable.
3. Choose how often you would like to receive emails and coaching sessions.
4. Select the length and frequency of coaching and support sessions.
5. Enter any additional comments or notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intake Details** (`intake_details`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have any additional comments or concerns about your training plan.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name for reference and contact purposes.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number for us to reach out to you.
* **Email** (`email`, text, optional): Enter your email address for us to reach out to you.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): If applicable, enter your date of birth for age-relevant coaching.
* **Training Plan** (`training_plan`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have a training plan in place, 'No' if you do not.
* **Progress Tracking** (`progress_tracking`, text, optional): Enter any progress or milestones you have achieved.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your training plan.
* **Email Frequency** (`email_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you would like to receive emails from us, 'No' if you do not.
* **Client Support** (`client_support`, text, optional): Enter any support or help you need or would like to receive.
* **Coaching Session Frequency** (`coaching_session_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you would like to receive coaching sessions, 'No' if you do not.
* **Training Session Length** (`training_session_length`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you would like longer or shorter training sessions, 'No' for the standard length.
* **Training Days** (`training_days`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you would like to train more or less frequently, 'No' for the standard frequency.
* **Coaching Session Length** (`coaching_session_length`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you would like longer or shorter coaching sessions, 'No' for the standard length.
* **Follow-up** (`followup`, text, optional): Enter any follow-up or progress you would like to share.
* **Client Follow-up** (`client_followup`, text, optional): Enter any follow-up or progress you would like to share with us.
* **Follow Up Method** (`follow_up_method`, text, optional): Enter the method you would like to use for follow-up, e.g., phone call, email, etc.
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you would like follow-up, 'No' if you do not.
* **Support Frequency** (`support_frequency`, text, optional): Enter how often you would like to receive support or guidance.
* **Support Methods** (`support_methods`, text, optional): Enter the method you would like to use for support, e.g., phone call, email, etc.
* **Support Session Length** (`support_session_length`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you would like longer or shorter support sessions, 'No' for the standard length.
* **Support Days** (`support_days`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you would like to receive support more or less frequently, 'No' for the standard frequency.
* **Intake Session Duration** (`intake_session_duration`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you would like longer or shorter intake sessions, 'No' for the standard duration.
* **Intake Session Fees** (`intake_session_fees`, select_multiple, optional): Select any fees you would like to pay for sessions.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your training plan.

Please review and submit this form carefully to ensure that your information is accurate and complete.
