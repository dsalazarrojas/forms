# shopify_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shopify Questionnaire is designed to help you better understand a client's needs and goals in the e-commerce context. It's intended to gather information about their requirements, preferences, and expectations for their online presence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant client needs and goals that apply to your client.
2. Choose the pages and devices your client would like their online presence to be accessible on.
3. Provide a brief description of your client's needs and goals.
4. Confirm that you have reviewed and understand the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Client Needs and Goals
* **Client Needs and Goals** (`client_needs_goals`, text, optional): Briefly describe your client's needs and goals.

### Client Needs
* **Client Needs** (`client_needs`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the client needs that apply to your client.
  - Client needs 1
  - Client needs 2
  - Client needs 3

### Client Goals
* **Client Goals** (`client_goals`, text, optional): Describe your client's goals.

### Client Goals
* **Client Goals** (`client_goals_goals`, select_one, optional): Select the main client goal.
  - Goal 1
  - Goal 2
  - Goal 3

### Client Needs and Goals Description
* **Client Needs and Goals Description** (`client_needs_goals_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your client's needs and goals.

### Page Accessibility
* **Page Accessibility** (`page_accessibility`, select_multiple, optional): Select the pages your client would like their online presence to be accessible on.
  - Desktops
  - Mobiles

### Page Accessibility Description
* **Page Accessibility Description** (`page_accessibility_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe the page accessibility requirements.

### Client Needs and Goals Description
* **Client Needs and Goals Description** (`client_needs_goals_description`, text, optional): Confirm that you understand the client's needs and goals description.

### Form Accessibility
* **Form Accessibility** (`form_accessibility`, select_multiple, optional): Select the form accessibility requirements.
  - Desktops
  - Mobiles

### Form Accessibility Description
* **Form Accessibility Description** (`page_accessibility_description`, text, optional): Describe the form accessibility requirements.

## Tips
- Make sure to select the relevant client needs and goals that apply to your client.
- Choose the correct pages and devices for page accessibility.
- Keep the client needs and goals description concise and clear.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.
