# patient_health_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect in-depth information from patients about their health and medical history, aiding healthcare professionals in understanding their health status and providing better care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling out the **patient_details** section, providing your demographic information such as name, age, and contact details.
2. In the **presenting_complaints** section, describe any current symptoms or health concerns you are experiencing.
3. Move on to **lifestyle_habits**, where you can report your daily habits, diet, and exercise routines.
4. The **additional_information** section is for any other relevant details you'd like to share with your healthcare provider.
5. The **medical_history** and **family_medical_history** sections are crucial in understanding your medical background.
6. In **presenting_complaints_2**, describe any additional symptoms or concerns you may have.
7. The **medical_history_2** section is for any significant medical events or treatments in your past.
8. **Family_medical_history_2** is for any notable medical history in your family.
9. **Lifestyle_habits_2** is for any recent changes in your daily habits or routines.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_details** (Text, Required):
	+ A field for entering your name, age, and contact information.
* **presenting_complaints** (Text, Required):
	+ Describe any current symptoms or health concerns you're experiencing.
* **lifestyle_habits** (Text, Required):
	+ Report your daily habits, diet, and exercise routines.
* **additional_information** (Text, Required):
	+ Share any additional details about your health status or concerns.
* **medical_history** (Text, Required):
	+ Describe any significant medical events or treatments in your past.
* **family_medical_history** (Text, Required):
	+ Share any notable medical history in your family.
* **presenting_complaints_2** (Text, Required):
	+ Describe any additional symptoms or concerns.
* **medical_history_2** (Text, Required):
	+ Report any significant medical events or treatments in your past.
* **family_medical_history_2** (Text, Required):
	+ Share any notable medical history in your family.
* **lifestyle_habits_2** (Text, Required):
	+ Report any recent changes in your daily habits or routines.

## Tips
- Ensure you answer all required fields accurately and honestly.
- If you're unsure about any question, please consult your healthcare provider.
- If you have any medical records or documents, please attach them for reference.
- This form is for your health and safety; please do not hesitate to ask for assistance if you need help.
