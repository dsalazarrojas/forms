# Self Management Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Self Management Skills Assessment form is designed to evaluate your self-management skills, which are essential for achieving goals, maintaining a healthy work-life balance, and overall well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and choose the option that best describes your current level of skill in the respective area.
2. Answer all questions, as some questions are required.
3. Be honest and accurate in your answers, as the information collected will be used to support your growth and development.
4. You may need to refer to the options provided for each question if you're unsure about your current level of skill.
