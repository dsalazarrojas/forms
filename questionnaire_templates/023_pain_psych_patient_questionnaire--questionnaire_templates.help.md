# Pain Psych Patient Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pain Psych Patient Questionnaire is a form used to collect information about a patient's pain experience, including its location, medical history, psychological history, medication, pain characteristics, frequency, duration, intensity, treatment goals, current treatments, other treatments, mental status, and pain scale.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer it according to your actual experience.
2. Enter your responses in the spaces provided.
3. Be as accurate as possible when answering the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: A field to record the patient's name.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To identify the patient's name.
* **Pain Location**: A field to record the location of the patient's pain.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To determine the area affected by the pain.
* **Medical History**: A field to record the patient's medical history.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To gather information about the patient's medical background.
* **Psychological History**: A field to record the patient's psychological history.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To gather information about the patient's psychological background.
* **Medication**: A field to record any medication the patient is currently taking.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To gather information about the patient's medication regimen.
* **Pain Characteristics**: A field to describe the patient's pain experience.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To gather information about the patient's pain experience.
* **Pain Frequency**: A field to record how often the patient experiences pain.
	+ Type: Number
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To determine the frequency of the patient's pain experience.
* **Pain Duration**: A field to record how long the patient experiences pain.
	+ Type: Number
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To determine the duration of the patient's pain experience.
* **Pain Intensity**: A field to record the intensity of the patient's pain experience.
	+ Type: Number
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To determine the intensity of the patient's pain experience.
* **Treatment Goal**: A field to record the patient's treatment goals.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To gather information about the patient's treatment goals.
* **Current Treatment**: A field to record the patient's current treatments.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To gather information about the patient's current treatments.
* **Other Treatment**: A field to record any other treatments the patient is receiving.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To gather information about any other treatments the patient is receiving.
* **Mental Status**: A field to record the patient's mental status.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To gather information about the patient's mental status.
* **Pain Scale**: A field to record the patient's pain experience on a scale.
	+ Type: Number
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To determine the patient's pain experience on a scale.
* **Patient Status**: A field to indicate the patient's status.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: Yes
	+ Purpose: To determine the patient's status.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
- If you are unsure about any question, please do not hesitate to ask for guidance.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the healthcare provider.
