# Guide To Choosing Writing Services - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide provides a comprehensive template for students, agencies, and providers to select and match writing services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following pages and fields:

1. **Student Information** (Page 1)
	* **Student Information**: Please provide your name, student ID, and any other relevant details.
	* **Required**: This field is optional.
2. **What kind of writing are you looking for?** (Page 1)
	* **What kind of writing are you looking for**: Choose one of the options provided (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
	* **Required**: This field is optional.
3. **Deadline**: (Page 2)
	* **Deadline**: Enter the date by which you need the writing service to be completed.
	* **Required**: This field is optional.
4. **Budget**: (Page 2)
	* **Budget**: Enter the maximum amount you are willing to pay for the writing service.
	* **Required**: This field is optional.
5. **Agency Information**: (Page 3)
	* **Agency Information**: Provide any relevant details about the agency you are interested in, such as name, address, etc.
	* **Required**: This field is optional.
6. **Agency Budget**: (Page 3)
	* **Agency Budget**: Enter the amount you are willing to pay for the agency's services.
	* **Required**: This field is optional.
7. **Agency Deadline**: (Page 4)
	* **Agency Deadline**: Enter the date by which you need the agency to complete their services.
	* **Required**: This field is optional.
8. **Provider Information**: (Page 4)
	* **Provider Information**: Provide any relevant details about the provider you are interested in, such as name, qualifications, etc.
	* **Required**: This field is optional.
9. **Provider Budget**: (Page 5)
	* **Provider Budget**: Enter the amount you are willing to pay for the provider's services.
	* **Required**: This field is optional.
10. **Provider Deadline**: (Page 5)
	* **Provider Deadline**: Enter the date by which you need the provider to complete their services.
	* **Required**: This field is optional.
11. **Agency Experience**: (Page 6)
	* **Agency Experience**: Choose one or more options for the agency's experience, such as Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
	* **Required**: This field is optional.
12. **Provider Experience**: (Page 7)
	* **Provider Experience**: Choose one or more options for the provider's experience, such as Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
	* **Required**: This field is optional.
13. **Agency Services**: (Page 8)
	* **Agency Services**: Choose one or more options for the agency's services, such as Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
	* **Required**: This field is optional.
14. **Provider Services**: (Page 9)
	* **Provider Services**: Choose one or more options for the provider's services, such as Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
	* **Required**: This field is optional.
15. **Contact Information**: (Page 10)
	* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact details, such as name, email address, and phone number.
	* **Required**: This field is optional.
16. **Email**: (Page 10)
	* **Email**: Enter your email address.
	* **Required**: This field is optional.
17. **Notes**: (Page 11)
	* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your needs or preferences.
	* **Required**: This field is optional.
18. **Agency Notes**: (Page 12)
	* **Agency Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments from the agency.

19. **Provider Notes**: (Page 13)

20. **Agency Comments**: (Page 14)

22. **Student Comments**: (Page 16)

23. **Agency Agreement**: (Page 17)
