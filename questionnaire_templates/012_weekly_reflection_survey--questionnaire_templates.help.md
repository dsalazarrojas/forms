# Weekly Reflection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Reflection Survey is a form designed to help you reflect on your progress, goals, and experiences from the past week. This form is meant to be a personal or team-wide activity to help you identify areas of improvement, celebrate successes, and plan for the next week.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each field with a brief description or a short paragraph.
2. Be honest and objective about your experiences from the past week.
3. Try to be specific about what you achieved, learned, or faced.
4. Use the available fields to report on your personal or team progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What did you achieve this week** (1): Describe your accomplishments, successes, or notable events from the past week. 
* **What did you learn this week** (2): Share your insights, new skills, or knowledge gained during the week.
* **What are your goals for the week ahead** (3): Choose all the goals you plan to achieve or focus on in the next week.
* **What challenges do you face this week** (4): Describe any difficulties or obstacles you faced during the week.
* **What are your wins this week** (5): Select one or more options that best describe your achievements or successes.
	+ Success in my personal project: Choose this if you completed a personal project.
	+ Success in my work project: Choose this if you completed a project in your work.
	+ Other: Choose this for any other type of success.
* **What did you find difficult this week** (6): Share your challenges or difficulties from the past week.
* **What would you do differently next week** (7): Think about what you can do to improve next week.
* **What did you enjoy this week** (8): Share what you enjoyed or appreciated about the week.
* **What did you learn from** (9): Describe what you gained from your experiences, books, or learning activities.
* **What did you discover this week** (10): Share any new things you discovered or learned about yourself, others, or the world.
* **What did you improve this week** (11): Describe what you improved or accomplished.
* **What did you realize this week** (12): Share any new understandings or insights you gained.
* **What did you learn from others** (13): Describe any new knowledge or skills you gained from interacting with others.
* **What did you gain from** (14): Share any benefits or gains you experienced from your activities or interactions.
* **What will you do differently next week** (15): Think about how you can improve next week.
* **What will you do** (16): Describe your plans or actions for the next week.
* **What will you improve** (17): Share what you can do better next week.
* **What will you learn** (18): Describe what you plan to gain or learn next week.
* **What will you gain from** (19): Share what benefits or advantages you expect next week.
* **What will you face** (20): Describe any challenges or difficulties you anticipate next week.
* **What will you realize** (21): Share any new understandings or insights you expect next week.
* **What will you gain** (22): Share any benefits or advantages you expect next week.
* **What will happen** (23): Describe any events or consequences you anticipate next week.
* **What will you learn from** (24): Describe what you expect to gain or learn from your experiences next week.
* **What will happen next** (25): Describe any outcomes or events you expect to occur next week. 

Note: Please be sure to answer these questions briefly and concisely, as the form is meant to be a quick and easy reflection activity.
