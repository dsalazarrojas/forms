# Independent Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and record information from evaluators about the assessment of a project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Project Name** field with the name of the project being evaluated.
2. Enter your **Evaluator Name** and **Evaluator's Rating** (if applicable).
3. Enter the **Date of Evaluation** and **Time of Evaluation** (if applicable).
4. Provide a **Comment** about your evaluation, if desired.
5. Select the **Rating Scale** that best describes your evaluation.
6. Enter your **Submitted By** email address.
7. If applicable, provide your **Phone Number**, **Project Link**, **Project Description**, and **Submitted By** email address.
8. Select the **Evaluation Form Type** (Independent, Collaborative, Both, or Other) and **Submission Status** (Draft, Published, Archived, In Progress, Reopened).
9. Enter the **Project Category**, if applicable.
10. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project being evaluated.
* **Evaluator Name**: Enter your name, as the evaluator of the project.
* **Evaluator's Rating**: Optional field for entering your rating (if applicable).
* **Score**: Enter your score for the project, if applicable.
* **Comment**: Enter any comments or feedback you have about the project.
* **Date of Evaluation**: Enter the date on which the evaluation was completed.
* **Time of Evaluation**: Optional field for entering the time of evaluation.
* **Rating Scale**: Select the rating scale that best describes your evaluation (Excellent, Good, Fair, Not Good).
* **Submitted By**: Enter your email address associated with the evaluation.
* **Phone Number**: Optional field for entering your phone number.
* **Project Link**: Optional field for entering the project link.
* **Project Description**: Optional field for entering the project description.
* **Submitted By**: Optional field for entering your name or username.
* **Submitted On**: Enter the date and time on which the form was submitted.
* **Evaluation Form Type**: Select the type of evaluation form being used (Independent, Collaborative, Both, or Other).
* **Submission Status**: Select the status of the form submission (Draft, Published, Archived, In Progress, Reopened).
* **Project Category**: Enter the category of the project, if applicable.
* **Project Link URL**: Optional field for entering the project link URL.
* **Submitter's Phone**: Optional field for entering your phone number.
* **Evaluator Comments**: Optional field for entering any comments or feedback from the evaluator.
