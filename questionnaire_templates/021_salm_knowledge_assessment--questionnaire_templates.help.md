# Salm Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Salm Knowledge Assessment form aims to gather information about your knowledge and understanding of Salm. This includes its definition, principles, and support methods. The form is used to assess your knowledge and gather feedback for future improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer them to the best of your knowledge.
2. Answer all required questions (marked with an asterisk *) and provide additional comments or suggestions if you have any.
3. Fill in your contact information (name, email, phone number, etc.) so we can get back to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is Salm** (question 1): A question to assess your understanding of Salm's definition.
* **What do you think is Salm** (question 2): A question to explore your thoughts on Salm's nature.
* **How did you learn about Salm** (question 3): A question to learn about your learning sources and experiences with Salm.
* **What do you think is key to understanding Salm** (question 4): A question to gather your insights on Salm's key aspects.
* **What are the key principles of Salm** (question 5): A question to evaluate your understanding of Salm's core principles.
* **What is the most significant factor in Salm** (question 6): A question to understand your perspective on Salm's importance.
* **What do you think is the main difference between Salm and** (question 7): A question to explore your understanding of Salm's differences with others.
* **How does your group currently support Salm development** (question 8): A question to learn about your group's support methods for Salm.
* **What methods do you recommend for Salm development** (question 9): A question to gather your recommendations for Salm development.
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions** (question 10): A question to provide feedback and suggestions for future improvement.
* **Name** (contact_name): A field to fill in your name for contact purposes.
* **Email** (contact_email): A field to fill in your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (contact_phone): A field to fill in your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Message** (contact_message): A field to provide any additional comments or messages.
* **File Upload** (contact_file_upload): A field for uploading any supporting files or documents.
* **Website** (contact_website): A field to fill in your website address for contact purposes.
* **Comment** (contact_comment): A field to provide any additional comments.
* **Social Media** (contact_social): A field to fill in your social media handle or profile.
* **Instagram** (contact_instagram): A field to fill in your Instagram handle or profile.

* **Twitter** (contact_twitter): A field to fill in your Twitter handle or profile.
