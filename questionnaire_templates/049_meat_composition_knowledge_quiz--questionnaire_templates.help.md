# Meat Composition Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your knowledge on meat composition, including nutrients, products, and production. It's a quiz for educators, trainers, and researchers who need to demonstrate their expertise or share their knowledge with others.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select your options carefully for each question.
2. Fill in the required fields accurately.
3. Review your responses for completeness and consistency.
4. Submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`): Enter your details such as name, age, and location to help us better understand your profile.
  * A brief introduction to who you are and where you come from.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
  * Your real name, first and last.
* **Age** (`age`): Enter your age in years.
  * Your actual age, for our records.
* **Gender** (`gender`): Select your sex.
  * Male, Female, or Other for our purposes.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`): Select your educational background.
  * High School, Bachelor, Master, or PhD to help us understand your qualifications.
* **Knowledge Area** (`knowledge_area`): Select the area of knowledge you have about meat.
  * Meat anatomy, Meat science, or Meat production for our records.
* **Participant Occupation** (`participant_occupation`): Select your occupation related to meat.
  * Educator, Trainer, or Researcher for our purposes.
* **Participant Location** (`participant_location`): Select where you are from.
  * Urban, Rural, or Coastal for our records.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`): Enter any additional information about yourself.
  * This is optional and used for internal purposes only.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`): Enter your official email address.
  * This is used for communication with us.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
  * We may contact you for further information or clarification.
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`): Enter any additional information you feel is relevant.
  * This is an open field for your comments.
* **Meat Nutrients** (`meat_nutrients`): Select the nutrients you are familiar with.
  * Choose one or more of the given options.
* **Energy Content** (`energy_content`): Enter the energy content of a particular meat product.
  * A number representing the energy content.
* **Meat Products** (`meat_products`): Select the meat products you are familiar with.
  * Choose one or more from the given options.
* **Participant Rating** (`participant_rating`): Rate your confidence level in your knowledge.
  * Choose High, Medium, or Low for self-assessment.

**Tips**

* Please take your time and answer the questions carefully.
* Double-check your responses for accuracy.
* If you need to, use external sources for information before submitting the form.
* This quiz is for educational purposes only, and the results will be kept confidential.
