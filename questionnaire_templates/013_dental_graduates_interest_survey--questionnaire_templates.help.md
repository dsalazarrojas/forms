# Dental Graduates Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from dental graduates about their current position, career preferences, and job characteristics. It will help us understand their interests and needs as they enter the job market.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide an introduction to your current position and background.
2. Indicate if you are currently employed or not.
3. Select the fields that best describe your current degree.
4. For each preference, select all that apply.
5. For each job characteristic, select the option that best describes your job.
6. Provide information about your years of experience in the field.
7. Select your job status.
8. Enter your expected job level.
9. Enter your expected job salary.
10. Select your level of job satisfaction.
11. Indicate if you have job leverage or not.
12. Select your level of job security.
13. Select your work culture.
14. Select your job mission.
15. Select if you would recommend this job to others.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **introduction**: A short description of your current position and background.
* **current_position**: Indicate if you are currently employed or not.
* **current_degree**: Select the fields that best describe your current degree.
* **career_preferences**:
	+ Yes: Select this option if you prefer a career in dental research.
	+ No: Select this option if you prefer a career in clinical practice or other areas.
* **job_preferences**:
	+ Yes: Select this option if you prefer a job that involves patient interaction.
	+ No: Select this option if you prefer a job that does not involve patient interaction.
* **research_interest**:
	+ Yes: Select this option if you are interested in dental research.
	+ No: Select this option if you are not interested in dental research.
* **job_title**: Enter your expected job title.
* **location**: Enter your preferred job location.
* **job_type**: Select the option that best describes your job type.
* **job_category**: Select the option that best describes your job category.
* **job_subcategory**: Select the option that best describes your job subcategory.
* **job_sector**: Select the option that best describes your job sector.
* **job_location**: Select the option that best describes your job location.
* **work_experience**: Enter your years of experience in the field.
* **job_status**: Select your job status.
* **job_level**: Select your expected job level.
* **job_description**: Enter a brief description of your expected job.
* **job_salary**: Enter your expected job salary.
* **job_satisfaction**: Select the option that best describes your job satisfaction.
* **job_leverage**: Indicate if you have job leverage or not.
* **job_security**: Select the option that best describes your job security.
* **job_culture**: Select the option that best describes your work culture.
* **job_mission**: Select the option that best describes your job mission.
* **job_recommendation**: Indicate if you would recommend this job to others.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can select "Not sure" or "No preference" as an option.
* If you need help or have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.
