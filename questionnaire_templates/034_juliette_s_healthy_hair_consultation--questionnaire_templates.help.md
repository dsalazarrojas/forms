# Juliettes_Healthy_Hair_Consultation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the health and habits of someone's hair, providing a comprehensive overview of various aspects of hair care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
2. For select multiple and select one fields, choose the options that best fit your hair care habits.
3. If you have any additional comments or concerns, use the text fields to provide more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hair Problem** (`hair_problem`, text, required: false): Describe any hair problems you're experiencing, such as dandruff, itching, or color loss.
* **Hair Frequency** (`hair_frequency`, text, required: false): Tell us about your hair care frequency, including how often you wash, condition, and style your hair.
* **Hair Type** (`hair_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the types of hair care practices you engage in, such as using heat styling tools, hair dyes, or hair treatments.
* **Hair Color** (`hair_color`, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether you have color-treated hair or not.
* **Hair Texture** (`hair_texture`, text, required: false): Describe the texture of your hair, including any issues like dryness or brittleness.
* **Hair Smell** (`hair_smell`, text, required: false): Report any unusual smells or odors from your hair.
* **Hair Feeling** (`hair_feeling`, text, required: false): Share your feelings about your hair, such as feeling dry, brittle, or fragile.
* **Hair Diet** (`hair_diet`, text, required: false): Discuss any dietary habits that may affect your hair health, including excessive sugar, protein, or vitamin deficiencies.
* **Hair Hygiene** (`hair_hygiene`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate your hair care habits, including washing, conditioning, or using products.
* **Hair Care** (`hair_care`, text, required: false): Describe your hair care routine, including products used and frequency of use.
* **Hair Style** (`hair_style`, text, required: false): Share any hairstyle or styling habits that may be causing hair problems.
* **Hair Growth** (`hair_growth`, text, required: false): Report any concerns or experiences with hair growth, such as slow growth or sudden hair loss.
* **Hair Loss** (`hair_loss`, text, required: false): Discuss any hair loss experiences, such as excessive shedding or breakage.
* **Hair Fatigue** (`hair_fatigue`, text, required: false): Share any feelings of hair fatigue, such as feeling tired or brittle.
* **Hair Breakage** (`hair_breakage`, text, required: false): Report any hair breakage or snapping issues.
* **Frequent Use of Heated Styles** (`hair_frequent_use_of_heated_styles`, text, required: false): Indicate how often you use heated styling tools.
* **Frequent Use of Heat Styling** (`hair_frequent_use_of_heat_styling`, text, required: false): Discuss how often you use heat styling tools.
* **Frequent Use of Styling Products** (`hair_frequent_use_of_styling_products`, text, required: false): Share how often you use hair styling products.
* **Frequent Use of Hair Dyes** (`hair_frequent_use_of_hair_dyes`, text, required: false): Report how often you dye your hair.
* **Frequent Use of Hair Products** (`hair_frequent_use_of_hair_products`, text, required: false): Discuss how often you use hair products.
* **Frequent Use of Heat** (`hair_frequent_use_of_heat`, text, required: false): Indicate how often you expose your hair to heat.
* **Frequent Use of Stylist** (`hair_frequent_use_of_stylist`, text, required: false): Share how often you visit a stylist.
* **Frequent Use of Shampoo** (`hair_frequent_use_of_shampoo`, text, required: false): Report how often you wash your hair with shampoo.
* **Frequent Use of Conditioner** (`hair_frequent_use_of_conditioner`, text, required: false): Discuss how often you use conditioner.
* **Frequent Use of Hair Mask** (`hair_frequent_use_of_hair_mask`, text, required: false): Indicate how often you use hair mask.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* If you're unsure about any question, use the text fields to provide more information.
* This form is designed to help us better understand your hair care habits and provide guidance and recommendations.
