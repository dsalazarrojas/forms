# COVID 19 Self Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals assess their eligibility for the COVID-19 vaccine based on their health status. It is used to determine whether an individual has a medical reason that would prevent or defer vaccination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the field labeled "Full Name".
2. Enter your age in the field labeled "Age".
3. Select your primary occupation from the list "Primary Occupation Category".
4. Check all that apply for any existing medical conditions from the list "Do you have any of the following medical conditions?"
5. Answer "True", "False", or "Unsure" to the question "Have you ever had a severe allergic reaction to a vaccine?"
6. Select one of the options for "Are you currently pregnant or breastfeeding?"
7. Answer "True" or "False" to the question "Have you previously been infected with COVID-19?"
8. If you have previously been infected with COVID-19, provide an approximate date of infection in the field "If yes, approximate date of infection".
9. Select one of the options for "How would you describe your immune system status?"
10. Choose one of the options for "Based on your self-assessment, do you believe you are eligible for the vaccine?"
11. (Optional) If eligible, please refer to the link provided in the field "Next Steps" to schedule your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be recorded.
* **Age**: Enter your age in years.
* **Primary Occupation Category**: Select the category that best describes your primary occupation.
	+ Healthcare: Healthcare professionals (e.g., doctors, nurses, etc.)
	+ Education: Teachers, professors, etc.
	+ Retail/Food: Retail, food service, etc.
	+ Transportation: Drivers, etc.
	+ Remote Worker: Working from home or remotely
	+ Retired/Unemployed: Not working, retired, or unemployed
* **Do you have any of the following medical conditions?**: Check all that apply for any existing medical conditions.
	+ Asthma
	+ Diabetes
	+ Heart Disease
	+ Kidney Disease
	+ Immunocompromised
	+ None
* **Have you ever had a severe allergic reaction to a vaccine?**: Answer with:
	+ True: If you have had a severe allergic reaction to a vaccine.
	+ False: If you have not had a severe allergic reaction to a vaccine.
	+ Unsure: If you are unsure or do not know.
* **Are you currently pregnant or breastfeeding?**: Answer with:
	+ True: If you are currently pregnant or breastfeeding.
	+ False: If you are not currently pregnant or breastfeeding.
	+ Not Applicable: If this does not apply to you.
* **Have you previously been infected with COVID-19?**: Answer with:
	+ True: If you have previously been infected with COVID-19.
	+ False: If you have not previously been infected with COVID-19.
* **If yes, approximate date of infection**: Enter the approximate date (mm/dd/yyyy) of when you were infected with COVID-19.
* **How would you describe your immune system status?**: Answer with:
	+ Strong/Healthy: If you have a strong immune system.
	+ Average: If you have an average immune system.
	+ Weakened/Suppressed: If you have a weakened or suppressed immune system.
* **Based on your self-assessment, do you believe you are eligible for the vaccine?**: Answer with:
	+ Yes - Priority Group: If you believe you are eligible for the vaccine due to your medical status.
	+ Yes - General Population: If you believe you are eligible for the vaccine as a general population.
	+ No - Not currently eligible: If you do not believe you are eligible for the vaccine.
* **Next Steps**: (Optional) If you believe you are eligible for the vaccine, please refer to the link provided to schedule your appointment.
* **I certify this self-assessment is for my own information and use**: Answer with:
	+ True: If this self-assessment is for your own information and use.
	+ False: If this self-assessment is not for your own information and use.
