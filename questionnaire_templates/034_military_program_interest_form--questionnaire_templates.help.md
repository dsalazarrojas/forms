# Military Program Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Military Program Interest Form is a questionnaire designed to gather information from potential candidates interested in pursuing a military career. This form will help the organization understand their needs and provide them with relevant information and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your School Name in the "School Name" field.
2. Select your Program Type(s) of interest from the list provided.
3. Enter your Contact First Name and Last Name in the "Contact First Name" and "Contact Last Name" fields.
4. Enter your Contact Email and Phone number in the "Contact Email" and "Contact Phone" fields.
5. Enter the location of the program in the "Program Location" field.
6. Select the type of training or commissioning program you are interested in from the "Interested In" field.
7. Enter the desired start date of the program in the "Program Start Date" field.
8. Enter the expected graduation date of the program in the "Graduation Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school or institution you are currently attending or have attended.
* **Program Type**: Select one or more of the following program types:
	+ Army
	+ Navy
	+ Air Force
	+ Marine Corps
* **Contact First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Contact Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Program Location**: Enter the location of the program you are interested in.
* **Interested In**: Select one or more of the following:
	+ Basic Training
	+ Advanced Training
	+ Officer Candidate School
	+ Commissioned Officer
* **Program Start Date**: Enter the date you plan to start the program.
* **Graduation Date**: Enter the date you plan to graduate from the program.
