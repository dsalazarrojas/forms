# Community Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather community feedback from residents and users of the community to improve the quality of services provided. It covers various aspects of community services, including parks and recreation, library, public transportation, public safety, cleanliness, and communication. The feedback collected will help identify areas of improvement and inform decision-making to better serve the community.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out your name and email address (optional) on Page 1.
* Select your residency status on Page 2.
* Choose the community services you have used on Page 3.
* Rate the quality of each service on Page 4-8.
* Provide feedback on the best and worst services on Page 9.
* Suggest improvements on Page 10.
* Rate your satisfaction with local events on Page 11.
* Provide suggestions for future improvements on Page 12.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Respondent Name
* **Name (Optional)**: Enter your name if you wish to share it. If not, leave this field blank.
### Page 2: Residency Status
* **Residency Status**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Resident: If you live in the community.
	+ Business Owner: If you own a business in the community.
	+ Employee: If you work in the community.
	+ Visitor: If you are visiting or have visited the community.
### Page 3: Community Services Used
* **Community Services Used**: Select all services you have used:
