# Fitness Business Sales Readiness Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This questionnaire aims to assess the sales readiness of your fitness business. It will help us understand your business's operational and management systems, sales and marketing strategies, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about your business.
2. For fields that are not required, you can leave them blank if you do not have the necessary information.
3. Please provide as much detail as possible for fields that allow text input.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name (`business_name`)**: The official name of your fitness business.
* **Contact Person (`contact_person`)**: The person we can contact for further information or clarification.
* **Business Address (`business_address`)**: The address of your fitness business location.
* **Phone Number (`phone_number`)**: The phone number of your fitness business.
* **Email Address (`email_address`)**: The email address of your fitness business.
* **Business Ownership (`business_ownership`)**: The type of business ownership (Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, Corporation, or Limited Liability Company).
* **Business Start Date (`business_start_date`)**: The date your fitness business started.
* **Number of Locations (`number_of_locations`)**: The number of locations your fitness business operates from.
* **Number of Employees (`number_of_employees`)**: The number of employees in your fitness business.
* **Annual Revenue (`annual_revenue`)**: The annual revenue of your fitness business.
* **Management System (`management_system`)**: Describe your management system, including any software or tools used.
* **Sales System (`sales_system`)**: Describe your sales system, including any software or tools used.
* **Accounting System (`accounting_system`)**: Describe your accounting system, including any software or tools used.
* **Technology Stack (`technology_stack`)**: List the technology stack used in your fitness business.
* **Security Measures (`security_measures`)**: Describe any security measures in place in your fitness business.
* **Customer Service Platform (`customer_service_platform`)**: Describe your customer service platform.
* **Sales Strategy (`sales_strategy`)**: Describe your sales strategy.
* **Marketing Strategy (`marketing_strategy`)**: Describe your marketing strategy.
* **Product Offers (`product_offers`)**: Describe any product offers.
* **Sales Tactics (`sales_tactics`)**: Describe any sales tactics.
* **Sales Process (`sales_process`)**: Describe your sales process.
* **Sales Pipeline (`sales_pipeline`)**: Describe your sales pipeline.
* **Lead Generation (`lead_generation`)**: Describe your lead generation process.
* **Lead Conversion (`lead_conversion`)**: Describe your lead conversion process.
* **Sales Funnel (`sales_funnel`)**: Describe your sales funnel.

Note: Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for further assistance.
