# Tourism Services Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This Tourism Services Feedback Questionnaire is designed to gather honest and constructive feedback from tourists who have used our tourism services. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement, address any concerns you may have, and develop our services further to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions and rating as accurately as possible, providing specific examples or details where possible.
2. Choose the best option that represents your experience from the provided select_one fields.
3. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes, if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion.
4. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Comments" field.
5. Click "Submit" to complete the form and send us your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intro Question**: This is an open-ended question where you can tell us about your recent experience with our tourism services. Please be as specific as possible.
* **Rating**: Rate your overall experience with our tourism services, using the numbers 1-5. A higher number indicates a more positive experience.
* **Service Quality**: Choose the best option that best describes your experience with our services.
	+ Option 1: Unacceptable
	+ Option 2: Below Average
	+ Option 3: Average
	+ Option 4: Above Average
	+ Option 5: Excellent
	+ Option 6: Outstanding
* **Tour Guide**: If you had a tour guide, please describe their performance in this field.
* **Comments**: Use this field to provide any additional feedback, suggestions, or questions you may have.
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion or follow-up.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
* **Submit**: Click this button to complete and submit your feedback form.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and accurately, as your feedback is crucial to our improvement.
* You can provide as much detail as you'd like in the "Comments" field.
* If you'd like to be contacted for further discussion, please enter your email and/or phone number.
* If you have any concerns or issues, please describe them in the "Comments" field.
