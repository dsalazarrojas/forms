# CGPA Conversion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CGPA Conversion Survey is a form used to collect information about your academic history and requirements for converting your grades from one institution to another. This form will help you provide accurate and up-to-date information for academic or job applications.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details such as student name, ID number, and email address. These fields are mandatory and will help us identify you correctly.
2. Provide your current institution's name and country.
3. Select your current grading system and enter your current CGPA.
4. Choose your target institution and country.
5. Select your target grading system and program level.
6. Enter your field of study and total credits completed (if applicable).
7. Specify the purpose of the conversion and your application deadline (if applicable).
8. Indicate whether you have attached your official transcript.
9. List any additional documents submitted.
10. If there are any special circumstances affecting your GPA, describe them briefly.
11. Finally, choose your preferred contact method for further communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (1): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your university documents.
* **Student ID Number** (2): Enter your university ID number for identification purposes.
* **Email Address** (3): Enter your institutional email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (4): Enter your best contact number.
* **Current Institution** (5): Enter the name of your current university or college.
* **Country of Institution** (6): Choose the country where your institution is located.
* **Current Grading System** (7): Select your current grading system used by your institution.
* **Current CGPA** (8): Enter your current cumulative grade point average.
* **Target Institution** (9): Enter the name of the institution you are applying to.
* **Target Country** (10): Choose the country where your target institution is located.
* **Target Grading System** (11): Select the grading system used by your target institution.
* **Program Level** (12): Select your current academic level (e.g., undergraduate, master's, doctoral).
* **Field of Study** (13): Enter your major or specialization.
* **Total Credits Completed** (14): Enter the number of credits completed if applicable.
* **Purpose of Conversion** (15): Specify why you need CGPA conversion (e.g., graduate school application, job application).
* **Application Deadline** (16): Enter the deadline for your application if applicable.
* **Official Transcript Attached** (17): Indicate whether you have attached your official transcript.
* **Additional Documents** (18): List any other documents submitted.
* **Special Circumstances** (19): Describe any factors affecting your GPA.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (20): Choose your preferred contact method for further communication.
