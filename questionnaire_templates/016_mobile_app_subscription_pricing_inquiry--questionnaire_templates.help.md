# Mobile App Subscription Pricing Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from customers about their mobile app subscription pricing preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the "Subscription Pricing" page.
2.  Fill in the "Billing Preferences" field by selecting the billing options that apply to your account.
3.  Enter any specific "Value Perceptions" related to your subscription in the text field.
4.  Choose the "Billing Frequency" option that best suits your billing schedule.
5.  Select the "Billing Method" that you prefer for payment.
6.  Choose the "Billing Cycle" that aligns with your payment frequency.
7.  Select the "Payment Gateway" options that apply to your account.
8.  Fill in any additional "Payment Gateway Details" that might be relevant.
9.  Choose the "Billing Cycle Frequency" that fits your billing needs.
10.  Select the "Billing Cycle Type" that suits your billing preferences.
11.  Finally, indicate if you have any "Pricing Inquiry" needs or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Billing Preferences** (Billing Preferences, select multiple, false):
    *   This field allows you to select multiple billing preferences for your account.
*  **Value Perceptions** (Value Perceptions, text, false):
    *   Use this field to describe any specific value perceptions you have regarding your subscription.
*  **Billing Frequency** (Billing Frequency, select one, false):
    *   Choose the billing frequency that best fits your schedule.
*  **Billing Method** (Billing Method, select one, false):
    *   Select the method you prefer for billing.
*  **Billing Cycle** (Billing Cycle, select one, false):
    *   Choose the billing cycle that aligns with your payment frequency.
*  **Payment Gateway** (Payment Gateway, select multiple, false):
    *   Select the payment gateways that apply to your account.
*  **Payment Gateway Details** (Payment Gateway Details, text, false):
    *   Enter any additional details relevant to your payment gateway settings.
*  **Billing Cycle Frequency** (Billing Cycle Frequency, select one, false):
    *   Select the billing cycle frequency that fits your needs.
*  **Billing Cycle Type** (Billing Cycle Type, select one, false):
    *   Choose the type of billing cycle that suits your preferences.
*  **Pricing Inquiry** (Pricing Inquiry, select one, false):
    *   If you have any pricing concerns or inquiries, select this option to let us know.
