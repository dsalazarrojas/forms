# Father's Day Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a person's favorite Father's Day memories and experiences. It is intended to be a fun and engaging way to collect feedback and reflections from individuals about their dads.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1.  Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2.  Select your favorite gift that you received from your dad from the options provided.
3.  Write down your favorite memory of your dad in the dedicated text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the happiest Father's Day memory you have?** (`happy_dad_story`, text, required: false)
	+ A text field where you can write down your favorite Father's Day memory.
* **What is your Dad's name?** (`dad_s_name`, text, required: false)
	+ A text field where you can enter your dad's name.
* **What is your favorite gift that you received from your Dad?** (`favorite_gift`, text, required: false)
	+ A text field where you can select your favorite gift from the options provided.
* **Favorite Gift Options** (`favorite_gift_option_1`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ A set of pre-defined options for your favorite gift, including:
		- A tie
		- A book
		- A game
* **What is one thing that your Dad is especially good at?** (`dad_s_special_skill`, text, required: false)
	+ A text field where you can describe one thing that your dad is especially good at.
* **What is something funny that your Dad does that makes you laugh?** (`dad_s_funny_quirks`, text, required: false)
	+ A text field where you can write down something funny that your dad does.
* **What is something that you think is the best quality about your Dad?** (`dad_s_best_quality`, text, required: false)
	+ A text field where you can describe something that you think is the best quality about your dad.
* **What is your favorite memory of your Dad?** (`favorite_dad_memory`, text, required: false)
	+ A text field where you can write down your favorite memory of your dad.
