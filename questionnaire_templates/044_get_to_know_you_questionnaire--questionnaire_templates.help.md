# Get To Know You Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about themselves, including personal details such as name, email, and demographic information. The form is intended to be a general survey to get to know users better.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions on each page to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
