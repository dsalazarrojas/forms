# Match Maker Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Match Maker Form is designed to collect information about a potential match or connection between two individuals. This form helps you create a personalized match based on general information, match type, date, time, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the General Information section with any relevant details about the individual you are trying to match.
2. Select the type of match you are looking for (Romantic, Friendship, Business, etc.).
3. Enter the date and time of the match you are looking for.
4. Add any additional notes or comments in the Note section.
5. Enter the email and phone number of the individual you are trying to match.
6. In the Ideal Match section, enter the date and time you would ideally like the match to occur.
7. Add any additional information you would like to convey to the individual in the Ideal Additional Info section.
8. Finally, fill in the Ideal Email and Phone fields with your preferred contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (match_maker_form_general_info, text, required: false): Enter any general information about the individual you are trying to match, such as name, age, location, etc.
* **Match Type** (match_maker_form_match_type, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of match you are looking for (Romantic, Friendship, Business, etc.).
* **Match Date** (match_maker_form_match_date, date, required: false): Enter the date you would like the match to occur.
* **Match Time** (match_maker_form_match_time, time, required: false): Enter the time you would like the match to occur.
* **Note** (match_maker_form_note, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about the match.
* **Email** (match_maker_form_email, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the individual you are trying to match.
* **Phone** (match_maker_form_phone, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the individual you are trying to match.
* **Ideal Match Date** (match_maker_form_ideal_match_date, date, required: false): Enter the ideal date you would like the match to occur.
* **Ideal Match Time** (match_maker_form_ideal_match_time, time, required: false): Enter the ideal time you would like the match to occur.
* **Ideal Note** (match_maker_form_ideal_note, note, required: false): Add any ideal notes or comments about the match.
* **Ideal Email** (match_maker_form_ideal_email, email, required: false): Enter your ideal email address to be reached for the match.
* **Ideal Phone** (match_maker_form_ideal_phone, text, required: false): Enter your ideal phone number to be reached for the match.
* **Ideal Additional Info** (match_maker_form_ideal_additional_info, note, required: false): Add any ideal additional information you would like to convey to the individual.
