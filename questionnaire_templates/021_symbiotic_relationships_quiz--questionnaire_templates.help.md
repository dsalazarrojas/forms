# Symbiotic Relationships Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about symbiotic relationships between entities. Symbiotic relationships are mutualistic relationships where two or more entities interact in a way that benefits all parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and choose the correct answer based on your understanding of the symbiotic relationship.
2. Select the options that best describe the relationship you are thinking of.
3. If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask for help or clarification.
4. Double-check your answers to ensure they accurately reflect the relationship you're trying to describe.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Symbiotic relationships quiz** (`symbiotic_relationships_questionnaire`, text, optional): This is the main question where you need to describe the symbiotic relationship you have in mind. Please write a brief description of the relationship.
* **Relationship type description** (`symbiotic_relationship_description`, text, optional): This is a text field where you can provide additional context or information about the relationship you're thinking of. It can help clarify your answer to the previous question.
* **Symbiotic relationship 1** (`symbiotic_relationship_1`, select_one, optional): Choose the option that best describes the first relationship in your list. Please select only one option.
* **Symbiotic relationship 2** (`symbiotic_relationship_2`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the options that best describe the second relationship in your list. You can select multiple options if they apply.
* ... (This pattern continues until **Symbiotic relationship 22**)
 
## Tips

* If you're unsure about any relationship, you can always ask for help or clarification.
* You can also ask if you have any questions or concerns about the form itself.
* Remember to double-check your answers to ensure they accurately reflect the relationships you're trying to describe.
