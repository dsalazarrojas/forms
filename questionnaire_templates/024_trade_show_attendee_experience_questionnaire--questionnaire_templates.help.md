## Trade Show Attendee Experience Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from trade show attendees about their experience at the event. The information collected will help us understand what we did well and what we can improve on for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply read through each section carefully and answer the questions as they appear. Make sure to fill out all required fields, and don't hesitate to skip optional fields if they don't apply to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you if we need more information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further information.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Rate your overall experience at the event, on a scale of 0-10.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Describe your experience in the event, including any positive or negative comments.
* **Attended Days** (`attended_days`, number, required): If you attended the event for multiple days, enter the number of days you were present.
* **Attended Sessions** (`attended_sessions`, number, required): Indicate how many sessions you attended during the event.
* **Rated Sessions** (`rated_sessions`, number, required): Indicate which sessions you rated as part of this survey.
* **Satisfied** (`satisfied`, select_one, required): Choose if you are satisfied or not with your overall experience.
* **Would Recommend** (`would_recommend`, select_multiple, required): Choose which attendees you would recommend the event to (yourself, colleagues, manager, etc.).
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for us to improve the event, please enter them here.
* **Comments 2** (`comments2`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or thoughts you might have about the event.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): If you work for a company, enter the name of your company.
* **Position** (`position`, text, optional): If you have a position within your company, enter it here.
* **Attended Sessions 2** (`attended_sessions2`, number, optional): If you want to provide more details on sessions you attended, you can enter the number of sessions you attended.

Please take a few minutes to review this guide before proceeding to the form. If you have any questions, don't hesitate to ask.
