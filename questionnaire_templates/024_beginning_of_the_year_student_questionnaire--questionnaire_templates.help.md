# Beginning Of The Year Student Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you, the student, share your thoughts, feelings, and experiences with your teacher at the beginning of the year. It's a chance to express yourself and help your teacher get to know you better.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read through each section and understand what is being asked.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, being honest and thorough.
3. Use the hints provided for each question to guide you through the process.
4. Take your time, and do not hesitate to ask your teacher for help if you are unsure about anything.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (page 1, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to be known by your teacher.
* **Age** (page 1, required): Enter your age as of the current year.
* **Grade Level** (page 1, required): Select the grade level you are currently in.
* **Teacher Name** (page 1, required): Enter the name of your teacher.
* **Favorite Subject** (page 2, required): Select your favorite subject from the list.
* **Why Is This Your Favorite** (page 2, not required): If you chose a favorite subject, explain why you like it.
* **Most Challenging Subject** (page 2, required): Select the subject that you find most difficult.
* **How Do You Learn Best** (page 3, required): Select all the ways you learn best, such as by reading, listening, or working with technology.
* **Homework Habits** (page 3, required): Select how you usually approach homework.
* **Do You Enjoy Reading** (page 4, required): Choose how you feel about reading.
* **Favorite Books or Series** (page 4, not required): If you enjoy reading, list your favorite books or series.
* **Math Confidence** (page 4, required): Select how confident you are in math.
* **Hobbies and Interests** (page 5, required): Select all the activities you enjoy doing for fun.
* **Extracurricular Activities** (page 5, not required): If you are involved in any extracurricular activities, list them.
* **Best Friend in Class** (page 5, not required): If you have a best friend in class, list their name.
* **Classroom Preference** (page 6, required): Select where you prefer to sit in class.
* **Group Work Preference** (page 6, required): Choose how you feel about working in groups.
* **Goals for This Year** (page 7, not required): If you have any goals for the year, list them.
* **Worries About This Year** (page 7, not required): If you have any worries, list them.
* **Excited About This Year** (page 7, not required): If there's anything you are looking forward to this year, list it.
* **How Can Your Teacher Help You** (page 8, not required): If there's anything your teacher can do to help you, list it.
* **Something Special About You** (page 8, not required): If there's anything special or unique about you, share it.
* **Favorite School Memory** (page 8, not required): Share a happy school memory.
* **Best Thing About Your Summer** (page 8, not required): Share the best part of your summer.
* **Anything Else to Share** (page 9, not required): If there's anything else you'd like to share with your teacher, do so.

## Tips

* Take your time and answer each question carefully.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, ask your teacher for clarification.
* This form should not be filled out multiple times.
* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
