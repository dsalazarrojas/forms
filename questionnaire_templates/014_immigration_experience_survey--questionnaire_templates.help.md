# Immigration Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Immigration Experience Survey is designed to collect feedback and insights from individuals about their experiences with immigration-related processes. This form helps us better understand your experiences, which can inform improvements to these processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your title and name for the survey in the corresponding fields.
2. Answer the questions and scales according to your experiences, using numbers or text as required.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "survey_comment" field.
4. Enter your email address and phone number (if available) for follow-up or further clarification.
5. Submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **survey_title** (text, optional): Enter a brief title for your survey, e.g., "Recent Immigration Experience".
* **survey_name** (text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to appear on any reports or communications related to your survey.
* **survey_satisfaction** (number, optional): On a scale of 1-10, rate how satisfied you are with your immigration experience.
* **survey_support** (text, optional): Describe any challenges you faced with support services during your immigration process.
* **survey_challenge** (text, optional): Describe the biggest challenge you faced during your immigration process.
* **survey_satisfactionscale** (number, optional): On a scale of 1-10, rate how satisfied you are with the satisfaction you felt during your immigration process. Please note this is different from the overall **survey_satisfaction** field.
* **survey_suggest** (text, optional): Suggest improvements or changes that could enhance your immigration experience.
* **survey_comment** (note, optional): Include any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
* **survey_email** (email, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up or further clarification.
* **survey_phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number (if available) for follow-up or further clarification.
* **survey_challenge_1**, **survey_challenge_2**, ... (text, optional): Describe any additional challenges you faced during your immigration process.
* **survey_support_1**, **survey_support_2**, ... (text, optional): Describe any support services you received during your immigration process.
* **survey_suggestion_1**, **survey_suggestion_2**, ... (text, optional): Suggest improvements or changes that could enhance your immigration experience.
* **survey_satisfaction_1**, **survey_satisfaction_2**, ... (number, optional): On a scale of 1-10, rate your satisfaction with specific aspects of your immigration experience.
* **survey_satisfaction_scale_1**, **survey_satisfaction_scale_2**, ... (number, optional): On a scale of 1-10, rate the satisfaction you felt with specific aspects of your immigration experience.
