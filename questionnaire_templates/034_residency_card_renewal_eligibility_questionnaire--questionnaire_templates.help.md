# Residency Card Renewal Eligibility Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess if you are eligible for a residency card renewal. Please review the form carefully and ensure that all information is accurate and complete.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details:
	* First Name: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
	* Last Name: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
	* Address: Enter your current address.
2. Confirm your relationship status:
	* Has Dependent: If you have any dependents, select "Yes" and provide their names.
	* Has Spouse: If you have a spouse, select "Yes" and provide their name.
3. Provide your identity and contact information:
	* Date of Birth: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
	* Card Number: Enter your residency card number.
	* Expiration Date: Enter the expiration date of your residency card.
	* Issue Date: Enter the issue date of your residency card.
4. Confirm your renewal details:
	* Phone Number: Enter your phone number.
	* Email Address: Enter your email address.
5. Verify your identity:
	* Verification Date: Enter the date of verification for your residency card renewal.
6. Confirm your letter of verification:
	* Letter of Verification: Upload a letter of verification from your landlord or employer.
