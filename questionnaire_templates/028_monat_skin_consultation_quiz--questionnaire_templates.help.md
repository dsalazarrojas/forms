# Monat Skin Consultation Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Monat Skin Consultation Quiz" is a questionnaire designed to help users learn about and find the right Monat skin care products for their individual skin type and needs. This quiz asks users about their skin condition, type, and preferences to provide personalized recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name.
2. Enter your last name.
3. Select your birth date.
4. Choose the Monat product you are interested in.
5. Select the Monat product you have used or liked.
6. Select the Monat product you would recommend to others.
7. Choose your current skin condition.
8. Choose your skin type.
9. Select the Monat product that is suitable for you.
10. Enter your current skin care habits.
11. Select the Monat skin care products you use.
12. Enter the ingredients you like or dislike in Monat products.
13. Select the ingredient you would recommend.
14. Enter a description of your current skin care routine.
15. Enter any additional information or comments about Monat products.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Birth date** (`birth_date`, date, required): Select your birth date.
* **Interested In** (`monat_product_interested`, select, required): Choose a Monat product that interests you.
* **Used** (`monat_product_used`, select, required): Select the Monat product you have used or liked.
* **Loved** (`monat_product_liked`, select, required): Select the Monat product you have used or liked.
* **Recommended** (`monat_product_recommendation`, select, required): Select the Monat product you would recommend to others.
* **Skin Condition** (`monat_skin_condition`, select, required): Choose your current skin condition.
* **Skin Type** (`monat_skin_type`, select, required): Choose your skin type.
* **Suitable** (`monat_product_suitable`, select, required): Select the Monat product that is suitable for you.
* **Skin Care Habits** (`monat_skin_care_habits`, text, required): Enter your current skin care habits.
* **Skin Care Products** (`monat_skin_care_products`, select, required): Select the Monat skin care products you use.
* **Ingredients** (`monat_ingredients`, text, required): Enter the ingredients you like or dislike in Monat products.
* **Ingredients Liked** (`monat_ingredients_liked`, select, required): Select the ingredients you like in Monat products.
* **Ingredients Disliked** (`monat_ingredients_disliked`, select, required): Select the ingredients you dislike in Monat products.
* **Ingredients Recommendation** (`monat_ingredients_recommendation`, select, required): Select the ingredient you would recommend.
* **Skin Care Routine** (`monat_skin_care_routine`, text, required): Enter a description of your current skin care routine.
* **Monat** (`monat_monat_ingredients`, text, required): Enter any additional information or comments about Monat products.
