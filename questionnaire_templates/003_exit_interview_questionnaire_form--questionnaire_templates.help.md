# Exit Interview Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from departing employees about their reasons for leaving the organization. The purpose of this form is to help identify areas that can be improved to enhance employee retention, job satisfaction, and overall organizational performance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your work email address for contact and follow-up purposes.
3. Provide your job title and department information, if applicable.
4. Share the name of your manager, if you have one.
5. Describe the reason for your departure in the 'Why are you leaving?' field.
6. If you have any additional comments, feel free to add them in the 'Comments' field.
7. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (Text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (Email, required): Enter your work email address for contact and follow-up purposes.
* **What was your job title?** (Text, optional): If you have a specific job title, please provide it. This will help us understand your role and responsibilities.
* **What department did you work in?** (Text, optional): If you were part of a department, please specify it. This will aid in understanding the organizational structure.
* **Who was your manager?** (Text, optional): If you have a manager, please provide their name. This will help us understand the management structure.
* **Why are you leaving?** (Note, required): Describe the reason for your departure. This will help us identify areas that can be improved to reduce turnover.
* **Comments** (Note, optional): If you have any additional insights or comments, please share them here. This is an opportunity to provide further information or suggestions. 

## Tips

* Please be honest and provide a detailed reason for your departure.
* Keep your answers professional and respectful.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to reach out to your HR representative or contact the form creators.
