# Nutritional Assessment Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nutritional Assessment Questionnaire Form is designed to collect information about an individual's nutritional habits, health, and medical history. This form will help identify potential risks and areas for improvement in your diet and lifestyle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions honestly and accurately.
2. For numerical fields (e.g., Blood Sugar Level, Inflammation Level, etc.), provide your actual values.
3. For select fields (e.g., Food Frequency, Blood Type, etc.), choose the correct option.
4. For text fields (e.g., Eating Habits, Medication, etc.), provide a brief description.
5. If you have any allergies, please list them in the "Allergies" field.
6. For date fields (e.g., Date of Birth), use the correct format (DD-MM-YYYY).
