# Understandability Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide provides a step-by-step walkthrough on how to complete the Understandability Assessment Survey form. The form is designed to gather feedback on the understandability of a document. It is meant to be filled out by a reviewer of a document who has reviewed the content and is being asked to provide their honest assessment of how easy or difficult it was to understand the document.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the survey form completely and honestly.
2. Answer the question "how_easy_to_understand" with a number (1-5) that indicates how easy or difficult it was to understand the document.
3. Provide any comments or suggestions you have about the document in the "comments" field.
4. If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them in the "suggestions_for_improvement" field.
5. Fill out the "document_name", "document_url", "reviewer_name", "reviewer_title", "reviewer_department", and "reviewer_email" fields with the relevant information about the document you reviewed.
6. Finally, answer the question "understandability_scale" with "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether or not you think the document is easy to understand.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How Easy to Understand** (`how_easy_to_understand`, number, required): This question asks you to rate how easy or difficult it was to understand the document, on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Instructions** (`instructions`, text, required): Please provide any instructions or feedback you have about the document.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Please provide any comments you have about the document.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggest_improvements`, text, required): If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them in this field.
* **Action Items** (`action_items`, text, required): Please describe any action items you have for the document.
* **Understandability Scale** (`understandability_scale`, select_one, required): This question asks you to indicate whether or not you think the document is easy to understand. Answer "Yes" if you think the document is easy to understand, and "No" if you do not.
* **Action Items Improvement** (`action_items_improvement`, text, required): If you have any suggestions for improvement on the action items, type them in this field.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, required): If you have any other suggestions for improvement, type them in this field.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): This field is a duplicate of the "comments" field above, and is intended to be used for any additional comments or feedback.
* **Document Name** (`document_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the document you reviewed.
* **Document URL** (`document_url`, text, required): Please enter the URL of the document you reviewed.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, required): Please enter your name.
* **Reviewer Title** (`reviewer_title`, text, required): Please enter your title or position.
* **Reviewer Department** (`reviewer_department`, text, required): Please enter the department or group you belong to.
* **Reviewer Email** (`reviewer_email`, email, required): Please enter your email address.

## Tips
* Answer the questions honestly and accurately.
* Fill out the form completely and thoroughly.
* Use specific examples and descriptions when providing feedback or suggestions.
* Review the form before submitting it to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
