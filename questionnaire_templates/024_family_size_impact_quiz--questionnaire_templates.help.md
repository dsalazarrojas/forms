# Family Size Impact Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Size Impact Quiz is designed to collect information about the number of people in a household and their impact on various aspects of family life, such as finances and the environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and fill out the relevant fields with accurate information about your household. You can select from multiple choice options for questions that have options, and simply type in the number of people in your household for numerical fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your household size** (`household_size`, number, required): This field asks for the number of people currently living in your household. Be as accurate as possible.
* **What is your household income** (`household_income`, number, required): This field asks for the annual income of your household. If you are unsure, you can estimate or leave blank.
* **Number of children in your household** (`number_of_children`, number, required): This field asks for the number of children currently living in your household.
* **What type of family do you have** (`family_type`, select_one, required): This field asks for the type of family you have (e.g. married with children, single parent, etc.). Select the option that best describes your household.
* **What type of arrangements do you have for childcare** (`family_arrangements`, select_multiple, required): This field asks about the types of childcare arrangements you have in place (e.g. day care, after school program, etc.). Select all that apply.
* **How do you think your household size affects your finances?** (`financial_impact`, select_one, required): This field asks for your opinion on how having a larger household affects your financial situation.
* **How do you think your household size affects the environment?** (`environmental_impact`, select_one, required): This field asks for your opinion on how having a larger household affects the environment.
* **What are the arrangements in your household** (`household_arrangements`, select_multiple, required): This field asks about the types of arrangements you have in place for your household members (e.g. work from home, etc.). Select all that apply.
* **What are the living arrangements in your household** (`living_arrangements`, select_multiple, required): This field asks about the types of living arrangements you have in your household (e.g. shared space, separate rooms, etc.). Select all that apply.
* **What type of housing do you live in** (`housing_type`, select_one, required): This field asks for the type of housing you live in (e.g. house, apartment, etc.).
* **What type of family structure do you have** (`family_structure`, select_multiple, required): This field asks for the types of family structure you have (e.g. single parent, married couple, etc.). Select all that apply.
* **What is the location of your household** (`household_location`, select_one, required): This field asks for the location of your household (e.g. city, rural area, etc.).
* **What was your household size 5 years ago?** (`household_size_5_years`, number, required): This field asks for the number of people in your household 5 years ago.
* **What is your family status** (`family_status`, select_multiple, required): This field asks for your current family status (e.g. single, married, divorced, etc.). Select all that apply.
* **How big was your household 5 years ago?** (`family_size_5_years`, number, required): This field asks for the number of people in your household 5 years ago.
* **How big was your household 10 years ago?** (`family_size_10_years`, number, required): This field asks for the number of people in your household 10 years ago.
* **How big was your household 20 years ago?** (`family_size_20_years`, number, required): This field asks for the number of people in your household 20 years ago.
* **How big was your household 30 years ago?** (`family_size_30_years`, number, required): This field asks for the number of people in your household 30 years ago.
* **How big was your household 40 years ago?** (`family_size_40_years`, number, required): This field asks for the number of people in your household 40 years ago.
