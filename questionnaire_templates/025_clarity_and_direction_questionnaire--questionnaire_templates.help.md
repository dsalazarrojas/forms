# Clarity And Direction Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you understand your current level of clarity and direction in various life areas. It will guide you through a series of questions that will help you identify your strengths, weaknesses, values, and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question carefully and honestly. You may need to refer to your past experiences or goals when answering some questions. Remember that this form is a personal reflection exercise, and the results will help you gain a deeper understanding of yourself and your goals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (1): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (2): Please enter your email address, which will be used for follow-up communication. We respect your privacy and will not share your email address with anyone.
* **Phone Number** (3): Enter your best contact number. This will help us reach you for further clarification if needed.
* **Occupation or Profession** (4): What do you do? This will help us understand your context and background.
* **How Clear Are You About Your Current Goals?** (5): Rate your level of clarity about your current goals on a scale of Very clear, Somewhat clear, Neutral, Somewhat unclear, or Very unclear.
* **What Is Your Primary Challenge Right Now?** (6): Select the main challenge you're currently facing from the options provided.
* **Describe Your Challenge in Detail** (7): Provide more context about your current challenge.
* **What Is Your Desired Timeframe for Goals?** (8): Select the timeframe in which you'd like to see results on a scale of Immediate (within 1 month), Short-term (1-3 months), Medium-term (3-6 months), Long-term (6-12 months), or No specific timeframe.
* **Which Life Areas Need Clarity?** (9): Select all life areas that you'd like to gain clarity in. This will help us identify areas that require attention.
* **Have You Tried to Address This Before?** (10): Select how many times you've tried to address this challenge or goal before.
* **What Did You Try Before?** (11): Describe any previous attempts you've made to address this challenge or goal.
* **Do You Have a Support System?** (12): Select the level of support you have from friends, family, or mentors.
* **How Do You Typically Make Decisions?** (13): Select your decision-making style.
* **What Are Your Key Strengths?** (14): Describe your personal strengths that you're proud of.
* **What Areas Do You Want to Improve?** (15): Identify areas you'd like to grow or improve in.
* **What Values Are Most Important to You?** (16): Select the values that are most important to you.
* **What Would Success Look Like?** (17): Describe your ideal outcome.
* **What Obstacles Do You Anticipate?** (18): Identify potential barriers that may hinder your progress.
* **How Committed Are You to Making Changes?** (19): Rate your level of commitment on a scale from Fully committed to Just exploring.
* **What Type of Guidance Do You Prefer?** (20): Select the type of guidance you prefer, such as structured action plans, regular check-ins, or self-directed resources.
* **How Much Time Can You Dedicate Weekly?** (21): Select the amount of time you can dedicate to working on your goals.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (22): Share any additional comments or questions you have, or any further clarification that may be useful to answer the form.
