# Mpox Vaccine Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This Mpox Vaccine Questionnaire is designed to gather essential information about your vaccination status, medical history, and other relevant details to ensure you receive accurate and efficient information for vaccine administration.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, navigate through each page and fill in the required information.
2. Start with the contact information page, where you will be asked for your name, email, and phone number.
3. On the next page, select your current vaccine status from the provided options.
4. Proceed to the page for your Date of Birth and enter the requested date.
5. Next, enter your phone number and occupation.
6. The following pages will ask for your Medical Status, Medical Note (if applicable), Consent, and other information (select multiple options as needed).
7. Finally, provide your Vaccination Date and Follow-up Appointment details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information (Page 1)** 
  * Contact Information: Enter your contact information in the format requested (e.g., name, phone number, and email).
* **Vaccine Status (Page 2)**
  * vaccine_status: Select your current vaccine status from the provided options (Not Eligible, Eligible, Partially Eligible).
* **Date of Birth (Page 3)**
  * Date of Birth: Enter your date of birth in the requested format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Phone Number (Page 4)**
  * Phone Number: Enter your phone number in the format requested.
* **Email (Page 5)**
  * Email: Enter your email address in the format requested.
* **Occupation (Page 6)**
  * Occupation: Enter your current occupation or job title.
* **Medical Status (Page 7)**
  * Medical Status: Select your medical status from the provided options (Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Medical Note (Page 8)**
  * Medical Note: Enter any additional medical notes or concerns you have.
* **Consent (Page 9)**
  * Consent: Select one or more of the provided consent options (Agree, Disagree, Not Sure).
* **Other (Page 10)**
  * Other: Select one or more options that apply to you (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Vaccination Date (Page 11)**
  * Vaccination Date: Enter the date you received your vaccination in the format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Follow-up Appointment (Page 12)**
  * Follow-up Appointment: Enter the date and time of your scheduled follow-up appointment in the format (YYYY-MM-DD).

Note: If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to the relevant authorities for clarification and support.
