# Calmness Coaching Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients to help coaches understand their current stress levels, identify areas that require attention, and set goals for coaching sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the client's full name in the "Client Name" field (page 1).
* Select the date of the assessment or session in the "Session Date" field (page 1).
* Rate your current stress level in the "Current Stress Level" field (page 2), using one of the following options: Very Low, Low, Moderate, High, Very High.
* Rate your sense of calmness in the "Calmness Rating" field (page 2), using one of the following options: Very Calm, Calm, Neutral, Uneasy, Very Uneasy.
* If applicable, list the top contributors to your stress in the "Primary Stressors" field (page 3).
* Describe the techniques you currently use to manage stress in the "Current Coping Strategies" field (page 3).
* Rate your sleep quality in the "Sleep Quality" field (page 4), using one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* Specify the changes you'd like to make through coaching in the "Goals for Coaching" field (page 5).
* Indicate how ready you are to make changes in the "Readiness to Change" field (page 5), using one of the following options: Very Ready, Ready, Unsure, Not Ready.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, please share them in the "Additional Comments" field (page 6).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name (Page 1, Required)**: Enter your full name.
* **Session Date (Page 1, Required)**: Select the date of the assessment or session.
* **Current Stress Level (Page 2, Required)**: Rate your overall stress level today.
	+ Very Low: You feel very relaxed and calm.
	+ Low: You feel relatively calm.
	+ Moderate: You feel somewhat stressed, but manageable.
	+ High: You feel stressed and overwhelmed.
	+ Very High: You feel extremely stressed.
* **Calmness Rating (Page 2, Required)**: Rate your sense of calmness today.
	+ Very Calm: You feel very calm and serene.
	+ Calm: You feel relatively calm.
	+ Neutral: You feel neither calm nor stressed.
	+ Uneasy: You feel somewhat stressed.
	+ Very Uneasy: You feel highly stressed.
* **Primary Stressors (Page 3, Optional)**: List the top contributors to your stress.
* **Current Coping Strategies (Page 3, Optional)**: Describe the techniques you use to manage stress.
* **Sleep Quality (Page 4, Required)**: Rate your sleep quality recently.
	+ Excellent: You slept very well.
	+ Good: You slept relatively well.
	+ Fair: You had some sleep issues.
	+ Poor: You had a poor night's sleep.
* **Goals for Coaching (Page 5, Required)**: Specify the changes you'd like to make through coaching.
* **Readiness to Change (Page 5, Required)**: Indicate how ready you are to make changes.
	+ Very Ready: You're very eager to make changes.
	+ Ready: You're willing to make changes.
	+ Unsure: You're unsure about making changes.
