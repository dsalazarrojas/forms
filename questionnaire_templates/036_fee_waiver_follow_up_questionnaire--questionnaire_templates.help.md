# Fee Waiver Follow Up Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fee Waiver Follow Up Questionnaire is designed to gather information about the changes that have occurred in a student's financial situation after a fee waiver has been granted. This form helps to ensure that the waiver is still valid and that the student's financial circumstances have not changed significantly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Applicant Details: Please provide your name, ID number, and other relevant identifying information.
2. Upload any required documents: If there are any documents required by the school or financial aid office, please upload them as per the instructions provided.
3. Describe any financial changes: If there have been any changes in your financial situation since the fee waiver was granted, please provide a detailed explanation.
4. Select any school changes: Indicate if there have been any changes in your school enrollment, academic progress, or other relevant information.
5. Provide review comments: If you have any comments or feedback regarding the review process, please share them with us.
6. Add any other school changes: If there is any other information that you would like to share with the reviewer, please use this section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Details**: Please enter your name and ID number to confirm your identity.
* **Document Uploads**: Upload any required documents, such as proof of income or tax returns, as instructed by the school or financial aid office.
* **Financial Changes**: Describe any changes in your financial situation since the fee waiver was granted. This could include changes in income, employment, or other relevant financial information.
* **School Changes**: Select any changes that have occurred in your school enrollment, such as a change in major or withdrawal from the current semester.
* **Review Comments**: Share any comments or feedback you have about the review process.
* **School Changes Other**: If you have any other information that you would like to share with the reviewer, please use this section to describe it.
* **Review Comments**: Share any comments or feedback you have about the review process.
