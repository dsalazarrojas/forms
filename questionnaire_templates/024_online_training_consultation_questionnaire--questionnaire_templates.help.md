# Online Training Consultation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about you and your training goals, progress, and preferences in order to provide a personalized online training consultation. The consultation will help you create a tailored training plan that meets your specific needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the following pages:

* Page 1: Goals - Describe your training goals in 50 words or less.
* Page 2: Progress - Enter your current progress in the training program (numeric value).
* Page 3: Health details - Provide any health-related information that may impact your training, such as weight, height, etc.
* Page 4: Training Frequency - Enter the number of times you train per week.
* Page 5: Nutrition Plan - Describe your current nutrition plan.
* Page 6: Motivation - Share any motivation or goals you have for achieving your training goals.
* Page 7: Exercise Routine - Describe your current exercise routine, including any focus on cardio, strength, endurance, etc.
* Page 8: Medical Conditions - If you have any medical conditions, please describe them.
* Page 9: Relevance to Doctor - Select if this consultation is relevant to your doctor.
* Page 10: Training Plan - Select if you would like to create a personal training plan.
* Page 11: Contact Details - Select how you would like to be contacted (phone, email, mail).
* Page 12: Submitter Details - Select who is submitting this form (individual, group) and provide their name, email, phone number, organization, department, job title, and location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are your goals** (Page 1) (`goals`, text, required: false)
  This field is where you can describe your training goals, such as running a marathon, losing weight, or improving overall fitness.
* **Current Progress** (Page 2) (`current_progress`, number, required: false)
  Enter your current progress in the training program as a numeric value.
* **Health details** (Page 3) (`health_details`, text, required: false)
  Provide any health-related information that may impact your training, such as weight, height, etc.
* **How often do you train per week** (Page 4) (`training_frequency`, number, required: false)
  Enter the number of times you train per week.
* **Nutrition Plan** (Page 5) (`nutrition_plan`, text, required: false)
  Describe your current nutrition plan, including any dietary restrictions or preferences.
* **Motivation** (Page 6) (`motivation`, text, required: false)
  Share any motivation or goals you have for achieving your training goals.
* **Exercise routine** (Page 7) (`exercise_routine`, text, required: false)
  Describe your current exercise routine, including any focus on cardio, strength, endurance, etc.
* **Medical Conditions** (Page 8) (`medical_conditions`, text, required: false)
  If you have any medical conditions, please describe them.
* **Is this relevant to your doctor?** (Page 9) (`doctor_relevant`, select_one, required: false)
  Select if this consultation is relevant to your doctor.
* **Would you like to create a personal training plan?** (Page 10) (`training_plan`, select_one, required: false)
  Select if you would like to create a personal training plan.
* **How would you like to be contacted?** (Page 11) (`contact_details`, select_multiple, required: false)
  Select how you would like to be contacted (phone, email, mail).
* **Who is the submitter?** (Page 12) (`submitter_type`, select_multiple, required: false)
  Select who is submitting this form (individual, group).
* **Submitter Name** (Page 12) (`submitter_name`, text, required: false)
  Provide the submitter's name.
* **Submitter Email** (Page 12) (`submitter_email`, email, required: false)

* **Submitter Location** (Page 12) (`submitter_location`, text, required: false)

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure you receive the most accurate and personalized consultation.
* If you are unsure about any field, please describe your best effort or approximation.
* Please note that your submission will be reviewed and used to provide personalized advice only for your specific training goals and goals.
* Please be respectful and considerate of other users who may have similar goals or progress.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, please reach out to us at [support email].
