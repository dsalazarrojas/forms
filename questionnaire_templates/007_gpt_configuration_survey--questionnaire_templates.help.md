# gpt_configuration_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The gpt_configuration_survey is a form designed to gather information from users about their experience with a GPT (Generative Pre-trained Transformer) model. This form aims to collect feedback on the usability and configuration of the GPT model, providing valuable insights for improvement and optimization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Review the form sections**: The form is divided into several sections, each with its own set of questions or fields.
2. **Answer each field**: Go through each section and answer the questions or fill out the fields as instructed.
3. **Submit your feedback**: Once you have completed all the sections, click on the "Submit" button to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Survey Questions (Page 1)
• **Survey Questions** (`survey_questions`, text, optional): Describe your experience with the GPT model. You can provide any relevant details about your experience, such as successes, challenges, or areas where you'd like to see improvement.
### GPT Version (`gpt_version`, select_one, optional): Select the version of the GPT model you used or are currently using. This will help us understand the specific capabilities and limitations of the model.
### Setup Preferences (`setup_preferences`, text, optional): If you had to set up or configure the GPT model for your use case, please describe the process and any preferences you used.
### Usability Issues (`usability_issues`, select_multiple, optional): Check any usability issues you encountered while using the GPT model. You can select multiple options that apply to your experience.
	+ Not applicable: No issues were reported.
	+ Somewhat applicable: There were some minor issues reported.
	+ Very applicable: Severe issues were reported.
### Submit Comments (`submit_comments`, note, optional): Leave any additional comments or suggestions you have for improving the GPT model.

**Tips**:
* Please be as detailed and specific as possible when answering the questions or filling out the fields.
* If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, feel free to include them in the "Submit Comments" field.
* Your feedback is invaluable in helping us improve the GPT model. Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
