# Renter Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Renter Questionnaire is a form used to collect information from renters for property management purposes. It gathers essential data to help landlords or property managers understand the renter's details, lease terms, and property requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Renter Questionnaire, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective text fields.
2. Provide your Email address for contact purposes.
3. Enter your Phone number for communication.
4. Indicate your Renter Status (Active, Non-Active, or Pending).
5. Enter the Lease Start Date and Lease End Date.
6. Select the Move In Date and Move Out Date.
7. Choose the Property Type (Apartment, House, Condo, Duplex, or Single Family Home).
8. Indicate the Renter Type (Individual, Family, or Organization).
9. Optionally, provide the Landlord Name and Property Address.
10. Select any additional Property Types if applicable.
11. Optionally, provide the Unit Number and Lease Length.
12. Enter the Rent amount.
13. If applicable, enter the Move In Date 2 and Move Out Date 2.
14. Enter the Property Size, Bedrooms, Bathrooms, and Square Footage.
15. Optionally, indicate if there is a Parking Lot available.
16. Add any Notes if necessary.
