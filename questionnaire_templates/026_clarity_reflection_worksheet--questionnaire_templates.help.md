# Clarity Reflection Worksheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Clarity Reflection Worksheet is a self-assessment tool designed to help individuals gain insight into their mental clarity and focus. It's a 23-question form that guides users through a series of questions to identify their strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Answer the demographic questions
1. **Full Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your first and last name.
2. **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): For worksheet records.
3. **Date of Reflection** (`reflection_date`, date, required): Enter the current date.
4. **What Period Are You Reflecting On** (`reflection_period`, select_one, required): Select the timeframe you're reflecting on (Today, This week, This month, This quarter, This year, Specific event or situation).

### Step 2: Answer the reflection questions
1. **What Wins or Achievements Did You Have?** (`wins_achievements`, text, required): List your successes.
2. **What Challenges Did You Face?** (`challenges_faced`, text, required): Describe obstacles encountered.
3. **What Lessons Did You Learn?** (`lessons_learned`, text, required): Key takeaways from this period.
4. **What Emotions Did You Experience Most?** (`emotions_experienced`, select_multiple, required): Select all emotions you experienced (Joy, Excitement, Contentment, Frustration, Anxiety, Sadness, Anger, Fear, Hope, Gratitude).
5. **How Aligned Were Your Actions With Your Values?** (`values_alignment`, select_one, required): Rate your alignment (Completely aligned, Mostly aligned, Somewhat aligned, Slightly aligned, Not aligned at all).
6. **What Activities Gave You Energy?** (`energy_sources`, text, optional): What energized you.
7. **What Activities Drained Your Energy?** (`energy_drains`, text, optional): What depleted you.
8. **How Are Your Key Relationships?** (`relationships_reflection`, select_one, required): Select the status of your key relationships (Thriving, Good, Stable, Strained, Needing attention).
9. **How Is Your Progress Toward Goals?** (`goals_progress`, select_one, required): Rate your progress (Exceeding expectations, On track, Slightly behind, Significantly behind, No progress made).
10. **What Adjustments Do You Need to Make?** (`adjustments_needed`, text, required): Changes for improvement.
11. **Which Strengths Did You Use?** (`strengths_used`, text, optional): Your personal strengths.
12. **What Areas Need Growth or Development?** (`growth_areas`, text, optional): Skills or habits to develop.
13. **What Are You Grateful For?** (`gratitude_items`, text, required): List things you appreciate.
14. **What Action Steps Will You Take Next?** (`action_steps`, text, required): Specific next steps.
15. **What Support Do You Need?** (`support_needed`, text, optional): Help from others.
16. **What Is Your Commitment Moving Forward?** (`commitment_statement`, text, required): Your personal commitment.
17. **How Confident Are You in Your Plan?** (`confidence_level`, select_one, required): Rate your confidence (Very confident, Confident, Somewhat confident, Not very confident, Uncertain).
18. **Additional Notes or Thoughts?** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Any other reflections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your name to identify yourself in the worksheet.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for future reference and tracking.
* **Date of Reflection**: Enter the current date to reflect on your progress.
* **What Period Are You Reflecting On**: Select the timeframe you're reflecting on to help you focus on your thoughts and experiences.
* **What Wins or Achievements Did You Have?**: List your successes to highlight your accomplishments.
* **What Challenges Did You Face?**: Describe obstacles you encountered to identify areas for improvement.
* **What Lessons Did You Learn?**: Record key takeaways from this period to aid in future decision-making.
* **What Emotions Did You Experience Most?**: Select all emotions you experienced to gain insight into your emotional state.
* **How Aligned Were Your Actions With Your Values?**: Rate your alignment to evaluate how well you're living up to your values.
* **What Activities Gave You Energy?**: List activities that energized you to identify sources of motivation.
* **What Activities Drained Your Energy?**: Describe activities that depleted you to identify areas to avoid in the future.
* **How Are Your Key Relationships?**: Select the status of your key relationships to assess their impact on your life.
* **How Is Your Progress Toward Goals?**: Rate your progress to evaluate how effectively you're working toward your goals.
* **What Adjustments Do You Need to Make?**: Identify changes you need to make to improve.
* **Which Strengths Did You Use?**: List your personal strengths to highlight your capabilities.
* **What Areas Need Growth or Development?**: Identify skills or habits that need improvement.
* **What Are You Grateful For?**: List things you appreciate to cultivate a positive outlook.
* **What Action Steps Will You Take Next?**: Plan specific actions to move forward.
* **What Support Do You Need?**: Identify areas where you need help from others.
* **What Is Your Commitment Moving Forward?**: Record your personal commitment to yourself and your goals.
* **How Confident Are You in Your Plan?**: Rate your confidence in your plan.
* **Additional Notes or Thoughts?**: Record any additional thoughts or reflections.
* **Confidence Level**: Rate your confidence level in your ability to achieve your goals.
* **Additional Notes**: Any additional thoughts or reflections.

## Tips
* Be honest and authentic in your reflections.
* Answer questions thoughtfully and to the best of your ability.
* Use this form regularly to track your progress and changes over time.
* Review your answers and adjust your approach as needed.
* Consider discussing your reflections with a trusted friend or mentor for further insight.
