# Urine Color Analysis Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users about their urine color observations for medical research or diagnostic purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the questions and fields carefully before starting.
2. Answer the first question by selecting one of the two options provided.
3. Enter a numerical value for the second question.
4. Choose a time of day for your urine color observation.
5. Provide any additional comments or details in the note field.
6. If desired, enter your email address and/or phone number so we can contact you.
7. Finally, submit the form to complete the quiz.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Question** (First Question, Select One, Optional): This is a multiple-choice question to gather information about your urine color observation.
* **Second Question** (Second Question, Number, Required): Please enter a numerical value to answer this question.
* **Time of Day** (Time of Day, Time, Optional): Select the time of day for your urine color observation.
* **Note** (Note, Note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or details about your urine color observation.
* **Email** (Email, Email, Optional): If you wish to be contacted, please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, Text, Optional): If you wish to be contacted, please enter your phone number.
* **Submitted At** (Submitted At, Date, Optional): This field is automatically populated when you submit the form, and you do not need to fill it in.
