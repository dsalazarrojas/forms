# Urine Color Analysis Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users about their urine color observations for medical research or diagnostic purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the questions and fields carefully before starting.
2. Answer the first question by selecting one of the two options provided.
3. Enter a numerical value for the second question.
4. Choose a time of day for your urine color observation.
5. Provide any additional comments or details in the note field.
6. If desired, enter your email address and/or phone number so we can contact you.
7. Finally, submit the form to complete the quiz.
