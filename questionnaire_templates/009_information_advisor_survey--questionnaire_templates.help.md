# Information Advisor Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Information Advisor Survey is a form designed to collect information from users about their experiences with our information advisors. This form helps us understand how our advisors are meeting your needs and identify areas for improvement. Your feedback is crucial in helping us provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
4. Enter the name of the advisor you are providing feedback about in the "advisor name" field.
5. Choose a date for the survey. Select a date you visited or interacted with the advisor.
6. Choose a time for the survey. Select the time you visited or interacted with the advisor.
7. Rate your experience with the advisor on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
8. Provide any additional comments about your experience with the advisor in the "comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name, which will be used to personalize your feedback.
    *   Note: This field is not required for the survey.
*   **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name, which will be used to identify you for feedback purposes.
    *   Note: This field is not required but recommended for proper identification.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address to contact you about your feedback.
    *   Note: This field is required and must be a valid email address.
*   **Advisor name** (`advisor_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the advisor you are providing feedback about.
    *   Note: This field is not required but recommended for accurate feedback.
*   **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Choose the date you visited or interacted with the advisor.
*   **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Choose the time you visited or interacted with the advisor.
*   **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required): Rate your experience with the advisor on a scale of 1-5.
    *   Note: The lower the rating, the lower the quality of service; the higher the rating, the better the service.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the advisor.
    *   Note: This field is not required but recommended for detailed feedback.
