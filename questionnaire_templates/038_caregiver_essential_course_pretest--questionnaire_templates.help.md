# Caregiver Essential Course Pretest - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge the knowledge, skills, and experience of caregivers before they start the Caregiver Essential Course. The purpose of this pretest is to help the course developers understand the starting level of the learners and tailor the course content and structure to their needs. It is essential to fill out this form honestly and thoroughly to ensure you get the most out of the course.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Start by filling out your personal information**, such as your name, email address, and which course you are taking. Please be honest and accurate when filling out this section.
2. **Answer the questions** in the following sections, selecting the options that best describe your experience and knowledge:
   - **Caregiving Experience**: Select the number of years you have been a caregiver.
   - **Type of Caregiving Experience**: Choose the types of care you have provided in the past.
   - **Formal Training**: Indicate if you have completed formal caregiving training or not.
   - **CPR Certification**: Confirm your current CPR certification status.
   - **Primary Motivation for Taking This Course**: Briefly explain why you want to take this course.
   - **Confidence in Personal Care Skills**, **Confidence in Medication Management**, **Confidence in Mobility and Transfers**, **Confidence in Communication Skills**, **Confidence in Providing Emotional Support**, **Knowledge of Infection Control**, **Knowledge of Safety Procedures**, **Knowledge of Dementia or Cognitive Decline**, **Knowledge of Elder Abuse and Neglect**, and **Learning Goals**: Choose the options that best reflect your current skills and confidence levels.
3. **Select up to 3 skills you want to improve** from the list provided.
4. **Identify your greatest challenge as a caregiver**, if you have one.
5. **Mention any support or resources you need** to help you learn and complete the course.
6. **Choose your preferred learning style**.
7. **List any accessibility or accommodation needs** you may have.
8. **Provide any additional comments or information** that you think would be helpful for the course developers to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: This field is for your full name, which will be used to identify you in the course.
* **Email Address**: This field is for your email address, which will be used for course communication.
* **Course Name**: This field is for the name of the course you are taking.
* **Pretest Date**: This field is for the date you are completing this pretest.
* **Years of Caregiving Experience**: This field is for the number of years you have been a caregiver.
* **Type of Caregiving Experience**: This field is for the types of care you have provided in the past.
* **Formal Caregiving Training**: This field is for indicating if you have completed formal caregiving training or not.
* **CPR or First Aid Certification**: This field is for confirming your current CPR or first aid certification status.
* **Primary Motivation for Taking This Course**: This field is for briefly explaining why you want to take this course.
* **Confidence in Personal Care Skills**: This field is for choosing your confidence level in providing personal care.
* **Confidence in Medication Management**: This field is for choosing your confidence level in managing medications.
* **Confidence in Mobility and Transfers**: This field is for choosing your confidence level in assisting with mobility and transfers.
* **Confidence in Communication Skills**: This field is for choosing your confidence level in communicating with care recipients and family.
* **Confidence in Providing Emotional Support**: This field is for choosing your confidence level in providing emotional support.
* **Knowledge of Infection Control**: This field is for choosing your knowledge level in infection control.
* **Knowledge of Safety Procedures**: This field is for choosing your knowledge level in safety procedures.
* **Knowledge of Dementia or Cognitive Decline**: This field is for choosing your knowledge level in dementia or cognitive decline.
* **Knowledge of Elder Abuse and Neglect**: This field is for choosing your knowledge level in elder abuse and neglect.
* **Learning Goals**: This field is for choosing up to 3 skills you want to improve.
* **Greatest Challenge**: This field is for mentioning your greatest challenge as a caregiver, if you have one.
* **Support Needs**: This field is for mentioning any support or resources you need to help you learn and complete the course.
* **Preferred Learning Style**: This field is for choosing your preferred learning style.
* **Accessibility or Accommodation Needs**: This field is for listing any special accommodations you may need.
