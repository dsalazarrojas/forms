# Supervised Visit Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from supervisors about the outcomes of visits. The goal is to collect information about the purpose of the visit, the main issue discussed, and any other relevant details. The supervisor's name, email, phone number, visit date, and visit time can also be entered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit Feedback" button to begin.
2. Select a visit to provide feedback for by selecting the "Visit Date" and "Visit Time".
3. Enter the "Supervisor Name", "Supervisor Email", and "Supervisor Phone" fields with the relevant information.
4. In the "What was the purpose of this supervised visit?" field, enter a brief description of the visit's purpose.
5. In the "What was the main issue discussed during the visit?" field, enter the primary issue or topic discussed during the visit.
6. If the issue discussed during the visit was not listed in the provided options, select "If other, please specify" and enter a brief description of the issue.
7. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was the purpose of this supervised visit? (Text)**: Enter a brief description of the visit's purpose.
* **What was the main issue discussed during the visit? (Text)**: Enter the primary issue or topic discussed during the visit.
* **If other, please specify (Text)**: Select this option if the issue discussed during the visit was not listed in the provided options, and enter a brief description of the issue.
* **Supervisor Name (Text)**: Enter the supervisor's name who conducted the visit.
* **Supervisor Email (Email)**: Enter the supervisor's email address.
* **Supervisor Phone (Text)**: Enter the supervisor's phone number.
* **Visit Date (Date)**: Select the date of the visit.
* **Visit Time (Time)**: Select the time of the visit.

## Tips

* Ensure that you are providing feedback for a visit that actually occurred.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to reach out to the form administrator for clarification.
