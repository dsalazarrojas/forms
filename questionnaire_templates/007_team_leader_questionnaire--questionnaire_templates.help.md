# Team Leader Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Team Leader Questionnaire is used to assess the skills and abilities of team leaders within your organization. It helps identify their strengths and areas for development, enabling them to grow and improve their leadership capabilities. This form is typically used for internal evaluation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly, based on your self-assessment.
2. Select all the skills that apply to you from the provided options.
3. Make sure to answer all required questions (indicated by `required: true`).
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Leadership Skills (`leadership_skills`, select_multiple, required: false)
*   **Leadership Skills**: Select all the leadership skills that you currently possess, such as strategic thinking, problem-solving, decision-making, and communication. This will help us understand your overall leadership ability and capacity to lead the team effectively.

### Decision Making (`decision_making`, select_multiple, required: false)
*   **Decision Making**: Indicate whether you possess the ability to make sound and informed decisions, considering multiple perspectives and factors. Select 'Yes' if you feel confident in your decision-making skills.

### Emotional Intelligence (`emotional_intelligence`, select_multiple, required: false)
*   **Emotional Intelligence**: Choose all the aspects of emotional intelligence you feel you possess, such as empathy, self-awareness, and social skills. This will help us understand how you interact with your team and manage conflicts.

### Conflict Resolution (`conflict_resolution`, select_multiple, required: false)
*   **Conflict Resolution**: Select all the skills you use to resolve conflicts, including negotiation, mediation, and communication. This will help us evaluate your ability to manage and resolve disputes within the team.

### Team Motivation (`team_motivation`, select_multiple, required: false)
*   **Team Motivation**: Indicate which methods you use to motivate and engage your team, such as setting clear goals, providing feedback, and creating a positive work environment. Select 'Yes' if you feel you're effective in motivating your team members.

### Communication Skills (`communication_skills`, select_multiple, required: false)
*   **Communication Skills**: Choose all the skills you employ to communicate effectively within the team, such as verbal, non-verbal, and written communication. This will help us assess your ability to convey ideas and collaborate with your team.

### Leadership Styles (`leadership_styles`, select_multiple, required: false)
*   **Leadership Styles**: Select all the leadership styles that you identify with, such as autocratic, democratic, or transformational leadership. This will help us understand your approach to leading the team and making decisions.

## Tips
*   Take your time to complete the form honestly and thoroughly.
*   If unsure about any skill, select 'No' and add a brief explanation in the 'Additional Comments' field.
*   Be specific and provide examples when describing your skills and abilities.
*   If you need assistance or clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
