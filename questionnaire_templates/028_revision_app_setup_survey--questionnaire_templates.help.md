# Revision App Setup Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Revision App Setup Survey is designed to gather information about your setup preferences for the revision app. This form will help us tailor the app to your specific needs and ensure a smooth experience with our services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions as accurately as possible based on your preferences and needs.
2. Select the relevant options for subjects, device preferences, reminder frequency, email notification, and device notification.
3. Add any additional notes if you have any specific feedback or requests.
4. Specify your preferred reminder time and email address.
5. Decide whether you'd like to receive email notifications and how often.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Subjects (<name>: Subjects)**: Select the subjects you're working on or interested in for which you'd like to use the revision app.
* **Device Preferences (<name>: Device Preferences)**: Choose the devices you prefer to use for the app. You can select multiple options.
* **Reminder Frequency (<name>: Reminder Frequency)**: Select how often you'd like to receive reminders for the app. Choose from Daily or Weekly.
* **Feature Requests (<name>: Feature Requests)**: Add any specific feature requests you have for the app.
* **Additional Notes (<name>: Additional Notes)**: Add any additional information or feedback you have about the app.
* **Reminder Time (<name>: Reminder Time)**: Specify the time of day you'd like to receive reminders for the app. You can choose the time that suits you best.
* **Email (<name>: Email)**: Provide your email address where you'd like to receive updates and notifications about the app.
* **Phone (<name>: Phone)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email Notification (<name>: Email Notification)**: Decide if you'd like to receive email notifications for the app. Select 'True' to enable notifications.
* **Email Notification Frequency (<name>: Email Notification Frequency)**: If you've enabled email notifications, select how often you'd like to receive them, between Daily or Weekly.
* **Device Notification (<name>: Device Notification)**: Decide if you'd like to receive device notifications for the app. Select 'True' to enable notifications.
