# Modpack Development Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback from users on modpack development, including development challenges, satisfaction with the modpack, and ideas for future modpack development projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you have all the information required.
2. Answer all required fields (those marked as "required" in the form).
3. Enter accurate and clear information in each field.
4. Make sure to review your responses before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Community Feedback
* **Community Feedback** (`community_feedback`, `text`, required): This field is for providing your feedback on modpack development.
### 2. Development Challenges
* **Development Challenges** (`development_challenges`, `select_one`, required): Please select the challenges you are facing with modpack development.
### 3. Future Ideas
* **Future Ideas** (`future_ideas`, `text`, optional): If you have any ideas for future modpack development projects, enter them here.
### 4. Satisfaction
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `number`, required): Rate your satisfaction with modpack development on a scale.
### 5. Email Address
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address, which will be used to verify your identity and for future correspondence.
### 6. Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for future contact.
