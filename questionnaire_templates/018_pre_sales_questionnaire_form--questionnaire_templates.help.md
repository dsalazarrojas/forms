## Pre Sales Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Sales Questionnaire Form is a tool to gather essential information about potential sales opportunities. It helps identify key aspects of sales leads and provides a structured approach to assess their potential. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide valuable insights to your sales team, helping them to prioritize and pursue leads more effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the relevant fields for each section.
2.  Fill out the required fields.
3.  Provide as much detail as possible in the optional fields.
4.  Double-check your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Pre Sales Questionnaire (Page 1)
* **Pre Sales Questionnaire**: A summary of the form's purpose and instructions.
* **Sales Details**: This section is required and asks for specific business details to help identify the sales lead.
	+ **Business Name**: The name of the business.
	+ **Business Description**: A brief description of the business.
	+ **Business Address**: The address of the business.
	+ **Business Phone**: The phone number of the business.
	+ **Business Email**: The email of the business.
* **Sales Person**: The name of the sales person responsible for the lead.
* **Sales Manager**: The name of the sales manager responsible for the lead (optional).
* **Sales Representative**: The name of the sales representative responsible for the lead (optional).

### Sales Contact (Page 2)
* **Sales Contact**: The email address of the sales contact person.
* **Contact Method**: The method of contact (Email, Phone, Phone and Email, None).
* **Contact Details**: This section is not required, but any additional contact information can be provided here (e.g., contact person's name, department, etc.).
* **Sales Channel**: The sales channel through which the lead was generated (In Person, Phone, Email, None).
* **Sales Medium**: The medium used to generate the sales lead (Social Media, Online Advertising, Referral, None).
* **Sales Process**: The sales process followed for the lead (Discovery Meeting, Sales Meeting, None).
* **Contact Time**: The expected time of the next contact with the sales contact (e.g., today, next week, etc.).
* **Sales Stage**: The current stage of the sales stage (Qualified, Unqualified, None).

### Sales Opportunities (Page 3)
* **Deal Size**: The expected deal size (Less than $10,000, $10,000-$50,000, $50,000-$100,000, Greater than $100,000).
* **Sales Status**: The current status of the deal (Won, Lost, None).
* **Sales Probability**: The probability of closing the deal (High, Low, None).
* **Lead Time**: The expected time to close the deal (date).
* **Lead Status**: The current status of the lead (Qualified, Unqualified, None).
* **Lead Stage**: The current stage of the lead (Qualified, Unqualified, None).
* **Sales Stage**: The current stage of the sales stage (Qualified, Unqualified, None).
* **Lead Value**: The value of the lead (Less than $10,000, $10,000-$50,000, $50,000-$100,000, Greater than $100,000).
* **Deal Value**: The expected deal value (Less than $10,000, $10,000-$50,000, $50,000-$100,000, Greater than $100,000).
* **Sales Owner**: The name of the sales owner responsible for the lead (optional).
* **Sales Manager 2**: The name of the sales manager 2 (optional).
* **Sales Manager 3**: The name of the sales manager 3 (optional).

## Tips
* Always provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Use the options provided to help you guide your answers.
* If you are unsure about a field, refer to the form's purpose and instructions.
* Remember to review your answers before submitting the form.
