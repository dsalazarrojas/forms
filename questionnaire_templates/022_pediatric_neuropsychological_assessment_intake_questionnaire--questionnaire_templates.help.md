# Pediatric Neuropsychological Assessment Intake Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pediatric Neuropsychological Assessment Intake Questionnaire is a comprehensive form designed to gather essential information about a child's behavioral and medical history, medical conditions, allergies, and other relevant details. This information will be used to inform diagnosis and treatment plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Begin by reviewing the sections below and ensure you have permission to complete the form.
2. Select the relevant options for each question, and provide detailed answers where required.
3. Be as accurate and thorough as possible, as the information gathered will be used to inform diagnosis and treatment plans.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Introduction** (Page 1)
* **Introduction**: This page serves as an introduction to the form and provides some context for the questions that follow.

### 2. **Patient Information** (Page 2)
* **Patient Information**: Select the relevant options to describe the child's age group, gender, and parent's relationship to the child.

### 3. **Contact Information** (Page 3)
* **Contact Information**: Enter the email address where the child's treatment plan will be sent or shared.

### 4. **Medical History** (Page 4)
* **Medical History**: Provide a detailed description of the child's medical history, including any relevant allergies or conditions.

### 5. **Behavioral History** (Page 5)
* **Behavioral History**: Describe any behavioral concerns or issues that may be relevant to the child's treatment.

### 6. **Medical Conditions** (Page 6)
* **Medical Conditions**: Select the medical conditions that apply to the child, such as high blood pressure, diabetes, or epilepsy.

### 7. **Allergies** (Page 7)
* **Allergies**: Select the allergies that apply to the child, such as peanuts, gluten, or dairy.

### 8. **Medications** (Page 8)
* **Medications**: Provide a detailed description of any medications the child is currently taking.

### 9. **Family History** (Page 9)
* **Family History**: Describe any relevant family history that may be relevant to the child's treatment.

### 10. **Behavioral Concerns** (Page 10)
* **Behavioral Concerns**: Describe any behavioral concerns or issues that may be relevant to the child's treatment.

### 11. **Developmental History** (Page 11)
* **Developmental History**: Describe the child's developmental history, including any relevant milestones or challenges.

### 12. **Behavioral Deviations** (Page 12)
* **Behavioral Deviations**: Select the behavioral deviations that apply to the child, such as ADHD, autism, or PDD.

### 13. **Psychological Status** (Page 13)
* **Psychological Status**: Select the psychological status that applies to the child, such as normal, high, or low.

### 14. **Family Dynamics** (Page 14)
* **Family Dynamics**: Describe the family dynamics, including any relevant relationships or interactions.

### 15. **Developmental Status** (Page 15)
* **Developmental Status**: Describe the child's developmental status, including any relevant milestones or challenges.

### 16. **Medical Neuropsychological History** (Page 16)
* **Medical Neuropsychological History**: Provide a detailed description of the child's medical and psychological history.

### 17. **Behavioral Neuropsychological History** (Page 17)
* **Behavioral Neuropsychological History**: Describe any behavioral and psychological concerns or issues that may be relevant to the child's treatment.

### 18. **Medical Neuropsychological Treatment** (Page 18)
* **Medical Neuropsychological Treatment**: Provide any relevant information about the child's medical and psychological treatment.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have permission to complete this form before proceeding.
* Be as accurate and thorough as possible when answering each question.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask for clarification.
* This form is intended to be used as a guide, and you may need to modify it based on the specific needs of the child and their family.
