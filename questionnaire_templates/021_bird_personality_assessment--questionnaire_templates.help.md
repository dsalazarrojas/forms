# Bird Personality Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide a fun and interactive way to assess your personality and behavior. You'll be presented with a series of questions that cover different aspects of your personality, preferences, and values. By answering these questions, you'll get insights into your strengths, weaknesses, and areas for growth, and you'll be able to discover which bird type you are most like.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and choose the option that best describes you.
2. Answer all questions, even if you're not sure or don't have a preference.
3. Be honest and authentic in your responses, as this will help you get the most accurate results.
4. Don't worry if you need to take your time or come back to the form later; you can save your progress and continue later.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): This is the first step in getting started with the assessment. Please enter your first and last name to help us get to know you better.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, required): This is the email address where we'll send your results once you've completed the assessment. Make sure to enter a valid email address that you have access to.
* **Date of Assessment** (`assessment_date`, date, required): This is the date when you're completing the assessment. Enter the current date to get the most accurate results.
* **-- Welcome to the Bird Personality Assessment --** (`section_intro`, note, not required): This is just a friendly welcome message to get you started.
* **Discover your personality type through this fun avian-themed assessment** (`intro_text`, note, not required): This is just a brief introduction to the assessment.
* **When faced with a challenge, I tend to** (`question_1`, select_one, required): Choose the option that best describes your approach to handling challenges. Think "dive in immediately", "think it through carefully", "seek advice from others", "look for creative solutions".
* **In social situations, I am usually** (`question_2`, select_one, required): Select your typical behavior in social situations. Choose "the center of attention", "a careful observer", "in small close groups", "helping others feel welcome".
* **My ideal work environment is** (`question_3`, select_one, required): Select the option that best describes your ideal work environment. Think "fast-paced and dynamic", "structured and organized", "collaborative and social", "independent and quiet".
* **When making decisions, I rely on** (`question_4`, select_one, required): Choose your primary approach to decision-making. Select "gut instinct", "logic and analysis", "what feels right emotionally", or "input from trusted people".
* **I describe myself as** (`question_5`, select_one, required): Select the option that best describes how you see yourself. Choose "adventurous and bold", "thoughtful and analytical", "warm and nurturing", "creative and imaginative".
* **In conflict, I tend to** (`question_6`, select_one, required): Select your typical response to conflict. Think "address it head-on", "avoid confrontation", "try to understand both sides", "look for a win-win solution".
* **What motivates you most** (`question_7`, select_one, required): Select the option that best describes what motivates you most. Choose "achievement and success", "security and stability", "helping and connecting with people", "learning and growth".
* **My approach to new ideas is** (`question_8`, select_one, required): Select how you handle new ideas. Choose "jump at new opportunities", "proceed with caution", "value tried-and-true methods", "blend old and new approaches".
* **With your time, I prefer to** (`question_9`, select_one, required): Select how you like to spend your time. Think "stay active and on the go", "plan and organize", "connect with others", "focus deeply on interests".
* **My friends would say I am** (`question_10`, select_one, required): Select how your friends see you. Choose "fun and energetic", "reliable and dependable", "kind and supportive", "original and insightful".
* **When learning something new, I prefer** (`question_11`, select_one, required): Select your preferred learning style. Choose "learning by doing", "reading and research", "discussion and collaboration", "hands-on experimentation".
* **My natural habitat tends to be** (`question_12`, select_one, required): Select where you feel most at home. Think "where the action is", "a safe, familiar place", "among good people", "somewhere inspiring".
* **-- Reflection Questions --** (`section_reflection`, note, not required): This section is where you can reflect on your answers and insights.
* **What surprised you most about your responses** (`question_13`, text, not required): Reflect on what you've discovered about yourself.
* **Which bird type do you think you are most like** (`question_14`, select_one, not required): Select the bird type that resonates with you the most.
* **How would you like to use these insights** (`question_15`, text, not required): Think about how you can use the insights you've gained from this assessment.
* **-- Preferences and Feedback --** (`section_preferences`, note, not required): This is where you can give feedback and suggestions for improvement.
* **Difficulty of Assessment** (`assessment_difficulty`, select_one, required): Select how difficult or easy you found the assessment.
* **How Relevant Were the Questions** (`assessment_relevance`, select_one, required): Select how relevant the questions felt to you.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`feedback_comments`, text, not required): Share your thoughts on how we can improve the assessment.

Note: This guide is meant to be a general help resource, not a substitute for the actual assessment experience. Be sure to read the entire guide and follow the instructions carefully to get the most out of this fun and interactive assessment.
