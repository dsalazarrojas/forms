# women_s_health_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about women's health, including routine check-ups, medical history, menstrual cycling, and other health-related questions. This form is intended for use by healthcare professionals to gather relevant health information from patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer each field to the best of your ability.
2. Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please consult with your healthcare professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Routine Check-up** (`Routine Check-up`, `text`, required: false): This field is for recording any routine check-ups or health screenings you have undergone recently.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `text`, required: false): Please provide any relevant medical history you have, including past illnesses, allergies, or other health-related information.
* **Menstrual Cycling** (`Menstrual Cycling`, `number`, required: false): If you have a regular menstrual cycle, please enter the frequency of your cycle in days.
* **Menopause Status** (`Menopause Status`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select whether you are currently experiencing menopause (Active) or not (Inactive).
* **Breast Exams** (`Breast Exams`, `text`, required: false): Describe any recent breast exams you have undergone.
* **Breast Pain** (`Breast Pain`, `text`, required: false): Have you experienced breast pain or tenderness recently? If yes, describe.
* **Breast Abnormality** (`Breast Abnormality`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any breast abnormalities you have experienced (Yes/No).
* **Pelvic Exams** (`Pelvic Exams`, `text`, required: false): Describe any recent pelvic exams you have undergone.
* **Pelvic Pain** (`Pelvic Pain`, `text`, required: false): Have you experienced pelvic pain or tenderness recently? If yes, describe.
* **Abdominal Exams** (`Abdominal Exams`, `text`, required: false): Describe any recent abdominal exams you have undergone.
* **Medical Conditions** (`Medical Conditions`, `text`, required: false): List any medical conditions you are currently experiencing or have experienced in the past.
* **Treatment Plan** (`Treatment Plan`, `text`, required: false): Describe your current treatment plan or any treatments you are undergoing.
* **Patient Allergies** (`Patient Allergies`, `text`, required: false): List any allergies you have.
* **Medications** (`Medications`, `text`, required: false): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical History 2** (`Medical History 2`, `text`, required: false): Please provide any additional medical history you would like to share.
* **Menstrual Cycle Frequency** (`Menstrual Cycle Frequency`, `number`, required: false): If you have a regular menstrual cycle, please enter the frequency in days.
