# AMS Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AMS Questionnaire is designed to gather information about your physical and mental health status. This information will help healthcare professionals make informed decisions about your treatment and care.

## How To Complete This Form
Complete the form by following the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name** (`patient_full_name`, text, required): Enter your complete legal name.
* **Date of Birth** (`patient_dob`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone Number** (`patient_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email Address** (`patient_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (optional).
* **Current Age** (`current_age`, number, required): Enter your current age in years.
* **Height** (`height`, number, required): Enter your height in centimeters.
* **Weight** (`weight`, number, required): Enter your weight in kilograms.
* **Physical Activity Level** (`physical_activity_level`, select_one, required): Select your physical activity level from the options Sedentary, Lightly Active, Moderately Active, Very Active, Extremely Active.
* **Smoking Status** (`smoking_status`, select_one, required): Select your smoking status from the options Never Smoked, Former Smoker, Current Smoker.
* **Alcohol Consumption** (`alcohol_consumption`, select_one, required): Select how often you consume alcohol from the options Never, Occasionally, Moderately, Frequently.
* **General Health Rating** (`general_health_rating`, select_one, required): Select your general health rating from the options Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Sleep Quality** (`sleep_quality`, select_one, required): Select your sleep quality from the options Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor.
* **Fatigue Level** (`fatigue_level`, select_one, required): Select how often you feel tired or fatigued from the options Never, Rarely, Sometimes, Often, Always.
* **Decline in Physical Strength** (`physical_strength_decline`, select_one, required): Select if you have noticed a decline in your physical strength from the options No Decline, Slight Decline, Moderate Decline, Significant Decline.
* **Mood Changes** (`mood_changes`, select_one, required): Select if you have experienced mood changes or irritability from the options No Changes, Occasional, Frequent, Constant.
* **Sexual Desire** (`sexual_desire`, select_one, required): Select how you would rate your current sexual desire from the options Very High, High, Normal, Low, Very Low.
* **Erectile Function** (`erectile_function`, select_one, required): Select how you would rate your erectile function from the options Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor.
* **Hot Flushes** (`hot_flushes`, select_one, required): Select if you experience hot flushes or night sweats from the options Never, Rarely, Sometimes, Often, Always.
* **Concentration Difficulty** (`concentration_difficulty`, select_one, required): Select if you have difficulty concentrating or remembering things from the options Never, Rarely, Sometimes, Often, Always.
* **Depression Symptoms** (`depression_symptoms`, select_one, required): Select if you have experienced symptoms of depression from the options Never, Rarely, Sometimes, Often, Always.

Please take the time to answer each question carefully and accurately.
