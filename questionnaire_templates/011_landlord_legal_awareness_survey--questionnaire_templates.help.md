# Landlord Legal Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Landlord Legal Awareness Survey is designed to assess your knowledge and understanding of key legal concepts related to being a landlord. This survey will help us understand where you may need additional training or support, allowing us to provide more effective educational resources for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting your response for each question based on your understanding and experience.
2. Enter any relevant comments or information that will help us understand your answers.
3. When you have completed all the questions, select that you have completed the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Landlord Type** (`landlord_type`): This question asks you about the type of entity you are (Individual, Group, or Organization). Please select the one that best describes you.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`): This question asks about the type of property you own or manage (Residential, Commercial, or Both). Select the one that best describes your properties.
* **How Long Have You Been Leasing** (`years_living`): This question asks about how many years you have been leasing properties. Enter a number to indicate how many years you have been actively engaged in leasing activities.
* **Number of Properties** (`property_count`): This question asks about the total number of properties you own or manage. Enter the number of properties you are responsible for.
* **Location of Your Properties** (`location`): This question asks about the location of your properties. Enter a brief description of the general area or region where your properties are located.
* **Size of Your Properties** (`property_size`): This question asks about the size of your properties. Enter a brief description of the size of the properties you own or manage (e.g., studio, one-bedroom, etc.).
* **Legal Issues** (`legal_issues`): This question asks about the types of legal issues you have experience with as a landlord. Select all that apply from the list.
* **Have You Ever Received Training** (`training_history`): This question asks if you have ever received any formal training or education on landlord-tenant law. Select True or False.
* **Do You Need Training** (`training_need`): This question asks if you feel you need additional training or support on landlord-tenant law. Select True or False.
* **Survey Complete** (`survey_complete`): This question asks if you have completed the survey. Select True or False.
* **Comments** (`comments`): This field is for any additional comments or information you may want to provide about your answers.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): This field is for your contact information, in case we need to follow up with you.
* **Signature** (`signature`): This field is for your signature, which will be used to confirm your identity.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): This field is for any additional comments or information you may want to provide.
* **Landlord Awareness Survey** (`landlord_awareness_survey`): This field is for any additional information or comments you may want to provide about your survey experience.
