# Metabolic Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect metabolic assessment data from clients. It is intended to gather information about the client's metabolic rate burned during various activities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **First page**: Fill out the form by providing the necessary information.
2. Select your response for each field.
3. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First page** (`metabolic_assessment_form_first_page`, text, optional): This is the start of the form where you can enter any additional context or remarks about the assessment.
* **Client name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the client being assessed.
* **Assessment date** (`metabolic_assessment_form_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the metabolic assessment. The format should be in the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Assessment time** (`metabolic_assessment_form_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the assessment in 12-hour format (hh:mm a.m./p.m.).
* **Metabolic rate burned** (`metabolic_rate_burned`, text, optional): Describe the metabolic rate burned during the activity being assessed. This could include physical activities such as exercise, sports, or other strenuous activities.
