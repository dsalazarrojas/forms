# home_delivery_service_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from customers about their home delivery experience. This will help the delivery service provider to understand the level of satisfaction and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the pages by using the navigation buttons. For each page, read the label and hint for each field, and fill in the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Area** (`delivery_area`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you were delivered to the correct area, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your order.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Write your thoughts and suggestions about your delivery experience here.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your delivery experience on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, text, optional): Describe your experience with the service quality of the delivery service.
* **Delivery Person** (`delivery_person`, text, optional): If you want to provide feedback about the person who delivered your order, please describe your experience with them here.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted for more information, please provide your contact info here.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback, so that we can better understand your experience.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, please mention them in the Feedback field.
* If you would like to be contacted, please make sure to provide your contact info.
