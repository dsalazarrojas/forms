# ministry_member_questionnaire_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ministry Member Questionnaire Form is used to collect information from ministry members about their contact details, communication skills, music skills, and other relevant information. This form is meant to assess their abilities and qualifications for certain roles or projects within the ministry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions on each page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information
* **contact information** (`contact_information`, `text`, optional)
  Please enter your name and other relevant contact information.
### Communication Skills
* **communication skills** (`communication_skills`, `select_multiple`, optional)
  Please choose from the following options to describe your communication skills:
  - Singing
  - Playing an Instrument
  - Composing
  - Other (please specify in the "Other" field)
### Music Skills
* **music skills** (`music_skills`, `select_multiple`, optional)
  Please choose from the following options to describe your music skills:
  - Singing
  - Playing an Instrument
  - Composing
  - Other (please specify in the "Other" field)
### Hospitality Skills
* **hospitality skills** (`hospitality_skills`, `select_multiple`, optional)
  Please choose from the following options to describe your hospitality skills:
  - Excellent
  - Good
  - Poor
  - Other (please specify in the "Other" field)
### First Name
* **first name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional)
  Please enter your first name.
### Last Name
* **last name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional)
  Please enter your last name.
### Email
* **email** (`email`, `email`, optional)
  Please enter your email address.
### Phone
* **phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional)
  Please enter your phone number.
### Address
* **address** (`address`, `text`, optional)
  Please enter your address.
### Other
* *Note: This form is for assessing your skills and qualifications for ministry roles. Please answer honestly and accurately.*
