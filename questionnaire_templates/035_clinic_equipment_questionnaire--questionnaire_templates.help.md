# Clinic Equipment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Clinic Equipment Questionnaire is designed to gather information about the medical equipment used in a clinic, including its type, age, and maintenance schedule. This information will help clinics assess their equipment needs and make informed decisions about maintenance, upgrades, and future purchases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide information about your clinic, including its name, location, and type.
2. List the primary types of medical equipment used in your clinic.
3. Choose the approximate age of your equipment and select your maintenance schedule, who performs maintenance, and annual maintenance costs (if applicable).
4. Confirm the certification status of your equipment and report any current safety concerns.
5. Indicate whether your staff is trained on equipment operation and select your training frequency (if applicable).
6. Finally, list any equipment needs for the next 2-3 years and indicate the budget available for upgrades.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Clinic Information (Pages 1-3)
* **Clinic Name**: Enter the full name of your clinic. This will help us understand which clinic this information is about.
* **Location**: Enter the address or location of your clinic.
* **Type of Clinic**: Select the type of clinic you have (e.g., Hospital, Standalone, Community health center, Private practice, etc.).

### Equipment Overview (Pages 4-8)
* **Primary types of medical equipment used**: Select all the types of medical equipment your clinic uses. These include Diagnostic, Imaging, Monitoring, Surgical, Laboratory, Treatment, Support, and Other equipment. This will help us understand the scope of your medical equipment.
* **Age of equipment (average)**: Choose the approximate age of your medical equipment. This will help us understand when your equipment was purchased or introduced.
* **Equipment maintenance schedule**: Select how often you perform maintenance on your equipment.
* **Who performs maintenance**: Indicate who performs maintenance on your equipment (e.g., In-house staff, External service provider, Manufacturer service, or Mixed).
* **Annual maintenance costs**: Enter the approximate annual maintenance costs for your equipment (if applicable).

### Compliance and Safety (Pages 9-11)
* **Equipment certification status**: Confirm the certification status of your equipment.
* **Any current safety concerns**: Report any safety concerns you have about your equipment.

### Staff Training (Pages 12-14)
* **Staff trained on equipment operation**: Confirm whether your staff is trained on equipment operation.
* **Training frequency**: Select how frequently your staff is trained on equipment operation (e.g., Annual, Biennial, Every 3 years, As needed, No scheduled training).

### Future Needs (Pages 15-17)
* **Equipment needs for next 2-3 years**: List any equipment needs you have for the next 2-3 years.
* **Budget available for equipment upgrades**: Enter the approximate budget available for equipment upgrades.
