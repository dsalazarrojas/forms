# public_policy_initiative_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The public policy initiative feedback questionnaire is designed to collect feedback from individuals and organizations on public policy initiatives. Your input will help shape the future of these initiatives and ensure they align with the needs and expectations of the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your User Information, including your name and email address.
2.  Rate your feedback on the policy initiative using a scale of 1-5.
3.  Provide a brief comment on your feedback.
4.  Select all policy areas of interest.
5.  Provide your email address and phone number for feedback.
6.  Choose the submission date and time.
7.  Confirm your submission date and time.
8.  Click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. User Information
*   **User Information (1)**: This field requires your name and email address.
    *   Please enter your name and email address for reference and communication.
    *   This will enable us to contact you with updates on the policy initiative.

### 2. Feedback Rating
*   **Feedback Rating (2)**: Rate your feedback on the policy initiative using a scale of 1-5.
    *   Your rating will help us understand the level of satisfaction with the initiative.
    *   A rating of 1 indicates a low level of satisfaction, while 5 indicates high satisfaction.

### 3. Feedback Comment
*   **Feedback Comment (3)**: Provide a brief comment on your feedback.
    *   This will enable us to understand the reasons behind your rating and feedback.
    *   Keep your comment concise and clear for better understanding.

### 4. Policy Areas of Interest
*   **Policy Areas of Interest (4)**: Select all policy areas that apply to your feedback.
    *   This will help us understand the areas of interest and relevance to your feedback.
    *   Choose all the areas that relate to your feedback.

### 5. Feedback Email
*   **Feedback Email (5)**: Provide your email address for feedback.
    *   We will use this for communication and follow-up on your feedback.
    *   Ensure you enter a valid email address that you regularly check.

### 6. Feedback Phone
*   **Feedback Phone (6)**: Provide your phone number for feedback.
    *   This will enable us to contact you for further discussions or clarification.
    *   Ensure you enter a valid phone number that we can use to contact you.

### 7. Submission Date
*   **Submission Date (7)**: Select the submission date.
    *   This will help us track the submission timeline.
    *   Choose the correct date for your submission.

### 8. Submission Time
*   **Submission Time (8)**: Select the submission time.
    *   This will enable us to track the submission time.
    *   Choose the correct time for your submission.

### 9. Submission Date and Time
*   **Submission Date and Time (9)**: Select the submission date and time.
    *   This will help us confirm your submission details.
    *   Choose the correct date and time for your submission.

### 10. Submit
*   **Submit (10)**: Click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.
    *   This will send your feedback to us for review and analysis.
    *   Ensure you review your feedback for accuracy before submitting.

### 11. Policy Area 1
*   **Policy Area 1 (11)**: Select the policy area.
    *   This will help us understand your areas of interest.
    *   Choose the policy area that relates to your feedback.

### 12. Policy Area 2
*   **Policy Area 2 (12)**: Select the policy area.
    *   This will help us understand your areas of interest.
    *   Choose the policy area that relates to your feedback.

Note: Ensure you complete all required fields (indicated by *) and submit your feedback accurately and truthfully to support the development of public policy initiatives.
