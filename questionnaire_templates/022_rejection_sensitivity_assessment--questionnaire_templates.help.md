# Rejection Sensitivity Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rejection Sensitivity Assessment is a tool designed to help individuals understand their emotional and behavioral responses to rejection and disappointment. It aims to identify patterns and areas of potential improvement for personal growth and self-awareness.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each question carefully and select the options that best describe your typical reactions to rejection or disappointment.
2. Be honest and sincere in your responses.
3. Do not overthink or rationalize your answers.
4. Take your time and complete the form at your own pace.
5. If you feel uncomfortable or overwhelmed, you can stop and save your progress. You can resume later when you feel ready.
