# Community Interest In New Library Programs Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about what kind of programs our community would be interested in. This will help us create new programs that cater to their needs and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Respondent name
* **Respondent name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, optional): Please provide your name as a respondent.

### 2. Email address
* **Email address** (`email`, `email`, optional): If you'd like to receive notifications about new programs, provide your email address.

### 3. Preferred program types
* **Preferred program types** (`preferred_program_types`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all the program types that interest you, such as:
	+ Story time for children
	+ Adult education
	+ Technology workshops
	+ Arts and crafts
	+ Book clubs

### 4. Preferred times for programs
* **Preferred times for programs** (`preferred_times`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all the times that you'd like to attend programs, such as:
	+ Weekday mornings
	+ Weekday evenings
	+ Weekend mornings
	+ Weekend afternoons

### 5. Accessibility needs
* **Accessibility needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `text`, optional): If you have any accommodations or needs that we should be aware of, please specify them.

### 6. Willing to volunteer
* **Willing to volunteer** (`willing_to_volunteer`, `select_one`, optional): If you'd like to help run or support our programs, select "True" below.

### 7. Program suggestions
* **Program suggestions** (`program_suggestions`, `text`, optional): If you have any other program ideas or suggestions, please let us know here.

### 8. Number of household members
* **Number of household members** (`number_of_household_members`, `number`, optional): This will help us tailor our family-oriented programs to your needs.

### 9. Consent to contact
* **Consent to contact** (`consent_to_contact`, `select_one`, required): We will not contact you if you select "False" below, but we cannot inform you about new programs or events.
