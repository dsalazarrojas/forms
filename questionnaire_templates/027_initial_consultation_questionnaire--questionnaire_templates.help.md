The Initial Consultation Questionnaire form is designed to gather information about a client's initial consultation details. This form is used to collect data on client name, email, service provider name and contact information, consultation date, meeting duration, type, location, time, and follow-up call details. To complete this form, users should ensure that all relevant fields are filled out accurately and completely. This form is a crucial part of the onboarding process, and ensuring its accuracy will help in providing better service to the client.

## Initial Consultation Questionnaire - Help Guide

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the necessary information required to fill out the form accurately.
* Start by filling out the client's name and email address on page 1.
* Fill out the service provider's name and contact information on page 2.
* On page 3, select the meeting type (Onboarding, Consultation, Other, or Meeting) and specify the meeting length.
* Choose the meeting location (Office, Home, Other, or Remote) and the day of the meeting (Monday to Sunday).
* Select the start time and date of the meeting.
* Specify the meeting duration and whether or not to schedule a follow-up call.
* If a follow-up call is scheduled, select the follow-up call method (Phone, Email, or In-person) and specify the follow-up call time and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter the client's full name.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Service Provider Name** (`service_provider_name`, text, optional): Enter the service provider's name.
* **Service Provider Phone** (`service_provider_phone`, text, optional): Enter the service provider's phone number.
* **Client Notes** (`client_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about the client.
* **Service Provider Notes** (`service_provider_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about the service provider.
* **Onboarding Date** (`onboarding_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the onboarding consultation.
* **Meeting Length** (`meeting_length`, number, optional): Enter the length of the meeting in hours or minutes.
* **Meeting Type** (`meeting_type`, select multiple, optional): Choose the type of meeting (Onboarding, Consultation, Other, or Meeting).
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, select one, optional): Choose the meeting location (Office, Home, Other, or Remote).
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the meeting.
* **Meeting Day** (`meeting_day`, select one, optional): Choose the day of the meeting (Monday to Sunday).
* **Meeting Start Time** (`meeting_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the meeting.
* **Meeting Start Date** (`meeting_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the meeting.
* **Meeting Duration** (`meeting_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the meeting.
* **Follow-up Call** (`follow_up_call`, select one, optional): Choose whether to schedule a follow-up call (True or False).
* **Follow-up Call Time** (`follow_up_call_time`, time, optional): Enter the follow-up call time.
* **Follow-up Call Date** (`follow_up_call_date`, date, optional): Enter the follow-up call date.
* **Follow-up Call Method** (`follow_up_call_method`, select one, optional): Choose the follow-up call method (Phone, Email, or In-person).
* **Follow-up Call Notes** (`follow_up_call_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about the follow-up call.

## Tips

* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely to avoid any confusion or errors.
* If a follow-up call is scheduled, ensure you have the necessary contact information of the client and service provider.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all necessary information is included.
