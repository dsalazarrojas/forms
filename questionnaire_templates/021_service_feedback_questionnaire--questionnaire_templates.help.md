# Service Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with a service. The collected feedback will help the service provider identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, start at the top and work your way down.
2.  Answer each question in as much detail as needed.
3.  If you have any additional information or comments, please use the "Additional Feedback" field at the end.
4.  Make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was your overall experience with the service?** (`service_feedback_questionnaire_1`, `text`, required): Please describe your overall experience with the service. Be as detailed as you need to help us understand your experience.
*   **Was the service representative friendly and helpful?** (`service_feedback_questionnaire_2`, `select_one`, required): Please choose an option that best describes how friendly and helpful you found the service representative.
*   **What was the quality of the service?** (`service_feedback_questionnaire_3`, `text`, required): Describe the quality of the service you received. Be as detailed as you need to help us understand your experience.
*   **How likely are you to recommend this service?** (`service_feedback_questionnaire_4`, `select_one`, required): Please choose an option that best describes how likely you are to recommend the service.
*   **What can we improve on to make it better?** (`service_feedback_questionnaire_5`, `text`, required): Provide suggestions on how we can improve the service to better meet your needs.
*   **How would you rate the speed of the service?** (`service_feedback_questionnaire_6`, `select_one`, required): Please choose an option that best describes the speed of the service.
*   **What can we improve on to make it faster?** (`service_feedback_questionnaire_7`, `text`, required): Provide suggestions on how we can improve the speed of the service to better meet your needs.
*   **Additional Feedback** (`service_feedback_questionnaire_8`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional comments or information you'd like to share with us, please use this field.

## Tips
*   Please be as detailed as you need when answering each question.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
*   Your feedback is greatly appreciated and helps us improve our service.
