# Personalized Medicine Innovation Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personalized Medicine Innovation Evaluation Form is designed to assess the innovation, evaluation, and submission of personalized medicine projects. This form serves as a comprehensive tool to evaluate the projects and help innovators understand what they need to improve to make their projects more competitive.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Introduction Page (Page 1)**: The introduction page provides a brief overview of the form. You can read the background information and understand the purpose of the form without answering any questions.
2. **Background (Page 2)**: In this section, you can provide a detailed background of your project, including relevant research or data that supports your innovation.
3. **Project (Page 3)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if your project has a clear 'Yes' or 'No' answer to the project-related questions.
4. **Team (Page 4)**: Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the presence or absence of a team for your project.
5. **Innovation (Page 5)**: Please enter a numeric value to evaluate the innovation level of your project. For example, 1-5 or 1-10.
6. **Evaluation (Page 6)**: Similarly, enter a numeric value to evaluate the evaluation level of your project.
7. **Score (Page 7)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each option that applies to your project.
8. **Comments (Page 8)**: Provide any additional comments about your project, including areas of improvement or strengths.
9. **Submission (Page 9)**: A note for any additional information that might be needed for submission.
10. **Submit (Page 10)**: Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm that you have completed the form and are ready for evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Background**: This section is where you can provide detailed background information about your project, such as research data or relevant studies that support your innovation.
* **Project**: This field assesses whether your project has a clear 'Yes' or 'No' answer to the project-related questions. This will help us understand the project's feasibility and relevance.
* **Team**: Please indicate if your project has a team behind it. This is crucial in understanding the project's development and implementation phases.
* **Innovation**: This field requires you to evaluate the level of innovation of your project, which is a crucial aspect of personalized medicine.
* **Evaluation**: Similarly, this field is where you evaluate the level of evaluation of your project.
* **Score**: In this section, you will be selecting 'Yes' or 'No' for each option that applies to your project.
* **Comments**: This field is for providing additional comments or insights about your project. If you have any feedback, please share it here.
* **Submission**: A note for any additional information that might be needed for submission.
* **Submit**: Please confirm that you have completed the form and are ready for evaluation.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer each question truthfully and accurately.
* Provide as much detail as possible in the background and comments sections.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the relevant team for assistance.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
