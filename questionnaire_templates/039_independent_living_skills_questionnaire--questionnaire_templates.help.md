# independent_living_skills_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The independent living skills questionnaire is designed to assess an individual's ability to perform daily activities, communicate effectively, and manage time. This form is used to collect information on how well an individual can perform tasks related to independent living, which is essential for various applications such as healthcare, social services, and education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each page carefully and provide answers to the questions.
2. For each question, select the most accurate answer from the options provided.
3. Make sure to fill in all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **introduction**: This is the first page of the form where you will be introduced to the questionnaire and its purpose. Please read it carefully to understand what to expect and what the form is asking you to do.
* **daily_activities**: On this page, you will be asked to select which daily activities you can perform. Select the activities you can do without any help or assistance.
* **communication_skills**: In this section, you will be asked to describe your ability to communicate effectively. Please provide a detailed answer about your communication skills, including any strategies you use to convey your thoughts and ideas.
* **problem_solving**: On this page, you will be asked to select which problem-solving skills you possess. Select all the skills you have that are relevant to solving problems.
* **time_management**: In this section, you will be asked to describe your ability to manage time effectively. Please provide a detailed answer about how you manage your time, including any strategies or routines you use.
