# Wedding Hair And Makeup Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Hair And Makeup Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information about the hair and makeup preferences of the bride and groom in a wedding event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Ensure you are filling out this form for a legitimate wedding event.
*   If you are filling out this form for the first time, proceed with the form completion.
*   If you are filling out this form for the second time, please provide updated information if any details have changed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **wedding_hair_and_makeup_questionnaire**:
    This is the title of the form. Do not fill this field.
*   **hair_type**: 
    Please select the type of hair you have (e.g. curly, straight, etc.).
*   **hair_color**: 
    Please select the natural color of your hair (e.g. black, brown, blonde, etc.).
*   **eye_color**: 
    Please select the natural color of your eyes (e.g. blue, brown, etc.).
*   **makeup_type**: 
    Please select if you will be having makeup on your wedding day (Yes/No).
*   **hair_styling**: 
    Please select a hair style (e.g. updo, curls, etc.) you would like to have on your wedding day.
*   **makeup_styling**: 
    Please select a makeup style (e.g. natural, glamour, etc.) you would like to have on your wedding day.
*   **wedding_location**: 
    Please enter the location of your wedding event.
*   **wedding_date**: 
    Please enter the date of your wedding event.
*   **wedding_time**: 
    Please enter the time of your wedding event.
*   **wedding_venue**: 
    Please enter the venue where the wedding event will take place.
*   **hair_color_2**: 
    This field is for any other hair color details (e.g. highlights, color variation).
*   **hair_type_2**: 
    This field is for any other details about your hair type (e.g. length, texture).
*   **eye_color_2**: 
    This field is for any other details about your eye color (e.g. shape, color variation).
*   **hair_styling_2**: 
    This field is for any other details about your hair style (e.g. style variation).
*   **makeup_styling_2**: 
    This field is for any other details about your makeup style (e.g. style variation).

## Tips
*   Be as detailed as possible when filling out this form.
*   If you are unsure of any of the fields, please consult with the person who requested this form to be filled out.
