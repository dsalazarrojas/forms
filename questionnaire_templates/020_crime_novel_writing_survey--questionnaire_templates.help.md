# crime novel writing survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about writers' habits, experiences, and preferences related to writing crime novels.

## How To Complete This Form
This form consists of multiple fields that ask various questions to help us understand your writing habits, goals, and preferences. Please read each question carefully and answer according to your best understanding. You can select all applicable options for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you write?** (`crime_novel_writing_survey_form`, number, required)
	+ This question is designed to understand your writing frequency.
	+ Please select a number from 1 to 10, with 1 indicating writing less than once a year and 10 indicating writing every day.
* **Writing Experience** (`writing_experience`, select_one, required)
	+ This question is to understand your writing experience level.
	+ Please select one of the following options:
		- Less than 1 year
		- 1-5 years
		- 5-10 years
		- More than 10 years
* **Writing Goals** (`writing_goals`, select_multiple, required)
	+ This question is to understand your writing goals.
	+ Please select all applicable options:
		- Complete a novel
		- Finish a few short stories
		- Develop a writing habit
		- Publish a book
* **Favorite Genre** (`favorite_genre`, select_one, required)
	+ This question is to understand your favorite genre.
	+ Please select one of the following options:
		- Crime
		- Romance
		- Science Fiction
		- Fantasy
		- Horror
		- Mystery
		- Thriller
		- Historical
* **What inspires your writing?** (`writing_inspirations`, select_multiple, required)
	+ This question is to understand what inspires your writing.
	+ Please select all applicable options:
		- Real-life events
		- Dreams
		- Research
		- Conversations
		- Travel
		- Reading
		- Other
* **What challenges do you face while writing?** (`writing_challenges`, select_multiple, required)
	+ This question is to understand the challenges you face while writing.
	+ Please select all applicable options:
		- Finding time
		- Lack of confidence
		- Self-doubt
		- Rejection
		- Writer's block
		- Other
* **What are your writing habits?** (`writing_habits`, select_multiple, required)
	+ This question is to understand your writing habits.
	+ Please select all applicable options:
		- Write regularly
		- Write at any time
		- Write when inspired
		- Write for a specific project
		- Write for a specific genre
* **What do you like most about crime fiction?** (`crime_fiction`, select_multiple, required)
	+ This question is to understand what you like most about crime fiction.
	+ Please select all applicable options:
		- The thrill of the chase
		- The complexity of plot
		- The moral ambiguity
		- The character development
		- The setting
		- The plot twist
* **Do you belong to a writing group?** (`writing_group`, select_one, required)
	+ This question is to understand if you belong to a writing group.
	+ Please select one of the following options:
		- Yes
		- No
		- Undecided
* **Have you published anything?** (`published`, select_multiple, required)
	+ This question is to understand if you have published anything.
	+ Please select all applicable options:
		- Novel
		- Short story
		- Essay
		- Article
		- Other

## Tips

* Please answer each question carefully and honestly.
* You can select all applicable options for each question.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
