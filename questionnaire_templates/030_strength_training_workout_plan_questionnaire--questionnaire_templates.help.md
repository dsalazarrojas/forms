# Strength Training Work Out Plan Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from clients about their current fitness goals, experience, and availability for a strength training workout plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each field carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Enter your name in the "Client Name" field.
3. Enter your age in the "Client Age" field.
4. Select your exercise experience level (beginner, intermediate, or advanced) in the "Client Exercise Experience" field.
5. Choose your primary fitness goals (weight loss, muscle gain, endurance, strength gain, or flexibility) in the "Client Fitness Goals" field.
6. Enter your desired training frequency (number of times you want to train per week) in the "Training Frequency" field.
7. Enter the start and end dates for your workout plan in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields.
8. Enter your name if you are interacting with a trainer in a one-on-one setting.
9. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Client Age**: Enter your age in numerical format (e.g. 25).
* **Client Exercise Experience**: Choose your level of experience with exercise and physical activity (beginner, intermediate, or advanced).
* **Client Fitness Goals**: Select one or more of your primary fitness goals (weight loss, muscle gain, endurance, strength gain, or flexibility).
*   **Training Frequency**: Enter the number of times per week you want to train.
*   **Start Date**: Enter the date you want to start your workout plan.
*   **End Date**: Enter the date you want to end your workout plan.
*   **Trainer Name**: If you are interacting with a trainer in a one-on-one setting, enter your name.
*   **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your workout plan.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form, as the information provided will help the trainer create a personalized workout plan for you.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, ask your trainer for guidance.
* This form should not be filled out by clients without the involvement of a trainer.
* This form should be completed in a one-on-one setting with a trainer, where the trainer can review and discuss the information with you.
