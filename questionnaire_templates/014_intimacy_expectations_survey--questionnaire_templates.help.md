# Intimacy Expectations Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intimacy Expectations Survey is designed to help individuals understand their own and their partner's intimacy expectations in a relationship. It's a tool to facilitate open and honest communication about what each person wants and needs in their relationship, and to help identify areas of potential conflict.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the survey questions and understand what each one is asking.
* Answer each question based on your honest and personal experiences and feelings.
* Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions, and type your answer for text fields.
* Take your time and be as thorough as possible, but don't feel obligated to answer every question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Relationship Expectations**
 (`relationship_expectations`, `select_multiple`, optional): 
This question is asking about your expectations for your relationship, such as how much time you want to spend together or how you want to be affectionate. Select all that apply to indicate what you expect from your partner and what you're willing to offer.

*Note: This question is optional, but answering it will help you and your partner understand each other's expectations better.*

### 2. **Intimacy Levels**
 (`intimacy_levels`, `select_one`, optional):
This question is asking about your desired level of intimacy, with options ranging from Intimacy Level 1 (casual) to Intimacy Level 3 (very intimate). Select the level that best describes your preferences.

*Note: This question is optional, but answering it will help you and your partner understand each other's comfort levels.*

### 3. **Communication Styles**
 (`communication_styles`, `select_one`, optional):
This question is asking about how you like to communicate with your partner, such as being assertive, avoidant, passive, or passive-aggressive. Select the style that best describes your communication approach.

*Note: This question is optional, but answering it will help you and your partner understand each other's communication styles.*

### 4. **Emotional Expression**
 (`emotional_expression`, `select_multiple`, optional): 
This question is asking about how you express your emotions to your partner, such as whether you're willing to be open and honest or more reserved. Select all that apply to indicate how you usually express yourself.

*Note: This question is optional, but answering it will help you and your partner understand each other's emotional expression patterns.*

### 5. **Conflict Resolution**
 (`conflict_resolution`, `text`, optional):
This question is asking you to describe how you usually resolve conflicts with your partner. Please type a brief answer in your own words.

*Note: This question is optional, but answering it will help you and your partner understand each other's conflict resolution strategies.*

### 6. **Boundaries**
 (`boundaries`, `select_multiple`, optional): 
This question is asking about your boundaries in the relationship, such as whether you're comfortable with physical touch or not. Select all that apply to indicate what you're comfortable with and what you're not.

*Note: This question is optional, but answering it will help you and your partner understand each other's boundaries.*

### 7. **Emotional Intimacy**
 (`emotional_intimacy`, `select_one`, optional):
This question is asking about the type of emotional intimacy you prefer, such as just emotional or both emotional and physical. Select the option that best describes your preferences.

*Note: This question is optional, but answering it will help you and your partner understand each other's emotional intimacy preferences.*

### 8. **Intimacy Expectations**
 (`intimacy_expectations`, `text`, optional):
This question is asking you to describe your overall intimacy expectations in a few sentences. Please type a brief answer in your own words.

*Note: This question is optional, but answering it will help you and your partner understand each other's overall expectations.*
