# Study Results Validity Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Study Results Validity Assessment Form is used to assess the validity of a study's results. It helps researchers and peer reviewers evaluate the quality and reliability of a study's outcome.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the study title, journal publication, study authors, and study year.
2. Select the peer reviewers who have reviewed the study.
3. Provide a brief summary of the reviewer comments and recommendations.
4. Choose the study outcome (Successful, Unsuccessful, or Neutral).
5. Provide any additional remarks about the study.
6. Sign and date the form to confirm that you have completed the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This field is required and should be a brief introduction to the study.
* **Study Title**: This field is required and should be the title of the study being assessed.
* **Journal Publication**: This field is required and should be the publication where the study was published.
* **Study Authors**: This field is required and should be the names of the authors of the study.
* **Study Year**: This field is required and should be the year the study was conducted.
* **Peer Reviewers**: This field is a dropdown menu where you should select all the peer reviewers who have reviewed the study.
*   *Select Dr. John Doe, Dr. Jane Doe, or Dr. Joe Doe from the list.*
* **Reviewer Comments**: This field is a text area where you should provide a brief summary of the reviewer comments.
*   *Provide a brief summary of the comments, including any concerns or questions raised.*
* **Reviewer Recommendations**: This field is a text area where you should provide a brief summary of the recommendations made by the reviewers.
*   *Provide a brief summary of the recommendations, including any suggestions or changes suggested.*
* **Study Outcome**: This field is a dropdown menu where you should select the study outcome (Successful, Unsuccessful, or Neutral).
*   *Choose the outcome based on the study's results.*
* **Study Outcome**: This field is a text area where you can provide any additional remarks about the study.
*   *Provide any additional comments or observations about the study.*
* **Peer Reviewer Signature**: This field is a text area where you should sign and date to confirm that you have completed the assessment.

Note: The fields marked with an asterisk (\*) are examples and should be replaced with the actual values for your study.
