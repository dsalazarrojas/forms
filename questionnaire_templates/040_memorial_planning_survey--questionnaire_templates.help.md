## memorial_planning_survey - Help Guide

### Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for memorial planning and funeral arrangements. It is intended for individuals or organizations providing funeral services to collect necessary details from clients or families arranging a memorial service.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill in your funeral home's name in the "Funeral home name" field.
3. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact email" field.
4. Provide your contact phone number in the "Contact phone" field.
5. Enter the name of the funeral director in the "Funeral director name" field (if applicable).
6. Indicate the person who will be the contact person in the "Contact person" field (if applicable).
7. Select the type of service from the "Service" field (e.g., burial, cremation, etc.).
8. Choose the location(s) of the service from the "Location" field (e.g., cemetery, church, etc.).
9. Select the time of the service from the "Time of Service" field (if applicable).
10. Choose the burial grounds from the "Burial grounds" field (e.g., cemetery, etc.).
11. Select the casket options from the "Casket selection" field (e.g., wooden, metallic, etc.).
12. Choose the flower options from the "Flowers" field (e.g., bouquet, arrangements, etc.).
13. Select the music options from the "Music selection" field (e.g., classical, contemporary, etc.).
14. Choose the readings from the "Readings" field (e.g., scriptural, etc.).
15. Enter any final comments or special requests in the "Final comments" field.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Funeral home name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your funeral home's name and location.
* **Contact email** (`2`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact phone** (`3`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Funeral director name** (`4`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the funeral director (if applicable).
* **Contact person** (`5`, `text`, optional): Indicate the person who will be the contact person (if applicable).
* **Service** (`7`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of service (e.g., burial, cremation, etc.).
* **Date of Service** (`8`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the service (if applicable).
* **Location** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the location(s) of the service (e.g., cemetery, church, etc.).
* **Time of Service** (`10`, `time`, optional): Select the time of the service (if applicable).
* **Burial grounds** (`11`, `select_one`, required): Choose the burial grounds (e.g., cemetery, etc.).
* **Casket selection** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the casket options (e.g., wooden, metallic, etc.).
* **Flowers** (`13`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the flower options (e.g., bouquet, arrangements, etc.).
* **Music selection** (`14`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the music options (e.g., classical, contemporary, etc.).
* **Readings** (`15`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the reading options (e.g., scriptural, etc.).
* **Eulogies** (`16`, `note`, required): Enter any final comments or special requests.
* **Final comments** (`17`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional final comments or requests.
