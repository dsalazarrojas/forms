# Body Perception Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This questionnaire is designed to collect self-reported data on body awareness, a person's ability to notice and perceive their physical sensations, changes, and sensations within their body. The goal is to gather insights on how well individuals are attuned to their bodily experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly, considering your genuine experiences and perceptions.
2. Choose the response that best fits your current state.
3. For questions with select_one type, select only one option.
4. If you have any additional comments or observations, use the text field at the end.
