# Market Research Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Market Research Checklist Form is designed to collect essential information about your market research project. This form helps you gather all the necessary details about your project, making it easier to track and manage your research.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Project Name** field with the actual name of your project.
2. Provide a brief **Project Description** in the space provided.
3. Identify your **Target Audience** by selecting one or more relevant options from the list. You can select multiple audiences if applicable.
4. Specify your **Research Objective** in the field provided.
5. Select your **Research Questionnaire** in the field provided.
6. Choose your **Market Segment** from the list of available options.
7. Choose one or more **Industry(ies)** that apply to your project.
8. Specify your **Research Methodology** in the field provided.
9. Select your **Target Location** from the available options.
10. Fill in your **Date**, if applicable.
11. Fill in your **Time**, if applicable.
12. Provide your **Contact Name** and **Contact Email** for future reference.
13. Enter your **Contact Phone** number for easy communication.
14. Set a **Project Deadline**, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of your project to keep track of it.
* **Project Description** (text, optional): Provide a brief description of your project for reference.
* **Target Audience** (select_multiple, optional): Select one or more groups of people you are conducting research on, such as Senior Management, Middle Management, Junior Management, Marketing, Sales, or Customer Support.
* **Research Objective** (text, optional): State the main goal of your research.
* **Research Questionnaire** (text, optional): Specify the questionnaire you will be using for your research.
* **Market Segment** (select_one, optional): Choose the type of market you are researching, like B2B, B2C, Consumer Goods, Durable Goods, Niche, or Specialty.
* **Industry** (select_multiple, optional): Select the industries related to your project, including Automotive, Banking, Healthcare, Manufacturing, Retail, or Technology.
* **Research Methodology** (text, optional): Describe the approach you will take to complete your research.
* **Target Location** (select_one, optional): Choose the location for your research, like North America, Europe, Asia, Africa, South America, or Australia/Oceania.
* **Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of your research, if applicable.
* **Time** (time, optional): Enter the time of your research, if applicable.
* **Contact Name** (text, optional): Enter your name for future reference.
* **Contact Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address for future reference.
* **Contact Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for future reference.
* **Project Deadline** (date, optional): Set a deadline for your project completion, if applicable.

# Tips

* Double-check the **Required** fields to ensure your form is submitted successfully.
* Make sure to fill in all relevant fields to avoid missing information.
* If a field is not required but still applicable, fill it in for better organization.
* Review and proofread your form carefully before submitting.
