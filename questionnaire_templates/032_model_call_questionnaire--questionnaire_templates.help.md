# model_call_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The model_call_questionnaire form is designed to gather information about an individual's call model, including their contact information, experience, and rate.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions for each field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Model Type** (select_one, required: false): Select the type of model you are providing services as, either "Model" or "Non Model".
* **Contact Method** (select_one, required: false): Select the method you prefer to be contacted, either "In person", "Email", or "Phone".
* **Availability** (time, required: false): Enter your available time for services, using 24-hour clock notation (e.g., 12:00, 13:00, 14:00).
* **Experience** (select_multiple, required: false): Select your level of experience in the field, either 1-2 years, 2-5 years, or 5+ years.
* **Portfolio** (text, required: false): Enter a link to your portfolio, showcasing your work or experience.
* **Portfolio Type** (select_one, required: false): Select the type of portfolio you are providing, either "Website", "Social Media", or "Other".
* **Portfolio Link 2** (text, required: false): Enter an optional link to another portfolio item.
* **Portfolio Link 3** (text, required: false): Enter an optional link to another portfolio item.
* **Rate** (number, required: false): Enter your rate for services, using the provided options ($0-$50, $50-$100, $100+).
* **Why Call** (note, required: false): Enter a brief note about why you call your services, or any other relevant information.

## Tips
* Please make sure to complete all required fields, but you can skip optional fields if not relevant to your services.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* You can use the provided options to help guide your responses, but feel free to add any additional information if necessary.
