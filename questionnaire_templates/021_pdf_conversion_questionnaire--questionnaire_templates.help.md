# pdf_conversion_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect metadata for PDF file conversions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Upload a file**: Click on the "File Uploads" field and select your PDF or other supported file from your computer.
2. Click on the "File Format" dropdown menu and select the format of your uploaded file.
3. Choose your conversion priority: Low, Medium, or High.
4. If necessary, provide additional information about the urgency of your conversion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **File Uploads** (`file_uploads`, text, optional)
This field is where you will upload your PDF or other supported file. Click on the field and select the file from your computer. You can upload multiple files if needed.

### **File Format** (`file_format`, select_one, optional)
Select the format of your uploaded file. The supported formats are:
- `doc`
- `jpg`
- `png`
- `pdf`
- `csv`
- `excel`
- `txt`
- `zip`
- `rar`
- `tar`
- `mp4`
- `avi`
- `flv`
- `m4v`
- `mov`
- `mpg`
- `mpeg`

### **Priority** (`priority`, select_one, optional)
Choose how urgent you need the conversion to be. Select Low, Medium, or High to indicate the level of priority.

### **Urgency** (`urgency`, text, optional)
If there's any additional information you'd like to provide about the urgency of your conversion, you can type it here.
