# Trapping Techniques Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trapping Techniques Quiz is a tool designed to evaluate your knowledge and understanding of trapping techniques, an important aspect of safety protocols in various industries such as pest control, agriculture, and more. This quiz is meant to be a quick and easy way to assess your understanding of trapping methods and identify areas where you need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. The form is divided into multiple pages.
2. For each page, review the question or instruction carefully before selecting an answer.
3. If you are unsure of an answer, try to answer it based on your best judgment or knowledge.
4. Once you have completed all the pages, review your answers and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **trapping_methods** (`trapping_methods`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you have experience or knowledge about trapping methods, such as bait traps, snap traps, or glue traps.
* **safety_concerns** (`safety_concerns`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you have experience or knowledge about safety concerns related to trapping, such as proper handling, inspection, or emergency procedures.
* **trapping_practices** (`trapping_practices`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you have experience or knowledge about trapping practices, such as proper placement, checking traps, or disposal of trapped animals.

Note: All questions are optional and the form is meant to be a self-assessment tool.
