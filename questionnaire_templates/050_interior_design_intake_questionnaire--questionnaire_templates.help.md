Interior Design Intake Questionnaire - Help Guide
=====================================

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from clients to better understand their interior design project requirements. Please answer the questions honestly, and provide as much detail as possible to help the design team create a project that meets your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the step-by-step instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you've answered all the necessary questions.
3. If you have any additional comments or notes, feel free to include them in the "Client Notes" field.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required: false)
Provide the name of the client, as we will use this to address the project and communicate with you.
### 2. **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false)
Enter your email address so we can stay in touch and update you on the project progress.
### 3. **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required: false)
Add your phone number for us to contact you when needed.
### 4. **Address** (`address`, text, required: false)
Specify your current address for the project location, or where you would like us to deliver the project documents.
### 5. **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required: false)
Describe your project, including the space(s) you'd like us to design, and any specific requirements or constraints.
### 6. **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, number, required: false)
Provide an estimate of the project budget, including any specific budget allocations for furniture, materials, or other expenses.
### 7. **Color Scheme** (`color_scheme`, select_multiple, required: false)
Choose the color scheme(s) you prefer for your project (Light, Dark, Neutral).
### 8. **Furniture Style** (`furniture_style`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select the style of furniture you're looking for (Modern, Traditional, Contemporary).
### 9. **Design Style** (`design_style`, select_multiple, required: false)
Choose the design style you're interested in (Minimalist, Luxury, Art Deco).
### 10. **Project Completion Date** (`project_completion_date`, date, required: false)
Set a target completion date for your project.
### 11. **Design Completion Date** (`design_completion_date`, date, required: false)
Set a target completion date for your design, before construction or implementation.
### 12. **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, required: false)
Choose the current status of your project (In Progress, Completed, On Hold).
### 13. **Client Notes** (`client_notes`, note, required: false)
Include any additional comments or notes about your project that you'd like to share with the design team.
### 14. **Designer Name** (`designer_name`, text, required: false)
Name the designer assigned to your project, or provide a name of your preferred designer.

Tips:
* Be specific and detailed in your answers to help us better understand your project.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
* We'll review your answers carefully and contact you promptly with any questions or concerns.
