# Heath Challenge Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Heath Challenge Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from participants who have completed the Heath Challenge. This form aims to gather information on the participant's experience, overall rating, and suggestions for improvement. Your feedback is crucial to help us better understand your experience and make improvements to future challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Participant ID** field with your unique participant ID provided to you by the challenge organizers.
2. Enter the **Challenge ID** if you have it.
3. Select your feedback type from the drop-down menu, such as **Challenge**, **Program**, or **Initiative**.
4. In the **Feedback** field, provide a brief description of your experience with the challenge.
5. If you have any specific **Suggestions** for improvement, enter them in this field.
6. If you have any feedback regarding the **Participant Feedback**, enter it in this field.
7. If you have any feedback for the **Challenge**, enter it in this field.
8. If you have any feedback for the **Program**, enter it in this field.
9. If you have any feedback for the **Initiative**, enter it in this field.
10. Select your overall rating from the **Rating Scale** drop-down menu, such as **Excellent**, **Very Good**, **Good**, **Fair**, or **Poor**.
11. In the **Comments** field, provide any additional comments you'd like to share about your experience.
12. In the **Additional Comments** field, provide any further details you'd like to mention.
13. Enter the **Challenge Name** if you have it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter your unique participant ID provided to you by the challenge organizers.
* **Challenge ID** (`challenge_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter the ID of the challenge you participated in, if you have it.
* **Feedback Type** (`feedback_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of feedback you'd like to provide (Challenge, Program, or Initiative).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of your experience with the challenge.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, required: false): Enter any specific suggestions you have for improvement.
* **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Enter any feedback about your experience as a participant.
* **Challenge Feedback** (`challenge_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Enter any feedback about the challenge itself.
* **Program Feedback** (`program_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Enter any feedback about the program.
* **Initiative Feedback** (`initiative_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Enter any feedback about the initiative.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, `number`, required: false): Select your overall rating of the challenge (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the rating scale you'd like to use (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments you'd like to share.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any further details you'd like to mention.
* **Challenge Name** (`challenge_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the challenge, if you have it.
