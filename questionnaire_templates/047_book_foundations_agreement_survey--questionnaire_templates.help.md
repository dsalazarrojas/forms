# Book Foundations Agreement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the clarity and agreement of a book's foundational concepts. It will help to identify areas that need improvement and provide insight into how well the concepts are understood.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read the survey questions carefully and answer them as honestly as possible.
2. Fill in your respondent name (if you're not providing anonymous feedback).
3. Rate the level of clarity of the foundational concepts using the provided scale.
4. Choose your level of agreement with the core principles.
5. If you have any suggested improvements, type them in the provided field.
6. Finally, provide any other feedback or comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Agreement Survey --** (`survey_header`, note)
This field is a header for the survey questions to come.

### **Respondent name** (`respondent_name`, text)
This is a field for you to provide your name if you're not submitting anonymous feedback. If you prefer to remain anonymous, just type "anonymous" and we'll respect your wishes.

### **Clarity rating** (`clarity_rating`, select_one)
Rate the level of clarity of the foundational concepts. Choose from the following options: Very unclear, Unclear, Neutral, Clear, Very clear.

### **Agreement with principles** (`agreement_with_principles`, select_one)
Choose your level of agreement with the core principles of the book. Select from the following options: Strongly disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly agree.

### **Suggested improvements** (`suggested_improvements`, text)
If you have any suggestions on how to improve the clarity of the foundational concepts, type them in here.

### **Overall comments** (`overall_comments`, text)
Use this field to provide any other feedback or comments you may have about the book's foundations.

### **Tips**
Please be honest and thorough in your feedback. Your input will help improve the book's foundations and make it better for everyone.
