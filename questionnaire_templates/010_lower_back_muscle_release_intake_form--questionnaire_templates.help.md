# lower_back_muscle_release_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients for a lower back muscle release session. It is used to gather session details, symptoms, medical conditions, client consent, and contact information to ensure the practitioner can tailor the session to the client's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your session details in the **Session Details** field.
2. Select all the symptoms you are experiencing in the **Symptoms** field. Choose from the options Back, Neck, Hip, Knee, or Shoulders.
3. If you have any medical conditions that may impact your session, enter them in the **Medical Conditions** field.
4. In the **Consent** field, indicate your client consent for bodywork sessions.
5. Provide your contact information in the **Contact Details** fields, including **Phone**, **Email**, and **Address**.
6. Enter the date and time of your scheduled session in the **Scheduled Session** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, optional): Enter your session details, including the date and time of your session, in this field.
- **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the symptoms you are experiencing from the options Back, Neck, Hip, Knee, or Shoulders.
- **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions that may impact your session, enter them in this field.
- **Consent** (`consent`, note, optional): In this field, indicate your client consent for bodywork sessions.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Provide your contact information in this field, including your phone number, email, and address.
- **Phone** (`contact_details_1`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Email** (`contact_details_2`, text, optional): Enter your email address.
- **Address** (`contact_details_3`, text, optional): Enter your address.
- **Scheduled Session** (`scheduled_session`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of your scheduled session.
