# Medical Content Specialist Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Content Specialist Survey is a form designed to collect information about your demographic details as a Medical Content Specialist. This data will help us better understand our community's diversity and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the survey link or submit it through our platform.
2. Review the questions on each page, one by one.
3. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
4. Submit your completed form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Question 1: How old are you?
* **How old are you?** (Number): This question asks for your age to help us understand your age range within our community.

### Question 2: What is your job title?
* **What is your job title?** (Text): This question allows you to describe your job title within the Medical Content Specialist field. Please provide a brief description of your role.
