# 401(k) Rollover Decision Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you make an informed decision about your 401(k) rollover options. It will guide you through a series of questions to determine your needs and preferences for a successful transition.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Please select the most accurate options for each question.
3. If you're unsure about any question, select the option that best reflects your current situation or feelings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your legal name as it appears on your account records.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, `email`, `required`): Enter your preferred email address for follow-up and any relevant communications.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `phone`, `required`:): Enter your mobile or best contact number, including your country code (if outside the United States).
* **Submission date** (`submission_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Current employer name** (`employer_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of your current employer sponsoring the 401(k) plan.
* **Type of current 401(k) plan** (`current_plan_type`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the type of 401(k) plan you currently have, such as Traditional, Roth, or Safe Harbor.
* **Estimated plan balance** (`estimated_plan_balance`, `decimal`, `required`): Enter an approximate total balance in dollars, without commas.
* **Preferred rollover option** (`desired_rollover_option`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the option you prefer for your 401(k) rollover, such as leaving funds in the current employer plan or rolling into a new plan.
* **Primary factors influencing your decision** (`decision_factors`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Choose up to 6 factors that matter most to your decision, such as investment options, fees, tax consequences, or employer match availability.
* **Anticipated timeline for rollover** (`anticipated_timeline`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the timeframe you expect to act within, such as within 30 days or more than 6 months.
* **Concerns about tax implications** (`tax_concern`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the level of concern you have about tax implications, such as significant concern or no concern at all.
* **Years until expected retirement** (`years_to_retirement`, `integer`, `required`): Enter the number of years until you expect to retire, or 0 if you're already retired.
* **Current contribution rate** (`current_contribution_rate`, `integer`, `required`): Enter the percentage of your salary contributed to your 401(k) plan.
* **Desired features in the destination account** (`service_preferences`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Choose the features you value most in your destination account, such as low-cost index funds or personalized financial advice.
* **Consulted a financial advisor** (`advisor_consultation`, `select_one`, `required`): Indicate if you've already consulted a financial advisor about this rollover, or if you're planning to.
* **Additional comments or concerns** (`additional_comments`, `text`, `required`): Include any special circumstances or questions you have about your 401(k) rollover decision.
