# Neuro Agility Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Neuro Agility Assessment Survey is a form used to measure a person's ability to adapt and respond to changing situations. It is a self-reporting tool that asks you to answer a series of questions and provide your thoughts, feelings, and behaviors in response to different scenarios.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please read each question carefully and answer as honestly as possible. You can select multiple options for some questions, while others may only have one option. Some questions may also have a note field where you can provide additional information. Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
