# Neurodivergent Identification Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Neurodivergent Identification Survey is designed to collect information about an individual's neurodivergent identity, experiences, and support systems. This information will help identify areas of potential support and accommodations that may be needed.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Introduction**: This section is an optional introduction to the form. You can skip it if you feel uncomfortable.
2. **Neurodivergent Identity**: This section asks about your neurodivergent identity, including any conditions such as ADHD, Asperger, or PDD.
3. **Age**: Please provide your age to help us understand your neurodevelopmental stage.
4. **Email**: Please provide a valid email address to allow us to contact you for follow-up questions or support.
5. **Neurodivergent Experience**: This section is optional and asks about your experiences with neurodivergence.
6. **Neurodivergent Support Systems**: This section is optional and asks about any support systems you have in place.
7. **Neurodivergent Self-Awareness**: This section is optional and asks about your self-awareness of your neurodivergent traits.
8. **Neurodivergent Cognitive Difficulties**: This section is optional and asks about any cognitive difficulties you experience.
9. **Neurodivergent Support Needs**: This section is optional and asks about any support needs you have.
10. **Neurodivergent Communication Difficulties**: This section is optional and asks about any communication difficulties you experience.
11. **Neurodivergent Sensory Difficulties**: This section is optional and asks about any sensory difficulties you experience.
12. **Neurodivergent Executive Functions**: This section is optional and asks about any executive function difficulties you experience.
13. **Neurodivergent ADHD**: This section asks about any ADHD experiences.
14. **Neurodivergent Asperger**: This section asks about any Asperger experiences.
15. **Self-Harm**: This section asks about any self-harm experiences.
16. **Self-Injurious**: This section asks about any self-injurious experiences.
17. **Self-Destructive**: This section asks about any self-destructive experiences.
18. **Self-Sabotage**: This section asks about any self-sabotage experiences.
19. **Other Self-Harm**: This section asks about any other self-harm experiences.
20. **Other Supports**: This section asks about any other supports you have.
21. **Other Dangers**: This section asks about any other dangers you face.
22. **Other Harms**: This section asks about any other harms you experience.
23. **Other Difficulties**: This section is optional and asks about any other difficulties you experience.
24. **Submit**: Please submit your form to complete the survey.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, please select "unsure" or ask for clarification.
* Please provide a valid email address to allow us to contact you for follow-up questions or support.
* If you experience any difficulties completing the form, please contact us for assistance. 

Note: Please keep in mind that this form is not a substitute for professional medical or psychological evaluation. If you are experiencing any difficulties or concerns, please seek help from a qualified professional.
