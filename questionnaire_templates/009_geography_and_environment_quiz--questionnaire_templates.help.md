# Geography And Environment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains how to complete the Geography And Environment Quiz form, a tool used to assess your knowledge on geography and environmental topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and choose your answers from the provided options.
2. Make sure to fill out every question, as they are all required.
3. Enter your answers in the text fields provided for each question.
4. Once you have completed all the questions, click the submit button to save your progress.
