# Community Manager Election Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Manager Election Form is a democratic tool that allows eligible members of the community to elect a new leader. This form enables each member to express their opinion and preference for the next Community Manager.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details: Enter your full legal name and membership or unit ID number in the respective fields.
2. Confirm your eligibility: Select "I Certify Eligibility" to confirm that you are a voting member in good standing as per the community bylaws.
3. Rank your top three candidates: Choose your top three preferred candidates from the options provided.
4. Identify your top quality preference: Select the quality that is most important to you in a Community Manager.
5. Add any general comments: If you have any comments or feedback about the election or candidates, you can type them in the "General Comments" field.
6. Sign your ballot: Type your name to sign the ballot, ensuring the confidentiality of your vote.
7. Set the date of submission: Select the date when you are submitting your ballot.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Voter Full Legal Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your membership documents.
* **Membership or Unit ID Number**: Enter your membership or unit ID number as issued by the community.
* **I certify I am a voting member in good standing**: This field confirms that you are a valid member of the community and that you understand the community bylaws.
* **Rank Your Candidates - 1st Choice (Primary Vote)**: Select your top choice for the next Community Manager.
* **Rank Your Candidates - 2nd Choice**: Select your second choice for the next Community Manager.
* **Rank Your Candidates - 3rd Choice**: Select your third choice for the next Community Manager.
* **Top quality you look for in a Community Manager**: Identify the quality that is most important to you in a Community Manager.
* **General Comments on the Election or Candidates**: If you have any comments or feedback, type them in this field.
* **Voter Electronic Signature**: Type your name to sign the ballot, ensuring the confidentiality of your vote.
* **Date of Ballot Submission**: Select the date when you are submitting your ballot.
* **I understand my individual vote remains confidential**: Select "I Understand" to confirm that you understand that your vote is confidential and only aggregate results are published.
