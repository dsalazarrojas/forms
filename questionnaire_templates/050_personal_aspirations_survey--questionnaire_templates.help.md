# Personal Aspirations Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about your personal aspirations, goals, and feelings of support. Please take a few minutes to complete this form to provide valuable insights about your personal development and aspirations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. This guide will walk you through each field of the form.
2. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your administrator for clarification.
4. You can submit the form only once.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`first_page`, text, not required):
	+ This is a brief introduction to the form, and it's okay to leave it blank if you don't have any comments.
* **What are your long-term dreams or goals?** (`dreams_and_goals`, text, not required):
	+ Please share your long-term dreams or goals. Be as specific as possible.
* **What are you currently working on?** (`current_projects`, text, not required):
	+ Share the details of your current projects or activities.
* **Who do you turn to for support?** (`support_network`, text, not required):
	+ Share the people you turn to for support, e.g., friends, family, mentors, or colleagues.
* **What score would you give your current aspirations?** (`aspirations_score`, number, not required):
	+ Use a score from 1 to 10 to rate how well your current aspirations are meeting your expectations.
* **What score would you give your current projects?** (`current_projects_score`, number, not required):
	+ Use a score from 1 to 10 to rate how well your current projects are meeting your expectations.
* **How supportive do you feel your network is?** (`support_score`, number, not required):
	+ Use a score from 1 to 10 to rate how supportive you feel your network is.
* **What type of aspirations do you have?** (`aspirations_type`, select_one, not required):
	+ Select one of the following:
		- Career aspirations
		- Personal aspirations
		- Financial aspirations
		- Social aspirations
		- Recreational aspirations
		- Spiritual aspirations
* **What type of projects do you have?** (`current_projects_type`, select_multiple, not required):
	+ Select all that apply:
		- Career projects
		- Personal projects
		- Financial projects
		- Social projects
		- Recreational projects
		- Spiritual projects

## Tips

* Please be honest and open when sharing your personal aspirations and experiences.
* If you need help or clarification on any of the questions, please ask your administrator.
* This form is for your personal use, and the information gathered will help us understand your personal development and aspirations.
