# Service Continuity Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Service Continuity Assessment form is designed to evaluate the readiness and preparedness of your organization to maintain essential services during and after a potential disruption. It helps identify potential risks, assesses the business continuity plan, and prioritizes areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Service Continuity Assessment form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the answers to the questions on the form, based on your organization's current state.
2.  Review each field to provide accurate and relevant information.
3.  Save your answers to access them later.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Service Disruption Readiness** (`disruption_readiness`, Select Multiple, Optional): 
    This question asks how prepared your organization is to handle a service disruption. Select all that apply, such as having a disaster recovery plan, backup systems, or regular maintenance schedules.
*   **Continuity Risks** (`risk_identification`, Text, Optional): 
    Describe the potential risks to your organization's continuity, such as natural disasters, cyber attacks, or supply chain disruptions.
*   **Business Continuity Plan** (`business_continuity_plan`, Select One, Optional): 
    Indicate if your organization has a business continuity plan in place. Select "Yes" if you have a comprehensive plan, "No" otherwise.
*   **Continuity Gaps** (`continuity_gaps`, Date, Optional): 
    Report the date when you last reviewed your organization's continuity plan. Enter the date if you have not reviewed the plan.
*   **Improvement Priorities** (`improvement_priorities`, Select Multiple, Optional): 
    List the areas where your organization needs to improve its service continuity, such as backup systems or training for staff. Select all that apply.
