# Preclinical Data Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather information about your preclinical data management practices, tools, and priorities for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please select all applicable options for each field, unless otherwise specified.
- Answer each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
- You can type in free-form text in the "Comments" field if you have any additional comments or explanations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Intro Page** (`intro_page`, note, required: false): This page provides a brief introduction to the form and explains the purpose of this survey.
- **Data Management Tools** (`tools`, select_multiple, required: true): Select all the tools you currently use for preclinical data management, such as Electronic Data Capture (EDC), Data Warehouse, and Collaboration Platform.
- **Data Workflows** (`workflows`, select_multiple, required: true): Select all the workflows you currently use or would like to use for preclinical data management, such as Clinical Data Management, Data Management and Sharing, and Data Analysis and Reporting.
- **What is your top priority for data management tools?** (`tools_priority`, select_one, required: true): Choose your top priority for data management tools, which could be Data Quality, Data Security, Data Usability, or Collaboration.
- **How would you rate your current data management tools?** (`tools_rating`, select_multiple, required: true): Select how you would rate your current data management tools, with options being Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
- **How often do you collaborate with your team?** (`collaboration_practices`, select_multiple, required: true): Select how often you collaborate with your team, with options being Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Rarely.
- **What are your top priorities for improvement?** (`improvement_priorities`, select_multiple, required: true): Select all the areas you would like to improve, which could be Data Quality, Data Security, Data Usability, or Collaboration.
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or explanations about your preclinical data management practices, tools, or priorities.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required: true): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you if needed.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: true): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
