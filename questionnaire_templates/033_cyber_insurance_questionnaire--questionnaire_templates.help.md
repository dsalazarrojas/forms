# Cyber Insurance Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Cyber Insurance Questionnaire is designed to gather information about your cyber insurance needs. This form will help you assess your level of cyber insurance coverage, identify potential risks, and determine the type and scope of coverage required.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
2. Make sure to select the correct options that apply to you.
3. Review your responses carefully to ensure you are providing the most relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cyber Insurance Questions** (2, select_multiple):
	+ Choose one or more of the options that best describe your cyber insurance needs.
* **Coverage** (3, select_one):
	+ Select the level of cyber insurance coverage you need, such as Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Insurance Company** (4, text):
	+ Enter the name of the insurance company you are interested in or currently working with.
* **Risk Level** (5, select_one):
	+ Choose your risk level, such as Low, Medium, or High.
* **Coverage Needed** (6, select_multiple):
	+ Select one or more of the options that best describe the type of coverage you need, such as Data Protection, Security Consulting, or Cyber Security.
* **Coverage Type** (7, select_multiple):
	+ Choose one or more of the options that best describe the type of coverage you need, such as Network Security, Data Protection, or Security Consulting.
* **Provider** (8, select_one):
	+ Select the insurance provider that you are interested in or currently working with, such as Provider 1, Provider 2, or Provider 3.
* **Coverage Amount** (9, number):
	+ Enter the amount of coverage you need.
* **Coverage Period** (10, select_multiple):
	+ Choose one or more of the options that best describe the coverage period you need, such as 1 year, 3 years, or 5 years.
* **Coverage Type** (11, select_multiple):
	+ Select one or more of the options that best describe the coverage type you need, such as Annual, Monthly, or Quarterly.
* **Frequency** (12, select_multiple):
	+ Choose one or more of the options that best describe the frequency of coverage you need, such as Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.

## Tips

* Be accurate and honest when answering the questions.
* Review your responses carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any question, please contact your insurance provider or seek guidance from a qualified professional.
* This form is for informational purposes only and not a substitute for actual insurance coverage.
