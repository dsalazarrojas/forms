# employee_health_screening_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about employees' health and medical history, including any health risks and trends. It is meant to ensure that all employees are screened and provided with necessary health resources in a timely and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer all questions accurately.
2. Complete all required fields, indicated by *required*.
3. For the "Health Risks" field, select all applicable options that describe your health risks.
