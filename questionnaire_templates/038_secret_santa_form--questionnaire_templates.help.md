# Secret Santa Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Secret Santa Form is a tool for creating a fun and surprise gift-giving experience for a group of people, where each participant chooses a gift to give to another participant. The form helps in maintaining the secrecy of the gift-giver and the recipient, making the gift-giving experience more enjoyable and surprising.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and the name of the participant you would like to give a gift to (Recipient Name).
2. Enter a gift ID, which can be any identifier for the gift you have in mind (Gift ID).
3. Enter a name for the gift you would like to give (Gift Name).
4. Enter the price of the gift you would like to give (Gift Price).
5. Choose a budget category for the gift (Budget).
6. Enter a personal message to the recipient with the gift (Message).
7. Enter a message that will be shown to the recipient (Recipient Message).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name so we can identify you as the gift-giver.
  - This is the first field to be filled. Please fill it in carefully to avoid confusion.
- **Recipient Name** (`name_2`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person you would like to give a gift to.
  - This is the second field to be filled. Please fill it in carefully to avoid confusion.
- **Gift ID** (`gift_id`, `number`, optional): Enter a unique identifier for the gift you have in mind.
  - This is a unique identifier for the gift you have picked out. You can use any number or code.
- **Gift Name** (`gift_name`, `text`, optional): Enter a name for the gift you would like to give.
  - This is a name for the gift that will be shown to the recipient.
- **Gift Price** (`price`, `number`, optional): Enter the price of the gift you would like to give.
  - This is the price of the gift you would like to give to the recipient.
- **Budget** (`budget`, `select_one`, optional): Choose a budget category for the gift you would like to give.
  - Choose from Low, Medium, or High to indicate the price of the gift you would like to give.
- **Message** (`message`, `text`, optional): Enter a personal message to the recipient with the gift.
  - This is a personal message that will be shown to the recipient with the gift.
- **Recipient Message** (`recipient_message`, `text`, optional): Enter a message that will be shown to the recipient.
  - This is a message that the recipient will see when they get their gift.
