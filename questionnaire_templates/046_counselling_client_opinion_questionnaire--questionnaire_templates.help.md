# Counselling Client Opinion Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Counselling Client Opinion Questionnaire is designed to collect feedback from clients who have received counselling services. The form is used to gather information on their overall satisfaction with the therapy sessions and identify areas where the therapy was effective or where there is room for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Complete each question thoroughly and concisely.
2. Answer each question based on your personal experience with the therapy sessions.
3. Provide specific examples and details when answering the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Form** (`feedback_form`): Describe your overall impression of the therapy sessions. Provide any feedback you have on the sessions, including positive and negative aspects.
* **Client Satisfaction** (`client_satisfaction`): Select one of the following options that best represents your overall satisfaction with the therapy sessions:
	+ Option 1: Very Satisfied
	+ Option 2: Satisfied
	+ Option 3: Neutral
	+ Option 4: Dissatisfied
	+ Option 5: Very Dissatisfied
* **Therapy Effectiveness** (`therapy_effectiveness`): Select all the options that best describe the aspects of the therapy where you felt it was effective:
	+ Option 1: Emotional Support
	+ Option 2: Problem Identification
	+ Option 3: Goal Setting
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`): Describe specific areas where you feel the therapy could be improved or enhanced.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for the therapist or the therapy sessions.
