# Prenuptial Agreement Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prenuptial Agreement Questionnaire is a form designed to collect detailed information about a couple's financial situation and assets to facilitate the process of creating a comprehensive prenuptial agreement.

## How To Complete This Form

### To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with accurate and detailed information about yourself and your partner.
2. Answer each question carefully, considering the potential implications on your financial situation and relationship.
3. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand its purpose and how it relates to your situation.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Prenuptial Agreement Questionnaire 1** (Text field, not required): This field is not a required field and is intended to serve as a general description of your situation.
* **name** (Text field, not required): Enter the name(s) you will use to identify each other.
* **spouse1name** (Text field, not required): Enter your partner's name.
* **spouse1age** (Number field, not required): Enter your partner's age.
* **spouse1income** (Number field, not required): Enter your partner's annual income.
* **spouse2name** (Text field, not required): Enter your partner's name.
* **spouse2age** (Number field, not required): Enter your partner's age.
* **spouse2income** (Number field, not required): Enter your partner's annual income.
* **property** (Text field, not required): Describe any property you own or will share jointly.
* **spouse1property** (Text field, not required): Describe any property you own.
* **spouse1debt** (Text field, not required): List any debts your partner has.
* **spouse2debt** (Text field, not required): List any debts your partner has.
* **premaritalassets** (Number field, not required): Enter a total value of any assets you have before marriage.
* **premaritalliabilities** (Number field, not required): Enter a total value of any liabilities you have before marriage.
* **jointproperty** (Text field, not required): Describe any property you will share jointly.
* **jointdebt** (Text field, not required): List any joint debts you will incur.
* **jointassets** (Text field, not required): Describe any joint assets you will acquire.
* **jointliabilities** (Text field, not required): List any joint liabilities you will incur.
* **children** (Number field, not required): Enter the number of children you expect to have.
* **childrencount** (Number field, not required): Confirm the number of children you expect to have.
* **propertytype** (Select field, not required): Select the type of property you own or will share (Cash, Real Estate, Business, Vehicle, Other).
* **propertyvalue** (Number field, not required): Enter the total value of your property.
* **spouse1propertytype** (Select field, not required): Select the type of property your partner owns or will share (Cash, Real Estate, Business, Vehicle, Other).
* **spouse2propertytype** (Select field, not required): Select the type of property your partner owns or will share (Cash, Real Estate, Business, Vehicle, Other).
* **assets** (Text field, not required): Describe any assets you have acquired or will acquire.

### Tips

* Please answer each question carefully and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to reach out to your partner or a legal professional for guidance.
* This form is intended to serve as a starting point for your prenuptial agreement and should be reviewed and revised as necessary by a legal professional.
