# Daily Alcohol Intake Self Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your daily alcohol intake. It helps track the amount of beer, wine, and spirits consumed. Please complete this form to provide accurate information about your consumption.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on each field to select the applicable answer based on your intake today.
- Fill out the corresponding quantity for each type of alcohol consumed (e.g., Beer, Wine, Spirits).
- If applicable, provide details under "Other" for any unlisted alcohol intake.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`<name>: intro, <type>: note, <required>: false`): This field is a brief introduction to the form. It serves as a prompt to guide you through the process.
* **What is your alcohol intake today?** (`<name>: intake, <type>: select_one, <required>: false`): Select the type of alcohol you consumed today from the options provided. Choose only one option that best describes your intake.
* **How much beer did you drink today?** (`<name>: beer_consume, <type>: number, <required>: false`): If you selected "Beer" in the previous question, answer with a number of units consumed. For example, 2 for 2 units.
* **How much wine did you drink today?** (`<name>: wine_consume, <type>: number, <required>: false`): If you selected "Wine" in the previous question, answer with a number of units consumed. For example, 2 for 2 units.
* **How much spirits did you drink today?** (`<name>: spirits_consume, <type>: number, <required>: false`): If you selected "Spirits" in the previous question, answer with a number of units consumed. For example, 2 for 2 units.
* **Other (if applicable)** (`<name>: other_consume, <type>: text, <required>: false`): If you consumed any other type of alcohol not listed, provide details here.
* **How much water did you drink today?** (`<name>: water_consume, <type>: number, <required>: false`): Answer with the total number of units of water consumed.
