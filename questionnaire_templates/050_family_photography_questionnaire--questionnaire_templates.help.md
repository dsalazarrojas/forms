# Family Photography Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Photography Questionnaire is designed to gather essential details from clients who require family photography services. Completing this form will help the photographer understand their preferences and provide a personalized service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Review each section carefully to ensure you provide the necessary details.
3. If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or guidance from the photographer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Photography Questionnaire Form**: This is the main form section where you will provide a brief description of your photography needs.
* **Family Photography Details**: Please describe the family photography details, including the number of family members, children's ages, and any specific requirements you may have.
* **Photography Type**: Please select one of the following options:
	+ Family: This type of photography is designed for family portraits, including parents, children, and other family members.
	+ Event: This type of photography is for events such as weddings, birthdays, or graduations.
	+ Other: If you have any other type of photography in mind, please select this option.
* **Date of Photography Session**: This is the date when you'd like to schedule the photography session.
* **Session Start Time**: This is the start time of the photography session.
* **Session End Time**: This is the end time of the photography session.
* **Photographer Details**: Please provide any additional details about the photographer you've chosen or would like to choose.
* **Photographer Name**: If you've already chosen a photographer, please provide their name.
* **Photographer Experience**: If you'd like to know more about the photographer's experience, please provide a brief description.
* **Photographer Email**: If you'd like to contact the photographer directly, please provide their email address.
* **Photographer Phone**: If you'd like to contact the photographer directly, please provide their phone number.
* **Studio Address**: This is the address of the photographer's studio or location where the session will take place.
* **Studio Hours**: This is the photographer's studio hours, where you can reach them for any queries or bookings.
* **Website**: If the photographer has a website, please provide the URL.
* **Portfolio**: If you'd like to see the photographer's portfolio, please provide the URL or a brief description.
