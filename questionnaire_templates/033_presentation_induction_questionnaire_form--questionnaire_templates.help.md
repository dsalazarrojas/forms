# Presentation Induction Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Presentation Induction Questionnaire Form is designed to collect information about presentations, including background, content, and audience feedback. Fill out this form accurately to help maintain a precise record of the presentations.

## How To Complete This Form
- Open the form and select the first page labeled "Introduction" where you can briefly introduce yourself and your presentation topic.
- Fill out the "Background Information" field to provide details about your background and expertise in the topic.
- In the "Presenter Information" field, enter your name and any relevant contact information.
- The "Presentation Information" field is for providing details about the presentation, including the topic and any notable points.
- The "What was the content of the presentation?" field is where you should describe the main points of your presentation.
- For "Audience", select the number of attendees who participated in the presentation.
- In "Feedback", choose whether you received positive or negative feedback from the audience.
- Finally, select the date and time of the presentation in the "Date and Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, optional): Briefly introduce yourself and your presentation topic.

### Background Information
* **Background Information** (`background_info`, text, optional): Provide details about your background and expertise in the topic.

### Presenter Information
* **Presenter Information** (`presenter_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and any relevant contact information.

### Presentation Information
* **Presentation Information** (`presentation_info`, text, optional): Provide details about the presentation, including the topic and any notable points.

### What was the content of the presentation?
* **What was the content of the presentation?** (`presentation_content`, text, optional): Describe the main points of your presentation.

### Audience
* **Audience** (`audience_info`, number, optional): Select the number of attendees who participated in the presentation.

### Feedback
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, select_multiple, optional): Choose whether you received positive ('Yes') or negative feedback ('No') from the audience.

### Date and Time
* **Date and Time** (`date_time`, date, optional): Select the date and time of the presentation.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, refer to your presentation materials or records for clarification.
- This form is for tracking and record-keeping purposes, so ensure you are not filling out a duplicate form.
