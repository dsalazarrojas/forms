# online-training-questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their online training preferences, health status, and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each question and its relevance to you.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Fill in the required fields, denoted by the "Required" label.
4. Select all applicable options for each field as prompted.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the questions.
