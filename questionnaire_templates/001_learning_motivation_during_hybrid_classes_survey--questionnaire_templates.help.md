# Learning Motivation During Hybrid Classes Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback from students about their motivation and experience in hybrid classes. Your responses will help us understand the effectiveness of our hybrid learning environment and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the designated fields.
2. Select your answers to the questions by choosing one or multiple options from the dropdown menus.
3. Enter any additional notes or comments in the note field.
4. Choose the date and time of the hybrid class you attended.
5. Submit the survey once you have completed all the fields.
