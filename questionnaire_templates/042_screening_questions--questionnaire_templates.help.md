# Screening Questions - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Screening Questions" form is a tool used to gather information about marine screening questions. This form is designed to help users provide detailed answers to a series of questions related to marine screening.

## How To Complete This Form

* Start by reviewing the form sections, each with a set of questions that need to be answered.
* Begin with Section 1, "Screening Questions Form", which is a free-text section for you to provide any information you feel is relevant to marine screening.
* Section 2, "Marine Screening Questions", is also a free-text section, similar to Section 1.
* In Section 3, "Marine Screening Questions 2", choose the option that best answers your question from the options provided.
* Section 4, "Marine Screening Questions 3", is similar to Section 3, where you can select one option from the list.
* Section 5, "Marine Screening Questions 4", is a numerical field where you can provide a number to answer the question.
* Section 6, "Screening Questions 1", is for entering a date in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* Section 7, "Screening Questions 2", is for entering a time in the format of HH:MM:SS.
* Section 8, "Screening Questions 3", is a free-text field for additional comments or notes.
* Section 9, "Screening Questions 4", is for entering an email address.
* Section 10, "Screening Questions 5", is a free-text section for more information.
* Section 11, "Screening Questions 6", is a numerical field where you can provide another number to answer the question.
* Section 12, "Screening Questions 7", is another free-text field for your comments or answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Screening Questions Form** (`Screening Questions Form`, `text`, not required):
    - This is the first section of the form where you can provide any information related to marine screening.
* **Marine Screening Questions** (`Marine Screening Questions`, `text`, not required):
    - This section is also for providing information related to marine screening.
* **Marine Screening Questions 2** (`Marine Screening Questions 2`, `select_one`, not required):
    - Choose one option from the list that best answers your question.
* **Marine Screening Questions 3** (`Marine Screening Questions 3`, `select_multiple`, not required):
    - Select multiple options that best answer your question.
* **Marine Screening Questions 4** (`Marine Screening Questions 4`, `number`, not required):
    - Enter a number to answer the question.
* **Screening Questions 1** (`Screening Questions 1`, `date`, not required):
    - Enter the date in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Screening Questions 2** (`Screening Questions 2`, `time`, not required):
    - Enter the time in the format of HH:MM:SS.
* **Screening Questions 3** (`Screening Questions 3`, `note`, not required):
    - Provide additional comments or notes related to marine screening.
* **Screening Questions 4** (`Screening Questions 4`, `email`, not required):
    - Enter your email address to help us contact you.
* **Screening Questions 5** (`Screening Questions 5`, `text`, not required):
    - Provide more information related to marine screening.
* **Screening Questions 6** (`Screening Questions 6`, `number`, not required):
    - Enter another number to answer the question.
* **Screening Questions 7** (`Screening Questions 7`, `text`, not required):
    - Provide your comments or answers to the question.
