# Validation And Requalification Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Validation And Requalification Survey" is designed to collect information about your current status, qualification, and processes related to changes and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. For questions with options, select the response that best matches your situation.
3. Provide detailed answers when required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### What is your current status?
**Current Status** (Required): This question is designed to know your current standing. Please enter a brief description of your current status in this box.

### What is your current qualification status?
**What is your current qualification status?** (Optional): Check the option that best describes your current status:

*   Active: You are fully qualified in your role.
*   Inactive: You are not currently qualified in your role.

### Have you documented any changes?
**Have you documented any changes?** (Optional): Check all changes that you have documented:

*   Yes: You have documented changes.
*   No: You have not documented changes.

### What are your review processes like?
**What are your review processes like?** (Optional): Describe your review processes in detail.

### What feedback do you have?
**What feedback do you have?** (Optional): Provide any feedback or comments you have about your processes.

### How often do you document changes?
**How often do you document changes?** (Optional): Check how often you document changes:

*   Yes: You regularly document changes.
*   No: You rarely document changes.

### How often do you give feedback?
**How often do you give feedback?** (Optional): Check how often you give feedback:

*   Yes: You regularly give feedback.
*   No: You rarely give feedback.

### How often do you review processes?
**How often do you review processes?** (Optional): Check how often you review processes:

*   Yes: You regularly review processes.
*   No: You rarely review processes.

## Tips
*   Take your time to answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
*   If you are unsure about any question, please contact your supervisor or team leader for guidance.
*   This survey is intended to be a regular activity to track progress over time.
