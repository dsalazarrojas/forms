# Employee Motivation Research Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather research data on employee motivation, engagement, and feedback for the purpose of improving job satisfaction and employee well-being. Please fill out this form to contribute to our research and help us better understand what drives job satisfaction and motivation in our organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company's name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Select your current position within the company from the list in the "Employee Position" field.
3. Type your current job title in the "Job Title" field.
4. Choose the motivation factors that motivate you from the list in the "Motivation Factors" field. You can select multiple factors.
5. Select the engagement factors that motivate you from the list in the "Engagement Factors" field. You can select multiple factors.
6. Enter the frequency of feedback you receive from the "Feedback Frequency" field.
7. Choose the source of feedback you receive from the list in the "Feedback Source" field.
8. Select the channels through which you receive feedback from the list in the "Feedback Channel" field. You can select multiple channels.
9. Rate your job satisfaction level from the list in the "Job Satisfaction" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (Company Name, text, optional): Enter your company's name.
* **Employee Position** (Employee Position, select_one, optional):
	+ Manager: Choose this option if you hold a management role.
	+ Director: Choose this option if you hold a director role.
	+ Supervisor: Choose this option if you are a supervisor.
	+ Team Member: Choose this option if you are not in a management role.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, text, optional): Enter your current job title.
* **Motivation Factors** (Motivation Factors, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Motivation: Choose this option if motivation is a key driver of your motivation.
	+ Engagement: Choose this option if engagement is a key driver of your motivation.
	+ Feedback: Choose this option if feedback is a key driver of your motivation.
* **Engagement Factors** (Engagement Factors, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Engagement: Choose this option if engagement is a key driver of your motivation.
	+ Motivation: Choose this option if motivation is a key driver of your engagement.
	+ Feedback: Choose this option if feedback is a key driver of your engagement.
* **Feedback Frequency** (Feedback Frequency, number, optional): Enter the frequency of feedback you receive. This field is not required but will help us understand how often you receive feedback.
* **Feedback Source** (Feedback Source, select_one, optional):
	+ Colleagues: Choose this option if colleagues provide feedback.
	+ Manager: Choose this option if your manager provides feedback.
	+ Supervisor: Choose this option if your supervisor provides feedback.
	+ Self: Choose this option if you receive feedback from yourself.
* **Feedback Channel** (Feedback Channel, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Email: Choose this option if you receive feedback through email.
	+ Phone: Choose this option if you receive feedback through phone calls.
	+ In-Person: Choose this option if you receive feedback in person.
* **Job Satisfaction** (Job Satisfaction, select_one, optional):
	+ Very Satisfied: Choose this option if you are very satisfied with your job.
	+ Somewhat Satisfied: Choose this option if you are somewhat satisfied with your job.
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied: Choose this option if you are somewhat dissatisfied with your job.
	+ Very Dissatisfied: Choose this option if you are very dissatisfied with your job.
