# Family Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Questionnaire is a comprehensive form designed to collect information about a person's family, preferences, and interests. The form is intended for use in various settings, such as family planning or research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: 
    * Family Session Details (Page 1)
    * Session Date (Page 1)
    * Session Time (Page 1)
    * Location (Page 1)
    * Preferred Contact Method (Page 1)
    * Photo Style (Page 2)
    * Photo Preferences (Page 2)
    * Family Vacation (Page 3)
    * Favorite Hobbies List (Page 4)
2. Select all the relevant fields for each question, as some questions offer multiple options. 
3. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, as this information will be used for the intended purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Family Session Details (Page 1)
* **Family Session Details** (`family_session_details`, `text`, required): A brief description of the family session, including the purpose and context.
### Family Members (Page 1)
* **Family Members** (`family_members`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the family members who will be participating in the session.
### Session Date (Page 1)
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, `date`, required): The date of the family session.
### Session Time (Page 1)
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, `time`, required): The time of the family session.
### Location (Page 1)
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required): The location of the family session.
### Preferred Contact Method (Page 1)
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, required): Select the preferred contact method for the session. Options: Email, Phone, Text.
### Special Requests (Page 1)
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, required): Any special requests or requirements for the family session.
### Favorite Colors (Page 2)
* **Favorite Colors** (`favorite_colors`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the favorite colors of the family.
### Photo Style (Page 2)
* **Photo Style** (`photo_style`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the photo style for the session. Options: Formal, Casual, Outdoor, Indoor, Beach.
### Photo Preferences (Page 2)
* **Photo Preferences** (`photo_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the photo preferences for the session. Options: Portrait, Landscape, Square, Panoramic, Close-up.
### Special Occasions (Page 2)
* **Special Occasions** (`special_occasions`, `text`, required): Any special occasions or events to be captured during the session.
### Favorite Hobbies (Page 3)
* **Favorite Hobbies** (`favorite_hobbies`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the favorite hobbies of the family. Options: Hiking, Cooking, Traveling, Reading, Sports.
### Family History (Page 3)
* **Family History** (`family_history`, `text`, required): A brief description of the family history.
### Family Background (Page 3)
* **Family Background** (`family_background`, `text`, required): A brief description of the family background.
### Favorite Travel (Page 4)
* **Favorite Travel** (`favorite_travel`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the favorite travel destinations of the family. Options: Europe, Asia, Africa, South America, North America.
### Family Vacation (Page 4)
* **Family Vacation** (`family_vacation`, `text`, required): A brief description of the family vacation plans.
### Favorite Hobbies List (Page 4)
* **Favorite Hobbies List** (`favorite_hobbies_list`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the favorite hobbies of the family. Options: Hiking, Cooking, Traveling, Reading, Sports.

## Tips
* Be thorough and honest when answering each question to ensure the most accurate information is collected.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please contact [Contact: form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx)
