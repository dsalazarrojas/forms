# Video Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Video Recommendation Form is used to collect information about a video, including its category, rating, and video type. This form helps users provide detailed descriptions and recommendations for videos.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, including:
	* Video Preference: This field is used to describe the video's category, such as Action or Romance.
	* Video Type: This field is used to specify whether the video is live or pre-recorded.
2. Provide additional information as needed, including:
	* Category: Select a category that best matches the video's genre.
	* Rating: Enter a numerical rating for the video, such as 1-5.
	* Genre: Provide a brief description of the video's genre, such as "Action Adventure".
	* Video Length: Enter the length of the video in minutes or seconds.
	* Audio: If the video has an audio component, provide a brief description.
	* Description: Enter a detailed description of the video.
	* Video ID or URL: Enter the video's ID or URL.
	* Video URL: Provide the URL of the video.
	* Tags: Enter any relevant tags or keywords for the video.
	* Created and Modified: These fields are automatically populated and do not require input.
	* Email and Phone: These fields are not required, but can be used for further contact or follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Video Preference** (Text, Required): Describe the video's category, such as Action or Romance.
* **Category** (Select One, Optional): Select a category that best matches the video's genre, such as Action or Comedy.
* **Rating** (Number, Optional): Enter a numerical rating for the video, such as 1-5.
* **Genre** (Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of the video's genre, such as "Action Adventure".
* **Video Type** (Select One, Optional): Specify whether the video is live or pre-recorded.
* **Video Length** (Number, Optional): Enter the length of the video in minutes or seconds.
* **Audio** (Note, Optional): If the video has an audio component, provide a brief description.
* **Description** (Text, Optional): Enter a detailed description of the video.
* **Video ID** (Text, Optional): Enter the video's ID or URL.
* **Video URL** (Text, Optional): Provide the URL of the video.
* **Tags** (Text, Optional): Enter any relevant tags or keywords for the video.
* **Created** (Date, Optional): This field is automatically populated and does not require input.
* **Modified** (Time, Optional): This field is automatically populated and does not require input.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): This field can be used for further contact or follow-up.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): This field can be used for further contact or follow-up.
* **Created At** (Date, Optional): This field is automatically populated and does not require input.
