# Global Citizenship Education Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about the global citizenship education from various stakeholders, including educators, NGOs, researchers, and others.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select one of the options for each question. Please answer all questions within each section before proceeding to the next.
2. For multiple-choice questions, choose one of the options provided.
3. For text-based questions, provide as much detail as possible.
4. If you are unsure about any question, please provide feedback in the "Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Educators** (`Yes`, `No`, optional): This question asks if you are aware of global citizenship education among educators.
* **Familiarity levels among Educators** (`0-10`, optional): This question asks how familiar you are with global citizenship education among educators, with higher numbers indicating greater familiarity.
* **Opinions among Educators** (text, optional): Share your opinion about global citizenship education among educators.
* **Feedback from Educators** (text, optional): Provide feedback about global citizenship education among educators.
* **NGOs** (`Yes`, `No`, optional): This question asks if you are aware of global citizenship education among NGOs.
* **Familiarity levels among NGOs** (`0-10`, optional): This question asks how familiar you are with global citizenship education among NGOs, with higher numbers indicating greater familiarity.
* **Opinions among NGOs** (text, optional): Share your opinion about global citizenship education among NGOs.
* **Feedback from NGOs** (text, optional): Provide feedback about global citizenship education among NGOs.
* **Researchers** (`Yes`, `No`, optional): This question asks if you are aware of global citizenship education among researchers.
* **Familiarity levels among Researchers** (`0-10`, optional): This question asks how familiar you are with global citizenship education among researchers, with higher numbers indicating greater familiarity.
* **Opinions among Researchers** (text, optional): Share your opinion about global citizenship education among researchers.
* **Feedback from Researchers** (text, optional): Provide feedback about global citizenship education among researchers.
* **Other** (`Yes`, `No`, optional): This question asks if you are aware of global citizenship education among other stakeholders.
* **Global citizenship education** (text, optional): Share your opinion about global citizenship education.
* **Importance** (`Important`, `Not Important`, `Very Important`, optional): Rate the importance of global citizenship education.
* **Relevance** (`Very Relevant`, `Somewhat Relevant`, `Not Relevant`, `Not at all relevant`, optional): Rate the relevance of global citizenship education.
* **Frequency** (`Often`, `Sometimes`, `Rarely`, optional): Rate how often you engage with global citizenship education.
* **Comments** (text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Email** (text, optional): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for further discussion.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for further discussion.
* **Location** (text, optional): Provide your location to help us better understand your perspective.
* **Time** (time, optional): Select the time you spent on this survey.
* **Date** (date, optional): Select the date you completed this survey.
* **Date of survey** (date, optional): Select the date when this survey was conducted.

Note: This help guide is for general guidance and may not be exhaustive. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
