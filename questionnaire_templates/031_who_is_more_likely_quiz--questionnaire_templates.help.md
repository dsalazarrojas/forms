# Who Is More Likely Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through completing the "Who Is More Likely Quiz" form to assess your opinions on a fictional character or team in a game or scenario.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
- Click on a page with the "Introduction" text and read through the information.
- On each subsequent page, fill out the form questions as instructed. You can add your comments in the "Comments" field if you feel the need to elaborate on your answers.
- Select your responses from the options provided in the select fields.
- Fill out any additional fields like email, phone, date, time, and time zone as needed for your submission.
- Click on the "Submit" button once you're finished filling out the form.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`intro`, text, optional): A brief introduction to the form. Read it before proceeding.
* **What is the name of the game or movie?** (`question1`, text, optional): Enter the name of the game or movie.
* **Score (1-10)** (`question1_score`, number, optional): Rate your confidence level in the character or team's likelihood of winning, on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Who do you think is more likely to win?** (`question2`, select_one, optional): Select one option from the provided list.
* **Question2 Score** (`question2_score`, number, optional): Rate the importance of your selection, on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Why do you think this character is more likely to win?** (`question3`, text, optional): Provide a reason or explanation for your selection.
* **Do you want to be on the team?** (`question4`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more options from the provided list.
* **Question4 Score** (`question4_score`, number, optional): Rate the importance of your selection, on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **How do you feel about this choice?** (`question5`, text, optional): Share your thoughts about your selection.
* **Result** (`result`, select_one, optional): Select one option from the provided list.
* **Result Score** (`result_score`, number, optional): Rate the importance of your selection, on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or explanations.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, text, optional): This field is not used in this form, you can ignore it.
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information that might be helpful in your submission.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button once you're finished filling out the form.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your contact information if you wish to receive updates or feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you wish to receive updates or feedback.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date you're completing the form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time you're completing the form.
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, select_one, optional): Select your time zone.
* **Time Zone Score** (`time_zone_score`, number, optional): Rate the importance of your time zone, on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **User's Time Zone** (`user_time_zone`, text, optional): Enter your time zone information if you wish to provide it.
* **User's Time Zone Score** (`user_time_zone_score`, number, optional): Rate the importance of your time zone, on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, date, optional): This field is auto-generated and not editable.

Note: Some fields might not be necessary or required, but filling them out will provide more context to your submission.
