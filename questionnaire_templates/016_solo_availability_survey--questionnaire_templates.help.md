# Solo Availability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to determine a person's availability for solo activities or projects. It is designed to gather information on the number of days they are available and the specific time slots they can commit to.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:
1. On each page, you will see a series of questions or options. Please select the options that apply to your situation.
2. For the "Days Available" question, select all the days of the week you are available to work.
3. For the "Time Slots" question, select all the time slots you are available to work during the day.
4. For the "Flexibility Details" question, select any additional flexibility you have, such as flexible hours, availability to work evenings or weekends.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Days Available** (`days_available`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the days of the week you are available to work. Please choose from the options provided:
	+ Monday
	+ Tuesday
	+ Wednesday
	+ Thursday
	+ Friday
	+ Saturday
	+ Sunday
* **Time Slots** (`time_slots`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the time slots you are available to work during the day. Please choose from the options provided:
	+ Morning (9am-12pm)
	+ Afternoon (1pm-4pm)
	+ Evening (5pm-8pm)
	+ Flexible (any time slot)
* **Flexibility Details** (`flexibility_details`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select any additional flexibility you have, such as:
	+ Flexible hours (able to work outside of regular time slots)
	+ Available to work evenings
	+ Available to work weekends

## Tips
- Make sure to select all the options that apply to your situation.
- If you have any other specific availability or flexibility not listed, please contact us to discuss further.
