# Subscription Service Experience Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and information from users about their subscription service experience. It collects data on contact information, subscription details, satisfaction score, and payment methods, which will help us understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
2. Answer the questions in the "Subscription Details" section about your subscription status, type, length, start/end dates, and payment methods.
3. Indicate your satisfaction score in the "Satisfaction Score" section.
4. Provide feedback about your experience in the "Feedback" section.
5. If you want, you can add additional comments in the "Additional Comments" section.
6. If you want, you can specify follow-up actions from the customer service in the "Follow-up Actions" section.
7. If you want, you can mention your next steps or plans in the "Next Steps" section.
8. You can also leave your contact information for customer service in the "Customer Service" section, but it's not required.
9. Please specify if you would like to review the subscription service in the "Review" section.
10. If you have any additional comments, feel free to add them in the "Additional Comments 2" section, but it's not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `email`, required): Provide your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Subscription Details** (`subscription_details`, `select_one`, required): Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your subscription status.
* **Satisfaction Score** (`satisfaction_score`, `number`, required): Rate your satisfaction with the subscription service from 1 to 10.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required): Tell us about your experience with the subscription service.
* **Product or Service** (`product_or_service`, `select_one`, required): Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you subscribe to a product or service.
* **Trends** (`trends`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you see any trends in your subscription experience.
* **Support** (`support`, `time`, required): Please select the time you have spent on customer support.
* **Follow-up Actions** (`follow_up_actions`, `select_one`, required): Please select "Yes" or "No" if you want follow-up actions from the customer service.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `date`, required): Please specify your next steps or plans.
* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, `email`, required): Provide your email address so we can contact you about your subscription service.
* **Review** (`review`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you would like to review the subscription service.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required): Please add any additional comments about your subscription experience.
* **Additional Comments 2** (`additional_comments_2`, `text`, required): Please add any further comments about your subscription experience.
* **Subscription Type** (`subscription_type`, `select_one`, required): Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your subscription type.
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate how often you use the subscription service.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your payment method.
* **Subscription Status** (`subscription_status`, `select_one`, required): Please select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate your subscription status.
* **Customer Type** (`customer_type`, `select_one`, required): Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your customer type.
* **Billing Cycle** (`billing_cycle`, `select_one`, required): Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your billing cycle.
* **Payment Method 2** (`payment_method_2`, `select_one`, required): Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your payment method.
* **Subscription Start Date** (`subscription_start_date`, `date`, required): Please enter your subscription start date.
* **Subscription End Date** (`subscription_end_date`, `date`, required): Please enter your subscription end date.
* **Subscription Length** (`subscription_length`, `number`, required): Please enter the length of your subscription.
* **Last Payment Date** (`last_payment_date`, `date`, required): Please enter your last payment date.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Be honest and accurate when providing feedback and answers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact information].
* We appreciate your feedback and will use it to improve our subscription service.
