# Wedding Day Questionnaire Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form template is designed to gather essential details and preferences for a wedding day. It covers key areas such as the wedding party, vendors, location, ceremony and reception details, and other miscellaneous information relevant for a successful and enjoyable wedding day.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these general steps:

1. Fill in the Wedding Day Details (page 1).
2. Choose the composition of the wedding party (page 2).
3. Select the vendors and services required for the event (page 3).
4. Choose the wedding location (page 4).
5. Describe the ceremony details (page 5).
6. Add notes about the reception (page 6).
7. Decide on wedding favors (page 7).
8. Specify photo-related details and timing (page 8).
9. Add any vendor details and notes (page 9).
10. Add any other relevant notes (page 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Wedding Day Details** (`wedding_day_details`): Enter the date and other essential details about the wedding day.
- **Wedding Party** (`wedding_party`): Choose who will be participating in the wedding (e.g., Bride and Groom, Jointly owned by both, Family members, Friends, etc.).
- **Wedding Vendors** (`wedding_vendors`): Select the necessary vendors and services for the event, such as a Wedding Planner, Photographer, Videographer, Wedding Cake Designer, Florist, etc.
- **Wedding Location** (`wedding_location`): Decide on where the wedding will be held (Outdoor, Indoor, Hotel, Other (Specify)).
- **Wedding Ceremony Details** (`wedding_ceremony_details`): Provide any necessary details for the wedding ceremony.
- **Wedding Reception Details** (`wedding_reception_details`): Add notes about the reception.
- **Wedding Favors** (`wedding_favors`): Decide whether or not to include wedding favors (Favors are not included, Favors are included and need to be purchased, Favors are included and will be provided by the couple).
- **Photo Details** (`photo_details`): Set a time for photo opportunities.
- **Vendor Details** (`vendor_details`): Add any necessary details for the vendors.
- **Other Notes** (`other_notes`): Add any other relevant information about the wedding day.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when describing each field to ensure clarity and avoid confusion.
- Review the form before submitting to ensure all required and relevant details are covered.
- Consider sharing this form with all parties involved in the wedding planning process to ensure everyone is on the same page.
- Adjust the form as needed based on your specific wedding needs and preferences.
