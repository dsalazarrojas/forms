The Phone Vocabulary Assessment form is designed to collect information about a person's phone-related vocabulary and experience. The form is intended to be a quick and easy way to gather data on phone-related topics, such as telecommunications types, frequency bands, network types, service providers, call quality, and emergency numbers. This form will help users provide a clear understanding of their phone-related knowledge and will likely be used in a research or educational setting.

# Phone Vocabulary Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a person's phone-related vocabulary and experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the pages and fill in the required and optional fields as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **telecom_types** (`1`, text, required/optional): Please select one of the following phone types: Fixed, Mobile, or Satellite. 
* **Frequency** (`2`, number, required/optional): Please enter your frequency band, e.g., 2G, 3G, 4G, etc.
* **network_types** (`3`, select_one, required/optional): Please select your network type: Cellular, Fixed, Fiber-optic, or Satellite.
* **service_provider** (`4`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please select all the service providers that you use, such as AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, or Sprint.
* **call_quality** (`5`, select_one, required/optional): Please rate your call quality as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **emergency_number** (`6`, text, required/optional): Please enter your emergency contact number, if applicable.
* **call_duration** (`7`, text, required/optional): Please enter the average duration of your calls, e.g., 5 minutes, 10 minutes, etc.

## Tips
Please answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
