## travel_planning_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The travel planning questionnaire is designed to collect information about travel plans, such as destination, travel dates, duration of stay, budget, and travel frequency. This form helps to understand user preferences and requirements for planning purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select a destination from the options provided.
2.  Enter the date of travel.
3.  Enter the duration of stay (number of days).
4.  Enter a travel budget (number of dollars).
5.  Select travel frequency (how often you travel).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **destination_selection**: (Required: False) Select the destination for your travel plan.
    *   This field allows you to specify the destination of your trip.
*   **date_of_travel**: (Required: False) Enter the travel dates.
    *   Enter the dates in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
*   **duration_of_stay**: (Required: False) Enter the duration of your stay.
    *   Enter the number of days you plan to stay at your destination.
*   **travel_budget**: (Required: False) Enter your travel budget.
    *   Enter the number of dollars you are willing to spend on your trip.
*   **travel_frequency**: (Required: False) Select how often you travel.
    *   Choose how often you travel, options are 'once', 'twice', 'thrice'.
