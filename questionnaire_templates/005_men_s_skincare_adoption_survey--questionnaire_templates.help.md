# Mens Skincare Adoption Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about men's skincare habits, preferences, and adoption rates. The collected data will help the survey creators better understand men's attitudes towards skincare and their purchasing behaviors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling in your demographic information, including your age, location, and other relevant details.
2. Answer the questions regarding your skincare routines and products usage.
3. Indicate your openness to trying new skincare products.
4. Select your primary motivations for purchasing skincare products.
5. Share your skincare goals and frequency of usage.
6. Report your current skincare budget and frequency of purchasing new products.
7. Indicate whether you purchase skincare products and the frequency of such purchases.
8. Estimate your skincare budget and the frequency of purchasing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Demographics** (`id: 1`, `type: number`): This section asks for some basic information about yourself, such as age and location.
* **2. Skincare Routines** (`id: 2`, `type: text`): Describe your current skincare routines, including any specific routines or methods you use.
* **3. Skincare Products** (`id: 3`, `type: select_multiple`): Select all the skincare products you currently use.
* **4. Openness** (`id: 4`, `type: select_one`): Indicate whether you consider yourself open to trying new skincare products, or whether you prefer to stick with what you know.
* **5. Motivations** (`id: 5`, `type: select_multiple`): Choose your primary motivations for purchasing skincare products, such as new products or recommendations.
* **6. Skincare Goals** (`id: 6`, `type: note`): Briefly describe your skincare goals and what you aim to achieve through your skincare routine.
* **7. Routine Frequency** (`id: 7`, `type: number`): Report how often you practice your skincare routine, on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **8. Skincare Importance** (`id: 8`, `type: number`): Rate how important skincare is to you, on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **9. Skincare Budget** (`id: 9`, `type: number`): Report your current skincare budget, which will help us understand your financial constraints.
* **10. Skincare Frequency** (`id: 10`, `type: select_multiple`): Indicate how often you purchase new skincare products.
* **11. Skincare Purchased** (`id: 11`, `type: select_multiple`): Choose whether you purchase skincare products, and if yes, indicate how often.
* **12. Skincare Purchased Value** (`id: 12`, `type: number`): Report the value you assign to skincare products, on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **13. Skincare Purchased Frequency** (`id: 13`, `type: number`): Report how often you purchase new skincare products, on a scale from 0 to 10.
