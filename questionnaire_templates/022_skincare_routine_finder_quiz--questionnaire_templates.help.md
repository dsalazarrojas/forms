# Skincare Routine Finder Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to find a personalized skincare routine for a user based on their characteristics, such as skin type, skin tone, and concerns. The goal is to provide tailored recommendations for products and routines that suit the user's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First name and Last name in the respective text fields.
2. Enter your Email address, which will be used to receive your personalized skincare routine recommendations.
3. Select your preferred skin type from the options provided.
4. Choose your skin concerns from the options listed (e.g., acne, aging, dehydration, hyper-pigmentation).
5. Indicate how often you perform your skincare routine, choosing from the options daily, twice a week, three times a week, or weekly.
6. Select the products you prefer from the options listed, which will help tailor the recommendations to your liking.
7. Provide your age, which will help with more specific product recommendations.
8. Select your skin tone from the options provided.
9. Choose the products you're looking for, and you'll receive tailored recommendations in the next step.
10. Indicate your desired skin protection level, choosing from always, some, or never.
