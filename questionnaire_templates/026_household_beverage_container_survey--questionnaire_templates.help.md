# Household Beverage Container Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Household Beverage Container Survey is designed to gather information about your drinking habits and preferences regarding various beverages and their containers. Your responses will help us understand your behavior and contribute to improving our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully, as it is designed to gather accurate and helpful information.
2. You can skip any question that does not apply to you or your household.
3. If you have a question or need help, feel free to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the main reason why you drink water?** (`household_beverage_container_survey_1`, text, optional)
	+ This question aims to understand your motivation for drinking water. Please answer with a brief description or reason.
* **Do you drink coffee or tea?** (`household_beverage_container_survey_2`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select one of the following options: coffee, tea, or None (if you don't drink either).
* **Do you have a coffee maker at home?** (`household_beverage_container_survey_3`, select_one, optional)
	+ Choose True if you have a coffee maker, False if you don't.
* **What type of coffee or tea do you drink?** (`household_beverage_container_survey_4`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Select all that apply: coffee, tea, or None (if you don't drink either).
* **How many bottles of water do you have in your house?** (`household_beverage_container_survey_5`, number, optional)
	+ Enter the number of bottles you have, or 0 if you don't have any.
* **What is the type of container for your water?** (`household_beverage_container_survey_6`, select_one, optional)
	+ Choose from: plastic, glass, paper, or other.
* **Have you ever drank from a tap?** (`household_beverage_container_survey_7`, select_one, optional)
	+ Choose True if you have, False if you haven't.
* **How many bottles of juice do you have in your house?** (`household_beverage_container_survey_8`, number, optional)
	+ Enter the number of bottles you have, or 0 if you don't have any.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when answering.
* If you are unsure or have any questions, feel free to reach out to us.
* Your responses will help us improve our products and services.
