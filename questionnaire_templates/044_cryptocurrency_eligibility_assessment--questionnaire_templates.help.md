# cryptocurrency_eligibility_assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to assess the eligibility of individuals to participate in cryptocurrency-related activities, such as investment or trading. It helps to determine if the individual has the necessary knowledge and understanding to make informed decisions about cryptocurrency.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, select the relevant options on each page and submit your response.
2. For each field, please answer with the highest level that applies to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Suitability

* **User Suitability** (`user_suitability`, `number`, required: `false`):
	+ This field assesses your familiarity with cryptocurrencies.
	+ Please select a number between 0 and 10 to indicate your level of knowledge about cryptocurrencies.

### User Experience

* **User Experience** (`user_experience`, `number`, required: `false`):
	+ This field evaluates your experience with cryptocurrencies.
	+ Select a number between 0 and 10 to represent your level of engagement with cryptocurrencies.

### Risk Awareness

* **Risk Awareness** (`risk_awareness`, `number`, required: `false`):
	+ This field checks your understanding of the risks associated with cryptocurrencies.
	+ Choose a number between 0 and 10 to indicate your awareness of potential risks.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
* This form is not a substitute for professional advice or investment guidance.
