# Study Abroad Personality Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Study Abroad Personality Quiz form is designed to gather information from users about their preferences and interests in studying abroad. This form will help users to identify which study abroad programs are best suited for them based on their needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the School Type field with your school type (e.g., American, Public, Private, etc.).
2. Select your preferred Language from the list of options (e.g., English, Spanish, French, etc.).
3. Choose your preferred Degree (e.g., Bachelors, Masters, Doctoral).
4. Select your preferred Study Area (e.g., Business, Engineering, Humanities, etc.).
5. Fill in the Student Preferences field with any additional information you would like to share about yourself.
6. Enter your goals for studying abroad in the Goals field.
7. Fill in the School Preferences field with any additional information you would like to share about your preferred school.
8. If you have any follow-up information, enter it in the Follow-up field.
9. Select whether you would like to be contacted for follow-up (e.g., True or False).
10. If you would like to share any comments, enter them in the Comments field.
11. If you have any additional information about the user who submitted this form, enter it in the Submitted By field.
12. Enter the Submission Date and Submission Time fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **School Type** (1): Select your school type (e.g., American, Public, Private, etc.).
- **Country** (3): Enter your country of origin.
- **Degree** (4): Choose your preferred degree (e.g., Bachelors, Masters, Doctoral).
- **Study Area** (5): Select your preferred study area (e.g., Business, Engineering, Humanities, etc.).
- **Student Preferences** (6): Enter any additional information you would like to share about yourself.
- **Goals** (7): Enter your goals for studying abroad.
- **School Preferences** (8): Enter any additional information you would like to share about your preferred school.
- **Language** (9): Select your preferred language (e.g., English, Spanish, French, etc.).
- **Contact Info** (10): Enter your contact information.
- **Email** (11): Enter your email address.
- **Follow-up** (12): Select whether you would like to be contacted for follow-up (e.g., True or False).
- **Follow-up Info** (13): Enter any follow-up information.
- **Comments** (14): Enter any comments you would like to share.
- **Submitted By** (15): Identify the user who submitted this form.
- **Submission Date** (16): Enter the date of submission.
- **Submission Time** (17): Enter the time of submission.
- **Form Type** (18): Select the type of form submitted (e.g., Study Abroad, General Application).
- **User ID** (19): Enter your user ID.
- **Submission IP** (20): Enter the IP address of submission.
- **Submission User Agent** (21): Enter the user agent of submission.
- **Submission User Browser** (22): Enter the user browser of submission.
- **Submission Postal Code** (23): Enter the postal code of submission.
- **Submission User Country** (24): Enter the country of submission user.
