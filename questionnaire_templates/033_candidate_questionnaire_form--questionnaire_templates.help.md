## Candidate Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather relevant information about a candidate's profile, skills, and experience to help us understand their qualifications and fit for a position.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and labels to ensure you understand what is required.
2. Fill in the form with accurate and relevant information about yourself.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields. If a field is marked as "optional", feel free to provide additional information if you wish.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Full Name** (`candidate_full_name_q`, text, required): Please provide your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Position Applied For** (`position_applied_for_q`, text, required): Specify the job title or role you are applying for.
* **Current Job Title** (`current_job_title_q`, text, required): Your current or most recent position in your career.
* **Current Salary** (`current_salary_amount`, number, optional): If you feel comfortable sharing, provide your current salary amount and currency.
* **Expected Salary** (`expected_salary_amount`, number, required): Indicate your desired annual compensation.
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period_text_q`, text, required): Specify the period of notice you are willing to provide to your current employer.
* **Most Relevant Skill For This Role** (`relevant_skill_main`, text, required): Briefly explain why you are a good fit for this role based on your skills and experience.
* **Preferred Work Environment** (`work_environment_pref`, select_one, required): Select your preferred work environment, which can be Fully Remote, Hybrid, or In-Office.
* **Are You Open to Business Travel** (`travel_openness`, select_one, required): Indicate your willingness to travel for business, with options ranging from 0 to 50% or more frequency.
* **Years of Experience with Relevant Software/Tools** (`software_experience_years`, number, required): Provide an approximate count of years you have used relevant software or tools.
* **What Motivates You Professionally** (`professional_motivations`, text, required): Describe what drives you and keeps you engaged in your work.
* **Biggest Career Achievement to Date** (`career_achievement_description`, text, required): Share a notable accomplishment that you are proud of in your career.
* **Reason for Leaving Current Position** (`leaving_reason`, text, required): Explain why you are looking for a change in your current role.
* **Ideal Leadership Style in a Manager** (`manager_qualities_pref`, text, optional): Describe the qualities you look for in a manager.
* **Earliest Start Date** (`earliest_start_date_q`, date, required): Indicate your earliest available start date for the position.
* **May We Contact Your Current Employer for a Reference** (`employer_contact_consent`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted for a reference, with options Yes, immediately, Only after an offer, or False.
* **Reference 1 - Name and Phone** (`reference_1_details`, text, optional): Provide the name and phone number of a professional or personal reference.
* **Reference 2 - Name and Phone** (`reference_2_details`, text, optional): Provide the name and phone number of another professional or personal reference.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date_q`, date, required): Automatically populated with the current date.
