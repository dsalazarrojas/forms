# Mpox Case Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mpox Case Questionnaire is a form used to collect information about monkeypox cases. It is intended to gather data for the diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of monkeypox.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the contact name and phone number in the "Contact Name" and "Contact Phone" fields, respectively.
3. Enter the contact's email address in the "Contact Email" field.
4. Enter the date of symptom onset in the "Date of Symptom Onset" field using the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the case.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person responsible for the case.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person responsible for the case.
* **Date of Symptom Onset** (`date_of_symptom_onset`, date, optional): Enter the date when the symptoms started in the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have permission to share the patient's information before completing this form.
* If you are a healthcare professional, please refer to the standard reporting procedures for healthcare professionals to report a case.
