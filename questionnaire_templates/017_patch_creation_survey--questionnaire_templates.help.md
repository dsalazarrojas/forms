# Patch Creation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Patch Creation Survey is a form to gather information about your team's patch creation process. This information will help us improve our processes and tools.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Select the relevant options for your team's workflow.
3. Provide any additional notes or feedback in the "Submission Notes" field.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workflow Name** (`workflow_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the workflow you are reporting on.
* **Communication Method** (`communication_method`, text, optional): Describe the method your team used for communication (e.g., email, Slack, phone).
* **Tooling** (`tooling`, text, optional): List any tools your team used during the patch creation process.
* **Workflow Rating** (`workflow_rating`, number, optional): Rate your team's overall workflow experience (1-5).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any feedback or comments about the workflow.
* **Workflow Improvement** (`workflow_improvement`, text, optional): Suggest any improvements for future workflows.
* **Tooling Improvement** (`tooling_improvement`, text, optional): Suggest any improvements for the tools used during the patch creation process.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience with the patch creation process (1-5).
* **Submission** (`submission`, text, optional): Enter any submission notes or comments.
* **Submission Notes** (`submission_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional submission notes or comments.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your team.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, text, optional): Enter the number of members on your team.
* **Team Location** (`team_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your team.
* **Jira** (`jira`, select_one, optional): If you use Jira, select 'Yes' to indicate usage.
* **GitHub Issues** (`github_issues`, select_multiple, optional): If you use GitHub Issues, select all relevant options.
* **Trello** (`trello`, select_multiple, optional): If you use Trello, select all relevant options.
* **Basecamp** (`basecamp`, select_multiple, optional): If you use Basecamp, select all relevant options.
* **Slack** (`slack`, select_multiple, optional): If you use Slack, select all relevant options.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the submitter.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the patch creation process.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the patch creation process.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, select_one, optional): If you are the submitter, select 'Yes' to indicate.
* **Email** (`submitter_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the submitter.

Please take the time to fill out this form carefully and accurately. If you have any questions or need further guidance, feel free to reach out.
