# Scoliosis Quality Of Life Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scoliosis Quality Of Life Questionnaire is a form used to collect data about a person's quality of life regarding scoliosis. This form is used to assess the condition of patients with scoliosis and provide guidance on treatment options.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter the date of your first scoliosis diagnosis.
3. Answer the following questions about your scoliosis:
	* Have you undergone surgery for scoliosis?
	* What type of scoliosis do you have?
	* On a scale of 0-10, what is your average pain score?
	* Where do you experience your pain?
	* Describe your pain in more detail.
	* Do you experience any functional limitations due to scoliosis?
	* How often do you experience pain?
	* Do you wear a brace for scoliosis?
	* Have you undergone back surgery for scoliosis?
	* Do you have kyphosis (a condition where the spine is curved in an irregular shape)?
	* Do you have other conditions like scoliosis?
	* Do you experience fatigue due to scoliosis?
	* Do you experience difficulty breathing due to scoliosis?
	* Do you experience difficulty sleeping due to scoliosis?
	* Do you experience pain interference in your daily activities due to scoliosis?
	* Do you have difficulty engaging in physical activities due to scoliosis?
	* How many times have you visited a scoliosis clinic in the last year?
	* How frequently do you visit a scoliosis clinic?
	* Do you visit a scoliosis clinician other than your primary doctor for any reason?
	* How frequently do you visit a scoliosis clinician other than your primary doctor?

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, not required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date First Scoliosis Diagnosis** (`date_first_scoliosis_diagnosis`, date, not required): Enter the date you were first diagnosed with scoliosis.
* **Have You Undergone Scoliosis Surgery?** (`scoliosis_surgery`, select_one, required): Choose whether you have undergone scoliosis surgery.
* **Scoliosis Type** (`scoliosis_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of scoliosis you have.
* **Pain Average Score** (`pain_average_score`, number, not required): Enter your average pain score on a scale of 0-10.
* **Pain Location** (`pain_location`, text, not required): Describe the location of your pain.
* **Pain Description** (`pain_description`, text, not required): Provide more details about your pain experience.
* **Functional Limitations** (`functional_limitations`, text, not required): Describe any functional limitations you experience due to scoliosis.
* **Pain Frequency** (`pain_frequency`, text, not required): Describe how often you experience pain.
* **Wear Scoliosis Brace?** (`scoliosis_bracing`, select_one, required): Choose whether you wear a scoliosis brace.
* **Back Surgery** (`scoliosis_back_surgery`, select_one, required): Choose whether you have undergone back surgery.
* **Kyphosis** (`scoliosis_kyphosis`, select_one, required): Choose whether you have kyphosis.
* **Other Scoliosis Conditions** (`scoliosis_scoliosis`, select_one, required): Choose whether you have other scoliosis conditions.
* **Fatigue** (`scoliosis_fatigue`, select_one, required): Choose whether you experience fatigue due to scoliosis.
* **Breathing Difficulty** (`scoliosis_breathing_difficulty`, select_one, required): Choose whether you experience difficulty breathing due to scoliosis.
* **Sleep Difficulty** (`scoliosis_sleep_difficulty`, select_one, required): Choose whether you experience difficulty sleeping due to scoliosis.
* **Pain Interference** (`scoliosis_pain_interference`, select_one, required): Choose whether pain interferes with your daily activities.
* **Physical Activity** (`scoliosis_physical_activity`, select_one, required): Choose whether you experience difficulty engaging in physical activities due to scoliosis.
* **Scoliosis Clinic Visits** (`scoliosis_clinician_visits`, number, not required): Enter the number of times you have visited a scoliosis clinic in the last year.
* **Scoliosis Clinic Frequency** (`scoliosis_clinician_frequency`, text, not required): Describe how frequently you visit a scoliosis clinic.
* **Clinician Visits** (`scoliosis_clinician_other_visits`, text, not required): Describe any other clinician visits unrelated to scoliosis.
* **Clinician Visit Frequency** (`scoliosis_clinician_other_visit_frequency`, text, not required): Describe how frequently you visit a clinician other than your primary doctor for scoliosis.
* **Notes** (`scoliosis_notes`, text, not required): Provide any additional comments or notes about your scoliosis condition.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any question, please describe your situation in more detail.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact your healthcare provider for guidance.
* Please note that this form is used for medical purposes only and will be kept confidential.
