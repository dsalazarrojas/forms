# Airline Passenger Preference Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from passengers about their preferences for their flight, enabling the airline to provide a more personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as it appears on your travel documents.
2. If you have a frequent flyer number, enter it in the corresponding field.
3. Select your preferred seat type from the options provided.
4. Choose your preferred cabin area from the options provided.
5. Select your special dietary requirements from the options provided.
6. Enter your preferred welcome beverage (optional).
7. Select your preferred amenity preferences from the options provided.
8. Indicate whether you require extra pillows or blankets.
9. Choose your preferred newspaper or magazine (optional).
10. Select the primary purpose of your travel from the options provided.
11. Indicate whether you require priority baggage handling (optional).
12. If you have any other specific requests or preferences, enter them in the text field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Passenger Full Name** (required): Enter your full name as it appears on your travel documents.
* **Frequent Flyer Number** (optional): If you have a frequent flyer number, enter it here.
* **Preferred Seat Type** (required): Select your preferred seat type from the options provided.
	+ Window
	+ Aisle
	+ Extra Legroom
	+ Bulkhead
	+ No Preference
* **Preferred Cabin Area** (optional): Choose your preferred cabin area from the options provided.
	+ Front of Cabin
	+ Over Wing
	+ Rear of Cabin
* **Special Dietary Requirements** (required): Select your special dietary requirements from the options provided.
	+ None
	+ Vegetarian
	+ Vegan
	+ Gluten-Free
	+ Halal
	+ Kosher
	+ Nut-Free
	+ Diabetic
* **Preferred Welcome Beverage** (optional): Enter your preferred welcome beverage.
* **Amenity Preferences** (optional): Select your preferred amenity preferences from the options provided.
* **Require extra pillow or blanket?** (optional): Indicate whether you require extra pillows or blankets.
	+ True
	+ False
* **Preferred Newspaper or Magazine** (optional): Enter your preferred newspaper or magazine.
* **Primary Purpose of Travel** (required): Select the primary purpose of your travel from the options provided.
	+ Business
	+ Leisure
	+ Visiting Family/Friends
	+ Education
* **Priority Baggage Handling** (optional): Indicate whether you require priority baggage handling.
	+ True
	+ False
* **Any other specific requests or preferences?** (optional): Enter any other specific requests or preferences.
