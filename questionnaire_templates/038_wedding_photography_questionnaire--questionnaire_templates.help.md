# Wedding Photography Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This wedding photography questionnaire is designed to collect essential information from couples about their wedding event, including details about the event, their preferences, and special requests. This will help the photographer understand what they need to do to make the day special and provide the best possible service to their clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer all questions as accurately as possible, and make sure to provide all the necessary details.
2. If a question does not apply to you, please feel free to leave it blank.
3. If you have any special requests or requirements, please make sure to mention them in the appropriate field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Details** (`wedding_details`, text, required: false): This is where you can provide detailed information about your wedding event, including the date, time, location, and any specific requirements you may have.
* **Photographer Information** (`photographer_info`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide information about the photographer, including their name, contact number, and any other relevant details.
* **Wedding Preferences** (`wedding_preferences`, text, required: false): Here, you can share your preferences for the photography services, such as the type of photography you would like, the location for the shoot, and any other specific requirements you may have.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, required: false): If you have any special requests or requirements, please mention them in this field. This could include specific locations, time constraints, or any other unique requests.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide information about how you would like to be invoiced, such as payment methods and any other financial details.
* **Photographer Availability** (`photographer_availability`, text, required: false): Please provide information about the photographer's availability, including their work schedule and any limitations they may have.
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide information about how you would like to be contacted, such as email, phone, or in-person meetings.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide your preferred contact method, such as phone, email, or in-person meetings.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide the name of the person who should be contacted about any inquiries or concerns.
