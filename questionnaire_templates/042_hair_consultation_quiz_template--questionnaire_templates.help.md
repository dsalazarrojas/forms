# Hair Consultation Quiz Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a questionnaire template designed to collect information about a person's hair characteristics and preferences for a hair consultation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering the title of the form, which is the Hair Consultation Quiz Template.
2. On the first page, enter your name (or the client's name) in the "Form Start" field.
3. On the second page, provide details about your hair, including your height, weight, and other relevant characteristics (if desired).
4. On the third page, answer questions about your hair length and texture.
5. Choose the color and style of your hair from the list of options provided.
6. Select up to two preferred hair styles from the list of options provided.
7. Choose your preferred hair color from the list of options provided.
8. On the final page, enter your hair goals and any problems you have with your hair.
9. Provide a brief description of your hair goals (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Start** (`form_start`, text, required): Enter your name or the client's name.
* **Hair Details** (`hair_details`, text, required): Provide details about your hair, including height, weight, and other relevant characteristics.
* **Hair Length** (`hair_length`, number, required): Enter the length of your hair, measured in inches.
* **Hair Texture** (`hair_textur`, text, optional): Describe the texture of your hair.
* **Hair Color** (`hair_color`, select_one, required): Choose the color of your hair from the list of options provided.
* **Hair Style** (`hair_style`, select_one, optional): Choose the style of your hair from the list of options provided.
* **Preferred Hair Style** (`hair_preferred_style`, select_multiple, required): Select up to two preferred hair styles from the list of options provided.
* **Preferred Hair Color** (`preferred_hair_color`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred hair color from the list of options provided.
* **Hair Goal** (`hair_goal`, text, required): Enter your hair goals, including any specific styles or colors you want to achieve.
* **Hair Problems** (`hair_problems`, text, required): Enter any problems you have with your hair.
* **Hair Goal Description** (`hair_goal_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your hair goals (if desired).
* **Form End** (`form_end`, text, optional): Enter your name or confirmation of completion (if desired).

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions, as the information will be used to provide personalized hair advice.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, feel free to provide additional information or clarification.
* The form should only take a few minutes to complete, and it will help us to better understand your hair needs.
