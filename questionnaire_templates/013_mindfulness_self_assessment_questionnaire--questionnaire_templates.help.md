# Mindfulness Self Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mindfulness Self Assessment Questionnaire is a tool to help you evaluate your current level of mindfulness practice and identify areas for improvement. It is designed to be a self-administered questionnaire that guides you through a series of questions to assess your mindfulness skills, habits, and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question honestly and thoroughly, using the space provided for each section. You may refer to the explanations below for guidance on each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (1)**: This is a note section where you can introduce yourself and provide any relevant background information that may be helpful for the assessor to understand your answers.
* **Self Awareness (2)**: This question is rated on a scale of 1-10, where 1 means you rarely or never pay attention to your thoughts, feelings, and bodily sensations, and 10 means you always or almost always pay attention to your thoughts, feelings, and bodily sensations.
* **Mindfulness Practices (3)**: Select all the mindfulness practices you currently engage in, such as Mindfulness of breath, Mindfulness of body, etc.
* **Mindfulness Benefits (4)**: Select one or more benefits you experience as a result of your mindfulness practice, such as Reduced stress, Improved focus, etc.
* **Mindfulness Barriers (5)**: This question is not applicable as it is currently not configured.
* **Mindfulness Strategies (6)**: Select all the mindfulness strategies you currently use, such as Mindfulness of breath, Mindfulness of body, etc.
* **Mindfulness Goals (7)**: Select one or more goals you aim to achieve through mindfulness, such as Reduced stress, Improved focus, etc.
* **Mindfulness Practice Schedule (8)**: Select a date for when you last practiced mindfulness.
* **Mindfulness Reflections (9)**: This is a text area where you can provide any additional thoughts or reflections about your mindfulness practice.
* **Mindfulness Progress Tracking (10)**: This is a note section where you can record your progress or any notable events related to your mindfulness practice.
* **Mindfulness Self-Assessment (11)**: This question is rated on a scale of 1-10, where 1 means you do not assess your mindfulness practice regularly and 10 means you regularly assess your practice.
* **Mindfulness Self-Assessment Frequency (12)**: Rate how often you self-assess your mindfulness practice.
* **Mindfulness Reflection Duration (13)**: This question asks how long you typically spend on reflecting on your mindfulness practice, with options being 5 minutes, 10 minutes, 30 minutes, 60 minutes.
* **Mindfulness Reflection Frequency (14)**: Select how frequently you reflect on your mindfulness practice, such as Daily, Weekly, etc.
* **Mindfulness Reflection Location (15)**: Select where you typically reflect on your mindfulness practice, such as Yes or No.

Please note that some fields may not be currently configured to be filled in, but are intended to be part of the form in the future.
