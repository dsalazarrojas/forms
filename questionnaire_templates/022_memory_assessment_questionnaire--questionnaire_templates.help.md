# Memory Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a user's memory and attention abilities. It helps to track changes over time and is typically completed by a clinician or researcher who is administering the assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `user_name` field.
2. Provide the date of your last visit or interaction with the system.
3. Answer the questions in the subsequent fields, using the options provided for the `rating_of_focal_points`, `rating_of_distractions`, and `rating_of_focus` questions.
4. Enter your score for memory in the `memory_score` field.
5. Add any additional comments or information in the `notes` field, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user_name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **date_of_last_visit**: Provide the date of your last visit or interaction with the system. This date should be in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **memory_score**: Enter a number that best represents your level of memory, with higher scores indicating better memory performance.
* **rating_of_focal_points**: Choose the rating that best describes how many points you are able to maintain focus on in your daily activities.
	+ 0-5: You are unable to hold focus on one point at a time.
	+ 6-10: You are able to hold focus on a few points at a time.
	+ 11-15: You are able to hold focus on a few more points than before, but it is difficult.
	+ 16-20: You are able to hold focus on many points simultaneously.
	+ 21-25: You are able to hold focus on all points simultaneously.
* **rating_of_distractions**: Choose all the ratings that best describe the level of distractions you experience in your daily activities.
	+ No distractions: You do not experience any distractions.
	+ Moderate distractions: You experience some distractions, but they do not affect your focus.
	+ Severe distractions: You experience frequent and significant distractions that affect your focus.
* **rating_of_focus**: Choose the rating that best describes your level of focus in your daily activities.
	+ Very focused: You are able to maintain a high level of focus on your tasks.
	+ Moderately focused: You are able to focus on your tasks, but with some difficulty.
	+ Not very focused: You are unable to maintain focus on your tasks.
* **notes**: Provide any additional comments or information that you think is relevant to your assessment.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
- If you are unsure about any question, please ask the clinician or researcher for clarification.
- This form is designed to track changes over time, so please complete it with the same level of detail and accuracy each time you fill it out.
