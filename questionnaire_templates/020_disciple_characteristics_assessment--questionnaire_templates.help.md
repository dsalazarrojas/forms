# Disciple Characteristics Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about an individual's characteristics and their relationship with a church. It is intended for use by church leaders and staff to assess the characteristics of those who have joined their church.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Please enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Please enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. If you have a phone number, please enter it in the "Phone" field.
5. Please enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
6. If you are joining the church, please enter your date of joining in the "Date of Joining" field.
7. Please select all the characteristics that apply to you from the "Disciple Characteristics" field.
8. Please enter your growth reflections in the "Growth Reflections" field.
9. If you have a church affiliation, please enter the name of your church in the "Church Name" field.
10. If you have a church address, please enter it in the "Church Address" field.
11. If you have a church phone number, please enter it in the "Church Phone" field.
12. If you have a church email address, please enter it in the "Church Email" field.
13. If you have a church leader, please enter their name in the "Church Leader Name" field.
14. If you have a church leader's phone number, please enter it in the "Church Leader Phone" field.
15. If you have a church leader's email address, please enter it in the "Church Leader Email" field.
16. If you have a church leader's title, please enter it in the "Church Leader Title" field.
17. If you know the date of the church leader's ordination, please enter it in the "Church Leader Date of Ordination" field.
18. If you know the capacity of the church, please enter it in the "Church Capacity 1", "Church Capacity 2", "Church Capacity 3", or "Church Capacity 4" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (3, email, optional): Please enter your email address if you have one.
* **Phone** (4, text, optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it.
* **Date of Birth** (5, date, required): Please enter your date of birth.
* **Date of Joining** (6, date, required): If you are joining the church, please enter your date of joining.
* **Disciple Characteristics** (7, select_multiple, required): Please select all the characteristics that apply to you.
* **Growth Reflections** (8, text, required): Please enter your growth reflections.
* **Church Name** (9, text, required): If you have a church affiliation, please enter the name of your church.
* **Church Address** (10, text, optional): If you have a church address, please enter it.
* **Church Phone** (11, text, optional): If you have a church phone number, please enter it.
* **Church Email** (12, email, optional): If you have a church email address, please enter it.
* **Church Leader Name** (13, text, required): If you have a church leader, please enter their name.
* **Church Leader Phone** (14, text, optional): If you have a church leader's phone number, please enter it.
* **Church Leader Email** (15, email, optional): If you have a church leader's email address, please enter it.
* **Church Leader Title** (16, text, optional): If you have a church leader's title, please enter it.
* **Church Leader Date of Ordination** (17, date, optional): If you know the date of the church leader's ordination, please enter it.
* **Church Capacity 1** (22, number, optional): If you know the capacity of the church, please enter it.
* **Church Capacity 2** (23, number, optional): If you know the capacity of the church, please enter it.
* **Church Capacity 3** (24, number, optional): If you know the capacity of the church, please enter it.
* **Church Capacity 4** (25, number, optional): If you know the capacity of the church, please enter it.

## Tips
* Please make sure to complete all required fields.
* You can select multiple characteristics from the "Disciple Characteristics" field.
* You can enter your phone number and email address in the corresponding fields if you have them.
* If you have multiple capacities for the church, please enter all of them in the "Church Capacity 1", "Church Capacity 2", "Church Capacity 3", or "Church Capacity 4" fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask a church leader or staff member for assistance.
