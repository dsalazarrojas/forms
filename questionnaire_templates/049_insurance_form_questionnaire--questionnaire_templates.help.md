# Insurance Form Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your insurance needs and medical history for the purpose of assessing risk factors and providing relevant insurance quotes or recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each page (1-10) with the requested information.
2. Make sure to answer each question thoroughly and accurately.
3. Review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Info (Page 1)
• **customer_info**: Enter your personal details, including your name, address, and contact information.

### Contact Info (Page 2)
• **contact_info**: Provide contact information for yourself or a representative, including phone numbers and email addresses.

### Employment Info (Page 3)
• **employment_info**: Describe your current employment status, including job title, employer, and length of employment.

### Family Info (Page 4)
• **family_info**: List the members of your household, including their names, ages, and relationships to you.

### Household Info (Page 5)
• **household_info**: Describe your living situation, including the number of people living with you and their ages.

### Medical History (Page 6)
• **medical_history**: List any previous medical conditions, including allergies, illnesses, or injuries.

### Financial Info (Page 7)
• **financial_info**: Provide information about your income, assets, and debts.

### Risk Questions (Page 8)
• **risk_questions**: Answer questions about your lifestyle and habits, including smoking, alcohol consumption, and travel history.

### Policy Info (Page 9)
• **policy_info**: Describe the type of insurance you are interested in (e.g., health, life, auto).

### Review Info (Page 10)
• **review_info**: Review and confirm your answers, ensuring accuracy and completeness.

## Tips
- Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form.
- If you need help or clarification on any question, please reach out to the form administrator.
- You can save and come back to the form later if needed.
- This form is for general information purposes only and does not guarantee insurance coverage or quotes.
