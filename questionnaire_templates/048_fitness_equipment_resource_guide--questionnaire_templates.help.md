# fitness_equipment_resource_guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "fitness_equipment_resource_guide" form helps you select the perfect fitness equipment for your needs, preferences, and environment. Fill out this form to determine the best fit for your goals and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To get the most out of this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For fields with options, select the best fit for your needs from the provided choices.
3. Be as specific as you can with your answers to ensure accurate results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Goals** (`user_goals`, `text`, required: false): Please describe your current fitness goals, such as losing weight, gaining strength, or improving overall health.
* **Space Requirements** (`space_requirements`, `text`, required: false): Tell us about the available space for your equipment, including size, height, and any obstacles.
* **Budget Details** (`budget_details`, `number`, required: false): Share your budget range for the equipment, and we'll help you find options within your means.
* **Preferred Equipment** (`preferred_equipment`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Choose the type of equipment you're leaning towards (Stationary Bike, Treadmill, or Station Prophets)
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select the types of exercise you enjoy (Strength Training, Cardio, or Functional Training)
* **Equipment Location** (`equipment_location`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Where you plan to use your equipment (Home, Gym, or Commercial)
* **Purchase Method** (`equipment_purchase_method`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ How you plan to buy your equipment (Online, Retail, or In-Store)
* **Additional Equipment Notes** (`additional_equipment_notes`, `text`, required: false): If you have any other requirements or preferences not covered in the above fields, please let us know here.

## Tips
Remember to answer honestly and thoroughly to get the most helpful results. If you're unsure about any field or option, feel free to ask for clarification. The form is designed to assist you in finding the right equipment for your fitness journey.
