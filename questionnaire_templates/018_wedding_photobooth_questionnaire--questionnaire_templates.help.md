# Wedding Photobooth Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and details about a wedding photobooth event. It includes sections for guest list, photo list, event details, and photo details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by selecting the number of photos you would like to have in your photobooth from the "Photo Order" field.
2. Provide a list of your guests, including their names and any additional information in the "Guest List" field.
3. Answer the "Event Details" questions regarding the event's specifics.
4. For the "Photo Details" section, select the applicable options and provide any necessary details for each photo.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest List (guest_list)**: Enter the list of guests attending the wedding, including their names and any other relevant details.
* **Photo List (photo_list)**: Enter a list of photos to be taken during the event.
* **Photo Order (photo_order)**: Select the number of photos you would like to have in your photobooth.
* **Photo Order Hint (photo_order_hint)**: This field is for additional explanation regarding the number of photos selected.
* **Event Details (event_details)**: Answer the questions regarding the event's specifics, such as whether it's a wedding or not.
* **Event Details 1-5 (event_details_1-5)**: Provide additional details about the event.
* **Photo Details (photo_details)**: Select the applicable options for the photo details and provide any necessary information.
* **Photo Details 1-12 (photo_details_1-12)**: Provide additional details about the photos.
* **Tips**: Refer to the provided tips for additional guidance on completing the form.

Note: This help guide is for informational purposes only and is subject to change based on the form's design and requirements.
