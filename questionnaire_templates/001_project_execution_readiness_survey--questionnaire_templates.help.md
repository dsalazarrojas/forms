# Project Execution Readiness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form helps project teams evaluate their readiness for project execution. It's a comprehensive assessment tool that covers various aspects of project management, including progress, confidence, and urgency.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields, marked as "Required".
2. Be as accurate as possible when answering questions.
3. Select options from dropdown menus carefully, based on your project's status.
4. If you're unsure about any question, contact the project lead or project manager for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name** (`project_execution_readiness_survey_name`, text, required): Enter the project name.
* **Project Team Readiness** (`project_execution_readiness_survey_team_readiness`, number, required): Rate your project team's readiness on a scale of 1-5.
* **Project Launch Date** (`project_execution_readiness_survey_launch_date`, date, required): Enter the project launch date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Project Launch Time** (`project_execution_readiness_survey_launch_time`, time, required): Enter the project launch time in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Project Status** (`project_execution_readiness_survey_status`, select_one, required): Select the current project status from the options `Active`, `Delayed`, or `Cancelled`.
* **Project Comment** (`project_execution_readiness_survey_comment`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments about the project.
* **Project Email** (`project_execution_readiness_survey_email`, email, required): Enter the project email address.
* **Project Phone** (`project_execution_readiness_survey_phone`, text, optional): Enter the project phone number.
* **Project Urgency** (`project_execution_readiness_survey_urgency`, number, required): Rate your project's urgency on a scale of 1-5.
* **Project Importance** (`project_execution_readiness_survey_importance`, number, required): Rate your project's importance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Project Confidence** (`project_execution_readiness_survey_confidence`, number, required): Rate your confidence in the project on a scale of 1-5.
* **Project Comments** (`project_execution_readiness_survey_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments about the project.
* **Project Confidence Level** (`project_execution_readiness_survey_confidence_level`, number, required): Rate your confidence level on a scale of 1-5.
* **Project Progress** (`project_execution_readiness_survey_progress`, select_multiple, required): Select the current project progress from the options `Not Started`, `In Progress`, or `Completed`.
* **Project Execution Readiness Survey Status 2** (`project_execution_readiness_survey_status_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select the current project status from the options `Active`, `Delayed`, or `Completed`.
* **Project Execution Readiness Survey Urgency 2** (`project_execution_readiness_survey_urgency_2`, number, required): Rate your project's urgency on a scale of 1-5.
* **Project Execution Readiness Survey Importance 2** (`project_execution_readiness_survey_importance_2`, number, optional): Rate your project's importance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Project Execution Readiness Survey Confidence 2** (`project_execution_readiness_survey_confidence_2`, number, optional): Rate your confidence in the project on a scale of 1-5.
* **Project Confidence Levels** (`project_execution_readiness_survey_confidence_levels`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple confidence levels for your project.
* **Project Execution Readiness Survey Confidence 3** (`project_execution_readiness_survey_confidence_3`, number, optional): Rate your confidence in the project on a scale of 1-5.

Note: This form is designed to be filled out by project managers or team members. Please answer all questions honestly and accurately to ensure a thorough assessment of your project's readiness.
