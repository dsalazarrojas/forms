# psychology_questionnaire_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The psychology_questionnaire_template is a comprehensive form for gathering information about a patient's mental health and treatment preferences. It is designed to collect details about their identification, demographics, symptoms, mental and medical history, social environment, family history, treatment options, and follow-up requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each page and ensure you understand what information is being requested.
2. Enter the patient's identification details, including their name and other relevant identifying information.
3. Provide general demographic information, such as age, sex, and contact details.
4. Describe the patient's psychological symptoms and mental status.
5. Share their medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or treatments.
6. Detail their social environment, family history, and personal relationships.
7. Assess their mental status and psychological symptoms.
8. Discuss their medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or treatments.
9. Choose treatment options that apply to the patient's needs.
10. Indicate any other treatment options that may be relevant.
11. Schedule follow-up appointments, including the frequency and method of follow-up.
12. Determine the location of follow-up appointments.
13. Specify the duration of follow-up appointments.
14. Indicate any other follow-up duration options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Identification** (`Patient Identification`, text, required): Enter the patient's name and other identifying information.
* **General Information** (`demographics`, text, required): Provide the patient's age, sex, contact details, and other relevant demographic information.
* **Psychological Symptoms** (`psychological_symptoms`, text, required): Describe the patient's psychological symptoms.
* **Mental Status** (`mental_status`, text, required): Describe the patient's mental status.
* **Personal History** (`personal_history`, text, required): Share the patient's personal history.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Share the patient's medical history.
* **Social Environment** (`social_environment`, text, required): Describe the patient's social environment.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, text, required): Describe the patient's family history.
* **Mental Status Assessed** (`mental_status_assessed`, text, required): Assess the patient's mental status.
* **Psychological Symptoms Assessed** (`psychological_symptoms_assessed`, text, required): Assess the patient's psychological symptoms.
* **Medical History Assessed** (`medical_history_assessed`, text, required): Assess the patient's medical history.
* **Mental Status Assessment** (`mental_status_assessment`, text, required): Assess the patient's mental status.
* **Psychological Status Assessment** (`psychological_status_assessment`, text, required): Assess the patient's psychological status.
* **Treatment Options** (`treatment_options`, text, required): Choose the treatment options that apply to the patient's needs.
* **Other Treatment Option** (`other_treatment_option`, text, required): Indicate any other treatment options that may be relevant.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, date, required): Schedule a follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, number, required): Indicate the frequency of follow-up appointments.
* **Other Follow-up Frequency** (`other_follow_up_frequency`, number, required): Specify any other follow-up frequency options.
* **Other Follow-up Frequency** (`other_follow_up_frequency_other`, text, required): Describe any other follow-up frequency options.
* **Follow-up Method** (`follow_up_method`, select multiple, required): Choose the follow-up method, such as phone or video call.
* **Follow-up Location** (`follow_up_location`, select multiple, required): Specify the location of follow-up appointments, such as home or clinic.
* **Follow-up Duration** (`follow_up_duration`, number, required): Specify the duration of follow-up appointments.
* **Follow-up Duration Unit** (`follow_up_duration_unit`, text, required): Describe the unit of time for follow-up appointments, such as weeks or months.
* **Other Follow-up Duration** (`follow_up_duration_other`, text, required): Specify any other follow-up duration options.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you answer all questions accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that you have completed it accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the healthcare professional or researcher who is administering the form.
