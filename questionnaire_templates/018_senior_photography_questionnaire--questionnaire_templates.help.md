# Senior Photography Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Senior Photography Questionnaire is designed to collect information about your preferences, needs, and expectations for your photoshoot. This form will help us to better understand your requirements and provide the best possible experience for your photoshoot.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Scroll through the questions and select the options that best match your preferences and expectations.
2. Answer all questions that apply to you.
3. If you have any additional information that you feel is relevant to your photoshoot, please provide it in the "Comments" section at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info** (1): Enter your name and contact information to help us get in touch with you.
* **Social Media** (2): Select any social media platforms where you are active.
* **Favorite Food** (3): What is your favorite food? This will help us understand your personal preferences.
* **Preferred Color** (4): Select your favorite color from the options provided.
* **Date of Birth** (5): Enter your date of birth.
* **Time of Photoshoot** (6): Select the time of your photoshoot.
* **Photoshoot Location** (7): Enter the location of your photoshoot.
* **Photoshoot Duration** (8): Enter the estimated duration of your photoshoot.
* **How Did You Find Us?** (9): Select how you found us.
* **What Brought Your Attention** (10): Select all that apply how you found our work.
* **Why Did You Choose Us?** (11): Enter why you chose us for your photoshoot.
* **What Do You Like About Us?** (12): Select what you like about our style or service.
* **What Do You Dislike About Us?** (13): Select any aspects of our service that you do not like.
* **Rate Us!** (14): Rate your overall experience with us.
* **What Can We Improve?** (15): Enter any feedback on how we can improve our service.
* **Have You Met Previous Requirements?** (16): Select yes or no if you have met our previous requirements.
* **Previous Photographers** (17): Select any previous photographers you have worked with.
* **Type of Photoshoot** (18): Select the type of photoshoot you are interested in.
* **Photoshoot Date** (19): Enter the date of your photoshoot.
* **Photoshoot Time** (20): Select the time of your photoshoot.
* **Photoshoot Location** (21): Enter the location of your photoshoot.
* **Photoshoot Equipment** (22): Enter any specific equipment you need for your photoshoot.
* **Photoshoot Style** (23): Select the style of your photoshoot (natural, studio, or mix).
* **Photoshoot Notes** (24): Enter any additional notes about your photoshoot.
* **Comments** (25): Enter any additional information that you feel is relevant to your photoshoot.
