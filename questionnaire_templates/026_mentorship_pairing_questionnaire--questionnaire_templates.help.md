# Mentorship Pairing Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mentorship Pairing Questionnaire is designed to guide mentors and mentees in creating an effective mentorship relationship. It will help them set clear expectations and goals for their partnership, ensuring a productive and beneficial experience for both parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question clearly and concisely, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the most relevant options for each field.
3. Make sure to include all required fields (indicated by a asterisk *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### What is the purpose of this questionnaire? *
* **title**: This field is for the title of the mentorship pairing questionnaire.

### What type of mentorship program is this for? *
* **mentorship_program_type**: Choose the type of mentorship program that best describes the purpose of this pairing. The options are:
	+ Mentee is seeking career guidance: This type of program is focused on providing guidance for career development.
	+ Mentee is seeking skill development: This type of program is focused on skill development and learning.
	+ Other (please specify): If the program doesn't fit into the above categories, choose this option and describe the specific purpose.

### What is the length of the mentorship program? *
* **mentorship_program_length**: Select the expected length of the mentorship program. The options are:
	+ 3-6 months
	+ 6-12 months
	+ 1-2 years

### What are the expected outcomes of this mentorship? *
* **expected_outcome**: Describe the expected outcomes of this mentorship program. For example, "Career advancement", "Skill acquisition", "Personal growth", etc.

### What are the primary focus areas of this mentorship program? * 
* **program_focus**: Select all relevant areas that best describe the focus of the mentorship. The options are:
	+ Career Development
	+ Personal Growth
	+ Skill Development

### What is your role in this mentorship program? 
* **mentee_profile**: Choose your role in the mentorship program. The options are:
	+ Mentee: You are seeking guidance and development in your career or skills.
	+ Mentor: You are providing guidance and development to a mentee.
	+ Other (please specify): If you don't fit into the above categories, describe your specific role.

### What is your role in this mentorship program? 
* **mentor_profile**: Choose your role in the mentorship program. The options are:
	+ Mentor: You are providing guidance and development to a mentee.
	+ Mentee: You are seeking guidance and development in your career or skills.
	+ Other (please specify): If you don't fit into the above categories, describe your specific role.

### What is the start date of this mentorship program? 
* **program_start_date**: Enter the start date of the mentorship program.

### What is the end date of this mentorship program? 
* **program_end_date**: Enter the end date of the mentorship program.

## Tips
* Make sure to clearly and concisely answer each question.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask your mentor or mentee for clarification.
