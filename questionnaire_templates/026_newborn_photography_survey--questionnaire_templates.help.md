# Newborn Photography Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Newborn Photography Survey is designed to gather information from parents who have recently had their newborns photographed. The form aims to collect details about the photographer, parent, session preferences, and other relevant information to help us improve our photography services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the questions and fill in the required and optional sections as needed.
2. Provide as much detail as possible in each field.
3. Submit the completed form to proceed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photographer details** (`photographer_details`, text, required: false): This is the section where you can provide details about the photographer who took your newborn's photos. Please fill in the name and any other relevant information.
* **Parent details** (`parent_details`, text, required: false): Here, you can share information about yourself and your family, including your relationship with the newborn and any other family members involved in the photo session.
* **Session preferences** (`session_preferences`, text, required: false): This section is for you to describe your ideal photo shoot session preferences, such as the date, time, and location of the session.
* **Studio policy** (`studio_policy`, text, required: false): Please let us know about any policies or restrictions that might affect our photo studio, such as photography studio closures or special equipment requirements.
* **Parent consent** (`parent_consent`, text, required: false): Here, you can provide your consent for our team to contact you and your family for any additional information or to provide updates about your session.
* **Photographer consent** (`photographer_consent`, text, required: false): As a photographer, please let us know about your consent for us to share your contact information with your clients for follow-up purposes.
* **Additional questions** (`additional_questions`, text, required: false): This is a space for any additional questions or comments you might have about your photo session or our services.
* **Session details** (`session_details`, text, required: false): Describe the details of your photo session, including any specific themes or poses you'd like to focus on.
* **Session preferences 2** (`session_preferences_2`, text, required: false): Another section for you to describe your preferences for the photo session, if any.
* **Contact details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Provide your contact information, such as phone numbers and email addresses, so we can get in touch with you.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): This is an optional note-taking field for you to jot down any important information or reminders about your photo session.
* **E-mail** (`email`, email, required: false): Please provide your email address so we can contact you for any updates or follow-up questions.

Note: This form does not have any required fields, so you can complete it at your own pace and with the information you feel is necessary.
