# Client Education Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a client's educational background, learning style, and preferences to help our team assess their needs and provide effective support. Please fill out this questionnaire to the best of your abilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose the most relevant option from the list.
3. For required fields, ensure you fill them out completely.
4. For optional fields, you can choose not to fill them out, but it will help our team understand your needs better if you do provide the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`student_first_name`, required, text): Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name** (`student_last_name`, required, text): Enter your legal last name.
* **Email Address** (`student_email`, required, email): Enter your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`student_phone`, required, text): Enter your best contact number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, required, date): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Current Grade Level** (`grade_level`, required, select_one): Select your current grade level from the options provided.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, optional, text): If you are a student, enter the name of your current institution. If not, please leave this blank.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, optional, text): If you are a minor, enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_guardian_email`, optional, email): If you are a minor, enter the email of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`parent_guardian_phone`, optional, text): If you are a minor, enter the phone number of your parent or guardian.
* **Subject Areas of Interest** (`subject_areas`, required, select_multiple): Select all the subject areas you need help with.
* **Specific Topics or Concepts** (`specific_topics`, required, text): Describe exactly what you need help with in the subject areas you selected.
* **Current Grades in Subject** (`current_grades`, optional, text): Describe your current performance in the subject areas you selected.
* **Learning Goals** (`learning_goals`, required, text): Describe what you want to achieve.
* **Learning Challenges** (`learning_challenges`, optional, text): Describe any difficulties you face in learning.
* **Do You Have Any Learning Disabilities?** (`learning_disabilities`, optional, select_one): Select 'Yes' if you have any learning disabilities for accommodation purposes.
* **Preferred Learning Style** (`learning_style`, required, select_one): Select how you prefer to learn from the options provided.
* **Have You Had Tutoring Before?** (`previous_tutoring`, required, select_one): Select 'True' if you have had tutoring before.
* **Was Previous Tutoring Helpful?** (`previous_tutoring_effective`, optional, select_one): If you have had tutoring before, select how helpful it was.
* **Current Study Habits** (`study_habits`, required, select_one): Select how often you study from the options provided.
* **Available Study Time** (`available_study_time`, optional, text): Describe when you can study.
* **Motivation Level** (`motivation_level`, required, select_one): Select how motivated you are from the options provided.
* **Preferred Session Frequency** (`session_frequency`, required, select_one): Select how often you want to meet from the options provided.
* **Preferred Session Duration** (`session_duration`, required, select_one): Select the duration of each session from the options provided.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, required, date): Enter the date you are available for assessment.

Note: If you have any questions or concerns while filling out this form, please contact us.
