# Career Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help you explore your career interests and preferences. It will guide you through a series of questions to help you identify your strengths, interests, and goals, and provide insights into your potential career paths.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form by entering your information in the provided fields.
2. Select the options that best match your answers to each question.
3. Be as accurate and honest as possible when answering each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed in the responses.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address to help us contact you with any questions or to send you updates.
* **Age Range** (`age_range`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your age range to help us understand your perspective.
	+ Under 18: If you are under 18, please answer this question as a parent or guardian on behalf of the minor.
	+ 18-25: This age range is often associated with young adults exploring their interests and career options.
	+ 26-35: This age range is often associated with professionals establishing their careers.
	+ 36-45: This age range is often associated with mid-career professionals.
	+ 46-55: This age range is often associated with mature professionals.
	+ 56+: This age range is often associated with seasoned professionals.
* **Are You Currently a Student?** (`current_student`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate whether you are currently enrolled in high school, college, a graduate program, or if you have finished your education.
* **Current Grade Level or Major** (`current_grade_major`, `text`, required/optional): If you are a student, enter your current grade level or major.
* **Academic Interests** (`academic_interests`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all that apply from the list of academic interests to help us understand your strengths and interests.
	+ Science and Mathematics: Mathematics, physics, chemistry, etc.
	+ Language and Literature: English, language arts, literature, etc.
	+ Social Sciences: Sociology, psychology, anthropology, etc.
	+ Arts and Design: Art, music, drama, design, etc.
	+ Technology and Computing: Programming, IT, engineering, etc.
	+ Engineering: Electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, etc.
	+ Business and Economics: Marketing, finance, economics, etc.
	+ Health Sciences: Medicine, nursing, health sciences, etc.
	+ Agriculture and Environmental Science: Agriculture, environmental science, etc.
	+ Trades and Vocational Studies: Carpentry, electrical work, etc.
* **Strongest Subjects or Areas** (`strongest_subjects`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the subjects or areas where you excel or have a strong interest in.
* **Most Challenging Subjects** (`challenging_subjects`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the subjects or areas that you find difficult or challenging.
* **Career Preference** (`career_preference`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of work that interests you most.
	+ Working with people: Customer service, teaching, etc.
	+ Working with ideas and concepts: Research, writing, etc.
	+ Working with things and hands-on work: Trades, construction, etc.
	+ Working with data and information: Data analysis, research, etc.
	+ Creative work: Art, music, etc.
	+ Problem-solving: Problem-solving, critical thinking, etc.
	+ Teaching or mentoring: Education, coaching, etc.
	+ Leadership and management: Leadership, management, etc.
	+ Entrepreneurship: Starting a business, entrepreneurship, etc.
* **Industries of Interest** (`industry_interests`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select up to 5 industries that interest you the most.
* **Job Satisfaction Factors** (`job_satisfaction_factors`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select up to 3 job satisfaction factors that are most important to you.
	+ Good salary and benefits
	+ Job security
	+ Work-life balance
	+ Meaningful work
	+ Helping people
	+ Learning and growth
	+ Creativity and innovation
	+ Stability
	+ Flexible schedule
	+ Leadership opportunities
* **Preferred Work Style** (`work_style`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your preferred work style.
	+ Independent work: Working alone, self-directed work.
	+ Team-based work: Collaborative work, team environment.
	+ A mix of both: Combination of independent and team-based work.
	+ No preference: No preference.
* **Preferred Work Location** (`work_location`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all that apply from the list of preferred work locations.
	+ Office: Traditional office setting.
	+ Remote or home-based: Working from home or remotely.
	+ Outdoor: Outdoor work, construction, etc.
	+ Travel: Traveling for work.
	+ Multiple locations: Traveling or working in different locations.
	+ No strong preference: No preference.
* **How Important Is Salary?** (`salary_importance`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select how important salary is to you.
	+ Very important - top priority
	+ Important - significant factor
	+ Somewhat important
	+ Not very important
	+ Not important
* **Plans for Advanced Education** (`advanced_education`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any plans for advanced education, certifications, or training.
* **Extracurricular Activities or Hobbies** (`extracurricular_activities`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any extracurricular activities or hobbies that you enjoy.
* **Special Talents or Abilities** (`special_talents`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any special talents or abilities that you possess.
* **Career Role Models or Mentors** (`role_models`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any individuals who inspire your career choices.
* **Career Concerns or Uncertainties** (`career_concerns`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any concerns or uncertainties you have about your career.
* **Support Needed for Career Planning** (`support_needs`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all that apply from the list of support needed for career planning.
	+ Guidance and counseling
	+ Career information
	+ Mentoring
	+ Internship or work-study opportunities
	+ Skill development training
	+ Financial planning advice
	+ Family conversations
	+ Other support
* **Additional Comments About Your Career Interests** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your career interests.

# Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
* Take your time to think about each question carefully.
* If you are unsure about any question, select the option that best fits your situation.
* You can always return to this form to update your answers at any time.
