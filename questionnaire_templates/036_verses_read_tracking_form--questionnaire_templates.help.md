# Verses Read Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for tracking your progress, reflections, and ratings on different books or passages.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form for each "verse" you are tracking.
2. Start by filling out the "Introduction" section, if provided.
3. Answer the "Reading Progress" questions for each "verse".
4. Provide a "Reflection" for each "verse" if you feel it's necessary.
5. Add any "Notes" for each "verse" if you have additional comments or thoughts.
6. Enter the date you finished reading each "verse".
7. Estimate the time you spent reading each "verse".
8. Enter the number of pages you read for each "verse".
9. Rate each "verse" on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the least favorite and 5 being the most favorite.
10. Repeat the process for each "verse" you are tracking.
