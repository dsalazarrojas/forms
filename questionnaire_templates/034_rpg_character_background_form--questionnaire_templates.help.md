# RPG Character Background Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you create a detailed and immersive character background for your RPG game or story. Please fill out this form to provide the essential details about your character, including their name, race, class, backstory, and level of involvement in the game world.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Start by giving your character a name** (`character_name`). Enter the name of your character, which will help us get to know them better.
2. **Describe your character's background and history** (`character_backstory`). This is a chance to tell us about your character's past, their motivations, and their relationships.
3. **Choose your character's involvement level** (`player_involvement`): 
	* If you're playing as your character, select "Player" to indicate your role.
	* If you're designing or developing this game, select "Game Master" or "Game Developer" to indicate your role.
4. (Optional) **Add any additional background information** (`background_info`): This field is optional, but if you have any additional details about your character that you'd like to share, such as their personality or notable skills, please feel free to add them here.
5. (Optional) **List your character's organization or affiliation** (`organization`): If your character belongs to a specific organization or has a notable affiliation, please provide more details about it.
6. (Optional) **Link to your character sheet** (`character_sheet_link`): If you have a separate character sheet or document with more information about your character, please provide the link here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Character Name** (`character_name`, required): Enter the name of your character, which will be used to refer to them throughout the game or story.
* **Character Race** (`character_race`, optional): If you have a specific racial background for your character, please enter it here. This can include details like species, nationality, or cultural identity.
* **Character Class** (`character_class`, optional): If your character has a specific class or profession, such as a warrior, wizard, or rogue, please enter it here.
* **Character Backstory** (`character_backstory`, optional): This is a chance to tell us about your character's past, their motivations, and their relationships.
* **Player Involvement** (`player_involvement`, required): Choose your level of involvement as a player or game creator.
* **Background Information** (`background_info`, optional): If you have any additional details about your character that you'd like to share, please add them here.
* **Organization** (`organization`, optional): If your character belongs to a specific organization or has a notable affiliation, please provide more details about it.
* **Character Sheet Link** (`character_sheet_link`, optional): If you have a separate character sheet or document with more information about your character, please provide the link here.
