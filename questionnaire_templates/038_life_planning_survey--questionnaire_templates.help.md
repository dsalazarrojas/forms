# Life Planning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive questionnaire to help you reflect on your life goals, priorities, relationships, finances, education, and career development. It will provide you with valuable insights into your current situation and help you plan for your future.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Carefully read each question and provide honest answers.
2. Answer all the questions, even if you're not sure about something.
3. For select multiple and select one fields, choose as many options as apply to you.
4. For text fields, provide a brief but detailed description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Personal Goals
* **Personal Goals** (personal_goals, text, not required): Describe your personal goals, such as personal growth, relationships, or career development.

### 2. Current Priorities
* **Current Priorities** (current_priorities, select multiple, not required): Check all the priorities that apply to your current life, such as family, health, finances, or career.

### 3. Future Priorities
* **Future Priorities** (future_priorities, select_one, not required): Choose one priority that you think is most important for your future, such as Goal 1, Goal 2, or Goal 3.

### 4. Personal Values
* **Personal Values** (personal_values, text, not required): Share your personal values, such as honesty, respect, or kindness.

### 5. Personal Vision
* **Personal Vision** (personal_vision, text, not required): Describe your personal vision, such as a dream job or a desired lifestyle.

### 6. Current Challenges
* **Current Challenges** (current_challenges, text, not required): List your current challenges, such as time management, relationships, or career stagnation.

### 7. Personal Strengths
* **Personal Strengths** (personal_strengths, text, not required): Share your personal strengths, such as leadership, communication, or adaptability.

### 8. Current Skills
* **Current Skills** (current_skills, text, not required): List your current skills, such as language, programming, or leadership.

### 9. Future Skills
* **Future Skills** (future_skills, text, not required): Describe the skills you want to develop in the future, such as public speaking, project management, or data analysis.

### 10. Current Goals
* **Current Goals** (current_goals, select_multiple, not required): Check all the goals that apply to your current life, such as personal growth, career development, or financial stability.

### 11. Future Goals
* **Future Goals** (future_goals, select_multiple, not required): Choose all the goals that you aspire to achieve in the future, such as entrepreneurship, leadership, or personal fulfillment.

### 12. Personal Relationships

### 13. Current Relationships
* **Current Relationships** (current_relationships, text, not required): List your current relationships, such as colleagues, mentors, or family members.

### 14. Future Relationships

### 16. Future Networks

### 17. Personal Career

### 21. Current Finances
* **Current Finances** (current_finances, text, not required): Describe your current financial situation, such as income, expenses, or debt.

### 22. Future Finances
* **Future Finances** (future_finances, text, not required): Describe your future financial goals, such as saving for retirement, investing, or financial independence.

### 23. Personal Education

### 24. Current Education

### 25. Future Education
* **Future Education** (future_education, text, not required): Describe your future education goals, such as further study, specialization, or professional certification.

## Tips

* Be honest and detailed in your answers.
* Take your time to reflect on each question.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* Consider sharing your results with a trusted advisor or mentor.
* Use this survey as a starting point for your life planning and goal setting.
* Remember, this is a personal and individual assessment; there's no right or wrong answers.
