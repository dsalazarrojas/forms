# Mobile Device Security Camera Setup Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Mobile Device Security Camera Setup Guide form. This form is used to configure camera settings for mobile devices, ensuring you have a clear understanding of your camera's capabilities and settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's pages and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. For each page, answer the questions and select options as prompted.
3. Make sure to review each field's label and options carefully, as some fields have binary choices (Yes/No) while others require numerical values or select_one options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Camera Details (Page 1)
* **Camera Details** (`camera_details`, note, optional): This field is for any additional camera details or notes. You can enter any relevant information about your camera here.
* **Camera Type** (`camera_type`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if your camera is currently enabled or disabled.
* **Camera Connection** (`camera_connection`, select_multiple, optional): Choose from 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if your camera is connected to the mobile device.
* **Camera Resolution** (`camera_resolution`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value for your camera's resolution.
* **Camera Angle** (`camera_angle`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value for your camera's angle.
* **Camera Position** (`camera_position`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if your camera is currently positioned at a specific location.
* **Camera Zoom** (`camera_zoom`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value for your camera's zoom.
* **Camera Focal Length** (`camera_focal_length`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value for your camera's focal length.
* **Camera Lens** (`camera_lens`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if your camera has a lens.
* **Camera Magnification** (`camera_magnification`, select_one, optional): Select 'Good' or 'Poor' to indicate your camera's image quality.
* **Camera Flash** (`camera_flash`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if your camera has a flash.
* **Camera Lens Correction** (`camera_lens_correction`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if your camera has lens correction.
* **Camera Flash Auto** (`camera_flash_auto`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if your camera's flash is auto-enabled.
* **Camera Auto Flash** (`camera_auto_flash`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if your camera's flash is auto-enabled.
* **Camera Auto White Balance** (`camera_auto_white_balance`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if your camera's white balance is auto-enabled.
* **Camera White Balance** (`camera_white_balance`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if your camera has white balance.
* **Camera Auto Exposure** (`camera_auto_exposure`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if your camera's exposure is auto-enabled.
* **Camera Exposure** (`camera_exposure`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value for your camera's exposure.
* **Camera Auto Metering** (`camera_auto_metering`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if your camera's metering is auto-enabled.
* **Camera Metering** (`camera_metering`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if your camera has metering.
* **Camera Auto White Level** (`camera_auto_white_level`, select_one, optional): Select 'High' or 'Low' to indicate your camera's white level.
* **Camera White Level** (`camera_white_level`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value for your camera's white level.
* **Camera Auto Gain** (`camera_auto_gain`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if your camera's gain is auto-enabled.
* **Camera Gain** (`camera_gain`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value for your camera's gain.
* **Camera Image Quality** (`camera_image_quality`, select_one, optional): Select 'Good' or 'Poor' to indicate your camera's image quality.

## Tips
* Make sure to review each field carefully and select the correct options.
* If you're unsure about a field, refer to the field's label and options for guidance.
* If you need to make any changes, you can go back and edit your previous answers.
* Save your changes before proceeding to the next page.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
