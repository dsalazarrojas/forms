# Pet Brand Loyalty Study Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Pet Brand Loyalty Study Survey is designed to gather information about pet owners' satisfaction with pet-related products and services. This survey aims to understand the factors that influence pet owners' loyalty to their preferred brands, including their level of satisfaction, purchase frequency, and drivers of loyalty.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select all applicable options for "pet_owners" that apply to you.
2. Answer the question about your satisfaction level on a scale of 1-10.
3. Indicate your purchase frequency by selecting one of the options (Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never).
4. Choose all drivers of loyalty that apply to you (Satisfaction with Product, Price, Recommendation, or Store Experience).
5. If you wish to provide additional comments, you may type them in the "open-ended-question" section.
6. Enter your email address in the required "email_input" field.
7. Provide your phone number in the "phone_input" field.
8. Enter the date in the "date_input" field, using the standard date format (dd/mm/yyyy).
9. Enter the time in the "time_input" field, using the standard time format (HH:MM AM/PM).
10. Enter the date and time in the "date_time_input" field, using the standard date and time format (dd/mm/yyyy HH:MM AM/PM).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **pet_owners**: Select all pet owners you have.
	+ This field allows you to choose which pet owners you have at home.
* **satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction level on a scale of 1-10.
	+ This field measures how satisfied you are with your pet-related experiences.
* **purchase_frequency**: Indicate how frequently you make purchases.
	+ This field asks about your purchase frequency for pet-related products or services.
* **loyalty_drivers**: Choose all factors that contribute to your loyalty.
	+ These options represent the factors that make you loyal to a pet brand.
* **open-ended-question**: Provide additional comments or insights.
	+ This field is optional and allows you to share any additional information not covered in the other questions.
* **email_input**: Enter your email address.
	+ This is the contact information for follow-up communications.
* **phone_input**: Enter your phone number.
	+ This is your phone number for follow-up calls or further communication.
* **date_input**: Enter the date in the standard format (dd/mm/yyyy).
	+ This field is for your date of birth or another relevant date.
* **time_input**: Enter the time in the standard format (HH:MM AM/PM).
	+ This field is for your time of day or another relevant time.
* **date_time_input**: Enter the date and time in the standard format (dd/mm/yyyy HH:MM AM/PM).
	+ This field is for a specific date and time.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please feel free to contact us.
* This survey is designed to be completed quickly, and it should take less than 10 minutes to finish.
