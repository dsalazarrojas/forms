# Anxiety Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help assess your anxiety levels, identify triggers, and gather information about your coping strategies and support systems. Please answer the questions as honestly as possible to help us better understand your anxiety experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name in the "Your name" field.
2. Choose the correct age range from the "Age range" dropdown menu.
3. Select one of the options for "Do you experience anxiety?"
4. Choose how long you've experienced anxiety for "How long have you experienced anxiety?"
5. Select how often you experience anxiety symptoms in "How often do you experience anxiety symptoms?"
6. Choose the intensity of your anxiety symptoms in "Intensity of anxiety symptoms."
7. Select all that apply for "What triggers your anxiety?"
8. Select all that apply for "Physical symptoms experienced."
9. Select all that apply for "Emotional symptoms experienced."
10. Choose how anxiety impacts your daily life in "How does anxiety impact your daily life?"
11. Select how anxiety affects your work or studies in "Does anxiety affect your work or studies?"
12. Select how anxiety affects your social relationships in "Does anxiety affect your social relationships?"
13. Choose your current coping strategies in "Coping strategies you currently use."
14. Select how effective you find your coping strategies in "How effective are your current coping strategies?"
15. Choose whether you have sought professional help in "Have you sought professional help for anxiety?"
16. Select the type of professional help you have received in "Type of professional help received."
17. Select how helpful you found professional help in "How helpful was the treatment?"
18. Choose how supportive you feel your network is in "Do you have a supportive network?"
19. If you have any additional symptoms or concerns, describe them in the "Any additional symptoms or concerns?"
20. (Optional) Indicate your interest in learning about anxiety treatment options in "Are you interested in learning about anxiety treatment options?"
 
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your name**: Please fill in your name to help us understand your identity.
* **Age range**: Select the age group that best describes you.
* **Do you experience anxiety?**: Choose one of the options that best describes your anxiety experience.
* **How long have you experienced anxiety?**: Select the time frame that best describes how long you've experienced anxiety.
* **How often do you experience anxiety symptoms?**: Choose how frequently you experience anxiety symptoms.
* **Intensity of anxiety symptoms**: Select the intensity of your anxiety symptoms.
* **What triggers your anxiety?**: Please select all that apply.
* **Physical symptoms experienced**: Select all that apply.
* **Emotional symptoms experienced**: Select all that apply.
* **How does anxiety impact your daily life?**: Choose the impact that best describes how anxiety affects you.
* **Does anxiety affect your work or studies?**: Select how anxiety impacts your work or studies.
* **Does anxiety affect your social relationships?**: Choose how anxiety affects your social relationships.
* **Coping strategies you currently use**: Please select all that apply.
* **How effective are your current coping strategies?**: Choose how effective you find your coping strategies.
* **Have you sought professional help for anxiety?**: Select whether you have sought help.
* **Type of professional help received**: Please select all that apply.
* **How helpful was the treatment?**: Choose how helpful you found the treatment.
* **Do you have a supportive network?**: Choose how supportive you feel your network is.
* **Any additional symptoms or concerns?**: If you have additional symptoms or concerns, describe them here.
* **Are you interested in learning about anxiety treatment options?**: (Optional) Indicate your interest. 

This help guide should be easy to understand and navigate. Let me know if you need any further clarification or modifications. 

Please note that some fields are marked as 'required' but not all of them, if a field is not required it will be indicated on the form but can be left blank. 

Also, some fields will ask for "Select all that apply" which are typically multiple selection fields where you can select more than one option. 

Finally, some fields might be marked with "null" or "N/A" in the hints, this is because they might not be applicable for everyone's situation.
