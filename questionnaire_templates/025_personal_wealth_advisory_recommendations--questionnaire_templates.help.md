## Personal Wealth Advisory Recommendations - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Personal Wealth Advisory Recommendations form is designed to gather information about a client's goals, risk tolerance, investment preferences, financial objectives, and investment experience. This data will be used to provide personalized investment recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On each page, you will be presented with a series of questions. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. For the "Client Goals" page, enter a brief description of your financial goals.
3. For the "Risk Tolerance" page, indicate your comfort level with taking risks in your investments.
4. For the "Investment Preferences" page, select all options that apply to your investment preferences.
5. For the "Financial Objectives" page, select your primary financial objective.
6. For the "Investment Experience" page, indicate your level of investment experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Client Goals (Page 1)
• **Client Goals** (`client_goals`, text, optional): Please enter a brief description of your financial goals, such as saving for retirement, a down payment on a house, or a specific investment goal.

### Risk Tolerance (Page 1)
• **Risk Tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`, text, optional): This question will help us understand your comfort level with taking risks in your investments. You can answer "Low", "Medium", or "High" to indicate your risk tolerance.

### Investment Preferences (Page 2)
• **Investment Preferences** (`investment_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all options that apply to your investment preferences:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### Financial Objectives (Page 2)
• **Financial Objectives** (`financial_objectives`, select_one, optional): Please select your primary financial objective:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### Investment Experience (Page 2)
• **Investment Experience** (`investment_experience`, select_multiple, optional): Please indicate your level of investment experience:
	+ Yes
	+ No

## Tips
- Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
- If you are unsure about any question, you can always ask for clarification.
- If you need help with completing the form, please contact our support team.
