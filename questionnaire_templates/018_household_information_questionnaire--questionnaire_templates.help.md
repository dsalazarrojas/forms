# Household Information Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the household, including the head of the household and the number of people living there. The goal is to gather essential data for further analysis and processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the "Household Head" field with the name of the person who resides at the address.
2. Enter the number of people living in the household in the "Number of People" field.
3. If you want to provide information about the household income, you can do so in the "Household Income" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Household Head**: Enter the name of the person who resides at the address.
* **Number of People**: Enter the number of people living in the household.
* **Household Income**: Enter the income of the household if you want to provide this information.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can always contact the administrator for clarification.
* Please be honest and accurate when filling in the information, as it will be used for further analysis and processing.
