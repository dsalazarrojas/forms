# Wealth Preservation Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wealth Preservation Assessment is a comprehensive form designed to help individuals and organizations assess their wealth preservation needs. This form is intended to gather information about an individual's financial situation, goals, and risks to provide a comprehensive overview of their wealth preservation status.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2. For text fields (e.g., Financial Adviser), provide a clear and concise response.
3. For select fields, choose the most accurate option from the provided list.
4. For numerical fields (e.g., Risk Level), enter a number that best represents your risk tolerance.
5. Review each page before submitting to ensure all fields are complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Financial Adviser**: This field is intended for you to provide information about your current financial adviser or planner. Please list their name and contact information.
* **2. Risk Tolerance**: This field measures your risk tolerance, with higher numbers indicating a greater risk tolerance.
* **3. Assets Owned**: In this text field, list all assets you own, including cash, investments, and other valuable items.
* **4. Asset Allocation**: Select one or more options that best describe your current asset allocation strategy. Options include:
	+ Cash
	+ Stocks
	+ Bonds
	+ Alternative Investments
* **5. Financial Goals**: Check all goals that apply to your current financial situation. Options include:
	+ Retirement
	+ Wealth Creation
	+ Debt Reduction
* **6. Income Streams**: Check all income streams that apply to your current financial situation. Options include:
	+ Salary
	+ Investments
	+ Rental Income
* **7. Investment Strategy**: This field is currently not used in the form. Please ignore it.
* **8. Tax Filing Status**: Select one option that best describes your current tax filing status. Options include:
	+ Single
	+ Joint
	+ Married
* **9. Risk Level**: This field measures your risk tolerance, with higher numbers indicating a greater risk tolerance.
* **10. Time Frame**: Select one option that best describes your time frame for achieving financial goals. Options include:
	+ Short Term

	+ Long Term
* **11. Asset Protection**: Check one or more options that best describe your current asset protection strategies. Options include:
	+ 'True'

	+ 'True'
