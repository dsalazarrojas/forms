# honeymoon_planning_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Honeymoon Planning Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information from couples planning a dream honeymoon. The form collects details about the couple's preferences and requirements for their trip, allowing planners to create a personalized plan tailored to their needs and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your destination for the honeymoon from the options provided.
2. Choose your travel dates or specify your preferred date range.
3. Indicate your budget for the trip, using the budget fields provided.
4. Select the activities and experiences you're interested in, such as visiting specific attractions or engaging in certain hobbies.
5. Choose your preferred type of accommodations and transportation.
6. Specify your dining preferences.
7. Add any additional notes or comments about your preferences.
8. Provide your contact information and email address for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Destination**: Select the location you're planning to visit for your honeymoon. This can be a city, a country, or a specific region.
* **Travel Dates**: Choose your ideal travel dates or specify a date range. This helps us understand when you're planning to take your trip.
* **Budget**: Indicate your budget for the trip, using the numerical fields provided. Please note that these fields are not limited to specific numbers, so feel free to enter any amount you're comfortable with.
* **Budget Breakdown**: Select the specific aspects of your budget you'd like us to consider, such as food, accommodations, activities, or transportation.
* **Activities**: Choose the activities and experiences you're interested in, such as visiting museums, going on a wine tour, or engaging in water sports.
* **Accommodations**: Select the type of accommodations you prefer, such as hotels, resorts, or vacation rentals.
* **Transportation**: Choose the mode of transportation you'd like to use, such as flights, trains, or car rentals.
* **Dining**: Specify your dining preferences, such as fine dining, casual dining, or specific cuisines.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your preferences, such as any special requests or allergies.
* **Contact Info**: Provide your contact information, including your name and phone number, so we can reach out to you for follow-up.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, which we'll use to communicate with you about your trip plan.
* **Budget2-16**: These fields are additional budget fields for specific aspects of your trip, such as food, accommodations, activities, or transportation. You can enter any amount you're comfortable with, as these fields are not limited to specific numbers.
* **Budget17-26**: These fields are similar to Budget2-16, allowing you to provide more specific budget details for your trip.

Please fill out the form according to your preferences and interests, and we'll create a personalized honeymoon plan tailored just for you!
