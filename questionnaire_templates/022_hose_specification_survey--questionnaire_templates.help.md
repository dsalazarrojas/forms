# Hose Specification Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hose Specification Survey is designed to collect essential information about your hose, including customer contact details, preferences, and specifications. This data will help us better understand your needs and improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for the select fields.
3. If you have any issues or questions, contact us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (Page 1)** (`title`, required): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, required): Enter your contact number where we can reach you.
* **Customer Email (Page 1)** (`customer_email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Select 1** (`select_1`, required): Choose one of the options provided. If "Other" is selected, please provide the details in the "Customer Name" field.
* **Select 1 Options** (`survey_page_2`, required): Select one of the options provided. If "Other" is selected, please provide the details in the "Customer Name" field.
* **Date and Time** (`date_and_time`, required): Select one or multiple dates and times from the provided options.
* **Customer Info 2** (`customer_info_2`, optional): Enter any additional information about yourself, if needed.
* **Survey Page 5** (`survey_page_5`, required): Select one of the options provided. If "Other" is selected, please provide the details in the "Customer Name" field.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, required): Enter your name if you chose "Other" in any of the previous fields.
* **Customer Info 3** (`customer_info_3`, optional): Enter any additional information about yourself, if needed.
* **Date** (`date`, optional): Select a date from the provided options.
* **Time** (`time`, optional): Select a time from the provided options.

Please note that some fields are optional, and you can skip them if not necessary. Ensure to fill in all the required fields to submit your survey correctly.
