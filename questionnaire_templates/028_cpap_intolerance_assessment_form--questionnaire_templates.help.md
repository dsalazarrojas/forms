# CPAP Intolerance Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CPAP Intolerance Assessment Form is a tool used to identify areas where patients may be experiencing difficulties or discomfort with their CPAP therapy. This information helps healthcare providers understand the needs of their patients and tailor their treatment plans accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please enter your full name as it appears on your medical record.
2. Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
3. Enter your patient ID or medical record number (optional).
4. Enter your email address.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Enter the date of your CPAP therapy assessment.
7. Enter the date you started CPAP therapy.
8. Select the duration of your CPAP therapy use from the options provided.
9. Assess your overall tolerance of CPAP therapy.
10. Check if you experience any difficulty with mask fit or comfort. If yes, select all that apply.
11. Check if you experience claustrophobia or anxiety with the mask. If yes, select 'True'.
12. Check if you experience nasal congestion or irritation. If yes, select 'True'.
13. Check if you experience dry mouth or throat. If yes, select 'True'.
14. Check if you experience eye discomfort or dryness. If yes, select 'True'.
15. Check if you experience noise disturbance from the machine. Select 'Yes, very noisy', 'Somewhat noisy', or 'Not noisy'.
16. Check if you experience difficulty with pressure adjustments. If yes, select 'True'.
17. Enter the number of nights per week you use your CPAP machine.
18. Check if you have contacted your physician about your issues. If yes, select 'True'.
19. Enter any additional comments or concerns you may have about your CPAP therapy experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name**: Your full name as it appears on your medical record.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Patient ID or Medical Record Number**: Your patient ID or medical record number (optional).
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of Assessment**: The date of your CPAP therapy assessment.
* **Date CPAP Therapy Started**: The date you started CPAP therapy.
* **CPAP Therapy Usage Duration**: Select the duration of your CPAP therapy use from the options provided.
* **Overall Tolerance of CPAP Therapy**: Assess your overall tolerance of CPAP therapy. Select 'Excellent', 'Good', 'Fair', 'Poor', or 'Cannot tolerate'.
* **Difficulty with Mask Fit or Comfort**: Check if you experience difficulty with mask fit or comfort. Select all that apply.
* **Claustrophobia or Anxiety with Mask**: Check if you experience claustrophobia or anxiety with the mask. If yes, select 'True'.
* **Nasal Congestion or Irritation**: Check if you experience nasal congestion or irritation. If yes, select 'True'.
* **Dry Mouth or Throat**: Check if you experience dry mouth or throat. If yes, select 'True'.
* **Eye Discomfort or Dryness**: Check if you experience eye discomfort or dryness. If yes, select 'True'.
* **Noise Disturbance from Machine**: Select if you experience noise disturbance from the machine. Select 'Yes, very noisy', 'Somewhat noisy', or 'Not noisy'.
* **Difficulty with Pressure Adjustments**: Check if you experience difficulty with pressure adjustments. If yes, select 'True'.
* **Nights Per Week Using CPAP**: Enter the number of nights per week you use your CPAP machine.
* **Have You Contacted Physician**: Check if you have contacted your physician about your issues. If yes, select 'True'.
* **Additional Comments or Concerns**: Enter any additional comments or concerns you may have about your CPAP therapy experience.

## Tips
- Be honest and provide as much detail as possible when answering the questions.
- Select all that apply for mask problems.
- Enter your phone number and email address where you can be reached.
- Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
- Select the duration of your CPAP therapy use from the options provided.
