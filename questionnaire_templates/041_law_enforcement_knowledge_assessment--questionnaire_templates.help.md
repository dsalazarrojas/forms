# Law Enforcement Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Law Enforcement Knowledge Assessment is a tool used to evaluate the knowledge and skills of law enforcement personnel.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Choose the correct answers from the provided options for questions with options.
3. Enter your answers in the text boxes for open-ended questions.
4. Make sure to complete all sections of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, `text`, required: false): This is a text box where you can enter your name and other relevant personal information.
* **Law Enforcement Background** (`law_enforcement_background`, `text`, required: false): In this field, please provide your background and experience in law enforcement.
* **Legal Understanding** (`legal_understanding`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all the options that you are familiar with or have knowledge about, including:
	+ Police Code of Conduct
	+ Use of Force
	+ Deadly Force
	+ Search and Arrest
	+ Arrest and Detention
* **Core Duties** (`core_duties`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the options that you are familiar with or have experience in, including:
	+ Patrol Procedures
	+ Defensive Tactics
	+ Communication
	+ Emergency Response
* **Judgment** (`judgment`, `text`, required: false): In this field, please provide a brief answer about your ability to make sound judgments in critical situations.
* **Law Enforcement Equipment** (`law_enforcement_equipment`, `text`, required: false): Please describe the equipment you are familiar with or have experience in using.
* **Legal Resources** (`legal_resources`, `text`, required: false): In this field, please provide information about the resources you use to stay up-to-date with legal knowledge and procedures.
* **Emergency Response** (`emergency_response`, `text`, required: false): Describe your experience and knowledge about emergency response procedures.
* **Law Related Issues** (`law_related_issues`, `text`, required: false): In this field, please provide information about law-related issues you are familiar with or have experience in.
