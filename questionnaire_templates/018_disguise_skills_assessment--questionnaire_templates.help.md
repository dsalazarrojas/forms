# Disguise Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and document the details of a training session, including the skills assessed, self-evaluation, date, time, location, comments, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose the training session that you want to assess.
2. Select the skills that were assessed during the session.
3. Provide your self-evaluation of the skills assessed.
4. Enter the date and time of the session.
5. Choose the location where the session was held.
6. Add any comments about the session.
7. Enter your email address and phone number.
8. Select the person who was assigned to lead the session.
9. Enter any comments from the reviewer.
10. Select the person who submitted the form.
11. Enter the date and time when the form was submitted.
12. Choose the location where the form was submitted.
13. Enter your phone number.
14. Enter your email address.
15. Add any comments about the submission.
16. Select the type of session that was held.
17. Enter the name of the session.
18. Enter a brief description of the session.
19. Select the participants that attended the session.
20. Select the date when the form was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Session** (`training_session`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the ID number of the training session you want to assess.
* **Skills Assessed** (`skills_assessed`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the skills that were assessed during the session.
* **Self Evaluation** (`self_evaluation`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief self-evaluation of the skills assessed.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the session.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the session.
* **Location** (`location`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the location where the session was held.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required/optional): Add any comments about the session.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the person who was assigned to lead the session.
* **Reviewer Comments** (`reviewer_comments`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any comments from the reviewer.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when the form was submitted.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time when the form was submitted.
* **Submitted Location** (`submitted_location`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the location where the form was submitted.
* **Submitted Phone** (`submitted_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number at the time of submission.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitted_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address at the time of submission.
* **Submitter Comments** (`submitted_comments`, `note`, required/optional): Add any comments about the submission.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of session that was held.
* **Session Name** (`session_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the session.
* **Session Description** (`session_description`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the session.
* **Participants** (`participants`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the participants that attended the session.
* **Submitted** (`submitted`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when the form was submitted.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the person who submitted the form.
* **Review** (`review`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any comments about the review.
