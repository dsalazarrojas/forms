# Civil War Map Worksheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide students in analyzing the Civil War era by having them mark key battles on a map and consider the effects of troop movements and terrain on outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, students will be required to fill out the following fields:

1. **Student name** (Student name): Enter your full name as recorded in your school records.
2. **Class period** (Class period): Enter the name of your class period (e.g., Period 3).
3. **Map region studied** (Map region studied): Specify the theater or state you are studying (e.g., Eastern Theater).
4. **Key battles to mark on map** (Key battles to mark on map): List the key battles and their corresponding dates.
5. **Troop movements analysis** (Troop movements analysis): Describe the general directions and reasons behind the troop movements.
6. **Terrain effects on outcomes** (Terrain effects on outcomes): Explain how the terrain influenced the outcomes of battles.
7. **Sources used for map** (Sources used for map): Cite any books, articles, or other sources used for the map.
8. (Optional) **Critical thinking question** (Critical thinking question): Answer a question that explores alternative strategies or outcomes.
9. (Optional) **Teacher notes** (Teacher notes): This field is for your instructor's use only.
10. **Submission date** (Submission date): Enter the current date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student name**: This field is used to record your full name.
* **Class period**: Enter the name of your class period (e.g., Period 3).
* **Map region studied**: Specify the theater or state you are studying (e.g., Eastern Theater).
* **Key battles to mark on map**: List the key battles and their corresponding dates.
* **Troop movements analysis**: Describe the general directions and reasons behind the troop movements.
* **Terrain effects on outcomes**: Explain how the terrain influenced the outcomes of battles.
* **Sources used for map**: Cite any books, articles, or other sources used for the map.
* **Critical thinking question**: This field is optional and is meant to encourage critical thinking and exploration of alternative strategies or outcomes.
* **Teacher notes**: This field is for your instructor's use only.
* **Submission date**: Enter the current date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

## Tips
* Use a standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD) for the submission date field.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask your instructor for guidance.
