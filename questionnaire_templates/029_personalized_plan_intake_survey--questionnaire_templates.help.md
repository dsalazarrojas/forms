# Personalized Plan Intake Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Personalized Plan Intake Survey is designed to gather essential information from clients or customers during the planning process. It helps in creating tailored plans that cater to their specific needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in the form completely and accurately.
- Provide your name and preferred contact method(s) (Phone, Email, or both).
- Choose your business type (Small Business, Medium Business, or Large Business).
- Fill out the corresponding business type field(s) with more specific details.
- Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Header**: The form header is the first section of the form. It is not a mandatory field.
- *What is your name?*: Provide your full name as it appears on your business or personal records.
- *Preferred contact method*: Select the preferred method(s) to contact you (Phone, Email, or both).
  - *Phone*: Enter your phone number if you selected "Phone" as your preferred contact method.
  - *Email*: Enter your email address if you selected "Email" as your preferred contact method.
- *Business Type*: Select the type of business you have (Small Business, Medium Business, or Large Business).
  - *Small Business*: Enter more details about your small business.
  - *Medium Business*: Enter more details about your medium business.
  - *Large Business*: Enter more details about your large business.
- *Form Footer*: This field is not a mandatory field and is meant for any additional comments or notes.
