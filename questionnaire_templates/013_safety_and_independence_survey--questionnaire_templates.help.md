# Safety And Independence Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety And Independence Survey is a questionnaire designed to assess an individual's safety and independence in a healthcare setting. It helps healthcare professionals and caregivers to evaluate the individual's ability to perform daily activities and their level of safety awareness.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select one of the options for each field based on the individual's current situation.
2. If a field is not applicable, select "No" or "N/A" (Not Applicable).
3. Enter any relevant details or comments in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **survey** (select_one):
	+ Select "Yes" if the individual has completed a previous survey with similar questions. If not, select "No".
* **caregiver-info** (number):
	+ Enter the number of caregivers providing support to the individual.
* **support-needs** (note):
	+ Describe any support needs the individual requires, such as medical equipment, medication, or transportation.
* **safety-score** (select_multiple):
	+ Select "Yes" for each of the following that applies:
	+ Is the individual at risk of harm or injury?
	+ Has the individual experienced falls or near-falls in the past?
	+ Does the individual have a history of medical events that may impact safety?
* **independence** (select_multiple):
	+ Select "Yes" for each of the following that applies:
	+ Can the individual perform daily activities without assistance?
	+ Can the individual manage their own finances?
	+ Can the individual communicate effectively with others?
* **other-needs** (text):
	+ Describe any other needs the individual requires that are not listed above.
* **caregiver-assessment** (time):
	+ Enter the time spent providing support to the individual per day.
* **survey-submission** (note):
	+ Enter any notes or comments from the individual or healthcare provider.
