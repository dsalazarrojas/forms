**Essay Evaluation Rubric Questionnaire - Help Guide**

## Purpose

This form is used to evaluate student essays based on 5 specific rubric criteria. It helps teachers gather feedback and scores for each essay, providing valuable insights for student improvement and assessment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student" field.
2. Enter the teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
3. Enter the essay title in the "Essay Title" field.
4. For each rubric criterion (1-5), enter a score from 0 to 9, where 0 is the lowest score and 9 is the highest.
5. Provide feedback for each rubric criterion in the "Feedback" field.
6. Enter the rubric score for the essay.
7. Enter any additional feedback in the "Feedback" field.
