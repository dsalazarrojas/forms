# Professional Risk Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess professional risks and hazards in a workplace setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please read through each page carefully and answer the questions that apply to you. You can skip any questions that do not relate to your situation. Please make sure to answer all required fields, indicated by a "required" label next to the field name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Risk Hazards
* **Risk Hazards** (`Risk Hazards`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the types of hazards you are exposed to in your workplace. This could include physical hazards, such as machinery or tools, or chemical hazards, such as hazardous materials.

### Page 2: Training Practices
* **Training Practices** (`Training Practices`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the types of training you have received related to risk management. Please choose only one option that applies to you:
	+ Hazardous Materials
	+ Safety Procedures
	+ Safety Equipment

### Page 3: Risk Management Processes
* **Risk Management Processes** (`Risk Management Processes`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the risk management processes you follow in your workplace. Please choose all that apply:
	+ Risk Assessment
	+ Hazard Prevention
	+ Incident Investigation

## Tips
* Please answer all required fields.
* Be honest and provide accurate information.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
