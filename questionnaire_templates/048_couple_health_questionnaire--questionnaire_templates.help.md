# Couple Health Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Couple Health Questionnaire is designed to help couples assess their relationship dynamics, health status, and overall satisfaction. By completing this form, you and your partner will gain insights into your relationship's strengths and weaknesses, which can be used to identify areas for improvement and support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please choose the correct answer that best reflects your feelings and experiences in the relationship.
2. For select multiple questions, choose all applicable options.
3. You do not need to answer all questions, but the more information you provide, the more accurate the results will be.
4. Please answer the questions honestly and from your own perspective.
