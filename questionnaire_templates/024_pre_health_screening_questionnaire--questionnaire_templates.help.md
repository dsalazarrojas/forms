# Pre Health Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Health Screening Questionnaire is designed to collect essential health information from patients for pre-screening purposes. It's a comprehensive tool to gather data on patients' medical history, health status, and other critical health parameters.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the patient's basic information on Page 1, including their name, contact details, and emergency contact information.
- Move on to Page 2, where you'll enter the patient's health screening data, including any medical conditions, allergies, and vaccination status.
- On Page 3, enter any allergies or medical certificates the patient has received.
- Page 4 is for the patient's medication list and medical history.
- On Page 5, enter the patient's vaccination status and medical certificates.
- Finally, review and complete the emergency contact information on Page 6.
- Sign and date the form to confirm completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information (Page 1)**:
  • **Name** (`patient_info`): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on official documents.
  • **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Enter the patient's contact details, including phone and email.
  • **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): Enter the name and contact details of the patient's emergency contact.

  • **Allergies** (`allergies`): List any allergies or sensitivities the patient has.
  • **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`): Enter any current or chronic medical conditions the patient has.
  • **Medication List** (`medication_list`): List the patient's current medications.
  • **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Enter any past medical events or history.
  • **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`): Confirm the patient's current vaccination status.
  • **Medical Certificates** (`medical_certificates`): Enter any medical certificates or records the patient has received.
  • **Signature** (`signature`): Sign and date the form to confirm completion.
  • **Date** (`date`): Enter the current date.
  • **Time** (`time`): Enter the current time.
  • **Email** (`email`): Enter the patient's email address.
  • **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the patient's contact phone number.

- **Tips**: Please fill out each section accurately and thoroughly, and ensure you have the necessary permissions and clearances for data collection and use, as applicable under local, regional, or national regulations. If you're unsure about any information or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to your healthcare provider or relevant authorities.
