# Military Regulations Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Military Regulations Assessment form is a self-reporting tool for service members to assess their adherence to military regulations and procedures. It helps identify areas where service members may need additional training or review to ensure they are meeting military standards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and honestly to the best of your ability.
2. Ensure that each field is filled out as expected.
3. Submit the completed form according to your unit's procedures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Member** (Required, Text): Enter your service member information, including your name and ID number.
* **Policy Procedure** (Required, Text): Describe the specific policy or procedure you are reporting on.
* **Military Regulations** (Required, Text): Specify which military regulations you are referring to.
* **Procedures Followed** (Required, Text): List the procedures you have followed to comply with the regulation.
* **Procedures Not Followed** (Required, Text): Describe any procedures not followed that may be relevant to this report.
* **Training Review** (Required, Text): Provide any relevant training or review received.
* **Tracking Form Submission** (Required, Text): Provide your tracking number or any other relevant submission details.
* **Submit** (Select One, Yes/No): Confirm that your submission is complete and accurate.
* **Submitters** (Select Multiple, Yes/No): Confirm if you are submitting on behalf of someone else.
* **Submit Date** (Date): Enter the date of your submission.
* **Submit Time** (Time): Enter the time of your submission.
* **Note 1-12** (Note): Provide any additional comments or information as needed.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Note 2-12** (Note, Optional): Additional comments or information.
* **Submitter's Name** (Note): Provide the name of the submitter, if different from the service member.

Note: The submitter's name field is only required if the service member is submitting on behalf of another person. 

Please ensure that each field is completed accurately and thoroughly to ensure a comprehensive assessment of military regulations compliance.
