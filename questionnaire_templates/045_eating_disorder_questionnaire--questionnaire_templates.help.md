Eating Disorder Questionnaire Help Guide
======================================

# Purpose
The Eating Disorder Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information about eating habits, emotional stressors, and eating patterns to help assess the presence of an eating disorder. This form is intended to be completed by individuals who are experiencing or suspecting they may be experiencing an eating disorder.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the form instructions and questions carefully.
2.  Answer each question honestly to the best of your ability.
3.  Be as detailed as possible in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What is an eating disorder**:
    *   Label: What is an eating disorder
    *   Type: text
    *   Description: This is the introduction section of the form where you will describe what you understand about eating disorders. Please provide a brief summary of your knowledge or concerns about eating disorders.
*   **Demographic Information**:
    *   Label: demographic_info
    *   Type: number
    *   Description: Please enter your age or a number corresponding to your age group (e.g. 18-24).
*   **Do you eat irregularly?**:
    *   Label: eating_habits
    *   Type: select_one
    *   Description: Please select 'Yes' if you have experienced irregular eating patterns, including eating at irregular times or consuming food for emotional reasons.
*   **How many hours per week do you spend thinking about food or eating?**:
    *   Label: emotional_stressors
    *   Type: time
    *   Description: Please enter the number of hours per week you spend thinking about food or eating.
*   **Do you have any eating patterns that make you feel guilty or ashamed?**:
    *   Label: eating_patterns
    *   Type: select_multiple
    *   Description: Please select 'Yes' if you experience any eating patterns that make you feel guilty or ashamed, such as binge eating, purging, or excessive exercise.
*   **When did your eating disorder symptoms start?**:
    *   Label: eating_disorder_history
    *   Type: date
    *   Description: Please enter the date when your eating disorder symptoms started.

**Tips**

*   Be honest and detailed in your responses.
*   If you are unsure about any question, please explain your reasoning.
*   If you need help or support, please contact a healthcare professional.
