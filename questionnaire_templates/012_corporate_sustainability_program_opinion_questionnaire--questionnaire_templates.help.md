# Corporate Sustainability Program Opinion Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather opinions and feedback from employees on the company's corporate sustainability program. The purpose of this form is to collect information on the program's adoption, effectiveness, and future development. Your input will help us understand the current state of our sustainability efforts and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the questions carefully and answer them to the best of your ability.
2. Select all that apply for each question, if applicable.
3. Provide thoughtful and detailed feedback in the "Feedback On Initiatives" section.
4. Ensure that you have answered all required questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Adoption** (`program_adoption`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ This question asks about the adoption of our corporate sustainability program within the company. Select all that apply from the options below:
	+ Yes: This indicates that the program is being effectively implemented and utilized.
	+ No: This indicates that the program is not being implemented or utilized.
* **Sustainability Goals** (`sustainability_goals`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ This question asks about the alignment of our sustainability goals with the company's overall mission and vision. Select one of the options below:
	+ Yes: This indicates that our sustainability goals are aligned with the company's overall mission and vision.
	+ No: This indicates that our sustainability goals are not aligned with the company's overall mission and vision.
* **Feedback On Initiatives** (`feedback_on_initiatives`, `note`, required: false)
	+ This is an open-ended question for you to provide feedback on our current sustainability initiatives. Please be as detailed as possible, and let us know what we are doing well and what we could improve on.
* **Future Efforts** (`future_efforts`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ This question asks about future efforts to improve and develop our corporate sustainability program. Select all that apply from the options below:
	+ Yes: This indicates that you think we should continue or expand on certain initiatives.
	+ No: This indicates that you do not think we should continue or expand on certain initiatives.

## Tips

* Answer all questions thoroughly and honestly, as your feedback will help us improve our sustainability efforts.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas, please include them in the "Feedback On Initiatives" section.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
