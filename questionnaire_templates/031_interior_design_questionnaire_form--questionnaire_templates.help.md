# interior_design_questionnaire_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for interior design projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name and last name in the **first_name** and **last_name** fields, respectively.
2. Provide a valid email address in the **email** field.
3. Enter your phone number in the **phone_number** field.
4. Select your preferred color scheme from the **color_scheme** dropdown menu.
5. For the **preferred_room_size** field, choose the desired size of the room.
6. Choose your preferred style for the project from the **preferred_style** dropdown menu.
7. Enter your budget for the project in the **design_budget** field.
8. Select your preferred color palette(s) from the **preferred_color_palette**, **preferred_color_palette2**, and **preferred_color_palette3** fields.
9. Select any additional comments or notes about the project in the **additional_comments** field.
10. Enter the start and end dates for the project in the **project_start_date** and **project_end_date** fields, respectively.
11. Enter the time frame for the project in the **time_frame** field.
12. If applicable, enter additional time frames for the project in the **preferred_time_frame_1**, **preferred_time_frame_2**, and **preferred_time_frame_3** fields.
13. Enter any additional notes about the project in the **additional_notes** field.
14. Select any features that you would like to include in the room from the **preferred_room_features**, **preferred_room_features2**, and **preferred_room_features3** fields.
