# Covid Questionnaire For Doctors Office - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from patients about their Covid-19 symptoms and contact information for the purpose of providing medical care and guidance. Please note that the form is not intended for patients with mild or asymptomatic cases. It is recommended to call your doctor's office to confirm their specific requirements and guidelines before filling out this form.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, follow the steps below.
2. Fill out the form on a device with a stable internet connection.
3. Enter your details accurately and thoroughly.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your doctor's office directly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your name, please.
  - Ensure that the name is accurate and complete.
  - Please use the format "First Name Last Name".

- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information such as email, phone number or physical address.
  - You may enter any contact method you are comfortable with (phone number, email, etc.).
  - Ensure that your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.

- **Date Of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, optional): Enter the date of your visit to the doctor's office.
  - Format: YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 2022-03-01).
  - If you don't know the exact date, please enter the best estimate.

- **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, select_multiple, optional): Select all of the symptoms you are experiencing.
  - Please check all that apply.
  - Select from the available options: Fever, Coughing, Sore Throat.

- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the person you would like to be contacted.
  - Enter the name and contact information of a person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
  - Ensure that the contact information is accurate and up-to-date.

- **Doctor Notes** (`doctor_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes for the doctor.
  - This field is optional, but may be helpful for the doctor to know more about your condition.
  - Please keep the comments brief and relevant.

- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact.
  - Please enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
  - Ensure that the phone number is accurate.
