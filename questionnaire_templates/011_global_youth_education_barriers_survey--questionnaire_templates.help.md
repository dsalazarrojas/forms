# Global Youth Education Barriers Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Youth Education Barriers Survey is a tool designed to collect information on the barriers to education experienced by young individuals. This information will help in identifying areas where support and resources can be directed to address these barriers, ultimately improving access to education for young individuals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
2. Answer each question according to your personal experiences and circumstances.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the relevant authorities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is a note section where you can provide any additional information or comments that you think might be relevant to your responses.
* **What's Your Age**: This question asks for your age to help us understand your demographic background.
* **What Country Do You Live In?**: Please type in the name of the country you currently reside in.
* **What Is Your Gender?**: Please select your gender from the options provided.
* **What Is Your Education Status?**: This question asks about your current education status to help us understand your educational background.
* **Which Language Do You Speak?**: Please select all the languages you are proficient in.
* **What Are Some Of Your Main Barriers To Education?**: This question asks you to describe the main barriers you face in accessing education.
* **What Other Barriers Do You Experience?**: Please describe any other barriers you may be experiencing.
* **What Type of Support Do You Need?**: Please select all the types of support you require to overcome your barriers.
* **Where Do You Expect to Get the Support?**: Please select all the sources where you expect to get the support you need.
* **Other Types of Support Do You Need**: If there are any other types of support you need, please describe them here.
* **Do You Have Any Other Comments?**: If you have any other comments or suggestions, please include them here.
