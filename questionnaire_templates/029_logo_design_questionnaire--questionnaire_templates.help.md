# Logo Design Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a customer's logo design preferences and details from the customer or a person responsible for making decisions about a company's branding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form and ensure you understand the questions and required information.
2. Fill in your name and email address for contact purposes.
3. Provide details about yourself and your company, including the company name and industry.
4. Specify your logo design preferences, including color scheme, style, and any specific requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`): This is where you will enter your name and email address for contact purposes.
* **Company Details** (`company_details`): Here, you will provide details about your company, including the company name and industry.
* **Brand Details** (`brand_details`): This field is for you to describe your brand, including any specific characteristics or attributes.
* **Style Preferences** (`style_preferences`): In this section, you will specify your logo design preferences, including color scheme, style, and any specific requirements.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when answering the questions to ensure accurate information.
* If you have any specific requirements or constraints, please mention them in the relevant fields.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
