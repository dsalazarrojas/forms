# Personal Growth Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Growth Intake Form is a questionnaire designed to help coaches and professionals understand a client's personal growth areas of focus, goals, challenges, and readiness for coaching. This form serves as a starting point for the coaching process, allowing coaches to tailor their approach to each client's unique needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page of the form carefully.
2. For each page, provide as much detail as possible about the corresponding section.
3. If a field has a hint, use it as a guideline to understand the field's purpose.
4. If you need help with a specific field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Growth Intake Form Questions** (Page 1):
	+ A text field where you can briefly describe the client's current personal growth areas of focus.
* **Area of Focus** (Page 2):
	+ Select one or more areas where the client would like to focus on for personal growth, such as Goal Setting, Life Purpose, Mindfulness, and others.
	+ Please choose the areas that best match the client's current interests and goals.
* **Client Goals** (Page 3):
	+ Describe the client's current goals, whether short-term or long-term, specific or general.
	+ This can include personal, professional, or relational goals.
* **Challenges** (Page 4):
	+ Describe the client's current challenges or obstacles that are holding them back from achieving their goals.
* **Challenges Detail** (Page 5):
	+ Provide more detail about the challenges the client faces, such as specific situations, emotions, or experiences that trigger them.
* **Personal Growth Readiness** (Page 6):
	+ Rate the client's readiness for personal growth, using the options 1 - Ready to make changes, 2 - Some challenges remain, or 3 - Not very ready.
	+ This will help the coach understand the client's level of motivation and potential resistance to change.
* **Coaching Readiness** (Page 7):
	+ Rate the client's coaching readiness, using the options 1 - Fully, 2 - Somewhat, or 3 - Not very.
	+ This will help the coach understand the client's level of engagement and openness to coaching.
* **Coaching Readiness Detail** (Page 8):
	+ Describe the client's current level of engagement with the coaching process, such as their willingness to explore new ideas, ask questions, or take action.
* **Client Notes** (Page 9):
	+ Add any additional comments or notes about the client that the coach might find useful.
* **Coach Notes** (Page 10):
	+ Add any personal notes or observations about the client that the coach might find helpful in the coaching process.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when providing information about the client.
- If you're unsure about a particular field, refer to your coach or the field-by-field explanation.
- The more detail you provide, the better the coach will be able to tailor their approach to the client's needs.
