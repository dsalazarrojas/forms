# Pet Owner Preventive Care Habits Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to collect information about your pet's health habits and history. This information will help us provide you with personalized recommendations to ensure your pet stays healthy and happy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Please select the type of pet you own from the list.
2. Answer the questions about your pet's vaccination history and schedules.
3. Share your pet's parasite prevention habits and history.
4. Provide information about your pet's medical history and any regular veterinary visits.
5. Answer the questions about your pet's parasite frequency and control habits.
6. Select the type of pet you have (dog, cat, or other).
7. Provide your pet's age and weight.
8. Enter your pet's breed and any relevant medical notes.
9. Enter your contact information and email.
10. Finally, provide any additional notes about your pet's history and care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: Please read this section carefully to understand the purpose and instructions of the form.
* **Pet info**: Select the type of pet you own (Dog, Cat, or Both). This will help us understand the specific needs of your pet.
* **Vaccination**: Choose how often you vaccinate your pet (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Never). This will help us understand your pet's vaccination history.
* **Parasite prevention**: Answer the question about your pet's parasite prevention habits. You can select from the options below or provide additional information.
* **Checkup**: Enter the date of your pet's last checkup. This is optional, but it will help us track your pet's medical history.
* **Checkup info**: Provide any additional information about your pet's checkup, such as the results or any notable findings.
* **Parasite frequency**: Select how frequently you have found parasites on or in your pet (Rarely, Occasionally, Frequently, or Always). This will help us understand the parasite control habits of your pet.
* **Veterinary visits**: Select how often you visit the vet with your pet (Every 6 months, Every 12 months, or More than 6 months). This will help us understand your pet's medical history and needs.
* **Parasite control**: Answer the question about your pet's parasite control habits. You can select from the options below or provide additional information.
* **Pet type**: Select the type of pet you have (Dog, Cat, or Other). This will help us understand the specific needs of your pet.
* **Age**: Enter your pet's age. This is an important factor in determining their medical needs.
* **Weight**: Enter your pet's weight. This will help us understand their size and potential health needs.
* **Breed**: Enter your pet's breed. This will help us understand their genetic and health needs.
* **Contact info**: Enter your contact information, including your name and phone number. This will help us stay in touch with you about your pet's care.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This will help us contact you about your pet's care and any important updates.
* **Address**: Enter your address. This will help us track your veterinary visits and send reminders.
* **Veterinarian**: Enter the name of your veterinarian. This will help us track your pet's medical history.
* **Medical history**: Enter any relevant medical history about your pet. This will help us understand their health needs.
* **Vaccination history**: Enter any relevant vaccination history about your pet. This will help us understand their vaccination needs.
* **Vaccination schedules**: Enter any relevant vaccination schedules about your pet. This will help us track their vaccination needs.
* **Parasite history**: Enter any relevant parasite history about your pet. This will help us understand their parasite control needs.
* **Preventative services**: Enter any preventative services your pet receives, such as heartworm medication or flea and tick control.
* **Medication**: Enter any medication your pet is currently taking. This will help us understand their health needs.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your pet's health and care. This will help us provide personalized recommendations.
