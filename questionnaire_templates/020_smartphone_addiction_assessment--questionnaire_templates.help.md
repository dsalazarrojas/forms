# Smartphone Addiction Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smartphone Addiction Assessment is a tool to evaluate the impact of smartphone usage on various aspects of your life.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer according to your true experiences and feelings.
2. Choose the response that best matches your situation for each question.
3. For select_one, select_one_multiple, and select_multiple questions, choose one or multiple options that apply to you.
4. For numerical questions (number and time), enter the correct value.
