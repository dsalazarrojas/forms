# City Information Collection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a city, including its basic details like name, state/province/region, and country, as well as its economic and demographic characteristics, such as population size, area, and median household income. The form also gathers information about the city's infrastructure, major landmarks, climate, and the source of the data. This form is intended to provide a comprehensive overview of a city's profile.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the city in the "Name of the City" field.
2. Select the state, province, or region of the city from the "State or Province or Region" field.
3. Enter the country in the "Country" field.
4. (Optional) Enter the estimated total population of the city.
5. (Optional) Enter the total area of the city in square kilometers.
6. Select the primary economic sectors that apply to the city.
7. (Optional) Enter the median household income of the city.
8. (Optional) Select the available public transportation types in the city.
9. (Optional) List up to three major landmarks or attractions in the city.
10. Select the climate type of the city.
11. Enter the source of the information used to fill out this form.
12. (Optional) Add any additional research notes about the city.
13. Set the collection date of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name of the City** (`city_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the city.
* **State or Province or Region** (`state_province_region`, text, required): Select the state, province, or region where the city is located.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required): Enter the country where the city is located.
* **Total Population** (`population_size`, number, optional): Enter the estimated total population of the city.
* **Total Area (sq km)** (`city_area_km`, number, optional): Enter the total area of the city in square kilometers.
* **Primary Economic Sectors** (`primary_economic_sectors`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the options (Technology, Manufacturing, Tourism, Agriculture, Finance, Healthcare).
* **Median Household Income** (`median_household_income`, number, optional): Enter the median household income of the city.
* **Available Public Transportation** (`public_transport_types`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the options (Subway/Metro, Bus, Light Rail/Tram, Ferry, Shared Bicycles).
* **Major Landmarks or Attractions** (`major_landmarks`, text, optional): List up to three major landmarks or attractions in the city.
* **Climate Type** (`climate_type`, select_one, optional): Select the climate type from the options (Tropical, Dry/Arid, Temperate, Continental, Polar).
* **Source of this Information** (`data_source`, text, required): Enter the source of the information used to fill out this form (e.g., Census 2020, City Website).
* **Additional Researcher Notes** (`submission_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional research notes about the city.
* **Date of Collection** (`collection_date`, date, required): Set the date when this form was collected.
