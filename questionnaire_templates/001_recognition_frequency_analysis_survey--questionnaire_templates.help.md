# Recognition Frequency Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recognition Frequency Analysis Survey is used to collect data on the frequency and characteristics of recognition received or given within a team or organization. This form helps to understand individual experiences and provide insights into recognition patterns.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply answer the questions based on your personal experiences and preferences. You can select one or multiple options for each question, depending on your answers.

1. **Employee Name**: Please enter your name to help identify your responses.
2. **Team**: Select your team affiliation.
3. **Give Recognition**: How often do you give recognition to others?
4. **Receive Recognition**: How often do you receive recognition?
5. **Barriers**: (Optional) If you have any challenges or obstacles to recognition, please describe them.
6. **Suggestions**: (Optional) If you have any suggestions for recognition, please describe them.
7. **Manager Support**: How supportive is your manager when it comes to recognition?
8. **Peer Support**: How supportive are your peers when it comes to recognition?
9. **Department Support**: How supportive is your department when it comes to recognition?
10. **Recognition Frequency**: Please enter the total number of times you've received recognition.
11. **Recognition Type**: What type of recognition do you prefer?
12. **Recognition Value**: How valuable do you find recognition?
13. **Manager Recognition**: How often do you receive recognition from your manager?
14. **Peer Recognition**: How often do you receive recognition from your peers?
15. **Department Recognition**: How often do you receive recognition from your department?
16. **Feedback**: How often do you receive feedback on your work?
17. **Barriers Description**: (Optional) If you have any challenges or obstacles to recognition, please describe them.
18. **Suggestions Description**: (Optional) If you have any suggestions for recognition, please describe them.
19. **Manager Suggestions**: How often do you receive suggestions from your manager?
20. **Peer Suggestions**: How often do you receive suggestions from your peers?
21. **Department Suggestions**: How often do you receive suggestions from your department?
22. **Total Recognition Score**: Please enter the total recognition score.
23. **Average Recognition Score**: Please enter the average recognition score.
24. **Standard Deviation Recognition Score**: Please enter the standard deviation of recognition score.
25. **Total Frequency Score**: Please enter the total frequency score.
