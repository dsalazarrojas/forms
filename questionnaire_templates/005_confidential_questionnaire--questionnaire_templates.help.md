# Confidential Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Confidential Questionnaire is designed to gather sensitive information for professional assessment. Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and understand the purpose of this questionnaire before completing it. If you have already completed this questionnaire, there is no need to repeat the process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and answer all required fields.
2. Select the correct answers for each field, and ensure that you have the necessary permissions to complete the form.
3. Enter your Participant ID Code, if provided to you.
4. Complete the date completed field accurately.
5. Choose your current age group and highest level of education completed.
6. Rate your current stress level and select all applicable lifestyle habits.
7. Choose your primary motivation for this assessment.
8. Provide more detail on your motivation, if applicable.
9. Read and understand the confidentiality agreement and data usage consent, and select the correct options.
10. Sign the form electronically with your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Purpose of Questionnaire --** (Section 1): This is an introductory section to explain the purpose of the questionnaire.
* **Participant ID Code** (Section 2): If you have been provided with a Participant ID Code, enter it here. This code will be used to identify you in the assessment process.
* **Date Completed** (Section 3): Enter the date you complete this questionnaire accurately.
* **Current Age Group** (Section 4): Select your current age group from the options provided (Under 18, 18-25, 26-35, 36-50, 51-65, Over 65).
* **Highest Level of Education Completed** (Section 5): Select your highest level of education completed from the options provided (High School, Associate Degree, Bachelor Degree, Master Degree, Doctorate, Other).
* **-- Behavioral Assessment --** (Section 6): This is an introductory section to explain the behavioral assessment process. Please answer honestly.
* **On a scale of 1 to 10, rate your current stress level** (Section 7): Rate your current stress level from 1 (low) to 10 (high).
* **Which of the following lifestyle habits apply to you?** (Section 8): Select all the lifestyle habits that apply to you. You can select multiple options.
* **What is your primary motivation for this assessment?** (Section 9): Choose your primary motivation from the options provided (Personal Growth, Professional Requirement, Health Improvement, Curiosity, Other).
* **Please provide more detail on your motivation** (Section 10): If you have more detail to provide on your motivation, enter it here.
* **-- Confidentiality Agreement --** (Section 11): This is an introductory section to explain the confidentiality agreement. Please read and understand before proceeding.
* **I consent to my data being used for anonymous statistical analysis** (Section 12): Select "True" if you consent to your data being used for anonymous statistical analysis.
* **I understand that my information will not be shared with third parties** (Section 13): Select "I understand" if you understand that your information will not be shared with third parties.
* **Electronic Signature (Type Full Name)** (Section 14): Enter your full name as an electronic signature.
