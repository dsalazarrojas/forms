# Exercise Readiness Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your baseline physical condition, including your fitness level, physical status, and exercise frequency. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your current fitness level from the options provided.
2. Choose your current physical status from the available options.
3. If applicable, select your exercise frequency in days per week.
4. Enter the duration of your exercise sessions in minutes.
5. Provide your age, height, weight, and body fat percentage.
6. Enter your resting heart rate and max heart rate (if applicable).
7. Select your stress level, motivation level, preferred exercise type, and exercise frequency (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **what_is_your_current_fitness_level** (Select One, Required): This field asks about your current level of physical fitness. Choose the option that best describes your current fitness level.
* **what_is_your_current_physical_status** (Select Multiple, Required): This field asks about your current physical status, including any injuries or illnesses that may impact your exercise routine. Choose all options that apply.
* **how_many_days_do_you_train_per_week** (Number, Optional): If you regularly exercise, select the number of days you engage in physical activities per week.
* **how_many_minutes_do_you_spend_exercising_per_session** (Number, Optional): Enter the duration of your exercise sessions in minutes.
* **Age** (Number, Required): Provide your current age to help us understand your physical development and maturity.
* **Height** (Number, Required): Enter your height in your preferred unit of measurement.
* **Weight** (Number, Required): Provide your current weight in your preferred unit of measurement.
* **Body Fat Percentage** (Number, Required): Enter your body fat percentage to help us better understand your body composition.
* **Resting Bp** (Number, Optional): Enter your resting blood pressure if you have access to that information.
* **Stress Level** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose your stress level, as it may impact your exercise routine.
* **Motivation Level** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select your motivation level, as it affects your exercise adherence and consistency.
* **which_type_of_exercise_do_you_prefer** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the types of exercises you prefer, such as cardiovascular, resistance, or high-intensity interval training.
* **how_many_days_per_week** (Select One, Optional): Select the number of days you engage in physical activities per week, based on your preferred exercise frequency.
