# Study Time Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Study Time Preferences Survey is a simple questionnaire designed to collect data about an individual's study habits and preferences. Your responses will help us understand how people study and what influences their productivity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer the questions as accurately as possible.
2. Fill in the required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
3. Select your preferred options from the dropdown menus.
4. Submit your survey when you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Study Time Preferences Survey Page 1**: This is the introduction page of the survey. Please take a moment to read through the instructions and questions before proceeding.
* **Study Routine Frequency**: How often do you typically study? Please select a number to represent how many days per week you study.
* **Study Routine Location**: Where do you usually study? Please select one of the options below:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Study Routine Duration**: How long do you usually study each time? Please select a number of minutes or hours.
* **Productivity Patterns**: How do you tend to approach your study sessions? Please select all that apply:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Time of Day**: At what time of day do you usually study? Please enter a 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00, 14:30).
* **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button to send your survey.
