# Principles Of Management Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to help managers evaluate their leadership style, skills, and approach to different management aspects. It is a tool for self-assessment and personal development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select one option for each question from the provided options.
2. Make sure to answer all questions, as most are marked as optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Manager Leadership Style** (`manager_leadership_style`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the three leadership styles:
	+ **autocratic**: You have a top-down leadership approach.
	+ **participative**: You involve your team members in decision-making processes.
	+ **laissez-faire**: You give your team a lot of freedom to make decisions.
* **Leadership Skills** (`leadership_skills`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the skills that you possess:
	+ **effective communication**: You can clearly and effectively communicate with your team.
	+ **problem solving**: You are skilled at analyzing and solving problems.
	+ **adaptability**: You can quickly adapt to new situations.
* **Conflict Management** (`conflict_management`, select_one, optional): Choose one approach to conflict:
	+ **avoid conflict**: You tend to avoid conflicts altogether.
	+ **face conflict**: You directly address conflicts as they arise.
	+ **seek mediation**: You bring in a third party to resolve conflicts.
* **Communication Strategy** (`communication_strategy`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred communication approach:
	+ **verbal communication**: You prefer to communicate verbally.
	+ **nonverbal communication**: You use body language and gestures to communicate.
	+ **listening skills**: You focus on listening to understand others better.
* **Time Management** (`time_management`, number, optional): You can select a number between 1-10 to indicate how well you manage your time.
* **Goal Setting** (`goal_setting`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the three goal-setting approaches:
	+ **smart goals**: You set specific, measurable, and achievable goals.
	+ **goal-oriented**: You set goals based on your values and priorities.
	+ **goal set**: You set goals for specific objectives.
* **Decision Making** (`decision_making`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the decision-making approaches that apply to you:
	+ **data-driven**: You rely on data to make decisions.
	+ **intuitive**: You rely on your instincts.
	+ **consensus**: You involve your team in decision-making processes.
* **Problem Solving** (`problem_solving`, select_one, optional): Choose one problem-solving approach:
	+ **analyze**: You thoroughly analyze problems before finding solutions.
	+ **deduce**: You quickly deduce solutions.
	+ **improve**: You continuously improve processes.
* **Adaptability** (`adaptability`, select_one, optional): Choose one adaptability approach:
	+ **quickly**: You can adapt quickly to new situations.
	+ **slowly**: You take time to adapt.
	+ **rarely**: You seldom adapt.
* **Situational Awareness** (`situational_awareness`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the approaches that apply to you:
	+ **high**: You have a high situational awareness.
	+ **moderate**: You have a moderate situational awareness.
	+ **low**: You have a low situational awareness.
* **Emotional Intelligence** (`emotional_intelligence`, select_one, optional): Choose one emotional intelligence approach:
	+ **self-awareness**: You are aware of your own emotions.
	+ **self-regulation**: You can regulate your own emotions.
	+ **motivation**: You are motivated to improve your relationships.
* **Situational Priorities** (`situational_priorities`, select_one, optional): Choose one situational priority:
	+ **high**: You prioritize tasks based on urgency and importance.
	+ **moderate**: You prioritize tasks based on a balance of urgency and importance.
	+ **low**: You prioritize tasks randomly.
* **Situational Analysis** (`situational_analysis`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the situational analysis approaches that apply to you:
	+ **in-depth**: You analyze situations thoroughly.
	+ **wide**: You analyze a wide range of situations.
	+ **shallow**: You analyze situations superficially.
* **Conflict Resolution** (`conflict_resolution`, select_one, optional): Choose one conflict resolution approach:
	+ **conflict avoidance**: You try to avoid conflicts altogether.
	+ **conflict resolution**: You directly address conflicts.
	+ **seek help**: You seek external help to resolve conflicts.
* **Situational Management** (`situational_management`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the situational management approaches that apply to you:
	+ **high**: You can manage situations effectively.
	+ **moderate**: You can manage situations fairly well.
	+ **low**: You struggle with situational management.

## Tips
1. Make sure to answer honestly and accurately.
2. Review your answers and consider potential biases or inconsistencies.
3. Use this form as a self-assessment tool for personal growth and development.
