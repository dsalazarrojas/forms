# oppositional_defiant_disorder_odd_assessment_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Oppositional Defiant Disorder (ODD) Assessment Questionnaire is a tool used to evaluate an individual's behavior and symptoms associated with Oppositional Defiant Disorder. This form is designed to help healthcare professionals assess the severity of ODD symptoms and develop a treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. For select_multiple fields (e.g., "odd_symptoms", "behavior_patterns", "emotional_response", "treatment_plan", "follow_up"), select all applicable options that apply to the individual being assessed.
3. For select_one fields (e.g., "social_interactions"), select only one option that best describes the individual's behavior.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the Oppositonal Defiant Disorder (ODD) / What is the Oppositonal Defiant Disorder (ODD)** (`title`, text, required/true): This field is an opportunity to briefly describe Oppositional Defiant Disorder and its relevance to the assessment.
* **odd_symptoms** (`oodd_symptoms`, select_multiple, false): Select all applicable symptoms of Oppositional Defiant Disorder that apply to the individual being assessed. These symptoms can help identify the severity and specific characteristics of the disorder.
* **Behavior Patterns** (`behavior_patterns`, select_multiple, false): Select all applicable behavior patterns that are associated with Oppositional Defiant Disorder. These patterns can help healthcare professionals understand the individual's behavior and develop a treatment plan.
* **Social Interactions** (`social_interactions`, select_one, true): Select one option that best describes the individual's social interactions. This field helps healthcare professionals understand how the individual interacts with others and how this may be related to their Oppositional Defiant Disorder symptoms.
* **Emotional Response** (`emotional_response`, select_multiple, true): Select all applicable emotional responses that are associated with Oppositional Defiant Disorder. These responses can help healthcare professionals understand how the individual responds emotionally to situations and how this may be related to their symptoms.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, text, false): This field is not required, but providing information about the individual's family history can be helpful in understanding their Oppositional Defiant Disorder.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, select_multiple, true): Select all applicable treatment plans that may be beneficial for the individual with Oppositional Defiant Disorder. This field helps healthcare professionals develop a comprehensive treatment plan.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, select_multiple, false): Select all applicable follow-up plans that may be beneficial for the individual with Oppositional Defiant Disorder. This field helps healthcare professionals schedule follow-up appointments and ensure the individual receives ongoing care.
* **Review** (`review`, text, false): This field is for any additional comments or notes about the individual's Oppositional Defiant Disorder.
