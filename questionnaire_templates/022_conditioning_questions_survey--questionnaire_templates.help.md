# Conditioning Questions Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Conditioning Questions Survey is a questionnaire designed to assess your understanding and familiarity with conditioning concepts, including classical and operant conditioning. It aims to gauge your knowledge and experience in applying conditioning principles in real-life situations, as well as your willingness to learn more about the topic.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Survey Completion" button at the end of the form to confirm that you have completed the survey.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
3. For select_one fields, choose the answer that best fits your understanding or experience.
4. For note fields, provide a brief explanation or answer in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent ID**: A unique identifier for you as a respondent, optional but recommended for tracking purposes.
* **Survey Date**: The date you completed the survey.
* **Age Group**: Select the age range that best applies to you.
	+ Under 18: Under 18 years old
	+ 18 to 24: 18 to 24 years old
	+ 25 to 34: 25 to 34 years old
	+ 35 to 44: 35 to 44 years old
	+ 45 to 54: 45 to 54 years old
	+ 55 to 64: 55 to 64 years old
	+ 65 or older: 65 years old or older
* **Education Level**: The highest education level you have completed.
	+ High School or Less: High school or less than high school education
	+ Some College: Some college education
	+ Bachelor Degree: Bachelor's degree
	+ Graduate Degree: Graduate degree
* **Familiarity with Conditioning**: Rate your familiarity with the topic of conditioning.
	+ Very Familiar: You have a high level of knowledge and understanding
	+ Somewhat Familiar: You have some knowledge and understanding
	+ Neutral: You have little knowledge and understanding
	+ Somewhat Unfamiliar: You have limited knowledge and understanding
	+ Very Unfamiliar: You have little to no knowledge and understanding
* **Classical Conditioning**: Rate your understanding of classical conditioning.
	+ Excellent: You have a high level of understanding
	+ Good: You have a fair level of understanding
	+ Fair: You have a limited understanding
	+ Poor: You have little understanding
	+ No Understanding: You have no understanding
* **Operant Conditioning**: Rate your understanding of operant conditioning.
	+ Excellent: You have a high level of understanding
	+ Good: You have a fair level of understanding
	+ Fair: You have a limited understanding
	+ Poor: You have little understanding
	+ No Understanding: You have no understanding
* **Pavlov Experiment Knowledge**: Are you familiar with Pavlov's dogs experiment.
	+ Yes Very Familiar: You have a high level of knowledge and understanding
	+ Somewhat Familiar: You have some knowledge and understanding
	+ Heard of It: You have heard of it but not fully understood it
	+ No Not Familiar: You have little to no knowledge and understanding
* **Understanding of Reinforcement**: Do you understand positive and negative reinforcement.
	+ Yes Completely: You have a complete understanding
	+ Mostly: You have a good understanding
	+ Somewhat: You have some understanding
	+ Not Really: You have limited understanding
* **Application in Daily Life**: Have you observed conditioning in daily life.
	+ Frequently: You have observed conditioning in your daily life
	+ Occasionally: You have observed conditioning occasionally
	+ Rarely: You have observed conditioning rarely
	+ Never: You have never observed conditioning
* **Training Experience**: Have you used conditioning in training.
	+ Yes With Animals: You have used conditioning with animals
	+ Yes With People: You have used conditioning with people
	+ Yes With Both: You have used conditioning with both animals and people
	+ No Never: You have never used conditioning
* **Preferred Learning Style**: How do you learn best.
	+ Visual: You learn best through visual aids
	+ Auditory: You learn best through sound or audio
	+ Kinesthetic: You learn best through hands-on experience
	+ Reading and Writing: You learn best through reading and writing
* **Open Ended Questions**: Share your thoughts on the topic of conditioning.
* **Personal Experience**: Describe a conditioning experience.
* **Ethical Concerns**: Any ethical concerns you have about conditioning.
* **Interest in Further Learning**: Would you like to learn more about conditioning.
	+ Yes Very Interested: You have a high level of interest
	+ Somewhat Interested: You have some interest
	+ Neutral: You have little interest
	+ Not Interested: You have no interest
* **Recommend to Others**: Would you recommend this topic to others.
	+ Definitely Yes: You would definitely recommend it
	+ Probably Yes: You would probably recommend it
	+ Maybe: You are unsure
	+ Probably No: You would probably not recommend it
	+ Definitely No: You would definitely not recommend it
* **Additional Comments**: Any other thoughts you have about the topic.
* **Contact for Follow Up**: May we contact you for more research.
	+ Yes Contact Me: You are willing to be contacted
	+ No Do Not Contact: You are not willing to be contacted
* **Survey Completion**: Confirm that you have completed the survey.
	+ I Complete This Survey: You have completed the survey

Note that the form fields are required for the following sections:
- Survey Date
- Age Group
- Education Level
- Classical Conditioning
- Operant Conditioning
- Pavlov Experiment Knowledge
- Understanding of Reinforcement
- Application in Daily Life
- Pavlov Experiment Knowledge
- Survey Completion
