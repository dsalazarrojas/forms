# fiction_and_structure_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on the structure and story quality of submitted content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your name and submission details in the "Submission Details", "Story Information", and "Submission Type" sections.
2. Rate the structure and story quality of the content using the "Fiction Rating" and "Structure Rating" fields.
3. Provide any additional feedback or comments in the "Feedback" and "Feedback Text" fields.
4. If applicable, select the submission type (e.g. short story, novel, etc.) and enter the submission date and time in the "Submission Date", "Submission Time", and "Submission Timezone" fields.
5. Enter any relevant geographical information in the "Submission Location", "Submission Latitude", "Submission Longitude", "Submission Elevation", "Submission Accuracy", and "Submission Altitude" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What is your name?
* **What is your name?** (`1`, `text`, required): Please enter your name.
### 2. Submission Details
* **Submission Details** (`2`, `text`, required): Please enter a brief description of your submission.
### 3. Story Information
* **Story Information** (`3`, `text`, required): Please enter any relevant story information.
### 4. Structure
* **Structure** (`4`, `text`, required): Please enter your assessment of the structure of the content.
### 5. Fiction Rating
* **Fiction Rating** (`5`, `number`, required): Rate the fiction quality of the content using a number from 1-10.
### 6. Structure Rating
* **Structure Rating** (`6`, `number`, required): Rate the structure quality of the content using a number from 1-10.
### 7. Submission Type
* **Submission Type** (`7`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of submission (e.g. short story, novel, etc.).
### 8. Feedback
* **Feedback** (`8`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments.
### 9. Submission Date
* **Submission Date** (`9`, `date`, required): Enter the date of submission.
### 10. Review Date
* **Review Date** (`10`, `date`, required): Enter the date of review.
### 11. Submission Status
* **Submission Status** (`11`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the submission (e.g. in progress, completed, etc.).
### 12. Submission Notes
* **Submission Notes** (`12`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes about the submission.
### 13. Reviewer Name
* **Reviewer Name** (`13`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the reviewer.
### 14. Reviewer Email
* **Review Email** (`14`, `email`, required): Enter the email of the reviewer.
### 15. Structure Information
* **Structure Information** (`15`, `text`, required): Please enter any relevant structure information.
### 16. Feedback Text
* **Feedback Text** (`16`, `text`, required): Provide any additional feedback or comments.
### 17. Submission Time

### 18. Submission Timezone
* **Submission Timezone** (`18`, `select_one`, optional): Select the time zone of submission.
### 19. Submission Timezone Offset
* **Submission Timezone Offset** (`19`, `text`, optional): Enter any time zone offset.
### 20. Submission Location
* **Submission Location** (`20`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of submission.

* **Submission Latitude** (`21`, `number`, optional): Enter the latitude of submission location.
### 22. Submission Longitude

### 23. Submission Elevation

### 24. Submission Accuracy
* **Submission Accuracy** (`24`, `number`, optional): Enter the accuracy of submission location.
### 25. Submission Altitude

## Tips
