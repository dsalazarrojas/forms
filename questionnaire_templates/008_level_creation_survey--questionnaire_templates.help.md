# Level Creation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information and feedback from level creators in the game development process. It helps identify areas for improvement and provides valuable insights for future level designs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form carefully.
2. Fill in the required information, marked as "required" in the form.
3. If you have any comments or feedback, type them in the corresponding text field.
4. Ensure you are providing accurate and up-to-date information.
5. Submit the form when completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Level Creation** (`level_creation_survey`, `text`, required: false): This is the title of the survey.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, `text`, required: false): List the team members participating in the level design process.
* **Game Level Designer** (`game_level_designer`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose from High or Low to indicate the level of experience and skill of the game level designer.
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose from 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the team lead is involved in the level design process.
* **Team Lead Contact** (`team_lead_contact`, `text`, required: false): Provide the contact information of the team lead.
* **Game Designer Feedback** (`game_designer_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide any feedback from the game designer.
* **Game Developer Feedback** (`game_developer_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide any feedback from the game developer.
* **Game Publisher Feedback** (`game_publisher_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide any feedback from the game publisher.
* **Level Creation Process** (`game_level_creation_process`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose from Design, Development, Testing, and Review to indicate the level creation process.
* **Other Level Creation Process** (`game_level_creation_process_other`, `text`, required: false): If none of the above options apply, type in your own level creation process.
* **Level Creation Time** (`level_creation_time`, `number`, required: false): Provide the actual time it took to create the level in minutes.
* **Level Creation Satisfaction** (`level_creation_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose from Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied to indicate your level creation satisfaction.
* **Improvement Opportunities** (`improvement_opportunities`, `text`, required: false): Provide any improvement opportunities for future level designs.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required: false): Provide the date you submitted the level to the client.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, required: false): Provide the time you submitted the level to the client.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Provide your phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or notes.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required: false): Provide the tool used for level creation.
* **Other Team Members** (`team_members_other`, `text`, required: false): If there are other team members involved, type them in.
* **Other Team Lead** (`team_lead_other`, `text`, required: false): If there is another team lead involved, type them in.
* **Other Game Level Designer** (`game_level_designer_other`, `text`, required: false): If there is another game level designer involved, type them in.
* **Other Game Developer** (`game_level_designer_other`, `text`, required: false): If there is another game developer involved, type them in.
* **Other Game Publisher** (`game_publisher_other`, `text`, required: false): If there is another game publisher involved, type them in.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, required: false): Type in the name of the person submitting the form.

Please note that some fields are optional but it's highly recommended to fill them in for accurate and complete feedback.
