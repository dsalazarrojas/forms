# Strategist Or Sequencer Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an individual's strategic planning and problem-solving skills, including their approach to projects, conflict resolution, and adaptation to changing circumstances. The Strategist Or Sequencer Assessment is intended to provide insight into one's ability to think critically, prioritize tasks, and make informed decisions under pressure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question on the subsequent pages, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Please be honest and thorough in your responses.
3. You can skip questions that are not applicable to your experience or skills.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **what_does_planning_mean_to_you** (`what_does_planning_mean_to_you`, text, optional):
	+ A text field where you can describe what planning means to you. Please explain your definition of planning in as much detail as possible.
* **how_do_you_approach_a_new_project** (`how_do_you_approach_a_new_project`, text, optional):
	+ A text field where you can explain your approach to tackling a new project. Describe your strategy, steps, and any relevant experience you have had.
* **how_do_you_handle_conflict** (`how_do_you_handle_conflict`, text, optional):
	+ A text field where you can describe your approach to conflict resolution. Explain how you handle disagreements, disputes, or challenging situations.
* **what_skills_do_you_use_to_solve_problems** (`what_skills_do_you_use_to_solve_problems`, text, optional):
	+ A text field where you can list the skills you use to solve problems. Examples may include critical thinking, communication, or creative problem-solving.
* **how_do_you_handle_tight_deadlines** (`how_do_you_handle_tight_deadlines`, text, optional):
	+ A text field where you can explain how you manage your time and prioritize tasks when faced with tight deadlines.
* **what_is_your_approach_to_planning** (`what_is_your_approach_to_planning`, text, optional):
	+ A text field where you can describe your planning process. Explain how you set goals, prioritize tasks, and allocate resources.
* **how_do_you_handle_complex_problems** (`how_do_you_handle_complex_problems`, text, optional):
	+ A text field where you can explain your approach to tackling complex problems. Describe your strategy, steps, and any relevant experience you have had.
* **what_skills_do_you_use_to_solve_complex_problems** (`what_skills_do_you_use_to_solve_complex_problems`, text, optional):
	+ A text field where you can list the skills you use to solve complex problems. Examples may include analytical thinking, adaptability, or creativity.
* **how_do_you_approach_a_new_project** (`how_do_you_approach_a_new_project`, text, optional):
	+ A text field where you can explain your approach to tackling a new project. Describe your strategy, steps, and any relevant experience you have had.
* **what_is_your_planning_process** (`what_is_your_planning_process`, text, optional):
	+ A text field where you can describe your planning process. Explain how you set goals, prioritize tasks, and allocate resources.
* **how_do_you_handle_unforeseen_circumstances** (`how_do_you_handle_unforeseen_circumstances`, text, optional):
	+ A text field where you can explain how you handle unexpected situations or changes in plans.
