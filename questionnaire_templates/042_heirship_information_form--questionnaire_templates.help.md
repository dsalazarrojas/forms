# Heirship Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Heirship Information Form is a probate form used to collect sensitive and personal information about a deceased individual's estate, family members, and property. This form is essential for accurate and efficient recording of information for probate purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure that you have all the necessary information before starting the form.
2. Select the relevant family members and their relationship status.
3. Provide contact information for the estate administrators.
4. Enter property details and update information.
5. Enter update frequency and follow-up information.
6. Enter any additional notes or remarks.
7. Enter phone number and email contact information.
8. Enter address information.
9. Enter date of birth and time of death.
10. Enter probate status and last update date.
11. Enter next update date and update frequency.
12. Enter probate case number and form submission ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **family_members** (`family_members`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all family members relevant to the deceased individual.
* **heir_info** (`heir_info`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of heirship information to provide (Name, Address, or Contact Info).
* **heirship_details** (`heirship_details`, `text`, required): Please provide heirship details.
* **relationship_status** (`relationship_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the relationship status of the family members (Single, Married).
* **estate_administrators** (`estate_administrators`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all estate administrators.
* **property_details** (`property_details`, `text`, required): Please provide property details.
* **beneficiary_updates** (`beneficiary_updates`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all beneficiaries.
* **property_accounts** (`property_accounts`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all property accounts.
* **updates** (`updates`, `text`, required): Please provide updates.
* **follow_ups** (`follow_ups`, `text`, required): Please provide follow-up information.
* **notes** (`notes`, `note`, required): Please provide any additional notes or remarks.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of contact information to provide (Land Line, Mobile, Email).
* **contact_info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required): Please provide contact information.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required): Please provide email contact information.
* **address** (`address`, `text`, required): Please provide address information.
* **date_of_birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Please provide date of birth.
* **time_of_death** (`time_of_death`, `time`, required): Please provide time of death.
* **probate_status** (`probate_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the probate status (In Progress, Complete, Ongoing).
* **follow_up_frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select the follow-up frequency (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **last_update** (`last_update`, `date`, required): Please provide last update date.
* **next_update** (`next_update`, `date`, required): Please provide next update date.
* **update_frequency** (`update_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all follow-up frequencies.
* **probate_case_number** (`probate_case_number`, `number`, required): Please provide probate case number.
* **form_submission_id** (`form_submission_id`, `text`, required): Please provide form submission id.

**Tips:**

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
* Double-check the information before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all information is up-to-date and correct.
* Use relevant contact information to facilitate communication with the estate administrators and family members.
* Be precise with the probate status and follow-up information to ensure accurate tracking of the estate's status.
