# Ankle Disability Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's ankle disability to aid in clinical assessment and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer all fields truthfully and accurately.
- Ensure that you enter your contact information if you wish to be contacted for further questions.
- Rate your pain levels honestly, considering your current condition.
- Choose all that apply for mobility limitations.
- Select the best description for observed swelling or inflammation.
- Provide any additional notes or concerns you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient full name** (`patient_name`, required): Provide the name as it appears in medical records if applicable.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, optional): Enter the date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions.
* **Contact phone number** (`contact_phone`, optional): Include your country code if outside local area.
* **Pain level at rest** (`pain_level_rest`, required): Rate your pain level at rest on a scale of 0 to 10, where 0 means no pain and 10 means worst pain imaginable.
* **Pain level during activity** (`pain_level_activity`, required): Rate your pain level during activity on a scale of 0 to 10, where 0 means no pain and 10 means worst pain imaginable.
* **Mobility limitations** (`mobility_limitations`, required): Choose all that apply regarding mobility limitations you experience.
* **Observed swelling or inflammation** (`swelling_or_inflammation`, required): Select the description that best fits your current condition.
* **Current treatments or therapies** (`current_treatment`, optional): Provide details about any medications, braces, physical therapy, or recent procedures you're undergoing.
* **Additional notes or concerns** (`additional_notes`, optional): Enter any other information that may be useful for the clinical assessment.
