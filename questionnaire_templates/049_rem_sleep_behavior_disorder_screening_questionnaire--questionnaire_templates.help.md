# Rem Sleep Behavior Disorder Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rem Sleep Behavior Disorder Screening Questionnaire is a tool used to collect information about sleep behavior disorder. This form aims to identify potential indicators of sleep behavior disorder by asking respondents about their sleep habits, symptoms, and screening questions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Please ensure you have enough space to provide comprehensive answers for text fields.
3. If you experience any difficulties or have questions, do not hesitate to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rem Sleep Behavior Disorder Screening Questionnaire**: This is the main title of the form.
* **Rem Disorder Screening Questions**: Please provide your responses to the screening questions related to sleep behavior disorder.
* **Rem Disorder Screening Questionnaire Frequency**: Describe how often you have experienced the symptoms of sleep behavior disorder.
* **Rem Disorder Screened Symptoms**: List the symptoms of sleep behavior disorder you have experienced.
* **Rem Screening Symptom Description**: Provide a detailed description of each symptom you have experienced.
* **Rem Screened Symptoms Frequency**: Describe the frequency of each symptom you have experienced.

## Tips
- Please answer each question carefully and honestly.
- If you have any concerns or questions, do not hesitate to contact us.
- This form is for general information purposes only and should not be considered a substitute for professional medical advice.
