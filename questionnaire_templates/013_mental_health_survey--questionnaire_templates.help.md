# mental_health_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This mental health survey is designed to gather information about your mental well-being and help healthcare professionals understand your mental health status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Choose the best answer that reflects your current mental state or situation.
3. Enter your answers in the corresponding text or select fields.
4. Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form.
