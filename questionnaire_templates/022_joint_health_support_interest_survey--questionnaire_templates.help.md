# Joint Health Support Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information from users about their interests and preferences regarding joint health support. The data collected will be used to improve joint health support services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page to provide information about your interests and preferences.
2. Use the text box on each page to type your response to the question.
3. Use the select_one option to choose the correct answer for the question.
4. Use the date and time fields to select the time you prefer for the question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your main interest in joint health support?** (`joint_health_support_interest_survey_page_1`, text, required: false)
This question is asking you to provide a brief description of your main interest in joint health support. Please type your response in the text box.
* **What are you looking for?** (`joint_health_support_interest_survey_page_2`, select_one, required: false)
Please select one of the options provided to indicate what you are looking for in joint health support. The options are:
	+ Physical Therapy
	+ Occupational Therapy
	+ Rehabilitation
	+ Support Group
* **What is your expected duration of joint health support?** (`joint_health_support_interest_survey_page_3`, date, required: false)
This question is asking about the length of time you think you will need for joint health support. Please use the date format (e.g., days, weeks, months).
* **What is your preferred time of day?** (`joint_health_support_interest_survey_page_4`, time, required: false)
Please select the time you prefer for joint health support. You can choose any time of day.
* **What is your preferred time of day?** (`joint_health_support_interest_survey_page_5`, time, required: false)
This is another question asking about the time you prefer for joint health support. Please select the time you prefer.
* **What is your preferred date and time?** (`joint_health_support_interest_survey_page_6`, date, required: false)
This question is asking you to select a specific date and time for joint health support. Please use the date and time format.
* **What is your preferred contact method?** (`joint_health_support_interest_survey_page_7`, select_one, required: false)
Please select one of the options to indicate your preferred contact method. The options are:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Mail
	+ In-person
* **What time of day do you prefer for a call?** (`joint_health_support_interest_survey_page_8`, time, required: false)
Please select the time you prefer for a call for joint health support.
* **What time of day do you prefer for an email?** (`joint_health_support_interest_survey_page_9`, time, required: false)
This question is asking about the time you prefer for an email for joint health support. Please select the time.
* **What is your preferred email subject?** (`joint_health_support_interest_survey_page_10`, text, required: false)
This question is asking you to provide a brief subject line for an email related to joint health support. Please type your response in the text box.
* **Do you have any other questions or requests?** (`joint_health_support_interest_survey_page_11`, text, required: false)
Please type any additional questions or requests you have in the text box.
