# Story Themes Assessment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the themes present in a story or narrative. It is used to identify and analyze the various themes that emerge from a story, which can be useful for understanding the underlying messages, motifs, and emotions conveyed by the story. The form is composed of multiple pages, each with a series of questions or fields that aim to elicit specific information about the themes present in the story.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the story or narrative carefully and reflect on the themes that emerge from it.
2. Answer each question or field on the form based on your understanding of the themes present in the story.
3. Provide clear and concise answers for each question, elaborating on your thoughts and insights about the themes.
4. Use the options provided for select_multiple and select_one questions to select the most relevant answers.
5. For text fields, provide detailed and descriptive answers that demonstrate your understanding of the themes.
