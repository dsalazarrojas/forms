# Newborn photography questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Newborn photography questionnaire is a form designed to collect essential information from clients or customers interested in scheduling a newborn photography session. This form helps the photographer to gather all necessary details for a successful and personalized photo shoot.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Baby Details section with the baby's name, date of birth, and any other relevant information.
2. In the Parent Preferences section, describe your preferences and requirements for the photo shoot, such as the theme, style, and number of photos you want taken.
3. In the Photoshoot Details section, select the date and time for the photography session, and add any additional information about the photoshoot.
4. Enter your contact information in the Contact Details section, including your email, phone number, and address.
5. Finally, select the photo types and positions you prefer in the Photo Types and Photo Positions sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Baby Details** (`baby_details`): Enter the baby's name, date of birth, and any other relevant information about the baby.
* **Parent Preferences** (`parents_preferences`): Describe your preferences and requirements for the photo shoot, such as the theme, style, and number of photos you want taken.
* **Photoshoot Details** (`photoshoot_details`): Select the date and time for the photography session, and add any additional information about the photoshoot.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`): Enter your contact information, including your email, phone number, and address.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`): Add any additional information you feel is relevant to the photo shoot.
* **Photographer Details** (`photographer_details`): Enter the photographer's name and contact information.
* **Photoshoot Schedule** (`photoshoot_schedule`): Select the date and time for the photography session.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`): Enter any payment information required for the photo shoot.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any notes or comments you have about the photo shoot.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`): Select the date for the photography session.
* **Time** (`time`): Select the time for the photography session.
* **Location** (`location`): Enter the location for the photography session.
* **Photographer** (`photographer`): Enter the photographer's name.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter the contact person's email.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Photo Types** (`photo_types`): Select the types of photos you want taken (e.g. Background 1, Background 2, Background 3).
* **Photo Positions** (`photo_positions`): Select the positions for the photos (e.g. Background 1, Background 2, Background 3).

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the photographer.
* The photographer will contact you to discuss and confirm the details of the photo shoot.
* Please be prepared to discuss and agree on the photo shoot details with the photographer.
* The photographer will work with you to create beautiful and memorable photos for your baby.
