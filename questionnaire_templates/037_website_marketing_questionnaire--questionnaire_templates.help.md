# website_marketing_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Website Marketing Questionnaire" is a form used to gather information about your website's marketing strategy and performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please review the form carefully and answer the questions accurately.
2. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
3. If you're unsure about any question, you can ask for clarification or guidance from your supervisor or team lead.
4. Once you've completed the form, review it carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction** (`introduction_label`, text, not required): This field is for a brief introduction to your website's marketing strategy and performance. Please provide a short paragraph explaining your current marketing situation.

### Website Performance
* **Website Performance** (`website_performance_label`, text, not required): This field is for you to describe your website's performance metrics, such as traffic, engagement, and conversion rates. Please provide a brief summary of your website's current performance.

### Marketing Objectives
* **Marketing Objectives** (`marketing_objectives_label`, select_one, not required): This field is for you to choose the primary marketing objective of your website. Please select the option that best describes your website's marketing goal.
	+ Option 1: Brand awareness
	+ Option 2: Lead generation
	+ Option 3: Sales conversion

### Target Audience
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience_label`, text, not required): This field is for you to describe your target audience, such as demographics, interests, or needs. Please provide a brief description of your website's target audience.

### Current Marketing Channels
* **Current Marketing Channels** (`current_marketing_channels_label`, select_multiple, not required): This field is for you to choose the current marketing channels you are using for your website. Please select all the applicable options.
	+ Option 1: Social media
	+ Option 2: Email marketing
	+ Option 3: Content marketing

### Website Features
* **Website Features** (`website_features_label`, text, not required): This field is for you to describe the unique features of your website. Please provide a brief description of your website's key features.

### Feedback
* **Feedback** (`feedback_label`, date, not required): This field is for you to record any feedback or suggestions you have about the website. Please provide a date and a brief description of your feedback.

### Additional Information
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info_label`, note, not required): This field is for you to provide any additional information that may be relevant to your website's marketing strategy and performance. Please use this field for any additional comments or notes.
