# Anxiety Self Reflection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Anxiety Self Reflection Form" is designed to help you track your anxiety levels, symptoms, and coping strategies over a specific time period. It's meant to be a personal and private assessment tool to aid in your mental health and wellness journey.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by selecting the time period for which you wish to reflect on (Reflection date). You can choose from "Today", "Past week", "Past month", or select "Custom period" and specify the dates.
2.  For the "Overall anxiety level during this period", choose a number between 1 and 10, with 1 being the lowest level of anxiety and 10 being the highest.
3.  In the "Anxiety compared to last reflection" section, choose how your anxiety has changed compared to your previous reflection period.
4.  In the "Primary anxiety triggers during this period" field, enter your primary sources of anxiety for this time period.
5.  In the "Any patterns noticed in your triggers?" field, describe any patterns you've noticed in your anxiety triggers.
6.  The "Physical symptoms experienced" field allows you to select multiple symptoms you've experienced during this period. Choose all that apply.
7.  In the "Emotional symptoms experienced" field, select multiple symptoms you've experienced during this period.
8.  For "Frequency of anxiety symptoms", choose how often you've experienced symptoms during this period.
9.  In "Coping strategies used during this period", choose all the strategies you've used to cope with anxiety.
10. In "Which coping strategy was most helpful?", describe the one strategy that had the most impact on your anxiety.
11. For "Overall effectiveness of coping strategies", choose how effective you feel each strategy has been.
12. In "Did you avoid any situations or triggers?" field, select yes or no based on whether you've actively avoided anything that triggers your anxiety.
13. If yes, choose how often you've done so in the "How did avoidance impact your life?" field, describe how avoidance affected you.
14. In "Sleep quality during this period", choose your perceived sleep quality for this period.
15.  How often did you exercise during this period? 
16.  What is your diet quality like this period?
17.  How connected did you feel socially during this period?
18.  Have you engaged in professional support this period? If yes, describe your experience.
19.  "Positive moments or wins" describes any moments that stood out as positive during this period.
20.  In "What have you learned about your anxiety?" describe any insights or understanding you've gained.
21.  What are your goals for the next reflection period?
22.  Describe the type of support you would find helpful for moving forward.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reflection date** (`reflection_date`, `date`, required): The date you wish to reflect on. You can choose from "Today", "Past week", "Past month", or select "Custom period" and specify the dates.
* **This reflection covers what time period** (`reflection_period`, `select_one`, required): Choose the time period for which you wish to reflect on.
* **Overall anxiety level during this period** (`overall_anxiety_level`, `number`, required): On a scale of 1-10, rate your overall anxiety level during this period.
* **Anxiety compared to last reflection** (`anxiety_compared_to_last`, `select_one`, required): Describe how your anxiety has changed compared to your last reflection.
* **Primary anxiety triggers during this period** (`primary_triggers_this_period`, `text`, required): Enter your primary sources of anxiety for this time period.
* **Any patterns noticed in your triggers?** (`trigger_patterns`, `text`, required): Describe any patterns you've noticed in your anxiety triggers.
* **Physical symptoms experienced** (`physical_symptoms`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the physical symptoms you've experienced during this period.
* **Emotional symptoms experienced** (`emotional_symptoms`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the emotional symptoms you've experienced during this period.
* **Frequency of anxiety symptoms** (`symptom_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Choose how often you've experienced anxiety symptoms during this period.
* **Coping strategies used during this period** (`coping_strategies_used`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all the strategies you've used to cope with anxiety during this period.
* **Which coping strategy was most helpful?** (`most_helpful_strategy`, `text`, required): Describe the one strategy that had the most impact on your anxiety.
* **Overall effectiveness of coping strategies** (`strategy_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required): Choose how effective you feel each strategy has been.
* **Did you avoid any situations or triggers?** (`avoided_triggers`, `select_one`, required): Select yes or no based on whether you've actively avoided anything that triggers your anxiety.
* **How did avoidance impact your life?** (`avoidance_impact`, `text`, required): Describe how avoidance affected you.
* **Sleep quality during this period** (`sleep_quality`, `select_one`, required): Choose your perceived sleep quality for this period.
* **How often did you exercise?** (`exercise_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Choose how often you've exercised during this period.
* **Diet quality during this period** (`diet_quality`, `select_one`, required): Choose your diet quality for this period.
* **How connected did you feel socially?** (`social_connection`, `select_one`, required): Choose how connected you felt socially during this period.
* **Did you engage in professional support this period?** (`professional_support`, `select_one`, required): Select yes or no based on whether you've engaged in professional support.
* **Positive moments or wins** (`positive_moments`, `text`, required): Describe any moments that stood out as positive during this period.
* **What have you learned about your anxiety?** (`growth_observations`, `text`, required): Describe any insights or understanding you've gained about your anxiety.
* **Goals for the next reflection period** (`goals_next_period`, `text`, required): Write down your goals for the next reflection period.
* **What support would be helpful going forward?** (`support_needed`, `text`, required): Describe the type of support you would find helpful for moving forward.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* Take your time to think about each question carefully.
* Don't worry if you're unsure or need help, just ask!
