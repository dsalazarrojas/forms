# Business Planning Self Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Planning Self-Assessment is a tool designed to help business owners and leaders evaluate their company's progress in planning and preparation. This assessment is meant to be a self-reflection exercise to identify areas of strength and weakness, and to help businesses identify potential opportunities and threats. The assessment is used to determine the overall readiness of a business to execute its plan.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your name and business name as prompted.
2.  Select the date you are completing this assessment.
3.  For the age of your business, choose the most accurate option from the provided list.
4.  Rate the clarity of your business objectives using the provided scale.
5.  Indicate your confidence level in your market analysis using the provided scale.
6.  Rate your understanding of your competitive positioning using the provided scale.
7.  Rate your confidence in financial projections using the provided scale.
8.  Rate the clarity of your revenue model using the provided scale.
9.  Indicate how ready your marketing strategy is using the provided scale.
10. Rate your operational readiness using the provided scale.
11. Identify your main business strengths from the list provided.
12. Identify areas of your business needing improvement from the list provided (if applicable).
13. List potential external market opportunities.
14. List potential external market threats.
15. Provide your top 3 priorities for the next quarter.
16. Indicate your current funding needs.
17. Rate your overall business readiness using the provided scale.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (1): A text field where you can enter your name.
* **Business Name** (2): A text field where you can enter your business's name.
* **Assessment Date** (3): A date field where you can select the date you are completing this assessment.
* **How Long Has Your Business Existed** (4): A dropdown list with the following options:
	+ Pre-launch
	+ Less than 1 year
	+ 1-3 years
	+ 3-5 years
	+ 5+ years
* **Clarity of Business Objectives** (5): A dropdown list with the following options:
	+ Very Clear
	+ Clear
	+ Somewhat Clear
	+ Unclear
	+ Very Unclear
* **Market Analysis Confidence** (6): A dropdown list with the following options:
	+ Highly Confident
	+ Confident
	+ Moderately Confident
	+ Not Confident
	+ Very Uncertain
* **Competitive Positioning** (7): A dropdown list with the following options:
	+ Excellent Understanding
	+ Good Understanding
	+ Fair Understanding
	+ Limited Understanding
	+ No Understanding
* **Financial Projections Confidence** (8): A dropdown list with the following options:
	+ Very Confident
	+ Confident
	+ Moderately Confident
	+ Not Confident
	+ Uncertain
* **Revenue Model Clarity** (9): A dropdown list with the following options:
	+ Very Clear
	+ Clear
	+ Somewhat Clear
	+ Unclear
	+ Not Defined
* **Marketing Strategy Readiness** (10): A dropdown list with the following options:
	+ Fully Ready
	+ Mostly Ready
	+ Partially Ready
	+ Needs Work
	+ Not Developed
* **Operational Readiness** (11): A dropdown list with the following options:
	+ Fully Ready
	+ Mostly Ready
	+ Partially Ready
	+ Needs Work
	+ Not Ready
* **Key Business Strengths** (12): A select multiple checkbox list where you can choose from the following options:
	+ Strong Management Team
	+ Unique Product/Service
	+ Market Demand
	+ Financial Resources
	+ Strategic Location
	+ Brand Recognition
* **Main Business Weaknesses** (13): A select multiple checkbox list where you can choose from the following options (if not required):
	+ Insufficient Capital
	+ Limited Experience
	+ Weak Brand Recognition
	+ Limited Market Access
	+ Operational Challenges
* **External Opportunities** (14): A text field where you can list potential market opportunities.
* **External Threats** (15): A text field where you can list potential external market threats.
* **Top Action Priorities for Next Quarter** (16): A text field where you can list your top 3 priorities.
* **Current Funding Needs** (17): A dropdown list with the following options:
	+ No additional funding needed
	+ Minor funding required
	+ Moderate funding required
	+ Significant funding required
	+ Critical funding needed
* **Overall Business Readiness** (18): A dropdown list with the following options:
	+ Fully Ready
	+ Mostly Ready
	+ Moderately Ready
	+ Needs More Preparation
	+ Not Ready

## Tips

* Please answer each question honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure of an answer, choose the option that best represents your current situation.
* This assessment is a self-reflection tool, and your answers will help you identify areas of strength and weakness in your business.
* The assessment is not a substitute for professional advice or consulting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
