# Life Insurance Underwriting Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a life insurance policy, including contact details and policy-related information. It is intended for individuals applying for a life insurance policy or for underwriters evaluating an existing policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" section, including your name, address, and any other relevant contact details.
2. In the "Policy Details" section, select the type of policy you are applying for or evaluating (e.g. Health, Term, Whole).
3. Enter the term of your policy (e.g. 1 year, 5 years, etc.).
4. Enter the amount of your policy.
5. Enter the start date of your policy.
6. Enter the end date of your policy (if applicable).
7. Select any relevant risk factors that may affect your policy (e.g. Smoking, Medical).
8. Enter any relevant medical information.
9. In the "Insurance Agencies" section, select the type of insurance agency or broker involved in your policy (e.g. Agency, Broker, Agent, Insurance Company).
10. Enter the name of the underwriter evaluating or issuing your policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (1/10): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Policy Holder** (2/10): Enter the name of the policy holder.
* **Insurance Type** (3/10): Select the type of policy you are applying for or evaluating (e.g. Health, Term, Whole).
* **Policy Term** (4/10): Enter the term of your policy (e.g. 1 year, 5 years, etc.).
* **Policy Amount** (5/10): Enter the amount of your policy.
* **Policy Start Date** (6/10): Enter the start date of your policy.
* **Policy End Date** (7/10): Enter the end date of your policy (if applicable).
* **Policy Status** (8/10): Select the status of your policy (e.g. Active, Inactive).
* **Risk Factors** (9/10): Select any relevant risk factors that may affect your policy (e.g. Smoking, Medical).
* **Medical Info** (10/10): Enter any relevant medical information.
* **Insurance Agencies** (11/10): Select the type of insurance agency or broker involved in your policy (e.g. Agency, Broker, Agent, Insurance Company).
* **Underwriter Name** (12/10): Enter the name of the underwriter evaluating or issuing your policy.

## Tips

* Be sure to answer all questions accurately and completely to ensure that your policy application or evaluation is processed correctly.
* If you are unsure about any question, please consult with the underwriter or insurance agency involved.
* This form is designed to collect information in a way that is easy to understand and complete. If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us.
