# Pedestrian_Safety_Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pedestrian_Safety_Survey form is designed to collect data about users' feelings of safety while walking in their community. This information will help us understand how safe people feel and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best match your feelings and opinions.
3. If you have additional comments or thoughts, please use the text fields to provide more information.
4. Make sure to enter your email address and phone number so we can follow up with you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction**: This is the first page of the form. Please read it carefully and make sure you understand the purpose of the form.
### do_you_feel_safely_in_your_community
* **do_you_feel_safely_in_your_community**: Please select one of the options that best describes how you feel about pedestrian safety in your community.
	+ Very Likely
	+ Likely
	+ Not Likely
	+ Not Very Likely
	+ Not at All Likely
### how_do_you_feel_about_your_safety
* **how_do_you_feel_about_your_safety**: Please select one of the options that best describes how you feel about your safety while walking in your community.
	+ Very Safe
	+ Somewhat Safe
	+ Not very Safe
	+ Not Safe at All
### reasons_why_your_feel_safe
* **reasons_why_your_feel_safe**: Please select all the reasons that make you feel safe while walking in your community.
	+ Good road lighting
	+ Good traffic flow
	+ Good road maintenance
	+ Good pedestrian signage
	+ Good pedestrian infrastructure
	+ Good police presence
	+ Good community spirit
	+ Good traffic calming
	+ Good road user behavior
	+ Other (please specify)
### other_reasons
* **other_reasons**: If you selected "Other" in the previous question, please specify why you feel safe in your community.
### specify_other_reasons
* **specify_other_reasons**: This is a text field for you to provide more information about why you feel safe in your community.
### additional_comments
* **additional_comments**: Do you have any additional comments or thoughts about pedestrian safety in your community?
### comments_to_follow_up
* **comments_to_follow_up**: Do you have any comments or thoughts that you would like to follow up on?
*   Please enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
*   This field is optional.
### your_email
* **your_email**: Please enter your email address so we can follow up with you if needed.
*   This field is optional.
### comments_to_follow_up_phone
* **comments_to_follow_up_phone**: This field is not used in this version of the form.
*   It is here to be used in a future version of the form.
*   Please disregard this field for now.
