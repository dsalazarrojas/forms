# Puppy Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Puppy Questionnaire form is used to collect information about puppies and their owners. It helps in tracking and managing the adoption process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "What is this?" field on page 1 with a brief description of the puppy.
2. On the second page, enter the details of the puppy you are providing information about, such as its name, age, breed, etc.
3. On the third page, enter your information as the owner of the puppy.
4. On the fourth page, enter your contact information so we can reach out to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is this?** (Buyer Info, Number, Optional)
	+ This field is used to provide a brief description of the puppy, e.g., "My new puppy!"
* **Puppies info** (Puppy Info, Text, Optional)
	+ This field is used to enter details about the puppy, such as its name, age, breed, etc.
* **Owners info** (Owner Info, Text, Optional)
	+ This field is used to enter your information as the owner of the puppy.
* **Contact info** (Contact Info, Email, Optional)
	+ This field is used to enter your contact information so we can reach out to you.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information you enter is accurate and complete.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
- If you have any questions or need further assistance, please contact us.
