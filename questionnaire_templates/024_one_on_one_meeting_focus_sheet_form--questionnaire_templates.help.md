# one_on_one_meeting_focus_sheet_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about one-on-one meetings, including the meeting topic, date, time, outcomes, next steps, and any other relevant details. The form is used to document and follow up on meetings, and to help teams stay organized and on track.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the meeting topic from the provided options.
2. Choose the meeting date and time from the calendar.
3. Describe the meeting outcomes and next steps.
4. (Optional) Select the next meeting date, if applicable.
5. Evaluate the importance of minimalism in the meeting.
6. Provide any additional meeting notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Topic** (`meeting_topic`, text, required): Please describe the meeting topic.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, required): Please select the meeting date.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, optional): Please select the meeting time.
* **Meeting Outcomes** (`meeting_outcomes`, text, required): Please describe the meeting outcomes.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, required): Please describe the next steps.
* **Next Meeting** (`next_meeting`, date, optional): Please select the next meeting date, if applicable.
* **Importance of Minimalism** (`minimalism`, select_multiple, optional): Select how important is minimalism in this meeting.
	+ Very important
	+ Somewhat important
	+ Not at all important
* **Meeting Notes** (`meeting_notes`, note, optional): Please describe the meeting notes.
* **Minimalism Relevant** (`minimalism2`, select_one, optional): Do you think minimalism is relevant in this meeting?
	+ True
	+ False
* **Minimalism Importance** (`minimalism3`, number, optional): Please select the importance of minimalism in this meeting.
* **Next Meeting2** (`next_meeting2`, date, optional): Please select the next meeting date, if applicable.
* **Minimalism Follow-up** (`minimalism4`, text, optional): Please describe the minimalism follow-up.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you're unsure about the meeting topic, date, or time, contact the meeting organizer for clarification.
* Use the meeting outcomes and next steps to help the team stay on track and follow up on action items.
