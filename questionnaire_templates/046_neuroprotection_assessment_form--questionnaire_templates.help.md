# Neuroprotection Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Neuroprotection Assessment Form is a medical questionnaire used to assess an individual's neuroprotection status, risk factors, and symptoms.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete each section of the form carefully and accurately.
2.  Answer all required fields truthfully and thoroughly.
3.  Use the available space for any additional comments or notes.
4.  Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Neuroprotection Assessment Form Part 1** (text, required, false):
    This field is a brief description of the form's purpose and the individual's understanding of neuroprotection.
* **Symptoms** (text, required, true):
    Describe any neurological symptoms you are experiencing, such as headaches, dizziness, or memory loss.
* **Risk Factors** (select multiple, required, true):
    Indicate whether you have any risk factors that could be affecting your neuroprotection status, such as smoking, lack of exercise, or stress.
* **Lifestyle Data** (select one, required, true):
    Indicate your current lifestyle data, such as your diet, sleep patterns, or work environment.
* **Neurological Symptoms Part 1** (text, required, true):
    Describe any additional neurological symptoms you are experiencing, such as tremors, seizures, or numbness.
* **Neurological Symptoms Part 2** (text, required, true):
    Describe any other neurological symptoms you are experiencing, such as confusion, hallucinations, or difficulty concentrating.
* **Notes and Comments** (note, required, false):
    Provide any additional comments or notes about your neuroprotection status or concerns.
* **Email Address** (email, required, true):
    Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Phone Number** (text, required, true):
    Enter your phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Date** (date, required, true):
    Enter the date of your assessment.
* **Time** (time, required, true):
    Enter the time of your assessment.
* **Neurological Symptoms Part 3** (text, required, false):
    Describe any other neurological symptoms you are experiencing, such as loss of coordination or difficulty speaking.
* **Neurological Symptoms Part 4** (text, required, false):
    Describe any other neurological symptoms you are experiencing, such as difficulty walking or numbness.
* **Neurological Symptoms Part 5** (text, required, false):
    Describe any other neurological symptoms you are experiencing, such as difficulty swallowing or vision changes.
* **Neuroprotection Plan** (text, required, false):
    Describe any plans you have for improving your neuroprotection status, such as diet changes or exercise routines.
* **Follow-up** (text, required, false):
    Describe any follow-up plans or appointments you have scheduled.
* **Follow-up Date** (date, required, false):
    Enter the date of your next follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up Time** (time, required, false):
    Enter the time of your next follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up Phone Number** (text, required, false):
    Enter your phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Follow-up Email Address** (email, required, false):
    Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Follow-up Notes** (text, required, false):
    Provide any additional comments or notes for the follow-up appointment.
* **Neuroprotection Plan 1** (text, required, false):
    Describe any plans you have for improving your neuroprotection status, such as diet changes or exercise routines.
* **Neuroprotection Plan 2** (text, required, false):
    Describe any additional plans you have for improving your neuroprotection status, such as stress management or social support.
* **Follow-up Notes 1** (text, required, false):
    Provide any additional comments or notes for the follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up Notes 2** (text, required, false):
    Provide any additional comments or notes for the follow-up appointment.
