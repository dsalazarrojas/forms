# Restaurant Chain Dining Experience Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the quality of service, food, and overall experience at a restaurant chain. It's meant to be filled out by customers after each visit, allowing the restaurant to gauge their satisfaction and make necessary improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your honest feedback about your recent visit to the restaurant chain.
2. Provide clear and concise answers to each question, using the options provided for select questions.
3. If a question does not apply to your experience, please answer "No" or "N/A" (not applicable) as appropriate.
4. Use the "Overall Experience" field to provide any additional comments about your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Location** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the name of the restaurant chain location you visited.
* **Date of Visit** (`2`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your visit in the format MM/DD/YY or MM/DD/ZZ.
* **Time of Visit** (`3`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of your visit in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Staff Friendliness** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose from the following options:
	+ Yes (the staff was friendly and welcoming)
	+ No (the staff was unfriendly or unwelcoming)
* **Cleanliness** (`5`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose from the following options:
	+ Yes (the restaurant was clean and well-maintained)
	+ No (the restaurant was dirty or poorly maintained)
* **Food Quality** (`6`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose from the following options:
	+ Good (the food was well-prepared and of good taste)
	+ Poor (the food was not well-prepared or of poor taste)
* **Food Speed** (`7`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose from the following options:
	+ Yes (your food was served quickly)
	+ No (your food was not served quickly)
* **Value for Money** (`8`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose from the following options:
	+ Yes (you felt the prices were reasonable)
	+ No (you felt the prices were not reasonable)
* **Will You Return?** (`9`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose from the following options:
	+ Yes (you would return to this restaurant chain)
	+ No (you would not return to this restaurant chain)
* **Overall Experience** (`10`, `text`, required: false): Please provide any additional comments about your visit.
* **Comments** (`11`, `text`, required: false): Is there anything else you'd like to comment on about your visit?
