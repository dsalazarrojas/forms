# Researcher Type Identification Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be completed by researchers to help us identify their characteristics and preferences. It will take approximately 2-3 minutes to complete and is crucial for our team to provide you with relevant support and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your researcher type(s) from the dropdown list in the "Researcher Type" field.
2. Enter your research habits, motivations, and any relevant details in the "Research Habits", "Research Motivations", and "Research Location" fields.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Outreach Programs", "Training Programs", and "Support Programs" fields based on your current involvement or interest.
4. Enter your research team, institution, and focus area in the respective fields.
5. Provide your contact email and phone number in the "Contact Email" and "Contact Phone" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Researcher Type** (`researcher_type`, multiple selection, required): Select all the categories that apply to you as a researcher. You can select one or multiple options.
* **Research Habits** (`research_habits`, text, required): Describe your research habits, such as your typical workflow, research style, or any relevant habits that affect your research.
* **Research Motivations** (`research_motivations`, text, required): Explain your motivations for doing research, such as personal interest, funding, or other relevant reasons.
* **Outreach Programs** (`outreach_programs`, select_one, required): Check "Yes" if you are currently participating in or have interest in outreach programs.
* **Training Programs** (`training_programs`, select_one, required): Check "Yes" if you have attended or are interested in training programs.
* **Support Programs** (`support_programs`, select_one, required): Check "Yes" if you are currently using or have interest in support programs.
* **Research Team** (`research_team`, text, required): Enter any relevant information about your research team, such as department or lab affiliation.
* **Research Institution** (`research_institution`, text, required): Enter your research institution, such as university or organization.
* **Research Location** (`research_location`, text, required): Enter your research location, such as city or state.
* **Research Focus Area** (`research_focus_area`, text, required): Enter your research focus area, such as topic or area of study.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.

## Tips

* Please answer these questions accurately to ensure we provide you with the best support.
* If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to contact us.
* Remember to keep your answers concise and focused on your relevant experiences and preferences.
* You can save and revisit your answers later if needed.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask us for clarification.
