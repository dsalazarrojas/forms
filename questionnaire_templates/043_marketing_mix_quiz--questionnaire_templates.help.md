# Marketing Mix Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Mix Quiz is designed to test your understanding of the 4 Ps of Marketing Mix, which are: Product, Price, Promotion, Place, and other related components.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the quiz, follow these steps:
1. Carefully read each question and select the most suitable answer.
2. Ensure that you have selected one or more options in each field, as indicated by the required status in the field description.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product** (id: 2, product, select_one, Product Mix, Other, false):
	+ This question asks about the type of product or service you are marketing.
	+ Select one of the provided options, either "Product Mix" or "Other".
* **Price** (id: 3, price, text, none, false):
	+ This field is intended to capture any pricing-related information you have about your product or service.
* **Place** (id: 4, place, text, none, false):
	+ This field is meant for any place-related information about your product or service.
* **Promotion** (id: 5, promotion, text, none, false):
	+ This field is for any promotion-related details about your product or service.
* **Process** (id: 6, process, select_multiple, Process Mix, Other, false):
	+ This question is concerned with the production process or service delivery process.
	+ Select one or more of the provided options, either "Process Mix" or "Other".
* **Performance** (id: 7, performance, text, none, false):
	+ This field is for capturing any performance-related information about your product or service.
* **Policy** (id: 8, policy, text, none, false):
	+ This field is intended for any policy information related to your product or service.
* **People** (id: 9, people, text, none, false):
	+ This field is meant for capturing information about the people involved in your marketing.
* **Part** (id: 10, part, text, none, false):
	+ This field is for capturing any part-related information about your product or service.

## Tips
Please ensure that you provide accurate and clear answers to help us better understand your knowledge of the marketing mix components. If you have any doubts, feel free to ask the form administrator for assistance.
