# Imaging Techniques Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Imaging Techniques Assessment" form is used to evaluate the effectiveness and applicability of various imaging techniques in a particular setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose whether you use each of the listed imaging techniques or not.
2. For select multiple questions, choose all the techniques that apply to your situation.
3. Make sure to answer all the required questions, as indicated by the "Required" label.
