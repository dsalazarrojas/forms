<# Parking Facilities Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide>
## Purpose
The Parking Facilities Feedback Questionnaire is designed to gather information on the condition, cleanliness, and overall satisfaction of parking facilities within a given building or campus.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Save your progress by clicking the "Save Progress" button at the bottom of each page.
* Review each page carefully, taking note of the questions and options available.
* Choose the options that best describe your experience for each question.
* Submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`parking_facilities_feedback_questionnaire_page`): This is the first page of the form, where you will find a brief introduction to the parking facilities feedback questionnaire.
* **Parking facilities** (`parking_facilities_feedback_questionnaire_page_2`): On this page, you will be asked to rate the overall condition of the parking facilities. Choose the option that best describes your experience:
	+ Excellent: The facilities were in excellent condition, well-maintained and clean.
	+ Good: The facilities were in good condition, with some minor issues.
	+ Fair: The facilities were in fair condition, with notable issues.
	+ Poor: The facilities were in poor condition.
* **Parking facilities** (`parking_facilities_feedback_questionnaire_page_3`): On this page, you will be asked to select all that apply from the options below regarding the parking facilities. Choose the options that best describe your experience:
	+ Parking signs and signage: The signs and signage were clear and easy to read.
	+ Parking lot size and layout: The parking lot was spacious and well-designed.
	+ Parking lot accessibility: The parking lot was accessible to all users.
	+ Parking lot cleanliness: The parking lot was clean and well-maintained.
	+ Parking lot condition: The parking lot was in good condition.
	+ Accessible parking options: There were accessible parking options available.
	+ Parking signage clearness: The signs were clear and easy to read.
	+ Parking availability: There was enough parking available.
	+ Overall condition: The parking facilities overall condition.
* **Contact information** (`parking_facilities_feedback_questionnaire_page_4`): On this page, you will be asked to provide your email address so that we can follow up with you regarding your feedback.
* **Additional comments** (`parking_facilities_feedback_questionnaire_page_5`): On this page, you will have the opportunity to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have regarding the parking facilities.

## Tips

* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback, as it will help us to better understand your experience.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* Your feedback is highly valued and will contribute to improving the parking facilities.
