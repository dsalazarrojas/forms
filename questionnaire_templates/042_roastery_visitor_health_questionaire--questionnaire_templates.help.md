# Roastery Visitor Health Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a visitor's health status before entering the roastery area. The purpose of this form is to ensure that visitors do not pose a risk to themselves or others, and to provide a safe and healthy environment for everyone. Please note that this form is a requirement for all visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the correct options for each question.
3. Fill in the text fields with relevant information.
4. Do not submit the form without filling in all required fields.
5. Once you have completed the form, click the "Submit" button to send it to the server.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Have you had any symptoms or illness in the past 2 weeks?** (`symptoms`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the symptoms or illnesses you have experienced in the past 2 weeks. This includes, but is not limited to, cough, fever, sore throat, etc.
* **Do you have a fever?** (`temperature`, number, required): Please enter your current body temperature in degrees Celsius (°C) if you have a fever, or type "none" if you do not.
* **What is your recent travel history?** (`travel_history`, text, required): Please describe your recent travel history, including any countries or regions you have visited and when you returned.
* **Have you had close contact with anyone who has a confirmed case of [disease] in the past 2 weeks?** (`close_contact`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" if you have had close contact with someone who has a confirmed case of [disease] in the past 2 weeks, or select "No" otherwise.
* **Contact Description** (`contact_description`, text, optional): If you selected "Yes" to the previous question, please provide a brief description of your contact with the person who has [disease].

## Tips
* Please answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask a staff member for assistance.
* If you have any underlying medical conditions, please disclose them to the staff before proceeding.
* Please note that this form is a requirement for all visitors, and failure to complete it may result in denied access to the roastery area.

This help guide is designed to be concrete, concise, and practical, and it's based on the provided YAML fields. If you have any further questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to ask a staff member.
