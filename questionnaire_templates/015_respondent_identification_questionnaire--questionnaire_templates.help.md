# Respondent Identification Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic information from respondents for research or study purposes. It is used to identify and categorize respondents for specific studies or programs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting to complete the form.
- Please fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the designated contact person for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your study ID? (#1)** (`study_id`, text, optional): This field is a unique identifier for your study or program. If you have a study ID, please enter it here. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **How would you like to be contacted? (#2)** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional): This field asks for your preferred contact method (Phone, Email, SMS, or Mail). Choose the method that best suits your preferences.
* **Date of birth (#3)** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the required format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Time (#4)** (`time`, time, optional): Enter your time in the required format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Email (#5)** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone (#6)** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Do you consent to being contacted? (#7)** (`consent`, select_one, optional): Please indicate if you give permission to be contacted for further information. Choose 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Study type (#8)** (`study_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the study types that apply to you (Study, Program, Survey).
* **Program name (#9)** (`program_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the program you are associated with.
* **Program start date (#10)** (`program_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the program you are associated with.
