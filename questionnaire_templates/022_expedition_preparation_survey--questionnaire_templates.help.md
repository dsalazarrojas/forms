# Expedition Preparation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expedition Preparation Survey is a crucial form designed to collect essential information from individuals going on expeditions or trips with our team. This information helps us ensure everyone's safety and well-being during the journey. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer all required fields.
2. Provide accurate and detailed information about your experience, health, and personal details.
3. Make sure to answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What kind of adventure are you going on?** (`participant_experience`): This question helps us understand your experience level and familiarity with the type of adventure you are going on. Please provide a brief description of your experience with similar expeditions or trips.
* **Who should we contact in case of an emergency?** (`emergency_contacts`): Please provide the name and contact information of a person we can reach out to in case of an emergency. This could be a friend, family member, or colleague.
* **Do you have any health issues we should know about?** (`health_details`): This is an optional but important question. If you have any health issues that may impact your ability to participate in the expedition, please provide a detailed description of your conditions.
* **Medical history** (`medical_history`): If you have any pre-existing medical conditions or allergies, please provide a brief description.
* **Do you have any allergies?** (`allergies`): If you have any allergies, please select all that apply from the list provided.
* **Are you taking any medications?** (`medications`): If you are taking any medications, please select all that apply from the list provided.
* **Trip itinerary** (`trip_itinerary`): Please provide a detailed description of your trip itinerary, including dates, locations, and any other relevant details.
* **What is the relationship between you and your emergency contact?** (`emergency_contact_relationship`): Please describe your relationship with the emergency contact, such as family member, friend, or colleague.
* **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact_name`): Please provide the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency contact phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): Please provide the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency contact email** (`emergency_contact_email`): Please provide the email address of your emergency contact.
* **How do you know this person?** (`emergency_contact_relationship`): Please describe how you know this person, such as friend, family member, or colleague.

Note: All questions marked as "required" must be answered accurately and truthfully.
