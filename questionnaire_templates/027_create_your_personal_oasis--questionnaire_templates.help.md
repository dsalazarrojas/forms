# create_your_personal_oasis - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "create_your_personal_oasis" form is designed to help users create a tailored space that addresses their specific needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields on each page. Each field is labeled with a brief description of what it is intended to capture.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **create_your_personal_oasis** (Page 1): This is the title of your personal oasis.
* **questionnaire_templates_request_forms** (Page 2): Please provide a brief description of the forms you are interested in creating.
* **create_your_personal_oasis_consultation_form** (Page 3): Describe your consultation form.
* **helps_designers_and_service_providers_collect_project_preferences** (Page 4): Share any preferences you have for collecting project preferences.
* **through_jotform_supporting_consistent_data_collection** (Page 5): Tell us about your experience with Jotform and how it supports consistent data collection.
* **supports_faster_follow_up_from_each_form_submission** (Page 6): Share your thoughts on how this feature supports faster follow-up from each form submission.
* **create_your_own_personal_oasis** (Page 7): This is your opportunity to create your own personal oasis.
* **project_preferences** (Page 8): List your project preferences here.
