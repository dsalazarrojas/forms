# photo_shoot_questionnaire_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a questionnaire template for collecting client information, shoot details, and contact information for photo shoots. It is designed to be flexible and adaptable to various photo shoot scenarios, making it an ideal tool for photographers, event planners, and other professionals in the industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the Client Information page**:
	* On the first page, enter the client's name, address, and any other relevant details.
2. **Provide Shoot Details**:
	* On the second page, describe the photo shoot in a brief and concise manner, including the location, date, and any specific requirements or requests.
3. **Enter Contact Information**:
	* On the third page, provide the contact information of the person submitting the form, including their name, email, and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Information
* **Client Information (Client Information)** (`<name>`): Enter the name of the client.

### Shoot Details
* **Shoot Details (Shoot Details)** (`<type>`): Describe the photo shoot in a brief and concise manner, including the location, date, and any specific requirements or requests. Please provide as much information as possible to help the photographer or planner prepare for the shoot.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Info (Contact Information)** (`<type>`): Enter the name, email, and phone number of the person submitting the form.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the Client Information is accurate and up-to-date to facilitate efficient communication with the client.
* For Shoot Details, be as specific as possible to ensure that the photographer or planner can plan and prepare for the shoot.
* For Contact Information, double-check that you have entered the correct name, email, and phone number to ensure that responses can be directed to the right person.
