# Employee Involvement Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather employee feedback and involvement on various aspects of their work. It helps management assess and improve the work environment, departmental engagement, and overall employee satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to provide detailed feedback.
2. Select the relevant departments, roles, and feedback frequencies that apply to you.
3. Rate your satisfaction levels on a scale of High, Medium, or Low for each question.
4. Provide specific feedback comments and recommendations where possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Introduction**
* This is a note field where you can provide any introduction or context about your feedback.

### 2. **Employee Name** (`Employee Name`, text, optional)
* Please enter your name as it appears on your employee badge or ID.

### 3. **Department** (`Department`, select multiple, optional)
* Select all departments that you are involved with or have feedback on.

### 4. **Role** (`Role`, select one, optional)
* Choose your primary role within the company.

### 5. **Manager Name** (`Manager Name`, text, optional)
* If applicable, enter the name of your direct manager.

### 6. **Feedback Date** (`Feedback Date`, date, optional)
* Enter the date when you provided this feedback.

### 7. **Feedback Time** (`Feedback Time`, time, optional)
* Enter the time when you provided this feedback.

### 8. **Feedback Location** (`Feedback Location`, select one, optional)
* Choose where you provided this feedback, whether it's Office, Remote, or Home.

### 9. **Feedback Comments** (`Feedback Comments`, text, optional)
* Provide any comments or suggestions you have for improvement.

### 10. **Feedback Recommendations** (`Feedback Recommendations`, text, optional)
* Share any specific recommendations you have for improvement.

### 11. **Feedback Frequency** (`Feedback Frequency`, select one, optional)
* Rate how often you provide feedback, whether it's Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.

### 12. **Feedback Urgency** (`Feedback Urgency`, select one, optional)
* Rate how urgently you need feedback, whether it's High, Medium, or Low.

### 13. **Feedback Confidence** (`Feedback Confidence`, select one, optional)
* Rate how confident you are in your feedback, whether it's High, Medium, or Low.

### 14. **Employee Satisfaction** (`Employee Satisfaction`, select multiple, optional)
* Rate your satisfaction with your current department, team, or work environment.

### 15. **Overall Satisfaction** (`Overall Satisfaction`, select one, optional)
* Rate your overall satisfaction with your job or work experience.

### 16. **Implementation** (`Implementation`, select one, optional)
* Rate the implementation of your feedback, whether it's High, Medium, or Low.

### 17. **Departmental Satisfaction** (`Departmental Satisfaction`, select one, optional)
* Rate your satisfaction with your department, whether it's High, Medium, or Low.

### 18. **Feedback Frequency 2** (`Feedback Frequency 2`, select one, optional)
* Rate how often you provide feedback, whether it's Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.

### 19. **Feedback Urgency 2** (`Feedback Urgency 2`, select one, optional)
* Rate how urgently you need feedback, whether it's High, Medium, or Low.

### 20. **Feedback Confidence 2** (`Feedback Confidence 2`, select one, optional)
* Rate how confident you are in your feedback, whether it's High, Medium, or Low.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough when providing feedback to help management improve the work environment.
* Select only the departments and roles that apply to you.
* Rate your satisfaction levels accurately based on your experience.
