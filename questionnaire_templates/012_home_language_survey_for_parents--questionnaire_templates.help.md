# home_language_survey_for_parents - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Home Language Survey for Parents" is a tool designed to help parents assess their child's language skills in various aspects, including listening, speaking, reading, writing, communication, math, and problem-solving.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer according to your child's skills at home.
2. For each question, choose the corresponding answer from the provided options.
3. Make sure to complete all questions, as they all contribute to a better understanding of your child's language skills.
4. When you have completed all questions, click the "Submit" button to save your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Details (`survey_details`)**: This field allows you to provide any additional comments or information about the survey.
* **What is the child's language survey level? (`language_survey`)**: This question asks you to rate the child's language skills level on a scale from "High" to "Low".
* **What is the child's listening skills level? (`listening_skills`)**: This question asks you to rate the child's listening skills level on a scale from "High" to "Low".
* **What is the child's speaking skills level? (`speaking_skills`)**: This question asks you to rate the child's speaking skills level on a scale from "High" to "Low".
* **What is the child's reading skills level? (`reading_skills`)**: This question asks you to rate the child's reading skills level on a scale from "High" to "Low".
* **What is the child's writing skills level? (`writing_skills`)**: This question asks you to rate the child's writing skills level on a scale from "High" to "Low".
* **What is the child's communication skills level? (`communication_skills`)**: This question asks you to rate the child's communication skills level on a scale from "High" to "Low".
* **What is the child's math skills level? (`math_skills`)**: This question asks you to rate the child's math skills level on a scale from "High" to "Low".
* **What is the child's problem-solving skills level? (`problem_solving`)**: This question asks you to rate the child's problem-solving skills level on a scale from "Yes" to "No".
* **Overall language level (`overall_language_level`)**: This question asks you to rate the child's overall language level on a scale from "High" to "Low".

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering each question, as this will help provide a true representation of your child's language skills.
* If you are unsure or have questions about any of the questions, feel free to contact us for clarification.
* The data collected from this form will be used to inform educational and support planning for your child, so please answer as thoroughly as possible.
