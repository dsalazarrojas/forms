# digital_vs_print_reader_preferences_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form, "digital_vs_print_reader_preferences_questionnaire," is designed to gather information about an individual's reading habits and preferences. The goal is to collect data on how often, where, and in what format individuals read, and the reasons behind their preferred reading methods.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the following questions to help us understand your reading habits.
2. Select the options that best match your reading frequency and habits.
3. Choose one preferred format from the provided options.
4. Select all the devices you prefer for reading.
5. Provide any additional comments or explanations for your preferences.
6. If you'd like to provide more information, feel free to add any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Introduction**
* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `Note`, required: false): This is a placeholder field where you can write a brief introduction or message to yourself or to us.

### **Reading Habits**
* **reading-habits** (`reading habits`, `Select Multiple`, required: false): This field asks about your reading frequency and devices used for reading. Please select all the options that apply to you.
  * **Reading in print**: You read physical books.
  * **Reading on e-reader**: You read on an e-reader device.
  * **Reading on tablet**: You read on a tablet.
  * **Reading on smartphone**: You read on a smartphone.
  * **Other (please specify)**: You have a different reading device or habit not listed here.

### **Format Preference**
* **format-preference** (`format preference`, `Select One`, required: false): Choose your preferred format for reading:
  * **E-book**: You prefer digital books.
  * **Digital audiobook**: You prefer listening to audiobooks.
  * **Print book**: You prefer physical books.
  * **None of the above**: You have a different preference not listed here.

### **Frequency of Reading**
* **frequency-of-reading** (`Frequency of Reading`, `Select Multiple`, required: false): Indicate how often you read:
  * **Once a week or less often**: You read once a week or less.
  * **2-3 times a week**: You read 2-3 times a week.
  * **1-2 times a day**: You read 1-2 times a day.
  * **3 or more times a day**: You read 3 or more times a day.

### **Preferred Device**
* **preferred-device** (`Preferred Device`, `Select One`, required: false): Choose your preferred reading device:
  * **E-reader**: You prefer reading on an e-reader device.
  * **Tablet**: You prefer reading on a tablet.
  * **Smartphone**: You prefer reading on a smartphone.
  * **Laptop**: You prefer reading on a laptop.
  * **None of the above**: You prefer a different device not listed here.

### **Reasons for Preference**
* **reasons-for-preference** (`Reasons for Preference`, `Text`, required: false): Provide a brief explanation for your preferred reading device and format.

### **Additional Comments**
* **additional-comments** (`Additional Comments`, `Text`, required: false): Provide any additional information or comments about your reading habits and preferences.
