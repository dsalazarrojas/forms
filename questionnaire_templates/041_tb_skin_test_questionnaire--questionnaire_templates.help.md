# tb_skin_test_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The tb_skin_test_questionnaire form is designed to collect patient information, test results, treatment plans, and contact details for skin testing purposes. The form consists of five pages, each with a distinct set of fields.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide patient information on **page 1 (Patient Information)**.
2. Enter test results on **page 2 (Test Results)**.
3. Outline the treatment plan on **page 3 (Treatment Plan)**.
4. Identify the healthcare professional involved on **page 4 (Healthcare Professional)**.
5. Enter contact information on **page 5 (Contact Info)**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Patient Information
* **Patient Information (patient_info)** (`text`, optional): Enter the patient's name, age, and any other relevant details as required.

### Page 2: Test Results
* **Test Results (test_results)** (`text`, optional): Record the test results, including any notable skin test findings.

### Page 3: Treatment Plan
* **Treatment Plan (treatment_plan)** (`text`, optional): Outline the recommended treatment for the patient's skin condition.

### Page 4: Healthcare Professional
* **Healthcare Professional (healthcare_professional)** (`text`, optional): Identify the healthcare professional involved in the patient's care.

### Page 5: Contact Info
* **Contact Info (contact_info)** (`text`, optional): Enter contact details for further communication or follow-up.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly, as this information will be used for patient care and future reference. If you have any doubts or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out for assistance.
