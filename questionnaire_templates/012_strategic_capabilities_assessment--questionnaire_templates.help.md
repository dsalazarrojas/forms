# Strategic Capabilities Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect strategic information about an organization's current state, goals, and challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer all required fields (if any), which are marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Provide clear and concise answers to each question, using bullet points or short paragraphs as needed.
3. Review and edit your answers before submitting the form.
