# Hair Porosity Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather detailed information about your hair porosity, scalp condition, and preferences for hair care products or services. Please ensure that you have a clear understanding of your hair porosity level and scalp condition before starting this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question honestly, providing as much detail as possible in the required sections.
2.  For sections with multiple options, select all that apply.
3.  For sections with free text fields, provide a brief description of your answer.
4.  For sections with "Yes" or "No" options, select "Yes" if you agree with the statement, and "No" if you do not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hair Porosity Assessment** (`page_1`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide a brief description of your hair porosity level, including any relevant details about your hair type and texture.
* **Porosity** (`hair_porosity`, `number`, required/true): Please select a number that corresponds to your hair porosity level, with 1 being very fine and 5 being very coarse.
* **Porosity Level** (`hair_porosity_level`, `select_one`, required/true): Please select one of the options that best describes your hair porosity level: Very fine, Fine, Medium, Coarse, or Very coarse.
* **Hair Length** (`hair_length`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Please select all that apply to your hair length: Short, Medium, Long.
* **Hair Color** (`hair_color`, `text`, required/true): Please provide a brief description of your hair color.
* **Scalp Condition** (`scalp_condition`, `select_one`, required/true): Please select one of the options that best describes your scalp condition: Normal, Dry, Oily, Itchy, Acne-prone.
* **Conservative Condition** (`conservative_condition`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Please select all that apply to your scalp condition: Normal, Dry, Oily, Itchy, Acne-prone.
* **Scalp Condition Duration** (`scalp_condition_duration`, `number`, required/true): Please select a number that corresponds to the duration of your scalp condition.
* **Hair Porosity Insights** (`hair_porosity_insights`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Please select all that apply to your hair porosity insights: Hair porosity affects my scalp comfort, Has an impact on overall scalp health, Is influenced by my scalp type, Is affected by styling products, Other.
* **Hair Porosity Recommendations** (`hair_porosity_recommendations`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Please select all that apply to your hair porosity recommendations: Use sulfate-free products, Use a gentle scalp massager, Keep hair out of the face, Other.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required/true): Please provide any additional comments or concerns you have about your hair porosity or scalp condition.
* **Hair Porosity Consultant** (`hair_porosity_consultant`, `text`, required/true): Please provide the name of your hair porosity consultant.
* **Hair Porosity Consultant Phone** (`hair_porosity_consultant_phone`, `text`, required/true): Please provide the phone number of your hair porosity consultant.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/true): Please provide your email address.
* **Hair Porosity Consultant Address** (`hair_porosity_consultant_address`, `text`, required/true): Please provide the address of your hair porosity consultant.
* **Scalp Images** (`scalp_images`, `note`, required/true): Please attach any relevant scalp images or provide a brief description of your scalp condition.
* **Scalp Images Description** (`scalp_images_description`, `text`, required/true): Please provide a brief description of the attached scalp images or the information they contain.
* **Product Guidance** (`product_guidance`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Please select all that apply to your product guidance: Yes, No.
* **Scalp Images Link** (`scalp_images_link`, `text`, required/true): Please provide a link to the attached scalp images or a description of how to access them.
* **Video Consultation** (`video_consultation`, `time`, required/true): Please select a time for your video consultation.
* **Video Consultation Link** (`video_consultation_link`, `text`, required/true): Please provide a link to your video consultation or a description of how to access it.
* **Product Guidance Link** (`product_guidance_link`, `text`, required/true): Please provide a link to your product guidance or a description of how to access it.
* **Hair Porosity Service** (`hair_porosity_service`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Please select all that apply to your hair porosity service: Yes, No, Other.
* **Hair Porosity Service Link** (`hair_porosity_service_link`, `text`, required/true): Please provide a link to your hair porosity service or a description of how to access it.
* **Video Record** (`video_record`, `time`, required/true): Please select a time for your video record.
* **Video Record Link** (`video_record_link`, `text`, required/true): Please provide a link to your video record or a description of how to access it.
* **Product Guidance Link** (`product_guidance_link`, `text`, required/true): Please provide a link to your product guidance or a description of how to access it.
