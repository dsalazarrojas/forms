# Virtual Event Public Engagement Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather feedback from attendees of virtual events, focusing on their experience, satisfaction, and engagement with the event and its speakers. It helps event organizers and speakers to understand their audience's needs and preferences, enabling them to improve future events and provide better services. Please fill out this form only if you have attended a virtual event that we have organized.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the pages of the form carefully to understand what information is required and which questions apply to your experience.
2.  Fill out the form by selecting the relevant options for each page according to your experiences and feelings about the virtual event you attended.
3.  Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated as required).
4.  Use the provided options as a starting point; feel free to add any additional comments or thoughts you have about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Session Feedback
**Session Feedback** (Session Feedback, note, required: false)
*   A space to provide any comments or feedback about the session, such as the content, presentation, or any issues that you experienced.

### Event Attendance
**Event Attendance** (Event Attendance, select_multiple, required: false)
*   Please select all the options that describe your attendance at the virtual event.

### Session Quality
**Session Quality** (Session Quality, number, required: false)
*   Rate the quality of the session(s) you attended, from 1 to 3.

### Session Engagement
**Session Engagement** (Session Engagement, number, required: false)
*   Rate your engagement with the session(s) you attended, from 1 to 3.

### Event Satisfaction
**Event Satisfaction** (Event Satisfaction, select_one, required: false)
*   Please select one of the options to rate your overall satisfaction with the event.

### Speaker Satisfaction
**Speaker Satisfaction** (Speaker Satisfaction, select_one, required: false)
*   Please select one of the options to rate your overall satisfaction with the speakers of the virtual event.

### Speaker Quality
**Speaker Quality** (Speaker Quality, select_multiple, required: false)
*   Please select all the options that describe the quality of the speakers, such as their presentation, communication style, or ability to engage the audience.

### Event Improvement
**Event Improvement** (Event Improvement, select_one, required: false)
*   Please select one of the options to suggest how we could improve the event.

### Overall Satisfaction
**Overall Satisfaction** (Overall Satisfaction, select_one, required: false)
*   Please select one of the options to rate your overall satisfaction with your experience at the virtual event.

Tips:

*   Please be as specific and detailed as possible in your responses.
*   Your feedback is invaluable in helping us to improve our virtual events.
