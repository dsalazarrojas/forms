# overview-of-policing-systems-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on the current state of policing systems in various regions, including police models, public trust, reform priorities, and community involvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Select the relevant police model(s) that apply to your region.
2. Choose the level of public trust in the police force in your area.
3. Indicate the reform priorities that are most relevant to your region.
4. Enter the community involvement level in your region.
5. Select the methods of community engagement that are most relevant to your region.
6. Choose the types of collaboration between organizations that are most relevant to your region.
7. Select the methods of collaboration with the community that are most relevant to your region.
8. Indicate the methods of communication with the community that are most relevant to your region.
9. Select the methods of data collection that are most relevant to your region.
10. Select the level of public input that is most relevant to your region.
11. Select the level of other input that is most relevant to your region.
12. Choose the types of public consultation that are most relevant to your region.
13. Indicate the level of community satisfaction with the police force in your area.
14. Select the level of public acceptance of the police force in your area.
15. Select the types of collaboration between internal units that are most relevant to your region.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Police Models**: Choose the police model(s) that best describe the policing system in your region. Please select all that apply.
* **Public Trust**: Choose the level of public trust in the police force in your region.
* **Reform Priorities**: Indicate the reform priorities that are most relevant to your region. Please select all that apply.
* **Community Involvement**: Enter the level of community involvement in your region.
* **Community Engagement**: Select the methods of community engagement that are most relevant to your region. Please select all that apply.
* **Collaboration Between Organizations**: Choose the types of collaboration between organizations that are most relevant to your region. Please select all that apply.
* **Collaboration With Community**: Select the methods of collaboration with the community that are most relevant to your region. Please select all that apply.
* **Communication With Community**: Indicate the methods of communication with the community that are most relevant to your region. Please select all that apply.
* **Data Collection**: Choose the methods of data collection that are most relevant to your region. Please select all that apply.
* **Public Input**: Select the level of public input that is most relevant to your region.
* **Other Input**: Select the level of other input that is most relevant to your region.
* **Public Consultation**: Choose the types of public consultation that are most relevant to your region. Please select all that apply.
* **Community Satisfaction**: Indicate the level of community satisfaction with the police force in your area.
* **Public Acceptance**: Select the level of public acceptance of the police force in your area.
* **Collaboration Between Internal Units**: Choose the types of collaboration between internal units that are most relevant to your region. Please select all that apply.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are completed.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult the region's police force or relevant authorities.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact the form administrators.
- The form is designed to be completed by one person per region.
