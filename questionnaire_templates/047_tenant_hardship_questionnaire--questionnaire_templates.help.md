# Tenant Hardship Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from tenants about their financial hardship situation, including business details, financial aid requests, and government programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the requested information for each field. You can choose one or multiple options for select multiple fields. Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure that we can process your request efficiently.
