# Client Management Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your knowledge and skills in client management, covering various aspects of communication, conflict handling, onboarding, and reflection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name (1)** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as a participant in this form.
* **Department (2)** (`department`, `text`, required): Enter your department or role in your organization.
* **Communication (3)** (`section_communication`, `note`, optional): This section is for your thoughts on communication with clients. You can add any relevant information or comments here.
* **Late Meeting Strategy (4)** (`late_meeting_strategy`, `select_one`, required): Select the most effective way to handle a client who is late for a meeting.
	+ Reschedule immediately
	+ Wait 15 mins then call
	+ Send a polite reminder after 5 mins
	+ Ignore it
* **Bad News Delivery (5)** (`bad_news_delivery`, `select_one`, required): Choose the best way to deliver bad news about a project delay.
	+ Via email
	+ Wait for next scheduled call
	+ Immediate phone call with a solution
	+ Let the manager handle it
* **Conflict Handling (6)** (`section_conflict`, `note`, optional): This section is for your thoughts on handling conflicts with clients. You can add any relevant information or comments here.
* **Unhappy Client First Step (7)** (`unhappy_client_first_step`, `select_one`, required): Select the first step when a client is unhappy with a deliverable.
	+ Explain why they are wrong
	+ Listen and validate their concerns
	+ Offer a full refund
	+ Redo the work immediately
* **Scope Creep Handling (8)** (`scope_creep_handling`, `select_one`, required): Choose the best way to handle a "scope creep" request.
	+ Do it for free
	+ Say no firmly
	+ Discuss budget and timeline implications
	+ Ignore the request
* **Onboarding (9)** (`section_onboarding`, `note`, optional): This section is for your thoughts on onboarding clients. You can add any relevant information or comments here.
* **Discovery Call Goal (10)** (`discovery_call_goal`, `select_one`, required): Select the primary goal of the first discovery call.
	+ Close the sale
	+ Understand client needs
	+ Showcase the team
	+ Get the deposit
* **Update Frequency (11)** (`update_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Choose how often you should provide project status updates.
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Only when asked
	+ At project milestones
* **Final Reflection (12)** (`section_reflection`, `note`, optional): This section is for your final thoughts and reflections. You can add any relevant information or comments here.
* **Challenging Area (13)** (`challenging_area`, `text`, optional): If you have any thoughts on the most challenging area of client management, please enter them here.
