The purpose of this Hearing History Questionnaire is to collect information about a patient's hearing history, including any hearing loss they may have experienced. This form is designed to help healthcare providers understand the patient's hearing history, which can be useful for diagnosis and treatment planning. This information can help identify any potential underlying conditions that may be contributing to the patient's hearing loss.

The form will not be completed by patients themselves, but rather by healthcare providers who are collecting information about the patient's medical history.

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the patient's medical history and identify any hearing loss or tinnitus they may have experienced.
2. Select the relevant options from the drop-down menu to indicate the type of hearing loss or tinnitus the patient has experienced.
3. Enter the duration of the hearing loss or tinnitus in the corresponding field.
4. Provide a brief description of the hearing loss or tinnitus in the text fields provided.
5. Select the relevant options for any other conditions that may be present.
6. Enter the dates and times of any significant events in the patient's hearing history.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when completing the form.
* Review the patient's medical history and take note of any significant events.
* Use the drop-down menus to select the most accurate options.
* Enter dates and times in the format MM/DD/YYYY HH:MM:SS.
