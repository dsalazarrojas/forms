# Stool Consistency Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This questionnaire is designed to assess an individual's bowel habits, symptoms, and intake and follow-up frequencies and durations. The goal is to collect comprehensive data on bowel movements, symptoms, and research-related aspects of bowel health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions on each page according to your experiences and habits.
2. Choose the options that best describe your bowel habits, symptoms, and other relevant details.
3. Enter any additional comments or concerns you may have in the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Review and provide any follow-up information as requested in the "Follow-up Questions" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intake Page**: This page provides an opportunity to report your typical bowel habits and frequency of bowel movements.
	+ **Bowel Habits**: Choose one or more of the options that best describe your bowel habits, such as "Very loose", "Loose", "Normal", "Firm", or "Firm" stools.
	+ **Stool Consistency**: Select the type of stool consistency that best describes your typical bowel movements.
* **Bowel Habits**: This field provides a more detailed overview of your bowel habits. Please select one or more of the options that best describe your bowel movements.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or concerns you may have about your bowel movements or symptoms.
* **Follow-up Questions**: Answer the follow-up questions to provide further information about your bowel movements and habits.
* **Research Questions**: Answer the research questions to provide more detailed information about your bowel habits and symptoms.
* **Intake Frequency**: Select the frequency of your bowel movements, such as "Daily", "Every other day", "3-4 times a week", or "Less than 3 times a week".
* **Intake Duration**: Enter the duration of your bowel movements in hours.
* **Follow-up Frequency**: Select the frequency of your follow-up bowel movements, such as "1-2 months", "Every 3-6 months", or "Never".
* **Follow-up Duration**: Enter the duration of your follow-up bowel movements in hours.
* **Research Frequency**: Select the frequency of your bowel movements for research purposes, such as "Daily", "Every other day", or "Weekly".
* **Research Duration**: Enter the duration of your bowel movements for research purposes in hours.
* **Bowel Movement Frequency**: Select the frequency of your bowel movements, such as "Daily", "Every other day", "3-4 times a week", or "Less than 3 times a week".
* **Bowel Movement Duration**: Enter the duration of your bowel movements in hours.
* **Bowel Movement Frequency 24h**: Enter the frequency of your bowel movements in 24 hours.
* **Bowel Movement Duration 24h**: Enter the duration of your bowel movements in 24 hours.
* **Bowel Movement Frequency 12h**: Enter the frequency of your bowel movements in 12 hours.
* **Bowel Movement Duration 12h**: Enter the duration of your bowel movements in 12 hours.
* **Follow-up Notes**: Enter any additional follow-up notes or comments.
* **Research Notes**: Enter any additional research notes or comments.
