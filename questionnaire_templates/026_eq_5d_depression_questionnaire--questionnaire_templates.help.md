# EQ 5D Depression Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The EQ 5D Depression Questionnaire is a self-reporting tool designed to measure the level of depression in individuals. It is composed of eight questions, each addressing different aspects of mental health. Please take your time and answer the questions as honestly as possible.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. For each question, select the answer that best describes your feelings or experiences.
2. Take the time you need to answer each question, as there are no time limits.
3. Please answer all questions, even if you are unsure or uncomfortable with some of the options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This section serves as a general introduction to the questionnaire and is not scored.
* **Anxiety** (`anxiety`, `number`, required/optional): This question asks about feelings of anxiety or nervousness. Please select the number that best describes how often you have felt anxious in the past two weeks.
* **Vitality** (`vitality`, `number`, required/optional): This question is about feelings of energy and motivation. Please select the number that best describes how often you have felt alive and active in the past week.
* **Fear** (`fear`, `number`, required/optional): This question is about feelings of fear or apprehension. Please select the number that best describes how often you have felt afraid or fearful in the past two weeks.
* **Sleep** (`sleep`, `number`, required/optional): This question is about your sleep patterns and quality. Please select the number that best describes your average sleep duration in the past week.
* **Self** (`self`, `number`, required/optional): This question is about your sense of purpose and fulfillment. Please select the number that best describes how often you have felt useful and fulfilled in the past week.
* **Social** (`social`, `number`, required/optional): This question is about social activities and interactions. Please select the number that best describes how often you have had contact with friends or family members in the past week.
* **Depression** (`depression`, `number`, required/optional): This question is about feelings of sadness or emptiness. Please select the number that best describes how often you have felt depressed or sad in the past two weeks.

## Tips

* Take your time and answer each question carefully.
* If you are unsure or uncomfortable with some of the options, please answer as honestly as possible.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please consult with a qualified professional.
