# Environmental Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Environmental Impact Survey is designed to gather information about your perspective on the environmental impact of a specific action or organization. Your feedback will help us understand your views and opinions on various aspects of environmental performance.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please answer all questions thoroughly and honestly.
* You can submit the form once you have completed all the fields.
* If you need assistance or have any questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How did you hear about us:** Please enter the source that directed you to this survey.
* **Your Name**: Enter your name as you would like to be known.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (if you want to be contacted for any follow-up questions).
* **Company**: Enter the name of your organization or company.
* **Department**: If applicable, enter the name of your department.
* **Role**: Enter your role or position within the organization.
* **How long have you known us**: Enter the length of time you have known this organization.
* **What do you do**: A brief description of your job or responsibilities.
* **Company Size**: Enter the approximate number of employees in your organization.
* **Industry**: Enter the industry or sector your organization operates in.
* **Location**: Enter the location of your organization.
* **Company Type**: Enter the type of company you represent (e.g., public or private).
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Departmentalized**: Enter any additional information about your department or team.
* **How often do we maintain contact with your organization**: Enter the frequency of contact between our organization and yours.
* **How do you rate the environmental impact**: Rate the environmental impact of our organization, with options: Good, Fair, or Very Low.
* **How do you rate the environmental impact**: Rate the environmental impact, with options: Very Low, Low, Medium, High, or Very High.
* **What do you believe is the main reason we should be investing in environmental impact**: Select all reasons that apply (e.g., reduce carbon footprint, improve public health, create jobs, reduce costs).
* **Do you agree with the statements below**: Select all statements that you agree with.
* **Do you agree with the statements below 2**: Select all statements that you agree with.
* **Do you agree with the statements below 3**: Select all statements that you agree with.
* **How do you rate the quality**: Rate the quality of our environmental performance, with options: Very Low, Low, Medium, High, Very High.
* **What would you like to see more of**: Select all options that you would like to see more of (e.g., more sustainability, more community involvement, more transparency, more responsibility).
* **How do you rate the effectiveness**: Rate the effectiveness of our environmental performance, with options: Very Uneffective, Ineffective, Somewhat Effective, Effective, Very Effective.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about our environmental performance.
