# Scene Transition Effects Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form, Scene Transition Effects Survey, is designed to collect information about your experience with scene transition effects in our product. Your responses will help us understand how you currently use scene transition effects and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question thoroughly, and provide as much detail as possible.
2. If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the "Editor Survey Response" field to share them.
3. For the "Project Type" field, select one of the provided options that best describes your project type.
4. The "Transition Usage" field requires you to provide a number between 0 and 100, indicating how frequently you use scene transition effects in your project.
5. In the "Satisfaction Level" field, provide a number between 0 and 100, indicating how satisfied you are with our scene transition effects.
6. Finally, use the "Improvement Needs" field to describe any areas where you would like to see improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Editor Survey Response** (editor_survey_response, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions about our scene transition effects.
* **Project Type** (project_type, select_one, optional): Select the option that best describes the type of project you are working on.
* **Transition Usage** (transition_usage, number, optional): Provide a number between 0 and 100 to indicate how frequently you use scene transition effects in your project.
* **Satisfaction Level** (satisfaction_level, number, optional): Provide a number between 0 and 100 to indicate how satisfied you are with our scene transition effects.
* **Improvement Needs** (improvement_needs, text, optional): Describe any areas where you would like to see improvement in our scene transition effects.

## Tips

* Answer honestly and thoroughly to help us better understand your experience.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey! Your feedback is invaluable to us.
