# Healthcare Outpatient Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from patients on the quality of care provided by our outpatient services. It helps us understand your experience and identify areas for improvement. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and let us know about your experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. For questions with rating options (e.g., how_would_you_rate_the_nurses), please select a number between 1 (very poor) and 5 (excellent) to indicate your level of satisfaction.
3. For questions with text fields (e.g., suggestion), type your answers in the provided space.
4. For the contact info question, select 'True' to share your contact information with us for follow-up purposes.
5. Make sure to fill in your email address and phone number (including area code) in the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (1, text, required): This is where you will start by reading a brief introduction about our outpatient services.
* **How would you rate the nurses?** (2, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the care provided by our nurses, with 1 being very poor and 5 being excellent.
* **How would you rate the doctors?** (3, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the care provided by our doctors, with 1 being very poor and 5 being excellent.
* **How would you rate the clinics?** (4, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the overall clinic experience, with 1 being very poor and 5 being excellent.
* **How would you rate the services?** (5, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the services provided, with 1 being very poor and 5 being excellent.
* **How would you rate the clinics?** (6, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the clinics, with 1 being very poor and 5 being excellent. (This question is a duplicate of question 4, please note that your feedback on this question will not affect your overall rating.)
* **Do you have any suggestions for improvement?** (7, text, optional): Share any suggestions or ideas you have on how we can improve our outpatient services.
* **Do you agree to share your contact info for follow-up?** (8, select_one, required): Select 'True' if you are willing to share your contact information with us for follow-up purposes.
* **Email** (9, email, required): Please provide your email address for us to follow up with you.
* **Phone** (10, text, required): Please provide your phone number, including area code, for us to follow up with you.
