# Neuropathy Symptom Score Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about neuropathy symptoms from patients, helping healthcare professionals to assess the condition and develop a treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions and questions carefully.
2. Answer the questions honestly.
3. Select the options that best describe your symptoms.
4. Complete all fields, if possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **neuropathy_symptom_score_questionnaire**: This is the title of the form.
* **patient_information**: Select the level of your symptom:
	+ 0 - None
	+ 1 - Mild
	+ 2 - Moderate
	+ 3 - Severe
* **numbness_score_1_2_3**: Report how often you experience numbness in the last 2 weeks:
	+ 0 - None
	+ 1 - Less than half the time
	+ 2 - About half the time
	+ 3 - More than half the time
* **numbness_score_4_5_6**: Report how often you experience numbness in your limbs:
	+ 0 - None
	+ 1 - Less than half the time
	+ 2 - About half the time
	+ 3 - More than half the time
* **pain_score_1_2_3**: Report the level of pain in your limbs:
	+ 0 - None
	+ 1 - Mild
	+ 2 - Moderate
	+ 3 - Severe
* **pain_score_4_5_6**: Report the level of pain in your limbs:
	+ 0 - None
	+ 1 - Mild
	+ 2 - Moderate
	+ 3 - Severe
* **weakness_score_1_2_3**: Report the level of weakness in your limbs:
	+ 0 - None
	+ 1 - Mild
	+ 2 - Moderate
	+ 3 - Severe
* **weakness_score_4_5_6**: Report the level of weakness in your limbs:
	+ 0 - None
	+ 1 - Mild
	+ 2 - Moderate
	+ 3 - Severe

## Tips

- Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
- If you need help or clarification on any question, please do not hesitate to ask your healthcare professional.
- Completing this form will help healthcare professionals understand your symptoms and develop a treatment plan tailored to your needs.
