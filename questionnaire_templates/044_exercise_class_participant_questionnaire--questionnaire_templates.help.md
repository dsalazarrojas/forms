# exercise_class_participant_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The exercise class participant questionnaire helps trainers and instructors gather essential information from participants, ensuring a safe, engaging, and effective exercise class experience. It includes sections for participant details, exercise schedules, workout plans, goals, comments, and trainer notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email, and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your exercise class schedule, specifying the class dates, times, and frequencies you plan to attend.
3. Share your workout goals, whether it's to lose weight, improve cardiovascular health, or enhance flexibility.
4. Offer any additional comments or remarks you'd like to share with the trainer.
5. Provide any relevant notes, such as medical conditions or exercise-related goals.
6. For trainers: Enter your training qualifications and experience in the provided field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you can enter your contact information, such as your name and email address.
* **Exercise Class Schedule** (`exercise_class_schedule`, `text`, required: false): Share your schedule for attending the exercise class, including dates, times, and frequencies.
* **Participant Workout Plan** (`participant_workout_plan`, `text`, required: false): Describe your current workout routine, including exercises, activities, and any specific goals or objectives.
* **Participant Goals** (`participant_goals`, `text`, required: false): Specify your personal fitness goals, such as weight loss, improved cardiovascular health, or increased strength.
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or remarks you have regarding your participation in the exercise class.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, `text`, required: false): Share any relevant medical conditions, injuries, or other notes that may impact your exercise participation.
* **Trainer Information** (`trainer_information`, `text`, required: false): Trainers: Enter your professional qualifications, experience, and any relevant certifications.
* **Trainer Comments** (`trainer_comments`, `text`, required: false): Trainers: Add any additional comments or feedback about the participant's progress or needs.
