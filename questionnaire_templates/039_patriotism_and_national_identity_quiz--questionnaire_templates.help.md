# patriotism_and_national_identity_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect opinions and thoughts on patriotism and national identity. It is intended to gather information on the values, responsibilities, and challenges associated with these topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read the introduction on the first page.
* Answer each of the six questions in the format specified (e.g., text, select multiple, select one).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is patriotism to you?**: This question asks you to describe your personal understanding of patriotism. Please provide a brief written response.
* **How do you define national identity?**: This question is asking about your thoughts on what makes you feel connected to your country. Again, please provide a brief written response.
* **How do you think your country's history and culture shape your sense of national identity?**: This question is asking for your opinions on how your country's past influences your current sense of national identity. Please select one or multiple options that best answer this question.
* **How do you think your national identity influences your relationships with others?**: Similar to the previous question, this one is asking about the impact of your national identity on your relationships. Select one or multiple options that best answer this question.
* **What are the responsibilities that come with being a national?**: This question is inquiring about your thoughts on what duties or obligations you feel come with being a part of a nation. Select one option that you feel best answers this question.
* **What are some challenges faced by individuals in maintaining their national identity?**: This question seeks your insights on difficulties people might encounter in maintaining a strong national identity. Please provide a brief written response.

## Tips
Please answer these questions as truthfully as possible, and feel free to provide any additional information or thoughts that you think are relevant to the topic.
