# Financial Budgeting Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant to collect financial information from clients, including income, expenses, and debt details. It is designed to help clients provide a clear picture of their financial situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the following steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand each section.
2. Provide accurate and honest answers to each question.
3. Complete all required fields.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Income Details**: Please enter your total monthly income.
* **Financial Goals**: Choose the financial goals that apply to you. You can select multiple options.
* **Income 1**: Please enter your monthly income from the first source (e.g., salary, investments, etc.).
* **Income 2**: Please enter your monthly income from the second source (e.g., another salary, investments, etc.).
* **Expenses 1**: Please enter your monthly expenses for the first category (e.g., rent, utilities, etc.).
* **Expenses 2**: Please enter your monthly expenses for the second category (e.g., transportation, food, etc.).
* **Debt 1**: Please enter your total monthly debt (e.g., credit cards, loans, etc.).
* **Debt 2**: Please enter your total monthly debt (e.g., other credit cards, loans, etc.).
* **Financial Goals 1**: Choose the first financial goal that applies to you.
* **Financial Goals 2**: Choose the second financial goal that applies to you.
* **Financial Goals 3**: Choose the third financial goal that applies to you.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and honest answers to each question.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your financial advisor for guidance.
* Please complete all required fields before submitting the form.
