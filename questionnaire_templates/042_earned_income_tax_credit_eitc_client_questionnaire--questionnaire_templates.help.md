# Earned Income Tax Credit (EITC) Client Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients about their earned income tax credit (EITC) eligibility and benefits for the 2018 tax year.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions on each page of the form in order. The form is divided into multiple pages with clear instructions and labels.
2. Please enter your information in the format required. For example, dates should be in MM/DD/YYYY format.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
4. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name (1)** (`client_name`, text, Optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **First Name (2)** (`first_name`, text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (3)** (`last_name`, text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Address (4)** (`address`, text, Optional): Enter your address as it appears on your identification.
* **City (5)** (`city`, text, Optional): Enter the city where you live.
* **State (6)** (`state`, select_one, Required): Select the state where you live.
* **Zip Code (7)** (`zip_code`, text, Optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Phone (8)** (`phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email (9)** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Date of Birth (10)** (`date_of_birth`, date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Income Earned 2018 (11)** (`income_earned_2018`, number, Required): Enter the income you earned in 2018.
* **Dependents 2018 (12)** (`dependents_2018`, number, Required): Enter the number of dependents you had in 2018.
* **Earned Income 2018 (13)** (`earned_income_2018`, number, Required): Enter the earned income from your employer in 2018.
* **Earned Income Tax Credit 2018 (14)** (`earned_income_tax_credit_2018`, number, Required): Enter the earned income tax credit you are eligible for in 2018.
* **Earned Income Tax Credit Amount 2018 (15)** (`earned_income_tax_credit_2018_amount`, number, Required): Enter the amount of earned income tax credit you are eligible for in 2018.
* **Earned Income Tax Credit Percentage 2018 (16)** (`earned_income_tax_credit_2018_percentage`, number, Required): Enter the percentage of earned income tax credit you are eligible for in 2018.
* **Earned Income Tax Credit Start Date 2018 (17)** (`earned_income_tax_credit_2018_start_date`, date, Required): Enter the start date of the earned income tax credit period in 2018.
* **Earned Income Tax Credit End Date 2018 (18)** (`earned_income_tax_credit_2018_end_date`, date, Required): Enter the end date of the earned income tax credit period in 2018.
* **Earned Income Tax Credit Start Year 2018 (19)** (`earned_income_tax_credit_2018_start_year`, select_one, Required): Select the month when the earned income tax credit period started in 2018.
* **Earned Income Tax Credit End Year 2018 (20)** (`earned_income_tax_credit_2018_end_year`, select_one, Required): Select the month when the earned income tax credit period ended in 2018.
* **Total Earned Income Tax Credit Amount 2018 (21)** (`total_earned_income_tax_credit_amount_2018`, number, Required): Enter the total earned income tax credit amount you are eligible for in 2018.
* **Eligible for EITC (22)** (`is_eligible_for_eitc`, select_one, Required): Select 'True' if you are eligible for the earned income tax credit.
* **EITC Start Date (23)** (`eitc_start_date`, date, Optional): Enter the start date of the earned income tax credit period. This is only visible if you select 'True' for Eligible for EITC.
* **EITC End Date (24)** (`eitc_end_date`, date, Optional): Enter the end date of the earned income tax credit period. This is only visible if you select 'True' for Eligible for EITC.
* **Signature (25)** (`signature`, note, Required): Sign and date this form to confirm your answers.
