# Digital Inclusion Among Low Income Groups Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about digital inclusion among low-income groups. Your responses will help us understand the current state of digital access and the challenges faced by low-income households in accessing and utilizing digital technologies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully, providing the best description of your situation or circumstances.
2. For fields with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask for help or clarification.
4. Please note that this form is designed to be completed by individuals from low-income households, so please answer based on your personal experiences and circumstances.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **household_income** (`household_income`, `number`, required/optional): This question asks about the total monthly income of your household. Please provide the exact amount or a rough estimate, considering all household members' earnings.

* **Household Devices** (`household_devices`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This section lists various devices commonly found in households. Please select all the devices you currently use or have access to, such as smartphones, desktop computers, laptops, smart TVs, gaming consoles, or none of the above.

* **internet_reliability** (`internet_reliability`, `number`, required/optional): This question assesses the reliability of your internet connection. Please rate your internet connection's stability on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "very unreliable" and 5 is "very reliable".

* **digital_barriers** (`digital_barriers`, `select_one`, required/optional): This section asks about the main reasons you face challenges in accessing digital technologies. Please select the barrier that best describes your situation, such as lack of device access, lack of internet access, affordability, literacy, or trust.

* **device_access** (`device_access`, `number`, required/optional): This question investigates your access to digital devices. Please report the number of devices you have access to, such as smartphones, desktop computers, or other devices.
