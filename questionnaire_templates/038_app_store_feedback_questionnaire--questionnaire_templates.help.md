# App Store Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback about your experience with our app on the App Store. Your input will help us understand how you found our app, what features you think are missing, and how we can improve our store listing to make it more appealing to you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the rating question in **What rating did you give us on the App Store?** by choosing a number from 1 to 5 stars.
2. Select the option that best describes how you found our app in **How did you find our app?**.
3. If you searched for our app, type the keywords you used in **If you searched, what keywords did you use?** (optional).
4. Answer the question about the clarity of our app's screenshots in **Did the App Store screenshots clearly explain the app?** by choosing one of the options.
5. Choose whether you think our app's description accurately represents the experience in **Did the app description accurately represent the experience?**.
6. Type in the primary reason you decided to download our app in **What was the primary reason you decided to download?**.
7. Provide suggestions to improve our App Store listing in **Suggestions to improve our App Store listing** (optional).
8. Type in the device you are using to access our app in **What device are you using?**.
9. Select your geographic region in **Your Geographic Region**.
10. If you would give us a 5-star rating, tell us what feature you think would make it happen in **What feature would make you give us a 5-star rating?** (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What rating did you give us on the App Store?**: This question asks for your subjective rating of our app. Please choose a number from 1 to 5 stars that reflects your overall satisfaction with our app.
* **How did you find our app?**: This question helps us understand the marketing channels you used to discover our app. Select the option that best describes how you found our app.
* **If you searched, what keywords did you use?**: If you searched for our app, please type the keywords you used in this field.
* **Did the App Store screenshots clearly explain the app?**: This question evaluates the clarity of our app's screenshots. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **Did the app description accurately represent the experience?**: This question assesses the accuracy of our app's description. Choose the option that best reflects your opinion.
* **What was the primary reason you decided to download?**: This question helps us understand why you downloaded our app. Type in the primary reason that made you decide to download.
* **Suggestions to improve our App Store listing**: If you have any suggestions to improve our store listing, please type them in this field.
* **What device are you using?**: This question helps us understand the device you are using to access our app.
* **Your Geographic Region**: Select the option that best describes your geographic region.
* **What feature would make you give us a 5-star rating?**: If you would give us a 5-star rating, this question helps us understand what feature you think would make it happen. Type in the feature that you think would make a difference.
