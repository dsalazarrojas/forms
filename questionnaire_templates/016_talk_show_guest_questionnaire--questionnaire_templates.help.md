# Talk Show Guest Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from potential talk show guests, making it easier for event organizers to find the best fit for their show.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and any other relevant details in the **Guest Details** field.
2. Choose your preferred topics of discussion or areas of expertise in the **Topics Preferences** field.
3. Indicate your availability for the talk show in the **Availability** field.
4. Provide your consent to participate in the talk show in the **Participation Consent** field.
5. Select the topics you are willing to discuss in the **Guest Topics** field.
6. Fill in your contact information, such as email or phone number, in the **Contact Info** field.
7. Add any additional booking details, if applicable, in the **Booking Details** field.
8. Confirm your booking details, including availability and topics, in the **Confirm Booking** field.
9. Verify your participation consent in the **Confirm Participation Consent** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Details**: This field is for your name and any other relevant details that you'd like to share with the talk show organizers.
* **Topics Preferences**: Choose the topics that you are comfortable discussing or have expertise in.
* **Availability**: Indicate your availability for the talk show, including specific dates and times.
* **Participation Consent**: Confirm that you are willing to participate in the talk show.
* **Guest Topics**: Select the topics you are willing to discuss or have expertise in.
* **Contact Info**: Provide your contact information, such as email or phone number, so that we can reach out to you.
* **Booking Details**: Add any additional details about your booking, if applicable.
* **Confirm Booking**: Verify your booking details, including availability and topics.
* **Confirm Participation Consent**: Confirm your participation consent to ensure that you are willing to participate in the talk show.

## Tips
* Please fill in each field accurately and thoroughly to ensure that we have the necessary information to match you with the best topics and schedule.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* Thank you for your participation in our talk show!
