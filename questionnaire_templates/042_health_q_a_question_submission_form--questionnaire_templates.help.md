# Health Q&A Question Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Health Q&A Question Submission Form is designed to collect health-related question submissions from users. It's essential to use this form for this specific purpose to ensure that the collected data is accurate and relevant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Choose the correct question type for your question (General, Medical, or Social).
2. Enter the question text in the "Question Text" field.
3. If you have another question, select "Form 2" as your question submission form.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Select the date of submission.
7. Select the time of submission (if applicable).
8. Choose whether you want to receive follow-up reminders or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Question Text (Required)**: This is the text of your health-related question.
* **Question Type (Required)**: Select the type of question you're submitting. This will help us categorize and prioritize your question.
* **Question Text 2 (Optional)**: If you have additional information or another question related to your submission, enter it here.
* **Question Type 2 (Optional)**: If you selected "Form 2" as your question submission form, select the type of question you're submitting.
* **Question Submission Form 2 (Optional)**: This field is only applicable if you selected "Form 2" as your question submission form. Select the correct form type for your question.
* **Email (Required)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates or follow-ups.
* **Phone (Required)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you with updates or follow-ups.
* **Submission Date (Required)**: Select the date you're submitting your question.
* **Submission Time (Optional)**: If applicable, select the time you're submitting your question.
* **Follow-up Reminders (Optional)**: Choose whether you want to receive follow-up reminders or not.
