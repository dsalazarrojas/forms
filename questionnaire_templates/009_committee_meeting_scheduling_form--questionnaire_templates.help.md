# Committee Meeting Scheduling - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to schedule a committee meeting with clear and concise information about the meeting details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the Organizer's name, who is responsible for coordinating the meeting dates.
2. Enter the Committee name, such as a specific board or finance committee.
3. Choose the preferred meeting format: In person, Virtual, or Hybrid.
4. List up to three potential dates for the meeting.
5. Select the preferred time of day for the meeting: Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
6. Brevity list the Proposed agenda items to cover during the meeting.
7. Estimate the meeting duration in minutes.
8. (Optional) Mention any Room or technical requirements for the meeting.
9. (Optional) Set an RSVP deadline date for attendees to confirm their attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organizer name**: This field is required and should be filled with the name of the person coordinating the meeting dates. This is the person responsible for finalizing and confirming the meeting schedule.
* **Committee name**: This field is required and should be filled with the specific name of the committee, such as "Board of Directors" or "Finance Committee".
* **Preferred meeting format**: This field is required and should be selected to indicate whether the meeting should be held in person, virtually, or in a hybrid format.
* **Potential meeting dates**: This field is required and should be filled with up to three potential dates for the meeting.
* **Preferred time of day**: This field is required and should be selected to indicate the preferred time of day for the meeting.
* **Proposed agenda items**: This field is optional and should be used to briefly list the topics to be covered during the meeting.
* **Estimated meeting duration**: This field is required and should be filled with the estimated duration of the meeting in minutes.
* **Room or technical requirements**: This field is optional and should be used to mention any specific room or technical requirements for the meeting.
* **RSVP deadline**: This field is optional and should be set to indicate the date by which attendees should confirm their attendance.

Note: The fields marked as "required" are necessary to complete the form and ensure meeting scheduling can proceed.
