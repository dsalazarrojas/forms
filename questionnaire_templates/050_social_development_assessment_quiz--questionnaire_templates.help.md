# Social Development Assessment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about the user's social development, including their name, contact information, and preferences. The form will help assess the user's social status and provide insights into their potential for social growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Enter your First Name**: Please type your first name in the text field provided.
2. **Enter your Last Name**: Please type your last name in the text field provided.
3. **Enter your Email Address**: Please type your email address in the text field. Ensure that your email address is a valid address (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
4. **Enter your Phone Number**: Please type your phone number in the text field.
5. **Choose a Date**: Select a date from the calendar or type in a date in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
6. **Choose a Time**: Select a time from the clock or type in a time in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
7. **Select One**: Choose one option from the dropdown menu:
	* Option 1
	* Option 2
	* Option 3
8. **Select Multiple**: Choose one or more options from the dropdown menu:
	* Option 1
	* Option 2
	* Option 3

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your personal name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Choose or type a date in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Choose or type a time in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, required/optional): Choose one option from the dropdown menu.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose one or more options from the dropdown menu.
