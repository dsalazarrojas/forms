# Personal Training Questionnaire STC - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a tailored workout plan for each individual.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your given name that your parents or you gave you, for example, John.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname, for example, Doe.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you check frequently.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Training Experience**: Describe your background in sports or physical activities, for example, I have been playing soccer for 5 years.
* **Goals**: Describe your goals for this training program, for example, I want to improve my endurance.
* **Training Goals**: Select one or more options that best describe your goals, for example, lose weight, improve flexibility.
* **Preferred Work-out Time**: Enter your preferred time of day to work out, for example, morning.
* **Available Days**: Select the days you are available to work out, for example, Monday, Wednesday, Friday.
* **Available Time**: Enter the time you are available to work out, for example, 6:00 am.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of a person that we can contact in case of an emergency.
