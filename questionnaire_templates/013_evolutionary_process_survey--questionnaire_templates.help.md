This form is a "Evolutionary Process Survey" aimed at gathering information about users' understanding of evolutionary biology, specifically their views on the process of evolution itself. The purpose of this form is to gauge users' familiarity with the fundamental principles of evolution and their understanding of how species change over time. It will help us to better understand users' perspectives on this topic and identify areas where educational resources may be needed.

## Evolutionary Process Survey - Help Guide
## 
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each question carefully and answer according to your understanding of the subject matter.
2. For each question, select the option that best answers your view or understanding.
3. You can skip questions that do not apply to you or your experience.
4. Use the text fields to provide more information or clarification on your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (Page 1)**: This is a note field where you can provide any relevant background information or context about your knowledge of evolutionary biology.
* **evolution_facts (Page 2)**: These are statements about the scientifically proven facts of evolution, such as the process of evolution over time.
  * Select all the options that you agree are factually true about evolution.
* **view_levels (Page 3)**: This question asks about your general view or perception of the evolution process. 
  * Select one option: High, Medium, Low. If unsure, choose "None of the above".
* **knowledge_levels (Page 4)**: This question assesses your knowledge of the evolution process.
  * Select all options that reflect your level of understanding, from High to Low, or "None of the above" if not applicable.
* **views (Page 5)**: This question checks your personal views about evolution.
  * Select one option: Agree, Disagree, or Neither agree nor disagree.
* **educational_background (Page 6)**: This questions inquires about your educational background related to evolution.
  * Select all relevant options about your educational background, from None of the above to Ph.D.
* **evolutionary_process (Page 7)**: This is a text field where you can provide a brief description of how you think the evolutionary process works.
* **evolutionary_process_2 (Page 8)**: This is another text field to provide additional information or clarification about your understanding of the evolutionary process.
* **evolutionary_process_3 (Page 9)**: This is another text field to further elaborate on your views about evolution.
* **evolutionary_process_4 (Page 10)**: This is the last text field to provide any final thoughts or conclusions about the evolutionary process.

## Tips

* Answer each question truthfully and honestly.
* Use clear and concise language in the text fields.
* If unsure about any question, choose the option that best reflects your uncertainty.
* Take your time to complete this form and double-check your answers.
* If you need help or clarification, contact our support team.
