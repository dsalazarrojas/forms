# Content Strategy Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Content Strategy Assessment is a questionnaire designed to help content strategists and marketing teams evaluate their current content marketing practices. This assessment will help you identify areas of improvement and provide valuable insights for informed decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Company Name**: Enter the name of your organization.
2. **Respondent Name**: Enter your full name.
3. **Respondent Role**: Enter your job title.
4. **Assessment Date**: Enter the date of this assessment.
5. **Industry**: Select the industry you are in from the options provided.
6. **Company Size**: Select the number of employees in your company.
7. **Current Content Team Size**: Select the number of people on your content team.
8. **Primary Content Goals**: Select all the main content objectives your team has, such as Brand Awareness, Lead Generation, etc.
9. **Content Planning Process**: Choose how you plan your content, whether it's formal, informal, or ad-hoc.
10. **Content Calendar Used**: Select whether you use a content calendar.
11. **Publishing Frequency**: Choose how often you publish content.
12. **Content Types Produced**: Select all the types of content you create, such as Blog Posts, Social Media Posts, Videos, etc.
13. **Distribution Channels**: Select all the channels where you publish your content, such as Company Website, Blog, Facebook, etc.
14. **Content Marketing Budget**: Select your annual content budget.
15. **Content Tools Used**: Select all the tools you use for content creation, such as Content Management System, Social Media Scheduler, etc.
16. **Measurement Practices**: Choose how you measure content success.
17. **KPIs Tracked**: Select all the metrics you track, such as Website Traffic, Engagement Rates, etc. (Note: This field is not mandatory)
18. **Biggest Challenges**: Select all the main content challenges you face.
19. **Content Quality Rating**: Rate your content quality.
20. **Content Consistency Rating**: Rate how consistent your publishing is.
21. **Audience Understanding**: Rate how well you know your audience.
22. **Content Gaps**: Provide any gaps you see in your content strategy.
23. **Improvement Priorities**: Describe what you want to improve most.
24. **Additional Comments**: Provide any other information about your content strategy.
