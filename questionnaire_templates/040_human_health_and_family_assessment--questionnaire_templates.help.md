# Human Health And Family Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about an individual's health status, family history, and other relevant factors. It's essential for healthcare providers to understand these factors to provide personalized care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and ensure you understand the questions and options provided.
2. Answer all required questions (indicated by a asterisk \*). For optional questions, select the relevant information or choose an option that best represents your situation.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Health Status** (`health_status`, `text`, \*:): This field is used to describe your current health status. Please provide a concise but thorough explanation of your health status, including any relevant medical conditions or concerns.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, `text`, \*:): This field asks about the health history of your family members. Please provide a brief overview of any significant health issues or concerns in your family.
* **Lifestyle Factors** (`lifestyle_factors`, `text`, ): This field is used to gather information about your lifestyle habits that may impact your health. Please provide details about your exercise habits, diet, smoking, or other relevant lifestyle factors.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, \*:): Please select one of the options that best describes your consent for treatment or participation in medical research.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `date`, ): This field asks for your contact details, including your contact number and address. Please provide this information accurately to ensure we can reach you for follow-up or further communication.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, ): This field is used to gather information about your past medical history, including any surgeries, illnesses, or other relevant health events.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `select_multiple`, ): Please select any allergies or sensitivities you have, such as food or medication allergies, or environmental sensitivities.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `select_multiple`, ): Select any medical conditions you have been diagnosed with, such as diabetes, hypertension, or other chronic conditions.

## Tips

* Be as specific and thorough as possible when answering each question.
* If you're unsure about any question, please consult with a healthcare professional or seek guidance.
* Ensure you answer all required questions to ensure accurate and complete information.
* If you have any concerns or need help with the form, please don't hesitate to reach out to our support team.
