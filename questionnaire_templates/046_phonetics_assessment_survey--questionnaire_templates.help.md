# phonetics_assessment_survey - Help Guide

This form is designed to collect phonetics-related information from speakers to assess their language skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your title for the phonetics assessment.
2.  Describe your language background.
3.  Rate your pronunciation and language proficiency.
4.  Indicate whether you have audio samples.
5.  Add any comments.
6.  Upload background audio samples if applicable.
7.  Upload audio samples of your pronunciation.
8.  Indicate whether you'll upload background audio samples.
9.  Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Phonetics Assessment Survey (1)**: Enter a title for the assessment you're undergoing.
* **Language Background (2)**: Describe your language background, including your native or non-native language status.
* **Pronunciation Ratings (3)**: Rate your pronunciation, choosing from "Good" or "Fair".
* **Language Proficiency (4)**: Indicate your language proficiency, choosing from "Yes" or "No".
* **Audio Sample (5)**: Indicate whether you have an audio sample to provide.
* **Comments (6)**: Add any additional comments or remarks related to your phonetics assessment.
* **Background Audio Uploads (7)**: If you have background audio samples, indicate that you'll upload them.
* **Pronunciation Audio Uploads (8)**: If you have audio samples of your pronunciation, indicate that you'll upload them.
* **Background Audio Uploads (9)**: Indicate whether you'll upload background audio samples.
* **Submit (10)**: Once you've completed all the fields, click "Submit" to finish the form.
