# New Graduate Nurse Interview Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Graduate Nurse Interview Questionnaire is a form designed to gather information about a candidate's background, experience, and skills to determine their eligibility for a specific job posting.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Please fill out the form in a quiet and distraction-free environment.
*   Ensure that you answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
*   If you are unsure about any question, you may leave it blank or provide as much information as you can.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Job Posting Details** (`job_posting_details`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect the details of the job posting that you are applying for. Please provide a brief description of the job and any relevant information that may be useful for the interviewer to understand your application.
*   **Candidate Contact Info** (`candidate_contact_info`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect your contact information, such as your name, email, and phone number. Please ensure that your contact information is up-to-date and accurate.
*   **Education Background** (`education_background`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect information about your educational background, including your highest level of education and any relevant academic achievements.
*   **Experience Confidence Level** (`experience_confidence_level`, number, required: false): This field is used to gauge your confidence level in your experience. Please select a number between 0 and 100 to indicate your confidence level.
*   **Clinical Experience** (`clinical_experience`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect information about your clinical experience, including any relevant work history or rotations you have completed.
*   **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to select your highest level of education, which may include High School, Diploma, Associate's, Bachelor's, Master's, or Doctorate.
*   **Years of Education** (`years_of_education`, number, required: false): This field is used to collect the number of years you have spent in education.
*   **Nursing License** (`nursing_license`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to select whether you have an active, active and expired, or inactive nursing license.
*   **Certification** (`certification`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to select any relevant certifications you have, which may include BLS, ACLS, PALS, or None.
*   **Confidence Level** (`confidence_level`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to gauge your confidence level in leadership, communication, time management, and problem-solving areas.
*   **Skills Confidence Level** (`skills_confidence_level`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to gauge your confidence level in basic life support, IV therapy, medication management, and other relevant skills.
*   **Interview Topics** (`interview_topics`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to select which topics you would like to be discussed during the interview, which may include communication, time management, leadership, problem-solving, or None.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by new graduate nurses during an interview process, and the results should be reviewed by the hiring team to determine eligibility for a specific job posting.
