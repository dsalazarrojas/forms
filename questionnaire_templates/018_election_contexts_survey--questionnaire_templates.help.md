# Election Contexts Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Election Contexts Survey is designed to collect data on the contexts in which elections are held, covering various aspects of participation, information sources, fairness, and outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form by answering the questions listed on each page. You can access the answers for each question by clicking on the question mark next to the question label.
- You must answer all questions that are marked as required. Non-required questions can be answered at your discretion.
- Use the dropdown menus to select options for questions 2 (Information Sources), 4 (Voter Participation Barriers), 6 (Election Administration), 8 (Civic Engagement Barriers), 9 (Election Outcomes), 11 (Civic Outcomes) when selecting options from the given dropdown list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Election Participation (Select All That Apply)
- **Election Participation** (`Election Participation`, `Select Multiple`, Required): Select all options that apply to your situation, such as Yes, No, or other options that may have been omitted from the list.

### 2. Information Sources (Select One)
- **Information Sources** (`Information Sources`, `Select One`, Required): Choose the information source that best applies to your situation, such as Yes or No.

### 3. Perceived Election Fairness (Number)
- **Perceived Election Fairness** (`Perceived Election Fairness`, `Number`, Required): Enter a numerical value between 0 and 10 to indicate your perceived election fairness, where 0 means very unfair and 10 means very fair.
