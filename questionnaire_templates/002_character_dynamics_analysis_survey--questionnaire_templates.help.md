# Character Dynamics Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to analyze the dynamics between two characters in a story or project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the title of the story or project in question.
2. Enter the names of the two characters you want to analyze.
3. Identify the primary role of each character in the story.
4. Describe their relationship type (e.g., allies, rivals, mentors, etc.).
5. Determine the level of conflict between the characters.
6. List the top 3 personality traits for each character.
7. Describe their dynamic and key interactions.
8. Determine who holds more influence in the relationship.
9. Choose how their relationship changes over time.
10. Provide any additional analysis notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Analyst Name** (`Analyst Name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name or pen name.
* **Story or Project Title** (`Story or Project Title`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the story or project being analyzed.
* **Character One Name** (`Character One Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the first character in the dynamic.
* **Character One Role** (`Character One Role`, `select_one`, required): Choose the primary role of Character One in the story (e.g., Protagonist, Antagonist, Deuteragonist, Supporting, Minor).
* **Character Two Name** (`Character Two Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the second character in the dynamic.
* **Character Two Role** (`Character Two Role`, `select_one`, required): Choose the primary role of Character Two in the story (e.g., Protagonist, Antagonist, Deuteragonist, Supporting, Minor).
* **What is their relationship type** (`What is their relationship type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the primary relationship dynamic between the characters (e.g., Allies, Rivals, Mentor and student, Family members, Romantic partners, Enemies, Complicated).
* **Conflict Level** (`Conflict Level`, `select_one`, required): Choose the level of tension between the characters (e.g., None, Low, Moderate, High, Extreme).
* **Character One Key Traits** (`Character One Key Traits`, `text`, required): List the top 3 personality traits of Character One.
* **Character Two Key Traits** (`Character Two Key Traits`, `text`, required): List the top 3 personality traits of Character Two.
* **Describe their dynamic** (`Describe their dynamic`, `text`, required): Describe how the characters interact and influence each other.
* **Key Interactions or Scenes** (`Key Interactions or Scenes`, `text`, optional): List important moments between the characters.
* **Power Balance** (`Power Balance`, `select_one`, required): Determine who holds more influence in the relationship (e.g., Character One dominates, Character One slightly leads, Equal balance, Character Two slightly leads, Character Two dominates).
* **How does their relationship change** (`How does their relationship change`, `select_one`, required): Choose how their relationship changes over time (e.g., Strengthens, Weakens, Remains stable, Fluctuates, Transforms completely).
* **Additional Analysis Notes** (`Additional Analysis Notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any other observations or insights about the characters.

By following these steps and completing this form, you will gain a deeper understanding of the character dynamics in your story or project.
