# Healing Arts Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us understand your interests and preferences related to our healing arts workshop. Your input will help us tailor the workshop content to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in its entirety, answering all required fields.
2. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
3. You can save and resume your progress at any time using the "Save Progress" feature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Briefly introduce yourself and let us know why you are interested in the healing arts workshop.
* **Contact Information** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your name, email, and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Scheduling Preferences** (`3`, `date`, required/optional): Select a date and time that works best for you to attend the workshop.
* **Workshop Preferences** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose which types of workshops you are interested in attending.
* **Interests** (`5`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select one of the options that best describes your interests.
* **Availability** (`6`, `time`, required/optional): Indicate your availability for the workshop, and we will do our best to accommodate your schedule.
* **Schedule** (`7`, `time`, required/optional): Choose a schedule that works best for you.
* **Workshop Scheduling** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select multiple dates and times that you are available for the workshop.
* **Payment Preferences** (`9`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your preferred payment method.
* **Contact Method** (`10`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone, or mail).
* **Workshop Interests** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select which types of workshops you are interested in.
* **Email** (`12`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can send you workshop updates.
* **Phone Number** (`13`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Address** (`14`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your address so we can send you workshop materials.
* **Workshop Speakers** (`15`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose which speakers you are interested in.
* **Workshop Topics** (`16`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select which topics you are interested in.
* **Workshop Length** (`17`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the length of the workshop you prefer.
* **Payment Details** (`18`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your preferred payment details.
* **Workshop Date** (`19`, `date`, required/optional): Select a date for the workshop.
* **Workshop Time** (`20`, `time`, required/optional): Choose a time for the workshop.
* **Workshop Place** (`21`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the workshop location.
* **Note** (`22`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your workshop preferences.
* **Note2** (`23`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your workshop preferences.
* **Note3** (`24`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your workshop preferences.
* **Submitted** (`25`, `select_one`, required/optional): Confirm that you have submitted your interest in the workshop.

Note: Please fill out all required fields, and we will contact you to confirm your interest in the workshop.
