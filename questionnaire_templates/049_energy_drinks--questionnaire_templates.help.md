# Energy Drinks - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the frequency, purpose, and location of energy drink consumption. It is intended to help us better understand the habits and behavior of individuals who consume energy drinks. Please note that this form is meant for individuals who consume energy drinks and are 18 years or older.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Demographic Information** (demographic_info, text, required: false): This section is for demographic information such as name and age. Please provide your name and age to help us better understand your background.
2. **Drink Frequency** (drink_frequency, number, required: true): Please enter how often you consume energy drinks, e.g., 1-2 times a week, daily, etc.
3. **Drink Purpose** (drink_purpose, select_one, required: true): Choose the reason(s) why you consume energy drinks:
	* Energy Boost
	* Relaxation
	* Focus
4. **Drink Time** (drink_time, time, required: false): If you'd like to share the specific time you usually consume energy drinks, please enter it in the format `HH:MM`.
5. **Drink Location** (drink_location, text, required: false): If you'd like to share where you usually consume energy drinks, please describe the location.

## Tips
* Please be as accurate as possible when answering the questions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
* You can skip the **Drink Location** field if you don't want to share this information.
* If you've completed this form before, you can skip questions that don't change.
