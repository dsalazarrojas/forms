# Chemistry Exam Study Guide Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to guide students in preparing for their chemistry exam by helping them track their progress, identify areas requiring extra attention, and set realistic goals for their preparation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all the required fields with accurate and honest information about your current status and goals.
2. Select all the topics you have covered or need help with.
3. Choose your preferred study methods and resources.
4. Indicate your target grade for the exam.
5. Specify any additional help you need.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student name** (`Student name`, text, **required**): Enter your name as it appears on your student ID or identification card.
* **Email address** (`Email address`, email, **required**): Enter your active university email address or other communication channel.
* **Course name** (`Course name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the course for which you are preparing the exam.
* **Instructor name** (`Instructor name`, text, **optional**): Enter your instructor's name if you know it. This will help in case of further communication.
* **Exam date** (`Exam date`, date, **required**): Enter the date of the exam.
* **How many weeks until your exam?** (`How many weeks until your exam?`, number, **required**): Calculate the number of weeks you have until the exam and enter it here.
* **Current exam readiness level** (`Current exam readiness level`, select_one, **required**): Rate your current level of preparation, from 1 (Not ready) to 5 (Fully ready).
* **Main topics covered in exam** (`Main topics covered in exam`, select_multiple, **required**): Choose the topics covered in the exam from the list.
* **Your strongest topic** (`Your strongest topic`, text, **required**): Specify the topic you feel most confident about.
* **Topic needing most review** (`Topic needing most review`, text, **required**): Identify the topic that needs the most attention.
* **How many hours per week can you study?** (`How many hours per week can you study?`, select_one, **required**): Choose the number of hours you can dedicate to studying each week.
* **Your preferred study methods** (`Your preferred study methods`, select_multiple, **required**): Select the study methods you prefer, such as textbook reading, practice problems, study groups, and more.
* **Available study resources** (`Available study resources`, select_multiple, **required**): Choose the resources you have access to, including textbooks, online resources, and more.
* **How many practice exams have you completed?** (`How many practice exams have you completed?`, number, **optional**): If you have completed practice exams, enter the number here.
* **Average score on practice exams** (`Average score on practice exams`, number, **optional**): If you have completed practice exams, enter your average score here.
* **Exam format** (`Exam format`, select_one, **required**): Choose the format of the exam, such as multiple choice, short answer, or a mix.
* **What is your target grade for this exam?** (`What is your target grade for this exam?`, select_one, **required**): Choose your target grade, from A to Just passing.
* **Major challenges in studying for this exam** (`Major challenges in studying for this exam`, text, **optional**): Specify any major challenges you face in studying for the exam.
* **What additional help do you need?** (`What additional help do you need?`, text, **optional**): If you need help, specify what kind of help you require.
