# Cheesecake Preferences Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This questionnaire is designed to gather information about your personal preferences when it comes to cheesecake. It includes questions about your demographic information, eating habits, flavor preferences, and purchasing behavior.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please answer all questions honestly**. This information will help us better understand your preferences and tailor our products to meet your needs.
2. **Read each question carefully** and choose the most accurate answer from the provided options.
3. **Take your time to answer each question**. Don't rush through the form, as this will help ensure accurate and thoughtful responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Please provide your full name, if you feel comfortable sharing it.
* **Email Address**: This is an optional field for follow-up surveys or communication. If you don't want to share your email, you can leave it blank.
* **Age Group**: Choose the age range that best describes you.
* **Gender**: Select the gender that best identifies you.
* **Location**: Enter the city or region where you live.
* **How Often Do You Eat Cheesecake**: Choose how frequently you consume cheesecake.
* **Favorite Flavors**: Select your top favorite flavors from the list provided.
* **Other Favorite Flavors**: If there are any other flavors you enjoy, please list them here.
* **Preferred Toppings**: Choose your favorite toppings for cheesecake.
* **Crust Preference**: Select the type of crust you prefer.
* **Texture Preference**: Choose the texture you prefer for your cheesecake.
* **Sweetness Level**: Select how sweet you like your cheesecake.
* **Preferred Serving Size**: Choose the size of cheesecake you typically order.
* **Where Do You Usually Buy**: Select where you usually purchase cheesecake from the provided options.
* **Price Range**: Choose the price range you are willing to pay for cheesecake.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Check any dietary restrictions you may have.
* **Willing to Pay Premium**: Answer whether you would pay more for premium ingredients.
* **Organic Preference**: Select whether you prefer organic ingredients.
* **Occasion for Purchase**: Choose the occasion when you typically buy cheesecake.
* **Brand Loyalty**: Select how loyal you are to a particular brand.
* **Favorite Brand**: If you have a favorite brand, please enter its name here.
* **Interest in New Flavors**: Answer whether you would try new or exotic flavors.
* **Flavor Suggestions**: If you have any suggestions for new flavors, please list them here.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Select how satisfied you are with the available cheesecake options.

This guide is designed to help you complete the form accurately and efficiently. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to ask.
