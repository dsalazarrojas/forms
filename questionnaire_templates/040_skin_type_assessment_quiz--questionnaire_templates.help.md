# Skin Type Assessment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the skin type of individuals. The form consists of questions that help to categorize your skin type into one of the two main categories: 'Yes' or 'No' responses to skin concerns, and a final review section for additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions in the sections below with a 'Yes' or 'No' response.
2. Review your answers for completeness and accuracy.
3. Provide any additional comments or concerns in the final review section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **skin_type_1** (required/optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are concerned about skin type 1.
* **skin_type_2** (required/optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are concerned about skin type 2.
* **skin_concern_1** (required/optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are concerned about skin concern 1.
* **skin_concern_2** (required/optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are concerned about skin concern 2.
* **skin_concern_3** (required/optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are concerned about skin concern 3.
* **skin_concern_4** (required/optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are concerned about skin concern 4.
* **skin_concern_5** (required/optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are concerned about skin concern 5.
* **skin_concern_6** (required/optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are concerned about skin concern 6.
* **review** (optional): Provide any additional comments or concerns you have about your skin type.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by individuals who are interested in assessing their skin type. If you have any questions or concerns, please refer to our skin care experts.
