# Pessimism And Transformation Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Pessimism And Transformation Quiz is a self-assessment tool designed to help individuals understand and reflect on their levels of pessimism and transformation. It is a starting point for personal growth and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
* For questions with multiple options, select the response that best fits your situation.
* You can type in free text to answer questions that allow for more detailed responses.
* If you need help or clarification, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This page is an introduction to the quiz. Please read it carefully and then proceed to the next page.
* **Pessimism Scale**: This question assesses your level of pessimism on a scale of 1 to 5. Choose the response that best describes your current state:
	+ Pessimism 1: I'm feeling very positive and optimistic about my situation.
	+ Pessimism 2: I'm feeling somewhat neutral, but have some concerns.
	+ Pessimism 3: I'm feeling somewhat negative, but can see some potential for improvement.
	+ Pessimism 4: I'm feeling very negative about my situation.
	+ Pessimism 5: I'm feeling extremely negative and hopeless.
* **Transformation Scale**: This question assesses your level of transformation on a scale of 1 to 5. Choose the response that best describes your current state:
	+ Transformation 1: I'm feeling very stagnant and unmotivated.
	+ Transformation 2: I'm feeling somewhat motivated, but need some help.
	+ Transformation 3: I'm feeling somewhat motivated, but have some challenges.
	+ Transformation 4: I'm feeling very motivated and making progress.
	+ Transformation 5: I'm feeling extremely motivated and achieving my goals.
* **Follow-up Resources**: This question is for providing any additional resources or information that might be helpful in your current situation. Please provide as much detail as possible.
* **Self-Reflection Date**: This question is for recording your self-reflection date. Please enter the date when you completed this form.
* **Outlook Pattern**: This question is for recording your current outlook pattern. Please choose a time of day that best represents your current mood.
* **Coach Support**: This question is for indicating the level of support you need from a coach. Please select all that apply:
	+ Support 1: I need a lot of support and guidance.
	+ Support 2: I need some support, but can also take initiative.
	+ Support 3: I need occasional support, but can mostly work independently.
	+ Support 4: I need minimal support, but appreciate guidance when needed.
	+ Support 5: I don't need any support, I'm self-sufficient.
* **Resources Sharing**: This question is for indicating the level of resources you are willing to share with others. Please select all that apply:
	+ Resources 1: I'm not comfortable sharing any resources.
	+ Resources 2: I'm willing to share some resources, but need to consider each request.
	+ Resources 3: I'm willing to share some resources, but only with people I trust.
	+ Resources 4: I'm willing to share resources freely, but with some boundaries.
	+ Resources 5: I'm very open and willing to share resources with others.
* **Notes**: This question is for providing any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with your coach or supporter.
* **Submit**: This question is for selecting how you feel about submitting your answers. Please choose the response that best fits your situation:
	+ Submit 1: I'm not ready to submit, I need more time.
	+ Submit 2: I'm ready to submit, but need some support.
	+ Submit 3: I'm ready to submit, but have some concerns.
	+ Submit 4: I'm ready to submit, and feel confident in my answers.
	+ Submit 5: I'm extremely ready to submit, and feel very confident.
* **Email**: This question is for providing your email address. Please enter your email address if you'd like to receive any follow-up resources or support.
* **Phone**: This question is for providing your phone number. Please enter your phone number if you'd like to receive any follow-up calls or support.
* **Additional Comments**: This question is for providing any additional comments or thoughts you'd like to share with your coach or supporter.
* **Follow-up Question**: This question is for asking any follow-up questions or seeking clarification on any of the previous questions.
* **Follow-up Resources (1-11)**: These questions are for providing any additional resources or information that might be helpful in your current situation. Please provide as much detail as possible for each question.
