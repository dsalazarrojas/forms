# Eye And Ear Infection Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your health and medical history related to eye and ear infections.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form in sections, one page at a time.
- Answer all questions accurately and honestly to ensure the most accurate results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your age** (`Age`, `number`, optional): Please enter your age.
* **Have you experienced any of these ear infection symptoms** (`Ear Infection Symptoms`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all symptoms that apply, such as ear pain, discharge, fever, swelling, redness, or hearing loss.
* **Have you experienced any of these eye infection symptoms** (`Eye Infection Symptoms`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all symptoms that apply, such as blurred vision, eye pain, sensitivity to light, swollen eyelid, or redness.
* **Have you had contact with another person who has an ear infection** (`Contact with Another Person with Ear Infection`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select true if you have had contact with someone who has an ear infection, false otherwise.
* **Have you had contact with another person who has an eye infection** (`Contact with Another Person with Eye Infection`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select true if you have had contact with someone who has an eye infection, false otherwise.
* **Do you have any medical history** (`Medical History`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select true if you have any medical history, false otherwise.
* **Do you have any eye medical history** (`Eye Medical History`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select true if you have any eye medical history, false otherwise.
* **What is your follow-up date** (`Follow-up Date`, `date`, optional): Enter your follow-up date if necessary.

Note: Please answer all questions accurately and honestly to ensure the most accurate results.
