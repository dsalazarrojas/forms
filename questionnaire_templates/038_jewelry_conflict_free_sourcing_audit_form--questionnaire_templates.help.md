# Jewelry Conflict Free Sourcing Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help organizations and individuals in the jewelry industry assess their conflict-free sourcing practices, identify areas of improvement, and create a plan to source materials without conflicts.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Review each page and make sure you have completed all required fields.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
4. Once you have finished, submit the form to get your conflict-free sourcing audit report.

* **First Page (Page 1)** (`first_page`, text, optional): This is the first page of the form. You can provide any additional information or comments related to your conflict-free sourcing practices.
