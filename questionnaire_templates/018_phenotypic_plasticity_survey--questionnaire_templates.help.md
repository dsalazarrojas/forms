# Phenotypic Plasticity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on the phenotypic plasticity of traits, including general information, participant details, and specific data on traits and their characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the general information at the top of the form.
2. Provide participant details, including any relevant contact information.
3. Answer questions about the trait variation and data sources in the relevant fields.
4. Describe the trait characteristics in the provided text fields.
5. Select the data collection methods and frequencies used in the survey from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): Fill in any general information that is relevant to the survey.
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, optional): Enter the participant's contact information, such as name, email, or phone number.
* **Trait Variation** (`trait_variation`, text, optional): Describe the variation of the trait among different individuals or groups.
* **Data Sources** (`data_sources`, text, optional): List the sources of data used for the survey.
* **Trait Characteristics** (`trait_characteristics`, text, optional): Describe the characteristics of the trait, such as its definition, measurement, or observation methods.
* **Data Collection** (`data_collection`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all the applicable data collection methods used, such as Method, Frequency, Sampling Location, Sampling Time, Sampling Duration, or Sampling Frequency.
* **Participant Info Details** (`participant_info_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the participant that may be relevant to the survey.
* **Trait Characteristics 2** (`trait_characteristics_2`, text, optional): Describe any additional trait characteristics that are relevant to the survey.
* **Data Sources 2** (`data_sources_2`, text, optional): List any additional sources of data used for the survey.

## Tips
Please fill in all the relevant fields accurately and honestly, and ensure that the collected data is used ethically and responsibly. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please contact the form creator at <form@gic.mx>.
