# Business Online Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Online Questionnaire is designed to gather information about your business to provide tailored support and guidance. It will help us understand your company's current state and identify areas where you may need assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and fill out each field with the most accurate information.
2. Make sure to complete all the required fields (indicated by an asterisk*) as they are essential for us to understand your business.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please feel free to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`client_name`, text, *): Your full name as the business owner or representative.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, *): Your primary email address associated with your business.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, *): Your contact phone number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, *): The name of your business.
* **Year Business Established** (`business_established`, number, ): The year your business was established. If you are a startup, please enter the year you started.
* **Current Annual Revenue** (`current_annual_revenue`, select_one, *): Please select the approximate annual revenue of your business. This will help us understand your business scale.
* **Year-over-Year Revenue Growth** (`revenue_growth_rate`, number, ): The rate of your annual revenue growth. This will help us understand your business growth prospects.
* **Primary Business Challenge** (`primary_challenge`, text, *): The biggest challenge your business is currently facing.
* **Greatest Growth Opportunity** (`growth_opportunity`, text, *): The area where you see the most potential for growth.
* **Primary Customer Acquisition Method** (`customer_acquisition_method`, select_one, *): The primary method you use to acquire customers. For example, direct sales, marketing, referrals, partnerships, etc.
* **Current Monthly Marketing Spend** (`current_marketing_spend`, select_one, ): The approximate monthly marketing budget for your business.
* **How Strong is Your Digital Presence** (`digital_presence`, number, *): Please rate your digital presence from 1 to 5, where 1 is weak and 5 is strong.
* **Number of Team Members** (`team_size`, number, *): The current number of team members in your organization.
* **Plans to Hire** (`hiring_plans`, select_one, ): Are you planning to hire new team members in the next 3 or 6 months?
* **Technology Infrastructure Adequacy** (`technology_infrastructure`, number, *): How adequate is your technology infrastructure? Please rate from 1 to 5, where 1 is inadequate and 5 is strong.
* **5-Year Strategic Direction** (`strategic_direction`, text, *): What direction do you see your business heading in the next 5 years?
* **Competitive Advantage** (`competitive_advantage`, text, *): What sets your business apart from competitors?
* **Business Goals for Next Year** (`business_goals_next_year`, text, ): What specific goals do you have for your business in the next year?
* **Areas Where You Need Support** (`support_areas`, select_multiple, *): Which areas of your business need support? Please select all relevant options.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, ): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please feel free to share.
