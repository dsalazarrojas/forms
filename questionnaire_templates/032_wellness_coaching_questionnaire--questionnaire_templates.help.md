# Wellness Coaching Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and medical information from users for the purpose of providing wellness coaching services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately to the best of your ability.
2. Select the relevant options for your answers, if applicable.
3. Enter any medical notes or details as required.
4. Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your given first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your given last name.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the standard format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Weight** (number, required/optional): Enter your current weight in pounds.
* **Height** (number, required/optional): Enter your current height in inches.
* **Blood Pressure** (select one, required/optional): Select your current blood pressure reading from the provided options.
* **Smoker Status** (select multiple, required/optional): Select the frequency of smoking status.
* **Alcohol Consumption** (select multiple, required/optional): Select the frequency of alcohol consumption.
* **Medication List** (select multiple, required/optional): Select any medications you currently take.
* **Allergy List** (select multiple, required/optional): Select any allergies you have.
* **Medical History** (text, required/optional): Enter any past medical history or concerns.
* **Family Doctor** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of your family doctor.
* **Doctor Phone Number** (text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of your doctor.
* **Doctor 2 Phone Number** (text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of your second doctor.
* **Doctor 3 Phone Number** (text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of your third doctor.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (text, required/optional): Enter your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 2 Number** (text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of your second emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 3 Number** (text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of your third emergency contact.
* **Medical Insurance** (text, required/optional): Enter your medical insurance details.
* **Insurance Provider** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of your insurance provider.
* **Medical Group** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of your medical group.
* **Emergency Procedure** (text, required/optional): Enter any emergency procedure details.
* **Other Medical Notes** (text, required/optional): Enter any additional medical notes.

Tips: Please be accurate and honest when filling out this form, as the information will be used to provide personalized wellness coaching services. If you are unsure about any of the questions or have concerns, please consult your doctor or medical professional before completing this form.
