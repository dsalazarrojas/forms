# Hoof Boot Fit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hoof Boot Fit Survey is a comprehensive form designed to collect detailed information about a horse's hoof boot fit. This form is intended for equine professionals, such as farriers or horse shoeing specialists, to gather data on various aspects of a horse's hoof, boot size, shape, and other relevant measurements.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter the Horse ID number. This is a unique identifier for the horse.
2.  Enter the horse's name.
3.  Upload a photo of the horse's hoof (if required).
4.  Enter the hoof size.
5.  Enter the hoof shape.
6.  Enter the measurement for the first point of the hoof.
7.  Enter the goal or specific use case for the hoof boot (e.g., trail riding, racing, etc.).
8.  Enter the measurement for the second point of the hoof.
9.  Enter a description for the uploaded photo.
10. Enter the photo orientation (e.g., landscape, portrait).
11. Enter the measurement for the third point of the hoof.
12. Select the boot type from the options provided (Size, Style, Brand, or Model).
13. Enter the measurement for the fourth point of the hoof.
14. Enter the measurement for the fifth point of the hoof.
15. Enter the boot height.
16. Enter the boot width.
17. Enter the boot circumference.
18. Enter the boot weight.
19. Enter the measurement for the sixth point of the hoof.
20. Enter the measurement for the seventh point of the hoof.
21. Enter the Boot Size 1 (a text field for additional boot size information).
22. Enter the Boot Size 2 (a text field for additional boot size information).
23. Enter the Boot Size 3 (a text field for additional boot size information).
24. Enter the Boot Size 4 (a text field for additional boot size information).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Horse Id** (`horse_id`, `number`, required: false): This is a unique identifier for the horse. Enter the horse's ID number.
*   **Horse Name** (`horse_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the horse's name.
*   **Photo** (`photo`, `note`, required: false): This is an optional field for uploading a photo of the horse's hoof. If required, enter a photo description and orientation details.
*   **Hoof Size** (`hoof_size`, `text`, required: false): Enter the size of the horse's hoof.
*   **Hoof Shape** (`hoof_shape`, `text`, required: false): Enter the shape of the horse's hoof.
*   **Measurement 1** (`measurement_1`, `number`, required: false): Enter the measurement for the first point of the hoof.
*   **Goal** (`goal`, `text`, required: false): Enter the specific use case for the hoof boot, such as trail riding or racing.
*   **Measurement 2** (`measurement_2`, `number`, required: false): Enter the measurement for the second point of the hoof.
*   **Photo Description** (`photo_description`, `text`, required: false): If you've uploaded a photo, add a description of it.
*   **Photo Orientation** (`photo_orientation`, `text`, required: false): If you've uploaded a photo, indicate its orientation.
*   **Measurement 3** (`measurement_3`, `number`, required: false): Enter the measurement for the third point of the hoof.
*   **Boot Size** (`boot_size`, `text`, required: false): Enter the boot size information for the horse.
*   **Boot Type** (`boot_type`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the boot type from the options provided.
*   **Measurement 4** (`measurement_4`, `number`, required: false): Enter the measurement for the fourth point of the hoof.
*   **Measurement 5** (`measurement_5`, `number`, required: false): Enter the measurement for the fifth point of the hoof.
*   **Boot Height** (`boot_height`, `number`, required: false): Enter the height of the boot.
*   **Boot Width** (`boot_width`, `number`, required: false): Enter the width of the boot.
*   **Boot Circumference** (`boot_circumference`, `number`, required: false): Enter the circumference of the boot.
*   **Boot Weight** (`boot_weight`, `number`, required: false): Enter the weight of the boot.
*   **Measurement 6** (`measurement_6`, `number`, required: false): Enter the measurement for the sixth point of the hoof.
*   **Measurement 7** (`measurement_7`, `number`, required: false): Enter the measurement for the seventh point of the hoof.
*   **Boot Size 1** (`boot_size_1`, `text`, required: false): Enter additional boot size information for the horse.
*   **Boot Size 2** (`boot_size_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter additional boot size information for the horse.
*   **Boot Size 3** (`boot_size_3`, `text`, required: false): Enter additional boot size information for the horse.
*   **Boot Size 4** (`boot_size_4`, `text`, required: false): Enter additional boot size information for the horse.

## Tips

*   Please ensure you have a clear photo of the horse's hoof for better results.
*   Enter accurate measurements for each point of the hoof.
*   Choose the correct boot type for the horse's specific needs.
*   Enter additional boot size information for the horse as needed.
*   Ensure you've filled out all required fields for a complete and accurate assessment.
