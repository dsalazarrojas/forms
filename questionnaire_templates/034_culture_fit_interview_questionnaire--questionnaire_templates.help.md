# Culture Fit Interview Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This questionnaire is designed to assess an individual's or organization's culture fit, work environment, and work style. It's meant to be a comprehensive tool to understand how well an organization aligns with one's own values and work style.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Read each question carefully and select the option that best describes your current or desired work environment.
2.  Answer all questions as thoroughly as possible, but don't worry if you're unsure about some questions. You can always come back to them later.
3.  For select multiple options, choose as many options as apply.
4.  For select one options, choose the best single option that fits your current or desired work environment.
5.  If you have any comments or questions, you can include them in the submission field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A short introduction explaining the purpose and context of this questionnaire.
	+ This is a required field as it provides an overview of the questionnaire and its goals.
* **Company**: Enter the name of the company you're working with or interested in.
	+ This field is required as it helps in understanding the specific company context.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or the job title of the individual you're assessing.
	+ This field is required as it helps in understanding the specific job context.
* **Work Environment**: Select the best option that describes your current or desired work environment.
	+ Options: 9-to-5, Monday-to-Friday, 8-12 hours/day; Flexible or remote work; Flexible or part-time work; Variable hours or project-based work; Flexible or flexible hours (not 9-to-5)
	+ This field is required as it helps in understanding how you prefer to work.
* **Work Style**: Select the best options that describe your work style.
	+ Options: Structured schedule; Flexible schedule
	+ This field is required as it helps in understanding how you prefer to work.
* **Team**: Select the best option that describes your team size and dynamics.
	+ Options: Small team or 1-10 people; Medium team or 11-50 people; Large team or 51+ people
	+ This field is required as it helps in understanding your team context.
* **Communication**: Select the best options that describe your preferred communication methods.
	+ Options: Face-to-face; Email or instant messaging; Phone calls or video calls
	+ This field is required as it helps in understanding how you prefer to communicate.
* **Decision Making**: Select the best options that describe your decision-making style.
	+ Options: Autonomous or individual decisions; Collaborative or team decisions; Manager or team lead decisions; Some autonomy, but mostly manager decisions
	+ This field is required as it helps in understanding how you make decisions.
* **Work-Life Balance**: Select the best option that describes your preferred work-life balance.
	+ Options: Strong emphasis on work-life balance; Some emphasis on work-life balance; No emphasis on work-life balance
	+ This field is required as it helps in understanding how you balance work and personal life.
* **Culture**: Select the best options that describe your preferred culture.
	+ Options: Formal or structured; Flexible or casual
	+ This field is required as it helps in understanding how you prefer to work in terms of culture.
* **Growth Opportunities**: Select the best options that describe your preferred growth opportunities.
	+ Options: Formal, regular training or mentorship programs; Some opportunities for growth and development; No formal growth or development opportunities
	+ This field is required as it helps in understanding how you prefer to grow and develop professionally.
* **Feedback**: Select the best options that describe your preferred feedback style.
	+ Options: Regular, constructive feedback; Some feedback, informal; Rare or little feedback
	+ This field is required as it helps in understanding how you prefer feedback.
* **Autonomy**: Select the best options that describe your level of autonomy.
	+ Options: Some autonomy and freedom; Moderate or average autonomy; High or almost full autonomy
	+ This field is required as it helps in understanding how much freedom you prefer to have in your work.
* **Creativity**: Select the best options that describe your preferred creativity freedom.
	+ Options: Emphasized or encouraged; Moderate or average creativity freedom; Little or no creativity encouraged
	+ This field is required as it helps in understanding how much creativity you prefer to have in your work.
* **Work from Home**: Select the best options that describe your preferred work-from-home policy.
	+ Options: Allowed, with flexibility; Allowed, but only occasionally; Not allowed or discouraged
	+ This field is required as it helps in understanding your preferences for working from home.
* **Work from Office**: Select the best options that describe your preferred work-from-office policy.
	+ Options: Required or expected; Allowed or flexible; Allowed, but not required
	+ This field is required as it helps in understanding your preferences for working from the office.
* **Travel**: Select the best options that describe your travel policy.
	+ Options: Frequent or regular travel required; Some or occasional travel required; No or little travel required
	+ This field is required as it helps in understanding your travel preferences.
* **Work Hours**: Select the best options that describe your preferred work hours.
	+ Options: Fixed or standard hours; Flexible or flexible hours
	+ This field is required as it helps in understanding your schedule preferences.
* **Compensation**: Select the best options that describe your preferred compensation.
	+ Options: Competitive salary, bonus, or stock options; Moderate or base salary; Low or base salary only
	+ This field is required as it helps in understanding your compensation expectations.
* **Benefits**: Select the best options that describe your preferred benefits.
	+ Options: Comprehensive benefits (health, retirement, etc.); Some or partial benefits; No or limited benefits
	+ This field is required as it helps in understanding your benefits expectations.
* **Job Security**: Select the best options that describe your preferred job security.
	+ Options: Job security, stability; Moderate or average job security; Low or little job security
	+ This field is required as it helps in understanding your job security expectations.
* **Company Size**: Select the best option that describes the size of the company you're interested in.
	+ Options: Small company or under 50 people; Medium company or 50-100 people; Large company or 100-1000 people; Enterprise or 1000+ people
	+ This field is required as it helps in understanding the context of the company.
* **Company Type**: Select the best option that describes the type of company you're interested in.
	+ Options: Sole Proprietor or Individual; Small Business or Partnership; Public Company or Corporation
	+ This field is required as it helps in understanding the type of company you're interested in.
* **Company Stage**: Select the best option that describes the stage of the company you're interested in.
	+ Options: Startup or Seed stage; Scaling or Growth stage; Established or Mature stage
	+ This field is required as it helps in understanding the stage of the company.
* **Submission**: You can include any additional comments or questions here.
	+ This field is required as it helps in capturing any further information.
* **Contact**: Enter your name and contact information.
	+ This field is required as it helps in reaching back to you for any further questions.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
	+ This field is required as it helps in communicating with you.

## Tips

* Answer all required fields as thoroughly as possible.
* Don't overthink any question, just choose the best option you think fits your current or desired work environment.
* Take your time, and answer honestly.
* If you're unsure about any question, come back to it later.
* If you have any further questions, feel free to ask.
* Please submit the form as completely as possible.
