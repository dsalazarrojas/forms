# open_ended_student_response_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open-Ended Student Response Form is a tool for students to provide a comprehensive response to a question, which can be a part of a more complex assessment or evaluation. This form aims to collect detailed information about a student's thoughts, understanding, and reflection on the question. The form is used to gather data for educational purposes and to facilitate student feedback and evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Student Email" field (optional).
3. Type your response to the question in the "What is your response to the question" field. This is the main section where you can provide a detailed answer.
4. You can add any additional comments in the "Additional comments" field (optional).
5. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have agreed to the school's terms of service in the "Have you agreed to the school terms of service" field.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" for each term of service in the "Terms of service" field.
7. Enter the date and time you completed the form in the "Date Completed" and "Time Completed" fields.
8. If you have any comments for your teacher, type them in the "Teacher Comment" field (optional).
9. If you have any notes from your teacher, type them in the "Teacher Note" field (optional).
10. Reflect on your learning process in the "Student Reflection" field (optional).
11. Provide feedback for your teacher in the "Student Feedback" field (optional).
12. Your teacher can add comments and notes in the "Teacher Comment" and "Teacher Note" fields.
13. Enter your school's name, email, and website in the "School Name", "School Email", and "School Website" fields.
14. Enter your student phone number in the "Student Phone" field.
15. Enter your teacher phone number in the "Teacher Phone" field (optional).
16. Enter your address in the "Student Address" field (optional).
17. Enter your teacher's address in the "Teacher Address" field (optional).
18. Select the submission status as "Submitted", "Pending review", or "Approved" in the "Submission Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your student identification card or any other identifying document.
* **Student Email**: Enter your student email address, if you have one. If you don't have a student email, you can leave this field blank.
* **What is your response to the question**: This is the main section where you can provide a detailed answer to the question posed.
* **Additional comments**: You can add any additional comments or explanations to support your response.
* **Have you agreed to the school terms of service**: Select "Yes" if you have read and agreed to the school's terms of service. 
* **Terms of service**: Select "Yes" or "No" for each term of service. This is to ensure you have read and agreed to each term.
* **Date Completed**: Enter the date you completed the form.
* **Time Completed**: Enter the time you completed the form.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter your teacher's name, if applicable.
* **Teacher Email**: Enter your teacher's email address, if applicable.
* **Teacher Comment**: If you have any comments for your teacher, type them here.
* **Teacher Note**: If you have any notes from your teacher, type them here.
* **Student Reflection**: Reflect on your learning process and how it has helped you grow.
* **Student Feedback**: Provide feedback for your teacher on how they can improve.
* **Teacher Feedback**: Provide feedback for your teacher on how they can improve.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your school.
* **School Email**: Enter the school's email address.
* **School Website**: Enter the school's website.
* **Student Phone**: Enter your student phone number.
* **Teacher Phone**: Enter your teacher's phone number, if applicable.
* **Student Address**: Enter your home address.
* **Teacher Address**: Enter your teacher's home address, if applicable.
* **Submission Status**: Select the status of your submission as "Submitted", "Pending review", or "Approved".
