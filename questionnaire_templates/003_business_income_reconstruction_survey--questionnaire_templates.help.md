# Business Income Reconstruction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Income Reconstruction Survey is designed to collect information about your business's financial history and current status to help us better understand your unique situation and provide personalized support for income reconstruction. This survey is only meant for businesses that experienced a significant disruption to their income. Please ensure you have a genuine reason for filling this form and it is not used for marketing or other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your business name, type, and tax ID number on pages 1-3.
2. Select the primary industry category that your business operates in on page 4.
3. Enter the start and end dates of the income reconstruction period on pages 6-7.
4. Provide details on your pre-disruption average monthly income on page 8 and select all sources of your business income on page 9.
5. Indicate if your business experiences seasonal income fluctuations and select the peak months on page 11.
6. Provide any available historical financial records on page 12.
7. Check if you have access to industry comparable data on page 13.
8. Select the cause of the income disruption on page 14.
9. Describe the impact of the disruption on page 15.
10. Select measures taken to recover income on page 16.
11. Provide your current average monthly income on page 17.
12. Enter your estimated projected recovery date, if applicable on page 18.
13. Fill in your total monthly fixed expenses on page 19.
14. Indicate the percentage of revenue spent on variable expenses on page 20.
15. Check if you have filed any insurance claims on page 21.
16. Add any additional documentation that supports your reconstruction on page 22.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name (1)**: Enter the legal name of your business.
* **Business Type (2)**: Select the structure of your business (Sole proprietorship, Partnership, Limited Liability Company, Corporation, S Corporation, Non-profit).
* **Tax ID Number (3)**: Enter your Employer Identification Number or SSN.
* **Business Address (4)**: Enter the full physical address of your business location.
* **Industry Classification (5)**: Select the primary industry category that your business operates in (Retail trade, Food service, Professional services, Healthcare, Construction, Manufacturing, Technology, Real estate, Other).
* **Reconstruction Period Start Date (6)**: Enter the start date of the income reconstruction period.
* **Reconstruction Period End Date (7)**: Enter the end date of the income reconstruction period.
* **Pre-Disruption Average Monthly Income (8)**: Enter your average monthly income before the disruption occurred.
* **Income Sources (9)**: Select all sources of your business income (Product sales, Service fees, Subscription revenue, Licensing fees, Rental income, Commission, Other).
* **Seasonal Variation (10)**: Indicate if your business experiences seasonal income fluctuations (Yes - significant variation, Yes - moderate variation, Yes - minor variation, No - consistent year-round).
* **Peak Season Months (11)**: Select the months with the highest business income, if applicable.
* **Historical Financial Records Available (12)**: Select all available financial documentation (Tax returns, Profit and loss statements, Balance sheets, Bank statements, Sales records, Invoices, Accounting software reports, None available).
* **Comparable Business Data (13)**: Check if you have access to industry comparable data (Yes - industry reports, Yes - competitor analysis, Yes - trade association data, False - not applicable).
* **Cause of Income Disruption (14)**: Select the reason for the income disruption (Natural disaster, Pandemic or health crisis, Economic downturn, Equipment failure, Supply chain disruption, Regulatory changes, Other).
* **Disruption Impact Description (15)**: Describe how the disruption affected your business operations.
* **Recovery Measures Taken (16)**: Select all measures taken to recover income (Cost reduction, Staff reduction, Product line changes, Marketing increase, Price adjustments, New revenue streams, Loan acquisition, None).
* **Current Monthly Income (17)**: Enter your current average monthly income at the time of the survey.
* **Projected Recovery Date (18)**: Enter your estimated projected recovery date, if applicable.
* **Monthly Fixed Expenses (19)**: Enter your total monthly fixed operating expenses.
* **Variable Expenses as Percentage of Revenue (20)**: Indicate the approximate percentage of revenue spent on variable expenses.
* **Insurance Claims Filed (21)**: Check if you have filed any insurance claims related to income loss (Yes - approved, Yes - pending, Yes - denied, No - not applicable, No - planning to file).
* **Additional Documentation Notes (22)**: List any additional documentation available to support reconstruction.
