# Boat Service Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Boat Service Questionnaire is a tool to help service providers gather necessary information about a boat's maintenance and service history. This questionnaire is designed to be filled out by boat owners prior to a service appointment to ensure that the service provider has all the necessary information to perform a thorough and effective service.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Boat Owner Name to identify yourself.
2. Enter your Email Address to allow us to contact you with service reminders and updates.
3. Enter your Phone Number in case we need to contact you urgently.
4. Enter the Boat Name and Boat Manufacturer to help us understand the boat's specifics.
5. Enter the Year of Manufacture to help us determine the appropriate maintenance schedule.
6. Enter the Boat Length in Feet to help us determine the appropriate service intervals.
7. Select the Engine Type from the provided options.
8. Enter the Date of Last Service to help us understand your service history.
9. Select the Type of Last Service to help us understand the scope of the service.
10. Enter the Name of Last Service Provider if you had a previous service.
11. Enter the Current Engine Hours to help us understand your engine's condition.
12. Select how well you follow the maintenance schedule.
13. Select all the Regular Maintenance Performed on your boat.
14. Enter any Recent Issues or Problems you are experiencing.
15. Select the Boat Warranty Status to help us understand your warranty coverage.
16. Select where the boat is typically stored.
17. Enter the Estimated Annual Usage Hours to help us understand your usage pattern.
18. Select your satisfaction with the last service.
19. Select whether you would use the same service provider again.
20. Provide any Suggestions for Service Improvement.
21. Enter what service you need next.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Boat Owner Name** (`boat_owner_name`, Text, Required): Enter your name as the boat owner.
* **Email Address** (`boat_owner_email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you with service reminders and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`boat_owner_phone`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you urgently.
* **Boat Name** (`boat_name`, Text, Not Required): Enter the name of your boat.
* **Boat Manufacturer** (`boat_manufacturer`, Text, Required): Enter the manufacturer of your boat.
* **Year of Manufacture** (`boat_year`, Number, Required): Enter the year your boat was manufactured.
* **Boat Length in Feet** (`boat_length`, Number, Not Required): Enter the length of your boat in feet.
* **Engine Type** (`engine_type`, Select One, Required): Select the type of engine your boat has.
* **Date of Last Service** (`last_service_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date of your last service.
* **Type of Last Service** (`last_service_type`, Text, Not Required): Select the type of last service you received.
* **Name of Last Service Provider** (`service_provider`, Text, Not Required): Enter the name of the last service provider.
* **Current Engine Hours** (`engine_hours`, Number, Not Required): Enter your current engine hours.
* **Maintenance Schedule Adherence** (`maintenance_schedule_adherence`, Select One, Required): Select how well you follow the maintenance schedule.
* **Regular Maintenance Performed** (`regular_maintenance_items`, Select Multiple, Required): Select all the regular maintenance performed on your boat.
* **Recent Issues or Problems** (`recent_issues`, Text, Not Required): Enter any recent issues or problems you are experiencing.
* **Boat Warranty Status** (`warranty_status`, Select One, Required): Select the status of your boat warranty.
* **Where is the boat typically stored** (`storage_location`, Select One, Required): Select where your boat is typically stored.
* **Estimated Annual Usage Hours** (`annual_usage_hours`, Number, Not Required): Enter your estimated annual usage hours.
* **Satisfaction with Last Service** (`satisfaction_last_service`, Select One, Required): Select your satisfaction with the last service.
* **Would you use the same service provider again** (`preferred_service_provider`, Select One, Required): Select whether you would use the same service provider again.
* **Suggestions for Service Improvement** (`service_improvements`, Text, Not Required): Enter any suggestions for service improvement.
* **What service do you need next** (`next_service_needs`, Text, Not Required): Enter what service you need next.
