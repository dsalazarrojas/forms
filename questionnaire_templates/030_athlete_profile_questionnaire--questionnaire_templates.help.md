# Athlete Profile Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This questionnaire is designed to gather information about the athlete's personal background, academic and sports-related information, physical profile, and other relevant details that can help identify their unique characteristics and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by answering the questions in the Personal Background section, which includes questions about the athlete's name, date of birth, nationality, and place of birth.
2. Move on to the Academic and Sport Information section, where you will answer questions about their school or university, expected graduation year, current GPA, primary sport, secondary sports, and current team or club name.
3. Next, provide information about their physical profile, including height, weight, dominant hand or foot, and other relevant details.
4. In the Contact and Social Media section, provide your email address, phone number, Instagram handle, Twitter handle, and parent or guardian contact information.
5. Finally, answer the Additional Insights questions, which include your favorite professional athlete, favorite pre-game meal, and long-term career goals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name** (`athlete_full_name`, `text`, required): Your full name is required to identify you as an athlete. Please provide your name in the format "First Name Last Name".
* **Date of Birth** (`athlete_dob`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format "Month/Day/Year" (e.g., 02/12/1999).
* **Place of Birth** (`place_of_birth`, `text`, required): Provide the city and state or province where you were born.
* **Nationality** (`athlete_nationality`, `text`, required): Enter your country of origin or nationality.
* **Current School or University** (`school_university`, `text`, required): List the name of your current school or university.
* **Expected Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, `number`, required): Enter the year you expect to graduate.
* **Current Cumulative GPA** (`current_gpa`, `number`, optional): If applicable, provide your current cumulative GPA.
* **Primary Sport** (`primary_sport`, `text`, required): Choose the sport you are most passionate about or compete in.
* **Secondary Sport(s)** (`secondary_sports`, `text`, optional): List any other sports you compete in.
* **Current Team or Club Name** (`current_team`, `text`, required): Provide the name of your current team or club.
* **Position or Specialization** (`position_specialization`, `text`, required): Enter your position or specialization in the sport you compete in.
* **Height** (`height_cm`, `number`, required): Measure your height in centimeters.
* **Weight** (`weight_kg`, `number`, required): Measure your weight in kilograms.
* **Dominant Hand or Foot** (`dominant_side`, `select_one`, required): Choose your dominant hand or foot.
* **Email Address** (`athlete_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address in the format "example@domain.com".
* **Phone Number** (`athlete_phone`, `text`, required): Provide your phone number.
* **Instagram Handle** (`instagram_handle`, `text`, optional): If you have an Instagram account, provide your handle (e.g., @athlete_name).
* **Twitter / X Handle** (`twitter_handle`, `text`, optional): If you have a Twitter/X account, provide your handle (e.g., @athlete_name).
* **Parent or Guardian Contact Information** (`parent_contact_info`, `text`, required): Provide the name and phone number of your parent or guardian.
* **Who is your favorite professional athlete?** (`favorite_athlete`, `text`, optional): Choose your favorite professional athlete.
* **What is your favorite pre-game meal?** (`favorite_pregame_meal`, `text`, optional): Choose your favorite pre-game meal.
* **What are your long-term career goals?** (`career_goals`, `text`, optional): Enter your long-term career goals, both inside and outside of sports.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
- Use clear and concise language in your answers.
- If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
