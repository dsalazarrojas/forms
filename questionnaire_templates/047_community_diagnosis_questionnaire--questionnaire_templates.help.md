# Community Diagnosis Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Community Diagnosis Questionnaire is a tool designed to gather information about the needs and concerns of our community residents. Your feedback is crucial in helping us understand what's working well and what areas need improvement. This form collects both structured and unstructured data to ensure comprehensive coverage of the topics of interest to you.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name of Resident" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Provide your primary contact phone number in the "Primary Contact Phone Number" field.
4. Enter your residential address in the "Residential Address" field.
5. Indicate the areas and issues that matter most to you in the "Diagnosis and Needs Assessment" section.
6. Select your top priority area for attention or development in the "Which area in our community needs the most attention or development" field.
7. Choose any relevant issues that you see in our community in the "What are the biggest issues you see in our community" field. Select all that apply.
8. Rate your satisfaction with the community's conditions using the "Rate your overall satisfaction with our community's conditions" field.
9. Rate your satisfaction with our community services using the "Rate your satisfaction with current community services" field.
10. Indicate how well you feel engaged in community decision-making using the "How well do you feel engaged in community decision-making" field.
11. Provide any ideas for diagnosing and improving our community using the "What are your ideas for diagnosing and improving our community" field.
12. Enter your digital signature (full name) in the "Digital Signature (Type Full Name)" field.
13. Enter the date you complete this form in the "Date of Diagnosis" field.
14. Choose whether you'd like to receive our newsletter in the "I would like to receive the community center's newsletter" field.
15. Indicate how you heard about this questionnaire using the "How did you hear about this diagnosis questionnaire" field.
16. Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Person" and "Emergency Contact Phone Number" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Resident** (`resident_full_name_dia`, `text`, required): Enter your full name, as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`resident_email_dia`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for follow-up and results delivery.
* **Primary Contact Phone Number** (`resident_phone_dia`, `text`, required): Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Residential Address** (`resident_address_dia`, `text`, required): Enter your street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Diagnosis and Needs Assessment** (`section_diagnosis_needs_assess_dia`, `note`, required): Assess the areas and issues that matter most to you.
* **Which area in our community needs the most attention or development** (`community_needs_priority_dia`, `select_one`, required): Select the area that you feel needs the most attention or development.
* **What are the biggest issues you see in our community** (`top_community_issues_dia`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply regarding the issues you see in our community.
* **Rate your overall satisfaction with our community's conditions** (`overall_community_satisfaction_dia`, `number`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction using 1 (Very Dissatisfied) to 5 (Very Satisfied).
* **Rate your satisfaction with current community services** (`service_satisfaction_dia`, `number`, required): Rate your satisfaction with our services using 1 (Very Dissatisfied) to 5 (Very Satisfied).
* **How well do you feel engaged in community decision-making** (`community_engagement_opinion_dia`, `select_one`, required): Choose how well you feel engaged in our community decision-making using Very well Engaged, Somewhat Engaged, or Unengaged.
* **What are your ideas for diagnosing and improving our community** (`improvement_ideas_diagnosis_dia`, `text`, required): Provide any ideas for diagnosing and improving our community.
* **Digital Signature (Type Full Name)** (`resident_signature_digital_dia`, `text`, required): Enter your digital signature.
* **Date of Diagnosis** (`submission_date_final_dia`, `date`, required): Enter the date you complete this form.
* **I would like to receive the community center's newsletter** (`newsletter_opt_in_dia`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you'd like to receive our newsletter.
* **How did you hear about this diagnosis questionnaire** (`referral_source_dia`, `select_one`, required): Indicate how you heard about this questionnaire.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_contact_full_name_dia`, `text`, required): Enter the name of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone_dia`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of an emergency contact person.

## Tips

* Please be thorough and honest in your answers.
* Ensure that you're providing information about yourself and not someone else.
* If you have questions or need help, feel free to contact us.
* You can also reach out to us if you'd like to provide additional feedback or input.
