# Human System Pressure Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Human System Pressure Assessment form is a questionnaire designed to gather information about an individual's work-related stress and pressure. It aims to help identify potential stressors and provide a baseline for further assessment and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. For each question, select the response that best describes your current state or situation.
3. If you're unsure about any question, please ask for clarification or guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, text, required: false): This is a brief text field where you can introduce yourself and provide any additional context about your situation.
* **How do you feel?** (`how_do_you_feel`, select_one, required: false): Please select how you feel about your current work situation. You can choose from the following options:
	+ Very good
	+ Somewhat good
	+ Not very good
	+ Very bad
* **Workplace Pressure** (`workplace_pressure`, text, required: false): Describe any pressure you're experiencing at work, such as deadlines, workload, or other work-related stressors.
* **Work hours** (`work_hours`, number, required: false): Report the number of hours you work per week.
* **Work experience** (`work_experience`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your work experience levels from the following options:
	+ 1-5 years
	+ 5-10 years
	+ 10+ years
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required: false): Choose your role at work from the following options:
	+ CEO
	+ Manager
	+ Employee
* **Stress Symptoms** (`stress_symptoms`, text, required: false): Describe any stress symptoms you're experiencing, such as anxiety, fatigue, or burnout.
* **Stress Factors** (`stress_factors`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the stress factors that contribute to your stress, such as:
	+ Lack of work-life balance
	+ Lack of recognition
	+ Conflict with colleagues
	+ Poor communication
* **Support Services** (`support_services`, select_one, required: false): Choose the support services you're currently using or would like to use:
	+ Mental health support
	+ Financial support
	+ Career development
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, time, required: false): Report a specific time for follow-up or any upcoming events.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, required: false): Describe the next steps you plan to take to manage your work-related stress and pressure.

Note: The required field status indicates whether a field is mandatory to complete the form.
