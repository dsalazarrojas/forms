# Hair History Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair History Questionnaire is a form designed to gather information about a person's hair history, including medical and personal details. This data helps healthcare professionals and stylists make informed decisions about treatments and recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Read each section carefully and answer the questions accurately.
* For each question, choose the best answer from the options provided, or fill in the text fields as required.
* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional)
    Enter the client's name as they are referred to in their records.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, optional)
    Please enter the client's date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 12/25/1990).
* **Hair Color** (`hair_color`, select_one, optional)
    Select 'Yes' if you have a specific hair color or 'No' if you have none (e.g., bald or shaved head).
* **Hair Condition** (`hair_condition`, select_multiple, optional)
    Select all the hair conditions that apply to you, such as dry, damaged, or overprocessed hair.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, note, optional)
    Describe any medical conditions that affect your skin, scalp, or hair growth.
* **Medical Treatments** (`medical_treatments`, select_multiple, optional)
    Select any medical treatments you've received that may be relevant to your hair health, such as medication or surgery.
* **Hair Care** (`hair_care`, text, optional)
    Describe your current hair care routine, including shampoo, conditioner, and styling products used.
* **Hair Concerns** (`hair_concerns`, text, optional)
    List any specific hair concerns or issues you're experiencing, such as dandruff, itchiness, or thinning.
* **Previous Services** (`previous_services`, text, optional)
    List any previous hair treatments, surgeries, or other services you've received.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, select_multiple, optional)
    Select any medical history that may be relevant to your hair health, such as family history or previous diagnoses.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, optional)
    Select any allergies you have that may be relevant to hair health or treatments.
