# Browsing History Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on respondents' internet browsing habits, including demographics, online behavior, and privacy concerns. The survey aims to gather insights on how individuals use the internet, their preferred devices, browsers, and websites, as well as their attitudes towards online advertising and data protection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Respondent ID** (optional) in the first field.
2. Select your **Age Group** from the provided options.
3. Choose your **Gender** (optional) from the options.
4. Enter your **Occupation** (optional) in the text field.
5. Select the **Primary Device for Internet Access** from the options.
6. Choose your **Daily Internet Usage** from the provided options.
7. Select your **Preferred Web Browser** from the options.
8. Select all the **Frequently Visited Website Types** that apply to you.
9. Choose your **Online Shopping Frequency** from the options.
10. Select all the **Social Media Platforms** you actively use.
11. Choose your level of **Privacy Concerns** from the options.
12. Select all the **Privacy Measures Taken** that apply to you.
13. Choose the **Primary Browsing Purpose** for your internet use.
14. Select the **Website Trust Factors** that influence your trust in a website.
15. Choose how often you **Click on Online Ads**.
16. Select how your **Browsing Habits Have Changed** in the past year.
17. Add any **Additional Comments** about your browsing habits in the text field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent ID**: Your optional identifier for this survey response.
* **Age Group**: Select your age range from 18 to 65 or older.
* **Gender**: Choose your gender identity.
* **Occupation**: Enter your current occupation (if applicable).
* **Primary Device for Internet Access**: Select the device you use most frequently for internet access.
* **Daily Internet Usage**: Choose how many hours you spend online per day.
* **Preferred Web Browser**: Select your most frequently used browser.
* **Frequently Visited Website Types**: Select all website types that apply to you.
* **Online Shopping Frequency**: Choose how often you shop online.
* **Social Media Platforms**: Select all social media platforms you actively use.
* **Privacy Concerns**: Choose your level of concern about online privacy.
* **Privacy Measures Taken**: Select all measures you take to protect your online privacy.
* **Primary Browsing Purpose**: Choose the main reason for your internet use.
* **Website Trust Factors**: Select all factors that influence your trust in a website.
* **Click on Online Ads**: Choose how often you click on online ads.
* **Browsing Habits Change**: Select how your browsing habits have changed in the past year.
* **Additional Comments**: Share any other thoughts about your browsing habits.
