# military_questionnaire_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal, employment, medical, and contact information from service members for administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Provide accurate and truthful information for each field.
3. Fill in the relevant fields with your service member's details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Member Info** (`service_member_info`, text): Enter the service member's name, rank, and ID number.
* **Service History** (`service_history`, text): Describe the service member's military experience, including their branch of service and years of service.
* **Personal Info** (`personal_info`, text): Enter the service member's personal details, such as their address and phone number.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text): Enter the contact information for the service member, including their email and emergency contact's name and number.
* **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, text): Enter any relevant medical information, such as medications or medical conditions.
* **Education** (`education`, text): Enter the service member's educational background, including their highest degree earned.
* **Employment History** (`employment_history`, text): Describe the service member's employment history, including their job title and years of service.
* **Military Service** (`military_service`, text): Describe the service member's military service, including their branch of service and years of service.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text): Enter the name and contact information for the service member's emergency contact.
* **Dependent Info** (`dependent_info`, text): Enter information about any dependents, including their names, ages, and relationship.
* **Military Status** (`military_status`, text): Describe the service member's current military status, such as active duty or veteran.
* **Discharge Info** (`discharge_info`, text): Enter any relevant information about the service member's discharge from service.
* **Veteran Status** (`veteran_status`, text): Describe the service member's veteran status.
* **Disability Status** (`disability_status`, text): Describe the service member's disability status.
* **Other Info** (`other_info`, text): Enter any additional information that may be relevant to the service member.
* **Admin Notes** (`admin_notes`, text): Enter any administrative notes or comments about the service member.
* **Medical Clearance** (`medical_clearance`, text): Enter any medical clearance information, such as a medical doctor's signature.
* **Discharge Medical Clearance** (`discharge_medical_clearance`, text): Enter any discharge medical clearance information.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text): Enter the service member's medical history, including any allergies or medical conditions.
* **Medical Procedures** (`medical_procedures`, text): Enter any medical procedures or treatments that the service member has undergone.
* **Physical Exam** (`physical_exam`, text): Enter the results of the service member's physical exam.
* **Mental Health** (`mental_health`, text): Enter any information about the service member's mental health.
* **Substance Use** (`substance_use`, text): Enter any information about the service member's substance use.
* **Housing Info** (`housing_info`, text): Enter the service member's housing information, including their address and lease duration.
* **Signature Info** (`signature_info`, text): Enter the service member's signature and date.
