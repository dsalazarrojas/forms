# Cardiovascular Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your knowledge about cardiovascular health, including recognizing signs of a heart attack, major risk factors, and effective preventive measures. It will also help us understand your confidence level about heart health topics and gather any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions carefully, selecting all that apply for each section.
2. For "Recognize heart attack signs," select all the symptoms that you think are common signs of a heart attack.
3. For "Major heart disease risk factors," select all the factors that you know are known risk factors for heart disease.
4. For "Effective preventive measures," select all methods that you know can help prevent heart disease.
5. If you have any additional comments or feedback about heart health, please type them in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Recognize heart attack signs** (`q_heart_attack_signs`, select multiple, required): This question asks you to select all the symptoms that you think are common signs of a heart attack. Common signs include:
	+ Chest discomfort
	+ Shortness of breath
	+ Nausea or lightheadedness
	+ Back or jaw pain
* **Major heart disease risk factors** (`q_heart_risk_factors`, select multiple, required): This question asks you to select all the known risk factors for heart disease. Choose all that apply:
	+ High blood pressure
	+ Smoking
	+ High cholesterol
	+ Diabetes
* **Effective preventive measures** (`q_preventive_measures`, select multiple, required): This question asks you to select all methods that can help prevent heart disease. Choose all that apply:
	+ Regular exercise
	+ Healthy diet
	+ Smoking cessation
	+ Regular screening
* **Self-rated heart health knowledge** (`self_rated_knowledge`, select one, optional): This question asks you to rate your confidence level about heart health topics.
	+ Very confident
	+ Somewhat confident
	+ Neutral
	+ Not confident
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): This is an open-ended question where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about heart health.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions carefully, as the form is designed to assess your knowledge about cardiovascular health.
* If you are unsure about any question, please select all that apply or choose the best answer you can.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
