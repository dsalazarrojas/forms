# Beta Product Early Adopter Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from early adopters of a beta product. Your input will help us understand your experiences, likes, and dislikes, enabling us to make improvements and create a better product.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully, providing accurate and honest answers.
2. Ensure you've answered all required fields ( marked as required).
3. Use the options provided for select fields.
4. For text fields, type your response in the field.
5. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the "Additional Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required)

* Enter your full name.

### 2. **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, `email`, required)

* We will use this to send follow-up surveys or communicate with you.
* Enter your email address.

### 3. **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, optional)

* If you have an organization, enter your company name.
* Otherwise, leave it blank.

### 4. **Your Role** (`role`, `text`, required)

* Enter your job title or role.

### 5. **Industry** (`industry`, `select_one`, required)

* Choose your industry from the list:
	+ Technology
	+ Healthcare
	+ Finance
	+ Education
	+ Retail
	+ Manufacturing
	+ Media and Entertainment
	+ Professional Services
	+ Government
	+ Other

### 6. **How Long Have You Had Beta Access** (`beta_access_duration`, `select_one`, required)

* Choose how long you've had beta access:
	+ Less than 1 week
	+ 1 to 2 weeks
	+ 2 to 4 weeks
	+ 1 to 3 months
	+ Over 3 months

### 7. **How Often Do You Use the Beta Product** (`usage_frequency`, `select_one`, required)

* Choose your usage frequency:
	+ Multiple times daily
	+ Once daily
	+ Several times a week
	+ Once a week
	+ Occasionally

### 8. **Primary Use Case** (`primary_use_case`, `text`, required)

* Describe the main purpose or use of the product.

### 9. **First Impression Rating** (`first_impression`, `select_one`, required)

* Rate your first impression of the product:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

### 10. **Ease of Use Rating** (`ease_of_use`, `select_one`, required)

* Rate how easy the product is to use:
	+ Very Easy
	+ Easy
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Difficult
	+ Very Difficult

### 11. **Feature Satisfaction Rating** (`feature_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required)

* Rate your satisfaction with current features:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied

### 12. **Missing Features** (`missing_features`, `text`, required)

* List any missing features you think are needed.

### 13. **Most Valuable Feature** (`most_valuable_feature`, `text`, required)

* Identify the feature you find most useful.

### 14. **Least Valuable Feature** (`least_valuable_feature`, `text`, optional)

* If applicable, list the least valuable feature.

### 15. **Did You Encounter Any Bugs** (`bugs_encountered`, `text`, required)

* Describe any bugs or issues you've experienced.

### 16. **Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, `select_one`, required)

* Rate the product's performance:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

### 17. **Design and User Interface Rating** (`design_rating`, `select_one`, required)

* Rate the visual design and interface:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

### 18. **Does This Product Solve Your Problem** (`solves_problem`, `select_one`, required)

* Rate how well the product addresses your needs:
	+ Completely Solves
	+ Mostly Solves
	+ Partially Solves
	+ Barely Solves
	+ Does Not Solve

### 19. **Would You Recommend This Product** (`would_recommend`, `select_one`, required)

* Would you recommend to colleagues or friends:
	+ Definitely Yes
	+ Probably Yes
	+ Not Sure
	+ Probably No
	+ Definitely No

### 20. **Would You Pay for This Product** (`would_pay`, `select_one`, required)

* Would you purchase the product:
	+ Definitely Yes
	+ Probably Yes
	+ Not Sure
	+ Probably No
	+ Definitely No

### 21. **What Improvements Would You Suggest** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, required)

* Suggest any improvements to the product.

### 22. **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, `text`, optional)

* Provide any other thoughts or comments.
