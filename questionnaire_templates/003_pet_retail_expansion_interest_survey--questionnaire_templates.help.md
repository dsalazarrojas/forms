# Pet Retail Expansion Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Retail Expansion Interest Survey is a form designed to gather information from retailers about their interest in expanding their pet retail offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all that apply for each question by choosing the relevant options from the dropdown lists.
2. For the region and pet type questions, choose one or multiple options as applicable.
3. In the feedback section, provide detailed comments about your current pet retail experience.
4. In the expansion regions section, indicate your interest in expanding to new regions.
5. Finally, provide any additional feedback or comments about your pet retail expansion interest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **pet_retail_expansion_interest_survey** (`pet_retail_expansion_interest_survey`): This is a required field to track the number of the survey.
* **region** (`region`): Select one or multiple regions you are currently operating in.
* **pet_type** (`pet_type`): Choose one or multiple pet types you are interested in selling or expanding into.
* **region_list** (`region_list`): Enter the number of regions you are interested in expanding into.
* **feedback** (`feedback`): Provide detailed comments about your current pet retail experience.
* **interest_level** (`interest_level`): Indicate your current level of interest in expanding your pet retail offerings.
* **expansion_regions** (`expansion_regions`): Indicate if you are interested in expanding into new regions.
* **feedback_details** (`feedback_details`): Provide additional comments or feedback.
* **expansion_channels** (`expansion_channels`): Indicate if you are interested in expanding through new channels.
* **pet_retail_expansion_interest_survey_id** (`pet_retail_expansion_interest_survey_id`): Enter the unique identifier for this survey.
* **assigned_tool** (`assigned_tool`): Choose the tool assigned to you for this task.
* **output_file** (`output_file`): Enter the name of the output file to be generated. 

Note: This form is not intended for completion without a valid reason for interest in expanding your pet retail offerings.
