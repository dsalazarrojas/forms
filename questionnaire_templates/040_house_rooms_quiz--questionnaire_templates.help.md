# House Rooms Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your house and your interaction with it.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply go through the questions and select the answers that apply to you. Each question is designed to gather specific information about your house. If you're unsure about any of the questions, please ask a team member for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **house_rooms_quiz_questions** (Select Multiple): Choose all the questions that apply to your house.
	+ This field is for selecting multiple questions that are relevant to your house.
* **house_rooms_quiz_answers** (Select One): Choose the correct answer to the question you selected in the previous field.
	+ This field is for providing a specific answer to the question you chose.
* **house_rooms_quiz_score** (Number): Enter your current house score.
	+ This field is for rating how well you think your house is performing in terms of its condition or features.
* **house_rooms_quiz_time** (Time): Enter the time you spend in your house each day.
	+ This field is for tracking how much time you use your house.
* **house_rooms_quiz_date** (Date): Enter the date you moved into your house or when you last updated your information.
	+ This field is for keeping track of when you last updated your house information.
* **house_rooms_quiz_note** (Note): Add any additional comments or notes about your house.
	+ This field is for providing any extra information that doesn't fit in the other fields.
* **house_rooms_quiz_email** (Email): Enter your email address.
	+ This field is for contacting you with any further questions or updates.
* **house_rooms_quiz_phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
	+ This field is for contacting you with any further questions or updates.

## Tips
Please be as accurate and detailed as possible when answering the questions. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to our team.
