# Refugee Youth Education Barriers Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to collect data on the barriers faced by refugee youth in accessing education, which will help organizations providing education services to better understand their needs and provide targeted support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
3. For the select_one fields, choose the most suitable option from the list provided.
4. For the select_multiple fields, choose all the relevant barriers that apply to you.
5. For the date field, select the date you wish to enroll in education services.
6. For the email field, enter your email address if you wish to be contacted for further information.
7. For the phone field, enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted for further information.
8. For the time field, select the approximate time you spend on education per week.
9. For the language field, select your primary language for communication.
10. You can save your progress and come back to complete the form at a later time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Refugee Youth Education Barriers Survey (1)**: This field is the title of this survey.
* **Age (2)**: Please select your age.
* **Education Level (3)**: Please select the highest level of education you have completed.
	+ Primary School
	+ Secondary School
	+ Higher Education
* **Barriers to Education (4)**: Please select all the barriers that you face in accessing education.
	+ Financial
	+ Language
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Needed Education Support (5)**: Please select the type of education support you need.
	+ Academic Support
	+ Psychological Support
	+ Social Support
* **Contact Person (6)**: Please enter the name and contact information of a person who can be contacted for further information.
* **School (7)**: Please select the type of school you are interested in enrolling in.
	+ Primary
	+ Secondary
	+ Tertiary
* **Language (8)**: Please select your primary language for communication.
	+ Arabic
	+ English
	+ Other
* **Time (9)**: Please select the approximate time you spend on education per week.
* **Time Period (10)**: Please select the date you wish to enroll in education services.
* **Email (11)**: Please enter your email address if you wish to be contacted for further information.
* **Phone (12)**: Please enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted for further information.
