# New Member Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The New Member Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information from new members about their background, contact details, and medical history. It helps us better understand who you are and how you can best support you during your time with us. Please complete this form to ensure we have up-to-date and accurate information about you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your information. Each field is labeled with a brief description to guide you through the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### New Member Info (Page 1)
* **New Member Info** (`new_member_info`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description about yourself, including your name, and any other relevant details you'd like to share.

### Email (Page 1)
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address. This will be used for communication purposes.

### Contact Phone (Page 2)
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.

### Contact Email (Page 2)
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your contact email address.

### Contact Phone 2 (Page 2)
* **Contact Phone 2** (`contact_phone_2`, text, required/optional): Enter another contact phone number (if applicable).

### Address (Page 3)
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your home address.

### Zip Code (Page 3)
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number, required/optional): Enter your zip code.

### Country (Page 3)
* **Country** (`country`, text, required/optional): Enter your country of residence.

### Education (Page 4)
* **Education** (`education`, text, required/optional): Enter your educational background.

### Occupation (Page 4)
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, text, required/optional): Enter your occupation.

### Experience (Page 4)
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, required/optional): Describe your work experience.

### Interests (Page 5)
* **Interests** (`interests`, text, required/optional): Enter your personal interests.

### Why Counseling (Page 5)
* **Why Counseling** (`why_counseling`, text, required/optional): Describe why you're seeking counseling.

### Counseling History (Page 6)
* **Counseling History** (`counseling_history`, text, required/optional): Enter any previous counseling history.

### Medical History (Page 7)
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): Enter any medical history.

### Medication (Page 7)
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, required/optional): Enter any medication you're taking.

### Emergency Contact (Page 8)
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.

### Relationship (Page 8)
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required/optional): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.

### Other (Page 8)
* **Other** (`emergency_contact_relationship_other`, text, required/optional): If your relationship with your emergency contact is not listed, please describe it here.

### Other Details (Page 8)
* **Other Details** (`emergency_contact_relationship_other_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any other details about your emergency contact.

### Name (Page 8)
* **Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.

### Phone (Page 8)
* **Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.

### Email (Page 8)
* **Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.

### Other Details 2 (Page 9)
* **Other Details 2** (`emergency_contact_relationship_other_details_2`, text, required/optional): Enter any other details about your emergency contact.

### Terms and Conditions (Page 9)
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, select_one, required): Please check the box below if you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.

Note: Please ensure you complete all required fields before submitting the form.
