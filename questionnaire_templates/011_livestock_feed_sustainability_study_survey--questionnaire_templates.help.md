# Livestock Feed Sustainability Study Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Livestock Feed Sustainability Study Survey is a form designed to collect information from farmers or farm owners about their current practices and challenges related to feed sustainability. This data will be used to improve feed sustainability in the livestock industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each question before proceeding.
2. Complete each section of the form according to your actual practices and experiences.
3. Answer each question as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
4. If you have any challenges related to feed sustainability, describe them in the "Challenges" section.
5. Add any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" section.
6. Select your preferred contact method for follow-up questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Farm Details**: Please provide a brief description of your farm, including its location, size, and main livestock operations.
* **What are your current feed practices?**: Select one of the options that best describes your current feed practices. If none of the options match, choose "Other (please specify)" and provide a brief explanation.
	+ Grains: You source your feeds from grain-based sources.
	+ Forages: You source your feeds from forage-based sources.
	+ Other (please specify): You have a different feed source that is not listed.
* **Where do you source your feeds?**: Select all that apply to describe where you source your feeds. You can select multiple options.
	+ Local feed mills: You source your feeds from local mills.
	+ National feed mills: You source your feeds from national mills.
	+ Other (please specify): You have a different feed source that is not listed.
* **What sustainability measures do you currently have in place?**: Select all that apply to describe the sustainability measures you have in place. You can select multiple options.
	+ Organic farming: You are certified organic.
	+ Regenerative agriculture: You practice regenerative agriculture.
	+ Other (please specify): You have a different sustainability measure that is not listed.
* **What are your challenges related to feed sustainability?**: If you have challenges related to feed sustainability, provide a detailed description of them in this field.
* **Do you have any additional comments?**: If you have any additional information or comments related to feed sustainability, provide them here.
* **How would you like to be contacted?**: Select one of the options that best describes how you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions.
	+ Email: You would like to be contacted via email.
	+ Phone: You would like to be contacted via phone.
	+ Other (please specify): You have a different contact preference that is not listed.
