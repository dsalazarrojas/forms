# Business Diagnosis Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Business Diagnosis Questionnaire is designed to help businesses identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions about their operations and strategy. This form gathers essential information about a business's current state and outlook, allowing you to assess your business's performance and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer each question truthfully, based on your business's current state.
2.  For questions with numerical answers, please provide the most recent data available.
3.  For questions with multiple-choice answers, select the option that best describes your business's current situation.
4.  If you're unsure about any question, please provide a brief explanation or comment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): This field asks for the name of your business, which will be used to identify and track your responses. Please enter your business's official name.
*   **Type of Business** (`business_type`, text, required): In this field, please describe your business's type, such as retail, services, or manufacturing.
*   **Years in Operation** (`years_operating`, number, required): This field asks for the number of years your business has been operational. Provide the most recent data available.
*   **Annual Revenue** (`annual_revenue`, number, required): Please provide your business's current annual revenue.
*   **Number of Employees** (`employee_count`, number, required): In this field, enter the current number of employees working for your business.
*   **Financial Health** (text, note, required): This section provides additional space for you to describe your business's current financial health.
*   **Revenue Trend** (select_one, required): Choose the option that best describes your revenue's trend: Increasing, Stable, Declining, or Volatile.
*   **Profit Margin** (`profit_margin`, number, required): Please enter your business's current profit margin percentage.
*   **Cash Flow Status** (select_one, required): Select the option that best describes your cash flow status: Strong positive, Adequate, Tight, Struggling, or Crisis.
*   **Debt Level** (`debt_level`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the amount of outstanding debt.
*   **Cost Control Status** (select_one, required): Choose the option that best describes your cost control: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
*   **Operational Issues** (text, note, optional): This field provides additional space for you to describe any operational issues your business is facing.
*   **Major Problems Facing Business** (`major_problems`, text, required): In this field, list the most significant issues affecting your business.
*   **Employee Productivity** (select_one, required): Select the option that best describes your team's productivity: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
*   **Quality Issues** (text, optional): This field is for you to describe any product or service quality issues affecting your business.
*   **Process Efficiency** (select_one, required): Choose the option that best describes your operations' efficiency: Very Efficient, Efficient, Average, Inefficient, or Very Inefficient.
*   **Market Performance** (text, note, optional): This section provides additional space for you to describe your business's current market performance.
*   **Market Share Position** (select_one, required): Choose the option that best describes your business's market position: Market Leader, Strong Competitor, Stable Player, Struggling, or New/Emerging.
*   **Customer Satisfaction** (select_one, required): Select the option that best describes your customers' satisfaction: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Average, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
*   **Competition Threat Level** (select_one, required): Choose the option that best describes the threat level from competitors: Low Threat, Moderate Threat, High Threat, or Critical Threat.
*   **Growth Strategy** (text, note, optional): This field provides additional space for you to describe your current growth strategy.
*   **Current Growth Rate** (`growth_rate`, number, required): Please enter your business's current growth rate as a percentage.
*   **Strategic Focus** (`strategic_focus`, text, required): In this field, describe your current strategic focus.
*   **Future Outlook** (select_one, required): Choose the option that best describes your perspective on the future: Very Optimistic, Optimistic, Uncertain, Pessimistic, or Very Pessimistic.

## Tips

*   Please answer each question truthfully, based on your business's current state.
*   For numerical answers, provide the most recent data available.
*   If you're unsure about any question, please provide a brief explanation or comment.
*   This form is designed to provide you with a comprehensive picture of your business's performance.
*   By completing this form, you'll be able to identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions about your operations and strategy.
