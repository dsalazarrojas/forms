# paleo_diet_assessment_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The paleo diet assessment survey is designed to gauge your current relationship with the paleo diet. The form will ask about your dietary habits, restrictions, and any dietary advice you have received.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully and to the best of your ability.
2. If a question does not apply to your situation, please select "No".
3. For the select_multiple and select_one fields, you can select multiple options.
4. You can add additional comments if you need to provide more information.
