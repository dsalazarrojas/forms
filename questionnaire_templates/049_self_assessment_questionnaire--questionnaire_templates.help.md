# Self Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This self-assessment questionnaire is designed to help you assess your current skills, knowledge, or experiences to identify areas for improvement. Fill out this form to provide a comprehensive overview of your skills and knowledge, and to help us better understand your needs and provide guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the pages and fill out the required fields. Each page contains one or more questions or prompts. Answer these questions to the best of your ability, and use the available space for additional comments or explanations if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Self Assessment Questionnaire Page 1** (`1`, `date`, optional): This page is where you start by indicating your current date.
* **Self Assessment Questionnaire Page 2** (`2`, `text`, optional): This page is a free-text field where you can provide a brief description of your current situation or a relevant comment.
* **Self Assessment Questionnaire Page 3** (`3`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the available options to indicate your current level of proficiency or experience with a particular skill or knowledge area.
* **Self Assessment Questionnaire Page 4** (`4`, `note`, optional): This page is a space for you to add any additional comments or explanations about your answers on previous pages.
