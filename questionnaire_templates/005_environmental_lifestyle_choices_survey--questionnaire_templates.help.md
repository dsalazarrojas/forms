# Environmental Lifestyle Choices Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their environmental lifestyle choices, including their energy usage, transportation methods, and waste management practices.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully and ensure you have answered all questions relevant to the page.
2. Fill out each field according to the guidelines below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: First Page
* **First Page** (`first_page`, Note, not required): This page serves as an introduction to the survey and provides instructions on how to complete it.
### Page 2: Second Page
* **Second Page** (`second_page`, Note, not required): This page is a continuation of the introduction and serves as a transition to the main survey questions.
### Page 3: Third Page
* **Third Page** (`third_page`, Note, not required): This page provides further instructions and guidance on how to complete the survey.
### Page 4: User Information
* **User Information** (`user_info`, Text, not required): Please provide your contact information, including your name and any relevant details we should know about you.
*   *Please make sure to enter your name and other relevant contact details in this field. This will allow us to follow up with you in case of any issues.*
### Page 5: User Email
* **User Email** (`user_email`, Email, not required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your survey response.
### Page 6: User Phone
* **User Phone** (`user_phone`, Text, not required): Enter your phone number, if you would like us to contact you by phone.
### Page 7: First Question
* **First Question** (`first_question`, Select One, not required): This question asks about your energy usage habits. Choose the correct option that best describes your situation.
### Page 8: Second Question
