# Cost Of Inaction Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Cost Of Inaction Assessment" form is designed to help users evaluate the potential consequences of delaying a project. This form guides users through a series of questions to assess the expected cost of delaying the project, the risk factors involved, and the impact on project outcomes, team morale, and project status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the project's current status and potential risks associated with delaying it.
2. Answer the questions on the following pages:
	* Introduction: A brief overview of the project and its context.
	* Delay Time: Estimate the expected delay time in months.
	* Expected Cost: Enter the expected cost of delaying the project.
	* Risk Factors: Select all applicable risk factors (Financial loss, Time loss, Resource loss).
	* Impact on Project Outcomes: Choose the impact on project outcomes (Financially impactful, Not financially impactful).
	* Cost of Delaying: Select the cost of delaying the project (Low, Medium, High).
	* Impact on Team: Choose the impact on team morale (Very Low, Low, High).
	* Risk Level: Select the risk level of the project (Low, Medium, High).
	* Mitigation Strategies: Choose all applicable strategies (Identify and assess, Develop and implement, Monitor and adjust).
	* Next Steps: Choose the next steps to take (Implement mitigation strategies, Continue planning, Delay the project).
	* Project Status: Select the current project status (Active, In progress, Completed).
	* Delay Date: Enter the expected delay date.
	* Delay Time: Enter the delay time in a 24-hour format (HH:MM).
	* Comments: Add any additional comments about the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief overview of the project and its context.
* **Delay Time (1)**: Enter a number of months that you expect to delay the project. This will help you understand the potential consequences of the delay.
* **Expected Cost (2)**: Enter a number that represents the expected cost of delaying the project. This will help you identify potential financial implications.
* **Risk Factors (4)**: Select all applicable risk factors that may impact the project. These include Financial loss, Time loss, and Resource loss.
* **Impact on Project Outcomes (5)**: Choose the impact on project outcomes, either Financially impactful or Not financially impactful.
* **Cost of Delaying (6)**: Select the cost of delaying the project, either Low, Medium, or High.
* **Impact on Team (7)**: Choose the impact on team morale, with Very Low, Low, or High.
* **Risk Level (8)**: Select the risk level of the project, with Low, Medium, or High.
* **Mitigation Strategies (9)**: Choose all applicable strategies to mitigate the risks, such as Identify and assess, Develop and implement, or Monitor and adjust.
* **Next Steps (10)**: Choose the next steps to take, such as Implement mitigation strategies, Continue planning, or Delay the project.
* **Project Status (11)**: Select the current project status, with Active, In progress, or Completed.
* **Delay Date (12)**: Enter the expected delay date in a format that you can use for planning purposes.
* **Delay Time (13)**: Enter the delay time in a 24-hour format (HH:MM) to provide more precise timing.
* **Comments (14)**: Add any additional comments or observations about the project that may be relevant to the assessment.

This help guide will walk you through the form, ensuring you complete it accurately and effectively.
