# Film Director Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from film directors, including their bio, contact details, filmography, and other relevant information. The form is intended to be used for research or other purposes where accurate and detailed information about film directors is needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your bio, including a brief introduction and any other relevant information about yourself.
2. Select the correct contact methods that you have, such as Email, Phone, or Website.
3. Indicate whether you have a filmography (list of films you've worked on).
4. Answer the question about whether you have been in an interview (if applicable).
5. If necessary, provide a bio image.
6. Indicate whether the film you are associated with is a true or false statement.
7. Answer questions about the production (if applicable).
8. Answer questions about the festival (if applicable).
9. Answer questions about the team member (if applicable).
10. Provide any additional information about the production company, country, and year of the film.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **bio**: A text field where you can provide a brief introduction about yourself as a film director.
* **contact_info**: A field where you can select your contact information, such as Email, Phone, or Website.
* **filmography**: A field where you can indicate whether you have a filmography or not.
* **interview**: A field where you can answer whether you have been in an interview (if applicable).
* **bio_image**: A text field where you can provide a bio image if necessary.
* **film**: A field where you can indicate whether the film you are associated with is true or false.
* **production**: A field where you can answer questions about the production (if applicable).
* **festival**: A field where you can answer questions about the festival (if applicable).
* **team_member**: A field where you can indicate whether you are a team member (if applicable).
* **production_company**: A text field where you can provide any additional information about the production company.
* **production_country**: A text field where you can provide any additional information about the production country.
* **production_year**: A text field where you can provide any additional information about the production year.
* **film_director**: A text field where you can provide any additional information about yourself as a film director.
* **film_director_bio**: A text field where you can provide a bio about yourself as a film director.
* **film_director_img**: A text field where you can provide an image of yourself as a film director.
* **film_name**: A text field where you can provide the name of the film.
* **film_year**: A text field where you can provide the year of the film.
* **film_genre**: A text field where you can provide the genre of the film.

Note: All fields are optional and can be filled in according to your convenience.
