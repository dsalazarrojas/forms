# Matching Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Matching Skills Assessment form is a tool to collect information about your personal details, including name, contact information, and professional experience, to help match your skills with relevant opportunities.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Describe your professional experience in the "experience" field.
6. List your relevant skills in the "skills" field.
7. Indicate your current role or position in the "role" field.
8. Describe your current or recent project in the "project" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (first_name, text, optional): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last name** (last_name, text, optional): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Experience** (experience, text, optional): Describe your relevant professional experience to help match your skills with opportunities.
* **Skills** (skills, text, optional): List your relevant skills and expertise to help match you with suitable opportunities.
* **Role** (role, text, optional): Indicate your current or recent role or position to help match your skills with job requirements.
* **Project** (project, text, optional): Describe your current or recent project to provide context to your skills and experience.
