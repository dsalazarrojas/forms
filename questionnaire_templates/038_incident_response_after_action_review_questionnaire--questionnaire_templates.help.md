# Incident Response After Action Review Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and review incidents that have occurred. It helps to gather details about the incident, identify areas for improvement, and provide recommendations for future prevention.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Date and Time**: Enter the date and time that the incident occurred.
2. **Location**: Provide the address of where the incident took place.
3. **Description**: Briefly describe what happened, including the circumstances leading up to and following the incident.
4. **Type**: Select the type of incident that occurred (Security, Network, or Other).
5. **Affected Units**: Choose which teams or departments were affected by the incident.
6. **Responding Teams**: Identify which teams or departments were involved in responding to the incident.
7. **Status**: Indicate the current status of the incident (Closed, Open, or In Progress).
8. **Root Cause**: Identify the root cause of the incident.
9. **Consequence**: Select the consequences of the incident (Financial, Operational, or Security).
10. **Recommendations**: Provide any relevant recommendations for preventing future incidents.
11. **Notes**: Include any additional comments or details about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date**: Enter the date and time of the incident. This helps to ensure that the incident is recorded accurately and can be referred to in the future.
* **Incident Location**: Enter the address of where the incident occurred. This can be helpful in determining the root cause and impact of the incident.
* **Incident Description**: Provide a brief description of what happened, including the circumstances leading up to and following the incident. This can help to identify patterns or causes that may lead to similar incidents.
* **Incident Type**: Select the type of incident that occurred (Security, Network, or Other). This helps to categorize and track incidents for future reference.
* **Incident Affected Units**: Choose which teams or departments were affected by the incident. This helps to identify which teams need to be involved in responding to and recovering from the incident.
* **Incident Responding Teams**: Identify which teams or departments were involved in responding to the incident. This helps to ensure that all relevant teams are aware of their roles and responsibilities.
* **Incident Status**: Indicate the current status of the incident (Closed, Open, or In Progress). This helps to track the incident's progress and ensure that it is being addressed.
* **Root Cause**: Identify the root cause of the incident. This can help to prevent similar incidents from happening in the future.
* **Incident Consequence**: Select the consequences of the incident (Financial, Operational, or Security). This helps to determine the impact of the incident and identify potential areas for improvement.
* **Recommendations**: Provide any relevant recommendations for preventing future incidents. This can help to improve incident response procedures and reduce the risk of similar incidents occurring.
* **Incident Notes**: Include any additional comments or details about the incident. This can be helpful for future reference and for identifying patterns or trends.

Note: The additional "Incident Recommendation" fields are intended for users to provide more detailed recommendations, if needed.
