# Global Health Equity Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Global Health Equity Awareness Survey is designed to gather information about your experiences with discrimination in the healthcare system. Please answer these questions to help us understand the prevalence and impact of discrimination in healthcare.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Please note that some questions may not apply to your situation.
3. Select all applicable options for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your current role** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_1`, select_one): Please select your current role in the healthcare system, such as Community Health Worker, Clinician, Health Manager, or Other.
* **Have you ever experienced discrimination in the healthcare system** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_2`, select_multiple): Answer yes or no to whether you have ever experienced discrimination in the healthcare system.
* **What kind of discrimination have you experienced** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_3`, select_multiple): Select all that apply from the options provided, such as Age, Disability, Ethnicity, Gender, Marital status, Race, Religion, Sexual orientation.
* **How long did you experience discrimination** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_4` / `global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_9`, date/number): If you answered yes to discrimination, please estimate how long you experienced it. You can select a date or choose how many months it lasted.
* **What was the result of discrimination** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_6`, text): Please describe what happened as a result of the discrimination you experienced.
* **Have you reported discrimination to anyone** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_7`, select_one): Answer yes or no whether you have reported discrimination to anyone.
* **Were you satisfied with the result** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_8`, select_multiple): Select all that apply from the options provided, such as True or False.
* **Most recent discrimination event** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_11`, text): Please describe the most recent discrimination event you experienced.
* **Did you experience discrimination in the past 30 days** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_12`, select_multiple): Answer yes or no to whether you experienced discrimination in the past 30 days.
* **Have you experienced discrimination more than 30 days ago** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_13`, select_multiple): Answer yes or no to whether you experienced discrimination more than 30 days ago.
* **Main reason for discrimination** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_14`, text): Please describe the main reason you think you were discriminated against.
* **Discrimination due to Age** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_15`, select_multiple): Answer yes or no to whether you experienced discrimination due to Age.
* **Discrimination due to Disability** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_16`, select_multiple): Answer yes or no to whether you experienced discrimination due to Disability.
* **Discrimination due to Ethnicity** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_17`, select_multiple): Answer yes or no to whether you experienced discrimination due to Ethnicity.
* **Discrimination due to Gender** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_18`, select_multiple): Answer yes or no to whether you experienced discrimination due to Gender.
* **Discrimination due to Marital status** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_19`, select_multiple): Answer yes or no to whether you experienced discrimination due to Marital status.
* **Discrimination due to Race** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_20`, select_multiple): Answer yes or no to whether you experienced discrimination due to Race.
* **Discrimination due to Religion** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_21`, select_multiple): Answer yes or no to whether you experienced discrimination due to Religion.
* **Discrimination due to Sexual orientation** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_22`, select_multiple): Answer yes or no to whether you experienced discrimination due to Sexual orientation.
* **Think you will experience discrimination in the future** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_23`, select_multiple): Answer yes or no to whether you think you will experience discrimination in the future.
* **Have you experienced other kind of discrimination** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_24`, select_multiple): Answer yes or no to whether you experienced other kind of discrimination.
* **How many people have experienced discrimination** (`global_health_equity_awareness_survey_page_25`, text): Please estimate how many people you believe have experienced discrimination in the healthcare system.
