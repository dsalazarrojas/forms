# Tea Making Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on your tea making habits, preferences, and experiences. Please answer the questions honestly to help us better understand your behavior and provide a more personalized experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the options that best fit your answers for each question.
2. Be honest and thorough in answering each question.
3. If you have any additional comments or thoughts, feel free to add them in the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (`General Info`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): This section is optional, but please select all that apply to you:
  + Yes: If you have general experience with tea making.
  + No: If you don't have experience with tea making.
* **Preferred Tea** (`preferred_tea`, `select_one`, required: `true`): Choose the type of tea you prefer from the list:
  + English Breakfast
  + Green Tea
  + Herbal Tea
  + White Tea
  + Yellow Tea
  + Yellow Tea with Honey
* **Brewing Method** (`brewing_method`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Check all the methods you prefer:
  + Yes: If you use this method.
  + No: If you don't use this method.
* **Temperature** (`temperature`, `select_one`, required: `true`): Select your preferred temperature for brewing tea:
  + 80
  + 85
  + 90
  + 95
* **Steep Time** (`steep_time`, `select_one`, required: `true`): Choose your preferred steep time for tea:
  + 15
  + 30
  + 45
  + 60
* **Sweeteners** (`sweeteners`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Check all that apply:
  + Yes: If you use sugar, honey, or other sweeteners.
  + No: If you don't use sweeteners.
* **Additives** (`additives`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Check all that apply:
  + Yes: If you add milk, sugar, or other ingredients to your tea.
  + No: If you don't add anything.
* **Serving Size** (`serving_size`, `select_one`, required: `true`): Choose your preferred serving size:
  + 1
  + 2
  + 3
  + 4
  + 5
* **Tea Shop** (`tea_shop`, `select_one`, required: `true`): Select the name of your preferred tea shop:
  + Tea Shop A
  + Tea Shop B
  + Tea Shop C
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, `select_one`, required: `true`): Choose how often you consume tea:
  + Daily
  + 2-3 times a week
  + Less than 2 times a week
  + Never
* **Tea Brewing Habits** (`tea_brewing_habits`, `note`, required: `false`): Add any additional information about your tea brewing habits (optional).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: `false`): Add any notes or thoughts you'd like to share about tea making (optional).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: `false`): Share any comments or feedback about this form or tea making (optional).
* **General Comments** (`general_comments`, `note`, required: `false`): Add any general comments about your experience (optional).
* **Favorite Brand** (`favorite_brand`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Check if you have a favorite tea brand:
  + Yes
  + No
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: `true`): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: `true`): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: `true`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: `true`): Enter your phone number.
* **Tea Brewing Skills** (`tea_brewing_skills`, `number`, required: `false`): Rate your tea brewing skills (1-5).
* **Tea Brewing Habits Score** (`tea_brewing_habits_score`, `number`, required: `false`): Rate your tea brewing habits (1-5).
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: `true`): Enter the current date.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: `true`): Enter the current time.
* **Tea Type** (`tea_type`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Check if you prefer a specific type of tea:
  + Yes
  + No
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, required: `false`): Add any additional comments about your experience (optional).
