# Favorite Quotes Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Favorite Quotes Survey is a form designed to collect information about quotes that users find inspiring, motivational, or meaningful. The survey aims to gather details about the quote, including its title, author, category, and the user's rating and favorite image or video associated with it. This information will be used to create a database of quotes that users can browse and explore.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Favorite Quotes Survey, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the quote title in the **Quote Title** field.
2. Provide the name of the quote author in the **Quote Author** field.
3. Select the category of the quote from the options provided in the **Quote Category** field.
4. Enter a brief description of the quote in the **Quote Description** field.
5. Choose the date you read the quote in the **Date Quote Was Read** field.
6. Select your favorite quote from the options provided in the **Favorite Quote** field.
7. Rate the quote with a score between 1 and 5 in the **Rating Score** field.
8. Provide the source of the quote in the **Quote Source** field.
9. Add any additional information about the quote source in the **Quote Source Link** field.
10. Upload an image related to the quote in the **Quote Image** field.
11. Upload a file related to the quote in the **Quote File** field.
12. Upload an image related to your favorite quote in the **Favorite Quote Image** field.
13. Add any audio or video related to the quote in the **Quote Audio** and **Quote Video** fields.
14. Upload an audio or video file related to the quote in the **Quote Audio File** and **Quote Video File** fields.
15. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
16. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
17. Provide the time you read the quote in the **Date Quote Was Read Time** field.
18. Enter the date you read the quote in the format of year, month, day (YYYY-MM-DD) in the **Date Quote Was Read (YMD)** field.
19. Enter the date and time you read the quote in the **Date Quote Was Read (DD MMM YYYY)** field.
20. Enter the date and time you read the quote in the format of year-month-day (YYYY-MM-DD) in the **Date Quote Was Read Time (YMD)** field.
21. Add any additional notes about the quote in the **Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quote Title** (Text): Enter the title of the quote.
* **Quote Author** (Text): Enter the name of the author of the quote.
* **Quote Category** (Select Multiple): Select the category of the quote from Drama, Comedy, Romance, or Thriller.
* **Quote Description** (Text): Enter a brief description of the quote.
* **Date Quote Was Read** (Date): Enter the date you read the quote.
* **Favorite Quote** (Select One): Select your favorite quote from the provided options.
* **Rating Score** (Number): Rate the quote with a score between 1 and 5.
* **Quote Source** (Text): Enter the source of the quote.
* **Quote Source Link** (Text): Enter any additional information about the quote source.
* **Quote Image** (Text): Upload an image related to the quote.
* **Quote File** (Text): Upload a file related to the quote.
* **Favorite Quote Image** (Text): Upload an image related to your favorite quote.
* **Quote Audio** (Text): Add any audio related to the quote.
* **Quote Video** (Text): Add any video related to the quote.
* **Quote Audio File** (Text): Upload an audio file related to the quote.
* **Quote Video File** (Text): Upload a video file related to the quote.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
* **Date Quote Was Read Time** (Time): Enter the time you read the quote.
* **Date Quote Was Read (YMD)** (Date): Enter the date you read the quote in the format of year, month, day (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Date Quote Was Read (DD MMM YYYY)** (Date): Enter the date and time you read the quote in the format of day, month, year (DD MMM YYYY).
* **Date Quote Was Read Time (YMD)** (Time): Enter the date and time you read the quote in the format of year, month, day (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Notes** (Text): Add any additional notes about the quote.
