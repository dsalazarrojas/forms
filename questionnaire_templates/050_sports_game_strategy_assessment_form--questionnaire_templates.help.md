# sports_game_strategy_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help assess an individual's or a team's sports game strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer all the fields as accurately as possible.
2. For each field, provide as much detail as you need to complete the question.
3. If you need assistance or clarification on any of the questions or fields, don't hesitate to reach out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Preparation** (`team_preparation`, text, not required): Describe the team's preparation for the game, including any specific strategies or routines they followed.
* **Game Adjustments** (`game_adjustments`, text, not required): Explain any adjustments made during the game, such as changes in strategy, team composition, or player positions.
* **Team Communication** (`team_communication`, text, not required): Describe the team's communication dynamics, including how players and coaches interact with each other.
* **Overall Strategy** (`overall_strategy`, text, not required): Outline the team's overall game strategy, including any specific objectives or goals.
* **Pre-Game Planning** (`pre_game_planning`, text, not required): Describe the team's pre-game preparations, such as warm-ups, team talks, or other specific activities.
* **Coaching Approach** (`coaching_approach`, text, not required): Explain the coaching style, including any specific methods or techniques used to motivate or instruct players.
* **Player Morale** (`player_morale`, text, not required): Describe the team's player morale, including any factors that may have affected it, such as injuries, conflicts, or celebrations.
* **Game Situation** (`game_situation`, text, not required): Describe the game situation, including any unique circumstances or challenges faced by the team.
* **Strategy Development** (`strategy_development`, text, not required): Outline the team's strategy development process, including any specific steps or decisions made during the game.
* **Team Workload** (`team_workload`, text, not required): Describe the team's workload, including any specific challenges or demands faced during the game.
* **Player Performance** (`player_performance`, text, not required): Evaluate the team's player performance, including any standout players or notable accomplishments.
* **Coaches' Involvement** (`coaches_involvement`, text, not required): Explain the coaches' involvement, including any specific roles or responsibilities they took during the game.

## Tips

* Be as detailed and specific as possible when completing each field.
* If you are unsure about a field, refer to the question or description provided to help guide your response.
* If you need to add any additional information or context, feel free to include it in the provided text fields.
