# Integrative Health Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Integrative Health Assessment is designed to help you evaluate your current state of integrative health, which encompasses physical, mental, and emotional well-being. This form will ask you questions to gather information about your health care approach, wellness principles, medical conditions, and other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability. You can select one or multiple options for questions that ask for multiple responses.
2. For text fields, provide a brief description or answer in the required format.
3. For selection fields, choose one or more options that apply to you.
4. Ensure you complete all required questions, even if you're unsure or haven't experienced something specific.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wellness Coaching** (id: 1, <name> Wellness Coaching, <type> text, required: false):
  This field is for you to briefly describe or explain your current wellness coaching or guidance practices, such as seeking advice from professionals or following self-help resources.
* **Health Care** (id: 2, <name> Health Care, <type> select_one, required: false): 
  Select one or more of the following healthcare approaches that you currently use or have used:
  + Allopathic: Traditional Western medicine.
  + Holistic: Considering the whole person - body, mind, and spirit.
  + Alternative: Non-traditional practices like acupuncture or herbalism.
  + Integrative: Combining multiple approaches to achieve better health.
  + Naturopathic: Focusing on natural remedies and prevention.
  + Osteopathic: Emphasizing the body's self-regulation and self-healing capacity.
* **Wellness Principles** (id: 3, <name> Wellness Principles, <type> select_multiple, required: false): 
  Choose one or more of the following wellness principles that you currently practice or find important:
  + Mindfulness: Being present and aware in the moment.
  + Goals: Setting and working towards personal objectives.
  + Nutrition: Eating a balanced and healthy diet.
  + Physical Activity: Engaging in regular exercise or physical activity.
  + Sleep: Prioritizing adequate rest and sleep.
* **Health History** (id: 4, <name> Health History, <type> note, required: false): 
  Please provide a detailed description of your health history, including any medical conditions, injuries, or significant health events.
* **Medical Conditions** (id: 5, <name> Medical Conditions, <type> select_multiple, required: false): 
  Select one or more medical conditions you have or have had:
  + None: Indicate you do not have any medical conditions.
  + Asthma: A respiratory condition affecting breathing.
  + Diabetes: A metabolic condition affecting blood sugar regulation.
  + Hypertension: High blood pressure.
  + Obesity: Excess body weight or weight gain.
* **Medications** (id: 6, <name> Medications, <type> text, required: false): 
  List any medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past.
* **Allergies** (id: 7, <name> Allergies, <type> text, required: false): 
  List any allergies or sensitivities you have.
* **Health Principles** (id: 8, <name> Health Principles, <type> select_multiple, required: false): 
  Select one or more healthcare principles you follow:
  + Allopathic: Traditional Western medicine.
  + Holistic: Considering the whole person - body, mind, and spirit.
  + Alternative: Non-traditional practices like acupuncture or herbalism.
  + Integrative: Combining multiple approaches for better health.
  + Naturopathic: Focusing on natural remedies and prevention.
  + Osteopathic: Emphasizing the body's self-regulation and self-healing capacity.
