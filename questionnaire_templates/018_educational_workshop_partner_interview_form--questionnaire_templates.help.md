# Educational Workshop Partner Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from potential partners about their educational workshops. The form is meant to be filled out by the partners to provide us with relevant details about their workshop.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Partner Name
2. Enter your Partner Email
3. Enter your Workshop Name (if applicable)
4. Write a brief description of your workshop
5. List the topics covered in your workshop
6. Mention your experience in education/training
7. Provide your workshop location
8. Specify the duration and frequency of your workshop
9. List your relevant qualifications
10. Choose the format of your workshop
11. Mention your availability
12. Provide references for your workshop (if applicable)
13. Write a description of your references (if applicable)
14. Select the language of your workshop
15. Select the language you speak
16. Choose your time zone
17. Select your time zone abbreviation
18. Specify your time zone offset
19. Provide your workshop time zone
20. Select your workshop time zone abbreviation
21. Specify your workshop time zone offset

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization or individual partner.
* **Partner Email** (`partner_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of your partner. This is how we will contact them.
* **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, text, optional): If you have a workshop, enter its name.
* **Workshop Description** (`workshop_description`, text, optional): Write a brief description of your workshop.
* **Workshop Topics** (`workshop_topics`, text, optional): List the topics covered in your workshop.
* **Partner Experience** (`partner_experience`, text, optional): Mention your experience in education/training.
* **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`, text, optional): Provide the location of your workshop.
* **Workshop Duration** (`workshop_duration`, text, optional): Specify the duration of your workshop.
* **Workshop Frequency** (`workshop_frequency`, text, optional): Specify the frequency of your workshop.
* **Partner Qualifications** (`partner_qualifications`, text, optional): List your relevant qualifications.
* **Workshop Format** (`workshop_format`, text, optional): Choose the format of your workshop.
* **Partner Availability** (`partner_availability`, text, optional): Mention your availability.
* **Partner References** (`partner_references`, text, optional): Provide references for your workshop.
* **Partner References Text** (`partner_references_text`, text, optional): Write a description of your references.
* **Workshop Language** (`workshop_language`, text, optional): Select the language of your workshop.
* **Partner Language** (`partner_language`, text, optional): Select the language you speak.
* **Partner Time Zone** (`partner_time_zone`, text, optional): Choose your time zone.
* **Partner Time Zone Abbreviation** (`partner_timezone_abbreviation`, text, optional): Select your time zone abbreviation.
* **Partner Time Zone Offset** (`partner_timezone_offset`, text, optional): Specify your time zone offset.
* **Workshop Time Zone** (`workshop_timezone`, text, optional): Provide your workshop time zone.
* **Workshop Time Zone Abbreviation** (`workshop_timezone_abbreviation`, text, optional): Select your workshop time zone abbreviation.
* **Workshop Time Zone Offset** (`workshop_timezone_offset`, text, optional): Specify your workshop time zone offset.
* **Partner Phone** (`partner_phone`, text, optional): Provide your contact phone number.
