# demographic_information_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic information and consent from participants for the purpose of research, surveys, or other studies. It is used to gather data about the participant's background, consent to participate, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form's title and label as it appears in the top section.
2. On the first page, enter your background information in the "participant_background" field.
3. On the second page, choose whether you consent to participate in the study by selecting "True" or "False" in the "consent_to_participate" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field on the third page, if required.
5. On the fourth page, enter your age in the "age" field.
6. Select your gender on the fifth page using the options provided in the "gender" field.
7. On the sixth page, enter your occupation in the "occupation" field.
8. On the seventh page, select your level of education from the options provided in the "education" field.
9. On the eighth page, enter your country of residence in the "country" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_background**: Enter a brief description of your background, including any relevant information about your work, education, or experience.
* **consent_to_participate**: Choose "True" if you agree to participate in the study, or "False" if you do not.
* **email**: Enter a valid email address to receive further instructions or updates about the study.
* **age**: Enter your age in numerical form (e.g., 25, 30, etc.).
* **gender**: Select your gender from the options provided.
* **occupation**: Enter a brief description of your occupation or profession.
* **education**: Select the level of education you have completed from the provided options.
* **country**: Enter the country of residence where you currently live.
