# Artist Monetization Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artist Monetization Consultation Form is designed to provide a clear and comprehensive guide for artists to share their information and goals with the consultation services team. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide detailed information about your art career, current income, and areas for growth, allowing the consultation team to provide personalized guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Select your primary art medium or genre from the drop-down menu.
3. Enter your years of experience as an artist.
4. Choose your current annual income from the provided ranges.
5. Select all the income sources that apply to you.
6. Enter your monthly online engagement or followers across various platforms.
7. Choose the active social media platforms you have.
8. Check if you have an active website or e-commerce platform.
9. Provide a brief description of your consultation goals.
10. List the main revenue challenges you are facing.
11. Share areas for growth or expansion.
12. Describe your business or marketing experience.
13. Choose the type of consultation you prefer (one-time, ongoing, or strategy intensive).
14. Enter the desired timeline for results.
15. Enter your budget for the consultation.
16. Add any additional information or questions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Artist or Creator Name** (`artist_name`, text, required): Enter your name as an artist or creator.
* **Email Address** (`artist_email`, email, required): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and follow-up.
* **Phone Number** (`artist_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, which will be used for additional contact.
* **Primary Art Medium or Genre** (`art_medium`, text, required): Select your primary art medium or genre from the provided list.
* **Years Active as Artist** (`years_active`, number, optional): Enter your years of experience as an artist.
* **Current Annual Income from Art** (`current_income`, select_one, optional): Select your current annual income from art from the provided ranges.
* **Current Income Sources** (`income_sources`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the income sources that apply to you.
* **Monthly Online Engagement or Followers** (`monthly_engagement`, number, optional): Enter your approximate monthly online engagement or followers across various platforms.
* **Active Social Media Platforms** (`social_platforms`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the active social media platforms you have.
* **Active Website or E-Commerce** (`website_active`, select_one, optional): Check if you have an active website or e-commerce platform.
* **Consultation Goals** (`consultation_goals`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your consultation goals.
* **Main Revenue Challenges** (`revenue_challenges`, text, required): List the main revenue challenges you are facing.
* **Areas for Growth or Expansion** (`growth_areas`, text, optional): Share areas for growth or expansion.
* **Business or Marketing Experience** (`business_experience`, select_one, optional): Describe your business or marketing experience.
* **Type of Consultation Preferred** (`consultation_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of consultation you prefer (one-time, ongoing, or strategy intensive).
* **Desired Timeline for Results** (`timeline`, text, optional): Enter the desired timeline for results.
* **Budget for Consultation** (`budget`, number, optional): Enter your budget for the consultation.
* **Additional Information or Questions** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional information or questions you have.

Note: Please make sure to review your submission carefully before submitting it, as you will not be able to make changes once the form is submitted.
