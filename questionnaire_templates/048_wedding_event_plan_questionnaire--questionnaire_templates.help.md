# Wedding Event Plan Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a wedding event plan for the purpose of planning and coordination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the wedding date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
2. Choose a valid wedding location from the available options.
3. Select the type of wedding package you require.
4. Provide your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number.
5. If you have people you would like to contact for any reason, select their names from the list.
6. If you have a wedding coordinator, enter their name and contact details.
7. Describe any family situations that may be relevant to the event planner.
8. Indicate whether you require wedding photography services.
9. Enter any specific wedding logistics needs, such as transportation or catering requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Date** (`wedding_date`, `date`, **Required:** No): Enter the date of the wedding in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Wedding Location** (`wedding_location`, `text`, **Required:** No): Enter the location of the wedding, e.g., a venue or a city.
* **Wedding Package** (`wedding_package`, `select_one`, **Required:** No): Choose from the options provided to select the type of wedding package you require.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, **Required:** No): Enter your name, email address, and phone number so the event planner can get in touch with you.
* **People to Contact** (`people_to_contact`, `select_multiple`, **Required:** No): List the names of the people you would like the event planner to contact in case of any emergencies.
* **Wedding Coordinator** (`wedding_coordinator`, `text`, **Required:** No): Enter the name and contact details of your wedding coordinator if you have one.
* **Family Situations** (`family_situations`, `text`, **Required:** No): Describe any family situations that may be relevant to the event planner, such as allergies or medical conditions.
* **Wedding Photography** (`wedding_photography`, `select_one`, **Required:** No): Select whether you require wedding photography services.
* **Wedding Logistics** (`wedding_logistics`, `text`, **Required:** No): Enter any specific requirements you have for the wedding logistics, such as transportation or catering needs.
