# internship_experience_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your internship experience. It will help us understand your work experience, skills, and achievements during your internship. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required information on each page (indicated by the `*` symbol).
2. For select multiple-choice questions, choose all applicable answers that apply.
3. Enter your responses in the text fields and date fields.
4. Submit the form when you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Main Section
* **Main Section** (`main_section`, text, required): This is the title of your internship. Enter a brief description of your internship experience.

### Internship Type
* **Internship Type** (`internship_type`, select multiple, required): Check all types of internships you participated in (e.g. Research, Teaching, Design, etc.).

### Internship Start Date
* **Internship Start Date** (`internship_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your internship.

### Internship End Date
* **Internship End Date** (`internship_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of your internship.

### Work Days
* **Work Days** (`work_days`, number, required): Enter the number of days you worked during the internship.

### Worked Days
* **Worked Days** (`worked_days`, number, required): Enter the number of days you actually worked during the internship (i.e., not holidays or leave).

### Hours Per Week
* **Hours Per Week** (`hours_per_week`, number, required): Enter the average number of hours you worked per week during the internship.

### Salary
* **Salary** (`salary`, number, optional): Enter your monthly salary during the internship.

### Company
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company where you interned.

### Supervisor
* **Supervisor** (`supervisor`, text, optional): Enter the name of your supervisor during the internship.

### Tasks
* **Tasks** (`tasks`, text, optional): Describe the tasks you performed during the internship.

### Skills Used
* **Skills Used** (`skills_used`, text, optional): List the skills you used during the internship.

### Skills Learned
* **Skills Learned** (`skills_learned`, text, optional): List the skills you learned during the internship.

### Internship Outcome
* **Internship Outcome** (`internship_outcome`, text, optional): Describe the outcome of your internship (e.g., successful project, publication, etc.).

### Feedback
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any feedback or comments about your internship experience.

### Suggestions for Improvement
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, optional): Suggest any areas for improvement for future interns.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your internship experience.

### Attachments
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, text, optional): Attach any relevant documents or files related to your internship experience.
