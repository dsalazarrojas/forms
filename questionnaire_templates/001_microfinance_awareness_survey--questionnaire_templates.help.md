# Microfinance Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Microfinance Awareness Survey is a questionnaire designed to assess individuals' knowledge and understanding of microfinance concepts, services, and their benefits. This survey aims to gather information on respondents' awareness levels, services utilization, and interest in microfinance services. The data collected will be used to inform programs and initiatives that promote financial inclusion and education.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read each question carefully and answer according to your understanding.
2.  Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
3.  Provide detailed answers for text-based questions.
4.  Ensure you answer all questions that are marked as "required."
5.  Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the survey. Please feel free to provide any additional information that will help us better understand your background and context.
*   **What is Microfinance** (`what_is_microfinance`, `text`, required: false): This question is an opportunity for you to share your understanding or perception of microfinance. Please provide your honest answer, even if it's not entirely accurate.
*   **Awareness Levels** (`awareness_levels`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the statements that accurately describe your level of awareness about microfinance. You can select multiple options.
    *   option 1: I have a basic understanding of microfinance concepts.
    *   option 2: I have an intermediate understanding of microfinance concepts.
    *   option 3: I have a high understanding of microfinance concepts.
*   **Microfinance Services** (`microfinance_services`, `select_one`, required: false): Which microfinance service is of most interest to you? Please select one option.
    *   option 1: Savings services (e.g., savings accounts, loans)
    *   option 2: Payment services (e.g., mobile money, payment cards)
    *   option 3: Insurance services
*   **Interest Levels** (`interest_levels`, `number`, required: false): On a scale of 1-5, how interested are you in using microfinance services?
*   **Benefits** (`benefits`, `text`, required: false): If you have used microfinance services, what benefits have you experienced or expect to experience from them?
*   **Concerns** (`concerns`, `text`, required: false): If you have not used microfinance services, what concerns have prevented you from using them?
*   **Demographic Information** (`demographic_info`, `note`, required: false): This is a note field for demographic information. Please provide any relevant details about your location, education level, income, or occupation that might be helpful for our analysis.
*   **Demographic Information 2** (`demographic_info_2`, `text`, required: false): Additional information about your location or education level might be relevant to this question.
*   **Demographic Information 3** (`demographic_info_3`, `text`, required: false): Please provide details about your income or occupation.
*   **Demographic Information 4** (`demographic_info_4`, `text`, required: false): You can provide any other relevant information about yourself that would be helpful for our analysis.
*   **Demographic Information 5** (`demographic_info_5`, `text`, required: false): Please provide any other information about your location, education, or occupation.
*   **Demographic Information 6** (`demographic_info_6`, `text`, required: false): Provide details about your current financial situation or income.
*   **Demographic Information 7** (`demographic_info_7`, `text`, required: false): Any other comments or questions about your answers might be helpful for us.

Note:
-   The required fields are marked as "required: true".
-   Please ensure you answer all required fields to submit the form.
-   You can skip fields marked as "required: false" if you're unable to answer them.
-   If you're unable to answer a question, please provide an explanation to help us understand the reason.
