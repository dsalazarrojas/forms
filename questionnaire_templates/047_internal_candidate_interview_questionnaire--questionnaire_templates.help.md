# Internal Candidate Interview Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internal Candidate Interview Questionnaire is a structured form used to collect feedback and evaluation from interviewers and evaluators about internal candidates. This form helps to standardize the evaluation process, gather comprehensive feedback, and make informed decisions about candidate advancement within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and documents required to fill out the form.
2. Read each section carefully and understand what is being asked.
3. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
4. If you're unsure about any section, refer back to the section explanations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **what is your role** (`title`, `text`, **required**): Please enter a brief description of your role during the interview process.
  - For example, "Interviewer", "Evaluator", or "HR Representative".

- **Interview Panel** (`id`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Select the members of the interview panel that participated in the interview. Options: CEO, Manager, HR Representative, Other.

- **Interview Date** (`id`, `date`, **optional**): Enter the date of the interview. This will help track the timeline of the evaluation process.

- **Time** (`id`, `time`, **optional**): Indicate the time of the interview. This could be the start and end time, or the duration of the interview.

- **Location** (`id`, `text`, **optional**): Provide the location where the interview took place.

- **Interview Duration** (`id`, `time`, **optional**): Record the duration of the interview. This helps in planning future interviews.

- **Evaluation Criteria** (`id`, `text`, **optional**): Describe the key criteria used to evaluate the candidate. This helps to maintain consistency in the evaluation process.

- **Evaluation Weightage** (`id`, `text`, **optional**): Explain the weightage or importance of each evaluation criterion.

- **Interview Score** (`id`, `number`, **optional**): Assign a score based on the candidate's performance during the interview.

- **Score Ratio** (`id`, `number`, **optional**): Calculate the ratio of the score to determine the candidate's standing.

- **Evaluations 2-5** (`id`, `text`, `optional`): Similar to Evaluation Criteria and Evaluation Weightage, provide a description and weightage for each subsequent evaluation criteria.

## Tips
- Be thorough and accurate when filling out this form.
- If you're unsure about any section, refer back to the section explanations.
- Ensure that the form is completed for each candidate separately.
- The information gathered from this form will help to make informed decisions about candidate advancement within the organization.
