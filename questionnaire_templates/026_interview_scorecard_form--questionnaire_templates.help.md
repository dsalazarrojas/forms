# Interview Scorecard Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Interview Scorecard Form is designed to collect comprehensive feedback from interviewers on the effectiveness and quality of interviews.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review the form carefully to understand the questions and sections you need to complete.
2.  For each question or section, write a detailed description or explanation based on your experience.
3.  Ensure that your feedback is clear, concise, and relevant to the interview.
4.  If you need to add more details or comments, you can use additional pages.

*   **first_page** (Text): This is the first page where you can start writing your feedback on the interview.

## Tips
*   Be thorough in your feedback, providing specific examples and details where possible.
*   Ensure your feedback is objective and fair, focusing on the interview process rather than personal preferences.
*   Use clear and concise language to convey your points.
*   Proofread your responses to avoid errors or misunderstandings.
*   Use this form as a guide, but feel free to add or remove pages as needed based on the specific interview or context.
