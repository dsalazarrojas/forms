# Dynamic Content Code Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dynamic Content Code Survey is a tool for gathering feedback and data about users' experiences with our product. This survey is used to identify areas of improvement and to make informed decisions about product development and feature enhancements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select a page to navigate to the corresponding section of the survey.
2. Fill out each section carefully, providing as much information as you can about your experience.
3. Be sure to include your contact email and phone number if you have any questions or concerns about our product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey usage** (`survey_usage`, text, required):
	+ This field is used to record how you've been using our product.
* **Satisfaction level** (`satisfaction_level`, number, required):
	+ This field is used to measure your level of satisfaction with our product. Please enter a numerical value between 0 and 10.
* **Improvement priorities** (`improvement_priorities`, select_multiple, required):
	+ This field is used to identify areas where you think our product could be improved. Please select one or more of the following options:
		+ Usage frequency
		+ Content relevance
		+ Ease of use
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for writing any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our product.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, optional):
	+ This field is for entering your preferred contact email in case you have any questions or concerns about our product.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for entering your preferred contact phone number in case you have any questions or concerns about our product.
* **Contact time** (`contact_time`, time, optional):
	+ This field is for entering your preferred contact time in case you have any questions or concerns about our product.
* **Date of last use** (`date_of_last_use`, date, required):
	+ This field is used to record the date of your last use of our product.
* **Time of last use** (`time_of_last_use`, time, required):
	+ This field is used to record the time of your last use of our product.
* **Frequency of use** (`frequency_of_use`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for describing how often you use our product.
