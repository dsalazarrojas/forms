# investors_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Investors Questionnaire" form is designed to collect information from investors about their company, investment interests, and contact details. This form aims to gather relevant data for internal analysis and decision-making purposes. Please note that this form is not mandatory, and you can choose to fill it in at your discretion.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is being collected.
2. Fill in your company name, contact email, phone number, and address.
3. Select the type of financial institution you represent (Bank, Insurance, or Fund) or provide an alternative option if not listed.
4. Choose the type of investments you are interested in (Equity, Debt, or Hybrid).
5. Indicate the amount of investment you are willing to consider.
6. Select the level of interest in the company (Strong, Medium, or Low).
7. Provide a brief description of your company.
8. Fill in your name as the contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction**: A note field where you can briefly describe your interest in this opportunity.
- **company_name**: Enter your full company name.
- **contact_email**: Enter your contact email address.
- **contact_phone**: Enter your company's phone number.
- **address**: Enter your company's physical address.
- **financial_institution**: Select "Bank", "Insurance", or "Fund" as your financial institution type.
- **other_financial_institution**: If not listed as "Bank", "Insurance", or "Fund", enter an alternative type of financial institution.
- **investment_interest**: Select the types of investments you are interested in ("Equity", "Debt", or "Hybrid").
- **investment_amount**: Enter the amount of investment you are willing to consider.
- **company_interest**: Indicate the level of interest in the company ("Strong", "Medium", or "Low").
- **company_description**: Briefly describe your company.
- **contact_name**: Fill in your name as the contact person.
