# multi_section_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The multi_section_intake_form is designed to collect contact details, service details, and follow-up information from users in multiple sections. This form aims to gather comprehensive information for efficient processing and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Scroll down the form sections to locate the relevant section for your current response. 
2.  Fill out the required fields. 
3.  Provide accurate and detailed information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Contact Details (<name>, <type>:** This field is designed to capture the user's contact details.
    -   Fill out this field with the user's name, email, and phone number.
*   **Service Details (<name>, <type>:** This field is designed to gather information related to the service.
    -   Fill out this field with any specific requirements or requests the user may have.
*   **Follow-up Details (<name>, <type>:** This field is designed to gather information for any follow-up interactions.
    -   Fill out this field with any relevant follow-up information.

## Tips
-   Ensure that each field is filled out accurately and thoroughly.
-   Double-check your responses before submission.
-   If you have any questions or need clarification, contact us at form@gic.mx.
