# Lighting Design Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from users on their experiences and preferences related to lighting design.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information and ratings.
2. Provide feedback and ratings on the provided questions.
3. Submit the form to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info**: This field is for collecting your basic contact information. Please provide your name, email, and phone number.
* **Project Info**: This field is for collecting information about the project you are working on.
* **Lighting Design**: This is the main field for collecting feedback and ratings on our lighting design.
* **Feedback**: This field is for providing any additional feedback or comments on our lighting design.
* **Email**: This field is for collecting your email address.
* **Phone**: This field is for collecting your phone number.
* **Date**: This field is for collecting the date of completion.
* **Time**: This field is for collecting the time of completion.
* **Note**: This field is for providing any additional notes or comments.
* **What is your favorite lighting style?**: This question is for rating your favorite lighting style.
* **What types of lighting are you interested in?**: This question is for selecting multiple types of lighting you are interested in.
* **Date of completion**: This field is for collecting the date of completion.
* **Time of completion**: This field is for collecting the time of completion.
* **How many stars do you give this design?**: This question is for rating this design.
* **What features do you like most?**: This question is for selecting multiple features you like most.
* **What is your favorite color?**: This question is for rating your favorite color.
* **How would you rate our service?**: This question is for rating our service.
* **Email 2**: This field is for collecting a second email address.
* **Phone 2**: This field is for collecting a second phone number.
* **Date 3**: This field is for collecting a date.
* **Time 3**: This field is for collecting a time.
* **How will you use the lighting design?**: This question is for selecting how you will use the design.
* **Select One 5**: This question is for rating a preference.
* **Select One 6**: This question is for rating a preference.
* **Submit**: This field is for submitting your feedback.

## Tips

Please fill in the fields accurately and honestly to provide quality feedback. If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us.
