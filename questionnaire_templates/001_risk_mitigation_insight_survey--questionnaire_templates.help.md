# Risk Mitigation Insight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Risk Mitigation Insight Survey is used to gather information on risk awareness, mitigation strategies, and assessment frequencies. This form is a tool for risk assessment and mitigation planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions for each section:

* Answer each question according to your best knowledge and expertise.
* Be as detailed as possible, but keep responses concise.
* Do not repeat information already provided in previous questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Risk Awareness** (`risk_mitigation_insight_survey`, `note`, required: false): This section is a note field where you can provide general information about your risk awareness. Please use it to describe your current risk awareness.
* **Risk Prio** (`risk_prio`, `select_one`, options: [High, Medium, Low], required: false): Choose the risk level that you consider most relevant to your situation. You can select one of the three options: High, Medium, or Low.
* **Risk Priority** (`risk_priority`, `select_multiple`, options: [High, Medium, Low], required: false): Select all risk priorities that apply to your situation. You can choose multiple options.
* **Risk Mitigation Effectiveness** (`risk_mitigation_effectiveness`, `number`, required: false): Please provide a numerical value (e.g., 0-10) to express your risk mitigation effectiveness.
* **Risk Awareness** (`risk_awareness`, `text`, required: false): Describe your current risk awareness in this text field.
* **Risk Mitigation Plan** (`risk_mitigation_plan`, `time`, required: false): If you have a risk mitigation plan, describe it in this time field.
* **Risk Mitigation Strategy** (`risk_mitigation_strategy`, `number`, required: false): Provide a numerical value (e.g., 0-10) to express your risk mitigation strategy.
* **Risk Awareness Training** (`risk_awareness_training`, `select_one`, options: [Basic, Intermediate, Advanced], required: false): Choose the level of risk awareness training you have received.
* **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`, `number`, required: false): Provide a numerical value (e.g., 0-10) to express your risk assessment.
* **Risk Mitigation Review** (`risk_mitigation_review`, `text`, required: false): Describe your risk mitigation review in this text field.
* **Risk Mitigation Meeting** (`risk_mitigation_meeting`, `select_multiple`, options: [Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly], required: false): Select the frequency of your risk mitigation meetings.
* **Risk Assessment Frequency** (`risk_assessment_frequency`, `date`, required: false): Provide a date to express your risk assessment frequency.
