# Hospital Waste Reduction Practices Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on hospital waste reduction practices, including the types of waste streams, reduction targets, and strategies implemented.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by reading the introduction on the first page, which provides a brief context and purpose of the form.
2. On the second page, select one or more of the available waste streams from the dropdown menu (e.g., Food Waste, Liquid Waste, Solids (non-food)).
3. On the third page, enter your desired reduction targets, which should be in date format (dd/mm/yyyy).
4. On the fourth page, select one or more of the available waste reduction strategies (e.g., Reduce, Reuse, Recycle, Other).
5. On the fifth page, enter any potential barriers you are experiencing in reducing waste.
6. On the sixth page, provide any suggestions or comments from staff members.
7. Finally, on the seventh page, enter any additional responses or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, not required): A brief introduction to the form and its purpose.
* **Waste Streams** (`2`, `select_one`, not required): Select one or more types of waste streams that are present in your hospital (e.g., Food Waste, Liquid Waste, Solids (non-food)).
* **Waste Reduction Targets** (`3`, `date`, not required): Enter your desired waste reduction targets in date format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Waste Reduction Strategies** (`4`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select one or more of the available waste reduction strategies (e.g., Reduce, Reuse, Recycle, Other).
* **Barriers** (`5`, `text`, not required): Enter any potential barriers you are experiencing in reducing waste.
* **Staff Suggestions** (`6`, `text`, not required): Provide any suggestions or comments from staff members.
* **Responses** (`7`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional responses or comments.
