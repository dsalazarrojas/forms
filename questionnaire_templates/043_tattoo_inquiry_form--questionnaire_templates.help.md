# Tattoo Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tattoo Inquiry Form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in getting a tattoo.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your **Contact Number** in the "Contact Number" field.
5. Select a **Date** for your potential tattoo appointment in the "Date" field.
6. Choose your **Preferred Tattoo Location** from the dropdown menu.
7. Select your desired **Tattoo Style** from the dropdown menu.
8. Describe your desired **Design Description** in the text field.
9. Check the **Available Dates** that you are available to get a tattoo on.
10. Select a **Start Time** for your appointment.
11. Select an **End Time** for your appointment.
12. Enter your **Budget** for the tattoo in the number field.
13. Choose your desired **Tattoo Theme** from the dropdown menu.
14. Select the size of your desired **Tattoo Size** from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Contact Number** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date** (Date, Optional): Select a date that you are available to get a tattoo on.
* **Preferred Tattoo Location** (Select One, Optional): Choose from "Yes" or "No" whether you prefer to get a tattoo at home, in a parlor, or elsewhere.
* **Tattoo Style** (Select One, Optional): Choose from "Yes" or "No" your desired style of tattoo, such as color, black and white, etc.
* **Design Description** (Text, Optional): Describe your desired design in as much detail as possible.
* **Available Dates** (Select Multiple, Optional): Check the dates that you are available to get a tattoo on.
* **Start Time** (Time, Optional): Select a start time for your appointment.
* **End Time** (Time, Optional): Select an end time for your appointment.
* **Budget** (Number, Optional): Enter your budget for the tattoo.
* **Tattoo Theme** (Select One, Optional): Choose from "Yes" or "No" your desired theme, such as a quote, portrait, etc.
* **Tattoo Size** (Select One, Optional): Choose from "Yes" or "No" your desired size of tattoo.
