# Stylist Match Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Stylist Match Quiz is a form designed to help match you with the right stylist for your hair needs. This quiz will guide you through a series of questions to determine your preferences, hair type, and requirements, and provide you with a suggested stylist based on your answers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions carefully, choosing the options that best fit your preferences and characteristics.
2. Fill in all the required fields (marked as "required") to ensure you are matched with the right stylist.
3. Review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Which type of stylist are you looking for?** (`stylist_type`, select_one, required): This question helps us understand what type of stylist you are looking for, such as a colorist or balayage expert.
* **What type of hair do you have?** (`hair_type`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the types of hair you have, such as dry, curly, or straight.
* **Which stylist type do you prefer?** (`preferred_stylist`, select_one, required): This question helps us match you with a stylist who specializes in the type you prefer, such as color or balayage.
* **What style would you like your hair cut?** (`preferred_haircut`, select_multiple, required): Choose the style you like best, such as fade or other options.
* **When do you usually get your hair cut?** (`schedule`, date, required): Please select a date to help us schedule your appointment accordingly.
* **What is your name?** (`client_name`, text, required): Please enter your name for our records.
* **What is your email?** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **What is your phone number?** (`client_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **What is your bio?** (`client_bio`, note, required): Briefly describe yourself and your experience.
* **What services do you offer?** (`stylist_services`, select_one, required): Please select the services you offer as a stylist, such as haircut or balayage.
* **What is your price range?** (`stylist_price`, number, required): Enter your price range for your services.
* **What is your location?** (`stylist_location`, text, required): Enter your location to help us understand where you operate.
* **What is your rating?** (`stylist_rating`, text, required): Please enter your rating as a stylist.
* **How long have you been a stylist?** (`stylist_experience`, time, required): Please enter the length of time you have been a stylist.
* **When are you available?** (`stylist_hours`, time, required): Enter your available hours to schedule appointments.
* **Description?** (`stylist_description`, text, required): Briefly describe your services and expertise.
* **Are you a real person?** (`client_confirmation`, select_one, required): Please confirm that you are a real person and not a robot.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms`, note, required): Please read and agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Profile image?** (`stylist_image`, text, optional): If you have a professional image, please upload it.
* **What is your bio?** (`stylist_bio`, note, optional): If you have a professional bio, please enter it.
* **Profile image?** (`client_image`, text, optional): If you have a profile image, please upload it.
