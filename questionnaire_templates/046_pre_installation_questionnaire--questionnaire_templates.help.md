# Pre Installation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre-Installation Questionnaire is a form designed to collect crucial information from the client before the installation process commences. This form is used to gather details such as contact information, installation site specifics, required equipment, and other important details that will aid the installation team in preparing for and executing the installation process accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form correctly, follow these steps:

* Fill out each section thoroughly, providing accurate information about the installation site, client contact information, required equipment, and any special requirements.
* For the `installation_date` field, enter the date of the installation in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* For the `installation_time` field, enter the start and end times of the installation in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* In the `client_preferred_language` field, select the client's preferred language (English, French, or Spanish).
* For the `client_email` field, enter the client's email address.
* For the `client_phone` field, enter the client's phone number.
* In the `assigned_contact` field, select the contact person from the given options (chatjimmy, John Doe, or Jane Doe).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **installation_site** (`installation_site`, text, optional):
  * Enter the address or location of the site where the installation will take place.
* **client_info** (`client_info`, text, optional):
  * Provide any additional information about the client or their representative.
* **required_equipment** (`required_equipment`, text, optional):
  * List any essential equipment or tools needed for the installation process.
* **contact_info** (`contact_info`, text, optional):
  * Enter the contact information for the client or their representative.
* **installation_date** (`installation_date`, date, optional):
  * Enter the date of the installation in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **installation_time** (`installation_time`, time, optional):
  * Enter the start and end times of the installation in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **client_preferred_language** (`client_preferred_language`, select_one, optional):
  * Select the client's preferred language (English, French, or Spanish).
* **installation_contact** (`installation_contact`, text, optional):
  * Provide the contact information for the client's representative.
* **special_requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, optional):
  * List any special requirements for the installation process.
* **client_email** (`client_email`, email, optional):
  * Enter the client's email address.
* **client_phone** (`client_phone`, text, optional):
  * Enter the client's phone number.
* **assigned_contact** (`assigned_contact`, select_one, optional):
  * Select the contact person from the given options (chatjimmy, John Doe, or Jane Doe).
