# Women Clothing Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your body measurements and personal preferences for clothing. This will help us better understand your requirements for a custom fit, so we can create the perfect fit for you. Please answer these questions to help us provide you with a more personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on each field to start answering the questions.
- Answer each question according to your body measurements and preferences.
- Make sure to fill out all fields marked as "required".
- Select the most suitable option from the dropdown menus for your preferred body type, preferred waist type, and preferred shoe type.
- Enter your measurements and sizes in the corresponding fields.
- If you have any additional information you'd like to share, use the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Body Type** (`body_type`, `select_one`, **Petite** or **Tall** or **Average** or **Athletic**): Please select your body type, as it relates to your overall height and build. This will help us understand your proportions for a more accurate fit. 
  * If you're unsure, you can choose the option that most closely describes you.
- **Height** (`height`, `number`): Enter your height in inches.
- **Neck Measurement** (`measurement_neck`, `number`): Measure the distance from the base of your neck (just below your hairline) to the top of your shoulders. Record the measurement in inches.
- **Bust Measurement** (`measurement_bust`, `number`): Measure around the fullest part of your bust, keeping the tape measure level and parallel to the floor. Record the measurement in inches.
- **Hip Measurement** (`measurement_hip`, `number`): Measure around the widest part of your hips, keeping the tape measure level and parallel to the floor. Record the measurement in inches.
- **Weight** (`weight`, `number`): Enter your weight in pounds.
- **Preferred Waist Type** (`preferred_waist_type`, `select_one`, **Petite** or **Slim** or **Fitted** or **Classic** or **Relaxed**): Select the most comfortable and flattering waist style for you.
- **Preferred Waist Measurement** (`preferred_waist_measurement`, `number`): Enter the measurement around your natural waistline.
- **Top Size Small** (`top_size_small`, `number`): Enter the size of the shirt or top you prefer for your small sizes.
- **Top Size Medium** (`top_size_medium`, `number`): Enter the size of the shirt or top you prefer for your medium sizes.
- **Top Size Large** (`top_size_large`, `number`): Enter the size of the shirt or top you prefer for your large sizes.
- **Pant Size Small** (`pant_size_small`, `number`): Enter the size of the pants you prefer for your small sizes.
- **Pant Size Medium** (`pant_size_medium`, `number`): Enter the size of the pants you prefer for your medium sizes.
- **Pant Size Large** (`pant_size_large`, `number`): Enter the size of the pants you prefer for your large sizes.
- **Dress Size Small** (`dress_size_small`, `number`): Enter the size of the dress you prefer for your small sizes.
- **Dress Size Medium** (`dress_size_medium`, `number`): Enter the size of the dress you prefer for your medium sizes.
- **Dress Size Large** (`dress_size_large`, `number`): Enter the size of the dress you prefer for your large sizes.
- **Bra Size Small** (`bra_size_small`, `number`): Enter the size of the bra you prefer for your small sizes.
- **Bra Size Medium** (`bra_size_medium`, `number`): Enter the size of the bra you prefer for your medium sizes.
- **Bra Size Large** (`bra_size_large`, `number`): Enter the size of the bra you prefer for your large sizes.
- **Preferred Shoe Type** (`preferred_shoe_type`, `select_one`, **Heel** or **Flat** or **Wedge** or **Sandal** or **Sneaker**): Select the type of shoe you prefer.
- **Preferred Shoe Size Small** (`preferred_shoe_size_small`, `number`): Enter the size of the shoe you prefer for your small sizes.
- **Preferred Shoe Size Medium** (`preferred_shoe_size_medium`, `number`): Enter the size of the shoe you prefer for your medium sizes.
- **Preferred Shoe Size** (`preferred_shoeistency`, `number`): Enter the size of the shoe you prefer.
- **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, `text`): Use this field to provide any additional details that you think will help us better understand your preferences.
