# Global Project Needs Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Global Project Needs Assessment form is a tool to gather information about a project's objectives, challenges, resource needs, and other essential details. This form helps project managers and team leads to identify and document project requirements, resource availability, and timelines, ensuring that projects are well-planned and executed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in each section with relevant information about your project.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed (if any).
3. Review your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Objectives**: Describe the main objective of your project. What is the project's overall goal?
* **Challenges**: List any challenges or obstacles that your project may face.
* **Resource Needs**: Outline the resources required for your project, such as personnel, equipment, or materials.
* **Project Description**: Provide a brief description of your project.
* **Business Impact**: Select the level of business impact, such as:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
	+ Option 5
	+ Option 6
* **Resource Availability**: Enter information about the resources available for your project.
* **Budget Allocation**: Enter a budget allocation for your project.
* **Resource Request**: Provide any additional resource requests or needs.
* **Priority**: Choose a priority level for your project:
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High
	+ Critical
* **Deadline**: Select the project's deadline.
* **Contact Information**: Enter contact information for project stakeholders or team leads.
* **Team Name**: Enter your team's name.
* **Team Size**: Enter the number of team members.
* **Team Location**: Enter your team's location.
* **Team Lead**: Enter the name of your team lead.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Project Status**: Select the project status:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
	+ On Hold
	+ Completed
* **Project Type**: Select multiple project types (if applicable):
	+ Type 1
	+ Type 2
	+ Type 3
	+ Type 4
	+ Type 5
* **Project Priority**: Enter a numerical priority value.
* **Project Start Date**: Enter the project's start date.
* **Project End Date**: Enter the project's end date.
* **Project Description**: Provide a brief description of your project (optional).
* **Resource Requirements**: Outline any resource requirements for your project.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your project.
* **Project Start Time**: Enter the time of day when the project should start.

Tips

* Please ensure that all sections are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If a field is not relevant to your project, leave it blank or enter "N/A" (not applicable).
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your project manager or team lead.
