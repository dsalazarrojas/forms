# Infected Blood Incident Inquiry Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Infected Blood Incident Inquiry Questionnaire is designed to gather information about incidents involving blood infection, ensuring that all relevant details are collected in a structured manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide details about the incident, including any relevant dates and times.
2. Describe the incident in the provided text area.
3. Enter the date of the incident.
4. Enter the time of the incident.
5. Specify the location where the incident occurred.
6. Enter any personal contact information or account details.
7. Enter the email address to be used for follow-up communications.
8. Specify any follow-up information or instructions.
9. Add any additional notes about the incident.
10. Identify the advocacy group involved in the incident.
11. Select the tool assigned to address the incident.
12. Select one or more types of incidents that this form is reporting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Details**: This field is for providing specific details about the incident, such as dates and times.
* **Incident Description**: This field is for writing a detailed description of the incident.
* **Incident Date**: This field is for entering the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time**: This field is for entering the time of the incident.
* **Incident Location**: This field is for specifying the location where the incident occurred.
* **Personal Account**: This field is for entering any personal contact information or account details.
* **Contact Info**: This field is for entering any contact information.
* **Email Address**: This field is for entering the email address to be used for follow-up communications.
* **Follow up**: This field is for providing any follow-up information or instructions.
* **Notes**: This field is for adding any additional notes about the incident.
* **Advocacy Group**: This field is for specifying the advocacy group involved in the incident.
* **Assigned Tool**: This field is for selecting the tool assigned to address the incident.
* **Incident Type**: This field is for selecting one or more types of incidents that this form is reporting.
