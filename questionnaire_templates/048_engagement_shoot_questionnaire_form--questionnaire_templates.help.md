# Engagement Shoot Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential details for a successful engagement shoot. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure that the photographer has a clear understanding of your requirements and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please read the instructions below carefully and fill out each field accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Engagement Shoot Details** (<name>, <type>: text, required: <false>): Please provide a brief description of the engagement shoot, including any specific details or requests you have.
* **Partner Name** (<name>, <type>: text, required: <true>): Enter the name of your partner (or fiancé(e)).
* **Photographer Info** (<name>, <type>: text, required: <true>): Enter your contact information and any relevant details about the photographer.
* **Photo Shoot Date** (<name>, <type>: date, required: <true>): Select the date of the engagement shoot.
* **Start Time** (<name>, <type>: time, required: <true>): Enter the start time of the engagement shoot.
* **End Time** (<name>, <type>: time, required: <true>): Enter the end time of the engagement shoot.
* **Location Details** (<name>, <type>: text, required: <false>): Provide any additional details about the location where the engagement shoot will take place.
* **Photographer Experience** (<name>, <type>: text, required: <true>): Describe your photographer's experience level, such as beginner, intermediate, or advanced.
* **Photo Session Duration** (<name>, <type>: number, required: <true>): Enter the duration of the photo session in minutes.
* **Number of Guests** (<name>, <type>: number, required: <true>): Enter the number of guests attending the engagement shoot.
* **Number of Photographers** (<name>, <type>: number, required: <true>): Enter the number of photographers attending the shoot.
* **Photo Session Notes** (<name>, <type>: text, required: <false>): Provide any additional comments or notes about the photo session.
* **Photo Shoot Location** (<name>, <type>: text, required: <false>): Enter the location where the photo shoot will take place.
* **Photographer Equipment** (<name>, <type>: text, required: <false>): Check if you would like to bring any specific photography equipment.
* **Additional Comments** (<name>, <type>: text, required: <false>): Enter any other comments or requests you have.
* **Contact Info** (<name>, <type>: text, required: <false>): Enter your contact information, such as phone number or email.
* **Engagement Ring Details** (<name>, <type>: text, required: <false>): Describe your engagement ring, including its size and any other details.
* **Wedding Details** (<name>, <type>: text, required: <false>): Provide any details about the wedding.
* **Wedding Date** (<name>, <type>: date, required: <false>): Enter the date of the wedding.
* **Wedding Location** (<name>, <type>: text, required: <false>): Enter the location of the wedding.

Please ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately and provide any additional information as necessary. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
