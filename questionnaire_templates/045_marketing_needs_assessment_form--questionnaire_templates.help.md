# Marketing Needs Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Marketing Needs Assessment Form is a tool to help assess your marketing needs and gather information about your target audience, marketing efforts, and budget.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For select and multiple select questions, choose all that apply.
3. Use clear and concise language in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **TargetAudience** (Select One, required): Identify the specific target audience you are trying to reach with your marketing efforts.
* **CurrentEffort** (Text, required): Describe your current marketing efforts, including any campaigns, promotions, or strategies you are currently using.
* **Goals** (Text, required): State your marketing goals, including what you want to achieve with your marketing efforts.
* **Budget** (Number, required): Specify your marketing budget for the project.
* **MarketingChannels** (Select Multiple, required): Indicate which marketing channels you are using or plan to use (e.g., social media, email, advertising).
* **TargetChannels** (Select Multiple, required): Identify the specific marketing channels you want to target (e.g., social media, email, advertising).
* **MarketingBudget** (Number, required): Indicate your marketing budget for the project.
* **CurrentMarketingStrategies** (Text, optional): Describe your current marketing strategies, including any successful or unsuccessful tactics.
* **MarketingObjectives** (Text, optional): Specify your marketing objectives, including any specific outcomes you want to achieve.
* **MarketingMessage** (Text, optional): Craft a brief marketing message that captures the essence of your marketing strategy.
* **NextSteps** (Text, optional): Outline the next steps you plan to take with your marketing efforts.
* **MarketingInsights** (Select One, optional): Indicate if you have any marketing insights, such as customer feedback or data analysis.
* **MarketingNeeds** (Text, optional): Describe any additional marketing needs or areas you feel need improvement.
* **AdditionalComments** (Text, optional): Add any additional comments or questions you have about your marketing efforts.
* **Email** (Email, required): Enter your email address for follow-up contact.
* **Phone** (Text, required): Enter your phone number for follow-up contact.

Please note that the fields marked as "optional" can be left blank if not applicable.
