# Field Fire Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about an individual's interest in participating in a program, project, or event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Contact Details (Page 1)
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your personal contact details such as name, address, and any other relevant information.

### Experience (Page 2)
* **Experience** (`experience`, number, optional): Answer this question to tell us about your relevant experience level.

### Skills (Page 3)
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, optional): List all your relevant skills.

### Interest Levels (Page 4)
* **Interest Levels** (`interest_levels`, select_one, optional): Select your interest level from the options provided.

### Email (Page 5)
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.

### Phone (Page 6)
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

### Notes (Page 7)
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about yourself or your project.

### Category (Page 8)
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select your category from the options provided.

### Sub Category (Page 9)
* **Sub Category** (`sub_category`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all applicable subcategories that you are interested in.

### Location (Page 10)
* **Location** (`location`, select_one, optional): Select your location from the provided options.

## Tips
Remember to fill in all the required fields to ensure that your application is complete and suitable for the program, project, or event. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
