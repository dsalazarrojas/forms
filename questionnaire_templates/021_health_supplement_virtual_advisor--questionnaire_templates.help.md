# Health Supplement Virtual Advisor - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Supplement Virtual Advisor is a form designed to collect information about a person's health goals, medical history, and supplement preferences. This guide will walk you through each field of the form to help you complete it accurately and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions on each page carefully and to the best of your ability.
2. If you have any concerns or questions, contact a healthcare professional for guidance.
3. Please select "None" for any field that does not apply to you.
4. You can select multiple options for fields that allow multiple selections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your contact information, including your name and any other relevant details. This information will be used to contact you with personalized recommendations.
* **Height** (`height`, `number`, required: false): Enter your height in inches.
* **Weight** (`weight`, `number`, required: false): Enter your weight in pounds.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Describe any medical conditions or health issues you are currently experiencing.
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, `text`, required: false): Provide more details about your medical history, if necessary.
* **Health Goals** (`health_goals`, `text`, required: false): Describe your health goals, such as weight loss, muscle gain, or overall wellness.
* **Preferred Diet** (`preferred_diet`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your preferred diet type, such as Vegan, Omnivore, Flexitarian, Low-Carb, Paleo, or Other.
* **Medical Concerns** (`medical_concerns`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any medical concerns you are currently experiencing, such as Blood Pressure, Weight, Cholesterol, Diabetes, or Other.
* **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, required: false): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, required: false): List any allergies you may have.
* **Medical History 3** (`medical_history_3`, `text`, required: false): Provide more details about your medical history, if necessary.
* **Medical History 4** (`medical_history_4`, `text`, required: false): Provide more details about your medical history, if necessary.
* **Preferred Supplements** (`preferred_supplements`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any supplements you prefer, such as Multivitamin, Omega-3, Probiotics, or Other.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, `date`, required: false): Provide a date for follow-up contact, if desired.

Note: This form is not intended to be a substitute for professional medical advice. Always consult a healthcare professional before starting any new supplements or making significant changes to your lifestyle.
