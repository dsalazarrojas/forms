# Academic Integrity Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Integrity Awareness Survey aims to gauge the understanding and awareness of academic integrity policies within the institution, identifying areas where further education is needed to foster a culture of honesty and integrity.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select your role at the institution by choosing between Student, Faculty, or Staff.
2.  Indicate how familiar you are with academic integrity policies. You can choose between Very familiar, Somewhat familiar, or Not familiar.
3.  Report on the frequency at which you have observed academic misconduct. You can select from Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never.
4.  Rate your confidence in the institution's reporting mechanisms. Choose from Very confident, Confident, Neutral, or Not confident.
5.  Check if you have received any formal training on academic integrity policies. Select 'True' if you have received any training.
6.  If you have suggestions or ideas on how to improve awareness on academic integrity, provide them in the text box.
7.  Indicate your willingness to participate in awareness programs by selecting True, Maybe, or False.
8.  If you are willing to be contacted for follow-up, provide your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Role** (required): Your role at the institution.
*   **Awareness of policies** (required): How familiar are you with the institution's academic integrity policies.
*   **Observed misconduct frequency** (required): How often have you observed academic misconduct within the institution.
*   **Confidence in reporting** (required): How confident do you feel about the institution's reporting mechanisms.
*   **Received integrity training** (required): Have you received any formal training on academic integrity.
*   **Suggestions for improvement** (optional): Suggest any practical steps or resources that could improve awareness on academic integrity.
*   **Willingness to participate** (required): Would you like to join awareness programs or participate in academic integrity initiatives?
*   **Contact email** (optional): If you are willing to be contacted for follow-up, provide your email address for this purpose.
