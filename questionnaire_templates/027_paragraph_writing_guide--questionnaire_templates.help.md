# Paragraph Writing Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help guide writers create high-quality paragraphs. It collects feedback on the writing style, self-reflection, and feedback requests of the writer, which can be useful for writers to improve their writing skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form on one page at a time, starting with the first page.
* Read each field carefully and fill it out according to the instructions provided.
* Don't worry about filling out fields that are not applicable to you; just leave them blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **introduction**: This is the first page of the form, where you'll provide an introduction to your paragraph writing style. This can be a brief statement about your approach to writing, or a description of your writing experience.
* **self_reflection**: This field is a space for you to reflect on your writing style. Think about what you did well and what you could improve on in your paragraph writing.
* **feedback_requests**: This field allows you to specify what kind of feedback you would like to receive on your paragraph writing. Choose one or more of the options to let us know what you're looking for.
* **paragraph_draft**: This field is for you to write a sample paragraph. Try to write a good paragraph according to your best efforts.
* **paragraph_self_reflection**: This field is a space for you to reflect on your paragraph writing. Think about what you did well and what you could improve on in your paragraph writing.
* **feedback_requests**: This field is a space for you to specify what kind of feedback you would like to receive on your paragraph writing.
* **submitted**: This field allows you to indicate what you have submitted your paragraph writing for feedback. Choose one or more of the options to let us know what you're submitting.
* **teacher_feedback**: This field is for the teacher to provide feedback on your paragraph writing. Do not fill this out as it is for their input only.

## Tips
* Be honest with yourself about your writing style and what you could improve on.
* Take your time to reflect on your writing and think about what you can do better.
* Don't be too hard on yourself; this form is meant to be a helpful guide for improvement, not a criticism.
