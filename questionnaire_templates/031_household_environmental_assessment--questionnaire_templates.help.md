# Household Environmental Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Household Environmental Assessment form aims to collect information on the environmental footprint of households. This includes data on energy use, transportation habits, water consumption, food habits, plastic usage, and electricity use. The goal is to gather accurate and up-to-date information from households for better environmental management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Ensure you're familiar with the purpose of the form, which is to report on the environmental impact of your household activities.
2. Fill out each field according to the guidelines provided.
3. Enter your honest and accurate answers to each question.
4. Review your submission for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Household Energy Use** (`household_energy_use`, text, Optional): Please report your household's energy use habits, including electricity, gas, and other energy sources.
* **Household Transportation** (`household_transportation`, text, Optional): Describe your household's transportation methods, including cars, public transport, walking, or cycling.
* **Household Recycling** (`household_recycling`, text, Optional): Share your household's recycling practices, including materials recycled, frequency, and methods.
* **Household Water Use** (`household_water_use`, text, Optional): Report on your household's water consumption habits, including usage patterns and conservation efforts.
* **Household Waste Disposal** (`household_waste_disposal`, text, Optional): Describe your household's waste management practices, including disposal methods, materials recycled, and frequency.
* **Household Food Consumption** (`household_food_consumption`, text, Optional): Share your household's food consumption habits, including dietary preferences and frequency of food waste.
* **Household Plastic Usage** (`household_plastic_usage`, text, Optional): Report on your household's plastic usage, including types, frequency, and disposal methods.
* **Household Electricity Use** (`household_electricity_use`, text, Optional): Describe your household's electricity use habits, including appliances, duration, and energy-saving practices.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate in your submissions.
* Ensure you've filled out all required and optional fields.
* Review your answers for completeness and consistency before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
