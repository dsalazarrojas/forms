# Pessimism and Optimism Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help assess your current levels of pessimism and optimism, as well as their impact on your relationships and overall well-being. The form consists of a series of questions that will guide you through your current emotional state, relationships, and self-care practices. Your responses will help you gain a better understanding of your emotional balance and provide suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly, rating or choosing the options that best reflect your feelings and experiences.
2. For select_one questions, choose the answer that best describes your current state or feeling.
3. For select_multiple questions, select all options that apply to your experiences.
4. For text questions, provide a brief description or answer in a few sentences.
5. Take your time to answer each question, and don't worry if you need to think before answering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pessimism Scale**: Rate how often you experience pessimism, with 0 being never and 100 being always.
* **Optimism Scale**: Rate how often you experience optimism, with 0 being never and 100 being always.
* **Pessimism Frequency**: How often do you experience pessimism?
* **Optimism Frequency**: How often do you experience optimism?
* **Negative Outlook**: Do you have a negative outlook on the future? (Yes/No)
* **Positive Outlook**: Do you have a positive outlook on the future? (Yes/No)
* **Impact of Pessimism**: Describe how your pessimism affects your relationships.
* **Impact of Optimism**: Describe how your optimism affects your relationships.
* **Current Feeling**: How do you feel right now? (Yes/No)
* **Past Feeling**: How have you felt in the past? (Yes/No)
* **Relationships**: Select all relationships that have been negatively impacted by your pessimism or positively impacted by your optimism.
* **Other Relationship**: If there's another relationship that's impacted, describe it here.
* **Other Relationship Details**: Provide more information about this relationship.
* **Self-Care**: Do you practice self-care? (Yes/No)
* **Self-Care Activities**: Describe your self-care activities.
* **Self-Care Plan**: Describe your self-care plan.
* **Sleep**: Describe your current sleep habits.
* **Exercise**: Describe your exercise habits.
* **Relaxation**: Describe your relaxation practices.
* **Social Interaction**: Describe your social interaction habits.
* **Self-Care Self-Check**: Do you regularly check in with yourself to prioritize self-care? (Yes/No)
* **Self-Care Self-Check Reasons**: If you do, what are the reasons why?
* **Self-Care Plan Plan**: Describe your self-care plan. 

Note: This form is for your personal use only. Your responses will not be shared or used for any other purpose.
