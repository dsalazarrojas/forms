# PCOS Health Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

The PCOS Health Assessment Questionnaire is a tool used by healthcare providers to assess the health and symptoms of women with Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS). This questionnaire helps healthcare providers to identify potential issues and provide appropriate guidance and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the following questions about your menstrual cycle:
	* Menstrual Cycle: Enter the date of your last menstrual cycle.
2. Report your symptoms:
	* Symptom Frequency: Select all the symptoms that you experience regularly. Examples include Menstrual Pain, Fatigue, Headache, Mood swings, etc.
3. Provide family history:
	* Family History: Select your family history of PCOS/PCOD, or select "Mother (Polycystic Ovary syndrome (PCOS))" if it was not specified.
	* Father: Provide information about your father's health history.
	* Mother: Provide information about your mother's health history.
4. Report your menstrual regularity:
	* Menstrual Regularity: Select whether your menstruation is regular or irregular.
5. Provide more information:
	* Hirsutism: Provide more information about hirsutism.
	* Acne: Provide more information about acne.
	* Dizziness: Provide more information about dizziness.
	* Abdominal pain: Provide more information about abdominal pain.
	* Breast Tenderness: Provide more information about breast tenderness.
	* Mood Swings: Provide more information about mood swings.
	* Mood Changes: Provide more information about mood changes.
	* Weight Gain: Provide more information about weight gain.
	* Weight Loss: Provide more information about weight loss.
	* Bloating: Provide more information about bloating.
	* Other: Provide more information about other symptoms not listed.
