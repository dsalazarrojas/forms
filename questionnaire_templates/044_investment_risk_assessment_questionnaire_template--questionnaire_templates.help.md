# Investment Risk Assessment Questionnaire Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investment Risk Assessment Questionnaire Template is designed to help users understand and assess their investment risk tolerance and preferences. By filling out this form, users will be able to create a comprehensive investment strategy that aligns with their goals and risk comfort level.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and select the options that best reflect your investment goals and risk tolerance.
2. Ensure that you are answering honestly and accurately, without overestimating or underestimating your risk tolerance.
3. Select the options that best fit your investment preferences and goals.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure that they accurately reflect your investment strategy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. investment_risk_assessment_questionnaire_intro
* **investment_risk_assessment_questionnaire_intro** (`intro`, `text`, optional): This is the introduction to the form, explaining the purpose and importance of the questionnaire. Please fill this out with a brief description of your investment goals and risk tolerance.

### 2. risk_tolerance
* **risk_tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`, `number`, optional): This question assesses your risk tolerance on a scale of 1-3 (Low, Medium, High). Please select the number that best represents your risk appetite.

### 3. investment_horizon
* **investment_horizon** (`investment_horizon`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks about your investment horizon, which can be Low, Medium, or High. Please select the one that best fits your investment timeframe.

### 4. investment_objective
