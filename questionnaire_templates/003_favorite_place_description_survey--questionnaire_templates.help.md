# Favorite Place Description Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Favorite Place Description Survey is a questionnaire used to collect information about your favorite place. This form is designed to gather details about the place you love, including its category, name, description, location, rating, and memories associated with it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. For each question, enter your response in the provided text field.
3. If you have a memory associated with your favorite place, try to provide as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your favorite place?** (`favorite_place_description_survey_form`, text, required): Enter a short description of your favorite place.
* **Category** (`place_category`, select_one, optional): Select a category that best describes your favorite place (e.g., Beach, Mountain, City, etc.).
* **Place Name** (`place_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your favorite place.
* **Description** (`place_description`, text, optional): Write a short description of your favorite place, including what makes it special to you.
* **Location** (`place_location`, select_one, optional): Choose the continent where your favorite place is located (e.g., North America, Europe, Asia, etc.).
* **Rating (1-5)** (`place_rating`, number, optional): Rate your favorite place on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Favorite Memory** (`favorite_memory`, text, optional): Share a memorable experience or story associated with your favorite place.
* **Details** (`favorite_memory_details`, text, optional): Provide more details about your favorite memory, including when and why it's special.
* **Date** (`favorite_memory_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the memory you associated with your favorite place.
* **Time** (`favorite_memory_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of day or year when the memory occurred.
