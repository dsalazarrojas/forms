# Online Assessment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This online assessment consent form is used to collect demographic information and consent from participants for research or survey purposes. Please use this form only for its intended purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete the form, please follow these steps:
  1. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
  2. Review the information provided and select the correct answer for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents. This is a text field, so type your name as you would normally write it.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents. This is also a text field.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, which will be used for further communication.
* **Consent**: Select 'Yes' if you have reviewed and understood the information provided, and 'No' if you have not.
* **Agreement**: Select all the statements that apply to you, or choose 'I do not have time to finish reading, and I do not consent to participate.'
* **Notes**: If you have any additional information you'd like to provide, or if you'd like to add any comments, type them here.
* **Additional Consent**: If you have any other consent or agreements, you can type them here.
