# Social Media Activism Fatigue Study - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about social media usage and its potential impact on a person's mental health, specifically looking for signs of social media activism fatigue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the frequency of your social media usage from the options provided for the "Social Media Usage" question.
2. Choose your emotional exhaustion score from the options provided for the "Emotional Exhaustion Score" question.
3. Select your sleep quality from the options provided for the "Sleep Quality" question.
4. Choose the amount of time you spend on social media from the options provided for the "Time Spent on Social Media" question.
5. Indicate how often you experience social media overload from the options provided for the "Social Media Overload" question.
6. Select the strength of your supportive network from the options provided for the "Supportive Network" question.
7. Choose how often you practice self-care from the options provided for the "Self-Care" question.
8. Answer "True" or "False" to the question about experiencing anxiety or depression.
9. Select how often you use coping mechanisms from the options provided for the "Coping Mechanisms" question.
10. Choose how often you practice digital detox from the options provided for the "Digital Detox" question.
11. Answer "True" or "False" to the question about help-seeking behavior.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Social Media Usage**: This question asks about the frequency of your social media usage. Please select the option that best describes your daily social media usage habits.
* **Emotional Exhaustion Score**: This question measures how emotionally exhausted you feel after using social media. Please choose a score that best represents your emotional state.
* **Sleep Quality**: This question asks about the quality of your sleep. Please select the option that best describes your sleep quality.
* **Time Spent on Social Media**: This question asks about the amount of time you spend on social media. Please choose the option that best represents your daily social media usage time.
* **Social Media Overload**: This question asks about how often you experience social media overload. Please select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Supportive Network**: This question asks about the strength of your supportive network. Please select the option that best describes your social support system.
* **Self-Care**: This question asks about your self-care practices. Please select the option that best describes how often you practice self-care.
* **Social Media Fatigue**: This question asks about the level of social media fatigue you experience. Please select a number that best represents your level of fatigue.
* **Anxiety/Depression**: This question asks about whether you experience anxiety or depression. Please answer "True" if you experience these emotions frequently or "False" if you do not.
* **Coping Mechanisms**: This question asks about how often you use coping mechanisms. Please select the option that best describes your coping practices.
* **Digital Detox**: This question asks about how often you practice digital detox. Please select the option that best describes your behavior.
* **Help-Seeking Behavior**: This question asks about whether you seek help when experiencing mental health issues. Please answer "True" if you do seek help and "False" if you do not.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
- If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact us.
- All answers will be kept confidential and anonymous.
