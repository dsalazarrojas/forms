# Sizing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the user's personal and product usage preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, simply follow the on-screen instructions and fill in the required fields.
2. Each field is labeled clearly, and you can ask for help if you are unsure about any field.
3. You can navigate through the form using the navigation buttons or by using the keyboard to scroll.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your height?**: This field is a number input field where you can enter your height in inches or any other unit you prefer.
* **What is your weight?**: This field is also a number input field where you can enter your weight in pounds or any other unit you prefer.
* **Section 2**: This is a text field for any additional comments or information you'd like to share.
* **What is your age?**: This field is a number input field where you can enter your age.
* **What is your zip code?**: This field is a select-one field with two options: 'Yes' and 'No'. Please select the option that best applies to you.
* **Section 3**: This is a text field for any additional comments or information you'd like to share.
* **What is your email address?**: This field is a required field for your email address.
* **What is your phone number?**: This field is a required text field for your phone number.
* **When would you like to schedule?**: This field is a date field where you can select a date for your appointment.
* **What time of day?**: This field is a time field where you can select a time of day for your appointment.
* **What is your zip code?**: This field is a select-one field with two options: 'Yes' and 'No'. Please select the option that best applies to you.
* **What is your country?**: This field is a select-multiple field with two options: 'Yes' and 'No'. Please select all the options that best apply to you.
* **How often do you plan to use this product?**: This field is a select-multiple field with two options: 'Yes' and 'No'. Please select all the options that best apply to you.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your submission is complete and accurate.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for clarification.
* This form is for testing and development purposes only, so please don't worry about production data entry.
