# Secret Valentine Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Secret Valentine Questionnaire is a series of questions designed to gather information about participant relationships, interactions, and behaviors related to Valentine's Day. The form is meant to be completed by participants who are familiar with the concept of Secret Valentine's Day, where individuals exchange anonymous gifts and messages with their crushes or loved ones.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the participant name field, which is where you will enter your name or the name of the participant you are responding to.
2. Choose how you know the participant (friend, classmate, neighbor, family member, colleague, or romantic partner).
3. If you have a message to send to the participant, type it in the corresponding field.
4. If you have received a message from the participant, type it in the corresponding field.
5. If you have given or received a gift, type it in the corresponding field.
6. Select your favorite participant from the provided options.
7. Type your favorite message or reason for choosing that participant.
8. Type your favorite gift or reason for why you chose that gift.
9. Provide any additional information on why participants exchange messages and gifts.
10. Indicate how often participants exchange messages and gifts.
11. Explain why you chose your favorite participant and provide any additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name or the name of the participant you are responding to.
* **How do you know the participant?** (`participant_relationship`, select_one, optional): Choose how you know the participant (friend, classmate, neighbor, family member, colleague, or romantic partner).
* **What message do you want to send?** (`message_to_participant`, text, optional): Type a message to send to the participant.
* **What message did you receive?** (`received_message`, text, optional): Type the message you received from the participant.
* **What gift do you want to give?** (`gift_to_participant`, text, optional): Type the gift you want to give to the participant.
* **What gift did you receive?** (`received_gift`, text, optional): Type the gift you received from the participant.
* **Favorite Participant** (`favorite_participant`, select_one, optional): Select the participant you favor (Participant 1, Participant 2, or Participant 3).
* **Favorite Message** (`favorite_message`, text, optional): Type the reason for choosing your favorite participant.
* **Favorite Gift** (`favorite_gift`, text, optional): Type the reason for choosing your favorite gift.
* **Why do participants exchange messages and gifts?** (`why_participants_exchange`, text, optional): Provide any additional information on why participants exchange messages and gifts.
* **How often do participants exchange messages and gifts?** (`participant_exchange_frequency`, text, optional): Indicate how often participants exchange messages and gifts.
* **Why is this participant your favorite?** (`favorite_participant_reason`, text, optional): Explain why you chose your favorite participant.
* **Other reason for choosing participant** (`favorite_participant_other`, text, optional): Provide any other reason for choosing your favorite participant.
* **Other reason** (`favorite_participant_other_reason`, text, optional): Type the other reason for choosing your favorite participant.
* **Other reason for choosing gift** (`favorite_participant_other_reason`, text, optional): Type the other reason for choosing your favorite gift.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions relevant to your relationship with the participant.
* Be honest and provide accurate information.
* Use the provided options for selecting the participant you favor.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
