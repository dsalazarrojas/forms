# Audio Initial Project Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Audio Initial Project Questionnaire is designed to gather information from audio professionals to register their talent, experience, and technical setup for potential collaborations. By completing this form, you will be able to provide detailed information about your voice classification, primary languages, experience in the audio/voice industry, technical setup, project preferences, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by providing your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Select your voice classification from the options provided in the "Voice Classification" field.
3. List your primary languages in the "Primary Languages" field, including any dialects relevant to your work.
4. Enter your years of experience in the audio/voice industry in the "Years of Audio/Voice Experience" field.
5. Indicate your availability for live directed sessions in the "Are you available for live directed sessions?" field.
6. Provide a link to your demo or portfolio in the "Link to Demo or Portfolio" field.
7. Finally, enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`talent_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be referred to professionally.
* **Voice Classification** (`voice_classification`, select_one, required): Select your voice classification from the options provided.
* **Primary Languages** (`primary_languages`, text, required): List your primary languages, including any dialects relevant to your work.
* **Years of Audio/Voice Experience** (`experience_years`, number, required): Enter your years of experience in the audio/voice industry.
* **Do you have a professional home studio?** (`home_studio_availability`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have a professional home studio.
* **Primary Microphone Model** (`microphone_model`, text, optional): Enter your primary microphone model, if applicable.
* **Audio Interface Model** (`audio_interface`, text, optional): Enter your audio interface model, if applicable.
* **Digital Audio Workstation (DAW)** (`daw_used`, text, optional): Enter the DAW software you use, e.g. Pro Tools, Audition, Logic.
* **Types of Projects interested in** (`project_interest`, select_multiple, required): Select all the types of projects you are interested in, such as Commercial/Advertising, Audiobook Narration, etc.
* **Link to Demo or Portfolio** (`demo_portfolio_link`, text, required): Provide a link to your demo or portfolio, e.g. URL to website or SoundCloud.
* **Are you available for live directed sessions?** (`live_direction_ready`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you are available for live directed sessions, e.g. SourceConnect, Zoom, Riverside.
* **Typical Weekly Availability** (`availability_notes`, text, optional): Enter your typical weekly availability, if applicable.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
