# Occupational Health And Safety Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Occupational Health And Safety Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information from employees about their workplace address, supervisor details, safety training, hazards, and incident reporting. This form helps the management and HR team to ensure a safe working environment and identify potential risks.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer all the questions honestly and accurately to the best of your knowledge.
2.  Provide your workplace address, including the name of your department and floor number (if applicable).
3.  Provide your supervisor's name and title.
4.  Report any hazards that you have encountered in the workplace.
5.  Provide any incident reports, including incident date, time, location, type, and description.
6.  Specify any prevention measures taken after the incident.
7.  Specify the cause and consequence of the incident.
8.  Specify any actions taken to investigate the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Workplace Address** (`workplace_address`, text, optional): This field is used to collect your current workplace address. Please provide your department name and floor number (if applicable).
*   **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional): This field is used to collect your supervisor's name. Please provide the name of your immediate supervisor.
*   **Supervisor Title** (`supervisor_title`, text, optional): This field is used to collect your supervisor's title. Please provide your supervisor's official title.
*   **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, text, optional): This field is used to collect your supervisor's phone number. Please provide your supervisor's phone number for contact.
*   **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, optional): This field is used to collect your supervisor's email address. Please provide your supervisor's email address for contact.
*   **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the name of the manager to whom the incident report will be submitted.
*   **Safety Training** (`safety_training`, text, optional): This field is used to collect information about your safety training. Please provide details about your safety training received.
*   **Hazards Present** (`hazards_present`, text, optional): This field is used to collect information about hazards present in your workplace. Please provide details about any hazards you have encountered.
*   **Hazard Control Measures** (`hazard_control_measures`, text, optional): This field is used to collect information about control measures taken to mitigate hazards. Please provide details about any control measures taken.
*   **Incident Report** (`incident_report`, text, optional): This field is used to submit a report about any incidents you have experienced in the workplace. Please provide details about the incident, including date, time, location, type, and description.
*   **Incident Frequency** (`incident_frequency`, text, optional): This field is used to collect information about the frequency of incidents. Please provide details about how often incidents have occurred in your department.
*   **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, optional): This field is used to collect information about the type of incident that occurred. Please select the type of incident from the available options.
*   **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, optional): This field is used to collect information about the location of the incident. Please provide details about the location where the incident occurred.
*   **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, optional): This field is used to collect the date of the incident. Please provide the date of the incident.
*   **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, optional): This field is used to collect the time of the incident. Please provide the time of the incident.
*   **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, optional): This field is used to collect details about the incident. Please provide a description of what happened.
*   **Incident Investigation** (`incident_investigation`, text, optional): This field is used to collect information about the investigation after the incident. Please provide details about any actions taken after the incident.
*   **Incident Outcome** (`incident_outcome`, text, optional): This field is used to collect information about the outcome of the incident. Please provide details about how the incident was resolved.
*   **Incident Cause** (`incident_cause`, text, optional): This field is used to collect information about the cause of the incident. Please provide details about what caused the incident.
*   **Incident Consequence** (`incident_consequence`, text, optional): This field is used to collect information about the consequences of the incident. Please provide details about the effects of the incident.
*   **Prevention Measures** (`prevention_measures`, text, optional): This field is used to collect information about measures taken to prevent future incidents. Please provide details about any prevention measures taken.

## Tips
*   Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
*   This form is only to be completed by employees or supervisors, not others.
*   Ensure that the form is filled out completely and accurately.
*   This form is designed to help the management and HR team create a safe working environment and identify potential risks. Therefore, please take this seriously and contribute to the health and safety of your workplace.
