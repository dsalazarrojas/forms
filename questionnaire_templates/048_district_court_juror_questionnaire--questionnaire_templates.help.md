# District Court Juror Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The District Court Juror Questionnaire is designed to collect information from potential jurors to evaluate and select suitable individuals to serve in the court. The collected data will be used to assess the qualifications and suitability of each respondent for juror service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and requirements.
2. Fill out the form accurately and truthfully, providing all required information.
3. Submit the form once complete, and do not duplicate efforts by filling out the form multiple times.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Juror Selection Process** (`juror_selection_process`, text, optional): This field is a free-form text field where you can describe your understanding of the juror selection process. Please provide your thoughts on how the process works and what is expected of you as a potential juror.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): This field is used to collect your contact details, including your name, address, phone number, and email. Please provide accurate and up-to-date information to facilitate communication with the court.
* **Juror Background** (`juror_background`, note, optional): This field is a note field where you can provide any relevant background information about yourself. Please be as brief and concise as possible, and avoid sensitive or personally identifiable information.
* **Juror Experience** (`juror_experience`, text, optional): This field is used to collect information about your experience in similar roles or positions. Please describe any relevant experience you have that may be useful for juror service.

## Tips
- Be truthful and accurate when filling out the form.
- Do not duplicate efforts by filling out the form multiple times.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the District Court for assistance.
