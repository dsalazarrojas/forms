# euro_millions_number_submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information and their preferences for the Euro Millions project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your participant information in the "Participant Info" field.
2. Select your numbers picked for the Euro Millions game in the "Numbers Picked" field.
3. Enter your contact details in the "Contact Details" field.
4. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
6. Enter your player ID in the "Player ID" field.
7. Enter your name in the "Player Name" field.
8. Enter your player name alias in the "Player Name (Alias)" field.
9. Enter your player email address in the "Player Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Info**: This is the first field, where you will enter your participant information. Please fill in your relevant details.
* **Numbers Picked** (select multiple): Choose the numbers you have picked for the Euro Millions game.
* **Contact Details** : Please provide your contact information for us to reach you.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Player ID**: Enter your player ID for the game.
* **Player Name**: Enter your name as a player.
* **Player Name (Alias)**: Enter a short alias for your name as a player.
* **Player Email**: Enter your player email address.
