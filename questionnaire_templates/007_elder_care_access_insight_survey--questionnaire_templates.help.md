# Elder Care Access Insight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Elder Care Access Insight Survey is designed to gather information about your experiences with care services, any barriers you face in accessing these services, and your satisfaction levels with these services. This survey is essential in helping us understand your needs and preferences, which will enable us to create more effective care services for the elderly community.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. Select all relevant options that apply to your experiences with care services.
3. For text fields, please provide as much information as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Experiences with Elder Care Services**
	+ This is a text field where you can provide detailed experiences with care services. Please describe your interactions, successes, or challenges with these services.
* **Barriers to Accessing Care Services**
	+ This field is used to identify any barriers that you face in accessing care services. Please select all the relevant options that apply to your experiences.
	+ **very poor**: You have a very poor experience in accessing care services.
	+ **poor**: You have a poor experience in accessing care services.
	+ **neutral**: You have a neutral experience in accessing care services.
	+ **good**: You have a good experience in accessing care services.
	+ **excellent**: You have an excellent experience in accessing care services.
* **Satisfaction with Care Services**
	+ This field is used to measure your satisfaction level with care services. Please select all the relevant options that apply to your experiences.
	+ **very satisfied**: You are very satisfied with care services.
	+ **satisfied**: You are satisfied with care services.
	+ **neutral**: You have a neutral experience with care services.
	+ **dissatisfied**: You are dissatisfied with care services.
	+ **very dissatisfied**: You are very dissatisfied with care services.
* **Regional Access to Care Services**
	+ This field is used to determine the availability of care services in your region. Please select all the relevant options that apply to your experiences.
	+ **very poor**: Your region has a very poor access to care services.
	+ **poor**: Your region has a poor access to care services.
	+ **neutral**: Your region has a neutral access to care services.
	+ **good**: Your region has a good access to care services.
	+ **excellent**: Your region has an excellent access to care services.
* **Demographic Characteristics**
	+ This field is used to collect demographic information about you. Please select your gender.
	+ **male**: You are male.
	+ **female**: You are female.
	+ **other**: You are of other gender.
* **Region of Residence**
	+ This field is used to identify the region where you reside. Please enter your region of residence.
* **Region Served**
	+ This field is used to determine the region where you are served by care services. Please select the relevant option that applies to your experiences.
	+ **True**: You are served by care services in your region.
	+ **False**: You are not served by care services in your region.
* **Benefits of Care Services**
	+ This field is used to identify the benefits you derive from care services. Please select all the relevant options that apply to your experiences.
	+ **True**: You have accessed care services and have experienced the benefits.
	+ **False**: You have not accessed care services or do not derive any benefits from them.
* **Care Services Barriers**
	+ This field is used to identify any barriers you face in accessing care services. Please select all the relevant options that apply to your experiences.
	+ **True**: You have experienced barriers in accessing care services.
	+ **False**: You do not face any barriers in accessing care services.
* **Satisfaction with Services**
	+ This field is used to measure your overall satisfaction level with care services. Please select the relevant option that applies to your experiences.

	+ **dissatisfied**: You are dissatisfied with care services.
* **Care Services Satisfaction**
	+ This field is used to measure your satisfaction level with care services. Please select all the relevant options that apply to your experiences.
	+ **very satisfied**: You are very satisfied with care services.
	+ **satisfied**: You are satisfied with care services.
	+ **neutral**: You have a neutral experience with care services.
	+ **dissatisfied**: You are dissatisfied with care services.
	+ **very dissatisfied**: You are very dissatisfied with care services.
* **Regional Coverage**
	+ This field is used to determine if your region has access to care services. Please select the relevant option that applies to your experiences.
	+ **True**: Your region has access to care services.
	+ **False**: Your region does not have access to care services.
* **Access to Care Services**

	+ **True**: You have access to care services in your region.
	+ **False**: You do not have access to care services in your region.
* **Region Served by Care Services**
