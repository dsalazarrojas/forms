# Owner Trauma Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Owner Trauma Assessment Survey is a tool used to help owners assess their trauma and identify the specific services they need to address it. This form is designed to gather information on the owner's reasons for seeking services and the types of services they require.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and questions being asked.
2. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Select the services you need help with from the provided options.
4. Submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your reason for seeking services?** (`trauma_assessment_survey`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ This question is asking you to explain why you are seeking services. Please select all the reasons that apply to you.
* **What services do you need help with?** (`trauma_services_needed`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This question is asking you to choose the services you need help with. Please select one or more of the services listed.
* **Which services do you need help with?** (`trauma_services_needed_2`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ This question is asking you to choose additional services you need help with. Please select all the services that apply to you.
* **What services do you need help with?** (this is a duplicate question, please skip)

Note: The last question is a duplicate and can be skipped as it is not necessary to answer it. 

Tips:

* Please answer all questions thoroughly and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask for clarification.
* If you need help with a service that is not listed, please mention it in the comments section.
* Please submit the form when you are finished.
