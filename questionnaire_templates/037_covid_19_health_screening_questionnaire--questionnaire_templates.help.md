# COVID-19 Health Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The COVID-19 Health Screening Questionnaire is designed to assess the health status of individuals, particularly in the context of the COVID-19 pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your age in the "Age" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter the date of your screening in the "Screening Date" field.
5. Answer the symptom questions:
	* Have you had a fever in the past 14 days? 
	* Do you have a persistent cough?
	* Do you have difficulty breathing or shortness of breath?
	* Do you have a sore throat?
	* Have you experienced loss of taste or smell?
	* Do you have muscle or body aches?
6. Answer the exposure questions:
	* Have you had close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case?
	* If yes, when did you have contact?
	* Have you traveled to an area affected by COVID-19?
	* If yes, where did you travel?
7. Answer the medical history questions:
	* Do you have any chronic health conditions (e.g., diabetes, hypertension, heart disease, lung disease, kidney disease, immunocompromised)?
	* Are you currently taking any medications?
8. Answer the vaccination questions:
	* Are you fully vaccinated with a booster?
	* Are you fully vaccinated without a booster?
	* Are you partially vaccinated?
	* Are you not vaccinated?
	* Do you prefer not to disclose your vaccination status?
9. Answer the screening result question:
	* What is your screening result (low risk, moderate risk, or high risk)?
10. Add any additional notes or concerns.
11. Enter the name of the screening staff member.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your name.
* **Age**: Enter your age.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Screening Date**: Enter the date of your screening.
* **Fever in Past 14 Days**:
	+ Answer "Yes" if you have had a fever in the past 14 days.
	+ Answer "No" if you do not have a fever in the past 14 days.
* **Persistent Cough**: 
	+ Answer "Yes" if you have a persistent cough.
	+ Answer "No" if you do not have a persistent cough.
* **Difficulty Breathing or Shortness of Breath**: 
	+ Answer "Yes" if you have difficulty breathing or shortness of breath.
	+ Answer "No" if you do not have difficulty breathing or shortness of breath.
* **Sore Throat**: 
	+ Answer "Yes" if you have a sore throat.
	+ Answer "No" if you do not have a sore throat.
* **Loss of Taste or Smell**: 
	+ Answer "Yes" if you have lost your taste or smell.
	+ Answer "No" if you do not have lost your taste or smell.
* **Muscle or Body Aches**: 
	+ Answer "Yes" if you have muscle or body aches.
	+ Answer "No" if you do not have muscle or body aches.
* **Close Contact with Confirmed COVID-19 Case**: 
	+ Answer "Yes" if you have had close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case.
	+ Answer "No" if you do not have close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case.
* **If yes, when was the contact**: 
	+ Enter the date of your contact.
* **Travel to COVID-19 Affected Area**: 
	+ Answer "Yes" if you have traveled to an area affected by COVID-19.
	+ Answer "No" if you do not have traveled to an area affected by COVID-19.
* **Location of Travel**: 
	+ Enter the location of your travel.
* **Chronic Health Conditions**: 
	+ Select all the chronic health conditions that apply (e.g., diabetes, hypertension, heart disease, lung disease, kidney disease, immunocompromised).
* **Currently Taking Medications**: 
	+ Answer "Yes" if you are currently taking any medications.
	+ Answer "No" if you are not currently taking any medications.
* **Vaccination Status**: 
	+ Answer your vaccination status (fully vaccinated with booster, fully vaccinated without booster, partially vaccinated, not vaccinated, or prefer not to disclose).
* **Screening Result**: 
	+ Answer your screening result (low risk, moderate risk, or high risk).
* **Additional Notes or Concerns**: 
	+ Enter any additional notes or concerns.
* **Screening Staff Name**: 
	+ Enter the name of the screening staff member.

Note: If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us for assistance.
