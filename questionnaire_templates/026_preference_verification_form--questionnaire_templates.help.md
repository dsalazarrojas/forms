# Preference Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preference Verification Form is a simple, multi-page form used to collect contact information and preferences for service selection. The form is designed for users to provide accurate and up-to-date contact details and service preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **Contact Info** section with your name and email address.
2. Select your preferred contact methods and services in the respective sections.
3. Review your selections to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`contact_info_1`): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`contact_info_2`): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address. Make sure to enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Preference 1** (`preference_1`): Select how you would like to be contacted. This can be **Email**, **Phone**, or **Both**.
* **Contact Preference 2** (`preference_2`): Select one or more ways you would like to be contacted. This can be **Email**, **Phone**, or both.
* **Contact Type 1** (`contact_type_1`): Select one or more ways you would like to receive contacts. This can be **Phone**, **Email**.
* **Contact Type 2** (`contact_type_2`): Select one or more ways you would like to receive contacts. This can be **Phone**, **Email**.
* **Service 1** (`service_selection_1`): Select one service you are interested in. This can be **Membership**, **Membership and Services**, or **Services**.
* **Service 2** (`service_selection_2`): Select one or more services you are interested in. This can be **Membership**, **Services**.
* **Service 3** (`service_selection_3`): Select one or more services you are interested in. This can be **Membership**, **Services**.

## Tips

* Make sure to review your selections carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please feel free to contact us for assistance.
* This form will help us tailor our services to your preferences and needs.
