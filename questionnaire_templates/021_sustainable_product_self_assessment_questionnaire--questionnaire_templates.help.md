# Sustainable Product Self Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you assess the sustainability of a product. It is intended for evaluating and reviewing products in terms of their environmental impact and sustainability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing a detailed response for each field.
2. Make sure to fill out all the required fields (marked as "required").
3. You can skip the optional fields if you don't have any additional information to provide.
4. Review your responses for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **product_materials (Text)**: Please describe the materials used in the product, including any notable materials that have significant environmental impact.
* **manufacturing_impact (Text)**: Please explain the environmental impact of the product's manufacturing process.
* **packaging (Text)**: Describe the product's packaging, including any materials used and potential environmental implications.
* **end_of_life (Text)**: Describe the product's end-of-life process, including disposal, recycling, or repurposing methods.
* **sustainability_review (Text)**: Please conduct a review of the product's sustainability, including its environmental, social, and economic impacts.
* **sustainability_reporting (Text)**: Provide any relevant sustainability reports or data related to the product.
* **additional_comments (Text)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the product's sustainability.
* **assigned_tool (Text)**: You can provide any additional tools or resources that are assigned to this product for sustainability evaluation.
