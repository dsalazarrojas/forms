# Estate Planning Client Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Estate Planning Client Questionnaire is designed to gather essential information about you and your estate planning goals. This information will be used to create a personalized estate plan that meets your unique needs and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question carefully and thoroughly. For select_one, select_multiple, and yes/no questions, please select the appropriate option that best describes your situation. For text and number questions, please enter your response in the provided field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): This is your name as it appears on your identification documents. Please enter your full name as it is on your driver's license, passport, or government ID.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): This is your first name. Please enter your given name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): This is your last name. Please enter your surname as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, required): This is your birth date. Please enter the date of your birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Estate Location** (`estate_location`, text, optional): This is the location of your estate or property. Please enter the city, state, or country where your estate is located.
* **Assets** (`assets`, text, required): This is a list of your assets. Please describe the type and value of your assets, such as cash, property, or investments.
* **Estate Planning Goal** (`estate_planning_goal`, text, required): This is your goal for your estate. Please describe your objective, such as ensuring the well-being of your family or minimizing tax liabilities.
* **Family Members** (`family_members`, text, required): This is a list of your family members. Please describe the names and relationships of your family members, such as spouse, children, or siblings.
* **Power of Attorney** (`power_of_attorney`, select_one, required): Do you have a power of attorney in place? If yes, please select the type of power of attorney you have.
* **Will** (`will`, select_multiple, required): Do you have a will in place? If yes, please select the type of will you have.
* **Trust** (`trust`, select_one, required): Do you have a trust in place? If yes, please select the type of trust you have.
* **Guardian** (`guardian`, select_one, required): Do you have a guardian for your minor children or incapacitated individuals? If yes, please select the type of guardian you have.
* **Power of Attorney Documents** (`power_of_attorney_documents`, select_multiple, required): Do you have power of attorney documents in place? If yes, please select the type of documents you have.
* **Executor** (`executor`, select_one, required): Do you have an executor for your estate? If yes, please select the type of executor you have.
* **Beneficiaries** (`beneficiaries`, select_multiple, optional): This is a list of beneficiaries. Please describe the names and relationships of your beneficiaries, such as spouse, children, or siblings.
* **Beneficiary Details** (`beneficiary_details`, text, optional): This is additional information about your beneficiaries. Please describe their ages, relationships, or other relevant details.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): This is your emergency contact information. Please enter the name and phone number of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): This is a text field for any additional information or notes about your situation.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_contact_person`, select_one, optional): Do you have an emergency contact person? If yes, please select the type of emergency contact person you have.
* **Estate Address** (`estate_address`, text, required): This is the address of your estate or property. Please enter the street address and any additional details.
* **Estate Type** (`estate_type`, select_one, required): What type of estate do you have? Please select the type of estate you have, such as individual or joint.
* **Estate Size** (`estate_size`, select_one, required): What is the size of your estate? Please select the size of your estate, such as small, medium, or large.
* **Estate Value** (`estate_value`, number, required): This is the value of your estate. Please enter the value of your estate in the format $X,XXX.XX.
* **Estate Currency** (`estate_currency`, text, required): This is the currency of your estate. Please enter the currency of your estate, such as USD or CAD.
* **Estate Distribution** (`estate_distribution`, select_multiple, required): How do you want to distribute your estate? Please select the type of distribution you prefer, such as equal or unequal.
