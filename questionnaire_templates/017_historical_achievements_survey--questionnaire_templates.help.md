# Historical Achievements Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This survey is designed to gather information about your experiences, preferences, and familiarity with history. It will take approximately 10-15 minutes to complete and will ask you a series of questions about your occupation, involvement with history, and perceptions of its importance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
2. You can select only one option for multiple-choice questions.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact information].

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. User Response
* **user_response** (text): This field is for any additional comments or thoughts you have about history or this survey.

### 2. How Do You Know This?
* **how_do_you_know_this** (text): Briefly explain how you know or learned about history (e.g., school, personal research, online, etc.).
