# DNS Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The DNS Assessment Survey form is designed to collect user feedback on DNS practices, infrastructure, and security readiness. The form helps the DNS team or organization gain insights into their services' performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

- Start by reading the form title, "DNS Assessment Survey," to understand the form's purpose.
- Answer each question in the form according to your honest experience or knowledge.
- Fill out all the required fields. In this form, fields with a "required: true" attribute are marked as required.
- Use the provided options for select_multiple type fields to choose one or more options that best describe your experience.
- Type any additional comments or feedback in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **DNS Assessment Survey** (`survey_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief description or title for your feedback or experience with the DNS service.
* **DNS Practices** (`dns_practices`, text, optional): Describe your current DNS practices, including any challenges or successes you've had.
* **Infrastructure Satisfaction** (`infrastructure_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): How satisfied are you with the DNS service's infrastructure? Choose all that apply:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Security Readiness** (`security_readiness`, text, optional): Share your thoughts on the DNS service's security posture, including any concerns or strengths you've observed.
* **DNS Management Practices** (`dns_management_practices`, text, optional): Describe your current DNS management practices, including any challenges or successes you've had.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the DNS service or your experience with it.
