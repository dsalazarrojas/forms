# Bio Activation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about your experiences with our bio-activation programs. Your responses will help us improve future programs and provide valuable feedback for better service quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the step-by-step instructions below:

1.  Please review the consent and data use note at the bottom of this form.
2.  Fill in the requested information as accurately as possible.
3.  Select the methods you used during activation.
4.  Choose your perceived benefit level from the provided options.
5.  Describe any challenges you faced while using the activation methods.
6.  Indicate your interest in future programs.
7.  Provide any additional feedback or comments you may have.
7.  Finally, read through the questions and fill in all the required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Survey Summary --** (<span style="font-size: small">survey_header</span>): This section is an optional introduction and summary of the survey.
* **Name** (<span style="font-size: small">respondent_name</span>): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents or ID.
* **Organization** (<span style="font-size: small">organization</span>): If you are affiliated with an institution or company, please provide the name.
* **Role or title** (<span style="font-size: small">role_or_title</span>): Describe your position or role in the organization.
* **Program participation date** (<span style="font-size: small">participation_date</span>): Enter the date you participated in or used the activation methods.
* **Methods tried** (<span style="font-size: small">methods_tried</span>): Choose all the methods you used during activation. Select at least one option from the dropdown list.
*   \* **Perceived benefit level** (<span style="font-size: small">perceived_benefit</span>): Choose one option that reflects your overall experience with the activation methods.
* **Challenges encountered** (<span style="font-size: small">challenges_encountered</span>): Describe any challenges you faced during activation, whether technical or logistical.
* **Interest in future programs** (<span style="font-size: small">interest_in_future_programs</span>): Select one option that indicates your interest level for future programs.
* **Additional feedback** (<span style="font-size: small">additional_feedback</span>): Share any other thoughts or comments about the activation methods or the survey.
* **Consent and data use note** (<span style="font-size: small">consent_note</span>): Review the consent and data use note before providing your feedback.
