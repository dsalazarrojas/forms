# urban-air-quality-awareness-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their perception and behavior related to air quality awareness. The survey aims to gather insights on how users perceive and act upon air quality issues in their location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start with the introduction page, which is page 1.
2. On each page, read the label and hint for each field.
3. Fill in the required fields (indicated by the required label).
4. Choose your answers for the select_one and select_multiple fields.
5. Enter your email address and phone number in the email and phone fields.
6. Add any additional comments or notes in the note field.
7. Finally, submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Urban Air Quality Awareness Survey** (`1`, `text`, not required): This is the introduction page of the survey.
* **What is your location** (`2`, `text`, not required): Enter your current location or where you live.
* **Perception** (`3`, `select_one`, not required): Answer how you perceive air quality in your location. You can choose from:
	+ strongly disagree
	+ disagree
	+ neutral
	+ agree
	+ strongly agree
* **Concern** (`4`, `select_multiple`, not required): Check all the concerns you have about air quality in your location. You can select from:
	+ very concerned
	+ somewhat concerned
	+ not very concerned
	+ not concerned at all
* **Behavior** (`5`, `select_one`, not required): Describe how often you stay indoors to avoid air quality issues. You can choose from:
	+ mostly stay indoors
	+ sometimes stay indoors
	+ rarely stay indoors
	+ always stay indoors
* **Perception Time** (`6`, `time`, not required): Report the time of day when you first noticed air quality issues in your location.
* **Concern Time** (`7`, `time`, not required): If you have concerns about air quality in your location, report when you first started to worry about it.
* **Behavior Time** (`8`, `time`, not required): Describe when you started to take actions to avoid air quality issues in your location.
* **Email** (`9`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`10`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Note** (`11`, `note`, not required): Add any additional comments or notes about your air quality awareness.
* **Submission** (`12`, `date`, not required): Report when you submitted this form.
* **Submission Time** (`13`, `date`, not required): Describe when you submitted this form.
* **Submission Date** (`14`, `date`, not required): Report the date of your submission.

**Tips**

* Please answer honestly and accurately to help us understand your perception and behavior related to air quality awareness.
* If you have any concerns or issues, please contact us so we can help.
