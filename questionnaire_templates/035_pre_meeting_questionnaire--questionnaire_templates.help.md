# pre_meeting_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "pre_meeting_questionnaire" form is used to collect information about upcoming meetings. It is recommended to be filled out before a meeting to ensure that all necessary details are documented and discussed. Please fill out this form if you have scheduled a meeting and want to gather relevant information to help facilitate a productive and efficient meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, navigate to the "pre_meeting_questionnaire" form in the application. Select the relevant options and fill in the required fields with the correct information. If you need assistance or clarification on any field, refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Objectives** (`meeting_objectives`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe the primary objectives of the meeting. This field allows you to outline the main goals and outcomes you expect to achieve during the meeting.
* **Meeting Attendance** (`meeting_attendance`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the expected number of attendees for the meeting. This field helps ensure that necessary arrangements are made for sufficient seating and refreshments.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the physical location of the meeting, including the address or a detailed description of the venue.
* **Meeting Start Time** (`meeting_start_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the start time of the meeting in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM). This field is useful for scheduling reminders and notifications.
* **Meeting End Time** (`meeting_end_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the end time of the meeting in 24-hour format (e.g., 15:00 for 3:00 PM). This field helps plan for the duration of the meeting and ensure a timely conclusion.
* **Meeting Duration** (`meeting_duration`, `number`, required: false): Estimate the expected duration of the meeting in minutes. This field aids in planning for the necessary time and resources.
* **Meeting Description** (`meeting_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a more detailed description of the meeting, including any important background information or context. This field helps ensure that all attendees are well-prepared and informed.
