# travel_preferences_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "travel_preferences_questionnaire" form is designed to collect information about your travel preferences. This information will help us understand your habits and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each field according to your travel experiences and preferences.
2. For select fields, choose one of the available options that best suits your preference.
3. For numerical fields, enter a number that corresponds to your preference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Travel Preferences** (`general_travel_preferences`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect any additional information about your general travel preferences. You can type anything you like in this field.
* **Travel Destinations** (`travel_destinations`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all the destinations you usually travel to. You can select multiple options.
* **Travel Frequency** (`travel_frequency`, select_one, required: false): Select the frequency of your travel:
	+ Option 1: Once a week
	+ Option 2: Once a month
	+ Option 3: Rarely
* **Preferred Time of Year** (`preferred_time_of_year`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all the times of the year when you prefer to travel:
	+ Option 1: Spring
	+ Option 2: Summer
	+ Option 3: Winter
* **Preferred Travel Style** (`preferred_travel_style`, select_one, required: false): Select the travel style that suits you best:
	+ Option 1: Adventure
	+ Option 2: Relaxation
	+ Option 3: Culture
* **Budget** (`budget`, number, required: false): Enter the budget for your travels. Please note that this is a numerical field.
* **Duration** (`duration`, select_one, required: false): Select the duration of your travels:
	+ Option 1: Less than 5 days
	+ Option 2: 5-10 days
	+ Option 3: More than 10 days
* **Travel Mode** (`travel_mode`, select_one, required: false): Select the mode of travel you prefer:
	+ Option 1: By car
	+ Option 2: By plane
	+ Option 3: By train
* **Travel Style** (`travel_style`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all the travel styles that suit you:
	+ Option 1: Adventure
	+ Option 2: Relaxation
	+ Option 3: Culture
* **Travel Frequency 2** (`travel_frequency_2`, number, required: false): Enter the frequency of your travels (2). This is a numerical field.
* **Budget 2** (`budget_2`, number, required: false): Enter the budget for your travels (2). This is a numerical field.
* **Duration 2** (`duration_2`, select_one, required: false): Select the duration of your travels (2):
	+ Option 1: Less than 5 days
	+ Option 2: 5-10 days
	+ Option 3: More than 10 days

## Tips
Remember to answer honestly and according to your actual travel preferences. If you have any questions or need further clarification, feel free to ask.
