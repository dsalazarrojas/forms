# Series Analysis Evaluation Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on a series from a teacher or critic who has watched or read it. The goal is to assess the series' analysis, focusing on various aspects such as its development, plot structure, and overall quality.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have watched or read the entire series before filling out this form.
2. Answer each question based on your honest and informed opinion.
3. You may select multiple options for questions that allow multiple selections.
4. Please provide a brief explanation for each question, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Series Title** (`series_title`, text, optional): Please enter the title of the series.
* **Teacher/Reviewer Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as the person filling out this form.
* **Series Characters Development** (`series_characters`, select multiple, optional): Indicate whether the series' characters were well-developed and believable.
	+ 'Yes': The characters were well-developed and believable.
	+ 'No': The characters were not well-developed and believable.
* **Theme Development** (`theme_development`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation of the series' theme.
* **Plot Structure** (`plot_structure`, select one, optional): Indicate how well the series' plot was structured.
	+ Option 1: The plot was well-structured and engaging.
	+ Option 2: The plot was somewhat well-structured, but with some room for improvement.
	+ Option 3: The plot was not well-structured.
* **Characters Development** (`characters_development`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation of the series' character development.
* **Series Plot** (`series_plot`, select multiple, optional): Indicate which types of plot were present in the series.
	+ Option 1: Character development and growth.
	+ Option 2: Plot twist and turns.
	+ Option 3: Multiple plot threads.
* **Reflection** (`reflection`, text, optional): Provide any final reflections or comments about the series.
