# Car Rental System Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Car Rental System Questionnaire is designed to collect feedback and information from customers to help improve the car rental system.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete the form, start by reading each page carefully and follow the instructions provided.
- Fill out each page in order, from 1 to 10.
- Make sure to provide honest and accurate answers to each question.
- For select options, choose the best fitting option for your answer.
- For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you can.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Questionnaire -- (1) Introductory Note**: This is a note to introduce the questionnaire and explain its purpose.
* **How often do you rent vehicles? (2)**: Select the frequency at which you rent vehicles. This helps us understand your rental habits.
* **Primary purpose for renting (3)**: Choose the main reason you rent vehicles. This helps us understand your needs and preferences.
* **Most important factor when renting (4)**: Select all the factors that influence your decision when renting. This helps us understand what matters most to you.
* **Satisfaction with current rental systems (5)**: Rate your satisfaction level with our current rental systems, from 1 to 5. This helps us know how we're doing.
* **Preferred booking method (6)**: Select your preferred way of booking, whether online, via mobile app, telephone hotline, or in-person.
* **Desired new system features (7)**: Check all the features that would improve your experience, such as contactless pickup, real-time vehicle tracking, or 24/7 virtual support.
* **Additional comments or feedback (8)**: Provide any extra details or comments about your experience with our car rental systems.
* **May we contact you for further details? (9)**: Select if you're willing to be contacted for further information.
* **Email Address for follow-up (10)**: If you selected "Yes" for the previous question, provide your email address for follow-up contact.
