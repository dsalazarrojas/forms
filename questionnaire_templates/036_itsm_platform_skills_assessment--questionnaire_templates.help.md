# itsm-platform-skills-assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess your skills and proficiency level in the ITSM (IT Service Management) platform. This form is used to evaluate your expertise and understanding of the platform's features, functionality, and best practices. By completing this form, you will be able to demonstrate your skills and knowledge in areas such as platform proficiency, training, and skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully, providing your best estimate of your skills and proficiency level in the ITSM platform.
2. For questions with multiple choice options, select the option that best describes your current skill level.
3. For open-ended questions, provide a brief explanation in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title (1)**: This is the title of this form, which is used to identify its purpose. Please ignore this field.
* **itsm-platform-proficiency (2)**: Evaluate your proficiency level in the ITSM platform using the scale:
	+ Excellent: You have a deep understanding and strong skills in the platform.
	+ Good: You have a good understanding and some skills in the platform.
	+ Fair: You have some understanding but may need further training or guidance.
* **itsm-platform-proficiency-training (3)**: Select all applicable areas where you have received training in the ITSM platform:
	+ hiring
	+ internal-benchmarking
	+ training
* **itsm-platform-skill (4)**: Provide a brief description of your skills in the ITSM platform. This is a free-text field.
* **Why Do You Need to Complete This Form (5)**: This is an optional field for you to provide a brief explanation for your evaluation. It's not required, but it can help us better understand your context.
* **itsm-platform-evaluation-methods (6)**: This field is not used in this form. Please ignore it.
* **itsm-platform-assessment-date (7)**: Enter the date of your most recent ITSM platform assessment.
* **itsm-platform-assessment-time (8)**: Enter the time of your most recent ITSM platform assessment.
* **itsm-platform-assessment-email (9)**: Enter your email address associated with your ITSM platform account.
* **itsm-platform-skills-assessment-phone (10)**: Provide your phone number associated with your ITSM platform account.
* **itsm-platform-skills-assessment-external-source (11)**: Select where you found this form:
	+ Google
	+ Jotform
	+ Other
* **itsm-platform-skills-external-source-url (12)**: If you selected "Other" in the previous field, enter the URL of the external source where you found this form.
* **itsm-platform-skills-source-author (13)**: If you selected "Other" in the previous field, enter the author of the external source where you found this form.

Note: All fields marked as "required" must be filled out before submitting the form.
