# Toulouse Olfactory Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess an individual's olfactory (smell) abilities and experiences, gathering information that may help healthcare professionals evaluate their olfactory status and provide guidance for further evaluation or actions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form by selecting the relevant options from the provided fields. 
2. Answer each question carefully and thoroughly, as your responses will be used to guide healthcare professionals in their assessment.
3. If you are unsure or have any questions, please consult with a healthcare professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name** (`first_page`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **patient_id** (`patient_id`, `number`, required: false): This is a unique identifier for your patient record. If you don't have one, please leave it blank.
* **visit_date** (`visit_date`, `date`, required: false): The date of your visit or assessment.
* **visit_time** (`visit_time`, `time`, required: false): The time of your visit or assessment.
* **Olfactory Ability** (`olfactory_ability`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the options to describe your current olfactory ability:
	+ Olfactory Ability 1
	+ Olfactory Ability 2
	+ Olfactory Ability 3
	+ Olfactory Ability 4
	+ Olfactory Ability 5
	+ Olfactory Ability 6
	+ Olfactory Ability 7
	+ Olfactory Ability 8
	+ Olfactory Ability 9
	+ Olfactory Ability 10
* **nasal_discharge** (`nasal_discharge`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all that apply to describe your nasal discharge:
	+ Normal
	+ Yellow-green
	+ White-yellow
	+ Yellow-green-white
	+ Yellow
	+ White
	+ Green-yellow
	+ Green
	+ Green-white
* **nasal_smell** (`nasal_smell`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the options to describe your sense of smell:
	+ Strong
	+ Weak
	+ Moderate
	+ None
	+ Pungent
	+ Musty
	+ Sweet
	+ Sour
* **anosmia** (`anosmia`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether you have difficulty smelling:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Unknown/other (please specify)
* **age** (`age`, `number`, required: false): Enter your age.
* **gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your gender:
	+ Male
	+ Female
	+ Other
* **race** (`race`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your race:
	+ White
	+ African or African American
	+ Asian
	+ Latino/Hispanic
	+ Native American
* **height** (`height`, `number`, required: false): Enter your height in inches.
* **weight** (`weight`, `number`, required: false): Enter your weight in pounds.
* **visit_note** (`visit_note`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes from your visit.
* **olfactory_habit** (`olfactory_habit`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select how often you experience olfactory sensations:
	+ Frequent
	+ Occasional
	+ Rare
	+ Never
	+ Always
* **olfactory_pain** (`olfactory_pain`, `number`, required: false): Enter any olfactory pain level you are experiencing.
* **other_olfactory_condition** (`other_olfactory_condition`, `select_one`, required: false): Select any other olfactory condition you may have:
	+ Sinusitis
	+ Allergies
	+ Other (please specify)
* **other_condition_description** (`other_condition_description`, `text`, required: false): Enter a description of your other condition if selected.
* **follow_up** (`follow_up`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether you will follow up with further assessment or action:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Unknown/other (please specify)
* **follow_up_date** (`follow_up_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you will follow up if selected.
* **follow_up_note** (`follow_up_note`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes for your follow up if selected.
