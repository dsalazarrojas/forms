# Gaming Style Personality Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to determine a user's gaming style by asking a series of questions about their preferences and characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that best fit your preferences and characteristics. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply. For text fields, type your answer in the provided space. Be honest and accurate in your responses, as this will help to get a more accurate assessment of your gaming style.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What type of games do you like?** (`gamingistency_quiz_input`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to select all the types of games that you enjoy playing. Please choose from the following options: Fantasy, Action, Strategy, Adventure, Role Playing, Puzzle, Sports, and Simulation.
* **How often do you play games?** (`game_frequency`, select_one, required): This question asks how often you play games. Please select the option that best reflects your gaming frequency.
* **What type of questions do you like?** (`questions_answers`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to select all the types of questions that you prefer. Please choose from the following options: Easy, Medium, Hard, and Complex.
* **Which game style do you like best?** (`game_style_answers_prophets`, select_one, required): This question asks you to select the game style that you prefer. Please choose between Fast-paced, Slow-paced, and Balanced.
* **What kind of games do you play?** (`gameplayment`, select_one, required): This question asks you to select the type of games you play. Please choose between Single-player, Multi-player, and Co-op.
* **What type of gamer are you?** (`gamer_type`, select_one, required): This question asks you to select the type of gamer you are. Please choose between Casual, Serious, and Competitive.
* **Gamer Type 2** (`gamer_type_2`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to select the types of gamers you are. Please choose from the following options: Casual, Serious, and Competitive.
* **What's your gaming experience?** (`gaming_experience`, text, required): This question asks you to describe your gaming experience. Please provide a brief description.
* **What's your name?** (`gamer_name`, text, required): This question asks for your name. Please enter your full name.
* **What's your email?** (`gamer_email`, email, required): This question asks for your email address. Please enter your email address.
* **What's your phone?** (`gamer_phone`, text, optional): This question asks for your phone number. Please enter your phone number if you have one.
* **How old are you?** (`gamer_age`, number, optional): This question asks for your age. Please enter your age if you wish to provide it.
* **What's your gender?** (`gamer_gender`, select_one, optional): This question asks for your gender. Please select from the following options: Male, Female, and Other.
* **What's your occupation?** (`gamer_occupation`, text, optional): This question asks for your occupation. Please enter your occupation if you wish to provide it.
* **What's your education level?** (`gamer_education`, select_one, optional): This question asks for your education level. Please select from the following options: High School, College, University, Bachelor, Master, and PhD.
* **How do you take care of your questions?** (`gamer_questions_answers_2`, select_multiple, optional): This question asks you to select how you take care of your questions. Please choose from the following options: Read instructions carefully, Think critically, Ask for help, Read the manual, Look for help online, and Other.
* **Notes?** (`gamer_notes`, note, required): This question asks you to provide any additional information about yourself.
* **Questions Answers 2** (`questions_answers_2`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to select all the types of questions that you prefer. Please choose from the following options: Fantasy, Action, Strategy, Adventure, Role Playing, Puzzle, Sports, Simulation, Multiplayer, Roleplay, and Survival.
* **Questions Answers 2 1** (`questions_answers_2_1`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to select all the types of questions that you prefer. Please choose from the following options: Yes and No.
* **Questions Answers 2 2** (`questions_answers_2_2`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to select all the types of questions that you prefer. Please choose from the following options: Yes and No.
