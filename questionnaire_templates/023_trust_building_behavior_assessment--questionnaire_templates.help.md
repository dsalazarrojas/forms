# Trust-Building Behavior Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trust-Building Behavior Assessment is a tool used by organizations to evaluate and improve their employees' ability to build trust with their colleagues, customers, and stakeholders. This assessment helps to identify individual's strengths and weaknesses in trust-building behaviors, enabling the organization to provide guidance and support for personal and professional growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Trust-Building Behavior Assessment, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label.
2. Be honest and accurate in your responses.
3. Select the appropriate options for the "Trust Building Score Scale" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it is commonly known.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it is commonly known.
* **Email** (3, email, required/optional): Enter your work email address to enable us to contact you with feedback and results.
* **Organization** (4, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the organization you represent.
* **Job Title** (5, text, required/optional): Enter your job title within the organization.
* **Department** (6, text, required/optional): Enter the department or team you belong to within the organization.
* **Reporting Manager** (7, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your reporting manager within the organization.
* **Direct Manager** (8, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your direct manager within the organization.
* **Years Served** (9, number, required/optional): Enter the number of years you have served in the organization.
* **Trust Building Score** (10, number, required/optional): Enter a number indicating your self-assessment of your trust-building score. Please refer to the "Trust Building Score Scale" below.
* **Trust Building Score Scale** (11, select_multiple, required/optional):
	+ **Low**: I rarely or never display trust-building behaviors.
	+ **Medium**: I sometimes display trust-building behaviors.
	+ **High**: I consistently display trust-building behaviors.
* **Trust Building Comments** (12, text, required/optional): Add any additional comments you believe will help us understand your trust-building behaviors and provide guidance on areas of improvement.

Tips:
- Be accurate and honest in your responses to ensure accurate results.
- Select the option that best represents your self-assessment of your trust-building score.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your manager or HR representative for support.</thinking>
