# Client Branding And Marketing Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your client's branding and marketing needs. By filling out this form, you will be able to clearly communicate your goals, objectives, and requirements to potential partners or service providers in the branding and marketing field. This will help you to find the best fit for your project and get started with your branding and marketing journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reading through each question carefully and making sure you understand what is being asked.
2. For questions that are marked as "required," please ensure you provide an answer.
3. If you're unsure about a question, you can always add additional details in the "Any additional comments" section at the end.
4. Please provide accurate and detailed answers to each question, as this will help potential partners or service providers understand your needs better.
5. Finally, review your answers before submitting the form to ensure everything is complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. -- Branding and Marketing Discovery --
This is an introductory note page, which is meant to be read through before proceeding.

### 2. Company Name
* **Company Name** (`client_company_name`, text, required): This is where you will input your company's name.

### 3. Primary Contact Name
* **Primary Contact Name** (`primary_contact`, text, required): This is where you will input your primary contact person's name.

### 4. -- Project Objectives --
This is a note page that is meant to be read through before proceeding.

### 5. What is your primary goal for this project?
* **What is your primary goal for this project?** (`primary_project_goal`, text, required): This is where you will describe your primary objective for this project. Some examples of goals include:
	+ Brand Refresh
	+ New Product Launch
	+ Increased Lead Gen

### 6. Who is your target audience?
* **Who is your target audience?** (`target_audience`, text, required): Describe the demographics and psychographics of your target audience.

### 7. What are your core brand values? (Top 3)
* **What are your core brand values? (Top 3)** (`brand_values`, text, required): Select the top 3 values that are most important to your brand.

### 8. Which services are you interested in?
* **Which services are you interested in?** (`services_requested`, select multiple, required):
	+ Logo Design & Visual Identity
	+ Brand Voice & Messaging
	+ Social Media Strategy
	+ Content Marketing
	+ SEO/SEM
	+ Email Marketing
	+ Paid Advertising (PPC)

### 9. -- Budget and Timeline --
This is a note page that is meant to be read through before proceeding.

### 10. Estimated Budget Range ($)
* **Estimated Budget Range ($)** (`estimated_budget`, select one, required): Select the estimated budget range for your project.

### 11. Ideal Launch Date
* **Ideal Launch Date** (`desired_launch_date`, date, required): Select the ideal date for the launch of your project.

### 12. List your top 3 competitors
* **List your top 3 competitors** (`competitors_list`, text, optional): If you have competitors, list the top 3 that are most relevant to your project.

### 13. Do you have existing brand assets (Brand Book, Logos)?
* **Do you have existing brand assets (Brand Book, Logos)?** (`existing_assets_check`, select one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Yes, ready to provide
	+ Some, but need updating
	+ None, starting from scratch

### 14. Are you available for a 30-minute discovery call next week?
* **Are you available for a 30-minute discovery call next week?** (`discovery_call_interest`, select one, required):
	+ Yes, please send booking link
	+ Not yet, just send a quote

### 15. Any additional details we should know?
* **Any additional details we should know?** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please add them here.

### 16. Best email for follow-up
* **Best email for follow-up** (`contact_email`, email, required): This is the email address to which you would like to receive further communication about your inquiry.

### 17. -- Thank you for your inquiry --
This is a note page that indicates our strategy team will review your submission and contact you within 2 business days.
