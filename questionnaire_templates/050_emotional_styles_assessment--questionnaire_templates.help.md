# Emotional Styles Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emotional Styles Assessment is a tool used to help individuals better understand their emotional styles, patterns, and levels. It's designed to provide insight into how people perceive and express their emotions, which can be useful for personal growth, team development, and organizational improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question on this form to the best of your ability.
2. Select only one answer for each question, even if it's a multiple-choice option.
3. Don't worry about the length or complexity of your answers; just provide a straightforward response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intro** (`intro`, `note`, required: false): This section is a brief introduction to the form, and it's not required to answer it. You can skip it if you're short on time or not comfortable with writing.
* **Emotional Styles** (`emotional_styles`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose one or more of the following emotional styles that resonate with you:
	+ Emotional Style 1
	+ Emotional Style 2
	+ Emotional Style 3
	+ Emotional Style 4
* **Emotional Pattern** (`emotional_pattern`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the emotional pattern that best describes your typical behavior when faced with challenging situations:
	+ Dominant
	+ Balanced
	+ Deficient
* **Emotional Level** (`emotional_level`, `number`, required: false): Please provide a numerical value for your emotional level. If you're not comfortable providing a number, you can skip this field.
* **Emotional Mood** (`emotional_mood`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the emotional mood that best describes your general state of mind:
	+ Happy
	+ Sad
	+ Calm
	+ Angry
* **Dominant Mood** (`dominant_mood`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the mood that best describes your dominant emotional state:
	+ Happy
	+ Sad
	+ Calm
	+ Angry
* **Balanced Mood** (`balanced_mood`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the mood that best describes your balanced emotional state:
	+ Happy
	+ Sad
	+ Calm
	+ Angry
* **Deficient Mood** (`deficient_mood`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the mood that best describes your deficient emotional state:
	+ Happy
	+ Sad
	+ Calm
	+ Angry
* **Total Emotional Level** (`total_emotional_level`, `number`, required: false): Please provide a numerical value for your overall emotional level. If you're not comfortable providing a number, you can skip this field.
* **Emotional Style Score** (`emotional_style_score`, `number`, required: false): This field will display your calculated emotional style score. Please note that it's not editable and is for your information only.
* **Emotional Pattern Score** (`emotional_pattern_score`, `number`, required: false): This field will display your calculated emotional pattern score. Like the previous field, it's for your information only.
* **Emotional Level Score** (`emotional_level_score`, `number`, required: false): This field will display your calculated emotional level score. Again, it's for your information only.
