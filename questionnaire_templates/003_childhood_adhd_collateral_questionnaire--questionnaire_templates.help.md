# Childhood ADHD Collateral Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Childhood ADHD Collateral Questionnaire is a tool to gather information about a child's behavior and performance from a teacher, coach, or caregiver who knows the child well. This form is used to help identify potential signs of attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) in a child. It is not intended for diagnosis, but rather as a tool to collect observations and insights from those who know the child best.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions based on your observations and experiences with the child.
2. For questions with "select_one" options, choose the option that best describes the child's behavior or performance.
3. For questions with "text" options, provide a brief description of the child's behavior or performance.
4. Please answer all questions truthfully, and do not hesitate to ask if you have any concerns or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child Name**: Please enter the child's full name.
* **Observer Name**: Please enter your name and title (e.g., teacher, coach, or caregiver).
* **Relationship to Child**: Please describe your relationship to the child (e.g., 2nd Grade Teacher, Soccer Coach).
* **How long have you known this child?**: Please enter the length of time you have known the child.
* **Often fails to give close attention to details or makes careless mistakes**: Choose the option that best describes the child's behavior.
* **Often has difficulty sustaining attention in tasks or play activities**: Choose the option that best describes the child's behavior.
* **Often does not seem to listen when spoken to directly**: Choose the option that best describes the child's behavior.
* **Often fidgets with hands or feet or squirms in seat**: Choose the option that best describes the child's behavior.
* **Often leaves seat in situations where remaining seated is expected**: Choose the option that best describes the child's behavior.
* **Often talks excessively**: Choose the option that best describes the child's behavior.
* **Describe the child's social interactions with peers**: Please provide a brief description of the child's social interactions with peers.
* **Describe the child's current academic performance**: Please provide a brief description of the child's current academic performance.
* **What are the child's greatest strengths?**: Please describe the child's greatest strengths.
* **Are there any other concerns you would like to mention?**: Please describe any additional concerns you have about the child.
* **Date of Report**: Please enter the date you are completing this form.

Note: This form is for internal use only and is not intended for public distribution. The information collected will be used to support the child's care and development.
