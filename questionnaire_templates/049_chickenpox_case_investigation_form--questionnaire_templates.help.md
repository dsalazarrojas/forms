# Chickenpox Case Investigation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document a case of chickenpox (varicella) investigation, gathering information about the patient's symptoms, medical history, and exposure to the virus.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in all required fields (marked as "required").
2. Be as accurate and thorough as possible when providing information.
3. Review each field for any additional comments or clarifications needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Patient Name** (Patient Name, text, required)

* Enter the full name of the patient.

### 2. **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required)

* Enter the patient's date of birth in the correct format (e.g., 1990-01-01).

### 3. **Age** (Age, number, required)

* Enter the patient's age.

### 4. **Gender** (Gender, select_one, required)

* Select the patient's gender.
* Options: Male, Female, Non-binary, Prefer not to say

### 5. **Address** (Address, text, required)

* Enter the patient's full address.

### 6. **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required)

* Enter the patient's contact number.

### 7. **Email Address** (Email Address, email, optional)

* Enter the patient's email address (if applicable).

### 8. **Investigation Date** (Investigation Date, date, required)

* Enter the date of investigation.

### 9. **Investigator Name** (Investigator Name, text, required)

* Enter the name of the healthcare investigator.

### 10. **Case ID Number** (Case ID Number, text, required)

* Enter a unique case identifier.

### 11. **Symptom Onset Date** (Symptom Onset Date, date, required)

* Enter the date when symptoms first appeared.

### 12. **Rash Onset Date** (Rash Onset Date, date, required)

* Enter the date when the rash appeared.

### 13. **Symptoms** (Symptoms, select_multiple, required)

* Select all symptoms present in the patient.
* Options: Fever, Rash, Headache, Fatigue, Loss of appetite, Sore throat, Cough, Runny nose, Muscle aches

### 14. **Rash Distribution** (Rash Distribution, select_multiple, required)

* Select all areas where the rash is located.
* Options: Face, Scalp, Trunk, Arms, Legs, Mouth, Genital area, Widespread

### 15. **Approximate Lesion Count** (Approximate Lesion Count, select_one, required)

* Select the approximate number of lesions.
* Options: Less than 50, 50-100, 101-300, 301-500, More than 500

### 16. **Lesion Stages Present** (Lesion Stages Present, select_multiple, required)

* Select all stages of lesions visible.
* Options: Macules, Papules, Vesicles, Pustules, Crusts

### 17. **Fever Present** (Fever Present, select_one, required)

* Select if the patient had fever.
* Options: True, False

### 18. **Highest Temperature** (Highest Temperature, number, optional)

* Enter the highest recorded temperature (if applicable).

### 19. **Varicella Vaccination Status** (Varicella Vaccination Status, select_one, required)

* Select the patient's vaccination status.
* Options: Yes, 2 doses, Yes, 1 dose, No, never vaccinated, Unknown

### 20. **Vaccination Dates** (Vaccination Dates, text, optional)

* Enter the dates of vaccinations (if applicable).

### 21. **Previous Chickenpox Infection** (Previous Chickenpox Infection, select_one, required)

* Select if the patient had chickenpox before.
* Options: True, False, Unknown

### 22. **Underlying Medical Conditions** (Underlying Medical Conditions, text, optional)

* List any chronic medical conditions.

### 23. **Immunocompromised Status** (Immunocompromised Status, select_one, required)

* Select if the patient is immunocompromised.
* Options: True, False, Unknown

### 24. **Current Medications** (Current Medications, text, optional)

* List the patient's current medications.

### 25. **Pregnancy Status** (Pregnancy Status, select_one, optional)

* Select the patient's pregnancy status.
* Options: True, False, Not applicable, Unknown

### 26. **Healthcare Visit** (Healthcare Visit, select_one, required)

* Select if the patient saw a healthcare provider.
* Options: True, False

### 27. **Healthcare Visit Date** (Healthcare Visit Date, date, optional)

* Enter the date of the healthcare visit (if applicable).

### 28. **Healthcare Provider Name** (Healthcare Provider Name, text, optional)

* Enter the healthcare provider's name (if applicable).

### 29. **Lab Testing Performed** (Lab Testing Performed, select_one, required)

* Select if lab testing was done.
* Options: Yes, PCR, Yes, serology, Yes, culture, False, Pending

### 30. **Lab Results** (Lab Results, text, optional)

* Enter the lab testing results.

### 31. **Known Exposure Source** (Known Exposure Source, text, optional)

* Enter where the patient was exposed to the virus (if applicable).

### 32. **Exposure Date** (Exposure Date, date, optional)

* Enter the date when the patient was exposed (if applicable).

### 33. **Number of Contacts Identified** (Number of Contacts Identified, number, required)

* Enter the number of close contacts.

### 34. **High-Risk Contacts** (High-Risk Contacts, number, optional)

* Enter the number of high-risk contacts (if applicable).

### 35. **Isolation Status** (Isolation Status, select_one, required)

* Select if the patient is in isolation.
* Options: Yes, at home, Yes, hospital, False, Not compliant

### 36. **Work or School Status** (Work or School Status, select_one, required)

* Select if the patient is missing work or school.
* Options: Yes, excluded, Yes, self-isolating, No, still attending, Not applicable

### 37. **Complications** (Complications, select_multiple, required)

* Select any complications present.
* Options: None, Bacterial superinfection, Pneumonia, Encephalitis, Dehydration, Other

### 38. **Case Outcome** (Case Outcome, select_one, required)

* Select the current status of the case.
* Options: Recovered, Recovering, Hospitalized, Fatal, Unknown

### 39. **Hospitalization Required** (Hospitalization Required, select_one, required)

* Select if hospitalization was needed.
* Options: True, False

### 40. **Investigator Signature** (Investigator Signature, select_one, required)

* Select to confirm that the investigation is complete.
* Options: Yes, confirmed, False

### 41. **Investigation Complete Date** (Investigation Complete Date, date, required)

* Enter the date the investigation was completed.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form.
* Review each field for any additional comments or clarifications needed.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
