# Adult Personal Analysis Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be a comprehensive assessment tool for analyzing an individual's personality, behavior, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and labels to understand what each field is asking.
2. Answer the questions honestly, and select the options that best fit your personality and behavior.
3. Be as specific as possible with your answers.
4. Review your responses to ensure they are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Self-Assessment (Page 1)

* **Social Engagement Level** (`social_engagement_level`, Select One, Required): This question asks how you typically recharge. Please select the option that best describes your behavior.
	+ Highly Extroverted: You often seek social interaction and enjoy being around people.
	+ Mostly Extroverted: You enjoy social interaction, but also value alone time.
	+ Balanced: You have a mix of both social and alone time.
	+ Mostly Introverted: You prefer to be alone and avoid social interaction.
	+ Highly Introverted: You tend to avoid social interaction and prefer to be alone.
* **Stress Management Style** (`stress_management_style`, Select One, Required): This question asks about your primary reaction to difficult situations. Please select the option that best describes your behavior.
	+ Task-oriented: You focus on solving the problem.
	+ Emotion-oriented: You focus on how you feel about the situation.
	+ Avoidance-oriented: You tend to avoid the situation.
	+ Collaborative: You work with others to find a solution.
* **Decision Making Preference** (`decision_making_preference`, Select One, Required): This question asks about your preference for decision-making. Please select the option that best describes your behavior.
	+ Logic-based: You make decisions based on facts and data.
	+ Value-based: You make decisions based on your values and principles.
	+ Intuition-based: You make decisions based on your intuition.
	+ Group-consensus: You make decisions with others.
* **Openness to New Experiences** (`openness_to_new_experiences`, Select One, Required): This question asks about your willingness to try new things. Please select the option that best describes your behavior.
	+ Very Open: You are always willing to try new things.
	+ Somewhat Open: You are somewhat willing to try new things.
	+ Neutral: You are neutral about trying new things.
	+ Resistant: You tend to resist trying new things.

### Contrast with Others (Page 2)

* **Compared to Peers I am** (`compared_to_peers_i_am`, Select One, Required): This question asks how you perceive yourself in relation to your peers, in terms of ambition. Please select the option that best describes your behavior.
	+ More ambitious: You are more ambitious than your peers.
	+ Similarly ambitious: You are as ambitious as your peers.
	+ Less ambitious: You are less ambitious than your peers.
* **Compared to Peers I am Emotional** (`compared_to_peers_i_am_emotional`, Select One, Required): This question asks how you perceive yourself in relation to your peers, in terms of emotional levels. Please select the option that best describes your behavior.
	+ More emotional: You are more emotional than your peers.
	+ Similarly emotional: You are as emotional as your peers.
	+ Less emotional: You are less emotional than your peers.
* **Perception of Work Ethic** (`perception_of_work_ethic`, Select One, Required): This question asks about your perception of work ethic. Please select the option that best describes your behavior.
	+ Hard worker: You work hard and strive to meet deadlines.
	+ Average: You work hard, but have some flexibility.
	+ Easy-going: You have a relaxed attitude towards work.

### Behavioral Patterns (Page 3)

* **Core Values** (`core_values`, Select Multiple, Required): Please select up to 3 values that define you.
	+ Integrity: You value honesty and integrity.
	+ Innovation: You value creativity and innovation.
	+ Compassion: You value kindness and compassion.
	+ Stability: You value stability and reliability.
	+ Achievement: You value achieving goals and success.
* **Description of Ideal Environment** (`description_of_ideal_environment`, Text, Required): This question asks about your ideal work environment. Please describe the place where you feel most productive and comfortable.
* **Areas of Personal Growth Interest** (`areas_of_personal_growth_interest`, Text, Required): This question asks about areas where you would like to improve about yourself. Please describe your interests.

Note: Each field is optional unless marked as Required.
