# Loop Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Loop Selection Survey is a questionnaire designed to gather information about your preferences and experiences related to loops. It is a tool to help you share your opinions and insights on the topic of loop selection. Your responses will help to identify patterns and trends in how loops are perceived and utilized.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand each question and its purpose.
2. Read each question carefully and answer truthfully to the best of your ability.
3. Fill in all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*). Note that all fields in this form are optional.
4. If you have any trouble understanding a question, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Loop Selection Survey (Page 1)**
	+ <label>Loop Selection Survey</label> (`title`, `text`, optional): This is the introduction page of the survey.
* **This is a test loop (Page 2)**
	+ <label>This is a test loop</label> (`intro2`, `text`, optional): This field is intended to provide a brief description of the purpose or context of the survey.
* **project_name (Page 3)**
	+ <label>project_name</label> (`project_name`, `text`, optional): This field allows you to specify the name of your project related to the loop selection topic.
* **purpose (Page 4)**
	+ <label>purpose</label> (`loop_purpose`, `text`, optional): This field is designed to understand the primary purpose or objective of your project or experience with loops.
* **Ease of Use (Page 5)**
	+ <label>ease_of_use</label> (`ease_of_use`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks you to rate the ease of use of the loop. Choose from the following options:
		- Easy
		- Moderate
		- Difficult
* **Challenges (Page 6)**
	+ <label>challenges</label> (`challenges`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks you to select all the challenges you have faced with the loop. Choose from the following options:
		- Easy
		- Moderate
		- Difficult
* **comments (Page 7)**
	+ <label>comments</label> (`comments`, `text`, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional comments or thoughts about your experience with loops.
	+ **Note**: Since all fields are optional, you can skip any questions that do not apply to you or your experience.

## Tips
- Please answer truthfully and accurately to provide valuable insights.
- If you need help with understanding a question or field, refer back to this help guide.
- You can skip any question that does not apply to you or your experience.
- The more detailed and honest your responses, the more informative and valuable they will be for others who may use this data.
