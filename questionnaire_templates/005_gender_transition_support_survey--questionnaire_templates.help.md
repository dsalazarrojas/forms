# Gender Transition Support Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals about their experiences and feelings regarding their gender transition, including their age, gender identity, transition process, support services, and satisfaction with those services. This information will help the organization providing these services to better understand the needs and concerns of its clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. For questions that require a selection (e.g., select_one, select_multiple), choose the option that best represents your experience or feelings.
3. For questions that allow for free text responses (e.g., text), provide a clear and concise explanation of your thoughts and feelings.
4. Make sure to answer all required questions, as indicated by the asterisk (*) symbol.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What Is Your Age?** (*required*): This question is to collect your age. Please enter your age in the provided text field.
* **How Do You Identify?** (*select_one*): This question is to collect information about your gender identity. Choose the option that best represents your identity:
	+ Male
	+ Non-Binary
	+ Other
	+ Trans Female
	+ Trans Male
* **When Did You Begin Your Gender Transition?** (*date*): This question is to collect the date you started your transition process. Please enter the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Do You Have A Plan For Transition?** (*select_multiple*): This question is to collect information about your transition plan. Choose the options that best represent your plan:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Unsure
	+ Not applicable
* **Transition** (*required*): This question is to collect information about your transition progress. Please enter the number that best represents your progress.
* **How Did You Feel About Your Transition?** (*note*): This question is for any additional comments or feelings you'd like to share about your transition.
* **What Type Of Support Services Do You Find Useful?** (*select_one*): This question is to collect information about the type of support services you find useful. Choose the option that best represents your needs:
	+ Counseling/Therapy
	+ Medical
	+ Support Groups
	+ Online
* **Please Explain The Type Of Support Services You Find Useful?** (*text*): This question is for any additional comments or information about the type of support services you find useful.
* **How Often Do You Attend Support Services?** (*select_multiple*): This question is to collect information about the frequency of your attendance to support services. Choose the options that best represent your attendance:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **Please Explain The Frequency Of Your Attendance To Support Services?** (*text*): This question is for any additional comments or information about your attendance to support services.
* **How Satisfied Are You With Support Services?** (*select_multiple*): This question is to collect information about your satisfaction with support services. Choose the options that best represent your satisfaction:
	+ Very Unsatisfactory
	+ Unsatisfactory
	+ Neutral
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Very Satisfactory
* **Please Explain Your Satisfaction With Support Services?** (*text*): This question is for any additional comments or information about your satisfaction with support services.
* **Gender Identity Support Services Satisfaction Providers?** (*text*): This question is for any additional comments or information about your satisfaction with support services providers.
* **Suggestions For Improving Support Services?** (*text*): This question is for any suggestions or ideas you have to improve the support services provided.
