# Student Loan Assistance Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Loan Assistance Intake Form is a tool used to collect information from students who are seeking assistance with their student loans. This form is designed to gather essential details about the student's loan status, employment status, income level, and other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your student loan details, including your loan status (e.g., Delinquent, Deferment, Deferment Forgiveness, or Default).
2. Enter your school name and address.
3. Select your current employment status (e.g., Employed or Not Employed).
4. Choose your income level (e.g., Less than 20,000, 20,000 - 40,000, 40,000 - 60,000, or Greater than 60,000).
5. Enter your monthly and annual net income.
6. (Optional) Enter your monthly and annual family income.
7. Enter phone numbers for all borrowers (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Student Loan Details
* **Student Loan Borrower**: Enter the name of the student loan borrower.
	+ This is the name of the person who owns the student loan.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school attended.
	+ This is the name of the institution where the loan was obtained.
* **School Address**: Enter the address of the school attended.
	+ This is the address of the institution where the loan was obtained.
* **Loan Status**: Choose the current status of your loan (e.g., Delinquent, Deferment, Deferment Forgiveness, or Default).
	+ This indicates the current state of your loan.
* **Current Employment Status**: Select your current employment status (e.g., Employed or Not Employed).
	+ This indicates your current employment status.
* **Income Level**: Choose your income level (e.g., Less than 20,000, 20,000 - 40,000, 40,000 - 60,000, or Greater than 60,000).
	+ This indicates your income level.
* **Monthly Net Income**: Enter your monthly net income.
	+ This is your monthly income after taxes.
* **Annual Income**: Enter your annual income.
	+ This is your annual income.
* **Annual Family Income**: (Optional) Enter your annual family income.
	+ This is the annual income of your family.
* **Monthly Family Income**: (Optional) Enter your monthly family income.
	+ This is the monthly income of your family.
* **Borrower 1 Phone**: Enter the phone number of borrower 1.
	+ This is the phone number of the first borrower.
* **Borrower 2 Phone**: Enter the phone number of borrower 2.
	+ This is the phone number of the second borrower.
* **Borrower 3 Phone**: Enter the phone number of borrower 3.
	+ This is the phone number of the third borrower.
* **Borrower 4 Phone**: Enter the phone number of borrower 4.
	+ This is the phone number of the fourth borrower.
* **Borrower 5 Phone**: Enter the phone number of borrower 5.
	+ This is the phone number of the fifth borrower.
