# Publishing Experience Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with our publishing process. The goal is to understand your perspective and identify areas where we can improve to better serve you and our other users.

## How To Complete This Form
Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly, and provide as much detail as you can about your experience. The form is divided into sections with different questions, which are designed to gather specific information from you. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. What is your role in the publishing process?**: This question helps us understand your position within the publishing process. Please select the option that best describes your role.
* **2. How would you rate the clarity of our process?**: This question measures your perception of how clear and easy to follow our publishing process is. Rate your experience on a scale from 1-5, where 1 is unclear and 5 is very clear.
* **3. What do you use to communicate with us?**: This question is to understand how you prefer to communicate with us. Select all methods that apply to you.
* **4. How many times have you interacted with us in the last 6 months?**: This question helps us understand your frequency of interactions with us.
* **5. What do you think sets us apart from other publishers?**: This question is for any positive feedback or unique experiences you've had with us.
* **6. Would you use us again?**: This question gauges your likelihood of returning to our service.
* **7. What do you think is missing from our process?**: This question is to identify areas where we can improve.
* **8. How would you rate your overall experience with us?**: This question measures your overall satisfaction with our service.
* **9. What could we improve on?**: This question is for specific areas where you feel we can improve.
* **10. Do you have any further feedback for us?**: This question is for any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **11. What is your preferred method of contact?**: This question is to understand how you would like to be contacted in the future.
* **12. How would you rate our customer support?**: This question measures your satisfaction with our customer support.
