# Practice Launch Pre Consult Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Practice Launch Pre Consult Questionnaire is designed to collect information from patients before a practice launch consultation. This form will help you understand their goals, objectives, and other essential details to provide personalized care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, ensure you have all the necessary information and data required to complete the form. Review each field carefully and fill it out accurately.
2. Start with the pre-session details section (Page 1), where you'll be asked to provide information about the patient's experiences before the practice launch.
3. Move on to the goals and objectives section (Page 2) where you'll be asked to specify the patient's aims and objectives.
4. In the launch pre-consult question section (Page 3), please provide any additional questions or concerns you have for the patient.
5. Next, provide the follow-up details (Page 4), where you'll schedule and plan the follow-up meetings.
6. Select the launch date and time for the practice launch on Page 5.
7. Choose the launch status (Page 6) of the patient, from the options provided: Active, Inactive, or Unknown.
8. Finally, provide the launch consultant details on Page 7.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pre session details** (Page 1, Pre session details): This field is used to collect information about the patient's experiences before the practice launch.
* **Goals and objectives** (Page 2, Goals and objectives): This field is used to specify the patient's aims and objectives.
* **Launch pre-consult question** (Page 3, Launch pre-consult question): This field is used to ask any additional questions or concerns you have for the patient.
* **Follow up details** (Page 4, Follow up details): This field is used to schedule and plan the follow-up meetings.
* **Launch date** (Page 5, Launch date): This field is used to select the launch date for the practice launch.
* **Launch time** (Page 5, Launch time): This field is used to select the launch time for the practice launch.
* **Launch status** (Page 6, Launch status): This field is used to choose the launch status of the patient, from the options provided: Active, Inactive, or Unknown.
* **Launch consultant details** (Page 7, Launch consultant details): This field is used to provide any additional details about the launch consultant.
