# pre_wedding_videography_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "pre_wedding_videography_questionnaire" is a form designed to collect information from couples about their upcoming wedding videography needs. This form helps videographers and photographers understand the couple's expectations and requirements for their special day.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your personal details and preferences.
2. Be as detailed as possible in your responses.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Couple Information**: Enter your names, contact information, and any relevant details about your wedding.
* **Wedding Details**: Describe your wedding date, venue, and any specific requests or requirements you have.
* **Venue and Time**: Provide the location and time of your wedding ceremony and reception.
* **Photo Requirements**: Specify any photo requirements you have, such as number of photos, poses, and styles.
* **Video Requirements**: Describe your video requirements, including length, format, and any specific scenes or shots you want captured.
* **Equipment List**: List any equipment you have or would like to use for the video production.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Special Instructions**: Provide any special instructions or requests you have for the videographer or photographer.
* **Payment Information**: Enter any payment information or details about how you will be paying for the services. 

This form is meant to help couples communicate their needs and expectations to videographers and photographers. It is not meant to be used by service providers to fill out their own information.
