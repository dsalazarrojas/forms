# Patient Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Satisfaction Questionnaire is a tool used to collect feedback from patients about their overall satisfaction with their healthcare experience. It helps us to understand their perception of the care they received and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review each page carefully and ensure you are completing the form for the correct purpose.
* Answer all the questions honestly and thoroughly.
* Use the provided options for select fields, or type your answer in the text fields.
* Do not skip any questions, but you can leave a question blank if you do not have an answer.
* Sign and date the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`1`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the healthcare experience. Select Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Care Team** (`2`): Please select all the care team members you interacted with during your visit. Options: Nurse Practitioner, Nurse, Physician, Other (please specify).
* **Wait Time** (`3`): Enter the amount of time you had to wait before your visit or procedure.
* **Communication** (`4`): Describe how you felt about the communication from the healthcare team during your visit.
* **Pain Management** (`5`): Rate your experience with pain management during your visit. Select Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Staff Attitude** (`6`): Rate the attitude of the healthcare staff during your visit. Select Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Facilities** (`7`): Please select all the facilities you used during your visit. Options: Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Care Plan** (`8`): Please select all the care plans you received during your visit. Options: Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Overall Experience** (`9`): Describe your overall experience with the healthcare provider.
* **Patient Suggestion** (`10`): Share any suggestions you have for improving the healthcare experience.
* **Staff Suggestion** (`11`): Share any suggestions you have for improving the healthcare team's performance.
* **Additional Comments** (`12`): Share any additional comments about your experience.
* **Patient Signature** (`13`): Sign your name to confirm completion of the form.
* **Staff Signature** (`14`): Please sign your name as the care provider.
* **Care Provider** (`15`): Enter the name of the care provider who treated you.
* **Date** (`16`): Enter the date of your visit.
* **Time** (`17`): Enter the time of your visit.
* **Contact Number** (`18`): Enter your contact information for follow-up.
* **Email** (`19`): Enter your email address for follow-up.
* **Address** (`20`): Enter your address for follow-up.
* **How did you feel about this experience?** (`21`): Share your thoughts about the experience.
* **How likely are you to recommend this healthcare provider to others?** (`22`): Share how likely you are to recommend the healthcare provider.
* **What could be improved about this experience?** (`23`): Share suggestions for improvement.
* **Is there anything else you would like to share about your experience?** (`24`): Share any additional comments or thoughts.
* **Do you have any other comments about your experience?** (`25`): Share any additional comments or thoughts.
