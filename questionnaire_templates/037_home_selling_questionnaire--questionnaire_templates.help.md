# Home Selling Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a home's characteristics and features, helping you to effectively list and sell your property. It is essential to answer all questions accurately to ensure that your home's listing is accurate and appealing to potential buyers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your home's location in the "Home Location" field.
2. Provide the year the home was built in the "Year Built" field.
3. Enter the square footage of the home in the "Square Footage" field.
4. Describe the lot size in the "Lot Size" field.
5. Select the type of home you're selling (e.g., single-family home, condominium, townhouse) in the "Home Type" field.
6. Enter the number of bedrooms and bathrooms in the "Number of Bedrooms" and "Number of Bathrooms" fields, respectively.
7. Check any fireplaces in the "Number of Fireplaces" field.
8. Select the type(s) of pool you have (if any) in the "Pool" field.
9. Specify the type(s) of parking available in the "Parking Type" field.
10. Describe the lot's features in the "Lot Description" field.
11. Choose the neighborhood where the home is located in the "Neighborhood" field.
12. Select the school district(s) that serve the area in the "School District" field.
13. Enter the zoning information in the "Zoning" field.
14. Select the HOA (Homeowners Association) type (if any) in the "HOA" field.
15. Describe any notable features of the lot in the "Lot Features" field.
16. Enter the year the property was rented (if applicable) in the "Year Rented" field.
17. Enter the year the property was sold (if applicable) in the "Year Sold" field.
18. Enter the sale price of the property in the "Sale Price" field.
19. Enter the listing price of the property in the "Listing Price" field.
20. Choose the type of property (e.g., residential, commercial) in the "Property Type" field.
21. Select the use of the property (e.g., single-family home, condominium) in the "Property Use" field.
22. Enter the property's address in the "Address" field.
23. Enter the city where the home is located in the "City" field.
24. Enter the state where the home is located in the "State" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Location** (`home_location`, text, required): Enter the exact location of the home, including street name, city, and state.
* **Year Built** (`year_built`, number, required): Enter the year the home was built.
* **Square Footage** (`square_footage`, number, required): Enter the total square footage of the home.
* **Lot Size** (`lot_size`, text, required): Describe the size of the lot where the home is located.
* **Home Type** (`home_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of home you're selling (e.g., single-family home, condominium, townhouse).
* **Number of Bedrooms** (`number_of_bedrooms`, number, required): Enter the number of bedrooms in the home.
* **Number of Bathrooms** (`number_of_bathrooms`, number, required): Enter the number of bathrooms in the home.
* **Number of Fireplaces** (`number_of_fireplaces`, number, required): Check if the home has a fireplace.
* **Pool** (`pool`, select_multiple, required): Select the type(s) of pool you have (if any).
* **Parking Type** (`parking_type`, text, required): Specify the type(s) of parking available in the home.
* **Lot Description** (`lot_description`, text, required): Describe any notable features of the lot.
* **Neighborhood** (`neighborhood`, text, required): Choose the neighborhood where the home is located.
* **School District** (`school_district`, select_multiple, required): Select the school district that serves the area.
* **Zoning** (`zoning`, text, required): Enter the zoning information for the property.
* **HOA** (`hoa`, select_multiple, required): Select the type(s) of HOA (if any).
* **Lot Features** (`lot_features`, text, required): Describe any notable features of the lot.
* **Year Rented** (`year_rental`, date, optional): Enter the year the property was rented (if applicable).
* **Year Sold** (`year_sold`, date, optional): Enter the year the property was sold (if applicable).
* **Sale Price** (`sale_price`, number, optional): Enter the sale price of the property.
* **Listing Price** (`listing_price`, number, optional): Enter the listing price of the property.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of property (e.g., residential, commercial).
* **Property Use** (`property_use`, select_multiple, optional): Select the use of the property (e.g., single-family home, condominium).
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the property's address.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where the home is located.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state where the home is located.

Note: The fields marked as `required` are mandatory fields that must be filled in to complete the form.
