# Event Planning Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Planning Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information about an event to help in its planning and organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Click on the "event_planning_questionnaire" field and enter the title of the event.
- Select the type of event from the provided options (e.g., Conference, Wedding, Meeting).
- In the "location" field, enter the location of the event.
- Enter the date of the event using the format mm/dd/yyyy or dd/mm/yyyy, depending on your regional date format.
- Select the time of the event from the available options.
- Enter the expected number of attendees for the event.
- Enter the budget allocated for the event.
- Provide a brief description of the event in the "event_description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### event_planning_questionnaire (Text)
**Title of the Event**: Enter the title of the event, which will help in identifying and organizing the event.

### event_type (Select One)
**Type of Event**: Select the type of event (e.g., Conference, Wedding, Meeting) to help in planning and organization.

### location (Text)
**Location of the Event**: Enter the location of the event, which will aid in arranging the venue and other logistical requirements.

### date (Date)
**Date of the Event**: Enter the date of the event using the format mm/dd/yyyy or dd/mm/yyyy, depending on your regional date format.

### time (Time)
**Time of the Event**: Select the time of the event from the available options.

### attendees (Number)
**Number of Attendees**: Enter the expected number of attendees for the event.

### event_budget (Number)
**Event Budget**: Enter the budget allocated for the event.

### event_description (Text)
**Event Description**: Provide a brief description of the event, including any relevant details that will help in planning and execution.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out completely before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to the provided options or consult with the relevant team member for clarification.
- The form is intended to be used for general purposes and may not cover all specific event planning scenarios. If you have specific requirements, please contact the event planning team for additional support.
