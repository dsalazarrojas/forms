# market_driver_analysis_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The market_driver_analysis_survey form is used to gather information about the market driver, its goals, characteristics, and impact on the customer's purchasing decision. It is designed to be completed by market researchers and analysts to collect data from customers or stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and understand the questions asked.
2. Select the correct options for each question.
3. Provide clear and concise answers for each field.
4. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are the goals of the driver?** (`driver_goals`, select_multiple, required): This field asks you to select all the goals that apply to the market driver.
	+ Select all goals that are relevant to the market driver.
* **What is the priority of the driver's impact on the customer's purchasing decision?** (`priority`, select_one, required): This field asks you to select the priority of the driver's impact.
	+ Choose the correct priority from the options provided (Very High, High, Medium, Low).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): This field is for any additional information you would like to provide about the market driver.
	+ Keep your answer clear and concise.
* **What is the customer's email address?** (`email`, email, optional): This field asks for the customer's email address.
	+ Enter the customer's email address if available.
* **What is the customer's phone number?** (`phone`, text, optional): This field asks for the customer's phone number.
	+ Enter the customer's phone number if available.
* **What are the characteristics of the driver?** (`driver_characteristics`, select_multiple, required): This field asks you to select all the characteristics that apply to the market driver.
	+ Select all characteristics that are relevant to the market driver.
* **What are the drivers of the market?** (`market_drivers`, select_multiple, required): This field asks you to select all the drivers of the market.
	+ Select all drivers that are relevant to the market.
* **What is the driver's impact on the customer's purchasing decision?** (`driver_impact`, select_multiple, required): This field asks you to select all the impacts that apply to the market driver.
	+ Select all impacts that are relevant to the market driver.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes_1`, note, optional): This field is for any additional information you would like to provide about the market driver.
	+ Keep your answer clear and concise.
* **More Notes** (`notes_2`, note, optional): This field is for any further additional information you would like to provide about the market driver.
	+ Keep your answer clear and concise.
