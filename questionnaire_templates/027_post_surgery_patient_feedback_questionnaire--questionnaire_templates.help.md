# Post Surgery Patient Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from patients on their experience after surgery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions based on your personal experience. You may fill out this form at any time after your surgery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: This is the field where you enter your name. Please provide your full name as it appears on your medical records.
* **Surgery Date**: This is the field where you enter the date of your surgery. Please use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Type of Surgery**: Select the type of surgery you underwent.
* **Pain Level**: This field asks about your pain level after surgery. Please enter a number between 1 and 10, with 1 being "Pain Free" and 10 being "Extreme Pain".
* **Overall Satisfaction**: This field asks about your overall satisfaction with your care. Please select one of the options below:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Communication with Doctor**: This field asks about your experience with your doctor's communication. Please select one of the options below:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Neutral
	+ Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **Doctor's Attentiveness**: This field asks about your doctor's attentiveness to your needs. Please enter a number between 1 and 10, with 1 being "Very Poor" and 10 being "Excellent".
* **Nurse Care**: This field asks about your experience with the care provided by the nurses. Please select one of the options below:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Neutral
	+ Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **Follow-Up Care**: This field asks about the follow-up care you received after surgery. Please select one of the options below:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Neutral
	+ Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **Would you Recommend**: This field asks if you would recommend this hospital to others. Please select "True" or "False".
* **Additional Comments**: This is an optional field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Contact Info**: This field allows you to provide your contact information, such as email, phone number, or mail address. Please select all that apply.
