# Academic Status Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Academic Status Survey is designed to collect student feedback on academic programs. It aims to gather information on students' current enrollment experience, satisfaction, and plans after graduation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by providing your student name in the "Student full name" field.
2. Enter your official student email address in the "Email address" field.
3. Enter your student ID number in the "Student ID number" field.
4. Select your current enrollment status from the drop-down menu in the "Current enrollment status" field.
5. Select your current academic level from the drop-down menu in the "Current academic level" field.
6. Enter the name of your program or major in the "Program or major" field.
7. If you are pursuing a minor or concentration, enter its name in the "Minor or concentration (if applicable)" field.
8. Enter your expected graduation date in the "Expected graduation date" field.
9. Select your current academic standing from the drop-down menu in the "Current academic standing" field.
10. Enter the approximate number of credits you have completed in the "Approximate number of credits completed" field.
11. Rate your satisfaction with your academic program in the "How satisfied are you with your academic program" field.
12. Rate your satisfaction with academic advising in the "How satisfied are you with academic advising" field.
13. Select any challenges you are currently facing from the list in the "What challenges are you currently facing (select all that apply)" field.
14. Select your plans after graduation from the drop-down menu in the "What are your plans after graduation" field.
15. If you have any additional comments or feedback, enter them in the "Any additional comments about your academic experience" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Student full name** (`student_name`, **text**, required): Enter your full name as recorded in the university's student information system.
- **Email address** (`student_email`, **email, required): Enter your official email address.
- **Student ID number** (`student_id`, **text, required): Enter your student ID number.
- **Current enrollment status** (`enrollment_status`, **select_one, required): Select your current status, whether full-time, part-time, auditing, on leave, or withdrawn.
- **Current academic level** (`academic_level`, **select_one, required): Select your current academic level, such as freshman, sophomore, junior, senior, graduate, or post-doctoral.
- **Program or major** (`program_of_study`, **text, required): Enter the name of your current program or major.
- **Minor or concentration (if applicable)** (`minor_concentration`, **text, optional): Enter the name of your minor or concentration, if applicable.
- **Expected graduation date** (`expected_graduation`, **date, required): Enter your expected graduation date.
- **Current academic standing** (`academic_standing`, **select_one, required): Select your current academic standing, such as good standing, academic probation, or not sure.
- **Approximate number of credits completed** (`credits_completed`, **number, required): Enter the approximate number of credits you have completed.
- **How satisfied are you with your academic program** (`satisfaction_program`, **select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with your academic program.
- **How satisfied are you with academic advising** (`satisfaction_advising`, **select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with academic advising.
- **What challenges are you currently facing (select all that apply)** (`challenges`, **select_multiple, optional): Select any challenges you are currently facing.
- **What are your plans after graduation** (`plan_after_graduation`, **select_one, optional): Select your plans after graduation.
- **Any additional comments about your academic experience** (`additional_comments`, **text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about your academic experience.
