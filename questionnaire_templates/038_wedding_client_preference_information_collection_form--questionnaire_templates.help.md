# Wedding Client Preference Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from the wedding clients about their preferences for their event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the form.
2. Select the correct options for the "vendor" and "planner" fields.
3. Enter the event date in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter the event time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
5. Enter the wedding client's name, email, and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Wedding Client Preference Information Collection Form 1
• **wedding_client_preference_information_collection_form_1** (`text`, optional)

### Vendor
• **vendor** (select one): Choose "Yes" if you have a preferred vendor for your wedding, "No" otherwise.

### Event Date
• **event date** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of your event in MM/DD/YYYY format.

### Event Time
• **event time** (`time`, optional): Enter the time of your event in 24-hour format (HH:MM).

### Wedding Client Name
• **wedding client name** (`text`, optional)

### Wedding Client Email
• **wedding client email** (`email`, optional)

### Wedding Client Phone
• **wedding client phone** (`text`, optional)

### Planner
• **planner** (select one): Choose "Yes" if you will be working with a planner for your wedding, "No" otherwise.
