# Story Ranking Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Story Ranking Survey form is designed to gather feedback and ratings on stories from users. This form helps us understand how users perceive a story and its elements, providing valuable insights for improvement and evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Enter your Author ID**: If you're familiar with the story, please enter the actual story ID. This will help us reference the story correctly. If not, you can leave this field blank.
2. **Rank the Story**: Choose how you rank the story from the options provided. This will give us an idea of your overall impression of the story.
3. **Rate the Story**: Provide a rating in seconds for how long you think the story should be.
4. **Provide Feedback**: Share your thoughts and comments about the story. This is an opportunity for you to give us specific feedback on what you liked or didn't like about the story.
5. **Submit**: Once you've completed the form, click the "Submit" button to send your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Story ID**: This field is optional, but if you're familiar with the story, please enter the actual story ID. This will help us reference the story correctly.
* **Author ID**: This is a required field, and you must enter your Author ID to complete the form. This helps us identify your submission.
* **Rank Story**: Choose how you rank the story from the options provided.
* **Rate Story**: Provide a rating in seconds for how long you think the story should be.
* **Feedback**: Share your thoughts and comments about the story, providing specific feedback on what you liked or didn't like.
* **Comments**: This field is optional, and you can provide additional comments or feedback if you'd like.
* **Story Rating (time)**: Provide a rating in seconds for how long you think the story should be.
* **Submit**: Once you've completed the form, click this button to send your response.
