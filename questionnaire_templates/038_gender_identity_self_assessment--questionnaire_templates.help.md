# Gender Identity Self Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to assess and understand your gender identity in various aspects.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Select the options that best describe your gender identity.
3. Provide comments for each question if you'd like to elaborate.
