# early_pregnancy_self_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from patients for early pregnancy health assessment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Name**: Please enter your name in the required format (e.g., "John Doe").
2. **Date Of Last Period**: Enter the date of your last menstrual cycle, if applicable. If you are unsure, estimate the date or leave blank.
3. **Gestational Age**: Enter the gestational age of your pregnancy in weeks.
4. **Date Of Expected Delivery**: Enter the expected date of delivery, if known.
5. **Medical Condition**: Select any applicable medical conditions from the list, such as Pregnancy, High Risk, or Multiple Pregnancy.
6. **Symptoms**: List all your symptoms, such as morning sickness or dizziness.
7. **Medical History**: Provide any relevant medical history, such as previous medical conditions or surgeries.
8. **Medications**: List all medications you are currently taking, including supplements and over-the-counter medications.
9. **Allergies**: Select any allergies you have from the list, such as Penicillin.
10. **Medical Contact**: Enter the contact information for your medical provider.
