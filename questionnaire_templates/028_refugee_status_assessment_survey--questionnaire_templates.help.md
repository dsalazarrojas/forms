# Refugee Status Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the status of refugees, their safety concerns, living conditions, needs, follow-up, contact method, contact frequency, and any additional contact details and notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select one of the options for each field to indicate your status or response.
2. For fields with the option "Other" or "Yes/No", provide a brief explanation in the text field below the "Other" option.
3. Fill in the required fields carefully and accurately.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure that they are complete and correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Refugee Status** (`refugee_status`, select_one, required): Choose whether you are a refugee or not.
  - Select "Yes" if you are a refugee.
  - Select "No" if you are not a refugee.
  - Select "Other" if you don't fit into any of the above categories.

- **Safety Concerns** (`safety_concerns`, select_multiple, required): Identify any safety concerns you may be facing.
  - Select "Yes" for any safety concerns you are facing.
  - Select "No" if you do not have any safety concerns.

- **Living Conditions** (`living_conditions`, select_multiple, required): Describe your current living conditions.
  - Select "Yes" for any living conditions you are currently experiencing.
  - Select "No" if you are not experiencing any living conditions.

- **Needs** (`needs`, text, optional): Describe any needs you may have.
  - Provide a brief explanation of your needs, if any.

- **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, date, optional): Schedule a follow-up meeting or contact, if desired.
  - Enter the date and time of your follow-up meeting or contact.

- **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted.
  - Select "Yes" for any method you would like to be contacted through.
  - Select "No" if you do not prefer any of the above contact methods.

- **Contact Frequency** (`contact_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Indicate how frequently you would like to be contacted.
  - Select "Yes" for any frequency you would like to be contacted.
  - Select "No" if you do not prefer any of the above contact frequencies.

- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): Provide your contact details, including a phone number and email address.
  - Fill in your phone number and email address for contact purposes.

- **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
  - Provide any additional information you think is relevant or useful.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask for help.
- This form is intended to assist in providing support and resources; if you need help, please don't hesitate to reach out.
