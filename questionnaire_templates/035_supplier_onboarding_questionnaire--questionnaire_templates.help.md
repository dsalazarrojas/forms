# Supplier Onboarding Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Onboarding Questionnaire is a form designed to gather essential information from suppliers to facilitate a smooth onboarding process. This form is used to collect contact information, contract details, and other relevant data about a supplier.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Supplier Onboarding Questionnaire, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your supplier's general information in the "Supplier Info" section.
2. In the "Contract Terms" section, select the contract terms that apply to your supplier.
3. Enter your contact details in the "Contact Details" section.
4. If you have a business email address, enter it in the "Email" field.
5. If your supplier has a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
6. Select the date of the last contact with your supplier.
7. Select the time of the last contact with your supplier.
8. If you have any additional notes about your supplier, enter them in the "Note" field.
9. Enter your supplier's address in the "Address" field.
10. Finally, enter your supplier's name in the "Supplier Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Info**: Enter your supplier's general information.
* **Contract Terms**: Select all contract terms that apply to your supplier (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Contact Details**: Enter your supplier's contact details, such as name and other relevant information.
* **Email**: Enter your supplier's business email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your supplier's phone number.
* **Date**: Select the date of the last contact with your supplier.
* **Time**: Select the time of the last contact with your supplier.
* **Note**: Enter any additional notes about your supplier.
* **Address**: Enter your supplier's address.
* **Supplier Name**: Enter your supplier's name.
