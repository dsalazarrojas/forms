# Materiality Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about a company's materiality assessment.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Please complete each field with accurate and truthful information about the company.
*   Select the correct options for each field based on the company's characteristics.
*   Enter the required contact information for the company representative.
*   Use the notes field to provide any additional information about the company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Company** (`1`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of company (Yes/No) that is being assessed.
    *   This field determines whether the company is the primary focus of the materiality assessment.
*   **Company Type** (`2`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of company (Yes/No) that is being assessed.
    *   This field determines whether the company is a material company.
*   **Reporting Currency** (`3`, `select_one`, required): Select the reporting currency (Yes/No) that is being used by the company.
    *   This field indicates whether the company uses a specific reporting currency.
*   **Reporting Frequency** (`4`, `select_one`, required): Select the reporting frequency (Yes/No) that is being used by the company.
    *   This field indicates how frequently the company reports.
*   **Materiality Issues** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all materiality issues (Yes/No) that apply to the company.
    *   This field determines whether the company has any materiality issues.
*   **Sustainability Issues** (`7`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all sustainability issues (Yes/No) that apply to the company.

    *   This field determines whether the company has any ESG issues.
