# report-form-title - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **report-form-title** form is designed to collect user input on volunteer availability and role interests. This form is meant to be a flexible tool for gathering data on various topics, and its structure is adaptable to different use cases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's purpose and relevance to your needs.
2. Start by filling out the **Volunteer Availability** page, where you can select one or multiple options from the provided list.
3. Proceed to the **Role Interests** page, where you can choose one option from the list that best represents your interests.
4. Finally, select your preference from the **support-Feedback** page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Volunteer Availability** (`form_page_1`, Select Multiple, required: false)
Select one or more options that best describe your volunteer availability.

### **Role Interests** (`form_page_2`, Select One, required: false)
Choose the option that best represents your interests.

### **support-Feedback** (`form_page_3`, Select One, required: false)
Select your preferred feedback option.
