# Video Duration Preference Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your video preferences. We want to understand how long you like to watch videos on different platforms, which platforms you prefer, and other related details. This will help us improve our content creation and distribution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your general information on the "General Information" page.
2. On the second page, select your preferred video length for different videos on various platforms.
3. On the third page, select your preferred platform for watching videos.
4. On the fourth page, describe your viewing habits and any other relevant details.
5. On the fifth page, choose the time of day you typically watch videos.
6. On the sixth page, leave any additional notes or comments.
7. On the seventh page, enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted.
8. On the last page, enter any other relevant information, or add a note.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`General Information`, `text`, optional): This is a general section for you to provide any additional information you'd like to share.
* **Video Length** (`video_length`, `select_one`, optional): Select how long you prefer to watch videos on different platforms. Options include:
	+ Less than 1 minute
	+ 1-2 minutes
	+ 2-5 minutes
	+ More than 5 minutes
* **Preferred Platform** (`Preferred Platform`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select which platforms you prefer for watching videos. Options include:
	+ YouTube
	+ Facebook
	+ Instagram
	+ TikTok
	+ Twitter
	+ Twitch
	+ LinkedIn
	+ Vimeo
* **Viewing Habits** (`Viewing Habits`, `text`, optional): Please describe your viewing habits, such as how often you watch videos or at what times.
* **Time of Day** (`Time of Day`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the time of day you typically watch videos. Options include:
	+ Morning
	+ Afternoon
	+ Evening
* **Note** (`Note`, `note`, optional): Leave a note or comment about your preferences.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **General Note** (`General Note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts you'd like to share.
