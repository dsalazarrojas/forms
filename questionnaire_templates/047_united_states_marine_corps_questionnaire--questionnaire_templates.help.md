# United States Marine Corps Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this questionnaire is to gather information from individuals about their demographic and health-related characteristics for administrative or research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required): Enter your contact information in this field.
* **Family Status** (Family Status, select_one, required): Choose your marital status.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Select your date of birth using the provided date input field.
* **Military History** (Military History, text, required): Enter your military history in this text input field.
* **Military Status** (Military Status, select_one, required): Choose your current military status.
* **Emergency Contacts** (Emergency Contacts, select_multiple, required): List the emergency contacts that best fit your situation.
* **Social Security Number** (Social Security Number, text, required): Type your social security number in this text input field.
* **Medicaid Status** (Medicaid Status, select_one, required): Choose whether you have Medicaid or not.
* **Medicare Status** (Medicare Status, select_one, required): Choose whether you have Medicare or not.
* **Veterans Status** (Veterans Status, select_one, required): Choose whether you are a veteran or not.
* **Disability Status** (Disability Status, select_one, required): Choose whether you have a disability or not.
* **Military Disability** (Military Disability, select_one, required): Choose whether you are a military disability beneficiary or not.
