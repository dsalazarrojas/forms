# traditional_healing_practices_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information on individuals' awareness and perception of traditional healing methods. The information gathered will be used to better understand the general public's views on traditional healing methods, their efficacy, and safety.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. For select_multiple type questions, select all applicable options that apply to your situation.
3. Enter your answers in the designated fields.
4. Make sure to answer all required questions (indicated by a star \*).
