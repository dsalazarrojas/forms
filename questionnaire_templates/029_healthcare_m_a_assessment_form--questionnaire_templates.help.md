# Healthcare M&A Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthcare M&A Assessment Form is a comprehensive guide to assess a target company in the healthcare industry for M&A purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully and ensure you understand each question before answering.
- Answer each question truthfully and accurately based on the target company's characteristics.
- Review and update your answers as needed.
