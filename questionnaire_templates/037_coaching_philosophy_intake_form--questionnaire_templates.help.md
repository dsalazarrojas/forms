# Coaching Philosophy Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coaching Philosophy Intake Form is designed to help coaches reflect on their personal coaching philosophy, values, and approaches. This form will help us understand your coaching style, goals, and priorities, ensuring we can work together effectively and find the best fit for you and our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each section carefully, providing detailed answers to help us understand your coaching philosophy. You may find it helpful to review the form before starting, so you can organize your thoughts and have a clear understanding of the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (1)**: Please enter your legal full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address (2)**: Enter your contact email address where we can reach you.
* **Phone Number (3)**: Provide your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Sport or Coaching Specialty (4)**: Describe the sport or specialty you coach, such as soccer, basketball, or swimming.
* **Current Coaching Level (5)**: Select the level of coaching you currently hold, such as Youth Recreational, Youth Competitive, High School, College, Professional, or Club.
* **Years of Coaching Experience (6)**: Choose the number of years you have coached, from Less than 1 year to Over 10 years.
* **Describe Your Coaching Philosophy (7)**: Share your core coaching belief, values, and principles that guide your coaching practice.
* **Primary Coaching Goals (8)**: Outline what you want your athletes to achieve through your coaching.
* **Definition of Success (9)**: Explain how you define success, including what it looks like and how you measure it.
* **Winning vs. Development (10)**: Prioritize between winning and athlete development, selecting what is most important to you.
* **Coaching Style (11)**: Choose your approach to coaching: Authoritative, Democratic, Collaborative, Laissez-Faire, or Adaptive.
* **Motivation Approach (12)**: Describe how you motivate your athletes, including your methods and strategies.
* **Handling Mistakes (13)**: Explain your approach to handling athlete mistakes, including how you address errors and provide feedback.
* **Discipline Approach (14)**: Describe how you handle behavioral issues, including your expectations and consequences.
* **Communication Style (15)**: Select your communication style with athletes: Direct and Clear, Supportive and Encouraging, Analytical and Detailed, Inspirational, or Mixed Approach.
* **Parent Interaction (16)** (Optional): If applicable, describe how you interact with parents.
* **Athlete Safety Priority (17)**: Explain how you prioritize athlete safety, including your safety protocols and emergency procedures.
* **Inclusion Approach (18)**: Describe how you ensure all athletes feel included and valued.
* **Handling Diverse Abilities (19)**: Explain how you coach mixed-ability groups, including how you adapt your teaching methods.
* **Feedback Style (20)**: Choose how you provide feedback: Immediate and Direct, Delayed and Reflective, Sandwich Method, Question-Based, or Demonstration-Based.
* **Practice Structure (21)**: Describe your typical practice approach, including your structure and organization.
* **Skill Development vs. Fun (22)**: Prioritize between skill development and fun, selecting what is primary to you.
* **Role Model Beliefs (23)**: Describe your role as a role model, including how you embody this responsibility.
* **Continuous Learning (24)**: Explain how you continue to develop your coaching skills and knowledge.
* **Coaching Influences (25)** (Optional): If applicable, list coaches or philosophies that influence your coaching approach.
* **Scenario Response (26)**: Describe how you handle a losing streak, including your approach to staying positive and motivated.
* **Team Culture (27)**: Explain how you build team culture, including your values and expectations.
* **Additional Philosophy Notes (28)** (Optional): If you have any further thoughts or insights about your coaching philosophy, add them here.

## Tips

* Be as detailed and specific as possible when answering each question.
* Use examples or anecdotes to illustrate your points.
* If you have any additional information that is not covered by the questions, feel free to add it in the "Additional Philosophy Notes" section.
* Remember, this form is not a test, and there are no right or wrong answers. We are here to learn and understand your coaching philosophy, not to evaluate or judge you.
