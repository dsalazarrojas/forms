# Prospect Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about a prospect to help us better understand their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the contact information fields: First name, Last name, Email, Phone, and Company name.
2. Provide the prospect's job title, department, and position.
3. Enter their company name and address.
4. Choose their industry and company type.
5. Select their social media presence (if any).
6. Review and submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (1, text, required): Enter the prospect's first name.
* **Last name** (2, text, required): Enter the prospect's last name.
* **Email** (3, text, required): Enter the prospect's email address.
* **Phone** (4, text, required): Enter the prospect's phone number.
* **Company name** (5, text, required): Enter the prospect's company name.
* **Job title** (6, text, required): Enter the prospect's job title.
* **Department** (7, text, required): Enter the prospect's department.
* **Company** (8, text, required): Enter the prospect's company name.
* **Department** (9, text, required): Enter the prospect's department.
* **Street address** (10, text, required): Enter the prospect's street address.
* **Apartment/Suite** (11, text, required): Enter the prospect's apartment or suite number.
* **City** (12, text, required): Enter the prospect's city.
* **State** (13, select, optional): If you know the prospect's state, select it; otherwise, leave blank.
* **Zip** (14, text, required): Enter the prospect's zip code.
* **Country** (15, text, required): Enter the prospect's country.
* **Industry** (16, text, optional): Enter the prospect's industry.
* **Company type** (17, select, optional): If you know the prospect's company type, select it; otherwise, leave blank.
* **Size** (18, select, optional): If you know the prospect's company size, select it; otherwise, leave blank.
* **Website** (19, text, optional): Enter the prospect's website URL.
* **Facebook** (20, text, optional): Enter the prospect's Facebook URL.
* **Twitter** (21, text, optional): Enter the prospect's Twitter URL.
* **Instagram** (22, text, optional): Enter the prospect's Instagram URL.
* **Pinterest** (23, text, optional): Enter the prospect's Pinterest URL.
* **Youtube** (24, text, optional): Enter the prospect's Youtube URL.
* **Other social** (25, text, optional): Enter any other social media URL.
