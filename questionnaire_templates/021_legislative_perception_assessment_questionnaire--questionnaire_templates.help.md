# Legislative Perception Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about the legislative perception of a particular entity, individual, or situation. This assessment is designed to gauge the level of awareness, support, or opinion held by the public or stakeholders regarding a specific legislative issue or policy.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Read the introduction, which provides background information about the form and its purpose.
2.  Review the instructions on how to fill out the form, which will guide you through the process.
3.  Answer the demographic questions (id 3) to provide context about yourself or the entity in question.
4.  Respond to questions 1-5 (id 4) by selecting the appropriate answer from the list.
5.  Answer question 6 (id 5) by selecting the appropriate answer from the list.
6.  Select multiple responses for question 7 (id 6) by choosing as many options as applicable.
7.  Enter a specific date for question 8 (id 7).
8.  Answer question 9 (id 8) by selecting the appropriate time of day.
9.  Provide a written response to question 10 (id 9).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, optional): This page provides a brief overview of the form and its purpose.
*   **How to fill out the form** (`how_to_fill_out_the_form`, `text`, optional): This page provides step-by-step instructions on how to complete the form.
*   **Demographic info** (`demographic_info`, `number`, optional): This section collects information about yourself or the entity in question, such as age, occupation, or location. Please answer all questions that apply.
*   **Questions 1-5** (`questions_1_5`, `select_one`, optional): These questions are designed to gauge the level of awareness or support for a specific legislative issue or policy. Select the answer that best applies to your situation.
*   **Question 6** (`question_6`, `select_one`, optional): This question is similar to questions 1-5, and you should select the answer that best applies to your situation.
*   **Question 7** (`question_7`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question allows you to select multiple answers that apply to your situation.
*   **Question 8** (`question_8`, `date`, optional): This question asks for a specific date, so please enter a valid date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Question 9** (`question_9`, `time`, optional): This question asks for a specific time of day, so please select the correct time from the list.
*   **Question 10** (`question_10`, `text`, optional): This is an open-ended question that allows you to provide a written response to question 9.

## Tips

*   Please answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
*   If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification before proceeding.
*   The information collected will be used to assess the legislative perception and inform decision-making processes.
