# Animal Health Policy Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information on respondent's role, familiarity with existing animal health policies, and other relevant aspects related to animal health policy awareness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, please review each question and choose the appropriate answer based on your actual situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Respondent role**: Select the role that best describes you. This will help us understand your perspective and experience with animal health policies.
	+ Options: Veterinarian, Shelter staff, Volunteer, Pet owner, Researcher
* **Organization name**: If you are affiliated with an organization, please provide its name. This information is optional.
	+ Type: text
* **Familiarity level**: Rate your familiarity with existing animal health policies. This will help us understand your level of understanding and experience.
	+ Options: Very familiar, Somewhat familiar, Not very familiar, Not familiar at all
* **Have you received formal training on policies?**: Indicate if you have received formal training on animal health policies.
	+ Options: 'True', 'False'
* **Policy clarity rating**: Rate how clear the written policies are. This will help us understand your experience with policy clarity.
	+ Options: Very clear, Clear, Somewhat unclear, Very unclear
* **Examples of policy applicability challenges**: Describe any situations where the policy applicability was unclear to you. This is optional and will help us understand where policies may need improvement.
	+ Type: text
* **Suggested improvements to policy or training**: Provide concrete suggestions for improvement. This is also optional and will help us refine the policies and training materials.
	+ Type: text
* **Contact for follow-up**: If you are willing to be contacted for further discussion or clarification, please provide your email address. This is optional.
	+ Type: email
* **Consent to share responses**: Allow us to share your aggregated and anonymized responses with other stakeholders. This will help us improve the policies and training materials.
	+ Options: 'True', 'False'
