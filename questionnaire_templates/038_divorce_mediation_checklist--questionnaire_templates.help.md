# Divorce Mediation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a divorce mediation session. It includes fields for session details, readiness info, mediator and client notes, dates and times, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the session details, including date and time.
2. Provide any relevant readiness info.
3. Take notes for the mediator and client.
4. Enter the client's and mediator's names and organisation.
5. Select a co-mediator's name (if applicable).
6. Enter the session goals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Session Details (session_details)**: Enter the date and details of the session.
- **Readiness Info (readiness_info)**: Provide any relevant information about the client's readiness for mediation.
- **Mediator Notes (mediator_notes)**: Note any important details about the mediation session.
- **Client Notes (client_notes)**: Record any comments or concerns from the client.
- **Session Date (session_date)**: Enter the date of the mediation session.
- **Start Time (start_time)**: Enter the start time of the mediation session.
- **End Time (end_time)**: Enter the end time of the mediation session.
- **Mediator Name (mediator_name)**: Enter the name of the mediator.
- **Mediator Organisation (mediator_organisation)**: Enter the name of the mediator's organisation.
- **Client Name (client_name)**: Enter the client's name.
- **Co-Mediator Name (co_med_name)**: Enter the name of the co-mediator (if applicable).
- **Session Goals (session_goals)**: Outline the goals and objectives of the mediation session.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
* Use clear and concise language when filling in the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to the form creators: form@gic.mx.
