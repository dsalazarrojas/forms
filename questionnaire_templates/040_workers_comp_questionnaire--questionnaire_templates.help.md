# Workers Comp Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workers Comp Questionnaire is designed to gather information about work-related incidents, including details about the incident, the employer, the worker, and medical certifications. This information is crucial for processing workers' compensation claims.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing general information about the incident.
2. Answer questions about the details of the incident, such as what happened and where it occurred.
3. Provide information about your employer and work-related details.
4. Enter relevant details about yourself, the worker involved.
5. Answer questions about the injury, including any medical certifications or documentation required.
6. Review and submit your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **incident_info**
(`incident_info`, `text`, required: false)
Provide a brief description of the incident that occurred, including what happened and where it happened.

### **incident_details**
(`incident_details`, `text`, required: false)
Elaborate on the details of the incident, including any specific events or circumstances that led to the injury.

### **employer_info**
(`employer_info`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the name and address of your employer, as well as any other relevant company information.

### **worker_info**
(`worker_info`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your name and any other relevant personal information.

### **injury_info**
(`injury_info`, `text`, required: false)
Describe the injury and its impact, including any medical certifications or documentation provided.

### **medical_certification**
(`medical_certification`, `text`, required: false)
Attach or upload any relevant medical certifications, such as medical records or doctor's notes.

## Tips
* Please provide clear and concise answers to the questions.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, contact the relevant authorities or HR representative for guidance.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you need to edit the form, please save and submit a new version for review.
