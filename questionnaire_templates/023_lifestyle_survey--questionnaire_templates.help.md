# lifestyle_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The lifestyle survey is a form used to collect background information about the user. This information is likely being used for research or data collection purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Please review each field carefully and provide accurate information.
*   Ensure you have the authority to complete this form.
*   Please do not duplicate effort, if you have already completed this form, do not submit it again.
*   Contact the relevant authority if you are unsure about any field or have concerns about your eligibility to complete this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **What is your background information** (`intro_section`, `text`, not required):
    This field is a text area where you can provide any relevant background information about yourself.
*   **What is your age** (`age`, `number`, not required):
    Please enter your age in numerical format (e.g., 25).
*   **Your Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, not required):
    Please select one of the following options: male, female, or other.
*   **Your Occupation** (`occupation`, `text`, not required):
    This field is for you to enter your occupation or work type.
*   **Income** (`income`, `number`, not required):
    Please enter your income in numerical format (e.g., 40000).
*   **Your Email** (`email`, `email`, not required):
    Please enter your email address.
*   **Your Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required):
    This field is for you to enter your phone number.
*   **Tips**
    *   Please use a real email address and phone number to ensure we can contact you regarding any follow-up questions.
    *   If you are using a shared device or public network, please be cautious when submitting sensitive information.
