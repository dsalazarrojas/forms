# Organizational Health Index Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organizational Health Index Survey is a comprehensive assessment tool that evaluates various aspects of an organization's health, providing a comprehensive index to identify areas of improvement and inform strategic decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and thoroughly, taking the time to read and understand each question before responding.
2. Ensure that you complete each page before moving on to the next one, so that your answers are up-to-date and accurate.
3. Select the options that best answer the question for each field.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have read and understood each question before selecting an option.
* Select the options that best describe your current situation or perception.
* If you are unsure or need help with any question, please ask for assistance.
* Please be honest and provide accurate answers, as they will help in obtaining a clear picture of the organization's health.
