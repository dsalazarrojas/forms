# Community Energy Interview Questions - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to gather information about your energy preferences and habits to help us better understand your needs and offer more effective community-based clean energy solutions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Answer all questions, as some are required.
3. Select the most accurate option for each question.
4. Use the space provided for the "Additional Comments" field to add any further thoughts or comments.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (Optional)** (`interviewee_name`, `text`, optional): Your name if you wish to share it with us.
* **Interview Date** (`interview_date`, `date`, required): The date of the interview or the date you are answering this form.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Your ZIP code to determine your location.
* **Do You Own Your Home** (`homeowner_status`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes, Own' if you own the home, 'No, Rent' if you rent, or 'Other' if other options apply.
* **Household Size** (`household_size`, `number`, required): Enter the number of people living in your household.
* **Primary Energy Source** (`current_energy_source`, `select_one`, required): Select your main energy source (e.g., Electric, Natural Gas, Propane, Oil, Solar, or Other).
* **Average Monthly Energy Bill** (`monthly_energy_cost`, `select_one`, required): Choose your approximate monthly energy bill.
* **Energy Concerns** (`energy_concerns`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply from High Costs, Environmental Impact, Reliability, Power Outages, Lack of Options, or None.
* **Interest in Renewable Energy** (`renewable_interest`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your level of interest in renewable energy (Very Interested, Somewhat Interested, Neutral, Not Interested).
* **Interest in Solar Installation** (`solar_interest`, `select_one`, required): Select Yes, Already Have, Yes, Planning To, Interested, But Not Ready, or Not Interested.
* **Barriers to Solar Adoption** (`solar_barriers`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all that apply from High Upfront Cost, Renting Home, Roof Not Suitable, Lack of Information, HOA Restrictions, or Not a Barrier.
* **Energy Efficiency Upgrades Made** (`energy_efficiency_upgrades`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all that apply from LED Lighting, Smart Thermostat, Energy Efficient Appliances, Improved Insulation, New Windows, or None.
* **Do You Own an Electric Vehicle** (`ev_ownership`, `select_one`, required): Select 'True' if you own an EV, 'No, But Considering' if you're considering, or 'False' if you don't own an EV.
* **EV Charging Needs** (`ev_charging_needs`, `select_one`, required): Choose your EV charging needs (Home Charging, Public Charging, 'False', or Not Applicable).
* **Interest in Community Energy Programs** (`community_program_interest`, `select_one`, required): Select your level of interest in local community energy programs (Very Interested, Somewhat Interested, Not Interested).
* **Programs of Interest** (`program_types`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all that apply from Bulk Solar Purchase, Energy Efficiency Rebates, Community Solar, Educational Workshops, Low-Income Assistance, or EV Charging Stations.
* **Willingness to Pay for Clean Energy** (`willingness_to_pay`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your willingness to pay more for clean energy (Yes, Up to 5% More, Yes, Up to 10% More, Yes, Up to 20% More, or False).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional thoughts or comments about energy or community energy initiatives.

Please answer honestly and carefully, as this form is designed to help us better understand your energy needs and preferences.
