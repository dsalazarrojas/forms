# Government Agency Strategic Planning Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Government Agency Strategic Planning Audit Form is designed to assess the effectiveness of an agency's strategic planning efforts. This form helps auditors evaluate an agency's progress in creating and implementing a strategic plan, identify areas for improvement, and provide recommendations for future growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your agency's location.
2. Indicate the type of your agency (Government Agency, Non-Profit, or Other).
3. Enter the name and contact information (email and phone number) of the person responsible for the agency's strategic planning.
4. Enter the start and end dates of your agency's strategic plan.
5. Determine the frequency of your agency's strategic planning audits (Quarterly, Semi-Annual, or Annually).
6. Report the dates of your last audit and the date scheduled for your next audit.
7. Rate your agency's overall performance using the provided scale (1-3).
8. Describe any areas where your agency needs improvement.
9. Provide comments or observations regarding your agency's strategic planning process.
10. Outline any recommendations for future improvements.
11. Describe follow-up actions taken or planned.
12. Report on the status of your follow-up actions.
13. Enter any follow-up notes.
14. Record the result of your last audit (Passed, Failed, or Pending).
15. Record any final notes from the audit.
16. Assess the overall risk level of your agency's strategic planning (Low, Medium, or High).
17. Identify areas that pose risks.
18. Describe the impact of these risks.
19. Enter any additional comments or information regarding your agency's strategic planning.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agency Location**: Enter your agency's location to help auditors track the agency's presence and accessibility.
* **Agency Type**: Select the type of your agency (Government Agency, Non-Profit, or Other) to help auditors understand your agency's nature.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information (email and phone number) of the person responsible for the agency's strategic planning.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Strategic Plan**: Enter a brief description of your agency's strategic plan.
* **Plan Start Date**: Enter the start date of your agency's strategic plan.
* **Plan End Date**: Enter the end date of your agency's strategic plan.
* **Audit Frequency**: Select the frequency of your agency's strategic planning audits (Quarterly, Semi-Annual, or Annually).
* **Last Audit Date**: Enter the date of your last audit.
* **Next Audit Scheduled Date**: Enter the scheduled date of your next audit.
* **Overall Rating**: Rate your agency's overall performance using the provided scale (1-3).
* **Areas For Improvement**: Describe any areas where your agency needs improvement.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or observations regarding your agency's strategic planning process.
* **Recommendations**: Outline any recommendations for future improvements.
* **Follow-up Action**: Describe any follow-up actions taken or planned.
* **Follow-up Due Date**: Enter the due date of your follow-up actions.
* **Follow-up Status**: Report the status of your follow-up actions.
* **Follow-up Notes**: Enter any follow-up notes.
* **Audit Result**: Record the result of your last audit (Passed, Failed, or Pending).
* **Final Notes**: Enter any final notes from the audit.
* **Overall Risk Rating**: Assess the overall risk level of your agency's strategic planning (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Risk Area**: Identify areas that pose risks.
* **Risk Description**: Describe the impact of these risks.
* **Risk Impact**: Describe the impact of these risks.
* **Overall Risk Rating**: Enter the overall risk rating.
* **Risk Area**: Enter any additional comments or information regarding your agency's strategic planning.
* **Audit Result**: Enter any additional comments or information regarding your agency's strategic planning.

Note: The provided information should be entered based on your agency's actual experience and knowledge. Be as specific and accurate as possible.
