# Credentialing Questionnaire Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect background information and credential verification details from individuals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in all required fields, marked as such.
2. If you are unsure about a particular field, please contact the relevant authority for clarification.
3. Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Background Information** (Background Information, text, false)
	+ This field is not a required field and can be left blank.
* **Education** (Education, select_multiple, false)
	+ Select all relevant options that apply to your educational background.
* **Professional Experience** (Professional Experience, select_one, false)
	+ Select 'Yes' if you have professional experience, 'No' otherwise.
* **Credentials** (Credentials, text, false)
	+ This field is not required, but you can enter any relevant credentials you may have.
* **Other Information** (Other Information, text, false)
	+ Enter any additional information that may be relevant to your background.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, false)
	+ Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Time of Birth** (Time of Birth, time, false)
	+ Enter your time of birth in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Address** (Address, text, false)
	+ Enter your full address, including street number and postal code.
* **City** (City, text, false)
	+ Enter the name of the city where you reside.
* **State** (State, text, false)
	+ Enter the name of the state where you reside.
* **Zip** (Zip, text, false)
	+ Enter your postal code.
* **Email** (Email, email, false)
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, false)
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Date Completed** (Date Completed, date, false)
	+ Enter the date you completed your training or certification.
* **Time Completed** (Time Completed, time, false)
	+ Enter the time you completed your training or certification.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, false)
	+ This field is not editable, but rather for the user to take notes.
* **Verification** (Verification, select_one, false)
	+ Select the relevant option for verification.
* **Verification Date** (Verification Date, date, false)
	+ Enter the date of verification.
* **Verification Time** (Verification Time, time, false)
	+ Enter the time of verification.
* **Verification Notes** (Verification Notes, text, false)
	+ Enter any additional notes about the verification process.
* **Other Verification Info** (Other Verification Info, text, false)
	+ Enter any other information about the verification process.
* **Certification Info** (Certification Info, text, false)
	+ Enter any relevant certification information.
* **Other Certification Info** (Other Certification Info, text, false)
	+ Enter any other certification information.
* **Medical License Info** (Medical License Info, text, false)
	+ Enter any relevant medical license information.
* **Other Medical License Info** (Other Medical License Info, text, false)
	+ Enter any other medical license information.
