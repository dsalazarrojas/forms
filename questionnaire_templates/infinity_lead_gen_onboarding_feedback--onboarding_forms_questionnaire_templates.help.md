# (INFINITY LEAD GEN) Onboarding Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The (INFINITY LEAD GEN) Onboarding Feedback form is designed to collect feedback from new hires about their onboarding experience. This form will help us evaluate the effectiveness of our onboarding program and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections: The form is divided into two main sections: "Personal Information" and "Onboarding Experience".
2. Fill in the required fields: All fields marked as "required" must be filled in with accurate and truthful information.
3. Rate your feedback: Answer the rating questions (e.g., satisfaction, management communication, work-life balance, team integration) with the corresponding scale (e.g., Very satisfied, Excellent, 1-10).
4. Provide additional feedback: If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Improvement suggestions" and "Additional comments or notes" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full legal name as per your identification documents.
- **Email address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Provide a valid work email address that we can use to contact you.
- **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Enter your department name, e.g., Sales, Engineering.
- **Job title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your official job title.
- **Date of hire** (`hire_date`, `date`, optional): If applicable, enter the date you started working.
- **Years at organization** (`years_employed`, `integer`, optional): Approximate years you've been employed with the organization.
- **Onboarding start date** (`onboarding_start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the start date of the onboarding program if you're aware of it.
- **Onboarding end date** (`onboarding_end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the end date of the onboarding program if you're aware of it.
- **Manager name** (`manager_name`, `text`, optional): If you have a direct manager, enter their name.
- **Training completed** (`training_completed`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you completed all required training.
- **Training feedback** (`training_feedback`, `text`, optional): Provide any feedback on the training sessions.
- **Satisfaction with onboarding** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your satisfaction with the onboarding experience (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied).
- **Management communication rating** (`management_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate your manager's communication during onboarding (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
- **Support for professional development** (`growth_opportunities`, `select_one`, required): Indicate how strongly you agree that you feel supported in professional development during onboarding (Strongly agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, Strongly disagree).
- **Work-life balance rating** (`work_life_balance`, `integer`, required): Rate your work-life balance during onboarding (1-10).
- **Team integration rating** (`team_integration`, `integer`, required): Rate how well you were integrated into the team (1-10).
- **Preferred learning style** (`preferred_learning_style`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred learning style (Visual, Auditory, Kinesthetic, Reading/Writing).
- **Preferred feedback channels** (`feedback_channel_preference`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your preferred channels for future feedback (Email, Chat, In-person, Survey).
- **Recommend onboarding** (`would_recommend`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you would recommend this onboarding program to new hires (True, False).
- **Anonymous feedback preference** (`anonymous_feedback`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you prefer to submit feedback anonymously (True, False).
- **Improvement suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Provide any suggestions to improve the onboarding program.
- **Additional comments or notes** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
- **Feedback submission date** (`feedback_submission_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you're submitting this feedback.
- **Feedback submission date**: This date will be automatically populated and should not be filled in by the user.
