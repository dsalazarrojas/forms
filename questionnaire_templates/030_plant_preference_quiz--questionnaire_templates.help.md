# Plant Preference Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Plant Preference Quiz" is a form designed to collect your personal preferences for a plant that suits your lifestyle. It asks questions about your favorite type of plant, its size, location, and other characteristics to help you find the perfect plant for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer each question carefully, and make sure to select all that apply.
* You can select multiple options for fields that allow multiple selections.
* Make sure to fill out all fields, as some are required.
* If you have no preference for a particular field, you can select "None" or leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your favorite houseplant?**: This is the first question that asks for your favorite type of plant. Choose the one that you like best from the options provided.
* **Plant Type**: This question asks you to select the type of plant you prefer. You can choose one of the three options: Fiddle Leaf, Fern, or Succulent.
* **Plant Size**: This question asks you to select the size of the plant you prefer. You can choose multiple options: Small, Medium, or Large.
* **Where do you live?**: This question asks you to select where you live, which will help determine the plant that suits your environment. You can choose one of two options: Urban or Rural.
* **Sensitivity**: This question asks you to select the level of sensitivity your plant should have. You can choose multiple options: Light, Medium, or Dark.
* **Plant Color**: This question asks you to select the color of the plant you prefer. You can choose one of three options: Green, Blue, or Red.
* **Plant Ownership**: This is a text field where you can write a little about your plant ownership experience.
* **Plant Purpose**: This question asks you to select the purpose of your plant. You can choose multiple options: Decoration, Health, or Aesthetic.
* **Plant Situation**: This question asks you to select where you would like to place your plant. You can choose multiple options: Outdoor or Indoor.
* **Plant Dedication**: This is a text field where you can write a little about your plant dedication.
