# Personal Rights Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Rights Assessment Form is used to evaluate a person's awareness, confidence, and environment regarding their personal rights. The form helps to identify gaps in knowledge, areas of improvement, and provides a platform for educators, counselors, and community team members to provide feedback and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your general information, including submission date and time if necessary.
2. Rate your awareness of your personal rights on a scale from High to Low.
3. Choose the environments where you currently have access to and how confident you are in your rights in each environment.
4. Select any additional comments or notes to provide more context to your assessment.
5. Review and fill in your contact information, including email and phone number.
6. Add any additional comments or feedback from educators, counselors, or community team members.
7. Press the Submit button to complete the form.
