# Sacroiliac Ligament Self Release Guide Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sacroiliac Ligament Self Release Guide Form is a document that guides participants in self-releasing the sacroiliac ligament. It is a form that collects information from both the participant and the practitioner who will be performing the release.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction carefully to understand the purpose and instructions of the form.
2. Answer all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), thoroughly and accurately.
3. Provide the participant's contact information for any follow-up purposes.
4. Make sure to enter the correct dates for the participant's and practitioner's dates.
5. If necessary, add any additional notes about the release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is where you will find a brief introduction explaining the purpose and instructions of the form.
* *Release Instructions*: This is where you will provide detailed instructions on how to perform the sacroiliac ligament release.
* **Participant Feedback**: Provide any feedback about your experience with the release, including any difficulties you may have faced.
* **Acknowledgements**: This field is used to confirm that you understand and have been informed about the procedure.
	+ 'Yes' - You understand and have been informed about the procedure.
	+ 'No' - You do not understand or have not been informed about the procedure.
* **Practitioner Name**: This is where the practitioner who will perform the release will be listed.
* **Practitioner Title**: This is where the practitioner's title will be listed.
* **Participant Email**: This is where you will enter your email address for any follow-up purposes.
* **Participant Phone**: This is where you will enter your phone number for any follow-up purposes.
* **Participant Date**: This is where you will enter the date of the release.
* **Practitioner Date**: This is where you will enter the date of the practitioner who performed the release.
* **Notes**: This is where you can provide any additional information about the release, if necessary.
