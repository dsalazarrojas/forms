# Nursing School Interview Preparation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather relevant information from nursing school applicants in preparation for their interview. It is intended to provide valuable insights for the interview panel in evaluating the applicants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to understand what information is requested.
2. Answer each question clearly and concisely.
3. Ensure you select the most relevant option for each field.
4. Fill out the form completely, but do not worry if you are unsure of some information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Background Information
* **Background Information (Page 1)** (`background_information`, `text`, optional): This field is intended for the applicant to provide any relevant background information they feel is necessary for the interview panel to understand, such as work experience or education. Please be brief and focus on the most important information.

### Contact Details
* **Contact Details (Page 2)** (`contact_details`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the ways you can be contacted during the interview process, such as email, phone, or text. This will ensure that the interview panel can contact you with any further questions or information.

### Medical History
* **Medical History (Page 3)** (`medical_history`, `text`, optional): If you have any medical conditions or allergies that may be relevant to your application, please provide this information in this field. 

### Education and Employment
* **Education and Employment (Page 4)** (`education_and_employment`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the options that best describes your current education level or employment status. This is a required field to ensure that the interview panel understands your current status.

### Nursing School Information
* **Nursing School Information (Page 5)** (`nursing_school_info`, `text`, optional): This field is intended for you to provide any relevant information about your nursing school experience or education. Please be brief and focus on the most important information.

### Personal Statement
* **Personal Statement (Page 6)** (`personal_statement`, `text`, optional): This is an opportunity for you to tell the interview panel about your personal goals and aspirations as a nursing student. Please be sincere and concise in your statement.

### References
* **References (Page 7)** (`references`, `text`, optional): If you have any professional or academic references, please provide their names and contact information in this field.

### Nursing School Interview
* **Nursing School Interview (Page 8)** (`nursing_school_interview`, `time`, optional): Please select a date and time that is most convenient for you to schedule an interview. 

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate in your responses.
* If you are unsure of any information, it is okay to ask for clarification.
* This form is for internal use only, so please do not share it with anyone outside of the nursing school.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the nursing school.
