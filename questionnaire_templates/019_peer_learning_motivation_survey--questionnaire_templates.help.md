# Peer Learning Motivation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to understand the motivations and preferences of students who engage in peer learning activities. By providing honest and accurate responses, you will help us better understand what drives students to learn from their peers and how to improve our peer learning initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Choose the most common reason you learn from peers from the options provided.
2. Enter your student name and email address.
3. Enter the name of the course you are currently taking or intend to take.
4. Provide your motivation reasons for peer learning in the text box.
5. Select your level of motivation for peer learning from the scale provided.
6. Indicate the amount of time you spend on peer learning activities.
7. Select the scale for the time you spend on peer learning.
8. Enter your date of birth.
9. Enter your year of birth.
10. Choose your gender.
11. Choose your country.
12. Enter your city.
13. Enter your state or province.
14. Enter your zip or postal code.
15. Enter your phone number.
16. Enter your email address (if different from your student email).
17. Enter your website (if applicable).
18. Add any additional comments about your peer learning experience.
19. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the most common reason students learn from peers?** (`1`): 
  * This question asks about the main reason you engage in peer learning activities.
  * Please select one or more of the options provided.
* **Student Name** (`2`): 
  * Enter your name as it appears in your official records.
* **Student Email** (`3`): 
  * Enter your university-assigned email address or the email address associated with your student account.
* **Teacher Email** (`4`): 
  * If applicable, enter your teacher's email address or the email address associated with your teacher's account.
* **Course Name** (`5`): 
  * Enter the name of the course you are currently taking or intend to take.
* **What are your motivation reasons for peer learning?** (`6`): 
  * Provide a short description of why you engage in peer learning activities.
* **How motivated you are?** (`7`): 
  * Select a scale to indicate your level of motivation for engaging in peer learning activities.
* **Level of Motivation for Peer Learning** (`8`): 
  * Select a level of motivation to indicate how much you value peer learning.
* **Time Spent on Peer Learning** (`9`): 
  * Indicate the amount of time you spend on peer learning activities.
* **Time Spent Scale** (`10`): 
  * Select a scale to indicate how much time you spend on peer learning.
* **Time Spent Value** (`11`): 
  * Enter the actual time spent on peer learning.
* **Date Of Birth** (`12`): 
  * Enter your date of birth (DD/MM/YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Year Of Birth** (`13`): 
  * Enter your year of birth.
* **Gender** (`14`): 
  * Choose your gender.
* **Country** (`15`): 
  * Select the country where you are located.
* **City** (`16`): 
  * Enter the name of your city or town.
* **State** (`17`): 
  * Enter the name of your state or province.
* **Zip** (`18`): 
  * Enter your zip or postal code.
* **Phone** (`19`): 
  * Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`20`): 
  * Enter your email address (if different from your student email).
* **Website** (`21`): 
  * If applicable, enter your website address.
* **Comments** (`22`): 
  * Add any additional comments about your peer learning experience.
* **Submit** (`23`): 
  * Click the "Submit" button to complete the survey.
