# learning_motivation_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The learning motivation questionnaire is a survey designed to gauge your learning motivation and time management practices. It collects information on several factors, including your demographic information, learning motivation level, motivation level over time, time management practices, and submission date.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what each question is asking.
2. Select the options that best match your answers for each question.
3. Fill out each page of the form, which are labeled with a page title.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by *).
5. If a question allows multiple options, select all that apply.
6. For the submission date, select the correct date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`intro_page`, text, optional): This is the first page of the form, which introduces the purpose of the form. Read this page carefully before proceeding.
* **Demographic Info** (`demographic_info`, select_multiple, optional): Select all options that apply to your demographic information. This will help us understand your background and experiences.
* **Questions** (`questions_page`, select_one, optional): Select the option that best describes your current situation related to the question.
* **Motivation Level** (`motivation_level`, select_multiple, optional): Select all options that apply to your current motivation level. This will help us understand your drive to learn.
* **Motivation Level 2** (`motivation_level2`, select_multiple, optional): Select all options that apply to your motivation level over time. This will help us understand how your motivation has changed.
* **Time Management** (`time_management`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value that describes your current time management practices. Please note that this field is not related to a specific scale.
* **Time Management Scale** (`time_management_scale`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value that describes your current time management scale. This is a separate question from the previous one and should be answered accordingly.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Select the date you are submitting this form. This is an important field to ensure we have accurate data.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your answers to provide the best possible insights.
* Select all options that apply to ensure we have a complete picture of your experiences.
* If you are unsure of an option, select the one that best describes your situation.
