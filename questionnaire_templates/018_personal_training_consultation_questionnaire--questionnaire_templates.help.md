# personal-training-consultation-questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a consultation questionnaire for personal training purposes. It helps our trainers better understand your preferences, goals, and medical history to provide you with the best possible services. Please ensure you answer all questions truthfully, and we will use this information to create a customized plan tailored to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select your preferred session type from the Session Preference dropdown menu.
2.  Enter your name in the Name field.
3.  Provide your contact information in the Contact Info field.
4.  Share any medical conditions you may have in the Medical Conditions field (if any).
5.  Specify your fitness goals in the Goals field.
6.  List any exercise areas you want to improve in the Exercise Areas to Improve field (if any).
7.  If you have any injury history, please indicate in the Injury History field.
8.  Provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact in the Emergency Contact field.
9.  Enter your emergency contact's phone number in the Emergency Phone field.
10.  Finally, read and agree to the terms and conditions in the Consent field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Session Preference**: Choose your preferred type of training session (Minimalist workout, Session 2, Session 3).
*   **Name**: Please enter your name accurately and truthfully.
*   **Contact Info**: Provide your contact information, which may include your address, email, and/or phone number.
*   **Medical Conditions**: If you have any medical conditions, please describe them in this field (e.g., heart condition, diabetes).
*   **Goals**: Clearly state your fitness goals (e.g., lose weight, increase endurance, improve flexibility).
*   **Exercise Areas to Improve**: Specify which exercise areas you want to improve (e.g., cardiovascular, strength, flexibility).
*   **Injury History**: Indicate if you have any previous injuries or concerns related to your current health condition.
*   **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and phone number of a person we can contact in case of an emergency.
*   **Emergency Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
*   **Consent**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of our services, and select the option to confirm your consent.
