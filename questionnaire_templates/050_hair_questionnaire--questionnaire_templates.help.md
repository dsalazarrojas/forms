# Hair Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help our stylists and consultants understand your hair needs, preferences, and requirements. Please answer the questions accordingly, and we will create a personalized plan tailored to your hair type and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all the questions to the best of your ability, using the options and fields provided.
2. Make sure to select relevant options for your hair length, color, type, condition, dryness, and damage.
3. If you're interested in specific products or treatments, type them in the respective fields.
4. Provide the date of your previous visit, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hair Length** (`hair_length`, number, required: false): Please enter your hair length in centimeters or inches.
* **Hair Color** (`hair_color`, text, required: false): Type in your hair color, e.g., black, blonde, brown, etc.
* **Hair Type** (`hair_type`, select_one, required: false): Select from the options: Curly, Straight, Wavy, Other.
* **Hair Condition** (`hair_condition`, text, required: false): Describe your hair condition, e.g., dry, damaged, oily, etc.
* **Dryness** (`hair_dryness`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all that apply: Dry, Normal, Oily.
* **Hair Damage** (`hair_damage`, select_one, required: false): Select from the options: Damaged, Not Damaged.
* **Scalp Condition** (`scalp_condition`, text, required: false): Describe your scalp condition, if any.
* **Product Interest** (`product_interest`, text, required: false): Type in the products you are interested in, e.g., shampoo, conditioner, etc.
* **Treatment Interest** (`treatment_interest`, text, required: false): Type in the treatments you are interested in, e.g., hair color, haircut, etc.
* **Previous Visit** (`previous_visit`, date, required: false): If this is your first visit, leave this field blank. If you have visited before, enter the date of your previous visit.
* **Hair Service** (`hair_service`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all that apply: Haircut, Coloring, Chemical Treatment, Other.
