# Country Entry Health and Travel Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Country Entry Health and Travel Questionnaire is a form designed to collect vital information about your health and travel history. This information is essential for our health and safety protocols and helps us ensure that you are fit to enter and stay in the country without posing any risks to yourself or others.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each field honestly and accurately. If you are unsure about any question, please seek clarification from our staff.
2. Please complete all the fields, even if you are not sure about the answer. We will use this information to assess your eligibility for entry.
3. If you are traveling with companions, please ensure that each of them also completes this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pre Arrival Travel** (`pre_arrival_travel`, text, optional): Please describe your travel plans before arriving in the country, including any flights, boats, or other modes of transportation you plan to use.
* **Health Screening** (`health_screening`, select_multiple, optional): Please indicate if you have any health conditions or allergies that may affect your health while traveling. You can select multiple options if applicable.
	+ 'True': You have a health condition that may affect your health while traveling.
	+ 'False': You do not have any health conditions that may affect your health while traveling.
* **Travel History** (`travel_history`, text, optional): Please provide a detailed account of your travel history, including any recent international travel and any travel restrictions you may be under.
* **Travel Frequency** (`travel_frequency`, select_one, optional): Please select the frequency of your travels:
	+ Daily: You travel daily.
	+ Weekly: You travel weekly.
	+ Monthly: You travel monthly.
	+ Quarterly: You travel quarterly.
	+ Yearly: You travel yearly.
* **Flight Info** (`flight_info`, text, optional): Please provide any additional flight information, such as flight numbers, departure and arrival times, and any layovers or connecting flights.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed as possible when completing this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to our staff.
* If you are traveling with companions, please ensure that each of them also completes this form.

Note: This form is only to be filled by individuals who are new to the country or have had a change in their travel status. If you are unsure, please seek clarification from our staff.
