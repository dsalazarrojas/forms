# Cuban Missile Crisis Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cuban Missile Crisis Quiz is designed to test your knowledge about the Cuban Missile Crisis, a pivotal event in international relations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the provided fields.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all applicable options.
3. For open-ended questions, type your answer in the text field.
4. Fill in your name and email for submission purposes.
5. Review your answers before submitting.

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): A short text field where you can type any information you would like to provide about yourself before starting the quiz.
* **What happened in Cuba?** (`question_1`, `text`, required: false): A text field where you can type your answer to this question.
* **Who were the superpowers involved?** (`question_2`, `text`, required: false): A text field where you can type your answer to this question.
* **What was the main cause of the crisis?** (`question_3`, `text`, required: false): A text field where you can type your answer to this question.
* **What was the outcome?** (`question_4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): A multiple-select field where you can select all applicable options.
	+ Options: 
		- Option 1
		- Option 2
		- Option 3
* **What were the long-term effects?** (`question_5`, `select_one`, required: false): A single-select field where you can select one option.
	+ Options: 
		- Option 1
		- Option 2
		- Option 3
* **Conclusion** (`conclusion`, `note`, required: false): A short text field where you can type any final thoughts or comments.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): A text field for your name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): A field for your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): A text field for your phone number.

Note that all fields are optional, but filling in your name and email is recommended for submission purposes.
