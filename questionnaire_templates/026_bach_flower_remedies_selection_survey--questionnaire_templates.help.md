# Bach Flower Remedies Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information on your mental health and wellness status, enabling the practitioner to create a tailored remedy plan.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions honestly, and choose the options that best describe your current state.
2. If you have any concerns or questions, do not hesitate to reach out to us for support.
3. Ensure you fill out the entire form as accurately as possible to ensure the best outcome.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Please enter your first and last name as you would like to be addressed. This will help the practitioner know you better.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for receiving remedy recommendations and future consultations. This will be used solely for this purpose.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number, if you prefer a phone consultation. This is optional, but it will help with scheduling.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Primary Emotional Concern**: Describe your primary emotional concern that brings you to seek flower remedies.
* **Current Overall Mood**: Select how you would describe your overall mood lately. Choose the option that best fits your current state.
* **Fear or Anxiety Symptoms**: Select any fear or anxiety symptoms that apply to you. Choose as many or as few as necessary.
* **Uncertainty or Indecision Symptoms**: Select any uncertainty or indecision symptoms that apply to you. Choose as many or as few as necessary.
* **Present Mood Issues**: Select any present mood issues that apply to you. Choose as many or as few as necessary.
* **Overconcern for Others**: Select any overconcern for others that apply to you. Choose as many or as few as necessary.
* **Sensitivity to Influence**: Select any sensitivity to influence that apply to you. Choose as many or as few as necessary.
* **Despondency or Despair**: Select any despondency or despair symptoms that apply to you. Choose as many or as few as necessary.
* **Overwork or Exhaustion**: Select any overwork or exhaustion symptoms that apply to you. Choose as many or as few as necessary.
* **Sleep Patterns**: Choose how you would rate your sleep quality.
* **Daily Energy Level**: Rate your daily energy level as per your typical state.
* **Current Stress Level**: Rate your current stress level, from 1 to 10.
* **Previous Flower Remedy Experience**: Indicate if you have used flower remedies before. If yes, specify how regularly.
* **Other Treatments Currently Using**: List any other therapies or medications you are currently using.
* **Wellness Goals**: Describe your wellness goals. What do you hope to achieve.
* **Consultation Preference**: Choose your preferred method of consultation.
* **Additional Notes**: Add any additional information that may help the practitioner understand your situation.

This form is designed to be completed in a clear and concise manner, helping the practitioner provide tailored recommendations for your specific needs. Please follow these guidelines carefully to ensure accurate completion and a successful outcome.
