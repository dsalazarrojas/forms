# Photo Limit Efficiency Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photo Limit Efficiency Quiz is a tool to collect information on your photo management practices. It's designed to help us understand your approach to photo quality and storage efficiency in your work. Please answer the questions honestly, and if you need help, don't hesitate to ask.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully before starting to ensure you understand each field.
2. Answer the questions based on your real experiences and practices.
3. For questions with multiple options, choose the one that best describes your situation.
4. If you're unsure about a field, you can always ask for help or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (1)**: Please provide your basic information, such as your name and email address. This will help us contact you if needed.
* **User Photos (2)**: Describe your current photo storage system, including the number of photos you store and how you manage them.
* **Photo Quality (3)**: Rate the importance of photo quality in your work. For example, do you prioritize high-quality photos over storing more photos?
* **Storage Efficiency (4)**: Choose how you balance photo quality and storage efficiency in your work. Do you aim to store more photos while maintaining quality or prioritize quality over storage capacity?
* **Field of Study or Subject Area (5)**: If you teach or support a specific field of study or subject area, please choose the one that best describes your work.
* **Storage Capacity (6)**: Estimate your average monthly storage capacity, in GB.
* **Average File Size (7)**: Describe the average file size of your photos.
* **Storage Budget (8)**: Estimate your average monthly storage budget for photos.
* **Storage Savings (9)**: Calculate the average storage cost savings per month.
* **Photo Savings (10)**: Rate the importance of photo quality over storage in your work.
* **Time Savings (11)**: Calculate the time savings of storing fewer photos.
* **Comments (12)**: Add any additional comments or suggestions about your photo management practices.
* **Upload (13)**: This field is not applicable for this form. Please ignore it.
* **Submit (14)**: Once you've completed the form, click this button to submit your answers.
* **Cancel (15)**: If you need to stop mid-form, click this button to cancel your progress.
