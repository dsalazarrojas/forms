# Public Service Retirement Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Public Service Retirement Assessment form is designed to gather information about your retirement plans, benefits, and concerns in the public service. This information will help you understand your options and make informed decisions about your retirement planning.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Carefully review each field to ensure you select the relevant information.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked as "required".
3. If a field is not applicable, select "Not Applicable" or "Other (please specify)".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your current salary**: This field asks for your current salary to help us understand your financial situation.
* **What is your retirement planning strategy**: Choose the level of planning you have in place for your retirement. This will help us understand your approach to retirement planning.
* **Employee concerns about retirement**: Select all concerns you have about retirement. This will help us understand areas where we can provide guidance or support.
* **Would you like to schedule a follow-up call or meeting**: If you would like to discuss your results and receive guidance, select "Yes".
* **What benefits do you have available to you**: Select all benefits you have, such as a pension or 401(k) plan.
* **Which of these benefits are you employed by**: Select all the employers who provide your benefits.
* **Years you have worked in public service**: Enter the approximate number of years you have worked in public service.
* **What is your target retirement date**: Enter your target retirement date.
* **What is your current age**: Enter your current age.
* **How many years of service do you have**: Enter the approximate number of years of service you have.
* **Which employer do you work for**: Select all the employers you work for, such as Federal Government or State.
* **What is your job title**: Enter your job title.
* **What department do you work in**: Enter the department you work in.
* **What is your location**: Enter your location.
* **Who is your supervisor**: Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Do you have a retirement plan**: Select "Yes" if you have a retirement plan.
* **Please describe your retirement plan**: Enter a brief description of your retirement plan.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your Employee ID.
* **Employer Name**: Enter your employer's name.
* **What type of retirement plan do you have**: Select all the types of plans you have, such as Defined Benefit or Defined Contribution.
* **What is the approximate number of years of service?**: Enter the approximate number of years of service.
* **Who provides your retirement plan?**: Select all those who provide your retirement plan.
* **Additional comments**: Enter any additional comments.
