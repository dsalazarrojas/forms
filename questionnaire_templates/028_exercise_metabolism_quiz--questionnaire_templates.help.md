# Exercise Metabolism Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exercise Metabolism Quiz form is designed to collect information on various aspects of exercise and metabolism-related topics. This form will help you create a comprehensive quiz for assessing participants' knowledge.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the title and description of your quiz.
2. Choose the category of your quiz.
3. Select the type of assessment (Aerobic, Anaerobic, or Both).
4. Select the types of participants involved (Students, Clients, or Wellness participants).
5. Choose the programs associated with the quiz (Training programs or Educators).
6. Select the type of review (Simple or Detailed).
7. Add any comments or notes regarding the quiz.
8. Upload any relevant files.
9. Enter the email and phone number of the person to contact.
10. Set the date and time of the quiz.
11. Answer the multiple-choice questions (Answer 3, Answer 4, and Answer 5) as per the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exercise Metabolism Quiz Title** (`exercise_metabolism_quiz_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief title for your quiz.
* **Category** (`exercise_metabolism_quiz_category`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose a category for your quiz from the options (Questionnaire Templates or Other).
* **Description** (`exercise_metabolism_quiz_description`, `text`, required: false): Enter a detailed description of your quiz.
* **Assessment** (`exercise_metabolism_quiz_assessment`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the type of assessment (Aerobic, Anaerobic, or Both) for your quiz.
* **Participants** (`exercise_metabolism_quiz_participants`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the types of participants involved (Students, Clients, or Wellness participants).
* **Programs** (`exercise_metabolism_quiz_programs`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the programs associated with the quiz (Training programs or Educators).
* **Review** (`exercise_metabolism_quiz_review`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of review (Simple or Detailed) for your quiz.
* **Comments** (`exercise_metabolism_quiz_comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any comments or notes regarding the quiz.
* **File upload** (`exercise_metabolism_quiz_file_upload`, `text`, required: false): Upload any relevant files related to the quiz.
* **Email** (`exercise_metabolism_quiz_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email of the person to contact.
* **Phone** (`exercise_metabolism_quiz_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person to contact.
* **Note** (`exercise_metabolism_quiz_note`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes.
* **Date** (`exercise_metabolism_quiz_date`, `date`, required: false): Set the date of the quiz.
* **Time** (`exercise_metabolism_quiz_time`, `time`, required: false): Set the time of the quiz.
* **Answer 3** (`exercise_metabolism_quiz_3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Answer the third multiple-choice question (Yes or No).
* **Answer 4** (`exercise_metabolism_quiz_4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Answer the fourth multiple-choice question (Yes or No).
* **Answer 5** (`exercise_metabolism_quiz_5`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Answer the fifth multiple-choice question (Yes or No).

## Tips
