# Triangle Measurement Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Triangle Measurement Survey aims to gather information on user perceptions and preferences about triangles. Your responses will help us understand your views on various aspects of triangles.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form by answering each question to the best of your ability.
2.  Review each page carefully, and select the correct options for each question.
3.  If you have any questions or concerns, refer to the Field-by-Field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What is the shape of the triangle** (page_1, text, optional): Answer this question with a brief description of the shape of the triangle you are thinking of.
*   **Please select a type of triangle** (page_2, select_one, optional): This question asks about the type of triangle you prefer. You can choose from the following options: 
    • Equilateral
    • Isosceles
    • Scalene
*   **Is there a specific type of triangle you are interested in?** (page_3, select_multiple, optional): You can choose multiple types of triangles that interest you. Choose from the following options: 
    • Acute
    • Right
    • Obtuse
*   **What is the color of the first point** (page_4, select_one, optional): Select the color of the first point of the triangle.
*   **What is the color of the second point** (page_5, select_one, optional): Select the color of the second point of the triangle.
*   **What is the color of the third point** (page_6, select_one, optional): Select the color of the third point of the triangle.
*   **How would you rate the triangle?** (page_7, time, optional): This question is likely asking for your time estimate on how long you would spend on the triangle, but since it's a "time" type, it seems to be more about the duration of time you'd spend, which is unusual. If you have any concerns or confusion, please contact the form owner.
*   **How would you rate the triangle on a scale of 0-10** (page_8, number, optional): Rate the triangle from 0 to 10, with 0 being the worst and 10 being the best.
*   **Is there anything else you'd like to add?** (page_9, note, optional): This is a text field for any additional comments or information you'd like to share.
*   **Email address** (page_10, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone number** (page_11, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
