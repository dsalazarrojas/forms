The goal of this form is to gather information from users for research purposes, likely for annotation of images in a scientific or academic context. The form is divided into two main sections: Survey Form, Upload Images, and Annotation Details. Please follow the steps below to complete the form:

## Purpose
The Image Annotation Survey is a tool used to gather information from users for research purposes. It collects metadata and annotations from images to support various studies and investigations. By providing accurate and truthful answers, you will help researchers understand and analyze images better.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Navigate to the "Survey Form" page and start by filling out the provided form. Please provide information as requested and make sure to select the correct options for each field.
2.  Proceed to the "Upload Images" section and select the images you want to annotate. Please make sure you have uploaded the correct images and select the correct options.
3.  In the "Annotation Details" section, please select the correct annotation details for each image.
4.  Review your answers and submit the form once you have completed all fields.
