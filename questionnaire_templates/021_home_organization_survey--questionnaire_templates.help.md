# Home Organization Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the areas of a home that are organized.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form and answer each question based on your current home's organization status.
2. Select your answers for each area, using the options provided for each question.
3. Submit your completed form to help us understand the current state of home organization in your area.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Home Organization Survey** (Page 1, Intro): This is the title of the form, and a brief introduction to its purpose.
* **Living Room** (Select Multiple): This question asks about the number of living rooms in your home that are organized. Select all that apply:
	+ 1: One living room is organized.
	+ 2: Two living rooms are organized.
	+ 3: Three living rooms are organized.
	+ 4: Four living rooms are organized.
	+ 5: Five living rooms are organized.
* **Kitchen** (Select One): This question asks if your kitchen is organized. Select one of the following options:
	+ Yes: Your kitchen is organized.
	+ No: Your kitchen is not organized.
* **Bathroom** (Select Multiple): This question asks about the number of bathrooms in your home that are organized. Select all that apply:
	+ 1: One bathroom is organized.
	+ 2: Two bathrooms are organized.
	+ 3: Three bathrooms are organized.
	+ 4: Four bathrooms are organized.
	+ 5: Five bathrooms are organized.
* **Bedroom** (Select Multiple): This question asks about the number of bedrooms in your home that are organized. Select all that apply:
	+ 1: One bedroom is organized.
	+ 2: Two bedrooms are organized.
	+ 3: Three bedrooms are organized.
	+ 4: Four bedrooms are organized.
	+ 5: Five bedrooms are organized.
* **Office/Home Office** (Select One): This question asks if your home office is organized. Select one of the following options:
	+ Yes: Your home office is organized.
	+ No: Your home office is not organized.
* **Garage** (Select One): This question asks if your garage is organized. Select one of the following options:
	+ Yes: Your garage is organized.
	+ No: Your garage is not organized.
* **Laundry Room** (Select Multiple): This question asks about the number of laundry rooms in your home that are organized. Select all that apply:
	+ 1: One laundry room is organized.
	+ 2: Two laundry rooms are organized.
	+ 3: Three laundry rooms are organized.
	+ 4: Four laundry rooms are organized.
	+ 5: Five laundry rooms are organized.
* **Storage** (Select Multiple): This question asks about the number of storage areas in your home that are organized. Select all that apply:
	+ 1: One storage area is organized.
	+ 2: Two storage areas are organized.
	+ 3: Three storage areas are organized.
	+ 4: Four storage areas are organized.
	+ 5: Five storage areas are organized.
* **Other Areas** (Select Multiple): This question asks about any other areas in your home that are organized. Select all that apply, and provide a brief description of the area in the comments field if necessary:
	+ Yes: Select this option if you have other areas that are organized.
	+ No: Select this option if you do not have other organized areas.
