# learning_experience_personalization_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "learning_experience_personalization_survey" form is designed to gather information about your learning experience, personalization preferences, and schedule availability. This form will help us provide a more personalized experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (*). These are mandatory fields that are necessary to complete the form.
2. Select the options that best suit your preferences for each question.
3. Enter any additional comments or questions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (`form_header`, text, not required): Enter the title of the form.
* **Form Title** (`form_header_title`, text, required*): Enter the title of the form.
* **Form Description** (`form_header_description`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the form.
* **What is your main goal for taking this training course?** (`question1`, select_one, required*):
	+ Choose the option that best describes your main goal for taking this training course.
* **Which learning style best suits you?** (`question2`, select_multiple, required*):
	+ Select all the options that best describe your learning style.
* **What are your learning preferences?** (`question3`, select_one, required*):
	+ Choose the option that best describes your learning preferences.
* **What is your availability to start this course?** (`question4`, select_multiple, required*):
	+ Select all the options that best describe your availability.
* **What type of course do you want to attend?** (`question5`, select_one, required*):
	+ Choose the type of course you are interested in.
* **What type of format would you like to attend?** (`question6`, select_one, required*):
	+ Choose the format you would like to attend.
* **What is your preferred schedule for attending this course?** (`question7`, date, required*):
	+ Enter your preferred schedule for attending the course.
* **What time would you like to attend this course?** (`question8`, time, required*):
	+ Enter the time you would like to attend the course.
* **Additional Information** (`question9`, note, not required): Enter any additional information you would like to provide.
* **How did you hear about this training?** (`question10`, select_one, not required):
	+ Choose how you heard about this training.
* **Email Address** (`question11`, email, required*): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`question12`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **What is your name?** (`question13`, text, required*): Enter your name.
* **What is your department?** (`question14`, select_multiple, required*):
	+ Select all the options that best describe your department.
* **What is your role in the company?** (`question15`, select_one, required*):
	+ Choose your role in the company.
* **Additional Comments** (`question16`, note, not required): Enter any additional comments.
* **Do you have any other comments?** (`question17`, text, not required): Enter any other comments you have.
* **What would you like to learn more about next?** (`question18`, select_multiple, required*):
	+ Select all the options that interest you.
* **Additional Questions** (`question19`, text, not required): Enter any additional questions you have.
* **Do you have any other questions for us?** (`question20`, text, not required): Enter any other questions you have.
* **Would you like to provide a photo of yourself?** (`question21`, select_one, not required):
	+ Choose if you would like to provide a photo.
* **What would you like to do next?** (`question22`, select_one, not required):
	+ Choose what you would like to do next.

Note: The * symbol indicates a required field. Please fill in all required fields to complete the form.
