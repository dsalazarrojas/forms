# Pet Nutrition Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about pet owners' or caregivers' knowledge and practices regarding pet nutrition. It's meant to gauge their understanding of pet care and nutrition.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*):
	* "Note" fields are not required but provide additional context or information.
2. Enter the requested information in the corresponding fields:
	* For "select_one" and "select_multiple" fields, choose one or multiple options as applicable.
	* For "email" and "text" fields, enter the relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Page** (`Main Page`, `text`, not required): This is the introduction page to the survey. It does not require any input.
* **Pet Info** (`Pet Info`, `text`, not required): Provide information about your pet, such as breed, age, and species.
* **Feeding Habits** (`Feeding Habits`, `select_one`, not required): Choose how often you feed your pet:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Nutrition Knowledge** (`Nutrition Knowledge`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select what you know about pet nutrition:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Pet Health** (`Pet Health`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the current health status of your pet:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Feeding Practices** (`Feeding Practices`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select how often you follow pet-specific feeding practices:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Nutrition Importance** (`Nutrition Importance`, `select_one`, not required): Rate the importance of nutrition for your pet:
	+ Very Important
	+ Not Important
* **Pet Care** (`Pet Care`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select your pet care habits:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Survey Completion** (`Survey Completion`, `select_one`, not required): Confirm that you've completed the survey:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Note** (`Note`, `text`, not required): Provide any additional comments or information about your pet or experience.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address for follow-up contact.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number for follow-up contact.
