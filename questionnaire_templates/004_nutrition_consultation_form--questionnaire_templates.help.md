# Nutrition Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Nutrition Consultation Form" is used to document and track patient consultations and nutritional assessments. It helps healthcare professionals gather essential information about patient consultations, including patient details, medical conditions, and follow-up plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill in the consultation details section (page 1) with the relevant information.
3. In the patient info section (page 2), provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
4. In the patient nutritional status section (page 3), select the correct status from the options provided.
5. In the nutritional goals section (page 4), choose whether the patient has nutritional goals or not.
6. In the medical conditions section (page 5), provide any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
7. In the medications section (page 6), select any medications the patient is currently taking.
8. In the follow-up section (page 7), add any follow-up plans or instructions for the patient.
9. In the contact info section (page 8), provide the patient's contact information.
10. In the healthcare provider section (page 9), indicate if a healthcare provider is involved in the patient's care.
11. Finally, in the date visited section (page 10), enter the date of the last consultation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consultation details** (page 1): This section is for providing brief details about the consultation.
* **Patient info** (page 2): This section is for entering your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Patient nutritional status** (page 3): Select the correct status from the options provided to indicate the patient's current nutritional status.
* **Nutritional goals** (page 4): Choose whether the patient has nutritional goals or not.
* **Medical conditions** (page 5): Provide any relevant medical conditions or allergies the patient may have.
* **Medications** (page 6): Select any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Follow-up** (page 7): Add any follow-up plans or instructions for the patient.
* **Contact info** (page 8): Provide the patient's contact information.
* **Healthcare provider** (page 9): Indicate if a healthcare provider is involved in the patient's care.
* **Date visited** (page 10): Enter the date of the last consultation.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before proceeding.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information provided.
* If you are unsure about any section, please refer to your healthcare provider for guidance.
