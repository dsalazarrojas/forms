# Air Force Qualification Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Air Force Qualification Questionnaire is a comprehensive form designed to assess an individual's qualifications for a position within the Air Force. This guide will walk you through each section of the form to ensure a smooth and accurate completion process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please begin by filling out the personal information section, which includes your full legal name and age.
2. Next, select your citizenship status and proof required.
3. Move on to the education section, where you will select your highest education level completed or in progress.
4. If applicable, enter your previous ASVAB score.
5. Answer the physical standards questions, including your height and weight.
6. Disclose any medical conditions or issues you may have.
7. Indicate whether you have any tattoos on your face, neck, or hands.
8. Finally, disclose your criminal and drug use history.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Applicant Name (Required: True)
* **Applicant Name**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 2. Age (Required: True)
* **Age**: Enter your current age in numerical format.

### 3. Citizenship Status (Required: True)
* **Citizenship Status**: Select "U.S. Citizen", "Permanent Resident (Green Card)", or "Non-Citizen" to indicate your citizenship status. Proof may be required.

### 4. Highest Education Level (Required: True)
* **Highest Education Level**: Choose your highest level of education completed or in progress, such as "High School Diploma", "GED", "Some College", "Associate's Degree", "Bachelor's Degree", or "Master's Degree or higher".

### 5. ASVAB Score (Optional: False)
* **ASVAB Score**: If you have taken the ASVAB previously, enter your score (0-99).

### 6. Height (Required: True)
* **Height (inches)**: Enter your height in inches, e.g. 70.

### 7. Weight (Required: True)
* **Weight (lbs)**: Enter your weight in pounds, e.g. 175.

### 8. Medical Conditions (Required: True)
* **Medical Conditions**: Disclose any chronic health issues you may have, such as asthma or heart conditions.

### 9. Medical Explanation (Optional: False)
* **Medical Explanation**: If you have disclosed any medical conditions, provide a brief explanation.

### 10. Tattoos (Required: True)
* **Tattoos**: Indicate whether you have any tattoos on your face, neck, or hands.

### 11. Criminal Record (Required: True)
* **Criminal Record**: Answer "True" if you have been arrested or charged.

### 12. Drug Use History (Required: True)
* **Drug Use History**: Answer "True" if you have used illegal drugs in the past.
