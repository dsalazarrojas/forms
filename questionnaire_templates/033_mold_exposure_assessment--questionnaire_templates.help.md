# Mold Exposure Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mold Exposure Assessment form is designed to collect relevant information about a specific property to help assess the level of mold exposure present. This form is intended to be used by occupants, property owners, or inspectors to provide a comprehensive picture of the property's condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form in sections**: Break down your responses into each section, starting from the Property Details section.
2. **Provide accurate information**: Answer each question truthfully and provide as much detail as possible.
3. **Submit your responses**: Once you've completed all sections, review and submit the form for further analysis.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Property Details**
 Property Details: 
 * (Optional) A text field for providing property-specific information

* **Label:** Property Details
* **Type:** Text
* **Required:** No
* **Description:** In this section, you can provide any relevant property information that may be useful for the assessment. This can include the address, type of building, or any notable features about the property.

### 2. **Occupant Concerns**
 Occupant Concerns: 
 * (Optional) A text field for expressing concerns or observations made by the occupant

* **Label:** Occupant Concerns
* **Type:** Text
* **Required:** No
* **Description:** If you have any personal concerns or observations about the property's condition, this is where you can express them. This can include any physical symptoms or health issues you've experienced.

### 3. **Inspection Report**
Inspection Report: 
 * (Optional) A free-form text field for writing notes and observations

* **Label:** Inspection Report
* **Type:** Note
* **Required:** No
* **Description:** Use this section to record any observations or findings from a property inspection. Describe any issues, defects, or notable points about the property's condition.

### 4. **Property Inspection Details**
 Property Inspection Details: 
 * (Optional) A text field for providing more detailed property information

* **Label:** Property Inspection Details
* **Type:** Text
* **Required:** No
* **Description:** If you have more detailed information about the property's condition, this is the place to provide it. This can include specifics about the property's layout, systems, or equipment.

## Tips
* **Be thorough but concise**: Provide as much information as you can while keeping your answers clear and concise.
* **Check for accuracy**: Double-check your responses for accuracy before submitting the form.
* **Use proper formatting**: If providing text responses, use proper punctuation and capitalization for clarity.
