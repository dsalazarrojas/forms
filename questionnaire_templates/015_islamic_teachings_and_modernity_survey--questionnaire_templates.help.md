# Islamic Teachings And Modernity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about the relevance and duplication of specific Islamic teachings and modernity-related topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that best fit your responses and provide clear and concise answers in the text fields. If you have more to say, don't hesitate to elaborate. Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as `required` in the form).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Title (form_title, text, optional)
This field is the title of your response to this form. Please enter a brief and descriptive title that summarizes your feedback.

### Category (category, select_one, optional)
This field asks if the selected Islamic teaching is relevant or modern. Please select 'Yes' if you believe the teaching is relevant and modern, or 'No' if you don't think it is.
