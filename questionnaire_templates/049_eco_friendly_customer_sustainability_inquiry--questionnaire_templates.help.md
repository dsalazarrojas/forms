## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Sustainability Interest** (`customer_sustainability_interest`, select_multiple, required: false): 
	+ Please select all the types of sustainability interest you have (e.g., environmental, social, economic).
* **Sustainability Priority** (`sustainability_priority`, select_one, required: false): 
	+ Select the priority level of your sustainability interest (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
* **Program Interest** (`program_interest`, select_one, required: false): 
	+ Please select if you are interested in our sustainability programs.
* **Customer Sustainability Feedback** (`customer_sustainability_feedback`, text, required: false): 
	+ Type any feedback you have about our sustainability initiatives.
* **Follow-up** (`easy_follow_up`, select_multiple, required: false): 
	+ How would you like to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions? (e.g., email, phone, in-person).
* **Brands, Programs, Partners** (`brands_programs_partners`, select_one, required: false): 
	+ Are you interested in our brands, programs, or partners?
* **Ongoing Data Collection** (`ongoing_data_collection`, select_multiple, required: false): 
	+ Would you like us to continuously collect data from you?
* **Service Teams** (`service_teams`, select_multiple, required: false): 
	+ Are you currently working with any of our service teams?

Note: The form has been simplified to remove duplicate fields and improve clarity. The field-by-field explanations are concise and provide clear guidance for customers to complete the form.
