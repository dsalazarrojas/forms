# Remote Work Conditions Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the work conditions and conditions of employees who are working remotely. It is meant to be completed by employees who are working outside of the office environment and helps to identify the strengths and areas for improvement of the remote work set-up. This form is typically used to ensure that remote work arrangements are conducive to productivity, collaboration, and overall well-being of employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for each section to describe your current remote work conditions.
2. Please be as specific as possible when providing your feedback and comments.
3. If you are unsure about any of the options, please ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Setup and Tools** (`1`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Please select the tools and resources provided to you while working remotely.
	+ Provided: You have access to all necessary tools and resources required for your work.
	+ Not Provided: You do not have access to necessary tools and resources or are unsure about what resources are available.
* **Work Arrangement** (`2`, `select_one`, **required**): Describe the current work arrangement in place for your remote work set-up.
	+ Conducive: Your work arrangement is suitable for productivity and work satisfaction.
	+ Not Conducive: Your work arrangement is not suitable for productivity and work satisfaction.
* **Collaboration and Communication** (`3`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select the level of collaboration and communication you experience while working remotely.
	+ Good: You experience good collaboration and communication with colleagues and team members.
	+ Fair: You experience moderate collaboration and communication with colleagues and team members.
	+ Poor: You experience poor collaboration and communication with colleagues and team members.
* **Productivity** (`4`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your current productivity level while working remotely.
	+ High: You are able to meet your work goals and deadlines.
	+ Medium: You are able to meet some of your work goals and deadlines.
	+ Low: You are not able to meet some of your work goals and deadlines.
