# Modular Home Design Selections - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information on users' preferences for a modular home design. It helps us understand their needs and provide a personalized experience.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select the options that best match your preferences for each field.
3. Make sure to enter your phone number and email address correctly.
4. Date and time fields should be in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD and HH:MM AM/PM, respectively).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Page 1**: This is the first page of the form. You can leave this field blank if not applicable.
- **Page 2** (*): Enter the number of bedrooms in your new home.
- **Home Design Selections** (*): Select all the design preferences that apply to you.
  - This field allows you to choose multiple design options.
  - Options include Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3.
- **Floor Plan Type** (*): Choose the type of floor plan you prefer.
  - Options include Floor Plan 1, Floor Plan 2, and Floor Plan 3.
- **Number of Bedrooms** (*): Enter the number of bedrooms you need in your new home.
- **Number of Bathrooms** (*): Select whether you need a bathroom in your new home.
  - Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
- **Square Footage**: This field is not required, but it's a good place to provide any additional details about the square footage of your new home.
- **Design Preference** (not required): Select all the design preferences that apply to you.
  - Options include 'Yes' and 'No'.
- **Phone Number** (*): Enter your phone number for further contact.
- **Email Address** (*): Enter your email address for further contact.
- **Date** (*): Enter the date you are interested in moving into your new home.
  - Format should be YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Time** (*): Enter the time you are interested in moving into your new home.
  - Format should be HH:MM AM/PM.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your entries before submitting the form.
- If you have any special requests or questions, please contact us.
- We will be in touch with you soon after submission to confirm your selections.
