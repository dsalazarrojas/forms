# CT Imaging Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The CT Imaging Knowledge Assessment form is designed to evaluate the knowledge and understanding of CT imaging professionals, including technologists, radiologists, and nursing staff, on various aspects of CT imaging. This form assesses their knowledge on CT physics, protocols, contrast agents, radiation safety, and other relevant topics.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form carefully**: Please answer each question to the best of your knowledge.
2. **Select the correct options**: For multiple-choice questions, select the most accurate option.
3. **Provide clear explanations**: For text fields, provide clear and concise explanations.
4. **Complete all required fields**: Ensure you answer all questions that are marked as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your professional ID or badge.
* **Current Role**: Select your current role related to CT imaging (e.g., CT Technologist, Radiology Resident, Nursing Staff, Imaging Assistant, Student).
* **Years of Experience in CT**: Enter the number of years you have worked in CT imaging.
* **Hounsfield Units**: Select the description that best represents what a Hounsfield Unit is in CT imaging:
	+ Electron Density
	+ Radiodensity
	+ Physical Density
	+ Attenuation Coefficient relative to water
* **Common Artifact Identification**: Select the common artifact caused by metallic implants:
	+ Beam Hardening
	+ Partial Volume
	+ Motion Blur
	+ Ring Artifact
* **Understanding Dose Modulation**: Explain the purpose of tube current modulation in CT imaging.
* **Major Contraindications for Iodinated Contrast**: Select all contraindications that apply:
	+ Severe Renal Impairment
	+ History of Anaphylaxis
	+ Seafood Allergy
	+ Recent Barium Study
* **Minimum GFR for Standard Contrast Administration**: Enter the minimum GFR value according to current ACR guidelines.
* **Scan Delay for Portal Vein Phase**: Select the delay in seconds post-injection for the portal vein phase:
	+ 15-20
	+ 25-35
	+ 60-70
	+ 90-120
* **Radiation Safety**: Describe the ALARA principle in daily practice.
* **Effectiveness of Lead Shielding in Modern CT**: Select the effectiveness of lead shielding in CT imaging:
	+ Highly Effective
	+ Minimally Effective
	+ No longer recommended for routine scans
* **Assessment Completion Date**: Enter the date you completed this assessment.
* **Submission Acknowledgement**: Confirm that you completed this assessment without assistance by selecting True or False.
