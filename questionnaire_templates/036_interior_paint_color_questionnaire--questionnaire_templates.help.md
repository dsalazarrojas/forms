# Interior Paint Color Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for an interior paint color questionnaire.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by introducing yourself and the room you are painting.
2. Select your budget for the project.
3. Indicate your color preferences (Light, Dark, or Neutral).
4. Choose the type of paint you want to use (Latex, Acrylic, Oil-Based, or Water-Based).
5. Select the color scheme you prefer (Monochromatic, Complimentary, Analogous, or Triadic).
6. Enter the color schemes you would like to use for your walls.
7. Enter any additional details about the room.
8. Select the layout of the room.
9. Enter the brand of paint you prefer (Sherwin-Williams, Benjamin-Moore, Behr, or Farrow-Bruck).
10. Choose the finish of the paint (Flat, Semi-Gloss, Eggshell, Satin, Matte, Texturized, or High-Gloss).
11. Select any additional color schemes.
12. Enter the color of the paint you would like to use.
13. Enter the type of wall you are painting (e.g., drywall, plaster, etc.).
14. Enter the layout of the wall.
15. Enter the color of the wall (optional).
16. Select any additional color schemes.
