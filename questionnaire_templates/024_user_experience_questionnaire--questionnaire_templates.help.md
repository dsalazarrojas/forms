# user_experience_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **user_experience_questionnaire** form is designed to gather information about the user's experience with a product or service. This form is used to collect feedback and ratings from users to help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of product or service you are rating (e.g., Product or Service).
2. Provide a brief description of your experience with the product or service.
3. Rate your overall experience (optional).
4. Enter your contact information (email and phone number, optional).
5. Add any additional feedback or notes (optional).
6. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Or Service** (`product_or_service`, select_one, required): Select the type of product or service you are rating. This field is required to provide context for your feedback.
* **Product Or Service Name** (`product_or_service_name`, text, optional): If you selected "Product" in the previous field, enter the name of the product.
* **User Experience Rating** (`user_experience_rating`, number, optional): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your experience with the product or service.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Add any additional feedback or notes you would like to share.
* **Contact** (`contact`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your experience.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your experience.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your experience.
* **Product Or Service Type** (`product_or_service_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of product or service you are rating.
* **User Experience Rating Scale** (`user_experience_rating_scale`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your experience (optional).
* **Overall Experience Rating** (`overall_experience_rating`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your overall experience (optional).
