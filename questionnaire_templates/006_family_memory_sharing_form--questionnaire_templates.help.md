# Family Memory Sharing Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Family Memory Sharing Form is designed to collect information about family members, including their names, relationships, contact details, and other relevant information. This form is used to share memories, stories, and media files within a family, making it easier to connect and keep track of family members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the name of the family member you are sharing a memory about.
2. Indicate the relationship you share with the head of household (e.g., Parent, Spouse, Child, Other).
3. Enter the name of the head of household.
4. Share a story about the family member.
5. Select any media files (photo, video, or audio) that are relevant to the memory you want to share.
6. Enter the email and phone number of the family member (if applicable).
7. Enter the date of birth of the family member and the head of household (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Member Name**: Enter the name of the family member you are sharing a memory about.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: Provide the name of the family member.
* **Relationship To Head Of Household**: Select how you are related to the head of household (e.g., Parent, Spouse, Child, Other).
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Options: Parent, Spouse, Child, Other
* **Name Of Head Of Household**: Enter the full name of the head of household.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: Provide the name of the head of household.
* **Story**: Share a memory or story about the family member.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: Share a memory or story about the family member.
* **Media Files**: Select any media files (photo, video, or audio) you want to attach to the memory.
	+ Type: Select Multiple
	+ Options: Photo, Video, Audio
* **Family Member Email**: Enter the email address of the family member (if applicable).
	+ Type: Email
	+ Purpose: Provide the email address of the family member.
* **Family Member Phone**: Enter the phone number of the family member (if applicable).
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: Provide the phone number of the family member.
* **Head Of Household Phone**: Enter the phone number of the head of household (if applicable).
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: Provide the phone number of the head of household.
* **Family Member DOB**: Enter the date of birth of the family member (if applicable).
	+ Type: Date
	+ Purpose: Provide the date of birth of the family member.
* **Head Of Household DOB**: Enter the date of birth of the head of household (if applicable).
	+ Type: Date
	+ Purpose: Provide the date of birth of the head of household.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that the memory is correctly attributed to the family member.
* Use media files to attach relevant photos, videos, or audio recordings to make the memory more engaging.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the head of household for clarification.
