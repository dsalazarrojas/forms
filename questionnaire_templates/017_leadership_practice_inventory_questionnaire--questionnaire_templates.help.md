# leadership_practice_inventory_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The leadership practice inventory questionnaire is a self-evaluation tool used to assess an individual's leadership skills and practices. It is intended to help individuals identify their strengths and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question on the first page of the form, "practice_inventory_questions", by selecting all that apply from the provided options.
2. For each question answered, provide a brief explanation or description in the corresponding text field (e.g., "question1_answer", "question2_answer", etc.).
3. Select a score for your overall leadership score, "overall_leadership_score", on the scale of 1-10.
4. Choose a level of leadership practice, "leadership_practice_level", that best describes your current leadership habits, from "low", "moderate", or "high".
5. Add any additional comments about your leadership practices and areas for improvement in the "practice_inventory_comments" field.

* **practice_inventory_comments** (`note`): Add any additional comments or thoughts about your leadership practices and areas for improvement.
