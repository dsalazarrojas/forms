# invasive_plant_removal_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The invasive_plant_removal_survey form is used to collect data on invasive plant removal efforts, including site details, species list, removal effort, post-removal outcomes, photos, and contact information. This form is intended to help surveyors collect data in a structured and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling out the **Site Details** field with information about the removal site.
2. In the **Species List** field, select all species that were removed during the invasive plant removal effort.
3. Enter the amount of **Removal Effort** required to remove the invasive plants.
4. In the **Post Removal Outcomes** field, select whether the post-removal outcomes were successful or not.
5. If applicable, add up to 5 photos of the removal site or effort in the **Photos** field.
6. Enter the **Removal Team** responsible for the effort in the **Removal Team** field.
7. Record the date and time of the removal effort in the **Removal Date** and **Start Time** fields.
8. Enter the duration of the removal effort in the **Removal Effort Duration** field.
9. Record the distance of the removal site from a reference point in the **Removal Distance** field.
10. Enter the number of species removed during the effort in the **Species Count** field.
11. Record the area of the removal site in the **Removal Area** field.
12. In the **Removed Species** field, select all species that were removed during the effort.
13. Enter any additional removal notes in the **Removal Notes** field.
14. In the **Follow-up Actions** field, enter any follow-up actions planned for the site.
15. Enter contact information, including the surveyor's name, contact person's name, phone number, and email address in the **Surveyor**, **Contact Person**, **Contact Phone**, and **Contact Email** fields.
16. Finally, select the **Survey Status** to indicate whether the survey is active or inactive.

* **Site Details (2)**: Enter information about the removal site, including its location and any other relevant details.

* **End Time (13)**: Record the end time of the removal effort.

* **Removal Area (17)**: Record the area of the removal site.
* **Removed Species (18)**: Select all species that were removed during the effort.
