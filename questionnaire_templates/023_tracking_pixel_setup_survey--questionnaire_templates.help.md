# Tracking Pixel Setup Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for tracking pixel setup. The purpose of this form is to help us better understand how you use tracking pixels on your website or agency. Your responses will help us improve our services and ensure accurate tracking pixel setup.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
2. Fill out the form completely, as we need all the information provided.
3. If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask us for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: (`user_name`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for your name and contact information.
* **Agency**: (`agency`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for your agency or company name, so we can associate your responses with your organization.
* **Website URL**: (`website_url`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for your website's URL or the URL of your agency's website.
* **Tracking Pixel ID**: (`tracking_pixel_id`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for the tracking pixel ID, a unique identifier for your tracking pixel setup.
* **Tracking Pixel Status**: (`tracking_pixel_status`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This field allows you to select the status of your tracking pixel setup: enabled, disabled, or pending.
* **Tracking Pixel Type**: (`tracking_pixel_type`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This field allows you to select the type of tracking pixel you are using: Google, Facebook, or Google Analytics.
* **Tracking Pixel Setup**: (`tracking_pixel_setup`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for any additional setup information for your tracking pixel.
* **Tracking Pixel Setup Details**: (`tracking_pixel_setup_details`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for any additional details related to your tracking pixel setup.
* **Analytics Code**: (`analytics_code`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for the analytics code associated with your tracking pixel setup.
* **Tracking Code**: (`tracking_code`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for the tracking code associated with your tracking pixel setup.
* **Tracking Pixel Token**: (`tracking_pixel_token`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for the tracking pixel token, a unique identifier for your tracking pixel setup.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
* Your input will help us improve our services and provide better support for tracking pixel setup.
