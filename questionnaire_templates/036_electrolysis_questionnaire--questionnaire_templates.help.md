# Electrolysis Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This questionnaire is designed to gather information about a client's electrology treatment. The purpose of this form is to collect details about the client's skin condition, treatment type, and other relevant medical information. Please only fill out this form if you are a healthcare professional or have been authorized to do so by a healthcare professional.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information.
2. Select the correct options from the drop-down menus where applicable.
3. Enter any additional comments or notes in the relevant text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter the client's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Treatment Date** (`treatment_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the treatment.
* **Treatment Type** (`treatment_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of treatment the client received, such as 'Electrolysis', 'Chemical', or 'Facial'.
* **Skin Condition** (`skin_condition`, select_multiple, optional): Select all skin conditions that apply, such as 'Acne', 'Fine lines and wrinkles', or 'Rosacea'.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Enter any relevant medical conditions that may impact the treatment.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Enter any relevant medical history that may impact the treatment.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_one, optional): If the client has any allergies, select 'True'. Otherwise, select 'False'.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, optional): If the client has given consent for the treatment, select 'True'. Otherwise, select 'False'.
* **Medical Supervision** (`medical_supervision`, select_one, optional): If the client requires medical supervision, select 'True'. Otherwise, select 'False'.
* **Medical Supervision Reason** (`medical_supervision_reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for the medical supervision.
* **Medical Supervision Note** (`medical_supervision_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the medical supervision.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter the client's contact information.
* **Medical Contact** (`medical_contact`, select_one, optional): Select the medical contact, such as 'Doctor', 'Nurse', or 'Dentist'.
* **Medical Contact Phone** (`medical_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the medical contact's phone number.
