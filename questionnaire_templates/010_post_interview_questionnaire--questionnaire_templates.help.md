# Post Interview Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about you after an interview, to help us better understand your qualifications and suitability for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "What is your first name?" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "What is your last name?" field.
3. Provide your address line 1 in the "What is your address line 1?" field.
4. Provide your address line 2 in the "What is your address line 2?" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "What is your email?" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "What is your phone number?" field.
7. Upload a CV file in the "Please upload your CV file" field.
8. Enter a brief motivation statement in the "Why are you interested in this opportunity?" field.
9. Select one or more hobbies that apply to you in the "What are your hobbies?" field.
10. Choose your highest level of education completed in the "What is your highest level of education completed?" field.
11. Enter a brief description of your previous work experience in the "What is your previous work experience in this field?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your first name?** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **What is your last name?** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **What is your address line 1?** (`address_line_1`, text, required): Enter your physical address line 1.
* **What is your address line 2?** (`address_line_2`, text, required): Enter your physical address line 2.
* **What is your email?** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone`, text, required): Enter a valid phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Please upload your CV file** (`cv_file`, text, required): Upload a file with your CV in PDF or docx format.
* **Why are you interested in this opportunity?** (`motivation`, note, required): Enter a brief statement explaining why you're interested in this opportunity.
* **What are your hobbies?** (`hobby`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more hobbies that apply to you.
* **What is your highest level of education completed?** (`education`, select_one, required): Choose the highest level of education you've completed.
* **What is your previous work experience in this field?** (`experience`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of your previous experience. (This field is not required, but will be taken into consideration during the evaluation process.)
