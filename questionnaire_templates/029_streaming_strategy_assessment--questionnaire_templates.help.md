# Streaming Strategy Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Streaming Strategy Assessment form is a tool designed to help streamers reflect on their current streaming practices and goals. It is meant to be a safe and non-judgmental space for streamers to share their experiences and insights. This form will help you assess your streaming strategy and provide suggestions for improvement. Please complete the form with the intention of self-reflection and growth.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Use the options provided as a guide, but feel free to add any additional information that you think is relevant.
3. Take breaks if needed, but try to complete the form within 5-10 minutes.
4. Read through each question carefully and do not skip any.
5. Answer in a way that makes sense to you, even if the question seems unclear or too broad.
