# Military Intelligence Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from military personnel to better understand their training needs and preferences. Please fill out this form to help us improve our training programs and resources.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Enter your name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your military ID number in the "Military ID" field.
5. Select your rank from the "Rank" field.
6. Select your department from the "Department" field.
7. Enter the number of years you have served in the "Years of Service" field.
8. Select your confidence level from the "Confidence Level" field.
9. Select your military branch from the "Military Branch" field.
10. Select your military position from the "Military Position" field.
11. (Optional) Select the training programs you have completed from the "Training Programs" field.
12. Select the date you submitted this form in the "Submitted On" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter your name**: This field is used to store your name.
* **Email**: This field is used to store your email address.
* **Phone**: This field is used to store your phone number.
* **Military ID**: This field is used to store your military ID number.
* **Rank**: This field is used to store your military rank (e.g. Private, Officer, etc.).
* **Department**: This field is used to store your military department (e.g. Army, Navy, etc.).
* **Years of Service**: This field is used to store the number of years you have served.
* **Confidence Level**: This field is used to store your confidence level in your training and knowledge (e.g. High, Medium, Low).
* **Military Branch**: This field is used to store your military branch (e.g. Army, Navy, etc.).
* **Military Position**: This field is used to store your military position (e.g. Officer, NCO, Enlisted).
* **Training Programs**: (Optional) This field is used to store the training programs you have completed.
* **Submitted On**: This field is used to store the date you submitted this form.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the explanations above.
* If you have any questions or issues, please contact us for assistance.
