# Customer Success Story Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect customer success stories, which are used to showcase examples of how our customers have achieved their goals using our product or service.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Company Name field with the name of your company.
2. Enter your name in the Customer Name field.
3. If applicable, fill out the Story Start Date fields (Page 3 and 4).
4. Enter the location of your company in the Company Location field.
5. Select the number of employees in your company in the Company Size field.
6. Choose the type of your company in the Company Type field.
7. Fill out the Story Start Date Time field (Page 8) if necessary.
8. Select the number of people in your Customer Success Team field.
9. Choose the status of your story in the Story Status field.
10. Select the type of your story in the Story Type field.
11. Enter the category of your story in the Story Category field.
12. Fill out the Customer Success Manager field.
13. Enter any additional information about your story in the Story Start Date Range, Story End Date Range, Story Start Date Time, Story End Date Time, Story Start Date Time Zone, and Story End Date Time Zone fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name.
* **Story Start Date** (`story_start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when your story started (if applicable).
* **Company Location** (`company_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of your company.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your job title (if applicable).
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional): Enter your department (if applicable).
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Less than 10 employees
	+ 10-50 employees
	+ 51-100 employees
	+ More than 100 employees
* **Company Type** (`company_type`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Private
	+ Public
	+ Non-Profit
	+ Government Agency
* **Story Start Date Range** (`story_start_date_range`, `text`, optional): Enter a range of dates for when your story started (if necessary).
* **Employee Relationship** (`employee_relationship`, `text`, optional): Enter any details about your relationship with your employees (if applicable).
* **Story End Date** (`story_end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when your story ended (if applicable).
* **Story End Date Range** (`story_end_date_range`, `text`, optional): Enter a range of dates for when your story ended (if necessary).
* **Customer Success Team** (`customer_success_team`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ No one
	+ 1-5 people
	+ 6-10 people
	+ More than 10 people
* **Story Status** (`story_status`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Active
	+ Draft
	+ Cancelled
	+ Completed
* **Story Type** (`story_type`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Customer Success Story
	+ Other
* **Story Category** (`story_category`, `text`, optional): Enter the category of your story (if applicable).
* **Customer Success Manager** (`customer_success_manager`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your customer success manager (if applicable).
* **Story Start Date Time** (`story_start_date_time`, `date`, optional): Enter the start date and time of your story (if necessary).
* **Story End Date Time** (`story_end_date_time`, `date`, optional): Enter the end date and time of your story (if necessary).
* **Story Start Date Time Range** (`story_start_date_time_range`, `text`, optional): Enter a range of dates and times for when your story started (if necessary).
* **Story End Date Time Range** (`story_end_date_time_range`, `text`, optional): Enter a range of dates and times for when your story ended (if necessary).
* **Story Start Date Time Zone** (`story_start_date_time_zone`, `text`, optional): Enter the time zone of your story's start date (if necessary).
* **Story End Date Time Zone** (`story_end_date_time_zone`, `text`, optional): Enter the time zone of your story's end date (if necessary).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out each field carefully, as they are all connected and dependent on each other.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
* Please keep in mind that we review each story for completeness and accuracy before publishing it.

Please note that this help guide is based on the provided YAML fields and is subject to change as the form structure changes. If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please don't hesitate to contact us.
