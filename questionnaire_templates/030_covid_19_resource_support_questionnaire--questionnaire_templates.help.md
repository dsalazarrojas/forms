# COVID 19 Resource Support Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This questionnaire is designed to help us understand the needs and interests of households with youth in our community, in order to provide effective resource support during the COVID-19 pandemic. It will ask you to provide information about your household's current support level, resources needed, technology access, and interests in various services, including remote tutoring, food distribution programs, and mental health resources. Your participation is crucial in helping us tailor our support to your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant's full name.
2. Answer questions about your current level of community support and the resources you need.
3. Indicate your technology access and interests in remote tutoring, local food distribution programs, and mental health resources.
4. Select a preferred time for resource pickup or delivery.
5. Share any suggestions on how we can better support your community.
6. Enter your zip code to help us map resource needs in your area.
7. Confirm your consent to share data with local partner organizations for support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Participant Full Name** (`participant_name_full`): Please enter your participant's full name.
  
- **Youth Program Name** (`youth_program_name`): Indicate the name of the youth program your participant is involved in, e.g., After School Club, Summer Camp.
  
- **Current Level of Community Support** (`support_level_cat`): Select how supported you feel your household is at this time:
  * Fully supported
  * Somewhat supported
  * Neutral
  * Lacking support
  * Critical need

- **Resources Needed** (`resources_needed_list`): Select all resources your household currently needs:
  * Educational materials
  * Food/Groceries
  * Hygiene products
  * Mask/Sanitizer
  * Emotional support
  * Childcare information

- **Technology Access for Youth** (`tech_access_cat`): Indicate your youth's technology access:
  * Yes - Dedicated device
  * Yes - Shared device
  * No device
  * No internet access

- **Interest in Remote Tutoring** (`tutoring_interest_yn`): Select how interested you are in remote tutoring for your youth:
  * Very interested
  * Interested
  * Not at this time

- **Interest in Local Food Distribution** (`food_assistance_interest`): Select if you're interested in local food distribution programs for your household:
  * True
  * False

- **Interest in Mental Health Resources** (`mental_health_interest`): Select if you're interested in youth mental health resources:
  * True
  * False

- **Preferred Resource Pickup Time** (`pickup_time_pref`): Select a preferred time for resource pickup or delivery:
  * Weekday Mornings
  * Weekday Afternoons
  * Saturday Mornings

- **Suggestions for Community Support** (`community_suggestion_text`): Share any suggestions on how we can better support your community.

- **Zip Code** (`zip_code_val`): Enter your zip code to help us map resource needs in your area.

- **Consent to Data Sharing** (`info_sharing_consent`): Confirm your consent to share your data with local partner organizations for support:
  * True
  * I consent
