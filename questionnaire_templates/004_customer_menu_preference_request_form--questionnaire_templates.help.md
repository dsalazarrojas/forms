# Customer Menu Preference Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a customer's menu preferences for an event. Please fill it out to inform us of your dietary restrictions, preferred menu options, and any other special requests you may have. This will help us provide a personalized experience for you and your guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. On the first page, you will be asked to provide your name and a brief description of the event.
2. On the second page, select your dietary restrictions and preferred menu options.
3. On the third page, provide any special requests or comments you may have.
4. Finally, please enter your contact information so we can follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_one, required): Select your dietary restrictions from the list to inform us about any food allergies or sensitivities you may have. Please choose one of the following options:
	* None
	* No specific restrictions
	* Food allergy
	* Vegetarian
	* Vegan
	* Gluten-free
	* Low-carb
	* Low-calorie
- **Preferred Menu** (`preferred_menu`, select_one, required): Select your preferred menu option from the list. This will help us tailor our offerings to suit your tastes. Please choose one of the following:
	* None
	* Breakfast
	* Lunch
	* Dinner
	* Snack
- **Preferred Cuisine** (`preferred_cuisine`, select_one, required): Select your preferred cuisine from the list. This will help us understand your culinary preferences. Please choose one of the following:
	* None
	* Italian
	* Mexican
	* Chinese
	* Indian
	* Japanese
	* American

Note to the user: This form is designed to provide a personalized experience for you and your guests. Please be as specific as possible when answering the questions, and do not hesitate to add any additional comments or requests in the "Customer Comments" field. We value your feedback and look forward to accommodating your preferences.
