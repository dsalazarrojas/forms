# Oral History Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Oral History Interview Form is a standardized guide to collect personal histories and experiences from individuals or groups through a series of questions. This form is intended for internal documentation and research purposes, not for formal or official records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Read the form carefully**: Review each section and field carefully before starting to answer.
2. **Fill out the form**: Provide detailed and accurate answers to each question.
3. **Be truthful and honest**: Answer questions truthfully and honestly, without bias or distortion.
4. **Use your own voice**: Answer in your own words, without influence or prompting from others.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Introduction
* **Introduction**: A brief statement or explanation of the purpose and goals of the interview.
*   This is the initial statement that sets the tone for the rest of the conversation.
*   Be clear and concise in your introduction.

### 2. Introduction Date
*   **Introduction Date**: The date of the interview or when the event took place.
*   Please provide the date of the event being discussed.
*   Format: MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2022)

### 3. Introduction Time
*   **Introduction Time**: The time of the interview or when the event took place.
*   Please provide the time of the event being discussed.
*   Format: HH:MM AM/PM (e.g., 10:00 AM)

### 4. Introduction Location
*   **Introduction Location**: The place where the event took place.
*   Please provide the location where the event occurred.
*   Be as specific as possible.

### 5. Interviewer Notes
*   **Interviewer Notes**: A field for the interviewer to record their observations or notes.
*   Please provide any important details or insights gathered during the conversation.
*   Be concise and accurate.

### 6. Was the Introduction Interview Conducted?
*   **Was the Introduction Interview Conducted?**: Indicate if the introduction was conducted.
*   Select 'Yes' or 'No' based on the circumstances.
*   This question is important for understanding the scope and coverage of the interview.

### 7. Were Participants Present?
*   **Were Participants Present?**: Indicate if participants were present during the interview.
*   Select 'Yes' or 'No' based on the circumstances.
*   This question helps in understanding the dynamics and engagement of the interview.

### 8. Were Topics Covered?
*   **Were Topics Covered?**: Indicate if relevant topics were discussed during the interview.
*   Select 'Yes' or 'No' based on the circumstances.
*   This question helps in ensuring that the necessary topics were covered.

### 9. Other Notes
*   **Other Notes**: A field for any additional information or observations.
*   Please provide any further details that may be relevant to the interview.
*   Be concise and accurate.
