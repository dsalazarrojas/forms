# word_and_phrase_generation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about word and phrase generation preferences and habits, as well as contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select your answer to each question on each page.
2. For the "Page 2" section, select all that apply.
3. For the "Contact Information" section, enter your email address.
4. For the "Language Ideas and Preferences" section, select your preferred language generation approach.
5. For the "Contact Information 2" section, enter your name and email address.
6. For the "Time of Completion" sections, select the time it took you to complete the form or a task.
7. In the "Page 9" section, enter any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Page 1** (`Page 1`, select_one, required: false): Choose "Yes" if you are interested in generating words and phrases, or "No" if you are not.
* **Page 2** (`Page 2`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all that apply to indicate your interests and preferences for word and phrase generation.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your response.
* **Language Ideas and Preferences** (`Language Ideas and Preferences`, select_one, required: false): Select your preferred language generation approach.
* **Contact Information 2** (`Contact Information 2`, text, required: false): Enter your name and email address for contact purposes.
* **Time of Completion** (`Time of Completion`, time, required: false): Estimate the time it took you to complete this form.
* **Time of Completion 2** (`Time of Completion 2`, time, required: false): Estimate the time it took you to complete this task.
* **Page 9** (`Page 9`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have.
