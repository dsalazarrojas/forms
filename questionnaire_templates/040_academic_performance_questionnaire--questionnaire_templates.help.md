# Academic Performance Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Academic Performance Questionnaire is used to gather information about a student's academic performance, strengths, areas for improvement, and development actions to support their progress.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure that you have all the necessary information before starting to fill out this form.
2. Fill out the required fields, which are marked as "true" in the form. The required fields are:
	* Student name (field 2)
	* Recommended development actions (field 8)
3. The other fields are optional and can be filled out if relevant and useful for the assessment and follow-up process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student name** (Field 2): Enter the student's full name, as it appears on their identification or contact information.
* **Academic year** (Field 3): Enter the current study year or level of the student.
* **GPA or performance metric** (Field 4): Enter any numeric value related to the student's academic performance, if applicable.
* **Areas of strength** (Field 5): Describe the student's academic strengths or areas where they excel.
* **Areas for improvement** (Field 6): Identify specific skills or topics where the student needs to develop further.
* **Advisor comments** (Field 7): Enter feedback or comments from the student's academic advisor, if available.
* **Recommended development actions** (Field 8): Enter specific steps or actions to support the student's progress and development.
* **Review date** (Field 9): Enter the scheduled follow-up review date, if applicable.
* **Contact email** (Field 10): Enter an email address for follow-up or questions related to the student's progress.

## Tips
- Please be as specific and detailed as possible when filling out this form.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to the student, their advisor, or the relevant academic department for clarification.
- Remember to save the completed form for future reference and follow-up conversations.
