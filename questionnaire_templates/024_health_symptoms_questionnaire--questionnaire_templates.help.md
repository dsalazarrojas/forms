# Health Symptoms Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Symptoms Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information about a person's health symptoms. This questionnaire is intended for individuals who are experiencing health issues and need to report their symptoms to a healthcare professional. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question with a clear and concise description of your symptoms. Please make sure to answer all questions, but you do not have to answer all questions if a particular symptom does not apply to you. You can type your answer directly into the text box provided for each question. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Headache** (`headache`, text, optional): Describe any recent or recurring headaches or migraines you are experiencing.
* **Nausea** (`nausea`, text, optional): Describe any recent or recurring nausea you are experiencing.
* **Dizziness** (`dizziness`, text, optional): Describe any recent or recurring dizziness or loss of balance you are experiencing.
* **Shortness of Breath** (`shortness_of_breath`, text, optional): Describe any recent or recurring shortness of breath you are experiencing.
* **Chest Pain** (`chest pain`, text, optional): Describe any recent or recurring chest pain you are experiencing.
* **Abdominal Pain** (`abdominal_pain`, text, optional): Describe any recent or recurring abdominal pain you are experiencing.
* **Diarrhea** (`diarrhea`, text, optional): Describe any recent or recurring diarrhea you are experiencing.
* **Vomiting** (`vomiting`, text, optional): Describe any recent or recurring vomiting you are experiencing.
* **Loss of Appetite** (`loss_of_appetite`, text, optional): Describe any recent or recurring loss of appetite you are experiencing.
* **Fatigue** (`fatigue`, text, optional): Describe any recent or recurring fatigue you are experiencing.
* **Muscle Weakness** (`muscle_weakness`, text, optional): Describe any recent or recurring muscle weakness you are experiencing.
* **Sleep Issues** (`sleep_issues`, text, optional): Describe any recent or recurring sleep issues you are experiencing.
* **Sleep Quality** (`sleep_quality`, text, optional): Describe the quality of your sleep recently. 

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when describing your symptoms.
* If you are unsure about what you are experiencing, please do not hesitate to ask for help.
* If you are experiencing severe or persistent symptoms, please consult a healthcare professional immediately.
