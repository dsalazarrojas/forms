# Hormone Optimization Patient Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hormone Optimization Patient Survey is a tool designed to gather information about your physical and emotional well-being. This survey aims to help healthcare professionals better understand your hormone levels, health conditions, and lifestyle factors that may be affecting your hormone optimization. By completing this survey, you'll provide valuable insights to support your treatment and care plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by reading the introduction (page 1) to understand the purpose and relevance of the survey.
2. Answer the questions on each subsequent page, providing honest and accurate information about your current state.
3. Make sure to answer all questions marked as "required" to ensure the survey is completed thoroughly.
4. Review your responses before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Introduction
* **introduction** (text, optional): This page provides a brief explanation of the survey's purpose and importance. Please read through this page to understand what the survey is about.

### Page 2: Patient Name
* **patient_name** (text, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents. This helps us to accurately identify you and your medical history.

### Page 3: Age
* **age** (number, optional): Enter your current age in years.

### Page 4: Height
* **height** (text, optional): Record your height in centimeters or meters (e.g., 175 cm or 1.75 m).

### Page 5: Weight
* **weight** (number, optional): Enter your current weight in kilograms (e.g., 60 kg).

### Page 6: Blood Glucose Levels
* **blood_glucose_levels** (number, optional): Record your average blood glucose levels in mmol/L (e.g., 5.5 mmol/L).

### Page 7: Stress Level
* **stress_level** (number, optional): Rate your current stress level on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is low and 10 is high.

### Page 8: Sleep Quality
* **sleep_quality** (text, optional): Describe your sleep quality using a few sentences, focusing on how well you're sleeping, such as "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".

### Page 9: Medication
* **medication** (text, optional): List any medications you're currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.

### Page 10: Medical Conditions
* **medical_conditions** (text, optional): Mention any medical conditions you're currently experiencing or have experienced in the past. Be as detailed as possible.
