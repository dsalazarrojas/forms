# Ramadhan Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ramadhan Questionnaire is designed to gather information from users regarding their Ramadhan celebrations. This form will help in collecting data on various aspects of the celebration, including meal plans, food drives, programs, and other essential details.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
- Ensure that you provide all the required information.
- If you are unsure about any field or question, please refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Meal Plans
* **Meal Plans** (`meal_plans`, `text`, optional): Describe the meal plans for the Ramadhan celebration.

### Food Drives
* **Food Drives** (`food_drives`, `select_one`, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are planning a food drive as part of the celebration. If not, select 'No'.

### Programs
* **Programs** (`programs`, `text`, optional): Describe any programs or activities that will be part of the Ramadhan celebration.

### School Name
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, optional): Provide the name of the school or institution where the celebration will take place.

### School Type
* **School Type** (`school_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of school (Boys, Girls, Mixed, or Others).

### School Level
* **School Level** (`school_level`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the level of school (Elementary, Secondary, High School, or Other).

### Mosque Name
* **Mosque Name** (`mosque_name`, `text`, optional): Provide the name of the mosque or place of worship where the celebration will take place.

### Mosque Address
* **Mosque Address** (`mosque_address`, `text`, optional): Provide the address of the mosque or place of worship.

### Date of Iftar
* **Date of Iftar** (`date_of_iftar`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the Iftar celebration.

### Time of Iftar
* **Time of Iftar** (`time_of_iftar`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the Iftar celebration.

### Number of Children
* **Number of Children** (`number_of_children`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of children participating in the celebration.

### Children Age Range
* **Children Age Range** (`children_age_range`, `text`, optional): Describe the age range of the children participating.

### Number of Adults
* **Number of Adults** (`number_of_adults`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of adults participating in the celebration.

### Adults Age Range
* **Adults Age Range** (`adults_age_range`, `text`, optional): Describe the age range of the adults participating.

### Contact Name
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, optional): Provide your name as the contact person for the celebration.

### Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number as the contact person.

### Contact Email
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `text`, optional): Enter your email address as the contact person.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the celebration.

### Assigned Tool
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, optional): Describe any tools or resources assigned for the celebration.
