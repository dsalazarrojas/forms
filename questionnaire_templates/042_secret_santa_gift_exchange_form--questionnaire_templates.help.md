# Secret Santa Gift Exchange Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Secret Santa Gift Exchange Form is designed to facilitate a fun and stress-free gift exchange experience among participants. This form helps participants input their personal details and gift preferences to ensure a successful and enjoyable exchange.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Input your personal details** (`User Info`): Enter your name and any other relevant information you'd like to share with the group.
2. **Share your gift preferences** (`Gift Exchange Info`): Describe your favorite gifts, interests, or hobbies that you'd like to receive as a Secret Santa gift.
3. **Submit your form** (`Submit`): Once you've filled out all the required fields, click the `Submit` button to finalize your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info (`User Info`)**: This is the first section where you'll input your personal details, such as your name and any other relevant information you'd like to share with the group.
* **Gift Exchange Info (`Gift Exchange Info`)**: This section is where you'll describe your favorite gifts, interests, or hobbies that you'd like to receive as a Secret Santa gift. Be as specific as possible so that the gift-giver can get you an awesome gift!
* **Submit (`Submit`): This is the final step. Click the `Submit` button to finalize your entry and add it to the Secret Santa gift exchange rotation.
