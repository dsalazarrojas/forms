# Art Gallery Curator Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Gallery Curator Interview Form is designed to assess a candidate's suitability for a curator position in an art gallery. The form gathers information about the candidate's background, experience, and expertise, as well as their ability to develop a compelling exhibition narrative, manage artist relationships, and oversee budgets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief overview of the candidate's background and experience.
2. Fill in the candidate's name, date of the interview, and your name as the interviewer.
3. Answer the questions about the candidate's experience, academic background, primary artistic specialization, and previous exhibitions.
4. Assess the candidate's ability to develop a compelling exhibition narrative, manage artist relationships, and oversee budgets.
5. Provide a summary of the candidate's strengths and weaknesses.
6. Make a final hiring recommendation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the candidate being interviewed.
* **Date of Interview** (`interview_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the interview.
* **Interviewer Name** (`interviewer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the interviewer.
* **Years of Curatorial Experience** (`years_curatorial_experience`, number, required): Assess the candidate's years of curatorial experience.
* **Academic Background** (`academic_background`, select_one, required): Choose the candidate's relevant academic background (e.g., PhD in Art History, MA in Curatorial Studies, etc.).
* **Primary Artistic Specialization** (`primary_specialization`, text, required): Describe the candidate's primary artistic specialization (e.g., Contemporary Art, Renaissance, Photography, etc.).
* **Scale of Previously Curated Exhibitions** (`previous_exhibitions_scale`, select_one, required): Assess the scale of previously curated exhibitions (e.g., Major Museum Retrospectives, Commercial Gallery Shows, etc.).
* **Ability to develop a compelling exhibition narrative** (`vision_and_narrative_score`, select_one, required): Evaluate the candidate's ability to develop a compelling exhibition narrative.
* **Experience in artist relationship and contract management** (`artist_relationship_management`, select_one, required): Assess the candidate's experience in artist relationship and contract management.
* **Knowledge of conservation and installation standards** (`technical_installation_knowledge`, select_one, required): Evaluate the candidate's knowledge of conservation and installation standards.
* **Proficiency in exhibition budget oversight** (`budget_management_experience`, select_one, required): Assess the candidate's proficiency in exhibition budget oversight.
* **Summary of Candidate Strengths** (`curator_strengths_summary`, text, required): Summarize the candidate's strengths.
* **Summary of Areas for Concern** (`curator_weaknesses_summary`, text, optional): Summarize areas for concern, if any.
* **Final Hiring Recommendation** (`hiring_decision`, select_one, required): Make a final hiring recommendation.
* **Interviewer Digital Signature** (`interviewer_signature`, text, required): Enter your digital signature as the interviewer.
