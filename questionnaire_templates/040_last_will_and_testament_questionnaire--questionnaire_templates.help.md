# Last Will And Testament Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to create a comprehensive Last Will And Testament document, ensuring that your wishes for the distribution of your assets are clearly outlined after your passing.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your personal details, including your first and last name.
2.  Provide your date of birth, address, and phone number.
3.  List your emergency contact's name and phone number.
4.  Select the relationship type that best describes your relationship with your emergency contact.
5.  List the assets you own, selecting from the provided options or adding your own.
6.  If you have assets not listed, provide details in the 'Assets Owned Other' field.
7.  Identify the names and phone numbers of your chosen executors.
8.  Review and confirm your selections before finalizing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name (1)**: Your personal first name.
*   **Last Name (2)**: Your personal last name.
*   **Date of Birth (3)**: The date you were born.
*   **Address (4)**: Your current address.
*   **Phone Number (5)**: Your phone number for emergency contacts.
*   **Emergency Contact Name (6)**: The name of the person you want to contact in case of an emergency.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone (7)**: The phone number of the emergency contact.
*   **Relationship (8)**: Select the relationship type that best describes your relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., Spouse, Child, Parent, Friend, etc.).
*   **Relationship Other (9)**: If your relationship is not listed, describe it here.
*   **Assets Owned (10)**: Select the assets you own, such as Real Estate, Cash, Stocks, etc. You can also add 'Assets Owned Other' if not listed.
*   **Assets Owned Other (11)**: If you own assets not listed, provide details about them.
*   **Executor 1 Name (12)**: Name of the first executor you have chosen.
*   **Executor 1 Phone (13)**: Phone number of the first executor.
*   **Executor 2 Name (14)**: Name of the second executor you have chosen.
*   **Executor 2 Phone (15)**: Phone number of the second executor.
