# survey of reality check on open defection (nepal) - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Survey of Reality Check on Open Defection (Nepal)" is a form designed to gather information and feedback on open defection in Nepal. It is intended to help individuals provide their experiences and perceptions on this sensitive topic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form to ensure you understand each field and its purpose.
2. Complete each field accurately and truthfully to the best of your ability.
3. Submit your response when you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Info
* **General Info** (`general_info`, text, required/optional): This field is for providing a brief description of your experience or situation regarding open defection in Nepal. Please provide any relevant information you feel is essential to understanding your perspective.

### Open Defection
* **Open Defection** (`open_defection`, text, required/optional): This field is for providing more specific information about open defection in Nepal, if applicable. Please provide any details you think are relevant to your experience or perception.

### Reality Check
* **Reality Check** (`reality_check`, text, required/optional): This field is for providing your thoughts and feelings about open defection in Nepal. Please share any personal thoughts or reflections you have about this topic.

## Tips
* Please ensure you complete each field truthfully and accurately.
* If you're unsure about any question or field, contact us at form@gic.mx for assistance.
* All data collected will be treated with confidentiality and used only for analytical purposes.
