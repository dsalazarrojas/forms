# Progressive Counting Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Progressive Counting Survey is designed to gather information about individuals' counting abilities and strategies. This form is intended to collect data on how people count by tens, fives, or a combination of both, and their confidence and ability in counting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on the "Main Page" section. You will see options for counting by tens, fives, and a combination of both.
2. Select all that apply for counting by tens, fives, or a combination of both from the "Counting Questions" section.
3. Choose how you usually count from the "Counting Strategies" section.
4. Rate your counting confidence in the "Counting Confidence" section.
5. In the "Counting Ability" section, describe your counting skills and any difficulties you experience.
6. Add any additional comments or feedback about your counting experiences in the "Additional Comments" section.
7. Finally, provide the date you submitted this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Page** (`main_page`, `text`, required: false): This is the main page of the survey where you will answer the initial questions.
* **Counting Questions** (`counting_questions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all that apply for counting by tens, fives, or a combination of both. You can choose one or more options depending on your counting abilities.
* **Counting Strategies** (`counting_strategies`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how you usually count. You can select one of the options provided.
* **Counting Confidence** (`counting_confidence`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate your counting confidence. You can select all that apply for High, Medium, or Low.
* **Counting Ability** (`counting_ability`, `text`, required: false): Describe your counting skills and any difficulties you experience.
* **Counting Comments** (`counting_comments`, `note`, required: false): Add any comments or feedback about your counting experiences.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with this form.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you submitted this form.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you need help or clarification on any question, feel free to ask.
* You can submit this form as many times as you need to.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact us at form@gic.mx.

Note: This help guide is a general assistance to fill out the form. If you have specific questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
