# music_label_artist_interview_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about music artists for internal use by music labels or record labels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. On the Artist Bios page, type a brief description of the artist's work (required).
3. On the Artist Name page, enter the artist's name (optional).
4. On the Artist Email page, enter the artist's email address (optional).
5. On the Artist Phone page, enter the artist's phone number (optional).
6. On the Artist Website page, enter the artist's website (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Artist Bios:
* **Artist Bios** (`artist_bios`, text, required): A brief description of the artist's work, including their genre, style, and notable works.

### Artist Name:
* **artist_name** (`artist_name`, number, optional): The artist's name, e.g., 123. If you enter a number, please ensure it corresponds to the artist's name in the database.

### Artist Email:
* **artist_email** (`artist_email`, email, optional): The artist's email address, where they can be reached for collaborations or promotions.

### Artist Phone:
* **artist_phone** (`artist_phone`, text, optional): The artist's phone number, where they can be reached for interviews or other communications.

### Artist Website:
* **artist_website** (`artist_website`, text, optional): The artist's website, where they can be found on the web for more information.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have the artist's permission to collect this information.
* If you're not sure about any of the fields, please consult the artist directly or check our internal database for existing information.
