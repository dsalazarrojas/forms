# home_buyer_consultation_questionnaire_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help home buyers like you navigate the home buying process by gathering information about your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant pages (sections) of the form by clicking on the links at the top of the page.
2. For each page, fill in the required and optional fields as prompted.
3. Be sure to review and submit the form once you have completed all the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **address** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter the address you are interested in or currently live at.
* **home_buyer_status** (`home_buyer_status`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select multiple options that describe your current status as a home buyer (e.g., first-time homebuyer, resale, etc.).
* **home_type** (`home_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of home you are interested in (e.g., single-family home, condo, etc.).
* **budget** (`budget`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your expected budget for the home purchase.
* **loan_amout** (`loan_amout`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the amount you expect to borrow for the home purchase.
* **down_payment** (`down_payment`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the amount you expect to pay as a down payment.
* **home_buyer_goals** (`home_buyer_goals`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your primary goal for buying a home (e.g., primary residence, investment property, etc.).
* **loan_term** (`loan_term`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the length of time you plan to take to repay the loan.
* **interest_rate** (`interest_rate`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the expected interest rate on your loan.
* **monthly_payment** (`monthly_payment`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the expected monthly payment amount.
* **home_buyer_preferences** (`home_buyer_preferences`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your preferences regarding the home buying experience (e.g., open houses, private tours, etc.).
* **mortgage_institution** (`mortgage_institution`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the financial institution providing your mortgage.
* **closing_cost** (`closing_cost`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the closing costs you expect to pay.
* **property_tax** (`property_tax`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the expected property tax rate.
* **insurance** (`insurance`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the expected insurance cost.
* **property_type** (`property_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of property you are interested in (e.g., single-family home, condo, etc.).
* **mortgage_payment** (`mortgage_payment`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the expected mortgage payment amount.
* **property_status** (`property_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the status of the property you are interested in (e.g., resale, new construction, etc.).
