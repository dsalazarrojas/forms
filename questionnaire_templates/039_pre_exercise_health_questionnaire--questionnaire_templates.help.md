# Pre Exercise Health Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form, called the "Pre Exercise Health Questionnaire", is designed to collect vital health information from users before they begin an exercise program. The purpose of this form is to identify potential risks and health concerns that may impact a user's ability to safely participate in physical activity. By providing this information, users can get a better understanding of their own health status and take necessary precautions to ensure their safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select each question type (e.g., user_info, health_status, etc.) and enter the required information in the corresponding field.
2. Choose the options that best describe your current health status.
3. Enter any relevant details or information in the text fields.
4. Review your answers to ensure they are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required: false): Please provide your basic contact information, including your name, address, and other contact details. This information is used to keep track of your progress and communicate with you.
* **Health Status** (`health_status`, select_one, required: false): Describe your current health status as "Excellent", "Good", "Average", or "Bad".
* **Exercise Frequency** (`exercise_frequency`, select_multiple, required: false): Select how often you exercise, including "Rarely", "Occasionally", "Frequently", or "Often".
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, required: false): List any medical conditions you have, such as "Heart Conditions", "Diabetes", or "Other".
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, required: false): Enter any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any allergies you have, including "Option 1" and "Option 2".
* **Medical Procedures** (`medical_procedures`, select_multiple, required: false): Describe any medical procedures you have undergone, including "None", "Heart Surgery", or "Other".
* **Last Medical Check** (`last_medical_check`, select_one, required: false): Specify when your last medical check-up was, including "More than 6 months ago", "Less than 6 months ago", "1-2 months ago", or "Less than 1 month ago".
* **Exercise Risk Factors** (`exercise_risk_factors`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any risk factors that may impact your exercise ability, including "Heart Conditions", "High Blood Pressure", or "Other".
* **Exercise Contradictions** (`exercise_contraindications`, select_multiple, required: false): Identify any conditions that may contraindicate exercise, including "None", "Knee Problems", "Back Problems", or "Other".
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): Provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your emergency contact phone number.
* **Emergency Address** (`emergency_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, text, required: false): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, required: false): Select whether you have given consent to exercise, including "True" or "False".
* **Medical Procedure Details** (`medical_procedure_details`, text, required: false): Enter any relevant medical procedure details.
* **Emergency Notes** (`emergency_notes`, text, required: false): Provide any additional notes about emergency contact information.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Enter any relevant medical history information.
* **Medical History Details** (`medical_history_details`, text, required: false): Enter any additional medical history details.
* **Medical Certificate** (`medical_certificate`, text, required: false): Enter any relevant medical certificate information.

Note that this form is not a substitute for a medical professional's consultation or diagnosis. If you have any concerns about your health, please consult a qualified medical professional before proceeding with exercise.
