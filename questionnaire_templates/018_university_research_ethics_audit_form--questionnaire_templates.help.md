# University Research Ethics Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to audit research studies and ensure compliance with university research ethics guidelines. It helps researchers demonstrate their adherence to ethical standards and best practices in their studies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all research studies that you have conducted or plan to conduct by checking the boxes next to each relevant option.
2. Enter the title of the study in the text field provided.
3. Briefly describe the study in the text field provided.
4. Indicate whether the research was reviewed by a research ethics committee and if so, identify the committee member involved.
5. Identify the risk factors associated with the study.
6. If applicable, enter the date and time of the review.
7. Enter any comments or feedback from the reviewers in the text field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Research Studies
* **Research Studies** (`research_studies`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check all boxes for the research studies you have conducted or plan to conduct.
### Study Title
* **Study Title** (`study_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief and descriptive title for the study.
### Study Description
* **Study Description** (`study_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief and detailed description of the study.
### Research Ethics Committee
* **Research Ethics Committee** (`research_ethics_committee`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether the research was reviewed by a research ethics committee.
### Committee Member
* **Committee Member** (`committee_member`, `text`, required: false): If the research was reviewed, identify the committee member involved.
### Risk Factors
* **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Identify any risk factors associated with the study.
### Review Date
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the review if applicable.
### Review Time
* **Review Time** (`review_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the review if applicable.
### Reviewers Comments
* **Reviewers Comments** (`reviewers_comments`, `note`, required: false): Enter any comments or feedback from the reviewers.
