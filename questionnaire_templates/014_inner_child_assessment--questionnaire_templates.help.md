# inner_child_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The inner_child_assessment form is a tool used to evaluate the well-being and emotional state of children. It is designed to gather information about a child's feelings, relationships, and emotional states to provide insights for support and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the inner_child_assessment form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **Introduction** page, which is not required.
2. In the **feelings** section, select one response that best describes your feelings:
	* 1: I feel happy
	* 2: I feel sad
	* 3: I feel neutral
3. In the **childhood_memories** section, describe your childhood memories, if any.
4. In the **emotional_wellbeing** section, select all responses that apply to your emotional state:
	* Very good
	* Fair
	* Poor
5. In the **emotional_states** section, select the time period that best describes your emotional state (optional).
6. In the **relationships** section, take a moment to note any relationships that are currently influencing your emotional state.
7. In the **support_system** section, select all responses that apply to the support you receive or feel you have:
	* Yes
	* No
8. Finally, in the **self_care** section, describe any self-care activities that help you feel better.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (text): This section is a brief introduction to the form.
* **feelings** (select_one): Choose an option that best describes your feelings at the moment. 
* **childhood_memories** (text): Describe any memories or experiences from your childhood that might be influencing your current emotional state.
* **emotional_wellbeing** (select multiple): Select all that apply to describe your current emotional state.
* **emotional_states** (time): Choose a time period that best describes your current emotional state. 
* **relationships** (note): Write down any relationships that are currently influencing your emotional state.
* **support_system** (select_multiple): Select all that apply to describe the support you receive or feel you have.
* **self_care** (text): Describe any self-care activities that help you feel better.
