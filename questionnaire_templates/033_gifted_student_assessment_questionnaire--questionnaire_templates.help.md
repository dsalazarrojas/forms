# Gifted Student Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gifted Student Assessment Questionnaire is designed to gather information about a student's background and educational experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have access to the necessary information about the student.
* Review the form carefully and fill out each section with accurate information.
* Refer to this help guide if you're unsure about any field or option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID**: Enter the student's unique identifier or student ID number. This field is <u>optional</u>.
* **First Name**: Enter the student's first name. This field is <u>optional</u>.
* **Last Name**: Enter the student's last name. This field is <u>optional</u>.
* **Grade Level**: Select the student's current grade level from the available options: 2nd Grade, 3rd Grade, or 4th Grade. This field is <u>optional</u>.
* **Educator's Name**: Enter the educator's name who is submitting the assessment. This field is <u>optional</u>.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback about the student. This field is <u>optional</u>.
* **Caregiver's Name**: Enter the name of the student's caregiver or parent. This field is <u>optional</u>.
* **Caregiver Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback from the caregiver. This field is <u>optional</u>.
* **Submitted by**: Select who submitted the assessment: Student, Educator, or Caregiver.
* **Birthdate**: Enter the student's birthdate in the correct date format (YYYY-MM-DD). This field is <u>optional</u>.
* **Occupation**: Select the student's occupation from the available options: Athlete, Student, or Other.
* **Ethnicity**: Select the student's ethnicity from the available options: American Indian or Alaska Native, Asian, Black or African American, White, Pacific Islander or Hawaiian, or Other.
* **Language**: Select the student's native language from the available options: None, English, or Other.
* **Gender**: Select the student's gender from the available options: Male, Female, or Other.
* **Special Education Needs**: Select any special education needs or requirements for the student from the available options: Dyslexia, or Other.
* **Education Level**: Select the educator's level of education from the available options: Bachelor's degree, Master's degree, or Other.
* **Experience**: Select the educator's level of experience from the available options: 0-2 years, 3-5 years, or 6-10 years.
* **Education Field**: Select the subject area of the educator's expertise from the available options: Math, Reading, or Writing.
* **School**: Enter the name of the school where the educator works. This field is <u>optional</u>.
* **Email**: Enter the educator's email address. This field is <u>optional</u>.
* **Phone**: Enter the educator's phone number. This field is <u>optional</u>.
* **Student Educator Comments 2**: Enter any further comments or feedback from the student. This field is <u>optional</u>.
* **Caregiver Email**: Enter the email address of the student's caregiver. This field is <u>optional</u>.
* **Caregiver Phone**: Enter the phone number of the student's caregiver. This field is <u>optional</u>.
* **Submitted By 2**: Select who submitted the assessment: Student, Educator, or Caregiver.

Note: This help guide is not exhaustive, and you may need to refer to other documentation or resources for additional information.
