# inclusive_curriculum_review_committee_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register members of the inclusive curricular review committee.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are completing it as a member of the inclusive curricular review committee.
2. Fill in your member details, including your email and phone number.
3. Select your areas of expertise by checking the relevant boxes.
4. Provide your consent to participate in the review process.
5. Enter any additional submission notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inclusive Curricular Review** (form_header, text, not required): This field is the title of the form.
* **Member Details** (member_details, text, not required): Please enter your member details here. This includes any relevant information that will help us to contact you.
* **Areas of Expertise** (areas_of_expertise, select_multiple, not required): Please select the areas of expertise for which you are a member. You can choose from:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Consent** (consent, select_one, not required): If you consent to participate in the review process, please select "Yes". If you do not consent, please select "No".
* **Submission** (submission, time, not required): This field is not currently being used, but you can enter any time-sensitive information related to your submission.
* **Email** (email, email, not required): Please enter your email address so that we can contact you.
* **Phone** (phone, text, not required): Please enter your phone number so that we can contact you.
* **Comments** (comments, note, not required): Please enter any additional comments or notes you have about your submission.
* **Submission Notes** (submission_notes, note, not required): Please enter any additional submission notes here.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are completing this form as a member of the inclusive curricular review committee.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
* You can always contact us if you need any assistance with completing this form.
