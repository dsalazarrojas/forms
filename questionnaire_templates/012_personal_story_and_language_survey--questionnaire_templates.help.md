# Personal Story And Language Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal stories and language use information from individuals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on each field to access the corresponding input fields.
2. Enter your background info in the "background-info" field. This field is not required but is useful for providing general background information.
3. Share your personal story in the "background-narrative" field. This field is not required but is useful for sharing your personal story.
4. Select the languages you use daily in the "language-use-info" field. You can select multiple languages.
5. Share your personal experiences in the "personal-narrative-1", "personal-narrative-2", "personal-narrative-3", and "personal-narrative-4" fields. These fields are not required but are useful for sharing your personal experiences.
6. Select how often you use a language in the "language-use-frequency" field. You can select from "Daily", "Weekly", "Monthly", "Rarely", or "Never".
7. Enter your email address in the "email" field. This field is not required but is useful for communication purposes.
8. Select the language you use in the "language-use-language" field. You can select from "English", "Spanish", "Other", or "Please specify".
9. Share your personal language experience in the "personal-language-experience" field. This field is not required but is useful for sharing your personal language experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Background Info (background-info)**: A free-text field for users to provide general background information. This field is not required.
* **Background Narrative (background-narrative)**: A text area field for users to share their personal story. This field is not required.
* **Language Use Info (language-use-info)**: A multiple-choice field for users to select the languages they use daily. You can select multiple languages.
	+ Options: English, Spanish, Other, Please specify
* **Personal Narrative 1 (personal-narrative-1)**: A text area field for users to share their personal experiences. This field is not required.
* **Personal Narrative 2 (personal-narrative-2)**: A text area field for users to share their personal experiences. This field is not required.
* **Personal Narrative 3 (personal-narrative-3)**: A text area field for users to share their personal experiences. This field is not required.
* **Personal Narrative 4 (personal-narrative-4)**: A text area field for users to share their personal experiences. This field is not required.
* **Language Use Frequency (language-use-frequency)**: A multiple-choice field for users to select how often they use a language. You can select from "Daily", "Weekly", "Monthly", "Rarely", or "Never".
	+ Options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, Never
* **Email (email)**: An email field for users to input their email address. This field is not required.
* **Language Use Language (language-use-language)**: A multiple-choice field for users to select the language they use. You can select from "English", "Spanish", "Other", or "Please specify".
	+ Options: English, Spanish, Other, Please specify
* **Personal Language Experience (personal-language-experience)**: A text area field for users to share their personal language experience. This field is not required.
