# Takaful Career Guidance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide demographic information and career guidance needs to the Takaful organization, helping them to offer personalized guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the 'Submit' button at the end of the form when you have finished filling out all required fields.
2. Review the fields and ensure they are all filled out truthfully and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_id** (user_id, text, optional): This field is not used to collect any sensitive information. Please provide your user id for easier identification.
* **Sector of interest** (Sector of interest, select_one, optional): Choose the sector that you are most interested in.
* **name** (name, text, optional): Please enter your name for easier reference.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Provide your email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Date** (Date, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to start your Takaful program.
* **Time** (Time, time, optional): Choose a time of day for your Takaful program.
* **Career Level** (Career Level, select_multiple, optional): Choose the level of your career from the given options.
* **Readiness for Takaful** (Readiness for Takaful, number, optional): Please rate your readiness for Takaful from 1-10.
* **Interests in Takaful** (Interests in Takaful, text, optional): Describe your interests in Takaful and what motivates you to join.
* **Support Needs** (Support Needs, text, optional): Mention any support needs you may have while pursuing a Takaful program.
* **Submit** (Submit, note, optional): Click this button to submit your application.
* **Name of Program** (Name of Program, text, optional): If you are interested in a specific Takaful program, please enter the name.
* **Program Type** (Program Type, select_one, optional): Choose the type of your Takaful program from the given options.
* **Location** (Location, select_multiple, optional): Choose the location where you would like to take your Takaful program.

Tips:
- Please fill out all the fields accurately and truthfully for better assistance.
- You can select multiple options for "Career Level", "Location", and "Program Type".
- If you encounter any difficulties while filling out this form, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
