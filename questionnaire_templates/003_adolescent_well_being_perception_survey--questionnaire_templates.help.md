# Adolescent Well Being Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adolescent Well Being Perception Survey is a tool designed to assess the emotional and psychological well-being of adolescents. It aims to understand their self-reported experiences, attitudes, and perceptions regarding their well-being over the last two weeks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and answer them honestly to the best of your ability.
2. Select the most accurate answer for each question based on your experiences over the last two weeks.
3. Make sure to provide your initials if you prefer to remain anonymous.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### -- Well being Survey --
This is a header page that serves as a introduction to the survey.

### Name or initials
* **Name or initials** (`Name or initials`, `text`, optional): Provide your initials if you prefer to remain anonymous. You can enter any name or initials you are comfortable with.

### Age
* **Age** (`Age`, `number`, required): Enter your age in years. This is a necessary field to ensure the survey is relevant to you.

### Mood over last two weeks
* **Mood over last two weeks** (`Mood over last two weeks`, `select_one`, required): Choose the best option that describes your mood over the last two weeks.
	+ Very low: Feeling extremely sad or depressed.
	+ Low: Feeling sad or irritable.
	+ Moderate: Feeling neutral or average.
	+ High: Feeling happy or content.
	+ Very high: Feeling extremely happy or satisfied.

### Sleep quality
* **Sleep quality** (`Sleep quality`, `select_one`, required): Rate your typical sleep quality recently.
	+ Poor: Waking up frequently during the night or waking up early.
	+ Fair: Sleeping lightly or waking up occasionally.
	+ Good: Sleeping soundly but with some disturbance.
	+ Very good: Sleeping soundly and waking up refreshed.

### Perceived social support
* **Perceived social support** (`Perceived social support`, `select_one`, required): Rate support from family and friends.
	+ None: Feeling isolated or unsupported.
	+ Low: Some support, but not enough.
	+ Moderate: Enough support for most needs.
	+ High: Feeling very supported.

### Main sources of stress
* **Main sources of stress** (`Main sources of stress`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply. This could be school, family, peers, social media, or health concerns.

### Coping strategies
* **Coping strategies** (`Coping strategies`, `text`, optional): Describe any strategies you use to cope with difficulties or stress.

### Would you like follow up
* **Would you like follow up** (`Would you like follow up`, `select_one`, optional): Choose if you would like staff to follow up with you.
	+ True: Yes, for follow-up calls.
	+ False: No follow-up calls.

### Contact phone
* **Contact phone** (`Contact phone`, `text`, optional): Provide your contact phone number for follow-up calls, if applicable.
