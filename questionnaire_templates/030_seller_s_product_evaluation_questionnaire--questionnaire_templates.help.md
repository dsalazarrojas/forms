# SELLERS_PRODUCT_EVALUATION_QUESTIONNAIRE - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sellers Product Evaluation Questionnaire is designed to collect information about a product and its seller. This form helps evaluate the product's features, quality, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the product information, including its name, description, and category.
2. Rate the product from 1 to 5, depending on your satisfaction.
3. Provide a detailed description of the product and its features.
4. Select the product category from the options provided.
5. Enter the seller's information, including their name, description, and contact details.
6. Provide a brief description of the seller and their performance.
7. Upload a product image and a seller image (if applicable).
8. Evaluate the seller's rating and performance.
9. Provide additional comments or feedback about the seller and product.
10. Select the product status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
11. Enter the seller's feedback and recommendation.
12. Enter the evaluation time and seller rating time.
13. Enter the seller's rating.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Information** (`product_info`, text, optional):
	+ Enter a brief description of the product, including its name and key features.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, optional):
	+ Provide a detailed description of the product's features and benefits.
* **Product Category** (`product_category`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the category that best describes the product (e.g., Product A, Product B, or Product C).
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional):
	+ Rate the product from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Evaluation** (`evaluation`, note, optional):
	+ Provide a brief description of your overall evaluation of the product.
* **Seller Information** (`seller_info`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the seller's name and contact details (email or phone number).
* **Seller Description** (`seller_description`, text, optional):
	+ Provide a brief description of the seller and their performance.
* **Product Image** (`product_image`, text, optional):
	+ Upload a product image (if applicable).
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Seller Rating** (`seller_rating`, number, optional):
	+ Rate the seller from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Seller Evaluation** (`seller_evaluation`, text, optional):
	+ Provide a brief description of the seller's performance.
* **Product Image Upload** (`product_image_upload`, text, optional):
	+ Upload a product image (if applicable).
* **Seller Image Upload** (`seller_image_upload`, text, optional):
	+ Upload a seller image (if applicable).
* **Seller Signature** (`seller_signature`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the seller's signature (if applicable).
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, email, optional):
	+ Enter the seller's email or phone number.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the seller's phone number.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional comments or feedback about the seller and product.
* **Product Status** (`product_status`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the product status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Seller Feedback** (`seller_feedback`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any feedback about the seller.
* **Seller Recommendation** (`seller_recommendation`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any recommendation for the seller.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the time spent evaluating the product.
* **Seller Rating Time** (`seller_rating_time`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the time spent rating the seller.
* **Seller Rating** (`seller_rating`, number, optional):
	+ Rate the seller from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Seller Evaluation** (`seller_evaluation`, text, optional):
	+ Provide a brief description of the seller's performance.
* **Product Image Upload** (`product_image_upload`, text, optional):
	+ Upload a product image (if applicable).
* **Seller Image Upload** (`seller_image_upload`, text, optional):
	+ Upload a seller image (if applicable).
* **Seller Signature** (`seller_signature`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the seller's signature (if applicable).
