# Dual Representation Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information on a dual representation assessment, where participants and trainers can provide responses, translations, and feedback in different languages.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Participant Name field with the name of the participant or individual being assessed.
2. Select the Translation Format for the participant's response from the dropdown menu.
3. Provide the Participant Response in the selected translation format (English, Spanish, or French).
4. Repeat the same steps for Trainer Response and Trainer Translation Diff.
5. Add any additional notes or comments as needed in the Participant Notes and Trainer Notes fields.
6. Choose the Form Completion Status (Not Started, Started, or Completed) and Participant Completion Status (Not Started, Started, or Completed).
7. Enter the time spent by the participant and trainer on the form.
8. Enter the participant and trainer scores if applicable.
9. Add any additional comments or feedback in the Participant Comments and Trainer Comments fields.
10. Enter the Form ID if you want to track the form's unique identifier.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, required/optional): Enter the name of the participant being assessed.
* **Translation Format** (`translation_format`, required/optional): Select the translation format for the participant's response (English, Spanish, or French).
* **Participant Response English** (`participant_response_english`, required/optional): Enter the participant's response in English.
* **Participant Response Spanish** (`participant_response_spanish`, required/optional): Enter the participant's response in Spanish.
* **Participant Response French** (`participant_response_french`, required/optional): Enter the participant's response in French.
* **Trainer Response English** (`trainer_response_english`, required/optional): Enter the trainer's response in English.
* **Trainer Response Spanish** (`trainer_response_spanish`, required/optional): Enter the trainer's response in Spanish.
* **Trainer Response French** (`trainer_response_french`, required/optional): Enter the trainer's response in French.
* **Participant Translation Diff English** (`participant_translation_diff_english`, required/optional): Enter the participant's translation difference in English.
* **Participant Translation Diff Spanish** (`participant_translation_diff_spanish`, required/optional): Enter the participant's translation difference in Spanish.
* **Participant Translation Diff French** (`participant_translation_diff_french`, required/optional): Enter the participant's translation difference in French.
* **Trainer Translation Diff English** (`trainer_translation_diff_english`, required/optional): Enter the trainer's translation difference in English.
* **Trainer Translation Diff Spanish** (`trainer_translation_diff_spanish`, required/optional): Enter the trainer's translation difference in Spanish.
* **Trainer Translation Diff French** (`trainer_translation_diff_french`, required/optional): Enter the trainer's translation difference in French.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments from the participant.
* **Trainer Notes** (`trainer_notes`, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments from the trainer.
* **Form Completion Status** (`form_completion_status`, required/optional): Choose the status of the form (Not Started, Started, or Completed).
* **Participant Completion Status** (`participant_completion_status`, required/optional): Choose the status of the participant's completion (Not Started, Started, or Completed).
* **Participant Time Spent** (`participant_time_spent`, required/optional): Enter the time spent by the participant on the form.
* **Trainer Time Spent** (`trainer_time_spent`, required/optional): Enter the time spent by the trainer on the form.
* **Participant Score** (`participant_score`, required/optional): Enter the participant's score.
* **Trainer Score** (`trainer_score`, required/optional): Enter the trainer's score.
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or feedback from the participant.
* **Trainer Comments** (`trainer_comments`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or feedback from the trainer.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, required/optional): Enter the unique identifier for the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to complete the form accurately.
* Use the correct translation format for the participant's response.
* Double-check the participant and trainer responses for accuracy.
* Enter the correct score if applicable.
* Keep the participant and trainer notes and comments clear and concise.
