# Educational Program Impact Evaluation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Program Impact Evaluation Questionnaire is a tool used to assess the effectiveness of educational programs, such as workshops, training sessions, or educational sessions, to better understand their impact on participants. This form will help us evaluate the program's outcomes, identify areas for improvement, and inform future program development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose the correct participant information category from the list on the **First Page**.
2. Fill in the required information for **Program Type** and **Participant Name** on the **Participant Information** page.
3. Select the relevant **Evaluation Period** from the list for your program.
4. Enter the **Program Name** and **Session Duration** on the **Program Details** page.
5. Enter the **Location** and **Date** on the **Program Location and Dates** page.
6. Enter the number of **Participant Number** on the **Program Details** page.
7. Rate the **Participant Rating** on the **Program Details** page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`Participant Information`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct category for your participant information (e.g., Participant Information or Program Information).
* **Program Name** (`Program Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your educational program.
* **Evaluation Period** (`Evaluation Period`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the period in which your program took place (e.g., January - March, April - June, July - September, October - December).
* **Program Type** (`Program Type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of educational program (e.g., Training, Workshop, or Educational Session).
* **Location** (`Location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of your program.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your program.
* **Session Duration** (`Session Duration`, `number`, required): Enter the duration of your program in hours.
* **Participant Number** (`Participant Number`, `number`, required): Enter the number of participants in your program.
* **Participant Rating** (`Participant Rating`, `number`, required): Rate the participant rating of your program out of 5.
