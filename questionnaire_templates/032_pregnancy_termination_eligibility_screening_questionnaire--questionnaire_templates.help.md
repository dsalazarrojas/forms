# Pregnancy Termination Eligibility Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to screen patients for their eligibility for pregnancy termination. It gathers information about their medical history, family history, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your User Information, including your name and contact details.
2. Answer questions about your pregnancy details, such as your due date and any previous pregnancies.
3. Provide information about your medical history, including any previous medical conditions or allergies.
4. Share information about your family medical history, including any conditions that may affect your pregnancy.
5. Enter your contact details, including phone numbers and emergency contact information.
6. Answer questions about your medical contact information, including your doctor's contact details.
7. Provide information about any medical conditions that may affect your pregnancy.
8. Answer questions about the possibility of termination.
9. Answer questions about your pregnancy status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (1)**: Provide your first and last name, email address, and phone number.
* **Pregnancy Details (2)**: Answer questions about your current pregnancy, including your due date and any previous pregnancies.
* **Termination Eligibility (3)**: This field is for the healthcare provider to assess your eligibility for pregnancy termination.
* **Medical History (4)**: Provide information about any previous medical conditions or allergies.
* **Family History (5)**: Answer questions about any conditions that may affect your pregnancy in your family.
* **Contact Details (6)**: Enter your phone numbers and any emergency contact information.
* **Medical Contact Info (7)**: Provide your doctor's contact information.
* **Medical History Conditions (8)**: Answer questions about any medical conditions that may affect your pregnancy.
* **Termination Possibility (9)**: This field is for the healthcare provider to assess your eligibility for pregnancy termination.
* **Pregnancy Status (10)**: Answer questions about your current pregnancy status.
* **Medical Certification (11)**: This field is for the healthcare provider to sign and date, indicating they have reviewed your medical information.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, ask your healthcare provider for assistance.
* This form is intended for use in medical settings only, and the information collected will be used for patient care and assessment purposes.
