# Scenario Based Technical Interview Question Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Scenario Based Technical Interview Question Form** is designed to collect data on scenario-based technical interview questions. This form is meant to be filled out by a candidate after being presented with a technical problem, allowing the interviewer to assess the candidate's problem-solving skills and technical abilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Scenario Based Technical Interview Question** field with a brief description of the scenario presented to you.
2. Rate the scenario on a scale of 1-5 in the **Scenario Rating** field, where 1 means "not relevant" and 5 means "very relevant" to the technical interview.
3. Provide a detailed response to the scenario in the **Candidate Response** field. Be as concise and descriptive as possible.
4. Rate the candidate's skills as required in the **Technical Skill** and **Non Technical Skill** fields.
5. If applicable, fill in the **Date** and **Time** fields with the date and time of the technical interview.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <u>Scenario Based Technical Interview Question</u>
• **Scenario Based Technical Interview Question** (`scenario_based_interview_question`, `text`, required: false): This field is meant to be filled with a brief description of the scenario presented to you during the technical interview.

### <u>Scenario Rating</u>
• **Scenario Rating** (`scenario_rating`, `number`, required: false): This field is a rating system that allows you to assess the relevance of the scenario to the technical interview. A rating of 1 means the scenario was not relevant, and 5 means it was very relevant.

### <u>Candidate Response</u>
• **Candidate Response** (`candidate_response`, `text`, required: false): This field is meant to be filled with your response to the scenario. Be as descriptive and detailed as possible.

### <u>Interviewer Rating</u>
• **Interviewer Rating** (`interviewer_rating`, `number`, required: false): This field is a rating system that allows the interviewer to assess the candidate's response to the scenario. A rating of 1 means a poor response, and 5 means an excellent response.

### <u>Technical Skill</u>
• **Technical Skill** (`technical_skill`, `select_one`, required: false): This field allows you to rate the candidate's technical skills as required for the scenario. Choose the skill that best fits the candidate's abilities.

### <u>Non Technical Skill</u>
• **Non Technical Skill** (`non_technical_skill`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field allows you to rate the candidate's non-technical skills that are relevant to the scenario. Choose all the skills that the candidate has demonstrated.

### <u>Date</u>
• **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): If applicable, fill in the date of the technical interview.

### <u>Time</u>
• **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): If applicable, fill in the time of the technical interview.
