# Synthesizer Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Synthesizer Knowledge Quiz is designed to gauge users' knowledge on a specific subject matter. The form is a collection point for various questions related to the quiz input, results, and other relevant areas. Its purpose is to collect users' responses on their level of understanding on different aspects of the topic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Read the guidelines and the explanations provided for each field.
2. Answer the questions truthfully, taking into account the options available for select types.
3. Ensure that you have filled out all the required fields, even though the entire form is not mandatory.
4. Review and submit your answers for accurate tracking and assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Synthesizer Knowledge Quiz** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This is the title of the form, which helps in identifying and tracking the user's input.
- **Synthesizer Quiz Input** (`2`, `text`, required: false): This field allows you to enter any additional information related to your knowledge on the synthesizer.
- **Synthesizer Results** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Here, you can enter any results or feedback related to your synthesizer quiz.
- **Music Educators** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose whether or not you are a music educator from the available options.
- **Creator Communities** (`5`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose whether or not you are part of any creator communities from the options provided.
- **Simple Data Collection** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all that apply from the options provided for simple data collection methods.
- **Organized Form Submission Tracking** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all that apply from the options provided for organized form submission tracking methods.
- **Music Educators Info** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information related to music educators that you would like to provide.
