# Evening Availability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about an individual's evening availability. The purpose of this form is to gather details about a person's evening routine, such as their start and end times, availability in the evening, and whether they prefer to schedule meetings or activities during this time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct options for each field as per your evening routine and preference.
2. Choose your preferred start time for the day from the provided options.
3. Choose your preferred end time for the day from the provided options.
4. Select your availability in the evening using the provided time slots.
5. Indicate if you would like to schedule a recurring meeting or activity.
6. If desired, provide comments or additional information in the "Comments" field.
7. Enter your contact information (email or phone number) if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Morning Evening Availability** (`survey_day`): Choose the time slot that best represents your typical evening availability. 
* **What time do you usually start your day?** (`start_time`): Select the time that you usually begin your daily activities. 
* **What time do you usually end your day?** (`end_time`): Select the time that you usually end your daily activities. 
* **What time are you usually available in the evening?** (`available_time`): Choose the time slots that you are available in the evening. 
* **Schedule a recurring meeting or activity?** (`schedule`): Indicate if you would like to schedule a recurring meeting or activity during your evening hours.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Provide any additional information or comments about your evening availability. 
* **Contact information (e.g., email or phone number)** (`email` and `phone`): Enter your contact information so that we can reach out to you for further discussion.
