# monat_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The monat_referral_form is a questionnaire designed to gather information about how customers or visitors found out about your business. This information helps you understand the effectiveness of your marketing efforts and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, fill out the fields below with your information.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly, as this information will help us understand your referral source.
3. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please leave it blank and we will do our best to follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Fill out your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Fill out your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of day when you visited our business.
* **Referral Type** (`referral_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how you found out about our business:
	+ Referral: A friend or family member told you about us.
	+ Partner: One of our partners or business associates referred you.
	+ Other: Choose this option if none of the above applies.
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check all the ways you found out about our business:
	+ Social Media: You saw us on social media.
	+ Word of Mouth: You heard about us from a friend or family member.
	+ Online Ad: You saw us on an online ad.
	+ Other: Choose this option if none of the above applies.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about your referral experience.

## Tips

- If you have any questions or need clarification about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
- If you are using a mobile device, please use the zoom feature on your browser to make the text more readable.
- If you are using a screen reader, please use the navigation features to move between fields.
