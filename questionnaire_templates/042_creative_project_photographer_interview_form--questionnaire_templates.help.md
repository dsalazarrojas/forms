# Creative Project Photographer Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a photographer's details, contact, location, portfolio, and experience related to a specific project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact information in the "Photographer Details" field.
2. Enter your location in the "Location" field.
3. Share your website or portfolio URL in the "Portfolio Link" field.
4. Select the type of project you're working on by choosing between "Yes" or "No" in the "Project Type" field.
5. Enter the project's date in the "Project Date" field.
6. Describe the project in the "Project Description" field.
7. Add any additional notes you'd like to share about the photographer in the "Photographer Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Photographer Details** (Photographer Details): Enter your name and contact information.
- **Contact** (Contact): Enter your email or phone number.
- **Location** (Location): Where are you based?
  \* Use a city, state, or region to help us understand your location.
  \* This will help us understand your availability and travel requirements.
- **Portfolio** (Portfolio): Share a link to your portfolio or website.
- **Portfolio Items** (Portfolio Items): This field is not applicable for this form.
- **Experience** (Experience): This field is not applicable for this form.
- **Portfolio Image** (Portfolio Image): This field is not applicable for this form.
- **Portfolio Link** (Portfolio Link): Share a link to your portfolio or website.
- **Portfolio Type** (Portfolio Type): This field is not applicable for this form.
- **Project Type** (Project Type): Select "Yes" if you are working on a project, "No" otherwise.
  \* This helps us understand the scope of your project.
- **Project Location** (Project Location): Enter the location of the project.
- **Project Date** (Project Date): Enter the date of the project.
- **Project Description** (Project Description): Describe the project in a few sentences.
- **Photographer Notes** (Photographer Notes): Add any additional information you'd like to share about yourself or the project.
- **Project Manager** (Project Manager): This field is not applicable for this form.
