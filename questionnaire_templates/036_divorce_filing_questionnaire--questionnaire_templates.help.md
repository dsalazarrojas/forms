# Divorce Filing Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a divorce filing, including details about the marriage, properties, children, and other relevant factors. The form is used to provide a comprehensive and standardized way to collect data for divorce filings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to understand the fields and their requirements.
2. Enter your information accurately and truthfully in each field.
3. Ensure that you have the necessary documents and information required for a divorce filing in your jurisdiction.
4. Check the form for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **divorce_filing_info**: A brief text field where you can provide any additional information related to your divorce filing.
* **filing_court**: Select the court where your divorce will be filed (e.g., Court of Common Pleas, Federal Bankruptcy Court, etc.).
* **filing_type**: Choose the type of divorce filing (e.g., Full, Limited, Special).
* **joint_property**: Indicate whether you own property jointly or separately with your spouse.
* **divorce_date**: Enter the date of your divorce (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **divorce_time**: Enter the time of your divorce (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **spouse1_name**: Enter the name of your spouse.
* **spouse1_address**: Enter your spouse's address.
* **spouse1_phone**: Enter your spouse's phone number.
* **spouse2_name**: Enter the name of your spouse.
* **spouse2_address**: Enter your spouse's address.
* **spouse2_phone**: Enter your spouse's phone number.
* **marital_status**: Indicate your current marital status (e.g., Married, Separated, Divorced, Never Married).
* **children**: Indicate whether you have children with your spouse.
* **children_details**: Provide any additional information about your children.
* **property**: Select the type of property you own (e.g., Joint, Separate, Community).
* **custody**: Choose the custody arrangement for your children (e.g., Joint, Sole, Split).
* **alimony**: Provide any information about alimony.
* **alimony_amount**: Enter the amount of alimony you are seeking.
* **divorce_documentation**: Provide any documentation related to your divorce.
* **divorce_documentation_type**: Select the type of divorce documentation (e.g., Certified, Uncontested, Default).
* **court_order**: Enter any court orders related to your divorce.
* **other_court_orders**: Provide any additional information about other court orders.
* **other_court_orders_details**: Enter any additional details about other court orders.
* **divorce_filing_fee**: Select the fee for filing your divorce (e.g., $2500, $5000, Other).
* **divorce_filing_fee_details**: Enter any additional information about your divorce filing fee.

**Tips:**
* Be accurate and truthful when completing this form.
* Ensure that you have the necessary documents and information required for a divorce filing in your jurisdiction.
* Check the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all fields are completed correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field or have specific questions, please consult a legal professional.
