## Purpose
The Hepatobiliary Tumor Evaluation Survey is a form designed to collect information about a patient's medical history and characteristics related to hepatobiliary tumors. This form helps healthcare professionals assess the patient's condition and provide accurate diagnoses and treatments.

To complete the Hepatobiliary Tumor Evaluation Survey, follow these steps:

*   Indicate any previous liver surgery history in the "Liver Surgery History" field.
*   Specify the tumor location (Right lobe, Left lobe, or Other) in the "Tumor Location" field.
*   Fill in any additional medical history in the "Medical History Other" field.
*   Indicate the tumor size (10-20 mm, 21-50 mm, 51-100 mm, or 101-500 mm) in the "Tumor Size" field.

*   Provide patient information in the "Patient Info" field.
*   Specify any medical history treatments (Surgery, Chemotherapy, Radiotherapy, or Other) in the "Medical History Treatment" field.
*   Fill in any additional tumor characteristics in the "Tumor Characteristics Other" field.
*   Specify any other tumor location (Right lobe, Left lobe, or Other) in the "Tumor Location Other" field.
