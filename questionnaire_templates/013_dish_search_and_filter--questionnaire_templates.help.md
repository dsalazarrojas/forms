# Dish Search And Filter - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a user's preferences and dietary needs to facilitate searching and filtering of dishes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Dish Search And Filter" link to start the form.
2. Complete each page of the form in the order specified above.
3. On each page, fill in the required fields and select options as needed.
4. Use the search results page (Page 7) to filter and explore the search results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Dish Search And Filter Page (Page 1)**
* **Dish Search And Filter Page** (text, required): This is the title of the form, which serves as an introduction to the search and filter process.

### **Meal Preferences (Page 2)**
* **Meal Preferences** (text, optional): Provide any meal preferences you have, such as vegetarian or vegan.

### **Search Filters (Page 3)**
* **Search Filters** (text, optional): Select any filters you would like to apply to your search, such as price or rating.

### **Dietary Restrictions (Page 4)**
* **Dietary Restrictions** (text, optional): Enter any dietary restrictions you have, such as gluten-free or dairy-free.

### **What type of food (Page 5)**
* **What type of food** (select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple types of food you are interested in, such as Italian or Chinese.

### **Cooking Skills (Page 6)**
* **Cooking skills** (select_one, optional): Select one cooking skill you have, such as baking or grilling.

### **Ingredients (Page 7)**
* **Ingredients** (text, optional): If you know specific ingredients you would like to include or exclude from the search, enter them here.

### **Search results (Page 8)**
* **Search results** (text, optional): This is where you will find your search results, which you can filter and explore further.
