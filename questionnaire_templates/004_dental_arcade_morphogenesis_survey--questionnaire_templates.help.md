# Dental Arcade Morphogenesis Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Dental Arcade Morphogenesis Survey" form is designed to collect information about the patient's dental history, medical history, and demographic details. This form helps healthcare professionals gather essential data to assess the patient's oral health and identify potential risks or conditions that may affect their treatment outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer all questions in the fields marked as "Required".
2. For fields with multiple options, select all relevant answers.
3. Enter your information in the format specified for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required)
Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.

### 2. **First Visit Date** (`first_visit_date`, date, optional)
Enter the date of your first visit to the dentist or healthcare provider.

### 3. **Last Visit Date** (`last_visit_date`, date, optional)
Enter the date of your last visit to the dentist or healthcare provider.

### 4. **Age** (`age`, number, required)
Enter your current age in years.

### 5. **Sex** (`sex`, select_one, optional)
Select your sex: Male, Female, or Other.

### 6. **Race** (`race`, select_multiple, optional)
Select all relevant races: White, Black, Asian, Pacific Islander, or Other.

### 7. **Country of Origin** (`country_of_origin`, select_multiple, optional)
Select all relevant countries of origin: American Indian/Alaska Native, Asian, Black, Pacific Islander, White, or Other.

### 8. **Language** (`language`, select_multiple, optional)
Select all relevant languages: English, Spanish, French, or Other.

### 9. **Dental History** (`dental_history`, note, required)
Describe your dental history, including any relevant medical conditions, procedures, or treatments.
