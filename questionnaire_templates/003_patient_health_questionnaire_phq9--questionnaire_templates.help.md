# Patient Health Questionnaire PHQ9 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Health Questionnaire PHQ9 is a comprehensive tool used to evaluate a patient's physical, emotional, and social health. It helps healthcare professionals assess the patient's overall well-being and identify potential issues that may require further attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the patient's medical history to ensure you're familiar with their condition.
2. Carefully read each question and select the relevant answer.
3. Answer each question honestly, and choose only one option per question.
4. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Symptoms (<id: 1, label: General Symptoms>):**
	+ This question assesses the presence of general symptoms such as fever, headache, or other non-specific health issues.
	+ Please select all applicable symptoms that the patient is experiencing.
* **Physical Health (<id: 2, label: Physical Health>):**
	+ This question checks whether the patient is experiencing any physical health issues or conditions.
	+ Please select 'Yes' if you have any physical health concerns, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Social Relationships (<id: 3, label: Social Relationships>):**
	+ This question evaluates the patient's relationships with family and friends.
	+ Please select 'Yes' if you have a strong support system, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Emotional State (<id: 4, label: Emotional State>):**
	+ This question assesses the patient's emotional state, including feelings of happiness, sadness, or anxiety.
	+ Please select all applicable emotional states that the patient is experiencing.
* **Health Concerns (<id: 5, label: Health Concerns>):**
	+ This question asks about the patient's concerns or worries related to their health.
	+ Please provide a brief description of the patient's concerns.
* **Mental Health History (<id: 6, label: Mental Health History>):**
	+ This question checks whether the patient has a history of mental health issues or concerns.
	+ Please select 'Yes' if you have experienced mental health issues in the past, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Physical Symptoms (<id: 7, label: Physical Symptoms>):**
	+ This question assesses the presence of physical symptoms such as pain, fatigue, or numbness.
	+ Please select all applicable symptoms the patient is experiencing.
* **Social Support (<id: 8, label: Social Support>):**
	+ This question evaluates the patient's social support system, including friends, family, or healthcare professionals.
	+ Please select 'Yes' if you have a strong social support system, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Emotional Support (<id: 9, label: Emotional Support>):**
	+ This question assesses the patient's emotional support, including feeling heard or understood.
	+ Please select 'Yes' if you feel supported emotionally, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Mental Health Services (<id: 10, label: Mental Health Services>):**
	+ This question checks whether the patient has accessed mental health services such as counseling or therapy.
	+ Please select 'Yes' if you have accessed mental health services, and 'No' otherwise.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Answer each question honestly and accurately.
* If you're unsure about any question, please ask the patient for clarification.
* Review the patient's medical history before using this form.
* This form is designed to be used by healthcare professionals to assess patient health, not as a diagnostic tool.
