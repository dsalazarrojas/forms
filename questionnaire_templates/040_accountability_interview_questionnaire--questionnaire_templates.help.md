# Accountability Interview Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help interviewers conduct regular accountability interviews with employees or team members. The goal is to evaluate the interviewee's performance, progress, and areas for improvement, and to establish clear expectations and goals for the next period.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the necessary fields, ensuring all required fields are completed before moving on to the next section.
2. Use the provided fields to guide the discussion and evaluation of the interviewee's performance.
3. Take notes on any additional comments or observations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Section: -- Interview Information --
* **-- Interview Information --** (`section_interview_info`, `note`, required: false)
	+ This section is a placeholder for any general information about the interview, such as the purpose, date, and time of the meeting.
* **Interviewee Name** (`interviewee_name`, `text`, required: true)
	+ Please enter the name of the person being interviewed.
* **Job Title** (`interviewee_title`, `text`, required: true)
	+ Enter the job title or position of the interviewee.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required: true)
	+ Enter the department of the interviewee.
* **Interviewer Name** (`interviewer_name`, `text`, required: true)
	+ Enter your name and title as the interviewer.
* **Interview Date** (`interview_date`, `date`, required: true)
	+ Enter the date of the interview.

### Section: -- Accountability Questions --
* **Were the Goals Set in the Previous Review Period Achieved?** (`goals_met`, `select_one`, required: true)
	+ Select one of the options to indicate the interviewee's performance on achieving their goals.
* **Provide Details About Goal Achievement or Obstacles** (`goals_explanation`, `text`, required: true)
	+ Write a brief explanation of the interviewee's performance and any obstacles they faced.
* **How Would You Rate the Interviewee Taking Responsibility for Their Work?** (`responsibility_rating`, `select_one`, required: true)
	+ Select one of the options to rate the interviewee's level of responsibility.
* **How Consistently Were Deadlines Met?** (`deadline_adherence`, `select_one`, required: true)
	+ Select one of the options to indicate the interviewee's adherence to deadlines.
* **How Effectively Does the Interviewee Communicate Progress and Issues?** (`communication_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required: true)
	+ Select one of the options to rate the interviewee's communication skills.
* **Does the Interviewee Take Ownership When Problems Arise?** (`problem_ownership`, `select_one`, required: true)
	+ Select one of the options to indicate the interviewee's level of ownership.
* **How Does This Person Accountability Impact Their Team?** (`team_impact`, `text`, required: true)
	+ Write a brief description of the interviewee's impact on their team.
* **Key Strengths Observed** (`strengths`, `text`, required: true)
	+ List the key strengths observed in the interviewee.
* **Areas for Growth** (`areas_for_growth`, `text`, required: true)
	+ List areas where the interviewee can improve.
* **Action Items and Goals for Next Period** (`action_items`, `text`, required: true)
	+ Write a brief list of action items and goals for the next period.
* **Follow-Up Date** (`follow_up_date`, `date`, required: true)
	+ Enter the date for the next follow-up meeting or review.
* **Additional Notes or Observations** (`additional_notes`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Write any additional comments or observations about the interview.

## Tips

* Use this form as a guide to ensure a thorough and fair evaluation of the interviewee's performance.
* Be specific and honest when providing feedback and ratings.
* Use the follow-up date to schedule the next meeting or review.
* Keep the additional notes section for any additional comments or observations that did not fit in the other sections.
