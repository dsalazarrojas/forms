# Country Profile Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Country Profile Submission Form is designed to collect comprehensive information about a country, which will be used for internal purposes. This guide will help you understand what each field is for and how to fill it out correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Country Name field with the full name of the country you are submitting information for.
2. Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for the Country Flag field to indicate whether the country has a flag.
3. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the Country Location field to indicate the country's location (e.g., continent, region, or island).
4. Enter the population of the country in the Population field.
5. Enter the name of the capital city in the Capital City field.
6. Enter the official language of the country in the Official Language field.
7. Enter the region where the country is located in the Region field.
8. Choose a time zone for the country from the list of available options in the Time Zone field.
9. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the Currency field to indicate whether the country uses a unique currency.
10. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the Government Type field to indicate the type of government the country has (e.g., democracy, monarchy, or dictatorship).
11. Enter the area of the country in square kilometers in the Country Area field.
12. Enter the population density of the country in people per square kilometer in the Population Density field.
13. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the Official Holiday field to indicate whether the country has official holidays.
14. Enter the population growth rate of the country in percentage in the Population Growth Rate field.
15. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the Time Zone 2 field to indicate whether the country has a unique time zone.
16. Enter the population growth rate 2 of the country in percentage in the Population Growth Rate 2 field.
17. Enter the region 2 where the country is located in the Region 2 field.
18. Enter the population 2 of the country in the Population 2 field.
19. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the Government Type 2 field to indicate whether the country has a unique government type 2.
20. Enter the population density 2 of the country in people per square kilometer in the Population Density 2 field.
21. Enter the official language 2 of the country in the Official Language 2 field.
22. Select the time zone 3 for the country from the list of available options.
23. Enter the population 3 of the country.
24. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the Government Type 3 field to indicate whether the country has a unique government type 3.
25. Enter the region 3 where the country is located.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Country Name** (1, text, required): Enter the full name of the country.
* **Country Flag** (2, note, required): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the country has a flag.
* **Country Location** (3, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the country's location (e.g., continent, region, or island).
* **Population** (4, number, required): Enter the population of the country.
* **Capital City** (5, text, required): Enter the name of the capital city.
* **Official Language** (6, text, required): Enter the official language of the country.
* **Region** (7, text, required): Enter the region where the country is located.
* **Time Zone** (8, time, required): Select a time zone for the country from the list of available options.
* **Currency** (9, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the country uses a unique currency.
* **Government Type** (10, select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each type of government the country may have (e.g., democracy, monarchy, or dictatorship).
* **Country Area** (11, number, required): Enter the area of the country in square kilometers.
* **Population Density** (12, number, required): Enter the population density of the country in people per square kilometer.
* **Official Holiday** (13, select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the country has official holidays.
* **Population Growth Rate** (14, number, required): Enter the population growth rate of the country in percentage.
* **Time Zone 2** (15, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the country has a unique time zone.
* **Population Growth Rate 2** (16, number, required): Enter the population growth rate 2 of the country in percentage.
* **Region 2** (17, text, required): Enter the region 2 where the country is located.
* **Population 2** (18, number, required): Enter the population 2 of the country.
* **Government Type 2** (19, select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each type of government the country may have (e.g., democracy, monarchy, or dictatorship).
* **Population Density 2** (20, number, required): Enter the population density 2 of the country in people per square kilometer.
* **Official Language 2** (21, text, required): Enter the official language 2 of the country.
* **Time Zone 3** (22, time, required): Select the time zone 3 for the country from the list of available options.
* **Population 3** (23, number, required): Enter the population 3 of the country.
* **Government Type 3** (24, select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each type of government the country may have (e.g., democracy, monarchy, or dictatorship).
* **Region 3** (25, text, required): Enter the region 3 where the country is located.
