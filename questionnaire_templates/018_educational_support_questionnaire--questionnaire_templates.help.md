# Educational Support Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about trainees' goals, support needs, and resource requirements. The data collected will be used to identify areas of support needed by trainees and to allocate resources effectively.
## How To Complete This Form
Please complete this form by answering the following sections:

* **What are the trainees goals**
* **Select trainee(s)**
* **What are the educational support needs**
* **Select instructor(s)**
* **What tools are needed**
* **Tutoring hours requested**
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are the trainees goals**: Briefly describe what the trainee hopes to achieve through educational support.
* **Select trainee(s)**: Choose all trainees that apply to this educational support request.
* **What are the educational support needs**: List the specific areas where the trainee requires support. 
* **Select instructor(s)**: Choose all instructors that will provide support to the trainee.
* **What tools are needed**: List any educational tools or resources that the trainee requires to succeed.
* **Tutoring hours requested**: Indicate the number of hours of tutoring support required. 

# Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible in the required fields. This will help us to better understand your needs and provide more effective support.
* If you are unsure about any field, please do not hesitate to contact us and we will be happy to help.
