# Gaming Modding Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Gaming Modding Knowledge Quiz" form is designed to test your knowledge and experience in modding games. It will assess your understanding of modding concepts, tools, and practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully, as they are essential to understanding your knowledge and experience.
2. Enter your answers in the provided text fields.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
4. Click the "Submit" button to submit your answers.

* **What is modding** (`intro_text`, text, required: false): Please enter a brief description of what you understand modding to be.
* **Have you ever worked with modding tools** (`experience_years`, text, required: false): If you have experience with modding tools, describe how long you've been using them.
* **How often do you mod** (`modding_frequency`, text, required: false): Indicate how frequently you mod games, e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, or never.
* **What do you mod for** (`modding_goals`, text, required: false): Describe the reasons you mod games, e.g., to fix bugs, create new content, or improve performance.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: true): Click this button to submit your answers and complete the form.

## Tips
