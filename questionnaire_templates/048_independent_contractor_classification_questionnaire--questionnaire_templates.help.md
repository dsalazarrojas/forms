# Independent Contractor Classification Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Independent Contractor Classification Questionnaire is a tool used to gather information about independent contractors, their business structure, ownership, and payment terms to ensure accurate classification and compliance with relevant laws and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully to understand the different sections and what information is required.
2. Complete each section according to the instructions provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intro Section**: A brief introduction to the form and its purpose.
	+ This section is a note section meant to provide a brief overview of the form.
* **Contractor Type**: Select the type of independent contractor this is for. (e.g., Option 1, Option 2)
	+ This field requires you to classify the type of independent contractor, which can be used to further guide the completion of the form.
* **Classification Type**: Choose the correct classification type for the independent contractor. (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3)
	+ This field helps to determine the level of classification needed for the contractor, which can impact tax and employment laws.
* **Business Entity**: Enter the name of the business or entity the independent contractor is associated with. (e.g., ABC Corp, XYZ Ltd)
	+ This field is used to record the name of the business or entity associated with the independent contractor.
* **Business Structure**: Describe the structure of the independent contractor's business, if applicable. (e.g., partnership, LLC, sole proprietorship)
	+ This field allows you to document the structure of the business if it's applicable.
* **Ownership**: Identify the ownership type of the independent contractor's business. (e.g., sole, partnership, corporation)
	+ This field helps determine the level of ownership and its implications for tax and employment laws.
* **Business Relationship**: Describe the relationship between the independent contractor and the client company. (e.g., direct hire, subcontractor)
	+ This field is used to document the nature of the working relationship between the independent contractor and the client company.
* **Payment Terms**: Specify the payment terms agreed upon between the independent contractor and the client company. (e.g., hourly rate, project-based payment)
	+ This field helps establish the payment terms and conditions of the contract.
* **Tax Identifiers**: Enter any relevant tax identifiers (e.g., EIN, SSN) for the independent contractor. (e.g., 123-45-6789)
	+ This field records any relevant tax identifiers for the independent contractor.
* **Work Location**: Specify the location where the independent contractor works. (e.g., home office, client site)
	+ This field helps establish the work location and its implications for tax and employment laws.
* **Hours of Work**: Enter the total number of hours worked by the independent contractor. (e.g., 20 hours/week)
	+ This field records the total number of hours worked by the independent contractor.
* **Contract Start Date**: Enter the start date of the contract between the independent contractor and the client company. (e.g., 2022-01-01)
	+ This field records the start date of the contract.
* **Contract End Date**: Enter the end date of the contract between the independent contractor and the client company. (e.g., 2024-12-31)
	+ This field records the end date of the contract.
* **Contract Status**: Choose the current status of the contract. (e.g., active, inactive)
	+ This field helps establish the current status of the contract.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the independent contractor. (e.g., special skills, specific project details)
	+ This field provides a space for any additional comments or notes about the independent contractor.
* **Review Status**: Specify the current review status of the independent contractor. (e.g., pending, approved)
	+ This field helps establish the current review status of the independent contractor.
