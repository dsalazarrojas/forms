# Generational Diversity Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Generational Diversity Assessment is a tool to gauge the awareness and understanding of different generations, their communication styles, and the overall diversity of a team or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and select the most appropriate answer.
2. Answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Use the provided options for multiple-choice questions, or type in a short answer for open-ended questions.
4. Save your answers for future reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the assessment. Please read it carefully and proceed with the assessment.
* **Generation Awareness** (`2`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks about your awareness of different generations. Please select the option that best describes your understanding.
	+ Baby Boomer: Born between 1946 and 1964
	+ Gen X: Born between 1965 and 1980
	+ Gen Z: Born between 1997 and 2012
	+ Other: If you don't identify with any of the above options
* **Communication Style** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks about your communication style. Please select all options that best describe your approach to communication.
	+ Collaborative: You actively work with others to achieve a common goal.
	+ Assertive: You take charge and lead the conversation.
	+ Passive: You let others take charge and don't actively contribute.
	+ Aggressive: You dominate the conversation and don't listen to others.
* **Inclusion Policies** (`4`, `text`, required: false): This question asks about your organization's inclusion policies. Please provide a brief description of your organization's policies regarding diversity and inclusion.
* **Manager Leadership** (`5`, `text`, required: false): This question asks about your manager's leadership style. Please provide a brief description of your manager's approach to leadership.
* **Team Dynamics** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks about the team dynamics. Please select all options that best describe your experience with the team.
	+ High: The team is very cohesive and productive.
	+ Medium: The team has some challenges but is generally productive.
	+ Low: The team is not very cohesive and has significant challenges.
	+ None: You don't have a team or experience with teams.
* **Communication Channels** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks about the communication channels you use. Please select all options that best describe your communication style.
	+ In-person: You prefer face-to-face communication.
	+ Phone: You use phone calls to communicate.
	+ Email: You use email to communicate.
	+ Video: You use video calls or meetings.
* **Feedback** (`8`, `text`, required: false): This question asks about your experience with feedback. Please provide a brief description of how you receive and respond to feedback.
* **Inclusion Initiatives** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks about your organization's inclusion initiatives. Please select all options that best describe your organization's approach.
	+ True: Your organization has a clear commitment to diversity and inclusion.
	+ False: Your organization does not have a clear commitment to diversity and inclusion.
* **Action Plan** (`10`, `text`, required: false): This question asks about your action plan for improving diversity and inclusion. Please provide a brief description of your plan.
* **Next Steps** (`11`, `text`, required: false): This question asks about your next steps for improving diversity and inclusion. Please provide a brief description of your next steps.
* **Generational Diversity Impact** (`12`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks about the impact of generational diversity on your team or organization. Please select the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Positive: You experience a positive impact of generational diversity.
	+ Negative: You experience a negative impact of generational diversity.
	+ Neutral: You don't experience a significant impact of generational diversity.
* **Generational Diversity Score** (`13`, `number`, required: false): This question asks about your score for generational diversity. Please provide a brief description of your score.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`14`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the team or organization's diversity. Please select the option that best describes your experience.
	+ High: You are very satisfied with the diversity of your team or organization.
	+ Medium: You are somewhat satisfied with the diversity of your team or organization.
	+ Low: You are not very satisfied with the diversity of your team or organization.
	+ None: You don't have a team or organization to evaluate.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when completing this form.
* This form is for educational and exploratory purposes only.
* Please save your answers for future reference.
* This form is not a comprehensive tool for evaluating every aspect of a team or organization.
