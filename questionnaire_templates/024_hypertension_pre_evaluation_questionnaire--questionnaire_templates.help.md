## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Blood Pressure Measurement**: Enter your current blood pressure reading in mmHg (mm/Hg).
	+ Hint: This field is used to measure your blood pressure.
*  **Age**: Enter your age in years.
	+ Hint: This field is used to assess your age.
*  **Gender**: Select your gender.
	+ Hint: This field is used to understand your gender.
*  **Medical History**: Select any medical conditions you have been diagnosed with (Hypertension, Diabetes, Heart disease, Stroke, or Chronic kidney disease).
	+ Hint: This field helps us understand your medical history.
*  **Medication Use**: Select any medications you are currently taking (Beta blockers, ACE inhibitors, Calcium channel blockers, Potassium sparing diuretics, or Other).
	+ Hint: This field helps us understand your medication use.
*  **Physical Activity Level**: Select your level of physical activity (Sedentary, Moderately active, or Active).
	+ Hint: This field helps us understand your physical activity level.
*  **Smoking Status**: Select your smoking status (Yes, No, or Never smoked).
	+ Hint: This field helps us understand your smoking status.
*  **Alcohol Consumption**: Select your alcohol consumption level (Heavy drinker, Moderate drinker, Light drinker, Never drank, or Never drunk).
	+ Hint: This field helps us understand your alcohol consumption level.
*  **Height**: Enter your height in inches.
	+ Hint: This field is used to measure your height.
*  **Weight**: Enter your weight in pounds.
	+ Hint: This field is used to measure your weight.
*  **Family History**: Select any family members with a history of hypertension (Father, Mother, Sister, Brother, or Other).
	+ Hint: This field helps us understand your family history.
*  **Blood Test Results**: Enter the latest blood test results.
	+ Hint: This field is used to record your blood test results.
*  **Blood Test Results Date**: Enter the date of the blood test.
	+ Hint: This field is used to record the date of the blood test.
*  **Blood Test Results 1-9**: Enter the values for each blood test result.
	+ Hint: These fields are used to record the values for each blood test result.
*  **Blood Test Results 10**: Select the reference range for each blood test result (Low, Normal, or High).
	+ Hint: This field is used to select the reference range for each blood test result.
*  **Medication Dosage**: Select your medication dosage.
	+ Hint: This field is used to select your medication dosage.
*  **Blood Test Results 1-9**: Enter the values for each blood test result.
	+ Hint: These fields are used to record the values for each blood test result.

	+ Hint: This field is used to select the reference range for each blood test result.
