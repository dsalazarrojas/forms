# trauma_informed_care_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trauma-Informed Care Assessment form is designed to evaluate the level of trauma-informed care practices within an organization. This form helps staff assess their own practice and environment, as well as the overall facility and program, to identify areas where improvement is needed to create a trauma-informed environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
* Review each page and ensure you have answered all the questions.
* For questions with multiple options, select the one that best describes your practice or environment.
* For questions with a scale (e.g. Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied), consider the level of satisfaction you experience with each practice or environment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, optional): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please provide a short description of your experience with trauma-informed care practices.
* **Trauma-Informed Practices** (`2`, `select_one`, required): How often do you currently practice trauma-informed care? Always, Sometimes, or Never? Please select the one that best describes your practice.
* **Staff Training** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required): Have staff received training on trauma-informed care? Select True or False.
* **Resources Support** (`4`, `select_one`, required): How satisfied are you with the level of resources available to support staff in their practice? Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied? Please select the one that best describes your experience.
* **Communication** (`5`, `time`, required): How much time do you spend communicating with clients about their needs and experiences? Please enter a numeric value (e.g., 30 minutes, 1 hour, etc.).
* **Documentation** (`6`, `text`, optional): If you have any documentation or notes about your practice or environment that you would like to share, please enter them here.
* **Trauma-Informed Environment** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required): How difficult or easy is it to maintain a trauma-informed environment? Very Difficult, Somewhat Difficult, Neutral, Somewhat Easy, or Very Easy? Please select the one that best describes your experience.
* **Staff Support** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required): Do staff receive the support they need to maintain a trauma-informed practice? Select True or False.
* **Collaboration** (`9`, `select_one`, optional): How often do you collaborate with other departments or team members on trauma-informed care practices? Always, Sometimes, or Never? Please select the one that best describes your practice.
* **Resources Availability** (`10`, `select_one`, optional): How satisfied are you with the availability of resources to support staff in their practice? Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied? Please select the one that best describes your experience.
* **Trauma-Informed Practices (Facility)** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required): How satisfied are you with the level of trauma-informed care practices within the facility? Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied? Please select the one that best describes your experience.
* **Trauma-Informed Practices (Program)** (`12`, `select_one`, required): How often do you currently practice trauma-informed care within the program? Always, Sometimes, or Never? Please select the one that best describes your practice.
* **Resources (Facility)** (`13`, `select_multiple`, optional): Are there sufficient resources available to support trauma-informed care practices within the facility? Select Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
* **Resources (Program)** (`14`, `select_multiple`, optional): Are there sufficient resources available to support trauma-informed care practices within the program? Select Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about a question, consider discussing it with a staff member or supervisor.
* This form is designed to help you assess your current practice and identify areas where improvement is needed.
* Remember to review your answers and reflect on how they align with your goals and values as an organization.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or HR department.
