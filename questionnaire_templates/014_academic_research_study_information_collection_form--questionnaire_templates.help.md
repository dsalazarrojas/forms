# Academic Research Study Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to walk you through the steps to fill out the Academic Research Study Information Collection Form. Please read each section carefully and fill out the form accurately to ensure a successful match with relevant research studies.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely, paying attention to any required fields.
- Ensure you select all relevant research interests to increase your chances of being matched with suitable studies.
- Be honest when indicating your availability and preferred participation format.
- If you have any health conditions that may affect your participation, please disclose this information for the researchers' consideration.
- Finally, confirm your consent to be contacted about future research study opportunities and select your preferred method of contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`participant_first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name to help us get in touch with you.
* **Last name** (`participant_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name for our records.
* **Email address** (`participant_email`, email, required): This will be our primary contact method to communicate with you about research studies. Ensure it's accurate and up-to-date.
* **Phone number** (`participant_phone`, text, not required): This is your contact number in case we need to communicate with you via phone. It's optional, but it helps us reach you more efficiently.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Please enter your date of birth to verify your age and eligibility for some studies.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, not required): Select your gender to help us match you with studies suitable for your demographic.
* **Highest level of education completed** (`education_level`, select_one, required): Choose the level of education you've completed to help us match you with studies suitable for your background.
* **Current university or organization affiliation** (`current_affiliation`, text, required): Enter your current affiliation to help us match you with studies related to your work or institution.
* **Areas of research interest** (`research_interests`, select_multiple, required): Select all the areas of research that interest you to increase your chances of being matched with suitable studies.
* **Have you previously participated in academic research studies** (`previous_research_experience`, select_one, required): Indicate your experience in research to help us tailor the match process.
* **When are you generally available to participate in studies** (`availability`, select_multiple, required): Choose your available time slots to help us schedule studies that fit your schedule.
* **Preferred participation format** (`participation_format`, select_one, required): Select your preferred participation format, whether in-person or online.
* **Do you have any health conditions that may affect your participation?** (`health_conditions`, text, not required): If you have any health conditions that may impact your participation, please disclose this information for the researchers' consideration.
* **I consent to being contacted about future research study opportunities** (`contact_consent`, select_one, required): Confirm your consent to be contacted about future research studies.
* **Preferred method of contact** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred method of contact for future communication.
