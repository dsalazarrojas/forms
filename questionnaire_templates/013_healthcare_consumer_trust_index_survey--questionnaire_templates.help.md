# Healthcare Consumer Trust Index Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to measure healthcare consumers' trust levels in their healthcare experience. Please complete this survey to help us understand your perceptions and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and select the relevant options for each question.
2. For questions with multiple options (e.g., "Provide Provider Information"), choose the option that best describes your experience.
3. For questions with text fields (e.g., "questions", "questions2", etc.), provide detailed responses in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**:
	+ This is a brief overview of the survey.
	+ Please read this section carefully before proceeding.
* **Provide Provider Information**:
	+ Select the type of healthcare provider you are interacting with (e.g., Primary Care Doctor, Specialist Doctor, Both, or Other).
	+ This question helps us understand your relationship with your healthcare provider.
* **Patient Info**:
	+ Select one or more options to describe your demographic characteristics (e.g., Age, Gender, Ethnicity).
	+ This question helps us understand your background and demographics.
* **Trust Level**:
	+ Choose a level of trust that best describes your feelings towards your healthcare experience (High, Medium, Low).
	+ This question is critical in understanding your overall trust level in the healthcare system.
* **Questions 1-20**:
	+ Each of these questions requires a detailed text response.
	+ Please provide specific examples or descriptions that demonstrate your thoughts and feelings about your healthcare experience.
* **Demographic Info** (Optional):
	+ If you choose to provide additional demographic information, please do so in this text field.
	+ This is optional, but it can help us better understand your background and experiences.

**Tips**

* Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* If you have difficulty understanding a question, please ask for clarification.
* You can take as much time as you need to complete this survey.
* If you have any feedback or suggestions for improving this survey, please let us know.
