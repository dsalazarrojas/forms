# Personal Independence Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Independence Assessment form is a comprehensive tool used to evaluate an individual's capacity for personal independence. It is designed for care providers to assess the independence level of their clients, helping them make informed decisions about care and support needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Complete each section of the form carefully, ensuring all required fields are filled out accurately.**
2. **For pages with multiple options, select the most relevant answers that best describe the client's situation.**
3. **When answering care provider questions, consider the client's ability to perform daily tasks and manage their daily life.**
4. **For submission information, enter details about the completion of the form, including date and time of submission.**
