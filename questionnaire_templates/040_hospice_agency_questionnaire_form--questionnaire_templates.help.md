# Hospice Agency Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about a hospice agency to better understand its services and capabilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Read the form carefully and answer all the questions honestly and accurately.
* Provide information about the agency's contact person, address, phone number, and email.
* List the services offered by the agency, including hospice care, general medical care, and other services.
* Choose the type of agency, state, and type of services offered.
* Be sure to answer all questions in the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: A brief introduction to the agency.
*  **Name Of Agency** (`name_of_agency`): The name of the hospice agency.
*  **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): The name and contact information of the agency's representative.
*  **Address** (`address`): The physical address of the agency.
*  **Phone** (`phone`): The phone number of the agency.
*  **Email** (`email`): The email address of the agency.
*  **Agency Employees** (`agency_employees`): A list of the agency's employees.
*  **Services Offered** (`services_offered`): A list of services offered by the agency.
*  **Years In Business** (`years_in_business`): The number of years the agency has been in business.
*  **Certifications** (`certifications`): Any certifications the agency has.
*  **State License** (`state_license`): The state where the agency is licensed.
*  **Agency License Number** (`agency_license_num`): The agency's license number.
*  **State** (`state`): The state where the agency operates.
*  **Type of Agency** (`type_of_agency`): The type of agency (Home Health, Hospice, Skilled).
*  **Agency Type** (`agency_type`): The type of agency (Medicaid, Medicare, Private).
*  **Hospice Care Services** (`hospice_care_services`): A list of hospice care services offered.
*  **Hospice Services** (`hospice_services`): A list of hospice services offered.
*  **Hospice Services 2** (`hospice_services_2`): A list of additional hospice services offered.
*  **Hospice Care Services 2** (`hospice_care_services_2`): A list of additional hospice care services offered.
*  **Hospice Staffing** (`hospice_staffing`): A text field for information on the agency's staffing.
*  **Hospice Nursing Staffing** (`hospice_nursing_staffing`): A text field for information on the agency's nursing staffing.
*  **Hospice Physician Staffing** (`hospice_physician_staffing`): A text field for information on the agency's physician staffing.
*  **Hospice Other Staffing** (`hospice_other_staffing`): A text field for information on the agency's other staffing.
*  **Hospice Certification** (`hospice_certification`): A text field for information on the agency's certification.
*  **Hospice License** (`hospice_license`): A text field for information on the agency's license.

# Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for assistance.
* Please do not provide sensitive or confidential information.
* This form will be reviewed and verified by our team before any further action is taken.
