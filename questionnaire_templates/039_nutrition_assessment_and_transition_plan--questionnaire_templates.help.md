# nutrition-assessment-and-transition-plan - Help Guide
## Purpose
The nutrition-assessment-and-transition-plan form is designed to collect and create a detailed assessment and transition plan document. This will help individuals in need of a personalized plan to understand their dietary and nutritional needs, and create a clear path for achieving their health and wellness goals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review and understand the purpose of the form: To create an assessment and transition plan document for individuals or patients.
2. Start by filling out the `nutrition_assessment` page, providing the most relevant information for the assessment.
3. Progress to the `goals` page, where you'll define the objectives and outcomes for the transition plan.
4. Answer the `consent` question, confirming whether the plan is acceptable and understood.
5. In the `nutrition_plan` and `follow-up_plan` pages, outline the recommended nutrition plan and follow-up schedule, respectively.
6. Select the `nutrition-assessment-and-transition-plan-form-id` field to identify the form's unique ID.
7. Set the `nutrition-assessment-and-transition-plan-form-created-at` date to track when the form was created.
8. Confirm the `nutrition-assessment-and-transition-plan-form-updated-at` date to track any updates.
9. Indicate if the form was `nutrition-assessment-and-transition-plan-form-created-by` a specific person or entity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **nutrition_assessment** (1, text, required/optional): Describe the nutrition assessment results or any relevant medical history.
* **goals** (2, text, required/optional): Define the specific goals and objectives for the transition plan.
* **consent** (3, select_one, required/optional): Confirm that you understand and agree with the plan.
* **nutrition_plan** (4, text, required/optional): Outline the recommended nutrition plan, including dietary changes and recommendations.
* **follow-up_plan** (5, text, required/optional): Schedule and outline the follow-up plan, including check-ups and progress tracking.
* **nutrition_assessment_and_transition_plan_form_id** (6, select_one, required/optional): Select a unique form ID or provide an alternative ID if not using the provided options.
* **nutrition_assessment_and_transition_plan_form_created_at** (7, date, required/optional): Set the date when the form was created.
* **nutrition_assessment_and_transition_plan_form_updated_at** (8, date, required/optional): Confirm the date of any updates to the form.
* **nutrition_assessment_and_transition_plan_form_created_by** (9, select_one, required/optional): Indicate if the form was created by a specific person or entity.
