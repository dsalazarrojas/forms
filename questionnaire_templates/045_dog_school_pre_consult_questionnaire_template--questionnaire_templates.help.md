# Dog School Pre Consult Questionnaire Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog School Pre Consult Questionnaire Template is designed to gather information from pet owners before a pre-consultation meeting to discuss their dog's health, training, and behavioral concerns. This form aims to ensure that all relevant details are collected, enabling the consultant to provide the best possible guidance for the dog's well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the "Owner Information" section with your name, address, and contact details.
2.  Provide your dog's details in the "Dog Information" section.
3.  Answer the select questions according to the options provided.
4.  Add any additional information or notes about your dog in the "Notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Owner Information
*   **Owner Name**: A required field to collect your name.
*   **Owner Address**: An optional field for your address.
*   **Owner Phone**: An optional field for your phone number.
*   **Owner Email**: An optional field for your email address.
*   **Owner Notes**: A field for any additional comments or information about yourself.

### Dog Information
*   **Dog Name**: A required field to collect your dog's name.
*   **Dog Birthday**: An optional field for your dog's birthday.
*   **Dog Breed**: An optional field for your dog's breed.
*   **Dog Weight**: An optional field for your dog's weight.
*   **Dog Height**: An optional field for your dog's height.
*   **Allergies**: An optional field for any allergies or sensitivities your dog may have.
*   **Medical Conditions**: An optional field for any medical conditions your dog has.
*   **Dog Training Goals**: A select field to determine if your dog has any training goals or needs.
*   **Dog Training Level**: A select field to determine your dog's current training level.
*   **Dog Health Status**: A select field to determine your dog's overall health status.
*   **Dog Vaccinations**: A select field to determine if your dog is up-to-date on vaccinations.
*   **Dog Meds**: A select field to determine if your dog takes any medications.
*   **Training Schedule**: A select field to determine if you have any training schedule for your dog.
*   **Owner Consensus**: A select field to determine if you agree with the proposed training plan.
*   **Submission Status**: A select field to determine the current status of your submission.
*   **Notes**: A field for any additional comments or information about your dog.

### Contact Information
*   **Owner Notes**: A field for any additional comments or information about yourself.
