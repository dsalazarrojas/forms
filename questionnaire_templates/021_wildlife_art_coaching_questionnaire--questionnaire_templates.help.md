# Wildlife Art Coaching Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wildlife Art Coaching Questionnaire is designed to collect information about students' experiences and goals in the context of wildlife art coaching. It is intended to gather details about the student, their contact information, coaching sessions, art experience, art goals, art practices, art media, session notes, submission notes, and submission details.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each section carefully and complete the fields accordingly.
- Ensure you provide accurate and relevant information for each field.
- If a field is not relevant to your situation, leave it blank or indicate that it does not apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information (Page 1)**: This section includes fields for the student's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
  • **Student Information**: Enter the student's name, including their first and last name.
  • **Contact Information**: Enter the student's contact information, including their email address, phone number, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Information (Page 2)**: This section also includes fields for contact information and any other relevant details.
  • **Contact Information**: Enter the contact information, including email address, phone number, and any other relevant details.
* **Art Coaching (Page 3)**: This section is for the art coaching details.
  • **Art Coaching**: Enter any relevant details about the art coaching, including the coaching session date, time, and any other information.
* **Coaching Session (Page 4)**: This section is for the coaching session details.
  • **Coaching Session**: Enter any relevant details about the coaching session, including the session date, time, and any other information.
* **Art Experience (Page 5)**: This section is for the art experience details.
  • **Art Experience**: Enter any relevant details about the student's art experience, including their level of experience and any specific art media used.
* **Art Goals (Page 6)**: This section is for the art goals.
  • **Art Goals**: Enter any relevant details about the student's art goals, including their desired outcomes and any specific goals related to wildlife art.
* **Art Practices (Page 7)**: This section is for the art practices.
  • **Art Practices**: Enter any relevant details about the student's art practices, including their regular practice schedule and any specific art media used.
* **Art Media (Page 8)**: This section is for the art media used.
  • **Art Media**: Enter any relevant details about the art media used by the student, including their experience with different art forms.
* **Session Notes (Page 9)**: This section is for any notes from the coaching session.
  • **Session Notes**: Enter any relevant details about the coaching session, including any discussions or insights from the session.
* **Submission Notes (Page 10)**: This section is for any submission notes.
  • **Submission Notes**: Enter any relevant details about the submission, including any specific instructions or feedback received.
* **Submitted By (Page 11)**: This section is for the person submitting the form.
  • **Submitted By**: Select the correct option for the person submitting the form, such as Artist or Studio.
* **Submitted For (Page 11)**: This section is for who the form is submitted for.
  • **Submitted For**: Select the correct option for who the form is submitted for, such as Self or Parent/Teacher.
* **Submitted Date (Page 12)**: This section is for the submission date.
  • **Submitted Date**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Session Date (Page 12)**: This section is for the coaching session date.
  • **Session Date**: Enter the date of the coaching session.
* **Session Time (Page 12)**: This section is for the coaching session time.
  • **Session Time**: Enter the time of the coaching session.
* **Session Notes (Page 13)**: This section is for any notes from the coaching session.
  • **Session Notes**: Enter any relevant details about the coaching session, including any discussions or insights from the session.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when completing the form.
- Ensure you have permission from the student to collect their information.
- If you're unsure about any field, ask for clarification.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.
