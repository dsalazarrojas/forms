# Child And Youth Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from children and youth about their experiences in a program or activity. The questions cover various aspects of their involvement, including their personal experiences, relationships with staff, and overall satisfaction with the program. This information will help program administrators and staff improve the services and experiences they provide.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions truthfully and honestly.
2. Please answer all required questions (marked as "required: true").
3. If a question does not apply to you, answer "Not really" or "Prefer not to answer" as needed.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the "Any additional comments or suggestions?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your name** (`young_person_name`, required): Enter your name to identify yourself as the respondent.
* **Age** (`age`, required): Please enter your age.
* **Grade or school** (`grade_or_school`, optional): If you are currently in school, please enter your grade level or school name.
* **Program or activity** (`program_or_activity`, required): Enter the name of the program or activity you are involved in.
* **How long have you been in this program?** (`program_duration`, required): Choose how long you have been involved in the program.
* **How would you rate your overall experience?** (`overall_experience`, required): Rate your experience from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **What do you like most about the program?** (`favorite_aspect`, required): Share what you like most about the program.
* **What could be improved?** (`improvement_area`, optional): If you have any suggestions for improvement, enter them here.
* **Do the staff make you feel supported and valued?** (`staff_support`, required): Choose how you feel about the staff's support.
* **Have you made new friends through this program?** (`friends_made`, optional): Choose if you have made new friends through the program.
* **Have you learned new skills or knowledge?** (`learned_new_things`, required): Rate if you have learned new things through the program.
* **Has this program helped you gain confidence?** (`confidence_growth`, required): Rate if the program has helped you gain confidence.
* **Do you feel safe in this program?** (`feel_safe`, required): Choose if you feel safe in the program.
* **Do you feel welcomed and included?** (`welcome_feeling`, required): Choose how you feel about being welcomed and included.
* **Would you like to continue with this program?** (`future_participation`, required): Choose your intention to continue with the program.
* **Would you recommend this program to other young people?** (`recommend_program`, required): Choose if you would recommend the program to others.
* **Any additional comments or suggestions?** (`additional_comments`, optional): Share any additional thoughts or suggestions you have.
