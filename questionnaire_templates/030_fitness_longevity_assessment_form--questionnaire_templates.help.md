# Fitness Longevity Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Longevity Assessment Form is a questionnaire designed to help clients evaluate their current fitness levels and progress towards their goals. It is a critical tool for fitness professionals to understand the client's needs and create a tailored fitness plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out all required fields (marked as `required: true`).
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all applicable answers.
3. For text fields, provide as much detail as possible.
4. Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **clients-name (Text)** (`id: 2`): Client's Name
Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.

### 2. **clients-email (Email)** (`id: 3`): Client's Email
Enter your email address that you check regularly.

### 3. **program-type (Select One)** (`id: 4`): Program Type
Select one of the following:
* Yes
* No

Enter the duration of your current fitness program.

### 5. **frequency (Time)** (`id: 6`): Frequency
