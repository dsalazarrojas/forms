# Pharmaceutical Compliance Employment Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about employees in the pharmaceutical industry for compliance and auditing purposes. The data collected will help ensure that all employees are aware of and adhere to relevant regulations and guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your Company Information on page 1.
2. Enter Employee Information on page 2.
3. Enter Job Title and Job Description on page 3.
4. Enter Reporting Manager on page 4.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Information (1)** (`number`, required: false)
  This field is for providing company information such as the company name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Employee Information (2)** (`number`, required: false)
  This field is for providing employee information such as employee ID, name, and contact details.
* **Job Title (3)** (`text`, required: false)
  This field is for providing the job title of the employee, such as 'Pharmacist', 'Research Scientist', etc.
* **Job Description (4)** (`text`, required: false)
  This field is for providing a brief description of the job role and responsibilities of the employee.
* **Reporting Manager (5)** (`text`, required: false)
  This field is for providing the name and contact details of the reporting manager who the employee reports to.
