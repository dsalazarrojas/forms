# Send Your Message To The Universe - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a questionnaire designed to collect a user's thoughts, message, and contact information, with the intent of sending it to the universe. It is created using AI-generated form design and does not rely on any proprietary content from survey platforms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Type your message in the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (name)**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address to allow us to contact you, if necessary.
* **Message (message)**: Write your heartfelt message to the universe.
* **What do you want to share? (form_1)**: This field is intended to be a placeholder for a more specific question, but it's currently empty. Please feel free to type any message or thought you'd like to share with the universe.
