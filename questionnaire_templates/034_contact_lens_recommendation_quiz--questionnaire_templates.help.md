# Contact Lens Recommendation Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you get a personalized recommendation for contact lenses that suit your needs. Answer a few quick questions, and we'll provide you with tailored suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the form with your basic information, including your name (if you feel comfortable sharing it). This is optional, but it will help us understand your preferences better.
- Then, answer the questions about your age, which is helpful for recommending lenses suitable for your eye health.
- If you have a known prescription strength, feel free to enter it. This information will aid in narrowing down the options for you.
- After that, select the main reason you need contact lenses for (e.g., distance correction, near correction, etc.).
- Next, indicate how frequently you plan to wear contact lenses (e.g., daily, weekly, occasionally).
- Then, let us know your priority: do you care more about comfort or appearance?
- If you have any known eye sensitivities or allergies, choose that option.
- If you have a preference for a specific material type (soft, silicone hydrogel, rigid gas permeable, etc.), let us know.
- Finally, add any additional notes that might be helpful for us to provide you with the best recommendations.
