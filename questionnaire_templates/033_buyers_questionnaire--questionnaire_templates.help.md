# Buyers Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential buyers about their preferences and requirements for a home purchase. The goal is to understand their needs, expectations, and priorities to provide personalized guidance and recommendations for a successful home buying experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. Provide accurate and detailed information about your preferences and requirements.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Your full name as the potential buyer or contact person.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Your contact phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Your email address for communication and updates.
* **Alternate Contact Name** (`alternate_contact`, `text`, optional): If you have an alternate contact person, please provide their name and contact information.
* **Search Area** (`search_area`, `text`, required): The location or area you are looking to buy a home in.
* **Specific Neighborhoods Interested In** (`specific_neighborhoods`, `text`, optional): Any specific neighborhoods or areas you are particularly interested in.
* **Purchase Timeframe** (`purchase_timeframe`, `select_one`, required): When you plan to buy a home, choose from the following options:
	+ Ready now
	+ 30 days
	+ 60 days
	+ 90 days
	+ 6 months
	+ 1 year
	+ Flexible
* **Price Range** (`price_range`, `select_one`, required): Your budget for the home purchase:
	+ 150000 - 250000
	+ 250000 - 400000
	+ 400000 - 500000
	+ 500000 - 750000
	+ 750000 - 1000000
	+ 1000000+
* **Financing Ready** (`financing_ready`, `select_one`, required): Your pre-approval or pre-qualification status:
	+ Pre-approved
	+ Pre-qualified
	+ Getting loan approval
	+ Not yet
	+ Paying cash
* **Down Payment Available** (`down_payment`, `text`, optional): If you have a down payment amount or percentage ready, please provide it.
* **Preferred Property Type** (`property_type`, `select_one`, required): The type of home you are looking for:
	+ Single Family Home
	+ Condo
	+ Townhouse
	+ Multi-family
	+ Land
	+ New Construction
	+ Other
* **Number of Bedrooms** (`bedrooms`, `number`, required): The number of bedrooms you require.
* **Number of Bathrooms** (`bathrooms`, `number`, required): The number of bathrooms you require.
* **Lot Size Preference** (`lot_size`, `text`, optional): Your preference for lot size in acres or square feet.
* **Essential Features** (`essential_features`, `select_multiple`, optional): Any must-have amenities:
	+ Garage
	+ Backyard
	+ Pool
	+ Modern Kitchen
	+ Fireplace
	+ Hardwood Floors
	+ View
	+ Updated Bathroom
* **Lifestyle Preferences** (`lifestyle_preferences`, `text`, optional): Your lifestyle preferences, such as walkability or urban living.
* **School District Important** (`school_district`, `select_one`, required): How important is the quality of the school district:
	+ Very Important
	+ Important
	+ Somewhat Important
	+ Not Important
	+ Not Applicable
* **Commute Consideration** (`commute_consideration`, `select_one`, required): How important is commute distance:
	+ Very Important
	+ Important
	+ Not Important
	+ Flexible
* **Additional Needs or Comments** (`additional_needs`, `text`, optional): Any other information or needs you would like to share.

Note: This form is for general guidance and recommendations only. It is recommended that you provide your realtor with this information so they can better understand your needs and preferences.
