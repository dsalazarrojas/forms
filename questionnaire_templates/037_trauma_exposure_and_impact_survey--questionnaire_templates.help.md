# Trauma Exposure And Impact Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the trauma exposure and impact on individuals. It is a tool to help identify and understand the effects of traumatic events on a person's well-being and daily living.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Answer the questions truthfully to the best of your ability.
2. For the questions that have multiple options (e.g. "trauma_questions", "support_needs", "daily_living", "daily_living_score", "impact"), select all that apply.
3. Enter your answers in the required fields (e.g. "trauma_score", "impact_score") as a numerical value.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a brief introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Trauma Questions**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to each question about your recent traumatic exposure.
* **Trauma Score**: This is an optional numerical field where you can rate the intensity of your trauma experience. Please enter a whole number value (e.g. 1-10).
* **Support Needs**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have support needs due to your traumatic experience.
* **Daily Living**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate the impact of your traumatic experience on your daily living.
* **Daily Living Score**: This is an optional numerical field where you can rate the level of impact on your daily living. Please enter a whole number value (e.g. 1-10).
* **Emotional Reactions**: Please provide a brief description of your emotional reactions to your traumatic experience.
* **Coping Strategies**: Describe how you cope with your traumatic experience.
* **Impact Score**: This is an optional numerical field where you can rate the overall impact of your traumatic experience on your well-being. Please enter a whole number value (e.g. 1-10).
* **Impact**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate the overall impact of your traumatic experience on your well-being.
* **Emotional Reactions**: Please provide a brief description of your emotional reactions to your traumatic experience.

## Tips
* Please answer each question based on your personal experience and feelings.
* If you're unsure about any question, please answer "No" or don't worry about it.
* You can skip fields that are not applicable or relevant to your situation.
