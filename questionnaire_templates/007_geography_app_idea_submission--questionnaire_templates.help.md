# Geography App Idea Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your geography app idea submission. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to help us review and understand your idea.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select your user type (Student, Educator, or Product Team)
2.  Enter a brief description of your geography app idea.
3.  Choose a category (Geography, History, Social Studies, or Other)
4.  Describe your app's key features.
5.  Specify your target audience (Students, Educators, or General Public)
6.  Select the platforms your app is available on (Mobile, Web, or Desktop)
7.  Choose how you would like to follow-up with our team (Email, Phone, or Chat)
8.  Select the status of your submission (Approved, Rejected, or Pending)
9.  Enter the date of your submission.
10. Enter your email address.
11. Enter your phone number.
12. Select the date of your review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **UserType** (Student, Educator, Product Team): Select the type of user you are.
* **AppConcept** (text): Briefly describe your geography app idea.
* **IdeaDescription** (text): Briefly describe your geography app idea.
* **Category** (Geography, History, Social Studies, Other): Choose the category your app falls under.
* **Features** (text): Describe your app's key features.
* **TargetAudience** (Students, Educators, General Public): Specify your target audience.
* **Platform** (Mobile, Web, Desktop): Select the platforms your app is available on.
* **FollowUp** (Email, Phone, Chat): Choose how you would like to follow-up with our team.
* **ReviewStatus** (Approved, Rejected, Pending): Select the status of your submission.
* **SubmissionDate** (date): Enter the date of your submission.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **ReviewDate** (date): Select the date of your review.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Use proper format for dates (DD/MM/YYYY).
* Review and proofread your submission for errors before submitting.
