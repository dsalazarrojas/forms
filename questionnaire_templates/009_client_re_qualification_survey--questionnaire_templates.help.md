# Client Re Qualification Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Re Qualification Survey is designed to gather essential information about your clients' experiences, preferences, and needs to help improve our services and better serve your business interests. This survey will help us understand your satisfaction, identify areas for improvement, and determine potential new service opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information (Client Name, Primary Contact Person, Contact Email, and Contact Phone).
2. Select the current size of your company (Current Company Size).
3. If applicable, describe any recent changes in your business (Have there been recent changes in your business?).
4. Share your current business goals (What are your current business goals?).
5. Rate your satisfaction with our services (How satisfied are you with our services to date?).
6. Indicate the value you've received from our services (Have our services delivered value to your business?).
7. If you have new service needs or requirements, describe them (Do you have any new service needs?).
8. Select any additional services you're interested in (Are you interested in additional services?).
9. Choose the service areas you're most interested in (Which service areas interest you most?).
10. If your budget for services has changed, indicate how (Has your budget for services changed?).
11. Let us know if you'd recommend us to others (Would you recommend us to others?).
12. Provide suggestions for improving our services (Suggestions for improving our services).
12. Select your preferred contact method (Preferred Contact Method).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Please enter your company's or business's name.
* **Primary Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for contact and communication with us.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address we can use to contact you.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number we can use to contact you.
* **Current Company Size**: Select the number of employees in your company.
* **Have there been recent changes in your business?**: If applicable, describe any changes, such as personnel, structure, direction, or product lines.
* **What are your current business goals?**: Share your current business objectives or objectives you aim to achieve.
* **How satisfied are you with our services to date?**: Rate your satisfaction with a scale.
* **Have our services delivered value to your business?**: Indicate if our services have provided significant, good, some, little, or no value.
* **Do you have any new service needs?**: If applicable, describe any new requirements or needs you have.
* **Are you interested in additional services?**: Select if you're interested in exploring more services.
* **Which service areas interest you most?**: Choose all areas that interest you.
* **Has your budget for services changed?**: If applicable, indicate if your budget has increased, remains the same, or decreased.
* **Would you recommend us to others?**: Rate your willingness to recommend us.
* **Suggestions for improving our services**: Provide any ideas or suggestions for improvement.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select your preferred method of contact, such as email, phone, video call, or in-person meeting.
