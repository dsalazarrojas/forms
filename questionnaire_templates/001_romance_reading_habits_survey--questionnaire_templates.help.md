# Romance Reading Habits Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Romance Reading Habits Survey is designed to gather information about your reading habits, preferences, and experiences. This form will help us understand your favorite book genres, authors, formats, and reading behaviors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Start by filling out the intro page, which will give us a brief overview of your reading habits.
2. On the following pages, select your preferred book genres, favorite authors, and other reading habits.
3. Choose how often you read aloud, to whom, and in what format.
4. Indicate your reading speed and favorite reading format.
5. Finally, provide any additional information about your reading habits.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intro** (`1`, text, optional): This is the introduction page where you can briefly describe your reading habits.
* **reading_habits** (`2`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the book genres you enjoy reading, such as Romance, Historical Fiction, Mystery, etc.
* **Favorite Author** (`3`, text, optional): Type the name of your favorite author.
* **Favorite Book** (`4`, text, optional): Type the title of your favorite book.
* **Genre Preferred** (`5`, select_one, optional): Choose the genre you prefer among the options listed.
* **Age Range** (`6`, select_multiple, optional): Select the age range you fall into.
* **Read Aloud** (`7`, text, optional): Type how you read aloud (e.g., to yourself, to others, etc.).
* **Read Aloud Frequency** (`8`, select_one, optional): Select how often you read aloud, such as Daily, Weekly, Monthly, etc.
* **Read Aloud To** (`9`, text, optional): Type to whom you read aloud (e.g., spouse, children, friends, etc.).
* **Read Aloud To** (`10`, select_multiple, optional): Select to whom you read aloud, such as Spouse/Partner, Children, Friends, etc.
* **Read Aloud Format** (`11`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the format in which you read aloud, such as Audiobook, E-Book, Print Book, etc.
* **Reading Speed** (`12`, text, optional): Type how quickly you read.
* **Read Aloud Speed** (`13`, text, optional): Type how quickly you read aloud.
* **Favorite Format** (`14`, select_one, optional): Select your favorite format, such as Audiobook, E-Book, etc.
* **Bookshelf Size** (`15`, text, optional): Type the size of your bookshelf.
* **Bookshelf Type** (`16`, text, optional): Type the type of bookshelf you use.
* **Favorite Character** (`17`, text, optional): Type the name of your favorite book character.
* **Book Club** (`18`, text, optional): Type the name of your book club.
* **Book Club Size** (`19`, text, optional): Type the size of your book club.
* **Favorite Author** (`20`, text, optional): Type the name of your favorite author.
* **Author Pseudonym** (`21`, text, optional): Type the pseudonym of your favorite author.
* **Genre Favored** (`22`, select_one, optional): Choose the genre you favor, such as Romance, Historical, etc.
* **Read Aloud Time** (`23`, text, optional): Type the time you spend reading aloud.
* **Read Aloud Time Frequency** (`24`, select_one, optional): Select how often you read aloud, such as Daily, Weekly, etc.
* **Other Reading Habits** (`25`, text, optional): Type any other reading habits not listed above.
