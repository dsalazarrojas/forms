# Website Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Website Questionnaire Form is designed to collect feedback from users about a specific website. It is meant to gather information about the user's experience, preferences, and ratings of the website. By filling out this form, users can provide valuable insights that can help us improve and better serve them in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with your real name and contact information.
2. Select the type of website you visited and your rating of it.
3. Answer the questions about your experience and preferences on the website.
4. Add any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Website URL** (Text): Enter the URL of the website you visited.
* **User Email** (Email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **User Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **User Name** (Text): Enter your name to associate with your feedback.
* **Feedback** (Note): Share your thoughts and feedback about your experience on the website.
* **Comments** (Text): Add any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Website Purpose** (Select One): Choose the purpose of the website you visited.
* **Website Preferences** (Select Multiple): Choose the options that best describe your website preferences.
* **Date Visited** (Date): Enter the date you visited the website.
* **Time Visited** (Time): Enter the time you visited the website.
* **Rating** (Number): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments Count** (Number): Please do not fill out this field.
* **Rating Scale** (Select One): Choose the scale you used to rate your experience.
* **Feedback Frequency** (Select Multiple): Choose the frequency of your feedback.
* **Website Traffic** (Select Multiple): Choose the traffic level of the website.
* **User Experience** (Select One): Choose your level of experience on the website.
* **Website Design** (Select One): Choose the website design you prefer.
* **Website Usability** (Select One): Choose the website usability you prefer.
* **User Frustration Level** (Select Multiple): Choose your level of frustration on the website.
* **Website Purpose Scale** (Select Multiple): Choose the purpose of the website you visited.
* **User Satisfaction Level** (Select Multiple): Choose your level of satisfaction on the website.
* **Website Traffic Scale** (Select Multiple): Choose the traffic of the website.
* **User Experience Scale** (Select Multiple): Choose your level of experience on the website.
* **User Satisfaction Scale** (Select Multiple): Choose your level of satisfaction on the website.
* **Additional Comments** (Note): Add any additional comments or feedback you may have.
