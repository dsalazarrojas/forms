# Fitness Football Activity Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from footballers about their activities and medical conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, height, and age.
2. Select your weight range.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Provide your phone number.
5. Answer the medical condition and exercise activity questions.
6. Enter the date and time of your last injury.
7. Enter any additional notes.
8. Select the person this form is assigned to.
9. Confirm if an email or phone call has been sent to you.
10. Indicate if you have finished with the activities.
