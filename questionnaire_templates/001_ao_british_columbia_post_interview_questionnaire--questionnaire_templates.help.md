# AO - British Columbia Post Interview Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from interviewers about a candidate's performance after an interview. It helps assess their suitability for a position in British Columbia and provides valuable insights for future interviews.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the candidate's full name in the **Full name of the candidate interviewed** field.
2.  Enter the position the candidate applied for in the **Position the candidate applied for** field.
3.  Record the date of the interview in the **Date of the interview** field.
4.  Enter your name as the **Name of the interviewer**.
5.  Rate the candidate's **Overall impression**, **Communication skills**, **Technical knowledge**, and **Cultural fit** based on your assessment. Select the option that best reflects your opinion.
6.  List the candidate's key **Strengths observed during the interview**.
7.  (Optional) Provide areas for improvement in the **Areas could the candidate improve on** field.
8.  Rate the candidate's interest level in the position in the **How interested did the candidate seem in the position** field.
9.  Select whether you **Would you recommend this candidate for the position** based on your assessment.
10. Add any **Additional comments or observations** about the candidate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full name of the candidate interviewed**: Enter the candidate's full name as it appears on their resume or ID.
*   **Position the candidate applied for**: Record the position the candidate was interviewed for.
*   **Date of the interview**: Enter the date the interview took place.
*   **Name of the interviewer**: Enter your name as the person who conducted the interview.
*   **Overall impression**: Select the option that best reflects your overall impression of the candidate.
*   **Communication skills**: Rate the candidate's communication skills, with 1 being poor and 5 being excellent.
*   **Technical knowledge**: Rate the candidate's technical knowledge, with 1 being poor and 5 being excellent.
*   **Cultural fit**: Rate the candidate's cultural fit, with options for strongly aligned, somewhat aligned, neutral, somewhat misaligned, and not aligned.
*   **Key strengths**: List the candidate's key strengths observed during the interview.
*   **Areas could the candidate improve on**: Provide areas where the candidate could improve.
*   **How interested did the candidate seem in the position**: Rate the candidate's interest level, with options for very interested, somewhat interested, neutral, not very interested, and not interested at all.
*   **Would you recommend this candidate for the position**: Select whether you would recommend the candidate for the position.
*   **Additional comments or observations**: Provide any further notes about the candidate.
