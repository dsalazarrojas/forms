# TPMS Compatibility Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The TPMS Compatibility Assessment form is designed to gather information about a vehicle's compatibility with a TPMS (Tire Pressure Monitoring System) system. This form is used to evaluate the fitment and functionality of a TPMS system on a specific vehicle model.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the vehicle information sections with your vehicle's details.
* Select the correct options for transmission, drive type, engine type, drive position, drive wheel, and TPMS system from the provided options.
* Enter the vehicle length, width, height, and other relevant measurements in the correct units.
* Provide any additional remarks or comments about the vehicle.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, optional): Enter the make of your vehicle (e.g., Toyota, Ford, Honda).
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, optional): Enter the model of your vehicle (e.g., Camry, F-150, Civic).
* **Year** (`year`, number, required): Enter the year of your vehicle's manufacture.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, text, required): Enter the type of vehicle (e.g., Car, Truck, SUV).
* **Transmission** (`transmission`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of transmission your vehicle has (e.g., Automatic, Manual, CVT, DCT, HMT, SDC).
* **Drive Type** (`drive_type`, select_one, required): Select the drive type of your vehicle (e.g., FWD, RWD, AWD).
* **Vehicle Length** (`vehicle_length`, number, optional): Enter the length of your vehicle in inches.
* **Wheelbase** (`wheelbase`, number, optional): Enter the wheelbase of your vehicle in inches.
* **Engine Type** (`engine_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of engine your vehicle has (e.g., Gasoline, Diesel, Hybrid, Electric).
* **Drive Position** (`drive_position`, select_one, required): Select the drive position of your vehicle (e.g., Rear, Front, All).
* **Drive Wheel** (`drive_wheel`, select_one, required): Select the drive wheel of your vehicle (e.g., Left, Right).
* **Transmission Ratio** (`transmission_ratio`, number, optional): Enter the transmission ratio of your vehicle (e.g., gear ratio).
* **Gear Ratio** (`gear_ratio`, number, optional): Enter the gear ratio of your vehicle (e.g., gear ratio).
* **Vehicle Weight** (`vehicle_weight`, number, optional): Enter the weight of your vehicle in kg.
* **Vehicle Height** (`vehicle_height`, number, optional): Enter the height of your vehicle in inches.
* **Vehicle Width** (`vehicle_width`, number, optional): Enter the width of your vehicle in inches.
* **Vehicle Length Units** (`vehicle_length_units`, text, required): Enter the units used to measure the vehicle length (e.g., mm, ft, in).
* **Vehicle Width Units** (`vehicle_width_units`, text, required): Enter the units used to measure the vehicle width (e.g., mm, ft, in).
* **Vehicle Height Units** (`vehicle_height_units`, text, required): Enter the units used to measure the vehicle height (e.g., mm, ft, in).
* **TPMS System** (`tpms_system`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of TPMS system your vehicle has (e.g., Standard, Premium, Luxury, Sport).
* **TPMS System Type** (`tpms_system_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of TPMS system your vehicle uses (e.g., electronic, mechanical).
* **TPMS Version** (`tpms_version`, text, optional): Enter the version of the TPMS system on your vehicle (e.g., v1, v2, v3).
* **Manufacturer** (`manufacturer`, text, optional): Enter the manufacturer of your vehicle (e.g., Toyota, Ford, Honda).
* **Part Number** (`part_number`, text, optional): Enter the part number of your vehicle's TPMS system (e.g., ABC123, DEF456).
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or remarks about your vehicle.

Note: This form should only be completed by authorized personnel with access to vehicle information.
