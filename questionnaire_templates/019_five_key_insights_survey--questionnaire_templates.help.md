# Five Key Insights Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Five Key Insights Survey is designed to gather valuable information about a participant's experience and perceptions of a project or event. This form will help you identify key takeaways, comments, and other relevant details to improve future projects or events.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Project Title**: Enter the title of the project or event you are participating in.
2.  **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification or business card.
3.  **Event Date**: If applicable, enter the date of the project or event you are participating in.
4.  **Top 1 Takeaway**: Choose the most important takeaway from the provided options.
5.  **Top 2 Takeaway**: Choose the second most important takeaway from the provided options.
6.  **Top 3 Takeaway**: Choose the third most important takeaway from the provided options (optional).
7.  **Top 4 Takeaway**: Choose the fourth most important takeaway from the provided options (optional).
8.  **Top 5 Takeaway**: Choose the fifth most important takeaway from the provided options (optional).
9.  **Comment 1**: Enter any relevant comments or observations about the project or event.
10. **Comment 2**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the project or event.
11. **Comment 3**: Enter any further comments or observations about the project or event (optional).
12.  **Project Status**: Choose the current status of the project or event.
13.  **Project Outcome**: Choose the outcome of the project or event.
14.  **Event Type**: Choose the type of event or project.
15.  **Participant Type**: Choose your role in the project or event.
16.  **Position**: Choose your current position (optional).
17.  **Company**: Enter your company name.
18.  **Department**: Enter your department name (optional).
19.  **Job Title**: Enter your job title (optional).
20.  **Email**: Enter your email address (optional).
