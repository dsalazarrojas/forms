# Creative Writing Prompt Generator - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to generate a creative writing prompt based on your input. Fill out the form with your preferences, and you'll receive a prompt to help spark your writing inspiration.

## How To Complete This Form

To get started, simply select your options and fill out the form. You can choose to answer all or some of the questions, depending on your needs. The form consists of the following sections:

* Generate Prompt: Choose whether you want a good or fair prompt.
* First Name: Enter your first name (if desired).
* Last Name: Enter your last name (if desired).
* Email: Enter your email address (if desired).
* Writing Group: Select whether you are part of a writing group or not.
* Prompt Type: Choose whether you want a prompt for your writing group or not.
* Prompt Length: Select the length of the prompt you want.
* Prompt Description: Add a brief description of what you are looking for in your prompt.
* Notes: Add any additional notes about your prompt preferences.
* Prompt: This is where your generated prompt will be displayed.
* Share With: Select who you want to share this prompt with.
* Share Link: Select whether you want to share a link to the prompt or not.
* Share Link Type: This field is not applicable as it's a duplicate of the share_with field.
* Submit: Click this button to generate your prompt.

* **Generate Prompt** (`Generate Prompt`, `select_one`, optional):
