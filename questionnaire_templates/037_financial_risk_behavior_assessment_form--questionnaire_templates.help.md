# Financial Risk Behavior Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Financial Risk Behavior Assessment Form is a tool to help individuals evaluate and assess their financial risk behavior. It is designed to identify potential risks in an individual's financial decisions and actions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select each option that best describes your current financial situation or behavior. For example:
	* If you have taken out a loan in the past year, select "Yes" for the "Client Onboarding" field.
	* If you regularly review your investment portfolio, select "Yes" for the "Portfolio Reviews" field.
2. Answer each question accurately and honestly to get the most useful information from the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Risk Assessment** (`financial_risk_behavior_assessment`):
	+ Select all options that apply to your financial behavior.
* **Client Onboarding** (`client_onboarding`):
	+ If you have taken on debt in the past year, have had a loan application approved, or have engaged in other financial actions that may indicate a higher risk level, select "Yes".
* **Portfolio Reviews** (`portfolio_reviews`):
	+ If you regularly review your investment portfolio, either in person or online, select "Yes".
* **Risk Tolerance** (`risk_factors`):
	+ If you are comfortable taking on risks, such as investing in stocks or real estate, select "Yes". If you are more risk-averse, select "No".
* **Financial History** (`financial_history`):
	+ If you have a financial history of taking on debt, overspending, or engaging in other high-risk behaviors, select "Yes".
* **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors`):
	+ If you are aware of specific risk factors that affect your financial decisions, such as health issues or financial dependence on others, select "Yes". This field is meant to identify potential risk factors, not to assign blame.
* **Risk Tolerance Level** (`risk_tolerance_level`):
	+ This field is intended for users who have already identified specific risk levels. If you have a specific risk tolerance level (e.g., 2.5), select that number. If you don't have a specific risk level, select "No".
* **Risk-Free Time** (`risk_free_time`):
	+ If you feel you have a "risk-free" period, such as a financial cushion or emergency fund, select "Yes". If you do not have such a cushion, select "No".
