# Yoga Student Pre Session Readiness Check In - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess a yoga student's readiness for an upcoming session. It will help the instructor tailor the session to the student's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions on the first page (page 1) as honestly as possible.
2. Choose the options that best fit your situation for each question.
3. Leave blank any fields that are not applicable or not relevant to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your current level of flexibility** (`pre_session_questions`, select_multiple, required: false): This question is asking about your current level of flexibility. Please choose one or more options that best fit your situation. You can choose:
	+ Very flexible
	+ Somewhat flexible
	+ Not very flexible
* **Do you have any physical injuries or conditions** (`physical_injuries`, text, required: false): Please list any physical injuries or conditions you may have. If none, leave this field blank.
* **Yoga experience** (`yoga_experience`, select_one, required: false): This question is asking about your level of experience with yoga. Please choose the option that best fits your experience. You can choose:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
	+ Expert
* **Are you prepared for class** (`pre_session_preparations`, select_one, required: false): Please answer "Yes" if you are physically prepared for class, or "No" if not.
* **Availability** (`availability`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide any additional information about your availability.
* **Emergency contact information** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): Please provide the name and number of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Relationship with emergency contact** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required: false): Please describe your relationship with the person you listed as your emergency contact.

## Tips
* Answer each question honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
* If you are unsure or have any concerns, please contact the instructor.
* Please fill out this form at least 10 minutes before the session to ensure the instructor has time to review your answers and prepare accordingly.
