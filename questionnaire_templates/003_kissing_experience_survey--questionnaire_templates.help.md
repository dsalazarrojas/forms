# Kissing Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Kissing Experience Survey is a set of questions designed to collect information about users' preferences, experiences, and comfort levels related to kissing. This survey aims to gather data for research purposes, and by filling out this form, you will contribute to helping us better understand people's attitudes and feelings about kissing.

## How To Complete This Form

- Fill out the form on one page at a time.
- For each page, answer the questions or choose the options that best match your preferences or experiences.
- Make sure to fill in all required fields (marked as "true" in the field descriptions).
- You can save your progress and come back later to finish the form if you need more time.
- When you're ready to submit, click the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (1/10)
	+ A free text field where you can write any additional information you'd like to share about your kissing experiences.
* **How Often Do You Kiss** (2/10)
	+ Choose the frequency with which you kiss, from "1" (rarely) to "5" (often).
* **Favorite Kissing Positions** (3/10)
	+ Select your most enjoyable kissing positions from the options provided: Top, Side, Chin, Lips, Cheek, or Other (please specify).
* **What Is The Most Comfortable Kissing Position For You** (4/10)
	+ Choose the option that best describes your comfort level with kissing, from "True" (comfortable), "False" (uncomfortable), or "Undecided" (neutral).
* **What Is Your Preferred Frequency Of Kissing** (5/10)
	+ Select all the frequencies with which you prefer to kiss, such as "Yes" for frequent or "No" for infrequent.
* **How Long Do You Like To Kiss** (6/10)
	+ Choose the duration of your kisses, such as "Yes" for longer than usual or "No" for shorter than usual.
* **Note To Researcher** (7/10)
	+ This is an optional text field where you can provide any additional comments or insights about your kissing experiences.
* **Email** (8/10)
	+ Enter your email address to help us contact you about your survey responses.
* **Phone** (9/10)
	+ Enter your phone number, which will be used to reach out to you about your survey responses (optional).
* **Email (Optional)** (10/10)
	+ Enter an optional email address for the researcher to contact you.
* **Phone (Optional)** (11/10)
	+ Enter an optional phone number for the researcher to contact you.
* **Researcher Email** (12/10)
	+ Enter the researcher's email address to help us contact them about your survey responses.
* **Researcher Phone** (13/10)
	+ Enter the researcher's phone number to help us contact them about your survey responses.
* **Submission Date** (14/10)
	+ Enter the date of your submission, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

Please fill out the form carefully, and don't hesitate to contact us if you have any questions or need further clarification.
