# Language Practice Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Language Practice Survey is a form designed to collect information about learners and teachers' habits, challenges, and progress in practicing and teaching a language. The survey aims to gather data to help improve the effectiveness of language education programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible. You can use the "Hint" field for any additional information you want to provide.
2. Review your answers for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, `text`, optional):
	+ This field allows you to provide any additional information about your language practice habits or preferences.
* **Learner Habits** (`learner_habits`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Please select all the options that apply to your language practice habits:
		- Never
		- Daily
		- Several times a week
		- Almost every day
* **Progress Assessment** (`progress_assess`, `number`, optional):
	+ If applicable, please provide a numerical value for your progress assessment. This could be a percentage or a number representing your progress.
* **Challenge Assessment** (`challenge_assess`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Please select all the options that apply to your challenges when practicing a language:
		- Grammar
		- Vocab
		- Pronunciation
		- Listening
* **Feedback Request** (`feedback_req`, `text`, optional):
	+ If you have any feedback or suggestions for language education, please provide them here.
* **Support Assessment** (`support_assess`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Please select how often you provide support to learners:
		- Not at all
		- Rarely
		- Sometimes
		- Often
* **Teacher Input** (`teacher_input`, `text`, optional):
	+ This field is for teachers to provide any additional information about their teaching practices.
* **Learner Challenges** (`learner_challenges`, `text`, optional):
	+ Please describe some common challenges learners face when practicing a language.
* **Teacher Challenges** (`teacher_challenges`, `text`, optional):
	+ Please describe some common challenges teachers face when teaching a language.
* **Support Provided** (`support_provides`, `text`, optional):
	+ Please describe the kind of support you provide to learners.
* **Learner Progress** (`learner_progress`, `text`, optional):
	+ Please describe how you measure learner progress.
* **Teacher Progress** (`teacher_progress`, `text`, optional):
	+ Please describe how you measure teacher progress.
