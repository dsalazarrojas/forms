# Entry Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Entry Questionnaire" form is designed to collect information about entry or intake processes, including program details, dates, staff involvement, and contact information. This form helps ensure accurate and complete data collection for subsequent processing or analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Program name** in the "Program name" field, which is used to identify the specific program or initiative being processed.
2. Select the **Date of intake** in the "intake_date" field, which is typically the date when the program or process began.
3. Provide a brief **Reason for entry** in the "entry_reason" field, which helps explain the purpose or context of the process.
4. Enter the name of the **Staff member** responsible for the entry or intake process in the "intake_staff_member" field.
5. Specify the **Entry location**, which is the physical or virtual location where the process occurred.
6. Enter the **Contact email**, which is used to reach out for further information or clarification.
7. Provide the **Contact phone number**, which is an alternative contact method for communication.
8. If necessary, enter any additional **Other** details in the "entry_reason_other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Program name
* **Program name (program_name)**: The name of the program or initiative being processed.
### Intake Date
* **Intake Date (intake_date)**: The date when the program or process began.
### Reason for Entry
* **Reason for Entry (entry_reason)**: A brief explanation of the purpose or context of the process.
### Staff Member
* **Staff Member (intake_staff_member)**: The name of the staff member responsible for the entry or intake process.
### Entry Location
* **Entry Location (entry_location)**: The physical or virtual location where the process occurred.
### Contact Email
* **Contact Email (contact_email)**: The email address used for further information or clarification.
### Contact Phone Number
* **Contact Phone Number (contact_phone)**: An alternative contact method for communication.
### Other Details
* **Other (entry_reason_other)**: Additional details that don't fit in the previous categories.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely to avoid any confusion or issues in the processing or analysis of the form data.
* If unsure about any field, refer back to this help guide or consult with the relevant staff member for clarification.
* Use the "Other" field only when necessary and relevant to provide additional context.
* Double-check for any formatting or syntax issues in date fields to ensure correct parsing.
