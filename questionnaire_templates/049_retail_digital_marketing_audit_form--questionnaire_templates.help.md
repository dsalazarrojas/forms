# Retail Digital Marketing Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on a business's retail digital marketing channels, performance, and goals. The information gathered will help businesses understand their current state and identify opportunities for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select all the main retail channels your company uses (e.g., Online Store, Social Media, Email).
2. Rate the performance of each selected channel (e.g., Sales, Engagement, Conversion Rate).
3. Choose the KPIs (Key Performance Indicators) you use to measure performance.
4. Enter your current sales volume.
5. Enter your conversion rate.
6. Select the sales channel with the highest conversion rate.
7. Describe your target audience segment (optional).
8. Describe the key characteristics of your target audience (optional).
9. Select your primary goal for sales performance (e.g., Increase sales, Improve engagement, Increase brand awareness).
10. Rate your current sales volume (optional).
11. Select your primary goal for conversion rate (e.g., Increase conversion rate, Improve engagement, Increase brand awareness).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

	+ This question asks you to select all the main retail channels your company uses, such as Online Store, Social Media, and Email.

* **Which KPIs do you use to measure performance?**
	+ Choose the KPIs that are most relevant to your business, such as Sales, Engagement, and Conversion Rate.
* **What is your current sales volume?**
	+ Enter your current sales volume as a numerical value.
