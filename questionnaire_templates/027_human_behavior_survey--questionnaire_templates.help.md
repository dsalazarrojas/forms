# Human Behavior Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about your daily routines, stress levels, and other personal details to better understand human behavior.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please select the answer that best represents your situation for each question.
- If a question is not applicable, please select "None of the above" or "Not Applicable."
- For questions with text fields, provide a brief but clear explanation.
- You can save your progress and come back later if you need to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: Please read and acknowledge that your responses will be anonymous and used for research purposes. 
* **Routines Frequency**: This question inquires about how often you perform your daily routines.
	+ 1-2 times a week: You perform your routines 1-2 times a week.
	+ 3-4 times a week: You perform your routines 3-4 times a week.
	+ daily: You perform your routines every day.
	+ weekly: You perform your routines once a week.
	+ monthly: You perform your routines once a month.
	+ rarely: You rarely perform your routines.
* **Stress Level**: This question asks about your current stress level.
	+ High: You are currently feeling highly stressed.
	+ Low: You are currently feeling lowly stressed.
	+ Medium: You are currently feeling moderately stressed.
* **Work-Life Balance**: Please describe your current work-life balance.
* **Routines Description**: Provide a brief description of your daily routines.
* **Stressors**: Select all the stressors that apply to you.
	+ Work: Work-related stressors.
	+ Relationships: Relationship-related stressors.
	+ Personal issues: Personal problems that cause stress.
	+ Health: Health-related stressors.
	+ Finances: Financial stressors.
	+ Other: Other stressors not listed here.
* **Email Address**: Please provide your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth**: Please enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Time of Day**: Please select the time of day that best represents your current time zone.
* **Survey Conclusion**: Please provide any additional comments or conclusions you have after completing the survey.
* **Contact Information**: Provide any additional contact information you'd like to share.
* **Additional Information**: Please provide any additional information you'd like to share about yourself.
