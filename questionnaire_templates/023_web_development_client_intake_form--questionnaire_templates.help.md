# web_development_client_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The web_development_client_intake_form is a questionnaire template designed to collect information from clients about their web development projects. It helps our team to understand project specifics, which enables us to provide better services and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your project details, including the development stack, project status, and expected start and end dates.
2. Enter your contact information, such as your email address and phone number.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **1. Client** (text)
The "Client" field is a text field where you can enter any information about the client, such as their name or description.

### **2. Project Details** (text)
The "Project Details" field is a text field where you can describe your project in more detail, including any relevant project goals or objectives.

### **3. Development Stack** (select_multiple)
Select the development stack(s) you are using for your project from the options provided. This could be Python, Java, JavaScript, or Ruby.

### **4. Project Status** (select_one)
Choose the current status of your project from the options provided: active, inactive, or cancelled.

### **5. Expected Start Date** (date)
Enter the expected start date for your project.

### **6. Expected End Date** (date)
Enter the expected end date for your project.

### **7. Client** (text)
This is another "Client" field where you can enter additional information about the client.

### **8. Project Manager** (text)
Enter the name of the project manager for your project.

### **9. Client Email** (email)
Enter the email address of the client.

### **10. Client Phone** (text)
Enter the phone number of the client.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If you have any additional information about your project, please include it in the "Project Details" field.
* If you are unsure about the development stack or project status, feel free to ask our team for guidance.
