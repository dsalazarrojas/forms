# Buyers Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help real estate agents or lenders understand your needs and provide a tailored experience for your home buying process. It will gather information about your personal details, financial situation, and property requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your full name and phone number** (Page 1): Enter your full name and phone number to allow us to contact you for further information or clarification.
2. **Provide your email address** (Page 1): Enter your email address so we can communicate with you about your application.
3. **Select your marital status** (Page 2): Choose your current marital status to help us understand your family situation.
4. **Enter the number of people in your household** (Page 2): Enter the total number of people living in your current household.
5. **Confirm if you have children** (Page 2): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have children.
6. **Choose your planned purchase timeline** (Page 2): Select when you plan to buy a property within the given options.
7. **Enter your budget range** (Page 2): Choose your price range for the property you're looking for.
8. **Confirm your mortgage pre-approval status** (Page 3): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you've been pre-approved for financing.
9. **Describe your current property situation** (Page 3): Select the status of your current property.
10. **Confirm if you plan to sell your current property** (Page 3): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you plan to sell your current property.
11. **Select the type of property you're looking for** (Page 4): Choose the type of property you're interested in, such as a single-family home, condo, or townhouse.
12. **Enter your desired location** (Page 4): Enter the neighborhood or area you'd like to live in.
13. **Enter the number of bedrooms and bathrooms you need** (Page 4): Enter the number of bedrooms and bathrooms required for your new property.
14. **Enter the approximate square footage you're looking for** (Page 5): Enter the approximate square footage of the property you're interested in.
15. **List any must-have features** (Page 5): Select any essential features you need in your new property, such as a garage or updated kitchen.
16. **Confirm if you've contacted a real estate agent** (Page 5): Select 'Yes', 'No', or 'In Process' to indicate your current relationship with a real estate agent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Buyer Name** (`buyer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`buyer_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for further communication.
* **Email Address** (`buyer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Marital Status** (`married_status`, select_one, optional): Select your current marital status to help us understand your family situation.
* **Household Size** (`household_size`, number, required): Enter the total number of people living in your household.
* **Do You Have Children?** (`children`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have children.
* **Purchase Timeline** (`buy_timeline`, select_one, required): Choose when you plan to buy a property within the given options.
* **Budget for Purchase** (`budget`, select_one, required): Select your price range for the property you're looking for.
* **Mortgage Pre-approved** (`preapproved`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you've been pre-approved for financing.
* **Current Property Status** (`current_property`, select_one, required): Select the status of your current property.
* **Selling Current Property** (`selling_current`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you plan to sell your current property.
* **Type of Home Wanted** (`home_type_wanted`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of property you're interested in, such as a single-family home or condo.
* **Desired Location** (`desired_location`, text, required): Enter the neighborhood or area you'd like to live in.
* **Bedrooms Wanted** (`bedrooms_wanted`, number, required): Enter the number of bedrooms required for your new property.
* **Bathrooms Wanted** (`bathrooms_wanted`, number, required): Enter the number of bathrooms required for your new property.
* **Square Footage Preference** (`square_feet`, text, optional): Enter the approximate square footage of the property you're interested in.
* **Must Have Features** (`must_haves`, select_multiple, optional): Select any essential features you need in your new property, such as a garage or updated kitchen.
* **Have You Contacted Real Estate Agents?** (`agents_contacted`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes', 'No', or 'In Process' to indicate your current relationship with a real estate agent.
