# Profile Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about your profile, which will help us better understand your needs and provide personalized support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each field carefully, providing accurate and truthful information.
2. Each field has a specific purpose, so make sure to read the explanations for each field carefully.
3. If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
4. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. What is your name? (profile_information)
* Type: text
* Required: true
* Explanation: This field is for your name, please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.

### 2. What is your email address? (email)
* Type: email
* Required: true
* Explanation: This field is for your email address that you use for communication.

### 3. What is your phone number? (phone_number)
* Type: text
* Required: true
* Explanation: This field is for your phone number that we can use to contact you.

### 4. How would you like to be contacted? (marketing_information)
* Type: select_multiple
* Required: true
* Explanation: Please select all the ways you would like to be contacted, such as Social Media, Phone, or Email.

### 5. What is your project details? (project_details)
* Type: text
* Required: true
* Explanation: This field is for describing your project in detail, including its objectives and goals.

### 6. What is your business information? (business_information)
* Type: text
* Required: true
* Explanation: This field is for any relevant business information, including your business name or website.

### 7. What is your date of birth? (date)
* Type: date
* Required: true
* Explanation: Please enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YY.

### 8. What is your time of birth? (time)
* Type: time
* Required: true
* Explanation: Please enter your time of birth in 24-hour format (HH:MM).

### 9. Any additional information? (note)
* Type: note
* Required: true
* Explanation: This field is for any additional information that you want to share with us.

### 10. What are your interests? (profiling_questionnaire)
* Type: select_one
* Required: true
* Explanation: Please select one or more of your interests, such as Music, Sports, or Hobbies.

### 11. What is your age? (age)
* Type: number
* Required: true
* Explanation: Please enter your age as a number.

### 12. Where are you from? (origin)
* Type: text
* Required: true
* Explanation: This field is for your place of origin or birthplace.

### 13. What is your education level? (education_level)
* Type: select_one
* Required: true
* Explanation: Please select your education level, such as High School, College, or University.

### 14. What is your occupation? (occupation)
* Type: text
* Required: true
* Explanation: This field is for your current occupation or profession.

### 15. Why are you interested in this program? (interest)
* Type: text
* Required: true
* Explanation: Please explain why you're interested in this program in a few sentences.

### 16. What are the details about your project? (project_details2)
* Type: text
* Required: true
* Explanation: Please provide a detailed description of your project, including its objectives and goals.

### 17. Profiling Questionnaire (profiling_questionnaire)

### 18. Additional Comments (note_2)
