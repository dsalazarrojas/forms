# Contact Lens Color Selection Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form helps us understand your preferences for contact lens colors, allowing us to improve our shade offerings and provide better customer experiences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please read through the form carefully and answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. If you are unsure about any question, try to answer based on your typical behavior or preferences.
3. For select_multiple questions, select all that apply.
4. For select_one questions, choose the option that best describes your preference.
5. If you have any comments or suggestions, please fill in the "Any shades you would like to see us add?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Color Contact Lens Preference Survey** (`survey_intro`, note, required: false): This is an introductory note explaining the purpose of the form.
* **Do you currently wear contact lenses?** (`current_wearer`, select_one, required: true): Please select "Yes - Prescription" if you have a valid prescription, "Yes - Non-prescription" if you don't have a valid prescription, or "False" if you don't wear contact lenses.
* **Your Natural Eye Color** (`natural_eye_color`, select_one, required: true): Choose the color closest to your natural eye color.
* **Desired Color Effect** (`desired_color_effect`, select_one, required: true): Choose the color effect you prefer most.
* **Shades you are most interested in** (`preferred_shades`, select_multiple, required: true): Select all the shades you are interested in wearing.
* **How often do you wear color lenses?** (`wear_frequency`, select_one, required: true): Choose how often you wear color lenses.
* **Most important factor when choosing color lenses** (`priority_factor`, select_one, required: true): Choose the factor that is most important when selecting color lenses.
* **Where do you typically purchase lenses?** (`purchase_location`, select_one, required: false): If you have purchased lenses before, please select the location where you usually buy them.
* **Typical comfort rating of color lenses** (`comfort_rating`, number, required: false): Rate your comfort level with color lenses, with 10 being the most comfortable.
* **Any shades you would like to see us add?** (`new_shades_suggestions`, text, required: false): If you have any suggestions or ideas for new shades, please enter them here.
* **Email Address** (`email_for_results`, email, required: false): If you want to receive our color guide, please enter your email address.
