# Jury Qualification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The jury qualification form is used to collect demographic and contact information from potential jurors. It helps to ensure that the information is accurate and up-to-date for the purposes of jury service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you are not duplicating any existing records before proceeding.
* Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
* Enter your first name in the field labeled "first-name".
* Enter your last name in the field labeled "last-name".
* Provide your address in the field labeled "address".
* Enter your email address in the field labeled "email".
* Enter your phone number in the field labeled "phone".
* Enter your availability date in the format of mm/dd/yyyy in the field labeled "date".
* Enter your availability time in the format of hh:mm in the field labeled "time".
* Provide any additional comments or notes in the field labeled "notes".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First-name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last-name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Provide your residential or mailing address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter your availability date in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter your availability time in the format of hh:mm.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Provide any additional comments or notes about your availability or qualifications.
