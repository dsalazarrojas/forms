# Reaction Assignment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data about participant reactions and experiences after an experiment, exercise, or other activities. The purpose of this form is to gather information for quality assurance, research, or educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the text field with the title of the experiment or exercise.
2. Enter your name in the text field to identify yourself as a participant.
3. Select one or more reactions from the multiple-select field to describe your response.
4. Enter any materials and safety rules that were relevant to the experiment or exercise in the text field.
5. Enter the due date for the form submission, if applicable.
6. Confirm your participation in the single-select field.
7. Enter the name of the trainer or instructor who supervised the experiment or exercise.
8. Enter any confirmation from the trainer or instructor in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **what_was_the_experiment** (`title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the experiment, exercise, or activity you participated in.
* **participant_name** (`participant_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name to identify yourself as a participant.
* **reaction_tasks** (`reaction_tasks`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more reactions that describe your response to the experiment or exercise.
* **materials_and_safety_rules** (`materials_and_safety_rules`, text, required/optional): Enter any materials or safety rules that were relevant to the experiment or exercise.
* **due_date** (`due_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the due date for the form submission, if applicable.
* **participant_confirmations** (`participant_confirmations`, select_one, required/optional): Confirm your participation by selecting either Confirmed or Not Confirmed.
* **trainers_name** (`trainers_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the trainer or instructor who supervised the experiment or exercise.
* **trainer_confirmation** (`trainer_confirmation`, text, required/optional): Enter any confirmation from the trainer or instructor in the text field.
