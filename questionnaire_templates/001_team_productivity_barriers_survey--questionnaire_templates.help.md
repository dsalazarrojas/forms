# Team Productivity Barriers Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Team Productivity Barriers Survey is a tool used to identify and measure the productivity barriers that teams face. It's a questionnaire that helps us understand the challenges that teams encounter and how we can work together to overcome them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, simply follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the survey.
2. Select your response from each option for each question.
3. Make sure to provide accurate information.
4. Review your answers before submission.
5. Click "Submit" to complete the survey.
