# Evolve Challenge Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Evolve Challenge Interest Survey is a questionnaire designed to help us better understand your interest and preferences regarding the Evolve Challenge project. Your input will help us tailor the project to your needs and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Choose the correct options for each question.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy.
5. Click submit to submit your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, text, optional): This field is where you should enter your participant information, such as your name or ID.
- **Challenge Info** (`challenge_info`, text, optional): In this field, please describe your interest in the Evolve Challenge project.
- **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the options that apply to your availability for the project.
	* 'Yes' indicates that you are available for the project.
	* 'No' indicates that you are not available.
- **Preferences** (`preferences`, text, optional): Here, you can list any specific preferences you have regarding the project.
- **Challenge Activities** (`challenge_activities`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the activities that interest you for the project.
	* 'Yes' indicates that you are interested in the activity.
	* 'No' indicates that you are not interested.
- **Communications** (`communications`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the methods of communication that you would like to use for the project.
	* 'Yes' indicates that you prefer the method.
	* 'No' indicates that you do not prefer the method.
- **Participant Availability** (`participant_availability`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the options that apply to your availability as a participant.
	* 'Yes' indicates that you are available as a participant.
	* 'No' indicates that you are not available.
- **Participant Interest** (`participant_interest`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the interests that apply to you as a participant.
	* 'Yes' indicates that you are interested in the interest.
	* 'No' indicates that you are not interested.
- **Participant Preferences** (`participant_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Here, you can select all the preferences that apply to you as a participant.
	* 'Yes' indicates that you prefer the preference.
	* 'No' indicates that you do not prefer the preference.
- **Additional Questions** (`additional_questions`, text, optional): If you have any other information or questions regarding the project, please include them here.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): You can add any comments or feedback you have about the survey or the project.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you completed the survey.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you completed the survey.
- **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, text, optional): Enter your participant ID.
- **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your participant name.
- **Survey Completed By** (`survey_completed_by`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have completed the survey, 'No' otherwise.
- **Survey Completed On** (`survey_completed_on`, date, optional): Enter the date and time you completed the survey.
- **Survey Status** (`survey_status`, select_one, optional): Select 'Active' if your survey is active, 'Inactive' otherwise.
- **Participant Status** (`participant_status`, select_one, optional): Select 'Active' if you are an active participant, 'Inactive' otherwise.
- **Participant Location** (`participant_location`, text, optional): Enter your location.
- **Challenge Type** (`challenge_type`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are interested in the challenge type.
- **Participant Level** (`participant_level`, select_one, optional): Select 'High' if you have a high level of interest, 'Low' otherwise.
- **Participant Category** (`participant_category`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the categories that apply to you as a participant.
- **Participant Interest Level** (`participant_interest_level`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the interest levels that apply to you as a participant.
- **Participant Availability** (`participant_availability`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the options that apply to your availability.
- **Participant Preferences** (`participant_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the preferences that apply to you as a participant.
- **Survey Version** (`survey_version`, text, optional): Enter the version of the survey you completed.
- **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback you have about the survey or project.
- **Survey ID** (`survey_id`, text, optional): Enter the survey ID.
- **Participant Survey Status** (`participant_survey_status`, select_one, optional): Select 'Active' if your participant survey is active, 'Inactive' otherwise.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
- Make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the project administrator.
