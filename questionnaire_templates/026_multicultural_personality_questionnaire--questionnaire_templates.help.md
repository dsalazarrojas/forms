# Multicultural Personality Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Multicultural Personality Questionnaire (MPQ) is a tool designed to assess an individual's personality traits in a multicultural context. This form aims to evaluate your cultural adaptability, interpersonal relationships, and individualism, among other attributes. It is a required component in our organization's HR process for certain roles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your name in the "Name" field. This is a required field.
* Enter your age in the "Age" field. If you're unsure, you can estimate or leave this field blank.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is a required field.
* Choose the options that best describe your multicultural experience from the "Multicultural experience" field. You can select multiple options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Name (1)
**Name (text, text, required)**
Enter your full name, as it appears on official documents.

### Age (2)
**Age (number, number, optional)**
Enter your age or leave this field blank if you're unsure.

### Email (3)
**Email (email, email, required)**
Enter your email address as it appears in your official records.

### Multicultural experience (4)
**Multicultural experience (select_multiple, text, required)**
Choose the options that best describe your multicultural experience, such as interacting with people from different cultures or working in a multicultural environment.

## Tips
* Please be honest when answering the questions.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, ask your HR representative for guidance.
* This form will take approximately 5-7 minutes to complete.
