# Botanical Energy Map Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Botanical Energy Map Survey is a tool used to collect feedback from individuals about public spaces. This feedback can help urban planners and designers create more vibrant and safe environments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Respondent name" field.
2. Provide your contact email in the "Contact email" field, which will be used for follow-up communication if needed.
3. In the "Location of space" field, enter the name and address of the public space you are completing the survey for (e.g., a park or green space).
4. Choose your answer to "Perceived energy level" based on how vibrant you feel the space is.
5. In the "Activities observed" field, describe any common activities you have seen taking place in the space.
6. Select how safe you feel in the space using the "Safety perception" options.
7. If desired, add any amenities you believe would improve your experience in the "Amenities needed" field.
8. Finally, add any additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Respondent name (Respondent name)
* A text field to enter your full name, which is optional but can be used to follow up with you if needed.

### Contact email (Contact email)
* An email field to enter your contact information, which will be used for any follow-up communication.

### Location of space (Location of space)
* A text field to enter the name and address of the public space you are completing the survey for.

### Perceived energy level (Perceived energy level)
* A select-one field with three options: High, Moderate, or Low. Choose the one that best describes how vibrant you feel the space is.

### Activities observed (Activities observed)
* A text field to describe any common activities you have seen taking place in the space.

### Safety perception (Safety perception)
* A select-one field with three options: Very safe, Somewhat safe, or Unsafe. Choose the one that best describes how safe you feel in the space.

### Amenities needed (Amenities needed)
* A text field to add any amenities you believe would improve your experience in the space.

### Additional notes (Additional notes)
* A text field to enter any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Provide specific examples or descriptions when answering open-ended fields.
* If you have any additional thoughts or concerns, feel free to add them in the "Additional notes" field.
