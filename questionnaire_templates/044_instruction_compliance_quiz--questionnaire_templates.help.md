# Instruction Compliance Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about participants' instruction compliance, likely for a course or training program. The form gathers data on participants' roles, instructor names, course details, and other relevant information. This data is likely used to evaluate compliance with instructions or standards.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out each section accurately and completely.
2. Each section should be filled out for each participant only once.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required)
	+ Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required)
	+ Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Role** (3, select multiple, required)
	+ Indicate your role in the course or training program.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **Instructor Name** (4, text, required)
	+ Enter the name of the instructor who provided the instruction.
* **Instructor Title** (5, text, required)
	+ Enter the title of the instructor who provided the instruction.
* **Course Title** (6, text, required)
	+ Enter the title of the course or training program.
* **Course Code** (7, text, required)
	+ Enter the code or identifier of the course or training program.
* **Date** (8, date, required)
	+ Enter the date on which you received the instruction.
* **Time** (9, time, required)
	+ Enter the time of day on which you received the instruction.
* **Score** (10, number, required)
	+ Enter your score or assessment of the instruction's effectiveness.
* **Notes** (11, note, optional)
	+ Enter any additional comments or observations about the instruction.

## Tips

* Please fill out each section accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any section, please ask for clarification or guidance.
* This form is designed to be filled out for each participant only once.
