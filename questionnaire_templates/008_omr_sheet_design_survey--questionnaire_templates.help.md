# omr_sheet_design_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The omr_sheet_design_survey form is a questionnaire designed to gather information from educators and assessment teams on their design preferences and priorities for OMR sheets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the questions and their options.
2. Answer each question based on your actual preferences and experiences.
3. Provide honest and accurate information to inform design decisions.
4. Submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Form Title
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for the form, which will be displayed on the OMR sheet.

### Page 2: Category
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to indicate whether the form is used for education or assessment purposes.

### Page 3: Description
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the form, if necessary.

### Page 4: Educator Preferences
* **What are the top 3 design preferences of educators in your organization?** (`educator_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Select the top 3 design preferences of educators in your organization from the provided options.

### Page 5: Assessment Team Preferences
* **What are the top 3 design preferences of assessment teams in your organization?** (`assessment_team_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Select the top 3 design preferences of assessment teams in your organization from the provided options.

### Page 6: Priorities for Improvement
* **What are the top 3 priorities for improving the design?** (`priorities_for_improvement`, select_multiple, required): Select the top 3 priorities for improving the design from the provided options.

### Page 7: Submission Method
* **How will submissions be made in your organization?** (`submission_method`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to indicate how submissions will be made in your organization.

### Page 8: Preferred Design Feature
* **What design feature do you prefer?** (`preferred_design_feature`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to indicate your preferred design feature.

### Page 9: Submission Process
* **What is the submission process like?** (`submission_process`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the submission process, if necessary.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your responses to ensure that the designed OMR sheet meets the needs of your organization.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please refer to your colleagues or superiors for guidance.
* You can ask for help if you need assistance completing this form.
