# Improv Character And Costume Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps to collect information about your character and performance needs from participants. Please fill out the form to provide the details we need to ensure you have a great experience at our event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the character details section with your chosen character and costume information.
2. Select the options that apply to your needs for rehearsals and shows.
3. Choose your experience level as an improviser or performer.
4. Share your goals for the event or performance.
5. Provide any additional notes or comments you'd like to add.
6. Enter your contact information for us to get in touch with you.
7. Finally, select the date for your performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **character_details** (`Character and Costume`, required): Describe your chosen character and costume, including any relevant background, personality, or traits.
* **costume_needs** (`Rehearsal Needs`, required, multiple select): Select all the rehearsal and performance needs you require, such as props, sets, or special effects.
* **experience_level** (`Experience Level`, required): Choose your level of experience as an improviser or performer, which will help us tailor the event to your needs.
* **goals** (`Goals`, required): Share your goals for the event or performance, such as learning a new skill or achieving a specific outcome.
* **rehearsal_needs** (`Rehearsal Needs`, required, multiple select): Select all the rehearsal and performance needs you require, such as specific space or equipment needs.
* **show_needs** (`Show Needs`, required, multiple select): Select all the performance needs you require, such as specific stage or equipment needs.
* **notes** (`Notes`, required): Add any additional notes or comments you'd like to share with us.
* **contact_info** (`Contact Information`, required): Enter your email or other contact details so we can get in touch with you.
* **performance_info** (`Performance Date`, required): Select the date for your performance or show.
