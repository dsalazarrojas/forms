# home_dietetics_support_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on your health, medical history, and lifestyle to provide personalized recommendations for support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Dietetics Support Assessment Questions** (`home_dietetics_support_assessment_questions`, `note`, optional): This page is the introduction to the form. Please read through the questions and answer them as instructed.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `date`, optional): Please enter your medical history in the format MM/DD/YY. If you have multiple medical history events, you can enter multiple dates.
* **Height and Weight** (`height_and_weight`, `select_one`, optional): Please select your height and weight from the options provided.
* **Medication List** (`medication_list`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please list any medications you are currently taking. Select all that apply.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select any allergies you have. Select all that apply.
* **Health Problems** (`health_problems`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select any health problems you have. Select all that apply.
* **Health Insurance** (`health_insurance`, `select_one`, optional): Please select your health insurance status.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, optional): Please select your employment status.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select your education level.
* **Family Status** (`family_status`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select your family status.
* **Income** (`income`, `number`, optional): Please enter your income.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, optional): Please enter your age.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Please enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, `date`, optional): Please enter your medical history in the format MM/DD/YY. If you have multiple medical history events, you can enter multiple dates.
* **Height** (`height`, `number`, optional): Please enter your height in inches.
* **Weight** (`weight`, `number`, optional): Please enter your weight.
* **Blood Pressure** (`blood_pressure`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select your blood pressure level.

## Tips
Please answer the questions honestly and accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for support.
