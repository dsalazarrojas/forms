# Hotel Check In Eligibility Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Check In Eligibility Questionnaire is a mandatory form designed to gather essential guest information for a smooth check-in experience. Please complete this form accurately to ensure timely and efficient processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully fill out all required fields, marked as *required*.
2. Enter your name, email address, and phone number in the respective fields.
3. Select the correct arrival and travel dates.
4. Choose the correct room type (yes or no).
5. Enter your flight number and floor preference (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name** (required): Enter your full name, including first and last name.
* **Guest Email** (required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Check-in Date** (required): Select your arrival date using the calendar.
* **Arrival Time** (required): Choose your scheduled arrival time.
* **Phone** (required): Enter your phone number.
* **Emergency Contact** (required): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Travel Date** (required): Select your travel date.
* **Flight Number** (required): Enter your flight number.
* **Room Type** (required): Select "Yes" if you require a specific room type.
* **Room Floor** (required): Enter your preferred room floor number.

## Tips
- Please ensure accuracy while filling out the form to avoid delays or mistakes during check-in.
- Double-check your email address and phone number for correct formatting.
- If you are unsure about any field, please consult the hotel staff for assistance.
