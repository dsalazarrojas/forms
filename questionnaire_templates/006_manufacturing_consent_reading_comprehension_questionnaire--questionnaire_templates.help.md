# manufacturing_consent_reading_comprehension_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from participants about their experience with a manufacturing consent reading session.

## How To Complete This Form

- Please answer all the questions to the best of your ability. If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
- You can skip any questions that are not relevant to you or your experience.
- You can provide additional comments or feedback at the end of the form.
- Make sure to submit your answers to confirm that you've completed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your official ID or any other name by which you are known.
  * This will help us identify you and ensure your submission is correctly credited.
  * You can type in your full name, initials, or any other name by which you are known.

- **Reading Group** (`reading_group`, text, optional): Please enter the name or label of the reading group you were part of during the session.
  * This helps us understand the dynamic of your group and how it impacted your experience.
  * You can type in the exact group name or describe it in a few words.

- **Training Session** (`training_session`, text, optional): Please describe the length of the training session.
  * This helps us understand the scope and depth of the session.
  * You can type the exact length in minutes, hours, or a description of the session's content.

- **Participant Understanding** (`participant_understanding`, text, optional): Please rate your level of understanding of the session's content.
  * This helps us assess the session's effectiveness.
  * You can rate on a scale from 0 to 5, with 0 being "not at all" and 5 being "completely".

- **Reading Group Size** (`reading_group_size`, text, optional): Please describe the size of your reading group.
  * This helps us understand how many people were involved in the session.
  * You can type the exact number or describe it in a few words.

- **Class Length** (`class_length`, text, optional): Please describe the length of the class.
  * This helps us understand the scope and depth of the class.
  * You can type the exact length in minutes, hours, or a description of the class's content.

- **Session Frequency** (`session_frequency`, text, optional): Please describe how often you attended this session.
  * This helps us understand your engagement with the session.
  * You can type the exact frequency (e.g., once, twice, three times) or describe it in a few words.

- **Participant Engagement** (`participant_engagement`, text, optional): Please rate your level of engagement with the session.
  * This helps us assess your interest and attention during the session.
  * You can rate on a scale from 0 to 5, with 0 being "not at all" and 5 being "completely".

- **Class Attendance** (`class_attendance`, text, optional): Please describe your attendance in the class.
  * This helps us understand how many sessions you attended.
  * You can type the exact number or describe it in a few words.

- **Participant Satisfaction** (`participant_satisfaction`, text, optional): Please rate your satisfaction with the session.
  * This helps us assess the session's effectiveness.
  * You can rate on a scale from 0 to 5, with 0 being "not satisfied" and 5 being "completely satisfied".

- **Session Attendance** (`session_attendance`, text, optional): Please describe your session attendance.
  * This helps us understand how many sessions you attended.
  * You can type the exact number or describe it in a few words.

- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Please provide any additional comments or feedback about the session.
  * This helps us understand any additional insights or thoughts you have about the session.
  * You can type anything that comes to mind, and we'll take it into consideration.

- **Submission Confirmation** (`submission_confirmation`, select_one, optional): Have you submitted the form?
  * This helps us confirm that you've completed the form.
  * Please select "yes" if you've completed the form correctly, and "no" if you need assistance.
