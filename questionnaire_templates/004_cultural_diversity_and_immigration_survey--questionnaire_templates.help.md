# Cultural Diversity And Immigration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic information about the user's cultural background, immigration status, and personal characteristics. This data will help us understand the diversity of our community and better serve your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately based on your personal experiences.
2. Select only the options that best describe your situation.
3. Do not leave any fields blank unless explicitly instructed to do so.
4. If you have any special circumstances that are not listed, please use the 'Other' option and provide a brief explanation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, optional): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please take a moment to read it before proceeding.
* **Demographic Information** (`demographic_information`, `number`, optional): Please enter your age, which will help us understand your demographic characteristics.
* **Cultural Background** (`cultural_background`, `text`, optional): Describe your cultural background, including your ethnicity, country of origin, or other relevant cultural information.
* **Immigration Status** (`immigration_status`, `select_one`, optional): Please select 'True' if you are a immigrant or 'False' if you are not.
* **Language Spoken** (`language_speaking`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select all languages you speak, including English, Other languages, or Both.
* **Language Spoken At Home** (`language_spoken_at_home`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select all languages you speak at home, including English, Other languages, or Both.
* **Years In US** (`years_in_us`, `number`, optional): Please enter the number of years you have been living in the US.
* **Highest Education** (`highest_education`, `select_one`, optional): Please select the highest level of education you have completed, including Elementary School, High School, College, University, or Other.
* **Highest Education Other** (`highest_education_other`, `text`, optional): If your highest education is not listed, please provide a brief explanation.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, optional): Please select your employment status, including Employed, Unemployed, Retired, Student, or Other.
* **Income Level** (`income_level`, `number`, optional): Please enter your annual income.
* **Income Level Range** (`income_level_range`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select all income ranges that apply to you, including $0-20,000, $20,001-50,000, $50,001-100,000, or $100,001 or more.
* **Income Other** (`income_level_other`, `text`, optional): If your income level is not listed, please provide a brief explanation.
* **Marital Status** (`marital_status`, `select_one`, optional): Please select your marital status, including Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, or Other.
* **Family Size** (`family_size`, `number`, optional): Please enter the number of family members you have.
* **Family Size Range** (`family_size_range`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select all family size ranges that apply to you, including 1-2, 3-5, 6-10, 11 or more.
* **Family Size Other** (`family_size_other`, `text`, optional): If your family size is not listed, please provide a brief explanation.
* **Race** (`race`, `select_one`, optional): Please select your race, including Asian, Black or African American, Hispanic or Latino, White, or Other.
* **Ethnic Background** (`ethnic_background`, `select_one`, optional): Please select your ethnic background, including American Indian or Alaska Native, Asian, Black or African American, Native Hawaiian or Other Pacific Islander, or Some other race or ethnicity.
* **Sex** (`sex`, `select_one`, optional): Please select your sex, including Female, Male, or Other.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, optional): Please enter your age.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, `select_one`, optional): Please select your age group, including 0-17, 18-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55-64, or 65 or older.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, `select_one`, optional): Please select your education level, including Elementary School, High School, Some college, College, or Some other.
* **Income Range** (`income_range`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select all income ranges that apply to you, including $0-20,000, $20,001-50,000, $50,001-100,000, or $100,001 or more.
* **Other Income** (`other_income`, `text`, optional): If your income level is not listed, please provide a brief explanation.
