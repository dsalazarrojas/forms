# Workshop Participant Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Workshop Participant Satisfaction Questionnaire is a form designed to collect feedback from participants about their experience in a workshop. The form helps workshop organizers evaluate the effectiveness and quality of the workshop and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully to understand what it's asking.
2. Answer all required questions, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Choose the relevant answers for each question based on your experience in the workshop.
4. Fill in any relevant details in the text fields.
5. Submit the form to provide your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, required, select_one): This question asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with the workshop, on a scale of Satisfied or Dissatisfied.
* **Workshop Content** (`workshop_content`, required, number): This question asks you to rate how engaging and useful you found the workshop content.
* **Workshop Facilitator** (`workshop_facilitator`, required, select_one): This question asks you to rate how well the workshop facilitator performed.
* **Time Management** (`time_management`, optional, select_multiple): This question asks you to select all that apply regarding the time management of the workshop.
* **Materials** (`materials`, required, number): This question asks you to rate how well the workshop materials were provided.
* **Food and Beverages** (`food_and_beverages`, optional, select_multiple): This question asks you to select all that apply regarding the food and beverages provided.
* **Environment** (`environment`, optional, select_one): This question asks you to rate how well the workshop environment was conducive to learning.
* **Workshop Length** (`workshop_length`, required, time): This question asks you to rate how well the workshop length was suitable for the content.
* **Comments** (`comments`, optional, text): This question asks you to provide any additional comments about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, required, email): This question asks you to provide your email address so we can follow up with any further questions or concerns.
* **Phone** (`phone`, optional, text): This question asks you to provide a phone number, but it's not required.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, optional, date): This question is automatically generated and not relevant to your feedback.
 

## Tips
* Please answer all required questions.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the comments field.
* If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask the organizers.
* Your feedback is invaluable for improving future workshops, so please take a few minutes to complete this form.
