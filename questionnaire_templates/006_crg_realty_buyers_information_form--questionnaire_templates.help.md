# CRG Realty Buyers Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CRG Realty Buyers Information Form is designed to gather essential information from potential real estate buyers, enabling the agent to better understand their needs and provide personalized service. This form will help the agent tailor their approach to meet your specific requirements, making the buying process more efficient and effective.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name in the "Buyer name" field.
2.  Provide your preferred contact email in the "Contact email" field.
3.  Optionally, enter your contact phone number in the "Contact phone number" field.
4.  Select your primary property type of interest from the "Property type of interest" dropdown menu.
5.  Choose your estimated purchase budget from the "Budget range" dropdown menu.
6.  Enter your preferred locations or neighborhoods in the "Preferred locations" field.
7.  Select your planned timeline for purchasing from the "Timeline to purchase" dropdown menu.
8.  Optionally, provide any additional comments or details in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Buyer name**: Your full name as the potential buyer. Please enter your name in this field.
* **Contact email**: Your preferred contact email address. This will be used for communication and updates.
* **Contact phone number**: Your mobile or landline phone number. This is optional but helps the agent get in touch with you more efficiently.
* **Property type of interest**: Select your primary property type, whether it's Residential, Commercial, or Land. This helps the agent understand your needs more precisely.
* **Budget range**: Choose your estimated purchase budget from the provided options: Under 100000, 100000 to 300000, 300000 to 600000, or Over 600000. This information aids the agent in showing you relevant properties.
* **Preferred locations**: Enter the neighborhoods or cities you are interested in. This helps the agent know which areas to focus on during your search.
* **Timeline to purchase**: Choose when you plan to buy, whether it's Immediately, 1 to 3 months, 3 to 6 months, or 6+ months. This helps the agent understand your urgency and adjust their approach accordingly.
* **Additional comments**: Use this field to provide any further details or information that might help the agent better understand your needs and preferences.
