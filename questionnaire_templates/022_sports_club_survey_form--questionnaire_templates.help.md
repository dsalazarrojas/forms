# Sports Club Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Club Survey Form is a tool used to collect valuable information from its members. It is designed to improve the sports club's services and offerings by gathering insights into their preferences and habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred food option from the list of choices provided.
2. Choose how often you visit the sports club (if you answer this question, please select the corresponding number).
3. Select your preferred beverage from the list of choices provided.
4. Provide any ideas you have for improving the sports club's services.
5. Indicate your preferred time of day to visit the sports club.
6. Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and promotions.
7. Enter your phone number so we can contact you for any important announcements or reminders.
8. Share any additional comments or thoughts you have about the sports club.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Preferred Food Selection** (`preferred_food_selection`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the type of food that you prefer to eat at the sports club. Options include Grilled Chicken, Burgers, Salads, Fruits, and Veggies.
* **Visit Frequency** (`visit_frequency`, `number`, required: false): Please select the number of times you visit the sports club in a week. This can be a helpful metric for us to gauge the frequency of your visits.
* **Preferred Beverage** (`preferred_beverage`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select your preferred drink from the options provided. Options include Cola, Tea, Coffee, and Juice.
* **Ideas For Improvement** (`ideas_for_improvement`, `text`, required: false): If you have any ideas on how we can improve the sports club's services, please share them with us in this text field.
* **Preferred Time Of Day** (`preferred_time_of_day`, `time`, required: false): Please select your preferred time of day to visit the sports club. This can help us tailor our services to best suit your needs.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Please enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and promotions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your phone number so we can contact you for any important announcements or reminders.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, required: false): Please share any additional comments or thoughts you have about the sports club in this text field.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when completing this form.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out.
* We appreciate your input and look forward to using it to improve our services.
