# Peer To Peer Relationship Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Peer To Peer Relationship Survey is designed to collect information about relationships between two individuals. This form is used to gather data on characteristics such as demographics, relationship dynamics, and financial status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the instructions carefully and make sure you understand each question.
2. For questions with multiple options, select the option that best describes your relationship.
3. Answer accurately and truthfully, based on your knowledge of the relationship.
4. Do not skip any questions, as all information is required to complete the survey.
5. If you are unsure about any question, please contact the administrators for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, note, optional): This is a brief description of the form and its purpose.
* **Peer 1 First Name** (`peer1_first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of Peer 1.
* **Peer 2 First Name** (`peer2_first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of Peer 2.
* **Relationship Type** (`relationship_type`, select_multiple, required): Select all types of relationships that apply (Yes/No).
* **Peer 1 Age** (`peer1_age`, number, required): Enter Peer 1's age.
* **Peer 2 Age** (`peer2_age`, number, required): Enter Peer 2's age.
* **Time in Relationship** (`time_in_relationship`, select_one, required): Select the length of time in the relationship (Yes/No).
* **Communication Frequency** (`communication_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Select all types of communication that occur between the peers (Yes/No).
* **Peer 1 Interests** (`peer1_interests`, select_multiple, required): Select all interests or activities that Peer 1 enjoys (Yes/No).
* **Peer 2 Interests** (`peer2_interests`, select_multiple, required): Select all interests or activities that Peer 2 enjoys (Yes/No).
* **Relationship Satisfaction** (`relationship_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Select the level of satisfaction with the relationship (Satisfied/Dissatisfied).
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, select_multiple, required): Select all communication channels used between the peers (Yes/No).
* **Relationship Dynamics** (`relationship_dynamics`, select_multiple, required): Select all dynamics that describe the relationship (Yes/No).
* **Peer 1 Career** (`peer1_career`, select_one, required): Select the current career status of Peer 1 (Yes/No).
* **Peer 2 Career** (`peer2_career`, select_one, required): Select the current career status of Peer 2 (Yes/No).
* **Peer 1 Education** (`peer1_education`, select_one, required): Select the educational level of Peer 1 (Yes/No).
* **Peer 2 Education** (`peer2_education`, select_one, required): Select the educational level of Peer 2 (Yes/No).
* **Peer 1 Employment Status** (`peer1_employment_status`, select_one, required): Select the employment status of Peer 1 (Active/Inactive).
* **Peer 2 Employment Status** (`peer2_employment_status`, select_one, required): Select the employment status of Peer 2 (Active/Inactive).
* **Peer 1 Family Status** (`peer1_family_status`, select_one, required): Select the family status of Peer 1 (Active/Inactive).
* **Peer 2 Family Status** (`peer2_family_status`, select_one, required): Select the family status of Peer 2 (Active/Inactive).
* **Peer 1 Income** (`peer1_income`, number, required): Enter Peer 1's income.
* **Peer 2 Income** (`peer2_income`, number, required): Enter Peer 2's income.
* **Peer 1 Net Worth** (`peer1_net_worth`, number, required): Enter Peer 1's net worth.
* **Peer 2 Net Worth** (`peer2_net_worth`, number, required): Enter Peer 2's net worth.
