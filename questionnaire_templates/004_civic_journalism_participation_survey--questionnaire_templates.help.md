# Civic Journalism Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your background and experiences as a civic journalist or media professional. The goal is to better serve the needs of the civic journalism community and improve the overall quality of journalism in your community.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select all relevant options for each field, unless otherwise specified.
3. Be honest and accurate in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What best describes your role** (`respondent_type`):
  • Select the option that best describes your primary role as a civic journalist or media professional.
* **Years of Experience in Journalism or Media** (`years_experience`):
  • Enter the number of years you have worked in the field of journalism or media. If you are new to the field, enter 0.
* **What topics do you primarily cover** (`content_focus`):
  • Select all the topics that you primarily cover as a civic journalist or media professional.
* **Where do you publish your work** (`publishing_platforms`):
  • Select all the platforms where you publish your work as a civic journalist or media professional.
* **How would you describe your civic engagement** (`civic_engagement_level`):
  • Select the option that best describes your level of civic engagement in your community.
* **How much trust does your community have in local media** (`community_trust`):
  • Rate the level of trust your community has in local media.
* **What barriers do you face in civic journalism** (`barriers_faced`):
  • Select all the barriers you face as a civic journalist or media professional.
* **How transparent is your local government** (`government_transparency`):
  • Rate the level of transparency of your local government.
* **Do you have adequate access to government proceedings** (`press_access`):
  • Select the option that best describes your access to government meetings, records, and officials.
* **What training would benefit your civic journalism work** (`training_interest`):
  • Select all the trainings that you are interested in for your civic journalism work.
* **Would you collaborate with other civic journalists** (`collaboration_interest`):
  • Select the option that best describes your willingness to collaborate with other civic journalists.
* **Have your stories led to civic change** (`impact_stories`):
  • Select the option that best describes the impact of your stories on your community.
* **How is your journalism work funded** (`funding_sources`):
  • Select all the sources that fund your journalism work.
* **How optimistic are you about the future of civic journalism** (`future_outlook`):
  • Select the option that best describes your outlook on the future of civic journalism in your community.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`):
  • If you'd like to be kept up-to-date on survey results and networking opportunities, enter your email address here (optional).
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions** (`additional_feedback`):
  • Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have on improving civic journalism.
