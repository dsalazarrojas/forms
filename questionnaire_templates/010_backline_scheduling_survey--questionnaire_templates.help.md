# Backline Scheduling Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Backline Scheduling Survey is a questionnaire designed to collect information from performers and crew members to facilitate event scheduling.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Scroll down to each section and fill out the relevant fields as prompted.
2. Be sure to complete all required fields (marked as 'required') to avoid incomplete submissions.
3. Provide accurate and clear answers to ensure the information is used effectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performer/Crew Name** (`performer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the performer or crew member.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter the performer's or crew member's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, phone, required): Enter the performer's or crew member's phone number.
* **Role** (`role`, text, required): Select the role of the performer or crew member (e.g., artist, sound engineer, technician, etc.).
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, required): Enter the time the performer or crew member can arrive.
* **Available for Soundcheck** (`soundcheck_available`, select_one, required): Select whether the performer is available for soundcheck.
* If selected, enter the **Preferred Soundcheck Time** (`soundcheck_preferred_time`, time, optional).
* **Can Provide Setup Assistance** (`setup_assistance`, select_one, optional): Select whether the performer can provide setup assistance.
* **Can Provide Breakdown Assistance** (`breakdown_assistance`, select_one, optional): Select whether the performer can provide breakdown assistance.
* **Required Equipment** (`equipment_list`, text, optional): List the equipment required for the performance.
* **Equipment Provided vs. Needed** (`equipment_provided`, text, optional): Specify what equipment is provided by the venue and what the performer needs to bring.
* **Specialized Equipment Requirements** (`special_equipment`, text, optional): Enter any specialized equipment requirements.
* **Power Requirements** (`power_requirements`, select_one, optional): Select the power requirements (e.g., standard, 20 amp, 30 amp, etc.).
* **Technical Experience Level** (`technical_experience`, select_one, optional): Select the performer's or crew member's technical experience level (beginner, intermediate, advanced, expert).
* **Preferred Method for Technical Coordination** (`technical_contact_preference`, select_one, optional): Select the preferred method for technical coordination (email, phone, text, in-person).
* **Questions or Concerns** (`questions_concerns`, text, optional): Enter any questions or concerns.
* **Special Requests or Accommodations** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any special requests or accommodations.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, text, optional): Enter any accessibility needs (parking, wheelchair access, dietary, etc.).
* **I Confirm This Information is Accurate** (`confirmation_submitted`, select_one, required): Confirm that the provided information is accurate.

## Tips

* Please be thorough in filling out the form to ensure accurate scheduling.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the hints or ask your event coordinator for clarification.
* Double-check your submissions before confirming that the information is accurate.
