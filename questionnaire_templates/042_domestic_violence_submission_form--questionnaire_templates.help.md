# Domestic Violence Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The domestic violence submission form is a tool used to collect data on incidents of domestic violence. This form helps identify and document incidents of domestic violence, providing valuable insights for support and intervention. **Please note that this form should only be used for genuine incidents of domestic violence and not for other purposes. If you have previously submitted an incident, please do not resubmit. Duplicate submissions can cause confusion and may lead to unnecessary investigations.**

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the date of the incident you are reporting.
2.  Provide the location where the incident occurred.
3.  Describe the incident in as much detail as possible.
4.  Indicate if the perpetrator of the incident is male or female.
5.  Enter the name of the victim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date**: Enter the date when the incident of domestic violence occurred. This will help in tracking and investigating the incident.
* **Location**: Describe the place where the incident took place. This will be helpful for investigators to locate the incident site.
* **Description**: Provide a detailed account of what happened during the incident. Be as specific as you can without compromising the victim's identity or safety. 
*  **Perpetrator Gender**: Check 'Yes' if the perpetrator of the incident is male. Check 'No' if the perpetrator is female.
*  **Victim Name**: Enter the name of the victim. This will allow us to identify and support the victim effectively.
