# Geopolitical And Economic Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect general information about geopolitical and economic trends.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the survey in the "Survey Title" field (1).
2. Provide your information as a respondent, including any relevant details (2).
3. Select whether or not you have a country or region presence (3).
4. Indicate whether or not you have a presence in a specific sector (4).
5. Select your political sentiment (5).
6. Select your economic sentiment (6).
7. Provide any global trends information (7).
8. Enter any demographic information (8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Title** (1, text, required): Enter a brief title for the survey, describing its purpose and scope.
* **Respondent Information** (2, text, required): Provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Country/Region** (3, select_one, optional): Indicate whether or not you have a presence in a country or region.
* **Sector** (4, select_multiple, optional): Select all the sectors where you have a presence.
* **Political Sentiment** (5, select_one, optional): Select whether you are optimistic or pessimistic about the current political climate.
* **Economic Sentiment** (6, select_one, optional): Select whether you are optimistic or pessimistic about the current economic climate.
* **Global Trends** (7, text, optional): Provide any information about global trends you are aware of.
* **Demographic Information** (8, text, optional): Enter any demographic information you think is relevant to the survey.
