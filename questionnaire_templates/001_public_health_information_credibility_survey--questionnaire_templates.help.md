# Public Health Information Credibility Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect public health information credibility data from various sources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Answer the questions honestly and accurately.
*   Select the most relevant options for each question.
*   Fill out all the required fields.
*   Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Public Health Information Credibility Survey** (id: 1, <name>): This is the title of the survey, which is a text field that you should fill out with your name or the name of your organization.
*   **source_of_information** (id: 2, <name>): This is a select multiple field where you can choose multiple sources of information from the provided options.
*   **trust_level** (id: 3, <name>): This is a select one field where you can select the level of trust you have for the sources of information from the provided options.
*   **confidence_level** (id: 4, <name>): This is a number field where you can rate your confidence level in the information provided, with higher numbers indicating higher confidence.
*   **demographic_characteristics** (id: 5-11, <name>): These are text fields where you can provide demographic characteristics about the sources of information, such as age, gender, location, etc.
*   **comments** (id: 13, <name>): This is a note field where you can provide any additional comments or explanations for your answers.
*   **contact_number** (id: 14, <name>): This is a text field where you can provide a contact number for further follow-up.
*   **contact_email** (id: 15-24, <name>): These are email fields where you can provide email addresses for further follow-up.
*   **demographic_characteristics_2-8** (id: 6-12, <name>): These are text fields where you can provide additional demographic characteristics about the sources of information.

**Tips**

*   Please be as accurate and honest as possible when filling out the form.
*   Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
*   Review and submit your completed form carefully to ensure it is accurate and complete.
*   If you need help or have questions, contact your administrator or helpdesk for assistance.
