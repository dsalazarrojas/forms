# Healthy Habits Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help you assess your healthy habits and identify areas for improvement. It is not a diagnostic tool, but rather a tool for personal reflection and growth.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each question carefully and answer truthfully.
2. Choose the option that best describes your current habits.
3. Take your time to complete the form, and don't hesitate to ask for clarification if you are unsure about any of the questions.
