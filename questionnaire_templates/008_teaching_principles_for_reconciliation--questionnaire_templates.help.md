# Teaching Principles For Reconciliation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your experiences and reflections on reconciliation practices, specifically in the context of educator roles. Your responses will help inform strategies for effective reconciliation practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the answers that apply to you and provide any additional information that you feel is relevant to your experiences and reflections. Each question is labeled with a brief description to help guide your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Educator Roles**: Please select all the roles you have experience with as an educator. This could include primary school teacher, secondary school teacher, school principal, university teacher, or school administrator.
  * Primary School Teacher
  * Secondary School Teacher
  * School Principal
  * University Teacher
  * School Administrator
* **Experiences**: Please select the option that best describes your experience level with reconciliation in the following settings: primary school, secondary school, both primary and secondary school, or other settings. You can also select "Other" if none of the provided options apply.

  * I have taught in secondary school for 2-5 years
  * I have taught in primary and secondary school for 2-5 years
  * I have taught in other settings for 2-5 years
