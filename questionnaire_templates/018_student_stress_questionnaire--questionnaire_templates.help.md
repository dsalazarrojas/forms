# Student Stress Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Stress Questionnaire is designed to help us understand your current stress levels and experiences in various areas, allowing us to provide targeted support and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly, providing the best estimate of your current stress level or experience.
2. For questions that ask for frequency (e.g., "How often do you experience..."), consider the past week or recent time period when answering.
3. Select all applicable options for questions that allow multiple selections (e.g., "What activities give you a sense of pleasure and relaxation?").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **How would you rate your current stress level?** (`general_stress_level`, number, required): Rate your current stress level on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is very little stress and 10 is extremely high stress.
- **How often do you experience anxiety or worrying thoughts?** (`anxiety_and_worrying`, number, required): Answer on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is rarely and 10 is very often.
- **How would you rate your physical discomfort level?** (`physical_discomfort`, number, required): Rate your current physical discomfort level on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is very little discomfort and 10 is very high discomfort.
- **How often do you experience difficulty sleeping?** (`difficulty_sleeping`, number, required): Answer on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is rarely and 10 is very often.
- **What activities give you a sense of pleasure and relaxation?** (`lack_of_pleasure`, select_multiple, required): Select all activities that give you a sense of pleasure and relaxation, such as:
	+ Physical activity
	+ Hobbies
	+ Socializing
	+ Relaxation
	+ Exercise
- **How often do you experience anger or irritability?** (`anger_irritability`, number, required): Answer on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is rarely and 10 is very often.
- **How often do you feel socially withdrawn?** (`social_withdrawal`, number, required): Answer on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is rarely and 10 is very often.
- **How would you rate your current academic burden?** (`academic_burden`, number, required): Rate your current academic burden on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is very little burden and 10 is very high burden.
- **What activities interest you?** (`lack_of_interest`, select_multiple, required): Select all activities that interest you, such as:
	+ Physical activity
	+ Hobbies
	+ Socializing
	+ Relaxation
	+ Exercise
- **How often do you experience fear or panic?** (`fear_and_panic`, number, required): Answer on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is rarely and 10 is very often.
- **How would you rate your current energy level?** (`lack_of_energy`, number, required): Rate your current energy level on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is very little energy and 10 is very high energy.
- **How often do you experience difficulty concentrating?** (`difficulty_concentrating`, number, required): Answer on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is rarely and 10 is very often.
