# Social Media Ads Consumer Behavior Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This questionnaire aims to gather information about the behavior of consumers when interacting with social media ads. It will help understand how often people engage with social media ads, what level of trust they have in ads shown on social media, and how likely they are to purchase a product or service based on social media ads. The data collected will be used to improve social media ad strategies and better serve the consumer needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer each question to the best of your ability.
* Select the option that best matches your current behavior and attitudes.
* Make sure to answer all questions, but you can skip questions if you feel they don't apply to you.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
