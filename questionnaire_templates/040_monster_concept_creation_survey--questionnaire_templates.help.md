# Monster Concept Creation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your monster concept and help us understand its characteristics, name, type, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "What is your name" field.
2. Select the type of monster you are creating from the "Monster Type" dropdown menu.
3. Provide a name for your monster in the "Monster name" field.
4. Upload a relevant file, if applicable, in the "Upload file" field.
5. Write a short description of your monster in the "Creature Description 1" and "Creature Description 2" fields.
6. If you have a relevant image, provide its URL in the "Image URL" field.
7. Repeat the description process in the "Creature Description 2" field, if necessary.
8. Finally, upload another file, if applicable, in the "File Upload 1" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Monster Type** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the type of monster you are creating (e.g., "Yes" for a yes or no answer).
* **Monster name** (`text`, required: false): Provide a name for your monster, e.g., "Dragon" or "Griffin".
* **Description** (`text`, required: false): Write a short description of your monster.
* **Upload file** (`text`, required: false): Upload a file related to your monster concept, e.g., a image or a document.
* **Creature Description 1** (`text`, required: false): Write a short description of your monster.
* **Image URL** (`text`, required: false): If you have a relevant image, provide its URL.
* **Creature Description 2** (`text`, required: false): Repeat the description process, if necessary.
* **File Upload 1** (`text`, required: false): Upload another file, if applicable.

## Tips
* Please be accurate and clear when providing information about your monster concept.
* If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to ask for clarification.
* This form will help us better understand your monster concept and improve our services.
