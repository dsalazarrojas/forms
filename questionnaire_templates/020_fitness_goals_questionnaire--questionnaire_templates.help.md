# Fitness Goals Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your fitness goals, preferences, and availability. It will help us understand your goals and provide you with personalized advice or recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your First Name and Email address.
2. Enter your Phone number.
3. Provide your Height in centimeters.
4. Select your Fitness Goals and Workout Preferences.
5. Choose your Workout Frequency.
6. In the "Goals" field, type a brief description of your fitness goals.
7. Enter your Availability for each of the next 5 sections (Availability 1-5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Height** (number): Enter your height in centimeters.
* **Fitness Goals** (select multiple): Select all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Workout Preferences** (select multiple): Select all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Workout Frequency** (select one): Select one:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Goals** (note): Enter a brief description of your fitness goals.
* **Availability 1-5** (time): Enter your availability for each of the next 5 sections in the format "HH:MM".

Tips:

* Please answer all questions to ensure we can provide you with the best possible advice.
* Make sure to enter correct information to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
