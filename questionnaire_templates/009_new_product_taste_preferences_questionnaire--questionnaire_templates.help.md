# New Product Taste Preferences Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a customer's taste preferences and product ratings for multiple products. It is likely used for market research or customer feedback purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all the questions and fields as accurately and honestly as possible.
2. Select the taste preferences for each product by choosing 'Yes', 'No', or 'Other' for the corresponding questions.
3. Rate each product from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest rating.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the name of the product that you are providing feedback for.
* **Product Name Hint** (`product_name_hint`, `text`, `optional`): This field is for providing any additional information about the product name.
* **Taste Preferences** (`taste_preferences`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): Select the taste you prefer for each product by choosing 'Yes', 'No', or 'Other'.
* **Taste Preferences Hint** (`taste_preferences_hint`, `text`, `optional`): This field is for providing any additional information about your taste preferences.
* **Product Rating** (`product_rating`, `number`, `optional`): Rate the product from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest rating.
* **Product Rating Hint** (`product_rating_hint`, `text`, `optional`): This field is for providing any additional information about the product rating.
* **Time to Complete** (`time_to_complete`, `number`, `optional`): This field is for providing information about the time it took you to complete the form. It is not directly related to the product, so you can ignore it if you don't have an accurate answer.
* **Time to Complete Hint** (`time_to_complete_hint`, `text`, `optional`): This field is for providing any additional information about the time it took you to complete the form.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, `optional`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Email Hint** (`email_hint`, `text`, `optional`): This field is for providing any additional information about your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Phone Hint** (`phone_hint`, `text`, `optional`): This field is for providing any additional information about your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, `optional`): Enter the date you completed the form.
* **Date Hint** (`date_hint`, `text`, `optional`): This field is for providing any additional information about the date you completed the form.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, `optional`): Enter the time you completed the form.
* **Time Hint** (`time_hint`, `text`, `optional`): This field is for providing any additional information about the time you completed the form.
* **Product 2 Name** (`product_name_2`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the name of the second product that you are providing feedback for.
* **Product Name Hint 2** (`product_name_hint_2`, `text`, `optional`): This field is for providing any additional information about the second product name.
* **Taste Preferences 2** (`taste_preferences_2`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): Select the taste you prefer for the second product by choosing 'Yes', 'No', or 'Other'.
* **Taste Preferences 2 Hint** (`taste_preferences_2_hint`, `text`, `optional`): This field is for providing any additional information about your taste preferences for the second product.
* **Product 2 Rating** (`product_rating_2`, `number`, `optional`): Rate the second product from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest rating.
* **Product 2 Rating Hint** (`product_rating_2_hint`, `text`, `optional`): This field is for providing any additional information about the second product rating.
* **Time to Complete 2** (`time_to_complete_2`, `number`, `optional`): This field is for providing information about the time it took you to complete the form for the second product. It is not directly related to the product, so you can ignore it if you don't have an accurate answer.
* **Time To Complete 2 Hint** (`time_to_complete_2_hint`, `text`, `optional`): This field is for providing any additional information about the time it took you to complete the form for the second product.
