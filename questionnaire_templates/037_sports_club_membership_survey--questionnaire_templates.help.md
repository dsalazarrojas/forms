# sports_club_membership_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports club membership survey is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with the sports club. This information will help the management to identify areas of satisfaction and areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Rate your overall satisfaction with the sports club by selecting one of the two options in the "Overall Satisfaction" field.
2. Provide any comments you may have about your experience with the sports club in the "Comments" field.
3. Indicate your satisfaction level with the sports club in the "Satisfaction Level" field.
4. Confirm your status as a sports club member in the "Sports Club Membership" field.
5. Select your sports club from the list in the "Sports Club Name" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field, following the standard email format (username@domain.com).
7. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
8. Provide any additional feedback about your experience with the sports club in the "Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Satisfaction (1)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the sports club.
* **Comments (2)**: Type any comments or suggestions you may have about your experience with the sports club.
* **Satisfaction Level (3)**: Select the level of satisfaction you feel about your experience with the sports club.
* **Sports Club Membership (4)**: Confirm your status as a sports club member.
* **Sports Club Name (5)**: Select your sports club from the list.
* **Email Address (6)**: Enter your email address in the standard format (username@domain.com).
* **Contact Number (7)**: Enter your contact number.
* **Feedback (8)**: Provide any additional feedback you may have about your experience with the sports club.
