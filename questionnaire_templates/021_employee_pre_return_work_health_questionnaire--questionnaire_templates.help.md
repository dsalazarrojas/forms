# Employee Pre Return Work Health Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from employees about their health status and work-related risk factors before they return to work after a period of absence. This information will help management identify potential risks and take necessary steps to ensure a smooth transition and a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For questions with options, select the answer that best describes your current status.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please consult your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Pre Return Work Health Questionnaire (1)
* **Employee Pre Return Work Health Questionnaire**: This is the title of the form. Please enter a brief description of your current health status, including any relevant health issues or concerns you have.

### Employee Health Status (2)
* **Has Employees Health Status**: This question asks about your current health status. Please select the option that best describes your current health status:

  * Active: You are currently healthy and able to perform your work duties without any health restrictions.
  * Inactive: You are currently not healthy and unable to perform your work duties due to health reasons.

### Return Date (3)
* **Employee Return Date**: This question asks for your expected date of return to work. Please enter the date when you plan to return to work.

### Health Risk Factors (4)
* **Employee Health Risk Factors**: This question asks you to list any health-related risk factors that may affect your work performance or safety. Please provide a detailed list of any health concerns or risks you have.

### Work-Related Risk Factors (5)
* **Employee Work-Related Risk Factors**: This question asks you to list any work-related risks or hazards that may affect your health or well-being. Please provide a detailed list of any work-related concerns or hazards you have.

### Health Procedure (6)
* **Employee Health Procedure**: This question asks you to describe any health-related procedures or plans you have in place to maintain or improve your health. Please provide a detailed description of any health-related procedures or plans you have.

### Health Status (7)
* **Employee Health Status**: This question asks about your current health status. Please select the option that best describes your current health status:

  * Active: You are currently healthy and able to perform your work duties without any health restrictions.
  * Inactive: You are currently not healthy and unable to perform your work duties due to health reasons.

### Work Status (8)
* **Employee Work Status**: This question asks about your current work status. Please select the options that best describe your current work status:

  * Active: You are currently working and able to perform your work duties.
  * Inactive: You are currently not working due to health or other reasons.
  * Both: You are both working and not working due to health reasons.

### Health Risk Level (9)
* **Employee Health Risk Level**: This question asks you to rate the level of health risk you pose. Please select a number from 1-10, with 1 being low risk and 10 being high risk.

### Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and accurately, as the information gathered will be used for your benefit and to ensure a safe working environment.
* If you are unsure about any question, please consult your supervisor or HR representative for guidance. 
* Please provide detailed and specific answers for all questions to ensure accurate assessment. 
* This form will be reviewed and discussed with your supervisor or HR representative to ensure your safety and well-being while at work.
