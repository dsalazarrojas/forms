# Performing Ensembles Equipment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the equipment used in performing ensembles, including instrument type, condition, maintenance history, and purchase plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of instrument being surveyed.
2. Indicate the current condition of the instrument.
3. Enter the maintenance fund allocation for the instrument.
4. Describe the plan to replace or purchase the instrument.
5. Enter the date of the last maintenance.
6. Enter the date of the next scheduled maintenance.
7. Enter the date of the last inspection.
8. Enter the date of the next scheduled inspection.
9. (Optional) Enter the estimated budget to replace or purchase the instrument.
10. (Optional) Enter the replacement cost of the instrument.
11. (Optional) Enter the maintenance cost of the instrument.
12. (Optional) Enter the inspection cost of the instrument.
13. Enter the name of the last user of the instrument.
14. Add any additional notes about the instrument.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Instrument Type** (`instrument_type`, `select_one`): Select the type of instrument being surveyed. Choose from the list of available options: Bass, Drum Set, Guitar, Keyboard, Percussion, Piano, Strings, Vocal, Wind.
* **Instrument Condition** (`instrument_condition`, `select_multiple`): Indicate the current condition of the instrument. Choose from the list of available options: Good, Fair, Poor, Not Needed.
* **Maintenance Fund** (`maintenance_fund`, `number`): Enter the allocated maintenance fund for the instrument.
* **Purchase Plan** (`purchase_plan`, `text`): Describe the plan to replace or purchase the instrument.
* **Last Maintenance** (`last_maintenance`, `date`): Enter the date of the last maintenance performed on the instrument.
* **Next Maintenance** (`next_maintenance`, `date`): Enter the date of the next scheduled maintenance for the instrument.
* **Last Inspection** (`last_inspection`, `date`): Enter the date of the last inspection performed on the instrument.
* **Next Inspection** (`next_inspection`, `date`): Enter the date of the next scheduled inspection for the instrument.
* **Purchase Budget** (`purchase_budget`, `number`): (Optional) Enter the estimated budget to replace or purchase the instrument.
* **Replacement Cost** (`replacement_cost`, `number`): (Optional) Enter the replacement cost of the instrument.
* **Maintenance Cost** (`maintenance_cost`, `number`): (Optional) Enter the maintenance cost of the instrument.
* **Inspection Cost** (`inspection_cost`, `number`): (Optional) Enter the inspection cost of the instrument.
* **Last User** (`last_user`, `text`): Enter the name of the last user of the instrument.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`): Add any additional notes about the instrument.
