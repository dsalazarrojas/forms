# Healthcare Workforce Wellbeing Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the overall wellbeing of healthcare workers, evaluating their physical, mental, emotional, and social health, as well as their work-life balance and team support. The goal is to identify areas of concern and provide insights for improvements to promote a healthier and more supportive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Introduction**: This is a brief text field where you can provide any comments or context about your current state.
2. **Rate Your Overall Wellbeing**: Select a number from 1-5 to rate your overall wellbeing.
3. **Physical Health**: Rate your physical health on a scale of 1-5.
4. **Mental Health**: Rate your mental health on a scale of 1-5.
5. **Emotional Wellbeing**: Rate your emotional wellbeing on a scale of 1-5.
6. **Social Health**: Rate your social health on a scale of 1-5.
7. **Work-Life Balance**: Describe one thing you think is most important for improving your work-life balance.
8. **Work Environment**: Describe your ideal work environment.
9. **Team Support**: Rate how you feel about your team support on a scale of 1-5.
10. **Balance Between Work and Personal Life**: Select the importance of having a balance between work and personal life. This question is a scale with four options:
	* Work life balance is not important
	* Somewhat important
	* Very important
	* Extremely important
11. **Burnout**: Select all factors that cause you burnout (select multiple):
	* Physical demands of the job
	* Emotional demands of the job
	* Work environment
	* Lack of work life balance
	* Lack of recognition
	* Lack of control
	* Poor leadership
	* Poor communication
	* Lack of autonomy
	* Conflict with colleagues
	* Other (please describe)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Introduction**: A brief text field where you can provide any comments or context about your current state.
	* This field is optional and serves as an introduction to your responses.
* **2. Wellbeing Scale**: Rate your overall wellbeing on a scale of 1-5.
	* This question helps us understand your self-assessment of your current state.
* **3. Physical Health**: Rate your physical health on a scale of 1-5.
	* This question is about your self-assessment of your physical well-being.
* **4. Mental Health**: Rate your mental health on a scale of 1-5.
	* This question is about your self-assessment of your mental well-being.
* **5. Emotional Wellbeing**: Rate your emotional wellbeing on a scale of 1-5.
	* This question is about your self-assessment of your emotional well-being.
* **6. Social Health**: Rate your social health on a scale of 1-5.
	* This question is about your self-assessment of your social well-being.
* **7. Work-Life Balance**: Describe one thing you think is most important for improving your work-life balance.
	* This question is about identifying areas where you feel you need improvement.
* **8. Work Environment**: Describe your ideal work environment.
	* This question is about your preferences and expectations for your work environment.
* **9. Team Support**: Rate how you feel about your team support on a scale of 1-5.
	* This question is about your self-assessment of your team support.
* **10. Balance Between Work and Personal Life**: Select the importance of having a balance between work and personal life.
	* This question is about your priorities and expectations.
* **11. Burnout**: Select all factors that cause you burnout.
	* This question is about understanding sources of burnout for you.
