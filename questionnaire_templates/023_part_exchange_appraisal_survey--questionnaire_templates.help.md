# Part Exchange Appraisal Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect general information from customers related to a vehicle part exchange or appraisal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name as requested.
2.  Enter your email address as requested.
3.  Enter your phone number as requested.
4.  Enter the year of the vehicle in question.
5.  Select the make of the vehicle from the provided options.
6.  Enter the model of the vehicle.
7.  Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Name** (`customer-name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Customer Email** (`customer-email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address where you would like to receive updates and communications from us.
*   **Customer Phone Number** (`customer-phone-number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
*   **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle-year`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the year of the vehicle in question.
*   **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle-make`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the make of the vehicle from the options provided.
*   **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle-model`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the model of the vehicle.
*   **Vehicle VIN** (`vehicle-vin`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle.
