# Character Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect in-depth information about a character's personality, habits, relationships, and background. It's a comprehensive tool to help users understand the character's core traits and behaviors.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the **Creator and Character Basics** section, which includes the **Creator Full Name**, **Character Full Legal Name**, and **Character Primary Personality Traits** fields. These fields are essential to establishing the character's identity and core personality.
2.  Move on to the **Personality and Daily Habits** section, which includes **Character Daily Routine Brief**, **Favorite Pastime or Hobby**, and **Character Core Values System** fields. These fields help understand the character's daily life, interests, and guiding principles.
3.  In the **Values and Beliefs** section, fill out the **Character Core Values System** and **Major Belief or Philosophy** fields. These fields reveal the character's underlying values and outlook on life.
4.  The **Relationships and Social Dynamics** section includes **Closest Friend or Ally**, **Primary Rival or Enemy**, and **Nature of Their Social Circle** fields. These fields help understand the character's relationships and social interactions.
5.  In the **Conflict and Resolution** section, fill out the **Biggest Fear or Vulnerability** and **Typical Approach to Conflict** fields. These fields reveal the character's approach to conflict and how they handle disagreements.
6.  The **Questionnaire Finalization** section includes **Major Turning Point Event** and **Confirmation of Questionnaire Completion** fields. These fields help finalize the character's profile and confirm the completion of the questionnaire.
7.  Finally, fill out the **Date of Completion** field with the current date and any additional comments or thoughts in the **Additional Comments or Thoughts** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Creator Full Name**: Enter the full name of the character's creator, e.g., Author, Writer, or Student.
*   **Character Full Legal Name**: Enter the character's full legal name.
*   **Character Primary Personality Traits**: List 3-5 core characteristics that describe the character's personality.
*   **Character Daily Routine Brief**: Describe how the character spends a typical day.
*   **Favorite Pastime or Hobby**: What does the character do for fun or relaxation?
*   **Character Core Values System**: What principles guide the character's actions?
*   **Major Belief or Philosophy**: Does the character have a specific outlook on life?
*   **Religious or Spiritual Background**: Briefly describe the character's religious or spiritual background.
*   **Closest Friend or Ally**: Who is the character's closest friend or ally?
*   **Primary Rival or Enemy**: Who is the character's primary rival or enemy?
*   **Nature of Their Social Circle**: Is the character a loner or part of a group?
*   **Biggest Fear or Vulnerability**: What is the character most afraid of?
*   **Typical Approach to Conflict**: How does the character handle disagreement?
*   **Major Turning Point Event**: Briefly describe one moment that changed the character's life.
*   **Confirmation of Questionnaire Completion**: Confirm that you have completed the questionnaire thoughtfully and honestly.
*   **Date of Completion**: Enter the current date.
*   **Additional Comments or Thoughts**: Any other relevant information or context about the character.
