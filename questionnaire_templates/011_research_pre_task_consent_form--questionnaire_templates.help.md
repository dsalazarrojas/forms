# Research Pre-Task Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research Pre-Task Consent Form is designed to collect participant information and consent for research tasks. This form is a crucial step in the research process, ensuring that participants understand their role and responsibilities in the study.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the instructions carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and requirements of the form.
2. Fill out all fields accurately and honestly.
3. Review each field to ensure you have provided the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Research Pre-Task Consent Form** (`research_pre_task_consent_form_page`, text, required/optional): A page header that introduces the form and its purpose.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details_page`, text, required): A section to collect participant information, such as name, email, or other contact details.
* **Participant Informed Consent** (`participant_inform_consent`, text, required): A section to collect participant consent, which is a crucial aspect of research tasks.
* **Participant Informed Consent 2-23** (`participant_inform_consent_2-23`, text, required): A continuation of the previous field, collecting more detailed information about participant consent.

Note: Please fill out all fields accurately and honestly to ensure the participant's understanding and consent are documented correctly. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the research team for clarification.
