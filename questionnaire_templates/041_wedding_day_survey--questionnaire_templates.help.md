# wedding_day_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The wedding_day_survey form is designed to collect detailed information about a wedding event. This form helps in recording dates, venues, attire, and other related aspects, making it a valuable resource for planning and reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out each field as accurately as possible**. Each field is crucial in recording the specific details about the wedding event.
2. **Ensure that you have all the necessary information** before starting the form to avoid going back and forth.
3. **If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field explanations below**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reported date** (`reported_date`, `date`, **Required: false**): Enter the date of the reported wedding event.
* **Wedding day** (`wedding_day`, `time`, **Required: false**): Enter the time of the wedding event.
* **Wedding day holiday** (`wedding_day_holiday`, `select_multiple`, **Required: false**): Choose the holiday associated with the wedding event (e.g., Christmas, Valentine's Day).
* **Reported time** (`reported_time`, `time`, **Required: false**): Enter the time when the report was submitted.
* **Wedding Venue** (`wedding_venue`, `select_multiple`, **Required: false**): Select the venue where the wedding event took place (e.g., Home, Restaurant, Park, Garden, Beach, Hotel, Resort, Other, Venue).
* **Wedding time** (`wedding_time`, `number`, **Required: false**): Enter the duration of the wedding event.
* **Wedding venue details** (`wedding_venue_details`, `text`, **Required: false**): Provide any additional details about the wedding venue.
* **Wedding dress code** (`wedding_dress_code`, `select_one`, **Required: false**): Select the attire type required for the wedding event (e.g., Black Tie, Semi-formal, Formal, Casual, Beachwear, Other).
* **Number of guests** (`number_of_guests`, `number`, **Required: false`): Enter the number of guests attending the wedding.
* **Number of children** (`number_of_children`, `number`, **Required: false`): Enter the number of children attending the wedding.
* **Number of gifts** (`number_of_gifts`, `number`, **Required: false`): Enter the number of gifts given or received during the wedding.
