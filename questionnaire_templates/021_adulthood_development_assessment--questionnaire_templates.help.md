## Adulthood Development Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adulthood Development Assessment form is designed to help individuals evaluate their level of development in key areas of adulthood, such as life skills, financial management, employment status, and social engagement. This form is a self-assessment tool to help respondents gauge their growth and development in these critical domains.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Assessment Header** (`assessment_header`): This section serves as a placeholder for the assessment title.
2. **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`): This field is optional but allows you to input your name for identification purposes only.
3. **Life Skills Competence** (`life_skills_competence`): Rate your self-perceived level of competence in life skills, which includes skills such as communication, problem-solving, time management, and decision-making. Choose from the provided options: Low, Moderate, or High.
4. **Financial Management Skills** (`financial_management`): Assess your self-perceived level of financial management skills, including budgeting, saving, and investing. Choose from the provided options: Needs improvement, Adequate, or Strong.
5. **Employment Status** (`employment_status`): Select your current employment situation:
	* Employed full time
	* Employed part time
	* Unemployed
	* Student
	* Retired
6. **Social Engagement Level** (`social_engagement`): Rate the frequency of your social activities, such as meeting friends, attending events, or participating in hobbies. Choose from the provided options: Rarely, Sometimes, or Often.
7. **Development Goals** (`development_goals`): List your top 3 personal development goals. This field is optional and allows you to specify areas you'd like to improve or focus on.
8. **Supports Needed to Reach Goals** (`supports_needed`): Select any support systems that you may need to help you achieve your development goals, such as:
	* Coaching
	* Financial planning
	* Mentoring
	* Training programs
9. **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`): Enter the date you completed this form for record-keeping purposes.

## Tips
* Please answer questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* This form is a self-assessment tool; your responses will only be used for your personal development purposes.
* You can modify the form to suit your needs or use it as is.
