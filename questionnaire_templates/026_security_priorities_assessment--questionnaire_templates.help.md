# Security Priorities Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an individual's or team's security priorities, helping to identify areas of focus and risk levels. It is intended to be completed by someone familiar with the security landscape of the organization, such as a security specialist or risk manager. The form is meant to be used for internal purposes only.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select all relevant security focus areas for your assessment (Security Risks, Security Threats, or Compliance).
2.  Choose the level of threat to the organization (High, Medium, or Low).
3.  Enter a brief description of the security readiness of your team or department (Security Readiness).
4.  Assign a numerical risk score for each selected focus area (Risk Score).
5.  Identify the priority level of each risk (High, Medium, or Low).
6.  Describe the security planning and steps taken to address identified risks (Security Planning).
7.  (Optional) Select additional security focus areas and assign risk scores for each (Risk Focus Areas 2 and 3).
8.  Describe any additional security planning and steps taken to address new risks (Security Planning 2).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Security Focus Areas** (`Security Focus Areas`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is used to select all relevant security focus areas for your assessment. Choose from the options: Security Risks, Security Threats, or Compliance.
* **Threat Level** (`Threat Level`, `select_one`, required): This field determines the level of threat to the organization. Choose from the options: High, Medium, or Low.
* **Risk Focus Areas 1** (`Risk Focus Areas`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is similar to "Security Focus Areas" and allows for the selection of focus areas.
* **Risk Focus Areas 2** (`Risk Focus Areas 2`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field allows for the selection of additional focus areas if necessary.
* **Risk Focus Areas 3** (`Risk Focus Areas 3`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is similar to "Risk Focus Areas 2" and allows for the selection of additional focus areas.
* **Security Readiness** (`Security Readiness`, `text`, required): This field requires a brief description of the security readiness of your team or department.
* **Risk Score 1** (`Risk Score`, `number`, required): Assign a numerical risk score for each selected focus area.
* **Risk Score 2** (`Risk Score 2`, `number`, optional): Assign a numerical risk score for each selected focus area 2.
* **Risk Score 3** (`Risk Score 3`, `number`, optional): Assign a numerical risk score for each selected focus area 3.
* **Risk Priority** (`Risk Priority`, `select_one`, required): This field determines the priority level of each risk. Choose from the options: High, Medium, or Low.
* **Security Planning** (`Security Planning`, `text`, required): This field is used to describe the security planning and steps taken to address identified risks.
* **Security Planning 2** (`Security Planning 2`, `text`, optional): This field is similar to "Security Planning" and allows for the description of additional security planning.
* **Risk Focus Areas 1**, **Security Readiness**, **Risk Score 1**, **Risk Priority** and **Security Planning** are all required fields, while **Risk Focus Areas 2** and **Risk Score 2**, **Security Focus Areas 3** and **Risk Score 3**, and **Security Planning 2** are optional fields.
