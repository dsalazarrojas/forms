## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from account managers regarding the state of their accounts, specifically around overdue accounts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please go through each page and answer the questions accordingly.

3. In the "overdue account management survey" section, you will find questions about your current communication practices, data collection, and follow-up activities.
