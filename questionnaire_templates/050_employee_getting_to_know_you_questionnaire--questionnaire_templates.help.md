# Employee Getting To Know You Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This questionnaire is designed to help the management team get to know you, their colleagues, and the team's dynamics better. It is used to collect information about your personal details, interests, and work habits.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Start by clicking on the link to the questionnaire**, which will take you to the main page.
2. **Answer all the questions** on each page, and make sure to fill in all the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. **Save your progress and come back later if you need to**, as the form will remember your answers.
4. **Submit the form when you have completed all the questions**, and click on the "Submit" button to send your answers to the management team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Main Page
* **Main Page** (`main_page`, text, not required): This is the introduction page of the questionnaire. Please read it carefully before proceeding.

### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as you would like it to be known in the team.
*   This is the field where you should enter your first name.
*   Make sure to spell it correctly, as this is how your colleagues will address you.

### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title as per your current role within the company.
*   This is the field where you should enter your job title.
*   This will help the management team understand your position within the team.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your company email address.
*   This is the field where you should enter your company email address.
*   Make sure to use your company email address to avoid spam and security issues.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your company phone number.
*   This is the field where you should enter your company phone number.
*   Please use your company phone number to ensure that you can be reached by your colleagues.

### Favorite Food
* **Favorite Food** (`favorite_food`, select one, required): Choose your favorite food from the options:
	+ Pizza
	+ Salad
	+ Sushi
	+ Tacos
*   This is the field where you should select your favorite food.
*   You can choose only one option, as this is a select one question.

### Favorite Hobby
* **Favorite Hobby** (`favorite_hobby`, select multiple, required): Select all the hobbies that apply to you from the options:
	+ Reading
	+ Hiking
	+ Cooking
	+ Traveling
*   This is the field where you should select all your favorite hobbies.
*   You can select multiple options, as this is a select multiple question.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
*   This is the field where you should enter your date of birth.
*   Please use the correct format to avoid any errors.

### Time of Work
* **Time of Work** (`time_of_work`, time, required): Enter your time of work in the format (HH:MM AM/PM).
*   This is the field where you should enter your time of work.
*   Please use the correct format to avoid any errors.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): This is a field for any additional information you would like to share with the management team.
*   You can use this field to add any comments or notes that are not covered by the other questions.
*   This field is not required, so feel free to use it as you see fit.

## Tips
*   Make sure to answer all the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) as they are crucial for the management team to get to know you better.
*   Please be honest and accurate in your answers.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the management team.
