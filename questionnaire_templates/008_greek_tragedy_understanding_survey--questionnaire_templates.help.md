# Greek Tragedy Understanding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather data about participants' characteristics, interests, familiarity, perceptions, and past experiences related to the Greek Tragedy theme.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the pages listed below, and fill out each field according to your understanding of the question being asked.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey of the Participant Characteristics (1)** (`survey_participant_characteristics`, text, Optional): This section is where you can provide a short description of yourself. Please enter your relevant characteristics in a few sentences.
* **Participants Interest (2)** (`survey_participant_interest`, Select Multiple, Optional): Here, you can select all the interests that apply to you. Please note that you can choose multiple options.
* **Participants Familiarity (3)** (`survey_participant_familiarity`, Number, Required): This question is asking about your level of familiarity with the topic. Please respond with a number indicating how well-versed you are with the subject matter.
* **Participants Perceptions (4)** (`survey_participant_perceptions`, Select One, Required): In this question, you are asked to select one option that best represents your perception about the topic. Please choose the option that best fits your understanding.
* **Participants Interest (5)** (`survey_participant_interest`, Select Multiple, Required): This section is similar to the previous one, but here you are asked to select all the options that apply to you.
* **Date of Last Tragedy (6)** (`date_of_last_tragedy`, Date, Optional): If you have experienced a tragic event, please enter the date it occurred.
* **Time of Last Tragedy (7)** (`time_of_last_tragedy`, Time, Optional): If you have experienced a tragic event, please enter the time it occurred.
* **Notes (8)** (`notes`, Note, Optional): Here, you can provide any additional information that you think is relevant to your response.

## Tips
* Make sure to respond to all required fields (3, 4, 5). The rest are optional, but providing them will add more depth to your response.
* If you experience an issue while completing this form, contact the administrator for assistance.
