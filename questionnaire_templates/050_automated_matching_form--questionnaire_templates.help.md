# Automated Matching Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Automated Matching Form is designed to collect information about your personal profile, interests, and preferences to help find suitable matches.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Select all relevant areas of interest from the list.
3. Choose your primary matching goal.
4. Describe your skills offered and sought in the provided text fields.
5. Select your available days and timezone.
6. Indicate your consent to data processing and agree to participation rules.
7. Decide on match notifications and choose any additional matching criteria.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your complete name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for match notifications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide an optional contact method.
* **Areas of Interest** (`areas_interest`, select_multiple, required): Select all fields you are passionate about (e.g., Technology, Arts, Sports, Business, Education, Science).
* **Matching Goal** (`matching_goal`, select_one, required): Choose your primary outcome (e.g., Mentorship, Networking, Project Collaboration, Study Buddy).
* **Description of Skills Offered** (`skills_offered`, text, required): Describe what you can contribute to a match.
* **Description of Skills Sought** (`skills_sought`, text, required): Describe what you are looking for in a match.
* **Available Days** (`available_days`, select_multiple, required): Select when you are most active (e.g., Weekdays, Weekends, Evenings).
* **Timezone** (`timezone`, select_one, required): Choose your local time offset (e.g., UTC-5, UTC-8, UTC+0, UTC+1, Other).
* **Consent to Data Processing** (`allow_data_processing`, select_one, required): Indicate your consent for data processing.
* **Receive Match Notifications** (`match_notifications`, select_one, required): Decide if you want to receive match notifications.
* **Agreement to Participation Rules** (`participation_rules`, select_one, required): Agree to abide by community guidelines.
* **Additional Matching Criteria** (`additional_criteria`, text, optional): Provide any additional specific preferences.

By following these steps, you can complete the Automated Matching Form and increase the chances of finding a suitable match.
