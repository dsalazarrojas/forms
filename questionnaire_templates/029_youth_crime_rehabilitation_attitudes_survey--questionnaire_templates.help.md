# Youth Crime Rehabilitation Attitudes Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Crime Rehabilitation Attitudes Survey is designed to collect opinions and attitudes from youth about crime rehabilitation. It aims to gather information on their views on the effectiveness of different approaches to crime rehabilitation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in the form title at the top of the page.
* Answer each question in the corresponding section, using the response format required for each question.
* For numerical questions, enter a whole number.
* For multiple choice questions, select one or more options that apply to your opinion.
* For open-ended questions, provide a detailed answer.
* If you have additional comments or feedback, you can add them in the "Form Comments" field.
* Finally, provide your contact information if you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions.
