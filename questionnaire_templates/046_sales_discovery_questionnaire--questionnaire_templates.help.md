# Sales Discovery Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from potential leads to help our sales team better understand their requirements and needs. It will help to identify the priority level of the lead, their buying context, and other important details that can aid in making informed sales decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Email**, and **Phone** in the respective fields.
2. Select the **Priority** level of the lead (High, Medium, or Low).
3. Provide details about the **Buying Context**, which is a brief description of why you are looking for a sales solution.
4. Select the **Lead Details** relevant to your lead.
5. Enter the **First Call Details**, which includes the date and time for the first call.
6. Choose the **Second Call Details**, which includes the time for the second call.
7. Specify the **Lead Priorities**, if any.
8. Enter the **Lead Context**, which is a short description of the lead.
9. Select the **Lead Status**, whether the lead is Active or Inactive.
10. Choose the **Follow Up**, whether you want to follow up with the lead (Yes or No).
11. Select the **Lead Source**, which is where the lead originated from.
12. Enter the **Lead Source Info**, which is a brief description of the lead source.
13. Enter the **Lead Info**, which is any additional information about the lead.
14. Enter the **Follow Up Info**, which is any additional information for the follow-up conversation.
15. Select the **Follow Up Status**, which is the status of the follow-up conversation (Active or Inactive).
16. Select the **Lead Priority**, which is the priority level of the lead (High, Medium, or Low).
17. Select the **Lead Type**, which is the type of lead.
18. Enter the **Lead Status Date**, which is the date when the lead status was last updated.
19. Enter the **Lead Status Time**, which is the time when the lead status was last updated.
20. Select the **Lead Status Priority**, which is the priority level of the lead status (High, Medium, or Low).
21. Enter the **First Call Date**, which is the date of the first call.
22. Enter the **First Call Time**, which is the time of the first call.
23. Enter the **Second Call Date**, which is the date of the second call.
24. Select the **Follow Up Status**, which is the status of the follow-up conversation (Active or Inactive).
