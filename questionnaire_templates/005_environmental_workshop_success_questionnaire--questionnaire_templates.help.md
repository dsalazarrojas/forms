# Environmental Workshop Success Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the quality and effectiveness of environmental workshops. It helps organizers evaluate their sessions, gather feedback, and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Select the appropriate options for each question.
3. Fill in any relevant details in the text fields.
4. Submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Quality** (`session_quality`): Please describe the overall quality of the session, including its organization, content, and delivery.
* **Learning Impact** (`learning_impact`): Did the workshop have a positive impact on your learning or understanding of the topic? Choose one of the options: Yes or No.
* **Future Workshops** (`future_workshops`): Are you interested in participating in future workshops on this topic? 
* **Schools Nonprofits** (`schools_nonprofits`): Are you a representative of a school or nonprofit that might be interested in hosting a workshop on this topic? 
* **Community Programs** (`community_programs`): Are you interested in developing or promoting community programs related to the workshop topic? 
* **Session Length** (`session_length`): What is the ideal session length for future workshops on this topic? 
* **Session Frequency** (`session_frequency`): How often would you like to see workshops on this topic held? 
* **Session Date** (`session_date`): Select a date for a future workshop, if applicable. 
* **Session Time** (`session_time`): Select a time for a future workshop, if applicable. 
* **Participants** (`participants`): Please provide any additional comments or suggestions about the workshop participants. 
* **Comments** (`comments`): Any additional feedback or comments about the workshop? 
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Select the tool that was assigned to you during the workshop.
