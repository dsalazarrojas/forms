# Finder Qualification Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Finder Qualification Survey is a form designed to collect information from applicants to assess their eligibility and suitability for a particular position or opportunity. The survey aims to gather essential details about the applicant's skills, availability, and other qualifications to determine their fit for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, follow these steps:

1. Read the welcome message on the first page to understand the purpose and expectations of the survey.
2. Fill in your applicant details, including your name and any other relevant information.
3. Select your relevant skills from the list provided.
4. Choose your availability for the opportunity.
5. Enter your date of birth (if applicable).
6. Select your qualification process (if applicable).
7. Add any additional notes or comments about your qualifications.
8. Enter your email address and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): This is the title of the survey, which is not editable. It's here to give you an idea of what the survey is about.
* **Enter Applicant Details** (`applicant_details`, `text`, required/optional): Please fill in your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **What Skills Do You Have** (`skills`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the skills that you have and are relevant to the opportunity. This will help us understand your suitability for the role.
* **What Is Your Availability** (`availability`, `text`, required/optional): Choose your availability for the opportunity. This will help us understand when you can start or commit to the position.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your date of birth, if this information is relevant to the opportunity.
* **Qualification Process** (`qualification_process`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): If applicable, select the qualification process you are interested in.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your qualifications. This is an opportunity to provide more context or explanations.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further information or next steps.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further information or next steps.
