# Hair Salon Client Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help hair salon staff gather valuable insights about clients' preferences, visit habits, and experiences. The information collected will aid the salon in providing better services and building stronger relationships with clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the prompts and answer the questions truthfully. Fill in all the fields as completely as possible, but feel free to leave fields blank if you're unsure or don't have the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be known by the salon staff.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Type in your email address so the salon can stay in touch with you.
- **Hair Color** (`hair_color`, `text`, required): Write in your hair color (e.g., blonde, red, brown).
- **Favorite Hair Product** (`favorite_hair_product`, `select_one`, required): Choose the hair product you use most (e.g., conditioner, shampoo, serum).
- **Hair Consultation** (`hair_consultation`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate which hair consultation options you're interested in (e.g., color analysis, style advice).
- **Services** (`services`, `select_one`, required): Choose which hair services you would like to receive during your visit (e.g., cut, color, highlights).
- **Visit Frequency** (`visit_frequency`, `date`, required): Select the date you typically visit the salon (e.g., weekly, monthly, yearly).
- **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, `time`, required): Choose the time of day you usually visit the salon (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening).
- **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Leave any additional comments or notes for the salon staff to know.

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate when filling in your information.
- If you're unsure about any of the options, feel free to ask the salon staff for guidance.
- This form will help the salon tailor their services to your needs and preferences.
