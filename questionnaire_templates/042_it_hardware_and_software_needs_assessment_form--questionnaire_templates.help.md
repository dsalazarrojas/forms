# it_hardware_and_software_needs_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT Hardware and Software Needs Assessment Form is used to gather information about the current state of IT hardware and software in an organization, helping to identify areas that require upgrade or replacement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what each field is asking for.
2. Fill in the fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3. Make sure to select the correct options for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **IT Hardware Needs**: This field is for providing a brief description of your current IT hardware needs.
* **IT Software Needs**: This field is for describing your current IT software needs.
* **Hardware Description**: This field is for providing a detailed description of your current hardware.
* **How old is your current hardware?**: This field is a select-one type field that asks for the age of your current hardware. The options provided are for different years ago (e.g., 2 years ago, 3 years ago, etc.).
* **How old is your current software?**: Similar to the hardware field, this is a select-one type field that asks for the age of your current software.
* **Is this item budgeted?**: This field is a select-one type field that asks if the equipment or software is budgeted. The options are True, False, and Other.
* **Which department uses this equipment or software?**: This field is a select-multiple type field that asks for the department(s) that use this equipment or software. The options are IT Department, Finance Department, HR Department, Marketing Department, and Other.
* **Which location uses this equipment or software?**: This field is a select-multiple type field that asks for the location(s) that use this equipment or software. The options are Headquarters, Branch Office, Remote Location, and Other.
* **Which employees are affected by this equipment or software?**: This field is a select-multiple type field that asks for the employee(s) affected by this equipment or software. The options are Only me, My team, My team and other teams, Entire company, and Other.
* **What is the model of your current hardware?**: This field is for providing the model of your current hardware.
* **What is the version of your current software?**: This field is for providing the version of your current software.
* **Comments**: This field is for leaving any additional comments or information about your current hardware or software.
 
**Tips**

* Please be as specific as possible when filling in the fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can leave a comment in the Comments field.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, contact your IT department.
