# transformers_team_coach_explore - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants in the transformers program related to weight loss. It will help us understand their weight loss commitment, goals, and progress, and identify areas for improvement in the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer the ones that apply to you.
2. Fill in the "Title" field with your name or a nickname you prefer to be referred to by.
3. Answer questions 2-7 as they apply to your experiences in the transformers program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`title`, text, optional): Please enter your name or a nickname you'd like to be referred to by. This will help us address you personally in our feedback responses.
* **Weight Loss Commitment** (`weight_loss_commitment`, number, optional): Rate your level of commitment to weight loss on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is "not committed" and 10 is "very committed".
* **Weight Loss Goals** (`weight_loss_goals`, select_multiple, optional): Check the boxes that apply to your weight loss goals. For example, you can check "Yes" for "Yes, I have weight loss goals" and "No" for "No, I do not have weight loss goals".
* **Support System** (`support_system`, select_multiple, optional): Check the boxes that apply to your support system. For example, you can check "Yes" for "Yes, I have a support system" and "No" for "No, I do not have a support system".
* **Health Goals** (`health_goals`, select_one, optional): Select one option that best describes your health goals. You can choose from "Yes, I have health goals" or "No, I do not have health goals".
* **Program Participation** (`program_participation`, select_multiple, optional): Check the boxes that apply to your participation in the program. For example, you can check "Yes" for "Yes, I participated in the program" and "No" for "No, I did not participate in the program".
* **Weight Loss Progress** (`weight_loss_progress`, select_one, optional): Select one option that best describes your weight loss progress. You can choose from "Yes, I experienced weight loss progress" or "No, I did not experience weight loss progress".
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, text, optional): If you have any additional information about your experience in the program that we should know, please enter it here. For example, you can describe any challenges you faced or successes you achieved.

## Tips
- Answer questions honestly and as accurately as possible.
- If you have any further comments or feedback, please use the "Participant Info" field to provide them.
- This form will only be used to improve the transformers program, and your feedback will be kept confidential.
