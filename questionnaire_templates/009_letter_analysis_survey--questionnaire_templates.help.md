# Letter Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Letter Analysis Survey is a form designed to assess the quality and clarity of a letter's content, tone, and overall effectiveness. It aims to gather feedback from reviewers on the letter's strength and weaknesses, providing insights for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the letter provided and take note of your observations and thoughts.
2. Fill in the relevant sections with your feedback and comments.
3. Answer the questions about the letter's clarity, tone, and effectiveness.
4. Provide your email address to receive the feedback.
5. Optional: If you find any attachment or image relevant to the letter, please attach or provide a link.
6. Add any additional comments or suggestions.
7. Confirm that you have reviewed the letter and indicate whether a signature is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Letter Content (2)**: Describe the letter's content in your own words.
* **Clarity (3)**: Choose the level of clarity of the letter: High, Medium, Low.
* **Tone (4)**: Choose the tone of the letter: Formal, Informal, Neutral, Polite, Assertive, Objective.
* **Effectiveness (5)**: Rate the letter's effectiveness, using a numerical value (e.g., 1-10).
* **Feedback (6)**: Enter your email address to receive feedback on your comments.
* **Suggestions (7)**: Add any suggestions you have for improvement.
* **Additional Comments (8)**: Add any additional comments or remarks.
* **Attachment (9)**: If applicable, attach a document, email, or image related to the letter.
* **Signature (10)**: Confirm whether a signature is required for the letter.
* **Reviewer Comments (11)**: Add any comments or notes about the letter.
* **Reviewer Suggestions (12)**: Add any suggestions for improvement.
* **Additional Comments from Reviewer (13)**: Add any additional comments from the reviewer.
* **Final Comments (14)**: Add any final comments or remarks.

**Tips**

* Please be as detailed and honest as possible in your feedback.
* If you find any attachment or image relevant, please attach it or provide a link.
* This form is for your comments and suggestions only, not for formal responses or approval.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
