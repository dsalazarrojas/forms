# Female Archetype Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the female archetypes, a set of hypothetical female prototypes used to describe the characteristics and tendencies of women across various life stages and experiences. This assessment is intended to provide a comprehensive overview of the female individual's life, interests, and circumstances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Female Archetype Assessment, follow these steps:

* Please select the correct answers to each question based on your own experiences and circumstances.
* For select multiple options, choose all that apply.
* For select one options, choose only one answer.
* Make sure to answer all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What is your age?
* **What is your age?** (`female_archetype_1`, Number, Required): This question asks for your current age.
