# Jet Lag Sleep Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Jet Lag Sleep Assessment form is designed to help collect information about your experience with jet lag. This form will ask you questions about your travel dates, symptoms, and other factors related to jet lag. Providing accurate answers will help with understanding and addressing jet lag issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date you started traveling.
2. For each question, select all the answers that apply to your experience with jet lag.
3. If you have any concerns or difficulties with jet lag, please provide as much detail as you can.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Date**: This is the date you started traveling. Please select the date you arrived at your destination.
* **Travel Symptom**: 
*   **Yes** : You experienced symptoms while traveling.
  * No** : You did not experience symptoms while traveling.
* **Sleep Duration Pre Travel**:
  * **Yes** : Your sleep duration was longer than usual before traveling.
  * **No** : Your sleep duration was not affected by traveling.
* **Sleep Quality Pre Travel**:
  * **Good** : Your sleep quality was good before traveling.
  * **Poor** : Your sleep quality was poor before traveling.
* **Sleep Duration Post Travel**:
  * **Yes** : Your sleep duration was longer than usual after traveling.
  * **No** : Your sleep duration was not affected by traveling.
* **Sleep Quality Post Travel**:
  * **Good** : Your sleep quality was good after traveling.
  * **Poor** : Your sleep quality was poor after traveling.
* **Jet Lag**:
  * **Yes** : You experienced jet lag.
  * **No** : You did not experience jet lag.
* **Follow up Time**:
  * **Yes** : You experienced follow-up time (e.g., time zone changes).
  * **No** : You did not experience follow-up time (e.g., time zone changes).

## Tips

* Be accurate and honest when answering the questions.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, feel free to provide more information in the comments section.
* If you have any concerns or questions, contact your healthcare provider or a qualified professional for assistance.
