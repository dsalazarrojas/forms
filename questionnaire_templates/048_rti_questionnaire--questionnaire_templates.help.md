RTI Questionnaire
The purpose of this form is to collect information about an individual's request to access resources or services. This form is used by requesters to provide details about themselves and their requests.
To avoid duplication of effort, please note that the following fields are not currently being used:
  * request_type (ID: 3)
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "New Request" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are a requester or not (ID: 1).
3. If you are a requester, fill out your requested date (ID: 2).
4. Fill out the name of the person making the request (ID: 4).
5. Enter the phone number of the person making the request (ID: 5).
6. Enter the email of the person making the request (ID: 6).
7. Write a brief description of your request (ID: 7).
8. Explain the reason for your request (ID: 8).
9. Fill out the address of the person making the request (ID: 9).
10. Enter the city of the person making the request (ID: 10).
11. Enter the state of the person making the request (ID: 11).
12. Enter the zip code of the person making the request (ID: 12).

Please ensure that you complete all required fields to ensure that your request is properly processed.

## rti_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RTI Questionnaire is a form used to collect information from requesters about their requests for resources or services.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the section above.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **rti_requester** (ID: 1, Select One, Optional): Select "Yes" if you are a requester, "No" otherwise.
* **request_date** (ID: 2, Date, Optional): Enter the date you are requesting access to resources or services.
* **requestor** (ID: 4, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person making the request.
* **requestor_phone** (ID: 5, Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the person making the request.
* **requestor_email** (ID: 6, Email, Optional): Enter the email of the person making the request.
* **rti_request** (ID: 7, Text, Optional): Write a brief description of your request.
* **reason_for_request** (ID: 8, Text, Optional): Explain the reason for your request.
* **requestor_address** (ID: 9, Text, Optional): Enter the address of the person making the request.
* **requestor_city** (ID: 10, Text, Optional): Enter the city of the person making the request.
* **requestor_state** (ID: 11, Text, Optional): Enter the state of the person making the request.
* **requestor_zip** (ID: 12, Text, Optional): Enter the zip code of the person making the request.
