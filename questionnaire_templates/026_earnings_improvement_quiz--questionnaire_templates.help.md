# Earnings Improvement Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for users to evaluate their current financial situation and identify potential areas for improvement in their earnings. By answering a series of questions, users can gain a better understanding of their income, goals, and challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each section as thoroughly and honestly as possible. This form is not meant to be a time-consuming or stressful process; rather, it is a tool to help you achieve a better understanding of your current financial situation.
2. For fields with options, select the answer that best describes your current financial situation or preferences.
3. For text fields, provide as much detail as possible in a concise and coherent manner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**:
	+ Label: Introduction
	+ Description: This section is meant to be a brief introduction to the form and its purpose. Please read through this section to understand the context and goals of the form.
* **what_is_your_current_income**: 
	+ Label: What is your current income?
	+ Description: Enter your current annual income.
* **what_is_your_income_goal**:
	+ Label: What is your income goal?
	+ Description: Enter the amount you would like to earn.
* **what_is_your_current_income_period**:
	+ Label: What is your current income period?
	+ Description: Select the frequency of your current income (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
* **is_your_current_income_affected**:
	+ Label: Is your current income affected?
	+ Description: Indicate whether your income has been affected by any recent changes (True/False).
* **what_is_causing_your_current_income_affected**:
	+ Label: What is causing your current income affected?
	+ Description: If your income is affected, describe the reasons for this change.
* **how_much_would_you_like_to_improve_your_income**:
	+ Label: How much would you like to improve your income?
	+ Description: Enter the desired amount of income improvement.
* **what_is_holding_your_back_from_reaching_your_income_goal**:
	+ Label: What is holding you back from reaching your income goal?
	+ Description: Select all relevant reasons that are currently limiting your income growth (Financial constraints, Limited education/training, Career opportunities, Health/health issues, Personal issues, None of the above).
* **what_is_your_current_level_of_income_readiness**:
	+ Label: What is your current level of income readiness?
	+ Description: Select your current level of readiness to increase income (Very low, Low, Medium, High, Very high).
* **what_steps_would_you_take_to_increases_income**:
	+ Label: What steps would you take to increase income?
	+ Description: Select all relevant steps you would take to increase income (Research and planning, Seeking advice/counseling, Taking online courses/training, Seeking help from professionals, Other).
* **what_is_your_current_education_background**:
	+ Label: What is your current education background?
	+ Description: Select your current education level (High school, Vocational training, Bachelor's degree, Master's degree, Post-graduate degree, Other).
* **do_you_have_experience_with_our_coaching_services**:
	+ Label: Do you have experience with our coaching services?
	+ Description: Indicate if you have prior experience with the coaching services offered (True/False).
* **what_is_your_current_income_range**:
	+ Label: What is your current income range?
	+ Description: Select your current annual income range (Less than $10,000, $10,000-$20,000, $20,000-$30,000, etc.).
* **do_you_have_a_business_background**:
	+ Label: Do you have a business background?
	+ Description: Indicate if you have experience in business (True/False).
* **what_would_you_like_to_achieve**:
	+ Label: What would you like to achieve?
	+ Description: Enter your desired income or career goals.
* **do_you_agree_to_be_contacted**:
	+ Label: Do you agree to be contacted?
	+ Description: Indicate if you are willing to be contacted for further discussions or guidance (True/False).
* **contact_info**:
	+ Label: Contact Info
	+ Description: Enter your contact information (email, phone number, etc.).
* **follow_up**:
	+ Label: Follow-up
	+ Description: Indicate if you would like to be contacted for follow-up or guidance (True/False).
* **follow_up_info**:
	+ Label: Follow-up info
	+ Description: Enter any additional information for follow-up (e.g., specific dates or times).
* **what_is_your_current_job_status**:
	+ Label: What is your current job status?
	+ Description: Select your current employment status (Working full-time, Working part-time, Unemployed, Retired, Student, Other).
* **do_you_have_a_partners**:
	+ Label: Do you have a partner?
	+ Description: Indicate if you are in a partnership (True/False).

## Tips
- Answer each question with as much detail as possible.
- If you need to provide additional information, use the text fields.
- Review your answers for accuracy and consistency throughout the form.
- This form is not a substitute for professional financial advice, but rather a tool to help you understand your financial situation better.
