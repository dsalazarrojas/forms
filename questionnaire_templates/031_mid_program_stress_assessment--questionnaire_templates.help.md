# mid-program-stress-assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mid-program-stress-assessment form is used to gather feedback and data from participants in a program or project, specifically to assess their stress levels, support needs, and preferences for communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form sections and questions carefully.
2.  Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
3.  Use the provided options to select the correct response.
4.  If you have any additional comments or feedback, use the "Additional comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Feedback (participant_feedback)**: A text field for general participant feedback.
    - This section is where you can provide any general comments or feedback about your experience in the program or project.
- **Stress Trends (stress_trends)**: A text field to identify any patterns or changes in your stress levels.
    - This section is used to track any changes or trends in your stress levels.
- **Stress Level (stress_level)**: A numeric field to measure your current stress level.
    - This field is used to measure your current stress level, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
- **Support Needs (support_needs)**: A single-select field to determine if you feel you need support.
    - This section is used to determine if you feel you need support, where 'Yes' indicates you need support and 'No' indicates you don't need support.
- **Support Channel (support_channel)**: A multiple-select field to determine your preferred method of communication.
    - This section is used to determine your preferred method of communication, where you can select one or more channels (phone, email, text, chat).
- **Stressors (stressors)**: A text field to identify factors that contribute to your stress.
    - This section is used to identify any factors that contribute to your stress levels.
- **Suggestions**: A text field to gather suggestions from you.
    - This section is used to gather any suggestions or ideas you may have to improve the program or project.
- **Additional Comments (additional_comments)**: A text field for any additional comments or feedback.
    - This section is used to gather any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Tips
*   Be honest and thorough in your answers.
*   Use the provided options carefully, especially in the "Support Channel" section.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to the program team.
