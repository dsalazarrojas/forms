# deka_lash_favorite_things - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect users' preferences on a variety of topics, including food, color, animal, place, activity, music, book, movie, and game.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose your favorite food from the list provided.
2. Select your favorite color (text field).
3. Choose your favorite animal.
4. Select your favorite place.
5. Choose your favorite food (second question).
6. Select your favorite activity.
7. Select your favorite hobby.
8. Choose your favorite music genre.
9. Enter your favorite book title.
10. Choose your favorite movie.
11. Select your favorite book (second question).
12. Select your favorite TV show.
13. Choose your favorite game.
