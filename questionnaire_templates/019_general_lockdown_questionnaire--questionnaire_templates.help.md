# General Lockdown Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to collect data on students' experiences during a lockdown. It is intended to be completed by students, who will report on their feelings, reactions, and experiences with support systems, communication, and safety measures during the lockdown.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the prompts and answer each question to the best of your ability. You may select one or multiple answers depending on the type of question. If you need help or clarification on any question, please ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Page 1, id: 1): This is a text field where you can briefly describe your experiences during the lockdown.
* **student_id** (Page 1, id: 2): Enter your student ID number (if applicable).
* **student_name** (Page 1, id: 3): Enter your name.
* **school_id** (Page 1, id: 4): Enter your school ID number (if applicable).
* **Lockdown Type** (Page 1, id: 5): Select one of the options: Lockdown at school, Lockdown in the neighborhood, or Lockdown at home.
* **Reaction to Lockdown** (Page 1, id: 6): Select one of the options: Very anxious, Somewhat anxious, Not anxious, Don't know, or Other.
* **Reasons for Reaction** (Page 2, id: 7): Select all that apply from: The unknown, The noise, The confinement, The darkness, or Other.
* **Support System** (Page 2, id: 8): Select True if you had a support system during the lockdown, False otherwise.
* **Communication** (Page 2, id: 9): Select True if you could contact your family or friends during the lockdown, False otherwise.
* **Physical Safety** (Page 3, id: 10): Select True if you were physically safe during the lockdown, False otherwise.
* **Emotional Safety** (Page 3, id: 11): Select True if you were emotionally safe during the lockdown, False otherwise.
* **Psychological Safety** (Page 3, id: 12): Select True if you were psychologically safe during the lockdown, False otherwise.
* **Post-Lockdown Safety** (Page 3, id: 13): Select True if you are safe after the lockdown, False otherwise.
* **Student Response** (Page 4, id: 14): Select one of the options: Very positive, Somewhat positive, Neutral, Somewhat negative, or Very negative.
* **Teacher Response** (Page 4, id: 15): Select one of the options: Very positive, Somewhat positive, Neutral, Somewhat negative, or Very negative.
* **Student Support** (Page 4, id: 16): Select True if you felt supported by your classmates, False otherwise.
* **Teacher Support** (Page 5, id: 17): Select True if you felt supported by your teacher, False otherwise.
* **Parent Response** (Page 5, id: 18): Select one of the options: Very positive, Somewhat positive, Neutral, Somewhat negative, or Very negative.
* **Other** (Page 5, id: 19): Share any additional comments or thoughts about your experience during the lockdown.
* **Other Details** (Page 5, id: 20): Share any additional details about your experience during the lockdown.
* **Additional Comments** (Page 6, id: 21): Share any additional comments or thoughts about your experience during the lockdown.
* **Follow-up Questions** (Page 6, id: 22): Share any follow-up questions you have about the lockdown.
* **School Response** (Page 6, id: 23): Share how the school responded to the lockdown.
* **Emergency Contact** (Page 7, id: 24): Share who your emergency contact is.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (Page 7, id: 25): Share their phone number.
