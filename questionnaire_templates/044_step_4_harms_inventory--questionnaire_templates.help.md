# step_4_harms_inventory - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **step_4_harms_inventory** form is a questionnaire used to collect data on harms or negative effects experienced during the fourth step of a process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure it is relevant to your process.
2. For each field, provide the requested information as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### fourth_step (Note)
* **fourth_step** (`fourth_step`, `note`, required: false): This is a placeholder page to indicate that this is the fourth step of the process.

### moral_inventory (Number)
* **moral_inventory** (`moral_inventory`, `number`, required: false): Enter a number to indicate the severity of the moral harms experienced during this step of the process.

### harms (Multiple Select)
* **harms** (`harms`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all options that apply to indicate the types of harms experienced during this step of the process. Options include:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### date_of_occurrence (Date)
* **date_of_occurrence** (`date_of_occurrence`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date when the harm occurred.

### time_of_occurrence (Time)
* **time_of_occurrence** (`time_of_occurrence`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time when the harm occurred.

### location (Text)
* **location** (`location`, `text`, required: false): Describe the location where the harm occurred.

### description (Text)
* **description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the harm that occurred.

### actions_taken (Text)
* **actions_taken** (`actions_taken`, `text`, required: false): Describe the actions taken in response to the incident.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification or support.
* Remember to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure you have completed it correctly.
