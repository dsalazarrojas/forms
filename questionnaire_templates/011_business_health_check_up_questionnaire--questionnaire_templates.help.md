# Business Health Check Up Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Health Check Up Questionnaire is a form designed to assess the overall health of your business, helping you identify areas that require improvement and track your progress over time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your company's name in the "Company name" field.
2. Select the date you completed this assessment in the "Assessment date" field.
3. Evaluate your leadership's performance by selecting from the options: Poor, Fair, Good, or Excellent.
4. Rate your business's financial health by selecting from the options: Poor, Fair, Good, or Strong.
5. Determine your business's market position by selecting from the options: Weak, Moderate, or Strong.
6. Assess your workforce's capability by selecting from the options: Needs improvement, Adequate, or High performing.
7. If you'd like, provide up to three key performance indicators (KPIs) you're currently tracking in the "Key metrics" field.
8. Propose any improvement actions you'd like to take to boost your business's health in the "Improvement actions" field.
9. If you think further review is needed, select 'True' in the "Follow up required" field; otherwise, select 'False'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Company name (Company name, text, required: true)
Enter your company's legal or trading name.

### Assessment date (Assessment date, date, required: true)
Select the date you completed this assessment.

### Leadership effectiveness (Leadership effectiveness, select_one, required: true)
Rate your leadership's performance from Poor to Excellent.

### Financial health (Financial health, select_one, required: true)
Rate your business's financial health from Poor to Strong.

### Market position (Market position, select_one, required: true)
Determine your business's market position from Weak to Strong.

### Talent and skills (Talent and skills, select_one, required: true)
Assess your workforce's capability from Needs improvement to High performing.

### Key metrics (Key metrics, text, required: false)
Optional: provide up to three key performance indicators (KPIs) you're currently tracking.

### Improvement actions (Improvement actions, text, required: false)
Propose any actions you'd like to take to boost your business's health.

### Follow up required (Follow up required, select_one, required: false)
If you think further review is needed, select True; otherwise, select False.

## Tips
* Use this form regularly to track changes and progress over time.
* Review and update your answers to reflect any changes in your business's health.
* Use the information gathered from this form to make informed decisions and drive improvement efforts.
