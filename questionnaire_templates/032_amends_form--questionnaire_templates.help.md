# Amends Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Amends Form is a crucial tool in the recovery process, helping individuals work through their past mistakes and wrongdoings. It's designed to guide you in reflecting on your actions, identifying areas where you've caused harm, and preparing for making amends with those affected. Completing this form will help you take responsibility for your actions, understand the impact on others, and ultimately, make amends and grow as a person.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Amends Form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer the questions honestly, reflecting on your past actions and how they affected others.
2.  Provide specific details about the harm you caused, including the type of harm and when it occurred.
3.  Describe your plan for making amends, including the type of amends you plan to make and how you plan to implement them.
4.  Be prepared to discuss your feelings and thoughts about the amends process, including how you've grown as a person.
