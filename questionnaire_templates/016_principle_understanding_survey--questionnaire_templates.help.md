# principle_understanding_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to evaluate an individual's understanding and application of various principles in a professional context. It is designed to gauge awareness, skills, and actions related to these principles. The form is likely used for leadership development, performance assessment, or training purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for each question, depending on your level of understanding, application, or awareness.
3. You may select only one option for "select_one" type questions (e.g., 2, 4, 8, 10, 14).
4. You may select multiple options for "select_multiple" type questions (e.g., 3, 5, 7, 9, 11-13, 15-25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **team_awareness** (id: 2): Indicate whether you are aware of the principles in question.
* **training_needs** (id: 3): Identify areas where you need training or further guidance on applying principles.
* **manager_feedback** (id: 4): Reflect on feedback you receive from your manager on principle application.
* **action_items** (id: 5): List actions you take or intend to take to apply principles in your work.
* **principle_knowledge** (id: 6): Indicate areas where you have principle knowledge or experience.
* **principle_application** (id: 7): Describe how you apply principles in your work or role.
* **manager_leadership** (id: 8): Reflect on your manager's support or guidance on principle application.
* **principle_impact** (id: 9): Assess how your work impacts the principles or standards being applied.
* **manager_action** (id: 10): Describe actions your manager takes or supports to facilitate principle application.
* **principle_further_action_1-15** (id: 11-25): Identify further actions you intend to take to apply principles in your work or role.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
* Ensure you understand the question's intent before selecting an option.
* If unsure about a question, use your best judgment and consult with your manager or colleague if needed.
* This form is designed to promote self-assessment and personal development; it is not a performance evaluation tool.
