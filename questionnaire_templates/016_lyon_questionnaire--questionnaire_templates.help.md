# Lyon Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lyon Questionnaire is a form designed to collect feedback and ratings from users who have had a personal interaction with the service. This form is intended to gather contact information, impressions, and feedback from users to help improve the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please make sure you have had a recent interaction with the service before filling out this form.
* Fill out the form in the following order:
	+ Introduction: Please read the introduction and make sure you understand the form's purpose.
	+ Contact Details: Enter your contact information, including your email address and any other contact details you wish to provide.
	+ Your Impressions: Share your honest impressions of the service you interacted with.
	+ How would you rate: Select all the ratings that apply to your experience with the service.
	+ Feedback: Share any additional feedback you may have about the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is a note field where you can read the form's purpose and make sure you understand what it's asking for.
* **Contact Details**: This is a field to provide your contact information, such as your email address, phone number, or other contact details you wish to provide. (Required: False)
* **Your Impressions**: This is a text field where you can share your honest impressions of the service you interacted with. (Required: False)
* **How would you rate**: This is a select multiple field where you can select all the ratings that apply to your experience with the service. You can choose from the following options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
	+ Option 5
* **Feedback**: This is a text field where you can share any additional feedback you may have about the service. (Required: False)

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide your real contact information to help us improve the service.
* If you have any further comments or suggestions, please include them in the Feedback field.
* If you have already submitted feedback or ratings for the same service in the past, please do not fill out this form again.
