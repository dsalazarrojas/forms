# Childhood Celebration Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your experiences and memories of childhood celebrations. It will help us understand what types of events were special and meaningful to you during your childhood, and how they contributed to your social, emotional, and cultural development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer the questions that apply to your experiences.
2. Be as detailed and specific as possible when describing your answers.
3. Choose the options that best match your memories and experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was your favorite childhood celebration or event?** (`favorite_childhood_event`, text, **required**: true): This question asks about a specific event or celebration that stands out in your memory from your childhood. Think about a celebration that was particularly joyful, exciting, or meaningful to you.
* **Approximately what age were you during this event?** (`age_of_memory`, number, **optional**: false): This question helps us gauge your age during the celebration. Try to remember or estimate your age as accurately as possible.
* **What made this celebration particularly special or memorable?** (`what_made_it_special`, text, **required**: true): Reflect on what made the celebration stand out from others. Was it the decorations, music, or people involved? Describe what made it special.
* **Which activities do you associate most with childhood celebrations?** (`typical_activities`, select_multiple, **required**: true): Think about the types of activities you commonly found yourself engaging in during celebrations. Choose all that apply from the options provided.
* **How often did your family host or attend these types of celebrations?** (`celebration_frequency`, select_one, **required**: true): Consider how frequently your family or community hosted or participated in similar celebrations. Choose from the available options.
* **Who was typically the main organizer of these events?** (`organizer_role`, select_one, **optional**: false): Reflect on who usually took charge of planning and executing these celebrations. Choose from the options provided.
* **Describe any specific gift-giving traditions from your childhood** (`gift_traditions`, text, **optional**: false): If you remember any special gift-giving practices or customs, share them with us.
* **Where were these celebrations usually held?** (`venue_preference`, select_multiple, **optional**: false): Think about the typical locations where these celebrations took place. Choose from the options provided.
* **What ideas from your childhood would you like to see in modern celebrations?** (`modern_celebration_ideas`, text, **optional**: false): If you have any thoughts or ideas that you think would be valuable in modern celebrations, share them with us.
* **Name - Optional** (`respondent_name`, text, **optional**: false): If you'd like to share your name, you can do so here. This is optional and for your personal record only.
* **Date** (`survey_date`, date, **required**: true): This is the date you're completing the survey. Please ensure it's accurate.

Note: This survey is for personal, non-commercial use. Please answer honestly and accurately to help us better understand childhood celebrations.
