# Long COVID Patient Intake Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients about their experience with Long COVID, a condition characterized by persistent symptoms for an extended period after initial COVID-19 infection. The purpose of this form is to gather detailed patient information for medical professionals to better understand and manage this condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please ensure you have access to a computer or mobile device to fill out the form.
2. Answer each question thoroughly and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please feel free to ask for clarification.
4. Submit the completed form to the relevant medical professionals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, optional): This section is intended to collect basic patient information, such as name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Here, you will be asked about your previous medical history, including any previous illnesses, allergies, or medical conditions.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, optional): Describe your current symptoms, including duration, severity, and any factors that affect them.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): Report any allergies or sensitivities you have.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Share any pre-existing medical conditions you may have.
* **Social History** (`social_history`, text, optional): Provide information about your social and family history, including any significant events or relationships.
* **Patient Reporting** (`patient_reporting`, text, optional): Share your personal experiences and perceptions about your Long COVID condition.
* **Long COVID History** (`long_covid_history`, text, optional): Describe your experience with Long COVID, including duration, symptoms, and any factors that affect your condition.
* **Patient Contact Info** (`patient_contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, including phone number, email, or other relevant contact details.

## Tips
- Please answer each question accurately and honestly.
- If you have any difficulty accessing or filling out this form, please contact the relevant medical professionals.
- Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
