# explore_your_family_heritage - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "explore_your_family_heritage" form is designed to gather information about a person's family history, cultural background, and personal experiences. It's meant to be a comprehensive survey that helps users share their family's past, present, and future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Provide as much detail as you can about each section.
3. Use the hints provided to guide you through the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Family history** (`family_history`, `text`, required: false): This section is where you can share your personal account of your family's history, including any significant events, migrations, or changes that have shaped your family's story.
* **Family tree** (`family_tree`, `text`, required: false): Here, you can describe your family's genealogical structure, including the relationships between family members and any notable ancestors.
* **Family memories** (`family_memories`, `text`, required: false): Share any cherished memories or stories about your family that are worth passing down to future generations.
* **Family values** (`family_values`, `text`, required: false): Describe the values that are most important to your family, such as cultural traditions, social norms, or personal ethics.
* **Family story** (`family_story`, `text`, required: false): Write a brief narrative about your family's story, including significant events, struggles, or triumphs.
* **Family culture** (`family_culture`, `text`, required: false): Share your family's cultural background, including customs, practices, or traditions that are unique to your heritage.
* **Family rituals** (`family_rituals`, `text`, required: false): Describe any regular or special activities that are performed by your family, such as rituals, ceremonies, or celebrations.
* **Family traditions** (`family_traditions`, `text`, required: false): List any practices or customs that have been passed down through your family, such as recipes, stories, or songs.

## Tips

* Be as detailed and honest as possible when answering each question.
* If you need help or clarification, refer to the hints provided.
* Remember to save your progress and come back to the form at any time to continue working on it.
