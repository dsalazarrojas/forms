# retail_supplier_interview_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The retail_supplier_interview_form is a questionnaire used to collect information from retail suppliers. This form is intended for use with new retail suppliers or when updating information for an existing supplier.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the supplier's information in the "retail_supplier_info" field.
2. Provide the company's name in the "company_name" field.
3. Enter the supplier's email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter the phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Enter the website URL in the "website" field.
6. Enter the supplier's address in the "address" field.
7. Enter the contact person's name in the "contact_person" field.
8. Enter the job title of the contact person in the "job_title" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **retail_supplier_info** (required): This field is where you can enter any information about the supplier that doesn't fit into the other fields, such as their description or a brief overview.
* **company_name** (required): This field should contain the name of the retail supplier company.
* **email** (required): Enter the email address of the supplier's contact person.
* **phone** (required): Enter the phone number of the supplier.
* **website** (optional): This field is for entering the supplier's website URL.
* **address** (optional): Enter the supplier's business address.
* **contact_person** (optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **job_title** (optional): Enter the job title of the contact person.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
- Double-check that all information is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer back to the supplier's documentation or contact them for clarification.
