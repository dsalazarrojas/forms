# Information Gathering Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their audience insights, satisfaction feedback, contact details, email, and phone number.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the audience insights that best describe your current situation.
2. Choose your level of satisfaction with the given options.
3. If applicable, provide your contact details (email and phone number).
4. Review and submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Audience Insights (optional)** (`audience_insights`, note, required: false): This field is used to gather information about your current audience. Please select the insights that best describe your situation, such as "Very Dissatisfied", "Somewhat Dissatisfied", "Neutral", "Somewhat Satisfied", or "Very Satisfied".
* **Satisfaction Feedback (optional)** (`satisfaction_feedback`, select_multiple, required: false): In this field, you can select your level of satisfaction with the given options.
* **Contact Details (optional)** (`contact_details`, email, required: false): This field is used to provide your contact details. If you have an email address and/or phone number, please enter them in the respective fields.
* **Email (optional)** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address in this field.
* **Phone (optional)** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number in this field.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out each field accurately and honestly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
* You can submit the form once you have completed all the fields.
