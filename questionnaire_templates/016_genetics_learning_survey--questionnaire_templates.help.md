# Genetics Learning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please read through the following steps:

1. Choose the relevant options for each field based on your experience or understanding of the course.
2. Provide accurate and clear information for each field.
3. Fill in the required and optional fields as indicated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Feedback** (`user_feedback`): Select whether you found the course helpful or not.
  * Choose between 'Yes' and 'No' to indicate whether you found the course helpful or not.
* **Genetics Knowledge Level** (`genetic_knowledge_level`): Indicate your current knowledge level in genetics.
  * Enter a number to represent your current knowledge level.
* **Teaching Methods** (`teaching_methods`): Select your preferred teaching methods.
  * Choose one option from 'Option 1', 'Option 2', or 'Option 3' based on your preference.
* **Improvement Areas** (`improvement_areas`): Identify areas where the course can be improved.
  * Select multiple options from 'Yes' or 'No' based on your experience.
* **Learning Objectives** (`learning_objectives`): Select your primary learning objectives.
  * Choose one option from 'Option 1', 'Option 2', or 'Option 3' based on your learning goals.
* **Teaching Practices** (`teaching_practices`): Select your preferred teaching practices.
  * Choose one option from 'Option 1', 'Option 2', or 'Option 3' based on your preference.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
  * Use this text field to write any comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Demographic Details** (`demographic_details`): Provide your demographic details.
  * Select multiple options from 'Yes' or 'No' based on your demographic information.
* **Knowledge Levels** (`knowledge_levels`): Indicate your current knowledge level.
  * Enter a number to represent your current knowledge level.
* **Survey Completed** (`survey_completed`): Indicate the date and time of completion.
  * Enter the date and time you completed the survey.
* **Completion Time** (`completion_time`): Record the time taken to complete the survey.
  * Enter the time taken to complete the survey.
* **User ID** (`user_id`): Enter a unique identifier.
  * Enter a number to represent your user ID.
* **User Email** (`user_email`): Enter your contact email address.
  * Enter your email address.
* **User Phone** (`user_phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
  * Enter your phone number.
* **User Name** (`user_name`): Enter your name.
  * Enter your full name.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`): Enter the program name.
  * Enter the name of the program you are associated with.
* **Program Leader Name** (`program_leader_name`): Enter the name of the program leader.
  * Enter the name of the program leader.
* **Program Leader Email** (`program_leader_email`): Enter the contact email of the program leader.
  * Enter the email of the program leader.
* **Department** (`department`): Enter the department you are associated with.
  * Enter the name of the department.
* **Institution** (`institution`): Enter the institution name.
  * Enter the name of the institution.
* **Location** (`location`): Enter the location.
  * Enter the location where you completed the course.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`): Enter the name of the course.
  * Enter the name of the course.
* **Course Code** (`course_code`): Enter the course code.
  * Enter the code of the course.
* **Course Year** (`course_year`): Enter the year of the course.
  * Enter the year you completed the course.
* **Submission** (`submission`): Select whether you submitted the course work.
  * Choose between 'Yes' and 'No' to indicate whether you submitted the course work.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): Enter the date of submission.
  * Enter the date you submitted the course work.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`): Record the time of submission.
  * Enter the time you submitted the course work.

Note: Please refer to the fields that have a type of `select_multiple` or `select_one` for further instructions on selecting multiple or a single option.
