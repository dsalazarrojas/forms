# sleep_consultation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your sleep habits to help healthcare professionals understand your sleep patterns and provide personalized recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Provide honest information about your sleep habits.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your healthcare professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sleep Quality**: Rate how well you sleep at night.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: false
* **Sleep Duration**: How many hours do you sleep at night?
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: false
* **Sleep Schedule**: How many hours of sleep do you usually get?
	+ Options: 6-7 hours, 7-8 hours, 8-9 hours, 10 hours or more
	+ Required: false
* **Sleep Latency**: How long does it take you to fall asleep?
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: false
* **Sleep Wake-Up Time**: What time do you wake up?
	+ Type: time
	+ Required: false
* **Sleep Onset Insomnia**: How often do you experience difficulty falling asleep?
	+ Options: None, Rarely, Occasionally, Often, Almost Every Night
	+ Required: false
* **Snoring**: Do you snore while sleeping?
	+ Options: None, True, False
	+ Required: false
* **Sleep Disorder**: Do you have any sleep disorders?
	+ Options: None, Sleep Apnea, Insomnia, Narcolepsy, Other
	+ Required: false
* **Sleep Treatment**: What treatment do you currently use for sleep issues?
	+ Options: Medication, Lifestyle Changes, Combination
	+ Required: false
* **Sleep Recommendation**: Is your healthcare professional recommended any sleep treatment?
	+ Options: None, True, False
	+ Required: false
* **Comments**: Any additional comments about your sleep habits?
	+ Type: note
	+ Required: false
* **Sleep Consultation Form Comments**: Any comments about your sleep consultation form experience?
	+ Type: note
	+ Required: false
* **Sleep Consultation Form Recommendation**: Any recommendations for your sleep consultation form experience?
	+ Type: note
	+ Required: false
