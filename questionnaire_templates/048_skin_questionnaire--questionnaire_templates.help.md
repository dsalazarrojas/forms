# Skin Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The skin questionnaire is designed to collect information about a user's skin type and condition in order to suggest suitable skin care products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the **What is your skin condition?** field with a detailed description of your skin condition.
2.  Select your skin type from the options: **Sensitive**, **Dry**, **Oily**, or **Combination**.
3.  Choose your skin color from the options: **Fair**, **Medium**, **Dark**, or **Tanned**.
4.  Check the boxes for any skin proteins you have: **Sebum**, **Dandruff**, or **Acne**.
5.  Enter your skin pigmentation level between 0 and 100.
6.  Enter your skin sensitivity level between 0 and 100.
7.  Enter your skin pore size between 0 and 100.
8.  Check any skin venilation issues you have: **Redness**, **Puffiness**, **Dullness**, or **Discoloration**.
9.  Click **Submit** to submit your questionnaire.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   *skin_condition*: **What is your skin condition?** (Text, Optional)
    *   This field is for users to describe their skin condition in detail.
*   *skin_type*: **What is your skin type?** (Select One, Required)
    *   Choose your skin type from the options: Sensitive, Dry, Oily, or Combination.
*   *skin_color*: **What is your skin color?** (Select One, Required)
    *   Choose your skin color from the options: Fair, Medium, Dark, or Tanned.
*   *skin_protein*: **Do you have any skin proteins?** (Checkbox, Required)
    *   Check the boxes for any skin proteins you have: Sebum, Dandruff, or Acne.
*   *skin_pigmentation*: **What is your skin pigmentation?** (Number, Required)
    *   Enter your skin pigmentation level between 0 and 100.
*   *skin_sensitivity*: **How sensitive is your skin?** (Number, Required)
    *   Enter your skin sensitivity level between 0 and 100.
*   *skin_pore_size*: **What is your skin pore size?** (Number, Required)
    *   Enter your skin pore size between 0 and 100.
*   *skin_venilation*: **Do you have any skin venilation?** (Checkbox, Required)
    *   Check any skin venilation issues you have: Redness, Puffiness, Dullness, or Discoloration.
*   *submit*: **Submit** (Text, Required)
    *   Click to submit your questionnaire.
