# Sociology Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sociology Questionnaire is a set of questions designed to gather demographic and research-related information from respondents. This information will be used to better understand various aspects of sociology and research.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Review the provided questions carefully.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. Provide accurate and honest responses.
4. Ensure that all required fields are filled in (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sociolgy Questionnaire Form** (`sociolgy_questionnaire_form`, text, required/optional): This is the main form section where you will provide your demographic and research information.
* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, note, required/optional): This is an optional field where you can provide any additional information about yourself.
* **Demographic Information** (`demographic_information`, select_one, required/optional): Please select your demographic information (Male, Female, Other).
* **Age** (`Age`, number, required/optional): Enter your age.
* **Income** (`Income`, number, required/optional): Enter your income.
* **Occupation** (`Occupation`, select_one, required/optional): Please select your occupation (Student, Working, Retired).
* **Education Level** (`Education Level`, select_one, required/optional): Please select your education level (High School, Bachelor's Degree, Master's Degree).
* **Family Status** (`Family Status`, select_one, required/optional): Please select your family status (Single, Married, Divorced).
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please select how you would like to be contacted (Yes, No).
* **Contact Preference** (`Contact Preference`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please select your contact preference (Phone, Email, In-person).
* **Time Preference** (`Time Preference`, time, required/optional): Enter your time preference.
* **Date Preference** (`Date Preference`, date, required/optional): Enter your date preference.
* **Social Network** (`Social Network`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please select your social network (Yes, No).
* **Social Media** (`Social Media`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please select your social media usage (Yes, No).
* **Research Interests** (`Research Interests`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please select your research interests (Yes, No).
* **Academic Focus** (`Academic Focus`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please select your academic focus (Yes, No).

* **Demographic Comments** (`Demographic Comments`, text, required/optional): Provide any demographic comments.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional comments.
* **Research Question** (`Research Question`, text, required/optional): Enter your research question.
* **Research Area** (`Research Area`, text, required/optional): Enter your research area.
