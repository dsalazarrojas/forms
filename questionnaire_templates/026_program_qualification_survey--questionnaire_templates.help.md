# Program Qualification Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Program Qualification Survey is designed to collect information from applicants to assess their eligibility and suitability for a specific program. This form helps us to understand the applicant's background, qualifications, and readiness for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Enter your correct program name in the "Program Name" field.
3. Enter your name in the "Applicant Name" field.
4. Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field, in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX.
6. Briefly describe your program readiness in the "Program Readiness" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Name**: Enter the name of the program you are applying for.
* **Applicant Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your passport or government-issued ID.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX.
* **Program Readiness**: Describe your readiness for the program, including any relevant experience, skills, or qualifications you possess.
