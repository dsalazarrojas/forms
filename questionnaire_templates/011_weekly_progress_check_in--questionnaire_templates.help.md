# Weekly Progress Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Weekly Progress Check In" form is designed to collect information about learner's progress, participation, and goals. This form is used to track the progress of learners in our platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Learner Progress" field with your current progress status (Yes/No).
2. Enter any additional details about your participation in the "Participation" field.
3. Describe your current goals in the "Goals" field.
4. Provide any feedback or comments in the "Feedback" field.
5. Enter a participation rate (e.g., percentage) in the "Participation Rate" field.
6. Enter any comments about your participation in the "Participation Comments" field.
7. Enter any comments about your goals in the "Goals Comments" field.
8. Enter any feedback or comments in the "Feedback Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Learner Progress** (Yes/No): Select Yes if you have made progress, No if you haven't.
* **Participation**: Enter any additional details about your participation in this week's activities.
* **Goals**: Describe your current goals and what you aim to achieve.
* **Feedback**: Provide any feedback or comments about your progress or any suggestions for improvement.
* **Participation Rate**: Enter your participation rate as a percentage.
* **Participation Comments**: Enter any comments about your participation.
* **Goals Comments**: Enter any comments about your goals.
* **Feedback Comments**: Enter any feedback or comments about your feedback.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Use clear and concise language when filling in the form.
- Review your progress regularly to stay on track.
- If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us.
