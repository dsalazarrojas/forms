# Enterprise Financinq Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Enterprise Financinq Questionnaire is designed to help us understand your financial situation and preferences for a loan or investment proposal. Please answer the questions accurately and completely to help us assess your eligibility and suitability for our financial products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below and fill out each section carefully. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Name**: This is your company's name, which should be the name of the person submitting the questionnaire.
    - Type: text
    - Required: No
    - Hint: This is your company name
- **Company Name**: This is the name of your company, which should match your business registration name.
    - Type: text
    - Required: No
    - Hint: This should be your company's registered name
- **Company Type**: Please select the type of company you represent from the options below.
    - Type: select_one
    - Required: Yes
    - Hint: Select your company type
    - Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
- **Company Size**: Please select all that apply for the size of your company.
    - Type: select_multiple
    - Required: No
    - Hint: Select all that apply
    - Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
- **Financial Institution**: Please select the financial institution that you are associated with from the options below.
    - Type: select_one
    - Required: No
    - Hint: Select your financial institution
    - Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
- **Loan Officer**: Please select the loan officer that you are working with from the options below.
    - Type: select_one
    - Required: No
    - Hint: Select your loan officer
    - Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
- **Loan Amount**: Please enter the amount of the loan you are requesting.
    - Type: number
    - Required: No
    - Hint: Enter the loan amount
- **Loan Term**: Please select the term of the loan you are requesting (e.g., month, year).
    - Type: time
    - Required: No
    - Hint: Enter the loan term
- **Loan Interest**: Please enter the interest rate of the loan.
    - Type: number
    - Required: No
    - Hint: Enter the loan interest rate

## Tips
- Please answer the questions accurately and completely to ensure a smooth assessment process.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for clarification.
- This form is designed to be filled out once, so please only submit the form once to avoid duplication of effort.
