# Network Communication Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Network Communication Assessment Form is designed to help teams and organizations evaluate their communication practices, collaboration frequency, and communication channels.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review each page carefully and answer each question or select an option that best describes your team's or organization's practices.
2.  Provide additional comments or feedback where necessary.
3.  Submit the form once you have completed all the sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Network Communication Practices** (`network_communication_practices`, `note`, required: false): This is a free-form text area for you to describe your team's or organization's current network communication practices.
* **Team Communication** (`team_communication`, `note`, required: false): This is a free-form text area for you to describe your team's or organization's communication dynamics.
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This option allows you to select the communication channels your team or organization uses, such as Internet, Mobile, Email, Phone, or In-person.
* **Collaboration Frequency** (`collaboration_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): This option allows you to select how often your team collaborates, such as Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Rarely.
* **Data Collection** (`data_collection`, `note`, required: false): This is a free-form text area for you to provide any relevant information about data collection practices.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `note`, required: false): This is a free-form text area for you to provide feedback about the current state of network communication in your team or organization.
* **Collaboration Effectiveness** (`collaboration_effectiveness`, `number`, required: false): This option allows you to rate the effectiveness of collaboration in your team or organization.
* **Form Submit** (`form_submit`, `select_one`, required: false): This option allows you to confirm that you have submitted the form.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): This is a free-form text area for you to provide any comments about the form or your experience completing it.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, required: false): This is a free-form text area for you to provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required: false): This field is for the date you submit the form.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, required: false): This field is for the time you submit the form.
* **Submission Time Zone** (`submission_time_zone`, `select_one`, required: false): This option allows you to select the time zone where you submit the form.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): This field is for your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): This field is for your phone number.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required: false): This field is for the department you belong to.
* **Team Member** (`team_member`, `text`, required: false): This field is for the team member you are filling out the form as.
* **Team Leader** (`team_leader`, `text`, required: false): This field is for the team leader you report to.
* **Role** (`role`, `text`, required: false): This field is for your role in the team or organization.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): This field is for your job title.
* **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`, `text`, required: false): This field is for the manager you report to.
* **Reporting Team** (`reporting_team`, `text`, required: false): This field is for the team you report to.
* **Collaboration Tools** (`collaboration_tools`, `text`, required: false): This field is for any collaboration tools your team or organization uses.
* **Collaboration Frequency Other** (`collaboration_frequency_other`, `text`, required: false): This field is for any other collaboration frequency not listed in the previous options.
* **Submission Confirmation** (`submission_confirmation`, `select_one`, required: false): This option allows you to confirm that you have submitted the form.
