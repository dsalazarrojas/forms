# Employee Happiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your overall happiness and well-being as an employee. Your feedback will help the organization understand what's working well and where there's room for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer truthfully.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best represents your feelings or experiences.
3. For text fields, provide a brief but clear explanation of your thoughts and feelings.
4. Make sure to provide any additional comments or feedback you think would be helpful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Happiness Survey**:
	+ This is the title of the form and the main section where you'll answer the questions.
* **How would you rate your overall happiness at work?**:
	+ This is the first question, which asks about your overall happiness at work. Answer on a scale that feels right to you.
* **Do you feel supported by your manager?**:
	+ This question asks about your relationship with your manager. Select the option that best describes your feelings.
* **Are you satisfied with the level of autonomy you have in your work?**:
	+ This question is about the freedom you have to make decisions and take actions in your work. Choose the option that best represents your feelings.
* **How many hours do you spend working on your tasks?**:
	+ This question is about your workload and the amount of time you spend on tasks. Answer with a number that represents your typical working hours.
* **What is your role in the company?**:
	+ This question is about your job title and responsibilities. Type a brief description of your role.
* **How do you feel about the company culture?**:
	+ This question is about your feelings about the company's values and atmosphere. Select the option that best describes your feelings.
* **How do you feel about your work-life balance?**:
	+ This question is about the balance between your work and personal life. Choose the option that best represents your feelings.
* **Have you ever thought about leaving the company?**:
	+ This question is about your thoughts about leaving the company. Select the option that best describes your feelings.
* **Why did you feel the desire to leave the company?**:
	+ This question is for those who selected "Yes" to the previous question. Provide a brief explanation of why you felt that way.
* **What is the primary motivation for your departure?**:
	+ This question is for those who selected "Yes" to the previous question. Type a brief description of the primary motivation for your departure.
* **Do you have any suggestions for improving the workplace?**:
	+ This question is for any additional comments or feedback you might have about the workplace.
* **Is there anything else you would like to share with us?**:
	+ This is the final question, where you can provide any additional comments or feedback you might have.
* **Is there anything else you would like to share with us?** (Duplicate):
	+ This is another instance of the same question, which seems to be a duplicate.
