# Meeting Preferences Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect meeting preferences from participants, including their availability, meeting preferences, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Fill in the required fields:
	* **team_member**: Enter your name as a team member.
	* **availability**: Provide your availability for meetings.
	* **start_time**, **end_time**, and **duration**: Enter the preferred start time, end time, and duration of the meeting.
	* **location**: Specify the preferred location of the meeting.
	* **meeting_date**: Select the date of the meeting.
	* **meeting_note**: Add any notes or comments about the meeting.
	* **email** and **phone**: Enter your contact information.
3. Answer the following questions:
	* **meeting_preferences**: Select all meeting preferences that apply (Yes or No).
	* **virtual_meeting** and **in_person_meeting**: Choose your preference for meeting format (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **team_member**: This is a text field where you can enter your name as a team member.
* **availability**: This field is for you to specify your availability for meetings, e.g., mornings, afternoons, etc.
* **start_time**: Enter the preferred start time of the meeting.
* **end_time**: Enter the preferred end time of the meeting.
* **duration**: Enter the preferred duration of the meeting.
* **location**: This is a text field where you can specify the preferred location of the meeting.
* **meeting_date**: Select the date of the meeting from the calendar.
* **meeting_note**: This is a text field for adding any notes or comments about the meeting.
* **email**: Enter your email address.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **meeting_preferences**: Select all meeting preferences that apply (Yes or No).
* **virtual_meeting**: Choose your preference for virtual meetings (Yes or No).
* **in_person_meeting**: Choose your preference for in-person meetings (Yes or No).
