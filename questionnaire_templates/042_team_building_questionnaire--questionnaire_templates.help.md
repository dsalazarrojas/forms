# Team Building Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about team building activities, questions, and events that help teams bond and work more effectively together.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand what each field is asking.
2. Answer each field according to the instructions provided.
3. Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Building Questionnaire (title)**: This is the title of the form. Please enter a descriptive title for the team building activity.
* **team_building_questions (Select One)**: Choose one of the provided options to describe the type of team building activity:
	+ Option 1: Team building activity with a focus on problem-solving and collaboration.
	+ Option 2: Team building activity that promotes socialization and bonding.
	+ Option 3: Team building activity that emphasizes skill development and training.
* **first_page**: This field is not relevant to the team building activity as per the provided schema.
* **second_page**: Note down any additional information or comments about the team building activity.
* **third_page**: This field is for recording the duration of the team building activity, e.g., 1 hour, 1 day, etc.
* **fourth_page**: Choose a date for the team building activity.
* **fifth_page**: This field is not relevant to the team building activity as per the provided schema.
* **sixth_page**: This field is for recording the number of team members participating in the activity.
* **seventh_page**: Enter any additional comments or information about the team building activity.
* **eighth_page**: Enter any notes or observations about the activity.
* **nineth_page**: This field is not relevant to the team building activity as per the provided schema.

Note: The fields marked as "not relevant" or "not applicable" are either not required or have a type that is not applicable to the team building activity. Please ignore these fields when completing the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide detailed and accurate information for each field.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the designated team lead or administrator.
* This form is meant to be a helpful tool for collecting information about team building activities. Please use it as such.
