# Library Digital Resource Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on digital resources from patrons. It's a tool to help us assess and improve the quality of resources provided by the library.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your email address in the "Patron Email" field.
3. Enter the name of the resource you are providing feedback on in the "Resource Name" field.
4. Describe the resource in the "Resource Description" field.
5. Rate the resource by selecting one of the provided ratings.
6. In the "Feedback" field, write any additional comments or suggestions.
7. In the "Additional Comments" field, add any other comments or suggestions.
8. Finally, submit the form by filling out the "Submission Date" field and entering the current date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form**: A brief header that welcomes you to the form.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Patron Email**: Your email address will be collected for internal use and to identify the submitter. Please enter the email address associated with your library account.
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: yes
* **Resource Name**: The name of the resource you are providing feedback on.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: yes
* **Resource Description**: A brief description of the resource.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Rating**: Select one of the provided ratings to rate the resource.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: yes
* **Feedback**: Any additional comments or suggestions about the resource.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Additional Comments**: Any other comments or suggestions about the resource.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Submission Date**: The date of submission of the form.
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: yes
* **Form**: A brief footer that thanks you for completing the form.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the "Feedback" and "Additional Comments" fields.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us at form@gic.mx.
