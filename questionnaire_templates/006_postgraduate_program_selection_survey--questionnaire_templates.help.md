# Postgraduate Program Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from potential postgraduate students about their interests, fields of study, and preferred contact method. This information will be used to determine the best fit for each student and to facilitate communication during the application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your general information in the "General Information" field.
2. Select all the interests that apply to you from the "Interests" field.
3. Choose one field of study that best fits your interests from the "Fields of Study" field.
4. Select the study mode that suits your needs from the "Study Mode" field.
5. Add any additional comments about your background or experience in the "Additional Comments" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Finally, choose your preferred contact method from the "Contact Preferred Method" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (Text Field, Required: No): This field is a free text area where you can provide your general information such as your name, background, etc.
* **Interests** (Select Multiple, Required: No): Select all the interests that apply to you. Please note that these options are not exhaustive and you can select all or any of the options that best describe your interests.
* **Fields of Study** (Select One, Required: No): Choose one field of study that best fits your interests. Please select the one that you are most interested in pursuing.
* **Study Mode** (Select One, Required: No): Select the study mode that suits your needs. Please choose the option that best fits your schedule and preferences.
* **Additional Comments** (Note Field, Required: No): If you have any additional comments or information about your background or experience, please provide it here.
* **Email** (Email Field, Required: No): Enter your email address so that we can contact you.
* **Phone** (Text Field, Required: No): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you.
* **Contact Preferred Method** (Select One, Required: No): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g. phone, email, or in-person).
