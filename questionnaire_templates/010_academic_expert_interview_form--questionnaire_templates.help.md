**Academic Expert Interview Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Academic Expert Interview Form is a tool used to collect information about an expert's interview details, including their name, affiliation, and consent to record. This form serves as a guide for the interviewer to ensure a successful and informative interview.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Interviewer name (id: 2)
	* Expert name (id: 3)
	* Interview date (id: 5)
	* Consent to record (id: 7)
2. Optionally, provide the following information:
	* Expert affiliation (id: 4)
	* Interview time (id: 6)
	* Topics to cover (id: 8)
	* Follow-up contact (id: 9)
	* Interviewer notes (id: 10)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Interviewer name** (id: 2, required): Enter the name of the person conducting the interview.
* **Expert name** (id: 3, required): Enter the full name of the interviewee.
* **Expert affiliation** (id: 4, optional): Enter the institution or organization the expert is affiliated with.
* **Interview date** (id: 5, required): Enter the scheduled date of the interview.
* **Interview time** (id: 6, optional): Enter the scheduled start time of the interview.
* **Consent to record** (id: 7, required): Select 'True' if you have permission to audio or video record the interview.
* **Topics to cover** (id: 8, optional): Enter key questions or themes to discuss during the interview.
* **Follow-up contact** (id: 9, optional): Enter the email or phone number for follow-up contact.
* **Interviewer notes** (id: 10, optional): Enter internal notes after the interview.

**Tips**
* Make sure to get the expert's consent before recording the interview.
* Keep the topics to cover relevant and concise to ensure a productive conversation.
* Use the follow-up contact information for future references.
* Take detailed notes during the interview to help with follow-up actions.
