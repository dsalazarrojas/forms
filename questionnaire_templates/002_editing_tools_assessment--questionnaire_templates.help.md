# Editing Tools Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback about editing tools, including their usage, satisfaction, and potential improvements needed. It's meant for team members who have interacted with these tools to provide honest and constructive input.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to understand what each section represents.
2. Answer the questions truthfully, and provide detailed explanations when needed.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
4. If you're unsure about any question, don't hesitate to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tool usage** (Tool usage, select_multiple, required): Choose which of the provided options (Yes, No) best represents your experience with the editing tools. This section is used to understand how frequently you've used the tools.
* **Team satisfaction** (Team satisfaction, select_one, required): Indicate your team's overall satisfaction with the tools by selecting Satisfied or Dissatisfied.
* **Feature needs** (Feature needs, select_multiple, required): Choose the features you think are essential or lacking in the current tools.
* **Software usage** (Software usage, text, required): Please provide a detailed description of your software usage experience.
* **Satisfaction** (Satisfaction, number, required): Rate your personal satisfaction with the tools on a scale.
* **Training needs** (Training needs, select_multiple, required): Indicate which areas of the tools require training or support.
* **Tool selection** (Tool selection, select_one, required): Choose whether the tools meet your needs for editing.
* **Additional Notes** (Additional Notes, note, required): Share any thoughts, suggestions, or concerns you have about the tools.
* **Email Contact** (Email Contact, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, provide your email address.
* **Phone Contact** (Phone Contact, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, provide your phone number.

Note: Please answer each question thoroughly and honestly, and don't hesitate to ask for clarification if needed.
