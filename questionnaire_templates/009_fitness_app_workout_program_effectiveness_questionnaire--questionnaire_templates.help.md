# fitness_app_workout_program_effectiveness_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "fitness_app_workout_program_effectiveness_questionnaire" form is used to gather feedback on the effectiveness of the fitness app's workout programs. This form will help us identify areas of improvement and ensure the programs are meeting your fitness goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Go through each page of the form and answer the questions that apply to your experience with the fitness app.
2. For each field, choose the option that best represents your answer.
3. Make sure to review each page carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Satisfaction** (`program_satisfaction`, select_one, required): How satisfied are you with the current fitness program you are using? Choose 'Satisfied' if you feel the program is meeting your goals, or 'Dissatisfied' if it's not.
* **Program Outcomes** (`program_outcomes`, select_multiple, required): Check all outcomes that you have achieved through the fitness program.
* **Training Plan Satisfaction** (`training_plan_satisfaction`, number, required): How satisfied are you with the training plan provided by the fitness app? You can choose a number from 1 (not at all satisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **Program Outcome 1** (`program_outcome_1`, select_one, required): Have you achieved outcome 1? Choose 'Yes' if you have achieved this outcome.
* **Program Outcome 2** (`program_outcome_2`, select_multiple, required): Have you achieved outcome 2? Check all options that apply.
* **Program Outcome 3** (`program_outcome_3`, select_one, required): Have you achieved outcome 3? Choose 'Yes' if you have achieved this outcome.
* **Program Outcome 4** (`program_outcome_4`, select_multiple, required): Have you achieved outcome 4? Check all options that apply.
* **Program Outcome 5** (`program_outcome_5`, select_one, required): Have you achieved outcome 5? Choose 'Yes' if you have achieved this outcome.
* **Program Outcome 6** (`program_outcome_6`, select_multiple, required): Have you achieved outcome 6? Check all options that apply.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Take your time to review each page carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any feedback or suggestions, please feel free to include them in the comments section.
