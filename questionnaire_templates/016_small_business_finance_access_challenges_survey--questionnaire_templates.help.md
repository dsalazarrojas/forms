# Small Business Finance Access Challenges Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about small business finance access challenges. The purpose of this form is to gather insights on the challenges faced by small businesses when accessing financing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best describe your experience with small business finance access challenges.
3. If you have additional information or comments that do not fit the available options, please use the text fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Business Type (`business_type`)
  * **Business Type**: Select the type of your business from the list of options or enter it as "other" if it's not listed.
    * Hint: For example, select "Service" if your business provides services.

### 2. Loan Amount (`loan_amount`)
  * **Loan Amount**: Enter the amount of the loan you requested or applied for.
    * Hint: If you didn't request or apply for a loan, enter "0" or "none".

### 3. Financial Institution (`financial_institution`)
  * **Financial Institution**: Select the financial institution you dealt with, such as the Small Business Administration or a Local Nonprofit Lender, or select "Other" if it's not listed.
    * Hint: For example, select "Small Business Administration" if you interacted with them.

### 4. Funding Source (`funding_source`)
  * **Funding Source**: Indicate if you received funding from any of the listed sources or select "Other" if it's not listed.
    * Hint: For example, select "Yes" if you received funding from a government agency.

### 5. Time to Reach Decision (`time_to_reach_decision`)
  * **Time to Reach Decision**: Select how long it took to receive a decision after applying for funding or financing.
    * Hint: For example, select "Less than 1 week" if the decision was made within a week.

### 6. Business Location (`business_location`)
  * **Business Location**: Indicate if you have a physical location for your business in the selected options or select "Other" if it's not listed.
    * Hint: For example, select "Yes" if you have a physical location.

### 7. Funding Amount (`funding_amount`)
  * **Funding Amount**: Enter the amount of funding you requested or received.
    * Hint: If you didn't request or receive funding, enter "0" or "none".

### 8. Funding Type (`funding_type`)
  * **Funding Type**: Select the type of funding you received or applied for, such as a loan or grant.
    * Hint: For example, select "Loan" if you received a loan.

### 9. Application Process (`application_process`)
  * **Application Process**: Indicate if you went through a specific application process for funding or financing.
    * Hint: For example, select "Yes" if you went through a specific process.

### 10. Time to Reach Funding Decision (`time_to_reach_funding_decision`)
  * **Time to Reach Funding Decision**: Select how long it took to receive a funding decision.
    * Hint: For example, select "Less than 1 week" if the decision was made within a week.

### 11. Communication with Financial Institution (`communication_with_financial_institution`)
  * **Communication with Financial Institution**: Indicate if you had good communication with the financial institution during the process.
    * Hint: For example, select "Yes" if you felt well-informed and supported.

### 12. Access to Financial Resources (`access_to_financial_resources`)
  * **Access to Financial Resources**: Indicate if you had access to the financial resources you needed.
    * Hint: For example, select "Yes" if you had access to the resources.

### 13. Access to Funding (`access_to_funding`)
  * **Access to Funding**: Indicate if you had access to the funding you needed.
    * Hint: For example, select "Yes" if you had access to the funding.

### 14. Support to Business Owner (`support_to_business_owner`)
  * **Support to Business Owner**: Indicate if you received support from the financial institution to the business owner.
    * Hint: For example, select "Yes" if you received guidance or resources.

### 15. Financing Costs (`financing_costs`)
  * **Financing Costs**: Indicate if you incurred any costs or fees related to financing.
    * Hint: For example, select "Yes" if you paid fees.

### 16. Financing Terms (`financing_terms`)
  * **Financing Terms**: Indicate if the financing terms were favorable or unfavorable.
    * Hint: For example, select "Yes" if the terms were favorable.

### 17. Business Funding Goals (`business_funding_goals`)
  * **Business Funding Goals**: Indicate if you had clear goals for funding your business.
    * Hint: For example, select "Yes" if you had a clear plan.

### 18. Business Location Other (`business_location_other`)
  * **Other Business Location**: If your business location is not listed, enter the correct location in this field.
    * Hint: For example, enter the city or country if it's not on the list.

### 19. Business Type Other (`business_type_other`)
  * **Other Business Type**: If your business type is not listed, enter the correct type in this field.
    * Hint: For example, enter the industry or category if it's not on the list.

### 20. Financial Institution Other (`financial_institution_other`)
  * **Other Financial Institution**: If the financial institution you interacted with is not listed, enter the correct name in this field.
    * Hint: For example, enter the name of the bank or institution if it's not on the list.

### 21. Funding Source Other (`funding_source_other`)
  * **Other Funding Source**: If the funding source you received is not listed, enter the correct source in this field.
    * Hint: For example, enter the name of the government agency or non-profit.

### 22. Business Name (`business_name`)
  * **Business Name**: Enter your business name.
    * Hint: For example, enter your business name as it appears on your documents.

### 23. Business Address (`business_address`)
  * **Business Address**: Enter your business address.
    * Hint: For example, enter your business address as it appears on your documents.

### 24. Business City (`business_city`)
  * **Business City**: Enter the city where your business is located.
    * Hint: For example, enter the city name as it appears on your documents.

### 25. Business Postal Code (`business_postal_code`)
  * **Business Postal Code**: Enter the postal code for your business location.
    * Hint: For example, enter the postal code as it appears on your documents.

### Tips

1. Answer each question honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best describe your experience with small business finance access challenges.
3. If you have additional information or comments that do not fit the available options, please use the text fields provided.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
