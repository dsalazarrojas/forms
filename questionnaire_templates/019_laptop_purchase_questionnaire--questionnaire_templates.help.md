# Laptop Purchase Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information about laptop purchases, enabling efficient and accurate inventory management and procurement processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user name and the purchase date (if applicable) in the relevant fields.
2. Provide the purchase amount, if known.
3. Select the laptop model and type from the respective fields.
4. Indicate the laptop condition (new, refurbished, or used) and whether it comes with a warranty.
5. Choose the laptop price range from the options provided.
6. If applicable, indicate if the laptop was recommended by someone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input**: Enter your name, as you'll be the person filling out this form.
* **Purchase Date (if applicable)**: Enter the date the laptop was purchased, if this is a follow-up or update request. If the purchase date is not known or not applicable, simply leave this field blank.
* **Purchase Amount**: Enter the amount of money spent on the laptop, if known. If you're unsure or don't have this information, it's okay to leave this field blank.
* **User Name**: Enter your name to associate you with the purchase.
* **Laptop Model**: Choose the model of the laptop being purchased or updated.
* **Laptop Type**: Select the type of laptop being purchased (e.g., Lenovo, Apple, HP, Asus, Dell, or Acer).
* **Laptop Condition**: Indicate whether the laptop is new, refurbished, or used.
* **Laptop Warranty**: Check if the laptop still has a valid warranty. If you're unsure or not applicable, choose the 'False' option.
* **Laptop Price Range**: Select the price range of the laptop being purchased (e.g., $0-100, $100-200, $200-500).
* **Recommended By**: If the laptop was recommended by someone, enter their name or the recommendation source (e.g., a colleague, a vendor, or a salesperson).
