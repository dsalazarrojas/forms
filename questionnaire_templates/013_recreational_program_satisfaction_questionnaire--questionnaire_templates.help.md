# Recreational Program Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recreational Program Satisfaction Questionnaire is a form designed to gather feedback from program participants on their experiences in recreational programs. This form is meant to help us understand what they like or dislike about the program and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your program name in the "program name" field.
2. Enter the name of the activity you participated in in the "activity name" field.
3. Rate your satisfaction with the program on a scale from 1 to 5 in the "satisfaction rating" field.
4. Select the location of the program in the "program location" field.
5. Enter the time of the program in the "program time" field (if applicable).
6. Provide any additional comments about your experience in the "additional comments" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "email" field (if applicable).
8. Enter your phone number in the "phone number" field (if applicable).
9. Select the date of your participation in the "date" field (if applicable).
10. Select the time of day of your participation in the "time of day" field (if applicable).
11. Rate the program on a scale from 1 to 5 in the "program rating" field.
12. Select the value of the program in the "program value" field.
13. Select the important features of the program in the "program important" field.
14. Select the very important features of the program in the "program very important" field.
15. Enter any additional questions or comments in the "additional questions" field.
16. Enter your name in the "name" field.
17. Enter your age in the "age" field.
18. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
19. Enter your address in the "address" field.
20. Enter your city in the "city" field.
21. Enter your state in the "state" field.
22. Enter your zip code in the "zip" field.
23. Enter your country in the "country" field.
24. Enter any comments or feedback in the "comments" field.
25. Enter your contact information in the "contact" field.
