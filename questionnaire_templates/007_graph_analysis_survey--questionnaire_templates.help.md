# Graph Analysis Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your experience and usage of graph-related tools and data sources. It will help us understand your background, habits, and satisfaction with our graph analysis services. Please answer the questions honestly, and feel free to provide any additional information you think might be relevant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page, one at a time. For each question, read the label and any hint provided, and answer accordingly. If you're unsure about a question, try to provide the best answer you can based on your knowledge and experience. You can always add any additional comments or information you think might be helpful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a text box where you can write anything you'd like. It's not required, but it's a good place to provide context about your background and experience.
* **Graph background**: Describe the type of graph-related tools you've used before. This could be a programming language, a specific tool or software, or a data source.
* **Usage habits**: If you're able to provide a number, tell us about how many hours you spend using graph-related tools per week. If not, please explain why you can't provide a number.
* **Tool satisfaction**: Select one of the options that best describes your overall satisfaction with our graph analysis services. We use this to track our performance and improve our services.
* **What do you know about the graph?**: Describe any knowledge or experience you have with graph algorithms, databases, or data structures. This will help us understand your background and provide more relevant information.
* **What data sources do you use?**: Select all that apply, but please choose from the options provided. This helps us understand what data sources you rely on for your graph analysis.
* **How do you use the graph?**: Describe how you use the graph or graph-related tools in your work or daily activities. Be as detailed as you can, but don't worry about length.
* **Tips**: If you're unsure or have any comments about our graph analysis services, feel free to add them in this text box. It's your chance to let us know your thoughts!
