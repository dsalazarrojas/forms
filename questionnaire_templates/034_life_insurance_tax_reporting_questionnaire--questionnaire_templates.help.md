# Life Insurance Tax Reporting Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect tax reporting information from policyholders and their insurers for life insurance policies.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select a tax year**: Choose the tax year you are reporting for from the dropdown menu. The available options are 2020, 2021, and 2022.
2. **Policyholder Information**:
   * **Policy Holder**: Enter the name of the policyholder.
   * **Policy Holder Phone**: Enter the phone number of the policyholder.
   * **Policy Holder Email**: Enter the email address of the policyholder.
   * **Policy Holder Address**: Enter the address of the policyholder.
   * **Policy Holder City**: Enter the city of the policyholder.
   * **Policy Holder State**: Select the state of the policyholder from the dropdown menu.
   * **Policy Holder Zip**: Enter the zip code of the policyholder.
3. **Policy Insurer Information**:
   * **Policy Insurer**: Enter the name of the policy insurer.
   * **Policy Insurer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the policy insurer.
   * **Policy Insurer Email**: Enter the email address of the policy insurer.
   * **Policy Insurer Address**: Enter the address of the policy insurer.
   * **Policy Insurer City**: Enter the city of the policy insurer.
   * **Policy Insurer State**: Select the state of the policy insurer from the dropdown menu.
   * **Policy Insurer Zip**: Enter the zip code of the policy insurer.
   * **Policy Insurer Employer**: Enter the employer of the policy insurer.
4. **Policy Details**:
   * **Policy Insurance Value**: Enter the value of the policy.
   * **Policy Insurance Type**: Select the type of policy (Life or Annuity).
   * **Policy Insurance Term**: Enter the term of the policy.
5. **Relationship and Dates**:
   * **Policy Holder Relationship**: Select the relationship between the policyholder and the policy insurer (Single, Married, or Spouse).
   * **Policy Holder Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the policyholder.
   * **Policy Insurer Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the policy insurer.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and consistently.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the tax professional or accountant handling your tax reporting.
* This form is designed to collect information for tax reporting purposes, so please ensure that the information is up-to-date and accurate.
