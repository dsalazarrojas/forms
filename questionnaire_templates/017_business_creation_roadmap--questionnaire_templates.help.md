# Business Creation Roadmap - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you create a comprehensive business creation roadmap by gathering essential information about your business idea, structure, and goals. Please fill out this form to ensure you have a clear and structured approach to bringing your business to life.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Go through each page of the form, carefully reading the instructions and guidelines for each section.
2. Fill in your founder's information, including name, email, and phone number.
3. Provide details about your business, such as its name, description, industry, target market, and unique value proposition.
4. Decide on your business structure and select the number of co-founders.
5. Plan your finances, including estimated startup costs, funding sources, and total funding needed.
6. Set key dates for business registration and product/service launch.
7. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Founder Name** (`founder_name`, `text`, required): Your full name as the founder of the business.
* **Email Address** (`founder_email`, `email`, required): Your business email address.
* **Phone Number** (`founder_phone`, `text`, required): Your contact phone number.
* **Years of Business Experience** (`business_experience`, `number`, optional): Your experience in years as a business owner or entrepreneur.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): The name of your business.
* **Business Description** (`business_description`, `text`, required): A brief description of what your business does.
* **Industry Type** (`industry_type`, `text`, required): The type of industry your business falls under.
* **Target Market** (`target_market`, `text`, required): The demographic or market segment you're targeting.
* **Problem You Solve** (`problem_solved`, `text`, required): A brief description of the problem your business solves.
* **Unique Value Proposition** (`unique_value`, `text`, required): What sets your business apart from others in its industry.
* **Business Structure** (`business_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the structure for your business (Sole Proprietor, Partnership, Corporation, LLC, S-Corp, Non-Profit, Cooperative).
* **Number of Co-Founders** (`co_founders`, `number`, optional): The number of co-founders you have.
* **Estimated Startup Costs** (`startup_cost`, `number`, required): The total estimated investment needed to get your business started.
* **Funding Sources** (`funding_sources`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the sources of funding for your business (Personal Savings, Small Business Loan, Venture Capital, Angel Investment, Crowdfunding, Grants, Friends and Family).
* **Total Funding Needed** (`funding_needed`, `number`, required): The total amount of funding needed for your business.
* **Expected First Year Revenue** (`projected_revenue`, `number`, optional): Your expected revenue for the first year of business.
* **Business Registration Target Date** (`business_registration`, `date`, required): The target date for registering your business.
* **Product or Service Launch Date** (`product_launch`, `date`, required): The target date for launching your product or service.
* **Expected Break-Even Timeline** (`break_even_timeline`, `number`, optional): The number of months it's expected to take for your business to break even.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Any other important information about your business.

Note: This form is designed to be flexible and adaptable to your needs. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for support.
