# Landlord Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact and property information from a landlord for internal administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact details of the landlord, including their email address and phone number.
2. Enter the landlord's address.
3. Provide any additional notes about the landlord.
4. Enter the landlord's identity.
5. Enter the property details (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **contact_details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact details of the landlord.
* **contact_details_email** (`contact_details_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the landlord's email address.
* **contact_details_phone** (`contact_details_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the landlord's phone number.
* **contact_details_note** (`contact_details_note`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the landlord's contact details.
* **landlord_identity** (`landlord_identity`, text, required/optional): Enter the landlord's identity (e.g. name, title, etc.).
* **landlord_address** (`landlord_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the landlord's address.
* **rental_agency** (`rental_agency`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the rental agency (if applicable).
* **property_details** (`property_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant property details (e.g. property address, type, etc.).

## Tips

* Be as concrete and accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* Double-check the information before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, you can ask for clarification or guidance from the relevant staff member.
