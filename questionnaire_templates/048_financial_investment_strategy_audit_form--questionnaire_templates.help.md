# Financial Investment Strategy Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals and organizations assess their financial investment strategy and gather information for financial planning and analysis.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you have the necessary information before starting.
2. Select the relevant fields that apply to your financial situation, such as your investment goals, risk tolerance, and portfolio size.
3. Provide detailed and accurate information in each field to ensure a comprehensive financial picture is obtained.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Investment Strategy** (`investment_strategy`, `text`, required/optional): Please describe your current financial investment strategy, including any existing investments, goals, and risk tolerance.
  - If you are unsure, you can also mention any financial goals you have set for yourself or your organization, such as retirement savings or major purchases.

- **Investment Goals** (`investment_goals`, `text`, required/optional): List any specific financial goals you have, such as saving for a house, paying off debt, or funding education.
  - Be as specific as possible, and include any target dates or deadlines.

- **Risk Tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`, `text`, required/optional): Indicate your comfort level with risk, from very conservative (low risk) to very aggressive (high risk).
  - This will help us understand your investment approach and risk management strategies.

- **Risk Sensitivity** (`risk_sensitivity`, `text`, required/optional): Describe any specific risks you are sensitive to, such as market volatility, inflation, or interest rate changes.
  - This will help us understand your concerns and tailor your investment approach.

- **Portfolio Size** (`portfolio_size`, `text`, required/optional): Provide information about the size of your current portfolio, including the value of your investments.
  - If you are unsure, estimate the approximate value.

- **Investment Period** (`investment_period`, `text`, required/optional): Indicate the time horizon for your investments, such as short-term (less than 1 year), medium-term (1-5 years), or long-term (more than 5 years).
  - This will help us understand your investment time frame.

- **Investment Frequency** (`investment_frequency`, `text`, required/optional): Describe how often you invest, such as quarterly, semi-annually, or annually.
  - If you have variable investments, please list them all.

- **Investment Type** (`investment_type`, `text`, required/optional): List the types of investments you have, such as stocks, bonds, real estate, or cash.
  - Be specific and include any asset classes or sectors.

- **Financial Institutions** (`financial_institutions`, `text`, required/optional): Indicate the financial institutions you use, such as banks, brokerages, or investment firms.
  - List any specific relationships or accounts you have with them.

- **Investment Objects** (`investment_objects`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the types of assets you invest in, such as stocks, bonds, ETFs, or mutual funds.
  - Be specific and include any asset classes or sectors.

- **Investment Location** (`investment_location`, `text`, required/optional): Indicate the geographic location of your investments, such as local, domestic, or international.
  - If you invest in emerging markets, please mention them specifically.

- **Investment Currency** (`investment_currency`, `text`, required/optional): List the currencies in which you invest, such as USD, CAD, or EURO.
  - If you have investments in multiple currencies, list them all.

- **Investment Frequency 1-12** (`investment_frequency_1`, `investment_frequency_2`, `investment_frequency_3`, ..., `investment_frequency_12`, `text`, required/optional): List any additional frequencies or investment frequencies you have.
  - If you have variable investments, please list them all.

Note: Please review each field carefully to ensure you have provided all relevant information to help us understand your financial investment strategy.
Tips: If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for clarification.
