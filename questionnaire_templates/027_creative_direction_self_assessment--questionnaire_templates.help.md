# Creative Direction Self Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative Direction Self Assessment is a tool designed to help you evaluate your approach, goals, and skills as a creative professional. This form will guide you through a series of questions and prompts to help you identify areas where you excel and areas where you may need to improve.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select the options that best describe your current creative direction.
3. Be specific and provide as much detail as possible in your answers.
4. Take your time to complete the form, but do not hesitate to seek help if you need clarification on any question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (Text): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you would like it to appear on any creative work you produce. This will help us identify you in our records and associate your assessment with your account.
* **Job role** (Select one):
	+ Creative Director: A senior role responsible for overseeing and guiding the creative vision of a project or organization.
	+ Art Director: A mid-level role responsible for creating and executing visual concepts for a project or organization.
	+ Senior Art Director: A senior mid-level role responsible for overseeing the visual direction of a project or organization.
	+ Junior Art Director: A junior mid-level role responsible for assisting in the creation and execution of visual concepts.
	+ Art Assistant Director: An entry-level role responsible for assisting in the creation and execution of visual concepts.
* **Years of experience** (Number): Please enter the number of years you have been working in a creative field.
* **Work approach** (Select one):
	+ Creativity: Emphasizing innovation and originality in your work.
	+ Efficiency: Focusing on completing tasks and projects efficiently and effectively.
	+ Quality: Prioritizing high-quality work and attention to detail.
	+ Effectiveness: Focusing on achieving results and meeting deadlines.
	+ Time Management: Managing your time and resources effectively.
* **Main goals** (Select multiple):
	+ Innovation: Seeking to create new and original ideas and solutions.
	+ Originality: Emphasizing unique and distinctive work.
	+ Quality: Prioritizing high-quality work and attention to detail.
	+ Timeliness: Meeting deadlines and delivering work on time.
	+ Creativity: Emphasizing innovative and imaginative work.
* **Values** (Select multiple):
	+ Innovation: Embracing new and original ideas and solutions.
	+ Originality: Valuing unique and distinctive work.
	+ Quality: Prioritizing high-quality work and attention to detail.
	+ Timeliness: Meeting deadlines and delivering work on time.
	+ Time Management: Managing your time and resources effectively.
* **Time management skills** (Number): Please enter a number that reflects your ability to manage your time effectively.
* **Feedback** (Date): Please enter the date you last received constructive feedback on your work.
* **Communication skills** (Select multiple):
	+ Verbal: Effective communication through spoken words.
	+ Non-Verbal: Effective communication through non-verbal means such as body language or gestures.
	+ Written: Effective communication through written words.
	+ Verbal and Non-Verbal: Effective communication through both spoken and non-verbal means.
	+ Verbal and Written: Effective communication through both spoken and written words.
* **Adaptability** (Select one):
	+ Organization: Adapting to new and changing situations and tasks.
	+ Teamwork: Working effectively with a team to achieve common goals.
	+ Communication: Adjusting your communication style to suit different situations.
	+ Leadership: Leading by example and motivating others.
	+ Adaptability: Embracing change and adapting to new situations.
* **Final** (Note): Please provide any additional comments or thoughts you have on your creative direction and areas for improvement.

Note: The information provided in this form will be used to guide and support your personal growth and development as a creative professional.
