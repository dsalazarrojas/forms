# Criminal Defense Mistakes Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to gather information on common mistakes that occur during a criminal defense process. This will help users identify potential areas for improvement and develop strategies to reduce the risk of mistakes happening in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through each page carefully and select all the mistakes that apply to the criminal defense process.
2. On the "Mistakes" page, select the types of mistakes that occurred or could occur during a criminal defense process.
3. On the "Description" page, provide a brief description of each selected mistake.
4. On the "Impact" page, select all the options that indicate the mistake had an impact or did not have an impact on the case or client.
5. On the "Improvement Ideas" page, suggest any ideas or strategies that could be implemented to reduce the risk of similar mistakes in the future.
6. On the "Who are the stakeholders?" page, select all the stakeholders involved in the case or criminal defense process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mistakes** (`Mistakes`, select_one, required/optional): Select all the mistakes that apply to the criminal defense process. These include:
	+ Arbitration mistakes
	+ Discovery mistakes
	+ Witness interview mistakes
	+ Jury trial mistakes
	+ Prosecutorial mistakes
	+ Defensive mistakes
	+ Judicial mistakes
	+ Plea bargain mistakes

* **Description** (`Description`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of each selected mistake.

* **Impact** (`Impact`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the options that indicate the mistake had an impact or did not have an impact on the case or client:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'

* **Improvement Ideas** (`Improvement Ideas`, text, required/optional): Suggest any ideas or strategies that could be implemented to reduce the risk of similar mistakes in the future.

* **Who are the stakeholders?** (`Who are the stakeholders?`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the stakeholders involved in the case or criminal defense process:
	+ Client
	+ Prosecutor
	+ Judge
	+ Defense Attorney
	+ Lawyer
	+ Expert Witness

Note: This form is for educational and research purposes only. The information collected will not be used for any other purpose without explicit permission.
