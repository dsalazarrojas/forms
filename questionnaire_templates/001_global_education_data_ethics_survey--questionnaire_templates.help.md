# global_education_data_ethics_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The global_education_data_ethics_survey form is designed to collect insights and feedback on education data ethics in your region. It aims to gather information on the current state of education data ethics, challenges, and best practices to help improve data handling and usage in the education sector.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability, being as specific and detailed as possible.
2. Use the provided options for multiple-choice questions, or select "Other" if your answer does not fit within the given options.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional comments or suggestions" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **global_education_data_ethics_survey**: This is the title of the form, which does not require a specific answer.
* **What are the biggest challenges facing education data ethics in your region?**: Please describe the main challenges you face in terms of education data ethics in your region. This may include issues with data quality, privacy, security, accuracy, or other concerns.
* **What do you think is the main purpose of education data ethics?**: Share your understanding of the main purpose or goal of education data ethics in your region.
* **How do you think the main purpose of education data ethics should be?**: Describe your ideal vision for the main purpose of education data ethics in your region.
* **Education data ethics principles**: Select all the principles that you believe are essential for education data ethics in your region. These principles are:
	+ Principles for data quality
	+ Principles for data privacy
	+ Principles for data security
	+ Principles for data accuracy
	+ Principles for data authenticity
	+ Principles for data integrity
	+ Principles for data availability
	+ Principles for data accessibility
	+ Principles for data accountability
	+ Principles for data transparency
	+ Principles for data trust
	+ Principles for data responsibility
	+ Principles for data sustainability
* **Which of the following data types should have the highest priority for data ethics?**: Choose the type of data that you believe should be given the highest priority for data ethics in your region. Options include:
	+ Student data
	+ Teacher data
	+ Parent data
	+ School data
	+ District data
	+ State data
	+ National data
	+ Global data
* **How would you rate the current level of data ethics awareness in your region?**: On a scale of 1-10, rate the current level of awareness and understanding of data ethics in your region.
* **Which data practices should be implemented in your region?**: Select all the data practices that you believe are essential for education data ethics in your region. These practices include:
	+ Anonymization of data
	+ Data anonymization
	+ Data pseudonymization
	+ Data encryption
	+ Data masking
	+ Data tokenization
* **What should be the role of AI in education data ethics?**: Share your thoughts on the role of AI in addressing education data ethics concerns.
* **How do you rate the importance of data ethics in education?**: Choose how important you believe data ethics is for education, with options:
	+ Very important
	+ Somewhat important
	+ Not very important
	+ Not at all important
* **Would you like to share any additional comments or suggestions regarding education data ethics?**: Use this field to provide any additional insights, suggestions, or concerns you may have regarding education data ethics.
