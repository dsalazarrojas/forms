# Interview Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a candidate's contact details and background. It helps in creating a comprehensive profile that can be used for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Start by navigating to the "Contact Information" page. This is where you will find questions about your personal contact details.
2.  On the "Work Experience" page, select all the relevant options that apply to your work experience.
3.  In the "Educational Background" page, provide your educational qualifications, degrees, and certifications.
4.  On the "Language Skills" page, select all the languages you are proficient in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Introduction
No questions are asked on this page, but it provides a brief description of the form and its purpose.

### Page 2: Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `select_one`, required): This is where you can select your preferred contact method: Email, Phone, or Both.

### Page 3: Work Experience
* **Work Experience** (`Work Experience`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all the options that describe your work experience. 

### Page 4: Educational Background
* **Educational Background** (`Educational Background`, `text`, required): Please provide your educational qualifications, degrees, and certifications here.

### Page 5: Language Skills
* **Language Skills** (`Language Skills`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the languages you are proficient in.
