# supplemental_questions_for_police_chief - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect supplementary information from Police Chiefs to help our organization's initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:
1. Select the "Police Chief Supplemental Questions" page.
2. Fill out the questions on each page, one at a time, from top to bottom.
3. Make sure to provide accurate and truthful information.
4. Once you have filled out all the questions, click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Police Chief Supplemental Questions
* **Police Chief Supplemental Questions** (`police_chief_supplemental_questions`, text, required/optional): Please provide a brief explanation or answer to the question.

### Background and Experience
* **Background and Experience** (`background_and_experience`, text, required/optional): Please provide a detailed description of your background and experience in the Police Chief role.

### Education
* **Education** (`education`, note, required/optional): Please provide any relevant educational background or certifications you have.

### Training and Certifications
* **Training and Certifications** (`training_certifications`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please select all the relevant training and certifications you have completed.
    - Yes: You have completed this training or have this certification.
    - No: You have not completed this training or do not have this certification.

### Leadership Skills
* **Leadership Skills** (`leadership_skills`, select_one, required/optional): Please select one of the following leadership skills that you possess:
    - Yes: You possess this leadership skill.
    - No: You do not possess this leadership skill.

### References
* **References** (`references`, text, required/optional): Please provide a list of relevant references, including their contact information.

### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, email, required/optional): Please provide your email address or contact information.

### Submission Date
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required/optional): This field is automatically populated with the current date when the form is submitted. 

## Tips
- Please ensure that you are providing accurate and truthful information.
- If you have any difficulties or questions, please contact our support team.
- The form should only be completed if you have not previously completed it.
