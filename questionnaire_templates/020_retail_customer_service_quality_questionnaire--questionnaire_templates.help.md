# Retail Customer Service Quality Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Customer Service Quality Questionnaire is designed to gather customer feedback about their experiences with retail employees and stores. This form helps identify areas where service quality can be improved and ultimately enhances the overall customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Enter your feedback about the employee you interacted with in the "Employee Information" section.
2.  Share your feedback about the store in the "Store Information" section.
3.  Describe your experience with the service quality in the "Service Quality" section.
4.  Comment on the communication style of the employee in the "Communication Style" section.
5.  Share your overall experience with the store in the "Overall Experience" section.
6.  Indicate your store visit frequency in the "Store Visit Frequency" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Employee Information** (employee_info): This is the section where you can provide feedback about the employee who served you.
    *   Please enter a description of your feedback about the employee. This could include positive or negative comments about their service quality, communication style, or behavior.
*   **Store Information** (store_info): Share your feedback about the store itself.
    *   Enter your thoughts about the store's ambiance, facilities, or any other aspects that contributed to your experience.
*   **Service Quality** (service_quality): Describe your experience with the service provided by the employee.
    *   Be specific about what worked well and what didn't. This could include examples of how the employee helped you or failed to help you.
*   **Communication Style** (communication_style): Evaluate the employee's communication style during your interaction.
    *   Comment on how they listened, asked questions, or explained things in a clear and concise manner.
*   **Overall Experience** (overall_experience): Summarize your overall experience with the store.
    *   Try to be specific about what made your experience exceptional or disappointing.
*   **Store Visit Frequency** (store_visit_frequency): Indicate how often you visit this store.
    *   This will help us understand your loyalty and frequency of visits.
