# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) form is designed to collect user inquiries, submit questions, and provide answers. This form allows users to input their questions, select relevant tags, and submit their queries for resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Common Inquiries" field and provide a brief description of your question or concern.
2. If your question is related to a specific topic, select the relevant tag from the "Question Tags" dropdown menu.
3. Provide a detailed description of your question in the "Question Description" field.
4. Select the type of question you are asking (General, Technical, or Account) from the "Question Type" dropdown menu.
5. You can add additional tags to your question by selecting them from the "Question Answer Tags" dropdown menu.
6. Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the "Submit" button to send your question for resolution.
7. If you need to cancel the submission, click the "Cancel" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Common Inquiries** (`Common Inquiries`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your question or concern.
* **Submit Question** (`Submit Question`, text, optional): Enter the question you would like to ask.
* **Receive Answer** (`Receive Answer`, text, optional): Enter a description of the answer you would like to receive.
* **Question Description** (`Question Description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your question.
* **Question Type** (`Question Type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of question you are asking (General, Technical, or Account).
* **Question** (`Question`, text, optional): Enter the question you would like to ask.
* **Question Tags** (`Question Tags`, select_multiple, optional): Select additional tags related to your question.
* **Question Answer Tags** (`Question Answer Tags`, select_multiple, optional): Select tags related to the answer you are expecting (e.g., tag1, tag2, tag3).
* **Answer** (`Answer`, text, optional): Enter the answer to your question.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, optional): Click this button to send your question for resolution.
* **Cancel** (`Cancel`, text, optional): Click this button to cancel the submission.
* **Note** (`Note`, note, optional): Enter any additional note or comment about your question.
* **Tags for Answer** (`Tags for Answer`, select_multiple, optional): This field is not directly visible to the user but is used to store the tags related to the answer.
