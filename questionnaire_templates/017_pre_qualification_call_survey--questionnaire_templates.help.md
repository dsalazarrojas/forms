# pre-qualification-call-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pre-qualification call survey is used to gather information about a lead or prospect for a pre-qualification call or meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out each field accurately and completely.
2. Ensure that each field is correctly labeled and described.
3. If a field is not applicable, please fill out "N/A" or leave it blank.
4. Be sure to select the correct options from the dropdown menus.
5. Use the "submit" button to save your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **introduction** (`1`, optional): Write a brief introduction to your company and your role within it.
- **Name** (`2`, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
- **Company Name** (`3`, optional): Enter the name of the company you are representing.
- **Position** (`4`, optional): Enter your title or position within the company.
- **What Brought You Here** (`5`, optional): Briefly explain what led you to fill out this form.
- **Email** (`6`, optional): Enter your email address for correspondence.
- **Phone** (`7`, optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up.
- **Lead Source** (`8`, required): Select the source that led you to this form, e.g. social media, website, referral, etc.
- **Company** (`9`, optional): Enter the name of the company you are contacting us about.
- **Lead Stage** (`10`, required): Select the stage of the lead, e.g. lead generation, conversion, qualification, etc.
- **Company Size** (`11`, optional): Select the number of employees in your company.
- **Company Type** (`12`, optional): Select the type of company you are contacting us about, e.g. B2B, B2C, etc.
- **Company Industry** (`13`, optional): Select the industry your company operates in.
- **What Do You Sell** (`14`, optional): Briefly describe the products or services you sell.
- **What Do You Sell** (`15`, optional): (Duplicate of 14, please fill in one of them)
- **About Your Product** (`16`, optional): Describe your product or service.
- **Lead Status** (`17`, required): Select the status of the lead, e.g. new, existing.
- **Last Contact** (`18`, optional): Enter the date of your last contact with us.
- **Last Contact Time** (`19`, optional): Enter the time of your last contact with us.
- **Next Steps** (`20`, optional): Describe any next steps or follow-up meetings you would like to schedule.
- **Follow-up Date** (`21`, optional): Enter a date for follow-up.
- **Follow-up Time** (`22`, optional): Enter a time for follow-up.
- **Next Step** (`23`, required): Select the next step, e.g. call, email, meeting, etc.
- **Follow-up Message** (`24`, optional): Leave a message for follow-up.
- **Follow-up Message** (`25`, optional): (Duplicate of 24, please fill in one of them)

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Use clear and concise language in each field.
* Ensure that each field is filled out completely and accurately.
* If you are unsure about a field, please fill out "N/A" or ask for clarification.
