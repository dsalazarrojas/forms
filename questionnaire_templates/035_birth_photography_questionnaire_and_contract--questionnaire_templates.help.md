# Birth Photography Questionnaire And Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients to ensure a smooth and successful birth photography experience. It covers various aspects, including client details, birth information, photography preferences, service logistics, and contract agreements. Completing this form will help the photographer understand your needs and requirements, allowing for a more personalized and stress-free experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Start by reviewing and understanding the purpose and sections of the form.
2.  Begin with section 1, **Client Information**, where you'll provide your personal details such as your name, contact email, phone number, and mailing address.
3.  Move on to section 2, **Birth Information**, where you'll enter the expected due date, birth facility or location, and healthcare provider's name (if applicable).
4.  In section 3, **Photography Experience and Preferences**, you'll share your experience with a birth photographer, preferred photography style, and the moments you must capture during the birth process.
5.  Next, section 4, **Service Logistics**, covers the desired service coverage, any facility restrictions or special considerations, and who can be in the photos.
6.  Section 5, **Deliverables and Products**, allows you to choose your preferred number of final photos, how you want to receive them, and the editing style.
7.  In section 6, **Fees and Payment Terms**, you'll find the session fee, additional fees (if any), and the total service cost.
8.  Section 7, **Photo Rights and Usage**, explains the client's ownership of photos, commercial use, and social media sharing permission.
9.  Finally, **Contract Agreement** requires a confirmation of your understanding of the cancellation and refund policy and your agreement to all terms and conditions in this questionnaire and contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (id: 4): Your full name as you'd like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (id: 5): Contact email to discuss your photography session.
* **Phone Number** (id: 6): Contact phone number to reach out about your session.
* **Mailing Address** (id: 7): Full mailing address for any deliveries or communications.
* **Due Date** (id: 9): Expected due date for your child's birth.
* **Birth Facility or Location** (id: 10): Where the birth will take place (e.g., hospital, birth center, or home).
* **Healthcare Provider** (id: 11): Name of the healthcare provider (if applicable).
* **Have You Worked with a Birth Photographer Before** (id: 13): Previous experience with a birth photographer.
*   *Photographer Experience:*
	+ **True**: You've worked with a birth photographer before.
	+ **False**: This is your first time with a birth photographer.
	+ **Not Sure**: You're not sure if you've worked with a birth photographer before.
* **Photography Style Preferences** (id: 14): Choose the style that best fits your vision (e.g., Documentary and Natural, Posed and Artistic).
* **Must-Capture Moments** (id: 15): Select the most important moments you'd like the photographer to capture.
* **Vision for Birth Photography** (id: 16): Describe how you envision your birth photography experience.
*   *Example:* "I'd like natural, candid shots of my baby's first hour after birth."
* **Desired Service Coverage** (id: 18): Choose the hours or events you'd like the photographer to cover (e.g., Labor, Delivery, Immediate Postpartum).
*   *Service Coverage Options:*
	+ **Labor**: Labor and delivery.
	+ **Delivery**: Only delivery.
	+ **Immediate Postpartum**: Only immediate postpartum.
	+ **First Hour Bonding**: First hour after birth.
	+ **Hospital Photos**: Photos at the hospital after birth.
	+ **Extended Coverage**: Additional time for more photos.
* **Any Facility Restrictions or Special Considerations** (id: 19): Share any hospital policies or requests that might affect your photography session.
* **Who Can Be in Photos** (id: 20): Specify who can be in the photos (e.g., family, friends, medical staff).
* **Sensitive Moments** (id: 21): Share any moments you'd prefer not to be photographed (e.g., sensitive medical procedures).
*   *Example:* "I'd like to avoid photos during labor."
* **Preferred Number of Final Photos** (id: 23): Choose how many edited photos you'd like to receive.
*   *Options:* "20", "25", "30", "50", "Other".
* **How You Want to Receive Photos** (id: 24): Select your preferred delivery method (e.g., digital files, USB drive, online gallery).
*   *Options:* "Digital Files", "USB Drive", "Online Gallery", "Printed Album", "Individual Prints", "Canvas Prints".
*   *Example:* "I'd like digital files to review and share on social media."
* **Preferred Editing Style** (id: 25): Choose how much editing you'd like to have on your photos.
*   *Options:* "Minimal Natural Editing", "Moderate Enhancement", "Artistic Enhancement", "Film-Style Processing".
*   *Example:* "I'd like natural editing to preserve the original look."
* **Timeline for Photo Delivery** (id: 26): Select how quickly you'd like to receive your photos.
*   *Options:* "ASAP - Within 1 Week", "2-3 Weeks", "1 Month", "6-8 Weeks", "Custom Timeline".
*   *Example:* "I'd like to receive photos within 1 week after birth."
* **Session Fee** (id: 28): Enter the session fee for the photography service.
*   *Example:* "500"
* **Additional Fees** (id: 29): Share any additional fees for services like prints or albums.
*   *Example:* "Printing additional photos costs $50 each."
* **Total Service Cost** (id: 30): Enter the total amount due for the photography service.
* **Booking Deposit** (id: 31): Enter the amount due as a booking deposit.
*   *Example:* "200"
*   *Deposit Due Date:* The date the deposit is due.
*   *Example:* "Upon signing this contract."
* **Final Balance Due Date** (id: 33): Enter the date the final balance is due.
*   *Example:* "30 days after booking."
* **Accepted Payment Methods** (id: 34): Specify how you'd like to pay for the photography service.
*   *Options:* "Bank Transfer", "Check", "Credit Card".
*   *Example:* "I'd like to pay with a bank transfer."
* **Client Ownership of Photos** (id: 36): Choose who owns the rights to the photos.
*   *Options:* "Client Owns All Rights", "Shared Licensing", "Photographer Retains Copyright".
*   *Example:* "Client Owns All Rights"
*   *Commercial Use:* Whether the photographer can use your photos for their portfolio.
*   *Options:* "Yes, With Permission Only", "Yes, Without Permission", "No Commercial Use".
*   *Example:* "Yes, With Permission Only"
*   *Social Media Sharing Permission:* Whether you allow the photographer to share your photos on social media.
*   *Options:* "Yes, Full Permission", "Yes, With Watermark Only", "No Social Media Sharing".
*   *Example:* "Yes, Full Permission"
*   *I understand the Cancellation and Refund Policy* (id: 40): Confirm your understanding of the policy.
*   *Options:* "I Understand", "I Do Not Understand"
*   *I Agree to all Terms in this Questionnaire and Contract* (id: 41): Finalize your agreement to the terms and conditions.
*   *Options:* "I Agree", "I Do Not Agree"
