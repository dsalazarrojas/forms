# FAQ Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The FAQ template is a form designed to collect and store information about frequently asked questions and their corresponding answers, relevance, and contact information. This form will help users provide better customer service and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Start" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Answer each question in the corresponding field.
3. Select an option for questions that require multiple selections.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email address" field.
5. Click on the "Submit" button to submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Frequently asked questions** (`faq_1`, note, optional): This is a note field where you can provide a general description of the frequently asked questions.
* **What is this question?** (`faq_2`, text, optional): This is a text field where you can provide the actual question being asked.
* **Who is this question for?** (`faq_4`, select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to select the people or groups this question is relevant to.

* **How to contact us** (`faq_7`, text, optional): This is a text field where you can provide contact information for users who need to contact us.
* **Email address** (`faq_8`, email, optional): This is a field where you can enter the email address associated with this question.
* **What is the opening hours?** (`faq_9`, time, optional): This is a time field where you can provide the opening hours for this question.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete all the required fields to ensure your information is accurately recorded.
- Use a valid date format in the "When is this question relevant?" field.
- Enter a valid email address in the "Email address" field.
- This form should not be used to repeat information that already exists in our knowledge base.
