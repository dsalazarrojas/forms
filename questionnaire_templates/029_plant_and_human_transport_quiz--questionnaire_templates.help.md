# Plant And Human Transport Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data about the transportation habits of plants and humans. It will help researchers, scientists, or educators understand how plants and humans transport various substances, including water, chemicals, food, and waste. The form will help identify patterns and trends in their transportation behaviors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all that apply for each question. If you are unsure about the answer, choose "No" or "Unknown".
2. Answer all questions in the form. There are no required questions, so you can skip any questions that you do not know or are unsure about.
3. Use the provided options for questions with select types (e.g., select_multiple, select_one). For questions with text types, enter a brief description or answer in the provided field.
