# Tone Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tone Selection Survey is a questionnaire designed to help writers select the suitable tone for their text based on various contexts. The form will help writers adjust the tone of their writing to fit the specific use cases.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the form sections that correspond to your intended context(s) for writing.
2. Select the tone options that best match your text's intended audience and purpose.
3. Make your selections in each section, and the form will provide guidance on the tone selection.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **tone_selection_survey_form** (Required): This section is a note field for writers to provide a brief description of their text. Please provide a short paragraph or phrase that describes your text's intended purpose and audience.
* **tone_selection_survey_contexts_form** (Optional, Select Multiple): This section asks about the contexts in which your text will be used. Select all the contexts that apply to your text.
	+ 'Yes': The text will be used in formal situations, such as official documents or academic writing.
	+ 'No': The text will not be used in formal situations.
* **tone_selection_survey_context_form** (Optional, Select One): This section also asks about the contexts in which your text will be used. Select the one context that best matches your text's purpose.
	+ 'Yes': The text will be used in informal situations, such as social media or personal blogs.
	+ 'No': The text will not be used in informal situations.
* **tone_selection_survey_contexts_form_context** (Optional, Select Multiple): This section is another part of **tone_selection_survey_context_form** that asks for specific contexts. Select all the additional contexts that apply to your text.
* **tone_selection_survey_context_form_context** (Optional, Select One): This section is also part of **tone_selection_survey_context_form** that asks for a specific context. Select the one context that best matches your text's purpose.
* **tone_selection_survey_context_form_context_1** (Optional, Select One): This section is for another specific context that applies to your text.
* **tone_selection_survey_context_form_context_2** (Optional, Select One): This section is also for another specific context that applies to your text.
