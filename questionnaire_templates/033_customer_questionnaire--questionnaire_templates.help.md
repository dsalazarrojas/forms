# Customer Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer feedback and ratings on various aspects of your business, including customer service, packaging, customer experience, product quality, and overall experience. It will help us understand our customers' perceptions and opinions, allowing us to identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions to enhance our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the questions and options provided.
2. Select the answers that best describe your experience with our business.
3. Fill in the text field with any additional comments or feedback you may have.
4. Submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the ways in which you have encountered good or bad customer service. Options are 'Yes', 'No'.
  * If you answer 'No', you will be able to provide more details on how we can improve.
- **Packaging** (`packaging`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose all the options that describe your experience with our packaging. Options are 'Yes', 'No'.
  * If you answer 'No', you can provide more details on how we can improve.
- **Customer Experience** (`customer_experience`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the ways in which you have encountered good or bad customer experience. Options are 'Yes', 'No'.
  * If you answer 'No', you can provide more details on how we can improve.
- **Product Quality** (`product_quality`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose all the options that describe your experience with our product's quality. Options are 'Good', 'Poor'.
  * If you answer 'Poor', please provide more details on areas where we can improve.
- **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required/optional): Type any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience with our business.
- **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, text, required/optional): Provide more details on any positive or negative customer service experiences, if you answered 'No' to the first 'Customer Service' question.
- **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the ways in which you have had a good or bad overall experience. Options are 'Yes', 'No'.
  * If you answer 'No', you can provide more details on how we can improve.
- **Additional Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required/optional): Type any additional comments or suggestions you have about your experience with our business.
 
## Tips
* Be honest and provide detailed feedback to help us understand your experience.
* You may select multiple options for each question.
* You may provide more details in the text fields.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas on how we can improve, please provide them in the text fields.
