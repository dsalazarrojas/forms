# Housing Investment Criteria Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Housing Investment Criteria Survey is a form designed to collect information about your housing investment preferences. This form helps us understand your investment goals, risk tolerance, and other relevant criteria to provide personalized investment advice.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read each question carefully and answer based on your true investment preferences.
2. Use the options provided to choose the best fit for each question.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we can provide accurate investment advice.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Investor Preferences** (`investor_preferences`, `text`, optional)
	+ This is a text field where you can provide any additional information about your investment preferences or goals.
* **Property Location** (`property_location`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Please choose the location where you are interested in investing.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, `select_multiple`, optional)
	+ Select the type of property you are interested in investing in (e.g., residential, commercial, etc.).
* **Target Return** (`target_return`, `number`, optional)
	+ Enter the expected return on your investment (e.g., 5% or 10%).
* **Risk Tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`, `number`, optional)
	+ Enter your risk tolerance level (e.g., 1-5, where 1 is low risk and 5 is high risk).
* **Investment Term** (`investment_term`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Choose the length of time you are willing to invest for (e.g., short-term, mid-term, long-term).
* **Investment Amount** (`investment_amount`, `number`, optional)
	+ Enter the amount you are willing to invest (e.g., 1000, 5000, etc.).
* **Investment Frequency** (`investment_frequency`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Choose how often you plan to invest (e.g., monthly, quarterly, etc.).
* **Property Value** (`property_value`, `number`, optional)
	+ Enter the minimum property value you are willing to invest in (e.g., 500,000, 1,000,000 etc.).
* **Market Conditions** (`market_conditions`, `select_multiple`, optional)
	+ Select the market conditions you are interested in investing in (e.g., stable, growth, etc.).
* **Investment Objectives** (`investment_objectives`, `text`, optional)
	+ This is a text field where you can provide any additional information about your investment goals.
* **Investment Purpose** (`investment_purpose`, `select_multiple`, optional)
	+ Select the purpose of your investment (e.g., cash flow, appreciation, etc.).
* **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`, `number`, optional)
	+ Enter your risk assessment level (e.g., 1-5, where 1 is low risk and 5 is high risk).
* **Investment Period** (`investment_period`, `number`, optional)
	+ Enter the length of time you are willing to invest for (e.g., 1-5 years).
* **Property Type Frequency** (`property_type_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional)
	+ Select the type of property you are interested in investing in (e.g., single-family home, condo, etc.).
* **Market Trend** (`market_trend`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Choose the market trend you are interested in investing in (e.g., growth, stable, etc.).
* **Investment Goal** (`investment_goal`, `number`, optional)
	+ Enter your investment goal (e.g., 5% return or 1-year return).
* **Property Use Case** (`property_use_case`, `select_multiple`, optional)
	+ Select the type of property use case you are interested in investing in (e.g., rental, primary residence, etc.).
* **Market Condition Frequency** (`market_condition_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional)
	+ Select the market condition you are interested in investing in (e.g., high, medium, low).
* **Investment Fees** (`investment_fees`, `number`, optional)
	+ Enter the fees you are willing to pay on your investment (e.g., 1% or 2%).
* **Investment Fees Frequencies** (`investment_fees_frequencies`, `select_multiple`, optional)
	+ Select the frequencies of investment fees you are interested in (e.g., monthly, quarterly, etc.).
* **Investment Payout Frequency** (`investment_payout_frequency`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Choose the payout frequency you are interested in (e.g., monthly, quarterly, etc.).
* **Property Type Frequencies** (`property_type_frequencies`, `select_multiple`, optional)
	+ Select the type of property frequency you are interested in (e.g., single-family home, condo, etc.).
* **Investment Return** (`investment_return`, `number`, optional)
	+ Enter the expected return on your investment (e.g., 5% or 10%).
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `note`, optional)
	+ This is a text field where you can provide any additional information about your investment.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Use the options provided to ensure accurate and correct answers.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
