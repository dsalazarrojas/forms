# Athlete Mindset Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Athlete Mindset Assessment is a tool designed to help athletes evaluate their mental strengths and areas for improvement. This assessment is intended to provide athletes with a clear understanding of their current mental mindset and identify specific skills that can be developed to enhance their performance. By completing this assessment, athletes can gain valuable insights into their mental approach, set realistic goals, and develop strategies to improve their overall mental toughness.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Answer each question to the best of your ability.

1. Please provide honest and accurate responses to each question.
2. Select the most relevant option for each question.
3. Complete all required fields (marked with *).
4. Take your time and answer carefully, as this assessment is meant to be a thorough evaluation of your mental approach.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

**Athlete Full Name** (text)
Provide your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 2. Sport and Primary Position
