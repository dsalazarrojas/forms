# Client Personal Interest Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a questionnaire designed to collect information about a client's personal interests, goals, and preferences. It is an essential part of the onboarding process, helping both the client and the company get a better understanding of each other's needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name**, which is a required field.
2. Enter your **Email Address**, which is also a required field.
3. In the **What do you enjoy doing in your free time?** field, write a brief description of your hobbies and interests.
4. If you have any **dietary restrictions or food allergies**, please list them in the **Any dietary restrictions or food allergies we should be aware of?** field.
5. Select your preferred way to receive **updates or gifts** in the **Preferred way to receive updates or gifts** dropdown menu. You can choose from Email, Text, Phone Call, or In-Person Meeting.
6. Choose your preferred **time of day for communication** in the **Best time of day for communication** dropdown menu. You can select from Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
7. In the **Any favorite books, podcasts, or influencers you follow?** field, list your favorite books, podcasts, or influencers.
8. If there's anything else you'd like us to know to personalize your experience with us, please write it in the **Is there anything else we should know to personalize your experience with us?** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Please enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address where we can contact you.
* **What do you enjoy doing in your free time?**: Write a brief description of your hobbies and interests.
* **Any dietary restrictions or food allergies we should be aware of?**: List any dietary restrictions or food allergies we should be aware of.
* **What is your primary professional goal for this year?**: Write a brief description of your primary professional goal for this year.
* **What is a personal milestone you are currently working toward?**: Write a brief description of a personal milestone you are currently working toward.
* **Preferred way to receive updates or gifts**: Choose how you'd like to receive updates or gifts from us.
* **Best time of day for communication**: Choose the best time of day for us to contact you.
* **Any favorite books, podcasts, or influencers you follow?**: List your favorite books, podcasts, or influencers.
* **Is there anything else we should know to personalize your experience with us?**: If there's anything else you'd like us to know to personalize your experience with us, please let us know.
