# pre-training-covid-19-screening - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pre-training Covid-19 screening form is used to identify potential risks and take necessary precautions to ensure your health and safety before attending events or traveling.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2. Choose the options that best describe your situation.
3. Make sure to answer every question, even if it seems unrelated or trivial.
4. If you are unsure about any question, please reach out to our support team for guidance.
