# Nutrition And Lifestyle Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a client's nutrition and lifestyle habits to help health professionals assess their overall health and create a personalized plan to improve their well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question as accurately and thoroughly as possible. You may need to refer to your medical records or consult with your healthcare provider if you have any doubts or concerns.
