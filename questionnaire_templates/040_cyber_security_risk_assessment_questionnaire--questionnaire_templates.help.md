The Cyber Security Risk Assessment Questionnaire is designed to gather information on potential risks to an organization's cyber security. To complete this form, follow these steps:

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data on potential cyber security risks, including the likelihood and impact of these risks, in order to provide a comprehensive risk assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **User Input** (1): This field is for any additional information you want to provide about the risk. Please enter any relevant details in this text field.
2. **Risk Areas** (2): Select all the risk areas that apply to the potential risk. This will help us understand the scope of the risk.
3. **Risk Level** (3): Indicate the level of risk (Low, Medium, or High) for each selected risk area.
4. **Description** (4): Provide a brief description of the potential risk.
5. **Mitigation** (5): If applicable, enter any planned mitigation strategies for the risk.
6. **Frequency** (6): Indicate how often this risk is likely to occur (Low, Medium, or High).
7. **Severity** (7): Indicate the severity of the potential risk (Low, Medium, or High).
8. **Risk Assessment ID** (8): This field is for internal use only, please leave it blank.
9. **Date** (9): Enter the date of the risk assessment.
10. **Notes** (10): Provide any additional notes or comments about the risk.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input**: This field is for any additional information you want to provide about the risk.
* **Risk Areas**: Select all the risk areas that apply to the potential risk.
* **Risk Level**: Indicate the level of risk (Low, Medium, or High) for each selected risk area.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the potential risk.
* **Mitigation**: If applicable, enter any planned mitigation strategies for the risk.
* **Frequency**: Indicate how often this risk is likely to occur (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Severity**: Indicate the severity of the potential risk (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Risk Assessment ID**: This field is for internal use only, please leave it blank.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the risk assessment.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments about the risk.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required. If you're unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or the IT department for guidance.
