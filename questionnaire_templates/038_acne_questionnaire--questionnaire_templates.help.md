# Acne Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This Acne Questionnaire is designed to collect information about a patient's acne condition, including their demographics, skin condition details, and lifestyle habits. The form aims to gather comprehensive data to help healthcare professionals assess the patient's condition accurately and provide the most effective treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Provide your age in the "Age" field.
3. Select your gender from the options provided in the "Gender" field.
4. Describe your skin condition in the "Skin Condition Details" field.
5. Choose the severity of your acne in the "Acne Severity" field.
6. Select all affected areas of your skin in the "Affected Areas" field.
7. Provide the duration of your condition in months in the "Duration of Condition (Months)" field.
8. Select any previous treatments you have tried in the "Previous Treatments" field.
9. Indicate whether you have a family history of acne in the "Family History of Acne" field.
10. Describe your current skincare routine in the "Current Skincare Routine" field.
11. List any known allergies, especially medication or topical allergies, in the "Known Allergies" field.
12. Select your skin type in the "Skin Type" field.
13. Mention any additional medical concerns in the "Additional Medical Concerns" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (full_name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Age** (age, number, required): Provide your age in years.
* **Gender** (gender, select_one, required): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Skin Condition Details** (condition_header, note, required): Describe your skin condition in detail.
* **Acne Severity** (acne_severity, select_one, required): Choose the severity of your acne from the options provided.
* **Affected Areas** (affected_areas, select_multiple, required): Select all areas of your skin affected by acne.
* **Duration of Condition (Months)** (duration_of_condition_months, number, required): Provide the duration of your condition in months.
* **Previous Treatments** (previous_treatments, select_multiple, optional): Select any previous treatments you have tried for your acne.
* **Family History of Acne** (family_history_of_acne, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have a family history of acne.
* **Current Skincare Routine** (current_skincare_routine, text, optional): Describe your current skincare routine.
* **Known Allergies** (known_allergies, text, optional): List any known allergies, especially medication or topical allergies.
* **Skin Type** (skin_type, select_one, required): Select your skin type from the options provided.
* **Additional Medical Concerns** (additional_medical_concerns, text, optional): Mention any additional medical concerns.
