# Jessicas Color Match Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Jessicas Color Match Form is a simple, user-facing form that collects user information and color preferences. It's designed to be easy to complete and understand.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user information in the "Enter User Information" field.
2. Select your favorite color from the "Which color do you like?" dropdown list.
3. For the "Select Multiple Colors" field, choose as many colors as you like from the list.
4. If you have a specific date in mind, select it from the "Select Date" field. This field is optional.
5. Choose a time that suits you best from the "Select Time" field. This field is also optional.
6. In the "Note Entry" field, add any additional comments or notes you'd like to share.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email Entry" field.
8. For the "Phone Number" field, enter your phone number (optional).
9. Select multiple colors from the "Select Multiple" field.
10. Choose a single color from the "Select One" field.
11. For the second "Select Date" field, select a date (optional).
12. Finally, choose a single color from the "Select One 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Enter User Information** (User Information, text, required)
Enter your name, address, or any other relevant user information.

### 2. **Which color do you like?** (Which color do you like?, select_one, required)
Select one of the following colors: Blue, Red, Green, Yellow, Purple, or Orange.

### 3. **Select Multiple Colors** (Select Multiple Colors, select_multiple, required)
Choose as many colors as you like from the list: Blue, Red, Green, Yellow, Purple, Orange, Black, or White.

### 4. **Select Date** (Select Date, date, optional)
Select a specific date if you have a particular one in mind. This field is optional and not required to complete the form.

### 5. **Select Time** (Select Time, time, optional)
Choose a time that suits your needs. This field is optional and not required to complete the form.

### 6. **Note Entry** (Note Entry, note, optional)
Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to share.

### 7. **Email Entry** (Email Entry, email, required)
Enter your valid email address.

### 8. **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, optional)
Enter your phone number (optional).

### 9. **Select Multiple** (Select Multiple, select_multiple, required)
Choose as many colors as you like from the list: Blue, Red, Green, Yellow, Purple, Orange, Black, or White.

### 10. **Select One** (Select One, select_one, required)
Choose a single color from the list: Blue, Red, Green, Yellow, Purple, or Orange.

### 11. **Select Date (2)** (Select Date (2), select_one, optional)
Select a date from the list (optional).

### 12. **Select One 2** (Select One 2, select_one, optional)
Choose a single color from the list: Blue, Red, Green, Yellow, Purple, or Orange (optional).
