## sedentary_behaviour_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The sedentary behaviour questionnaire is designed to assess an individual's sedentary behaviour and related habits. This form will help us understand your daily activities and habits that may be contributing to your sedentary behaviour. Please answer these questions honestly to provide a clear picture of your daily habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Select the correct option for each question.
3. Fill in any additional information required in the text fields.
4. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sedentary Behaviour Questionnaire Questions** (`sedentary_behaviour_questionnaire_questions`, select_multiple): Choose all that apply regarding your sedentary behaviour:
	+ Yes: Do you have sedentary behaviour (e.g., sitting or lying down for most of the day)?
	+ No: Do you not have sedentary behaviour?
* **Sedentary Behaviour Inactivity** (`sedentary_behaviour_questionnaire_inactivity`, number): Please enter the number of hours spent in sedentary behaviour per day.
* **Sedentary Behaviour Frequency** (`sedentary_behaviour_questionnaire_frequency`, text): Describe your sedentary behaviour frequency (e.g., daily, weekly, rarely).
* **Sedentary Behaviour Duration** (`sedentary_behaviour_questionnaire_duration`, number): Please enter the average duration of sedentary behaviour per day.
* **Sedentary Behaviour Days** (`sedentary_behaviour_questionnaire_days`, select_one): Choose how many days a week do you engage in sedentary behaviour:
	+ Yes: How many days a week?
	+ No: Do you not engage in sedentary behaviour?
* **Sedentary Behaviour Days of the Week** (`sedentary_behaviour_questionnaire_days_of_week`, time): Please select the days you spend most of your time in sedentary behaviour.
* **Sedentary Behaviour Comments** (`sedentary_behaviour_questionnaire_comments`, note): Add any additional comments about your sedentary behaviour.
* **Sedentary Behaviour Email** (`sedentary_behaviour_questionnaire_email`, email): Enter your email address (if you'd like to receive follow-up questions or updates).
* **Sedentary Behaviour Phone** (`sedentary_behaviour_questionnaire_phone`, text): Enter your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted).

Note: Some fields may be optional or have multiple options. Answering these questions honestly will help us better understand your habits and provide valuable insights into your sedentary behaviour.
