# Vacation Package Preferences Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a traveler's preferences for a vacation package. The form is split into several pages, each with its own set of fields that will help us understand the traveler's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Traveler Details" page with your name, email, and phone number.
2. On the "Destination" page, select the type of destination you are interested in (Beach, City, Mountain, or Other).
3. On the "Accommodation" page, select the type of accommodation you prefer (Hotel, Hostel, or Other).
4. On the "Travel Date" page, select the dates of travel.
5. On the "Payment" page, select the payment method you prefer (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal).
6. On the "Additional Information" page, enter any additional notes or information about your trip.
