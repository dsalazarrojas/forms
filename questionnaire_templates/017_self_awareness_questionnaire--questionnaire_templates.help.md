# Self Awareness Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Self Awareness Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information about your thoughts, feelings, and experiences. It is intended to help you better understand yourself and gain insights into your mental well-being. Please take a few minutes to complete this form, and provide honest and accurate answers to each question.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. Type your answers in the provided text boxes.
3. You can answer each question in as much detail as you like, but please keep your answers concise and focused on the topic.
4. Make sure to complete all the questions before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How Do You Feel Today?**: This question is about your current emotional state. Please describe how you feel at this moment, using words or phrases that come to mind.
* **What Makes You Happy**: Think about things that bring joy and happiness to your life. Please list or describe activities, events, or people that make you feel happy.
* **What Makes You Unhappy**: Reflect on situations or events that may be causing you distress or discomfort. Try to identify specific things that are affecting your mood.
* **Feelings About Your Life**: This question is about your overall feelings about your life. Please describe any thoughts, concerns, or worries you may have.
* **Self Awareness Questionnaire 2-20**: These questions are similar to the ones above, with slight variations in wording. Please answer each one in a similar way, providing honest and detailed answers.

Note: Since the form has many identical questions with slight variations, please refer to the above explanations for each type of question. Each question is designed to help you gain more insight into your thoughts, feelings, and experiences.

## Tips
* Be honest and authentic in your answers.
* Take your time to think about each question carefully.
* If you need help or support, you can contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
