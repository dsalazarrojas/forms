# Student Learning Experience Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Learning Experience Questionnaire is a form designed to collect detailed information about a student's learning experience, including demographic information and specific details about their learning style, teacher, and school environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your school's name in the "School Name" field.
4. Select your school year in the "School Year" field.
5. Choose your student grade level from the available options.
6. Enter your teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
7. Enter your teacher's email address in the "Teacher Email" field.
8. Enter your teacher's phone number in the "Teacher Phone Number" field.
9. Choose your student's gender from the available options.
10. Enter your student's birthdate in the "Student Birthdate" field.
11. Enter your parent's name in the "Parent Name" field.
12. Enter your parent's email address in the "Parent Email" field.
13. Enter your parent's phone number in the "Parent Phone Number" field.
14. Choose your student's learning style from the available options.
15. Enter the number of hours you work per week in the "Student Hours Per Week" field.
16. Enter the subject you're learning from your teacher in the "Teacher Subject" field.
17. Enter the number of hours your teacher works per week in the "Teacher Hours Per Week" field.
18. Enter your teacher's salary in the "Teacher Salary" field.
19. Enter your student's salary in the "Student Salary" field.
20. Enter your teacher's experience in years in the "Teacher Experience" field.
21. Choose your school's funding source from the available options.
22. Enter any assigned tool or resource your teacher has given you in the "Assigned Tool" field.
23. Enter the file name or location for your output file in the "Output File" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it is known by your friends and family.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it is known by your friends and family.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your school.
* **School Year** (`school_year`, `number`, required): Enter the current school year you are in.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, `number`, required): Select your current grade level from the available options.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, `text`, required): Enter your teacher's name.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, `email`, required): Enter your teacher's email address.
* **Teacher Phone Number** (`teacher_phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your teacher's phone number.
* **Student Gender** (`student_gender`, `select_one`, required): Choose your student's gender from the available options.
* **Student Birthdate** (`student_birthdate`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required): Enter your parent's name.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, `email`, required): Enter your parent's email address.
* **Parent Phone Number** (`parent_phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your parent's phone number.
* **Student Learning Style** (`student_learning_style`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the way you learn best from the available options.
* **Student Attendance** (`student_attendance`, `number`, required): Enter the number of hours you attend school per week.
* **Student Hours Per Week** (`student_hours_per_week`, `number`, required): Enter the number of hours you work per week.
* **Teacher Subject** (`teacher_subject`, `text`, required): Enter the subject your teacher is teaching you.
* **Teacher Hours Per Week** (`teacher_hours_per_week`, `number`, required): Enter the number of hours your teacher works per week.
* **Teacher Salary** (`teacher_salary`, `number`, required): Enter your teacher's salary.
* **Student Salary** (`student_salary`, `number`, required): Enter your student's salary.
* **Teacher Experience** (`teacher_experience`, `number`, required): Enter your teacher's experience in years.
* **School Funding Source** (`school_funding_source`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your school's funding source from the available options.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required): Enter any assigned tool or resource your teacher has given you.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, `text`, required): Enter the file name or location for your output file.
