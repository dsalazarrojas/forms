# Monster Personality Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Monster Personality Quiz is a fun and interactive tool that helps users discover what kind of monster they would be. By answering a series of questions, users can learn which personality traits and preferences they align with.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin with the "Introduction" section, where you can read about what the quiz is all about.
2. In the "monster_name" section, enter your name (or your monster's name).
3. In the "Personality Traits" section, select one personality trait that you think best describes you.
4. In the "Favorite Food" section, select all your favorite foods.
5. In the "Favorite Activity" section, select all your favorite activities.
6. In the "Personality Type" section, select one personality type that you think best describes you.
7. If you'd like to, enter your email, phone number, and a specific date and time in the "Email", "Phone", and "Date/Time" sections.
