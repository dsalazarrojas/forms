# life_coaching_goal_planning_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you set and plan your personal goals in a life coaching context. It will gather information on your goals, areas where you need coaching, motivation levels, challenges, current and desired progress, goal setting dates, and follow-up planning requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully and make sure you have understood the purpose and instructions.
2. Answer each field as accurately and truthfully as possible.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields (indicated by asterisk *).
4. For fields with options, select the most relevant answer that applies to you.
5. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your life coach for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Goals** (`client_goals`, `text`, *: false*): This field is where you can describe your short-term and long-term goals. Please write down your goals in a clear and concise manner.
* **Coaching Areas** (`coaching_areas`, `select_multiple`, *: false*): Check the areas where you need coaching or guidance. This will help your life coach tailor their support to your specific needs.
* **Motivation Levels** (`motivation_levels`, `number`, *: false*): Please rate your motivation level for each of your goals. A higher rating indicates a greater level of motivation.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, `text`, *: false*): Describe the challenges you face in achieving your goals.
* **Current Progress** (`current_progress`, `select_one`, *: false*): Indicate your current level of progress towards your goals.
* **Desired Progress** (`desired_progress`, `select_one`, *: false*): Indicate your desired level of progress towards your goals.
* **Goal Setting Dates** (`goal_setting_dates`, `date`, *: false*): Enter the dates when you plan to set and review your progress.
* **Follow Up Planning** (`follow_up_planning`, `select_multiple`, *: false*): Check the frequency and method of follow-up planning you prefer.

Note: The required fields are marked with *asterisk*. Please make sure to complete them as they are essential for the coaching process.
