# Family Dynamics Self-Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Dynamics Self-Assessment Survey is designed to help families gain a better understanding of their relationships, communication styles, conflict resolution, and overall dynamics. This survey will provide valuable insights into family members' perceptions and interactions, promoting healthy family relationships and dynamics.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Household Information (2)**: Please describe your family's living situation, including the number of people and the type of accommodation you use.
*   **Family Relationships (3)**: Select all the family relationships you have, such as Mother, Father, Spouse, Grandparent, Sibling, or Child.
*   **Communication Styles (4)**: Think about how you typically interact with each other in your family, and select the style that best describes your interactions: Avoidant, Passive, Assertive, or Aggressive.
*   **Conflict Styles (5)**: Consider a time when you disagreed with family members, and select the style that best describes your approach: Collaborative, Avoidant, Aggressive, or Passive.
*   **Family Roles (6)**: Indicate who performs which tasks and responsibilities in your family, selecting from Primary Caregiver, Secondary Caregiver, or Equal Share.
*   **Family Values (7)**: Describe the most important things in your family, such as honesty, love, or respect.
*   **Family Goals (8)**: Write about the goals you want to achieve as a family.
*   **Communication Goals (9)**: Indicate the goals you want to achieve through communication within your family.
*   **Communication Challenges (10)**: Describe the challenges you face in communicating with each other.
*   **Emotional Intelligence (11)**: Think about how you handle emotions in your family, and note your approach.
*   **Problem Solving (12)**: Select how you approach problems in your family: Collaborative, Dominant, or Avoidant.
*   **Decision Making (13)**: Indicate how you make decisions in your family, selecting from Collaborative, Dominant, or Avoidant.
*   **Family Dynamics (14)**: Rate your satisfaction with your family dynamics, choosing from Satisfactory, Unsatisfactory, or Undecided.
*   **Family Stress (15)**: Describe the sources of stress in your family.
*   **Relationship Quality (16)**: Evaluate the quality of your relationships within the family, selecting from Very Good, Good, Fair, Bad, or Very Bad.
*   **Conclusion (17)**: Thank the survey participants for their time, and thank the user for participating.
*   **Contact Us (18)**: If you have further questions or comments, please contact us at [email address].
*   **Additional Comments (19)**: Write any additional comments about the survey.
*   **Family History (20)**: Indicate how long you have been together as a family.
*   **Family Size (21)**: Describe the number of people in your family.
*   **Family Age (22)**: Indicate your current age, and the age of other family members.
*   **Family Income (23)**: Describe your family's monthly income.
*   **Family Vehicles (24)**: Indicate the number of vehicles your family owns or uses.
*   **Family Education (25)**: Select the highest level of education achieved in your family.
*   **Family Culture (26)**: Describe the core values that define your family culture.
*   **Family Language (27)**: Indicate the language spoken in your home.
*   **Family Occupation (28)**: Describe the primary occupations of family members.
*   **Family Residence (29)**: Select the type of accommodation your family uses, such as House or Apartment.

Please note that this guide is based on the provided YAML fields and should be reviewed and updated according to any changes or updates made to the form.
