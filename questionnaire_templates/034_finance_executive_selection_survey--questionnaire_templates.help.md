# Finance Executive Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Finance Executive Selection Survey is a form designed to gather feedback and evaluations of candidates for executive positions within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the candidate's qualifications and experience based on the provided information.
2. Complete the sections as follows:
	* Candidate Selection Survey: Summarize your observations and thoughts on the candidate's qualifications and experience.
	* Feedback Form: Share any specific feedback on the candidate's strengths and weaknesses.
	* Evaluation Summary: Provide a brief summary of your overall impression of the candidate.
	* Evaluation Questions: Respond to each question based on your evaluation, providing details on the candidate's skills and experience.
	* Recommendations: Share any recommendations for the candidate's next steps, if applicable.
	* Evaluation Scale: This section is for selecting a scale that represents your evaluation of the candidate.
	* Evaluation Rating: Provide a numerical rating (1-5) that represents your evaluation.
	* Evaluation Comments: Add any additional comments or feedback on the evaluation.
	* Candidate Name: Enter the name of the candidate being evaluated.
	* Candidate Position: Enter the position the candidate is being evaluated for.
	* Evaluation Score: Enter a score out of 5 that represents your overall evaluation of the candidate.
	* Evaluation Score 1, Evaluation Score 2: Enter scores out of 5 for specific evaluation scores.
	* Evaluation Scale 2: Select a scale that represents your evaluation.
