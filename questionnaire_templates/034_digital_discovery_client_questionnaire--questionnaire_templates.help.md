# Digital Discovery Client Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Discovery Client Questionnaire is a crucial step in the digital transformation process, helping us understand your business needs and challenges. This form will guide you through a series of questions to ensure we have a clear understanding of your requirements. Please answer the questions as accurately as possible, as this information will help us provide effective solutions for your project.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form in one of the provided pages.
- Make sure to enter your information accurately and completely.
- If a question has a specific format (e.g., email), use the correct format (e.g., @example.com).
- You can save your progress and come back later to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as you would like it to be used in communication.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as you would like it to be used in communication.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your company email address in the format of `@example.com`.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number without any country codes or symbols (e.g., 1234567890).

### Tips
* Make sure to double-check your email address for any typos or formatting issues.
* If you have any difficulties or questions while filling the form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
