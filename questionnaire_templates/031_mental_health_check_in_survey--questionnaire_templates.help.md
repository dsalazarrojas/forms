# Mental Health Check In Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Check In Survey is designed to help individuals assess their mental health and well-being. This form is a tool for self-evaluation, allowing users to provide insight into their feelings, experiences, and concerns about their mental health. It's essential for those who may be struggling with mental health issues or need guidance on how to maintain a healthy mental state.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer honestly to the best of your abilities.
2. For questions with multiple options, select the one that best describes your current situation.
3. If you have any additional information or comments you'd like to share, use the text fields to provide more context.
4. Ensure you fill out all required fields to get the most out of the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, optional): This is an open-ended question where you can briefly introduce yourself and provide any necessary context before answering the survey questions.
* **Feeling Today** (`question1`, `number`, optional): Rate your current feelings on a scale, where 1 is "Not at all" and 5 is "Very much."
* **Feeling Yesterday** (`question2`, `number`, optional): Rate your feelings from yesterday on the same scale as above.
* **Overall Feeling** (`question3`, `number`, optional): Describe your overall feelings on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not at all" and 5 is "Very much."
* **Mental Health Concerns** (`question4`, `text`, optional): Share any mental health concerns or issues you're currently facing.
* **How Do You Feel?** (`question5`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe how you're feeling at the moment.
* **Have You Considered Self-Harm?** (`question6`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you've considered self-harm, with options "True," "False," or "Sometimes."
* **Have You Talked to Someone?** (`question7`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all the people you've talked to about your mental health concerns, with options "True," "False," and "Prefer not to say."
* **Support Level** (`question8`, `select_one`, optional): Rate how much support you have, with options "Very much," "Somewhat," and "Not at all."
* **Support Type** (`question9`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of support you have, with options "Family/Friends," "Professional," and "Other."
* **Other Support** (`question10`, `text`, optional): If you've received support from someone or something else, describe it here.
* **Additional Information** (`question11`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional context or comments related to your mental health.
* **Follow-up** (`question13`, `time`, optional): Choose a follow-up time for your next mental health check-in.
* **Scheduled Check-in** (`question14`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate if you've scheduled a mental health check-in, with options "True," "False," and "Prefer not to say."
* **Next Check-in Date** (`question15`, `date`, optional): Select a date for your next check-in, using the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Next Check-in Time** (`question16`, `time`, optional): Choose a time for your next check-in.
* **Follow-up Time** (`question17`, `time`, optional): Indicate a time for your follow-up.
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`question18`, `text`, optional): Describe how often you'd like to have follow-ups.
* **Contact Info** (`question19`, `text`, optional): Provide any necessary contact information for your mental health check-ins.
* **Notes** (`question20`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments for your mental health provider.
* **Email** (`question21`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address, if you'd like to be contacted via email.
* **Additional Comments** (`question22`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your mental health.
* **Note** (`question23`, `note`, optional): This field is not currently used.
