# Rider Host Home Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from hosts who have registered to participate in the host program. This information will be used to create a database of hosts with their respective details, availability, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the required information.
2. Fill in the form with accurate and complete information.
3. Please note that all fields marked as "required" must be filled in to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rider details**: Provide a short description of yourself as a host, including any relevant experience or skills you have.
* **Host details**: Enter your name and contact information so that riders can reach out to you directly.
* **Contact info**: This field is used to store your email address or phone number so that riders can contact you about hosting opportunities.
* **Emergency details**: In the unlikely event of an emergency, please provide information about your emergency contact person and their contact information.
* **Household details**: Describe the household you live in, including the number of rooms, amenities, and any other relevant features.
* **Host availability**: Indicate your availability for hosting riders, including dates and times you are available.
* **Visit details**: Provide information about your visiting preferences, including frequency and any specific times you are available.
* **Host neighbours**: If you have any neighbours who may be affected by the presence of a rider, please list their names and contact information.
* **Host vehicle**: If you have a vehicle that you will use to transport the rider, please provide the make, model, and year.
* **Additional comments**: Use this field to provide any additional information that may be helpful for riders, such as parking or storage options.
* **Host agreement**: Check the box if you are willing to host a rider. If not, please explain in the additional comments field.
* **Rider type**: Select the type of riders you are willing to host, including local, visiting, or both.

## Tips
* Please double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact the form administrators at form@gic.mx.
