# Mortality Themes Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mortality Themes Assessment is a self-assessment tool designed to evaluate your perspectives, fears, and beliefs related to mortality. This form will help you understand your own mortality themes, which may influence your attitudes and behaviors towards death and dying.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the form by following the instructions provided in each section.
2. Select the options that best align with your perspectives, fears, and beliefs related to mortality.
3. Be honest and accurate in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Perspectives** (`participant_perspective`, text, optional)
	+ This field asks you to describe your perspectives on mortality, including your views on the nature of death and its impact on individuals and society.
	+ Please provide a brief written response that reflects your personal beliefs and attitudes towards mortality.
* **Mortality Themes** (`mortality_themes`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ This field asks you to choose the themes that resonate with you most when thinking about mortality.
	+ Select all that apply from the options provided:
		- Threat
		- Loss
		- Transcendence
		- Immortality
		- Legacy
		- Self-Discovery
		- Acceptance
		- Acceptance/Rejection
		- Existential
* **Mortality Fears** (`mortality_fears`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ This field asks you to identify the fears that you associate with mortality.
	+ Select all that apply from the options provided:
		- Fear of the Unknown
		- Fear of Pain
		- Fear of Loss
		- Fear of Isolation
		- Fear of Abandonment

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* Select all options that apply to you.
* If you are unsure about an option, choose "Other" and provide a brief explanation.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
