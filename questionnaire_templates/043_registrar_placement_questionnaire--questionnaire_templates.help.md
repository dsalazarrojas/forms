# Registrar Placement Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a student's placement status within a school or institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name.
2. Select the school year.
3. Enter the student's ID.
4. Select the student's grade level.
5. Enter the school ID.
6. Select the student and school programs.
7. Choose the student's status.
8. Choose the school status.
9. Select the placement date and time.
10. Enter the reason for placement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's name as it is typically recorded in the institution's records.
* **School Year** (`school_year`, number, optional): Select the current school year from the options provided.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): Enter the student's unique identifier as assigned by the institution.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, number, optional): Select the student's current grade level from the available options.
* **School ID** (`school_id`, text, optional): Enter the school's unique identifier as assigned by the institution.
* **Student Program** (`student_program`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the student is enrolled in a specific program.
* **School Program** (`school_program`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable programs that the school is offering.
* **Placement Date** (`placement_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the student's placement.
* **Placement Time** (`placement_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the student's placement.
* **Placement Reason** (`placement_reason`, text, optional): Enter a brief reason for the student's placement.
* **Student Status** (`student_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the current status of the student (Active or Inactive).
* **School Status** (`school_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the current status of the school (Active or Inactive).
