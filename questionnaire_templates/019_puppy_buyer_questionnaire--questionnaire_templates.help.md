# Puppy Buyer Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a puppy's details, seller, and buyer. It is used to gather data about the puppy's characteristics and medical conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the puppy's details, including name, breed, age, weight, height, and diet.
2. Enter the seller's name, email, and phone number.
3. Enter the buyer's name, email, and phone number.
4. Select the puppy's size, activity level, socialization, trainability, behavior, health, and special requirements.
5. Select any sensitivities or allergies the puppy may have.
6. Enter any medications the puppy is currently taking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Puppy name** (`puppy_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the puppy.
* **Puppy breed** (`puppy_breed`, `text`, required: false): Enter the breed of the puppy.
* **Puppy age** (`puppy_age`, `number`, required: false): Enter the age of the puppy.
* **Puppy weight** (`puppy_weight`, `number`, required: false): Enter the weight of the puppy.
* **Puppy height** (`puppy_height`, `number`, required: false): Enter the height of the puppy.
* **Diet** (`puppy_diet`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the puppy's diet (raw, kibble, canned food, or mix).
* **Puppy size** (`puppy_size`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the puppy's size (small, medium, or large).
* **Puppy activity level** (`puppy_activity_level`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the puppy's activity level (low, medium, or high).
* **Puppy socialization** (`puppy_socialization`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the puppy's socialization level (low, medium, or high).
* **Puppy trainability** (`puppy_trainability`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the puppy's trainability level (low, medium, or high).
* **Puppy behavior** (`puppy_behavior`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the puppy's behavior (good, medium, or bad).
* **Puppy health** (`puppy_health`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the puppy's health (good, fair, or bad).
* **Seller name** (`seller`, `text`, required: false): Enter the seller's name.
* **Seller email** (`seller_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the seller's email.
* **Seller phone** (`seller_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the seller's phone number.
* **Buyer name** (`buyer`, `text`, required: false): Enter the buyer's name.
* **Buyer email** (`buyer_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the buyer's email.
* **Buyer phone** (`buyer_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the buyer's phone number.
* **Puppy sensitivities** (`puppy_sensitivities`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select any sensitivities the puppy may have (food, environment, or people).
* **Puppy allergies** (`puppy_allergies`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select any allergies the puppy may have (food, environment, or people).
* **Puppy medications** (`puppy_medications`, `note`, required: false): Enter any medications the puppy is currently taking.
* **Puppy special requirements** (`puppy_special_requirements`, `note`, required: false): Enter any special requirements the puppy may have.
* **Puppy behavior** (`puppy_behavior`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the puppy's behavior (good, medium, or bad).
* **Puppy health** (`puppy_health`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the puppy's health (good, fair, or bad).
