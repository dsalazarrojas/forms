# Graduate Student Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about graduate students, specifically their personal details and their experience in a program. The data collected will be used to improve the program and its services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Select your program participation in the "Program" field.
6. Select your program type in the "Program type" field.
7. Select your semester in the "Semester" field.
8. Enter your GPA in the "G.P.A." field.
9. Enter your expected graduation year in the "Graduation year" field.
10. Enter your expected graduation date in the "Graduation date" field.
11. Provide any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
12. Select your level of satisfaction in the "Satisfaction" field.
13. If you have any suggestions, type them in the "Suggestions" field.
14. Select your level of satisfaction with the program in the "Program satisfaction" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Program** (`program`, Select One, Optional): Select whether you are participating in the program.
* **Program type** (`program_type`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the type of program you are in (e.g., part-time, full-time).
* **Semester** (`semester`, Select One, Optional): Select your semester (e.g., fall, spring, summer).
* **G.P.A.** (`gpa`, Number, Optional): Enter your current GPA.
* **Graduation year** (`graduation_year`, Text, Optional): Enter your expected graduation year.
* **Graduation date** (`graduation_date`, Time, Optional): Enter your expected graduation date.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, Select One, Optional): Select how satisfied you are with the program.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, Note, Optional): If you have any suggestions, type them in this field.
* **Program satisfaction** (`program_satisfaction`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select how satisfied you are with the program.
