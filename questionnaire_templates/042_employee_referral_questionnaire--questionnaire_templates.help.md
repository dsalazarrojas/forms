### ### Employee Referral Questionnaire - Help Guide

### Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about an employee's job opening, allowing you to effectively refer a colleague for a new opportunity.

### How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the following information:
    *   **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee being referred.
    *   **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the position being referred.
    *   **Department**: Enter the department where the position is located.
    *   **Job Status**: Select the current status of the job (Active, Inactive, or Probationary).
    *   **Job Type**: Select the type of job (Full Time, Part Time, or Contract).
    *   **Job Location**: Enter the location of the job.
    *   **Job Description**: Enter a brief description of the job.
    *   **Manager Name**: Enter the name of the manager for the position.
    *   **Job Level**: Select the level of the job (Entry Level, Mid Level, or Senior Level).
    *   **Job Function**: Enter the function of the job.
    *   **Job Requirements**: Enter any specific requirements for the job.
    *   **Referral Date**: Enter the date of the referral.
    *   **Manager Approval**: Select the status of manager approval (Approved, Not Approved, or Pending).
    *   **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the referral.
2.  Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting it.
