# Income Contribution Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a household's income contribution. It's intended for households to assess their income and provide an estimate of their financial status. The data collected will help in making informed decisions and creating a better understanding of the household's financial situation.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, start by filling out the household income section, which includes entering your household's annual or monthly income.
- Next, select the type of income your household receives from the options provided.
- Finally, review and submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Household Income** (`household_income`, text, optional): Enter your household's income in the provided text field.
* **Income Type** (`income_type`, select multiple, optional): Select all the types of income your household receives from the options provided. If unsure, you can select all that apply.
* **Annual Income** (`annual_income`, number, optional): Enter your household's annual income.
* **Monthly Income** (`monthly_income`, number, optional): Enter your household's monthly income.

## Tips
- Make sure to be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, don't hesitate to ask for help.
- Review your answers carefully before submitting to ensure they accurately reflect your household's situation.
