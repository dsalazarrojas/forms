# Social Connection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to provide insights into your social connections and relationships. Please answer the questions honestly to get the most accurate results.

## How To Complete This Form

### General Instructions

* Answer each question with the most accurate option from the provided choices.
* For text fields, provide a brief and clear description or statement.

### Completing the Form

1. **Relationship Status**: Select your current relationship status from the options provided.
2. **Communication Frequency**: Choose how often you communicate with your partner from the provided options.
3. **Quality of Connection**: Indicate the quality of your connection with your partner from the options provided.
4. **Social Support**: Select all the ways in which you receive social support from your partner.
5. **Emotional Support**: Indicate if you receive emotional support from your partner.
6. **Emotional Intimacy**: Select all the ways in which you feel emotionally intimate with your partner.
7. **Conflict**: Indicate if you experience conflict with your partner.
8. **Emotional Distance**: Choose how emotionally distant or close you feel with your partner.
9. **Social Connection**: Rate the strength of your social connection with your partner.
10. **Community Involvement**: Describe your involvement with your community.
11. **Community Support**: Describe any support you receive from your community.
12. **Community Satisfaction**: Describe your level of satisfaction with your community.
13. **Workplace Support**: Describe any support you receive from your workplace.
14. **Workplace Satisfaction**: Describe your level of satisfaction with your workplace.
15. **School Support**: Describe any support you receive from your school or academic institution.
16. **School Satisfaction**: Describe your level of satisfaction with your school or academic institution.
17. **Other**: Provide any additional information or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Relationship Status**: Your current relationship status, e.g., single, married, in a relationship, etc.
* **Communication Frequency**: How often you interact with your partner, e.g., every day, several times a week, rarely, etc.
* **Quality of Connection**: The quality of your relationship, e.g., very good, good, fair, bad, etc.
* **Social Support**: The ways in which you receive social support from your partner, e.g., emotional, financial, physical, etc.
* **Emotional Support**: Whether you receive emotional support from your partner.
* **Emotional Intimacy**: The ways in which you feel emotionally intimate with your partner, e.g., sharing feelings, sharing activities, etc.
* **Conflict**: Whether you experience conflict with your partner.
* **Emotional Distance**: How emotionally close or distant you feel with your partner.
* **Social Connection**: The strength of your social connection with your partner.
* **Community Involvement**: Your level of involvement with your community.
* **Community Support**: The support you receive from your community.
* **Community Satisfaction**: Your level of satisfaction with your community.
* **Workplace Support**: The support you receive from your workplace.
* **Workplace Satisfaction**: Your level of satisfaction with your workplace.
* **School Support**: The support you receive from your school or academic institution.
* **School Satisfaction**: Your level of satisfaction with your school or academic institution.
* **Other**: Any additional information or comments you'd like to share.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Take your time to complete the form, and don't rush through it.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, feel free to ask for help.
