# Immigration Call Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide provides an overview of the Immigration Call Questionnaire, a form used to collect information for immigration-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the General Information section with basic details such as name, contact information, and other relevant details.
2. Provide family information, if applicable.
3. Enter contact information for the individual or entity.
4. Share travel history, including previous travels and any relevant information.
5. Specify residency status, employment status, and educational background.
6. Indicate language proficiency, reason for travel, and country of origin.
7. Enter date of birth, phone number, and email address.
8. Provide information about previous residences, reason for leaving, and reason for returning.
9. Enter country of birth, passport number, immigration status, citizenship status, and current visa status.
10. Enter visa application ID and previous visas.
11. Add any travel history notes and additional comments.
