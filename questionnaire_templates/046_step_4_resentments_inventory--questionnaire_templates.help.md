# Step 4 Resentments Inventory - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about resentments. A resentment is a feeling of discontent or displeasure towards something or someone. This form helps you to identify and document your resentments to better understand their root causes and potential solutions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as you can.
2. The form is divided into two pages. You will see two separate sections with text input fields.
3. For each field, provide a clear and concise answer to the question asked.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is a Resentment?** (`what_is_a_resentment`, text, optional): This field is where you should briefly describe or explain what you understand a resentment to be. Please keep your answer concise.
* **Resentments Description** (`resentments_description`, text, optional): This field is where you should provide a brief description of your resentments, including any details you have about the feelings or circumstances surrounding them.
* **Resentments** (`resentments_description_2`, text, optional): This field is similar to the previous one, but you should provide a more in-depth description of your resentments.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your answers, as they will help you better understand your resentments.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to skip it or ask for clarification.
* Remember that this form is for your personal use and should not be shared with others without your consent.
* If you have any issues or concerns while completing this form, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
