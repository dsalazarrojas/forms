# Spousal Sponsorship Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spousal Sponsorship Questionnaire is a form used to gather spousal details for various purposes such as verification and evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form instructions carefully before starting to fill out the form.
- Answer each question truthfully and completely.
- If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification or assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Spousal Name** (`spousal_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your spouse.
- **Spousal Date Met** (`spousal_date_met`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you met your spouse.
- **Spousal Address** (`spousal_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your spouse's home address.
- **Spousal City** (`spousal_city`, text, required/optional): Enter your spouse's city of residence.
- **Spousal State** (`spousal_state`, text, required/optional): Enter your spouse's state of residence.
- **Spousal Postal Code** (`spousal_postal_code`, text, required/optional): Enter your spouse's postal code (zip code).
- **Spousal Phone Number** (`spousal_phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your spouse's phone number.
- **Spousal Relationship Status** (`spousal_relationship_status`, text, required/optional): Choose the status of your relationship with your spouse.
- **Spousal Education Level** (`spousal_education_level`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your spouse's education level (High or Low).
- **Spousal Occupation** (`spousal_occupation`, text, required/optional): Enter your spouse's occupation.
- **Spousal Income** (`spousal_income`, number, required/optional): Enter your spouse's annual income.
