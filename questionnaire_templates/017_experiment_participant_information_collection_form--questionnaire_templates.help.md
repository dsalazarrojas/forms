# experiment-participant-information-collection-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The experiment-participant-information-collection-form is designed to collect essential participant details for research purposes. This form helps researchers gather accurate and up-to-date information about participants involved in their studies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand the required fields and their relevance to your participant information.
2. Provide accurate and clear information for each field.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed, marked as 'true' for 'required' fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`name`, `text`, required:false): Enter the participant's name.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required:false): Enter the participant's email address.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required:false): Enter the participant's phone number.
- **Participant Type** (`participant_type`, `select_multiple`, required:true): Select the participant type (Participant, Researcher, Lab).
- **Research Study Name** (`research_study_name`, `text`, required:false): Enter the name of the research study.
- **Schedule** (`schedule`, `date`, required:false): Enter the date and time of the participant's next visit.
- **Time** (`time`, `time`, required:false): Enter the time of the participant's next visit.
