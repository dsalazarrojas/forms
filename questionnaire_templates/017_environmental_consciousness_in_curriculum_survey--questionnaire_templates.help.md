# Environmental Consciousness In Curriculum Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on students' environmental awareness, curriculum coverage, and demographic information. It's meant to be completed by students and is an anonymous form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions according to your honest opinion.
2. Select all relevant options for each multiple-choice question.
3. Provide any relevant comments or suggestions for curriculum improvement.
4. Fill out the demographic information to provide context for the survey.
