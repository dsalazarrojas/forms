# Care Facility Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us better understand your needs and preferences for a care facility. Please answer the questions honestly, and we will use this information to find the best suitable facility for you or your loved ones.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. If a question does not apply to you, select the option that best fits the closest to your situation.
3. Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: This field is for you to enter your full name.
* **Relationship to Prospective Resident**: Select the relationship you have to the prospective resident, such as Self, Spouse or Partner, or Friend.
* **Prospective Resident Name**: Enter the name of the prospective resident.
* **Prospective Resident Age**: Enter the age of the prospective resident.
* **Survey Date**: Enter the date you are completing this survey.
* **Level of Care Needed**: Select all the levels of care you believe the prospective resident needs, such as Independent living, Assistance with daily activities, etc.
* **Relevant Medical Conditions**: Select all the medical conditions the prospective resident has, such as Alzheimer's disease, Heart disease, etc.
* **Mobility Status**: Select the level of mobility the prospective resident has, such as Fully mobile, Uses cane, etc.
* **Preferred Location**: Enter the city or area you prefer for the care facility.
* **Maximum Distance From Family**: Select the maximum distance from family you are willing to travel for the care facility.
* **Room Type Preference**: Select the type of room you prefer for the prospective resident, such as Private room, Shared room, etc.
* **Monthly Budget Range**: Select the monthly budget range you are willing to pay for the care facility.
* **Primary Payment Source**: Select all the payment sources you are using to pay for the care facility, such as Private pay, Insurance, etc.
* **Important Amenities**: Select all the amenities that are important to you, such as Dining services, Housekeeping, etc.
* **Activity Preferences**: Select all the activities you would like the care facility to offer, such as Exercise classes, Music programs, etc.
* **Dietary Requirements**: Select all the dietary requirements the prospective resident has, such as Regular diet, Low sodium, etc.
* **Does the Resident Have a Pet**: Select if the prospective resident has a pet.
* **Visitation Importance**: Select how important it is for you to have regular visits with the prospective resident.
* **Expected Move-in Timeline**: Select when you expect to move into the care facility.
* **Current Living Situation**: Select the current living situation of the prospective resident.
* **Most Important Decision Factors**: Select the top 3 factors that are most important to you when choosing a care facility.
* **Facility Visits Planned**: Select how many care facilities you have visited.
* **Additional Requirements**: Provide any other important considerations you have for the care facility.
* **Need Assistance with Facility Selection**: If you need assistance with selecting a care facility, select the appropriate option.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select your preferred method of contact for more information and guidance.
* **Additional Comments**: If you would like to add any additional information, please use this field.

Please answer these questions carefully, and we will use this information to provide you with suitable care facility options.
