# male_medical_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a male individual's medical history to provide a comprehensive understanding of their past health conditions.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer all required fields. Note that "required" fields are marked with a asterisk (*) symbol next to their labels.
2. Provide accurate and honest information about your medical history.
3. For select_multiple fields, choose all applicable answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Info** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This field is for you to provide your personal details. Enter your name, age, or any other relevant information.
* **Medical History** (`2`, `note`, required: false): This field is a free-text box where you can provide a brief description of your medical history, including any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or treatments.
* **Medical History Allergies** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check all that apply to indicate if you have any allergies:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Medical History Surgeries** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check all that apply to indicate if you have had any surgeries:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Medical History Medications** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check all that apply to indicate if you are currently taking any medications:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Medical History Injuries** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check all that apply to indicate if you have had any injuries:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* This form is intended for general use, not for diagnostic purposes. If you need to report specific medical conditions or concerns, please contact your healthcare provider.
