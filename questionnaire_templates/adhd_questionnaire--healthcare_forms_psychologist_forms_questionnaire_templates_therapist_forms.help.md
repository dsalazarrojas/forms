The purpose of this form is to gather information about a patient's demographics and symptoms related to Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD). This form will help us understand their personal details, such as name, date of birth, contact information, and medical history. Additionally, it will help us assess the frequency and severity of their ADHD symptoms, as well as any previous diagnoses or family history of mental health conditions.

This form should only be completed by the patient themselves, and the information provided will be kept confidential.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please enter your full legal name in the `Full name` field.
2. Enter your Date of birth in the `Date of birth` field in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
3. Enter your age in the `Age` field.
4. Select your gender from the `Gender` options.
5. Enter your phone number in the `Phone number` field (optional).
6. Enter your email address in the `Email address` field. This will be used to send follow-up information.
7. Select the date of your assessment in the `Date of assessment` field in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
8. If you were referred by someone, select the option that best describes how you heard about this assessment.
9. For the `ADHD Symptoms section`, answer the following questions:
	* How often do you have trouble focusing on tasks or activities? Select one of the frequency options: Never, Rarely, Sometimes, Often, Very often.
	* Do you experience restlessness or fidgeting? Select one of the frequency options: Never, Rarely, Sometimes, Often, Very often.
	* Rate your level of impulsive behavior on a scale of 1-10.
	* Did these symptoms begin before age 12? Select one of the options: True, False, Uncertain.
	* How much do these symptoms interfere with your daily life? Select one of the options: Not at all, Slightly, Moderately, Significantly, Extremely.
10. If you are currently taking any medications, list them in the `Current medications` field.
11. If you have been previously diagnosed with any mental health conditions, select all that apply in the `Previous diagnosis` field.
12. If there is a family history of ADHD or other mental health conditions, select one of the options in the `Family history` field.
13. Enter any additional comments or concerns you have in the `Additional comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Enter your full legal name.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Age**: Enter your age.
* **Gender**: Select your gender from the options: Male, Female, Non-binary, Prefer not to say.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Email address**: Enter your email address. We may send follow-up information to this address.
* **Date of assessment**: Select the date of your assessment in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **How did you hear about this assessment?**: If you were referred by someone, select the option that best describes how you heard about this assessment.
* **ADHD Symptoms section**: This section assesses the frequency and severity of your ADHD symptoms.
	+ **How often do you have trouble focusing on tasks or activities?**: Select one of the frequency options: Never, Rarely, Sometimes, Often, Very often.
	+ **Do you experience restlessness or fidgeting?**: Select one of the frequency options: Never, Rarely, Sometimes, Often, Very often.
	+ **Rate your level of impulsive behavior**: Rate your level of impulsive behavior on a scale of 1-10.
	+ **Did these symptoms begin before age 12?**: Select one of the options: True, False, Uncertain.
	+ **How much do these symptoms interfere with your daily life?**: Select one of the options: Not at all, Slightly, Moderately, Significantly, Extremely.
* **Current medications**: List all medications and dosages you are currently taking.
* **Previous diagnosis**: Select all mental health conditions you have been previously diagnosed with.
* **Family history**: Select one of the options if there is a family history of ADHD or other mental health conditions.
* **Additional comments**: Enter any additional comments or concerns you have.
