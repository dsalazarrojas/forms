# Business Structure Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the structure and effectiveness of an organization, helping you identify areas of strength and weakness to inform decisions about changes or improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability. Each question is designed to gather specific information about your organization's structure and processes. Be sure to select the option that best describes your experience or perception of each aspect.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as the person responding to this assessment.
* **Your Role in Organization** (`respondent_role`, `text`, required): Enter your position or title within the organization being assessed.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the organization being assessed.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date this assessment was completed.
* **Organizational Structure Clarity** (`organizational_clarity`, `select_one`, required): Rate the clarity of the organization's structure:
	+ Extremely Clear
	+ Clear
	+ Somewhat Clear
	+ Unclear
	+ Very Unclear
* **Role Definition Clarity** (`role_definition`, `select_one`, required): Rate the clarity of role definitions within the organization:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Reporting Structure Effectiveness** (`reporting_structure`, `select_one`, required): Rate the effectiveness of the reporting structure:
	+ Very Effective
	+ Effective
	+ Adequate
	+ Ineffective
	+ Very Ineffective
* **Communication Flow** (`communication_flow`, `select_one`, required): Rate the flow of information within the organization:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Adequate
	+ Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **Decision Making Process** (`decision_making_process`, `select_one`, required): Rate the effectiveness of the decision-making process:
	+ Very Efficient
	+ Efficient
	+ Adequate
	+ Slow
	+ Very Slow
* **Organizational Agility** (`organizational_agility`, `select_one`, required): Rate the organization's ability to adapt quickly:
	+ Highly Agile
	+ Agile
	+ Moderately Agile
	+ Rigid
	+ Very Rigid
* **Cross-functional Collaboration** (`cross_functional_collaboration`, `select_one`, required): Rate the effectiveness of cross-functional collaboration:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Adequate
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Accountability and Responsibility** (`accountability_levels`, `select_one`, required): Rate the clarity of accountability and responsibility within the organization:
	+ Very Clear
	+ Clear
	+ Somewhat Clear
	+ Unclear
	+ Very Unclear
* **Management Effectiveness** (`management_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required): Rate the effectiveness of management within the organization:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Employee Engagement** (`employee_engagement`, `select_one`, required): Rate the level of employee engagement:
	+ Highly Engaged
	+ Engaged
	+ Moderately Engaged
	+ Disengaged
	+ Highly Disengaged
* **Structure Alignment with Strategy** (`structure_alignment`, `select_one`, required): Rate the alignment of the organization's structure with its strategy:
	+ Excellent Alignment
	+ Good Alignment
	+ Adequate Alignment
	+ Poor Alignment
	+ Misaligned
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, `text`, required): Describe areas where the organization's structure can be improved.
* **Restructuring Recommendations** (`restructuring_recommendations`, `text`, optional): Suggest changes to the organization's structure, if any.
* **Overall Assessment** (`overall_assessment`, `select_one`, required): Rate the overall effectiveness of the organization's structure:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Needs Improvement
	+ Poor
