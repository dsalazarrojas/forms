# email_and_narrative_writing_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The email_and_narrative_writing_quiz form is a self-assessment tool designed to test your skills and knowledge in email and narrative writing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the correct options for each question and provide any necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Formal Email Etiquette** (`formal_email_etiquette`, select_multiple): Choose all that apply from the list of formal email etiquette rules. This section tests your knowledge of proper email formatting and communication.
* **Narrative Writing Techniques** (`narrative_writing_techniques`, select_one): Select the most effective technique from the list for writing a compelling narrative.
* **Formal Email Etiquette Score** (`formal_email_etiquette_score`, number): Enter the score you have in formal email etiquette, out of 10.
* **Narrative Writing Example 1** (`narrative_writing_example_1`, text): Write a short example of a narrative paragraph that demonstrates your skills.
* **Narrative Writing Example 2** (`narrative_writing_example_2`, text): Write a short example of a narrative paragraph that demonstrates your skills.
* **Date for Narrative Scenario** (`email_and_narrative_writing_quiz_6`, date): Enter a date for the hypothetical scenario in which you would use your narrative writing skills.
* **Time for Narrative Scenario** (`email_and_narrative_writing_quiz_7`, time): Enter a time for the hypothetical scenario in which you would use your narrative writing skills.
* **Narrative Writing Style** (`email_and_narrative_writing_quiz_8`, select_one): Select the style that best fits the narrative writing scenario.
* **Email and Narrative Writing Skills** (`email_and_narrative_writing_quiz_9`, select_multiple): Choose all that apply from the list of email and narrative writing skills.
* **Best Narrative Writing Technique** (`email_and_narrative_writing_quiz_10`, select_one): Select the most effective technique from the list for writing a compelling narrative.
* **Email and Narrative Writing Examples** (`email_and_narrative_writing_quiz_11`, select_multiple): Choose all that apply from the list of email and narrative writing examples.
* **Effective Narrative Writing Style** (`email_and_narrative_writing_quiz_12`, select_one): Select the style that best fits the narrative writing scenario.
* **Additional Email and Narrative Writing Skills** (`email_and_narrative_writing_quiz_13`, select_multiple): Choose all that apply from the list of email and narrative writing skills.

* **Additional Narrative Writing Examples** (`email_and_narrative_writing_quiz_15`, select_multiple): Choose all that apply from the list of narrative writing examples.
