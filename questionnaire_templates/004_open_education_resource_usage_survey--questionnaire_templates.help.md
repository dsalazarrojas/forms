# Open Education Resource Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form aims to collect information on your institution's awareness, usage, and experiences with Open Education Resources (OER). Your participation is invaluable for the development and improvement of OER.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select your institution type from the list.
2. Choose your answer for each question based on your knowledge about your institution.
3. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
4. For the "benefits" and "challenges" questions, provide a detailed description in the note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School type**: Select your institution type (Schools, Universities, Training providers, or Other). This information helps us understand the scope of OER usage in different types of institutions.
* **Awareness**: Indicate whether you are aware of Open Education Resources, if you are somewhat aware, or not aware at all. This will help us track the level of awareness about OER.
* **Use frequency**: Choose how frequently you use OER, if you use it at all. This information will help us understand the level of engagement with OER.
* **Satisfaction**: Indicate how satisfied you are with OER, from Very satisfied to Very dissatisfied. This helps us understand your overall experience with OER.
* **Benefits**: Describe the benefits you have experienced from using OER. This will help us identify the positive impact of OER.
* **Challenges**: Describe the challenges you face when using OER. This will help us identify areas for improvement.
* **Open awareness**: Indicate whether you agree or disagree with statements about open education. This will help us understand your perceptions about open education.
* **OER impact**: Choose how you think OER has impacted your institution, from High to No impact. This will help us understand the overall effect of OER on institutions.
