# Enterpise Pre Engagement Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This questionnaire is designed to help you gather information about a customer's pre-meeting preparation, needs, and expectations. It will help you understand their goals, objectives, and pain points, which will enable you to tailor your sales pitch and approach to their specific situation.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form with your honest and accurate answers to the best of your knowledge and understanding.
2.  Be as thorough as possible in your responses, as this will help ensure that you are prepared for the meeting.
3.  If you are unsure about any question, please provide a brief explanation or ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Information** (`Customer Information`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering any general information about the customer, such as their name, contact details, or any other relevant context.
*   **What are their business needs?** (`business_needs`, `text`, optional): This field is for gathering information about the customer's business needs and challenges. Be as specific as possible about their current situation and what they hope to achieve.
*   **What are their customer requirements?** (`customer_requirements`, `text`, optional): This field is for understanding what the customer expects from your product or service. Try to identify their must-haves and nice-to-haves.
*   **What are their customer expectations?** (`customer_expectations`, `text`, optional): This field is for gathering information about the customer's expectations from your product or service. Be clear about what they hope to achieve or get out of the meeting.
*   **What do you hope to get out of this meeting?** (`meeting_outcome`, `text`, optional): This field is for expressing your goals and objectives for the meeting. Be specific about what you hope to achieve or get out of the meeting.
*   **What are their pain points?** (`customer_pain_points`, `text`, optional): This field is for gathering information about the customer's pain points, such as any challenges or difficulties they are facing. Be as clear and specific as possible.
*   **How would you describe their business model?** (`business_model`, `text`, optional): This field is for understanding the customer's business model, such as their product or service offerings and revenue streams.
*   **What is your current relationship with the customer?** (`customer_relationship`, `text`, optional): This field is for describing your current relationship with the customer, such as how long you have worked with them or any previous interactions.
*   **What are the customer goals?** (`customer_goals`, `text`, optional): This field is for gathering information about the customer's short-term and long-term goals. Be as specific as possible about what they hope to achieve.
*   **What are the customer objectives?** (`customer_objects`, `text`, optional): This field is for understanding the customer's objectives and priorities. Be clear about what they hope to achieve or get out of the meeting.
*   **What are their biggest pain points?** (`customer_pain_point_1`, `text`, optional): This field is for gathering information about the customer's top pain point.
*   **What are their second biggest pain points?** (`customer_pain_point_2`, `text`, optional): This field is for gathering information about the second most significant pain point.
*   **What are their third biggest pain points?** (`customer_pain_point_3`, `text`, optional): This field is for gathering information about the customer's third most significant pain point.

## Tips

*   Please keep the answers concise and clear.
*   Use proper grammar and spelling.
*   If you are unsure about any question, please feel free to ask for clarification.
*   Please provide any additional context or information that may be relevant to the form. 
*   This form is for your benefit, as it will help you prepare for the meeting and tailor your approach to the customer's needs.
