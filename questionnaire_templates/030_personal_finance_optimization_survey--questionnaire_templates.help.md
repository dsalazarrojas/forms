# Personal Finance Optimization Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help users understand their personal finance habits and goals, and provide input on how to optimize their financial situation. It collects information on various aspects of personal finance, including income, spending, savings, and financial goals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Choose the option that best describes your current financial habits and goals.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Money Habits

* **Money Habits** (`money_habits`, `text`, required: false): This question asks about your current financial habits, such as saving, spending, and managing your money. Please answer this question honestly, and select the option that best describes your habits.

### 2. Financial Goals

* **Financial Goals** (`financial_goals`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks about your short-term or long-term financial goals. Select the option that best describes your goals.

### 3. Income Frequency

* **Income Frequency** (`income_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks about how often you receive your income. Select the option that best describes your income frequency.

### 4. Spending Frequency

* **Spending Frequency** (`spending_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks about how often you spend money. Select the option that best describes your spending frequency.

### 5. Money Savings

* **Money Savings** (`money_savings`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks about your current savings habits, such as saving for emergencies, short-term goals, or long-term goals. Select all that apply.

### 6. Emergency Funds

* **Emergency Funds** (`emergency_funds`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks about having emergency funds available for unexpected expenses. Select all that apply.

### 7. Bill Payment

* **Bill Payment** (`bill_payment`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks about your payment habits for bills and debts. Select all that apply.

### 8. Savings Amount

* **Savings Amount** (`savings_amount`, `number`, required: false): This question asks for an estimate of your current savings amount.

### 9. Income

* **Income** (`income`, `number`, required: false): This question asks for your current income.

### 10. Expense Tracking

* **Expense Tracking** (`expense_tracking`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks about tracking your expenses. Select all that apply.

### 11. Financial Planning

* **Financial Planning** (`financial_planning`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks about your financial planning habits. Select all that apply.

### 12. Financial Advice

* **Financial Advice** (`financial_advice`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks about seeking financial advice. Select all that apply.

## Tips

- Make sure to answer honestly and accurately.
- Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the financial planner or advisor you are working with.
