# Document Compatibility Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Document Compatibility Assessment form is designed to help users identify and troubleshoot issues related to document compatibility on different platforms. This form aims to gather information about the platforms used, the specific issues encountered, and the nature of these issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer what platform you are currently using.
2. Describe the issue you are experiencing with your document on this platform.
3. Indicate if this issue is also present on other platforms.
4. Specify if the issue is related to formatting or compatibility.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **What platform do you use?**
	* Platform 1: The first option is for users who use Platform 1.
	* Platform 2: The second option is for users who use Platform 2.
	* Platform 3: The third option is for users who use Platform 3.

### 2. **What is the issue with your document on this platform?**
	* This field is for describing the problem you are facing with your document on the selected platform in as much detail as possible.

### 3. **Is this an issue on multiple platforms?**
	* Yes: If the issue is also present on other platforms.
	* No: If the issue is only present on the selected platform.

### 4. **Is this issue related to formatting or compatibility?**
	* Formatting: If the issue is related to the way your document is formatted.
	* Compatibility: If the issue is related to how your document interacts with the platform.
	* Other: If the issue is not related to formatting or compatibility.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when describing the issue you are experiencing.
* If you are unsure about the nature of the issue, choose "Other" and provide a detailed description.
* If you have tried troubleshooting steps, share them as they might be helpful for further analysis.
* This form is designed to help us understand document compatibility issues. Your feedback is invaluable in helping us improve our services.
