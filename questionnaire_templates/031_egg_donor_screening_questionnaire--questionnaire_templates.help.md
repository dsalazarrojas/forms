# Egg Donor Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Egg Donor Screening Questionnaire is a comprehensive form designed to collect information from potential egg donors, helping to streamline the application process and ensure that all necessary details are gathered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have understood the questions and their relevance to the egg donation process.
2. Answer each question accurately and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Info (Required: false)
* **General Info** (`general_info`, `text`, required: false): This section is a brief introduction to collect basic information about yourself, such as your name, address, and contact details. 
Please provide your name, address, and contact information for our records.

### Medical History (Required: false)
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Please describe any medical conditions you have had in the past or are currently experiencing. 
This will help us understand your medical history and ensure you are fit to be an egg donor.

### Family History (Required: false)
* **Family History** (`family_history`, `text`, required: false): This section is to gather information about your family's medical history. 
Please provide details about any family members with a history of medical conditions or illnesses.

### Health Status (Required: false)
* **Health Status** (`health_status`, `text`, required: false): Describe your current health status, including any ongoing medical treatments or allergies. 
We need to understand your overall health status to ensure your suitability as an egg donor.

### Medical Conditions (Required: false)
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required: false): List any medical conditions you have or have had in the past. 
This includes any chronic conditions, illnesses, or allergies that may impact your health as an egg donor.

### Medication History (Required: false)
* **Medication History** (`medication_history`, `text`, required: false): If you are taking any medications, please list them, including dosages and frequency. 
This helps us understand your medication history and any potential interactions.

### Social History (Required: false)
* **Social History** (`social_history`, `text`, required: false): Please describe your social history, including any substance use, smoking or vaping habits. 
This will help us assess your lifestyle and habits as an egg donor.

### Contact Info (Required: false)
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): This section is for your contact information, including email and phone number. 
We need this information to communicate with you about your egg donation application.

### Emergency Contact (Required: false)
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false): Please provide the name and contact information for an emergency contact, such as a family member or friend. 
This is for our reference in case of an emergency.

### Egg Donors History (Required: false)
* **Egg Donors History** (`egg_donors_history`, `text`, required: false): If you have a history of donating eggs in the past, please describe your experience and any notable occurrences. 
This will help us understand your past egg donation history and experiences. 

## Tips
* Ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Please provide as much detail as possible for each question.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
* This form is for informational purposes only, and your responses will be reviewed as part of the egg donation process. 

Please note that this form is not meant to be a comprehensive medical evaluation, and any medical conditions or concerns should be discussed with a healthcare professional.
