# Airline Traveler Preference Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Airline Traveler Preference Check In Form is designed to collect your preferences and requirements for a seamless check-in experience. This form will be used by airline staff to tailor their services to meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the required personal details including your Full Name, Booking Reference (PNR), and Airline.
2.  Enter your flight number to ensure the correct flight details are associated with your preferences.
3.  Choose how you would like to be checked in, including Mobile/Web (Auto check-in), Airport Kiosk, or Counter Check-in.
4.  Select your preferred method of receiving your boarding pass, including Email (PDF), SMS (Mobile Link), Printed at Airport, or Apple/Google Wallet.
5.  Choose your preferred seat location, such as Window, Aisle, or Front of Aircraft.
6.  Indicate the number of checked bags you have.
7.  Specify any special meal requests, including Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Low Sodium, Halal, or Kosher.
8.  If you require special assistance, select the type of support needed, such as Wheelchair to gate or Wheelchair to seat.
9.  Enter your Frequent Flyer Number if you have one.
10.  Provide the best phone number for the airline to contact you for flight updates.
11.  Finally, confirm that the information provided is correct and accurate for this trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Traveler Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your passport or government-issued ID.
*   **Booking Reference (PNR)**: Enter the booking reference number associated with your flight.
*   **Airline**: Select your airline.
*   **Flight Number**: Enter the flight number for which you are checking in.
*   **Preferred Check-in Method**: Choose how you would like to be checked in, including Mobile/Web (Auto check-in), Airport Kiosk, or Counter Check-in.
*   **Boarding Pass Delivery Method**: Select how you would like to receive your boarding pass, including Email (PDF), SMS (Mobile Link), Printed at Airport, or Apple/Google Wallet.
*   **Seat Preference**: Choose your preferred seat location, such as Window, Aisle, or Front of Aircraft.
*   **Number of Checked Bags**: Indicate the number of bags you are checking.
*   **Special Meal Request**: Specify any special meal requests, including Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Low Sodium, Halal, or Kosher.
*   **Airport Assistance Required**: If you require special assistance, select the type of support needed, such as Wheelchair to gate or Wheelchair to seat.
*   **Frequent Flyer Number**: Enter your Frequent Flyer Number if you have one.
*   **Best Phone Number for Flight Updates**: Provide the best phone number for the airline to contact you for flight updates.
*   **Confirm Preferences**: Confirm that the information provided is correct and accurate for this trip.
