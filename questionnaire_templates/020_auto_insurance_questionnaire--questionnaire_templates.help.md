# Auto Insurance Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The auto insurance questionnaire is designed to collect relevant information from users for the purpose of providing accurate and personalized insurance quotes or applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your personal details, including full name, date of birth, marital status, occupation, and total years you have held a driver's license.
2. Select your marital status and occupation from the provided options.
3. Answer questions regarding your driving history, including whether you have had any violations or accidents in the last 5 years.
4. Provide details about your vehicle, including its year, make, and model.
5. Answer questions about your vehicle usage, including the distance driven to work and the number of days per week driven to work.
6. If applicable, fill out the section on existing insurance, providing details about your current carrier and monthly premium.
7. Indicate your interest in bundling your auto and home insurance.
8. Choose a preferred contact time for further communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`dob`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM-DD-YYYY.
* **Marital Status** (`marital_status`, select_one, required): Select your current marital status from the provided options.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, text, required): Provide your current job title or profession.
* **Years Licensed** (`years_licensed`, number, required): Enter the total number of years you have held a driver's license.
* **Any Violations in the Last 5 Years?** (`any_violations`, select_one, required): Select 'True' if you have had any tickets or accidents in the last 5 years, 'False' otherwise.
* **Details of Violations** (`details_of_violations`, text, required): Briefly describe any violations or accidents you have had in the last 5 years.
* **Vehicle Description** (`vehicle_description`, text, required): Enter the year, make, and model of your vehicle.
* **Miles One Way to Work** (`miles_one_way`, number, optional): Enter the distance driven to work each day.
* **Days per Week Driven to Work** (`days_per_week`, number, optional): Enter the number of days per week you drive to work.
* **Current Carrier** (`current_carrier`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of your current insurance carrier.
* **Current Monthly Premium** (`current_monthly_premium`, number, optional): Enter your current monthly premium.
* **Interested in Bundling** (`interested_bundling`, select_one, required): Select 'True' if you are interested in bundling your home and auto insurance, 'False' otherwise.
* **Preferred Contact Time** (`preferred_contact_time`, select_one, required): Choose a time of day for us to contact you.
