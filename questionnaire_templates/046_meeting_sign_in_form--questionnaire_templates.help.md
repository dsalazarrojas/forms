# Meeting Sign In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meeting Sign In Form is a tool to gather information about meetings, including meeting details, attendees, and meeting comments. This form is used to track meeting attendance and participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the meeting details, including the date and time of the meeting, and the location where it will be held.
2. Identify if attendees will be present at the meeting by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' for the 'Attendees' field.
3. If 'Yes' is selected, enter the attendee's name, email, and phone number.
4. Add any relevant meeting notes and comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Details** (`meeting_sign_in_form`): Enter a brief description of the meeting, including the date and time.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`): Select the date of the meeting using the date picker.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`): Select the time of the meeting using the time picker.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`): Enter the location where the meeting will be held.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`): Select 'Yes' if the attendee will be present, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`): Enter the name of the attendee if 'Yes' is selected for the 'Attendees' field.
* **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`): Enter the email of the attendee if 'Yes' is selected for the 'Attendees' field.
* **Attendee Phone** (`attendee_phone`): Enter the phone number of the attendee if 'Yes' is selected for the 'Attendees' field.
* **Meeting Notes** (`meeting_notes`): Enter any relevant notes about the meeting.
* **Meeting Comments** (`meeting_comments`): Add any additional comments about the meeting.
* **Meeting Sign In Status** (`meeting_sign_in_status`) and **Meeting Sign In Status 2** (`meeting_sign_in_status_2`): These fields are currently not being used.
