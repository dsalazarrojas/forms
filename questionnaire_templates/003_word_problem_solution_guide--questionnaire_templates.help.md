# Word Problem Solution Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Word Problem Solution Guide is a form designed to help students solve math word problems by breaking down solutions into steps and providing a space for reflection and teacher feedback. It's intended for use in educational settings to facilitate student learning and teacher assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the problem presented at the top of the page (page 1: `word_problem_solution_guide_1`).
2. Provide your solution steps for the problem in the `solution_steps` field (page 2).
3. Select the relevant steps that apply to your solution from the options provided.
4. Add your reflection on the problem and your solution in the `reflection` field (page 3).
5. Finally, add any comments or feedback from your teacher in the `teacher_comments` field (page 4).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the problem** (`word_problem_solution_guide_1`): This field presents the word problem to be solved. Read the problem carefully and make sure you understand what is being asked.
* **solution steps** (`solution_steps`): This field is for you to provide your solution steps to the problem. Select all the steps that apply to your solution from the options provided.
* **reflection** (`reflection`): In this field, reflect on your solution and think about what you did well and what you could do differently next time. What did you learn from the problem, and how will you apply it in the future?
* **teacher comments** (`teacher_comments`): Your teacher will add their comments and feedback on your solution in this field. This is your chance to get personalized feedback and guidance on your work.
