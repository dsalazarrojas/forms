# Pet Care Quality Owner Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from pet owners about their experiences with a pet care service. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the pet's care quality and owner satisfaction. This feedback will help the service providers to understand what they are doing well and where they need to improve.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your pet's type (e.g., Pet, Dog, Cat, etc.).
2. Enter your pet's age.
3. Select the gender of your pet (Male, Female, or Undisclosed).
4. If your pet has a breed, please enter it.
5. If you have visited the pet care service recently, please select the date of your visit.
6. If you have visited the pet care service during a specific time, please select the time.
7. Enter the name of the staff member who attended to your pet.
8. Rate your overall experience with the service (Very Good, Good, Fair, or Bad).
9. If you have any specific comments about your experience, please enter them here.
10. Select all the reasons why you would recommend the service to others (True or False).
11. If you would recommend the service, please enter the name of the person you would recommend it to.
12. Enter a brief reason why you would recommend the service to this person.
13. Select the purpose of your visit (Grooming, Boarding, Walking, or Clinic Visit).
14. Enter the duration of your visit.
15. If your visit was free or paid, please select the option that applies.
16. Rate your satisfaction with the service (Very Satisfied, Moderately Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, or Not Satisfied).
17. Select how often you visit the service (Regular, Occasional, or Rare).
18. Enter any additional comments about your experience.
19. Rate the service on a scale from 1 to 5.
20. Enter your comments about the service.
21. Select if you will return to the service (True or False).
22. Select if you would like to schedule a follow-up visit (True or False).

* **specific_comments** (10, text, required): If you have any specific comments about your experience, please enter them here.
* **will_recommend** (11, select_multiple, required): Select all the reasons why you would recommend the service to others (True or False).
* **will_recommend_to** (12, text, required): If you would recommend the service, please enter the name of the person you would recommend it to.
* **will_recommend_who** (13, text, required): Enter a brief reason why you would recommend the service to this person.
* **visit_purpose** (14, select_one, required): Select the purpose of your visit (Grooming, Boarding, Walking, or Clinic Visit).
* **visit_duration** (15, text, required): Enter the duration of your visit.
* **visit_cost** (16, select_one, required): If your visit was free or paid, please select the option that applies.
* **pet_owner_satisfaction** (17, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with the service (Very Satisfied, Moderately Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, or Not Satisfied).
