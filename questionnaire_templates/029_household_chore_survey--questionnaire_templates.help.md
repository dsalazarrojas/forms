# Household Chore Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Household Chore Survey is a form used to gather information from users about their household's cleaning and organization habits and opinions on fairness in household responsibilities. Your input will help inform decisions on household chore allocation and fairness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the space provided.
2. Indicate whether you have other household members who participate in cleaning and organization.
	* If yes, select the names of your other household members.
3. Rate your household's level of fairness in household responsibilities (1-10).
4. Provide the name of each household member who participates in cleaning and organization.
5. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Household Chore Survey** (Page 1)
    This is the title of the form, which you are already on.
- **Name** (`name`, text, required)
    Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Household Members** (`household_members`, select_multiple, optional)
    If you have other household members who participate in cleaning and organization, please select their names from the list. You can select one or multiple names.
- **Fairness Level** (`fairness_level`, number, optional)
    Rate your household's level of fairness in sharing responsibilities from 1 (not fair at all) to 10 (very fair).
- **Household Member Name** (`household_member_name`, text, optional)
    Enter the name of each household member who participates in cleaning and organization.
