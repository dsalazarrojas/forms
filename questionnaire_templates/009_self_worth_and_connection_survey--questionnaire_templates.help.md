# self_worth_and_connection_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form helps individuals understand their self-worth and social connection by guiding them through a series of questions that measure their emotional states and feelings. Completing this form will provide valuable insights into their self-perception, emotional state, and social connections.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer each question carefully, providing the best response that represents your feelings and perceptions.
2.  Select one option per question from the available options. If unsure, use the option that best describes your current situation.
3.  Fill out all questions, if possible, but note that some questions are optional if you feel uncomfortable providing an answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **self_worth** (Select One): This question measures how important you feel you are as a person. Please choose the option that best describes your self-worth.
*   **loneliness** (Select Multiple): This question explores feelings of loneliness. Select all options that describe how you have been feeling lately.
*   **social_connection** (Select One): This question assesses how connected you feel to others. Choose the option that best describes your social connections.
*   **self_worth_scale** (Number): If you answered "self_worth," you will be asked to provide a numerical value that represents your self-worth. Please enter a number that best describes your self-worth.
*   **self_worth_scale_value** (Text): This is a space for you to elaborate on your self-worth numerical value.
*   **loneliness_scale** (Number): If you answered "loneliness," you will be asked to provide a numerical value that represents your loneliness level. Please enter a number that best describes your loneliness.
*   **loneliness_scale_value** (Text): This is a space for you to elaborate on your loneliness numerical value.
*   **connection_scale** (Number): This question measures how connected you feel to others. Please enter a number that best describes your connections.
*   **connection_scale_value** (Text): This is a space for you to elaborate on your connection numerical value.
*   **self_worth_score** (Select One): This question will show you a summary of your self-worth assessment. Please choose the option that best describes your overall self-worth.
*   **loneliness_score** (Select One): This question will show you a summary of your loneliness assessment. Please choose the option that best describes your overall loneliness.
*   **connection_score** (Select One): This question will show you a summary of your connection assessment. Please choose the option that best describes your overall connection.
*   **self_worth_and_connection_score** (Select One): This question will show you a summary of your overall self-worth and social connection. Please choose the option that best describes your overall self-worth and connection.

## Tips

*   Be honest in your responses, as this will provide a more accurate assessment.
*   If you need more time or feel overwhelmed, you can pause and complete the form later.
*   Remember, this form is meant to be a self-assessment tool, and it's okay to take your time and be honest with yourself.
