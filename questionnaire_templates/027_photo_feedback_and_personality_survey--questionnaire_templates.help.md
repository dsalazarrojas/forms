# Photo Feedback And Personality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on photos and evaluate personality traits based on the reactions to these photos.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. For each photo, rate your reaction to it using the scale provided.
2. For each photo, select the personality traits that you think apply to the person in the photo.
3. Enter your rating for each photo using the numerical scale provided.
4. Answer all the questions honestly and thoroughly to provide a comprehensive feedback.
