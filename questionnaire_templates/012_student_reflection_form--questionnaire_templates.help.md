# Student Reflection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Reflection Form is a tool designed to gather information about students' thoughts, feelings, and experiences in a specific class. This form helps teachers collect feedback from students about their classes, allowing them to reflect on their progress, challenges, and successes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Fill in your **student_id** (1): Enter your student ID number as required.
* Enter your **student_name** (2): Type your name as it appears on your identification.
* Enter your **teacher_name** (3): Type the name of your teacher who assigned this form.
* Enter your **teacher_email** (4): Type your teacher's email address.
* Enter your **teacher_phone** (5): Type your teacher's phone number.
* Enter your **class_name** (6): Type the name of the class you are reflecting on.
* Choose a date for **reflection_date** (7): Select the date you are reflecting on.
* Choose a time for **reflection_time** (8): Select the time you are reflecting on.
* Answer **question_1** (9): Respond to the first question as instructed by your teacher.
* Answer **question_2** (10): Respond to the second question as instructed by your teacher.
* Answer **question_3** (11): Respond to the third question as instructed by your teacher.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **student_id** (`1`, `number`, required/optional): This field is used to input your student ID number.
* **student_name** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for your name as it appears on your identification.
* **teacher_name** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for your teacher's name who assigned this form.
* **teacher_email** (`4`, `email`, required/optional): This is the email address of your teacher.
* **teacher_phone** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for your teacher's phone number.
* **class_name** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for the name of the class you are reflecting on.
* **reflection_date** (`7`, `date`, required/optional): Choose a date for the reflection you are providing.
* **reflection_time** (`8`, `time`, required/optional): Choose a time for the reflection you are providing.
* **question_1** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): Answer the first question as instructed by your teacher.
* **question_2** (`10`, `text`, required/optional): Answer the second question as instructed by your teacher.
* **question_3** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): Answer the third question as instructed by your teacher.
