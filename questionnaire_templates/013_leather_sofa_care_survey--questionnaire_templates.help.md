# Leather Sofa Care Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experiences with their leather sofas. The goal is to improve the company's care recommendations and product offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in each section as accurately as possible.
2. Choose the options that best fit your experience and preferences.
3. Don't hesitate to share any additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Introduction
* **Introduction**: This is a brief note to introduce the form and explain its purpose.

### 2. Customer Information
* **Customer Information**: This field is optional, but if you choose to fill it out, please provide any relevant information about your leather sofa, such as its type, material, or age.

### 3. Maintenance Frequency
* **Maintenance Frequency**: This question asks about how often you clean your leather sofa. Please select one of the options:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
	+ Never

### 4. Cleaning Products
* **Cleaning Products**: If you have used any cleaning products on your leather sofa, please list them here.

### 5. Care Recommendations
* **Care Recommendations**: This field is for any general comments or suggestions you might have about the care guidance provided.

### 6. Product Recommendations
* **Product Recommendations**: If you have any specific product recommendations for cleaning or maintaining your leather sofa, please select all that apply:
	+ Leather soap
	+ Leather conditioner
	+ Fabric protector
	+ Microfiber cloth
	+ Other (please specify)

### 7. Customer Experience
* **Customer Experience**: Please rate your overall experience with the care guidance provided:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Neutral
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

### 8. Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any other comments or suggestions, please share them here.
