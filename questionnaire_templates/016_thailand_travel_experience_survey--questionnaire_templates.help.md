# Thailand Travel Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experiences during a trip to Thailand. Your feedback will help us understand your satisfaction, preferences, and any notable aspects of your trip.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your responses to the questions listed below.
2. Select the appropriate options from the dropdown menus for select fields.
3. Enter your email address and phone number to help us contact you (if needed).
4. Provide any additional comments or notes in the text fields.
5. Review and submit your form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trip Satisfaction**: Select how satisfied you were with your trip, choosing between "Satisfied" and "Dissatisfied".
* **Activities**: Indicate which activities you enjoyed or not during your trip, choosing between "Yes" and "No".
* **Trip Recommendations**: Share any suggestions or recommendations for future travelers.
* **Trip Satisfaction Score**: Rate your overall satisfaction with your trip on a numerical scale.
* **Trip Duration**: Enter the length of your trip in days.
* **Travel Team**: Describe the team or staff that assisted you during your trip.
* **Travel Dates**: Provide the dates of your trip.
* **Trip Travel Type**: Choose a category that best describes your trip type (e.g., leisure, business, adventure).
* **Trip Budget**: Enter the approximate budget for your trip.
* **Trip Location**: Describe the location(s) you visited during your trip.
* **Trip Hotel**: Mention any notable hotels or accommodations you used during your trip.
* **Trip Food and Drink**: Share your experiences with local food and drink.
* **Trip Transportation**: Describe your transportation methods (e.g., flight, taxi, walking).
* **Trip Accommodation**: Describe your accommodations (e.g., hotel, hostel, apartment).
* **Trip Activity**: Mention any notable activities or experiences you had during your trip.
* **Trip Satisfaction Notes**: Add any additional comments or feedback about your trip.
* **Trip Travel Experience**: Share any memorable moments or highlights from your trip.
* **Trip Travel Partner**: If you traveled with someone, mention their name.
* **Trip Travel Agency**: If you used a travel agency, mention their name.
* **Trip Travel Insurance**: If you had travel insurance, mention the provider.
* **Other (please specify)**: If there's anything else you'd like to share about your trip, enter it here.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Assigned Tool**: (Optional) If you were assigned a specific tool or equipment during your trip, mention it here.
* **Submitted by**: (Optional) If you'd like to specify who submitted the form, choose from the options provided.

Note: Some fields are optional, and you can skip them if not applicable.
