# Metamorphosis Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Metamorphosis Assessment form is designed to gather information about an individual's current state, goals, obstacles, self-rated progress, and the assessment of their coach or mentor. This form helps in understanding an individual's metamorphosis journey, which is a significant transformation or change in their personal or professional life. The form is meant to be completed by individuals undergoing this metamorphic process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the current state field with a brief description of your current situation or status.
2. In the goals field, list the objectives or changes you aim to achieve through the metamorphosis.
3. Identify and mention any obstacles that you're facing during this metamorphosis process in the obstacles field.
4. Evaluate and rate your progress so far in the self-rated progress field.
5. Select the stage of your coach or mentor's assessment from the coaches assessment dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **current state (text)** (`current_state`, text, required: false): This field is for you to describe your current state or situation. Please provide a brief and clear explanation of where you are in your metamorphosis journey.
* **goals (text)** (`goals`, text, required: false): In this field, list the specific objectives or changes you're aiming to make or achieve as a result of your metamorphosis. Be as detailed as possible, so you and your coach or mentor can track your progress effectively.
* **obstacles (text)** (`obstacles`, text, required: false): This field is for you to mention any challenges or barriers you're facing during your metamorphosis process. Identifying these obstacles will help you and your coach or mentor understand what needs to be addressed.
* **self_rated_progress (text)** (`self_rated_progress`, text, required: false): Evaluate and rate your progress so far in the metamorphosis process. This will help you and your coach or mentor gauge how far you've come and what still needs to be done.
* **coaches_assessment (select_one)** (`coaches_assessment`, select_one, required: false): Your coach or mentor will assess your progress and select the stage you're at in your metamorphosis journey. This will help them provide you with guidance and support tailored to your needs.

## Tips

* Be honest and detailed when filling out this form to get the most accurate assessment and guidance from your coach or mentor.
* Review the fields carefully and make sure to provide as much information as possible.
* If you're unsure about any field, don't hesitate to reach out to your coach or mentor for clarification.
* This form is designed to be a collaborative tool for your metamorphosis journey, so don't hesitate to use it as a reference point for discussion and growth.
