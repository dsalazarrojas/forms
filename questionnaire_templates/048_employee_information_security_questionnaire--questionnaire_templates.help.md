# Employee Information Security Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Information Security Questionnaire is designed to collect important employee information for security and compliance purposes. This form helps the organization to ensure the accuracy of employee data and maintain a secure work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the required fields.
2. Make sure to fill out the fields accurately and truthfully.
3. If you're unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask your supervisor or HR representative for help.
4. Please do not skip any fields, as incomplete forms may cause issues with security and compliance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
- **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your official email address associated with your employee account.
- **Security Question** (Security Question, text, required): Answer the provided security question accurately and truthfully.
- **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your official phone number associated with your employee account.
- **Question** (Note, note, required): This is a text box for you to provide additional information if needed.
- **Category** (Category, text, required): Enter the category that best describes your job role or department.
- **Department** (Department, text, required): Enter your department or team name as it appears on the organizational chart.
- **Job Title** (Job Title, text, required): Enter your official job title as it appears on your job description.
- **Job Level** (Job Level, select_one, required): Select your job level from the options provided.
  - Junior
  - Mid-level
  - Senior
- **Team** (Team, select_multiple, required): Select all teams you belong to from the options provided.
  - Engineering
  - Marketing
  - IT
  - Sales
  - Customer Support

## Tips
- Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues or concerns, do not hesitate to contact your supervisor or HR representative.
- Please keep your answers accurate and truthful throughout the form.
