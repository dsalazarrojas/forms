# Product Market Fit Confirmation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to confirm the product-market fit of a product or service. It helps ensure that the product meets the needs and expectations of the target market. The form is typically used by product managers and business owners to assess the potential success of a product in the market.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Open the form and review the pages.
2.  Fill out each page as instructed.
3.  Ensure you answer all the required fields (none of the fields in this form are marked as "required").
4.  Review your answers for completeness and accuracy.
5.  Submit the form once you're done.
