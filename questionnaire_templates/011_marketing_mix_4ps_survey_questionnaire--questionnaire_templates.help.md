# Marketing Mix (4Ps) Survey Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your marketing strategy for your product, price, place, and promotion. This information will help us understand your approach to marketing and identify areas where you can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the information requested for each section. You can enter text responses in the provided text boxes. If a field is marked as optional, you can choose not to fill it in if you don't have the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product** (`marketing_mix_4ps_survey_questionnaire_product`, text, optional): Describe your product or service, including its features and benefits.
* **Price** (`marketing_mix_4ps_survey_questionnaire_price`, text, optional): Describe your pricing strategy, including any discounts, promotions, or pricing tiers.
* **Place** (`marketing_mix_4ps_survey_questionnaire_place`, text, optional): Describe where your product is sold or distributed, including online marketplaces, physical stores, or other channels.
* **Promotion** (`marketing_mix_4ps_survey_questionnaire_promotion`, text, optional): Describe your promotional strategy, including advertising, social media, and other tactics used to reach customers.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when answering each question.
* If you have any additional information you'd like to share about your marketing strategy, feel free to add it in the "Additional Comments" section.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, don't hesitate to contact us for clarification.
