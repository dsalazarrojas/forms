# Low Text Communication Preference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Low Text Communication Preference Form is a tool used to collect and document your communication preferences. This form will help us tailor our communication to best meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:
1. Select one of the contact methods you would like to use to communicate with us (Phone, Email, Text Message, In Person, or Other).
2. Choose the best time for us to contact you (Once, Twice, Daily, As Needed).
3. If you have a preferred contact email address, enter it in the corresponding field.
4. If you have a contact phone number, enter it in the corresponding field.
5. You may also provide an alternate contact phone number for our records.
6. Indicate how often you would like to be contacted (Once, Twice, Daily, As Needed).
7. Lastly, specify your preferred language for communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Preferred Contact Method (Preferred Contact Method)**: Select one of the following contact methods: Phone, Email, Text Message, In Person, or Other.
- **Best Contact Time (Best Contact Time)**: Choose the best time for us to contact you (Once, Twice, Daily, As Needed).
- **Contact Email Address (Contact Email Address)**: Enter your preferred contact email address.
- **Contact Phone Number (Contact Phone Number)**: Enter your contact phone number.
- **Alternate Contact Phone Number (Alternate Contact Phone Number)**: Provide an alternate contact phone number (if applicable).
- **Communication Frequency (Communication Frequency)**: Indicate how often you would like to be contacted (Once, Twice, Daily, As Needed).
- **Preferred Language (Preferred Language)**: Specify your preferred language for communication.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
* If you need assistance with completing this form, don't hesitate to ask.
