# leadership_style_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The leadership_style_questionnaire is designed to help you assess your personal leadership style, preferences, and approaches. By completing this form, you will gain insight into your strengths and areas for growth as a leader.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Each question is presented on a separate page, allowing you to focus on each topic in turn.
2. Be as thorough as possible in your answers, as this will help you gain the most insight.
3. You can complete the form at your own pace, and there is no time limit.
4. Review your answers before submitting and make any necessary revisions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **leadership_style** (Page 1): Describe your personal leadership style, including your values, goals, and decision-making processes.
* **preferences** (Page 2): What are your preferred methods for leading and interacting with others? Describe your approach to communication, collaboration, and conflict resolution.
* **approaches** (Page 3): What leadership approaches do you typically use to achieve your goals and manage your team? Describe your methods for delegating tasks, managing time, and setting priorities.
* **summary** (Page 4): Summarize your key strengths and areas for growth as a leader, based on your previous answers.
* **notes** (Page 5): Add any additional thoughts, reflections, or questions you have about your leadership style and preferences.
