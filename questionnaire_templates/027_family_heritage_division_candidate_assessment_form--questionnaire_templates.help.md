# Family Heritage Division Candidate Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for evaluating a candidate's suitability for a position within the Family Heritage Division.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the candidate's name and contact information, including their phone number and email address.
2. Select the candidate's job title from the provided options.
3. Choose the candidate's experience level from the provided options.
4. Choose the division the candidate is being assessed for.
5. Evaluate the candidate's job qualifications, background, and education.
6. Select the candidate's recommended status (Highly Recommended, Recommended, Not Recommended) and evaluation (Very Good, Good, Fair, Bad).
7. Provide any additional comments or next steps for the candidate.
8. Enter the date of the assessment.
9. Enter your name and title for the assessor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the candidate's full name.
* **Candidates Phone** (`candidate_phone`, `text`, required/true): Enter the candidate's phone number.
* **Candidate Email** (`candidate_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the candidate's email address.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `select_one`, required/true): Select the candidate's job title from the options provided.
* **Candidate Experience** (`candidate_experience`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Choose the candidate's level of experience from the options provided.
* **Division** (`division`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the division the candidate is being assessed for.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the candidate's department (if applicable).
* **Reporting Line** (`reporting_line`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the candidate's reporting line (if applicable).
* **Team** (`team`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the candidate's team (if applicable).
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `note`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the job the candidate is applying for.
* **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any specific job requirements or qualifications.
* **Candidate Background** (`candidate_background`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any relevant background information about the candidate.
* **Candidate Education** (`candidate_education`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any relevant education information about the candidate.
* **Job Qualifications** (`job_qualifications`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any relevant job qualifications or skills.
* **Evaluation** (`evaluation`, `select_one`, required/optional): Evaluate the candidate's overall performance using the provided options.
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Select the candidate's recommended status from the options provided.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any next steps or follow-up actions for the candidate.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback for the candidate.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback for the candidate.
* **Final Decision** (`final_decision`, `select_one`, required/true): Select the final decision for the candidate (Hire or Not Hire).
* **Candidate References** (`candidate_references`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Evaluate the candidate's references using the provided options.
* **Final Comments** (`final_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any final comments or feedback for the candidate.

* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, `date`, required/true): Enter the date of the assessment.
