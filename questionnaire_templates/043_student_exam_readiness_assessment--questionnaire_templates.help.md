# Student Exam Readiness Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Exam Readiness Assessment is a form designed to help students evaluate their understanding and prepare for exams. It collects information about their pre-exam feedback, student ID, contact information, and extra support needs. This data helps teachers and administrators identify students who may require additional support during exams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions honestly and accurately. This will give the most accurate picture of your current understanding.
2. Fill out each section completely. Incomplete forms may not be able to be accurately evaluated.
3. If you are unsure about any of the fields, ask your teacher or instructor for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pre Exam Feedback** (`pre_exam_feedback`, text, required: false): Answer any comments or concerns you have about your current understanding or preparation for the exam.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required: false): Enter your student ID number as issued by the school or institution.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Extra Support Needed** (`extra_support_needed`, select_multiple, required: false): Check any of the following that you think you may need extra support with:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the exam.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the exam.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, ask your teacher or instructor for guidance.
* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* Complete the form as thoroughly as possible.
* If you are unable to complete the form, please contact your teacher or instructor for assistance.
