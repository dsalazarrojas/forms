# AI Mentor Customization Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to help mentors understand their students' preferences and tailor their support to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your user profile (optional).
2. Select your experience level with the subject matter (required).
3. Choose up to three primary learning goals (required).
4. Select your preferred mentor tone (required).
5. Choose how often you'd like to receive feedback from your mentor (required).
6. (Optional) Provide personalization preferences, such as topics to prioritize.
7. (Optional) Select your preferred communication channels (e.g., email, in-app messaging, video call, or instant chat).
8. (Optional) Specify any accessibility requirements.
9. (Optional) Add any additional notes for mentor customization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- User Profile --**: This section is optional and allows you to provide information about yourself. Please fill it out if you'd like to share your background and experience with your mentor.
* **Name**: Enter your name for reference and identification purposes.
* **Experience level with subject matter**: Choose the best fit from the options provided, as this will help your mentor understand your current skill level and provide relevant guidance.
	+ Beginner: You have little to no experience with the subject matter.
	+ Intermediate: You have some experience, but you're looking for more in-depth understanding and guidance.
	+ Advanced: You're skilled and looking for advanced topics and nuanced discussions.
	+ Expert: You're highly experienced and looking for expert-level feedback and guidance.
* **Primary learning goals**: Select up to three goals that align with your learning objectives. This will help your mentor tailor their approach to your needs.
	+ Improve fundamentals: You want to learn the basics and build a solid foundation.
	+ Apply to projects: You want to apply your knowledge to real-world projects and scenarios.
	+ Prepare for certification: You're preparing for a certification and want to focus on the exam format.
	+ Mentorship and feedback: You want a more personalized mentorship experience with regular feedback.
* **Preferred mentor tone**: Choose the tone you'd like your mentor to use during sessions. Some options include:
	+ Encouraging: Focus on encouragement and motivation.
	+ Direct and concise: Straightforward and to the point.
	+ Technical and detailed: In-depth and thorough explanations.
	+ Socratic and questioning: A more inquisitive and questioning approach.
* **Desired feedback frequency**: Choose how often you'd like to receive feedback from your mentor.
	+ After every session: Feedback after each session.
	+ Weekly: Regular feedback once a week.
	+ Biweekly: Feedback every other week.
	+ Monthly: Less frequent feedback.
* **Personalization preferences**: If you have specific topics you'd like your mentor to prioritize, provide them here.
* **Preferred communication channels**: Select your preferred method of communication with your mentor (e.g., email, in-app messaging, video call, or instant chat).
* **Accessibility requirements**: If you have any accessibility needs or requirements, such as accommodations for a disability, please specify them here.
* **Additional notes**: Provide any additional information you'd like to share with your mentor for their reference.

Please note that fields marked as "optional" are not required, and you can leave them blank if you don't have any information to provide.
