# Faculty Teaching Resources Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Faculty Teaching Resources Questionnaire is designed to collect feedback from faculty members about their teaching experiences, resources, and preferences. The information provided will help us understand how often faculty members use teaching resources, what resources they use, and what we can improve to better support them.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Make sure to select all applicable options for each question.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you use teaching resources?** (id: 2, required: true)
	+ This question helps us understand how frequently you use teaching resources.
	+ Select one of the options: Always, Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely.
* **Email address** (id: 3, required: true)
	+ Please provide your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Additional comments** (id: 4, required: false)
	+ Use this field to provide any additional comments or suggestions you might have.
* **Teaching time** (id: 5, required: true)
	+ Please enter your teaching time to help us understand your schedule better.
* **Date** (id: 6, required: true)
	+ Please enter the date for your teaching session or event.
* **Phone number** (id: 7, required: false)
	+ If you have a phone number, please provide it so we can contact you if needed.
* **What do you use?** (id: 8, required: false)
	+ Describe the teaching resources you use, such as textbooks, online materials, or software.
* **Do you find our resources helpful?** (id: 9, required: false)
	+ Select all options that apply to your experience with our resources.
* **What do you like best?** (id: 10, required: false)
	+ Select all options that apply to your favorite features or aspects of our resources.
* **What do you like least?** (id: 11, required: false)
	+ Select all options that apply to your least favorite features or aspects of our resources.
* **What could we improve?** (id: 12, required: false)
	+ Select all options that apply to areas where you think we could improve our resources.
* **What would you like to see?** (id: 13, required: false)
	+ Select all options that apply to features or resources you would like to see in the future.
* **Do you have any additional comments?** (id: 14, required: false)
	+ Use this field to provide any additional comments or suggestions you might have.
