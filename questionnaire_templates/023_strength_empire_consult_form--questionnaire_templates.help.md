# Strength Empire Consult Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Strength Empire Consult Form is designed to collect user information for the Strength Empire team to create tailored guidance for each user. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form to help us better understand your strengths and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each section carefully and accurately. Make sure to select all that apply when answering "Which one of the following is your goal?".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (`first_name`, `text`, required: false)
Please enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed.

### Last Name (`last_name`, `text`, required: false)
Please enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed.

### Email (`email`, `email`, required: false)
Enter your valid email address. This will be used for future communication with you.

### Phone (`phone`, `text`, required: false)
Please enter a valid phone number for contact purposes.

### Address (`address`, `note`, required: false)
If you'd like to share an address, this is the space to do so. Note that this field is not required and your address will not be saved if left blank.

### Date of Birth (`date_birth`, `date`, required: false)
Please enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YY format.

### Time of Day (`time_work`, `time`, required: false)
Enter your time of day as you typically like to work.

### What's your goal? (`goal_description`, `text`, required: false)
Tell us about a specific goal or objective you'd like to achieve. Be as detailed as you'd like!

### Which one of the following is your goal? (`selected_goals`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Select all that apply from the following options:
* Option 1
* Option 2
* Option 3

### Other (specify) (`other_goal`, `text`, required: false)
If none of the above options apply to you, please specify your own goal.

### Best Time (`best_time`, `time`, required: false)
Enter your most productive time of day.
