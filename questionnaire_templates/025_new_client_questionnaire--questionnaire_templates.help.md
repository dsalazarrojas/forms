# New Client Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information about new clients, including their company name, contact name, email address, and phone number. This data will be used by the business development team to facilitate follow-up communication and collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the company name of the new client.
2. Enter the name of the contact person responsible for communication.
3. Enter the email address of the contact person.
4. Enter the phone number of the contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person you will be contacting for business purposes.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address associated with the contact person. This will be used for communication and follow-up.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person. This will be used for scheduling calls and meetings.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you need to update or change the contact information, please contact your team lead.
- If you're unsure about any field or have any further questions, please reach out to your team lead for assistance.
