# Alcohol Craving Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the level of craving and triggers for individuals experiencing alcohol cravings. It helps clinicians evaluate the patient's current state and identify potential areas for treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Section 1: -- Craving Assessment --**: This section asks you to rate your level of agreement with statements related to your current craving. Please respond based on your immediate feelings, using the scale provided (1-7).
2.  **Patient Initials or ID**: Enter your name or ID as it appears in our system.
3.  **Date and Time**: Enter the current date and time.
4.  **Field-by-Field Explanation**

### 1. I have a desire for a drink right now (`craving_intensity_1`)

*   **Label:** I have a desire for a drink right now
*   **Type:** Number
*   **Required:** Yes
*   **Description:** This question asks about your current desire for a drink. Please rate your level of agreement on a scale of 1 (Strongly Disagree) to 7 (Strongly Agree).

### 2. Drinking would be very pleasant right now (`craving_intensity_2`)

*   **Label:** Drinking would be very pleasant right now
*   **Type:** Number
*   **Required:** Yes
*   **Description:** This question gauges your perception of drinking being pleasurable at this moment. Indicate your level of agreement on the 1 (1) to 7 (Very Agree) scale.

### 3. If I had a drink in front of me, I would drink it (`craving_intensity_3`)

*   **Label:** If I had a drink in front of me, I would drink it
*   **Type:** Number
*   **Required:** Yes
*   **Description:** This question assesses your inclination to drink if a drink were readily available. Rate your agreement on the 1 to 7 scale.

### 4. I feel I could easily limit my drinking if I started now (`craving_intensity_4`)

*   **Label:** I feel I could easily limit my drinking if I started now
*   **Type:** Number
*   **Required:** Yes
*   **Description:** This question explores your confidence in self-control over drinking. Indicate your level of agreement on the 1 (1) to 7 (Very Agree) scale.

### 5. I would feel better if I could have a drink (`craving_intensity_5`)

*   **Label:** I would feel better if I could have a drink
*   **Type:** Number
*   **Required:** Yes
*   **Description:** This question examines the perceived benefit of drinking. Rate your level of agreement on the 1 (1) to 7 (Very Agree) scale.

### 6. -- Triggers and Context -- (`section_triggers`)

*   **Label:** -- Triggers and Context --
*   **Type:** Note
*   **Required:** No
*   **Description:** This section explores the situations or stimuli that trigger cravings.

### 7. What triggered your current craving? (`current_trigger`)

*   **Label:** What triggered your current craving?
*   **Type:** Select One
*   **Required:** Yes
*   **Description:** Identify the specific trigger that prompted your current craving. Choose from the provided options or select "No obvious trigger" if none apply.

### 8. Are you experiencing physical symptoms of craving? (`physical_symptoms`)

*   **Label:** Are you experiencing physical symptoms of craving?
*   **Type:** Select Multiple
*   **Required:** Yes
*   **Description:** List any bodily sensations you're experiencing due to craving. Choose from the given options.

### 9. Current Coping Strategies (`coping_strategies`)

*   **Label:** Current Coping Strategies
*   **Type:** Text
*   **Required:** No
*   **Description:** Describe any strategies or actions you're using to manage your cravings.

### 10. How long has the current craving lasted? (`craving_duration`)

*   **Label:** How long has the current craving lasted?
*   **Type:** Select One
*   **Required:** Yes
*   **Description:** Indicate the duration of your current craving.

### 11. Clinical Observations (For Staff) (`additional_observations_clinical`)

*   **Label:** Clinical Observations (For Staff)
*   **Type:** Text
*   **Required:** No
*   **Description:** Provide any additional comments or observations to aid in your treatment.

### 12. I am committed to managing my cravings today (`affirmation_statement`)

*   **Label:** I am committed to managing my cravings today
*   **Type:** Select One
*   **Required:** Yes
*   **Description:** Commitment to manage cravings. Choose from the options to affirm your dedication.

## Tips

*   Be honest and accurate in your responses.
*   Rate your feelings and experiences as they are in the moment, without bias or influence.
*   For the triggers and physical symptoms sections, select all applicable options.
*   If you have any additional comments or concerns, use the clinical observations field.
*   Your commitment to managing your cravings is a critical part of this form.
