# Prequalification Questionnaire Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prequalification Questionnaire Form Template is designed to collect essential information about a company's characteristics and capabilities to help evaluate potential collaborations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the requested information about your company. If you're unsure about any field, you can refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required/optional): This is the name of your company. Please enter the name as it appears on your business card or website.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required/optional): This is the name of the person in charge of your company's operations or the person who will be handling this prequalification process.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your company's email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): This is the primary contact number for your company.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your company's address for location information.
* **Financial Details** (`financial_details`, `text`, required/optional): Share some financial information about your company, such as revenue or budget, to help us assess your financial stability.
* **Legal Details** (`legal_details`, `text`, required/optional): This is where you can provide any relevant legal information about your company, such as registration numbers or certifications.
* **Operational Details** (`operational_details`, `text`, required/optional): Share some operational information about your company, including any notable achievements or challenges.
* **Supplier Type** (`supplier_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of business you belong to: Small Business, Medium Business, or Large Business.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the size of your company: Less than 50, 50-100, 100-200, 200-500, 500-1000, 1000-5000, or More than 5000.
* **Annual Revenue** (`annual_revenue`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your company's annual revenue for the past year.
* **Employee Count** (`employee_count`, `number`, required/optional): Provide the number of employees in your company.
* **Company Status** (`company_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the status of your company: Active, Inactive, or Pending.
* **Certification** (`certification`, `select_one`, required/optional): Check if your company has any relevant certifications.
* **Time In Business** (`time_in_business`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select how long your company has been in business: Less than 1 year, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, or More than 5 years.
* **Industry** (`industry`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the industry your company operates in: Healthcare, Technology, Financial Services, Manufacturing, Construction, or Other.
* **Certification Date** (`certification_date`, `date`, required/optional): If your company has any certifications, provide the date it was obtained.
* **Contract Duration** (`contract_duration`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the duration of the contract: Less than 1 year, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, or More than 5 years.
* **Contract Value** (`contract_value`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the contract value for the prequalification process.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or explanations about your company or this prequalification process.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the title of the contact person for this prequalification process.

## Tips
Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date to avoid any potential issues or misrepresentation. If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
