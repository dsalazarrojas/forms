# Healthcare Necessities Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthcare Necessities Analysis Form is designed to gather essential information about a patient's health context and necessities. This form helps healthcare providers assess the patient's needs and plan appropriate care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide information about the patient, including their data, healthcare context, and access challenges (if any).
2.  Select the healthcare necessities the patient requires from the available options.
3.  Record the patient's medical history, if applicable.
4.  Plan follow-up actions and procedures with the patient.
5.  Document any necessary clinic reviews or consultations.
6.  Provide the patient's submitted information, including their email and the date and time they submitted the form.
7.  Optionally, provide additional comments or observations about the patient's clinic visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **patient_data** (text, required/optional): Enter the patient's personal details, such as their name, address, and contact information.
*   **healthcare_context** (text, required/optional): Describe the patient's healthcare context, including any relevant medical history, medications, or allergies.
*   **access_challenges** (number, required/optional): If the patient has any access challenges, such as language barriers or mobility issues, report them here.
*   **client_data** (select multiple, required/optional): Select from the options provided to indicate the patient's needs or conditions.
*   **healthcare_necessities** (select one, required/optional): Choose from the options provided to determine the patient's healthcare necessities.
*   **medical_history** (date, required/optional): Record the patient's medical history, including any relevant dates or events.
*   **follow_up_plans** (select multiple, required/optional): Plan the follow-up actions and procedures with the patient.
*   **clinic_review** (time, required/optional): Document the time of the clinic review or consultation.
*   **submitted_by** (email, required/optional): Enter the patient's email address to confirm their submission.
*   **submitted_date** (time, required/optional): Record the date and time the patient submitted the form.
*   **submitted_time** (text, required/optional): Provide a timestamp for when the patient submitted the form.
*   **clinic_comments** (text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or observations about the patient's clinic visit.
*   **patient_id** (text, required/optional): Enter the patient's ID number for reference.
*   **assigned_provider** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the healthcare provider assigned to the patient.
