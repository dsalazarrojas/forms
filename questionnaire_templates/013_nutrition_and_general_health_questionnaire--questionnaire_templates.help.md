# Nutrition And General Health Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a person's nutrition and general health habits, medical history, and contact details. It is meant to gather data for a specific purpose, such as health assessment or research. Please note that this form is not intended for emergency medical situations, and it is recommended to seek medical attention if you have an urgent health concern.

<thinking>Before filling out this form, please ensure that you are not duplicating the information and that it is relevant to your situation. If you are unsure about any of the questions or fields, please contact the administrator for further guidance.</thinking>

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Choose your answer for each question from the provided options.
2.  Enter any relevant information in the text fields.
3.  Select your age, height, and weight accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nutrition And General Health Questionnaire Page 1** (`nutrition_and_general_health_questionnaire_page1`, select_one, required):
    This question asks you about the overall health and wellness of your diet. Answering "Yes" indicates that you generally follow a healthy diet, while "No" indicates otherwise.
* **Health History** (`health_history`, text, optional):
    This field allows you to provide any relevant health history, such as previous medical conditions, allergies, or medical treatments. Please include any relevant information to help with your health assessment.
* **Food Intake** (`food_intake`, text, required):
    Please list any food or drinks you regularly consume, including portion sizes and frequency.
* **Food and Beverage Intake** (`food_beverage_intake`, select_multiple, required):
    This question asks about your consumption of food and beverages. Choose "Yes" if you consume these items regularly, and "No" if you do not.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_one, required):
    Please answer "Yes" if you have any medical conditions, such as diabetes, high blood pressure, or allergies, and "No" if you do not.
* **Medical Treatments** (`medical_treatments`, select_multiple, optional):
    This field allows you to list any medical treatments or therapies you are currently undergoing.
* **Medication Intake** (`medication_intake`, select_multiple, optional):
    Please list any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, required):
    Choose "Yes" for any known allergies, and "No" if you do not have any allergies.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, date, required):
    Please provide your medical history, including dates of any previous health issues.
* **Height** (`height_1`, number, required):
    Enter your height in centimeters.
* **Weight** (`weight_1`, number, required):
    Enter your weight in kilograms.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required):
    Enter your age in years.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number_1`, text, required):
    Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Email** (`email_1`, email, required):
    Enter your primary email address.
* **Time** (`time_1`, time, required):
    Enter the current time of day in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
