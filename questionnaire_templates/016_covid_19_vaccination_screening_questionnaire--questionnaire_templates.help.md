# COVID 19 Vaccination Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The COVID 19 Vaccination Screening Questionnaire is designed to collect essential information about individuals seeking COVID-19 vaccination. This includes demographic information, medical history, and vaccination history to ensure safety and efficacy of the vaccination process. The goal is to identify potential risks and allergies to provide a safe and effective vaccination experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter your demographic information**:
	* `Full Name`: Please fill in your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
	* `Date of Birth`: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY (e.g., 01/01/2020).
	* `Email Address`: Enter your valid email address for contact and verification purposes.
	* `Phone Number`: Enter a valid phone number for contact and follow-up purposes.
2. **Medical History**:
	* `Have you had symptoms of COVID-19 in the last 2 weeks?`: Answer "True" if you have experienced symptoms such as fever, cough, loss of taste or smell, etc. in the last 14 days.
	* `Have you tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 90 days?`: Answer "True" if you have tested positive for COVID-19 in the last 3 months.
	* `Close contact with confirmed COVID-19 case`: Answer "True" if you have been in close contact with someone who has tested positive for COVID-19 in the last 14 days.
3. **Health Conditions**:
	* `Have you been diagnosed with heart disease?`: Answer "True" if you have been diagnosed with heart disease.
	* `Have you been diagnosed with lung disease or asthma?`: Answer "True" if you have been diagnosed with lung disease or asthma.
	* `Do you have diabetes?`: Answer "True" if you have been diagnosed with diabetes.
	* `Do you have chronic kidney disease?`: Answer "True" if you have been diagnosed with chronic kidney disease.
	* `Are you immunocompromised or have HIV?`: Answer "True" if you have a weakened immune system or have been diagnosed with HIV.
	* `Currently undergoing cancer treatment?`: Answer "True" if you are currently undergoing cancer treatment.
	* `Have you had an organ or stem cell transplant?`: Answer "True" if you have undergone an organ or stem cell transplant.
	* `Other significant health conditions`: If you have other health conditions not listed, please provide details in the text field.
4. **Medications and Allergies**:
	* `Are you taking any medications?`: Answer "True" if you are currently taking any medications.
	* `If yes, list medications`: If you answered "True", please list your medications in the text field.
	* `Are you allergic to any vaccine components?`: Answer "True" if you are allergic to any vaccine components.
	* `History of severe allergic reactions or anaphylaxis?`: Answer "True" if you have experienced a severe allergic reaction or anaphylaxis.
	* `Describe allergy or reaction`: If you answered "True", please describe the allergy or reaction in the text field.
5. **Pregnancy Status**:
	* `Are you pregnant?`: Answer "True" if you are pregnant.
	* `If yes, describe pregnancy status`: If you answered "True", please provide additional details in the text field.
	* `Are you breastfeeding?`: Answer "True" if you are breastfeeding.
6. **Vaccination History**:
	* `Have you received a COVID-19 vaccine before?`: Answer "True" if you have received a COVID-19 vaccine in the past.
	* `If yes, which vaccine`: If you answered "True", please select the type of vaccine you received from the options.
	* `Date of last dose`: If you answered "True", please enter the date of your last dose in the format MM/DD/YY.
