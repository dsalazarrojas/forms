# Remote University Class Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from students about their experience with remote university classes. Your responses will help us understand what works well and what can be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Provide your name and email (if you choose to share it).
3. Select the relevant options for each question.
4. Add any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your university record or ID card.
* **Course Title**: Enter the title of the course you're taking or have taken.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: How satisfied are you with the course overall? (High, Low)
* **Instruction Quality**: How would you rate the quality of instruction in this course? (Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Below Expectations)
* **Communication**: How would you rate the communication from the instructor and other staff? (Good, Fair, Poor)
* **Content**: How engaging is the course content? (Highly engaging, Fairly engaging, Not engaging at all)
* **Interaction**: How engaging is the interaction between you and the instructor? (Good, Fair, Poor)
* **Feedback**: Share any feedback or comments you have about the course.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share.
* **Email** (optional): Provide your email address for follow-up or further discussion.
* **Phone Number** (optional): Provide your phone number for follow-up or further discussion.
* **Instructor Name**: Enter the name of your instructor (if applicable).
* **Instructor Title**: Enter the title of your instructor (if applicable).
* **Course Instructor**: Enter the name of the course instructor (if applicable).
* **Course Name**: Enter the name of the course (if applicable).
* **Course Level**: What level of study is the course? (Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, Senior)
* **Course Format**: What format is the course (Online, On-campus, Hybrid)?
* **Semester**: Which semester is the course taking place in? (Fall, Spring, Summer)
* **Time Commitment**: How much time commitment does the course require? (High, Medium, Low)
* **Difficulty Level**: What is the difficulty level of the course? (Easy, Medium, Hard)
* **Course Satisfaction**: How satisfied are you with the course overall? (High, Medium, Low)
* **Likely to Teach**: Are you likely to teach this course in the future? (Yes, No)
* **Overall Rating**: Rate the course overall (1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest).
* **Additional Comments**: Add any final comments or feedback you'd like to share.
