# Pre Test Assessment: Cooling Systems - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate a user's background and qualifications in cooling systems before they take a test. The information gathered will be used to gauge their knowledge and experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly and accurately based on your background and experience.
2. You can select multiple options for the "What type of certification do you hold?" question.
3. You can select only one option for the "How many years of experience do you have with HVAC systems?" and "What type of experience do you have with HVAC systems?" questions.
4. Fill out the form in the order of the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (1)**: This is a note field providing information about the form purpose and guidelines.
* **How many years of experience do you have with HVAC systems? (2)**: This question helps us gauge your level of experience with HVAC systems. Enter a numerical value that accurately represents your years of experience.
* **What type of experience do you have with HVAC systems? (3)**: Select one of the options that best describes your experience with HVAC systems. This will help us understand your specific experience in this field.
* **What type of certification do you hold? (4)**: Select all certifications that you hold related to HVAC systems. This will help us understand your qualifications.
* **What type of certification do you hold? (5)**: Select only one certification that you hold related to HVAC systems. This is a duplicate of the previous question but with a different type. 

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask for clarification before proceeding.
* Ensure you have taken this form previously, or have any certifications that are not listed as options.
