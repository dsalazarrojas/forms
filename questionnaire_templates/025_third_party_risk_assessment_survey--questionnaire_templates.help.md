# Third Party Risk Assessment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Third Party Risk Assessment Survey is a tool used to evaluate and manage risks associated with third-party vendors. It helps in assessing the level of risk and control measures in place for each vendor.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the vendor's name and address.
2. Enter the vendor's website and contact person's name and email.
3. Select the type of review (Risk Management, IT Security, Procurement, or Other) that applies to the vendor.
4. Choose the review frequency (Annually, Semi-Annually, Quarterly, Monthly, or Other) for the vendor.
5. Enter any additional comments or review comments for the vendor.
6. Indicate the type of vendor (Type A, Type B, Type C, or Type D) based on its risk level.
7. Enter the vendor's current risk level (numerical value).
8. Record the vendor's control measures (text response).
9. Check the status of the vendor (Active, Inactive, or Terminated).
10. Enter the date of the last review and the expected next review date.
11. Enter any comments on the vendor's status.
12. Enter any review notes or comments.
13. Enter the date and time of the review.
14. Assign a numerical rating to the vendor's review.
15. Enter any comments on the vendor's review status.
16. Finally, select the review status (Completed, In Progress, or Pending).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Third-Party Risk Assessment Survey**: This is the title of the form.
* **vendor_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the vendor being assessed.
* **vendor_address** (Text, Optional): Enter the address of the vendor.
* **vendor_website** (Text, Optional): Enter the website of the vendor.
* **contact_person** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the contact person for the vendor.
* **contact_email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email of the contact person for the vendor.
* **contact_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person for the vendor.
* **review_type** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose one or multiple review types that apply to the vendor (Risk Management, IT Security, Procurement, or Other).
* **review_frequency** (Select One, Optional): Choose the review frequency for the vendor (Annually, Semi-Annually, Quarterly, Monthly, or Other).
* **review_comment** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or review comments for the vendor.
* **vendor_type** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the type of vendor (Type A, Type B, Type C, or Type D) based on its risk level.
* **vendor_risk_level** (Number, Optional): Enter the vendor's current risk level (numerical value).
* **vendor_control_measure** (Text, Optional): Record the vendor's control measures.
* **vendor_status** (Select One, Optional): Check the status of the vendor (Active, Inactive, or Terminated).
* **vendor_last_review_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the last review.
* **vendor_next_review_date** (Text, Optional): Enter the expected next review date.
* **comments** (Note, Optional): Enter any comments on the vendor's status.
* **review_notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any review notes or comments.
* **review_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the review.
* **review_time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of the review.
* **vendor_review_rating** (Number, Optional): Assign a numerical rating to the vendor's review.
* **vendor_status_comment** (Text, Optional): Enter any comments on the vendor's review status.
* **review_comments** (Text, Optional): Enter any comments on the review.
* **review_comment** (Text, Optional): Enter any comments on the review.
* **review_status** (Select One, Optional): Select the review status (Completed, In Progress, or Pending).
