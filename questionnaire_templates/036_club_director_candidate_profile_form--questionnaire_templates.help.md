# Club Director Candidate Profile Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Club Director Candidate Profile Form is designed to collect information from potential candidates for the position of Club Director. It will be reviewed by the selection committee to determine each candidate's suitability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Be honest and provide as much detail as possible in each field.
3. Review your answers before submitting to ensure they are complete and make sense.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`candidate_name`, `text`, required): This field is for the candidate's full name, as it will be used to identify and address the candidate in all communications.
* **Email Address** (`candidate_email`, `email`, required): This field is for the candidate's email address, which will be used for communication and correspondence.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): This field is for the candidate's phone number, which will be used for communication and scheduling.
* **Current Position in Club** (`current_position`, `text`, required): This field is for the candidate's current position within the club, such as member, officer, or committee member.
* **Years Member of Club** (`years_in_club`, `number`, required): This field is for the number of years the candidate has been a member of the club.
* **Leadership Background** (`leadership_background`, `text`, required): This field is for the candidate's leadership experience, including prior roles held in the club or other organizations.
* **Relevant Skills and Competencies** (`relevant_skills`, `text`, required): This field is for the candidate's relevant skills and competencies, such as organizational, communication, and decision-making skills.
* **Prior Director or Chair Experience** (`prior_director_experience`, `select_one`, required): This field is for the candidate's experience as a director or chair of a club or organization. Options include: Yes - this club, Yes - other organization, No experience, Limited experience.
* **Vision for Club During Term** (`vision_statement`, `text`, required): This field is for the candidate's vision for the club during their term as director.
* **Key Initiatives or Projects Planned** (`key_initiatives`, `text`, required): This field is for the candidate's key initiatives or projects they plan to accomplish as director.
* **Challenges Facing the Club Currently** (`challenges_facing`, `text`, required): This field is for the candidate's identification of challenges facing the club and how they would address them.
* **Top 3 Leadership Strengths** (`leadership_strengths`, `text`, required): This field is for the candidate's top 3 leadership strengths.
* **Areas for Personal Growth** (`improvement_areas`, `text`, required): This field is for the candidate's areas of personal growth and development.
* **Decision-Making Style** (`decision_making`, `select_one`, required): This field is for the candidate's decision-making style, with options: Collaborative and consensus-based, Decisive and independent, Data-driven, Balanced approach.
* **How to Handle Conflict** (`conflict_resolution`, `text`, required): This field is for the candidate's approach to handling conflict.
* **Hours Per Week Available** (`time_commitment`, `number`, required): This field is for the number of hours the candidate is committed to dedicate per week to the director role.
* **Commitment to Full Term** (`full_term_commitment`, `select_one`, required): This field is for the candidate's commitment to serve the full term, with options: Yes, fully committed, Yes, mostly committed, Uncertain, No.
* **Endorsements or References** (`endorsements`, `text`, required): This field is for the names of club members who support the candidate's candidacy.
