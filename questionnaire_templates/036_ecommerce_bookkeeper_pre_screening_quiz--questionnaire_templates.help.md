# ecommerce-bookkeeper-pre-screening-quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ecommerce-bookkeeper-pre-screening-quiz form is designed to gather information from potential candidates to assess their qualification and fit for a bookkeeping role in an e-commerce setting. The form will ask a series of questions to gather details about the candidate's current salary, bonuses, work experience, availability, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer the questions accurately and honestly.
- Make sure to enter the correct date in the 'what is the date you started working for your current employer' field in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
- Enter your email address in the 'what is your work email' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Ecommerce Bookkeeper Pre Screening Quiz
- This page is an introduction to the form and its purpose.

### 2. What is your current annual salary
- **What is your current annual salary** (`annual_salary`, `number`, required): This field is for you to enter your current annual salary.

### 3. What is your current annual bonuses
- **What is your current annual bonuses** (`bonuses`, `number`, required): This field is for you to enter your current annual bonuses.

### 4. Have you ever worked as a bookkeeper
- **Have you ever worked as a bookkeeper** (`experience`, `select_one`, required): Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you have worked as a bookkeeper before.

### 5. What is the name of your current employer
- **What is the name of your current employer** (`employer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your current employer in this field.

### 6. What is the date you started working for your current employer
- **What is the date you started working for your current employer** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you started working for your current employer in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.

### 7. How many hours do you work per week
- **How many hours do you work per week** (`hours_per_week`, `number`, required): Enter the number of hours you work per week.

### 8. What is your work email
- **What is your work email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your work email address in this field.

### 9. What is your availability for an interview
- **What is your availability for an interview** (`availability`, `time`, required): Enter your availability for an interview in this field.

### 10. Do you have any relevant certifications
- **Do you have any relevant certifications** (`certifications`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you have any relevant certifications.

### 11. How can we reach you
- **How can we reach you** (`contact_method`, `text`, required): Enter the method of contact you prefer, such as phone, email, or text.

### 12. What is your preferred contact method
- **What is your preferred contact method** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, required): Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your preferred contact method.
Please make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure that your application is complete and can be properly reviewed.
