# Generation Z Parent Interview Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about Generation Z parents' habits, preferences, and behaviors, particularly regarding their digital habits and screen time. It's intended to help us understand their perspectives and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and fill out the relevant questions. For select multiple or select one fields, choose one or more options that apply to you. You can add notes or comments in the "notes" field if you have any additional information. For text fields, enter your answers in the space provided. For date fields, use the date format YYYY-MM-DD. For time fields, use the 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
