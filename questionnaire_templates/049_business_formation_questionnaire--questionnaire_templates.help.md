# Business Formation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps gather essential information about a business concept, including founder information, business idea, target market, and financial planning.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the required fields for the founder information, including full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Describe your business idea and the problem it solves.
3. Identify your target market and the size of the market.
4. Outline your competitive advantage and how you plan to achieve it.
5. Choose the preferred entity type for your business and the number of founders.
6. Describe the roles and responsibilities of each founder.
7. Determine how ownership will be divided and plan your financials, including startup capital and funding sources.
8. Estimate the break-even timeline for your business.
9. If applicable, identify any special licenses or permits required to operate your business.
10. Finally, note any key regulatory compliance areas to consider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Founder Name** (`Founder Name`, `text`, required): This field is for your full name as the founder.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, required): This is your business email address.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required): This is your contact phone number.
* **Previous Business Experience** (`Previous Business Experience`, `select_one`, required): This field helps us understand your background as a founder.
* **Business Concept** (`-- Business Concept --`, `note`, required): This is a note field for you to describe your business idea and concept.
* **Business Idea** (`Business Idea`, `text`, required): This is the core of your business concept.
* **Problem You Solve** (`Problem You Solve`, `text`, required): This field is to describe the problem your business solves.
* **Target Market** (`Target Market`, `text`, required): This is the description of your target market.
* **Market Size Estimate** (`Market Size Estimate`, `text`, optional): If known, this field is for estimating the size of the market.
* **Competitive Advantage** (`Competitive Advantage`, `text`, required): Describe what makes your business different from others.
* **Preferred Entity Type** (`Preferred Entity Type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the structure type that best fits your business needs.
* **Number of Founders** (`Number of Founders`, `number`, required): Enter the number of founders involved.
* **Founder Roles and Responsibilities** (`Founder Roles and Responsibilities`, `text`, optional): This field is for describing the roles and responsibilities of each founder.
* **Ownership Structure** (`Ownership Structure`, `text`, required): Describe how ownership will be divided among founders.
* **Estimated Startup Capital** (`Estimated Startup Capital`, `number`, required): Enter the estimated amount needed to start your business.
* **Planned Funding Sources** (`Planned Funding Sources`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the sources of funding for your business.
* **Break-Even Timeline** (`Break-Even Timeline`, `number`, optional): If applicable, enter the estimated number of months to reach break-even point.
* **Special Licenses or Permits Needed** (`Special Licenses or Permits Needed`, `text`, optional): If necessary, list any special licenses or permits required.
* **Key Regulatory Compliance Areas** (`Key Regulatory Compliance Areas`, `text`, optional): Note any regulatory compliance areas specific to your business.
