# real_estate_lease_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "real_estate_lease_questionnaire" form is designed to collect and manage information about a real estate lease. This form is a template used by real estate professionals to create and manage leases for their clients.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing the required information on each page.
2. Fill in the text fields with the relevant details about the lease.
3. Choose from the select_one and select_multiple options to specify the lease terms and conditions.
4. Enter the dates and times as required.
5. Enter the rent amount and frequency.
6. Review the form to ensure all required fields are filled in before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lease Details** (`lease_details`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the lease.
* **property_info** (`property_info`, text, required: false): Provide a description of the property involved in the lease.
* **tenant_info** (`tenant_info`, text, required: false): Enter information about the tenant(s) in the lease.
* **Lease Terms** (`lease_terms`, text, required: false): Describe the lease terms and conditions.
* **Lease Signing** (`lease_signing`, text, required: false): Record the date and time of lease signing.
* **Lease Duration** (`lease_duration`, text, required: false): Specify the duration of the lease.
* **Lease Term** (`lease_term`, number, required: false): Enter the lease term length in months or years.
* **Lease Type** (`lease_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose between a fixed or variable lease type.
* **Rent Frequency** (`rent_frequency`, select_one, required: false): Select the rent frequency (e.g., monthly, quarterly, semiannually).
* **Lease Start** (`lease_start`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of the lease.
* **Lease End** (`lease_end`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of the lease.
* **Rent Amount** (`rent_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the rent amount.
* **Rent Frequency** (`rent_frequency_2`, select_one, required: false): Choose between a fixed or variable rent frequency.
* **Lease Term** (`lease_term`, number, required: false): Specify the lease term length in months or years.
* **Move In Date** (`move_in_date`, date, required: false): Enter the move-in date.
* **Move In Time** (`move_in_time`, time, required: false): Enter the move-in time.
* **Move Out Date** (`move_out_date`, date, required: false): Enter the move-out date.
* **Move Out Time** (`move_out_time`, time, required: false): Enter the move-out time.
* **Security Deposit** (`security_deposit`, number, required: false): Enter the security deposit amount.
* **Utilities** (`utilities`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose between the utilities included in the lease (e.g., electricity, gas, water, internet, cable).
* **Other Utilities** (`other_utilities`, text, required: false): Enter any other utilities not listed above.
* **Renter Info** (`renter_info`, text, required: false): Enter information about the renter.
* **Renter Phone** (`renter_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the renter's phone number.
* **Renter Email** (`renter_email`, email, required: false): Enter the renter's email address.
* **Renter Name** (`renter_name`, text, required: false): Enter the renter's name.

## Tips

* Double-check each page to ensure all required fields are filled in.
* Review the form for completeness and accuracy before finalizing the lease.
* Ensure all dates and times are entered correctly.
* Verify the rent amount and frequency with the renter.
