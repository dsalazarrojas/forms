# Traits Inheritance Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the traits of an individual or group, likely for research, educational, or organizational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the required fields:
    *   First Name
    *   Last Name
    *   Email
    *   Student ID
    *   Grade Level
2.  For each "Family Trait" field, select the options that apply to the individual or group being assessed:
    *   Family Trait 1 through 50

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required):
    *   Enter the individual or group's first name.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required):
    *   Enter the individual or group's last name.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required):
    *   Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional):
    *   Enter a phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required):
    *   Enter a unique student ID.
*   **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required):
    *   Select the grade level of the individual or group being assessed.
*   **Family Traits** (`family_trait1` to `family_trait50`, select_multiple, required):
    *   For each "Family Trait" field, select "Yes" if the trait applies to the individual or group, otherwise select "No". 

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all required fields.
*   If unsure about any field, refer to the form's purpose and context.
*   Double-check your answers for accuracy.
*   If you have any questions, contact the form administrator.
