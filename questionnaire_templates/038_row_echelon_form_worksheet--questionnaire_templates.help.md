# Row Echelon Form Worksheet - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to guide users through the steps involved in transforming a given matrix into its Row Echelon Form, a fundamental concept in Linear Algebra.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form in order, from **Step 1 - Given A Matrix** to **Step 6 - Row Reduction Complete**.
2. Enter your name in the **Enter your name** field to receive personalized feedback on your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Enter your name** (`user_name`, text, required): Please enter your name to receive personalized feedback on your submission.
- **Describe your matrix** (`matrix_description`, text, required): Describe the given matrix you want to transform into Row Echelon Form.
- **Step 1 - Given A Matrix** (`step_1_given_matrix`, text, required): Enter the given matrix as text, for example: `[1 2; 3 4]`.
- **Step 2 - Find the Row Echelon** (`step_2_find_row_echelon`, text, required): Describe the row echelon form of the given matrix.
- **Step 3 - Perform Row Operations** (`step_3_perform_row_operations`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to describe the row operation to be performed: 
  - Option 1: Row Swap
  - Option 2: Row Scaling
  - Option 3: Row Addition
- **Step 4 - Find Inverse** (`step_4_find_inverse`, radio, required): Choose one of the options to describe the inverse: 
  - Option 1: Row Echelon Form Inverse
  - Option 2: Not Applicable
- **Step 5 - Find Determinant** (`step_5_find_determinant`, number, required): Enter the determinant of the given matrix, for example: `2`.
- **Step 6 - Row Reduction Complete** (`step_6_row_reduction_complete`, checkbox, required): Check the box when you have completed the row reduction process.
- **Prophet** (`step_7_prophet`, text, required): Enter the name of the mathematical concept or theorem that this example illustrates.
