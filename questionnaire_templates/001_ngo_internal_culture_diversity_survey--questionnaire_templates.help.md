# NGO Internal Culture Diversity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a self-assessment tool aimed at evaluating the internal culture diversity of your NGO, specifically in the areas of inclusion and workplace experience. Your answers will help us better understand the state of diversity in our organization and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select only one option for each question**, as indicated by the "Select One" label.
2.  Answer questions truthfully, as all responses are anonymous and confidential.
3. **Fill out all required questions**, marked with asterisks (\*) next to the question label.
4. **Provide detailed feedback and suggestions** for improving diversity and inclusion in your workplace.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Demographics:** Select your racial/ethnic origin to help us better understand the diversity of our workplace.
    *   **Asian:** If you identify as Asian or have an Asian ethnic origin.
    *   **Black:** If you identify as Black or have a Black ethnic origin.
    *   **Hispanic:** If you identify as Hispanic or have a Hispanic ethnic origin.
    *   **Native American:** If you identify as Native American or have a Native American ethnic origin.
    *   **White:** If you identify as White or have a White ethnic origin.
*   **Inclusion:** Check if you believe that your workplace is inclusive and supportive of diverse backgrounds.
*   **Experience:** Write down any thoughts, concerns, or experiences you may have related to diversity and inclusion in your workplace.
*   **Workplace Experience:** Report how many years you have been with the NGO, to help us better understand the continuity and consistency of our diversity efforts.
*   **Inclusion 2:** Check if you believe that your workplace is an inclusive environment for all employees, regardless of their background.
*   **Cultural Diversity:** Select the cultural activities and events that you have attended or would like to attend, to help us better understand the diversity of our workplace culture.
*   **Feedback:** Share your honest feedback on how diverse you feel our workplace is, and what we could do to improve it.
*   **Suggestions:** If you have any suggestions on how to improve diversity and inclusion in your workplace, please share them with us.
*   **Comments:** If you have any additional comments or concerns, feel free to share them with us.
*   **Submit:** Select "Submit" to finalize your survey and "Save for Later" to save it for later submission.
