# Survey Distribution Timing Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Survey Distribution Timing Questionnaire is a tool used to plan and schedule surveys for internal or external distribution. It ensures consistency in the timing and distribution of surveys, which is crucial for reliable and valid data collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the introduction section with a brief description of your survey.
2.  Enter the date and time you plan to start and end the survey distribution.
3.  Select the distribution channels you plan to use (e.g., email, phone, etc.).
4.  Provide email addresses and phone numbers for the recipients.
5.  Determine the number of days and hours you plan to survey the recipients.
6.  Enter notes and any additional information about the survey.
7.  Confirm your submission to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`): A brief introduction or description of your survey.
    A short paragraph describing the purpose and goals of your survey.
*   **Introduction Date** (`2`, `date`, required: `true`): The date you plan to start the survey distribution.
    Enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` to ensure accurate scheduling.
*   **Introduction Time** (`3`, `time`, required: `true`): The time you plan to start the survey distribution.
    Enter the time in the format `HH:MM:SS` to ensure accurate scheduling.
*   **Distribution Channels** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: `true`): The channels you will use for distributing the survey (e.g., email, phone, etc.).
    Select all the channels you plan to use for the survey distribution.
*   **Channels 1** (`5`, `text`, required: `false`): Additional details about channel 1.
    Enter any notes or details about channel 1.
*   **Channels 1 Options** (`6`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Options for Channel 1.
    Select one option for Channel 1.

*   **Channels 2 Options** (`8`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Options for Channel 2.
