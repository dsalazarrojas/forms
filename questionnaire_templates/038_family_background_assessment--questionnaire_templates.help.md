**Family Background Assessment - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Family Background Assessment form is designed to collect relevant information about an individual's family history, relationships, family structure, and health background. This form will be used for general assessment purposes only.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Use clear and concise language to help the reviewer understand your responses.
3. Ensure you answer all required fields, indicated by a asterisk (\*) next to the question label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Family History** (Family History, text, optional): This section asks about your family's past, including any significant events or experiences that may be relevant to your assessment.
  - **Relationships** (Relationships, text, optional): Describe your relationships with family members, including any siblings, parents, or extended family.
  - **Family Structure** (Family Structure, text, optional): Explain your current family structure, including your family composition, relationships, and living arrangements.
  - **Health Background** (Health Background, text, optional): Provide information about your health and medical history, including any relevant illnesses, conditions, or allergies.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your responses to ensure accurate assessment results.
* If you're unsure about a particular question or section, feel free to ask for clarification or guidance.
* Remember to review your responses carefully before submitting the form.
