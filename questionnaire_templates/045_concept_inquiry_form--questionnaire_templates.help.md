# Concept Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Concept Inquiry Form is a collection of questions that will help us understand the details and potential of your new idea or proposal.
It is intended to be a helpful guide for you to submit your concept and get feedback on its progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your full name in the "Submitter name" field.
2. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact email" field, so we can get back to you for follow-up.
3. Give a short title to your concept in the "Concept title" field.
4. Describe your concept, its purpose, and scope in the "Detailed description" field.
5. (Optional) Mention who benefits and how in the "Potential impact" field.
6. If necessary, list the required support resources for your concept in the "Required support" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Concept Inquiry --**: This is an introduction to the concept inquiry form. You can use it as a brief note about the form's purpose.
* **Submitter name**: Please enter your full name, as you will be the primary contact for this inquiry.
* **Contact email**: Provide your email address, so we can get back to you for follow-up on the inquiry.
* **Concept title**: Enter a short title of your concept, such as "AI-powered Robot Arm" or "Smart Home Security System".
* **Detailed description**: Describe your concept in detail, including its purpose, scope, and any relevant background information.
* **Potential impact**: If you think your concept has the potential to positively impact people or organizations, describe how and who it will benefit.
* **Required support**: If your concept requires specific resources, such as funding, equipment, or personnel, list them here.
Note that some fields are optional, and you can skip them if not applicable.
