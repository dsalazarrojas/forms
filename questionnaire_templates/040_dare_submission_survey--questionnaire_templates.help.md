# Dare Submission Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information from individuals who have submitted Dares, such as their contact information and the specific details of the Dare they have submitted.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the relevant information about yourself as the dare submitter.
2. Provide your email address and phone number (if applicable) so that we can contact you for more information if needed.
3. Specify the date and time the Dare was submitted.
4. Add any additional notes about the Dare submission.
5. Choose whether or not the submission is for multiple Dares.
6. Enter the phone number of the dare submitter (if different from your own).
7. Finally, enter the phone number of the submitter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Main Page
* **Main Page** (`main_page`, text, not required): This is the main section of the form where you can provide any additional information about the Dare submission.
### Main Page Section 1
* **Main Page Section 1** (`main_page_section_1`, text, not required): This is a secondary section within the main page where you can provide further details about the Dare submission.
### Dare Submitter
* **Dare Submitter** (`dare_submitter`, number, not required): This field is intended to capture the number of the dare submitter, but its purpose is unclear without further context.
### Email Address
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, not required): Enter your email address so that we can contact you for more information if needed.
### Date
* **Date** (`date`, date, not required): Provide the date the Dare was submitted.
### Time
* **Time** (`time`, time, not required): Enter the time the Dare was submitted.
### Note
* **Note** (`note`, note, not required): Add any additional notes or comments about the Dare submission.
### Multiple Dares
* **Multiple Dares** (`multiple_dares`, select_multiple, not required): Choose whether this submission is for multiple Dares or not.
### Dare Submitter Phone
* **Dare Submitter Phone** (`dare_submitter_phone`, text, not required): Enter the phone number of the dare submitter (if different from your own).
### Submitter Phone
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, not required): Enter your own phone number.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have permission to submit a Dare and that you are not duplicating an already submitted form.
* If you are having trouble filling out the form, feel free to ask for assistance.
