# Functional Ability Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Functional Ability Assessment Questionnaire (FAAQ) is a tool used to evaluate an individual's level of independence or assistance needed in various aspects of their daily life. This questionnaire helps care providers and social workers understand the individual's functional abilities and identify areas where they may need support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the individual's medical history and current condition to understand their overall health status.
2. Assess each question based on the individual's level of independence or assistance needed in that area.
3. Choose the corresponding option that best describes the individual's functional ability (Independent, Moderate, or Needs Assistance).
4. Complete all questions on the form to provide a comprehensive assessment.

## Tips
* Complete the form based on the individual's current condition and medical history.
* Be thorough and accurate in your assessment to ensure that the individual receives the right level of support and care.
* If you are unsure about an individual's functional ability, please ask for further guidance or consult with a healthcare professional.
* Please note that the individual's responses should reflect their current condition, not their potential or desired state.
* Ensure that the individual understands the purpose and use of this form.
* Review and update the form as needed to reflect changes in the individual's condition or functional ability.
