# Sleeping Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is to help you complete the Sleeping Questionnaire, a tool designed to gather information about your sleep patterns, quality, and experiences. It is a comprehensive form that will help healthcare professionals better understand your sleep habits, which can be essential for diagnosis and treatment of sleep-related issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your patient's name** (`patient_name`): Please fill in the first field, "Patient Name," with the name of the person being assessed or treated. This information is required for identification and medical record purposes.
2. **Describe sleep issues** (`sleep_issues`): In the next field, "Sleep Issues," write down any sleep-related problems you've been experiencing, such as insomnia, snoring, or sleep apnea.
3. **Specify sleep duration** (`sleep_duration`): In this field, "How long do you sleep at night?", select the correct duration from the options:
	* Less than 3 hours
	* 3-5 hours
	* 5-7 hours
	* 7-9 hours
	* More than 9 hours
4. **Rate sleep quality** (`sleep_quality`): In the "Sleep Quality" field, provide a written description of your sleep quality, such as "Good," "Fair," or "Poor."
5. **Report sleep times** (`sleep_timesleep_wake`): Enter the time you go to sleep and wake up in the format "HH:MM."
6. **Provide wake time** (`wake_timesleep_duration`): If applicable, report the time you wake up in the format "HH:MM" (optional field).
7. **Indicate sleep frequency** (`sleep_timesleep_frequency`): Select all the days or frequencies you sleep:
	* Daily
	* Every other day
	* 3 to 4 times a week
	* 2 times a month
	* Never
8. **List medical conditions** (`medical_conditions`): If you have any underlying medical conditions that might affect your sleep, type them in this field (optional).
9. **Mention medication** (`medication`): If you take any medication related to sleep, list the names of the medications in this field (optional).
10. **Describe other symptoms** (`other_symptoms`): If you're experiencing any other symptoms not related to sleep but might be connected, write them down in this field (optional).
11. **Provide medical history** (`medical_history`): In this field, provide any relevant medical history that might be linked to your sleep issues (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): Please fill in the first field with the patient's name.
* **Sleep Issues** (`sleep_issues`): Describe any sleep-related problems you have been experiencing.
* **How long do you sleep at night?** (`sleep_duration`): Choose the correct duration of your sleep from the provided options.
* **Sleep Quality** (`sleep_quality`): Rate your sleep quality in a few words or phrases.
* **Sleep Timesleep Wake** (`sleep_timesleep_wake`): Enter the time you go to sleep and wake up.
* **Wake Time** (`wake_timesleep_duration`): If applicable, report the time you wake up.
* **Sleep Frequency** (`sleep_timesleep_frequency`): Select all the days or frequencies you sleep.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`): List any underlying medical conditions that might affect your sleep.
* **Medication** (`medication`): If you take any medication related to sleep, list the names of the medications.
* **Other Symptoms** (`other_symptoms`): Describe any other symptoms not related to sleep but might be connected.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Provide any relevant medical history that might be linked to your sleep issues.
