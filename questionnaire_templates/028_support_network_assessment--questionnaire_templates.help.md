# Support Network Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help you identify your support network, including people and resources that can provide you with emotional, physical, and informational support in times of need.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question based on your actual support network.
2. For each question, select the options that best describe your support network. If you need to specify someone or something not listed, use the "Other" option and provide a brief description.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields (marked as "required" in red).
