## Purpose
The Research Hypothesis Evaluation Form is used to evaluate research hypotheses, backgrounds, objectives, methodology, and other related aspects of a study. This form helps researchers and reviewers to assess and provide feedback on the quality and feasibility of research proposals, ideas, or findings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Research Hypothesis" field with a clear and concise statement of your research question or problem.
2. Provide a brief description of the research background in the "Research Background" field.
3. Indicate whether or not you have well-defined research objectives in the "Research Objectives" field.
4. Describe your research methodology in the "Methodology" field.
5. Estimate the expected outcome of your research in the "Expected Outcome" field.
6. Enter the review date in the "Review Date" field.
7. Provide any additional research findings or comments in the "Research Findings" field (optional).
8. Enter the name of the person reviewing the research.
9. Enter the department or affiliation of the reviewer.
10. Enter the review date in the "Review Date 2" field.
11. Enter any additional research background information in the "Research Background (3)" field (optional).
12. Enter the title of the reviewer.
13. Enter the email address of the reviewer.
14. Enter the phone number of the reviewer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Hypothesis (1)** (`research_hypothesis`, `text`, required: false): Enter a clear and concise statement of your research question or problem.
* **Research Background (2)** (`research_background`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the research background.
