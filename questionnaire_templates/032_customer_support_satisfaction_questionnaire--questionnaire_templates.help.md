# Customer Support Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from customers about their experience with the support service provided by our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field according to your experience:

<ol>
  <li>Customer Feedback: Provide a brief description of your experience with our support service.</li>
  <li>Support Interaction: Select the type of interaction you had with our support service (e.g., phone, email, in-person).</li>
  <li>Interaction Type: This field is not applicable to this form.</li>
  <li>Rating (1-5): Rate your satisfaction with the service quality, from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).</li>
  <li>Service Quality: Select all the qualities that match your experience (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).</li>
  <li>Was the issue resolved? Select 'True' if the issue was resolved or 'False' otherwise.</li>
  <li>Additional Feedback: Provide any additional information or comments about your experience.</li>
  <li>Customer Service Level: Select all the levels that match your experience (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).</li>
  <li>Resolution Time: If applicable, provide the date and time the issue was resolved.</li>
  <li>How satisfied are you with resolution time? Rate your satisfaction on a scale from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).</li>
  <li>Communication: Select all the levels that match your experience (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).</li>
  <li>Resolution Method: Provide any additional information about the resolution method used.</li>
  <li>Contact Person: If you had direct contact with a support agent, provide their name.</li>
  <li>Contact E-mail/Phone: If you had contact with a support agent via email or phone, provide their email or phone number.</li>
</ol>

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Feedback**: This field is a text box where you can provide a brief description of your experience with our support service. Please keep it concise, but feel free to include any relevant details.
  * Field id: 1

* **Support Interaction**: Select the type of interaction you had with our support service (e.g., phone, email, in-person). This will help us understand which channel you used to contact us.
  * Field id: 2

* **Interaction Type**: This field is not applicable to this form.
  * Field id: 3

* **Rating (1-5)**: Rate your satisfaction with the service quality, from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
  * Field id: 4

* **Service Quality**: Select all the qualities that match your experience (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor). This will help us understand your perception of our service quality.
  * Field id: 5

* **Was the issue resolved?**: Select 'True' if the issue was resolved or 'False' otherwise. If you've selected 'False', please provide an explanation in the additional feedback field.
  * Field id: 6

* **Additional Feedback**: Provide any additional information or comments about your experience. If you've already selected 'False' for the "Was the issue resolved?" field, please provide an explanation for why the issue wasn't resolved.
  * Field id: 7

* **Customer Service Level**: Select all the levels that match your experience (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor). This will help us understand how we're performing in terms of customer service.
  * Field id: 8

* **Resolution Time**: If applicable, provide the date and time the issue was resolved.
  * Field id: 9

* **How satisfied are you with resolution time?**: Rate your satisfaction on a scale from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent) for the resolution time.
  * Field id: 10

* **Communication**: Select all the levels that match your experience (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor). This will help us understand how we're performing in terms of communication.
  * Field id: 11

* **Resolution Method**: Provide any additional information about the resolution method used.
  * Field id: 12

* **Contact Person**: If you had direct contact with a support agent, provide their name.
  * Field id: 13

* **Contact E-mail/Phone**: If you had contact with a support agent via email or phone, provide their email or phone number.
  * Field id: 14

* **Support Agent**: If you had direct contact with a support agent, provide their name.
  * Field id: 16

**Tips**

* Please be honest and objective when filling out this form.
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and relevant to your experience.
* If you've already filled out a similar form, please do not fill out this one again.
* If you have any further feedback or updates, feel free to provide those in the additional feedback field.
