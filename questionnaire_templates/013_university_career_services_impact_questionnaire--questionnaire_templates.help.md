# University Career Services Impact Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge the impact of university career services on students and recent graduates. It is used to gather feedback and assess the effectiveness of these services in achieving their intended goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide a comprehensive and accurate response, please answer each field carefully and thoroughly. You can complete the form by following these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and understand the questions being asked.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the "additional_comments" field to provide more information.
4. Submit your responses when you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (Page 1): This is a note section where you can provide any additional context or background information about your experience with university career services.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Page 2): This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the career services provided by the university. Please select one of the following options: Satisfied, Dissatisfied, or Neutral.
* **Satisfaction with Career Services** (Page 3): This question is related to your satisfaction with the career services provided by the university. Please select all that apply: Satisfied, Dissatisfied, or Neutral.
* **Career Outcomes** (Page 4): This question asks about the impact of career services on your career outcomes. Please select one of the following options: Yes, No, or Unsure.
* **Career Services Usage** (Page 5): This question is about your usage of career services provided by the university. Please select all that apply: Yes, No, or Unsure.
* **Student Outcomes** (Page 6): This question is about the impact of career services on student outcomes. Please select all that apply: Yes, No, or Unsure.
* **Alumni Outcomes** (Page 7): This question is about the impact of career services on alumni outcomes. Please select all that apply: Yes, No, or Unsure.
* **Satisfaction with Services** (Page 8): This question asks about your satisfaction with the services provided by the university. Please answer by typing a numerical value between 1 and 10.
* **Comments** (Page 9): This text field is for you to provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with university career services.
* **Additional Comments** (Page 10): This text field is for you to provide any additional information or concerns you have about the career services provided by the university.
* **University Name** (Page 11): This field is for you to type the name of the university you are providing feedback about.
* **Contact Person** (Page 12): This field is for you to type the name of the contact person responsible for the career services.
* **Email Address** (Page 13): This field is for you to type the email address of the contact person responsible for the career services.

Please note that all fields are optional, and you can provide as much information as you need to answer the questions truthfully. If you have any difficulties or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
