# Group And Role Information Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a group and its roles. The goal is to gather data on the group's composition, including its members, roles, and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly to the best of your ability.
2. For each field, select the correct response from the available options.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Member Details**
 Member Details (`member_details`, `text`, optional): This field is a text field where you can provide any additional information about the group members.

### 2. **Roles**
 Roles (`roles`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all the roles that apply to the group. Available options are:

* Yes
* No

### 3. **Responsibilities**
 Responsibilities (`responsibilities`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all the responsibilities that apply to the group. Available options are:

* Yes
* No

### 4. **Tenure**
 Tenure (`tenure`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of years the group has been active.

### 5. **Roles and Responsibilities**
 Roles and Responsibilities (`roles_and_responsibilities`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all the roles and responsibilities that apply to the group. Available options are:

* Yes
* No

### 6. **Member Status**
 Member Status (`member_status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the status of the group. Available options are:

* Active
* Inactive

### 7. **Member Role**
 Member Role (`member_role`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether the group has a member role. Available options are:

* Yes
* No

### 8. **Member Tenure**
 Member Tenure (`member_tenure`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of years the group has been maintaining its member role.

### 9. **Team Members**
 Team Members (`team_members`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all the team members that apply to the group. Available options are:

* Yes
* No

Note: If you need further clarification or have questions, please don't hesitate to reach out.
