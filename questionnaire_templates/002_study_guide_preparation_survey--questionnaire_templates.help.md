# Study Guide Preparation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a study guide preparation survey.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form and ensure all required fields are completed.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about your school or course.
3. Review and verify all provided data before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **school_id** (1, number, required/optional): Enter your school's ID number.
* **learning_styles** (2, text, required/optional): Describe the learning styles used in your school or course.
* **preferred_formats** (3, select_one, required/optional): Indicate if you prefer yes or no to specific formats.
* **study_challenges** (4, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all challenges related to your study guide preparation.
* **feedback** (5, note, required/optional): Provide your feedback about the study guide preparation process.
* **school_id_2** (6, number, required/optional): Enter your school's ID number again.
* **learning_styles_2** (7, text, required/optional): Describe the learning styles used in your school or course again.
* **preferred_formats_2** (8, select_one, required/optional): Indicate if you prefer yes or no to specific formats again.
* **study_challenges_2** (9, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all challenges related to your study guide preparation again.
* **feedback_2** (10, note, required/optional): Provide your feedback about the study guide preparation process again.
* **school_name** (11, text, required/optional): Enter your school's name.
* **contact_number** (12, text, required/optional): Enter your contact number.
* **email** (13, email, required/optional): Enter your school's email address.
* **website** (14, text, required/optional): Enter your school's website.
* **school_address** (15, text, required/optional): Enter your school's address.
* **contact_person** (16, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **feedback_message** (17, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all feedback messages related to your study guide preparation.
* **school_logo** (18, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate if you have a school logo or not.
* **course_name** (19, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the course.
* **course_description** (20, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the course.
* **course_duration** (21, date, required/optional): Enter the duration of the course.
* **course_start_date** (22, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of the course.
* **course_end_date** (23, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of the course.
* **course_status** (24, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the status of the course (Active or Inactive).
* **course_level** (25, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the level of the course (High or Low).
