# Virtual Assistance Client Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Assistance Client Questionnaire is a form designed to gather feedback from clients about their experience with your virtual assistance services. It helps you understand how well you've met their needs and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question as accurately and thoroughly as possible. You may skip questions if they do not apply to you.

* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): A brief introduction to your experience with our virtual assistance services. Please share a few sentences about your interaction with our team.

	+ Choose one of the options: 'Yes', 'No'

* **Additional Comments** (`9`, `note`, required/optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions about your experience.
