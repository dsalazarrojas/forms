# Skin Analysis Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your skin type, pain areas, sensitive areas, habits, and treatment plans to help medical professionals analyze and provide effective treatment for your skin conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **User Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **User Email** in the second field.
3. Select your **Skin Type** from the options provided (Normal, Sensitive, Oily, Combination).
4. Choose areas of skin pain or discomfort from the list (Face, Neck, Arms, Legs).
5. Select areas of skin sensitivity (Sensitive Skin, Skin Irritation).
6. If applicable, describe your **Skin Habits** in the text field.
7. Enter the number of **Skin Product Use** in the corresponding field.
8. Use the **Treatment Plans** field to note any existing treatment plans or recommendations from medical professionals.
9. Use the **Note** fields (1-12, 15-17) to add any additional notes or comments from medical professionals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: Enter your name for reference purposes.
* **User Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates or follow-ups.
* **Select Skin Type**: Choose the best description of your skin type from the options provided.
* **Skin Pain Areas**: Select all areas of your skin that experience pain or discomfort.
* **Skin Sensitive Areas**: Select areas of your skin that are sensitive or irritated.
* **Skin Habits**: Describe your skincare routine, product use, or any other relevant information about your skin habits.
* **Skin Product Use**: Enter the number of skin products you use (e.g., 2 for 2 products).
* **Treatment Plans**: Note any existing treatment plans or recommendations from medical professionals.
* **Note 1-12, 15-17**: Use these fields to add any additional notes or comments from medical professionals.
