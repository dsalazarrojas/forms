# Symmetry Concepts Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about an individual's understanding of symmetry concepts, specifically line and rotational symmetry. The input will help us better understand their knowledge and thoughts on these concepts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the Line Symmetry Questions section with your choice of 'Yes' or 'No' for each question.
2. Select either 'Yes' or 'No' for the Rotational Symmetry question.
3. Provide your answer to the Line Symmetry question in the Answer Line Symmetry section.
4. Provide your answer to the Rotational Symmetry question in the Answer Rotational Symmetry section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Line Symmetry Questions** (`select_multiple`, optional)
	+ This section asks you to choose your response to four line symmetry questions. Select all that apply:
		- Yes
		- No
* **Rotational Symmetry** (`select_one`, optional)
	+ Choose your response to the rotational symmetry question by selecting either:
		- Yes
		- No
* **Answer Line Symmetry** (`text`, optional)
	+ Please provide your answer to the line symmetry question.
* **Answer Rotational Symmetry** (`text`, optional)
	+ Provide your answer to the rotational symmetry question.
