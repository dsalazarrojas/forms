# Snake Awareness Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Snake Awareness Quiz is designed to evaluate your understanding of snake safety, habitats, and treatment procedures. Please answer the questions honestly, and do not worry if you are unsure about any of the answers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section of the form carefully.
2. Answer all questions accurately and honestly.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select the most appropriate answer from the provided options.
4. For text fields, provide detailed and descriptive answers.
5. When finished, review your answers for completeness and accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Snake Safety Risks Awareness** (text): This field is designed to gauge your awareness of the potential risks associated with snake bites, including venomous and non-venomous species.
* **Snakes to Avoid** (select_multiple): Identify which types of snakes are commonly known to be venomous and should be avoided.
* **Snake Venom Symptoms** (text): Describe the symptoms of a venomous snake bite, including pain, swelling, and other effects.
* **Snake Bites Risks** (text): List the risks associated with snake bites, such as cardiac arrest, respiratory distress, and renal failure.
* **Common Snake Habitats** (text): Describe the typical habitats where snakes are commonly found, including forests, deserts, and wetlands.
* **What to Do If Bitten** (select_one): Indicate what action you would take if you were bitten by a snake, including seeking medical attention or providing first aid.
* **Follow Up Treatment** (select_one): Identify the most appropriate course of treatment after a snake bite, including first aid, antivenom, antibiotics, or hospitalization.
* **Snake Awareness Quizz Completed** (select_one): Indicate whether you have completed the quiz honestly and thoroughly.
* **Snake Awareness Quizz Completed Date** (date): Enter the date on which you completed the quiz.
* **Snake Awareness Quizz Completed Time** (time): Enter the time on which you completed the quiz.
* **Completed By** (text): Enter your name, initials, or alias to identify yourself as the person completing the quiz.
* **Organization Name** (text): Enter the name of your organization, if applicable.
* **Contact Number** (text): Enter your contact phone number, if you would like to be reached.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address, if you would like to be contacted.
* **Comments** (text): Provide any additional comments or suggestions for the quiz.

Please note that this form is not intended to be a comprehensive resource for snake handling or medical emergency procedures. If you are concerned about snake safety or require medical attention after a snake bite, please contact your local emergency services or seek medical attention promptly.
