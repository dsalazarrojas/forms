# e_learning_user_insight_information_collection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The eLearning User Insight Information Collection Form is designed to gather information about your eLearning experience, preferences, and behaviors. This form helps us understand how to improve our services to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by reading the form carefully and ensure you have understood the purpose and the questions asked.
2. Fill out the required fields (marked as **required**).
3. Provide accurate and honest answers to all questions.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to use the "additional_comments" field.
5. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name (`user_name`):** Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email (`email`):** Enter your email address.
* **User Role (`user_role`):** Choose one of the following options:
	+ Student: If you are a student using our eLearning platform.
	+ Educator: If you are an educator using our eLearning platform.
	+ Other: If you have another role in our eLearning platform.
* **eLearning Platform Used (`e_learning_platform_used`):** Select all eLearning platforms you use (optional):
	+ Udacity
	+ Coursera
	+ edX
* **Feedback on Our Services (`feedback_on_our_services`):** Choose how satisfied you are with our services:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Not Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
	+ Not at all Satisfied
* **Frequency of Uses (`frequency_of_uses`):** Select how often you use our eLearning services (required):
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
* **Total Hours Spent (`total_hours_spent`):** Enter the total hours you have spent on our eLearning platform (optional).
* **Average Time Spent (`average_time_spent`):** Enter the average time you spend on our eLearning platform per session (optional).
* **Last Visit (`last_visit`):** Enter the date of your last visit to our eLearning platform (optional).
* **Last Login (`last_login`):** Enter the time of your last login to our eLearning platform (optional).
* **Comments (`comments`):** Enter any additional comments or feedback about our eLearning platform.
* **Suggestions (`suggestions`):** Enter any suggestions you have for improving our eLearning platform.
* **Email Preferences (`email_preferences`):** Enter your email preferences (e.g., how often you want to receive emails from us).
* **Phone Preferences (`phone_preferences`):** Enter your phone preferences (e.g., whether you want to receive phone calls or texts from us).
* **Other Preferences (`other_preferences`):** Enter any other preferences you have that are not listed above.
* **Additional Comments (`additional_comments`):** Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about our eLearning platform.
* **Page 2 (`page_2`):** Enter any additional information or comments about your eLearning experience.
