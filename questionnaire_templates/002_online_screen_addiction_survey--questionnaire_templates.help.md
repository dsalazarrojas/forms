# Online Screen Addiction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to help researchers and experts understand individual perceptions and behaviors related to screen addiction. By filling out this form, you will be contributing to ongoing research on this topic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please follow these steps:

1. Start by answering the introductory questions on the first page.
2. On the second page, rate your screen use on a scale from 1 to 4, where 1 means "Not at all" and 4 means "Extremely".
3. Select all the screen addiction behaviors that you identify with on the third page.
4. If you have any additional comments or thoughts, please use the notes section at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intro Questionnaire** (`intro_questionnaire`, `text`, optional): This is the first page of the survey, where you will find introductory questions that help us understand your background and context.
* **Screen Use** (`screen_use`, `number`, optional): This question asks you to rate your screen use on a scale from 1 to 4. Please select the number that best describes your behavior.
* **Screen Addiction Scale** (`screen_addiction_scale`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks you to rate how much you identify with certain screen addiction behaviors. Please select one of the options that best describes your feelings.
* **Screen Addiction Behaviors** (`screen_addiction_behaviors`, `select_multiple`, required): On this page, please select all the screen addiction behaviors that you identify with. This will help us understand your specific behaviors and patterns.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional comments or thoughts about your screen use or the questions asked, please use this section to provide more information.
