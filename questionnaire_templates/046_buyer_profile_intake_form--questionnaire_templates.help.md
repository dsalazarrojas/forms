# Buyer Profile Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Buyer Profile Intake Form is a crucial tool for the sales team to gather information about the buyer's preferences, needs, and expectations from the property.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **First Name** (`buyer_first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
2. **Last Name** (`buyer_last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.
3. **Email Address** (`buyer_email`, email, required): Please enter your email address.
4. **Phone Number** (`buyer_phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number.
5. **Best Time and Method to Contact** (`best_contact_method`, text, optional): Please select your preferred time and method for communication.
6. **When Do You Plan to Buy?** (`buying_timeline`, select_one, required): Please select the timeframe for your intended purchase.
7. **Current Living Situation** (`current_living_situation`, select_one, required): Please select your current living situation.
8. **First-Time Home Buyer** (`first_time_buyer`, select_one, required): Please select your experience level as a home buyer.
9. **Pre-Approval Status** (`preapproval_status`, select_one, required): Please select your pre-approval status.
10. **Price Range** (`price_range`, select_one, required): Please select the price range you are looking for.
11. **Down Payment Available** (`down_payment`, text, optional): Please enter the amount of down payment you have available.
12. **Property Type Preference** (`property_type`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the property types you prefer.
13. **Location Preferences** (`location_preference`, text, required): Please enter your location preferences.
14. **Number of Bedrooms Desired** (`bedrooms_wanted`, select_one, optional): Please select the number of bedrooms you desire.
15. **Must-Have Features** (`must_have_features`, text, optional): Please select all the features that are must-haves for you.
16. **Deal-Breakers** (`deal_breakers`, text, optional): Please select all the features that would definitely be a deal-breaker.
17. **Lifestyle Priorities** (`lifestyle_priorities`, text, optional): Please select all the lifestyle priorities that are important to you.
18. **Household Composition** (`household_composition`, text, optional): Please enter the number and ages of people in your household.
19. **Is This an Investment Property?** (`investment_property`, select_one, required): Please select whether this is an investment property or not.
20. **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Please enter any other information that you think would be helpful for us to know.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Please provide as much information as possible, even if the fields are optional.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask the sales team for guidance.
- The form will be used to guide the sales team in their conversation with you, so please be as specific as possible.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the sales team.
