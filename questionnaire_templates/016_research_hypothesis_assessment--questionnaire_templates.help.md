# Research Hypothesis Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the research hypothesis submitted by the user.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your research hypothesis ID (if applicable)
2. Provide a clear and concise title for your research project
3. Describe the research question you aim to answer
4. Define your research hypothesis in a few sentences
5. Specify the research objectives and expected outcomes
6. Choose the most suitable methodology for your research
7. Summarize your expected research results
8. Discuss the implications of your research
9. Explain the potential impact of your research

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Research Hypothesis ID** (`research_hypothesis_id`, number, optional): Enter your research hypothesis ID if you have one.
* **Title** (`title`, text, optional): A clear and concise title for your research project.
* **Research Question** (`research_question`, text, optional): Describe the research question you aim to answer.
* **Research Hypothesis** (`research_hypothesis`, text, optional): Define your research hypothesis in a few sentences.
* **Research Objectives** (`research_objectives`, select_multiple, optional): Specify the research objectives and expected outcomes.
	+ Select all relevant options for your research objectives.
* **Research Methodology** (`research_methodology`, select_one, optional): Choose the most suitable methodology for your research from the provided options.
	+ Select the most appropriate option for your research methodology.
* **Research Results** (`research_results`, text, optional): Summarize your expected research results.
* **Research Discussion** (`research_discussion`, text, optional): Discuss the implications of your research.
* **Research Impact** (`research_impact`, text, optional): Explain the potential impact of your research.
* **Research Conclusion** (`research_conclusion`, text, optional): Summarize your research conclusion.
 

## Tips
* Please provide clear and concise answers for each field.
* Use the provided options for Research Objectives and Research Methodology to ensure consistency with existing research standards.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please feel free to ask your supervisor or colleague for clarification.
