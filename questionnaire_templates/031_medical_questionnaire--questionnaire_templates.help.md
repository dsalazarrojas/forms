# Medical Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect medical information from clients, including their age, health status, medical condition, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your age, if applicable.
2. Select your health status from the options provided.
3. Describe your medical condition, if any.
4. List any current medication you are taking.
5. Provide any doctor recommendation or advice you have received.
6. Select relevant medical test results, if applicable.
7. Specify whether you have health insurance.
8. List any allergies or medical conditions you have.
9. Provide a medical history, if applicable.
10. Enter the date of your last medical test, if applicable.
11. Select test results from the medical test you had.
12. Provide your doctor's name, contact information, and specialty.
13. Enter the address of your doctor's office.
14. Leave a note for your doctor, if needed.
15. Leave a note for the client, if needed.
16. Specify whether you have a medical certificate, if applicable.
17. Enter the certificate number, if applicable.
18. Select the type of medical certificate you have.
19. Upload an attachment, if applicable.
20. Sign the form with your signature, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fill-in on Any Device** (`id: 1`, `text`, optional): This field is intended for any comments or information you may have about your experience with this form.
* **Client Age** (`id: 2`, `number`, optional): Please enter your age, if you wish to provide this information.
* **Health Status** (`id: 3`, `select_one`, optional): Select your current health status from the options provided.
* **Client Medical Condition** (`id: 4`, `text`, optional): Describe your current medical condition, if applicable.
* **Client Medication** (`id: 5`, `text`, optional): List any current medication you are taking.
* **Doctor Recommendation** (`id: 6`, `select_multiple`, optional): Provide any doctor recommendation or advice you have received.
* **Medical Test Results** (`id: 7`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select relevant medical test results, if applicable.
* **Health Insurance** (`id: 8`, `select_one`, optional): Specify whether you have health insurance or not.
* **Client Allergies** (`id: 9`, `text`, optional): List any allergies you have.
* **Client Medical History** (`id: 10`, `text`, optional): Provide a medical history, if applicable.
* **Medical Test** (`id: 11`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your last medical test, if applicable.
* **Test Results** (`id: 12`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select test results from the medical test you had.
* **Doctor Name** (`id: 13`, `text`, optional): Enter your doctor's name.
* **Doctor Phone** (`id: 14`, `text`, optional): Enter your doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor Email** (`id: 15`, `email`, optional): Enter your doctor's email address.
* **Doctor Specialty** (`id: 16`, `text`, optional): Enter your doctor's specialty.
* **Doctor Facility** (`id: 17`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of your doctor's office.
* **Doctor Note** (`id: 18`, `note`, optional): Leave a note for your doctor, if needed.
* **Client Note** (`id: 19`, `note`, optional): Leave a note for the client, if needed.
* **Medical Certificate** (`id: 20`, `select_multiple`, optional): Specify whether you have a medical certificate, if applicable.
* **Medical Certificate Number** (`id: 21`, `text`, optional): Enter the number of your medical certificate, if applicable.
* **Medical Certificate Type** (`id: 22`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of medical certificate you have.
* **Client Signature** (`id: 23`, `text`, optional): Sign the form with your signature, if required.
* **Upload Attachment** (`id: 24`, `text`, optional): Upload an attachment, if applicable.
* **Upload Attachment** (id: 25, text, optional): This field is intended for uploading attachments, such as medical records or test results.
