# High School Student Skills Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This questionnaire is designed to assess the skills and readiness of high school students for future career opportunities. It aims to gather information about the student's personal and professional attributes, career interests, and skills. The data collected will help identify areas of strength and weakness, and provide guidance for students to improve their skills and career readiness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first name, last name, and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
3. Specify your career goals in the career goals field.
4. List your assessed skills in the skills assessed field.
5. Write a self-assessment of your skills and strengths in the self-assessment field.
6. Enter your school name in the school name field.
7. Provide your expected graduation date, including month and year.
8. Select your top career interests from the career interests dropdown menu.
9. Choose the skills you possess that match your career interests in the skills list field (using the select multiple option).
10. Indicate your strengths and areas of improvement in the strengths and weakness fields.
11. Evaluate your career and academic readiness in the career and academic readiness fields.
12. Assess your self-confidence and motivation levels in the self-confidence and self-motivation fields.
13. Select follow-up methods and frequency in the follow-up frequency and method fields.
14. Provide additional comments for follow-up in the follow-up comments field.
15. If necessary, specify other follow-up frequency and methods in the follow-up frequency other and method other fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (email): Enter a valid email address that you regularly use.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Career Goals** (text): Describe your short-term and long-term career aspirations.
* **Skills Assessed** (text): List the skills you believe you possess, such as communication, problem-solving, and leadership.
* **Self-Assessment** (text): Write a self-reflection of your skills and strengths, including areas you excel in and areas that need improvement.
* **School Name** (text): Enter the name of your current or previous high school.
* **Graduation Date** (date): Enter the date you expect to graduate from high school, including the month and year.
* **Graduation Year** (number): Enter the year you expect to graduate from high school.
* **Graduation Month** (number): Enter the month you expect to graduate from high school.
* **Graduation Day** (number): Enter the day you expect to graduate from high school.
* **Career Interests** (select one): Select your top career interest from the dropdown menu:
	+ Business
	+ Computer Science
	+ Engineering
	+ Health
	+ Law
* **Skills List** (select multiple): Choose the skills that match your career interest:
	+ Communication
	+ Time Management
	+ Problem Solving
	+ Team Work
	+ Leadership
* **Strengths** (text): List your personal and professional strengths.
* **Weakness** (text): List areas you struggle with or need improvement.
* **Career Readiness** (select one): Evaluate your career readiness:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Academic Readiness** (select one): Evaluate your academic readiness:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Self-Confidence** (select one): Evaluate your self-confidence:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Self-Motivation** (select one): Evaluate your self-motivation:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Follow-up Frequency** (select multiple): Choose the methods you're willing to use for follow-up:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ No
* **Follow-up Method** (text): Specify the methods you're willing to use for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Comments** (text): Provide any additional information for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Frequency Other** (text): If other follow-up methods are needed, specify them here.
* **Follow-up Method Other** (text): If other follow-up methods are needed, specify them here.
* **Follow-up Comments** (text): Provide any additional information for other follow-up methods.
* **Follow-up Frequency** (text): Specify the frequency of follow-up.
* **Follow-up Method** (text): Specify the methods used for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Comments** (text): Provide any additional information for follow-up.
