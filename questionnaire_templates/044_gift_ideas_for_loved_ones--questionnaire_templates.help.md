# Gift Ideas For Loved Ones - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you generate gift ideas for loved ones by collecting information about the recipient, their interests, and the occasion. Fill out this form to receive a list of thoughtful gift ideas tailored to your loved one's preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the recipient's details in the "Recipient Details" field.
2. Indicate the recipient's interests in the "Recipient Interests" field. Select all that apply.
3. Set a budget for the gift in the "Budget" field.
4. Choose the occasion for the gift (e.g., Gift, Birthday, Anniversary, Christmas) in the "Occasion" field.
5. Add any gift suggestions or ideas you have in the "Gift Suggestion" field.
6. Personalize the gift with a personal message in the "Personal Touch" field.
7. Write a brief message for the recipient in the "Message" field.
8. Enter the recipient's contact information (name, address, email, and phone number) in the relevant fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Recipient Details** (text, required): This field is for entering the recipient's name, relation (e.g., child, parent, friend), or any other identifying information.
- **Recipient Interests** (select multiple, required): Choose the recipient's interests from the options provided. Select all that apply.
- **Budget** (number, required): Enter the budget you have set for the gift.
- **Occasion** (select one, required): Choose the occasion for which the gift is intended (e.g., Gift, Birthday, Anniversary, Christmas).
- **Gift Suggestion** (text, optional): Add any gift ideas you have in mind.
- **Personal Touch** (text, optional): Personalize the gift with a message or a special touch.
- **Message** (text, optional): Write a brief message for the recipient.
- **Contact Info** (text, optional): Enter the recipient's contact information (e.g., name, address, email, phone number).
- **Recipient Name** (text, optional): Enter the recipient's name (if not already provided in "Contact Info").
- **Budget Details** (number, optional): Enter any additional budget details or specifications.
- **Recipient Address** (text, optional): Enter the recipient's address (if not already provided in "Contact Info").
- **Recipient Email** (email, optional): Enter the recipient's email (if not already provided in "Contact Info").
- **Personal Message** (text, optional): Write a personal message for the recipient.
- **Recipient Phone** (text, optional): Enter the recipient's phone number (if not already provided in "Contact Info").
