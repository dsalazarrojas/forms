# Marital Settlement Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to create a marital settlement agreement between two spouses. It helps to clarify the terms of the agreement, including asset division, support arrangements, and other essential details.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the details of Spouse 1 and Spouse 2 sections, including their names and any other relevant information.
2.  Describe the agreement details in the Agreement Details section.
3.  Specify how assets will be divided between the spouses in the Asset Division section. Select all applicable options.
4.  Enter any support arrangements agreed upon between the spouses in the Support Arrangements section.
5.  Sign and date the Signatures section, indicating agreement with the terms.
6.  If applicable, provide the mediator's name in the Mediator section.
7.  Enter the legal professional's name in the Legal Professional section, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Spouse 1 Information** (Text, Optional): Enter the name and other relevant information of the first spouse.
*   **Spouse 2 Information** (Text, Optional): Enter the name and other relevant information of the second spouse.
*   **Agreement Details** (Text, Optional): Describe the essential terms of the marital settlement agreement.
*   **Asset Division** (Select Multiple, Required): Specify how assets will be divided between the spouses. Select all applicable options.
    *   Spouse 1
    *   Spouse 2
*   **Support Arrangements** (Text, Optional): Enter any agreed-upon support arrangements between the spouses.
*   **Signatures** (Text, Optional): Sign and date the agreement, indicating acceptance of the terms.
*   **Mediator** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the mediator who facilitated the agreement, if applicable.
*   **Legal Professional** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the legal professional who assisted with the agreement, if applicable.
