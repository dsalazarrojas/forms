# Department Of Classics Alumni Career Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from Department of Classics alumni about their career experiences, educational background, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Read each question carefully and choose the most relevant option for each field.
*   Fill out all required fields (indicated by a red asterisk \*).
*   If you're unsure about any question, feel free to contact the Department of Classics for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Department of Classics Experiences** (`department_of_classics_experiences`): 
    This question asks about your experiences within the Department of Classics. Please select all relevant options.
*   **Department of Classics Educational Background** (`department_of_classics_educational_background`): 
    This question asks about your educational background, specifically relevant courses or programs you've completed within the Department of Classics. Choose the option that best describes your educational level.
*   **Department of Classics Relevant Projects** (`department_of_classics_relevant_projects`): 
    This question asks about any projects you've worked on that are relevant to the Department of Classics. Please provide a brief description of each project.
*   **Department of Classics Current Workplace** (`department_of_classics_current_workplace`): 
    This question asks about your current workplace or work environment. Please provide a brief description of your workplace.
*   **Department of Classics Educational Level** (`department_of_classics_educational_level`): 
    This question asks about your educational level, specifically relevant degrees or certifications you've earned within the Department of Classics. Choose the option that best describes your educational level.
*   **Department of Classics Degree Awarded** (`department_of_classics_degree_awarded`): 
    This question asks about your degree earned within the Department of Classics. Choose the option that best describes your degree.

## Tips

*   Please make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by a red asterisk \*).
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the Department of Classics for assistance.
*   This form is not intended to replace or duplicate existing records, such as those found in LinkedIn or other professional networks.
