# Cult Warning Signs Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Cult Warning Signs Checklist" is a tool designed to help individuals identify potential warning signs of cults or manipulative groups. This form is meant to be used by anyone who suspects they or someone else may be in a manipulative group or a cult.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions on the first page, "potential_cult_warning_signs," about the group's financial exploitation.
2. On the second page, "concern_level," choose a level of concern about the situation.
3. On the third page, "follow_up_support," indicate if you need follow-up support.
4. The remaining pages, "potential_cult_signs_1," "potential_cult_signs_2," "potential_cult_signs_3," "cult_warning_signs_1," "cult_warning_signs_2," and "cult_warning_signs_3," ask about specific warning signs of cults or manipulative groups. Answer each question according to your observations or knowledge about the group.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Has the group or individual been involved in the cult's financial exploitation?** (`potential_cult_warning_signs`, select_one, required: false)
    - This question is about whether the group or individual has been involved in the cult's financial exploitation, such as using manipulative tactics to control members' finances.
* **What level of concern do you have about this situation?** (`concern_level`, select_one, required: false)
    - This question asks you to rate your level of concern about the situation on a scale of High, Low, or None.
* **Request follow-up support for this situation** (`follow_up_support`, select_one, required: false)
    - This question is about whether you need additional support or resources to address the situation.
* **Do they have a strong leader who is demanding and controlling?** (`potential_cult_signs_1`, select_multiple, required: false)
    - This question is about whether the group has a leader who uses demanding and controlling behavior.
* **Are there any members who have left the group or have been asked to leave?** (`potential_cult_signs_2`, select_multiple, required: false)
    - This question is about whether there are members who have left the group or have been asked to leave, which can be a sign of a cult or manipulative group.
* **Does the group have a complex, hard-to-define structure or hierarchy?** (`potential_cult_signs_3`, select_multiple, required: false)
    - This question is about whether the group has a complex structure or hierarchy, which can be a sign of a cult or manipulative group.
* **What level of concern do you have about this situation?** (`concern_level_2`, select_one, required: false)
    - This question is about your level of concern about the situation on a scale of High, Low, or None.
* **Request follow-up support for this situation** (`follow_up_support_2`, select_one, required: false)
    - This question is about whether you need additional support or resources to address the situation.
* **Do they use loaded language that is designed to appeal to emotions?** (`cult_warning_signs_1`, select_multiple, required: false)
    - This question is about whether the group uses language that is designed to appeal to emotions, which can be a sign of a cult or manipulative group.
* **Do they have a clear and defined goal that benefits all members, but potentially hurts non-members?** (`cult_warning_signs_2`, select_multiple, required: false)
    - This question is about whether the group has a goal that seems beneficial but can potentially harm others.
* **Do they use tactics like love bombing, isolation, or mind games?** (`cult_warning_signs_3`, select_multiple, required: false)
    - This question is about whether the group uses manipulative tactics like love bombing, isolation, or mind games.
