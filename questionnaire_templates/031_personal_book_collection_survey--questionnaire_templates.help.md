# Personal Book Collection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal data about your book collection, including your name, contact information, reading habits, and favorite books.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields: First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2. Optional fields: Phone, Book Count, Year Read, Genre, Book Title, Author, Publisher, Publication Year, Favorite Book, Favorite Genre, and Note.
3. Use the drop-down menu for Genre and Favorite Genre fields to select one of the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted for further information.
* **Book Count**: Enter the total number of books you have read.
* **Year Read**: Enter the year you read the book.
* **Genre**: Select the genre of the book from the available options.
* **Book Title**: Enter the title of the book.
* **Author**: Enter the author of the book.
* **Publisher**: Enter the publisher of the book.
* **Publication Year**: Enter the publication year of the book.
* **Favorite Book**: Select your favorite book from the available options.
* **Favorite Genre**: Select your favorite genre from the available options.
* **Note**: Enter any additional notes about the book or your reading experience.
* **Form**: Enter the name of the form.
* **Created By**: Enter the name of the person who created the form.

## Tips
* Please fill in the required fields accurately to ensure reliable data.
* Use the available options for Genre and Favorite Genre fields to avoid errors.
* If you have already submitted your data, do not submit duplicate entries.
