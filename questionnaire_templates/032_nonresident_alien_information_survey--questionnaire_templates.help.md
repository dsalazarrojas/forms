# Nonresident Alien Information Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from nonresident aliens visiting our institution, ensuring their identity, travel plans, and immigration status are verified.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your First Name.
2. Provide your Last Name.
3. Enter your Date of Arrival.
4. Indicate your Visa Status.
5. Specify the Country of Origin.
6. State the Purpose of Stay.
7. Enter your Passport Number.
8. Enter your Date of Birth.
9. Provide your Email (optional, but recommended for communication).
10. Provide your Phone (optional).
11. Select Nonresident Category (non-citizen or citizen).
12. Indicate Nonresident Status (True or False).
13. Select Arrival Date (if applicable).
14. State Length of Stay (if applicable).
15. Indicate Citizenship by Nation (True or False).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your travel documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your travel documents.
* **Date of Arrival**: Enter the date you arrived at our institution.
* **Visa Status**: Check if you have a visa to stay in our country or not.
* **Country of Origin**: Enter the country where you originate from.
* **Purpose of Stay**: Enter the reason for your visit or stay.
* **Passport Number**: Enter your passport number, if you have one.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Email**: Optional, but recommended for communication purposes. Enter your work or personal email address.
* **Phone**: Optional, but recommended for communication purposes. Enter your contact phone number.
* **Nonresident Category**: Select 'Non-citizen' or 'Citizen', as you are classified.
* **Nonresident Status**: Indicate whether you are a nonresident or not.
* **Arrival Date**: Enter the date you arrived, if you want to track your stay duration.
* **Length of Stay**: Enter the number of days or months you plan to stay.
* **Citizenship by Nation**: Indicate whether you are a citizen or not by nation.

Note: Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth interaction with our institution staff.
