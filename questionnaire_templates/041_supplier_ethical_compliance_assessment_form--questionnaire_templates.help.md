# Supplier Ethical Compliance Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Ethical Compliance Assessment Form is a questionnaire designed to gather information about a supplier's business practices, policies, and procedures related to ethical compliance. This form helps you assess the supplier's ability to meet your company's ethical standards and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about the supplier, including their name and any other relevant details.
2. Enter the supplier's vendor details, such as their contact person and contact information.
3. Assess the supplier's compliance status, including any certifications or accreditations they hold.
4. Enter any documentation requests, including any specific documents or records you require from the supplier.
5. Provide information about any ethical reviews or assessments you have conducted on the supplier.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about the supplier.
7. Enter the supplier's email address and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info (id: 1)**: This field is used to enter general information about the supplier. Please provide any relevant details about the supplier, such as their company name, business address, and any other information you deem necessary.
* **Vendor Details (id: 2)**: This field is used to enter the contact person and contact information for the supplier.
* **Compliance Status (id: 3)**: This field is used to assess the supplier's compliance status, including any certifications or accreditations they hold.
* **Contact Info (id: 4)**: This field is used to enter the supplier's contact information.
* **Documentation Requests (id: 5)**: This field is used to request specific documentation from the supplier.
* **Ethical Reviews (id: 6)**: This field is used to provide information about any ethical reviews or assessments you have conducted on the supplier.
* **Notes (id: 7)**: This field is used to add any additional notes or comments about the supplier.
* **Email (id: 8)**: This field is used to enter the supplier's email address.
* **Phone (id: 9)**: This field is used to enter the supplier's phone number.
