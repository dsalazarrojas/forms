# Sozo Ministry Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about individuals who are seeking ministry services from Sozo Ministries. It helps our team understand each person's unique situation, allowing us to provide tailored support and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: These are marked with an asterisk (\*) and are crucial for us to understand your situation.
2. Answer the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Information**: This field is a general description of the application and its purpose. You can type a brief introduction to your situation.
* **Father**: Please provide information about your father, including his name and any relevant details.
* **Relationship with Son**: Describe your relationship with your son, including any relevant experiences or challenges you're facing.
* **Relationship with Spirit**: Share about your relationship with the Spirit, including any experiences of feeling guided or connected.
* **Healing History**: Briefly describe any healing experiences you've had, including any spiritual or emotional healing.
* **Current Health Status**: Report your current health status, including any medical conditions or concerns.
* **Medical History**: Share any relevant medical history or experiences.
* **Family History**: Provide information about your family, including any family dynamics or concerns.
* **Emotional History**: Describe any emotional challenges or experiences you've had, including feelings of anxiety, depression, or trauma.
* **Spiritual History**: Share about your spiritual journey, including any significant experiences or milestones.
* **Relationships**: Choose how you would describe your current relationship status: Married, Single, or In a Relationship.
* **Children**: Indicate whether you have children and if so, how many.
* **Education**: Briefly describe your educational background and any relevant degrees or certifications.
* **Employment**: Share about your employment situation, including job title and industry.
* **Church History**: Provide information about your church background and any experiences.
* **Prayer History**: Share about your prayer practice, including any regular prayer habits.
* **Tips**

* Make sure to answer each question as thoroughly as possible.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
