# Personal Space Boundary Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Space Boundary Assessment form is designed to help individuals assess their personal boundaries, comfort levels, and preferences. It's a self-administered assessment that can be used in various contexts, such as personal growth, relationships, or professional settings, to gain insight into an individual's boundaries and comfort zone.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by reviewing each page and selecting the relevant questions that apply to you.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, considering your current thoughts and feelings.
3. Take your time and don't hesitate to skip pages if they don't pertain to your situation.
4. Once you've completed the form, review your answers to ensure they accurately reflect your thoughts and feelings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**:
	+ A brief text input field for you to explain the purpose and context of this assessment.
* **Boundary Preferences**:
	+ Select all that apply from the options provided. This question is optional and helps identify your preferred boundaries.
	+ For example, you might select "Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3" if you prefer all three options.
* **Comfort Levels**:
	+ Select your most comfortable level from the options provided. This question helps assess your comfort level in specific situations.
	+ For example, you might select "Option 1" as your most comfortable level.
* **Boundary Violation**:
	+ Select all that apply from the options provided. This question helps identify situations where you feel your boundaries have been violated.
	+ For example, you might select "Option 1, Option 2" if you've experienced boundary violations in two areas.
* **Notes**:
	+ A free-form text input field for you to provide additional insights or comments about your answers.
	+ Use this field to elaborate on your thoughts or provide any further explanation that might be helpful.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers, as they will help you gain a better understanding of your personal boundaries and comfort levels.
* Take your time and review each question carefully before selecting an option.
* If you're unsure about an option, feel free to ask for help or consult with a professional.
* Remember, this form is for your personal use, and your answers are private and confidential.
