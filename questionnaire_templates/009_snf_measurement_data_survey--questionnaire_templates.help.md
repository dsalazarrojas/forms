# SNF Measurement Data Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The SNF Measurement Data Survey is a collection of questions designed to gather information on the measurement data collected during the deployment and operation of SNF systems. This data will help identify trends, patterns, and challenges faced by the SNF community, and inform research and development decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. For each question, provide as much detail as possible.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1: first_page** (text, required/required): This page is the introduction to the survey.
* **Page 2: second_page** (text, optional/required): This page asks about the measurement data collected during the deployment and operation of SNF systems.
* **Page 3: third_page** (text, optional/required): This page allows you to add any additional comments or information not covered in the previous questions.

## Tips
* Please answer each question as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* If you have any difficulty understanding a question or have any suggestions, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey! Your input is greatly appreciated and will help inform the future of SNF systems.
