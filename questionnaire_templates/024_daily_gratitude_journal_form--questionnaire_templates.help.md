# Daily Gratitude Journal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Gratitude Journal Form is a tool designed to help individuals cultivate a habit of reflecting on the things they are grateful for each day. By using this form, you can identify and acknowledge the positive aspects of your life, promoting a more optimistic outlook and improved mental well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "date" field with the current date.
2. In the "morning gratitude" field, write down three to five things you are grateful for today.
3. In the "evening gratitude" field, write down three to five things you are grateful for today again.
4. In the "reflect" field, write down your thoughts on the entries you have made today, and how they have impacted your day.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **morning_gratitude** (`morning_gratitude`, text, optional): Write down three to five things you are grateful for today in the morning.
* **date** (date, optional): Enter the current date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **evenin_gratitude** (evenin_gratitude, text, optional): Write down three to five things you are grateful for today in the evening.
* **reflect** (reflect, note, optional): Reflect on your gratitude journal entries, noting any insights or changes in your perspective.
