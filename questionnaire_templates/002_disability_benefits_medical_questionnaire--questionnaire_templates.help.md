**Disability Benefits Medical Questionnaire - Help Guide**

## Purpose

This form is used to collect information about an individual's medical history and benefits status. This information will be used to determine eligibility for disability benefits. Please answer the questions accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each page of the form carefully.
2. Answer all questions in the spaces provided.
3. Use the options provided for select questions.
4. If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): This is a free-text field where you can provide any relevant medical history or information about your condition.
* **Condition** (`condition`, text, optional): Describe your medical condition or diagnosis.
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, text, optional): Provide the name of your medical provider or healthcare professional.
* **Medical Record** (`medical_record`, text, optional): Enter your medical record number or any other identifying information about your medical records.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): List any medication you are currently taking or have taken in the past.
* **Disability Status** (`disability_status`, select_one, optional): Select the correct disability status from the options:
	+ Disability
	+ Non-Disability
	+ Undetermined
* **Benefits Applied** (`benefits_applied`, text, optional): If you have applied for benefits, enter the details about your application.
* **Date of Disability** (`date_of_disability`, date, optional): Enter the date of your disability or the date you became aware of your disability.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, optional): Describe any symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Treatment** (`treatment`, text, optional): Provide information about any treatment or therapy you are currently receiving.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, text, optional): Enter your medical record number or any other identifying information about your medical records.
* **Medical Record Date** (`medical_record_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your most recent medical record update.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
* **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your healthcare provider.
* **Doctor Address** (`doctor_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your healthcare provider.
* **Doctor License** (`doctor_license`, text, optional): Enter your healthcare provider's license number or any other identifying information.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Patient Date of Birth** (`patient_date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, text, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Patient Employer** (`patient_employer`, text, optional): Enter your employer's name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Job Duties** (`job_duties`, text, optional): Describe your job duties or responsibilities.
* **Benefits Enrolled** (`benefits_enrolled`, select_multiple, optional): Select from the options:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Undetermined

Note: Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure the best possible experience. If you have any questions or concerns, please ask for clarification.
