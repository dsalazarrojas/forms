# Visa Advice Strategy Session Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients to assist in providing tailored visa advice and guidance for their specific travel plans. Please fill out this form to ensure that you receive the most accurate and relevant information for your immigration needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your information in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Select the options that apply to your situation from the dropdown menus.
3. Use the text fields to provide more detailed information as needed.
4. Submit the form to receive guidance on your visa application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (1): Your full name as it appears on your passport.
* **Client Email** (2): Your email address that we can use to contact you with updates on your application.
* **Client Phone** (3): Your phone number in case we need to reach you with further questions.
* **Country of Application** (4): The country where you are planning to apply for a visa.
	+ Select the country where you will be applying for a visa.
* **Purpose of Travel** (5): The reason for your travel plans.
	+ Select all that apply (e.g., "Work", "Study", "Business", "Tourist").
* **Preferred Travel Date** (6): The date you would like to start your travel plans.
* **Travel Type** (7): The type of travel arrangement you are planning.
	+ Select the number of people traveling (e.g., "Single", "Double", "Triple", "Family").
* **Accommodation Type** (8): The type of accommodation you plan to use during your trip.
	+ Select all that apply (e.g., "Hotel", "Hostel", "Airbnb", "Other").
* **Travel Documents** (9): The documents you will need for your visa application.
	+ Select all that apply (e.g., "Passport", "ID Card", "Birth Certificate", "Other").
* **List of Travel Documents** (10): A list of the specific documents you are providing (e.g., passport number, ID number, birth certificate number).
* **Travel Details** (11): Any additional information about your travel plans.
* **Immigration Consultant** (12): The specific immigration consultant assigned to your case.
* **Consultant Experience** (13): The number of years of experience the consultant has in handling immigration cases.
* **Consultation Date** (14): The date of your scheduled consultation.

Note: All dates are in YYYY-MM-DD format.
