# Physical Fitness Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physical Fitness Questionnaire is designed to gather information about your physical fitness, including your general questions, medical history, exercise habits, and body measurements. This questionnaire is meant to be completed by individuals who want to assess their current physical fitness level.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Start by reading the introduction page, which explains the purpose and guidelines for completing the questionnaire.
2. Answer the general questions to the best of your ability, selecting the most accurate response.
3. Provide information about your medical history, including whether you have any major medical conditions or illnesses.
4. Answer the exercise questions, specifying how often you engage in physical exercise.
5. Indicate your physical activity level and select the option that best fits your level.
6. Enter your height and weight, if you wish to provide this information.
7. Select your body mass index (BMI) from the given options.
8. Record your resting heart rate and indicate whether you have had high blood pressure issues.
9. Specify how frequent you exercise, selecting the option that best fits your schedule.
10. If you want to provide your height and weight, select the corresponding option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (1, note, optional): This page serves as an introduction to the questionnaire, explaining its purpose and guidelines.
- **General Questions** (2, select multiple, optional): Answer these questions to the best of your ability, selecting the most accurate response from the options.
- **Medical History** (3, select one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have had a major medical condition or illness, 'No' otherwise.
- **Exercise Questions** (4, select multiple, optional): Specify how often you engage in physical exercise, choosing from the provided options.
- **Physical Activity Level** (5, select one, optional): Indicate your physical activity level, selecting 'High' if you are quite active or 'Low' if you are less active.
- **Height and Weight** (6, select multiple, optional): If you wish to provide your height and weight, select 'Yes', otherwise select 'No'.
- **Body Mass Index** (7, select one, optional): Select your body mass index, which is usually calculated as weight in kilograms divided by height in meters squared.
- **Resting Heart Rate** (8, select one, optional): Record your resting heart rate, and indicate whether you have had high blood pressure issues.
- **Blood Pressure** (9, select one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have had high blood pressure issues, 'No' otherwise.
- **Exercise Frequency** (10, select multiple, optional): Specify how frequent you exercise, choosing from the provided options.
- **Height Weight 2** (11, select multiple, optional): Similar to the previous question, you can provide your height and weight, or select 'No' if you do not wish to do so.
