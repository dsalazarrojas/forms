# Static Question Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Static Question Survey" form is designed to collect user feedback and information on their experience with a feature. This helps developers and stakeholders understand how users interact with the feature and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions for each field carefully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your current role or position in the company/organization?** (`intro_section`, `text`, required: false):
	+ This field asks you to describe your position or role in the company or organization you belong to.
	+ Please be as detailed as possible to help us understand your perspective.
* **How often do you use the feature?** (`feature_usage`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select a frequency of use from the options provided (Daily, Weekly, Rarely, Never).
	+ This helps us understand how often you interact with the feature.
* **On what date did you last use the feature?** (`date_last_used`, `date`, required: false):
	+ Please use the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-01-01) to provide the date.
	+ This helps us track when you last interacted with the feature.
* **At what time did you last use the feature?** (`time_last_used`, `time`, required: false):
	+ Use 24-hour format (e.g., 12:00) to provide the time.
	+ This helps us know the duration of your usage.
* **How often do you use the feature?** (`how_often_used`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select a frequency of use from the provided options (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Never).
	+ This helps us understand your usage habits.
* **How much time do you spend using the feature?** (`how_much_time_used`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select a time duration from the provided options (1-5 minutes, 5-30 minutes, 30-60 minutes, 1-2 hours).
	+ This helps us understand the duration of your usage sessions.
* **How much time did you spend using the feature last time?** (`how_much_time_last_used`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select a time duration from the provided options (1-5 minutes, 5-30 minutes, 30-60 minutes, 1-2 hours).
	+ This helps us know the duration of your last interaction.
* **How satisfactory do you find the feature?** (`how_satisfying_is_the_feature`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select a level of satisfaction from the provided options (Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied).
	+ This helps us know your overall opinion of the feature.
* **Do you have any other comments or feedback about the feature?** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Please provide any additional comments or feedback you might have.
	+ This helps us understand your thoughts and concerns about the feature.
* **What is your name?** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Please provide your name so we know who to contact.
	+ This helps us respond to your feedback or follow up on any questions.
* **What is your email address?** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false):
	+ Please provide your email address so we can contact you.
	+ This helps us get in touch with you for further discussion or clarification.
* **What is your phone number?** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Please provide your phone number so we can contact you.
	+ This helps us reach out to you if we need more information.
* **Would you recommend the feature to others?** (`would_you_recommend`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select a level of recommendation from the provided options (Highly, Somewhat, Not at all).
	+ This helps us know if you would suggest the feature to others.
* **Do you have any suggestions for improvement or new features?** (`suggestion`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Please provide any suggestions or ideas you might have.
	+ This helps us understand your expectations and improve the feature.

Note: The required fields are marked as such but are not strictly enforced in this help guide to avoid confusion.
