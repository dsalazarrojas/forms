# Hair Color Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers to provide personalized hair styling recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter your customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
3. Choose the customer's current hair color from the list of options in the "What is the customer's hair color?" field.
4. Enter the customer's desired hair color in the "What is the customer's desired hair color?" field.
5. Select the customer's hair length from the list of options in the "What is the customer's hair length?" field.
6. Choose the customer's hair type from the list of options in the "What type of hair do they have?" field.
7. Select the customer's face shape from the list of options in the "What is the customer's face shape?" field.
8. Select the customer's skin tone from the list of options in the "What is the customer's skin tone?" field.
9. Select the customer's face frame shape from the list of options in the "What is the customer's face frame shape?" field.
10. Choose the texture of the customer's hair from the list of options in the "What is the texture of the customer's hair?" field.
11. Select the style you are looking for from the list of options in the "What style are you looking for?" field.
12. Select the products you recommend for the customer in the "What products do you recommend?" field.
13. Enter any additional customer notes in the "Any customer notes?" field.
14. Enter the color you think you match with best in the "What color do you think you match with best?" field.
15. Enter your name as a stylist in the "Your name as a stylist?" field.
