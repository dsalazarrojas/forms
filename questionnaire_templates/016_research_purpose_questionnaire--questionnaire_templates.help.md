# Research Purpose Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for researchers to document the purpose and scope of their research projects, ensuring accurate and complete information is collected.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by reading through each section carefully to understand the required fields and the information you need to provide.
2. Fill in the form completely and accurately, using the available options and text fields as needed.
3. Make sure to check all required fields before submitting the form.
