# Novel Comprehension Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Novel Comprehension Quiz is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with a novel. This feedback is used to improve the quiz and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question based on your actual experience with the novel.
2. Fill out the form only once for each novel you've read.
3. Use the options provided as a guide, but feel free to provide detailed explanations in text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was your favorite part of the book** (`question_1`, text, required: false): This question asks about the most memorable or enjoyable part of the book for you.
* **What was the main character's name** (`question_2`, select_one, required: false): Please choose the main character's name from the options provided or type in the correct name if not listed.
* **How would you rate your experience** (`question_3`, text, required: false): Rate your experience with the novel on a scale of your choice.
* **What was the author's name** (`question_4`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the author's name from the options provided.
* **What was the book's setting** (`question_5`, text, required: false): Describe the setting of the novel.
* **What was the main plot** (`question_6`, text, required: false): Summarize the main plot of the novel.
* **Was the book a good read** (`question_7`, select_one, required: false): Rate the book as a good read or not.
* **How would you rate the book** (`question_8`, text, required: false): Rate the book on a scale of your choice.
* **Was the book easy to understand** (`question_9`, select_one, required: false): Rate the book as easy or not to understand.
* **Do you plan to read more from the author** (`question_10`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose if you plan to read more from the author or not.
