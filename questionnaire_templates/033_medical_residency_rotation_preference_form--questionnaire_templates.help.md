# prophets_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "prophets_form" is a form designed to collect medical residency rotation preferences from users. This form is used to gather information about the user's rotation choices, which can be used for administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Select your choices for each rotation from the dropdown menu, or enter a unique Medical Resident ID.
3. Ensure that each field is filled in correctly to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical Residency Rotation Preference Form** (`resident_rotation_preference_form`, `text`, optional): 
  This is the title of the form, and is not a field that needs to be filled in.
* **Medical Resident ID** (`medical_resident_id`, `number`, optional): 
  Enter your unique Medical Resident ID here.
* **Rotation Choice 1** (`rotation_choice_1`, `select_one`, optional): 
  Select "Yes" or "No" for Rotation Choice 1.
* **Rotation Choice 2** (`rotation_choice_2`, `select_one`, optional): 
  Select "Yes" or "No" for Rotation Choice 2.
* **Rotation Choice 3** (`rotation_choice_3`, `select_one`, optional): 
  Select "Yes" or "No" for Rotation Choice 3.
* **Rotation Choice 4** (`rotation_choice_4`, `select_one`, optional): 
  Select "Yes" or "No" for Rotation Choice 4.
* **Rotation Choice 5** (`rotation_choice_5`, `select_one`, optional): 
  Select "Yes" or "No" for Rotation Choice 5.
* **Rotation Choice 6** (`rotation_choice_6`, `select_one`, optional): 
  Select "Yes" or "No" for Rotation Choice 6.

## Tips
* Please fill in each field carefully to ensure accurate results.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact your administrator for assistance.
