# Three Factor Eating Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Three Factor Eating Questionnaire is a tool to gather data about an individual's eating habits and behavior.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that best describe your eating habits and behaviors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How do you usually eat?**
	+ **Rarely**: You rarely eat
	+ **Sometimes**: You sometimes eat
	+ **Often**: You often eat
	+ **Always**: You always eat
* **Hunger level when eating**
	+ **Not Hungry**: You usually don't feel hungry when eating
	+ **A little hungry**: You feel a little hungry when eating
	+ **Hungry**: You feel hungry when eating
	+ **Very hungry**: You feel very hungry when eating
* **What is your usual portion size?**
	+ **Small**: You usually eat small portions
	+ **Medium**: You usually eat medium portions
	+ **Large**: You usually eat large portions
	+ **Very Large**: You usually eat very large portions
* **How many meals do you usually eat per day?**
	+ **1**: One meal per day
	+ **2**: Two meals per day
	+ **3**: Three meals per day
	+ **4+**: Four or more meals per day
* **How much water do you drink daily?**
	+ Enter the amount of water you drink per day
* **What level of physical activity do you perform on a daily basis?**
	+ **None**: No physical activity
	+ **Low**: Light physical activity (e.g., walking, light exercise)
	+ **Medium**: Moderate physical activity (e.g., running, swimming)
	+ **High**: Heavy physical activity (e.g., weightlifting, marathon running)
* **How would you rate your stress level?**
	+ **0**: No stress
	+ **1**: Mild stress
	+ **2**: Moderate stress
	+ **3**: High stress
	+ **4**: Very high stress
	+ **5**: Extreme stress
* **Eating frequency**
	+ **Breakfast**: You usually eat breakfast
	+ **Snack**: You usually eat snacks
	+ **Lunch**: You usually eat lunch
	+ **Dinner**: You usually eat dinner
* **Do you have any eating patterns or habits?**
	+ Enter any eating patterns or habits you have
* **Do you have any food preferences?**
	+ Enter any food preferences you have
* **Are there any foods you are not willing to eat?**
	+ Enter any foods you are not willing to eat
* **Do you have any medical conditions?**
	+ Enter any medical conditions you have
* **What medications are you taking?**
	+ Enter any medications you are taking
* **Do you have any food allergies?**
	+ **No**: No food allergies
	+ **Yes**: You have food allergies
* **What is your sleep quality?**
	+ **Poor**: Poor sleep quality
	+ **Fair**: Fair sleep quality
	+ **Good**: Good sleep quality
	+ **Excellent**: Excellent sleep quality
* **How many hours of sleep do you get per night?**
	+ **Less than 1**: Less than one hour
	+ **1-2**: One to two hours
	+ **3-4**: Three to four hours
	+ **5 or more**: Five or more hours
* **What is your wake-up time?**
	+ **Morning**: Early morning
	+ **Afternoon**: Late afternoon
	+ **Evening**: Late evening
* **At what time do you usually wake up?**
	+ Enter your wake-up time
* **If other, specify wake-up time**
	+ Enter any other wake-up time
* **At what time do you usually go to bed?**
	+ Enter your bedtime
* **Do you have a set sleep schedule?**
	+ **Yes**: You have a set sleep schedule
	+ **No**: No set sleep schedule
	+ **Sometimes**: Sometimes you have a set sleep schedule
* **Do you plan your meals?**
	+ **Yes**: You plan your meals
	+ **No**: No meal planning
* **Which foods do you usually plan?**
	+ **Breakfast**: You usually plan breakfast
	+ **Lunch**: You usually plan lunch
	+ **Dinner**: You usually plan dinner
* **How often do you plan your meals?**
	+ **Daily**: Daily meal planning
	+ **Weekly**: Weekly meal planning
* **Additional comments**
	+ Enter any additional comments you have
