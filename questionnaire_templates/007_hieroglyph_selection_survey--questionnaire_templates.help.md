# Hieroglyph Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hieroglyph Selection Survey is designed to collect information on hieroglyphs, Egyptian symbols, and demographic data from users. This form aims to gather your thoughts, experiences, and preferences related to these symbols.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the relevant pages to complete by clicking on the "Next" and "Back" buttons.
2. For each page, read the instructions and question carefully.
3. Fill in the fields as prompted, and use the options provided for each question.
4. If you need to go back to a previous page, click on the "Back" button.
5. Once you have completed all the pages, click on the "Submit" button to submit your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Page** (Survey Page, text, required/optional): This page is the main introduction to the survey.
* **Egyptian Symbol** (Egyptian Symbol, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ The Ankh
	+ The Djed
	+ The Eye of Ra
	+ The Scarab
	+ The Was
* **Preferred Symbol** (Preferred Symbol, select_multiple, required): Select all the Egyptian symbols you prefer:
	+ The Ankh
	+ The Djed
	+ The Eye of Ra
	+ The Scarab
	+ The Was
* **What is your age?** (What is your age?, number, required/optional): Enter your age.
* **Favorite Symbol** (Favorite Symbol, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the Egyptian symbols you like:
	+ The Ankh
	+ The Djed
	+ The Eye of Ra
	+ The Scarab
	+ The Was
* **When did you visit Egypt?** (When did you visit Egypt?, date, required/optional): Enter the date you visited Egypt.
* **What is your job title?** (What is your job title?, text, required/optional): Enter your job title.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Goals** (Goals, text, required/optional): Enter your goals.
* **What is your income?** (What is your income?, number, required/optional): Enter your income.
* **How often do you visit museums?** (How often do you visit museums?, number, required/optional): Select the frequency of your visits.
* **What is your native language?** (What is your native language?, select_one, required/optional): Select your native language.
* **How long have you been working on Egyptian symbols?** (How long have you been working on Egyptian symbols?, time, required/optional): Enter the time you have been working on Egyptian symbols.
* **Do you have any other questions?** (Do you have any other questions?, text, required/optional): Enter any other questions or comments you have.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes you have.
