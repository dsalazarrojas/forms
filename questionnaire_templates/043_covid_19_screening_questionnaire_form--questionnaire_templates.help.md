# Covid 19 Screening Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about a patient's health and potential exposure to COVID-19. The purpose of this form is to help assess the patient's risk level and provide guidance on any necessary precautions or treatments.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name and contact information in the first two fields.
2. If you have any symptoms, please describe them in the "Symptoms" field.
3. Indicate your vaccination status, including the number of doses you have received and the date of your last vaccination.
4. If you are a healthcare worker, please indicate whether you are at high risk of exposure.
5. Report any recent travel to high-risk areas.
6. Describe your exposure risk level, which can be Low, Medium, or High.
7. If you have symptoms, indicate the start and end dates of your symptoms.
8. Enter any relevant medical history, allergies, medication, medical conditions, and medical tests.
9. Finally, review your answers carefully to ensure accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification or medical record.
* **Contact Information**: Enter the patient's contact information, including address, phone number, and email.
* **Symptoms**: Describe any COVID-19 symptoms you are experiencing, such as fever, cough, or shortness of breath.
* **Vaccination Status**: Select your current vaccination status:
	+ Never: You have not received any COVID-19 vaccinations.
	+ Once: You have received at least one dose of the COVID-19 vaccine.
	+ Ongoing: You are receiving ongoing vaccination series.
* **Healthcare Worker**: Indicate whether you are a healthcare worker who may be at high risk of exposure:
	+ True: You are a healthcare worker and may be at high risk of exposure.
	+ False: You are not a healthcare worker and are not at high risk of exposure.
* **Recent Travel**: Indicate whether you have recently traveled to high-risk areas:
	+ Never: You have not recently traveled to high-risk areas.
	+ True: You have recently traveled to high-risk areas.
	+ Partial: You have traveled to high-risk areas, but not recently.
* **Exposure Risk**: Select your exposure risk level:
	+ Low: You are at low risk of exposure.
	+ Medium: You are at moderate risk of exposure.
	+ High: You are at high risk of exposure.
* **Symptoms Start Date**: Enter the start date of your symptoms, if you are experiencing any.
* **Symptoms End Date**: Enter the end date of your symptoms, if you are no longer experiencing them.
* **Vaccination Dose**: Enter the number of COVID-19 vaccination doses you have received.
* **Vaccination Date**: Enter the date of your last vaccination.
* **Vaccination Time**: Enter the time of your last vaccination.
* **Medical History**: Describe any relevant medical history, such as previous illnesses or allergies.
* **Allergies**: Describe any allergies or sensitivities you may have.
* **Medication**: Describe any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Conditions**: Describe any medical conditions you have, such as diabetes or heart disease.
* **Medical Tests**: Describe any recent medical tests or procedures you have undergone.

**Tips**

* Please answer all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any question, please consult with a healthcare professional.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
