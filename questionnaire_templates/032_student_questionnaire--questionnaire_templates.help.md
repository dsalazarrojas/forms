# Student Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students about their experiences in a school. It helps the school understand their strengths and areas for improvement, enabling them to make informed decisions and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the relevant information about your school experience and fill out each field accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### School Rating (ID: 1)
* **School Rating** (number): Rate your overall experience with the school on a scale of 1-5.
* **Label:** `School Rating`
* **Type:** number
* **Required:** No

### Teacher Rating (ID: 2)
* **Teacher Rating** (number): Rate your experience with your teachers on a scale of 1-5.
* **Label:** `Teacher Rating`
* **Type:** number
* **Required:** No

### Classroom Rating (ID: 3)
* **Classroom Rating** (number): Rate your experience with your classroom on a scale of 1-5.
* **Label:** `Classroom Rating`
* **Type:** number
* **Required:** No

### School Facilities (ID: 4)
* **School Facilities** (multi-select): Select the facilities that you have experience with at your school. Options are:
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Label:** `School Facilities`
* **Type:** select_multiple
* **Required:** No

### School Services (ID: 5)
* **School Services** (multi-select): Select the services that you have experience with at your school. Options are:
	+ Always
	+ Sometimes
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **Label:** `School Services`
* **Type:** select_multiple
* **Required:** No

### Teacher Availability (ID: 6)
* **Teacher Availability** (multi-select): Select the availability of your teachers. Options are:
	+ Very Responsive
	+ Somewhat Responsive
	+ Not Very Responsive
* **Label:** `Teacher Availability`
* **Type:** select_multiple
* **Required:** No

### Overall Experience (ID: 7)
* **Overall Experience** (note): Describe your overall experience with the school in a short paragraph.
* **Label:** `Overall Experience`
* **Type:** note
* **Required:** No

### School Culture (ID: 8)
* **School Culture** (multi-select): Select the culture of your school. Options are:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Label:** `School Culture`
* **Type:** select_multiple
* **Required:** No

## Tips
* Answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Please select all the facilities and services you have experience with, and rate your experience accordingly.
* You can provide additional feedback in the overall experience field.
