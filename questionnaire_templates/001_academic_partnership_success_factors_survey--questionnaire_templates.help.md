# Academic Partnership Success Factors Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on the success factors and experiences of academic partnerships between institutions. It aims to gather information on various aspects of partnerships, including partner information, partnership type, communication efficiency, resource sharing, goal alignment, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the "Partner Information" section, which includes:
	* "Name of Partner Institution or Organization": Enter the legal name of the collaborating entity.
2. Select the "Type of Partnership" from the list:
	* Research Collaboration
	* Student Internship Program
	* Faculty Exchange
	* Curriculum Development
	* Community Outreach
3. Choose your "Primary Role" in the partnership:
	* Lead Researcher
	* Project Coordinator
	* Administrative Staff
	* Faculty Mentor
4. Evaluate the "Communication Effectiveness" of the partnership by selecting:
	* Excellent
	* Good
	* Average
	* Poor
5. Rate the "Efficiency of Resource Sharing" between institutions by choosing:
	* Very Efficient
	* Efficient
	* Neutral
	* Inefficient
	* Very Inefficient
6. Assess the "Alignment of Partnership Goals" with your institution's objectives by selecting:
	* Fully Aligned
	* Mostly Aligned
	* Partially Aligned
	* Not Aligned
7. Identify the "Key Factors Contributing to Success" by selecting all that apply:
	* Mutual Trust
	* Clear Governance
	* Shared Vision
	* Adequate Funding
	* Strong Leadership
	* Effective Technology
8. Enter the "Duration of Partnership" in years, which represents the total time of active collaboration.
9. Provide an "Overall Satisfaction Rating" between 1.0 and 10.0.
10. Offer "Additional Comments or Insights" for any feedback or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Partner Institution or Organization**: Enter the legal name of the collaborating entity.
* **Type of Partnership**: Select the primary nature of the collaboration.
* **Your Primary Role**: Choose your main responsibility in the partnership.
* **Communication Effectiveness**: Evaluate the frequency and clarity of communication between partners.
* **Efficiency of Resource Sharing**: Rate the efficiency of sharing data, facilities, and personnel between institutions.
* **Alignment of Partnership Goals**: Assess the degree to which partnership goals align with your institution's objectives.
* **Key Factors Contributing to Success**: Identify all factors contributing to the partnership's success.
* **Duration of Partnership**: Enter the total time of active collaboration.
* **Overall Satisfaction Rating**: Enter a value between 1 and 10 to express your satisfaction.
* **Additional Comments or Insights**: Provide any additional feedback or suggestions.
