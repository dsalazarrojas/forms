# Social Media Ethics Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Social Media Ethics Assessment form is designed to evaluate an individual's or organization's social media ethics by asking them to rate their awareness and understanding of social media's impact on their personal and professional life.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please read each question carefully and choose the correct option that reflects your answer.
2.  If a question has multiple options, select the one that best describes your answer.
3.  Be honest and accurate in your responses as they will be used to guide you or your organization in improving social media practices.
