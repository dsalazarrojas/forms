# Social Movements And Their Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Movements And Their Impact Survey is designed to collect information about social movements and their effects on society and participants. This form helps gather insights on the awareness, impact, and characteristics of social movements. Please fill out this form if you are interested in participating in or have experience with social movements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Read the instructions and each field explanation carefully before starting.
2. Answer every question that applies to you.
3. If you have any doubts or concerns, refer to the field-by-field explanation for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This is the starting point for the survey. Please write a brief introduction about the social movement you are filling out the form for, including any relevant background information.
* **Social Movement Awareness** (`2`, `number`, required: true): Rate your level of awareness about the social movement. Answer with the number corresponding to your level of awareness (e.g., 1-5).
* **Participation in Social Movements** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Answer the following questions with a 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your level of participation in the social movement. You can select multiple options.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Perceived Outcomes** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the outcomes you perceive from the social movement. Answer with a 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Impact on Society** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Describe the impact of the social movement on society.
* **Impact on Participants** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Describe the impact of the social movement on its participants.
* **Role in Movement** (`7`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the role you played in the social movement. Answer with a 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Social Movement Characteristics** (`8`, `text`, required: false): List any characteristics of the social movement that are not listed in the options provided.
* **Other Social Movement Characteristics** (`9`, `text`, required: false): If there are any other characteristics of the social movement not listed, please describe them here.
* **Other Social Movement Characteristics** (`10`, `text`, required: false): (This field is a duplicate of field 9, please fill this out only if you have new information that was not mentioned in field 9.)
* **Conclusion** (`11`, `text`, required: false): Summarize your experience or insights from the social movement you are filling out this form for.
