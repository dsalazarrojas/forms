# web_design_client_questionnaire_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be used by clients and their representatives to provide detailed information about their web design project, enabling designers to effectively communicate and meet their expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and provide accurate information about your project.
2. Select the relevant options for each field to ensure that you provide the best possible answers.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that the designer can proceed with the project accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Client Information
* **Client Information** (`client_info`, text, optional): Please provide your name and contact details.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): Describe your project in brief.
* **Client Terms** (`client_terms`, text, optional): List any specific terms or requirements you have.

### Page 2: Project Details
* **Project Design** (`project_design`, text, optional): Outline your design preferences.
* **Client Wants** (`client_wants`, text, optional): Specify what you want to achieve with this project.
* **Client Contact** (`client_contact`, text, optional): Provide your contact details.

### Page 3: Project Schedule
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, optional): List any project-specific terms and conditions.
* **Website Features** (`website_features`, text, optional): Specify the features you require for your website.
* **Client Comments** (`client_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or questions.
* **Client Requirements** (`client_requirements`, text, optional): Specify any requirements or restrictions.

### Page 4: Project Status
* **Design Info** (`design_info`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the best-suited design option for your project.
* **Designer Info** (`designer_info`, text, optional): Provide information about your designer.
* **Designer Comments** (`designer_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or questions for your designer.

### Page 5: Payment and Timeline
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): Outline your payment terms.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Provide payment details.
* **Project Schedule** (`project_schedule`, date, optional): Specify the project timeline.
* **Project Deadline** (`project_deadline`, date, optional): Set a deadline for the project.
* **Project Launch** (`project_launch`, date, optional): Plan for the project launch.

### Page 6: Project Management
* **Designer Experience** (`designer_experience`, number, optional): Specify your designer's level of experience.
* **Design Hours** (`design_hours`, number, optional): Estimate the design hours required.
* **Project Hours** (`project_hours`, number, optional): Specify the project hours.
* **Total Hours** (`total_hours`, number, optional): Calculate the total hours required.

Note: The fields marked as required are essential for the design process, but you are not obligated to provide them. However, providing accurate and complete information ensures a smooth project progression.
