# Stage Presence Improvement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on stage presence improvement opportunities for performers at a school. The form is intended to gather quantitative and qualitative data on how well performers are doing regarding their stage presence, coaching, and performance.

The survey aims to identify areas of improvement and suggestions on how to enhance stage presence and overall performance quality. The data collected will be used to inform future development and coaching strategies.

## How To Complete This Form

### Introduction
This page is just a introduction to the survey. Please continue to the next page to start the survey.

### Self-Assessment
* **Self-Assessment** (`self_assessment`, `text`, optional): Please provide a brief self-assessment of your stage presence and performance quality. This can be anything from 'excellent' to 'needs improvement'. 

### Coaching Feedback
* **Coaching Feedback** (`coaching_feedback`, `text`, optional): What feedback do you think you've received from your coaches? Please provide a brief description.

### Performer Satisfaction
* **Performer Satisfaction** (`performer_satisfaction`, `number`, optional): On a scale of 1-5, how satisfied are you with your current stage presence and performance quality?

### Student Satisfaction
* **Student Satisfaction** (`student_satisfaction`, `number`, optional): On a scale of 1-5, how satisfied are your students with your current stage presence and performance quality?

### Speaker Satisfaction
* **Speaker Satisfaction** (`speaker_satisfaction`, `number`, optional): On a scale of 1-5, how satisfied are your speakers with your current stage presence and performance quality?

### Performer Coaching
* **Performer Coaching** (`performer_coaching`, `select_multiple`, optional): Do you receive regular coaching on your stage presence and performance quality? Please select 'Yes' if you do, 'No' if you don't.

### Performance Coaching
* **Performance Coaching** (`performance_coaching`, `select_multiple`, optional): Do you receive regular coaching on your overall performance quality? Please select 'Yes' if you do, 'No' if you don't.

### Performer Coaching Feedback
* **Performer Coaching Feedback** (`performer_coaching_feedback`, `select_multiple`, optional): What coaching feedback do you receive on your stage presence? Please select 'Yes' if you do, 'No' if you don't.

### Speaker Coaching Feedback
* **Speaker Coaching Feedback** (`speaker_coaching_feedback`, `select_multiple`, optional): What coaching feedback do you receive on your overall performance quality? Please select 'Yes' if you do, 'No' if you don't.

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Is there anything else you'd like to add about your stage presence and performance quality? Please provide any additional comments or suggestions.

### Tips
- Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible in your answers.
- You can refer to specific performances or events when providing feedback.
- If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide them in the "Additional Comments" field.
