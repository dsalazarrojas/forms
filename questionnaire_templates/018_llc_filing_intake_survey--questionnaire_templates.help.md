# LLC Filing Intake Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form gathers information for the purpose of filing an LLC in a state or local government.

## How To Complete This Form
- To begin, fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Enter your first name and last name in the respective fields.
- Enter the business name and its address, city, state, and zip code in the respective fields.
- Select the entity type for your business (Corporation, Partnership, or LLC).
- Enter your email and phone number for contact purposes.
- If applicable, enter the date of birth for the business.
- If your business has an attorney representing it, enter the attorney's name, phone number, and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name here.
- **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name here.
- **Business Name** (`Business Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your business here.
- **Business Address** (`Address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of your business here.
- **City** (`City`, `text`, required: false): Enter the city where your business is located here.
- **State** (`State`, `text`, required: false): Enter the state where your business is located here.
- **Zip Code** (`Zip Code`, `text`, required: false): Enter the zip code where your business is located here.
- **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email here.
- **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number here.
- **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, required: false): If you're an individual, enter your date of birth here. If you're a business, you can leave this field blank.
- **Purpose of Form** (`Purpose of Form`, `text`, required: false): Enter the purpose for filing this form here.
- **Entity Type** (`Entity Type`, `text`, required: false): Enter the type of entity you're representing here (e.g., Corporation, Partnership, LLC).
- **Entity Address** (`Entity Address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of your business here.
- **Entity City** (`Entity City`, `text`, required: false): Enter the city where your business is located here.
- **Entity State** (`Entity State`, `text`, required: false): Enter the state where your business is located here.
- **Entity Zip Code** (`Entity Zip Code`, `text`, required: false): Enter the zip code where your business is located here.
- **Attorney Name** (`Attorney Name`, `text`, required: false): If you have an attorney representing your business, enter their name here.
- **Attorney Phone** (`Attorney Phone`, `text`, required: false): If you have an attorney representing your business, enter their phone number here.
- **Attorney Email** (`Attorney Email`, `email`, required: false): If you have an attorney representing your business, enter their email here.
- **Business Description** (`Business Description`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your business here.
- **Business Industry** (`Business Industry`, `text`, required: false): Enter the industry your business operates in here.
- **Business Entity Type** (`Business Entity Type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Enter the type of entity you're representing here (e.g., Corporation, Partnership, LLC).
- **Entity Date of Birth** (`Entity Date of Birth`, `date`, required: false): If you're representing a business with a date of birth, enter it here.
- **Entity Date of Birth** (`Entity Date of Birth`, `date`, required: false): This field is identical to the above field; please fill it in accordingly.
