# Dental Loan Eligibility Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Dental Loan Eligibility Assessment" form is used to collect information from users about their loan requirements for dental expenses. This form helps determine eligibility for a loan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide user information, including your contact number and email address. This will help us get in touch with you with any further questions or updates.
2. Describe the loan details, including the amount and terms you are looking for.
3. Specify the interest rate and loan term you are interested in.
4. Choose the loan frequency (one-time, ongoing, or both).
5. Select the loan purpose (financing for dental work only, dental and other medical expenses, or other).
6. Enter your date of birth.
7. Enter any additional comments or information that might be helpful for us to consider your loan request.
8. Finally, select the loan status (Active, Denied, Approved, or Pending).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (User Information, text, required: false)**: Enter your name and other relevant information to help us contact you.
* **Loan Details (Loan Details, text, required: false)**: Provide a description of the loan you are applying for.
* **Loan Amount (Loan Amount, number, required: false)**: Enter the loan amount you are interested in.
* **Loan Interest Rate (Loan Interest Rate, number, required: false)**: Enter the interest rate you are willing to accept.
* **Loan Term (Loan Term, select_one, required: false)**: Choose the loan term you are interested in (12 months, 24 months, 36 months, 48 months, 60 months, 72 months).
* **Loan Frequency (Loan Frequency, select_multiple, required: false)**: Choose how often you need a loan (One-time, Ongoing, Both).
* **Loan Purpose (Loan Purpose, select_one, required: false)**: Select why you are applying for a loan (Financing for dental work only, Financing for dental and other medical expenses, Other).
* **Date Of Birth (Date Of Birth, date, required: false)**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Contact Number (Contact Number, text, required: false)**: Enter your contact number.
* **Email Address (Email Address, email, required: false)**: Enter your email address.
* **Additional Comments (Additional Comments, note, required: false)**: Enter any additional comments or information that might be helpful.
* **Loan Status (Loan Status, select_one, required: false)**: Select the loan status you are currently in (Active, Denied, Approved, Pending).
