# Personal Lines Insurance Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about insurance policies. The data collected will be used for administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select the type of policy you are dealing with from the "Policy Type" dropdown menu.
2. Choose the type of insurance you are dealing with from the "Insurance Type" dropdown menu.
3. If required, enter the coverage amount in the "Coverage" field.
4. Enter the start and end dates of the policy in the "Policy Start Date" and "Policy End Date" fields.
5. Enter the annual premium amount in the "Policy Annual Premium" field.
6. Enter the first and last names of the contact person in the "Contact First Name" and "Contact Last Name" fields.
7. Enter the email address of the contact person in the "Contact Email" field.
8. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the "Contact Phone" field.
9. Enter the location of the policy in the "Policy Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Policy Type
* **Policy Type** (`Policy Type`, Select One, Required): Select the type of policy you are dealing with.
### Insurance Type
* **Insurance Type** (`Insurance Type`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose the type of insurance you are dealing with.
### Coverage
* **Coverage** (`Coverage`, Number, Optional): If required, enter the coverage amount.
### Policy Start Date
* **Policy Start Date** (`Policy Start Date`, Date, Required): Enter the start date of the policy.
### Policy End Date
* **Policy End Date** (`Policy End Date`, Date, Required): Enter the end date of the policy.
### Policy Annual Premium
* **Policy Annual Premium** (`Policy Annual Premium`, Number, Optional): Enter the annual premium amount.
### Contact First Name
* **Contact First Name** (`Contact First Name`, Text, Required): Enter the first name of the contact person.
### Contact Last Name
* **Contact Last Name** (`Contact Last Name`, Text, Required): Enter the last name of the contact person.
### Contact Email
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, Email, Required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
### Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
### Policy Location
* **Policy Location** (`Policy Location`, Text, Optional): Enter the location of the policy.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that you select the correct policy type and insurance type.
* Double-check that you have entered accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
