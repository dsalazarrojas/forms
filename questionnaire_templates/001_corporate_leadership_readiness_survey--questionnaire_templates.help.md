# Corporate Leadership Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Leadership Readiness Survey is a tool to help corporate leaders assess their own readiness to take on new challenges, identify areas for personal growth, and provide feedback and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and answer them to the best of your ability.
2. Answer the questions honestly and concisely.
3. If you are unsure about any of the questions, you can leave them blank or note a "Not Applicable" answer.
4. Please provide specific examples and examples whenever possible to support your answers.
5. Proofread your answers for accuracy and clarity before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): A brief introduction about yourself.
* **Name** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Your name, which will be used to identify you in the survey results.
* **Role** (`3`, `select_one`, required/optional): Your current role within the organization.
* **Years of Service** (`4`, `number`, required/optional): Your number of years of service with the organization.
* **Location** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Your work location or department.
* **Department** (`6`, `select_one`, required/optional): The department you belong to (e.g., Human Resources, Finance, etc.).
* **Supervisor Name** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): Your supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Title** (`8`, `text`, required/optional): Your supervisor's title.
* **Job Title** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): Your job title.
* **Leadership Style** (`10`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all leadership styles that apply to you (e.g., Collaborative, Results-driven, Decisive, Innovative).
* **Strengths** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): A brief description of your strengths.
* **Weaknesses** (`12`, `text`, required/optional): A brief description of your weaknesses.
* **Development Needs** (`13`, `text`, required/optional): Suggestions for areas you would like to develop or improve.
* **Feedback** (`14`, `text`, required/optional): Feedback or suggestions you have for the organization or your supervisor.
* **Suggestions** (`15`, `text`, required/optional): Additional suggestions or ideas for improvement.
* **Comments** (`16`, `text`, required/optional): Any additional comments you would like to add.
* **Questions** (`17`, `text`, required/optional): Any questions or concerns you have.
* **Action Items** (`18`, `text`, required/optional): Any action items or tasks you need to take.
* **Next Steps** (`19`, `text`, required/optional): Plans or next steps for improvement.
* **Date** (`20`, `date`, required/optional): The date you complete this form.

Please note that all answers are confidential and will be used to improve leadership development programs and initiatives within the organization.
