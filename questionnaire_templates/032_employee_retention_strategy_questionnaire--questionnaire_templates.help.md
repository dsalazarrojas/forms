# Employee Retention Strategy Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help organizations identify the key factors that influence employee retention. By answering these questions, you can gain insights into the drivers of employee satisfaction, company culture, values, image, career growth, communication, job fit, job security, and job satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

*   Read each question carefully and select the options that best answer each question.
*   You can select multiple options for most questions.
*   Make sure to answer all questions, as they are all optional.
