# Strategy Workshop Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users for the purpose of preparing for the strategic workshop. It will help you and the workshop organizers understand your needs and expectations, ensuring that you are well-prepared and have a clear understanding of the workshop's objectives.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Read through each section carefully and make sure to answer all required fields.
*   Select the relevant options for each section, based on your needs and preferences.
*   Enter any additional information that you think is relevant to the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **intro_section** (`intro_section`, `note`, required): This is an introductory section that provides a brief overview of the workshop's objectives and expectations. Please read through this section carefully to get a clear understanding of what the workshop is about.
*   **section_a** (`section_a`, `select_multiple`, required): This section requires you to select multiple options that best describe your needs for the workshop. Please choose the options that apply to you.
*   **section_b** (`section_b`, `select_one`, required): In this section, please select one option that best describes your preference for the workshop.
*   **section_c** (`section_c`, `date`, required): This section requires you to enter a date in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **section_d** (`section_d`, `time`, required): This section requires you to enter a time in the format 12:00 PM/HH:MM AM/PM.
*   **section_e** (`section_e`, `text`, required): In this section, please enter any additional information that you think is relevant to the workshop.
*   **section_f** (`section_f`, `email`, required): This section requires you to enter your email address.
*   **section_g** (`section_g`, `text`, required): In this section, please enter any additional information that you think is relevant to the workshop.
*   **section_h** (`section_h`, `number`, required): This section requires you to enter a numerical value.
*   **section_i** (`section_i`, `number`, required): In this section, please enter a numerical value.
*   **section_j** (`section_j`, `text`, required): In this section, please enter any additional information that you think is relevant to the workshop.
*   **section_k** (`section_k`, `text`, required): In this section, please enter any additional information that you think is relevant to the workshop.
*   **section_l** (`section_l`, `note`, required): This section is a note-taking section where you can enter any additional comments or notes that you think are relevant.
*   **section_m** (`section_m`, `select_one`, required): In this section, please select one option that best describes your preference.
*   **section_n** (`section_n`, `text`, required): In this section, please enter any additional information that you think is relevant to the workshop.

## Tips

*   Make sure to answer all required fields.
*   Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure that they are accurate and complete.
*   If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the sections, please contact the workshop organizers.
