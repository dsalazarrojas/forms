# retirement_contribution_gap_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps users evaluate and potentially close the gap in their retirement plan contributions.
## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions as accurately as possible, given that this is a self-reporting form.
2. Review all the questions and ensure you have answered truthfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_page** (1, Select Multiple, Optional): This field asks if you have contributed to your retirement plans. Please select all that apply, if applicable.
* **contribution_frequency** (2, Select One, Optional): Choose how often you contribute to your retirement plans (monthly, quarterly, or annually).
* **contribution_amount** (3, Number, Optional): Enter the amount you usually contribute to your retirement plans.
* **start_date** (4, Date, Optional): Select the date when you started making contributions to your retirement plans.
* **expected_retirement_date** (5, Date, Optional): Enter the expected date you plan to retire.

## Tips

* Answer all questions truthfully to accurately assess your retirement plan.
* If you're not sure about a particular question, try your best to make an informed estimation.
* Remember, this form is a tool to help identify potential gaps in your retirement strategy; it is not a replacement for professional financial advice.
