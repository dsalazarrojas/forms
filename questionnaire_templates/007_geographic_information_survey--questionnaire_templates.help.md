## Geographic Information Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic and geographic information from users to help us better understand the diversity of our community. Your participation will help us to create more tailored and relevant content and services for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please select your home location from the options provided.
2. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian for permission before proceeding.
3. Select your education level from the options provided.
4. Choose your income range from the options provided.
5. If you select "other" for your income range, please provide more details in the text field below.
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Select your gender from the options provided.
9. Select the languages you speak fluently.
10. Finally, click the submit button to submit your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Location** (`home_location`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select your home location from the options provided. This will help us understand your geographic location.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required: false): Please enter your age. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian for permission before proceeding.
* **Ethnicity** (`ethnicity`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your ethnicity. This will help us understand your cultural background.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select your education level from the options provided.
* **Income Range** (`income_range`, `select_one`, required: false): Please choose your income range from the options provided.
* **Income Range Other** (`income_range_other`, `text`, required: false): If you select "other" for your income range, please provide more details in this text field.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Please enter your email address.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select your gender from the options provided.
* **Language Spoken** (`language_spoken`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select the languages you speak fluently.
