# COVID 19 Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a questionnaire designed to collect information from individuals or households affected by the COVID-19 pandemic. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the household's needs and circumstances for non-profit support purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately, providing the required information for each field.
2. Make sure to review each field's required status before proceeding.
3. If you need help with any field, please refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of your family or household.
* **Number of Household Members** (2, number, required): Include yourself in the count.
* **Primary Contact Phone Number** (3, text, required): Enter the primary phone number of your household.
* **Current Home Address** (4, text, required): Enter the current address of your household.
* **Has your employment status been affected by the pandemic?** (5, select_one, required): Select one of the options provided to indicate the impact of the pandemic on your employment status.
* **Does your household have any immediate needs?** (6, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply from the options provided to indicate the immediate needs of your household.
* **Do all household members have access to the internet for remote learning or work?** (7, select_one, required): Select one of the options provided to indicate the household's internet access status.
* **Number of household members over age 65** (8, number, required): Enter the number of household members over the age of 65.
* **List any specific medical or health supplies needed** (9, text, optional): If your household has specific medical or health needs, list them here, e.g. insulin, specialized formula, masks.
* **Preferred time for assistance delivery or pickup** (10, select_one, required): Select one of the options provided to indicate the preferred time for assistance delivery or pickup.
* **Certification** (11, note, optional): This section certifies that the information provided is for non-profit support use. Please select 'True' to confirm.
* **I certify that the information provided is true and correct** (12, select_one, required): Select 'True' to confirm that the information provided is accurate.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section for clarification.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us.
