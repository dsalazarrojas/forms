# Diversity & Inclusion Advisory Panel Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for collecting information from individuals interested in joining or participating in our Diversity & Inclusion Advisory Panel.

## How To Complete This Form

Before proceeding, please ensure that you have all required and optional information ready for completion. 

1. Fill in each field accurately and honestly.
2. Select your desired role from the provided options in "Role" section.
3. Review your submission for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`Name`, text, required): 
  Please enter your full name.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): 
  Enter a valid email address where you can be reached by our team.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): 
  Enter your primary phone number for easy contact.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, optional): 
  If you prefer to be contacted at a specific address, please provide it.
* **Experience** (`Experience`, text, optional): 
  Share any experience relevant to your interest in the panel, including work history, education, or other notable achievements.
* **Motivation** (`Motivation`, text, optional): 
  Explain your reasons for applying to join or participate in the panel.
* **Role** (`Role`, select_one, required):
  Choose one of the following roles:
  + **Chair**: A leadership position overseeing the panel's activities.
  + **Member**: A participant contributing to discussions and activities.
  + **Alternate**: A reserve member filling in for others as needed.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, note, not required):
  Review and finalize your submission for accuracy.
