# Warm Up Sizes Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers about their warm-up garment requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please fill out the form thoroughly and accurately.
* If you're unsure about any question, please ask your coach or contact person for clarification.
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Warm-up Garment Size** (`size_question`): Select your warm-up garment size from the dropdown menu. This will help us get an accurate fit for your garment.
* **Fit Preference** (`fit_preference`): Choose your preferred fit: Relaxed, Comfort, or Performance. This will guide our garment creation process.
* **Size Chart** (`size_chart`): Select your size chart type: S, M, L, or XL. This will help us ensure accurate sizing for your garment.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`): Choose the type of order you're making: New, Replacement, Gift, or Other. This will help us process your order efficiently.
* **Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Mobile/Phone** (`contact_number`): Enter your mobile or phone number for communication purposes.
* **Coach Details** (`coach_details`): Select the relevant coach details: Coach Name, School/Club, Contact Email, or Contact Number. This will help us get in touch with your coach for any clarification or support.
* **School Details** (`school_details`): Select the relevant school details: School Name, School Code, or Location. This will help us get in touch with your school for any clarification or support.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`): Select the relevant order details: Number of Orders, Order Date, or Order Type. This will help us process your order efficiently.
* **Garment Details** (`garment_details`): Select the relevant garment details: Garment Type, Garment Size, or Garment Color. This will help us create your garment according to your specifications.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`): Enter any additional comments or information about your order.

Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your coach or contact person for assistance.
