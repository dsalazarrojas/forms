# Prospective Traveler Vacation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from prospective travelers about their upcoming vacation plans. The purpose of the form is to help travel agencies understand the traveler's preferences, budget, and other relevant details to provide personalized services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the "Travel planner" page to provide a brief description of your travel plans.
2. Enter your preferred destination on the "Destination" field.
3. Specify your travel dates on the "Travel dates" field.
4. Mention the duration of your trip on the "Travel duration" field.
5. Indicate whether you have a budget for your trip on the "Travel budget" field.
6. Specify if you have a budget for your trip on the "Budget" field.
7. Provide any additional comments or preferences on the "Additional comments" field.
8. Mention your travel frequency on the "Travel frequency" field.
9. Provide your contact information on the "Contact information" field.
10. Enter your email address on the "Email" field.
11. If applicable, indicate if you have been assigned a travel agency on the "Assigned travel agency" field.
12. If applicable, provide the label for your assigned travel agency on the "Assigned travel agency label" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Travel planner
* **Travel planner**: A brief description of your travel plans.
*   This field is a free-text field where you can provide a brief description of your travel plans.

### 2. Destination
* **Destination**: Your preferred travel destination.
*   Please enter the city, country, or region you would like to travel to.

### 3. Travel dates
* **Travel dates**: The dates of your trip.
*   Enter the start and end dates of your trip.

### 4. Travel duration
* **Travel duration**: The length of your trip.
*   Enter the number of days you plan to travel.

### 5. Travel budget
* **Travel budget**: Whether you have a budget for your trip.
*   Select "Yes" if you have a budget for your trip, or "No" if you do not have a budget.

### 6. Budget
* **Budget**: Your travel budget.
*   Select "Yes" if you have a budget, or "No" if you do not have a budget.

### 7. Travel frequency
*   **Travel frequency**: How often you travel.
*   Select "Yes" if you travel frequently, or "No" if you do not travel often.

### 8. Additional comments
* **Additional comments**: Any additional information you would like to provide.
*   This field is for any additional comments or preferences you may have.

### 9. Contact information
* **Contact information**: Your contact information.
*   Please provide your name, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.

### 10. Email
*   **Email**: Your email address.
*   Enter your email address.

### 11. Assigned travel agency
*   **Assigned travel agency**: Whether you have been assigned a travel agency.
*   Select "Yes" if you have been assigned a travel agency, or "No" if you have not.

### 12. Assigned travel agency label
*   **Assigned travel agency label**: The label for your assigned travel agency.
*   Please provide the label for your assigned travel agency.
