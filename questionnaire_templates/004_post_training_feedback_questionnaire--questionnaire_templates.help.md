# post_training_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from trainers and participants on their recent training session. Your input will help us understand your experience and improve future training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Begin by reading the introduction page to understand the purpose of this form.
2. Fill in your name as a trainer or participant.
3. Provide your rating for the trainer on a scale of 1-5.
4. Provide your rating for the session on a scale of 1-5.
5. Write any additional feedback you would like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (1/5):
	+ This is the first page of the form, providing a brief introduction to the feedback process.
* **Trainers Name** (2/5):
	+ Please enter your name as a trainer or participant who attended the session.
* **Trainer Rating** (3/5):
	+ Rate the trainer's performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Session Rating** (4/5):
	+ Rate the session's overall effectiveness on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Feedback** (5/5):
	+ Write any additional comments or suggestions you would like to share about the training session.
