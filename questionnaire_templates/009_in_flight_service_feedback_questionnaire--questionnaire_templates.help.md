# In Flight Service Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The In Flight Service Feedback Questionnaire is designed to collect feedback from passengers about their in-flight experience. This form will help us improve our services and ensure that you, our valued customer, have a positive experience on our flights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions and choose your answers from the dropdown menus.
2. Be as honest and thorough as possible in your answers.
3. Fill out the form only once, with your personal experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Flight details** (`flight_details`, `text`, required: false): Please describe your flight experience, including the flight number, date, and any other relevant details.
- **Flight number** (`flight_number`, `number`, required: false): This is the number of your flight.
- **Flight date** (`flight_date`, `date`, required: false): This is the date of your flight.
- **Flight time** (`flight_time`, `time`, required: false): This is the time of your flight.
- **Cabin name** (`cabin_name`, `text`, required: false): This field is not used in the current form. Please fill it out if you have any comments about the cabin.
- **Seat number** (`seat_number`, `number`, required: false): This is the number of your seat.
- **Flight rating** (`flight_rating`, `select_one`, required: true): Please choose how you rate your flight experience.
- **Cabin rating** (`cabin_rating`, `select_one`, required: true): Please choose how you rate your cabin experience.
- **Overall experience** (`overall_experience`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Please choose all the experiences that apply to your overall experience.
- **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Please share any comments or suggestions you have about your flight experience.
- **Passenger type** (`passenger_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Please choose your passenger type.
- **Airline name** (`airline_name`, `text`, required: false): This field is not used in the current form.
- **Crew rating** (`crew_rating`, `select_one`, required: true): Please choose how you rate your crew experience.
- **Ground staff rating** (`ground_staff_rating`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is not used in the current form.
- **In-flight staff rating** (`in_flight_staff_rating`, `select_one`, required: true): Please choose how you rate your in-flight staff experience.
- **Food and beverage rating** (`food_and_beverage_rating`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is not used in the current form.
- **In-flight entertainment rating** (`in_flight_entertainment_rating`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is not used in the current form.
- **In-flight service rating** (`in_flight_service_rating`, `select_one`, required: true): Please choose how you rate your in-flight service experience.
- **Overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: true): Please choose how you rate your overall satisfaction.
- **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, required: false): Please share any suggestions or comments about your flight experience.
