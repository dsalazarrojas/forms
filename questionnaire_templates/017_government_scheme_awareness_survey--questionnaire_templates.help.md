# Government Scheme Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Government Scheme Awareness Survey form, explaining its purpose and how to complete it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Answer each question according to your experience and opinion about the government scheme.
2. Select the relevant options for each question based on your response.
3. Fill in any necessary details such as email, phone number, and date/time.
4. Once you've completed all the questions, submit the form to provide your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the government scheme?** (`survey_program`, select_one, required): This question asks you to indicate whether you are aware of the government scheme. Select "Yes" if you are familiar with it, and "No" if you are not.
* **How have you participated in the scheme?** (`program_participation`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to indicate how you have participated in the scheme. Select all applicable options (e.g., "Yes" for each way you have participated).
* **How satisfied are you with the government scheme?** (`program_satisfaction`, select_one, required): This question asks you to rate your level of satisfaction with the scheme. Select "Yes" if you are satisfied, and "No" if you are not.
* **What barriers do you face in accessing the scheme?** (`access_barriers`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to identify any barriers you face in accessing the scheme. Select all applicable options (e.g., "Yes" for each barrier you face).
* **How often do you participate in the scheme?** (`program_participation_frequency`, select_one, required): This question asks you to rate how often you participate in the scheme. Select "Yes" for regular participation, and "No" for infrequent or no participation.
* **How satisfied are you with the accessibility of the scheme?** (`program_access_satisfaction`, select_one, required): This question asks you to rate your level of satisfaction with the accessibility of the scheme. Select "Yes" if you are satisfied, and "No" if you are not.
* **Do you think the scheme is well funded?** (`program_funding`, select_one, required): This question asks you to rate your opinion on the funding of the scheme. Select "Yes" if you think it is well funded, and "No" if you do not.
* **How often do you see advertisements for the scheme?** (`program_advertisement`, select_one, required): This question asks you to rate how often you see advertisements for the scheme. Select "Yes" for regular advertisements, and "No" for infrequent or no advertisements.
* **Do you think the scheme is well promoted?** (`program_promotion`, select_one, required): This question asks you to rate your opinion on the promotion of the scheme. Select "Yes" if you think it is well promoted, and "No" if you do not.
* **Do you think you are well supported in the scheme?** (`program_support`, select_one, required): This question asks you to rate your level of support in the scheme. Select "Yes" if you feel well supported, and "No" if you do not.
* **What do you think can be done to improve the scheme?** (`citizen_feedback`, note, required): This question allows you to provide any suggestions or feedback on how to improve the scheme.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This field asks for your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): This field asks for your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Program Participation Frequency** (`program_participation_frequency`, number, required): This question asks you to rate your frequency of participation in the scheme. Select a number to indicate how often you participate.
* **How satisfied are you with the scheme?** (`program_satisfaction_satisfaction_level`, number, required): This question asks you to rate your level of satisfaction with the scheme. Select a number to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **How accessible is the scheme?** (`program_access_satisfaction_accessibility_level`, number, required): This question asks you to rate the accessibility of the scheme. Select a number to indicate how accessible you think it is.
* **How well funded do you think the scheme is?** (`program_funding_funding_level`, number, required): This question asks you to rate your opinion on the funding of the scheme. Select a number to indicate how well funded you think it is.
* **Program Advertisement Advertisement Frequency** (`program_advertisement_advertisement_frequency`, number, required): This question asks you to rate how often you see advertisements for the scheme. Select a number to indicate how often you see them.
* **How often do you see promotions for the scheme?** (`program_promotion_promotion_frequency`, number, required): This question asks you to rate how often you see promotions for the scheme. Select a number to indicate how often you see them.
* **How well supported do you feel in the scheme?** (`program_support_support_level`, number, required): This question asks you to rate your level of support in the scheme. Select a number to indicate how well supported you feel.
* **When did you participate in the scheme?** (`date`, date, required): This question asks for the date you participated in the scheme.
* **What time did you participate in the scheme?** (`time`, time, required): This question asks for the time you participated in the scheme.
* **When do you expect to participate in the scheme again?** (`date_2`, date, required): This question asks for the date you expect to participate in the scheme again.
* **Time 2** (`time_2`, time, required): This question asks for the time you expect to participate in the scheme again.

## Tips
Please take your time to answer each question honestly and according to your experience and opinion.
