# Phone System Setup Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a business's phone system setup, which will be used to guide the setup and integration process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Business Information**: Fill in the basic contact information for your business.
2. **Phone System ID**: Enter a unique ID for your phone system.
3. **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for contact and communication.
4. **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
5. **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
6. **Contact Address**: Enter the physical address of your business.
7. **Business Type**: Select the type of business (telecom provider, IT team, or service partner).
8. **Business Size**: Select the size of your business (small, medium, or large).
9. **Number of Employees**: Enter the total number of employees in your business.
10. **Employee Count**: Enter the current number of employees.
11. **Phone System Details**: Describe the details of your phone system.
12. **Existing System**: Indicate whether you already have a phone system in place.
13. **Existing System Name**: If you have an existing system, enter its name.
14. **Existing System Info**: Provide information about your existing phone system.
15. **Existing System Notes**: Add any additional notes about your existing phone system.
16. **Phone System Requirements**: Specify the features required for your phone system, such as voicemail and call forwarding.
17. **Required Features**: Select additional features required or desired for your phone system.
18. **System Requirements 1-4**: Describe any specific requirements for your phone system.
19. **Additional Features**: List any additional features desired for your phone system.
20. **Next Steps**: Describe the next steps for setup and integration.
21. **Comments**: Add any comments or notes about your phone system.
