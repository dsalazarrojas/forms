# Attachment Style Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This questionnaire is designed to assess your attachment style, which is a crucial aspect of building and maintaining healthy relationships. It will ask you to rate your agreement or disagreement with various statements related to emotional closeness, intimacy, and dependence. The goal is to identify areas where you may need to grow and improve in your relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select a response for each statement, indicating how much you agree or disagree with it.
2. Answer the questions honestly, and try to reflect on your feelings and experiences in previous relationships.
3. Take your time to think carefully before making your selections.
4. You can skip any questions that don't apply to you or make you uncomfortable.
5. If you have any additional comments or thoughts, please use the space at the end of the form.
