# UK Visa Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The UK Visa Questionnaire is a document used to gather necessary information for a UK visa application. It collects personal details and date of birth to identify the applicant accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below and fill out the required information:

1. Provide your **First name** in the field marked "First name". This is a text field where you can enter your given name at birth.
2. Enter your **Last name** in the field marked "Last name". This is another text field for your surname.
3. Enter your **Date of birth** in the format "dd/mm/yyyy" in the field marked "Date of birth". This is a date field to capture your birthdate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name** (required): Enter your first name here.
- **Last name** (required): Enter your last name here.
- **Date of birth** (required): Enter your date of birth in the format "dd/mm/yyyy".
 
## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid any delays or issues with your visa application.
- If you're unsure about any information, you can contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
- Review the form before submitting to ensure you haven't missed any fields.
