# Dog Training And Behavior Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a dog's training and behavior. It will help gather essential details about the dog's owner, training goals, behavior questions, and veterinary information. This form is crucial for veterinarians and dog trainers to have a comprehensive understanding of the dog's needs and develop an effective training plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `owner_info` page with the owner's contact information.
2. Provide details about the dog in the `dog_info` page.
3. Answer the `training_goals` questions in the `behavior_questions` page.
4. Select the appropriate response for `training_needs` and `veterinary_agreement`.
5. Answer the `training_methods` question, if applicable.
6. Enter veterinary information in the `veterinary_info` page.
7. Add any additional medical notes in the `medical_notes` page, if necessary.
8. Provide the emergency contact information in the `emergency_contact` page.
9. Enter veterinary contact information in the `veterinary_contact` page.
10. Confirm the veterinary agreement in the `veterinary_agreement` page.
11. Provide any veterinary notes, if necessary.
12. Answer the `training_agreement` question.
13. Add any additional comments in the `additional_comments` page, if necessary.
14. Enter owner comments in the `owner_comments` page, if necessary.
15. Add any medical comments in the `medical_comments` page, if necessary.
16. Confirm the veterinary certification.
17. Confirm the owner certification.
18. Enter veterinary signature and date of completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **owner_info** (`owner_info`, text, required): Enter the owner's name, address, phone number, and email.
* **dog_info** (`dog_info`, text, required): Provide details about the dog, including its name, age, breed, and species.
* **training_goals** (`training_goals`, text, required): Describe the training goals for the dog.
* **behavior_questions** (`behavior_questions`, select_multiple, required): Answer the following questions:
	+ Yes, No
* **training_needs** (`training_needs`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **training_methods** (`training_methods`, text, required): Describe the training methods used.
* **veterinary_info** (`veterinary_info`, text, required): Enter the veterinary information, including name, address, and phone number.
* **medical_notes** (`medical_notes`, note, required): Enter any additional medical notes about the dog.
* **emergency_contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Provide the contact information for emergency situations.
* **veterinary_contact** (`veterinary_contact`, text, required): Enter the contact information for the veterinarian.
* **veterinary_agreement** (`veterinary_agreement`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **veterinary_notes** (`veterinary_notes`, note, required): Enter any additional veterinary notes.
* **veterinary_certification** (`veterinary_certification`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **owner_certification** (`owner_certification`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **veterinary_signature** (`veterinary_signature`, text, required): Enter the veterinarian's signature.
* **date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of completion.
* **owner_signature** (`owner_signature`, text, required): Enter the owner's signature.
* **veterinarian_signature** (`veterinarian_signature`, text, required): Enter the veterinarian's signature.
* **additional_comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments about the dog.
* **owner_comments** (`owner_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments from the owner.
* **medical_comments** (`medical_comments`, text, required): Enter any medical comments.
* **veterinary_certification_page** (`veterinary_certification`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **owner_certification_page** (`owner_certification`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **veterinary_signature_page** (`veterinary_signature`, text, required): Enter the veterinarian's signature.
* **owner_signature_page** (`owner_signature`, text, required): Enter the owner's signature.
* **veterinarian_signature_page** (`veterinarian_signature`, text, required): Enter the veterinarian's signature.
* **form_completion** (`form_completion`, text, required): Enter the date of completion.
