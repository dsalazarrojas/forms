# Virtual Event Quality Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Event Quality Questionnaire is designed to collect feedback and quality ratings from attendees of virtual events. This form helps event organizers to improve their events by identifying areas of improvement and providing actionable insights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and answer each question based on your experience at the virtual event.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and honest information.
3. Use the rating scales to provide a rating for each question.
4. Use the "Additional Comments" field to provide any additional comments or feedback.
5. Review your answers and make sure everything is accurate and complete before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Details
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you attended.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Speaker**: Enter the name of the speaker.

### Ratings
* **Rating Event**: Rate the overall quality of the event.
* **Speaker Rating**: Rate the presentation skills of the speaker.
* **Materials Rating**: Rate the quality of the materials provided.
* **Overall Rating**: Rate the overall experience of the event.
* **Content Rating**: Rate the relevance and interest of the content.
* **Presentation Rating**: Rate the presentation skills of the speaker.
* **Time Rating**: Rate the time management of the event.
* **Organization Rating**: Rate the organization and logistics of the event.
* **Technology Rating**: Rate the technology used during the event.
* **Value Rating**: Rate the value provided by the event.
* **Clarity Rating**: Rate the clarity of the content and instructions.
* **Engagement Rating**: Rate the engagement of the audience.
* **Relevance Rating**: Rate the relevance of the content.
* **Interest Rating**: Rate the interest of the content.
* **Liking Rating**: Rate how much you enjoyed the event.
* **Willingness Rating**: Rate the willingness to participate in future events.
* **Satisfaction Rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the event.

### Comments
* **Additional Comments**: Use this field to provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the event.
