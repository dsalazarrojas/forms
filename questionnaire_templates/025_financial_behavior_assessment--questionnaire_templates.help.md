# Financial Behavior Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Behavior Assessment is a questionnaire designed to evaluate your financial habits, attitudes, and confidence levels related to spending, budgeting, saving, and risk tolerance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question according to your actual financial behavior and attitude.
2. Select only one option for questions with "select_one" type fields.
3. Select all applicable options for questions with "select_multiple" type fields.
4. Enter numerical values for questions with "number" type fields.
5. Enter dates for questions with "date" type fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Behaviors** (2, select_one, required): Indicate whether you currently practice saving money.
* **Financial Behaviors 2** (3, select_multiple, required): List all the ways you make money.
* **Budgeting Frequency** (4, date, required): Enter the frequency with which you create and follow a budget.
* **Budgeting Confidence** (5, time, required): Estimate how confident you are in your ability to create and follow a budget.
* **Saving Confidence** (6, number, required): Indicate your level of confidence in your ability to save money.
* **Spending Amount** (7, number, required): Enter the total amount you spend each month.
* **Savings Strategies** (8, select_one, required): Select the strategy you use for savings.
* **Financial Education** (9, select_multiple, required): Choose all the financial education you have received.
* **Budgeting Tools** (10, select_one, required): Select the budgeting tool you use.
* **Budgeting Difficulties** (11, select_multiple, required): List all the difficulties you face when budgeting.
* **Confidence** (12, number, required): Estimate your confidence level in your financial decisions.
* **Risk Tolerance** (13, select_one, required): Indicate your risk tolerance level.
* **Risk Tolerance 2** (14, select_one, not required): This question is not required.
* **Risk Tolerance 3** (15, select_one, not required): This question is not required.
* **Financial Goal** (16, select_one, required): Choose your financial goal.
* **Time Management** (17, select_multiple, required): List all the techniques you use to manage your time.
* **Self Efficacy** (18, select_one, not required): This question is not required.
* **Financial Awareness** (19, select_one, required): Indicate your level of financial awareness.
* **Financial Awareness 2** (20, select_one, required): Choose your level of financial awareness.
* **Financial Goal 2** (21, select_multiple, required): List all the financial goals you have set.
* **Spending Confidence** (22, select_one, required): Indicate your level of confidence in your spending decisions.
* **Budgeting Confidence** (23, select_one, required): Indicate your confidence level in your budgeting abilities.
* **Risk Tolerance 4** (24, select_one, not required): This question is not required.
* **Risk Tolerance 5** (25, select_one, not required): This question is not required.
