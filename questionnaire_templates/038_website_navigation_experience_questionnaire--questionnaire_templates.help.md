# Website Navigation Experience Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this questionnaire is to collect user feedback on your experience with our website's navigation. This will help us to identify areas for improvement and optimize the user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions as accurately as possible. Each section is designed to gather specific information that will help us improve the website's user experience. Please take a few minutes to fill out the form, and do not hesitate to provide as much detail as you feel necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Homepage Experience** (`homepage_experience`, `text`, optional)
This field is designed to gather information on your experience with the homepage. Please describe your experience, including any positive or negative comments you may have.

### 2. **Navigation Menu** (`navigation_menu`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select all the menu items you have used during your visit. This will help us to understand which menu items are most useful to you.

### 3. **Footer** (`footer`, `select_one`, optional)
Select the section of the footer that you found most useful during your visit.

### 4. **Search** (`search`, `text`, optional)
Please describe your experience with the search function on the website.

### 5. **Breadcrumbs** (`breadcrumbs`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select all the breadcrumb items you have used during your visit.

### 6. **Contact** (`contact`, `text`, optional)
Please describe your experience with the contact information on the website, including any difficulties you may have had.

### 7. **Accessibility** (`accessibility`, `select_one`, optional)
Select the accessibility feature that you found most useful during your visit.

### 8. **Device Experience** (`device_experience`, `select_one`, optional)
Select the device you used to access the website (e.g., desktop, tablet, mobile).

### 9. **Visitor Type** (`visitor_type`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select the type of visitor you are (e.g., returning, first-time, regular).

### 10. **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional)
Please provide any additional feedback you may have about your experience with the website's navigation.

### 11. **Other** (`other`, `text`, optional)
Please use this field to provide any other information that may help us improve the website's navigation.

### 12. **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional)
Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted about your feedback.

### 13. **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional)
Please provide your phone number if you would like to be contacted about your feedback.

### 14. **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional)
Please use this field to provide any additional information that may help us improve the website's navigation.

### 15. **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional)
Please enter the date of your visit.

### 16. **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional)
Please enter the time of your visit.

### 17. **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional)
Please click submit once you have completed all the fields. 

Note that fields with an optional status will only be filled in if you have something to report. If not, please leave it blank.
