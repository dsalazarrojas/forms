# HPC Cluster Setup Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about HPC cluster setups to help identify and improve cluster resources, support, and sustainability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question to the best of your ability. Be as detailed and specific as possible when providing information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hpc team members**: Check if there are any team members associated with the HPC cluster.
* **IT lead**: Enter the name of the IT lead responsible for the cluster.
* **IT lead email**: Enter the email address of the IT lead.
* **IT lead phone**: Enter the phone number of the IT lead (optional).
* **Research lead**: Enter the name of the research lead associated with the cluster.
* **Research lead email**: Enter the email address of the research lead.
* **Research lead phone**: Enter the phone number of the research lead (optional).
* **Engineering lead**: Enter the name of the engineering lead associated with the cluster.
* **Engineering lead phone**: Enter the phone number of the engineering lead (optional).
* **Cluster type**: Indicate if the cluster is for production or research purposes.
* **Cluster name**: Enter a unique name for the cluster.
* **Cluster description**: Provide a brief description of the cluster.
* **Cluster location**: Enter the location of the cluster.
* **Cluster operating system**: Rate the cluster's operating system quality (Good or Fair).
* **Cluster storage**: Indicate if the cluster has storage capabilities (Yes or No).
* **Cluster networking**: Indicate if the cluster has networking capabilities (Yes or No).
* **Cluster software**: Indicate if the cluster has software (Yes or No).
* **Cluster security**: Indicate if the cluster has security features (Yes or No).
* **Cluster support**: Indicate if the cluster has support (Yes or No).
* **Support team**: Indicate if the cluster has a support team (Yes or No).
* **Support contact**: Indicate if the cluster has a support contact (Yes or No).
* **Timelines**: Enter any relevant timelines for the cluster.
* **Cluster purpose**: Indicate if the cluster is for production or research purposes (Yes or No).
* **Cluster budget**: Enter the cluster's budget.
* **Cluster sustainability**: Indicate if the cluster is sustainable (Yes or No).
