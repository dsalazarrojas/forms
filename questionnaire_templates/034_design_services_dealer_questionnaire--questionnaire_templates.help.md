# Design Services Dealer Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects general information from design services dealers for our internal use.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply fill in the required and optional information. If a field is marked as "optional" in this guide, it means you can leave it blank if you don't have that information.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (Text, Optional): Enter your general business information.
* **Design Services** (Select multiple, Optional): Choose all the design services you provide (e.g. Graphic Design, Web Design).
* **Service Requirement** (Note, Optional): If you have any specific service requirements for your business, describe them here.
* **Preferred Contact** (Select one, Optional): Indicate your preferred contact method (e.g. Email, Phone, In-person).
* **Project Deadline** (Date, Optional): If you have a project deadline, select the date.
* **Project Details** (Select one, Optional): Indicate the project size (e.g. Small, Medium, Large).
* **Project Budget** (Number, Optional): Enter your project budget.
* **Service Level Agreement** (Select multiple, Optional): Check if you have a service level agreement in place (e.g. 'Yes', 'No').
