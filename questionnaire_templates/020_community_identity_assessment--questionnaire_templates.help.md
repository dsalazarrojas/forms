# Community Identity Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Community Identity Assessment form is a tool to gather information about residents' perceptions of their community's identity, strengths, and challenges. This information will be used to make informed decisions and improve the community's quality of life.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select only one option from the provided choices for each question.
3. For open-ended questions, provide a brief and concise answer.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Your Neighborhood** (`neighborhood_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of your neighborhood or community.
* **How many years have you lived in this community?** (`residency_years`, number, required): Please enter the number of years you have lived in this community.
* **Primary Reason for Choosing to Live Here** (`residency_reason`, select_one, required):
	+ Family and Heritage: You chose to live in this community for family ties or cultural heritage.
	+ Proximity to Work: You chose this community for its proximity to your workplace or job opportunities.
	+ Housing Affordability: You chose this community for its affordable housing options.
	+ Lifestyle and Amenities: You chose this community for its lifestyle and amenities offered.
	+ Historical Significance: You chose this community for its historical significance and cultural attractions.
* **How strong is your sense of belonging to this community?** (`belonging_sense`, select_one, required):
	+ Very Strong: You strongly feel connected to this community.
	+ Strong: You feel connected to this community.
	+ Neutral: You neither strongly feel nor don't feel connected to this community.
	+ Weak: You don't feel very connected to this community.
	+ Very Weak: You don't feel connected to this community at all.
* **Most Valued Community Asset** (`valued_asset`, select_one, required):
	+ Public Parks and Green Spaces: You value the community's parks and green spaces.
	+ Local Small Businesses: You value the community's local businesses and entrepreneurship spirit.
	+ Schools and Libraries: You value the community's education and learning resources.
	+ Cultural and Ethnic Diversity: You value the community's cultural and ethnic diversity.
	+ Safety and Low Crime: You value the community's safety and low crime rate.
* **What is your perception of how the community is changing?** (`change_perception`, select_one, required):
	+ Improving Significantly: You perceive the community as improving significantly over the last 5 years.
	+ Staying about the same: You perceive the community as staying about the same over the last 5 years.
	+ Declining in Quality: You perceive the community as declining in quality over the last 5 years.
* **Level of Cultural Heritage Representation in Public Spaces** (`cultural_representation`, select_one, required):
	+ Excellent - Very Visible: You see a strong representation of the community's cultural heritage in public spaces.
	+ Good - Some representation: You see some representation of the community's cultural heritage in public spaces.
	+ Fair - Minimal representation: You see minimal representation of the community's cultural heritage in public spaces.
	+ Poor - Not represented: You don't see any representation of the community's cultural heritage in public spaces.
* **Frequency of Positive Interaction with Neighbors** (`neighbor_interaction`, select_one, required):
	+ Daily: You interact with your neighbors daily.
	+ Weekly: You interact with your neighbors weekly.
	+ Monthly: You interact with your neighbors monthly.
	+ Rarely or Never: You rarely or never interact with your neighbors.
* **What is the single biggest challenge facing this community today?** (`top_challenge`, text, required): Please describe the biggest challenge facing the community in a brief and concise manner.
* **One word to describe this community identity** (`identity_word`, text, required): Please enter one word that best describes this community's identity.
* **Suggestions for strengthening the local community identity** (`strengthening_suggestions`, text, optional): Please provide any suggestions for events, projects, or improvements to strengthen the community's identity.
* **Are you interested in joining a community identity committee?** (`interest_in_committee`, select_one, required):
	+ True: Yes, you are interested in joining the committee.
	+ False: No, you are not interested in joining the committee.
	+ Send more info: You are interested in joining but need more information.
