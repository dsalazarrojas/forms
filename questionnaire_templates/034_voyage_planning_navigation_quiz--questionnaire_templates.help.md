# Voyage Planning Navigation Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Voyage Planning Navigation Quiz is a form designed to assess an individual's knowledge and understanding of navigation concepts related to voyage planning, navigation systems, and chart reading. It is intended to help evaluate the skills and abilities of sailors, boaters, or anyone interested in navigation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by answering the question on the "Voyage Planning Navigation Quiz" page.
2. Next, provide your thoughts on "Navigation Planning" in the text field provided.
3. Finally, enter your "Passage Planning" thoughts in the text field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Voyage Planning Navigation Quiz** (`1`, `text`, required: false`): This is the main question of the form, where you are asked to demonstrate your understanding of voyage planning concepts. Please provide your answer in a concise and clear manner.
* **Navigation Planning** (`2`, `text`, required: false`): In this field, please describe your approach to navigation planning, including any relevant techniques or methods you use. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Passage Planning** (`3`, `text`, required: false`): Here, you are asked to provide your thoughts on passage planning, including any considerations you take into account when planning a passage. Please be specific and thorough.

## Tips
* Please answer each question to the best of your knowledge and ability.
* Be as clear and concise as possible in your answers.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
