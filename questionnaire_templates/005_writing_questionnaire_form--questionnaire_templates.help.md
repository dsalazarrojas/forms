# Writing Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the writing habits and experiences of users. It's a questionnaire that helps us understand the frequency, enjoyment, and challenges users face when writing. The answers will help us to refine our writing resources and tools to better support our community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer according to your personal experience.
2. Select the correct response for each question, even if it's not an easy one.
3. Don't worry about running out of space; you can answer all questions on every page, and submit when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Writing Question 1**: Do you enjoy writing? 
    * (`writing_question1`, select_one, required)
    * Answer: Yes or No, indicating your level of enjoyment when writing.
* **Writing Question 2**: How often do you write? 
    * (`writing_question2`, select_multiple, required)
    * Answer: Choose all that apply from the options provided.
