# Selection And Time Tracking Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Selection and Time Tracking Survey" form is a tool to gather data on user activities, time allocation, and satisfaction levels. It helps in understanding user preferences and experiences related to selection and time tracking processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Start by providing your name and selecting the relevant Activity Choices** in the "Activity Choices" field. This field is a multiple-choice question with options A, B, and C.
2. **Provide your time allocation information in the "Time Allocation" field**. This is a free-text field where you can provide details on how you allocate your time.
3. **Rate your satisfaction level** in the "Satisfaction" field. This field is designed to measure your satisfaction levels on a scale.
4. **Enter details about the assigned tool** in the "Assigned Tool" field. This field is a text field where you can provide information about the tool assigned to you.
5. **Add any additional comments or feedback** in the "Selection and Time Tracking Survey Form Template for Jotform" field. This field is a note field where you can write any comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Activity Choices**: Select the relevant activities that apply to you (A, B, or C).
* **Time Allocation**: Provide your time allocation details, e.g., how you allocate your time between tasks.
* **Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction level on a scale (not specified).
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter details about the tool assigned to you, e.g., its name or description.
* **Selection and Time Tracking Survey Form Template for Jotform**: Add any additional comments or feedback about the form.
