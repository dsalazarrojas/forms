# refer a friend questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a friend or acquaintance for internal purposes, such as marketing or networking.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information about your friend or acquaintance.
2. Select the correct option for the relationship between you and your friend (Friend, Coworker, or Family Member).
3. Provide the friend's contact information (email, phone number, and birthday).
4. Answer the questions about your friend's education, occupation, revenue, and net worth.
5. Optionally, provide a message to your friend or a question to help us better understand their relationship.
