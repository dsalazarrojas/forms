# Preventive Care Literacy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preventive Care Literacy Survey is a tool designed to measure the level of understanding patients have about preventive care, specifically regarding heart disease, stroke, and healthy weight. The survey aims to identify knowledge gaps and provide a basis for patient education.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please enter your name as it appears on your medical record.
2. Enter your age.
3. Enter your address.
4. Enter your email address. (Please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.)
5. Answer each question with a simple "Yes" or "No" or select from the available options.
6. For multiple-choice questions, select all applicable answers.
7. Rate your current level of knowledge about heart disease prevention on a scale of High, Low, or No answer.
8. Answer the question regarding the best way to prevent stroke, heart disease, and diabetes.
9. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your understanding of a healthy weight.
10. Answer the question about the best way to prevent diabetes.
11. If you have any other health concerns, please describe them in detail.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your medical record.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter your age.
* **Address** (`address`, note, required): Enter your current address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your accurate and up-to-date email address.
* **What is the best way to prevent heart disease?** (`question1`, select_one, required): Answer with a "Yes" or "No".
* **Do you understand what is meant by a healthy diet?** (`question2`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable answers.
* **How do you usually get your health information?** (`question3`, select_one, required): Answer with "Yes" or "No".
* **What is the purpose of the Preventive Care Literacy Survey?** (`question4`, note, optional): Note field for additional information.
* **How do you rate your current level of knowledge about heart disease prevention?** (`question5`, select_one, required): Select a rating (High, Low, or No answer).
* **What is the primary source of your health information?** (`question6`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable answers.
* **What is the best way to prevent stroke?** (`question7`, select_one, required): Answer with "Yes" or "No".
* **Do you understand what is meant by a healthy weight?** (`question8`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" or "No".
* **What is the best way to prevent diabetes?** (`question9`, select_one, required): Answer with "Yes" or "No".
* **Do you have any other health concerns?** (`question10`, text, optional): Describe your concerns in detail.

## Tips
* Please answer each question accurately and honestly.
* Select only applicable answers for multiple-choice questions.
* Rate your knowledge level truthfully.
* Provide detailed information for any other health concerns.
* If you need help or clarification, please don't hesitate to ask your healthcare provider.
