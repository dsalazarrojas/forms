# entry_filter_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The entry_filter_survey form is designed to collect information about participants and filter them based on certain criteria. This form is intended to be used in conjunction with other forms to filter participants based on their status and information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the participant's information carefully.
2. Select the correct status for the participant.
3. Enter any additional information about the participant as needed.
4. Review the options for participant_info_1 to participant_info_6 fields for any additional information that may be relevant to filtering the participant.
5. Submit the form when complete.
