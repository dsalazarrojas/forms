# Real Estate Investor Biography Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Investor Biography Form is designed to gather information about real estate investors. This form is used for [INSERT PRACTICAL USE CASE, e.g., investment reports, marketing materials, or investor directory]. The fields included in this form help us understand the investor's characteristics, such as their investment history, business operations, and personal details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the following steps:

1. Determine if the investor is real estate investor by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the first field.
2. Provide the investor's name in the "Investor Name" field.
3. Enter the name of the investor's company in the "Company" field.
4. If the investor has a website, enter it in the "Website" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Is Real Estate Investor?** (`real_estate_investor_biography_form`, select_multiple, optional): This field helps us understand if the individual is a real estate investor. Please select "Yes" if you are a real estate investor, and "No" otherwise.
* **Investor Name** (`investor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the real estate investor. This is a text field where you can type in the name as it appears on any public records or the investor's business card.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the investor's company. This is helpful for our records and future reference.
* **Website** (`website`, text, optional): If the investor has a website, enter the URL here. This can be a public or private website, depending on the investor's preference.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your responses for accuracy, especially the investor's name and company name.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to contact us for guidance.
