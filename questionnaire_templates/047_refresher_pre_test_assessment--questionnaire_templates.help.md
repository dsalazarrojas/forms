# Refresher Pre Test Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Refresher Pre Test Assessment is a tool used by the organization to evaluate your current level of knowledge and confidence in your role. This form will help us understand your current state and identify areas where additional training is needed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully, ensuring you have answered all required fields.
2. Select the most accurate responses for each question.
3. Provide detailed answers whenever you are asked to describe your thoughts or feelings.
4. Sign the form with your name and date at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: `false`): This is a series of questions to help us understand your current level of knowledge and confidence in the organization.
* **Section 1** (`section1`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Please select all that apply to your situation.
	+ **What is your role in the organization?** (`question1`, `select_one`, required: `true`): Please be specific about your job title and responsibilities.
	+ **How long have you been in the role?** (`question1_answer`, `number`, required: `true`): Enter the number of months or years you have been in your current role.
	+ **What type of training have you received?** (`question2`, `select_multiple`, required: `true`): Select all relevant training options.
* **Section 2** (`section2`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): 
	+ **How do you feel about your current level of knowledge?** (`question4`, `select_one`, required: `true`): Select one of the options.
	+ **How do you feel about your current level of confidence?** (`question4_answer`, `text`, required: `true`): Describe your confidence level.
* **Section 3** (`section3`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): 
	+ **Do you feel like you need additional training?** (`question5`, `select_one`, required: `true`): Select one of the options.
	+ **What type of training do you need?** (`question5_answer`, `text`, required: `true`): Specify the type of training you need.
* **Would you like to receive more information about upcoming training events?** (`question6`, `select_one`, required: `true`): Select one of the options.
	+ **Do you need help with any other specific topic?** (`question6_answer`, `text`, required: `true`): Specify the topic.
* **Do you have any other comments or suggestions?** (`question7`, `text`, required: `true`): Please provide your feedback.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required: `false`): Please add your name and date.
