# pre-assessment-questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals about their background, English language proficiency, and preferences for language assessment. It will help assess their current language skills and prepare them for potential language assessments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide a brief description of your background and experience in English.
2. Select your level of English language competency from the options provided.
3. Rate your current English reading comprehension level.
4. Rate your current English writing comprehension level.
5. Rate your current English listening comprehension level.
6. Rate your current English speaking comprehension level.
7. If you have completed a level of education higher than high school, provide it.
8. Choose the type of English language assessment you would like to undergo (listening, speaking, reading, or writing).
9. If available, provide the date and time of the language assessment.
10. Indicate the center or department where the language assessment will be held.
11. Specify who will administer the language assessment (a teacher, computer program, or other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Please provide a brief description of your background and experience** (`title`): This field is for you to briefly describe your background and experience in English. Please provide a concise and honest answer.
* **What is your level of English language competency?** (`language_competency`): Choose one of the options provided to indicate your level of English language proficiency.
	+ Basic
	+ Elementary
	+ Pre-Intermediate
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
	+ Proficient
* **How would you rate your current English reading comprehension level?** (`english_reading_comprehension`): Select all the levels that apply to your current reading comprehension level.
	+ Very Basic
	+ Basic
	+ Elementary
	+ Proficient
* **How would you rate your current English writing comprehension level?** (`english_writing_comprehension`): Select all the levels that apply to your current writing comprehension level.
	+ Very Basic
	+ Basic
	+ Elementary
	+ Proficient
* **How would you rate your current English listening comprehension level?** (`english_listening_comprehension`): Select all the levels that apply to your current listening comprehension level.
	+ Very Basic
	+ Basic
	+ Elementary
	+ Proficient
* **How would you rate your current English speaking comprehension level?** (`english_speaking_comprehension`): Select all the levels that apply to your current speaking comprehension level.
	+ Very Basic
	+ Basic
	+ Elementary
	+ Proficient
* **What is your highest level of education completed?** (`education_and_training`): If you have completed a level of education higher than high school, provide it.
* **What kind of English language assessment would you like to undergo?** (`english_language_assessment`): Choose all the options that apply to the type of English language assessment you would like to undergo.
	+ Listening
	+ Speaking
	+ Reading
	+ Writing
* **If the date for the language assessment is available, please provide the date and time of the assessment.** (`language_assessment_date`): If the date and time for the language assessment is available, provide it.
* **Please indicate the center or department where the language assessment will be held.** (`language_assessment_center`): If the center or department where the language assessment will be held is known, provide it.
* **Please indicate who will administer the language assessment (e.g. a teacher or a computer program).** (`language_assessment_facilitator`): Choose who will administer the language assessment.
	+ Teacher or Instructor
	+ Computer Program
	+ Other
