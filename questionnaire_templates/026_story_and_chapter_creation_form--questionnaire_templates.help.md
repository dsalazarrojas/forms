# Story And Chapter Creation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Story And Chapter Creation Form is designed to collect information about a story, its author, genre, characters, and submission status. This form is intended to be completed by users who want to submit a new story or chapter for evaluation or review.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as prompted, and optionally select from the provided options where applicable.
2. For the "Chapter Content" field, enter the text of the chapter in the designated space.
3. Review your entries before submitting the form to ensure that all required fields are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Story Title** (`story_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of your story, briefly describing its theme or subject.
* **Story Author** (`story_author`, text, optional): Enter your name or pseudonym, or that of the story's author.
* **Story Genre** (`story_genre`, select_one, optional): Select the genre of your story (e.g., fantasy, sci-fi, romance, etc.).
* **Story Character** (`story_character`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the main character(s) or protagonist(s) in your story.
* **Chapter Number** (`chapter_number`, number, optional): Enter the chapter number, which helps in navigating the story's structure.
* **Chapter Content** (`chapter_content`, text, optional): Enter the text of the chapter, providing the narrative and details of your story.
* **Story Tags** (`story_tags`, select_multiple, optional): Select any relevant tags for your story, such as genre-specific or thematic keywords.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current submission status of your story, such as "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the story was submitted for evaluation or review.
* **Submission Date** (no additional explanation needed as it's a date field)

Note: The optional fields are not required to be filled in, but providing accurate and complete information will enhance the evaluation and review process.
