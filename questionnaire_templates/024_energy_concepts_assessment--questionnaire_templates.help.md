# Energy Concepts Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Energy Concepts Assessment is designed to evaluate your understanding of energy-related concepts. Please answer the questions and provide any additional comments or energy-related answers you have. This form will help us better understand your energy knowledge and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each page carefully.
2. For each page, fill out the form by providing the requested information.
3. Make sure to answer all questions and provide any additional comments.
4. When asked to select multiple options, choose all that apply.
5. When asked to select one option, choose the most accurate answer.
6. Enter your email and phone number for contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Intro** (1)
* This page is a note section, so you can type any introductory information about yourself and the energy concept.

### **Energy Questions** (2)
* This page asks about energy questions. Answer all the questions as accurately as you can, considering your knowledge about energy concepts.

### **Energy Knowledge** (3)
* This field is a text area for you to provide more information about what you know about energy concepts.

### **Energy Answers** (4)
* Select all that apply from the options provided. This will help us understand your energy-related answers and identify areas for improvement.

### **Energy Answers Labels** (5)
* This field is for you to provide a label for your energy answers. This will help us understand your energy-related answers and identify areas for improvement.

### **Energy Concepts Questions** (6)
* Select the most accurate answer from the options provided. This will help us understand your energy-related questions and identify areas for improvement.

### **Comments** (7)
* Provide any additional comments or thoughts you have about energy concepts.

### **Email** (8)
* Enter your email address so we can contact you for further information.

### **Phone** (9)
* Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further information.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions and provide any additional comments or energy-related answers you have.
* Make sure to answer all questions accurately and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
* This form is for evaluation purposes only, and your responses will be kept confidential.
