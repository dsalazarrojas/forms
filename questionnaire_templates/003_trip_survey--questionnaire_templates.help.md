# trip_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The trip_survey form is designed to collect information about a user's trips, including travel dates, locations, modes of transportation, reasons for travel, travel destinations, costs, and employee information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select "User Input Form"**: Choose this form as the starting point for your trip survey.
2. **Fill out the travel dates**: Select one of the three options for travel dates from the dropdown menu.
3. **Choose a travel location**: Select one of the three options for travel locations from the dropdown menu.
4. **Select a travel mode**: Choose one of the three options for travel modes from the dropdown menu.
5. **Choose a travel reason**: Select one of the three options for travel reasons from the dropdown menu.
6. **Enter travel destination**: Provide the name of the travel destination in the text field.
7. **Enter travel costs**: Enter the cost of the trip in the number field.
8. **Select employee information**: Choose one of the three options for employee information from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input Form (1)**: This is the form that allows you to input information about your trip. Please fill out the other fields in this section.
* **Travel Dates (2)**: Select one of the three options for travel dates. The options are:
	+ 01/01/2023
	+ 01/02/2023
	+ 01/03/2023
* **Travel Locations (3)**: Choose one of the three options for travel locations. The options are:
	+ New York
	+ Chicago
	+ San Francisco
* **Travel Mode (4)**: Select one of the three options for travel modes. The options are:
	+ car
	+ train
	+ plane
* **Travel Reasons (5)**: Select one of the three options for travel reasons. The options are:
	+ personal
	+ business
	+ other
* **Travel Destination (6)**: Enter the name of the travel destination in this text field.
* **Travel Costs (7)**: Enter the cost of the trip in this number field.
* **Employee Information (8)**: Choose one of the three options for employee information. The options are:
	+ John
	+ Jane
	+ Joe

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out all required fields to submit the form correctly. If you need help or have questions, contact your supervisor or administrator.
