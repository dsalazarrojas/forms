# fitness_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from you to better understand your fitness goals, habits, and any potential health concerns. This will help your fitness coach or advisor understand your needs and create a tailored plan for you.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please fill out the following information, following the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Please enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Age**: Enter your age in years, as accurately as possible.
* **Height**: Enter your height in centimeters or feet/inches (optional, but helpful for calculations).
* **Weight**: Enter your weight in kilograms or pounds (optional, but helpful for calculations).
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, including the area code and country code (if applicable).
* **Exercise Habits**: Describe your regular exercise habits, including frequency and type (e.g., running, swimming, or weightlifting).
* **Nutrition**: Briefly describe your typical diet or nutrition plan.
* **Exercise Frequency**: Describe how often you exercise per week.
* **Health Issues**: If you have any known health concerns, list them here.
* **Exercise Injuries**: If you've experienced any injuries related to exercise, list them here.
* **Medications**: If you take any prescription or over-the-counter medications, list them here.
* **Medical Conditions**: If you have any known medical conditions, list them here.
* **Medical Procedures**: If you've undergone any medical procedures, list them here.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of a person we can contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Exercise Professional**: Choose the type of exercise professional who will be working with you (e.g., exercise scientist, personal trainer, or doctor).
* **Doctor (if applicable)**: If you have an existing doctor, enter their name.
* **Doctor Phone (if applicable)**: If you have an existing doctor, enter their phone number.
* **Doctor Email (if applicable)**: If you have an existing doctor, enter their email address.
* **Doctor Specialty (if applicable)**: If you have an existing doctor, list their specialty.
* **Years Practice (if applicable)**: If you have an existing doctor, list their years of practice.
* **Doctor License (if applicable)**: If you have an existing doctor, list their license number or type.
* **Doctor Clinic (if applicable)**: If you have an existing doctor, list their clinic or practice name.
