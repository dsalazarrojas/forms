# Healthcare Patient Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's medical history and relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2. Answer each question accurately and thoroughly.
3. Be honest and provide as much detail as possible.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical history** (`medical_history`, text, optional): This field is for providing a brief summary of the patient's medical history, including any past illnesses, injuries, or medical procedures they have experienced.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): Please list any allergies or sensitivities the patient has, including any reactions or symptoms they have experienced in the past.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): List all the medications the patient is currently taking, including dosage and frequency.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Describe any chronic or acute medical conditions the patient has, including any current or past conditions.
* **Surgical Procedures** (`surgical_procedures`, text, optional): List any past or upcoming surgical procedures the patient has undergone or is scheduled to undergo.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, text, optional): Provide a brief summary of the patient's family medical history, including any inherited conditions or genetic disorders.
* **Social History** (`social_history`, text, optional): Describe the patient's social situation, including occupation, education, and any relevant social or lifestyle factors that may impact their health.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person who can be reached in case of a medical emergency.
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, text, optional): Name and contact information of the patient's primary medical provider.
* **Medical Tests** (`medical_tests`, text, optional): List any recent or upcoming medical tests the patient has undergone or is scheduled to undergo.

## Tips

* Please answer each question carefully and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask a healthcare professional for guidance.
* This form is confidential and should only be completed with the patient's informed consent.
