# Client Session Preference Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect valuable information from clients to ensure a productive and effective working relationship. By providing your preferences, you'll help us tailor our sessions to meet your needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly. This will help us get to know you better and provide the best possible experience. You can complete this form in a few minutes, and it's essential to do so before your first session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (Client Name)** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Your full name as we'll address you in our sessions.
* **Email Address (Client Email)** (`client_email`, `email`, required): Your email address where we'll communicate with you.
* **Phone Number (Client Phone)** (`client_phone`, `text`, required): Your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Preferred Contact Method (Preferred contact method)** (`preferred_contact`, `select_one`, required): How do you prefer to be contacted? (Email, Phone Call, Text Message, In-Person)
* **Primary Goal (What is your primary goal for working together?)** (`primary_goal`, `text`, required): Be specific about what you hope to achieve in our sessions.
* **Specific Topics or Issues (Specific topics or issues you want to address)** (`specific_topics`, `text`, required): List the specific topics or issues you want to address during our sessions.
* **Previous Experience (Have you worked with a coach/consultant/provider like this before?)** (`previous_experience`, `select_one`, required): Have you worked with a coach or consultant before?
* **Session Frequency (Preferred session frequency)** (`session_frequency`, `select_one`, required): How often would you like to have sessions? (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, As needed)
* **Session Duration (Preferred session duration)** (`session_duration`, `select_one`, required): How long would you prefer our sessions to be? (30 minutes, 45 minutes, 60 minutes, 90 minutes)
* **Preferred Day (Preferred day of week for sessions)** (`preferred_day`, `select_one`, required): Which day of the week would you prefer for our sessions? (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday)
* **Preferred Time (Preferred time of day for sessions)** (`preferred_time`, `select_one`, required): What time of day would you prefer for our sessions? (Early Morning, Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night, Flexible)
* **Timezone (Your timezone)** (`timezone`, `text`, required): If you're in a different timezone, please specify so we can adjust our schedule accordingly.
* **Session Format (Preferred session format)** (`session_format`, `select_one`, required): How would you prefer our sessions to be? (In-Person, Video Call, Phone Call, No Preference)
* **Location or Platform (If in-person, preferred location. If remote, platform preference?)** (`location_or_platform`, `text`, required): If you prefer in-person sessions, please specify your location. If remote, which platform do you prefer (Zoom, Skype, Teams, etc.)?
* **Accessibility Needs (Do you have any accessibility needs?)** (`accessibility_needs`, `text`, required): If you have any physical or other accessibility needs, please let us know so we can accommodate you.
* **Communication Style (How do you prefer to be communicated with?)** (`communication_style`, `select_one`, required): How do you prefer to be communicated with? (Direct and Structured, Conversational and Flexible, Mix of Both)
* **Feedback Preference (How do you prefer to receive feedback?)** (`feedback_preference`, `select_one`, required): How do you prefer to receive feedback? (Direct and Honest, Gentle and Supportive, Data-Driven, Other)
* **Learning Style (Your preferred learning style)** (`learning_style`, `select_one`, required): What learning style do you prefer? (Visual, Auditory, Kinesthetic, Reading-Writing, Mixed)
* **Preparation (Are you willing to prepare materials or do homework between sessions?)** (`preparation`, `select_one`, required): Are you willing to prepare materials or do homework between sessions? (Yes, No, Only if Brief)
* **Additional Preferences or Information (Any other preferences or important information?)** (`additional_preferences`, `text`, required): Any other preferences or information you'd like to share with us?
