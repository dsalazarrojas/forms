# Dave's Personal Training Consultation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This questionnaire is designed to gather information about you and your preferences for a personal training consultation. Please answer the questions honestly to ensure that we can tailor a training program that suits your needs and goals.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Please note that some fields are required, indicated by an asterisk (\*), while others are optional.
3. If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): This is your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): This is your last name.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your email address, which will be used for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (4, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Phone** (5, text, required): Enter your phone number, which will be used for emergency contact.
* **Marital Status** (6, select_one, required): Please select your current marital status.
* **Martial Arts Interest** (7, select_multiple, required): Select the martial arts you are interested in. (Karate, Judo, Kickboxing, Aikido)
* **Training Experience** (8, select_one, required): Please select your current training experience level.
* **Goals** (9, text, optional): Enter your training goals.
* **Emergency Contact** (10, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions** (11, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions that may affect your training, please list them here.
* **Fitness Level** (12, select_one, required): Please select your current fitness level.
* **Nutrition** (13, select_multiple, required): Select your dietary preferences.
* **Goals Frequency** (14, select_multiple, required): Select how often you aim to train.
* **Training Schedule** (15, select_multiple, required): Select your preferred training duration.
* **Martial Arts** (16, select_one, optional): If you are interested in a specific martial art, select it. (Kickboxing, Judo, Karate, Aikido)
* **Additional Notes** (17, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, please enter them here.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (18, select_multiple, optional): If you have a relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., brother, sister, mother, etc.).
* **Medical Conditions 2** (19, text, optional): If you have any additional medical conditions, please list them here.
* **Fitness Level 2** (20, select_one, optional): If you have a different fitness level for a specific martial art, select it.
* **Goals Frequency 2** (21, select_multiple, optional): If you have different training frequency goals for a specific martial art, select it.
* **Training Schedule 2** (22, select_multiple, optional): If you have different training schedule preferences for a specific martial art, select it.
* **Martial Arts 2** (23, select_one, optional): If you are interested in a different martial art, select it. (Kickboxing, Judo, Karate, Aikido)
