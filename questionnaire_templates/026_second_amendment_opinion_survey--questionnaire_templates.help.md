# Second Amendment Opinion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather opinions on various aspects related to gun rights, safety concerns, and policies. Your responses will help us understand public sentiment on these topics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully, considering the provided options.
2. Make sure to fill in all required fields, indicated by the "required" label.
3. For fields with multiple options, select one or more that best fit your opinion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `text`, optional): Briefly introduce yourself and provide any relevant context.
* **Gun rights** (`Gun rights`, `number`, optional): Rate your level of agreement with gun rights on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is "Strongly disagree" and 10 is "Strongly agree".
* **Safety concerns** (`Safety concerns`, `number`, optional): Rate your level of concern for gun safety on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is "Not concerned at all" and 10 is "Very concerned".
* **Gun laws** (`Gun laws`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you think current gun laws are effective (True) or ineffective (False) in addressing safety concerns.
* **Gun safety measures** (`Gun safety measures`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more measures you think are essential for improved gun safety, such as Mandatory background checks, Red flag laws, or Gun safety education.
* **Age group** (`Age group`, `select_one`, optional): Select the age group you belong to.
* **Region** (`Region`, `select_one`, optional): Select the region where you reside.
* **Gun ownership** (`Gun ownership`, `number`, optional): Rate your level of gun ownership on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is "None" and 10 is "Many firearms".
* **Safety policies** (`Safety policies`, `text`, optional): Describe any specific safety policies you think are necessary or effective.
* **Gun safety education** (`Gun safety education`, `text`, optional): Suggest or describe any gun safety education programs or resources you think are valuable.
* **Opinion** (`Opinion`, `time`, optional): Express your personal opinion or thought on gun safety.
* **Final comments** (`Final comments`, `note`, optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments you have on gun safety.

Please fill in the form carefully, and thank you for participating!
