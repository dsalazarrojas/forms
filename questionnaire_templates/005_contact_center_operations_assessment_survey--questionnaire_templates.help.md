# Contact Center Operations Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your experience and opinions on the performance of the contact center's operations. It will help us identify areas of strength and areas that require improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your name and date of this assessment in the corresponding fields.
2. Rate your satisfaction with the average handle time (how quickly issues are resolved).
3. Evaluate the quality of training provided to agents.
4. Assess the effectiveness of the current communication tools used by agents.
5. List any common operational issues or obstacles you've encountered.
6. Indicate whether the current staffing levels meet the demands of the center.
7. Select up to three priorities for improvement from the provided options.
8. Suggest any recommended next steps to address the assessment findings.
9. Provide a follow-up email address for receiving the assessment report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Assessor name**: Please enter your name for reference and record-keeping purposes.
* **Assessment date**: The date you are completing this assessment.
* **Center location**: The city or site name of the contact center you are assessing.
* **Satisfaction with average handle time**: Rate your satisfaction on a scale from Very satisfied to Very dissatisfied.
* **Training quality for agents**: Evaluate the quality of training provided to agents, using the scale from Excellent to Poor.
* **Communication tools effectiveness**: Rate how effective the current communication tools are, using the scale from Very effective to Not effective.
* **Common operational issues**: List any recurring problems or hurdles you've encountered in your role or interactions with the center.
* **Staffing adequacy**: Indicate whether the current staffing levels meet the demands of the center, using the scale from Always to Rarely.
* **Top improvement priorities**: Select up to three areas that the center needs to improve, from the options provided.
* **Recommended next steps**: Suggest any actions that the center should take to address the assessment findings.
* **Follow up contact email**: Enter an email address for receiving the assessment report.

Please note that this form is for internal use and feedback only. The information gathered will be used to identify areas of improvement for the contact center. All responses are kept confidential and only accessed by authorized personnel.
