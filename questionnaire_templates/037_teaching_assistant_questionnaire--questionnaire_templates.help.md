# teaching_assistant_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from users about their experiences with teaching assistants. The goal is to understand the user's preferences and expectations regarding the role and interaction with teaching assistants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Select the options that best match your preferences for each question.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Comments on the teaching assistant" field to provide more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Role of teaching assistant** (`role_of_teaching_assistant`, select_one, optional): This question asks you to choose the role of the teaching assistant that best describes your experience. Select the option that best fits your situation.
* **How often would you like to receive updates from teaching assistants?** (`teaching_assistant_questionnaire_response`, select_multiple, optional): This question asks you to select how often you would like to receive updates from the teaching assistant. Choose one or more options that fit your preferences.
* **How would you like to contact the teaching assistant?** (`teaching_assistant_questionnaire_response_2`, select_multiple, optional): This question asks you to select how you would like to contact the teaching assistant. Choose one or more options that fit your preferences.
* **Description of the teaching assistant's role** (`teaching_assistant_questionnaire_description`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of the role of the teaching assistant in your experience.
* **Are you satisfied with the teaching assistant?** (`teaching_assistant_questionnaire_response_3`, select_multiple, optional): This question asks you to indicate your level of satisfaction with the teaching assistant. Choose one or more options that fit your feelings.
* **Comments on the teaching assistant** (`teaching_assistant_questionnaire_response_4`, text, optional): This is an optional field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the teaching assistant.

## Tips
* Please answer this form honestly and thoroughly to provide the most accurate feedback.
