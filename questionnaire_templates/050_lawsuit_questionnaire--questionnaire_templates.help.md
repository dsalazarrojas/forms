# Lawsuit Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lawsuit Questionnaire is a tool used to collect information about a lawsuit, including plaintiff and defendant details, case status, and medical information.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Read through each page of the form carefully, ensuring you have all necessary information before proceeding.
*   Complete all required fields (identified as "required" in the form metadata).
*   Fill in the relevant fields with accurate and complete information.
*   Ensure that you have permission to fill out fields related to sensitive or confidential information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the plaintiff's first name.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the plaintiff's last name.
*   **Plaintiff Email** (`plaintiff_email`, text, optional): Enter the plaintiff's email address.
*   **Defendant Name** (`defendant_name`, text, optional): Enter the defendant's name.
*   **Defendant Email** (`defendant_email`, text, optional): Enter the defendant's email address.
*   **Date of Injury** (`date_of_injury`, date, optional): Enter the date the injury occurred.
*   **Date of Filing** (`date_of_filing`, date, optional): Enter the date the lawsuit was filed.
*   **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the injury occurred.
*   **Plaintiff Phone** (`plaintiff_phone`, text, optional): Enter the plaintiff's phone number.
*   **Defendant Phone** (`defendant_phone`, text, optional): Enter the defendant's phone number.
*   **Case Type** (`case_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of case (Civil or Criminal).
*   **Case Status** (`case_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the current status of the case (Open, Closed, or Pending).
*   **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether or not the case involves an insurance provider.
*   **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of the medical provider involved.
*   **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the doctor involved.
*   **Doctor Title** (`doctor_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the doctor involved.
*   **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`, text, optional): Enter the doctor's phone number.
*   **Doctor Email** (`doctor_email`, text, optional): Enter the doctor's email address.
*   **Doctor Address** (`doctor_address`, text, optional): Enter the doctor's address.
*   **Doctor Specialty** (`doctor_specialty`, select_multiple, optional): Select the doctor's specialty (General Practice, Specialist, or Other).
*   **Medical License** (`medical_license`, text, optional): Enter the doctor's medical license number.
*   **Medical License Number** (`medical_license_number`, text, optional): Enter the doctor's license number.
*   **Medical Facility** (`medical_facility`, text, optional): Enter the name of the medical facility involved.
*   **Medical Date** (`medical_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the medical treatment.
*   **Medical Time** (`medical_time`, text, optional): Enter the time of the medical treatment.
