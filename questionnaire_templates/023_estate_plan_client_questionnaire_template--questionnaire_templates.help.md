# Estate Plan Client Questionnaire Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This questionnaire template is designed to collect information about the client's personal and financial details to facilitate the estate planning process. Please fill out this form to provide a comprehensive picture of your client's estate, including their income, assets, liabilities, and other relevant information. This template is intended for use by estate planning professionals and should not be completed by clients themselves.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about your client. For text fields, enter a brief description or answer. For numerical fields, enter a number. For select multiple fields, choose one or more options from the list. You can skip fields that are not relevant or required, but please note that incomplete or missing information may lead to inaccuracies in the estate plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's name as they wish to be referred to in the estate plan.
* **Spouse Name** (`spouse_name`, text, required): Enter the spouse's name, if applicable.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the client's date of birth in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the client's address, if applicable.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required): Enter the client's contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Other Dependents** (`other_dependents`, select multiple, optional): Check all applicable boxes for other dependents, such as "John", "Jane", or "Joe".
* **Client Employees** (`client_employees`, select multiple, required): Check all applicable boxes for client employees, such as "John", "Jane", or "Joe".
* **Client Children** (`client_children`, number, required): Enter the number of children.
* **Spouse Children** (`spouse_children`, number, optional): Enter the number of spouse's children, if applicable.
* **Other Dependent Income** (`other_dependent_income`, number, optional): Enter the income of other dependents, if applicable.
* **Other Dependent Assets** (`other_dependent_assets`, number, optional): Enter the assets of other dependents, if applicable.
* **Other Dependent Liabilities** (`other_dependent_liabilities`, number, optional): Enter the liabilities of other dependents, if applicable.
* **Client's Net Worth** (`client_net_worth`, number, optional): Enter the client's net worth, if applicable.
* **Spouse's Net Worth** (`spouse_net_worth`, number, optional): Enter the spouse's net worth, if applicable.
* **Estate Administration** (`estate_administration`, text, optional): Enter any special estate administration instructions or notes.
* **Estate Administrator** (`estate_administrator`, text, optional): Enter the name of the estate administrator, if applicable.
* **Estate Distribution** (`estate_distribution`, text, optional): Enter any special estate distribution instructions or notes.
* **Estate Taxation** (`estate_taxation`, text, optional): Enter any special estate taxation instructions or notes.
* **Client Fees** (`client_fees`, text, optional): Enter any fees associated with the client's estate plan.
* **Client Documents** (`client_documents`, text, optional): Upload any relevant client documents.
* **Estate Executors** (`estate_executors`, text, optional): Enter the names of any estate executors, if applicable.
* **Estate Domicile** (`estate_domicile`, text, optional): Enter the location where the estate is to be administered.
* **Estate Domicile City** (`estate_domicile_city`, text, optional): Enter the city where the estate is to be administered.
* **Estate Domicile State** (`estate_domicile_state`, text, optional): Enter the state where the estate is to be administered.
* **Estate Domicile Zip** (`estate_domicile_zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code where the estate is to be administered.
