# Camp Ronald McDonald Reference Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect references from individuals who have had personal or professional interactions with an applicant, typically for a job or volunteer opportunity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select the type of relationship you have with the applicant (e.g., colleague, supervisor, mentor).
2. Enter the applicant's name.
3. Evaluate the applicant's personal traits, such as compassion, patience, and reliability, using the provided scale.
4. Answer questions about your experience working with individuals with special needs and your overall opinion of the applicant's suitability for working in a pediatric health environment.
5. Provide your overall recommendation and any additional comments or concerns you have about the applicant.
6. Confirm that we may contact you for further information and provide your electronic signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Relationship to Applicant
* **Name of Applicant**: Please enter the full name of the applicant as you know them.
### 2. Name of Applicant
* **Name of Applicant**: Enter the full name of the applicant as you know them.
### 3. Your Full Name (Reference)
* **Your Full Name (Reference)**: Enter your full name as the person providing the reference.
### 4. Relationship to Applicant
* **Relationship to Applicant**: Select the type of relationship you have with the applicant (e.g., colleague, supervisor, mentor).
### 5. How long have you known the applicant?
* **How long have you known the applicant?**: Describe how long you have known the applicant, including any relevant dates or timeframes.
### 6. Personal Traits and Skills
* **Compassion and Empathy**: Evaluate the applicant's level of compassion and empathy, using the provided scale (Exceptional, Strong, Average, Weak).
* **Patience and Flexibility**: Evaluate the applicant's level of patience and flexibility, using the provided scale (Exceptional, Strong, Average, Weak).
* **Reliability and Maturity**: Evaluate the applicant's level of reliability and maturity, using the provided scale (Exceptional, Strong, Average, Weak).
* **Experience with Individuals with Special Needs**: Answer whether you believe the applicant is comfortable and capable in this environment, using the provided scale (Highly Capable, Capable, Minimal Experience, No Experience).
* **Teamwork and Collaboration Skills**: Evaluate the applicant's teamwork and collaboration skills, using the provided scale (Excellent Team Player, Good, Prefer Individual Work).
* **Suitability for Working in a Pediatric Health Environment**: Provide any concerns or thoughts you have regarding the applicant's suitability for working in a pediatric health environment.
### 7. Overall Recommendation
* **Overall Recommendation**: Select your overall recommendation for the applicant, using the provided scale (Highly Recommend, Recommend, Recommend with Reservations, Do Not Recommend).
### 8. Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any other information you think would be helpful in evaluating the applicant.
### 9. May we contact you for more information?
* **May we contact you for more information?**: Confirm that you are willing to be contacted for further information.
### 10. Electronic Signature (Typed Name)
* **Electronic Signature (Typed Name)**: Enter your typed name for the purpose of this reference.
### 11. Date of Completion
* **Date of Completion**: Enter the current date, as this is the completion date of your reference.

**Tips:**

* Please answer honestly and as specifically as possible.
* Be sure to provide any relevant dates or timeframes when describing how long you have known the applicant.
* Use the provided scales to evaluate the applicant's personal traits, as this will help in the evaluation process.
* Remember to include any additional comments or concerns you have about the applicant.
* Ensure your electronic signature is accurate and up to date.
