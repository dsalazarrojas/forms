# ABA Therapy Eligibility Screening - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ABA Therapy Eligibility Screening form is designed to gather essential information about a child and their family to determine eligibility for ABA therapy.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Refer to the help text for each question if you are unsure or need clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Child's Full Name**: Enter the child's full name, as it appears on their official documents.
* **Child's Date of Birth**: Enter the child's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Child's Gender**: Select the child's gender from the provided options (Male, Female, Other/Prefer not to say).
* **Parent/Guardian Name**: Enter the parent or guardian's name.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter the contact phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Contact Email Address**: Enter the contact email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Has the child received a formal diagnosis of Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)?**: Select 'True' if the child has been formally diagnosed with ASD, 'False' if not, or 'Pending Evaluation' if an evaluation is pending.
* **Diagnosing Provider/Clinic**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider or clinic that diagnosed the child.
* **Date of Diagnosis**: Enter the date the child was diagnosed with ASD (format: MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Primary Areas of Concern**: Select all applicable areas of concern for the child, such as Communication/Speech, Social Skills, Daily Living Skills, etc.
* **Additional Comments or Specific Behaviors**: Enter any additional comments or specific behaviors you would like to address through ABA therapy.
* **Preferred Therapy Location**: Select the preferred therapy location (In-Home, Clinic-Based, School-Based, Telehealth).
* **Preferred Time of Day**: Select all applicable time slots for therapy (Mornings, Afternoons, Late Afternoons).
* **Primary Insurance Carrier**: Enter the primary insurance carrier for the child.
* **Insurance Member ID**: Enter the insurance member ID for the child.
* **How did you hear about us?**: Select how you learned about our services (Pediatrician Referral, School District, Insurance Provider List, Online Search/Social Media, Friend/Family).
