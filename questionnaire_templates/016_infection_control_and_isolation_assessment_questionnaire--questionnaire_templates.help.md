# Infection Control And Isolation Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Infection Control And Isolation Assessment Questionnaire is a form used to assess a patient's risk of infection and isolation. It helps healthcare providers identify potential risks and take necessary measures to prevent the spread of infection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Describe the patient's symptoms in the "Symptoms" field.
3. Select the relevant exposure risk from the "Exposure Risk" dropdown.
4. Provide any additional exposure risk details in the "Exposure Risk Other" field.
5. Enter the contact trace location in the "Contact Trace Location" field.
6. Enter the duration of contact trace in the "Contact Trace Duration" field.
7. Select the relevant contact trace method from the "Contact Trace Method" dropdown.
8. Enter the symptoms date and duration in the "Symptoms Date" and "Symptoms Duration" fields.
9. Enter the isolation location in the "Isolation Location" field.
10. Enter the start and end dates of isolation in the "Isolation Start Date" and "Isolation End Date" fields.
11. Enter the isolation dose in the "Isolation Dose" field.
12. Provide any additional exposure risk details in the "Exposure Risk Other" field.
13. Enter any other contact trace location in the "Contact Trace Location Other" field.
14. Enter any other contact trace duration in the "Contact Trace Duration Other" field.
15. Select the relevant contact trace status from the "Contact Trace Status" dropdown.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (2)** (`text`, required): Enter the patient's name.
* **Healthcare Provider (3)** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the healthcare provider who assessed the patient.
* **Date Of Service (4)** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of service.
* **Symptoms (5)** (`text`, optional): Describe the patient's symptoms.
* **Exposure Risk (6)** (`select_multiple`, required): Select the relevant exposure risk (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4).
* **Exposure Risk Other (7)** (`text`, optional): Provide any additional exposure risk details.
* **Contact Trace Location (8)** (`text`, optional): Enter the contact trace location.
* **Contact Trace Duration (9)** (`text`, optional): Enter the duration of contact trace.
* **Contact Trace Method (10)** (`text`, optional): Select the relevant contact trace method (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Symptoms Date (11)** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of symptoms.
* **Symptoms Duration (12)** (`number`, optional): Enter the duration of symptoms.
* **Isolation Location (13)** (`text`, optional): Enter the isolation location.
* **Isolation Start Date (14)** (`date`, optional): Enter the start date of isolation.
* **Isolation End Date (15)** (`date`, optional): Enter the end date of isolation.
* **Isolation Dose (16)** (`text`, optional): Enter the isolation dose.
* **Exposure Risk Other (17)** (`text`, optional): Provide any additional exposure risk details.
* **Contact Trace Location Other (18)** (`text`, optional): Enter any other contact trace location.
* **Contact Trace Duration Other (19)** (`text`, optional): Enter any other contact trace duration.
* **Contact Trace Method Other (20)** (`text`, optional): Select the relevant contact trace method (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Patient Transfer (21)** (`text`, optional): Enter any patient transfer information.
* **Contact Trace Transfer (22)** (`text`, optional): Enter any contact trace transfer information.
* **Symptoms Other (23)** (`text`, optional): Describe any other symptoms.
* **Symptoms Other Details (24)** (`text`, optional): Provide any other symptoms details.
* **Contact Trace Status (25)** (`select_one`, required): Select the relevant contact trace status (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
