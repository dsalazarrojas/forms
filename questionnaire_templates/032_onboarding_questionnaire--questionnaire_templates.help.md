# Onboarding Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Onboarding Questionnaire is a tool used to gather essential information about a new employee's contact details, job specifics, and onboarding status. This guide is designed to help you complete the form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please ensure you have all the required information before starting the form.**
2. **Answer all questions truthfully and accurately.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **Not required**): Enter the new employee's first name as it is spelled.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **Not required**): Enter the new employee's last name as it is spelled.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **Not required**): Enter the new employee's work email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **Not required**): Enter the new employee's work phone number.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, **Not required**): Enter the new employee's job title as per the company's records.
* **Department** (`department`, text, **Not required**): Enter the new employee's department as per the company's records.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, **Not required**): Enter the new employee's manager's name as per the company's records.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, **Not required**): Enter the new employee's manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, **Not required**): Enter the new employee's manager's phone number.
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, **Not required**): Enter the date the new employee started their job.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, **Not required**): Enter the name of the person who should be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, **Not required**): Describe the relationship between the new employee and the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, **Not required**): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, **Not required**): Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Job Title** (`emergency_contact_job_title`, text, **Not required**): Enter the job title of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Department** (`emergency_contact_department`, text, **Not required**): Enter the department of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Manager Name** (`emergency_contact_manager_name`, text, **Not required**): Enter the manager's name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Manager Title** (`emergency_contact_manager_title`, text, **Not required**): Enter the title of the emergency contact manager.
* **Emergency Contact Start Date** (`emergency_contact_start_date`, date, **Not required**): Enter the start date of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact End Date** (`emergency_contact_end_date`, date, **Not required**): Enter the end date of the emergency contact (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, **Not required**): Describe the relationship between the new employee and the emergency contact.
* **Onboarding Start Date** (`onboarding_start_date`, date, **Not required**): Enter the date the new employee started the onboarding process.
* **Onboarding End Date** (`onboarding_end_date`, date, **Not required**): Enter the expected date the new employee will complete the onboarding process.
* **Onboarding Status** (`onboarding_status`, select_one, **Not required**): Select the onboarding status of the new employee. Options include:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, **Required**): Enter any relevant notes or comments about the new employee's onboarding process.
