# Geometry Skills Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Geometry Skills Survey is designed to assess an individual's proficiency level in various aspects of geometry. It is used to gather information about the respondent's understanding of geometric concepts and identify areas that require improvement in education or training settings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have not already completed this survey.
2. Select the relevant options for each field according to your understanding.
3. Provide any additional comments or notes in the "Comments" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, if you want to be contacted for follow-up or additional information.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, if you have any specific requirements or needs.
6. Answer "geometry_level" field with your current level of understanding of geometry, on a scale of 1-10.
7. Select the topic challenges you currently face or would like to face in "topic_challenges" field.
8. Choose the training needs you require in "training_needs" field.
9. Finally, provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **geometry_level (number)**: Enter your current level of understanding of geometry, on a scale of 1-10.
* **topic_challenges (Select multiple)**: Choose the topic challenges you currently face or would like to face, such as:
	+ Option 1
	+ Measurement
	+ Graphs
	+ Transformations
	+ Angles
	+ Shapes
* **training_needs (Select one)**: Choose the training needs you require, such as:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
	+ Option 5
* **Comments (Note)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you have.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address, if you want to be contacted for follow-up or additional information.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number, if you have any specific requirements or needs.
* **geometry_level_2 (number)**: This field is currently not in use.
