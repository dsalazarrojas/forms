# Science Advisory Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Science Advisory Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from participants who have attended a science advisory session. This form will help us understand how you experienced the session and what we can do to improve future sessions. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

* Answer all questions in this form, providing as much detail as possible.
* For select questions, such as "Which session did you attend?", please choose the option that best describes your experience.
* For open-ended questions, such as "Additional comments", please provide as much context as you can.
* Ensure that you have answered all required questions (marked as "required" in the form).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What was your experience at the science advisory session** (`science_advisory_feedback_form`): This field asks you to describe your experience at the science advisory session. Please provide a brief summary of what you found valuable or challenging about the session.
* **Which session did you attend** (`science_advisory_session`): This question asks you to select which science advisory session you attended. Please choose the session from the options provided.
* **How would you rate your experience** (`science_advisory_rating`): This question asks you to rate your overall experience. Please rate from 1-5, with 1 being very poor and 5 being excellent.
* **Additional comments** (`science_advisory_comments`): This field is for any additional thoughts or comments you may have about the session. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Email** (`email`): This field asks for your email address. Please enter a valid email address so that we can contact you if needed.
* **How did you hear about us** (`participant_contact`): This question asks you to select how you heard about the science advisory session. Please choose all applicable options.
* **Which session could improve the most** (`science_advisory_session_improve`): This question asks you to select which aspect of the session could be improved. Please choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **How would you rate our rating system** (`science_advisory_rating_scale`): This question asks you to rate our rating system. Please rate from 1-5, with 1 being very poor and 5 being excellent.
* **Do you have any additional feedback** (`science_advisory_additional_feedback`): This field is for any additional thoughts or comments you may have about the session. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **How satisfied are you with the session** (`science_advisory_session_satisfaction`): This question asks you to rate how satisfied you are with the session. Please rate from 1-5, with 1 being very dissatisfied and 5 being very satisfied.
* **How relevant was the session** (`science_advisory_session_relevance`): This question asks you to rate how relevant the session was. Please rate from 1-5, with 1 being very irrelevant and 5 being very relevant.
* **How useful was the session** (`science_advisory_session_usefulness`): This question asks you to rate how useful the session was. Please rate from 1-5, with 1 being very useless and 5 being very useful.
