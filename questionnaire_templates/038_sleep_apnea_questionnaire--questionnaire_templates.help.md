Sleep Apnea Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's sleep patterns and medical history to help identify potential sleep apnea conditions. It is intended for medical professionals to gather data and assess a patient's risk factors for sleep apnea.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have provided accurate and complete information.
2. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Enter your responses in the text boxes provided.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **patient_info** (patient-info)
* Label: **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, required: false)
* Explanation: This field is for providing a brief description of the patient's information. Please enter any relevant details about the patient, such as their name, age, or medical ID number.

### 2. **sleep_pattern** (sleep-pattern)
* Label: **Sleep Pattern** (`sleep_pattern`, `text`, required: false)
* Explanation: Describe the patient's typical sleep pattern, including the number of hours slept, time of bedtime and wake-up, and any unusual sleep habits.

### 3. **medical_history** (medical-history)
* Label: **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false)
* Explanation: This field is for detailing the patient's medical history, including any previous diagnoses, illnesses, or surgeries related to sleep or other health conditions.

Tips:

* Be accurate and clear when filling out the form.
* Use standard date and time formats for any dates and times you enter.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact the relevant medical professional.
