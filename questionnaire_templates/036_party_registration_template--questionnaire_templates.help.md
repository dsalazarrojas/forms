# party_registration_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The party registration template is used to collect information from party attendees.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First name** (id: 1) in the text field.
2. Enter your **Last name** (id: 2) in the text field.
3. Provide your **E-mail** (id: 3) address.
4. Enter your **Phone number** (id: 4) in the text field.
5. Choose your **Date of birth** (id: 5) from the date picker.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (id: 1, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (id: 2, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **E-mail** (id: 3, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone number** (id: 4, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of birth** (id: 5, date, optional): Choose your date of birth from the date picker.
