## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read each symptom carefully and consider how it applies to your current experiences or feelings.
* For each symptom, choose one of the three options:
	+ "True" if you are currently experiencing this symptom.
	+ "False" if you are not experiencing this symptom.
	+ "unsure" if you are unsure or don't know how to respond.
