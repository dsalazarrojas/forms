# Market Segmentation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Market Segmentation Questionnaire is a tool to help you identify and select specific market segments for your business. It consists of a series of questions that will guide you through the process of segmenting the market.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of market segmentation you would like to do (page 1, "segment_the_market").
2. Choose the variable based on which you would like to segment the market (page 2, "segment_based_on_variable").
3. Select the market segment you are interested in (page 3, "market_segment").
4. Identify the target audience for your market segment (page 4, "target_audience").
5. Choose your preferred interaction channel (page 5, "preferred_interaction_channels").
6. If other than the listed options, specify your preferred interaction channel (page 6, "preferred_interaction_channel_other").
7. Estimate the size of your market segment (page 7, "market_segment_size").
8. Estimate the percentage of the market your segment represents (page 8, "market_segment_percentage").
9. Provide a brief description of your market segment (page 9, "market_segment_description").
10. Specify the customer segment for your market segment (page 10, "customer_segment").
11. Describe the target customers for your market segment (page 11, "target_customers").
12. Provide a detailed description of the target customers for your market segment (page 12, "target_customers_description").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **segment_the_market** (Page 1, Select multiple, Required: No): This field asks for the type of market segmentation you would like to do. You can select one or more of the options: Business, Customer, Product, Service, or Other (please specify).
* **segment_based_on_variable** (Page 2, Select one, Required: No): This field asks for the variable based on which you would like to segment the market. You can select one of the options: Demographics, Behavioral, Geographic.
* **market_segment** (Page 3, Select multiple, Required: No): This field asks for the market segment you are interested in. You can select one or more of the options: A, B, C.
* **target_audience** (Page 4, Select multiple, Required: No): This field asks for the target audience for your market segment. You can select one or more of the options: Men, Women, Both.
* **preferred_interaction_channels** (Page 5, Select multiple, Required: No): This field asks for your preferred interaction channel. You can select one or more of the options: Email, Phone, In-app, Other (please specify).
* **preferred_interaction_channel_other** (Page 6, Text, Required: No): If you selected "Other (please specify)" in the previous field, please provide a description of your preferred interaction channel.
* **market_segment_size** (Page 7, Number, Required: No): This field asks for the estimated size of your market segment. You can enter a numerical value.
* **market_segment_percentage** (Page 8, Number, Required: No): This field asks for the estimated percentage of the market your segment represents. You can enter a numerical value.
* **market_segment_description** (Page 9, Text, Required: No): This field asks for a brief description of your market segment.
* **customer_segment** (Page 10, Text, Required: No): This field asks for the customer segment for your market segment.
* **target_customers** (Page 11, Text, Required: No): This field asks for the target customers for your market segment.
* **target_customers_description** (Page 12, Text, Required: No): This field asks for a detailed description of the target customers for your market segment.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when selecting your market segment.
- Use the estimated values for the market segment size and percentage to guide your marketing efforts.
- Ensure that your market segment description is clear and concise.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please refer to your company's marketing strategy for guidance.
