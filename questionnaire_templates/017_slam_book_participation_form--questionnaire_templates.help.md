# Slam Book Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the user's participation in an event or activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "name" field.
2. If applicable, enter your grade in the "grade" field.
3. If applicable, enter your school in the "school" field.
4. In the "favorite_food" field, select all the foods that you enjoy eating.
5. In the "favorite_color" field, select your favorite color.
6. Enter your birthday in the "birthday" field.
7. If available, enter the time you are available in the "time_available" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
9. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
10. In the "message" fields, enter any additional information or comments you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **grade** (`grade`, number, required/optional): Enter your current grade level, if applicable.
* **school** (`school`, text, required/optional): Enter your school's name, if applicable.
* **message** (`message`, note, required/optional): Enter a personal message or any comments you have.
* **favorite_food** (`favorite_food`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the foods you enjoy eating.
	+ Options: Pizza, Sushi, Tacos, Burgers, Sandwiches, Fries, Salads, Fruit, Cake
* **favorite_color** (`favorite_color`, select_one, required/optional): Select your favorite color.
	+ Options: Blue, Green, Red, Yellow, Orange, Purple, Pink, Black, White
* **birthday** (`birthday`, date, required/optional): Enter your birthday.
* **time_available** (`time_available`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you are available.
* **email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **message_2** (`message_2`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or information.
