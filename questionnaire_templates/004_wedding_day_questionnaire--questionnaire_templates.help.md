# Wedding Day Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

This questionnaire is used to gather details about the couple's wedding day, including ceremony and reception information, contact details, and other relevant specifics.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question in the space provided.
2.  Make sure to fill out all required fields.
3.  Double-check your answers for accuracy.
