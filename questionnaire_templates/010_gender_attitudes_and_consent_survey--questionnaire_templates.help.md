# Gender Attitudes And Consent Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gender Attitudes And Consent Survey is designed to gather information about your thoughts, feelings, and experiences related to gender and consent. This form is part of a research project aimed at understanding gender-related attitudes and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer them as honestly as possible.
2. Select your responses from the provided options, if applicable.
3. You can skip questions that do not apply to you or your experiences.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gender Roles** (`gender_roles`): This question asks about your self-identification as a man, woman, non-binary, or other gender identities. Please select the option that best describes your gender identity.
* **Consent Levels** (`consent_levels`): This question inquires about your consent levels in different situations. Please select all that apply to you, as consent can vary across different contexts.
	+ High: You generally feel comfortable and consensual.
	+ Low: You may feel hesitant or uncomfortable, but still consent.
	+ Very Low: You rarely or never feel comfortable with consent.
* **Gender Identity** (`gender_identity`): This question explores your gender identity, which might be different from your gender roles or expressions. Select the option that best describes your understanding of yourself.
	+ Man: You identify as male.
	+ Woman: You identify as female.
	+ Non-Binary: You identify as non-binary or genderqueer.
	+ Genderqueer: You identify as genderqueer or gender non-conforming.
	+ Other: Please describe your gender identity in the space provided.
* **Age Range** (`age_range`): This question asks about your age group, which can influence your experiences and attitudes towards gender. Please select all that apply to you.
	+ 18-24: You are between 18 and 24 years old.
	+ 25-34: You are between 25 and 34 years old.
	+ 35-44: You are between 35 and 44 years old.
	+ 45-54: You are between 45 and 54 years old.
	+ 55-64: You are between 55 and 64 years old.
	+ 65 or older: You are 65 years old or older.
* **Gender Expression** (`gender_expression`): This question inquires about how you express your gender, which might be different from your gender identity. Select all that apply to you.
	+ Man: You express yourself as male.
	+ Woman: You express yourself as female.
	+ Non-Binary: You express yourself as non-binary or genderqueer.
	+ Genderqueer: You express yourself as genderqueer or gender non-conforming.
	+ Other: Please describe your gender expression in the space provided.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`): If you have any thoughts, comments, or suggestions about this form or its content, please share them in the space provided.
* **Gender Role At Home** (`gender_role_at_home`): This question asks about your gender role or expression at home. Please describe how you typically express yourself in your home environment.
* **Gender Role At School** (`gender_role_at_school`): This question inquires about your gender role or expression at school. Please describe how you typically express yourself at school.
* **Gender Role At Work** (`gender_role_at_work`): This question asks about your gender role or expression at work. Please describe how you typically express yourself at work.
* **Gender Role With Friends** (`gender_role_with_friends`): This question explores your gender role or expression with friends. Please describe how you typically express yourself with friends.

## Tips
If you're unsure about any question or option, it's okay to ask for clarification. If you have any feedback or suggestions for the form, please let us know.
