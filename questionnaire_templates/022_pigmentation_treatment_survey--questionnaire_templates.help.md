# Pigmentation Treatment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a client's pigmentation concerns, prior treatments, expected outcomes, goals, and client's expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your information in the **Client Information** page (page 1).
2. Describe your **Pigmentation Problems** in the **What pigmentation problems have you noticed?** field.
3. List your **Prior Treatments** in the **What prior treatments have you undergone?** field.
4. Indicate your **Expected Outcome** in the **Expected outcome** field.
5. Describe your **Client Goals** in the **Client Goals** field.
6. Add any **Aesthetician Notes** or additional information in the **Aesthetician Notes** field.
7. Share your **Client Expectations** in the **Client Expectations** field.
8. Finally, outline your **Treatment Plan** in the **Treatment Plan** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information**: This field is for your personal details, such as name, contact information, and other identifying characteristics.
* **What pigmentation problems have you noticed?**: Describe any skin pigmentation issues you are experiencing, such as freckles, age spots, or hyperpigmentation.
* **What prior treatments have you undergone?**: List any previous treatments or therapies you have tried for your skin pigmentation concerns.
* **Expected outcome**: Indicate what you hope to achieve through treatment or therapy.
* **Client Goals**: Describe your goals for your treatment or therapy, such as improving skin tone or achieving a certain appearance.
* **Aesthetician Notes**: Add any additional information or notes you want the aesthetician to consider during your treatment or therapy.
* **Client Expectations**: Share your expectations for the treatment or therapy, such as desired frequency of sessions or communication style.
* **Prior Treatment Notes**: Add any additional information or notes related to your prior treatments.
* **Treatment Plan**: Outline your proposed treatment or therapy plan, including any specific procedures or products you are interested in trying.
