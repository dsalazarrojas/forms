# MSQ Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a person's experiences with migraines, including their frequency, severity, and impact on their daily life. It is essential to complete it carefully to provide a clear and comprehensive picture of your condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the required fields (User Id, Pain frequency, Disability days) accurately.
2.  For the multiple-choice questions, select the option that best describes your experiences.
3.  If you have any additional information or comments, please use the space provided.
4.  Review each page carefully to ensure all questions are answered.
