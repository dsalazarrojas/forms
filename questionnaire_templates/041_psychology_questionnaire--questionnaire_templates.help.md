# psychology_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a patient, including their identification, medical history, test scores, and assessment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Patient Identification" field with the patient's accurate and up-to-date information.
2. In the "Questions" field, provide any relevant details about the patient's medical history.
3. In the "Patient History" field, provide any relevant medical history information.
4. In the "Test Scores" field, select one of the provided options for the patient's test score.
5. In the "Test Date" field, provide the date of the test.
6. In the "Assessment Time" field, provide the time of the assessment.
7. In the "Patient Status" field, select one of the provided options for the patient's status.
8. In the "Patient Outcome" field, provide any relevant information about the patient's outcome.
9. In the "Psychologist Notes" field, provide any additional notes from the psychologist.
10. In the "Email" field, provide the patient's email address.
11. In the "Phone" field, provide the patient's phone number.
12. In the "Address" field, provide the patient's address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Identification** (`patient_identification`, text, required): Fill in the patient's name, date of birth, and ID number.
* **Questions** (`questions`, text, optional): Provide any relevant details about the patient's medical history.
* **Patient History** (`patient_history`, text, optional): Provide any relevant medical history information.
* **Test Scores** (`test_scores`, select_one, required): Select one of the provided options for the patient's test score.
	+ Options: score1, score2, score3
* **Test Date** (`test_date`, date, optional): Provide the date of the test.
* **Assessment Time** (`assessment_time`, time, optional): Provide the time of the assessment.
* **Patient Status** (`patient_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select one of the provided options for the patient's status.
	+ Options: active, inactive, unknown
* **Patient Outcome** (`patient_outcome`, text, optional): Provide any relevant information about the patient's outcome.
* **Psychologist Notes** (`psychologist_notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes from the psychologist.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide the patient's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide the patient's phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Provide the patient's address.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide any relevant medical history information.
