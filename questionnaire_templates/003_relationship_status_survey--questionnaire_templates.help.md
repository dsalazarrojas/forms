# Relationship Status Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Relationship Status Survey is designed to gather information about your relationship status, including your relationship type, duration, and satisfaction levels. This form will help us understand your current relationship dynamics and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your current relationship status from the options provided.
2. Choose the duration of your relationship from the available timeframes.
3. Indicate the type of your relationship.
4. Describe any changes to your relationship status, if applicable.
5. Rate your satisfaction with your relationship, including the reasons why.
6. Rate your communication with your partner, including the channels used and topics discussed.
7. Indicate the frequency of conflicts and their topics.
8. Rate your trust level with your partner.
9. Describe any other reasons for your satisfaction or dissatisfaction.
10. Specify any other communication channels and topics used.
11. Describe any other conflict topics and frequencies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Single** (relationship_status, select_one): Choose your current relationship status.
* **Relationship Duration** (relationship_duration, select_one): Select the duration of your relationship from the provided options.
* **Relationship Type** (relationship_type, select_one): Choose the type of your relationship, such as romantic or family.
* **Relationship Status Change** (relationship_status_change, select_one): Describe any changes to your relationship status, if applicable.
* **Relationship Satisfaction** (relationship_satisfaction, number): Rate your overall satisfaction with your relationship.
* **Relationship Satisfaction Scale** (relationship_satisfaction_scale, select_one): Select the level of your satisfaction with your relationship, from very satisfied to very dissatisfied.
* **Reasons for Relationship Satisfaction/Disatisfaction** (relationship_satisfaction_reasons, text): Describe the reasons why you are satisfied or dissatisfied with your relationship.
* **Other reasons** (relationship_satisfaction_reasons_other, text): Specify any other reasons for your satisfaction or dissatisfaction.
* **Communication** (communication, select_one): Rate your communication with your partner, from very good to poor.
* **Intimacy** (intimacy, select_one): Rate your level of intimacy with your partner, from very intimate to very distant.
* **Conflict** (conflict, select_one): Rate the frequency of conflicts with your partner, from very few to many.
* **Conflict Topics** (conflict_topics, text): Describe the topics that lead to conflicts with your partner.
* **Other conflicts** (conflict_topics_other, text): Specify any other conflicts with your partner.
* **Conflict Topics Frequency** (conflict_topics_frequency, text): Describe the frequency of conflict topics with your partner.
* **Other conflict topics** (conflict_topics_frequency_other, text): Specify any other frequencies of conflict topics with your partner.
* **Trust** (trust, select_one): Rate your level of trust with your partner, from very much to not at all.
* **Trustworthiness** (trustworthiness, text): Describe any other reasons for your trust or distrust.
* **Other trustworthiness** (trustworthiness_other, text): Specify any other reasons for your trust or distrust.
* **Communication Channels** (communication_channels, select_multiple): Select the channels used for communication, such as text messages or video calls.
* **Other communication channels** (communication_channels_other, text): Specify any other communication channels used.
* **Communication Topics** (communication_topics, text): Describe the topics discussed with your partner.
* **Other communication topics** (communication_topics_other, text): Specify any other communication topics discussed.
* **Communication Topics Frequency** (communication_topics_frequency, text): Describe the frequency of communication topics with your partner.
* **Other communication topics frequency** (communication_topics_frequency_other, text): Specify any other frequencies of communication topics with your partner.
