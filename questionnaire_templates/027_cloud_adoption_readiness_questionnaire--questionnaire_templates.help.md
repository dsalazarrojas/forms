# Cloud Adoption Readiness Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cloud Adoption Readiness Questionnaire is designed to provide a comprehensive assessment of an organization's cloud adoption level. This questionnaire is intended to be a guiding tool for executives, IT teams, and other stakeholders to evaluate their organization's current state and identify areas that require improvement or attention.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and provide honest answers to the questions.
2. For select-one and select_multiple fields, choose the options that best match your organization's current state.
3. For text fields, enter your answers in the provided space.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name**: Enter the name of your company or institution.
* **Respondent Name**: Enter your full name as the person completing the form.
* **Job Title**: Enter your current position or role within the organization.
* **Email Address**: Enter your work email address.
* **Industry**: Select your primary industry sector.
* **Organization Size**: Choose the size of your organization from the provided options.
* **Years in Business**: Enter the number of years your organization has been operating.
* **Primary IT Infrastructure Type**: Select your organization's primary IT infrastructure type.
* **Current IT Maturity Level**: Select your organization's current IT maturity level.
* **Current Cloud Usage**: Select your organization's current cloud usage level.
* **Primary Drivers for Cloud Adoption**: Choose the factors that drive your organization's decision to adopt cloud computing.
* **Cloud Adoption Aligns with Business Strategy**: Select how well your organization's cloud adoption aligns with its business strategy.
* **Executive Leadership Commitment to Cloud**: Select the level of commitment from your executive leadership to cloud adoption.
* **Budget Availability**: Select your organization's current budget availability for cloud adoption.
* **Current IT Team Cloud Skills**: Select the level of cloud skills within your IT team.
* **Organizational Change Readiness**: Select your organization's current readiness to adapt to change.
* **Key Challenges for Cloud Adoption**: Choose the potential barriers to cloud adoption that your organization faces.
* **Organization Risk Tolerance**: Select your organization's risk tolerance level.
* **Preferred Cloud Adoption Timeline**: Select your preferred timeline for cloud adoption.
* **Additional Comments or Concerns**: Enter any additional comments or concerns about cloud adoption.

Note: This guide is intended to be a helpful reference for completing the form. Please answer honestly and accurately to get the most benefit from the assessment.
