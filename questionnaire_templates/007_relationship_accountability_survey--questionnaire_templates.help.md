# Relationship Accountability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Relationship Accountability Survey is a form designed to help individuals evaluate the health and growth of their relationships. It covers various aspects, including communication, responsibility, and growth, allowing you to reflect on your relationships and identify areas that need attention.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best describe your relationship.
3. Use the available space for any additional comments or explanations.
4. Submit your completed form for a personalized report on your relationship's strengths and weaknesses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Relationship Accountability Survey** (1, text, required): This is the main topic of the survey. Please provide a brief description of the relationship you are referring to.
* **Communication** (2, text, required): Describe the current state of communication in your relationship. This could include how often you communicate, the effectiveness of your communication, or any challenges you're facing.
* **Responsibility** (3, text, required): Reflect on the distribution of responsibilities within your relationship. Are there clear expectations and agreements? Are tasks and responsibilities evenly distributed?
* **Growth in Relationships** (4, select one, required): Indicate whether your relationship has shown growth and improvement over time.
	+ Choose "Yes" if you've noticed positive changes, such as increased trust, deeper connections, or shared goals.
	+ Choose "No" if you haven't experienced growth or have faced challenges.
* **Communication Patterns** (5, select multiple, required): Select the communication patterns you've noticed in your relationship.
	+ Choose all that apply:
		- Active listening
		- Clear expectations
		- Emotional support
		- Frequent check-ins
* **Responsibility Patterns** (7, select multiple, required): Select the responsibility patterns you've observed in your relationship.
	+ Choose all that apply:
		- Balanced task distribution
		- Clear roles and responsibilities
		- Effective delegation
		- Respectful conflict resolution
* **Growth Patterns** (8, select multiple, required): Indicate the growth patterns you've noticed in your relationship.
	+ Choose all that apply:
		- Shared goals and aspirations
		- Open communication
		- Respectful feedback
		- Supportive personal growth
* **Relationship Status** (9, select one, required): Select the current status of your relationship.
	+ Choose "Active" if your relationship is healthy and active.
	+ Choose "Inactive" if your relationship is struggling or on hold.
* **Relationship Type** (10, select one, required): Describe the type of relationship you are in (e.g., romantic partnership, friendship, business partnership).
* **Growth Stage** (11, select one, required): Indicate the growth stage of your relationship.
	+ Choose "Yes" if you've experienced significant growth and learning together.
	+ Choose "No" if you haven't experienced growth or face challenges.
* **Relationship Type Patterns** (12, select one, required): Select the relationship type patterns you've observed.
	+ Choose "Yes" if you've noticed positive changes in your relationship type (e.g., moving from acquaintances to close friends).
	+ Choose "No" if you haven't experienced changes in your relationship type.
* **Growth Stage Patterns** (13, select one, required): Indicate the growth stage patterns you've noticed.
	+ Choose "Yes" if you've experienced a significant shift in your relationship's growth stage (e.g., from dependent to interdependent).
	+ Choose "No" if you haven't experienced changes in your relationship's growth stage.
* **Relationship Growth** (14, text, required): Describe any notable growth or changes in your relationship.
* **Communication Frequency** (15, text, required): Estimate how often you communicate with your partner.
* **Responsibility Takers** (16, text, required): List the individuals responsible for tasks and responsibilities in your relationship.
* **Relationship Status Patterns** (17, select one, required): Select the relationship status patterns you've observed.
	+ Choose "Active" if your relationship is healthy and active.
	+ Choose "Inactive" if your relationship is struggling or on hold.
* **Growth in Relationships Takers** (18, text, required): Describe any growth or progress in your relationship's growth stage.
* **Relationship Type Takers** (19, text, required): Describe the type of relationship you are in.
* **Growth Stage Takers** (20, text, required): Describe the current growth stage of your relationship.
* **Communication Frequency Takers** (21, text, required): Estimate how often you communicate with your partner.
* **Responsibility Takers** (22, text, required): List the individuals responsible for tasks and responsibilities in your relationship.

## Tips

* Be honest and reflective in your answers to ensure accurate results.
* Use the available space to provide additional comments or explanations for each field.
* If you're unsure about any question, refer to the provided options for guidance.
* This survey is not a diagnostic tool for professional relationship counseling or therapy.
* Please submit your completed form for a personalized report on your relationship's strengths and weaknesses.
