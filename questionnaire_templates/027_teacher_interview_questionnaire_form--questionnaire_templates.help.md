# Teacher Interview Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Interview Questionnaire Form is designed to collect information from teachers to create accurate and up-to-date profiles.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you provide the correct information for each field.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure that they are correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address that you use for official communications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Teaching Certifications** (`teaching_certifications`, select_multiple, optional): Select all your relevant teaching certifications from the list.
* **Years of Experience** (`experience_years`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have been teaching.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
* Please provide your information in a format that can be easily read and understood.
