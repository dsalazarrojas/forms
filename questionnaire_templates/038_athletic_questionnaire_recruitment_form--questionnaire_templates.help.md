# Athletic Questionnaire Recruitment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather detailed information from potential recruits interested in our athletic program. It includes questions about the athlete's personal details, academic and athletic achievements, and their interest in our program.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, please follow these steps:
    1. Fill in your **Athlete Full Name**, which should include both your first and last name.
    2. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the MM-DD-YYYY format.
    3. Provide your **Email Address** as a contact point.
    4. Enter your **Phone Number** for communication purposes.
    5. Enter your **Street Address**, including your city, **State**, and **ZIP Code**.
    6. Select your current **School Name**.
    7. Choose your current **Grade Level** from the options provided.
    8. Enter your **Current GPA**.
    9. Select your **Primary Sport** from the available options.
    10. Enter your **Primary Position** played in your sport.
    11. Enter your **Height** in feet and inches or cm.
    12. Enter your **Weight** in pounds or kg.
    13. Specify the **Years of Experience** you have with your sport.
    14. Enter the name of your **Current Team**.
    15. Enter the name of your **Coach**.
    16. If available, provide your **Coach Email** and **Coach Phone Number** for communication.
    17. List your **Athletic Achievements**, such as awards, honors, and records.
    18. If applicable, list your **Personal Records or Stats**, such as times, distances, or scores.
    19. If you have video footage, select **Yes - Will Provide** or **Yes - Link Available**.
    20. If available, provide the **Video Link**.
    21. List your **Academic Achievements**, including honors, AP courses, and test scores.
    22. List your **Extracurricular Activities**, such as clubs, organizations, and leadership roles.
    23. List your **Community Service** activities, such as volunteer work.
    24. If you have any additional comments or information, please use the **Additional Comments** field.
    25. Finally, indicate your interest in a **Campus Visit** and whether you're interested in **Athletic Scholarships**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Athlete Full Name**: Your name is required for identification purposes.
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the MM-DD-YYYY format.
*   **Email Address**: This will be used as a contact point for communication.
*   **Phone Number**: This will be used for communication purposes.
*   **Street Address**: Include your city, state, and ZIP Code for address purposes.
*   **School Name**: Choose your current school.
*   **Grade Level**: Select your current grade level.
*   **Current GPA**: Enter your current grade point average.
*   **Primary Sport**: Select your primary sport.
*   **Primary Position**: Enter your primary position played in your sport.
*   **Height**: Enter your height in feet and inches or cm.
*   **Weight**: Enter your weight in pounds or kg.
*   **Years of Experience**: Specify the number of years you have played the sport.
*   **Current Team**: Enter the name of your current team.
*   **Coach Name**: Enter the name of your current coach.
*   **Coach Email**: If available, provide your coach's email address.
*   **Coach Phone**: If available, provide your coach's phone number.
*   **Athletic Achievements**: List your athletic achievements, such as awards, honors, and records.
*   **Personal Records or Stats**: If applicable, list your personal records or stats.
*   **Video Footage Available**: If you have video footage, select **Yes - Will Provide** or **Yes - Link Available**.
*   **Video Link**: If available, provide the video link.
*   **Academic Achievements**: List your academic achievements, including honors, AP courses, and test scores.
*   **Extracurricular Activities**: List your extracurricular activities, such as clubs, organizations, and leadership roles.
*   **Community Service**: List your community service activities, such as volunteer work.
*   **Video Link**: If available, provide the video link.
*   **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or information, please use this field.
*   **Interest in Campus Visit**: Indicate your interest in visiting the campus.
*   **Scholarship Interest**: Indicate whether you're interested in athletic scholarships.
*   **Other Schools Recruiting You**: If applicable, list any other schools interested in you.
*   **How You Heard About Our Program**: Select the source of your referral, such as coach recommendation, camp or clinic, social media, or friend or teammate.
