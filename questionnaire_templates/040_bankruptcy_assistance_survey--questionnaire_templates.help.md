# Bankruptcy Assistance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who are considering or have filed for bankruptcy. The purpose of this form is to gather relevant details about the respondent's financial situation, bankruptcy type, and preferences for assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully, providing accurate information for each field.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`respondent_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`respondent_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (`respondent_address`, text, required): Enter your mailing address (street, city, state, and ZIP).
* **Bankruptcy Type Filed** (`bankruptcy_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of bankruptcy you are filing for (e.g., Chapter 7, Chapter 11, Chapter 13, Considering Bankruptcy, Not Sure).
* **Filing Status** (`filing_status`, select_one, required): Choose where you are in the bankruptcy process (e.g., Considering, Preparing to File, Filed Recently, In Progress, Completed).
* **Primary Reason for Bankruptcy** (`primary_reason`, select_one, required): Choose the main reason for your bankruptcy filing (e.g., Medical Expenses, Job Loss, Divorce, Business Failure, Credit Card Debt, Unexpected Expenses, Other).
* **Total Debt Amount** (`total_debt_amount`, select_one, required): Choose your approximate total debt amount (e.g., Under $10,000, $10,000-$25,000, $25,000-$50,000, $50,000-$100,000, $100,000-$250,000, Over $250,000).
* **Types of Debt** (`debt_types`, select_multiple, required): Choose the types of debt you have (e.g., Credit Cards, Medical Bills, Personal Loans, Auto Loans, Mortgage, Student Loans, Tax Debt, Business Debt).
* **Annual Income** (`annual_income`, select_one, required): Choose your gross yearly income (e.g., Under $25,000, $25,000-$49,999, $50,000-$74,999, $75,000-$99,999, $100,000-$149,999, $150,000 or more).
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required): Choose your current employment status (e.g., Employed Full-Time, Employed Part-Time, Self-Employed, Unemployed, Retired, Disabled).
* **Home Ownership** (`home_ownership`, select_one, required): Choose your current home ownership status (e.g., Own with Mortgage, Own Free and Clear, Renting, Living with Family).
* **Vehicle Ownership** (`vehicle_ownership`, select_one, required): Choose your vehicle ownership status (e.g., Own Multiple Vehicles, Own One Vehicle, Leasing, None).
* **Legal Representation** (`legal_representation`, select_one, required): Choose whether you have an attorney (e.g., Yes, have attorney, No, filing pro se, Looking for attorney, Cannot afford attorney).
* **Previous Bankruptcy Filing** (`previous_bankruptcy`, select_one, required): Choose whether you have filed for bankruptcy before (e.g., Yes, within 8 years, Yes, over 8 years ago, No, first time).
* **Financial Counseling** (`financial_counseling`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you have completed financial counseling (e.g., Yes, completed, No, not yet, Not required).
* **Assistance Needed** (`assistance_needed`, select_multiple, required): Choose the types of assistance you need (e.g., Legal Advice, Document Preparation, Credit Counseling, Debt Management, Financial Planning, Post-Bankruptcy Support).
* **Satisfaction with Process** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, optional): Choose how satisfied you are with the bankruptcy process (e.g., Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied, Not Applicable).
* **Challenges Faced** (`challenges_faced`, text, optional): Enter any challenges you have faced related to the bankruptcy process (e.g., difficulty finding an attorney, high court fees).
* **Additional Services Interest** (`additional_services_interest`, text, optional): Enter any additional services you are interested in (e.g., credit repair, debt consolidation).
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`referral_source`, select_one, optional): Choose where you heard about us (e.g., Online Search, Attorney Referral, Friend or Family, Advertisement, Other).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information you would like to share.

**Tips**

* Please answer the questions to the best of your ability.
* If you need help with any of the questions, feel free to ask.
* This form will help us better understand your needs and provide tailored support and guidance.
* If you have already filled out this form in the past, please do not submit it again.
