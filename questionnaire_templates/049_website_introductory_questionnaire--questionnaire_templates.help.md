# website_introductory_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The website introductory questionnaire is a tool used to gather essential information from new clients about their website development project. This form ensures that the development team has a clear understanding of the client's needs, preferences, and requirements, enabling them to provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your new client details in the "New Client Details" field.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
3. Share your website preferences in the "Website Preferences" field.
4. Specify your design requirements in the "Design Requirements" field.
5. Describe your workflow preferences and experiences in the "Workflow" field.
6. Select the communication channels you prefer to use in the "Communication Channels" field.
7. Choose the web development tools you're familiar with in the "Web Development Tools" field.
8. Select your preferred web hosting options in the "Web Hosting" field.
9. Determine the payment terms for your project in the "Payment Terms" field.
10. Confirm your understanding of the client agreement in the "Client Agreement" field.
11. Set the project deadline in the "Project Deadline" field.
12. Specify the project start date in the "Project Start Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Client Details** (`new_client_details`, text, optional): Please provide your name, company name, or any other identifying information about your company or organization.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your email address and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Website Preferences** (`website_preferences`, text, optional): Share any specific preferences you may have for your website, such as color schemes, font styles, or other visual requirements.
* **Design Requirements** (`design_requirements`, text, optional): Specify any design requirements or expectations you have for your website, such as layout, content, or branding.
* **Workflow** (`workflow`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the workflow that best describes your current development process:
	+ Option 1: Waterfall
	+ Option 2: Agile
	+ Option 3: Hybrid
	+ Option 4: Other
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, select_multiple, optional): Select the channels you prefer to use for communication:
	+ Option 1: Email
	+ Option 2: Phone
	+ Option 3: Video Conference
	+ Option 4: Instant Messaging Apps
* **Web Development Tools** (`web_development_tools`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the web development tools you're familiar with:
	+ Option 1: Adobe Dreamweaver
	+ Option 2: Visual Studio Code
	+ Option 3: WordPress
	+ Option 4: Other
* **Web Hosting** (`web_hosting`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred web hosting options:
	+ Option 1: Shared Hosting
	+ Option 2: VPS Hosting
	+ Option 3: Dedicated Hosting
	+ Option 4: Cloud Hosting
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select_multiple, optional): Determine your preferred payment terms:
	+ Option 1: Hourly
	+ Option 2: Project-Based
	+ Option 3: Fixed Price
	+ Option 4: Other
* **Client Agreement** (`client_agreement`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm your understanding of the client agreement:
	+ Option 1: I understand and agree
	+ Option 2: I have questions
	+ Option 3: I disagree
	+ Option 4: Other
* **Project Deadline** (`project_deadline`, date, optional): Set a realistic project deadline.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, optional): Specify the date you'd like to start the project.

## Tips

* Please fill in all the required fields as accurately as possible to ensure a smooth project experience.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We'll use the information you provide to create a tailored project proposal and plan.
* Review each field carefully before proceeding to avoid errors or omissions.
