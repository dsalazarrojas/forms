# nth_largest_number_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and survey results from users about their experience with finding the nth largest number in a list. The data collected will help improve mathematical education and training materials within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Id** (`user_id`, number, required/optional): This field is intended to collect your unique identifier. If you have one, please enter it here. If not, you can leave it blank.
* **Lists of numbers** (`lists_of_numbers`, text, optional): Enter any lists of numbers you have used to try and find the nth largest number. This can be a list of numbers you've actually used in a real problem or a hypothetical example.
* **Nth largest answer** (`nth_largest_answer`, text, optional): Enter your answer to the question of what the nth largest number is. This can be a numerical value or a description of your process for arriving at an answer.
* **Method used** (`method_used`, select_one, optional): Choose how you found the nth largest number from the following options:
	+ Manual Counting
	+ Online Calculator
	+ Other
* **Confidence rating** (`confidence_rating`, number, optional): Rate your confidence in your answer on a scale from 0 (not at all confident) to 100 (completely confident).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes you have about your experience or method for finding the nth largest number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion or follow-up questions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone for further discussion or follow-up questions.
* **Other methods used** (`other_methods_used`, text, optional): If you chose "Other" in the "Method used" field, enter any additional details about the methods you used to find the nth largest number.
* **Other method details** (`other_method_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the "Other" method you used to find the nth largest number.
