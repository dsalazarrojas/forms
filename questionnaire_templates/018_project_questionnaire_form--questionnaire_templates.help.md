# Project Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Project Questionnaire Form is designed to collect project-related information from users.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your project's title to provide a brief description of the project.
2. Fill in your name and email address to ensure we can contact you regarding project updates.
3. Provide your phone number so we can reach you via phone if needed.
4. Answer the yes/no questions according to your project needs.
5. Add any additional notes about your project.
6. Enter your email address and phone number again, this time for your project team's contact information.
7. Provide the date and time for project meetings and events.
8. Answer the additional yes/no questions.
9. Finally, submit the form to complete your project questionnaire.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Questionnaire Form** (`project_questionnaire_form`, `text`, not required): Enter a brief description of your project.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, not required): Enter your name to associate it with the project.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address for project updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number for project contact.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of project meetings and events.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, not required): Enter the time of project meetings and events.
* **Question 1** (`question_1`, `text`, not required): Answer the first yes/no question regarding your project.
* **Select One 1** (`select_one_1`, `select_one`, not required): Answer the first select one question regarding your project.
* **Select Multiple 1** (`select_multiple_1`, `select_multiple`, not required): Answer the first select multiple question regarding your project.
* **Question 2** (`question_2`, `text`, not required): Answer the second yes/no question regarding your project.
* **Select One 2** (`select_one_2`, `select_one`, not required): Answer the second select one question regarding your project.
* **Select Multiple 2** (`select_multiple_2`, `select_multiple`, not required): Answer the second select multiple question regarding your project.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, not required): Add any additional notes about your project.
* **Email 2** (`email_2`, `email`, not required): Enter your team member's email address.
* **Phone 2** (`phone_2`, `text`, not required): Enter your team member's phone number.
* **Date 2** (`date_2`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of project meetings and events for your team.
* **Time 2** (`time_2`, `time`, not required): Enter the time of project meetings and events for your team.
* **Question 3** (`question_3`, `text`, not required): Answer the third yes/no question regarding your project.
* **Select One 3** (`select_one_3`, `select_one`, not required): Answer the third select one question regarding your project.
* **Select Multiple 3** (`select_multiple_3`, `select_multiple`, not required): Answer the third select multiple question regarding your project.
* **Select One 4** (`select_one_4`, `select_one`, not required): Answer the fourth select one question regarding your project.
* **Select Multiple 4** (`select_multiple_4`, `select_multiple`, not required): Answer the fourth select multiple question regarding your project.
* **Select One 5** (`select_one_5`, `select_one`, not required): Answer the fifth select one question regarding your project.
* **Select Multiple 5** (`select_multiple_5`, `select_multiple`, not required): Answer the fifth select multiple question regarding your project.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, not required): Click this button to complete and submit your project questionnaire.
