# Step 4 Fears Inventory - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Step 4 Fears Inventory form is designed to collect information about your fears and anxieties related to the topic or industry being surveyed. It's a series of text fields that ask you to describe your concerns and worries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you understand the questions being asked.
2. Answer each question in the space provided, describing your fears and anxieties as accurately as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Title** (`step_4_fears_inventory_page_1`, **Step 4 Fears Inventory Page 1**, not required): This page serves as an introduction to the fears inventory section.
- **Title** (`step_4_fears_inventory_page_2`, **Step 4 Fears Inventory Page 2**, not required): This page is another section of the fears inventory.
- **Title** (`step_4_fears_inventory_page_3`, **Step 4 Fears Inventory Page 3**, not required): This page is part of the fears inventory, continuing from the previous sections.
- **Title** (`step_4_fears_inventory_page_4`, **Step 4 Fears Inventory Page 4**, not required): This page is the final section of the fears inventory.
- **Title** (`step_4_fears_inventory_page_5`, **Step 4 Fears Inventory Page 5**, not required): This page is an additional section of the fears inventory.

## Tips
- Please answer each question in the space provided, being as detailed as possible.
- If you have additional concerns or worries that don't fit in the provided space, you can add them in the "Additional Comments" field, which is not included in this form but should be used for any further elaboration.
- Remember to save your progress regularly, as the form is not automatically saved.
