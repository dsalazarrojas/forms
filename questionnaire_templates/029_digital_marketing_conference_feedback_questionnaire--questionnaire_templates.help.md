# Digital Marketing Conference Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Marketing Conference Feedback Questionnaire is designed to gather feedback from attendees on their experience at the conference. This form will help us understand what worked well and what didn't, and use that information to improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the sections below, providing as much detail as you can. You can submit your feedback and suggestions anonymously, and your responses will be kept confidential.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Conference Information
* **Conference Information** (`conference_info`, text, optional): This is the main section where you can provide any additional information about the conference that you think is relevant to your feedback.
* **Attendee Information** (`attendee_info`, text, optional): Please fill in this section with any relevant information about yourself as an attendee, such as your role, company, or how you got to know about the conference.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Share your thoughts and impressions about the conference. What did you enjoy, what didn't you enjoy, and what would you recommend to improve?
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience at the conference out of 5, with 5 being the best.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, note, optional): If you have any suggestions on what we could improve or change for future conferences, now is the time to share them!
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide us with your contact information so we can reach out to follow up with you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address where we can contact you for any further questions or follow-ups.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please fill in your name so we can address you in our follow-up communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Your phone number where we can contact you for any further questions or follow-ups.
