# Ancient Human Sites Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Ancient Human Sites Survey" form is designed to collect information about ancient human sites, including their location, type, age, and condition. This data will help researchers, institutions, and organizations track and preserve these sites.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To complete this form, please start by filling in your personal details, including your full name, email address, and affiliation with a research institution.
2.  After providing your personal details, move on to fill in the site information, including the site's name, location, and age.
3.  Select the correct type of site and its current condition from the provided options.
4.  Indicate the site's excavation status and its cultural significance.
5.  If applicable, select the visible features and threats to the site's preservation.
6.  Finally, add any research notes and indicate whether further investigation is needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Researcher Full Name** (`researcher_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
*   **Researcher Email** (`researcher_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Research Institution** (`institution`, text, required): Enter the name of your affiliated research institution.
*   **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your site visit or report.
*   **Site Name** (`site_name`, text, required): Enter the official or common name of the archaeological site.
*   **Site Location** (`site_location`, text, required): Enter the site's geographic location, including the country and region.
*   **GPS Coordinates** (`gps_coordinates`, text, optional): Enter the site's latitude and longitude if available.
*   **Type of Site** (`site_type`, select_one, required): Select the primary classification of the site from the provided options.
*   **Estimated Age Period** (`estimated_age`, select_one, required): Select the archaeological period or date range of the site from the provided options.
*   **Current Site Condition** (`site_condition`, select_one, required): Select the site's current preservation state from the provided options.
*   **Excavation Status** (`excavation_status`, select_one, required): Indicate the site's current excavation or research status from the provided options.
*   **Cultural Significance** (`cultural_significance`, select_one, required): Indicate the site's cultural significance from the provided options.
*   **Visible Features** (`visible_features`, select_multiple, optional): Select all features present at the site from the provided options.
*   **Threats to Site Preservation** (`threats_to_site`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable threats to the site's preservation from the provided options.
*   **Site Accessibility** (`accessibility`, select_one, required): Indicate how accessible the site is to researchers from the provided options.
*   **Research Notes** (`research_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional observations or comments about the site.
*   **Are Photographs Available** (`photographs_available`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether photographs are available for the site from the provided options.
*   **Is Follow-up Research Needed** (`follow_up_needed`, select_one, optional): Indicate if further investigation is needed for the site from the provided options.

## Tips

Please make sure to fill in all required fields as they are crucial for accurate data collection and site analysis. If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance. The submitted data will be used for public good, and any commercial or proprietary use is strictly prohibited.
