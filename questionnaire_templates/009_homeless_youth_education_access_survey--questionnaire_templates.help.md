## Homeless Youth Education Access Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the educational needs and preferences of homeless youth to identify potential barriers to education and access to support services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Please note that this form is completely anonymous and your responses will not be linked to your personal identity.** If you are completing this form on behalf of a minor, please make sure to have their permission before proceeding.
2. Make sure you are in a quiet and private space to complete the form without distractions.
3. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
4. You can complete the form in multiple pages, but please make sure to submit it before moving on to the next page.
5. If you need help or have any questions, please contact our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Barriers to Education** (`barriers_to_education`):
	+ This section is a free-text field where you can describe any barriers you are facing to accessing education.
* **Education Support Needs** (`education_support_needs`):
	+ Select all the support needs you have to access education. This may include but is not limited to: 
		- Access to school supplies (e.g., school books, pens, pencils)
		- Food and clothing for school events
		- Access to online resources (e.g., laptops, internet)
		- Support from teachers or peers
	+ Please select all that apply.
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`):
	+ Please enter your preferred method of contact, e.g., phone call, text message, email, or in-person meeting.
	+ Please note that if you have selected more than one option, we may contact you through multiple methods.
* **Age** (`age`):
	+ Please enter your age in years.
	+ This field is required for demographic data collection purposes.
* **Contact Preferences (again)** (`contact_preferences`):
	+ You can enter additional contact preferences here, if not already done in the previous field.
	+ Please use the format: "method1, method2, method3, etc."

## Tips
