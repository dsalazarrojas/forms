# Wakeup Your Makeup Color Match Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wakeup Your Makeup Color Match Form is designed to help match your makeup preferences with suggested products. The form collects information about your skin tone, hair color, eye color, and other personal characteristics to generate a personalized color match score.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select your answer for each field by clicking on the options provided.
2. Answer all the required fields (indicated by *) to ensure you get accurate results.
3. Once you've answered all the questions, click submit to generate your color match score.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Color Match Form** (`color_match_form`, Select Multiple, *): This field helps us determine your color matching preferences.
* **Skin Tone** (`skin_tone`, Select One, *): Please select the closest match to your natural skin tone.
	+ Yes: Fair, cool skin tone
	+ No: Tanned or olive skin tone
* **Hair Color** (`hair_color`, Select One, *): Please select your natural hair color.
	+ Yes: Light hair color (blonde, platinum blonde)
	+ No: Dark hair color (brunette, black, brown)
* **Eye Color** (`eye_prophet_eye_color`, Select One, *): Please select your natural eye color.
	+ Yes: Blue or green eyes
	+ No: Brown or hazel eyes
* **Natural Skin Tone** (`natural_skin_tone`, Select Multiple, *): Please select the natural skin tone you're most likely to have.
	+ Yes: Fair skin
	+ No: Tanned or olive skin
* **Makeup Product Recommendations** (`makeup_product_recommendations`, Select Multiple, *): If you want personalized makeup product recommendations, please select the products that best match your skin tone.
	+ Yes: Match with fair skin products
	+ No: Match with tanned or olive skin products
* **Color Match Score** (`color_match_score`, Number, ): This is the score generated based on your answers. The higher the score, the better the match.
* **Color Match Score Range** (`color_match_score_range`, Number, ): This is the range of the color match score. A higher range indicates a more accurate match.
* **Skin Type** (`skin_type`, Select One, *): Please select the skin type that best describes you.
	+ Yes: Normal skin
	+ No: Oily skin
* **Skin Tone Range** (`skin_tone_range`, Select Multiple, *): Please select the skin tone range you're most likely to have.
	+ Yes: Fair skin tone
	+ No: Tanned or olive skin tone
* **Eyebrow Color** (`eyebrow_color`, Select One, *): Please select your natural eyebrow color.
	+ Yes: Light eyebrow color (blonde, light brown)
	+ No: Dark eyebrow color (black, dark brown)
* **Complexion** (`complexion`, Select One, *): Please select the complexion that best describes you.
	+ Yes: Normal complexion
	+ No: Pale or sallow complexion
* **Skin Complexion** (`skin_complexion`, Select One, *): Please select the skin complexion that best describes you.
	+ Yes: Normal skin
	+ No: Dull or dry skin
* **Face Shape** (`face_shape`, Select One, *): Please select your face shape.
	+ Yes: Oval face shape
	+ No: Round face shape
* **Hair Length** (`hair_length`, Select One, *): Please select your natural hair length.
	+ Yes: Long hair length
	+ No: Short hair length
* **Face Shape Range** (`face_shape_range`, Number, ): This is the range of face shape. A higher range indicates a more accurate match.
* **Hair Length Range** (`hair_length_range`, Number, ): This is the range of hair length. A higher range indicates a more accurate match.
* **Age** (`age`, Number, ): Please select your age.
* **Height** (`height`, Number, ): Please select your height.
* **Weight** (`weight`, Number, ): Please select your weight.
