# Leadership Training Impact Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of leadership training sessions by collecting relevant information about the attendees, training details, and measured outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form for each leadership training session you want to evaluate.
2. Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email in the corresponding fields.
3. Select the Training Session Date and Time for the relevant session.
4. Enter the Training Topic and Location for the session.
5. Provide a brief description of the outcomes measured during the training.
6. Enter the values achieved for the outcomes, using one field per outcome.
7. Optionally, add Comments about the session if needed.
8. Choose whether the training session was Assigned to you or Not.
9. Select whether a specific Assigned Tool was used or Not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your official email address.
* **Training Session Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the leadership training session in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Training Session Time** (time, required): Enter the start and end time of the training session in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Training Topic** (text, required): Enter a brief description of the training topic or subject matter.
* **Training Location** (text, required): Enter the location where the training session took place.
* **Training Outcomes Measured** (text, required): Enter a brief description of the specific outcomes measured during the training session.
* **Value 1**, **Value 2**, **Value 3**, **Value 4**, **Value 5** (text, required): Enter a value or achievement related to the outcome measured, using one field per outcome.
* **Comments** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or observations about the training session.
* **Assigned to** (select_one, required): Choose whether you were assigned to lead or participate in the training session.
* **Assigned Tool** (select_one, required): Select whether a specific tool was assigned to you for the training session.
