# Canine Anxiety Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to assess the behavioral, emotional, and situational factors that contribute to canine anxiety. It will gather information about the owner's relationship with their dog, the dog's behavior and characteristics, and the impact of anxiety on the household. This data will help veterinarians and animal behaviorists understand the root causes of canine anxiety and develop effective treatment plans.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out each page, following the instructions on each page for specific guidance.
2. Answer the questions honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. When selecting multiple options for any question, use commas to separate them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Full Name** (1, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Owner Email Address** (2, email, required): Type your email address for contact purposes.
* **Owner Phone Number** (3, text, required): Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Dog Name** (4, text, required): Type the name of your dog.
* **Dog Breed** (5, text, required): Enter your dog's breed or mix.
* **Dog Age** (6, number, required): Enter your dog's age in years.
* **Dog Gender** (7, select_one, required): Select 'Male - neutered', 'Male - not neutered', 'Female - spayed', or 'Female - not spayed' to describe your dog's sex.
* **Dog Weight** (8, number, required): Enter your dog's weight in pounds.
* **How Long Have You Had Your Dog** (9, select_one, required): Choose 'Less than 6 months', '6 months to 1 year', '1 to 3 years', '3 to 5 years', or 'More than 5 years' to describe your dog's ownership duration.
* **Anxiety Symptoms Observed** (10, select_multiple, required): Select all the anxiety symptoms that apply to your dog, using commas to separate them:
	+ Excessive barking
	+ Destructive behavior
	+ Pacing or restlessness
	+ Panting without exertion
	+ Trembling or shaking
	+ Hiding or cowering
	+ Excessive licking
	+ House soiling
	+ Loss of appetite
	+ Clinginess
* **How Often Do Symptoms Occur** (11, select_one, required): Choose how often your dog's anxiety symptoms occur:
	+ Multiple times daily
	+ Once daily
	+ Several times weekly
	+ Once weekly
	+ Rarely
* **Severity of Symptoms** (12, select_one, required): Select the level of severity of your dog's anxiety symptoms:
	+ Mild - barely noticeable
	+ Moderate - noticeable but manageable
	+ Severe - disruptive to daily life
	+ Very severe - constant distress
* **Known Anxiety Triggers** (13, select_multiple, required): Select all the triggers that cause your dog's anxiety:
	+ Loud noises - thunderstorms - fireworks
	+ Being left alone
	+ Strangers or visitors
	+ Other dogs
	+ Car rides
	+ Vet visits
	+ New environments
	+ Changes in routine
	+ Separation from owner
	+ Unknown triggers
* **Does Your Dog Have Separation Anxiety** (14, select_one, required): Choose how severe your dog's separation anxiety is:
	+ Yes - severe
	+ Yes - moderate
	+ Yes - mild
	+ 'False'
	+ Unsure
* **Maximum Time Alone** (15, select_one, required): Select the maximum time your dog can be left alone:
	+ Less than 30 minutes
	+ 30 minutes to 1 hour
	+ 1 to 2 hours
	+ 2 to 4 hours
	+ More than 4 hours
* **Noise Sensitivity Level** (16, select_one, required): Choose your dog's reaction to loud sounds:
	+ Very sensitive - panics
	+ Moderately sensitive
	+ Slightly sensitive
	+ Not sensitive
* **Socialization Level** (17, select_one, required): Select your dog's comfort level with people and dogs:
	+ Very social - loves everyone
	+ Selectively social
	+ Cautious with strangers
	+ Not social - prefers solitude
* **Daily Exercise Routine** (18, select_one, required): Choose your dog's activity level:
	+ High - 2 or more hours
	+ Moderate - 1 to 2 hours
	+ Low - 30 minutes to 1 hour
	+ Minimal - less than 30 minutes
* **Training History** (19, select_one, required): Select the training your dog has received:
	+ Professional training completed
	+ Obedience classes attended
	+ Basic home training
	+ No formal training
* **Has Dog Received Anxiety Treatment** (20, select_one, required): Choose if your dog has received anxiety treatment:
	+ Yes - medication
	+ Yes - behavioral therapy
	+ Yes - natural remedies
	+ No - never treated
* **Current Medications or Supplements** (21, text, optional): Type any current medications or supplements your dog is taking.
* **Have You Consulted a Vet About Anxiety** (22, select_one, required): Choose if you have consulted a vet about your dog's anxiety:
	+ Yes - recently
	+ Yes - in the past
	+ No - planning to
	+ No - not planning to
* **How Does Anxiety Impact Your Household** (23, select_one, required): Choose the effect of anxiety on your household:
	+ Significant disruption
	+ Moderate disruption
	+ Minor disruption
	+ Minimal impact
* **Your Stress Level Related to Dog Anxiety** (24, select_one, required): Choose how stressful your dog's anxiety makes you:
	+ Very high stress
	+ Moderate stress
	+ Mild stress
	+ No stress
* **What Are Your Goals for Treatment** (25, text, required): Type your goals for treating your dog's anxiety.
* **Additional Information** (26, text, optional): Type any additional information that may be relevant to your dog's anxiety.
* **Survey Date** (27, date, required): Enter the date you completed this survey.

## Tips
- Be honest and as specific as possible when answering each question.
- Remember that the more information you provide, the better we can understand your dog's anxiety.
- If you are unsure about any question, choose the best fit or leave it blank.
- You can save your progress and come back later to finish the survey.
- If you need help, contact us for assistance.
