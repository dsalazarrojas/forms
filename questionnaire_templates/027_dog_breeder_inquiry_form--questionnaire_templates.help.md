# Contact and Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from end-users for contact and inquiry purposes.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your contact information in the "contact_info" section, including your name and email address.
2. Provide details about your inquiry in the "inquiry_info" section.
3. Indicate your preferred contact method for the inquiry.
4. If applicable, specify your dog breed and puppy type.
5. For date of birth, select the correct date or fill in the correct date if not provided.
6. Provide a reason for your inquiry.
7. Attach any relevant files or documents for submission.
8. Select the status of your submission as Active or Inactive.
9. If necessary, select any attachments to be attached with your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information (contact_info)** (`contact_info`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for providing your contact information, including your name and email address.
* **Inquiry Information (inquiry_info)** (`inquiry_info`, text, optional):
	+ Please provide details about your inquiry or question.
* **Contact Method (contact_method)** (`contact_method`, text, optional):
	+ Indicate your preferred contact method for the inquiry.
* **Dog Breed (dog_breed)** (`dog_breed`, text, optional):
	+ If your inquiry is related to a dog breed, please specify it here.
* **Puppy Type (puppy_type)** (`puppy_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select Yes or No if your inquiry is related to a puppy type.
* **Date of Birth (date_of_birth)** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional):
	+ Select or fill in the correct date of birth if not provided.
* **Inquiry Reason (inquiry_reason)** (`inquiry_reason`, text, optional):
	+ Explain the reason for your inquiry.
* **Submission Info (submission_info)** (`submission_info`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional information about your submission.
* **Attachment Upload (attachment_upload)** (`attachment_upload`, text, optional):
	+ Attach any relevant files or documents for submission.
* **Submission Status (submission_status)** (`submission_status`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select Active or Inactive to indicate the status of your submission.
* **Attachments (attachments)** (`attachments`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select Yes or No if you have attachments to attach with your submission.
