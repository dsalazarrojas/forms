# Podcast Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help podcasters and content creators gather information about their audience's preferences and interests related to podcast content. It's meant to be used during or after a podcast session, and the data collected will help create more engaging and relevant content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Session Topic" field with a brief description of the topic discussed during the session.
2. Choose the interest level of the topic (Topic Interest) by selecting the most relevant option.
3. Enter the length of the session (Session Length) in a 24-hour clock format (e.g., 1 hour, 1:30, etc.).
4. Select the relevance of the topic (Relevance) from the available options.
5. Choose the frequency of the session (Session Frequency) by selecting the most relevant options.
6. Indicate the importance of the topic (Topic Importance) by entering a numerical value.
7. Rate the audience engagement (Audience Engagement) by selecting the most relevant option.
8. Evaluate the topic's value (Topic Value) by entering a numerical value.
9. Assess the session's value (Session Value) by entering a numerical value.
10. For additional topics, select the ones that were discussed but not listed, and indicate if they were of any relevance.
11. Repeat the same process for additional topics.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Topic**: Describe the topic discussed during the session.
* **Topic Interest**: Select the most relevant option to indicate the interest level of the topic.
* **Session Length**: Enter the length of the session in a 24-hour clock format.
* **Relevance**: Select the most relevant option to indicate the relevance of the topic.
* **Session Frequency**: Choose the frequency of the session by selecting the most relevant options.
* **Topic Importance**: Enter a numerical value to indicate the importance of the topic.
* **Audience Engagement**: Select the most relevant option to indicate the engagement level of the audience.
* **Topic Value**: Enter a numerical value to indicate the value of the topic.
* **Session Value**: Enter a numerical value to indicate the value of the session.
* **Additional Topics**: Select additional topics that were discussed but not listed, and indicate if they were of any relevance.
* **Additional Topics 2**: Repeat the same process as Additional Topics.

## Tips
* Be specific and honest when filling in the form to ensure accurate data.
* Use the options provided to make selections.
* If you're unsure about a field, refer to the provided instructions or ask for clarification.
* The data collected will be used to create more engaging and relevant content for you and your audience.
