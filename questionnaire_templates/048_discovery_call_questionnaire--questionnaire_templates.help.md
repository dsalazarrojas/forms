# Discovery Call Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the startup's background, target market, and target audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps above and fill in the fields as described.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Startup Founder** (required): A brief description of your company or project.
* **Target Market** (required): A description of your target market, including its location, industry, or niche.
* **Company Info** (optional): Any relevant details about your company, such as its location, size, or founding date.
* **Target Audience** (required): A description of your target audience, including their demographics, needs, or preferences.
