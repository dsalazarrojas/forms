# Formal Communication Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your formal communication skills in various areas, including active listening, verbal and written communication, adapting to change, communicating with diversity, conflict resolution, communication under stress, and communication skills for leaders. It is a helpful tool for personal or professional development, such as employee evaluations or performance reviews.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions according to your experiences and opinions. 
   - You can choose to answer all the questions, and some questions are marked as optional, so feel free to skip them if you wish.
2. Each question is a self-assessment, so answer honestly and to the best of your abilities.
3. Please be as specific as possible when answering each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Formal Communication Skills** (`formal_communication_skills`, text, required): This is the primary section to rate your proficiency in formal communication skills.
* **Active Listening Skills** (`active_listening_skills`, text, required): Rate your ability to actively listen to others and understand their perspectives.
* **Verbal Communicating** (`verbal_communicating`, text, required): Assess your skills in verbal communication, including speaking and presenting information.
* **Written Communicating** (`written_communicating`, text, required): Rate your ability to write and convey information clearly and effectively.
* **Adapting to Change** (`adapting_to_change`, text, required): Evaluate your ability to adapt to changes and adjust to new information.
* **Communicating with Diversity** (`communication_with_diversity`, text, optional): This section is not required, but it can help you rate your ability to communicate with people from different backgrounds and cultures.
* **Conflict Resolution** (`conflict_resolution`, text, required): Rate your ability to resolve conflicts and negotiate with others.
* **Communication under Stress** (`communication_in_stress`, text, required): Assess your communication skills when under stress or pressure.
* **Communication Skills for Leaders** (`communication_skills_for_leaders`, text, required): Rate your ability to communicate effectively as a leader or leader-in-training.

## Tips

* Be honest and objective when answering the questions. 
* Take as much time as you need to answer each question. 
* If you're unsure about a question, feel free to skip it or come back to it later. 
* Remember, this is a self-assessment tool, so it's okay to ask for help or feedback from others if needed.
