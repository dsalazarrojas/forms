# Find The Best Beginner Program For You - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide users in finding the best beginner program that suits their needs. The form collects information about the user's goals, experience, and learning style to provide a personalized recommendation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select your primary goal for the beginner program from the options provided.
2. Choose your level of experience to help us understand your current skills and knowledge.
3. Select all the formats you are interested in (e.g., online course, offline course, video tutorial, book).
4. Choose the type of program you are looking for (e.g., to learn a new skill, improve physical condition, mental well-being).
5. Select your preferred pace of learning (fast, medium, slow).
6. Indicate the number of hours you are willing to commit to learning each week.
7. Choose the type of results you are expecting from the program (e.g., physical, mental, social, professional).
8. If you have any health concerns, select 'Yes' or 'No'.
9. Check if you require any special accommodations.
10. Confirm your selections by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### What is your goal for this beginner program?
• **Goal** (`select_one`): Select your primary goal for the beginner program, which is to either improve your career development, skill development, or have fun and leisure. This will help us understand what you hope to achieve with the program.

### What is your level of experience?
• **Level of Experience** (`select_one`): Choose your level of experience, which is either beginner, intermediate, or advanced. This will help us understand your current skills and knowledge level.

### How much time do you have available each week?
• **Time Commitment** (`number`): Enter the number of hours you are willing to commit to learning each week. This will help us understand your available time for the program.

### What is your preferred format of learning?
• **Format of Learning** (`select_multiple`): Select all the formats you are interested in, such as online courses, offline courses, video tutorials, or books. This will help us understand your learning style and preferences.

### What type of program are you looking for?
• **Program Type** (`select_multiple`): Choose the type of program you are looking for, such as learning a new skill, improving physical condition, or mental well-being. This will help us understand what you want to achieve with the program.

### What is your preferred pace of learning?
• **Pace of Learning** (`select_one`): Select your preferred pace of learning, which is fast, medium, or slow. This will help us understand how quickly you want to progress with the program.

### How many hours per week would you commit to learning?
• **Time Commitment** (`number`): Enter the number of hours you are willing to commit to learning each week. This will help us understand your available time for the program.

### What are your expected results?
• **Expected Results** (`select_multiple`): Choose the type of results you are expecting from the program, such as physical, mental, social, or professional improvements. This will help us understand what you want to achieve with the program.

### Do you have any health concerns?
• **Health Concerns** (`select_one`): If you have any health concerns, select 'Yes' or 'No'. This will help us understand any potential limitations or needs you may have.

### Do you require any special accommodations?
• **Accommodations** (`select_one`): Check if you require any special accommodations. This will help us understand any additional requirements you may need.

### Can you confirm?
• **Confirmation** (`select_one`): Confirm your selections by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'. This will help us verify your answers and provide a personalized recommendation.
