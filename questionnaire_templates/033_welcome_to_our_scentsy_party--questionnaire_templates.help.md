# welcome_to_our_scentsy_party - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from our customers who are interested in attending our Scentsy party. Please complete this form to help us understand your preferences and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your name and contact information in the corresponding fields.
2. Review your information before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your general information about yourself.
* **First Name** (`customer_info_first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`customer_info_last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`customer_info_email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`customer_info_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
## Tips

* Make sure to enter your email address correctly, as this is how we will contact you with updates about our Scentsy party.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask us, and we will be happy to help.
