# Advanced Biology And Genetics Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in biology and genetics, particularly those with a professional background or academic interest in the field.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
2. Read each question carefully and choose the most relevant option.
3. Fill in all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
4. If you have any issues or concerns, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_information_section**: This section is a placeholder for participant information, but it is not required to be filled out.
* **Name**: Your name is optional and only required if you wish to be contacted about your responses.
* **Educational Background**: Select the highest level of education you have achieved in biology or genetics. This can be a high school diploma or higher.
* **Professional Role**: Choose the job title or position you hold in the biology or genetics field.
* **Gene Expression Understanding**: Rate your understanding of gene expression mechanisms from "Expert level" to "Little to no knowledge".
* **CRISPR-Cas9 Knowledge**: Rate your familiarity with CRISPR-Cas9 gene editing technology from "Very familiar" to "Not familiar".
* **Genetic Disorders Knowledge**: Rate your knowledge of genetic disorders from "Extensive knowledge" to "No knowledge".
* **Genomics Technology Familiarity**: Select all the genomics technologies you are familiar with.
* **Primary Area of Interest**: Choose the area of genetics that interests you most, such as medical genetics or molecular genetics.
* **Research Interests**: This question is optional and allows you to express your interests in genetics research topics.
* **Applications Interest**: Select all the applications of genetics that you find most promising.
* **Gene Editing Ethics View**: Express your view on human germline gene editing, from "Strongly support" to "Strongly oppose".
* **Genetic Testing Attitude**: Rate your attitude towards genetic testing, from "Very positive" to "Very concerned".
* **Privacy Concerns**: Rate your concern for genetic privacy, from "Very concerned" to "Not concerned".
* **Continuing Education Interest**: Indicate your interest in continuing education in genetics, from "Yes, definitely" to "Definitely not".
* **Preferred Learning Format**: Choose your preferred format for learning about genetics, such as online courses or peer-reviewed articles.

Tips:

* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification or contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
* This form is designed to be completed in a few minutes, so please take your time and answer carefully.
* Thank you for participating in this survey and contributing to the advancement of biology and genetics research!
