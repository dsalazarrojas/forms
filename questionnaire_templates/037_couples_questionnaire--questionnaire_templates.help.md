# couples_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about a couple's relationship to help users understand their dynamics and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the couple in the "Couple's Name" field.
2. Fill out the demographic information for both partners, including their first name, last name, age, education level, job title, and income.
3. Indicate the length of time you have been together.
4. Select the current relationship status.
5. Choose the communication styles and emotional intelligence of both partners.
6. Rate the frequency of communication between partners.
7. Answer questions about conflict resolution methods and topics.
8. Describe the level of emotional support and physical touch you have in your relationship.
9. Share your thoughts on your relationship satisfaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Couple's Name** (`couple_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the couple, such as John and Jane Doe.
* **Partner 1 First Name** (`partner1_first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of the first partner.
* **Partner 1 Last Name** (`partner1_last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of the first partner.
* **Partner 1 Age** (`partner1_age`, number, required): Enter the age of the first partner.
* **Partner 2 First Name** (`partner2_first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of the second partner.
* **Partner 2 Last Name** (`partner2_last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of the second partner.
* **Partner 2 Age** (`partner2_age`, number, required): Enter the age of the second partner.
* **How Long Together** (`how_long_together`, text, required): Indicate how long you have been together as a couple, e.g., "5 years", "3 months", etc.
* **Relationship Status** (`relationship_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose all that apply from the following options:
	+ None
	+ Single
	+ Together
	+ Separated
	+ Divorced
* **Partner 1 Education Level** (`partner1_education_level`, select_one, required): Choose the education level of the first partner:
	+ High School
	+ Some College
	+ College
	+ Graduate School
	+ Some Other
* **Partner 1 Job Title** (`partner1_job_title`, text, required): Enter the job title of the first partner.
* **Partner 1 Income** (`partner1_income`, number, required): Enter the income of the first partner.
* **Partner 2 Education Level** (`partner2_education_level`, select_one, required): Choose the education level of the second partner:
	+ High School
	+ Some College
	+ College
	+ Graduate School
	+ Some Other
* **Partner 2 Job Title** (`partner2_job_title`, text, required): Enter the job title of the second partner.
* **Partner 2 Income** (`partner2_income`, number, required): Enter the income of the second partner.
* **Communication Style** (`communication_style`, select_multiple, required): Choose all that apply from the following options:
	+ Active
	+ Passive
	+ Aggressive
	+ Assertive
* **Emotional Intelligence** (`emotional_intelligence`, select_one, required): Choose the emotional intelligence level:
	+ High
	+ Average
	+ Low
* **Conflict Resolution** (`conflict_resolution`, text, required): Describe how you resolve conflicts with your partner.
* **Communication Frequency** (`communication_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Choose all that apply from the following options:
	+ Daily
	+ Often
	+ Sometimes
	+ Rarely
* **Conflict Topics** (`conflict_topics`, text, required): Describe common topics that lead to conflict.
* **Conflict Resolution Methods** (`conflict_resolution_methods`, text, required): Describe how you resolve conflicts.
* **Emotional Support** (`emotional_support`, text, required): Describe the level of emotional support you receive from your partner.
* **Physical Touch** (`physical_touch`, text, required): Describe the level of physical touch in your relationship.
* **Shared Space** (`shared_space`, text, required): Describe your shared living or workspace.
* **Relationship Satisfaction** (`relationship_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Choose the level of satisfaction:
	+ High
	+ Average
	+ Low
