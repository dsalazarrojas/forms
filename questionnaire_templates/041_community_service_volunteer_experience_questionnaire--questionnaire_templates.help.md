# Community Service Volunteer Experience Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your volunteer experience to help us evaluate our volunteer program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your actual volunteer experience.
2. Be honest and thorough in your answers.
3. Select all options that apply to your experience.
4. If a field is not applicable, select "None" or "Not Applicable".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Information** (Volunteer Information, note, Optional): This field is for you to briefly describe your volunteer experience.
* **First Name** (First Name, text, Optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, Optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, Required): This field is for us to contact you for follow-up purposes. Please enter a valid email address.
* **How Long Have You Volunteered** (How Long Have You Volunteered, select_one, Required): Select the duration of your volunteer experience from the options provided.
* **Volunteer Experience** (Volunteer Experience, note, Optional): This field is for you to describe your recent project in detail.
* **Project Name** (Project Name, text, Required): Please enter the name of your project.
* **Project Date** (Project Date, date, Required): Please enter the date when you worked on your project.
* **Your Role** (Your Role, text, Required): Please describe your role in the project.
* **Hours Contributed** (Hours Contributed, number, Required): Please enter the number of hours you contributed to the project.
* **Satisfaction Ratings** (Satisfaction Ratings, note, Optional): This field is for you to rate your overall satisfaction with your experience.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Overall Satisfaction, select_one, Required): Select how satisfied you are with your experience from the options provided.
* **Organization Support Rating** (Organization Support Rating, select_one, Required): Select how well the organization supported you from the options provided.
* **Training Quality Rating** (Training Quality Rating, select_one, Required): Select how good the training was from the options provided.
* **Supervisor Support Rating** (Supervisor Support Rating, select_one, Required): Select how well your supervisor supported you from the options provided.
* **How Much Impact Did You Feel You Make** (How Much Impact Did You Feel You Made, select_one, Required): Select how much impact you felt you made from the options provided.
* **Was Your Work Meaningful** (Was Your Work Meaningful, select_one, Required): Select how meaningful you felt your work was from the options provided.
* **Skills and Development** (Skills and Development, note, Optional): This field is for you to describe your growth and development during the project.
* **Skills You Used** (Skills You Used, select_multiple, Required): Select all the skills you used during your project from the options provided.
* **New Skills You Learned** (New Skills You Learned, text, Optional): Please describe any new skills you learned during your project.
* **Did Your Confidence Improve** (Did Your Confidence Improve, select_one, Required): Select how much confidence you gained from the experience from the options provided.
* **Challenges You Faced** (Challenges You Faced, select_multiple, Optional): Select all the challenges you faced during the project from the options provided.
* **What Support Would Have Helped** (What Support Would Have Helped, text, Optional): Please describe what kind of support would have helped you during your project.
* **Recommendations** (Recommendations, note, Optional): This field is for you to suggest improvements or recommendations for our volunteer program.
* **What Could Be Improved** (What Could Be Improved, text, Optional): Please describe any areas that could be improved.
* **Will You Continue Volunteering** (Will You Continue Volunteering, select_one, Required): Select if you will continue volunteering with us or elsewhere.
* **Would You Recommend to Friends** (Would You Recommend to Friends, select_one, Required): Select if you would recommend our volunteer program to your friends.
* **What Was Your Highlight** (What Was Your Highlight, text, Optional): Please describe the best moment of your volunteer experience.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text, Optional): Please add any additional comments or suggestions.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* Select all options that apply to your experience.
* If a field is not applicable, select "None" or "Not Applicable".
