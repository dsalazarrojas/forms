# Church Growth Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us assess the current state of your church's growth, identify areas of strength and weakness, and inform our future direction. By filling out this form, you will be providing valuable input to help us make informed decisions about how to improve and grow our church.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions on each page. Each question is designed to help us understand different aspects of church growth, so please answer them as accurately and completely as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How clearly do you understand the church mission?** (`clear_mission_perception`, select_one)
  This question helps us gauge how well we're communicating our church's mission to our members.
  Please select one of the three options: Very Clear, Somewhat Clear, or Unclear.
* **Rate how well our current activities align with our mission** (`mission_alignment_rating`, select_one)
  This question helps us understand how well our actions align with our stated mission.
  Please select one of the four options: Strongly Aligned, Aligned, Neutral, or Misaligned.
* **What are our top 3 strengths as a church?** (`top_church_strengths`, text)
  Think of the areas where we excel. What are our top 3 strengths that support the growth of our church?
  Please enter up to three answers, separated by commas.
* **What are the main barriers to our growth?** (`top_growth_barriers`, text)
  Consider factors like facility limits, lack of volunteers, communication barriers, etc.
  Please enter your answers in free-text format.
* **Rate the effectiveness of our current community outreach** (`outreach_effectiveness_rating`, select_one)
  This question helps us gauge the effectiveness of our outreach efforts.
  Please select one of four options: Highly Effective, Effective, Ineffective, or Unknown.
* **How often do you invite new people to church?** (`invitation_frequency`, select_one)
  This question helps us understand our current invitation habits.
  Please select one of four options: Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never.
* **What tools would help you invite others more easily?** (`tools_needed_for_invitation`, select_multiple)
  Consider tools like invitation cards, better social media presence, more community events, etc.
  Please select all that apply.
* **Where should we invest most in the next 12 months?** (`priority_ministry_investment`, select_one)
  This question helps us prioritize our investments for the next 12 months.
  Please select one of the six options: Children/Youth, Missions/Outreach, Facilities/Property, Small Groups/Discipleship, Staffing, or Media/Technology.
* **Do you feel we have adequate staff for our current size?** (`staffing_needs_perception`, select_one)
  This question helps us gauge our staffing needs.
  Please select one of three options: True, Needs more staff, or Needs different focus.
* **Is our facility ready for 20% growth?** (`facility_growth_readiness`, select_one)
  This question helps us evaluate our facility's ability to support future growth.
  Please select one of three options: True, No - Needs renovation, or No - Needs new location.
* **Are you actively volunteering in a leadership or service role?** (`active_volunteer_status`, select_one)
  This question helps us understand our volunteer engagement levels.
  Please select one of three options: Yes - Leadership, Yes - Service Role, or False.
* **Would you like to serve more if the right opportunity arose?** (`desire_to_serve_more`, select_one)
  This question helps us gauge your willingness to serve.
  Please select one of three options: True, Already at capacity, or False.
* **Any other insights on how we can grow healthily?** (`additional_growth_insights`, text)
  This question is for any additional thoughts or ideas you may have on how we can grow and improve.
  Please enter your answers in free-text format.
