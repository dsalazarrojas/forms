# Creator Payment Setup Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from creators to set up their payment preferences for working with our organization. The form is divided into sections that cover creator details, payment options, payout preferences, onboarding details, and form submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the **Creator Details** section with your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
2. In the **Payment Details** section, select the payment method you would like to use for your payments (e.g., bank transfer, PayPal, etc.).
3. In the **Payout Preferences** section, choose how you would like to receive your payouts (e.g., monthly, weekly, etc.).
4. In the **Onboarding Details** section, provide any additional information about your onboarding experience, such as the date you were onboarded and any specific details about your onboarding process.
5. In the **Form Submissions** section, select how you would like to receive form submissions (e.g., email, PDF, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Creator Details** (`creator_details`): This field is where you can enter your name and contact information.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`): This field is where you can select the payment method you would like to use for your payments.
* **Payout Preferences** (`payout_preferences`): This field is where you can choose how you would like to receive your payouts.
* **Onboarding Details** (`onboarding_details`): This field is where you can provide additional information about your onboarding experience.
* **Form Submissions** (`form_submissions`): This field is where you can select how you would like to receive form submissions.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`): This is the button you click to complete and submit the form.
