# Logo Design Brief Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Logo Design Brief Form is designed to collect information about your logo design preferences and requirements from our design team. This will help us better understand your needs and create a logo that accurately reflects your brand identity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information. You can choose from the provided options for color scheme, logo type, and other questions. If you have any additional comments or information, you can provide them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Input** (`client_input`, text, optional): Enter your company name or a brief description of your business.
* **Color Scheme** (`color_scheme`, select_one, optional): Choose the color scheme you prefer for your logo from the provided options: Red, Blue, Green, Black.
* **Logo Type** (`logo_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of logo you want: Iconic, Wordmark, Letterform, Abstract.
* **Use Icon** (`icon_used`, select_multiple, optional): Check if you want to use an icon in your logo.
* **Logo Size** (`logo_size`, number, optional): Enter the size of the logo you need (e.g., 100x100, 200x200, etc.).
* **Use Color** (`color_used`, select_multiple, optional): Check which colors you want to use in your logo.
* **Logo Position** (`logo_position`, select_multiple, optional): Select where you want the logo to be placed: Left, Right.
* **Color Scheme Description** (`color_scheme_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your color scheme.
* **Logo Type Description** (`logo_type_description`, text, optional): Describe the type of logo you selected.
* **Logo Size Description** (`logo_size_description`, text, optional): Explain the size of the logo you need.
* **Use Icon Description** (`icon_used_description`, text, optional): Describe the icon you want to use.
* **Use Color Description** (`color_used_description`, text, optional): Describe the colors you want to use in your logo.
* **Logo Position Description** (`logo_position_description`, text, optional): Explain where you want the logo to be placed.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Leave any other comments or information about your design preferences.

Note: You can always reach out to us if you have any questions or need further clarification on any of the fields.
