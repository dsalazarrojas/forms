# Adolescent Body Image Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect data on an adolescent's body image and its potential impacts on their daily life.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you are in the correct age group and have not already completed this survey.
2.  Read each section carefully and provide accurate answers.
3.  Choose the correct options based on your experiences and feelings.
4.  If unsure, ask for clarification or contact the survey administrator.
5.  Complete all required fields and provide optional details if possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Age** (`respondent_age`, number, required): Enter your age in years.
*   **Gender identity** (`gender_identity`, select_one, required): Select the option that best describes your gender identity.
*   **Satisfaction with body image** (`satisfaction_with_body`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction over the past month.
*   **Factors influencing body image** (`influence_factors`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the factors that affect your body image.
*   **Eating habits concerns** (`eating_habits_concerns`, select_multiple, required): Select any eating habits that concern you.
*   **Body image impact on daily life** (`body_image_impact`, text, required): Describe how body image affects your school or activities.
*   **Type of support desired** (`support_needed`, select_multiple, required): Select any type of support you would find helpful.
*   **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Provide your contact email for follow-up purposes.
*   **Final comments** (`final_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional information.

## Tips

*   Please answer honestly and accurately based on your experiences and feelings.
*   If unsure, ask for clarification or contact the survey administrator.
*   Complete all required fields and provide optional details if possible.
*   Review your answers before submission to ensure accuracy.
