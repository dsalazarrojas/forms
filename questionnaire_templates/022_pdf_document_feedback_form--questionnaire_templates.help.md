# pdf_document_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about a PDF document. The form will help the document author to understand user perceptions and make improvements and updates to the document template.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your **User Name** in the first field. This will help the author associate your feedback with you.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the second field. This will allow the author to contact you if needed.
3. Enter the **Document Title** in the third field. This should be the title of the PDF document you are providing feedback on.
4. In the **What's your feedback?** field, enter a specific and detailed description of your feedback. Be as concise as possible while still providing useful information.
5. Rate the document by selecting a number from 1 to 5 in the **How would you rate this document?** field.
6. If you have any additional comments about the document, enter them in the **Do you have any comments?** field.
7. If you have any suggestions for improvements, enter them in the **Suggest improvements** field.
8. If you have any specific changes you would like to see in the document template, enter them in the **Suggest changes** field.
9. Enter the **Document ID**. This should match the ID of the document you are providing feedback on.
10. Finally, enter your **Submitted by** in the last field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name**: A text field where you should enter your name.
* **Email**: A field where you should enter your email address.
* **Document Title**: A text field where you enter the title of the document you are providing feedback on.
* **What's your feedback?**: A text field where you should enter a specific and detailed description of your feedback.
* **How would you rate this document?**: A field where you rate the document from 1 to 5.
* **Do you have any comments?**: A text field where you can enter any additional comments you have.
* **Suggest improvements**: A text field where you can enter any suggestions for improvements.
* **Suggest changes**: A text field where you can enter any specific changes you would like to see in the document template.
* **Document ID**: A field where you should enter the ID of the document you are providing feedback on.
* **Submitted by**: A field where you should enter your name.
