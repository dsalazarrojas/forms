# Novel Search Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Novel Search Survey form is designed to gather user experiences and opinions about how they discover new novels.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the form as completely as possible.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How did you discover this novel** (`novel_discovery`, text, required: false): This question is about how you typically find new novels. Please enter your answer in the text field.
* **Favorite genre** (`favorite_genre`, select_one, required: false): This question asks about your favorite genre of novels. Choose the option that best describes your preference.
* **Challenge level** (`challenge_level`, select_multiple, required: false): This question is about the level of difficulty you face when discovering new novels. Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Satisfaction level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, required: false): This question is about your current satisfaction level with discovering new novels. Choose the option that best describes your current state.
* **What challenges do you face when discovering new novels?** (`discover_challenges`, select_multiple, required: false): This question is about any challenges you face when finding new novels to read. Select all that apply from the options provided.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate in your responses.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out for assistance.
