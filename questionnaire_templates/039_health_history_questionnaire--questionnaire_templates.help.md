# Health History Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health History Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information about your medical history, including your current medical condition, prescription medications, and supplements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Details, including any identifying information you want to provide.
2. Answer the Medical History questions, selecting all that apply to your current situation.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Details (user_details)
* **User Details** (`user_details`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any identifying information you want to share, such as your name, contact information, or other relevant details.

### Medical History (medical_history)
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): This field is for you to describe your current medical condition, if any. Be as specific as possible to help with the intended purpose of the form.

### Medical History Questions (medical_history_questions)
* **Medical History Questions** (`medical_history_questions`, select_multiple, required): This field is a multiple-choice question with three options:
	+ What is your current medical condition?
	+ Do you take any prescription medications?
	+ Are you currently taking any supplements?
Select all that apply to your current situation.
