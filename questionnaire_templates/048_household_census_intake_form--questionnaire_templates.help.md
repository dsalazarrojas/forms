# Household Census Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Household Census Intake Form is designed to collect information about the demographics and financial situation of a household. This form is used to gather essential details about the household size, income, location, and other relevant factors to help us better understand the household's needs and provide more effective services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your household size in the first field.
2. Answer the income question accurately, selecting the number that best represents your household's monthly income.
3. Type in your household's monthly rent in the numerical field.
4. Select the frequency at which you pay your household's rent.
5. Choose the type of utilities your household uses.
6. Select any additional services your household uses.
7. Choose your household's primary language.
8. Indicate if your household owns a vehicle.
9. Select the frequency at which you receive income.
10. Select any additional income sources.
11. Select any additional expenses your household has.
12. Indicate any other employment sources.
13. Select any other services your household uses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Household Head (1)**: This field is used to identify the person representing the household, usually the head or a representative. Please enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Household size (2)**: Please select the number of people living in your household.
* **Household income (3)**: Enter your household's monthly income.
* **Household location (4)**: Type in your household's address or location.
* **Household income source (5)**: Select how your household's income is generated.
* **Household employed (6)**: Check if all household members are employed.
* **Household education (7)**: Select the highest level of education among household members.
* **Household employment (8)**: Indicate if any household members are employed.
* **Household rent (9)**: Enter your household's monthly rent.
* **Household rent frequency (10)**: Select how often you pay your rent.
* **Household utilities (11)**: Choose the types of utilities your household uses.
* **Household services (12)**: Select any additional services your household uses.
* **Household language (13)**: Select your household's primary language.
* **Household vehicle (14)**: Indicate if your household owns a vehicle.
* **Household income frequency (15)**: Select how often you receive income.
* **Household other income (16)**: Select any additional income sources.
* **Household other expense (17)**: Select any additional expenses your household has.
* **Household other employment (18)**: Indicate any other employment sources.
* **Household other services (19)**: Select any other services your household uses.
