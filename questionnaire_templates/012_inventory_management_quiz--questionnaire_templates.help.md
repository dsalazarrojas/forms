# Inventory Management Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

This questionnaire helps collect information about your company's inventory management practices. Your input will assist us in understanding your business's strengths and weaknesses in inventory management and help identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions as accurately as possible to provide a true representation of your company's inventory management practices.
2. Each section is self-explanatory, so take your time and carefully review each question.
3. You may skip questions that do not apply to your company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Use**: Please describe how your company uses inventory.
* **Inventory Type**: Select all types of inventory that your company manages. You can select multiple options.
* **Current Inventory Level**: Enter the current quantity of your inventory.
* **Inventory Reorder Point**: Enter the quantity at which you would like to reorder or replenish your inventory.
* **Reorder Quantity**: Enter the quantity you would like to reorder or replenish at the reorder point.
* **Lead Time**: Enter the number of days or weeks it takes for an order to be fulfilled or delivered.
* **Reorder Lead Time**: Enter the number of days or weeks it takes for your inventory to be replenished or reordered.
* **Inventory Manager Role**: Please indicate if this person is responsible for inventory management.
* **Inventory Manager Name**: Enter the name of the person responsible for inventory management.
* **Inventory Manager Email**: Enter the email of the person responsible for inventory management.
* **Inventory Manager Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person responsible for inventory management.
* **Inventory Manager Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments about your inventory management practices.
* **Inventory Manager Comments**: Provide any additional comments about your inventory management practices.

## Tips

* Please be as accurate as possible when completing this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
* If you are unsure about a particular question, feel free to provide a brief explanation to help us understand your company's practices better.
