# Semi Structured Interview Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Semi Structured Interview Guide is a tool used to gather information from participants through a structured and clear approach. The form is designed to guide the interviewer in gathering relevant information and taking notes during the conversation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Introduction** page with a brief description of the participant's background.
2. Select the relevant **Interviewer Details** (HR, Researcher, or Consultant) and any other details you deem necessary.
3. Fill out the **Participant Background** section with either 'True' or 'False' to indicate their level of experience or background in the relevant field.
4. Write down the **Questions** that need to be explored during the interview in the text area provided.
5. Take notes on any additional comments or points you want to remember in the **Notes** section.
6. Add any additional **Interviewer Notes** to keep track of your own thoughts and next actions.
7. Write down any **Follow-up questions** you want to ask the participant.
8. Document any **Post-Interview** actions or next steps in the final section.
9. Summarize any additional **Next Steps** or actions for the participant in the final section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Label:** Introduction
* **Type:** Note
* **Purpose:** To provide a brief description of the participant's background, setting the tone for the interview.
### Interviewer Details
* **Label:** interviewer-details
* **Type:** Select multiple
* **Purpose:** To select the relevant role of the interviewer (HR, Researcher, or Consultant)
### Participant Background
* **Label:** participant-background
* **Type:** Select one
* **Purpose:** To determine the participant's level of experience or background in the relevant field.
### Questions

* **Type:** Text
* **Purpose:** To write down any questions or topics you want to explore with the participant.
### Notes

### Follow-up Questions

### Post-Interview
