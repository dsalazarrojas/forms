This form is a Training Evaluation Questionnaire, designed to collect feedback from participants who have attended a workshop or training session. The goal of this form is to gather information about the participant's experience, the effectiveness of the workshop, and their overall satisfaction with the training.

## Purpose
The form is used to evaluate the effectiveness of a training or workshop session, and to collect feedback from participants. This information will help organizers and facilitators to improve future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each field's purpose.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to provide clear and concise answers to each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Evaluation Questionnaire** (`Training Evaluation Questionnaire`, `note`, optional): This is the main title of the form, indicating what it's about.
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, `text`, optional): Provide your contact information, including your name and email address.
* **Workshop Title** (`workshop_title`, `text`, optional): Enter the title of the workshop you participated in.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you attended the workshop.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your participant badge or ID.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address as it appears on your participant badge or ID.
* **Workshop Facilitator Name** (`workshop_facilitator_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the workshop facilitator.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you rate your overall experience with the workshop, using the scale:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Rating Justification** (`rating_justification`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief explanation for your rating.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Share any additional comments or thoughts you have about the workshop.

## Tips
Remember to answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability. If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
