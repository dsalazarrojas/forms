# Substance Use Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about a patient's substance use history, medical history, and treatment history. The form is intended to be used by healthcare professionals to assess the patient's situation and provide accurate medical care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information in the "Patient Information" section (page 1).
2. Provide a brief description of the patient's medical history in the "Medical History" section (page 2).
3. Share your substance use history, including any relevant details, in the "Substance Use History" section (page 3).
4. If applicable, describe your treatment history, including any relevant details, in the "Treatment History" section (page 4).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (Page 1)**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant identifying information.
* **Medical History (Page 2)**: Describe the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, allergies, or previous medical treatments.
* **Substance Use History (Page 3)**: Share your history with substances, including the types of substances used, frequency and amount of use, and any related health issues.
* **Treatment History (Page 4)**: Describe any previous treatments or therapies you have received for substance use or related health issues.
* **Medical History (Page 5)**: Provide any additional medical information that may be relevant to your substance use history or treatment.
