# Family Cruise Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from family members about their participation in a family cruise. Please answer all questions carefully and accurately to ensure that the family cruise organizer has the correct information to plan a successful and enjoyable trip for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each page of the form carefully and answer all questions that apply to you.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each question.
3. Fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Headcount**: \* Enter the number of family members you are traveling with. This will help the organizer plan for the correct number of people.
  • Please enter a whole number (e.g., 2, 3, 4, etc.).
- **Travel Date**: Enter the date you plan to travel. This will help the organizer schedule the cruise accordingly.
  • Use the format `MM/DD/YYYY` (e.g., 06/15/2024).
- **Special Needs**: Please specify any medical or disability needs that you have. This will help the organizer make necessary arrangements for your comfort and safety.
  • Be as detailed as possible, so the organizer can make necessary accommodations.
- **Travel Preferences**: Select your preferred travel time. This will help the organizer schedule the cruise activities accordingly.
  • Please select one of the available options (9am-10am, 10am-11am, 11am-12pm).
- **Cabin Choice**: Select your preferred cabin type. This will help the organizer assign you to a suitable cabin.
  • Please select one or more of the available options (Standard, Deluxe).
- **Headcount (Confirmed)**: \* This will confirm the number of family members you are traveling with.
  • Please match this number to the Headcount field above.
- **Special Needs (Confirmed)**: \* This will confirm any special needs you have.
  • Please match this to the Special Needs field above.
- **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes you have about your cruise plans.
  • This is an optional field for any extra information you want to provide.

## Tips
- Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the family cruise organizer.
- Please submit the form only once you have finished filling it out to avoid duplication.
