# Template | Fitness Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants interested in joining a fitness program. It will help the program administrators to assess whether the applicant is a good fit for the program and allocate resources effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Make sure to provide your current fitness level, preferred program type, phone number, email address, fitness goals, available days for fitness, hours for fitness per day, and commitment to the program.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your current fitness level?** (`fitness_program_application`): This question helps us understand your current physical condition and how we can tailor the program to your needs.
* **What type of program are you interested in?** (`program_interest`): Please choose the type of program that best suits your interests and goals. If you have any specific preferences or requirements, feel free to provide additional information.
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone`): Please provide your correct phone number where we can contact you.
* **What is your email address?** (`email`): This is the address we'll use for official correspondence.
* **What are your fitness goals?** (`program_goals`): Be specific about what you want to achieve through our program, and we'll do our best to help you get there.
* **How many days a week can you dedicate to fitness?** (`available_days`): Please be honest about how much time you can commit to the program.
* **How many hours can you dedicate to fitness per day?** (`available_hours`): This will help us plan your schedule and allocate resources effectively.
* **How many months can you commit to a program?** (`available_months`): This is crucial in determining the duration of your program and ensuring a smooth transition.
