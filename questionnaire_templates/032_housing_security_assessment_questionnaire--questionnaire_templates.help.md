# Housing Security Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through completing the Housing Security Assessment Questionnaire form. The form is designed to collect information about a resident's housing security situation. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Introduction**: Read through this page to understand what the form is about and what you need to do.
2. **Resident Details**: Select your resident details from the list of options.
3. **Safety Assessment**: Rate your safety concerns by selecting one of the options.
4. **Fire Safety**: Select your level of concern about fire safety.
5. **First Aid**: If you have a first aid kit, please indicate so; otherwise, describe your first aid training.
6. **Emergency Contact**: Provide your emergency contact information.
7. **Additional Comments**: Use this section to provide any additional information or feedback.
8. **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.
