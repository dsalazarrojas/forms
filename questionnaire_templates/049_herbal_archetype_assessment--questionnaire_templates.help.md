# Herbal Archetype Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Herbal Archetype Assessment is a comprehensive questionnaire designed to gather information on various aspects of a person's life, including demographic details, wellness topics, physical and mental health status, relationships, work-life balance, financial status, social connections, self-care, overall satisfaction, and submission and review status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Introduction**: This section provides a brief overview of the form's purpose and importance.
2. **Demographic Questions**: Answer questions about your background, including age, location, and other relevant details.
	* **demographic_questions**: This field is used to gather demographic information, such as age, location, and other relevant details. Please provide accurate information to help us understand your background.
3. **Wellness Topics**: Answer questions about your physical, mental, and emotional well-being.
	* **wellness_topics**: This field is used to gather information about your wellness topics, such as stress levels, energy levels, and other relevant details. Please describe your current state of well-being.
4. **Health Status**: Answer questions about your physical and mental health.
	* **health_status**: This field is used to gather information about your health status, such as any medical conditions you may have. Please provide accurate information about your physical and mental health.
5. **Herbal Principles**: Select all that apply from the list of herbal principles.
	* **herbal_principles**: This field is used to gather information about your understanding and application of herbal principles. Please select all that apply from the list.
6. **Physical, Mental, Spiritual, Emotional, and Energy Balance**: Answer questions about your balance in these areas.
	* **physical_wellness**: This field is used to gather information about your physical balance, such as your exercise frequency. Please select the option that best describes your current state.
	* **mental_wellness**: This field is used to gather information about your mental balance, such as your stress levels. Please select the option that best describes your current state.
	* **spiritual_wellness**: This field is used to gather information about your spiritual balance, such as your connection to nature. Please select the option that best describes your current state.
	* **emotional_wellness**: This field is used to gather information about your emotional balance, such as your relationships. Please select the option that best describes your current state.
	* **energy_balance**: This field is used to gather information about your energy levels, such as your exercise frequency. Please select the option that best describes your current state.
7. **Sleep Pattern and Exercise Frequency**: Answer questions about your sleep and exercise habits.
	* **sleep_pattern**: This field is used to gather information about your sleep pattern. Please select the option that best describes your current sleep schedule.
	* **exercise_frequency**: This field is used to gather information about your exercise frequency. Please select the option that best describes your current exercise habits.
8. **Nutrition Plan**: Answer questions about your nutrition plan.
	* **nutrition_plan**: This field is used to gather information about your nutrition plan, such as your diet. Please describe your current nutrition plan.
9. **Relaxation Techniques**: Answer questions about your relaxation techniques.
	* **relaxation_techniques**: This field is used to gather information about your relaxation techniques, such as meditation. Please describe your current relaxation techniques.
10. **Relationships Status**: Answer questions about your relationships.
	* **relationships_status**: This field is used to gather information about your relationships, such as your social connections. Please select the option that best describes your current relationships status.
11. **Work-Life Balance**: Answer questions about your work-life balance.
	* **work_life_balance**: This field is used to gather information about your work-life balance, such as your work hours. Please select the option that best describes your current work-life balance.
12. **Financial Status**: Answer questions about your financial status.
	* **financial_status**: This field is used to gather information about your financial status, such as your income. Please provide accurate information about your current financial status.
13. **Social Connection**: Answer questions about your social connections.
	* **social_connection**: This field is used to gather information about your social connections, such as your social media presence. Please select the option that best describes your current social connections.
14. **Self-Care Plan**: Answer questions about your self-care plan.
	* **self_care_plan**: This field is used to gather information about your self-care plan, such as your exercise routine. Please describe your current self-care plan.
15. **Overall Satisfaction**: Answer questions about your overall satisfaction.
	* **overall_satisfaction**: This field is used to gather information about your overall satisfaction, such as your happiness. Please rate your overall satisfaction level.
16. **Submission Status**: Answer questions about your submission status.
	* **submission_status**: This field is used to gather information about your submission status, such as your willingness to submit. Please select the option that best describes your current submission status.
17. **Submission Notes**: Provide any additional notes about your submission status.
	* **submission_notes**: This field is used to gather information about your submission notes, such as any comments or questions you have. Please provide any additional notes or comments about your submission status.
18. **Review Status**: Answer questions about your review status.
	* **review_status**: This field is used to gather information about your review status, such as your willingness to review. Please select the option that best describes your current review status.
19. **Review Notes**: Provide any additional notes about your review status.
	* **review_notes**: This field is used to gather information about your review notes, such as any comments or questions you have. Please provide any additional notes or comments about your review status.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
- Please select all that apply for the multiple-choice questions.
- If you are unsure about any question, please provide additional information in the comments or notes fields.
- If you need help or have questions, please contact us.
