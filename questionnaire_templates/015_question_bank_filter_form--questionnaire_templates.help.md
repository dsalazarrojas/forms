# Question Bank Filter Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Question Bank Filter Form is designed to help you filter and refine a question bank to suit your specific needs. This form allows you to narrow down a large question bank by selecting specific attributes such as subject, grade level, and instructional needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the Question Bank Id of the question bank you want to filter.
2. Choose one or more subjects from the list of available subjects (e.g., Math, Science, English, Social Studies).
3. Choose a grade level from the list of available grade levels (e.g., 6th Grade, 7th Grade, 8th Grade, 9th Grade, 10th Grade, 11th Grade, 12th Grade, High School).
4. Optionally, provide a specific instructional need or enter a free-form description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Question Bank Id**: Enter the unique identifier of the question bank you want to filter. This field is optional.
* **Subject**: Select one or more subjects from the list of available subjects to narrow down your search. The available subjects are:
	+ Math
	+ Science
	+ English
	+ Social Studies
* **Grade**: Choose a grade level to filter questions based on their intended age range. Select from:
	+ 6th Grade
	+ 7th Grade
	+ 8th Grade
	+ 9th Grade
	+ 10th Grade
	+ 11th Grade
	+ 12th Grade
	+ High School
* **Instructional Needs**: If you have a specific instructional need or a particular requirement for the questions, enter a brief description. This is an optional field.
* **Subject Subtopics**: This field is currently not included in the form but is listed in the schema.

## Tips

* Make sure to select a valid question bank id if you want to filter questions within a specific question bank.
* You can select more than one subject to filter questions across multiple subjects.
* You can enter a specific instructional need or description to filter questions that meet a particular requirement.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can always contact the support team for assistance.
