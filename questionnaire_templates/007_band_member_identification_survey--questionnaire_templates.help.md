# Band Member Identification Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Band Member Identification Survey is a form designed to collect personal and musical details from potential band members. This information will help the band leader identify suitable candidates and make informed decisions about auditions and band composition.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the personal details section, which includes your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. In the Musical Role and Experience section, select your primary instrument and list any secondary instruments you play.
3. Choose your role in the band, such as Lead Performer or Rhythm Section.
4. Enter your years of musical experience and formal training background.
5. Indicate your availability for rehearsals and performances, including your willingness to travel.
6. Describe your musical goals and interests.
7. Provide the emergency contact name and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for emergency contact and scheduling purposes.
* **Primary Instrument**: Select the instrument you play best, such as Guitar, Drums, or Vocals.
* **Secondary Instruments**: List any other instruments you play at a performance level.
* **Role in the Band**: Choose your primary function, such as Lead Performer, Rhythm Section, or Supporting Musician.
* **Years of Musical Experience**: Enter the total years you have played your primary instrument.
* **Formal Training Background**: Select the highest level of music education you have completed, such as Self-Taught, Private Lessons, or University Degree.
* **Available Weekly Rehearsal Hours**: Enter the number of hours you can commit per week for rehearsals and performances.
* **Are you willing to travel for performances?**: Select how far you are willing to travel for performances.
* **Do you own professional-grade performance gear?**: Indicate whether you have professional-grade instruments, amplifiers, and other equipment.
* **Musical Goals and Interests**: Briefly describe your goals and interests as a musician.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
