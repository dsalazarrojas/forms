# Disability Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a student's medical conditions, history, and treatment. It's meant to assess the student's functional capacity and impact of their medical conditions on their daily life. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Student Patient Information" page from the top navigation bar.
2. Fill out the "Student Patient Information" form with the required details.
3. Select the "Medical Conditions" page from the navigation bar.
4. List your current medical conditions.
5. For each medical condition listed, fill out the corresponding form fields, such as "Medical Treatment", "Medical History", etc.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Patient Information** (`student_patient_info`, text, optional): This section is to provide general information about the student, such as their name, date of birth, etc.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List your current medical conditions, if applicable.
* **Disability Assessment** (`disability_assessment`, text, optional): This is a text field for any disability assessment related comments.
* **Functional Impact** (`functional_impact`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select 'True' if your medical conditions impact your daily life.
	+ Select 'False' if your medical conditions do not impact your daily life.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your medical history.
* **Mental Health** (`mental_health`, text, optional): Provide a description of your mental health status.
* **Other Health Issues** (`other_health_issues`, text, optional): Provide any other health issues or concerns you'd like to discuss.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, text, optional): This section is for providing information about your education background.
* **Medical Professional Info** (`medical_professional_info`, text, optional): This section is for providing information about your medical professional's contact information.
* **Medication Use** (`medication_use`, text, optional): List your current medication use.
* **Medical History Family** (`medical_history_family`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your family medical history.
* **Mental Health Family** (`mental_health_family`, text, optional): Provide a description of your family mental health status.
* **Functional Capacity** (`functional_capacity`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select 'True' if you have reduced functional capacity due to your medical conditions.
	+ Select 'False' if you do not have reduced functional capacity.
* **Medical Treatment** (`medical_treatment`, text, optional): Provide a description of your medical treatment.
* **Other Medical Treatment** (`medical_treatment_other`, text, optional): Provide any other medical treatment information.
* **Medical Treatment History** (`medical_treatment_history`, text, optional): Provide a description of your medical treatment history.
* **Other Treatment History** (`other_treatment_history`, text, optional): Provide a description of your other treatment history.
* **Other Medical History** (`medical_history_other`, text, optional): Provide any other medical history information.
* **Mental Health History** (`mental_health_history`, text, optional): Provide a description of your mental health history.
* **Medical History Medication** (`medical_history_medication`, text, optional): Provide a description of your medical history related to medication use.
* **Education History** (`education_history`, text, optional): Provide a description of your education history.
* **Other Medical History** (`medical_history_other`, text, optional): Provide any other medical history information.
* **Medical Condition History** (`medical_condition_history`, text, optional): Provide a description of your medical condition history.
* **Medical History Allergies** (`medical_history_allergies`, text, optional): Provide a description of your medical history related to allergies.
* **Medical History Surgeries** (`medical_history_surgeries`, text, optional): Provide a description of your medical history related to surgeries.
