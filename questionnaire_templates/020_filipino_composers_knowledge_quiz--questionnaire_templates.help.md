# filipino_composers_knowledge_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Filipino Composers Knowledge Quiz is a form designed to collect information about Filipino composers. The form is used to gather knowledge about Filipino composers for research or educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the name of the composer.
2. Provide the birth date of the composer.
3. List the musical works of the composer.
4. Select the musical style of the composer.
5. Choose the music genres of the composer.
6. Fill in the birth place of the composer.
7. Fill in the birth country of the composer.
8. List the musical instruments played by the composer.
9. List the musical collaborations of the composer.
10. Fill in the musical awards received by the composer.
11. Select the musical genres of the composer.
12. Fill in the birth year of the composer.
13. Fill in the composer's name (up to 4 options).
14. Fill in the composer's name for each option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Composer Name**: Enter the full name of the composer.
* **Birth Date**: Enter the date of birth of the composer.
* **Musical Works**: List the notable musical works of the composer.
* **Composer Birth Year**: Enter the year of birth of the composer.
* **Musical Style**: Select the style of music that the composer is known for (classical, contemporary, folk, etc.).
* **Music Genre**: Select the type of music that the composer specializes in (jazz, classical, pop, etc.).
* **Birth Place**: Enter the location where the composer was born.
* **Birth Country**: Enter the country where the composer was born.
* **Musical Instruments**: List the musical instruments played by the composer.
* **Musical Collaborations**: List the musical collaborations of the composer.
* **Musical Awards**: List the musical awards received by the composer.
* **Musical Genres**: Select the genres of music that the composer is known for (jazz, classical, folk, etc.).
* **Birth Year**: Enter the year of birth of the composer.
* **Composer Name 1**: Enter the name of the composer (if applicable).
* **Composer Name 2**: Enter the name of the composer (if applicable).
* **Composer Name 3**: Enter the name of the composer (if applicable).
* **Composer Name 4**: Enter the name of the composer (if applicable).

Note: Please fill in the required fields accurately and completely to ensure that the collected data is reliable and useful.
