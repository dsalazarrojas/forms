# Word Puzzle Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Word Puzzle Quiz" form is designed to collect user responses to a series of yes/no questions. The form is intended to be a simple and quick assessment tool that gauges the user's understanding or opinion on various topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the "name" and "email" fields, respectively. Although these fields are not required, they can be useful if you want to provide contact information.
2. For each question (labelled as "question 1", "question 2", "question 3", "question 4", and "question 5"), select one or multiple options that best answer the question. The questions are designed to be self-explanatory, so make sure to read each question carefully before answering.
3. Once you have completed all the questions, click the "submit" button to submit your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (text, optional): Enter your name if you wish to provide contact information.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter your email address if you wish to provide contact information.
* **phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number if you wish to provide contact information. (Note: This field could be more descriptive, such as "Contact Number")
* **question 1** (select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple options that best answer question 1.
* **question 2** (select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple options that best answer question 2.
* **question 3** (select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple options that best answer question 3.
* **question 4** (select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple options that best answer question 4.
* **question 5** (select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple options that best answer question 5.
* **submit** (text, optional): Click to submit your responses. (Note: This field seems to be a placeholder for a submission button.)

## Tips
* Make sure to read each question carefully before answering to ensure you understand what is being asked.
* You can select one or multiple options for each question, depending on your response.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form administrators for assistance.
