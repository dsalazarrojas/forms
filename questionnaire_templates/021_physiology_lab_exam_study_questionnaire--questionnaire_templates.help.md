# physiology_lab_exam_study_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "physiology_lab_exam_study_questionnaire" form is designed to collect information and feedback from students about their experiences and challenges while preparing for a lab exam in a physiology course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. For "Which course section are you taking?" select the option that best describes your current or upcoming course section.
3. Enter your academic year of the course.
4. Leave any additional feedback or comments you have about lab exam preparation in the text field.
5. If you have an email address, provide it so we can follow up with you.
6. If you have a phone number, provide it so we can contact you.
7. If you're taking the exam soon, enter the date and time of the exam.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is your biggest challenge in preparing for the exam?** (`physiology_lab_exam_preparation`, text, optional): Describe your greatest challenge in preparing for the lab exam.
- **Which course section are you taking?** (`course_section`, select_one, required): Select the course section you are currently or will be taking.
- **Academic year of the course** (`academic_year`, number, required): Enter the academic year of the course.
- **What feedback do you have for our team on lab exam preparation?** (`feedback`, note, optional): Share any feedback or suggestions you have for improving lab exam preparation resources.
- **Email address** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
- **Phone number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
- **Date of exam** (`date`, date, optional): If you're taking the exam soon, enter the date and time of the exam.
- **Time of exam** (`time`, time, optional): If you're taking the exam soon, enter the time of the exam.
- **Any other comments?** (`other_comments`, text, optional): Leave any additional comments or questions you have.
