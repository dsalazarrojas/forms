# Volunteer Program Effectiveness Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the effectiveness of a volunteer program. It is meant to be completed by individuals who have participated in or have knowledge about the program, such as volunteers, program staff, or community members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **General Information** page (Page 1).
2. Provide an overview of the program on this page.
3. Move on to the **Volunteer Information** page (Page 2). Here, you will be asked about the demographics and characteristics of the volunteers who participate in the program.
4. If you have information about the program quality, feel free to share it on the **Program Quality** page (Page 3).
5. Finally, reflect on the community impact of the program on the **Community Impact** page (Page 4). Share any information about how the program affects the community it serves.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Information
* **General Information** (Page 1): This page is a brief description of the volunteer program. Please provide a concise and clear summary of the program's mission, goals, and objectives. (< 100 words)

### Volunteer Information
* **Volunteer Information** (Page 2, `volunteer_info`, text): On this page, please provide information about the volunteers who participate in the program. This may include demographics like age, location, or profession. Be as specific as possible to help us understand the volunteer pool.

### Program Quality
* **Program Quality** (Page 3, `program_quality`, text, optional): If you have any information about the program's quality, please share it here. This could include aspects like the program's effectiveness in meeting its goals, the qualifications of the volunteers, or any notable achievements.

### Community Impact
* **Community Impact** (Page 4, `community_impact`, text, optional): On this page, please describe the impact the program has on the community it serves. This may include how the program addresses social issues, fosters community engagement, or supports local development. Be as detailed as possible to help us understand the program's effect on the community.

## Tips

* Please answer each question as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
* If you are unsure about any information, feel free to ask for clarification or guidance.
* The more specific and detailed your responses, the more helpful this form will be in assessing the program's effectiveness.
* If you have any additional information or comments about the program, please include them in the space provided at the end of the form.
