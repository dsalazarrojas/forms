# Sperm Donor Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant information about your medical history, health status, medical eligibility, and other important details related to sperm donation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
2. Select the correct options for each field as requested.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Information** (`donor_info`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect your basic contact information.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect your contact information, which will be used to get in touch with you for further instructions.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, optional): In this field, please describe any significant medical events or conditions that have affected your health.
* **Health Status** (`health_status`, `text`, optional): Here, you can describe your current health status, including any ongoing medical conditions or treatments.
* **Medical Eligibility** (`medical_eligibility`, `select_one`, required): Please select your medical eligibility status:
	+ **Eligible**: You are eligible to donate sperm.
	+ **Not Eligible**: You are not eligible to donate sperm due to medical reasons.
	+ **Pending**: Your eligibility is pending evaluation.
* **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Tests** (`medical_tests`, `text`, optional): Describe any medical tests you have undergone or are scheduled to undergo.
* **Blood Donation** (`blood_donation`, `checkbox`, required): Please indicate if you have donated blood in the past or are currently a blood donor:
	+ **Donor**: You have donated blood in the past or are currently a blood donor.
	+ **Not Donor**: You have not donated blood in the past or are not a blood donor.
* **Organ Donation** (`organ_donation`, `checkbox`, required): Please indicate if you are eligible to donate organs:
	+ **Donor**: You are eligible to donate organs.
	+ **Not Donor**: You are not eligible to donate organs.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): In this field, list any ongoing or past medical conditions that may affect your eligibility to donate.
* **Eligibility for Surrogacy** (`medical_eligibility_1`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select your eligibility status for surrogacy:
	+ **Eligible**: You are eligible for surrogacy.
	+ **Not Eligible**: You are not eligible for surrogacy.
	+ **Pending**: Your eligibility is pending evaluation.
* **Eligibility for Egg Donation** (`medical_eligibility_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select your eligibility status for egg donation:
	+ **Eligible**: You are eligible for egg donation.
	+ **Not Eligible**: You are not eligible for egg donation.
	+ **Pending**: Your eligibility is pending evaluation.

## Tips
