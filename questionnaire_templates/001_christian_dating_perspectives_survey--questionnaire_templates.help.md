# Christian Dating Perspectives Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information on your views and experiences related to dating, relationships, and faith. Your responses will help us create resources and guidance for community leaders to support and counsel individuals in the Christian community.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the "Submit" button after filling out all the required fields.
2. If you need to skip a field, make sure you have selected the "Skip" option.
3. Use a computer or mobile device to access this survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### -- Personal Information --

* **Full name**: Please provide your first and last name.
* **Email address**: Please enter a valid email address that will be used for follow up or study results.
* **Age group**: Select the age range that applies to you.
* **Relationship status**: Choose the description that best fits your current situation.
* **Church attendance frequency**: Select how often you attend church services.
* **Importance of shared faith**: Rate the importance of shared faith in a partner, from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **Preferred communication methods**: Select all methods you would use to communicate with a partner. (Optional)
* **Main concerns when dating**: Briefly describe any concerns or priorities you have when dating. (Optional)
* **Additional comments or suggestions**: If you have any feedback or suggestions for community leaders, please include them here. (Optional)

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields (marked with \*).
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
* Your responses will be kept confidential and used to create resources to support the Christian community.
