# Microsoft Discovery Questions Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Microsoft Discovery Questions Form is designed to gather essential information about a business, enabling you to analyze its market position, customer base, and competitors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and fill in the required information for the correct business.
2. For each field, provide accurate and concise answers.
3. Do not fill out the form for the same business multiple times.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Business Description** (text)
• A brief description of your business, including its products/services and main offerings.

### 2. **Business Location** (text)
• The physical or virtual location of your business, including city and country.

### 3. **Business Size** (text)
• The number of employees or size of your business, if applicable.

### 4. **Business Industry** (text)
• A brief description of your business's primary industry or niche.

### 5. **Customer Relationship** (text)
• A description of your business's customer relationship, e.g., B2B, B2C, etc.

### 6. **Customer Interaction** (text)
• A description of how customers interact with your business, e.g., online, offline, etc.

### 7. **Business Products/Services** (text)
• A description of the products or services offered by your business.

### 8. **Target Audience** (text)
• A description of your business's target audience, including demographics and characteristics.

### 9. **Competition** (text)
• A brief description of your business's main competitors, including their strengths and weaknesses.

### 10. **Customer Feedback** (text)
• Any feedback or reviews from customers, which can be positive or negative.

Tips:
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask for help or clarification.
* This form is a tool for gathering information, and your participation is greatly appreciated.
* You can modify this form as needed to suit your business needs.
