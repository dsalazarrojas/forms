# Prophecy And Authority Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will assist you in completing the Prophecy and Authority Survey. The survey is designed to gather information about your experiences and beliefs related to prophecies and spiritual authority.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully, providing the best information possible about your experiences and opinions.
2. Select all relevant options that apply to your experiences or beliefs.
3. For text fields, provide as much detail as you can about your answers.
4. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose all that apply from the options provided.
5. For date, time, and number fields, ensure that your entries follow the correct format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Survey Questions

* **Survey Questions** (`survey_questions`, `text`, optional): This field is a free-text area where you can provide any questions or topics you have regarding prophecies and spiritual authority. 

### 2. Questions And Answers

* **Questions And Answers** (`questions_and_answers`, `select_one`, optional): This field presents four options related to your understanding of prophecies and spiritual authority. Select all that apply to indicate your understanding.
  - How do you understand the concept of spiritual authority?
  - What is your understanding of the relationship between prophecies and spiritual authority?
  - Have you ever experienced any prophecies in your life?
  - Do you believe the Bible is the ultimate authority in all matters of faith and practice?

### 3. Spiritual Authority

* **Spiritual Authority** (`spiritual_authority`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field allows you to select one or more sources of spiritual authority that you believe in. You can choose from the following options:
  - The Bible
  - The Holy Spirit
  - The church or denomination
  - Other (please specify)

### 4. Personal Experiences

* **Personal Experiences** (`personal_experiences`, `text`, optional): In this field, provide any personal experiences you have had with prophecies, including details about the prophecies and how they affected you.

### 5. Other Influences

* **Other Influences** (`other_influences`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field presents options for other sources of influence that may have contributed to your experiences with prophecies. Select all that apply:
  - Family
  - Friends
  - Social Media
  - Other (please specify)

### 6. Additional Comments

* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, optional): This field is for any additional comments or thoughts you have about your experiences and beliefs related to prophecies and spiritual authority.

### 7. Email

* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): In this field, enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive more information.

### 8. Phone

* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone for follow-up questions or to receive more information.

### 9. Date Of Last Prophecy

* **Date Of Last Prophecy** (`date_of_last_prophecy`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your last prophetic experience if you are willing to share that information.

### 10. Time Of Last Prophecy

* **Time Of Last Prophecy** (`time_of_last_prophecy`, `time`, optional): If you are willing to share, enter the time of your last prophetic experience.

### 11. Frequency Of Prophecies

* **Frequency Of Prophecies** (`frequency_of_prophecies`, `number`, optional): If you have experienced prophecies, select the frequency of these events.

### 12. Duration Of Last Prophecy

* **Duration Of Last Prophecy** (`duration_of_last_prophecy`, `number`, optional): If you are willing to share, select the duration of your last prophetic experience.

### 13. Impact Of Last Prophecy

* **Impact Of Last Prophecy** (`impact_of_last_prophecy`, `number`, optional): Indicate the impact of your last prophetic experience by selecting a number.

### 14. Other Influences (2)

* **Other Influences** (`other_influences_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is similar to option 5, with the following options:
  - Family
  - Friends
  - Social Media
  - Other (please specify)

### 15. Other Comments

* **Other Comments** (`other_comments`, `note`, optional): This field is for any additional comments or thoughts you have about your experiences and beliefs related to prophecies and spiritual authority.
