# Depression Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Depression Questionnaire is a comprehensive tool designed to help healthcare professionals assess the severity and presence of depressive symptoms in individuals. It's essential to use this tool only for those who have been screened for potential depression risk factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions honestly, and select one option from the dropdown menu that best describes your current state or condition. If you have any difficulty completing a question, please consult the Field-by-Field Explanation section below for further guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction**: A brief overview of the questionnaire and its purpose. Please read this section carefully to understand the context of the questions that follow.

### Depression Scale
* **Depression Scale**: A numerical scale to assess the severity of depressive symptoms. Please select a number from 0 to 10 to indicate the severity of your symptoms, where 0 is "Not Depressed" and 10 is "Very Depressed".

### Depressive Mood
* **Depressive Mood**: A text field to describe your current mood. Please type a few sentences to explain how you've been feeling lately.

### Depressive Symptoms
* **Depressive Symptoms**: A multiple-choice list to select all symptoms that apply to you. Please choose all that describe your current condition.
	+ 'Yes' for symptoms you are experiencing
	+ 'No' for symptoms you are not experiencing

### Feelings
* **Feelings**: A single-choice list to select one that best describes your current feelings.
	+ 'Yes' for experiencing the emotion
	+ 'No' for not experiencing the emotion

### Thoughts
* **Thoughts**: A single-choice list to select one that best describes your current thoughts.
	+ 'Yes' for experiencing the thought
	+ 'No' for not experiencing the thought

### Sleep
* **Sleep**: A numerical scale to assess the quality of your sleep. Please select a number from 0 to 10, where 0 is "Poor" and 10 is "Excellent".

### Energy
* **Energy**: A numerical scale to assess your energy levels. Please select a number from 0 to 10, where 0 is "Low" and 10 is "High".

### Appetite
* **Appetite**: A single-choice list to select one that best describes your current appetite.
	+ 'Yes' for experiencing an appetite change
	+ 'No' for not experiencing an appetite change

### Weight
* **Weight**: A numerical scale to assess your weight changes. Please select a number from 0 to 10, where 0 is "Significant Weight Loss" and 10 is "No Change".

### Social
* **Social**: A numerical scale to assess your social interaction. Please select a number from 0 to 10, where 0 is "Rarely Interact" and 10 is "Frequent Interaction".

### Relationships
* **Relationships**: A text field to describe your relationships. Please type a few sentences to explain your current relationships.

### Work Performance
* **Work Performance**: A numerical scale to assess your work performance. Please select a number from 0 to 10, where 0 is "Poor" and 10 is "Excellent".

### Self-Worth
* **Self-Worth**: A multiple-choice list to select all that apply to you. Please choose all that describe your current self-worth perception.
	+ 'Yes' for experiencing self-worth changes
	+ 'No' for not experiencing self-worth changes

### Self-Care
* **Self-Care**: A text field to describe your self-care activities. Please type a few sentences to explain how you take care of yourself.

### Suicidal Thoughts
* **Suicidal Thoughts**: A single-choice list to select one that best describes your suicidal thoughts.
	+ 'Yes' for experiencing suicidal thoughts
	+ 'No' for not experiencing suicidal thoughts

### Suicidal Plans
* **Suicidal Plans**: A single-choice list to select one that best describes your suicidal plans.
	+ 'Yes' for having suicidal plans
	+ 'No' for not having suicidal plans

### Suicidal Attempts
* **Suicidal Attempts**: A single-choice list to select one that best describes your suicidal attempts.
	+ 'Yes' for having suicidal attempts
	+ 'No' for not having suicidal attempts

### Suicidal Danger
* **Suicidal Danger**: A single-choice list to select one that best describes your suicidal danger.
	+ 'Yes' for feeling suicidal danger
	+ 'No' for not feeling suicidal danger

### Medical History
* **Medical History**: A text field to describe your medical history. Please type a few sentences to explain any health conditions you have.

### Family History
* **Family History**: A text field to describe your family history. Please type a few sentences to explain any mental health conditions or history of mental health issues in your family.

### Personal History
* **Personal History**: A text field to describe your personal history. Please type a few sentences to explain any major life events or experiences.

### Mental Health History
* **Mental Health History**: A text field to describe your mental health history. Please type a few sentences to explain any mental health conditions or issues you have.

### Medication
* **Medication**: A text field to describe any medications you are currently taking. Please type a few sentences to explain your current medication regimen.

### Notes
* **Notes**: A text field for any additional comments or concerns. Please type a few sentences to explain any further information that may be helpful for your healthcare provider.

## Tips
- Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
- Take your time and complete the form at your own pace.
- If you're feeling overwhelmed or unsure about any question, please take a break and return to it later.
- This form is meant to help you and your healthcare provider better understand your mental health, so feel free to ask for help if you need it!
