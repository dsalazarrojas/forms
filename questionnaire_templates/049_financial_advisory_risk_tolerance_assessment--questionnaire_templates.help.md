# Financial Advisory Risk Tolerance Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Advisory Risk Tolerance Assessment is a form designed to gauge an investor's risk tolerance and provide a comprehensive picture of their financial goals, objectives, and risk attitudes. This assessment helps financial advisors and planners understand an investor's needs and preferences, ensuring they receive suitable investment recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the appropriate options for each field based on your personal preferences and circumstances.
2.  Answer each question truthfully, as the information you provide will help us understand your investment needs.
3.  If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Risk** (`risk_tolerance`, select_multiple): Select the level of financial risk you are willing to take:
	+ Low: You are risk-averse and prioritize stability over potential gains.
	+ Medium: You are willing to take moderate risk for potential returns.
	+ High: You are risk-tolerant and willing to take higher risk for potentially higher returns.
* **investment_objective** (`investment_objective`, select_one): What is your primary investment objective?
	+ Conservative: You aim for steady returns and low volatility.
	+ Aggressive: You seek high returns, even if it means higher risk.
	+ Balanced: You aim for a mix of stability and potential for higher returns.
* **Timeframe** (`timeframe`, select_one): How long do you plan to keep your investment for?
	+ Short term: You aim for returns within 1-5 years.
	+ Medium term: You aim for returns within 5-10 years.
	+ Long term: You aim for returns within 10 years or more.
* **Investment Horizon** ( `investment_hozren`, text): Describe your long-term investment horizon or goals.
* **Financial Goals** (`financial_goals`, text): Describe your short-term and long-term financial goals.
* **Financial Status** (`financial_status`, select_one): Your current financial status:
	+ Employed: You have a steady income.
	+ Unemployed: You are currently without a steady income.
	+ Retired: You have a steady income through retirement or pension.
* **Income Level** (`income_level`, number): Enter your monthly income.
* **Investment Experience** (`investment_experience`, select_one): How much experience do you have with investments?
	+ Beginner: You have limited experience with investments.
	+ Intermediate: You have some experience with investments.
	+ Advanced: You have extensive experience with investments.
* **Investment Frequency** (`investment_frequency`, select_one): How often do you invest?
	+ Rarely: You invest infrequently.
	+ Occasionally: You invest occasionally.
	+ Regularly: You invest regularly.
* **Financial Independence** (`financial_independence`, select_one): How independent are you financially?
	+ High: You have a high level of financial independence.
	+ Medium: You have some financial independence.
	+ Low: You have limited financial independence.
* **Risk Attitude** (`risk_attitude`, select_one): What is your general attitude towards risk?
	+ Risk Averse: You are risk-averse and prefer lower risk.
	+ Risk Neutral: You are neutral towards risk.
	+ Risk Taker: You are willing to take higher risk.
* **Time Preference** (`time_preference`, select_one): What time of day do you prefer to work on financial matters?
	+ Morning: You prefer to work on financial matters during the morning.
	+ Afternoon: You prefer to work on financial matters during the afternoon.
	+ Evening: You prefer to work on financial matters during the evening.
* **Financial Assets** (`financial_assets`, number): Enter your current financial assets.
* **Investment Return** (`investment_return`, number): Enter your expected investment return.
* **Cash Flow** (`cash_flow`, text): Describe your cash flow or income.
* **Financial Insights** (`financial_insights`, text): Describe any relevant financial insights or considerations.
