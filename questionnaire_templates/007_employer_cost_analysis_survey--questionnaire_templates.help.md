# Employer Cost Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employer Cost Analysis Survey form is designed to collect information about an employer's labor costs, benefits, and expenses. This data will be used to help understand the overall cost structure of the employer and aid in making informed decisions for budgeting and financial planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Make sure to fill out each field accurately and completely.
3. If a field is not applicable, please leave it blank instead of guessing or making assumptions.
4. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Labor Cost Categories** (`labor_cost_categories`, text, optional): This field is for listing the different categories of labor costs that the employer incurs, such as salaries, wages, or benefits.
* **Labor Benefits** (`labor_benefits`, select_one, optional): This field is for selecting one of the available labor benefits that the employer provides, such as Labor Union Fees or Stock Options.
* **Employer Expenses** (`employer_expenses`, number, optional): This field is for entering a numerical value for the employer's expenses, such as rent or utilities.
* **Labor Expense Categories** (`labor_expense_categories`, text, optional): This field is for listing the different categories of labor expenses that the employer incurs, such as travel or training.
* **Benefits and Incentives** (`benefits_incentives`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for selecting multiple labor benefits and incentives that the employer provides, such as Stock Options or Pensions.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, text, optional): This field is for describing any challenges that the employer faces, such as regulatory issues or financial constraints.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): This field is for providing any additional comments or feedback about the form or the data collected.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is for entering the employer's email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): This field is for entering the employer's phone number for communication purposes.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): This field is for specifying the tool assigned to the employer for their cost analysis.
* **Assigned Form Ids** (`assigned_form_ids`, text, optional): This field is for listing the IDs of forms assigned to the employer.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, optional): This field is for specifying the user assigned to the employer's cost analysis.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, date, optional): This field is for displaying the date and time the form was created.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, date, optional): This field is for displaying the date and time the form was last updated.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, date, optional): This field is for displaying the date and time the form was submitted.
* **Form Id** (`form_id`, text, optional): This field is for displaying the ID of the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to provide the best possible data for analysis.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your assigned user or tool for clarification.
* Please review the form before submission to ensure that all necessary fields are completed.
