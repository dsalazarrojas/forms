# market_positioning_readiness_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess a company's market positioning readiness.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page as accurately and completely as possible.
2. For each field, enter the relevant information about your target segments and brand positioning.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **target_segments** (`target_segments`, text, required: false): Describe the target segments your product or service is aimed at.
* **brand_positioning** (`brand_positioning`, text, required: false): Describe the brand positioning of your product or service.
* **strategy_maturity** (`strategy_maturity`, number, required: false): Enter a number indicating the level of maturity in your market positioning strategy (e.g., 1-10).
* **target_segments_2** (`target_segments_2`, text, required: false): Describe the target segments for the second set of questions.

## Tips
* Please answer each field as thoroughly as possible, as this information will be used to help inform market positioning decisions.
