# Portrait Questionnaire For Women - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Portrait Questionnaire For Women" is a form designed to gather information about your needs and preferences for a photo shoot. It helps our photographers understand your expectations and provide the best experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (page 1)** (`Client Information`, `text`, required/optional): 
This field is where you can provide your name, address, and phone number. Please make sure to fill it out as completely and accurately as possible.
* **Shoot Details (page 1)** (`Shoot Details`, `text`, required/optional): 
Please select the type of shoot you are looking for (e.g. glamour, fashion, commercial, etc.).
* **Contact Method (page 1)** (`Contact Method`, `text`, required/optional): 
Choose your preferred method of contact (e.g. phone, email, or mail).
* **Contact Information (page 2)** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required/optional): 
Provide your email address or phone number.
* **Photo Location (page 2)** (`Photo Location`, `text`, required/optional): 
Tell us where the shoot will take place.
* **Photo Date and Time (page 2)** (`Photo Date and Time`, `date/number`, required/optional): 
Choose the date and time you are available for the shoot.
* **Photo Style (page 3)** (`Photo Style`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): 
Select the style of the shoot (e.g. glamour, fashion, commercial, etc.).
* **Special Requests (page 3)** (`Special Requests`, `text`, required/optional): 
If you have any special requests or notes, please let us know.
* **Photo Notes (page 4)** (`Photo Notes`, `note`, required/optional): 
Add any additional notes about the shoot.
* **Shoot Notes (page 4)** (`Shoot Notes`, `note`, required/optional): 
Add any additional notes about the shoot.
* **Special Requests Notes (page 5)** (`Special Requests Notes`, `note`, required/optional): 
Add any additional notes about any special requests.
* **Contact Address (page 5)** (`Contact Address`, `text`, required/optional): 
Provide your address.
* **Contact City (page 5)** (`Contact City`, `text`, required/optional): 
Enter your city.
* **Contact State (page 5)** (`Contact State`, `text`, required/optional): 
Enter your state.
* **Contact Zip (page 5)** (`Contact Zip`, `text`, required/optional): 
Enter your zip code.
* **Photo Camera (page 5)** (`Photo Camera`, `text`, required/optional): 
Let us know the camera you will be using.
* **Photo Equipment (page 5)** (`Photo Equipment`, `text`, required/optional): 
Let us know the equipment you will be using.
* **Photo Model (page 5)** (`Photo Model`, `text`, required/optional): 
Let us know the model you will be using.
* **Photo Studio (page 5)** (`Photo Studio`, `text`, required/optional): 
Let us know the studio you will be using.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out this form completely and accurately. If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please don't hesitate to ask.
