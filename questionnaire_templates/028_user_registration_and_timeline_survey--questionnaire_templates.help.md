**User Registration And Timeline Survey - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about participants in a program or activity. It is divided into several sections that cover different aspects of participant progress and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*) next to the label.
2. Review the available options for select_multiple and select_one fields to ensure you are selecting the correct information.
3. Enter dates for timeline fields in the correct format (mm/dd/yy).
