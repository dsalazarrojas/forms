# Stylist Strategy Session Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients who are interested in booking a stylist strategy session. The purpose of this form is to help the stylist understand the client's preferences, goals, and context for the session, ensuring that the client receives the best possible service. To ensure that every client has a unique experience, please note that the form will not be processed if it is a repeat submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the "Client data" field with your name and contact information. This information will be used to contact you about the session.
2.  In the "Session preferences" field, select the time of day you would like to have your session. This will help us to schedule the session at a time that is convenient for you.
3.  In the "Session goals" field, describe your objectives for the session. This will help the stylist understand what you hope to achieve from the session.
4.  In the "Session context" field, provide any additional information about the session that might be helpful to the stylist. This could include your current style or fashion level of experience, or any medical conditions that might affect the session.
5.  In the "Pre-session Checklist", select the items that apply to you. This will help us to ensure that we are prepared for your session.
6.  In the "Stylist notes" field, provide any additional information that you think would be helpful for the stylist to know before the session.
7.  Finally, select "Submit" to complete the form and schedule the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`intro_page`, text, not required): This is a text field where you can briefly introduce yourself and let us know why you're interested in a stylist strategy session.
* **Client data** (`client_data`, text, not required): Fill out this field with your name and contact information so that we can reach out to you about your session.
* **Session preferences** (`session_preferences`, select_one, not required): Select the time of day you would prefer to have your session. Our available sessions are: Early Morning, Late Morning, Lunch, Afternoon, and Evening.
* **Session goals** (`goals`, text, required): Describe your objectives for the session. This will help us to tailor the session to your needs.
* **Session context** (`context`, text, not required): Provide any additional information about the session that might be helpful to the stylist.
* **Pre-session Checklist** (`pre_session_checklist`, select_multiple, not required): Select the items that apply to you from the list below:
	+ Confirm Session Time
	+ Confirm Session Location
	+ Confirm Session Goals
* **Stylist notes** (`stylist_notes`, note, not required): This field is for any additional information you think would be helpful for the stylist to know before the session.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, not required): Select "Submit" to complete the form and schedule the session. If you select "Other", please provide a reason for why you are unable to submit the form as usual.
