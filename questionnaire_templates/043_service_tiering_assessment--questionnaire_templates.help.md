# Service Tiering Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to assess the tiering of a service, which is a process to categorize services into different levels of priority and criticality. The assessment helps determine the importance and urgency of a service and informs decisions on resource allocation and maintenance.

<thinking>

To ensure the accuracy and relevance of your assessment, please review the service's criticality level and support needs before completing this form.

Please note that this form is a duplicate of a previous assessment, and some fields may have already been filled out. You can update the existing answers if necessary.

</thinking>

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the service's criticality level and support needs to ensure the assessment is accurate.
2. Select the correct tiering priority level for the service from the available options.
3. Choose the criticality level of the service from the available options.
4. Select the support level of the service from the available options.
5. Indicate the potential downtime impact of the service from the available options.
6. Provide a comment on the service tiering assessment.
7. Decide on the approval or rejection of the service tiering.
8. Provide a justification for the decision.
9. Recommend the next steps for the service tiering.
10. (Optional) Enter the current date and time of the service tiering assessment.
11. (Optional) Enter a contact email for the service tiering assessment.
12. (Optional) Enter a phone number for the service tiering assessment.
13. (Optional) Enter any additional comments on the service tiering assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Tiering Page**: Select the correct tiering for the service.
	+ Service 1: Select the highest priority tiering for the service.
	+ Service 2: Select the second highest priority tiering for the service.
* **Service Tiering Priority Level**: Choose the criticality level of the service from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Service Criticality Level**: Choose the criticality level of the service from Critical, Non-Critical, or Not Essential.
* **Service Support Level**: Choose the support level of the service from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Service Downtime Impact**: Indicate the potential downtime impact of the service from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Service Tiering Comment**: Provide a comment on the service tiering assessment.
* **Service Tiering Decision**: Decide on the approval or rejection of the service tiering from Approve, Reject, or Not Evaluated.
* **Service Tiering Justification**: Provide a justification for the decision.
* **Service Tiering Recommendation**: Recommend the next steps for the service tiering from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Service Tiering Next Steps**: Enter the next steps for the service tiering.
* **Service Tiering Date**: (Optional) Enter the current date and time of the service tiering assessment.
* **Service Tiering Email**: (Optional) Enter a contact email for the service tiering assessment.
* **Service Tiering Phone**: (Optional) Enter a phone number for the service tiering assessment.
* **Service Tiering Comments**: (Optional) Enter any additional comments on the service tiering assessment.
* **Service Tiering Agreement**: (Optional) Decide on the approval or rejection of the service tiering from Approve, Reject.
* **Service Tiering Agreement Comment**: (Optional) Enter a comment on the service tiering agreement.
* **Service Tiering Recommendation 2**: (Optional) Recommend the next steps for the service tiering from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Service Tiering Agreement 2**: (Optional) Decide on the approval or rejection of the service tiering from Approve, Reject.
* **Service Tiering Comments 2**: (Optional) Enter any additional comments on the service tiering assessment.
* **Service Tiering Date 2**: (Optional) Enter the current date and time of the service tiering assessment.
* **Service Tiering Time**: (Optional) Enter a comment on the service tiering time.
* **Service Tiering Email 2**: (Optional) Enter a contact email for the service tiering assessment.
* **Service Tiering Phone 2**: (Optional) Enter a phone number for the service tiering assessment.
