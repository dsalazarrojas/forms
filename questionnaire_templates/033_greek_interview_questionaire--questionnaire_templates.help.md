# Greek Interview Questionaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and track the status of attendees' interviews. The purpose of this form is to ensure that all relevant details are captured and documented accurately, and to facilitate the decision-making process for event approvals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the corresponding information.
2. Review and provide any additional details in the `Approval Notes` field if necessary.
3. Select `Yes` or `No` in the `Approval` field to indicate the decision for the attendee's interview.
4. If the interview is denied, provide a reason in the `Deny Reason` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event where the interview took place.
* **Group Name** (`group_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the group or department that is associated with the event.
* **Date & Time** (`date_time`, date, not required): Enter the date and time of the interview.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, not required): Enter the location where the interview took place.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the attendee who was interviewed.
* **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`, email, not required): Enter the email address of the attendee.
* **Attendee Phone** (`attendee_phone`, text, not required): Enter the phone number of the attendee.
* **Approval Notes** (`approval_notes`, note, not required): If necessary, enter any additional notes or comments for the approval or denial decision.
* **Approval** (`approval`, select_one, not required): Select `Yes` to approve the interview, `No` to deny it.
* **Deny Reason** (`deny_reason`, text, not required): If the interview is denied, enter the reason for the denial. 

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
* Be thorough and accurate when providing additional details in the `Approval Notes` field.
* If denying the interview, provide a clear reason for the denial.
