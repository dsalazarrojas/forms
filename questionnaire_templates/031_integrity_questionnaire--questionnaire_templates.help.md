# Integrity Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Integrity Questionnaire is a form designed to capture information about an employee's departure from the company, including reasons for leaving, reasons for termination, and any integrity issues that may have arisen during their employment. This form helps management and HR teams gather relevant data for future reference and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully, ensuring you answer all required fields.
2. Select relevant answers for each field, using the options provided for the department and reason for termination fields.
3. Provide a clear and concise explanation for each field, as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Employee Name
* `Employee Name` is the name of the employee who is departing from the company. Please enter their full name.
* Required: Yes

### Department
* Select the department the employee was working in during their employment. Options include:
	+ Engineering
	+ Management
	+ Finance
	+ Marketing
* Required: Yes

### Job Title
* Enter the job title of the employee during their employment. 
* Required: Yes

### Date Hired
* Enter the date the employee was hired.
* Required: Yes

### Reason For Leaving
* Provide a clear and concise explanation for the reason for the employee's departure. This may include circumstances leading up to their termination or resignation.
* Required: No

### Supervisor Name
* If applicable, enter the name of the employee's supervisor during their employment.
* Required: No

### Date Terminated
* Enter the date the employee's employment terminated.
* Required: Yes

### Reason For Termination
* Provide a clear and concise explanation for the reason for the employee's termination. This may include circumstances leading up to their termination.
* Required: Yes

### Reference
* Enter any relevant references or comments about the employee's performance during their employment.
* Required: Yes

### Integrity Issue
* Select any integrity issues that may have arisen during the employee's employment. Options include:
	+ Dishonesty
	+ Insubordination
	+ Theft
	+ Incompetence
	+ Negligence
* Required: Yes

### Recommendation
* If applicable, provide a clear and concise recommendation for future employee interactions or references.
* Required: No

### Comments
* Enter any additional comments or observations about the employee's departure.
* Required: No

## Tips

* Please answer all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* Ensure you provide clear and concise explanations for each field, as prompted.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with HR or your supervisor for guidance.
