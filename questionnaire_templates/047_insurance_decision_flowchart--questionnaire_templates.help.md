# Insurance Decision Flowchart - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Decision Flowchart form is designed to collect information about your insurance preferences, risk factors, and contact details. This data will help us provide you with personalized insurance coverage recommendations that fit your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page as honestly and accurately as possible.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields.
3. Review your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Main Page
* **Main Page**: This is the first page of the form where you can begin your journey.
* **Contact Info**: This section is to provide your contact details.
	+ **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number for us to reach out to you.
	+ **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so we can communicate with you effectively.
	+ **Contact Address**: Enter your physical address for any further communication.
* **Risk Factors**: This section is to assess your risk profile.
	+ **Do you smoke?**: Answer "True" if you smoke, "False" if you don't, or "Unsure" if you're not sure.
	+ **How old are you?**: Enter your age in numbers.
	+ **What is your occupation?**: Choose your occupation from the provided options.
	+ **Do you have a family history of cancer?**: Answer "True" if you do, "False" if you don't.
* **Coverage Preferences**: This section is to help us understand your insurance needs.
	+ **Do you have any medical conditions?**: Answer "True" if you have a medical condition, "False" if you don't.
	+ **Do you have any dependents?**: Answer "True" if you have dependents, "False" if you don't.
	+ **Do you have any other insurance?**: Answer "True" if you have other insurance, "False" if you don't.
	+ **Do you have any pets?**: Answer "True" if you have pets, "False" if you don't.
	+ **Do you have any other risk factors?**: Answer "True" if you have other risk factors, "False" if you don't.
* **Submit**: Click submit to send your information to us.
* **Risk Factors**: This section is to further assess your risk profile.
	+ **Do you drink alcohol?**: Answer "True" if you drink, "False" if you don't.
	+ **What is your coverage type?**: Choose your coverage type from the provided options.
	+ **Do you have a family member with a medical condition?**: Answer "True" if you have, "False" if you don't.
	+ **Do you have any dependents with a medical condition?**: Answer "True" if you have, "False" if you don't.
	+ **Do you have any other risk factors?**: Answer "True" if you have, "False" if you don't.
* **Contact Details**: This section is to confirm your contact information.
	+ **Do you have any other medical conditions?**: Answer "True" if you have any, "False" if you don't.

Please make sure to review your answers carefully before submission. If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
