# Individual Impact On Marine Environment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Individual Impact On Marine Environment" form is designed to collect information about the impact of individual actions on marine environments. It helps to assess the awareness and involvement of individuals in marine conservation and their participation in conservation actions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Navigate through the form's pages, starting with "Survey Context" at page 1.
2.  Read each question carefully and provide accurate answers.
3.  Select options for questions that require selections (e.g., "Ocean habits", "Marine protection status", etc.).
4.  If you need to enter a text response, provide as much detail as possible in the "Marine protection" field.
5.  Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Context** (`survey_context`, Note, Not Required):
    This field is a brief description of the context in which the form is being filled out. It is not required but helps to provide additional context for the answers provided.
* **Ocean habits** (`ocean_habits`, Select Multiple, Not Required):
    This question asks whether you regularly engage in activities that impact the marine environment, such as fishing, boating, or snorkeling.
* **Personal involvement** (`personal_involvement`, Select One, Not Required):
    This question assesses your level of personal involvement in marine conservation efforts, such as participating in beach cleanups or conservation organizations.
* **Conservation awareness** (`conservation_awareness`, Number, Not Required):
    This field is intended for you to rate your level of awareness about marine conservation, but it appears to be currently empty. Please provide a number that represents your understanding of marine conservation efforts.
* **Marine protection** (`marine_protection`, Text, Not Required):
    This field is intended for you to describe any actions you take to protect the marine environment, such as reducing plastic use or supporting local conservation efforts.
* **Marine protection actions** (`marine_protection_actions`, Select Multiple, Not Required):
    This question asks whether you participate in activities that protect the marine environment, such as reducing fishing gear or supporting marine protected areas.
* **Marine protection status** (`marine_protection_status`, Select One, Not Required):
    This question assesses the status of marine protection in your area, such as whether it is well-protected or threatened.
* **Personal involvement status** (`personal_involvement_status`, Select One, Not Required):
    This question evaluates the level of personal involvement in marine conservation efforts in your area, such as the presence of local conservation groups.
* **Conservation actions** (`conservation_actions`, Select Multiple, Not Required):
    This question asks whether you participate in conservation actions, such as reducing carbon footprint or supporting conservation research.
