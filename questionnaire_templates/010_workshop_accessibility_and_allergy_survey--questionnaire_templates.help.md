# Workshop Accessibility And Allergy Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your accessibility needs and allergy information to ensure a safe and inclusive experience at the workshop. Please answer the questions accurately and honestly to help us tailor our services to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Answer all the questions on each page, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Use the select options for multiple choice questions.
3. If you have any additional comments or information, you can use the text fields for free-form input.
