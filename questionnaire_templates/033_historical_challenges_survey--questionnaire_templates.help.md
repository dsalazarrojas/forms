# Historical Challenges Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on historical events from users. The form aims to gather information on various aspects of historical events, including their impact, importance, interest, and cultural significance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user ID.
2. Provide a brief introduction to the historical event you are responding to.
3. Choose the historical event you are referring to from the provided options.
4. Select the research area that best describes your approach to studying the historical event.
5. Choose the frequency with which you experience the historical event.
6. Select the participants involved in the historical event.
7. Enter the date and duration of the historical event.
8. Choose the impact, importance, interest, and cultural significance of the historical event.
9. Select the factors that influence your opinion of the historical event.
10. Provide any personal opinions or comments you have about the historical event.
11. Provide any additional comments you have about the historical event.
12. Choose the options that best describe your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user id**: Enter your unique user ID.
* **introduction**: Provide a brief introduction to the historical event you are responding to.
* **historical event**: Choose the historical event that best matches the event you are referring to.
* **historical challenge**: Describe the challenge or difficulty you faced during the historical event.
* **research area**: Choose the research area that best describes your approach to studying the historical event.
* **experience frequency**: Enter the frequency with which you experience the historical event.
* **historical event participants**: Select the participants involved in the historical event.
* **historical event date**: Enter the date of the historical event.
* **historical event duration**: Enter the duration of the historical event.
* **historical event impact**: Choose the impact of the historical event.
* **historical event importance**: Choose the importance of the historical event.
* **historical event interest**: Choose the interest of the historical event.
* **historical event cultural impact**: Choose the cultural impact of the historical event.
* **historical event cultural importance**: Choose the cultural importance of the historical event.
* **historical event cultural interest**: Choose the cultural interest of the historical event.
* **historical event cultural significance**: Choose the cultural significance of the historical event.
* **historical event influenced**: Choose the factors that influence your opinion of the historical event.
* **cultural event**: Provide a description of a cultural event related to the historical event.
* **personal opinion**: Provide a personal opinion about the historical event.
* **recommendation**: Provide a recommendation related to the historical event.
* **additional comments**: Provide any additional comments or thoughts about the historical event.
* **historical event submission**: Choose the options that best describe your submission.

## Tips
- Please provide honest and accurate responses to the questions.
- Be specific when describing historical events or their impact.
- Take as much time as needed to complete the form.
- If you have any doubts or questions, contact your supervisor or the form administrator.
- Ensure that you have a clear understanding of the questions being asked before submitting the form.
- Double-check your responses before submitting the form.
