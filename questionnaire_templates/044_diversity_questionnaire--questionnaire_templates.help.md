# diversity_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Diversity Questionnaire is designed to collect information about your demographics and opinions. This form will help us better understand the diversity of our organization, and your responses are anonymous and will be treated as confidential.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply answer each question to the best of your ability, and be honest about your demographics and opinions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **ID** (`<id>`): Enter your ID as a numerical value. (Required: No)
* **Opinions** (`<opinion_info>`): Share your thoughts and opinions about diversity and inclusion in the organization. This is an open-ended question, so answer as briefly or elaborately as you'd like.
* **Idea Info** (`<idea_info>`): Provide any ideas or suggestions you have for improving diversity and inclusion in the organization.
* **Demographic Info** (`<demographic_info>`): Select one or more of the following demographics to reflect your identity: White, Black or African American, Asian, American Indian or Alaskan Native, Native Hawaiian or Other Pacific Islander, Prefer not to disclose, or Other (please specify).
* **Diversity Questionnaire** (`<title>`): This is just a title and not a question to be answered.

Please note that you are not required to answer all questions, and you can answer as many or as few questions as you feel comfortable with.
