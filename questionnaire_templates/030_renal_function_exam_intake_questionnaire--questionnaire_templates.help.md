# Renal Function Exam Intake Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a patient's renal function exam, including medical history, current medications, allergies, and laboratory testing results.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's information, including their name, date of birth, and contact details.
2. **Medical History**: Provide a brief summary of the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, surgeries, or medical conditions.
3. **Current Medications**: List any medications the patient is currently taking.
4. **Allergies**: List any allergies the patient has.
5. **Medical History and Conditions**: Provide more detailed information about the patient's medical history and conditions.
6. **Additional Laboratory Testing**: List any additional laboratory testing the patient has undergone.
7. **Urine Analysis**: Provide information about the patient's urine analysis results.
8. **Renal Function**: Answer questions about the patient's renal function, including glomerular filtration rate (GFR) and serum creatinine.
9. **Additional Laboratory Testing 2**: List any additional laboratory testing the patient has undergone.
10. **Glomerular Filtration Rate (GFR) and Serum Creatinine**: Provide more information about the patient's renal function.
11. **Urine Protein to Creatinine Ratio**: Provide information about the patient's urine protein to creatinine ratio.
12. **Urine Specific Gravity**: Answer questions about the patient's urine specific gravity.
13. **Blood Urea Nitrogen (BUN)**: Provide information about the patient's blood urea nitrogen (BUN) levels.
14. **Blood Gases**: Answer questions about the patient's blood gas levels.
15. **Complete Blood Count (CBC)**: Provide information about the patient's complete blood count (CBC) results.
16. **Complete Metabolic Profile**: Answer questions about the patient's complete metabolic profile.
17. **Lipid Profile**: Provide information about the patient's lipid profile.
18. **Blood Electrolytes**: Answer questions about the patient's blood electrolytes.
19. **Renal Function 2**: Provide more information about the patient's renal function.
20. **Urine Culture**: Provide information about the patient's urine culture results.
21. **Urine Microscopy**: Answer questions about the patient's urine microscopy results.
22. **Urine Cytology**: Provide information about the patient's urine cytology results.
