# TikTok Growth Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The TikTok Growth Assessment form is designed to help businesses evaluate their growth on TikTok, covering various aspects such as target audience, content strategy, advertising budget, marketing channels, and performance metrics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the **Target audience** that best describes your business on TikTok (Follower growth, Engagement rate, Conversion rate, Average order value, or Average order frequency).
2. Select the **Growth goals** you aim to achieve on TikTok (Growth rate, Conversion rate, Average order value, or Average order frequency).
3. Indicate whether you have **TikTok performance** (Yes or No).
4. Choose the **Content strategy** you use on TikTok (Video, Live, Post, or Hashtag campaign).
5. Select the **Content frequency** you use on TikTok (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, or Never).
6. Enter your **TikTok advertising budget** (number).
7. Select the **Marketing channels** you use on other platforms (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, or TikTok).
8. Fill out the **Performance metrics** section:
	* Choose the **Growth rate** (Fast, Medium, or Slow) for your business on TikTok.
	* Choose the **Conversion rate** (High, Medium, or Low) for your business on TikTok.
9. Enter your **Average order value** and **Average order frequency**.
10. Enter your **Average conversion rate**.
11. Enter your **Customer acquisition cost**.
12. Enter your **ROI** (Return on Investment).
13. Enter your **Lead generation cost**.
14. In the **Additional Information** section:
	* Enter any **Additional comments** about your business on TikTok.
	* Write down the **Next steps** you plan to take.
	* Enter the **Assigned user** who filled out the form.
	* Enter the **Assigned date** when the form was filled out.
	* Enter the **Follow-up date** when you plan to follow up on the assessment.
	* Choose the **Status** of the assessment (Open, Closed, Won, Lost, or In Progress).
	* Enter the **Follow-up reason** for the assessment.
15. Finally, review and submit the form.
