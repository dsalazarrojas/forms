# Annual Check Up Experience Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Annual Check-Up Experience Questionnaire is designed to gather feedback from patients about their overall experience with a healthcare provider during their annual check-up. This feedback helps providers evaluate their performance and make improvements for future patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

* Please fill out the form on a computer or mobile device.
* For each section, answer the questions based on your actual experience with the healthcare provider.
* If you're unsure about any question, try to answer based on your best recollection or ask your provider for clarification.
* Be as specific as possible when answering questions.
* Take your time to provide thoughtful and honest feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your medical record.
* **Patient ID or Medical Record Number** (`patient_id`, `text`, required): Enter your medical record number if you have one.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, optional): If you have an email address associated with your medical record, enter it here. This can be used for follow-up communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you have a phone number associated with your medical record, enter it here. This can be used for follow-up calls.
* **Date of Annual Check-Up** (`visit_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your actual appointment.
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (`provider_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
* **Facility or Clinic Name** (`facility_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the clinic or facility where you visited.
* **Check-In Experience** (`check_in_experience`, `select_one`, required): Rate your experience with the check-in process. Choose from:
	+ Excellent - Very smooth
	+ Good - Efficient
	+ Satisfactory - Adequate
	+ Poor - Needs improvement
	+ Terrible - Very problematic
* **Wait Time** (`wait_time`, `select_one`, required): Estimate how long you waited before being seen by the provider. Choose from:
	+ No wait - Seen immediately
	+ Short wait - Under 15 minutes
	+ Moderate wait - 15-30 minutes
	+ Long wait - 30-60 minutes
	+ Very long wait - Over 1 hour
* **Quality of the Medical Exam** (`exam_quality`, `select_one`, required): Rate the thoroughness of the medical exam. Choose from:
	+ Excellent - Very thorough
	+ Good - Thorough
	+ Satisfactory - Adequate
	+ Poor - Incomplete
	+ Terrible - Very inadequate
* **Did Provider Listen to Your Concerns** (`provider_listening`, `select_one`, optional): Rate how well the provider listened to your concerns. Choose from:
	+ Yes, very attentive
	+ Yes, attentive
	+ Somewhat - Could have listened better
	+ No - Did not seem to listen
	+ Not applicable
* **Clarity of Health Information** (`communication_clarity`, `select_one`, required): Rate how clearly the provider explained health information. Choose from:
	+ Very clear - Easy to understand
	+ Clear - Mostly understood
	+ Neutral - Some confusion
	+ Unclear - Hard to understand
	+ Very unclear - Not explained
* **Time Spent with Provider** (`time_spent_with_provider`, `select_one`, required): Rate if enough time was spent with the provider. Choose from:
	+ Plenty of time
	+ Adequate time
	+ Somewhat rushed
	+ Very rushed
	+ Not enough time
* **Received Wellness Recommendations** (`wellness_advice`, `select_one`, optional): Did you receive any wellness recommendations? Choose from:
	+ Yes, clear recommendations
	+ Yes, some recommendations
	+ Minimal recommendations
	+ No recommendations
	+ Not applicable
* **Understood Follow-up Instructions** (`follow_up_instructions`, `select_one`, required): Rate if you understood the follow-up instructions clearly. Choose from:
	+ Very clear
	+ Clear
	+ Somewhat clear
	+ Unclear
	+ None provided
* **Overall Satisfaction with Check-Up** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the check-up. Choose from:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Would You Recommend This Provider?** (`recommend_provider`, `select_one`, required): Would you recommend this provider to others? Choose from:
	+ Yes, definitely
	+ Yes, probably
	+ Maybe
	+ No, probably not
	+ No, definitely not
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, `text`, optional): If you have any suggestions for improvement, enter them here.
* **Additional Comments or Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, `text`, optional): If you have any other thoughts or comments, enter them here.
