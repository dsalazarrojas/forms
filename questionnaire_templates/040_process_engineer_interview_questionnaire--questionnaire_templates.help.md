# Process Engineer Interview Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from candidates applying for a Process Engineer position. It's essential to answer all the questions accurately and completely to help the hiring manager assess your qualifications and experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read through each question and ensure you understand the type of information being requested.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title**:
	+ Label: Job Title
	+ Type: Select one option
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To indicate your current or previous job title as a Process Engineer.
* **Job Type**:
	+ Label: Job Type
	+ Type: Select one option
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To identify the type of Process Engineer you are or have been (Yes/No).
* **Experience**:
	+ Label: Experience
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To describe your relevant experience as a Process Engineer, including job titles, company names, and dates.
* **Education**:
	+ Label: Education
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To list your educational background relevant to Process Engineering, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
* **Skills**:
	+ Label: Skills
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To list your relevant technical skills, including software proficiency, programming languages, and tools used.
* **Work Experience**:
	+ Label: Work Experience
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To describe your previous work experience as a Process Engineer, including job responsibilities and dates.
* **Job Responsibilities**:
	+ Label: Job Responsibilities
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To list your job responsibilities as a Process Engineer, including specific tasks and duties.
* **Previous Job**:
	+ Label: Previous Job
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To describe your previous job as a Process Engineer, including job title, company, and dates.
* **Previous Salary**:
	+ Label: Previous Salary
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To report your previous salary as a Process Engineer, including currency of the salary.
* **Previous Salary Currency**:
	+ Label: Previous Salary Currency
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: To report the currency of your previous salary as a Process Engineer.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
* Provide as much detail as possible to help the hiring manager assess your qualifications.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the hiring manager for clarification.
