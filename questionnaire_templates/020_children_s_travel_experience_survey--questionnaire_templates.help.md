# Children Travel Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a child's travel experience. It aims to gather details such as their name, destination, travel dates, and overall satisfaction with their trip.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Enter Child Name**: Please type the child's name, especially if they are younger and cannot fill out the form themselves. This field is not required.
2. **Destination**: Enter the name of the city or location visited during the trip.
3. **Travel Dates**: Enter the date range of the trip, including the start and end dates.
4. **Favorite Activity**: Describe the favorite activity or experience the child had during the trip. This field is optional.
5. **Comfort Level During Travel**: Select the comfort level of the child during the trip from the options provided: Very comfortable, Comfortable, Uncomfortable, or Very uncomfortable. This field is required.
6. **Food and Meal Experience**: Describe any issues or favorite meals the child had during the trip. This field is optional.
7. **Behavioral Notes**: Record any behavioral observations or comments made by the child during the trip. This field is optional.
8. **Overall Trip Rating**: Select the overall satisfaction of the trip from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor. This field is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Child Name** (Child Name, text, optional): Enter the child's name.
* **Trip Destination** (Trip Destination, text, required): Enter the name of the city or location visited.
* **Travel Dates** (Travel Dates, text, required): Enter the date range of the trip.
* **Favorite Activity** (Favorite Activity, text, optional): Describe what the child enjoyed most on the trip.
* **Comfort Level During Travel** (Comfort Level During Travel, select_one, required): Select the comfort level of the child during the trip.
* **Food and Meal Experience** (Food and Meal Experience, text, optional): Describe any issues or favorite meals.
* **Behavioral Notes** (Behavioral Notes, text, optional): Record any behavioral observations or comments made by the child.
* **Overall Trip Rating** (Overall Trip Rating, select_one, required): Select the overall satisfaction of the trip.

## Tips
- Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out the form.
- Take into account the child's age and reading level when filling out the form.
- If the child is younger, you may need to fill out the form on their behalf.
