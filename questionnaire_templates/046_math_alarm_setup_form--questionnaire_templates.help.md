# Math Alarm Setup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Math Alarm Setup Form is a configuration tool for the math application, allowing users to customize their settings for notifications and challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the desired options for each field, and choose whether to save your changes or not. The form is designed to be user-friendly and intuitive, with clear labels and options for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Math Type**: Select the type of math problem or challenge you want to focus on, such as Addition, Subtraction, or Mixed.
* **Math Level**: Choose the level of difficulty you want to tackle, ranging from Beginner to Advanced.
* **Alarm Type**: Select the type of alarm you want to receive, such as Math Problem, Math Challenge, or Math Game.
* **Time Interval**: Choose the time interval for alarms, with options 30 minutes, 60 minutes, or 90 minutes.
* **Notification Time**: Select the time of day for notifications, with options 08:00, 12:00, or 17:00.
* **Language**: Choose the language for the math application, with options English, Spanish, French, or Chinese.
* **Time Zone**: Select the timezone for the math application, with options Eastern, Central, Mountain, or Pacific.
* **Challenge Type**: Select the type of math challenge, such as Math Problem Challenge or Math Game Challenge.
* **Challenge Interval**: Choose the interval for math challenges, with options Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Challenge Duration**: Select the duration for math challenges, with options 30 minutes, 60 minutes, or 90 minutes.
* **Notification Method**: Select the method for receiving notifications, with options Email or Coach/Manager.
* **Save Changes**: Choose whether to save your changes to apply them to the math application, with options True or False.

## Tips
* Take your time to carefully consider your options before making your selections.
* If you're unsure about any of the options, you can always ask for help.
* Remember to save your changes if you want the settings to be applied to the math application.
