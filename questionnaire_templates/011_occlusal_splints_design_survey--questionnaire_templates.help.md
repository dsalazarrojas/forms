# Occlusal Splints Design Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback on the design of occlusal splints, which are custom-made devices used in dentistry to guide the occlusal relationship between the upper and lower teeth. The data collected will be used to improve the design and functionality of occlusal splints.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that apply to your occlusal splints design preferences, challenges, and outcomes. You can choose from the provided options or type in your own answer for the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **occlusal_splints_design_preferences** (`Select Multiple`, required: false)
This field asks about your preferences for occlusal splints design. Select all the options that apply to you.

### **occlusal_splints_preferences** (`Text`, required: false)
Please provide any additional information about your occlusal splints preferences.

### **occlusal_splints_challenges** (`Date`, required: false)
This field asks about the challenges you have faced with occlusal splints design. If you have experienced any challenges, please select a date when you faced the challenge.

### **occlusal_splints_outcomes** (`Text`, required: false)
Please describe any positive outcomes or results you have experienced with occlusal splints design.

### **Tips**
- Please answer all questions accurately and thoroughly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask.
- Thank you for taking the time to provide feedback on occlusal splints design!
