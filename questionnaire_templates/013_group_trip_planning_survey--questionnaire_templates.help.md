**Group Trip Planning Survey - Help Guide**

### Purpose

This form is designed to help users provide input on their preferences for a group trip, including the type of vacation, duration, budget, and other relevant factors. This information will help us to better understand your needs and provide personalized suggestions for your group trip.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the type of vacation you are looking for from the list of options.
2. Choose the length of time you would like to stay for the trip.
3. Indicate your budget for the trip.
4. Prioritize what is most important to you for this trip.
5. Select the destinations you are interested in visiting.
6. Choose the expected duration of your trip.
7. Choose your expected travel budget.
8. Indicate how important it is for you to have a priority on this trip.
9. Enter the number of people you will be traveling with.
10. Indicate how often you plan to travel.
11. Decide if you are planning a solo trip or with a group.
12. If traveling with a group, select the languages spoken by the majority of travelers.
13. Check if you plan to book travel insurance.
14. Enter your expected daily budget per person.
15. Indicate how important it is to have a hotel with a pool.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of vacation are you looking for?** (`trip_type`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): This question helps us understand the type of trip you are looking for, whether it's a beach vacation, city exploration, or adventure in the mountains.
* **How long would you like to stay?** (`trip_duration`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): Choose the length of time you would like to stay for the trip. Please select from 3 days, 5 days, or 7 days.
* **What is your budget for the trip?** (`trip_budget`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): This question helps us understand your budget for the trip. Please select from Less than $500, $500-$1000, or More than $1000.
* **What is your priority for this trip?** (`trip_priority`, `select_one`, `required: false`): This question helps us understand what is most important to you for this trip. Please select between Destination, Budget, or Duration.
* **Which destinations are you interested in visiting?** (`trip_destinations`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): Choose the destinations you are interested in visiting for your trip.
* **What is your expected trip duration?** (`trip_durations`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): This question helps us understand the expected duration of your trip. Please select from 3 days, 5 days, or 7 days.
* **What is your expected travel budget?** (`trip_budgets`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): This question helps us understand your expected travel budget. Please select from Less than $500, $500-$1000, or More than $1000.
* **How important is it for you to have a priority on this trip?** (`trip_priority_2`, `select_one`, `required: false`): This question helps us understand how important it is for you to have a priority on this trip. Please select from Very important, Somewhat important, or Not at all important.
* **How many people will you be traveling with?** (`group_size`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): Please choose the number of people you will be traveling with, from 1-3 people, 4-6 people, or More than 6 people.
* **How often do you plan to travel?** (`travel_frequency`, `select_one`, `required: false`): Choose how often you plan to travel, from Rarely, Occasionally, or Often.
* **Are you planning a solo trip or with a group?** (`travel_mode`, `radio`, `required: false`): Please select if you are planning a solo trip or with a group.
* **How many languages do you speak fluently?** (`travel_language`, `number`, `required: false`): This question helps us understand your language proficiency. Please enter a number.
* **Are you planning to book travel insurance?** (`travel_insurance`, `checkbox`, `required: false`): Please check if you plan to book travel insurance.
* **What is your expected daily budget per person?** (`daily_budget`, `number`, `required: false`): This question helps us understand your expected daily budget per person. Please enter a number.
* **How important is it for you to have a hotel with a pool?** (`pool_importance`, `select_one`, `required: false`): This question helps us understand how important it is for you to have a hotel with a pool. Please select from Very important, Somewhat important, or Not at all important.
