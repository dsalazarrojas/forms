# hair Consultation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hair Consultation Request Form is a tool to collect information about a client's hair needs for a consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant category for your request.
2. Choose your hair length (short, medium, or long).
3. Select your hair color.
4. Describe your hair type.
5. Specify the hair condition (select multiple options if applicable).
6. Choose a date and time for your appointment.
7. Provide any additional notes about your consultation.
8. Enter your contact information (email or phone number) so we can reach out to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **hair consultation request** (`hair_consultation_request`, note, optional): A brief description of your hair consultation request.
* **category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Choose the category for your request (color, style, or texture).
* **hair length** (`hair_length`, select_one, optional): Select your hair length (short, medium, or long).
* **hair color** (`hair_color`, text, optional): Enter your hair color.
* **hair type** (`hair_type`, text, optional): Describe your hair type.
* **hair condition** (`hair_condition`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple hair conditions that apply to your hair (dry, oily, curly, damaged, or normal).
* **schedule** (`schedule`, date, optional): Choose a date for your appointment.
* **preferred time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): Choose a preferred time for your appointment.
* **notes** (`notes`, select_multiple, optional): Provide any additional notes about your consultation (e.g., appointment, follow up, or other).
* **contact information** (`contact_information`, email, optional): Enter your email or phone number so we can reach out to you.
* **phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
