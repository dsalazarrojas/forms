# Salad Ingredients Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Salad Ingredients Knowledge Quiz form is used to gather information about users' knowledge of salad ingredients and their preferences for salad dressings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  General Information: Enter your name and other general information as prompted.
2.  Salad Ingredients: Select the salad ingredients you are familiar with from the list of options provided.
3.  Salad Dressings: Select your preferred salad dressing from the list of options provided.
4.  Trainers Information (Optional): Enter your trainer's information, including a date, if applicable.
5.  Food Creators Information (Optional): Enter your food creator's information, including a time, if applicable.
6.  Quiz Start Time (Optional): Note the start time of the quiz.
7.  Quiz End Time (Optional): Note the end time of the quiz.
8.  Quiz Durations (Optional): Input the duration of the quiz.
9.  Quiz Score (Optional): Input your score.
10. Submission Date (Optional): Input the date of submission.
11. Submission Time (Optional): Input the time of submission.
11. Submission Status (Optional): Select the status of your submission (Success, Failure, or Aborted).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **General Information** (`General Information`, `text`, `required`): Enter your name and any other general information as prompted.
*   **Salad Ingredients** (`Salad Ingredients`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select the salad ingredients you are familiar with from the list of options provided.
*   **Salad Dressings** (`Salad Dressings`, `select_one`, `required`): Select your preferred salad dressing from the list of options provided.
*   **Trainers Information** (`Trainers Information`, `date`, `optional`): Enter your trainer's information, including a date, if applicable.
*   **Food Creators Information** (`Food Creators Information`, `time`, `optional`): Enter your food creator's information, including a time, if applicable.
*   **Quiz Start Time** (`Quiz Start Time`, `note`, `optional`): Note the start time of the quiz.
*   **Quiz End Time** (`Quiz End Time`, `time`, `optional`): Note the end time of the quiz.
*   **Quiz Durations** (`Quiz Durations`, `number`, `optional`): Input the duration of the quiz.
*   **Quiz Score** (`Quiz Score`, `number`, `optional`): Input your score.
*   **Submission Date** (`Submission Date`, `date`, `optional`): Input the date of submission.
*   **Submission Time** (`Submission Time`, `time`, `optional`): Input the time of submission.
*   **Submission Status** (`Submission Status`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select the status of your submission (Success, Failure, or Aborted).
