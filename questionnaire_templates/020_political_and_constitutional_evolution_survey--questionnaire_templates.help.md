# Political And Constitutional Evolution Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information on political awareness, constitutional knowledge, and civic engagement from the respondent. Please answer the questions to the best of your ability to provide valuable insights for researchers or policymakers.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your personal details such as first name, last name, email address, and phone number. These are all required fields.
2.  Provide your date of birth.
3.  Choose your country of citizenship from the list provided.
4.  Indicate your highest level of education attained.
5.  Select your level of political awareness.
6.  Choose your opinion on the political system.
7.  Express your attitudes towards constitutional change.
8.  Indicate your level of civic engagement.
9.  Choose your level of constitutional knowledge.
10. Select your first language.
11. Choose your highest level of education.
12. Provide details on the number of dependents you have.
13. Enter the number of years you've been employed.
14. Enter the number of years you've been with your current organisation.
15. Enter the number of years you've been a member of your current organisation.
16. Enter the years you've had seniority.
17. Enter the number of children you have.
18. Enter your income.
19. Enter your occupation.

*   **First Name**: Enter your first name.

*   **Civic Engagement**: Indicate your level of civic engagement. This can be High, Medium, or Low.
