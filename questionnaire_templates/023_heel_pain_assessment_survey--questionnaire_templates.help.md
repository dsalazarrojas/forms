# Heel Pain Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients about their heel pain, which will aid in further assessment and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Patient Name** field.
2. Provide your **Contact Number** so we can reach out to you for further questions or updates.
3. If you have an email, please enter it in the **Email** field (optional).
4. Describe any **Previous Injuries** that may be relevant to your current heel pain.
5. Answer the following questions about your heel pain:
	* **Pain Type**: Select the type of pain you're experiencing (Pain, Other).
	* **Duration**: Enter how many days you've been experiencing heel pain.
	* **Severity**: Rate your pain severity on a scale (likely 1-10).
	* **Location**: Select the location of your heel pain (Right Heel, Left Heel, Both Heels).
	* **Impact**: Select which activities are affected by your pain (Walking, Running, Standing, Sleeping).
6. Press submit to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: This is where you enter your name. Please use your full name as it appears on your ID.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you for further questions or updates.
* **Email**: This field is optional. If you have an email address, please enter it so we can communicate with you more efficiently.
* **Previous Injuries**: Describe any previous injuries that may be related to your current heel pain.
* **Pain Type**: Select the type of pain you're experiencing, which will help us better understand your condition.
* **Duration**: Enter how many days you've been experiencing heel pain.
* **Severity**: Rate your pain severity on a scale (likely 1-10).
* **Location**: Select which part of your heel is affected by pain (Right Heel, Left Heel, Both Heels).
* **Impact**: Choose which activities are affected by your pain (Walking, Running, Standing, Sleeping).

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and accurately to ensure we have a clear understanding of your heel pain.
- If you're unsure about any field, please let us know so we can further assist.
