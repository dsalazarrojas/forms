# employee_exit_interview_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from departing employees, helping the organization understand the reasons behind their departure and identify areas for improvement to prevent future departures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First name**, **Last name**, and **Date of birth**.
2. Provide a brief description of your **Reason for leaving**.
3. Specify the **Company name**, where you last worked.
4. Choose the **Department** where you last worked (Sales, Marketing, or Finance).
5. Enter your **Job title**.
6. If applicable, provide the **Notice period** before your last working day.
7. Enter your **Salary** (or 0, if you prefer not to disclose).
8. If you are eligible, select the **Benefits** you are entitled to.
9. Enter your **Email** address.
10. Finally, provide your **Phone number**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your given name.
* **Last name**: Enter your surname.
* **Date of birth**: Format: YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Reason for leaving**: A brief description of why you are leaving the company. 
*   **Company name**: Enter the name of the company you last worked for.
*   **Department**: Select the department where you last worked.
*   **Job title**: Enter your job title at the time of your departure.
*   **Notice period**: If you provided a notice period, enter it here. A higher value indicates a longer notice period.
*   **Salary**: Enter your salary (or 0, if you prefer not to disclose).
*   **Benefits**: Select all benefits you are eligible for.
*   **Email**: Enter your official email address. 
*   **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.

Note: This form is a sample and might need to be adapted based on your company's specific needs and policies.
