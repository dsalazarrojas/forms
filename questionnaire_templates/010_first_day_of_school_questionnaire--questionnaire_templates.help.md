# First Day Of School Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students on their first day of school. It is intended to be a helpful tool for teachers to get to know their students better, understand their interests, and prepare for the new academic year. This form is meant to be completed by students and is not intended for parents or guardians.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the required fields with your personal and academic information.
2.  Provide details about your interests, family background, and previous schooling.
3.  List any relevant student achievements or accomplishments.
4.  Share any additional student notes or comments.
5.  Enter your phone number and email address for communication purposes.
6.  Provide the name of your school and your grade level.
7.  Share any teacher notes or comments with your teacher.
8.  Finally, provide your age and the school year you are currently in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information (1)**: Enter your name, grade level, and any other personal details you feel comfortable sharing.
* **Interests (2)**: List any hobbies, activities, or interests you enjoy in your free time.
* **Family Background (3)**: Describe your family situation, including your parents' names, occupation, and relationship status.
* **Previous Schooling (4)**: Share details about your previous schools, including the name, location, and any notable achievements.
* **Student Interests (5)**: List any interests or activities you enjoy in your free time, such as sports, music, or reading.
* **Student Family (6)**: Describe your family dynamics, including your parents' names, occupation, and relationship status.
* **Teacher Info (7)**: Enter any relevant information about your teacher, such as their name, contact number, or email address.
* **Student Achievements (8)**: List any notable achievements or accomplishments you are proud of, such as awards or recognition.
* **Student Notes (9)**: Share any additional comments or notes you think your teacher should know about you.
* **Student Phone (10)**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Student Email (11)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **School Name (12)**: Enter the name of your school.
