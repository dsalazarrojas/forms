# Country Culture Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Country Culture Quiz is used to collect data on users' cultural proficiency, preferences, and demographics to analyze cultural trends and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the first name field with your first name.
2. Enter your last name in the last name field.
3. Provide your email address in the email field.
4. Select your country of origin from the options provided.
5. Choose your preferred language from the options.
6. Select the type of quiz you would like to take, whether it's fun, serious, or both.
7. If you have a time zone, select it from the options.
8. If you have a preferred date, enter it in the date field.
9. If you have a preferred time, enter it in the time field.
10. Add any additional notes or comments in the notes field.
11. If you have a phone number, enter it in the phone field.
12. If you have information about the first page you visited, enter it in the first page field.
13. Select your country of origin from the options (if not already selected).
14. Select the language of culture you prefer from the options.
15. Choose your cultural level, whether beginner, intermediate, or advanced.
16. Enter your form ID, if available.
17. Enter your form name, if available.
18. Enter your form version, if available.
19. Enter a brief description of the form, if available.
20. Enter the category of the form, if available.
21. Enter tags related to the form, if available.
22. If you have submitted this form before, enter the date and time you submitted it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (first_name, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (last_name, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Country** (country, text, optional): Select your country of origin.
* **Language** (language, select_one, required): Choose your preferred language.
* **Quiz Type** (quiz_type, select_multiple, required): Select the type of quiz you prefer, whether fun, serious, or both.
* **Time Zone** (time_zone, select_one, optional): If you have a time zone, select it.
* **Date** (date, date, optional): If you have a preferred date, enter it.
* **Time** (time, time, optional): If you have a preferred time, enter it.
* **Notes** (note, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it.
* **First Page** (first_page, text, optional): Enter information about the first page you visited.
* **Country Of Origin** (country_of_origin, select_one, optional): Select your country of origin.
* **Language Of Culture** (language_of_culture, select_one, optional): Select the language of culture you prefer.
* **Cultural Level** (cultural_level, select_multiple, required): Choose your cultural level, whether beginner, intermediate, or advanced.
* **Form ID** (form_id, text, optional): Enter your form ID, if available.
* **Form Name** (form_name, text, optional): Enter your form name, if available.
* **Form Version** (form_version, text, optional): Enter your form version, if available.
* **Form Description** (form_description, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the form, if available.
* **Form Category** (form_category, text, optional): Enter the category of the form, if available.
* **Form Tags** (form_tags, text, optional): Enter tags related to the form, if available.
* **Form Created At** (form_created_at, date, optional): If you have information about when the form was created, enter it.
* **Form Updated At** (form_updated_at, date, optional): If you have information about when the form was updated, enter it.
* **Form Submitted By** (form_submitted_by, text, optional): Enter the name or ID of the person who submitted this form.
* **Form Submitted At** (form_submitted_at, date, optional): Enter the date and time this form was submitted.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out to ensure that your data is accurate and complete.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the form administrator.
* This form is intended for anonymous data collection and does not collect sensitive or personally identifiable information.
