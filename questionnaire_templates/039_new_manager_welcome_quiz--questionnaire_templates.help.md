# new_manager_welcome_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form, "new_manager_welcome_quiz", is designed for new managers to quickly get started with their new role. It helps them understand their role, leadership style, and expectations in an easy and structured way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Choose the correct answers to each question based on your role and expectations.
2. Fill in any text fields with a short description of your answers.
3. Select multiple answers for questions that ask for multiple responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role?** (`new_manager_welcome_quiz_page_1`, Select One, Required): Please select 'Yes' if this is your role, and 'No' otherwise.
* **What is your job title?** (`new_manager_welcome_quiz_page_1`, Text, Required): Enter your official job title.
* **How would you describe your leadership style?** (`new_manager_welcome_quiz_description`, Text, Required): Provide a short description of your approach to leadership (e.g., supportive, directive, collaborative).
* **How would you describe your leadership style?** (`new_manager_welcome_quiz_page_2`, Select One, Required): Please select 'Yes' if your leadership style is more hands-on, 'No' otherwise.
* **What is your biggest challenge as a new manager?** (`new_manager_welcome_quiz_page_3`, Text, Required): Share your main concern or challenge as a new manager (e.g., learning the team, adjusting to new processes).
* **How do you plan to measure success as a manager?** (`new_manager_welcome_quiz_page_3`, Select Multiple, Required): Select all the ways you plan to measure success (e.g., team performance, project completion, team satisfaction).
* **How many direct reports do you have?** (`new_manager_welcome_quiz_page_4`, Number, Required): Enter the number of people you directly manage.
* **How do you prioritize your tasks?** (`new_manager_welcome_quiz_page_5`, Select One, Required): Please select how you typically prioritize your tasks (e.g., by urgency, by importance, by deadlines).
* **What is your preferred communication style?** (`new_manager_welcome_quiz_page_5`, Text, Required): Describe your approach to communication (e.g., frequent updates, in-person meetings, email).

## Tips
* Take your time and answer each question thoughtfully.
* Be as detailed as possible in your descriptions and answers.
* Review your answers before submitting to ensure they accurately reflect your role and expectations.
