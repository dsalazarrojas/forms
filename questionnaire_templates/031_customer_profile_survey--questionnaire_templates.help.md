# Customer Profile Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Customer Profile Survey" is designed to collect information about customers, including their demographics, interests, and preferences. This data will help us to better understand our customers' needs and provide more tailored services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

* Fill out the form in its entirety, as it is required for the survey.
* For questions marked as optional, answer to the best of your ability.
* Ensure that you answer truthfully and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Profile Survey** (`customer_profile_survey`): This is the title of the form. Please ignore this field as it is for identification purposes only.
* **Customer Interests** (`customer_interests`): Answer 'Yes' if you are interested in our products or services. Answer 'No' if you are not interested.
* **Customer Habits** (`customer_habits`): Select all the habits that apply to you. For example, if you are a morning person, select 'Yes' for morning person.
* **Customer Preferences** (`customer_preferences`): This field is for any additional information you would like to provide. You can type in anything you think would be relevant to your profile.
* **Email** (`email`): Please enter your email address. If you do not have an email address, please select 'No' or provide a fake email address.
* **Customer Age** (`customer_age`): Answer this question with your age. You can type in your age as a number.
* **Customer Gender** (`customer_gender`): Answer 'Yes' if you identify as male, 'No' if you identify as female, or 'Other' if you identify as something else.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): Please enter your phone number. You can type in your phone number in the format ###-###-####.
* **Customer Location** (`customer_location`): Select all the locations that apply to you. For example, if you live in the city or country of the customer, select 'Yes'.
* **Customer Interests Hobbies** (`customer_interests_hobbies`): Select all the interests and hobbies that apply to you. For example, if you enjoy reading, select 'Yes'.
* **Customer Occupation** (`customer_occupation`): Please type in your occupation. For example, if you are a student or teacher, type that in.
* **Customer Income** (`customer_income`): Answer this question with your income. You can type in your income as a number.
* **Customer Education** (`customer_education`): Select all the levels of education that apply to you. For example, if you have a high school diploma, select 'Yes'.
* **Customer Marital Status** (`customer_marital_status`): Answer 'Active' if you are married, 'Inactive' if you are not married.
* **Customer Anniversary** (`customer_anniversary`): Please enter the date of your anniversary. Use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Customer Birthdate** (`customer_birthdate`): Please enter your birthday. Use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
