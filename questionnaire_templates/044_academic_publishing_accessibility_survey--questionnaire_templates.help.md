# Academic Publishing Accessibility Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Publishing Accessibility Survey is designed to gather information from individuals involved in the publishing process about their experiences, challenges, and suggestions for improving accessibility in academic publishing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the role that best describes your involvement in the publishing process.
2.  Choose all the accessibility challenges you face in publishing (e.g., documents not accessible, platform limitations, lack of training, or limited funding for accessibility).
3.  Describe any accessibility practices currently in place or suggest areas for improvement.
4.  Select the priority areas where accessibility improvement is needed.
5.  Indicate your interest in accessibility training.
6.  Provide practical suggestions for improving accessibility.
7.  (Optional) Enter your contact email to be contacted for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Role in publishing process (`role`, `select_one`, required):** Please choose the option that best describes your role in the publishing process. This will help us understand your perspective and experiences.
*   **Accessibility challenges faced in publishing (`accessibility_challenges`, `select_multiple`, required):** Select all the challenges you face in publishing that relate to accessibility. This will help us identify areas where support is most needed.
*   **Current accessibility practices in place (`current_practices`, `text`, optional):** Describe any accessibility practices you currently follow or suggest areas for improvement.
*   **Priority areas for accessibility improvement (`priority_areas`, `select_multiple`, optional):** Choose key areas where accessibility improvement is most needed.
*   **Training needs (`training_needs`, `select_one`, required):** Indicate your interest in accessibility training to help us tailor support to your needs.
*   **Recommendations to improve accessibility (`recommendations`, `text`, optional):** Provide practical suggestions for improving accessibility in academic publishing.
*   **Contact email for follow-up (`contact_email`, `email`, optional):** Enter your email address if you agree to be contacted for follow-up on accessibility initiatives.
