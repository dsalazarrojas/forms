# Life History Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Life History Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information about the respondent's early life, occupation, education, family and employment status. It is intended to be used to gather data on a person's life history to help inform research or decision-making processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the correct answers for each field based on your personal experiences and circumstances.
2. Fill in the date of your birth in the "Birthdate" field.
3. Choose your occupation in early life from the options provided in the "Occupation in Early Life" field.
4. Select your current occupation from the options provided in the "Current Occupation" field.
5. Choose your education level from the options provided in the "Education" field.
6. Select your family status from the options provided in the "Family Status" field.
7. Choose your employment status in early life from the options provided in the "Employment in Early Life" field.
8. Select your place of residence in early life from the options provided in the "Place of Residence" field.
9. Choose your employment status from the options provided in the "Employment Status" field.
10. Select whether you have a family income from the options provided in the "Family Income" field.
11. Choose your family type from the options provided in the "Family Type" field.
12. Select the size of your family from the options provided in the "Family Size" field.
13. Select whether you had a family income in early life from the options provided in the "Family Income in Early Life" field.
14. Select whether you have a family income currently from the options provided in the "Family Income Current" field.
15. Select the size of your family in early life from the options provided in the "Family Size in Early Life" field.
16. Select the type of your current employment in the "Employment Early Life 2" field.
17. Select the income of your family currently 2 from the "Family Income Current 2" field.
18. Select your marital status from the options provided in the "Marital Status" field.
19. Select your employment status in early life 2 from the options provided in the "Employment Early Life 2" field.
20. Select whether you had a family income 2 in early life from the options provided in the "Family Income 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Birthdate**: The date of your birth.
* **Occupation in Early Life**: Your occupation in early life, for example, High School, College, University, etc.
* **Current Occupation**: Your current occupation, for example, Employed, Self employed, Retired, etc.
* **Education**: Your level of education, for example, High School, College, University, etc.
* **Family Status**: Your family status, for example, Single, In a relationship, Married, etc.
* **Employment in Early Life**: Your employment status in early life, for example, Employed, Self employed, Retired, etc.
* **Place of Residence**: Your place of residence in early life, for example, Rural, Urban, etc.
* **Employment Status**: Your current employment status, for example, Employed, Unemployed, Other, etc.
* **Family Income**: Whether you have a family income or not, for example, True, False, etc.
* **Family Type**: The type of your family, for example, Nuclear family, Single parent family, Other family, etc.
* **Family Size**: The size of your family, for example, 1-2, 3-4, 5-6, etc.
* **Family Income in Early Life**: Whether you had a family income in early life or not, for example, True, False, etc.
* **Family Income Current**: Whether you have a family income currently or not, for example, True, False, etc.
* **Family Size in Early Life**: The size of your family in early life, for example, 1-2, 3-4, 5-6, etc.
* **Family Income Current 2**: The income of your family currently, for example, True, False, etc.
* **Marital Status**: Your marital status, for example, Single, In a relationship, Married, etc.
* **Employment Early Life 2**: Your employment status in early life, for example, Employed, Self employed, Retired, etc.
* **Family Income 2**: Whether you had a family income 2 in early life or not, for example, True, False, etc.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure of any of the options, please select the option that best fits your circumstances.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
