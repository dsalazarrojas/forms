# Elf Character Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you create a comprehensive profile of a fictional elf character. It includes questions about the elf's physical appearance, personality traits, involvement in a story, motivations, skills, relationships, and other characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by reading through each section carefully and choosing the options that best fit your character.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
3. For text fields, provide a brief description or answer to the question.
4. Don't worry about completing all the questions at once. Fill out the form in sections, and come back to it later if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief introduction to the elf character.
* **What do you think an elf would look like?**: Choose an appearance that fits your character, such as a tall and slender build or a short and sturdy build.
* **What personality traits does the elf have?**: Select all that apply from the list, such as Adventurous, Brave, and Clever.
* **How involved is the elf in the story?**: Choose how much the elf is involved, such as Main character, Supporting character, or Background character.
* **What drives the elf?**: A brief description or answer to what motivates the elf, such as a desire for adventure or a need to protect others.
* **What is the elf's name?**: A brief name or nickname for the elf.
* **How old is the elf?**: A numerical age for the elf, such as 200 or 500.
* **Where does the elf live or travel to?**: Select all that apply from the list, such as Forests, Mountains, or Cities.
* **Who is the elf related to?**: Select all that apply from the list, such as Parent, Sibling, Spouse, or Friend.
* **What skills does the elf have?**: Select all that apply from the list, such as Archery, Archery (bows and arrows), Singing, or Magic.
* **What languages does the elf speak?**: Select all that apply from the list, such as Elf-speak, Common speech, or Singing.
* **What is the elf's background?**: A brief description or answer to the elf's backstory.
* **What are the elf's interests?**: Select all that apply from the list, such as Music, Dancing, Art, or Traveling.
* **What is the elf's environment or habitat?**: Select all that apply from the list, such as Woodland, Mountain, Forests, or Cities.
* **What is the elf's health status?**: Select all that apply from the list, such as Healthy, Sick, or Injured.
* **What is the elf's diet?**: Select all that apply from the list, such as Omnivorous, Herbivorous, or Carnivorous.
* **What is the elf's religion or spirituality?**: Select all that apply from the list, such as Spiritual, Secular, or Agnostic.
* **What is the elf's ethnicity?**: Select all that apply from the list, such as Elf, Human, Dwarf, or Other (please specify).
* **What is the elf's occupation?**: Select all that apply from the list, such as Hunter, Warrior, Healer, or Other (please specify).
* **What is the elf's income?**: A brief description or answer to the elf's income, such as a noble's stipend.
* **What is the elf's family status?**: Select all that apply from the list, such as Single, Partnered, or Married.
* **What are the elf's abilities?**: Select all that apply from the list, such as Flight, Telekinesis, Telepathy, or Shape shifting.
* **What are the elf's disabilities?**: Select all that apply from the list, such as Blind, Deaf, or Wheelchair.
* **Additional Notes**: A space to add any additional information about the elf.
