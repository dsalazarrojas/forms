# pre_qualification_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pre-qualification questionnaire is a form used to gather information about a candidate's background and qualifications. This form is intended to help evaluate a candidate's suitability for a position or program and is typically used in the initial stages of the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions carefully and answer the questions as accurately as possible.
2. Enter your contact information in the "contact_info" field.
3. Describe your relevant experience in the "experience" field.
4. List your relevant skills in the "skills" field.
5. Provide references for your work experience in the "references" field.
6. If applicable, provide information about your previous work experience in the "previous_work" field.
7. Select your availability from the options in the "availability" field.
8. List any additional relevant skills you have in the "skills_2" field.
9. Provide references for your additional skills in the "references_2" field.
10. If necessary, provide further information about your skills in the "skills_3" field.
11. Provide additional references for your skills in the "references_3" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **contact_info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
* **experience** (`experience`, text, optional): Describe your relevant work experience, highlighting any achievements or accomplishments.
* **skills** (`skills`, text, optional): List your relevant skills, including any certifications or training you have received.
* **references** (`references`, text, optional): Provide references for your work experience, including names and contact information.
* **previous_work** (`previous_work`, text, optional): If applicable, provide information about your previous work experience.
* **availability** (`availability`, select_one, optional): Select your availability from the options.
* **skills_2** (`skills_2`, select_multiple, optional): List any additional relevant skills you have.
* **references_2** (`references_2`, text, optional): Provide references for your additional skills.
* **skills_3** (`skills_3`, text, optional): If necessary, provide further information about your skills.
* **references_3** (`references_3`, text, optional): Provide additional references for your skills.
