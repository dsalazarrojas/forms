# Riddle Creation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you create and share riddles. It gathers information about the riddle, its answer, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of your riddle in the "Riddle Input" field.
2. Provide the answer to your riddle in the "Answer" field.
3. Write a brief explanation for your riddle in the "Explanation" field.
4. Enter information about the person who created the riddle in the "Creator Info" field.
5. Choose the type of answer you expect ("Riddle", "Answer", or "Both").
6. Select the type of riddle you have created ("Classic" or "Cryptic").
7. Set the difficulty level of your riddle as a number (e.g., 1-5).
8. If applicable, set a time limit for solving the riddle.
9. Finally, click the "Submit" button to create your riddle.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Riddle Input** (`Riddle Input`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the text of your riddle here.
* **Answer** (`Answer`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the answer to your riddle, which should be a single number.
* **Explanation** (`Explanation`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your riddle, including any hints or clues.
* **Creator Info** (`Creator Info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter information about the person who created the riddle, such as their name and date.
* **Answer Type** (`Answer Type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose how you want the answer to be treated ("Riddle", "Answer", or "Both").
* **Riddle Type** (`Riddle Type`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the type of riddle you created ("Classic" or "Cryptic").
* **Difficulty Level** (`Difficulty Level`, `number`, required/optional): Enter a number to indicate how difficult you think the riddle is (e.g., 1-5).
* **Time Limit** (`Time Limit`, `time`, required/optional): If you want to set a time limit for solving the riddle, enter the time in minutes and seconds (e.g., 00:30).
* **Submit** (`Submit`, `text`, required/optional): Click this button to submit your riddle creation information.
