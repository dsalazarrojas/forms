# veneer_comfort_and_speech_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The veneer_comfort_and_speech_survey is a form designed to collect feedback from individuals who have undergone a treatment. It helps to gauge their comfort level, sensitivity, and overall satisfaction with the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the most appropriate answer for each question based on your experience with the treatment.
2. For the "comfort_level", "sensitivity_level", and "overall_satisfaction" fields, choose from the available options (e.g., Very Comfortable, Not Noticeable, Very Satisfied).
3. For the "speech_level" field, select one of the three options (e.g., No, Yes - Slightly, Yes - Noticeably).
4. Fill in all required fields (indicated by *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. comfort_level
*   <label> (comfort_level, select_multiple, *)</label>: How would you rate your overall comfort level after treatment?

### 2. sensitivity_level
*   <label> (sensitivity_level, select_multiple, *)</label>: How would you rate your level of sensitivity after treatment?

### 3. speech_level
*   <label> (speech_level, select_multiple, *)</label>: Has your speech changed after treatment?

### 4. overall_satisfaction
*   <label> (overall_satisfaction, select_multiple, *)</label>: How satisfied are you with the overall treatment?

## Tips
*   Please answer all required fields to ensure accurate feedback.
*   Select one option per field, as multiple selections are not allowed.
*   If you're unsure about any question, please ask your healthcare provider or treatment administrator for guidance.
