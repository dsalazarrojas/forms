# corporate_diversity_initiatives_impact_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps departments report on the impact and progress of their diversity and inclusion initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
Before you start, make sure you are familiar with your department's diversity and inclusion initiatives and progress. Answer the questions honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Diversity and Inclusion Efforts** (`demographic_info`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of your department's diversity and inclusion efforts.
* **Departmental Initiatives** (`department_prophets`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the departmental initiatives that your department is currently implementing. (e.g. Diversity and Inclusion Committee, Employee Resource Groups, Leadership Development Programs, Mentorship Programs, Training and Development)
* **Employee Feedback** (`employee_feedback`, `text`, optional): If desired, you can provide feedback from employees regarding the initiatives.
* **Departmental Initiatives** (`departmental_initiatives`, `select_one`, required): Select one departmental initiative that is currently the most effective.
* **Perceived Impact** (`perceived_impact`, `number`, required): Estimate the perceived impact of your department's diversity and inclusion initiatives on employees.
* **Departmental Impact** (`departmental_impact`, `time`, required): Estimate the time required to see the impact of your department's diversity and inclusion initiatives.
* **Departmental Impact Frequency** (`departmental_impact_frequency`, `select_one`, required): How frequently do you see the impact of your department's diversity and inclusion initiatives? (Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely)
* **Departmental Progress** (`departmental_progress`, `date`, optional): If desired, provide a date for when the impact of your department's diversity and inclusion initiatives is expected.
* **Departmental Progress Frequency** (`departmental_progress_frequency`, `select_one`, required): How frequently do you see progress with your department's diversity and inclusion initiatives? (Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely)
* **Departmental Challenges** (`departmental_challenges`, `text`, optional): If desired, provide any challenges you face with your department's diversity and inclusion initiatives.
* **Departmental Challenges Frequency** (`departmental_challenges_frequency`, `select_one`, required): How frequently do you face challenges with your department's diversity and inclusion initiatives? (Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely)
* **Departmental Suggestions** (`departmental_suggestions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Suggest any new diversity and inclusion initiatives that could benefit your department.
* **Departmental Suggestions Frequency** (`departmental_suggestions_frequency`, `select_one`, required): How frequently do you suggest new diversity and inclusion initiatives? (Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely)
* **Departmental Suggestions Impact** (`departmental_suggestions_impact`, `number`, optional): Estimate the potential impact of your new diversity and inclusion initiatives suggestions.
* **Departmental Suggestions Impact Frequency** (`departmental_suggestions_impact_frequency`, `select_one`, required): How frequently do you think new diversity and inclusion initiatives would have an impact on your department? (Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely)
* **Departmental Suggestions Progress** (`departmental_suggestions_progress`, `date`, optional): If desired, provide a date for when you expect to see progress with new diversity and inclusion initiatives.
* **Departmental Suggestions Progress Frequency** (`departmental_suggestions_progress_frequency`, `select_one`, required): How frequently do you expect to see progress with new diversity and inclusion initiatives? (Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely)
* **Departmental Suggestions Challenges** (`departmental_suggestions_challenges`, `text`, optional): If desired, provide any challenges you face with new diversity and inclusion initiatives.
* **Departmental Suggestions Challenges Frequency** (`departmental_suggestions_challenges_frequency`, `select_one`, required): How frequently do you face challenges with new diversity and inclusion initiatives? (Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely)
* **Departmental Suggestions Implementation** (`departmental_suggestions_implementation`, `select_multiple`, optional): Suggest which diversity and inclusion initiatives you would like to implement.
* **Departmental Suggestions Implementation Frequency** (`departmental_suggestions_implementation_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): How frequently do you suggest to implement new diversity and inclusion initiatives? (Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely)
* **Departmental Suggestions Implementation Status** (`departmental_suggestions_implementation_status`, `select_one`, optional): Report the status of new diversity and inclusion initiatives that have been implemented.
* **Departmental Suggestions Implementation Status Frequency** (`departmental_suggestions_implementation_status_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): How frequently do you report the status of new diversity and inclusion initiatives? (Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely)
* **Departmental Suggestions Implementation Status Progress** (`departmental_suggestions_implementation_status_progress`, `date`, optional): If desired, provide a date for when you expect to report the status of new diversity and inclusion initiatives.
* **Departmental Suggestions Implementation Status Progress Frequency** (`departmental_suggestions_implementation_status_progress_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): How frequently do you report the status of new diversity and inclusion initiatives progress? (Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely)

Note: Please answer all required fields to the best of your ability.
