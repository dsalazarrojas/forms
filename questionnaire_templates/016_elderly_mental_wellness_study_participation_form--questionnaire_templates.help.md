# Elderly Mental Wellness Study Participation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the mental wellness of elderly individuals. It is part of a study aimed at understanding the mental health and wellbeing of seniors. The form is intended for individuals aged 65+.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please answer each question honestly to the best of your ability.
2. Rate your answers on a scale of 1-4, where 1 is "Not at all" and 4 is "Always".
3. Fill out all the questions, as they are all relevant to the study.
