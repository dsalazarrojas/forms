# Emotional AI Safety Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to help organizations identify and assess potential risks and benefits associated with the implementation of AI in the workplace. It helps gather information from stakeholders about the type and level of risk, and potential benefits of using AI.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form with the name of the individual or entity that will be affected by the implementation of AI.
* Select the type of risk (AI, Data, or User) associated with the implementation of AI.
* Choose the level of risk (Low, Medium, or High) associated with the implementation of AI.
* Provide a description of the risk.
* If applicable, describe a safeguard or mitigation measure to address the risk.
* Describe the expected benefit of using AI.
* Enter a submission review note (if any).
* Optionally, provide your email address and phone number for further contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stakeholder**: This field is used to enter the name of the individual or entity that will be affected by the implementation of AI. For example, "John Doe" or "Department of Marketing".
* **Risk Type**: This field is used to select the type of risk associated with the implementation of AI. The options are:
	+ AI: Risk associated with the AI system itself.
	+ Data: Risk associated with the data used to train or feed the AI system.
	+ User: Risk associated with the users interacting with the AI system.
* **Risk Level**: This field is used to select the level of risk associated with the implementation of AI. The options are:
	+ Low: Minimal risk, no significant impact on the organization.
	+ Medium: Moderate risk, potential impact on the organization.
	+ High: High risk, significant impact on the organization.
* **Risk Description**: This field is used to provide a description of the risk.
* **Safeguard**: This field is used to describe any safeguard or mitigation measure to address the risk. For example, "Regular security audits and penetration testing".
* **Benefit**: This field is used to describe the expected benefit of using AI.
* **Submission Review**: This field is used to enter any submission review note. This is an optional field for the reviewer to add any additional comments or concerns.
* **Email**: This field is used to enter your email address for further contact.
* **Phone**: This field is used to enter your phone number for further contact.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide a clear and concise description of the risk and benefit.
* Be specific when selecting the risk type and level.
* Use the submission review field to add any additional comments or concerns.
* Ensure to keep your email and phone fields up to date in case of further communication.
