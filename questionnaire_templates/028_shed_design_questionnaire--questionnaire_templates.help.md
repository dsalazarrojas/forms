# Shed Design Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This shed design questionnaire is a tool to help gather information about a client's requirements and needs for a shed design project. It collects details on client preferences, site specifics, and other relevant information to inform the design process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page and make sure you understand what information is being requested.
2. Answer all required fields (if any) as thoroughly as possible.
3. If you're unsure about any question or field, don't hesitate to reach out to the contact provided for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Requirements (Page 1)
* **Client Requirements**: This is a note field where you can add any relevant information about your shed design project. Use this space to provide context or details that might impact the design.

### Preferred Sizes (Page 1)
* **Preferred Sizes**: Select all the preferred sizes for your shed from the available options. You can choose one or multiple sizes depending on your needs.

### Materials (Page 1)
* **Materials**: This is a text field where you can specify the materials you would like to use for your shed design. Be as detailed as possible to help the designer understand your preferences.

### Site Details (Page 1)
* **Site Details**: This is a date field where you can select a specific date for the shed's construction or installation. Note that this field is not required, so feel free to leave it empty if not applicable.

### Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible for each field to ensure the designer understands your needs and preferences.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the contact provided.
* You can modify this form to suit your specific needs or use it as a starting point to create your own form.
