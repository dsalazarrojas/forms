## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Job Site
**Job Site** (1, select_one, No): This field determines the location where the worker is exposed to heat-related hazards.

*   Select "Yes" if the job site is outdoors
*   Select "No" if the job site is indoors

### Job Type
**Job Type** (2, select_multiple, No): This field identifies the type of job or activity that exposes the worker to heat-related hazards.

*   Select all applicable options (e.g., construction, maintenance, landscaping)
*   Do not select if the job type is not applicable

### Weather Station ID
**Weather Station ID** (3, number, No): This field is not applicable for this form. It is a placeholder for future development.

*   Enter the weather station ID if it is available
*   If not available, leave this field blank

### Wind Speed MPH
**Wind Speed MPH** (4, number, No): This field asks for the wind speed at the job site.

*   Enter the wind speed in miles per hour (MPH) if available
*   If not available, leave this field blank

### Max Heat Index
**Max Heat Index** (5, number, No): This field asks for the maximum heat index at the job site.

*   Enter the maximum heat index if available
*   If not available, leave this field blank

### Exposure Hours
**Exposure Hours** (6, number, No): This field asks for the duration of exposure to heat-related hazards.

*   Enter the total exposure hours if available
*   If not available, leave this field blank

### Temperature Range
**Temperature Range** (7, select_one, No): This field asks about the temperature range at the job site.

*   Select "Yes" if the temperature range is high
*   Select "No" if the temperature range is not high

### Wind Direction
**Wind Direction** (8, select_one, No): This field asks about the wind direction at the job site.

*   Select "Yes" if the wind direction is from an open ocean or sea
*   Select "No" if the wind direction is not from an open ocean or sea

### Humidity Range
**Humidity Range** (9, select_one, No): This field asks about the humidity range at the job site.

*   Select "Yes" if the humidity range is high
*   Select "No" if the humidity range is not high

### Note
**Note** (10, note, No): This field is for additional comments or notes about the job site and the worker's exposure to heat-related hazards.

*   Provide any relevant information about the job site and the worker's exposure
*   This field is optional but can be useful for further evaluation and assessment

## Tips

*   Complete this form accurately and honestly to ensure a thorough heat exposure risk assessment
*   If you have concerns about your heat exposure risk, please consult a medical professional
*   This form is a self-assessment tool and not a substitute for medical evaluations
*   Keep a record of your completed forms for future reference and evaluation
