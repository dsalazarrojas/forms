# information_retrieval_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on user's search performance, search engine usage, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Select your search skills from the options provided.
2. Provide explanations for your search queries (if applicable).
3. Choose your confidence level in the answer(s) provided.
4. Select the search engine used for the search.
5. Enter any search terms used.
6. Specify if results are displayed on the search engine's results page.
7. Enter your user ID (if applicable).
8. Enter the date and time of the search.
9. Specify the duration of the search.
10. Choose the location where the search was performed.
11. Specify if any search devices were used.
12. Choose the method used for the search.
13. Enter any additional comments or notes.
14. Select the submission status of the form.
15. Enter your name as the form submitter.
16. Enter the date of form submission.
17. Enter your email address.
18. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
19. Enter your address (if applicable).
20. Enter any additional comments.
21. Choose your rating for the search performance.
22. Enter your confidence level for the rating.
23. Enter any submitted text.
24. Enter the time and date of submission (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Search Skills** (`search_skills`, select_multiple, required: false): This field asks you to select the search skills you possess. Choose all that apply.
- **Explanations** (`explanations`, text, required: false): Provide any explanations or comments for your search queries.
- **Confidence Rating** (`confidence_rating`, select_one, required: true): Rate your confidence level in the answer provided.
- **Search Engine** (`search_engine`, select_one, required: true): Choose the search engine used for the search.
- **Search Terms** (`search_terms`, text, required: false): Enter any search terms used.
- **Results Display** (`results_display`, select_multiple, required: false): Specify if results are displayed on the search engine's results page.
- **User ID** (`user_id`, number, required: false): Enter your user ID (if applicable).
- **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments.
- **Date Searched** (`date_searched`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the search.
- **Time Searched** (`time_searched`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the search.
- **Search Duration** (`search_duration`, text, required: false): Enter the duration of the search.
- **Search Location** (`search_location`, select_one, required: true): Choose the location where the search was performed.
- **Search Device** (`search_device`, select_multiple, required: false): Specify if any search devices were used.
- **Search Method** (`search_method`, select_one, required: true): Choose the method used for the search.
- **Notes** (`notes`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes.
- **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, required: true): Select the status of form submission.
- **Form Submitter** (`form_submitter`, text, required: false): Enter your name as the form submitter.
- **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of form submission.
- **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
- **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your address (if applicable).
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments.
- **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, required: true): Choose your rating for the search performance.
- **Rating Confidence** (`rating_confidence`, text, required: false): Enter your confidence level for the rating.
- **Submitted** (`submitted`, text, required: false): Enter any submitted text.
- **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of form submission (if applicable).

## Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the data collected is reliable and useful for analysis. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
