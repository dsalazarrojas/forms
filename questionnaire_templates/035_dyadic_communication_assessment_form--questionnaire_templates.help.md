# Dyadic Communication Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dyadic Communication Assessment Form is a tool to evaluate the quality of communication between two individuals, focusing on both verbal and nonverbal aspects of interaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each question and rate the communication between the two participants accordingly. You can choose from the provided options for each question, and you don't need to answer all questions. You can skip any question if it doesn't apply to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`page1`, `note`, required: false): This is an introduction to the form. Please read it carefully to understand the purpose and scope of the assessment.
* **How do the two participants communicate with each other?** (`dyadic_communication`, `text`, required: false): Describe the overall quality of communication between the two participants.
* **Verbal Communication** (`verbal_communication`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the verbal communication between the two participants. Choose from Very good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Nonverbal Communication** (`nonverbal_communication`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the nonverbal cues you have observed, such as facial expressions, body language, eye contact, or paralanguage.
* **Conflict Resolution** (`conflict_resolution`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate how easily conflicts are resolved between the two participants. Choose from Very easy, Easy, Neutral, Hard, or Very hard.
* **Supportiveness** (`supportiveness`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the ways in which the two participants provide support to each other, such as positive feedback or encouragement.
* **Emotional Intelligence** (`emotional_intelligence`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the emotional intelligence of the two participants. Choose from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Emotional Resilience** (`emotional_resilience`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the emotional resilience of the two participants. Choose from Very high, High, Medium, Low, or Very low.
* **Emotional Expression** (`emotional_expression`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the ways in which the two participants express their emotions, such as openly or closed off.
* **Communication Style** (`communication_style`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the communication styles you have observed, such as direct or indirect.
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the communication skills you have observed, such as good or poor.
* **Teamwork** (`teamwork`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the ways in which the two participants work together, such as jointly or separately.
* **Trust** (`trust`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the ways in which the two participants trust each other, such as high or low.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the ways in which the two participants give or receive feedback, such as positive or negative.
* **Advice** (`advice`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the ways in which the two participants give or receive advice, such as yes or no.
* **Decision Making** (`decision_making`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the ways in which the two participants make decisions, such as jointly or separately.
* **Goal Orientation** (`goal_orientation`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the ways in which the two participants have goals, such as shared or unshared.
* **Goal Concordance** (`goal_concordance`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate the goal concordance between the two participants. Choose from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Communication Style** (`communication_style`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the communication styles you have observed, such as formal or informal.
* **Verbal Cues** (`verbal_cues`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the verbal cues you have observed, such as yes or no.
* **Emotional Expression Level** (`emotional_expression_level`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate the emotional expression level of the two participants. Choose from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Openness to Change** (`openness_to_change`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate if the two participants are open to change. Choose from Yes or No.
* **Openness to Feedback** (`openness_to_feedback`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate if the two participants are open to feedback. Choose from Yes or No.
* **Cooperation** (`cooperation`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate the cooperation level of the two participants. Choose from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Cooperation Level** (`cooperation_level`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the ways in which the two participants cooperate with each other, such as high or low.

Note: This is just a sample form and should not be considered an official assessment tool. If you want to use the results for professional purposes, please refer to an actual expert in communication assessment.
