# Trail Camera Battery Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on your trail camera usage and needs to provide a recommendation for the best battery type.

## How To Complete This Form
To get a recommendation, simply answer the questions in the following sections:

### Usage Pattern
Choose your usage pattern:
* Field: For field observations or wildlife tracking
* Game: For hunting or game monitoring
* Trail: For tracking or monitoring on a trail
* Security: For security cameras
* Monitoring: For general monitoring purposes

### Environment
Select your environment:
* Cold: For use in very cold environments
* Hot: For use in extremely hot environments
* Humid: For use in humid environments
* Wet: For use near water
* Dry: For use in dry environments

### Other Sections
For the other fields, please answer according to your specific requirements and needs.
