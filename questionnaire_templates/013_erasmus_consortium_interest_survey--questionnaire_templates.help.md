# Erasmus+ Consortium Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from partner organizations about their interest in participating in the Erasmus+ Consortium. The purpose of this form is to gauge the level of interest and readiness of partner organizations to join the consortium. It will help us understand how many partner organizations are interested in joining the consortium and what their specific interests and requirements are.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer all questions honestly.
2. If you are a partner organization interested in joining the consortium, select "Yes" for the "Partner Organizations" question.
3. Fill in your organization's name, email address, phone number, and submitter's information in the required fields.
4. For the "Readiness Score" and "Interests Score" questions, provide a numerical score out of 100.
5. Select your organization's specific interests from the list provided.
6. Provide any additional comments or information you would like to share in the "Submitter Comments" and "Partner Comments" fields.
7. Review your form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Partner Organizations** (`survey_partners`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" if you are a partner organization interested in joining the consortium.
* **Readiness Score** (`readiness_score`, number, optional): Provide a numerical score out of 100 to indicate your organization's readiness to join the consortium.
* **Interests Score** (`interests_score`, number, optional): Provide a numerical score out of 100 to indicate your organization's interests in participating in the consortium.
* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, required): Fill in your organization's name.
* **Partner Email** (`partner_email`, email, required): Enter your organization's email address.
* **Partner Phone** (`partner_phone`, text, optional): Fill in your organization's phone number (if applicable).
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required): Fill in the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Fill in the phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Affiliation** (`submitter_affiliation`, text, optional): Fill in the affiliation of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Institution** (`submitter_institution`, text, optional): Fill in the institution of the person submitting the form.
* **Partner Interests** (`partner_interests`, select_one, required): Select your organization's specific interests from the list provided.
* **Submitter Comments** (`submitter_comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or information you would like to share.
* **Partner Comments** (`partner_comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or information your organization would like to share.
