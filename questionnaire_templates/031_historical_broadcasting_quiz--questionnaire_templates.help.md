# Historical Broadcasting Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect historical broadcasting information from users. It is intended to be a quiz that tests users' knowledge on a specific topic.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the required information on each page as requested.
*   Select the correct answer for each question.
*   If you have any additional comments or thoughts, you can provide them in the "historical_broadcasting_quiz_note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Historical Broadcasting Quiz** (`historical_broadcating_quiz`, text, optional): This is the title of the form.
    *   Enter a title for the quiz.
*   **Historical Broadcasting Quiz Question 1** (`historical_broadcasting_quiz_question_1`, select_one, optional): This is the first question of the quiz.
    *   Select one of the options provided.
*   **Historical Broadcasting Quiz Question 2** (`historical_broadcasting_quiz_question_2`, select_multiple, optional): This is the second question of the quiz.
    *   Select all that apply from the options provided.
*   **Historical Broadcasting Quiz Question 3** (`historical_broadcasting_quiz_question_3`, date, optional): This is the third question of the quiz.
    *   Enter a date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
*   **Historical Broadcasting Quiz Question 4** (`historical_broadcasting_quiz_question_4`, time, optional): This is the fourth question of the quiz.
    *   Enter a time in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
*   **Historical Broadcasting Quiz Note** (`historical_broadcasting_quiz_note`, note, optional): This is a field for additional comments or thoughts.
    *   Enter any additional comments or thoughts you may have.
*   **Historical Broadcasting Quiz Email** (`historical_broadcasting_quiz_email`, email, optional): This is a field for entering an email address.
    *   Enter a valid email address.
*   **Historical Broadcasting Quiz Phone** (`historical_broadcasting_quiz_phone`, text, optional): This is a field for entering a phone number.
    *   Enter a phone number.
