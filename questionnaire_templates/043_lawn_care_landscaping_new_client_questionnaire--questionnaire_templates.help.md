# Lawn Care Landscaping New Client Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about new clients seeking lawn care and landscaping services. By filling out this form, you will help us better understand your lawn's needs, preferences, and budget, enabling us to provide tailored solutions that meet your expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lawn Care Service** (`lawn_care_service`, text, optional): Briefly describe the service you are interested in, such as lawn mowing, fertilization, or pruning.
* **Lawn Size** (`lawn_size`, number, optional): Provide an approximate size of your lawn, in square meters or acres.
* **Lawn Shape** (`lawn_shape`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose the shape of your lawn from the options provided.
* **Grass Type** (`grass_type`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all the types of grass you have on your lawn.
* **Care Preferences** (`care_preferences`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose your preferred frequency of lawn care, such as weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly.
* **Landscaping Style** (`landscaping_style`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select the style of landscaping you would like to have on your lawn, such as modern, traditional, or natural.
* **Budget** (`budget`, number, optional): Estimate your available budget for the services.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further discussion or clarification.

## Tips
- Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.
- Use the options provided for select fields to ensure correct selections.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
