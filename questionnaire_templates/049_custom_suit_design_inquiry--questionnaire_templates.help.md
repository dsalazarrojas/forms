# Custom Suit Design Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your preferences for custom suit design. Please provide your input to ensure we create a well-fitted and stylish suit tailored to your needs. We'll use the information collected here to guide our design process and create a perfect suit for you.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions truthfully, as your preferences will directly impact the design of your custom suit.
2. For select fields, choose the options that best fit your style and preferences.
3. Enter your email address in the format of `example@example.com` or `your_name@email.com`.
4. Enter your phone number in the format of `123-456-7890` or `+1-123-456-7890`.
5. If you have any additional comments or requests, please type them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Custom Suit Design Inquiry Form**: This field is a text input where you can describe your design preferences or requirements for your custom suit.
* **Custom Suit Design Preferences**: This field is a select menu where you can choose one of the following options:
	+ What color would you prefer? - Choose between White, Red, and other colors (if available).
* **Measurement Details**: This field is a text input where you can provide more detailed information about your measurements (if you're unsure about what to input, you can leave it blank).
* **What is your body type?**: This field is a select menu where you can choose between:
	+ Slim Fit
	+ Regular Fit
	+ Athletic Fit
* **What is your preferred jacket length?**: This field is a select menu where you can choose between:
	+ Short
	+ Long
	+ Three-quarter length
* **What is your preferred sleeve length?**: This field is a select menu where you can choose between:
	+ Short
	+ Long
	+ Three-quarter sleeves
* **What is your preferred pant length?**: This field is a select menu where you can choose between:
	+ Short
	+ Long
	+ Three-quarter pants
* **What style of pants would you prefer?**: This field is a select menu where you can choose between:
	+ Formal
	+ Casual
	+ Athletic
* **What fabric type would you prefer?**: This field is a multiselect menu where you can choose between:
	+ Cotton
	+ Wool
	+ Linen
* **What color of pants would you prefer?**: This field is a multiselect menu where you can choose between:
	+ Black
	+ Gray
	+ Beige
* **Email**: Enter your email address in the format `example@example.com` or `your_name@email.com`.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in the format `123-456-7890` or `+1-123-456-7890`.
* **Additional Comments**: This field is a text input where you can provide any additional comments or requests.
* **What is your shirt size?**: Enter your shirt size as a number.

Please note that fields marked as required will be indicated in the form as such, and you will need to fill them out.
