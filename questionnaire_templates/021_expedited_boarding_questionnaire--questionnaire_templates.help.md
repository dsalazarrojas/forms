# Expedited Boarding Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Expedited Boarding Questionnaire is a form designed to collect essential information from passengers to facilitate efficient boarding processes. It is intended to gather details from passengers, their traveling companions, and contact information for the flight.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Answer each question carefully, ensuring you provide the most accurate details.
3. Review your answers for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Page
* **First Page**: This is the initial page of the form where you can provide any relevant comments or questions that need to be addressed before proceeding.
* Note: This field is not required.

### Passenger Details
* **Passenger Details**: Enter the personal details of the passenger(s) including name, contact information, and any other relevant information that may be required for expedited boarding.
* Note: This field is not required.

### Contact Info
* **Contact Info**: Provide contact information of the passenger or their representative, including email, phone number, and any other relevant contact methods.
* Note: This field is not required.

### Travel Details
* **Travel Details**: Enter travel dates, flight numbers, or any other relevant travel-related details to facilitate efficient boarding processes.
* Note: This field is not required.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check all the information provided before submitting the form to avoid any errors or mistakes.
* If you have any difficulties or questions while filling the form, please contact us at form@gic.mx for assistance.
