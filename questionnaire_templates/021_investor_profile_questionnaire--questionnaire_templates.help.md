# Investor Profile Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investor Profile Questionnaire is designed to collect information from investors about their characteristics, preferences, and circumstances to help us better understand their needs and tailor our services accordingly. This helps us provide more relevant content, offers, and experiences to our investors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the next steps:

1.  Select the correct contact information.
2.  Choose the investment preferences that best suit your needs.
3.  Answer questions about your risk tolerance, financial goals, investment experience, and portfolio.
4.  Indicate your financial status, tax status, and investment horizon.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **contact_information** (Required: False, Type: note): This field is for providing your contact details, which will be used to get in touch with you about our services and offerings.
* **investment_preferences** (Required: False, Type: select_multiple): Please select all the investment preferences that you have (Yes, No).
* **risk_tolerance** (Required: False, Type: select_one): Indicate your risk tolerance by selecting one of the options (Yes, No).
* **financial_goals** (Required: False, Type: select_multiple): Choose all your financial goals (Yes, No).
* **investment_profile** (Required: False, Type: select_multiple): Select your investment profile options (Yes, No).
* **financial_status** (Required: False, Type: select_multiple): Select your financial status options (Active, Inactive).
* **tax_status** (Required: False, Type: select_one): Indicate your tax status by selecting one of the options (Active, Inactive).
* **investment_experience** (Required: False, Type: select_multiple): Select your investment experience options (Yes, No).
* **portfolio** (Required: False, Type: select_multiple): Choose your portfolio options (Yes, No).
* **investment_horizon** (Required: False, Type: select_one): Indicate your investment horizon by selecting one of the options (Yes, No).
