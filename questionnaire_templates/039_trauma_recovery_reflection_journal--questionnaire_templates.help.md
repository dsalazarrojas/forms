# Trauma Recovery Reflection Journal - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trauma Recovery Reflection Journal is designed to help individuals track their recovery progress after experiencing a traumatic event. This journal allows you to reflect on your daily experiences, emotions, and thoughts to better understand your recovery journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. For each page, select the options that best describe your current state or feelings.
3. Be as detailed as possible in your answers to help you track your progress.
