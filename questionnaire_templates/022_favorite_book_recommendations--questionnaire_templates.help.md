# Favorite Book Recommendations - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for users to provide recommendations for two different books, providing specific details about each book such as genre, reasons for liking, and ratings. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Select "Yes" for both "Favorite Book 1" and "Favorite Book 2" options.
- Select a genre for both "Favorite Book 1" and "Favorite Book 2" from the provided options.
- Provide a reason for liking both "Favorite Book 1" and "Favorite Book 2" in the text box.
- Rate both "Favorite Book 1" and "Favorite Book 2" on a scale of 1-5.
- Enter your contact information for both "Favorite Book 1" and "Favorite Book 2" in the text box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Favorite Book 1** (`1`, `number`, required): Select a favorite book from 1 to 5.
  - This field is used to identify the first favorite book.
- **Genre 1** (`genre_1`, `select_one`, required): Select a genre for the first favorite book.
  - Choose a genre for the book you like.
  - Options: option-1, option-2, option-3.
- **Reason 1** (`reason_1`, `text`, required): Provide a reason for liking the first favorite book.
  - Write a brief explanation for why you like the first book.
  - Example: "I loved the plot."
- **Rating 1** (`rating_1`, `number`, required): Rate the first favorite book on a scale of 1-5.
  - Provide a rating from 1 to 5.
- **Contact 1** (`contact_1`, `email`, required): Enter your contact information for the first favorite book.
  - Enter your email or other valid contact information.
- **Favorite Book 2** (`favorite_book_2`, `text`, required): Select a second favorite book.
  - Enter the name of the second favorite book.
- **Genre 2** (`genre_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select multiple genres for the second favorite book.
  - Choose multiple genres for the book you like.
  - Options: option-1, option-2, option-3.
- **Reason 2** (`reason_2`, `select_one`, required): Select a reason for liking the second favorite book.
  - Choose a reason from the provided options.
  - Options: option-1, option-2, option-3.
- **Rating 2** (`rating_2`, `number`, required): Rate the second favorite book on a scale of 1-5.
  - Provide a rating from 1 to 5.
- **Contact 2** (`contact_2`, `text`, required): Enter your contact information for the second favorite book.
  - Enter your email or other valid contact information.
