# Wedding Day Photography Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Day Photography Questionnaire is designed to collect essential information from clients about their wedding day photography requirements. This form is intended to be used by photography services or studios to tailor their services to the client's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each section carefully, providing detailed information about your wedding day requirements.
2. Review each section before submitting to ensure all the necessary information is provided.
3. If any information is missing or unclear, please consult with your service provider or contact us at form@gic.mx.
4. Once completed, the form can be saved or submitted for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Client Details**
* Label: Client Details
* Type: Text Input
* Required: No
* Explanation: Enter your name and contact information so that we can keep in touch with you throughout the process.

*Example:* "John Doe" (Name), "john.doe@example.com" (Email), "01234 56789" (Phone)

*Please make sure to enter accurate contact information to avoid delays.*

### 2. **Photographer Details**
* Label: Photographer Details
* Type: Text Input
* Required: No
* Explanation: Enter your photographer's name and contact information so that we can reach out to them with any questions or concerns.

*Example:* "Jane Doe" (Name), "jane.doe@example.com" (Email), "67890 1234" (Phone)

### 3. **Services Offered**
* Label: Services Offered
* Type: Select Multiple Input
* Required: No
* Explanation: Select the photography services you are interested in, including:
	+ Wedding Album
	+ Album Editing
	+ Album Design
	+ Photo Shooting
	+ Video Shooting

*Please select all services that apply to your needs.*

### 4. **Date and Time**
* Label: Date and Time
* Type: Date Input
* Required: No
* Explanation: Enter the date and time of your wedding ceremony. Please use the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD) and time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).

*Example:* 2024-02-15 (Date), 14:00 (Time)

*Make sure to enter the correct date and time for your wedding ceremony.*

### 5. **Wedding Details**
* Label: Wedding Details
* Type: Text Input
* Required: No
* Explanation: Provide any additional information about your wedding details, such as location, size, or theme.

*Example:* "Small and intimate garden wedding with 50 guests"

*Please provide any relevant information about your wedding to help us serve you better.*

### 6. **Wedding Party**
* Label: Wedding Party
* Type: Text Input
* Required: No
* Explanation: List the members of your wedding party, including the bride, groom, and other important participants.

*Example:* "Bride: Jane Doe; Groom: John Doe; Bridesmaids: Jane's sisters, Jane's sister's friends"

*Make sure to include all essential members of your wedding party.*

### 7. **Reception Details**
* Label: Reception Details
* Type: Text Input
* Required: No
* Explanation: Provide any information about your reception, including location, food, and decorations.

*Example:* "Reception will be held at the Grand Ballroom, with a sit-down dinner and live music"

*Please provide any relevant details about your reception to help us plan better.*

### 8. **Photo Session**
* Label: Photo Session
* Type: Text Input
* Required: No
* Explanation: Describe your photo session requirements, including duration, location, and any specific needs.

*Example:* "Two-hour photo session at the park, with a mix of posed and candid shots"

*Make sure to specify your photo session requirements clearly.*

### 9. **Photo Delivery**
* Label: Photo Delivery
* Type: Text Input
* Required: No
* Explanation: Choose how you would like to receive your photos, including digital or print copies, and any specific formats.

*Example:* "Digital copies via USB drive and print copies in a photo album"

*Please select your preferred photo delivery method.*

### 10. **Payment**
* Label: Payment
* Type: Text Input
* Required: No
* Explanation: Enter your payment information, including method and amount.

*Example:* "Credit card: Visa; Amount: $1,000"

*Please ensure that your payment information is accurate and complete.*

### 11. **Notes**
* Label: Notes
* Type: Text Input
* Required: No
* Explanation: Add any additional comments or requests you have, such as specific photo requests or special instructions.

*Example:* "Please take a few shots with the wedding cake"

*Please use this section to note any additional requirements or requests.*
