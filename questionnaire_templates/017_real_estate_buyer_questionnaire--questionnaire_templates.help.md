# real_estate_buyer_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The real_estate_buyer_questionnaire is a form designed to collect information from potential real estate buyers. This form is used to gather data on the buyer's preferences, contact details, and agent information. The collected data helps real estate agents to better understand the buyer's needs and provide personalized services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the first name field.
2. Select the type of property you are looking to purchase.
3. Enter your estimated purchase price.
4. Provide your contact phone number.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Select your preferred contact time.
7. Enter your address.
8. Enter the date you would like to move in.
9. Enter your contact address.
10. Select how you found us.
11. Add any additional comments.
12. Enter the agent's name.
13. Enter the agent's phone number.
14. Enter the agent's email address.
15. Enter the agent's address.
16. Select the agent's preferred contact time.
17. Add any additional comments for the agent.
18. Enter the agent's license number.
19. Enter the agent's certification number.
20. Enter the agent's certification expiration.
21. Select the agent's certification status.
22. Select the agent's license status.
23. Enter the agent's certification number 2.
24. Enter the agent's certification number 3.
25. Enter the agent's certification number 4.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (`1`)
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.

### Property Type (`2`)
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of property you are looking to purchase.

### Estimated Purchase Price (`3`)
* **Estimated Purchase Price** (`estimated_purchase_price`, number, optional): Enter the estimated purchase price of the property.

### Contact Phone (`4`)
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.

### Email (`5`)
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.

### Preferred Contact Time (`6`)
