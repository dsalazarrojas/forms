# Bride And Groom Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from couples planning their wedding. It is meant to gather essential details about the couple, their preferences, and requirements for the wedding ceremony and reception.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your full name, including your surname, in the respective fields for the bride and groom.
2.  Enter your email address and phone number so that we can contact you easily.
3.  Select the date of your wedding.
4.  Choose the ceremony and reception venues for your wedding.
5.  Enter the expected number of guests.
6.  Describe your wedding style and theme.
7.  Select the formality level of your wedding.
8.  Enter any special color scheme you have in mind.
9.  Describe any religious or cultural elements that will be included.
10.  Share your vision for the wedding, if you have any specific ideas.
11.  Identify any non-negotiable elements you require for the wedding.
12.  Specify any elements you would like to avoid.
13.  Share details about your profession, if applicable.
14.  Describe how you met your partner.
15.  Share the story of your proposal.
16.  Indicate how long you have been together.
17.  If applicable, enter your overall wedding budget.
18.  Select the services you require from the vendors list.
19.  Choose how much time you have for planning.
20.  Share your honeymoon plans.
21.  If applicable, specify any special accommodations you might need.
22.  Use the final comments box to provide any other details or questions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Bride Name**: Enter your full name, including your surname. (<em>Required</em>)
* **Groom Name**: Enter your full name, including your surname. (<em>Required</em>)
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for easy communication. (<em>Required</em>)
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for easy contact. (<em>Required</em>)
* **Wedding Date**: Select the date of your wedding. (<em>Required</em>)
* **Ceremony Venue**: Choose the location of your ceremony. (<em>Required</em>)
* **Reception Venue**: Choose the location of your reception. (<em>Required</em>)
* **Expected Number of Guests**: Enter the approximate number of guests. (<em>Required</em>)
* **Wedding Style and Theme**: Describe your wedding style and theme. (<em>Required</em>)
* **Formality Level**: Select the formality level of your wedding. (<em>Required</em>)
* **Wedding Colors**: Enter any special color scheme you have in mind. (<em>Optional</em>)
* **Religious or Cultural Elements**: Describe any religious or cultural elements that will be included. (<em>Optional</em>)
* **Bride's Wedding Vision**: Share your vision for the wedding, if you have any specific ideas. (<em>Optional</em>)
* **Groom's Wedding Vision**: Share your vision for the wedding, if you have any specific ideas. (<em>Optional</em>)
* **Must-Have Elements**: Identify any non-negotiable elements. (<em>Optional</em>)
* **Elements to Avoid**: Specify any elements you would like to avoid. (<em>Optional</em>)
* **Bride's Profession**: Share your profession, if applicable. (<em>Optional</em>)
* **Groom's Profession**: Share your profession, if applicable. (<em>Optional</em>)
* **How Did You Meet**: Describe how you met your partner. (<em>Optional</em>)
* **Engagement Story**: Share the story of your proposal. (<em>Optional</em>)
* **How Long Have You Been Together**: Indicate how long you have been together. (<em>Optional</em>)
* **Overall Wedding Budget**: Enter your overall wedding budget, if applicable. (<em>Optional</em>)
* **Vendors You Need**: Select the services you require from the vendors list. (<em>Optional</em>)
* **Planning Timeline**: Choose how much time you have for planning. (<em>Required</em>)
* **Honeymoon Plans**: Share your honeymoon plans. (<em>Optional</em>)
* **Special Accommodations Needed**: Specify any special accommodations you might need. (<em>Optional</em>)
* **Final Comments or Questions**: Use this box to provide any other details or questions you have. (<em>Optional</em>)
