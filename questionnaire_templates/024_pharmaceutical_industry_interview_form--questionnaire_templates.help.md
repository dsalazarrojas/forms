# Pharmaceutical Industry Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals in the pharmaceutical industry, including contact details, work experience, education, and company information. The form aims to gather relevant data that can be used for internal purposes, such as market research or analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the form in a new tab or browser window.
2. Scroll down to the first field, "Contact Information", and enter your name, email, and phone number as requested.
3. Move on to the next field, "Work Experience", and describe your relevant work experience in the pharmaceutical industry.
4. Next, fill out "Education and Training", and provide information about your educational background and any relevant training.
5. Then, fill out "Job Responsibilities" and describe your current job responsibilities.
6. Finally, provide "Company Information" and enter your company name, address, and any other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information (Required/Optional)**: Enter your name, email, and phone number to help us contact you.
* **Work Experience (Optional)**: Describe your relevant work experience in the pharmaceutical industry. This could include your job title, company, and dates of employment.
* **Education and Training (Optional)**: Enter information about your educational background, including degrees earned, institutions attended, and relevant courses or training programs completed.
* **Job Responsibilities (Optional)**: Describe your current job responsibilities in the pharmaceutical industry.
* **Company Information (Optional)**: Enter your company name, address, and any other relevant details.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
