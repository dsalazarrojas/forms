# Jewelry Customer Design Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers who are interested in custom jewelry design. It is intended to be used by customers who want to inquire about custom jewelry design services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" section.
3. Specify your design preferences in the "Design Preferences" section.
4. Provide any additional design details in the "Design Details" section.
5. Choose your jewelry type, ring size, and ring metal in the "Jewelry Type" section.
6. Select your preferred diamond type, shape, and material in the "Diamond Type" section.
7. Choose your preferred color scheme and ring material in the "Color Scheme" and "Ring Material" sections.
8. Upload a design image or select a design type in the "Design Image" section.
9. Enter your email, phone number, name, address, city, and country in the required fields.
10. Enter a message in the "Message" field.
11. Click the "Submit" button to submit your inquiry.
