# cryptography_knowledge_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to assess your understanding and knowledge of various cryptography concepts, including types, methods, and algorithms. This form is designed to evaluate your ability to think critically and apply your knowledge in real-world scenarios.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please take a few minutes to read each question carefully and think about your answers.
2. Answer each question in the best way you can, based on your knowledge and experience.
3. You can choose multiple answers for questions that allow it.
4. Do not worry if you're not sure about some questions, but try your best.
5. When you're finished, review your answers to ensure they make sense.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is cryptography?** (`question_1`, text, required): A brief explanation of what cryptography is and its importance.
* **Types of cryptography** (`question_2`, select_multiple, required): Please choose all types of cryptography that you know (symmetric key exchange, asymmetric key exchange, hashing).
* **Key Exchange methods** (`question_3`, select_multiple, required): Choose all methods of key exchange that you're familiar with (public key exchange, private key exchange, Diffie-Hellman).
* **What is a key?** (`question_4`, text, required): Describe your understanding of what a key is.
* **Methods of Key Management** (`question_5`, select_multiple, required): Please select all methods of key management that you're familiar with (public key management, key warehouse, key escrow).
* **Types of symmetric key encryption** (`question_6`, select_multiple, required): Choose all types of symmetric key encryption that you know (AES, DES, block cipher).
* **What is encryption?** (`question_7`, text, required): A brief explanation of what encryption is.
* **Methods of asymmetric key encryption** (`question_8`, select_multiple, required): Please select all methods of asymmetric key encryption that you're familiar with (RSA, Elliptic Curve).
* **Key Exchange methods in real-world scenarios** (`question_9`, select_multiple, required): Choose all methods of key exchange used in real-world scenarios (SSL, TLS).
* **What is a private key?** (`question_11`, text, required): A description of what a private key is.
* **Methods of Key Management in real-world scenarios** (`question_12`, select_multiple, required): Please select all methods of key management used in real-world scenarios (HSM, Key Management Server).
* **Question 10** (`question_10`, select_multiple, required): Choose all methods of encryption used in real-world scenarios (block cipher, stream cipher).
