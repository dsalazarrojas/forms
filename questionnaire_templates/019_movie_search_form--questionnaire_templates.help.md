# Movie Search Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you find movies based on your search preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Start by selecting your search preferences from the options provided in each field.
2.  Make sure to fill out all the fields that are marked as required.
3.  Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the "Submit" button to receive your movie recommendations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **search_preferences** (text, required/optional): This field is used to gather any additional information about your search preferences. You can use this field to specify any specific details that you would like to be considered when searching for movies.
*   **genre** (select multiple, optional): Select one or more genres that you are interested in (e.g. action, comedy, drama, horror, mystery).
*   **rating** (select one, optional): Select a rating from 1 to 5 to specify the quality of the movie you are looking for.
*   **release_year** (number, optional): Select the release year of the movie you are looking for.
*   **director** (select multiple, optional): Select one or more directors who you are interested in (e.g. Quentin Tarantino, James Cameron, Christopher Nolan, Other).
*   **actor** (select multiple, optional): Select one or more actors who you are interested in (e.g. Tom Hanks, Leonardo DiCaprio, Tom Cruise, Other).
*   **movie_duration** (select one, optional): Select the length of the movie you are looking for (e.g. 90, 120, 150, 180, 210).
*   **watched** (select one, optional): Select whether you have watched the movie or not.
*   **favorite** (select one, optional): Select whether you consider the movie a favorite or not.
*   **share_with** (select one, optional): Select who you would like to share the movie with (e.g. self, friends, family).
*   **review** (text, optional): Leave any notes or comments about the movie.
*   **review_rating** (select one, optional): Rate your review of the movie (e.g. 1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
*   **review_text** (text, optional): Write your review of the movie.
*   **submit** (text, required/optional): Enter any additional information you would like to provide when submitting your form.

Note: Some fields are optional, but are required for the form to function properly. Please fill out all required fields before submitting.
