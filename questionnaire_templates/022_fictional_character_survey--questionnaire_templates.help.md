# Fictional Character Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your favorite fictional characters. Please answer the questions honestly, and feel free to provide as much detail as you like.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the "Favorite Fictional Character" question with the name of the character you're thinking of.
2. Tell us why you love this character in the "Why do you love this character?" question.
3. Select all the types that apply to the character's personality in the "What kind of character would you say this is?" question. You can select one or multiple options.
4. Choose how you relate to this character in the "How do you relate to this character?" question.
5. Answer the "Why is this character your favorite?" question with your thoughts on why this character is special to you.
6. Select how you were introduced to this character in the "How did you get introduced to this character?" question.
7. If you'd like, provide the name of the person who introduced you to this character in the "Who introduced you to this character?" question.
8. Finally, select all the people you think would also enjoy this character in the "Who else do you think would like this character?" question. You can select one or multiple options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Favorite Fictional Character** (`FavoriteFictionalCharacter`, text, required): This is the name of the character that you're thinking of.
* **Why do you love this character?** (`favoriteFictionalCharacterDescription`, text, required): Please tell us about this character's charm, personality, or what makes them special to you.
* **What kind of character would you say this is?** (`favoriteFictionalCharacterType`, select multiple, required): You can choose one or multiple options that apply to the character's personality, such as hero, villain, or protagonist.
* **How do you relate to this character?** (`favoriteFictionalCharacterRelation`, select one, required): Please choose how you personally relate to this character, such as as a fan, as a similar personality, or as an inspiration.
* **Why is this character your favorite?** (`favoriteFictionalCharacterWhy`, text, required): This is an opportunity to share your thoughts on why this character is special to you.
* **How did you get introduced to this character?** (`favoriteFictionalCharacterWhoIntroduced`, select one, required): Please select how you were introduced to this character, such as through a book, movie, or TV show.
* **Who introduced you to this character?** (`favoriteFictionalCharacterWho`, text, optional): If you remember, please share the name of the person who introduced you to this character.
* **Who else do you think would like this character?** (`favoriteFictionalCharacterWhoRelated`, select multiple, required): Choose all the people you think would enjoy this character as much as you do.
