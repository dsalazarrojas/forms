# Blogger Lifestyle Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Blogger Lifestyle Survey is a tool designed to gather information from bloggers about their blogging journey, including their background, blogging practices, and future plans. This survey aims to help us understand the blogging landscape and provide valuable insights for research and analysis purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Start Survey" button to begin.
2. Fill out each section of the form, providing accurate and honest information about your blogging journey.
3. Make sure to answer each question thoroughly, as your responses will help us better understand your experiences and challenges.
4. Click on the "Submit" button to submit your survey once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Blogger Name or Pseudonym** (`blogger_name`, text, required)
Enter your name or pseudonym as you would like to be referred to in our research data.

### 2. **Blog Name** (`blog_name`, text, required)
Please enter the name of your blog, including the full URL if applicable.

### 3. **Blog URL** (`blog_url`, text, optional)
If you would like to include your blog's URL for further research and analysis, enter it here.

### 4. **Primary Blog Niche** (`primary_niche`, select_one, required)
Select the primary topic or category of your blog, from the options provided:

* Lifestyle
* Fashion
* Travel
* Food
* Tech
* Business
* Health
* Parenting
* Beauty
* Other

### 5. **Years of Blogging Experience** (`blogging_experience`, select_one, required)
Indicate your level of blogging experience:

* Less than 1 year
* 1-2 years
* 2-5 years
* 5-10 years
* 10+ years

### 6. **How Often Do You Publish?** (`publishing_frequency`, select_one, required)
Select how often you publish content on your blog:

* Multiple times per week
* Once per week
* 2-3 times per month
* Once per month
* Less frequently

### 7. **Time Spent on Content Creation** (`content_creation_time`, number, required)
Please enter the average number of hours spent on content creation per week.

### 8. **Estimated Monthly Blog Traffic** (`monthly_traffic`, select_one, required)
Select your estimated monthly blog traffic:

* Less than 1,000 visitors
* 1,000 - 10,000
* 10,000 - 50,000
* 50,000 - 100,000
* 100,000+

### 9. **Monetization Methods Used** (`monetization_methods`, select_multiple, required)
Select all the methods you use to monetize your blog:

* Ads (Google AdSense, etc.)
* Sponsored content
* Affiliate marketing
* Product sales
* Consulting/Services
* Not monetized

### 10. **Annual Income from Blogging** (`annual_income`, select_one, optional)
If applicable, please select your annual income from blogging:

* $0
* $1 - $1,000
* $1,000 - $10,000
* $10,000 - $50,000
* $50,000+

### 11. **Biggest Challenge in Blogging** (`biggest_challenge`, text, optional)
If you'd like to share, describe the biggest challenge you face in blogging.

### 12. **Rate Audience Engagement** (`audience_engagement`, select_one, required)
Assess your audience engagement:

* Low
* Moderate
* Good
* Very good
* Excellent

### 13. **Social Media Platforms Used** (`social_media_usage`, select_multiple, required)
Select all the social media platforms you use:

* Instagram
* Twitter/X
* Pinterest
* TikTok
* YouTube
* Facebook
* LinkedIn

### 14. **Future Blogging Plans** (`future_plans`, text, optional)
If applicable, describe your future plans for your blog.

### 15. **Survey Completion Date** (`survey_date`, date, required)
Please enter the date you completed this survey, as it will help us track our data collection.

Note: If you have any concerns or questions, please do not hesitate to reach out to us. We appreciate your contribution to our research!
