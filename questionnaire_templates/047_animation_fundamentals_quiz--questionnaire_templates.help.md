# Animation Fundamentals Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to test the understanding of animation fundamentals, specifically the 12 principles of animation defined by Disney. It is intended for students or professionals who want to evaluate their knowledge on this subject.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student name in the first field.
2. Answer the questions about the number of animation principles, the correct principle related to weight and volume, and the standard frame rate for traditional film animation.
3. Provide a brief description of the concept of Anticipation.
4. Choose the correct principle related to smoothness and organic feel of an action.
5. Explain the purpose of Onionskinning in animation software.
6. Define the term Keyframes.
7. Self-assess your understanding of the 12 principles by rating a scale from 1 to 10.
8. (Optional) Write down any feedback or comments you have for the instructor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name_full`, text, required): Enter your name as a student or animator.
* **How many principles of animation were defined by Disney?** (`question_principles_count`, number, required): Enter the correct number of principles that Disney defined.
* **Which principle creates the illusion of weight and volume?** (`question_weight_illusion`, select_one, required): Choose the principle that creates the illusion of weight and volume.
* **What is the standard frame rate for traditional film animation?** (`question_film_fps`, number, required): Enter the correct frame rate for traditional film animation.
* **Briefly describe the concept of Anticipation in animation** (`question_anticipation_desc`, text, required): Write a brief description of the concept of Anticipation.
* **Which principle relates to the smoothness and organic feel of an action?** (`question_smoothness_principle`, select_one, required): Choose the principle that relates to smoothness and organic feel of an action.
* **What is the purpose of Onionskinning in animation software?** (`question_onionskinning_def`, text, required): Explain the purpose of Onionskinning in animation software.
* **Define the term Keyframes** (`question_keyframes_def`, text, required): Define the term Keyframes.
* **Self-Assessment of Understanding of the 12 Principles** (`self_principles_rating`, number, required): Rate your understanding of the 12 principles on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Instructor Feedback and Comments** (`instructor_feedback_notes`, text, optional): Write down any feedback or comments you have for the instructor.
