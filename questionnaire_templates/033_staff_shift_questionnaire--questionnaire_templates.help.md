# staff_shift_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The staff shift questionnaire is a form used to collect information from staff members about their shifts, including start and end times, breaks, and assigned tools.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are filling out the correct form for your shift period.
2. Fill out the shift start time in the format of `HH:MM` (e.g., 08:00 for 8:00 AM).
3. Enter the break duration in minutes (e.g., 15 for 15 minutes).
4. Enter the break start time in the format of `HH:MM` (e.g., 12:30 for 12:30 PM).
5. Enter the break end time in the format of `HH:MM` (e.g., 12:45 for 12:45 PM).
6. Enter any additional notes or suggestions in the text field.
7. Select the assigned tool from the provided options.
8. Click "Submit" to send the completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### shift_start_time
* **shift start time**: The time when your shift started.
### break_duration
* **break duration**: The length of your break in minutes.
### break_start_time
* **break start time**: The time when your break began.
### break_end_time
* **break end time**: The time when your break ended.
### staff_notes
* **staff notes**: Any additional comments or suggestions you have regarding your shift.
### staff_suggestions
* **staff suggestions**: Any suggestions for improvements or changes you think could be made to the shift or your work.
### assigned_tool
* **assigned tool**: The tool assigned to you during your shift.
