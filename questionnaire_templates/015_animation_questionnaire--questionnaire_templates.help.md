# Animation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from clients about their animation project. It helps us understand their needs, preferences, and goals, ensuring that we can provide an accurate quote and deliver a high-quality animation that meets their expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following sections:

1. **Client Information**:
	* Enter your name or company name
	* Enter your contact email address
	* Enter your contact phone number (optional)
2. **Project Overview**:
	* Enter your project title
	* Provide a detailed description of your project
3. **Project Details**:
	* Select the purpose of your animation project
	* Describe your target audience
	* Select the age range of your target audience (optional)
	* Choose your preferred animation style
	* Describe the visual style and tone you are looking for
	* Provide any reference materials or inspiration for your project
4. **Technical Specifications**:
	* Select the desired video length
	* Choose the required video resolution
	* Select the desired frame rate
5. **Project Timeline and Budget**:
	* Enter the desired project completion date
	* Select your estimated budget range (optional)
	* Provide any specific requests or requirements
6. **Additional Notes**:
	* Enter any additional comments or notes about your project

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name or company name.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Project Title** (`project_title`, `text`, required): Enter a title for your project.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description of your project.
* **Purpose of This Animation** (`project_purpose`, `select_one`, required): Select the purpose of your animation project.
* **Who is the target audience** (`target_audience`, `text`, required): Describe your intended viewers.
* **Age Range of Target Audience** (`audience_age_range`, `select_one`, optional): Select the age range of your target audience.
* **Preferred Animation Style** (`animation_style`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred animation style.
* **Visual Style and Tone** (`visual_style_notes`, `text`, optional): Describe the look and feel you want for your animation.
* **Reference Materials or Inspiration** (`reference_materials`, `text`, optional): Provide any reference materials or inspiration for your project.
* **Desired Video Length** (`video_length`, `select_one`, required): Select the desired video length.
* **Required Video Resolution** (`video_resolution`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the required video resolution.
* **Desired Frame Rate** (`frame_rate`, `select_one`, optional): Select the desired frame rate.
* **Desired Project Completion Date** (`project_deadline`, `date`, required): Enter the desired project completion date.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, optional): Select your estimated budget range.
* **Specific Requests or Requirements** (`specific_requests`, `text`, optional): Enter any specific requests or requirements.
* **Additional Notes or Comments** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your project.
