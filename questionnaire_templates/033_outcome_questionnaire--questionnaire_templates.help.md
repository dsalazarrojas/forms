# Outcome Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you evaluate and report on your outcomes and progress in a structured manner. It's essential to provide accurate and relevant information to help you and your team focus on the right areas and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you understand the questions and prompts.
2. For each section, select the most accurate options from the provided lists or enter your own text in the relevant fields.
3. Make sure to answer each question thoroughly and truthfully.
4. If you're unsure about any question or prompt, don't hesitate to reach out to your team for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief overview of the outcome questionnaire. Please provide a short introduction to give context to your answers.
* **Outcome Areas** (`outcome_areas`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the provided options (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3) that best describes your outcome.
* **Target Areas** (`target_areas`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more of the provided options (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3) that you would like to focus on.
* **Other Target Areas** (`other_target_areas`, `text`, required: false): If there's an area not listed that you'd like to focus on, please enter it here.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers to ensure accurate and reliable data.
* If you're unsure about any question or prompt, don't hesitate to reach out to your team for guidance.
* Please save your progress regularly to avoid losing your work.
