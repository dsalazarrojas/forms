# Focus Session Log Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Focus Session Log Form is designed to record and track focused work sessions. This form captures essential information about each session, such as whether a session was focused or not, the time spent on it, any notes taken, and the email address of the user.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select whether the session was focused (Yes or No).
2. Enter the time spent on the session.
3. Provide any additional notes about the session.
4. Enter your email address (if you want to be contacted about the session).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Focused Work Sessions**: 
  - This field allows you to mark whether the session was focused or not.
  - Select 'Yes' if the session was focused and 'No' if it was not.
* **Time Spent**: 
  - Enter the amount of time you spent on the session.
  - This could be in hours, minutes, or any other unit of time that makes sense to you.
* **Notes**: 
  - Enter any additional notes or comments about the session.
  - This is a text field where you can add any relevant information.
* **Email Address**: 
  - Enter your email address.
  - This is required so that we can contact you about your session.
