# Introduction To Trademark Basics - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide a brief introduction to the basics of trademarks, helping learners to understand the essential concepts and principles related to trademarks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Select the rating of your experience with trademarks (page 2).
2. Answer the follow-up questions related to trademarks (page 3).
3. Choose the method of data collection for trademarks (page 4).
4. Select the submission management options for your trademark (page 5).
5. (Optional) Add any comments or notes about the form (page 6).
6. Enter your email address for any further contact or communication (page 7).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Introduction To Trademark Basics** (`learner_feedback`, number, optional)
This is a rating field where you can rate your experience with trademarks.

### 2. **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional)
Rate your experience with trademarks, with 1 being "very little" and 5 being "extensive".

### 3. **Follow-up questions** (`follow_up_questions`, text, optional)
Answer any follow-up questions about trademarks that you have.

### 4. **Data Collection** (`data_collection`, select_one, required)
Select one of the following data collection methods for trademarks: 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5.

### 5. **Submission Management** (`submission_management`, select_multiple, required)
Select one or more of the submission management options for your trademark: 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5.

### 6. **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional)
Add any comments or notes about trademarks, if you have any.

### 7. **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
Enter your email address for any further contact or communication related to trademarks.
