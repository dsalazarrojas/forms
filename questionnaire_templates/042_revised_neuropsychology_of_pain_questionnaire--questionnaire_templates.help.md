# revised_neuropsychology_of_pain_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The revised neuropsychology of pain questionnaire is a tool used to collect patient information about their pain experiences. This form will help clinicians understand patients' pain scales, history, and characteristics to develop an effective treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as follows:

- Fill in your patient information in the first field (1).
- For the "Pain Scales" field (2), select all applicable options that describe your pain experience. You can select multiple options.
- In the "Pain History" field (3), provide a detailed description of your pain experiences, including when you first noticed pain and how long you have been experiencing it.
- In the "Medical History" field (4), list any significant medical conditions you are currently experiencing or have experienced in the past.
- In the "Neuropsychological History" field (5), describe any mental health conditions or neurological disorders you have, including any treatments or interventions you have received.
- In the "Pain Scales Scores" field (6), enter your pain scores as per your clinician's instructions.
- For the "Medication" field (7), select if you are currently taking any medication for pain or related conditions.
- In the "Pain Characteristics" field (8), provide a detailed description of your pain characteristics, including its type, location, duration, and intensity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information (1)**: Enter your name, date of birth, contact information, and any other relevant identifying details.
* **Pain Scales (2)**: Select all options that describe your pain experience, such as "Yes" for experiencing pain.
* **Pain History (3)**: Provide a detailed description of your pain experiences, including when you first noticed it and how long you have been experiencing it.
* **Medical History (4)**: List any significant medical conditions you are currently experiencing or have experienced in the past.
* **Neuropsychological History (5)**: Describe any mental health conditions or neurological disorders you have, including any treatments or interventions you have received.
* **Pain Scales Scores (6)**: Enter your pain scores as per your clinician's instructions.
* **Medication (7)**: Select if you are currently taking any medication for pain or related conditions.
* **Pain Characteristics (8)**: Provide a detailed description of your pain characteristics, including its type, location, duration, and intensity.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when completing this form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your clinician or healthcare provider.
- If you have already completed this form in the past, please ensure you update your information accurately and thoroughly.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask your clinician or healthcare provider for clarification.
