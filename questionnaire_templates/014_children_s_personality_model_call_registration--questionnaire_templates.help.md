# Children Personality Model Call Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Children Personality Model Call Registration form is designed to gather information about a child's personality and needs, which will be used to schedule a call with a professional or counselor. This call will help you better understand your child's personality and provide you with guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the child's name and your name (as the parent/caregiver).
2. Select your preferred contact email or phone number.
3. Choose a preferred date and time for the call.
4. Select the topics you would like to discuss during the call (optional).
5. Indicate whether you would like to have the call recorded for training purposes.
6. Add any additional notes or information that may help prepare for the call.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, **text**, required): Enter the name of the person completing this form.
* **Child Name** (`child_name`, **text**, required): Enter the name of the child for whom the call is scheduled.
* **Preferred Contact Email or Phone** (`preferred_contact`, **text**, required): Enter your preferred contact email or phone number.
* **Preferred Call Date** (`preferred_date`, **date**, required): Select a date for the call.
* **Preferred Call Time** (`preferred_time`, **time**, required): Select a time window for the call.
* **Topics of Interest** (`topics_of_interest`, **text**, optional): Choose any topics you would like to discuss during the call (e.g., behavior, education, health).
* **Consent to Recording** (`consent_recording`, **select_one**, required): Indicate whether you consent to having the call recorded.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, **text**, optional): Add any additional information that may help prepare for the call.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are the correct person to complete this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities or support staff.
* The information gathered from this form will be used to schedule an effective and productive call with the child.
