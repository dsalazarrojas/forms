# Mental Health Clinic Patient Intake Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Clinic Patient Intake Questionnaire is designed to collect comprehensive information from patients to aid mental health professionals in understanding their medical history, circumstances, and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your basic demographic information, including your name, date of birth, and contact details.
2. Share your mental health information, such as your mental health concerns and goals.
3. Offer your medical history, including any allergies, medications, and medical conditions.
4. List your emergency contacts and their contact details.
5. Provide information about your medical insurance and medical provider.
6. Mention any mental health care givers you are currently seeing.
7. Add any comments or remarks you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Demographics** (`patient_demographics`, `text`, required: false): Enter your basic information, such as your name, date of birth, and contact details.
- **Mental Health Info** (`mental_health_info`, `text`, required: false): Share your mental health concerns and goals.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Provide a thorough medical history.
- **Treatment Plans** (`treatment_plans`, `text`, required: false): Describe any treatment plans you are currently following.
- **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, `text`, required: false): List your emergency contacts and their contact details.
- **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, required: false): Mention any allergies you have.
- **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, required: false): List any medications you are currently taking.
- **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required: false): Describe any medical conditions you are managing.
- **Mental Status** (`mental_status`, `text`, required: false): Share your current mental state.
- **Family Medical History** (`family_medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Share your family medical history.
- **Social Status** (`social_status`, `text`, required: false): Describe your social circumstances.
- **Patient Goals** (`patient_goals`, `text`, required: false): Share your goals and objectives.
- **Patient Identification** (`patient_identification`, `text`, required: false): Provide any unique identifiers for yourself.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required: false): Share your contact information.
- **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Share your address.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your email address.
- **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_contact_person`, `text`, required: false): Mention your emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your emergency contact number.
- **Emergency Contact E-Mail** (`emergency_contact_email`, `text`, required: false): Enter your emergency contact email.
- **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, `text`, required: false): Share your medical insurance information.
- **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, `text`, required: false): Mention your medical provider.
- **Mental Health Caregiver** (`mental_health_caregiver`, `text`, required: false): Share your mental health care giver information.
- **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any comments or remarks you may have.
