# Special Needs Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Needs Questionnaire is a form designed to gather essential information about a patient's medical history, current medications, medical conditions, and other special needs. This form helps healthcare professionals assess the patient's medical status and plan for their care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the patient's **Patient Information** on Page 1. This includes details such as their name, contact information, and any relevant medical history.
2. On Page 2, provide the **Medical History** section, where you can list any previous medical procedures, surgeries, or conditions that may impact their current care.
3. The **Current Medications** section on Page 3 requires a list of all medications the patient is currently taking.
4. The **Medical Needs** section on Page 4 is a multiple-choice selection to identify any specific needs the patient may have, such as oxygen or dialysis.
5. In the **Medical Conditions** section on Page 5, list any existing medical conditions that affect the patient.
6. For each medical condition listed, provide a brief **Description** in the **Medical Conditions Description** field on Page 6.
7. If applicable, record the **Medical Procedure** date on Page 7.
8. On Page 8, enter the expected length of the hospital stay.
9. The **Special Needs** field on Page 9 is a free text area for healthcare professionals to record any additional comments or special needs that may arise.
10. Finally, select the patient's **Medical Specialist** from the dropdown options on Page 10.
11. If necessary, schedule a **Follow Up Visit** date on Page 11.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (patient_information, text, required): Enter the patient's name, contact information, and relevant medical history.
* **Medical History** (medical_history, text, required): List any previous medical procedures, surgeries, or conditions that may impact the patient's care.
* **Current Medications** (current_medications, text, required): List all medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Medical Needs** (medical_needs, select_multiple, required): Select any specific needs the patient may have, such as oxygen or dialysis.
* **Medical Conditions** (medical_conditions, text, required): List any existing medical conditions that affect the patient.
* **Medical Conditions Description** (medical_conditions_description, text, required): Provide a brief description of each medical condition listed.
* **Medical Procedure** (medical_procedure, date, optional): Record the date of any upcoming medical procedure.
* **Hospital Stay Length** (hospital_stay_length, number, required): Enter the expected length of the hospital stay.
* **Special Needs** (special_needs, note, optional): Record any additional comments or special needs that may arise.
* **Medical Specialist** (medical_specialist, select_one, required): Select the patient's healthcare specialist, such as a doctor or nurse.
* **Follow Up Visit** (follow_up_visit, date, optional): Schedule a follow-up visit if necessary.
