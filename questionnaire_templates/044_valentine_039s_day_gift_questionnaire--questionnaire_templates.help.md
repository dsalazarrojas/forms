# Valentine's Day Gift Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you gather information about your loved one's preferences for Valentine's Day gifts. By answering the questions below, you'll be able to suggest a thoughtful and personalized gift that will show your appreciation and love.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Click on each field to edit the text or select an option.
2. For text fields, type your answer directly into the field.
3. For select fields, choose one or multiple options from the dropdown menu.
4. For numerical fields, enter a whole number value.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Valentine's Day Gift Questionnaire** (`valentines_day_gift_questionnaire`, text, optional): A placeholder title for your gift idea. You can leave this field blank or add a title to your gift.
* **Recipient's Name** (`recipient_name_label`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person you're buying a gift for.
* **Gift Suggestion** (`gift_suggestion_label`, text, optional): Share your idea for a Valentine's Day gift. Be creative and think of something thoughtful!
* **Gift Budget** (`gift_budget_label`, number, optional): Set a budget for the gift you're planning. This will help you narrow down your options.
* **Budget Tip** (`gift_budget_hint`, note, optional): You can add any specific details or constraints about your budget here, e.g. "I want to stay under $50".
* **Preferred Color** (`preferred_color_label`, select multiple, optional): What color does your loved one have a special affinity for? Choose all that apply.
* **Gift Category** (`gift_category_label`, select one, optional): What type of gift would you like to give? Choose from our categories.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions_label`, text, optional): Do you have any special requests or instructions for the gift?
* **Gift Wishes** (`gift_wishes_label`, note, optional): Is there something specific you'd like to hear from your loved one on Valentine's Day?
