# voice-message-creation-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback on your experience with our voice messages. It helps us understand how satisfied you are with our voice messages, what type of voice messages you prefer, and what improvements we can make to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each page and answer the questions accordingly. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own set of questions or prompts. Please answer all the questions truthfully, and if you have any additional comments or concerns, feel free to provide them in the "Additional Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, optional): This is a brief introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully and then proceed with the questions.
* **Voice Message Satisfaction** (`2`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks how satisfied you are with our voice messages. Please select the option that best represents your level of satisfaction.
* **Voice Message Habits** (`3`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks about your habits related to our voice messages. Please select all the options that apply to you.
* **Message Satisfaction Ratings** (`4`, `number`, optional): This question asks you to rate your satisfaction with our messages. Please enter a number between 1 and 10 to represent your level of satisfaction.
* **Average Message Satisfaction** (`5`, `number`, optional): This question asks you to rate your average satisfaction with our messages. Please enter a number between 1 and 10 to represent your level of satisfaction.
* **Voice Message Preference** (`6`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks about your preferred type of voice message. Please select the option that best represents your preference.
* **Message Sentiment** (`7`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks about the sentiment of your last message. Please select the option that best represents the sentiment of your last message.
* **Additional Comments** (`8`, `note`, optional): This section is for you to provide any additional comments or concerns you may have.
* **Email** (`9`, `email`, optional): If you want to be contacted about your feedback, please provide your email address here.
* **Phone** (`10`, `text`, optional): If you want to be contacted about your feedback, please provide your phone number here.
* **Age** (`11`, `number`, optional): This question asks for your age. Please enter a number to represent your age.
* **Location** (`12`, `text`, optional): This question asks for your location. Please enter a text to represent your location.

Note: Please answer all the questions truthfully, and if you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
