# Architectural Design Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Architectural Design Interview Form is designed to collect detailed information from candidates for an architectural design interview. This form helps us assess your qualifications, experience, and fit for the position.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Make sure to answer all required fields.
3.  Use clear and concise language when providing answers.
4.  If you have any issues or concerns while filling out the form, please contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`candidate_name`, text, **required**): Enter your full name as it should be listed on your identification documents.
*   **Email Address** (`candidate_email`, email, **required**): Enter your professional email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`candidate_phone`, text, **required**): Enter your mobile or business phone number.
*   **Position Applying For** (`position_applying`, text, **required**): Specify the position you are applying for.
*   **Interview Date** (`interview_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date of the interview.
*   **Years of Design Experience** (`years_experience`, number, **required**): Enter the total years of experience in architectural design.
*   **Areas of Specialization** (`specializations`, select_multiple, **required**): Select all the areas of specialization you have experience in, such as Residential, Commercial, Interior, etc.
*   **Previous Notable Projects** (`previous_projects`, text, **required**): List 3-5 of your most notable projects.
*   **Design Software Proficiency** (`software_proficiency`, text, **required**): List all CAD, BIM, 3D modeling tools, etc., you are proficient in.
*   **BIM Experience** (`bim_experience`, select_one, **required**): Choose your level of BIM experience.
*   **Design Philosophy** (`design_philosophy`, note, **required**): Describe your design approach and philosophy.
*   **Describe Your Design Approach** (`design_approach`, text, **required**): Explain how you approach design problems.
*   **What Inspires Your Design Work?** (`inspiration_sources`, text, **required**): Specify what inspires your design work.
*   **Current Design Trends** (`design_trends`, text, **required**): Describe current design trends you follow.
*   **Team Size Preference** (`team_preference`, select_one, **required**): Choose your team size preference.
*   **Experience Working with Architects and Engineers** (`collaboration_experience`, select_one, **required**): Choose your experience level working with architects and engineers.
*   **How Do You Handle Design Disagreements?** (`conflict_resolution`, text, **required**): Describe how you handle design disagreements.
*   **Portfolio or Website URL** (`portfolio_url`, text, **required**): Provide a link to your portfolio or website.

Please fill out the form carefully and accurately, as it will be used to assess your qualifications for the position. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
