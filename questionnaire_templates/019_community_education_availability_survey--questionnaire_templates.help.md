# Community Education Availability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your education and learning preferences to help us better serve the community with tailored programs and resources.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your name, email address, and zip code.
2. Select your age group, current education level, employment status, and preferred schedule.
3. Indicate your interests in learning, preferred learning format, and time commitment available for learning.
4. Answer questions about childcare and transportation needs, affordable cost range, financial aid needs, and previous adult education experience.
5. Optionally, provide any additional comments or information about your educational goals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Name (Optional)**
	* (`respondent_name`, Text, Optional): Your name if you wish to share.

### 2. **Email Address**
	* (`email_address`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address for program updates and contact.

### 3. **ZIP Code**
	* (`zip_code`, Text, Required): Your ZIP code.

### 4. **Age Group**
	* (`age_group`, Select One, Required): Select your age group.

### 5. **Current Education Level**
	* (`education_level`, Select One, Required): Indicate your highest level of education completed.

### 6. **Employment Status**
	* (`employment_status`, Select One, Required): Choose your current employment status.

### 7. **Learning Interests**
	* (`learning_interests`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the subjects that interest you.

### 8. **Preferred Learning Format**
	* (`preferred_format`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose your preferred learning format.

### 9. **Preferred Schedule**
	* (`schedule_preference`, Select Multiple, Required): Select your preferred schedule for learning.

### 10. **Time Commitment Available**
	* (`time_commitment`, Select One, Required): Indicate the number of hours you can commit to learning per week.

### 11. **Childcare Needs**
	* (`childcare_needs`, Select One, Required): Do you need childcare during classes?

### 12. **Transportation Needs**
	* (`transportation_needs`, Select One, Required): Do you need transportation assistance?

### 13. **Affordable Cost Range**
	* (`cost_range`, Select One, Required): Indicate the maximum cost you can afford per course.

### 14. **Financial Aid**
	* (`financial_aid`, Select One, Required): Do you need assistance with costs?

### 15. **Previous Adult Education Experience**
	* (`prior_experience`, Select One, Required): Have you taken adult education classes before?

### 16. **Learning Goals**
	* (`goals`, Text, Optional): Provide any information about your educational goals.

### 17. **Additional Comments**
	* (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional): Share any other information about your education and learning needs.

Note: Required fields are marked with an asterisk (*).
