# Socioeconomic Status Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Socioeconomic Status Survey is a form designed to collect data on a household's socioeconomic status. This information is used for research, policy-making, or other organizational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. For fields with multiple options, select the one that best describes your household's status.
3. Ensure that you answer all required fields. If you have trouble filling out a particular field, please seek help from the survey administrator.
