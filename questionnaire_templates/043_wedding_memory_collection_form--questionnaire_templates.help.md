# Wedding Memory Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Memory Collection Form is a tool for collecting memories, stories, and details about a wedding. This form is designed to help you record and save important moments from the special day.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each section with the requested information. This will help us collect as much detail as possible about the wedding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wedding Memory Collection Form** (`1`, `title`, required/optional): This is the main heading of the form.
* **story** (`2`, `story`, required): Please provide a brief story or anecdote from the wedding day.
* **photo** (`3`, `photo`, optional): You can add a note to describe the photo you're providing.
* **email** (`4`, `email`, optional): If you'd like to provide your email address for us to contact you, please enter it here.
* **planner_name** (`5`, `planner`, optional): If you're a planner who was involved with the wedding, you can provide your name and description of your role.
* **couple_name** (`6`, `couple`, required): Please provide the names of the couple getting married.
* **date** (`7`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the wedding.
* **time** (`8`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the wedding, if possible.
* **photo_description** (`9`, `Photo Description`, optional): Provide a description of the photo you've uploaded.
* **story_description** (`10`, `Story Description`, optional): Provide a description of the story you've written.
* **planner_description** (`11`, `Planner Description`, optional): If you're a planner, provide a description of your role in the wedding.
* **couple_description** (`12`, `Couple Description`, optional): Provide a description of the couple getting married.
* **email_description** (`13`, `Email Description`, optional): If you'd like to provide a description of your email address.
* **photo_date** (`14`, `Photo Date`, optional): Enter the date of the photo you've uploaded.
* **photo_time** (`15`, `Photo Time`, optional): Enter the time of the photo you've uploaded.
* **photo_email** (`17`, `Photo Email`, optional): If you'd like to provide an email address related to the photo.
* **story_time** (`16`, `Story Time`, optional): Enter the time of the story you've written.
* **story_email** (`18`, `Story Email`, optional): If you'd like to provide an email address related to the story.
* **planner_email** (`19`, `Planner Email`, optional): If you're a planner and would like to provide an email address.
* **couple_email** (`20`, `Couple Email`, optional): If you'd like to provide an email address related to the couple.
* **photo_couple** (`21`, `Photo Couple`, optional): Enter a description of the photo you've uploaded, including the couple.
* **story_couple** (`22`, `Story Couple`, optional): Enter a description of the story you've written, including the couple.
* **planner_couple** (`23`, `Planner Couple`, optional): If you're a planner, provide a description of your role in the wedding, including the couple.
* **couple_story** (`24`, `Couple Story`, optional): Provide a description of the couple's story.
* **couple_photo** (`25`, `Couple Photo`, optional): Enter a description of the photo you've uploaded, including the couple.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and descriptive as possible when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for guidance.
* Thank you for taking the time to fill out this form and help us collect memories from the wedding!
