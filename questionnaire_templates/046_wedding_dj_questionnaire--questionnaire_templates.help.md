# Wedding DJ Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding DJ Questionnaire is a form designed to gather information from wedding DJs about their music preferences and availability. It's a simple and essential tool for wedding event planners and coordinators to collect valuable data from potential wedding DJs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill out the form completely and honestly.
* Select your favorite music genre from the "Music Preferences" field.
* Ensure you provide the correct "Date and Time" you are available to work.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (first_name): A text field to input your given name.
* **Last Name** (last_name): A text field to input your surname.
* **Music Preferences** (music_preferences): A multiple-select field that allows you to choose one or more music genres you enjoy playing. Select all that apply: Rock, Pop, Jazz, Classical, Hip Hop.
* **Date and Time** (date_and_time): A time field to input your availability for the wedding event. Please use the standard 24-hour clock format (e.g., 14:00 or 2:00 PM).
