# Public School Parent/Student Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians of students in public schools. The form is intended to gather data on the student's details, contact information, and educational level for informational purposes only.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's details in the `student_info` field.
2. Select the student's grade level in the `student_grade_level` field.
3. Enter the teacher's name in the `teacher_name` field.
4. Enter the student's age in the `student_age` field.
5. Enter the student's grade in the `student_grade` field.
6. Enter the number of days the student attends school in the `student_attendance` field.
7. Enter the student's behavior score in the `student_behavior` field.
8. Select the method(s) of contact you would like to use to communicate with you in the `contact_method` field.
9. Enter your phone number in the `contact_phone` field.
10. Enter your email in the `contact_email` field.
11. Select the method(s) of contact you would like to use to contact you in the `teacher_contact_method` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Page**: This is the title of the form.
* **Student Info**: Enter the student's name, date of birth, and other identifying information.
* **Student Details**: Enter any additional information about the student that is relevant.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email.
* **Student Grade Level**: Select the student's grade level (Pre-K, Grade 1-2, Grade 3-4, etc.).
* **Teacher Name**: Enter the teacher's name who is handling the student's case.
* **Student Age**: Enter the student's age in years.
* **Student Grade**: Enter the student's grade level (e.g. 3/4, 5/6, etc.).
* **Student Attendance**: Enter the number of days the student attends school.
* **Student Behavior**: Enter the student's behavior score (e.g. 1-10).
* **Contact Method**: Select the method(s) of contact you would like to use to communicate with you (Phone, Email, Mail).
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Teacher Contact Method**: Select the method(s) of contact you would like to use for the teacher to contact you (Phone, Email, Mail).
