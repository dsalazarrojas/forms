# data_warehouse_assessment_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Data Warehouse Assessment Questionnaire is a tool designed to help assess the performance and operations of your data warehouse. This form aims to collect feedback and information from those who have hands-on experience with the warehouse, and it will help us identify areas of improvement and potential risks.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Select the Platform
- Select the platform associated with the data warehouse you are evaluating. This will help us track the specific performance and issues related to each platform.

### Step 2: Evaluate Governance
- Answer the question about governance in the data warehouse. This includes any issues or concerns you have about data access, security, and overall management.

### Step 3: Determine Refresh Cadence
- Enter your thoughts on the refresh cadence of the data warehouse. This includes any issues related to data frequency, data quality, and data availability.

### Step 4: Identify Improvement Priorities
- Select the improvement priorities for the data warehouse. This includes High, Medium, or Low, indicating the level of focus needed for each area of improvement.

### Step 5: Provide Comments
- Add any additional comments or observations you have about the data warehouse. This is an open-ended field for you to provide any extra information you think would be helpful for improvement.

### Step 6: Evaluate Stakeholder Feedback
- Enter any feedback from stakeholders about the data warehouse. This will help us understand how the warehouse is perceived by others and identify any areas of improvement.

### Step 7: Assess Governance Risk
- Select the level of governance risk associated with the data warehouse. This includes High, Medium, or Low, indicating the level of concern.

### Step 8: Determine Refresh Cycle
- Enter your thoughts on the refresh cycle of the data warehouse. This includes any issues related to data frequency, data quality, and data availability.

### Step 9: Plan Next Steps
- Add any next steps or recommendations you have for the data warehouse. This is a place for you to suggest actions or tasks that could be taken to improve the warehouse.
