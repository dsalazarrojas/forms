# Business English Placement Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business English Placement Quiz is designed to evaluate your English language proficiency and skills in a business setting. This form will help you assess your ability to understand, speak, read, and write in English.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your full name, email address, and company name.
2. Answer the questions in the following sections:
	* English Language Background
	* Listening Comprehension
	* Reading Comprehension
	* Writing Skills
	* Speaking Skills
	* Training Goals

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your complete name as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your business email address.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your organization or company.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your current job title or position.
* **Native Language** (`native_language`, `text`, required): Enter the language you speak as your first language.
* **Years of English Study** (`english_study_years`, `select_one`, required): Select the number of years you have studied English:
	+ Less than 1 year
	+ 1-3 years
	+ 3-5 years
	+ 5-10 years
	+ 10+ years
* **English Proficiency Level** (`english_proficiency`, `select_one`, required): Self-assess your current English level:
	+ Beginner
	+ Elementary
	+ Intermediate
	+ Upper-Intermediate
	+ Advanced
	+ Fluent
* **English Certifications** (`english_certifications`, `text`, optional): If you have any English certifications or test scores, enter them here.
* **English Use in Current Role** (`current_role`, `select_one`, required): Select how much you use English in your current role:
	+ Rarely or Never
	+ Occasionally
	+ Regularly
	+ Frequently
	+ Daily
* **I can understand native English speakers in meetings** (`listening_q1`, `select_one`, required): Rate your ability to understand native English speakers in meetings:
	+ Strongly Disagree
	+ Disagree
	+ Neutral
	+ Agree
	+ Strongly Agree
* **I understand business terminology in English** (`listening_q2`, `select_one`, required): Rate your understanding level of business terminology:

	+ Strongly Agree
* **I can understand professional documents and reports** (`reading_q2`, `select_one`, required): Rate your understanding level of professional documents and reports:
	+ Never

* **I can write clear business emails** (`writing_q1`, `select_one`, required): Rate your ability to write clear business emails:
	+ Strongly Disagree
	+ Disagree

	+ Agree
	+ Strongly Agree
* **I can write reports and formal documents** (`writing_q2`, `select_one`, required): Rate your writing ability for reports and formal documents:
	+ Never

* **I can participate in business meetings in English** (`speaking_q1`, `select_one`, required): Rate your ability to participate in business meetings:

## Tips
* Be honest when answering the questions.
* Select the options that best describe your current English language proficiency and skills.
* If you are unsure about any question, choose the option that you think best represents your ability.
* This form is for personal evaluation and not for formal assessment.
