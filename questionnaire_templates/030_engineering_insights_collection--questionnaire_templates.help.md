# engineering_insights_collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your expertise areas, challenges, recommendations, and progress towards project goals. It helps to get a comprehensive overview of your team's performance and identify areas where improvements are needed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the areas of expertise that are most relevant to your job or project.
2. Type any specific challenges or obstacles you're currently facing in your work.
3. If you have any recommendations or ideas for process improvements, enter them in the text field.
4. Indicate whether your team is supporting product decisions.
5. Select the areas where your team has done technical research.
6. Enter the name of your engineering team or department.
7. Indicate whether your team lead is involved in the project.
8. Select the status of project management support from your team lead.
9. Select the status of product ownership from your team lead.
10. Indicate whether your team is following the project plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Expertise Areas** (select_multiple): Select all areas that apply to your job or project. This will help us understand your areas of expertise.
* **Challenges** (text): If you have any specific challenges or obstacles, please describe them here. This will help us identify potential roadblocks.
* **Recommendations** (text): If you have any ideas or suggestions for process improvements, enter them in this field.
* **Product Decisions** (select_one): Indicate whether your team is supporting product decisions. Please select 'Yes' if your team is actively involved in product decisions.
* **Process Improvements** (select_one): Select 'Yes' if you believe your team has done technical research to improve the process. This will help us evaluate your team's progress.
* **Technical Research** (select_multiple): Select all areas where your team has done technical research. This will help us understand the scope of your team's efforts.
* **Engineering Team Name** (text): Enter the name of your engineering team or department. This is a required field.
* **Engineering Team Location** (text): Enter the location of your engineering team or department. This is an optional field.
* **Engineering Team Lead** (select_one): Indicate whether your team lead is involved in the project. This is a required field.
* **Project Manager** (select_one): Select whether your team is following the project plan. This is a required field.
* **Product Owner** (select_one): Indicate whether your team has product ownership. This is a required field.
