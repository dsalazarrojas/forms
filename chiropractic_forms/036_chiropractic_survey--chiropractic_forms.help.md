# Chiropractic Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from patients to help us understand their experience with our chiropractic practice. Your input is valuable to us, and we appreciate your honesty.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Select the rating options for each question based on your actual experience.
3. If you have any additional feedback or comments, you can provide them in the text box at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (1)** (`Patient Name`, text, required): Please enter your actual name.
* **Patient ID Number (3)** (`Patient ID Number`, text, optional): If you have an ID number, please enter it. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Survey Date (4)** (`Survey Date`, date, required): Please select the date you visited our practice.
* **How long have you been a patient (5)** (`How long have you been a patient`, select_one, required): Choose how long you have been a patient.
	+ Less than 1 month
	+ 1-3 months
	+ 3-6 months
	+ 6-12 months
	+ 1-3 years
	+ 3+ years
* **Treatment Satisfaction (6)** (`-- Treatment Satisfaction --`, note, optional): This is an optional note field for you to provide any additional information about your treatment satisfaction.
* **How effective has your treatment been (7)** (`How effective has your treatment been`, number, required): Rate how effective you think your treatment has been, from 1 (not effective) to 5 (very effective).
* **Improvement in pain or symptoms (8)** (`Improvement in pain or symptoms`, number, required): Rate the improvement in your pain or symptoms, from 1 (no improvement) to 5 (significant improvement).
* **Improvement in mobility or flexibility (9)** (`Improvement in mobility or flexibility`, number, required): Rate the improvement in your mobility or flexibility, from 1 (no improvement) to 5 (significant improvement).
* **Improvement in overall quality of life (10)** (`Improvement in overall quality of life`, number, required): Rate the improvement in your overall quality of life, from 1 (no improvement) to 5 (significant improvement).
* **Current Treatment Frequency (11)** (`Current Treatment Frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose how often you visit our practice.
	+ Once per week
	+ Twice per week
	+ Once per month
	+ As needed
	+ Other
* **-- Clinical Experience -- (12)** (`-- Clinical Experience --`, note, optional): This is an optional note field for you to provide any additional information about your clinical experience.
* **Chiropractor Professionalism (13)** (`Chiropractor Professionalism`, number, required): Rate the chiropractor's professionalism, from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Communication and Explanation Quality (14)** (`Communication and Explanation Quality`, number, required): Rate the communication and explanation quality, from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Attentiveness to Your Concerns (15)** (`Attentiveness to Your Concerns`, number, required): Rate the attentiveness to your concerns, from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Time Spent During Appointments (16)** (`Time Spent During Appointments`, number, required): Rate the time spent during appointments, from 1 (too little) to 5 (appropriate).
* **-- Facility and Experience -- (17)** (`-- Facility and Experience --`, note, optional): This is an optional note field for you to provide any additional information about your facility and experience.
* **Facility Cleanliness (18)** (`Facility Cleanliness`, number, required): Rate the facility cleanliness, from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Reasonable Wait Time (19)** (`Reasonable Wait Time`, number, required): Rate the reasonable wait time, from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Staff Friendliness and Professionalism (20)** (`Staff Friendliness and Professionalism`, number, required): Rate the staff friendliness and professionalism, from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **-- Overall Satisfaction -- (21)** (`-- Overall Satisfaction --`, note, optional): This is an optional note field for you to provide any additional information about your overall satisfaction.
* **Overall Satisfaction (22)** (`Overall Satisfaction`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction, from 1 (very unsatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **Would you recommend this practice (23)** (`Would you recommend this practice`, select_one, required): Choose whether you would recommend our practice.
	+ Yes, definitely
	+ Yes
	+ Neutral
	+ No
	+ No, definitely not
* **Areas for Improvement (24)** (`Areas for Improvement`, text, optional): Provide any areas where you think we can improve.
* **Additional feedback or comments (25)** (`Additional feedback or comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments.
