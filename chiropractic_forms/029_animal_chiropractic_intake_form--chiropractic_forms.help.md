# Animal Chiropractic Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from pet owners to help the chiropractor understand the animal's health and medical history, identify potential chiropractic care needs, and consent to treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please start by filling out the required fields with accurate information about yourself and your pet.
2.  Review and complete the sections about your pet's medical history, symptoms, and treatment goals.
3.  Answer the questions about your pet's activity level and living environment.
4.  If applicable, provide information about previous chiropractic care and response.
5.  Read and agree to the risk acknowledgment and consent to treatment.
6.  Complete the form by signing and dating the final page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Pet Owner Full Name** (page 1): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Email Address** (page 1): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
*   **Phone Number** (page 1): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
*   **Address** (page 1): Enter your street address, city, state, and zip code.
*   **Animal Chiropractic Care Intake Form** (page 2): This is a note section for you to provide a brief introduction about your pet and the reason you are seeking chiropractic care.
*   **Were You Referred by a Veterinarian** (page 2): Select the answer that best describes your referral status. If you were referred, select "Yes - veterinarian referral" or "Yes - other practitioner."
*   **Primary Veterinarian Name** (page 2): If you were referred by a veterinarian, enter their name and the name of their clinic.
*   **Clinic Phone Number** (page 2): If you were referred by a veterinarian, enter their phone number.
*   **Pet Information** (page 3): Provide a note about your pet's details.
*   **Pet's Name** (page 3): Enter the name of your pet.
*   **Type of Animal** (page 3): Select the species of your pet. Choose from "Dog", "Cat", "Horse", or "Other."
*   **Breed** (page 4): Enter your pet's breed or mix.
*   **Age** (page 4): Enter your pet's age in years and months.
*   **Sex** (page 4): Select the sex of your pet. Choose from "Male" or "Female."
*   **Weight** (page 4): Enter your pet's weight in pounds.
*   **Primary Reason for Seeking Chiropractic Care** (page 5): Enter the main reason you are seeking chiropractic care for your pet.
*   **Duration of Symptoms** (page 5): Select the duration of your pet's symptoms. Choose from "Less than 1 week," "1-4 weeks," "1-3 months," "3-6 months," "More than 6 months," or "Chronic."
*   **Symptoms** (page 5): Select all the symptoms that apply to your pet. Choose from "Lameness," "Stiffness," "Decreased activity," "Pain when touched," "Behavioral changes," "Performance issues," "Posture problems," "Gait abnormalities," "Muscle spasms," or "None visible."
*   **Symptom Severity** (page 5): Select the severity level of your pet's symptoms. Choose from "Mild - barely noticeable," "Moderate - affects activity," "Severe - significant limitation," or "Very severe - constant discomfort."
*   **What Makes Symptoms Worse** (page 6): Enter any factors that make your pet's symptoms worse.
*   **What Provides Relief** (page 6): Enter any factors that provide relief for your pet's symptoms.
*   **Relevant Medical History** (page 6): Enter any past medical history that may be relevant to your pet's care.
*   **Current Medications or Supplements** (page 6): List any current medications or supplements your pet is taking.
*   **Has Your Pet Received Chiropractic Care Before** (page 7): Select the answer that best describes your pet's previous chiropractic care experience. Choose from "Yes - multiple times," "Yes - once or twice," or "No - first time."
*   **How Did Your Pet Respond to Previous Care** (page 7): If applicable, enter how your pet responded to previous chiropractic care.
*   **Current Activity Level** (page 7): Select the level of activity your pet is currently experiencing. Choose from "Very active - working or competing," "Active - regular exercise," "Moderately active," or "Sedentary."
*   **Type of Activities** (page 7): Enter the type of activities your pet is involved in.
*   **Living Environment** (page 8): Select the environment where your pet lives. Choose from "House with yard," "House without yard," "Apartment," "Barn or stable," or "Other."
*   **Primary Flooring Type** (page 8): Select all the flooring types your pet is exposed to. Choose from "Carpet," "Hardwood," "Tile," "Laminate," "Concrete," or "Grass or dirt."
*   **I Consent to Chiropractic Evaluation and Treatment** (page 9): Select "Yes - I consent" to confirm you understand the risks associated with chiropractic care.
*   **I Understand There Are Risks Associated with Chiropractic Care** (page 9): Select "Yes - I understand" to confirm you understand the risks associated with chiropractic care.
*   **I Authorize Communication with My Veterinarian** (page 9): Select "Yes - full authorization" to confirm you want to share information with your veterinarian.
*   **What Are Your Goals for Chiropractic Care** (page 10): Enter your goals for your pet's chiropractic care.
*   **Additional Comments** (page 10): Enter any additional comments or notes for the chiropractor.
*   **Owner's Signature** (page 10): Enter your full name as the pet's owner.
*   **Date** (page 10): Enter the date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
