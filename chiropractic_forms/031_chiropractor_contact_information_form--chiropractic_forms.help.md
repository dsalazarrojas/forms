We are creating a Chiropractor Contact Information Form to collect patient data for our chiropractic practice. This form is crucial for our team to gather relevant information about patients before scheduling appointments and communicating with them effectively. Please note that this form is only for patients who have been referred to us or have searched for us online; we will not be collecting sensitive information or selling this data in any way.

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect contact information and demographic details from patients for our chiropractic practice. The form gathers essential data such as name, contact number, date of birth, address, and other relevant information. This information enables our team to schedule appointments efficiently and communicate effectively with patients.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the First Name field.
2.  Enter your last name in the Last Name field.
3.  Enter your middle initial (if applicable) in the Middle Initial field.
4.  Select your preferred name in the Preferred Name field.
5.  Enter your date of birth in the Date of Birth field using the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
6.  Select your gender from the options provided in the Gender field.
7.  Enter your primary phone number in the Primary Phone Number field.
8.  Select your primary phone type from the options provided in the Primary Phone Type field.
9.  Enter your secondary phone number (if applicable) in the Secondary Phone Number field.
10.  Select your secondary phone type from the options provided in the Secondary Phone Type field (if applicable).
11.  Enter your email address in the Email Address field.
12.  Select your preferred contact method from the options provided in the Preferred Contact Method field.
13.  Select your preferred time to contact from the options provided in the Best Time to Contact field.
14.  Enter your street address in the Street Address field.
15.  Enter your apartment or suite number (if applicable) in the Address Line 2 field.
16.  Enter your city in the City field.
17.  Enter your state in the State field.
18.  Enter your zip code in the Zip Code field.
19.  Enter your county in the County field (if applicable).
20.  Enter the name of the person to be contacted in case of an emergency in the Emergency Contact Name field.
21.  Select the relationship with the emergency contact in the Emergency Contact Relationship field.
22.  Enter the phone number of the emergency contact in the Emergency Contact Phone field.
23.  Enter any other important contacts in the Additional Contacts field (if applicable).
24.  Select your preferred method of receiving appointment reminders from the options provided in the Appointment Reminders field.
25.  Select your preferred method of receiving health and wellness updates from the options provided in the Health Updates field.
26.  Select whether you want to receive promotional offers from us in the Promotional Offers field.
27.  Enter any additional information that might be helpful for our staff in the Additional Information field.
28.  Select how you heard about us from the options provided in the How Did You Hear About Us field.
29.  If applicable, enter the name of the person who referred you to us in the Referred By field.
30.  Enter any additional notes about your visit in the Additional Notes field.
31.  Confirm that you consent to be contacted by our office in the Consent Agreement field.
32.  Confirm that the information provided is accurate in the Consent to Information Accuracy field.
31.  Finally, sign your digital signature in the Patient Signature field using the provided text field and date your signature in the Date field.

    This section is for general information about you, the patient.
