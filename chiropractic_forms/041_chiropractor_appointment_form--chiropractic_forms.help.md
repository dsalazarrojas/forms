# Chiropractor Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information and schedule appointments for chiropractic care.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Fill out all required fields marked as "Required: true".
2. Enter accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Review and verify the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, Required: true): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, Required: true): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, Required: true): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, Required: true): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Patient Status** (`patient_status`, `select_one`, Required: true): Select whether the patient is a "New Patient", "Returning Patient", or "Follow-up".
* **Chief Complaint** (`chief_complaint`, `text`, Required: true): Describe why the patient is scheduling an appointment.
* **Area of Pain or Concern** (`area_of_pain`, `select_one`, Required: true): Select the area of pain or concern, such as "Neck", "Upper back", etc.
* **How Long Have You Had Symptoms** (`symptom_duration`, `select_one`, Required: true): Select the duration of symptoms, such as "Less than 1 week", "1-4 weeks", etc.
* **Preferred Appointment Date** (`preferred_date`, `date`, Required: true): Select the preferred date for the appointment.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, `select_one`, Required: true): Select the preferred time for the appointment, such as "8:00 AM", "9:00 AM", etc.
* **Type of Appointment** (`appointment_type`, `select_one`, Required: true): Select the type of appointment, such as "Initial Consultation", "Treatment", etc.
* **Pain Severity** (`pain_severity`, `number`, Required: true): Rate the pain severity from 1 (mild) to 10 (severe).
* **Recent Accidents or Injuries** (`recent_accidents`, `select_one`, Required: true): Select whether the patient has had a recent accident or injury.
* **Pain Severity** (`pain_severity`, `number`, Required: true): Rate the pain severity from 1 (mild) to 10 (severe).
* **Previous Chiropractic Care** (`previous_treatment`, `select_one`, Required: true): Select whether the patient has received previous chiropractic care.
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, `select_one`, Required: true): Confirm that the information is accurate.

Note: Some fields are optional and are marked as "Required: false". Please fill out these fields if necessary and according to your needs.
