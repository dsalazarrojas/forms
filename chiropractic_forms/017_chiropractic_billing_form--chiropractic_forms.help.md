# Chiropractic Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chiropractic Billing Form is used to gather billing information from patients for chiropractic services rendered. The form helps ensure accurate and efficient processing of payments.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out patient information with your details, including name, date of birth, phone number, and address.
2. Answer yes or no questions regarding insurance coverage and accident-related visits.
3. Provide insurance information if applicable.
4. List services rendered, including procedure codes and descriptions.
5. Enter payment details, including total charges, copay amount, and payment method.
6. Confirm patient responsibility and assignment of benefits.
7. Sign the form with your digital signature.
