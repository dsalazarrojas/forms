# Chiropractic Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a patient's health and treatment preferences, as well as their understanding of the risks and benefits associated with chiropractic treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the label.
- If you are unsure about any section, ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
- Take your time and answer each question honestly, as this will help ensure that you are fully aware of your treatment options and any associated risks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Full Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Date of Birth** (`patient_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Form Date** (`form_date`, date, required): Enter the date this form is being completed.
- **Chiropractor Name** (`chiropractor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your chiropractor.
- **Understanding of Treatment** (`treatment_understanding`, note, required): This section is for the healthcare provider to explain the treatment plan and associated risks and benefits.
- **I understand the treatment plan** (`understand_treatment`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to confirm that you have been informed about the treatment plan.
- **Risks and Benefits Were Discussed** (`risks_benefits_discussion`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to confirm that you have discussed the risks and benefits associated with treatment.
- **I understand the potential risks** (`risks_understood`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to confirm that you understand the potential risks associated with treatment.
- **I understand the potential benefits** (`benefits_understood`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to confirm that you understand the potential benefits associated with treatment.
- **Consent Acknowledgment** (`consent_acknowledgment`, note, required): This section is for the healthcare provider to acknowledge that you have read and understood the form.
- **I voluntarily agree to treatment** (`voluntary_participation`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to confirm that you are voluntarily agreeing to treatment.
- **Alternative treatments were explained** (`alternative_options`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to confirm that alternative treatments were discussed.
- **All my questions were answered** (`questions_answered`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to confirm that all your questions were answered.
- **Risk Acknowledgment** (`risk_acknowledgment`, note, required): This section is for the healthcare provider to acknowledge that you have read and understood the form.
- **I understand the very rare risk of stroke** (`stroke_risk`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to confirm that you understand the very rare risk of stroke associated with cervical manipulation.
- **I understand potential nerve damage risk** (`nerve_damage_risk`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to confirm that you understand the potential risk of nerve damage associated with treatment.
- **I understand I may experience muscle soreness** (`muscle_soreness_risk`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to confirm that you understand that you may experience muscle soreness associated with treatment.
- **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, note, required): This section is for the healthcare provider to record any medical conditions that may impact treatment.
- **I have disclosed all relevant medical conditions** (`disclosed_conditions`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to confirm that you have disclosed all relevant medical conditions.
- **I have disclosed all current medications** (`medications_disclosed`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to confirm that you have disclosed all current medications.
- **Release and Waiver** (`release_and_waiver`, note, required): This section is for the healthcare provider to acknowledge that you have released the chiropractor from liability for inherent risks of treatment.
- **I release the chiropractor from liability** (`release_agreement`, select_one, required): Select "I agree" to confirm that you release the chiropractor from liability for inherent risks of treatment.
- **By signing, I confirm my informed consent** (`signature_consent`, select_one, required): Select "I consent" to confirm that you have read and understood the form.
- **Additional Notes or Questions** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or questions you may have.

## Tips
- Take your time and answer each question honestly, as this will help ensure that you are fully aware of your treatment options and any associated risks.
- If you are unsure about any section, ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
- This form is only complete once all required fields have been filled out.
- Make sure to review this form carefully before signing.
