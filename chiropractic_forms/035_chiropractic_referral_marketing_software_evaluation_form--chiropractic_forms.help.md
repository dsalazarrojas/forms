# Chiropractic Referral Marketing Software Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from chiropractors or staff members about their experience with a specific referral marketing software. The information collected will help evaluate the software's features, user interface, effectiveness, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form with your honest and detailed feedback.
2.  Ensure all required fields are completed as they are crucial for a thorough evaluation.
3.  You can skip optional fields if you don't have any information to provide.
4.  For rating fields, please use the provided scale (e.g., 1-5) and explain the reasons behind your rating if possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Name (`respondent_name`):** Please enter your name as you are authorized to use this software.
*   **Your Role (`respondent_role`):** Select your role within the practice (e.g., Chiropractor, Office Manager, Administrative Staff, Marketing Manager, or Other).
*   **Practice Name (`practice_name`):** Enter the name of the practice you use this software for.
*   **Evaluation Date (`evaluation_date`):** Enter the date you started using the software or the date of your evaluation.
*   **Software Name Being Evaluated (`software_name`):** Enter the name of the software being evaluated.
*   **Usage Duration (`usage_duration`):** Rate how long you have used the software (e.g., Less than 1 month, 1-3 months, 3-6 months, 6-12 months, 1-2 years, 2+ years).
*   **Ease of Use (`ease_of_use`):** Rate how easy or difficult you found the software to use (e.g., 1 (difficult) to 5 (very easy)).
*   **Feature Functionality and Completeness (`feature_functionality`):** Rate the software's feature set and its completeness (e.g., 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent)).
*   **User Interface and Design (`user_interface`):** Rate the software's user interface and overall design (e.g., 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent)).
*   **Effectiveness for Referral Tracking (`referral_tracking_effectiveness`):** Rate the software's effectiveness in tracking referrals (e.g., 1 (ineffective) to 5 (very effective)).
*   **Impact on Patient Acquisition (`patient_acquisition`):** Rate the software's impact on patient acquisition (e.g., 1 (no impact) to 5 (significant impact)).
*   **Quality of Referral Reports (`referral_reporting`):** Rate the quality of the referral reports provided by the software (e.g., 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent)).
*   **Overall Satisfaction with Software (`satisfaction_score`):** Rate your overall satisfaction with the software (e.g., 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied)).
*   **Value for Money (`value_for_money`):** Rate the software's value compared to its cost (e.g., 1 (poor value) to 5 (excellent value)).
*   **Implementation and Support Quality (`implementation_support`):** Rate the quality of the software's implementation and support (e.g., 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent)).
*   **Main Strengths of the Software (`main_strengths`):** Enter the main strengths of the software you have encountered.
*   **Main Weaknesses or Areas for Improvement (`main_weaknesses`):** Enter the main weaknesses or areas for improvement of the software you have encountered.
*   **Would you recommend this software (`recommendation`):** Choose whether you would recommend this software to others (Yes, strongly, Yes, Neutral, No, or No, definitely not).
*   **Additional Comments or Suggestions (`additional_comments`):** Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have for the software provider.
*   **Form Completion (`form_completion`):** Confirm that you have completed this evaluation.
