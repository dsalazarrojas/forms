**Medicare Chiropractic Documentation & Billing Checklist - Help Guide**
================================================================

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Medicare Chiropractic Documentation & Billing Checklist form, which is designed to collect and document essential information for accurate billing and reimbursement.

## How To Complete This Form
--------------------------------

To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1.  **Collect Patient Information**: On page 1, enter the patient's name, date of birth, and address.
2.  **Determine Billing Status**: On page 2, select the billing status (New, Pending, Paid, or Rejected) to reflect the current state of the bill.
3.  **Enter Insurance Information**: On page 3, enter the patient's insurance provider details.
4.  **Enter Patient Information**: On page 4, enter any additional patient information.
5.  **Diagnosis and Treatment Plan**: On page 5, enter the diagnosis and treatment plan for the patient.
6.  **Services Rendered**: On page 6, describe the services provided to the patient.
7.  **Medicare Coverage**: On page 7, indicate whether the services provided are covered under Medicare.
8.  **Billing Comments**: On page 8, enter any additional comments regarding the billing.
9.  **Signature**: On page 9, sign and date the form to indicate that it has been completed accurately.
