# Chiropractic Treatment Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chiropractic Treatment Booking Form is designed to collect necessary information from patients for their initial consultation or appointment with a chiropractor. This form helps us to better understand your medical history and preferences to provide effective treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email Address** in the respective fields.
2. Select your **Patient Status** from the provided options (New Patient, Returning Patient, or Follow-up Appointment).
3. Describe your **Area of Pain or Concern** (e.g., neck, back, shoulder).
4. Rate your **Pain Severity** from 1 (mild) to 10 (severe).
5. Provide a detailed description of your **Symptoms**.
6. Choose when your **Symptoms Started** from the provided options.
7. If applicable, select your **Appointment Preferences** (morning, afternoon, evening, or any time).
8. Select the **Type of Appointment** you want (initial consultation, treatment session, follow-up session, or evaluation only).
9. Choose the **Preferred Duration** of your appointment (30 minutes, 45 minutes, 1 hour, or 1.5 hours).
10. Indicate whether you have **Health Insurance** and provide your **Insurance Provider** and **Policy Number** if applicable.
11. List any **Current Medications** you are taking.
12. Specify any **Known Allergies**.
13. Confirm you agree to the booking terms by selecting **Yes**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: If you're willing to share, enter your date of birth for us to better understand your medical history.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Patient Status**: Select whether you're a new or returning patient, or if this is a follow-up appointment.
* **Area of Pain or Concern**: Describe where your pain or concern is located (e.g., neck, back, shoulder).
* **Pain Severity**: Rate your pain from 1 (mild) to 10 (severe).
* **Symptoms**: Provide a detailed description of your symptoms.
* **When Did Symptoms Start**: Select when your symptoms began.
* **Appointment Preferences**: Choose your preferred appointment time.
* **Type of Appointment**: Select the type of appointment you want (initial consultation, treatment session, follow-up session, or evaluation only).
* **Preferred Duration**: Choose the length of time you'd like your appointment to be.
* **Do you have health insurance?**: Indicate whether you have health insurance.
* **Insurance Provider**: If you have health insurance, enter your insurance provider's name.
* **Policy Number**: If you have health insurance, enter your policy number.
* **Current Medications**: List any medications you're currently taking.
* **Known Allergies**: List any allergies you have.
* **I agree to the booking terms**: Confirm you agree to the booking terms by selecting **Yes**.
