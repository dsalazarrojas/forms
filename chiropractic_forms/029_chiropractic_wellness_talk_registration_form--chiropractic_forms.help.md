# Chiropractic Wellness Talk Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for attendees to register for a chiropractic wellness talk. It gathers information about the attendee, including contact details, event preferences, and registration information. The form helps us to better understand the attendee's needs and preferences, ensuring a smooth and enjoyable experience for them.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the **Attendee Information** section with your contact details, including **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Phone Number**, and **Email Address**.
*   Choose the **Wellness Talk Session** you are interested in by selecting one of the options in the **Event Selection** field.
*   Select your preferred **Event Date** and **Event Location** from the respective dropdown menus.
*   If you are bringing any guests, enter their names in the **Names of Additional Attendees** field.
*   Indicate whether you have any **Dietary Restrictions** and provide further details if applicable.
*   Let us know if you have any **Accessibility Needs** and provide further details if applicable.
*   Select your **Registration Category** (Individual, Couple, Family, etc.).
*   Choose how you **Hear about this Event** (Email invitation, Social media, etc.).
*   Select your preferred **Registration Fee** and **Payment Method**.
*   Indicate your **Payment Status** (Paid in full, Pending, etc.).
*   If you have any **Additional Information** or **Questions for the Speaker**, please provide them in the respective fields.
*   Finally, sign the form with your digital signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Attendee Information**: Enter your personal contact details.
*   **First Name**: Your first name.
*   **Last Name**: Your last name.
*   **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
*   **Email Address**: Your email address.
*   **Event Selection**: Choose the wellness talk session you are interested in.
*   **Event Date**: Select your preferred event date.
*   **Event Location**: Select your preferred event location.
*   **Names of Additional Attendees**: If you are bringing guests, enter their names here.
*   **Dietary Restrictions**: If you have any dietary restrictions, select the relevant option and provide further details if applicable.
*   **Do you have any dietary restrictions**: Indicate whether you have any dietary restrictions.
*   **Please Specify Dietary Restrictions**: If you have dietary restrictions, provide further details.
*   **Accessibility Needs**: If you have any accessibility needs, let us know and provide further details.
*   **Do you require any accessibility accommodations**: Indicate whether you require any accessibility accommodations.
*   **Please Specify Accessibility Needs**: If you require accessibility accommodations, provide further details.
*   **Registration Category**: Select your registration category (Individual, Couple, Family, etc.).
*   **How did you hear about this event**: Choose how you heard about this event (Email invitation, Social media, etc.).
*   **Registration Fee**: Select your preferred registration fee.
*   **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method (Credit card, Debit card, etc.).
*   **Payment Status**: Indicate your payment status (Paid in full, Pending, etc.).
*   **Additional Information**: If you have any further questions or comments, please provide them here.
*   **Do you have specific questions for the speaker**: If you have questions for the speaker, please provide them here.
*   **Special Requests**: If you have any special requests, please provide them here.
*   **Would you like to be notified about future events**: Indicate whether you would like to be notified about future events.
*   **Would you like to join our wellness newsletter**: Indicate whether you would like to join our wellness newsletter.
*   **I consent to being contacted about this event**: Indicate whether you consent to being contacted about this event.
*   **Attendee Signature**: Sign the form with your digital signature.
*   **Date of Registration**: This field will be auto-populated with the current date.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
*   Make sure to select the correct event session and location.
*   If you have any dietary restrictions, please indicate them and provide further details.
*   If you require any accessibility accommodations, please indicate them and provide further details.
*   Ensure that your payment method is selected correctly.
*   If you have any questions or comments, please provide them in the **Additional Information** field.
