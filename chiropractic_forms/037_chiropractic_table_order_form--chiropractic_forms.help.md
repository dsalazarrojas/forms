## Purpose

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

- Please fill in the customer information section with your business details.
- Select the type of business you have (Chiropractic Office, Medical Clinic, Physical Therapy, Massage Therapy, Wellness Center, or Other).

- Select your preferred delivery date and payment method.

- Finally, confirm your order with the quantity of tables and any special requests or notes.
