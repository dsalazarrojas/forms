# Veterinary Vaccine Consent And Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from pet owners or their representatives for veterinary services, specifically vaccinations, and to schedule an appointment with a veterinarian.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide pet information: Enter the pet's name, age, and species in the pet_info field.
2. Enter owner/representative information: Fill out the owner_info field with your contact information, including your name and email address. This is a required field.
3. Schedule an appointment: Select a date and time for the appointment in the appointment_time field.
4. Choose vaccines: Select the vaccines the pet needs from the vaccine_info field. This field allows for multiple selections.
5. Add any additional notes: If you have any additional information or concerns, you can add them in the note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **pet_info** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the pet's name, age, and species.
* **owner_info** (`text`, required): Fill out your contact information, including your name and email address.
* **contact_info** (`email`, required): Enter the email address associated with the account.
* **appointment_time** (`date`, required): Select a date and time for the appointment.
* **vaccine_info** (`select_multiple`, required): Select the vaccines the pet needs from the list of available options (Rabies, Bordatella, Flu).
* **note** (`note`, optional): Add any additional information or concerns you may have.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the information you enter before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the veterinary clinic or hospital before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully to ensure that you have selected the correct vaccines for your pet.
