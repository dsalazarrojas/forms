# Spay And Neuter Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spay And Neuter Consent Form is a document used by veterinary clinics to obtain informed consent from pet owners for spay and neuter procedures. It serves as a legal record of the owner's agreement and helps establish a clear understanding of the benefits and risks associated with these operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please read the form carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2.  Provide your contact information, including your phone number and email address.
3.  Sign the form to indicate your agreement to the spay and neuter procedure.
4.  Date the form to confirm the date of the procedure.
5.  If a veterinarian performs the spay and neuter procedure, please confirm that you have given your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner's Name (owner_consent, text, required)**: Please provide your name as the pet's owner.
* **Contact Number (owner_phone, text, required)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for any questions or concerns.
* **Email (owner_email, email, required)**: Provide your email address so we can contact you.
* **Signature (owner_signature, note, required)**: Sign your name above to indicate your informed consent for the spay and neuter procedure.
* **Date of Consent (date, date, required)**: Enter the date you are signing this consent form.
* **Did you sign it? (signed_by, select_one, required)**: Confirm that you have signed this form.
* **Did the veterinarian sign? (vet_signature, select_one, required)**: If a veterinarian performed the procedure, please confirm that they have signed this form.
* **Veterinarian's Name (vet_name, text, optional)**: If a veterinarian performed the procedure, enter their name here.
* **Veterinary Clinic Name (clinic_name, text, optional)**: If a veterinarian performed the procedure, enter the clinic name here.
* **Spay/Neuter Date (spay_neuter_date, date, optional)**: If a veterinarian performed the procedure, enter the date they performed it.

## Tips

* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your veterinarian for guidance.
* Review each field carefully before signing the form to ensure your accuracy.
* Ensure your contact information is up-to-date so we can reach you.
* Please keep a copy of this form for future reference.
