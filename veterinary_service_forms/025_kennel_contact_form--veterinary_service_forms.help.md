# Kennel Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kennel Contact Form is a tool for kennel staff, owners, or visitors to get in touch with the kennel with their inquiries, questions, or feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Contact Details** section with your name, phone number, and any other relevant information.
2. Provide details about your pet(s) in the **Pet Information** section.
3. Choose your preferred contact method from the **Contact Method** dropdown.
4. Write your message in the **Message** field, clearly stating your question or inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Details
* **Name** (`text`, required): This is where you enter your name, so our team can address you personally.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Provide your phone number so our team can contact you directly.

### Pet Information
* **Pet Information** (`text`, optional): Describe your pet, including their name, species, age, and any other relevant details.

### Contact Method
* **Contact Method** (select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to be contacted:
	+ Phone: We'll call you to discuss your inquiry.
	+ Email: We'll send you a response via email.
	+ Other: Please specify an alternative contact method in your message.

### Message
* **Message** (text, required): Briefly describe your question, inquiry, or feedback, and we'll do our best to respond promptly.

## Tips
* Keep your message clear and concise for a faster response time.
* If you require a call back, be sure to provide your contact number.
* If you have a question about our services, please provide as much detail as possible, so we can better assist you.
