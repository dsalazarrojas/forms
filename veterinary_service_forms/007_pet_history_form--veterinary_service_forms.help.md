# Pet History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet History Form is a tool used by veterinarians and pet care professionals to gather a comprehensive overview of a pet's past medical history, health status, and other important factors that can inform its treatment and care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand the different sections and fields.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, taking into account the pet's current health status and medical conditions.
3. Be prepared to provide any necessary supporting documentation or medical records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Pet Info
* **Pet Info** (ID: 1): Enter the pet's name, age, breed, and any other relevant information.

### Health Issues
* **Health Issues** (ID: 2): List any known health issues or medical conditions affecting the pet.

### Vaccination History
* **Vaccination History** (ID: 3): Record the pet's vaccination history, including dates and details.

### Medical Treatment History
* **Medical Treatment History** (ID: 4): Document any previous medical treatments or surgeries.

### Pet Type
* **Pet Type** (ID: 5): Select the pet's species (e.g. dog, cat, etc.).

### Pet Status
* **Pet Status** (ID: 6): Record the pet's current health status (e.g. healthy, sick, deceased).

### Owner Info
* **Owner Info** (ID: 7): Record the pet's owner's contact information (name, phone number, email).

### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (ID: 8): Record any additional contact information for the pet's owner.

### Veterinary Services
* **Veterinary Services** (ID: 9): Select any veterinary services that the pet has received (e.g. surgery, vaccinations, etc.).

### Pet Registration Date
* **Pet Registration Date** (ID: 10): Record the date the pet was registered with the veterinary clinic.

### Pet Adoption Date
* **Pet Adoption Date** (ID: 11): Record the date the pet was adopted or obtained.

### Pet DOB
* **Pet DOB** (ID: 12): Record the pet's date of birth.

### Pet Weight
* **Pet Weight** (ID: 13): Record the pet's weight.

### Pet Owner Name
* **Pet Owner Name** (ID: 14): Record the pet owner's name.

### Pet Owner Address
* **Pet Owner Address** (ID: 15): Record the pet owner's address.

### Pet Breed
* **Pet Breed** (ID: 16): Record the pet's breed.

### Pet Color
* **Pet Color** (ID: 17): Record the pet's color.

### Pet Dental Status
* **Pet Dental Status** (ID: 18): Record the pet's dental status.

### Pet Behavior
* **Pet Behavior** (ID: 19): Record any notable behaviors or habits.

### Pet Health Condition
* **Pet Health Condition** (ID: 20): Record any current health conditions.

### Pet Medical Condition
* **Pet Medical Condition** (ID: 21): Record any previous medical conditions.

### Pet Medication
* **Pet Medication** (ID: 22): Record any medication the pet is currently taking.

### Pet Microchip Info
* **Pet Microchip Info** (ID: 23): Record any microchip information.

### Pet Identification
* **Pet Identification** (ID: 24): Record any unique identification numbers or tags.

### Comments
* **Comments** (ID: 25): Add any additional comments or notes about the pet.

Please note that this is a general guide and the actual content and structure of the form may vary depending on the specific use case and requirements.
