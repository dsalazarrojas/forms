# Dog Pain Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a dog's pain levels, areas, and characteristics in order to provide a comprehensive assessment and support for veterinary services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions on each page and answer the questions accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pain Level** (`pain_level`, Select One, Optional): Select the level of pain your dog is experiencing (High or Low). If your dog is experiencing high pain, select High. If your dog is experiencing low pain, select Low.
* **Pain Area** (`pain_area`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose all the areas where your dog is experiencing pain (Yes or No). You can select multiple options if necessary.
* **Pain Duration** (`pain_duration`, Text, Optional): Describe the duration of your dog's pain (e.g., hours, days, or weeks).
* **Pain Intensity** (`pain_intensity`, Select One, Optional): Indicate if your dog is experiencing pain intensity (Yes or No).
* **Pain Characteristics** (`pain_characteristics`, Text, Optional): Provide a description of your dog's pain characteristics (e.g., sharp, dull, or burning).
* **Pain Type** (`pain_type`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose all the types of pain your dog is experiencing (Yes or No). You can select multiple options if necessary.
* **Pain Description** (`pain_description`, Text, Optional): Enter a detailed description of your dog's pain, including any relevant details such as severity, location, and duration.

## Tips
* Please answer each question truthfully and accurately to ensure a thorough assessment of your dog's pain.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, you can leave the field blank or select "Unknown".
* This form is for the benefit of your dog's health, so please take your time to answer each question carefully.
* If you have any questions or concerns about your dog's pain, consult with a veterinarian or a veterinary professional before submitting this form.
