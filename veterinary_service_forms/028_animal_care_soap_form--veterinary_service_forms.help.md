# Animal Care SOAP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from the pet owner about the animal's condition, medical history, and treatment plan in order to provide comprehensive care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Information**: Fill out the patient's name, owner's name, and contact information.
2. **Visit Details**: Enter the date and time of the visit.
3. **Physical Exam**: Describe the animal's appearance, behavior, and any notable physical characteristics.
4. **Subjective Section**: Report any information about the animal's health, such as appetite and thirst status, energy level, and any behavior changes.
5. **History of Present Illness**: Describe the animal's medical history, including any symptoms or conditions.
6. **Medications**: List any medications the animal is currently taking.
7. **Diagnostic Tests**: Describe any laboratory tests, imaging, or other diagnostic tests performed on the animal.
8. **Test Results**: Enter the results of any diagnostic tests performed.
9. **Assessment Section**: Enter the veterinarian's diagnosis and assessment of the animal's condition.
10. **Treatment Plan**: Describe the treatment plan, including any medications, procedures, or follow-up care.
11. **Veterinarian Information**: Enter the veterinarian's name, signature, and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: The name of the pet being seen.
	+ (Required)
* **Owner Name**: The name of the pet's owner.
	+ (Required)
* **Visit Date**: The date of the visit.
	+ (Required)
* **Visit Time**: The time of the visit.
	+ (Optional)
* **SOAP Medical Record Form**: A note field for any additional information.
	+ (Optional)
* **Patient Information**: A note field for any additional information about the pet.
	+ (Optional)
* **Species**: The type of animal (e.g., dog, cat, bird).
	+ (Required)
* **Breed**: The breed or mix of the pet.
	+ (Optional)
* **Age**: The age of the pet in years and months.
	+ (Required)
* **Sex**: The sex of the pet (e.g., male, female).
	+ (Required)
* **Weight**: The weight of the pet in pounds or kg.
	+ (Required)
* **Subjective Section**: Any information about the pet's health reported by the owner.
	+ (Optional)
* **Chief Complaint**: The main reason for the visit.
	+ (Required)
* **History of Present Illness**: A detailed description of the pet's medical history.
	+ (Required)
* **Owner Observations**: Any observations made by the owner.
	+ (Optional)
* **Any Behavior Changes**: Any behavioral changes noticed by the owner.
	+ (Optional)
* **Appetite and Thirst Status**: The pet's appetite and thirst level.
	+ (Required)
* **Energy Level**: The pet's energy level.
	+ (Required)
* **Vomiting or Diarrhea**: Any gastrointestinal symptoms.
	+ (Optional)
* **Current Medications**: Any medications the pet is currently taking.
	+ (Optional)
* **Objective Section**: A note field for any clinical findings.
	+ (Optional)
* **Temperature**: The pet's body temperature.
	+ (Optional)
* **Heart Rate**: The pet's heart rate.
	+ (Optional)
* **Respiratory Rate**: The pet's respiratory rate.
	+ (Optional)
* **Mucous Membranes**: The pet's mucous membranes' color and moisture level.
	+ (Optional)
* **Capillary Refill Time**: The pet's capillary refill time.
	+ (Optional)
* **Physical Examination Findings**: A description of the pet's physical exam.
	+ (Required)
* **Diagnostic Tests**: A description of any laboratory tests or imaging performed.
	+ (Optional)
* **Test Results**: The results of any laboratory tests or imaging.
	+ (Optional)
* **Assessment Section**: A note field for the veterinarian's diagnosis and assessment.
	+ (Optional)
* **Diagnosis**: The veterinarian's diagnosis.
	+ (Required)
* **Problem List**: A list of any identified problems.
	+ (Optional)
* **Severity**: The severity of the condition.
	+ (Optional)
* **Prognosis**: The expected outcome.
	+ (Optional)
* **Plan Section**: A note field for the treatment plan.
	+ (Optional)
* **Treatment Plan**: A description of the treatment plan.
	+ (Required)
* **Medications Prescribed**: A description of any medications prescribed.
	+ (Optional)
* **Follow-up Recommendations**: Any follow-up recommendations.
	+ (Required)
* **Client Home Care Instructions**: Instructions for the owner to follow at home.
	+ (Required)
* **Veterinarian Name**: The name of the veterinarian providing care.
	+ (Required)
* **Veterinarian Signature**: The signature of the veterinarian.
	+ (Required)
* **Date**: The date of the visit.

Note: Some fields are marked as optional but are still important for a complete and accurate record of the animal's care. Please ensure to fill out all required fields and provide as much information as possible for the veterinarian to make an informed decision about the animal's care.
