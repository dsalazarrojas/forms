# Veterinary Operations Participant Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Veterinary Operations Participant Information" form is used to gather essential details from participants involved in veterinary operations, ensuring accurate and up-to-date information is recorded for clinical purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant information, including pet details, owner details, and consent status.
2. Select the contact method and emergency contact information as applicable.
3. Enter your email and phone number (in E.123.456.7890 format).
4. Provide the address and emergency contact relationship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_form**: Enter a short description of the participant, e.g., "Veterinarian performing the procedure."
* **pet_details**: Enter the pet's species, breed, and any other relevant health information.
* **owner_details**: Enter the owner's name, address, and contact information.
* **consent_form**: Provide a brief statement of consent for the treatment or procedure.
* **consent_status**: Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the participant is providing their consent.
* **contact_method**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you want to contact the participant directly.
* **email**: Enter the participant's or owner's email address in a standard E.123.456.7890 format.
* **phone_number**: Enter the participant's or owner's phone number in a standard E.123.456.7890 format.
* **address**: Enter the address of the participant or owner.
* **emergency_contact**: Provide the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
* **emergency_contact_relationship**: Specify the relationship between the participant and the emergency contact, e.g., "spouse" or "guardian."

## Tips

* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and correctly.
* Double-check the form before submission to prevent errors or missing information.
* If any field is missing or incomplete, further investigation should be conducted to determine the reason why the form is not being used correctly.
* This form should only be used for veterinary operations and not for other purposes that could compromise sensitive information.
