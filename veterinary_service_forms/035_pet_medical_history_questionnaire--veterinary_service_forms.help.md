# Pet Medical History Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect medical history information from pet owners to provide better care and services to their pets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question based on your pet's medical history.
2. Fill in the fields with accurate and complete information.
3. Make sure to provide as much detail as possible in each field.
4. Submit the form when you have answered all questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Please describe your pet's medical history, including any previous medical conditions, treatments, or relevant medical events.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required: false): List any known medical conditions that your pet has or has had, such as allergies, chronic conditions, or previous surgeries.
* **Medical Test** (`medical_test`, text, required: false): Describe any medical tests or exams your pet has undergone, including blood tests, X-rays, or other diagnostic procedures.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, required: false): List any medications your pet is currently taking or has taken in the past, including dosage and frequency.

## Tips
* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when answering each question.
* If you're unsure about any information, please provide the best-est guess or the information you have available.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the veterinary service for assistance.
