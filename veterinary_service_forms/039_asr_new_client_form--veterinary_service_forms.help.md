# ASR New Client Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from new clients and their pets to provide personalized and effective veterinary services. It will help us understand your pet's needs, medical history, and preferences, allowing us to offer the best possible care and attention during your visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details, including your name, phone number, email address, and residential address.
2. Provide information about your emergency contact person and their relationship to you.
3. Select the species and sex of your pet.
4. Enter your pet's age, weight, and vaccination status.
5. Choose the reason for your visit, if applicable.
6. Finally, acknowledge your consent to receive necessary veterinary treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to All Souls Retreat Veterinary Services**: A note from us to welcome you and your pet.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary contact number.
* **Alternate Phone Number**: Enter an alternate contact number if available.
* **Email Address**: Enter your active email address.
* **Street Address**: Enter your complete residential address.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State**: Enter your state.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter your postal code.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of someone we can contact in case of an emergency.
* **Relationship to Emergency Contact**: Describe your relationship to this person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number for your emergency contact.
* **How did you hear about All Souls Retreat**: Select how you learned about our services.
* **Pet's Name**: Enter your pet's name.
* **Species**: Select your pet's species.
* **Breed**: Enter your pet's breed or mix.
* **Sex**: Select your pet's sex.
* **Age**: Enter your pet's age in years.
* **Weight**: Enter your pet's weight in pounds or kilograms.
* **Is your pet spayed or neutered**: Select if your pet is spayed or neutered.
* **Are vaccinations up to date**: Select if your pet's vaccinations are current.
* **Previous Veterinary Clinic**: If transferring from another clinic, enter the name of the clinic.
* **Reason for Visit**: Briefly describe why you're bringing your pet to us.
* **I authorize All Souls Retreat to provide necessary veterinary treatment**: Select to acknowledge your understanding and consent to treatment.
