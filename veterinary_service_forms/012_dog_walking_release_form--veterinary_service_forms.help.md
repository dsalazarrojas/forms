# Dog Walking Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide necessary information about a dog walking service, including details about the dog, its owner, and the service provider. The form is a critical document that helps ensure the health, safety, and well-being of the dog.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your dog's details in the "Dog Walking Details" field.
2.  Enter your contact information as the "Dog Owner" in the "Dog Owner Details" field.
3.  If you have authorized another party to provide service to your dog, enter their information in the "Service Provider Details" field.
4.  Enter your dog's medical and contact information in the "Dog Details" field.
5.  Provide details about the dog walking service in the "Service Details" field.
6.  In the "Emergency Contact Details" field, enter information about who to contact in case of an emergency.
7.  Finally, confirm whether the service provider will be authorized to provide care to your dog by checking "Yes" or "No" in the "Signature" field.
8.  Submit the form by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Form Submit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Dog Walking Details**
* `dog_walking_details` (Text): This field is where you provide details about the dog walking service, including any specific instructions or requests you have for the service provider.

### 2. **Dog Owner Details**
* `dog_owner_details` (Text): This field is used to enter your name and contact information as the dog owner, so the service provider can reach out to you for any questions or concerns.

### 3. **Service Provider Details**
* `service_provider_details` (Text): If you have authorized another party to provide service to your dog, enter their name and contact information here.

### 4. **Dog Details**
* `dog_details` (Text): This field is where you enter your dog's medical and contact information, including any relevant medical conditions or medications they may be taking.

### 5. **Service Details**
* `service_details` (Text): In this field, provide details about the dog walking service, including the date, time, and duration of the walk.

### 6. **Emergency Contact Details**
* `emergency_details` (Text): In case of an emergency, this field is where you enter information about who to contact, including their name, phone number, and email.

### 7. **Signature**
* `signature` (Select one): Check "Yes" to confirm that the service provider is authorized to provide care to your dog, and "No" to decline.

### 8. **Form Submit**
* `form_submit` (Select multiple): Select "Yes" to submit the form and authorize the service, and "No" to decline or cancel the service.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information in all fields.
* Review and review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Ensure the service provider is aware of any medical conditions or allergies your dog may have.
* Keep a copy of the completed form for your records.
