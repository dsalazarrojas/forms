# veterinary_curriculum_innovation_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help veterinarians and educators propose new ideas for veterinary curricula.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief title for your proposal.
2. Write a detailed description of your innovation, explaining what it is and how it will benefit the veterinary community.
3. Specify the expected outcomes of your proposal.
4. Attach any relevant documents or materials that support your idea.
5. Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
6. Enter the date you are submitting the proposal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data**: This is the main section where you can enter your proposal title and description.
* **Educator Institution**: Enter the name of your institution or organization, if applicable.
* **Proposal Title**: Enter a concise title for your proposal.
* **Proposal Description**: Write a detailed description of your innovation, explaining its significance and benefits.
* **Curriculum Component**: Specify which area of veterinary curriculum this proposal is related to.
* **Teaching Methodology**: Describe your proposed teaching methodology for this innovation.
* **Expected Outcome**: Specify what you hope to achieve with this innovation.
* **Form Attachments**: Attach relevant files, images, or documents that support your proposal.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Date**: Enter the date you are submitting this proposal.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your proposal for review.
