# veterinary Case Review Access Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by veterinarians or animal care professionals to request access to a specific case review record. The goal is to provide access to relevant and sensitive information for efficient case review.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Clinician Name**, the name of the veterinarian or animal care professional requesting access.
2. Enter the **Clinic Name**, the name of the facility or institution where you work.
3. Select the **Date** of when you require access to the case review record.
4. Choose the **Time** you wish to access the record.
5. Provide the **Case Number**, if available, to help identify the specific case.
6. Enter a clear **Justification** for why you need access to this case review record.
7. Check the **Confidentiality Acknowledgment** box to confirm that you understand and respect the confidentiality of the case record.
8. Click the **Submit** button to send the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Clinician Name** (`clinician_name`, text, required): The name of the veterinarian or animal care professional requesting access.
* **Clinic Name** (`clinic_name`, text, required): The name of the facility or institution where you work.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): The date you require access to the case review record.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): The time you wish to access the record.
* **Case Number** (`case_number`, text, optional): The specific case number, if available.
* **Justification** (`justification`, text, required): A clear explanation for why you need access to this case review record.
* **Confidentiality Acknowledgment** (`confidentiality_acknowledgment`, text, required): A confirmation that you understand and respect the confidentiality of the case record.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): The final step to send the request.
