# Canine Vaccination Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Canine Vaccination Consent Form is a crucial document that ensures the safe and informed administration of vaccinations to your dog. By filling out this form, you're authorizing our veterinary clinic to administer the necessary vaccinations to your dog, while also confirming that you've been informed of the potential risks and benefits of vaccination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  **Owner Information**: Provide your contact details, including your:
	* **Owner Name**: Your full name
	* **Email Address**: Your email address for communication and updates
	* **Phone Number**: Your contact number in case of emergencies
	* **Street Address**: Your full address for record-keeping
	* **City**: Your city
	* **State/Province**: Your state or province
2.  **Dog Information**: Enter your dog's details, including:
	* **Dog Name**: Your dog's name
	* **Breed**: Your dog's breed or mix
	* **Age**: Your dog's age
	* **Microchip ID (if applicable)**: If your dog has a microchip, provide the ID number
3.  **Health History**: Answer questions about your dog's health, including:
	* **Has your dog had any previous vaccine reactions?**: Select "Yes" if your dog has had a reaction, "No" if not, or "Unsure" if you're not sure
	* **If yes, please describe the reaction**: Provide details about the reaction if applicable
	* **Is your dog currently taking any medications?**: Select "Yes" if your dog is on medication, "No" if not, or "Unsure" if you're not sure
	* **If yes, please list medications**: List the medications your dog is currently taking if applicable
	* **Does your dog have any known allergies?**: Select "Yes" if your dog has an allergy, "No" if not, or "Unsure" if you're not sure
	* **If yes, please describe allergies**: Provide details about the allergies if applicable
	* **Does your dog have any existing health conditions?**: Select "Yes" if your dog has a condition, "No" if not, or "Unsure" if you're not sure
	* **If yes, please describe conditions**: Provide details about the conditions if applicable
4.  **Vaccination Details**: Choose the vaccinations your dog needs:
	* **Which vaccines does your dog need today?**: Select all the vaccinations that apply
	* **Is this your dog's first vaccination series?**: Select "Yes, first vaccines" if this is your dog's first set of vaccines, "No, booster vaccines" if this is a booster shot, or "Unsure" if you're not sure
	* **Date of Last Vaccination (if applicable)**: If your dog has had previous vaccinations, provide the date of the last vaccination
5.  **Consent and Authorization**: Confirm your understanding and authorization:
	* **I authorize the veterinary clinic to administer the above vaccinations**: Select "Yes, I authorize treatment" to confirm your dog will receive the authorized vaccinations
	* **I have been informed of potential risks and benefits of vaccination**: Select "Yes, I understand" to confirm you've been informed of the risks and benefits
	* **I understand post-vaccination care and monitoring requirements**: Select "Yes, I understand" to confirm you're aware of post-vaccination care
	* **I accept liability for any adverse reactions that may occur**: Select "Yes, I acknowledge" to confirm you accept liability for any adverse reactions
