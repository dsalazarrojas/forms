# Veterinary Prescription Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The veterinary prescription form is a document used to record and communicate the diagnosis, treatment, and care of an animal. It serves as a tool for veterinarians to provide detailed instructions to pet owners and for pet owners to document any allergies, medical conditions, or other relevant information about their pet.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Owner Instructions**: This field is optional and allows pet owners to provide any additional instructions for the veterinarian.
2. **Vet. Instructions**: This field is required and should include any allergies or special instructions for the veterinarian to follow during treatment.
3. **Date of Service**: Select the date of the veterinary visit to ensure accurate records of the service date.
4. **Treatment**: Select the type of treatment or service provided to the pet, such as medication, procedure, or diagnostic testing.
5. **Medication**: If applicable, provide a prescription for the medication given to the pet.
6. **Procedure**: Enter the name of the procedure performed on the pet.
7. **Diagnostic Testing**: Enter the name of the diagnostic testing performed on the pet.
8. **Veterinarian Signature**: This is a required field for the veterinarian to sign and date the form, indicating that they have reviewed and approved the information.
9. **Owner Signature**: This is a required field for the pet owner to sign and date the form, indicating their understanding and approval of the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Instructions** (`Owner Instructions`, text, optional): Enter any additional instructions or notes from the pet owner for the veterinarian to follow.
* **Vet. Instructions** (`Vet. Instructions`, text, required): Enter any allergies or special instructions for the veterinarian to consider during treatment.
* **Date of Service** (`Date of Service`, date, optional): Enter the date of the veterinary visit.
* **Treatment** (`Treatment`, select_one, required): Select the type of treatment or service provided (Medication, Procedure, Surgery, Diagnostic Testing, etc.).
* **Medication** (`Medication`, text, optional): Enter the prescription information if medication was provided.
* **Procedure** (`Procedure`, text, optional): Enter the name of the procedure performed.
* **Diagnostic Testing** (`Diagnostic Testing`, text, optional): Enter the name of the diagnostic testing performed.
* **Veterinarian Signature** (`Veterinarian Signature`, text, required): Verify and sign as the veterinarian, indicating review and approval of the information.
* **Owner Signature** (`Owner Signature`, text, required): Verify and sign as the pet owner, indicating understanding and approval of the information.
