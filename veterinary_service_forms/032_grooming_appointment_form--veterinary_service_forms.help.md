# Grooming Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grooming Appointment Form is used to schedule a veterinary service appointment for a customer. This form allows customers to provide their information, select a date and time for the appointment, and choose the services they would like to receive. The form helps our veterinary team to efficiently manage appointments and ensure that customers receive the best possible care for their pets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Customer Information" page and fill in the necessary details such as customer name, phone number, and email.
2. Choose the "Appointment Time" and "Appointment Date" pages to select the desired time and date for your appointment.
3. Add any "Notes" that the customer would like to share with the veterinary team.
4. Select the "Service" page to choose the specific services you would like to receive, such as dog grooming or cat bathing.
5. Submit the form to schedule your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Information**: Enter the customer's name, phone number, and email to allow us to contact them and provide updates on their appointment.
  - **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer.
  - **Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
  - **Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
- **Appointment Time**: Select the time of day you would like to schedule the appointment. Please note that appointment times may vary based on availability.
  - **Time**: Choose the time of day for your appointment.
- **Appointment Date**: Select the date you would like to schedule the appointment. Please note that appointment dates may vary based on availability.
  - **Date**: Choose the date for your appointment.
- **Notes**: Add any additional information or notes about your appointment that the veterinary team should be aware of.
- **Service**: Select the services you would like to receive during your appointment.
  - **Service**: Choose from the list of available services to schedule the desired services.
- **Customer Message**: Provide any additional questions or messages you would like to convey to the veterinary team.
  - **Customer Message**: Enter any messages or questions you have.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the appointment date and time are accurate and available.
- Select the services that best meet your pet's needs.
- If you have any special requests or questions, please add them in the "Customer Message" field.
