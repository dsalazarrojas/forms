# Grooming Salon Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to conduct an internal inspection of a grooming salon. The information collected will help assess the salon's facility condition, services offered, and staff certification.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the "Salon Address" field to enter the address of the grooming salon.
2.  Select the services offered by the salon from the list of options in the "Services Offered" field.
3.  For "Staff Certification", choose the relevant level of certification held by the staff members.
4.  Assess the facility condition by selecting one of the options in the "Facility Condition" field.
5.  Enter the number of staff members currently working in the salon in the "Number of Staff" field.
6.  Enter the price of the services offered in the "Service Price" field.
7.  Select the date of the inspection from the calendar in the "Inspection Date" field.
8.  Choose the time of the inspection in the "Inspection Time" field.
9.  Enter any additional notes about the inspection in the "Notes" field.
10.  Enter the email address of the salon in the "Email" field.
11.  Enter the phone number of the salon in the "Phone" field.
12.  Select the category that best fits the salon from the list of options in the "Category" field.
13.  Enter the name of the tool assigned to the inspector in the "Assigned Tool" field.
14.  Enter the name of the output file to be generated after completing the form in the "Output File" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Salon Address**: Enter the address of the grooming salon.
* **Services Offered**: Select all the services offered by the salon from the list of options.
* **Staff Certification**: Choose the level of certification held by the staff members.
* **Facility Condition**: Assess the condition of the salon facilities by selecting one of the options.
* **Number of Staff**: Enter the number of staff members currently working in the salon.
* **Service Price**: Enter the price of the services offered.
* **Inspection Date**: Select the date of the inspection from the calendar.
* **Inspection Time**: Choose the time of the inspection.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the inspection.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the salon.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the salon.
* **Category**: Select the category that best fits the salon from the list of options.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the name of the tool assigned to the inspector.
* **Output File**: Enter the name of the output file to be generated after completing the form.
