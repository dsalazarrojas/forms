# Pet Wellness Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Wellness Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information about a pet's health status, medical history, and other relevant details from their owner or caregiver. This form will help us better understand a pet's overall well-being and provide guidance on any necessary actions or recommendations for their care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the provided fields and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Select the correct pet breed from the options provided.
3. Enter the pet's age in years.
4. If applicable, select the pet's weight in pounds.
5. Evaluate the pet's health status by selecting one or multiple options from the list.
6. Specify the pet's vaccination status.
7. Provide any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
8. Enter any medical treatment or medication information.
9. Add any veterinarian notes or comments.
10. Enter contact information (email or phone number).
11. Enter the pet's name.
12. Enter your name as the pet owner or caregiver.
13. Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the pet's care.
14. Enter any relevant medical history.
15. Enter any medication or medical device information.
16. If applicable, provide details about the pet's type (e.g., dog, cat, or other).
17. Add a brief description of the pet.
18. Add a photo or image of the pet (optional).
19. Provide any additional notes about the pet's medical condition.
20. Add any notes about medical devices used on the pet.
21. Finally, indicate if the veterinarian has any recommendations for the pet's care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Breed**: Select the correct breed of your pet from the provided options. (e.g., Golden Retriever, Labrador, Poodle)
* **Pet Age**: Enter the age of your pet in years. (e.g., 1-2 years old, 5-6 years old, etc.)
* **Pet Weight**: If applicable, enter the weight of your pet in pounds. (e.g., 10-20 pounds, 50-60 pounds, etc.)
* **Pet Health**: Evaluate your pet's health status by selecting one or multiple options from the list. (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor)
* **Vaccination Status**: Select the current vaccination status of your pet. (e.g., Up to date, Not up to date, Unknown)
* **Medical Condition**: Provide any relevant medical conditions or allergies affecting your pet. (e.g., arthritis, diabetes, skin allergies)
* **Treatment Plan**: If applicable, enter any medical treatment or medication information.
* **Veterinarian Note**: Add any additional notes or comments from your veterinarian.
* **Contact Info**: Enter the email or phone number of the person responsible for your pet's care.
* **Pet Name**: Enter the name of your pet.
* **Owner Name**: Enter your name as the pet owner or caregiver.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for your pet's care.
* **Medical History**: Enter any relevant medical history.
* **Medication**: Enter any medication information.
* **Allergy**: Enter any allergy information.
* **Surgery**: Enter any surgery information.
* **Medical Device**: Enter any medical device information.
* **Pet Type**: Select the type of pet you have. (e.g., dog, cat, other)
* **Pet Description**: Add a brief description of your pet.
* **Pet Image**: Add a photo or image of your pet (optional).
* **Medical Condition Note**: Add any additional notes about your pet's medical condition.
* **Medical Device Note**: Add any notes about medical devices used on your pet.
* **Veterinarian Recommendation**: Indicate if the veterinarian has any recommendations for your pet's care.
