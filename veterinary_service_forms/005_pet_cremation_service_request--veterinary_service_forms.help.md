# Pet Cremation Service Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request cremation services for a deceased pet. It will guide you through the necessary steps to provide the required information and help ensure that the cremation process goes smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the name of the pet.
2. Enter the pet's breed.
3. Enter the age of the pet.
4. Select the cremation date.
5. Choose the burial location.
6. Select the cremation service provider.
7. Enter any additional cremation preferences.
8. Enter any other cremation preferences or services required.
9. Enter your contact information as the pet owner.
10. Confirm the cremation service request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Name**: This is the name of the pet that requires cremation services.
* **Pet Breed**: Enter the breed of the pet to help the cremation service provider understand the pet's identity.
* **Pet Age**: Enter the age of the pet to help the cremation service provider understand the pet's size and weight.
* **Cremation Date**: Select the date for the cremation service.
* **Burial Location**: Enter the location where the cremation service will take place.
* **Authorization Confirmation**: Confirm if the pet owner is authorized to request cremation services on behalf of the pet's owner.
* **Cremation Preferences**: Select the type of cremation service desired (Cremation, Burial, or Resomation).
* **Cremation Service Provider**: Select the cremation service provider of choice.
* **Cremation Preferences Other**: Enter any additional cremation preferences that are not listed.
* **Cremation Preferences Other Comments**: Enter any comments related to the additional cremation preferences.
* **Cremation Service**: Select the type of service required (Service 1, Service 2, Service 3, Service 4).
* **Cremation Service Other**: Enter any other cremation services required.
* **Cremation Service Other Comments**: Enter any comments related to the other cremation services required.
* **Pet Owner Contact Name**: Enter your name as the pet owner.
* **Pet Owner Contact Email**: Enter your email address as the pet owner.
* **Pet Owner Phone**: Enter your phone number as the pet owner.
* **Pet Owner Relationship**: Enter your relationship with the pet.
* **Cremation Service Confirmation**: Confirm that you have requested cremation services for the pet.
* **Cremation Service Confirmation Comments**: Enter any comments related to the cremation service confirmation.
* **Cremation Service Date**: Select the date for the cremation service.
* **Cremation Service Time**: Select the time for the cremation service.
* **Cremation Service Other**: Enter any other cremation services required.
* **Cremation Service Other Comments**: Enter any comments related to the other cremation services required.
* **Cremation Service Other Other Comments**: Enter any other comments related to the other cremation services required.
