# Veterinary Public Health Resource Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Public Health Resource Request Form is a tool for submitting requests for resources related to public health concerns in the veterinary field. This form should only be used for requests that are not addressed through other means.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a unique ID for your form in the "Form ID" field.
2. Provide a clear and concise name for your form in the "Form Name" field.
3. Select the category of your form from the drop-down menu for "Form Category".
4. Briefly describe the purpose and scope of your request in the "Form Description" field (optional).
5. Identify the public health concern(s) relevant to your request from the list in "Public Health Concern".
6. Enter the date of when you need the resource(s) by in the "Resource Urgency" field.
7. Provide the location where the resource(s) are needed in the "Location" field (optional).
8. Enter the contact information for the person submitting the request in the "Contact Person" field (optional).
9. Enter the phone number of the person submitting the request in the "Phone Number" field.
10. Enter the email address of the person submitting the request in the "Email Address" field.
11. If necessary, provide any additional information that will help with the processing of your request in the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, `number`, required): A unique identifier for your form.
* **Form Name** (`form_name`, `text`, required): Enter a clear and concise name for your form.
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, `select_one`, required): Select the category for your form from the options "Veterinary Service Forms" or "Other".
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, `text`, optional): A brief description of your request.
* **Public Health Concern** (`public_health_concern`, `select_multiple`, required): Identify the public health concern(s) relevant to your request from Animal Diseases, Human Diseases, or Environmental Concerns.
* **Resource Urgency** (`resource_urgency`, `date`, required): Enter the date by when you need the resource(s).
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, optional): Provide the location where the resource(s) are needed.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the person submitting the request.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the request.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the person submitting the request.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): If necessary, provide any additional information that will help with the processing of your request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information entered is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any issues or need assistance, contact the designated contact person.
* This form is intended for submitting requests for resources, not general inquiries.
* Please do not resubmit requests that have already been addressed through other means.
