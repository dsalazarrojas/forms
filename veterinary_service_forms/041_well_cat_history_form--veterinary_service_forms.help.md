# Well Cat History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Well Cat History Form is a tool used by veterinary clinics to collect essential information about patients and their pets. This form helps gather a comprehensive history of the pet's medical history, including vaccinations and treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient and pet information (page 1).
2. Provide a brief description of the patient's visit history (page 2).
3. Enter any relevant medical information about the pet (page 3).
4. Document any medications or allergies (page 4).
5. Record the pet's medical history (page 5).
6. Provide vaccination information (page 6).
7. Enter the date of vaccination (page 7).
8. Document who administered the vaccination (page 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, required): Enter the patient's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Pet Info** (`pet_info`, text, required): Enter the pet's name, species, and age.
* **Visit Info** (`visit_info`, text, required): Describe the reason for the visit and any notable events or observations from the visit.
* **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, text, required): Provide a detailed account of the pet's medical history, including any diagnoses, treatments, or procedures.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or observations about the patient's visit.
* **Medication Info** (`medication_info`, text, optional): List any medications the pet is currently taking or has taken in the past.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): Document any allergies or sensitivities the pet may have.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any medical conditions the pet has been diagnosed with.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Record the pet's medical history, including any past treatments or surgeries.
* **Vaccination Info** (`vaccination_info`, text, required): Describe the type of vaccination(s) administered.
* **Vaccination Date** (`vaccination_date`, date, required): Enter the date the vaccination was administered.
* **Vaccination Administered By** (`vaccination_administered_by`, text, required): Record who administered the vaccination.

Note: Some fields are optional, but it's recommended to fill in as much information as possible to provide a comprehensive record of the patient's and pet's medical history.
