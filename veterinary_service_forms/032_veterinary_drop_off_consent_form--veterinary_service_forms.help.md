# Veterinary Drop Off Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the pet being dropped off for treatment or services at a veterinary clinic. It gathers essential details about the pet, its owner, and the drop-off instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the pet's name in the "Pet Name" field.
2. Provide the owner's name in the "Owner Name" field.
3. Enter the owner's email address in the "Owner Email" field.
4. Enter the phone number of the owner in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select the drop-off date in the "Drop Off Date" field.
6. Choose the drop-off time in the "Drop Off Time" field.
7. Enter the name of the veterinarian in the "Veterinarian Name" field.
8. List any medications the pet is currently taking in the "Medications List" field.
9. Add any special instructions for the pet's care in the "Special Instructions" field (optional).
10. Select the type of pet in the "Pet Type" field.
11. List any medical conditions the pet has in the "Medical Condition" field.
12. Enter the medical record number in the "Medical Record Number" field.
13. Enter the date of the last medical record in the "Medical Record Date" field.
14. Sign the form with the veterinarian's signature in the "Veterinarian Signature" field.
15. Sign the form with the pet owner's signature in the "Pet Owner Signature" field.
16. Enter the date of service in the "Date of Service" field.
17. Choose the time of service in the "Time of Service" field.
18. Enter the reason for the visit in the "Reason for Visit" field.
19. Provide follow-up instructions in the "Follow Up Instructions" field.
20. Enter the follow-up date in the "Follow Up Date" field.
21. Choose the follow-up time in the "Follow Up Time" field.
22. Enter the contact information for follow-up in the "Follow Up Contact" field.
23. Add any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Name** (text, required): The name of the pet being dropped off for treatment.
* **Owner Name** (text, required): The name of the pet's owner.
* **Owner Email** (email, required): The email address of the pet's owner.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): The phone number of the pet's owner.
* **Drop Off Date** (date, required): The date the pet is being dropped off for treatment.
* **Drop Off Time** (time, required): The time the pet is being dropped off for treatment.
* **Veterinarian Name** (text, required): The name of the veterinarian treating the pet.
* **Medications List** (text, required): A list of medications the pet is currently taking.
* **Special Instructions** (note, optional): Any special instructions for the pet's care.
* **Pet Type** (select_one, required): The type of pet (Cat, Dog, or Other).
* **Medical Condition** (select_multiple, required): Any medical conditions the pet has (None, Diabetic, Kidney, Liver).
* **Medical Record Number** (text, required): The medical record number of the pet.
* **Medical Record Date** (date, required): The date of the last medical record.
* **Veterinarian Signature** (text, required): The signature of the veterinarian.
* **Pet Owner Signature** (text, required): The signature of the pet's owner.
* **Veterinarian's Name** (text, required): The name of the veterinarian (optional).
* **Date of Service** (date, required): The date of service.
* **Time of Service** (time, required): The time of service.
* **Reason for Visit** (text, required): The reason for the visit.
* **Follow Up Instructions** (text, required): Any follow-up instructions for the pet's care.
* **Follow Up Date** (date, required): The date for follow-up.
* **Follow Up Time** (time, required): The time for follow-up.
* **Follow Up Contact** (text, required): The contact information for follow-up.
* **Notes** (text, optional): Any additional notes about the pet's treatment.
* **Additional Comments** (text, optional): Any additional comments about the pet's care.

This guide should help you complete the Veterinary Drop Off Consent Form accurately and efficiently. If you have any further questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the veterinary clinic for assistance.
