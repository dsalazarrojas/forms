# Veterinary Telemedicine Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from pet owners for telemedicine services, such as online consultations with veterinarians. It helps ensure that the owner understands and agrees to the terms of telemedicine services, including consent to share pet health information and consent to the use of telehealth tools and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the owner's information: Enter your name, phone number, and email address in the relevant fields.
2. Provide pet information: If you have a pet, select the type of information that best describes your pet (e.g., cat, dog, etc.) or choose "Other" and provide more details.
3. Enter pet's name: Enter your pet's name in the dedicated field.
4. Check the consent boxes: You will be asked to give your consent to share your pet's health information and to use telehealth tools and services. Select the option that best reflects your agreement.
5. Date and sign the form: Enter the date you are signing the form and print your signature. If you cannot print, select "Other" for the signature field and provide more details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Owner Information** (`owner_information`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Phone** (`owner_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Pet Information** (`pet_information`, select_one, optional): Select the type of pet you own (e.g., cat, dog, etc.) or choose "Other" and provide more details.
* **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, text, optional): Enter your pet's name.
* **Email** (`owner_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Owner Signature** (`owner_signature`, select_one, optional): Select the option that best reflects your signature method (e.g., printed, digital, etc.) or choose "Other" and provide more details.
* **Date** (`date_signature`, date, optional): Enter the date you are signing the form.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): You will be asked to give your consent to share your pet's health information and to use telehealth tools and services. Select the option that best reflects your agreement.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are giving your consent.
* **Signed By** (`signed_by`, text, optional): Enter your name as you sign the form.
