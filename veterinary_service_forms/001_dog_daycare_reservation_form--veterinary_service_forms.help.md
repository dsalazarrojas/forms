# Dog Daycare Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Dog Daycare Reservation Form, which is designed to collect essential information for your pet's stay at our daycare facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the reservation date for your pet's stay.
2. Enter your pet's name.
3. Provide your contact name and details (name, phone number, and email).
4. Choose the type of pet (cat, dog, or other).
5. Select the reservation time.
6. Choose the services you require (daycare, boarding, or grooming).
7. Add any special instructions for your pet's care.
8. Select the reservation status (pending, confirmed, or cancelled).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date, not required): Enter the date for your pet's stay at the daycare facility.
- **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, text, not required): Enter your pet's name.
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, not required): Provide your name.
- **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, select_one, not required): Choose the type of pet (cat, dog, or other).
- **Reservation Time** (`reservation_time`, time, not required): Select the time for your pet's stay.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, not required): Enter your contact phone number.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
- **Services** (`services`, select_multiple, not required): Choose the services you require (daycare, boarding, or grooming).
- **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, note, not required): Add any special instructions for your pet's care.
- **Reservation Status** (`reservation_status`, select_one, not required): Select the reservation status (pending, confirmed, or cancelled).

## Tips
- Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth and comfortable stay for your pet.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the special instructions field.
- If you need to cancel or change your reservation, please update the reservation status field accordingly.
