# Dog Grooming Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from pet owners for a dog grooming application. It will help us schedule a dog grooming appointment for your pet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the pet information that applies to you.
2.  Enter the name of your pet.
3.  Enter your name and contact number so we can contact you to schedule the appointment.
4.  Choose the type of pet you are scheduling an appointment for (if applicable).
5.  Select the appointment date and time that works best for you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Information**: Select the pet information that applies to you.
	+ Select one or multiple options as needed.
* **Pet Name**: Enter the name of your pet.
	+ Enter the name of your dog.
* **Owner Name**: Enter your name.
	+ Enter your name so we can contact you to schedule the appointment.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number.
	+ Enter your phone number so we can contact you to schedule the appointment.
* **Pet Type**: Choose the type of pet you are scheduling an appointment for (if applicable).
	+ Select one option if applicable.
* **Appointment Date**: Select the date for the appointment.
	+ Select a date for the appointment.
* **Appointment Time**: Select the time for the appointment.
	+ Select a time for the appointment.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information about your appointment.
	+ Enter any additional comments or information about your appointment.

## Tips
* Please make sure to select the correct pet information and type.
* Please enter a valid phone number so we can contact you to schedule the appointment.
* If you have any additional comments or information, please enter them in the notes field.
