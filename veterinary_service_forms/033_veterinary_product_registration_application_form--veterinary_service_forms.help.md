# Veterinary Product Registration Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from manufacturers or sellers about their veterinary products, allowing us to gather accurate and up-to-date information about the products used in veterinary settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the product information fields with accurate and complete details about the product.
2. Enter clear and concise product descriptions and labels.
3. Ensure that the product category is correctly classified.
4. Upload or link relevant product documents, such as user manuals or certifications.
5. Enter contact information for the manufacturer or seller.
6. Review and submit the form to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Information (product_info)**: This field is for entering product details such as product name, description, and category.
* **Manufacturer Information (manufacturer_info)**: This field is for entering information about the manufacturer, such as name, address, and contact details.
* **Product Documents (product_documents)**: This field is for uploading or linking relevant product documents, such as user manuals or certifications.
* **Product Details (product_details)**: This field is for entering additional product details, such as features and benefits.
* **Contact Information (contact_info)**: This field is for entering contact information for the manufacturer or seller.
* **Submit Form (submit_form)**: This field is for submitting the completed form.
* **Product Label (product_label)**: This field is for entering a label or nameplate for the product.
* **Product Description (product_description)**: This field is for entering a detailed description of the product.
* **Product Category (product_category)**: This field is for selecting the product category, which will help veterinary professionals find the product easily.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Use clear and concise language when entering product descriptions and labels.
* Ensure that the product category is correctly classified.
* Upload or link relevant product documents to provide more information about the product.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
