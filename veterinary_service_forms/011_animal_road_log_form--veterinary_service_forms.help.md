**Animal Road Log Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Animal Road Log Form is a crucial tool for collecting data on animal observations, behaviors, and health status. Its purpose is to provide a standardized and structured way of reporting, ensuring that the information collected is accurate, comprehensive, and consistent.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Ensure you have all the necessary information and materials before starting the form.
*   Enter the observer's name and ID in the respective fields.
*   Select the correct organization and date for the observation.
*   Record the start and end time of the observation.
*   Identify the route or path name where the observation took place.
*   Describe the weather conditions during the observation.
*   Record the temperature, if applicable.
*   Select all the animal species observed.
*   Provide details on the number of animals observed.
*   Describe the location of the observation.
*   Record any behaviors observed, including selecting all that apply.
*   Provide detailed notes on the behaviors observed.
*   Assess the health status of the animals observed.
*   Record any health notes or concerns.
*   Identify the age group of the animals observed.
*   Describe the group composition of the animals observed.
*   Record any human interaction with the animals.
*   Provide details on human interaction, if applicable.
*   Indicate whether photos or videos were taken during the observation.
*   Add any additional notes or observations.
*   Decide whether follow-up action is required and provide details, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Observer Name** (1, text, required): The full name of the person performing the observation.
*   **Observer ID Number** (2, text, optional): The badge or employee ID number of the observer.
*   **Organization** (3, text, required): The agency or institution responsible for the observation.
*   **Observation Date** (4, date, required): The date of the observation.
*   **Start Time** (5, time, required): The start time of the observation.
*   **End Time** (6, time, required): The end time of the observation.
*   **Route or Path Name** (7, text, required): The designated route or path identifier.
*   **Weather Conditions** (8, select_one, required): Select the weather conditions during the observation.
*   **Temperature** (9, number, optional): Record the temperature in degrees, if applicable.
*   **Animal Species Observed** (10, select_multiple, required): Select all the animal species observed.
*   **Total Number of Animals Observed** (11, number, required): Record the total number of animals observed.
*   **Location Description** (12, text, required): Describe the specific area or GPS coordinates of the observation.
*   **Behavior Observed** (13, select_multiple, required): Select all the behaviors observed, including grazing or feeding, walking or moving, etc.
*   **Detailed Behavior Notes** (14, text, optional): Provide detailed notes on the behaviors observed.
*   **Apparent Health Status** (15, select_one, required): Assess the health status of the animals observed.
*   **Health Observations** (16, text, optional): Record any health notes or concerns.
*   **Age Group Observed** (17, select_multiple, optional): Select the age group of the animals observed.
*   **Group Composition** (18, select_one, required): Select the group composition, such as solitary, pair, small group, large group, or herd.
*   **Was There Any Human Interaction** (19, select_one, required): Indicate whether there was any human interaction with the animals.
*   **Interaction Details** (20, text, optional): Provide details on human interaction, if applicable.
*   **Were Photos or Videos Taken** (21, select_one, required): Indicate whether photos or videos were taken during the observation.
*   **Additional Notes** (22, text, optional): Add any additional notes or observations.
*   **Is Follow-up Action Required** (23, select_one, required): Decide whether follow-up action is required.
*   **Follow-up Details** (24, text, optional): Provide details on follow-up action, if applicable.
