# veterinary_research_footage_usage_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and consent from the owner of an animal for research footage usage in a veterinary setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the owner's name in the "Owner Name" field.
2. Enter the owner's email address in the "Owner Email" field.
3. Enter the owner's phone number in the "Owner Phone" field.
4. Enter a brief description of the animal in the "Animal Description" field.
5. Describe the purpose of the footage in the "Purpose of Footage" field.
6. Specify the recording conditions in the "Recording Conditions" field.
7. Select the storage and sharing preferences for the footage in the "Storage and Sharing" field.
8. Choose whether or not to give consent for the usage of the animal's footage in the "Consent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Owner Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the owner's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Owner Email** (email, required/optional): Enter the owner's email address where they can be reached for further questions or concerns.
* **Owner Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter the owner's phone number where they can be reached for further questions or concerns.
* **Animal Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the animal being filmed.
* **Animal Description** (text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the animal, including breed, species, and any notable characteristics.
* **Purpose of Footage** (text, required/optional): Describe the purpose of the footage, including what research or study it will be used for.
* **Recording Conditions** (text, required/optional): Specify any conditions under which the footage was recorded, such as weather, lighting, or time of day.
* **Storage and Sharing** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select whether the footage should be stored privately or shared with others.
* **Consent** (select_one, required/optional): Choose whether or not to give consent for the usage of the animal's footage.
