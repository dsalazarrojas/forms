# Pet Sodium Intake Tracker Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Sodium Intake Tracker Form is designed to help pet owners and veterinary staff track the pet's sodium intake in a structured and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the "pet_sodium_intake_date" field to record the date of the pet's sodium intake.
2. Enter the time of the sodium intake in the "pet_sodium_intake_time" field.
3. In the "pet_sodium_intake_owner_name" field, enter the name of the pet's owner or the person who administered the sodium intake.
4. In the "pet_sodium_intake_dose" field, record the dose of the sodium taken by the pet.
5. In the "pet_sodium_intake_frequency" field, select the frequency of the sodium intake (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, etc.).
6. In the "pet_sodium_intake_dose_unit" field, record the unit of the dose (e.g., mg, g, etc.).
7. In the "pet_sodium_intake_provider_name" field, enter the name of the person or organization that administered the sodium intake.
8. In the "pet_sodium_intake_provider_phone" field, enter the phone number of the person or organization that administered the sodium intake.
9. In the "pet_sodium_intake_provider_email" field, enter the email of the person or organization that administered the sodium intake.
10. In the "pet_sodium_intake_notes" field, enter any additional notes or comments about the pet's sodium intake.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **pet_sodium_intake_date** (Date): Record the date of the pet's sodium intake.
* **pet_sodium_intake_time** (Time): Enter the time of the pet's sodium intake.
* **pet_sodium_intake_owner_name** (Text): Enter the name of the pet's owner or the person who administered the sodium intake.
* **pet_sodium_intake_dose** (Number): Record the dose of the sodium taken by the pet.
* **pet_sodium_intake_frequency** (Select): Select the frequency of the sodium intake (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, etc.).
* **pet_sodium_intake_dose_unit** (Text): Record the unit of the dose (e.g., mg, g, etc.).
* **pet_sodium_intake_provider_name** (Text): Enter the name of the person or organization that administered the sodium intake.
* **pet_sodium_intake_provider_phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the person or organization that administered the sodium intake.
* **pet_sodium_intake_provider_email** (Email): Enter the email of the person or organization that administered the sodium intake.
* **pet_sodium_intake_notes** (Note): Enter any additional notes or comments about the pet's sodium intake.
* **pet_sodium_intake_dose_frequency_1** (Select): Select if the sodium intake is done daily.
* **pet_sodium_intake_dose_frequency_2** (Select): Select if the sodium intake is done weekly.
* **pet_sodium_intake_dose_frequency_3** (Select): Select if the sodium intake is done monthly.
* **pet_sodium_intake_dose_frequency_4** (Select): Select if the sodium intake is done biweekly.
* **pet_sodium_intake_dose_frequency_5** (Select): Select if the sodium intake is done yearly.
* **pet_sodium_intake_dose_frequency_6** (Select): Select if the sodium intake is done never.
* **pet_sodium_intake_dose_frequented** (Select One): Select yes if the sodium intake is frequented.
* **pet_sodium_intake_dose_unit_amount** (Text): Record the unit amount of the dose.
* **pet_sodium_intake_dose_unit_amount_frequency** (Text): Record the unit amount frequency.
* **pet_sodium_intake_dose_amount** (Number): Record the dose amount.
* **pet_sodium_intake_dose_notes** (Note): Enter any additional notes or comments about the dose.
* **pet_sodium_intake_provider_name** (Text): Enter the name of the person or organization that administered the sodium intake.
* **pet_sodium_intake_provider_phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the person or organization that administered the sodium intake.
* **pet_sodium_intake_provider_email** (Email): Enter the email of the person or organization that administered the sodium intake.
