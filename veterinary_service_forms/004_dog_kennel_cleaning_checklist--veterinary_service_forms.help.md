# Dog Kennel Cleaning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by kennel staff or administrators to document and report on the cleaning of a dog kennel. The form helps to ensure that the kennel is cleaned according to a scheduled and recorded cleaning schedule, and that all areas of the kennel are properly maintained.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Go to the designated area of the kennel that needs to be cleaned.
* Select the type of area to be cleaned (Dirt, Waterproof, or Other).
* Describe the area in detail, providing any necessary information about the state of the area or any issues that need to be addressed (Area Details).
* Indicate the cleaning schedule (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Area to Clean** (`kennel_area`): This is the area of the kennel that needs to be cleaned. Select the option that best describes the type of area to be cleaned.
* **Area Details** (`kennel_area_details`): Please describe the area you cleaned in detail, including any notes or issues that need to be addressed.
* **Cleaning Schedule** (`kennel_cleaning_schedule`): Indicate when the area was cleaned according to the schedule. This will help track consistency and ensure that all areas are cleaned according to plan.

## Tips
* Be sure to select the correct area type to ensure accurate reporting.
* Provide detailed descriptions in the Area Details field to help other staff members understand the situation.
* Ensure that all areas are cleaned according to the scheduled schedule to maintain consistency and cleanliness of the kennel.
