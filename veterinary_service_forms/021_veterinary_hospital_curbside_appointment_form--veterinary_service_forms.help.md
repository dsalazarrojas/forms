# Veterinary Hospital Curbside Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for scheduling a curbside appointment at the veterinary hospital. It collects information about the pet, its owner, and the appointment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your pet's information, including its name and type.
2. Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. Select the service you would like to schedule, such as bath, nail trim, or grooming.
4. Choose the appointment date and time.
5. Select the location for the appointment, such as inside the hospital or at the owner's car.
6. Indicate the status of the service, such as active or inactive.
7. Add any additional notes or comments about the appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, **Required**): Enter the name of the person making the appointment.
* **Owner E-mail** (`owner_email`, email, **Required**): Enter the email address of the person making the appointment.
* **Owner Phone** (`owner_phone`, text, **Required**): Enter the phone number of the person making the appointment.
* **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, text): Enter the name of the pet.
* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, select_one): Select the type of pet (Dog, Cat, or Other).
* **Pet Age** (`pet_age`, number): Enter the age of the pet.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date): Select the date of the appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time): Select the time of the appointment.
* **Appointment Location** (`appointment_location`, select_one): Select the location of the appointment (Inside the hospital, At the owner's car, or Other).
* **Select Service** (`service_type`, select_one): Choose the service you want to schedule (Bathing, Nail Trimming, Grooming, or Other).
* **Service Description** (`service_description`, note): Add any additional details about the service.
* **Service Status** (`service_status`, select_one): Select the status of the service (Active, Inactive).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note): Add any additional notes or comments about the appointment.
* **Owner Note** (`owner_note`, note): Add any additional comments or notes from the owner.
* **Appointment Confirmation** (`appointment_confirmation`, select_one): Select the confirmation status of the appointment (Confirmed, Not Confirmed, No Response).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your contact information accurately to ensure you receive appointment confirmations and updates.
* Double-check the appointment details and service selection to ensure accuracy.
* Use the additional notes field to provide any relevant information that may affect the appointment.
