# ASR Assistance Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information to help All Souls Retreat provide assistance to individuals and their pets. Please provide accurate information to ensure we can assess your needs effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information to help us assess your needs.
2. Answer all required fields. If you have any doubts or concerns, please ask for help or clarification.
3. Enter your contact information, including your phone number and email address.
4. Provide details about your household size, pets, and their species.
5. Select the type of assistance you are requesting.
6. Describe any specific issues or needs of your pets.
7. Provide an emergency contact name and phone number.
8. Confirm that you agree to provide accurate information and use assistance appropriately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **All Souls Retreat Assistance Application** (`application_header`): This is a note field to introduce the form and its purpose.
* **Full Name** (`applicant_full_name`): Please enter your legal name as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`): Enter your primary contact number.
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email`): Enter your active email address.
* **Current Address** (`applicant_address`): Enter your complete residential address.
* **Date of Birth** (`applicant_date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth.
* **Are you able to provide proof of income?** (`income_verification`): Please select 'True' if you can provide proof of income. This may be required for certain assistance programs.
* **Total number of people in your household** (`household_size`): Include all individuals living in your residence.
* **How many pets do you currently have?** (`pet_count`): Include all animals in your care.
* **What species are your pets?** (`pet_species`): Select all that apply.
* **What type of assistance are you requesting?** (`assistance_needed`): Select the services you need help with.
* **Are you experiencing financial hardship?** (`financial_hardship`): Select the option that best describes your situation.
* **Have you received assistance from All Souls Retreat before?** (`previous_assistance`): Select if you have previously applied for or received aid.
* **Describe the specific issues or needs of your pets** (`pet_issues_description`): Include health concerns, behavioral issues, or other needs.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): Enter the name of someone we can contact if needed.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): Enter the phone number for your emergency contact.
* **I agree to provide accurate information and use assistance appropriately** (`consent_agreement`): Please confirm that you understand and agree to these terms.
* **Additional Comments or Special Circumstances** (`additional_comments`): Include any other information that might be relevant to your application.
