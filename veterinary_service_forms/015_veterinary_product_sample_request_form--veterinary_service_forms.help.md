# Veterinary Product Sample Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Product Sample Request Form is used to request veterinary products, categories, and samples from suppliers for evaluation and testing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field. Make sure to use a valid email address that you can be reached at.
3. Enter your Phone Number in the Phone Number field. This will allow us to contact you for further questions or to discuss your order.
4. Select the product category you are interested in by choosing from the options in the Product Category dropdown. You can only select one option from this field.
5. Indicate the type(s) of samples you are interested in by selecting all relevant options from the Sample Type dropdown. You can select multiple options from this field.
6. Enter your Address in the Address field. Please note that we will only ship samples to the address you have provided.
7. In the Message field, type any additional information you would like to provide about your request.
8. In the Terms and Conditions field, indicate that you have reviewed and agreed on our terms. Please note that we will not provide samples without agreeing to our terms.
9. Finally, type in the Agreement field a confirmation that you have read and agreed on our terms and conditions, as well as any additional comments you would like to add.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your business or organization card.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter a valid email address that we can contact you with.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your contact phone number that we can reach you at.
* **Product Category** (select_one, optional): Select the category of product you are interested in.
* **Sample Type** (select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant sample types you are interested in.
* **Address** (text, optional): Enter the address where you would like us to ship the samples.
* **Message** (note, optional): Type any additional information about your request.
* **Terms and Conditions** (select_multiple, optional): Indicate that you have read and agreed on our terms and conditions.
* **Agreement** (text, optional): Enter a confirmation that you have read and agreed on our terms and conditions, as well as any additional comments.
