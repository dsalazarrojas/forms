# Puppy Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Puppy Application Form is a document used to gather information about a pet's health and well-being. It is intended to be completed by pet owners or guardians to provide essential details about their pet, including contact information, pet details, and veterinary records. This form helps ensure that the pet's health is accurately documented, and necessary information is collected for proper care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Puppy Application Form, follow these steps:

1. **Review each page carefully**: The form is divided into several sections, each with its own set of questions and fields. Please take your time to review each page and ensure you understand the purpose of each section.
2. **Choose the correct option**: For select_one fields, select the most accurate option that best describes the information being requested.
3. **Enter accurate information**: For text, email, phone, and address fields, enter accurate and complete information as required.
4. **Date and time fields**: Use the date and time fields to record the date and time of the pet's adoption, vet visits, or other relevant events.
5. **Required fields**: Make sure to fill out all required fields, as indicated by an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info** (`id: 1`): Select your title (Mr, Mrs, Dr).
* **Owner Info** (`id: 2`): Select your title (Mr, Mrs, Dr).
* **Pet Info** (`id: 3`): Select the type of pet you are applying for (Dog, Cat, Other).
* **Contact Info** (`id: 4`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`id: 5`): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`id: 6`): Enter your address.
* **Notes** (`id: 7`): Provide any additional information about your pet that may be helpful for our reference.
* **Type of Pet** (`id: 8`): Select the type of pet you are applying for (Dog, Cat).
* **Vet Referral** (`id: 9`): Enter your veterinarian's name, if applicable.
* **Vet's Name** (`id: 10`): Enter the name of your veterinarian.
* **Vet Clinic** (`id: 11`): Enter the name of your veterinarian's clinic or practice.
* **Vet's Phone** (`id: 12`): Enter your veterinarian's phone number.
* **Owner's Signature** (`id: 13`): Select whether you are the pet's owner or guardian.
* **Date** (`id: 14`): Record the date of your pet's adoption, vet visit, or other relevant event.
* **Time** (`id: 15`): Record the time of your pet's adoption, vet visit, or other relevant event.
* **Age** (`id: 16`): Enter your pet's age.
* **Health Problems** (`id: 17`): Enter any health problems or concerns you have about your pet.
* **Medical History** (`id: 18`): Enter any medical history or treatments your pet has received.
* **Medication** (`id: 19`): Enter any medication your pet is currently taking.
* **Vaccination Record** (`id: 20`): Enter your pet's vaccination record.
* **Spay/Neuter** (`id: 21`): Select whether your pet is spayed or neutered.
* **Adoption Date** (`id: 22`): Record the date of your pet's adoption.
* **Adoption Time** (`id: 23`): Record the time of your pet's adoption.
* **Owner's Comments** (`id: 24`): Provide any additional comments or information about your pet.
* **Assigned to** (`id: 25`): Enter any assigned personnel or department (if applicable).

Please note that this form is for informational purposes only, and the information collected will be used to support your pet's care and well-being. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
