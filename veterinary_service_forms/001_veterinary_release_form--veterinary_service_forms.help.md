# Veterinary Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to provide veterinary clinics with the necessary information to deliver effective care and treatment to animals in their facility. It serves as a release form for the clinic, ensuring that the owner understands and agrees to the treatment process and terms of care.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have all necessary information about the animal and owner before starting the form.
2.  Review each field carefully and select the correct options for your animal and situation.
3.  Fill out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
4.  Leave optional fields blank if not applicable or irrelevant.
5.  Submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Owner Info

*   **Owner Info** (`owner_info`, Select One, Optional): Select whether the owner's contact information is up-to-date and accurate for the veterinary clinic to contact.
*   **Animal Info** (`animal_info`, Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate if the animal has any medical conditions, allergies, or special needs that the clinic should be aware of.
*   **Release Info** (`release_info`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional information relevant to the treatment or care of the animal.
*   **Veterinarian Info** (`veterinarian_info`, Select One, Optional): Select whether the owner has consulted with a veterinarian about the treatment or care of the animal.
*   **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, Text, Optional): Add any relevant medical history or notes that the clinic should be aware of.
*   **Authorization Info** (`authorization_info`, Select One, Optional): Indicate whether the owner has authorized the clinic to treat the animal with certain medications or procedures.
*   **Veterinary Info** (`veterinary_info`, Date, Optional): Record the date when the owner's last visit was to the clinic (not used in this form, but kept for reference).
*   **Signature Info** (`signature_info`, Note, Optional): This field is reserved for any additional comments or remarks from the clinic or owner.
*   **Owner Signature** (`owner_signature`, Text, Optional): The owner's signature, confirming that they understand and agree to the terms of care.
*   **Veterinarian Signature** (`veterinarian_signature`, Text, Optional): The veterinarian's signature, confirming that they have discussed the treatment plan with the owner and that the owner understands and agrees to the terms of care.

## Tips

*   Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all relevant information is provided.
*   Be accurate and truthful in providing information to ensure proper care and treatment.
*   If you're unsure about any field, consult with the veterinarian or contact the clinic staff for guidance.
