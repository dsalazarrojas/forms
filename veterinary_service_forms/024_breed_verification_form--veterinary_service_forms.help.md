# Breed Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Breed Verification Form is used to collect detailed information about a dog, its owner, and the reasons for breed verification. This form helps ensure that breed verification is accurate and complete.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your dog's information, including its name, date of birth, age, and approximate weight.
2. Provide your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
3. Describe your dog's breed, coat color, and coat type.
4. Answer questions about your dog's behavior, health, and breeding history.
5. Provide any relevant information about your dog's registration or pedigree.
6. Specify the reason for breed verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dog Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the dog's owner.
* **Owner Email Address** (`owner_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Owner Phone Number** (`owner_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Dog's Name** (`dog_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of your dog.
* **Dog's Gender** (`dog_gender`, select_one, required): Select Male or Female.
* **Microchip ID Number** (`dog_microchip_id`, text, optional): If available, enter the microchip ID number.
* **Dog's Date of Birth** (`dog_date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the date of birth or approximate age of your dog.
* **Dog's Age** (`dog_age`, number, required): Enter the age of your dog in years.
* **Primary Breed Claimed** (`breed_claimed`, text, required): Enter the primary breed you believe your dog is.
* **Other Breeds or Mixed Breeding** (`secondary_breeds`, text, optional): If mixed breed, list other breeds.
* **Coat Color and Markings** (`coat_color`, text, required): Describe the dog's coat color and markings.
* **Coat Type** (`coat_type`, select_one, required): Select Type of coat.
* **Dog Size** (`dog_size`, select_one, required): Select the size category of your dog.
* **Dog's Current Weight** (`dog_weight`, number, optional): Enter the weight of your dog in pounds or kilograms.
* **Distinctive Features or Marks** (`distinctive_features`, text, optional): Describe any distinctive features or marks on your dog.
* **Previous Registration or Pedigree** (`registration_history`, select_one, optional): Select if previously registered or not.
* **Registration Number if Available** (`registration_number`, text, optional): If previously registered, enter the registration number.
* **Breeding or Parentage Information** (`breeding_history`, text, optional): If known, describe parents or breeding history.
* **Reason for Breed Verification** (`reason_for_verification`, select_one, required): Select the reason for breed verification.
* **Dog's Behavioral Temperament** (`behavioral_temperament`, text, optional): Describe the dog's personality.
* **Relevant Health Information** (`health_information`, text, optional): Enter any relevant health information about your dog.
* **Current or Previous Veterinarian Name** (`veterinarian_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your current or previous veterinarian.
* **Veterinarian Contact Information** (`veterinarian_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact information of your veterinarian.

## Tips
- Be as accurate as possible when filling out this form.
- If you're unsure about any information, try to estimate or approximate it.
- Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you need help or have questions, contact your veterinarian or the form administrators.
