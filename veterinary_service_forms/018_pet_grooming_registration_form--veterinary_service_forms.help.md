# Pet Grooming Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register your pet for bath, groom, and trim services. Please fill out this form to provide your pet's information and schedule a service.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Pet Details**: Enter your pet's details in this field.
2. **Pet Name**: Enter your pet's name in this field.
3. **Owner Details**: Enter your owner's details in this field.
4. **Owner Name**: Enter your owner's name in this field.
5. **Pet Type**: Select your pet's type from the options:
	* Dog
	* Cat
	* Bird
6. **Service Type**: Select the service(s) you require for your pet from the list:
	* Bath
	* Groom
	* Trim
7. **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number in this field.
8. **Email**: Enter your email address in this field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Details**: 
	+ <pet_details> (text, optional): A field for entering pet details.
* **Pet Name**: 
	+ <pet_name> (text, optional): A field for entering the pet's name.
* **Owner Details**: 
	+ <owner_details> (text, optional): A field for entering owner details.
* **Owner Name**: 
	+ <owner_name> (text, optional): A field for entering the owner's name.
* **Pet Type**: 
	+ <pet_type> (select, optional): A field for selecting the pet's type.
* **Service Type**: 
	+ <service_type> (select, multi-select, optional): A field for selecting the required service(s) for your pet.
* **Contact Number**: 
	+ <contact_number> (text, optional): A field for entering the contact number.
* **Email**: 
	+ <email> (email, optional): A field for entering the email address.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields (if any).
* Double-check your contact number and email for accuracy.
