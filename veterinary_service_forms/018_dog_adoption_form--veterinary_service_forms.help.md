# dog_adoption_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "dog_adoption_form" is used to collect information for adopting dogs from our veterinary service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide details about the pet you're interested in adopting on the "pet_info" field.
2. Fill in your contact information on the "contact_info" field.
3. Select the type of pet you're interested in adopting from the "pet_type" field.
4. Enter the name of the pet you're interested in adopting on the "pet_name" field.
5. If you have any additional information or comments, please use the "message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **pet_info (Text)**: Enter a brief description of the pet you're interested in adopting.
* **contact_info (Text)**: Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **pet_type (Select One)**: Choose the type of pet you're interested in adopting (cat, dog, or other).
* **pet_name (Text)**: Enter the name of the pet you're interested in adopting.
* **adopter_info (Text)**: Enter any additional information about yourself that you feel is relevant for the adoption process.
* **contact_phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **contact_email (Email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about the adoption process.
* **message (Note)**: Leave a message or any comments you have about the pet or the adoption process.
* **submit (Text)**: Review and submit your completed form.
