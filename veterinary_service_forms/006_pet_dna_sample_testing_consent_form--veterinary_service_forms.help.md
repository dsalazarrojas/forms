# Pet DNA Sample Testing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from pet owners for DNA sample testing. The form is typically submitted by veterinarians or authorized personnel to collect genetic information from pets, which is essential for various testing purposes such as general health analysis, insurance claims, or research. 

## How To Complete This Form

*   To begin, please fill out the "Pet Owner Authorization" field with the pet owner's ID number. Enter the number of the pet owner's authorization code that was provided by the veterinary clinic or animal shelter. This field is not required.
*   In the "Pet Name" field, enter the name of the pet that will be undergoing DNA sample testing. This is an optional field.
*   Choose the "Pet Type" from the options provided in the dropdown menu: Cat, Dog, Other, or Horse.
*   If you choose "Other" under "Pet Type", please describe the type of pet in the "Pet Breed" field.
*   Select the "Testing Type" that applies to the pet's DNA sample testing. Options are DNA Testing, Blood Testing, or Microchip Testing.
*   Indicate the purpose for which the DNA testing is being conducted. Select all applicable options from General Health, Insurance, Research, or Other.
*   Decide how the test results will be reported to the pet owner. Options are True, False, or Only Owner.
*   In the "Field 8" field, please enter any additional comments or details regarding the DNA testing. This field is optional.
*   Enter the email address of the person who submitted the form. This field is not required.
*   If you are the pet owner, please sign your name in the "Owner Signature" field to confirm that you have read and understood the consent form. If you are an authorized representative, please sign your name in this field.
*   Enter the date on which you signed the form in the "Owner Date Signed" field.
*   Enter the time at which you signed the form in the "Owner Time Signed" field.
*   If you are the veterinarian, please sign your name in the "Veterinarian Signature" field to confirm that you have obtained the pet owner's consent for DNA sample testing. If you are an authorized representative, please sign your name in this field.
*   Enter the date on which you signed the form in the "Veterinarian Date Signed" field.
*   Enter the time at which you signed the form in the "Veterinarian Time Signed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Pet Owner Authorization**: Enter the pet owner's ID number, which is usually provided by the veterinary clinic or animal shelter.
*   **Pet Name**: Enter the name of the pet that will undergo DNA sample testing.
*   **Pet Type**: Choose the correct type of pet from the provided options: Cat, Dog, Other, or Horse.
*   **Pet Breed**: If you choose "Other" under "Pet Type", describe the type of pet in this field.
*   **Testing Type**: Select the type of testing for which the DNA sample is being collected: DNA Testing, Blood Testing, or Microchip Testing.
*   **Testing Purpose**: Indicate the reason for conducting the DNA testing, which could be for General Health, Insurance, Research, or Other.
*   **Test Results Reporting**: Decide how the test results will be reported to the pet owner: True, False, or Only Owner.
*   **Field 8**: Enter any additional comments or details regarding the DNA testing.
*   **Form Submitted By**: Enter the email address of the person who submitted the form.
*   **Owner Signature**: Sign your name as the pet owner or representative to confirm that you have read and understood the consent form.
*   **Owner Date Signed**: Enter the date on which you signed the form.
*   **Owner Time Signed**: Enter the time at which you signed the form.
*   **Veterinarian Signature**: Sign your name as the veterinarian or representative to confirm that you have obtained the pet owner's consent.
*   **Veterinarian Date Signed**: Enter the date on which you signed the form.
*   **Veterinarian Time Signed**: Enter the time at which you signed the form.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that the pet owner's consent is obtained before submitting the form.
*   Make sure to keep the "Field 8" field for additional comments or details optional, and only fill it out if necessary.
*   Enter your email address correctly in the "Form Submitted By" field.
*   Sign the form completely and accurately to avoid any errors or disputes.
