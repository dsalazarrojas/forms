# Veterinary Care Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from pet owners for veterinary care services. It includes fields for pet health, treatment details, owner information, and more. Please take a few minutes to complete this form accurately to ensure a smooth and efficient care process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all necessary information about your pet, such as their health status and medical history.
2.  Choose the correct pet health option that best describes your pet's current health status.
3.  Provide detailed treatment details, including any medications or procedures your pet is undergoing.
4.  Fill out the owner information section with accurate contact details.
5.  Select any relevant emergency contact information if you wish to designate someone else to receive updates.
6.  Check the medical history box if your pet has a known medical condition.
7.  Review the liability agreement and select the option that best aligns with your understanding.
8.  Enter any payment information if applicable.
9.  Provide accurate details about your veterinarian or current care provider.
10.  If necessary, include any additional comments or information about your pet's care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Info** (pet_info): This field is for providing information about your pet, including their species, age, and any other relevant details.
* **Pet Health** (pet_health): This field is a selection menu where you can choose the current health status of your pet from the provided options.
* **Owner Info** (owner_info): This field is for providing your contact information as the pet owner, including your name, address, and phone number.
* **Treatment Details** (treatment_details): This field is for describing any treatment or medication your pet is currently undergoing.
* **Consent** (consent): This field is for selecting any additional information you are consenting to, such as sharing your pet's health information with third parties.
* **Emergency Contact** (emergency_contact): This field is for designating someone to contact in case of an emergency with your pet.
* **Medical History** (medical_history): This field is a checkbox to indicate if your pet has any known medical conditions.
* **Liability Agreement** (liability_agreement): This field is a selection menu where you can choose the terms of the liability agreement.
* **Payment Info** (payment_info): This field is for providing payment details if applicable.
* **Veterinarian Info** (veterinarian_info): This field is for providing information about your veterinarian or current care provider.
* **Additional Comments** (additional_comments): This field is for including any additional comments or information about your pet's care.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to avoid any potential issues during the care process.
