# emergency_veterinary_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information in emergency situations involving pets, allowing veterinary clinics or pet-related services to gather essential details from pet owners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the relevant information. Please make sure to provide as much detail as possible for each field. If you need help with any of the fields, please refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Pet Information (pet_info)
* **Pet Information** (`pet_info`, `text`, required/optional): Fill in the details about the pet in need of service, including its name, species, age, breed, and any relevant medical conditions.

### Owner Information (owner_info)
* **Owner Information** (`owner_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your own contact information, including your name, address, phone number, and any other relevant details that can help with communication.

### Emergency Contact (emergency_contact)
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required/optional): If there is an emergency contact person, provide their name, phone number, and relationship to the pet owner.

### Referral Information (referral_info)
* **Referral Information** (`referral_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional information that might be helpful for the veterinary clinic or pet-related service, such as a brief description of the situation or any specific requests for care.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* If you need to provide any additional information, please use the Referral Information field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant veterinary clinic or pet-related service directly.
