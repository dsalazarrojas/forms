# Pet Microchip Vendor Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pet Microchip Vendor Application Form is a tool for Pet Microchip Vendors to apply to become certified. This form allows you to provide your business information, contact details, and other relevant information required for certification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your vendor information in the "Vendor Information" field.
2. Provide your business name, contact person, phone number, email, address, and business hours in their respective fields.
3. Answer the question about being a certified microchip vendor and provide a certification number if applicable.
4. Share a little about your business in the "About Us" field.
5. Finally, enter your years of experience and any equipment information if relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Vendor Information** (`vendor_info`, text, optional)
Enter your vendor information.

### 2. **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, optional)
Enter your business name.

### 3. **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional)
Enter the name of the contact person for your business.

### 4. **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional)
Enter your business phone number.

### 5. **E-Mail** (`email`, email, optional)
Enter your business email address.

### 6. **Address** (`address`, text, optional)
Enter your business address.

### 7. **Business Hours** (`business_hours`, time, optional)
Enter your business hours in the format `HH:MM`.

### 8. **About Us** (`about_us`, text, optional)
Share a little about your business in this field.

### 9. **Microchip Vendor License** (`microchip_vendor_license`, select_one, optional)
Select "Yes" if your business holds a microchip vendor license. If yes, provide the certification number.

### 10. **Certification Number** (`certification_number`, text, optional)
Enter your microchip vendor certification number if selected as a microchip vendor.

### 11. **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, text, optional)
Enter the years of experience of your business.

### 12. **Equipment** (`equipment`, text, optional)
Enter any additional equipment information relevant to your business.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are completed accurately and truthfully to ensure a smooth application process.
* If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to contact us for clarification.
* This form is for application purposes only and not for general business information sharing.
