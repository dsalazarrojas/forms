# veterinary_technology_prototype_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The veterinary_technology_prototype_application form is used for submitting and managing veterinary technology prototype applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant prophet(s) that apply to your application.
2. Enter your submission information in the text field.
3. Choose the correct stage of your application from the dropdown menu.
4. Upload any required files to support your application.
5. Enter your university details.
6. Provide technical details in the text field.
7. Select the date and time of your application.
8. Enter your email address.
9. Provide your contact information.
10. If required, enter additional information about later stages of your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Prophets** (required): Select the prophet(s) that applies to your application.
	+ This field is required because it helps us understand the scope and context of your application.
* **Submission** (required): Enter a brief description of your submission.
	+ This field is required because it helps us understand the core of your application.
* **Descriptions** (required): Enter a detailed description of your submission.
	+ This field is required because it helps us assess the relevance and feasibility of your application.
* **Stage** (required): Choose the current stage of your application from the dropdown menu.
	+ This field is required because it helps us track the progress of your application.
* **Files** (required): Upload any supporting files related to your application.
	+ This field is required because it helps us assess the feasibility and completeness of your application.
* **University** (required): Select your university from the dropdown menu.
	+ This field is required because it helps us identify your institution and affiliations.
* **Tech** (required): Enter technical details about your application.
	+ This field is required because it helps us understand the technical aspects of your application.
* **Date** (required): Select the date of your application.
	+ This field is required because it helps us track the timeline of your application.
* **Time** (required): Select the time of your application.
	+ This field is required because it helps us understand the timing of your application.
* **File Upload** (required): Upload any additional files that support your application.
	+ This field is required because it helps us assess the completeness of your application.
* **Email** (required): Enter your email address.
	+ This field is required because it helps us contact you for further information.
* **Address** (required): Enter your address.
	+ This field is required because it helps us contact you for further information.
* **Contact** (required): Enter your contact information.
	+ This field is required because it helps us contact you for further information.
* **Stage 2**, **Stage 3**, ..., **Stage 13** (optional): If applicable, enter additional information about later stages of your application.
	+ These fields are optional because they are only relevant for applications with multiple stages.
