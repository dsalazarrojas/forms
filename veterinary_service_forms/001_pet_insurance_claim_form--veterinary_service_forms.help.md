# Pet Insurance Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Pet Insurance Claim Form" is a form for pet owners to submit claims related to their insured pets. This form helps to provide necessary information about the pet, the veterinary care received, and insurance details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields as accurately as possible.
2. If a field is optional, fill it out if it's relevant to your situation.
3. Review all fields before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`): Select the type of pet that received the veterinary care (e.g., cat, dog, bird).
* **Insurance Company** (`insurance_company`): Choose the insurance company that covers your pet (e.g., Trupanion, Petco).
* **Veterinarian Name** (`veterinarian_name`): Enter the name of the veterinarian who provided care to your pet.
* **Veterinary Clinic** (`veterinary_clinic`): Enter the name of the veterinary clinic where your pet received care.
* **Medical Expenses** (`medical_expenses`): Enter the total amount of medical expenses related to your pet's care. This can include costs for procedures, tests, and other veterinary services.
* **Medical Expenses Description** (`medical_expenses_description`): Provide a description of the medical expenses, such as the reason for the care and any specific procedures performed.
* **Veterinary Care** (`veterinary_care`): Select the type of veterinary care your pet received (e.g., Routine check-ups, Medical procedures, Surgeries).
* **Claim Status** (`claim_status`): Select the current status of your claim (e.g., Pending, Approved, Rejected).
* **Claim Date** (`claim_date`): Enter the date of the claim submission.
* **Claim Time** (`claim_time`): Enter the time of the claim submission.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional information about your claim that may be helpful.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your claim.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your claim.
* **Upload File** (`upload_file`): Upload any supporting files related to your claim (e.g., receipts, invoices, photos).
* **Upload Document** (`upload_document`): Upload any additional documents related to your claim (e.g., medical records, test results).
