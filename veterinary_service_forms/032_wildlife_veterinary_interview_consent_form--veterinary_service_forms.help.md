# wildlife_veterinary_interview_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture consent information from participants in a wildlife veterinary interview. It is used to record contact details, interview specifics, and consent status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully to understand the purpose of each field.
2. Enter accurate and consistent information for each field.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.

* **Consent Information** (page 1):
    * **Consent Information** (Consent Information, text, required): Enter a brief description of the consent process, including any relevant details or conditions.
* **Contact Details** (page 2):
    * **contact_details** (Contact Details, select_one, required): Select the method of contact (Phone, Email, or Other).
