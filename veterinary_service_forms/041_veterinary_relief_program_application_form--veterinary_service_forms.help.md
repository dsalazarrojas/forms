# Veterinary Relief Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users seeking relief services from the veterinary organization. It is used to gather essential details about the user's service needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **User Input Form** (Page 1) with your contact information, including your email address.
2. In the **Service Request** field (Page 2), describe your service needs in a clear and concise manner.
3. Select your **Location** (Page 3) from the provided options (Veterinary Clinic, Animal Hospital, or Independent Vet).
4. If applicable, specify the **Date Required** and **Time Required** for the requested service (Pages 4 and 5).
5. Provide any additional **Notes** (Page 6) about your service needs or requirements.
6. Choose the **Assigned Tool** (Page 7) you would like to be assigned for the service (Chatjimmy or Other).
7. The **Output File** field (Page 8) is not required, but you can enter any additional information or attachments related to your service request.
8. Select the **Category** (Page 9) for your service request from the provided options (Veterinary Service Forms, Forms, or Templates).
9. Provide a brief **Description** (Page 10) of your service needs.
10. Enter your desired **Form ID** (Page 11) if you have one.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input Form** (`user_input_form`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address to contact you for further communication.
* **Service Request** (`service_request`, Text, Optional): Describe your service needs in a clear and concise manner.
* **Location** (`location`, Select One, Optional): Choose your location from the provided options (Veterinary Clinic, Animal Hospital, or Independent Vet).
* **Date Required** (`date_required`, Date, Optional): If applicable, specify the date you require the service.
* **Time Required** (`time_required`, Time, Optional): If applicable, specify the time you require the service.
* **Notes** (`notes`, Note, Optional): Provide any additional information or comments about your service needs.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, Select One, Optional): Choose the tool you would like to be assigned for the service (Chatjimmy or Other).
* **Output File** (`output_file`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional information or attachments related to your service request.
* **Category** (`category`, Select One, Optional): Select the category for your service request from the provided options (Veterinary Service Forms, Forms, or Templates).
* **Description** (`description`, Text, Optional): Briefly describe your service needs.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, Text, Optional): Enter your desired Form ID if you have one.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you complete all required fields to ensure a successful application.
* If you have any attachments or additional information, you can upload them in the **Output File** field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the veterinary organization for assistance.
