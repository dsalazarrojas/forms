# Veterinary Diagnostic Evaluation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from pet owners for a veterinary diagnostic evaluation. The purpose of this form is to collect relevant details about the pet's owner and medical history, as well as their consent for diagnostic procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each section carefully, ensuring that the information is accurate and up-to-date.
2. If a field is not applicable, please leave it blank.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please consult with the veterinarian or veterinary clinic staff for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Details** (Page 1):
	+ This section is for collecting general information about the pet's owner.
	+ Enter the owner's name, address, and contact number in the corresponding fields.
* **Pet Details** (Page 2):
	+ This section is for collecting information about the pet, including their species, breed, age, and weight.
	+ Enter the pet's name, species, breed, age, and weight in the corresponding fields.
* **Symptoms** (Page 3):
	+ This section is for describing the pet's symptoms, including any health issues or concerns.
	+ Enter a detailed description of the pet's symptoms in the text field.
* **Consent** (Page 4):
	+ This section is for obtaining consent from the pet owner for diagnostic procedures.
	+ Select one of the options to indicate your consent.
* **Diagnostic Procedures** (Page 5):
	+ This section is for selecting the diagnostic procedures to be performed on the pet.
	+ Choose one or more of the options that apply to the pet's condition.
* **Contact Info** (Page 6):
	+ This section is for providing contact information for the pet owner.
	+ Select one of the options to indicate the best way to contact the pet owner.
* **Medical History** (Page 7):
	+ This section is for providing any relevant medical history about the pet.
	+ Enter a detailed description of the pet's medical history in the text field.
* **Allergies** (Page 8):
	+ This section is for listing any allergies the pet may have.
	+ Enter a list of any allergies the pet may have in the text field.
* **Medications** (Page 9):
	+ This section is for listing any medications the pet may be taking.
	+ Enter a list of any medications the pet may be taking in the text field.
* **Owner Signature** (Page 10):
	+ This section is for the pet owner to sign the form, indicating they have reviewed and agreed to the information provided.
	+ Please sign and date the form.
* **Client Signature** (Page 11):
	+ This section is for the veterinarian or veterinary clinic staff to sign and date the form, indicating they have reviewed and will follow the agreed-upon treatment plan.
	+ Please sign and date the form.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any question, please consult with the veterinarian or veterinary clinic staff for clarification.
* This form is designed to be completed by the pet owner, but may also be reviewed and signed by the veterinarian or veterinary clinic staff.
