# Pet Travel Assistance Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Travel Assistance Program Application Form is designed to gather information about pet owners who are traveling with their pets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of pet you are traveling with.
2. Enter your pet's name.
3. Enter your contact name and phone number.
4. Enter the travel date and duration.
5. Select the travel location.
6. Enter the travel distance.
7. Choose the reason for your travel (Personal or Business).
8. Select the method of travel (Car, Airplane).
9. Choose how often you travel with your pet (Rarely, Occasionally, Frequently).
10. Enter any additional travel notes.
11. Enter any other travel notes.
12. Select the travel assistance amount you are requesting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Type** (Select Multiple): Choose the type of pet you are traveling with (e.g. dog, cat).
* **Pet Name** (Text): Enter your pet's name.
* **Contact Name** (Text): Enter your name as the contact person for this travel.
* **Contact Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
* **Travel Date** (Date): Enter the date you will be traveling.
* **Travel Start Date** (Date): Enter the start date of your travel.
* **Travel End Date** (Date): Enter the end date of your travel.
* **Travel Location** (Text): Enter the location you will be traveling to.
* **Travel Duration** (Number): Enter the length of your travel.
* **Travel Distance** (Text): Enter the distance of your travel.
* **Reason For Travel** (Select Multiple): Choose the reason for your travel (Personal or Business).
* **Travel Method** (Select One): Choose the method of your travel (Car, Airplane).
* **How Often** (Select One): Choose how often you travel with your pet (Rarely, Occasionally, Frequently).
* **Travel Cost** (Number): Enter the cost of your travel.
* **Travel Notes** (Note): Enter any additional notes about your travel.
* **Other Travel Notes** (Note): Enter any other notes about your travel.
* **Travel Assistance Amount** (Select One): Choose the travel assistance amount you are requesting ($10-$50, $51-$100, $101-$200, or Other).

## Tips
* Make sure all required fields are filled out.
* Enter accurate information about your pet and travel.
* If you need to enter other travel notes, use the "Other Travel Notes" field.
* If you need to request a specific travel assistance amount, use the "Travel Assistance Amount" field.
