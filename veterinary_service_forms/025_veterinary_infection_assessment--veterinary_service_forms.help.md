# Veterinary Infection Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help veterinarians and animal health professionals assess and identify potential infections in animals. It collects information about the pet's symptoms and the suspected infection site, enabling the professional to take informed decisions on treatments and care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions carefully and answer them accurately.
2. For the "Symptoms" field, select all the symptoms that you have observed or reported for the pet.
3. In the "Suspected Infection Site" field, choose the specific area where you suspect the infection is located.
4. In the "Treatments" field, describe the treatments or care that you have given to the pet.

* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, select_multiple): Select all the symptoms you have observed or reported for the pet, such as:

	+ Dry skin
	+ Droopy eyes

	+ Excessive yawning

	+ Excessive shedding
	+ Fever

	+ Increased appetite

	+ Loss of appetite

**Tips**

* Please be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, consult with a veterinarian or animal health professional for guidance.
* The information collected on this form will help the veterinarian or animal health professional make informed decisions about the pet's care and treatment.
