# Pet Health Record Duplication Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a duplication of a pet's health record. A duplication is only required if the original record is incomplete, missing, or corrupted. Ensure you are requesting a duplication for a valid reason before submitting the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out all required fields, marked as (Required).
*   Enter the pet's information accurately and truthfully.
*   Select the reason for requesting a duplication in the "Duplicate For" field.
*   Choose the options for duplication in the "Duplicate For Options" field (if necessary).
*   Check your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Pet Owner Information** (`pet_owner_information`, text, required: false): Please enter the pet owner's contact information, including any relevant details.
*   **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the pet whose health record you are requesting a duplication for.
*   **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as the pet owner.
*   **Owner Email** (`owner_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address to contact you for further information or updates.
*   **Veterinarian Phone** (`veterinarian_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the contact information of the veterinarian who treated your pet.
*   **Medical Record Details** (`medical_record_details`, text, required: false): Provide any additional details about the pet's medical record that might be helpful for the duplication process.
*   **Duplicate For** (`duplicate_for`, select_one, required: false): Select a valid reason for requesting a duplication from the options provided.
*   **Duplicate For Options** (`duplicate_for_options`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the specific options for duplication from the options provided (if applicable).
