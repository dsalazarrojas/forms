# Pet Boarding Waiver And Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Pet Boarding Waiver And Consent Form is a mandatory document that you must complete before leaving your pet with us for boarding. This form outlines your understanding of our services, including liability, payment, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please carefully review each field and follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Boarding Waiver And Consent** (id: 1, text, required/optional): This is the name of the form, which is self-explanatory.
* **Terms And Conditions** (id: 2, text, required/true): Please provide a brief explanation of our terms and conditions, including but not limited to our liability, payment, and cancellation policies.
* **Owner Name** (id: 3, text, required/true): Please provide your full name as the owner of the pet.
* **Owner Address** (id: 4, text, required/false): Please provide your address where you can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Owner Phone** (id: 5, text, required/false): Please provide your phone number where you can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Owner Email** (id: 6, email, required/false): Please provide your email address where you can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (id: 7, text, required/true): Please provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (id: 8, text, required/false): Please provide the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (id: 9, email, required/false): Please provide the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Vet Clinician Name** (id: 10, text, required/true): Please provide the name of the veterinarian who will be caring for your pet.
* **Vet Clinician Phone** (id: 11, text, required/false): Please provide the phone number of the veterinarian who will be caring for your pet.
* **Vet Clinician Email** (id: 12, email, required/true): Please provide the email address of the veterinarian who will be caring for your pet.
* **Boarding Start Date** (id: 13, date, required/true): Please provide the date your pet will start boarding with us.
* **Boarding End Date** (id: 14, date, required/true): Please provide the date your pet will end boarding with us.
* **Payment Method** (id: 15, select_one, required/true): Please select the payment method you will use (Cash, Credit Card, Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Amount** (id: 16, number, required/false): Please provide the total cost of boarding your pet, including any additional fees.
* **Payment Terms** (id: 17, text, required/true): Please provide any specific payment terms or instructions.
* **Liability** (id: 18, select_multiple, required/false): Please select any liabilities you agree to, such as damages or injuries to your pet or our facility.
* **Pet Name** (id: 19, text, required/true): Please provide your pet's name.
* **Pet Type** (id: 20, select_multiple, required/true): Please select the type of pet (e.g., dog, cat, etc.).
* **Pet Health Issues** (id: 21, text, required/false): Please list any health issues or medical conditions your pet has.
* **Pet Medication** (id: 22, text, required/false): Please list any medication your pet is currently taking.
* **Pet Medication Dosage** (id: 23, text, required/false): Please provide the dosage instructions for your pet's medication.
* **Boarding Fees** (id: 24, number, required/true): Please provide the total cost of boarding your pet, including any additional fees.
* **Owner Signature** (id: 25, note, required/true): Please sign this form to confirm your agreement to our terms and conditions.
