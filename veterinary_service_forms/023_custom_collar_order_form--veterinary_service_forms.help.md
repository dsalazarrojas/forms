# Custom Collar Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Collar Order Form is a tool used to collect information for custom collar orders, allowing retailers or veterinarians to place or manage orders for customers.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, select the appropriate options for your order.
2.  Fill in the relevant fields with your information and the customer's details.
3.  Review your order details before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Retail Store** (`retail_store`, select_one, required: false)
    This field allows you to select whether the order is for your store or not.
* **Custom Collar Type** (`custom_collar_type`, select_multiple, required: false)
    This field gives you the option to choose from various custom collar types.
* **Collar Size** (`collar_size`, select_one, required: false)
    Select the size of the collar you need.
* **Dog Breed** (`dog_breed`, select_multiple, required: false)
    Choose the dog breed that the collar is for.
* **Color** (`color`, select_one, required: false)
    Pick the color you want the collar to be.
* **Ribbon Size** (`ribbon_size`, select_one, required: false)
    Select the size of the ribbon you want.
* **Collar Material** (`collar_material`, select_one, required: false)
    Choose the material you want the collar to be made of.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required: false)
    Enter the date you want to place the order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, required: false)
    Enter the time you want to place the order.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false)
    Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false)
    Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false)
    Enter the customer's email address.
* **Custom Order Details** (`custom_order_details`, note, required: false)
    Enter any additional custom order details.
* **Retail Store Name** (`retail_store_name`, text, required: false)
    Enter your store's name.
* **Retail Store Phone** (`retail_store_phone`, text, required: false)
    Enter your store's phone number.
* **Retail Store Email** (`retail_store_email`, email, required: false)
    Enter your store's email address.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required: false)
    Enter the shipping address.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, required: false)
    Enter the billing address.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: false)
    Select the payment method you're using.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, required: false)
    Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, required: false)
    Select the status of the order.
* **Custom Options** (`custom_options`, select_multiple, required: false)
    Choose any custom options you want.
* **Retail Store Manager** (`retail_store_manager`, text, required: false)
    Enter the manager's name.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required: false)
    Enter the manager's phone number.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required: false)
    Enter the manager's email address.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
* Review the order details before submitting the form.
* Double-check your selections to ensure accuracy.
* Contact the retailer or veterinarian if you have any questions or issues with the order.
