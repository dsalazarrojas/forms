# Detection Dog Daily Training Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to record the daily training activities of detection dogs. It is designed to collect information about the location, weather conditions, dog characteristics, and training details to help improve the effectiveness of detection dog training programs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the training location where the training took place.
2. Enter the date of the training session.
3. Choose the number of dogs that were trained.
4. Select the duration of the training session.
5. Record any notes about the training session.
6. Enter the name of the dog(s) that were trained.
7. Provide information about the weather conditions at the training location.
8. Select any additional notes to record any specific details about the training session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Location** (`training_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where the training took place, such as a park or a specific building.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the training session.
* **Number of Dogs** (`number_of_dogs`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of dogs that were trained.
* **Training Duration** (`training_duration`, number, required/optional): Enter the duration of the training session in minutes.
* **Training Notes** (`training_notes`, note, required/optional): Record any notes about the training session, such as any notable events or issues that occurred.
* **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the trainer.
* **Trainer Phone** (`trainer_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the trainer's phone number.
* **Trainer Email** (`trainer_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the trainer's email address.
* **Weather Condition** (`weather_condition`, select_one, required/optional): Select the current weather condition at the training location, such as sunny or rainy.
* **Temperature** (`temperature`, select_one, required/optional): Select the temperature at the training location, such as hot or cold.
* **Location Weather** (`location_weather`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any additional weather conditions at the training location, such as clear or windy.
* **Location Surface** (`location_surface`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the surface type at the training location, such as grass or dirt.
* **Dog Breed** (`dog_breed`, select_one, required/optional): Select the breed of dog that was trained.
* **Dog Age** (`dog_age`, number, required/optional): Enter the age of the dog.
* **Dog Weight** (`dog_weight`, number, required/optional): Enter the weight of the dog.
* **Dog Name** (`dog_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the dog.
* **Trainer Notes** (`trainer_notes`, note, required/optional): Record any additional notes from the trainer.
* **Location Notes** (`location_notes`, note, required/optional): Record any additional notes about the training location.
* **Weather Notes** (`weather_notes`, note, required/optional): Record any additional weather notes.
* **Other Notes** (`other_notes`, note, required/optional): Record any other additional notes.
* **Training Notes 2** (`training_notes_2`, note, required/optional): Record any additional training notes.
* **Weather Notes 2** (`weather_notes_2`, note, required/optional): Record any additional weather notes.
* **Trainer Signature** (`trainer_signature`, text, required/optional): Enter the trainer's signature.

Note: Fields with `required/optional` status indicate whether the field is required or optional to be filled in the form.
