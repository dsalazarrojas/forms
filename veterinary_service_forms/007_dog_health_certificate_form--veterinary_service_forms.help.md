# Dog Health Certificate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Health Certificate Form is a document that verifies a dog's health status. It is usually required for travel or other specific situations where a dog's health is a concern.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your dog's information and the veterinarian's details.
2. Select the relevant health certifications or vaccinations that your dog has received.
3. Enter your dog's weight and age.
4. Provide your information as the dog's owner.
5. Select the signature of the veterinarian who has examined the dog.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dog Health Certificate Form** (`dog_health_certificate_form`, text, required): This is the main section of the form where you will enter the dog's health status.
* **Dog Health Certifications/Vaccinations** (`dog_health_certificate_form_vaccinations`, select_multiple, optional): Check the vaccinations or health certifications that apply to your dog, such as Rabies Vaccine, Distemper Vaccine, Parvovirus Vaccine, Heartworms, and Flea/Tick Control.
* **Dog Weight** (`dog_weight`, number, required): Enter your dog's current weight in pounds or kilograms.
* **Dog Breed** (`dog_breed`, text, optional): Enter your dog's breed, if applicable.
* **Dog Age** (`dog_age`, text, optional): Enter your dog's age in years or months.
* **Owner Information** (`owner_info`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Veterinarian Information** (`veterinarian_info`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the veterinarian who examined the dog.
* **Dog Health Certificate Number** (`dog_health_certificate_number`, text, optional): Enter a unique identifier for this health certificate, if required.
* **Date of Exam** (`date_of_exam`, date, optional): Enter the date the veterinarian examined the dog.
* **Date of Issue** (`date_of_issue`, date, optional): Enter the date the health certificate was issued.
* **Veterinary Clinic** (`veterinary_clinic`, text, optional): Enter the name and address of the veterinary clinic where the examination took place.
* **Veterinary License** (`veterinary_license`, text, optional): Enter the license number of the veterinarian.
* **Signature** (`signature`, select_one, required): Select the signature of the veterinarian who signed the health certificate, such as Dr. Smith, Dr. Johnson, or Dr. Doe.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
* Review the form carefully before submission.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult the veterinarian or a trusted resource.
* This form is only valid for the period specified by the veterinarian.
