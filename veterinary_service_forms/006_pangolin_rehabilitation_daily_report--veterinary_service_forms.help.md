# Pangolin Rehabilitation Daily Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to record details for the rehabilitation and care of pangolins in animal shelters or veterinary clinics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the **Pangolin Details** form (page 1) with the relevant information, including the Pangolin ID, Species, Sex, and any other necessary details.
2. Move on to filling out the **Care Progress** form (page 2), where you will find fields to record progress and any additional progress notes.
3. In the **Care Provider** section (page 3), select all the care providers responsible for the pangolin's care.
4. Move on to the **Species Info** section (page 4), where you will find fields to record any relevant information about the species.
5. In the **Weight** and **Weight Unit** sections (page 5), record the weight of the pangolin and select the unit of measurement.
6. In the **Care Location** section (page 6), select the location where the care is being provided.
7. In the **Medical Notes** section (page 7), record any medical notes or observations about the pangolin.
8. Finally, fill out the **Food Intake** and **Water Intake** sections (pages 8 and 9) to record the intake of food and water for the pangolin.
