# Pet Boarding Check In Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from pet owners when checking in their pets for boarding services. It gathers essential details about the pet, including owner details, pet details, and boarding information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Pet Boarding Check In** page with any notes or comments about the check-in process.
2. Select your answer to **Owner Details**:
    * Yes: The pet is an authorized owner or caretaker.
    * No: The pet is not an authorized owner or caretaker.
3. Enter **Pet Details** and fill in any notes about your pet.
4. Select a **Date Of Check In** for when you checked in the pet.
5. Select a **Date Of Check Out** for when you would like the pet to be checked out.
6. Choose a **Time Of Check In** for when you brought the pet in for boarding.
7. Choose a **Time Of Check Out** for when you would like the pet to be picked up.
8. Enter any **Notes** about the pet's boarding.
9. Enter your **Email** address for contact information.
10. Enter your **Phone** number for emergency contact.
11. Choose a **Category** for the pet (Pet Boarding or Other).
12. Select a **Type** for the pet (Dog, Cat, or Other).
13. Choose the **Breed** of the pet (Golden, Poodle, Bulldog, Persian, or Other).
14. Choose the **Color** of the pet (Black, White, Brown, or Other).
15. Choose the **Size** of the pet (Small, Medium, Large, or Other).
16. Enter the **Weight** of the pet.
17. Enter the **Date Of Birth** of the pet.
18. Enter the **Microchip ID** (if the pet has one).
19. Enter any **Medical Conditions** the pet has.
20. Enter any **Medical Notes** about the pet.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Boarding Check In**: A brief description of what you want to convey about this check-in.
* **Owner Details**: Select yes if you are an authorized owner or caretaker of the pet.
* **Pet Details**: Enter all the details about the pet, including notes about it.
* **Date Of Check In**: Select a date for when you checked the pet in for boarding.
* **Date Of Check Out**: Select a date for when you would like the pet to be checked out.
* **Time Of Check In**: Select a time for when you brought the pet in for boarding.
* **Time Of Check Out**: Select a time for when you would like the pet to be picked up.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the pet's boarding.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact information.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for emergency contact.
* **Category**: Choose a category for the pet (Pet Boarding or Other).
* **Type**: Select a type for the pet (Dog, Cat, or Other).
* **Breed**: Select a breed for the pet (Golden, Poodle, Bulldog, Persian, or Other).
* **Color**: Select a color for the pet (Black, White, Brown, or Other).
* **Size**: Select a size for the pet (Small, Medium, Large, or Other).
* **Weight**: Enter the weight of the pet.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the pet.
* **Microchip ID**: Enter the microchip ID if the pet has one.
* **Medical Conditions**: Enter any medical conditions the pet has.
* **Medical Notes**: Enter any additional medical notes about the pet.
