# Veterinary Software Demo Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for veterinarians and veterinary clinics interested in demoing our software. It's a simple, multi-page form that collects your contact information and preferred demo time.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form with your clinic's name and your personal information.
2. Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you with more information about the demo.
3. Provide your phone number so we can follow up with you about the demo schedule.
4. Choose a preferred demo time that works for you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Clinic Name** (text, required): Enter the name of your veterinary clinic.
- **Your Name** (text, required): Enter your personal name as the representative of the clinic.
- **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address to receive communication from us about the demo.
- **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
- **Preferred Demo Time** (date, required): Choose a date and time that works best for you to schedule the demo.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to help us better understand your needs and preferences.
* If you have any specific questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us at your earliest convenience.
