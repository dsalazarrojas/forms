# Vet Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you schedule an appointment with a veterinarian or pet care professional. Select from a list of options to choose the best professional for your pet's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of professional you want to schedule an appointment with from the list of options on page 1 of the form. Select "Veterinarian" for a doctor consultation or "Pet Care Professional" for other pet care services.
2. If you've selected "Veterinarian", choose your preferred doctor from the list on page 2 of the form. Select "Dr Smith", "Dr Johnson", or "Dr Thompson" for an appointment with one of our experienced veterinarians.
3. If you've selected "Pet Care Professional", choose "Jane Doe" or "John Doe" for an appointment with one of our pet care experts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Schedule Appointment**: On page 1 of the form, select one or more options from the list to choose the type of professional you want to schedule an appointment with.
* **Select Veterinarian**: On page 2 of the form, choose a doctor for an appointment. This field allows you to select one option from the list of available veterinarians.
* **Select Pet Care Professional**: On page 2 of the form, choose a pet care professional for an appointment. This field allows you to select one option from the list of available professionals.

## Tips
* Be sure to select the type of professional that best suits your pet's needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form or your appointment, feel free to ask us for assistance.
