# Veterinary Services Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a pet's health and medical history for the veterinary clinic's records. It is used to schedule an appointment for the pet to receive veterinary services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Pet Owner's Name** in the designated field.
2. Select the **Pet Species** from the list provided (e.g., Cat, Dog, Other).
3. Enter the **Pet Breed** if it is not listed, provide a description of the pet's breed.
4. Enter the **Pet Age**.
5. Provide the **Owner Contact Information**.
6. List any **Pet Medical Conditions**.
7. Select the **Veterinary Clinic** where the appointment is to be made (e.g., Dr. Smith, Dr. Johnson, Other).
8. Choose a date and time for the **Appointment Date** and **Appointment Time**.
9. If necessary, provide **Notes** about the appointment.
10. Optionally, provide additional information, such as **Prophets Name**, **Medical Records**, **Veterinary Certificate**, **Medical History**, **Pet Microchip Number**, **Veterinary Certificate Number**, and **Pet License**.
11. Enter the **Pet License** number.
12. Provide the **Owner Signature**, **Veterinary Signature**, and **Client Signature**.
13. If necessary, add **Comments** about the appointment.
14. Finally, enter your **Phone Number** and **Email Address** (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Owners Name**: Enter the name of the pet owner.
* **Pet Species**: Select the species of the pet (e.g., Cat, Dog, Other).
* **Pet Breed**: If the breed is not listed, provide a description of the pet's breed.
* **Pet Age**: Enter the age of the pet.
* **Owner Contact Info**: Enter the owner's contact information.
* **Pet Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions the pet may have.
* **Veterinary Clinic**: Select the clinic where the appointment is to be made (e.g., Dr. Smith, Dr. Johnson, Other).
* **Appointment Date**: Choose a date for the appointment.
* **Appointment Time**: Choose a time for the appointment.
* **Notes**: Optionally, add notes about the appointment.
* **Prophets Name**: This field is likely a typo and not applicable. It is not required.
* **Medical Records**: Optionally, attach any medical records related to the pet.
* **Veterinary Certificate**: Optionally, attach any veterinary certificate related to the pet.
* **Medical History**: Optionally, provide any relevant medical history for the pet.
* **Pet Microchip Number**: Optionally, enter the pet's microchip number.
* **Veterinary Certificate Number**: Optionally, enter any veterinary certificate number.
* **Pet License**: Optionally, enter the pet's license number.
* **Owner Signature**: Enter the owner's signature.
* **Veterinary Signature**: Enter the veterinary signature.
* **Client Signature**: Enter the client signature.
* **Veterinary Stamp**: This field is not applicable and not required.
* **Comments**: Optionally, add comments about the appointment.
* **Submitted By**: This field is not applicable and not required.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the owner or contact person.
* **Email Address**: Optionally, enter the email address of the owner or contact person.
