# Veterinary Anesthesia Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for veterinarians and veterinary clinics to obtain informed consent from pet owners before performing anesthesia on their pets. It's a crucial document that outlines the patient's information, medical history, and anesthesia details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide the patient's information, including their name, age, and contact details.
2. In the "Anesthesia Information" section, describe the procedure that will be performed and the type of anesthesia used.
3. In the "Medical History" section, list any medical conditions or allergies the pet may have that could impact the anesthesia procedure.
4. In the "Procedure Information" section, provide details about the procedure, including any risks or complications that may arise.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (patient_info, text, optional):
  This section is for collecting the pet's basic information, such as name, age, and contact details. Please provide the pet's name, age, and any other relevant details.

* **Anesthesia Information** (anesthesia_info, text, optional):
  In this section, describe the type of anesthesia procedure that will be performed on the pet. Be specific about the type of anesthesia used and any potential risks or complications associated with it.

* **Medical History** (medical_history, text, optional):
  List any medical conditions or allergies the pet may have that could impact the anesthesia procedure. This will help the veterinarian understand the pet's overall health and adjust the procedure accordingly.

* **Procedure Information** (procedure_info, text, optional):
  Provide details about the procedure, including any risks or complications that may arise. This will help the pet owner understand the procedure and make informed decisions.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all sections are filled out accurately and completely to ensure the veterinarian has the necessary information to perform the procedure safely and effectively.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please ask your veterinarian for clarification.
* Review the form carefully before signing it to ensure you understand the information provided.
