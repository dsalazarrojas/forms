# Veterinary Client Communication Style Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from veterinary clinic staff on their communication preferences with pet owners, helping the clinic tailor their communication style to better meet the needs of their clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the pages and fill in the fields as requested. Each page has a specific set of questions related to the category. For example, Page 1 asks for pet owner information, while Page 2 requests contact preferences. You can move back and forth between pages using the navigation buttons to review and edit your answers as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Owner Information** (`pet_owner_info`, text, optional)
  This field is for you to provide some additional information about the pet owner, such as their relationship with the pet (e.g. parent, caregiver, etc.).
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, text, optional)
  This is where you can specify your preferred method of contact, such as phone, email, or in-person.
* **Update Frequency** (`update_frequency`, number, optional)
  Please enter the frequency at which you would like to receive updates from the clinic, e.g. daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Tone Choices** (`tone_choices`, select_multiple, optional)
  Select all the tones that you feel are most suitable for communicating with pet owners. This can include options like friendly, professional, or neutral.
* **Reminder Preferences** (`reminder_preferences`, text, optional)
  If you have any specific preferences for reminders, such as time of day or method of delivery, please enter them here.
* **Follow Up Preferences** (`follow_up_preferences`, text, optional)
  Describe your preferred follow-up method, if applicable. This can be in-person, phone call, or email.
* **Communication Style** (`communication_style`, select_one, required)
  Choose the style that best suits your communication with pet owners. This can be friendly, professional, or a combination of both.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional)
  If you have any additional thoughts or comments, feel free to enter them in this free-text field.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
  Enter your preferred email address for receiving updates and reminders.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional)
  If you have a preferred phone number, please enter it here. This will be used for follow-up calls or other notifications.
