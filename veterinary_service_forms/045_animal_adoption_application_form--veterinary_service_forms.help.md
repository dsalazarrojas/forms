# Animal Adoption Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a crucial part of the animal adoption process, allowing us to gather essential information about you and your suitability for adopting a pet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full name** in the **Full name** field.
2. Enter your **Email address** in the **Email address** field. This will be used for communication purposes.
3. Provide your **Phone number** in the **Phone number** field. This will help us contact you if needed.
4. Select your **Preferred pet type** from the options provided (Dog, Cat, or Other).
5. Briefly describe your **Experience with pets** in the text field.
6. Indicate the **Home suitability for pets** by describing the type of living arrangement you have (Yard, Apartment, or Fenced area).
7. If requested, provide **References** to support your application.
8. Confirm that you **Agree to meet adoption requirements** by selecting "True" from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email address**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Preferred pet type**: Choose the type of pet you are interested in adopting (Dog, Cat, or Other).
* **Experience with pets**: Briefly describe your experience with pets, including any relevant training or experience caring for animals.
* **Home suitability for pets**: Describe the type of living arrangement you have, such as a yard or a fenced area.
* **References**: If requested, provide references that can vouch for your ability to care for a pet.
* **I agree to meet adoption requirements**: Confirm that you are committed to meeting the requirements for adopting a pet.
