# Pet Sitting Pet Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Sitting Pet Form is a general-purpose form designed to collect information about a pet during a visit or stay at a location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the pet's details in the "Pet" field.
2. Choose a date and time for the visit or stay using the "Date" and "Time" fields.
3. Optionally, enter the purpose of the visit or stay in the "Purpose" field.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet** (`pet`, text, required): Enter the pet's name and other identifying information.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Choose a date for the visit or stay.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Choose a time for the visit or stay.
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the visit or stay purpose.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Use the correct date and time format (DD/MM/YY, HH:MM AM/PM).
* If the "Purpose" field is not applicable, you can leave it blank.
