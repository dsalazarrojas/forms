# Veterinary Business Promotion Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain consent from clients to use their information for business promotion purposes. It will help your veterinary practice to stay connected with clients and promote your business to them through various channels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Client Information page with the client's details.
2. Select the marketing channels you are willing to use to promote your business to the client.
3. Provide your signature to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information**:
	+ **Client Name**: Enter the client's name as per your records.
	+ **Client Email**: Enter the client's email address (if available).
	+ **Client Phone**: Enter the client's phone number (if available).
* **Business Name**: Enter your veterinary business name.
* **Business Address**: Enter your business address.
* **Marketing Use**:
	+ **Yes**: Select this option if you are willing to use the client's information for marketing purposes.
	+ **No**: Select this option if you do not want to use the client's information for marketing purposes.
* **Consent Date**: Select the date you are granting consent for marketing use.
* **Consent Time**: Select the time of day you are granting consent for marketing use.
* **Consent Note**: Add any additional comments or notes about your consent.
* **Marketing Channels**:
	+ **Social Media**: Select this option if you want to promote your business through social media.
	+ **Email**: Select this option if you want to promote your business through email.
	+ **Phone**: Select this option if you want to promote your business through phone calls.
	+ **Text Message**: Select this option if you want to promote your business through text messages.
* **Additional Marketing Channels**:
	+ **Facebook**: Select this option if you want to promote your business through Facebook.
	+ **Instagram**: Select this option if you want to promote your business through Instagram.
	+ **Twitter**: Select this option if you want to promote your business through Twitter.
	+ **LinkedIn**: Select this option if you want to promote your business through LinkedIn.
* **Client Signature**: This is a note field where you can add any comments or notes about the client's consent.
* **Promotion Channels 2**:
	+ Select additional marketing channels you want to use for promoting your business.
* **Promotion Channels 3**:
	+ Select additional marketing channels you want to use for promoting your business.
* **Promotion Channels 4**:
	+ Select additional marketing channels you want to use for promoting your business.
* **Client Signature**: Add your signature to confirm that you have read and understood the consent form.
