# veterinary_study_publication_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for veterinary studies. It includes fields for pet owner information, study details, and communication status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the pet owner information section, including the pet's name and the owner's contact details.
2. Provide the study name and description.
3. Enter the publication date and consent date for the study.
4. Select the veterinary clinic where the study was conducted.
5. Enter the contact phone and email details of the person responsible for the study.
6. Confirm that an email and phone call have been sent to the pet owner.
7. Set the study status as Published or Unpublished.
8. Optionally, provide additional notes and follow-up information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Owner**: Enter the pet owner's name.
* **Study Name**: Enter the name of the veterinary study.
* **Study Description**: Briefly describe the purpose and objective of the study.
* **Publication Date**: Enter the date of publication for the study.
* **Consent Date**: Enter the date when the pet owner gave their consent for the study.
* **Pet**: Enter the name of the pet participating in the study.
* **Veterinary Clinic**: Select the clinic where the study was conducted.
* **Veterinarian**: Enter the name of the veterinarian responsible for the study.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person. **(Required)**
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the study.
* **Email Sent**: Confirm if an email has been sent to the pet owner. **(Required)**
* **Email Status**: Set the status of the email sent to the pet owner (Sent, Not Sent, Pending).
* **Phone Sent**: Confirm if a phone call has been made to the pet owner. **(Required)**
* **Phone Status**: Set the status of the phone call (Sent, Not Sent, Pending).
* **Study Status**: Set the status of the study as Published or Unpublished. **(Required)**
* **Study ID**: Enter a unique ID for the study.
* **Publication ID**: Enter the ID of the publication.
* **Participant ID**: Enter the ID of the participant.
* **Publication URL**: Enter the URL of the publication.
* **Publication Type**: Enter the type of publication (e.g., article, study).
* **Follow-up**: Enter any follow-up information.
* **Follow-up Notes**: Add any follow-up notes.

## Tips

Please make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it. Some fields are required, and it is essential to fill out all required fields accurately and completely. If you have any questions or need assistance, please do not hesitate to contact us.
