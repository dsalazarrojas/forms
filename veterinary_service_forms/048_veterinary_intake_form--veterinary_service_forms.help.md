# Veterinary Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from pet owners when their pet visits a veterinary service. This form will help the veterinary service create a treatment plan tailored to the pet's specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out each page carefully**: The form is divided into multiple pages, each covering a specific aspect of the pet's information. Make sure to complete each page thoroughly and accurately.
2. **Provide as much detail as possible**: The more information you provide, the better the veterinary service can care for your pet.
3. **Use correct formatting**: Ensure that you enter the correct format for each field, e.g., email addresses should be in the correct email format (e.g., `example@example.com`).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Pet Information

* **Pet Information (pet_info)**: Enter the name, age, and species of your pet.
* **Owner Information (owner_info)**: Provide your contact information so that the veterinary service can reach out to you for updates or follow-ups.

### Page 2: Contact Information

* **Contact Information (contact_info)**: Enter your contact information, including your name and phone number.
* **Medical Information (medical_info)**: Provide any relevant medical information about your pet, such as previous conditions or medications.
* **Medical Conditions (medical_conditions)**: List any medical conditions or allergies your pet may have.
* **Treatment Plans (treatment_plans)**: Describe any previous treatment plans or regimens your pet is currently following.

### Page 3: Veterinary Information

* **Veterinarian Information (veterinarian)**: Enter the name and contact information of the veterinarian who will be caring for your pet.
* **Veterinarian Phone (veterinarian_phone)**: Enter the phone number of the veterinarian's office.
* **Veterinarian Email (veterinarian_email)**: Enter the email address of the veterinarian's office.
* **Pet Type (pet_type)**: Select the species of your pet from the options provided.
* **Pet Condition (pet_condition)**: Select any medical conditions your pet may have from the options provided.

### Page 4: Additional Information

* **Contact Phone (contact_phone)**: Enter your phone number for any additional communication.
* **Contact Email (contact_email)**: Enter your email address for any follow-up communication.
