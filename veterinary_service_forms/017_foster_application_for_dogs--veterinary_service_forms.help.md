# Foster Application For Dogs - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for potential foster families to apply to become foster homes for dogs in need. Please fill out this form to provide information about yourself and your family, so we can determine if you're a good match to provide a loving and safe environment for a dog.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all applicable answers for the following questions. You can choose multiple options for fields that allow multiple selections.
2. Fill out all required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Make sure to provide detailed answers to optional fields as much as possible, this will help us get a better understanding of your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Type** (Select Multiple, id: 1): 
    Select all the types of fostering you are interested in (e.g., short-term, long-term, emergency).
* **Name** (Text, id: 2): Your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Address** (Text, id: 3): The address where you currently reside.
* **City** (Text, id: 4): The city where you currently reside.
* **State** (Text, id: 5): The state where you currently reside.
* **Zip** (Text, id: 6): Your zip code.
* **Phone** (Text, id: 7): Your phone number.
* **Email** (Email, id: 8): Your email address.
* **Pet Name** (Text, id: 9): The name of the dog you would like to apply for.
* **Pet Type** (Select One, id: 10): Select 'Yes' if you're applying for a specific breed or type of dog.
* **Reason for Fostering** (Text, id: 11): Describe why you're interested in fostering a dog.
* **How Long Would You Like to Foster** (Select Multiple, id: 12): 
    Select all the time frames you're available to foster a dog (e.g., short-term, long-term).
* **Experience with Dogs** (Select Multiple, id: 13): Select all the types of experiences you have with dogs (e.g., owned, volunteered, worked).
* **Previous Experience** (Text, id: 14): Describe any previous experience you have with dogs.
* **Available for Visit** (Select Multiple, id: 15): Select all the times you're available for visits (e.g., during the week, on weekends).
* **Availability Dates** (Date, id: 16): The dates you're available to start fostering a dog.
* **Availability Time** (Time, id: 17): The time of day you're available to foster a dog.
* **Availability Notes** (Text, id: 18): Any additional information about your availability.
* **Why This Foster Home** (Text, id: 19): Describe why you're a good fit for fostering a dog.
* **Number of Children** (Text, id: 20): The number of children living in your home.
* **Number of Fostered Dogs** (Text, id: 21): The number of dogs you've fostered in the past.
* **How Would You Prevent Bites** (Text, id: 22): Describe how you would prevent bites or attacks by a fostered dog.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Text, id: 23): The name of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Text, id: 24): The phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Select Multiple, id: 25): Select all the relationships you have with the emergency contact person (e.g., parent, friend, family).
