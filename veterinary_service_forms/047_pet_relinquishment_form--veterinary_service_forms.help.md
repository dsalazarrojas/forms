# Pet Relinquishment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Relinquishment Form is used to provide information about your pet, including its type, name, reason for relinquishment, and other relevant details. This information will be used to create a profile for your pet and help potential adopters make informed decisions about adopting your pet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of pet you are relinquishing.
2.  Enter your pet's name.
3.  Provide the reason for relinquishment.
4.  Enter your name and contact information.
5.  Provide the date and time you last saw your pet.
6.  Enter any notes or comments about your pet.
