# Veterinary Care Agreement Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about a pet's veterinary care extension. This form is used to add to or extend a pet's veterinary care agreement. It is used by veterinary clinics to record and document additional information about a pet's species, breed, age, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information about the pet.
2. Select the relevant options for the pet's species, breed, age, and sex.
3. Add any additional comments or notes as needed.
4. Sign the form to confirm that the information is accurate and complete.
5. Sign the form as the pet owner to confirm that the information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet owners information**: This field is used to gather information about the pet owner's contact details.
  * A text field where you can enter the pet owner's name, address, and other relevant contact information.
  * Required: No
* **Contact information**: This field is used to gather contact information about the pet owner.
  * A text field where you can enter the pet owner's phone number and email address.
  * Required: No
* **Appointment information**: This field is used to gather information about the pet's appointment details.
  * A text field where you can enter the date, time, and other relevant details about the pet's appointment.
  * Required: No
* **Veterinary clinic information**: This field is used to gather information about the veterinary clinic.
  * A text field where you can enter the clinic's name, address, and other relevant details.
  * Required: No
* **Extension reason**: This field is used to gather information about the reason for the veterinary care extension.
  * A text field where you can enter a brief description of the reason for the extension.
  * Required: No
* **Additional comments**: This field is used to gather any additional comments or notes about the pet's care.
  * A text field where you can enter any additional information about the pet's care.
  * Required: No
* **Signature**: This field is used to sign the form to confirm that the information is accurate and complete.
  * A text field where you can enter your signature.
  * Required: No
* **Veterinarian signature**: This field is used to sign the form as the veterinarian.
  * A text field where you can enter your signature.
  * Required: No
* **Date**: This field is used to enter the date of the veterinary care extension.
  * A date field where you can select the date of the extension.
  * Required: No
* **Time**: This field is used to enter the time of the veterinary care extension.
  * A time field where you can select the time of the extension.
  * Required: No
* **Pet species**: This field is used to select the species of the pet.
  * A select_one field with options "Yes" and "No" to select the pet's species.
  * Required: No
* **Pet breed**: This field is used to select the breed of the pet.
  * A select_multiple field with options "Yes" and "No" to select the pet's breed.
  * Required: No
* **Pet age**: This field is used to enter the age of the pet.
  * A number field where you can enter the pet's age.
  * Required: No
* **Pet sex**: This field is used to select the sex of the pet.
  * A select_one field with options "Yes" and "No" to select the pet's sex.
  * Required: No

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult with the veterinarian or pet owner.
* Double-check the information before submitting the form.
