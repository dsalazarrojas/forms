# Pet Adoption Reference Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Adoption Reference Questionnaire is a form used to collect reference information about pet adoptions. It is meant to be filled out by users who have adopted pets in the past or are looking to adopt a new pet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of pet you are adopting (Pet, Service Animal, or Other).
2. Provide a brief reason for the adoption (e.g., "I wanted to give a home to a deserving animal").
3. Enter the name of the pet you are adopting (if applicable).
4. Enter a contact number and email (if applicable).
5. If you selected "Other" as the pet type, provide a description of the type of pet you are adopting.
6. If you selected "Service Animal" as the pet type, select all service animals that apply to you.
7. Enter any additional information about the pet you are adopting (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Pet Type (required)
* **Pet Type** (`Pet`, `type`, required): Select the type of pet you are adopting. You can choose from "Pet", "Service Animal", or "Other".

### Adoption Reason (optional)
* **Adoption Reason** (`adoption_reason`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief reason for the adoption. This will help us understand your circumstances and provide better service to you and your pet.

### Name (optional)
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the pet you are adopting (if applicable).

### Contact Number (optional)
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, optional): Enter a contact number where we can reach you.

### Email (optional)
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address where we can reach you.

### Other Pet Type (optional)
* **Other Pet Type** (`pet_type_other`, `text`, optional): If you selected "Other" as the pet type, provide a description of the type of pet you are adopting.

### Service Animal (optional)
* **Service Animal** (`pet_type_service_animal`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you selected "Service Animal" as the pet type, select all service animals that apply to you.

### Pet (optional)
* **Pet** (`pet`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information about the pet you are adopting (if applicable).
