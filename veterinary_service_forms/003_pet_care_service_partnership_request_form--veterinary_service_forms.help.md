# Pet Care Service Partnership Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Care Service Partnership Request Form is used to collect information from pet owners who are interested in partnering with the organization to provide pet care services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select the type of service you are interested in by choosing one of the options under "Pet Care Service Partnership Request Form".
2. Enter your name and contact information (phone number and email) to allow the organization to contact you.
3. Specify the type of pet you are interested in partnering with the organization.
4. Provide the partner's name, phone number, and email (if applicable).
5. Enter the service address (if different from the partner's address).
6. Write a message to provide more information about your partnership request.
7. Finally, agree to the terms and conditions and privacy consent by selecting "I Agree" with both options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Care Service Partnership Request Form**: Please select the service you are interested in partnering with our organization for the care of your pet. This selection will help us to determine which department will be handling your request.
* **Owner Name**: This is the name of the pet's owner. Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone Number**: This is your phone number where you can be reached for follow-up calls and updates. If you don't have a phone number, please leave this field blank.
* **Email**: This is your email address where you can be reached for follow-up emails and updates. If you don’t have an email address, please leave this field blank.
* **Pet Type**: Please select the type of pet you are interested in partnering with our organization for.
* **Service Address**: This is the address where the pet will be receiving service. Please enter a valid address where the pet can be dropped off and picked up.
* **Partner Name**: This is the name of the person or organization you are partnering with.
* **Partner Phone Number**: This is the phone number of the person or organization you are partnering with.
* **Partner Email**: This is the email address of the person or organization you are partnering with.
* **Partner Service Address**: This is the address of the person or organization you are partnering with.
* **Message**: Please provide any additional information about your partnership request.
* **Agreement**: Please select "I Agree" with both "Terms & Conditions" and "Privacy & Consent" to confirm your agreement with our organization's policies.
* **I Agree**: This is the final step to confirm your agreement with our organization's terms and conditions, privacy policies, and consent. Please select the options to confirm.
