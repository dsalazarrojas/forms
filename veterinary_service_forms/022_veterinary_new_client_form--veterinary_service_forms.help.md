# Veterinary New Client Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from new clients and their pets. It is used to ensure the clinic has accurate records and can provide proper care for your animal.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to help the veterinarians understand your pet's history, health, and medical requirements.
2. For each field, use the guidelines below to help you complete the form correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your official identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your official identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to help us contact you if needed.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to ensure we can quickly contact you.
* **Address**: Enter your full address, including street number and apartment number (if applicable).
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State**: Enter the state where you reside (e.g., CA, NY, etc.).
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code to help us locate your address.
* **Species**: Enter the species of your pet (e.g., dog, cat, bird, etc.).
* **Breed**: Enter your pet's breed or type (e.g., Labrador, Persian, etc.).
* **Age**: Enter your pet's age.
* **Sex**: Select 'Yes' if your pet is male, 'No' if it is female, or 'Unknown' if you are unsure.
* **Weight**: Enter your pet's weight in kilograms or pounds.
* **Height**: Select 'Yes' if your pet has a unique height or 'No' if it does not.
* **Medical History**: Enter any medical history or allergies your pet may have.
* **Owner Signature**: Date and sign this section to confirm the information you have provided. This is required for us to verify your identity.

# Tips

* Please be as accurate and complete as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask our staff for assistance.
* We strive to provide you and your pet with the best possible care, and we appreciate your cooperation.
