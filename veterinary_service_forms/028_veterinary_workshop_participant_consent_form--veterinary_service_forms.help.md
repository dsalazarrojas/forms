# Veterinary Workshop Participant Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to obtain consent from participants in a veterinary workshop, ensuring they understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Participant Details (page 1).
2. Provide emergency contact information for a family member or friend (page 2).
3. Indicate your relationship with your emergency contact (page 2).
4. Answer questions about your participation in the workshop (not applicable in this case, as the "workshop details" field is not required).
5. Sign and date the form (page 5) to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`Participant Details`, text, required): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`Emergency Contacts`, text, not required): List at least one emergency contact with their name, phone number, and email.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`Emergency Contact Relationship`, select_one, required): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact, e.g., "spouse", "parent", "sister", etc.
* **Signed Consent** (`Signed Consent`, text, required): Sign and date the form to confirm your consent to participate in the workshop.
