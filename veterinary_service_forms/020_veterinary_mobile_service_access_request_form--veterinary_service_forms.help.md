# Veterinary Mobile Service Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Mobile Service Access Request Form is designed for clients to request access to the veterinary service. The form collects essential information to streamline the client's request process, ensuring the service can be provided efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **First name** in the "First name" field.
2.  Enter your **Last name** in the "Last name" field.
3.  Enter your **Phone number** in the "Phone" field.
4.  Enter your **Email address** in the "Email" field.
5.  Provide your **Address** in the "Address" field.
6.  Enter your **City** in the "City" field.
7.  Enter your **State** in the "State" field.
8.  Enter your **Zip code** in the "Zip" field.
9.  Select a **Date** for your visit in the "Date" field.
10. Select a **Time** for your visit in the "Time" field.
11. Enter the **Reason for visit** in the "Reason for visit" field.
12. Select the **Veterinarian** to be assigned to you from the dropdown list.
13. (Optional) Provide any additional **Comments** related to your visit.
14. (Optional) Specify the **Assigned to** veterinarian in the "Assigned to" field.
15. (Optional) Select the **Status** of your visit (e.g., "Assigned", "In Progress", "Completed").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First name** (`First name`, text, required): Enter your first name to help us identify you.
*   **Last name** (`Last name`, text, required): Enter your last name to help us identify you.
*   **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
*   **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address so we can communicate with you.
*   **Address** (`Address`, text, required): Enter your address to ensure our team can locate you.
*   **City** (`City`, text, required): Enter the city where you would like to receive service.
*   **State** (`State`, text, required): Enter the state where you live.
*   **Zip** (`Zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code to ensure accurate location.
*   **Date** (`Date`, date, required): Select a date for your visit.
*   **Time** (`Time`, time, required): Select a time for your visit.
*   **Reason for visit** (`Reason for visit`, text, required): Explain why you are requesting a mobile service visit.
*   **Veterinarian** (`Veterinarian`, select_one, required): Select the veterinarian you would like to be assigned to.
*   **Comments** (`Comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes.
*   **Assigned to** (`Assigned to`, text, optional): If you have a preferred veterinarian, enter their name.
*   **Assigned time** (`Assigned time`, text, optional): If you want to specify a specific time, enter it here.
*   **Status** (`Status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your visit (e.g., "Assigned", "In Progress", "Completed").
