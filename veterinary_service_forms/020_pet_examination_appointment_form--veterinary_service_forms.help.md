# Pet Examination Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Examination Appointment Form is designed to gather information for a pet examination, including pet details, examination date and time, and other necessary information for the veterinarian to conduct a thorough examination.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that you provide clear and concise answers for each field.
3. Double-check your input for any errors or inconsistencies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Pet Information
• **Pet Information** (`pet_info`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of the pet, including its species, age, and any relevant identification information.

### Examination Date
• **Examination Date** (`examination_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the examination, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

### Examination Time
• **Examination Time** (`examination_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the examination, in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30 for 2:30 PM).

### Pet Owner Information
• **Pet Owner Information** (`pet_owner_info`, `text`, optional): Provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself as the pet owner.

### Examination Notes
• **Examination Notes** (`examination_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant examination notes or instructions, such as specific areas of concern or special handling requirements.

### Veterinarian Information
• **Veterinarian Information** (`veterinarian_info`, `text`, optional): Provide the veterinarian's name, contact information, or any other relevant details.

### Contact Information
• **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number, so we can reach you for any questions or updates.

### Medical History
• **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, optional): If the pet has any pre-existing medical conditions or previous examinations, please include relevant details.

### Examination Result
• **Examination Result** (`examination_result`, `text`, optional): Enter any examination results or findings.

### Pet Medical Conditions
• **Pet Medical Conditions** (`pet_medical_conditions`, `select_multiple`): Check all relevant medical conditions that apply to the pet, such as 'Yes' for conditions that are currently present and 'No' for conditions that are not present.

### Medication Administered
• **Medication Administered** (`medication_administered`, `select_one`): Check if the pet has received any medication, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.

Note: If a field is marked as `optional`, you are not required to fill it in. However, providing accurate and complete information will ensure a thorough examination and better care for your pet.
