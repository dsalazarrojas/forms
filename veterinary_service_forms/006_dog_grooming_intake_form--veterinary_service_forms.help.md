# Dog Grooming Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Grooming Intake Form is used to collect information about a dog and its owner before providing grooming services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide basic information about your dog, such as its name, age, and breed.
2. Enter your contact information, including your name, address, and phone number.
3. Select the grooming services you would like to have performed on your dog.
4. Indicate whether your dog is up-to-date on its vaccinations.
5. List any medical conditions or allergies your dog has.
6. If your dog is currently taking any medication, please specify.
7. Provide any additional comments or information you would like to share about your dog.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Dog Information
* **Dog Information**: Enter your dog's name, age, and breed to help us better understand your dog's needs.

### Owner Information
* **Owner Information**: Enter your name, address, and phone number so we can contact you with any questions or updates.

### Services
* **Services**: Select the services you would like to have performed on your dog, such as a bath, clip, brush, haircut, nail trim, dematting, or teeth brushing.

### Medical Conditions
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions or health issues your dog has, such as arthritis or diabetes.

### Vaccination Records
* **Vaccination Records**: Indicate whether your dog is up-to-date on its vaccinations. If not up-to-date, please provide more information.

### Contact Info
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information, including your name, address, and phone number, so we can contact you with any questions or updates.

### Medical Info
* **Medical Info**: Provide any additional medical information about your dog, such as surgeries or medical procedures.

### Medication
* **Medication**: If your dog is currently taking any medication, please specify the name and dosage.

### Allergies
* **Allergies**: List any allergies your dog has, such as allergies to food, pollen, or certain substances.

### Owner Comments
* **Owner Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about your dog, such as its behavior or habits.
