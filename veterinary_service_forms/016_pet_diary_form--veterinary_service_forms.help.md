# Pet Diary Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Diary Form is designed to record and manage information about your pet, including its name, type, activities, and care details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Pet Diary Form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the pet's name**: Type the name of your pet in the "pet_name" field.
2. **Select the pet type**: Choose the correct pet type from the "pet_type" dropdown list. Options include Dog, Cat, and Fish.
3. **Enter the date**: Type the date of the event or entry in the "date" field. This is a required field.
4. **Describe the activities**: Type any activities or notes about your pet's activities in the "activities" field.
5. **Add any notes**: If you have any additional comments or thoughts about your pet, you can add them in the "note" field.
6. **Enter your name**: Type your name in the "owner_name" field.
7. **Enter your email**: Type your email address in the "owner_email" field.
8. **Enter your phone number**: Type your phone number in the "owner_phone" field.
9. **Assign a tool**: If applicable, type the name of the tool or equipment used for the pet's care in the "assigned_tool" field.
10. **Assign a user**: If applicable, type the name of the user responsible for the pet's care in the "assigned_user" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **pet_name** (text, required/optional): Type the name of your pet.
* **pet_type** (select multiple, required/optional): Choose the correct pet type (Dog, Cat, Fish).
* **date** (date, required): Type the date of the event or entry.
* **activities** (text, required/optional): Describe any activities or notes about your pet's activities.
* **note** (note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts about your pet.
* **owner_name** (text, required/optional): Type your name.
* **owner_email** (email, required/optional): Type your email address.
* **owner_phone** (text, required/optional): Type your phone number.
* **assigned_tool** (text, required/optional): If applicable, type the name of the tool or equipment used for the pet's care.
* **assigned_user** (text, required/optional): If applicable, type the name of the user responsible for the pet's care.
