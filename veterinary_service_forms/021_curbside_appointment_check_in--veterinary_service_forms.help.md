# curbside_appointment_check_in - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to schedule and confirm curbside appointments with veterinarians. It is intended for pet owners to provide necessary information about their pet's visit, including the patient's name, appointment date and time, reason for visit, and other relevant details. This form is a crucial step in the appointment process, ensuring that the veterinarian is prepared to attend to the pet's needs upon arrival.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, making sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information about your pet and their appointment.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are completed correctly.
3. Once submitted, the form will be reviewed and confirmed by our team. You will be contacted to schedule a suitable appointment time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of your pet, as it appears on their identification or medical records.
* **Appointment Date**: Enter the date of your appointment in the format MM/DD/YY. This date will be used to schedule your appointment.
* **Phone**: If you have any urgent matters or concerns, provide your contact phone number so we can reach you for further questions or to confirm the appointment time.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can communicate with you regarding your appointment schedule and any other updates.
* **Notes**: If you have any additional comments or concerns regarding your pet's visit, use this field to provide them to our team.
* **Veterinarian**: Select one or more veterinarians from the list who will be attending to your pet during the visit.
* **Pet Type**: Select the type of pet that your pet is (e.g., Dog, Cat, Other).
* **Pet Name**: Enter your pet's name as it appears on their identification or medical records.
* **Reason for Visit**: Provide a brief description of the reason for your pet's visit, such as "Routine check-up," "Vaccinations," or "Treatment for illness."
* **Appointment Time**: Enter the time of your appointment in the format HH:MM. This time will be used to schedule your appointment.
* **Confirm**: Provide a brief confirmation statement indicating that you have reviewed the form and are prepared for your appointment.
