# Veterinarian Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Veterinarian Offboarding Form, which is used to collect essential information about a pet when they leave the clinic's care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Provide pet information**: Fill out the "pet_info" field with the pet's details, including their name, age, and weight.
2. **Select pet type**: Choose the correct type of pet from the "pet_type" field, which will help the new care provider understand the pet's specific needs.
3. **Enter medical history**: In the "medical_history" field, provide any relevant medical information about the pet, including any allergies or conditions.
4. **List medications**: If the pet takes any medications, list them in the "medication_list" field.
5. **Share contact information**: Provide the owner's name, email, and phone number in the "owner_name", "owner_email", and "owner_phone" fields, respectively.
6. **Add any additional comments or feedback**: Use the "feedback" fields to provide any additional information or comments about the pet's care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **pet_info**: Provide a brief description of the pet, including their name, age, and weight.
* **pet_type**: Select the correct type of pet (e.g., "Dog", "Cat", "Bird", etc.) from the dropdown menu.
* **pet_age**: Enter the pet's age in years.
* **pet_weight**: Enter the pet's weight in pounds or kilograms.
* **medical_history**: Provide any relevant medical information about the pet, including allergies or conditions.
* **medical_condition**: Select any relevant medical conditions from the dropdown menu.
* **medication_list**: List any medications the pet is currently taking.
* **owner_name**: Enter the owner's name.
* **owner_email**: Enter the owner's email address.
* **owner_phone**: Enter the owner's phone number.
* **owner_notes**: Use this field to add any additional comments or feedback about the pet's care.
* **feedback**: Provide any additional information or comments about the pet's care.
* **comments**: Use this field to add any additional comments or feedback about the pet's care.
* **feedback_comments**: Use this field to provide any additional information or comments about the pet's care.
* **signature**: This field is not typically used by the owner, but it's a placeholder for any additional information that might be required in the future.
