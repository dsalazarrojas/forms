# Pet Imaging Study Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Pet Imaging Study Form. The form is designed to collect relevant information about a patient's imaging study, including details about the patient, the scan itself, and the veterinary team involved.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all required information available before starting the form.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that you enter accurate information.
3. Enter your contact information in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient's basic information, including their name and any relevant details.
- **Imaging Request** (`imaging_request`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the type of scan required, such as CT or MRI scan.
- **Scan Location** (`scan_location`, `select_one`, optional): Select the location where the scan will be performed.
- **Radiologist Notes** (`radiologist_notes`, `text`, optional): Record any notes from the radiologist, if applicable.
- **Patient Weight** (`patient_weight`, `number`, optional): Enter the patient's weight, if relevant.
- **Patient Height** (`patient_height`, `number`, optional): Enter the patient's height, if relevant.
- **Scan Date** (`scan_date`, `date`, optional): Record the date of the scan.
- **Scan Time** (`scan_time`, `time`, optional): Record the time of the scan.
- **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, `select_one`, optional): Identify the type of pet being imaged (e.g., Dog, Cat, or Other).
- **Veterinary Team Name** (`veterinary_team_name`, `text`, optional): Record the name of the veterinary team involved.
- **Veterinary Team Contact** (`veterinary_team_contact`, `text`, optional): Record the contact information for the veterinary team.
- **Veterinary Team Phone** (`veterinary_team_phone`, `text`, optional): Record the phone number for the veterinary team.
- **Veterinary Team Email** (`veterinary_team_email`, `email`, optional): Record the email address for the veterinary team.

## Tips

- Be thorough and accurate when filling out this form to ensure that all necessary information is captured correctly.
- Double-check your entries for completeness and correctness before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about a field, refer to the provided options or contact the relevant team for guidance.
