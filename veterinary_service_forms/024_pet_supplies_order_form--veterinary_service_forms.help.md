# Pet Supplies Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from pet owners to facilitate ordering pet supplies from a veterinary clinic or pet care business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of pet you are ordering supplies for from the options listed under "Pet Type".
2. If applicable, select the specific breed of your pet from the options listed under "Pet Breed".
3. Choose the date you would like to have your order delivered on.
4. Enter your contact information, including your email address.
5. Provide any additional order notes or instructions in the "Order Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ This field allows you to select the type of pet you are ordering supplies for, such as cat, dog, or other.
* **Pet Breed** (`pet_breed`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ This field allows you to select the specific breed of your pet, such as Labrador, Poodle, or Siamese Cat.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional):
	+ Select the date you would like to have your order delivered on.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, email, optional):
	+ Enter your contact information, including your email address.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, optional):
	+ Provide any additional notes or instructions for your order, such as special requests or concerns.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all applicable fields accurately to ensure timely and correct order fulfillment.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the veterinary clinic or pet care business for guidance.
