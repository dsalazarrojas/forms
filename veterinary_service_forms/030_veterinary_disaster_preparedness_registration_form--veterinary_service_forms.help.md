# veterinary-disaster-preparedness-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from pet owners and veterinary clinics to ensure the proper care of animals during and after a disaster. It helps veterinary clinics to identify key contacts and animals, and provide necessary services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the general information section with your contact details.
2. Provide details about your animal, including its type, breed, age, weight, and sex.
3. Enter your contact information and emergency contact information if applicable.
4. Fill in the veterinary information section with details about your pet's veterinary clinic, including their name, address, phone number, and fax number.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm that you have read and understood the information provided.

* **General Info** (1): This section is for you to fill in your contact details, including your name, phone number, and email.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your local veterinary clinic for assistance.
* If you are filling in the form on behalf of someone else, please make sure to have their authority to do so.
