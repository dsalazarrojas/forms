# Pharmaceuticals Consent Form For Pets - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather consent from pet owners for veterinary treatment and to document the details of the treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the pet's information, including its species, age, and breed.
2. Enter the owner's contact information, including their name, address, and phone number.
3. Select any relevant medical history for the pet.
4. Select any treatment plans that will be used for the pet.
5. Enter any medications that the pet is currently taking.
6. Sign and date the form, indicating that you have reviewed and agreed to the treatment plan.
7. Sign and date the form as the veterinarian, indicating that you have reviewed and agreed to the treatment plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Pet Information (pet_info)
*   **pet_info** (`text`, required: false): Enter the pet's details, including species, age, and breed.

### Owner Information (owner_info)
*   **owner_info** (`text`, required: false): Enter your contact information, including name, address, and phone number.

### Medical History (medical_history)
*   **Medical history 1**, **Medical history 2**, **Medical history 3** (`select_one`, required: false): Select any relevant medical history for the pet.

### Treatment Plan (treatment_plan)
*   **Option 1**, **Option 2**, **Option 3** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select any treatment plans that will be used for the pet.

### Medication List (medication_list)
*   **medication_list** (`text`, required: false): Enter a list of medications that the pet is currently taking.

### Signature (signature)
*   **signature** (`note`, required: false): Sign and date your signature, indicating that you have reviewed and agreed to the treatment plan.

### Date (date)
*   **date** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date the form was signed.

### Time (time)
*   **time** (`time`, required: false): Enter the time the form was signed.

### Veterinarian Signature (veterinarian_signature)
*   **veterinarian_signature** (`note`, required: false): Sign and date your signature, indicating that you have reviewed and agreed to the treatment plan.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields.
*   Review and agree to the treatment plan before signing and dating the form.
*   Ensure that the pet receives proper care and treatment.
