# Grooming Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be filled out by pet owners or their representatives to agree to a grooming service for their pet.
## How To Complete This Form

This form requires no special preparation. You can access each field to enter your information as described below:

- Ensure to review each field carefully to provide accurate and relevant details.
- Enter your information in the corresponding fields and submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **pet_details**: This is a text field where you can describe your pet's details such as breed, age, and any medical conditions that need to be considered for the grooming process.
* **services**: Please select the services you wish to have performed for your pet from the list. These services include:
	+ Grooming
	+ Nail Trim
	+ Haircut
	+ Bath
	+ Other (please specify)
* **appointment_time**: Choose the appointment date and time for the grooming service. 
* **signed_consent**: A place for the pet owner to sign and date the consent form, acknowledging that they have read and understood this form and the agreed-upon services.
* **client_name**: Please enter your name as the person agreeing to these services.
* **owner_name**: Please enter the name of the pet's owner.
* **pet_name**: Enter the name of the pet receiving the grooming service.
* **contact_email**: Enter your contact email address in case we need to contact you regarding your pet's grooming appointment.
* **contact_phone**: Enter your phone number for any urgent communication regarding your pet's grooming needs.
* **appointment_date**: Choose the date for the grooming appointment.

**Tips**

- Please ensure all fields are completed accurately and honestly.
- In case of any changes to the services agreed upon or appointment details, contact us as soon as possible to avoid any inconvenience.
- Ensure your pet is available for the agreed-upon appointment date and time.
- This form is a contract for services agreed upon between the pet's owner and the service provider.
