# Dog Training Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Training Discharge Form is used to document the discharge of a dog from a training program. It helps to ensure that the client understands the current status of their dog's training and the next steps they need to take.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the purpose and the information required.
2. Answer all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the veterinarian or training staff member who instructed you to complete this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information (1)**: This section is used to collect basic information about the client who has brought their dog for training.
	+ *Client*: Please select the answer based on your understanding of the client's response to our staff.
* **Dog (3)**: This field is used to determine if the dog has completed the training program or not.
	+ *Yes* if the dog has completed the training program and is ready for discharge.
	+ *No* if the dog has not completed the training program and requires further training.
* **Date of Discharge (4)**: This field is used to record the date the dog is discharged from the training program.
	+ *Please enter the date in the format of MM/DD/YYYY*.
* **Field 5 (5)**: This field is used to capture additional information about the dog's training progress or any issues.
	+ *You can enter any relevant details, but please keep it concise*.
* **Note (6)**: This field is used to capture any further comments or notes from the veterinarian or training staff about the dog's discharge.
	+ *This will help us understand any specific requirements or conditions for the dog's discharge*.
* **Additional Comments (7)**: This field is used to capture any additional comments or feedback from you or the client about the dog's training experience.
	+ *Please keep it brief and concise*.
* **Email (8)**: This field is used to capture the email address of the client or the client's contact person.
	+ *Please enter the email address in the format [name@example.com](mailto:name@example.com)*.
* **Phone (9)**: This field is used to capture the phone number of the client or the client's contact person.
	+ *Please enter the phone number in the format 123-456-7890*.
* **Additional Contacts (10)**: This field is used to determine if there are any additional contacts involved in the dog's care.
	+ *Yes* if there are additional contacts involved in the dog's care.
	+ *No* otherwise.
