# Veterinary Hospital Treatment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Hospital Treatment Form is a crucial tool for veterinary staff to gather vital information about pet owners and their pets when they come to the hospital for treatment. This form helps ensure that the pet receives the best possible care and attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact information (Pet Owner Name, Pet Owner Phone) so that the veterinary staff can contact you if needed.
2. Enter the date of your visit to the hospital.
3. Enter the name of your pet and its weight.
4. Indicate if there are any complications with your pet's condition. Please select "True" if you have any concerns or issues, "False" if everything is normal, and "Undetermined" if you are unsure.
5. Provide the name of the user assigned to your pet's treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Owner Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Pet Owner Phone**: Enter your contact phone number so that we can reach you if needed.
* **Date**: Enter the date you are visiting the hospital for your pet's treatment.
* **Pet Name**: Enter the name of your pet.
* **Pet Weight**: Enter your pet's weight in pounds or kilograms.
* **Complications**: Check "True" if you have any concerns or issues with your pet's condition, "False" if everything is normal, and "Undetermined" if you are unsure.
* **Assigned User**: Enter the name of the user assigned to your pet's treatment. This will help us to keep track of who is providing care to your pet.
