# Veterinary Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Appointment Form is a tool used by veterinarians to prepare for appointments with clients and their pets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the client's name in the **Client Name** field.
2. Select the desired **Appointment Date** and **Appointment Time** for the appointment.
3. Choose the services required for the appointment from the list in the **Service Requested** field (Pet Grooming, Pet Vaccination, Veterinary Consultation, or multiple services).
4. Provide **Pet Information**, including any relevant details about the pet's health, vaccination history, or special care needs.
5. Enter the **Contact Number** where the client can be reached for any questions or reminders.
6. Enter the client's **Email** for any further correspondence.
7. Add any additional **Appointment Notes** if necessary.
8. Choose the veterinarian assigned to the appointment from the list in the **Veterinarian** field (Dr. Smith, Dr. Johnson, or Dr. Thompson).
9. Select the **Clinic Location** where the appointment will take place.
10. Set the **Appointment Duration** in minutes, considering the expected length of the appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client who is visiting the clinic.
  - **Appointment Date**: Select the desired date for the appointment. This can be any date in the future.
  - **Appointment Time**: Select the desired time of the appointment.
  - **Service Requested**: Choose the services needed from the list:
    - Pet Grooming: Services related to the pet's physical appearance.
    - Pet Vaccination: Services related to vaccination.
    - Veterinary Consultation: Services related to a consultation with a veterinarian.
  - **Pet Information**: Provide any relevant details about the pet's health, vaccination history, or special care needs.
  - **Contact Number**: Enter the client's phone number where they can be reached for any questions or reminders.
  - **Email**: Enter the client's email for any further correspondence.
  - **Appointment Notes**: Add any additional notes or instructions about the appointment.
  - **Veterinarian**: Choose the assigned veterinarian from the list:
    - Dr. Smith: A veterinarian with expertise in various fields of veterinary medicine.
    - Dr. Johnson: A veterinarian with expertise in specific fields of veterinary medicine.
    - Dr. Thompson: A veterinarian with expertise in general practice.
  - **Clinic Location**: Select the location where the appointment will take place.
  - **Appointment Duration**: Set the expected length of the appointment in minutes.
