# Veterinary Emergency Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Emergency Intake Form is a critical tool for veterinary professionals to gather essential information about a pet's emergency situation. This form helps identify the pet's condition, medical history, and treatment needs to provide the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the pet's basic information, including its name, contact info, and medical history.
2. Provide details about the emergency situation, including the pet's symptoms and any allergies or special conditions.
3. Choose the relevant medical condition and treatment options for the pet's current situation.
4. Select the emergency contact person and their phone number.
5. Verify the owner's signature and contact information.
6. Add any additional medical information or treatment plans.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **pet_info** (`pet_info`, text, optional): Enter the pet's name and any relevant identifying information.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter the owner's contact information, including their name and phone number.
* **Emergency Details** (`emergency_details`, text, optional): Describe the pet's emergency situation, including any symptoms or conditions.
* **Owner Info** (`owner_info`, text, optional): Enter the owner's contact information, including their name and phone number.
* **Contact Relationships** (`contact_relationships`, text, optional): Identify any relationships between the owner and other emergency contacts.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter the owner's contact information, including their name and phone number ( duplicate of previous field, please provide additional information).
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide any relevant medical history for the pet.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List the pet's medical conditions or allergies.
* **Treatment Plans** (`treatment_plans`, text, optional): Outline any treatment plans or medications for the pet.
* **Medications** (`medication_list`, text, optional): List any medications the pet is currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies or sensitivities the pet has.
* **Special Conditions** (`special_conditions`, text, optional): Provide any special conditions or concerns for the pet.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Provide a signature for the owner or emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_one, optional): Choose the emergency contact person from the provided options.
* **Owner Signature** (`owner_signature`, text, optional): Provide a signature from the owner or emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, number, optional): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Owner Phone** (`owner_phone`, number, optional): Enter the owner's phone number.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, select_one, optional): Choose the medical condition that best describes the pet's current situation.
* **Treatment** (`treatment`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the relevant treatment options for the pet's current situation.
* **Veterinary Clinics** (`veterinary_clinics`, select_one, optional): Select the preferred veterinary clinic for the pet's care.
* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of pet (e.g., cat, dog, other).
* **Veterinary Specialties** (`veterinary_specialties`, select_one, optional): Choose any relevant veterinary specialties for the pet's care.
* **Owner Info 2** (`owner_info_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional owner information.
* **Medical Condition 2** (`medical_condition_2`, select_one, optional): Choose any additional medical conditions for the pet.
* **Treatment 2** (`treatment_2`, select_multiple, optional): Choose any additional treatment options for the pet.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please ask a veterinarian or staff member for help.
* This form is confidential and will only be used for the pet's treatment and care.
