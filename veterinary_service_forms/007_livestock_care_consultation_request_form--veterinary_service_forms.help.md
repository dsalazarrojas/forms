# Livestock Care Consultation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for farmers or ranchers to request consultations or appointments for their livestock care needs. It helps farm administrative or management staff organize and prioritize consultations to ensure accurate and efficient allocation of resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information about your farm (Farm Information, Farm Location, and Contact Information)
2. Provide details about the animals you need consultations for (Animal Details and Animal Type)
3. Specify any specific consultation requirements (Animal Health Issue and Consultation Priority)
4. Schedule the consultation (Schedule Type, Schedule Date, and Schedule Time)
5. Add any additional notes or comments (Schedule Note)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Farm Information** (Text): Enter a brief description of your farm, including its name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Farm Location** (Text): Enter the address of your farm, including the city, state, and country.
* **Farm Type** (Select One): Check if you have a farm or ranch.
* **Farm Phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of your farm or main office.
* **Farm Email** (Email): Enter the email address of your farm or main office.
* **Animal Details** (Text): Briefly describe the animal(s) requiring consultation or attention.
* **Animal Type** (Select Multiple): Check the type(s) of animals you have (e.g., cattle, poultry, etc.).
* **Animal Name** (Text): Enter the name(s) of the animal(s) requiring consultation or attention.
* **Animal Age** (Select One): Check if the animal's age is relevant to the consultation.
* **Animal Health Issue** (Text): Describe any health issues or concerns related to the animal(s).
* **Consultation Priority** (Select One): Specify the priority level of the consultation (e.g., high, medium, low).
* **Schedule Type** (Select One): Check if you require an in-person or remote consultation.
* **Schedule Date** (Date): Choose a date for the consultation.
* **Schedule Time** (Time): Choose a specific time for the consultation.
* **Schedule Note** (Text): Add any additional notes or comments for the consultation.
* **Submit Button** (Text): Click to submit your request.
