# Pet Walking And Play Session Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Pet Walking And Play Session Guide" form is designed to collect information from pet owners about their pet's walking and play session preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Go to the "Pet Owner Details" page and enter your pet's owner information.
2. Move to the "Session Preferences" page and describe your pet's preferences for walking and play sessions.
3. On the "Consent" page, provide your consent for the walking and play sessions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Pet Owner Details (required: false)
* **Pet Owner Details** (text): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant information about yourself as the pet owner.

### Session Preferences (required: false)
* **Session Preferences** (text): Describe your pet's preferences for walking and play sessions, such as their favorite toys, activities, and any medical conditions that may affect their behavior.

### Consent (required: false)
* **Consent** (text): Provide your consent for the walking and play sessions, stating your understanding of the session's purpose and risks.
