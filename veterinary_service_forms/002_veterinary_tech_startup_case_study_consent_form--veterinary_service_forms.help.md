# Veterinary Tech Startup Case Study Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about veterinary technology startups, including their category and details of their services. It is intended for use by veterinarians, veterinary technicians, or other relevant parties to sign off on a service agreement or contract.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Form Title** field with a brief description of the veterinary technology startup.
2. Provide a detailed description of the startup in the **Form Description** field.
3. Select the category that best describes the startup from the options provided in the **Form Category** field.
4. If the category is not listed, you can specify it in the **Form Category Other** field.
5. Provide additional details about the category in the **Form Category Other Details** field.
6. Sign the form with your name in the **Form Signature** field.
7. Date and time the signature in the **Form Signature Date** and **Form Signature Time** fields.
8. Specify your notary status in the **Form Signature Notary** field.
9. Provide the date and title of the person signing the form in the **Form Signature Date Signed** and **Form Signature Date Signed By** fields.
10. Finally, enter your title in the **Form Signature Date Signed By Title** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Title**: A brief description of the veterinary technology startup. 
  (Text field)
- **Form Description**: A detailed description of the startup and its services. 
  (Text field)
- **Form Category**: The category that best describes the startup, such as "Veterinary Service Forms" or "Veterinarian Forms". 
  (Select multiple options)
- **Form Category Other**: If the category is not listed, select "Other" and specify the category. 
  (Text field)
- **Form Category Other Details**: Provide additional details about the category. 
  (Text field)
- **Form Signature**: Sign your name to confirm that you understand and agree with the terms of the startup's services. 
  (Text field)
- **Form Signature Date**: Date of the signature. 
  (Date field)
- **Form Signature Time**: Time of the signature. 
  (Time field)
- **Form Signature Location**: Location where the form was signed. 
  (Text field)
- **Form Signature Notary**: Specify your notary status, such as "Notary Public" or "Veterinarian". 
  (Select one option)
- **Form Signature Date Signed**: Date the form was signed. 
  (Date field)
- **Form Signature Date Signed By**: Date the form was signed by. 
  (Date field)
- **Form Signature Date Signed By Title**: Title of the person signing the form. 
  (Text field)

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Use clear and concise language when describing the startup and its services.
* Ensure that you have the authority to sign the form on behalf of the startup.
* Keep a record of the signed form for future reference.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, ask for clarification before proceeding.
