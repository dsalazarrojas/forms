# Euthanasia Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the details of an animal's euthanasia procedure. It helps the veterinary clinic staff to keep track of the animal's name, reason for euthanasia, and other important information to provide a professional and accurate record.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the animal's name in the **animal_name** field.
2. Enter the owner's name in the **owner_name** field.
3. Provide a brief description of the reason for euthanasia in the **reason_for_euthanasia** field.
4. Enter the date of the euthanasia procedure in the **euthanasia_date** field.
5. Enter the time of the euthanasia procedure in the **euthanasia_time** field.
6. Enter the location where the euthanasia procedure was performed in the **location** field.
7. Enter the name of the veterinary clinician who performed the procedure in the **veterinary_clinician** field.
8. Provide any additional notes about the procedure in the **notes** field (optional).
9. Enter the owner's contact number in the **owner_contact_number** field (optional).
10. Enter the owner's email address in the **owner_email** field (optional).
11. Select the signature of the clinician who performed the procedure from the **signature** dropdown menu.
12. Enter the date and time of the clinician's signature in the **signature_date** and **signature_time** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **animal_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the animal that underwent euthanasia.
* **owner_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the animal's owner.
* **reason_for_euthanasia** (text, required): Provide a brief description of the reason for euthanasia.
* **euthanasia_date** (date, required): Enter the date of the euthanasia procedure.
* **euthanasia_time** (time, required): Enter the time of the euthanasia procedure.
* **location** (text, required): Enter the location where the euthanasia procedure was performed.
* **veterinary_clinician** (text, required): Enter the name of the veterinary clinician who performed the procedure.
* **notes** (note, optional): Provide any additional notes about the procedure.
* **owner_contact_number** (text, optional): Enter the owner's contact number.
* **owner_email** (email, optional): Enter the owner's email address.
* **signature** (select_one, required): Select the signature of the clinician who performed the procedure.
* **signature_date** (date, required): Enter the date of the clinician's signature.
* **signature_time** (time, required): Enter the time of the clinician's signature.
