# ASR Adoption Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ASR Adoption Application form is used to collect information from potential adopters about their background, preferences, and capabilities to ensure a suitable match between the applicant and the pet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question honestly and accurately.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Select the correct options for each question.
4. Provide clear and concise answers to each question.
5. Ensure that you have answered all questions completely and accurately before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (2, text, \*): Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (3, date, \*): Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1990-02-25).
* **Phone Number** (4, text, \*): Enter your primary contact number that we can use to reach you.
* **Email Address** (5, email, \*): Enter your active email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Current Address** (6, text, \*): Enter your complete residential address.
* **Housing Status** (7, select_one, \*): Select your current living situation:
	+ Own home
	+ Rent house
	+ Rent apartment
	+ Live with family/friends
	+ Other
* **Landlord Permission** (8, select_one, \*): If you rent, select "True" if you have permission from your landlord to have pets, otherwise select "False".
* **Who lives in your Household** (9, text, \*): List the number of adults, children, and other pets living in your household.
* **Do you currently have pets** (10, select_one, \*): If you have pets, select "True", otherwise select "False".
* **If yes, list your current pets** (11, text, ): Provide details about your current pets, including species, age, and spay/neuter status.
* **Describe your previous experience with pets** (12, text, \*): Share your previous experiences with pets, including ownership, care responsibilities, etc.
* **Why do you want to adopt a pet from All Souls Retreat** (13, text, \*): Explain your motivation for adopting a pet from us.
* **What type of pet are you looking to adopt** (14, text, \*): Specify your preferred age, size, breed, and temperament preferences.
* **Describe your typical daily schedule** (15, text, \*): Outline your daily routine, including how much time you will spend with your new pet.
* **Emergency Plan** (16, text, \*): Describe your arrangements for pet care when you're unavailable.
* **Veterinary Care Plan** (17, select_one, \*): Select one of the following options:
	+ True
	+ I have a vet selected
	+ I will find a vet
	+ False
	+ but I will arrange care
* **Adoption Fee Agreement** (18, select_one, \*): Select one of the following options:
	+ True
	+ I understand and agree
	+ False
	+ I need more information

Note: This form must be completed in its entirety to be considered for adoption.
