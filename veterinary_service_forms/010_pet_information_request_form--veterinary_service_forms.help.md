This form is intended to collect information about a pet, including its name, breed, age, medical history, and other relevant details. The purpose of this form is to gather accurate and comprehensive data about the pet, which will be used to provide effective veterinary care and services. 

To prevent duplication, please note that this form should only be filled out once per pet.

## Pet Information Request Form - Help Guide

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Pet Information" page with a brief description of the pet.
2. Enter the pet's name and select its breed from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter the pet's age.
4. Add any medical information, if applicable.
5. Provide additional comments or notes about the pet.
6. Enter the owner's contact information.
7. Add medical history, medical conditions, and medical tests related to the pet.
8. List any current medical treatment or medications for the pet.
9. Enter any medical costs related to the pet's care.
10. Select the type of pet (dog, cat, or other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Pet Information** (`id: 1`, `name: pet_info`): Enter a brief description of the pet.

- **Pet Name** (`id: 2`, `name: pet_name`): Enter the pet's name.

- **Pet Breed** (`id: 3`, `name: pet_breed`): Select the pet's breed from the dropdown menu.

- **Owner Name** (`id: 4`, `name: owner_name`): Enter your name as the owner of the pet.

- **Contact Info** (`id: 5`, `name: contact_info`): Enter a contact number or email to reach you about the pet.

- **Age** (`id: 6`, `name: pet_age`): Enter the pet's age.

- **Medical Info** (`id: 7`, `name: medical_info`): Add any relevant medical information about the pet.

- **Additional Comments** (`id: 8`, `name: additional_comments`): Add any additional comments or notes about the pet.

- **Phone Number** (`id: 9`, `name: phone_number`): Enter your contact phone number.

- **Email** (`id: 10`, `name: email`): Enter your email address.

- **Veterinary Info** (`id: 11`, `name: veterinary_info`): Add any veterinary-related information about the pet.

- **Medical History** (`id: 12`, `name: medical_history`): Add a medical history for the pet.

- **Medication List** (`id: 13`, `name: medication_list`): List any current medications for the pet.

- **Medical Conditions** (`id: 14`, `name: medical_conditions`): Select any medical conditions affecting the pet.

- **Medical Tests** (`id: 15`, `name: medical_tests`): Enter dates of any relevant medical tests for the pet.

- **Medical Treatment** (`id: 16`, `name: medical_treatment`): Enter the time of medical treatment for the pet.

- **Medical Cost** (`id: 17`, `name: medical_cost`): Enter the medical cost related to the pet's care.

- **Pet Type** (`id: 18`, `name: pet_type`): Select the type of pet (dog, cat, or other).

- **Medical Conditions** (`id: 14`, `name: medical_conditions`): Select all conditions that apply.
