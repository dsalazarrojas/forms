# Adopter Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals or families interested in adopting animals. It will help us assess their suitability for adopting animals and ensure a smooth adoption process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Make sure to select the correct species and activity level preference that best suits your lifestyle.
- If you have current pets, please provide your current veterinarian's name and contact number for us to verify your pet care history.
- If you have previously rehomed pets, please explain the reason for rehoming in the "Reason for Rehoming Previous Pets if Any" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`Full Name`, `text`, required): Please enter your complete legal name.
* **Primary Phone** (`Primary Phone`, `text`, required): Enter the best number to reach you during business hours.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address. We will use this to communicate with you about the adoption process.
* **Household Income Range** (`Household Income Range`, `text`, not required): If applicable, please enter your household's income range to help us ensure the pet will receive proper care.
* **Species Interested In** (`Species Interested In`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of animal you wish to adopt. Options include Canine, Feline, Equine, or Other.
* **Activity Level Preference** (`Activity Level Preference`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate your preferred activity level for the adopted pet. Choose from High energy, Moderate energy, or Low energy -- Senior.
* **Outdoor Access** (`Outdoor Access`, `select_one`, required): Describe the type of outdoor space you have available for the pet. Choose from Secure yard, Balcony only, Public parks, or None.
* **Current Veterinarian Name** (`Current Veterinarian Name`, `text`, not required): If you have current pets, please provide your current veterinarian's name.
* **Vet Phone Number** (`Vet Phone Number`, `text`, not required): If you have current pets, please provide your veterinarian's phone number.
* **Permission to Contact Vet** (`Permission to Contact Vet`, `select_one`, not required): Grant us permission to contact your veterinarian to verify your pet care history.
* **Are Current Pets Vaccinated** (`Are Current Pets Vaccinated`, `select_one`, not required): Indicate if your current pets are vaccinated.
* **Reason for Rehoming Previous Pets if Any** (`Reason for Rehoming Previous Pets if Any`, `text`, not required): If you have previously rehomed pets, please explain the reason for rehoming in this field.

## Tips
- Please ensure accuracy and completeness when filling out the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [contact information].
