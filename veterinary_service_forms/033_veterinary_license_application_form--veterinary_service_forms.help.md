# Veterinary License Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a structured and organized manner for applicants to apply for a veterinary license.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out your personal details, including your first and last name, address, phone number, and email.
2. If you are applying for a license, select the type of license you are applying for (License, Registration, or Renewal).
3. If you are applying for a license, enter your graduation date from veterinary school.
4. If you are applying for a license, enter your veterinary school information.
5. If you are renewing a license, enter your license number, issue date, and expiration date.
6. Enter your practice details, including its name, address, phone number, and email.
7. If you are applying for a license, enter your veterinary license number, issue date, and expiration date.
8. Select your veterinary license type (Doctor, Technician, or Veterinarian).
9. Enter your years of experience.
10. Enter your practice license number, issue date, and expiration date.
11. Select the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Address**: Enter your physical address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **License Type**: Select the type of license you are applying for (License, Registration, or Renewal).
* **Veterinary School**: Enter your veterinary school information.
* **Graduation Date**: Enter your graduation date from veterinary school.
* **License Number**: Enter your veterinary license number.
* **Issue Date**: Enter the issue date of your veterinary license.
* **Expiration Date**: Enter the expiration date of your veterinary license.
* **Practice Name**: Enter the name of your practice.
* **Practice Address**: Enter the address of your practice.
* **Practice Phone**: Enter the phone number of your practice.
* **Practice Email**: Enter the email address of your practice.
* **Veterinarian**: Enter your name as a veterinarian.
* **Degree**: Enter your degree as a veterinarian.
* **Years of Experience**: Enter the number of years you have been in practice.
* **Veterinarian License Type**: Select your type of license as a veterinarian (Doctor, Technician, or Veterinarian).
* **Practice License Number**: Enter your practice license number.
* **Practice Issue Date**: Enter the issue date of your practice license.
* **Practice Expiration Date**: Enter the expiration date of your practice license.
* **Submit**: Select this button to submit your application.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to avoid errors.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask for assistance.
