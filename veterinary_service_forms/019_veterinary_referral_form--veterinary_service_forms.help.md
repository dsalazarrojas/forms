# Veterinary Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Referral Form is used by veterinarians to refer patients to specialist services when they require additional expertise or resources.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill in the patient's information in the first field.
*   In the second field, briefly describe the reason for the referral.
*   Select the specialist you would like to refer the patient to from the third field.
*   In the fourth field, enter the expected date and time of the referral.
*   If needed, add any additional notes or comments in the sixth field.
*   Finally, enter the patient's phone number in the last field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name, species, breed, age, and any other relevant information.
*   **Referral Reason**: Briefly describe the reason why the patient needs specialist services. This will help the specialist understand the patient's needs.
*   **Specialist**: Select the specialist you would like to refer the patient to. Please choose one of the options provided.
*   **Expected Date**: Enter the expected date for the referral. This is the date when the specialist can see the patient.
*   **Expected Time**: Enter the expected time for the referral. This is the time when the specialist can see the patient.
*   **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the patient or referral.
*   **Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number so that the specialist can contact them for further information.

## Tips
*   Please make sure to fill in all the required fields.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for help.
*   The specialist will contact you after reviewing the referral form to confirm the details.
