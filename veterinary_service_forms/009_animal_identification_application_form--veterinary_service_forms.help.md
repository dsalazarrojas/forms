# Animal Identification Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Animal Identification Application Form is a crucial document used to collect and verify information about an animal and its owner. It is essential for identifying animals accurately and ensuring the legitimacy of ownership. Completing this form correctly will help us process your request efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Owner Information**: Please fill in the required details about yourself, including your full name, mailing address, primary phone number, and email address.
2.  Provide accurate and up-to-date information about yourself to avoid any delays in processing your request.
3.  **Animal Identification**: Select the correct identification type (e.g., Microchip, Ear Tag, Tattoo, Radio Collar, or Leg Band) to match your animal's identification method.
4. **Identification ID Number**: Enter the full numeric or alphanumeric ID number associated with your animal's identification type.
5.  **Animal Details**: Choose the correct sex for your animal (Male, Female, Neutered Male, or Spayed Female) and select its primary breed.
6.  **Additional Details**: If applicable, provide the date of birth or approximate age of your animal.
7.  **Distinctive Markings**: Describe any distinctive markings on your animal (e.g., scars, spots, or color patterns).
8.  **Photo Agreement**: Select "True" to agree to provide a current photo of your animal when requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Full Name** (`owner_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, `text`, required): Provide your current mailing address to ensure we can contact you about your animal's identification.
* **Primary Phone Number** (`primary_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your primary phone number to facilitate communication with us.
* **Email Address** (`owner_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to receive updates and requests related to your animal's identification.
* **Identification Type** (`identification_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct identification method used for your animal (e.g., Microchip, Ear Tag, Tattoo, Radio Collar, or Leg Band).
* **Identification ID Number** (`id_number`, `text`, required): Enter the full numeric or alphanumeric ID number associated with your animal's identification type.
* **Animal Sex** (`animal_sex`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct sex for your animal (Male, Female, Neutered Male, or Spayed Female).
* **Primary Breed** (`primary_breed`, `text`, required): Enter the primary breed of your animal.
* **Date of Birth or Approximate Age** (`animal_age_dob`, `text`, required): If known, provide the date of birth or approximate age of your animal.
* **Distinctive Markings** (`distinctive_markings`, `text`, optional): Describe any distinctive markings on your animal (e.g., scars, spots, or color patterns).
* **Photo Agreement** (`photo_upload_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Select "True" to agree to provide a current photo of your animal when requested.
