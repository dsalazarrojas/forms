# Veterinary Innovation Funding Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Innovation Funding Application form is designed to collect information from applicants seeking funding for veterinary innovations, research, education, or community development projects. This form helps us understand your project's goals, budget, and other essential details to review and evaluate your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Applicant Details**, including your name and contact information.
2. Provide a **Project Summary** that briefly describes your project's goals and objectives.
3. Enter your **Budget** details, if applicable.
4. Upload **Supporting Documents**, if required for your project.
5. Select the **Funding Type** that best fits your project's category (Research, Education, or Community Development).
6. Choose the **Project Category** that best describes your project (Veterinary, Education, or Research).
7. Enter your **Email** and **Phone** details.
8. Set your desired **Deadline** for the project.
9. Choose your **Start Time**, if applicable.
10. Add any **Additional Comments** about your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Details**: Enter your name and contact information to help us identify and contact you.
* **Project Summary**: Provide a brief description of your project, including its goals and objectives.
* **Budget**: Enter your budget details, if applicable.
* **Supporting Documents**: Upload any supporting documents required for your application, such as certificates or references.
* **Funding Type**: Select the type of funding you are applying for: Veterinary Research, Veterinary Education, or Veterinary Community Development.
* **Project Category**: Choose the category that best describes your project: Veterinary, Education, or Research.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your application.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Deadline**: Set your desired deadline for the project completion.
* **Start Time**: Choose your start time, if applicable.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional information about your project that you feel is essential to share.
