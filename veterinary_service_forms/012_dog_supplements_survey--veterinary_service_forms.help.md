# Dog Supplements Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about dog owners' supplement use habits, preferences, and experiences to help veterinarians and pet care professionals make informed decisions about product development and recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your dog's breed from the list provided.
2. Choose your dog's age group.
3. (Optional) Select your dog's weight range.
4. Answer if you use dog supplements.
5. If you use dog supplements, select the type(s) you are using.
6. Choose how often you administer dog supplements.
7. Select how much you administer the supplements (if you use them).
8. Answer if you have experienced any side effects from using dog supplements.
9. Choose your level of satisfaction with the supplements.
10. Provide any additional comments you may have about your experience with dog supplements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dog Breed**: Select the breed of your dog from the list provided.
* **Age**: Choose your dog's age group from the options provided.
* **Weight**: (Optional) Select your dog's weight range.
* **Do you use dog supplements?**: Answer whether you use dog supplements.
* **What type of supplement?**: Select the type(s) of supplement you are using (e.g., Joint, Skin, Coat, Digestive, etc.).
* **How often do you use dog supplements?**: Choose how often you administer dog supplements (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely).
* **How much do you administer?**: Select how much you administer the supplements (if you use them).
* **Have you experienced any side effects?**: Answer whether you have experienced any side effects from using dog supplements.
* **How satisfied are you with the supplements?**: Choose your level of satisfaction with the supplements (e.g., Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Not satisfied, Not at all satisfied).
* **Additional comments**: Provide any additional comments you may have about your experience with dog supplements.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* You can skip questions that do not apply to you or your pet.
* If you are unsure about any question, please provide a response that best reflects your experience or situation.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey!
