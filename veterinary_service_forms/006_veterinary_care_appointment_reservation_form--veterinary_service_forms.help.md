# Veterinary Care Appointment Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Care Appointment Reservation Form is used to schedule a veterinary care appointment for your pet. To complete this form, please provide the required information, and we will process your request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with the necessary information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Select a suitable appointment time from the available options.
4. Double-check your submission before confirming.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Owner Info** (`owner_info`, `text`, optional):
	+ This field is used to provide additional information about the owner.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the pet owner.
* **Owner Phone** (`owner_phone`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter the owner's contact phone number.
* **Owner Email** (`owner_email`, `email`, optional):
	+ Enter the owner's email address.
* **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the pet.
* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if the pet is a service animal or 'No' if it is not.
* **Pet Age** (`pet_age`, `number`, optional):
	+ Enter the age of the pet.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Select a suitable appointment time from the available options (09:00, 06:00, 06:30, 07:00, 07:30, 08:00, 08:30, 09:00, 09:30, 10:00).
