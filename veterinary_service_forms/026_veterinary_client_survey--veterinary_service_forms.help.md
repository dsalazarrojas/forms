# veterinary_client_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The veterinary_client_survey form is used to gather information from clients, including contact information, practice services and equipment, and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Client Info** field with any relevant information about the client.
2. Answer the **Practice Info** field with any relevant information about the practice.
3. Select a **Survey Date** to record the date of the survey.
4. Choose the services you provided to the client using the **Practice Services** field.
5. Choose the equipment used during the visit using the **Practice Equipment** field.
6. Provide any additional **Client Feedback** in the note field.
7. Fill out the **Contact Info** field with the client's contact information.
8. Enter the client's **Email** address if applicable.
9. Select the **Practice Hours** of your visit with the client.
10. Provide any additional information about the **Vet Clinic** using the note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Info** (client_survey): Enter any relevant information about the client.
- **Practice Info** (practice_info): Enter any relevant information about the practice.
- **Survey Date** (survey_date): Choose a date for the survey.
- **Practice Services** (practice_services): Select services you provided to the client (Yes or No).
- **Practice Equipment** (practice_equipment): Select equipment you used during the visit (Yes or No).
- **Client Feedback** (client_feedback): Provide any additional feedback from the client.
- **Contact Info** (contact_info): Enter the client's contact information.
- **Email** (email): Enter the client's email address if applicable.
- **Practice Hours** (practice_hours): Select the practice hours of your visit with the client.
- **Vet Clinic** (vet_clinic): Enter any additional information about the practice.
