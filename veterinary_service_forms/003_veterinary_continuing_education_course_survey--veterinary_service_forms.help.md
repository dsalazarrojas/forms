# Veterinary Continuing Education Course Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Continuing Education Course Survey is designed to collect information about veterinary continuing education courses. This form helps us gather data about these courses for internal purposes such as course management, evaluation, or reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Survey Title** (1): This is the title of the course.
2. Enter the **Survey Name** (2): This is the name of the survey associated with the course.
3. Enter the **Course Name** (3): This is the name of the course being surveyed.
4. Enter a brief **Course Description** (4): This is a short description of the course.
5. Select the **Course Date** (5) when the course was held or planned to be held.
6. Enter the **Course Time** (6): This is the duration of the course in hours or minutes.
7. Enter the **Course Location** (7): This is the location where the course was held or will be held.
8. (Optional) Enter the **Provider** (8) if the course was provided by a specific organization or person.
9. Select the **Level** (9) of the course (e.g., introductory, advanced, expert).
10. Select the **Survey Language** (10) in which the course was conducted.
11. Select the **Target Audience** (11) for the course (yes or no).
12. (Optional) If the survey language is not listed, enter the **Survey Language Other** (12) and provide a brief description.
13. (Optional) If the survey language is not listed, enter the **Survey Language Other** (13) for a brief description.
14. (Optional) Select the **Survey Format** (14) in which the course was conducted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Title (1)**: Enter the title of the course. This title will be used to identify the course in the database.
* **Survey Name (2)**: Enter the name of the survey associated with the course. This is a required field and should match the name of the survey exactly.
* **Course Name (3)**: Enter the name of the course being surveyed. This is a required field and should match the name of the course exactly.
* **Course Description (4)**: Enter a brief description of the course. This should provide a clear understanding of what the course covers.
* **Course Date (5)**: Select the date when the course was held or planned to be held. Ensure the date is formatted correctly (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Course Time (6)**: Enter the duration of the course in hours or minutes. For example, 3 hours or 1.5 hours.
* **Course Location (7)**: Enter the location where the course was held or will be held. This could be a city, state, or country.
* **Provider (8)**: If the course was provided by a specific organization or person, enter their name. This field is optional.
* **Level (9)**: Select the level of the course from the options provided (e.g., introductory, advanced, expert). This field is required.
* **Survey Language (10)**: Select the language in which the course was conducted from the options provided.
* **Target Audience (11)**: Select whether the course is targeted at veterinarians, veterinary students, or others.
* **Survey Language Other (12)**: If the survey language is not listed, enter a brief description of the survey language used.
* **Survey Language Other (13)**: This field is a repeat of the previous field and provides an opportunity to add more details if needed.
* **Survey Format (14)**: If the survey format is not listed, select the survey format used (e.g., online, offline).
