# Canine Cushing's Quality Of Life Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us understand the quality of life of dogs with Cushing's Disease. Please answer the questions based on your observations over the past month.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer based on your observations.
2. For select-one questions, choose the best answer that reflects your dog's condition.
3. For select-all questions, select all options that apply to your dog's condition.
4. For text fields, provide a brief description of your dog's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Owner Name (Required)**: Your name as the dog's owner.
* **Email Address (Required)**: Your email address for follow-up contact.
* **Pet Name (Required)**: Your dog's name.
* **Date of Cushing's Diagnosis (Optional)**: The date when your dog was diagnosed with Cushing's Disease.
* **Current Treatment Status (Required)**: Select one option that reflects your dog's current treatment status.
* **Excessive Thirst (Polydipsia) (Required)**: Rate the frequency of excessive thirst in your dog (Not observed, Mild, Moderate, Severe).
* **Excessive Urination (Polyuria) (Required)**: Rate the frequency of excessive urination in your dog (Not observed, Mild, Moderate, Severe).
* **Changes in Appetite (Required)**: Select one option that reflects the changes in your dog's appetite (Decreased, Normal, Increased, Variable).
* **Hair Loss or Thinning (Required)**: Rate the severity of hair loss or thinning in your dog (Not observed, Mild, Moderate, Severe).
* **Skin Conditions (Optional)**: Rate the severity of skin conditions in your dog (Not observed, Mild, Moderate, Severe).
* **Abdominal Appearance (Required)**: Describe the appearance of your dog's belly.
* **Overall Energy Level (Required)**: Rate your dog's energy level (Very Low, Low, Normal, High, Very High).
* **Exercise Tolerance (Required)**: Rate your dog's exercise tolerance (Very Limited, Limited, Moderate, Good, Excellent).
* **Behavioral Changes (Optional)**: Select all options that apply to your dog's behavioral changes (Restlessness or pacing, Anxiety or nervousness, Aggression or irritability, Depression or withdrawal, Improved temperament, No significant changes).
* **Sleep Patterns (Required)**: Select one option that reflects your dog's sleep patterns.
* **Current Medication(s) (Optional)**: List the current medication(s) your dog is taking.
* **Medication Tolerance (Required)**: Rate your dog's tolerance to medication (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Unknown).
* **Overall Response to Treatment (Required)**: Rate your dog's overall response to treatment (Excellent, Good, Moderate, Minimal, No improvement).
* **Overall Quality of Life (Required)**: Rate your dog's quality of life (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Critical).
* **Additional Concerns or Symptoms (Optional)**: Describe any additional concerns or symptoms you've observed in your dog.

Please note that for some questions, you may select multiple options. The goal is to provide a comprehensive picture of your dog's quality of life with Cushing's Disease. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
