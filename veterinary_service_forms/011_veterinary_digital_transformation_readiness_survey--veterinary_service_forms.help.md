# Veterinary Digital Transformation Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Digital Transformation Readiness Survey is designed to assess your clinic's preparedness and readiness for digital transformation. This survey aims to help you identify areas that need improvement and provide insights to inform your digital transformation strategy. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the role that best describes your position in the clinic.
2. Rate your current level of digital maturity.
3. Indicate your leadership team's level of support for digital transformation.
4. Describe your current technology infrastructure (select all that apply).
5. Identify who in your clinic has the digital skills to support digital transformation.
6. Confirm whether training programs are available to support digital transformation.
7. Determine your clinic's budget for digital transformation.
8. Select the strategy that best describes your approach to digital transformation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What's your role in the clinic?**: This question helps us understand who will be completing the survey and what their perspective is on the digital transformation process. Please select the role that best describes your position in the clinic.
	+ Clinic Administrator
	+ Veterinarian
	+ DVM
	+ DVM Assistant
	+ Other (please specify)
* **How would you rate your current level of digital maturity?**: Please rate your clinic's digital maturity on a scale that best describes your current state. This will help us understand where you are starting from and what areas need improvement.
* **Does your leadership team support digital transformation?**: This question helps us gauge the level of support for digital transformation within your leadership team. Select the option that best describes their level of support.
	+ Strongly support
	+ Somewhat support
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat do not support
	+ Strongly do not support
* **What is the current state of your technology infrastructure?**: Please select all that apply to describe your current technology infrastructure. This will help us understand what you're currently using and where you might need to improve.
	+ Cloud-based systems
	+ Legacy systems
	+ Hybrid model
	+ No systems
* **Who in your clinic has the digital skills to support digital transformation?**: This question helps us identify who in your clinic has the necessary skills to support digital transformation. Please select all that apply.
	+ Veterinarian
	+ DVM
	+ DVM Assistant
	+ IT Department
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Are there training programs available to support digital transformation?**: This question helps us understand whether training is available to support digital transformation.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **How much does your clinic budget for digital transformation?**: Please select the budget range that best describes your clinic's budget for digital transformation.
	+ $0-$1,000
	+ $1,000-$5,000
	+ $5,000-$10,000
	+ $10,000-$50,000
	+ $50,000+
* **Which strategy best describes your approach to digital transformation?**: This question helps us understand your approach to digital transformation. Please select the strategy that best describes your approach.
	+ Top-down approach
	+ Bottom-up approach
	+ No strategy
* **Additional tips**: Please provide any additional information or comments about your clinic's digital transformation strategy. 

Note: This is not a mandatory field, but providing additional information will help us better understand your clinic's unique needs and challenges.
