# puppy_aptitude_test_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by veterinarians to assess the aptitude of a puppy for adoption. It gathers information about the puppy's characteristics and history to help determine its suitability for adoption.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field as accurately as possible with the corresponding information about the puppy.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out. If a field is not applicable, select "None" or leave it blank.
3. If a field has options, select the most accurate option from the list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Puppy Name** (`puppy_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the puppy.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the puppy's owner or breeder.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the date of the puppy's birth.
* **Sex** (`sex`, select_one, optional): Select the sex of the puppy (Male, Female, or Unknown).
* **Breed** (`breed`, text, optional): Enter the breed of the puppy.
* **Size** (`size`, select_one, optional): Select the size of the puppy (Small, Medium, Large, or Extra Large).
* **Coat Length** (`coat_length`, number, optional): Enter the length of the puppy's coat.
* **Coat Type** (`coat_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type(s) of coat the puppy has (Short, Medium, Long).
* **Color** (`color`, text, optional): Enter the color(s) of the puppy.
* **Behavior Type** (`behavior_type`, text, optional): Enter the behavior type of the puppy.
* **Training Status** (`training_status`, select_one, optional): Select the training status of the puppy (None, Basic Obedience, Intermediate Training, Advanced Training).
* **Socialization Status** (`socialization_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the socialization status of the puppy (None, Fully Socialized).
* **Health Issues** (`health_issues`, select_multiple, optional): Select any health issues the puppy may have (None, Allergies, Hip Dysplasia, Other).
* **Medical Issues** (`medical_issues`, select_multiple, optional): Select any medical issues the puppy may have (None, Allergies, Other).
* **Temperament** (`temperament`, text, optional): Enter the temperament of the puppy.
* **Energy Level** (`energy_level`, select_one, optional): Select the energy level of the puppy (None, Low, Medium, High).
* **Activity Level** (`activity_level`, select_one, optional): Select the activity level of the puppy (None, Low, Medium, High).
* **Training Method** (`training_method`, text, optional): Enter the training method of the puppy.
* **Training Frequency** (`training_frequency`, number, optional): Enter the frequency of the puppy's training.
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the vaccination status of the puppy (Up to date, Not up to date).
