# Animal Product Innovation Testing Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from participants in animal product innovation testing studies. It gathers information about the participant's identity, study details, and their understanding of the study procedures, risks, and benefits.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your date of birth in the format MM-DD-YYYY.
3. If you are affiliated with an organization, provide their name.
4. Read and understand the study title, description, and purpose.
5. Select the options that accurately reflect your understanding of the study procedures, risks, and benefits.
6. Choose how you would like your data to be used.
7. Confirm that animal welfare standards will be maintained.
8. Provide information about your emergency contact.
9. Enter your electronic signature and the date of consent.
10. Optionally, provide a witness name if a witness is present.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM-DD-YYYY.
* **Organization Affiliation**: If you are affiliated with an organization, enter their name (optional).
* **Study Title**: This is a brief description of the study. Read and understand the study details.
* **Study Description**: This is a detailed description of the study. Read and understand the study details.
* **Purpose of Study**: Select the option that best describes your understanding of the study's purpose.
* **Study Procedures**: Select the option that accurately reflects your understanding of the study procedures.
* **Potential Risks and Benefits**: Select the option that accurately reflects your understanding of the risks and benefits involved.
* **Confidentiality Agreement**: Select the option that indicates your agreement to keep your data confidential.
* **Voluntary Participation Statement**: Select the option that indicates your understanding of your right to withdraw from the study at any time without penalty.
* **Right to Withdraw**: Select the option that indicates your understanding of your right to withdraw from the study at any time without penalty.
* **Consent for Data Usage**: Select the options that accurately reflect how you would like your data to be used.
* **Animal Welfare Confirmation**: Select the option that indicates your understanding that all animal welfare standards will be maintained.
* **Compensation Information**: If applicable, provide details about participant compensation.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Principal Investigator Contact Information**: Provide contact details for study questions (optional).
* **Electronic Consent Signature**: Type your full name as an electronic signature.
* **Date of Consent**: Enter the date of consent.
* **Witness Name (if applicable)**: If a witness is present, enter their name.
* **I Voluntarily Consent to Participate**: Select the option that indicates your consent to participate in the study.
