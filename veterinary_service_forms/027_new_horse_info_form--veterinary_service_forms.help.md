# New Horse Info Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and store information about a new horse in the veterinary clinic's database. It is essential to provide accurate and up-to-date information about the horse to ensure proper care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the "Horse Breed" field and enter the breed of the horse.
2. Enter the horse's "Age" in the "Horse Age" field.
3. Select the "Horse Color" field and enter the horse's color.
4. Enter the horse's "Weight" in the "Horse Weight" field.
5. Provide the "Owner Name" and "Contact Phone" for the horse's owner.
6. If necessary, add "Medical Notes" for the horse's health history.
7. Enter any relevant "Breeders Info" for the horse's lineage.
8. Finally, enter the horse's "Microchip Number" if available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Horse Breed** (`horse_breed`, text, required: false): Enter the horse's breed, such as Quarter Horse or Arabian.
* **Horse Age** (`horse_age`, number, required: false): Enter the horse's age in years.
* **Horse Color** (`horse_color`, text, required: false): Enter the horse's color, such as black or chestnut.
* **Horse Weight** (`horse_weight`, number, required: false): Enter the horse's weight in pounds.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the horse's owner.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the contact phone number of the horse's owner.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, note, required: false): Add any relevant medical notes for the horse's health history.
* **Breeders Info** (`breeders_info`, text, required: false): Enter any relevant information about the horse's breeders.
* **Microchip Number** (`horse_microchip`, text, required: false): Enter the horse's microchip number if available.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check the entered information for accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your veterinarian for guidance.
* This form is not intended for emergency situations, please contact your veterinarian if you have any urgent concerns.
