# estate_planning_client_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Estate Planning Client Questionnaire is a comprehensive form designed to gather essential information about your estate planning needs, financial situation, and goals. This information will help your financial advisor or planner understand your requirements and provide tailored guidance and recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question thoroughly and accurately. You can navigate between pages using the 'Previous' and 'Next' buttons. Each section has a brief explanation of what is expected from you. If you have any questions or concerns, please consult your financial advisor or planner for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Estate Planning Client Details**:
	+ This is the first section of the form where you'll provide general information about yourself. Please enter your details accurately and completely.
* **Primary Residence**:
	+ This field is used to record your primary residence. Please enter the address of your primary residence.
* **Spouse or Partner**:
	+ In this field, select one of the options that best describes your relationship with your spouse or partner. Please choose one of the following:
		+ Option 1: Married
		+ Option 2: Divorced
		+ Option 3: Single
* **Children**:
	+ Select all that apply from the given options to indicate the number of children you have.
		+ Option 1: 1-2 children
		+ Option 2: 3-4 children
		+ Option 3: 5 or more children
* **Estate Details**:
	+ This field is used to record any additional information about your estate. Please enter any relevant details.
* **Assets**:
	+ Please enter the value of your assets. This includes cash, investments, and other property.
* **Liabilities**:
	+ Record the value of your liabilities, such as loans, debts, and other financial obligations.
* **Assets Overview**:
	+ This field is used to provide an overview of your assets. Please enter any relevant information.
* **Risk Tolerability**:
	+ This is a note field where you can provide any additional information about your risk tolerability.
* **Financial History**:
	+ Select all that apply from the given options to indicate your financial history. Please choose one or more of the following:
		+ Option 1: Stable
		+ Option 2: Growth-oriented
		+ Option 3: Conservative
* **Estate Planning Goals**:
	+ In this field, select one of the options that best describes your estate planning goals. Please choose one of the following:
		+ Option 1: Wealth accumulation
		+ Option 2: Retirement planning
		+ Option 3: Estate tax management
* **Financial Planning Recommendations**:
	+ This field is used to record any financial planning recommendations or advice provided by your financial advisor or planner.
* **Estate Distribution**:
	+ Select all that apply from the given options to indicate how you wish to distribute your estate. Please choose one or more of the following:
		+ Option 1: Equal division among beneficiaries
		+ Option 2: Unequal division among beneficiaries
		+ Option 3: To a specific beneficiary
* **Tax Status**:
	+ In this field, select one of the options that best describes your current tax status. Please choose one of the following:
		+ Option 1: Single
		+ Option 2: Married filing jointly
		+ Option 3: Head of household

## Tips

* Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure you receive proper guidance from your financial advisor or planner.
* Consult your financial advisor or planner if you have any questions or concerns about any of the fields.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure you have provided all necessary information.
