# Beauty Service Client Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for potential clients to apply for beauty services at our salon. It collects essential information about you, including your personal details, health conditions, and consent for treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Full Name** field with your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
2. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the format `dd/mm/yyyy` (e.g., 12/02/1990).
3. Provide your **Phone Number** in the format `XXX-XXX-XXX-XXX` or `+X XXX XXX XX`.
4. Enter your **Email Address** in the format `example@example.com`.
5. Answer **Yes/No** to whether you have any **Medical Conditions** we should know about.
6. If you have any **Allergies**, provide a list of them.
7. Check the box to confirm you are **Pregnant** if applicable.
8. Select your **Skin Type** from the options provided.
9. Check the boxes to indicate your consent to receive **Treatment**, **Cancellation/Rescheduling Terms**, and **Promotional Emails and Offers**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Required, Text): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Date of Birth** (Required, Date): Enter your date of birth in the format `dd/mm/yyyy`.
* **Phone Number** (Required, Text): Enter your phone number in the format `XXX-XXX-XXX-XXX` or `+X XXX XXX XX`.
* **Email Address** (Required, Email): Enter your email address in the format `example@example.com`.
* **Medical Conditions** (Optional, Text): Answer **Yes/No** to whether you have any medical conditions we should know about.
* **Allergies** (Optional, Text): If you have any allergies, provide a list of them.
* **Pregnant** (Optional, Select One): Check the box to confirm if you are pregnant.
* **Skin Type** (Required, Select One): Select your skin type from the options provided.
* **Treatment** (Required, Select One): Check the box to indicate your consent to receive beauty treatments.
* **Cancellation/Rescheduling Terms** (Required, Select One): Check the box to indicate your understanding of the salon's cancellation and rescheduling terms.
* **Promotional Emails and Offers** (Optional, Select One): Check the box to indicate whether you wish to receive promotional emails and offers.
* **Date of Application** (Required, Date): Enter the date you are submitting this application.
* **Application Date** (Required, Date): This field is automatically populated with the current date.
