# Patch Test Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Patch Test Form is used to collect information about a patient's patch test results, including test dates, times, locations, and results. This form is typically used in medical settings to record test results for patch testing, which is a medical process used to diagnose allergic reactions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "email" field. This will be used for communication purposes.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Enter your date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field.
5. Record the date of the patch test in the "patch_test_date" field.
6. Enter the start time of the patch test in the "patch_test_start_time" field.
7. Enter the end time of the patch test in the "patch_test_end_time" field.
8. Enter the name of the doctor who conducted the patch test in the "patch_test_doctor" field.
9. Enter the location where the patch test was conducted in the "patch_test_location" field.
10. Record any medical history in the "medical_history" field.
11. List any allergies in the "allergy" field.
12. List any medications in the "medication" field.
13. Record who conducted the patch test in the "patch_test_conducted_by" field.
14. Record the title of the person who conducted the patch test in the "patch_test_conducted_by_title" field.
15. Sign your name as the patient in the "patient_signature" field.
16. Sign your name as the doctor in the "doctor_signature" field.
17. Record the date the doctor signed the form in the "doctor_signature_date" field.
18. Record the time the doctor signed the form in the "doctor_signature_time" field.
19. Record the results of the patch test in the "patch_test_result" field.
20. Record the date the patch test results were recorded in the "patch_test_result_date" field.
21. Record the time the patch test results were recorded in the "patch_test_result_time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your full first name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Provide your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, number, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Date of Birth (Participant)** (`birthdate_participant`, date, optional): Enter the date of birth of the test participant.
* **Date of Birth (Doctor)** (`birthdate_doctor`, date, optional): Enter the date of birth of the doctor who conducted the test.
* **Date of Birth (Nurse)** (`birthdate_nurse`, date, optional): Enter the date of birth of the nurse who conducted the test.
* **Patch Test Date** (`patch_test_date`, date, optional): Record the date of the patch test.
* **Patch Test Start Time** (`patch_test_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the patch test.
* **Patch Test End Time** (`patch_test_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the patch test.
* **Patch Test Doctor** (`patch_test_doctor`, text, optional): Enter the name of the doctor who conducted the patch test.
* **Patch Test Location** (`patch_test_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the patch test was conducted.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, optional): Record any relevant medical history.
* **Allergy** (`allergy`, text, optional): List any allergies you have.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): List any medications you are taking.
* **Patch Test Conducted By** (`patch_test_conducted_by`, text, optional): Record who conducted the patch test.
* **Patch Test Conducted By Title** (`patch_test_conducted_by_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the person who conducted the patch test.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, optional): Sign your name as the patient.
* **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`, text, optional): Sign your name as the doctor.
* **Doctor Signature Date** (`doctor_signature_date`, date, optional): Record the date the doctor signed the form.
* **Doctor Signature Time** (`doctor_signature_time`, time, optional): Record the time the doctor signed the form.
* **Patch Test Result** (`patch_test_result`, text, optional): Record the results of the patch test.
* **Patch Test Result Date** (`patch_test_result_date`, date, optional): Record the date the patch test results were recorded.
* **Patch Test Result Time** (`patch_test_result_time`, time, optional): Record the time the patch test results were recorded.
