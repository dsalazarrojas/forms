# Barber Shop Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps us provide you with a personalized experience by understanding your preferences, hair type, and any allergies or concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2. For each section, fill in the required information. If a question is not applicable, leave it blank.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name to help us get to know you better.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required): For appointment reminders and promotions, we'll use this email address. Please enter a valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, required): Leave your phone number so we can contact you for appointments or updates.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`referral_source`, select_one, required): Choose how you found our shop. This helps us understand our marketing efforts.
* **Your Hair Type** (`hair_type`, select_one, required): Select your hair type from the options provided.
* **Scalp Condition / Concerns** (`scalp_condition`, text, optional): Describe any scalp concerns or conditions you may have.
* **Known Allergies to Hair Products or Chemicals?** (`chemical_allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies or sensitivities to hair products or chemicals.
* **Are you taking any medications that affect skin or hair?** (`medication_notes`, text, optional): Inform us of any medications that may impact your service.
* **When was your last professional haircut?** (`last_haircut_date`, select_one, required): Choose when you last had a haircut.
* **Describe your desired look** (`desired_look`, text, required): Tell us about your style goals.
* **Services you are interested in today** (`services_interested`, select_multiple, required): Select the services you'd like to receive today.
* **How often do you typically get a haircut?** (`frequency_of_visit`, select_one, required): Choose your usual haircut frequency.
* **Cancellation and Late Policy Agreement** (`terms_acceptance`, select_one, required): Please agree to our cancellation and late policy.
* **Date of Intake** (`signature_date`, date, required): Select the date of this intake form submission.
