# Fashion Brand Customer Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fashion Brand Customer Information Form is designed to collect essential information from customers, helping us understand their style preferences and tailor our marketing efforts to their tastes. This form is mandatory for all customers and should be completed accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your **Customer Name** in the first field. This is the name you'd like to be known by.
2. Enter your **Email Address**, following the format [username@example.com](mailto:example@example.com). This will help us keep you updated on our latest news and offers.
3. Type in your **Phone Number**, making sure it is a 10-digit number.
4. Select your **Style Preferences** from the dropdown menu. Choose from Casual, Formal, Active, or Sporty to indicate your personal style.
5. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the format YYYY/MM/DD, as this will help us better understand your demographic preferences.
6. For **Style Preferred Color**, choose one or more colors that appeal to you from the options provided.
7. Select your preferred **Style Preferred Size** from the dropdown menu, choosing from Small, Medium, Large, or XL.
8. Choose your preferred **Style Preferred Fit** from the options: Loose, Comfortable, or Fitted.
9. Select your **Marketing Opt-in** choice, indicating whether you'd like to receive marketing materials or not.
10. For **Style Preferred Brand**, choose the brand that best represents your style preferences.
11. Finally, add any **Additional Comments** you'd like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`Customer Name`, text, required): Enter your name here.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required): Enter a 10-digit phone number.
* **Style Preferences** (`Style Preferences`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred style: Casual, Formal, Active, or Sporty.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in YYYY/MM/DD format.
* **Style Preferred Color** (`Style Preferred Color`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more colors that appeal to you from Red, Blue, Green, or Yellow.
* **Style Preferred Size** (`Style Preferred Size`, select_one, required): Select your preferred size: Small, Medium, Large, or XL.
* **Style Preferred Fit** (`Style Preferred Fit`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred fit: Loose, Comfortable, or Fitted.
* **Marketing Opt-in** (`Marketing Opt-in`, select_multiple, required): Choose whether you'd like to receive marketing materials or not.
* **Style Preferred Brand** (`Style Preferred Brand`, select_one, required): Choose the brand that best represents your style preferences.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments you'd like to share with us.

Tips:
* Please ensure the email address and phone number formats are correct.
* Select only one size and color for each preference field.
* Choose only one brand for your style preferences.
* Enter additional comments if you have any specific feedback or requests.
* Review and confirm your submission before submitting the form.
