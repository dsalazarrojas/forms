# Beauty Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential customers about their beauty concerns, preferences, and expectations from a beauty consultation. It helps our consultant to tailor the consultation to your individual needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, such as your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Enter your preferred consultation date.
3. Select the type of consultation you are interested in (Makeup, Hair Care, Skincare, etc.).
4. Describe your main beauty concerns.
5. Choose your skin type (Dry, Normal, Oily, Combination, Sensitive).
6. Choose your hair type (Straight, Wavy, Curly, Coily, Not Applicable).
7. Enter your beauty goals (e.g., what you want to achieve with the consultation).
8. Mention any lifestyle and maintenance commitment you are willing to invest.
9. Select your budget range (if applicable).
10. Specify any product preferences (natural, organic, etc.).
11. Mention any dietary restrictions or lifestyle limitations.
12. Describe your typical work and daily lifestyle.
13. Describe your climate and living environment.
14. Enter any additional information or previous professional services you have received.
15. Specify any specific requests or preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Please enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Please enter a valid email address where you can be reached for follow-up communications.
* **Phone Number**: Please enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Preferred Consultation Date**: Choose a date and time for your consultation, taking into account our availability.
* **Consultation Type**: Select the type of consultation you are interested in (e.g., Makeup, Hair Care, Skincare, etc.).
* **Main Beauty Concerns**: Describe your primary beauty concerns (e.g., acne, uneven skin tone, etc.).
* **Skin Type**: Choose your skin type from the options provided (Dry, Normal, Oily, Combination, Sensitive).
* **Hair Type**: Choose your hair type from the options provided (Straight, Wavy, Curly, Coily, Not Applicable).
* **Beauty Goals**: Describe what you want to achieve with the consultation (e.g., glowing skin, healthy hair, etc.).
* **Lifestyle and Maintenance Commitment**: Mention how much time and effort you are willing to invest in maintaining your beauty routine.
* **Budget Range**: Select your budget range (Budget-friendly, Moderate, Premium, No Budget Limit) if you have one.
* **Product Preferences**: Specify any product preferences (natural, organic, etc.).
* **Dietary or Lifestyle Restrictions**: Mention any dietary restrictions or lifestyle limitations (e.g., vegan, cruelty-free, etc.).
* **Work and Daily Lifestyle**: Describe your typical day and activities to help us understand your needs.
* **Climate and Living Environment**: Describe your climate and living environment.
* **Additional Information**: Enter any additional information or previous professional services you have received.
* **Previous Professional Services**: Specify any previous professional services you have received.
* **Specific Requests or Preferences**: Enter any specific requests or preferences you have.
* **Questions for the Consultant**: Ask any questions or concerns you have for the consultant.
