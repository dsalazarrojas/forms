# Marketing Client Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Client Registration Form is used to collect information from potential customers or clients about their marketing preferences, contact details, and interest in various products or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the "Marketing Client Registration Form" fields as follows:
	* Enter the "Lead Source" by selecting one of the available options or type "Other" if it's not listed.
	* Provide your "Contact Details" in the required fields, including "First Name", "Last Name", and "Email".
	* Indicate your interest in various "Product/Service" categories, such as houses, apartments, condos, land, or other.
	* Select the "Interested In" options that apply to your needs, such as buying, renting, selling, or leasing.
	* Optionally, provide a "Note" for the sales team to reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Registration Form**: This is the main section of the form where you can enter your registration information.
* **Lead Source**: Select the source where you heard about us, such as Social Media, Google Ads, Email Marketing, or Referral.
* **Contact Details**: Provide your first name, last name, and email address to ensure we can contact you.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name for our records.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name for our records.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Product/Service**: Indicate which product or service you are interested in, such as houses, apartments, or land.
* **Interested In**: Select how you are interested in the product or service, such as buying, renting, or selling.
* **Lead Source Other**: If you have provided "Lead Source" as "Other", describe the source here.
* **Lead Source Other Description**: Provide a brief description of the lead source other.
* **Assigned Tool**: This field is not required, and you can leave it blank.
* **Created By**: This field is not required, and you can leave it blank.
* **Updated By**: This field is not required, and you can leave it blank.
* **Note**: Provide any additional information you would like to share with the sales team.

## Tips
* Be accurate when filling out the form to ensure we can follow up on your interests.
* If you select "Lead Source Other", provide a brief description to help us understand the lead source.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
