# veterinary_new_client_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for registering new customers for veterinary services. It collects essential information such as client name, contact details, and payment information to create a new account.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form by providing your **Client Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, and **Address** in the corresponding fields.
2. Select your **Pet Type** (e.g., Cat, Dog, Other) to help the veterinary service provider tailor their services to your needs.
3. Enter your **Veterinarian's Name**, **Veterinarian's Phone**, and **Notes** (if any) to ensure the veterinary service provider has all necessary contact information.
4. Confirm your **Payment Type** (e.g., Credit Card, Cash, Other) to determine the payment method for your services.
5. Enter your **CVV**, **Card Number**, **Zip Code**, and **Card Expiration Date** for payment card information.
6. Select your **Card Expiration Month** and **Card Expiration Year** for accurate payment processing.
7. For **CVV Number**, select multiple options (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8) if applicable.
8. Finally, click **Confirm** to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter your name as it should be used by the veterinary service provider.
* **Email**: Provide your email address for secure communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Address**: Enter your address for billing and contact purposes.
* **Pet Type**: Select your pet type from the options provided (e.g., Cat, Dog, Other).
* **Veterinarian's Name**: Enter the name of your chosen veterinarian for personalized care.
* **Veterinarian's Phone**: Enter the phone number of your chosen veterinarian.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments for the veterinary service provider.
* **Payment Type**: Select your preferred payment method (e.g., Credit Card, Cash, Other).
* **CVV**: Enter the 3-digit code found on the back of your credit card.
* **Card Number**: Enter the 16-digit number found on your credit card.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your postal zip code for billing purposes.
* **Card Expiration Date**: Enter the expiration date of your credit card.
* **Card Expiration Month**: Select the expiration month of your credit card.
* **Card Expiration Year**: Enter the expiration year of your credit card.
* **CVV Number**: Select multiple CVV options (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8) if applicable.
* **Card Name**: Enter the name on your credit card.
* **Card Expiration Year**: Confirm the expiration year of your credit card.
* **Card Expiration Month**: Select the expiration month of your credit card.
* **Confirm**: Confirm that all information is accurate before submitting your registration.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are completed to avoid delays in processing your registration.
* Double-check your credit card information for accuracy to prevent errors.
