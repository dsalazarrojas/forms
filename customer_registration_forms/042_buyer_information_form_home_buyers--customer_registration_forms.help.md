# Buyer Information Form Home Buyers - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about home buyers, including their personal details, property preferences, and financial situation. This data will help us understand their needs and provide a personalized home buying experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Review the form carefully**: Ensure you understand each field's purpose and requirements.
2. **Fill in your basic contact information**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
3. **Select your preferred property type**: Choose from the list of options for the type of property you're interested in.
4. **Specify your preferred location**: Enter the city, neighborhood, or region where you'd like to live.
5. **Provide your budget and financing details**: Choose a budget range and specify your down payment availability and financing status.
6. **Add any additional notes or information**: Use the text field to provide any relevant additional context or details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`Buyer First Name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name to help us get started with your home buying journey.
* **Last Name** (`Buyer Last Name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name to help us understand your full name.
* **Email Address** (`Buyer Email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (`Buyer Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your primary phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Current Residence** (`Current Residence`, `text`, optional): Indicate your current living arrangement, which will help us understand your current situation.
* **When Are You Looking to Buy?** (`Looking Timeframe`, `select_one`, required): Choose when you're ready to start looking for a home, from "Right now" to "Just browsing".
* **Property Type Interested In** (`Property Type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the property types that interest you, from "Single-family home" to "Undecided".
* **Number of Bedrooms** (`Bedrooms Needed`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the number of bedrooms you require in your new home.
* **Number of Bathrooms** (`Bathrooms Needed`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the number of bathrooms you require in your new home.
* **Preferred Square Footage** (`Square Footage`, `text`, optional): Enter your desired home size in square feet.
* **Preferred Location or Area** (`Location`, `text`, required): Enter the city, neighborhood, or region where you'd like to live.
* **Commute or Proximity Requirements** (`Commute Preference`, `text`, optional): Specify any commute or proximity requirements, such as distance to work or schools.
* **Budget Range for Home Purchase** (`Budget Range`, `select_one`, required): Choose a budget range for your home purchase.
* **Down Payment Available** (`Down Payment`, `select_one`, optional): Select the percentage or amount of down payment you have available.
* **Financing Status** (`Financing Status`, `select_one`, required): Specify your financing status, from "Pre-approved for mortgage" to "Still determining".
* **Buyer Type** (`Buyer Type`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate whether you're a first-time homebuyer, experienced buyer, investor, or relocating professional.
* **Special Features or Must-Haves** (`Special Features`, `text`, optional): Enter any special features or must-haves you need in your new home, such as a pool or garage.
* **Property Condition Preference** (`Property Condition`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred property condition, from "Move-in ready" to "Fixer-upper ok".
* **Are You Working With an Agent?** (`Agent Representation`, `select_one`, required): Specify whether you're working with an agent or not.
* **Additional Information or Notes** (`Additional Notes`, `text`, optional): Use this field to provide any additional context or details about your home buying journey.
