# Join Our Service Loyalty Program - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from customers who want to join our service loyalty program. By completing this form, you'll be able to enjoy exclusive benefits and personalized service experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your information accurately and completely.
2. Review each field for correctness before submitting the form.
3. Submit the form once you've verified all information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates and offers.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for follow-up calls or messages.
* **Service Location** (`service_location`, text, required: false): Describe the location where you usually receive our services.
* **Loyalty ID** (`loyalty_id`, select_one, options: ['Yes', 'No'], required: false): Indicate if you've previously registered for our loyalty program. 
* **Service Provider** (`service_provider`, text, required: false): Name the person who has recommended our services to you or is your preferred service provider.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, text, required: false): Specify how you'd like to be followed-up on (e.g. phone call, email).
* **Service Frequency** (`service_frequency`, select_one, options: ['Yes', 'No'], required: false): Tell us how often you usually receive our services.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional information that might help us better serve you.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask our customer support for assistance.
