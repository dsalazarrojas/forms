# good_hart_february_release_pre_order - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customer registration and pre-ordering for the February release of a product or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name**, **Email**, and **Phone** fields with accurate information.
2. Select your **Favorite Color** from the options provided.
3. If applicable, select your **Interests** from the list of options.
4. Enter your **Date of Birth** and **Time of Birth** if required.
5. Add any additional **Note** if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (Customer Name, Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (Email, Email, Required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted about your pre-order.
* **Phone** (Phone, Text, Required): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted about your pre-order.
* **Favorite Color** (Favorite Color, Select One, Required): Select the color you prefer from the options provided.
* **Interests** (Interests, Select Multiple, Optional): Select any interests you have from the list of options. This will help us tailor your experience with our product or service.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Birth** (Time of Birth, Time, Optional): Enter your time of birth in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Note** (Note, Note, Optional): Add any additional information about your pre-order if needed.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately.
- Check your email and phone number for correct formatting.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us directly.
