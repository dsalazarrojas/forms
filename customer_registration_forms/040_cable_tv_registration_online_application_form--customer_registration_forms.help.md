# Cable TV Registration Online Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help new customers register for cable TV services online. It gathers essential information about the subscriber, their contact details, and billing preferences to process their application efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the "Subscriber Information" page, which will prompt you to provide your full name.
2. Enter your full legal name in the "Full Legal Name" field.
3. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Provide your government-issued ID number, ensuring to enter only the last 4 digits for identification purposes.
5. Enter your contact email address.
6. Provide your primary phone number.
7. Proceed to the "Service Installation" page, where you will be prompted to provide your installation address.
8. Choose the type of residence you live in from the provided options (Single Family Home, Apartment/Condo, Townhouse, or Commercial Office).
9. Indicate your status as an owner or renter of the residence.
10. On the "Package and Options" page, select your preferred base channel package and choose any additional premium add-on packs you'd like to include.
11. Specify the number of TV receivers/boxes needed for your subscription.
12. On the "Billing and Payment" page, select your preferred billing cycle (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually with a 10% discount).
13. Choose whether you'd like to enroll in paperless billing and auto-pay (saving you 5 dollars per month).
14. Finally, enter your electronic signature by typing your full name, indicating that you agree to the service contract.
15. Confirm your application date and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Legal Name** (Applicant Full Name): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format for credit verification purposes.
* **Govt Issued ID Number (SSN/DL)**: Enter your government-issued ID number, with only the last 4 digits for identification.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address that you'd like to be contacted for any updates or notifications.
* **Primary Phone Number**: Enter your primary phone number.
* **Type of Residence** (Residence Type): Choose the type of residence you live in (Single Family Home, Apartment/Condo, Townhouse, or Commercial Office).
* **Status** (Owner/Renter): Indicate whether you own or rent your residence.
* **Base Channel Package** (Base Channel Package): Select your preferred base channel package (Basic Economy, Standard Digital, Preferred Plus, or Ultimate Platinum).
* **Number of TV Receivers/Boxes Needed**: Specify the number of TV receivers/boxes you need for your subscription.
* **Preferred Billing Cycle** (Billing Cycle): Choose your preferred billing cycle (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually with a 10% discount).
* **Enroll in Paperless Billing and Auto-pay** (Auto-pay Enrollment): Opt-in for paperless billing and auto-pay, saving you 5 dollars per month.
* **Electronic Signature** (Electronic Signature): Enter your electronic signature by typing your full name, signifying your agreement to the service contract.
* **Date of Application** (Application Date): Confirm the date of your application submission.
