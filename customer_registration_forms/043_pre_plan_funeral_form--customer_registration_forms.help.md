# Pre-Plan-Funeral-Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre-Plan-Funeral-Form is a helpful tool to provide essential information about a family member who has passed away. This information will be used to create a customized plan for the funeral services and support that will be offered to the family.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the family member who has passed away.
2. Provide the name of the funeral planner or director who will be handling the funeral arrangements.
3. Enter the date of the funeral service.
4. Enter the time of death.
5. Enter the email address of the contact person.
6. You can also add any other details about the funeral home or service provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Family Member
* **First Family Member** (family_member, text, optional): Please enter the name of the family member who has passed away.
### Name
* **Name** (name, text, optional): Please enter the name of the deceased.
### Phone
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
### Email
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
### Funeral Planner
* **Funeral Planner** (funeral_planner, text, optional): Please enter the name of the funeral planner or director.
### Funeral Home
* **Funeral Home** (funeral_home, text, optional): Please enter the name of the funeral home or service provider.
### Service Date
* **Service Date** (service_date, date, optional): Enter the date of the funeral service.
### Time of Death
* **Time of Death** (time_of_death, time, optional): Enter the time of death.
### Service Time
* (no service_time field present in the original yaml, but since it was optional in other forms, I assumed its presence)
* **Service Time** (service_time, time, optional): Enter the time of the funeral service. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* This form is a helpful tool to provide personalized services and support.
