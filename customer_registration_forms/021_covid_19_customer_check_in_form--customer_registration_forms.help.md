# Covid 19 Customer Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from customers visiting vaccination sites for COVID-19 vaccination. This information is used to track and manage the vaccination process effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the **Customer Information** section.
    *   Enter your **name** in the "name" field.
    *   Enter your **date of birth** in the "date_of_birth" field in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
    *   If you have been vaccinated, enter the **date of vaccination** in the "date_of_vaccination" field in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
    *   Enter the **vaccination site** where you received your vaccination.
2.  Next, provide your **phone number** and **email address** for contact and communication purposes.
3.  Select your **check-in date** and **check-in time** for the vaccination process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be recorded.
*   **date_of_birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 1995-02-22).
*   **date_of_vaccination**: If you have been vaccinated, enter the date of your vaccination in the format YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 2021-06-10).
*   **vaccination_site**: Enter the name of the vaccination site where you received your vaccination.
*   **phone_number**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you regarding any vaccination-related issues or appointments.
*   **email_address**: Enter your email address for us to communicate with you efficiently.
*   **check_in_date**: Select the date you plan to visit the vaccination site for your appointment.
*   **check_in_time**: Select the time you plan to arrive at the vaccination site for your appointment.
