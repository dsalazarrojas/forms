# customer_loyalty_gamification_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register customers for a customer loyalty gamification program, allowing us to understand their preferences and tailor the program to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer details, including your email and phone number.
2. Select your loyalty program participation status.
3. Choose your rewards card type and number (if applicable).
4. List any additional customer programs you are enrolled in.
5. If you have enrolled in a customer program not listed, provide the name and details of that program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details**
	+ **Customer Details** (1): Please enter your customer details, including your name and contact information.
* **Loyalty Programs**
	+ **Loyalty Programs** (2): Select "Yes" if you are interested in participating in our loyalty program.
* **Rewards Card Type**
	+ **Rewards Card Type** (3): Select "Yes" if you have a rewards card and want to link it to your account.
* **Rewards Card Number**
	+ **Rewards Card Number** (4): If you have a rewards card, enter the number on the card.
* **Email**
	+ **Email** (5): Enter your email address to receive updates and promotions.
* **Phone Number**
	+ **Phone Number** (6): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your account.
* **Customer Programs**
	+ **Customer Programs** (7): Select "Yes" for each customer program you are enrolled in.
* **Other Customer Programs**
	+ **Other Customer Programs** (8): If you are enrolled in a customer program not listed, type the name of the program in this field.
* **Other Customer Program**
	+ **Other Customer Program** (9): Enter the details of the other customer program you are enrolled in.
* **Other Customer Program Details**
	+ **Other Customer Program Details** (10): Enter any additional information about the other customer program you selected.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter your email and phone number correctly to ensure we can contact you about your account.
* If you are unsure about any of the customer programs listed, feel free to ask us for help.
* If you select "Yes" for any of the customer programs, make sure to provide the details of that program in the corresponding field.
