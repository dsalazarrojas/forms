# Food Delivery Agent Quiz Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Food Delivery Agent Quiz Form is designed to assess the qualities and skills of a potential food delivery agent. The form is used to determine the suitability of a candidate for the role of a food delivery agent.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please enter your first name in the `First Name` field.
2. Please enter your last name in the `Last Name` field.
3. Please enter your email address in the `Email` field.
4. Please enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
5. Please enter your company information in the `Company` field.
6. Please describe your experience in the `Experience` field.
7. Please answer the question regarding your attitude by selecting `Yes` or `No` in the `Attitude` field.
8. Please provide more information regarding your company in the `Company Info` field.
9. Please share any customer cases you have handled in the `Customer Cases` field.
10. Please describe your company's values in the `Company Values` field.
11. Please describe your company's policy in the `Company Policy` field.
12. Please share any attitude cases you have encountered in the `Attitude Cases` field.
13. Please list your goals in the `Goals` field.
14. Please describe your motivation in the `Motivation` field.
15. Please describe your expectations from the role in the `Expectations` field.
16. Please provide any feedback in the `Feedback` field.
17. Please describe your communication skills in the `Communication` field.
18. Please describe your collaboration skills in the `Collaboration` field.
19. Please describe your decision-making process in the `Decision Making` field.
20. Please describe your problem-solving skills in the `Problem Solving` field.
21. Please describe your adaptability in the `Adaptability` field.
22. Please describe your leadership qualities in the `Leadership` field.
23. Please describe your teamwork skills in the `Teamwork` field.
24. Please describe your time management skills in the `Time Management` field.
25. Please describe your work ethic in the `Work Ethic` field.
