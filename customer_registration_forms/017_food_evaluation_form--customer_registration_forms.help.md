# Food Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Food Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback from customers after they have had a food-related experience at a food establishment. This form will help the establishment to evaluate their food quality, service, and overall experience provided to customers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your honest feedback on your recent visit to the food establishment.
2. Select the relevant options for each field, and fill in any text fields as needed.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields (indicated by *).
4. Provide additional comments if you have any further feedback or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a brief description of your experience at the food establishment. Please provide a short introduction to your feedback.
* **Rating**: Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Food Quality**: Describe your experience with the food quality, including the taste, presentation, and overall satisfaction.
* **Taste**: Describe your experience with the taste of the food, including any standout flavors or dislikes.
* **Service**: Describe your experience with the service provided by the establishment, including the friendliness of the staff, response times, and overall satisfaction.
* **Environment**: Describe your experience with the environment of the establishment, including the cleanliness, ambiance, and overall atmosphere.
* **Overall Experience**: Rate your overall experience at the establishment, using the same 1-5 scale as the Rating field.
* **Will Visit Again?**: Select all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Will Recommend?**: Select all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for the establishment, including improvements or areas of concern.
