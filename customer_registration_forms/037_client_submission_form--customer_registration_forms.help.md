# Client Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Client Submission Form is designed to collect information from clients who want to submit requests, inquiries, or feedback to the organization. It helps the organization's team to efficiently respond and address client requests.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully and fill out each section thoroughly.
- Enter your first name, last name, and email address accurately.
- Select the correct type of submission or request you are making.
- Provide a brief summary of your submission or request in the subject field.
- Describe your submission or request in detail in the submission details field.
- Choose your preferred contact method and availability.
- If applicable, provide a reference number or case ID.
- Attach any relevant files or documents to support your submission.
- Confirm that you give consent to be contacted regarding your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as you would like it to be used for communication.
- **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as you would like it to be used for communication.
- **Email Address** (email, required): Enter a valid email address that you can be reached at.
- **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter a valid phone number that you can be reached at.
- **Company Name** (text, optional): If you are submitting on behalf of a company, enter the name.
- **Type of Submission or Request** (select_one, required): Select one of the options provided to describe the purpose of your submission.
- **Subject** (text, required): Enter a brief summary of your submission or request.
- **Detailed Description** (text, required): Provide a complete description of your submission or request.
- **Priority Level** (select_one, optional): If you believe your submission requires urgent attention, select the corresponding option.
- **Preferred Contact Method** (select_one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted regarding your submission.
- **Best Time to Contact You** (text, optional): Enter your preferred time slots or availability for contact.
- **Reference Number or Case ID** (text, optional): If you have a previous case or reference number, enter it here.
- **Attachments or Additional Information** (text, optional): Attach any relevant files or documents to support your submission.
- **Consent to be Contacted** (select_one, required): Confirm that you give consent to be contacted regarding your submission.
