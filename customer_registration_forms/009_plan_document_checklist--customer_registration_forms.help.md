# plan_document_checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect and manage customer and plan information for internal staff use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields.
2. Enter your first name, last name, and email address in the corresponding fields.
3. Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
4. Select the correct plan type and duration for your customer.
5. Choose the status of your plan (e.g., Status 1, Status 2, Status 3).
6. Optionally, provide additional details such as assigned tools and the names of customers you are working with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents. (Required)
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents. (Required)
* **Email**: Enter your email address to receive communications and updates. (Required)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes. (Required)
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth for identification purposes. (Optional)
* **Address**: Enter your current address if you need to provide it. (Optional)
* **Plan ID**: Enter the unique ID assigned to your plan. (Required)
* **Plan Type**: Select the type of plan you are working on (e.g., -ประก -ประก). (Required)
* **Customer Name**: Select the customer(s) you are working with. (Optional)
* **Plan Start Date**: Enter the start date of your plan. (Optional)
* **Plan End Date**: Enter the end date of your plan. (Optional)
* **Plan Duration**: Choose the duration of your plan (e.g., 1, 2, 3). (Required)
* **Plan Status**: Select the status of your plan (e.g., Status 1, Status 2, Status 3). (Required)
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter any tools assigned to your plan (e.g., tools, equipment). (Optional)
* **Created By**: Enter the person who created this plan. (Optional)
* **Updated By**: Enter the person who updated this plan. (Optional)
* **Updated Date**: Enter the date of the last update. (Optional)
* **Updated Time**: Enter the time of the last update. (Optional)
* **Deleted At**: Enter the date when this plan was deleted. (Optional)
* **Created At**: Enter the date when this plan was created. (Optional)
* **Updated At**: Enter the date when this plan was last updated. (Optional)
* **Created By 2**: Enter the person who created this record. (Optional)
* **Updated By 2**: Enter the person who updated this record. (Optional)
* **Updated Time 2**: Enter the time when this record was last updated. (Optional)
* **Deleted At 2**: Enter the date when this record was deleted. (Optional)
* **Plan Start Date**: Enter the start date of your plan. (Optional)
* **Plan End Date**: Enter the end date of your plan. (Optional)

Note: Fields with "Required" status have a red asterisk (\*) at the end of the field label.

Please ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
