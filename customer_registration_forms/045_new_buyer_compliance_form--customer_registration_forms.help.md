# New Buyer Compliance Form - Help Guide

**Purposes:**

* This form is used to capture information from new buyers, including their personal details, address, and real estate transaction information.
* The form ensures that all required information is collected in a structured and organized manner, reducing the risk of errors and inconsistencies.
* It also helps to maintain accurate records and facilitate communication between the real estate agents, buyers, and other stakeholders involved in the transaction.

**How To Complete This Form:**

1. Fill in the required fields with your personal details, such as your first name, last name, email, phone number, address, and city.
2. Select the real estate property type from the options provided (e.g., Land, Home, Condo, Townhouse, or Apartment).
3. Choose the status of the property (e.g., Active, Sold, or Leased).
4. Enter the price of the property.
5. Provide details about the property's area and address.
6. Specify the location of the file and its size.
7. Add any additional notes or file descriptions if necessary.

**Field-by-Field Explanation:**

* **First Name** (`user_first_name`): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`user_last_name`): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`user_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`user_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`user_address`): Enter your address.
* **City** (`user_city`): Enter your city.
* **State** (`user_state`): Enter your state.
* **Zip** (`user_zip`): Enter your zip code.
* **Purpose** (`user_purpose`): Enter the purpose of the form (e.g., new buyer compliance).
* **Real Estate Address** (`real_estate_address`): Enter the address of the real estate property.
* **Real Estate Agent** (`real_estate_agent`): Enter the name of the real estate agent.
* **Buyer Agent** (`buyer_agent`): Enter the name of the buyer agent.
* **Buyer Name** (`buyer_name`): Enter the name of the buyer.
* **File Name** (`file_name`): Enter the name of the file (optional).
* **File Date** (`file_date`): Enter the date of the file.
* **File Type** (`real_estate_file_type`): Select the type of the file (e.g., document, image).
* **Property Type** (`real_estate_property_type`): Select the type of the property (e.g., Land, Home, Condo).
* **Status** (`real_estate_status`): Select the status of the property (e.g., Active, Sold, Leased).
* **Price** (`real_estate_price`): Enter the price of the property.
* **Area** (`real_estate_area`): Enter the area of the property.
* **File Location** (`file_location`): Enter the location of the file.
* **File Size** (`file_size`): Enter the size of the file.
* **File Description** (`file_description`): Enter a description of the file.
* **File Notes** (`file_notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments about the file.
