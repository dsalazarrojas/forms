# Healthy Weight Loss Transformation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers who are interested in weight loss programs or services. We will use this data to provide personalized recommendations and support to help customers achieve their weight loss goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address to ensure that we can contact you regarding your weight loss progress.
3. Provide your Phone Number so we can reach out to you for any additional information or support.
4. Describe your Weight Loss Goal in the designated field. This will help us understand your specific needs and tailor our recommendations accordingly.
5. Enter your Current Weight and Target Weight in the respective fields. This will help us calculate your weight loss progress and provide customized advice.
6. Enter your Height in the field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`user_input`, text, optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your active email address where we can contact you regarding your weight loss progress.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number so we can reach out to you for any additional information or support.
- **Weight Loss Goal** (`weight_loss_goal`, text, optional): Please describe your weight loss goal, e.g., "lose 10 pounds" or "improve overall health".
- **Current Weight** (`current_weight`, number, optional): Please enter your current weight in pounds or kilograms.
- **Target Weight** (`target_weight`, number, optional): Please enter your target weight in pounds or kilograms.
- **Height** (`height`, number, optional): Please enter your height in inches or centimeters.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when providing your contact information, as this is how we will reach out to you for support.
- Clearly describe your weight loss goal, as this will help us provide tailored recommendations.
- Please note that the fields marked as "optional" are still important for us to know, but you can choose not to fill them in if you prefer.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
