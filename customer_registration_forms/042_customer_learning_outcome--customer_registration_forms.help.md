# Customer Learning Outcome - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the learning outcomes of customers who have completed a course. It helps in tracking and understanding the effectiveness of our courses.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer information fields with the customer's details.
2. Provide organization information, such as the company the customer represents.
3. Record the customer's learning outcomes and personal outcomes.
4. Select the relevant course title, level, and dates of the course taken.
5. Choose the assigned tool used during the course.
6. Provide the customer's phone number and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_information`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name and any relevant details.
* **Organization Information** (`organization_information`, text, optional): Enter the name of the customer's organization.
* **Course Information** (`course_information`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the course taken.
* **Personal Outcome** (`personal_outcome`, text, optional): Describe the customer's personal learning outcome from the course.
* **Customer Outcome** (`customer_outcome`, text, optional): Describe the customer's outcome from the course.
* **Organization Outcome** (`organization_outcome`, text, optional): Describe the organization's outcome from the course.
* **Course Title** (`course_title`, text, optional): Select the title of the course completed.
* **Course Level** (`course_level`, select, optional): Choose the level of the course completed (High or Low).
* **Course Start Date** (`course_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the course started.
* **Course End Date** (`course_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the course ended.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select, optional): Choose the tool assigned to the customer during the course (Tool A, Tool B, or Tool C).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you only fill out this form for customers who have completed a course.
* Double-check the customer's details to ensure accuracy.
* Use clear and concise language when answering the personal and organization outcomes questions.
* If any fields are not applicable, you can leave them empty.
