# Lics_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The LICS Registration Form is a crucial step for customers to create their LICS account. This form collects customer's personal details and information for their LICS profile.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the text field next to it.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the email field, following the standard email format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the text field, including the country code if necessary.
5. Fill in your **Date of Birth** in the date field (DD-MM-YYYY format).
6. Fill in your **Time of Birth** in the time field (HH:MM format).
7. Select one option from the **Select One** dropdown menu.
8. Select multiple options from the **Select Multiple** dropdown menu (you can select up to 3 options).
9. (Optional) You may leave a **Note** about yourself in the text area.
10. Enter your **LICS ID**, if you already have one.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: This field is a text field where you can enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: This field is a text field where you can enter your last name.
* **Email**: This field is an email field where you should enter your email address in the standard format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
* **Phone**: This field is a text field where you can enter your phone number, including the country code if necessary.
* **Date of Birth**: This field is a date field where you can enter your date of birth in the format DD-MM-YYYY (e.g., 12-01-1999).
* **Time of Birth**: This field is a time field where you can enter your time of birth in the format HH:MM (e.g., 10:30).
* **Select One**: This field is a dropdown menu where you must select one option.
* **Select Multiple**: This field is a dropdown menu where you must select up to 3 options.
* **Note**: This is an optional text area where you can provide more information about yourself.
* **LICS ID**: If you already have a LICS ID, please enter it here. Otherwise, you can leave it blank.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you enter accurate information in each field.
* If you're having trouble selecting an option in the **Select Multiple** field, click on the option to select it.
* You can only select up to 3 options in the **Select Multiple** field.
* Please review your form carefully before submitting it to avoid missing any essential details.
