# Customer Enquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Customer Enquiry Form is designed to collect information from customers, including contact details, customer information, and other relevant data. This form helps in understanding customer needs and preferences, and it can be used to assign tasks, track status, and provide feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's name and other relevant details in the "Customer Details" field.
2. Provide contact information in the "Contact Details" field, selecting multiple options as necessary.
3. Enter the customer's email address in the "Email" field.
4. Fill in the customer's phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the preferred contact time from the "Preferred Contact Time" dropdown.
6. Describe the customer's enquiry in the "Enquiry Description" field.
7. Assign the task to a team member or department in the "Assigned to" field.
8. Set the status of the enquiry in the "Status" field.
9. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Customer Enquiry Form Comments" field.
10. Attach relevant files or documents in the "Customer Enquiry Form Attachments" field.
11. Tag the form with relevant keywords or categories in the "Customer Enquiry Form Tags" field.
12. Add additional notes or comments in the "Customer Enquiry Form Notes" field.
13. Select relevant options from the "Customer Enquiry Form Custom Fields" and "Customer Enquiry Form Subforms" fields as necessary.
14. Set any conditions or requirements for the enquiry in the "Customer Enquiry Form Conditions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details**: Enter the customer's name and other relevant details.
* **Contact Details**: Select multiple options for the customer's contact information (e.g., phone, email, address).
* **Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Date of Visit**: Enter the date of the customer's visit or interaction.
* **Preferred Contact Time**: Select the preferred time of contact for the customer.
* **Enquiry Description**: Describe the customer's enquiry or request.
* **Assigned to**: Assign the task to a team member or department.
* **Status**: Set the status of the enquiry (e.g., new, in progress, completed).
* **Created by**: Enter the name of the person who created the form.
* **Created at**: Enter the date and time the form was created.
* **Updated by**: Enter the name of the person who last updated the form.
* **Updated at**: Enter the date and time the form was last updated.
* **Deleted by**: Enter the name of the person who deleted the form.
* **Deleted at**: Enter the date and time the form was deleted.
* **Created at**: Enter the date the form was created.
* **Updated at**: Enter the date the form was last updated.
* **Customer Enquiry Form Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the enquiry.
* **Customer Enquiry Form Attachments**: Attach relevant files or documents to the form.
* **Customer Enquiry Form Tags**: Select relevant keywords or categories for the form.
* **Customer Enquiry Form Notes**: Add additional notes or comments about the form.
* **Customer Enquiry Form Custom Fields**: Select relevant custom fields as necessary.
* **Customer Enquiry Form Subforms**: Select relevant subforms as necessary.
* **Customer Enquiry Form Conditions**: Set any conditions or requirements for the enquiry.
