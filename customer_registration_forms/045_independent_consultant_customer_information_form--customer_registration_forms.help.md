## independent_consultant_customer_information_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about independent consultants who are interested in becoming customers. The purpose of this form is to gather essential details about the consultant's identity, company, and professional background.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, in the format `user@example.com`.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your company name in the "Company" field.
6. Select your job title from the options provided in the "Job Title" field.
7. Indicate your role within your company by selecting one or multiple options in the "Role" field.
8. Choose your job function by selecting one or multiple options in the "Job Function" field.
9. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
10. Enter your city in the "City" field.
11. Choose the state in which you reside or operate your business using the drop-down menu in the "State" field.
12. Enter your postal code in the "Postal Code" field.
13. Enter your country of residence or operation in the "Country" field.
14. Enter your website (if applicable) in the "Website" field.
15. Indicate your industry by entering the relevant information in the "Industry" field.
16. (Optional) If you have a second job function, select one or multiple options in the "Job Function 2" field.
17. (Optional) If you have a second job title, select one option from the "Job Title 2" field.
18. (Optional) Enter any additional job titles you hold in the "Job Title 3" and "Job Title 4" fields.
19. Indicate the type of company you represent by selecting one option from the "Company Type" field.
20. Enter the size of your company in the "Company Size" field.
21. Enter your company's annual revenue in the "Annual Revenue" field.
22. (Optional) Enter any additional job titles you hold in the "Job Title 5" and "Job Title 6" fields.
23. (Optional) Enter any additional job titles you hold in the "Job Title 7" field.
