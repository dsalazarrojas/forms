# new_client_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new client registration form is designed to collect essential information from potential new clients, enabling our team to understand their identity, contact details, and expectations better. This form helps in the initial assessment and setup process for new clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have all the required information readily available.
2. Fill in the form with accurate and up-to-date details.
3. Review your entries before submission to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`id: 3`, `type: email`, `required: false`): Enter a valid email address here.
* **Phone** (`id: 4`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter your phone number here.
* **Life Status** (`id: 5`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Describe your current life situation here.
* **Expectations** (`id: 6`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Specify your expectations from our services or products here.
* **Agreement Terms** (`id: 7`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Accept or decline our agreement terms here.
* **Consent** (`id: 8`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Confirm your consent to our terms and conditions here.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are accurately filled in before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* Review and update your information as needed after the initial submission.
