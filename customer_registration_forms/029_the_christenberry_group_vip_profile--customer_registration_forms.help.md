# The Christenberry Group VIP Profile - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register as a VIP member of The Christenberry Group. It collects various information about you, including your contact details, job-related information, and personal data. Completing this form will help us tailor our communication and services to meet your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) or a bold font.
2. Select the correct options from the drop-down menus.
3. Make sure to review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, *): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., ID, passport, etc.). Ensure it matches your government-issued ID for verification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, *): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., ID, passport, etc.). Ensure it matches your government-issued ID for verification purposes.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, *): Enter your primary phone number for communication with us.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, *): Enter your official email address (e.g., work or personal email). This will be used as the primary contact point.
* **Verify Email** (`Verify Email`, email, ): This field requires you to re-enter your email address for verification purposes.
* **Company** (`Company`, text, ): If you represent a company, enter your company name here.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, text, ): Enter your job title within your company (e.g., CEO, Manager, etc.).
* **Department** (`Department`, text, ): Enter the department you belong to within your company (e.g., Sales, Marketing, etc.).
* **Location** (`Location`, text, ): Enter your current location (e.g., city, state, country).
* **Job Function** (`Job Function`, text, ): Enter a brief description of your job function or role.
* **Job Type** (`Job Type`, select_one, ): Select the type of job you hold (CE0, Sales, Marketing, etc.).
* **Job Category** (`Job Category`, select_multiple, ): Select all job categories that apply to your role (Software Development, Sales, Marketing, etc.).
* **Job Level** (`Job Level`, select_one, ): Select your job level (Junior, Senior, Manager, etc.).
* **Job Level 2** (`Job Level 2`, select_multiple, ): Select all job levels that apply to your role (Junior, Senior, Manager, etc.).
* **Job Family** (`Job Family`, select_multiple, ): Select all job families that apply to your role (Software Development, Sales, Marketing, etc.).
* **Job Family 2** (`Job Family 2`, select_multiple, ): Select all job families that apply to your role (Software Development, Sales, Marketing, etc.).
* **Job Family 3** (`Job Family 3`, select_multiple, ): Select all job families that apply to your role (Software Development, Sales, Marketing, etc.).
* **Job Family 4** (`Job Family 4`, select_multiple, ): Select all job families that apply to your role (Software Development, Sales, Marketing, etc.).
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, ): Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Date of Hire** (`Date of Hire`, date, ): Enter the date you started your current job.
* **Time of Hire** (`Time of Hire`, time, ): Enter the time you started your current job (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Years of Service** (`Years of Service`, number, ): Enter the number of years you have been serving your current company.
* **Years of Experience** (`Years of Experience`, number, ): Enter the number of years you have in your profession.
* **Years in Role** (`Years in Role`, number, ): Enter the number of years you have been in your current role.
* **Years Employed** (`Years Employed`, number, ): Enter the number of years you have been employed overall.

## Tips
- Please ensure your submission is accurate and truthful.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact us for assistance.
- If you submit duplicate or incorrect information, it may cause delays or affect our communication with you.
