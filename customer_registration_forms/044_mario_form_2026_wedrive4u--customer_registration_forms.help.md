# mario_form_2026_wedrive4u - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect comprehensive customer information for internal use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX` or `XXX XXX XXXX`.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address where you can be reached.
* **Country** (`country`, select_one, required): Select your country of residence.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city of residence.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter your state of residence.
* **Zip** (`zip`, number, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information you think might be helpful.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, optional): This field seems to be redundant with other fields, but if it's necessary for internal use, enter your unique form ID.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_multiple, optional): If you have been assigned a specific tool, select 'Yes'.
