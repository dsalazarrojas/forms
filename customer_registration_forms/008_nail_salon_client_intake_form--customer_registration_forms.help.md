# Nail Salon Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from clients visiting your nail salon. Completing this form will help us understand your preferences, ensure a smooth check-in process, and provide you with a personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your name and other identification information.
2. Fill in your contact details.
3. Enter your payment information.
4. Add your signature.
5. Specify your booking preferences.
6. (Optional) Share any additional review information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info (1)**: Please enter your name and other relevant identification details.
* **Contact Info (2)**: Fill in your contact information, including phone number and email address.
* **Payment Info (3)**: Enter your payment method and preferred method of payment.
* **Signature Info (4)**: Add your signature as proof of identification.
* **Booking Info (5)**: Specify your preferred time slot, service, and any special requests.
* **Review Info (6)**: (Optional) Share any additional information about your visit, such as your satisfaction with the services provided.

## Tips
- Please ensure you fill in all required fields.
- Review your information before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
