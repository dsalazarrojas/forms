# Accounting Firms Client Profile Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about the client's company profile. It helps us understand your business's details, which is crucial for providing personalized and efficient accounting services. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that we can best serve your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the company's full legal name in the **Company Legal Name** field.
2.  If the company operates under a different name, enter it in the **Company Trading Name** field (optional).
3.  Enter the primary contact person's full name in the **Primary Contact Name** field.
4.  Enter the primary contact person's job title in the **Primary Contact Position** field.
5.  Enter the primary contact person's business email address in the **Primary Contact Email** field.
6.  Enter the direct phone number of the primary contact person in the **Primary Contact Phone** field.
7.  Enter the complete business address, including street, city, state, and postal code, in the **Company Address** field.
8.  Enter the federal tax identification number or equivalent in the **Company Tax ID** field.
9.  Select the legal structure of your business (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, etc.) in the **Business Type** field.
10. Choose the primary industry sector your company operates in from the list (e.g., Healthcare, Retail, etc.) in the **Industry Sector** field.
11. Choose the approximate annual revenue range of your company from the list (e.g., Under $100K, $500K - $1M, etc.) in the **Annual Revenue Range** field.
12. Enter the total number of employees in your company in the **Number of Employees** field.
13. Select all the accounting services you require from the list (e.g., Bookkeeping, Payroll Processing, etc.) in the **Accounting Services Needed** field.
14. Choose how you prefer to receive updates and communications from us (e.g., Email, Phone Call, etc.) in the **Preferred Communication Method** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Legal Name**: Enter the full legal name of the client company.
* **Company Trading Name**: Enter the name the company operates under if different from the legal name.
* **Primary Contact Name**: Enter the full name of the main contact person at the company.
* **Primary Contact Position**: Enter the job title of the main contact person.
* **Primary Contact Email**: Enter the business email of the main contact person.
* **Primary Contact Phone**: Enter the direct phone number of the main contact person.
* **Company Address**: Enter the complete business address including street, city, state, and postal code.
* **Company Tax ID**: Enter the federal tax identification number or equivalent.
* **Business Type**: Select the legal structure of the business (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, etc.).
* **Industry Sector**: Choose the primary industry sector the company operates in from the list.
* **Annual Revenue Range**: Choose the approximate annual revenue range of the company.
* **Number of Employees**: Enter the total number of employees in the company.
* **Accounting Services Needed**: Select all the accounting services required (e.g., Bookkeeping, Payroll Processing, etc.).
* **Preferred Communication Method**: Choose how you prefer to receive updates and communications from us (e.g., Email, Phone Call, etc.).
