# Winery Customer Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers to help us better understand their preferences and needs. This data will be used to tailor our wine club offerings and services to their interests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all the required fields (indicated by a star \*) and any other fields you feel are relevant to providing accurate information.
2. Select the correct options for the select fields (e.g., "Yes" or "No" for wine preferences).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Page** (id: 1, label: First Page, type: text, required: false): This is the first page of the form, where you can enter any additional comments or information about your experience with us.
* **Wine Preferences** (id: 3, label: Wine Preferences, type: select_multiple, required: false): Select all the wine preferences you are interested in (e.g., "Yes" for red wine, "Yes" for white wine, etc.).
* **Follow Ups** (id: 4, label: Follow Ups, type: select_multiple, required: false): Check all the types of follow-up communications you would like to receive from us (e.g., "Yes" for email, "Yes" for phone, etc.).
* **Contact Information** (id: 5, label: Contact Information, type: text, required: false): Provide your full contact information, including your name, email, phone number, and any other relevant details.
* **Notes** (id: 6, label: Notes, type: note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments you would like to share with us.
* **Wine Club** (id: 7, label: Wine Club, type: select_one, required: false): Choose whether you are interested in joining our wine club (select "Yes" if you are interested).
* **Social Media** (id: 8, label: Social Media, type: select_multiple, required: false): Check the social media platforms you are present on (e.g., "Yes" for Facebook, "Yes" for Instagram, etc.).

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out the form to ensure we can best serve you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us directly.
