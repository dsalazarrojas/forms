# Neuromuscular Massage Therapy Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential information from clients seeking neuromuscular massage therapy services, ensuring a safe and effective treatment experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form carefully and accurately, as it will help the therapist understand your needs and provide personalized care.
2. If you have any medical conditions, allergies, or concerns, please be sure to list them in the Medical History section.
3. Answer all fields honestly and thoroughly, as they will help the therapist create a tailored treatment plan.
4. If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their contact information for your safety and well-being.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Please provide your name, phone number, and email address for us to contact you.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Share any medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies you may have.
* **Health Info** (`health_info`, text, required: false): Provide any additional health-related information that may impact your treatment.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required: false): Specify any chronic or acute medical conditions you're experiencing.
* **Massage Info** (`massage_info`, text, required: false): Share your massage preferences and goals.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): Provide the name, phone number, and relationship to your emergency contact person.
* **Medical History 1** (`medical_history_1`, text, required: false): Please list any medical conditions or surgeries you may have.
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, text, required: false): Add any additional medical history information.
* **Medical History 3** (`medical_history_3`, text, required: false): Provide further details about your medical history.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly to ensure the best possible treatment experience.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask the therapist.
* This form is confidential and will be reviewed by the therapist before your treatment session.
