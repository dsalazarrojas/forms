# Cosmetology Client Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information from customers about their cosmetology service needs and preferences. It helps the service team provide effective communication and tailored services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of service you need from the options provided.
2.  Choose how you would like to be contacted by the service team.
3.  Enter your email address and phone number, which will be used to contact you for the service appointment.
4.  Describe the details of the service you require in the "Service Details" field.
5.  Select the tool you want to be assigned for your service.
6.  Confirm whether the tool has been assigned correctly.
7.  Add any additional notes or comments you have for the service team.
8.  Enter your service address where the cosmetology service will be performed.
9.  Indicate who submitted this form (Self, Staff, or Manager).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **What type of service needed** (`service_needed`, `select_one`): This field allows you to select the type of cosmetology service you need, such as Haircut, Haircolor, or Makeup.
*  **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, `select_multiple`): Choose how you would like to be contacted by the service team, such as Email, Phone, or In Person.
*  **Email** (`client_email`, `email`): Enter your email address that the service team will use to contact you.
*  **Phone** (`client_phone`, `text`): Enter your phone number where the service team can reach you.
*  **Service Details** (`service_details`, `text`): Describe the details of the service you require, such as specific hair styles or color options.
*  **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_multiple`): Select the tool you want to be assigned for your service.
*  **Tool Assigned** (`tool_assigned`, `select_one`): Confirm whether the tool has been correctly assigned for your service.
*  **Notes** (`notes`, `note`): Add any additional comments or notes you have for the service team.
*  **Service Address** (`service_address`, `text`): Enter your service address where the cosmetology service will be performed.
*  **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_multiple`): Indicate who submitted this form, such as Self, Staff, or Manager.
