# Customer And Contract Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer And Contract Details Form is a crucial document for registering new customers or updating customer information for existing contracts. This form gathers essential details about the customer and their contract, ensuring accurate and up-to-date customer data.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about the customer.
2. Select the correct options for contract type and contract status.
3. Enter relevant information for the customer address, email, phone number, and contract dates.
4. Add any additional contract notes or description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Person** (ID: 3, Required): Enter the name of the contact person for the customer.
* **Contract Type** (ID: 4, Required): Select the type of contract (Domestic or International).
* **Contract Status** (ID: 9, Required): Choose the status of the contract (Active or Inactive).
* **Contract Description** (ID: 19, Optional): Add any additional details or notes about the contract.
* **Customer Name** (ID: 1, Optional): Enter the name of the customer.
* **Customer Address** (ID: 2, Optional): Enter the customer's address.
* **Customer Phone** (ID: 6, Optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Email** (ID: 5, Optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Contract Start Date** (ID: 7, Optional): Enter the start date of the contract.
* **Contract End Date** (ID: 8, Optional): Enter the end date of the contract.
* **Customer Address 2** (ID: 11, Optional): Enter any additional address information for the customer.
* **Customer Country** (ID: 12, Optional): Enter the customer's country of origin.
* **Contract Start Time** (ID: 13, Optional): Enter the start time of the contract.
* **Contract End Time** (ID: 14, Optional): Enter the end time of the contract.
* **Customer City** (ID: 15, Optional): Enter the customer's city.
* **Customer Postal Code** (ID: 16, Optional): Enter the customer's postal code.
* **Customer State** (ID: 17, Optional): Enter the customer's state or region.
* **Customer Country Code** (ID: 18, Optional): Enter the customer's country code.
* **Contract Notes** (ID: 10, Optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the contract.

## Tips

* Double-check the accuracy of the entered information before submitting the form.
* Use the correct format for dates and times (dd/mm/yyyy and 00:00:00).
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the customer or relevant personnel.
