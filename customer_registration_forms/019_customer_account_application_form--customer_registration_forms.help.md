# Customer Account Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you understand how to complete the Customer Account Application Form, which is used to capture information about a customer's account details for internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your information.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date details about yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your last name.
* **Company Name** (`company`, `text`, **required**): Enter your company name.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, **required**): Enter a valid email address. Please note that this will be used as your contact email.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, **required**): Enter your address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your phone number.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, **required**): Enter your job title within the company.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, **required**): Enter the department you belong to within the company.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Manager** (`manager`, `select_one`, **optional**): Select 'Yes' if you have a supervisor, 'No' otherwise.
* **Supervisor Job Title** (`job_title_supervisor`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your supervisor's job title if you have a supervisor.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, **required**): Enter your date of birth in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **Date of Hiring** (`date_of_hiring`, `date`, **optional**): Enter the date you were hired in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **Date Prophets** (`date_prophets`, `date`, **optional**): Enter the date prophets were hired in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **Date of Hiring Prophets** (`date_of_hiring_prophets`, `date`, **optional**): Enter the date prophets were hired in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **Prophet Job Title** (`job_title_prophet`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the job title of the prophet.
* **Date of Hiring Manager** (`date_of_hiring_manager`, `date`, **optional**): Enter the date you were hired as manager in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **Date of Hire Manager** (`date_of_hire_manager`, `date`, **optional**): This field appears to be redundant with 'Date of Hiring Manager'. Enter the date you were hired as manager in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **Manager Job Title** (`job_title_manager`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your job title as manager, if applicable.
* **Assistant Job Title** (`job_title_assistant`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your job title as assistant, if applicable.
