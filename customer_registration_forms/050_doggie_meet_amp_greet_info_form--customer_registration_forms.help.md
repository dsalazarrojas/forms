# Doggie Meet & Greet Info Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about you and your pet for a meet and greet event. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure your pet's information is up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in the required fields as follows:
  - **Pet Name**: Enter your pet's name.
  - **Pet Date of Birth**: Enter your pet's date of birth.
  - **Owner Name** (optional): If you're not the owner of the pet, enter the owner's name.
  - **Owner Phone** (optional): Enter the owner's phone number.
  - **Owner Email** (optional): Enter the owner's email address.
  - **Meet Date**: Enter the date of the meet and greet event.
- Ensure to fill in all the required fields accurately to avoid missing important information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Pet Name**: Enter your pet's name.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: None
- **Pet Date of Birth**: Enter your pet's date of birth.
  - Type: Date
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: None
- **Owner Name**: If you're not the owner of the pet, enter the owner's name.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: No
  - Hint: None
- **Owner Phone**: Enter the owner's phone number (if you're not the owner).
  - Type: Text
  - Required: No
  - Hint: None
- **Owner Email**: Enter the owner's email address (if you're not the owner).
  - Type: Email
  - Required: No
  - Hint: None
- **Meet Date**: Enter the date of the meet and greet event.
  - Type: Date
  - Required: No
  - Hint: None

## Tips
- Please ensure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this help guide for clarification.
