# real_estate_customer_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The real_estate_customer_intake_form is designed to collect information from potential customers about their preferences and interests in buying or renting a property. This form helps the sales team understand what type of property they are looking for, what price range they are comfortable with, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the First name field.
2. Enter your last name in the Last name field.
3. Enter your street address in the Street address field.
4. Enter your postal code (zip code) in the Postal code field.
5. Enter your email address in the Email field. Please use the format [your email address].
6. Enter your phone number in the Phone number field.
7. Select one or more options for your Desired property type (e.g. Single family home, Townhouse, etc.).
8. Choose a desired property price range from the available options.
9. Enter your preferred location (city/neighborhood) in the Preferred location field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name (First name, text, required: false)**: Enter your first name as it is commonly written.
* **Last name (Last name, text, required: false)**: Enter your last name as it is commonly written.
* **Street address (Street address, text, required: false)**: Enter the street address where you live or want to live.
* **Postal code (Postal code, text, required: false)**: Enter the postal/zip code of your street address.
* **Email (Email, email, required: false)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone number (Phone number, text, required: false)**: Enter your phone number for follow up calls.
* **Desired property type (Desired property type, select_multiple, required: false)**: Select one or more types of property you are interested in (e.g. Single family home, Townhouse, etc.).
* **Desired property price range (Desired property price range, select_one, required: true)**: Choose a price range for your desired property from the available options.
* **Preferred location (Preferred location, text, required: false)**: Enter the city or neighborhood where you prefer to live.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any special requirements or requests, please let us know in the preferred location field.
* The sales team will contact you based on the information you provide.
* If you have any questions or need more information, please don't hesitate to contact us.
