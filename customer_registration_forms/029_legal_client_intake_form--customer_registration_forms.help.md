# Legal Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from legal clients about their contact method and concern for the legal representation.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Choose your contact method by selecting "Yes" if you would like to provide a phone number and address. If you select "No", you will not be contacted by phone or email. If you select "Other", please specify your contact method in the "Contact Method Other" field.
5. If you selected "Other" in the previous step, enter your contact method in the "Contact Method Other" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field if you selected "Yes" in the previous step.
7. Enter your address in the "Address" field if you selected "Yes" in the previous step.
8. Describe your concern or issue in the "Concern" field.
9. Review and agree to the terms of the form in the "Agreement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`legal_client_intake_form_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (`legal_client_intake_form_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (`legal_client_intake_form_email`, email, required): Enter your email address in this field.
* **Contact Method** (`legal_client_intake_form_contact_method`, select_one, required): Choose your contact method from the options provided.
	+ Select "Yes" to provide a phone number and address.
	+ Select "No" to not be contacted.
	+ Select "Other" to specify your own contact method.
* **Contact Method Other** (`legal_client_intake_form_contact_method_other`, text, optional): Enter your contact method if you selected "Other" in the previous field.
* **Phone Number** (`legal_client_intake_form_contact_method_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you selected "Yes" in the "Contact Method" field.
* **Address** (`legal_client_intake_form_contact_method_address`, text, optional): Enter your address if you selected "Yes" in the "Contact Method" field.
* **Concern** (`legal_client_intake_form_concern`, text, required): Describe your concern or issue in this field.
* **Agreement** (`legal_client_intake_form_agreement`, text, optional): Review and agree to the terms of the form in this field.
