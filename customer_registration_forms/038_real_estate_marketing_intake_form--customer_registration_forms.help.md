# Real Estate Marketing Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Marketing Intake Form is a comprehensive form designed to gather information about your real estate marketing strategies. This data will be used to optimize your marketing efforts and improve their effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select the correct page number (1-8) to begin your answers.
2. Fill out the required fields for each page, and leave the optional fields blank if not necessary.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
4. Click the "Submit" button to submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Page 1**: This page is where you will start by providing a brief description of your current real estate marketing strategy.
* **First Page 2**: On this page, you will provide more details about your target audience and marketing channels.
* **Third Page 1**: This page is where you will specify the marketing messages you use in your marketing campaigns.
* **Third Page 2**: Here, you will provide information about your marketing budget and goals.
* **Fourth Page 1**: On this page, you will describe your current marketing activities and tactics.
* **Fourth Page 2**: This page is where you will specify your competition analysis and market research.
* **Fifth Page 1**: This page is where you will discuss your marketing metrics and performance evaluation.
* **Fifth Page 2**: Here, you will provide information about your future marketing plans and goals.
* **Sixth Page 1**: On this page, you will specify your marketing team structure and composition.
* **Sixth Page 2**: This page is where you will provide information about your marketing communication channels.
* **Seventh Page 1**: This page is where you will discuss your marketing technology and tools used.
* **Seventh Page 2**: Here, you will provide information about your marketing partnerships and collaborations.
* **Eighth Page 1**: On this page, you will describe your real estate marketing strategies and goals.
* **Eighth Page 2**: This page is where you will provide any additional information that you think is important for your real estate marketing strategy.
