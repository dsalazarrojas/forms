# real_estate_client_registration_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for real estate clients to register their information for various purposes, such as property ownership or rental agreements. Please fill out this form with accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that your registration process is completed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your contact information in the first section, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2.  Provide the contact information for the person who will be handling the real estate transaction.
3.  Enter the name of the client or the person who will be using the real estate service.
4.  Enter the address of the property, including the type of property (residential, commercial, industrial, or land).
5.  Select the type of property (residential, commercial, industrial, or land).
6.  Enter the address of the property.
7.  Enter the date and time of birth of the client.
8.  Enter the date and time of move-in.
9.  Add any additional notes or comments from the agent or client.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, `optional`): Please enter your name, email address, and phone number in this field.
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the name of the person who will be handling the real estate transaction.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, `optional`): Enter the email address of the person who will be handling the real estate transaction.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the phone number of the person who will be handling the real estate transaction.
*   **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the name of the client or person who will be using the real estate service.
*   **Address** (`address`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the address of the property.
*   **Agent Name** (`agent_name`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the name of the agent or representative handling the real estate transaction.
*   **Real Estate Type** (`real_estate_type`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select the type of property (residential, commercial, industrial, or land).
*   **Property Address** (`property_address`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): Select the address of the property from the provided options.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, `optional`): Enter the date of birth of the client.
*   **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, `time`, `optional`): Enter the time of birth of the client.
*   **Date of Move-in** (`date_of_move_in`, `date`, `optional`): Enter the date of move-in for the property.
*   **Time of Move-in** (`time_of_move_in`, `time`, `optional`): Enter the time of move-in for the property.
*   **Agent Note** (`agent_note`, `note`, `optional`): Enter any additional notes or comments from the agent.
*   **Client Note** (`client_note`, `note`, `optional`): Enter any additional notes or comments from the client.
