# Cemetery Mapping Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cemetery Mapping Form is designed to collect relevant information from customers to help map their deceased loved one's plot in a cemetery. This form will guide you through the process of selecting a suitable plot for burial or interment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please start by filling out the personal information section, which includes your name, email address, and phone number. This information will help us identify you as the authorized representative for the plot mapping process.
2. Next, provide the deceased's full name, email address, and phone number, if available.
3. Select the Date of Passing, if known, or leave it blank if not.
4. Choose the preferred sector and plot type for the plot.
5. If applicable, enter the specific section number or location if known.
6. Confirm your selection of the plot by choosing the "Yes - I have selected the plot" option.
7. Finally, provide the Date of Request and any additional requests or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as the authorized representative for the plot mapping process.
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for updates or inquiries.
* **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Deceased Full Name** (`deceased_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the deceased.
* **Date of Passing** (`passing_date`, date, optional): If known, enter the date of passing.
* **Preferred Cemetery Sector** (`preferred_sector`, select_one, required): Select the preferred sector of the cemetery where you would like to plot to be buried or interred.
* **Plot Type** (`plot_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of plot required (e.g. Standard Single, Double Depth, etc.).
* **Specific Section Request** (`specific_section_request`, text, optional): Enter the specific section number or location if known.
* **Interment Type** (`interment_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of interment (e.g. In-ground Burial, Mausoleum Crypt, etc.).
* **Confirmation of Selection** (`confirmation_of_choice`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have reviewed the plot map and made a choice.
* **Date of Request** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the current date.
* **Additional Requests or Information** (`additional_requests`, text, optional): Share any other relevant details.
