# Client And Company Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect primary contact information from clients, including their name, job title, email, phone number, and other essential details such as company name, website, industry, size, and address. This form is intended to provide a comprehensive overview of the client and their company.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Scroll through the form** and fill out each section carefully.
2. **Enter your information** in the designated fields, ensuring that you enter your full name, job title, email, phone number, and all other required fields accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your name as it appears on official records.
* **Job Title**: Your professional position in the company.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address used for communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter a valid phone number for contact purposes.
* **Company Legal Name**: Enter the official name of your company.
* **Company Website**: Enter your company's website URL (optional).
* **Industry / Sector**: Select your company's industry or sector from the provided options.
* **Company Size (Employees)**: Select the approximate number of employees in your company.
* **Street Address**: Enter your company's street address.
* **City**: Enter the city where your company is located.
* **State / Province**: Enter the state or province where your company is located.
* **ZIP / Postal Code**: Enter the postal code of your company's location.
* **Country**: Enter the country where your company is located.
* **Tax ID / VAT Number**: (Optional) Enter your company's tax ID or VAT number for invoicing purposes.
* **Accounts Payable Email**: (Optional) Enter the email used for accounts payable purposes.
* **How did you hear about us?**: (Optional) Describe how you found out about our service.
* **Additional Notes or Special Requirements**: Enter any additional information or specific requirements that should be known about your company.
* **Please note**: This form is not intended to be a formal contract or agreement, it is only for informational purposes. Make sure to verify all information before submission.
