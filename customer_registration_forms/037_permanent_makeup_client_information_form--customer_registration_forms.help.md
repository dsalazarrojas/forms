# Permanent Makeup Client Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential client information for permanent makeup procedures. It will be used to store and manage client data for your practice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Provide your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field (in mm/dd/yyyy format).
6. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
7. Enter the city of your address in the "City" field.
8. Enter the state of your address in the "State" field.
9. Enter your zip code in the "Zip Code" field.
10. If applicable, provide your medical history in the "Medical History" field.
11. If applicable, list any medical conditions in the "Medical Condition" field.
12. Enter the name of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
13. Enter the phone number of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
14. Describe your relationship with the emergency contact in the "Relationship with Client" field.
15. If applicable, list any allergies in the "Allergies" field.
16. Enter any medications you are currently taking in the "Medication" field.
17. If applicable, provide information on any medical devices you are currently using in the "Medical Devices" field.
18. If applicable, provide information on any previous surgeries in the "Surgery" field.
19. Enter your health insurance information in the "Health Insurance" field.
20. If applicable, provide information on your doctor's contact in the "Doctor Information" field.
21. Enter your doctor's name in the "Doctor" field.
22. Enter your doctor's phone number in the "Doctor Phone" field.
23. Enter your doctor's email in the "Doctor Email" field.
24. Select any permanent makeup services you would like to receive in the "Permanent Masters" field. Options include Microblading, Tattooing, Sclerotherapy, Permanent Eyeliner, Permanent Eyebrow, Permanent Lip Liner, Permanent Blush, Permanent Brows, and Permanent Lashes.
25. Finally, indicate your consent in the "Consent" field by selecting either "True" or "False".

* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city of your address.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state of your address.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): If applicable, provide your medical history.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): If applicable, list any medical conditions.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship with Client** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): If applicable, list any allergies.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): Enter any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Devices** (`medical_device`, text, optional): If applicable, provide information on any medical devices you are using.
* **Surgery** (`surgery`, text, optional): If applicable, provide information on any previous surgeries.
* **Health Insurance** (`health_insurance`, text, optional): Enter your health insurance information.
* **Doctor Information** (`doctor_info`, text, optional): If applicable, provide information on your doctor's contact.
