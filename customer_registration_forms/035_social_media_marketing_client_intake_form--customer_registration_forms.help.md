# Social Media Marketing Client Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about your social media marketing needs. It helps us understand your goals, budget, and expected launch date for your marketing campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting.
3. Ensure that all fields are complete and consistent.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your account manager for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required: false): This field is where you can enter your name or your company's name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): This field is where you enter your email address that we will use to communicate with you about your marketing campaign.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): This field is where you enter your phone number for us to reach out to you if needed.
* **Expected Launch Date** (`expected_launch_date`, date, required: false): This field is where you enter the date you expect your marketing campaign to launch. If unsure, you can enter the date you would like to start planning for.
* **Marketing Channels** (`marketing_channels`, select_one, required: false): This field allows you to select one or multiple marketing channels where you would like to reach your audience (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, Email, or Other). Selecting one or multiple channels will help us create a comprehensive marketing plan tailored to your needs.
* **Marketing Budget** (`marketing_budget`, number, required: false): This field is where you enter the amount of budget allocated for your marketing campaign.
* **Marketing Goals** (`marketing_goals`, text, required: false): This field is where you describe the specific goals you would like to achieve through your marketing campaign. Be as specific as possible so we can create an effective plan that meets your objectives.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are complete and accurate before submitting.
* Double-check your email address for correct formatting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your account manager for guidance.
* This form is not a contract, it's an intake form for your marketing campaign.
* We'll review and contact you within [timeframe] to discuss your campaign details.
* We value your feedback and look forward to working with you to achieve your marketing goals!
