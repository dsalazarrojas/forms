# Customer Success Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Success Registration Form is a tool used to collect and store customer information, allowing us to provide tailored services and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the step-by-step guide above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted directly.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter your company name or affiliation.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Job Title Description** (`job_title_desc`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your job title.
* **Job Title Type** (`job_title_type`, select_one, optional): Select one of the provided options that best describes your job title type.
* **Job Title Type Description** (`job_title_type_desc`, text, optional): This field is a description of your selected Job Title Type.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply to describe your company size.

## Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure we can provide you with the best possible service. If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact us.
