# Medical History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical History Form is designed to collect essential medical information from users, helping healthcare professionals make informed decisions about their care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully, and make sure to answer all relevant questions.
2. Select the correct answers for each question, using the provided options.
3. If you have any medical conditions or allergies that are not listed, please add them in the "Medical History Note" field.
4. Ensure that you are filling out the form accurately and honestly, as this information will be used by healthcare professionals to make decisions about your care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Allergies
* **Allergies** (medical_history_form_1, select_multiple, required/optional): Please select all the allergies you have or are suspected to have. If you are unsure, please answer "No".

### 2. Past Surgeries
* **Past Surgeries** (medical_history_form_2, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all past surgeries you have undergone.

### 3. Family History
* **Family History** (medical_history_form_3, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all medical conditions that are prevalent in your family.

### 4. Medical History
* **Medical History** (medical_history_form_4, select_one, required/optional): Please select "Yes" if you have any medical conditions, and "No" otherwise.

### 5. Medications
* **Medications** (medical_history_form_5, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past.

### 6. Past Medical Conditions
* **Past Medical Conditions** (medical_history_form_6, select_one, required/optional): Please select "Yes" if you have any past medical conditions, and "No" otherwise.

### 7. Medical History Note
* **Medical History Note** (medical_history_form_7, note, required/optional): Please add any additional medical notes or information that is not listed here.

### 8. Medical History Date
* **Medical History Date** (medical_history_form_8, date, required/optional): Please enter the date of your last medical check-up or the date of your first medical condition.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask a healthcare professional for guidance.
- This form is an essential part of the customer registration process, and it will help healthcare professionals provide the best possible care for you.
