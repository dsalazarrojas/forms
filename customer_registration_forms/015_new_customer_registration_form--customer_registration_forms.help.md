# New Customer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from new customers to create a profile and enable them to access our services. Please complete this form to provide accurate and complete information about yourself.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information.
2. Make sure to enter a valid email and date of birth.
3. Select the correct state and country from the provided options.
4. Choose the correct customer type from the options.
5. Once you have filled out all the required fields, submit the form to proceed with your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Text, Required)**: Enter your first name, as it is known to you.
* **Last Name (Text, Required)**: Enter your last name, as it is known to you.
* **Email (Email, Required)**: Enter a valid email address that you use and check regularly.
* **Phone (Text, Required)**: Enter your primary phone number.
* **Date of Birth (Date, Required)**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Address (Text, Required)**: Enter your physical address.
* **Address 2 (Text, Optional)**: Enter any additional address information (e.g. apartment number).
* **Zip (Number, Required)**: Enter your zip code.
* **City (Text, Required)**: Enter the city where you live.
* **State (Select, Required)**: Choose your state from the list (California or New York).
* **Country (Select, Required)**: Choose your country from the list (United States or Canada).
* **Customer Type (Select, Required)**: Choose whether you are an Individual or a Business.
