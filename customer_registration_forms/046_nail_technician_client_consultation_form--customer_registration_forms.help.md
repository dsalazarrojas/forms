# nail_technician_client_consultation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "nail_technician_client_consultation_form" is a form designed to collect information from clients about their preferences and needs for nail care services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your preferred service from the "Preferred Service" dropdown menu. Please choose from manicure, pedicure, polish, or nail extension.
2. If you have specific preferences or requests for your nail care, please enter them in the "Notes" field.
3. You are not required to provide any personal details, such as date of birth, phone number, or email, unless you choose to do so.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Preferred Service**: (`preferred_service`, select_one, required) 
Please select your preferred service from the available options.
* **Nail Care Frequency**: (`nail_care_frequency`, number, optional) 
Select how often you get nail care services (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, etc.).
* **Nail Care Budget**: (`nail_care_budget`, number, optional) 
Enter your estimated budget for nail care services.
* **Email**: (`email`, email, optional) 
Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted by us.
* **Phone Number**: (`phone_number`, text, optional) 
Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by us.
* **Date of Birth**: (`date_of_birth`, date, optional) 
If you'd like to provide your date of birth for our records.
* **Time of Visit**: (`time_of_visit`, time, optional) 
Select the time you'd like to book your appointment.
* **Notes**: (`note`, note, optional) 
Enter any additional information or requests you have for your nail care services.
