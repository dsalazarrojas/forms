# Customer Loyalty Portal Access Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Customer Loyalty Portal Access Form is used to verify a customer's identity and determine their eligibility for access to the customer loyalty portal. Please fill out this form with your accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth and secure access process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure you have permission from your company to access the loyalty portal.
3. Review all fields carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Registration Form** (`customer_registration_page`, text, optional): This is a brief description of the customer's account, which can be a brief company name or any other relevant information.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, such as name, address, and phone number.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number for us to reach out to you with any updates or concerns.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for us to contact you with any updates or concerns.
* **Loyalty Programs** (`loyalty_programs`, select_multiple, optional): Select any loyalty programs you are enrolled in or interested in. If you don't select any, you will still be granted access to the portal.
* **Customer Type** (`customer_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of customer you are (e.g., "Individual", "Business", or "Other"). This will help us determine your access level to the loyalty portal.
* **Account Status** (`account_status`, select_one, required): Choose your current account status (e.g., "Active", "Inactive", or "Other"). This will help us determine your eligibility for access to the loyalty portal.
