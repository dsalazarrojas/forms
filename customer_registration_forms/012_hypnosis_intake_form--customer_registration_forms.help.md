# Hypnosis Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hypnosis Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from clients to provide a comprehensive understanding of their needs and preferences. This guide will walk you through each field and help you complete the form accurately and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by filling out all required fields marked as required. These fields are marked with a red asterisk (\*) and are essential for our hypnotherapist to tailor your sessions.
2.  Ensure you answer all questions truthfully and accurately to provide the best possible experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Email** (`3`, `text`, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
*   **Phone** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
*   **Date Of Birth** (`5`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/1999).
*   **Height** (`6`, `text`, required): Enter your height in inches as a numerical value (e.g., 68). Please note that this field requires a numerical value only.
*   **Weight** (`7`, `text`, required): Enter your weight in pounds as a numerical value (e.g., 120). Please note that this field requires a numerical value only.
*   **Height Unit** (`8`, `select_one`, required): Choose the unit of measurement for your height (inches, centimeters, meters, feet).
*   **Weight Unit** (`9`, `select_one`, required): Choose the unit of measurement for your weight (pounds, kilograms, ounces, grams).
*   **Medical History** (`10`, `note`, not required): Please provide any medical history or concerns that might impact your hypnosis sessions.
*   **Allergies** (`11`, `note`, not required): List any allergies or sensitivities we should be aware of when preparing for your sessions.
*   **Medications** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required): List any medications you are currently taking, or select "Other" if you have a different medication regimen.
*   **Emergency Contact** (`13`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you have an emergency contact (Yes/No).
*   **Emergency Phone** (`14`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
*   **Relationship** (`15`, `select_one`, not required): If you have an emergency contact, choose the relationship between you and the contact (None, Other).
*   **Emergency Contact’s Name** (`16`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact’s Address** (`17`, `text`, not required): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
*   **Consent** (`18`, `text`, not required): Please sign below to consent to the use of this information.
*   **Signature** (`19`, `text`, required): Please sign below to confirm that you have read and agreed to our terms.
*   **Emergency Contact’s Name** (`20`, `text`, not required): This field is not used for client intake purposes.
*   **Emergency Contact’s Phone** (`21`, `text`, not required): This field is not used for client intake purposes.
*   **Emergency Contact’s Email** (`22`, `text`, not required): This field is not used for client intake purposes.
*   **Emergency Contact’s Address** (`23`, `text`, not required): This field is not used for client intake purposes.
*   **Medical Notes** (`24`, `note`, not required): Please note any additional medical information or concerns you would like to share with your hypnotherapist.
*   **Additional Notes** (`25`, `note`, not required): Please include any additional information you would like to share with your hypnotherapist.

## Tips

*   Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please do not hesitate to ask your hypnotherapist.
*   Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
