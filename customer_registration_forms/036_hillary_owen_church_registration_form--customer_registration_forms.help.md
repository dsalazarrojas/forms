# hillary_owen_church_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customers to register their information for a church event or program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the User Info section with your relevant details.
2.  Answer the Meal Reservation question with your preference.
3.  Enter your Email address for contact purposes.
4.  Provide your Phone number for communication.
5.  Add any additional comments or notes in the Note section if necessary.
6.  If the event has a specific date and time, select it in the Date and Time fields.
7.  Indicate your availability by selecting your reservation status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **User Info** (`user_info`, `text`, required: false): This section is for you to enter your basic information.
*   **Meal Reservation** (`meal_reservation`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks about your preference for meal options. Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your interest.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so that we can contact you.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Provide your phone number for easier communication.
*   **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about your registration.
*   **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): If there's a specific date for the event, select it here.
*   **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): If there's a specific time for the event, select it here.
*   **Reservation Status** (`reservation_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate your availability by selecting 'Available', 'Not Available', or 'Cancelled'.
*   **User Info 2** (`user_info_2`, `text`, required: false): This second User Info section is for additional details.
