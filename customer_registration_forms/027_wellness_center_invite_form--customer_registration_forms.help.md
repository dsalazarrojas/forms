# Wellness Center Invite Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers for invite purposes at the Wellness Center. It helps us to understand the customer's expectations and purpose of visit, and ensures that we have the necessary information to register the guest properly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the customer's information, including their phone number and email.
2. Specify the guest's purpose and expectation for the visit.
3. If you are a form submitter, please select your department and position.
4. Add any additional notes or comments if needed.
