# invisible_bead_extension_consultation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The invisible bead extension consultation form is a form designed to collect information from customers about their hair and personal preferences to provide them with accurate and personalized hair extension services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please ensure that it is a valid email address.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter any relevant user notes in the "User Notes" field (optional).
6. Select your hair type from the options.
7. Enter your hair length in the text field.
8. Enter your hair color in the text field.
9. Select your hair style from the options.
10. Enter your hair history in the text field.
11. Enter any preferences you have for your hair extension in the text field.
12. If you have any allergies, please enter them in the "Allergies" field. (optional)
13. If you have any medications, please enter them in the "Medications" field. (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (required): Enter your first name as it is spelled.
* **Last Name** (required): Enter your last name as it is spelled.
* **Email** (required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **User Notes** (optional): Enter any additional information that you would like to share with us.
* **Hair Type** (required): Select the type of hair that you have.
* **Hair Length** (required): Enter the length of your hair.
* **Hair Color** (required): Enter the color of your hair.
* **Hair Style** (required): Select the style of your hair.
* **Hair History** (required): Enter any relevant hair history information.
* **Preferences** (required): Enter any preferences you have for your hair extension.
* **Allergies** (optional): Enter any allergies that you have. If you have no allergies, please enter "none".
* **Medications** (optional): Enter any medications that you are currently taking. If you have no medications, please enter "none".
