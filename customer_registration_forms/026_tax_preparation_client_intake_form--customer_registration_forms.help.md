# Tax Preparation Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Tax Preparation Client Intake Form" is used to collect information from clients about their tax-related needs. It helps our tax preparation services team understand your requirements for accurate tax preparation.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately with your required and optional information.
2.  Please use the format "MM/DD/YYYY" for dates where applicable.
3.  If you are unsure about any field, please contact our tax preparation services team for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY" (e.g., 12/25/1990).
*   **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, number, optional): Enter your Social Security number without dashes or parentheses.
*   **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your residential address for tax purposes.
*   **Tax Return Date** (`tax_return_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to receive your tax return by (if known).
*   **Tax Year** (`tax_year`, number, optional): Enter the tax year for which you are preparing returns (e.g., 2022).
*   **Self-Employed?** (`is_self_employed`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you are self-employed or not.
