# Hair Salon Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair Salon Client Intake Form is a tool used to gather essential information from customers when they visit the hair salon. The form helps the staff to understand the customer's requirements, preferences, and contact details, allowing for more personalized service and efficient communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your general information, such as name, address, and phone number.
2. Share your appointment preferences, including your preferred date and time for an appointment.
3. Provide your contact information, including your email address and any other relevant contact details.
4. Enter your payment information, if applicable.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information (Page 1)**: This section is designed to collect your basic contact information. Please fill in your name, address, and phone number to ensure we can contact you.
* **Appointment Preferences (Page 2)**: In this section, specify your preferred date and time for an appointment. Be as specific as possible to ensure we can schedule your appointment accordingly.
* **Contact Information (Page 3)**: Enter your email address, phone number, and any other contact details you'd like to share with us.
* **Payment Information (Page 4)**: If you have any payment details or requests, such as payment methods or amounts, please provide them in this section.
* **Note (Page 5)**: This is an optional field where you can add any additional comments or requests you may have. Feel free to share any special requests or instructions with us.
* **Email (Page 6)**: Please enter your email address so we can contact you with updates or confirmations about your appointment. This is the primary method of communication we will use.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Be as specific as possible when sharing your appointment preferences.
* If you have any special requests or comments, please use the "Note" field.
* Our staff will contact you based on the information provided in this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us through our contact information.
