# new_client_profile_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new_client_profile_form is designed to collect basic information from new clients to create or update their profiles. This form is intended for individuals who do not already have a profile in the system. Please ensure that you are not duplicating an existing profile before completing this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "client_name" field with the client's name.
2. Enter the client's email address in the "email" field.
3. Provide the client's phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Enter the client's date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field. Please use the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
5. Select the client's occupation from the provided options (if any) in the "occupation" field.
6. Enter the client's address in the "address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **client_name** (text): Enter the client's name.
* **email** (email): Enter the client's email address. This field is used to contact the client.
* **phone** (text): Provide the client's phone number.
* **date_of_birth** (date): Enter the client's date of birth in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **occupation** (text): Select the client's occupation from the provided options.
* **address** (text): Enter the client's address.

## Tips

* Ensure that the information you enter is accurate and up-to-date.
* Please do not use this form if you are already aware of a similar profile in the system.
* If you have any issues or doubts while filling this form, contact the system administrator or support team.
