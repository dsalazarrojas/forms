# Returning User Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for returning users who need to register or update their account information to access certain features or areas of the application.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address.
2. Choose your Role (Administrator, User, or Guest) from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter your Password and confirm it by entering it again in the Email Confirmation field.
4. Optionally, provide your Company information, Phone number, Date of Birth, and/or Note if applicable.
5. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your registered email address to associate with your account.
* **Password** (`password`, text, required): Enter a password of your choice to secure your account.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): If you are associated with a company, enter the name of your organization.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Choose your role within the application (Administrator, User, or Guest).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format of month/day/year.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, optional): Enter your time of birth in the format of 24-hour time (e.g., 12:00 PM).
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional information you would like to share with our team.
* **Email Confirmation** (`email_confirmation`, email, required): Re-enter your email address to confirm it with us.
