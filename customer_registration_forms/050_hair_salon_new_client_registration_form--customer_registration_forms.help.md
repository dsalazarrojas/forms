# Hair Salon New Client Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from new clients to be used by the hair salon for client management and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields with your personal information.
2. Make sure to use a valid email address.
3. Select your gender and marital status from the provided options.
4. Fill in your contact details, including phone and address.
5. If applicable, fill in your occupation and education level.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that you have access to.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Date of Birth** (`dob`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
- **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your current address.
- **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city you currently reside in.
- **Pin** (`pin`, `text`, required): Enter your postal pin/zip code.
- **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter your state/province.
- **Country** (`country`, `text`, required): Enter your country of residence.
- **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Select your gender from the provided options.
- **Marital Status** (`marital_status`, `select_one`, required): Select your current marital status from the provided options.
- **Occupation** (`occupation`, `text`, optional): If you have a job, enter your occupation.
- **Education Level** (`education_level`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your highest level of education from the provided options.
