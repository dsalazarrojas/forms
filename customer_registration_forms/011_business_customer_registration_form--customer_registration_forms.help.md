# Business Customer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Customer Registration Form is for businesses that want to register their company information for partnerships, collaborations, or other business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of customer you are (Individual, Business, Government, Non-Profit, or Other).
2. Enter your first and last names.
3. Provide your primary email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. If you're a business, provide your company name.
6. If you're a business, you can also provide your business registration number.
7. Select your industry.
8. Choose the size of your company.
9. Enter your company website (if applicable).
10. Provide your street address.
11. Enter your city.
12. Enter your state or province.
13. Enter your postal code.
14. Select your country.
15. Choose how you'd like to be contacted (email, phone, mail, or text message).
16. Select how often you'd like to hear from us (weekly, monthly, quarterly, annually, or as needed).
17. Select any services you're interested in (products, consulting, support services, training, or maintenance).
18. Provide any additional information we should know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Type** (`Customer Type`, select_one, required): Select your customer type to categorize your business correctly.
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, required): Provide your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required): Enter your best contact number.
* **Company Name** (`Company Name`, text, required): If you're a business, enter your company name.
* **Business Registration Number** (`Business Registration Number`, text, optional): If you're a business, you can enter your business registration number.
* **Industry** (`Industry`, select_one, optional): Select your industry to help us understand your business better.
* **Company Size** (`Company Size`, select_one, optional): Choose the size of your company.
* **Company Website** (`Company Website`, text, optional): If you have a website, provide the URL.
* **Street Address** (`Street Address`, text, required): Enter your street address.
* **City** (`City`, text, required): Enter your city.
* **State/Province** (`State/Province`, text, required): Enter your state or province.
* **Postal Code** (`Postal Code`, text, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Country** (`Country`, text, required): Select your country.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`Preferred Contact Method`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to be contacted (email, phone, mail, or text message).
* **How often would you like to hear from us?** (`How often would you like to hear from us?`, select_one, optional): Select how often you'd like to hear from us (weekly, monthly, quarterly, annually, or as needed).
* **Services you are interested in** (`Services you are interested in`, select_multiple, optional): Select any services you're interested in (products, consulting, support services, training, or maintenance).
* **Additional Information** (`Additional Information`, text, optional): Provide any additional information we should know.
