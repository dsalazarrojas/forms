# New Client Intro Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect basic information about a new client who is not yet registered in our system. 

## How To Complete This Form

This form has multiple pages with different fields. Please follow these steps to complete it:

1. **First Name**: Enter the client's first name. 
2. **Last Name**: Enter the client's last name. 
3. **Email**: Enter the client's email address. 
4. **Phone Number**: Enter the client's phone number. 
5. **Date of Birth**: Enter the client's date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy). 
6. **Address 1**: Enter the client's first line of address. 
7. **Address 2**: Enter the client's second line of address. 
8. **City**: Enter the client's city. 
9. **State**: Enter the client's state. 
10. **Zip**: Enter the client's zip code. 
11. **Company Name**: Enter the client's company name. 
12. **Job Title**: Enter the client's job title. 
13. **Department**: Enter the client's department. 
14. **Job Title Description**: Enter a brief description of the client's job title. 
15. **Work Phone**: Enter the client's work phone number. 
16. **Job Title Level**: Select the level of the client's job title (Senior, Junior, Manager, or Other). 
17. **Job Title Level Other**: If the client's job title level is not one of the options above, enter a custom description here. 
18. **Job Title Level Hint**: Enter a hint for the custom job title level entered above. 
19. **Work Email**: Enter the client's work email address. 
20. **Work Address**: Enter the client's work address. 
21. **Work Address 2**: Enter the client's work address 2. 
22. **Work City**: Enter the client's work city. 
23. **Work State**: Enter the client's work state. 
24. **Work Zip**: Enter the client's work zip code. 
25. **Emergency Contact**: Enter the emergency contact person's name and relationship.
