# Family Service Center Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic information from individuals seeking services at the Family Service Center. The data collected will help our organization better understand the needs and requirements of our clients, enabling us to provide more effective and personalized support.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your name and contact information, including your first and last name, email address (if applicable), and phone number.
3. Select the type of service you are seeking, such as Family Support, Child Support, Parenting, or Other.
4. Indicate your family status and any other relevant demographic information.
5. If applicable, select your participant's characteristics, including age range, gender, race, ethnicity, income range, employment status, education level, marital status, county of residence, language preference, and any other relevant details.
6. Once you have filled out all the required fields, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1) (`First Name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents or as you would like to be known.
* **Last Name** (2) (`Last Name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents or as you would like to be known.
* **Email** (3) (`Email`, `email`, optional): If you would like to be contacted via email, enter a valid email address. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Phone** (4) (`Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted via phone. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Birth Date** (5) (`Birth Date`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth, if applicable.
* **Service Type** (6) (`Service Type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of service you are seeking, such as Family Support, Child Support, Parenting, or Other.
* **Family Status** (7) (`Family Status`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate your family status, such as Married, Single, Widowed, Divorced, or Separated.
* **Service Frequency** (8) (`Service Frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the frequency of the service you are seeking, such as Weekly, Biweekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually.
* **Notes** (9) (`Notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information you would like to provide about your needs or requirements.
* **Assigned Tool** (10) (`Assigned Tool`, `select_one`, optional): If applicable, indicate whether you have been assigned a specific tool or resource.
* **Family Size** (11) (`Family Size`, `number`, required): Enter the number of people in your family.
* **Family Income** (12) (`Family Income`, `number`, required): Enter your family's annual income.
* **Participant Count** (13) (`Participant Count`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of participants in your family who will be receiving services.
* **Participant Age** (14) (`Participant Age`, `number`, optional): Enter the age of the participant(s) for whom you are seeking services.
* **Participant Age Range** (15) (`Participant Age Range`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the age range of the participants, such as 0-5, 6-12, 13-18, 19-24, etc.
* **Participant Gender** (16) (`Participant Gender`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the gender(s) of the participant(s), such as Male, Female, Other.
* **Participant Race** (17) (`Participant Race`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the race(s) of the participant(s), such as White, Black or African American, Asian, etc.
* **Participant Ethnicity** (18) (`Participant Ethnicity`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the ethnicity(s) of the participant(s), such as Not Hispanic or Latino, Hispanic or Latino.
* **Participant Income Range** (19) (`Participant Income Range`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the income range of the participant(s), such as Less than $20,000, $20,000 - $34,999, etc.
* **Participant Employment Status** (20) (`Participant Employment Status`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the employment status of the participant(s), such as Working, Unemployed, Retired, etc.
* **Participant Education** (21) (`Participant Education`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the education level of the participant(s), such as Less than High School, High School Grad, Some College, etc.
* **Participant Marital Status** (22) (`Participant Marital Status`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the marital status of the participant(s), such as Never Married, Married, Widowed, etc.
* **Participant Lived In County** (23) (`Participant Lived In County`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the county where the participant(s) reside, such as Alameda, Contra Costa, San Francisco, etc.
* **Participant Language** (24) (`Participant Language`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the language(s) spoken by the participant(s), such as English, Spanish, Other Language.

## Tips

* Ensure that you fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Be as accurate and complete as possible when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support staff for assistance.
* The information collected through this form will help us better serve you and other clients.
