# Client Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Client Registration" form is used to collect essential information from clients for account management, billing, or marketing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by entering your personal information in the "Personal Information" section.
   a. Enter your First Name.
   b. Enter your Last Name.
   c. Enter your Primary contact email address (with your domain if possible).
   d. Enter your phone number (with your country code if international).
2. Provide your business information in the "Business Information" section.
   a. Enter your company's name.
   b. Describe your type of business.
   c. Enter your job position in the company.
   d. If applicable, enter your company's website.
3. Provide your billing and shipping addresses in the "Billing Address" and "Shipping Address" sections.
   a. Enter your street address for both billing and shipping (if same, select "Yes" in the "Shipping address is the same as billing address" field).
   b. Enter your city for both billing and shipping.
   c. Enter your state or province for both billing and shipping.
   d. Enter your ZIP or postal code for both billing and shipping.
   e. Enter your country for both billing and shipping.
4. If you have any special requirements or notes, please enter them in the "Special Requirements or Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email address (with your domain if possible).
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number with your country code (if international).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): If you want to provide your date of birth, please enter it in the format of "mm/dd/yyyy".
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's name (organization or business name).
* **Type of Business** (`business_type`, text, required): Describe what your company does.
* **Your Position in the Company** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job position in the company.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your company's website.
* **Street Address** (`billing_street`, text, required): Enter your billing street address.
* **City** (`billing_city`, text, required): Enter your billing city.
* **State or Province** (`billing_state`, text, required): Enter your billing state or province.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`billing_zip`, text, required): Enter your billing ZIP or postal code.
* **Country** (`billing_country`, text, required): Enter your billing country.
* **Shipping address is the same as billing address** (`same_as_billing`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if your shipping address is the same as your billing address.
* **Shipping Street** (`shipping_street`, text, optional): If your shipping address is different from your billing address, enter your shipping street address.
* **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, text, optional): If your shipping address is different from your billing address, enter your shipping city.
* **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`, text, optional): If your shipping address is different from your billing address, enter your shipping state or province.
* **Shipping Zip** (`shipping_zip`, text, optional): If your shipping address is different from your billing address, enter your shipping ZIP or postal code.
* **Shipping Country** (`shipping_country`, text, optional): If your shipping address is different from your billing address, enter your shipping country.
* **Special Requirements or Notes** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): If you have any special requirements or notes, enter them here.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out all required fields as they are marked with an asterisk (\*).
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us and we'll be happy to assist you.
