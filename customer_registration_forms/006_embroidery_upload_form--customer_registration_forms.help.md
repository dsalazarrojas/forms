# Embroidery Upload Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Embroidery Upload Form is a customer-facing form designed to collect information from customers who want to upload their embroidery designs for review and approval.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Customer Name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
3. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
4. Select a file to upload in the **File** field.
5. Provide any additional notes about the upload in the **Notes** field.
6. Select the **Upload Type** of your design.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as you want it to be displayed in our records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your upload.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **File** (`file`, text, optional): Upload a file containing your embroidery design. Please ensure the file is in a supported format.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): If you have any additional information about the upload, please enter it here.
* **Upload Type** (`upload_type`, select one, optional): Select the type of upload you want to make, such as "New Design" or "Update Existing Design".
