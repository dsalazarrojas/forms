# Tax Preparation Virtual Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Preparation Virtual Client Intake Form is designed to collect information from new clients to provide personalized tax preparation services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required information for General Information, Tax ID, Name, Email, Phone, and Address.
2. Enter your Zip, City, and State in the respective fields.
3. Select your Education Level, Work Status, Marital Status, and Tax Filing Status.
4. Enter your Date of Birth, Social Security Number, Income, Investment Income, and Net Worth.
5. Select your Occupation and Years of Work Experience.
6. Enter your Dependents and Annual Income.
7. Select your Tax Year.
8. Sign the form by filling in the Signature field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information**: Fill in your basic contact information.
* **Tax ID (id: 2)**: Enter your Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) or Individual Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN).
* **Name (id: 3)**: Enter your full name.
* **Email (id: 4)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (id: 5)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address (id: 6)**: Enter your home address.
* **Zip (id: 7)**: Enter your zip code.
* **City (id: 8)**: Enter the city where you live.
* **State (id: 9)**: Enter the state where you reside.
* **Country (id: 10)**: Enter the country where you reside.
* **Date of Birth (id: 11)**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Social Security Number (id: 12)**: Enter your Social Security Number.
* **Income Tax Identification Number (id: 13)**: Enter your Income Tax Identification Number (ITIN).
* **Occupation (id: 14)**: Select your occupation.
* **Years of Work Experience (id: 15)**: Enter the number of years you have been working.
* **Education Level (id: 16)**: Select your education level (High or Low).
* **Work Status (id: 17)**: Select your work status (Active or Inactive).
* **Marital Status (id: 18)**: Select your marital status (Active or Inactive).
* **Dependents (id: 19)**: Enter the number of dependents you have.
* **Annual Income (id: 20)**: Enter your annual income.
* **Annual Investment Income (id: 21)**: Enter your annual investment income.
* **Net Worth (id: 22)**: Enter your net worth.
* **Tax Filing Status (id: 23)**: Select your tax filing status (Active or Inactive).
* **Tax Year (id: 24)**: Enter the tax year you are preparing.
* **Signature (id: 25)**: Sign your name to confirm your identity.
