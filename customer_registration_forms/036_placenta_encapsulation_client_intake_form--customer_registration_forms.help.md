# Placenta Encapsulation Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients interested in placenta encapsulation services, ensuring that our staff is well-prepared to provide personalized care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Client Information section with your details.
2. Provide the necessary information in the Birth Information section.
3. If applicable, answer the questions in the Encapsulation Information section.
4. If necessary, add any relevant Medical Information.
5. Enter your payment preferences in the Payment Information section.
6. Add any Additional Information that is not already covered in the previous sections.
7. Review your entries and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information (Client Information, text, required)**: This section requires your basic contact information, including your name, email, and phone number. Please fill in your details accurately for us to get in touch with you.
* **Birth Information (Birth Information, text, required)**: Please provide details about your birth, including the date, time, and any relevant medical information from your pregnancy or delivery.
* **Encapsulation Information (Encapsulation Information, text, optional)**: If you have any specific requirements or preferences for the encapsulation service, please describe them here.
* **Medical Information (Medical Information, text, optional)**: If you have any medical conditions or concerns that may affect your placenta encapsulation, please share them with us here.
* **Payment Information (Payment Information, text, optional)**: In this section, you can choose your payment preference and provide any additional payment information.
* **Additional Information (Additional Information, note, optional)**: If you have any other information that is not covered in the previous sections, please add it here for our reference.
