# Marketing Agents Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for marketing agents to register with their information, which will then be used to create a profile in our system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your Agency Name in the "Agency Name" field.
6. Enter your Address in the "Address" field.
7. Enter your City in the "City" field.
8. Select your State from the "State" dropdown menu.
9. Select your Country from the "Country" dropdown menu.
10. Optionally, enter any notes or additional details in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Agency Name**: Enter the name of your marketing agency.
* **Address**: Enter your work address.
* **City**: Enter the name of the city where you work.
* **State**: Select your state of residence.
* **Country**: Select your country of residence.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or details about your registration.
* **Agent Status**: Select your current status as a marketing agent ("active" or "inactive").
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool you are currently using (if applicable).
* **Assigned Agent**: Select if you have been assigned an agent.
* **Created**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited.
* **Updated**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited.
* **Created By**: Enter the name of the user who created this profile.
* **Updated By**: Enter the name of the user who updated this profile.
* **Created At**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited.
* **Updated At**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited.
* **Status**: Select the current status of your profile ("active" or "inactive").
* **Active Status**: Select your active status ("active" or "inactive").
* **Inactive Status**: Select your inactive status ("inactive" or "active").
* **Active Status 0**: Select your active status ("active" or "inactive").
* **Inactive Status 0**: Select your inactive status ("inactive" or "active").
* **Created At Time**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited.

Note: The "Status" and "Active Status" fields are used to track the current status of your profile, while the "Inactive Status" and "Active Status 0" fields are used for historical tracking purposes. The "Created At Time" field is populated with the time the profile was created.
