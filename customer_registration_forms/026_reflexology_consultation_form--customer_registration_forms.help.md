# Reflexology Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a client's health status and medical history in relation to reflexology consultations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Carefully read each section and field to ensure you understand its purpose and relevance.
2. Fill in the form with accurate and complete information.
3. Be thorough in your responses, as this information will help the reflexologist provide the best possible care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Health Problem** (`client_health_problem`, text, optional):
  This field is for the client to describe their health problem or concern.
* **Client Medical History** (`client_medical_history`, text, optional):
  This field is for the client to provide their medical history, if any.
* **Reflexologist Experience** (`reflexologist_experience`, text, optional):
  This field is for the client to describe their experience with reflexology.
* **Medical Certificate** (`medical_certificate`, select_one, optional):
  This field is for the client to select which medical certificate they have.
* **Doctor Recommended** (`doctor_recommended`, text, optional):
  This field is for the client to report if their doctor has recommended reflexology.
* **Doctor Recommendation** (`doctor_recommendation`, select_multiple, optional):
  This field is for the client to report any doctor recommendations.
* **Client Pain Level** (`client_pain_level`, text, optional):
  This field is for the client to report their pain level.
* **Client Diet** (`client_diet`, select_one, optional):
  This field is for the client to select their diet.
* **Therapist Note** (`therapist_note`, note, optional):
  This field is for the therapist to add any notes.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, select_one, optional):
  This field is for the client to report their medical condition.
* **Medical Certificate Issued By** (`medical_certificate_issued_by`, select_one, optional):
  This field is for the client to report who issued their medical certificate.
* **Medical Certificate Issue Date** (`medical_certificate_issue_date`, date, optional):
  This field is for the client to report the date of their medical certificate.
* **Medical Certificate Expiration Date** (`medical_certificate_expiration_date`, date, optional):
  This field is for the client to report the expiration date of their medical certificate.
* **Medical Certificate Number** (`medical_certificate_number`, number, optional):
  This field is for the client to report their medical certificate number.
* **Reflexology Consultation Form Category** (`reflexology_consultation_form_category`, text, optional):
  This field is for the client to report the reflexology consultation form category.
* **Doctor Recommended Reflexologist** (`doctor_recommended_reflexologist`, text, optional):
  This field is for the client to report their doctor's recommended reflexologist.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional):
  This field is for the client to report their medical history.
* **Reflexologist Recommended** (`reflexologist_recommended`, text, optional):
  This field is for the client to report their reflexologist's recommendation.
* **Doctor Recommended Reflexologist Experience** (`doctor_recommended_reflexologist_experience`, text, optional):
  This field is for the client to report their doctor's recommended reflexologist experience.
* **Medical Certificate Relevance** (`medical_certificate_relevance`, text, optional):
  This field is for the client to report the relevance of their medical certificate.
* **Medical Certificate Relevance Description** (`medical_certificate_relevance_description`, text, optional):
  This field is for the client to describe the relevance of their medical certificate.
* **Medical Certificate Relevance Issued By** (`medical_certificate_relevance_issued_by`, text, optional):
  This field is for the client to report who issued their medical certificate relevance.
* **Reflexologist Note** (`reflexologist_note`, note, optional):
  This field is for the reflexologist to add any notes.
* **Doctor Reflexologist Experience** (`doctor_reflexologist_experience`, text, optional):
  This field is for the client to report their doctor's reflexologist experience.
* **Medical Certificate Relevance Description Description** (`medical_certificate_relevance_description_description`, text, optional):
  This field is for the client to describe the relevance of their medical certificate relevance description.

## Tips
* Please be thorough and accurate in your responses to ensure the reflexologist provides the best possible care.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask a medical professional for guidance.
* This form is designed to be completed by the client, so please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
