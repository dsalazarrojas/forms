# Reflexology Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a client's health status and medical history in relation to reflexology consultations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Carefully read each section and field to ensure you understand its purpose and relevance.
2. Fill in the form with accurate and complete information.
3. Be thorough in your responses, as this information will help the reflexologist provide the best possible care.
