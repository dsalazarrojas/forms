# Market Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Market Registration Form is a tool to collect customer and business information for market registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name, email, and any other relevant customer information on the "Customer Info" page.
2. Enter your business details on the "Business Info" page.
3. Share your contact information on the "Contact Info" page.
4. Review and select the terms and conditions that apply to your registration on the "Terms Conditions" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Info (1)
* **Customer Info (customer_info)** (`text`, optional): This field is intended for customer name, email, and other contact details. You may enter any relevant customer information here.

### Business Info (2)
* **Business Info (business_info)** (`text`, optional): This field is meant for your business name, address, and other business details. You can enter any relevant business information here.

### Contact Info (3)
* **Contact Info (contact_info)** (`text`, optional): This field is for sharing your contact name, email, and phone number. You can enter any relevant contact information here.

### Terms and Conditions (4)
* **Terms and Conditions (terms_conditions)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Please select all applicable terms and conditions for your market registration. You can choose from the options provided.
