# Prospective Travel Client Vacation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prospective Travel Client Vacation Form is designed to gather essential information about prospective travel clients. This information will be used to provide personalized travel experiences and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, address, and contact information (fields 1, 2, and 3).
2. Indicate your travel agency affiliation (field 5).
3. Select whether or not you are affiliated with a travel agent (field 6).
4. Enter your travel dates (fields 7 and 8).
5. Choose your travel preferences (field 9).
6. Enter your desired destination (field 10).
7. If applicable, add any special requests (field 11).
8. Add any comments about your travel plans (field 12).
9. Select whether or not you are assigned a travel agent (field 13).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Address** (2, text, required: false): Enter your home or work address.
* **Email** (3, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (4, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Travel Agency** (5, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you are affiliated with a travel agency.
* **Travel Agent** (6, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you are assigned a travel agent.
* **Departure Date** (7, date, required: false): Enter your departure date.
* **Return Date** (8, date, required: false): Enter your return date.
* **Travel Type** (9, select_multiple, required: false): Choose your travel preferences (e.g., leisure, business, adventure).
* **Destination** (10, text, required: false): Enter your desired destination.
* **Special Requests** (11, note, required: false): Add any special requests or requirements for your travel plans.
* **Comments** (12, note, required: false): Add any additional comments about your travel plans.
* **Assigned Agent** (13, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you are assigned a travel agent for your trip.

Note: Please fill in the required fields to ensure that we can provide you with the best possible travel experience.
