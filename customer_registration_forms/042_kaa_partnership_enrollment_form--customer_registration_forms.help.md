# Kaa Partership Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Kaa Partership Enrollment Form is designed for partners to register their details for the partnership program. To complete this form, please follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the required and optional fields as needed. You can navigate through the different pages using the arrows at the top of the page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: This field is for your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: This field is for your last name. Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: This field is for your email address. Please enter a valid email address that you have access to.
* **Phone**: This field is for your phone number. Please enter your phone number as it appears on your official documents.
* **Enrollment Date**: This field is not required. Please enter the date you would like to be enrolled in the program.
* **Role**: This field is to select your role in the partnership program. Please choose from the options provided:
	+ Administrator
	+ Agent
	+ Affiliate
	+ Ministry
* **Ministry**: This field is not required. Please enter the name of your ministry if applicable.
* **Website**: This field is not required. Please enter your website URL if you have one.
* **Message**: This field is not required. Please enter any additional information you would like to provide to the partnership team.
* **Partner Name**: This field is not required. Please enter your name as it will appear in the partnership program.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact the partnership team.
* Please note that the partnership program has specific rules and regulations. By submitting this form, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
