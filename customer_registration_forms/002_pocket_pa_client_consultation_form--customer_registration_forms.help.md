# Pocket PA Client Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Pocket PA Client Consultation Form" is a registration form for new clients. It is used to gather contact information, select services, and schedule appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and fill out the required information accurately.
2. Fill out the "New Client" page with your basic contact information.
3. On the "Contact Details" page, select the contact methods you prefer to use for communication.
4. On the "Services" page, select the services you require from the list provided.
5. On the "Appointment" page, select a date and time for your appointment.
6. On the "Preferred Contact" page, select your preferred method of communication.
7. On the "Appointment Time" page, select the desired time for your appointment.
8. On the "Services Description" page, provide a brief description of the services you require.
9. Finally, on the "Additional Notes" page, provide any additional information you would like to include.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### New Client
* **New Client** (Number, Required): This page is for you to enter your basic contact information.
* **First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select your first name, last name, email address, and phone number from the dropdown list.
* **Services** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the services you require from the list provided.

### Contact Details
* **First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone** (Select Multiple, Required): Select your contact information from the dropdown list.
* **Services** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select any additional services you require from the list provided.

### Appointment
* **Date** (Date, Required): Select a date for your appointment from the calendar.

### Preferred Contact
* **Phone, Email, In Person** (Select One, Optional): Select your preferred method of communication.
* **Appointment Time** (Time, Optional): Select the desired time for your appointment.
* **Services Description** (Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of the services you require.
* **Additional Notes** (Note, Optional): Add any additional information you would like to include about your appointment.
