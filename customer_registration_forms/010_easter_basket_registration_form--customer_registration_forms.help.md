# easter_basket_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Easter basket registration form is used to register a new non-profit organization for the Easter basket program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information for the form before starting.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review the form for any errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school or organization.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the student.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required): Select the grade level of the student (Elementary, Middle School, or High School).
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number for the organization.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the contact email address for the organization.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the student (if applicable).
* **City** (`student_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the student (if applicable).
* **State** (`student_state`, text, optional): Enter the state of the student (if applicable).
* **Zip** (`student_zip`, number, optional): Enter the zip code of the student (if applicable).
* **School City** (`school_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the school (if applicable).
* **School State** (`school_state`, text, optional): Enter the state of the school (if applicable).
* **School Zip** (`school_zip`, number, optional): Enter the zip code of the school (if applicable).
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the school (if applicable).
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the school (if applicable).
* **Non Profit Organization Name** (`non_profit_organization_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the non-profit organization.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the school (if applicable).
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Non Profit Organization Address** (`non_profit_organization_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the non-profit organization.
* **Non Profit Organization City** (`non_profit_organization_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the non-profit organization.
* **Non Profit Organization State** (`non_profit_organization_state`, text, optional): Enter the state of the non-profit organization.
* **Non Profit Organization Phone** (`non_profit_organization_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the non-profit organization.
* **Non Profit Organization Email** (`non_profit_organization_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the non-profit organization.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the registration.

Review the form carefully before submitting and ensure all required fields are filled out.
