# BossyBabe Brand Ambassador Application - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "BossyBabe Brand Ambassador Application" form is designed for individuals interested in becoming a representative or promoter of the BossyBabe brand. By filling out this form, you will be sharing your personal and professional information to help us determine your suitability for this role.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your **Applicant name** in the text field. This is your full legal name.
2.  Enter your **Contact email** in the email field. This is your best email address for contact.
3.  If you have a **phone number**, enter it in the text field. This can be your mobile or primary phone number.
4.  If you have **Social media handles**, list them in the text field. Please provide the names of the platforms and your usernames.
5.  If you have a **summary of your relevant experience**, enter it in the text field. Please try to be brief but provide enough detail.
6.  Answer the question "Why interested" in a text field, explaining why you want to represent the BossyBabe brand.
7.  If you have a specific **availability schedule**, enter it in the text field, including your typical hours and time zone.
8.  If you have a **portfolio or work samples**, share links to them in the text field. This can include any relevant work or press.
9.  Finally, confirm your acceptance of the **Terms Agreement** by selecting "I agree" or "I do not agree" from the dropdown list. This is a required field.
10.  In the last text field, enter any **additional comments or questions** you might have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Applicant name**: This is your full legal name. Please enter it accurately.
*   **Contact email**: This is the best email address for us to contact you for further information or discussions.
*   **Phone number**: If you have a phone number, this is where you should enter it. It can be your mobile or primary phone number. Note that this is not required, but it can be helpful for us to contact you if needed.
*   **Social media handles**: If you have profiles on social media platforms, list them here along with your usernames. This will help us understand your online presence.
*   **Experience summary**: This is your opportunity to briefly explain your relevant experience that makes you a suitable candidate for this role.
*   **Why interested**: Here, please clearly state why you are interested in representing the BossyBabe brand and what you can bring to this role.
*   **Availability**: If you have a regular schedule or time zone you are available for work, please share this with us.
*   **Portfolio links**: If you have any work or press samples that demonstrate your skills or suitability, share links to them here.
*   **Terms agreement**: This is a confirmation that you understand and accept the terms of being a brand ambassador. Please select "I agree" if you understand and accept these terms.
*   **Additional comments**: Finally, if you have any other information or questions, this is the field to share them.
