# Animal Care Consultant Certification Application - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Animal Care Consultant Certification Application form is used by individuals to apply for certification as animal care consultants. This certification demonstrates expertise in providing care services, consulting, or educating others about animal care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your legal name as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter your preferred email address for application correspondence.
3. If applicable, provide your phone number with country code.
4. If applicable, provide your current organization or affiliation.
5. Enter the number of whole years you have experience in animal care.
6. Select all areas of expertise that apply to your experience.
7. Briefly describe your goals and motivations for seeking certification.
8. List any relevant certifications or training you have received.
9. Provide contact information and name of two professional references.
10. Consent to background checks as part of the certification process.
11. If applicable, provide a link to your portfolio or resume.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant full name (text, name, required):** Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact email (email, contact_email, required):** Provide your preferred email address for application correspondence.
* **Phone number (text, phone_number, optional):** If you are outside of the local area, include your country code.
* **Current organization or affiliation (text, current_organization, optional):** If you are affiliated with an organization or company, provide the name and details here.
* **Years of animal care experience (number, years_experience, required):** Enter the total number of years you have experience in animal care.
* **Areas of expertise (select multiple, areas_of_expertise, required):** Select all areas of expertise that apply to your experience.
	+ Shelter operations
	+ Medical care
	+ Animal behavior
	+ Rehabilitation
	+ Education and outreach
* **Certification goals and motivation (text, certification_goals, required):** Briefly describe why you are seeking certification.
* **Relevant certifications or training (text, relevant_certifications, optional):** List any relevant certifications or training you have received.
* **Professional references (text, references, required):** Provide the name and contact information of two professional references.
* **Background check consent (select one, background_check_consent, required):** Consent to background checks as part of the certification process.
* **Portfolio or resume link (text, portfolio_or_resume, optional):** If applicable, provide a URL to your portfolio or resume.
* **Signature name (text, signature_name, required):** Enter your full name as an electronic signature.

## Tips

* Be accurate and honest in your application to ensure the best possible outcome.
* Ensure that you have the necessary contact information and documents required for your application.
* Proofread your application carefully before submitting to avoid errors and ensure it is complete.
