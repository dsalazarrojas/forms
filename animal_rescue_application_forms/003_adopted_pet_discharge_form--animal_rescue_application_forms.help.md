# Adopted Pet Discharge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Adopted Pet Discharge Form is a mandatory document for all adopted pets. It provides a comprehensive overview of the pet's history, care requirements, and medical information to the adopter, ensuring they are well-equipped to provide the best possible care. 

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out the Adopted Pet Discharge Form, follow these steps:

1. **Adoption Date**: The date the pet was adopted. Enter the date you took ownership of the pet.
2. **Pet Name**: Enter the name given to the pet.
3. **Species**: Select the type of animal (e.g., Dog, Cat, Rabbit).
4. **Age**: Enter the age of the pet at adoption.
5. **Weight**: Enter the current weight of the pet.
6. **Microchip Number**: Enter the identification microchip number if applicable.
7. **New Owner Name**: Enter the full name of the adopting party.
8. **Owner Email Address**: Enter the email address for follow-up communication.
9. **Owner Phone Number**: Enter the contact phone number.
10. **Medical History**: Provide a summary of any known medical conditions.
11. **Current Vaccinations**: List any completed vaccinations.
12. **Last Veterinary Visit**: Enter the date and purpose of the last veterinary visit.
13. **Current Medications**: Enter any medications the pet is taking.
14. **Behavioral Notes**: Record any important behavioral information.
15. **Current Diet**: Enter the type of food and feeding schedule.
16. **Special Care Instructions**: Provide any specific care requirements.
17. **Spay - Neuter Status**: Select whether the pet has been spayed or neutered.
18. **Shelter Contact Person**: Enter the name of the staff member who released the pet.
19. **Follow-up Check**: If the shelter would like to follow up, mark 'True'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Adoption Date**: The date the pet was adopted. Required for documentation and follow up.
* **Pet Name**: Enter the name given to the pet. This will be used for identification and address labeling.
* **Species**: Select the type of animal (e.g., Dog, Cat, Rabbit). This is essential for providing species-specific care and advice.
* **Age**: Enter the age of the pet at adoption. This will help the adopter understand the pet's developmental stage.
* **Weight**: Enter the current weight of the pet. This is helpful for monitoring weight gain or loss.
* **Microchip Number**: Enter the identification microchip number if applicable. This ensures easy identification and return of the pet if lost.
* **New Owner Name**: Enter the full name of the adopting party. This is for contact and follow-up purposes.
* **Owner Email Address**: Enter the email address for follow-up communication. This ensures the adopter can be reached for any updates or concerns.
* **Owner Phone Number**: Enter the contact phone number. This is for immediate contact in case of emergencies.
* **Medical History**: Provide a summary of any known medical conditions. This ensures the adopter is aware of any potential health concerns.
* **Current Vaccinations**: List any completed vaccinations. This is for monitoring and follow-up care.
* **Last Veterinary Visit**: Enter the date and purpose of the last veterinary visit. This provides a record for future visits.
* **Current Medications**: Enter any medications the pet is taking. This is for safe and effective administration.
* **Behavioral Notes**: Record any important behavioral information. This helps the adopter understand and address any behavioral concerns.
* **Current Diet**: Enter the type of food and feeding schedule. This ensures the adopter provides optimal nutrition.
* **Special Care Instructions**: Provide any specific care requirements. This ensures the adopter understands unique needs.
* **Spay - Neuter Status**: Select whether the pet has been spayed or neutered. This is for reproductive health management.
* **Shelter Contact Person**: Enter the name of the staff member who released the pet. This is for contact and follow-up purposes.
* **Follow-up Check**: If the shelter would like to follow up, mark 'True'. This ensures the adopter is available for future contact.
