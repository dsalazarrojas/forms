# Dog Foster Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Dog Foster Application Form** is designed to help our organization find the best match for a dog in need. By filling out this form, you will be providing us with essential information about yourself and your household. This information will help us decide if our dog is a good fit for you and your family.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **dog_foster_application_form** field with a brief introduction or personal statement.
2. Provide your **personal_info** with your contact information and any other relevant details.
3. Tell us about the dog you are interested in fostering or adopting, including its **dog_info**.
4. Share details about your **household_info** to help us understand your living situation.
5. If you have any health or physical conditions that may impact your ability to care for a dog, please describe them in the **physical_conditions** field.
6. Share your **contact_info** so we can reach out to you.
7. If you are interested in adopting a dog, please select the **applicant_1** field to indicate who will be taking care of the dog.
8. Let us know how you would like to be contacted (phone, email, or chat) in the **contact_method** field.
9. Finally, provide any **additional_info** you would like to share about yourself or your household.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **dog_foster_application_form**: A brief introduction or personal statement about your interest in fostering or adopting a dog. Please keep it short and concise.
* **personal_info**: A field to provide your contact information and any other relevant details about yourself and your household.
* **dog_info**: A field to provide details about the dog you are interested in fostering or adopting.
* **household_info**: A field to share details about your living situation, including any other people or pets living in the household.
* **physical_conditions**: If you have any health or physical conditions that may impact your ability to care for a dog, please describe them here.
* **contact_info**: A field to provide your contact information so we can reach out to you.
* **applicant_1**: Select the type of individual who will be taking care of the dog (Dog Owner, Foster Parent, Volunteer, or Other). If you select "Other", please provide a description in the **applicant_1_other** field.
* **applicant_1_other**: A field to provide a description of who will be taking care of the dog if you select "Other".
* **contact_method**: A field to choose how you would like to be contacted (phone, email, or chat).
* **contact_details**: A field to provide any additional contact details, such as a contact method or email address.
* **additional_info**: A field to provide any additional information about yourself or your household that may be helpful for us to know.
