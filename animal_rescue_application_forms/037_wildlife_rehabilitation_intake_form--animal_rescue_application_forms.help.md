# Wildlife Rehabilitation Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about wildlife rescued animals, ensuring their proper care and rehabilitation. Please fill out this form with accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the animal species from the provided options that best matches the rescued animal.
2. Choose the status of the animal at the time of the rescue.
3. Provide the date of the rescue event.
4. Specify the number of species being submitted.
5. Enter a detailed description of the rescue event.
6. List any medical conditions affecting the animal.
7. Outline the treatment plans required for the animal's care.
8. Select the rehabilitation facility responsible for caring for the animal.
9. Enter any additional notes about the submission.
10. Provide the submitter's information: name, email, and phone number.
11. Enter contact information for the submitter: email, phone number, name, and address.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Animal Species (<select_multiple>)**: Select all the species rescued in the event. This can be multiple options as some animals may be of multiple species (e.g., a raccoon and a possum).
* **Animal Status (<select_one>)**: Choose the correct status of the animal at the time of the rescue (e.g., Injured, Orphaned, Dead on arrival, or Other).
* **Date of the Rescue Event (<date>)**: Enter the date of the rescue event in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Number of Species (<number>)**: Provide the total number of species rescued in the event.
* **Description of the Event (<text>)**: Enter a detailed description of what happened during the rescue event.
* **Medical Conditions (<text>)**: List any medical conditions affecting the animal.
* **Treatment Plans (<text>)**: Outline the necessary treatment plans for the animal's care.
* **Rehabilitation Facility (<select_one>)**: Select the facility where the animal will be taken for rehabilitation.
* **Notes (<text>)**: Enter any additional information that may be helpful for the rehabilitation process.
* **Submitter Information (<text>)**: Enter your name, email, and phone number.
* **Contact Email (<email>)**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Contact Phone Number (<text>)**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **Contact Name (<text>)**: Enter your name.
* **Organization (<text>)**: If you represent an organization, enter its name.
* **Address (<text>)**: Enter the address of the person or organization submitting the form.
* **City (<text>)**: Enter the city of the submitter's address.
* **State (<text>)**: Enter the state of the submitter's address.
* **Zip (<text>)**: Enter the zip code of the submitter's address.
* **Fax Number (<text>)**: Enter the fax number of the submitter.
* **Website (<text>)**: Enter the website of the person or organization submitting the form.
