# Animal Foster Agreement Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Foster Agreement Renewal Form is a document that allows foster parents to renew their foster care agreement with the shelter. This form is used to update information, confirm continued care, and ensure that the foster parent is still committed to providing quality care for the animals in their charge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. **Foster Parent Full Name**: Enter your full name as a foster parent.
2. **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address for communication with the shelter staff.
3. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for emergency contact.
4. **Home Address**: Enter your street address, city, state, and zip code.
5. **Current Agreement Information**: Provide a brief description of your current foster care agreement, including the start and end dates.
6. **Foster Parent ID Number**: Enter your foster parent ID number assigned by the shelter.
7. **Current Agreement Start and End Dates**: Enter the dates of your current agreement.
8. **How Many Animals Have You Fostered**: Select the number of animals you currently care for.
9. **Are You Currently Fostering Animals**: Select your current status as a foster parent.
10. **Type of Renewal**: Choose the type of renewal that best describes your situation.
11. **Are You Changing Your Foster Capacity**: Select if you are increasing or decreasing your foster capacity.
12. **New Foster Capacity**: If changing your capacity, select the new number of animals you can care for.
13. **Species You Are Willing to Foster**: Select all species you are willing to foster.
14. **Have There Been Changes to Your Home Situation**: Select if you have made changes to your living situation.
15. **Have There Been Changes to Your Household**: Select if you have added or removed household members.
16. **Have You Added New Pets**: Select if you have added new pets to your household.
17. **Has Your Veterinarian Changed**: Select if you have changed veterinarians.
18. **Current Veterinarian Name**: Enter your current veterinarian's name.
19. **Veterinarian Phone**: Enter your current veterinarian's phone number.
20. **Experience as a Foster Parent**: Rate your experience as a foster parent.
21. **What Challenges Did You Face**: Describe any difficulties you have faced as a foster parent.
22. **Additional Support Needed**: Describe any additional support you require.
23. **Have You Completed Required Training**: Confirm if you have completed required training.
24. **Are You Willing to Update Background Check**: Confirm if you are willing to update your background check.
25. **Have You Read the Updated Agreement Terms**: Confirm if you have reviewed the updated agreement terms.
26. **Commitment to Providing Quality Foster Care**: Confirm your commitment to providing quality care.
27. **Renewal Period**: Select the duration of your renewed agreement.
28. **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about your situation.
29. **Foster Parent Signature**: Sign your name to confirm your commitment.
30. **Date**: Enter the date of your signature.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth renewal process.

* This form is confidential and for the use of the shelter staff.
