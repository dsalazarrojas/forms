# Hairy Buns Adoption Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hairy Buns Adoption Application form is designed to gather essential information from individuals interested in adopting a pet. This form is a crucial step in the adoption process, as it helps us understand the applicant's background, lifestyle, and preferences to find the best match for the animal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your application information, including your name and contact details.
2. Select the type of animal you are interested in adopting (bunnies, hamsters, or kittens).
3. Enter your address and phone number so we can contact you.
4. Enter your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
5. Confirm that you will care for the animal you are adopting.
6. Review and agree to the adoption terms.
7. If applicable, select additional animals you have adopted or are interested in adopting.
8. Add any additional notes or comments about your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Information** (User Input, text): This field is where you provide your name and contact details, including address and phone number.
* **Name** (Text): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on the adoption certificate.
* **Address** (Text): Enter your address where the animal will be living with you.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for any further information.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Animal Type** (Select One): Choose the type of animal you are interested in adopting from the options: bunnies, hamsters, or kittens.
* **Adoption Date** (Date): Although not required, providing your available adoption date can help us plan accordingly.
* **Signature** (Note): This is where you can add any additional comments or questions you have regarding the adoption process.
* **Confirm** (Select One): Please confirm that you will care for the animal by selecting "I confirm that I will care for the animal."
* **Review Agreement** (Select Multiple): Review and confirm that you have read and agree to the adoption terms.
* **Other Animals** (Select Multiple): If applicable, select any additional animals you have adopted or are interested in adopting.
* **Additional Notes** (Note): Add any additional information or questions you have regarding your application.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your application is complete and accurate before submitting.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any section, please don't hesitate to ask.
* The more information you provide, the better we can find a suitable match for you and the animal.
