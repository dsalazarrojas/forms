# Bushfire Supporter Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather information from supporters of our animal welfare efforts during bushfires. We aim to understand who is backing our cause, how they heard about us, and how they would like to contribute or help. Your input will help us tailor our outreach and support programs to better serve you and other animal lovers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your profile information (Fields 2-3, below).
2. Choose how you heard about our bushfire relief efforts (Field 4).
3. Share what motivates you to support animal welfare (Field 6).
4. Select how you'd like to support us (Field 7).
5. Indicate if you have any previous volunteering experience (Field 8).
6. Choose any specialized skills you have (Field 9).
7. Decide on your contribution frequency (Field 10).
8. Join our emergency alert mailing list (Field 11).
9. Finally, submit your survey (Field 12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`supporter_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as you'd like to be recognized as a supporter.
- **Email Address** (`supporter_email`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive updates and emergency alerts.
- **How did you hear about our bushfire relief efforts** (`referral_source`, select_one, required): Select how you discovered us:
	+ Social Media
	+ News / Radio
	+ Word of Mouth
	+ Email Newsletter
	+ Community Event
- **What motivates you to support animal welfare during bushfires** (`motivation_statement`, text, required): Share a brief statement on what drives you to support our cause.
- **Primary Way You Prefer to Support** (`preferred_support_type`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to support us:
	+ Monetary Donations
	+ On-the-ground Volunteering
	+ Animal Fostering
	+ Resource/Supply Donations
	+ Awareness/Social Media Advocacy
- **Have you volunteered for animal rescue before** (`previous_volunteer_history`, select_one, required): Indicate if you've volunteered for animal rescue:
	+ Yes, frequently
	+ Yes, once or twice
	+ No, but I want to start
- **Do you have any of the following specialized skills** (`relevant_skills_selection`, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant skills you possess:
	+ Veterinary/Medical Training
	+ Wildlife Handling Experience
	+ Driving (Truck/Heavy Vehicle)
	+ Logistics / Coordination
	+ Search and Rescue Training
- **How often would you like to contribute or help** (`support_frequency_intent`, select_one, required): Choose how often you'd like to contribute:
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Only during active emergencies
	+ One-time contribution
- **Would you like to join our emergency alert mailing list** (`mailing_list_opt_in`, select_one, required): Select to join our mailing list:
	+ Yes, sign me up
	+ No, thank you
- **Survey Date** (`survey_completion_date`, date, required): This will be automatically filled in by the system, so don't worry about this field.
