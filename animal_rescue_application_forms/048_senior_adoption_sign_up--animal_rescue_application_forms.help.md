# Senior Adoption Sign Up - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help individuals or organizations who are interested in adopting a senior pet from a local animal rescue organization. The purpose of this form is to collect essential information from applicants to ensure a smooth and successful adoption process. It's crucial to be honest and provide accurate details to increase the chances of being matched with a senior pet that suits your lifestyle and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form successfully:

1. Fill in the `Application Form` page with your contact details and information about yourself.
2. Provide your `Contact Details` on the subsequent page, including your name, phone number, and email address.
3. Fill out the `Home Info` page with information about your living situation, such as the number of people in your household, the type of home you live in, and any other relevant details.
4. On the `Senior Pet Info` page, provide information about the senior pet you're interested in adopting, including the breed, age, and any special needs or requirements you're looking for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Application Form (Page 1)**
	* **Title (text)**: Enter a brief title or name that will be used to identify your application.
	* **Application Reason (text)**: Explain why you're interested in adopting a senior pet and what qualities you're looking for in a pet.
- **Contact Details (Page 2)**
	* **Name (text)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or driver's license.
	* **Phone Number (text)**: Provide a valid phone number where you can be reached.
	* **Email Address (text)**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
- **Home Info (Page 3)**
	* **Household Size (text)**: Describe the number of people living in your household, including children or pets.
	* **Home Type (text)**: Indicate the type of home you live in (e.g., apartment, house, condo).
- **Senior Pet Info (Page 4)**
	* **Breed (text)**: Select the breed of senior pet you're interested in adopting.
	* **Age Range (text)**: Specify the age range of the senior pet you're interested in adopting.
	* **Special Needs (text)**: Check off any special needs or requirements you're looking for in a senior pet (e.g., mobility issues, requires medication, specific dietary needs).

## Tips

* Be honest and provide accurate information to increase the chances of being matched with a senior pet that suits your lifestyle and preferences.
* If you're unsure about any section, please contact us for guidance.
* We recommend reviewing the form carefully before submitting it to ensure everything is accurate and complete.
