# LYR AdoptionFoster Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for prospective adopters or foster parents to provide information about themselves and their household in order to be considered for adopting or fostering a pet from a local animal rescue group. The goal is to ensure that both the animal and the applicant are matched well and have a successful adoption or fostering experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your information.
2. Select the type of animal you are interested in adopting or fostering.
3. Choose your preferred adoption or fostering option (Adopt or Foster).
4. Consider the space and type of animal you are able to provide a home for.
5. Provide any additional information about your household and your experience with animals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **rescue_group_name (Text)**: Enter the name of the animal rescue group you are working with.
* **address (Text)**: Provide the address where the animal would be living.
* **contact_email (Email, Required: false)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **contact_phone (Text)**: Provide your phone number where you can be reached.
* **desired_animal_type (Select One)**: Choose the type of animal you are interested in adopting or fostering (cat or dog).
* **foster_or_adopt (Select One)**: Choose between Adopt or Foster.
* **foster_experiences (Select Multiple)**: Indicate if you have previous experiences with fostering or adopting animals.
* **foster_home (Select One)**: Indicate if you have a foster home available.
* **foster_type (Select One)**: Choose the type of foster home you are able to provide (Feline, Canine, or Both).
* **foster_size (Select One)**: Choose the size of foster home you are able to provide (Small, Medium, Large, or Extra Large).
* **foster_gender (Select One)**: Choose the gender of the animal you are interested in fostering (Male or Female).
* **foster_age (Select One)**: Choose the age of the animal you are interested in fostering (0-2, 2-5, or 5-10).
* **foster_status (Select One)**: Choose the status of the animal you are interested in fostering (Active or Retired).
* **adoption_info (Select One)**: Indicate if you are okay with spayed/neutered or not.
* **vet_info (Select One)**: Indicate if you have any veterinary information about the animal (True or False).
* **microchip_info (Select One)**: Indicate if the animal you are interested in has a microchip (True or False).
* **other_info (Text)**: Provide any additional information about your experience with animals.
* **signature (Note)**: Sign this section to confirm that you have accurately filled out the form.
* **date (Date)**: Date on which you submit this application.
* **time (Time)**: Time on which you submit this application.
* **zip (Text)**: Enter your zip code.
* **city (Text)**: Enter your city name.
* **state (Text)**: Enter your state name.
* **notes (Text)**: Provide any additional comments or questions you have about the application process.
* **submit (Text)**: Once you have filled out all the fields, click this button to submit the form.

This form is intended to be a straightforward guide for users to complete and provide the necessary information for the animal rescue group. Please answer the questions truthfully and accurately to facilitate a smooth and successful adoption or fostering process.
