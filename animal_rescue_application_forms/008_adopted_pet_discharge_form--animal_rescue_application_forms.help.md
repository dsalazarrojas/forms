# Adopted Pet Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from adopters about their adopted pets. It will help our organization ensure that we have up-to-date information about the pet's characteristics, medical history, and care requirements, as well as the adopter's contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Adoption Date** field with the date the pet was adopted.
2. Select the **Type of Pet** that best describes the pet (Dog, Cat, Rabbit, Hamster, Bird, Reptile, or Other).
3. Enter the pet's **Name** as it appears in the adoption contract.
4. Provide a description of the pet's **Color and Markings**.
5. Select the pet's **Gender** (Male or Female).
6. Enter the pet's **Weight** (including units).
7. If the pet has a microchip, enter the **Microchip Number**.
8. Enter the adopter's **Full Name**.
9. Provide the adopter's **Phone Number**.
10. Enter the adopter's **Email Address**.
11. Enter the adopter's **Home Address**.
12. If you would like us to contact you in two weeks, select **Yes, please contact me**.
13. For the remaining sections, please provide any relevant information about the pet's **Medical History and Care Instructions**, **Vaccinations Administered**, **Microchip Registered**, **Known Medical Conditions**, **Current Medications or Treatments**, **Feeding Instructions and Diet**, **Behavioral Notes and Temperament**, and **Special Care Requirements**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Adoption Date**: The date the pet was adopted from our organization.
* **Pet Information**: A brief description of the pet's characteristics, including its type, name, and any notable features.
* **Pet Name**: The name of the pet as it appears in the adoption contract.
* **Type of Pet**: The category of the pet (Dog, Cat, Rabbit, Hamster, Bird, Reptile, or Other).
* **Breed**: The specific breed of the pet (if known).
* **Age**: The age of the pet.
* **Color and Markings**: A description of the pet's coloring and distinctive features (e.g., scars, tattoos, or birthmarks).
* **Gender**: The sex of the pet (Male or Female).
* **Weight**: The weight of the pet (including units, e.g., pounds or kilograms).
* **Microchip Number**: The identification number of the pet's microchip (if applicable).
* **Adopter Information**: A note section for the adopter to provide any additional information about themselves.
* **Adopter Full Name**: The full name of the person adopting the pet.
* **Phone Number**: The phone number of the adopter.
* **Email Address**: The email address of the adopter.
* **Home Address**: The address of the adopter's home.
* **Medical History and Care Instructions**: A section for the adopter to provide any relevant information about the pet's medical history and care requirements.
* **Vaccinations Administered**: A list of vaccinations the pet has received.
* **Microchip Registered**: Whether the pet's microchip is registered or not.
* **Known Medical Conditions**: Any known medical conditions the pet has.
* **Current Medications or Treatments**: Any current medications or treatments the pet is receiving.
* **Feeding Instructions and Diet**: A description of the pet's feeding schedule and dietary requirements.
* **Behavioral Notes and Temperament**: A description of the pet's personality and behavioral traits.
* **Special Care Requirements**: Any special care requirements the pet has.
* **Follow-up Contact**: Whether you would like us to contact you in two weeks to check on the pet's well-being.
