# Pet Insurance Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Insurance Claim Form is used to file claims for medical expenses related to your pet's treatment. This form is designed to gather information about your pet, the medical condition, and the expenses submitted for reimbursement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are essential for processing your claim.
2. Provide accurate and truthful information in all fields.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
4. Make sure to attach any supporting documentation or receipts as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Pet Name** (\*)
* **Pet Name**: This is the name of your pet.
	+ Enter the name of your pet as it is registered with us.
	+ Make sure to use the correct spelling and capitalization.

### **Pet Type**
* **Pet Type**: Select the type of pet you are claiming for.
	+ Choose from the options: Cat, Dog, or Other.

### **Vet Bills Submitted**
* **Vet Bills Submitted**: This is where you will enter the date of when you submitted bills for your pet's medical expenses.
	+ If you have not submitted bills, select "N/A" or "Not Applicable".

### **Medical Condition**
* **Medical Condition**: This is a field for you to describe the medical condition your pet is experiencing.
	+ Provide a brief description of the condition, including any relevant details.

### **Medical Condition Description**
* **Medical Condition Description**: This is a field for you to elaborate on the medical condition your pet is experiencing.
	+ Provide as much detail as possible, including any relevant medical history or test results.

### **Medical Expenses Submitted**
* **Medical Expenses Submitted**: This is where you will enter the total amount of medical expenses submitted for reimbursement.
	+ Enter the exact amount, including any taxes or fees.

### **Veterinary Clinic**
* **Veterinary Clinic**: This is the name of the veterinary clinic where your pet received treatment.
	+ Enter the exact name of the clinic, as provided on your receipt or invoice.

### **Veterinary Clinic Address**
* **Veterinary Clinic Address**: This is the address of the veterinary clinic where your pet received treatment.
	+ Enter the exact address of the clinic, as provided on your receipt or invoice.

### **Medical Expense Date**
* **Medical Expense Date**: This is the date of when you submitted bills for your pet's medical expenses.
	+ If you have not submitted bills, select "N/A" or "Not Applicable".

### **Medical Expense Amount**
* **Medical Expense Amount**: This is where you will enter the total amount of medical expenses submitted for reimbursement.
	+ Enter the exact amount, including any taxes or fees.

### **Claim Amount**
* **Claim Amount**: This is the amount you are claiming for reimbursement.
	+ Enter the exact amount, including any taxes or fees.

### **Claim Reason**
* **Claim Reason**: This is a field for you to provide a brief reason for your claim.
	+ Describe the reason for your claim, including any relevant details.

### **Claim Status**
* **Claim Status**: Select the status of your claim.
	+ Choose from the options: Open, Closed, Pending.

### **Claim Date**
* **Claim Date**: This is the date of when your claim was submitted.
	+ If you have not submitted a claim, select "N/A" or "Not Applicable".

### **Claim Reimbursed**
* **Claim Reimbursed**: Select whether your claim has been reimbursed.
	+ Choose from the options: Yes, No.

### **Claim Reimbursed Reason**
* **Claim Reimbursed Reason**: This is a field for you to provide a brief reason for your claim being reimbursed or not.
	+ Describe the reason, including any relevant details.

### **Claim Reimbursed Amount**
* **Claim Reimbursed Amount**: This is the amount you were reimbursed for your claim.
	+ Enter the exact amount, including any taxes or fees.

### **Claim Reimbursed Date**
* **Claim Reimbursed Date**: This is the date when you were reimbursed for your claim.
	+ Enter the exact date, if applicable.

### **Claim Reimbursed Status**
* **Claim Reimbursed Status**: Select the status of your claim reimbursement.
	+ Choose from the options: Active, Inactive.

### **Claim Reimbursed Notes**
* **Claim Reimbursed Notes**: This is a field for you to provide any additional notes about your claim reimbursement.
	+ Enter any relevant details or comments.

### **Claim Reimbursed Veterinary Clinic**
* **Claim Reimbursed Veterinary Clinic**: This is the name of the veterinary clinic where your pet received treatment.
	+ Enter the exact name of the clinic, as provided on your receipt or invoice.

### **Claim Reimbursed Veterinary Clinic Address**
* **Claim Reimbursed Veterinary Clinic Address**: This is the address of the veterinary clinic where your pet received treatment.
	+ Enter the exact address of the clinic, as provided on your receipt or invoice.

### **Claim Reimbursed Veterinary Clinic Phone**
* **Claim Reimbursed Veterinary Clinic Phone**: This is the phone number of the veterinary clinic where your pet received treatment.
	+ Enter the exact phone number, as provided on your receipt or invoice.

### **Claim Reimbursed Veterinary Clinic Note**
* **Claim Reimbursed Veterinary Clinic Note**: This is a field for you to provide any additional notes about your claim reimbursement.
	+ Enter any relevant details or comments.
