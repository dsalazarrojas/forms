# Pet Safety Awareness Event Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Safety Awareness Event Consent Form is designed to collect critical information about a pet's safety and well-being for a specific event. This form is used to ensure that the pet's needs are considered and that the owner or caregiver has given permission for the pet to participate in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review each section carefully to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
* Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* Make sure to provide all required information for each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details (Page 1)**: 
  * Enter a brief description of the event and its date and time.
* **Pet Information (Page 2)**: 
  * Provide the pet's name, species, age, and any medical conditions.
* **Emergency Contact Information (Page 3)**: 
  * Provide the contact information (name, phone number, and address) for at least one emergency contact person.
* **Consent (Page 4)**: 
  * Select all options that apply to the pet's safety and well-being during the event.
* **Risks (Page 5)**: 
  * Describe any potential risks to the pet's safety, such as allergies or medical conditions.
* **Event Host Information (Page 6)**: 
  * Provide the name and contact information for the event host.
* **Pet Care (Page 7)**: 
  * Describe how the pet will be taken care of during the event (e.g., with food, water, and shelter).
* **Pet Behavior (Page 8)**: 
  * Describe the pet's behavior, such as training, habits, and any behavioral issues.
* **Pet Health (Page 9)**: 
  * Describe the pet's health, including any medical conditions or allergies.
* **Veterinarian Information (Page 10)**: 
  * Provide the name and contact information for the pet's veterinarian.
* **Emergency Contact (Page 11)**: 
  * Provide at least one emergency contact person's name, phone number, and address.
* **Pet Safety Measures (Page 12)**: 
  * Describe any measures taken to ensure the pet's safety, such as vaccination records or emergency equipment.
* **Pet Medication (Page 13)**: 
  * Describe any medication the pet is currently taking.
* **Pet Equipment (Page 14)**: 
  * Describe any equipment used by the pet, such as a harness or leash.
* **Pet Housing (Page 15)**: 
  * Describe the pet's living arrangements during the event.
* **Pet Training (Page 16)**: 
  * Describe the pet's training history, including any obedience training or behavioral modification.
* **Pet Behavior History (Page 17)**: 
  * Describe the pet's behavioral history, including any behavioral issues or patterns.
* **Pet Health History (Page 18)**: 
  * Describe the pet's health history, including any medical conditions or treatments.
* **Medical Condition (Page 19)**: 
  * Describe any medical conditions that may impact the pet's safety.
* **Medical Treatment (Page 20)**: 
  * Describe any medical treatments the pet is currently receiving.
* **Pet Allergies (Page 21)**: 
  * Describe any allergies the pet has.
* **Medical Allergies (Page 22)**: 
  * Describe any medical allergies the owner or caregiver has.
* **Emergency Contact Relation (Page 23)**: 
  * Describe the relationship between the emergency contact person and the pet owner or caregiver.
* **Pet Adopted (Page 24)**: 
  * Check if the pet has been adopted.
* **Event Host Information (Page 25)**: 
  * Provide the name and contact information for the event host.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check all fields before submitting the form.
* Provide clear and accurate information about the pet's safety and well-being.
* If you're unsure about any information, please ask for help or clarification.
