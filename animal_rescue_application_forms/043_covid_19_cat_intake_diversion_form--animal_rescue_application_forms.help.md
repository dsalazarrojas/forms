# COVID 19 Cat Intake Diversion Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The COVID 19 Cat Intake Diversion Form is a document used to collect information from individuals surrendering their cat due to the challenges posed by the COVID-19 pandemic. This form helps the animal rescue organization assess the situation and provide assistance or alternative solutions to ensure the cat's well-being.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal contact information:
    *   Enter your full name, email address, and phone number.
2.  Provide your address:
    *   Enter your complete address, including city, state, and ZIP code.
3.  Enter information about the cat you are surrendering:
    *   Provide the date of surrender, cat's name, age, gender, breed, color, and vaccination status.
    *   Describe the cat's health status, any health issues, and behavior.
4.  Choose the reason for surrender:
    *   Select the reason for surrender from the provided options.
5.  Discuss diversion options:
    *   Select alternative solutions that you are interested in exploring, such as pet food assistance, veterinary care, or temporary foster care.
6.  Confirm your surrender decision:
    *   Indicate if you want the cat returned or if you are willing to surrender it permanently.
7.  Pay the surrender fee (if applicable):
    *   Choose to pay the surrender fee, waive it, or set up a payment plan.
8.  Provide medical records (if applicable):
    *   Confirm if you are providing veterinary records or if none are available.
9.  Confirm supplies provided:
    *   Select which supplies (food, litter, etc.) are included with the cat.
10. Provide any additional notes:
    *   Enter any further information about the cat or your situation.
11. Sign and date the form:
    *   Sign and date the form with your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Owner Name (`owner_name`, text, required):** Enter your full legal name.
*   **Email Address (`email_address`, email, required):** Provide an email address for communication.
*   **Phone Number (`phone_number`, text, required):** Enter your best contact phone number.
*   **Street Address (`address`, text, required):** Enter your complete address.
*   **City (`city`, text, required):** Enter the city of your residence.
*   **State (`state`, text, required):** Enter the state or province.
*   **ZIP Code (`zip_code`, text, required):** Enter the postal code.
*   **Intake Date (`intake_date`, date, required):** Enter the date of surrender.
*   **Cat Name (`cat_name`, text, required):** Enter the cat's name.
*   **Cat Age (`cat_age`, number, required):** Enter the cat's age in years.
*   **Cat Gender (`cat_gender`, select_one, required):** Select the cat's gender (Male, Female, or Unknown).
*   **Cat Breed (`cat_breed`, text, optional):** If known, enter the cat's breed.
*   **Cat Color (`cat_color`, text, required):** Enter the primary color of the cat.
*   **Spayed or Neutered (`spayed_neutered`, select_one, required):** Select the cat's spayed or neutered status (True, False, or Unknown).
*   **Vaccination Status (`vaccination_status`, select_one, required):** Select the cat's current vaccination status (Yes - Current, No - Overdue, Never vaccinated, or Unknown).
*   **Microchipped (`microchipped`, select_one, required):** Select if the cat has an ID chip implanted (True, False, or Unknown).
*   **Microchip Number (`microchip_number`, text, optional):** If known, enter the microchip number.
*   **Current Health Status (`health_status`, select_one, required):** Select the cat's current health status (Healthy, Minor issues, Chronic condition, Serious illness, or Unknown).
*   **Health Details (`health_details`, text, optional):** Describe any health conditions.
*   **Behavior and Temperament (`behavior_temperament`, select_one, required):** Select the cat's behavior and temperament (Friendly and social, Shy but friendly, Independent, Aggressive or fearful, or Unknown).
*   **Good with Children (`good_with_children`, select_one, optional):** Select if the cat is good with children (True, False, or Unknown).
*   **Good with Other Cats (`good_with_other_cats`, select_one, optional):** Select if the cat is good with other cats (True, False, or Unknown).
*   **Good with Dogs (`good_with_dogs`, select_one, optional):** Select if the cat is good with dogs (True, False, or Unknown).
*   **Litter Box Trained (`litter_box_trained`, select_one, required):** Select if the cat is house-trained (True, False, or Sometimes).
*   **Indoor or Outdoor (`indoor_outdoor`, select_one, required):** Select where the cat lives (Indoor only, Outdoor only, or Both).
*   **Reason for Surrender (`reason_for_surrender`, select_one, required):** Select the reason for surrender (Housing issues, Financial constraints, Allergies, Behavior problems, etc.).
*   **Surrender Details (`surrender_details`, text, required):** Enter a brief description of the reason for surrender.
*   **Diversion Options (`diversion_options`, select_multiple, required):** Select alternative solutions you are interested in exploring.
*   **Owner Wants Return (`owner_wants_return`, select_one, required):** Indicate if you want the cat returned (Yes - Temporary, No - Permanent surrender, or Unsure).
*   **Surrender Fee (`surrender_fee`, select_one, optional):** Select if you are paying the surrender fee, waiving it, or setting up a payment plan.
*   **Medical Records Provided (`medical_records_provided`, select_one, required):** Confirm if you are providing veterinary records or if none are available.
*   **Supplies Included (`supplies_included`, select_multiple, optional):** Select which supplies (food, litter, etc.) are included with the cat.
*   **Additional Notes (`additional_notes`, text, optional):** Enter any further information about the cat or your situation.
*   **Owner Signature (`owner_signature`, text, required):** Sign the form with your full name.
*   **Staff Name (`staff_name`, text, optional):** Enter the intake coordinator's name (if different from yours).
*   **Staff Signature (`staff_signature`, text, optional):** Enter the intake coordinator's name (if different from yours).
*   **Date Completed (`date_completed`, date, required):** Date and time the form is completed.

## Tips

*   Please fill out the form accurately and honestly to ensure the best possible outcome for the cat.
*   If you are unable to complete the form, please contact us for assistance.
*   We appreciate your cooperation and understanding in this challenging time for our community.
*   We are committed to supporting you and your pet in any way we can.
*   Thank you for your understanding and cooperation.
