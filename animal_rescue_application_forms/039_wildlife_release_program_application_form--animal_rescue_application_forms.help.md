# Wildlife Release Program Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wildlife Release Program Application Form is for individuals or organizations seeking to release animals that are under care or custody into the wild.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with detailed and accurate information.
2. Ensure that you're submitting a new and original application. If you've already submitted an application, please do not resubmit.
3. Be as thorough as possible with the animal's information, including species, age, weight, length, and any distinguishing features.
4. Provide accurate contact information, including name, email, phone number, and address.
5. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wildlife Release Request**: A brief description of the animal and the reason for the release. This is a text field where you can provide a brief explanation of the animal and the circumstances surrounding the release.
* **Animal Type**: Select 'Yes' if the animal is a wild animal, 'No' if it's a domesticated animal. This helps us understand the animal's species and habitat requirements.
* **Shelter Information**: Provide details about the animal's shelter or enclosure, including any relevant details about its size, materials, and location.
* **Conservation Group**: If applicable, provide the name and contact information of the conservation group or organization involved in the release.
* **Release Date**: Enter the date you plan to release the animal. This helps us coordinate the release schedule and ensure it's not conflicting with other releases.
* **Release Time**: Enter the time you plan to release the animal. This helps us ensure a safe and efficient release process.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or details about the animal, release location, or release process.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name and title (if applicable) as the person responsible for this application.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address where we can reach you for further questions or updates.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you for further questions or updates.
* **Contact Message**: Add any additional comments or questions you have for us.
* **Conservation Group**: If applicable, provide the name and contact information of the conservation group or organization involved in the release.
* **Animal Species**: Provide the species of the animal, including any subspecies or breed.
* **Animal Age**: Enter the age of the animal.
* **Release Location**: Provide the exact location where you plan to release the animal.
* **Veterinary Care**: Provide details about the animal's veterinary care, including any relevant medical records or treatment.
* **Medical Condition**: Report any known medical conditions or health issues with the animal.
* **Veterinary Records**: Provide veterinary records or reports relevant to the animal's health or condition.
* **Contact Address**: Enter your physical address where we can reach you for further questions or updates.
* **Animal Weight**: Enter the weight of the animal, if applicable.
* **Animal Length**: Enter the length of the animal, if applicable.
* **Distinguishing Features**: Describe any distinguishing features of the animal, such as color, shape, or pattern.
* **Animal Diet**: Provide details about the animal's diet, including what it eats and any dietary restrictions.
* **Special Instructions**: Add any special instructions or requirements for the release, such as specific release procedures or equipment needed.
