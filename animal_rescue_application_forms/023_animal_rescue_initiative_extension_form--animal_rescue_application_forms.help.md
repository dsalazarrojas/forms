# Animal Rescue Initiative Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from potential foster families about their ability and willingness to care for animals in need.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields as thoroughly as possible.
2. Make sure to select all relevant options for each field.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Full Name** (`applicant_full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number so we can contact you for further information.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Please enter your email address so we can contact you for further information.
* **Home Type** (`home_type`, select_one, required): Please select the type of home you live in, such as House, Apartment or Condo, Townhouse, Farm or Rural Property, or Other.
* **Yard or Outdoor Space Type** (`outdoor_space`, select_one, required): Please select the type of outdoor space you have available, such as Fully Fenced Yard, Partially Fenced Yard, Unfenced Yard, etc.
* **Current Pets in Home** (`current_pets`, text, optional): Please list any pets you currently have in your home, including species and ages.
* **Previous Experience with Rescues or Fostering** (`rescue_experience`, text, optional): If applicable, please describe your previous experience with rescuing or fostering animals.
* **Preferred Animal Species for Fostering** (`foster_species_preference`, select_multiple, required): Please select the animal species you are willing to foster, such as Dogs, Cats, etc.
* **Maximum Number of Foster Animals at One Time** (`max_foster_capacity`, number, required): Please select the maximum number of animals you are able to care for at a time.
* **Availability for Emergency or Short-term Fostering** (`emergency_foster_availability`, select_one, required): Please select whether you are available for emergency or short-term fostering.
* **Agreement to Initial Home Inspection** (`inspection_agreement`, select_one, required): Please select whether you agree to an initial home inspection.
