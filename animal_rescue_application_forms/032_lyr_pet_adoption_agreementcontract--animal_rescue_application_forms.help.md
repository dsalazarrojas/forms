# lyr_pet_adoption_agreementcontract - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for pet adoption purposes. It's designed to capture essential details about the adoption process, pet history, and care requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the required fields marked as `required: true` with accurate and complete information.
2.  For fields with no specified hints, you can enter any additional information you think is relevant to the adoption process.
3.  Review your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Adoption Information (<name>: Adoption Information, <type>: text, required)**: Enter the name(s) of the pet you are interested in adopting and any additional information about the adoption process.
* **Adoption History and Health (<name>: Adoption History and Health, <type>: text, required)**: Provide any relevant history about the pet, including medical conditions or previous living situations.
* **Care Requirements (<name>: Care Requirements, <type>: text, required)**: Describe the environment and living arrangements you can provide for the pet, including space, nutrition, and socialization needs.
* **Contract Agreement (<name>: Contract Agreement, <type>: text, required)**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the adoption, including any specific requirements or responsibilities.
* **Rescue Information (<name>: Rescue Information, <type>: text, required)**: Enter any relevant information about the rescue organization or individual providing the pet, including their contact details.
* **Additional Information (<name>: Additional Information, <type>: text, required)**: Provide any other relevant details not covered by previous fields, such as your personal or professional experience caring for animals.
 
## Tips
- Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
- Ensure you have the necessary resources and space for the pet's care and well-being.
- Consider attaching supporting documents, such as veterinary records or behavioral assessments, to provide a more comprehensive understanding of the pet's needs and history.
