# Animal Control Contractor Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Animal Control Contractor Application form is designed to gather information about your background, services offered, and experience in working with government agencies. This information will be used to evaluate your suitability for working with the city or local government.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your full name, company name, email address, phone number, and business address.
2. Select your business type and years in operation.
3. Provide your business license number and the state where it was issued.
4. Indicate whether you have liability insurance and provide the relevant details if applicable.
5. List the services you offer, including animal capture and removal, wildlife management, pet rescue, and more.
6. Specify your geographic service area and availability.
7. Choose your typical response time and the number of trained staff you have on board.
8. Select any relevant staff certifications and equipment you have available.
9. Share your experience working with government agencies, if applicable.
10. Provide details about any previous contracts you have worked on.
11. List your professional references, including names and contact information.
12. Indicate whether you have a standard pricing structure.
13. Confirm your willingness to undergo background checks.
14. Add any additional information that may be relevant to your application.
15. Sign and date the form to confirm your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Person Full Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Your full name as the primary contact person for your business.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Your business name as it appears on your licenses and other official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Your business email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Your business phone number for contact purposes.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, required): Your business address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Animal Control Contractor Application** (`application_intro`, note, required): A brief introduction to your application and the services you offer.
* **Type of Business Entity** (`business_type`, select_one, required): Your business structure, such as sole proprietorship, partnership, LLC, corporation, non-profit, or other.
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, select_one, required): The number of years your business has been operating.
* **Business License Number** (`business_license`, text, required): Your business license number as issued by the state or local government.
* **State of Issuance** (`license_state`, text, required): The state where your business license was issued.
* **Insurance Information** (`insurance_info`, note, required): A note section for you to provide details about your insurance coverage.
* **Do You Have Liability Insurance?** (`liability_insurance`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have liability insurance.
* **Insurance Carrier Name** (`insurance_carrier`, text, optional): If you have liability insurance, provide the name of the insurance carrier.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, text, optional): If you have liability insurance, provide the policy number.
* **Coverage Amount** (`coverage_amount`, text, optional): If you have liability insurance, provide the coverage amount.
* **Services You Offer** (`services_offered`, select_multiple, required): A list of services you offer, including animal capture and removal, wildlife management, pet rescue, and more.
* **Geographic Service Area** (`service_area`, text, required): The area where you operate and provide services.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_one, required): Your availability, such as 24/7, business hours only, or extended hours.
* **Typical Response Time** (`response_time`, select_one, required): The time it takes you to respond to a call or request.
* **Number of Trained Staff** (`staff_count`, select_one, required): The number of trained staff you have on board.
* **Staff Certifications** (`certifications`, select_multiple, optional): A list of certifications your staff members have, such as animal control officer, wildlife rehabilitation, and humane handling.
* **Equipment Available** (`equipment`, select_multiple, optional): A list of equipment you have available, such as capture poles, live traps, and transport vehicles.
* **Have You Worked with Government Agencies Before?** (`experience_government`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have worked with government agencies before.
* **Describe Previous Relevant Contracts** (`contract_examples`, text, optional): If you have worked with government agencies, provide details about your previous contracts.
* **Professional References** (`references`, text, required): A list of professional references, including names and contact information.
* **Do You Have a Standard Pricing Structure?** (`pricing_structure`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have a standard pricing structure.
* **Are You Willing to Undergo Background Checks?** (`background_check`, select_one, required): Confirm your willingness to undergo background checks.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Any additional information you would like to provide.
* **Applicant Signature** (`applicant_signature`, text, required): Your full name as the applicant.
* **Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): The date you sign and submit the application.
