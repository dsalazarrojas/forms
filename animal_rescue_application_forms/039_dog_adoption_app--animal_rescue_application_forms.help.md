# Dog Adoption App - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Adoption App is a form designed to aid in the dog adoption process by gathering relevant information from both the adopter and the potential adopted dog.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please provide the following information in the fields below: 
- Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Dog Information (Dog Info)** (`dog_info`, text, required: false)
	+ Provide a brief description of the dog you are interested in adopting, including breed, age, size, and any other relevant details.
- **Adopter Information (Adopter Info)** (`adopter_info`, text, required: false)
	+ Share information about yourself, including name, address, contact details, and any other pertinent information.
- **Contact Details (Contact Info)** (`contact_details`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter any additional contact details, such as email address or phone numbers, where we can reach you.
- **Pet Details (Pet Info)** (`pet_details`, text, required: false)
	+ Provide additional information about the dog you are adopting, including personality traits, special needs, and any other relevant details.

## Tips
- Be as thorough as possible when filling out the form to ensure we can best understand your needs.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
- Once you submit the form, we will review it and contact you with further instructions on the adoption process.
