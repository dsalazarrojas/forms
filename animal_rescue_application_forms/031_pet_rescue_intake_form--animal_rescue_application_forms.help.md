# Pet Rescue Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a pet that needs rescue. It will help the shelter staff to quickly and efficiently intake the pet and its details.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Enter Pet Information

To begin, please enter the following information:

* **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the pet.
* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of pet (e.g., cat, dog, other).
* **Shelter Name** (`shelter_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the shelter where the pet is being taken care of.

### Step 2: Provide Details

* **Description** (`pet_description`, text, required): Describe the pet's condition and any relevant medical information.
* **Pet Condition** (`pet_condition`, select_one, required): Select the pet's condition (e.g., healthy, sick, injured, deceased).
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter a contact number for the pet's owner or caregiver.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter an email address for the pet's owner or caregiver.

### Step 3: Additional Information

* **Rescue Date** (`rescue_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the pet was rescued.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): If applicable, enter any tools or equipment used in the rescue operation.

### Tips

* Please provide as much information as possible about the pet to ensure a smooth transition to its new home.
* If the pet's condition changes or requires special care, please update the information immediately.
* If you need help or assistance, do not hesitate to contact us.

This form is designed to be user-friendly and help you quickly and efficiently provide the necessary information about the pet. Thank you for using it to help make a difference in the life of a deserving animal!
