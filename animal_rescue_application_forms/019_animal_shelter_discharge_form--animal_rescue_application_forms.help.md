# Animal Shelter Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Shelter Discharge Form is used to document the details of an animal's discharge from the shelter. This form helps ensure that the animal's new owner has all the necessary information and instructions for proper care and handling.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Discharge Date**: Enter the date the animal is leaving the shelter.
2. **Staff Member Discharging**: Enter the full name of the staff member processing the discharge.
3. **Animal ID Number**: Enter the shelter's identifier for the animal.
4. **Animal Name**: Enter the name of the animal.
5. **Species**: Select the species of the animal (e.g., Dog, Cat, Rabbit, etc.).
6. **Age**: Enter the age of the animal at the time of discharge.
7. **Sex**: Select the sex of the animal (e.g., Male, Female).
8. **Type of Discharge**: Choose the reason for the animal's discharge (e.g., Adoption, Transfer, Return to Owner, etc.).
9. **Adopter or Recipient Name**: Enter the full name of the person or organization taking custody of the animal.
10. **Adopter Email**: Enter the contact email of the adopter or recipient.
11. **Adopter Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the adopter or recipient.
12. **Adopter Address**: Enter the street address of the adopter or recipient.
13. **City**: Enter the city of the adopter or recipient.
14. **State or Province**: Enter the state or province of the adopter or recipient.
15. **ZIP or Postal Code**: Enter the ZIP or postal code of the adopter or recipient.
16. **Adoption Fee Paid**: If applicable, enter the amount of the adoption fee paid.
17. **Payment Method**: Select the method of payment used (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, Debit Card, etc.).
18. **Medical Records Provided**: Indicate whether medical records were provided to the adopter.
19. **Vaccination Status**: Select the vaccination status of the animal at discharge (e.g., Up to Date, Due Soon, Overdue).
20. **Spay or Neuter Status**: Select the spay or neuter status of the animal (e.g., Altered Before Discharge, Scheduled for Later, etc.).
21. **Microchip Number**: If applicable, enter the microchip number of the animal.
22. **Microchip Registered**: Indicate whether the microchip is registered to the new owner.
23. **Medications Sent**: Indicate whether medications were sent home with the animal.
24. **Medication Details**: If medications were sent, list the details and dosages of the medications.
25. **Behavioral Notes**: Enter any important behavioral notes for the new owner.
26. **Follow-up Scheduled**: Indicate whether follow-up care is scheduled.
27. **Follow-up Date**: If scheduled, enter the date and time of the follow-up.
28. **Post-Adoption Support**: Indicate whether post-adoption support information was provided to the adopter.
29. **Return Policy Explained**: Indicate whether the return policy was explained to the adopter.
30. **Adopter Electronic Signature**: The adopter must sign this form electronically, typing their full name.
31. **Staff Electronic Signature**: The staff member processing the discharge must sign this form electronically, typing their full name.
32. **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional discharge information.
