# Animal Cruelty Investigator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Cruelty Investigator Application Form is designed to gather information from applicants interested in joining the animal welfare team as an investigator. This form helps assess the applicant's qualifications, background, and experience to determine their suitability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Full Name**: Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address where you can be reached for communication.
3. **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number for further communication.
4. **Home Address**: Provide your street address, city, state, and zip code where you reside.
5. **Animal Cruelty Investigator Application**: This section is for you to write a brief introduction about why you're interested in this position and how you can contribute to the animal welfare team.
6. **Position Type**: Select the type of employment you're interested in: Full-time investigator, Part-time investigator, Volunteer investigator, Reserve officer, or Intern.
7. **Available Start Date**: Provide the date you can start the position.
8. **Salary Expectations**: If applicable, enter your expected compensation for the role.
9. **Experience Section**: Briefly describe your relevant background and experience.
10. **Years of Investigative Experience**: Select the total number of years you've had experience as an investigator.
11. **Law Enforcement Background**: Check if you have any law enforcement experience (Yes - sworn officer, Yes - civilian staff, Yes - military police, or No).
12. **Experience in Animal Welfare Field**: Describe your relevant experience in the animal welfare field.
13. **Education Section**: Briefly describe your educational background, including the highest level of education you've achieved and the field of study.
14. **Highest Level of Education**: Select your highest education level (High school or equivalent, Some college, Associate degree, Bachelor's degree, Master's degree or higher).
15. **Field of Study**: Enter your major or field of study.
16. **Relevant Coursework or Training**: List any relevant classes, certifications, or training you've undergone.
17. **Skills and Qualifications**: List all the skills and qualifications that apply to you:
	* Crime scene documentation
	* Evidence collection
	* Interview techniques
	* Report writing
	* Case management
	* Legal procedures
	* Surveillance
18. **Animal Handling Skills**: Select all the skills you have related to animal handling:
	* Safe restraint techniques
	* Behavior assessment
	* Species identification
	* Health assessment
	* Transport procedures
	* Humane euthanasia certified
19. **Legal Knowledge**: Select the level of your knowledge of animal protection laws (Extensive - can cite statutes, Moderate - familiar with laws, Basic - general awareness, Limited - need training).
20. **Relevant Certifications**: List any relevant certifications you hold.
21. **Employment History**: Briefly describe your previous employers, highlighting any relevant experience.
22. **Professional References**: Provide the names and contact information for your professional references.
23. **Physical Requirements**: Check if you have any physical limitations that may impact your ability to perform the role (Yes - no limitations, Yes - with accommodations, No - have limitations).
24. **Schedule Flexibility**: Select your availability for irregular hours (Yes - fully flexible, Yes - with limitations, No - regular hours only).
25. **Travel Ability**: Check if you're willing to travel within the jurisdiction (Yes - no restrictions, Yes - limited area, No - restricted).
26. **Why You're Interested**: Explain why you want this position and how you can contribute to the team.
27. **Stressful Situations**: Describe how you handle stressful situations.
28. **Background Check**: Check if you're willing to undergo a background check (Yes - I consent, No - restricted).
29. **Drug Screening**: Check if you're willing to undergo a drug screening (If required, Yes - I consent, No - restricted).
30. **Additional Information**: Enter any other relevant information that's not covered in other sections.
31. **Applicant Signature**: Sign your name in full.
32. **Date**: Sign today's date.

**Tips**

* Double-check each section to ensure accurate and complete information.
* If unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
* This form is a critical part of the application process, and your answers will be reviewed carefully.
