# Animal Shelter Policy Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from various categories of individuals related to an animal shelter on proposed policy changes. Your input will help inform decision-making processes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out your information:
	* Full Name: Enter your full name as it appears on your official ID.
	* Email Address: Enter your contact email address.
	* Phone Number: Enter your contact phone number (optional).
	* Voter Category: Select your relationship to the shelter (e.g., Shelter Staff, Board Member, Volunteer, etc.).
	* Voter ID or Member Number: If applicable, enter your shelter ID or member number.
2. Review the policy proposals:
	* Each policy proposal is followed by a select-one option for voting (Yes-Approve, No-Reject, Abstain). Select your vote for each proposal.
3. Identify your priority:
	* Select the policy change that is most important to you.
4. Preferred implementation timeline:
	* Choose how quickly you'd like approved policies to be implemented.
5. Share your concerns and suggestions:
	* If you have concerns or suggestions, select "True" and describe them. If you have additional suggestions, select "True" and share them.
6. Sign and date your vote:
	* Type your full name for electronic signature and date of vote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official ID.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number (optional)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Voter Category**: Select your relationship to the shelter.
* **Voter ID or Member Number (if applicable)**: If you're a shelter staff member or have an ID, enter it here.
* **Vote on Extended Visiting Hours**: Vote on the proposal to extend weekend visiting hours from 5 PM to 7 PM.
* **Vote on New Adoption Fee Structure**: Vote on adjusting adoption fees based on age and species.
* **Vote on Volunteer Training Requirements**: Vote on requiring monthly training sessions for all volunteers.
* **Vote on Foster Program Expansion**: Vote on expanding foster program capacity by 50 percent.
* **Vote on Community Outreach Initiative**: Vote on launching monthly community education events.
* **Which Policy Change Is Most Important to You**: Select the policy change that is most important to you.
* **Preferred Implementation Timeline**: Choose how quickly you'd like approved policies to be implemented.
* **Do You Have Any Concerns About These Proposals?**: If you have concerns, select "True" and explain them.
* **Please Describe Your Concerns (if yes)**: Describe your concerns.
* **Do You Have Additional Policy Suggestions?**: If you have suggestions, select "True" and share them.
* **Please Share Your Suggestions (if yes)**: Describe your suggestions.
* **Would You Be Willing to Help Implement Approved Policies?**: Select your willingness to help implement approved policies.
* **Electronic Signature**: Type your full name for electronic signature.
* **Date of Vote**: Enter the date of your vote.
* **I Certify That I Am Eligible to Vote on These Policies**: Select "Yes I Certify" to confirm your eligibility to vote.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and accurately to ensure your feedback is taken into account.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, please share them to help inform decision-making processes.
* This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions; it's not meant for actual voting.
