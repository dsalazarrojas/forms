# Dog Walking Service Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather necessary information about your dog so we can provide a safe and enjoyable experience for them during their walk. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure we can tailor the service to your dog's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and any relevant contact information.
2. Provide information about your dog, including their name, breed, age, and weight.
3. Indicate the date and time you would like the walking service to take place.
4. Click the 'Submit' button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dog Walking Service Intake Form** (`dog_walking_service_intake_form`, text, optional): Enter a brief description or notes about your request.
* **Dog Walking Service Intake Form Owner** (`dog_walking_service_intake_form_owner`, text, optional): Enter your name and contact information so we can reach out to you to confirm your request.
* **Dog Walking Service Intake Form Dog** (`dog_walking_service_intake_form_dog`, text, optional): Enter your dog's name.
* **Dog Walking Service Intake Form Dog Breed** (`dog_walking_service_intake_form_dog_breed`, text, optional): Enter your dog's breed.
* **Dog Walking Service Intake Form Dog Age** (`dog_walking_service_intake_form_dog_age`, text, optional): Enter your dog's age.
* **Dog Walking Service Intake Form Dog Weight** (`dog_walking_service_intake_form_dog_weight`, text, optional): Enter your dog's weight.
* **Dog Walking Service Intake Form Service Date** (`dog_walking_service_intake_form_service_date`, text, optional): Enter the date for the dog walking service.
* **Dog Walking Service Intake Form Service Time** (`dog_walking_service_intake_form_service_time`, text, optional): Enter the time for the dog walking service.

## Tips
Please make sure to enter the correct information so we can provide the best experience for your dog. If you have any special needs or concerns, please let us know in the notes section at the top of the form.
