# Animal Advocates Adoption Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Advocates Adoption Application Form is a critical step in the animal adoption process, designed to collect essential information from potential adopters about their identity, contact details, and household situation to facilitate a successful and stress-free adoption experience for both the animals and the adopters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Enter your contact information (email address and phone number).
3. Provide a valid address (street, city, state, and postal code).
4. Describe your household composition, including adults and children.
5. Share your experience with pets, including past pet ownership and care.
6. Provide names and contact information for any references.
7. Confirm that you agree to the adoption policies and procedures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email address** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for application updates.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your contact phone number for follow-up calls or messages.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your street address, city, state, and postal code.
* **Household composition** (`household`, text, required): Describe your household, including adults and children.
* **Experience with pets** (`experience_with_pets`, text, optional): Share your past experience with pets, including their type, care, and ownership.
* **References** (`references`, text, optional): Provide names and contact information for people who can vouch for your pet care skills.
* **I agree to the adoption policies and checks** (`agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the adoption policies and procedures.
  * Select 'True' to proceed with the adoption application.
  * Select 'False' to withdraw your application.
