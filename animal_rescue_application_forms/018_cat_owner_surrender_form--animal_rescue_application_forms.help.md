# Cat Owner Surrender Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cat Owner Surrender Form is designed to collect information from cat owners who are surrendering their cats to the rescue organization. This form helps the organization understand the circumstances surrounding the cat's surrender and ensures that the owner's rights are transferred properly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Current Owner Information" section with your details.
2. Provide information about your cat, including its name, age, breed, and primary color.
3. Indicate whether the cat is spayed or neutered.
4. Select the primary reason for surrendering the cat.
5. Provide any relevant medical history or behavioral information.
6. Indicate whether you are providing veterinary records with the cat.
7. If applicable, provide microchip information.
8. Review and agree to the legal relinquishment terms.
9. Sign the form electronically.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`owner_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Phone and Email** (`owner_contact`, text, required): Enter your contact information so we can reach you.
* **Cat Name** (`cat_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the cat.
* **Age of Cat** (`cat_age_approx`, text, required): Estimate the age of the cat.
* **Breed and Primary Color** (`breed_color`, text, required): Enter the breed and primary color of the cat.
* **Is the cat spayed or neutered** (`spay_neuter_status`, select_one, required): Select whether the cat is spayed or neutered.
* **Primary Reason for Surrendering** (`main_surrender_reason`, select_one, required): Select the primary reason for surrendering the cat (e.g., financial reasons, behavioral issues, etc.).
* **Does the cat have a history of biting or scratching** (`behavioral_history`, text, required): Provide any relevant behavioral information.
* **Veterinary Records Provided** (`veterinary_records_provided`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you are providing veterinary records with the cat.
* **Is the cat microchipped** (`microchip_info`, text, required): Enter the microchip number if applicable.
* **Legal Relinquishment** (`legal_agreement`, select_one, required): Review and agree to the legal relinquishment terms.
* **Electronic Signature** (`owner_signature`, text, required): Type your full legal name to sign the form electronically.
* **Date of Surrender** (`surrender_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are surrendering the cat.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form to ensure the best possible outcome for your cat.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact the rescue organization for guidance.
* The form is designed to be completed by the cat's owner, so please ensure you are the owner before submitting the form.
