# Bayside Animal Shelter Volunteer Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bayside Animal Shelter Volunteer Application form is designed to gather information about you, your experience, and your availability to volunteer with us. This will help us assess your suitability for various roles at the shelter and ensure a smooth onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* Enter your full name in the **Full Name** field.
	* Enter your age to ensure you are 16 or older to volunteer independently.
2. Provide your contact information:
	* Enter a valid email address in the **Email Address** field.
	* Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
3. Share your experience and interests:
	* Describe your previous experience working with animals in the **Do you have previous experience working with animals?** field.
	* Select all the roles you are interested in helping with in the **Which roles are you interested in?** field.
4. Choose your availability:
	* Select all the days of the week you are available to volunteer in the **Which days of the week are you available?** field.
	* Choose your preferred shift time in the **Preferred Shift Time** field.
5. Confirm your safety and medical details:
	* Answer **True** or **False** to whether you have any allergies to animals or cleaning supplies in the **Do you have any allergies to animals or cleaning supplies?** field.
6. Enter emergency contact information:
	* Enter the name and phone number of a contact person in case of an emergency in the **Emergency Contact Name and Phone** field.
7. Agree to our liability release and shelter rules:
	* Select **I Agree** to confirm you have read and understood the liability release and shelter rules.
8. Finally, submit your application:
	* Enter the date you are submitting your application in the **Date of Application** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Your full name as you would like to be known at the shelter.
* **Your Age**: Your age, please ensure it is 16 or older to volunteer independently.
* **Email Address**: A valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Do you have previous experience working with animals?**: Describe any experience you have working with animals. This could include pet ownership, volunteering, or work experience at another animal shelter.
* **Which roles are you interested in?**: Select all roles you are interested in, such as Dog Walking and Socialization, Cat Care and Socialization, etc.
* **Which days of the week are you available?**: Select all days of the week you are available to volunteer.
* **Preferred Shift Time**: Choose the time of day you prefer to work, such as Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **Do you have any allergies to animals or cleaning supplies?**: Answer **True** or **False** to whether you have any allergies.
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone**: Enter the name and phone number of a contact person in case we need to reach you in an emergency.
* **I agree to the volunteer liability release and shelter rules**: Select **I Agree** to confirm you have read and understood the liability release and shelter rules.
* **Date of Application**: Enter the date you are submitting your application.
