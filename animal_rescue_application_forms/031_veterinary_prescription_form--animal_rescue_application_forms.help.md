# Veterinary Prescription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Prescription Form is used to collect essential information for veterinary prescription purposes. It's a crucial document that helps veterinarians make informed decisions about a pet's treatment and care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of pet you're seeking treatment for (Dog, Cat, or Other).
2.  Fill in the pet's owner's information, including their contact details.
3.  Choose the veterinarian who will be treating your pet (Dr. Smith or Dr. Johnson).
4.  Specify the required treatment details, including any vaccinations, medications, or lab tests.
5.  Add any additional notes about your pet's condition or treatment.
6.  Sign and date the form to confirm your acknowledgement and acceptance of the treatment plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Pet info**: Please select the type of pet you're seeking treatment for (e.g., Dog, Cat, or Other).
*   **Owner info (Owner info)**: Fill in your contact information as the pet's owner, including your name and address.
*   **Vet info**: Select the name of the veterinarian who will be treating your pet.
*   **Treatment info**: Check all that apply to specify the required treatment details (e.g., vaccination, medication, lab test).
*   **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your pet's condition or treatment.
*   **Owner signature**: Sign and date this section to confirm your acknowledgement and acceptance of the treatment plan.
*   **Vet signature**: The veterinarian will sign this section to confirm they've reviewed the form and discussed the treatment details with you.
*   **Date**: Please fill in the date of the appointment or treatment session.
*   **Time**: Enter the time of the appointment or treatment session.
