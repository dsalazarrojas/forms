# Volunteer Animal Rescue Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from volunteers who wish to help with animal rescue efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information about yourself.
2. Review the available options for each field and select the ones that apply to you.
3. Be sure to sign the volunteer agreement and date it to confirm your commitment to the rescue efforts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Information** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This field is intended for you to provide some general information about yourself and your interests in volunteering with the animal rescue efforts. Please provide a brief description of your relevant experience and qualifications.
* **What kind of animal do you rescue?** (`2`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of animal you are interested in rescuing (Dog, Cat, or Other).
* **Rescue Location** (`3`, `text`, required: false): If applicable, enter the location where you would like to participate in the rescue efforts.
* **Volunteer Name** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it should be displayed for the purposes of the rescue efforts.
* **Address** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address to help us understand where you are located.
* **Phone** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you.
* **Email** (`7`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Volunteer Experience** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe your relevant experience with animals or volunteering.
* **Rescue Date** (`9`, `date`, required: false): If you have a specific date in mind for the rescue efforts, you can enter it here.
* **Availability** (`10`, `text`, required: false): Describe your availability to help with the rescue efforts.
* **Availability** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the time slots you are available to help with the rescue efforts (7am-12pm, 12pm-3pm, 3pm-6pm).
* **Availability** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the time slots you are available to help with the rescue efforts (7pm-10pm, 10pm-1am, 1am-4am).
* **Availability** (`13`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the days you are available to help with the rescue efforts (Weekends, Weekdays, Flexible).
* **Volunteer Interest** (`14`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the areas where you are interested in volunteering (Animal Handling, Animal Socialization, Other).
* **Vehicles** (`15`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the type of vehicle you have access to (Car, Truck, Bike).
* **Notes** (`16`, `text`, required: true): Enter any additional comments or information about your experience and qualifications.
* **Volunteer Agreement** (`17`, `note`, required: true): Please read the volunteer agreement and indicate your understanding and acceptance of its terms.
* **Signature** (`18`, `note`, required: false): Sign the volunteer agreement to confirm your understanding and acceptance of its terms.
* **Date** (`19`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you signed the agreement.
* **Date Signed** (`20`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you signed the agreement.
* **Time** (`21`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you signed the agreement.
* **Signed** (`22`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to confirm that you signed the agreement.
* **Signed** (`23`, `select_one`, required: false): Confirm that you signed the agreement (True, False).
* **Signature** (`25`, `note`, required: false): This field is not required.
