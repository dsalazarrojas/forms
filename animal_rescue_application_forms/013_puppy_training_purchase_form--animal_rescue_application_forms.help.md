# Puppy Training Purchase Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Puppy Training Purchase Form is designed to collect information from customers interested in purchasing puppy training services. This form helps us understand their needs and preferences to provide them with the best possible training experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **User ID** field with your name as you want it to be displayed on our records.
2. Enter your **Contact Number** field with your preferred contact number where you can be reached for follow-ups.
3. Provide your **Email** address to ensure we can contact you with updates and information about your training sessions.
4. In the **Dog Information** field, describe your dog's breed, age, size, and any specific needs or concerns you have about your furry friend's training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User ID**: A unique identifier for our records, please enter your name as you'd like it to be displayed.
* **Contact Number**: A phone number or alternative contact method where you can be reached by our team.
* **Email**: Your email address for us to communicate with you about your training sessions and any updates.
* **Dog Information**: A brief description of your dog's breed, age, size, and any specific needs or concerns you'd like us to address during the training session.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate contact information so we can reach you efficiently.
* Be as detailed as possible in the **Dog Information** field to help us understand your dog's needs.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, feel free to ask our support team after submitting the form.
