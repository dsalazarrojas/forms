# Pet Rescue Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Rescue Time Off Form is a form used by staff members to request time off for pet rescue activities. This form allows staff to provide essential information about their time off request, including the staff member requesting time off, the start and end dates of the time off, and the reason for the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the staff member requesting time off from the "Staff Requesting" field.
2. Choose the staff ID from the dropdown list.
3. Enter the start date for which you are requesting time off.
4. Enter the end date for which you are requesting time off.
5. Provide a brief reason for your time off request in the "Reason" field.
6. If necessary, add any additional comments in the "Comment" field.
7. Finally, select the "Submit" option that best describes your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Requesting** (`staff_requesting`, select_one, required: false): Select the staff member who is requesting time off.
* **Staff ID** (`staff_id`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the staff ID from the provided list.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date from which you are requesting time off.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date for which you are requesting time off.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, required: false): Provide a brief reason for your time off request.
* **Comment** (`comment`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or information related to your time off request.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required: false): Select the reason for your time off request.
