# Animal Rescue Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Animal Rescue Intake Form" is used to collect essential information about animals that come into the rescue facility. This data helps the rescue staff to better understand the animal's history, medical status, and behavior, which facilitates their care and adoption process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information for each field.
2. Ensure that you answer each question thoroughly and accurately.
3. Review your answers for completeness and consistency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Date of Intake (1)
**Date of Intake**: Enter the date the animal arrived at the rescue facility.

### Animal Name - if known or temporary (2)
**Animal Name**: If the animal's name is known, enter it here. If not, enter a temporary name or describe how you came up with a temporary name for the animal.

### Found or Surrender Location (3)
**Found or Surrender Location**: Enter the street address or crossroads where the animal was found or surrendered.

### Species and Estimated Breed (4)
**Species and Estimated Breed**: Describe the animal's species (e.g., dog, cat, etc.) and estimated breed or mix (e.g., Labrador Retriever Mix).

### Approximate Age or Life Stage (5)
**Approximate Age or Life Stage**: Estimate the animal's age or describe its life stage (e.g., puppy, adult, senior).

### Animal Gender (6)
**Animal Gender**: Choose from the options: Male, Female, or Unknown - Not yet examined.

### Microchip Status (7)
**Microchip Status**: Choose from the options: Microchip Found, No Microchip Found, or Needs Scanning or Check.

### Initial Temperament Observation (8)
**Initial Temperament Observation**: Describe the animal's temperament (e.g., friendly, aggressive, fearfully, shy).

### Known or Visible Medical Issues (9)
**Known or Visible Medical Issues**: List any known or visible medical issues affecting the animal (e.g., wounds, limping, skin conditions).

### Current Weight - lbs (10)
**Current Weight - lbs**: Enter the animal's current weight.

### Initial Treatment Administered on Intake (11)
**Initial Treatment Administered on Intake**: Describe any initial treatment administered to the animal on intake (e.g., flea treatment, vaccines, hydration).

### Intake Staff Member Name (12)
**Intake Staff Member Name**: Enter your name and title (e.g., Animal Control Officer) to ensure accountability and accurate tracking of intake information.
