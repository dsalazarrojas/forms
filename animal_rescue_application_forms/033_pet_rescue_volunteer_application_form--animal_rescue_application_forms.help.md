# pet_rescue_volunteer_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from individuals interested in becoming volunteers at our animal rescue organization. By completing this form, you will provide us with the necessary information to determine your eligibility and suitability for volunteering. Please note that this form is not a formal application, and you will be contacted for further information after submitting it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page as thoroughly as possible. If a field is not required, feel free to leave it blank if it's not relevant to you.
2. Ensure you enter a valid email address in the "Email" field.
3. If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their name, phone number, and address.
4. Enter your start and end dates for volunteering, if applicable. If you're unsure of your end date, you can leave it blank.
5. Once you've completed all the pages, review your answers for accuracy and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Information**: This section is used to gather basic information about yourself as a volunteer. Please provide your name and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Information**: This section is used to gather your contact information, which will help us reach out to you with further instructions and details.
* **Email**: Please use a valid email address that you check regularly. This is how we will communicate with you about your application.
* **Phone**: If you have a phone number, please provide it. This will help us contact you if needed.
* **Emergency Contact**: If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their name, phone number, and address. This will help us reach out to them in case of an emergency.
* **Address**: This section is used to gather your address, which will help us keep in touch with you.
* **Volunteer Start Date**: If you have a specific date when you can start volunteering, please provide it. If unsure, you can leave it blank.
* **Volunteer End Date**: If you have a specific date when you plan to end your volunteer period, please provide it. If unsure, you can leave it blank.
