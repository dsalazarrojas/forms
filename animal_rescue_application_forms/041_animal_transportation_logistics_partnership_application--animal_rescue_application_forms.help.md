This form is designed to collect information from potential partners in animal transportation services. The form's purpose is to assess a company's capabilities, experience, and interest in partnering with our organization for animal transportation logistics. By filling out this form, companies will be evaluated based on their:

* Company details (name, address, type, years in business)
* Contact information (first name, last name, job title, email, phone number)
* Transportation services offered (types of animals, transport distance capability, transport methods)
* Experience and references (years of experience, approximate total animals transported, current partner organizations)
* Compliance and standards (liability insurance, animal welfare standards followed)
* Special capabilities (special services, technology systems, cost structure)
* Partnership interest (goals, commitment level, availability for regular transports)

The form is designed to help our organization determine which companies are suitable partners for our animal transportation logistics. It will help us assess their capabilities, experience, and interest in partnering with us, and provide us with a comprehensive understanding of their services and limitations.

# Animal Transportation Logistics Partnership Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us evaluate your company's capabilities and interest in partnering with us for animal transportation logistics. By filling out this form, you will be assessed on various aspects of your company, including your company details, contact information, transportation services, experience, and compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question carefully and thoroughly. Make sure to select all applicable options for each question. If you are unsure about any question, please provide additional information in the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Company Name** (`company_name`): Provide your company's legal name.
### 2. **Company Type** (`company_type`): Select the type of company you are (e.g., Transportation Company, Logistics Coordinator, etc.).
### 3. **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`): Enter the number of years your company has been operating.
### 4. **Company Address** (`company_address`): Enter your company's street address.
### 5. **City** (`company_city`): Enter the city where your company is located.
### 6. **State** (`company_state`): Enter the state or province where your company is located.
### 7. **Zip Code** (`company_zip`): Enter your company's zip code.
### 8. **Company Website** (`company_website`): Enter your company's website URL (optional).
### 9. **First Name** (`contact_first_name`): Enter your first name.
### 10. **Last Name** (`contact_last_name`): Enter your last name.
### 11. **Job Title** (`contact_title`): Enter your job title.
### 12. **Email Address** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address.
### 13. **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`): Enter your phone number.
### 14. **Secondary Contact Name** (`contact_secondary_name`): Enter a backup contact person's name (optional).
### 15. **Secondary Contact Phone** (`contact_secondary_phone`): Enter a backup contact person's phone number (optional).
### 16. **Types of Animals Transported** (`animal_types_transported`): Select all types of animals you transport (e.g., Dogs, Cats, etc.).
### 17. **Maximum Transport Distance** (`transport_distance_capability`): Select the maximum distance you can transport animals.
### 18. **Transport Methods** (`transport_methods`): Select all transport methods you offer (e.g., Ground transport, Air transport, etc.).
### 19. **Monthly Transport Volume** (`volume_capacity`): Enter the approximate number of animals you transport per month.
### 20. **Emergency Transport Available** (`emergency_transport`): Select if you can provide urgent transport services.
### 21. **Climate Controlled Transport Available** (`climate_controlled_transport`): Select if you have temperature-controlled transport services.
### 22. **Years of Animal Transport Experience** (`years_animal_transport`): Enter the total years of experience in animal transportation.
### 23. **Approximate Total Animals Transported** (`total_animals_transported`): Enter the approximate total number of animals you have transported.
### 24. **Current Partner Organizations** (`current_partners`): Enter the organizations you currently work with (optional).
### 25. **Relevant Certifications or Licenses** (`certifications_licenses`): Enter any relevant certifications or licenses.
### 26. **Liability Insurance** (`liability_insurance`): Enter the amount of liability insurance coverage.
### 27. **Animal Welfare Standards** (`animal_welfare_standards`): Select all applicable animal welfare standards you follow.
### 28. **Safety and Incident Record** (`safety_record`): Provide an overview of your safety history.
### 29. **Any Complaints or Violations** (`complaints_history`): Select if you have had any past regulatory issues.
### 30. **Details of Any Complaints** (`complaints_details`): Provide information about any past complaints (optional).
### 31. **Special Services Offered** (`special_services`): Enter any special services you offer beyond basic transport services.
### 32. **Tracking or Monitoring Technology** (`technology_systems`): Enter any tracking or monitoring technology you use (optional).
### 33. **General Cost Structure** (`cost_structure`): Describe your general pricing structure.
### 34. **Partnership Goals** (`partnership_goals`): Enter the goals you hope to achieve through our partnership.
### 35. **Commitment Level** (`partnership_commitment`): Select the level of commitment you have for our partnership.
### 36. **Available for Regular Transports** (`availability_for_partnership`): Select if you can participate in our regular transport services.
### 37. **Additional Information** (`additional_information`): Enter any other relevant details about your company.

Please fill out this form carefully and thoroughly to help us understand your company's capabilities and interest in partnering with us.
