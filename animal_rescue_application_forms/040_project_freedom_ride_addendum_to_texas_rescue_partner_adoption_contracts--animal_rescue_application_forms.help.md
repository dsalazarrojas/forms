# Project Freedom Ride Addendum To Texas Rescue Partner Adoption Contracts - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by Texas Rescue Partners to provide additional information required for the Project Freedom Ride adoption process. It's essential to complete this form accurately to ensure a smooth adoption process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Complete each field accurately and thoroughly.
3. Please note that all fields are optional, unless marked as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, optional): This field is for entering the name of the individual or entity applying for the adoption contract.
* **Rescue Name** (`rescue_name`, text, optional): This field is for entering the name of the rescue organization providing the animal for adoption.
* **Contract Start Date** (`contract_start_date`, date, optional): This field is for entering the date when the adoption contract starts.
* **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`, date, optional): This field is for entering the date when the adoption contract ends.
* **Adoption Fees** (`adoption_fees`, number, optional): This field is for entering the amount of adoption fees, if any.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): This field is for entering the contact number of the applicant or rescue organization.

## Tips
* Ensure all information is accurate and up-to-date to avoid any delays or errors in the adoption process.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the Project Freedom Ride team for assistance.
