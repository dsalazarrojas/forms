# Dog Temperament Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Temperament Questionnaire Form is a tool used by shelters and rescue groups to gather information about a dog's behavior, health, and other essential details. This form helps ensure accurate and consistent data entry for your organization's records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and ensure all required fields are filled out.
2. For questions that require a numerical answer, use numbers only.
3. For questions that require a yes or no answer, select the corresponding option.
4. Provide as much detail as possible for text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **dog_health (Page 1)** (`dog_health`, text, not required): Please describe the dog's overall health. For example, "healthy" or "has a medical condition."
* **socialization (Page 1)** (`socialization`, text, not required): Describe the dog's socialization level. For example, "highly social" or "low."
* **behavioral_issues (Page 1)** (`behavioral_issues`, text, not required): List any behavioral issues the dog may have, such as barking, chewing, or digging.
* **training_level (Page 1)** (`training_level`, number, not required): Indicate the dog's training level, from 1 (no training) to 5 (highly trained).
* **vaccination_status (Page 2)** (`vaccination_status`, select one, not required): Select whether the dog is vaccinated, not vaccinated, or unknown.
* **medical_condition (Page 2)** (`medical_condition`, text, not required): Describe any medical conditions the dog may have, such as hip dysplasia or heart conditions.
* **behavioral_issues_2 (Page 2)** (`behavioral_issues_2`, text, not required): List any additional behavioral issues the dog may have.
* **medical_condition_2 (Page 2)** (`medical_condition_2`, text, not required): Describe any additional medical conditions the dog may have.
* **notes (Page 3)** (`notes`, note, not required): Add any additional comments or notes about the dog.
* **shelter_id (Page 3)** (`shelter_id`, number, not required): Enter the shelter's ID number.
* **rescue_group_id (Page 3)** (`rescue_group_id`, number, not required): Enter the rescue group's ID number.
* **submitted_by (Page 3)** (`submitted_by`, text, not required): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **submitted_by_name (Page 3)** (`submitted_by_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **contact_phone (Page 3)** (`contact_phone`, text, not required): Enter the contact phone number for the shelter or rescue group.
* **contact_email (Page 3)** (`contact_email`, email, not required): Enter the email address for the shelter or rescue group.
* **shelter_email (Page 3)** (`shelter_email`, email, not required): Enter the email address for the shelter or rescue group.
* **submitted_date (Page 3)** (`submitted_date`, date, not required): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **dog_weight (Page 3)** (`dog_weight`, number, not required): Enter the dog's weight.
* **dog_age (Page 3)** (`dog_age`, number, not required): Enter the dog's age.
* **dog_breed (Page 4)** (`dog_breed`, select one, not required): Indicate whether the dog is a purebred or a mix.
* **dog_color (Page 4)** (`dog_color`, select one, not required): Select the dog's coat color.

# Tips
* Please fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and consistent data entry.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.
* If you are unsure about a particular question, refer to the shelter or rescue group's policies and procedures for guidance.
