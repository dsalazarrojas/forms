# wildlife_rescue_discharge_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The wildlife_rescue_discharge_form is designed to record the details of a rescued animal and its release from the wildlife rescue facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the species of the animal being released (e.g. Mammal, Bird, Reptile).
2. Enter the species name of the animal being released.
3. Choose the status of the animal being released (e.g. Healthy, Injured, Sick).
4. Enter the reason for releasing the animal.
5. Select a release date and time for the animal.
6. Enter the location where the animal will be released.
7. If applicable, enter the name of the animal's guardian.
8. If applicable, enter the phone number of the animal's guardian.
9. If applicable, enter the email of the animal's guardian.
10. Enter any additional comments or notes about the release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **animal_id** (Number): Enter the identification number of the animal being released.
* **species** (Text): Enter the species of the animal being released (e.g. Mammal, Bird, Reptile).
* **species_id** (Select One): Select the species of the animal being released (e.g. Mammal, Bird, Reptile).
* **species_name** (Text): Enter the name of the species of the animal being released.
* **animal_status** (Select Multiple): Select the status of the animal being released (e.g. Healthy, Injured, Sick).
* **discharge_reason** (Text): Enter the reason for releasing the animal.
* **discharge_date** (Date): Enter the date and time the animal will be released.
* **release_location** (Text): Enter the location where the animal will be released.
* **guardian_name** (Text): Enter the name of the animal's guardian, if applicable.
* **guardian_phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the animal's guardian, if applicable.
* **guardian_email** (Email): Enter the email of the animal's guardian, if applicable.
* **notes** (Note): Enter any additional comments or notes about the release.
* **release_location_other** (Text): Enter a custom location where the animal will be released if the default options do not apply.
