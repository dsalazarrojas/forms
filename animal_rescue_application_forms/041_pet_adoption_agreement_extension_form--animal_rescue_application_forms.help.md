# Pet Adoption Agreement Extension Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about an adopter's agreement to extend an existing adoption. The goal is to understand their commitment to care for the pet and ensure a smooth transition for both the pet and the adopter. This form typically accompanies pets that are currently in foster care and ready for permanent adoption.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Pet Adopter** information:
	* Type the adopter's name in the text field.
2. Provide the **Pet Name**:
	* Enter the name of the pet you are interested in adopting.
3. Enter your **Adopter Phone Number**:
	* Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
4. Enter your **Adopter Email**:
	* Enter a valid email address where you can receive updates and communications from the organization.
5. If applicable, check the box for the **Pet Surrender Agreement**:
	* This section is for adopters who are taking ownership of a pet, but may not be able to care for it permanently. This ensures the organization can help with pet placement if the situation changes.
6. Review and acknowledge our **Pet Return Policy**:
	* This policy outlines the steps and procedures for returning the pet to the organization if you are unable to care for it.
7. If applicable, provide any **Medical Conditions**:
	* This section is for adopters who have medical conditions that may impact their ability to care for the pet.
8. Enter **Vaccination History**:
	* If the pet has a history of vaccinations, please provide this information to ensure their continued health.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Adopter**: Enter your name as the adopter.
* **Pet Name**: Enter the name of the pet you are interested in adopting.
* **Adopter Phone**: Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
* **Adopter Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can receive updates and communications from the organization.
* **Pet Surrender Agreement**: Check this box if you are taking ownership of a pet, but may not be able to care for it permanently.
* **Pet Return Policy**: Review and acknowledge our policy for returning the pet to the organization if you are unable to care for it.
* **Medical Conditions**: Enter any medical conditions that may impact your ability to care for the pet.
* **Vaccination History**: Enter any vaccination history for the pet to ensure their continued health.

## Tips
* Please review the entire form carefully before submitting.
* Make sure to answer all required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us directly.
