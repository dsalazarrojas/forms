# Animal Rescue Event Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback from participants to assess the impact of the animal rescue event. Your input will help us improve future events and better serve our community.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out this form carefully, ensuring all required fields are completed. If a field is marked as "optional," you may provide information if you have it, but it is not required. You can save your progress and come back later if you need to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name or Title** (`event_name_title`, text, required): Enter the name or title of the event you participated in.
* **Your Role at the Event** (`participant_role`, select_one, required): Choose the role you played during the event:
	+ Attendee or Visitor
	+ Event Volunteer
	+ Organization Staff
	+ Partner or Sponsor
* **Overall Event Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Indicate your general satisfaction with the event:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Number of Animals Adopted - if known** (`animals_adopted_count`, number, optional): If known, enter the number of animals adopted during the event.
* **Estimated Funds Raised - if known** (`funds_raised_estimate`, number, optional): If known, enter the estimated amount of funds raised in USD. Enter the amount without dollar signs.
* **What was the most impactful part of the event?** (`impactful_moment`, text, required): Share a brief story or describe the most impactful moment you experienced during the event.
* **Quality of Event Organization** (`organization_rating`, select_one, required): Evaluate the organization's performance during the event:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Effectiveness of Event Promotion** (`promotion_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Indicate how well you heard about the event:
	+ Very Effective
	+ Effective
	+ Somewhat Effective
	+ Not Effective
* **Suggestions for Future Events** (`future_suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions or ideas for improving future events.
* **Success Stories or Testimonials** (`success_story_testimonial`, text, optional): Share any positive outcomes or success stories you observed or were a part of during the event.
