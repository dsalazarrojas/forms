# Doggie Meet & Greet Info Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Doggie Meet & Greet Info Form is used to gather information about a pet's meet and greet session with its owner. This form is typically used by animal shelters to collect relevant details about the pet's visit, including the pet's name, type, meet date, start and end times, location, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide the pet's name in the "pet_name" field.
2. Select the type of pet from the "pet_type" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the date of the meet and greet session in the "meet_date" field.
4. Specify the start time of the meet and greet session using the "start_time" field.
5. Enter the end time of the meet and greet session using the "end_time" field.
6. Select the duration of the meet and greet session in the "duration" field.
7. Enter the location where the meet and greet session will take place in the "location" field.
8. Provide your contact information, including a phone number, in the "contact_number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **pet_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the pet that will be attending the meet and greet session.
* **pet_type** (select_one, optional): Select the type of pet (Dog, Cat, or Other).
* **meet_date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the meet and greet session.
* **start_time** (time, optional): Enter the start time of the meet and greet session.
* **end_time** (time, optional): Enter the end time of the meet and greet session.
* **duration** (number, optional): Enter the duration of the meet and greet session in hours.
* **location** (text, optional): Enter the location where the meet and greet session will take place.
* **contact_number** (text, optional): Enter your contact information, including a phone number.
