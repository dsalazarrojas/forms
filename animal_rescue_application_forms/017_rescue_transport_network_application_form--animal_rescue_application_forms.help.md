# Rescue Transport Network Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from volunteer drivers who wish to participate in the rescue transport network.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and provide accurate information about yourself and your vehicle.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Driver Info (1)** (Number, Not Required): This section is used to collect basic information about the volunteer driver.
* **Transport Group (2)** (Select One, Not Required): Select the transport group you are interested in joining (e.g. Animal Rescue).
* **Experience (3)** (Text, Not Required): Describe your previous experience as a volunteer or relevant skills you have.
* **Availability (4)** (Time, Not Required): Provide your availability for transport services, including days and times of the week you are available.
* **Contact Details (5)** (Email, Not Required): Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Application Date (6)** (Date, Not Required): Enter the date you are applying to join the rescue transport network.
* **Vehicle Make (7)** (Text, Not Required): Enter the make of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model (8)** (Text, Not Required): Enter the model of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year (9)** (Number, Not Required): Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle License Plate (10)** (Text, Not Required): Enter the license plate number of your vehicle.
* **Volunteer Experience (11)** (Text, Not Required): Describe your volunteer experience and relevant skills.
* **Volunteer Availability (12)** (Time, Not Required): Provide your availability for volunteer work, including days and times of the week you are available.
* **Vehicle Capacity (13)** (Number, Not Required): Enter the capacity of your vehicle (e.g. number of people it can transport).
* **Vehicle Insurance (14)** (Select One, Not Required): Indicate whether you have vehicle insurance.
* **Vehicle Registration (15)** (Text, Not Required): Enter the registration status of your vehicle.
* **Volunteer Phone (16)** (Text, Not Required): Enter a valid phone number for communication purposes.
* **Volunteer Email (17)** (Email, Not Required): Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Vehicle Maintenance Status (18)** (Select One, Not Required): Indicate the maintenance status of your vehicle (e.g. Active, Inactive).
* **Vehicle Safety Equipment (19)** (Select Multiple, Not Required): Select the safety equipment your vehicle is equipped with (e.g. Fire extinguisher, First aid kit).
* **Vehicle Safety Training (20)** (Select Multiple, Not Required): Indicate whether you have received vehicle safety training (e.g. Yes, No).
* **Vehicle Mileage (21)** (Number, Not Required): Enter the mileage of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Maintenance Cost (22)** (Number, Not Required): Enter the estimated maintenance cost of your vehicle.
* **Volunteer Driver Notes (23)** (Text, Not Required): Add any additional notes about yourself or your vehicle.
* **Volunteer Referral (24)** (Text, Not Required): If you were referred by another volunteer, enter their name and contact information.
* **Vehicle Status (25)** (Select One, Not Required): Indicate the status of your vehicle (e.g. Active, Retired, Available for transport).
* **Vehicle Status 2 (26)** (Select One, Not Required): This field is a duplicate of Vehicle Status, likely used for redundancy or future updates.
