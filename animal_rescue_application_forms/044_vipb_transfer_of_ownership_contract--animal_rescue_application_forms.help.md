# VipbTransferOfOwnershipContract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the transfer of ownership of a Very Important Pet or Animal (VIPB) from one owner to another.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of animal being transferred (e.g. Cat, Dog, etc.).
2. Enter the name of the animal being transferred.
3. Enter the name and contact information of the current owner.
4. Check if the owner is available for contact (Yes/No).
5. Enter the date of the transfer (e.g. mm/dd/yyyy).
6. Enter the location where the transfer took place.
7. Select if the transfer was by agreement or not (Yes/No).
8. Enter any notes about the transfer.
9. Sign and date the form as the owner.
10. Have one or two witnesses sign and date the form to attest to the transfer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Animal Type** (`animal_type`, select_one): Select the type of animal being transferred (e.g. Cat, Dog, etc.).
* **Animal Name** (`animal_name`, text): Enter the name of the animal being transferred.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text): Enter the name of the current owner.
* **Owner Contact Method** (`owner_contact_method`, select_multiple): Check if the owner is available for contact (Yes/No).
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date): Enter the date that the owner signs this form (e.g. mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Report Location** (`report_location`, text): Enter the location where the transfer took place.
* **Transfer Date** (`transfer_date`, date): Enter the date of the transfer (e.g. mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Transfer By** (`transfer_by`, select_one): Select if the transfer was by agreement or not (Yes/No).
* **Transfer Note** (`transfer_note`, note): Enter any notes about the transfer.
* **Owner Signature** (`owner_signature`, text): Sign and date the form as the owner.
* **Witness 1** (`witness_1`, text): Sign and date the form as the first witness to the transfer.
* **Witness 2** (`witness_2`, text): Sign and date the form as the second witness to the transfer.
* **Transfer Agreement** (`transfer_agreement`, select_one): Select if the transfer is agreed upon (Agreed/Not Agreed).
* **Transfer Agreed** (`transfer_agreed`, select_one): Select if the transfer is agreed upon (Yes/No).
* **Reason for Transfer** (`reason_for_transfer`, text): Enter the reason for the transfer.
* **Owner Address** (`owner_address`, text): Enter the address of the current owner.
* **Transfer by Witness** (`transfer_by_witness`, select_one): Check if a witness is present for the transfer (Yes/No).
* **Owner DOB** (`owner_dob`, date): Enter the date of birth of the current owner.
