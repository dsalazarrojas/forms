# Exotic Animal Welfare Collaboration Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exotic Animal Welfare Collaboration Application Form is used to facilitate collaboration between rescue organizations and individuals on the welfare of exotic animals. It gathers essential information about the animal, the rescue organization, and contact details to facilitate smooth and effective collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Review and ensure all required fields are filled in.
3. Submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rescue Name (rescue_name)**: A text field for entering the name of the rescue organization or individual applying to collaborate.
* **Email (email)**: An email field for entering the contact email of the rescue organization or individual.
* **Upload File (upload_file)**: A field for uploading supporting files for the application.
* **Animal Type (animal_type)**: A dropdown menu to select the type of exotic animal (Cat, Dog, Other).
* **Rescue Organization (rescue_organization)**: A dropdown menu to select the name of the rescue organization (Rescue Group 1, Rescue Group 2, Rescue Group 3).
* **Location (location)**: A text field for entering the location of the rescue organization.
* **Phone Number (phone_number)**: A text field for entering the phone number of the rescue organization.
* **Date (date)**: A date field for selecting the date of the application.
* **Time (time)**: A time field for selecting the time of the application.
* **Note (note)**: A field for entering any additional notes or comments.
* **Animal Breed (animal_breed)**: A text field for entering the breed of the exotic animal.
* **Species (species)**: A text field for entering the species of the exotic animal.
* **Animal Size (animal_size)**: A text field for entering the size of the exotic animal.
* **Animal Weight (animal_weight)**: A text field for entering the weight of the exotic animal.
* **Animal Color (animal_color)**: A text field for entering the color of the exotic animal.
* **Animal Gender (animal_gender)**: A text field for entering the gender of the exotic animal.
* **Animal Age (animal_age)**: A text field for entering the age of the exotic animal.
* **Animal Health (animal_health)**: A text field for entering any health issues of the exotic animal.
* **Animal Housing (animal_housing)**: A text field for entering the housing requirements of the exotic animal.
* **Rescue Contact (rescue_contact)**: A text field for entering the name of the contact person at the rescue organization.
* **Rescue Phone (rescue_phone)**: A text field for entering the phone number of the contact person at the rescue organization.
* **Rescue Email (rescue_email)**: An email field for entering the email of the contact person at the rescue organization.
* **Organization (organization)**: A text field for entering the name of the organization.
* **Application Reason (application_reason)**: A text field for entering the reason for the application.
* **Signature (signature)**: A field for entering the digital signature of the applicant.

Note: Please ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
