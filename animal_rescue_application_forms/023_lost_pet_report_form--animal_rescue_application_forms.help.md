# lost_pet_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The lost pet report form is designed to gather information about a lost pet with the goal of helping the pet's owner or authorities locate the pet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide a title for the report that summarizes the situation.
2.  Describe what happened to the pet and why it may be lost.
3.  Enter the pet's name.
4.  Describe the location where the pet was last seen.
5.  Enter the date when the pet was last seen.
6.  Enter the time when the pet was last seen.
7.  Enter your contact phone number so that the owner or authorities can reach out to you.
8.  Enter your contact email address so that the owner or authorities can reach out to you.
9.  Provide a description of the pet.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Title**: A brief summary of the report, describing the situation.
* **Report Description**: A detailed description of what happened to the pet and why it may be lost.
* **Pet Name**: The name of the pet, so it can be identified and located.
* **Last Seen Location**: The address or location where the pet was last seen.
* **Last Seen Date**: The date when the pet was last seen.
* **Last Seen Time**: The time when the pet was last seen.
* **Contact Phone**: The phone number of the person reporting the lost pet.
* **Contact Email**: The email address of the person reporting the lost pet.
* **Pet Description**: A description of the pet, including breed, color, size, and any other distinctive features that can help locate it.
