# Animal Sanctuary Admission Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for an animal that is being submitted for admission into the animal sanctuary. It's a required step in the admission process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details as the primary applicant.
2. Provide a contact email and phone number for updates and communication.
3. Enter the name of the animal being submitted for admission.
4. Select the species of the animal.
5. If known, enter the animal's age in years.
6. If known, select the sex of the animal.
7. Summarize any medical history or current concerns for the animal.
8. Choose a preferred intake date for the animal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant full name**: This is your personal contact information and will be used for updates and communication related to the animal's admission.
* **Contact email**: This is the email address we will use to contact you about the animal's admission and any updates.
* **Contact phone number**: This is your best contact number for us to reach out to you about the animal's admission.
* **Animal name**: Enter the name of the animal being submitted for admission. If you don't know the name, please enter "unknown".
* **Species**: Select the species that best matches the animal.
* **Animal age in years**: If known, enter the approximate age of the animal in years.
* **Sex**: If known, select the sex of the animal. If unknown, select "Unknown".
* **Medical history and current concerns**: Summarize any known medical issues or medications for the animal.
* **Requested intake date**: Choose a preferred date for the animal's admission.
