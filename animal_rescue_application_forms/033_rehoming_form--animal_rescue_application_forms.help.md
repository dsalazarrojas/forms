# rehoming_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rehoming_form is designed to collect information from pet owners who are rehoming their pets. This form helps us understand the pet's details, the owner's information, and their home environment to facilitate a smooth adoption process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Introduction** (Text): A brief welcome message introducing the form. You can type a short message explaining why you're rehoming your pet.
2. Complete the remaining fields with the following information:
   - **Pet Details** (Text): Provide a description of your pet, including their name, breed, age, and any relevant health or behavioral issues.
   - **Owner Details** (Text): Share your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself as the pet owner.
   - **Home Info** (Text): Describe your home environment, including the number of people and pets living there, and any relevant information about your home.
   - **Pet Info** (Text): Give us more specific details about your pet, such as their habits, favorite toys, and any special needs.
