# Rescue Animal Adoption Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information from potential adopters to determine their eligibility and preferences for adopting an animal from the rescue organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Address, Email, Phone Number, and other required information.
2. Select the species of animal you are interested in adopting (e.g. dog, cat, etc.).
3. Enter your age and income in the required fields.
4. Choose your household type (e.g. Homeowner, Renter, etc.).
5. Select your available vehicle (e.g. Car, Truck, etc.).
6. Provide any additional notes or comments you would like to make about your application.
7. Enter your availability for adoption (Date and Time).
8. Choose the type of animal you are interested in adopting (e.g. Dog, Cat, etc.).
9. Select the size of the animal you are interested in adopting (e.g. Small, Medium, etc.).
10. Enter the date and time you are available to adopt the animal.
11. Enter the ID of the animal you are interested in adopting.
12. Enter the ID of the person adopting the animal.
13. Provide any additional notes or comments about the animal.
14. Confirm that you are interested in adopting the animal.
15. Enter the name of the animal you are interested in adopting.
16. Enter the age of the animal you are interested in adopting.
17. Select the color of the animal you are interested in adopting (e.g. Brown, Black, etc.).
18. Select the breed of the animal you are interested in adopting (e.g. Labrador, Poodle, etc.).
19. Select the photo(s) of the animal you would like to view (e.g. Front Face, Back Face, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Address** (3, text, required): Enter your current address.
* **Email** (4, email, required): Enter your email address (example: `example@example.com`).
* **Phone** (5, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Species Adopting** (6, select one, optional): Select the type of animal you are interested in adopting (e.g. dog, cat, etc.).
* **Age** (7, number, required): Enter your age.
* **Income** (8, number, required): Enter your income in thousands of dollars.
* **Household** (9, select multiple, optional): Choose your household type (e.g. Homeowner, Renter, etc.).
* **Vehicle** (10, select one, optional): Select your available vehicle (e.g. Car, Truck, etc.).
* **Note** (11, note, required): Provide any additional notes or comments about your application.
* **Date Available** (12, date, required): Enter the date and time you are available to adopt the animal (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Time Available** (13, time, required): Enter the time you are available to adopt the animal (HH:MM:SS 24H).
* **Animal Type** (14, select one, optional): Select the type of animal you are interested in adopting (e.g. dog, cat, etc.).
* **Animal Size** (15, select one, optional): Select the size of the animal you are interested in adopting (e.g. small, medium, etc.).
* **Adoption Date** (16, date, required): Enter the date you are available to adopt the animal (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Adopted Animal ID** (17, text, required): Enter the ID of the animal you are interested in adopting.
* **Adopter ID** (18, text, required): Enter the ID of the person adopting the animal.
* **Notes** (19, note, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about the animal.
* **Confirm Adoption** (20, select one, optional): Confirm that you are interested in adopting the animal.
* **Animal Name** (21, text, required): Enter the name of the animal you are interested in adopting.
* **Animal Age** (22, text, required): Enter the age of the animal you are interested in adopting.
* **Animal Color** (23, select one, optional): Select the color of the animal you are interested in adopting (e.g. brown, black, etc.).
* **Animal Breed** (24, select one, optional): Select the breed of the animal you are interested in adopting (e.g. Labrador, Poodle, etc.).
* **Photo** (25, select multiple, optional): Select the photo(s) of the animal you would like to view (e.g. Front Face, Back Face, etc.).
