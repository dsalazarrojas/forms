# Exotic Pet Breeding License Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect application details for an exotic pet breeding license. Please fill out this form to provide the necessary information for the application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Fill out the Application Details form on page 1.
2.  Provide breeder information on page 2.
3.  Complete the Facility Information page 3.
4.  Attach documentation in the Documentation page 4.
5.  Submit your License Details on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Application Details** (`application_details`, text, required):
    This is the first page of the form where you will provide a brief overview of your application. Please enter a concise description of your application, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
*   **Breeder Information** (`breeder_info`, text, required):
    On this page, please provide information about yourself as a breeder, including your name, address, and contact details. This will help the authorities verify your identity and contact you about your application.
*   **Facility Information** (`facility_info`, text, required):
    In this section, describe the facility where you plan to breed your exotic pets, including the address, size, and any other relevant details.
*   **Documentation** (`documentation`, text, required):
    Attach any supporting documents or evidence required for your application, such as licenses, permits, or certifications. Please make sure to upload the correct documents and ensure they are legible and complete.
*   **License Details** (`license_details`, text, required):
    On the final page, please provide any additional information about your license application, such as the type of exotic pet you plan to breed, the number of animals you will be breeding, and any other relevant details.

## Tips
Please ensure you answer all questions truthfully and accurately. Incomplete or inaccurate information may lead to delays or rejection of your application. Double-check your answers before submitting to avoid any potential issues.
