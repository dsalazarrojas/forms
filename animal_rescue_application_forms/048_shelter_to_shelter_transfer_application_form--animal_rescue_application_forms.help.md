# Shelter To Shelter Transfer Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for shelters to request the transfer of animals between shelters. It helps to gather necessary information for a smooth and efficient transfer process. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "General Information" section with your basic contact information.
2. In the "Animal Information" section, provide details about the animal you are requesting to transfer.
3. In the "Transfer Details" section, specify the transfer date and time if applicable.
4. In the "Submitter Information" section, provide your basic contact information as the person submitting the form.
5. In the "Submitter Notes" and "Submitter Signature" sections, provide any additional comments or signatures that are relevant to your transfer request.
6. Select the shelters you are requesting the transfer from in the "Transfer Requesting Shelters" section.
7. Specify the animal's status and condition in the "Animal Status" and "Animal Condition" sections.
8. If applicable, attach animal health certificates and specify any other services required.
9. Confirm your signature in the "Transferor Signature Confirmation" section.
10. Finally, review and submit the form to complete your transfer request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information**: Please provide your basic contact information, such as your name and email address.
* **Animal Information**: Describe the animal you are requesting to transfer, including its species, age, and any relevant health conditions.
* **Transfer Details**: If you are requesting a specific transfer date and time, enter it here.
* **Submitter Information**: Provide your name, email address, and phone number so that the shelters can contact you.
* **Submitter Notes**: Add any additional comments or requests you have for the transfer.
* **Submitter Signature**: Sign your name to confirm that you are the authorized person submitting this request.
* **Transfer Requesting Shelters**: Select all the shelters you are requesting the transfer from. 
* **Transfer Reason**: Briefly describe why you are requesting the transfer of this animal.
* **Animal Status**: Choose how the animal is currently being kept (e.g., adopted, fostered, etc.).
* **Animal Condition**: Select any health conditions the animal has (e.g., healthy, sick, injured, etc.).
* **Transfer Date**: If applicable, specify the date for the transfer.
* **Transfer Time**: If applicable, specify the time for the transfer.
* **Shelter Transfer Agreement**: Confirm whether you have a transfer agreement with the shelters selected.
* **Transfer Requesting Shelter's Name**: Enter the name of the shelter making the transfer request.
* **Transfer Requesting Shelter's Address**: Enter the address of the shelter making the transfer request.
* **Transfer Requesting Shelter's Phone**: Enter the phone number of the shelter making the transfer request.
* **Transfer Requesting Shelter's Email**: Enter the email address of the shelter making the transfer request.
* **Transferor's Transfer Agreement**: If applicable, provide a copy of the transfer agreement with the selected shelters.
* **Transferor Notes**: Add any additional comments or requests you have for the transfer.
* **Transferor Signature Confirmation**: Confirm your signature as the transferor.
* **Animal Health Certificates**: If applicable, attach any relevant health certificates for the animal.
* **Other Services**: Describe any other services required for the transfer (e.g., transportation, etc.).
* **Transfer Notes**: Add any final comments or requests for the transfer.
