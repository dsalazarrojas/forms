# Quarantine Pet Food Assistance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help pet owners who are in need of assistance with providing food for their pets during the quarantine period. By filling out this form, you will be able to request assistance for your pet, and our organization's staff will review and process your request as soon as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and provide accurate information about your pet.
2. Fill out the form completely, as required fields are marked.
3. Make sure to answer "Yes" or "No" for the pet type question, and select the correct breed of your pet.
4. Enter your contact name to ensure we can reach you for further information or clarification.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, select_one, required: false): Please answer "Yes" if you have a pet that requires special care due to the quarantine, or "No" if not.
* **Pet Breed** (`pet_breed`, text, required: false): If your pet has a specific breed, please enter it here to help us better understand its needs.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name and contact information so we can reach out to you for further information or clarification.
