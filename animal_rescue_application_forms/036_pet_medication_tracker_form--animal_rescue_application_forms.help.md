# Pet Medication Tracker Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Medication Tracker Form is designed to help pet owners track and monitor their pets' medication schedules, ensuring that their pets receive their necessary treatments on time and in the right amounts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the pet being treated.
2. Provide the name of the medication being administered.
3. Enter the dosage and frequency of the medication.
4. Record the start and end dates of the medication schedule.
5. Optionally, add a note for further information.
6. Provide the name and phone number of the contact person responsible for administering the medication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the pet being treated.
* **Medication Name** (`medication_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the medication being administered.
* **Dosage** (`dosage`, text, required): Enter the dosage and frequency of the medication (e.g., 1 tab per day or 2 pills twice a day).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Record the date when the medication schedule starts.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Record the date when the medication schedule ends.
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, text, optional): If necessary, specify the frequency of the medication administration (e.g., once a day, every 8 hours, etc.).
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Provide an optional reason or note for administering the medication.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional information or concerns for the medication schedule.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for administering the medication.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
