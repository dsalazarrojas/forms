# Pet Rescue Adoption Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pet Rescue Adoption Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals or organizations interested in adopting a pet from our animal rescue organization. This information will be used to assess the suitability of the adoption process for the applicant and to ensure the best possible match between the applicant and the pet.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information where required.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
3. If you have any questions or need help, contact us directly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Type** (`Pet Type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of pet you are interested in adopting (Cat, Dog, or Other).
* **Pet Name** (`Pet Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the pet you are interested in adopting (if applicable).
* **Pet Age** (`Pet Age`, number, optional): Enter the age of the pet you are interested in adopting (if applicable).
* **Owner Name** (`Owner Name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the potential adopter.
* **Owner Email** (`Owner Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Owner Phone** (`Owner Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Pet Details** (`Pet Details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the pet you are interested in adopting (e.g., breed, health issues).
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your contact preferences or availability.
* **Pet History** (`Pet History`, text, optional): Provide any relevant information about the pet you are interested in adopting (e.g., previous owner, medical history).
* **Adopter Information** (`Adopter Information`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about yourself as a potential adopter (e.g., experience with pets).
* **Pet Preferences** (`Pet Preferences`, text, optional): Provide any preferences or requirements for the pet you are interested in adopting (e.g., energy level, training needs).
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, optional): Click the submit button to submit your application.

Note: Please be aware that all fields are optional, but some fields may be required depending on the specific circumstances of your adoption interest. If you have any doubts, please contact us directly for assistance.
