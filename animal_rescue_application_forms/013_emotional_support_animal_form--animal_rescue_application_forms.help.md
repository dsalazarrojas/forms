# Emotional Support Animal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals apply for emotional support animals, providing essential information about their mental health status and the animal's species and purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the first field.
2. Confirm your phone number for contact purposes.
3. Identify the species of the animal you are applying for.
4. Provide the reason you need a mental support animal.
5. Specify whether you are taking medications or not.
6. Declare your mental health status.
7. Confirm whether you have any medical providers for your mental health condition.
8. Sign the form to confirm your identity and authenticity of the information provided.
9. (Optional) Enter the date of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submitter Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Submitter Email**: Provide a valid email address for contact purposes. This information will be used to contact you regarding your application.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Species of Animal**: Select the species of the animal you are applying for emotional support.
*   - **Yes**: Choose this option if the animal is a species of animal you are applying for.
*   - **No**: Choose this option if the animal is not a species of animal you are applying for.
* **Reason for Emotional Support**: Explain in detail why you need an emotional support animal.
* **Medication List**: Select **Yes** if you are currently taking any medications for your mental health condition.
*   - **Yes**: Choose this option if you are taking medications.
*   - **No**: Choose this option if you are not taking medications.
* **Emotional Disability**: Declare your mental health status.
*   - **Yes**: Choose this option if you have a diagnosed mental disability.
*   - **No**: Choose this option if you do not have a diagnosed mental disability.
* **Medical Providers**: Select **Yes** if you have a medical provider for your mental health condition.
*   - **Yes**: Choose this option if you have a medical provider.
*   - **No**: Choose this option if you do not have a medical provider.
* **Submitter Signature**: Sign this form to confirm your identity and authenticity of the information provided.
* **Date**: (Optional) Enter the date of submission.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate in your responses to ensure the authenticity of your application.
- Ensure you have all necessary supporting documents, such as medical records, before submitting your application.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to our support team.
