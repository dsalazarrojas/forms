# LYR Home Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The LYR Home Inspection Checklist is a form used to collect information from animal rescue inspectors about homes they have inspected. This form is designed to gather details about the homes, including address, owner information, and comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the page with the form fields.
2. Fill out the form by entering the required information in each field.
3. Review and submit the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Home Address** (1, text, required: false): Enter the address of the home that was inspected.
  - This field is used to record the address of the home.
- **Owner Name** (2, text, required: false): Enter the name of the owner of the home.
  - This field is used to record the name of the person who owns the home.
- **Owner Phone** (3, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the owner.
  - This field is used to record the phone number of the owner.
- **Inspection Date** (4, date, required: false): Enter the date of the inspection.
  - This field is used to record the date of the inspection.
- **Inspector Name** (5, text, required: false): Enter the name of the inspector performing the inspection.
  - This field is used to record the name of the person performing the inspection.
- **Inspector Signature** (6, text, required: false): Enter the signature of the inspector.
  - This field is used to record the signature of the person performing the inspection.
- **Inspector Phone** (7, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the inspector.
  - This field is used to record the phone number of the person performing the inspection.
- **Comments** (8, text, required: false): Enter any comments or notes about the inspection.
  - This field is used to record any additional comments or notes about the inspection.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, if any.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Contact us if you have any questions or need further assistance.
