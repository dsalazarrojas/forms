# Wildlife Rescue Internship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in participating in a wildlife rescue internship program. It will help us understand your educational background, motivation for joining the program, relevant experience, and availability to contribute to the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked as "Required".
3. Review your application before submitting to ensure it's accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Education** (`education`, `text`, Required): Please provide a detailed description of your educational background, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
* **Motivation** (`motivation`, `text`, Required): Explain why you are interested in joining the wildlife rescue internship program and what you hope to achieve or gain from the experience.
* **Experience** (`experience`, `text`, Required): Share your relevant experience in wildlife rescue, conservation, or related fields, including any notable achievements or accomplishments.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `text`, Required): Specify your availability to participate in the internship program, including the time period you are available to work and any other commitments you have during that time.

## Tips
- Be specific and detailed in your answers to provide a clear picture of your qualifications and interests.
- Proofread your application to ensure it's error-free and easy to read.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
