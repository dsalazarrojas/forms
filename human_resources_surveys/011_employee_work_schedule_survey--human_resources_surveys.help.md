# Employee Work Schedule Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your work schedule preferences, satisfaction, and other related aspects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully.
2. Select your answers from the options provided or type your answer.
3. Provide as much detail as you can when prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Work Shifts** (`shift_preferences`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks about your preferences for work shifts. Please select all shifts you prefer to work, if any.
  - Example: If you're willing to work only morning and evening shifts, select "Morning" and "Evening".
- **Schedule Satisfaction** (`schedule_satisfaction`, `text`, optional): This section is about your satisfaction with your current schedule. Please provide a brief description of your thoughts.
  - Example: "I'm satisfied with my schedule, it allows me to balance work and personal life."
- **Shift Hours** (`shift_hours`, `number`, optional): If you could work longer or shorter shifts, what would be ideal for you?
  - Example: "8-10 hours a day."
- **Break Durations** (`break_durations`, `select_one`, optional): How long would you like your breaks to be?
  - Example: "10-15 minutes."
- **Break Frequency** (`break_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): How often would you like to have breaks?
  - Example: "Every 2 hours."
- **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, `number`, optional): What are your ideal work hours?
  - Example: "9-12 hours a day."
- **Schedule Satisfaction 2** (`schedule_satisfaction_2`, `note`, optional): This is another question about your satisfaction with your schedule.
  - Example: "I like the flexibility to choose my shifts."
- **Break Type** (`break_type`, `select_one`, optional): What type of break would you prefer?
  - Example: "Short break."
- **Break Duration** (`break_duration`, `select_one`, optional): How long would you like your breaks to be?
  - Example: "15-30 minutes."
- **Shift Satisfaction** (`shift_satisfaction`, `number`, optional): Another question about your satisfaction with your schedule.
  - Example: "I'm neutral."
- **Work Shift** (`work_shift`, `select_one`, optional): Which shift do you prefer?
  - Example: "Morning."
- **Schedule Satisfaction 3** (`schedule_satisfaction_3`, `note`, optional): More information about your schedule satisfaction.
  - Example: "I appreciate the variety of shifts."
- **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any suggestions or complaints, please type them here.
  - Example: "It would be great if we had more flexible scheduling."
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Please enter your email address for follow-up communication.
  - Example: "johndoe@example.com".
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Please enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
  - Example: "123-456-7890".
- **Schedule Satisfaction 4** (`schedule_satisfaction_4`, `note`, optional): Another question about your satisfaction with your schedule.
  - Example: "I like the balance between work and personal life."
- **Schedule Satisfaction 5** (`schedule_satisfaction_5`, `note`, optional): Final question about your schedule satisfaction.
  - Example: "I appreciate the consideration for employees' needs."
