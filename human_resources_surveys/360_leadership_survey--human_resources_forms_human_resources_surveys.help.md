# 360 Leadership Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 360 Leadership Survey is designed to gather feedback from various perspectives to help leaders understand their strengths and areas for improvement. This guide will walk you through each section of the form.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please select the "Evaluator" category to which you belong (Direct report, Peer, Supervisor, or External stakeholder).
2.  Enter your name and email address for follow-up purposes.
3.  Identify the department and job title of the leader being evaluated.
4.  Choose the dates for the evaluation period (start and end dates).
5.  Rate the leader's performance on the provided scale (e.g., Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree).
6.  Provide specific examples or descriptions for open-ended questions.
7.  Rate the leader's overall effectiveness (1-10).
8.  Consider whether you would be willing to work under this leader again.
9.  Add any additional comments or observations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your full name**: Enter your name as the survey participant. This is for identification purposes.
*   **Email address**: Provide your email address for follow-up correspondence. This is for communication with the survey administrator, not for public display.
*   **Department and Job title**: Enter the leader's department and current position within the organization.
*   **Elevator Relationship**: Select the most accurate description of your relationship to the leader being assessed.
*   **Leader full name**: Enter the full name of the leader being evaluated.
*   **Leader Department**: Enter the department the leader is responsible for.
*   **Evaluation Period Start and End**: Select the dates for the period being evaluated.
*   **Communication Effectiveness**: Rate the leader's communication skills.
*   **Decision Making**: Rate the leader's decision-making abilities.
*   **Team Development Support**: Rate the leader's support for team growth and development.
*   **Collaboration Promotion**: Rate the leader's ability to promote collaboration among teams.
*   **Conflict Resolution Skills**: Rate the leader's conflict resolution abilities.
*   **Innovation Encouragement**: Rate the leader's encouragement of innovation.
*   **Stress Management**: Rate the leader's stress management and pressure handling.
*   **Feedback Receptivity**: Rate the leader's willingness to receive feedback.
*   **Accountability Practices**: Rate the leader's accountability.
*   **Key Strengths**: Enter the leader's key strengths in leadership.
*   **Improvement Areas**: Identify areas where the leader could improve.
*   **Development Recommendations**: Suggest specific development recommendations for the leader.
*   **Overall Effectiveness Rating**: Rate the leader's overall effectiveness.
*   **Willingness to work under the Leader**: Rate your willingness to work under this leader again.
*   **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the leader's performance.

## Tips

*   Be honest and specific in your feedback.
*   Focus on the leader's behavior, not on personality traits.
*   Use concrete examples to illustrate your feedback.
*   Consider the context and situation when providing feedback.
*   Be respectful and constructive in your comments.
