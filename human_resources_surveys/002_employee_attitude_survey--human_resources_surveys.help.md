# Employee Attitude Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This Employee Attitude Survey is designed to help managers and HR teams understand the opinions and concerns of employees within the organization. It aims to gather feedback on various aspects of workplace culture, employee engagement, and job satisfaction. The survey will help identify areas of strength and weakness, allowing the organization to make informed decisions and take steps to improve employee well-being and productivity.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer each question honestly, providing your opinions and feelings about your work experience.
2. For select-one and select_multiple questions, choose the answer that best reflects your experience.
3. For text fields, provide a brief explanation or description of your thoughts.
4. You can answer all or some of the questions, depending on your personal experience and feelings.
