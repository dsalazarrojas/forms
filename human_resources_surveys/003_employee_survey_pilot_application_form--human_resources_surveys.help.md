# Employee Survey Pilot Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect employee data for the pilot survey initiative, aiming to gauge employee engagement and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal details and job information.
2. Ensure all required fields (Job Category and Role) are completed accurately.
3. Enter your job title, team ID, email, contact number, department, and job status (if applicable).
4. Select your job category from the available options.
5. Indicate whether you are a manager or supervisor (if applicable).
6. Select your role in the team (if applicable).
7. Enter your job description (if applicable).
8. Specify your role in the organization (if applicable).
9. Enter your job start and end dates (if applicable).
10. Note the hours you've worked (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Survey Pilot Application Form**: This is the title of the form.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title as it appears on your employment contract or company directory.
* **Team ID**: Enter your team ID, if applicable.
* **Email**: Enter your official email address used for work communication.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number, if applicable.
* **Department**: Enter the department you belong to.
* **Job Status**: Select your current job status from the available options (Active, Inactive, or Probation).
* **Job Category**: Select the category of your job from the options (Management, Support, Development). This field is required.
* **Manager**: If you have a manager, enter their name.
* **Supervisor**: If you have a supervisor, enter their name.
* **Role**: Select whether you are a manager or non-manager.
* **Team Name**: Enter the name of your team.
* **Role in Team**: Select or enter your role in the team.
* **Job Description**: Enter a brief description of your job responsibilities.
* **Team Lead**: Enter the name of your team lead.
* **Role in Organization**: Enter your role in the organization.
* **Job Start Date**: Enter the date you started your job (if applicable).
* **Job End Date**: Enter the date your job ended (if applicable).
* **Hours Worked**: Enter the number of hours you've worked (if applicable).
