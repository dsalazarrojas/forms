# HR Policy Compliance Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees understand and declare their compliance with company policies. It's crucial that employees complete this form accurately to ensure they have a clear understanding of the policies and to help maintain a transparent and compliant work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2. For each question, select the relevant option or provide a detailed response in the text field.
3. Fill in the Company Name and Employee Name fields with your actual company name and your employee ID or name.
4. Provide the date for the Reporting Date field, if applicable.
5. Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company prophets** (`company_policies`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your company's policies that you are familiar with. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Employee Confimration** (`employee_confirmation`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have read and understand the company policies. You can also select 'No' if you haven't had a chance to review the policies yet.
* **Policy Understanding** (`policy_understanding`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have a clear understanding of the company policies. If unsure, select 'No' and we will provide additional guidance.
* **Reporting Frequency** (`reporting_frequency`, number, optional): If you've noticed any policy changes, indicate how often you report these changes (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly).
* **Policy Changes** (`policy_changes`, note, optional): If there have been any policy changes, please describe these changes in detail in this text field.
* **Policy Changes Date** (`policy_changes_date`, date, optional): If there have been policy changes, provide the date these changes took effect.
* **Policy Changes Frequency** (`policy_changes_frequency`, number, optional): If there have been policy changes, indicate how frequently you report these changes (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly).
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the actual company name as it appears on your official company documents.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your actual employee ID or name as it appears on your personnel file.
* **Reporting Date** (`reporting_date`, date, optional): If you are reporting policy changes, provide the date you noticed these changes.
