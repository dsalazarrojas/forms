# Corporate Diversity Initiatives Impact Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Diversity Initiatives Impact Questionnaire is a form designed to assess the effectiveness and progress of corporate diversity initiatives within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please answer each question honestly and genuinely, reflecting your personal experiences and feelings.
2.  If you have any suggestions or comments, feel free to share them in the "Suggestions" and "Comments" sections.
3.  Select the correct options for each question, following the explanations provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Demographics** (page 2, label: Demographics, type: select_multiple, required: false)
	+ This section collects information on your demographics to help identify the diversity of the workforce. Please select all options that apply to you.
* **Diversity** (page 3, label: Diversity, type: number, required: false)
	+ This question measures how important diversity is to you in the workplace. Please select a number that reflects your level of agreement.
* **Inclusion** (page 4, label: Inclusion, type: note, required: false)
	+ This section is for you to share any thoughts or feelings about inclusion in the workplace.
* **Engagement** (page 5, label: Engagement, type: select_one, required: false)
	+ This question measures your level of engagement in diversity initiatives. Please select the option that best describes your level of engagement.
* **Department** (page 6, label: Department, type: select_multiple, required: true)
	+ Please select all departments you belong to or have been part of.
* **Manager** (page 7, label: Manager, type: select_one, required: true)
	+ Please answer 'True' if you have a manager who supports diversity initiatives, or 'False' if you do not.
* **Action** (page 8, label: Action, type: note, required: false)
	+ This section is for you to share any actions you have taken or suggestions for future diversity initiatives.
* **Impact** (page 9, label: Impact, type: number, required: false)
	+ This question measures the impact of diversity initiatives on you. Please select a number that reflects your level of agreement.
* **Satisfaction** (page 10, label: Satisfaction, type: select_one, required: true)
	+ Please select the option that best describes your level of satisfaction with diversity initiatives.
* **Suggestions** (page 11, label: Suggestions, type: text, required: false)
	+ Feel free to share any suggestions or ideas you have for improving diversity initiatives in the workplace.
* **Comments** (page 12, label: Comments, type: text, required: false)
	+ If you have any additional comments or thoughts about diversity initiatives, please share them here.
* **Close** (page 13, label: Close, type: note, required: false)
	+ Thank you for taking the time to complete this form. Your feedback is valuable and will help shape the future of diversity initiatives in our organization.

Note: The explanations above are based on the provided YAML fields, and any unclear or weak field labels have been clarified best-effort to reflect the likely intent of the form designer.
