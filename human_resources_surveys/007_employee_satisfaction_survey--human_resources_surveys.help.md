# Employee Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee satisfaction survey is a tool for gathering feedback and information about employee satisfaction. Your responses will help management understand your needs and improve your work experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your satisfaction level for the employee survey questions.
2. Enter your name (if you choose to).
3. Enter your email address (if you choose to).
4. Choose your department from the provided options.
5. Provide any additional feedback in the text box.
6. Select the date and time of your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Survey** (`select_multiple`, required: false): This section asks for your opinion on the overall satisfaction level of your work experience.
	+ Very Satisfied: You are extremely pleased with your job and work environment.
	+ Somewhat Satisfied: You are somewhat pleased, but with some reservations.
	+ Neither Satisfied nor Dissatisfied: You are neutral, neither extremely pleased nor extremely dissatisfied.
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied: You are somewhat disappointed with your job and work environment.
	+ Very Dissatisfied: You are extremely displeased with your job and work environment.
* **Name** (`text`, required: false): This is an optional field for you to enter your name, if you choose to do so.
* **Email** (`email`, required: false): This is an optional field for you to enter your email address, if you choose to do so.
* **Department** (`select_one`, required: false): This field asks you to choose your department from the provided options.
* **Feedback** (`text`, required: false): This text box is for you to provide any additional feedback or comments about your employee experience.
* **Date** (`date`, required: false): This field asks for the date of your submission.
* **Time** (`time`, required: false): This field asks for the time of your submission.

## Tips
* Please be honest and sincere in your responses.
* Remember that your feedback is anonymous, unless you choose to reveal your identity.
* If you have any technical issues or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
