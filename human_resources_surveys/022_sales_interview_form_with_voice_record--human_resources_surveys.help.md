# Sales Interview Form With Voice Record - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for a sales interview, allowing users to record their voice and provide additional details through text fields. The form is intended for sales professionals to capture relevant data for their interactions with customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Click on the "Sales Interview Form With Voice Record" page to access the form.
- Fill in the text field for the "sales_interview_form" section, providing a brief description of the sales interview.
- If necessary, record your voice using the "voice_record" field, which is a note-taking area that allows you to capture audio notes.
- Enter your email address in the "email" field, if you want to be contacted for further information.
- Select a date for the sales interview using the calendar in the "sales_interview_date" field.
- Choose the time for the sales interview using the time picker in the "sales_interview_time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **sales_interview_form (Text)**: A text field for a brief description of the sales interview.
* **voice_record (Note)**: A note-taking area where you can record your voice to capture audio notes.
* **email (Email)**: Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for further information.
* **sales_interview_date (Date)**: Select a date for the sales interview using the calendar.
* **sales_interview_time (Time)**: Choose the time for the sales interview using the time picker.
