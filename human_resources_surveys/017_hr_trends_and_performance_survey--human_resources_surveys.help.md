# HR Trends And Performance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a tool for HR departments to collect employee feedback and insights on their work experience, satisfaction, and performance. It helps to understand employee engagement levels, identify areas of improvement, and provide a more accurate picture of their work.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the "Submit" button to start filling the form.
2.  Review each section carefully to ensure you are answering each question accurately.
3.  Fill out all the required fields, which will be marked as such in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Satisfaction**: This is where you can provide your overall satisfaction with your work experience. Answer this question as truthfully as possible.
*   **Engagement Level**: Please select one of the three options that best describe your level of engagement with your work. This will help us understand how motivated you are in your role.
*   **Performance Indicators**: Please select all the options that apply to your performance. This will help us understand your strengths and areas for improvement.
*   **Feedback Frequency**: Select how frequently you receive feedback on your work. This will help us understand how often we should provide feedback to you.
*   **Date Submitted**: This is where you can select the date you submit this form. This will help us track the submission timeline.
*   **Time Submitted**: This is where you can select the time you submit this form. This will help us track the submission timeline.
*   **Email**: Please enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
*   **Phone**: Please enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
*   **Additional Notes**: This is an optional field for any additional comments or concerns you may have.
*   **Category 1**: Please select the most relevant category for your feedback.
*   **Category 2**: Please select all the relevant categories for your feedback.
