# Racism Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Racism Survey" form is designed to collect information on individuals' experiences with racism, including demographic information and their experiences with racism in different contexts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out your personal details: enter your first and last name in the provided fields. Please note that these fields are required.
2.  Select the racial category you identify with, using the multiple-selection dropdown. You can select more than one option if applicable.
3.  Enter your age, which is a required field.
4.  If you have an email address, please enter it in the "Email" field. This field is required.
5.  If you have a phone number, please enter it in the "Phone Number" field. This field is required.
6.  Answer the question of whether you have ever experienced racism. You can select more than one option if applicable.
7.  Answer the question of whether you have experienced racism at work. You can select only one option.
8.  Select how many times you have experienced racism.
9.  Rate your experiences with racism using the dropdown options.
10.  Rate your experiences with racism again using the dropdown options. This is a self-assessment.
11.  If you have experienced racism, please describe your response to the question of what you would do if you experienced racism.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What is your first name?** (text, required)
    This field is used to collect your first name.
*   **What is your last name?** (text, required)
    This field is used to collect your last name.
*   **What race do you identify with?** (select\_multiple, required)
    This field allows you to select the racial category you identify with. You can select more than one option if applicable.
*   **What is your age?** (number, required)
    This field is used to collect your age.
*   **Do you have an email address?** (email, required)
    This field is used to collect your email address. Please enter a valid email address.
*   **Do you have a phone number?** (text, required)
    This field is used to collect your phone number.
*   **Have you ever experienced racism?** (select\_multiple, required)
    This field is used to collect information on whether you have ever experienced racism. You can select more than one option if applicable.
*   **Have you experienced racism at work?** (select\_one, required)
    This field is used to collect information on whether you have experienced racism at work. You can select only one option.
*   **How many times have you experienced racism?** (number, required)
    This field is used to collect information on how many times you have experienced racism.
*   **How would you rate your experience with racism?** (select\_one, required)
    This field is used to collect a subjective rating of your experiences with racism. You can select one of the provided options.
*   **How do you rate your experience with racism?** (select\_one, required)
    This field is used to collect a self-assessment of your experiences with racism.
*   **What would you do if you experienced racism?** (note, optional)
    This field is used to collect information on your response to experiencing racism.

**Tips**

*   Please ensure that you are honest and accurate when filling out this form.
*   All fields marked as required must be filled out.
*   If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask for help.
