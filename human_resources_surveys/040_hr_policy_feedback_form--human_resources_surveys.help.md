# HR Policy Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and suggestions from employees on our HR policies, allowing us to make informed decisions and improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant Employee ID of the employee who is submitting the feedback.
2. Enter a brief description of the policy that you are providing feedback on, if it's not already populated in the field.
3. In the Feedback field, provide a clear and concise description of your thoughts and suggestions.
4. In the Suggestion field, if applicable, provide a specific idea for improvement.
5. You can optionally select multiple company policies that you are providing feedback on.
6. If you have any additional comments, please use the Comment field.
7. Finally, select a submission date and time by clicking on the calendar icon in the Submitted On field.
8. Enter your name or other identifier to be credited for the feedback, this information will be stored but not displayed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (<type number>): This field requires you to select your Employee ID from the list.
* **Policy Name** (<type text>): Enter a brief description of the policy on which you are providing feedback.
* **Feedback** (<type text>): This field is open for you to describe in detail your thoughts and suggestions on the policy.
* **Suggestion** (<type text>): In case you have a specific idea for improvement, provide it here.
* **Company Policies** (<type select_multiple>): If you are providing feedback on one or more company policies, select them from the provided options.
* **Submitted On** (<type date>): Select a date and time when you are providing the feedback.
* **Submitted By** (<type text>): Enter your name or other identifier to be credited for the feedback. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that your feedback is specific and concrete, as this will help us improve our policies more effectively.
* Avoid providing duplicate or irrelevant feedback, as it will not be taken into consideration.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, please use the Comment field.
