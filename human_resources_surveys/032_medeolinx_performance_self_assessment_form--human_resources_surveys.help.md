# MedeoLinx Performance Self-Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The MedeoLinx Performance Self-Assessment Form is a tool for employee evaluations and growth. This form helps employees and their supervisors assess performance, identify areas for improvement, and set goals for future development.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information as accurately as possible.
2. Ensure that you're answering questions truthfully and thoroughly.
3. Review and revise your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it is written on your official personnel documents.
* **supervisor_id** (`supervisor_id`, select_one, required): Select your supervisor's role: Manager, Supervisor, Director, or Executive.
* **job_title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title as it is listed in the company directory.
* **job_level** (`job_level`, select_multiple, optional): Select your job level: Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3.
* **work_hours** (`work_hours`, text, optional): Enter your average weekly work hours.
* **work_week** (`work_week`, time, required): Enter your standard work week in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00-17:00).
* **performance_level** (`performance_level`, select_one, required): Select your performance level: Excellent, Good, or Needs Improvement.
* **feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Add any additional feedback or comments about your performance.
* **evaluation_date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of this performance evaluation.
* **evaluation_period** (`evaluation_period`, select_one, required): Select the evaluation period: Q1, Q2, or Q3.
* **supervisor_name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **supervisor_title** (`supervisor_title`, text, required): Enter the title of your supervisor.
* **employee_email** (`employee_email`, email, required): Enter your company email address.
* **supervisor_email** (`supervisor_email`, email, required): Enter your supervisor's company email address.
* **manager_comments** (`manager_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments from your manager.
* **employee_comments** (`employee_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments from you.
* **overall_satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Select your overall satisfaction: Excellent, Satisfactory, or Needs Improvement.
* **evaluation_frequency** (`evaluation_frequency`, select_one, required): Select the evaluation frequency: 1 Month, 3 Months, or 6 Months.
* **next_evaluation_date** (`next_evaluation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the next performance evaluation.
* **employee_aware** (`employee_aware`, select_one, required): Select whether you are aware of this evaluation: True or False.
* **supervisor_aware** (`supervisor_aware`, select_one, required): Select whether your supervisor is aware of this evaluation: True or False.
* **performance_improvement_areas** (`performance_improvement_areas`, text, optional): Identify areas for improvement.
* **action_plan** (`action_plan`, select_multiple, optional): Select areas to focus on for improvement.
* **completed_tasks** (`completed_tasks`, text, optional): List tasks completed during the evaluation period.
* **next_steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): Outline next steps for future development.
* **overall_satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Select your overall satisfaction: Excellent, Satisfactory, or Needs Improvement.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough when answering questions.
* Review and revise your answers before submitting the form.
* Ensure that your supervisor reviews and provides comments on your performance.
* Use this form to set goals and areas for improvement for future development.
