# Team Building Activity Success Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Team Building Activity Success Assessment" form is a tool used to evaluate the success of team-building activities. It helps us understand what worked well and what could be improved for future team-building efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields, including Team Building Activity Name, Activity Date, Participants Number, Team Leaders, and Activity Outcome.
2. Optional fields, such as Location, are not required for this form, but you can provide more information if you'd like.
3. Be sure to select the correct Activity Outcome from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Building Activity Name** (`team_building_activity_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the team-building activity you participated in.
* **Activity Date** (`activity_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date of the activity, using the date format your system uses.
* **Participants Number** (`participants_number`, `number`, required): Report the total number of participants in the activity.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide more context, enter the location where the activity took place.
* **Team Leaders** (`team_leaders`, `select_one`, required): Select the person who led the team during the activity. You can choose from 'CEO', 'Manager', or 'Other'.
* **Activity Outcome** (`activity_outcome`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply from the options 'Very Satisfying', 'Satisfying', 'Somewhat Satisfying', 'Not Satisfying', 'Not at All Satisfying' to describe your experience with the activity.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or thoughts about the activity, you can enter them here.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, try to best-effort explain your intent behind your answer.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
