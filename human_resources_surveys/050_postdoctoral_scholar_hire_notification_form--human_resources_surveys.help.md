# Postdoctoral Scholar Hire Notification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to notify the relevant parties of a postdoctoral scholar's hire, including their contact information, job details, and employment dates. This form helps to keep track of the scholar's status and ensure a smooth transition into the role.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Notification Form**: This is the main section of the form where you can type in the hire notification details.
2. To complete this form, start by filling out the Notification Form section with the relevant information about the postdoctoral scholar's hire.
3. Move on to the candidate's information section:
   * **Candidate Name**: Enter the name of the postdoctoral scholar being hired.
   * **Candidate Contact Information**: Enter the postdoctoral scholar's contact information, including their address, email, and phone number.
4. Next, select the job details:
   * **Job Title**: Enter the title of the job being offered.
   * **Position**: Enter the position or department of the scholar's employment.
   * **Supervisor's Name**: Enter the name of the postdoctoral scholar's supervisor.
   * **Department**: Enter the department the postdoctoral scholar will be working in.
5. Set the employment and job dates:
   * **Job Start Date**: Enter the start date of the postdoctoral scholar's employment.
   * **Job End Date**: Enter the end date of the postdoctoral scholar's employment.
   * **Employment Start Date**: Enter the date the postdoctoral scholar's employment began.
   * **Employment End Date**: Enter the date the postdoctoral scholar's employment ended.
6. Finally, add the scholar's salary and manager's information:
   * **Salary**: Enter the postdoctoral scholar's salary.
   * **Benefits**: Optional field for any benefits offered.
   * **Department ID**: Enter the ID of the department the postdoctoral scholar will be working in.
   * **Manager's Name**: Enter the name of the manager responsible for the postdoctoral scholar.
   * **Manager's Email**: Enter the email address of the manager responsible for the postdoctoral scholar.
   * **Manager's Phone**: Enter the phone number of the manager responsible for the postdoctoral scholar.
7. Review and submit the form to complete the hire notification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Notification Form** (`postdoctoral_notification_form`, text, required/optional): The main section of the form where you can type in the hire notification details.
### 2. **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the postdoctoral scholar being hired.
### 3. **Candidate Contact Information** (`candidate_contact_info`, text, required): Enter the postdoctoral scholar's contact information, including their address, email, and phone number.
### 4. **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the job being offered.
### 5. **Position** (`position`, text, required): Enter the position or department of the scholar's employment.
### 6. **Supervisor's Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the postdoctoral scholar's supervisor.
### 7. **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department the postdoctoral scholar will be working in.
### 8. **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the postdoctoral scholar's employment.
### 9. **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the postdoctoral scholar's employment. (Note: this field is optional)
### 10. **Employment Start Date** (`employment_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date the postdoctoral scholar's employment began.
### 11. **Employment End Date** (`employment_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date the postdoctoral scholar's employment ended.
### 12. **Salary** (`salary`, number, required): Enter the postdoctoral scholar's salary.
### 13. **Benefits** (`benefits`, text, optional): Optional field for any benefits offered.
### 14. **Department ID** (`department_id`, text, required): Enter the ID of the department the postdoctoral scholar will be working in.
### 15. **Manager's Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the manager responsible for the postdoctoral scholar.
### 16. **Manager's Email** (`manager_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the manager responsible for the postdoctoral scholar.
### 17. **Manager's Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the manager responsible for the postdoctoral scholar.
### 18. **Job Category** (`job_category`, text, required): Enter the category of the job.
### 19. **Job Status** (`job_status`, text, required): Enter the status of the job.
### 20. **Job Type** (`job_type`, text, required): Enter the type of the job.
### 21. **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required): Enter a description of the job.
### 22. **Job Start Status** (`job_start_status`, text, required): Enter the status of the job's start.
### 23. **Job End Status** (`job_end_status`, text, optional): Enter the status of the job's end. (Note: this field is optional)
### 24. **Manager's Title** (`manager_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the manager responsible for the postdoctoral scholar.
### 25. **Form Submitter** (`form_submitter`, text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check the information you enter for accuracy.
* If you need help with any of the fields, contact your supervisor or HR department.
