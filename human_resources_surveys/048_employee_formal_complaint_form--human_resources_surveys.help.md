# Employee Formal Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Formal Complaint Form is used to document and track employee complaints within the organization. This form helps to provide a structured and systematic approach to addressing and resolving employee complaints in a fair and timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant complaint type from the options provided in the **Complaint Type** field.
2. Enter your employee ID in the **Employee ID** field.
3. Provide your employee name in the **Employee Name** field.
4. Choose the department relevant to your complaint in the **Department** field.
5. Select the date of the incident in the **Date of Incident** field.
6. Provide a detailed description of the complaint in the **Description** field.
7. Choose whether the complaint was reported to the relevant **Reporting Manager**.
8. Enter the date the complaint was reported in the **Reporting Date** field.
9. Select the current **Resolution Status** of the complaint.
10. Enter the **Resolution Date** if the complaint has been resolved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Complaint Type**: Select the type of complaint you would like to report (e.g., harassment, unfair treatment, etc.).
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number, which can be found on your ID badge or in your employee profile.
* **Employee Name**: Provide your full name as it appears on your ID badge or in your employee profile.
* **Department**: Choose the department where the incident occurred or is relevant to the complaint.
* **Date of Incident**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the complaint, including relevant dates, times, and details.
* **Reporting Manager**: Select whether the complaint was reported to the relevant manager.
* **Reporting Date**: Enter the date the complaint was reported.
* **Resolution Status**: Choose the current status of the complaint (e.g., Active, Inactive).
* **Resolution Date**: Enter the date the complaint was resolved, if applicable.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the complaint.
* Make sure to report the complaint to the relevant manager.
* Keep the **Resolution Date** field blank if the complaint has not been resolved.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your HR representative for guidance.
