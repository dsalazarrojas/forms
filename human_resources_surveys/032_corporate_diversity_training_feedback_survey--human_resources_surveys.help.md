# Corporate Diversity Training Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for gathering feedback on corporate diversity training sessions. It will help us understand your experience and provide valuable insights for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
- Complete the form in order, section by section.
- Read each section carefully and select the options that best describe your experience.
- You can skip sections that are not relevant to the specific training session.

## Tips

- Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
- Use the correct date format (YYYY-MM-DD) for the training date.
- Ensure that you provide feedback on all relevant sections.
- If you have any technical issues, please contact the IT department for assistance.
