# HR Analytics Panel Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Analytics Panel Registration Form is a tool designed to gather information from users who want to register for the HR Analytics Panel. This form helps in accurately collecting and storing user details for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the necessary details in the provided fields.
2. Select the relevant options for the Panel Participants and Panel Topics fields.
3. Provide any additional notes or comments in the Notes field.
4. Review your form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (1, text, optional): Enter your name and other relevant personal details here.
* **Email** (2, email, optional): Enter your work email address here. This will be used to communicate with you regarding the panel.
* **Job Title** (3, text, optional): Enter your job title in the organization.
* **Reporting Manager** (4, text, optional): Enter your reporting manager's name here.
* **Department** (5, text, optional): Enter your department name here.
* **Reporting Manager Phone** (6, text, optional): Enter your reporting manager's phone number here.
* **Manager Phone** (7, text, optional): Enter your manager's phone number here.
* **Manager Email** (8, email, optional): Enter your manager's email address here.
