# Career Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to measure your career satisfaction as an employee of the organization. It will help us understand your overall satisfaction with your job, responsibilities, and career goals. Your feedback is crucial in identifying areas of improvement to enhance your career growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with the requested information.
2. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose one or multiple options that best describe your current situation.
3. Enter your thoughts and comments in the text fields (e.g., "What Could Improve Your Career Satisfaction?" and "Additional Comments").
4. Make sure to answer all required fields to ensure accurate and complete data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name as recorded in our HR system.
* **Employee ID** (2, text, required): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Department** (3, text, required): Type your department name (e.g., "Marketing", "Sales", etc.).
* **Job Title** (4, text, required): Type your job title (e.g., "Marketing Manager", "Software Engineer", etc.).
* **Tenure with Organization** (5, number, required): Indicate how many years you have worked with the organization.
* **Survey Date** (6, date, required): Type the date when you completed this survey.
* **Satisfaction with Current Role** (7, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Responsibility Fit** (8, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Perfect match
	+ Good fit
	+ Adequate fit
	+ Some misalignment
	+ Poor fit
* **Career Growth Opportunities** (9, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Excellent opportunities
	+ Good opportunities
	+ Some opportunities
	+ Limited opportunities
	+ No opportunities
* **Likelihood of Advancement** (10, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Very likely
	+ Likely
	+ Possible
	+ Unlikely
	+ Very unlikely
* **Manager Support** (11, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Very supportive
	+ Supportive
	+ Neutral
	+ Unsupportive
	+ Very unsupportive
* **Professional Development** (12, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Excellent access
	+ Good access
	+ Some access
	+ Limited access
	+ No access
* **Career Goals Clarity** (13, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Very clear
	+ Clear
	+ Somewhat clear
	+ Unclear
	+ Very unclear
* **Achievement of Goal-Related Training** (14, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ All training completed
	+ Most training completed
	+ Some training completed
	+ Little training completed
	+ No training completed
* **Opportunity to Develop New Skills** (15, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Excellent opportunities
	+ Good opportunities
	+ Adequate opportunities
	+ Limited opportunities
	+ No opportunities
* **Satisfaction with Compensation and Benefits** (16, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Obstacles to Career Advancement** (17, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or multiple obstacles that hinder your career advancement (e.g., Limited positions available, Lack of necessary skills, etc.).
* **Career Expectations Reality** (18, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Exceeds expectations
	+ Meets expectations
	+ Mostly meets expectations
	+ Falls short of expectations
	+ Significantly exceeds or falls short
* **Job Security Satisfaction** (19, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Work-Life Balance Satisfaction** (20, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Likelihood of Staying with Organization** (21, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Very likely - plan to stay long-term
	+ Likely - no plans to leave soon
	+ Neutral - undecided
	+ Unlikely - considering leaving
	+ Very unlikely - actively job hunting
* **What Could Improve Your Career Satisfaction?** (22, text, optional): Enter your thoughts and suggestions.
* **Access to Mentors or Role Models** (23, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Yes - have active mentor
	+ Yes - but informal relationship
	+ No - would like mentorship
	+ No - not interested
* **Additional Comments** (24, text, optional): Enter any other thoughts or comments about your career satisfaction.
