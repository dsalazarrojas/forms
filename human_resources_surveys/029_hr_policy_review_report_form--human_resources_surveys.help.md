# HR Policy Review Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Policy Review Report Form is used to gather feedback and suggestions on HR policies from employees. This form is used to help HR personnel evaluate and improve existing policies, identify areas for revision, and ensure that policies are meeting the needs of employees and the organization as a whole.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your Employee Name.
2. Answer the following questions:
	* If the policy is currently applicable to your role, select 'Yes' or 'No' in the policy_category field.
	* If applicable, rate the feedback_score and suggestion_score fields with a number between 0 and 10, where 0 means not applicable or no suggestion and 10 means very applicable or very good suggestion.
	* In the policy_comments field, select 'Yes' or 'No' for each policy that applies to your role.
	* Enter your Department Name and Location as relevant.
	* Enter the Employee ID and Policy for the policy being reviewed (if applicable).
	* Enter the Policy Version for the policy being reviewed (if applicable).
	* Select the policy_status field as 'Active' or 'Inactive' (if applicable).
	* For each policy being reviewed, select 'Yes' or 'No' for policy_effectiveness fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter your employee name as it is on the HR system.
* **Policy Category**: If the policy applies to your role, select 'Yes', otherwise select 'No'.
* **Feedback Score**: Rate the policy's applicability to your role, where 0 means not applicable or no suggestion and 10 means very applicable or very good suggestion.
* **Suggestion Score**: Rate your suggestion for the policy, where 0 means not suggested and 10 means very good suggestion.
* **Policy Comments**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each policy that applies to your role. This will help identify which policies need to be reviewed or revised.
* **Department Name**: Enter your department name as it is on the HR system.
* **Location**: Enter your department's or unit's location as it is on the HR system.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your Employee ID as it is on the HR system.
* **Policy**: Enter the name of the policy being reviewed (if applicable).
* **Policy Version**: Enter the version of the policy being reviewed (if applicable).
* **Policy Status**: Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' for the policy status as it is on the HR system.
* **Policy Effectiveness**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each policy being reviewed. This will help identify which policies are effective and which are not.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have the necessary permissions to access and complete this form.
* Complete the form honestly and accurately to help improve HR policies within the organization.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, please provide them in the policy_comments field.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact your HR representative for assistance.
