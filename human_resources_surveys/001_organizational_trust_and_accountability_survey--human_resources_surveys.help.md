# Organizational Trust And Accountability Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Organizational Trust And Accountability Survey is designed to evaluate the level of trust and accountability within an organization. It's essential to complete this survey honestly and thoughtfully to provide valuable insights for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select a single answer from the provided options for each question. If an option doesn't apply, choose "No" or "Not Applicable" accordingly.
2. Be sure to answer all required questions, as indicated by the asterisk (*) symbol.
3. For select_multiple questions, choose as many relevant answers as needed.
4. For select_one and select_one questions, choose only one answer.
5. Review and adjust your answers before submission to ensure accuracy.
