# Retail Employee Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to understand your experiences and perceptions as a store employee. It will help us gather insights on how often you feel supported and motivated in your role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select your response for each question from the options provided.
3. Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Store Location
* **Store Location**: This is the name of the store you work at. Please enter the name of the store you are currently working at.

### How often do you feel supported in your role?
* **How often do you feel supported in your role?**: This question is designed to understand how often you feel you have the resources and information needed to complete your tasks effectively. Please select one of the options:
	+ **Always**: You always feel supported in your role.
	+ **Frequently**: You feel supported most of the time.
	+ **Sometimes**: You feel supported some of the time.
	+ **Rarely**: You rarely feel supported.
	+ **Never**: You never feel supported.

### How often do you feel motivated to improve your performance?
* **How often do you feel motivated to improve your performance?**: This question is designed to understand how often you feel inspired to improve your skills and knowledge to excel in your role. Please select one of the options:
	+ **Always**: You always feel motivated to improve your performance.
	+ **Frequently**: You feel motivated to improve your performance most of the time.
	+ **Sometimes**: You feel motivated to improve your performance some of the time.
	+ **Rarely**: You rarely feel motivated to improve your performance.
	+ **Never**: You never feel motivated to improve your performance.

## Tips
* Take a moment to think about each question before answering.
* Choose the answer that best represents your honest opinion.
* If you have any specific examples or stories that illustrate your answers, feel free to include them in the comments section.
