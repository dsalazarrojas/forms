# Intern Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intern Survey is a form designed to gather feedback and ratings from interns about their experiences and the program they participated in. This feedback is essential for improving the program and making it more beneficial for future interns.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review each page of the form carefully before proceeding.
2.  Fill out each field with your honest and thoughtful responses.
3.  Submit your completed form at the end of the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
*   **Program** (`program`, select_one, required/optional): Select the program you participated in from the provided options.
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you started your internship.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you ended your internship.
*   **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor`, select_one, required/optional): Select the name of your supervisor from the provided options.
*   **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required/optional): Share any comments or suggestions you have about your internship experience.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Share any additional comments you have about your internship experience.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether or not you are comfortable sharing your feedback.
*   **Rating** (`rating`, select_multiple, required/optional): Rate your overall experience with one of the following options:
    *   Good
    *   Bad
*   **Time Spent** (`time_spent`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount of time you spent on the internship.
*   **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, number, required/optional): Rate your level of satisfaction with the internship on a scale of 1-10.
*   **Will You Recommend** (`will_join`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether or not you would recommend this program to others.
*   **Comments 2** (`comments_2`, text, required/optional): Share any additional comments you have about your experience.
*   **Rating 2** (`rating_2`, select_multiple, required/optional): Rate your experience with one of the following options:
    *   Good
    *   Fair
*   **Time Estimate** (`time_estimate`, time, required/optional): Enter an estimate of the time it took to complete the internship.
*   **Rating Estimate** (`rating_estimate`, select_one, required/optional): Rate your estimate of the difficulty of the internship.
*   **Comments 3** (`comments_3`, text, required/optional): Share any additional comments you have about your experience.
*   **Supervisor Rating** (`supervisor_rating`, select_multiple, required/optional): Rate your supervisor's performance with one of the following options:
    *   Good
    *   Fair
*   **Manager Rating** (`manager_rating`, select_multiple, required/optional): Rate your manager's performance with one of the following options:
    *   Good
    *   Fair
*   **Overall** (`overall`, number, required/optional): Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-10.
*   **Will You Join** (`will_join`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether or not you would join this program again.
*   **Comments 4** (`comments_4`, text, required/optional): Share any additional comments you have about your experience.
*   **Submit 2** (`submit_2`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether or not you are comfortable sharing your feedback.
