## Exit Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from departing employees on their reasons for leaving, job satisfaction, and areas of improvement for the company.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Choose the options that best describe your experience.
3. If you have additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the text fields provided.
