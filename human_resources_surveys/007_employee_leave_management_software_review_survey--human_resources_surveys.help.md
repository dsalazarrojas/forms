# employee_leave_management_software_review_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on employee leave management software usage and satisfaction. It aims to gather information from employees about their experiences with leave management software, including their current software of choice, satisfaction levels, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Choose the correct options for the select questions (e.g., Leave Management Software Used, Satisfaction Level, Team).
3. Enter your Email and Employee ID in the respective fields as they are required.
4. If you have used multiple Leave Management Software, please select all that apply in the 'Leave Management Software Used' field.
5. For the 'Satisfaction Level' field, choose one or more options that best describe your experience.
6. In the 'Improvement Priorities' field, type any areas you think are most important for improvement.
7. If you want to add any additional comments, please use the 'Comments' field.
8. Finally, submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, required: false): This field is not a formal field but a general text area for any user input you may have.
* **Leave Management Software Used** (`leave_management_software_used`, select_one, required: true): This field asks you to choose the leave management software you are currently using. Please select one of the options provided (Microsoft, Google, Other).
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_multiple, required: true): This field allows you to select one or more options that best describe your level of satisfaction with the leave management software. You can choose from Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
* **Improvement Priorities** (`improvement_priorities`, text, required: true): This field is for you to type any areas you think are most important for improvement.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): This field is for any additional comments you want to add.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: true): This field requires your work email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): This field is for your work phone number.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required: true): This field requires your employee ID number.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required: false): This field is for your team name.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, text, required: false): This field is for the type of leave you take.
* **Leave Days** (`leave_days`, number, required: false): This field is for the number of leave days you take.
* **Leave Dates** (`leave_dates`, date, required: false): This field is for the dates of your leave.
* **Leave Time** (`leave_time`, time, required: false): This field is for the time of your leave.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required: false): This field is for your manager's name.
* **Team Leader Name** (`team_leader_name`, text, required: false): This field is for your team leader's name.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: true): This field requires your department name.
* **Team** (`team`, select_multiple, required: true): This field asks you to select one or more teams you belong to. Please choose from Engineering, Marketing, or HR.
* **Reporting Manager Name** (`reporting_manager_name`, text, required: false): This field is for your reporting manager's name.
* **Reporting Team Lead Name** (`reporting_team_lead_name`, text, required: false): This field is for your reporting team lead's name.
* **Reporting Team** (`reporting_team`, select_multiple, required: false): This field asks you to select one or more teams you report to. Please choose from Engineering, Marketing, or HR.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): This field is for your job title.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required: false): This field is for your job description.
* **Reporting Level** (`reporting_level`, select_multiple, required: false): This field asks you to select one or more reporting levels. Please choose from Manager, Team Lead, or Individual.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, text, required: false): This field is for the size of your team.
* **Leave Balance** (`leave_balance`, number, required: false): This field is for your leave balance.
