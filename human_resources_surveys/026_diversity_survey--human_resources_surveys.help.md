# Diversity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Diversity Survey is a tool to collect information about employees' demographic characteristics, such as age, ethnicity, and job title, to help the organization better understand the diversity of its workforce. This information will be used to identify areas of diversity that need to be addressed and to develop targeted initiatives to promote inclusion and diversity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Diversity Survey, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name.
2. Select your job title from the list of options.
3. Enter your age.
4. Choose your ethnicity from the list of options.
5. Select your gender from the list of options.
6. Choose your job category (Management or Non-Management).
7. Enter the number of years you have been working with the company.
8. Indicate your company's diversity commitment (Yes or No).
9. Enter your department (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official personnel records.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Select your job title as it is listed on your official personnel records.
- **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter your age.
- **Ethnicity** (`ethnicity`, select_one, required): Select one of the options that best describe your ethnicity.
- **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select one of the options that best describe your gender.
- **Job Category** (`job_category`, select_one, required): Select whether you are in a Management or Non-Management position.
- **Years of Service** (`years_of_service`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been working with the company.
- **Diversity Commitment** (`company_diversity_commitment`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether your company is committed to diversity.
- **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department (if applicable).
