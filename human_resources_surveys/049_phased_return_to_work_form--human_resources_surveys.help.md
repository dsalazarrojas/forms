# Phased Return to Work Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Phased Return to Work Form is designed to facilitate the planning and coordination of an employee's phased return to work, allowing them to gradually transition back into their work role after an extended period of absence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Select your expected return date from the options provided. If you are returning to work immediately, select "Immediately (No Notice)". If you are returning to work within a specific timeframe, select the corresponding option.
2.  Select your notice period from the options provided. This will determine the time between your last day off and your expected return date.
3.  Select your return status from the options provided. This will indicate your current status, whether Active, Inactive, or Active with restrictions.
4.  Enter your work status update in the text field provided.
5.  Enter any comments regarding your return to work plan in the text field provided.
6.  Select the manager comments that apply to your situation.
7.  Select the team lead comments that apply to your situation.
8.  Enter the name of your manager in the text field provided.
9.  Enter the name of your team lead in the text field provided.
10. Select your job title from the options provided.
11. Enter your department from the options provided.
12. Enter your manager's email address in the text field provided.
13. Select who confirmed your return date from the options provided.
14. Review your form and submit it for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Expected Return Date** (`return_date`): Select the date you expect to return to work. If you are returning to work immediately, select "Immediately (No Notice)".
*   **Notice Period** (`notice_period`): Select the time between your last day off and your expected return date.
*   **Return Status** (`return_status`): Select your current status, whether Active, Inactive, or Active with restrictions.
*   **Work Status Update** (`work_status_update`): Enter any updates regarding your work status.
*   **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any comments regarding your return to work plan.
*   **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`): Select the comments that apply to your situation.
*   **Manager Name** (`manager_name`): Enter the name of your manager.
*   **Team Lead Comments** (`team_lead_comments`): Select the comments that apply to your situation.
*   **Team Lead Name** (`team_lead_name`): Enter the name of your team lead.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`): Select your job title.
*   **Department** (`department`): Enter your department.
*   **Manager Email** (`manager_email`): Enter your manager's email address.
*   **Return Date Confirmed By** (`return_date_confirmed_by`): Select who confirmed your return date.
*   **Return to Work Form** (`return_to_work_form`): Select whether you are willing to return to work.

## Tips
*   Ensure that you have discussed your return to work plan with your manager and team lead before completing this form.
*   Review your form carefully before submitting it for approval.
*   Be aware that this form is not intended for immediate returns. If you are ready to return to work immediately, please select "Immediately (No Notice)" for the return date.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult your manager or team lead for guidance.
