# Long Service Award Recipient Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Long Service Award Recipient Survey is a tool for employees who have achieved a long service milestone in their career with our organization. This form helps to gather feedback and suggestions for future milestone celebrations to recognize and reward their dedication and commitment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your name as it appears on your badge/ID badge or in our HR system.
2. Enter the number of years you have been serving the organization.
3. Select the department(s) you belong to.
4. Provide comments to express your appreciation for this recognition.
5. If you would like to suggest any future milestone celebrations, please select the option that best fits your idea.
6. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the space provided.
7. Enter your email address so we can contact you to confirm receipt of this form.
8. Please provide your contact information so we can reach out to you for further discussion.
9. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (1: `long_service_award_recipient_survey`):
	+ This field requires your name as it appears on your badge/ID badge or in our HR system.
* **Years serving** (2: `years_serving`):
	+ Please enter the number of years you have been serving the organization.
* **Department** (3: `department`):
	+ Please select the department(s) you belong to.
* **Long Service Award Recipient Survey Comments** (4: `long_service_award_recipient_survey_comments`):
	+ This field is for you to express your thoughts and feelings about this recognition.
* **Future Milestone Celebrations** (6: `future_milestone_celebrations`):
	+ Please select one of the options that best fits your idea for future milestone celebrations.
* **Suggestions for future celebrations** (7: `suggestions_for_future_celebrations`):
	+ This field is optional, but if you have any additional ideas or comments, please provide them here.
* **Email** (8: `email`):
	+ Please enter your email address so we can contact you to confirm receipt of this form.
* **Contact** (9: `contact`):
	+ Please provide your contact information so we can reach out to you for further discussion.
* **Comments** (10: `comments`):
	+ This field is optional, but if you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them here.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are completing this form for a valid long service milestone achievement.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
