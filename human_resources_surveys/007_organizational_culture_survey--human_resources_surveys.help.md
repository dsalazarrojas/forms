# Organizational Culture Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Organizational Culture Survey form, helping you understand its purpose and how to complete it. The purpose of this survey is to gather your opinions and perceptions about our organization's culture and values. Your feedback will help us better understand what we do well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by reading the introduction on page 1, "Welcome to our survey."
2. Select all the values that you consider important for our organization on page 2.
3. On each subsequent page, rate your opinion about each value by using the number rating system.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Welcome to our survey** (`page 1`, `note`, required: false): This is an introduction page where you'll be informed about the purpose of the survey and its importance.
* **What values do you consider important for our organization?** (`page 2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the values that you think are essential for our organization to be successful. You can select one or more of the following values:
	+ Leadership
	+ Communication
	+ Respect
	+ Fairness
	+ Trust
* **How would you rate our leadership style** (`page 3`, `number`, required: false): On a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high), rate how you perceive our leadership style.
* **How would you rate our communication style?** (`page 4`, `number`, required: false): On a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high), rate how you perceive our communication style.
* **How would you rate our team work environment?** (`page 5`, `number`, required: false): On a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high), rate how you perceive our team work environment.
* **How would you rate our fairness?** (`page 6`, `number`, required: false): On a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high), rate how you perceive our fairness.
* **How would you rate our feedback style?** (`page 7`, `number`, required: false): On a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high), rate how you perceive our feedback style.
* **How would you rate our culture climate?** (`page 8`, `number`, required: false): On a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high), rate how you perceive our culture climate.
* **How would you rate our management support?** (`page 9`, `number`, required: false): On a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high), rate how you perceive our management support.
* **How would you rate your job satisfaction?** (`page 10`, `number`, required: false): On a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high), rate how satisfied you are with your job.
* **How would you rate your engagement level?** (`page 11`, `number`, required: false): On a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high), rate how engaged you feel in your work.
* **What is your overall impression?** (`page 12`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall impression of our organization on a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high).

## Tips
- Please answer honestly, and take your time to complete the survey.
- If you're unsure about a particular value or question, feel free to ask for clarification.
- Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your participation!
