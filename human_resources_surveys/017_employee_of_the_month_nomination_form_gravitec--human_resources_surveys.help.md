# employee_of_the_month_nomination_form_gravitec - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Employee of the Month" nomination form is used to recognize and reward outstanding employee performance and contributions within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the "Employee Nominator" option: Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are nominating an employee.
2.  Select the "Employee Nominated": Choose "Yes" or "No" to confirm if you are nominating an employee.
3.  Provide the "Nominating Manager" details: Fill in the name of the manager nominating the employee.
4.  Specify the "Reason for Nomination": Provide a clear and concise reason for nominating the employee.
5.  Enter the "Date Nominated": Select the date the employee was nominated.
6.  Select the "Month Nominated": Choose "Yes" or "No" to confirm if the employee was nominated during the current month.
7.  Enter the "Year Nominated": Enter the year the employee was nominated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Nominator** (`Employee Nominator`, select_one, required: false): Choose "Yes" to confirm that you are nominating an employee, or "No" if you are not.
*   **Employee Nominated** (`Employee Nominated`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" to confirm that you are nominating an employee, or "No" if you are not.
*   **Nominating Manager** (`Nominating Manager`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the manager nominating the employee.
*   **Reason for Nomination** (`Reason for Nomination`, text, required: false): Provide a clear and concise reason for nominating the employee.
*   **Date Nominated** (`Date Nominated`, date, required: false): Select the date the employee was nominated.
*   **Month Nominated** (`Month Nominated`, select_one, required: false): Choose "Yes" to confirm if the employee was nominated during the current month, or "No" otherwise.
*   **Year Nominated** (`Year Nominated`, number, required: false): Enter the year the employee was nominated.

## Tips
*   Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
*   Review the form before submitting to avoid errors in the nomination process.
*   If you are unable to complete the form, contact the HR department for assistance.
