# Interview Scorecard Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the outcome of an interview. It captures the candidate's performance and the interviewer's observations to help evaluate the candidate's suitability for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Candidate Name` field with the name of the candidate who was interviewed.
2. Enter the `Interview Date` of the interview in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
3. Enter a numerical value from 1 to 5 in the `Score` field, indicating the candidate's performance.
4. Provide any additional comments about the candidate's performance in the `Comments` field.
5. If applicable, enter a numerical value from 1 to 5 in the `Candidate Interviewer Score` field, indicating the candidate's impression of the interview process.
6. Enter any comments from the candidate about their experience in the `Candidate Comments` field.
7. Enter your name as the `Interviewer Name` in the `Interviewer Name` field.
8. Provide any additional comments about the interview in the `Interviewer Comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Name**: Enter the name of the candidate who was interviewed.
* **Interview Date**: Enter the date of the interview in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Score**: Rate the candidate's performance on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments about the candidate's performance.
* **Candidate Interviewer Score**: If applicable, rate the candidate's impression of the interview process on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Candidate Comments**: Enter any comments from the candidate about their experience.
* **Interviewer Name**: Enter your name as the interviewer.
* **Interviewer Comments**: Provide any additional comments about the interview.
