# Colleague Performance Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help managers, supervisors, and peers evaluate the performance of a colleague in the workplace. It is a comprehensive survey that assesses various aspects of a colleague's work quality, reliability, communication, teamwork, problem-solving skills, initiative, adaptability, time management, job knowledge, and leadership potential. The survey is meant to provide valuable feedback to help colleagues grow and improve their performance.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Introduction
Before starting the evaluation, please ensure that you have a good understanding of the colleague being evaluated and their work habits. This form is designed to be completed by people who work closely with the colleague, such as managers, supervisors, or peers.

### Step 2: Fill Out the Form
1. **Your Name**: Enter your name for the evaluation, if you wish to remain anonymous, you can leave this field blank.
2. **Your Email**: Enter your email address, in case the colleague needs to contact you for further information.
3. **Your Department**: Select the department where you work.
4. **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date of this evaluation.
5. **Colleague Being Evaluated**: Enter the name of the colleague being evaluated.
6. **Colleague Department**: Select the department of the colleague being evaluated.
7. **Colleague Job Title**: Enter the job title of the colleague being evaluated.
8. **Your Working Relationship**: Choose how you work with the colleague, such as Direct Team Member, Cross-Functional Partner, etc.
9. **How Long Have You Worked Together**: Choose how long you have worked with the colleague.
10. **Projects You Have Collaborated On**: Describe the key projects you have worked on with the colleague.
11. **Work Quality Rating**: Rate the colleague's work quality, using the provided scale (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
12. **Work Quality Comments**: Provide specific examples of the colleague's work quality.
13. **Reliability Rating**: Rate the colleague's reliability, using the provided scale (Always Reliable, Usually Reliable, Sometimes Reliable, Rarely Reliable, Not Reliable).
14. **Reliability Comments**: Provide specific examples of the colleague's reliability.
15. **Communication Skills Rating**: Rate the colleague's communication skills, using the provided scale (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
16. **Communication Comments**: Provide specific observations on the colleague's communication skills.
17. **Teamwork Rating**: Rate the colleague's teamwork, using the provided scale (Excellent Team Player, Very Good Team Player, Good Team Player, Fair Team Player, Poor Team Player).
18. **Teamwork Comments**: Provide specific examples of the colleague's teamwork.
19. **Problem Solving Skills Rating**: Rate the colleague's problem-solving skills, using the provided scale (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
20. **Problem Solving Comments**: Provide specific examples of the colleague's problem-solving skills.
21. **Initiative Rating**: Rate the colleague's initiative, using the provided scale (Always Takes Initiative, Usually Takes Initiative, Sometimes Takes Initiative, Rarely Takes Initiative, Waits to Be Told).
22. **Initiative Comments**: Provide specific examples of the colleague's initiative.
23. **Adaptability Rating**: Rate the colleague's adaptability, using the provided scale (Very Adaptable, Adaptable, Somewhat Adaptable, Resistant to Change, Very Resistant).
24. **Adaptability Comments**: Provide specific examples of the colleague's adaptability.
25. **Time Management Rating**: Rate the colleague's time management, using the provided scale (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
26. **Time Management Comments**: Provide specific observations on the colleague's time management.
27. **Job Knowledge and Expertise Rating**: Rate the colleague's job knowledge and expertise, using the provided scale (Expert Level, Very Knowledgeable, Knowledgeable, Developing, Needs Improvement).
28. **Job Knowledge and Expertise Comments**: Provide specific areas of strength for the colleague's job knowledge and expertise.
29. **Top Strengths**: Describe the colleague's top strengths.
30. **Areas for Improvement**: Describe areas where the colleague could improve.
31. **Notable Achievements**: Describe recent accomplishments of the colleague.
32. **Leadership Potential**: Rate the colleague's leadership potential, using the provided scale (Strong Leadership Potential, Some Leadership Potential, Individual Contributor Focus, Not Leadership Oriented, Not Observed).
33. **Would You Want to Work With Them Again**: Choose whether you would want to work with the colleague again.
34. **Overall Recommendation**: Rate the colleague's overall performance, using the provided scale (Highly Recommend, Recommend, Recommend With Reservations, Cannot Recommend).
35. **Additional Feedback**: Provide any additional comments or feedback for the colleague.

### Step 3: Submit the Form
Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to send the evaluation to the colleague and your team.
