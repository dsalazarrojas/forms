# Resume Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resume Questionnaire is a form designed to collect personal and professional information from individuals, including contact details, work experience, educational background, and career goals. This form is intended for job applicants, candidates for internships, or professionals looking to update their resume or LinkedIn profile.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email**, and **Phone** fields with your current and up-to-date information.
2. Enter your **Experience Start Date** and **Experience End Date** for your past work experience.
3. Select your relevant **Work Experience** from the dropdown options (Software developer, Data analyst, Data scientist).
4. Enter your **Education Start Date** and **Education End Date** if applicable.
5. List your current and desired **Career Goals**.
6. Select your relevant **Skills** from the dropdown options (Software development, Data analysis, Data science).
7. Finally, provide a brief **Objective** describing your professional goals and aspirations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your up-to-date email address for professional contact.
* **Phone**: Enter your up-to-date phone number.
* **Experience Start Date**: Enter the date you started your current or previous work experience.
* **Experience End Date**: Enter the date you ended your current or previous work experience.
* **Current company**: Enter the name of your current or previous company.
* **Current position**: Enter your current or previous job title.
* **Skills**: Select all relevant skills that you possess, such as Software development, Data analysis, or Data science.
* **Career goals**: Enter a brief description of your short-term and long-term career goals.
* **Objective**: Provide a brief description of your professional goals and aspirations.
* **Work Experience 1-12**: Select the relevant work experience that best describes your job title.
