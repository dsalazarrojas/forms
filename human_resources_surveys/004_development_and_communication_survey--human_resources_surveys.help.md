# Development And Communication Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback and input from team members on their experiences, opinions, and suggestions related to team collaboration, manager feedback, growth opportunities, communication channels, work hours, job satisfaction, and manager performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions on each page, and click "Submit" at the end. You can save your progress and come back later if needed. The form is divided into the following sections:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What do you think about collaboration within the team?** (`collaboration`, text, required: false): Think about your interactions with your team members. What do you like about working with them? What could be improved?
* **How do you rate your manager's feedback?** (`feedback`, number, required: false): Rate your manager's feedback on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "not helpful" and 5 being "very helpful".
* **What opportunities do you see for growth within the company?** (`growth_opportunities`, text, required: false): Think about areas where you'd like to see the company grow and develop in the future.
* **What communication channels within the team?** (`communication_channels`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the communication channels you use or would like to use within the team (e.g., email, internal tools, phone).
* **What hours do you work?** (`work_hours`, text, required: false): Describe your typical work hours or schedule.
* **How do you rate your job satisfaction?** (`job_satisfaction`, number, required: false): Rate your job satisfaction on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "very dissatisfied" and 5 being "very satisfied".
* **How do you rate your manager's performance?** (`manager_performance`, number, required: false): Rate your manager's performance on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "very poor" and 5 being "excellent".
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback about the Development and Communication Survey?** (`development_and_communication_survey_comments`, text, required: false): Share any additional thoughts or suggestions you have about the survey or its questions.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* You can provide as much or as little information as you feel comfortable sharing.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to ask your manager or HR representative.
