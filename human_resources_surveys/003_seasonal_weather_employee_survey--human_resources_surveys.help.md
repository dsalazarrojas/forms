# Seasonal Weather Employee Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees about their experiences with seasonal weather on site. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and provide better support during extreme weather conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the page you want to answer, and start filling out the form.
2. For each question, provide as much detail as possible in the text fields or select one of the available options.
3. Submit your responses when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Winter Work Conditions
* **How was your winter work experience?** (`winter_work_conditions`, text, required: false): Please describe your experience working on site during winter conditions. What challenges did you face, and how did you handle them?

### Page 2: Extreme Weather Support
* **How was extreme weather support on site?** (`extreme_weather_support`, text, required: false): Share your thoughts on how we supported you during extreme weather conditions. Were our procedures effective, or were there areas of improvement?

### Page 3: Safety Practices
* **Were safety practices effective in extreme weather?** (`safety_practices`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the options that best describes your experience:
	+ Option 1: Effective and well-communicated
	+ Option 2: Somewhat effective, but room for improvement
	+ Option 3: Not effective

### Page 4: Feedback and Suggestions
* **Do you have any feedback or suggestions for improving safety practices?** (`feedback_and_suggestions`, text, required: false): Share any thoughts you have on how we can improve our safety practices and better support you during extreme weather conditions.

## Tips
* Please be honest and as detailed as possible with your responses.
* If you have any additional information or context that may help us improve our practices, please include it in the text fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
