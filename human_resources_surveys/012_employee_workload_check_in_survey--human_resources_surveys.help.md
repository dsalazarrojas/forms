# Employee Workload Check In Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help team managers and HR assess the workload and well-being of each team member. Your honest responses will help us better understand your current workload and identify areas where we can support you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately** to ensure the data is useful for team management and support.
2. **Use the "Yes/No" options** to report your current workload and stress levels. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your team manager or HR representative.
3. **Enter your work hours per week**, including any overtime or extra hours you have worked recently.
4. **Mark your days off** on the designated date field. If you are unsure about your days off or work hours, please contact your team manager or HR representative for guidance.
5. **Submit the form** once you have filled out all required fields. If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, contact your team manager or HR representative for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name as it is recorded in our HR system.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your last name as it is recorded in our HR system.
* **Job Title** (Text): Enter your current job title as assigned by your team manager.
* **Team Member ID** (Text): Enter your unique team member ID provided by your HR representative.
* **Workload** (Select multiple): Report your current workload status. Choose 'Yes' if you are feeling overwhelmed or have a heavy workload, and 'No' if you have a manageable workload.
* **Stress** (Select multiple): Report your current stress levels. Choose 'Yes' if you are feeling stressed, and 'No' if you are feeling calm.
* **Burnout** (Select multiple): Report your current burnout levels. Choose 'Yes' if you are feeling burnt out, and 'No' if you are feeling energized.
* **Work Hours** (Number): Enter the number of hours you have worked in the past week. If you have worked extra hours, include those in the total.
* **Work Hours Per Week** (Number): Enter your regular working hours per week.
* **Days Off** (Date): Mark any dates you have scheduled or planned for time off. If you are unsure about your days off, please contact your team manager or HR representative for guidance.
