# employee_engagment_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee engagement survey is a tool used to gather feedback from employees on their role and how they contribute to the team's overall well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, including all required and optional fields.
2. For fields with multiple options, select the most accurate answer for your role.
3. If a field is not applicable to your role, please select "No" or leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name, as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Last Name**: Enter your last name, as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Role**: Select your current role within the organization (Yes/No).
*   **Department**: Select your department within the organization (Yes/No).
*   **Date**: Enter the date you are submitting the form.
*   **Time**: Enter the time you are submitting the form.
*   **Email**: Enter your official email address.
*   **Phone**: Enter your official phone number.
*   **Feedback**: Provide any additional feedback or comments about your role in the organization.
*   **Manager**: Select your manager's status (Yes/No).
*   **Team Lead**: Select your team lead status (Yes/No).
*   **Direct Reports**: Select if you have direct reports (Yes/No).
*   **Indirect Reports**: Select if you have indirect reports (Yes/No).
*   **Team Members**: Select if you have team members (Yes/No).
