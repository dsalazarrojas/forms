# Facebook Ad Marketer Recruitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential candidates for Facebook Ad Marketer roles. It will be used to evaluate and shortlist potential candidates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Facebook Ad Marketer Recruitment Form**: Enter a title for the job posting.
2. **Recruiter Name**: Enter your name as the recruiter for the job.
3. **Job Title**: Enter the title of the job you are recruiting for.
4. **Job Budget**: Enter the budget allocated for the job.
5. **Ad Frequency**: Select how often you want to advertise:
	* Daily
	* Weekly
	* Monthly
6. **Ad Budget**: Enter the budget for the ad campaign.
7. **Target Audience**: Enter details about the target audience for the ad.
8. **Ad Criteria**: Enter any specific criteria required for the ad campaign.
9. **Ad Duration**: Enter the duration of the ad campaign in hours/minutes.
10. **Ad Start Date**: Enter the start date of the ad campaign.
11. **Ad End Date**: Enter the end date of the ad campaign.
12. **Ad Frequency** (Repeat): Select how often you want to advertise (this is a repeat field to capture multiple frequencies):
	* Daily
	* Weekly
	* Monthly
13. **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
14. **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
15. **Note**: Enter any additional information you want to share about the job.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facebook Ad Marketer Recruitment Form** (`form_title`, `text`, **required: false`): Enter a short and concise title for the job posting.
* **Recruiter Name** (`recruiter_name`, `text`, **required: false`): This field captures the name of the recruiter for the job posting.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, **required: false`): Enter the title of the job you are recruiting for.
* **Job Budget** (`job_budget`, `text`, **required: false`): Enter the budget allocated for the job.
* **Ad Frequency** (`ad_frequency`, `select_one`, **required: false`): Select how often you want to advertise:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
* **Ad Budget** (`ad_budget`, `number`, **required: false`): Enter the budget for the ad campaign.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, `text`, **required: false`): Enter details about the target audience for the ad campaign.
* **Ad Criteria** (`ad_criteria`, `text`, **required: false`): Enter any specific criteria required for the ad campaign.
* **Ad Duration** (`ad_duration`, `time`, **required: false`): Enter the duration of the ad campaign in hours/minutes.
* **Ad Start Date** (`ad_start_date`, `date`, **required: false`): Enter the start date of the ad campaign.
* **Ad End Date** (`ad_end_date`, `date`, **required: false`): Enter the end date of the ad campaign.
* **Ad Frequency** (Repeat) (`ad_frequency`, `select_multiple`, **required: false`): Select how often you want to advertise (this is a repeat field to capture multiple frequencies):
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **required: false`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, **required: false`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, **required: false`): Enter any additional information you want to share about the job.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure that your job posting is complete.
* Use the "Ad Frequency" field to capture multiple frequencies for the ad campaign.
* Use the "Note" field for any additional information about the job.
