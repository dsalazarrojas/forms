# Staff Skills Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Skills Survey is a form to gather information about your interests, skills, and experience to help us better understand your strengths and areas for development. This information will be used to identify potential opportunities for you to take on new challenges and grow in your role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the "Staff Survey" text field with a brief description of your current role and responsibilities.
2. In the "Which tasks are you interested in taking on?" section, select the tasks that you're interested in taking on.
3. In the "How much experience do you have with responsibility?" section, select the level of experience you have with taking on responsibilities.
4. In the "How interested are you in developing your skills?" section, select the level of interest you have in developing your skills.
5. If you have time available, enter the number of hours you can commit to the project in the "Time" field.
6. If you have any additional comments or notes, you can add them in the "Additional comments" field.
7. Finally, enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Survey** (`staff_survey`, text, required/optional): This field is a text area where you can describe your current role and responsibilities.
* **Which tasks are you interested in taking on?** (`task_interest`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field allows you to select the tasks you're interested in taking on. Select as many tasks as you're interested in.
* **How much experience do you have with responsibility?** (`responsibility_experience`, select_one, required/optional): This field asks about your level of experience with taking on responsibilities. Select High, Medium, or Low based on your experience.
* **How interested are you in developing your skills?** (`skill_interest`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field asks about your level of interest in developing your skills. Select as many options as you're interested in.
* **How much time can you commit to this project?** (`time_commitment`, number, required/optional): This field is optional, but if you have time available, please enter the number of hours you can commit to the project.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): This field is for your convenience, but not required. If you want to keep track of when you completed the survey, you can enter a date.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): This field is for your convenience, but not required. If you want to keep track of the time you completed the survey, you can enter a time.
* **Additional comments** (`note`, note, required/optional): This is a text area where you can add any additional comments or notes about your interests and skills.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): This field is for your contact information, in case we need to follow up with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): This field is for your phone number, in case we need to contact you. Please enter a 10-digit number.
