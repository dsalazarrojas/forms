# Home Office Allowance Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant for employees to apply for home office allowance. If you're an employee seeking to work from home, this form will help us understand your needs and determine the feasibility of allowing you to work from home.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have the permission to work from home from your manager or supervisor.
2. Fill out the form carefully, providing all required information.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Office Allowance Application Form**: This is the title of the form, which you are currently filling out.
* **Employee Name**: This field is for your employee ID or name, which helps us identify you in our system.
* **Prophets**: Select the correct prophet that you would like to work with, if any. 
* **Allowance Amount**: Enter the amount of time (in hours) you are requesting as home office allowance. This will be used to calculate your actual allowance. 
* **Employee Type**: Select the type of employee you are (e.g. Employee A, Employee B, etc.).
* **Start Date** and **End Date**: These fields are used to determine the dates you would like to work from home. 
* **Work Hours**: Enter the number of hours you work per week. 
* **Benefits**: If you'd like to add any notes about your benefits, please use this field to explain.
* **Email**: This is your contact email address, where we will send you any follow-up questions or approval notifications.
* **Phone Number**: If you'd like, please provide your contact phone number for easy communication.
* **Address**: Enter your work address, which is where you'd like to work from home.
* **Work Location**: If you'd like, please specify where your work location will be.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title, which will help us understand your role.
* **Department**: Enter your department, which will help us understand your work context.
* **Supervisor**: Enter your supervisor's name, which will help us understand your work flow.
* **Prophets**: Select the correct prophets that you would like to work with.
* **Work Hours Per Week**: Enter the number of hours you work per week.
* **Work Hours Per Day**: Enter the number of hours you work per day.
* **Days Worked Per Week**: Enter the number of days you work per week.
* **Start Time** and **End Time**: These fields are used to determine your work hours and schedule.
* **Hours Prophets**: Select the number of prophets you have worked with in the past.
* **Submitted Date**: This field is automatically populated with the date you submit the form.
* **Phone Number**: This is your contact phone number, which will be used for communication. 

Please fill out this form carefully, and don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or need help with any of the fields.
