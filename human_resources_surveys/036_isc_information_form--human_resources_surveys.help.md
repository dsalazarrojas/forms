## isc_information_form - Help Guide
### Purpose
The form is used to collect information about jobs, including job title, department, job description, job status, job level, manager, and job type.

### How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the job title in the text field. This is a required field.
2. Select the department from the dropdown list. This field is optional.
3. Enter a brief description of the job in the text field. This field is optional.
4. Choose the current status of the job from the dropdown list. This field is optional.
5. Select multiple job levels that apply to the job. For example, if the job is a junior and a manager, select both options. This field is optional.
6. Type in the name of the manager's name. This field is optional.
7. Select the type of job from the dropdown list. This field is optional.

### Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title** (job_title, text, required): Enter a brief description of the job title. This will help us identify the job and its responsibilities.
* **Department** (department, select_one, optional): Choose the department that the job belongs to. This will help us categorize the job and its related tasks.
* **Job Description** (job_description, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the job duties and responsibilities. This will help us understand the job's requirements and expectations.
* **Job Status** (job_status, select_one, optional): Choose the current status of the job, such as "Open", "Closed", or "On-Hold". This will help us track the job's progress and completion.
* **Job Level** (job_level, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple job levels that apply to the job. For example, if the job is both junior and manager, select both options. This will help us understand the job's complexity and requirements.
* **Manager** (manager, text, optional): Enter the name of the manager's name. This will help us identify the manager's name and role.
* **Job Type** (job_type, select_one, optional): Choose the type of job from the dropdown list. This will help us categorize the job and its characteristics.

### Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that we receive a complete and accurate submission.
