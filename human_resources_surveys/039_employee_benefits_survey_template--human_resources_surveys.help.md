# Employee Benefits Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

This template is designed to collect information about employee benefits and job performance. It's intended to help the company understand what benefits are currently offered, how they are received by employees, and what areas may need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have a clear understanding of the purpose and the questions being asked.
2. Complete each field thoroughly and accurately.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.
4. If a field is optional, only answer it if the information is available or relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Information** (`general_information`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the employee's general information.
  This field is intended for any additional comments or explanations about the employee's benefits.
- **Benefits** (`benefits`, select_multiple, required): Select all the benefits you have access to.
  This field asks about the employee's benefits, such as Life Insurance, Health Insurance, 401K, Vision, and Dental.
- **Job Performance** (`job_performance`, date, optional): Enter the date of your last performance review.
  This field is intended to capture information about the employee's job performance and any relevant dates.
- **Work Status** (`work_status`, select_one, required): Select where you work from.
  This field asks about the employee's current work status, which could be working from home, working from office, or working from both.
- **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, number, required): Rate your job satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
  This field asks you to rate your job satisfaction on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
- **Benefits Manager** (`benefits_manager`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for benefits.
  This field is intended to capture information about the person responsible for benefits.
- **Company Benefits** (`company_benefits`, note, optional): Enter any additional company benefits information.
  This field is a free-text field for any additional information about company benefits.
- **Job Title Changes** (`job_title_changes`, text, optional): Enter any job title changes since last survey.
  This field is intended to capture any job title changes since the last survey.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when completing the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the benefits manager.
* This survey is intended to be completed once per employee.
