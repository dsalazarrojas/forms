### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the job category from the dropdown menu.
2.  Enter the job title and description.
3.  Choose the job type (full-time, part-time, or contract).
4.  Enter the job location.
5.  List the job requirements.
6.  Select relevant tags for the job.
7.  Choose the job posting status (active, archived, or cancelled).
8.  Enter the job posting date and expected start date.
9.  Choose the job posting time.
10. Enter the contact name and email.
11. Enter the job advertisement ID and code.
12. Select the job advertisement type.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Category**: Select the category under which the job ad will be posted.
* **Job Title**: Enter the title of the job opening.
* **Job Description**: Provide a detailed description of the job.
* **Job Type**: Choose the type of job (full-time, part-time, or contract).
* **Job Location**: Enter the location of the job.
* **Job Requirements**: List the requirements and qualifications for the job.
* **Job Tags**: Select relevant tags for the job ad.
* **Job Posting Status**: Choose the status of the job posting (active, archived, or cancelled).
* **Job Posting Date**: Enter the date the job was posted.
* **Job Expected Start Date**: Enter the expected start date of the job.
* **Job Posting Time**: Choose the time the job was posted.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the contact name for the job ad.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact email for the job ad.
* **Job Advertisement ID**: Enter a unique ID for the job ad.
* **Job Advertisement Code**: Enter a code for the job ad.
* **Job Advertisement Type**: Select the type of job ad (Type 1, Type 2, or Type 3).
