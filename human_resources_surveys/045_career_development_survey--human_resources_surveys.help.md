# Career Development Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Career Development Survey is designed to gather information from employees about their career development needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by reviewing the form**: Take a moment to understand what each field is asking for.
2. **Complete each field**: Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. **Submit your feedback**: Once you've completed the form, click "Submit" to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee name**: This is your name, which will help us identify your responses.
* **Interested in training opportunities**: Select how interested you are in training and development opportunities. Please choose one of the following options:
	+ Very interested
	+ Somewhat interested
	+ Not interested
* **Preferred training topics**: Choose one or more topics you're interested in learning more about:
	+ Leadership
	+ Technical skills
	+ Communication
	+ Diversity and inclusion
* **Preferred delivery method**: Select how you prefer to receive training:
	+ In person
	+ Online live
	+ Self-paced
* **Barriers to participation**: If you have any time budget or access issues that prevent you from participating in training, please describe them in this field.
* **Expected outcomes from training**: Share what you hope to achieve or accomplish through training, such as skills or certifications.
* **Timeline for development**: Indicate when you expect to start your career development journey:
	+ Within 3 months
	+ Within 6 months
	+ Within 12 months
* **Manager support available**: Check if your manager is supportive of your career development goals:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Additional comments**: Share any other thoughts or comments you have about your career development needs and preferences.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when completing the form, as your feedback will help us create more effective development programs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to your manager or HR representative.
* Remember, this is your opportunity to share your thoughts and preferences, so take a few minutes to think carefully about each question.
