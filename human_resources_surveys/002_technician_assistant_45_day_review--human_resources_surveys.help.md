# Technician Assistant 45 Day Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and insights from new technicians on their experience in their first 45 days of work as assistant technicians. This information will be used to identify areas of improvement and provide support to help new technicians succeed in their position.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow the instructions below to complete this form:

1. Answer the questions on each page to the best of your ability.
2. Use the text fields (like the ones labeled as "What did you learn...") to provide detailed explanations.
3. Select the best answer from the options provided for the "How was your first month of work?" question.
4. Make sure to provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Further comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Technician Assistant 45-Day Review** (`title`, `text`, optional): A brief description of your experience as a new technician assistant.
* **How was your first month of work?** (`first_month_experience`, `select_one`, optional): This question helps us understand how you felt about your initial experience as a technician assistant. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Needs Improvement
* **Further comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Please add any additional comments or feedback about your experience as a technician assistant.
* **What did you learn in your first month as a technician assistant?** (`first_month_comments`, `text`, optional): This is your opportunity to share any specific knowledge or skills you gained in your first month.
* **What do you see as your biggest accomplishment in your second month?** (`second_month_comments`, `text`, optional): Share your greatest achievement from your second month as a technician assistant.
