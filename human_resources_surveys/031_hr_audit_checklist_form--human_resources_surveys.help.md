# HR Audit Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help HR teams conduct an audit of various HR-related processes and practices within an organization. It covers essential areas such as company policies, employee data, benefits, compliance reporting, background checks, training, separation and termination, employee classified information, and employee classifications. By using this form, HR teams can identify areas that need improvement and develop strategies for maintaining compliance and best practices.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these general steps:

1. Review each page and select "Yes" or "No" for each field, indicating whether the relevant process or practice is currently in place within your organization.
2. Select "Yes" if the process or practice is being followed, and "No" if it is not.
3. If you need more information or clarification about a particular field, use the space provided to provide additional comments or explanations.
4. Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Policies** (`company_policies`): Select "Yes" if your organization has policies in place for topics such as employee conduct, performance evaluation, and termination. Select "No" if these policies are missing or need revision.
* **Employee Data** (`employee_data`): Check if your organization has policies and processes in place for maintaining accurate and up-to-date employee records, including personal data, employment history, and performance reviews.
* **Benefits** (`benefits`): Select "Yes" if your organization provides various employee benefits such as health insurance, retirement plans, and paid time off. Select "No" if your organization does not offer these benefits.
* **Compliance Reporting** (`compliance_reporting`): Check if your organization has processes in place for reporting compliance with laws and regulations, such as tax laws, labor laws, and data protection laws.
* **Background Checks** (`background_checks`): Select "Yes" if your organization conducts background checks for all employees, including new hires, contractors, and vendors. Select "No" if background checks are not done.
* **Training** (`training`): Check if your organization has training programs for employees on topics such as workplace safety, sexual harassment prevention, and diversity and inclusion.
* **Separation and Termination** (`separation_termination`): Select "Yes" if your organization has clear procedures in place for employee separations and terminations, including notice periods, exit interviews, and final payments.
* **Employee Classified Information** (`employee_classified_info`): Check if your organization has policies and procedures in place for handling sensitive employee information, such as mental health, disability, or financial data.
* **Employee Classifications** (`employee_classifications`): Select "Yes" if your organization has clear categories and definitions for different employee roles, including classifications for management, non-exempt, and exempt employees.
* **Employee Histories** (`employee_historics`): Check if your organization maintains accurate and up-to-date employee histories, including employment dates, job changes, and performance evaluations. 

**Tips**

* Make sure to review the form carefully and complete all required fields.
* If you need help or clarification about any field, use the space provided for additional comments.
* Keep the form up-to-date to reflect changes in your organization's HR practices and policies.
