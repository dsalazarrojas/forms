# Timekeeping Compliance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Timekeeping Compliance Survey is designed to gather information on the compliance of timekeeping processes and policies within the organization. This survey will help identify areas of concern and opportunities for improvement in timekeeping processes and policies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the most relevant option for each question.
2. If none of the options match your situation, select "Other" and provide a brief explanation in the "Comments" field.
3. Enter your Employee ID number in the "Employee ID" field.
4. Fill out the "Date" field with the date you are completing this survey.
5. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Manager Comments" field.
6. Complete any other questions as needed, following the same guidelines.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Timekeeping Survey**: This question asks you to select the best option for what timekeeping process you have in place for keeping track of your work hours.
* **Payroll and Managers**: Select all managers and payroll systems that you have interacted with or reported to in your current role.
* **Training Needs**: Identify which training programs you need to improve your timekeeping skills.
* **Timekeeping Processes**: Select all timekeeping processes that you have used in your role.
* **Policies and Systems**: Indicate which policies and systems are relevant to your job.
* **Feedback**: List any feedback or comments you have on the timekeeping process or policy you selected.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback you have on your experience with the timekeeping process or policy.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your unique employee ID number.
* **Manager Comments**: List any comments or feedback your manager has on the timekeeping process or policy you selected.
* **Date**: Enter the date you are completing this survey.
* **Time**: Enter the time you are completing this survey.
* **Policies Compliance**: Select the level of compliance with timekeeping policies.
* **Training Programs**: Select all training programs that you have attended or would like to attend to improve your timekeeping skills.
* **Payroll Systems**: Select the payroll system(s) you have interacted with or reported to in your role.
* **Systems Improvement**: List any areas where the timekeeping system could be improved.
* **Survey Completed**: Indicate that you have completed this survey.

**Tips**:
* Please select all relevant options for each question.
* Be honest in your responses, as this will help identify areas for improvement.
* If you are unsure about any question, select "Other" and provide a brief explanation.
* Make sure to enter the correct date and time you are completing this survey.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, enter them in the "Comments" field. 

Please note that all fields are optional except for fields marked as "required" in the form. If a field is required, please make sure to fill it out accordingly. 

This help guide is not exhaustive, and you can always reach out to the survey administrators if you have any questions or need further clarification.
