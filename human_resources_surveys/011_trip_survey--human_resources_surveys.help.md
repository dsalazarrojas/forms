# Trip Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about employee trips. Please provide accurate and honest information to help the organization track and manage employee travel.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field accurately and thoroughly, using the options provided.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. Double-check your answers for spelling and accuracy.
4. Submit the form once you have filled out all necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trip Survey Page** (`trip_survey_page`, text, required/optional): This page is a general description of your trip.
* **Trip Details** (`trip_details`, text, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of your trip, including any necessary context or background information.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your trip.
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you plan to return from your trip.
* **Flight Number** (`flight_number`, text, required/optional): If you took a flight, enter the flight number.
* **Flight Carrier** (`flight_carrier`, text, required/optional): If you took a flight, enter the flight carrier name.
* **Travel Class** (`travel_class`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of travel class (First, Economy, Business).
* **Travel Agency** (`travel_agent`, select_multiple, required/optional): If applicable, select the travel agency you used.
* **Trip Status** (`trip_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the current status of your trip (Approved, Denied, Pending).
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your trip.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your work phone number.
* **Travel Type** (`travel_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of travel (Business, Personal, Other).
* **Travel Purpose** (`travel_purpose`, text, required/optional): Describe the purpose of your trip.
* **Travel Duration** (`travel_duration`, number, required/optional): Enter the duration of your trip in days.
* **Travel Funds** (`travel_funds`, number, required/optional): Indicate the amount of travel funds allocated for the trip.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask a supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
* If your trip is not a business trip, please select 'Other' for travel type.
