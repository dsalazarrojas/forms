# Role Rotation Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Role Rotation Feedback Survey is designed to gather information about your experience with a specific rotation, allowing us to understand your overall satisfaction and suggestions for improvement. Please take a few minutes to provide your feedback, and it will help us make the rotation process better for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Read each question carefully and answer them accordingly.
* Select the most suitable option for each field.
* If you have any comments or suggestions, please provide them in the "Any comments or suggestions?" field.
* Make sure to complete all questions that are marked as "required" if you have an opinion or experience to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was your overall experience with the rotation?** (`rotation_experience`, Select One, Optional)
  This question asks about your overall experience with the rotation. Choose the option that best represents how you felt about the rotation.
  - Excellent
  - Good
  - Fair
  - Poor

* **How valuable was the experience to you?** (`rotation_value`, Select Multiple, Optional)
  This question helps us understand how valuable you found the rotation to be. Choose all options that apply to your experience.
  - Very Valuable
  - Somewhat Valuable
  - Not Very Valuable
  - Not Valuable

* **How satisfied are you with your role?** (`role_satisfaction`, Select One, Optional)
  This question measures your satisfaction level with your role in the rotation. Select the option that best describes how you feel.
  - Very Satisfied
  - Somewhat Satisfied
  - Not Very Satisfied
  - Not Satisfied

* **Would you recommend this rotation to others?** (`rotation_recommend`, Select One, Optional)
  If you would recommend this rotation to others, please select "True". If not, select "False".

* **How can we improve the rotation process?** (`rotation_improve`, Text, Optional)
  This is your chance to share any suggestions or ideas on how we can improve the rotation process.

* **Any comments or suggestions?** (`comments`, Note, Optional)
  If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use this field to share them with us.

* **When did you start the rotation?** (`rotation_date`, Date, Optional)
  Please enter the date you started the rotation.

* **When did the rotation end?** (`rotation_end_date`, Date, Optional)
  Please enter the date the rotation ended.

* **How long was the rotation?** (`rotation_length`, Time, Optional)
  Please enter the length of the rotation in hours, minutes, or days.

* **Overall rating of rotation experience** (`role_rotation_rating`, Number, Optional)
  Rate your overall experience with the rotation on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

Note: This guide is designed to be user-friendly and easy to understand. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please let us know.
