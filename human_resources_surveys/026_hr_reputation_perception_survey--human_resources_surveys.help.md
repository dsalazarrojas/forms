# HR Reputation Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to collect feedback on how employees perceive the Human Resources (HR) department.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
2. You can select one option for each question from the provided list.
3. For question 4, you can type in any suggestions or ideas you have for how HR could improve.
4. For question 5, you can provide any additional comments or thoughts you have about HR.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you rate HR's responsiveness to your queries?** (`question_1`, select_one)
This question asks about your overall experience with HR in responding to your queries or requests. Please select the option that best describes your experience.

* **Is HR approachable?** (`question_2`, select_one)
This question is about whether you feel comfortable approaching HR with your questions or concerns. Select 'True' if you find HR approachable, and 'False' if you do not.

* **How likely are you to recommend HR to a colleague?** (`question_3`, select_one)
This question is about your willingness to recommend HR to a colleague based on your experience. Select the option that best describes your likelihood.

* **What would you like to suggest that HR could improve?** (`question_4`, note)
This question is an opportunity for you to provide any suggestions or ideas you have on how HR could improve. Please be as specific as possible with your feedback.

* **Is there anything else you'd like to tell us?** (`question_5`, note)
This question is for any additional comments or thoughts you have about HR that may not be directly related to the previous questions.
