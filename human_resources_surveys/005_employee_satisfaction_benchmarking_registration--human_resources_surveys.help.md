# Employee Satisfaction Benchmarking Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from employees about their satisfaction levels with their organization. This form is designed to help organizations benchmark their employee satisfaction and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Organization Details (Page 1)**: Fill in the following details about your organization:
	* Provide the name of your organization.
	* Add any other relevant information about your organization (optional).
2. **Objectives (Page 2)**: Describe the objectives of your organization, including your mission, vision, and values.
3. **Participant Details (Page 3)**: Fill in the participant's details, including their name, job title, and department.
4. **Enroll Participants (Page 4)**: List the participants who will be taking the satisfaction survey.
5. **Registration Confirmation (Page 5)**: Confirm your registration and submit the form.
