# Supervisor Leadership Skills Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess a supervisor's leadership skills, providing a comprehensive evaluation of their abilities in areas such as leadership skills, interpersonal skills, communication skills, and more. The results of this form will help in providing feedback and guidance for the supervisor's development and growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by reading through the form carefully and making sure you understand what is being asked.
2. Answer all required sections (indicated by an asterisk *).
3. For the "Supervisor Leadership Skills" question, select all the skills you feel the supervisor demonstrates effectively.
4. For the "Feedback Frequency" question, choose the most common frequency with which feedback is given to the supervisor.
5. For the "Action Items" question, select all the actions that the supervisor should take to improve their leadership skills.
6. Finally, schedule the next meeting in the provided date option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction** (text, required): This is a placeholder section for an introduction to the form. Please fill it in with a brief description of the purpose and objectives of the survey.

### Supervisor Leadership Skills
* **Supervisor Leadership Skills** (select_multiple, required): This section asks you to rate the supervisor's leadership skills. Please select all the skills that you feel the supervisor demonstrates effectively. The options include:
	+ Leadership skills
	+ Interpersonal skills
	+ Communication skills
	+ Problem-solving skills
	+ Decision-making skills
	+ Time management skills
	+ Adaptability
	+ Teamwork
	+ Flexibility

### Feedback Frequency
* **Feedback Frequency** (select_one, required): This question asks about the frequency with which feedback is given to the supervisor. Please choose the most common frequency from the options:
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Semi-annually
	+ Annually

### Action Items
* **Action Items** (select_multiple, required): This section is used to identify specific actions the supervisor should take to improve their leadership skills. Please select all the actions that you feel are relevant. The options include:
	+ Develop a development plan to address areas needing improvement
	+ Provide feedback on the development plan to the participant
	+ Follow up on progress of action items

### Follow Up
* **Follow Up** (date, required): This section asks for the date to schedule the next meeting with the supervisor to discuss the development plan and progress. Please select the date for the next meeting.

### Next Steps
* **Next Steps** (select_one, required): This question asks about the next meeting schedule with the supervisor. Please choose the schedule option for the next meeting. The options include:
	+ Schedule the next meeting in 30 days
	+ Schedule the next meeting in 60 days
	+ Schedule the next meeting in 90 days

### Manager Feedback
* **Manager Feedback** (text, optional): This section is for the manager's feedback on the supervisor. Please provide any comments or suggestions you have for the supervisor's development and growth.

### Peer Feedback
* **Peer Feedback** (text, optional): This section is for peer feedback on the supervisor. Please provide any comments or suggestions you have for the supervisor's development and growth.

### Self-Assessment
* **Self-Assessment** (text, optional): This section is for the supervisor's self-assessment. Please provide any comments or suggestions you have for your own development and growth.

### Development Plan
* **Development Plan** (text, optional): This section is for the supervisor to provide their development plan. Please outline any areas you feel need improvement and any steps you plan to take to address them.

### Next Steps
This is the last section of the form. Please make sure to complete all required sections and review your answers before submitting the form.
