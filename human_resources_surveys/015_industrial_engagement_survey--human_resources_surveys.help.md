# Industrial Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your engagement and participation within the organization. This information will help us understand your level of involvement in work-related activities and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to enter your first and last name in the first name and last name fields.
3. Provide your job title and department where you currently work.
4. Enter your work phone number and email address in the work phone and work email fields.
5. Select your job function from the options provided.
6. Choose your manager's name from the options provided.
7. Select your work shift (if applicable) using the time field.
8. Provide a note (if desired) to elaborate on any specific comments or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Your first name should be entered here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Your last name should be entered here.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your current job title.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required/optional): Enter your department where you work.
* **Manager Name** (`manager`, text, required/optional): Choose your manager's name from the options provided.
* **Work Phone** (`work_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your work phone number.
* **Work Phone Extension** (`work_phone_ext`, text, required/optional): Enter your work phone extension (if applicable).
* **Work Email** (`work_email`, text, required/optional): Enter your work email address.
* **Work Email Extension** (`work_email_ext`, text, required/optional): Enter your work email extension (if applicable).
* **Job Title 2** (`job_title_2`, text, required/optional): Enter your current job title.
* **Manager Extension** (`manager_ext`, text, required/optional): Choose your manager's extension from the options provided.
* **Work Extension** (`work_ext`, text, required/optional): Enter your work extension (if applicable).
* **Manager Extension 2** (`manager_ext2`, text, required/optional): Choose your manager's extension 2 from the options provided.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Select the date you were hired.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Select your work shift (if applicable).
* **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): Provide any specific comments or concerns.
* **What** (`select_one_1`, select, required/optional): Choose the option that best describes your engagement level.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple_1`, select multiple, required/optional): Choose multiple options that describe your engagement level.
* **Select One 2** (`select_one_2`, select, required/optional): Choose the option that best describes your engagement level.
* **Select Multiple 2** (`select_multiple_2`, select multiple, required/optional): Choose multiple options that describe your engagement level.
* **Select One 3** (`select_one_3`, select, required/optional): Choose the option that best describes your engagement level.

## Tips
* Please make sure to be as accurate and truthful as possible when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any information, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
* This form is not for sharing sensitive or confidential information.
* If you have any further questions, concerns, or need help, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
