# self_assessment_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is used for employees to evaluate their own performance and development in various areas of their job, such as leadership, communication, and time management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email, and phone number as requested.
2. Select your current job title if applicable.
3. Rate your performance in the given areas (Excellent, Good, Average, or Needs improvement).
4. Choose your department and select which training programs you have participated in (Leadership and Management, Communication and Interpersonal Relations, Time Management, or Other).
5. Fill in the number of hours you have trained in the last 5 years.
6. Rate your job performance again for comparison with previous assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address as it appears in the system.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your work phone number.
* **Date of Survey**: This is the date that corresponds to this assessment. Please ensure it's up to date.
* **Training Hours**: Enter the number of hours you have trained in the last 5 years.
* **Job Performance**: Rate your job performance on a scale of Excellent, Good, Average, or Needs Improvement.
* **Department**: Select your current department from the given options.
* **Training Programs**: Choose which training programs you have participated in.
* **Job Title**: Enter your current job title.
* **Training Hours 5 Years Ago**: Enter the number of hours you have trained 5 years ago.

## Tips

* Be honest when rating your job performance.
* Ensure you have selected all relevant training programs you have participated in.
* If you have any other training programs, please add them under "Other" in the training programs field.
* Ensure you have filled out this form before, and you are up-to-date with your last assessment.
