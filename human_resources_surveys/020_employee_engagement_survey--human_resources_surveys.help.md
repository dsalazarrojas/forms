# Employee Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect employee feedback on various aspects of their work experience, including satisfaction with their job, communication with management, and support from their team and manager. This information helps the company understand employee engagement and make informed decisions to improve the work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Answer all the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best fit your experience.
3. Be as descriptive as possible in the text fields.
4. Submit your feedback when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Questions** (`survey_questions`, text, optional): Briefly describe your current job and its responsibilities.
* **Employee Satisfaction** (`employee_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with your job on a scale of 1-10.
* **Working Conditions** (`working_conditions`, text, optional): Describe your work environment, including your workspace, equipment, and overall conditions.
* **Manager Support** (`manager_support`, text, optional): Describe the level of support you receive from your manager, including guidance, resources, and feedback.
* **Team Support** (`team_support`, select_multiple, optional): Select the teams or departments that provide you the most support:
	+ Employee Support Team
	+ Human Resources
	+ Management
	+ Operations
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Communication** (`communication`, select_one, optional): Rate the quality of communication between you and your team/manager on a scale of Very Good to Very Bad:
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Very Bad
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Rate your job satisfaction on a scale of Very Good to Very Bad:
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Very Bad
* **Job Security** (`job_security`, select_one, optional): Rate your level of job security on a scale of Very Good to Very Bad:
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Very Bad
* **Company Values** (`company_values`, select_one, optional): Rate the importance of company values to you on a scale of Very Important to Not Very Important:
	+ Very Important
	+ Somewhat Important
	+ Somewhat Unimportant
	+ Not Very Important
