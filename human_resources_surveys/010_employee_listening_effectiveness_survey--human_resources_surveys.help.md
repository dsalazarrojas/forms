# Employee Listening Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback on the effectiveness of your manager's communication. Your responses will help us understand how well they are doing and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each page carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best match your experience and feelings.
3. Answer all questions, but don't worry if you're not sure about something.
4. Submit your survey when you have completed all the pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company** (`company_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the company.
* **Manager** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of your manager.
* **How would you rate the effectiveness of your manager's communication?** (`communication_effectiveness`, number, optional): Rate your experience from 1-10, with 1 being very dissatisfied and 10 being very satisfied.
* **How engaged do you feel in your job?** (`employee_engagement`, number, optional): Rate your experience from 1-10, with 1 being not satisfied and 10 being very satisfied.
* **Team satisfaction** (`team_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Select how satisfied you are with your team.
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Not satisfied
	+ Not very satisfied
* **Department satisfaction** (`department_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Select how satisfied you are with your department.
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Not satisfied
	+ Not very satisfied
* **Which of the following channels do you currently use for communication?** (`communication_channels`, select_multiple, optional): Select all channels you regularly use.
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ In-app messaging
	+ Video conference call
	+ In-person meeting
	+ Other
* **How often do you use these channels?** (`communication_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select how often you use each channel.
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Annually
	+ Never
* **How open is your manager in communication?** (`manager_openness`, select_one, optional): Select how open your manager is.
	+ Highly open
	+ Somewhat open
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat closed
	+ Highly closed
* **How often are employee suggestions taken into consideration?** (`employee_suggestions`, select_one, optional): Select how often employee suggestions are taken into consideration.
	+ Always
	+ Almost always
	+ Sometimes
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **Which of the following communication skills do you find most effective?** (`communication_skills`, select_multiple, optional): Select all skills you find most effective.
	+ Active listening
	+ Empathy
	+ Nonverbal communication
	+ Conflict resolution
	+ Other
* **What would you suggest to improve communication?** (`manager_suggestion`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions you have for improving communication.
* **Do you feel like you have a way to talk to your manager about your work and well-being?** (`open_door`, select_one, optional): Select if you can talk to your manager about your work and well-being.
	+ True
	+ False
* **What do you think would be a good feedback mechanism for communication?** (`feedback_mechanism`, select_multiple, optional): Select all mechanisms that would be good for communication.
	+ Email
	+ In-app messaging
	+ One-on-one meeting
	+ Team meeting
	+ Other
