# Employee Competency Verification Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Competency Verification Form Template is used to collect and verify an employee's competency and job details for HR evaluation and decision-making purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by completing the "Job Title" field, which is a text input field that requires you to enter your current job title.
2. In the "Job Summary" field, provide a brief summary of your job responsibilities and duties.
3. For the "Education" field, select all relevant options from the list that describe your educational background.
4. In the "Job Skills" field, enter any relevant skills you possess that are relevant to your job.
5. For the "Experience" field, select all relevant options from the list that describe your work experience.
6. In the "Employer Verification" field, select the option that best describes your relationship with your employer.
7. For the "Education Verification" field, select the option that best describes your educational verification status.
8. In the "Employee References" field, enter any relevant references that can vouch for your skills and experience.
9. For the "Employer" field, enter your current or previous employer's name.
10. Finally, in the "Employee Status" field, enter your current employee status (e.g., active, inactive, retired, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
• **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your current job title.
• **Job Summary** (`job_summary`, text, required): Provide a brief summary of your job responsibilities and duties.
• **Education** (`education`, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant options from the list that describe your educational background.
• **Job Skills** (`job_skills`, text, optional): Enter any relevant skills you possess that are relevant to your job.
• **Experience** (`experience`, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant options from the list that describe your work experience.
• **Employer Verification** (`employer_verification`, select_one, optional): Select the option that best describes your relationship with your employer.
• **Education Verification** (`education_verification`, select_one, optional): Select the option that best describes your educational verification status.
• **Employee References** (`employee_references`, text, optional): Enter any relevant references that can vouch for your skills and experience.
• **Employer** (`employer`, text, optional): Enter your current or previous employer's name.
• **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, text, optional): Enter your current employee status (e.g., active, inactive, retired, etc.).

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as "required" in the form schema.
