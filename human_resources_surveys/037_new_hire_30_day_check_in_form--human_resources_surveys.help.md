# New Hire 30 Day Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to gather feedback and information about new hires within the first 30 days of their employment. The purpose of this form is to monitor the progress, satisfaction, and performance of new employees during this critical period. This will help supervisors and HR teams to assess the fit of new employees and identify potential issues early on.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are filling out the form for a new employee who has been with the company for less than 30 days.
2. Provide accurate and honest feedback on the new employee's performance, progress, and feedback on their first day of work.
3. Select the correct feedback options for each field, as specified below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter the new employee's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter the new employee's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter the new employee's email address.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Please enter the new employee's job title.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Please enter the new employee's department.

### Page 2
* **Supervisor First Name** (`supervisor_first_name`, `text`, optional): If applicable, please enter the supervisor's first name.
* **Supervisor Last Name** (`supervisor_last_name`, `text`, optional): If applicable, please enter the supervisor's last name.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, `email`, required): If applicable, please enter the supervisor's email address.
* **Feedback Frequency** (`feedback_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required): Select how often you'd like to receive feedback:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly

### Page 3
* **Communication Style** (`communication_style`, `select_one`, required): Select the most suitable communication style for your interactions with the new employee:
	+ Direct
	+ Indirect
	+ Collaborative

### Page 4
* **Goals for Next 30 Days** (`goals_for_next_30_days`, `note`, optional): Enter any goals or expectations you have for the new employee for the next 30 days.
* **Questions or Comments** (`questions_or_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any questions or comments you may have about the new employee.

### Page 5
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the new employee's job and their responsibilities.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the company the new employee works for.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location where the new employee works.

### Page 6
* **Supervisor Notes** (`supervisor_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any notes or observations about the new employee.
* **New Employee's Name** (`new_employees_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the new employee.
* **New Employee's Email** (`new_employees_email`, `text`, optional): Enter the new employee's email address.
* **New Employee's Job Title** (`new_employees_job_title`, `text`, optional): Enter the new employee's job title.

### Page 7
* **First Day of Work** (`first_day_of_work`, `date`, required): Enter the date the new employee started work.
* **Feedback on First Day** (`feedback_on_first_day`, `select_one`, required): Select your feedback on the new employee's first day:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Bad
* **Feedback on First Two Weeks** (`feedback_on_first_two_weeks`, `text`, optional): Enter your feedback on the new employee's performance during their first two weeks.
* **Feedback on First Three Months** (`feedback_on_first_three_months`, `text`, optional): Enter your feedback on the new employee's performance during their first three months.
* **Feedback on First Six Months** (`feedback_on_first_six_months`, `text`, optional): Enter your feedback on the new employee's performance during their first six months.

### Page 8
* **New Employee's Status** (`new_employees_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the new employee's status:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
	+ On Leave

## Tips
* Please provide honest and constructive feedback to help new employees grow and improve.
* Ensure that you are filling out the form within the 30-day window to get accurate and timely feedback.
* Review and update your answers as needed to reflect any changes in the new employee's progress.
