# Employee Onboarding Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from new employees about their onboarding experience with the company. The survey consists of 12 questions that will help us understand your initial impressions, the effectiveness of the onboarding process, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best describe your experience.
3. If a question asks for a free-form text response, provide as much detail as you can.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First impression of company culture** (page 1)
	+ A brief description of your initial impression when you first joined the company.
* **Onboarding process** (page 1)
	+ How would you rate the effectiveness of our onboarding process? (Excellent, Good, Fair)
* **Gaps in the onboarding process** (page 1)
	+ Describe any gaps or shortcomings you experienced in the onboarding process.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (page 1)
	+ Provide specific suggestions on how we can improve our onboarding process.
* **Overall experience** (page 1)
	+ How would you rate your overall experience with our onboarding process? (Excellent, Good, Fair)
* **Efficiency and effectiveness** (page 1)
	+ Please provide a numerical value between 1 (low) and 10 (high) to rate the efficiency and effectiveness of our onboarding process.
* **Gaps in the onboarding process (2)** (page 2)
	+ Describe any further gaps or shortcomings you experienced in the onboarding process.
* **Suggestions for improvement (2)** (page 2)
	+ Provide specific suggestions on how we can improve our onboarding process.
* **Overall experience (2)** (page 2)
	+ How would you rate your overall experience with our onboarding process? (Excellent, Good, Fair)
* **Issues and challenges** (page 3)
	+ Describe any issues or challenges you encountered during the onboarding process.
* **Suggestions for improvement (3)** (page 3)
	+ Provide specific suggestions on how we can improve our onboarding process.
* **Quality of the onboarding process** (page 3)
	+ How would you rate the quality of our onboarding process? (Excellent, Good, Fair)
* **Issues and challenges** (page 3)
	+ Please describe any other issues or challenges you encountered during the onboarding process.
