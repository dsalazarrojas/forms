# Exit Interview Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exit Interview Checklist is a form designed to gather information from employees about their departure from the company. The goal is to collect feedback and comments about their overall experience, reasons for leaving, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and ensure you understand what each question is asking.
2. Answer all required questions (indicated by an asterisk *).
3. Provide as much detail as possible for each question.
4. If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to reach out to the HR department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Welcome to the Exit Interview Checklist** (`welcome`, `text`, not required): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please read through it carefully before starting.
* **exit_date** (`exit_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date you last worked for the company.
* **Manager's Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, not required): If applicable, enter your direct manager's name.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Select the department you were a part of while working at the company. Please choose one of the options provided.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, not required): Enter your job title while working at the company.
* **Reason for Departure** (`reason_for_departure`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable reasons why you are leaving the company. You can choose more than one option.
	+ Laid off
	+ Quit
	+ Retired
	+ Relocated
	+ New opportunity
* **Last Day of Work** (`last_day_of_work`, `date`, not required): Enter the date you last worked for the company.
* **next_steps** (`next_steps`, `text`, not required): If you have any next steps or plans for your future, please share them with us.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, not required): Share any comments or feedback you have about your experience at the company.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, `text`, not required): Share your overall experience while working at the company.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, not required): Provide any feedback you have about your experience and the company.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, `text`, not required): Share any recommendations you have for improving the company.
* **Next Steps 2** (`next_steps_2`, `text`, not required): If you have any next steps or plans for your future, please share them with us.
* **Company Information** (`company_info`, `text`, not required): Share any company information you'd like to provide (e.g., website, email, phone number, etc.).
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, not required): Share any contact information you'd like to provide (e.g., email, phone number, etc.).
* **Comments 2** (`comments_2`, `text`, not required): Share any additional comments or feedback you have.
* **Overall Experience 2** (`overall_experience_2`, `text`, not required): Share your overall experience while working at the company.
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, `text`, not required): Share your level of satisfaction with your job.
* **Manager's Satisfaction** (`manager_satisfaction`, `text`, not required): Share your level of satisfaction with your manager.
* **Communication** (`communication`, `text`, not required): Share your level of satisfaction with the company's communication.
* **Team Satisfaction** (`team_satisfaction`, `text`, not required): Share your level of satisfaction with your team.
* **Company Satisfaction** (`company_satisfaction`, `text`, not required): Share your level of satisfaction with the company.
* **Benefits** (`benefits`, `text`, not required): Share your level of satisfaction with the company benefits.
* **Training and Support** (`training_and_support`, `text`, not required): Share your level of satisfaction with the company's training and support.
* **Job Security** (`job_security`, `text`, not required): Share your level of satisfaction with your job security.
