# HR Process Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Process Experience Survey is designed to gather information about your experience with HR processes. This will help us improve the way we provide services and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Start by filling out the required fields: First Name, Last Name, and Department.
- Choose your answer to the Department field according to your team or department affiliation.
- For the Job Title field, enter your current job title.
- For each Feedback field, provide your thoughts and opinions about your experience with our HR processes.
- Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
- Click the Submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Department** (select multiple, optional): Select the department that you belong to.
* **Job Title** (text, optional): Enter your current job title.
* **Manager Name** (text, optional): If you have a manager, enter their name.
* **Feedback 1, 2, 3, 4, 5** (text, optional): Provide your thoughts and opinions about your experience with our HR processes.
* **Submit** (text, required): Click this button to send your feedback.
