# Work Life Balance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Life Balance Survey is designed to help individuals assess their balance between work and personal life. The survey asks a series of questions to gather information on various aspects of work-life balance, including scores and open-ended responses. The goal is to provide insights for improving work-life balance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Start" button to begin the survey.
2. Fill out each field according to the instructions below.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.
4. Click "Submit" to send your answers to the survey administrators.
