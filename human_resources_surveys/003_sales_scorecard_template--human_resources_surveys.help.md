We're creating a user-facing help guide for a form used to manage sales scorecards. A sales scorecard is a tool that helps businesses track and measure progress towards their goals. This form is called the "Sales Scorecard Template". The purpose of this form is to capture information about the sales scorecard's category, description, and other relevant details.

Before filling out this form, ensure that you're not duplicating existing data and that the scorecard is relevant to the category being tracked.

# Sales Scorecard Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Scorecard Template is a tool for tracking and measuring sales performance by capturing key details such as category, description, and other relevant metrics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the category for the sales scorecard from the options provided. This will help identify the specific area of focus.
2. Provide a brief description of the sales scorecard, including its purpose and how it will be used.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category (Category)** (`category_name`, Select One, Required): Select the category that this sales scorecard belongs to. This will help in organizing and tracking progress within that specific area.
* **Description (Description)** (`description`, Text, Required): Enter a brief description of the sales scorecard, including its purpose and how it will be used.
* **Is Achieving Business Goals (Is Achieving Business Goals)** (`is_achieving_business_goals`, Select Multiple, Optional): Check the box if this sales scorecard is currently achieving business goals. This helps in tracking progress towards goal accomplishment.
* **Is Measuring Progress (Is Measuring Progress)** (`is_measuring_progress`, Select Multiple, Optional): Check the box if this sales scorecard is currently measuring progress. This helps in monitoring and evaluating the effectiveness of the scorecard.
* **Is Customizable (Is Customizable)** (`is_customizable`, Select Multiple, Optional): Check the box if this sales scorecard is customizable to fit specific needs. This allows for adaptability and flexibility in tracking and measurement.
