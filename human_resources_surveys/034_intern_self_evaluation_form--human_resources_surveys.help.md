# Intern Self Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intern Self Evaluation Form is a tool used to assess your performance and progress during your internship. This form will guide you through a series of questions to help you reflect on your experiences, skills gained, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Upload your hours worked during the internship in the "Upload Hours" field.
2.  Answer the self-assessment questions (1-4) in the text fields provided.
3.  If applicable, upload any log sheets related to your work in the "Upload Log Sheet" field.
4.  Your supervisor will review and sign the form, providing their assessment and comments in the "Supervisor Comments" and "Supervisor Name" fields.
5.  Finally, upload any relevant spreadsheets or documents in the "Uploaded Spreadsheets" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Upload Hours** (Text): Enter the total number of hours you have worked during your internship. This is a required field.
*   **What did you learn during your internship?** (Text): Please describe what you learned during your internship, highlighting any new skills or knowledge gained. This is a required field.
*   **How do you think you improved during your internship?** (Text): Share how you think you improved your skills or performance during your internship. This is a required field.
*   **What did you enjoy doing during your internship?** (Text): Describe any activities, projects, or tasks you enjoyed performing during your internship. This is a required field.
*   **What could you improve on?** (Text): Identify areas where you would like to improve or develop further. This is a required field.
*   **Supervisor Signature** (Text): This field is for your supervisor to sign and date their assessment of your performance.
*   **Supervisor Comments** (Text): Your supervisor will provide their comments and feedback on your performance in this field.
*   **Supervisor Name** (Text): Enter your supervisor's name for verification purposes.
*   **Upload Log Sheet** (Text): If you have any log sheets or records related to your work, please upload them here.
*   **Uploaded Spreadsheets** (Text): Upload any relevant spreadsheets or documents related to your internship work.
