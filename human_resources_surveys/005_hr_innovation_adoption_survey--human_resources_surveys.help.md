# HR Innovation Adoption Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the adoption of various HR innovations by employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Ensure you are logged in to the system with your valid credentials.
*   Start with the first page, which asks for your basic information such as Employee ID, Name, and Manager ID.
*   Proceed to the subsequent pages, which ask about the HR innovations you have adopted in the last 6 months, their usage frequency, and benefits experienced.
*   You can skip questions that are not relevant to your job role or department.
*   Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as you can.
*   You will be able to submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee ID (Page 1)**: Enter your employee ID number as required or leave it blank if not applicable.
*   **Name (Page 1)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your company ID.
*   **Manager ID (Page 1)**: Enter your manager's ID number as required or leave it blank if not applicable.
*   **Role (Page 1)**: Select your job role within the company.
*   **Department (Page 1)**: Select your department within the company.
*   **Innovation 1 (Page 2)**: Select all the HR innovations you have adopted in the last 6 months.
*   **Frequency 1 (Page 2)**: Indicate how often you use the selected innovation(s).
*   **Innovation 2 (Page 3)**: Select all the additional HR innovations you have adopted in the last 6 months.
*   **Frequency 2 (Page 3)**: Indicate how often you use the selected innovation(s).
*   **Innovation 3 (Page 4)**: Select all the other HR innovations you have adopted in the last 6 months.
*   **Frequency 3 (Page 4)**: Indicate how often you use the selected innovation(s).
*   **Benefit (Page 5)**: Describe how the adoption of the innovation(s) has influenced your work or personal life.
*   **Benefit 1 (Page 5)**: Describe the improvement in efficiency you have experienced.
*   **Benefit 2 (Page 5)**: Describe the enhancement in productivity you have experienced.
*   **Benefit 3 (Page 5)**: Describe the increase in accuracy you have experienced.
*   **Challenge (Page 6)**: Describe any challenges encountered during the adoption and implementation phase.
*   **Challenge 1 (Page 6)**: Describe the technical issues with data security and privacy.
*   **Challenge 2 (Page 6)**: Describe the lack of user engagement and motivation.
*   **Challenge 3 (Page 6)**: Describe the limited budget and resources.
*   **Challenge 4 (Page 6)**: Describe any other challenges you have faced.

Note to self: Please ensure to keep the explanations concise, practical, and concrete to the form's purpose and fields.
