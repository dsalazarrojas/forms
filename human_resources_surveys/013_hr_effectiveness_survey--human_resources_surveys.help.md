# HR Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to assess individual employee performance and provide feedback for improvement. It includes sections for self-assessment, peer assessment, and manager feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant fields for your specific situation.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Provide clear and honest feedback for each assessment.
4. Ensure that you are completing the form within the specified performance period.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company Name** (1): Enter the name of your department or organization.
- **Job Title** (2): Enter your job title or position.
- **Job Function** (3): Enter a brief description of your job function or responsibilities.
- **Reporting Manager** (4): Enter the name of your direct manager or supervisor.
- **Direct Manager** (5): Enter the name of your direct manager or supervisor.
- **Department** (6): Enter the name of your department.
- **Job Level** (7): Enter your job level or level within the company.
- **Job Family** (8): Enter the job family or category you belong to.
- **Supervisor** (9): Enter the name of your immediate supervisor.
- **Team** (10): Enter the name of your team or work group.
- **Work Unit** (11): Enter the name of your work unit or team.
- **Performance Period** (12): Enter the specific performance period being assessed.
- **Manager Score** (13): Enter a numerical score between 1-5 to assess your performance.
- **Self Score** (14): Enter a numerical score between 1-5 to assess your performance.
- **Peer Score** (15): Enter a numerical score between 1-5 to assess your performance.
- **Team Score** (16): Enter a numerical score between 1-5 to assess your team's performance.
- **Overall Score** (17): Enter a numerical score between 1-5 to assess overall performance.
- **Performance Improvement Action Items** (18): Enter specific actions or items for improvement.
- **Comments** (19): Enter any comments or feedback for performance improvement.
- **Manager Comments** (20): Enter comments or feedback from your manager.
- **Action Items** (21): Enter specific actions or items for improvement.
- **Action Items - Manager** (22): Enter specific actions or items from your manager for improvement.
- **Follow-up Comments** (23): Enter any follow-up comments or feedback.
- **Follow-up Items** (24): Enter specific follow-up items or actions for improvement.
- **Overall Recommendations** (25): Enter any overall recommendations for improvement.
