# Remote Work Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remote Work Survey is designed to collect information about remote work arrangements and the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on the workforce.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category that best describes the type of survey you are taking.
2. Provide a brief description of your remote work arrangement (if applicable).
3. Indicate if you have worked with a coronavirus-related project or if you have been working from a remote workplace.
4. Choose your response to the question about syncing responses with 100+ apps.
5. Select the tool assigned to you for remote work (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select the category that best describes the type of survey you are taking. This will help us to better understand the context of your responses.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): This field is for providing a brief description of your remote work arrangement, if applicable. Keep your response concise and focused on the key details.
* **Coronavirus Workforce** (`coronavirus_workforce`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you have worked with a coronavirus-related project or if you have been working from a remote workplace. This will help us to understand the impact of the pandemic on your work.
* **Sync Responses** (`sync_responses`, select_one, optional): Choose your response to the question about syncing responses with 100+ apps. This will help us to understand how you manage your tasks and projects.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool assigned to you for remote work (if applicable). This will help us to understand the tools and technologies used for remote work.

## Tips

* Please be as accurate as possible when answering the questions.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, feel free to ask for clarification.
* This survey is intended to be completed once, but if your circumstances change, you can update your responses at any time.
