# Employee Benefits Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Benefits Preference Survey is designed to gather information on your opinions and preferences regarding various benefits offered by your employer. Your input will help your employer understand what benefits matter most to you and your colleagues, allowing them to tailor their benefits package to better meet the needs of their workforce.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2.  Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
3.  Provide detailed answers for text-based questions.
4.  Make sure to submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What benefits matter most to you?** (`health`, `financial`, `family`): This question is a multiple-choice question where you can select all the benefits that matter most to you. Please choose all the options that apply.
* **What's most important for work-life balance?** (`remote work options`, `flexible work schedule`, `family days off`): This question is a single-choice question where you can select one option that you think is most important for your work-life balance.
* **How important is health insurance to you?** (`number`: This question is a numerical question where you can rate the importance of health insurance to you. Please rate it from 1-5, where 1 is least important and 5 is most important.
* **What benefit options do you currently have at work?** (`text`: This question is a text-based question where you can describe the benefits you currently have at work.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`note`: This question is a text-based question where you can provide any additional comments or questions you have.
* **What's the most valuable benefit you've ever received from your current or past employer?** (`health insurance`, `financial support`, `family leave`): This question is a single-choice question where you can select the most valuable benefit you've ever received.
* **How do you want to be contacted for future surveys?** (`email`: This question is an email address question where you can enter your preferred email address.
* **What are your thoughts on having a mentor at work?** (`yes`, `no`, `maybe`): This question is a multiple-choice question where you can select one or more options that apply.
* **Do you think having a mentor would be beneficial for you?** (`text`: This question is a text-based question where you can describe your thoughts on having a mentor.
* **Do you have any other feedback or suggestions?** (`text`: This question is a text-based question where you can provide any other feedback or suggestions.
* **Have you had any issues with benefits at your workplace so far?** (`text`: This question is a text-based question where you can describe any issues you've had with benefits.
