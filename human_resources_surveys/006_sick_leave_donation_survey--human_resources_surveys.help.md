# Sick Leave Donation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your thoughts and opinions on the sick leave policy in your workplace. Your responses will help management understand your views on the current state of the policy and provide valuable feedback that can shape its future development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

* Please answer each question carefully, taking into account your genuine thoughts and opinions.
* For multiple-choice questions, select all the options that apply to you.
* For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you feel is necessary to express your thoughts.
* If you have any concerns or comments, please include them in the designated fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Support** (`overall_support`, select_one, required): Please indicate whether you support the current sick leave policy or not. Choose "Yes" if you generally support the policy, "No" if you do not. If you are neutral or unsure, select the option that best represents your views.
* **Sentiment** (`sentiment`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the options that reflect your current views on the sick leave policy. You can select multiple options, depending on your opinions.
* **Concerns** (`concerns`, select_multiple, required): This question is designed to help us understand potential issues with the policy. Choose all the options that represent your concerns or difficulties you face with the policy.
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions or ideas to improve the sick leave policy, please provide them in this field. Your input will be valuable in helping us shape the future of the policy.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): If you have any other thoughts, concerns, or comments that don't fit into the previous fields, please share them here.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly, and your responses will be kept confidential.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the policy, feel free to contact your HR representative.
* Thank you for taking the time to participate in this survey and provide valuable feedback.
