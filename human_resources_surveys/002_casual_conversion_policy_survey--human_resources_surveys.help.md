# Casual Conversion Policy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey is designed to gauge employees' experiences with the casual conversion policy, gathering information on their overall satisfaction, perceived support, and potential areas for improvement. It is an anonymous and confidential assessment to help us better understand how to refine our conversion process for future employees.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete the survey in its entirety to provide as accurate and comprehensive feedback as possible.
2. Select the most applicable response for each question based on your genuine experience.
3. Provide specific examples or details to support your answers when possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, Text, Required): This field is for your full name as it appears on your personnel file.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, Number, Required): This field is for your employee identification number.
* **Department** (`department`, Select One, Required): Select the department you belong to (e.g., Human Resources, Sales, Marketing, Operations, IT, Finance, or Other).
* **Casual Employment Start Date** (`employment_start_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date you started as a casual employee.
* **Current Conversion Status** (`conversion_status`, Select One, Required): Select your current status regarding casual to permanent conversion (e.g., Not Yet Eligible, Eligible - Not Applied, Applied - Pending, Converted to Permanent, Converted to Fixed-term, Not Interested).
* **Aware of Policy** (`awareness_of_policy`, Select One, Required): Indicate if you were aware of the conversion policy (e.g., Yes - Fully Aware, Somewhat Aware, No - First Time Hearing).
* **Policy Clarity Rating** (`policy_clarity`, Select One, Required): Rate how clear the conversion policy explanation was (e.g., Very Clear, Clear, Neutral, Unclear, Very Unclear).
* **HR Support Rating** (`hr_support_rating`, Select One, Not Required): Rate the support received from HR during the process (e.g., Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Very Poor). 
*   If not applicable, select "Not Applicable".
* **Process Timeline Satisfaction** (`process_timeline`, Select One, Not Required): Rate if the conversion process was timely (e.g., Faster Than Expected, As Expected, Slower Than Expected, Much Slower, Not Applicable). 
*   If not applicable, select "Not Applicable".
* **Documentation Provided** (`documentation_provided`, Select One, Required): Indicate if you received all necessary documents (e.g., Yes - All Documents, Some Documents, No Documents).
* **Challenges Faced** (`challenges_faced`, Select Multiple, Not Required): Select any challenges you faced during the conversion process (e.g., Lack of Information, Delayed Response, Confusing Process, Manager Support Issues, Documentation Problems, None).
*   Select all applicable options.
* **Overall Experience Rating** (`overall_experience`, Select One, Required): Rate your overall experience with the conversion process (e.g., Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Would Recommend** (`would_recommend`, Select One, Required): Indicate if you would recommend this process to others (e.g., Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Neutral, Probably No, Definitely No).
* **Improvements Suggested** (`improvements_suggested`, Text, Not Required): Suggest any improvements to the conversion process.
*   Provide specific and concrete suggestions.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_feedback`, Text, Not Required): Share any other feedback you would like to convey.

Please note that all responses are anonymous and will be treated confidentially. Your input will help us refine the conversion process for future employees.
