# Employment Advocacy Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to understand employee engagement with employment advocacy initiatives, helping organizations to assess and improve their current state of employment advocacy.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant option for each question. 
2. Provide honest and accurate information in the fields provided.
3. Ensure you fill out the entire form without leaving any sections blank.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employment Advocacy Level (Awareness, Participation, Communication, Others)**:
	+ This question assesses your level of engagement with employment advocacy initiatives. Choose the option that best represents your current state.
* **Questions Section**:
	+ This section is a space for you to provide any additional information or comments about the employment advocacy initiatives within your organization.
* **Formal Presentations, Social Media, Email, Intranet, Others**:
	+ Select all the options that you have experienced or have been part of during your employment with the organization, regarding formal presentations, social media, email, intranet or other channels.

## Tips
- Answer honestly and thoroughly, as this will help the organization understand the current state of employment advocacy.
- If you are unsure about any question, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
- Please note that this form is intended to be completed by employees only, and not by external stakeholders or third-party users.
