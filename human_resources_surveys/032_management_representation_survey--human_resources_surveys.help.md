# Management Representation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information from employees about their experience with their manager and the department or team they are part of.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, simply answer each question with your honest feedback about your experience with your manager and department.
2. Please provide your response in a concise and clear way.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Manager Feedback**:
	+ **Department**: This field is to help us understand which department you are part of.
	+ **Management Effectiveness**: This field is for your overall assessment of how effective you feel your manager is in leading your department.
* **Management Effectiveness**: This field is to help us understand the extent to which you feel your manager is effective or ineffective.
* **Effective**: This field is for selecting the level of effectiveness you perceive in your manager.
* **Not Effective**: This field is for selecting the level of ineffectiveness you perceive in your manager.
* **Neutral**: This field is for selecting the level of neutrality you perceive in your manager.
* **Manager's Leadership**:
	+ **Inspiring**: This field is for your assessment of how inspiring your manager is.
* **Supportive**:
	+ **Supportive**: This field is for your assessment of how supportive your manager is.
* **Culture**:
	+ **Open**: This field is for selecting the level of open culture you perceive in your department or team.
	+ **Closed**: This field is for selecting the level of closed culture you perceive in your department or team.
	+ **Neutral**: This field is for selecting the level of neutrality you perceive in your department or team.
* **Communication**:
	+ **Excellent**: This field is for your assessment of how excellent you think communication is within your team and organization.
	+ **Good**: This field is for your assessment of how good you think communication is within your team and organization.
	+ **Poor**: This field is for your assessment of how poor you think communication is within your team and organization.
* **Teamwork**:
	+ **Collaborative**: This field is for your assessment of how collaborative you think your team is.
	+ **Good**: This field is for your assessment of how good you think your team's collaboration is.
	+ **Needs Improvement**: This field is for your assessment of how much improvement your team needs in collaboration.
* **Support**:
	+ **Available**: This field is for your assessment of how available your manager is to support you.
	+ **Not Available**: This field is for your assessment of how not available your manager is to support you.

## Tips

* Please answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask your manager or HR representative for guidance.
* This survey is completely anonymous, so your feedback will not be shared with your manager or anyone in your department.
