# Coronavirus Self Declaration Form For Church Staff - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from church staff members about their health status in relation to COVID-19. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and truthfully to ensure the health and safety of yourself and others.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately.** Please answer each question truthfully to the best of your ability.
2. **Provide clear and detailed answers.** If you are unsure about any question, please provide as much information as possible.
3. **Submit the form.** Once you have filled out the form, click the submit button to send it to your supervisor or HR representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Please enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Please enter your last name.
* **Email**: Please enter your work email address.
* **Phone**: Please enter your work phone number.
* **Address**: Please enter your work address.
* **Date visited**: Please enter the date you visited the church (if any).
* **Symptoms or contact with someone with COVID-19?**:
	+ Select 'Yes' if you have experienced any COVID-19 symptoms or have been in contact with someone who has COVID-19.
	+ Select 'No' if you have not experienced any COVID-19 symptoms or have not been in contact with someone who has COVID-19.
* **Have you had a fever?**: Please enter 'Yes' if you have experienced a fever, or 'No' if you have not.
* **Have you had a cough?**: Please enter 'Yes' if you have experienced a cough, or 'No' if you have not.
* **Have you had body aches?**: Please enter 'Yes' if you have experienced body aches, or 'No' if you have not.
* **Have you had a headache?**: Please enter 'Yes' if you have experienced a headache, or 'No' if you have not.
* **Have you had a runny nose?**: Please enter 'Yes' if you have experienced a runny nose, or 'No' if you have not.
* **Have you had a sore throat?**: Please enter 'Yes' if you have experienced a sore throat, or 'No' if you have not.
* **Travel history**: Please enter any recent travel history that may be relevant to your health status.
* **Contact history**: Please enter any recent contact history that may be relevant to your health status.
* **Hospitalization history**: Please enter any recent hospitalization history that may be relevant to your health status.
* **Vaccination status**: Please enter your current vaccination status.
* **Have you tested positive for COVID-19?**:
	+ Select 'True' if you have tested positive for COVID-19.
	+ Select 'False' if you have not tested positive for COVID-19.
* **Notes**: Please enter any additional information that may be relevant to your health status.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* If you are unsure about any question, please seek guidance from your supervisor or HR representative.
* Keep the form up to date with any changes to your health status.
