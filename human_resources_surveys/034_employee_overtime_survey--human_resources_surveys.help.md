# Employee Overtime Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to report overtime work performed by employees, which will help management track overtime pay and related information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields carefully.
2. Answer questions accurately and truthfully.
3. Leave optional fields blank if the information is not applicable or not necessary to report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name** (id: 1, text): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
- **Last Name** (id: 2, text): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
- **Email** (id: 3, email): Enter your work email address.
- **Department** (id: 4, select_one): Select the department you belong to (e.g., Accounting, Human Resources, IT).
- **Suggestion** (id: 5, note): A field to provide any suggestions or comments about overtime pay.
- **Feedback** (id: 6, note): A field to provide any feedback about the overtime pay process.
- **Rendered Overtime Hours** (id: 7, number): Enter the total hours worked overtime.
- **Rendered Overtime Days** (id: 8, select_one): Select the days worked overtime (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday, Other).
- **Rendered Overtime Start Time** (id: 9, time): Enter the start time of the overtime period.
- **Rendered Overtime End Time** (id: 10, time): Enter the end time of the overtime period.
- **Overtime Pay** (id: 11, number): Enter the total amount of overtime pay earned.
- **Rendered Overtime Pay Period** (id: 12, select_one): Select the time period for which overtime pay was earned (e.g., Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly).
- **Rendered Overtime Pay Rate** (id: 13, select_one): Select the rate at which overtime pay was earned (e.g., Hourly, Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
- **Rendered Overtime Pay Amount** (id: 14, select_multiple): Select the range of overtime pay earned (e.g., $50-$100, $100-$200, More than $200).
- **Rendered Overtime Frequency** (id: 15, select_one): Select the frequency of overtime worked (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly, Other).
- **Rendered Overtime Comment** (id: 16, note): A field to provide any comments about the overtime work.
- **Manager Name** (id: 17, text): Enter the name of your manager.
- **Manager Email** (id: 18, email): Enter your manager's email address.
- **Manager Phone** (id: 19, text): Enter your manager's phone number.
- **Manager Department** (id: 20, select_one): Select your manager's department (e.g., Accounting, Human Resources, IT).
- **Manager Title** (id: 21, text): Enter your manager's title.
- **Manager Overtime Pay** (id: 22, number): Enter the total amount of overtime pay earned by your manager.
- **Manager Overtime Frequency** (id: 23, select_one): Select the frequency of overtime worked by your manager (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly, Other).
- **Manager Overtime Pay Rate** (id: 24, select_one): Select the rate at which your manager's overtime pay was earned (e.g., Hourly, Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
- **Manager Overtime Comment** (id: 25, note): A field to provide any comments about your manager's overtime work.

**Tips**:

* Please fill out all required fields accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your manager or HR representative for guidance.
* Please make sure to save your survey before submitting.
