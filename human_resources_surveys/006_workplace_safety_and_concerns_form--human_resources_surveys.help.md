# Workplace Safety And Concerns Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Safety And Concerns Form is designed to help employees report potential safety and concerns issues that require attention from management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your department from the list.
2. Choose the type of concern you are reporting (e.g. Safety and Security, Work-Life Balance, etc.).
3. Describe the concern or issue you are reporting.
4. Indicate the impact of the concern on your work.
5. Select the departments involved or affected by the concern.
6. Enter your employee ID and email address.
7. Enter your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Department** (`Department`, select_one, required): Select the department you belong to.
- **Concern Type** (`Concern Type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of concern you are reporting (e.g. Safety and Security, Work-Life Balance, etc.).
- **Concern Description** (`Concern Description`, text, required): Describe the concern or issue you are reporting in as much detail as possible.
- **Impact On Work** (`Impact On Work`, select_one, required): Indicate the effect your concern has on your work (Significant, Somewhat Significant, Not Significant).
- **Concerned Department** (`Concerned Department`, select_multiple, required): Select the departments involved or affected by the concern.
- **Concerned Location** (`Concerned Location`, select_one, optional): If the concern is related to a specific location, choose from the list.
- **Employee ID** (`Employee ID`, number, required): Enter your employee ID number.
- **Employee Email** (`Employee Email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Employee Name** (`Employee Name`, text, required): Enter your name.
