# Worker Conduct Policy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess employees' understanding and perception of the Worker Conduct Policy. It aims to gather feedback on the policy's awareness, training, and clarity, which will help the company to identify areas for improvement and provide better support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Rate your level of awareness on the Worker Conduct Policy (question 2).
2. Evaluate the effectiveness of the training sessions (question 3).
3. Provide any comments or feedback on the policy (question 4).
4. Rate the clarity of the policy (question 6).
5. Evaluate the support provided by the policy (question 7).
6. Rate the policy's clarity (question 11).
7. Rate the enforcement of the policy (question 13).
8. Evaluate the overall familiarity with the policy (question 14).
9. Rate the adherence to the policy (question 15).
