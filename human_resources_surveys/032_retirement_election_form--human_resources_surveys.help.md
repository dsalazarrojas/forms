# Retirement Election Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Retirement Election Form" is a document used to collect information from employees who are choosing to retire. This form helps the HR department understand the employee's retirement date, name, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have all necessary information before filling out this form.
2. Enter your Name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
6. Enter your Date of Retirement in the "Date of Retirement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (Name, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Please enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your work phone number, or the number of the HR department you can be reached at.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Date of Retirement** (Date of Retirement, date, optional): Enter your planned or actual retirement date.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Avoid using outdated or incorrect information.
* If you need help or have questions, contact your HR department for assistance.
* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately, as this information is used for official purposes.
