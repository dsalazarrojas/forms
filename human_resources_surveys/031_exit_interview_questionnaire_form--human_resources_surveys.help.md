# Exit Interview Questionnaire Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from employees who are leaving the company. It helps to identify reasons for departure, areas of improvement, and overall experience in the workplace. Please complete this form to provide detailed feedback on your time with the company.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each section of the form, starting with the first page (page 1).
2. Answer each question in the space provided. If a question does not apply to you, mark "N/A" or provide a brief explanation in the note field.
3. Review your answers before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
