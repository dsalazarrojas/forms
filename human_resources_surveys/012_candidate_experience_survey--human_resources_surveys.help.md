# Candidate Experience Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from candidates on their experience during the application process for our company. The feedback will help us identify areas of improvement and ensure that our processes are fair, efficient, and candidate-friendly. Please take a few minutes to answer these questions to help us better understand your experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Each question is designed to help us understand your experience, so take your time and answer each question carefully.
3. You may skip questions that you do not feel comfortable answering.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
5. Once you have completed the form, click the "Submit" button to send us your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (text, optional): This is your name as you want to be known in the survey. It's optional, and you can choose to remain anonymous if you prefer.
* **Email Address** (email, optional): If you'd like us to follow up with you regarding your feedback, please provide your email address.
* **Position You Applied For** (text, required): This is the job title you applied for.
* **When Did You Apply** (date, required): Please approximate the date when you submitted your application.
* **Current Application Status** (select_one, required): Choose the status that best describes where you are in the application process.
* **Overall Experience Rating** (select_one, required): Rate your overall experience with our company.
* **Job Description Clarity** (select_one, required): Rate how clear the job description was.
* **Ease of Application Process** (select_one, required): Rate how easy it was to apply.
* **How Long Did Application Take** (select_one, required): Estimate the time it took you to complete the application.
* **Communication from Company** (select_one, required): Rate the quality of communication you received from us.
* **Timeliness of Communication** (select_one, required): Rate how promptly you received responses from us.
* **Interviewer Professionalism** (select_one, optional): Rate the behavior of the interviewer(s).
* **Interview Process Clarity** (select_one, optional): Rate how clear the interview process was.
* **Interview Difficulty Level** (select_one, optional): Rate how challenging the interview was.
* **Interview Question Relevance** (select_one, optional): Rate how job-related the questions were.
* **Was Interview Length Appropriate** (select_one, optional): Rate if the interview length was suitable for you.
* **Office or Facility Rating** (select_one, optional): Rate the condition of our office or facility (if you visited in person).
* **Technology or Platform Experience** (select_one, optional): Rate the quality of our video interview platform (if used).
* **Did You Feel Respected** (select_one, required): Rate how respected you felt during the process.
* **Did You Observe Diversity and Inclusion** (select_one, optional): Rate if you observed a focus on diversity and inclusion.
* **Would You Recommend Applying Here** (select_one, required): Rate if you would recommend applying to our company.
* **What Went Well** (text, optional): Share any positive aspects of your experience.
* **What Needs Improvement** (text, optional): Share areas for improvement.
* **Additional Comments** (text, optional): Add any other feedback or comments you have.
* **Survey Completion Date** (date, required): Please enter the date when you complete this survey.

Note: Please remember that your feedback is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your time and honesty.
