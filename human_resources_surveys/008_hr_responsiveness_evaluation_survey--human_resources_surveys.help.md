# HR Responsiveness Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Responsiveness Evaluation Survey is designed to measure the performance of the HR department in responding to employees' questions and concerns. This survey aims to gauge the speed, professionalism, and effectiveness of the HR department, as well as gather any additional feedback or comments from employees. The data collected from this survey will help the HR department identify areas of improvement and make necessary adjustments to provide better service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through the survey questions carefully.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best represents your opinion.
4. If you have any comments or additional feedback, type them in the corresponding text fields.
5. Once you have completed all the questions, click the "Submit" button to submit your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the survey where you can add any information about your participation.
* **Employee ID** (`Employee ID`, `number`, required: false): This field is not used for any calculations, but rather as a placeholder for your employee ID. If you have one, please enter it.
* **How quickly does the HR department respond to your questions?** (`How quickly does the HR department respond to your questions?`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ **Always**: The HR department responds immediately to your questions.
	+ **Most of the time**: The HR department responds promptly to your questions.
	+ **Sometimes**: The HR department responds occasionally to your questions.
	+ **Rarely**: The HR department rarely responds to your questions.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, required: false): Please add any comments or feedback regarding the HR department's response time.
* **How would you rate the professionalism of the HR department?** (`How would you rate the professionalism of the HR department?`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ **Very Good**: The HR department is highly professional in their interactions with employees.
	+ **Good**: The HR department is generally professional in their interactions with employees.
	+ **Fair**: The HR department is somewhat professional in their interactions with employees.
	+ **Very Poor**: The HR department is not professional in their interactions with employees.
* **Question1 Comment** (`Question1 Comment`, `text`, required: false): Please add any comments about the HR department's professionalism.
* **How would you rate the effectiveness of the HR department?** (`How would you rate the effectiveness of the HR department?`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ **Very Good**: The HR department is highly effective in their work.
	+ **Good**: The HR department is generally effective in their work.
	+ **Fair**: The HR department is somewhat effective in their work.
	+ **Very Poor**: The HR department is not effective in their work.
* **Question3 Comment** (`Question3 Comment`, `text`, required: false): Please add any comments about the HR department's effectiveness.
* **How do you rate the speed of response of HR department?** (`How do you rate the speed of response of HR department?`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ **Very Good**: The HR department responds very quickly to questions.
	+ **Good**: The HR department responds promptly to questions.
	+ **Fair**: The HR department responds slowly to questions.
	+ **Very Poor**: The HR department rarely responds to questions.
* **Question4 Comment** (`Question4 Comment`, `text`, required: false): Please add any comments about the HR department's response speed.
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, `text`, required: false): Is there any other feedback you would like to provide about the HR department?
* **Submit** (`Submit`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select "HR" to submit feedback to the HR department. Select "Manager" to submit feedback to the HR manager. Select "Other" to submit feedback to a different person.

Tips: Please be as honest and detailed as possible in your answers, and do not hesitate to provide any comments or feedback. This will help the HR department understand your needs and make necessary adjustments to improve their services.
