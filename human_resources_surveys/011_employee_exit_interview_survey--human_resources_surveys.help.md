# Employee Exit Interview Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Exit Interview Survey is designed to collect information about your reasons for leaving the company. This information will help us understand what we can do better to support our employees and improve our work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions on each page as honestly and thoroughly as possible.
2. Use the provided text fields for detailed explanations.
3. Select one or multiple options for multiple-choice questions.
4. If you're unsure about any question, feel free to skip it or provide a comment in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <h2>1. Reasons for Leaving</h2>
* **Reasons for Leaving**: <i>Reasons for Leaving</i> (Text, Optional)
	+ Provide a brief explanation for your reasons for leaving the company.

### <h2>2. How long at Company</h2>
* **How long at Company**: <i>How long at Company</i> (Number, Optional)
	+ Enter the length of time you've been with the company.

### <h2>3. Reasons for Leaving</h2>
* **Reasons for Leaving**: <i>Reasons for Leaving</i> (Select Multiple, Optional)
	+ Select all that apply from the provided options:
		- 'Yes'
		- 'No'

### <h2>4. New Job Title</h2>
* **New Job Title**: <i>New Job Title</i> (Text, Optional)
	+ If applicable, enter your new job title.

### <h2>5. New Company</h2>
* **New Company**: <i>New Company</i> (Text, Optional)
	+ If applicable, enter the name of your new company.

### <h2>6. Next Steps</h2>
* **Next Steps**: <i>Next Steps</i> (Select One, Optional)
	+ Select one of the provided options:
		- 'Yes'
		- 'No'

### <h2>7. Reasons for Leaving (Details)</h2>
* **Reasons for Leaving (Details)**: <i>Reasons for Leaving (Details)</i> (Text, Optional)
	+ Provide more details about your reasons for leaving the company.

### <h2>8. Recommendation Score</h2>
* **Recommendation Score**: <i>Recommendation Score</i> (Text, Optional)
	+ If you have a recommendation for the company, enter it here.

### <h2>9. Comments</h2>
* **Comments**: <i>Comments</i> (Text, Optional)
	+ Leave any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share with us.
