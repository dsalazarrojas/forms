# HR Communication Tone Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the internal communication tone within the HR department, aiming to improve employee satisfaction and perception of the communication tone.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the following questions based on your experience and perception of the HR department's internal communication.
2. Be as accurate as possible when providing your input.
3. If you have any suggestions or improvements, please provide them in the relevant fields.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Satisfaction (Very High, High, Medium, Low, Very Low)**: Please select the level of your satisfaction with the current internal communication tone.
* **Tone Perceived (text field)**: Describe the tone you perceive when interacting with the HR department, providing any relevant details or examples.
* **Suggested Areas (select one)**: Choose the area that you think requires improvement in the internal communication tone, such as Communication style and clarity, Tone and language, or Frequency of communication.
* **Feedback (text field)**: Share any specific feedback or suggestions you have for improving the internal communication tone.
* **Email (email input)**: If you have any specific feedback or concerns regarding emails, please provide your email address for follow-up.
* **Phone (text field)**: If you have any specific feedback or concerns regarding phone calls, please provide your response in this field.
* **Note (note input)**: You can attach any additional notes or examples that may support your answers.
* **Category (select one)**: If you have feedback for the HR, Leadership, or Department, please select the relevant category.
* **Date (date input)**: The date you are currently working on this survey.
* **Time (time input)**: The time you are currently working on this survey.
* **Other (text field)**: If you have any other comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
* **Other 2 (text field)**: If you have any other comments or suggestions, please provide them here.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and accurately.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please reach out to the HR department.
* You can skip any fields that you don't have information on.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before submitting the form.
