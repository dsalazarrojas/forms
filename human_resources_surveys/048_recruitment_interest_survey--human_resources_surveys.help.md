# recruitment_interest_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your interests and qualifications for potential job opportunities within our organization. It will guide you through a series of questions to provide us with the most relevant information about your availability and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
2.  Enter your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
3.  Enter your Email address for contact purposes.
4.  Select up to three interests from the provided options.
5.  Enter your current Job Title and Department.
6.  Choose a Job Category from the dropdown options.
7.  Provide a brief description of the job you are interested in.
8.  Select your available work hours and work days.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your active and valid email address for contact purposes.
*   **Interest 1** (`interest_1`, `text`, optional): Choose one of the provided interests that you are interested in.
*   **Interest 2** (`interest_2`, `text`, optional): Choose another interest you are interested in.
*   **Interest 3** (`interest_3`, `text`, optional): Choose a third interest that you are interested in.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter the job title you are interested in.
*   **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Enter the department you are currently a part of or the one you are interested in.
*   **Job Category** (`job_category`, `select_one`, required): Choose a Job Category from the provided options.
*   **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of the job you are interested in.
*   **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, `number`, required): Enter the number of work hours you are available to work per week.
*   **Work Days** (`work_days`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the specific days you are available to work.

## Tips
Make sure to fill out the form carefully, as any mistakes or omissions may lead to delays in the recruitment process. If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please don't hesitate to contact us.
