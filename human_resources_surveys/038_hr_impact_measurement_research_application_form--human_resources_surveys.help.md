# HR Impact Measurement Research Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about research proposals, projects, and their impact on the organization. It will help HR departments measure the effectiveness of research initiatives and identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are providing accurate and relevant information.
2. Fill out the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*). While most fields are optional, it's crucial to provide as much detail as possible to ensure a comprehensive understanding of your research proposal.
3. If you are unsure about any field or have any questions, please reach out to your HR department for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Proposal** (`research_proposal`, text, optional):
	+ A brief description of your research proposal.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all documents that support your research proposal.
* **Impact Details** (`impact_details`, select_one, optional):
	+ Indicate if your research proposal will have a positive or negative impact on the organization.
* **Research Proposal Description** (`research_proposal_description`, text, optional):
	+ A detailed description of your research proposal.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the budget associated with your research proposal.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the start date of your research proposal.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the end date of your research proposal.
* **Funder** (`funder`, select_one, optional):
	+ Indicate if your research proposal is funded.
* **Researcher** (`researcher`, select_one, optional):
	+ Indicate if a dedicated researcher is working on your research proposal.
* **Research Area** (`research_area`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the area of research associated with your proposal.
* **Impact Area** (`impact_area`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the area where your research proposal will have its impact.
* **Project Lead** (`project_lead`, select_one, optional):
	+ Indicate if a project lead is assigned to your research proposal.
* **Research Team** (`research_team`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all researchers associated with your research proposal.
* **Supporting Organization** (`supporting_organization`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all organizations supporting your research proposal.
* **Research Funding** (`research_funding`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all funding sources associated with your research proposal.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the status of your research proposal (Active/Inactive).
* **Research Type** (`research_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the type of research associated with your proposal.
* **Research Location** (`research_location`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select the location where your research will take place.

## Tips

* Please provide as much detail as possible to ensure a comprehensive understanding of your research proposal.
* If unsure, don't hesitate to reach out to your HR department for guidance.
* This form will help HR departments measure the impact and effectiveness of research initiatives within your organization.
