# Retail Employee Amenities Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from retail employees on the amenities they value most in their workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form with your name and department in the required fields at the top.
* Select up to three amenities you like best from the list of options.
* Choose your top priority amenity from the same list.
* Write any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* Rate your overall satisfaction with a number rating.
* Write any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Store Name** (store\_name, text, required): Please enter the name of your store.
* **Favorite Amenities** (amenities\_liked, select\_multiple, required): Select up to three amenities you value most. These are amenities that you feel are most valuable to you in your workplace.
* **Priority Amenity** (priority\_amenity, select\_one, required): Choose the one amenity that is most important to you. This will help us understand what matters most to you.
* **Comments** (comments, text, required): Write any additional comments or feedback you have about the amenities.
* **Additional Comments** (additional\_comments, text, required): Write any other comments or feedback you may have about the amenities.
* **Feedback** (employee\_feedback, text, required): Share any other feedback or suggestions you have about the workplace.
* **Rating** (rating, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with a number rating (1-5).
* **Name** (employee\_name, text, required): Please enter your name.
* **Department** (department, text, required): Please enter your department.
