# job-applicant-reference-report-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a reference report for job applicants, gathering information about a candidate's job history, qualifications, and contact details. It's used in the hiring process to assess a candidate's fit for a role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Ensure that you have all necessary permission and clearance to access and share the information on this form.
3. Double-check that you are not duplicating effort with similar forms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Applicant Reference Form** (`job_applicant_reference_form`): Enter a brief description of the job applicant.
* **Job Details** (`job_applicant_details`): Enter a brief description of the job the applicant is or was applying for.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter the job title of the applicant's job.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`): Enter the type of job the applicant held (e.g., full-time, part-time, freelance).
* **Employer Name** (`job_employer_name`): Enter the name of the applicant's employer.
* **Employment Date** (`job_employment_date`): Enter the date the applicant started and ended their employment (if applicable).
* **Reference Details** (`reference_details`): Enter a brief description of the reference, including their job title and department.
* **Reference Contact Information** (`reference_contact_information`): Enter the contact information of the reference, including phone number and email (if available).
* **Reference Phone** (`reference_phone`): Enter the reference's phone number.
* **Reference Email** (`reference_email`): Enter the reference's email address.
* **Employer Contact Information** (`employer_contact_information`): Enter the contact information of the employer, including phone number and email (if available).
* **Job Status** (`job_status`): Select the job status of the applicant (Active or Inactive).
* **Job Application ID** (`job_application_id`): Enter the ID of the job application (if applicable).

Note: Ensure that you provide accurate and truthful information when filling out this form. If you're unsure about any of the fields, consult with the hiring team or the job applicant.
