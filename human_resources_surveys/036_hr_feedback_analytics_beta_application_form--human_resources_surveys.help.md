# HR Feedback Analytics Beta Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Feedback Analytics Beta Application Form is a tool used to gather feedback and user experience data from potential customers and existing users of the HR Feedback Analytics Beta Application. This form is essential for understanding users' goals, motivations, and specific use cases for the platform.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **First name**: Enter your first name.
2. **Last name**: Enter your last name.
3. **Department**: Select the department you are part of.
4. **Email**: Enter your email address.
5. **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
6. **Why do you want to try our platform?**: Briefly describe your motivations for using the HR Feedback Analytics Beta Application.
7. **How will our platform help you?**: Explain how the HR Feedback Analytics Beta Application will help you achieve your goals.
8. **What are your specific use cases for our platform?**: Describe your specific use cases for the HR Feedback Analytics Beta Application.
9. **What is your role?**: Select your job role.
10. **Assigned tool(s)**:
	* **Assigned Tool 1**: Enter your assigned tool 1.
	* **Assigned Tool 2**: Enter your assigned tool 2.
	* **Assigned Tool 3**: Enter your assigned tool 3.
	* **Assigned Tool 4**: Enter your assigned tool 4.
11. **Tips**

* Please keep your answers brief and clear.
* Make sure to provide specific and concrete information about your goals and motivations.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for clarification.
