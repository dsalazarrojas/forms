# Corporate Work Life Balance Initiative Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback on the effectiveness and impact of our corporate work-life balance initiatives. Your input will help us understand what programs and policies are working well and where we can make improvements to better support your well-being and job satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant answers for each question, and provide any additional feedback in the "Feedback On Programs" text box. Please answer all questions that apply to you, and provide specific examples or details if possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Work Life Balance Programs** (`work_life_balance_programs`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all programs that you are currently enrolled in or interested in, as part of our company's work-life balance initiatives.
* **Current Program Satisfaction** (`current_program_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): How satisfied are you with our current work-life balance programs? Please select either "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied".
* **Feedback On Programs** (`feedback_on_programs`, `note`, required: false): If you have any feedback or comments about our work-life balance programs, please provide them here.
* **Work Life Balance Policies** (`work_life_balance_policies`, `text`, required: false): Are there any work-life balance policies that you would like to see implemented in our company? If so, please describe them briefly.
* **Impact Of Programs** (`impact_of_programs`, `select_one`, required: false): Do you think our work-life balance programs have had a positive impact on your work-life balance? Please select either "Yes" or "No".
* **Satisfaction Trends** (`satisfaction_trends`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Are you experiencing any trends of increasing or decreasing satisfaction with our work-life balance programs? Please select all that apply.
* **Support Services** (`support_services`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Have you utilized any of our support services? If yes, please select all that apply.
* **Communication Plan** (`communication_plan`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Are you satisfied with our communication plan and frequency of updates? If no, please let us know how we can improve it.
