# interview_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal and professional information from individuals, including their contact details and answers to specific questions. The data collected will be used for internal purposes only.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details in the "Personal details" section.
2. Enter your contact information in the "Contact details" section.
3. Answer the questions in the "Answers" section, which is a text area for providing more in-depth responses.
4. If applicable, describe your motivation for applying or participating in the process in the "Motivation" section.
5. Assess your suitability for the opportunity or position in the "Suitability" section.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" section.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" section.
8. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Note" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal details
* **Personal details** (`Personal details`, `text`, required/optional): This section collects your basic information, such as your name, address, and birthdate.

### Contact details
* **Contact details** (`Contact details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.

### Answers
* **Answers** (`Answers`, `note`, required/optional): This is a text area for you to provide more in-depth responses to specific questions.

### Motivation
* **Motivation** (`Motivation`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your motivation for applying or participating in the process.

### Suitability
* **Suitability** (`Suitability`, `text`, required/optional): Assess your suitability for the opportunity or position by describing your relevant skills and experience.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.

### Email
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address.

### Note
* **Note** (`Note`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your application or participation.
