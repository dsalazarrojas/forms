# Human Capital Metrics Analysis Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Human Capital Metrics Analysis Request Form is for requesting analysis of specific employee data. This form is intended to be used for internal purposes only, such as analyzing workforce trends, identifying areas of improvement, and providing insights for HR-related decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the required information.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out.
3. Provide valid justification for your request.
4. Submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`user_input`): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Last Name** (`user_last_name`): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Email** (`user_email`): Please enter a valid email address associated with your organization.
- **Phone** (`user_phone`): Please enter your work phone number.
- **Company** (`company`): Please enter the name of your department or team.
- **Department** (`department`): Please enter the name of your department or team.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`): Please enter your official job title.
- **Location** (`location`): Please enter the location where you work.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Please enter your date of birth in MM/DD format.
- **Date of Hire** (`date_of_hire`): Please enter your date of hire in MM/DD format.

## Tips
- Please ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- If you are requesting for a manager or supervisor, please provide valid justification for your request.
- If you are requesting for an employee, please provide valid justification for your own request.
- This form will be reviewed by the HR team before processing your request. If the request is a duplicate, a note will be sent to you explaining why it cannot be processed.
