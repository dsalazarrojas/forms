# Leadership Skills Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leadership Skills Survey is a self-assessment tool designed to help leaders evaluate their leadership skills and identify areas for improvement. This form is meant to be completed by team members, and the feedback collected will be used to support leadership development and growth.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, providing examples or specific instances to support your answers.
2. For the rating questions, rate your skills on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "Needs Improvement" and 5 being "Excellent".
3. You can add any additional comments or feedback in the "self-motivation", "leadership style", "leadership style 2", "self-mastery", "leadership mastery", "team work", "team work 2", "team leadership", "leadership training", and "leadership skills" fields, if you feel that your answers require more explanation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Leadership Skills Survey Questions**: This section is meant to be completed by team members who will provide feedback on your leadership skills. Please provide clear and specific answers to help support your growth and development as a leader.
* **Communication Skills**: This question is to assess your ability to effectively communicate with team members, stakeholders, and other leaders. Please answer this question honestly, providing examples of your communication strategies and approaches.
* **Leadership Skills Rating**: This rating question asks you to evaluate your leadership skills on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "Needs Improvement" and 5 being "Excellent".
* **Social Interaction Rating**: This rating question is to assess your ability to interact and build relationships with team members, stakeholders, and other leaders. 
* **Interpersonal Skills Rating**: This rating question asks you to evaluate your interpersonal skills on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "Needs Improvement" and 5 being "Excellent".
* **Self-Motivation**: This question is to assess your motivation and drive to lead and grow as a leader. Please provide specific examples or instances that support your self-motivation.
* **Leadership Style**: This question is to evaluate your leadership style, please describe how you approach leadership.
* **Leadership Style 2**: This question is to provide additional feedback on your leadership style.
* **Self-Mastery**: This question is to assess your self-awareness, reflectiveness, and self-awareness of your abilities as a leader.
* **Leadership Mastery**: This question is to assess your leadership skills and competencies, please describe what you have learned and how you have applied it to grow as a leader.
* **Team Work**: This question is to evaluate your ability to work with and lead your team, please provide specific examples or instances that support your team work.
* **Team Work 2**: This question is to provide additional feedback on your team work.
* **Team Leadership**: This question is to assess your leadership of your team, please describe how you lead your team.
* **Leadership Training**: This question is to evaluate your participation in training and development activities to grow as a leader.
* **Leadership Skills**: This question is to provide additional feedback on your leadership skills.

Note: Please answer all questions, but you can skip the optional "self-motivation", "leadership style", "leadership style 2", "self-mastery", "leadership mastery", "team work", "team work 2", "team leadership", "leadership training", and "leadership skills" fields if you do not feel that they are relevant to you.
