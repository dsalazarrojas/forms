# New Hire Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Hire Feedback Form is a tool for managers and team leads to provide feedback on new hires who have completed their onboarding process and have been working for more than 2 weeks. This form helps the HR department identify areas of improvement and provide guidance on how to support new hires in their roles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have been working with the new hire.
2. If you select "Yes", fill out the feedback form with your honest opinions on the new hire's performance.
3. Provide a title for the feedback (e.g., "Strengths and Weaknesses of New Hire").
4. Enter any comments you have about the new hire's performance, behavior, or areas for improvement.
5. Select a rating for the new hire's performance (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
6. If you have any recommendations for the new hire, enter them in the text box.
7. Select a date for the frequency of the feedback (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly).
8. Choose the next action to take for the new hire (Submit, Review, or Assign).
9. If you select "Submit" as the next action, enter any comments for the HR department to take next steps.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Hire Feedback Form Page**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have been working with the new hire.
* **Feedback Title**: Enter a title for the feedback, e.g., "Strengths and Weaknesses of New Hire".
* **Feedback Comments**: Provide a detailed description of your feedback, including any specific strengths and weaknesses you have observed in the new hire.
* **Feedback Rating**: Select a rating for the new hire's performance (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Feedback Recommendations**: Enter any specific recommendations you have for the new hire, e.g., additional training or support.
* **Feedback Frequency**: Select a date for the frequency of the feedback (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly).
* **Feedback Next Action**: Choose the next action to take for the new hire (Submit, Review, or Assign).
* **Comments Next Action**: Enter any additional comments for the HR department to take next steps if you select "Submit" as the next action.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form for new hires who have completed their onboarding process and have been working for more than 2 weeks.
* Do not repeat this form for new hires who have already been evaluated; use the "Re-evaluation" form instead.
* Be as specific and detailed as possible when filling out the feedback form.
* Select the most suitable options for your feedback, e.g., if the new hire needs additional training, select "Assign".
* Make sure to enter any additional comments for the HR department to take next steps if you select "Submit" as the next action.
