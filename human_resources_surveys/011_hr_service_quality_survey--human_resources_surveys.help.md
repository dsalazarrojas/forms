# HR Service Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees on the quality of services provided by the HR department. Your responses will help us identify areas of improvement and ensure that we're meeting your expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions carefully, as they are essential in providing a comprehensive overview of your experience with the HR department.
2. Select all that apply to questions that ask for multiple options, such as "Do you feel that the HR department has provided good quality services?".
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, please provide them in the space provided.
4. Ensure that you fill out all required fields, marked as such.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Do you feel that the HR department has provided good quality services?** (`employee_survey`, select_multiple, required)
	+ This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the HR department's services.
* **Which department did you interact with recently?** (`employee_survey_department`, select_one, required)
	+ Choose the department you've recently interacted with, such as HR, Finance, or Marketing.
* **Comments and suggestions for improvement?** (`comments_and_suggestions`, text, optional)
	+ Provide any comments or suggestions you have for us to improve our services.
* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with the HR service?** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, required)
	+ Rate your satisfaction from 1 to 5.
* **Do you have any comments about your rating?** (`rating_comments`, text, optional)
	+ Provide any comments or feedback about your rating.
* **Are you satisfied with the services provided by the HR department across all departments?** (`satisfaction_across_departments`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Select all that apply, indicating your overall satisfaction across all departments.
* **What are the next steps for the HR department to improve services?** (`next_steps`, text, optional)
	+ Provide any suggestions for improvements.
* **Have you had any contact with the HR department recently?** (`email`, email, optional)
	+ If yes, provide your email address.
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone_number`, text, optional)
	+ Provide your phone number.
* **What is your name?** (`name`, text, required)
	+ Please enter your name.
* **What is your job title?** (`job_title`, text, required)
	+ Please enter your job title.
* **Which department do you work in?** (`department`, select_one, required)
	+ Choose the department you work in.
* **What date and time did you interact with the HR department?** (`time`, time, optional)
	+ Provide the date and time of your interaction.
* **What date did you interact with the HR department?** (`date`, date, optional)
	+ Provide the date of your interaction.
* **How long did you take to respond?** (`time_taken`, number, optional)
	+ Provide the time it took you to respond.
* **Do you have any comments or feedback?** (`comments`, text, optional)
	+ Provide any comments or feedback.
