# Intern Performance Scorecard - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Intern Performance Scorecard is used to evaluate and document the performance of interns during their tenure at the company. This scorecard helps the supervisor assess various aspects of the intern's performance, providing a snapshot of their strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and resources before starting.
2. Read each field carefully and enter the requested information accurately.
3. Answer all the questions thoroughly, using the available space for comments and explanations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter the intern's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the intern's last name.
* **Email**: Enter the intern's email address.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter your name as the supervisor.
* **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project the intern was working on.
* **Task Title**: Enter the title of the task or assignment the intern was given.
* **Performance Rating**: Select one of the four performance ratings: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor. This rating reflects the intern's overall performance during the review period.
* **Areas For Improvement**: List specific areas where the intern could improve or grow in their performance.
* **Comments**: Provide a brief explanation or comment about the intern's performance.
* **Supervisor Comments**: Add any additional comments or suggestions from the supervisor.
* **Rating Scale**: Rate the intern's performance on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the performance review.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the performance review.
* **Score**: Enter a numerical score, which will be calculated based on the rating scale.

## Tips
* Be honest and objective when completing the form.
* Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your ratings and comments.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Keep the tone professional and respectful throughout the form.
