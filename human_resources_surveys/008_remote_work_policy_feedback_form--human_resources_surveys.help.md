# Remote Work Policy Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees regarding their experiences and challenges with the remote work policy.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately with your information.
2. Be honest in your responses, as your feedback is valuable to us.
3. You can choose one or multiple options for certain fields, depending on your preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Please enter your full first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Please enter your full last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Please enter your work email address.
* **Team** (`Team`, number, optional): Please select the team you are part of.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, text, optional): Please enter your job title.
* **Department** (`Department`, text, optional): Please enter your department name.
* **Work From** (`Work From`, select_one, optional): Please select where you work from:
	+ Home
	+ Office
	+ Other
	+ Hybrid
* **Hours Worked** (`Hours Worked`, number, optional): Please enter the number of hours you work per week.
* **What challenges are you facing?** (`What challenges are you facing?`, text, optional): Please describe any challenges you are facing while working remotely.
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, text, optional): Please provide your feedback on the remote work policy.
* **Manager** (`Manager`, text, optional): Please enter your manager's name.
* **Department Head** (`Department Head`, select_one, optional): Please select if you have a department head:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Department Head Name** (`Department Head Name`, text, optional): Please enter your department head's name.
* **Manager Name** (`Manager Name`, text, optional): Please enter your manager's name.
* **Team Lead** (`Team Lead`, select_one, optional): Please select your team lead:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Team Lead Name** (`Team Lead Name`, text, optional): Please enter your team lead's name.
* **Prophets Work Life** (`Prophets Work Life`, select_one, optional): Please select your experience with prophets work life:
	+ Happy
	+ Sad
	+ Angry
* **Team Lead Response** (`Team Lead Response`, text, optional): Please enter any response from your team lead.
* **Feedback Response** (`Feedback Response`, text, optional): Please enter any feedback you received.
* **Department Head Response** (`Department Head Response`, text, optional): Please enter any response from your department head.
* **Manager Response** (`Manager Response`, text, optional): Please enter any response from your manager.
* **Follow-up with Team Lead** (`Follow-up with Team Lead`, select_one, optional): Please select if you want to follow-up with your team lead:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Follow-up with Department Head** (`Follow-up with Department Head`, select_one, optional): Please select if you want to follow-up with your department head:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Follow-up with Manager** (`Follow-up with Manager`, select_one, optional): Please select if you want to follow-up with your manager:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Submitted** (`Submitted`, select_one, optional): Please select if you have already submitted your feedback:
	+ True
	+ False

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled out completely.
* Be truthful and honest in your responses.
* Your feedback is valuable and will help improve the remote work policy.
