# Employment Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employment Engagement Survey is a tool for measuring employee engagement and satisfaction with the company. It is used to gather data on overall happiness and satisfaction levels, as well as perception of the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. For the overall happiness and satisfaction level questions, select a number that best represents your current feelings.
3. For the perception question, select a number that best represents your feelings about the company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Overall happiness** (required: false)
* This question asks about your current level of happiness with your job. Think about your feelings over the past week.

### **Satisfaction level** (required: false)
* This question asks about your current level of satisfaction with your job. Think about your feelings about your work and your experience at the company.

### **Perception** (required: false)
* This question asks about your perception of the company. Think about your feelings about the company culture, values, and overall environment.

## Tips
* Be honest and objective when answering the questions.
* Think about your feelings over the past week or two when answering the questions.
* If you're unsure about what to answer, try to choose the number that best represents your overall feelings.
