# HR Policy Change Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Policy Change Request Form is a tool for employees and managers to submit and track requests for changes to HR policies. This form helps the HR department to gather information about proposed changes, ensuring that they are processed efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit a New Request" button to start filling out the form.
2. In the "Requesting A Policy Change" field, briefly describe the policy you'd like to change.
3. In the "Reason For Changing The Policy" field, provide a thorough explanation for your request.
4. Enter your name and email address in the "Employee Name" and "Employee Email" fields.
5. Enter your manager's name and email address in the "Manager Name" and "Manager Email" fields.
6. Select the expected date and time of the policy change in the "Expected Date of Change" and "Expected Time of Change" fields.
7. In the "Which HR Policy Would You Like to Change?" field, select all the applicable options that correspond to the policy you'd like to change.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requesting A Policy Change**: This field is where you describe the policy you'd like to change. Please be concise, but provide enough information for the HR department to understand your request.
* **Reason For Changing The Policy**: This field is where you provide a thorough explanation for your request. Please include all relevant details and context.
* **Employee Name**: This field is where you enter your name as the person submitting the request.
* **Employee Email**: This field is where you enter your email address as the person submitting the request.
* **Manager Name**: This field is where you enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email**: This field is where you enter your manager's email address.
* **Expected Date of Change**: This field is where you select the date when you expect the policy change to take place.
* **Expected Time of Change**: This field is where you select the time when you expect the policy change to take place.
* **Which HR Policy Would You Like to Change?**: This field is where you select all applicable options that correspond to the policy you'd like to change. Please only select options that are relevant to your request.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide all required information to ensure your request is processed efficiently.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, it's always best to consult with your manager or HR representative.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your HR department.
