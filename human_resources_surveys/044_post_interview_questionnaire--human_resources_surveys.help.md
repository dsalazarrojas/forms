# Post Interview Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Post Interview Questionnaire is a tool used to collect information from candidates after an interview, helping the hiring team to assess their qualifications, motivation, and fit for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out each field accurately and completely.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, based on the information provided during the interview.
3. Do not repeat information already provided during the interview.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details** (`id: 1`, `name: personal_details`, required: false): Please provide your name, address, and other personal details as per your interview.
* **Contact Information** (`id: 2`, `name: contact_information`, required: false): Enter your email address and phone number as per your interview.
* **CV** (`id: 3`, `name: cv`, required: false): Upload your CV as per your interview.
* **Motivation** (`id: 4`, `name: motivation`, required: false): Briefly explain why you want to join this team.
* **Email** (`id: 5`, `name: email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`id: 6`, `name: phone`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`id: 7`, `name: date_of_birth`, required: false): Enter your date of birth.
* **Nationality** (`id: 8`, `name: nationality`, required: false): Select your nationality.
* **Language** (`id: 9`, `name: language`, required: false): Select your primary language.
* **Resume** (`id: 10`, `name: resume`, required: false): Upload your resume.
* **Motivation Reason** (`id: 11`, `name: motivation_reason`, required: false): Briefly explain why you are a good fit for this role.
* **Work Experience** (`id: 12`, `name: work_experience`, required: false): Select your relevant work experience.
* **Education** (`id: 13`, `name: education`, required: false): Select your highest level of education.
* **Skills** (`id: 14`, `name: skills`, required: false): Select your relevant skills.
* **Location** (`id: 15`, `name: location`, required: false): Select your preferred location.
* **Available Start Date** (`id: 16`, `name: available_start_date`, required: false): Enter the date you are available to start work.
* **Available End Date** (`id: 17`, `name: available_end_date`, required: false): Enter the date you are available to end work.
* **Available Duration** (`id: 18`, `name: available_duration`, required: false): Enter the duration you are available to work.
* **Salary** (`id: 19`, `name: salary`, required: false): Enter your salary expectation.
* **Salary Negotiable** (`id: 20`, `name: salary_negotiable`, required: false): Briefly mention if your salary is negotiable.
* **Job Title** (`id: 21`, `name: job_title`, required: false): Enter your current or desired job title.
* **Current Company** (`id: 22`, `name: current_company`, required: false): Enter your current company.
* **Previous Companies** (`id: 23`, `name: previous_companies`, required: false): List previous companies you have worked for.
* **References** (`id: 24`, `name: references`, required: false): Provide references.
* **Job References** (`id: 25`, `name: job_references`, required: false): Job references for previous companies you worked for.

## Tips

* Please provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* Do not exaggerate or misrepresent your qualifications or experiences.
* This form is meant to be a quick reference for the hiring team, so please keep your answers brief and to the point.
