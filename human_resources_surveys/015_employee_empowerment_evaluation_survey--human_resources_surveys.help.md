# Employee Empowerment Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this survey is to gather your thoughts and feelings about your role and experience within the company. Your feedback is crucial in helping us understand what we're doing well and where we need improvement. Your input will help us create a more positive and empowering work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please ensure you have a stable internet connection and a suitable device to access the form.
2.  Review the form carefully to understand what each question is asking.
3.  Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
4.  For select fields (communication_level, feedback_frequency, overall_job_satisfaction, level_of_empowerment), choose the option that best represents your perception.
5.  For text fields (employee_ambassadors, suggestions, open_feedback), write your thoughts and opinions in the space provided.
6.  Ensure you fill in your email and phone number (if applicable) for communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What do you value most about your role** (`employee_empowerment_evaluation_survey_1`): This question is designed to understand what aspects of your role you find most meaningful and valuable.
*   **Level of Communication** (`communication_level`): This question helps us understand how often you receive updates and information from your manager, HR, or other teams. Choose the option that best represents your experience.
*   **Employee Ambassadors** (`employee_ambassadors`): If you feel like you have a strong support system at work, you may select multiple options. This question is not mandatory, and you can choose as many or as few options that apply to you.
*   **Feedback Frequency** (`feedback_frequency`): This question helps us understand how often you receive feedback from your manager or HR. Choose the option that best represents your experience.
*   **Overall Job Satisfaction** (`overall_job_satisfaction`): This question helps us understand your overall satisfaction with your job. Choose the option that best represents your feelings.
*   **Level of Empowerment** (`level_of_empowerment`): This question helps us understand how much control and authority you feel you have in your role. Choose the option that best represents your experience.
*   **Suggestions** (`suggestions`): This question is an open opportunity for you to provide any suggestions or ideas you have for improving the work environment.
*   **Open Feedback** (`open_feedback`): This question is for any additional comments or thoughts you'd like to share about your experience.
*   **Email** (`email`): Please provide your company email address for us to contact you if needed. This is optional but helps us communicate with you about your feedback.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Provide your phone number if you'd like to be reachable via phone for feedback discussion. This is optional.

This guide is designed to help you complete the Employee Empowerment Evaluation Survey with ease. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out.
