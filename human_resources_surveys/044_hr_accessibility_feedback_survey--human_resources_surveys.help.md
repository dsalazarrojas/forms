# hr_accessibility_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hr_accessibility_feedback_survey form is designed to collect feedback from employees on accessibility features and barriers in the workplace. This survey aims to gather information to help the organization improve accessibility and provide better support to employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you understand the purpose and scope.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Select the relevant options for each field.
4. Submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, note, required: false): Please provide your honest feedback on your experiences with accessibility features and barriers in the workplace.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Name** (`Name`, text, required: false): Enter your name so that we can address you personally.
* **Department** (`Department`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all departments you are familiar with or have experience in.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, text, required: false): Enter your job title.
* **Accessibility Features** (`Accessibility Features`, select_one, required: false): Select the accessibility features you are familiar with or have experience in.
* **Accessibility Barriers** (`Accessibility Barriers`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the accessibility barriers you have encountered.
* **Feedback Frequency** (`Feedback Frequency`, select_one, required: false): Select how often you experience accessibility barriers.
* **Accessibility Resources** (`Accessibility Resources`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the resources you would like to see to address accessibility barriers.
* **Accessibility Barriers Impact** (`Accessibility Barriers Impact`, select_one, required: false): Select the impact of accessibility barriers on your work or experience.
* **Accessibility Barriers Frequency** (`Accessibility Barriers Frequency`, select_multiple, required: false): Select how frequently you experience accessibility barriers.
* **Employee Support** (`Employee Support`, select_one, required: false): Select the level of support you receive regarding accessibility.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible in your feedback.
* Select all relevant departments or job titles.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improving accessibility, please include them in your feedback.
