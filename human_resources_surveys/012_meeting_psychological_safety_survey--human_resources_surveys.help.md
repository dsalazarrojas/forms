# Meeting Psychological Safety Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to evaluate the psychological safety and satisfaction of meeting participants, aiming to create a more comfortable and effective meeting environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly, considering how you feel about each aspect of the meeting.
2. Select the response that best fits your sentiment for each question.
3. Make sure to complete all required questions.
