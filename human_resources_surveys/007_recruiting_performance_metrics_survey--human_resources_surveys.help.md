# Recruiting Performance Metrics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the performance of recruiters in terms of their skills and behavior. It will help us understand how well they're doing in different areas and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the job opening details provided on the first page to ensure you have a clear understanding of the role and requirements.
2. Answer the questions on each page, providing feedback based on your experience with the recruiter.
3. For select_one and number fields, choose the answer that best reflects your opinion.
4. For text fields, type your answer in the space provided.
5. Review all pages to ensure you've answered all the questions relevant to the job opening.
6. Submit the form once you've completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Opening Details** (text, required): Please provide a brief description of the job opening, including the job title, job description, and any other relevant details.
* **How would you rate this job opening?** (select_one, optional): Choose one of the five options provided:
	+ Strongly Disagree
	+ Somewhat Disagree
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Agree
	+ Strongly Agree
* **How would you rate the communication skills of the recruiter?** (number, optional): Choose a number from 1 to 5, where:
	+ 1: Strongly Disagree
	+ 2: Somewhat Disagree
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Somewhat Agree
	+ 5: Strongly Agree
* **How would you rate the time management of the recruiter?** (number, optional): Choose a number from 1 to 5, where:
	+ 1: Strongly Disagree
	+ 2: Somewhat Disagree
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Somewhat Agree
	+ 5: Strongly Agree
* **How would you rate the problem-solving skills of the recruiter?** (text, optional): Please provide a brief description of the recruiter's problem-solving skills, including any strengths or weaknesses you've observed.
* **Additional comments?** (note, optional): Please provide any additional comments or feedback about the job opening, including any areas for improvement.
