# Startup Workplace Culture Employment Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees on the workplace culture of a startup. It aims to gather insights on what they liked and disliked about their experience, what they think the startup did right and wrong, and to gather their overall feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by writing a brief introduction to your feedback, if you wish to do so.
2. Provide your team name in the "Team Name" field.
3. Specify your role within the team in the "Team Role" field.
4. Choose how long you have been with the company in the "How long" field.
5. In the "What did you like about our startup culture?" field, describe specific things you enjoyed about your experience.
6. In the "What did you dislike about our startup culture?" field, describe specific things you did not enjoy about your experience.
7. In the "What did we get right?" field, describe specific things you think the startup did well.
8. In the "What did we get wrong?" field, describe specific things you think the startup did poorly.
9. Provide any additional feedback you have in the "What feedback do you have for us?" field.
10. Enter your email address in the "Email" field so that we can reach out to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
This is a text field where you can provide a brief introduction to your feedback.

### Team Name
* *Team Name* (`Team Name`, text, not required): Enter the name of your team.

### Team Role
* *Team Role* (`Team Role`, text, not required): Enter your role within the team.

### How long
* *How long* (`How long`, select_one, not required): Choose how long you have been with the company:
	+ 1 month or less
	+ 2-6 months
	+ 7-12 months
	+ more than 1 year

### What did you like about our startup culture?
* *What did you like about our startup culture?* (`What did you like about our startup culture?`, text, not required): Describe specific things you enjoyed about your experience.

### What did you dislike about our startup culture?
* *What did you dislike about our startup culture?* (`What did you dislike about our startup culture?`, text, not required): Describe specific things you did not enjoy about your experience.

### What did we get right?
* *What did we get right?* (`What did we get right?`, text, not required): Describe specific things you think the startup did well.

### What did we get wrong?
* *What did we get wrong?* (`What did we get wrong?`, text, not required): Describe specific things you think the startup did poorly.

### What feedback do you have for us?
* *What feedback do you have for us?* (`What feedback do you have for us?`, text, not required): Provide any additional feedback you have for the startup.

### Email
* *Email* (`Email`, email, not required): Enter your email address so that we can reach out to you.

### Team Size
* *Team Size* (`Team Size`, select_multiple, not required): Choose the size of your team:
	+ Small
	+ Medium
	+ Large

### Department
* *Department* (`Department`, text, not required): Enter your department name.

## Tips
* Please be specific and honest in your feedback.
* If you have already provided feedback in the past, please do not fill out this form again.
* If you need help or have questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.
