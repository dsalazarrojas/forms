**Staff Appraisal Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Staff Appraisal Form is used to evaluate and document the performance of staff members in an organization. It is an essential tool to ensure that staff are performing their duties effectively and to identify areas that require improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
* Review the form carefully and answer all questions accurately.
* Complete each section as instructed, using the options provided.
* Ensure that you have attached all required documents and files.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **job_description_upload** (Required/Optional): This field is used to upload your job description for the past year. Please attach a clear and concise document that outlines your job responsibilities and expectations.
* **job_objectives_upload** (Required/Optional): Similar to the previous field, this is used to upload your job objectives for the past year. Please be sure to attach a document that clearly outlines your objectives and how they were met.
* **performance_evaluation** (Yes/No): In this field, you will select your performance evaluation for the past year. Please answer 'Yes' if you believe your performance was satisfactory, and 'No' if you believe it was not.
* **tasks_achievements_failures_2022** (Select Multiple/Yes/No): In this field, you will select all tasks, achievements, and failures that you experienced in the past year. Please be specific and provide clear examples.
* **expectations_2023** (Select Multiple/Yes/No): In this field, you will select your expectations for the upcoming year. Please be sure to clearly outline what you expect to achieve and accomplish.
* **uploaded_files** (Select Multiple/Yes/No): This field is used to select all files that you would like to upload with your appraisal form. Please be sure to attach only relevant and supporting documents.
* **submit** (Required/Not Applicable): In this field, you will submit your completed form. Please ensure that you have attached all required documents and completed all fields accurately.

Note: The form is designed to be completed by staff members, and the information provided will be used to inform and improve future performance and development opportunities.
