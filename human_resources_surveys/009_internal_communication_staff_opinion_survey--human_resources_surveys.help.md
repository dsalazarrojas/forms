# Internal Communication Staff Opinion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather your opinions and feedback on our internal communication methods. Your responses will help us understand how we can improve communication within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the questions on each page. You can select multiple options for questions that allow multiple selections. For text fields, type your answer in the provided space. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What do you think about our internal communication?** (`staff_opinion_survey`, text, optional): This question is to gather your overall opinion on our internal communication. Please share your thoughts in the provided text field.
- **Which internal communication channels do you use most?** (`communication_channels`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the channels you use most frequently. Options include Email, Team meetings, Intranet, Social media, and Instant messaging.
- **How often do you receive internal communication?** (`staff_communication_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose how often you receive internal communication. Options are Daily, Weekly, Monthly, and Rarely.
- **What is the effectiveness of internal communication?** (`communication_effectiveness`, select_one, optional): Rate the effectiveness of our internal communication. Options are Highly effective, Somewhat effective, and Not effective.
- **What internal communication improvements would you suggest?** (`staff_suggestions`, text, optional): Share any specific improvements you think we can make to our internal communication.
- **Which communication channels do you prefer for sending feedback?** (`feedback_channels`, select_multiple, optional): Select the channels you prefer for sending feedback. Options are Email, Instant messaging, In-person, and Phone.
- **What is your preferred method for receiving internal communication?** (`communication_preferences`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred method for receiving internal communication. Options are Email, Instant messaging, In-person, and Phone.
- **Any other comments or suggestions?** (`staff_opinion`, text, optional): Share any additional thoughts or suggestions you have.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and share your true opinions.
- You can select multiple options for questions that allow multiple selections.
- For text fields, type in the provided space. 

This help guide is provided to make your experience smooth and to ensure you complete the survey with accurate information. If you have any questions, contact us at form@gic.mx.
