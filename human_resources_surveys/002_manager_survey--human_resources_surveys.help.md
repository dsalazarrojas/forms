# Manager Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Manager Survey is a tool for managers to provide feedback on team members. This feedback is essential for helping teams improve and grow. The survey is designed to gather information on various aspects of team performance, including contact details, time-related information, and specific feedback on team members.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Choose the correct answer for each question based on your understanding of the situation.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked with an asterisk *).
