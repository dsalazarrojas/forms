# Best HR Management Tool Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The **Best HR Management Tool Nomination Form** is a form designed to help HR professionals recommend and nominate their go-to tools for various HR management tasks. This form will help us identify the most effective tools used by HR teams across the organization, providing insights for future development and improvement of our HR management systems.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your information as the nominator in the sections labeled **-- Nominator Information --** and **-- Final Submission --**.
2. Enter the details of the nominated tool in the sections labeled **-- HR Tool Nominee Details --** and **-- Nomination Narrative --**.
3. Provide a detailed description of the tool's features and benefits in the section labeled **What is the one killer feature that makes this tool stand out**.
4. Rate the tool's performance in various categories using the rating system (1-5).
5. Submit your nomination by filling in the required fields and providing a clear and concise description of the tool's impact on your HR department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Your Full Name** (`nominator_full_name`, text, **Required**): 
Enter your full name as the nominator.

### 2. **Job Title** (`job_title_text`, text, **Required**): 
Enter your job title in the organization.

### 3. **Company Name** (`company_name_text`, text, **Required**): 
Enter the name of your company.

### 4. **Work Email** (`work_email_address`, email, **Required**): 
Enter your work email address.

### 5. **HR Tool Name** (`hr_tool_name`, text, **Required**): 
Enter the official name of the software or tool.

### 6. **Vendor/Company Name** (`vendor_company_name`, text, **Required**): 
Enter the name of the tool's developer or vendor.

### 7. **Tool Website** (`tool_website_url`, text, **Optional**): 
Enter the URL for product information.

### 8. **Tool Type Category** (`tool_type_category`, **select_one**, **Required**): 
Select the primary function of the software from the options provided.

### 9. **Usage Duration** (`usage_duration`, **select_one**, **Required**): 
Select how long your company has been using this tool.

### 10. **Evaluation Criteria** (`evaluation_criteria_header`, note, **Required**): 
This section is for your reference as the nominator to evaluate the tool.

### 11. **Ease of Use and UI Design** (`ease_of_use_rating`, **select_one**, **Required**): 
Rate the tool's user interface and overall usability from 1-5.

### 12. **Feature Richness and Capabilities** (`feature_richness_rating`, **select_one**, **Required**): 
Rate the tool's features and capabilities from 1-5.

### 13. **Customer Support and Training** (`customer_support_rating`, **select_one**, **Required**): 
Rate the tool's customer support and training from 1-5.

### 14. **Value for Money** (`value_for_money_rating`, **select_one**, **Required**): 
Rate the tool's value for money from 1-5.

### 15. **Integration Capabilities** (`integration_capabilities_rating`, **select_one**, **Required**): 
Rate the tool's integration capabilities with other systems from 1-5.

### 16. **Impact Description** (`impact_description`, text, **Required**): 
Describe the biggest impact this tool has had on your HR department.

### 17. **Killer Feature Description** (`killer_feature_desc`, text, **Required**): 
Describe the single best thing about the software.

### 18. **Recommendation** (`recommendation_intent`, **select_one**, **Required**): 
Select whether you would recommend this tool to other HR professionals.

### 19. **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date_input`, date, **Required**): 
Enter the date you are submitting this nomination.
