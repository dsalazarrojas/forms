# Employee Incident Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information about employee incidents, including events or situations that affect employee safety, well-being, or work performance. The goal is to gather accurate and detailed information about the incident to support investigation and resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Report An Incident" to confirm that you are reporting an actual incident.
2. Describe the incident in detail in the "Incident Description" field.
3. Choose the type of incident that occurred from the "Incident Type" dropdown list.
4. Select the person who observed the incident from the "Observed By" field. If no one observed the incident, you can leave this field blank.
5. Choose the person who reported the incident to from the "Reported To" dropdown list.
6. Enter the date and time the incident was reported in the "Date Reported" and "Time Reported" fields.
7. Enter the date and time the incident occurred in the "Incident Date" and "Incident Time" fields.
8. Provide a list of witnesses in the "Witness List" field.
9. Upload any supporting documentation or media, such as photos, videos, or audio recordings, in the "Incident Photo", "Incident Video", "Incident Audio Record", and "Incident Document" fields.
10. Enter any additional notes or comments about the incident in the "Incident Notes" and "Employee Comments" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report An Incident**: Select "Yes" to confirm that you are reporting an actual incident.
* **Incident Description**: Describe the incident in detail, including what happened, where it occurred, and any relevant details.
* **Incident Type**: Choose the type of incident that occurred, such as Equipment Failure or Human Error.
* **Observed By**: If someone witnessed the incident, select their name or role. If no one observed the incident, leave this field blank.
* **Supervisor Observed**: If your supervisor witnessed the incident, select their name or role. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Reported By**: Select who reported the incident to: your manager or Human Resources.
* **Reported To**: Select who you reported the incident to: your immediate supervisor or Human Resources.
* **Date Reported**: Enter the date the incident was reported.
* **Time Reported**: Enter the time the incident was reported.
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Location**: Enter a brief description of the location where the incident occurred.
* **Witness List**: Provide a list of individuals who witnessed the incident.
* **Incident Photo**: Upload a photo or images related to the incident.
* **Incident Video**: Upload a video or videos related to the incident.
* **Incident Audio Record**: Upload an audio recording related to the incident.
* **Incident Document**: Upload any supporting documentation related to the incident.
* **Incident Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the incident.
* **Employee Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about the incident from the employee reporting it.
* **Location**: Enter a brief description of the location where the incident occurred.
