# Employee Motivation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees on their level of motivation and satisfaction within the company. Your input will help us understand what we do well and where we can improve to foster a more motivated and engaged work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each section and answer the questions according to your honest feelings and experiences. Make sure to select the options that best fit your situation. If you have any trouble or questions, don't hesitate to contact your HR representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Motivation Survey**: This is the main question for this page. Please select all the options that describe your level of motivation in the workplace.
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Not satisfied
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Overall Satisfaction Level**: This is a numerical value representing your overall satisfaction with the company. Please enter a number between 1 (very dissatisfied) and 5 (very satisfied).
* **Job Satisfaction Level**: This is a text field where you can describe your level of job satisfaction. Please provide a brief but clear answer.
* **Recommend Company**: This question asks whether you would recommend our company to others. Please select 'True' if you would definitely recommend, and 'False' otherwise.
* **Recognition and Valuation**: This question asks how often you feel recognized and valued in the workplace. Please enter a number between 1 (rarely) and 5 (always).
* **Motivation to Learn New Skills**: This is a time input field. Please enter the amount of time you would like to spend learning new skills.
* **Motivation to Perform Extra Effort**: This is another number input field. Please enter the number that best represents your motivation to perform extra work.
* **Motivation to Stay**: This is a text field where you can describe your motivation to stay with the company. Please provide a brief but clear answer.

## Tips
* Be honest in your answers. Your feedback is valuable to us and will help shape our policies and procedures.
* You can answer as many or as few questions as you like, but please try to answer all fields if possible.
* If you have any comments or questions, don't hesitate to reach out to your HR representative.
