# Talent Management Strategy Effectiveness Poll - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect opinions and feedback from employees on the current talent management strategies and practices within the organization. The goal is to understand how well these strategies are working and identify areas for improvement. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly to provide valuable insights for the company's talent management team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following sections, making sure to provide answers that reflect your personal experiences and opinions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Feedback** (`employee_feedback`, text, optional): This question is an open-ended field where you can share your thoughts and opinions about the talent management strategies and practices within the company. Please be as specific and detailed as possible in your answer.
* **Recruiting Practices** (`recruiting_practices`, select_multiple, optional): This question asks about your experiences and preferences regarding the company's recruiting practices. Select all the options that apply to you:
	+ Recruiting from within the company
	+ Recruiting from outside the company
	+ Both
* **Onboarding Process** (`onboarding_process`, select_one, optional): This question is about your experiences with the onboarding process. Choose the option that best describes your onboarding process:
	+ Onboarding program
	+ Regular check-ins
	+ Manager meetings
	+ None of the above
* **Development Opportunities** (`development_opportunities`, select_one, optional): This question is about your access to development opportunities within the company. Choose the option that best describes your experiences:
	+ Performance goals aligned with company goals
	+ Performance goals aligned with team goals
	+ Performance goals aligned with both company and team goals
	+ None of the above
* **Performance Practices** (`performance_practices`, select_multiple, optional): This question is about your experiences with performance management and development practices within the company. Select all the options that apply to you:
	+ 360 degree feedback
	+ Regular performance reviews
	+ Both
	+ None of the above
* **Talent Pipeline** (`talent_pipeline`, select_one, optional): This question is about your understanding of the company's talent pipeline. Choose the option that best describes your experiences:
	+ Internal talent pipeline
	+ External talent pipeline
	+ Both internal and external talent pipeline
	+ None of the above
* **How do you collect feedback?** (`feedback_collection`, select_multiple, optional): This question is about how you receive feedback within the company. Select all the options that apply to you:
	+ Regular surveys
	+ One-on-one meetings
	+ Both
	+ None of the above
* **How often do you receive feedback?** (`feedback_frequency`, select_one, optional): This question is about the frequency of feedback you receive. Choose the option that best describes your experiences:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Never

## Tips
* Please answer truthfully and thoroughly to provide valuable insights for the talent management team.
* If you have any comments or additional feedback, feel free to share them in the "Employee Feedback" field.
* This form is designed to be completed individually, so please do not share your answers with anyone.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the talent management team for assistance.
