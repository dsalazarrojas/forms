# new_hire_orientation_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new hire orientation checklist is a form used to collect information from new hires for their orientation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review each page carefully and make sure you have filled out all required fields.
* Enter your information in the designated fields.
* Please note that some fields are optional, but it's recommended to fill them out for a more comprehensive orientation experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire Company Info** (`company_info`, `text`, `false`): Enter your new hire company information.
* **Employee Info** (`employee_info`, `text`, `false`): Please enter your employee information, such as your name and job title.
* **New Hire Benefits** (`benefits`, `text`, `false`): List your benefits and any other details about your benefits.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, `false`): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Choose the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, `text`, `false`): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, `text`, `false`): Enter your emergency contact's address.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, `text`, `false`): Enter your emergency contact's email.
* **Emergency Contact Job Title** (`emergency_contact_job_title`, `text`, `false`): Enter your emergency contact's job title.
* **Emergency Contact Department** (`emergency_contact_department`, `text`, `false`): Enter your emergency contact's department.
* **Emergency Contact Building** (`emergency_contact_building`, `text`, `false`): Enter your emergency contact's building.
* **Emergency Contact Floor** (`emergency_contact_floor`, `text`, `false`): Enter your emergency contact's floor.
* **Emergency Contact Office** (`emergency_contact_office`, `text`, `false`): Enter your emergency contact's office.
* **Emergency Contact Notes** (`emergency_contact_notes`, `note`, `false`): Enter any notes about your emergency contact.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, `false`): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `text`, `false`): Enter your manager's email.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, `false`): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager Title** (`manager_title`, `text`, `false`): Enter your manager's title.
* **Manager Department** (`manager_department`, `text`, `false`): Enter your manager's department.
* **Manager Supervisor** (`manager_supervisor`, `text`, `false`): Enter your manager's supervisor.
* **Manager Supervisor Title** (`manager_supervisor_title`, `text`, `false`): Enter your manager's supervisor title.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, `false`): Enter your job title.
* **Job Classification** (`job_classification`, `text`, `false`): Enter your job classification.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, `false`): Choose your job type (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Intern, or Other).
* **Emergency Contact Notes** (`emergency_contact_notes`, `note`, `false`): Enter any notes about your emergency contact.

Please note that some of these fields are optional, but it's recommended to fill them out for a more comprehensive orientation experience.

## Tips
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to our HR team for assistance.
* This form will be reviewed by our HR team to ensure your information is accurate and up-to-date.
