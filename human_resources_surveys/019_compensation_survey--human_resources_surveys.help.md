# Compensation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about employee compensation needs and satisfaction. The feedback will help HR teams make informed decisions about compensation adjustments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the current annual salary range that applies to you.
2. Enter your target annual salary for the next fiscal year.
3. Choose which part of your compensation package you value the most (e.g. Base Salary, Bonus / Incentives, etc.).
4. Indicate your satisfaction with the current frequency of pay (e.g. bi-weekly, monthly, etc.).
5. Choose how much you understand the taxes and deductions on your pay stub (e.g. completely, somewhat, etc.).
6. Select how much you understand your eligibility for bonuses or incentives (e.g. completely, somewhat, etc.).
7. Indicate how much inflation has impacted your satisfaction with your current pay (e.g. major impact, moderate impact, etc.).
8. If you have any other feedback or comments about compensation, please provide them in the open comments section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Current Salary Band Range** (`current_salary_band_range`): This question asks for your current annual salary range. Please select the range that applies to you.
* **Target Salary for Next Fiscal Year** (`desired_salary_increase`): This question asks for your target annual salary for the next fiscal year. Please enter your target salary based on performance and market value.
* **Valued Part of Compensation Package** (`valuest_part_of_package`): This question asks which part of your compensation package you value the most. Please select the primary benefit that matters most to you.
* **Satisfaction with Pay Frequency** (`pay_frequency_satisfaction`): This question asks about your satisfaction with the current frequency of pay. Please select the frequency that best describes your feelings.
* **Understanding of Taxes and Deductions** (`understanding_of_deductions`): This question asks about your understanding of the taxes and deductions on your pay stub. Please select how much you understand the clarity of your payroll documentation.
* **Understanding of Bonus Eligibility** (`bonus_eligibility_understanding`): This question asks about your understanding of your eligibility for bonuses or incentives. Please select how much you understand your current status.
* **Inflation Impact on Satisfaction with Pay** (`inflation_impact_perception`): This question asks about how much inflation has impacted your satisfaction with your current pay. Please select the impact that best describes your feelings.
* **Additional Feedback on Compensation** (`compensation_feedback_open`): This question is an open-ended question for any other feedback or comments you would like to share about your compensation.
* **Date of Submission** (`comp_survey_date_submitted`): This question captures the current date and time of submission.
