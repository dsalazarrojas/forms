# Employer Cost Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employer Cost Analysis Survey is designed to collect data on various aspects of an employer's costs, including labor costs, benefits, and other related expenses. This data will help employers identify and categorize their costs, challenges, and categories, enabling them to make informed decisions and improve their organization's efficiency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Ensure that you answer all fields marked as "Required" to ensure accurate and complete data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information
* **General Information**: This section is meant to gather basic information about your organization. Please provide a brief description of your company or organization.
* **Labor Costs**: Enter your labor costs and expenses.
* **Benefits**: Enter your benefits expenses.
* **Company Information**: This section is meant to provide additional information about your company. Please provide any relevant details.
* **Finance Manager**: Enter your finance manager's contact information.
* **Employee Benefits**: Enter your employee benefits expenses.
* **Total Compensation**: Enter your total compensation expenses.
* **Labor Categories**: Enter your labor categories.
* **Benefits Categories**: Enter your benefits categories.
* **Cost Challenges**: Enter your cost challenges.
* **Total Cost Categories**: Enter your total cost categories.
* **Labor Cost Categories**: Enter your labor cost categories.
* **Cost Categories**: Enter your cost categories.
* **Duplicate Categories**: This section is meant to avoid duplication of categories. Please ensure that all categories are listed only once.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed.
* If you need to update or recomplete the form, please correct any discrepancies and avoid duplication of information.
