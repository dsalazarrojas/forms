# Holiday Office Hours Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on employee's awareness, satisfaction, and preferences regarding the current holiday office hours policy. It will help the management team to understand the employee's opinions and needs, and make informed decisions to improve the policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Select the page with the corresponding category (Awareness, Satisfaction, Preferences, etc.) and answer the questions on that page.
2. For each field, provide as much detail as you need to answer the question clearly.
3. Please note that some fields are optional, but your feedback is valuable to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Awareness (1)
* **Awareness**: This field is intended to gauge your current understanding of the holiday office hours policy. Please describe your level of awareness on the policy.
### Satisfaction (2)
* **Satisfaction**: Please rate your satisfaction with the current holiday office hours policy.
### Preferences (3)
* **Preferences**: Check all the options that apply to your preferences for holiday office hours.
	+ **Yes**: Select this option if you prefer this option.
### Future Preferences (4)
* **Future Preferences**: Select one option that best represents your preference for future holiday office hours.
	+ **Yes**: Select this option if you prefer this option.
### Holiday Office Hours (5)
* **Holiday Office Hours**: Please select the holiday office hours that you are aware of.
### Office Hours (6)
* **Office Hours**: Please select the office hours that you are aware of.
### Work Hours (7)
* **Work Hours**: Check all the options that apply to your work hours during holidays.
	+ **Yes**: Select this option if you prefer this option.
### Work Hours Other (8)
* **Work Hours Other**: If you have any other work hours during holidays that do not fit the previous options, please describe them in this field.
### Email (9)
* **Email**: Please enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for further discussions.
### Phone (10)
* **Phone**: Please enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted for further discussions.
### Other Phone (11)
* **Other Phone**: If you have an alternative phone number that you would like to be contacted, please enter it here.

Please note that some fields are intended for optional feedback. However, your input is highly valued, and every piece of feedback will contribute to the improvement of the holiday office hours policy.
