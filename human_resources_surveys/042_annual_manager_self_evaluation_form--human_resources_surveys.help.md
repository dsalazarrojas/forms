# Annual Manager Self Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Manager Self Evaluation Form is a comprehensive self-assessment tool for managers to evaluate their performance, goals, and challenges over the past year. It's designed to help managers reflect on their strengths and weaknesses, identify areas for improvement, and set objectives for the next period.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please make sure you are eligible to complete this form before proceeding. If you're not sure, check with your supervisor or HR representative.
2. Answer all the required questions truthfully and thoroughly. Provide specific examples and explanations for each question.
3. Be sure to answer all questions in a way that is respectful and constructive.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, not required): This is your unique employee identification number. If you're not sure what to enter, ask your HR representative.
- **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your work email address.
- **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select your department from the provided options.
- **Position Title** (`position_title`, text, required): Enter your current job title.
- **Direct Supervisor** (`direct_supervisor`, text, required): Identify who you report to.
- **Review Period** (`review_period`, select_one, required): Choose the review period for your self-evaluation.
- **Team Size** (`team_size`, number, required): Enter the number of direct reports you have.
- **Leadership Effectiveness** (`leadership_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Rate your leadership effectiveness over the past year.
- **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, select_one, required): Rate your communication skills with your team and stakeholders.
- **Decision Making** (`decision_making`, select_one, required): Rate your decision-making abilities.
- **Problem Solving** (`problem_solving`, select_one, required): Rate your problem-solving abilities.
- **Team Development** (`team_development`, select_one, required): Rate your team's growth and development.
- **Performance Management** (`performance_management`, select_one, required): Rate your performance management skills.
- **Goal Achievement** (`goal_achievement`, select_one, required): Rate your goal completion over the past year.
- **Key Accomplishments** (`key_accomplishments`, text, required): Provide a list of major accomplishments over the past year.
- **Challenges Faced** (`challenges_faced`, text, required): Describe any significant challenges you faced over the past year.
- **How Challenges Were Overcome** (`challenges_overcome`, text, not required): Provide a detailed explanation of how you overcame challenges.
- **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, required): Identify areas where you need to improve.
- **Professional Development** (`professional_development`, text, not required): List any relevant training or courses you've completed to enhance your skills.
- **Skills Developed** (`skills_developed`, text, not required): Describe any new skills you've gained over the past year.
- **Goals for Next Period** (`goals_next_period`, text, required): Set objectives for the next review period.
- **Team Feedback Summary** (`team_feedback_summary`, text, not required): Gather feedback from your team members and summarize their key points.
- **Resources Needed** (`resources_needed`, text, not required): Identify any additional resources you need to support your role.
- **Support from Organization** (`support_from_organization`, select_one, not required): Rate the level of support you receive from the organization.
- **Work-Life Balance** (`work_life_balance`, select_one, not required): Rate your work-life balance over the past year.
- **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall job satisfaction.
- **Career Aspirations** (`career_aspirations`, text, not required): Describe your future career goals.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, not required): Provide any additional information or comments about your self-evaluation.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your answers.
- Use specific examples and evidence to support your ratings and claims.
- Proofread your answers for spelling and grammar errors.
- Attach any supporting documents or evidence to your answers if possible.
