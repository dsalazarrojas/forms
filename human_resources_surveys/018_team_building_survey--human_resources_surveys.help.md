# Team Building Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Building Survey is a tool designed to collect information about team dynamics, communication, leadership, and collaboration within an organization. This form aims to gather honest and objective feedback from team members to help identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the most suitable options from the provided lists for each question.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by a '*').
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Building** (1, text, optional): This is the title of the form, which you can use to briefly describe the team you're working with.
* **Team Dynamics** (2, text, optional): Describe the dynamics within your team, including any strengths and weaknesses.
* **Communication** (3, text, optional): Share your thoughts on how communication works within your team, including any successes or challenges you've faced.
* **Team Leadership** (4, text, optional): Comment on the leadership style and effectiveness of your team's leaders.
* **Collaboration** (5, text, optional): Discuss how collaboration works within your team, including any successes or challenges you've faced.
* **Team Satisfaction** (6, select_one, optional): Select your level of satisfaction with the team, using the provided options (Satisfied or Dissatisfied).
* **Team Satisfaction Answers** (7, text, optional): If you selected 'Dissatisfied', provide more details on why you felt that way.
* **Team Satisfaction Scale** (8, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the team, on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Communication Channels** (9, select_multiple, optional): Select the communication channels you use to interact with your team (e.g., email, Slack, phone, etc.).
* **Team Size** (10, number, optional): Provide your team's size.
* **Team Size Hint** (11, text, optional): If you're unsure about your team size, provide more information.
* **Communication Frequency** (12, select_one, optional): Select how often you communicate with your team, using the provided options (e.g., daily, weekly, etc.).
* **Collaboration Frequency** (13, select_one, optional): Select how often you collaborate with your team, using the provided options (e.g., frequently, occasionally, etc.).
* **Collaboration Frequency Hint** (14, text, optional): If you're unsure about your collaboration frequency, provide more information.
* **Collaboration Channels** (15, select_multiple, optional): Select the channels you use for collaboration (e.g., team meetings, Slack channels, etc.).
* **Collaboration Channels Hint** (16, text, optional): If you're unsure about your collaboration channels, provide more information.
* **Feedback** (17, text, optional): Share any feedback you have about your team or the team building process.
* **Feedback Scale** (18, number, optional): Rate your willingness to provide feedback, on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Feedback Frequency** (19, select_one, optional): Select how often you provide feedback, using the provided options (e.g., frequently, occasionally, etc.).
* **Feedback Frequency Hint** (20, text, optional): If you're unsure about your feedback frequency, provide more information.
* **Team Meetings** (21, number, optional): Provide the number of meetings your team has per week.
* **Team Meetings Hint** (22, text, optional): If you're unsure about the number of meetings, provide more information.
* **Meeting Length** (23, select_one, optional): Select the average length of your team meetings, using the provided options (e.g., 30 minutes, 1 hour, etc.).
* **Meeting Length Hint** (24, text, optional): If you're unsure about the meeting length, provide more information.
* **Meeting Frequency** (25, select_one, optional): Select how often your team meets, using the provided options (e.g., daily, weekly, etc.).
