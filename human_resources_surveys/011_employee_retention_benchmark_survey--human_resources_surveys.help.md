# Employee Retention Benchmark Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information on employee retention and engagement metrics to help organizations understand the health of their workforce and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select your current job title from the list provided.
2. Choose how satisfied you are with your current job.
3. Choose your estimated turnover rate.
4. Select how satisfied you are with your current job level of satisfaction.
5. Select the main reasons for potential turnover that apply to you.
6. Choose your level of employee engagement.
7. Select the effectiveness of our retention programs.
8. If you wish to provide additional comments, you can enter them in the next steps field.
9. If you want, you can also enter the submission date and time.
10. If you wish, you can enter your employee name and department.
11. Enter your number of years of service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Current Job Title** (`current_job_title`, Select One): Choose your current job title from the list provided.

*   **How Satisfied Are You with Your Current Job?** (`job_satisfaction`, Select One): Choose how satisfied you are with your current job.
    *   Very Satisfied
    *   Somewhat Satisfied
    *   Neutral
    *   Somewhat Dissatisfied
*   **Estimated Turnover Rate** (`turnover_rate`, Select One): Choose your estimated turnover rate.
    *   Low
    *   Medium
    *   High
    *   Very High
*   **Job Satisfaction Level** (`job_satisfaction_level`, Select One): Choose how satisfied you are with your current job level of satisfaction.
    *   Highly Satisfied
    *   Somewhat Satisfied
    *   Neutral
    *   Somewhat Dissatisfied
*   **Main Reasons for Potential Turnover** (`turnover_reasons`, Select Multiple): Select all the main reasons for potential turnover that apply to you.
    *   Career Growth Opportunities
    *   Salary or Benefits
    *   Job Security
    *   Other
*   **Employee Engagement Level** (`employee_engagement`, Select One): Choose your level of employee engagement.
    *   Highly Engaged
    *   Somewhat Engaged
    *   Neutral
    *   Somewhat Disengaged
*   **Retention Programs** (`retention_programs`, Select One): Select the effectiveness of our retention programs.
    *   Very Effective
    *   Somewhat Effective
    *   Not Very Effective
    *   Not at All Effective
*   **Next Steps for Improving Retention** (`next_steps`, Note): If you wish to provide additional comments, you can enter them here.
*   **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, Date): If you want, you can enter the submission date.
*   **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, Time): If you want, you can enter the submission time.
*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, Text): If you want, you can enter your employee name.
*   **Department** (`department`, Select Multiple): Select your department from the list provided.
    *   Operations
    *   Management
    *   Human Resources
*   **Years of Service** (`years_of_service`, Number): Enter your number of years of service.
