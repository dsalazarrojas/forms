# HR Investigation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Investigation Form is a tool used by the HR department to gather information and track the progress of investigations related to employees. This form helps to ensure that all necessary details are captured, and the investigation is conducted efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter a detailed description of the event or incident that needs to be investigated in the `Event Description` field (Field 1).
2.  Fill in the name and email of the employee involved in the investigation (Fields 2 and 3).
3.  Select the type of investigation required from the list provided (Field 4).
4.  Enter the date of the event (Field 5).
5.  Choose the current status of the investigation from the list provided (Field 6).
6.  Enter any additional notes or comments about the investigation (Field 7).
7.  Select the person assigned to handle the investigation (Field 8).
8.  Enter the date the investigation was assigned (Field 9).
9.  Set a due date for the completion of the investigation (Field 10).
10.  Record the date the investigation is complete (Field 11).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the event or incident that needs to be investigated.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee involved in the investigation.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the employee involved in the investigation.
* **Investigation Type** (`investigation_type`, select_one, not required): Select the type of investigation required from the options provided.
	+ **Discriminatory**: Investigation related to discriminatory practices or actions.
	+ **Illegal**: Investigation related to illegal activities or behaviors.
	+ **Harassment**: Investigation related to harassment or hostile work environment.
	+ **Other**: Investigation related to any other type not specified above.
* **Date of Event** (`date_of_event`, date, required): Enter the date of the event or incident being investigated.
* **Investigation Status** (`investigation_status`, select_one, not required): Select the current status of the investigation from the options provided.
	+ **In Progress**: Investigation is ongoing.
	+ **Complete**: Investigation is complete.
	+ **Pending**: Investigation is pending or not started.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments about the investigation.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, not required): Select the person assigned to handle the investigation from the options provided.
	+ **John**
	+ **Jane**
	+ **Jim**
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, date, required): Enter the date the investigation was assigned.
* **Due Date** (`due_date`, date, required): Set a due date for the completion of the investigation.
* **Investigation Complete Date** (`investigation_complete_date`, date, required): Record the date the investigation is complete.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete the form accurately and in its entirety to ensure that the investigation is conducted efficiently and effectively.
- Ensure that all necessary details are captured and the investigation is thorough.
- If you are unsure about any field, please consult with HR department or the person assigned to handle the investigation.
- If you need to make any changes to the form, please use the provided contact information to reach out to the form creators.
