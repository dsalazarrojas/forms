# Weekly Status Report Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used by employees to submit their weekly status report, providing an update on their work progress, challenges, and next steps to their manager.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the start and end dates of the week you are reporting on in the `Week Start` and `Week End` fields, respectively.
2. Enter your `Employee ID`, which is likely a unique identifier assigned to you by your HR department or manager.
3. Provide your `Employee Name` and `Project` details.
4. Enter the location where you worked on the project, if applicable, in the `Project Location` field.
5. In the `Work Summary` field, write a brief summary of what you accomplished during the week.
6. If you faced any challenges, describe them in the `Challenges` field.
7. In the `Challenges Details` field, provide more information about the challenges you faced.
8. List any challenges you anticipate for the next week in the `Work Plan` field.
9. Enter your current `Week Status` as a number (e.g., 50, 75, 100, etc.) and any additional details in the `Week Status Details` field.
10. In the `Action Items` field, list any tasks or actions you need to take in the next week.
11. In the `Action Items Details` field, provide more information about the action items you listed.
12. Choose whether you have completed any milestones, and if so, provide details in the `Completed Milestone Details` field.
13. Add any attachments related to your weekly report in the `Attachments` field.
14. Enter your email address and phone number if you want to be contacted by your manager.
15. Enter your manager's name and any additional details in the `Manager` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Week Start**: The start date of the week you are reporting on.
* **Week End**: The end date of the week you are reporting on.
* **Employee ID**: Your unique identifier assigned by your HR department or manager.
* **Employee Name**: Your name.
* **Project**: The name of the project you are working on.
* **Project Location**: The location where you worked on the project, if applicable.
* **Work Summary**: A brief summary of what you accomplished during the week.
* **Challenges**: Any challenges you faced during the week.
* **Challenges Details**: More information about the challenges you faced.
* **Work Plan**: Any challenges you anticipate for the next week.
* **Week Status**: Your current week status as a number (e.g., 50, 75, 100, etc.).
* **Week Status Details**: Additional details about your current week status.
* **Action Items**: Any tasks or actions you need to take in the next week.
* **Action Items Details**: More information about the action items you listed.
* **Completed Milestone**: Whether you have completed any milestones.
* **Completed Milestone Details**: Details about the completed milestones.
* **Attachments**: Any attachments related to your weekly report.
* **Email**: Your email address for your manager to contact you.
* **Phone**: Your phone number for your manager to contact you.
* **Manager**: Your manager's name and any additional details.
* **Submit**: Confirm that you have completed the form and submit it.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate information to ensure timely and effective reporting.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* Attach any relevant documents or files to support your report.
* Review and proofread your report carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your manager or HR department.
