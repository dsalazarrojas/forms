# Internal Communications Transparency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Internal Communications Transparency Survey is designed to gather your opinions and experiences about internal communications within our organization. Your feedback will help us understand the effectiveness and openness of internal communications, identifying areas for improvement, and making informed decisions to enhance the workplace environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. You may select only one option for each question, even if multiple options apply.
3. Please provide feedback on the questions that best reflect your personal experiences.
4. If you have any suggestions or ideas, feel free to share them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Employee Feedback (employee_feedback)
* **Employee feedback** (`employee_feedback`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide your thoughts, opinions, or suggestions on internal communications. Share your ideas on what works and what doesn't.
### 2. Communication channels (communication_channels)
* **Communication channels** (`communication_channels`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate which channels you use to receive internal communications (e.g., Email, Phone, Online platform).
### 3. How often do you receive internal communications? (feedback_frequency)
* **How often do you receive internal communications?** (`feedback_frequency`, `select_one`, required/optional): Answer this question based on your actual experiences (e.g., Every day, A few times a week, A few times a month, Less than once a month, Never).
### 4. How effective are internal communications? (communication_effectiveness)
* **How effective are internal communications?** (`communication_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required/optional): Evaluate the effectiveness of internal communications (e.g., Very effective, Somewhat effective, Not very effective, Not at all effective).
### 5. How much trust do you have within your team? (trust_within_team)
