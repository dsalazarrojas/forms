# Orientation Completion Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Orientation Completion Checklist is a form used to track employee orientation completion status. It's a key tool for Human Resources to ensure that all employees are properly onboarded and up-to-date on company policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and fill out all required fields. Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information for each section.
2. Fill in the general information section with the employee's name and other relevant details.
3. Enter the employee's ID number in the Employee ID field.
4. Enter the employee's date of birth.
5. Select the employee's department from the list.
6. Choose the employee's orientation status (New Hire, Orientation Complete, or Not Complete).
7. Enter the date the employee completed the orientation.
8. Choose the completion status (Complete or Incomplete).
9. Fill in the supervisor's name.
10. Enter the supervisor's phone number.
11. Enter the supervisor's email address.
12. The employee and supervisor are both required to sign the form.
13. Finally, enter the date the form was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (Required): This field is for entering the employee's name and other general information.
* **Employee ID** (Required): Enter the employee's ID number.
* **Date of Birth** (Required): Enter the employee's date of birth.
* **Department** (Required): Select the employee's department from the list.
* **Orientation Status** (Required): Choose the employee's orientation status (New Hire, Orientation Complete, or Not Complete).
* **Date Completed** (Required): Enter the date the employee completed the orientation.
* **Completion Status** (Required): Choose the completion status (Complete or Incomplete).
* **Supervisor Name** (Required): Enter the supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Phone** (Required): Enter the supervisor's phone number.
* **Supervisor Email** (Required): Enter the supervisor's email address.
* **Employee Signature** (Required): The employee must sign the form to confirm completion.
* **Supervisor Signature** (Required): The supervisor must sign the form to confirm completion.
* **Date Submitted** (Required): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (Required): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check the form before submitting it to avoid errors.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the relevant section of this guide for clarification.
