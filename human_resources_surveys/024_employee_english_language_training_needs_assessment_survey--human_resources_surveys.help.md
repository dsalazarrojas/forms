# Employee English Language Training Needs Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee English Language Training Needs Assessment Survey is a tool used by Human Resources (HR) and training teams to collect data on employees' English language training needs. It helps prioritize learning goals and reviews each form submission in Jotform.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please read the form carefully before completing it.
- Answer all the questions accurately and truthfully.
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- If you are unsure about any question, contact your HR representative for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Human Resources Surveys** (`category`, `text`, **required**): Select the category "Human Resources Surveys" from the dropdown list to categorize this form submission.
* **Employee English Language Training Needs Assessment Survey supports HR** (`description`, `note`, **false**): This is a note field that provides more information about the form. Please read it carefully, but do not need to fill this field in.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, `number`, **required**): Enter a unique number to identify this form submission.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose one of the options from the dropdown list to select the tool assigned to you for English language training. Options are "chatjimmy" and "Other".
