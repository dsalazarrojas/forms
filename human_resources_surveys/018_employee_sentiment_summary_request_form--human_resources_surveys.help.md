# Employee Sentiment Summary Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from employees and managers about the sentiment and performance of their team members. It will help the HR department to track and analyze employee performance and sentiment data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions and authorization to complete this form.
2. Review the form to understand the fields and their purposes.
3. Complete each field according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID (1)** (text, optional): Enter the ID of the employee you are reporting on.
* **Manager ID (2)** (text, optional): Enter the ID of the manager of the employee you are reporting on.
* **Email (3)** (email, optional): Enter the email of the employee you are reporting on.
* **Sentiment Summary (4)** (text, optional): Provide a brief description of the employee's current sentiment and performance.
* **Request Date (5)** (date, optional): Enter the date the sentiment summary was requested.
* **Reporting Date (6)** (date, optional): Enter the date the sentiment summary was reported.
* **Submitted By (7)** (select_one, optional): Choose the person submitting this form (HR, Manager, or Other).
* **Submitted By (Text) (8)** (text, optional): Enter the name or title of the person submitting this form (if Other is selected).
