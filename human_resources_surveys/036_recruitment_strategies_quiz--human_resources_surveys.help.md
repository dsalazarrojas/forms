# recruitment_strategies_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on recruitment strategies and metrics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by reading the introduction page.
2. Select all relevant options for "assessment" that apply to your recruitment process.
3. Choose the relevant options for "Training" that align with your current practices.
4. Enter the relevant metrics for "metrics" related to your recruitment process.
5. Select the department for "department" that corresponds to your area of responsibility.
6. Enter the number of recruiters with "recruiter_experience" related to the number of recruiters in your team.
7. Select all relevant options for "insights" that apply to your recruitment process.
8. Enter the date and time you wish to submit the form with "submit_date" and "submit_time" fields.
9. Enter your form ID, Department ID, Recruiter ID, and Submitter ID with "form_id", "department_id", "recruiter_id", and "submitter_id" fields.
10. Enter your email and phone number with "submitter_email" and "submitter_phone" fields.
11. Select "submit" to submit the form.
12. Optionally, enter any comments with "comments" and "submission_comment" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required): This page provides the background information about the form's purpose.
- **assessment** (`assessment`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all relevant options that apply to your recruitment assessment process.
	* If you have a thorough assessment process, select "Yes".
	* If you don't have a assessment process, select "No".
- **Training** (`training`, `select_one`, required): Choose the relevant option that best describes your training process.
	* If you regularly provide training, select "Yes".
	* If you don't regularly provide training, select "No".
- **metrics** (`metrics`, `number`, required): Enter the number of metrics related to your recruitment process.
- **department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Select the department that corresponds to your area of responsibility.
	* If you're in HR, select "Yes".
	* If you're not in HR, select "No".
- **recruiter_experience** (`recruiter_experience`, `number`, required): Enter the number of recruiters with experience related to your team.
- **insights** (`insights`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all relevant options that apply to your recruitment process.
	* If you have insights into your recruitment process, select "Yes".
	* If you don't have insights, select "No".
- **tracking** (`tracking`, `date`, required): Enter the date for tracking purposes.
- **comments** (`comments`, `text`, required): Optionally, enter any comments related to your recruitment process.
- **email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email for contact purposes.
- **phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **submit** (`submit`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to submit the form.
- **submitter** (`submitter`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the submitter.
- **submit_date** (`submit_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you're submitting the form.
- **submit_time** (`submit_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you're submitting the form.
- **form_id** (`form_id`, `number`, required): Enter your unique form ID.
- **department_id** (`department_id`, `number`, required): Enter your department ID.
- **recruiter_id** (`recruiter_id`, `number`, required): Enter your recruiter ID.
- **submitter_id** (`submitter_id`, `number`, required): Enter your submitter ID.
- **submitter_email** (`submitter_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email.
- **submitter_phone** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
- **submission_date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of submission.
- **submission_time** (`submission_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of submission.
- **submission_comment** (`submission_comment`, `text`, required): Optionally, enter any comments on submission.

## Tips
- Please ensure you're completing the form accurately and honestly.
- Review the form before submission to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact your supervisor or HR department.
