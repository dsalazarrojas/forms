# Business Culture Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge employee perceptions of the company culture, including their alignment with organizational values, trust in leadership, communication, collaboration, innovation, and work environment. The survey's findings will help organizations identify areas of strength and weakness, enabling informed decision-making to foster a positive company culture.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions based on your personal experiences and opinions.
2. For select-one questions, choose the answer that best reflects your agreement or confidence level (e.g., Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree).
3. For text fields, provide a clear and concise description of your thoughts and suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID (optional)**: Enter your employee ID, if applicable. This is an optional field and does not affect your survey responses.
* **Department**: This field is used to determine which department you belong to.
* **Position Level**: Choose your role in the organization from the provided options (Entry Level, Mid-Level, Supervisor, Manager, Director, Executive).
* **Tenure**: Select how long you have worked here, approximately (Less than 1 year, 1-2 years, 3-5 years, 6-10 years, More than 10 years).
* **Our company values align with my personal values**: Rate your agreement with the company's core values (Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree).
* **I understand the company's core values**: Assess how well you comprehend the company's values (Not at all, Slightly, Moderately, Very, Extremely).
* **I trust the leadership of this organization**: Express your confidence in the organization's leadership (Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree).
* **My manager supports my professional growth**: Assess your manager's support for your career development (Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree).
* **Open and honest communication is encouraged here**: Rate the level of open communication within the organization (Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree).
* **Management is receptive to employee feedback**: Evaluate the organization's receptivity to feedback (Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree).
* **Our teams work well together**: Rate the teamwork and collaboration within the organization (Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree).
* **I feel supported by my colleagues**: Assess the level of support from colleagues (Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree).
* **New ideas and innovation are encouraged**: Rate the level of innovation and new ideas within the organization (Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree).
* **Opportunities for career growth are available**: Evaluate the availability of growth opportunities (Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree).
* **This company supports work-life balance**: Assess the level of support for work-life balance (Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree).
* **Diversity and inclusion are valued here**: Rate the organization's value on diversity and inclusion (Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree).
* **What could improve our company culture?**: Use this text field to provide suggestions and ideas for improving the company culture.

## Tips

* Be honest and sincere in your responses.
* Take your time to reflect on your experiences and opinions before answering each question.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact your HR department or the form administrators.
