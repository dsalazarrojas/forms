# Supervisor Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supervisor Survey form is designed to gather information from supervisors about their surveying practices, including frequency and content of their surveys.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Indicate whether you administer manager and employee surveys, and specify how often.
3. Describe the questions you include in your manager survey, if applicable.
4. Provide any comments about your manager survey, if applicable.
5. Specify the schedule for your survey event, including the date and time.
6. Indicate whether your survey schedule repeats, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supervisor Survey Information** (`supervisor_survey_info`, note, optional): This field is for any additional information you'd like to share about your surveying practices.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as the supervisor completing this form.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for any follow-up communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted directly.
* **Manager Survey Frequency** (`manager_survey_frequency`, select_one, optional): Indicate how often you administer a manager survey.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you administer a manager survey.
	+ Select 'No' if you do not administer a manager survey.
* **Employee Survey Frequency** (`employee_survey_frequency`, select_one, optional): Indicate how often you administer an employee survey.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you administer an employee survey.
	+ Select 'No' if you do not administer an employee survey.
* **Manager Survey Questions** (`manager_survey_questions`, text, optional): If you administer a manager survey, please describe the questions you include.
* **Manager Survey Comments** (`manager_survey_comments`, text, optional): If you have any comments about your manager survey, please share them.
* **Employee Survey Comments** (`employee_survey_comments`, text, optional): If you have any comments about your employee survey, please share them.
* **Schedule Event** (`schedule_event`, date, optional): Specify the date for your survey event.
* **Schedule Time** (`schedule_time`, time, optional): Specify the time for your survey event.
* **Schedule Duration** (`schedule_duration`, number, optional): Specify the duration of your survey event.
* **Schedule Repeat** (`schedule_repeat`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether your survey schedule repeats.
	+ Select 'Yes' if your survey schedule repeats.
	+ Select 'No' if your survey schedule does not repeat.
