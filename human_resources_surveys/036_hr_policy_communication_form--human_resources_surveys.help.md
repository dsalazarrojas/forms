## hr_policy_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Policy Communication Form is a tool used to gather information from employees regarding their understanding and acknowledgment of company policies. This form is essential for HR to track policy communications and ensure employees' knowledge and understanding of company policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, as it will be used for communication purposes.
3. Indicate your department in the "Department" field.
4. For the "Policy Acknowledgments" field, select all that apply to indicate which company policies you have acknowledged.
5. Enter the "Date of Submission" field to mark the current date of submission.
6. Enter the "Date of Review" field to mark the date of review by your manager.
7. Select the "Time of Submission" field to mark the time of submission.
8. In the "Notes" field, provide any additional comments or information related to the submission.
9. Indicate your acknowledgment of employee policies in the "Employee Acknowledgment" field.
10. Indicate your manager's acknowledgment in the "Manager Acknowledgment" field.
11. Indicate the department head's acknowledgment in the "Department Head Acknowledgment" field.
12. Finally, indicate acknowledgment of other employees' acknowledgment in the "Other Employees Acknowledgment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your company records.
* **Email**: Enter your company email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Department**: Select your department from the list of options.
* **Policy Acknowledgments**: Select all policies that you have acknowledged, e.g., "Yes" for each policy you have read and understood.
* **Date of Submission**: Enter the current date in the format of "YYYY-MM-DD" (e.g., 2022-07-25).
* **Date of Review**: Enter the date when your manager reviews this form.
* **Time of Submission**: Select the time of submission from the options.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or information related to the submission.
* **Employee Acknowledgment**: Indicate whether you have acknowledged company policies.
* **Manager Acknowledgment**: Indicate whether your manager has acknowledged company policies.
* **Department Head Acknowledgment**: Indicate whether the department head has acknowledged company policies.
* **Other Employees Acknowledgment**: Indicate whether other employees have acknowledged company policies.
