# Organizational Clarity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organizational Clarity Survey is designed to gather feedback and information about the level of clarity within an organization. This form is meant to be filled out by team members to provide insight into how clear their roles and communication within their teams are.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below for each page.

### Page 1: Organizational Clarity Survey

* **Organizational Clarity Survey** (select_multiple): Please select all that apply to describe your experience with the clarity of your current team. Options: Yes, No
* **What is your role in the team?** (select_one): Please select how clear your role in the team is. Options: Very clear, Somewhat clear, Somewhat unclear, Not clear at all
* **How clear is your role?** (select_one): Please select how clear your role is. Options: Very clear, Somewhat clear, Somewhat unclear, Not clear at all
* **How clear is the communication from your manager?** (select_one): Please select how clear the communication from your manager is. Options: Very clear, Somewhat clear, Somewhat unclear, Not clear at all
* **What is your feedback on the current communication style?** (note): Please provide any additional feedback you have about the communication style in this team.
* **How often do you receive clear communication about your role?** (select_one): Please select how often you receive clear communication about your role. Options: Very frequently, Somewhat frequently, Somewhat infrequently, Never
* **How often do you give feedback to your team about your role?** (select_one): Please select how often you give feedback to your team about your role. Options: Very frequently, Somewhat frequently, Somewhat infrequently, Never
* **What communication channels do you prefer?** (select_multiple): Please select all that apply to describe the communication channels you prefer. Options: Yes, No
* **What communication channels do you use?** (select_multiple): Please select all that apply to describe the communication channels you use. Options: Yes, No
