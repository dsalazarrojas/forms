# Employee Of The Month Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Employee Of The Month" recognition program aims to acknowledge and reward employees who have demonstrated exceptional performance, teamwork, or dedication to their role and the company. This form is used to collect nominations from employees who want to recognize their peers' outstanding contributions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and honest information.
2. Make sure to provide the correct employee ID for the nominee.
3. Enter the correct nomination period for the period when the employee's outstanding performance took place.
4. Provide a clear and concise reason for the nomination.
5. Enter the date you're nominating the employee for this recognition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Form**:
	+ <nomination_form>: This is a free-text field where you can provide any additional comments or information that support your nomination.
* **Employee Name**:
	+ <employee_name>: Enter the name of the employee you're nominating. This is typically the employee's full name as it appears on their ID badge or personnel records.
* **Employee ID**:
	+ <employee_id>: Enter the unique employee ID assigned to the nominee. This helps us verify their employment status and performance records.
* **Manager Name**:
	+ <manager_name>: Enter the name of the employee's manager or supervisor who can vouch for their performance.
* **Nominator Name**:
	+ <nominator_name>: Enter your name and title/position to identify who's making the nomination.
* **Nomination Period**:
	+ <nomination_period>: Select the date range when the nominee's outstanding performance took place. This helps us evaluate their performance during a specific period.
* **Nomination Reason**:
	+ <nomination_reason>: Provide a clear and concise reason for nominating the employee. This should highlight their outstanding contributions, achievements, or behaviors that demonstrate their value to the team.
* **Nomination Date**:
	+ <nomination_date>: Enter the date you're submitting this nomination. This helps us track the nomination process and ensure timely processing.

## Tips

* Make sure to keep your nomination concise and focused on the nominee's achievements and contributions.
* Avoid repeating information that can be easily verified through our HR records.
* If you're unsure about any information or have questions, please contact your HR department for guidance.
