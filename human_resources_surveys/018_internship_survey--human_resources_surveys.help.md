# Internship Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internship Survey form is designed to gather information from interns about their experience at our company. This form helps us understand their opinions, suggestions, and feedback about their internship, which will aid us in making improvements to our internship program. The survey is optional and will only take a few minutes to complete.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Internship Survey, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the questions honestly and briefly.
2. Make sure to fill in all relevant fields, but you can skip any optional fields you're not comfortable sharing information about.
3. Be as specific as possible when answering the open-ended questions.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Internship Survey Form** (`Internship Survey Form`, text, not required)
Please answer a brief description of your experience at our company.

*   2. **What do you like** (`What do you like`, text, not required)
Please answer a brief description of what you liked about your internship.

*   3. **Rate Employers** (`Rate Employers`, select_one, not required)
Please rate your experience working with your supervisors and colleagues on a scale from 1 to 5.

*   4. **Comments** (`Comments`, text, not required)
Please provide any comments or feedback you have about your internship experience.

*   5. **Would Recommend** (`Would Recommend`, select_one, not required)
Please answer whether you would recommend our company to other job-seekers.

*   6. **Time of Internship** (`Time of Internship`, time, not required)
Please provide the dates you participated in the internship.

*   7. **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, text, not required)
Please answer any contact information you'd like to share with us.

*   8. **Name** (`Name`, text, not required)
Please answer your full name.

*   9. **Email** (`Email`, email, not required)
Please answer your email address.

*   10. **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, not required)
Please answer your phone number.

*   11. **Company** (`Company`, text, not required)
Please answer the name of your company.

*   12. **Department** (`Department`, text, not required)
Please answer the name of your department.

*   13. **Position** (`Position`, text, not required)
Please answer your job title.

*   14. **Supervisor** (`Supervisor`, text, not required)
Please answer your supervisor's name.

*   15. **Supervisor Email** (`Supervisor Email`, email, not required)
Please answer your supervisor's email address.

*   16. **Supervisor Phone Number** (`Supervisor Phone Number`, text, not required)
Please answer your supervisor's phone number.

*   17. **Job Title** (`Job Title`, text, not required)
Please answer your job title.

*   18. **Start Date** (`Start Date`, date, not required)
Please answer the start date of your internship.

*   19. **End Date** (`End Date`, date, not required)
Please answer the end date of your internship.

*   20. **Supervisor Start Date** (`Supervisor Start Date`, date, not required)
Please answer your supervisor's start date.

*   21. **Supervisor End Date** (`Supervisor End Date`, date, not required)
Please answer your supervisor's end date.

*   22. **Supervisor Hours** (`Supervisor Hours`, text, not required)
Please answer the number of hours you worked with your supervisor.

*   23. **Supervisor Hours Range** (`Supervisor Hours Range`, text, not required)
Please answer the range of hours you worked with your supervisor.

*   24. **Supervisor Hours Range Unit** (`Supervisor Hours Range Unit`, text, not required)
Please answer the unit of the supervisor's working hours.

*   25. **Notes** (`Notes`, note, not required)
Please use this field to provide any additional comments or feedback.
