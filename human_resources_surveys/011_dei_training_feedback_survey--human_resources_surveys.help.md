# DEI Training Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The DEI Training Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback from trainees on the effectiveness and satisfaction of Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) training sessions. This feedback will help the training organizers understand what worked well and what can be improved in future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each page carefully to ensure that you are providing accurate and relevant feedback.
2.  Select the options that best fit your experience and opinions.
3.  Make sure to enter your contact information accurately.
4.  Use the navigation buttons to move between pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction (Page 1)
*   **Introduction**: This page serves as an introduction to the survey and provides a general overview of the feedback questions.

### Training Effectiveness (Page 2)
*   **How effective was the training session?**: Please select "Yes" if you found the session to be effective, and "No" if you did not.
*   **Reasons for your selection:** (optional) If you answered "No", please provide a brief explanation of why you did not find the session effective.

### Session Satisfaction (Page 3)
*   **On a scale of 1 to 5, how satisfied are you with this training session?**: Rate your satisfaction with the session on a scale of 1 to 5.

### Feedback Comments (Page 4)
*   **Do you have any comments or suggestions?**: If you have any comments or suggestions for improvement, please enter them here.

### Future Sessions (Page 5)
*   **Would you like to attend another session?**: Select "Yes, I would like to attend another session." if you are interested in attending another session, and "No, I am not interested." if you are not interested.

### Contact Information (Page 6)
*   **Is this your contact information correct?**: Select "True" if your contact information is accurate, and "False" if it is not.
*   **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your business card or email signature.
*   **Department**: Enter your department or team name.
*   **Job title**: Enter your job title.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address.

Note: The contact information page is optional and only needs to be completed if you are interested in future sessions or need to receive follow-up communication.
