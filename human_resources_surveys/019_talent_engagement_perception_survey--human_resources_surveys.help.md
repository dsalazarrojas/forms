# Talent Engagement Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This talent engagement perception survey is designed to gather feedback from employees and their managers on the employee's engagement with their job and the organization. The survey aims to capture both positive and negative sentiments, as well as gather feedback on the employee's role and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page and select all the options that apply to you.
2. For the sentiment scale, select a number that corresponds to your current sentiment.
3. In the employee feedback and manager feedback fields, provide a brief description of your thoughts.
4. Select your department and job title from the provided options.
5. Finally, review your selections to ensure you've answered all the questions accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Survey** (`employee_survey`, `select_multiple`): Select all the options that apply to your experience as an employee. This includes feeling engaged, motivated, or having a positive sentiment towards your role.
* **Manager Survey** (`manager_survey`, `select_multiple`): Select all the options that apply to your experience with your manager. This includes having a positive or negative sentiment towards your manager's leadership and support.
* **Sentiment Scale** (`sentiment_scale`, `number`): Select a number from 1 to 10 that corresponds to your current sentiment towards your role. A higher number indicates a more positive sentiment.
* **Engagement Level** (`engagement_level`, `number`): Select a number from 1 to 10 that corresponds to your current engagement level with your job. A higher number indicates a higher level of engagement.
* **Employee Feedback** (`employee_feedback`, `text`): Provide a brief description of your thoughts on your experience as an employee.
* **Manager Feedback** (`manager_feedback`, `text`): Provide a brief description of your thoughts on your experience with your manager.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`): Select the department you belong to from the provided options.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`): Enter your job title.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, `text`): Enter a brief description of your job function.
* **Job Classification** (`job_classification`, `text`): Enter a brief description of your job classification.
