# Employee Benefits Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather data on employee satisfaction with the benefits package offered by your organization.

## How To Complete This Form

Please answer the questions honestly, and make sure to submit the form only once. 

1. The **Introduction** field is a free text input where you can provide any general feedback or comments about the benefits package.
2. The **Benefits Package** field is a text field where you can describe the benefits package offered by your organization. 
3. The **Benefits Satisfaction Level** field is a select_one field where you can rate your level of satisfaction with the benefits package. You can choose from: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied. 
4. The **Job Security** field is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback related to job security. 
5. The **Benefits Value** field is a numerical input where you can rate on a scale of 0-100 the value of the benefits package. 
6. The **What Matters Most** field is a select_multiple field where you can choose up to 3 options that you feel are the most important aspects of the benefits package. You can choose from: Financial stability, Personal well-being, Job satisfaction, Work-life balance, Job security. 
7. The **Feedback** field is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions about the benefits package. 
8. The **Contact** field is a email field where you can enter your email address. 
9. The **Phone Number** field is a text field where you can enter your phone number. 
10. The **Comments** field is a text area where you can provide any additional comments or feedback. 
11. The **Assigned To** field is a select_one field where you can choose who this form has been assigned to. You can choose from: chatjimmy or someone else. 
12. The **Assigned To Other** field is a text field where you can enter the name of someone else to whom this form has been assigned. 

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly to provide accurate feedback. 
* Make sure to submit the form only once. 
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please don't hesitate to ask someone for clarification.
