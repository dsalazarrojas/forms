The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and information about fans' engagement with our organization from various perspectives: how they engage with our brand (Fan Engagement), what kind of fans we have (Fan Passion), how many fans we have (Fan Fans), and how we can reward them (Fan Fees and Loyalty). This form will help us better understand our fans' behavior and preferences, which will guide us in making informed decisions to improve our fan engagement efforts.

This form is divided into several sections, each with a different type of question. To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select one of the options for "Fan Engagement".
2. If required, select one or more options for "Fan Fans" and "Fan Fees and Loyalty".
3. Enter a valid email address in the "Fan Email" field.
4. Optionally, you can provide any additional information or comments in the "Additional Comments" field.
5. Finally, you can add any notes about fan loyalty in the "Fan Loyalty Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fan Engagement** (Fan Engagement, select_one, required): This question asks about how you engage with our brand, such as being a fan, having passion, or being loyal.
* **Fan Fans** (Fan Fans, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to select how many fans you have, which can be Fans, Enthusiasts, or Supporters.
* **Fan Fees and Loyalty** (Fan Fees and Loyalty, number, required): This question asks you to report how much you pay or contribute to our organization as a fan.
* **Fan Email** (Fan Email, email, required): This question asks you to enter your email address, which we will use to contact you.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text, optional): This field is for any additional comments or information you would like to share.
* **Fan Loyalty Notes** (Fan Loyalty Notes, note, optional): This field is for any additional notes about fan loyalty you would like to provide.
