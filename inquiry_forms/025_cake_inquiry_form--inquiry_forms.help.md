# Cake Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cake Inquiry Form is designed to collect information from potential customers interested in purchasing a custom cake for an event or occasion.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your information and preferences.
2. Make sure to include all required fields.
3. If you have any additional requests or questions, use the "Additional Requests" field to provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter the best email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Inquiry Date** (`inquiry_date`, date, required): Enter today's date.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of event (e.g. Birthday, Wedding, Anniversary, etc.).
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, optional): Approximately how many guests will be attending the event.
* **Cake Type of Interest** (`cake_type_interest`, select_one, required): Select the type of cake you are interested in (e.g. Layer cake, Sheet cake, Cupcakes, etc.).
* **Preferred Flavors** (`preferred_flavors`, select_multiple, optional): Select the flavors you are interested in.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, optional): Select your approximate budget for the cake.
* **Design Ideas** (`design_ideas`, text, optional): Describe your vision or theme for the cake.
* **Color Preferences** (`color_preferences`, text, optional): Enter your preferred colors for the cake design.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, optional): Check any dietary restrictions (e.g. Gluten-free, Dairy-free, etc.).
* **Inspiration Photos** (`inspiration_photos`, select_one, optional): If you have photos to share, select the option that best describes how you would like to share them.
* **Consultation Preference** (`consultation_preference`, select_one, optional): Select how you would like to consult with us (e.g. In-person meeting, Phone call, etc.).
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, optional): Where did you find us (e.g. Website, Social media, etc.).
* **Timeline Flexibility** (`timeline_flexibility`, select_one, optional): Select how flexible you are with your event date.
* **Additional Requests** (`additional_requests`, text, optional): Enter any other questions or special requests you may have.
