# Housing Support Services Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information for the Housing Support Services, to provide assistance to individuals or families in need of housing support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact details in the "Contact Details" section.
2. Describe your housing needs in the "Housing Needs" section.
3. Indicate your urgency level in the "Urgency" section.
4. Provide your household size and contact method in the "Household Size" and "Contact Method" sections.
5. Enter your organization or individual contact information in the "Organization Contact" or "Individual Contact" sections.
6. Provide any additional information about your contact method in the "Contact Method Other Info" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details**: Please enter your name and organization (if applicable) in the "Contact Details" section.
  * This will help us to contact you regarding your inquiry.
* **Housing Needs**: Describe your current housing needs, such as rental assistance, homeownership, or other housing-related needs.
  * This will help us understand your housing requirements.
* **Urgency**: Indicate whether your housing situation is urgent or not.
  * If yes, please provide additional information in the "Urgency Other Info" section.
* **Household Size**: Indicate your household size.
  * If your household size is not listed, please use the "Household Size Other" field.
* **Contact Method**: Select how you would like to be contacted.
  * Choose one of the options: Email, Phone, Chat, SMS, or Voice Call.
  * If you prefer to be contacted through another method, please use the "Contact Method Other" field.
* **Organization Contact**: If you are representing an organization, please provide your organization's name and contact information.
  * If you are an individual, please leave this field blank.
* **Individual Contact**: If you are an individual, please provide your contact information.
  * If you are an organization, please leave this field blank.
* **Email**: If you have an email address, please enter it.
  * This will help us to contact you regarding your inquiry.
* **Phone**: If you have a phone number, please enter it.
  * This will help us to contact you regarding your inquiry.
* **Address**: Please enter your address.
  * This will help us to understand your location and provide services accordingly.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional notes about your inquiry.
  * This will help us to better understand your needs.
* **Consent**: Confirm that you consent to being contacted regarding your inquiry.
  * Please select "Yes" to confirm.
* **Contact Method Other**: If you prefer to be contacted through another method, please enter it.
  * Please provide additional information in the "Contact Method Other Info" field.
* **Housing Type**: Indicate whether you are looking for active or inactive housing.
  * Choose one of the options: Active or Inactive.
* **Housing Status**: Indicate the status of your current housing situation.
  * Choose one of the options: Active or Inactive.
* **Household Size Other**: If your household size is not listed, please enter it.
* **Urgency Other**: If your urgency level is not listed, please enter it.
* **Urgency Other Info**: Please provide additional information about your urgency level.
* **Housing Needs Other**: If you have other housing needs, please describe them.
* **Housing Needs Other Info**: Please provide additional information about your housing needs.
* **Contact Method Other Info**: If you have other contact method preferences, please provide additional information.
* **Notes Other**: If you have additional notes, please provide them. 

Please note that all fields marked as "required" are mandatory.
