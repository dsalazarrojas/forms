# wire_mesh_material_inquiry_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The wire mesh material inquiry form is used to collect information from customers to provide them with quotes or to process their wire mesh material inquiries. This form helps in understanding the customer's requirements and preferences for the wire mesh material they are interested in.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the inquiry type by choosing the appropriate option from the dropdown menu.
2. Choose the wire mesh type that you are interested in.
3. Provide the mesh size required.
4. Choose the materials you are interested in.
5. Enter the price you are willing to pay or if you want us to quote.
6. Provide the quantity you need.
7. Confirm your availability to receive the material.
8. Share any additional customer inquiries or comments.
9. Provide your email address so we can contact you.
10. Enter your phone number so we can reach you.
11. Add any additional customer notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inquiry Type** (inquiry_type, select_one, required: false): Please select the type of inquiry you are making, whether it's for a quote or another reason.
* **Wire Mesh Type** (wire_mesh_type, select_multiple, required: true): Choose the type of wire mesh material you are interested in.
* **Mesh Size** (mesh_size, select_one, required: false): Select the size of the wire mesh material you need.
* **Wire Mesh Materials** (wire_mesh_materials, select_multiple, required: false): Select the materials you would like to have in the wire mesh.
* **Price** (price, number, required: true): Enter the price you are willing to pay or if you want us to quote.
* **Quantity** (quantity, number, required: true): Provide the quantity of wire mesh material you need.
* **Availability** (availability, select_one, required: false): Confirm your availability to receive the material.
* **Customer Inquiry** (customer_inquiry, text, required: false): Share any additional customer inquiries or comments.
* **Customer Message** (customer_message, text, required: true): Enter a message to us so we can assist you better.
* **Customer Email** (customer_email, email, required: true): Provide your email address so we can contact you.
* **Customer Phone** (customer_phone, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can reach you.
* **Customer Notes** (customer_notes, text, required: false): Add any additional customer notes or comments.
* **Sales Person** (sales_person, select_one, required: false): If you have a preferred sales person, please select them here.
* **Customer Company** (customer_company, text, required: false): Enter the customer company name.
* **Customer Address** (customer_address, text, required: false): Enter the customer address.
* **Customer City** (customer_city, text, required: false): Enter the customer city.
* **Customer State** (customer_state, text, required: false): Enter the customer state.
* **Customer Zip** (customer_zip, text, required: false): Enter the customer zip code.
* **Customer Country** (customer_country, text, required: false): Enter the customer country.
* **Customer State/Province** (customer_state_province, text, required: false): Enter the customer state or province.
* **Customer Reference** (customer_reference, text, required: false): Enter the customer reference.
* **Ship To** (customer_ship_to, text, required: false): Enter the ship to address.
* **Instructions** (customer_instructions, text, required: false): Enter any specific instructions.
* **Comments** (customer_comments, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments.
* **Terms and Conditions** (customer_terms, text, required: false): Review the terms and conditions of the wire mesh material.
