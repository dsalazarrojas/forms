# Affordable Accommodation Inquiry - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about your housing needs and preferences. It will help us understand what type of accommodation you're looking for and what features are essential for you.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by *).
3. If you're unsure about any question, please provide as much information as you can and we'll follow up to clarify.
4. For select multiple options, choose as many as apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (2, text, required): Enter your complete legal name.
* **Email Address** (3, email, required): Your email address will be used for accommodation updates and communication. Please ensure it's accurate and active.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, required): Your phone number will be used for contact and updates. Please provide a valid number.
* **Date of Birth** (5, date, optional): Your date of birth is helpful for understanding your age and potential eligibility for certain housing programs.
* **Current Address** (6, text, optional): Your current residential address is helpful for determining your location and proximity to desired areas.
* **Preferred Move-In Date** (8, date, required): Choose the date when you'd like to move in.
* **Type of Accommodation** (9, select_one, required): Select the type of accommodation you're looking for (e.g., Studio Apartment, One Bedroom, etc.).
* **Occupancy Count** (10, number, required): Enter the number of people who will be living in the accommodation.
* **Accessibility Needs** (11, select_one, required): If you have any accessibility requirements, please select 'True'.
* **Accessibility Details** (12, text, optional): If you selected 'True' for accessibility needs, please provide more details.
* **Monthly Rent Budget** (14, select_one, required): Choose your maximum monthly rent budget.
* **Income Source** (15, select_one, required): Select your primary source of income.
* **Income Verification** (16, select_one, required): If you're able to provide income verification, please select 'True'.
* **Housing Assistance Eligibility** (17, select_one, optional): If you're eligible for any housing assistance programs, please select 'True'.
* **Preferred Location** (19, text, required): Enter the area or neighborhood you're interested in.
* **Proximity Requirements** (20, select_multiple, required): Select the locations you need to be close to.
* **Transportation Access** (21, select_one, required): If you have access to reliable transportation, please select 'True'.
* **Required Amenities** (23, select_multiple, required): Choose the amenities that are essential for you (e.g., kitchen, laundry, etc.).
* **Pet Information** (24, select_one, required): If you have pets or plan to get them, please select 'True'.
* **Pet Details** (25, text, optional): If you selected 'True' for pet information, please provide details about your pets.
* **Special Requirements** (26, text, optional): If you have any special requirements or preferences, please describe them.
* **Application Priority** (28, select_one, required): Choose the urgency of your housing need (e.g., immediate, short-term, etc.).
* **Previous Housing History** (29, text, optional): Briefly describe your previous housing situation.
* **Additional Information** (30, text, optional): Provide any other information that might help with your housing search.
* **Electronic Signature** (31, text, required): Sign to confirm your inquiry.
