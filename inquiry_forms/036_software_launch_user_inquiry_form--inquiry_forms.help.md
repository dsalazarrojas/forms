# software_launch_user_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for users to express their interest in launching software with our company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required information, marked as required in the form.
2. Select the options that best describe your software launch interest.
3. Enter any additional comments or details about your software launch interest in the comments field.
4. Submit the form to send us your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your name, company, and job title. Please provide us with your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, text, optional): This field is where you can specify how you would like us to contact you. Please select one of the options provided.
* **Launch Interest** (`launch_interest`, number, optional): This field is where you can rate your level of interest in launching software with our company. Please select a number between 1 and 5.
* **Launch Date** (`launch_date`, date, optional): This field is where you can enter the date you would like to launch your software.
* **Product Feedback** (`product_feedback`, select_multiple, optional): This field is where you can express your interest in launching our software products. Please select all that apply.
* **Product Details** (`product_details`, select_one, required): This field is where you can specify the type of software you are interested in launching. Please select one of the options provided.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is where you can enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter any additional comments or details about your software launch interest.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your name.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your company name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your job title.
* **Product Team** (`product_team`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter the name of the product team you are interested in launching.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter the department you are interested in launching software within.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your job function.
* **Job Function Other** (`job_function_other`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter an other job function not listed in the options.
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your role.
* **Role Other** (`role_other`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter an other role not listed in the options.
* **Launch Interest Other** (`launch_interest_other`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter an other launch interest not listed in the options.
* **Job Function Other Description** (`job_function_other_description`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter a description of your other job function.
* **Role Other Description** (`role_other_description`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter a description of your other role.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter the size of your company.
* **Company Size Other** (`company_size_other`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter the size of your company not listed in the options.
* **Company Size Other Description** (`company_size_other_description`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter a description of your company size not listed in the options.

Note: Please provide accurate and honest information in this form to ensure that we can better understand your software launch interest.
