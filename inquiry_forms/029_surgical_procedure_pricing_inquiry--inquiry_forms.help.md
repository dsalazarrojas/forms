# surgical_procedure_pricing_inquiry - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about surgical procedure pricing and related details. It is intended to be used by patients or healthcare professionals to inquire about or provide information regarding surgical procedures, including fees, dates, and contacts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the form with the relevant information about the surgical procedure, including the procedure type and any other details as required.
2. Enter the patient's information, including any relevant medical history.
3. Provide details about the procedure, including the surgeon, anesthesiologist, and facility information.
4. Enter the fees associated with the procedure, including the surgeon's fee, anesthesiologist's fee, and facility fee.
5. Enter the date and time of the surgery, if applicable.
6. Choose the contact method and provide any additional comments or notes.
7. Enter the contact email and phone number.
8. Add any additional comments or notes for the contact person.
9. Upload any relevant medical records or patient statements.
10. Review and submit the form for completion.
