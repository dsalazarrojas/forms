# electric_vehicle_charging_station_inquiry_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for users to inquire about electric vehicle charging stations. It allows users to provide information about their electric vehicle, such as type, and preferred payment method for charging. The form also includes contact information in case they need to get in touch with us. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the User Input field with your name and other identifying information.
2. Select your Electric Vehicle Type from the dropdown list.
3. Choose your Preferred Payment Method for charging from the list.
4. Choose your Contact Method(s) in case we need to reach out to you.
5. Enter your Email Address and Phone Number (if applicable).
6. Provide any additional information in the Message field if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input**: This is a free-text field where you can enter your name and other identifying information.
* **Electric Vehicle Type**: This field allows you to select your vehicle type, which helps us determine the best charging options for you. You can choose from Petrol, Diesel, Hybrid, Electric, or Other (please specify).
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method for charging. You can choose between Cash, Card, or Other (please specify).
* **Contact Method**: Choose one or more ways you'd like us to contact you, such as Email or Phone. You can select multiple options.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address if you'd like us to contact you via email.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number if you'd like us to contact you via phone.
* **Message**: This is a free-text field where you can provide any additional information about your inquiry.
 
## Tips
- Please ensure that your Email Address and Phone Number are accurate and up-to-date.
- If you select Other as your Electric Vehicle Type, please specify in the Message field.
- If you select Other as your Preferred Payment Method, please specify in the Message field.
- Be sure to submit the form completely before selecting submit.
