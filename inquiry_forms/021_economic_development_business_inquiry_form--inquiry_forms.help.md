# Economic Development Business Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The economic development business inquiry form is a crucial tool for collecting information from potential partners, customers, or other stakeholders for economic development projects. It gathers essential details about contacts, projects, and other relevant information for business development purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. **Provide your Contact Information**: Fill in your contact name and phone number in the respective fields. This information helps us understand how to best reach out to you for further discussions or follow-ups.
2. **Project Details**: Give us a brief description of your project or business idea. This will enable us to understand your needs and provide you with targeted support.
3. **Business Questions**: Select the option that best describes your business question or concern. This will help us direct our resources accordingly.
4. **Company Information**: If you wish to provide more information about your company, you can do so in the text field.
5. **Additional Contact Person**: If another person is the main contact for your project or business, please provide their details.
6. **Email**: Enter your primary email address. This will be our main point of contact with you.
7. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, ensuring we can reach you for further discussions or follow-ups.
8. **Business Question 2**: Select the option that best describes your additional business question or concern.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your name and phone number to enable us to get in touch with you.
* **Project Details** (`Project Details`, `text`, required: false): Give us a brief description of your project or business idea to help us understand your needs.
* **Business Questions** (`Business Questions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the option that best describes your business question or concern to help us direct our resources accordingly.
* **Company Info** (`Company Info`, `text`, required: false): If you wish to provide more information about your company, you can do so here.
* **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, `text`, required: false): If another person is the main contact for your project or business, please provide their details.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: true): Enter your primary email address for our main point of contact with you.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: true): Enter your phone number for us to reach you for further discussions or follow-ups.
* **Business Question 2** (`Business Question 2`, `text`, required: false): Select the option that best describes your additional business question or concern to help us direct our resources accordingly.
