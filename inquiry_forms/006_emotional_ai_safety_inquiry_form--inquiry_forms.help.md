# Emotional AI Safety Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emotional AI Safety Inquiry Form is designed to gather information from users about potential safety concerns related to emotional AI development. This form is used to identify and address potential risks and safeguards associated with emotional AI systems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the risks you perceive in the "Emotional AI Risks" field.
2. Provide additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field if you have more information to share.
3. Select the safeguards you perceive as in place in the "Emotional AI Safeguards" field.
4. Provide your contact information, including name, email, phone number, job title, department, and location, in the relevant fields if you want to be contacted for further discussion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (User Input, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional information you think is relevant to the safety inquiry.
* **Emotional AI Risks** (Emotional AI Risks, select_multiple, required): Select all the risks you perceive in the emotional AI development process. 
    + 'Yes' - You have identified potential risks in the emotional AI development process.
    + 'No' - You have not identified potential risks in the emotional AI development process.
* **Safeguards** (Safeguards, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional information about the safeguards you have in place.
* **Benefits** (Benefits, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional information about the benefits of the emotional AI system.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional comments or information you think is relevant to the safety inquiry.
* **Emotional AI Safeguards** (Emotional AI Safeguards, select_multiple, required): Select all the safeguards you perceive as in place for the emotional AI system. 
    + 'Yes' - You have identified potential safeguards in the emotional AI system.
    + 'No' - You have not identified potential safeguards in the emotional AI system.
* **Emotional AI Safeguards Impact** (Emotional AI Safeguards Impact, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any information about the impact of the safeguards on the AI system.
* **Submit** (Submit, text, optional): This is the submit button to send the form.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Name** (Name, text, optional): Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Organization** (Organization, text, optional): Enter your organization for contact purposes.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, text, optional): Enter your job title for contact purposes.
* **Department** (Department, text, optional): Enter your department for contact purposes.
* **Location** (Location, text, optional): Enter your location for contact purposes.

Note: This form is for reporting potential safety concerns and is not for general feedback or comments. If you have general feedback or comments, please use the regular contact form.
