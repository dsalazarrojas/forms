# Adult Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adult Inquiry Form is designed to collect information from adult individuals who are seeking assistance or have specific questions about our organization's services, programs, or resources. This form helps us understand your needs and provide you with the most suitable support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal details:
	*   Full Name: Enter your name as it should be displayed.
	*   Phone Number: Provide your contact phone number for us to reach you.
	*   Email Address: Enter your email address so we can contact you via email.
2.  Select your area of interest:
	*   Choose from the options: Education Programs, Counseling Services, Support Groups, Volunteer Opportunities, or Other.
3.  Describe your question or request:
	*   Enter a specific question or request for the support you need.
4.  Choose your preferred contact method:
	*   Select how you would like to be contacted: Phone Call, Email, or SMS.
5.  Choose a best time to reach you:
	*   Select a time of day when you are most available to be contacted.
6.  Share any additional context or notes:
	*   Provide any additional information that might help us better understand your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it should be displayed.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Provide your contact phone number for us to reach you.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you via email.
*   **Area of Interest** (`area_of_interest`, select_one, required): Choose from the options: Education Programs, Counseling Services, Support Groups, Volunteer Opportunities, or Other.
*   **Specific Question or Request** (`specific_question_or_request`, text, required): Enter a specific question or request for the support you need.
*   **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required): Select how you would like to be contacted: Phone Call, Email, or SMS.
*   **Best Time to Reach You** (`best_time_to_reach_you`, select_one, optional): Select a time of day when you are most available to be contacted.
*   **How You Found Us** (`how_you_found_us`, select_one, optional): Help us improve our outreach by selecting how you found us: Online Search, Referral, Social Media, or Community Event.
*   **Additional Notes or Context** (`additional_notes_or_context`, text, optional): Provide any additional information that might help us better understand your needs.
*   **Best Time to Reach You** (optional): This field is not required but will allow you to select a time of day when you are most available to be contacted. This is helpful for us to contact you at a convenient time.
*   **How You Found Us** (optional): This field will help us improve our outreach efforts. Choose how you found us: Online Search, Referral, Social Media, or Community Event.
