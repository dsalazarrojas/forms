# Education Participant Course Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from education participants about a course they are interested in or are currently participating in.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields: First name, Last name, Email.
2. Fill out any additional optional fields that apply to your inquiry.
3. If you have any follow-up questions or comments, use the corresponding fields to provide more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (Text, Required): Your first name.
* **Last name** (Text, Required): Your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Your email address.
* **Program name** (Text, Optional): The name of the program you are inquiring about.
* **Program type** (Select One, Optional): The type of program you are interested in (e.g., Bachelor's, Master's, etc.).
* **Start date** (Date, Optional): The start date of the program you are inquiring about.
* **End date** (Date, Optional): The end date of the program you are inquiring about.
* **Contact phone** (Text, Optional): Your phone number for follow-up.
* **Contact note** (Note, Optional): Any additional notes or comments.
* **Program location** (Text, Optional): The location where the program is held.
* **Program language** (Text, Optional): The language of the program.
* **Contact email** (Email, Optional): Your email address for follow-up.
* **Program status** (Select Multiple, Optional): The status of the program you are inquiring about (Active, Pending, Inactive).
* **Program description** (Text, Optional): A brief description of the program.
* **Additional comments** (Text, Optional): Any additional comments or questions.
* **Follow up question** (Text, Optional): Your question about the program.
* **Follow up answer** (Text, Optional): The answer to your question.
* **Program URL** (Text, Optional): The URL of the program.
* **Follow up question 2** (Text, Optional): Your second question about the program.
* **Follow up answer 2** (Text, Optional): The answer to your second question.
* **Follow up question 3** (Text, Optional): Your third question about the program.
* **Follow up answer 3** (Text, Optional): The answer to your third question.
* **Follow up question 4** (Text, Optional): Your fourth question about the program.
* **Follow up answer 4** (Text, Optional): The answer to your fourth question.
* **Follow up question 5** (Text, Optional): Your fifth question about the program.
* **Follow up answer 5** (Text, Optional): The answer to your fifth question.
