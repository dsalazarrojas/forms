# Fashion Brand E Commerce Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be used by customers to contact us about their interest in our e-commerce fashion brand. It allows customers to submit their inquiries, questions, or style requests for our products. Please fill out this form to ensure our team can better understand and respond to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **customer_name** field. This is where you can provide your full name as you would like it to be used by our team.
2. Enter your email address in the **customer_email** field. This is the email address that we will use to respond to your inquiry.
3. In the **product_details** field, describe the product(s) you are inquiring about. This could be a specific product, product line, or style.
4. If you have a particular style request or customization in mind, enter it in the **customer_style_request** field.
5. If you have a phone number you would like us to contact you on, enter it in the **customer_phone** field.
6. In the **customer_message** field, provide any additional information or comments you would like to share with our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer_name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name as you would like it to be used by our team.
* **customer_email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address that we will use to respond to your inquiry.
* **product_details** (`product_details`, `text`, required: false): Describe the product(s) you are inquiring about.
* **customer_style_request** (`customer_style_request`, `text`, required: false): If you have a specific style request, enter it here.
* **customer_phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number to contact you.
* **customer_message** (`customer_message`, `text`, required: false): Any additional information or comments you would like to share with our team.

Tips:
* Please fill out as much information as you can to help us better understand your inquiry.
* If you have any attachments or files to support your request, you can upload them separately.
* If you have any follow-up questions, we will be happy to answer them via email or phone.
