# Interest Inquiry Phone Call Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from customers or potential customers about their interest in inquiring about a product or service.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including phone number and email.
2. Select the type of inquiry you are making (e.g., product information, pricing, etc.).
3. Choose how you would like to be contacted for follow-up (phone, email, or text message).
4. Specify the best time to contact you for follow-up (day and hour).
5. Add any additional comments or information that may be helpful for our representatives to follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`form_input`): This field is used to capture your contact information, including phone number and email. You can enter your phone number in the format `xxx-xxx-xxxx` and your email address in the format `example@example.com`.
* **What is your inquiry about** (`inquiry_type`): Select the type of inquiry you are making. This will help us understand your interest and provide you with relevant information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter your phone number to enable us to contact you for follow-up.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address to receive follow-up communication from us.
* **Message** (`message`): If you have any additional information or comments, enter them here. This will help our representatives to tailor their follow-up communication with you.
* **How do you prefer to be contacted** (`follow_up_method`): Choose how you would like to be contacted for follow-up (phone, email, or text message).
* **Best Time to Contact** (`follow_up_time`): Select the day and hour when it's best to contact you for follow-up.
* **Best Day to Contact** (`follow_up_day`): Choose the day of the week when it's best to contact you for follow-up.
* **Best Hour to Contact** (`follow_up_hour`): Select the hour of the day when it's best to contact you for follow-up.
* **Additional Comments** (`follow_up_comment`): Add any additional comments or information that may be helpful for our representatives to follow up with you.
* **Phone Type** (`follow_up_phone_type`): Select the type of phone you have (Home, Work, or Cell).

Note: All fields with `required: false` are optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
