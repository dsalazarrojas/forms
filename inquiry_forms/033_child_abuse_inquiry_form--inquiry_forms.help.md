# Child Abuse Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about child safety and welfare concerns. It is intended for use by mandatory reporters, such as teachers, school staff, and healthcare providers. The form helps to document concerns and provide a structure for reporting them in a clear and concise manner.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the appropriate option for each field based on the information you have about the child's situation.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information.
3. Provide a clear and concise description in the `inquiry_intro` field to briefly introduce the reason for filling out this form.
4. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Date**: The date you are reporting the concern.
	+ Format: MM/DD/YYYY
	+ Enter a valid date in the format above.
* **Reporter Name**: Your name, as the person completing this form.
	+ Enter your name as it appears on official records.
* **Your Relationship to the Child**: The role you have in relation to the child.
	+ Choose the option that best describes your relationship to the child.
* **Child's First Name**: The first name of the child.
	+ Enter the first name of the child.
* **Child's Last Name**: The last name of the child.
	+ Enter the last name of the child.
* **Child's Date of Birth**: The date of birth of the child.
	+ Format: MM/DD/YYYY
	+ Enter a valid date in the format above.
* **Child's Age**: The age of the child.
	+ Enter the age of the child.
* **Child's Gender**: The gender of the child.
	+ Choose the option that best describes the child's gender.
* **Child's Address**: The address of the child.
	+ Enter the address of the child.
* **City**: The city where the child resides.
	+ Enter the city where the child lives.
* **State**: The state where the child resides.
	+ Enter the state where the child lives.
* **Zip Code**: The zip code of the child's address.
	+ Enter the zip code of the child's address.
* **Type of Concern**: The type of concern you have about the child's safety and welfare.
	+ Choose all that apply from the options provided.
* **Detailed Description of Observations**: A clear and concise description of what you have observed about the child.
* **Description of Injuries or Concerns**: Provide a description of any injuries or concerns you have noticed about the child.
* **Behavioral or Emotional Changes**: Provide a description of any behavioral or emotional changes you have noticed about the child.
* **Statement Made by the Child (if any)**: If the child has made a statement about their situation, enter it here.
* **Information about Suspected Perpetrator (if known)**: If you have any information about the suspected perpetrator, enter it here.
* **Previous Concerns or Incidents Reported**: Have you reported any previous concerns or incidents about this child?
	+ Choose an option from the choices provided.
* **Is the Child in Immediate Danger?**: Is the child in immediate danger?
	+ Choose an option from the choices provided.
* **Safety Plan in Place**: Is a safety plan in place for the child?
	+ Choose an option from the choices provided.
* **Have Authorities Been Contacted?**: Have authorities been contacted regarding this concern?
	+ Choose an option from the choices provided.
* **Additional Information or Context**: If you have any additional information or context that might be helpful, enter it here.

Remember to carefully review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or a trusted authority figure.
