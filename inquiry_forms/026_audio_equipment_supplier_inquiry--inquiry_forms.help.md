# Audio Equipment Supplier Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Audio Equipment Supplier Inquiry form is designed to gather information from clients who are inquiring about audio equipment suppliers. This form helps the supplier's sales team understand the client's needs and provide a more personalized response.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your contact information, including your name, company or organization name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide the name of the project or event you are inquiring about.
3. Select the types of audio equipment categories you need (e.g., PA Systems and Speakers, Mixing Consoles, etc.).
4. List specific items and quantities you are interested in.
5. Choose the type of inquiry (e.g., rental, purchase, lease, etc.).
6. Enter your estimated budget (optional).
7. Specify the required delivery date.
8. Provide details about the venue and location where the equipment will be used.
9. Check if you require any additional services (e.g., on-site technician, delivery and setup, etc.).
10. Add any additional notes or comments for the sales team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Company or Organization Name**: Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for follow-up calls.
* **Project or Event Name**: Enter the name of the project or event you are inquiring about.
* **Equipment Categories Needed**: Select all the types of audio equipment you need (e.g., PA Systems and Speakers, Mixing Consoles, etc.).
* **Specific Items and Quantities**: List specific items and quantities you are interested in, including model numbers if known.
* **Inquiry Type**: Choose the type of inquiry (e.g., rental, purchase, lease, etc.).
* **Estimated Budget**: Enter your estimated budget for the inquiry (optional).
* **Required Delivery Date**: Specify the date by which you need the equipment delivered.
* **Venue and Location Details**: Provide details about the venue and location where the equipment will be used.
* **Additional Services Required**: Check if you require any additional services (e.g., on-site technician, delivery and setup, etc.).
* **Any other notes for our sales team?**: Add any additional comments or notes for the sales team.
