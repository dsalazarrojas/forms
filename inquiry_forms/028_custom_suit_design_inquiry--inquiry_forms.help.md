# Custom Suit Design Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is to help users understand how to fill out the Custom Suit Design Inquiry form to specify their preferences for a custom suit.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Category**: Choose the category for your suit: Men, Women, Junior, or Other.
2. **Fabric Type**: Select multiple fabric types for your suit, such as wool, cotton, linen, or silk.
3. **Color Scheme**: Choose multiple colors for your suit, such as navy, black, gray, or beige.
4. **Sleeve Length**: Select the desired sleeve length for your jacket, such as Long, Short, or Adjustable.
5. **Pocket Type**: Decide on the type of pockets you want, such as None, Slit, or Flap.
6. **Button Holes**: Choose the number of button holes for your jacket, such as None, One, or Two.
7. **Collar Style**: Select the collar style for your shirt, such as Spread, Point, or Wing.
8. **Lapel Width**: Choose the lapel width for your jacket, such as Regular or Long.
9. **Shoulder Type**: Decide on the shoulder type for your jacket, such as Notch or Pad.
10. **Sleeve Buttons**: Choose the number of sleeve buttons for your jacket, such as None, Single, or Double.
11. **Waistcoat**: Select the type of waistcoat for your suit, such as None, Single, or Double.
12. **Jacket-Pant Suit**: Choose whether you want a suit with a jacket and pants or just pants.
13. **Pant-Pant Suit**: Similar to 12.
14. **Pant-Jacket Suit**: Similar to 12.
15. **Jacket-Only Suit**: Choose a suit with just a jacket.
16. **Waistcoat Type**: Decide on the type of waistcoat, such as None, Single, or Double.
17. **Shirt Collar Style**: Choose the collar style for your shirt, such as Button, Tab, or Stand.
18. **Shirt Sleeve Style**: Select the sleeve style for your shirt, such as Short, Long, or Extra Long.
19. **Shirt Sleeve Length**: If you're not satisfied with the previous options, enter a custom sleeve length.
20. **Shirt Pocket Type**: Choose the pocket type for your shirt, such as None, Flap, or Pocket.
21. **Shirt Cuff Style**: Select the cuff style for your shirt, such as French, Button, or Link.
22. **Pant Pocket Type**: Choose the pocket type for your pants, such as None, Flap, or Pocket.
23. **Pant Hem Style**: Select the hem style for your pants, such as False, Single, or Double.
24. **Pant Vent Style**: Select the vent style for your pants, such as False, Single, or Double.

## Tips

* Make sure to choose your fabric type carefully, as it affects the overall quality and feel of your suit.
* Consider the color scheme and sleeve length when selecting your jacket and pants to ensure they match.
* If you want to customize your sleeve length, use the `Shirt Sleeve Length` field.
* Ensure that your pocket type and hem style are consistent with your desired use of the pockets (e.g., functional or decorative).
