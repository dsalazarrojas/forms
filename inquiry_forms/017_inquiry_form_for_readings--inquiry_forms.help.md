# Inquiry Form For Readings - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from clients for readings, such as appointments, consultations, or sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Enter your client's email address in the "Client Email" field. Please ensure that this is the correct email address where you can be reached.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Reading Type" to indicate the type of reading you are seeking.
4. Enter your preferred time for the reading in the "Preferred Time" field.
5. Enter any additional message or information you would like to share with the practitioner in the "Inquiry Message" field.
6. Specify your availability for the reading by selecting "Yes" or "No" for "Availability" and "Availability 2".
7. Indicate whether you would like to schedule the reading by selecting "Yes" or "No" for "Scheduling".
8. Enter any additional details about the reading in the "Reading Details" field.
9. Enter any notes for the practitioner in the "Practitioner Notes" field.
10. Specify whether you would like follow-up inquiries by selecting "Yes" or "No" for "Follow-up Inquiries".
11. If applicable, select the next follow-up date and time in the "Next Follow-up" field.
12. Specify the follow-up method by selecting "Yes" or "No" for "Follow-up Method".
13. Enter any follow-up message for the practitioner in the "Follow-up Message" field.
14. Select the follow-up status by selecting "Active" or "Inactive" for "Follow-up Status".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name (Client Name, text, required:**): Enter your name as the client seeking the reading.
* **Client Email (Client Email, email, required):** Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Reading Type (Reading Type, select_one, required):** Select "Yes" if you are seeking a reading, "No" if you are not.
* **Preferred Time (Preferred Time, time, required):** Enter your preferred time for the reading.
* **Inquiry Message (Inquiry Message, text, required):** Enter any additional message or information you would like to share with the practitioner.
* **Availability (Availability, select_multiple, required:**): Specify your availability for the reading by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Availability 2 (Availability 2, select_multiple, required:**): Repeat your availability specification for "Availability 2".
* **Scheduling (Scheduling, select_multiple, required:**): Indicate whether you would like to schedule the reading by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Reading Details (Reading Details, text, required:**): Enter any additional details about the reading.
* **Practitioner Notes (Practitioner Notes, text, required:**): Enter any notes for the practitioner.
* **Follow-up Inquiries (Follow-up Inquiries, select_multiple, required:**): Specify whether you would like follow-up inquiries by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Next Follow-up (Next Follow-up, date, required:**): If applicable, select the next follow-up date and time.
* **Follow-up Method (Follow-up Method, select_multiple, required:**): Specify the follow-up method by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Follow-up Message (Follow-up Message, text, required:**): Enter any follow-up message for the practitioner.
* **Follow-up Status (Follow-up Status, select_multiple, required:**): Select the follow-up status by selecting "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Follow-up 1 (Follow-up 1, text, required:**): Enter any follow-up information for the first follow-up.
* **1 (Follow-up 2, text, required:**): Enter any follow-up information for the second follow-up.
* **Follow-up 2 (Follow-up 2, text, required:**): Repeat any follow-up information for "Follow-up 2".
* **Follow-up 3 (Follow-up 3, text, required:**): Enter any follow-up information for the third follow-up.

# Tips
* Ensure that you have filled in all required fields.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting.
* Use clear and concise language in your responses.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the practitioner or support team.
