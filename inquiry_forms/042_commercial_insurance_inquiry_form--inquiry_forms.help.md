# Commercial Insurance Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The commercial insurance inquiry form is designed to gather information from business owners and managers to provide them with insurance coverage recommendations tailored to their specific needs. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure we can assess your business's risks and provide the best possible solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Fill out each page sequentially, using the following pages as a guide.
3. Enter your business information, including your name, contact person, and contact information.
4. Provide details about your business, such as the industry or sector, years in operation, number of employees, and annual revenue.
5. Select the types of insurance coverage you are interested in.
6. If you have current insurance coverage, provide information about your existing policy.
7. Answer questions about your claims history and desired coverage start date.
8. Specify your preferred contact method and best time to contact you.
9. Add any additional comments or information about your business that you feel is relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of your business as it appears on your business card or license.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of the person completing the form.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, `text`, `required`): Enter the title of the person completing the form.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your business email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, `required`): Enter your business phone number.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the type of business you operate (e.g., Retail, Office, Manufacturing, etc.).
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the number of years your business has been operating.
* **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, `number`, `required`): Enter the total number of employees in your organization.
* **Annual Revenue** (`annual_revenue`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the range of your business's annual revenue.
* **Do You Own Business Property?** (`property_owned`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether you own your business property or not.
* **Property Value** (`property_value`, `number`, `optional`): Enter the estimated value of your business property (if applicable).
* **Business Contents Value** (`business_contents_value`, `number`, `optional`): Enter the estimated value of business equipment and inventory (if applicable).
* **Coverage Types Interested In** (`coverage_types`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select all types of coverage you are interested in (e.g., General Liability, Property Insurance, etc.).
* **Do You Have Current Insurance?** (`current_insurance`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether you have current insurance coverage or not.
* **Current Insurance Carrier** (`current_carrier`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the name of your current insurance carrier (if applicable).
* **Policy Renewal Date** (`policy_renewal_date`, `date`, `optional`): Enter the date of your current policy renewal (if applicable).
* **Previous Claims** (`previous_claims`, `select_one`, `required`): Select your business's claims history in the past 5 years.
* **Claims Details** (`claims_details`, `text`, `optional`): Enter a brief description of your previous claims (if applicable).
* **Desired Coverage Start Date** (`coverage_start_date`, `date`, `required`): Select the date you would like to start coverage.
* **Quote Timeline** (`quote_timeline`, `select_one`, `required`): Select how soon you need a quote (e.g., Immediately, Within 1 week, etc.).
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, `required`): Select how you would like to be contacted (e.g., Email, Phone, etc.).
* **Best Time to Contact** (`best_time_to_contact`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the best time for us to contact you.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any additional information about your business.
