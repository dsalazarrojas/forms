# Health Insurance Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Insurance Inquiry Form is used to collect information from customers or employees regarding their health insurance policies. This form helps gather details such as first name, last name, email, phone number, date of birth, policy number, policy name, inquiry type, policy status, policy start and end dates, reason for cancellation, and assigned agent.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure you have permission to submit this form on behalf of the policyholder.
3. Double-check that all required fields are completed before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Policy Number**: Enter the policyholder's policy number.
* **Policy Name**: Enter the policyholder's policy name.
* **Inquiry Type**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Renewal: For policy renewals or inquiries.
	+ New Policy: For new policy inquiries.
	+ Inquiries: For other types of inquiries.
* **Policy Status**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Active: The policy is currently active.
	+ Inactive: The policy is currently inactive.
	+ Pending: The policy is pending cancellation or activation.
* **Policy Start Date**: Enter the policy start date (if applicable).
* **Policy End Date**: Enter the policy end date (if applicable).
* **Reason for Cancellation**: Provide the reason for the policy cancellation (if applicable).
* **Assigned Agent**: Select the assigned agent:
	+ Jim
	+ Jane
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes regarding the policy.
