# Franchise Drop Shipping Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Franchise Drop Shipping Inquiry form is designed for potential customers or partners to express interest or inquire about a drop shipping business or partnership opportunity. This form is used to collect contact information and other relevant details from the inquirers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact details in the respective fields.
2. Select your country from the drop-down menu.
3. Enter any additional details or message about your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (name_field): Enter your name, which will be used to address you in our communication.
* **Email** (email_field): Enter your email address, where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Phone** (phone_field): Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted via phone for more information.
* **Company** (company_field): Enter your company name, if you have one.
* **Website** (website_field): Enter your company website, if you have one.
* **Address** (address_field): Enter your physical address, which is not required but helps us understand your location.
* **Country** (country_field): Select your country from the drop-down menu.
* **State** (state_field): Enter your state or province, if you'd like to provide more specific location information.
* **City** (city_field): Enter your city or town, if you'd like to provide more specific location information.
* **Postal** (postal_field): Enter your postal or zip code, if you'd like to provide more specific location information.
* **Message** (message_field): Enter any additional details or questions you may have about the drop shipping opportunity.

## Tips
Please ensure that you enter accurate and complete information to help us process your inquiry efficiently. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
