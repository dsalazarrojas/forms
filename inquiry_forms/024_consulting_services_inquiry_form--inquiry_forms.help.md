# Consulting Services Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential clients about their consulting needs and project requirements. It will help us understand their project goals, constraints, and expectations, allowing us to provide tailored solutions and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
* Enter your Primary Email Address and Phone Number for contact purposes.
* Select your Company Name (if applicable).
* Choose your Job Title (if applicable).
* Select your Industry.
* Choose the Company Size (if applicable).
* Select the Service of Interest.
* Choose the Type of Inquiry.
* Describe your Project Description.
* List your Current Challenges.
* Outline your Desired Outcomes.
* Choose the Project Timeline.
* (Optional) Estimate your Budget Range.
* (Optional) Determine your Decision Timeframe.
* (Optional) Identify your Decision Makers.
* (Optional) Select if you have used consulting services before.
* (Optional) Choose how you heard about us.
* (Optional) Identify who referred you.
* Choose your Preferred Contact Method.
* Select the Best Time to Contact.
* Add any Additional Comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your primary email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your best contact number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your organization (if applicable).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your position (if applicable).
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select the industry sector that best describes your project.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, optional): Choose the number of employees in your organization (if applicable).
* **Service of Interest** (`service_interest`, select_one, required): Select the service you are interested in.
* **Type of Inquiry** (`inquiry_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of inquiry that best describes your project.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required): Describe your project or needs.
* **Current Challenges** (`current_challenges`, text, required): List the challenges you are currently facing.
* **Desired Outcomes** (`desired_outcomes`, text, required): Outline the results you hope to achieve.
* **Project Timeline** (`project_timeline`, select_one, required): Choose when you need the project completed.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, optional): Estimate your budget range (if applicable).
* **Decision Timeframe** (`decision_timeframe`, select_one, optional): Determine when you will decide on the project (if applicable).
* **Decision Makers** (`decision_makers`, text, optional): Identify who will be involved in the decision-making process (if applicable).
* **Have You Used Consultants Before** (`previous_consulting`, select_one, optional): Select "True" if you have used consulting services before.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, optional): Choose how you heard about us (if applicable).
* **Referred By** (`referral_source`, text, optional): Identify who referred you (if applicable).
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required): Choose the preferred method of contact.
* **Best Time to Contact** (`best_time_to_contact`, select_one, optional): Select the best time to contact (if applicable).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any other relevant information (if applicable).
