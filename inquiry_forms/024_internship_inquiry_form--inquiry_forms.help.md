# Internship Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internship Inquiry Form is a tool to gather information from users who want to inquire about internships within the company. It is meant to be used by individuals who are interested in exploring internship opportunities but have not yet submitted an inquiry or been accepted into an internship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields (denoted by *) with your information.
2. Ensure that you have not already submitted an inquiry or been accepted into an internship.
3. Upload any relevant files (if applicable).
4. Click the "Submit" button to send your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user_info**: Enter your name and contact information. This is the first step in allowing us to get in touch with you about potential internship opportunities.
* **program_info**: Describe the program or department you are interested in and what specific skills or areas of interest you have.
* **application_info**: Share details about your academic background, work history, and any relevant experience you have.
* **contact_info**: Provide your contact information so we can reach out to you about internship opportunities.
* **upload_file**: If you have any relevant documents or files that demonstrate your skills or experience, you may upload them here.
* **submit**: Click this button to send your completed inquiry.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your inquiry is complete.
* Only submit if you have not already done so or been accepted into an internship.
* You will receive a response from us once we have reviewed your application.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
