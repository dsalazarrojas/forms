# Personalized Search Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect specific details about search requests from users, enabling us to provide a tailored search experience. 

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter a search term in the text field provided. This is the keyword or phrase you are looking for.
2.  In the "search_type" field, select the type of search you are requesting. Options include:
    *   Keyword: Searching for a specific keyword or phrase.
    *   Category: Searching within a specific category.
    *   Author: Searching for content written by a particular author.
    *   Date: Searching for content by a specific date.
    *   File: Searching for a specific file.
3.  For "priority", select the level of priority for your search request. Options include:
    *   High
    *   Medium
    *   Low
4.  If you are searching for a specific date, enter it in the "search_date" field.
5.  If your search is location-specific, enter the location in the "search_location" field.
6.  Enter the time of your search in the "search_time" field.
7.  In the "additional_info" field, provide any additional information you would like us to know about your search request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **search_term**: Enter the specific keyword or phrase you are searching for.
*   **search_type**: Select the type of search you are requesting:
    *   Keyword
    *   Category
    *   Author
    *   Date
    *   File
*   **priority**: Set the priority level for your search request:
    *   High
    *   Medium
    *   Low
*   **search_date**: Enter the date you are searching for.
*   **search_location**: Enter the location where you are searching.
*   **search_time**: Enter the time for your search.
*   **additional_info**: Add any further details about your search request.
