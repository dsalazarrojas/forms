# Outdoor Lighting Inquiry - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for users who want to inquire about outdoor lighting-related services or products. You can use this form to provide your contact information, address details, and any other relevant information to receive assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "Contact First Name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "Contact Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Contact Email" field, following the standard email format (username@domain).
4. Provide your phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
5. Fill in your address line 1 in the "Address Line 1" field.
6. Fill in your address line 2 in the "Address Line 2" field.
7. Enter your city in the "City" field.
8. Select your state from the options in the "State" field.
9. Fill in your zip code in the "Zip Code" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact First Name (text)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Last Name (text)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Email (email)**: Enter your email address in the standard format (username@domain).
* **Contact Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number in the format (XXX-XXX-XXXX).
* **Address Line 1 (text)**: Enter your street address or address line 1.
* **Address Line 2 (text)**: Enter your apartment or suite number, or any additional address information.
* **City (text)**: Enter the city where you live.
* **State (select_one)**: Select the state where you live from the options provided.
* **Zip Code (text)**: Enter your zip code in the standard format (XXX-XXX).
