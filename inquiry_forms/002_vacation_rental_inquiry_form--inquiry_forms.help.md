# Vacation Rental Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from users for a vacation rental inquiry. It is used to process your request for a vacation rental stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your valid Email address in the Email field.
3. Provide your Phone number in the Phone field.
4. Select the Start and End Dates of your stay.
5. Specify the Number of Guests you will be traveling with.
6. Choose your Arrival Date and preferred Checkin Time.
7. Enter your preferred Checkout Date.
8. If you have any additional Note, you can include it in the Note field (optional).
9. Select the Type of Inquiry (Group, Private, Family, or Other).
10. Finally, enter the date you are submitting this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): This field is for your first name. Please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): This field is for your last name. Enter it as you would like it to appear on your rental documents.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for updates and communication.
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Start Date** (Date, Required): Select the date you plan to start your stay.
* **End Date** (Date, Required): Select the date you plan to end your stay.
* **Number of Guests** (Number, Required): Specify the number of people traveling with you.
* **Arrival Date** (Date, Required): Choose the date you plan to arrive for your stay.
* **Checkin Time** (Time, Required): Select your preferred Checkin Time.
* **Checkout Date** (Date, Required): Choose the date you plan to check out.
* **Note** (Text, Optional): If you have any special requests or notes, you can include them here.
* **Type of Inquiry** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of inquiry you are making (Group, Private, Family, or Other).
* **Inquiry Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you are submitting this form (will be automatically populated).
