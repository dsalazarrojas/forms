# Free Zone Company Registration Cost Inquiry - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Free Zone Company Registration Cost Inquiry form is used to gather information about a company that is interested in registering under the 'Free Zone' category. The information collected will help us understand the details of the company and its representative, which will aid in the process of registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as required and submit the form. You can refer to the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section below for a detailed explanation of each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Free Zone Company Registration Cost Inquiry**: Please provide a brief description of your inquiry about the registration cost.
* **Name**: Enter your name as the representative of the company.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company that is interested in registering under the 'Free Zone' category.
* **Business Activity**: Select the type of business activity your company is involved in, which is either 'Free Zone' or 'Non-Free Zone'. This will help us understand your business type.
* **Location**: Enter the location of your company's address.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number for follow-up purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth (if required).
* **Time of Birth**: Enter your time of birth (if required).
* **Follow-up Date**: Enter the date for follow-up (if required).
* **Follow-up Time**: Enter the time for follow-up (if required).
* **Follow-up Contact Number**: Enter your contact number for follow-up (if required).
* **Follow-up Email**: Enter your email address for follow-up (if required).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately to avoid any delays in the registration process.
* If you need any clarifications, please do not hesitate to contact us.
