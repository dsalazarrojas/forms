# packaging-innovation-inquiry-contact-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and inquiry details from users, allowing us to better understand their interests and needs. Fill out this form to inquire about innovation opportunities within our packaging division.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit an inquiry, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your contact information and inquiry details.
2. Choose your preferred contact method for follow-up communication.
3. Provide any additional context or information about your inquiry.
4. Click the "Submit" button to send your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other identifying information you'd like to share.
* **Inquiry Type** (`inquiry_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of inquiry you'd like to make (Project Brief, Innovation Inquiry, or Collaboration Request).
* **Project Brief Project Name** (`project_brief_project_name`, text, optional): If you're inquiring about a project, provide the name of the project.
* **Project Brief Project Status** (`project_brief_project_status`, text, optional): If you're inquiring about a project, provide the current status of the project.
* **Collaboration Request Collaboration Type** (`collaboration_request_collaboration_type`, select_one, optional): If you're requesting collaboration, select the type of collaboration you'd like to pursue (Partnership, Joint Development, or Other).
* **Collaboration Request Collaboration Description** (`collaboration_request_collaboration_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the collaboration request.
* **Collaboration Request Preferred Contact Method** (`collaboration_request_collaboration_preferred_contact_method`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred method of contact for follow-up communication (Email, Phone, In-Person).
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, optional): Enter your business email address.
* **User Telephone** (`user_telephone`, text, optional): Enter your business phone number.
* **User Address** (`user_address`, text, optional): Enter your business address.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Company URL** (`company_url`, text, optional): Enter your company URL.
* **Company Description** (`company_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your company.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, optional): Enter your job function.
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_multiple, optional): Select the industries your company operates in (Food and Beverage, Personal Care, Pharmaceutical, Cosmetic, Health & Wellness).
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, optional): Select the size of your company (Small, Medium, Large, Other).
* **Job Title 2** (`job_title_2`, text, optional): Enter your job title (if different from the first).
* **Job Function 2** (`job_function_2`, text, optional): Enter your job function (if different from the first).
* **Company Employees** (`company_employees`, text, optional): Enter the number of employees in your company.
* **Company Revenue** (`company_revenue`, text, optional): Enter your company revenue.
* **Job Function 2 2** (`job_function_2_2`, text, optional): Enter your job function (if you'd like to specify it).
* **Job Title 2 2** (`job_title_2_2`, text, optional): Enter your job title (if you'd like to specify it).
* **Submit** (`submit`, note, optional): This is the submit button.
