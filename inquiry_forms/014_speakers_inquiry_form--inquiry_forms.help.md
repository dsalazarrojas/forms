The form is designed to collect information from speakers for an event. It is meant to be filled out by speakers to provide details about their event, such as the event title, speaking formats, audience info, budget, and additional event details. The form helps the organizers understand the speakers' requirements and preferences to better organize and accommodate their needs.

# Speakers Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speakers Inquiry Form is used to collect information from speakers about their event, to ensure that their needs are met and the event is well-organized.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your event details.
2. Select your preferred speaking formats.
3. Provide information about your audience.
4. Specify your budget.
5. Add any additional event details if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`event_details`):
	+ A brief description of your event.
	+ Enter a short text describing your event.
* **Speaking Formats** (`speaking_formats`):
	+ Check "Yes" if your event requires speaking.
	+ This will help the organizers know your speaking format preferences.
* **Audience Info** (`audience_info`):
	+ Provide details about your expected audience, such as their size and demographics.
	+ This will help the organizers understand your audience's needs and preferences.
* **Budget** (`budget`):
	+ Enter your expected budget for the event.
	+ This will help the organizers understand your financial constraints and preferences.
* **Additional Event Details** (`event_details_2`, `speaking_formats_2`, `audience_info_2`, `event_details_3`):
	+ Enter any additional information about your event that is not covered by the other fields.
	+ This could include specific requirements, preferences, or expectations.
