# Sales Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information from customers or prospects, allowing your sales team to initiate contact and potentially follow up with them.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Select your preferred contact method (Phone, Email, or In-person).
3. Ask your question or ask for advice, and we'll get back to you shortly.
4. Optionally, provide additional questions or information that you'd like to discuss further.
5. Leave any other questions or comments you have for us at the bottom of this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number and email address for follow up.
- **Preferred Contact** (`preferred_contact`, select_one, required: false): Choose how you'd like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or In-person).
- **Question** (`question`, text, required: false): Ask your question or ask for advice.
- **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, date, required: false): Schedule a follow-up meeting or call.
- **Question 2-20** (`question2`, `question3`, etc.): Provide more details or ask more questions.
- **Additional Questions** (`additional_questions`, text, required: false): Leave any other questions or comments you have for us.
