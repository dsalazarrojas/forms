# Artist Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artist Inquiry Form is designed to gather essential information from artists for various purposes, including exhibition interest, gallery representation, art classes, commission inquiries, collaborations, or other types of inquiries. This form helps us understand the artist's background, medium, experience, and preferred contact method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** (Artist's First Name) in the "Artist First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** (Artist's Last Name) in the "Artist Last Name" field.
3. Provide your **Email Address** (Artist's Email Address) in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** (Artist's Phone Number) in the "Phone Number" field.
5. If applicable, enter your **Studio or Gallery Name** in the "Studio or Gallery Name" field.
6. Enter your **Studio or Gallery Address** in the "Studio or Gallery Address" field (optional).
7. Select your **Primary Artistic Medium** in the "Primary Artistic Medium" dropdown menu.
8. Enter the number of **Years of Experience** in the "Years of Experience" field.
9. Select the type of **Inquiry Type** that best describes your interest in the "Type of Inquiry" dropdown menu.
10. Provide a brief description of your **Inquiry Details** in the "Inquiry Details" field.
11. If you have a **Website or Portfolio URL**, enter it in the "Website or Portfolio URL" field.
12. List your **Social Media Handles** in the "Social Media Handles" field (optional).
13. Confirm if you have **Work Samples or Images Attached** by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Work Samples or Images Attached" dropdown menu.
14. Choose your **Preferred Contact Method** by selecting "Email", "Phone", or "Text Message" in the "Preferred Contact Method" dropdown menu.
15. Provide a brief description of your **Availability for Discussion** in the "Availability for Discussion" field (optional).
16. Lastly, enter any **Additional Information** about yourself or your work in the "Additional Information" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your artist's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your artist's last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for us to reach out to you.
* **Studio or Gallery Name**: Enter the name of your studio or gallery (optional).
* **Studio or Gallery Address**: Enter the address of your studio or gallery (optional).
* **Primary Artistic Medium**: Select your primary medium of art (e.g., painting, sculpture, photography, etc.).
* **Years of Experience**: Enter the number of years you have been practicing your art.
* **Type of Inquiry**: Select the type of inquiry that best describes your interest (e.g., exhibition interest, gallery representation, art classes, commission inquiry, collaboration, etc.).
* **Inquiry Details**: Briefly describe the reason for your inquiry.
* **Website or Portfolio URL**: Enter your website or portfolio URL (optional).
* **Social Media Handles**: List your social media handles (optional).
* **Work Samples or Images Attached**: Confirm if you have attached work samples or images to your inquiry.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted (email, phone, text message).
* **Availability for Discussion**: Provide a brief description of your availability for discussion.
* **Additional Information**: Enter any additional information about yourself or your work.
