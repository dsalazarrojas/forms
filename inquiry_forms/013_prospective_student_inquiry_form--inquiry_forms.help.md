# Prospective Student Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prospective Student Inquiry Form is a tool designed to help prospective students get in touch with us for information about our programs. This form allows us to collect your contact information and your interests in our programs so that we can provide you with the best possible support and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first name in the 'First Name' field.
2. Enter your last name in the 'Last Name' field.
3. Provide a valid email address in the 'Email' field.
4. Enter your phone number in the 'Phone' field, if you wish to be contacted via phone.
5. Select the type of inquiry you are making in the 'Inquiry Type' field. You can choose from 'Admission', 'Program', 'Course', or 'General'.
6. If applicable, select one or more programs of interest from the 'Program of Interest' field. You can choose from 'Master', 'Bachelor', 'Certificate', or 'Diploma'.
7. If you have a specific preferred date in mind, enter it in the 'Preferred Date' field.
8. If you have a preferred time, select it from the 'Time of Day' field.
9. If you have any additional comments or questions, enter them in the 'Note' field.
10. If you have any specific requirements for communication, you can specify this in the 'Assigned Tool' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **Required**): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **Optional**): If you wish to be contacted via phone, enter your phone number here.
* **Inquiry Type** (`inquiry_type`, select_one, **Optional**): Select the type of inquiry you are making:
	+ 'Admission': General admissions inquiries.
	+ 'Program': Program-specific inquiries.
	+ 'Course': Course-related inquiries.
	+ 'General': Other general inquiries.
* **Program of Interest** (`program_of_interest`, select_multiple, **Optional**): If applicable, select one or more programs that interest you:
	+ 'Master': Master's degree programs.
	+ 'Bachelor': Bachelor's degree programs.
	+ 'Certificate': Certificate programs.
	+ 'Diploma': Diploma programs.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, **Optional**): If you have a specific date in mind, enter it here.
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, time, **Optional**): If you have a preferred time for inquiry, select it from the options provided.
* **Note** (`note`, note, **Optional**): If you have any additional comments or questions, enter them here.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, **Optional**): If you have any specific requirements for communication, specify them here.
