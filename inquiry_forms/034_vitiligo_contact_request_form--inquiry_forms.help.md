# Vitiligo Contact Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and requests from customers or patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your contact information.
2. Select the relevant clinic location from the dropdown list.
3. Enter your preferred appointment date and time.
4. Write in any additional messages or requests in the provided text fields.
