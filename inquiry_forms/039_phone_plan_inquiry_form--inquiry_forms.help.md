# Phone Plan Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers about their phone plans. It is intended for customers to inquire about their current or future phone plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **Customer Name** in the `customer_name` field. This is a required field, so make sure to enter your full name.
2.  Enter your **phone number** in the `phone_number` field, if you would like to be contacted about your phone plan.
3.  Select your **preferred plan provider** from the list:
	*   Verizon
	*   AT&T
	*   T-Mobile
	*   Sprint
	*   Cricket
4.  Enter your **email address** in the `email_address` field. This is a required field for us to contact you about your phone plan.
5.  Optionally, provide any additional **phone plan details** in the `phone_plan_details` field.
6.  If you would like to be contacted in a specific **language**, select it from the list:
	*   English
	*   Español
	*   Français

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, Required): Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted about your phone plan.
* **Preferred Plan Provider** (`preferred_plan`, Required): Select your preferred phone plan provider from the list.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, Required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your phone plan.
* **Phone Plan Details** (`phone_plan_details`, Optional): Provide any additional information about your phone plan.
* **Language of Contact** (`preferred_language`, Optional): Select your preferred language for communication.
* **Phone** ( `phone`, Optional): Enter your phone number in the format XXX-XXXXXXX (XXX-1234567).

Note: This form is intended to gather information about your phone plan, and all the fields are designed to help us understand your needs. Please fill in the form accurately and truthfully. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
