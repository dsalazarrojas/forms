# General Inquiry Contact Form With Dark Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for users to submit general inquiries or contact information for support or feedback. It is intended to be filled out by users who need assistance or have questions regarding our services or business.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the purpose of your contact: Choose the reason for your inquiry from the available options (Feedback, Support, or Other).
2. Enter your name: Type in your full name as you would like to be addressed.
3. Enter your email address: Provide the email address you would like to be contacted at.
4. Enter your phone number: If you have a phone number, enter it so we can contact you more easily.
5. Write your message: Provide a brief description of your inquiry or question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inquiry** (`Contact Form`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter a brief description of your question or concern.
* **Your Name** (`Your Name`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Your Email** (`Your Email`, `email`, required/optional: false): Enter the email address you would like to be contacted at. Please format it correctly as a standard email address (e.g., name@domain.com).
* **Your Phone** (`Your Phone`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **Message** (`Message`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter a detailed description of your question or concern. 

## Tips

* Please make sure to enter accurate and complete information to help us better assist you.
* If you need a response, we will contact you via the email or phone number you provided.
