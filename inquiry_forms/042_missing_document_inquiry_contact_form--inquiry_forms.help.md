# Missing Document Inquiry Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit an inquiry about a missing document. This form is used to gather information about the document and the user's contact details for further investigation and resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant category of the missing document.
2. Enter your contact email address.
3. Enter your contact phone number (optional).
4. Provide a brief description of the missing document.
5. (Optional) Attach any relevant files or documents.
6. Choose the date and time you submitted the inquiry.
7. Select the user who should be assigned to handle your inquiry.
8. Add any additional notes or comments.
9. Select who submitted the form (e.g., your name or department).
10. Click the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, required): Select the relevant category of the missing document.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so that we can contact you for further investigation and resolution.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Briefly describe the missing document.
* **Uploaded File** (`uploaded_file`, text, optional): Attach any relevant files or documents that may help with the investigation.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, required): Select the date you submitted the form.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time, required): Select the time you submitted the form.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, optional): Select the user who should be assigned to handle your inquiry.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your inquiry.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Select who submitted the form (e.g., your name or department).
