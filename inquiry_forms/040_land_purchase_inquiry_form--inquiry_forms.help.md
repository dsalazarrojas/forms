# Land Purchase Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Land Purchase Inquiry Form is designed to gather information from users who are interested in purchasing land. This form will guide you through the necessary steps to provide your contact and property details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Select the type of property you are interested in (Single Family Home, Condo/Co-op, Townhouse, Land/Lot, Multi-Unit Home, or Other)
* Enter your contact information (name, email, and phone number)
* Enter the address of the property you are interested in purchasing
* Enter the name of the realtor or representative from the realtor office you are working with

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, required): Select the type of property you are interested in purchasing.
	+ This field will determine the subsequent fields that follow.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, required): Enter the address of the property you are interested in purchasing.
	+ This is a text field where you can enter the address of the property.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, required): Enter your name as the contact person.
	+ This is a text field where you can enter your name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, required): Enter your email address.
	+ This is a text field where you can enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, required): Enter your phone number.
	+ This is a text field where you can enter your phone number.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, optional): If you have any additional information about the property or your inquiry, enter it here.
	+ This is a text field where you can enter any additional information.
* **Realtor Name** (`realtor_name`, required): Enter the name of the realtor or representative you are working with.
	+ This is a text field where you can enter the realtor's name.
* **Realtor Office** (`realtor_office`, required): Enter the name of the realtor's office.
	+ This is a text field where you can enter the realtor's office.
