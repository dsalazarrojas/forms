# Livestock Monitoring System Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Livestock Monitoring System Inquiry Form is used to collect information about farm operations and management practices. This form is designed to help farm representatives gather data about their farm's activities, animal types, and feeding practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the farm you are inquiring about from the drop-down menu (not available in this example).
2. Fill out the farm information, including farm name, contact person, contact phone, and contact email.
3. Select the type of animals present on the farm (Pig, Sheep, Cattle, or Horse).
4. Enter the number of animals present on the farm.
5. Specify the type of animal count (Unit or Total).
6. Choose the farm size (Small, Medium, or Large).
7. Select the farm type (Organic, Conventional, or Organic/Conventional).
8. Choose the feed type (Grain, Silage, or Hay).
9. Specify the feed location (On Farm or Off Farm).
10. Select the water source (Well, Creek, or Pond).
11. Choose the water type (Spring, Well, or Creek).
12. Select the feed quality (High, Medium, or Low).
13. Enter the feed quality scale (not applicable for this field).
14. Select the feed frequency (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
15. Choose the feed location (On Farm or Off Farm).
16. Select the feed source (Local, Supplier, or Other).
17. Specify the farm location (On Farm or Off Farm).
18. Enter the farm address (not applicable for this example).
19. Schedule a follow-up date and time.
20. Add any additional information about the farm or its operations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Farm Name**: Enter the name of the farm or ranch you are inquiring about.
* **Contact Person**: Provide the name of the person responsible for managing or owning the farm.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Animal Type**: Choose the type of animals present on the farm (Pig, Sheep, Cattle, or Horse).
* **Number of Animals**: Enter the total number of animals present on the farm.
* **Animal Count Type**: Specify whether you are counting individual animals (Unit) or total counts (Total).
* **Farm Size**: Choose the size of the farm (Small, Medium, or Large).
* **Farm Type**: Select the farm type (Organic, Conventional, or Organic/Conventional).
* **Feed Type**: Choose the type of feed used on the farm (Grain, Silage, or Hay).
* **Feed Location**: Specify where the feed is located (On Farm or Off Farm).
* **Water Source**: Choose the source of water for the animals (Well, Creek, or Pond).
* **Water Type**: Select the type of water used for the animals (Spring, Well, or Creek).
* **Feed Quality**: Choose the quality of feed used on the farm (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Feed Quality Scale**: This field is not applicable for this form.
* **Feed Frequency**: Select how often the animals are fed (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Feed Location**: Specify where the feed is located (On Farm or Off Farm).
* **Feed Source**: Choose the source of feed used on the farm (Local, Supplier, or Other).
* **Farm Location**: Specify where the farm is located (On Farm or Off Farm).
* **Farm Address**: This field is not applicable for this example.
* **Follow Up**: Schedule a follow-up date and time for further discussion or inquiry.
* **Follow Up Time**: Enter the time of the follow-up.
* **Additional Info**: Add any additional information about the farm or its operations.
* **Confirm**: Confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.
