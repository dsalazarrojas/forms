# Marketing Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Inquiry Form is used by the marketing team to collect information from customers, partners, or other stakeholders to better understand their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields marked as `*Required`.
2. Enter your contact information (Company, Name, Email, and Contact Number) accurately.
3. Select the type of inquiry you would like to make (Inquiry Type).
4. Choose any additional details that apply to your inquiry (Inquiry Details).
5. If relevant, provide a specific date and time for your inquiry.
6. Add any follow-up information or notes.
7. Finally, type in your message and any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company** (`company`, `text`, *Required*): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, *Required*): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, *Required*): Enter your official email address.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, *Required*): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Inquiry Type** (`inquiry_type`, `select_one`, *Required*): Select the type of inquiry you would like to make.
* **Inquiry Details** (`inquiry_details`, `select_multiple`, *Optional*): Choose any additional details that apply to your inquiry.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, *Optional*): If applicable, enter the date you would like to inquire about.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, *Optional*): If applicable, enter the time you would like to inquire about.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, `note`, *Optional*): Provide any follow-up information or notes.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, *Optional*): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, *Required*): Type in your message or comments.
