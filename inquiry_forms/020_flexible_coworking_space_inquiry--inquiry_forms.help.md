# Flexible Coworking Space Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their flexible coworking space requirements. It is meant to help internal teams understand the needs and preferences of potential clients and inform business development and sales efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field as accurately and completely as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **team_name** (Optional): Enter the name of your team or organization.
* **team_size** (Optional): Enter the number of people that will be using the coworking space.
* **budget** (Optional): Enter the budget for your coworking space requirements.
* **timeline** (Optional): Enter a specific date or a date range for when you need the coworking space.
* **space_type** (Optional, Select one):
	+ Office Space: A traditional office setting with private or shared office rooms.
	+ Virtual Office: A remote working setting with no physical office space.
	+ Coworking Desk: A dedicated desk in a shared office space with access to common areas.
	+ Private Office: A private office with no shared space.
	+ Shared Office: A shared office space with others.
	+ Other: Please specify in the field below.
* **space_needs** (Optional, Select multiple):
	+ Fast internet: High-speed internet connectivity.
	+ Comfortable seating: Comfortable seating and working areas.
	+ High-speed Wi-Fi: High-speed Wi-Fi connectivity.
	+ Access to printers: Access to printers and other office equipment.
	+ Other: Please specify in the field below.
* **follow_up** (Optional, Select one):
	+ Email: Respond via email for follow-ups.
	+ Phone: Respond via phone for follow-ups.
	+ Mail: Respond via mail for follow-ups.
	+ Other: Please specify in the field below.
* **workspace_needs** (Optional, Select multiple):
	+ Fast internet: High-speed internet connectivity.
	+ Comfortable seating: Comfortable seating and working areas.
	+ High-speed Wi-Fi: High-speed Wi-Fi connectivity.
	+ Access to printers: Access to printers and other office equipment.
	+ Other: Please specify in the field below.
* **follow_up_message** (Optional): Enter any additional follow-up information you would like to provide.
* **workspace_address** (Optional): Enter the address of the coworking space.
* **coworking_providers** (Optional, Select multiple):
	+ Google Workspace: Google Workspace (G Suite) integration.
	+ Amazon Web Services: Amazon Web Services (AWS) integration.
	+ Microsoft Office 365: Microsoft Office 365 integration.
	+ Open-source office: Open-source office software integration.
	+ Other: Please specify in the field below.
* **workspace_phone** (Optional): Enter the phone number of the coworking space.
* **workspace_email** (Optional): Enter the email address of the coworking space.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing accurate and relevant information will help our team better understand your needs and preferences.
