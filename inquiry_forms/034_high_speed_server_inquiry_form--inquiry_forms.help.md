# High Speed Server Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The High Speed Server Inquiry Form is designed to collect information from users about their server inquiry needs and preferences. This form will help us provide the best possible experience for the user.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select the correct server type that best describes your needs.
3. If you selected "Other" for server type, please enter a brief description in the "Other" field.
4. Enter your server count and budget range.
5. Select the frequency of your budget.
6. If you selected "Other" for budget frequency, please enter a brief description.
7. Enter your contact information, including name, email, phone number, and address.
8. Provide any additional information about your needs and preferences.
9. Select the lead status.
10. If you selected "Other" for lead status, please enter a brief description.
11. Add any additional notes or comments.
12. Review your form submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data 1**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional information about your server inquiry.
* **Server Type**: Select the type of server that best describes your needs from the options provided.
* **Other**: If "Other" is selected, enter a brief description of your server type.
* **Server Count**: Enter the number of servers you need.
* **Budget Range**: Enter your budget range for the servers.
* **Budget Frequency**: Select the frequency of your budget from the options provided.
* **Other**: If "Other" is selected, enter a brief description of your budget frequency.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your contact information.
* **Company Name**: Enter your company name.
* **Company URL**: Enter your company URL.
* **Company Address**: Enter your company address.
* **Company City**: Enter your city.
* **Company State**: Enter your state.
* **Company Country**: Enter your country.
* **Server Specs**: Enter any additional information about your server specifications.
* **Other Specs**: If "Other Specs" is selected, enter a brief description of your server specs.
* **Additional Info**: Provide any additional information about your needs and preferences.
* **Additional Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your submission.
* **Lead Status**: Select the status of your lead from the options provided.
* **Other**: If "Other" is selected, enter a brief description of your lead status.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your submission.
* **Submitted**: This field is automatically populated with the date and time of your submission.
