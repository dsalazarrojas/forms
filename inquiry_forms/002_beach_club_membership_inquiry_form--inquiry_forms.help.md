# Beach Club Membership Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Beach Club Membership Inquiry Form is designed to collect information from potential members to determine the best fit for their membership needs. This form serves as an initial point of contact for those interested in joining the club.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the personal information section, including your name, email address, and phone number. This will help us contact you directly and provide you with the most relevant information about the club.
2. In the membership preferences section, select the type of membership that suits your needs best. This will help us tailor our response to your requirements.
3. Indicate the number of family members you wish to include in your membership, if applicable.
4. Choose your preferred method of contact (email, phone call, or text message).
5. Let us know how you heard about the beach club.
6. Select all areas of interest that apply to you.
7. If you have any questions or comments, you can add them in the final section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Your complete name, including first and last name.
* **Email Address**: A valid email address for us to contact you with updates and information about the club.
* **Phone Number**: A phone number where we can contact you directly.
* **Mailing Address**: If you wish to provide your mailing address for further communication, please enter it in this field. (This field is optional.)
* **Membership Type Interest**:
	+ **Individual Membership**: A standard membership for individuals.
	+ **Family Membership**: A membership for you and your family.
	+ **Junior Membership (Under 30)**: A membership for individuals under 30 years old.
	+ **Seasonal Membership**: A membership for seasonal access to the club facilities.
	+ **Corporate Membership**: A membership for corporate groups or teams.
* **Number of Family Members**: If you are applying for family membership, please enter the number of family members you wish to include.
* **Preferred Method of Contact**: Select how you would like to be contacted by the club.
	+ **Email**: We will contact you via email.
	+ **Phone Call**: We will contact you via phone call.
	+ **Text Message**: We will contact you via text message.
* **How did you hear about us?**:
	+ **Word of Mouth**: You were told about us by someone.
	+ **Social Media**: You found us on social media.
	+ **Local Advertisement**: You saw one of our local advertisements.
	+ **Referral from Member**: A member referred you to our club.
	+ **Other**: Please specify.
* **Areas of Interest**:
	+ **Private Beach Access**: You are interested in accessing the club's private beach.
	+ **Swimming Pool**: You are interested in using the club's swimming pool.
	+ **Fitness Center**: You are interested in the club's fitness center.
	+ **Dining and Social Events**: You are interested in attending social events and dining at the club.
	+ **Tennis Courts**: You are interested in using the club's tennis courts.
	+ **Youth Programs**: You are interested in youth programs or activities offered by the club.
* **Questions or Comments**: If you have any questions or comments, please enter them here.

Note: This help guide is intended to be a general guide and might need adjustments to fit specific needs and requirements of your organization. Make sure to review and update it regularly.
