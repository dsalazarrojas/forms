# Gas Selection Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gas Selection Contact Form is a form designed for users to submit inquiries and messages related to gas-related topics. This form can be used to ask questions or seek information on gas selection, pricing, or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `first_name` field.
2. Enter your last name in the `last_name` field.
3. Provide your email address in the `email` field. Please use the format `name@domain.com`.
4. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field.
5. Select the type of gas you are inquiring about from the `gas_type` field.
6. Select the type of gas service you are inquiring about from the `gas_service` field.
7. Indicate the intended usage of the gas from the `gas_usage` field. You can select multiple options if applicable.
8. Choose the type of inquiry you are making from the `inquiry_type` field.
9. Provide any additional message or information in the `message` field.
10. The `form_submission_id` field is automatically populated by the system and should not be filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### first_name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
### last_name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
### email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address in the format `name@domain.com`.
### phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
### gas_type
* **Gas Type** (`gas_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of gas you are inquiring about.
	+ **Natural Gas**
	+ **Propane Gas**
	+ **Other**
### gas_service
* **Gas Service** (`gas_service`, select_one, optional): Select the type of gas service you are inquiring about.
	+ **Gas Suppliers**
	+ **Gas Providers**
	+ **Other**
### gas_usage
* **Gas Usage** (`gas_usage`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the intended usage of the gas.
	+ **Residential**
	+ **Commercial**
	+ **Industrial**
### inquiry_type
* **Inquiry Type** (`inquiry_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of inquiry you are making.
	+ **Gas Inquiry**
	+ **Pricing Inquiry**
	+ **Service Inquiry**
### message
* **Message** (`message`, note, optional): Provide any additional message or information.
### form_submission_id
* **Form Submission ID** (`form_submission_id`, text, optional): This field is automatically populated by the system and should not be filled out.

## Tips
* All fields are optional, and you can fill out as many or as few fields as you need.
* If you need help or have questions, feel free to ask our staff, and we'll do our best to assist you.
* Please note that this form is intended for general inquiries and may not cover all possible gas-related topics. If you have specific questions or concerns, it's best to reach out to your gas supplier or provider directly.
