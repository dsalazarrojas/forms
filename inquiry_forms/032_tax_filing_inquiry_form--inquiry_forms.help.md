# Tax Filing Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tax Filing Inquiry Form is designed to collect user information and inquiry details to facilitate a response from our support team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, including your first and last name.
2. Enter your email address to allow us to contact you.
3. Provide your phone number if you would like to be contacted via phone.
4. Share your address if you would like to receive mail.
5. Select the type of inquiry you are submitting (e.g., Tax Preparation, Tax Review, Tax Audit, Tax Filing).
6. Write a brief message describing your inquiry.
7. If applicable, select the tax year related to your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (form_first_name)**: Your first name will be used to identify you in our records.
* **Last Name (form_last_name)**: Your last name will be used to identify you in our records.
* **Email (form_email)**: This is where we will contact you regarding your inquiry. Please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Phone (form_phone)**: If you would like to receive a call from us, please provide your phone number.
* **Address (form_address)**: If you would like to receive mail or other physical contact from us, please provide your address.
* **Inquiry Type (form_inquiry_type)**: Select the type of inquiry you are submitting (Tax Preparation, Tax Review, Tax Audit, Tax Filing).
* **Inquiry Message (form_inquiry_message)**: Briefly describe your inquiry or question.
* **Tax Year (form_tax_year)**: If applicable, please select the tax year related to your inquiry.
