# Ultherapy Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ultherapy Inquiry Form is designed to collect information from users inquiring about Ultherapy treatments. This form is used to gather details about the user's inquiry, such as their name, contact preferences, and preferred method of contact.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form fields with the required and optional information requested.
2. Ensure you provide your First Name and Last Name if they are required.
3. Enter your Email address if you'd like to be contacted through email.
4. Enter your Phone number if you'd like to be contacted through phone.
5. Select your Preferred Contact Method (e.g., Phone, Email, Text Message).
6. Choose your Preferred Contact Time (if applicable).
7. If inquiring about Ultherapy Treatment, select this option. Otherwise, provide details in the Other (please specify) field.
8. Enter any additional details about your inquiry in the Message field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. First Name
*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
### 2. Last Name
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
### 3. Email
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address to receive correspondence from us.
### 4. Phone
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number to receive a call or text from us.
### 5. Inquiry Type
*   **Inquiry Type** (`inquiry_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the reason for your inquiry (e.g., Ultherapy Treatment).
### 6. Inquiry Details
*   **Inquiry Details** (`inquiry_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional information about your inquiry.
### 7. Other (please specify)
*   **Other (please specify)** (`other_inquiry_type`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any other reason for your inquiry.
### 8. Preferred Contact Method
*   **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the method of contact you prefer (e.g., Phone, Email, Text Message).
### 9. Preferred Contact Time
*   **Preferred Contact Time** (`preferred_contact_time`, `time`, required/optional): Choose a time of day for us to contact you (if applicable).
### 10. Clinic Location
*   **Clinic Location** (`clinic_location`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the location where you would like to receive service (e.g., New York, Los Angeles, Chicago).
### 11. Message
*   **Message** (`message`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief message describing your inquiry.
## Tips
*   Be sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we can assist you with your inquiry.
*   If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact us for assistance.
