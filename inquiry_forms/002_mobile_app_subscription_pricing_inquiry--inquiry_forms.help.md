# Mobile App Subscription Pricing Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and inquiry about the mobile app subscription pricing from end-users. It is intended for internal use and should not be filled out by external parties without explicit request from our team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select all that apply from the options in "Pricing Feedback" section.
2. Choose your preferred billing cycle in "Billing Preferences" section.
3. Select the values that are important to you when choosing a subscription in "Value Perceptions" section.
4. Select the price point that you would be willing to pay for a subscription in "Price Point" section.
5. Choose your preferred payment method in "Payment Methods" section.
6. If you want to be contacted for more information, please enter your contact email in "Contact Info" section.
7. Please enter your billing address in "Billing Address" section.
8. Select your preferred billing method in "Billing Method" section.
9. Choose your billing frequency in "Billing Frequency" section.
10. Select your billing status in "Billing Status" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How do you feel about the current pricing of the app?** (`Pricing Feedback`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Select all that apply from the options: Free, Paid with a free trial, Paid subscription, Other (please specify)
* **What are your billing preferences?** (`Billing Preferences`, select_one, optional)
	+ Choose your preferred billing cycle: Monthly, Quarterly, Annually
* **How important is value to you when choosing a subscription?** (`Value Perceptions`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Select all that apply from the options: Cost, Functionality, Support, User experience
* **What price point would you be willing to pay for a subscription?** (`Price Point`, number, optional)
	+ Enter a price point that you would be willing to pay for a subscription
* **Which payment methods do you prefer?** (`Payment Methods`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Select all that apply from the options: Credit card, PayPal, Bank transfer
* **Contact Info?** (`Contact Info`, email, optional)
	+ If you want to be contacted for more information, enter your email address
* **Billing Address** (`Billing Address`, text, optional)
	+ Enter your billing address
* **Billing Method** (`Billing Method`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select your preferred billing method: One-time payment, Subscription
* **Billing Frequency** (`Billing Frequency`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Select all that apply from the options: Monthly, Quarterly, Annually
* **Billing Status** (`Billing Status`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select your billing status: Trial, Active, Inactive
