# travel_agent_business_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The travel agent business inquiry form is intended for travel agents to inquire about business opportunities with our company. Please use this form to capture relevant information about your business and your specific inquiry.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your travel agent name in the "travel agent name" field.
2. Provide your travel agent contact information in the "travel agent phone" and "travel agent email" fields.
3. Select all relevant business inquiries in the "travel agent business inquiries" field. (e.g., Hotel, Flight, Car Rental)
4. Select your available business hours in the "travel agent business hours" field.
5. Enter any additional comments or notes about your business in the "travel agent note" field.
6. Enter the date of travel for the inquiry in the "date of travel" field.
7. Enter the time of travel for the inquiry in the "time of travel" field.
8. Enter any additional comments or notes about the travel agent in the "travel agent additional comments" field.
9. Enter the agreed price for the business inquiry in the "travel agent agreed price" field.
10. Select the currency of the agreed price in the "travel agent agreed price currency" field.
11. Enter the agreed price symbol in the "travel agent agreed price symbol" field.
12. Enter the inquired price for the business inquiry in the "travel agent inquired price" field.
13. Select the currency of the inquired price in the "travel agent inquired price currency" field.
14. Enter the inquired price symbol in the "travel agent inquired price symbol" field.
15. Enter any notes about the agreed price in the "travel agent agreed price note" field.
16. Enter any notes about the inquired price in the "travel agent inquired price note" field.
17. Enter any additional notes about the agreed price in the "travel agent agreed price note 2" field.
18. Enter any additional notes about the inquired price in the "travel agent inquired price note 2" field.
19. Enter any additional notes about the agreed price in the "travel agent agreed price note 3" field.
20. Enter any additional notes about the inquired price in the "travel agent inquired price note 3" field.
21. Enter any additional notes about the agreed price in the "travel agent agreed price note 4" field.
22. Enter any additional notes about the inquired price in the "travel agent inquired price note 4" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Agent Name**: Enter your travel agent name.
* **Travel Agent Phone**: Enter your travel agent phone number.
* **Travel Agent Email**: Enter your travel agent email address.
* **Travel Agent Business Inquiries**: Select all relevant business inquiries (e.g., Hotel, Flight, Car Rental).
* **Travel Agent Business Hours**: Select your available business hours (e.g., Mon, Tue, Wed).
* **Travel Agent Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your business.
* **Date of Travel**: Enter the date of travel for the inquiry.
* **Time of Travel**: Enter the time of travel for the inquiry.
* **Travel Agent Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the travel agent.
* **Travel Agent Agreed Price**: Enter the agreed price for the business inquiry.
* **Travel Agent Agreed Price Currency**: Select the currency of the agreed price (e.g., USD, EUR, CNY).
* **Travel Agent Agreed Price Symbol**: Enter the agreed price symbol.
* **Travel Agent Inquired Price**: Enter the inquired price for the business inquiry.
* **Travel Agent Inquired Price Currency**: Select the currency of the inquired price (e.g., USD, EUR, CNY).
* **Travel Agent Inquired Price Symbol**: Enter the inquired price symbol.
* **Travel Agent Agreed Price Note**: Enter any notes about the agreed price.
* **Travel Agent Inquired Price Note**: Enter any notes about the inquired price.
* **Travel Agent Agreed Price Note 2**: Enter any additional notes about the agreed price.
* **Travel Agent Inquired Price Note 2**: Enter any additional notes about the inquired price.
* **Travel Agent Agreed Price Note 3**: Enter any additional notes about the agreed price.
* **Travel Agent Inquired Price Note 3**: Enter any additional notes about the inquired price.
* **Travel Agent Agreed Price Note 4**: Enter any additional notes about the agreed price.
* **Travel Agent Inquired Price Note 4**: Enter any additional notes about the inquired price.

## Tips
* Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Only fill out the form if you have a specific business inquiry in mind.
* Do not fill out the form multiple times for the same business opportunity.
* Review each field carefully to ensure that all required information is provided.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
