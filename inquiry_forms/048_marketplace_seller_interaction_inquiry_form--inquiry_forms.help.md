# Marketplace Seller Interaction Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Marketplace Seller Interaction Inquiry Form is designed to collect information about interactions between buyers and sellers on a marketplace platform. This form helps in tracking and resolving potential issues or inquiries between buyers and sellers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out your name, as the buyer, in the **Buyer Name** field.
2. Enter the seller's name in the **Seller Name** field.
3. Enter the date of the inquiry in the **Inquiry Date** field (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
4. Optionally, provide your email address in the **Buyer Email** field to facilitate communication with the seller.
5. Write your message or concern in the **Buyer Message** field.
6. The **Seller Response** field is for the seller to acknowledge or respond to your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form** (`marketplace_seller_interaction_inquiry_form`, text, Optional): This is the main form section where you can enter any additional information related to your inquiry.
* **Buyer Name** (`form_1`, text, Optional): Enter your name as the buyer to identify yourself in the inquiry.
* **Seller Name** (`form_2`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the seller you are interacting with.
* **Inquiry Date** (`form_3`, date, Required): Enter the date of the inquiry in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-07-15).
* **Buyer Email** (`form_4`, email, Optional): Provide your email address to enable easy communication with the seller.
* **Seller Response** (`form_5`, text, Optional): The seller can acknowledge or respond to your inquiry in this field.
* **Buyer Message** (`form_6`, text, Optional): Enter your message or concern related to the interaction with the seller.

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth interaction with the seller.
* Use the **Inquiry Date** field to keep track of the date of the interaction.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, you can always add more information in the **Buyer Message** field.
* The seller will respond to your inquiry in the **Seller Response** field.
