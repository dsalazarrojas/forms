# Goalkeeper Program Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Goalkeeper Program Inquiry Form is designed to gather information from potential customers or partners interested in the Goalkeeper Program, a program likely focused on mentorship, education, or career development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your coach or academy details, including your name and any relevant qualifications or experience.
2. Provide your availability for coaching or mentorship sessions, including the dates and times you are available.
3. Share your experience and expertise in the relevant field.
4. Enter your contact details, including your email address and phone number.
5. Add any additional information you'd like to share about your services or qualifications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Coach Details (`coach_details`)
* **Coach Details** (`name`, `type: text`, required/optional): Enter your name and any relevant details about your coaching or mentorship services.

### 2. Academy Details (`academy_details`)
* **Academy Details** (`name`, `type: text`, required/optional): Enter the name and details about your academy or educational institution.

### 3. Availability (`availability`)
* **Availability** (`date`, `type: date`, required/optional): Enter the dates and times you are available for coaching or mentorship sessions.

### 4. Experience (`experience`)
* **Experience** (`name`, `type: text`, required/optional): Share your experience and expertise in the relevant field.

### 5. Contact Details (`contact_details`)
* **Contact Details** (`name`, `type: text`, required/optional): Enter your email address and phone number so we can contact you.

### 6. Additional Info (`additional_info`)
* **Additional Info** (`name`, `type: text`, required/optional): Add any additional information about your services or qualifications.
