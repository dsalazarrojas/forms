# Content Services Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients interested in our content services, such as blog management, SEO content audit, social media strategy, email marketing campaigns, e-book/whitepaper production, and website copywriting. By filling out this form, you will help us understand your business needs and preferences, which will enable us to provide you with tailored content services that meet your goals and budget.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked with an `*`.
2. Select your industry category from the drop-down menu.
3. Provide your business name, email address, and desired start date for your content project.
4. Specify the services you are interested in and your estimated monthly budget for the project.
5. Share your biggest content challenge you are currently facing.
6. Finally, let us know how you found out about our agency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Enter your company's name.
* **Industry** (`industry_category`, `select_one`, required): Select the category that best describes your industry. Options include:
	+ Technology/SaaS
	+ Healthcare/Medical
	+ Finance/Legal
	+ E-commerce/Retail
	+ Education/Non-profit
	+ Other (specify below)
* **Company Website** (`website_url`, `text`, optional): If you have a website, enter its URL.
* **Your Full Name** (`contact_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Business Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your company email address.
* **Services Interested In** (`services_requested`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the services you are interested in from the list. Options include:
	+ Monthly Blog Management
	+ SEO Content Audit
	+ Social Media Strategy
	+ Email Marketing Campaigns
	+ E-book/Whitepaper Production
	+ Website Copywriting
* **Estimated Monthly Budget (USD)** (`estimated_monthly_budget`, `select_one`, required): Choose your estimated monthly budget for the project. Options include:
	+ 1,000 - 2,500
	+ 2,500 - 5,000
	+ 5,000 - 10,000
	+ 10,000 plus
* **Target Start Date** (`desired_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you would like to begin work on your content project.
* **Biggest Content Challenge** (`current_biggest_challenge`, `text`, required): Describe your biggest content challenge.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_did_you_find_us`, `select_one`, optional): Let us know how you found out about our agency. Options include:
	+ Google Search
	+ LinkedIn
	+ Referral/Word of mouth
	+ Podcast/Webinar
	+ Industry Event
