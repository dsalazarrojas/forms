Storage Service Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users about their storage service inquiries. The form collects details about the user's storage needs and preferences, including contact method, storage location, and inquiry description.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  In the **Storage Service Inquiry** field, provide a brief description of your storage service inquiry.
2.  Select your preferred **Contact Method** from the options provided.
3.  Choose the **Storage Location** where you would like to store your items from the available options.
4.  Provide additional details in the **Inquiry Description** field to help our team better understand your requirements.
5.  If you have any specific **Storage Options**, enter them in the designated field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Storage Service Inquiry**:
    +   A short description of your storage service inquiry.
*   **Contact Method** (Email, Phone, Chat):
    +   How would you like to be contacted to discuss your storage inquiry?
*   **Storage Location** (Downtown, Uptown, Eastside):
    +   Where would you like to store your items? Choose one or more from the available options.
*   **Inquiry Description**:
    +   Provide more details about your storage service inquiry to help our team better understand your requirements.
*   **Storage Options**:
    +   Enter any additional storage options or details you would like to specify.

## Tips
*   Make sure to provide a clear and concise description of your storage service inquiry.
*   Choose your preferred contact method to ensure a timely response to your inquiry.
*   Selecting multiple storage locations will ensure that your items are stored in the desired location.
*   Providing detailed inquiry description will help our team better understand your requirements.
*   Specify any additional storage options or details to help our team tailor their response to your needs.
