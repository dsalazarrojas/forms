# Video Production Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users to inquire about video production services. It's essential to have all the necessary details to ensure that your video project is completed efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Project Title** field with a brief description of your video project.
2. Provide a detailed **Project Description** in the **Project Description** field.
3. Select the **Production Services** required for your project from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter the **Contact Name** of the person responsible for your project.
5. Enter your **Email** address so we can reach out to you for further details.
6. Add your **Phone** number in case we need to contact you directly.
7. Enter your **Address** for us to send any necessary documents or information.
8. Set a realistic **Budget** for your project.
9. Specify the **Deadline** for your project completion.
10. Select the **Delivery Type** required for your project from the dropdown menu.
11. Choose the **Files** that you would like to include with your project from the dropdown menu.
12. Select the additional **File Uplads** you would like to include with your project from the dropdown menu.
13. Provide any additional **Comments** about your project.
14. Select the **Assigned To** team member or department that will handle your project.
15. Select the **Status** of your project (e.g., New, In Progress, Completed).
16. Set the **Follow Up** date for your project.
17. Select the **Follow Up By** team member or department that will follow up on your project.
18. Choose the **Priority** level of your project (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
19. Enter the **Assigned Tool** or software used for your project.
20. Enter the **Assigned User** responsible for handling your project.
21. Enter the **Created By** user who initiated your project.
22. Enter the **Updated By** user who last updated your project.
23. Enter the **Created At** date and time when your project was initiated.
24. Enter the **Updated At** date and time when your project was last updated.
25. Enter the **Deleted At** date and time when your project was deleted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Title** (Required): Enter a brief title of your video project.
* **Project Description** (Optional): Provide a detailed description of your project.
* **Production Services** (Required): Select the services required for your video production, such as animation, editing, or 3D modeling.
* **Contact Name** (Optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for your project.
* **Email** (Optional): Enter your email address so we can reach out to you for further details.
* **Phone** (Optional): Add your phone number in case we need to contact you directly.
* **Address** (Optional): Enter your address for us to send any necessary documents or information.
* **Budget** (Optional): Set a realistic budget for your project.
* **Deadline** (Optional): Specify the deadline for your project completion.
* **Delivery Type** (Required): Select the required delivery type, such as online or offline.
* **Files** (Required): Choose the files you would like to include with your project from the dropdown menu.
* **File Uplads** (Optional): Select additional file uploads you would like to include with your project.
* **Comments** (Optional): Provide any additional comments about your project.
* **Assigned To** (Required): Select the team member or department that will handle your project.
* **Status** (Required): Set the status of your project (e.g., New, In Progress, Completed).
* **Follow Up** (Optional): Set the follow-up date for your project.
* **Follow Up By** (Required): Select the team member or department that will follow up on your project.
* **Priority** (Required): Choose the priority level of your project (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
* **Assigned Tool** (Optional): Enter the software or tool used for your project.
* **Assigned User** (Optional): Enter the user responsible for handling your project.
* **Created By** (Optional): Enter the user who initiated your project.
* **Updated By** (Optional): Enter the user who last updated your project.
* **Created At** (Optional): Enter the date and time when your project was initiated.
* **Updated At** (Optional): Enter the date and time when your project was last updated.
* **Deleted At** (Optional): Enter the date and time when your project was deleted.
