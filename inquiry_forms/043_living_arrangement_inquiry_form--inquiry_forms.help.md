# Living Arrangement Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Living Arrangement Inquiry Form is a tool used to gather information about your living arrangement preferences and needs. This form will help us understand what type of living arrangement is most suitable for you, considering your lifestyle, budget, and personal circumstances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Select the correct options for each question.
3. Fill out the form completely, as incomplete submissions may lead to incorrect analysis.
4. If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please do not hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Living Arrangement Inquiry** (`living_arrangement_inquiry`):
	+ This field is an open-ended question where you can describe your current living arrangement preferences and needs.
* **What do you need?** (`what_do_you_need`):
	+ Select all the options that apply to you. This will help us understand what services you are looking for in a living arrangement.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Preferred Neighbourhood** (`preferred_neighbourhood`):
	+ Please enter the name of your preferred neighbourhood.
* **Budget** (`budget`):
	+ Enter your available budget for the living arrangement.
	+ This field is not visible if the field is not required.
* **Duration of Stay** (`living_arrangement_inquiry_form_5`):
	+ Please enter the duration of your stay in the desired living arrangement.
* **Date of Availability** (`living_arrangement_inquiry_form_6`):
	+ Enter the date you would like to move in.
* **What is your income?** (`what_is_your_income`):
	+ Select all the options that apply to your income.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **What is your employment status?** (`what_is_your_employment_status`):
	+ Select your current employment status.
	+ Options: Active, Inactive
* **How many rooms do you need?** (`how_many_rooms`):
	+ Enter the number of rooms you are looking for in the living arrangement.
* **What services do you require?** (`what_services_do_you_require`):
	+ Select all the options that apply to your services requirements.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Available Funds** (`available_funds`):
	+ Enter your available funds for the living arrangement.
* **How many people are in your family?** (`how_many_people`):
	+ Enter the number of people in your family.
* **Preferred Living Arrangement** (`preferred_living_arrangement`):
	+ Select your preferred type of living arrangement.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Preferred Type** (`preferred_type`):
	+ Select your preferred type of living arrangement.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **What are your family members?** (`what_are_your_family_members`):
	+ Select all the options that apply to your family members.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Other Information** (`living_arrangement_inquiry_form_17`):
	+ Please enter any other relevant information about your living arrangement preferences.
* **Additional Comments** (`living_arrangement_inquiry_form_18`):
	+ Please enter any additional comments or questions you have about the living arrangement.
