# inclusion_connection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The inclusion_connection_form is a tool for users to create an inclusion connection between programs, enabling seamless communication and collaboration among stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Program Name` field with the name of the program you are creating an inclusion connection for.
2. Enter your `Full Name` and `Email` to ensure that the connection is associated with the correct user.
3. Provide your `Phone Number` for contact purposes.
4. Select the relevant `Service Provider` from the available options to specify the service being connected.
5. If needed, enter your `Support Request` to provide context for the inclusion connection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, required/optional): Enter the name of the program you are creating an inclusion connection for.
* **Full Name** (`name`, required/optional): Fill in your full name to associate this connection with you as a user.
* **Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, required/optional): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Service Provider** (`service_provider`, required): Select the service provider that this connection is related to.
* **Support Request** (`support_request`, required/optional): Enter any support requests or context related to this inclusion connection.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your input for accuracy to avoid any errors.
- Use the correct format for your email address when filling in the `Email` field.
- For the `Service Provider` field, select the option that best matches the service you are connecting to.
