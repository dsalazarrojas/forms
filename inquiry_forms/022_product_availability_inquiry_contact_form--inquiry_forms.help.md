# Product Availability Inquiry Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers regarding product availability.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out all required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2.  Enter your contact details and preferred contact method (phone, email, or WhatsApp).
3.  Provide any additional comments or messages about the product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): Enter your email address.
    *   **Type:** Email
    *   **Required:** No
*   **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter your name.
    *   **Type:** Text
    *   **Required:** No
*   **Product ID** (`product_id`): Enter the product ID number.
    *   **Type:** Text
    *   **Required:** No
*   **Quantity** (`quantity`): Enter the quantity you are inquiring about.
    *   **Type:** Number
    *   **Required:** No
*   **Availability** (`availability_status`): Select the availability status of the product (Yes, No, or Other).
    *   **Type:** Select One
    *   **Required:** No
*   **Availability Label** (`availability_status_label`): Enter a label for the availability status.
    *   **Type:** Text
    *   **Required:** No
*   **Availability Status 2** (`availability_status_2`): Select the availability status of the product (Available, Out of Stock, Pre-Order, or Discontinued).
    *   **Type:** Select One
    *   **Required:** No
*   **Availability Status Label 2** (`availability_status_label_2`): Enter a label for the availability status.
    *   **Type:** Text
    *   **Required:** No
*   **Availability Status Options 2** (`availability_status_options_2`): Enter options for the availability status.
    *   **Type:** Text
    *   **Required:** No
*   **Product Description** (`product_description`): Enter a description of the product.

    *   **Required:** No
