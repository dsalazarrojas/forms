# Structured Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Structured Inquiry Form is a tool to collect and prioritize inquiries from users. It helps us understand the issue, its priority, and category to provide the best possible resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields to provide us with as much information as possible.
2. Ensure that you provide a clear and concise Description of the issue.
3. Choose the correct Category and Priority for your inquiry.
4. Select the Due Date by which you would like to see this issue resolved.
5. If applicable, choose the Status of your issue.
6. Submit the form once you have filled in all the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name (Contact Name)** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your name and contact information for us to get back to you.
* **Description (Description)** (`description`, text, optional): Please provide a brief and clear description of the issue you're experiencing.
* **Phone (Phone)** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you to gather more information.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where we can send updates on your issue.
* **Priority (Priority)** (`priority`, select_one, optional): Choose the level of urgency for your issue:
	+ High: Immediate attention is required.
	+ Medium: Normal priority issue.
	+ Low: Less urgent issue.
	+ Urgent: Extremely urgent issue.
* **Category (Category)** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select the category for your issue:
	+ Support: General support issues.
	+ Sales: Sales-related issues.
	+ Marketing: Marketing-related issues.
	+ Other: Other category.
* **Due Date (Due Date)** (`due_date`, date, optional): Choose the date by which you'd like to see this issue resolved.
* **Status (Status)** (`status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the current status of your issue:
	+ In Progress: Issue is being worked on.
	+ Resolved: Issue is fully resolved.
	+ Reopened: Issue has reopened.
	+ Cancelled: Issue has been cancelled.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you only submit one inquiry per form.
* If you have multiple issues, submit separate forms for each one.
* Keep your Description clear and concise for our understanding.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, don't hesitate to contact us.
