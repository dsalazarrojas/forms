# Flight Details Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about flights, including the type of flight, travel dates, number of travelers, passenger details, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill in the form**: Start by selecting the type of flight you are inquiring about from the options provided.
2. **Select travel dates**: Choose the dates you are planning to travel.
3. **Specify number of travelers**: Enter the number of people you are planning to travel with.
4. **Add passenger details**: Provide the names and IDs of the passengers.
5. **Add flight numbers**: Select any applicable flight numbers you have.
6. **Specify seat requirements**: Enter the number of seats you need.
7. **Choose travel class**: Select the class of travel you require (Economy, Business, or First Class).
8. **Add any special requests**: If you have any specific requirements, enter them in the text field.
9. **Confirm**: Check the box to confirm that the information provided is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of flight is this?**: Please select the type of flight you are inquiring about from the options provided.
* **When were you planning to travel?**: Enter the dates you are planning to travel.
* **How many travelers?**: Enter the number of people you are traveling with.
* **Passenger Names and IDs**: Please provide the names and IDs of all passengers.
* **Are there any special requests?**: If you have any specific requirements, enter them in the text field.
* **Which flight numbers do you have?**: Select the flight numbers you have.
* **How many seats do you need?**: Enter the number of seats you require.
* **What is the travel class?**: Please select the class of travel you require (Economy, Business, or First Class).
* **Do you need assistance with boarding?**: If you need help with boarding, select the option.
* **Do you have any special requirements?**: If you have any special requirements, enter them in the text field.
* **Add any flight details**: If you have any additional flight details, please provide them in the text field.
* **Confirm**: This is a confirmation check to ensure that the information provided is accurate.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your inquiry is processed correctly.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
