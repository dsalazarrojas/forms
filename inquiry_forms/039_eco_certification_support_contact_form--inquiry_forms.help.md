# Eco Certification Support Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and support inquiries related to eco-certification, allowing users to seek help and guidance on the certification process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your Last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter a detailed Message describing your support inquiry in the "Message" field.
6. Select the type of inquiry you are making in the "Inquiry type" field. Choose from Eco-Certification, General, or Other.
7. Read and agree to the Terms of use in the "Agree to terms of use" field by selecting "Yes".
8. If you want to view the Terms of use, you can copy and paste it into the "Terms of use" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name (First name)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name (Last name)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter a valid email address associated with your account or contact information.
* **Phone (Phone)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Message (Message)**: Describe your support inquiry in a few sentences.
* **Inquiry type (Inquiry type)**: Choose from:
	+ Eco-Certification: Select this if you are seeking help or information related to eco-certification.
	+ General: Select this if you have a general question or inquiry not related to eco-certification.
	+ Other: Select this for any other type of inquiry.
* **Agree to terms of use (Agree to terms of use)**: Read and agree to the Terms of use by selecting "Yes".
* **Terms of use (Terms of use)**: View and copy the Terms of use text into this field (optional).

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information in each field.
- If you have any issues with the form, contact the support team for assistance.
- The form is designed to be simple and easy to fill out; if you are unsure about any field, refer back to this help guide.
