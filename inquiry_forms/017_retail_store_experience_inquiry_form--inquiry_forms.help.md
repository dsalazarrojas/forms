# Retail Store Experience Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience in a retail store. It helps the store management to understand their customers' needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the category of the store you visited.
2. Rate the quality of the product you interacted with.
3. Rate the knowledge of the store employees.
4. Rate the speed and quality of the service you received.
5. Rate the clarity of the store layout.
6. Rate the store atmosphere.
7. Rate the friendliness of the store employees.
8. Provide an overall rating of your experience.
9. Add any additional comments about your visit.
10. Provide your contact information (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was the category of the store you visited?** (`category`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Please select the category that best describes the type of store you visited.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Please enter the name of the employee who helped you during your visit.
* **What type of product did you visit?** (`product_type`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Please select the type of product you interacted with during your visit.
* **How would you rate the quality of the product?** (`product_quality`, `number`, required: false)
	+ Rate the quality of the product on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
* **How would you rate the staff knowledge?** (`staff_knowledge`, `number`, required: false)
	+ Rate the knowledge of the store employees on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
* **How would you rate the service speed?** (`service_speed`, `number`, required: false)
	+ Rate the speed of the service you received on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is slow and 5 is fast.
* **How would you rate the service quality?** (`service_quality`, `number`, required: false)
	+ Rate the quality of the service you received on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
* **How clear was the store layout?** (`store_clarity`, `number`, required: false)
	+ Rate the clarity of the store layout on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is unclear and 5 is clear.
* **How would you rate the store atmosphere?** (`store_atmosphere`, `number`, required: false)
	+ Rate the store atmosphere on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is unpleasant and 5 is pleasant.
* **How friendly were the employees?** (`employee_friendly`, `number`, required: false)
	+ Rate the friendliness of the store employees on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is unfriendly and 5 is friendly.
* **Overall experience?** (`overall_experience`, `number`, required: false)
	+ Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Please add any additional comments or suggestions about your visit.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Please enter your name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required: false)
	+ Please enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Please enter your phone number.
* **Store Location** (`store_location`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Please enter the location of the store you visited.
* **Store Hours** (`store_hours`, `time`, required: false)
	+ Please enter the store hours of operation.

## Tips

* Be honest and objective in your ratings.
* Provide as much detail as possible in your additional comments.
* Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your time.
