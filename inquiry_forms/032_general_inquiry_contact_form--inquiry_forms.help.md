# General Inquiry Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The General Inquiry Contact Form is a tool that allows users to send messages or ask questions to the support team. It is designed to be a quick and easy way to get in touch with us for any general inquiries or concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, such as your Email and Message.
2. Select the relevant Category for your inquiry.
3. Choose whether you are a Prophet or not.
4. Select a tool to be assigned to your inquiry, if needed.
5. Submit the form once you have filled in all the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input**: This is a free text area where you can provide any additional information about your inquiry.
* **Email**: This is your email address that we will use to contact you. Please make sure it is correct.
* **Phone**: This is your phone number, which we will use to contact you if needed.
* **Message**: This is the main content of your inquiry, where you can describe your question or issue.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select a tool that you would like to be assigned to your inquiry. This will help us to better understand your issue and provide you with a more efficient resolution.
* **Category**: This is the category under which your inquiry will be listed. Please select the most relevant category for your issue.
* **Category**: This is another category selection field. It seems to be redundant with the Category field above, but it might have a different intention in the context of this form.
* **Prophets**: This field is used to indicate whether you are a Prophet or not. Please select 'Yes' if you are a Prophet, or 'No' if you are not.
* **Submitted At**: This field is not currently visible or used in the form.
* **Submitted Time**: This field is not currently visible or used in the form.
* **Submitted**: This field is not currently visible or used in the form.
* **Prophets**: It seems like this field was supposed to be a checkbox, but it's currently not visible or used in the form. Please disregard this field.
* **Description**: This field is a free text area where you can provide more details about your issue or question.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
* Use a clear and concise message to describe your issue or question.
* Select the correct category for your inquiry.
* Be honest about your Prophet status.
