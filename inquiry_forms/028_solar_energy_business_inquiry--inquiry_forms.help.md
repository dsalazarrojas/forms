# Solar Energy Business Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Solar Energy Business Inquiry form is designed to collect information from customers interested in learning more about our solar energy business. This form is intended for customers who want to get in touch with our team to discuss their solar energy needs or to inquire about our services. By providing us with their contact information, customers can help us to better understand their requirements and provide them with tailored solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Solar Energy Business Inquiry form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Customer Name** in the text field provided. This is a required field.
2. Provide your **Email** in the format of `username@example.com` or `yourname@example.com`. This will help us contact you with any updates or follow-ups.
3. Enter your **Phone** number. This is optional but recommended so we can contact you for further discussion.
4. Enter your **Address** in a clear and concise manner. This is optional but recommended if you have a specific location where you would like us to visit or contact you with a follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: A required field where you can enter your name.
	+ Enter your full name (first and last name).
* **Email**: A field where you can enter your email address.
	+ Use the format `username@example.com` or `yourname@example.com`.
* **Phone**: An optional field where you can enter your phone number.
	+ Enter your phone number with the format `123-456-7890` or `123 456 7890`.
* **Address**: An optional field where you can enter your address.
	+ Enter your address in a clear and concise manner, including street number, street name, and zip code.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your email address before submitting the form to ensure it is correct.
* If you have any attachments or documents you would like to send us, please contact us separately.
* Our team will contact you within 24-48 hours to discuss your inquiry and provide you with more information.
