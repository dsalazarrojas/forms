# Startup Launch Program Inquiry - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from startups who are interested in participating in the startup launch program. The form is used to collect data about the startup's start date, name, description, goals, industry, location, contact details, and other relevant information. By filling out this form, you will be providing essential data that will help the program's administrators to evaluate your startup and potentially offer support or resources to help you launch your business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the start date of your startup from the calendar picker.
2. Enter the name of your startup in the text field.
3. Provide a brief description of your startup in the text field.
4. Specify your startup's goals and objectives in the text field.
5. Select your startup's industry from the list of available options.
6. Enter your startup's location in the text field.
7. Provide your name and contact information in the text fields.
8. Enter your email address in the text field.
9. Enter your phone number in the text field.
10. Indicate whether your startup has participated in an accelerator program in the past by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown menu.
11. Select 'Yes' if you would like to be contacted for follow-up after submitting this form.
12. Indicate your preferred method of follow-up contact (e.g., email, phone call) by selecting 'Yes' from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Start Date
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date when your startup began operations.

### Start-up Name
* **Start-up-name** (`startup_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your startup.

### Start-up Description
* **Start-up-description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of your startup.

### Start-up Goals
* **Start-up-goals** (`goals`, `text`, required: false): Specify your startup's goals and objectives.

### Start-up Industry
* **Start-up-industry** (`industry`, `text`, required: false): Select your startup's industry from the list of available options.

### Start-up Location
* **Start-up-location** (`location`, `text`, required: false): Enter your startup's location.

### Start-up Contact Name
* **Start-up-contact-name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Provide your name and title (e.g., Founder, CEO).

### Start-up Email
* **Start-up-email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.

### Start-up Phone
* **Start-up-phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.

### Accelerator
* **Accelerator** (`accelerator`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate whether your startup has participated in an accelerator program in the past.

### Accelerator Program
* **Accelerator-program** (`accelerator_program`, `select_one`, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you would like to be considered for an accelerator program.

### Follow-up
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you would like to be contacted for follow-up after submitting this form.

### Follow-up Method
* **Follow-up-method** (`follow_up_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate your preferred method of follow-up contact (e.g., email, phone call).
