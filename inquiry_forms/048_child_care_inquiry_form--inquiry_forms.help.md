# Child Care Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about your child's care needs and preferences. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to help us understand your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your name and contact information.
2. Provide the child's name, age, and date of birth.
3. Select your preferred contact method (phone, email, or text message).
4. Specify the type of care needed (full-time, part-time, before-school, after-school, etc.).
5. Choose the start date and hours of care needed per week.
6. Indicate your schedule preference (e.g., Monday to Friday).
7. Describe your current child care situation and any special needs or interests.
8. Select specific programs you are interested in (e.g., Montessori, Reggio Emilia, etc.).
9. Enter your main question or inquiry.
10. Provide any budget considerations or flexibility.
11. Choose how you would like us to follow up with you (phone, email, or text message).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inquiry date** (id: 2): Enter the date you are inquiring about.
* **First name** (id: 3): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (id: 4): Enter your last name.
* **Email address** (id: 5): Enter your email address.
* **Phone number** (id: 6): Enter your phone number.
* **Preferred contact method** (id: 7): Select your preferred way to communicate with us (phone, email, or text message).
* **Child's name** (id: 8): Enter the child's name.
* **Child's age** (id: 9): Enter the child's age.
* **Child's date of birth** (id: 10): Enter the child's date of birth.
* **Do you have additional children?** (id: 11): Indicate if you have other children.
* **Ages of other children** (id: 12): If you have other children, enter their ages.
* **Type of care needed** (id: 13): Select all the types of care you require (full-time, part-time, before-school, etc.).
* **When do you need care to start?** (id: 14): Enter the start date of the care.
* **Hours of care needed per week** (id: 15): Enter the number of hours of care you need per week.
* **Preferred schedule** (id: 16): Describe your preferred schedule (e.g., Monday to Friday).
* **Current child care situation** (id: 17): Describe your current child care situation.
* **Special needs or specific interests** (id: 18): If your child has any special needs or interests, describe them here.
* **Specific programs you are interested in** (id: 19): Select specific programs you are interested in (e.g., Montessori, Reggio Emilia, etc.).
* **Main inquiry** (id: 20): Enter your main question or inquiry.
* **Budget considerations** (id: 21): Describe any budget considerations or flexibility.
* **Would you like us to follow up?** (id: 22): Indicate how you would like us to follow up with you (phone, email, or text message).
