# Customer Inquiry Status Login - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form allows customers to inquire about the status of their existing inquiries. You can use this form to check on the progress of your inquiries and get updates on any updates or changes made to them.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Customer Inquiry ID in the "inquiry_id" field.
2. Select the current status of your inquiry from the "current_status" dropdown menu.
3. Enter your Customer Name.
4. Enter your Customer Email Address.
5. Enter your Customer Phone Number.
6. Add any additional information or comments in the "additional_info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **inquiry_id**: Enter the ID number of your inquiry, which you can find in the original inquiry form or in your customer service records. (<type:text>, <required: false>)
* **current_status**: Select the current status of your inquiry from the following options: Open, In Progress, or Closed. (<type:select_one>, <required: false>)
* **name**: Enter your Customer Name as it appears on our records. (<type:text>, <required: false>)
* **email_address**: Enter your Customer Email Address as it appears on our records. (<type:email>, <required: false>)
* **phone_number**: Enter your Customer Phone Number as it appears on our records. (<type:text>, <required: false>)
* **additional_info**: If you have any additional information or comments about your inquiry, enter them here. (<type:note>, <required: false>)

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your inquiry status is updated correctly.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, contact our customer service team for assistance.
* You can also use this form to check on the status of multiple inquiries by filling out the "inquiry_id" field for each one.
