# Virtual Learning Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The virtual learning inquiry form is designed to gather information from students who are interested in taking online courses or programs. It is used to identify and address their needs, provide information on available courses, and facilitate communication between students and learning administrators.

## How to Complete This Form

This form has the following steps to complete:

1. Enter your Form ID.
2. Provide your Program name.
3. Enter your contact email address (if applicable).
4. Enter your contact phone number (if applicable).
5. Select the type of inquiry (Yes/No).
6. Provide your inquiry details.
7. Enter your inquiry message.
8. (Optional) Enter your Student name.
9. (Optional) Enter your Student email address.
10. (Optional) Enter your Student phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form ID** (`1`, `number`, required): Enter the ID of the form you are submitting.
* **Program name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the program you are inquiring about.
* **Contact Email** (`3`, `email`, false): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted about your inquiry.
* **Contact Phone** (`4`, `text`, false): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted about your inquiry.
* **Inquiry Type** (`5`, `select_one`, false): Select 'Yes' if you are inquiring about a program, 'No' if you are unsure about the program.
* **Inquiry Details** (`6`, `text`, required): Provide a clear and concise description of your inquiry.
* **Inquiry Message** (`7`, `text`, required): Enter a message about your inquiry.
* **Student Name** (`8`, `text`, false): Enter your name if you are a student submitting the form.
* **Student Email** (`9`, `email`, false): Enter your email address if you are a student submitting the form.
* **Student Phone** (`10`, `text`, false): Enter your phone number if you are a student submitting the form.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and clear information to help us better serve your needs.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for assistance.
* Please submit the form only once to avoid duplication of inquires.
