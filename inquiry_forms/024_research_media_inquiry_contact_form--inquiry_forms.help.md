# Research Media Inquiry Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from users interested in media research opportunities. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure that we can contact you for further discussion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of media you are inquiring about by selecting one or more options from the "What type of media are you inquiring about?" field.
2. Enter your contact information in the "What is your contact information?" field.
3. Indicate how you would like to be contacted for follow-up by selecting an option from the "How may we contact you for follow up?" field.
4. If you have any additional comments or information, you can add them in the "Is there any additional comments?" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What type of media are you inquiring about?**: This is a multiple-choice field where you can select one or more options that describe the type of media you are interested in. Please choose one or more from the list below:
  * Yes
  * No
- **What is your contact information?**: This field is for you to enter your contact information, such as your name, email address, or phone number. Please enter your contact information accurately.
- **How may we contact you for follow up?**: This is a single-choice field where you can select how you would like to be contacted for follow-up. Please select one of the options:
  * Yes
  * No
- **Is there any additional comments?**: This field is optional and allows you to add any additional comments or information that you would like to provide. Please use this field to provide any further details that are not covered in the previous fields. 

## Tips
* Ensure that you provide accurate contact information to ensure that we can contact you for further discussion.
* If you have any additional comments, please use the "Is there any additional comments?" field to provide further information.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
