# social media management inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The social media management inquiry form is a tool used to gather information from potential customers or clients about their needs and preferences for a particular service or product. This form helps to qualify leads, gather contact details, and understand their expected start date and time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of lead you are (or would like to be) - "New" or "Existing".
2. Provide your name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your contact number.
5. Provide a brief description of what you are looking for.
6. Choose where you heard about us - "Social Media", "Search Engine", "Referral", or "Other".
7. Select your preferred contact time - Monday to Sunday.
8. Select your preferred contact time in 24-hour format - "Morning (8-12)", "Afternoon (13-17)", "Evening (18-24)", or "Flexible".
9. Choose the type of lead you are (or would like to be) - "Sales", "Service", "Support", or "Other".
10. Enter your expected start date (if known).
11. Enter your expected start time (if known).
12. Select the services you need.
13. Finally, submit the form to send it to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Qualify lead**:
	+ Select "New" if you are a new customer or client.
	+ Select "Existing" if you are an existing customer or client.
* **Name**: Enter your full name for reference.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address for communication.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number for follow-up.
* **Message**: Provide a brief description of what you are looking for.
* **Lead Source**: Select where you heard about us - "Social Media", "Search Engine", "Referral", or "Other".
* **Preferred Contact Time**:
	+ Select your preferred contact time - Monday to Sunday.
* **Preferred Contact Time 24h**:
	+ Select your preferred contact time in 24-hour format - "Morning (8-12)", "Afternoon (13-17)", "Evening (18-24)", or "Flexible".
* **Lead Type**: Select the type of lead you are (or would like to be) - "Sales", "Service", "Support", or "Other".
* **Budget**: Enter your expected budget for the service (if known).
* **Expected Start Date**: Enter your expected start date (if known).
* **Expected Start Time**: Enter your expected start time (if known).
* **Services Needed**: Select the services you require.
* **Submit**: Click submit to send the form.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill all required fields.
- Be honest about where you heard about us.
- Choose your preferred contact time and date accurately.
- Select the services you really need.
- If you have any questions, contact us at [insert contact information].
