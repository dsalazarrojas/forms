# Research Lab Partnership Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for research organizations or individuals interested in partnering with our research lab. It helps us understand your needs and interests, enabling us to explore potential collaboration opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure your inquiry is successfully submitted, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **form title** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **organization** details.
3. Provide your **contact first name** and **contact last name**.
4. Enter your **email** address.
5. Describe your **research interests**.
6. Indicate the type of **collaboration** you are interested in (Partnership, Collaboration, or Research).
7. Add any **project details** you'd like to share.
8. Provide the name of the **project lead** (if applicable).
9. Specify your **department** and **university** (choose from the provided options).
10. Select your lab's **interests** (choose from the provided options, multiple selections allowed).
11. Enter your **contact title**, if applicable.
12. Enter your **contact email** and **contact phone** (if applicable).
13. Enter your department's **name**, **contact**, **phone**, **email**, and **website** (if applicable).
14. Add any **department notes**, if applicable.
15. Enter any **additional comments** you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form title**: A brief title summarizing your inquiry.
* **organization**: The name of your organization.
* **contact first name**: Your first name.
* **contact last name**: Your last name.
* **email**: Your email address.
* **research interests**: A description of your research interests.
* **potential partners**: Any other organizations or individuals you are interested in collaborating with.
* **collaboration type**: The type of collaboration you are interested in (Partnership, Collaboration, or Research).
* **project details**: Any specific project details you'd like to share.
* **project lead**: The name of the project lead (if applicable).
* **department**: The department you are associated with.
* **university**: The university you are affiliated with.
* **lab interests**: Your lab's research interests (multiple selections allowed).
* **contact title**: A title for your contact person (if applicable).
* **contact email**: Your contact email address.
* **contact phone**: Your contact phone number (if applicable).
* **department name**: The name of the department you are in.
* **department contact**: Your department contact person.
* **department phone**: The department's phone number (if applicable).
* **department email**: The department's email address.
* **department website**: The department's website (if applicable).
* **department address**: The department's address.
* **department notes**: Any additional comments about your department.
* **additional comments**: Any additional comments you'd like to share.
