# RF Shielding Product Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RF Shielding Product Inquiry Form is a tool used to collect information about potential customers' or internal teams' requirements for RF shielding products. This form is designed to be a quick and efficient way to gather necessary details for the sales team or engineering team to follow up with customers and provide the best possible solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the "Engineering Request" page. This is where you can provide a brief description of your project's specific needs.
2. On the "RF Shielding Specification" page, provide more details about the RF shielding products you are interested in, such as the type of material you are looking for (e.g., Aluminum, Copper, Silver, Mu Metal, PEEK).
3. On the "Shielding Materials" page, select the type of material you are interested in from the options listed.
4. On the "RF Shielding Thickness" page, enter the desired thickness of the shielding material. This is a required field.
5. On the "Shielding Area" page, enter the area that needs to be shielded. This is also a required field.
6. On the "Engineering Department" page, select the department that is working on the project (e.g., Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Software Engineering, Civil Engineering). If not sure, you can leave this field blank.
7. On the "Contact Email" page, enter the contact email address for follow-up communication. This is a required field.
8. On the "Contact Phone" page, enter your contact phone number, if applicable.
9. On the "Team Assignment" page, select the team(s) that will be working on the project (e.g., Electrical Team, Mechanical Team, Software Team, Civil Team). If not sure, you can leave this field blank.
10. On the "Notes" page, provide any additional comments or notes about your project.
11. On the "Follow-up Date" page, enter the date you would like to be followed up on. If not sure, you can leave this field blank.
12. On the "Follow-up Time" page, enter the time you would like to be followed up on. If not sure, you can leave this field blank.
13. On the "Follow-up Location" page, select the location(s) where you would like to be followed up on (e.g., Site 1, Site 2, Site 3). If not sure, you can leave this field blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Engineering Request**: A brief description of your project's specific needs.
* **RF Shielding Specification**: More details about the RF shielding products you are interested in.
* **Shielding Materials**: Select the type of material you are interested in (e.g., Aluminum, Copper, Silver, Mu Metal, PEEK).
* **RF Shielding Thickness**: The desired thickness of the shielding material (e.g., 0.001, 0.01, etc.).
* **Shielding Area**: The area that needs to be shielded.
* **Engineering Department**: The department that is working on the project (e.g., Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Software Engineering, Civil Engineering).
* **Contact Email**: The email address where you can be reached for follow-up communication.
* **Contact Phone**: Your contact phone number, if applicable.
* **Team Assignment**: The team(s) that will be working on the project (e.g., Electrical Team, Mechanical Team, Software Team, Civil Team).
* **Notes**: Any additional comments or notes about your project.
* **Follow-up Date**: The date you would like to be followed up on. Enter in the format MM/DD/YY or YYYY-MM-DD. 
* **Follow-up Time**: The time you would like to be followed up on.
* **Follow-up Location**: The location(s) where you would like to be followed up on.
