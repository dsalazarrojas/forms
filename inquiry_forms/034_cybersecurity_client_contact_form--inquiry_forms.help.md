# Cybersecurity Client Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cybersecurity Client Contact Form is designed to gather essential information from clients seeking assistance with cybersecurity services. This includes contact details, problem descriptions, and assigned team members. The collected information helps our team in addressing client concerns and providing swift support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client company or individual.
2. **Company Name**: Input the name of the client's company if applicable.
3. **Email**: Enter the client's email address for further communication.
4. **Phone**: Enter the client's phone number for potential follow-up calls.
5. **Contact Reason**: Briefly describe the purpose of the client's contact.
6. **Description**: Give a detailed description of the issue or concern.
7. **Assigned to (IT Consultant/Provider)**: Select the appropriate team member assigned to address the client's issue.
8. **Date**: Enter the date of the issue's occurrence or expected resolution.
9. **Follow-up Date**: Provide a date for follow-up communication if necessary.
10. **Status**: Select the status of the client's issue.
11. **Created**: This field is automatically populated and indicates when the record was created.
12. **Updated**: This field is automatically populated and indicates when the record was last updated.
13. **Note**: Include any additional notes related to the client's issue.
14. **Email Sent**: Indicate if an email has been sent to the client.
15. **Follow-up Sent**: Mark if follow-up communication has been sent to the client.
