# employment_law_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for inquiring about employment law-related issues, allowing you to provide detailed information and specific areas of concern regarding employment law.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form effectively, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your inquiry details in the "Inquiry Details" field.
2. Enter your contact information, including a name, email, and phone number.
3. Select the relevant employment law areas that apply to your inquiry.
4. Choose your preferred contact method (email or phone).
5. Provide any additional information you deem necessary to support your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inquiry Details**: Provide a brief description of your employment law-related issue or concern. This is an open-ended field where you can express your inquiry freely.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your name, email, and phone number to enable us to get in touch with you.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for handling your inquiry.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached regarding your inquiry.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached regarding your inquiry.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose between email or phone as your preferred method of contact.
* **Additional Information**: If you have any additional context or details about your inquiry, provide them here.
 
Note: Some fields are identical in their labels but have different IDs. Please refer to their respective IDs for further clarification.
