# General Inquiry Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant for users to submit general inquiries, comments, or questions that require attention from our team.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all the required fields (if any).
2. Make sure to provide your name, email address and any other information requested.
3. Clearly write out your message or question, so it can be properly understood.
4. Review your submission before clicking the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please enter your name for us to address you properly.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can get back to you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you prefer to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number.
* **Your question** (`question`, text, optional): Clearly write out your message or question for our team to understand.
* **Your message** (`message`, text, optional): You can add any additional information to support your message.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, email, optional): If you want us to contact you directly, please provide your email address.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted via phone.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Please select the date relevant to your inquiry.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time that relates to your inquiry.
*   If you need assistance with any of these fields or have any questions, please contact us directly.
