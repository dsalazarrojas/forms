# Vacation Home Rental Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you gather information about your inquiry for a vacation home rental. Please fill out this form to provide us with the necessary details about your rental needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the relevant information. If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Details** (`inquire_about_property`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the type of property you are interested in renting. This could include the type of property (e.g., house, apartment, etc.), its location, and any other relevant details.
* **Guest Information** (`inquire_about_guest`, text, optional): Share any information you have about the guests who will be staying in the property. This could include their names, ages, and any special requirements.
* **Check-in Date** (`inquire_about_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to check-in to the property. This will help us ensure that the property is available for your requested stay.
* **Check-out Date** (`inquire_about_time`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to check-out from the property. This will help us ensure that the property is available for your requested stay.
* **Location** (`inquire_about_location`, text, optional): Provide the location or the city you are interested in renting from.
* **Services** (`inquire_about_services`, select, optional): Select any services you would like to have available during your stay. Please choose from:
	+ Wi-Fi
	+ Kitchen
	+ TV
* **Notes** (`inquire_about_notes`, note, optional): If you have any special requests or requirements that we should know about, please provide them here. This could include any medical conditions or allergies that we should be aware of.
* **Contact Email** (`inquire_about_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Contact Phone** (`inquire_about_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out each field accurately and thoroughly to avoid delays in responding to your inquiry.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before proceeding.
* You will receive a personalized response to your inquiry based on the information provided.
