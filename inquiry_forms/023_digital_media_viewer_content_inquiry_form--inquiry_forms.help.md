# Digital Media Viewer Content Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit inquiries or requests related to digital media content. Use this form to ask questions, provide feedback, or seek clarification on specific content items.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Media Inquiry field with a brief description of the content you would like to inquire about.
2. In the Viewer Request field, provide any specific information or clarification you are seeking.
3. In the Feedback field, share your thoughts on the content.
4. Select an option from the Media Choice dropdown list to specify the type of media you are inquiring about.
5. In the Media Comment field, provide any additional comments or notes related to the media.
6. In the Content Comment field, provide any additional comments or notes related to the content.
7. In the Media Question field, pose any questions you have about the media.
8. In the Content Question field, pose any questions you have about the content.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Media Inquiry** (`media_inquiry`, text, required): A brief description of the content you would like to inquire about.
* **Viewer Request** (`viewer_request`, text, optional): Any specific information or clarification you are seeking on the content.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share your thoughts on the content.
* **Media Choice** (`media_choice`, select_one, required): Select the type of media you are inquiring about.
* **Media Comment** (`media_comment`, text, optional): Additional comments or notes related to the media.
* **Content Comment** (`content_comment`, text, optional): Additional comments or notes related to the content.
* **Media Question** (`media_question`, text, optional): Ask any questions you have about the media.
* **Content Question** (`content_question`, text, optional): Ask any questions you have about the content.
* **Media Choice Options**: Select from the following options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3

## Tips
* Please ensure that each form submission is unique and pertains to a specific content item.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the content, use the Media Question field.
* If you would like to provide feedback on the content, use the Feedback field.
* If you need additional information or clarification, use the Viewer Request field.
