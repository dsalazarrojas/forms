# magazine_content_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user input and feedback on magazine content ideas, pitches, and inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (if any).
2. Provide your contact information (email address).
3. Review and submit your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Input** (`user_input`): A text field for submitting your idea or pitch.
- **Contributor Info** (`contributor_info`): A text field for providing any relevant information about yourself as a contributor.
- **Story Pitch** (`story_pitch`): A text field for submitting your magazine content idea or pitch.
- **Materials Review** (`materials_review`): A text field for submitting any relevant materials or information related to your pitch.
- **Data Collection** (`data_collection`): A text field for submitting any data related to your pitch.
- **Editorial Team** (`editorial_team`): A text field for submitting any information about the editorial team you'd like to work with.
- **Review Status** (`review_status`): A select_one field to select the review status of your submission (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
- **Notes** (`notes`): A note field for adding any additional comments or notes.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`): A field to provide your email address for communication.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting.
- Use clear and concise language when filling in the text fields.
- Select the correct review status for your submission.
- Use the email address field for any further contact or communication.
