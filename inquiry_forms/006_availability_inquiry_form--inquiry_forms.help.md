# Pet Service Availability Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your pet's needs and schedule for service. Please fill out this form to inquire about pet service availability, and we will get back to you with the next available appointment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your owner information (Owner Full Name, Owner Email Address, and Owner Phone Number) to help us contact you with service updates and confirm appointments.
2. Enter your pet's information (Pet's Name, Pet's Species, Pet's Age, and Pet's Weight) to help us understand their needs and requirements.
3. Choose the services you are interested in (Full Grooming, Dog Walking, Overnight Boarding, Behavioral Training, Bath and Brush) and select the preferred service date and time slots.
4. If applicable, provide any special instructions or behavioral notes about your pet.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Owner Email Address**: Enter a valid email address where we can send service updates and confirm appointments.
* **Owner Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for service updates and confirmations.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Enter an alternate contact number in case we need to reach you urgently.
* **Pet's Name**: Enter your pet's full name.
* **Pet's Species**: Select one of the following species: Dog, Cat, Bird, Reptile, Small Mammal, or Other.
* **Pet's Age**: Enter your pet's age in years.
* **Pet's Weight**: Enter your pet's weight in pounds.
* **Requested Service**: Select all the services you are interested in: Full Grooming, Dog Walking, Overnight Boarding, Behavioral Training, Bath and Brush.
* **Preferred Service Date**: Select the earliest date you would like to schedule your pet's service.
* **Preferred Time Slot**: Select one or more of the following time slots: Morning (8am - 12pm), Afternoon (12pm - 4pm), Evening (4pm - 8pm).
* **Special Instructions or Behavioral Notes**: Enter any special requirements or instructions about your pet.
* **Vaccinations Up to Date?**: Confirm if your pet's vaccinations are up to date. Please note that this is required for boarding and grooming services.
