# Custom Tailoring Inquiry Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers who are interested in custom tailoring services. Fill out this form to inquire about our custom tailoring services and get in touch with our team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of service you are looking for (e.g., Alterations, Repairs).
2. Choose the location where you would like to receive the service (e.g., Home, Work, Office).
3. Select your preferred service time (e.g., 9am - 5pm, After 5pm - 10pm, Weekend).
4. Indicate how often you would like to get the service (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly).
5. Select the day of the week you would like to get the service (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
6. Choose the time frame for the service (e.g., 9am - 1pm, 1pm - 3pm, 3pm - 5pm).
7. Fill out your name and email address so we can contact you.
8. Provide your phone number and address for us to get in touch with you.
9. Enter a description of your inquiry.
10. Add any additional information you'd like to share with us.
11. Finally, select the service providers you would like to work with (e.g., Alterations, Sewing, Repair).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your style preference? (Custom Tailoring Inquiry Contact Form 1)**: This field is not applicable as there is no such field in the form.
* **What is the type of tailoring service? (Select Tailoring Service Type)**: Please select the type of service you are looking for (e.g., Alterations, Repairs). This will help us understand your needs better.
* **Which location do you want to receive service at? (Select Service Location)**: Choose the location where you would like to receive the service (e.g., Home, Work, Office).
* **What is the preferred service time? (Select Service Time)**: Select your preferred service time (e.g., 9am - 5pm, After 5pm - 10pm, Weekend).
* **How often would you like to get service? (Select Service Frequency)**: Indicate how often you would like to get the service (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly).
* **Which day of the week do you want to get service? (Select Service Day)**: Select the day of the week you would like to get the service (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
* **Time Frame (Select Service Time Frame)**: Choose the time frame for the service (e.g., 9am - 1pm, 1pm - 3pm, 3pm - 5pm).
* **Customer Name**: Fill out your name so we can get in touch with you.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Customer Phone**: Provide your phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Customer Address**: Enter your address so we can contact you.
* **Inquiry Description (Customer Inquiry)**: Enter a description of your inquiry.
* **Message**: Add any additional information you'd like to share with us.
* **Select Service Provider**: Select the service provider you'd like to work with (e.g., Alterations, Sewing, Repair).
* **Additional Service Provider**: Select any additional service providers you'd like to work with (e.g., Alterations, Sewing, Repair).
* **Custom Tailoring Inquiry Contact Form 2**: This field is not applicable as there is no such field in the form.
