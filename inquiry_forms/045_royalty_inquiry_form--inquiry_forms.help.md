# Royalty Inquiriy Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a way for users to submit their inquiries or concerns regarding royalty related matters. Please use this form to reach out to us with any questions or issues you have.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the requested information.
2. Ensure that you provide all required fields.
3. Review the form before submission to ensure you have included all necessary details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (<name>, text, required):** Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email (<email>, email, required):** Enter your active and valid email address. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number (<phone_number, text, required):** Enter your active and valid phone number. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Event Details (<event_details, text, required):** Provide a detailed description of the event or concern you are inquiring about.

## Tips
* Make sure to include all required fields to ensure the team can respond to your concerns effectively.
* Be as descriptive as possible in the Event Details field to give us a clear understanding of your inquiry.
* If you have any attachments or files to provide with your inquiry, please contact us separately for guidance on how to upload them.
