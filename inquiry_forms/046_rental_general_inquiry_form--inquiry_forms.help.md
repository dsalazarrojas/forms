# Rental General Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect general information from potential renters about their preferences and needs for renting a property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Choose your preferred contact method (phone or email) in the "Preferred Contact Method" field.
4. Select the type of property you are interested in renting in the "Property Type" field.
5. If you have specific move-in date and time preferences, enter them in the "Move In Date" and "Move In Time" fields, respectively.
6. Select your preferred move-in date and time in the "Preferred Move In Date" and "Preferred Move In Time" fields.
7. Choose the length of time you plan to rent the property in the "Rental Length" field.
8. Select your preferred contact method for the second preferred contact in the "Preferred Contact Method 2" field.
9. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
10. Enter your contact email in the "Contact Email" field.
11. Enter the size of the property you are interested in renting in the "Property Size" field.
12. Enter the rent you are willing to pay in the "Rent" field.
13. Enter the deposit you are willing to pay in the "Deposit" field.
14. Enter the utilities you are willing to pay in the "Utilities" field.
15. Enter any additional amenities you would like to have in the property in the "Amenities" field.
16. Enter any additional property features you would like to have in the "Property Features" field.
17. Enter a move-in date for the second preferred move-in date in the "Move In Date 2" field.
18. Enter a preferred move-in time for the second preferred move-in date in the "Preferred Move In Time 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (select_one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (phone or email).
* **Property Type** (select_multiple, optional): Select the type of property you are interested in renting.
* **Move In Date** (date, optional): Enter the date you would like to move in.
* **Move In Time** (time, optional): Enter the time you would like to move in.
* **Preferred Move In Date** (date, optional): Enter your preferred move-in date.
* **Preferred Move In Time** (time, optional): Enter your preferred move-in time.
* **Rental Length** (number, optional): Choose the length of time you plan to rent the property.
* **Preferred Contact Method 2** (select_one, optional): Choose your preferred contact method for the second preferred contact.
* **Contact Number** (text, optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Contact Email** (email, optional): Enter your contact email.
* **Property Size** (number, optional): Enter the size of the property you are interested in renting.
* **Rent** (number, optional): Enter the rent you are willing to pay.
* **Deposit** (number, optional): Enter the deposit you are willing to pay.
* **Utilities** (number, optional): Enter the utilities you are willing to pay.
* **Amenities** (text, optional): Enter any additional amenities you would like to have in the property.
* **Property Features** (text, optional): Enter any additional property features you would like to have.
* **Move In Date 2** (date, optional): Enter a move-in date for the second preferred move-in date.
* **Preferred Move In Time 2** (time, optional): Enter a preferred move-in time for the second preferred move-in date.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately to ensure that we can provide the best possible response to your inquiry.
* If you have already filled out the form, do not fill it out again.
* You can always contact us if you have any questions or need further clarification about the form.
