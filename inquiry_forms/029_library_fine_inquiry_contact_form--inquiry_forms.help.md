# library_fine_inquiry_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to inquire about library fines or overdue items.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether or not you are inquiring about a fine or overdue item.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field for your contact information.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field for easy contact.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field to ensure we can contact you directly.
5. Type your message in the "Message" field to provide more details about your inquiry.
