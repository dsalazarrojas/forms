# Accommodation Management Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential tenants or clients about their accommodation needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your inquirer information, including your full name and email address, which are mandatory fields.
2. Choose your preferred contact method, either email, phone, or text message.
3. Select the type of inquiry you are making, such as checking availability, asking about pricing and rates, or inquiring about pet policy.
4. Provide the name or address of the property you are inquiring about.
5. Indicate your desired accommodation type, such as a studio, one-bedroom, or two-bedroom apartment.
6. Enter your desired move-in or check-in date, if known.
7. Specify the expected duration of your stay, which can be less than a month, 1-3 months, or more.
8. Provide the number of occupants in your group.
9. If applicable, indicate your monthly budget range.
10. Share any specific requirements or preferences you may have, such as parking or pet-friendliness.
11. Finally, provide a brief message or any additional details about your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name to help us identify you.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Provide your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred contact method: Email, Phone, or Text Message.
* **Type of Inquiry** (`inquiry_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of inquiry you are making, such as Availability check, Pricing and rates, etc.
* **Property of Interest** (`property_of_interest`, `text`, required): Enter the name or address of the property you are inquiring about.
* **Accommodation Type Desired** (`accommodation_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of accommodation you are interested in, such as Studio, One-bedroom, etc.
* **Desired Move-In or Check-In Date** (`desired_move_in_date`, `date`, required): If known, enter your desired move-in or check-in date.
* **Expected Duration of Stay** (`stay_duration`, `select_one`, required): Specify the length of your stay, which can be Less than 1 month, 1-3 months, etc.
* **Number of Occupants** (`number_of_occupants`, `number`, required): Enter the number of people staying with you.
* **Monthly Budget Range** (`monthly_budget`, `select_one`, optional): If applicable, indicate your monthly budget range.
* **Specific Requirements or Preferences** (`specific_requirements`, `text`, optional): Share any specific requirements or preferences, such as parking or pet-friendliness.
* **Your Inquiry or Message** (`inquiry_message`, `text`, required): Provide a brief message or any additional details about your inquiry.
