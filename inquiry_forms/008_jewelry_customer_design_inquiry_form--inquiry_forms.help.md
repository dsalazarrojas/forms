# Jewelry Customer Design Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Jewelry Customer Design Inquiry Form is a tool for customers to submit their inquiry for a custom jewelry design. This form helps the business understand the customer's preferences and provide a quote or design concept tailored to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name to provide us with your personal details.
2. Enter your Email and Contact Phone number so we can get in touch with you.
3. Select the Jewelry Type that interests you (e.g., Diamond, Gold, Silver, Gemstone, Pearl, or Other).
4. Choose the Metal Type that suits your taste (e.g., Gold, Silver, or Other).
5. Select the Design Type that reflects your style (e.g., Classic, Modern, Artistic, or Custom).
6. Select the Stone Type that you're interested in (e.g., Diamond, Gemstone, Pearl, or Other).
7. If you have a specific size in mind for your custom design, enter the Size.
8. If you have a budget for your custom design, enter the Budget.
9. Describe your Message Details and Jewelry Description to help us understand your design preferences.
10. Finally, review the form for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name for our records.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name for our records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can call you back.
* **Message Details** (`message`, text, required): Describe your custom design preferences and requirements.
* **Jewelry Type** (`jewelry_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of jewelry that interests you (e.g., Diamond, Gold, Silver, Gemstone, Pearl, or Other).
* **Metal Type** (`metal_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the metal type that suits your taste (e.g., Gold, Silver, or Other).
* **Design Type** (`design_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the design type that reflects your style (e.g., Classic, Modern, Artistic, or Custom).
* **Stone Type** (`stone_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the stone type that you're interested in (e.g., Diamond, Gemstone, Pearl, or Other).
* **Size** (`size`, number, optional): If you have a specific size in mind, enter it here.
* **Budget** (`budget`, number, optional): If you have a budget for your custom design, enter it here.
* **Message Details** (`message_details`, text, optional): Describe your custom design preferences and requirements.
* **Jewelry Description** (`jewelry_description`, text, optional): Describe your custom design preferences and requirements.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): If you have a specific date in mind for your custom design, enter it here.
