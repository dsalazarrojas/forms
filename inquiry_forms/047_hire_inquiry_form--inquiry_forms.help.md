# Hire Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hire Inquiry Form is used to collect information from customers about their hiring needs. This form is intended for customers who are inquiring about hiring your company's services. Please make sure to only fill out this form if you are a genuine customer looking to hire our services. If you are an existing customer, please do not fill out this form as it will not update your current information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
2. Enter your contact information in the relevant fields (customer_name, phone, email).
3. Specify the services you are interested in (services and pickup options).
4. Provide any additional information or special instructions if necessary (special_instructions).
5. If you have a specific quote request, please enter it in the quote_request field.
6. If you have any further comments or questions, please use the customer_message field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name (customer_name)**: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address for us to contact you about your inquiry.
* **Delivery Address (delivery_address)**: Enter your delivery address if you would like us to deliver the service to your location.
* **Services Needed (services)**: Select all the services you are interested in (Delivery, Pickup, Drop off, Special Instructions).
* **Pickup Method (pickup)**: Select how you would like to receive the service (Delivery, Pickup, Drop off, In Person).
* **Special Instructions (special_instructions)**: Provide any additional information about your services needs or requests.
* **Quote Request (quote_request)**: If you have a specific quote request, please enter it here.
* **Customer Message (customer_message)**: If you have any further comments or questions, please use this field.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your contact information accurately to ensure we can get in touch with you.
* Please select all relevant services and pickup methods for our team to fulfill your needs.
* If you have any special instructions or requests, please provide them in the special_instructions field.
* Please be as specific as possible with your quote request so we can give you an accurate quote.
* Use the customer_message field to provide any additional information or ask questions about your inquiry.
