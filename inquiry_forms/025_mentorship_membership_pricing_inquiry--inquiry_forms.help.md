# Mentorship Membership Pricing Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in exploring mentorship membership opportunities. Please fill it out carefully, as it will help us provide tailored guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your valid Email address in the Email field.
3. Enter your Phone Number in the Phone Number field.
4. Select your desired Tier of membership from the Tier field.
5. If you're applying for membership, enter the number of weeks you'd like to dedicate to the program in the Pricing field.
6. If you'd like to apply for a discount, enter the percentage you're eligible for in the Discount field.
7. Briefly describe your goals in the What is Your Goal? field.
8. Enter the number of hours you'd like to dedicate per week in the How much time do you have per week? field.
9. Select your energy level from the How much energy do you have? field.
10. Choose your preferred Mentor(s) from the Preferred Mentor field.
11. Add any additional information or comments in the Notes field.
12. Select your preferred follow-up method from the Follow-up field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as you'd like to be addressed.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Tier** (`Tier`, select_one, required/optional): Select your desired membership tier (Basic, Premium, Ultimate).
* **Pricing** (`Pricing`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of weeks you'd like to dedicate to the program.
* **Discount** (`Discount`, number, required/optional): Enter the percentage of discount you're eligible for.
* **What is Your Goal?** (`What is Your Goal?`, text, required/optional): Briefly describe your goals for joining the mentorship program.
* **How much time do you have per week?** (`How much time do you have per week?`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of hours you'd like to dedicate per week.
* **How much energy do you have?** (`How much energy do you have?`, select_one, required/optional): Select your energy level (low, medium, high).
* **Preferred Mentor** (`Preferred Mentor`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your preferred mentor(s) from the list.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional information or comments about your application.
* **Follow-up** (`Follow-up`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your preferred follow-up method (Email, Phone, Video call, In-person).
