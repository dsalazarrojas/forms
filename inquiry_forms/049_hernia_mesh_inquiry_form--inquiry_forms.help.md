# Hernia Mesh Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hernia Mesh Inquiry Form is designed for patients or their representatives to report concerns or complications related to hernia mesh implants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information:
   - Patient Inquiry: Describe the concern or complication you are experiencing.
   - Date of Inquiry: Enter the date you first noticed the issue or when you are inquiring about.
   - Contact Number: Provide a contact number where you can be reached for follow-up.
2. Optionally, provide additional details:
   - Follow up Date: Enter the date you would like to be followed up on.
   - Follow up Time: Enter the time of day you would prefer for the follow-up.
   - Medical Condition: Describe your medical condition or symptoms.
   - Surgery Details: Provide any relevant details about your surgery.
   - Follow up Message: Enter any specific instructions or messages you have for the follow-up.
3. Select relevant options:
   - Medical Office: Choose the medical office where you received treatment.
   - Support Team: Select the support team member(s) you would like to be in contact with.
   - Follow up Method: Choose the preferred method of follow-up contact (e.g., call, email, or visit).
   - Follow up Status: Indicate the current status of your concern or complication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Inquiry**: A brief description of the concern or complication you are experiencing.
* **Date of Inquiry**: The date you first noticed the issue or when you are inquiring about.
* **Contact Number**: A valid phone number where the support team can reach you for follow-up.
* **Follow up Date**: The date you would like to be followed up on (optional).
* **Follow up Time**: The time of day you would like to be followed up on (optional).
* **Medical Condition**: A description of your current medical condition or symptoms.
* **Surgery Details**: Any relevant details about your surgery, such as the date or surgeon involved.
* **Follow up Message**: Any specific instructions or messages you have for the follow-up.
* **Medical Office**: The name of the medical office where you received treatment.
* **Support Team**: The name(s) of the support team member(s) you would like to be in contact with.
* **Follow up Method**: The method you prefer for follow-up contact (e.g., call, email, or visit).
* **Follow up Status**: The current status of your concern or complication (optional).

## Tips
Please fill out as much or as little information as needed, depending on your situation. The form will be reviewed and verified by medical staff before any action is taken.
