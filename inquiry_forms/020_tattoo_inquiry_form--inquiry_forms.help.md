# Tattoo Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tattoo Inquiry Form is a tool for customers to share their tattoo inquiries with the studio. Please use this form to provide us with your details, and we will contact you to discuss your options.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields as prompted.
2. Please ensure that all required fields (marked as "required") are completed before submitting the form.
3. If you have any questions or need assistance while filling out the form, feel free to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. First name (First name)
* Label: First name
* Type: text
* Required: Optional
* Explanation: This field is for your given name, which we will use to address you.

### 2. Email address (Email address)
* Label: Email address
* Type: email
* Required: Optional
* Explanation: This is where we will contact you regarding your inquiry.

### 3. Phone number (Phone number)
* Label: Phone number
* Type: text
* Required: Optional
* Explanation: If you have a phone number, please provide it so we can reach you by phone.

### 4. Date of birth (Date of birth)
* Label: Date of birth
* Type: date
* Required: Optional
* Explanation: This is your date of birth, which helps us to determine your age.

### 5. Tattoo inquiry details (Tattoo inquiry details)
* Label: Tattoo inquiry details
* Type: text
* Required: Optional
* Explanation: Please share any specific details about the tattoo you are interested in, such as the design, size, or desired location on your body.

### 6. Preferred artist (Preferred artist)
* Label: Preferred artist
* Type: select_multiple
* Required: Optional
* Explanation: If you have a specific artist in mind, please select them from the provided options.

### 7. Date of inquiry (Date of inquiry)
* Label: Date of inquiry
* Type: date
* Required: Optional
* Explanation: This is the date you are inquiring about getting a tattoo.

### 8. Tattoo type (Tattoo type)
* Label: Tattoo type
* Type: text
* Required: Optional
* Explanation: Please describe the type of tattoo you are interested in, such as color, black and white, or watercolor.

### 9. Tattoo size (Tattoo size)
* Label: Tattoo size
* Type: number
* Required: Optional
* Explanation: If you know the size of the tattoo you are interested in, please provide it.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need assistance while filling out the form, feel free to reach out to us.
* We will contact you to discuss your options and answer any questions you may have.
* Please note that this form is for general inquires only and should not be used for emergency contact or urgent requests.
