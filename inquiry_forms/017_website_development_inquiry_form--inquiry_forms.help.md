# Website Development Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool to gather information from users who are interested in developing a website. It helps us understand their project requirements, contact details, and other relevant information to follow up and provide more information about the website development process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your project details in the "project_details" field.
2. Provide your developer information in the "developer_details" field.
3. Enter your contact email in the "contact_email" field.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the "contact_phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **project_details** (text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your project idea or the website you want to develop. This could be a brief summary of the project's objectives, goals, or features.
* **developer_details** (number, required/optional): This field is not clearly defined, so it is assumed to be a field for entering any relevant developer information.
* **contact_email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication and updates about your project.
* **contact_phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further communication and updates about your project.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all the required fields (project_details, contact_email).
- If you have any attachments or other supporting documents, please attach them separately and mention it in the "contact_email" field.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, do not hesitate to contact us.
