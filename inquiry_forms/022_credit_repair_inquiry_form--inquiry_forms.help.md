# Credit Repair Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Credit Repair Inquiry Form is a collection of information to help us process and address your credit repair requests efficiently. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of credit repair inquiry you are making.
2. Answer whether you have phone inquiries or not.
3. Enter your contact information (email or phone number) if you have any.
4. Provide any additional notes about your inquiry.
5. Identify the credit company associated with your inquiry.
6. Specify whether you have any email inquiries or not.
7. Choose your preferred contact method (phone or email).
8. Enter your contact number or email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **User Inquiry** (`user_inquiry`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your credit repair inquiry.
  - Please provide a concise and clear description of your credit repair inquiry.
  - This field is not required, but providing a brief description will help us understand your request.

- **Type of Credit** (`credit_type`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are making an inquiry about a specific type of credit, or 'No' if you are not.
  - This field helps us understand the type of credit repair you are requesting.

- **Phone Inquiries** (`phone_inquiry`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have any phone inquiries, or 'No' if you do not.
  - This field indicates whether you have any phone-related inquiries.

- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address if you prefer email communication.
  - This field is optional, but it helps us contact you regarding your inquiry if needed.

- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional information about your credit repair inquiry.
  - This field is for any extra details you want to share.

- **Credit Company** (`credit_company`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are making an inquiry about a specific credit company, or 'No' if you are not.
  - This field is for identifying the credit company related to your inquiry.

- **Email Inquiries** (`email_inquiry`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have any email inquiries, or 'No' if you do not.
  - This field indicates whether you have any email-related inquiries.

- **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred contact method (phone or email).
  - This field helps us contact you regarding your inquiry.

- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number if you prefer phone communication.
  - This field is optional, but it helps us contact you if needed.

- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address if you prefer email communication.
  - This field is optional, and it helps us contact you regarding your inquiry.
