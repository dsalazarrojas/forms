# off-grid-energy-solutions-inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you understand how to complete the "Off-Grid Energy Solutions Inquiry" form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Review the form carefully** to ensure you are providing accurate and relevant information.
2. **Complete each section** in the form, one page at a time.
3. **Use the required fields** (marked as "required") to ensure your submission is complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Project Details (Page 1)
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your project.
* No additional information is required for this field.

### Customer Details (Page 1)
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the customer.
* No additional information is required for this field.

### Project Location (Page 1)
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your project.
* No additional information is required for this field.

### Contact Info (Page 1)
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information.
* No additional information is required for this field.

### Project Status (Page 2)
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
* This field is required to indicate the current status of your project.

### Project Size (Page 2)
* **Project Size** (`project_size`, number, optional): Enter a numeric value representing your project size.
* No specific unit is required.

### Project Power (Page 2)
* **Project Power** (`project_power`, number, optional): Enter a numeric value representing your project power.
* No specific unit is required.

### Customer Name (Page 2)
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the customer.
* No additional information is required for this field.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information in each field.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
* If you have any issues or questions while completing this form, please contact us for assistance.
