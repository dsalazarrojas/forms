# Math Lesson Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Math Lesson Inquiry Form is designed to allow students to request help from teachers or tutors with specific math lessons. This form helps to facilitate communication between students and educators, enabling students to receive guidance on math concepts they're struggling with.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the subject area you're inquiring about (e.g., Math, Science, Language Arts, Social Studies).
2. Choose the specific lesson plan for which you need help (e.g., Algebra I, Geometry, Pre-Calculus).
3. Select the format in which you'd like to receive help (e.g., In-person, Online, Phone, Video).
4. Choose your preferred time for the inquiry (select one or multiple time slots).
5. Provide any additional notes or context about your request.
6. Enter your student name and email.
7. Enter the name and contact information of the teacher or tutor you're inquiring about.
8. Finally, include a message describing your specific question or area of concern.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Subject
* **Subject** (`Subject`, `select_one`, required): Choose the subject area you're inquiring about (e.g., Math, Science, Language Arts, Social Studies).

### Lesson Plan
* **Lesson Plan** (`Lesson Plan`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the specific lesson plan for which you need help (e.g., Algebra I, Geometry, Pre-Calculus).

### Format
* **Format** (`Format`, `select_one`, required): Choose the format in which you'd like to receive help (e.g., In-person, Online, Phone, Video).

### Preferred Time
* **Preferred Time** (`Preferred Time`, `time`, required): Select one or multiple time slots that work best for you.

### Available Time
* **Available Time** (`Available Time`, `select_multiple`, required): This is used to select times when you're available for a tutoring session. This field is typically used in conjunction with the preferred time field to ensure there's a time that fits everyone's schedule.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, required): Provide any additional notes or context about your inquiry. This can include background information or specific questions you have about the lesson plan.

### Student Name
* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as a student.

### Grade Level
* **Grade Level** (`Grade Level`, `select_one`, required): Select your grade level.

### Email
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide an alternative contact method, enter your phone number here. This is not a required field.

### Contact
* **Contact** (`Contact`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional contact information for the student.

### Message
* **Message** (`Message`, `text`, required): Describe your specific question or area of concern about the lesson plan.

### Tutor Name
* **Tutor Name** (`Tutor Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the teacher or tutor you're inquiring about.

### Tutor Email
* **Tutor Email** (`Tutor Email`, `email`, optional): If you'd like to provide an email address for the teacher or tutor, enter it here. This is not a required field.

### Tutor Phone
* **Tutor Phone** (`Tutor Phone`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide an alternative contact method for the teacher or tutor, enter their phone number here. This is not a required field.

### Tutor Notes
* **Tutor Notes** (`Tutor Notes`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional notes or context about the teacher or tutor you're inquiring about. This can include background information or specific requests. This field is not required.

## Tips
* Ensure you're using the correct form for your intended purpose.
* Double-check that you're not duplicating an inquiry that may have already been submitted.
* Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to help the teacher or tutor assist you better.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the teacher or tutor directly.
