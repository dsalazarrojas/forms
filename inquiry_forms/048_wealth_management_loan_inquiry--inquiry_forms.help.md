# Wealth Management Loan Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wealth Management Loan Inquiry form is used to collect information from individuals or organizations seeking loans. This form helps our team gather essential details about the contact person, loan specifics, and the status of the loan application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. On the first page, enter the contact name and contact email address of the person seeking the loan.
2. On the second page, enter the loan amount, select the loan term, and choose the loan type (Personal, Commercial, or Home Equity).
3. Finally, select the loan status (Applied, Approved, Rejected, or Pending) to indicate the progress of the loan application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, Required): Enter the name of the contact person seeking the loan.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, Required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, Required): Enter the requested loan amount.
* **Loan Term** (`loan_term`, text, Required): Enter the duration of the loan term.
* **Loan Type** (`loan_type`, select_one, Required): Select the type of loan being applied for (Personal, Commercial, or Home Equity).
* **Loan Status** (`loan_status`, select_one, Required): Select the current status of the loan application (Applied, Approved, Rejected, or Pending).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- Double-check the loan status to ensure it reflects the current status of the loan application.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact our team.
