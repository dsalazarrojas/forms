# Graphic Design Services Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather inquiries for graphic design services. It collects information about the services needed, timeline, budget, and contact details from the user.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required and optional information as prompted.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your inquiry is processed correctly.
3. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inquiry Form** (graphic_design_services_inquiry, text, optional)
	+ This field is the main section where you can enter your inquiry message.
* **Services Needed** (services_needed, text, optional)
	+ Enter the specific graphic design services you need. For example, logo design, branding, or visual identity.
* **Budget** (budget, number, optional)
	+ Enter your estimated budget for the graphic design services. This will help us understand your expectations and provide more accurate quotes.
* **Timeline** (timeline, date, optional)
	+ Select a start and end date for when you need the services. This will help us plan and schedule your project accordingly.
* **Budget Details** (budget_details, text, optional)
	+ Provide additional information about your budget, such as any specific requirements or constraints.
* **Contact Info** (contact_info, text, optional)
	+ Enter your contact information, which will be used to get in touch with you for further discussion and clarification.
* **Name** (contact_info_name, text, optional)
	+ Enter your name, which will be used to personalize communication with you.
* **Email** (contact_info_email, email, optional)
	+ Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and updates.
* **Phone** (contact_info_phone, text, optional)
	+ Enter your phone number, which will be used for further discussion and clarification.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields for a complete inquiry.
* If you have any additional information or clarification, please provide it in the "Budget Details" field.
* If you have any specific requirements or deadlines, please mention it in the "Services Needed" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for further assistance.
