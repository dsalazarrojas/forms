# Hospital Patient Visit Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Patient Visit Inquiry Form is a tool used to gather information about a patient's visit to the hospital. This form helps hospital staff quickly identify the patient's details and reason for visit, which can be useful for various hospital operations such as tracking patient flow, identifying potential issues, and ensuring proper patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "patient_name" field.
2. Select the date of the patient's visit from the calendar in the "visit_date" field.
3. Choose the time of the patient's visit from the clock in the "visit_time" field.
4. Enter the name of the department the patient visited in the "hospital_department" field.
5. Enter the name of the visitor (e.g. family member, doctor, etc.) in the "visitor_name" field.
6. Enter the contact number of the visitor in the "contact_number" field.
7. Enter the email address of the visitor in the "email" field.
8. Enter the reason for the patient's visit in the "reason_for_visit" field.
9. Enter the name of the hospital staff member who interacted with the patient in the "staff_name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Name (patient_name)
* The patient's name is a required field and should be entered in the format used on the patient's identification documents.

### Visit Date (visit_date)
* Select a date from the calendar to indicate the date of the patient's visit.

### Visit Time (visit_time)
* Select a time from the clock to indicate the time of the patient's visit.

### Hospital Department (hospital_department)
* Enter the name of the department where the patient was seen by hospital staff.

### Visitor Name (visitor_name)
* Enter the name of the person who visited the patient, such as a family member or doctor.

### Contact Number (contact_number)
* Enter the contact number of the visitor, if available.

### Email (email)
* Enter the email address of the visitor, if available.

### Reason for Visit (reason_for_visit)
* Enter the reason why the patient visited the hospital, such as an appointment or emergency.

### Staff Name (staff_name)
* Enter the name of the hospital staff member who interacted with the patient.
