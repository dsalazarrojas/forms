# # Limousine Service Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Limousine Service Inquiry Form is designed to gather information from users who are interested in booking a limousine service for an event or occasion.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the pickup location where you would like to be picked up.
2. Select the date of the event or occasion.
3. Choose the time of the event or occasion.
4. Enter the number of people attending the event or occasion.
5. If you have any special requests, please enter them in the "Special Requests" field.
6. Enter your contact information, including phone and email.
7. Select your preferred limo type (Sedan, SUV, Bus, or Van).
8. Enter the drop-off location.
9. Confirm your pickup and drop-off locations.
10. Confirm your preferred limo type.
11. Confirm the date and time of the event or occasion.
12. Confirm the number of people attending the event or occasion.
13. Confirm your contact information.
14. Confirm your booking information by reviewing the form carefully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pickup location**: Enter the address or location where you would like to be picked up.
* **Date**: Select the date of the event or occasion.
* **Time**: Select the time of the event or occasion.
* **Number of people**: Enter the number of people attending the event or occasion.
* **Special Requests**: If you have any special requests, enter them here.
* **Contact information**: Enter your phone number and email address.
* **Preferred limo type**: Select the type of limo you prefer (Sedan, SUV, Bus, or Van).
* **Drop-off location**: Enter the address or location where you would like to be dropped off.
* **Confirm pickup and drop-off locations**: Confirm the locations you entered above.
* **Confirm preferred limo type**: Confirm the limo type you selected above.
* **Confirm date and time**: Confirm the date and time of the event or occasion.
* **Confirm number of people**: Confirm the number of people attending the event or occasion.
* **Confirm contact information**: Confirm your contact information.
* **Confirm booking information**: Review your booking information carefully to ensure accuracy.
* **Confirmation message**: Enter a confirmation message to confirm your booking.

Note: This form is for general information gathering purposes only and is not a formal booking form. Please contact the limousine service provider for further instructions and to confirm your booking.
