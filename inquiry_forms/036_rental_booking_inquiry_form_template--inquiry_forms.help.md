# Rental Booking Inquiry Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Rental Booking Inquiry Form, which is designed to collect essential information from potential renters. The form helps the landlord or property manager understand the tenant's needs and requirements for a rental property. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Review each field carefully and make sure you are providing the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Tenant Information** (`tenant_info`, text, optional): 
Please enter your name and contact information, such as your name and address.

### 2. **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): 
Enter your preferred contact method and any additional information about yourself that you would like the landlord or property manager to know.

### 3. **Rental Details** (`rental_details`, text, optional): 
Describe the type of rental property you are looking for (e.g. studio, 1 bedroom, 2 bedrooms etc.).

### 4. **Preferred Move-in Date** (`preferred_move_in_date`, date, optional): 
Select the date you would like to move in. If not specified, we will consider the earliest available date.

### 5. **Number of Guests** (`how_many_guests`, select_one, optional): 
Select how many guests will be living with you in the rental property.

### 6. **Rental Preferences** (`rental_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): 
Choose any additional preferences you have for the rental property (e.g. pet-friendly, near a park, etc.).

### 7. **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): 
If you have any special requests or requirements for the rental property, please list them here.

### 8. **Message** (`message`, text, optional): 
Add any additional comments or questions you have about the rental property.

### 9. **Email** (`email`, email, optional): 
Enter your email address so the landlord or property manager can contact you.

### 10. **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): 
Enter your phone number so the landlord or property manager can contact you.

### 11. **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, select_one, optional): 
Check this box to confirm that you have entered your correct email address.

### 12. **Send to Landlord** (`send_form_to_landlord`, select_one, optional): 
Choose whether you would like the landlord or property manager to contact you or not.

## Tips
Please be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out this form. It will help the landlord or property manager provide you with a more suitable and efficient rental search experience.
