## Airline Ticket Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from users about their airline ticket inquiry. Please fill out the form below to share your travel preferences with our customer service team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely to ensure that your inquiry is handled efficiently.
2. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Name", "Email Address", and "Phone Number" fields.
3. Provide details about your trip in the "Departure City/Airport", "Destination City/Airport", "Preferred Departure Date", and "Preferred Return Date (If round trip)" fields.
4. Choose your ticket type (One Way, Round Trip, or Multi-City) and preferred cabin class (Economy, Premium Economy, Business, or First Class).
5. Enter the number of adults and children (if applicable) traveling with you.
6. If you have an estimated budget in mind, enter it in the "Estimated Budget" field.
7. Indicate if your travel dates are flexible (yes or no).
8. Finally, provide any additional comments or special requests you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name so our customer service team can contact you.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
