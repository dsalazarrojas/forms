# Tax Equalization Inquiry Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create an inquiry request for tax equalization purposes. The form will be used to gather information about an employee's tax status, which will help determine whether they are eligible for certain tax benefits or credits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Please select whether the employee is requesting tax equalization (Yes/No).
2. Enter the tax year for which you are requesting equalization (if applicable).
3. Select the start and end dates for the period you are requesting equalization (if applicable).
4. Enter any additional comments or reasons for your request.
5. Provide information about the employee, including their name, email, phone number, job title, department, and location.
6. Enter the name and email of the tax authority's contact person.
7. Select the status of the employee (Active/Inactive).
8. Indicate whether the request was submitted by the employee (Yes/No).
9. Indicate whether the request was submitted to the tax authority (Yes/No).
10. If applicable, enter the date the request was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Request** (`employee_request`, select_one, required): This field requires you to select whether the employee is requesting tax equalization.
* **Tax Year** (`tax_year`, number, optional): If you are requesting tax equalization for a specific year, enter the year here.
* **Tax Period** (`tax_period`, time, optional): If you are requesting tax equalization for a specific period, enter the start and end dates of that period.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the period for which you are requesting tax equalization.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the period for which you are requesting tax equalization.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or reasons for your request.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or information about the request.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee for whom you are requesting tax equalization.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the employee for whom you are requesting tax equalization.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the employee for whom you are requesting tax equalization.
* **Employee Title** (`employee_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the employee for whom you are requesting tax equalization.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department of the employee for whom you are requesting tax equalization.
* **Location** (`employee_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the employee for whom you are requesting tax equalization.
* **Tax Authority** (`tax_authority`, text, optional): Enter the name of the tax authority's contact person.
* **Tax Officer** (`tax_officer`, text, optional): Enter the name of the tax authority's contact person.
* **Tax Officer Email** (`tax_officer_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the tax authority's contact person.
* **Tax Officer Phone** (`tax_officer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the tax authority's contact person.
* **Tax Officer Title** (`tax_officer_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the tax authority's contact person.
* **Tax Officer Department** (`tax_officer_department`, text, optional): Enter the department of the tax authority's contact person.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the employee (Active/Inactive).
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required): Indicate whether the request was submitted by the employee (Yes/No).
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, select_one, required): Indicate whether the request was submitted to the tax authority (Yes/No).
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date the request was submitted.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the tax authority or the HR department for assistance.
