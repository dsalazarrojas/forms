# wedding_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "wedding_inquiry_form" is designed to collect information from potential customers who are interested in our wedding services. This form helps us understand their needs and preferences better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Select the "wedding_services" field and choose the type of wedding service you are interested in.
3. In the "date" field, select the date for the wedding ceremony.
4. In the "time" field, select the time for the wedding ceremony.
5. Enter your name in the "customer_name" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "customer_email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "customer_phone" field.
8. In the "inquiry_message" field, type in a brief message or note about your inquiry.
9. Click on the "Submit" button to send your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **wedding_services**: This field allows you to select the type of wedding service you are interested in. Please choose the service that best describes your needs.
* **date**: This field is for entering the date of the wedding ceremony. Please use the date format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **time**: This field is for selecting the time of the wedding ceremony. Please choose the time from the available options.
* **customer_name**: This field is for entering your name.
* **customer_email**: This field is for entering your email address. Please ensure to enter a valid email address.
* **customer_phone**: This field is for entering your phone number.
* **inquiry_message**: This field is for typing a brief message or note about your inquiry.
* **form_submission_id**: This field is a unique identifier for each form submission. Please do not fill this field as it is auto-generated.

## Tips
Please ensure that you fill out all required fields correctly before submitting the form. If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can refer back to this help guide for clarification.
