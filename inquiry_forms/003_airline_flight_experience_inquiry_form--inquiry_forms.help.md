# Airline Flight Experience Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and ratings from passengers about their airline flight experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with a *:
	* *Passenger name*: The name as it appears on your booking.
	* *Flight number*: The flight number as indicated on your ticket.
	* *Contact email*: Your email address where you can be reached for follow up.
	* *Travel date*: The date you took the flight.
	* *Travel class*: The cabin class you traveled in.
	* *Overall experience*: A rating of how you felt about your flight experience.
2. Choose your ratings for each aspect of the flight:
	* *Seat comfort rating*: Rate how comfortable your seat was from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
	* *Cabin cleanliness rating*: Rate the cleanliness of the cabin from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
	* *Crew friendliness rating*: Rate how friendly and helpful the crew was from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
3. Optional, but encouraged:
	* *Meal satisfaction*: If a meal was served, rate your satisfaction with the meal from Very dissatisfied to Very satisfied.
4. Additional comments and suggestions:
	* *Additional comments*: If you have any other thoughts or suggestions about your flight experience, please share them in the text field.
