# Travel Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers inquire about travel plans and options. It will be reviewed by a travel consultant who will provide personalized guidance on destinations, prices, and travel options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant fields and provide as much detail as you can. You can choose one or multiple options for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Destination** (text)
This field is where you can specify the destination you are interested in traveling to. Enter the name of the city, state, or region you are interested in visiting.

### 2. **Start Date** (date)
Enter the start date of your trip, in the format of month/day/year (e.g., 06/20/2024). This will help the travel consultant understand when you are planning to travel.

### 3. **Price Range** (number)
Enter a price range you are looking for in your trip. For example, if you are looking to spend up to $2000, enter "2000". This will help the consultant narrow down options that fit your budget.

### 4. **Category** (select one)
Select a category that best describes the type of travel you are interested in:
*   Beach
*   City
*   Mountain
*   Cruise

### 5. **Customer Email** (email)
Enter your email address so that the travel consultant can contact you with more information and recommendations.

### 6. **Customer Phone** (text)
Enter your phone number so that the travel consultant can contact you with more information and recommendations.

### 7. **Note** (text)
This is an optional field where you can provide any additional information about your travel preferences or requirements.

### 8. **Submit** (select one)
Select "Submit" to send your inquiry to the travel consultant. If you are not ready to submit, you can select "Cancel" to save your progress.

### 9. **Travel Agent** (multiple select)
Select one or multiple travel agents from the list who will be able to assist you with your travel plans.
