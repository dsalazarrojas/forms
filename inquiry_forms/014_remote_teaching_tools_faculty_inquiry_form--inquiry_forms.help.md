# Remote Teaching Tools Faculty Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remote Teaching Tools Faculty Inquiry Form is designed to gather information from faculty members about their experiences and needs related to remote teaching tools. This form is intended for support teams to provide targeted assistance to faculty members based on their responses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Please provide your faculty feedback in [form_1](#form_1).
2. Indicate your experience with remote teaching technology in [form_2](#form_2).
3. If you need help with online courses, select 'Yes' or 'No' in [form_3](#form_3).
4. If you need support with other aspects of teaching, select 'Yes' or 'No' in [form_4](#form_4).
5. If you are a new faculty member, select 'Yes' or 'No' in [form_5](#form_5).
6. If you need help with anything else, select 'Yes' or 'No' in [form_6](#form_6).
7. Indicate your department affiliation in [form_7](#form_7).
8. If you teach face-to-face, select 'Yes' or 'No' in [form_8](#form_8).
9. If you teach online, select 'Yes' or 'No' in [form_9](#form_9).
10. If you are a graduate student, select 'Yes' or 'No' in [form_10](#form_10).
11. If you need help with other aspects of teaching, select 'Yes' or 'No' in [form_11](#form_11).
12. Enter your email address in [form_12](#form_12).
13. Enter your phone number in [form_13](#form_13).
14. If you need help with other aspects of teaching, select 'Yes' or 'No' in [form_14](#form_14).
15. If you teach online, select 'Yes' or 'No' in [form_15](#form_15).
16. If you are a graduate student, select 'Yes' or 'No' in [form_16](#form_16).
17. If you have any additional comments, please enter them in [form_17](#form_17).
18. If you need help with other aspects of teaching, select 'Yes' or 'No' in [form_18](#form_18).
19. Enter any comments or feedback in [form_19](#form_19).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Faculty Feedback** (`form_1`, text, required: false): Please provide any general feedback or comments about your experience with remote teaching tools.
  - This field is for you to share your overall thoughts and opinions about remote teaching tools.
- **What is your experience with remote teaching technology?** (`form_2`, text, required: false): Please describe your level of experience with remote teaching technology.
  - This field helps us understand your background and familiarity with remote teaching tools.
- **Do you need help with online courses?** (`form_3`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you need assistance with online courses.
  - This question is designed to help us identify areas where you need support with online courses.
- **What support do you need?** (`form_4`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the types of support you need for remote teaching tools.
  - This field allows you to specify the types of support you require, such as technical assistance, training, or resources.
- **Are you a new faculty member?** (`form_5`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you are a new faculty member.
  - This question helps us understand your level of experience and provide targeted support.
- **Do you need help with anything else?** (`form_6`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you need assistance with other aspects of teaching.
  - This field is for you to indicate if you need support with other aspects of teaching, such as course materials or classroom setup.
- **What is your role in the department?** (`form_7`, select_one, required: false): Please select your department role.
  - This field helps us understand your position within the department and provide relevant support.
- **Do you teach face-to-face?** (`form_8`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you teach face-to-face courses.
  - This question is designed to help us understand your teaching modality and provide targeted support.
- **Do you teach online?** (`form_9`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you teach online courses.
  - This field is for you to indicate if you teach online courses, and we can provide specific support.
- **Are you a graduate student?** (`form_10`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you are a graduate student.
  - This question helps us understand your academic level and provide relevant support.
- **Do you need help with other aspects of teaching?** (`form_11`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you need assistance with other aspects of teaching.
  - This field is for you to indicate if you need support with other aspects of teaching, such as course design or student support.
- **Email** (`form_12`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for further communication.
  - This field is for us to contact you and provide support.
- **Phone** (`form_13`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for further communication.
  - This field is for us to contact you and provide support.
- **Do you need help with other aspects of teaching?** (`form_14`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you need assistance with other aspects of teaching.
  - This field is for you to indicate if you need support with other aspects of teaching.
- **Do you teach online?** (`form_15`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you teach online courses.
  - This question is designed to help us understand your teaching modality and provide targeted support.
- **Are you a graduate student?** (`form_16`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you are a graduate student.
  - This field helps us understand your academic level and provide relevant support.
- **Do you have any other comments?** (`form_17`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have.
  - This field is for you to provide any additional thoughts or concerns.
- **Do you need help with other aspects of teaching?** (`form_18`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you need assistance with other aspects of teaching.

  - This field is for you to provide any final thoughts or concerns.

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when answering each question.
- If you need help with something that is not listed, please indicate 'Other' in the relevant field.
- If you are unsure about any question, please ask us and we will be happy to assist.
