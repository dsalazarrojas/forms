# postage_assessment_delay_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Postage Assessment Delay Inquiry Form is used to report issues with delayed postage assessments. Customers or support staff can use this form to report problems with their postage assessments.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct category for your inquiry (e.g., "Inquiry Forms").
2. Provide a brief description of the issue you are experiencing with your postage assessment.
3. Enter your name for identification purposes.
4. Enter your order number for reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`category`, optional): Select the category that best describes the type of form you are filling out. This field is used to help us quickly identify the type of issue you are experiencing.
* **Description** (`description`, optional): Describe the issue you are experiencing with your postage assessment. Please provide as much detail as possible so we can better understand your problem.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, optional): Enter your name for identification purposes. This will help us to contact you if we need more information.
* **Order Number** (`order_number`, optional): Enter your order number for reference. This will help us to locate your order and investigate the issue.

## Tips

* Please provide as much detail as possible in the description field to help us better understand the issue.
* If you are unable to complete this form, please contact our support team directly for assistance.
* If you have already reported the issue, please do not submit duplicate reports.
