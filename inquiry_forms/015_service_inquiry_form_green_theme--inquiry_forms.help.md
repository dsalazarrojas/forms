# Service Inquiry Form Green Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect service-related inquiries from customers, allowing the company to provide efficient assistance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the required fields**:
	* "What is your question about our service?" (Service Inquiry Question)
	* "How can we help you with your billing concerns?" (Billing Inquiry Question)
	* "What is your account-related inquiry?" (Account Inquiry Question)
2. **Provide additional information**:
	* "Can you provide more information about your account?" (Account Info) (optional)
	* "How can we assist you with your billing issues?" (Billing Assistance) (optional)
	* "Do you have any general questions about our service?" (General Inquiry Question) (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Service Inquiry Question
* **What is your question about our service?** (Required) - Type in a clear and concise description of your inquiry.
### Billing Inquiry Question
* **How can we help you with your billing concerns?** (Required) - Describe your billing concerns in a few sentences.
### Account Inquiry Question
* **What is your account-related inquiry?** (Required) - Explain your account-related question or concern.
### General Inquiry Question
* **Do you have any general questions about our service?** (Optional) - If you have a general question about our service, type it in here.
### Billing Assistance
* **How can we assist you with your billing issues?** (Optional) - If you need help with billing, describe your issue and we'll do our best to assist you.
### Account Info
* **Can you provide more information about your account?** (Optional) - If you have any additional account information that may help us assist you, type it in here.

## Tips
* Please keep your responses clear and concise.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask our support team for help.
* If you have any further questions or concerns after submitting the form, don't hesitate to reach out to us directly.
