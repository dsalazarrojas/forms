# Event Sponsorship Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals or organizations interested in sponsoring an event. It helps us understand their interest, event details, and contact information. Please fill out this form to initiate the sponsorship process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the event you are interested in sponsoring.
2. Provide the date of the event.
3. Select whether the event is yes or no.
4. Enter any additional event details if necessary.
5. Enter your organization's name.
6. Enter your contact person's name.
7. Provide your contact phone number.
8. Enter your email address.
9. Enter your company's name.
10. Enter your department or team name.
11. Enter your position within the organization.
12. Enter your physical address.
13. Enter your zip code.
14. Enter your city.
15. Select whether you are in the United States or not.
16. Select whether you have a country-specific focus or not.
17. Enter any sponsor details.
18. Explain why you are interested in sponsoring the event.
19. Provide your budget for the sponsorship.
20. Select how you would like to be contacted.
21. Enter any additional contact information.
22. Add any additional comments about your sponsorship request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **event name**: Enter the name of the event you are interested in sponsoring.
* **event date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **event type**: Select "Yes" if you are interested in sponsoring the event, "No" otherwise.
* **event details**: Enter any additional information about the event.
* **organization**: Enter the name of your organization.
* **contact person**: Enter the name of the person to contact.
* **phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **email**: Enter your email address.
* **company**: Enter the name of your company.
* **department**: Enter the name of your department or team.
* **position**: Enter your position within the organization.
* **address**: Enter your physical address.
* **zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **city**: Enter the city of your location.
* **country**: Select "Yes" if your organization has a country-specific focus, "No" otherwise.
* **sponsor details**: Enter any additional details about your sponsorship request.
* **why sponsor**: Explain why you are interested in sponsoring the event.
* **budget**: Provide your budget for the sponsorship.
* **contact method**: Select "Yes" if you would like to be contacted via phone, "No" otherwise.
* **contact info**: Enter any additional contact information.
* **additional comments**: Enter any additional comments about your sponsorship request.

Note: All fields are optional unless otherwise specified as required.
