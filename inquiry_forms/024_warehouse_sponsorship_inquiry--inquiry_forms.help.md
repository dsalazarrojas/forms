# Warehouse Sponsorship Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential sponsors about their interest in sponsoring a warehouse project. The form is used by project coordinators to gather relevant details and evaluate sponsorship opportunities. Please complete this form to express your interest in sponsoring a warehouse project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Fill in your contact information on Page 1.
3. Provide details about your sponsorship interest on Page 2.
4. Add any additional information on Page 3 (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Contact Information
* **Contact Information (Text)**: This field is required and should be filled with your name and contact details (email address, phone number, or any other contact method you prefer).

### Page 2: Sponsorship Details
* **Sponsorship Details (Text)**: This field is also required and should be filled with a brief description of your interest in sponsoring a warehouse project. Please be as specific as possible about what you are looking for.

### Page 3: Additional Information (Optional)
* **Additional Information (Text)**: This optional field is a space for you to add any extra details that you think might be helpful for project coordinators to know about your sponsorship interest. Feel free to use this space for any additional information you'd like to share.
