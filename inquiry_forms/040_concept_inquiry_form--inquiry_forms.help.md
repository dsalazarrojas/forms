# Concept Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Concept Inquiry Form is designed to collect information for new concept submissions. It will help our team understand your idea, its feasibility, and potential impact.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Inquiry ID, which is a unique identifier for your submission.
2. Provide the date and time of submission.
3. Enter your name and email address.
4. Choose the department that your concept belongs to.
5. Enter your job title.
6. Enter the title of your concept.
7. Select the category of your concept.
8. Provide a brief summary of your concept.
9. Describe the problem your concept solves and how it solves it.
10. Identify the target audience for your concept.
11. Estimate the market size or impact of your concept.
12. If applicable, describe any similar solutions in the competitive landscape.
13. Explain what makes your concept unique.
14. Outline the implementation requirements.
15. Estimate the time needed to implement your concept.
16. Estimate the cost of implementation.
17. Identify the resources needed to implement your concept.
18. If applicable, list the potential risks.
19. Describe how success will be measured.
20. If applicable, indicate if you will attach supporting documents.
21. Provide a priority recommendation for your concept.
22. Choose the current status of your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inquiry ID** (`inquiry_id`, text, required): A unique identifier for your submission.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): The date and time of submission.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required): Your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Your email address.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): The department that your concept belongs to.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Your job title.
* **Concept Title** (`concept_title`, text, required): The title of your concept.
* **Concept Category** (`concept_category`, select_one, required): The category of your concept.
* **Concept Summary** (`concept_summary`, text, required): A brief summary of your concept.
* **Problem Statement** (`problem_statement`, text, required): A description of the problem your concept solves.
* **Proposed Solution** (`proposed_solution`, text, required): A description of how your concept solves the problem.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, text, required): Who will benefit from your concept.
* **Market Size** (`market_size`, text, optional): Estimated market size or impact.
* **Competitive Landscape** (`competitive_landscape`, text, optional): Description of similar solutions.
* **Unique Value Proposition** (`unique_value_proposition`, text, required): What makes your concept unique.
* **Implementation Requirements** (`implementation_requirements`, text, required): A list of what is needed to implement your concept.
* **Estimated Timeline** (`estimated_timeline`, select_one, required): The estimated time needed to implement your concept.
* **Estimated Cost** (`estimated_cost`, select_one, required): The estimated cost of implementation.
* **Resources Needed** (`resources_needed`, select_multiple, required): A list of resources required to implement your concept.
* **Risks Identified** (`risks_identified`, text, optional): A list of potential risks.
* **Success Metrics** (`success_metrics`, text, optional): How success will be measured.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, select_one, optional): Will you attach documents.
* **Priority Recommendation** (`priority_recommendation`, select_one, required): A priority recommendation for your concept.
* **Inquiry Status** (`inquiry_status`, select_one, required): The current status of your submission.
