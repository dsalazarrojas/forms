# Graphic Design Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Graphic Design Inquiry Form is used to gather information from customers or stakeholders for graphic design projects. This form helps to collect data about the project description, contact details, design services required, project deadline, and other relevant information. It is intended to assist the graphic design team in understanding the project requirements and providing a prompt response.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of your project in the Project Description field.
2. Provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
3. Select the design services you require (e.g., Event Flyer, Business Card, Poster).
4. Specify the project deadline.
5. Optionally, select a category for the project (e.g., Inquiry Forms, Web Design Forms).
6. Enter any additional information in the Assigned Tool field.
7. Fill out the Form ID field (this is for internal reference).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, `text`, required: false): A brief description of your project.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
* **Design Services** (`design_services`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the design services you require (e.g., Event Flyer, Business Card, Poster).
* **Project Deadline** (`project_deadline`, `date`, required: false): Specify the project deadline in date format (e.g., 2024-03-12).
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information related to the project assignment or tool.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required: false): Select a category for the project (e.g., Inquiry Forms, Web Design Forms).
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, `text`, required: false): This is for internal reference, please fill it out (e.g., GD-001).

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the Project Description field to ensure the graphic design team understands the project requirements.
* Double-check the Design Services field to ensure you select all relevant services for your project.
* Make sure to enter the correct email and phone number in the Contact Info field.
* Be realistic when specifying the Project Deadline to avoid delays in project completion.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to reach out to the graphic design team for assistance.
