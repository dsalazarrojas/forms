# Title Transfer Status Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Title Transfer Status Inquiry Form is used to inquire about the status of a title transfer in real estate transactions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the following information as instructed below:
    *   Enter the name of the person buying the property (buyer name).
    *   Provide the address of the property.
    *   Choose the current status of the title transfer.
    *   Select the date of the title transfer.
    *   Choose the team handling the title transfer.
    *   Enter the email of the person inquiring.
    *   Enter the phone number of the person inquiring.
    *   Add any additional notes about the inquiry.
    *   Enter the ID of the tool assigned to the title transfer.
    *   Enter a unique ID for the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Title Transfer Status Inquiry Form** (`title_transfer_status_inquiry_form`, text, required): 
    This is the title of the form.
*  **Buyer name** (`buyer_name`, text, optional): 
    Enter the name of the person buying the property.
*  **Property address** (`property_address`, text, optional): 
    Enter the address of the property.
*  **Current status** (`current_status`, select_one, optional): 
    Choose the current status of the title transfer.
*  **Transfer date** (`transfer_date`, date, optional): 
    Select the date of the title transfer.
*  **Real estate team** (`real_estate_team`, select_multiple, optional): 
    Choose the team handling the title transfer.
*  **Email** (`email`, email, optional): 
    Enter the email address of the person inquiring.
*  **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): 
    Enter the phone number of the person inquiring.
*  **Note** (`note`, note, optional): 
    Add any additional notes about the inquiry.
*  **Assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): 
    Enter the ID of the tool assigned to the title transfer.
*  **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, optional): 
    Enter a unique ID for the form.

## Tips

- Please fill out each field completely and accurately to ensure that your inquiry is processed correctly.
- If you have any further questions or concerns about the title transfer status, please contact the real estate team directly.
- This form is not intended to be used for multiple instances of the same title transfer status inquiry.
