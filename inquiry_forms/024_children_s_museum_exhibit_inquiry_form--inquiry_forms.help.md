# Children's Museum Exhibit Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Children's Museum Exhibit Inquiry Form is designed to collect information from potential exhibitors and proposers for new exhibits. This form helps the museum understand the proposal's details, including the exhibit's title, description, learning objectives, target age group, and logistics.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the contact information section with your name, organization or company name, position, email address, and phone number.
2.  In the "Exhibit Proposal" section, provide a brief overview of your proposal, including the title, description, and learning objectives.
3.  In the "Exhibit Details" section, answer the following questions:
    *   What is the proposed duration of the exhibit (permanent, 1-3 months, 3-6 months, 6-12 months, or traveling exhibit)?
    *   What is the estimated square footage required for the exhibit?
    *   Are there any special equipment or requirements for the exhibit (e.g., electricity, water, climate control, etc.)?
    *   How will children engage with the exhibit (interactive or hands-on elements)?
    *   Are there any accessibility features or accommodations to ensure accessibility (e.g., ADA compliance)?
4.  In the "Educational Value" section, describe how the exhibit connects to learning objectives and standards.
5.  In the "Logistics and Support" section, answer the following questions:
    *   Will you provide setup and installation support for the exhibit?
    *   Will you provide maintenance support for the exhibit?
    *   What level of support will you provide (yes, full setup; yes, partial support; no, museum will handle)?
6.  In the "Budget and Costs" section, estimate the total cost for the exhibit.
