# Unclaimed Assets Recovery Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about unclaimed assets and guide staff through the recovery process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of inquiry you are making from the "Recovery Inquiries" dropdown menu.
2. Fill out the relevant fields for your selected inquiry type.
3. Provide any additional information or notes as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Recovery Inquiries** (`select_multiple`):
	+ A list of available inquires that can be made on this form.
	+ Select the type of inquiry you are making by clicking on the corresponding option.
* **Asset Amount** (`number`):
	+ The monetary value of the asset being inquired about.
	+ Enter the amount in the format of currency.
* **Asset Description** (`text`):
	+ A brief description of the asset being inquired about.
	+ This field is optional but can be useful for providing more context.
* **Asset Type** (`select_one`):
	+ The type of asset being inquired about.
	+ Choose from the available options: Cash, Securities, or Other.
* **Unclaimed Since** (`date`):
	+ The date since the asset has been unclaimed.
	+ Enter a date in the format of month/day/year (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Last Contact Date** (`date`):
	+ The date of the last contact made with the asset owner.
	+ Enter a date in the format of month/day/year (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Unclaimed Value** (`number`):
	+ The monetary value of the asset as of the last known date.
	+ Enter the amount in the format of currency.
* **Status** (`select_one`):
	+ The current status of the asset.
	+ Choose from the available options: Unclaimed, Claimed, or Recovered.
* **Notes** (`note`):
	+ Any additional information or comments regarding the asset.
	+ This field is for any extra details that may be relevant to the inquiry.
* **Requested Documents** (`select_multiple`):
	+ A list of documents that are required for recovery.
	+ Choose from the available options: Identification, Proof of ownership, or Other.
* **Recovery Services** (`select_multiple`):
	+ A list of recovery services that need to be provided.
	+ Choose from the available options: Legal, Financial, or Recovery Services.
