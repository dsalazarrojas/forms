## Banking Pricing And Billing Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Banking Pricing and Billing Inquiry Form is designed to help us understand your concerns and provide a clear and timely response. This form will guide you through a series of questions to gather relevant information and ensure that we address your inquiry efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your customer information:
	* Fill in your full name and email address.
	* Provide a valid phone number.
	* Enter your mailing address.
3. Choose your account details:
	* If you have an account number, please enter it.
	* Select your account type (e.g., Checking, Savings, Business, etc.).
4. Specify your inquiry details:
	* Choose what you want to inquire about (e.g., Account Fees, Service Charges, etc.).
	* Select which fee category is relevant to your inquiry.
	* Provide the transaction date, amount, and description of the issue.
5. Choose your preferred response method and urgency:
	* Select how you would like to be contacted (e.g., Email, Phone, etc.).
	* Indicate the level of urgency (e.g., Standard, Urgent, etc.).
6. Additional information:
	* If you have already made an inquiry about this issue, select "Yes, this is a follow-up."
	* Provide any relevant previous reference numbers.
	* Attach or provide any supporting documents.
	* Indicate your expected resolution.
	* Add any additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Full Name** (`customer_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification or account records.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required): Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
* **Mailing Address** (`customer_address`, `text`, required): Enter your full mailing address, including street address, city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, `text`, optional): If you have an account number, enter it here. If not, leave it blank.
* **Account Type** (`account_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of account you have (e.g., Checking, Savings, Business, etc.).
* **Inquiry Type** (`inquiry_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose what you want to inquire about (e.g., Account Fees, Service Charges, etc.).
* **Fee Category** (`fee_category`, `select_one`, required): Select which fee category is relevant to your inquiry (e.g., Monthly Maintenance, Overdraft, etc.).
* **Transaction Date** (`transaction_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the transaction that caused the concern.
* **Amount in Question** (`amount_in_question`, `number`, optional): Enter the dollar amount related to your concern.
* **Transaction Description** (`transaction_description`, `text`, optional): Describe the transaction as it appears on your statement.
* **Inquiry Details** (`inquiry_details`, `text`, required): Describe your concern or issue.
* **Statement Period** (`statement_period`, `text`, optional): Specify which statement period the transaction occurred.
* **Previous Inquiry** (`previous_inquiry`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes, this is a follow-up" if you have previously inquired about this issue.
* **Previous Reference Number** (`previous_reference_number`, `text`, optional): If you have a reference number, enter it here.
* **Urgency** (`urgency`, `select_one`, required): Choose how urgent your issue is (e.g., Standard, Urgent, etc.).
* **Preferred Response Method** (`preferred_response_method`, `select_one`, required): Select how you would like to be contacted (e.g., Email, Phone, etc.).
* **Best Time to Contact** (`best_time_to_contact`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate when you are available for contact (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, etc.).
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, `select_one`, optional): If you have supporting documents, select "Yes, will attach" or "Yes, will provide."
* **Expected Resolution** (`expected_resolution`, `text`, optional): Indicate your expected outcome or resolution.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional information or comments about your inquiry.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you submit this inquiry.

Please note that this form is designed to help us efficiently address your concerns and provide a timely resolution. If you have any questions or need assistance, please do not hesitate to contact us.
