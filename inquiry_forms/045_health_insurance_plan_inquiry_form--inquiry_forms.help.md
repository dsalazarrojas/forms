# Health Insurance Plan Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users about their health insurance plans. Please fill out this form accurately to help us better understand your insurance needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with the information requested.
2. Select the relevant options for "insurance_type" as per your health insurance plan details.
3. Provide clear and concise information in the "coverage_details" field.
4. Enter the premium rate of your health insurance plan.
5. If you have any additional questions or comments, use the "additional_questions" field.
6. Review the "confirm_info" field to make sure you have filled out the form correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your current address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address associated with your health insurance plan.
* **Insurance Type** (`insurance_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Coverage Details** (`coverage_details`, `text`, optional): Provide clear and concise information about the coverage of your health insurance plan.
* **Premium Rate** (`premium_rate`, `number`, optional): Enter the premium rate of your health insurance plan.
* **Additional Questions** (`additional_questions`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional questions or comments, please provide them here.
* **Confirm Information** (`confirm_info`, `note`, optional): Double-check your information to ensure it is accurate and complete.
