# Course Management Tools Instructor Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for instructors to provide feedback, report issues, and inquire about new features or support related to course management tools. It's a general inquiry form that can be used by both internal and external users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant fields that apply to your inquiry.
2. Fill in your email address in the `Email` field if you want to receive a response.
3. Provide your phone number in the `Phone` field if you want to be contacted by phone.
4. Fill in your school name, location, and other relevant details in the `School Name`, `School Location`, `Training Team`, `School Type`, and `Platform Type` fields as needed.
5. Answer the questions regarding your training experience and needs in the `Training Area`, `Training Level`, `Instructor Name`, and `Platform Version` fields.
6. Select your preferred follow-up method and frequency in the `Follow Up Method` and `Follow Up Frequency` fields.
7. Provide your comments and feedback in the `Comments` field.
8. Submit the form to send your inquiry.
