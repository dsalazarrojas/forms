# Garden Center Customer Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Garden Center Customer Inquiry Form is used to collect information from customers visiting the center. This information helps the center understand customer needs and preferences, allowing them to provide better services and offers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Customer Name in the first field.
2. Enter your Email address in the second field.
3. Write your question or inquiry in the third field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the fourth field.
5. Indicate if you would like to be followed up with more information by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the fifth field.
6. Select the type of plant you are inquiring about (Annuals, Perennials, or Trees) in the sixth field.
7. Enter the name of the specific plant you are inquiring about in the seventh field, if applicable.
8. Select your type of relationship with the center (New Customer or Existing Customer) in the eighth field.
9. Finally, select where you are visiting from (Local Garden Center, Local Nursery, or Online Store) in the ninth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Question** (`question`, text, required: false): Write a brief description of your question or inquiry.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, select_multiple, required: false): Check "Yes" if you would like to be followed up with more information about your inquiry.
* **Plant Type** (`plant_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of plant you are inquiring about (Annuals, Perennials, or Trees).
* **Plant Name** (`plant_name`, text, required: false): If you are inquiring about a specific plant, enter its name.
* **Customer Type** (`customer_type`, select_one, required: false): Select "New Customer" if you are visiting the center for the first time, or "Existing Customer" if you are a regular visitor.
* **Garden Center** (`garden_center`, select_one, required: false): Select where you are visiting from (Local Garden Center, Local Nursery, or Online Store).
