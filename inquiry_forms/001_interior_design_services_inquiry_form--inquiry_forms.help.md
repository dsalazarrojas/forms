# interior_design_services_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help interior designers gather information about a potential project. It is intended for clients who are looking to inquire about interior design services, and the form is used to collect their project details, contact information, and design preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in the "Project Details" field.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
3. Share your design preferences in the "Design Preferences" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details** (Project Details, text, required): This field is where you should provide a brief description of your project, including any relevant details about the location, type of project, and any specific requirements or goals.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required): This field is for you to enter your contact information, including your name, email, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Design Preferences** (Design Preferences, text, optional): This field is where you can share your design preferences, including any specific styles, themes, or styles of interior design you are interested in.
