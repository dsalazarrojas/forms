# Tour Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tour Consultation Form is designed to collect information from clients about their travel needs and preferences for a tour or travel consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your general inquiry in the "General Inquiry" field.
2. Select your travel agency from the provided options, or choose "No" if you don't have a travel agency.
3. Enter your travel agency's phone number in the "Travel Agency Phone" field.
4. Enter your travel agency's email in the "Travel Agency Email" field.
5. Select your travel operator from the provided options, or choose "No" if you don't have a travel operator.
6. Enter your travel operator's phone number in the "Travel Operator Phone" field.
7. Enter your travel operator's email in the "Travel Operator Email" field.
8. Select a date for your travel in the "Travel Date" field.
9. Select a time for your travel in the "Travel Time" field.
10. Enter the start date of your travel in the "Travel Start Date" field.
11. Enter the end date of your travel in the "Travel End Date" field.
12. Enter the duration of your travel in the "Travel Duration" field.
13. Enter the purpose of your travel in the "Travel Purpose" field.
14. Select the priority of your travel from the provided options, or choose "No" if you don't have a priority.
15. If you have any other travel priority, enter it in the "Travel Priority Other" field.
16. Enter any travel notes in the "Travel Notes" field.
17. Enter your client information in the "Client Info" field.
18. Enter your client relation in the "Client Relation" field.
19. Select which tour products you are interested in from the provided options, or choose "No" if you don't have any preferences.
20. If you have any other tour products, enter them in the "Tour Product Other" field.
21. Select the type of package you are interested in from the provided options, or choose "No" if you don't have a package type.
22. If you have any other package type, enter it in the "Package Type Other" field.
23. Enter the price of your tour in the "Tour Price" field.
24. Enter any tour terms in the "Tour Terms" field.
25. If you have any other tour terms, enter them in the "Tour Terms Other" field.
