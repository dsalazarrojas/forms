# University Thesis Support Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Thesis Support Inquiry Form is a tool used to gather information about your thesis support needs. This form is designed to help you get support from the university for your thesis work. Please fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure that you receive the support you need.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Name and Email address, as these are required fields.
2. Select your University Department from the list provided.
3. Choose your Academic Advisor from the list provided.
4. Enter your Expected Completion Date.
5. If applicable, select the Current Status of your thesis.
6. Provide a description of your support request in the Support Request field.
7. (Optional) You may also provide follow-up information to ensure that we follow up with you correctly, such as follow-up contact methods and follow-up actions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your university records.
* **Email**: Enter your university email address to ensure that we can contact you directly.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone. This is an optional field.
* **Department**: Select the department to which your thesis belongs. This will help us identify the relevant support staff.
* **Academic Advisor**: Select the academic advisor who is guiding your thesis work. This will help us identify the correct person to contact for support.
* **Thesis Topic**: Enter a brief description of your thesis topic, if applicable.
* **Expected Completion Date**: Enter the date by which you expect to complete your thesis.
* **Current Status**: If your thesis is currently in progress, completed, not started, on hold, or still a dissertation, select the correct status.
* **Support Request**: Describe the support you need from us, and we will follow up with the next steps.
* **Follow-up Question**: If you have any follow-up questions, enter them here.
* **Submitted to**: If you have submitted your thesis to someone, select the correct person or department.
* **Follow-up Contact**: Choose how you would like to be contacted for follow-up, such as email, phone, mail, or in-person.
* **Follow-up Note**: Any additional information about your follow-up contact preferences.
* **Follow-up Action**: Choose the action that will be taken after follow-up, such as sending a reminder, sending a report, scheduling a meeting, or assigning a task.
* **Follow-up Frequency**: Choose how often you would like to be contacted for follow-up, such as daily, weekly, biweekly, monthly, or quarterly.
* **Follow-up Date**: Enter the date by which you would like to be followed up on.
* **Follow-up Time**: Enter the time of day you would like to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Location**: Enter the location where you would like to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Note 2**: Any additional information about your follow-up preferences.
* **Submitted by**: If someone else submitted your thesis, select the correct person or department.
* **Submitted Date**: Enter the date your thesis was submitted.
* **Follow-up Contact 2**: Choose how you would like to be contacted for follow-up, such as email, phone, mail, or in-person.
* **Follow-up Note 3**: Any additional information about your follow-up preferences.
* **Follow-up Comment**: Enter any additional comments or information about your follow-up preferences.
* **Form Version**: Enter the version of this form you are using, if applicable.

Note: All fields marked as "required" are mandatory. Please fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
