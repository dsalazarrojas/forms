# Property Investment Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from potential property investors about their interests in investing in properties. Please fill out this form if you are interested in exploring investment opportunities in properties.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in all the required fields (marked with \***\*).
2. Select the relevant options from the dropdown menus where applicable.
3. Enter any additional comments or details you'd like to share in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name** (Required): Enter your name.
* **Contact Email** (Required): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Property Type** (Optional): Select the type of property you are interested in (e.g., single family residence, condominium, etc.).
* **Property Location** (Optional): Select the location type (e.g., urban, suburban, rural).
* **Investment Period** (Required): Enter the date you are interested in investing.
* **Property Price** (Optional): Enter the price of the property.
* **Property Value** (Optional): Enter the value of the property.
* **Property Size** (Optional): Enter the size of the property.
* **Property Area** (Optional): Enter the area of the property.
* **Investor Name** (Required): Enter the name of the investor or company.
* **Investor Organization** (Required): Enter the name of the investor's organization.
* **Investor Email** (Required): Enter the email address of the investor.
* **Investor Phone** (Required): Enter the phone number of the investor.
* **Property Use** (Required): Select the intended use of the property (e.g., residential, commercial, industrial).
* **Investor Experience** (Optional): Select your level of experience as an investor (e.g., beginner, intermediate, advanced).
* **Investment Amount** (Required): Enter the amount you are interested in investing.
* **Property Address** (Required): Enter the address of the property.
* **City** (Required): Enter the city of the property.
* **State** (Required): Enter the state of the property.
* **Zip** (Required): Enter the zip code of the property.
* **Property Description** (Optional): Enter a brief description of the property.
* **Additional Comments** (Optional): Add any additional comments or details you'd like to share.
* **Submission Date** (Optional): Leave this field blank, as it will be automatically filled in when you submit the form.
