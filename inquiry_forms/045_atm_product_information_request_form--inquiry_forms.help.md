# ATM Product Information Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to gather essential information about your business and ATM requirements. Please fill out this form to help us provide you with the most suitable quote or proposal for your ATM needs.

## How To Complete This Form

- Read each section carefully and fill out all required fields.
- Make sure to provide accurate and complete information about your business and ATM requirements.
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the legal name of your business.
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Full name of the person who will handle ATM services.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Position of the contact person.
- **Business Address** (`business_address`, `text`, required): Complete physical address of your business.
- **Type of Business** (`business_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the category that best describes your business.
- **Estimated Monthly Transactions** (`estimated_monthly_transactions`, `number`, optional): Approximate number of transactions per month.
- **ATM Location Details** (`atm_location_details`, `text`, required): Describe where the ATM will be located within your premises.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Business email for correspondence.
- **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Business phone number for follow-up calls.
- **Preferred ATM Features** (`preferred_atm_features`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all features you're interested in.
  - Bill Payment
  - Check Deposit
  - Cash Withdrawal
  - Balance Inquiry
  - Multi-Language Support
  - Accessibility Features
  - Receipt Printing
- **Desired Installation Timeline** (`installation_timeline`, `date`, optional): When would you like the ATM installed and operational?
- **Budget Range for Services** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, optional): Estimated monthly budget for ATM services.
  - Under $500
  - $500-$1,000
  - $1,000-$2,000
  - $2,000-$5,000
  - Over $5,000
- **Additional Comments or Special Requirements** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Any other information about your ATM needs.
