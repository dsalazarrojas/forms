# Medical Device Professional Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from professionals in the medical device industry for inquiry purposes. It is meant to be completed by individuals seeking guidance, support, or resources related to medical devices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone Number.
2. Specify the type of medical device you are inquiring about by selecting the correct Category.
3. Provide details about your company and department by selecting the appropriate options for Department and Role (if applicable).
4. Fill in the Contact Name, Contact Title, Contact Email, and Contact Phone fields with information about the person your inquiry is directed to.
5. Optionally, provide your First Name, Last Name, and Phone Number if you are a representative from your company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Provide your phone number for us to call you back.
* **Medical Device Name** (`medical_device_name`, `text`, optional): If you are inquiring about a specific medical device, please provide its name.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter the name of your company.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Please select the department that best represents your role or area of interest within your company.
    - Medical Device Company
    - Regulatory and Compliance
    - Manufacturing
* **Role** (`role`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you are inquiring as a representative of your company, please select all applicable roles.
    - Yes
    - No
* **Category** (`medical_device_category`, `select_one`, required): Please select the category that best describes the type of medical device you are inquiring about.
    - Class III
    - Class II
    - Class I
    - Software
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, optional): If you are inquiring as a representative of your company, please provide the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, `text`, optional): Enter the title of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `text`, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Provide the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Company** (`contact_company`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the contact company.

## Tips
- Please provide clear and accurate information to ensure that we can best assist with your inquiry.
- If you are inquiring about a specific medical device, please provide its name.
- If you are a representative of your company, please provide all applicable roles.
