# Hiring Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information about available job openings and gather inquiries from interested individuals.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a job title that matches your interest.
2. Provide your first name, last name, and email address.
3. Enter your phone number (optional).
4. Specify your job type preference (full-time, part-time, contract, or internship).
5. Choose the status of the job you are interested in (active, available, closed, pending, or retired).
6. Enter any additional notes or comments about the job you are interested in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Inquiry** (`new_inquiry`, text, required: false): This field allows you to enter any new inquiries or comments about available positions.
* **Applicant Info** (`applicant_info`, text, required: false): This field is optional and provides an opportunity to enter any additional information about yourself.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, number, required: false): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Select a job title that matches your interest.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the company you are interested in.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the job you are interested in.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, required: false): Choose the status of the job you are interested in (active, available, closed, pending, or retired).
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of job you are interested in (full-time, part-time, contract, or internship).
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, text, required: false): Enter the category of the job you are interested in.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the job you are interested in.
* **Job Date** (`job_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the job you are interested in.
* **Job Time** (`job_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the job you are interested in.
* **Additional Notes** (`note`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about the job you are interested in.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have already inquired about a job, please do not submit duplicate inquires.
* Please note that this form is for internal use only, and any submitted information will be reviewed by our team internally.
