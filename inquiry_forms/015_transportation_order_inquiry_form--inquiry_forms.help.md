# transportation_order_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers for transportation-related inquiries. It collects essential details such as customer name, company, contact number, email, date, time, and contract agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
* Provide the name of the company associated with the customer in the "Company" field.
* Enter the customer's contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
* Enter the customer's email address in the "Email" field.
* Select the date for the transportation order from the "Date" field.
* Select the time for the transportation order from the "Time" field.
* If necessary, provide any contract agreement details in the "Contract Agreement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the customer who is inquiring about the transportation order.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required/optional): Provide the name of the company associated with the customer.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact number of the customer.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the customer for easy communication.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Select the date for the transportation order.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Select the time for the transportation order.
* **Contract Agreement** (`contract_agreement`, text, required/optional): If necessary, provide any contract agreement details or terms for the transportation order.
* **Tips**: Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely to facilitate efficient processing of the inquiry.
