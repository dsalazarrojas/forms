# Telecom Billing Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for telecom billing inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Make sure to provide as much detail as possible in the issue description field.
3. Choose the correct telecom provider from the list.
4. Enter any additional information about the telecom billing issue in the issue details field.
5. Provide the customer's contact information, including phone number and email.
6. If assigned to an agent, provide their name and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, *): Enter the customer's name as it appears on their account.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, note, *): Describe the billing issue you're experiencing.
* **Billing Date** (`billing_date`, date): Enter the date of the billing issue.
* **Telecom Provider** (`telecom_provider`, select_one, *): Choose the telecom provider associated with your account.
* **Issue Details** (`issue_details`, note, *): Provide any additional information about the issue.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, *): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, *): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Assigned Agent** (`assigned_agent`, select_multiple, optional): Check if the issue is assigned to an agent.
* **Issue Status** (`issue_status`, select_one, *): Choose the current status of the issue.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the date for the follow-up.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, note, optional): Enter any notes from the customer.
* **Agent Notes** (`agent_notes`, note, optional): Enter any notes from the agent.
* **Telecom Billing Number** (`telecom_billing_number`, text, *): Enter the telecom billing number associated with your account.
* **Telecom Provider Name** (`telecom_provider_name`, text, *): Enter the name of the telecom provider associated with your account.
* **Customer Service Representative** (`customer_service_representative`, text, optional): Enter the customer service representative's name.
* **Customer Service Representative Phone** (`customer_service_representative_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer service representative's phone number.
* **Customer Service Representative Email** (`customer_service_representative_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer service representative's email address.
* **Customer Service Representative Name** (`customer_service_representative_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer service representative's name.
* **Telecom Billing Number 2** (`telecom_billing_number_2`, text, optional): Enter the second telecom billing number associated with your account.
* **Telecom Billing Number 3** (`telecom_billing_number_3`, text, optional): Enter the third telecom billing number associated with your account.
* **Telecom Number** (`telecom_number`, text, optional): Enter the telecom number associated with your account.
* **Telecom Number 2** (`telecom_number_2`, text, optional): Enter the second telecom number associated with your account.
* **Telecom Provider Number** (`telecom_provider_number`, text, optional): Enter the telecom provider number associated with your account.
* **Telecom Provider Phone** (`telecom_provider_phone`, text, optional): Enter the telecom provider's phone number.
* **Telecom Provider Email** (`telecom_provider_email`, email, optional): Enter the telecom provider's email address.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Provide as much detail as possible in the issue description field.
* If the issue is assigned to an agent, please ensure that the customer is aware of who is working on their issue.
