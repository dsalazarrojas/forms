# Custom Solar System Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers who are interested in inquiring about custom solar system solutions for their properties. The questions cover various aspects of the inquiry, such as energy usage, preferred installers, and property details, to provide a comprehensive understanding of the customer's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your property details in the "Property Details" field.
2. Choose your energy usage level: "Low", "Medium", or "High".
3. Select your energy preferences from the list of options.
4. Provide your email address.
5. Specify the source of your lead: Social Media, Website, Referral, or Other.
6. Enter your prospect details, if applicable.
7. Estimate your energy savings for your property.
8. Enter any installation details.
9. Choose whether you have a preferred installer(s).
10. If you have multiple preferred installers, enter their information.
11. Confirm if you have any other preferred installers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Details**: Enter your property's address, location, or any other relevant details to help us understand your inquiry.
* **Energy Usage**: This field is for you to select your energy usage level: Low, Medium, or High. This will help us understand how much solar power you might need.
* **Energy Preferences**: Here you can select your energy preferences, such as whether you want to prioritize energy efficiency, cost savings, or other factors.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address so we can reach out to you with updates and follow-ups.
* **Lead Source**: Select where you heard about us: Social Media, Website, Referral, or Other. This helps us understand how you found us.
* **Prospect Details**: Enter any additional details about the prospect you're inquiring for.
* **Energy Savings Estimation**: Enter an estimate of your energy savings for your property. This will help us understand your expectations.
* **Installation Details**: Enter any relevant installation details, such as roof type or size.
* **Preferred Installer(s)**: Check "Yes" if you have a preferred installer(s) and enter their information in the fields below.
* **Other Preferred Installer(s)**: If you have additional installers you prefer, enter their details here.
* **Preferred Installers Info**: Check "Yes" if you want us to contact your preferred installer(s) and enter their contact information.
* **Preferred Installers Address**: Enter the address of your preferred installer(s).

Note: Please be as detailed as possible when answering the questions, as this will help us better understand your needs and provide you with accurate information and solutions.
