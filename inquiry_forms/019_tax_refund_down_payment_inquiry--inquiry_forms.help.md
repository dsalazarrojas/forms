# Tax Refund Down Payment Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a tax refund down payment inquiry from a client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide your client's details such as their name, address, and contact information.
2. Specify the type of tax refund (yes or no).
3. Enter the amount of the tax refund.
4. Provide the payment date and time.
5. Add any comments or additional information about the payment.
6. Select the type of form this is (yes or no).
7. Select the category of the form (yes or no).
8. Enter a brief description of the form.
9. If assigned, enter the assigned user's name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Tax Refund Down Payment Inquiry** (`tax_refund_down_payment_inquiry_form`, text, required): This is the title of the form.
- **Client Information** (`client_info`, text, required): Enter your client's name, address, and contact information.
  - **Client First Name** (`client_first_name`, text, required): Enter your client's first name.
  - **Client Last Name** (`client_last_name`, text, required): Enter your client's last name.
  - **Client Address** (`client_address`, text, required): Enter your client's address.
  - **Client City** (`client_city`, text, required): Enter your client's city.
  - **Client State** (`client_state`, select_one, required): Select the state from the options provided.
  - **Client Postal Code** (`client_postal_code`, text, required): Enter your client's postal code.
  - **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter your client's email address.
  - **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required): Enter your client's phone number.
- **Client IP** (`client_ip`, text, required): Enter your client's IP address.
- **Tax Refund Type** (`tax_refund_type`, select_one, required): Specify the type of tax refund (yes or no).
- **Tax Refund Amount** (`tax_refund_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of the tax refund.
- **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the payment.
- **Payment Time** (`payment_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the payment.
- **Reason for Payment** (`reason_for_payment`, text, required): Enter a reason for the payment.
- **Form Type** (`form_type`, select_one, required): Specify the type of form this is (yes or no).
- **Form Category** (`form_category`, select_one, required): Select the category of the form (yes or no).
- **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the form.
- **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, optional): If assigned, enter the name of the assigned user.

## Tips

- Make sure to double-check your client's details before submitting the form.
- Ensure that you're providing accurate information for each field.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for assistance.
