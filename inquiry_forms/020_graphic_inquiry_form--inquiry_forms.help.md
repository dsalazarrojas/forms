# Graphic Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for graphic design or other related services, including service type and file attachments.

## How to Complete This Form
- **Fill out the form submission date**: Enter the date of the form submission.
- **Enter your name**: Provide your full name for contact purposes.
- **Enter your phone number**: Enter your phone number for communication.
- **Enter your email**: Provide your email address for communication.
- **Select your service**: Choose the type of service you need, such as Graphic Design, Web Development, or UI/UX.
- **Describe your service request**: Provide a brief description of your request, if needed.
- **Attach relevant files**: Choose which files are relevant to your request.
- **Choose the assigned tool**: Select which tool should be assigned to your request, with options like "chatjimmy" or other.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form submission (1)**: A date to track when the form was submitted.
* **Name (2)**: A field to store your name for contact purposes.
* **Phone number (3)**: The field to enter your phone number.
* **Email (4)**: The field for your email address.
* **Service (5)**: Choose which service you require, with options like Graphic Design or Web Development.
* **Description (6)**: A text field to provide more information about your service request.
* **Files (7)**: A multiple-choice field to select which files are relevant to your service request.
* **Assigned Tool (8)**: Select which tool should be assigned to your request, with options like "chatjimmy" or other.
