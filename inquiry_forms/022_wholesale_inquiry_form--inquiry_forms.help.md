# Wholesale Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The wholesale inquiry form is used to collect information from businesses or individuals who are interested in inquiring about wholesale products or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Business Name (*)
• A required field that asks for the business or individual's name.

### Contact Person
• A field to provide the contact person's name.

### Contact Email
• A field to provide the contact email.

### Phone Number
• A field to provide the contact phone number.

### Company Name
• A field to provide the company name.

### Product Name
• A field to provide the name of the product or service you are inquiring about.

### Product Description
• A field to provide a short description of the product or service.

### Company Address
• A field to provide the company address.

### Website URL
• A field to provide the company website URL.

### Sales Rep
• A field to provide the sales representative's name.

### Product Categories (Select all that apply)
• A field to select the product categories that interest you.

### Product Tags (Select all that apply)
• A field to select the product tags that interest you.

### Inquiry Type
• A field to select the type of inquiry (e.g., product inquiry, pricing inquiry, etc.).

### Inquiry Message
• A field to provide a brief message about your inquiry.

## Tips
• Please provide accurate and complete information for each field.
• If you have any questions or need further assistance, feel free to contact us.
• We will review and respond to your inquiry as soon as possible.
