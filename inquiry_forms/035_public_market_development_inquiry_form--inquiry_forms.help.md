# public_market_development_inquiry_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for users to submit inquiries about public market development, including feedback, questions, or suggestions. Please use this form to provide your input and suggestions to help us improve our public market development efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your organization and name in the respective fields.
3. Provide your email address to allow us to contact you in case we need more information.
4. Choose the type of inquiry you are submitting (Feedback, Question, or Suggestion).
5. Describe your inquiry in the inquiry description field.
6. Select your preferred level of participation initiative (True, False, or Undecided).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization** (text): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Name** (text): Enter your name.
* **Email** (email): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Department** (text): Enter your department or team name (optional).
* **Inquiry Type** (select_one): Choose one of the following options:
	+ **Feedback**: Provide feedback about our public market development efforts.
	+ **Question**: Ask a question related to our public market development.
	+ **Suggestion**: Share a suggestion for improvement or enhancement.
* **Inquiry Description** (text): Briefly describe your inquiry in this field.
* **Participation Initiative** (select_multiple): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ **True**: You are willing to participate in future initiatives.
	+ **False**: You are not willing to participate.
	+ **Undecided**: You are undecided about participating.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your inquiry is processed correctly.
* Be concise and clear in your description to help us understand your needs.
* If you have any attachments or supporting information, you can email them to us separately.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
