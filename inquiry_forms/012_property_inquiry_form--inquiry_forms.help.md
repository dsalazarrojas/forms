# Property Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Inquiry Form is designed to collect information from users about properties they are inquiring about.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form with the required fields marked with a `*` asterisk.
- You may fill out the optional fields if you have additional information to provide.
- If you are unsure about a field, please ask a form administrator for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What kind of property are you looking for** (`property_inquiry_form`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field is used to select the type of property you are inquiring about. Please choose one or more of the following options:
  - Residential
  - Commercial
  - Rental
  - Land
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
- **Property Type** (`property_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of property you are inquiring about. Please choose one of the following options:
  - Residential
  - Commercial
  - Rental
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Inquiry Date** (`inquiry_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the inquiry.
- **Inquiry Time** (`inquiry_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the inquiry.
- **Property Location** (`property_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address or region of the property.
- **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional details about the property.
- **Message** (`contact_message`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a brief message about the inquiry.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required: true): Please enter the tool assigned for this inquiry.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
- If you are unable to find the required information, please ask a form administrator for assistance.
- The form is designed to be completed by the individual making the inquiry.
