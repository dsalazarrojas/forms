# online_language_course_inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online language course inquiry form is designed to collect information about users who are interested in online language courses. This form will help us better understand their needs and preferences, and will enable us to provide them with relevant course suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the online course you are interested in from the "Which course are you interested in?" dropdown menu.
6. Select all the languages you are fluent in from the "What language are you fluent in?" dropdown menu.
7. Optionally, enter any additional comments or questions in the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (1, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (3, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
- **Phone** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Which course are you interested in?** (5, select_one, required): Select one of the available courses.
  * Options: '1', '2', '3'
- **What language are you fluent in?** (6, select_multiple, required): Select all the languages you are fluent in.
  * Options: English, Spanish
- **Message** (7, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you have.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
* Select only one course from the dropdown menu.
* You can select multiple languages you are fluent in.
* If you have any additional questions or comments, use the "Message" field to provide more information.
