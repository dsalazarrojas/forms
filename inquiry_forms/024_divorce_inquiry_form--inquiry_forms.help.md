# Divorce Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Divorce Inquiry Form is a tool used to collect essential information from clients about their divorce cases, helping law firms or legal professionals to efficiently manage and process cases.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form accurately, please follow the instructions below:

1.  **Client Information**
    - Please provide your basic contact details, including your name and address.
2.  **Case Details**
    - Share as much information as possible about your case, including any relevant dates, numbers, or other important details.
3.  **Communication History**
    - Explain how you've communicated with your spouse, ex-spouse, or other parties involved in the case.
4.  **Financials**
    - Share any financial information relevant to the case, such as assets, debts, or income.
5.  **Other Important Info**
    - Include any other information you think is essential to your case.
6.  **Client Consent**
    - Confirm that you've obtained consent from your spouse or ex-spouse for the collection and use of this information.
7.  **Attorney Contact**
    - Provide contact information for your attorney, including their name, firm, email, and phone number.
8.  **Divorce Status**
    - Indicate the current status of your divorce case (e.g., pending, finalized, or ongoing).
9.  **Case Type**
    - Select the type of divorce or court case you're dealing with (e.g., Divorce, Legal Separation, or other).
10. **Jurisdiction**
    - Specify the jurisdiction where your case is being handled (e.g., court, state, or country).
11.  **Court Filing**
    - Record the date when you filed your case with the court (if applicable).
12.  **Court Date**
    - Record the date of the court hearing or proceeding (if applicable).
13.  **Court Case Number**
    - Provide the unique case number assigned by the court (if applicable).
14.  **Case Number**
    - Record the case number used internally by your attorney or law firm (if different from the court's case number).
15.  **Client Address**
    - Provide your current address, including any relevant details like apartment number or floor.
16.  **Divorce Date**
    - Record the date you separated from your spouse or the date you expect to separate.
17.  **Lawyer Name**
    - Provide your attorney's name (if different from the other attorney information).
18.  **Lawyer Firm**
    - Specify your attorney's firm or law firm name.
19.  **Lawyer Email**
    - Provide your attorney's email address.
20.  **Lawyer Phone**
    - Record your attorney's phone number.
21.  **Lawyer Address**
    - Provide your attorney's address.
22.  **Other Attorney Info**
    - Include any additional information about your attorney or law firm.
23.  **Other Attorney Name**
    - Provide the name of the other attorney involved in your case.
24.  **Other Attorney Firm**
    - Specify the firm or law firm of the other attorney.
25.  **Other Attorney Phone**
    - Record the phone number of the other attorney.
