# Orlando Holiday Vacation Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your travel plans and preferences to provide you with the best-suited vacation package options. This form will help us tailor our offerings to fit your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form carefully, ensuring all fields are accurate and complete.
2.  Enter your traveler count to let us know how many people will be traveling with you.
3.  Select your start and end dates for your trip.
4.  If you have any special needs, please let us know by typing them in the text field.
5.  For each preference, select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your interest in that particular feature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Traveler count**: This field is used to determine the size of your group.
    (Type: number, Required: false)
*   **Start date**: This field is used to set the start date of your trip.
    (Type: date, Required: false)
*   **End date**: This field is used to set the end date of your trip.
    (Type: date, Required: false)
*   **Special needs**: If you have any special requirements or needs for your trip, please specify them here.
    (Type: text, Required: false)
*   **Preference 1**: Do you prefer a hotel with a pool?
    (Type: select_one, Options: 'Yes', 'No', Required: false)
*   **Preference 2**: Do you prefer a hotel with a kitchen?
    (Type: select_one, Options: 'Yes', 'No', Required: false)
*   **Preference 3**: Do you have a preference for a hotel with a fitness center?
    (Type: select_one, Options: 'Yes', 'No', Required: false)
*   **Preference 4**: Do you have a preference for a hotel with a free breakfast?
    (Type: select_one, Options: 'Yes', 'No', Required: false)

## Tips

*   Please fill out all fields accurately and completely.
*   If you have any questions or concerns about the form, feel free to contact us. 
*   We will use the information provided to provide you with the best-suited vacation package options.
