# Biotech Research Collaboration Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to allow organizations to express interest in potential collaborations with our biotechnology research team. We encourage you to provide detailed information about your organization, research interests, and collaboration goals to facilitate a smooth and effective collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please ensure that you only fill out this form if you have a genuine interest in collaborating with our team.**
2. **Please do not submit duplicate submissions as this will help us avoid unnecessary communication and prevent duplicate work.**
3. **Fill out the form carefully and accurately, and ensure that you have all necessary information ready before submitting.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (id 2): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (id 3): Your last name.
* **Professional Title** (id 4): Your job title or position in your organization.
* **Email Address** (id 5): Your professional email address.
* **Phone Number** (id 6): Your contact number.
* **Organization Information** (id 7): Enter your organization's details, if applicable.
* **Organization Name** (id 8): Your company or institution name.
* **Organization Type** (id 9): Select the type of organization you represent (e.g., Biotechnology Company, Academic Institution, etc.).
* **Organization Size** (id 10): Select the number of employees in your organization.
* **Organization Website** (id 11): The URL of your organization's website, if applicable.
* **Collaboration Inquiry** (id 12): Briefly describe the purpose of your collaboration interest.
* **Type of Inquiry** (id 13): Select the type of collaboration you are seeking (e.g., Research Collaboration, Licensing Opportunity, etc.).
* **Research Area of Interest** (id 14): Select relevant research areas that align with our team's expertise.
* **Collaboration Description** (id 15): Describe your collaboration proposal in detail.
* **Current Project Stage** (id 16): Select the current stage of your project (e.g., Early Research, Clinical Development, etc.).
* **Expected Timeline** (id 17): Choose when you would like to start the collaboration.
* **Resources You Can Offer** (id 18): Select the resources you can contribute to the collaboration (e.g., Research Expertise, Laboratory Facilities, etc.).
* **Resources You Need** (id 19): Describe the resources you require from a partner.
* **Funding Availability** (id 20): Indicate if funding is available for the collaboration (e.g., Yes - Fully Funded, Yes - Partially Funded, etc.).
* **Funding Range** (id 21): If applicable, enter the estimated funding amount.
* **Intellectual Property** (id 22): Briefly describe any intellectual property considerations.
* **IP Status** (id 23): Indicate the status of relevant intellectual property (e.g., Patents Filed, Patents Granted, etc.).
* **IP Description** (id 24): Describe your intellectual property in brief.
* **Confidentiality** (id 25): Indicate your willingness to sign a non-disclosure agreement (NDA).
* **Previous Collaborations** (id 26): Select if you have had previous successful collaborations.
* **References** (id 27): Indicate if you can provide references (e.g., Yes - Available Upon Request, etc.).
* **Reference Details** (id 28): List references if applicable.
* **Additional Information** (id 29): Enter any other relevant information about your organization or collaboration goals.
* **Follow-up Preference** (id 30): Choose how you would like to be contacted for follow-ups (e.g., Email, Phone Call, etc.).
* **Best Contact Time** (id 31): Select the best time of day to contact you (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, etc.).
* **Time Zone** (id 32): Enter your time zone (optional).
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (id 33): Help us improve outreach by indicating how you heard about us (e.g., Website, Social Media, etc.).
* **Urgency Level** (id 34): Indicate the level of urgency for your inquiry (e.g., Low - General Inquiry, High - Ready to Move Forward, etc.).
* **Submission Date** (id 35): Enter the date of your submission.

**Tips:**

* Please fill out the form thoroughly and accurately to ensure that we understand your collaboration interests and goals.
* If you are unsure about any field, please use the "help" or "support" options available on the form to clarify your questions.
* We will review and respond to all submissions in a timely manner.
