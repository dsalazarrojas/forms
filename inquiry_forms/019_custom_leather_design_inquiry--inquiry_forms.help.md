# Custom Leather Design Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Leather Design Inquiry form is a tool used to gather information from customers about their custom leather design needs and preferences. It is intended for customers who are interested in inquiring about custom leather design services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit Inquiry" button to start the form.
2. Fill in the required fields (customer name, email, and project description).
3. Optional fields (phone and custom_leather_design_inquiry) can be filled in or left blank.
4. Review your information and click "Submit" to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Custom Leather Design Inquiry** (`custom_leather_design_inquiry`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your custom leather design project, including details about what you are looking for in a custom product. For example, "I need a custom leather wallet with a specific design" or "I am looking for a custom leather bag with unique features."
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name if you'd like to provide it. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your inquiry.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to provide it. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of your custom leather project, including any specific materials, colors, or features you are looking for.
 
## Tips
- Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any additional questions or concerns, contact us directly.
- We will review your inquiry and respond as soon as possible.
