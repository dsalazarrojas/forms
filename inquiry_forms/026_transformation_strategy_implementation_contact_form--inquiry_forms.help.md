# Transformation Strategy Implementation Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from customers or stakeholders who have inquired about or are interested in discussing transformation strategy implementation. It helps us better understand their needs and preferences, allowing us to provide more effective support and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your information. If a field is optional, feel free to leave it blank if not applicable.
2. Ensure that all required fields are accurate and up-to-date.
3. Review your submissions before clicking the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consultant** (`consultant_name`, text, optional): Provide your name and title (e.g., Consultant, Project Manager, etc.). This will help us identify who you are and your role in the project.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address in the format `yourname@yourdomain.com`. This will help us contact you regarding your inquiry.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in the format `+1234567890` or `123-456-7890`. This will help us contact you regarding your inquiry.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, optional): Provide the name of the project you are interested in or have inquiries about. If not applicable, leave it blank.
* **Transformation Strategy** (`transformation_strategy`, select one, optional): Choose the relevant transformation strategy type (Inquiry or Implementation). This will help us understand the nature of your inquiry.
* **Team Member Name** (`team_member_name`, text, optional): If you are working with a team, please enter the name of your team member. If not applicable, leave it blank.
* **Contact Date** (`contact_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you contacted us about the transformation strategy. If not applicable, leave it blank.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to be followed up with regarding your inquiry. If not applicable, leave it blank.
* **Lead Status** (`lead_status`, select one, optional): Choose the status of your lead (Qualified or Unqualified). This will help us prioritize your inquiry.
* **Follow-up Method** (`follow_up_method`, select one, optional): Choose the method you would like to be followed up with (Phone, Email, or Meeting). This will help us contact you in the most effective way.
* **Next Follow-up Date** (`next_follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to be followed up with next regarding your inquiry. If not applicable, leave it blank.
* **Follow-up Description** (`follow_up_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the follow-up discussion or meeting you would like to have. If not applicable, leave it blank.
