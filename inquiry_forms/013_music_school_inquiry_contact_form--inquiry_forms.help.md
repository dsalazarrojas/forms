# Music School Inquiry Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users who wish to inquire about music-related services or products offered by the music school.

## How To Complete This Form
- Begin by providing a title for your inquiry in the "Music School Inquiry Contact Form" field.
- Provide your name and email address to contact you.
- Enter your phone number for easy communication.
- Select the specific area of interest you'd like to inquire about (Lessons, Performance Opportunities, Instrument Sales, or Other).
- Choose when you'd like to start scheduling appointments (Weekdays, Weekends, or Flexible).
- If you have any additional comments or questions, provide them in the "Do you have any additional comments" field.
- Click the "Submit" button to send your inquiry to the music school.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Music School Inquiry Contact Form** (id: 1, text, required: false): This field is for a brief title or description of your inquiry.
* **Student Name** (id: 2, text, required: false): Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Email** (id: 3, email, required: false): Enter your email address so the music school staff can contact you.
* **Phone** (id: 4, text, required: false): Provide your phone number for easy communication.
* **What do you want to inquire about** (id: 5, select_one, required: false): Select the specific service or product you are interested in.
	+ Lessons: Lessons with a music teacher or instructor.
	+ Performance Opportunities: Opportunities to perform in front of an audience.
	+ Instrument Sales: Buying music-related products or equipment.
	+ Other: Anything else related to music or the music school.
* **When would you like to start** (id: 6, select_multiple, required: false): Choose when you'd like to schedule your appointments or inquiries.
	+ Weekdays: Monday through Friday.
	+ Weekends: Saturday and Sunday.
	+ Flexible: Any day of the week.
* **Do you have any additional comments** (id: 7, note, required: false): Add any additional information or questions you'd like to share with the music school staff.
* **Submit** (id: 8, text, required: false): Click this button to send your inquiry to the music school.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you enter accurate and relevant information to help the music school staff better address your needs.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the music school staff directly.
- You can also visit the music school's website or social media pages for more information and updates.
