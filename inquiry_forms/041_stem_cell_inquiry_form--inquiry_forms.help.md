# Stem Cell Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stem Cell Inquiry Form is a tool used to collect information for various inquiries related to stem cells. This form helps in gathering details about patients, staff, or public inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Stem Cell Inquiry Form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your patient's name in the field "Patient Name".
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. If necessary, provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Select the type of inquiry from the options "Public Inquiry", "Patient Inquiry", or "Staff Inquiry" in the "Inquiry Type" field.
5. Select the type of stem cell from the options "Cord Blood", "Peripheral Blood", "Adipose Tissue", "Placenta", or "UM B" in the "Stem Cell Type" field.
6. If you are a patient, enter your date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field (MM/DD/YYYY).
7. Provide any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.
8. Enter the patient's ID number in the "Patient ID" field.
9. Select the relevant department from the options in the "Stem Cell Institute Name" field.
10. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
11. Provide your contact details in the "Contact Details" field.
12. Select who submitted the form from the options "Patient", "Public", or "Staff" in the "Stem Cell Inquiry Form Submitter" field.
13. Enter the date you submitted the form (MM/DD/YYYY) in the "Stem Cell Inquiry Form Submitter On" field.
14. Enter your email address in the "Stem Cell Inquiry Form Submitter Email" field.
15. Enter your phone number in the "Stem Cell Inquiry Form Submitter Phone" field.
16. Select the department where you work from the options in the "Stem Cell Inquiry Form Submitter Department" field.
17. Enter your job title in the "Stem Cell Inquiry Form Submitter Job Title" field.
18. Enter a unique ID in the "Stem Cell Inquiry Form Submitter Id" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the patient or person inquiring.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): If necessary, enter your phone number for contact.
* **Inquiry Type** (`inquiry_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of inquiry, either Public, Patient, or Staff.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Stem Cell Type** (`stem_cell_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of stem cell from the available options.
* **Stem Cell Therapist Name** (`stem_cell_therapist_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the stem cell therapist.
* **Stem Cell Therapist Phone Number** (`stem_cell_therapist_phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the stem cell therapist.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information.
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, text, required): Enter the unique ID of the patient.
* **Public Inquiry** (`public_inquiry`, select_multiple, required): Select whether the inquiry is public or not.
* **Stem Cell Institute Name** (`stem_cell_institute_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the stem cell institute.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address where you can be contacted.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter any contact details.
* **Stem Cell Inquiry Form Submitter** (`stem_cell_inquiry_form_submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Select who submitted the form, either Patient, Public, or Staff.
* **Stem Cell Inquiry Form Submitter On** (`stem_cell_inquiry_form_submitted_on`, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted the form (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Stem Cell Inquiry Form Submitter Email** (`stem_cell_inquiry_form_submitted_by_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Stem Cell Inquiry Form Submitter Phone** (`stem_cell_inquiry_form_submitted_by_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Stem Cell Inquiry Form Submitter Department** (`stem_cell_inquiry_form_submitted_by_department`, select_multiple, optional): Select the department where you work.
* **Stem Cell Inquiry Form Submitter Job Title** (`stem_cell_inquiry_form_submitted_by_job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Stem Cell Inquiry Form Submitter Id** (`stem_cell_inquiry_form_submitted_by_id`, text, optional): Enter a unique ID.
