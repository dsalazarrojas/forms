# fm_transmitter_license_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of the fm_transmitter_license_inquiry_form is to collect information from users for a transmitter license.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant fields to provide accurate information about your inquiry.
2. Enter the required fields for your inquiry.
3. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Inquiry** (1): This is the text area where you can provide a brief description of your inquiry.
* **Company Inquiry** (2): Enter the name of the company related to your inquiry.
* **Product Details** (3): Describe the product or service you are inquiring about.
* **Contact Person** (4): Enter the name of the contact person for your inquiry.
* **Email** (5): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your inquiry.
* **Phone** (6): Enter your phone number so we can contact you regarding your inquiry.
* **Supporting Documents** (7): Attach any supporting documents related to your inquiry, if applicable.
* **Technical Details** (8): Provide any additional technical details related to your inquiry.
* **License Type** (9): Select the type of license you are inquiring about, such as FM Broadcast, Internet, or TV Broadcast.
* **Inquiry Date** (10): Enter the date of your inquiry.
* **Note** (11): Add any additional notes or comments about your inquiry.
* **Category** (12): Select the category of your inquiry, such as FM, TV, or Internet.
* **Transmitter Power** (13): Select the power level of your transmitter, such as Low, Medium, or High.
* **Transmitter Frequency** (14): Select the frequency of your transmitter, such as AM, FM, or TV.
* **Antenna Height** (15): Select the height of your antenna, such as Low, Medium, or High.
* **Transmitter Type** (16): Select the type of transmitter, such as Low, Medium, or High.
* **Antenna Orientation** (17): Select the orientation of your antenna, such as Horizontal, Vertical, or Other.
* **Antenna Tilt** (18): Select the tilt of your antenna, such as Low, Medium, or High.
* **Channel** (19): Select the channel type, such as Analog or Digital.
* **Modulation** (20): Select the modulation type, such as Analog or Digital.
* **Modulation Type** (21): Select the modulation type, such as FM or DAB.
* **Polarisation** (22): Select the polarisation type, such as Vertical or Horizontal.
* **Antenna Length** (23): Select the length of your antenna, such as Long or Short.
* **Polarisation Type** (24): Select the polarisation type, such as Linear or Circular.
* **Note** (25): Add any additional notes or comments about your inquiry.
