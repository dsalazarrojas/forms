# Boutique Hotel Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers or potential customers to contact the hotel with inquiries, requests, or questions related to their stay, booking, or event planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your full name.
2. Provide your best email address for follow-up.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it for the hotel staff to contact you.
4. Select the nature of your inquiry (e.g., reservation, event booking, general question).
5. Indicate your preferred dates for your inquiry.
6. Add any additional details about your inquiry.
7. Choose whether you would like to receive promotional emails and offers from the hotel.
8. Select your preferred method of contact for follow-up (e.g., email, phone).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inquirer name** (`inquirer_name`, text, required): This is your full name, which will be used by the hotel staff to address you in their response.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address you would like the hotel to use for follow-up communication.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it for the hotel staff to contact you more easily.
* **Inquiry type** (`inquiry_type`, select_one, required): Choose the nature of your inquiry from the options provided (e.g., reservation, event booking, general question).
* **Preferred dates** (`preferred_dates`, text, optional): Enter the dates relevant to your inquiry, if known.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Add any additional details about your inquiry that will help the hotel staff understand your request.
* **Subscribe to updates** (`subscribe_updates`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you would like to receive promotional emails and offers from the hotel.
* **Follow up preference** (`follow_up_preference`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred method of contact for follow-up (e.g., email, phone).

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required, which is indicated by 'required: true' in the form schema.
