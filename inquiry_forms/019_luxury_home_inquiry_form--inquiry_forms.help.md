# Luxury Home Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users who are interested in inquiring about luxury homes. It helps real estate agents understand their preferences and requirements to better assist them in their search.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the "User Input" field.
2. Select the type of property you are interested in (Luxury Home, Townhouse, etc.) in the "Home Type" field.
3. Choose your preferred location for the property in the "Location" field.
4. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
6. Select a date for your visit in the "Date of Visit" field.
7. If you have a preferred real estate agent, select their name in the "Real Estate Agent" field.
8. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
9. If you have already submitted a similar form, select "Yes" in the "Form Submitted By" field.
10. Finally, select a date for when the form was submitted in the "Form Submitted On" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input (User Input, text, required/optional)**: Enter your name and any other relevant information that you would like to provide.
* **Home Type (Home Type, select_one, optional)**: Select the type of property you are interested in (Luxury Home, Townhouse, Condo, Apartment, or Land).
* **Location (Location, select_multiple, optional)**: Choose your preferred location for the property (Downtown, Uptown, Downtown South, or Uptown West).
* **Contact Number (Contact Number, text, optional)**: Enter your contact number so that we can reach out to you.
* **Email Address (Email Address, email, optional)**: Enter your email address so that we can send you updates.
* **Date of Visit (Date of Visit, date, optional)**: Select a date for when you would like to visit the property.
* **Real Estate Agent (Real Estate Agent, select_one, optional)**: If you have a preferred real estate agent, select their name.
* **Notes (Notes, note, optional)**: Add any additional comments or notes that you would like to include.
* **Form Submitted By (Form Submitted By, select_multiple, optional)**: If you have already submitted a similar form, select "Yes".
* **Form Submitted On (Form Submitted On, date, optional)**: Select the date that you previously submitted this form.

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when answering the questions to help us better understand your needs and preferences.
- If you have any questions or need further clarification, please do not hesitate to contact us.
- We will follow up with you to discuss your inquiry and provide more information about the properties that match your requirements.
