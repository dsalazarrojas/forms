# salon_furniture_package_inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients about their salon furniture package inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select the type of package you are inquiring about from the options provided.
2. Enter your name and contact information to allow us to get in touch with you.
3. Enter the address where the package will be installed.
4. Choose the installation date and time that suits you.
5. Enter any additional notes you have about the package.
6. Provide details about the supplier who will be delivering the package.
7. Select the installation method that will be used to install the package.
8. Choose the delivery method that will be used to deliver the package.
9. Enter the delivery date and time of the package.
10. Enter the status of the installation process.
11. Enter the ID of the package being inquired about.
12. Enter the version of the package being inquired about.
13. Select the type of package that is being inquired about (e.g. salon, home, office).
14. Enter the style of the design of the package.
15. Enter the materials used in the design of the package.
16. Enter details about the design of the package.
17. Provide a description of the package being inquired about.
18. Enter any notes from the supplier about the package.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Package Inquiries** (`1`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of package you are inquiring about from the options provided.
* **Client Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your name so we can get in touch with you.
* **Contact Number** (`3`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about the package.
* **Contact Email** (`4`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about the package.
* **Address** (`5`, `text`, required): Enter the address where the package will be installed.
* **Installation Date** (`6`, `date`, required): Choose the date of the installation.
* **Notes** (`7`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes about the package.
* **Supplier Details** (`8`, `text`, required): Enter details about the supplier who will be delivering the package.
* **Supplier Contact Number** (`9`, `text`, required): Enter the contact number of the supplier.
* **Supplier Email** (`10`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the supplier.
* **Supplier Address** (`11`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the supplier.
* **Design Team** (`12`, `text`, required): Enter details about the design team who designed the package.
* **Installation Method** (`13`, `text`, required): Choose the method of installation that will be used.
* **Delivery Method** (`14`, `text`, required): Choose the method of delivery that will be used.
* **Delivery Date** (`15`, `date`, required): Choose the date of delivery.
* **Delivery Time** (`16`, `time`, required): Choose the time of delivery.
* **Installation Status** (`17`, `select_one`, required): Choose the status of the installation process.
* **Package ID** (`18`, `text`, required): Enter the ID of the package being inquired about.
* **Package Version** (`19`, `text`, required): Enter the version of the package being inquired about.
* **Package Type** (`20`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the type of package that is being inquired about (e.g. salon, home, office).
* **Design Style** (`21`, `text`, required): Enter the style of the design of the package.
* **Design Materials** (`22`, `text`, required): Enter the materials used in the design of the package.

* **Package Description** (`24`, `text`, required): Enter a description of the package being inquired about.
* **Supplier Notes** (`25`, `text`, required): Enter any notes from the supplier about the package.
