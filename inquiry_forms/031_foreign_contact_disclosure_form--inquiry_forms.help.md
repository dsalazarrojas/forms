# Foreign Contact Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from foreign contacts for internal tracking and reference purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out each field with the requested information, including contact type, name, email, phone number, country, state, city, postal code, address, zip code, date, time, reason for contact, and any additional notes.
* Be sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure that the form is filled out correctly.
* If you have already completed this form in the past, your previous response may be automatically populated, so please review it carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Foreign Contact Info
* **Foreign Contact Info (foreign_contact_info)**: This field is for the foreign contact's name and information.
* **Contact Type (contact_type)**: Select the type of contact (e.g., business, personal, etc.).
* **Contact Name (contact_name)**: Enter the foreign contact's name.
* **Contact Email (contact_email)**: Enter the foreign contact's email address.
* **Contact Phone (contact_phone)**: Enter the foreign contact's phone number.
* **Contact Country (contact_country)**: Select the country where the foreign contact is from.
* **Contact State (contact_state)**: Select the state where the foreign contact is from. This field allows for multiple selections.
* **Contact City (contact_city)**: Enter the city where the foreign contact is from.
* **Contact Postal Code (contact_postalcode)**: Enter the foreign contact's postal code.
* **Contact Address (contact_address)**: Enter the foreign contact's address.
* **Contact Zip Code (contact_zip)**: Enter the foreign contact's zip code.
* **Contact Date (contact_date)**: Enter the date you had contact with the foreign contact.
* **Contact Time (contact_time)**: Enter the time you had contact with the foreign contact.
* **Reason for Contact (reason_for_contact)**: Explain why you had contact with the foreign contact.
* **Notes (notes)**: Add any additional notes or comments about the foreign contact.
