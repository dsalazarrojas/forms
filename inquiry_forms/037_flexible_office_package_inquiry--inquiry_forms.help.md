# Flexible Office Package Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flexible Office Package Inquiry form is designed to gather information from users to provide a flexible office package that meets their specific needs. This form is used to determine the type of office space that the user requires and will be used for internal processing and communication purposes only. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). 
2. Choose the space type that best suits your needs from the Space Type dropdown menu. 
3. Select the amenities you are interested in from the Space Amenities dropdown menu. 
4. Specify the space layout you require.
5. Choose a contact method for follow-up.
6. Provide any additional message details if required.
7. Finally, select a follow-up date and time for your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`User Input`, `text`, required: false): This field is a free-text input field where you can add any additional information that you feel is relevant to your inquiry.
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name for reference.
* **Company** (`Company`, `text`, required: false): If applicable, please enter your company name for reference.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Please enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, `text`, required: false): This is your contact number for follow-up.
* **Message** (`Message`, `text`, required: false): Please enter any additional details regarding your inquiry.
* **Space Type** (`Space Type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of space you require, such as office, serviced office, or coworking space.
* **Space Size** (`Space Size`, `number`, required: false): This field allows you to specify the size of space you require.
* **Space Location** (`Space Location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location where you require the space.
* **Space Floor Plan** (`Space Floor Plan`, `note`, required: false): This field is reserved for any additional floor plan details.
* **Space Amenities** (`Space Amenities`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the amenities you would like your space to have, such as a coffee machine, free wifi, free parking, air conditioning, or free meals.
* **Space Layout** (`Space Layout`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose a space layout, either open space or closed space.
* **Contact Method** (`Contact Method`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the method you would like to be contacted for follow-up, such as phone, email, or text.
* **Message Details** (`Message Details`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional details regarding your message.
* **Follow-up Date** (`Follow-up Date`, `date`, required: false): This field is for scheduling a follow-up date for your inquiry.
* **Follow-up Time** (`Follow-up Time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the follow-up time you prefer.
* **Follow-up Contact** (`Follow-up Contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact method for follow-up, if different from the previously selected contact method.
* **Assigned Agent** (`Assigned Agent`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is for selecting the assigned agent for your inquiry.
* **Message Details**, **Space Floor Plan**, and **Follow-up Contact** fields are for internal use and do not require input from the user. 

## Tips
- Please fill in all required fields to ensure accurate completion of your inquiry.
- If you are unsure of what to select for a particular field, please choose 'Other' from the dropdown menu.
- If you need to provide additional details, please use the space provided in the **Message** field.
- You will be contacted based on the contact method you selected.
