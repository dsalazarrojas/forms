# Home Care Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Care Inquiry Form is used to gather information from individuals seeking home care services for their loved ones. The form helps us understand their specific needs and preferences, enabling us to provide the best possible care and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of service you are looking for (Adult Care, Alzheimer's Care, Assisted Living, Home Health, or Other).
2.  Specify the type of service you require (Adult Care, Alzheimer's Care, Assisted Living, Home Health).
3.  Choose the frequency of service you need (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
4.  Enter the duration of service you require.
5.  Provide the start and end times for service if applicable.
6.  Add any additional notes about your inquiry.
7.  Enter your contact information (phone number and email).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Inquiry details:** (`home_care_inquiry_form_data`, `text`, optional) - Use this field to provide any specific details about your inquiry.
*   **Which service are you looking for?** (`home_care_service_required`, `select_one`, optional) - Select one of the provided options (Adult Care, Alzheimer's Care, Assisted Living, Home Health, or Other) to indicate the type of service you are seeking.
*   **What type of service are you looking for?** (`home_care_service_type`, `select_multiple`, optional) - Choose multiple services that best fit your needs from the provided options (Adult Care, Alzheimer's Care, Assisted Living, Home Health).
*   **How often do you need service?** (`home_care_service_frequency`, `select_one`, optional) - Select the frequency of service you require (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
*   **Service time** (`home_care_service_time`, `number`, optional) - Enter the duration of service you need.
*   **Service start time** (`home_care_service_start_time`, `time`, optional) - Enter the start time for service.
*   **Service end time** (`home_care_service_end_time`, `time`, optional) - Enter the end time for service.
*   **Additional notes** (`home_care_service_notes`, `note`, optional) - Add any additional information that may be relevant to your inquiry.
*   **Contact number** (`home_care_service_phone`, `text`, optional) - Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Contact email** (`home_care_service_email`, `email`, optional) - Enter your contact email.
*   **Additional info** (`home_care_service_additional_info`, `text`, optional) - Add any additional information about your inquiry.
