# Ask A Specialist Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Ask A Specialist Inquiry Form" is a crucial tool for users to submit requests or inquiries to our organization's specialized teams. It helps us understand your needs and route your request to the most suitable specialist.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the area of expertise you require assistance with from the "Area of Expertise Required" dropdown menu.
2. Enter a brief summary of your request in the "Subject of Inquiry" field.
3. Provide detailed information about your request in the "Detailed Question or Request" field.
4. Indicate your urgency level for a response.
5. Fill in your full name, email address, and phone number (if applicable).
6. Choose your preferred contact method for our response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Area of Expertise Required (<name>): **specialist_category**
* A field to choose from the following options: 
  + Technical Support
  + Product Consultation
  + Billing and Finance
  + Legal Compliance
  + Strategic Planning
* This field is required to ensure your request is directed to the correct team.

### Subject of Inquiry (<name>): **inquiry_subject**
* A field to enter a brief summary of your request.
* This field is required to help us understand the context of your inquiry.

### Detailed Question or Request (<name>): **detailed_description**
* A field to provide as much detail as possible about your request.
* This field is required to help our specialists prepare a thorough response.

### Urgency Level (<name>): **urgency_level**
* A dropdown menu with options: 
  + Low - General inquiry
  + Medium - Within 2-3 business days
  + High - Urgent response needed
  + Critical - Immediate assistance required
* This field helps us understand your time sensitivity and prioritize your request accordingly.

### Full Name (<name>): **full_name**
* A field to enter your full name.
* This field is required for our records and to confirm your identity.

### Email Address (<name>): **email_address**
* A field to enter your email address.
* This field is required for us to contact you regarding your inquiry.

### Phone Number (<name>): **phone_number**
* A field to enter your phone number (include country code if applicable).
* This field is not required, but it helps us contact you more efficiently if needed.

### Organization or Company Name (<name>): **organization_name**
* A field to enter your organization or company name (if applicable).

### Preferred Contact Method (<name>): **preferred_contact_method**
* A dropdown menu to choose from: 
  + Email
  + Phone Call
  + Video Conference
* This field is required to determine the best communication method for our response.

### Data Consent (<name>): **data_consent**
* A field to confirm your consent to the processing of your personal data for this inquiry.
* This field is required, please select 'True' to allow us to proceed.

Note: This guide provides a clear explanation of each field, helping users to navigate the form with confidence and accuracy. By following these steps and understanding each field's purpose, users can ensure that their inquiries are properly processed and addressed in a timely manner.
