# Home And Garden Item Search Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about home and garden items that you are looking for. Please fill in the form with your product search request, and our sales representative will be assigned to follow up with you.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the required fields with your product information and contact details.
2.  Select the relevant options for product category, sales channel, and region.
3.  Leave a note with any additional information about the product or your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Home And Garden Item Search Request** (text): Enter a brief description of the product you are looking for.
*   **Contact Email** (email): Enter your email address so that our sales representative can reach out to you.
*   **Product Name** (text): Enter the name of the product you are searching for.
*   **Product Description** (text): Provide a detailed description of the product you are interested in.
*   **Product Image URL** (text): If you have an image of the product, you can provide the URL.
*   **Product Price** (text): Enter the price you are expecting to pay for the product.
*   **Quantity Required** (number): If you need more than one product, specify the quantity.
*   **Contact Phone** (text): Enter your phone number so that our sales representative can contact you.
*   **Contact Name** (text): Enter your name for our records.
*   **Product Category** (select one): Select "Yes" if the product falls under home and garden category.
*   **Sales Channel** (select one): Select "Yes" if you are looking for sales through a specific channel.
*   **Region** (select one): Select "Yes" if the product is required for a specific region.
*   **Product Subcategory** (select multiple): Select all applicable subcategories for the product.
*   **Sales Representative Name** (text): Enter the name of the sales representative you would like to work with.
*   **Sales Representative Email** (text): Enter the email of the sales representative you would like to work with. **This field is required**.
*   **Sales Representative Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the sales representative you would like to work with.
*   **Sales Channel Manager** (select one): Select "Yes" if you are looking for sales through a specific channel manager.
*   **Sales Representative Department** (select multiple): Select all applicable departments for the sales representative.
*   **Sales Representative Company** (text): Enter the company name of the sales representative you would like to work with.
*   **Sales Representative Company Phone** (text): Enter the company phone number of the sales representative you would like to work with.
*   **Sales Representative Company Email** (text): Enter the email of the sales representative company you would like to work with.
*   **Sales Representative Note** (note): Leave a note for the sales representative with any additional information.
*   **Product Note** (note): Leave a note about the product with any additional information.
*   **Region Note** (note): Leave a note with any additional information about the region.
*   **Sales Note** (note): Leave a note about any sales-specific information.

## Tips

*   Ensure that all fields with a label marked as **required** are completed before submitting the form.
*   Double-check that your contact information is accurate.
*   Be specific with the product description and image URL to help the sales representative better understand your request.
