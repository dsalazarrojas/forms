# Clothing Line Feedback Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Clothing Line Feedback Inquiry Form" is designed to collect information on customers' experiences with the clothing line, gather feedback on product quality and satisfaction, and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Read through each section carefully and answer the questions accurately.
* For each field, provide as much detail as possible, but only answer the questions that apply to your experience.
* If you have no experience with the clothing line, please answer the questions based on your prospects or opinions.
* Make sure to submit only one form, even if you have multiple experiences with the clothing line.
* Take as much time as you need to answer all the questions thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`customer_name`, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you would like to be known.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for follow-up questions or communications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, optional): If you would like to be contacted by phone for follow-up, please enter your phone number. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Are you a customer or prospect?** (`customer_type`, required): Select one of the options that best describes your relationship with the clothing line:
	+ Current customer
	+ Past customer
	+ Prospect
	+ Other
* **Which items have you purchased or tried?** (`product_worn`, required): Select all the items that apply to your experience:
	+ T-shirts
	+ Dresses
	+ Pants
	+ Jackets
	+ Sweaters
	+ Accessories
	+ Multiple items
	+ None
* **Overall satisfaction with our clothing line** (`overall_satisfaction`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the clothing line from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Quality of fabric and construction** (`quality_rating`, required): Rate the quality of the fabric and construction of our clothing:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Below average
* **Design and style appeal** (`design_appeal`, required): Rate the design and style appeal of our clothing:
	+ Very appealing
	+ Appealing
	+ Neutral
	+ Not appealing
* **Value for money** (`price_value`, required): Rate the value for money of our clothing:
	+ Great value
	+ Good value
	+ Fair value
	+ Overpriced
* **Fit and comfort** (`fit_comfort`, required): Rate the fit and comfort of our clothing:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
* **Sizing runs true to size?** (`size_accuracy`, required): Rate the accuracy of our sizing:
	+ Yes, very accurate
	+ Mostly accurate
	+ Sometimes off
	+ Consistently small
	+ Consistently large
* **Colors match product photos?** (`color_accuracy`, optional): Rate the accuracy of our product colors:
	+ Yes, very accurate
	+ Mostly accurate
	+ Not accurate
* **Diversity of styles and designs** (`style_variety`, required): Rate the diversity of our styles and designs:
	+ Great variety
	+ Good variety
	+ Limited variety
	+ Very limited
* **What would you like us to improve?** (`what_to_improve`, optional): Please provide any specific feedback or suggestions you have for improving our clothing line.
* **What styles or items would you like to see?** (`desired_styles`, optional): If you have any specific style or item suggestions, please provide them here.
* **Likelihood to recommend our brand** (`likelihood_recommend`, required): Rate your likelihood to recommend our brand:
	+ 1 (unlikely)
	+ 2
	+ 3
	+ 4
	+ 5 (very likely)
* **Additional comments or feedback** (`additional_comments`, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them here.
