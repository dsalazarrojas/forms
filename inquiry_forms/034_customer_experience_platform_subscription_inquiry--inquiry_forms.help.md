# Customer Experience Platform Subscription Inquiry - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers who are interested in learning more about our Customer Experience Platform subscription.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your name and a brief description of your inquiry in the "Customer Experience Platform Subscription Inquiry" field.
2. Enter your customer information, including your name, email address, phone number, company name, and website (if applicable).
3. Select the type of customer you are (e.g., Enterprise, Small Business, or Large Business).
4. If you are not an Enterprise, Small Business, or Large Business, please describe your type of customer in the "Other (please describe)" field.
5. Let us know if you would like to be followed up with additional information (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
6. If you would like to be followed up, please provide more details in the "Follow-up Details" field.
7. If you have any additional comments or notes, please enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.
8. Finally, if you have any specific rollout details, please enter them in the "Rollout Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false)
Enter your name so we can address you personally.

### 2. **Description** (`form_description`, text, required: false)
A brief description of your inquiry will help us understand what you are looking for.

### 3. **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false)
Enter your email address so we can contact you with more information.

### 4. **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false)
Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

### 5. **Customer Company** (`customer_company`, text, required: false)
Enter your company name (if you have one).

### 6. **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, required: false)
If you have a website, enter the URL here.

### 7. **Sales Person** (`sales_person`, text, required: false)
Enter the name of the sales person who can follow up with you.

### 8. **Sales Department** (`sales_department`, text, required: false)
Enter the name of the sales department that can follow up with you.

### 9. **Customer Type** (`customer_type`, select_one, required: false)
Select the type of customer you are (e.g., Enterprise, Small Business, or Large Business).

### 10. **Other (please describe)** (`customer_type_other`, text, required: false)
If you are not an Enterprise, Small Business, or Large Business, describe your type of customer.

### 11. **Follow-up Plan** (`follow_up_plan`, select_one, required: false)
Let us know if you would like to be followed up with additional information (e.g., "Yes" or "No").

### 12. **Follow-up Details** (`follow_up_details`, text, required: false)
If you would like to be followed up, provide more details.

### 13. **Rollout Details** (`rollout_details`, text, required: false)
If you have any specific rollout details, enter them here.

### 14. **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false)
Enter any additional comments or notes you have.

### 15. **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, text, required: false)
Enter any customer notes or information.
