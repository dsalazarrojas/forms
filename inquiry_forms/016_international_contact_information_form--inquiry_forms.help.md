# International Contact Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The International Contact Information Form is a tool used to collect contact information from international contacts, including their name, contact details, global time zone, chat information, and any additional note. This form helps to streamline data collection and facilitate communication across different time zones and countries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the first field.
2. Provide your contact details in the second field.
3. Specify your global time zone in the third field.
4. Enter any relevant chat information in the fourth field.
5. If you have any additional comments or information, type them in the last field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (contact_info, text, required: false): Enter your name and other relevant contact information in this field.
* **Contact Details** (contact_details, text, required: false): Provide any additional contact details, such as email address, phone number, or website, if applicable.
* **Global Time Zone** (global_time_zone, text, required: false): Enter your current global time zone (e.g., UTC, EST, CST, etc.).
* **Chat Information** (chat_info, text, required: false): Enter any chat information, such as your preferred chat platform or your availability for chat.
* **Note** (note, text, required: false): If you have any additional comments or information, enter them here.
