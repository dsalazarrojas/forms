# Personal Data Accuracy Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to inquire about the accuracy of your personal data stored in our system. Fill out this form to let us know about any discrepancies or inaccuracies in your data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name.
2. Enter your last name.
3. Provide your email address.
4. Indicate the type of email you have (e.g., personal, work, other).
5. Specify the method you would like us to use to contact you to discuss your inquiry (e.g., phone, email, other).
6. Provide any additional information or notes about your inquiry (optional).
7. Choose the team member you would like to be assigned to follow up on your inquiry (optional).
8. Indicate the date you would like to follow up on your inquiry (optional).
9. Specify the method you would like us to use to follow up with you (e.g., phone, email, other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Data Accuracy Inquiry Form** (`personal_data_accuracy_inquiry_form`): This is the title of the form. Please do not fill this in.
* **First Name** (`first_name`): Enter your first name as it appears in our system.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`): Enter your last name as it appears in our system.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your email address as it appears in our system.
* **Email Type** (`email_type`): Indicate the type of email you have (e.g., personal, work, other). This helps us understand how to contact you.
* **Correct Method** (`correct_method`): Specify the method you would like us to use to contact you (e.g., phone, email, other). This could be to discuss your inquiry or to provide updates.
* **Correct Method Note** (`correct_method_note`): Provide any additional information or notes about your inquiry that will help us contact you correctly.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`): If you have a preferred date to follow up on your inquiry, please provide it. This helps us plan our follow-up communication.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`): Choose the team member you would like to be assigned to follow up on your inquiry (optional). If you don't choose, we'll assign someone from our Support Team.
* **Follow-up Method** (`follow_up_method`): Specify the method you would like us to use to follow up with you (e.g., phone, email, other). This ensures we contact you using your preferred method.
* **Follow-up Method Note** (`follow_up_method_note`): Provide any additional information or notes about your follow-up method that will help us understand your preference.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* We'll review and follow up on your inquiry as soon as possible.
