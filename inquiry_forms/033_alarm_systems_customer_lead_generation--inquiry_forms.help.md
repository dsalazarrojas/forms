# Alarm Systems Customer Lead Generation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from potential customers who are interested in alarm systems for their properties. This includes personal contact information, property details, and security requirements. The form helps us understand their needs and provide tailored solutions to their security concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal contact information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Specify your preferred time to contact you.
3. Enter your property type and address.
4. Indicate your security interests and any existing security systems you may have.
5. Choose a timeframe for installing the system.
6. Add any specific concerns or requirements.
7. Let us know how you heard about us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Personal Information
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.

### Property Information
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property you own or are interested in (e.g., Single Family Home, Apartment/Condo).
* **Property Address**: Enter the full address of your property.
* **City**: Enter the city where your property is located.

### Security Interests
* **Security Features of Interest**: Select all the security features you are interested in (e.g., Burglar Alarm System, Security Cameras (CCTV)).
* **Do you have an existing security system?**: If you have an existing system, indicate whether it is active or inactive.
* **When would you like to have a system installed?**: Choose a timeframe for installation.

### Additional Information
* **Any specific concerns or requirements?**: Enter any special requirements or concerns you have.
* **How did you hear about us?**: Let us know how you found our company.

### Consent and Agreement
* **I consent to receiving a quote and security information from your team**: Check this box to indicate your consent to receive further information.

Note: Please answer all required fields to ensure we can better understand your needs and provide a suitable solution.
