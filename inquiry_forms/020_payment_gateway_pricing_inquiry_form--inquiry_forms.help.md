# Payment Gateway Pricing Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payment Gateway Pricing Inquiry Form is a simple inquiry form designed to allow customers or partners to submit their information and get personalized pricing information for our Payment Gateway Service. This form is used to gather contact details, business information, and a message or question about payment gateway pricing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the appropriate option from the dropdown list to indicate which type of pricing you are interested in.
2.  Fill in the business name of your company or organization in the `business_name` field.
3.  Enter your contact number in the `contact_number` field.
4.  Provide your email address in the `email` field.
5.  Type in any message or question you have about the payment gateway pricing in the `message` field. This is the only field that supports multi-line text.
6.  Review your information before submitting the form. Ensure you've selected the correct option and filled in all necessary fields.
7.  Click the "submit" button to send your inquiry to our team for review and response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **payment_gateway_pricing** (`payment_gateway_pricing`, select_one, optional): Choose a pricing plan to get more information about our Payment Gateway pricing.
* **business_name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **contact_number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **message** (`message`, note, optional): Type a brief description or question about payment gateway pricing.
* **submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click to send your inquiry to our team for review and response.
