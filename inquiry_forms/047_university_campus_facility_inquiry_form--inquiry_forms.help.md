# University Campus Facility Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about facilities on a university campus. It's used to request help or support from campus services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the facility you're inquiring about in the "Facility name" field.
2. Select the location of the facility on the "Campus location" field.
3. Provide a brief description of the issue or inquiry in the "Description" field.
4. Specify which facilities you're inquiring about by selecting from the "Facility type" options.
5. Enter your name and contact information to enable us to contact you for further details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Facility name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the facility you're inquiring about.
- **Campus location** (Select One, Optional): Select the location of the facility on campus.
	+ Options:
		- Yes
		- No
- **Description** (Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of the issue or inquiry.
- **Facility type** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select which facilities you're inquiring about.
	+ Options:
		- Yes
		- No
- **Contact name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name so we can contact you about your inquiry.
- **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your inquiry.
- **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your inquiry.
- **Facility ID** (Number, Optional): Enter the facility ID (if available).
- **Tips**: Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to help us assist you promptly.
