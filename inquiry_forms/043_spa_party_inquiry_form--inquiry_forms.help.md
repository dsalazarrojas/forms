# spa_party_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The spa_party_inquiry_form is designed to collect information from customers for a spa party inquiry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the customer details section with your name, email, and phone number.
2. Choose your preferred services by selecting one or multiple options from the services dropdown list.
3. If you have any additional services you would like to include in your party, type them in the services_additional field.
4. Let us know if you would like to reserve a slot by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the reservation field.
5. Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that we can process your inquiry efficiently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Details
* **Customer Name (customer_name)**: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Email (customer_email)**: Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Customer Phone (customer_phone)**: Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
### Services
* **Services (spa_services)**: Choose one or multiple services you would like to include in your party. Options include:
	+ Massage
	+ Facial
	+ Manicure
* **Services Additional (services_additional)**: If you have any other services in mind, you can type them in here.
* **Services Details (services_details)**: If you would like to provide more information about the services you've chosen, please type it in this field.
### Reservation
* **Reservation (reservation)**: Let us know if you would like to reserve a slot. Please select "Yes" to reserve or "No" to indicate you're just inquiring.
* **Customer Notes (customer_notes)**: If you have any additional comments or notes for us, please type them in this field.
