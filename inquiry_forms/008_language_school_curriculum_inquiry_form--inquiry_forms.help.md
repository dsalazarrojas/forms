# Language School Curriculum Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Language School Curriculum Inquiry Form" is a tool used to gather information from potential students about their language learning preferences. This form helps us understand what you would like to learn, your preferred schedule, and other important details to better tailor our programs to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select a language you would like to learn from the options provided in the "What language would you like to learn?" field.
2. Choose the level of language you want to learn from the options provided in the "What level of language do you want to learn?" field.
3. Select your preferred schedule for learning from the options in the "Are you available on weekdays or weekends?" field.
4. Provide your desired start date for learning in the "What date would you like to start learning?" field in MM/DD format.
5. Decide on the duration of your language learning course in the "How long would you like to learn?" field.
6. Choose how frequently you would like to learn from the options in the "Would you like to learn on a regular schedule?" field.
7. Select your preferred method of contact from the options in the "How would you like to contact us?" field.
8. (Optional) Provide any additional information you feel is necessary in the "Do you have any additional information?" field.
9. Finally, agree to our terms by selecting "Option 1" in the "Do you agree with our terms?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What would you like to learn? (1)** (`text`, required): This field allows you to briefly describe what you are interested in learning.
* **What language would you like to learn? (2)** (`select_one`, required): Choose a language from the provided options that you are interested in learning.
* **What level of language do you want to learn? (3)** (`select_one`, required): Select the level of language you are looking to achieve, from beginner to advanced.
* **Are you available on weekdays or weekends? (4)** (`select_one`, required): Choose your preferred schedule for learning, whether weekdays or weekends.
* **What date would you like to start learning? (5)** (`date`, required): Enter a date you are available to start learning, in MM/DD format.
* **How long would you like to learn? (6)** (`number`, required): Decide on the duration of your course, in weeks or months.
* **Would you like to learn on a regular schedule? (7)** (`select_one`, required): Choose how frequently you want to learn, whether on a regular or irregular schedule.
* **How would you like to contact us? (8)** (`select_one`, required): Select a method of contact that suits you best, whether email, phone, or other.
* **Do you have any additional information? (9)** (`text`, optional): This field is for any extra details or comments you might have about your inquiry.
* **Do you agree with our terms? (10)** (`select_one`, required): Accept our terms and conditions before proceeding with your inquiry.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
- We appreciate your interest in our language school and look forward to assisting you in finding the right program for your needs.
