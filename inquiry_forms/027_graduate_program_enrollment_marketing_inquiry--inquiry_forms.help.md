# graduate_program_enrollment_marketing_inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for collecting information from prospective students about their interest in a graduate program. The form will help us understand your interest and preferences to better guide you through the enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read each question and answer them accurately and to the best of your ability.
- Ensure all required fields are filled out.
- You can submit the form multiple times if you have more than one interest in the graduate program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Graduate Program Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the name of the graduate program you are interested in.
- **Choose Graduate Program** (`program`, `select_one`, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you are interested in enrolling in the graduate program.
- **Start Term** (`start_term`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the terms you would be interested in enrolling in. For example, Spring 2024, Summer 2024, and so on.
- **Full Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your full name.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
- **What is your interest in this graduate program?** (`program_interest`, `note`, required: false): Describe your interest in the graduate program, if you have more to say.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or questions you might have.
- **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the person you would like to be assigned to.
- **How would you like to be followed up?** (`follow_up`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the ways you would like to be contacted, such as email, phone, chat, or text.
