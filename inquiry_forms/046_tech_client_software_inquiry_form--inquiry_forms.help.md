# Tech Client Software Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tech Client Software Inquiry Form is a tool used to gather information about your software development project. This form helps us understand your project requirements and preferences to provide you with the best possible solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name, last name, and email address in the "First name", "Last name", and "Email" fields, respectively.
2. Enter your company name in the "Company" field.
3. Select your role in the "Role" field.
4. Choose the type of project you are inquiring about in the "Inquiry type" field.
5. Select the type of software you are inquiring about in the "Software type" field.
6. If required, provide a detailed description of your project in the "Project description" field.
7. Choose how you would like to be followed up on in the "Follow up required" field.
8. Enter the date and time you are available for follow up in the "Follow up date" and "Follow up time" fields, respectively.
9. Select the time zone you are in.
10. Enter the email address and phone number you can be reached at in the "Follow up email" and "Follow up phone" fields, respectively.
11. Provide a brief note about the follow up location in the "Follow up location" field.
12. Enter any additional notes about the follow up in the "Follow up note" field.
13. Select who you would like to be assigned to in the "Assigned to" field.
14. Enter the tool(s) you would like to use for the project in the "Assigned tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your given name.
* **Last name**: Enter your family name.
* **Email**: Enter your company's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Company**: Enter your company name.
* **Role**: Select your role or position in the company (e.g. CTO, Project Manager, etc.).
* **Inquiry type**: Choose the type of project you are inquiring about (e.g. New client software project, Existing client software project, etc.).
* **Software inquires**: Select the type(s) of software you are inquiring about (e.g. Project development, Consulting, Maintenance, etc.).
* **Project description**: Provide a detailed description of your project.
* **Software type**: Select the type of software you are inquiring about (e.g. Web development, Mobile development, etc.).
* **Follow up required**: Choose how you would like to be followed up on (e.g. Yes, No).
* **Next steps**: Provide a brief description of the next steps to take after the inquiry.
* **Follow up date**: Enter the date you are available for follow up (e.g. MM/DD/YY).
* **Follow up time**: Enter the time you are available for follow up (e.g. 12:00 PM 12:00 PM).
* **Time zone**: Select the time zone you are in.
* **Follow up email**: Enter your email address you can be reached at.
* **Follow up phone**: Enter your phone number you can be reached at.
* **Follow up location**: Provide a brief note about the follow up location.
* **Follow up note**: Enter any additional notes about the follow up.
* **Assigned to**: Select who you would like to be assigned to (e.g. Jim, John, etc.).
* **Assigned tool**: Enter the tool(s) you would like to use for the project.

## Tips

* Please ensure your email and phone number are correct and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask us, and we will be happy to help.
* Please provide as much detail as possible in the "Project description" field to help us understand your needs.
* You can select multiple options in the "Software inquires" field by holding down the Ctrl key while selecting.
