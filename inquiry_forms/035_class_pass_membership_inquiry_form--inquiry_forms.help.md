# Class Pass Membership Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Class Pass Membership Inquiry Form is designed to gather information from potential members about their personal, location, fitness, and membership preferences. This data will help us understand their interests, needs, and budget to provide them with the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, users need to fill out their personal details, select their location and fitness preferences, choose their membership type, budget range, and preferred method of contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Information
• **First Name** (first_name, text, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.
• **Last Name** (last_name, text, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents.
• **Email Address** (email, email, required): Enter your valid email address where we can reach you.
• **Phone Number** (phone, text, required): Enter your phone number where we can contact you.

### Location Information
• **City** (city, text, required): Enter the city where you live or plan to live.
• **State or Province** (state, text, required): Enter the state or province where you live or plan to live.
• **Zip or Postal Code** (zip_code, text, required): Enter your zip or postal code.

### Fitness and Class Information
• **Primary Fitness Goals** (fitness_goals, select_multiple, required): Choose all the primary fitness goals you are looking to achieve, such as weight loss, building muscle, stress relief, or improving cardiovascular endurance.
• **Classes of Interest** (class_interests, select_multiple, required): Choose all the classes that interest you, such as yoga, Pilates, spinning, or strength training.
• **Current Fitness Level** (fitness_level, select_one, required): Indicate your current fitness level, whether you are a beginner, intermediate, or advanced.
• **Experience with Group Fitness Classes** (experience_level, select_one, optional): Choose your experience level with group fitness classes, whether you are brand new, have some experience, or are very experienced.
• **Age Group** (age, select_one, required): Choose your age group, from 18-25, 26-35, 36-45, 46-55, 56-65, or 65+.

### Membership Information
• **Membership Type** (membership_type, select_one, required): Choose the type of membership that interests you, such as month-to-month, quarterly, or annual.
• **Expected Classes Per Week** (frequency, select_one, required): Choose how often you plan to attend classes, 1-2 times per week, 3-4 times per week, or 5+ times per week.
• **Budget Range for Monthly Membership** (budget_range, select_one, optional): Choose your budget range for the monthly membership, under $50, $50-$100, $100-$150, $150-$200, or $200+.

### Budget and Pricing
• **Preferred Method of Contact** (follow_up, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted, via email, phone, text message, or no preference.
• **Additional Information or Questions** (additional_info, text, optional): Enter any additional information or questions you may have.
