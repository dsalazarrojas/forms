# Dining Table Product Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Dining Table Product Inquiry Form" is a tool for sales teams and customers to gather information about a specific dining table product. This form helps to gather details such as product specifications, customer information, and shipping address, allowing the sales team to provide accurate quotes and deliver products on time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of dining table product you are inquiring about. 
2. Provide your personal contact information, including First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone number.
3. Choose the product details that match your needs, such as Table Size, Material, Color, and Brand.
4. If you have any custom requests for your product, please provide the details in the "Custom Requests" field.
5. Enter your delivery lead time and shipping address.
6. Specify the sales channel that you prefer to receive your product from (Retailer, Showroom, or Custom Maker).
7. Add any additional follow-up information or requests in the "Follow Up Info" field.
8. Finally, include any additional follow-up requests in the "Follow Up Requests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dining Table Product** (`dining_table_product`, text, optional): 
	+ Enter the specific dining table product you are inquiring about.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, select_multiple, optional): 
	+ First Name: Enter your first name.
	+ Last Name: Enter your last name.
	+ Email: Enter your email address.
	+ Phone: Enter your phone number.
* **Product Details** (`product_details`, select_one, optional): 
	+ Table Size: Choose the size of the table you need.
	+ Material: Select the material of the table (e.g., wood, metal, etc.).
	+ Color: Select the color of the table.
	+ Brand: Choose the brand of the table.
* **Custom Requests** (`custom_requests`, text, optional): 
	+ Provide any custom requests or specifications you have for your dining table product.
* **Delivery Lead Time** (`delivery_lead_time`, date, optional): 
	+ Enter the delivery lead time for your product.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, number, optional): 
	+ Enter your shipping address (note: this field may not be applicable if you are inquiring about a product that is not delivered).
* **Sales Channel** (`sales_channel`, select_multiple, optional): 
	+ Retailer: Choose this if you want to purchase from an authorized retailer.
	+ Showroom: Choose this if you want to see the product in a showroom before purchasing.
	+ Custom Maker: Choose this if you want to deal directly with the manufacturer.
* **Follow Up Info** (`follow_up_info`, note, optional): 
	+ Add any additional follow-up information or requests.
* **Follow Up Requests** (`follow_up_requests`, text, optional): 
	+ Include any additional follow-up requests or questions you have.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when providing your product and customer information to ensure accurate quotes and delivery.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask your sales representative for clarification.
* This form is for general inquiries only, if you are ready to purchase, please contact your sales representative directly.
* Please note that some fields may not be applicable depending on the product you are inquiring about.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
