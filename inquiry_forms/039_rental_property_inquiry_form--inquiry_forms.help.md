# Rental Property Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Property Inquiry Form is a tool used by rental property agents to collect information about potential rental properties. The form serves as a means to gather relevant details such as the name, email, phone number, location, amenities, property type, price, and lease terms to facilitate the rental process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the designated fields.
2. Select your preferred location from the given options.
3. Choose the amenities you are interested in for your potential rental property.
4. Select the type of property you are looking for (residential or prophetic).
5. Enter the price range for the property you are interested in.
6. Choose a lease start date and end date.
7. Write a brief description of the property you are inquiring about.
8. Upload an image of the property (optional).
9. Provide any additional information about the rental agent and terms and conditions of the rental.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your full name to allow us to contact you regarding the rental property inquiry.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications about the rental property.
* **Phone**: If you have a preferred phone number, enter it for further communication.
* **Preferred Location**: Select one or more locations where you are looking for a rental property (Downtown, Uptown, Anywhere).
* **Amenities**: Choose the amenities you are interested in for the rental property (pool, gym, parking).
* **Terms and Conditions**: Please read and agree to the terms and conditions of the rental property.
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property you are looking for (residential, prophetic).
* **Property Price**: Enter the price range for the property you are interested in.
* **Lease Start Date**: Choose the start date of the lease.
* **Lease End Date**: Choose the end date of the lease.
* **Property Description**: Write a brief description of the property you are inquiring about.
* **Property Image**: If applicable, upload an image of the property.
* **Rental Agent Details**: If applicable, provide any additional information about the rental agent.
* **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or notes about the rental property, please include them here.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification about any of the fields, feel free to ask.
