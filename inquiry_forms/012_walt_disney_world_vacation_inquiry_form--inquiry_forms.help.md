# Walt Disney World Vacation Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from guests about their Walt Disney World vacation plans. The data collected will help us understand their preferences and provide a more personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant information that applies to you.
2. Answer all required fields to ensure you receive the necessary responses from us.
3. Submit the form to share your preferences with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Walt Disney World Vacation Information (Text)**: Enter a brief description of your Walt Disney World vacation plans. This field is optional, so feel free to add as much or as little information as you like.
* **Guest Information (Number)**: Enter the number of guests in your group. This helps us plan for the right number of tickets and accommodations.
* **Travel Plans (Select One)**: Select your travel dates to let us know when you plan to visit Walt Disney World. Please choose one of the following options:
	+ 1-7: You plan to visit within the next week.
	+ 7-14: You plan to visit within the next two weeks.
	+ 14+: You have not set a specific travel date.
* **Special Dietary Needs (Select One)**: If you or any of your guests have special dietary needs, select 'True' to let us know.
* **Accommodation Preferences (Select One)**: If you have a preferred type of accommodation, select from the following:
	+ Hotel
	+ Resort
	+ Vacation Home
* **Arrival Date (Date)**: Enter your planned arrival date in the format of mm/dd/yyyy. This helps us plan for the right accommodations and amenities.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter the email address of one of your guests. We'll use this to contact you with updates and information about your trip.
* **Phone Number (Text)**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you with any last-minute updates or changes. 

## Tips
* Please answer all required fields to ensure we receive the necessary information.
* Be as specific as possible when describing your travel plans.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
