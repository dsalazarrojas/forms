# COVID 19 Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals or organizations seeking support or guidance related to COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Choose the correct options from the dropdown menus where applicable.
3. If you need to attach any supporting documents, please indicate that you need to do so.
4. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Full Name (<inquirer_name>)
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.

### 2. Email Address (<inquirer_email>)
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.

### 3. Phone Number (<inquirer_phone>)
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.

### 4. Organization or Company (<organization>)
* **Organization or Company**: If applicable, enter the name of your organization or company.

### 5. Inquiry Date (<inquiry_date>)
* **Inquiry Date**: Enter the date of your inquiry.

### 6. Type of Inquiry (<inquiry_type>)
* **Type of Inquiry**: Select the category that best describes your inquiry:
	+ Health concern
	+ Policy question
	+ Resource request
	+ Testing question
	+ Vaccination question
	+ Economic impact
	+ General information
	+ Other

### 7. Subject of Inquiry (<subject>)
* **Subject of Inquiry**: Enter a brief description of your inquiry.

### 8. Detailed Inquiry or Question (<detailed_inquiry>)
* **Detailed Inquiry or Question**: Provide a detailed explanation of your inquiry.

### 9. Geographic Area or Jurisdiction (<affected_area>)
* **Geographic Area or Jurisdiction**: Enter the location relevant to your inquiry.

### 10. Priority Level (<priority_level>)
* **Priority Level**: Select the urgency of your inquiry:
	+ Urgent
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low

### 11. Health Status (<health_status>)
* **Health Status**: Select the current health status relevant to your inquiry:
	+ Currently symptomatic
	+ Recently diagnosed
	+ Exposed and monitoring
	+ Vaccinated
	+ General information seeker
	+ Other

### 12. Have You Contacted Us Before (<previous_contact>)
* **Have You Contacted Us Before**: Select if you have contacted us before:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'

### 13. Preferred Contact Method (<preferred_contact_method>)
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select the best method for us to contact you:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ Text message
	+ Mail

### 14. Best Time to Contact You (<best_time_contact>)
* **Best Time to Contact You**: Enter the best time for us to contact you (optional).

### 15. Additional Information (<additional_information>)
* **Additional Information**: Provide any additional information that may be helpful for your inquiry.

### 16. Need to Attach Supporting Documents (<attachment_needed>)
* **Need to Attach Supporting Documents**: Indicate if you need to attach any supporting documents.

### 17. Language Preference (<language_preference>)
* **Language Preference**: Select the language you prefer for communication:
	+ English
	+ Spanish
	+ French
	+ Chinese
	+ Arabic
	+ Other

### 18. Accessibility Needs (<accessibility_needs>)
* **Accessibility Needs**: Provide any special accessibility needs you have (optional).

### 19. I consent to follow-up communication (<consent_followup>)
* **I consent to follow-up communication**: Select if you consent to further communication:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'

### 20. I confirm the above information is accurate (<inquiry_confirmation>)
* **I confirm the above information is accurate**: Select if you confirm that the information above is accurate:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
