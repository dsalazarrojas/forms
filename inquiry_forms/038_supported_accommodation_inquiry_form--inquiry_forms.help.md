# Supported Accommodation Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supported Accommodation Inquiry Form is designed to gather information from clients who are seeking supported accommodation. It helps in providing the necessary details for efficient allocation and management of available resources.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Client Name** field with the client's full name.
2. Enter the client's **Client Email**.
3. Select the type of **Supported Accommodation Type** that suits the client's needs (e.g., Homeless, Emergency, Transitional, etc.).
4. Describe the client's **Accommodation Needs** in detail.
5. Provide the **Accommodation Location** where the client is looking for accommodation.
6. Choose the necessary **Accommodation Support Services** (e.g., Transportation, Food, Medical).
7. If additional support services are required, use the **Accommodation Support Services Other** field to describe them.
8. (Optional) Schedule a **Follow-up Date** and **Follow-up Time** for further contact.
9. (Optional) Provide any additional **Follow-up Contact** information.
10. Enter any additional comments or notes in the **Supported Accommodation Inquiry Form Comments** field.
11. Click the **Submit** button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (Required): Enter the client's full name.
* **Client Email** (Required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Supported Accommodation Type** (Required): Choose the type of supported accommodation the client needs.
*   - **Homeless**: Clients who are homeless or have no permanent address.
    - **Emergency**: Clients in emergency situations or need immediate assistance.
    - **Transitional**: Clients who are temporarily in need of accommodation.
    - **Short-term**: Clients who need accommodation for a short period.
    - **Long-term**: Clients who require long-term accommodation.
* **Accommodation Needs** (Required): Describe the accommodation requirements in detail.
* **Accommodation Location** (Required): Provide the location where the client is looking for accommodation.
* **Accommodation Support Services** (Required): Select the support services the client needs (e.g., Transportation, Food, Medical).
*   - **Transportation**: Clients require transportation services.
    - **Food**: Clients require food assistance.
    - **Medical**: Clients require medical services.
    - **Other**: Clients require additional support services not listed above.
* **Accommodation Support Services Other** (Optional): Describe any additional support services not listed above.
* **Follow-up Date** (Optional): Schedule a follow-up date for further contact.
* **Follow-up Time** (Optional): Schedule a follow-up time for further contact.
* **Follow-up Contact** (Optional): Provide any additional contact information.
* **Submit** (Optional): Click this button to submit the form.
* **Supported Accommodation Inquiry Form Comments** (Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the form.

## Tips

- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Use the **Supported Accommodation Type** options to ensure accurate allocation of resources.
- Be specific when describing **Accommodation Needs** and **Accommodation Support Services**.
- Use the **Accommodation Support Services Other** field only if you need to specify additional services not listed.
- The **Follow-up Date** and **Follow-up Time** fields are for scheduling follow-up contact; use them only if necessary.
- The **Follow-up Contact** field is for additional contact information; use it only if needed.
- The **Supported Accommodation Inquiry Form Comments** field is for any additional comments or notes; use it only if necessary.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
