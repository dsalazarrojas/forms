# Financial Services Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Services Intake Form is a tool used to gather information from clients regarding their financial services needs and requests. This form is meant to be completed by a client or a representative of an institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Client Name" field.
2. If you have any contact information, enter it in the "Contact Info" field.
3. In the "Service Request" field, describe the financial services you are seeking.
4. If applicable, select the financial institutions that require services in the "Financial Institutions" field.
5. In the "Services Requested" field, select the services you are requesting.
6. In the "Service Details" field, provide any additional information about your service request.
7. In the "Next Steps" field, describe the next steps you would like to take to address your service request.
8. If you wish to schedule a follow-up, enter the date in the "Follow-up" field.
9. If you wish to schedule a specific time for the follow-up, enter the time in the "Follow-up Time" field.
10. In the "Client Notes" field, provide any additional information regarding your service request.
11. In the "Financial Institutions Additional Info" field, provide any additional information regarding the financial institutions you selected.
12. If you wish to select additional financial institutions, select "Yes" in the "Financial Institutions" field.
13. In the "Financial Institutions Services Additional Info" field, provide any additional information regarding the services you selected.
14. Select "Yes" in the "Assigned Financial Specialist" field if you would like to be assigned a financial specialist.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Info** (text, optional): If you have any contact information, such as phone number or email, enter it here.
* **Service Request** (text, optional): Describe the financial services you are seeking.
* **Financial Institutions** (select multiple, optional): Select the financial institutions that require services.
* **Services Requested** (select multiple, optional): Select the services you are requesting.
* **Service Details** (note, optional): Provide any additional information about your service request.
* **Next Steps** (text, required): Describe the next steps you would like to take to address your service request.
* **Follow-up** (date, optional): If you wish to schedule a follow-up, enter the date.
* **Follow-up Time** (time, optional): If you wish to schedule a specific time for the follow-up, enter the time.
* **Client Notes** (note, optional): Provide any additional information regarding your service request.
* **Financial Institutions Additional Info** (text, optional): Provide any additional information regarding the financial institutions you selected.
* **Financial Institutions** (select multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if you wish to select additional financial institutions.
* **Financial Institutions Services Additional Info** (text, optional): Provide any additional information regarding the services you selected.
* **Assigned Financial Specialist** (select one, optional): Select "Yes" if you would like to be assigned a financial specialist.
