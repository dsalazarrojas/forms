# Equine Photography Session Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients or customers interested in booking an equine photography session. It will guide you through the necessary steps to provide your details, preferred date, package preference, and other relevant information to help us prepare for your photography session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details as a photographer, including your name and contact information.
2. Select your preferred date for the photography session.
3. Choose a package that suits your needs (Equine Photography Session Inquiry Form, Session Package A, or Session Package B).
4. Provide any additional session details.
5. Enter information about your trainer (if applicable).
6. Enter information about the barn (if applicable).
7. Specify your contact preferences.
8. Add any additional notes or comments about your session.
9. Enter your email address so we can get back to you with further information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photographer Details** (`photographer_details`, text, required/optional): Please fill in your name, contact information, and any other relevant details as a photographer.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required/optional): Select a date for your photography session.
* **Package Preference** (`package_preference`, select_one, required/optional): Choose from three options: Equine Photography Session Inquiry Form, Session Package A, or Session Package B.
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional information about your photography session.
* **Trainer Info** (`trainer_info`, text, required/optional): If applicable, enter information about your trainer.
* **Barn Info** (`barn_info`, text, required/optional): If applicable, enter information about the barn.
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, text, required/optional): Specify how you would like to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, etc.).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your session.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can get back to you with further information.
