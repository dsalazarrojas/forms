# Family Gaming Subscription Pricing Inquiry - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential customers who are interested in a family gaming subscription service. The goal is to understand family demographics, budget, and device preferences to tailor the service and provide a better gaming experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Determine how many family members will be using the service (Page 1: *family_members_count*).
2.  Select the age range of the family members (Page 2: *family_age_range*).
3.  Choose the family's annual income range (Page 3: *family_income_range*).
4.  Select the gaming devices the family members use (Page 4: *device_type*).
5.  Set a budget per family member per month (Page 5: *budget_per_member*).
6.  Set a budget for the service per month (Page 6: *budget_per_service*).
7.  Enter a valid email address (Page 7: *email*).
8.  Add any additional comments or questions (Page 8: *note*).
9.  Optionally, provide a phone number for follow-up (Page 9: *phone*).
10.  Select the "Submit" option to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Members Count** (`<name>`: *family_members_count*, `<type>`: *number*, `required/optional`: *false*) 
    Enter the number of family members who will be using the service.
* **Age of Family Members** (`<name>`: *family_age_range*, `<type>`: *select_multiple*, `required/optional`: *false*) 
    Select the age range of the family members.
* **Family Income Range** (`<name>`: *family_income_range*, `<type>`: *select_one*, `required/optional`: *true*) 
    Choose the family's annual income range to better understand their financial situation.
* **Device Type** (`<name>`: *device_type*, `<type>`: *select_multiple*, `required/optional`: *false*) 
    Select the types of gaming devices the family members use (Console, PC, Smartphone, Tablet, or Other).
* **Budget Per Member** (`<name>`: *budget_per_member*, `<type>`: *select_one*, `required/optional`: *true*) 
    Set the budget per family member per month to understand their financial constraints.
* **Budget for Service** (`<name>`: *budget_per_service*, `<type>`: *select_one*, `required/optional`: *true*) 
    Set the budget for the service per month to understand their financial constraints.
* **Email** (`<name>`: *email*, `<type>`: *email*, `required/optional`: *true*) 
    Enter a valid email address to allow for follow-up communication.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`<name>`: *note*, `<type>`: *note*, `required/optional`: *false*) 
    Add any additional comments or questions you have about the service.
* **Phone** (`<name>`: *phone*, `<type>`: *text*, `required/optional`: *false*) 
    Optionally, enter a phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Submit** (`<name>`: *family_gaming_pricing_inquiry_submit*, `<type>`: *select_one*, `required/optional`: *true*) 
    Select the "Submit" option to complete the form.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have not already submitted an inquiry for the same service to avoid duplicate submissions.
* Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form to ensure that the service is tailored to your needs.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, use the "Additional Comments or Questions" field to provide more information.
