This guide will help you understand the purpose and fields of the Waterway Infrastructure Inquiry Form. Before you start, make sure you are reporting an issue or concern related to a waterway infrastructure, and that you haven't already reported it through other channels. 

# Waterway Infrastructure Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Waterway Infrastructure Inquiry Form is designed to enable users to report issues, conduct inspections, or request improvements related to waterway infrastructure. This form serves as a means to communicate your concerns or needs about waterway infrastructure, such as water pipes, drainage systems, or other water-related facilities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form as completely and accurately as possible**. Each field is essential in providing the required information to process your inquiry.
2. **Select the correct report type** for your inquiry from the "Report Type" dropdown menu.
3. **Provide a clear description** of the issue or concern in the "Description" field.
4. **Specify the location** where the issue or concern is located, choosing from "Waterway," "Nearby Waterway," or "Not near a Waterway."
5. **Enter the date** and **time** of your report, if applicable.
6. **Add your contact information** so we can reach out to you, if needed.
7. **Include any additional information** about your report, such as attachments, in the "Attachments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Report Issue (1)**: Describe the issue or concern you're reporting. Be as detailed as possible.
  - **Report Type (2)**: Select one of the following options:
    - **Issue**: Report a problem or issue with the waterway infrastructure.
    - **Inspection**: Request an inspection or assessment of the waterway infrastructure.
    - **Improvement**: Suggest an improvement or proposal for the waterway infrastructure.
  - **Description (3)**: Provide a clear and concise explanation of the issue or concern.
  - **Report Location (4)**: Specify where the issue is located:
    - **Waterway**: The issue is directly related to the waterway infrastructure.
    - **Nearby Waterway**: The issue is nearby but not directly related to the waterway infrastructure.
    - **Not near a Waterway**: The issue is not related to the waterway infrastructure.
  - **Report Date (5)**: Enter the date when the issue was observed or occurred.
  - **Report Time (6)**: Enter the time when the issue was observed or occurred.
  - **Contact Info (7)**: Enter your contact information so we can get back to you.
  - **Email (8)**: Enter your email address so we can communicate with you.
  - **Attachments (9)**: Attach any relevant files or documents to support your report.

**Tips:**

- If you're reporting multiple issues, you can use this form multiple times or attach a single document with all the issues listed.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for assistance.
