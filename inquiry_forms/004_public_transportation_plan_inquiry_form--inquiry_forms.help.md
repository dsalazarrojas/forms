# public_transportation_plan_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their public transportation experiences, needs, and preferences. It is intended to help understand how public transportation plans and services are being used and how they can be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Public Transport Name" field. This is the field where you'll provide your name.
2. Enter your email address in the "Your Email" field. This is the field where you'll provide your email address.
3. Select the type(s) of transportation service you are inquiring about in the "Transport Service Type" field. You can select one or more options from the list:
	* Buses
	* Trains
	* Subways
	* Trams
	* Other (if you select this, please describe the other type of transport in the comments field below)
4. If you have any additional comments or information about your public transportation experience, please provide them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Public Transport Name** (`name`, text, optional): This field is where you'll provide your name.
* **Your Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is where you'll provide your email address. This will help us get in touch with you if we need more information.
* **Transport Service Type** (`service_type`, select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to select one or more types of transportation services you are inquiring about. The available options are:
	+ Buses
	+ Trains
	+ Subways
	+ Trams
	+ Other (please describe the other type of transport in the comments field below)
* **Additional Comments** (`comment`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional comments or information about your public transportation experience. 

## Tips
Please fill out the form as completely as possible to help us better understand your public transportation needs and preferences.
