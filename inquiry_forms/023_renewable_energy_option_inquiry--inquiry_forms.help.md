# renewable_energy_option_inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The renewable energy option inquiry form is designed to collect information from customers about their interest in renewable energy options. This form is used to gather details about the customer's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form header to begin.
2. In the "Customer Request" field, type a brief description of your inquiry.
3. In the "Name", "Email", and "Contact Phone" fields, provide your contact information.
4. In the "Preferred Contact Method" and "Contact Method" fields, select the preferred methods for communication.
5. In the "Address", "City", and "Postal/Zip" fields, enter your address information.
6. In the "State/Province" field, select the state or province where you reside.
7. In the "Renewable Energy Option" field, select the types of energy you are interested in.
8. In the "Capacity" field, enter the amount of energy you require.
9. In the "Capacity Unit" field, select the unit of measurement for the capacity.
10. In the "Start Date" and "Start Time" fields, enter the date and time you would like to begin your renewable energy option.
11. In the "End Date" and "End Time" fields, enter the date and time you would like to end your renewable energy option.
12. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Request**: In this field, briefly describe your inquiry for renewable energy.
* **Name**: Enter your name in this field.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in this field.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number in this field.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select the method you would prefer for communication (e.g., "Yes", "No").
* **Contact Method**: Select the methods you are comfortable with for communication (e.g., "Yes", "No").
* **Address**: Enter your address in this field.
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside in this field.
* **State/Province**: Select the state or province where you reside.
* **Postal/Zip**: Enter your postal or zip code in this field.
* **Renewable Energy Option**: Select the types of energy you are interested in (e.g., "Yes", "No").
* **Energy Type**: Select the types of energy you are interested in (e.g., "Yes", "No").
* **Capacity**: Enter the amount of energy you require.
* **Capacity Unit**: Select the unit of measurement for the capacity (e.g., "Yes", "No").
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you would like to begin your renewable energy option.
* **Start Time**: Enter the time you would like to begin your renewable energy option.
* **End Date**: Enter the date you would like to end your renewable energy option.
* **End Time**: Enter the time you would like to end your renewable energy option.
