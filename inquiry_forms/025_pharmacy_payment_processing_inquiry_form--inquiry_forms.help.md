# Pharmacy Payment Processing Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pharmacy Payment Processing Inquiry Form is used to collect information about a payment issue. This form helps our team to investigate and resolve payment-related concerns efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out all required and optional fields as accurately as possible.
2. For fields with multiple options, select the relevant answer.
3. Use the available space in the "Issue Description" field to describe the payment issue in detail.
4. Ensure the "Expected Resolution Time" field is filled out according to your desired timeline for resolving the issue.
5. If the issue is assigned to a team, check the "Assigned Team" field accordingly.
6. Choose the correct payment method from the options provided.
7. Specify the payment date and amount.
8. Indicate whether this is a yes or no issue for "Issue Type" and "Resolution Status".
9. Finally, provide any additional comments about the issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **payment_issue_details**: A short description of the payment issue. For example, "Overpayment on credit card."
* **Pharmacy Information**: Enter the name and address of the pharmacy involved in the payment issue.
* **Contact Information**: Provide the contact details of the person or organization involved in the payment issue.
* **Issue Description**: Describe the issue in detail, including any relevant dates, amounts, or other relevant information.
* **Expected Resolution Time**: Specify the time frame within which you expect the issue to be resolved.
* **Assigned Team**: Check "Yes" if you have been assigned a team to handle the issue. 
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method used for the payment (e.g., Visa, Mastercard, Amex, etc.).
* **Payment Date**: Enter the date of the payment.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount of the payment.
* **Issue Type**: Check "Yes" if this is a new issue or "No" if this is a recurring issue.
* **Payment Status**: Check "Active" if the payment is still pending or "Inactive" if it's been completed.
* **Comments**: Use this field for additional information or context about the payment issue.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out this form.
* Double-check your answers to ensure they are accurate.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to our support team.
* This form is intended for new payment issues only. If you need to follow up on an existing issue, contact us directly.
