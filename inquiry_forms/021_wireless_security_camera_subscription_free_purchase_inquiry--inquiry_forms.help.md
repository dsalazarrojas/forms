# wireless_security_camera_subscription_free_purchase_inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about potential buyers' preferences and requirements for a free purchase of wireless security cameras. It is not for immediate purchases, but for informational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your buyer name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Provide your phone number.
4. Select one or more wireless security camera types that interest you.
5. Choose the security level that suits your needs (Basic, Advanced, or Premium).
6. Select the date you would like to have the wireless security camera installed.
7. Optionally, enter a schedule for follow-up communication (e.g., phone number to call back).
8. Provide any additional comments or information about your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wireless Security Camera Subscription Free Purchase Inquiry**:
	+ This is the title of the form.
* **Buyer Name**:
	+ Enter your name as it appears on your identification or business card.
* **Email**:
	+ Enter your email address where you can be contacted for further information.
* **Phone**:
	+ Enter your phone number where you can be reached for follow-up communication.
* **Wireless Security Camera Type**:
	+ Select one or more wireless security camera types that interest you (e.g., Wireless Security System, Security Camera, Smart Doorbell, Video Security System).
* **Security Level**:
	+ Choose the level of security you require (Basic, Advanced, Premium).
* **Install Date**:
	+ Select the date you would like to have the wireless security camera installed.
* **Schedule Follow Up**:
	+ Optionally, enter a schedule for follow-up communication (e.g., 10:00 am tomorrow).
* **Additional Comments**:
	+ Enter any additional information or comments about your inquiry.
