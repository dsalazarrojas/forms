# Inquiry Call Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inquiry Call Request Form is used to gather information about a customer's inquiry, allowing our team to provide accurate support and address their questions or concerns. Fill out this form to initiate a call and get in touch with our support team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Inquiry Information field with a clear and concise description of your inquiry.
2. Select the Request Type that best categorizes your inquiry (Sales, Support, or Service).
3. Choose a date and time to schedule the call in the Schedule Call field.
4. Enter your Phone Number so we can contact you.
5. Provide your Email Address so we can reach out to you.
6. Add any Additional Notes about your inquiry in the Additional Notes field.
7. Select who will be submitting the form (Sales, Support, or Service) in the Submitter field.
8. Double-check your submission for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inquiry Information** (`inquiry_call_request_form_fields`, `text`, optional): A brief description of your inquiry. Please keep this field concise and clear for our team to quickly understand your question or concern.
* **Request Type** (`inquiry_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of request that best suits your inquiry (Sales, Support, or Service). This will help our team to prioritize and address your issue.
* **Schedule Call** (`schedule_call`, `date`, optional): Select a date and time for the call. Please note that our team will follow the selected schedule to the best of our abilities.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you to discuss your inquiry.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, optional): Provide your email address so we can reach out to you with updates or follow-ups.
* **Inquiry Details** (`inquiry_details`, `note`, optional): Add any additional information or context about your inquiry. This field is for any extra details that may be relevant to your request.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your inquiry. This field is for any extra information that may be helpful for our team to understand your request.
* **Submitter** (`inquiry_call_request_form_submitter`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select who is submitting the form (Sales, Support, or Service) to ensure that our team knows who will be responsible for following up on your inquiry.
