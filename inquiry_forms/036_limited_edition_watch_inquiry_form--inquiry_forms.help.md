# Limited Edition Watch Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users who are interested in learning more about limited edition watches. It is used to gather information from users who are looking for specific details about upcoming releases or existing limited edition watches.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second field.
3. Enter your **Email** in the third field. Please ensure that this is a valid email address.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the fourth field.
5. Select the **Inquiry Type** from the dropdown menu. You can choose between "Limited Edition Watch" and "Other".
6. Choose a **Release Date** for the limited edition watch you are inquiring about.
7. Select a **Release Time** for the limited edition watch you are inquiring about.
8. Add any additional **Message** about your inquiry in the text box.

* **First Name**: A text field where you can enter your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: A text field where you can enter your last name. Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: A field where you can enter your email address. Please ensure that this is a valid email address that you use regularly.
* **Phone**: A field where you can enter your phone number. Please ensure that this is a valid phone number that you can be contacted on.
* **Inquiry Type**: A dropdown menu where you can select the type of limited edition watch you are inquiring about. You can choose between "Limited Edition Watch" and "Other".
* **Release Date**: A field where you can select the release date of the limited edition watch you are inquiring about. This will help us understand when you are interested in purchasing the watch.
* **Release Time**: A field where you can select the release time of the limited edition watch you are inquiring about. This will help us understand when you are interested in purchasing the watch.
* **Message**: A text field where you can add any additional information about your inquiry. This is an optional field, but it will help us better understand your inquiry and respond accordingly.
