# Freight Forwarding Solution Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in freight forwarding solutions. It helps our team to understand your needs and provide tailored services that fit your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Service Inquiry**: Please select the type of service you are interested in from the options provided.
2. **Shipping Rates**: If applicable, provide your estimated shipping volume or any specific rate requirements.
3. **Service Options**: Choose the service type that suits your needs (Express, Ground, or Air).
4. **Delivery Timeline**: Specify your expected delivery date or timeframe.
5. **Package Details**: Describe the package you need to be shipped, including weight, dimensions, and any other relevant details.
6. **Contact Number**: Provide your contact number so we can reach out to discuss further.
7. **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
8. **Message**: If you have any additional information or questions, please include them here.
9. **Additional Comments**: Use this section to add any extra details or context that might help us better serve you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Inquiry**: This field helps us understand your initial inquiry. Please select the service that best suits your needs.
* **Shipping Rates**: This field is optional, but if you have specific rate requirements or estimates, please provide them here.
* **Service Options**: Choose the service type that best fits your requirements (Express, Ground, or Air).
* **Delivery Timeline**: Specify your expected delivery date or timeframe to help us plan and prioritize your shipment.
* **Package Details**: Describe your package's details to ensure we can properly handle and transport it.
* **Contact Number**: This is the number we will use to contact you for further discussion or updates.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address to receive communication and updates about your inquiry.
* **Message**: Use this field to add any additional context or questions you may have.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any further details or context, please include them here.

Note: Some fields are required or optional based on your selected answers. Please ensure you complete all required fields to prevent your inquiry from being incomplete.
