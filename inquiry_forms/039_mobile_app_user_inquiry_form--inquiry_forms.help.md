# Mobile App User Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users regarding their interaction with a mobile app. It helps us understand your experience, identify potential issues, and provide a platform for you to share your thoughts and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in your name**: In the "What's your name?" field, please enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed in our records. This will help us verify your identity.
2. **Provide issue details**: In the "More details about the issue?" field, please describe the problem you've encountered with the app in as much detail as possible. This will help us understand the nature of the issue.
3. **Enter your email address**: In the "Email address" field, please enter the email address you'd like us to use for further correspondence. This will enable us to contact you regarding your inquiry.
4. **Report the date**: In the "Date of reporting" field, please select the date you encountered the issue. This will help us track the timeline of your inquiry.
5. **Share your app usage**: In the "How often do you use our app?" field, please select the frequency of your app usage. This will help us understand your usage patterns.
6. **Specify the app version**: In the "App version" field, please specify the version of the app you're using. If you're unsure, you can leave this field empty.
7. **State your operating system**: In the "Operating system" field, please specify the operating system of your device. If you're unsure, you can leave this field empty.
8. **Share device information**: In the "Device information" field, please provide any additional information about your device that may be relevant to your issue.
9. **Add comments**: In the "Additional comments" field, please provide any further information or comments about your issue. This is an optional field, but it will help us better understand your concern.
10. **Submit your form**: Once you've completed all the fields, click the "Submit" button to send your inquiry to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What's your name?** (`title`, `text`, optional): Please enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **More details about the issue?** (`issue_details`, `text`, optional): Describe the problem you've encountered with the app in as much detail as possible.
* **Email address** (`email`, `text`, required): Enter the email address you'd like us to use for further correspondence.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
* **Date of reporting** (`date_reported`, `date`, required): Select the date you encountered the issue.
* **How often do you use our app?** (`app_usage`, `select_one`, optional): Select the frequency of your app usage.
* **App version** (`app_version`, `text`, optional): Specify the version of the app you're using.
* **Operating system** (`operating_system`, `text`, optional): Specify the operating system of your device.
* **Device information** (`device_info`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information about your device that may be relevant to your issue.
* **Additional comments** (`form_submission`, `note`, optional): Enter any further information or comments about your issue.
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, `text`, optional): Enter your name again, for verification purposes.
* **What are your goals with our app?** (`goals`, `text`, optional): Describe your goals with our app.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your email address is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you're experiencing any issues with the app, we'll get back to you as soon as possible.
* If you have any additional comments or information to share, please use the "Additional comments" field.
* This form is for your convenience and helps us provide better service, so please feel free to share your thoughts and feedback.
