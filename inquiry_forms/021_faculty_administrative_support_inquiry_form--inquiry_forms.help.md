# Faculty Administrative Support Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faculty Administrative Support Inquiry Form is designed to gather information about administrative support requests from faculty members. It helps to streamline the process of submitting and managing support requests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form and start filling it out with your request details.
2. Select your department from the dropdown menu in the "Department" field.
3. Enter your contact person's name and email address.
4. Provide any additional contact information in the "Contact Other Info" field.
5. If you need assistance with anything else, please let us know under "Contact Phone" and "Contact Other Info".
6. Review your form and submit it once you've entered all the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Details** (`request_details`, `note`, not required): Use this field to describe your administrative support request in a few sentences. Please be as clear and concise as possible.
* **Department** (`faculty_department`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the department you are affiliated with to help us direct your request to the correct team.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the person we should contact to discuss your request.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, not required): Provide your or your contact person's email address so we can reach out to you.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, not required): If you'd like us to call you to discuss your request, enter your phone number here.
* **Contact Other Info** (`contact_other_info`, `text`, not required): If there's any other information that might help us assist you, please add it here.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and clear as possible in your request.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask.
* If you have any attachments or additional documentation to support your request, please attach them to your form submission.
* We'll do our best to address your request within a timely manner.
