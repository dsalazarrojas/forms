# Handicraft Product Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from customers interested in a handicraft product. You will find fields for product details and customer contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, as required fields are marked. 
2. Enter your product name, description, and price in the respective fields.
3. Provide your email and phone number for us to contact you.
4. Answer the questions about how you found out about us, whether you want to be contacted, and any other questions we have asked.
5. Finally, let us know if you would like to be contacted by email and if you want to pay on delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Data** (`form_data`): 
  * This is a multi-line question field for you to provide any other information that you feel is relevant to your inquiry.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`): 
  * Enter the name of the product you are inquiring about.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`): 
  * Describe the product in a few sentences.
* **Price** (`price`): 
  * Enter the price of the product, if known.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): 
  * Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): 
  * Provide your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Message** (`message`): 
  * Type any additional comments or questions you have.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`): 
  * Select if you are inquiring about a specific type of product.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`): 
  * Enter your address so we can send you information about our product.
* **Customer City** (`customer_city`): 
  * Enter your city for us to know your location.
* **Customer State** (`customer_state`): 
  * Enter your state for us to know your location.
* **Customer Zip** (`customer_zip`): 
  * Enter your zip code for us to know your location.
* **Customer Country** (`customer_country`): 
  * Enter your country for us to know your location.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`form_data_1`): 
  * Answer how you found out about us, such as social media, online search, or word of mouth.
* **Do you have any other questions about our product?** (`form_data_2`): 
  * Type any other questions you may have about our product.
* **Any Other Information** (`form_data_3`): 
  * Type any additional information you feel is relevant to your inquiry.
* **Do you want to be contacted?** (`form_data_4`): 
  * Select yes or no if you wish to be contacted.
* **What is the best time to contact you?** (`form_data_5`): 
  * Type the best time of day or week for us to contact you.
* **How can we improve?** (`form_data_6`): 
  * Type any suggestions on how we can improve our services.
* **Do you wish to be contacted?** (`form_data_7`): 
  * Answer yes or no if you want to be contacted by email.
* **What is your budget for this purchase?** (`form_data_8`): 
  * Enter your budget for the product, if known.
* **How much are you willing to pay?** (`form_data_9`): 
  * Enter your maximum budget for the product.
* **Would you like to be contacted by email?** (`form_data_10`): 
  * Select yes or no if you wish to be contacted by email.
* **Do you want to pay on delivery?** (`form_data_11`): 
  * Select yes or no if you want to pay on delivery.
* **Any Other Question** (`form_data_12`): 
  * Type any other questions you may have about our product.
