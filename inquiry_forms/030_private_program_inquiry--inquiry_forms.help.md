# Private Program Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Private Program Inquiry form is a tool used to gather information from users about their interest in a private program. This form is used to help our team understand your requirements and preferences for a private program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of session you are interested in from the "Session Type" field.
2. Choose the start and end dates for your private program.
3. Select the type of program you are interested in from the "Program Type" field.
4. If you have a preferred date for your private program, enter it in the "Preferred Date" field.
5. Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number, in the "Contact Person" fields.
6. Provide any additional program details in the "Program Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form**: This is the header for the form, which contains the title "Private Program Inquiry".
* **Session Type**: Choose the type of session you are interested in for your private program. This can help us understand your preferences and better match you with a suitable program.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of your private program. Please be sure to select a date that is in the past or future, but not a range of dates.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of your private program. Like the start date, please select a date that is in the past or future, but not a range of dates.
* **Program Type**: Select the type of program you are interested in for your private program. This can help us understand your preferences and better match you with a suitable program.
* **Preferred Date**: If you have a preferred date for your private program, enter it here. This is not a required field, but it can help us understand your preferences.
* **Contact Person**: Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number, in this field. This is not a required field, but it is recommended so we can get in touch with you about your inquiry.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in this field. This will be used to contact you about your inquiry.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in this field. This will be used to contact you about your inquiry.
* **Program Details**: Enter any additional details about the program you are interested in. This can include any specific requirements or preferences you have.
