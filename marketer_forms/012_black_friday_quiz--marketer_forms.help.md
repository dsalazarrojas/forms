# Black Friday Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Black Friday Quiz is a 12-field questionnaire designed to test your knowledge and shopping habits on Black Friday. By completing this quiz, you'll receive results and be entered into a newsletter for future deals. The form is intended for users of all ages and experience levels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the "Your Name" field.
2.  Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3.  Select the correct date for Black Friday from the "When is Black Friday 2024" dropdown menu.
4.  Choose the correct term for the day after Black Friday from the "What Day Follows Black Friday" dropdown menu.
5.  Select the best time to shop on Black Friday from the "Best Time to Shop" dropdown menu.
6.  Choose the most popular item typically discounted on Black Friday from the "Popular Black Friday Item" dropdown menu.
7.  Select the best online shopping tip from the "Online Shopping Tip" dropdown menu.
8.  Choose whether most stores offer price matching from the "Price Matching" dropdown menu.
9.  Select the factor to check before buying from the "Return Policies" dropdown menu.
10. Select the best practice for Black Friday shopping from the "Budget Planning" dropdown menu.
11. Indicate your experience level with Black Friday shopping from the "Your Shopping Experience" dropdown menu.
12. Choose your favorite shopping category from the "Favorite Shopping Category" dropdown menu.
13. Finally, sign up for the newsletter by selecting "Yes, Sign Me Up" from the "Join Newsletter" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name to be entered into our newsletter and receive results.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for results and prize notification.
* **When is Black Friday 2024** (`question_1`, select_one, required): Select the correct date for Black Friday 2024.
* **What Day Follows Black Friday** (`question_2`, select_one, required): Choose the correct term for the day after Black Friday.
* **Best Time to Shop** (`question_3`, select_one, required): Select the best time to shop on Black Friday.
* **Popular Black Friday Item** (`question_4`, select_one, required): Choose the most popular item discounted on Black Friday.
* **Online Shopping Tip** (`question_5`, select_one, required): Select the best online shopping tip for Black Friday.
* **Price Matching** (`question_6`, select_one, required): Choose whether most stores offer price matching.
* **Return Policies** (`question_7`, select_one, required): Select the factor to check before buying.
* **Budget Planning** (`question_8`, select_one, required): Select the best practice for Black Friday shopping.
* **Your Shopping Experience** (`shopping_experience`, select_one, required): Indicate your experience level with Black Friday shopping.
* **Favorite Shopping Category** (`favorite_category`, select_one, required): Choose your favorite shopping category.
* **Join Newsletter** (`newsletter_signup`, select_one, required): Sign up for our newsletter to receive quiz results and future deals.
* **Thank You** (`score_display`, note, required): Your results will be emailed to you.
* 
Note that the "Thank You" field is not required to be filled in to submit the form, but it will display the results.
