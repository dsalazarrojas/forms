# Content Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Content Report Form is a tool for users to report content issues they have encountered on a platform or website. This form helps the content team to identify and resolve problems efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Provide Your Information
Fill in your name and email address to ensure you receive updates on the status of your issue report.

### Step 2: Describe the Issue
Select an issue category that best describes the problem and provide a detailed description of the issue. You can also take a screenshot of the issue if it's helpful for the content team to understand the problem.

### Step 3: Choose a Priority Level
Choose a priority level for your issue report. This will help the content team to prioritize and address the issues in a timely manner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: <span class="italic">Please enter your name to ensure you receive updates on the status of your issue report.</span>
* **Your Email Address**: <span class="italic">Enter a valid email address to receive notifications when the issue is resolved.</span>
* **URL of the Content**: <span class="italic">Provide the link to the specific page or post where the issue is located.</span>
* **Issue Category**: <span class="italic">Select an issue category that best describes the problem (e.g., Broken Link or Media, Typographical or Grammar Error, etc.).</span>
* **Detailed Description of the Issue**: <span class="italic">Explain the problem in detail, including any screenshots or additional information that may be helpful for the content team to understand the issue.</span>
* **Priority Level**: <span class="italic">Choose a priority level for your issue report to help the content team prioritize and address the issues in a timely manner (Low, Medium, High).</span>
* **Date Reported**: <span class="italic">Enter the date you reported the issue in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.</span>
* **Device or Browser Used**: <span class="italic">If applicable, enter the device or browser you were using when you encountered the issue.</span>
* **Suggested Fix** (optional): <span class="italic">If you know the correct information, provide it to help the content team resolve the issue more efficiently.</span>
* **Did you take a screenshot of the issue?**: <span class="italic">If you have a screenshot of the issue, select 'True'.</span>
