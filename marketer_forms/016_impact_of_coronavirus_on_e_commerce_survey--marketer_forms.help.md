# impact_of_coronavirus_on_e_commerce_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a self-reporting tool designed to gather information about how businesses were affected by the COVID-19 pandemic and how they responded to its effects.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all the questions honestly, and choose the options that best describe your experiences and plans.
2. If a question doesn't apply to your business, you can select "Other" and provide a brief explanation.
3. Take your time to review each question carefully, and make sure you have selected the correct options.
