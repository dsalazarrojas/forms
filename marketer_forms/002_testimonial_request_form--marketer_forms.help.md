# Testimonial Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Testimonial Request Form is used to gather customer feedback and testimonials about our services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Click on the form link to access the form.
* Review the form questions carefully and answer each question to the best of your ability.
* Fill in all required fields. If a field is not marked as required, you can leave it blank, but it is not essential for your testimonial submission.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* Click the submit button to send your testimonial request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **I am willing to give my testimonial** (`consent`, select_one, required):
	+ This field is a yes/no question to confirm your willingness to provide a testimonial.
* **Rate our service** (`rating`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Choose one or more of the provided options (Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor) to rate our service.
* **Testimonial** (`testimonial`, text, optional):
	+ Share a brief story or comment about your experience with our service.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter your contact email address, so we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, optional):
	+ Provide your phone number, in case we need to contact you.
* **Customer Services** (`customer_services`, text, optional):
	+ Describe any additional customer services you received from us.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional):
	+ Share any other comments or suggestions you may have about our service.

## Tips

* Be honest and provide as much detail as you can about your experience with our service.
* Keep your testimonial submission concise and clear.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
