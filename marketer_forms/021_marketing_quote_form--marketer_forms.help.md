# Marketing Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Quote Form is used to collect information from clients for marketing services, including quotes for potential projects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information, including your client's name, email, and phone number.
2. Choose the type of service you are looking for (e.g. Digital Marketing, Social Media, Content Creation, or Branding).
3. Enter a brief description of the service you are interested in, and a quote for the price.
4. Confirm that you have read and agreed to the Terms and Conditions.
5. Choose the submit button to finish filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Marketing Quote Form (Page 1)
* **Marketing Quote Form** (`marketing_quote_form_first_page`, `text`, required/optional): This is the main section of the form where you can enter your quote.
* **Client Information** (`marketing_quote_form_client_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name and a brief description of the service you are interested in.
* **Client Name** (`marketing_quote_form_client_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as the client.
* **Client Email** (`marketing_quote_form_client_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Client Phone** (`marketing_quote_form_client_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Service Type** (`marketing_quote_form_service_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of service you are interested in (e.g. Digital Marketing, Social Media, Content Creation, or Branding).
* **Quote** (`marketing_quote_form_quote`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief quote for the price of the service.
* **Service Information** (`marketing_quote_form_service_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a description of the service you are interested in.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`marketing_quote_form_terms_and_conditions`, `note`, required/optional): Read and agree to the Terms and Conditions.

### Terms and Conditions
* **Agreed by** (`marketing_quote_form_agreed_by`, `text`, required/optional): Confirm that you have read and agreed to the Terms and Conditions.
* **Agreed on** (`marketing_quote_form_agreed_on`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you agreed to the Terms and Conditions.
* **Agreed Price** (`marketing_quote_form_agreed_price`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the agreed price for the service.
* **Price Valid until** (`marketing_quote_form_price_valid_until`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date the price is valid until.
* **Submit** (`marketing_quote_form_submit`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the submit button to finish filling out the form.
* **Cancel** (`marketing_quote_form_submit`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the cancel button to cancel the form submission.

Note: All fields with `required/optional` label are only optional unless otherwise specified.
