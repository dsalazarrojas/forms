# Market Sizing Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Market Sizing Template is a tool designed to help market researchers and analysts size and understand the market size and potential of a specific market or industry. This template guides you through the process of defining the market, its segment, and other key characteristics that will help you estimate its revenue and growth potential.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully and understand each section and field before proceeding.
2. Answer each question truthfully and accurately, based on your research and analysis.
3. For each field, select the most suitable option from the provided list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Market Segment** (`market_segment`, select_one): 
Choose the segment you believe this market belongs to, based on your understanding of the market.

### **Target Market** (`target_market`, text): Enter the target market name or description.

### **Market Size** (`market_size`, select_multiple): Select all market sizes that apply to your target market.

### **Competition** (`competition`, select_multiple): Select all competition that exist in your target market.

### **Revenue** (`revenue`, number): Enter the total revenue for your target market.

### **Margin** (`margin`, number): Enter the margin for your target market.

### **Total Revenue** (`total_revenue`, number): Enter the total revenue for your target market.

### **Target Price** (`target_price`, text): Enter the target price for your product or service.

### **Pricing Strategy** (`pricing_strategy`, select_one): Choose the pricing strategy you would employ for your target market.

### **Distribution Channels** (`distribution_channels`, select_multiple): Select all distribution channels that you would use for your target market.

### **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, select_multiple): Select all target audience that you would cater to in your target market.

### **Geographic Location** (`geographic_location`, select_multiple): Select all geographic locations that you would target in your market.

### **Product Line** (`product_line`, select_multiple): Select all product lines that you would offer in your target market.

### **Sales Channels** (`sales_channels`, select_multiple): Select all sales channels that you would use for your target market.

### **Marketing Channels** (`marketing_channels`, select_multiple): Select all marketing channels that you would employ for your target market.
