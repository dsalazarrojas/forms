# Product Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Product Satisfaction Questionnaire" is a form designed to collect customer feedback on their experience with a specific product. It helps to measure customer satisfaction levels on various aspects of the product and gather overall satisfaction ratings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your response for each of the questions asked in the form.
2. Provide your answers in the format asked for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Information (Select one or more)
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or more of the provided options that best describe you as a customer. This will help us understand who you are as a customer and gather more accurate feedback.

### How would you rate the product? (question_1, number, required/optional)
* **How would you rate the product?** (`question_1`, `number`, required/optional): On a scale of 1-5, rate how well the product meets your expectations. Think about the product's features, performance, and overall impact. 

### How would you rate the overall satisfaction with the product? (question_2, number, required/optional)
* **How would you rate the overall satisfaction with the product?** (`question_2`, `number`, required/optional): On a scale of 1-5, rate your overall satisfaction with the product. Consider how the product's features, performance, and impact align with your expectations and goals. 

## Tips
* Please answer each question to the best of your ability and truthfully.
* If you have any additional feedback or comments, please include them in the provided text box.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form and provide your valuable feedback.
