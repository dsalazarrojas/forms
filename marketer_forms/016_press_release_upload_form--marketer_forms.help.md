# press_release_upload_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "press_release_upload_form" is a form used for uploading and managing press releases. It captures essential details such as release type, date, time, and content, and provides optional fields for additional context like company name, contact information, and a file upload.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the release type from the provided options.
2. Enter the date and time of the release in the respective fields.
3. Provide the content of the press release in the text field.
4. Add any additional context, such as company name, contact information, and file upload, as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company or organization issuing the press release.
* **Release Type**: Choose the correct option from the provided list to categorize the type of release (e.g., news article, announcement, etc.).
* **Date**: Enter the date of the press release.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the press release.
* **Content**: Enter the main content of the press release in this text field.
* **Uploaded By**: Enter the name of the person who uploaded the press release.
* **Email**: Enter the contact email of the person who uploaded the press release. This field is for contact purposes only.
* **Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the person who uploaded the press release. This field is for contact purposes only.
* **Uploaded Date**: Enter the date the press release was uploaded.
* **Uploaded Time**: Enter the time the press release was uploaded.
* **File**: Upload any additional files related to the press release, such as images or documents.
* **Note**: There is no field named "Note", but it seems you might be thinking of "Content" field which is the main description of the press release.
