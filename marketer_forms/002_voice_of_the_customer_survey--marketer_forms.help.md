# voice_of_the_customer_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experiences with a product or service. We value your opinions and encourage you to share them to help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction section, which provides some context for the feedback we are seeking.
2. If you have any comments or questions, please provide feedback in the text field.
3. Rate your experience by selecting a number from 1 to 5.
4. Provide a brief description of your experience to help us better understand your concerns.
5. Provide your name for us to contact you, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction Section** (intro_section): This is the starting point of the form, providing background information for the feedback we are seeking.
* **Feedback Question** (feedback_question): Use this space to type in any comments or questions you may have about our product or service.
* **Rating Question** (rating_question): Rate your experience from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent) to help us understand how well we performed.
* **Feedback Response** (feedback_response): Share a brief description of your experience, highlighting any concerns or suggestions you may have.
* **Name** (name): Please provide your name so we can get back to you, if needed.

## Tips
- Please be honest and open when providing feedback. Your input will help us improve our services.
- We value your time and will keep your feedback confidential.
- If you have any suggestions, feel free to share them, as they will help us improve our services.
