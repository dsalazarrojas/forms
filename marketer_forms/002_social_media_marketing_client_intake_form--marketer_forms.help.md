# Social Media Marketing Client Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Social Media Marketing Client Intake Form is designed to collect essential information about our social media marketing clients to help us tailor our strategies and provide a personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Email address for communication purposes.
3. Enter your Phone number for contact and follow-up.
4. Provide your Company name.
5. Enter your Job Title for reference.
6. Select the services you expect from us (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
7. Choose the marketing channels you'd like us to use (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
8. Briefly describe your Company for our reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your personal name, e.g., John Doe.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address we can use for communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number for follow-up and updates.
* **Company**: Enter your company name.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position in the company.
* **Expected Services**: Select 'Yes' if you expect services such as content creation, campaign management, or analytics.
* **Marketing Channels**: Choose 'Yes' if you'd like us to use channels like Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, or Instagram.
* **Company Description**: Briefly describe your company for our reference and understanding of your needs. 

## Tips
- Double-check your input to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask us for clarification.
- You can always contact us if you need any adjustments or modifications to your services.
