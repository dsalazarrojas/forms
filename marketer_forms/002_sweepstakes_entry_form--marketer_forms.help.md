# sweepstakes_entry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sweepstakes_entry_form is used to collect information about users who have entered a sweepstakes. This form helps track and manage sweepstakes entries for internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, follow these steps:
    1.  Fill in your **First and Last Name** in the text field provided.
    2.  Enter your valid **Email Address** to receive notifications and updates.
    3.  Provide the **Name of Sweepstakes** that you have entered.
    4.  Enter the **Sweepstakes ID** assigned to the sweepstakes you entered.
    5.  Enter the **Date Entered** when you initially entered the sweepstakes.
    6.  Select the **Time Entered** using the time picker input.
    7.  Choose the correct **Method of Entry** from the dropdown select options.
    8.  Select the relevant **Status of Entry** from the dropdown select options.
    9.  Add any additional comments or notes in the **Comments** field.
    10. If you want to receive email notifications about the sweepstakes, select "Yes" or "No" in the **Email Notification** field.
    11. Enter your **Phone Number** if you have one.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First and Last Name (First and Last Name)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Email Address (Email Address)**: Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted.
*   **Name of Sweepstakes (Name of Sweepstakes)**: Provide the name of the sweepstakes you entered.
*   **Sweepstakes ID (Sweepstakes ID)**: Enter the unique ID of the sweepstakes.
*   **Date Entered (Date Entered)**: Select the date you initially entered the sweepstakes.
*   **Time Entered (Time Entered)**: Select the time you initially entered the sweepstakes.
*   **Method of Entry (Method of Entry)**: Choose how you entered the sweepstakes (e.g., online, mail, in-person).
*   **Status of Entry (Status of Entry)**: Select the current status of your entry (e.g., pending, confirmed, disqualified).
*   **Comments (Comments)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your entry.
*   **Email Notification (Email Notification)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to receive email notifications about the sweepstakes.
*   **Phone Number (Phone Number)**: Enter your phone number if you have one and would like to be contacted.

## Tips

*   Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your entry is complete and valid.
*   If you are having trouble with the form, please contact our support team for assistance.
*   Make sure to check your email for notifications and updates about the sweepstakes.
