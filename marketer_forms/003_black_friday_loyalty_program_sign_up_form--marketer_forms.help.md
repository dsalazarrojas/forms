### Black Friday Loyalty Program Sign Up Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
The Black Friday Loyalty Program Sign Up Form is designed to collect information from customers so we can provide them with exclusive offers and personalized experiences.

### How To Complete This Form

To sign up for the Black Friday Loyalty Program, fill out the form with the required information, following each field's instructions and guidelines.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (1)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email Address (2)**: Enter a valid email address that will be used for account setup and to receive rewards and marketing communications.
* **Phone Number (4)**: Enter your phone number if you want to receive SMS alerts for exclusive offers.
* **Date of Birth (5)**: Enter your date of birth to receive age-based offers.
* **Mailing Address (6)**: Enter your physical address if you want to receive physical rewards.
* **Loyalty Tier Preference (7)**: Choose your preferred loyalty tier (Standard, Silver, Gold, or Platinum).
* **Receive Promotional Emails (8)**: Opt-in to receive marketing communications and promotional offers.
* **Referral Code (9)**: Enter any referral code provided by a friend if you've been referred.
* **Additional Notes (10)**: Enter any accessibility needs or special requests you may have.

### Tips
Please ensure you provide accurate and honest information to receive the best experience and rewards from the program. If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
