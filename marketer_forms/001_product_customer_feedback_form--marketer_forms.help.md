# Product Customer Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer feedback on a product, allowing us to understand their experiences and opinions. It is intended for customers to rate their satisfaction and provide suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your rating of the product from the "Product Rating" field. This will help us understand your overall satisfaction with the product.
2. Provide your honest feedback on the product in the "What do you think about this product?" field. Be as detailed as possible so we can understand your thoughts.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with the product in the "Overall Satisfaction" field.
4. If you have any specific suggestions or ideas for improving the product, enter them in the "How can we improve this product?" field.
5. If you have any additional comments or feedback, you can enter them in the "Additional feedback" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
8. If you have a preferred date and time, you can enter it in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
9. If you'd like to add any additional information, you can include it in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Product Rating
• **Product Rating** (`product_rating_1`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the rating that best reflects your satisfaction with the product. Choose from "Good", "Fair", or other options if available.

### What do you think about this product?
• **What do you think about this product?** (`product_name_1`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your honest feedback on the product. Share your thoughts on its features, usability, and anything else that comes to mind.

### Overall Satisfaction
• **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction_1`, `select_one`, required/optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the product. Choose from "Somewhat Satisfied", "Satisfied", or "Very Satisfied" to help us understand your level of satisfaction.

### How can we improve this product?
• **How can we improve this product?** (`product_improvement_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your suggestions for improving the product. If you have any ideas, we'd love to hear them.

### Additional feedback
• **Additional feedback** (`additional_feedback_1`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the product. This will help us understand your thoughts and concerns.

### Phone
• **Phone** (`phone_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion.

### Email
• **Email** (`email_1`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address, if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.

### Date
• **Date** (`date_1`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you used the product.

### Time
• **Time** (`time_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the time you used the product.

### Note
• **Note** (`note_1`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional information or notes you'd like to share about your experience with the product.
