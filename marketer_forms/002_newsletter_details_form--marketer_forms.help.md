# Newsletter Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Newsletter Details Form is used to collect information from users who are interested in subscribing to a newsletter.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your **Email** address to receive newsletters.
3. Enter your **Phone** number (optional).
4. Select your **Interested In** options from the list.
5. If you have a specific interest not listed, type it in the **Interested In Other** field.
6. Confirm your **Email** address to ensure it is correct.
7. Confirm your **Phone** number (if provided).
8. Agree to the **Terms and Conditions**.
9. Read the **Privacy Policy**.
10. Select your desired **Newsletter Frequency** (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly).
11. Choose **Newsletter Topics** that interest you (select multiple options).
12. Select how you would like to **Unsubscribe From** the newsletter (e.g., email, phone, both).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`4`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to receive newsletters.
* **Phone** (`5`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Interested In** (`6`, `select_one`, required): Select your interests from the list.
* **Interested In Other** (`7`, `text`, optional): If you have a specific interest not listed, type it here.
* **Confirm Email** (`8`, `email`, required): Confirm your email address to ensure it is correct.
* **Confirm Phone** (`9`, `text`, optional): Confirm your phone number (if provided).
* **Agree to Terms and Conditions** (`10`, `select_one`, required): Agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`11`, `text`, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Privacy Policy** (`12`, `text`, optional): Read our privacy policy.
* **Newsletter Frequency** (`13`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you want to receive the newsletter.
* **Newsletter Topics** (`14`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose newsletter topics that interest you.
* **Unsubscribe From** (`15`, `select_one`, required): Select how you would like to unsubscribe from the newsletter.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to complete the form successfully.
* Use the **Interested In Other** field to provide specific interests not listed.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
* Read and agree to the **Terms and Conditions** to ensure a smooth subscription process.
