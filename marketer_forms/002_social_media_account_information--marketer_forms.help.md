# Social Media Account Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Media Account Information form is designed to collect essential details about a social media account. This information is crucial for effective management and tracking of social media presence.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Platform** where the social media account is located from the drop-down list.
2. Enter the **Account name** in the text field.
3. Type the **Account ID** in the corresponding text field.
4. For security purposes, enter the **Password** to the account (if required).
5. Provide the **Account URL** for easy access to the account's homepage.
6. Enter the **Email** associated with the account (for platforms requiring email verification).
7. Enter the phone **Number** associated with the account (if required).
8. Specify whether the account is **Public** or **Private**.
9. Choose the **Account status** from the options (Active, Inactive, or Suspended).
10. Select the **Created date** and **Updated date** using the date picker.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Platform**: Select the social media platform the account belongs to.
*  **Account name**: Enter the name of the social media account.
*  **Account ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the account.
*  **Password**: Enter the password for the account (if required).
*  **Account URL**: Enter the URL of the account's homepage.
*  **Email**: Enter the email address associated with the account.
*  **Phone number**: Enter the phone number associated with the account (if required).
*  **Account type**: Specify whether the account is public or private.
*  **Account status**: Choose the current status of the account (Active, Inactive, or Suspended).
*  **Created date**: Enter the date the account was created.
*  **Updated date**: Enter the date the account was last updated.
