# Newsletter Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to collect information about your preferences regarding newsletters. It aims to gather your interests and expectations to help us better tailor our content and services to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each page carefully and fill out the required information.
2. Answer every question honestly, and to the best of your abilities.
3. Double-check your responses for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Newsletter Preferences Survey Page 1** (`note`, required: false): This page serves as an introduction to the survey, explaining its purpose and what to expect.
* **Newsletter Preferences Survey Page 2** (`text`, required: false): On this page, you will be asked to share your preferences regarding newsletters, such as topics or frequency of interest.
* **Newsletter Preferences Survey Page 3** (`date`, required: false): This page is for collecting your date of birth or any specific dates relevant to your preferences, such as your birthday or anniversary.
