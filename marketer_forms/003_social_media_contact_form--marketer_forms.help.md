# social_media_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This help guide explains how to complete the "social_media_contact_form", a contact form designed to gather user contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the "social_media_contact_form", follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, name and email, which are essential for us to get in touch with you.
2. Add a phone number (optional) if you would like to be contacted via phone.
3. In the "message" field, describe your inquiry or question in a clear and concise manner.
4. If applicable, select the desired options to further qualify or categorize your inquiry.
5. Click the "Submit" button to send your message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (text, required): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed when we respond to you.
- **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to get in touch with you.
- **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like us to contact you via phone.
- **Message** (text, required): Describe your inquiry or question in a clear and concise manner.
- **Options** (select multiple, optional): Select the options that apply to your inquiry (if applicable).
  - **Yes** (selectable option)
  - **No** (selectable option)
- **Submit** (text, required): Click this button to send your message and get in touch with us.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the email address you provide is valid and up-to-date.
- Keep your message clear and concise to help us respond efficiently.
- If you have any attachments or further information, consider adding them to your message.
- If you do not hear back from us within a reasonable timeframe, feel free to reach out again.
