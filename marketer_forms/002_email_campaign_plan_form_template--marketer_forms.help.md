# Email Campaign Plan Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to guide marketers in creating a structured email marketing campaign plan. It will help you define your campaign's goals, target audience, and email marketing strategy, including budget and scheduling details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field to the best of your ability, using the information you have available.
2. Ensure that the form is filled out completely before submitting it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Campaign Goals** (`campaign_goals`)
* Briefly describe the purpose and objectives of the email marketing campaign. This should include the desired outcome, such as increasing sales, promoting a new product, or building brand awareness.

### 2. **Target Audience** (`target_audience`)
* Identify the specific group of people you want to target with your email campaign. This could be a specific demographic, such as males between 25-45 years old, or a specific interest group, like customers who have purchased from your company before.

### 3. **Campaign Strategy** (`campaign_strategy`)
* Describe the overall strategy you plan to use for your email campaign. This could include tactics like segmentation, personalization, or using specific email marketing channels.

### 4. **Email Marketing Channels** (`email_marketing_channels`)
* Select the email marketing channels you plan to use for your campaign, such as Email, Social Media, or Text Message. You can choose multiple options.

### 5. **Target Audience Gender** (`target_audience_gender`)
* Identify the gender of the target audience for your campaign. You can choose Male, Female, or Both.

### 6. **Target Audience Age** (`target_audience_age`)
* Enter the age range of the target audience for your campaign. This is optional and only if you want to specify an age range.

### 7. **Email Marketing Budget** (`email_marketing_budget`)
* Enter the budget allocated for your email marketing campaign. This is a required field as it's essential for planning and execution.

### 8. **Email Marketing Tactics** (`email_marketing_tactics`)
* Describe specific tactics you plan to use in your email marketing campaign, such as email subject lines, content, or calls-to-action.

### 9. **Email Marketing Scheduling** (`email_marketing_scheduling`)
* Enter the scheduling details for your email marketing campaign, including send dates and times. This is optional but recommended for a well-planned campaign.
 
## Tips
* Ensure that your answers are clear and concise.
* Double-check that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Review and revise your answers to ensure they meet the campaign's objectives.
