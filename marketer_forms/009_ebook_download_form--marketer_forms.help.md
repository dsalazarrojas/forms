# Ebook Download Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Ebook Download Form" is used to collect information from users who are interested in downloading ebooks. The form is designed to ensure that users are eligible to receive the ebooks they want and to provide a seamless downloading experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the ebooks you are interested in downloading from the "Ebooks" dropdown menu.
6. Choose the price option for the selected ebook.
7. Select "Confirm" if you are ready to download the selected ebook.
8. Optionally, select "Decline" if you are not ready to download the selected ebook.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First name (First name)
* First name: This field is required and should contain your first name.

*Hint: Enter your first name*

### Last name (Last name)
* Last name: This field is required and should contain your last name.

*Hint: Enter your last name*

### Email (Email)
* Email: This field is required and should contain your valid email address.

*Hint: Enter a valid email address*

### Phone (Phone)
* Phone: This field is required and should contain your phone number.

*Hint: Enter your phone number*

### Ebooks (Ebooks)
* Ebooks: This field is a dropdown menu that allows you to select the ebooks you are interested in downloading.

*Hint: Select the ebooks you want to download*

### Price (Price)
* Price: This field is a numeric field that allows you to select the price option for the selected ebook.

*Hint: Enter a number corresponding to the selected ebook price*

### Download (Download)
* Download: This field is not required but allows you to confirm or decline the download of the selected ebook.

*Hint: Select "Confirm" to proceed with the download or select "Decline" if you are not ready*
