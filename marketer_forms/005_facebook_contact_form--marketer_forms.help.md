# Facebook Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact information and feedback from users. Please fill out this form to send a message to us.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields with your First Name, Last Name, Email, and Message.
2. Optionally, you can provide more information such as your Phone Number, Subject, and Date and Time.
3. Choose from the select options as needed.
4. Submit the form to send your message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`user_input`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email_input`, `email`, required): Enter your email address that we can use to respond to you.
* **Subject** (`subject`, `text`, optional): Choose a brief subject to help us understand your message better.
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, required): Type in your message or feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone_input`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you want us to contact you by phone.
* **Email (again)** (`email_input_2`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address again, if you want to be contacted by email.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you are available to be contacted.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you are available to be contacted.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one option from the list.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose multiple options from the list.
* **Select One Required** (`select_one_required`, `select_one`, required): Choose one required option from the list.
* **Select Multiple Required** (`select_multiple_required`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose multiple required options from the list.
* **Date and Time** (`date_time`, `text`, optional): Enter a date and time you are available to be contacted.
* **Note** (`note_input`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Phone 2** (`phone_input_2`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number again if you want us to contact you by phone.
* **Email 2** (`email_input_2`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address again, if you want to be contacted by email.
* **Message 2** (`message_2`, `text`, optional): Type in another message or feedback.
* **Select Multiple 2** (`select_multiple_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose multiple options from the list again.
* **Select One 2** (`select_one_2`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one option from the list again.
* **Select Multiple 3** (`select_multiple_3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose multiple options from the list again.
* **Select One 3** (`select_one_3`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one option from the list again.
* **Select Multiple 4** (`select_multiple_4`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose multiple options from the list again.
* **Email 4** (`email_input_4`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address again, if you want to be contacted by email.
* **Message 3** (`message_3`, `text`, optional): Type in another message or feedback.
