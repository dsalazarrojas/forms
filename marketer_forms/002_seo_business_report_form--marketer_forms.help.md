# seo_business_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The `seo_business_report_form` is used to submit and manage SEO-related data for business reports.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the `Report Name` in the first field.
2. Enter the target URL in the `Target URL` field.
3. Enter the target title in the `Target Title` field.
4. Enter the meta description in the `Meta Description` field.
5. Enter the meta keywords in the `Meta Keywords` field.
6. Enter the meta tags in the `Meta Tags` field.
7. Enter the meta author in the `Meta Author` field.
8. Enter the meta robots in the `Meta Robots` field.
9. Enter the meta canonical URL in the `Meta Canonical URL` field.
10. Enter the internal links in the `Internal Links` field.
11. Enter the external links in the `External Links` field.
12. Enter the canonical URL in the `Canonical URL` field.
13. Enter the meta description length in the `Meta Description Length` field.
14. Enter the meta keywords length in the `Meta Keywords Length` field.
15. Enter the meta tags length in the `Meta Tags Length` field.
16. Enter the meta author length in the `Meta Author Length` field.
17. Enter the meta robots length in the `Meta Robots Length` field.
18. Enter the meta canonical URL length in the `Meta Canonical URL Length` field.
19. Enter the internal links length in the `Internal Links Length` field.
20. Enter the external links length in the `External Links Length` field.
21. Enter the canonical URL length in the `Canonical URL Length` field.
22. Enter the meta description 2 in the `Meta Description 2` field.
23. Enter the meta keywords 2 in the `Meta Keywords 2` field.
24. Enter the meta tags 2 in the `Meta Tags 2` field.
25. Enter the meta author 2 in the `Meta Author 2` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Name** (`report_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the report you want to submit.
* **Target URL** (`target_url`, `text`, required: false): Enter the target URL for the report.
* **Target Title** (`target_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter the target title for the report.
* **Meta Description** (`meta_description`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the report.
* **Meta Keywords** (`meta_keywords`, `text`, required: false): Enter a list of relevant keywords related to the report.
* **Meta Tags** (`meta_tags`, `text`, required: false): Enter a list of meta tags related to the report.
* **Meta Author** (`meta_author`, `text`, required: false): Enter the author of the report.
* **Meta Robots** (`meta_robots`, `text`, required: false): Enter the meta robots directives for the report.
* **Meta Canonical URL** (`meta_canonical_url`, `text`, required: false): Enter the meta canonical URL for the report.
* **Internal Links** (`internal_links`, `text`, required: false): Enter the internal links related to the report.
* **External Links** (`external_links`, `text`, required: false): Enter the external links related to the report.
* **Canonical URL** (`canonical_url`, `text`, required: false): Enter the canonical URL for the report.
* **Meta Description Length** (`meta_description_length`, `number`, required: false): Enter the length of the meta description.
* **Meta Keywords Length** (`meta_keywords_length`, `number`, required: false): Enter the length of the meta keywords.
* **Meta Tags Length** (`meta_tags_length`, `number`, required: false): Enter the length of the meta tags.
* **Meta Author Length** (`meta_author_length`, `number`, required: false): Enter the length of the meta author.
* **Meta Robots Length** (`meta_robots_length`, `number`, required: false): Enter the length of the meta robots.
* **Meta Canonical URL Length** (`meta_canonical_url_length`, `number`, required: false): Enter the length of the meta canonical URL.
* **Internal Links Length** (`internal_links_length`, `number`, required: false): Enter the length of the internal links.
* **External Links Length** (`external_links_length`, `number`, required: false): Enter the length of the external links.
* **Canonical URL Length** (`canonical_url_length`, `number`, required: false): Enter the length of the canonical URL.
* **Meta Description 2** (`meta_description_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the meta description 2 for the report.
* **Meta Keywords 2** (`meta_keywords_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the meta keywords 2 for the report.
* **Meta Tags 2** (`meta_tags_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the meta tags 2 for the report.
* **Meta Author 2** (`meta_author_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the meta author 2 for the report.
