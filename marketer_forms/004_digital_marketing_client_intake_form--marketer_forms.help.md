# Digital Marketing Client Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential project information from clients for digital marketing projects. It serves as a single-page intake form that helps to ensure all necessary details are captured accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields and understand which ones are required and which ones are optional.
2. Fill out the form with the required information for your project.
3. Be as detailed as possible when providing information for the project objectives and additional notes.
4. Select the relevant options for target audience, content creation, and social media engagement.
5. Provide an estimate of the budget allocation for the project.
6. Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info**: This field is a text field where you can provide information about your company or organization.
* **Contact Details**: This field is a text field where you can provide contact information such as name, phone number, and email address.
* **Project Info**: This field is a text field where you can describe your project in detail.
* **Project Objectives**: This field is a text field where you can specify your project's goals and objectives.
* **Target Audience**: Select all applicable options for the target audience of your project (Yes or No).
* **Content Creation**: Select all applicable options for the content creation requirements (Yes or No).
* **Social Media**: Select all applicable options for social media engagement (Yes or No).
* **Project Timeline**: This field is a date and time input field where you can provide an estimated project timeline.
* **Budget Allocation**: This field is a numerical input field where you can estimate the budget for your project.
* **Additional Notes**: This field is a text area where you can provide any additional comments or information about the project.
