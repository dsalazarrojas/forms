# Event Attendee Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant information about event attendees to help event organizers tailor their events to better meet their attendees' needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing the name of the event and a brief description of the event.
2. Enter your email address so we can contact you about event updates and details.
3. Provide your phone number so we can reach you with any last-minute event updates.
4. Choose the date and start and end times for the event.
5. Select the location of the event.
6. Enter your age.
7. Indicate your gender.
8. Choose your interests from the list provided (you can select multiple interests).
9. Enter a brief description of your occupation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (1, Text): Enter the name of the event you will be attending.
* **Event Description** (2, Note): Provide a brief description of the event (optional).
* **Email** (3, Email): Enter your email address so we can contact you about event updates and details.
* **Phone** (4, Text): Enter your phone number so we can reach you with any last-minute event updates.
* **Event Date** (5, Date): Choose the date of the event.
* **Event Start Time** (6, Time): Choose the start time of the event.
* **Event End Time** (7, Time): Choose the end time of the event.
* **Event Location** (8, Text): Enter the location of the event.
* **Attendee Age** (9, Number): Enter your age.
* **Attendee Gender** (10, Select One): Indicate your gender (Male, Female, Other, or I prefer not to answer).
* **Attendee Interests** (11, Select Multiple): Choose up to 3 interests that apply to you from the list provided (Art, Culture, Sports, Music, Food, Travel, Technology, Business, Health, Environment, Politics, Social, Education, Other).
* **Attendee Occupation** (12, Text): Provide a brief description of your occupation (optional).
* **Tips**: Please answer all required fields truthfully, and provide as much detail as you need to fully describe your answers.
