# Post Purchase Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers after a purchase has been made.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a note field where you can provide any additional information about your purchase. Please use this space to introduce yourself and provide context for your feedback.
* **Product Purchased**: Select the product you purchased from the options provided.
* **Rating**: Rate your experience with the product, using the number field.
* **Comments**: Share any comments or feedback you have about your experience.
* **Contact Info**: Provide your contact information, including email and phone number.
* **Contact Method**: Choose your preferred method of contact for future communication. Select all methods that apply.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose your preferred method of contact for future communication. Select one method.
* **Date**: Provide the date of your purchase.
* **Time**: Provide the time of your purchase.
* **Additional Comments**: Share any additional comments or feedback.
* **Signature**: Add a digital signature, if required.
* **Email**: Provide your email address.
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number.
* **Contact Method2**: Choose your preferred method of contact for future communication. Select all methods.
* **Preferred Contact Method2**: Choose your preferred method of contact for future communication. Select one method.
* **Date2**: Provide the date of your purchase.
* **Time2**: Provide the time of your purchase.
* **Comments Section2**: Share any additional comments or feedback.
* **Signature2**: Add a digital signature, if required.
* **Product Purchased2**: Select the product you purchased from the options provided.
* **Comments2**: Share any comments or feedback you have about your experience.
* **Contact Info2**: Provide your contact information, including email and phone number.
* **Phone2**: Provide your phone number.
* **Contact Method2**: Choose your preferred method of contact for future communication. Select all methods.
* **Preferred Contact Method2**: Choose your preferred method of contact for future communication. Select one method.
* **Date2**: Provide the date of your purchase.
* **Time2**: Provide the time of your purchase.
* **Comments Section2**: Share any additional comments or feedback.
* **Signature2**: Add a digital signature, if required.

Note: Fields with similar names (e.g., contact_info, contact_info2) are identical fields with slight variations. The purpose of these fields is to gather multiple instances of the same type of information. 

This guide assumes that some fields are optional and not required. If a field is required, it will be marked accordingly. 

Please follow the instructions carefully to ensure accurate and complete feedback. Thank you for taking the time to help us improve our products and services! 

Note: This guide does not aim to explain every possible scenario or edge case. Please use your best judgment when completing the form.
