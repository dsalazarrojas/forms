# Advertising Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Advertising Order Form is used to place orders for advertising services. It gathers essential information for order processing, billing, and future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the required information ready.
2. Enter your client's name in the "Client Name" field.
3. Provide your client's organization (if applicable) in the "Organization" field.
4. Enter the email address you'd like to use for order confirmation in the "Contact Email" field.
5. Briefly describe the product or service to be promoted in the "Product or Service to Promote" field.
6. Select the ad creative format you'd like to use from the "Ad Format" dropdown list.
7. Specify the number of weeks you'd like the ad to run in the "Run Duration weeks" field.
8. Enter the estimated total cost for the ad in USD in the "Total Cost Estimate" field.
9. (Optional) Enter your billing contact name and email address in the "Billing Contact Name" and "Billing Email" fields respectively.
10. Confirm your acceptance of the advertising terms and conditions by selecting "I Agree" in the "Terms and Conditions Acknowledgement" field.
