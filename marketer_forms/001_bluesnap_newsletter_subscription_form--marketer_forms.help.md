# BlueSnap Newsletter Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The BlueSnap Newsletter Subscription Form is designed to collect essential information from subscribers to customize and send relevant content tailored to their interests. By filling out this form, you will be able to choose your preferred subscription format, duration, and frequency of news updates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your **First Name** in the First Name field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your **Email Address** where you will receive our newsletters.
4. Choose your preferred **Subscription Format** (Print Only, Digital Only, or Both Print and Digital).
5. Select your desired **Subscription Duration** (3 months, 6 months, 12 months, or Indefinite).
6. Choose areas of interest that resonate with you (Select all applicable options: Finance, Marketing, Technology, Business Strategy, Product Updates).
7. Indicate how often you would like to receive newsletters (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, Quarterly).
8. If you wish to include your **Phone Number** for additional contact (this is optional).
9. Confirm your **Industry** (this field is optional).
10. Accept or decline to receive **marketing communications**.
11. Finally, confirm that you **agree to the terms and conditions**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name for our records.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name for our records.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email where you will receive our newsletters.
* **Company Name**: This is an optional field for identifying your company (if applicable).
* **Preferred Subscription Format**: Choose how you would like to receive our newsletters, whether in print, digital, or both.
* **Subscription Duration**: Select how long you would like to subscribe (3 months, 6 months, 12 months, or indefinite).
* **Areas of Interest**: Select all areas that interest you, such as Finance, Marketing, Technology, etc.
* **Newsletter Frequency**: Choose how often you would like to receive our newsletters.
* **Industry**: If you would like to include your industry for our records (this is optional).
* **Phone Number**: This is optional and can be used for additional contact (if applicable).
* **I agree to receive marketing communications**: Check "Yes, I consent" to receive further marketing communications from us.
* **I accept the terms and conditions**: Confirm that you agree to our terms of service.
