# Contact Form Notepad Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose

This contact form is designed to collect information from users who want to get in touch with us for various reasons, such as general inquiries, product information, or partnership opportunities. The form is meant to be a simple and easy-to-use interface for sending messages to our team.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the form fields to focus on each section.
2. Read the hints provided for each field to understand what information is required.
3. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
4. Select the relevant options for each field as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome - Leave Us a Message**: A brief welcome message to let us know that you want to get in touch with us.
* **Your Name**: Please enter your full name as we would like to know who is contacting us.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address so we can get back to you.
* **Phone Number**: If you would like to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number. (Optional)
* **Company Name**: If you are representing a company, please enter your company name.
* **Subject**: Briefly describe the purpose of your inquiry or message.
* **Type of Inquiry**: Select the category that best fits your inquiry or message.
* **Your Message**: Write a detailed message explaining your inquiry or question.
* **Urgency Level**: If you need a response urgently, please select the relevant level of urgency.
* **Preferred Contact Time**: If you have a preferred time for us to contact you, please select it.
* **Newsletter Signup**: If you would like to receive updates and news from us, please select "Yes Please".
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: If you are a referral source, please select how you heard about us.
