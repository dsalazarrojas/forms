# Marketing Brief Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This template is designed to help you create a comprehensive marketing brief for your campaign. It includes the following sections:

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Campaign Name**: Enter a brief description of your campaign, including its name and objectives. This will serve as the title of your campaign document.
2. **Target Audience**: Identify the demographic, psychographic, and behavioral characteristics of your target audience. This information will help you tailor your marketing strategies and messages.
3. **Marketing Channels**: Select all the marketing channels you plan to use for your campaign, such as social media, email, or print media.
4. **Marketing Objectives**: Describe your campaign's specific objectives, including the desired outcomes and metrics for success.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Campaign Name** (`campaign_name`, `text`, required): Enter a clear and concise title for your campaign.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, `text`, required): Describe your target audience, including demographics, interests, and behaviors.
* **Marketing Channels** (`marketing_channels`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the marketing channels you plan to use for your campaign.
* **Marketing Objectives** (`marketing_objectives`, `text`, required): Describe your campaign's specific objectives, including desired outcomes and metrics for success.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and update your campaign brief regularly to reflect changes in your campaign strategy and progress.
* Use this template as a starting point and adapt it to your specific needs.
* Consider sharing your campaign brief with your team to ensure everyone is on the same page and working towards the same goals.
