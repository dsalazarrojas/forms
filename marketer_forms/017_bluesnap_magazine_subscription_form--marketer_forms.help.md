# BlueSnap Magazine Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for subscribers to provide their information for a magazine subscription. Please follow these steps to fill out the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these instructions carefully:

1. Provide your subscriber information, including your full name.
2. Enter your email address for delivery notifications and digital access.
3. Fill out the subscription length, choosing from 6 months, 12 months, or 24 months.
4. Select your format preference, either Print, Digital, or Both.
5. Choose your preferred payment method: Credit card, PayPal, or Bank transfer.
6. Indicate if this is a gift subscription by selecting True or False.
7. If it's a gift subscription, fill out the recipient's details.
8. Review and submit your form carefully to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Subscriber name**: This field is required and asks for your full name for the subscription.
  • Enter your name as you would like it to appear on the subscription.
- **Email address**: This field is required and is for delivery notifications and digital access.
  • Use the email address you want us to use for communication and content delivery.
- **Mailing address**: This field is optional and is for print delivery.
  • Enter your full mailing address for print subscription, if desired.
- **Subscription length**: This field is required and asks for the desired subscription period.
  • Choose from 6 months, 12 months, or 24 months for your subscription duration.
- **Format preference**: This field is required and asks for your preferred format.
  • Select Print, Digital, or Both for your format preference.
- **Payment method**: This field is required and asks for your preferred payment option.
  • Choose from Credit card, PayPal, or Bank transfer for payment.
- **Is this a gift subscription**: This field is required and asks if this is a gift subscription.
  • Select True if it's a gift subscription, False otherwise.
- **Recipient details**: This field is optional and is for filling out the recipient's details if it's a gift subscription.
  • Fill out the recipient's name, address, and any other relevant information if it's a gift subscription.

## Tips
- Be accurate and truthful when filling out your information.
- Review your form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the explanations above for clarification.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact us for assistance.
