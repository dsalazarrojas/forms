# Square Newsletter Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers interested in subscribing to the Square Newsletter. The goal is to collect their information and subscription preferences for marketing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Provide your name, email, and subscription preferences.
- Choose the subscription formats you are interested in (Print or Digital).
- Select the subscription duration (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
- Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field if needed.
- Confirm your subscription type (Human or Robot).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional):
  This field is for your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional):
  This field is for your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
  Enter your valid email address.
- **Confirm Email** (`email_confirmation`, email, optional):
  Re-enter your email address to confirm.
- **Subscription Type** (`subscription_type`, select_multiple, required):
  Select one or more subscription formats: Print or Digital.
- **Print Format** (`print_format`, select_one, required):
  Choose the print format: A4 or A5.
- **Digital Format** (`digital_format`, select_one, required):
  Select the digital format: PDF or Email.
- **Subscription Duration** (`subscription_duration`, select_one, required):
  Choose the subscription duration: Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional):
  Add any additional notes or comments about your subscription.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional):
  Enter your phone number (not required for subscription, but useful for future communication).
- **Confirm Subscription** (`confirm_subscription`, select_one, required):
  Confirm your subscription type: Human or Robot. 

**Tips:**
- Please ensure the provided email is correct and valid.
- Review your choices carefully before submitting the form.
