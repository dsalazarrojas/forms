# Customer Retention Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experiences with our company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields. Fields marked as "optional" are not mandatory, but filling them out can provide more detailed information about your experience.
2. Fill out the "Contact Info" field with your contact details if you wish to be contacted for further discussion.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field so that we can contact you directly.
4. Fill out your phone number in the "Phone Number" field if you wish to be contacted over phone.
5. For "customer_satisfaction", please select how satisfied you are with our company using the options provided.
6. For "Satisfaction 5", select all the options that describe your overall satisfaction.
7. For "questions_1", "questions_2", "questions_3", "questions_4", and "questions_5", please enter your open-ended feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info (id: 2)**: A text field where you can enter your name, phone number, or any other relevant contact information.
* **email (id: 3)**: A field where you enter your email address so we can contact you directly.
* **Phone Number (id: 4)**: A field where you enter your phone number for further discussion.
* **customer_satisfaction (id: 5)**: A dropdown menu where you select how satisfied you are with our company.
* **Satisfaction 5 (id: 6)**: A checkbox list where you select all options that describe your overall satisfaction.
* **questions_1 (id: 7)**: A text field for your feedback on the first question.
* **questions_2 (id: 8)**: A text field for your feedback on the second question.
* **questions_3 (id: 9)**: A text field for your feedback on the third question.
* **questions_4 (id: 10)**: A text field for your feedback on the fourth question.
* **questions_5 (id: 11)**: A text field for your feedback on the fifth question.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly, so we can understand your thoughts better.
- Fill out all fields carefully, as this will help us to provide better services.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us directly.
