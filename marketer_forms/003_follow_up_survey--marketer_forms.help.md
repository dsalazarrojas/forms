# follow_up_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The follow-up survey is a tool used to collect customer feedback and ratings on a specific topic or event.

## How To Complete This Form
- To fill out this form, please navigate through each page (customer_feedback, rating, etc.) and answer the questions accordingly.
- Each page will have a unique set of questions tailored to the specific topic or event.
- Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
