# Marketing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Consent Form is used to collect contact information from customers or partners to send marketing materials, such as emails, phone calls, and SMS messages, to their company. This form helps to ensure that marketing consent is obtained before sending out any promotional materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Marketing Consent Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your email address in the "Email" field if you wish to receive marketing communications from us.
2. Enter your company name in the "Company Name" field.
3. Select the permissions you wish to grant for marketing communications:
	* "Permission to email"
	* "Permission to phone"
	* "Permission to SMS"
	* "Permission to call"
4. You can choose whether to allow or block each type of marketing communication separately.
5. If you want to add a phone number for the company, you can enter it in the "Company Phone Number" field.
6. Finally, enter your company's physical address in the "Company Address" field if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Email** (email, required: false): Enter your personal email address to receive marketing emails from us.
*   **Company Name** (text, required: false): Enter your company's name as it appears on your business cards or marketing materials.
*   **Permission to email** (select_one, required: false): Choose "True" to grant permission to send marketing emails to your company.
*   **Permission to phone** (select_one, required: false): Choose "True" to grant permission to call your company.
*   **Permission to SMS** (select_one, required: false): Choose "True" to grant permission to send marketing SMS messages to your company.
*   **Permission to call** (select_one, required: false): Choose "True" to grant permission to call your company.
*   **Company Phone Number** (number, required: false): If you have a phone number for your company, you can enter it here.
*   **Company Address** (text, required: false): Enter your company's physical address if you wish. This field is optional but helpful for sending physical materials or for company visits.
