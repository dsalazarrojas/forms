# Sunglasses Recommendation Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you find your perfect sunglass match by asking a series of questions about your style, face shape, and preferences. The information you provide will be used to give you a customized recommendation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly about your current style, face shape, and preferences.
2. Select your preferred method of receiving your recommended sunglass type.
3. Choose your preferred color.
4. Select your preferred price range.
5. Choose your preferred frame color.
6. Select your preferred sunglass type.
7. Provide any additional next steps or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intro** (`intro`, `text`, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the form.
* **What is your current glasses style?** (`what_is_your_current_glasses_style`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the style that best describes your current glasses style.
	+ Aviator: A classic style with a rounded lens and a thin bridge.
	+ Cat-Eye: A style with a distinctive cat-eye shape at the outer edge.
	+ Oversized: A bold and oversized frame.
	+ Round: A classic, round frame.
	+ Teashade: A style with a teardrop-shaped lens.
* **What is your face shape?** (`what_is_your_face_shape`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select one or more of the following that best describes your face shape.
	+ Oval: A face that is longer than it is wide.
	+ Heart: A face that is wider at the temples.
	+ Square: A face that is roughly the same width and length.
	+ Triangular: A face that is wider at the jawline.
	+ Diamond: A face that is pointed at the chin.
* **How would you prefer to receive your sunglasses?** (`how_would_you_prefer_to_receive_your_sunglasses`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select one or more of the following methods you would prefer to receive your recommended sunglasses.
	+ In-person: In-store pickup or in-person delivery.
	+ Mail: Mail delivery.
	+ Online: Online purchase and delivery.
	+ In-store: In-store pickup or in-store delivery.
* **Preferred color** (`preferred_color`, `text`, required: false): This is where you can enter a preferred color for your sunglasses.
* **Preferred price range** (`preferred_price_range`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select a price range that you are comfortable with.
	+ Less than $100: A budget-friendly option.
	+ $100 - $150: A mid-range option.
	+ $151 - $200: A slightly higher range.
	+ $201 - $250: A higher-end option.
	+ More than $250: A premium option.
* **Preferred frame color** (`preferred_frame_color`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select a frame color that you like.
	+ Black: A classic, sophisticated choice.
	+ Gray: A neutral, versatile option.
	+ Brown: A warm, earthy choice.
	+ Blue: A cool, calm choice.
	+ Red: A bold, statement choice.
* **Recommended sunglass type** (`recommended_sunglasses_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select a sunglass type that you are interested in.
	+ Aviator: A classic style with a rounded lens and a thin bridge.
	+ Round: A classic, round frame.
	+ Oversized: A bold and oversized frame.
	+ Teashade: A style with a teardrop-shaped lens.
	+ Cat-Eye: A style with a distinctive cat-eye shape at the outer edge.
	+ Square: A frame with a square shape.
	+ Triangular: A frame with a triangular shape.
	+ Diamond: A frame with a diamond shape.
* **Next steps** (`next_steps`, `text`, required: false): If you have any additional comments or next steps, please provide them here.
* **Form submission** (`form_submission`, `text`, required: false): This is where you can enter any other relevant comments or next steps. 

## Tips

* Please be as honest as possible with your answers to get an accurate recommendation.
* If you have any additional comments or next steps, please provide them in the "Next steps" field.
* The recommended sunglass type will be based on your answers to the questions above.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, you can select "I'm not sure" or "Not applicable" to move forward with the process.
