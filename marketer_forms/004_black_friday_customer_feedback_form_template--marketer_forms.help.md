# Black Friday Customer Feedback Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback on your Black Friday shopping experience with us. Your input will help us improve our services and provide better experiences for future customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name and email address (if you want us to contact you for follow-up).
2.  Select the date you shopped with us.
3.  Choose where you shopped with us (online store, physical store, etc.).
4.  Rate your overall satisfaction with your shopping experience.
5.  Rate the ease of use of our website.
6.  Rate your experience with the checkout process.
7.  Let us know if the products you wanted were available.
8.  Rate your satisfaction with the discounts offered.
9.  Rate the speed of shipping.
10. Rate the quality of our customer service.
11. Indicate how likely you are to recommend us to friends.
12. Let us know if you plan to shop with us again.
13. (Optional) Share what you enjoyed most about your experience.
14. (Optional) Share any areas where we could improve.
15. (Optional) Provide any additional comments you'd like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, Text, Optional): Your name, so we can contact you for follow-up if needed.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, Email, Optional): Your email address, so we can send you a thank you discount.
* **Shopping Date** (`shopping_date`, Date, Required): The date you shopped with us.
* **Shopping Location** (`shopping_location`, Select One, Required): Where you shopped with us (online store, physical store, etc.).
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, Select One, Required): Rate your overall satisfaction with your shopping experience.
* **Website Ease of Use** (`website_ease_of_use`, Select One, Optional): Rate the ease of use of our website.
* **Checkout Experience** (`checkout_experience`, Select One, Required): Rate your experience with the checkout process.
* **Product Availability** (`product_availability`, Select One, Required): Let us know if the products you wanted were available.
* **Deal Satisfaction** (`deal_satisfaction`, Select One, Required): Rate your satisfaction with the discounts offered.
* **Shipping Speed** (`shipping_speed`, Select One, Optional): Rate the speed of shipping.
* **Customer Service Rating** (`customer_service_rating`, Select One, Optional): Rate the quality of our customer service.
* **Likelihood to Recommend** (`likelihood_to_recommend`, Select One, Required): Indicate how likely you are to recommend us to friends.
* **Will You Shop Again** (`will_shop_again`, Select One, Required): Let us know if you plan to shop with us again.
* **Best Part of Experience** (`best_part`, Text, Optional): Share what you enjoyed most about your experience.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, Text, Optional): Share any areas where we could improve.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_feedback`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share with us.

Please take a few minutes to fill out this form and help us improve your Black Friday shopping experience. Your feedback is valuable to us!
