# Black Friday Early Access Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Black Friday Early Access Signup Form is designed to gather information from customers interested in getting early access to exclusive deals on Black Friday. This form helps us understand your preferences and tailor-made offers to your interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email Address to receive early access codes and promotional communications.
3. Select your Customer Type: New Customer, Returning Customer, or VIP Member.
4. Choose the categories of products you are interested in (Electronics and Tech, Fashion and Accessories, etc.).
5. Select your preferred Shopping Time (Early Morning, Morning, Afternoon, etc.).
6. Choose how you would like to be notified (Email Only, SMS Only, Both).
7. Estimate how much you plan to spend (Under $50, $50-$150, etc.).
8. If you were referred by a friend, enter their Referral Code.
9. Choose your Birthday Month for special birthday offers.
10. Agree to our Terms and Conditions.
11. Specify how you heard about us (Social Media, Email Campaign, etc.).
12. Share any special requests or products you are interested in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Enter the email address where you want to receive early access codes and promotional communications.
* **Customer Type** (`customer_type`, `select_one`, **required**): Select New Customer, Returning Customer, or VIP Member.
* **Shopping Preferences** (`shopping_preferences`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Choose the categories of products you are interested in.
* **Preferred Shopping Time** (`preferred_shopping_time`, `select_one`, **optional**): Select your preferred shopping time.
* **Notification Preference** (`notification_preference`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose how you would like to be notified (Email Only, SMS Only, Both).
* **Estimated Spending** (`spend_estimate`, `select_one`, **optional**): Estimate how much you plan to spend.
* **Referral Code** (`referral_code`, `text`, **optional**): Enter any referral code if you were referred by a friend.
* **Birthday Month** (`birthday_month`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose your birthday month for special birthday offers.
* **Agree to Terms** (`agree_to_terms`, `select_one`, **required**): Agree to receive promotional communications.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_did_you_hear`, `select_one`, **optional**): Specify how you heard about us (Social Media, Email Campaign, etc.).
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, **optional**): Share any special requests or products you are interested in.
