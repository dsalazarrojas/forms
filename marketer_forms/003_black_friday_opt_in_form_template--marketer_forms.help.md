# Black Friday Opt In Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Black Friday Opt In Form Template is designed to collect user information for marketing purposes, specifically for exclusive early access to deals and offers. This form is intended for users to join the Black Friday list and receive relevant marketing emails.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Join Our Black Friday List --" label to access the form.
2. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective text fields.
3. Provide your Email Address, which will be used to send you exclusive Black Friday offers.
4. Confirm your Email Address to ensure its accuracy.
5. Optionally, provide your Phone Number for SMS notifications about deals.
6. Select the products that interest you most to receive targeted promotions.
7. Choose your preferred discount range for future offers.
8. Choose how you would like to receive updates (Email, SMS, or Push notifications).
9. Confirm that you consent to receive marketing emails about Black Friday deals.
10. Confirm that you have read and accept the privacy policy.
11. Select the date you subscribed to the Black Friday list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter the email address where you will receive exclusive Black Friday offers.
* **Confirm Email Address** (`confirm_email`, email, required): Confirm the email address you entered to ensure its accuracy.
* **Phone Number (optional)** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to receive SMS notifications about deals.
* **What products interest you most?** (`shopping_interests`, select_multiple, required): Select all the products that interest you most to receive targeted promotions.
* **Preferred discount range** (`discount_preference`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred discount range for future offers.
* **How would you like to receive updates?** (`communication_preference`, select_multiple, required): Choose how you would like to receive updates (Email, SMS, or Push notifications).
* **I consent to receive marketing emails about Black Friday deals** (`opt_in_consent`, select_one, required): Confirm that you consent to receive marketing emails about Black Friday deals.
* **I have read and accept the privacy policy** (`privacy_accepted`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and accept the privacy policy.
* **Subscription Date** (`subscriber_since`, date, required): Select the date you subscribed to the Black Friday list.

## Tips

- Ensure that you enter the correct email address to receive marketing emails.
- Choose the products that interest you most to receive targeted promotions.
- Select the preferred discount range that suits your needs.
- Confirm your consent to receive marketing emails about Black Friday deals.
- Confirm that you have read and accept the privacy policy.
