# digital-marketing-manager-job-application-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals interested in applying for a Digital Marketing Manager role within our organization. Please provide accurate and detailed information to increase your chances of being considered for the position.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have a suitable device and internet connection to fill out the form comfortably.
2. Carefully review each field to provide accurate and relevant information.
3. Fill out the form completely and honestly, as incomplete or inaccurate submissions may lead to delayed processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, not required): Enter your first name, as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, not required): Enter your last name, as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Provide a valid email address for communication purposes. This email will be used to send you updates and notifications related to your application.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you for further information.
* **Job Role** (`job_role`, select_one, not required):
	+ Select "Yes" if you are willing to take on a job role as a Digital Marketing Manager.
	+ Select "No" if you are not interested in this role.
* **Job Position** (`job_position`, text, not required): If you have previous experience in a similar position, describe it here.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, not required): Share your relevant work experience in the digital marketing field.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, not required): If applicable, provide the name of the company where you had a previous digital marketing experience.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, not required): If you have a specific job title or role you are interested in, mention it here.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, not required): Briefly describe the job responsibilities and expectations you have for this position.
* **Job Salary** (`job_salary`, number, not required): If you have a specific salary expectation, provide it here.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, not required): Attach your resume for further review and assessment.

Tips:

* Ensure you attach a valid resume to support your application.
* Use clear and concise language in your field answers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to our recruitment team for assistance.
