# PayPal Newsletter Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PayPal Newsletter Subscription Form is designed to collect information from users who want to subscribe to a PayPal newsletter. By filling out this form, users can choose their subscription preferences, including their name, email address, subscription format (digital or print), subscription duration, and subscription start date. This information will be used to tailor the newsletter content and send relevant updates to the subscriber.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your First Name
2. Fill in your Last Name
3. Enter a valid email address for Email
4. Confirm your email address for Email Verification
5. Choose your Subscription Format (Digital, Print, or Both)
6. Select your Subscription Duration (Month, Quarter, Half Year, or Year)
7. Choose your Subscription Start Date

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please fill in your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please fill in your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you'd like to receive newsletters.
* **Confirm Email** (`email_verification`, email, required): Confirm the email address entered earlier to prevent any errors.
* **Subscription Format** (`subscription_format`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you'd like to receive the newsletter (Digital, Print, or Both).
* **Subscription Duration** (`subscription_duration`, select_one, optional): Select how long you'd like to be subscribed to the newsletter (Month, Quarter, Half Year, or Year).
* **Subscription Start Date** (`subscription_start_date`, date, optional): Choose the date when you'd like to start receiving the newsletter.

## Tips

* Be sure to double-check your email address for accuracy to avoid any subscription errors.
* If you have any issues with the form, feel free to contact us for assistance.
