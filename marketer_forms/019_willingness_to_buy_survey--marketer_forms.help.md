# Willingness To Buy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help us better understand potential customers' willingness to buy a product or service. It's a crucial tool for our sales and marketing teams to gather valuable insights on customer's awareness, interest, and purchase intent.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction carefully.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for awareness of our product or service.
3. Choose the correct interest level from the options provided.
4. Enter your preferred email address (if you'd like to be contacted).
5. Provide your phone number (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Awareness** (Yes/No): This question helps us understand if you have some knowledge about our product or service. Choose "Yes" if you're familiar with it, and "No" if you're not.
* **Interest** (Select One): This question gauges your level of interest in our product or service. Choose "None" if you're not interested, or "Other" if you have specific reasons why you're interested or not.
* **Preferences** (Text): Here, you can share any specific preferences or requirements you may have for our product or service. Be as detailed as possible so we can better understand your needs.
* **Purchase Intent** (Select One): This question helps us understand if you plan to buy our product or service in the near future. Choose "-"report - " if you're waiting for a report before making a purchase, or "Other" if you have specific reasons why you're waiting or haven't purchased yet.
* **Email Address** (Email): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted by us for follow-up or additional information. This is optional.
* **Phone Number** (Text): Provide your phone number so we can contact you if needed. This is required for internal purposes, but not necessarily for follow-up communication.

## Tips

* Answer honestly and as briefly as possible.
* You may skip the email and phone fields if you don't want to be contacted.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.
