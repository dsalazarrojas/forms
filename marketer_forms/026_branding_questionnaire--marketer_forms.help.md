# Branding Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This questionnaire is a crucial step in the branding process, designed to help us understand your business needs, preferences, and goals. The information gathered here will be used to create a brand identity that accurately reflects your company and resonates with your target audience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question thoroughly and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Use the provided examples or suggestions to guide your responses.
3. Don't worry if you're not sure about a particular question - we'll work together to clarify any doubts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): This is the official name of your business. Please provide the name you use for business operations and communication.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): This is the name of the primary contact person for your business. This could be a person's name or a title (e.g., CEO, Marketing Manager).
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): This is the email address we'll use to communicate with you about your brand project.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): This is the phone number we can use to contact you about your project.
* **How Long in Business** (`business_age`, select_one, required): Choose the number of years your business has been operational to help us understand your business maturity.
	+ Less than 1 year
	+ 1-3 years
	+ 4-10 years
	+ 10+ years
* **Business Summary** (`business_summary`, text, required): Describe your business in a few sentences, highlighting what it does and what makes it unique.
* **Products or Services** (`products_services`, text, required): List the products or services your business offers.
* **Target Customer** (`target_customer`, text, required): Describe your ideal customer, including their demographics, needs, or pain points.
* **Customer Pain Points** (`customer_pain_points`, text, required): Identify the problems or challenges your customers face.
* **Unique Selling Proposition** (`unique_selling_proposition`, text, required): Clearly articulate what sets your business apart from the competition.
* **Main Competitors** (`main_competitors`, text, required): Name a few competitors in your industry.
* **Competitor Advantages** (`competitor_advantages`, text, required): Explain what advantages your competitors have that you'd like us to avoid in your brand identity.
* **Brand Keywords** (`brand_keywords`, select_multiple, required): Choose 3-5 words that best describe your brand's personality or values.
	+ Innovative
	+ Reliable
	+ Affordable
	+ Premium
	+ Friendly
	+ Professional
	+ Creative
	+ Trustworthy
	+ Bold
	+ Sustainable
* **Brand Voice** (`brand_voice`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like your brand to sound:
	+ Casual and Friendly
	+ Professional and Formal
	+ Witty and Fun
	+ Serious and Authoritative
	+ Inspirational
* **Existing Brand Assets** (`existing_brand_assets`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have any existing logos or color schemes you'd like to incorporate or avoid in the branding process.
	+ None
	+ Logo Only
	+ Colors Only
	+ Full Brand System
* **Logo Preferences** (`logo_preferences`, select_one, optional): Choose a style for your logo:
	+ Text Based
	+ Icon Based
	+ Combination
	+ Abstract
	+ Minimalist
* **Color Preferences** (`color_preferences`, text, optional): If you have a preferred color palette or colors, describe them here.
* **Colors to Avoid** (`colors_to_avoid`, text, optional): List any colors you'd prefer to avoid.
* **Where Will Brand Be Used** (`brand_applications`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the places where you'd like your brand identity to be applied.
	+ Website
	+ Social Media
	+ Business Cards
	+ Signage
	+ Packaging
	+ Advertising
	+ Merchandise
	+ Print Materials
* **Designs You Like** (`design_likes`, text, optional): If you have any brand designs that you particularly like or admire, describe them here.
* **Designs You Dislike** (`design_dislikes`, text, optional): If you have any brand designs that you dislike or would like to avoid, describe them here.
* **Project Timeline** (`project_timeline`, text, required): Provide an estimated timeframe for when you'd like this project to be completed.
* **Budget Expectations** (`budget_expectations`, text, required): Describe your estimated budget range for this project.

Please answer these questions to the best of your ability, and we'll work together to create a brand identity that accurately represents your business and resonates with your target audience.
