# free 5 day trial form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a critical tool for marketers to create and manage forms, specifically for the purpose of offering free 5 day trials. By completing this form, users can quickly and efficiently gather necessary information about a form, including its name, description, category, URL, status, assigned tool, and other relevant details. This helps ensure that forms are accurately and comprehensively listed in the database.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Form Details** section, which includes the **Form Name**, **Form Description**, and **Form Category**.
2. Enter the **Form URL**, which is the address of the form where it can be accessed.
3. Choose the correct **Form Status** (active or inactive).
4. Select the **Form Assigned Tool** from the available options.
5. Enter the **Form ID**, which is a unique identifier for the form.
6. Enter the **Form Last Modified** date and time, which represents when the form was last updated.
7. Enter the **Form Last Modified By**, which is the name of the person who last updated the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Details** (Form Name, text, required): This field is where you enter a brief title or name for the form.
- **Form Name** (text, optional): This field is for providing more detailed information about the form, but it's not required.
- **Form Description** (text, required): Enter a short description of the form to help others understand its purpose.
- **Form Category** (select one, required): Choose the category for the form from the available options (Marketer Forms, General Forms, User Forms).
- **Form URL** (text, required): Enter the URL where the form can be accessed.
- **Form ID** (number, required): Enter a unique identifier for the form.
- **Form Status** (select one, required): Choose the status of the form as active or inactive.
- **Form Assigned Tool** (select one, required): Select the tool that the form is assigned to from the available options.
- **Form Comments** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the form, if necessary.
- **Form Last Modified** (date, required): Enter the date and time when the form was last updated.
- **Form Last Modified By** (text, required): Enter the name of the person who last updated the form.
