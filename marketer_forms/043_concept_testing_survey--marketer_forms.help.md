# Concept Testing Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This survey is designed to gather feedback on a new concept and gauge its effectiveness in resonating with potential customers. The goal is to help you understand how well the concept appeals to your target audience and how it compares to existing products or services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully, providing accurate and honest answers to the questions.
2. Make sure to read each question carefully before answering.
3. Use the options provided for each question to guide your responses.
4. For questions that ask for free-text answers, provide a brief but descriptive response.
5. If you're unsure about a question or need more information, feel free to contact us at <form@gic.mx>.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey ID**: A unique identifier for the survey.
* **Survey Date**: The date the survey was completed.
* **Respondent Name**: Your name, for demographic tracking purposes (optional).
* **Email Address**: Your email address for follow-up communication (optional).
* **Age Range**: Select your age range to help us understand our target audience's demographics.
* **Gender**: Select your gender to provide insights into our audience's perspectives.
* **Product Category**: Select the category that best describes the product or service being offered.
* **Concept Name**: The name of the concept being tested.
* **Concept Description**: A brief description of the concept, which will help you understand the context.
* **Initial Reaction**: Rate your initial reaction to the concept using the provided scale.
* **Appeal Rating**: Rate how appealing you find the concept.
* **Uniqueness Rating**: Rate how unique you find the concept.
* **Relevance Rating**: Rate how relevant the concept is to your needs.
* **Believability Rating**: Rate how believable the concept is.
* **Purchase Intent**: Indicate your likelihood of purchasing the concept.
* **Price Expectation**: If applicable, provide your expected price range for the concept.
* **Value for Money**: Rate the perceived value of the concept.
* **Liked Aspects**: Describe what you liked most about the concept.
* **Disliked Aspects**: Describe what you liked least about the concept.
* **Improvement Suggestions**: Suggest ways to improve the concept.
* **Feature Requests**: Suggest features you would like to see added to the concept.
* **Comparison to Competitors**: Compare the concept to similar products or services you're familiar with.
* **Recommendation Likelihood**: Indicate how likely you are to recommend the concept.
* **Overall Rating**: Rate the concept overall using the provided scale.

Note: This help guide is a non-exhaustive explanation of the form, and it's recommended to read each question carefully and ask if you're unsure about any part of the form.
