# Contact Form Treehouse Theme - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from potential clients or customers interested in the services offered by the company. It serves as a starting point for the business development process.

## How To Complete This Form

To get in touch with us, please fill out the following form with your contact information and project details. Ensure that you complete all required fields for a proper response from our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome - Let Us Hear From You** (`welcome`, `note`, required: false): This is an introductory note to guide you through the form.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: true): Enter your first name to help us address you properly.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: true): Enter your last name to help us address you properly.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required: true): Provide your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact number if you prefer to be contacted via phone.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required: false): If you are representing a company, please enter your organization name.
* **Area of Interest** (`interest_area`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the area where you'd like our services:
	+ Marketing Services
	+ Digital Strategy
	+ Content Creation
	+ Social Media
	+ Analytics
	+ Consulting
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the type of project you are interested in:
	+ New Project
	+ Existing Project Enhancement
	+ Ongoing Support
	+ Consultation
	+ Not Sure Yet
* **Project Timeline** (`timeline`, `select_one`, required: true): When would you like to start the project:
	+ Immediately
	+ Within 2 Weeks
	+ Within a Month
	+ Within 3 Months
	+ Just Browsing
* **Budget Range** (`budget`, `select_one`, required: false): If you have an estimated budget for the project:
	+ Under $5000
	+ $5000 to $15000
	+ $15000 to $50000
	+ $50000 to $100000
	+ Over $100000
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, `text`, required: true): Describe your project in detail, so we can better understand your needs.
* **Project Goals** (`goals`, `text`, required: false): Share your project goals with us, so we can help you achieve them.
* **Communication Preference** (`communication_preference`, `select_one`, required: true): How would you like to be contacted:
	+ Email
	+ Phone Call
	+ Video Meeting
	+ In Person
* **Subscribe to Newsletter** (`subscribe`, `select_one`, required: false): Would you like to subscribe to our newsletter for updates:
	+ True
	+ False

## Tips
Please ensure that you fill out all required fields to help us get started on your project. If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, feel free to contact us via email or phone.
