# virtual_product_demo_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The virtual product demo registration form is used to collect information from users for virtual product demos. This form is a simple, text-based form that gathers essential details from users to facilitate the demo process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your product details in the "Product Details" section.
2.  Provide your customer information in the "Customer Details" section.
3.  Select your appointment preferences in the "Appointment" section.
4.  Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" sections.
5.  Provide details about the demo in the "Demo Details" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Details** (product_details): Enter the title and description of the virtual product you are interested in, or any other relevant details. (text, optional)
* **Customer Details** (customer_details): Provide your name, email, and any other relevant customer information. (text, optional)
* **Appointment** (appointment): Choose a preferred date and time for the demo. If no specific date and time is provided, the default will be used. (text, optional)
* **Contact Info** (contact_info): Enter your contact number and any other relevant contact information. (text, optional)
* **Demo Details** (demo_details): Provide any additional demo details or instructions. (text, optional)
