# Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Signup Form is used to collect information from users for marketing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter a valid Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter a hint for your Email address in the Email Hint field.
5. Confirm your Email address in the Confirm Email field.
6. Enter a Password in the Password field.
7. Enter a hint for your Password in the Password Hint field.
8. Confirm your Password in the Password Confirm field.
9. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
10. Select your country from the Country field.
11. Enter your Company in the Company field.
12. Select whether you accept the terms and conditions in the Accept Terms field.
13. Select whether you accept newsletters in the Accept Newsletters field.
14. Select whether you accept promotions in the Accept Promotions field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Email Hint** (`email_hint`, text, optional): Enter a hint for your email address.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, text, optional): Confirm your email address.
* **Password** (`password`, text, optional): Enter a strong password.
* **Password Hint** (`password_hint`, text, optional): Enter a hint for your password.
* **Confirm Password** (`password_confirm`, text, optional): Confirm your password.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Country** (`country`, select_multiple, optional): Select your country of residence.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Accept Terms** (`agree`, select_one, required): Accept or decline the terms and conditions.
* **Accept Newsletters** (`accept_newsletter`, select_one, required): Accept or decline newsletters.
* **Accept Promotions** (`accept_promotions`, select_one, required): Accept or decline promotions.
