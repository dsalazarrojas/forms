# Refer A Friend Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Refer A Friend Form is a tool to help you refer your friends to our company. By filling out this form, you will be entering your own information and that of your friend, which will help us follow up with you both to discuss potential business opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. On the first page, enter your name and contact information.
3. On the second page, enter your friend's name and contact information.
4. Click on the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referrer Name (<type:text>, required: true)**: Enter the name of the person referrring their friend (that's you!).
* **Referrer Email (<type:text, required: false)**: You can enter your contact email if you want to hear from us directly, but it's not required. 
* **Referrer Phone (<type:text, required: false)**: You can enter your contact phone number if you want to hear from us directly, but it's not required. 
* **Friend Name (<type:text, required: true)**: Enter the name of the friend you are referring.
* **Friend Email (<type:email, required: false)**: You can enter your friend's contact email if they have one. 
* **Friend Phone (<type:text, required: false)**: You can enter your friend's contact phone number if they have one. 

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date contact information for both you and your friend.
* If you have any issues or concerns while filling out the form, feel free to contact us at [support email].
