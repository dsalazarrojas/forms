# Promotional Contest Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Promotional Contest Registration form is designed to collect information from users who want to participate in a promotional contest. This form is used for entry confirmation and registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
2. If you've already registered for this promotional contest, do not fill out this form again.
3. Ensure that you've provided accurate contact information for entry confirmation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Registration Form**
* This field is for your name. Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* This field is optional, but it will help us confirm your entry.
* **Type: text**
* **Required: no**

### 2. **Entry Form**
* This field is for your entry or answer to the promotional contest. Please provide a complete and accurate answer.
* This field is optional, but it will help us confirm your entry.
* **Type: text**
* **Required: no**

### 3. **Prize Form**
* This field is for selecting the prize you're interested in, if applicable.
* This field is optional, but it will help us tailor the prize to your preferences.
* **Type: text**
* **Required: no**

### 4. **Entry Confirmation**
* This field is for confirming that you've filled out the form correctly and that you're aware of the contest rules.
* This field is optional, but it will help us confirm your entry is accurate.
* **Type: text**
* **Required: no**

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out to avoid any issues with your entry.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
* The information collected will only be used for contest purposes.
