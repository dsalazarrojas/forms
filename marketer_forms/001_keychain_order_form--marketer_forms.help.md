# Keychain Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Keychain Order Form is a tool used to collect information from customers for keychain orders. It helps streamline the order process and reduces manual entry by providing a digital interface for customers to input their details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start by entering the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Next, enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
3. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
4. Select the type of order the customer is placing by selecting one or multiple options in the "Order Type" field.
5. Enter the name of the product being ordered in the "Product Name" field.
6. Specify the quantity of the product being ordered in the "Quantity" field.
7. If the order is for delivery, select the date and time of delivery in the "Delivery Date" and "Delivery Time" fields respectively.
8. Optionally, enter a message for the customer in the "Minimalist Message" field.
9. Choose the size of the keychain from the available options in the "Keychain Size" field.
10. Choose the color of the keychain from the available options in the "Keychain Color" field.
11. If applicable, enter a custom message for the keychain in the "Custom Message" field.
12. If necessary, select the color of the product in the "Product Color" field.
13. Enter any comments or additional information about the order in the "Order Comments" field.
14. Enter the customer's address in the "Customer Address" field.
15. Optionally, select the payment method used for the order in the "Payment Method" field.
16. Finally, review and submit the form to complete the order.
