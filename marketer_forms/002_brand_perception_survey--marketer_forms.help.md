# Brand Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your perception of a brand, helping businesses and organizations understand their reputation and performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the answer that best matches your response to each question. You will be asked about your familiarity with the brand, your overall impression, and your experience with their products or services. Please answer all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Age Range** (respondent_age): Select your age group from the options provided.
- **How Familiar are You with Our Brand** (brand_familiarity): Rate your level of familiarity with our brand from Never heard of it to Extremely familiar.
- **Overall Impression of Our Brand** (brand_impression): Rate your overall impression of our brand from 1 (Very negative) to 5 (Very positive).
- **Which Qualities Best Describe Our Brand** (brand_qualities): Select all qualities that best describe our brand. You can choose multiple options.
- **How Likely Are You to Purchase from Us** (purchase_likelihood): Rate your likelihood to purchase from us from Very unlikely to Very likely.
- **Would You Recommend Our Brand to Others** (recommend_brand): Rate your willingness to recommend our brand to others from Definitely not to Definitely yes.
- **How Do You Perceive Our Pricing** (price_perception): Select your opinion on pricing from Far too expensive to Excellent value.
- **What Do You Think Are Our Brand Strengths** (brand_strengths): List the top strengths you perceive about our brand. (Optional)
- **What Areas Could We Improve** (brand_weaknesses): List areas for improvement. (Optional)
- **How Do We Compare to Our Competitors** (competitor_comparison): Rate our competitiveness from Much worse to Much better.
- **Where Have You Encountered Our Brand** (brand_awareness_channels): Select all channels where you have encountered our brand. (Optional)
- **Have You Purchased Our Products or Services** (product_experience): Select your experience level with our products or services from Never purchased to Long-time customer.
- **How Satisfied Are You with Your Experience** (satisfaction_level): Rate your satisfaction from Very dissatisfied to Very satisfied. (Optional)
- **Do Our Brand Values Align with Yours** (brand_value_alignment): Rate the alignment of our values with yours from Not aligned at all to Completely aligned.
- **Any Additional Comments or Suggestions** (additional_feedback): Share any feedback or suggestions you may have. (Optional)

## Tips
Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability. If you are unsure or have no opinion, select the middle option. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the last field to provide them.
