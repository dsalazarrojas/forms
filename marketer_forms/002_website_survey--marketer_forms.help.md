# Website Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect user feedback and information about your website. It is divided into several pages, each with its own set of fields. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and provide as much information as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the first page, which is the "home page".
2. On the second page, enter your email address in the "contact-page-label" field.
3. On the third page, choose one option from the dropdown list in the "feedback-page-label" field.
4. On the fourth page, select one or multiple options from the "comments-page-label" field.
5. On the fifth page, enter a number in the "rating-range-page-label" field.
6. On the sixth page, select a date from the calendar in the "date-picker-page-label" field.
7. On the seventh page, select a time from the clock in the "time-picker-page-label" field.
8. On the eighth page, enter a note in the "note-page-label" field.
9. On the ninth page, enter your phone number in the "phone-page-label" field.
10. On the tenth page, enter your website URL in the "website-page-label" field.
11. On the eleventh page, enter your email address in the "email-page-label" field.
12. On the twelfth page, click the "submit-form-label" field to submit your form.
13. Finally, you will see a success message on the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **home-page-label** (text, 1, optional): Enter a brief description of your website.
* **contact-page-label** (email, 2, required): Enter your email address.
* **feedback-page-label** (select one, 3, optional): Choose one of the options to provide feedback.
* **comments-page-label** (select multiple, 4, optional): Select one or multiple options to provide more feedback.
* **rating-range-page-label** (number, 5, optional): Enter a rating on a scale of 1-5.
* **date-picker-page-label** (date, 6, optional): Select a date from the calendar.
* **time-picker-page-label** (time, 7, optional): Select a time from the clock.
* **note-page-label** (note, 8, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **phone-page-label** (text, 9, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **website-page-label** (text, 10, optional): Enter your website URL.
* **email-page-label** (email, 11, optional): Enter your email address.
* **submit-form-label** (text, 12, required): Click this button to submit your form.
* **error-message-page-label** (text, 13, non-existent): This field will display any error messages.
* **success-message-page-label** (text, 14, non-existent): This field will display a success message.
* **help-message-page-label** (text, 15, non-existent): This field will display any help messages.
