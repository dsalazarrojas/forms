# Shoes Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Shoes Order Form is used to collect information from customers who want to place a new order for shoes. This guide will help you fill out the form correctly and provide the required details for a smooth order process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully before filling it out to ensure you have all the necessary information required.
2. Select the correct options for each field based on your specific needs.
3. Double-check your entries before submitting the form to avoid any errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Design (Select One)** (`design`, select_one, false): This field allows you to select whether you want the shoes with the current design or without it. If you select "Yes", it means you want the shoes with the current design. If you select "No", it means you want the shoes without the current design.
* **Size** (`size`, number, false): Enter the desired size of the shoes you want to order.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, false): Enter the quantity of shoes you want to order.
* **Further Requests** (`further_requests`, text, false): If you have any further requests or specific requirements for your order, please enter them here.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, false): Enter your name as it appears on your order documents.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, false): Enter your contact email address where we can reach you.
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, false): Enter any specific delivery instructions or requirements you may have.
* **Payment** (`payment`, select_multiple, false): Select the payment option that best suits your needs. Note that this field may not be available for all orders.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, false): Select the payment method you would like to use for this order.
* **Confirm Payment** (`confirm_payment`, select_one, false): Confirm whether you are ready to proceed with the payment.
* **Confirm Payment Method** (`confirm_payment_method`, select_one, false): Confirm whether you are ready to proceed with your selected payment method.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, text, false): Enter any notes or comments about your order that you would like our team to be aware of.

Note: If a field is marked as "false" in the required field, it means it is not required to be filled out, but it's recommended to fill it out for us to better understand your needs.
