# Sales Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential customers about their job title and role, allowing us to tailor our sales approach and conversation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Provide details about your job title and role.
3. Choose the correct industry and location for your company.
4. Select the correct contact method to reach out to you.
5. Enter any additional details that are relevant to your company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sales Lead Generation Form** (`sales_lead_generation_form`): Enter a brief description of why you are interested in our services. This can be a short message explaining your needs.
* **First Name** (`first_name`): Enter your first name, as it appears on your business cards or official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`): Enter your last name, as it appears on your business cards or official documents.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`): Enter your company's name, as it appears on your business cards or official documents.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter your job title, such as CEO, Sales Manager, or Marketing Director.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your work email address, as it appears on your business cards or official documents.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your work phone number, including any extension or dialing codes.
* **Website** (`website`): Enter your company's website URL.
* **Job Title Company** (`job_title_company`): Enter your company's name as it appears on your business cards or official documents.
* **Company Type** (`company_type`): Select the type of company you work for, such as LLC or Corporation.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`): Select the number of employees in your company.
* **Industry** (`industry`): Select the industry you work in, such as Automotive or Finance.
* **Location** (`location`): Select the location of your company, such as California or New York.
* **Job Title Department** (`job_title_department`): Enter the department you work in, such as Marketing or Sales.
* **Job Title Industry** (`job_title_industry`): Select the industries your company operates in, such as Automotive or Finance.
* **Job Title Location** (`job_title_location`): Select the locations your company operates in, such as California or New York.
* **Lead Source** (`lead_source`): Select how you found out about our services, such as through a referral or online search.
* **Job Title Employees** (`job_title_employees`): Enter the number of employees in your company.
* **Job Title Revenue** (`job_title_revenue`): Enter your company's annual revenue.
* **Job Title Monthly Recurring Revenue** (`job_title_monthly_recurring_revenue`): Enter your company's monthly recurring revenue.
* **Job Title Average Sale** (`job_title_average_sale`): Enter your company's average sale value.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`): Select the best way to contact you, such as phone or email.
* **Job Title Department** (`job_title_department`): Enter any additional department information.
* **Job Title Location** (`job_title_location`): Select any additional locations your company operates in.
* **Job Title Industry** (`job_title_industry`): Select any additional industries your company operates in.
