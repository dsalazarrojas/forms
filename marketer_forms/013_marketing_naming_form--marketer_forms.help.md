# marketing_naming_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for collecting marketing-related data for a product or project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Project Name** field with the name of the project/product.
2. Choose the **project_type** from the provided options that best describes the type of project/product.
3. Enter the **start_date** and **end_date** fields with the start and end dates of the project/product.
4. Select the **marketing_channels** fields with the marketing channels used for the project/product (select multiple options).
5. Enter the **target_audience** field with the target audience for the project/product.
6. Select the **target_segment** field with the target segment for the project/product.
7. Enter the **product_line** field with the product line of the project/product.
8. Enter the **marketing_budget** field with the budget allocated for marketing.
9. Enter the **target_location** field with the target location for the project/product.
10. Select the **target_language** field with the target language for the project/product.
11. Enter the **estimated_launch_date** field with the estimated launch date of the project/product.
12. Select the **launch_status** field with the launch status of the project/product.
13. Enter the **launch_channel** field with the launch channel of the project/product.
14. Enter the **launch_date** field with the launch date of the project/product.
15. Enter the **product_name** field with the name of the product.
16. Enter the **product_category** field with the category of the product.
17. Enter the **product_description** field with the description of the product.
18. Enter the **product_price** field with the price of the product.
19. Enter the **product_tags** field with the tags of the product.
20. Enter the **product_image_url** field with the URL of the product image.
21. Enter the **product_video_url** field with the URL of the product video.
22. Enter the **product_url** field with the URL of the product.
23. Select the **product_url_type** field with the type of the product URL.
24. Enter the **assigned_tool** field with the tool assigned for the project/product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the project or product.

* **marketing_budget** (9, number, required): Enter the budget allocated for marketing.
* **target_location** (10, text, required): Enter the target location for the project or product.
* **target_language** (11, select_one, required): Select the target language for the project or product.
* **estimated_launch_date** (12, date, required): Enter the estimated launch date of the project or product.
* **launch_status** (13, select_one, required): Select the launch status of the project or product.
* **launch_channel** (14, text, required): Enter the launch channel of the project or product.
* **launch_date** (15, date, required): Enter the launch date of the project or product.
* **product_name** (16, text, required): Enter the name of the product.
* **product_category** (17, text, required): Enter the category of the product.
* **product_description** (18, text, required): Enter the description of the product.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to ensure accurate data collection.
* Ensure that the form is filled out only once per project or product to avoid duplicate entries.
* If you need help with a specific field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section.
