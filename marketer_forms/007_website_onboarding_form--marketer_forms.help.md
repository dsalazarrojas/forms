# Website Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Website Onboarding Form" is designed to collect information about your company and website for marketing or onboarding purposes. Please fill out this form to help us better understand your business.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company.
2. **Website URL**: Enter the URL of your website.
3. **Email**: Enter a valid email address associated with your company.
4. **Company Size**: Select the size of your company from the options provided.
5. **Company Industry**: Select the industry your company belongs to from the options provided.
6. **Company Type**: Select the type of company you have from the options provided.
7. **Company Description**: Provide a brief description of your company.
8. **Website Domain**: Enter the domain name of your website.
9. **Phone**: Enter your company's phone number.
10. **Address**: Enter your company's address.
11. **Country**: Select the country where your company is located from the options provided.
12. **Region**: Select the region where your company is located from the options provided.
13. **City**: Enter the city where your company is located.
14. **Zip**: Enter the zip code of your company's location.
15. **Website Type**: Select the type of website you have from the options provided.
