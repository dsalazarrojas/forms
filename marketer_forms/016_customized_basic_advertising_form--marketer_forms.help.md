# Customized Basic Advertising Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic information from customers for marketing purposes. It will help us understand your preferences and tailor our marketing efforts to best suit your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Details** with your full name and any other relevant information.
2. Provide details about the ad you are interested in, including any additional preferences.
3. Share your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
4. Indicate your preferences for customer communication.
5. Select your preferred **Marketing Channel** from the dropdown menu.
6. If applicable, choose your **Ad Budget** by selecting one or more options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Customer Details
* **Customer Details** (text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional information about yourself that you think would be helpful for us to know.

### 2. Ad Details
* **Ad Details** (text, optional): Enter any details about the ad you are interested in, including any specific preferences.

### 3. Ad Preferences
* **Ad Preferences** (text, optional): Describe any specific preferences you have for the ad, such as format, location, or other features.

### 4. Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (text, optional): Share your contact information so we can get in touch with you about your marketing interests.

### 5. Customer Preferences
* **Customer Preferences** (text, optional): Indicate your preferences for how you'd like to be contacted, such as email, phone, or mail.

### 6. Ad Preferences 2
* **Ad Preferences 2** (text, optional): If there are any additional ad preferences you'd like to share, enter them here.

### 7. Marketing Channel
* **Marketing Channel** (select_one, optional): Select from the dropdown menu which marketing channel you'd like to use for our campaigns:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3

### 8. Ad Budget
* **Ad Budget** (select_multiple, optional): If applicable, select one or more options to indicate your ad budget:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible in your answers to help us better understand your needs.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, feel free to ask!
