# Product Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer information and preferences to provide personalized product recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your customer name in the field below.
2. Please select whether you are interested in recommended products or not.
3. If you are interested in recommended products, select the products that you prefer from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Product Interest** (`product_interest`, text): Indicate whether or not you are interested in recommended products.
* **Recommended Products** (`recommended_products`, select multiple): If you are interested in recommended products, select as many products as you like from the available options.
* **Preferred Products** (`preferred_products`, select one): Select a single product that you are most interested in, among the options provided. Note that this field is only visible if you are interested in recommended products.
* **Customer Preferences** (`customer_preferences`, text): Enter any additional information or comments about your product preferences.

## Tips
- Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for clarification.
- The information provided will be used to provide personalized product recommendations.
