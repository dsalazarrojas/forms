# share_a_testimonial_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect testimonials from users about a product or service. It allows users to share their experiences and feedback in a public forum.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide a brief description of your experience in the "Testimonial" field.
3. Choose whether you want to publish your testimonial by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Publish My Testimonial" field.
4. Read and agree to the publishing terms by selecting "Yes" or "I'll review the terms" in the "I Accept the Publishing Terms" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, text, required): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on your testimonial.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions or opportunities.
* **Testimonial** (`Testimonial`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your experience with the product or service.
* **Publish My Testimonial** (`Publish My Testimonial`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you want to publish your testimonial publicly.
* **I Accept the Publishing Terms** (`I Accept the Publishing Terms`, select_multiple, required): Read and agree to the publishing terms, selecting "Yes" or "I'll review the terms" depending on your preference.
