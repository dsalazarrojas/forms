# High Heels Market Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from customers about their purchasing habits and preferences regarding high heels. This information will be used to improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the survey.
2. Select the type of high heels you purchased (e.g. High Heels, Flat Shoes, etc.).
3. Choose your preferred shoe size from the options provided.
4. Select your favorite color(s) for high heels.
5. Enter the date you purchased your high heels.
6. Enter the time you purchased your high heels.
7. Choose where you purchased your high heels (e.g. online, in-store, etc.).
8. Enter any additional demographic information you would like to share.
9. Rate your satisfaction with your recent purchase.
10. Indicate how relevant you find our product to your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_feedback (Text)** (`customer_feedback`, text, optional): This field is a space for you to provide any feedback or comments about your recent purchase.
* **shoe_type (Select One)** (`shoe_type`, select_one, optional): This field asks you to select the type of high heels you purchased, such as High Heels, Flat Shoes, etc.
* **shoe_size (Select Multiple)** (`shoe_size`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks you to select your preferred shoe size from the options provided.
* **favorite_color (Select Multiple)** (`favorite_color`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks you to select your favorite color(s) for high heels.
* **when_purchased (Date)** (`when_purchased`, date, optional): This field asks you to enter the date you purchased your high heels.
* **purchase_time (Time)** (`purchase_time`, time, optional): This field asks you to enter the time you purchased your high heels.
* **purchase_channel (Select Multiple)** (`purchase_channel`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks you to choose where you purchased your high heels, such as online, in-store, etc.
* **customer_demographics (Text)** (`customer_demographics`, text, optional): This field is a space for you to provide any additional demographic information you would like to share.
* **customer_satisfaction (Select One)** (`customer_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): This field asks you to rate your satisfaction with your recent purchase.
* **product_relevance (Number)** (`product_relevance`, number, optional): This field asks you to indicate how relevant you find our product to your needs.
* **price_sensitivity (Number)** (`price_sensitivity`, number, optional): This field asks you to rate how sensitive you are to price when making a purchase.

Note: The required fields are marked as "optional" in the form to allow for maximum flexibility and understanding from the customer's perspective.
