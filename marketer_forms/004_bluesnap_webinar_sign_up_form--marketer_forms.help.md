# BlueSnap Webinar Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential attendees for a webinar on digital marketing and related topics. It will help us understand your interests and provide you with relevant content and updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your **Email Address**, which will be used to send you the webinar link and any future updates.
3. If you work for a company, enter your **Company Name**. This is optional, but it helps us understand your background.
4. Enter your **Job Title**, which is also optional.
5. Select your **Industry** from the dropdown menu.
6. Choose your **Company Size** from the dropdown menu.
7. Enter your **Phone Number** for any follow-up calls (optional).
8. Select your **Country** from the dropdown menu.
9. Choose the **Topics of Interest** you'd like to learn about by selecting multiple options.
10. Rate your **Experience Level** with the selected topics.
11. Select how you **Hear About This Webinar** from the dropdown menu.
12. If you have any questions for the presenter, type them in the **Questions for the Presenter** field.
13. Choose whether you agree to receive **Marketing Communications** from us.
14. Select your **Registration Type** (free, premium, or VIP access).
15. Choose your **Payment Method** (if applicable).
16. If you're paying by credit card, enter the **Cardholder Name**.
17. Finally, confirm that you've read and agree to the **Terms and Conditions**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name for registration purposes.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name for registration purposes.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address you'd like to receive the webinar link and updates.
* **Company Name**: If you work for a company, enter its name.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title, if applicable.
* **Industry**: Choose your industry from the dropdown menu.
* **Company Size**: Select your company size from the dropdown menu.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for follow-up calls (optional).
* **Country**: Choose your country from the dropdown menu.
* **Topics of Interest**: Select the topics you're interested in learning about.
* **Experience Level**: Rate your experience level with the selected topics.
* **How Did You Hear About This Webinar?**: Select how you heard about this webinar.
* **Questions for the Presenter**: Type any questions you'd like to ask the presenter.
* **Marketing Consent**: Choose whether you agree to receive marketing communications from us.
* **Registration Type**: Select your registration type (free, premium, or VIP access).
* **Payment Method**: Choose your payment method (if applicable).
* **Cardholder Name**: Enter the name as it appears on your credit card.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Confirm that you've read and agree to the terms and conditions.
