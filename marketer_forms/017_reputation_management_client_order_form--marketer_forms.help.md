# Reputation Management Client Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reputation Management Client Order Form is a tool used by clients to provide information about their orders for reputation management services. This form helps clients submit their orders and preferences for various reputation management tasks such as monitoring online reviews, responding to customer inquiries, and managing reviews on different platforms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information to help us get in touch with you regarding your order.
2. Enter your phone number and email to ensure we can contact you for any further questions or concerns.
3. Enter any special instructions or requests you have for your order.
4. Select the services you need for your reputation management.
5. Choose a review frequency that works for you.
6. Select the date range for which you would like to receive reviews.
7. Choose the status of your order.
8. Select the payment terms that apply to your order.
9. Enter the review frequency for any additional reviews you may need.
10. Once you have filled out all the required and optional fields, click submit to send your order to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Order Form** (`client_order_form`, text, required): This field is where you can enter the title of your order. Please enter a brief and descriptive title for your order.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): This field is where you can enter your contact information, including your name, address, and any other relevant details. This will help us get in touch with you regarding your order.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): This field is where you can enter your phone number, which we may use to contact you for any further questions or concerns.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This field is where you can enter your email address, which we will use to contact you for any updates or concerns.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required): This field is where you can enter any special instructions or requests you have for your order.
* **Services Needed** (`services_needed`, select_multiple, optional): This field is where you can select the services you need for your reputation management, such as monitoring online reviews, responding to customer inquiries, and managing reviews on different platforms.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter any additional instructions or requests you have for your order.
* **Review Frequency** (`review_frequency`, time, optional): This field is where you can select the frequency at which you would like to receive reviews.
* **Date Range** (`date_range`, date, optional): This field is where you can select the date range for which you would like to receive reviews.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, optional): This field is where you can select the status of your order.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, number, optional): This field is where you can select the payment terms that apply to your order.
* **Review Frequency 2** (`review_frequency_2`, time, optional): This field is where you can select the review frequency for any additional reviews you may need.
