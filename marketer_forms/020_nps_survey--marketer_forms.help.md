# NPS Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The NPS survey form is a tool used to measure customer satisfaction and gather feedback on how likely customers are to recommend your company, products, or services. It's a key indicator of your company's performance and helps identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page of the form carefully.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to provide your feedback and suggestions for improvement.
4. If you have any comments or suggestions, use the "Do you have any suggestions or comments for our company?" and "How can we improve?" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Introduction
This page provides a brief introduction to the survey and its purpose. Please read it carefully before proceeding.

### Page 2: How likely are you to recommend our company?
This question measures how likely you are to recommend our company to others. Choose the option that best describes your likelihood:
* 2 - Not Likely
* 3 - Maybe
* 4 - Very Likely

### Page 3: On a scale of 0-10, how likely are you to recommend our company?
This question is similar to the previous one, but asks you to provide a numerical value between 0 and 10. This will help us understand your level of confidence in recommending our company.
* On a scale of 0-10

### Page 4: How do you feel about our company's products or services?
Select all that apply:
* Excellent
* Good
* Average
* Poor

### Page 5: How likely is it that you will recommend our company to a friend or colleague?
Choose the option that best describes your likelihood:
* Not Likely
* Somewhat Likely
* Very Likely

### Page 6: What do you think is the main reason you wouldn't recommend our company?
Please provide a brief explanation of why you wouldn't recommend our company.

### Page 7: On a scale of 0-10, how would you rate the quality of our service?
Provide a numerical value between 0 and 10.

### Page 8: Have you used our company's products or services in the past 30 days?
Choose the option that best describes your experience:
* True
* False

### Page 9: If yes, how recently did you use our company's products or services?
If you answered "True" in the previous question, provide the date you last used our company's products or services.

### Page 10: How would you rate your overall experience with our company?
Choose the option that best describes your experience:
* Excellent
* Good
* Average
* Poor

### Page 11: Do you have any suggestions or comments for our company?
Please provide any suggestions or comments you may have for our company's improvement.

### Page 12: How can we improve?
Provide any ideas you have for how we can improve our company's products or services.

By following these steps and understanding each field's purpose, you'll be able to provide valuable feedback and help us improve your customer experience. Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey!
