# GDPR Consent Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The GDPR Consent Form Template is a form designed to collect user consent for data collection and processing under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). This form helps ensure that users understand what data will be collected and how it will be used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "User Information" page to provide your name, email address, and any other requested information.
2. Select "True" or "False" in the "Consent" field to indicate your agreement to data collection and processing.
3. On the "Contact Preferences" page, provide any additional contact information you are comfortable sharing with the organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Information (Page 1)
* **User Information** (`<name>`, `text`, required: false): This page collects general information about the user, such as name and email address. You can enter any additional details as you see fit.
* **User Information (Page 1)** (`<type>`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect additional user details. Please enter your details here.
### Consent (Page 2)
* **Consent** (`<name>`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "True" to indicate that you agree to data collection and processing. Select "False" to decline.
### Contact Preferences (Page 3)
* **Contact Preferences** (`<name>`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to collect any additional contact preferences you wish to share with the organization. Please enter your preferences here.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and understand what data will be collected and how it will be used before providing consent.
* If you decline consent, your data will not be collected or processed.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the organization's data protection officer.
