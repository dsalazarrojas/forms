# estimate_advertising_budget_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to estimate the advertising budget for a marketing campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and other relevant contact information if required.
2. Select the type of media you plan to use for your campaign (Video Ad, Image Ad, Social Media Ad).
3. Enter the ad budget for your campaign.
4. Specify the duration of your campaign (in days).
5. Choose the start and end dates for your campaign.
6. Choose the start and end time for your campaign.
7. Select the frequency of your campaign (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
8. Estimate the cost of your campaign.
9. Provide an optional breakdown of the estimated cost.
10. Finally, specify your marketing strategy (Digital, Print, Social Media).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, Text, Optional): This field is for entering any other relevant information that may be required for the campaign.
* **Media Type** (`media_type`, Select One, Required): Select the type of media you plan to use for your campaign. This can be Video Ad, Image Ad, or Social Media Ad.
* **Ad Budget** (`ad_budget`, Number, Required): Enter the budget for your campaign.
* **Campaign Duration** (`campaign_duration`, Number, Required): Specify the duration of your campaign in days.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, Date, Required): Choose the start date for your campaign.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, Date, Required): Choose the end date for your campaign.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, Time, Optional): Choose the start time for your campaign.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, Time, Optional): Choose the end time for your campaign.
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, Select Multiple, Required): Select how often you plan to run your campaign (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Estimated Cost** (`estimated_cost`, Number, Required): Estimate the total cost of your campaign.
* **Estimated Cost Breakdown** (`estimated_cost_breakdown`, Note, Optional): Provide a breakdown of your estimated campaign costs.
* **Marketing Strategy** (`marketing_strategy`, Select One, Required): Specify your marketing strategy for the campaign, which can be Digital, Print, or Social Media.
