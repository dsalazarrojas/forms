# Price Sensitivity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about how different demographics respond to varying prices. Please answer the questions as honestly as possible, and make sure you are answering in relation to a specific product or service being priced.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and thoroughly.
2. Select the options that best match your current situation.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields. You may skip optional fields if you are not comfortable sharing that information.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **price_sensitivity**: A number indicating your current price sensitivity. Please enter a number between 1 and 100.
* **product_name**: The name of the product or service you are considering purchasing or have purchased. Please enter the name of the product or service as it is known to you.
* **age**: Your age. Please enter a number representing your age.
* **income_range**: Select the income range that best describes your annual household income. Please note that these ranges are based on the income you expect to make in the next 6 months.
	+ Less than $20,000
	+ $20,000 - $40,000
	+ $40,000 - $60,000
	+ $60,000 - $80,000
	+ $80,000 - $100,000
	+ $100,000 - $150,000
	+ $150,000 - $200,000
	+ $200,000 - $250,000
	+ $250,000 - $500,000
	+ $500,000 - $1,000,000
	+ $1,000,000 or more
* **price_range**: Select the price ranges you are currently sensitive to. Please choose all that apply. You can select multiple options.
	+ $0 - $100
	+ $101 - $200
	+ $201 - $500
	+ $501 - $1,000
	+ $1,001 - $2,000
	+ $2,001 - $5,000
	+ $5,001 - $10,000
	+ $10,001 or more
* **price_point**: The price at which you are most sensitive. Please enter a number.
* **product_price**: The price of the product or service you are considering purchasing or have purchased. Please enter a number representing the price.
* **discount**: Select the discount that would most likely influence your purchasing decision. Please choose one option.
	+ 0% - 10%
	+ 10% - 20%
	+ 20% - 30%
	+ 30% - 40%
	+ 40% or more
