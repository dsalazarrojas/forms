# Market Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the overall customer satisfaction with a market offering. It will help us understand your experience and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill in this form, follow these steps:

1. Rate your overall customer satisfaction with the market offering using the numerical rating.
2. Provide a detailed description of your experience with the market offering in the "Describe your experience with this vendor" field.
3. If you've had a purchase experience with the market offering, fill in the "Time of purchase" field with the date of your purchase.
4. Indicate how likely you are to recommend the market offering to a friend or colleague using the "How likely are you to recommend this market to a friend or colleague" select multiple field.
5. Finally, provide another detailed description of your experience with the market offering in the "Describe your experience with this vendor" field (this field is similar to the previous one).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall customer satisfaction rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the market offering on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Describe your experience with this vendor**: Please provide a detailed description of your experience with the market offering. This will help us understand what you liked or disliked about the market.
* **Time of purchase**: If you've had a purchase experience with the market offering, fill in the date of your purchase. This will help us understand the context of your experience.
* **How likely are you to recommend this market to a friend or colleague?**: Indicate your likelihood to recommend the market offering to a friend or colleague using the options: High, Medium, or Low.
* **Describe your experience with this vendor**: Provide another detailed description of your experience with the market offering. This is similar to the first "Describe your experience with this vendor" field, but you can provide additional information or context if needed.
