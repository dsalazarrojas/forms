# social_media_insights_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers about their social media analytics needs. The purpose of this form is to gather specific details about the type of analytics they are looking for, how often they want to receive them, and how they want to be contacted.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and select your preferences for the type of analytics and communication frequency you need.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company** (`text`, optional): Enter your company name.
- **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address associated with your company or organization.
- **Phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
- **What kind of analytics are you looking for?** (`select_one`, optional): Choose from the options: Account Level Insights, Campaign Level Insights, Post Level Insights, or User Level Insights.
- **Company Name** (`text`, optional): Enter your company name.
- **Job Title** (`text`, optional): Enter your job title or role within your organization.
- **Contact Person** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the person to contact.
- **Message** (`text`, optional): Provide any additional information or context about your analytics request.
- **How often would you like to receive the requested analytics?** (`select_one`, optional): Choose from the options: Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
- **How often would you like to receive emails with your requested analytics?** (`select_one`, optional): Choose from the options: Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
- **Send notification when analytics are ready?** (`select_one`, optional): Choose from the options: True or False.
- **Date** (`date`, optional): Select a specific date for your analytics request.
- **Time** (`time`, optional): Select a specific time for your analytics request.
## Tips
Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date to ensure efficient processing of your analytics request. Also, make sure to select the correct options for analytics type and communication frequency that suit your needs.
