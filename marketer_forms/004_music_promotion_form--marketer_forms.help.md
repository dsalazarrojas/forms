# Music Promotion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Promotion Form is a tool for music promotion purposes. It helps gather information about an artist, their music, and the promotional package. The form is designed to be completed by artists or their representatives to promote their music to various channels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Artist** field with the name of the artist or band.
2. Enter a brief description of the **Music** field, which can be a single song, album, or EP.
3. Fill in the **Promotion Package** field with any relevant details about the promotion, such as the type of promotion and the duration.
4. Select one or multiple **Label Interests** that apply to the promotion.
5. Check the **Label Terms** field if you have any specific requirements or agreements for the promotion.
6. Select the **Label Agreements** that apply to the promotion, if any.
7. Finally, click the **Submit** button to send the promotion request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Artist
* **Artist** (`artist`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the artist or band.
### Music
* **Music** (`music`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the music to be promoted.
### Promotion Package
* **Promotion Package** (`promotion_package`, `text`, required): Enter details about the promotion package, such as the type and duration.
