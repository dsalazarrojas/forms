# Request For Proposal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Request For Proposal (RFP) form is designed to gather information about your project and proposal requirements. This form helps us create a comprehensive proposal that meets your project needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields accurately and completely.
2. Make sure to provide your project name and description.
3. Enter your contact information (contact person, email, and phone number).
4. Specify your project budget.
5. Choose the format of your proposal (PDF, DOCX, or ODT).
6. Select the type of project you are proposing (website, mobile app, e-commerce, or digital marketing).
7. Specify the status of your project (active, inactive, on hold, pending, or reopened).
8. Enter the name and email of your project manager (if applicable).
9. Select the status of your proposal (approved, in progress, rejected, reopened, or pending).
10. Choose the type of proposal you are submitting (initial, revised, or final).
11. Enter the date of your proposal.
12. Specify the review deadline for your proposal.
13. Select the status of your review (in progress, completed, or on hold).
14. Enter any review comments.
15. Select the result of your review (approved, rejected, reopened, or pending).
16. Enter your user ID and form ID (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request For Proposal Form** (`title`): Enter a brief title of your proposal.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`): Enter the name of your project.
* **Description** (`description`): Enter a detailed description of your project.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): Enter your name, and this field will be used as the primary point of contact for your proposal.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Due Date** (`due_date`): Enter the due date for your proposal.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`): Enter the budget for your project.
* **Proposal Format** (`proposal_format`): Choose the format of your proposal (PDF, DOCX, or ODT).
* **Project Type** (`project_type`): Select the type of project you are proposing (website, mobile app, e-commerce, or digital marketing).
* **Project Status** (`project_status`): Specify the status of your project (active, inactive, on hold, pending, or reopened).
* **Project Manager** (`project_manager`): Enter the name and email of your project manager (if applicable).
* **Client Name** (`client_name`): Enter the name of your client.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`): Enter the email of your client.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`): Enter the phone number of your client.
* **Proposal Status** (`proposal_status`): Select the status of your proposal (approved, in progress, rejected, reopened, or pending).
* **Proposal Type** (`proposal_type`): Choose the type of proposal you are submitting (initial, revised, or final).
* **Proposal Date** (`proposal_date`): Enter the date of your proposal.
* **Review Deadline** (`review_deadline`): Specify the deadline for the review of your proposal.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`): Select the status of your review (in progress, completed, or on hold).
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`): Enter any comments from the review process.
* **Review Result** (`review_result`): Select the result of your review (approved, rejected, reopened, or pending).
* **User ID** (`user_id`): Enter your user ID.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`): Enter your form ID.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any additional comments about your proposal.
