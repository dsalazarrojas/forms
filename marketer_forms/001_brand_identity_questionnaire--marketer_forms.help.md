# Brand Identity Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Brand Identity Questionnaire is designed to help us understand your brand's core values, unique characteristics, and communication style. This form will ask about your company name, industry, mission, and target audience to gain a deeper understanding of your brand's identity and goals. Your input will help us create a comprehensive brand strategy that aligns with your vision and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (marked as **required**) completely and accurately.
2. For fields with options, select the best fit from the provided choices.
3. If you have any doubts or uncertainties about a specific field, please ask yourself questions like:
	* What is the main purpose of my brand?
	* Who are my primary customers or users?
	* What problems do my products or services address?
	* What sets my brand apart from competitors?

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company or Brand Name** (1, text, required)
	* Please enter your brand's full legal name, including all words and characters.
- **Industry or Business Sector** (2, select_one, required)
	* Choose the industry category that best describes your business (e.g., Technology, Healthcare, Finance).
- **What is your brand mission** (3, text, required)
	* Describe the core purpose and values of your brand in a concise paragraph.
- **Primary Target Audience** (4, text, required)
	* Identify your main customers or users (e.g., consumers, businesses, etc.).
- **What problems does your brand solve** (5, text, required)
	* List the key problems your products or services address.
- **Brand Personality Traits** (6, select_multiple, required)
	* Select the characteristics that define your brand (e.g., Innovative, Trustworthy, Playful).
- **Current Business Concerns** (7, text, optional)
	* List any challenges or concerns your brand is facing.
- **Main Competitors** (8, text, optional)
	* Identify your primary competitors.
- **Unique Value Proposition** (9, text, required)
	* Describe what makes your brand different from competitors.
- **Short-term Brand Goals (6-12 months)** (10, text, required)
	* Outline your short-term objectives.
- **Long-term Brand Vision (2+ years)** (11, text, optional)
	* Describe your long-term vision for your brand.
- **Preferred Brand Colors** (12, text, optional)
	* Enter any specific colors associated with your brand.
- **Brand Tone of Voice** (13, select_one, required)
	* Choose how you want your brand to communicate (e.g., Formal and Professional, Casual and Friendly).
- **How would you rate your current branding** (14, select_one, required)
	* Self-assess your current branding level (1 - Needs major overhaul, 5 - Excellent and consistent).
- **Estimated Budget for Brand Development** (15, select_one, optional)
	* Approximate your budget for brand development.
- **Project Timeline** (16, date, required)
	* Choose a completion date for this project.

Note: Please fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible to help us create a well-informed brand strategy for your business.
