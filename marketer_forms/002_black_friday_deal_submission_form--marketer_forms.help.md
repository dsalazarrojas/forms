# Black Friday Deal Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user-submitted Black Friday deals from various online retailers for display on our platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields with accurate information about your Black Friday deal.
2. Ensure that you provide a valid email address to allow us to contact you about your submission.
3. Review your submission for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: A required field for identification purposes.
	+ Type: Text input field.
	+ Description: Enter your name as it appears on your business card or online profile.
* **Email Address**: A required field for contact and verification purposes.
	+ Type: Email input field.
	+ Description: Enter a valid email address where we can contact you about your submission.
* **Company or Brand Name**: A required field for identification and categorization.
	+ Type: Text input field.
	+ Description: Enter your company or brand name as it appears on your business card or online profile.
* **Website URL**: A required field for linking back to your website.
	+ Type: Text input field.
	+ Description: Enter the URL of your website where we can find more information about your offer.
* **Deal Title**: A required field for displaying on our platform.
	+ Type: Text input field.
	+ Description: Enter a short and catchy title for your Black Friday deal.
* **Deal Description**: A required field for explaining your offer.
	+ Type: Text input field.
	+ Description: Describe what your deal includes and any terms or conditions.
* **Discount Percentage**: A required field for calculating the discount.
	+ Type: Number input field.
	+ Description: Enter the percentage discount for your offer.
* **Original Price**: An optional field for providing additional context.
	+ Type: Number input field.
	+ Description: Enter the original price of the product or service.
* **Sale Price**: An optional field for providing additional context.
	+ Type: Number input field.
	+ Description: Enter the sale price of the product or service.
* **Deal Start Date**: A required field for determining the deal's start date.
	+ Type: Date input field.
	+ Description: Enter the date when your Black Friday deal starts.
* **Deal End Date**: A required field for determining the deal's end date.
	+ Type: Date input field.
	+ Description: Enter the date when your Black Friday deal ends.
* **Product Category**: A required field for categorizing your offer.
	+ Type: Select one input field.
	+ Description: Choose the category that best fits your Black Friday deal.
* **Key deal highlights**: An optional field for showcasing important details.
	+ Type: Text input field.
	+ Description: Enter any notable features or highlights of your Black Friday deal.
* **Deal Image URL**: An optional field for adding a promotional image.
	+ Type: Text input field.
	+ Description: Link to a high-quality image of the product or service.
* **Coupon Code (if applicable)**: An optional field for providing a coupon code.
	+ Type: Text input field.
	+ Description: Enter a coupon code for customers to use during the sale.
* **Submission Date**: A required field for tracking the submission date.
	+ Type: Date input field.
	+ Description: This field is automatically populated upon submission.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have the right to promote your Black Friday deal and that it complies with our platform's guidelines.
* Double-check your submission for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
* If you have any issues or need help, contact us at [your email address] for assistance.
