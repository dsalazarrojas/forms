# Demo Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Demo Request Form is a simple form used to collect contact information and demo requests from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in the contact form fields with the requested information:
	+ **contact_name** (`contact_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name.
	+ **company** (`company`, text, required: false): If you represent a company, enter the name of the company.
	+ **email** (`email`, text, required: false): Enter your email address.
	+ **phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
- Select one option from the **select_one_option_1** (`select_one_option_1`, select one, required: false): Choose an option that best represents your preferences.
- Select one option from the **select_one_option_2** (`select_one_option_2`, select one, required: false): Choose an option that best represents your preferences.
- Optionally, enter a brief **message** (`message`, text, required: false): If you have any additional information or comments, you can include them here.
- Optionally, enter **demo_request_form_2** (`demo_request_form_2`, text, required: false): This field is currently empty.
- Optionally, enter **contact_title** (`contact_title`, text, required: false): Enter a title or designation for the contact person.
- Optionally, enter **product_info** (`product_info`, text, required: false): Enter details about the product or service you are requesting a demo for.
- Optionally, enter **demo_request_form** (`demo_request_form`, text, required: false): This field is currently empty.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **contact_name**: Your name.
- **company**: The name of the company you represent, if any.
- **email**: Your email address.
- **phone**: Your phone number.
- **product_info**: Details about the product or service you are requesting a demo for.
- **contact_title**: A title or designation for the contact person.
- **message**: A brief message or comment about your demo request.
- **select_one_option_1**: Select one option that best represents your preferences.
- **select_one_option_2**: Select one option that best represents your preferences.
