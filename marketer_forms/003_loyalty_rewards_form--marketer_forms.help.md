# Loyalty Rewards Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Loyalty Rewards Form is designed to collect reward information from customers. This includes reward points, reward type, date and time earned, loyalty level, email, phone number, store location, and any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields with your relevant information. Make sure to review each field carefully to ensure you provide accurate data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Loyalty Rewards** (`loyalty_rewards_form`, text, optional): Enter your loyalty rewards program details in this field.
* **Reward Points** (`reward_points`, number, optional): Enter the number of reward points you have earned.
* **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, text, optional): Enter your customer ID, if applicable.
* **Reward Type** (`reward_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of reward you have earned (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Reward Amount** (`reward_amount`, number, optional): Enter the reward amount you have earned.
* **Date Earned** (`date_earned`, date, optional): Enter the date you earned your reward.
* **Time Earned** (`time_earned`, time, optional): Enter the time you earned your reward.
* **Loyalty Level** (`loyalty_level`, select_one, optional): Select your loyalty level (Level 1, Level 2, Level 3).
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your customer email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your customer phone number.
* **Store Location** (`store_location`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable store locations (Location 1, Location 2, Location 3, Location 4).
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your reward.
* **Submitted** (`submitted`, select_one, optional): Mark whether the form has been submitted (True, False).

Note: This form is designed to collect information for reward tracking purposes, please ensure accuracy and completeness of your submission.
