# website_client_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "website_client_feedback_form" is a form designed to gather feedback from clients about their overall experience with the website. This feedback helps us understand areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance the user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing a brief description of your overall experience with the website. This is your opportunity to share your thoughts on how the website met your needs.
2. Rate your overall experience with a score out of 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
3. Share what you liked about your experience with the website. This could be a feature, a page, or a specific interaction that impressed you.
4. Share what you didn't like about your experience with the website. This could be a feature, a page, or a specific interaction that didn't meet your expectations.
5. Rate how you found the website overall, with options for Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
6. If you have any thoughts on how we could improve the website, please share them in the text field provided.
7. Finally, provide your contact information so we can follow up with you and thank you for your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback** (Text): A text field where you can describe your overall experience with the website.
* **Overall Experience** (Text): Rate your overall experience with the website out of 5.
* **Experience Score** (Number): Enter a score between 1 and 5 to rate your experience.
* **What did you like?** (Text): Share what you enjoyed about your experience with the website.
* **What didn't you like?** (Text): Share any issues or disappointments you experienced with the website.
* **How did you find us?** (Select One): Choose from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor to rate your overall experience.
* **How did you find us? Why?** (Text): If you selected Fair or Poor, please provide more details on why you felt that way.
* **What would improve?** (Text): Suggest ways we could improve the website to better serve you.
* **Contact Info** (Email): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you and thank you for your feedback.

## Tips

- Be honest and concise in your feedback, as this helps us understand your experience better.
- If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please share them, as we value your input.
- Your feedback is anonymous, but if you provide your contact information, we can follow up with you and thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback.
