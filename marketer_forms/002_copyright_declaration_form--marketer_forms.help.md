# Copyright Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect copyright information from individuals or organizations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the content you are submitting in the "Content Title" field.
2. Identify yourself as the copyright holder in the "Copyright Holder Name" field.
3. Enter the date of the copyright in the "Copyright Date" field.
4. Select the country where the copyright was granted or registered in the "Country of Copyright" field.
5. Enter the state where the copyright was granted or registered in the "State of Copyright" field.
6. Enter the county where the copyright was granted or registered in the "County of Copyright" field.
7. Enter the city where the copyright was granted or registered in the "City of Copyright" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Copyright Form**: This is the main section where you can enter any additional comments or details about your copyright declaration.
* **Copyright Holder Name**: Enter your name as the copyright holder, if you are a human, or the name of your organization if you are a company.
* **Content Title**: Enter the title of the content you are submitting.
* **Copyright Date**: Enter the date when the copyright was granted or registered.
* **Country of Copyright**: Select the country where the copyright was granted or registered.
* **State of Copyright**: Enter the state where the copyright was granted or registered.
* **County of Copyright**: Enter the county where the copyright was granted or registered.
* **City of Copyright**: Enter the city where the copyright was granted or registered.
