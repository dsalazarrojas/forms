# Market Research Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather market research data from customers to create more effective marketing campaigns and strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps provided above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (text): This is the opening page that explains the purpose and context of the survey.
- **demographic_info** (text): Provide your general information, such as name, job title, and department.
- **company_info** (text): Enter your company details, including the company name and size.
- **contact_method** (select multiple): Select your preferred contact method, such as email, phone, or in-person.
- **customer_preferences** (select one): Choose your preferred communication channel for market research, such as email, phone, or social media.
- **customer_experience** (text): Describe your customer experience with our company or product.
- **additional_comments** (text): Provide any further comments or suggestions.
- **Contact Us** (text): If you have any questions or need assistance, you can contact us here.
- **email_address** (email): Enter your email address for further communication.
