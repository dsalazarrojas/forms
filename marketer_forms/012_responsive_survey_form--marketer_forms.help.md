# Responsive Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and opinions from users to improve the quality of our website.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the `FirstName` field. This is where you can input your name as it appears on your official documents.
2. Rate our website by selecting a number between 1 and 5 in the `RateOurWebsite` field.
3. Provide any additional feedback in the `Feedback` field. This is a free text area where you can type any comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **FirstName (`first_name`, text, **required**):** Enter your first name.
* **RateOurWebsite (`rate_website`, number, **required**):** Rate our website from 1 to 5, where 1 is "very poor" and 5 is "excellent".
* **Feedback (`feedback`, text, **optional**):** Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
 
## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* You can enter any additional text in the `Feedback` field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
