# Black Friday Blowout Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers about their shopping preferences, interests, and contact information to provide them with targeted promotions and offers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the first name field with your first name.
2. Fill out the last name field with your last name.
3. Enter your email address in the Email Address field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone Number field (if you want to receive SMS deal alerts).
5. Choose your preferred contact method in the Preferred Contact Method field.
6. Select your shopping preferences in the Shopping Preferences field.
7. Choose your budget range in the Budget Range field.
8. Select your deal type preference in the Deal Type Preference field.
9. Sign up for our newsletter in the Newsletter Signup field (if you want to receive weekly deals and promotions).
10. Choose how you found out about this promotion in the Referral Source field.
11. Optionally, fill out the Phone Number field if you want to receive SMS deal alerts.
12. Optionally, select a shopping time preference in the Shopping Time Preference field.
13. Optionally, enter your favorite brand in the Favorite Brands field.
14. Optionally, provide special requests in the Special Requests field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address. We will send deals to this email.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to receive SMS deal alerts.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact`, select_one, required): Choose how you want us to contact you:
	+ Email
	+ Phone Call
	+ SMS Text
* **Shopping Preferences** (`shopping_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Select your shopping preferences:
	+ Electronics
	+ Clothing and Fashion
	+ Home and Garden
	+ Beauty and Personal Care
	+ Sports and Outdoors
	+ Toys and Games
	+ Books and Media
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, required): Choose your budget range:
	+ Under $100
	+ $100-$250
	+ $251-$500
	+ $501-$1000
	+ Over $1000
* **Favorite Brands** (`favorite_brands`, text, optional): Enter your favorite brands.
* **Deal Type Preference** (`deal_type_preference`, select_one, required): Select your deal type preference:
	+ Percentage Discounts
	+ Dollar Amount Off
	+ Buy One Get One Free
	+ Free Shipping
	+ Bundle Deals
* **Shopping Time Preference** (`shopping_time_preference`, select_one, optional): Choose your shopping time preference:
	+ Early Morning
	+ Midday
	+ Evening
	+ Late Night
	+ Online Anytime
* **Newsletter Signup** (`newsletter_signup`, select_one, required): Choose whether to sign up for our newsletter:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source`, select_one, optional): Choose how you found out about this promotion:
	+ Social Media
	+ Email
	+ Friend or Family
	+ Search Engine
	+ Advertisement
	+ Other
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any specific products or deals you want.
