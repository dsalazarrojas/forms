# Website Content Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect website content requests from users. It is intended to gather specific information about the website and the user's requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the URL of the website that you are requesting content for.
2. Enter your email address so that we can contact you.
3. Provide a detailed description of the content you are requesting.
4. (Optional) Enter any additional information that is relevant to your request.
5. (Optional) Enter the URL of the website you are requesting content for.
6. Enter your email address for the second part of the request.
7. Provide a detailed description of the content you are requesting.
8. Enter any additional information that is relevant to your request.
9. Review and submit the form once you have entered all the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Website URL** (`website_url`, `text`, required): Enter the URL of the website that you are requesting content for.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so that we can contact you.
* **Content Request** (`content_request`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description of the content you are requesting.
* **Additional Information** (`page_2`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information that is relevant to your request.
* **Website URL 2** (`website_url_2`, `text`, required): Enter the URL of the website you are requesting content for (if applicable).
* **Email Address 2** (`email_2`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for the second part of the request.
* **Content Request 2** (`content_request_2`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description of the content you are requesting.
* **Additional Information 2** (`page_2`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information that is relevant to your request.

## Tips

* Ensure that the URL you enter is accurate and up-to-date.
* Be specific and detailed in your content requests.
* Review all fields carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
