# E Commerce Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about your online shopping habits, preferences, and behavior. The goal is to understand how you interact with e-commerce platforms, what you like to buy, and how you use your devices to access online stores.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the form with your information.
2. Make sure to fill in all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
3. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.
4. Select only one option for each field where applicable.
