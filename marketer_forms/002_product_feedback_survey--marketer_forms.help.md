# Product Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your thoughts and opinions about our product. Please answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly to help us better understand your experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, simply fill in the fields with your responses.
- Make sure to provide specific details for each field to help us understand your feedback.
- You may skip fields that are not relevant to your experience or opinions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Product Feedback** (`product_feedback`): This is the main area where you can express your thoughts, opinions, and suggestions about the product. Try to be as specific as possible.
- **Rating** (`rating`): This is a rating system to help us understand your satisfaction level with the product. Please select one of the options: very satisfied, somewhat satisfied, neutral, or dissatisfied.
- **Email** (`email`): If you'd like to share more details or have any questions, please enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): If you'd like to be contacted by our team for further discussion, please enter your phone number.
- **Date** (`date`): This field is not required, but if you'd like to specify the date when you used the product, please enter it.
- **Time** (`time`): This field is not required, but if you'd like to specify the time when you used the product, please enter it.
- **Select 1** (`select_1`): This is a multiple-choice question where you can select one of the options that best describes your experience with the product.
- **Select 2** (`select_2`): This is a multiple-choice question where you can select multiple options that best describe your experience with the product.
- **Note** (`note`): This is an optional field for you to add any additional comments or details about your experience.

## Tips
- Please be honest and objective in your feedback.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
- Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your input!
