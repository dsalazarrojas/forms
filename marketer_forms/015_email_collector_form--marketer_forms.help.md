# email_collector_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The `email_collector_form` is used to collect email addresses and other relevant contact information from users. This form is typically used for email marketing, customer communication, and other marketing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the `email_collector_form`, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second field.
3. Enter your **Email** in the third field.
4. Confirm your **Email** in the fourth field by selecting the correct option from the dropdown menu.
5. If desired, provide your **Phone** number in the fifth field.
6. Enter your desired **Date** in the sixth field.
7. Select your **Time** zone in the seventh field.
8. You can add a brief **Note** about your interest in the eighth field.
9. Choose to **Opt in for future communications** in the ninth field.
10. Confirm your choice to **Opt in for future communications** in the tenth field.
11. Accept the **Terms and Conditions** in the eleventh field.
12. Accept the **Privacy Policy** in the twelfth field.
13. Verify your **Age** in the thirteenth field.
14. Accept the **Terms of Service** in the fourteenth field.
15. Confirm your acceptance of the **Terms of Service** in the fifteenth field.
16. Select your **Country Code** in the sixteenth field.
17. Select your **State** in the seventeenth field.
18. Indicate whether you have a **Zip** code in the eighteenth field.
19. Enter your **Address** in the nineteenth field.
20. Enter your **City** in the twentieth field.
21. Select your **State Code** in the twenty-first field.
22. Indicate whether you have an **Address 2** in the twenty-second field.
23. Select your **Postal/Zip Code** in the twenty-third field.
24. Indicate whether you have an **Address 1** in the twenty-fourth field.
25. Select who **Form Subscribed** as in the twenty-fifth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name (2)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email (3)**: Enter a valid email address that you use to communicate with us.
* **Confirm Email (4)**: Re-enter your email address to verify its accuracy.
* **Phone (5)**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Date (6)**: Enter the date you are interested in.
* **Time (7)**: Select your time zone.
* **Note (8)**: Add any additional notes about your interest.
* **Opt in for future communications (9)**: Choose to receive future communications from us.
* **Confirm Opt in for future communications (10)**: Confirm your choice.
* **Accept terms and conditions (11)**: Accept our terms and conditions.
* **Accept privacy policy (12)**: Accept our privacy policy.
* **Verify Age (13)**: Verify your age to ensure eligibility.
* **Accept terms of service (14)**: Accept our terms of service.
* **Confirm Accept terms of service (15)**: Confirm your acceptance.
* **Country Code (16)**: Select your country of residence.
* **State (17)**: Select your state or province of residence.
* **Zip (18)**: Indicate whether you have a zip code.
* **Address (19)**: Enter your address.
* **City (20)**: Enter your city or town.
* **State Code (21)**: Select your state or province code.
* **Address 2 (22)**: Indicate whether you have a second address.
* **Postal/Zip Code (23)**: Select your postal/zip code.
* **Address 1 (24)**: Indicate whether you have a primary address.
* **Form Submitted by (25)**: Select who submitted the form.
