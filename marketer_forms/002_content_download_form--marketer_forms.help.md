# Content Download Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Content Download Form is designed to collect information from users interested in downloading specific content such as eBooks, whitepapers, research reports, case studies, template guides, and checklists. The form fields are carefully selected to ensure that the user experience is smooth and relevant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Full Name in the Full Name field.
2. Enter your Email Address in the Email Address field. This is where we will send the download link.
3. If you wish, enter your Company Name in the Company Name field.
4. If applicable, enter your Job Title in the Job Title field.
5. Select your Industry from the Industry dropdown list.
6. If you wish, select your Company Size from the Company Size dropdown list.
7. Choose the Content to Download from the Select Content to Download dropdown list.
8. Select your Topic of Interest from the Topic of Interest dropdown list.
9. If you wish, enter your Phone Number in the Phone Number field.
10. If you wish, let us know how you found us in the How Did You Find Us? field.
11. Choose whether you would like to receive Marketing Communications from the Marketing Communications dropdown list.
12. If you wish, enter any additional requests or topics you are interested in in the Additional Requests field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Full Name, text, required): Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): We will send the download link here.
* **Company Name** (Company Name, text, optional): Name of your organization.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, text, optional): Your current position.
* **Industry** (Industry, select_one, optional): Select your industry.
* **Company Size** (Company Size, select_one, optional): Number of employees.
* **Select Content to Download** (Select Content to Download, select_one, required): Choose the resource you want.
* **Topic of Interest** (Topic of Interest, select_one, required): What topic are you most interested in?
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, optional): Optional contact number.
* **How Did You Find Us?** (How Did You Find Us?, select_one, optional): Source of discovery.
* **Marketing Communications** (Marketing Communications, select_one, required): May we send you related content and updates?
* **Additional Requests** (Additional Requests, text, optional): Any specific topics you would like to learn about?
