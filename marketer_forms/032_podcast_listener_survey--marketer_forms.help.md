# Podcast Listener Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather information from podcast listeners to improve the podcast's content and engagement strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly to provide valuable insights.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
3. Use the available space to provide more detailed answers if you have any additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### How did you discover our podcast
* **How did you discover our podcast** (`How did you discover our podcast`, text, required: false): This is where you can tell us how you first found out about our podcast. You can mention online platforms, social media, word of mouth, or any other way you might have come across it.

### Rate our podcast
* **Rate our podcast** (`Rate our podcast`, number, required: false): We would like to know how much you enjoy our podcast. Please rate it from 1 (low) to 5 (high).

### Which episode is your favorite?
* **Which episode is your favorite** (`Which episode is your favorite`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all the episodes that you particularly enjoyed or found interesting. You can select one or multiple episodes.

### What do you want to improve?
* **What do you want to improve** (`What do you want to improve`, text, required: false): This is your chance to suggest any improvements or changes you would like to see in our podcast. Be specific and clear about what you would like to see changed or updated.

### How can we reach out to you?
* **How can we reach out to you** (`How can we reach out to you`, email, required: false): If you are comfortable with contact, provide your email address so we can reach out to you with updates and new content.

### What is your phone number?
* **Phone number** (`Phone number`, text, required: false): Please format your phone number as 123-456-7890. We will only use this information to reach out to you if you have given your consent.

### How old are you?
* **How old are you** (`How old are you`, number, required: false): We are interested in knowing your age to better understand our audience demographics.

### What genre do you like best?
* **What genre do you like best** (`What genre do you like best`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of content you enjoy most, such as comedy, news, education, or entertainment.

### How often do you listen?
* **How often do you listen** (`How often do you listen`, text, required: false): Tell us about your listening habits, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your contact information to ensure it is correct before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* Your feedback is valuable, and we appreciate your time in filling out this survey.
