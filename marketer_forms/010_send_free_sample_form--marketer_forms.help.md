# Send Free Sample Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow customers to request free samples from the company. Customers can submit their contact information and preferences for samples, which can be reviewed and processed by the company's staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
4. Select the type of sample you are interested in from the "Sample Type" dropdown menu.
5. Indicate the quantity of sample you would like to receive in the "Sample Quantity" field.
6. Enter your customer address in the "Customer Address" field.
7. Write any additional message to us in the "Message" field (optional).
8. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Send Sample" to indicate if you would like to receive the sample.
9. If you have selected to receive the sample, choose the date when you would like to receive it from the "Send Sample By" field.
10. Select the time when you would like to receive the sample from the "Send Sample To" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name so we can address the sample to you.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your sample.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Sample Type**: Select the type of sample you are interested in. (e.g., Sample Type, Sample Type 2)
* **Sample Quantity**: Enter the quantity of sample you would like to receive.
* **Customer Address**: Enter your address so we can know where to send the sample.
* **Message**: Write any additional information about yourself or your request.
* **Send Sample**: Select "Yes" to indicate that you would like to receive the sample, or "No" otherwise.
* **Send Sample By**: If you selected to receive the sample, choose the date when you would like to receive it.
* **Send Sample To**: If you selected to receive the sample, choose the time when you would like to receive it.
