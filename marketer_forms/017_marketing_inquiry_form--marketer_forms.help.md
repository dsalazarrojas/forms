# Marketing Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Inquiry Form is designed to collect information from customers or prospects who require marketing assistance, advice, or information.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields, as instructed.
2. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
3. Make sure to provide clear and concise information to ensure that we can effectively assist you with your marketing inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Marketing Inquiry Form (Page 1)** (`marketing_inquiry_form_page_1`, note, required: false): This field is for any relevant marketing information or comments that you might need to include about your marketing inquiry.
  
- **Marketing Inquiry Details (Page 2)** (`marketing_inquiry_form_page_2`, select_one, required: false):
  - **Yes**: This option is chosen when you're interested in getting marketing details or advice.
  - **No**: This option is chosen when you're not looking for marketing details or advice.

- **Marketing Inquiry Follow Up (Page 3)** (`marketing_inquiry_form_page_3`, select_multiple, required: false):
  - **Yes**: This option is selected when you want to be contacted for further marketing inquiry assistance or follow up on previous conversation.
  - **No**: This option is chosen when you do not want to be contacted or follow up.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide clear and concise information in the first page to ensure that our team can effectively assist you.
- Please select an option in the second page according to your true intention.
- Select all options in the third page that apply to your marketing inquiry follow up.
