# Branding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Branding Survey is designed to gather feedback on the brand's image, reputation, and overall impression from customers. Your input will help the brand understand how it is perceived and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the relevant options for each question, taking into account your genuine experience with the brand.
2. If a question is not applicable to you, choose "Neutral" or select "Not Applicable" as a default option.
3. For the "Brand Attributes" and "Brand Weaknesses" questions, you can select multiple options that best describe your experience with the brand.
4. For the "Brand Strengths" and "Brand Weaknesses" questions, you can type in free-form text to provide more detailed feedback.
5. Please answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly to provide valuable insights to the brand.
