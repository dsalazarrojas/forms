# Black Friday Waitlist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to register their interest in purchasing specific items during the Black Friday sale. By filling out this form, customers can express their interest in a particular item and provide their contact information to receive notifications when it becomes available.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name in the "Customer name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email address" field, so we can send you notifications.
3. Specify the item you are interested in purchasing in the "Desired item" field.
4. Choose your preferred store for pickup (if applicable) in the "Preferred store" field.
5. Enter the whole number of items you are interested in purchasing in the "Quantity interested" field.
6. Select your urgency level for receiving the item in the "Urgency level" field.
7. If you have an alternate contact method, such as a phone number, you can enter it in the "Alternate contact" field (optional).
8. Choose whether you want to opt-in for notifications in the "Opt in notifications" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name to help us identify you on the waitlist.
* **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address, and we'll send you notifications when your desired item becomes available.
* **Desired item** (`desired_item`, `text`, required): Type in the specific item you're interested in buying during Black Friday sales.
* **Preferred store** (`preferred_store`, `select_one`, optional): Choose where you want to pick up your item from our available stores.
* **Quantity interested** (`quantity`, `number`, required): Enter the number of items you're interested in purchasing.
* **Urgency level** (`urgency_level`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how urgently you need the item by selecting Low, Medium, or High.
* **Alternate contact** (`alternate_contact`, `text`, optional): If you want to provide an alternate contact method, such as a phone number, you can enter it here.
* **Opt in notifications** (`opt_in_notifications`, `select_one`, required): Agree to receive updates and notifications when your desired item becomes available.
