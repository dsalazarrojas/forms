# Facebook Fan Page Setup Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to set up a Facebook Fan Page for your business or service. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that your Fan Page is set up correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
3. Make sure to choose the correct options for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your business card or marketing materials.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your business card or marketing materials.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your business email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your business phone number.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required): Enter your company name as it appears on your business card or marketing materials.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required): Choose the category that best describes your business or service from the list:
	+ E-commerce
	+ Service
	+ Other (Please specify in the Description field)
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of your business or service.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required): Enter your business location (city, state, country).
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you would like to set up your Fan Page.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you would like to set up your Fan Page.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required): Choose the tool that you would like to use for your Fan Page setup:
	+ chatjimmy
	+ Other (Please specify in the Description field)
* **Category 2** (`category_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the category that best describes your business or service from the list:
	+ E-commerce
	+ Service
	+ Other (Please specify in the Description field)
* **Output File** (`output_file`, `text`, required): Enter the file path where you would like to save the Fan Page setup file.
* **Description 2** (`description_2`, `note`, required): Enter any additional information about your business or service.
* **Description 3** (`description_3`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of your business or service.
* **Output File 2** (`output_file_2`, `text`, required): Enter the file path where you would like to save the Fan Page setup file for backup purposes.
* **Description 4** (`description_4`, `text`, required): Enter any additional information about your business or service.
* **Description 5** (`description_5`, `text`, required): Enter any additional information about your business or service.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you need help with any of the fields, please reach out to our support team.
* Please submit the form only once for each Fan Page setup request.
