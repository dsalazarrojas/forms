# Text Only Advertisement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a request for creating text-only advertisements with our company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, which is a required field.
2. If you have a Company Name, enter it in the "Company Name" field. If not, you can leave this field blank.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field. This will be used to contact you about your request.
4. Enter your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field. This is an optional field but helps us to contact you more efficiently.
5. In the "Message" field, describe your text-only advertisement request. This is a required field.
6. If you want to schedule a specific date for your advertisement, enter it in the "Date" field. This is an optional field.
7. If you want to schedule a specific time for your advertisement, enter it in the "Time" field. This is an optional field.
8. If you have a preference for the options provided in "Select One", choose one that best suits your request. This is an optional field.
9. If you want to select multiple options for your advertisement, choose as many as you need from the "Select Multiple" field. This is an optional field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: A required field to identify you as a user.
* **Company Name**: An optional field to identify your company if you have one.
* **Email**: An optional field to contact you back about your request.
* **Phone Number**: An optional field to contact you more efficiently.
* **Message**: A required field where you describe your text-only advertisement request.
* **Date**: An optional field to schedule a specific date for your advertisement.
* **Time**: An optional field to schedule a specific time for your advertisement.
* **Select One**: An optional field to choose one option for your advertisement.
* **Select Multiple**: An optional field to choose multiple options for your advertisement.
* **Date Picker**: An optional field to select a date for your advertisement.
* **Time Picker**: An optional field to select a time for your advertisement.
