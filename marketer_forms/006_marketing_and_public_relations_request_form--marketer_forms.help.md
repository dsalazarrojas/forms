# Marketing And Public Relations Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from companies requesting marketing and public relations services. It helps us understand their needs and tailor our approach to their specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the company information section with the company's name, website, and contact details.
2. Select the media type and content topic that best describe the request.
3. Choose the request type and select any relevant options from the request details section.
4. Fill out the request details section with any additional information that might help us understand the request.
5. If applicable, provide the company logo and contact information for the marketing and public relations managers.
6. Finally, select any applicable agreement options and fill out the company address, city, state, zip, and country.
