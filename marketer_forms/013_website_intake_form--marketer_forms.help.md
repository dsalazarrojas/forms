# Website Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Website Intake Form is used to gather essential details about a new website project. This form helps us to understand the project's scope, requirements, and expectations from the client or internal team member.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Website Name** in the first field. This is the title of the website, which will be displayed on the web browser's address bar.
2. Enter the **Contact Email** in the second field. This is the email address we will use to communicate with you regarding the project.
3. Provide a brief description of the **Project Description** in the third field. This will help us to understand the project's objectives and scope.
4. Enter the **Project Budget** in the fourth field. This is the estimated budget for the project.
5. Enter the **Project Deadline** in the fifth field. This is the target date for completing the project.
6. Select the type of **Website Type** from the dropdown menu. This will help us to better understand the project's requirements.
7. Choose **Select Multiple** options from the dropdown menu. This field is optional and allows you to select multiple options.
8. Enter your answer in the **Text Area** field. This field is used for any additional information or comments.
9. Finally, click on the submit button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Website Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the website, which will be displayed on the web browser's address bar.
* **Contact Email** (2, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Project Description** (3, note, required): Provide a brief description of the project's objectives and scope.
* **Project Budget** (4, number, required): Enter the estimated budget for the project.
* **Project Deadline** (5, date, required): Enter the target date for completing the project.
* **Website Type** (6, select_one, required): Select the type of website (e.g., Web Development, E-commerce, Blogging, Landing Page).
* **Select Multiple** (7, select_multiple, required): Choose multiple options from the dropdown menu. This field is optional.
* **Text Area** (8, text, required): Enter any additional information or comments.
* **Time** (9, time, required): Enter time is not a required field and is not visible on this form.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and relevant information for us to better understand the project requirements.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before submitting the form.
* This form is designed to be easy to fill out, but if you have any trouble, contact us for assistance.
