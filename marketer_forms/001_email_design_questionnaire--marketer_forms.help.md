# Email Design Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from you to help us better understand your needs and preferences when it comes to designing or reviewing emails. It is intended for marketers, but anyone who is familiar with email marketing principles and terminology can use this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the pages as they come. Each page will have a clear heading and an explanation of what information is required. Please read each explanation carefully and fill out the form accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

**Customer Details (Page 1)**
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, `text`, required/optional): Please fill out this field with your contact information and any relevant details about yourself that will help us understand your background and context.
*   Ensure you enter a clear and concise description of your role, department, and company.

**Business Details (Page 2)**
