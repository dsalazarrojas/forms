# Black Friday Rebate Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to submit a rebate claim for purchases made on Black Friday. It's crucial to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a successful rebate claim.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit your rebate claim" link to access the form.
2. Review the instructions and fields carefully before starting to fill out the form.
3. Fill out all required fields with accurate and complete information.
4. Provide a valid receipt upload link for the purchase.
5. Select the rebate payment method you prefer.
6. Review and agree to the terms and conditions.
7. Submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Black Friday Rebate Submission Form**: This is the introduction page of the form, explaining its purpose and the process of submitting a rebate claim.
* **First Name**: Please enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address for rebate confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact number for communication and updates.
* **Address Line 1**: Enter your street address for delivery purposes.
* **Address Line 2**: Enter your apartment or suite number (if applicable).
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State**: Enter the state where you reside.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter the postal code for your location.
* **Purchase Date**: Enter the date you made the purchase.
* **Store Name**: Enter the store where you made the purchase.
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product purchased.
* **Model Number**: Enter the product model or SKU number.
* **Serial Number**: Enter the product serial number.
* **Purchase Price**: Enter the amount you paid for the product.
* **Rebate Amount Claimed**: Enter the rebate amount from the offer.
* **Receipt Number**: Enter the transaction or receipt number.
* **Receipt Upload URL**: Enter the link to the uploaded receipt image.
* **UPC Code**: Enter the product barcode number.
* **Rebate Payment Method**: Choose how you want to receive your rebate (Prepaid Card, Check by Mail, Direct Deposit, or Store Credit).
* **Terms Agreement**: Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the rebate process.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional information about your submission.
