# Special Offers Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Offers Sign Up Form is designed to collect information from users to provide them with relevant special offers. Please fill out the form carefully, and only select the options that apply to you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Sign Up" option.
2. Fill out the "Contact Info" section with your name and email address.
3. Choose the options that apply to you from the "Options" section.
4. Select the date and time you would like to receive the special offer.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" section.
6. Choose which types of communications you would like to receive from us (Newsletters, Blogs, or Social Media).
7. Confirm your email address and phone number.
8. Confirm your selected options.
9. Click the "Submit" button to complete your sign up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sign Up** (`1`, `sign_up_page`, required: false): This is the start of the form where you will select the option to sign up for the special offer.
* **Contact Info** (`2`, `contact_form`, required: false): Please fill out your name and email address in this section. This is the primary contact information that we will use to communicate with you.
* **Name** (`3`, `name`, required: false): Enter your name to help us address you personally.
* **Email** (`4`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you via email.
* **Phone** (`5`, `phone`, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you via phone.
* **Options** (`6`, `select_option`, required: false): Choose from the list of options that apply to you. These options are:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Date** (`7`, `date`, required: false): Select the date you would like to receive the special offer.
* **Time** (`8`, `time`, required: false): Select the time you would like to receive the special offer.
* **Note** (`9`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments you would like to share with us.
* **Newsletter** (`10`, `newsletter`, required: false): Choose the types of communications you would like to receive:
	+ Newsletters
	+ Blogs
	+ Social Media
* **Confirm Email** (`11`, `confirm_email`, required: false): Re-enter your email address to confirm it.
* **Confirm Phone** (`12`, `confirm_phone`, required: false): Re-enter your phone number to confirm it.
* **Confirm Note** (`13`, `confirm_note`, required: false): Re-enter any additional notes or comments you would like to share with us.
* **Confirm Newsletter** (`14`, `confirm_newsletter`, required: false): Re-enter the types of communications you would like to receive:
	+ Newsletters
	+ Blogs
	+ Social Media
* **Submit** (`15`, `submit`, required: false): Click this button to complete your sign up for the special offer.
