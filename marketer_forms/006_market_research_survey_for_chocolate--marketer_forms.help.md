# Market Research Survey For Chocolate - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather market research data about consumer behavior and preferences for chocolate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Market Research Survey For Chocolate (1)**: This is a placeholder title. You can enter a title for the survey, and it will appear at the top of the page.
* **Chocolate Preferences (2)**: Select the type of chocolate you prefer. This can be Chocolate, Vanilla, Strawberry, Orange, or Other (if you have a different preference not listed).
* **Purchasing Habits (3)**: This is a text area for you to describe your purchasing habits regarding chocolate. You can provide as much or as little information as you like.
* **Demographic Info (4)**: Select all that apply to describe your demographic information. You can choose from Age, Gender, Income, Marital Status, or Occupation.
* **Purchasing Frequency (5)**: Enter the frequency with which you purchase chocolate. This can be a numerical value (e.g., 1-5 times a week, 1-2 times a month, etc.).
* **How Often (6)**: Enter the date on which you last purchased chocolate.
* **Chocolate Purchasing Frequency (7)**: This is a time input field for you to indicate how often you purchase chocolate.
* **Preferred Type (8)**: Select the type of chocolate you prefer. This can be Dark, Milk, White, or Other.
* **Preferred Brand (9)**: Select the brand of chocolate you prefer. This can be Brand A, Brand B, Brand C, or Other.
* **Contact Info (10)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (11)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Comments (12)**: If you have any additional comments or concerns, please enter them here.
* **Conducted By (13)**: Select who conducted this survey. This can be John, Jane, Jim, or Other.
* **Organized By (14)**: Select who organized this survey. This can be Mark, Sarah, Mike, or Other.

Please note that all fields are optional, and you can skip any questions that don't apply to you. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask. Thank you for taking the time to complete this market research survey for chocolate!
