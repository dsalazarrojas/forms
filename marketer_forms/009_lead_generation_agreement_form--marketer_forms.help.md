# Lead Generation Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lead Generation Agreement Form is used to collect information from leads, including their contact information, lead source, and lead type. This form is typically used by marketing teams to gather data on potential customers and track their progress through the sales process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title with a brief description of the lead generation campaign.
2. Enter the company name and address in the respective fields.
3. Provide your contact information, including your phone number and email address.
4. Agree to the terms and conditions of the lead generation process.
5. Select the lead source and type from the provided options.
6. Optionally, select additional lead sources and types.
7. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the lead generation campaign.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company.
* **Legal Name** (`legal_name`, text, optional): Enter the legal name of the company.
* **Address 1** (`address1`, text, optional): Enter the first address line.
* **Address 2** (`address2`, text, optional): Enter the second address line.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Agreement Terms** (`agreement_terms`, text, optional): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the lead generation process.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the company.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where the company is located.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state where the company is located.
* **ZIP** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of the company's location.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This is the second email field, which is optional.
* **Agreement Terms 2** (`agreement_terms_2`, text, optional): Read and agree to additional terms and conditions.
* **Terms & Conditions** (`terms_conditions`, text, optional): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the lead generation process.
* **Lead Information** (`lead_info`, text, optional): Enter lead information.
* **Lead Source** (`lead_source`, select_one, optional): Select the source of the lead.
* **Lead Type** (`lead_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of lead.
* **Lead Status** (`lead_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the lead.
* **Lead Source 1** (`lead_source_1`, select_one, optional): Select an additional lead source.
* **Lead Source 2** (`lead_source_2`, select_one, optional): Select another lead source.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to submit the form.
