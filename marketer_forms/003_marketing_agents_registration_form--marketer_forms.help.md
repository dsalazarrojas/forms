# Marketing Agents Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Agents Registration Form is designed to collect information from marketing agents who wish to register with the company. This form helps the company gather essential details about the agent's agency, contact person, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Agency Name field with your marketing agency's name.
2. Enter the contact person's name in the Contact Person field.
3. Provide your email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
5. Enter your address in the Address field.
6. Select your city from the City dropdown menu.
7. Choose your state from the State dropdown menu.
8. Enter your zip code in the Zip Code field.
9. Provide the URL of your company's website in the Website field.
10. Describe your marketing agency in the Description field.
11. Upload your company logo in the Logo field.
12. Upload any other relevant image in the Image field.
13. Choose the category of your marketing agency from the Category dropdown menu.
14. Select the subcategory of your marketing agency from the Subcategory dropdown menu.
15. Choose your country from the Country dropdown menu.
16. Select your time zone from the Time Zone dropdown menu.
17. Enter any additional notes about your agency in the Notes field.
18. Choose your agreement status from the Agreement dropdown menu.
19. Confirm that you have read and agreed to the Terms and Conditions by selecting the correct option.
20. Confirm that you have accepted the terms of service by selecting the correct option.
21. Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions of this form.
22. Confirm the date of your acceptance of these terms.
23. Enter the date you confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.

* **Agency Name (Text)**: Enter the name of your marketing agency.
* **Contact Person (Text)**: Enter the name of the contact person for your marketing agency.

* **Zip Code (Number)**: Enter the zip code of your marketing agency's location.
* **Website (Text)**: Enter the URL of your marketing agency's website.
* **Social Media (Text)**: Enter any social media handle or link associated with your marketing agency.
* **Description (Text)**: Describe your marketing agency and its services.
* **Logo (Text)**: Upload an image of your marketing agency's logo.
* **Image (Text)**: Upload any other relevant image associated with your marketing agency.
