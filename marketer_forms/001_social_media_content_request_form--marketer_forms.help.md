# social_media_content_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The social media content request form is for marketers to request social media content from designated persons.
## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the social media channel for your post: Enter the social media platform where you'd like your post to be published.
2. Describe your social media post: Provide a brief description of the type of post you're requesting.
3. Specify your target audience: Choose the platform-specific audience you're targeting with your post.
4. Enter your name and department: Please provide your name and department information for contact purposes.
5. Provide your contact information: Enter your email address and a phone number for us to contact you.
6. Add any comments or instructions: If you have any specific requirements or requests for your post, please add them here.
7. Choose your preferred post time: Select a time for the post to be published.
8. (Optional) Specify the post type: If you want to request a specific post type, please select it here.
9. Set a priority level: You can prioritize your request for faster processing.
10. Verify your email address: Enter your email address to be contacted and to confirm your identity.
11. Confirm your phone number (if applicable): Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted via phone.
12. Add any additional comments: If you have any comments or requests, add them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **social_media_channel** (required):
	+ The social media platform you'd like your post to be published on.
	+ Example: Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, etc.
* **social_media_post** (required):
	+ Brief description of the type of post you're requesting (e.g., photo, video, story, etc.).
	+ Example: "We want a photo posted on Instagram with a specific hashtag."
* **content_type** (required):
	+ Specify the content type (text, photo, video, etc.) you'd like to be published.
	+ Example: "Text, photo, or video post."
* **target_audience** (required):
	+ The platform-specific audience you're targeting with your post.
	+ Example: General public, Facebook users, Twitter followers, etc.
* **content_requester** (required):
	+ Your name for contact purposes.
	+ Example: Your name.
* **contact_method** (required):
	+ Method you'd like to be contacted (phone, email, text message, etc.).
	+ Example: Email, phone, or text message.
* **social_media_post_type** (optional):
	+ Select the type of post you'd like to be published.
	+ Example: Photo, video, story, etc.
* **preferred_post_time** (required):
	+ Time when you'd like the post to be published.
	+ Format: 24-hour clock (e.g., 14:00 for 2 PM).
* **priority** (optional):
	+ Level of priority for faster processing.
	+ Example: Normal, High, Low.
* **content_requestor_email** (required):
	+ Your email address for contact purposes.
	+ Format: [email address].
* **content_requestor_phone** (optional):
	+ Your phone number for contact purposes.
	+ Example: 123-456-1234.
* **content_requestor_comments** (required):
	+ Additional comments or requests for your post.
	+ Example: "Please make sure it includes this hashtag."
* **content_requestor_department** (required):
	+ Your department information for contact purposes.
	+ Example: Marketing, Sales, etc.
* **content_requestor_phone** (optional):
	+ Your phone number for contact purposes.
	+ Example: 123-456-1234.
* **content_requestor_comments** (optional):
	+ Additional comments or requests.
	+ Example: "Please make sure it includes this specific request."
