# get_in_touch_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who want to get in touch with your contact or support team. Fill it out to send a message with your name, email address, subject, and any additional comments or questions you have.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your **first name** (text field) to share your name with the contact or support team.
2. Type in your **email** (email field) for the contact or support team to respond to you directly.
3. Briefly describe the **subject** (text field) of your message.
4. Share your **message** (text field) with the contact or support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Your name, which will be used to personalize the response from the contact or support team.
* **Email**: A valid email address to which the contact or support team will respond to.
* **Subject**: A brief description of the topic or reason for your message.
* **Message**: A detailed description of your question, comment, or request. This is the main body of your message.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out all required fields (if any) to ensure that your message is sent correctly.
* Use a clear and concise subject line to help the contact or support team quickly understand your inquiry.
* Keep your message brief and to the point, but be as detailed as needed to provide context for your question or request.
