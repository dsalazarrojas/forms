# Referral Program Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track referrals from clients or customers who have been referred to a program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the referral source's name in the "Referral's Name" field.
2. Enter the referral source's email address in the "Referral's Email" field.
3. Enter the referral source's phone number in the "Referral's Phone Number" field.
4. Enter the client's name in the "Client's Name" field.
5. Enter the client's email address in the "Client's Email" field.
6. Enter the client's phone number in the "Client's Phone Number" field.
7. Select the type of referral the client came from (e.g. social media, online ad, in-person) from the "Referral Type" field.
8. Select the status of the referral (e.g. active, inactive, pending) from the "Program Status" field.
9. Select the frequency of the referral (e.g. one-time, recurring) from the "Frequency" field.
10. Select all the channels that the referral came from (e.g. email, phone, social media) from the "Channels" field.
11. (Optional) Enter any additional comments or notes about the referral in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referral's Name** (`referral_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the referral source.
* **Referral's Email** (`referral_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the referral source.
* **Referral's Phone Number** (`referral_number`, number, required): Enter the phone number of the referral source.
* **Client's Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the client who was referred.
* **Client's Email** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the client who was referred.
* **Client's Phone Number** (`client_number`, number, required): Enter the phone number of the client who was referred.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the referral (not required).
* **Referral Type** (`referral_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of referral the client came from (e.g. social media, online ad, in-person).
* **Program Status** (`program_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the referral (e.g. active, inactive, pending).
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, select_one, required): Select the frequency of the referral (e.g. one-time, recurring).
* **Channels** (`channels`, select_multiple, required): Select all the channels that the referral came from (e.g. email, phone, social media).
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the referral (not required).

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field or have questions, contact your supervisor or a colleague for assistance.
