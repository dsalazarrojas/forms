# internet_usage_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user data about their internet usage habits and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Read the introduction to understand the purpose of the form.
2.  Select one of the following for "internet service usage" from the dropdown menu.
3.  Choose how often you use the internet from the options in the dropdown menu.
4.  For "How long do you spend on the internet each day", please use the provided time format to enter your duration.
5.  Select the most common activity you perform on the internet from the provided list.
6.  Choose your age from the given options.
7.  Choose your preferred contact methods for communication from the provided options.
8.  If you have any additional comments or feedback, type it in the text field provided.
9.  Indicate whether you agree to the survey terms and conditions by selecting either "Agree", "Disagree" or "Neutral".
10.  Choose whether you consent to data collection by selecting "Agree", "Disagree" or "Neutral".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction**: Please read this section to understand the purpose of the form.
*   **internet_service_usage**: Select the type of internet service you use.
    *   Mobile
    *   Desktop
    *   Laptop
    *   Other
*   **internet_service_frequency**: Choose how often you use the internet.
    *   Daily
    *   Several times a week
    *   Occasionally
    *   Rarely
    *   Never
*   **internet_service_length**: This field should be filled in a time format (e.g., "2 hours" or "1 day").
*   **most_common_internet_activity**: Select your most common internet activity.
    *   Social media
    *   Email
    *   Online shopping
    *   Streaming media
    *   Other
*   **demographic_age**: Choose your age group.
    *   18-24
    *   25-34
    *   35-44
    *   45-54
    *   55+
*   **contact_information**: Choose your preferred contact method.
    *   Email
    *   Phone
    *   Text message
    *   Other
*   **internet_usage_survey_comments**: Add any comments or feedback.
*   **internet_usage_survey_agreement**: Indicate your opinion about the survey terms.
    *   Agree
    *   Disagree
    *   Neutral
*   **consent**: Indicate whether you consent to data collection.
    *   Agree
    *   Disagree
    *   Neutral

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields.
*   Use the correct time format for "internet_service_length".
*   Select only one option for "internet_service_frequency" and "most_common_internet_activity" and "contact_information".
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the survey administrator.
