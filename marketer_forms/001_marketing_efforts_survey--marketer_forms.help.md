# Marketing Efforts Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Marketing Efforts Survey is a tool for marketing professionals to track and evaluate their efforts. It provides an overview of marketing strategies and their effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each section as thoroughly as possible, providing detailed information about your marketing efforts.
2. Be honest and accurate in your responses to ensure reliable data.
3. Make sure all required fields are filled out.
4. Review your answers for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Marketing Efforts
* **Marketing Efforts** (`marketing_efforts_survey_form_1`, text, required): Describe your marketing efforts, including the strategies and tactics used.
### Efforts Description
* **Efforts description** (`marketing_efforts_survey_form_2`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of your marketing efforts.
### Marketing Goal
* **Marketing goal** (`marketing_efforts_survey_form_3`, select_one, not required): Select whether the goal of your effort was 'Yes' or 'No'.
