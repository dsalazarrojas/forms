# Lip Balm Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is for users who are completing the Lip Balm Survey form. The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback and opinions from users about lip balm products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your contact email address in the "contact_email" field.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the "contact_phone" field.
5. Select your preferred lip balm type from the options provided in the "balm_type" field.
6. Choose your favourite lip balm product from the options provided in the "favourite_balm" field.
7. Select any important features of a lip balm that you value (e.g. Moisturizing, Fragrance, Protection, etc.) in the "important_feature" field.
8. If you have any other features that are important to you, enter them in the "other_feature" field.
9. Rate your opinion of lip balm products on a scale of 0-10 in the "opinion" field.
10. Rate how much you like the lip balm product with your favourite feature on a scale of 0-10 in the "balm_opinion" field.
11. Rate how much you think the lip balm product with your favourite feature is important on a scale of 0-10 in the "opinion_important" field.
12. Select how important it is to have a lip balm product with a certain feature on a scale of 0-3 in the "important_score" field.
13. Indicate if you would recommend the lip balm product to others in the "would_recommend" field.
14. Select any lip balm products that you would recommend in the "recommendation" field.
15. If you have any other comments about lip balm products, enter them in the "comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **first_name** (`survey_first_name`, text, required/optional): 
Enter your first name.

### **last_name** (`survey_last_name`, text, required/optional): 
Enter your last name.

### **contact_email** (`survey_email`, email, required/optional): 
Enter your contact email address.

### **contact_phone** (`survey_phone`, text, required/optional): 
Enter your contact phone number.

### **balm_type** (`survey_balm_type`, select_one, required/optional): 
Select your preferred lip balm type. Options are:
   * Lip Balm
   * Lip Gloss
   * Lipstick
   * Lip Balm with SPF
   * Lip Balm without SPF
   * Lip Gloss with SPF
   * Lipstick with SPF

### **favourite_balm** (`survey_favourite_balm`, select_multiple, required/optional): 
Choose your favourite lip balm product. Options are:
   * Lip Balm
   * Lip Gloss
   * Lipstick
   * Lip Balm with SPF
   * Lip Balm without SPF
   * Lip Gloss with SPF
   * Lipstick with SPF

### **important_feature** (`survey_important_feature`, select_multiple, required/optional): 
Select any important features of a lip balm product. Options are:
   * Moisturizing
   * Fragrance
   * Protection
   * Flavour
   * Texture
   * Other

### **other_feature** (`survey_important_feature_other`, text, required/optional): 
If there is an important feature of a lip balm product that is not listed, enter it here.

### **opinion** (`survey_opinion`, number, required/optional): 
Rate your opinion of lip balm products on a scale of 0-10.

### **balm_opinion** (`survey_opinion_balm`, number, required/optional): 
Rate how much you like the lip balm product with your favourite feature on a scale of 0-10.

### **opinion_important** (`survey_opinion_important`, number, required/optional): 
Rate how important the lip balm product with your favourite feature is on a scale of 0-10.

### **important_score** (`survey_important_score`, select_one, required/optional): 
Select how important it is to have a lip balm product with a certain feature on a scale of 0-3. Options are:
   * 1 - 2
   * 3 - 5

### **would_recommend** (`survey_would_recommend`, select_one, required/optional): 
Indicate if you would recommend the lip balm product to others. Options are:
   * True
   * False

### **recommendation** (`survey_recommendation`, select_multiple, required/optional): 
Select any lip balm products that you would recommend. Options are:
   * Lip Balm
   * Lip Gloss
   * Lipstick
   * Lip Balm with SPF
   * Lip Balm without SPF
   * Lip Gloss with SPF
   * Lipstick with SPF

### **other** (`survey_other`, text, required/optional): 
If there is anything else you would like to comment on, enter it here.

### **comments** (`survey_comments`, text, required/optional): 
Enter any additional comments about lip balm products.

### **comments_other** (`survey_comments_other`, text, required/optional):

### **comments_other_why** (`survey_comments_other_why`, text, required/optional):
