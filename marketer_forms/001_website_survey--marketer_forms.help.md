# Website Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Website Survey form is designed to collect feedback from users to help us improve our website. We want to hear your honest opinions, experiences, and suggestions to provide a better service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and answer each question honestly.
2. For each question, provide your opinion or answer as accurately as possible.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, type them in the corresponding field.
4. If you want to provide your email address, fill in your email in the "email" field (this is optional).
5. Finally, click "Submit" to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **What do you think about our website** (`website_survey`, text, optional)
This field is where you can share your overall opinion about our website. Please be honest and provide your thoughts on what you like or dislike about our website.

### 2. **Email** (`website_survey_email`, email, optional)
If you want to stay in touch or be contacted about your feedback, fill in your email address here. This is optional and your email address will only be used for this purpose.

### 3. **What is your experience on our website** (`website_survey_experience`, text, optional)
Describe your experience on our website, including any positive or negative feelings you had while visiting.

### 4. **What could we improve** (`website_survey_improve`, text, optional)
If you have any suggestions on how we can improve our website, type them here.

### 5. **Rate our website** (`website_survey_rate_our_website`, number, optional)
Rate our website from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

### 6. **Any suggestions or comments** (`website_survey_suggestions`, text, optional)
If you have any other comments or suggestions, type them here.

### 7. **How did you get here?** (`website_survey_contact_us`, text, optional)
Tell us how you found our website, so we can understand our audience better.

Note: The required fields are not present in this form, so all fields are optional.
