# gift_card_giveaway_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for a gift card giveaway. Please complete the form accurately to ensure your entry is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter the value of the gift card you would like to receive in the "Gift Card Value" field.
4. Indicate your interest in participating in the gift card contest by selecting "True" in the "Would you like to participate in the gift card contest" field.
5. If you have any additional information or comments, use the "Additional Info" field to provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (Text, Required)**: Enter your first name.
* **Email (Email, Required)**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Gift Card Value (Number, Required)**: Enter the value of the gift card you would like to receive.
* **Would you like to participate in the gift card contest (Select One, Required)**: Select "True" if you are interested in participating in the contest.
* **Additional Info (Note, Optional)**: Use this field to provide any additional context or comments about your entry.
