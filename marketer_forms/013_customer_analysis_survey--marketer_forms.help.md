# Customer Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Analysis Survey is designed to gather data on customers' behaviors, purchase frequencies, preferences, and satisfaction levels. By completing this survey, you will help us better understand your habits and preferences, which will enable us to improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please answer all questions thoroughly and honestly.
2. For questions with multiple options, choose the answer that best describes your habits and preferences.
3. If you have any additional comments, please use the "Additional Comments" field at the end of the form.
4. Once you have completed the form, click the "Submit" button to send your responses to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Behaviors** (`customer_behaviors`, select_multiple, not required): This question asks if you engage in certain behaviors related to our product or service. Please select all behaviors that apply to you.
* **Purchase Frequency** (`purchase_frequency`, select_one, not required): This question asks about how often you purchase our product or service. Please select the option that best describes your purchase frequency.
* **Customer Preferences** (`customer_preferences`, select_multiple, not required): This question asks about your preferences related to our product or service. Please select all preferences that apply to you.
* **Purchase Amount** (`purchase_amount`, number, not required): This question asks about the amount you typically purchase from us. Please enter a numerical value.
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, number, not required): This question asks about your level of satisfaction with our product or service. Please enter a numerical value between 1 and 10.
* **Age** (`age`, number, not required): This question asks about your age. Please enter a numerical value.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): This question asks for your email address. Please enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): This question asks for your phone number. Please enter a valid phone number.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, not required): This question allows you to provide any additional comments or feedback about our product or service. Please use this field to share any thoughts or suggestions you may have.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your responses.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* We value your feedback and will use it to improve our products and services.
