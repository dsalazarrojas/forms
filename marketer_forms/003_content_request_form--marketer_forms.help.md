# Content Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential details about your content request, ensuring that the content you create meets your needs and expectations. By filling out this form, you'll provide our team with the necessary information to craft high-quality content that resonates with your target audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your **Requester Name** (`requester_name`). This field is a required field and should be completed with your full name.
2. Enter your **Email Address** (`email_address`). Your contact email will be used to reach out to you for any questions or follow-ups.
3. Select your **Department** (`department`). Choose from the following options: Marketing, Sales, Product, Customer Success, Executive, or Other.
4. Enter the date you need the content by (`request_date`). Use the date format MM/DD/YY.
5. Choose the type of content you need (`content_type`). Select from: Blog Post, Whitepaper, Case Study, Social Media Post, Email Newsletter, Video Script, Infographic, or Landing Page Copy.
6. Define the main subject or theme of your content (`content_topic`). Enter a brief description of the topic.
7. Identify your **Target Audience** (`target_audience`). Choose from: Existing Customers, Prospects, Industry Professionals, General Public, Internal Team, or Partners.
8. (Optional) Estimate the **Word Count** (`word_count`) of the content you need. Choose from: Under 500 words, 500-1000 words, 1000-2000 words, 2000-3000 words, or 3000+ words.
9. Provide the **Key Messages** (`key_messages`) you want to convey in the content. Enter a brief description of the main points.
10. (Optional) Enter any **SEO Keywords** (`seo_keywords`) you'd like us to incorporate.
11. Define the **Call to Action** (`call_to_action`) you want readers to take.
12. (Optional) Provide any **Reference Materials** (`reference_materials`) we may need for research.
13. Choose the desired **Tone and Style** (`tone_style`) of the content: Professional, Conversational, Technical, Persuasive, Educational, or Entertaining.
14. Set the final due date for the project (`deadline`). Use the date format MM/DD/YY.
15. (Optional) Enter an internal **Budget Code** (`budget_code`) reference (if applicable).
16. Finally, add any **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`) you'd like us to consider while creating the content.
