## Black Friday Limited Time Offer Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect details about a Black Friday limited time offer, including business contact information and offer details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Provide your **Business name** (Field 2) to inform customers about who is hosting the offer.
2. Enter your **Contact email** (Field 3) so customers can get in touch with you about the offer.
3. Enter a **Offer title** (Field 4) that is short and descriptive, making it easy for customers to understand the promotion.
4. Provide a detailed **Offer description** (Field 5) that explains the offer terms and conditions.
5. Choose the **Offer start date** (Field 6) and **Offer end date** (Field 7) when the offer begins and ends.
6. Select the **Eligible products** (Field 8) that are included in the offer using the provided options.
7. Choose how customers **redeem this offer** (Field 9) using the provided options.
8. If necessary, add any **Internal notes** (Field 10) for campaign management.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business name** (Field 2): This is the name of the company hosting the offer.
* **Contact email** (Field 3): This is the email address customers can use to get in touch with you about the offer.
* **Offer title** (Field 4): This should be a short and descriptive title of the offer, making it easy for customers to understand.
* **Offer description** (Field 5): This is where you can provide detailed information about the offer, including any conditions or restrictions.
* **Offer start date** (Field 6): This is when the offer begins, in date format.
* **Offer end date** (Field 7): This is when the offer ends, in date format.
* **Eligible products** (Field 8): Select the categories of products that are included in the offer using the provided options.
* **Redemption method** (Field 9): This is how customers will redeem the offer, using one of the provided options.
* **Internal notes** (Field 10): These are additional notes for campaign management, if needed.

## Tips

- Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to effectively communicate the offer to customers.
- Double-check the dates and product categories to ensure they are correct.
- Use the internal notes field for any additional instructions or reminders for campaign management.
