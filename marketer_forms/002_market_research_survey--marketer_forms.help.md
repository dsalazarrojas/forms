# Market Research Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect market research data from customers to help us better understand their experiences, needs, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your honest answers.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and complete information.
3. If you are not sure about any field, try to answer based on your best knowledge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information**: Please provide your name, job title, and company name.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Demographics**: Choose one option that best describes your demographic characteristics.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: No
* **Company Overview**: Briefly describe your company overview.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Contact Details**: Select all applicable options to provide your contact information.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: No
* **Customer Service**: This field is for providing feedback about our customer service.
	+ Type: note
	+ Required: No
* **Product Info**: Select all applicable options to provide information about our products.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: No
* **Company History**: Choose one option to provide information about our company history.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: No
* **Additional Comments**: Share any additional comments or suggestions you might have.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
