# Responsive Wordpress Sidebar Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information for marketing or business purposes, such as lead generation, appointment scheduling, or customer communication. It is intended to be used in a Wordpress platform with a responsive layout, making it accessible on various devices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email, and phone number to provide contact information.
2. Select your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, Text, or Mail).
3. Choose your favorite color (Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, or Purple).
4. Indicate your availability for an appointment (Option A or Option B).
5. Select your preferred time for a meeting (8-12am, 1-2pm, 3-4pm, 5-6pm, 7-8pm, 9-10pm, 11pm-12am).
6. Choose your preferred time zone (UTC, EST, CST, PST, MST).
7. Select your preferred meeting day (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (field_1)**: Enter your name to identify yourself.
* **Email (field_2)**: Enter your email address to allow us to contact you.
* **Phone number (field_3)**: Enter your phone number for us to reach you.
* **Date of birth (field_4)**: Enter your date of birth for identification purposes.
* **Wake-up time (field_5)**: Select the time you wake up.
* **Favorite color (field_6)**: Choose your favorite color among Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, or Purple.
* **Are you available for an appointment? (field_7)**: Check this box if you are available for an appointment.
* **What is your preferred contact method? (field_8)**: Select how you would like us to contact you (Email, Phone, Text, or Mail).
* **What is the best time for you to meet? (field_9)**: Choose the time that suits you best.
* **What is your preferred time zone? (field_10)**: Select your preferred time zone (UTC, EST, CST, PST, MST).
* **What is the best day for you to meet? (field_11)**: Choose the day that suits you best.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- Be aware that the form's responsive design may adapt to different screen sizes, but it's still important to fill in each field carefully.
- If you have any issues with the form, please contact us for assistance.
