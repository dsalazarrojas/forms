# Client Outreach Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Outreach Form is a questionnaire designed to gather essential information about your business needs and preferences. This information will help us understand your marketing goals, challenges, and interests, enabling us to provide personalized guidance and solutions tailored to your business needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Job Title, and Company Name. These are all required fields and will help us verify your identity and company information.
2. Select your company size, industry, and current marketing budget. This information will help us understand your company's scale and potential marketing needs.
3. Choose the marketing challenges you are currently facing and the services you are interested in. This will help us determine the most suitable solutions for your business.
4. Select your preferred contact method and best time to contact you. This will ensure we can reach out to you efficiently and effectively.
5. Provide any additional comments or questions you may have. This is an optional field where you can share more about your specific needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your business card or email signature.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your business card or email signature.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position at your company.
* **Company Name**: Enter the full name of your company.
* **Company Size**: Select the number of employees at your company (e.g., 1-10, 11-50, etc.).
* **Industry**: Choose the industry your company operates in (e.g., Technology, Healthcare, Finance, etc.).
* **Business Email**: Enter your business email address.
* **Current Challenges**: Select all the marketing challenges you are currently facing (e.g., Lead Generation, Brand Awareness, etc.).
* **Current Marketing Budget**: Choose your current monthly marketing budget (e.g., Under $1000, $1000-$5000, etc.).
* **Decision Maker**: Indicate if you are the primary decision maker for marketing at your company.
* **Timeline**: Select when you are looking to start working with our agency (e.g., Immediately, Within 1 month, etc.).
* **Previous Agencies**: Indicate if you have worked with marketing agencies before.
* **Services Interested**: Select all the services you are interested in (e.g., Strategy Consulting, Content Marketing, etc.).
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: Choose how you heard about our agency (e.g., Referral, LinkedIn, etc.).
* **Referral Source**: If you selected "Referral" as the way you heard about us, enter the name or company of the person who referred you.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or questions you may have about your business needs.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose your preferred method of contact (e.g., Email, Phone Call, etc.).
* **Best Time to Contact**: Select the best time for us to contact you (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, etc.).
