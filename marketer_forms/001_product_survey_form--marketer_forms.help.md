# Product Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with your product. The information collected will help us understand our customers' satisfaction, impression of competition, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the relevant options for each question based on your experience with the product.
3. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
4. If you have any comments or additional feedback, please use the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How long have you been using** (`how_long_have_you_been_using`, number, optional): This question is designed to understand how long you have been using our product. Please enter the number of months or years you have been using it.
* **How do you compete** (`how_do_you_compete`, select_one, optional): This question is designed to understand how you typically compete in the market. Please select one of the options: Compete with others or Don't compete.
* **Impression of competition** (`impression_of_competition`, select_multiple, optional): This question is designed to understand how you perceive the competition in the market. Please select all that apply: Stronger, Weaker, or Similar.
* **Satisfaction of product** (`satisfaction_of_product`, select_multiple, optional): This question is designed to understand your satisfaction with our product. Please select all that apply: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, or Not Satisfied.
* **Impression of service** (`impression_of_service`, select_multiple, optional): This question is designed to understand your impression of our service. Please select all that apply: Yes or No.
* **Overall experience** (`overall_experience`, note, optional): This question is designed for any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our product. Please provide any relevant details you have.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): This question is also designed for any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our product. Please provide any relevant details you have.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This question is designed to collect your email address for any follow-up or further communication.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): This question is designed to collect your phone number for any follow-up or further communication.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
