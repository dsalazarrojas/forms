# Social Media Onboarding Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from new team members to ensure a smooth onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your social media management platforms from the list.
2. Fill in your personal details: first name, last name, and email.
3. Provide your phone number.
4. Enter your job title and social media management experience.
5. Select your work experience.
6. Choose your education level.
7. Indicate whether you are available for relocations.
8. Select the languages you speak.
9. Specify your work authorization and visa type.
10. Enter your visa expiration date and date of birth.
11. Provide an emergency contact and their phone number (if applicable).
12. Indicate whether you have previous work experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Social Media Management Platforms**: Please select all the social media platforms you have experience with.
	+ Facebook
	+ Twitter
	+ LinkedIn
	+ Instagram
	+ Pinterest
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Social Media Management Experience**: Please enter a brief description of your experience in social media management.
* **Work Experience**: Select from the options whether you have experience in content creation, content curation, or social media management.
* **Education Level**: Select your highest level of education from the options provided.
* **Available for Relocations**: Indicate whether you are open to relocations.
* **Languages**: Select all the languages you speak.
	+ English
	+ Spanish
	+ Mandarin
	+ Arabic
	+ French
* **Work Authorization**: Select your work authorization from the options provided.
	+ Work Permit
	+ Work Visa
	+ Green Card
* **Visa Type**: Select your visa type.
	+ Single-Entry Visa
	+ Multiple Entry Visa
* **Visa Expiration Date**: Enter your visa expiration date.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name of your emergency contact (if applicable).
* **Emergency Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact (if applicable).
* **Previous Work Experience**: Indicate whether you have previous work experience.

## Tips
- Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately to ensure a smooth onboarding process.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your team lead or HR representative.
- Please review and update your information regularly to ensure it remains accurate.
