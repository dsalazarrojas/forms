# Campaign Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from respondents to inform our campaign strategies and improve our communication with them.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field with the requested information.
2. Select the correct options for each field, where applicable.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`respondent_first_name`, text, Optional): Enter your first name as you would like to be known.
* **Last Name** (`respondent_last_name`, text, Optional): Enter your last name as you would like to be known.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, Required): Enter the ZIP code of your location.
* **Age Range** (`age_range`, select_one, Required): Select the age range that best fits you.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, Optional): Select the gender that best fits you.
* **Political Affiliation** (`political_affiliation`, select_one, Required): Select your political affiliation.
* **Are You Registered to Vote** (`voter_registration`, select_one, Required): Select whether you are registered to vote.
* **How Likely Are You to Vote** (`voting_likelihood`, select_one, Required): Rate your likelihood of voting.
* **Are You Aware of Our Campaign** (`campaign_awareness`, select_one, Required): Select whether you are aware of our campaign.
* **How Would You Rate Our Campaign Message** (`campaign_message_rating`, select_one, Required): Rate our campaign message from 1 to 5.
* **What Are Your Top Issues** (`top_issues`, select_multiple, Required): Select all the issues that you think are most important for our campaign.
* **How Do You Feel About Our Candidate** (`candidate_approval`, select_one, Required): Rate your approval of our candidate.
* **What Qualities Are Most Important in a Candidate** (`candidate_qualities`, select_multiple, Required): Select the top qualities that are most important to you in a candidate.
* **Have You Been Contacted by Our Campaign** (`campaign_contact`, select_one, Required): Select whether you have been contacted by our campaign.
* **How Were You Contacted** (`contact_method`, select_multiple, Optional): Select all the ways you have been contacted by our campaign.
* **Are You Interested in Volunteering** (`volunteer_interest`, select_one, Required): Select whether you are interested in volunteering.
* **What Volunteer Tasks Interest You** (`volunteer_tasks`, select_multiple, Optional): Select all the volunteer tasks that interest you.
* **Are You Interested in Donating** (`donation_interest`, select_one, Required): Select whether you are interested in donating.
* **How Could Our Campaign Improve** (`campaign_improvement`, text, Optional): Provide any suggestions for our campaign improvement.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, Optional): Any other feedback you have for us.
