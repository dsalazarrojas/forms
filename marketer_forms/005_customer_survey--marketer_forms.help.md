# Customer Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather customer feedback and opinions on our customer service and product. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to better serve our customers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Answer each question based on your actual experience with our product and service.
3. If you need help or have questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Survey Form Data** (`customer_survey_form_data`): This field is where you can provide any additional comments or information about your experience with our product. Please use this space to share any thoughts or concerns you may have.
* **Customer Service Satisfaction** (`customer_service_satisfaction`): Rate your satisfaction with our customer service, on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 10 is very satisfied.
* **Customer Concerns** (`customer_concerns`): Select all that apply from the list of options provided to indicate any concerns you have about our product.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`): Rate your overall satisfaction with our product, on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 10 is very satisfied.
* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`): Rate your experience with our customer service, selecting the option that best describes your opinion.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): If you wish to be contacted regarding your feedback, please provide your email address or other contact information.
* **Email** (`email`): If you want us to reach out to you regarding your feedback, please enter your email address.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact us.
* Your feedback is crucial to helping us improve our customer service and product.
