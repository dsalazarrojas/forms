# Compact Contact Form 2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Compact Contact Form 2 is a simple form designed to gather user contact information and inquiries. It should be used for users to submit general inquiries, request information about products or services, or seek partnership opportunities. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field (if required).
5. Enter the name of your company or organization in the "Company Name" field (if required).
6. Select the subject of your inquiry from the dropdown menu in the "Subject" field.
7. Enter a message in the "Message" field.
8. If desired, select your preferred contact method from the "Preferred Contact Method" dropdown menu.
9. If desired, check the box to receive our newsletter with marketing updates.
10. Finally, click the "Submit" button to send your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name here.
- **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name here.
- **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter your email address here.
- **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, optional): Enter your contact number here (if required).
- **Company Name** (Company Name, text, optional): Enter your organization's name here (if required).
- **Subject** (Subject, select_one, required): Select the subject of your inquiry from the dropdown menu.
  • General Inquiry
  • Product Information
  • Sales Question
  • Support Request
  • Partnership Opportunity
  • Media Inquiry
  • Feedback
  • Other
- **Message** (Message, text, required): Enter a brief message explaining your inquiry.
- **Preferred Contact Method** (Preferred Contact Method, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted.
  • Email
  • Phone
  • Either
- **Would you like to receive our newsletter?** (Would you like to receive our newsletter?, select_one, optional): Check the box if you would like to receive marketing updates.
- **Submission Date** (Submission Date, date, required): This field is automatically populated with today's date.
  Note: This field is not editable and is used for tracking purposes only.
