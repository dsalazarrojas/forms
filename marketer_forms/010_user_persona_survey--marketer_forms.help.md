# User Persona Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about you as a marketer, so we can better understand your needs and preferences when using marketing automation tools.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose your role in the organization from the options provided (Marketing Manager, Sales Manager, Product Manager, IT Manager, or Other).
2. Select your level of experience with marketing tools from the options (Novice, Intermediate, Advanced, Expert, or Other).
3. Indicate how you currently use marketing automation tools from the options (Social media automation, Email marketing, Content creation, Lead generation, or Other).
4. Select your preferred way of communication from the options (Email, Phone call, Messaging app, Video call, or In-person meeting).
5. Enter the number of years you have experience with marketing software.
6. Describe your primary goal when using marketing automation tools.
7. Describe your current pain points when using marketing automation tools.
8. Choose whether you prefer using desktop or mobile apps for marketing automation.
9. Select the features that are most important to you when using marketing automation tools (Personalization, Automation, Integration, Reporting, or Other).
10. Select your age range from the options (18-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55+).
11. Enter your job title.
12. Select your highest level of education completed (High school, Bachelor's degree, Master's degree, Doctoral degree, or Other).
