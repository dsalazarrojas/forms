# subscriber_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather information from subscribers about their preferences and characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Choose how often you subscribe from the options provided in the "How often do you subscribe" section.
2. Provide a brief reason for your subscription in the "Why do you subscribe" text field.
3. Select the channels you subscribe to from the options provided in the "Which channel do you subscribe to" section.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email address" field.
5. Provide your first name in the "First name" field.
6. Provide your last name in the "Last name" field.
7. Enter your company name in the "Company" field.
8. Enter your job title in the "Job title" field.
9. Select the size of your company from the options provided in the "What is the size of your company" section.
10. Select the industry you belong to from the options provided in the "Which industry do you belong to" section.
11. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact email" field.
12. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact phone" field.
13. Enter your company URL in the "Company URL" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you subscribe**: This question asks about your subscription frequency.
* **Why do you subscribe**: This field is for you to provide a brief reason for your subscription.
* **Which channel do you subscribe to**: This question asks you to select the channels you subscribe to. Please choose all that apply.
* **Email address**: This is where you enter your email address.
* **First name**: This is your first name.
* **Last name**: This is your last name.
* **Company**: This is your company name.
* **Job title**: This is your job title.
* **What is the size of your company**: This question asks you to select the size of your company.
* **Which industry do you belong to**: This question asks you to select the industry you belong to. Please choose all that apply.
* **Contact email**: This is your contact email address.
* **Contact phone**: This is your contact phone number.
* **Company URL**: This is your company URL.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
- Please provide accurate and up-to-date information.
- If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to contact us.
