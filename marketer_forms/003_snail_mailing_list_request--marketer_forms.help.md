# snail_mailing_list_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The snail_mailing_list_request form is used to collect information from users who need a snail mailing list. The form is designed to be filled out by users who require a custom mailing list and need to specify the type of form and tool they need.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the General Contact Information page with your name and contact email.
* Select the type of form you need (Marketer Forms or Other).
* Choose the specific tool you need (chatjimmy or Other).
* Enter the name or URL of the output file.
* Enter the list of IDs for the form.
* Select the date of the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name and Contact Email**: (`page_1`, text, required)
Enter your name and contact email to get in touch with us.
* **Type of Form**: (`page_2`, select_one, required)
Choose the type of form you need, either Marketer Forms or Other.
* **Specific Tool Assignment**: (`page_3`, select_one, required)
Select the specific tool you need, either chatjimmy or Other.
* **Output File Name**: (`page_4`, text, required)
Enter the name or URL of the output file.
* **Form IDs**: (`page_5`, text, required)
Enter the list of IDs for the form.
* **Date of Request**: (`page_6`, date, required)
Select the date of the request.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check the type of form and tool you select.
* Enter accurate and clear information in the output file name and form IDs fields.
