# market_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit and manage market survey information, including the survey's title, category, description, target market, survey date, survey time, and survey location. Additionally, it captures contact information for the person submitting the survey and the person or department responsible for receiving the results. The form also asks for information about the desired frequency and method of follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, enter the **Survey Name** field. This is the title of the market survey.
2. Select the **Category** of the survey from the provided options (Advertising, Marketing, or Other).
3. Enter a brief **Description** of the survey (optional).
4. Identify the **Target Market** where the survey will be conducted.
5. Select the **Survey Date** for the survey, using the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
6. Select the **Survey Time**, if applicable.
7. Enter the **Survey Location** where the survey will be conducted.
8. Enter the number of **Results Needed** for the survey.
9. Enter your **Contact Email**, **Contact Phone**, and **Contact Name**.
10. Enter your **Contact Company** and **Contact Job Title**.
11. For the **Results**, enter the contact information for the person or department receiving the results, including **Results Name**, **Results Company**, and **Results Job Title**.
12. Select the desired **Follow-Up** frequency (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly) and method (optional).
