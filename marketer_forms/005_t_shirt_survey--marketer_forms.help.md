# T Shirt Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The T Shirt Survey is a form designed to gather information about customers' preferences and specifications for T Shirts.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely, as each field is important for us to understand your preferences.
* Select the customer type that best describes you.
* Choose your age group and preferred shirt sizes.
* Select your preferred colors, designs, and pocket sizes.
* Choose your preferred pant and shoe sizes.
* Select your preferred shirt style, length, and sleeve style.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Type** (`Customer Type`): Please select the option that best describes you: Male, Female, or Other.
* **Age Group** (`Age Group`): Choose your age group from the list of options: 13-16, 17-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55 or older.
* **Shirt Sizes** (`Shirt Sizes`): Select all the shirt sizes you prefer: Small, Medium, Large, XL.
* **Preferred Color** (`Preferred Color`): Choose all the colors you like: Blue, Red, Green, Other.
* **Preferred Design** (`Preferred Design`): Select all the design types you prefer: Graphic, Text, Logo, Other.
* **Shirt Pocket Size** (`Shirt Pocket Size`): This field is not applicable as there are no options provided.
* **Preferred Pant Size** (`Preferred Pant Size`): Select all the pant sizes you prefer: Small, Medium, Large, XL.
* **Shirt Neck Size** (`Shirt Neck Size`): This field is not applicable as there are no options provided.
* **Sleeve Length** (`Sleeve Length`): Enter the length of your sleeves in inches.
* **Shirt Hem Length** (`Shirt Hem Length`): Select all the hem lengths you prefer: Short, Long, Extra Long.
* **Preferred Shirt Style** (`Preferred Shirt Style`): Choose all the shirt styles you prefer: Casual, Formal.
* **Preferred Shoe Size** (`Preferred Shoe Size`): Enter your preferred shoe size in inches.
* **Shirt Length** (`Shirt Length`): Select all the shirt lengths you prefer: Short, Long.
* **Sleeve Style** (`Sleeve Style`): Select all the sleeve styles you prefer: Short, Long, Extra Long.
