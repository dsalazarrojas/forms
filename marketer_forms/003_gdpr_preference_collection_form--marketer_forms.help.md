# GDPR Preference Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your preferences regarding data collection 
and usage. Please fill out this form to indicate your preferences 
regarding marketing, analytics, chatbots, push notifications, cookies, 
tracking, location services, contact methods, social media, and 
newsletters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each field and select your 
preferences from the provided options. Make sure to fill out all the 
fields, especially if they are marked as required. If you are unsure 
about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **I want to be in touch with you**: This field is a note field where 
  you can provide any comments or preferences you have that don't fit 
  into any of the other categories.
* **email**: Enter your email address if you want to be in touch with us 
  through email.
* **Marketing**: Select any marketing preferences you have, such as:
  - Newsletters: Receive regular emails from us about new content, 
    promotions, or events.
  - Promotions: Receive promotional emails or offers from us.
  - Surveys: Participate in surveys or polls to help us improve our 
    services.
  - Newsletters and promotional emails: Receive both regular news and 
    promotional emails.
* **Analytics**: Select whether you want us to use analytics to track 
  your interactions on our website or application. Options are:
  - Yes: Allow us to use analytics to track your behavior.
  - No: Do not allow us to use analytics.
* **Chatbot**: Select whether you want to interact with our chatbot. 
  Options are:
  - Yes: Allow chatbot interactions.
  - No: Do not allow chatbot interactions.
* **Push notifications**: Select whether you want to receive push 
  notifications from us. Options are:
  - Yes: Receive push notifications.
  - No: Do not receive push notifications.
* **Cookies**: Select which cookies you are comfortable with. Options are:
  - Cookies: Allow us to use cookies to improve our services.
  - Local storage: Allow local storage to improve our services.
* **Tracking**: Select which tracking methods you are comfortable with. 
  Options are:
  - Tracking: Allow us to track your behavior on our website or 
    application.
  - Advertising: Allow us to use tracking for advertising 
    purposes.
* **Location services**: Select whether you want to share your location 
  with us. Options are:
  - Yes: Share your location with us.
  - No: Do not share your location.
* **Contact**: Select which contact method you prefer. Options are:
  - Phone: Allow us to contact you through phone.
  - Email: Allow us to contact you through email.
* **Social**: Select which social media channels you prefer to interact 
  with us. Options are:
  - Facebook: Interact with us through Facebook.
  - Twitter: Interact with us through Twitter.
  - Instagram: Interact with us through Instagram.
* **Newsletters**: Select which newsletters you prefer. Options are:
  - Newsletters: Receive regular newsletters from us.
  - Newsletters and promotional emails: Receive both regular news and 
    promotional emails.

## Tips
Please fill out all fields marked as required and select your 
preferences clearly and accurately. If you have any questions or concerns 
about this form, feel free to reach out to us.
