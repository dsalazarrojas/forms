# social_media_manager_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from applicants for a Social Media Manager position. Please fill out each field accurately and completely to increase your chances of being considered for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all the required fields marked as "True" to ensure that your application is complete.
2. Double-check that you have entered your email address correctly in the "Email" field.
3. For the "Phone" field, please format your phone number according to the international format (e.g., +1-555-1234).
4. For the "Job Title" field, enter your current or desired job title.
5. In the "Experience" field, describe your relevant work experience, including the company name, job title, and duration of employment.
6. For "Education," describe your educational background, including the institution name, degree earned, and duration of study.
7. In the "About" field, write a brief introduction about yourself and your qualifications.
8. The "Work" field is an optional section where you can describe any relevant work experience or projects you have done.
9. In the "Skills" field, list any relevant skills you have, such as social media management tools, programming languages, or marketing certifications.
10. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Portfolio" if you have an online portfolio or website that showcases your work.
11. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Link" if you are willing to share a link to your LinkedIn profile or other professional networking sites.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your full first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your full last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in the international format (e.g., +1-555-1234).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your current or desired job title.
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, optional): Describe your relevant work experience, including the company name, job title, and duration of employment.
* **Education** (`education`, text, optional): Describe your educational background, including the institution name, degree earned, and duration of study.
* **About** (`about`, text, optional): Write a brief introduction about yourself and your qualifications.
* **Work** (`work`, text, optional): Describe any relevant work experience or projects you have done.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, optional): List any relevant skills you have, such as social media management tools, programming languages, or marketing certifications.
* **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if you have an online portfolio or website that showcases your work.
* **Link** (`link`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you are willing to share a link to your LinkedIn profile or other professional networking sites.

## Tips
* Double-check that you have completed all required fields.
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions, feel free to contact us for clarification.
