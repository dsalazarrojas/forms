# Black Friday Giveaway Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Black Friday Giveaway Form is designed to collect information from participants in a Black Friday giveaway. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own set of fields that require specific types of information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
6. Enter your street address in the "Address Line 1" field.
7. Enter your city in the "City" field.
8. Enter your state in the "State" field.
9. Enter your ZIP code in the "ZIP Code" field.
10. Select your preferred prize from the "Prize Preference" field.
11. Answer "Yes" if you follow us on social media in the "Follow Us on Social Media" field.
12. Answer "Yes" if you have shared this giveaway with friends in the "Shared with Friends" field.
13. Answer "Yes Sign Me Up" if you want to join our newsletter in the "Join Newsletter" field.
14. Select the source of information about the giveaway from the "How Did You Hear About This Giveaway" field.
15. Answer "I Agree" to the terms and conditions in the "Terms and Conditions" field.
16. If you have any bonus entry codes or notes, enter them in the "Additional Entries" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address where we will contact you if you win.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for winner notification.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth, which must be 18 or older.
* **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1`, `text`, required): Enter your street address.
* **Address Line 2** (`address_line_2`, `text`, not required): Enter your apartment or suite number (optional).
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Prize Preference** (`prize_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select which prize you prefer to win.
* **Follow Us on Social Media** (`follow_social_media`, `select_one`, not required): Answer "Yes" if you follow us on social media.
* **Shared with Friends** (`share_with_friends`, `select_one`, not required): Answer "Yes" if you shared this giveaway with friends.
* **Join Newsletter** (`newsletter_signup`, `select_one`, required): Answer "Yes Sign Me Up" if you want to join our newsletter.
* **How Did You Hear About This Giveaway** (`how_did_you_hear`, `select_one`, not required): Select the source of information about the giveaway.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Answer "I Agree" to the terms and conditions of the giveaway.
* **Additional Entries** (`additional_entries`, `text`, not required): Enter any bonus entry codes or notes.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter the correct email address for winner notification.
- Enter the correct phone number for winner notification.
- Ensure that you are 18 or older to be eligible to win.
- Select the correct prize preference.
- Answer truthfully about how you heard about the giveaway.
- Carefully read and agree to the terms and conditions.
- If you have any bonus entry codes or notes, enter them in the "Additional Entries" field.
