# website_suggestion_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The website suggestion form is designed to collect suggestions and feedback from users about the website. This form will help the website owner to understand what users like or dislike about the website, identify areas for improvement, and make data-driven decisions to enhance the user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Name" and "Email" fields, respectively.
2. Choose the type of suggestion you want to make by selecting "Feature Suggestion", "Bug Report", or "General Feedback" in the "Suggestion Type" field.
3. In the "Suggestion" field, provide a clear and concise description of your suggestion or feedback.
4. If you have additional information to provide, use the "Additional Info" field.
5. Fill in the "Submitted By" field with your name or a reference to your previous suggestion.
6. The "Submitted At" field will automatically be filled in with the current date and time.
7. The "Submitted IP" field will display the IP address of the device you are using to submit the form.
