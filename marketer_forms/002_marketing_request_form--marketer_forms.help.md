# Marketing Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Marketing Request Form is designed to collect marketing request information from customers, which will be used to follow up on their requests and ensure that their marketing needs are met.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with your information and a brief description of your marketing needs. Please make sure to provide a valid email address and phone number so that we can contact you for further follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name (<code>first_name</code>, Text, Required: No): Enter your first name for our reference.
* **Last name (<code>last_name</code>, Text, Required: No): Enter your last name for our reference.
* **Email (<code>email</code>, Email, Required: No): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you for further follow-up.
* **Phone (<code>phone</code>, Text, Required: No): Enter your phone number where we can contact you for further follow-up.
* **Description (<code>description</code>, Text, Required: No): Provide a brief description of your marketing needs.
* **Date (<code>date</code>, Date, Required: No): Enter the date for your marketing request (if applicable).

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to help us better understand your marketing needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will review your form submission to prevent duplication and ensure that we are not wasting our marketing efforts on non-qualified leads.
* We will follow up with you to discuss your marketing needs and provide further assistance.
