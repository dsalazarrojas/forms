# Social Media Contest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a social media contest registration form used to connect users with our social media teams. Please fill out this form to participate in the contest and potentially receive exclusive content or promotions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all fields are filled out, but only if required.
3. Review the form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address, following the standard format (e.g., [user@domain.com](mailto:user@domain.com)).
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number in the format "XXXX-XXXX-XXXX" where X is a digit.
* **Social media handles** (`social_media_handles`, `text`, required: false): Enter your social media handles if you have any, separated by commas.
* **Preferred social media** (`preferred_social_media`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the social media platform you want to connect with us on.
* **Social media account** (`social_media_account`, `text`, required: false): Enter your social media account name if you want to connect.
* **Consent to share info** (`consent_to_share_info`, `select_multiple`, required: false): You need to consent to share your information for the contest purpose.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): If you want to share more information or feedback, write it here.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields that require information are filled out accurately.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* You are only required to fill out the fields marked as "required".
