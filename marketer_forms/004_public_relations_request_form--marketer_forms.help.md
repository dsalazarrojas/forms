# Public Relations Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Relations Request Form is designed to collect information from users about their public relations requests, such as events, meetings, or other activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Company name, if applicable.
3. Provide your Email address.
4. Select your Department name, if applicable.
5. Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., phone or email).
6. Enter your Contact Phone number.
7. Enter your Contact Email address.
8. Provide the Date of the event or meeting you are requesting.
9. Select the Time of the event or meeting you are requesting.
10. Specify the length of the event or meeting.
11. Indicate if you need any services (e.g., catering, audiovisual equipment).
11. Write a brief Message about the purpose and details of your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: no): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: no): Enter your last name.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required: no): If you are representing a company, enter its name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: no): Enter your email address.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: no): If you are representing a department, enter its name.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, text, required: no): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., phone or email).
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: no): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: no): Enter the email address where you can be reached.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: no): Provide the date of the event or meeting.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required: no): Provide the time of the event or meeting.
* **Event Length** (`event_length`, text, required: no): Specify the length of the event or meeting.
* **Services** (`services`, select_multiple, required: no): Indicate if you need any services (e.g., catering, audiovisual equipment).
* **Message** (`message`, text, required: no): Write a brief message about the purpose and details of your request.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when filling out this form to ensure that your request is processed efficiently.
* If you have any attachments or supporting documents, you can upload them separately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the PR team directly.
* This form will be reviewed and processed in a timely manner.
