# New Year's Marketing Campaign Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance of the New Year's marketing campaign. It will help in collecting data on the campaign's goals, budget, target audience, marketing channels, start and end dates, status, and feedback. The information gathered through this form will be used to improve future marketing campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "New Year's Campaign Info" field and enter a brief description of the campaign.
2. In the "Marketing Goal" field, describe the primary objective of the campaign.
3. Enter the budget allocated for the campaign in the "Campaign Budget" field.
4. Select all the marketing channels used for the campaign in the "Marketing Channels" field.
5. Identify the target audience for the campaign in the "Target Audience" field.
6. Enter the start and end dates of the campaign in the respective fields.
7. Select the current status of the campaign from the options in the "Campaign Status" field.
8. Leave any feedback or comments about the campaign in the "Feedback" field.
9. In the "Additional Comments" field, provide any extra information that you think is relevant to the campaign.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Year's Campaign Info** (`new_years_campaign_info`, text, optional): A brief description of the campaign.
* **Marketing Goal** (`marketing_goal`, text, optional): The primary objective of the campaign.
* **Campaign Budget** (`campaign_budget`, number, optional): The allocated budget for the campaign.
* **Marketing Channels** (`marketing_channels`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the marketing channels used for the campaign.
	+ Options: Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, Email
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, text, optional): The target audience for the campaign.
* **Campaign Start Date** (`campaign_start_date`, date, optional): The start date of the campaign.
* **Campaign End Date** (`campaign_end_date`, date, optional): The end date of the campaign.
* **Campaign Status** (`campaign_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the campaign.
	+ Options: Launched, Completed, Cancelled, Ongoing
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Leave any feedback or comments about the campaign.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any extra information relevant to the campaign.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form to ensure that the data collected is reliable and actionable.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please don't hesitate to contact the marketing team.
* If you are completing the form for a duplicate campaign, please do not submit it.
