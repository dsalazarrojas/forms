# Video Content Marketing Strategy Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help marketing teams create a comprehensive video content marketing strategy by guiding users to provide necessary information about their video content goals, target audience, and distribution channels.


## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in one session to review and update your existing video content marketing strategy.
2. Answer all questions honestly and accurately to provide valuable information for internal decision-making and campaign planning.
3. If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to contact your marketing team for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, required: false): A brief overview of your video content marketing strategy. Please describe your current strategy, including your goals, target audience, and distribution channels.
* **Video** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Provide details about your existing video content. Please describe the type of content you've produced or plan to produce, such as explainer videos, product demos, or tutorial videos.
* **Video** (`3`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate if you have a designated video reporter or team handling video content creation.
* **Channel** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the channels you use to distribute your video content, such as YouTube, Vimeo, or social media.
* **Content** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Describe the type of content you plan to create or have created, such as educational, promotional, or entertaining content.
* **Distribution Channel** (`6`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate if you have a specific distribution channel for your video content, such as social media, email, or website.
* **Distribution Channel** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the multiple distribution channels for your video content.
* **Video 2** (`8`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate if you have a second video reporter or team member handling video content creation.
* **Title** (`9`, `text`, required: false): Provide a title for your video content, including its type and purpose.
* **Description** (`10`, `text`, required: false): Give a detailed description of your video content, including its target audience and goals.
* **Duration** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the length of your video content, such as short, medium, or long-form.
* **Select Video** (`12`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate if you have a specific video format, such as explainer, demo, or tutorial video.
