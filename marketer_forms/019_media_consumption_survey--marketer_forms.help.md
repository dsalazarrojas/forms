# Media Consumption Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the target audience's media consumption habits and demographics to help with market research or marketing efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully to the best of your ability.
2. Select the relevant options for each question by clicking on the corresponding checkbox or dropdown menu.
3. Fill in any required text fields with relevant information.
4. Make sure to complete all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **media_consumption_survey_form**: This is the main section of the form where you will answer questions about your media consumption habits.
* **media_consumption_survey_questions**: Select all the types of media you consume, such as radio, TV, movies, online video, podcasts, etc.
* **time_per_week**: Enter the average amount of time you spend consuming media per week.
* **average_time_per_medium**: Enter the average time you spend consuming each type of media per week.
* **most_common_time_of_day**: Select the time of day when you most commonly consume media.
* **media_consumption_survey_comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your media consumption habits.
* **media_consumption_survey_additional_comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the survey.
* **media_consumption_survey_survey_type**: Select the type of survey you are taking (e.g., for research, marketing, etc.).
* **media_consumption_survey_target_audience**: Select all the characteristics that apply to the target audience you are trying to reach with the media you consume.
* **media_consumption_survey_target_age_group**: Select the age group you belong to.
* **media_consumption_survey_target_gender**: Select your gender.
* **media_consumption_survey_target_income**: Select your income level.
* **media_consumption_survey_target_occupation**: Select your occupation.
* **media_consumption_survey_target_region**: Select your region or area where you live.
* **media_consumption_survey_target_city**: Select your city or town where you live.
* **media_consumption_survey_target_country**: Select your country of residence.
* **media_consumption_survey_target_postal_code**: Enter your postal code or zip code.
* **media_consumption_survey_target_ip**: Select your IP address (this is not a typical field, but it's included in the form, possibly for tracking or identification purposes).
* **media_consumption_survey_target_mac**: Select your MAC address (this is not a typical field, but it's included in the form, possibly for tracking or identification purposes).
* **media_consumption_survey_target_browser**: Select your web browser type.
* **media_consumption_survey_target_device**: Select the type of device you use to consume media.
* **media_consumption_survey_target_os**: Select your operating system.
* **media_consumption_survey_target_platform**: Select the platform or operating system on which you consume media (e.g., Windows, macOS, Linux, etc.).
* **media_consumption_survey_target_language**: Select the language you use to consume media.
* **media_consumption_survey_survey_date**: Select the date on which you are taking this survey.

Please provide your honest and accurate responses to help us better understand your media consumption habits.
