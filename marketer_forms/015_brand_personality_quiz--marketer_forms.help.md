# Brand Personality Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Brand Personality Quiz is designed to help individuals understand their unique brand personality. This quiz assesses various aspects of a person's preferences and traits to generate results that provide insight into their style, values, and behavior.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Brand Personality Quiz, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `Your Name` field (optional).
2. Enter your email address to receive your results.
3. Select your preferred color palette from the given options.
4. Choose a weekend activity that resonates with you.
5. Select a car that best matches your style.
6. Indicate how you use social media.
7. Describe your fashion style.
8. Choose a vacation spot that suits you.
9. Select a communication style that represents how you interact with others.
10. Decide how you approach decision-making.
11. Identify your party role.
12. Select a life motto that resonates with you.
13. Choose a season that you prefer.
14. Pick a superpower that you would like to have.
15. Finally, select a results category that you would like to receive your results in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: This is your name, which is optional and only for identification purposes.
* **Email Address**: This is the email address to which your results will be sent.
* **Choose a Color Palette**: Select a color palette that appeals to you, whether it's bold and vibrant or elegant and refined.
* **Pick a Weekend Activity**: Choose an activity you would rather do on a weekend, be it adventure sports or relaxing at home.
* **Choose a Car**: Select a car style that matches your personality, from sports cars to classic vintages.
* **Social Media Style**: Indicate how you use social media, whether to share life moments or post educational content.
* **Fashion Preference**: Describe your fashion style, whether you like to be trendy and bold or elegant and sophisticated.
* **Ideal Vacation**: Choose a vacation spot that you would love to visit, whether it's an adventure destination or a cultural city tour.
* **Communication Style**: Select a communication style that represents how you interact with others, whether directly and confidently or warmly and politely.
* **Decision Making**: Decide how you approach decision-making, whether quickly and boldly or carefully and thoughtfully.
* **Party Role**: Identify your party role, whether you're the life of the party or a quiet observer.
* **Life Mottos**: Choose a life motto that resonates with you, be it seizing the day or being yourself.
* **Favorite Season**: Select a season that you prefer, whether spring renewal or winter elegance.
* **Superpower Choice**: Pick a superpower that you would like to possess, be it flight or invisibility.
* **Results Category Preference**: Finally, select the level of detail you would like to receive in your results, whether a quick summary or a full report with recommendations.

**Tips**: Take your time to answer each question honestly, and have fun exploring your brand personality!
