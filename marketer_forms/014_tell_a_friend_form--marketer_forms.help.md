# tell_a_friend_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "tell_a_friend_form" is a form designed to help you suggest a product to a friend or acquaintance. It allows you to provide your friend's email address, name, and a message or suggestion, making it easy to share your thoughts with them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select one or more products you'd like to suggest to your friend.
2.  Enter your friend's email address in the "Friend Email" field.
3.  Type your friend's name in the "Friend Name" field.
4.  Enter your email address in the "Sender Email" field.
5.  Write a message or suggestion in the "Message" field.
6.  Click "Submit" to send the suggestion to your friend.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Suggest A Product**: Select one or more products you'd like to suggest to your friend. This field allows you to choose from the available options.
*   **Friend Email**: Enter your friend's email address so we can send the suggestion to them. Please ensure you have their consent to share their email address.
*   **Friend Name**: Type your friend's name in this field.
*   **Sender Email**: Enter your email address so we can identify you as the sender of the suggestion.
*   **Message**: Write a message or suggestion to share with your friend.
*   **Submit**: Click this button to send the suggestion to your friend.
