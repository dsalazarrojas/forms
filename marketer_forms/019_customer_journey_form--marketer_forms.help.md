# Customer Journey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and insights from customers at various stages of their journey with our company, helping us to identify areas of improvement and provide personalized experiences in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the text fields (How Customer Found, How Customer Navigated, Customer Feelings, Customer Suggestions, Customer Feedback, and Phone Number).
2. Select one or multiple options for Customer Survey.
3. Choose a contact method (First Name, Email, Phone, Live Chat, or In Person).
4. Enter a valid email address in the Email Address field.
5. Use the Customer Journey Map field for any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How Customer Found**: Please describe how you found out about our company, product, or service.
* **How Customer Navigated**: Describe how you navigated our website or interacted with our brand.
* **Customer Feelings**: Share your thoughts and feelings about your experience with our company.
* **Customer Suggestions**: Provide any suggestions or ideas you have for how we can improve.
* **Customer Feedback**: Share any feedback about your experience with our company.
* **Contact Method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted for future communication.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address so we can contact you about your feedback.
* **Phone Number**: Enter a valid phone number so we can contact you for follow-up conversations.
* **Customer Journey Map**: Use this field for any additional notes or comments about your experience.
* **Customer Survey**: Select one or multiple options that best describe your satisfaction with our product or service. 

## Tips
* Be honest and clear in your answers to provide valuable feedback.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
* We appreciate your input and look forward to making improvements based on your feedback.
