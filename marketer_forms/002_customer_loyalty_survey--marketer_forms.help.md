# customer_loyalty_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to measure customer satisfaction and loyalty towards a company or brand. It collects feedback on various aspects of the customer experience, including customer loyalty, satisfaction level, frequency of purchases, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the correct options for each field.
2. For text fields, provide a brief description or answer in the provided space.
3. For select fields, choose one of the available options.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Loyalty** (`customer_loyalty`, text, optional): 
This field asks about your level of loyalty towards the company or brand.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, number, optional): 
Indicate your level of satisfaction with your recent experience with the company.
* **Purchases** (`frequency_of_purchases`, number, optional): 
Provide the frequency of your purchases from this company in recent times.
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, text, optional): 
Write a few words about what you like or dislike about your recent experience.
* **Loyalty Program** (`loyalty_program`, select_one, optional): 
Indicate if you are part of the company's loyalty program.
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, select_multiple, optional): 
Check all the channels through which you have interacted with the company recently.
* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, select_one, optional): 
Rate the quality of customer service you received from the company.
* **Product Relevance** (`product_relevance`, select_one, optional): 
Indicate if the product or service offered by the company is relevant to your needs.
* **Customer Experience** (`customer_experience`, text, optional): 
Provide a detailed description of your experience with the company's product or service.
* **Communication Frequency** (`communication_frequency`, number, optional): 
Indicate the frequency of communication you have received from the company in recent times.
