# Advertising Quiz Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This template is designed to assess the advertising skills and preferences of marketing professionals, advertisers, or other relevant stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2. Select the options that best match your experience and preferences.
3. Provide a short answer to the case study question, describing an ad campaign you managed.
4. Add any additional comments or information you'd like to share.
5. If you'd like to receive the quiz results via email, enter your email address in the "Email to Receive Score" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Role or Title** (`role`, text, optional): Enter your job title or position, if applicable.
* **Advertising Experience Level** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Select the option that best matches your experience in advertising, from Beginner to Expert.
	+ Beginner: Limited experience in advertising.
	+ Intermediate: Moderate experience in advertising.
	+ Advanced: Significant experience in advertising.
	+ Expert: Highly experienced in advertising.
* **Platform Preference** (`platform_preference`, select_multiple, optional): Select the platforms you are most familiar with, such as Facebook, Google Ads, LinkedIn, Instagram, or TikTok.
* **Budget Planning Confidence** (`budget_planning`, select_one, required): Choose how comfortable you are with planning ad budgets, from Not Confident to Very Confident.
	+ Not Confident: You're not comfortable planning ad budgets.
	+ Somewhat Confident: You're somewhat comfortable planning ad budgets.
	+ Confident: You're comfortable planning ad budgets.
	+ Very Confident: You're very confident planning ad budgets.
* **Case Study Question** (`case_study_question`, text, optional): Provide a short answer describing an ad campaign you managed, including its goals, strategies, and results.
* **Analytic Tools Used** (`analytic_tools`, select_multiple, optional): Select the tools you use for measuring ad performance, such as Google Analytics, Tableau, Adobe Analytics, or Inhouse Tools.
* **Email to Receive Score** (`score_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive the quiz results.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any other information you'd like to share about your experience or preferences.
