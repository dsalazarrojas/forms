# Chargify Magazine Subscription - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chargify Magazine Subscription form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in subscribing to magazines. This form will guide you through the process of filling out the form to ensure you have a smooth and successful subscription experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2.  Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3.  Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field (optional).
5.  Enter your full mailing address in the "Mailing Address" field.
6.  Enter your city in the "City" field.
7.  Enter your state or province in the "State or Province" field.
8.  Enter your ZIP or postal code in the "ZIP or Postal Code" field.
9.  Select your country from the "Country" dropdown list.
10. Choose your preferred magazine subscription from the "Select Magazine Subscription" dropdown list.
11. Select the duration of your subscription from the "Subscription Term" dropdown list.
12. Choose your subscription format preference from the "Subscription Type" dropdown list.
13. If this is a gift subscription, select "Yes it is a gift" and enter the recipient's name and any message in the corresponding fields.
14. Choose your payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown list.
15. Select whether you want to enable auto-renewal of your subscription from the "Enable auto-renewal" dropdown list.
16. If you have a promotional code, enter it in the "Promotional Code" field.
17. Finally, select how you heard about us from the "How did you hear about us" dropdown list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`subscriber_first_name`, Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`subscriber_last_name`, Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`subscriber_email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address for subscription confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (`subscriber_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, Text, Required): Enter your full street address.
* **City** (`city`, Text, Required): Enter your city.
* **State or Province** (`state`, Text, Required): Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`zip_code`, Text, Required): Enter your postal code.
* **Country** (`country`, Select One, Required): Select your country from the list.
* **Select Magazine Subscription** (`magazine_selection`, Select One, Required): Choose your preferred magazine subscription.
* **Subscription Term** (`subscription_term`, Select One, Required): Select the duration of your subscription.
* **Subscription Type** (`subscription_type`, Select One, Required): Choose your subscription format preference.
* **Is this a gift subscription** (`gift_subscription`, Select One, Required): Select if this is a gift subscription or not.
* **Gift Recipient Name** (`gift_recipient_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the gift recipient if this is a gift subscription.
* **Gift Message** (`gift_message`, Text, Optional): Enter a message for the gift recipient.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, Select One, Required): Choose how you want to pay for your subscription.
* **Enable auto-renewal** (`auto_renewal`, Select One, Required): Select whether to enable auto-renewal of your subscription.
* **Promotional Code** (`promotional_code`, Text, Optional): Enter any promotional code you may have.
* **How did you hear about us** (`how_heard`, Select One, Optional): Select how you heard about us.
