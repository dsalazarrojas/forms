# Customer Interest Profile - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Customer Interest Profile form is used to collect information about a customer's interests, demographics, and contact details. This information will be used by marketers to understand the customer's preferences and tailor their interactions accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as instructed below.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information about the customer.
3. If you are not sure about a particular field, please do not leave it blank or use your best judgment to provide a reasonable answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Interest Profile Form** (`customer_interest_profile_form`): This is the main form label and is not a field to fill in. Please proceed to the next field.
* **What is the customer's name?** (`customer_interest_profile`): Please enter the customer's full name.
* **What is the customer's age?** (`customer_interest_profile_0`): Please enter the customer's age.
* **How did you get in touch with us?** (`customer_interest_profile_1`): Please describe how the customer got in touch with you.
* **What is your preferred communication channel?** (`customer_interest_profile_2`): Please select one of the following options:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Chat
* **What is the customer's preferred time zone?** (`customer_interest_profile_3`): Please enter the customer's preferred time zone (e.g., EST, PST, GMT).
* **What is the customer's available time?** (`customer_interest_profile_4`): Please enter the customer's available time in 24-hour format (e.g., 12:00, 14:00).
* **What is the customer's email address?** (`customer_interest_profile_5`): Please enter the customer's email address.
* **What is the customer's phone number?** (`customer_interest_profile_6`): Please enter the customer's phone number.
* **What is the customer's note?** (`customer_interest_profile_7`): Please enter any additional notes or comments about the customer.
* **What would you like to say to us?** (`customer_interest_profile_8`): Please enter any comments or messages for the customer.

## Tips

- Please make sure to review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled in.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or the marketing team for guidance.
- This form is designed to be completed by authorized personnel only.
