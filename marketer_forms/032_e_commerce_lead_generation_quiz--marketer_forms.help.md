**E Commerce Lead Generation Quiz - Help Guide**

### Purpose

The E Commerce Lead Generation Quiz is designed to gather information about potential customers and generate leads for e-commerce businesses. This form helps to identify individuals interested in e-commerce products or services, and provides valuable data to business owners, such as contact information, buying preferences, and demographics.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct answers for each field, based on your interests and preferences.
2. Fill in the required information, such as email address and contact details.
3. Provide as much information as possible to help us better understand your needs and generate more accurate leads.
