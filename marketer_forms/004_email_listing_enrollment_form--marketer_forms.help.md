# email_listing_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The email listing enrollment form is a data collection form designed to gather information from clients, marketers, and partners for email listing purposes. This form is intended to capture essential details such as names, contact information, and job roles for the purpose of creating email listings. Completing this form will allow you to get in touch with potential collaborators or partners for various industries and locations.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information**. Ensure that all required fields (indicated by an asterisk *) are filled correctly before submitting the form.
2.  **Use correct formatting for date and time fields**. If asked to enter a date or time, use the expected format (e.g., DD/MM/YY for date and HH:MM AM/PM for time).
3.  **Be precise with select multiple options**. Select all applicable options for fields with multiple options, and be sure to choose "None" if no options apply.
4.  **Review and confirm your agreement**. Confirm that you have read, understand, and agree with the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Email**: Enter a valid email address for communication. Ensure it matches the confirmation email.
*   **Company**: Enter your company or organization name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Role**: Select your job role from the available options: Client, Marketer, Partner.
*   **Industry**: Select your industry from the available options: Technology, Finance, Healthcare, and None.
*   **Location**: Select your location from the available options: New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, and None.
*   **Description**: Enter a brief description of your role or business.
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the expected date format (DD/MM/YY).
*   **Time of Birth**: Enter your time of birth in the expected time format (HH:MM AM/PM).
*   **Phone**: Enter your business or personal phone number.
*   **Confirm Email**: Re-enter your email address for verification.
*   **Agreement**: Confirm that you have read, understand, and agree with the terms and conditions.
*   **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or information that you want to share.
*   **Confirm Agreement**: Confirm your agreement with the terms and conditions, ensuring you have understood and agreed to them.
