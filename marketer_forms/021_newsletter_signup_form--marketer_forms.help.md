# Newsletter Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for subscribers to sign up for our newsletter. To use this form, simply follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the first field.
2. Enter your Email address in the second field.
3. Choose whether you want to subscribe to the Newsletter List in the third field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required: false): Please enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed in our newsletter communications.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter your valid email address where you would like to receive our newsletter.
* **Subscribe to Newsletter List** (select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you want to subscribe to our newsletter list, or 'No' if you don't want to subscribe.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter a valid email address to ensure you can receive our newsletter.
* If you have any issues with your email, please contact our support team.
* You can unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time by clicking on the unsubscribe link at the bottom of each email.
