# Path To Purchase Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Path To Purchase Survey form is designed to collect information from customers to help us understand their purchase journey and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the demographic questions (Age, Gender, Location) as accurately as possible.
2. Answer each question according to your actual behavior or preferences.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Home** (`home_page`, `note`, required/optional): This page serves as an introduction to the survey and explains its purpose.
- **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, required/optional): Please read and understand the purpose of this survey before proceeding.
- **Demographics** (`demographics_page`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each of the following questions:
	+ Do you identify as male or female?
	+ Do you prefer online shopping?
	+ Do you prefer physical store shopping?
- **Age** (`demographics_age`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your age group:
	+ Option 1: Less than 25
	+ Option 2: 26-45
	+ Option 3: 46 or older
- **Gender** (`demographics_gender`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your gender:
	+ Option 4: Male
	+ Option 5: Female
	+ Option 6: Other
- **Location** (`demographics_location`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your location:
	+ Option 7: Urban
	+ Option 8: Rural
	+ Option 9: Suburban
- **Questions** (`questions_page`, `note`, required/optional): Please answer the following questions with as much detail as possible:
- **Question 1** (`question_1`, `text`, required/optional): Question 1: Please describe your recent purchase experience.
- **Question 2** (`question_2`, `text`, required/optional): Question 2: Please describe how you typically make purchasing decisions.
- **Question 3** (`question_3`, `text`, required/optional): Question 3: Have you ever changed your mind after making a purchase? Why?
- **Question 4** (`question_4`, `text`, required/optional): Question 4: What are some common things you look for when making a purchasing decision?
- **Question 5** (`question_5`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Question 5: Do you prefer:
	+ Yes
	+ No
- **Question 6** (`question_6`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Question 6: Do you prefer:
	+ Yes
	+ No
- **Question 7** (`question_7`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Question 7: Do you prefer:
	+ Yes
	+ No
- **Question 8** (`question_8`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Question 8: Do you prefer:
	+ Yes
	+ No
- **Question 9** (`question_9`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Question 9: Do you prefer:

- **Question 10** (`question_10`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Question 10: Do you prefer:
	+ Yes
	+ No
- **Question 11** (`question_11`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Question 11: Do you prefer:
	+ Yes
	+ No
- **Question 12** (`question_12`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Question 12: Do you prefer:
	+ Yes
	+ No
- **Question 13** (`question_13`, `select_one`, required/optional): Question 13: How often do you buy new products?
	+ Option 14: Rarely
	+ Option 15: Sometimes
	+ Option 16: Often
- **Question 14** (`question_14`, `text`, required/optional): Question 14: What factors influence your purchasing decisions?
- **Question 15** (`question_15`, `text`, required/optional): Question 15: How do you typically find out about new products or services?
- **Question 16** (`question_16`, `text`, required/optional): Question 16: Do you have any other comments about our products or services?
- **Thank you** (`thank_you`, `note`, required/optional): A thank-you message will be displayed here once you have submitted your survey.

Please note that all questions are optional and you can submit the form with as much or as little information as you want.
