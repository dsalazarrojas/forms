# Email Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect email addresses from users interested in signing up for email communications from your business or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the text field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your Email address in the text field labeled "Email".
3. Confirm your Email address in the text field labeled "Confirm Email" to ensure accuracy.
4. Click the "Submit" button to complete your email signup.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as you prefer to be addressed.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Confirm Email** (`Confirm Email`, `email`, required/optional): Re-enter your email address to confirm it is correct.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, `text`, required/optional): Click to submit your email signup information.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly to ensure it can be used to contact you.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
