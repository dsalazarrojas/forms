# Brand Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from customers and prospects about a specific brand.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select your relationship with the brand from the options provided.
2. Choose how familiar you are with the brand.
3. Select how you first discovered the brand.
4. Choose multiple qualities that describe the brand.
5. Select your level of satisfaction with the brand's products.
6. Select your level of satisfaction with the brand's customer service.
7. Select your perception of the brand's pricing.
8. Rate the quality of the brand's products or services.
9. Select your level of loyalty to the brand.
10. Choose how often you purchase from the brand.
11. Select how likely you are to recommend the brand.
12. Compare the brand to its competitors.
13. If you have any suggestions on how the brand could improve, you can write them here (optional).
14. If you have any favorite products or services from the brand, you can write them here (optional).
15. Share your overall opinion of the brand.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Type** (`respondent_type`, `select_one`, required): This question asks you to select the type of respondent you are, such as a current customer, former customer, prospect, industry professional, or general public.
* **Brand Familiarity** (`brand_familiarity`, `select_one`, required): This question asks you to select how familiar you are with the brand, such as very familiar, somewhat familiar, heard of it, or not familiar at all.
* **First Discovery** (`first_discovery`, `select_one`, required): This question asks you to select how you first discovered the brand, such as social media, search engine, friend or family, advertisement, in store, or other.
* **Brand Qualities** (`brand_qualities`, `select_multiple`, required): This question asks you to select multiple qualities that describe the brand, such as innovative, reliable, affordable, premium, trustworthy, friendly, professional, creative, sustainable, or outdated.
* **Product Satisfaction** (`product_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): This question asks you to select your level of satisfaction with the brand's products.
* **Service Satisfaction** (`service_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): This question asks you to select your level of satisfaction with the brand's customer service.
* **Price Perception** (`price_perception`, `select_one`, required): This question asks you to select your perception of the brand's pricing, such as very expensive, somewhat expensive, fair, good value, or very affordable.
* **Quality Perception** (`quality_perception`, `select_one`, required): This question asks you to rate the quality of the brand's products or services.
* **Brand Loyalty** (`brand_loyalty`, `select_one`, required): This question asks you to select your level of loyalty to the brand, such as very loyal, somewhat loyal, neutral, not very loyal, or not loyal at all.
* **Purchase Frequency** (`purchase_frequency`, `select_one`, required): This question asks you to select how often you purchase from the brand.
* **Recommend Likelihood** (`recommend_likelihood`, `select_one`, required): This question asks you to select how likely you are to recommend the brand.
* **Competitor Comparison** (`competitor_comparison`, `select_one`, required): This question asks you to select how the brand compares to its competitors.
* **Brand Improvement** (`brand_improvement`, `text`, optional): This question asks you to provide any suggestions on how the brand could improve.
* **Favorite Products or Services** (`favorite_products`, `text`, optional): This question asks you to provide any favorite products or services from the brand.
* **Overall Opinion** (`overall_opinion`, `text`, required): This question asks you to share your overall opinion of the brand.

Note: This guide is provided as a reference for respondents who are using the form. The actual form fields may not exactly match this guide, but this should give you an idea of what to expect.
