# Infinity Marketing Onboarding Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your marketing needs and preferences for an online marketing campaign. The goal is to gather essential data to tailor our marketing services to your specific requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Introduce yourself and your company.
2. Provide contact information for the person responsible for marketing decisions.
3. Specify the type of company you represent.
4. Input contact email, phone number, and name.
5. Identify the primary goal of the campaign.
6. Choose the marketing channels you would like to use.
7. Set up your ad budget details, including currency, amount, frequency, duration, timezone, and timezone offset.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief welcome message to get started with the onboarding process.
* **Company Name**: Enter your company's name to help us understand who you are and what you do.
* **Company Type**: Select one of the following types of companies:
	+ Small Business
	+ Medium Business
	+ Large Business
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person responsible for marketing decisions.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person responsible for marketing decisions.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person responsible for marketing decisions.
* **Marketing Manager**: This field is not currently used, so please skip it.
* **Primary Goal**: Describe the main objective of your marketing campaign.
* **Marketing Channels**: Choose the marketing channels you would like to use:
	+ Social Media
	+ Email
	+ Google Ads
* **Ad Budget**:
	+ **Currency**: Select the currency for your ad budget:
		- USD
		- EUR
	+ **Amount**: Enter the budget for your campaign.
	+ **Frequency**: Choose when you would like to start the campaign:
		- Daily
		- Weekly
		- Monthly
	+ **Duration**: Enter the duration of your campaign.
	+ **Timezone**: Select the timezone for your campaign:
		- UTC
		- PST
		- EST
		- CST
	+ **Timezone Offset**: Enter the offset from your timezone.
	+ **Timezone Offset Unit**: Select the unit for your timezone offset:
		- minutes
		- hours
	+ **Timezone Offset Value**: Enter the value of your timezone offset.
* **Terms and Conditions**: This field is not currently used, so please read the terms and conditions and let us know if you have any questions.
