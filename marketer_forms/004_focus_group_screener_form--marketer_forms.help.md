# Focus Group Screener Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic information from potential customers/participants to ensure that our focus group is representative of the target audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your age range from the list.
2. Choose your preferred language.
3. Indicate your gender.
4. Select your income range.
5. Choose your employment status.
6. If applicable, enter your occupation, education level, and education field.
7. If you have any income other than the ones listed, please describe it in the "Family income other" field.
8. Answer the questions regarding family members, family income, and employment income.
9. If applicable, describe your occupation type, household size, education level other, family income other, and family income type.
10. If you have any other education institution, please enter it in the "Education institution" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Age range** (`age_range`, select_one, required): Select your age range from the options provided.
* **Language** (`language`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred language.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_multiple, required): Indicate your gender. You can select more than one option if applicable.
* **Income range** (`income_range`, select_one, required): Select your income range from the options provided.
* **Employment status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required): Choose your employment status.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your occupation.
* **Education level** (`education_level`, select_one, required): Select your education level from the options provided.
* **Family income** (`family_income`, select_one, required): Answer the question regarding your family income.
* **Family income other** (`family_income_other`, text, optional): If you have any income other than the ones listed, describe it in this field.
* **Employment income** (`employment_income`, select_one, required): Select your employment income.
* **Family members** (`family_members`, select_one, required): Choose the number of family members you have.
* **Occupation type** (`occupation_type`, select_one, required): Select your occupation type from the options provided.
* **Education field** (`education_field`, select_one, required): Choose your education field.
* **Household size** (`household_size`, select_one, required): Select your household size from the options provided.
* **Family income type** (`family_income_type`, select_one, required): Choose your family income type.
* **Other education level** (`education_other`, text, optional): If you have any education level other than the ones listed, describe it in this field.
* **Occupation other** (`occupation_other`, text, optional): If you have any occupation other than the ones listed, describe it in this field.
* **Education institution** (`education_institution`, text, optional): If you have any other education institution, enter it in this field.
* **Family income other** (`family_income_other`, text, optional): If you have any family income other than the ones listed, describe it in this field.
* **Education field other** (`education_field_other`, text, optional): If you have any education field other than the ones listed, describe it in this field.
* **Education level other** (`education_level_other`, text, optional): If you have any education level other than the ones listed, describe it in this field.
