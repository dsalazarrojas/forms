# Swim Wear Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and preferences from swim wear users, helping us create more comfortable, stylish, and affordable swim wear products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the option that best describes your swim wear preferences (Comfortable, Stylish, Affordable) in the "Swimwear Preferences" field.
2. Rate your satisfaction level with our swim wear products on a scale of 1-5 in the "Satisfaction Level" field.
3. Provide feedback on areas where you feel we can improve our swim wear products in the "Areas for Improvement" field.
4. Choose the option that best describes the assigned tool for this swim wear product in the "Assigned Tool" field.
5. Select the desired output file format for your survey responses in the "Output File" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field to receive any follow-up questions or information about your swim wear products.
7. Enter any additional comments or feedback about our swim wear products in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Swimwear Preferences** (`swimwear_preferences`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field helps us understand what you value most in a swim wear product. Select the option that best describes your preferences.
- **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, `number`, required/optional): This field helps us gauge your level of satisfaction with our swim wear products. Rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
- **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, `text`, required/optional): This field is where you can provide detailed feedback on areas where you feel we can improve our swim wear products.
- **Consumer Preferences** (`consumer_preferences`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field is similar to "Swimwear Preferences", but helps us understand your overall consumer preferences.
- **Trends** (`trends`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for any trends or observations you have about our swim wear products.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address to receive any follow-up information about your swim wear products.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication.
- **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for any additional feedback or comments about our swim wear products.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field is where you select the tool that was assigned for this swim wear product.
- **Output File** (`output_file`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field helps us understand how you want to receive your survey responses. Select the desired output file format.
