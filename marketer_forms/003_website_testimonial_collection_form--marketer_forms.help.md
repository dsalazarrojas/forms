# website_testimonial_collection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect website testimonials from users, which can be used to improve the website's reputation, promote products or services, and provide social proof.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for your testimonial in the `testimonial_title` field.
2. Share your testimonial in the `testimonial_description` field.
3. Provide your name in the `testimonial_name` field.
4. Enter your email address in the `testimonial_email` field.
5. Enter your message in the `testimonial_message` field.
6. Select whether you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions in the `testimonial_agreement` field.
7. Choose the type of file you want to upload (if applicable) in the `testimonial_uploads_type` field.
8. Select whether you want to upload a file (if applicable) in the `testimonial_uploads_value` field.
9. Enter the file name for your uploaded file in the `testimonial_uploads_file` field (if uploading a file).
10. (Optional) You can upload another file using the `testimonial_uploads_file_2` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Website Testimonial Collection Form** (`testimonial_title`): Enter a title for your testimonial.
* **Enter your testimonial** (`testimonial_description`): Share your testimonial in this field.
* **Your Name** (`testimonial_name`): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Your Email** (`testimonial_email`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Your Message** (`testimonial_message`): Enter your message or testimonial.
* **I have read and agreed to the terms and conditions** (`testimonial_agreement`): Select whether you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`testimonial_terms`): Read the terms and conditions (optional).
* **Uploads** (`testimonial_uploads`): Select whether you want to upload a file.
* **File Type** (`testimonial_uploads_type`): Choose the type of file you want to upload (if applicable).
* **Upload** (`testimonial_uploads_value`): Select whether you want to upload a file (if applicable).
* **Upload** (`testimonial_uploads_file`): Enter the file name for your uploaded file (if uploading a file).
* **Upload 2** (`testimonial_uploads_file_2`): (Optional) Upload another file.

**Tips:**

* Make sure to agree to the terms and conditions before submitting your testimonial.
* If you choose to upload a file, select the correct file type and enter the file name.
* You can upload multiple files by using both the `testimonial_uploads_file` and `testimonial_uploads_file_2` fields.
* Review and proofread your submission before submitting the form.
