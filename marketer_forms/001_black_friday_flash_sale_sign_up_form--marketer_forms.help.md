# Black Friday Flash Sale Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Black Friday Flash Sale Sign Up Form is designed to collect information from customers interested in participating in the Black Friday sale.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a clear understanding of the form's purpose.
2. Fill in your personal information (full name, email address, and phone number).
3. Select the product you are interested in (Wireless headphones, Smart home speaker, Limited edition jacket, or Other).
4. Enter the desired quantity of the product.
5. Choose your preferred store location (Downtown store, Mall location, or Online pickup).
6. If you have a promotional code, enter it in the designated field.
7. Check the box to opt-in for marketing communications (yes, I'm interested in future offers).
8. If you have any additional notes or requests, type them in the "Additional notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Enter your first and last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address, which we will use to send you confirmation and sale updates.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number, which is optional but helps us reach you in case of sale-related updates.
* **Product interest**: Select the item you are interested in buying during the Black Friday sale. This is crucial for us to provide you with relevant updates and information about the product.
* **Desired quantity**: Enter a whole number that reflects how many units of the selected product you would like to purchase.
* **Preferred store location**: Choose where you would like to pick up your purchased item (Downtown store, Mall location, or Online pickup). 
* **Promo code**: If you have a promotional code, enter it here to receive a discount on your purchase.
* **Opt in to marketing communications**: Check this box to receive future emails and updates about our promotions and offers.
* **Additional notes**: This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional information or requests you might have regarding your purchase. 

## Tips
- Be honest when selecting your product interest, as this will ensure you receive the most relevant information.
- Enter your desired quantity correctly to avoid errors during the sale.
- If you have a promo code, use it to receive a discount on your purchase.
- Check your email for confirmation and sale updates.
- We will use your email for marketing communications if you opt-in; you can opt-out at any time.
