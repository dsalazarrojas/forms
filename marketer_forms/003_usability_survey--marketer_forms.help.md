# usability_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The usability survey form is designed to gather feedback from users about their overall experience with the product or service. This feedback will help us to identify areas for improvement and create a more satisfying experience for all users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the fields with your honest and constructive feedback.
2. Start with the "Product name" field, where you can type in the name of the product or service you are providing feedback on.
3. Choose your level of satisfaction with the product or service using the "How satisfied" options (Not satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Very satisfied).
4. If you are not satisfied, provide more details in the "Why not satisfied" field.
5. Specify what aspects of the product or service were not satisfying using the "What not satisfied" field.
6. If you have had an overall good experience, select "Yes" in the "Overall experience" field.
7. In the "Feedback" field, provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the product or service.
8. If you have any suggestions for improvements, type them in the "Suggest improvements" field.
9. Finally, provide your email and phone number for us to contact you, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product name** (text, required: false): Type in the name of the product or service you are providing feedback on.
* **How satisfied** (select_one, required: false): Choose your level of satisfaction with the product or service:
	+ Not satisfied
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Very satisfied
* **Why not satisfied** (text, required: false): If you are not satisfied, explain why in this field.
* **What not satisfied** (text, required: false): Specify what aspects of the product or service were not satisfying.
* **Overall experience** (select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" if you had an overall good experience.
* **Feedback** (text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have.
* **Suggest improvements** (text, required: false): Type in any suggestions you have for improvements.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Provide your email for us to contact you.
* **Contact number** (text, required: false): Provide your phone number for us to contact you.
