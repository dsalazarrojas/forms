# Black Friday Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Black Friday Survey is a research form designed to collect data on consumer behavior and preferences related to Black Friday shopping. It aims to gather insights on how individuals plan, shop, and participate in Black Friday sales.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reading the instructions carefully.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
3. Use the provided options for select questions, and type in the text fields.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Black Friday Shopping Survey** (`survey_intro`, note): This is a brief introduction to the form, explaining its purpose.
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): Provide your name for identification purposes. (Note: This field is optional and not required.)
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, required): Select your age range from the provided options.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Select your gender for demographic purposes. (Note: This field is optional and not required.)
* **Annual Income Range** (`annual_income`, select_one, optional): Select your household income range from the provided options. (Note: This field is optional and not required.)
* **Black Friday Participation** (`black_friday_participation`, select_one, required): Select how often you shop on Black Friday.
* **Shopping Preference** (`shopping_preference`, select_one, required): Select your preferred shopping method: online or in-store.
* **Typical Black Friday Spending** (`typical_spending`, select_one, required): Select how much you usually spend on Black Friday.
* **Shopping Categories** (`shopping_categories`, select_multiple, required): Select all the categories you typically buy on Black Friday.
* **Planning Behavior** (`planning_behavior`, select_one, required): Select how you prepare for Black Friday shopping.
* **Deal Research Methods** (`deal_research`, select_multiple, required): Select all the methods you use to find deals.
* **Preferred Start Time** (`start_shopping_time`, select_one, required): Select when you start shopping on Black Friday.
* **Main Motivation** (`motivation`, select_one, required): Select your primary reason for shopping on Black Friday.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, required): Select how satisfied you are with Black Friday deals.
* **Future Shopping Plans** (`future_plans`, select_one, required): Select your plans for shopping on Black Friday next year.
* **Suggested Improvements** (`improvements_suggested`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions for improving the Black Friday shopping experience.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional thoughts or opinions about Black Friday shopping.

Note: This form does not collect any personally identifiable information, and all the data collected will be used for research purposes only to improve the shopping experience.
