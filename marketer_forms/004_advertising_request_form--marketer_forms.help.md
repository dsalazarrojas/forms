# Advertising Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help the marketing team understand the requirements and goals of the requested advertising campaign. It is meant to be filled out by internal requesters and will be used by the marketing team to plan and execute the campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out your name and department in the "Requester Name" and "Department" fields, respectively.
2. Enter your email address in the "Contact Email" field, so we can follow up with you regarding your campaign.
3. Describe the objective of your campaign in the "Campaign Objective" field.
4. If you have a preferred start date for your campaign, enter it in the "Preferred Start Date" field.
5. If you have an estimated budget for your campaign, enter it in the "Estimated Budget" field.
6. If you have specific creative requirements for your campaign, describe them in the "Creative Requirements" field.
7. If an approval is needed from an internal approver, enter their name in the "Approval Needed From" field.
8. If you have any additional files that support your campaign, indicate what type of files these are in the "Supporting Files Note" field.
9. If you have any additional instructions for the marketing team, enter them in the "Additional Instructions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester Name** (required): Enter your name to be contacted regarding your campaign.
* **Department** (optional): Enter your department or team name for reference.
* **Contact Email** (required): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **Campaign Objective** (required): Describe what your campaign should achieve.
* **Preferred Start Date** (optional): Enter the date you prefer the campaign to start.
* **Estimated Budget** (required): Enter your estimated budget for the campaign.
* **Creative Requirements** (optional): Describe any specific creative requirements for your campaign.
* **Approval Needed From** (optional): If an internal approver is needed, enter their name.
* **Supporting Files Note** (optional): Indicate if you have any files that support your campaign.
* **Additional Instructions** (optional): Enter any additional information for the marketing team.
