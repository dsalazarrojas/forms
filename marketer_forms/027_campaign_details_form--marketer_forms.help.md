# Campaign Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a campaign's details, including the campaign's name, type, goal, target audience, and budget.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the official name of the campaign in the "Campaign Name" field.
2. Select the type of campaign (e.g., Fundraising, Awareness, etc.) from the "Campaign Type" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the name of your organization in the "Organization Name" field.
4. Enter the contact person's name and email in the "Contact Person Name" and "Contact Email" fields, respectively.
5. Enter the contact person's phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
6. Select the main objective of the campaign (e.g., fundraising, awareness, etc.) in the "Campaign Goal" field.
7. Enter the dollar amount for the fundraising target (if applicable) in the "Fundraising Target Amount" field.
8. Enter the start and end dates of the campaign in the "Campaign Start Date" and "Campaign End Date" fields, respectively.
9. Select the target audience(s) for the campaign (e.g., individual donors, corporate sponsors, etc.) from the "Target Audience" dropdown menu.
10. Select the campaign channels to be used (e.g., email marketing, social media, etc.) from the "Campaign Channels" dropdown menu.
11. Enter the total budget for the campaign (if applicable) in the "Campaign Budget" field.
12. Select the size of the campaign team (e.g., 1-5 people, 6-10 people, etc.) from the "Campaign Team Size" dropdown menu.
13. Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether you have run similar campaigns before in the "Have You Run Similar Campaigns Before" field.
14. Provide a brief description of the results of previous campaigns (if applicable) in the "Results of Previous Campaigns" field.
15. Enter the main talking points or key messages of the campaign in the "Key Campaign Messages" field.
16. Select how you will measure the success of the campaign (e.g., total funds raised, number of donors, etc.) from the "How Will You Measure Success" dropdown menu.
17. Enter any potential challenges or obstacles anticipated for the campaign in the "Potential Challenges" field.
18. Select the types of support needed for the campaign (e.g., strategy consulting, marketing materials, etc.) from the "What Support Do You Need" dropdown menu.
19. Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether you are compliant with campaign finance laws in the "Are You Compliant with Campaign Finance Laws" field.
20. Select the tax-exempt status of the organization (if applicable) from the "Tax-Exempt Status" dropdown menu.
21. Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether you have a fiscal sponsor for the campaign in the "Do You Have a Fiscal Sponsor" field.
22. Enter any additional notes or comments about the campaign in the "Additional Notes or Comments" field.
