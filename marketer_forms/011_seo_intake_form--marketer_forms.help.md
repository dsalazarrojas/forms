# SEO Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SEO Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from clients or customers seeking search engine optimization services. This form helps the SEO team to understand the client's needs and expectations, allowing us to provide tailored solutions for their SEO projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and contact information in the "Client Name" and "Website" fields.
2. Enter your phone number (if applicable) in the "Phone Number" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter a brief description of your project or requirements in the "Description" field.
5. Enter any relevant keywords for your project in the "Keywords" field.
6. Enter your budget for the project in the "Budget" field.
7. Select a deadline for the project completion in the "Deadline" field.
8. Choose the relevant SEO strategy for your project from the "Seo Strategy" field.
9. Select the type of client you are (individual, company, or agency) in the "Client Type" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the client or customer.
- **Website** (`website`, text, required): Enter the URL of your website that needs SEO optimization.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, number, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by the SEO team.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
- **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your project or requirements.
- **Keywords** (`keywords`, text, optional): Enter any relevant keywords for your project.
- **Budget** (`budget`, number, optional): Enter your budget for the project.
- **Deadline** (`deadline`, date, required): Select a specific date for the project completion.
- **Seo Strategy** (`seo_strategy`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more of the available SEO strategies.
- **Client Type** (`client_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of client you are (individual, company, or agency).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your project requirements are met.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please reach out to the SEO team.
* The SEO team will review your form submission and get in touch with you shortly after receipt.
