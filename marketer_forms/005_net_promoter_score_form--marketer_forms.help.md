# Net Promoter Score Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Net Promoter Score Form is designed to collect feedback from customers on how likely they are to recommend a brand. This form aims to measure customer loyalty and provide insights for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the title of the form to understand the purpose.
2. Select one of the options for "customer_segment" to indicate how likely you are to recommend the brand.
3. If you want to provide a numerical rating, enter a number between 0 and 10 in the "Net Promoter Score" field.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, type them in the "Any further comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Segment**: Select one of the options to indicate how likely you are to recommend the brand. The options are:
	+ Very Likely
	+ Not very likely
	+ Not at all likely
* **Net Promoter Score**: If you want to provide a numerical rating, enter a number between 0 and 10 to indicate how likely you are to recommend the brand.
	+ The score should be between 0 and 10, with 0 indicating it is not likely at all and 10 indicating very likely.
* **Any further comments**: If you have any additional comments or feedback, type them in this field.

## Tips
- Be honest and genuine with your response.
- Provide as much detail as possible in the "Any further comments" field to help us understand your opinion better.
- If you are not sure or have any concerns, feel free to ask for clarification.
