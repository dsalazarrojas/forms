# Contact Form With Round Corners - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users for marketing inquiries and partnerships. It is designed to be completed by users who are interested in collaborating with our marketing team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Fill in your full name in the "Contact name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email address" field.
4. If you are representing a company, enter your organization's name in the "Company name" field.
5. Describe the type of campaign or partnership you are interested in, in the "Campaign interest" field.
6. If applicable, provide an approximate budget for marketing activities in the "Budget estimate" field.
7. Choose a desired start date or timeframe for the project in the "Project timeline" field.
8. Indicate your preferred contact method in the "Preferred contact method" field.
9. Optionally, provide any additional information that will help our marketing team in the "Additional information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact name** (required): Enter your full name to help us identify you.
* **Email address** (required): Enter your best email address for us to contact you.
* **Company name** (optional): If you represent a company, enter its name.
* **Campaign interest** (required): Describe the type of campaign or partnership you are interested in.
* **Budget estimate** (optional): Provide an approximate budget for marketing activities.
* **Project timeline** (optional): Choose a desired start date or timeframe for the project.
* **Preferred contact method** (optional): Indicate your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, Video call).
* **Additional information** (optional): Provide any other details that will help our marketing team.
