# Content Idea Suggestion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a suggestion box for content ideas. It helps us understand what topics you're interested in and what type of content would be most valuable to you. Your input will help us create content that matters to you and our community.

## How To Complete This Form
To get started, simply fill out the form with your idea, and we'll take care of the rest. You can skip any fields that you're not comfortable filling out, as they are marked as optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Share Your Ideas --** (`welcome_note`, note, not required): This is just a friendly welcome message.
* **Name (Optional)** (`name_optional`, text, not required): If you'd like to share your name with us, you can do so here.
* **Email Address (Optional)** (`email_optional`, email, not required): If you'd like to receive updates or questions from us, please provide your email address.
* **What topic should we cover next** (`suggested_topic`, text, required): Describe the topic you're interested in.
* **Why is this topic interesting to you** (`importance_factor`, text, required): Explain what problem it solves or what it explains.
* **Are there specific questions you want answered** (`specific_questions`, text, not required): If you have some questions about this topic, list them here.
* **How would you like to see this content** (`content_format_preference`, select_one, required): Choose the format that best suits your needs (e.g. Long-form Article, Short Video, etc.).
* **What is your current knowledge level on this topic** (`current_skill_level`, select_one, required): Tell us about your level of expertise, so we can tailor the content accordingly.
* **Would you like to be notified if we use your idea** (`notify_on_publish`, select_one, required): Let us know if you'd like to be contacted about your idea.

## Tips
* You can skip any fields that you're not comfortable filling out.
* Be as specific as possible when describing the topic and why it interests you.
* We'll take care of the content creation and formatting based on your input.
* If we decide to use your idea, we'll let you know and may ask for further questions or clarification.
