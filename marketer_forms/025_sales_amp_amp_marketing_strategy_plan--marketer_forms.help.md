# Sales & Marketing Strategy Plan - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help marketers and sales teams develop a comprehensive sales and marketing strategy plan that aligns with business goals and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form section by section, starting with the top section.
2. Answer each question carefully and accurately, using the options provided.
3. Review and update the form regularly to reflect changing market conditions and customer needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title**: (`form_title`, text, required)
	+ Enter a brief title for your sales and marketing strategy plan.
* **Marketing Strategy Plan**: (`marketing_strategy_plan`, text, required)
	+ Describe your marketing strategy plan in a few sentences.
* **Sales Strategy Plan**: (`sales_strategy_plan`, text, optional)
	+ Describe your sales strategy plan in a few sentences.
* **Marketing Goals**: (`marketing_goals`, select_one, required)
	+ Choose one of the following marketing goals:
		- Increase website traffic
		- Increase conversion rates
		- Increase customer engagement
		- Increase customer satisfaction
		- Increase sales
* **Sales Goals**: (`sales_goals`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Choose one or more of the following sales goals:
		- Increase revenue
		- Increase profitability
		- Increase market share
* **Marketing Channels**: (`marketing_channels`, text, required)
	+ List the marketing channels you plan to use to achieve your marketing goals.
* **Sales Channels**: (`sales_channels`, text, optional)
	+ List the sales channels you plan to use to achieve your sales goals.
* **Target Audience**: (`target_audience`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Choose one or more of the following target audience demographics:
		- Demographic: age group
		- Demographic: gender
		- Geographic location
		- Psychographic: interests
		- Psychographic: behavior
* **Sales Strategy**: (`sales_strategy`, text, required)
	+ Describe your sales strategy in a few sentences.
* **Marketing Budget**: (`marketing_budget`, number, required)
	+ Enter your marketing budget.
* **Sales Budget**: (`sales_budget`, number, required)
	+ Enter your sales budget.
* **Sales and Marketing Manager**: (`sales_and_marketing_manager`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the name and contact information of the sales and marketing manager responsible for this strategy.
* **Marketing Manager**: (`marketing_manager`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the name and contact information of the marketing manager responsible for this strategy.
* **Sales Manager**: (`sales_manager`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the name and contact information of the sales manager responsible for this strategy.
* **Start Date**: (`date_start`, date, required)
	+ Enter the start date of your sales and marketing strategy.
* **End Date**: (`date_end`, date, required)
	+ Enter the end date of your sales and marketing strategy.
* **Start Time**: (`time_start`, time, required)
	+ Enter the start time of your sales and marketing strategy.
* **End Time**: (`time_end`, time, required)
	+ Enter the end time of your sales and marketing strategy.
* **Email**: (`email`, email, optional)
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: (`phone`, text, optional)
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Note**: (`note`, note, optional)
	+ Add any additional notes or comments about your sales and marketing strategy.
* **Customer Service**: (`customer_service`, text, optional)
	+ Describe your customer service process.
* **Customer Support**: (`customer_support`, text, optional)
	+ Describe your customer support process.
* **Customer Feedback**: (`customer_feedback`, text, optional)
	+ Describe how you will collect and act on customer feedback.
* **Submitted**: (`submitted`, select_one, optional)
	+ Confirm whether your sales and marketing strategy has been submitted for approval.

## Tips

- Make sure to review and update your sales and marketing strategy regularly to reflect changing market conditions and customer needs.
- Use this form as a starting point for ongoing sales and marketing discussions and decision-making.
- Keep your sales and marketing strategy concise and focused on key goals and objectives.
