# newsletter_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with our newsletter. The goal is to gather information on what we're doing well and what we can improve to better serve our audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "What is your name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Rate your satisfaction with the newsletter's content, design, and overall experience using the "Satisfaction score out of 5" field.
4. If you'd like to share any thoughts or comments about your experience, use the "Comments" field.
5. Select the type of email client you use in the "Email client" field.
6. Optionally, you can add any additional comments or notes about your experience in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name** (`name`, text, required: false):
    Enter your name as it appears on your official records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false):
    Enter your email address that you use to receive our newsletter.
* **Satisfaction score out of 5** (`satisfaction_score`, number, required: false):
    Rate your satisfaction with the newsletter's content, design, and overall experience. Please use a score from 1 to 5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false):
    Share any thoughts or comments about your experience with our newsletter.
* **Email type** (`email_type`, select_one, required: true):
    Select the type of email client you use to receive our newsletter. Please choose from the options provided.
* **Email client** (`email_client`, select_one, required: true):
    Select the email client you use to access your email.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false):
    Enter the date you started or last used our newsletter.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false):
    Enter the time you last used our newsletter.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false):
    Add any additional notes or comments about your experience with our newsletter.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false):
    Enter your phone number (if you'd like to share it).

Tips:

* Please be as specific as possible when filling out the "Comments" and "Notes" fields.
* Make sure to rate your satisfaction accurately and honestly.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to reach out to us for clarification.
