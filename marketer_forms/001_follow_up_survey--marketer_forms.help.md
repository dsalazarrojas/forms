# Follow Up Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Follow Up Survey form is designed to gather customer feedback on a company or individual. It is a simple, easy-to-use form that allows customers to provide their opinions and suggestions.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in the form title.
2. Choose the relevant category for this follow up survey (Marketer Forms or Other).
3. Optionally, provide a short description of the survey.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Follow Up Survey
* **Follow Up Survey (1)** (`follow_up_survey`, text, Optional): Enter a brief title for the survey.
### Page 2: Category
* **Category (2)** (`category`, Select One, Optional): Choose the category for this follow up survey (e.g., Marketer Forms, Other).
### Page 3: Description
* **Description (3)** (`description`, note, Optional): A field for providing additional context about this follow up survey (e.g., a brief explanation of the purpose and goals of the survey). 

Note: The "Description" field is intended to provide further context and information about the survey, but it is not required to be filled in. The "Category" field is also optional, but selecting a relevant category will help in organizing and categorizing the survey. 

## Tips
- Make sure to select a clear and descriptive title for the survey.
- Choose the most relevant category for this follow up survey.
- If you have additional information about the survey, use the description field to provide more context.
