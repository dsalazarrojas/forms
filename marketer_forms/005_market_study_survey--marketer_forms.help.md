This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from customers who have purchased or are interested in purchasing a product or service. The goal is to gather information about their experiences and preferences, which can be used to improve the overall customer experience and inform business decisions.

Before starting, please ensure that you have not already completed this form, as it may help prevent duplicate submissions.

# market_study_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The market study survey is a tool used to collect feedback and opinions from customers, helping us to better understand their needs and preferences. Your input will help us improve our product or service and provide a better experience for all our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your **Customer Name** in the text field.
* Enter your **Email** address in the format of `username@example.com`.
* Provide your **Phone** number for contact purposes.
* Choose how you **Opinion of Service** by selecting one of the options.
* Select all that apply for your **Buying Habits**.
* Enter the **Date Purchased Product** in the format of `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* Enter the **Time Purchased Product** in the format of `HH:MM`.
* Add any additional **Notes** about your experience, if you have any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address in the format of `username@example.com`.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Provide your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Opinion of Service** (select_one, required/optional): Choose how you feel about our service. Options are:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Buying Habits** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply about your buying habits. Options are:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Date Purchased Product** (date, required/optional): Enter the date you purchased the product in the format of `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Time Purchased Product** (time, required/optional): Enter the time you purchased the product in the format of `HH:MM`.
* **Notes** (note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.
