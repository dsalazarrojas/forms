# Copywriter Job Application Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form template is designed to collect information from potential copywriters for a job application. It is meant to be completed by candidates who are interested in a copywriting position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your relevant information and submit. Please answer all questions to the best of your ability and truthfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact info** (1): Enter your name and contact details so that we can reach you.
* **Job Application** (2): Describe the job you are applying for. This can be a job title or a job type.
* **Experience** (3): Describe your work experience as a copywriter. This is an opportunity to showcase your skills and achievements.
* **Portfolio** (4): Share a link to your portfolio or attach a file of your work. This will give us an idea of your writing skills and style.
* **Education** (5): Select your level of education: Bachelor's, Master's, or PhD.
* **Job title** (6): Enter the job title you are applying for.
* **Desired salary** (7): Enter your desired salary. Please be realistic and reasonable.
* **Work experience** (8): Describe your work experience as a copywriter. This can be freelance or full-time.
* **Available start date** (9): Enter the date you are available to start working.
* **Availability** (10): Describe your availability, e.g., full-time, part-time, or flexible hours.
* **Job type** (11): Select the type of job you are applying for: Freelance, Full-time, or Part-time.
* **Resume** (12): Attach or link your resume. This will give us a better understanding of your qualifications.
* **Cover letter** (13): Attach or link your cover letter. This should be a brief introduction to your application.
* **Portfolio link** (14): Share a link to your portfolio or attach a file of your work.
* **Referee** (15): List at least two referees who can vouch for your experience and skills.
* **Job ID** (16): Enter the ID of the job you are applying for.
* **Job URL** (17): Enter the URL of the job you are applying for.
* **Job status** (18): Select the status of the job you are applying for: Open, Closed, or In Progress.
* **Submitted by** (19): Select who submitted the application: Jimmy, Jane, or John.
* **Submitted date** (20): Enter the date the application was submitted.
* **Updated by** (21): Select who updated the application: Jimmy, Jane, or John.
* **Updated date** (22): Enter the date the application was updated.
* **Assigned tool** (23): Enter the tool assigned to the job.
* **Created by** (24): Select who created the application: ChatJIMMY, Jane, or John.
* **Created date** (25): Enter the date the application was created.
