# Employee Timesheet Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Timesheet Template is a form used to record employee time spent on various tasks and projects. This form captures employee-specific information, time entries, and approval status to facilitate accurate time tracking and management.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Employee Timesheet Template form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your employee information in the "Employee Information" page.
2. Enter the time entry details, including start and end time, hours worked, and work location.
3. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field.
4. Select the "Submit" option to submit the form for approval.
5. Choose the desired approval status from the "Approve" options.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information** (`employee_timesheet_template_page_1`, text, required): Enter your employee name, ID, and other relevant details as required.
* **Time Entry** (`time_entry`, number, required): Record the total time spent on tasks and projects.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date for which you are logging time.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of your work period.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of your work period.
* **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, number, required): Enter the total hours worked.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, required): Specify the location where you worked.
* **Supervisor Comments** (`supervisor_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or feedback from your supervisor.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required): Choose to submit the form for approval.
* **Save Draft** (`save_draft`, select_multiple, optional): Select to save the form as a draft for later completion.
* **Approve** (`approve`, select_one, required): Choose to approve the form.
* **Reject** (`reject`, select_one, required): Choose to reject the form.
* **Delete** (`delete`, select_one, optional): Select to delete the form.
* **Submit and Approve** (`submit_and_approve`, select_one, optional): Choose to submit and approve the form.
* **Submit and Delete** (`submit_and_delete`, select_one, optional): Select to submit and delete the form.
* **Submit and Save Draft** (`submit_and_save_draft`, select_one, optional): Choose to submit and save the form as a draft.
* **Submit and Approve and Delete** (`submit_and_approve_and_delete`, select_one, optional): Select to submit, approve, and delete the form.
* **Save and Approve** (`save_and_approve`, select_one, optional): Choose to save and approve the form.
* **Save and Approve and Delete** (`save_and_approve_and_delete`, select_one, optional): Select to save, approve, and delete the form.
* **Save Draft and Approve** (`save_draft_and_approve`, select_one, optional): Choose to save as a draft and approve the form.
* **Save Draft and Approve and Delete** (`save_draft_and_approve_and_delete`, select_one, optional): Select to save as a draft and approve and delete the form.
* **Submit and Delete and Approve** (`submit_and_delete_and_approve`, select_one, optional): Select to submit, delete, and approve the form.
* **Save and Delete** (`save_and_delete`, select_one, optional): Choose to save and delete the form.
* **Save and Approve** (`save_and_approve`, select_one, optional): Choose to save and approve the form.

This template provides options for different actions, allowing you to customize the submission and approval process.
