# Personal Information Sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Information Sheet form is a collection of fields that gather essential details about an individual. It is intended to be filled out once to provide a comprehensive profile.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Date of Birth in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Insurance Number, which is a unique identifier.
3. Provide your address in two lines (Address 1 and Address 2).
4. Enter your Phone Number and Email address.
5. Double-check that all fields are correctly filled out before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (First Name)**: Enter your personal name as it is typically spelled.
* **Last Name (Last Name)**: Enter your surname or family name.
* **Date of Birth (Date of Birth)**: Provide your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY or YYYYMMDD.
* **Insurance Number (Insurance Number)**: Enter your unique identifier for insurance purposes.
* **Address 1 (Address 1)**: Enter your primary address line 1.
* **Address 2 (Address 2)**: Enter your primary address line 2.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your unique email address.

## Tips
- Make sure all fields are correctly filled out before submission.
- Ensure that your Insurance Number is entered correctly, as it may be used for identification purposes.
- Use the format MM/DD/YYYY for the Date of Birth field.
