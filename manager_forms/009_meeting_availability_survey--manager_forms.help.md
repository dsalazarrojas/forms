# Meeting Availability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to help meeting requesters provide detailed information about their meeting requests. It allows managers to review and respond to these requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the requested information for your meeting request. Make sure to include all required fields. For each field, refer to the explanations below for more details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Meeting Requester Info** (`meeting_requester_info`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the person requesting the meeting.
- **Meeting Title** (`meeting_title`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief title for the meeting.
- **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time_request`, `time`, optional): Enter the meeting time in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM).
- **Meeting Duration** (`meeting_duration`, `text`, optional): Enter the meeting duration in minutes or hours (e.g., 30 minutes or 1 hour).
- **Attendees** (`attendees`, `text`, optional): List the names and titles of the people attending the meeting.
- **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the meeting location.
- **Meeting Description** (`meeting_description`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of the meeting purpose.
- **Meeting Start Time** (`meeting_start_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the meeting start time in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM).
- **Meeting End Time** (`meeting_end_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the meeting end time in 24-hour format (e.g., 15:00 for 3:00 PM).
- **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the meeting date in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2024-02-15 for February 15, 2024).
- **Request Date** (`meeting_request_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the meeting was requested.
- **Manager Response Status** (`manager_response_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the manager's response: Approved, Denied, or Pending.
- **Manager Comment** (`manager_comment`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments or feedback from the manager.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to help the manager make an informed decision.
- If the manager response status is Pending, be sure to follow up to confirm the status.
