# Golf Scorecard Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Golf Scorecard Template is a form used to record and report player scores, rounds completed, and other information related to a golf game.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the player's name in the "Player Name" field.
2. Input the round number the player completed.
3. Select the golf course the player played at.
4. Enter the date and time of the scorecard.
5. Enter the player's scorecard number (if applicable).
6. Enter the number of rounds completed.
7. Enter the player's score.
8. Add any additional notes about the game (optional).
9. Enter the player's email address (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Player Name
* **Player Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the player who completed the round.

### Round Number
* **Round Number** (number, optional): Enter the round number the player completed.

### Golf Course
* **Golf Course** (text, optional): Select the golf course the player played at.

### Scorecard Date
* **Scorecard Date** (date, optional): Enter the date the scorecard was submitted.

### Scorecard Time
* **Scorecard Time** (time, optional): Enter the time the scorecard was submitted.

### Player Scorecard Number
* **Player Scorecard Number** (text, optional): Enter the player's scorecard number (if applicable).

### Rounds Completed
* **Rounds Completed** (number, optional): Enter the number of rounds completed by the player.

### Score
* **Score** (number, optional): Enter the player's score.

### Scorecard Notes
* **Scorecard Notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the game.

### Scorecard Email
* **Scorecard Email** (email, optional): Enter the player's email address.
