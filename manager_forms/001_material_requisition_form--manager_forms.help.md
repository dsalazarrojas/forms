# Material Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Material Requisition Form is used to request materials or equipment from the inventory management system for work projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the relevant information in each field as required.
2. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Section 1 - Order Form (Page 1)

* **Order Form** (`first_page`, text, required/optional): This is the title of your order.
* **Form Section** (`form_section`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of the form section you are requesting.
* **Section Title** (`form_section_label`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief title of the form section you are requesting.
* **Section Description** (`form_section_description`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the form section you are requesting.

### Section 2 - Date and Equipment Information (Page 2)

* **Date** (`form_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you are requesting the materials or equipment for.
* **Date End** (`form_date_end`, date, required/optional): Enter the end date for the materials or equipment request.
* **Prophet** (`prophet`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if the request is approved by a prophet or 'No' otherwise.
* **Type** (`equipment_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the type of equipment you are requesting.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the type of equipment you are requesting.
* **Equipment Id** (`equipment_id`, number, required/optional): Enter the ID of the equipment you are requesting.
* **Equipment Id** (`equipment_id`, number, required/optional): Enter the ID of the equipment you are requesting.

### Section 3 - Location and Comments (Page 3)

* **Location** (`location`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the location where the materials or equipment will be used.
* **Equipment Description** (`equipment_description`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the equipment you are requesting.
* **Comments** (`form_comments`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the request.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the user assigned to the request.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the user assigned to the request.

## Tips
* Be accurate and clear in your responses to ensure the request is processed correctly.
* Make sure to enter the correct information to avoid any delays or miscommunication.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to your supervisor or the inventory management team for guidance.
