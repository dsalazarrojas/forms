# Contact Form With Fancy Header And Footer - Help Guide
## Purpose
This contact form is designed to enable users to submit messages, reports, or feedback to management, with an emphasis on urgent or critical issues. It allows users to select the relevant department and provide additional context for the management team to review.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the form header to open the form.
2. Select the department that best relates to your message.
3. Fill in your name and email address (required fields).
4. Provide your phone number and website (if relevant).
5. Enter your message with details for management review.
6. If necessary, select the urgency level of your issue (Routine, Urgent, or Critical).
7. Add any additional notes or information for management to know.
8. Click the submit button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### header
* **-- Contact Us --** (header): Use this section to review the form and understand its purpose.
### name
* **Name** (text, required): Enter your full name for correspondence with management.
### email
* **Email address** (email, required): Provide your email address for management to respond to you.
### phone
* **Phone number** (text, optional): Enter your contact phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
### website
* **Website** (text, optional): Provide your company website if it's relevant to your message.
### department
* **Department** (select_one, optional): Select the department that best relates to your message, such as Sales, Operations, or Marketing.
### message
* **Message** (text, required): Enter your message with details for management review.
### urgency
* **Urgency level** (select_one, optional): Select the urgency level of your issue, which can be Routine, Urgent, or Critical.
### additional_notes
* **Additional notes** (text, optional): Provide any additional information for management to know.
### submit
* **Submit** (submit, required): Click this button to send the form to management for review.
