# Simple Training Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by employees to request training for themselves. Managers will review and approve or reject requests submitted through this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name in the `employee_name` field.
2. Select the type of training you are requesting in the `training_type` field.
3. Enter the date you would like to receive the training in the `date_requested` field.
4. If you have any additional comments or information, type them in the `additional_comments` field.
5. Enter your email address in the `employee_email` field.
6. Enter your phone number in the `employee_phone` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`title`, not applicable, required): This is the title of the form. You cannot edit this field.
* **Training Type** (`training_type`, select_one, required): Choose from options: Basic, Intermediate, Advanced.
	+ Select the type of training you are requesting. For example, if you choose "Basic", you will be eligible for basic-level training sessions.
* **Date Requested** (`date_requested`, date, required): Enter the date you would like to receive the training in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Manager Approval Status** (`manager_approval_status`, select_one, required): This field is automatically populated by the system.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or information, type them here.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so that the system can keep you informed about the status of your training request.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Training Request Form ID** (`training_request_form_id`, text, not applicable): This is an automatically generated field and not to be used by employees.
