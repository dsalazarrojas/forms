# Proof Of Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Proof Of Delivery Form is used to record the delivery of goods or services, ensuring that both the delivery person and the customer have acknowledged receipt and verification of the delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Delivery Date** field with the date of the delivery.
2. Enter the **Signature Date** if the signature was obtained on a different date than the delivery date.
3. In the **Customer Signature** field, enter the customer's name as they signed the document.
4. Enter the **Customer Name** field with the customer's full name.
5. Enter the **Delivery Person** field with the name of the person or company that delivered the goods or services.
6. Enter the **Delivery Note** field with any relevant notes about the delivery.
7. If applicable, upload images of the **Customer Signature**, **Customer Name**, **Delivery Person**, and **Delivery Note** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date the goods or services were delivered.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date the signature was obtained, if different from the delivery date.
* **Customer Signature**: Enter the customer's name as they signed the document.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's full name.
* **Delivery Person**: Enter the name of the person or company that delivered the goods or services.
* **Delivery Note**: Enter any relevant notes about the delivery.
* **Customer Signature Image**: Upload an image of the customer's signature.
* **Customer Name Image**: Upload an image of the customer's ID or document with their name.
* **Delivery Person Image**: Upload an image of the delivery person.
* **Delivery Person Note**: Enter any notes about the delivery person.
* **Delivery Date Note**: Enter any notes about the delivery date.
* **Customer Note**: Enter any notes about the customer.
* **Delivery Note Image**: Upload an image of the delivery note.
* **Customer Note Image**: Upload an image of the customer note.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and legible information in all fields.
* If an image is required, upload a clear and legible photo or scan of the document.
* Review the form before submitting to ensure all fields are complete and accurate.
