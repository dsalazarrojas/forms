# Executive Director Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluation on the performance of an Executive Director. It is meant to be used by evaluators to provide a structured and concise way to collect their thoughts and opinions on the director's performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Executive Director's First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the Executive Director's Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter the Director's Job Title in the "Job Title" field.
4. Select the Department they belong to in the "Department" field.
5. Enter the date of the evaluation in the "Evaluation Date" field.
6. Select the evaluation time of the Director's performance in the "Evaluation Time" field.
7. Choose a performance level for the Director's performance in the "Performance Level" field.
8. Provide any feedback on the Director's performance in the "Feedback" field.
9. Suggest any recommended improvements for the Director in the "Recommended Improvements" field.
10. Rate the Director's performance as Highly Satisfactory, Unsatisfactory, or Neutral in the "Overall Rating" field.
11. Suggest any recommended action for the Director in the "Recommended Action" field.
12. Enter any additional comments on the evaluation in the "Evaluation Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the Executive Director's first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the Executive Director's last name.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the Executive Director's job title.
- **Department** (`department`, text, required/optional): Select the department the Executive Director belongs to.
- **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the evaluation.
- **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, required/optional): Select the evaluation time for the Director's performance.
- **Performance Level** (`performance_level`, select_one, required/optional): Choose a performance level for the Director's performance (High, Medium, Low).
- **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required/optional): Provide any feedback on the Director's performance.
- **Recommended Improvements** (`recommended_improvements`, text, required/optional): Suggest any recommended improvements for the Director.
- **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, required/optional): Rate the Director's performance as Highly Satisfactory, Unsatisfactory, or Neutral.
- **Recommended Action** (`recommended_action`, text, required/optional): Suggest any recommended action for the Director.
- **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments on the evaluation.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete each field thoroughly and accurately to ensure a fair and structured evaluation.
* Be specific and objective when providing feedback and suggestions.
* Keep in mind that the form is meant to be a tool for constructive improvement, not for criticism.
* Evaluate the Director's performance based on their goals and objectives.
