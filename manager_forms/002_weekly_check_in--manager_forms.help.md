# Weekly Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Check In form is designed to gather feedback and insights from users on their experience with the service. This form helps the service providers to understand user satisfaction, areas of improvement, and gather suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question based on your current experience with the service.
2. Select the relevant options for each question that best describe your feelings or situation.
3. Provide any additional comments or questions you may have in the text fields.
4. Save or submit the form to send your feedback to the service providers.
