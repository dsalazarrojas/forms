# W4 Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The W4 Form is used for tax-related purposes, collecting information from both the employee and the employer to manage withholding taxes or other employment-related benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering the employee's details, including their name, address, city, state, and zip code.
2. Provide the employer's address, city, state, and zip code.
3. Select the correct answers for the employee's and employer's tax filing status and marital status.
4. Enter the employee's date of birth, date of last payroll, and number of dependents.
5. Mark the box to confirm that the employee's W4 form is signed.
6. Enter the date the employee signed the form.
7. Confirm that the employee's W4 form is certified.
8. Provide the name of the person certifying the employee's W4 form.
9. Mark the box to confirm that the employer's W4 form is signed.
10. Enter the date the employer signed the form.
10. Confirm that the employer's W4 form is certified.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (1, text, required): Enter the employee's full name.
* **Employer Address** (2, text, optional): Enter the employer's address.
* **Employer City** (3, text, optional): Enter the employer's city.
* **Employer State** (4, select_multiple, optional): Select the state where the employer is located.
* **Employer Zip** (5, number, optional): Enter the employer's zip code.
* **Employee Address** (6, text, optional): Enter the employee's address.
* **Employee City** (7, text, optional): Enter the employee's city.
* **Employee State** (8, select_multiple, optional): Select the state where the employee is located.
* **Employee Zip** (9, number, optional): Enter the employee's zip code.
* **Social Security Number** (10, number, optional): Enter the employee's social security number.
* **Date of Birth** (11, date, optional): Enter the employee's date of birth.
* **Date of Last Payroll** (12, date, optional): Enter the date of the last payroll period.
* **Employee Employer Tax Filing Status** (13, select_one, optional): Select the employee's tax filing status.
* **Employee Marital Status** (14, select_one, optional): Select the employee's marital status.
* **Employee Number of Dependents** (15, number, optional): Enter the number of dependents the employee has.
* **Employee W4 Form Signed** (16, note, optional): Confirm that the employee has signed the form.
* **Employee W4 Form Signed Date** (17, date, optional): Enter the date the employee signed the form.
* **Employee W4 Form Certified** (18, select_one, optional): Confirm that the employee's form is certified.
* **Employee W4 Form Certified by** (19, text, optional): Enter the name of the person certifying the employee's form.
* **Employer W4 Form Signed** (20, select_one, optional): Confirm that the employer has signed the form.
* **Employer W4 Form Signed Date** (21, date, optional): Enter the date the employer signed the form.
* **Employer W4 Form Certified** (22, select_one, optional): Confirm that the employer's form is certified.
