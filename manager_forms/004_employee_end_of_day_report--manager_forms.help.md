# employee_end_of_day_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee end of day report is used to track employee activity and progress at the end of each day. It captures essential information about the employee's status, allowing for efficient monitoring and analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the appropriate page for the current day.
2. Enter the required fields for each page, starting with Employee Id.
3. Fill out the remaining fields according to your daily progress and tasks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Id
* **Employee Id** (`number`, required): Enter the unique identifier of the employee filling out this form.
### Date
* **Date** (`date`, optional): Select the date this form is being completed. This field is optional, but it helps for tracking progress over time.
### Task Status
* **Task Status** (`select one`, optional): Choose from the following options:
	+ In Progress
	+ Done
	+ Not Started
### Notes
* **Notes** (`note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your daily progress.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number for reference.
### Email
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address for reference.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields for accurate tracking.
* Review your progress and adjust your task status accordingly.
* Keep your notes concise and relevant.
* Use your email address for direct contact, in case of any issues or queries.
