This form is designed to collect anonymous feedback from employees, customers, or any other individuals related to an organization. The purpose of this form is to gather information about areas that need improvement, concerns, or suggestions for the organization. This form will help the management to identify and address potential issues, improve processes, and enhance the overall work environment. This form is anonymous to ensure that users feel comfortable providing honest feedback without fear of repercussions.

Before providing feedback, please ensure that you haven't already submitted this feedback previously. This will help the management to avoid duplicate work and allocate resources effectively.

## Purpose
This form is intended to be a tool for the management to gather feedback and make informed decisions to improve the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of feedback you are providing (e.g., General Feedback, Management Concerns, etc.).
2. Choose your department, if you wish to help us categorize your feedback (optional).
3. Select the urgency level of your feedback (e.g., Low - Can wait, High - Within 48 hours, etc.).
4. Enter a brief title for your feedback.
5. Provide detailed feedback about your concerns or suggestions.
6. If your feedback is related to a specific incident, indicate if it is or isn't.
7. If applicable, provide the date of the incident.
8. Approximate the number of people involved.
9. Confirm if you have reported this issue before.
10. Describe how this issue has impacted you or your work.
11. If you have a suggested solution, share it with us.
12. Indicate if you would like follow-up on your feedback.
13. Add any additional comments or thoughts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Feedback Category**: Select the type of feedback you are providing (e.g., General Feedback, Management Concerns, etc.).
- **Department**: Select your department, if you wish to help us categorize your feedback (optional).
- **Urgency Level**: Choose the urgency level of your feedback (e.g., Low - Can wait, Medium - Within a week, etc.).
- **Main Topic of Feedback**: Enter a brief title for your feedback.
- **Detailed Feedback**: Provide as much detail as possible about your concerns or suggestions.
- **Is this regarding a specific incident?**: Indicate if your feedback is related to a specific incident.
- **Date of Incident**: If applicable, provide the date of the incident.
- **Number of People Involved**: Approximate the number of people involved.
- **Have you reported this before?**: Confirm if you have reported this issue previously.
- **How has this impacted you or your work?**: Describe the effects of this issue.
- **Do you have a suggested solution?**: Share your ideas for improvement, if applicable.
- **Would you like follow-up on this feedback?**: Indicate if you would like updates on your feedback.
- **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or thoughts.

The form submission date will be auto-filled with the current date.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible.
* Ensure that your feedback is accurate and relevant to the organization.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
