# Department Purchase Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Department Purchase Request Form is designed to help department managers request purchases for their respective departments. This form should only be filled out by authorized department managers to request items or services needed for departmental activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are an authorized department manager.
2. Fill out the form accurately and truthfully with the requested information.
3. Provide a clear and concise description of the item or service being requested.
4. Specify the store address where the item or service can be purchased.
5. Indicate the reason for the purchase.
6. Enter the date the item or service is needed.
7. Submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Department name** (text, optional): Enter the name of your department.
* **Store address** (text, optional): Enter the address of the store where the item or service can be purchased.
* **Store name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the store where the item or service can be purchased.
* **Purchase item description** (text, optional): Provide a brief description of the item or service being requested.
* **Purchase reason** (text, optional): Explain the reason for the purchase, including any relevant details.
* **Purchase date** (date, optional): Enter the date the item or service is needed.
