# Simple Time Sheet Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be used by employees to report their time worked and activities performed during a specific time period. The purpose of this form is to provide an accurate record of employee work hours and activities for managerial review and approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Employee Id field with your employee identification number.
2. Enter the Date of the period for which you are reporting work hours.
3. Report the total Hours Worked for the specified period.
4. Describe the Work Performed by providing a brief summary of your activities during the period.
5. If applicable, select the tool you were assigned to use during this period.
6. Add any additional Notes about your work or activities.
7. If you have any Manager Comments or instructions for review, enter them in the dedicated field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Id
* **Employee Id** (`employee_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter your employee identification number to associate your work hours with your personnel record.

### Date
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date for which you are reporting work hours.

### Hours Worked
* **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, `number`, required: false): Report the total hours worked for the specified period.

### Work Performed
* **Work Performed** (`work_performed`, `text`, required: false): Describe your activities and work performed during the period.

### Assigned Tool
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required: false): If you were assigned a tool for use during this period, select it from the list of options.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about your work or activities.

### Manager Comments
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, `text`, required: false): If you have any instructions or comments from your manager, enter them here.
