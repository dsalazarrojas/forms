# Identity Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Identity Verification Form is designed to collect information about an individual's identity, specifically for employment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out all the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Make sure to review your submission before submitting it, as any mistakes may cause issues with the verification process.
3. Only submit the form once you have completed all the required fields and have reviewed your information for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Employment** (`new_employment`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your new employment status.
* **Ssn** (`ssn`, number, required/optional): Enter your Social Security number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your current address.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format of month/day/year (MM/DD/YY).
* **Driver License Number** (`driver_license_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your driver's license number.
* **Driver License State** (`driver_license_state`, select_one, required/optional): Select the state where your driver's license was issued from the list below:
	+ California
	+ New York
	+ Florida
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required/optional): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Employee ID Type** (`employee_id_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select your employee ID type from the list below:
	+ W-2
	+ O-1
	+ O-4
* **Ssn Issue Date** (`ssn_issue_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date your Social Security number was issued (if applicable).
* **Driver License Issue Date** (`driver_license_issue_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date your driver's license was issued (if applicable).
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out all the required fields and review your submission carefully before submitting it. If you need any further assistance, please contact our support team.
