# Fitness Gym Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for users to register for a membership at a fitness gym. It collects personal and membership information, payment details, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name and contact information.
2. Select your membership details, including membership type, term, and start and end dates.
3. Choose your payment method and payment cycle.
4. Enter your emergency contact information, including their name, relationship to you, and contact details.
5. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member Information** (`member_name`, `member_phone`, `member_email`): Enter your personal details, including your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Member Information** (`member_details`): This field is not currently used in the form, but it is a required field for the form itself.
* **Membership Details** (`membership_type`, `membership_term`, `membership_start_date`, `membership_end_date`): Select your membership type, term, start date, and end date. The membership type options are Basic, Premium, and Advanced. The term options are Month, 3 months, 6 months, and 12 months.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_gateway`, `payment_method`, `payment_amount`, `payment_cycle`): Choose your payment gateway (Stripe, PayPal, or Authorize), payment method (Credit Card, Cash, or Bank Transfer), payment amount, and payment cycle (Monthly, Annually, or Quarterly).
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_name`, `emergency_contact_relationship`, `emergency_contact_phone`, `emergency_contact_email`, `emergency_contact_address`): Enter the name, relationship, phone number, email address, and address of your emergency contact.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`): Enter the amount you wish to pay for your membership.
* **Payment Cycle** (`payment_cycle`): Select your payment cycle (Monthly, Annually, or Quarterly).
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`): Select your membership type (Basic, Premium, or Advanced).
* **Membership Term** (`membership_term`): Select your membership term (Month, 3 months, 6 months, or 12 months).
* **Start Date** (`membership_start_date`): Enter the start date of your membership.
* **End Date** (`membership_end_date`): Enter the end date of your membership.
* **Payment Gateway** (`payment_gateway`): Choose your payment gateway (Stripe, PayPal, or Authorize).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Choose your payment method (Credit Card, Cash, or Bank Transfer).
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`): Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact (Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, or Friend).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
