# design_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from clients on a design submission. It ensures that the client's response and feedback are recorded and tracked for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please review the design carefully before completing this form.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Client Response" field to indicate whether you have reviewed the design.
3. Enter the date you reviewed the design in the "Date" field.
4. Enter the time you reviewed the design in the "Time" field.
5. Provide your feedback in the "Client Feedback" field.
6. Enter the name of the person who designed the design in the "Designer" field.
7. Enter the ID number of the design you are providing feedback on in the "Design ID" field.
8. (Optional) You may enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Custom Fields" fields, which are customizable and intended to capture specific information about your response and feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit Design**: This field is used to submit the design for review. Please make sure to enter the correct design ID in this field.
* **Client Response**: This field is used to indicate whether you have reviewed the design or not. Please select "Yes" if you have reviewed the design, and "No" if you have not.
* **Date**: This field is used to enter the date you reviewed the design. Please use the correct date format (MM/DD/YY).
* **Time**: This field is used to enter the time you reviewed the design. Please use the correct time format (HH:MM).
* **Client Feedback**: This field is used to provide any feedback or comments about the design.
* **Designer**: This field is used to enter the name of the person who designed the design.
* **Design ID**: This field is used to enter the ID number of the design you are providing feedback on.
* **Custom Fields**: These fields are used to capture any additional comments or feedback that may not be included in the other fields. Please use these fields as needed.
