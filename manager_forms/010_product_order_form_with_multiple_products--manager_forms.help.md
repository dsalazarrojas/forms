# Product Order Form With Multiple Products - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Order Form With Multiple Products is used to collect information for orders that require multiple products. This form is designed for users who need to select multiple products and provide details such as quantity, price, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the products you wish to order by choosing 'Yes' for the 'Select Product' field.
2. Once you have selected the products, choose the quantity for each product using the 'Quantity' field.
3. Enter the price of the order by filling in the 'Price' field.
4. Provide your contact information by entering your email address in the 'Email' field.
5. Enter your phone number in the 'Phone' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Product**: Choose 'Yes' for the products you wish to include in your order. This field allows you to select multiple products at once.
* **Product**: Select the specific product you wish to order.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of each selected product you wish to order.
* **Price**: Enter the total price for the selected products.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with order details.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you to confirm the order.

## Tips
* Make sure to select all products you wish to order and fill in the quantity for each one.
* Double-check your order details before submitting the form.
* If you need to order only one product, please use the [Other form] alternative.
