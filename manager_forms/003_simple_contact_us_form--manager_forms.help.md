# Simple Contact Us Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a simple way to contact us with your thoughts, feedback, and requests. Fill out the form to send your message directly to our team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide a brief subject or title for your message in the "Message Subject" field (optional).
4. Type your message in the "Your Message" field.
5. Select relevant options for "Assigned Tool", "Assigned To", "Category", "Tags", and "Phone" fields as per your requirements.
6. Choose the correct time for your message in the "Time" field (if applicable).
7. If you have a second email address, enter it in the "Email 2" field.
8. If you have a specific time for your message, choose it in the "Time 2" field.
9. Select the correct options for "Deleted At", "Updated At", "Created At", "Deleted By", "Updated By", "Created By", and "Deleted By" fields as per your requirements.
10. Finally, submit your message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (1, text, required): Enter your name for identification purposes.
- **Email** (2, text, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
- **Message Subject** (3, text, optional): Provide a brief title or subject for your message.
- **Your Message** (4, note, required): Type your message in this field.
- **Form ID** (5, number, optional): Enter a unique ID for the form (not currently used).
- **Assigned Tool** (6, select_one, required): Choose a tool or resource that this message is related to.
- **Created At** (7, date, optional): Select the date when this message was created (not currently used).
- **Updated At** (8, date, optional): Select the date when this message was updated (not currently used).
- **Deleted At** (9, date, optional): Select the date when this message was deleted (not currently used).
- **Assigned To** (10, select_multiple, optional): Choose people assigned to handle this message.
- **Category** (11, select_multiple, optional): Select relevant categories for your message.
- **Tags** (12, select_multiple, optional): Add relevant tags to your message.
- **Created By** (13, select_multiple, optional): Choose the person who created this message.
- **Updated By** (14, select_multiple, optional): Choose the person who updated this message.
- **Deleted By** (15, select_multiple, optional): Choose the person who deleted this message.
- **Phone** (16, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Time** (17, time, optional): Choose the time for your message (if applicable).
- **Email 2** (18, email, optional): Enter a second email address (if applicable).
- **Created At 2** (19, date, optional): Select the date when this message was created (not currently used).
- **Updated At 2** (20, date, optional): Select the date when this message was updated (not currently used).
- **Deleted At 2** (21, date, optional): Select the date when this message was deleted (not currently used).
- **Deleted At 3** (22, date, optional): Select the date when this message was deleted (not currently used).
- **Time 2** (23, time, optional): Choose the time for your message (if applicable).
- **Assigned To ID** (24, number, optional): Enter a unique ID for the person assigned to handle this message (not currently used).
- **Assigned To Id 2** (25, number, optional): Enter a unique ID for the person assigned to handle this message (not currently used).
