# Company Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Company Feedback Form is a crucial tool for us to gather insights from customers about their experiences with our company. It helps us understand what we're doing right and identify areas for improvement. By filling out this form, you'll be providing us with valuable feedback that we'll use to enhance our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
* Please fill in your customer name to help us better understand who provided the feedback.
* Provide your email address if you'd like us to contact you in case we need more information.
* Select the product or service you're providing feedback about.
* Rate your overall satisfaction with the product or service.
* Describe your experience in detail.
* If you'd like us to follow up on your feedback, please select the "Request follow up" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer full name (1)**: Please fill in your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact email (2)**: If you'd like us to contact you about your feedback, please provide your email address.
* **Product or service (3)**: Select the product or service you're providing feedback about.
* **Rating (4)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the product or service. You can choose from Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Very poor.
* **Feedback details (5)**: Describe your experience in as much detail as possible.
* **Request follow up (6)**: Check this box if you'd like us to contact you for more information.
