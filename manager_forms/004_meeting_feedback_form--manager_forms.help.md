# meeting_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The meeting_feedback_form is a tool used to collect feedback from clients after a meeting. This form helps us understand their experience and satisfaction with the meeting, allowing us to make improvements for future meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the first field.
2. Enter the client's email address in the second field.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the third field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client who attended the meeting. This field is required.
* **email**: Enter the email address of the client. This field is required and will help us contact the client in case we need more information.
* **Comment**: Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about the meeting. This field is optional, but your input will help us improve our service.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form completely and honestly, as your feedback is valuable to us.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
- If you want to provide more feedback or suggestions, we'd be happy to receive them in the comment field.
