# quick-emergency-contact-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "quick-emergency-contact-form" is a form used to gather contact information and emergency contact numbers for individuals in need. This form is typically used by emergency responders or healthcare professionals to quickly gather essential details in critical situations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each page of the form carefully and answer all questions relevant to the individual in need.
2. On the first page (Contact Numbers), select the relevant contact numbers by clicking on the options provided.
3. Enter any necessary contact information on the second page (Contact Information) and provide the contact names.
4. If applicable, enter emergency contact numbers and contact information for any emergency contacts.
5. On the third page (Relationship), select the relationship of the individual to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Numbers** (Select Multiple): Select all contact numbers that are relevant to the individual in need.
* **Employee Numbers** (Select Multiple): Select all employee numbers that are relevant to the individual in need. (Note: This field is likely used for staff members or individuals working with the organization.)
* **Patient Numbers** (Select Multiple): Select all patient numbers that are relevant to the individual in need. (Note: This field is likely used for healthcare settings or medical facilities.)
* **Contact Information** (Note): Enter any additional contact information for the individual, such as address or phone numbers.
* **Contact Names** (Note): Enter the names of the contacts mentioned in the "Contact Information" field.
* **Emergency Contact Numbers** (Select Multiple): Select all emergency contact numbers that are relevant to the individual in need.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (Note): Enter emergency contact information for the individual.
* **Emergency Contact Names** (Note): Enter the names of the emergency contacts mentioned in the "Emergency Contact Information" field.
* **Relationship** (Select One): Select the relationship between the individual and you, such as "Mom" or "Dad".
