# Sample Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for a sample request. It is intended to be filled out by users who are relevant to the business process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to ensure you are filling it out for the correct business purpose.
2. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
3. Make sure to enter all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Page 1**: This is the first page of the form, where you will enter your basic contact information.
  * **What is your name**: Please enter your full name, as it appears on your identification.
  * **What is your email address**: Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
  * **What is the name of your business**: Enter the name of the business or organization you are representing.
  * **What is the URL of your website**: Enter the URL of your business website, if applicable.
  * **What is your phone number**: Enter your business phone number, if applicable.
* **What is your date of birth**: Please enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **What time is it**: Please enter the current time, in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your request.
* **What type of example are you looking for**: Select one of the available options for the type of example you are seeking.
* **Select multiple examples**: Select all the examples that apply to your request.
* **What date is it**: Please enter the date that is relevant to your request.
* **What time is it**: Please enter the time that is relevant to your request.
* **Select a date**: Select one of the available date options that best suits your request.
* **Select a time**: Select one of the available time options that best suits your request.
* **Select multiple dates**: Select all the dates that apply to your request.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that we can process your request correctly.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your point of contact for clarification.
* If you have any additional information that is not covered in this form, please include it in the "Note" field.
