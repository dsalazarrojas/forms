# Committee Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Committee Nomination Form is used by committee members to nominate individuals for positions on the committee. This form collects information about the nominee, the nominator, and the nomination details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the full legal name of the nominee in the "Nominee full name" field.
2. Enter the contact email address of the nominee in the "Nominee email address" field.
3. If you have the nominee's phone number, enter it in the "Nominee phone number" field (optional).
4. Specify the position being nominated for in the "Position nominated for" field.
5. Enter your name in the "Nominator name" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Nominator contact" field, which will be used for follow-up communication.
7. If you have additional information about the nominee, you can provide a brief summary of their experience and qualifications in the "Nominee statement or qualifications" field (optional).
8. Confirm that the nominee has given their consent to serve on the committee by selecting "Nominee consents" from the dropdown menu in the "Consent from nominee to serve" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nominee full name** (Required): Enter the full legal name of the nominee.
* **Nominee email address** (Required): Enter the contact email address of the nominee.
* **Nominee phone number** (Optional): If you have the nominee's phone number, enter it here (if not, leave blank).
* **Position nominated for** (Required): Specify the position or role the nominee is being nominated for.
* **Nominator name** (Required): Enter your name as the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator contact** (Required): Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Nominee statement or qualifications** (Optional): Provide a brief summary of the nominee's experience and qualifications.
* **Consent from nominee to serve** (Required): Confirm that the nominee has given their consent to serve on the committee. Select "Nominee consents" from the dropdown menu.
