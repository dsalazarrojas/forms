# Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer information and order details for a product purchase. It helps us process and fulfill orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the product name you wish to purchase.
2. Enter the price you are willing to pay for the product.
3. Specify the quantity of the product you'd like to order.
4. Provide your email address so we can contact you about your order.
5. Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you if needed.
6. Enter the date and time you'd like to receive your order.
7. Add any additional notes to your order.
8. If applicable, select any product options (e.g., Apple, Banana, Cherry, Orange).
9. Lastly, enter your name so we can personalize your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the product you'd like to purchase.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the price you are willing to pay for the product.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `text`, required/optional): Specify the quantity of the product you'd like to order.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required/optional): Provide your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you'd like to receive your order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time you'd like to receive your order.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes to your order.
* **Product Options** (`product_options`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select any applicable product options (e.g., Apple, Banana, Cherry, Orange).
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name so we can personalize your order.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out all required fields to ensure efficient order processing.
* Select the correct product options if applicable.
* If you have any questions, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will be in touch with you via the email and phone number you provided.
