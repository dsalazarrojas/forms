# Sexual Harassment Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about incidents of sexual harassment that have occurred within our organization. The purpose of this form is to gather details about the incident, including the date, time, and location of the incident, as well as the names and titles of the individuals involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by providing a brief description of the incident in the "Incident Report" field.
2. Provide the name of the witness (if applicable) in the "Witness Name" field.
3. Enter the title of the witness (if applicable) in the "Witness Title" field.
4. If the harassment was perpetrated by someone, enter their name in the "Perpetrator Name" field.
5. If the perpetrator has a title or position, enter it in the "Perpetrator Title" field.
6. Enter the date of the incident in the "Date of Incident" field, using the format MM/DD/YYYY.
7. Enter the time of the incident in the "Time of Incident" field, using the format HH:MM AM/PM.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Report** (`incident_report`, text, required): A brief description of what happened.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, text, optional): The name of the person who witnessed the incident (if applicable).
* **Witness Title** (`witness_title`, text, optional): The title or position of the witness (if applicable).
* **Perpetrator Name** (`perpetrator_name`, text, optional): The name of the person who perpetrated the harassment.
* **Perpetrator Title** (`perpetrator_title`, text, optional): The title or position of the person who perpetrated the harassment.
* **Date of Incident** (`date_of_incident`, date, optional): The date of the incident, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Incident** (`time_of_incident`, time, optional): The time of the incident, in the format HH:MM AM/PM.

## Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible when describing the incident.
* If you are reporting on behalf of someone else, ensure that you are doing so with their permission.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please reach out to the appropriate authorities within your organization.
