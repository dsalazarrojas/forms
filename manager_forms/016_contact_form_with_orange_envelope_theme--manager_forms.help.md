# Contact Form With Orange Envelope Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Contact Form With Orange Envelope Theme" is a clean and simple contact form designed for general inquiries. This form is intended to gather essential information from users to facilitate efficient communication and response.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Contact Form With Orange Envelope Theme" link to access the form.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*) symbol.
3. Select the desired department from the dropdown menu, if applicable.
4. Enter your preferred contact time, if you have a specific time window for follow-up.
5. Provide any additional information that may aid in responding to your inquiry.
6. Click the submit button to send your message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Full name** (`name`, `text`, required)
Your full name is the first and last name you'd like to be addressed by.

### 2. **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required)
Enter your email address so we can reply to your message.

### 3. **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, optional)
Your phone number is optional but can help us contact you directly.

### 4. **Subject** (`subject`, `text`, required)
Enter a brief subject that describes the purpose of your message.

### 5. **Message** (`message`, `text`, required)
Describe your request or inquiry in this text field.

### 6. **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, optional)
Choose the department you'd like to contact, such as Sales, Support, Partnerships, or Media.

### 7. **Preferred contact time** (`preferred_contact_time`, `time`, optional)
Specify a time window for follow-up, if desired.

### 8. **Additional information** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional)
Add any other details that will help us respond to your inquiry effectively.

## Tips
- Ensure you enter a valid email address for a swift response.
- If you have a specific time window for follow-up, please enter it in the format `HH:MM` (e.g., 10:00).
- Be descriptive in the 'Message' field to ensure our team understands your inquiry accurately.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
