# Time Tracker Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Time Tracker Form is used to track the time spent by employees on various projects. It helps monitor and record time spent on projects, allowing project managers to make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm if the time spent is valid.
2. Fill out your employee name as per your official ID.
3. Enter the project name you are tracking time for.
4. Enter the start date of the project.
5. Enter the end date of the project.
6. Select the project status (Active or Inactive).
7. Enter the time spent on the project in hours and minutes.
8. Enter any comments related to the project or task.
9. Fill out the form ID.
10. Fill out the category ID.
11. Fill out the assigned tool.
12. Fill out the created, updated, deleted, and updated by fields (these are likely used for administrative purposes only).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Time Tracker Form** (`Yes/No`, required): Select 'Yes' if the time spent is valid, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, required): Enter your official name as per your ID.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, required): Enter the name of the project you are tracking.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, required): Enter the start date of the project.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, required): Enter the end date of the project.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, required): Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' based on the project status.
* **Employee Comments** (`employee_comments`, optional): Enter any comments related to the project or task.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, optional): Enter any comments for the manager to review.
* **Project Comments** (`project_comments`, optional): Enter any comments related to the project.
* **Project Status Description** (`project_status_description`, optional): Enter a brief description of the project status.
* **Time Spent** (`time_spent`, required): Enter the total time spent on the project.
* **Hours Spent** (`hours_spent`, required): Enter the hours spent on the project.
* **Minutes Spent** (`minutes_spent`, required): Enter the minutes spent on the project.
* **Total Minutes Spent** (`total_minutes_spent`, required): Enter the total minutes spent on the project.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, required): Fill out the form ID.
* **Category ID** (`category_id`, required): Fill out the category ID.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, required): Fill out the assigned tool.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, not required): Enter the date and time the form was created.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, not required): Enter the date and time the form was updated.
* **Deleted At** (`deleted_at`, not required): Enter the date and time the form was deleted.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, not required): Enter the username of the user who created the form.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, not required): Enter the username of the user who updated the form.
* **Deleted By** (`deleted_by`, not required): Enter the username of the user who deleted the form.
* **Version** (`version`, not required): Enter the version of the form.
* **User ID** (`user_id`, not required): Enter the ID of the user who filled out the form.
