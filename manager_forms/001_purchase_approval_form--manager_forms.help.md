# Purchase Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Purchase Approval Form is used for verifying purchase details made by employees or departments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form by providing the required and optional information.
2. Make sure to enter the correct date and the exact purchase amount.
3. Select the category that the purchase falls under from the provided options.
4. Check the status of the purchase (Active or Inactive).
5. Review the form for completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Purchase Approval (1)**:
	+ A short description of the purchase being requested.
* **Date Approved (2)**:
	+ Enter the date the purchase is approved.
* **Purchase Amount (3)**:
	+ Enter the exact amount spent on the purchase.
* **Purchase Category (4)**:
	+ Select the category that the purchase falls under (e.g., office supplies, equipment, etc.).
* **Purchase Status (5)**:
	+ Select whether the purchase is Active or Inactive.
	+ Note: Active means the purchase has been made and is being used, while Inactive means it is not currently being used.
