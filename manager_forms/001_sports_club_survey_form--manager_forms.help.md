# Sports Club Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sports Club Survey Form is designed to gather feedback and suggestions from sports club members about their experiences and preferences. This form helps us understand what we do well and where we can improve to better serve our members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page to ensure you have all the required information.
2. Select your preferred food option (if applicable).
3. Indicate how often you visit us (if applicable).
4. Provide any suggestions for improving our facility (if applicable).
5. Enter your email address (if applicable).
6. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
7. Add any additional comments or notes (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Food Selection** (`preferred_food_selection`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have a preferred food option, or 'No' if you do not have a preference.
* **Visit Frequency** (`visit_frequency`, number, optional): Indicate how often you visit us, using the number of times per week or month you come to our facility.
* **Beverage** (`preferred_beverage`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have a preferred beverage, or 'No' if you do not have a preference.
* **Improve Facility** (`improve_facility`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions or ideas you have for improving our facility. This is a text field for you to elaborate on your ideas.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter a valid phone number that we can use to contact you if needed.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your experience.
