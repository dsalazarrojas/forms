# Training Effectiveness Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate the effectiveness of a training course, including its overall rating, relevance to the job, and potential improvements. This form helps to gather feedback from participants to improve future training courses.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start at the top of the form and move down each page, filling in the required and optional fields as needed.
2. Answer each question honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Be sure to review the form before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Effectiveness Evaluation Form 1** (`training_effectiveness_evaluation_form_1`, text, optional): This field is for the participant to provide any comments or feedback about the training course.
* **Rating** (`training_effectiveness_evaluation_form_2`, select_one, optional): Select one of the four options to rate the overall training course: Very good, Good, Fair, or Bad.
* **What were the key takeaways from the training course?** (`training_effectiveness_evaluation_form_3`, text, optional): Describe the most important things you learned from the training course.
* **Do you think the training course was relevant to your job?** (`training_effectiveness_evaluation_form_4`, select_multiple, optional): If the training course was relevant, mark 'True.' If not, mark 'False.' You can also mark both options if the training course was somewhat relevant.
* **What would you do differently next time?** (`training_effectiveness_evaluation_form_5`, text, optional): Share any suggestions or changes you would make to improve the training course.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`training_effectiveness_evaluation_form_6`, select_one, optional): Select one of the four options to rate your overall satisfaction with the training course: Very High, High, Medium, or Low.
* **Training Course** (`training_effectiveness_evaluation_form_7`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the training course was helpful or not by marking 'True' or 'False.'
* **Training Department** (`training_effectiveness_evaluation_form_8`, select_one, optional): Select the department that offered the training course.
* **Trainer Name** (`training_effectiveness_evaluation_form_9`, select_one, optional): Select the trainer's name.
* **Training Date** (`training_effectiveness_evaluation_form_10`, date, optional): Enter the date of the training course.
* **Training Time** (`training_effectiveness_evaluation_form_11`, time, optional): Enter the duration of the training course.
* **Overall Rating** (`training_effectiveness_evaluation_form_12`, select_one, optional): Select one of the five options to rate the overall effectiveness of the training course: 5, 4, 3, 2, or 1.
* **Training Topic** (`training_effectiveness_evaluation_form_13`, text, optional): Enter the topic of the training course.
* **Training Location** (`training_effectiveness_evaluation_form_14`, text, optional): Enter the location of the training course.
* **Training Method** (`training_effectiveness_evaluation_form_15`, text, optional): Describe the method used in the training course.
* **Training Content** (`training_effectiveness_evaluation_form_16`, text, optional): Describe the content covered in the training course.
* **Training Resources** (`training_effectiveness_evaluation_form_17`, text, optional): List any resources provided during the training course.
* **Training Evaluation** (`training_effectiveness_evaluation_form_18`, text, optional): Share any feedback or comments about the evaluation process.
* **Overall Recommendation** (`training_effectiveness_evaluation_form_19`, select_one, optional): Select one of the two options to recommend the training course: True or False.
* **Trainer Evaluation** (`training_effectiveness_evaluation_form_20`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the trainer was helpful or not by marking 'True' or 'False.'
* **Training Evaluation Form** (`training_effectiveness_evaluation_form_21`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the training evaluation form was helpful or not by marking 'True' or 'False.'
* **Training Evaluation Method** (`training_effectiveness_evaluation_form_22`, text, optional): Describe the method used to evaluate the training course.
* **Training Evaluation Content** (`training_effectiveness_evaluation_form_23`, text, optional): Describe the content covered during the evaluation process.
* **Training Evaluation Resources** (`training_effectiveness_evaluation_form_24`, text, optional): List any resources used during the evaluation process.
* **Training Evaluation Overall Satisfaction** (`training_effectiveness_evaluation_form_25`, select_one, optional): Select one of the four options to rate your overall satisfaction with the evaluation process: Very High, High, Medium, or Low.
* **Participant Name** (`training_effectiveness_evaluation_form_26`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Participant Email** (`training_effectiveness_evaluation_form_27`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`training_effectiveness_evaluation_form_28`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Be sure to review the form before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled in.
* Answer each question honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the training department.
