# transportation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The transportation request form is a crucial tool for users to submit their transportation-related requests. It helps administrators or customer support teams gather necessary information to facilitate the request process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the transportation request form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Name** in the text field. This is a required field and should be filled out with your full name.
2. Provide your **Email** in the email field. This is also a required field and should be a valid email address.
3. If you have a **Phone**, enter it in the text field. This field is optional, but it's helpful for us to contact you.
4. Enter your **Address** in the text field. This field is optional, but it's useful for us to know your location.
5. Select your **Date of Travel** in the calendar format (YYYY-MM-DD). This is a required field and ensures we understand when you need transportation.
6. Enter your **Destination** in the text field. This is a required field and should be the specific location you're traveling to.
7. Enter your **Travel From** in the text field. This is a required field and should be the location you're traveling from.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (text, required): Enter your full name for our records.
* **Email** (email, required): Provide a valid email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it for us to contact you.
* **Address** (text, optional): Enter your address for location context.
* **Date of Travel** (date, required): Select the date you need transportation on.
* **Destination** (text, required): Enter the specific location you're traveling to.
* **Travel From** (text, required): Enter the location you're traveling from.
