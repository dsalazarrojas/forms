# workers_profile_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The workers_profile_form is designed to collect accurate and up-to-date information about workers in the organization. This data is crucial for maintaining a reliable record of employee profiles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: First Name, Manager ID, Select One Option, and Select Multiple Options.
2. Fill out the non-required fields: Last Name, Position, Email, Phone, Date of Birth, Job Title, Department, Job Status, and Salary.
3. Select the correct Date Range, Date Picker, and Time as per your needs.
4. Add any additional Note if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it is spelled.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name as it is spelled.
- **Position** (`position`, `text`, optional): Enter your position or job title in the organization.
- **Manager ID** (`manager_id`, `number`, required): Enter your manager's ID number.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your work email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your work phone number.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your job title in the organization.
- **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional): Enter the department you belong to.
- **Job Status** (`job_status`, `text`, optional): Enter your current job status.
- **Salary** (`salary`, `number`, optional): Enter your salary amount.
- **Select One Option** (`select_one_field`, `select_one`, required): Select only one option from the dropdown list.
- **Select Multiple Options** (`select_multiple_field`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more options from the dropdown list.
- **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional note or comment.
- **Date and Time** (`date_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the date and time.
- **Date Range** (`date_range`, `text`, optional): Enter the date range.
- **Date Picker** (`date_picker`, `date`, optional): Select the date using the date picker.
- **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time.
