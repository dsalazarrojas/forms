# free-background-check-authorization-form-template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The free-background-check-authorization-form-template is a form used to obtain background check authorization from the user for employment or business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the User Information section with your name, email address, and any other relevant details.
2. In the Contact Info section, provide your contact information such as phone number and address.
3. Select the "Background Check Authorization" option to indicate your consent to the background check.
4. If "Other" is selected for "Background Check Authorization", please provide a brief note in the "Background Check Authorization Note" field to specify the reason.
5. Choose whether you have given or not given consent in the "User Consent" field.
6. Optionally, you can fill out "Date Created", "Date Updated", and "Last Updated By" fields which are automatically filled out by the system and are not required for user input.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_information`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your user information such as name and email address.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): This field is for your contact information like phone number and address.
* **Background Check Authorization** (`background_check_authorization`, select_multiple, required): This is a multiple selection field where you can choose to grant or deny authorization for a background check. Please note that "Other" is an option for custom scenarios.
* **Background Check Authorization Note** (`background_check_authorization_date`, text, optional): This field is for providing additional information or a reason if you choose "Other" for background check authorization.
* **Background Check Authorization Date** (`background_check_authorization_time`, time, optional): This field is not used in this form and is for future reference.
* **User Consent** (`user_consent`, select_one, required): This field is where you can choose whether you have given or not given consent for the background check.
* **Date Created** (`date_created`, date, optional): This field is automatically filled out by the system and is not required for user input.
* **Date Updated** (`date_updated`, date, optional): This field is automatically filled out by the system and is not required for user input.
* **Last Updated By** (`last_updated_by`, select_one, optional): This field is automatically filled out by the system and is not required for user input.
