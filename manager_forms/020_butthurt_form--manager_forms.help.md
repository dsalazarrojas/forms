# Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Incident Report Form is a tool for reporting and tracking workplace incidents, such as incidents involving offensive comments, intimidation, or harassment. This form helps gather detailed information about the incident, including its date, location, and impact on the reportee.

## How to Complete This Form

1.  Fill out this form to report a workplace incident you experienced or witnessed.
2.  Enter your name, email address, and phone number to contact you about the incident.
3.  Provide your department or team information, if applicable.
4.  Specify the date and time of the incident.
5.  Describe the location where the incident occurred.
6.  Identify the person involved in the incident.
7.  Choose the category of incident that best describes what happened.
8.  Provide a detailed description of what happened and how you felt.
9.  If applicable, include specific words or phrases used during the incident.
10. Indicate if there were witnesses to the incident.
11.  If applicable, mention any prior incidents involving the same person.
12.  Describe the impact of the incident on you.
13.  State your desired resolution to the incident.
14.  Attach any supporting documentation, if available.
15.  Choose your preferred level of confidentiality for the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Name** (1): Enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
*   **Email Address** (2): Enter your work email address so we can contact you about the incident.
*   **Phone Number** (3): Enter your work phone number so we can contact you about the incident.
*   **Department** (4): If applicable, enter your department or team name.
*   **Incident Date** (5): Enter the date when the incident occurred.
*   **Incident Time** (6): Enter the approximate time of the incident.
*   **Location of Incident** (7): Describe the location where the incident occurred.
*   **Name of Person Involved** (8): Identify the person involved in the incident.
*   **Type of Incident** (9): Choose the category that best describes the incident from the options provided (e.g., Offensive Comment, Intimidation, Harassment, etc.).
*   **Detailed Description** (10): Provide a detailed description of what happened and how you felt.
*   **Specific Words or Phrases** (11): If applicable, include specific words or phrases used during the incident.
*   **Witnesses** (12): Indicate if there were any witnesses to the incident.
*   **Prior Incidents** (13): If applicable, mention any prior incidents involving the same person.
*   **Impact on You** (14): Describe the impact of the incident on you.
*   **Desired Resolution** (15): State your desired resolution to the incident.
*   **Supporting Documentation** (16): Attach any supporting documentation, if available.
*   **Confidentiality Preference** (17): Choose your preferred level of confidentiality for the report (e.g., Strictly Confidential, Share with Management Only, etc.). 

**Tips**

*   Make sure to provide as much detail as possible when describing the incident.
*   If you have any supporting documentation, attach it to the report.
*   Choose your preferred level of confidentiality carefully, as it will determine who has access to the report.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR or management representative.
*   If you have reported a prior incident, mention this in the "Prior Incidents" section.
*   If you have any other information or concerns not covered in this form, include them in the "Detailed Description" section.
