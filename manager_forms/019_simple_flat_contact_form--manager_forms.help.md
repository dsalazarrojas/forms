# Simple Flat Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from users. It is a simple and flat form that gathers basic contact details, such as first name, last name, email, phone number, and message.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **first_name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **last_name** field.
3. Enter your valid email address in the **email** field. Make sure to follow the standard email format (local-part@domain).
4. Enter your phone number in the **phone** field, including the area code and country code (if applicable).
5. In the **message** field, provide a short description of your inquiry or message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address. This will be used to contact you back.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, including the area code and country code (if applicable).
* **Message**: Provide a short description of your inquiry or message.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter valid and accurate information to ensure we can contact you successfully.
* If you have any attachments or additional information to share, you can upload them separately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask, and we will be happy to assist you.
