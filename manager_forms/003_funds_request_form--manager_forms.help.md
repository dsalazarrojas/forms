# Funds Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Funds Request Form is a collection of fields that gather basic information about an institution's project to request funds. This guide will walk you through each field, making sure you fill it out correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you have all required and supporting information about your institution and project before starting.
- Fill in your institution and contact person details accurately.
- Enter a valid contact email and phone number to ensure we can get in touch with you.
- Be as detailed as possible when providing project information (name, description, and request date).
- Enter the fund request amount and any supporting details for the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Institution Information**: Enter your institution's details here. This will help us identify who is making the request.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for this project.
* **Contact Email**: Enter a valid email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter a valid phone number for us to contact you.
* **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project you are requesting funds for. Be as descriptive as possible.
* **Project Description**: Provide a detailed description of the project, including its objectives and goals.
* **Fund Request Date**: Enter the date you are requesting funds for. This helps us track the timeline of your request.
* **Fund Request Amount**: Enter the amount of funds you are requesting.
* **Fund Request Details**: Enter any additional details or supporting information that can help us evaluate your request.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate in your submissions.
- Double-check all fields before submitting to avoid errors.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
