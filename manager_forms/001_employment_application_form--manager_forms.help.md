# employment_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employment application form is used to collect information from job applicants for internal hiring purposes within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly with your relevant details.
- Make sure to provide a professional resume attachment if required.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (id: 1, name: first_name, type: text, required: false): Please provide your first name as per your official government documents (ID, passport, or driver's license).
* **Last Name** (id: 2, name: last_name, type: text, required: false): Please provide your last name as per your official government documents (ID, passport, or driver's license).
* **Job Title** (id: 3, name: job_title, type: text, required: false): Please provide the title of the job you are applying for or the job you are most interested in.
* **Work Email** (id: 4, name: work_email, type: email, required: false): Please provide a valid email address where we can contact you regarding your application.
* **Contact Phone** (id: 5, name: contact_phone, type: text, required: false): Please provide a contact phone number where we can reach you during business hours.
* **Work Address** (id: 6, name: work_address, type: text, required: false): Please provide your current work address, including the company name (if applicable) or your last known work address.
* **Education** (id: 7, name: education, type: text, required: false): Please describe your educational background, including any relevant degrees, certifications, or courses.
* **Resume** (id: 8, name: resume, type: text, required: false): Please attach your resume in a compatible format (.pdf, .docx, or .txt). This will help us review your application more efficiently.
* **Work History** (id: 9, name: work_history, type: text, required: false): Please provide a brief description of your work experience, including job titles, company names, and dates of employment.
* **Work Experience** (id: 10, name: work_experience, type: text, required: false): Please describe your relevant work experience, including job descriptions and achievements.
* **Job Interest** (id: 11, name: job_interest, type: text, required: false): Please indicate the job or industry you are interested in applying for.
* **Job Reference** (id: 12, name: job_reference, type: text, required: false): Please provide a contact person's name and email for at least one professional reference.
* **Job Description** (id: 13, name: job_description, type: text, required: false): Please describe the job you are applying for or the job you are most interested in.
* **Job Type** (id: 14, name: job_type, type: text, required: false): Please select the type of job you are applying for (full-time, part-time, internship, etc.).
* **Job Industry** (id: 15, name: job_industry, type: text, required: false): Please select the industry you are interested in applying for.
* **Job Location** (id: 16, name: job_location, type: text, required: false): Please select the location where you are available to work or the location you are interested in.

**Tips**

- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for clarification.
- Make sure to review your submission carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- We will get back to you within 3-5 business days to let you know we have received your application.
