# Gym Cleaning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Cleaning Checklist is a form used by gym staff or management to report on the cleanliness and safety of various aspects of the gym facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section and determine the current state of the corresponding area in the gym.
2. Check the boxes or enter the relevant information for each section.
3. Take note of any areas that require attention or improvement.
4. Enter any relevant comments or notes for each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fitness Center Equipment**: Select all the equipment that are currently available in the fitness center.
	+ Equipment: This refers to any free weights, machines, and other exercise equipment.
	+ Accessories: This refers to any accessories such as weights, mats, or other equipment not included in the above category.
* **Floors and Carpets**: Enter the current state of the floors and carpets in the gym, including any areas that are dirty or need to be cleaned.
* **Bathrooms**: Check the state of the bathrooms, including toilet paper, soap, and toilet seat.
	+ True: The bathrooms are clean and well-stocked.
	+ False: The bathrooms are not clean or not well-stocked.
* **Trash and Recycling**: Enter the current state of the trash and recycling in the gym.
* **Locker Rooms**: Select all the locker rooms that are currently available in the gym.
	+ Lockers: This refers to any available lockers.
	+ Benches: This refers to any available benches.
	+ Showers: This refers to any available showers.
* **Showers**: Check the state of the showers, including whether they are clean or dirty.
	+ Clean: The showers are clean and well-maintained.
	+ Dirty: The showers are not clean or not well-maintained.
* **Mirrors and Shelving**: Enter any relevant information about the mirrors and shelving in the gym.
* **Windows and Doors**: Enter any relevant information about the windows and doors in the gym.
* **Safety Equipment**: Select all the safety equipment that are currently available in the gym.
	+ Fire Extinguishers: This refers to any available fire extinguishers.
	+ First Aid Kits: This refers to any available first aid kits.
	+ Safety Harness: This refers to any available safety harnesses.
* **Emergency Exit Routes**: Enter any relevant information about the emergency exit routes in the gym.
* **First Aid Kit**: Check the state of the first aid kit, including whether it's available or not.
	+ True: The first aid kit is available and well-stocked.
	+ False: The first aid kit is not available or not well-stocked.
* **Fire Extinguisher**: Check the state of the fire extinguisher, including whether it's available or not.
	+ True: The fire extinguisher is available and well-maintained.
	+ False: The fire extinguisher is not available or not well-maintained.
* **Safety Harness**: Check the state of the safety harness, including whether it's available or not.
	+ True: The safety harness is available and well-maintained.
	+ False: The safety harness is not available or not well-maintained.
* **Exit Signs**: Enter any relevant information about the exit signs in the gym.
* **Fire Alarms**: Check the state of the fire alarms, including whether they're available or not.
	+ True: The fire alarms are available and well-maintained.
	+ False: The fire alarms are not available or not well-maintained.
* **First Aid**: Enter any relevant information about the first aid, including any notes or comments.
* **Emergency Exit**: Enter any relevant information about the emergency exit in the gym.
* **Other Safety Equipment**: Enter any other safety equipment that are available in the gym.
* **Cleanliness Standards**: Enter a number between 1-5 to represent the current state of cleanliness standards in the gym.
* **Other First Aid Kit**: Enter any other first aid kit that are available in the gym.
* **Cleanliness Standards Notes**: Enter any notes or comments about the cleanliness standards in the gym.
* **Safety Equipment Notes**: Enter any notes or comments about the safety equipment in the gym.
* **Cleanliness Stars**: Enter a number between 1-5 to represent the current state of cleanliness in the gym.
* **Cleanliness Comments**: Enter any notes or comments about the cleanliness in the gym.
* **First Aid Comments**: Enter any notes or comments about the first aid in the gym.
