# Simple Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is intended to gather personal and educational background information from job applicants. The data collected will be reviewed and used to assess the applicant's qualifications for a position.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information in the "Personal Info" page.
2. Provide your educational background in the "Education Background" page.
3. If you have any relevant references or additional information, please enter it in the "Reference Info" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Info** (`personal_info`, text, required):
    Enter your name, phone number, and email address to contact you for further information.
* **Education Background** (`education`, text, optional):
    Describe your educational background, including institutions attended and relevant courses or degrees.
* **Reference Info** (`reference_info`, text, optional):
    If you have any professional references or additional information that may support your application, please enter it here.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* You can use this form to apply for multiple positions by filling it out once and using the "Submit" button.
