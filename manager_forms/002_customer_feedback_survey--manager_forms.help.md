# customer_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers on our service, allowing us to gauge their level of satisfaction and gather additional comments for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "How satisfied are you with our service" by selecting one of the options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
2. If you have any additional comments, you can enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.
3. If you would like to provide your contact information, you can enter your email address and phone number in the respective fields. Note that these fields are optional.
4. If you're unsure about the date and time you visited our service, you can leave these fields blank or enter an estimate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Feedback** (page_1, text, required: false): This is the main field where you can enter your feedback on our service.
* **How satisfied are you with our service** (customer_satisfaction, select_one, required: false): Select one of the options to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **Page 2** (page_2, note, required: false): This page is for additional comments or feedback, but it's not required.
* **Email** (email, email, required: false): If you'd like to provide your email address, enter it here. This is optional.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required: false): If you'd like to provide your phone number, enter it here. This is optional.
* **Date** (date, date, required: false): If you know the date you visited our service, enter it here. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Time** (time, time, required: false): If you know the time you visited our service, enter it here. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Additional Comments** (comments, note, required: false): Use this field to enter any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Tips
* Please be honest in your feedback, as it will help us improve our service.
* If you have any concerns or issues, feel free to mention them in the comments field.
* You can skip any fields you are not comfortable with, but please try to provide as much information as possible to help us understand your experience.
