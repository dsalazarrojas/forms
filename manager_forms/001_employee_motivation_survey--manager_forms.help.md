# Employee Motivation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Motivation Survey is designed to gather feedback from employees on various aspects of their job satisfaction, motivation, and engagement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer each question honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the relevant options for each question.
3. Make sure to submit the form only once.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What motivates you** (`employee_motivation_survey_page_1`, text): This section is open-ended and allows you to describe what motivates you at work. Please write your thoughts and ideas in the box provided.
* **How satisfied are you with your job** (`job_satisfaction`, select_one): This question asks how you rate your overall satisfaction with your job. Choose the option that best represents your feelings, from "Option 1" (completely dissatisfied) to "Option 3" (completely satisfied).
* **How much value do you place on your company** (`employee_value`, text): This question is about how important your company's mission, vision, and values are to you. Please describe the level of value you place on them in this box.
* **Your job satisfaction scale** (`employee_job_satisfaction_scale`, select_multiple): Select all the options that best describe your job satisfaction for the following aspects: "Option 1", "Option 2", and "Option 3".
* **How engaged are you at work** (`job_engagement`, select_one): This question measures your level of engagement at work. Choose "Option 1" (completely disengaged), "Option 2" (somewhat engaged), or "Option 3" (completely engaged).
* **What would your dream job be** (`employee_dream_job`, text): Imagine your ideal job scenario. Describe the job you've always dreamed of having.
* **What's your ideal work-life balance** (`work_life_balance`, time): Estimate how much time you spend on work and personal activities per week.
* **How much support do you get from your manager** (`manager_support`, select_one): This question measures the level of support you receive from your manager. Choose "Option 1" (completely dissatisfied), "Option 2" (somewhat dissatisfied), or "Option 3" (completely satisfied).
* **Additional Tips**

- Please be honest in your answers.
- If you have any suggestions for improvement or concerns, feel free to mention them in the "What motivates you" section.
- Remember, this is a confidential survey, and your feedback will help improve your work environment.
