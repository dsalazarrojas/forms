# Swimming Pool Waiver Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Swimming Pool Waiver Release Form is used to collect personal information and consent from individuals participating in swimming pool activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective text fields.
2. Enter your Date of Birth in the date field.
3. Enter your Address, Phone Number, and Email in the respective text fields.
4. Select your preferred Date and Time for the event.
5. If applicable, please indicate if you will be using a vehicle for this event.
6. If you have any medical condition, please provide a brief explanation in the Medical Condition text field.
7. Finally, indicate your Waiver status by selecting True or False.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`swimming_pool_waiver_release_form_first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`swimming_pool_waiver_release_form_last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`swimming_pool_waiver_release_form_dob`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (`swimming_pool_waiver_release_form_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your current address where we can contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Phone** (`swimming_pool_waiver_release_form_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number where we can contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Email** (`swimming_pool_waiver_release_form_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address where we can contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Date** (`swimming_pool_waiver_release_form_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date of the event or activity.
* **Time** (`swimming_pool_waiver_release_form_time`, time, required/optional): Select the time of the event or activity.
* **Event** (`swimming_pool_waiver_release_form_event`, select one, required/optional): Indicate if you are participating in the event.
* **Vehicles** (`swimming_pool_waiver_release_form_vehicles`, select multiple, required/optional): Select any vehicles you will be using for this event.
* **Medical Condition** (`swimming_pool_waiver_release_form_medical_condition`, note, required/optional): Provide a brief explanation of any medical condition you may have.
* **Waiver** (`swimming_pool_waiver_release_form_waiver`, select one, required/optional): Indicate your waiver status.
* **Signature** (`swimming_pool_waiver_release_form_signature`, date, required/optional): Your digital signature, indicating that you have read and understand the form's purpose.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form carefully and completely.
- If you have any medical condition, it's essential to inform us so we can take necessary precautions.
- Make sure to indicate your waiver status correctly to ensure a smooth participation experience.
