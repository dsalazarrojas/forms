# uniform_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The uniform_order_form is a standardized form used to gather information for uniform orders. It is designed to collect essential details from customers to facilitate the processing and delivery of their uniform orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that you have filled in all required fields before submitting the form.
3. Review your information carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the company you represent.
* **Contact Email** (2, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Contact Phone** (3, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Order Date** (4, date, required): Enter the date of the order.
* **Product Name** (5, text, required): Enter the name of the product being ordered.
* **Quantity** (6, number, required): Enter the quantity of the product being ordered.
* **Order Total** (8, number, required): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Order Details** (7, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the order if needed.
* **Manager Comments** (9, text, optional): Add any comments from the manager.
* **Customer Signature** (10, note, optional): A place for the customer to sign and date their agreement.
* **Manager Signature** (11, note, optional): A place for the manager to sign and date their approval.

## Tips
- Please fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
- Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
