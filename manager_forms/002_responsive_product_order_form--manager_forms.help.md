# Responsive Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Responsive Product Order Form is a form designed to collect information from customers to fulfill their product orders. It is used to gather order details, customer information, and payment details in a single, streamlined process.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the order details section with the required information, including any relevant notes or comments.
2. Provide the billing and shipping addresses for the order.
3. Enter the customer's details, including their contact information.
4. Enter the payment details, including the email and phone number.
5. Review and submit the form to complete the order process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details (order_details)**: Enter the order number, date, and any additional notes or comments about the order.
* **Billing Address (billing_address)**: Enter the billing address for the order, including the name, street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Shipping Address (shipping_address)**: Enter the shipping address for the order, including the name, street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Customer Details (customer_details)**: Enter the customer's name, email, and any additional contact information.
* **Contact Details (contact_details)**: Enter the customer's phone number and any additional contact information.
* **Payment Details (payment_details)**: Enter the payment method and amount due.
* **Customer Email (customer_email)**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone (customer_phone)**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Billing Address (customer_billing_address)**: Enter the customer's billing address.
* **Customer Shipping Address (customer_shipping_address)**: Enter the customer's shipping address.
