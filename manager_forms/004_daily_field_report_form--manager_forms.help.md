## Purpose
The daily_field_report_form is used to report daily work status and hours worked by employees. This information helps supervisors track progress and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the date of your work from the calendar input field.
2. Choose your start and end times for the work period.
3. Enter the number of hours you worked during this period.
4. Add any relevant notes or comments about your work.
5. If you were not actively working, select the "Inactive" status.
6. If you have a different status than what is listed, provide a detailed explanation in the "other" field.
7. Enter your supervisor's name for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of work** (`date_of_work`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of the work period.
* **Start time** (`start_time`, `time`, not required): Choose the start time of your work period.
* **End time** (`end_time`, `time`, not required): Select the end time of your work period.
* **Hours worked** (`hours_worked`, `number`, not required): Enter the number of hours you worked during this period.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, not required): Add any relevant comments or notes about your work.
* **Supervisor's name** (`supervisor_name`, `text`, not required): Enter your supervisor's name for review and approval.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, not required): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Crew ID** (`crew_id`, `number`, not required): Enter your crew ID number.
* **Work status** (`work_status`, `select_one`, not required): Choose whether you were active or inactive during this work period.
* **Other status** (`work_status_other`, `text`, not required): If your status is not listed, provide a detailed explanation.
