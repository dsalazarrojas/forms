# General Meeting Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to vote on the general meeting agenda items.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the agenda items listed below.
2. For each agenda item, select one of the options provided.
3. Make sure to fill in all the required fields (if any).
4. Once you have filled out all the fields, click the "Submit" button to cast your vote.
