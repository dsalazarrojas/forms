# Vehicle Handover Check Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Vehicle Handover Check Form is designed to collect information about the handover of vehicles between parties. It is intended to be used in situations where a vehicle is being transferred from one person or entity to another.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, select the appropriate option for "Vehicle Handover Check" from the list of options.
2. Enter the details of the vehicle's manufacturer in the "Manufacturer" field.
3. Enter the details of the vehicle in the "Vehicle" field.
4. Select the date of the handover in the "Handover Date" field.
5. Enter the name of the person or entity handing over the vehicle in the "Handover By" field.
6. Enter the details of the dealer or organization involved in the handover in the "Dealer" field.
7. If necessary, add any additional remarks or comments about the handover in the "Remarks" field.
8. If necessary, add any additional comments or information about the handover in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Handover Check** (`vehicle_handover_check_form_fields`, text, required: false): Select the type of vehicle handover being conducted.
* **Manufacturer** (`manufacturer_details`, text, required: false): Enter the details of the vehicle's manufacturer.
* **Vehicle** (`vehicle_details`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the vehicle being handed over.
* **Handover Date** (`handover_date`, date, required: false): Select the date of the vehicle handover.
* **Handover By** (`handover_by`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person or entity handing over the vehicle.
* **Dealer** (`dealer_details`, text, required: false): Enter the details of the dealer or organization involved in the handover.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, text, required: false): Enter any additional remarks or comments about the handover.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or information about the handover.
