# Sample Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from individuals interested in applying for a job within our organization. It collects various details such as contact information, job preferences, and supporting documents to help our team assess the applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This is the name by which you would like to be known in the organization.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. This is your surname.
3. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field. This must be a valid email address where you can be reached for communication.
4. Upload your **Resume** in the "Resume" field. This is the document that summarizes your work experience and qualifications.
5. Upload your **Cover Letter** in the "Cover Letter" field. This is a letter that explains your interest in the job and why you're a good fit.
6. Enter the **Job Title** you're applying for in the "Job Title" field. This is the specific role you're interested in applying for.
7. Select the **Job Type** you're looking for in the "Job Type" field. Choose from "Full-time", "Part-time", "Contract", or "Internship".
8. If you've selected "Job Type" as "Part-time", "Contract", or "Internship", you can also provide more details in the "Job Type Other" field.
9. Enter your available **Start Date** in the "Start Date" field. This is the earliest date you can start work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Your given name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Your surname.
* **Email** (Email, Required): A valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Resume** (Text, Required): Upload a document that summarizes your work experience and qualifications.
* **Cover Letter** (Text, Required): A letter explaining your interest in the job and why you're a good fit.
* **Job Title** (Text, Not Required): The specific role you're applying for. If you're unsure, you can ask our HR team for guidance.
* **Job Type** (Select One, Not Required): Choose from "Full-time", "Part-time", "Contract", or "Internship".
* **Job Type Other** (Text, Not Required): If you've selected "Part-time", "Contract", or "Internship", provide more details about the job duration.
* **Start Date** (Date, Not Required): The earliest date you can start work. If unsure, you can discuss it with our HR team.
