# Employment Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employment Contract Form is a tool used to collect essential employee information, including name, email, employment date and time, and employee signature. This form helps maintain accurate records and facilitates the process of managing employee contracts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Employee Name** field with the employee's name.
2. Enter the employee's **Email** address in the corresponding field.
3. Provide the **Employment Date** in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD' (Year-Month-Day) if required.
4. Enter the **Employment Time** in 24-hour format 'HH:MM' if required.
5. Select one of the provided **Employee Signature** options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required: false): Enter the employee's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the employee's official email address for communication and records.
* **Employment Date** (`employment_date`, date, required: false): If applicable, provide the date of the employment contract or agreement in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **Employment Time** (`employment_time`, time, required: false): If applicable, enter the start time of the employment contract or agreement in 24-hour format 'HH:MM'.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the provided options to indicate the employee's signature, which confirms their agreement to the terms of the employment contract.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill in all the required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check the provided options for **Employee Signature** to select the correct one.
* If any field is not applicable, leave it blank.
* Submit the form only when all required fields are filled accurately.
