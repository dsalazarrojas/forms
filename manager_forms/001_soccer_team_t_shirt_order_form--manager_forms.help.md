# Soccer Team T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers who want to order T-shirts for their soccer team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these general steps:

1. Fill out the "Order Details" page with your order requirements.
2. Provide your "Customer Info" details.
3. Fill out the "Shipping Info" details.
4. Provide your "Payment Info".
5. Add any additional comments or instructions in the "Order Note" field.
6. Confirm your contact information, including your "Phone", "Email", and "Name".
7. Select if your "Team" has any "Yes" or "No" requirements.
8. Specify the "Team size" and "Quantity" of players.
9. Select the "Team color" and "Player color".
10. Choose the "Select size" and "Select Color" for the T-shirt.
11. Confirm the "Player size" and "Player color" for each player.
12. Specify the "Price" for the order.
13. Choose the "Payment method".
14. Select the "Date" and "Time" for the order.
15. Confirm the "Yes" or "No" options for "Select type", "Team color", "Player color", "Select size", "Select Color", and "Player Size".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Order Details** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Fill out this page with your order requirements. This includes any specific instructions or comments about your order.
  - **Customer Info** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your name and any other information you want to share with us.
  - **Shipping Info** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your shipping details for us to know where to deliver the T-shirt.
  - **Payment Info** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your payment information so we can process your order.
  - **Order Note** (`5`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or instructions about your order.
  - **Phone** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
  - **Email** (`7`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can confirm your order.
  - **Name** (`8`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name for our records.
  - **Team** (`9`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select if your team has any "Yes" or "No" requirements.
  - **Team size** (`10`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Specify the size of your team.
  - **Player names** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the names of the players you want to order for.
  - **Quantity** (`12`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the quantity of T-shirts you want to order.
  - **Price** (`13`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the price for the order.
  - **Payment method** (`14`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your payment method.
  - **Date** (`15`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date for the order.
  - **Time** (`16`, `time`, required/optional): Select the time for the order.
  - **Select type** (`17`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Confirm if your team has "Yes" or "No" requirements for this option.
  - **Team color** (`18`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the team color for the T-shirt.
  - **Player color** (`19`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the player colors for the T-shirt.
  - **Select size** (`20`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the size of the T-shirt.
  - **Select Color** (`21`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the color of the T-shirt.
  - **Player size** (`22`, `select_one`, required/optional): Confirm the player size for each player.
  - **Player Size** (`23`, `select_one`, required/optional): Confirm the player size for each player.
