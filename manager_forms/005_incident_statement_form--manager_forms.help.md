# Incident Statement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Incident Statement Form is used to report and document incidents that may have occurred during work hours or otherwise. This form ensures that all relevant information about the incident is collected and documented for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title for the incident in the "Incident Statement Form" field.
2. Select a category for the incident in the "Category" dropdown menu. The two available options are "Manager Forms" and "Other".
3. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "Description" field.
4. If applicable, provide the name of the output file in the "Output File" field.
5. Enter the form IDs, if any, in the "Form IDs" field.
6. Select the tool that was involved in the incident from the "Assigned Tool" dropdown menu. The two available options are "chatjimmy" and "Other".
7. If necessary, provide additional information about the category of the incident in the "Category" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Statement Form** (1) (`title`, `text`, **required**): Enter a brief title for the incident that you are reporting.
* **Category** (2) (`category`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the category for the incident from the dropdown menu. Please note that the two available options are "Manager Forms" and "Other".
* **Description** (3) (`description`, `text`, **required**): Provide a detailed description of the incident you are reporting.
* **Output File** (4) (`output_file`, `text`, **optional**): If applicable, enter the name of the output file related to the incident.
* **Form IDs** (5) (`form_ids`, `number`, **required**): Enter the form IDs, if any, related to the incident.
* **Assigned Tool** (6) (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the tool that was involved in the incident from the dropdown menu. The two available options are "chatjimmy" and "Other".
* **Category** (7) (`category`, `text`, **optional**): If necessary, provide additional information about the category of the incident.
 
## Tips

* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and detailed.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure that you have filled in all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or IT department for guidance.
