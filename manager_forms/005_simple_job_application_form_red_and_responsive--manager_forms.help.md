# Simple Job Application Form Red And Responsive - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic contact and job application details from individuals interested in applying for a job opening. The form is intended for job seekers to provide their contact information, job-related details, and upload their resume for consideration.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your contact details, such as your name, email address, and phone number.
2.  Provide a brief description of the job you are applying for.
3.  If you have a resume, drag and drop it to upload it to the form. Alternatively, you can type in a link to your resume.
4.  Add any additional information that you believe would be helpful for us to consider your application.
5.  Click submit to finalize your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Please fill in your name, email address, and phone number to help us get in touch with you.
* **Job details** (`job_details`, text, required/optional): Briefly describe the job you are interested in applying for.
* **Upload resume** (`upload_resume`, text, required/optional): Upload your resume by dragging and dropping the file or providing a link to it.
* **Additional information** (`note`, text, required/optional): Add any relevant details that you would like to share with us about your application.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth application process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* We appreciate your interest in this job opportunity and look forward to reviewing your application.
