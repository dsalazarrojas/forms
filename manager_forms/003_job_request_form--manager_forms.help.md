# Job Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Request Form is a tool used for job seekers or their representatives to provide contact information and job specifics to our team. It is intended to be used to efficiently gather necessary details from applicants, and our team will use the information provided to evaluate job requests.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your personal details and job request information.
2. Ensure that you are using the correct format for your contact information (e.g. `example@example.com` for an email address).
3. If you have any additional documents related to your job request, you can provide them in the "Related Documents" field.
4. Make sure to enter a valid date in the "Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your contact email address in the format `example@example.com`.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX` or `XXX-XXX-XXXXX` (e.g. `123-456-7890` or `123-456-7890`).
* **Reference Number**: If you have a reference number or internal ID, enter it here.
* **Job Details**: Describe the job you are requesting, including any relevant details such as job title, department, or location.
* **Related Documents**: If you have any documents related to your job request, upload them here.
* **Date**: Enter the date of your job request in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
