# Step By Step Company Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect essential information about a company, including its name, address, contact details, service description, and service request. The form is meant to gather data for internal purposes, such as record-keeping or data analysis.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Step By Step Company Information Form" link to access the form.
2. Fill in the required fields as per the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name (`company_name`, text, required):* Enter the name of your company.
* **Company Address (`company_address`, text, required):* Enter the address of your company.
* **Company Phone (`company_phone`, text, required):* Enter the phone number of your company.
* **Contact Person (`contact_person`, text, required):* Enter the name of the person to contact.
* **Contact Phone (`contact_phone`, text, required):* Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email (`contact_email`, email, required):* Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Service Description (`service_description`, text, required):* Briefly describe the services offered by your company.
* **Service Request (`service_request`, text, required):* Specify the specific service request you have.
* **Service Frequency (`service_frequency`, text, optional):* If applicable, describe the frequency of the service. (This field is optional and not required for submission.)
* **Partner Name (`partner_name`, text, required):* Enter the name of the partner company or individual.
* **Relationship (`partner_relationship`, text, optional):* Describe the relationship with the partner company or individual. (This field is optional and not required for submission.)
* **Partner Request (`partner_request`, text, optional):* Specify any additional partner request. (This field is optional and not required for submission.)
* **Inquiry Description (`inquiry_description`, text, required):* Briefly describe the inquiry or issue you have.
* **Inquiry Request (`inquiry_request`, text, required):* Specify the specific inquiry or request you have.
