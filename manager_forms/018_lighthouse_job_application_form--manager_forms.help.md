# Lighthouse Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from job applicants for the Lighthouse organization. The form is used to assess your qualifications and suitability for a position at Lighthouse. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
6. Enter your Lighthouse ID (if applicable) in the "Lighthouse ID" field.
7. Select one or multiple relevant experience options in the "Experience" field.
8. Choose your availability in the "Availability" field.
9. Enter your Goal Description in the "Goal Description" field.
10. Enter your skills in the "Skills" field.
11. Enter the contact information for your Emergency Contact in the "Emergency Contact" field.
12. Enter the name of your Emergency Contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address to be contacted for further information.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Date of Birth** (date, optional): Enter your date of birth for age verification.
* **Lighthouse ID** (text, optional): Enter any Lighthouse ID you may have.
* **Experience** (select multiple, optional): Choose one or multiple experience levels that you have.
	+ Lighthouse Keeper
	+ Lighthouse Steward
	+ Lighthouse Assistant
* **Availability** (select one, optional): Choose when you are available to work.
	+ Available
	+ Unavailable
	+ Flexible
* **Goal Description** (text, optional): Enter your goal description for the position.
* **Skills** (text, optional): Enter any relevant skills you may have.
* **Emergency Contact** (text, optional): Enter the contact information for your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required.
