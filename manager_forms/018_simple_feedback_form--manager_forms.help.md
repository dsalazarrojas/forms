# Simple Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Simple Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from customers, clients, or users about our service. Your feedback is valuable to us and helps us improve and adapt to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page by providing a brief but clear description of your feedback.
2. If you have any suggestions, write them down in the "Do you have any suggestions?" field.
3. If you want to provide additional comments or information, use the "What do you want to say?" field.
4. If you would like to receive a response, provide your email address in the "Email address (if you wish to receive a response)" field.
5. If you would prefer to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number in the "Phone number (if you wish to contact you)" field.
6. Add any additional comments or information in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How do you like our service?** (`Feedback form fields`, text, required/optional): Describe your experience with our service. Please provide a brief but clear description of your feedback.
* **Do you have any suggestions?** (`Suggestion box`, text, required/optional): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please share them with us.
* **What do you want to say?** (`Feedback message`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or information you'd like to share with us.
* **Email address (if you wish to receive a response)** (`Email address`, email, required/optional): If you'd like to receive a response from us, please provide your email address.
* **Phone number (if you wish to contact you)** (`Phone number`, text, required/optional): If you would like us to contact you by phone, enter your phone number.
* **Additional comments** (`Additional comments`, text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or information you'd like to share with us.
