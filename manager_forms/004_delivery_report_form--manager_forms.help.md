# Delivery Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Delivery Report Form is used to document and track delivery activities, capturing details about the delivery date, time, address, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about the delivery.
2. Select the appropriate options for the "delivery_person" and "notes" fields, if applicable.
3. Upload any additional information, such as a customer signature or delivery person's phone number, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (customer_name)**: Enter the name of the person receiving the delivery.
* **Delivery Date (delivery_date)**: Enter the date the delivery was made.
* **Delivery Time (delivery_time)**: Enter the time the delivery was made.
* **Delivery Time Zone (delivery_time_zone)**: Enter the time zone where the delivery was made (e.g., EST, CST, etc.).
* **Delivery Address (delivery_address)**: Enter the address where the delivery was made.
* **Delivery Location (delivery_location)**: Enter the location where the delivery was made (e.g., store, office, etc.).
* **Package Details (package_details)**: Enter a brief description of the package or item delivered.
* **Customer Signature (customer_signature)**: If required, upload a scan or image of the customer's signature to confirm receipt of the delivery.
* **Customer Signature Image (customer_signature_image)**: If required, upload an image or scan of the customer's signature.
* **Delivery Person (delivery_person)**: Select whether the delivery was made by an employee or another person.
* **Notes (notes)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the delivery.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter the phone number of the person who received the delivery.
* **Email (email)**: Enter the email address of the person who received the delivery.
* **Customer Email (customer_email)**: Enter the email address of the customer.
* **Customer Phone (customer_phone)**: Enter the phone number of the customer.
* **Delivery Person Phone (delivery_person_phone)**: Enter the phone number of the person who made the delivery.
* **Delivery Person Email (delivery_person_email)**: Enter the email address of the person who made the delivery.

Note: The fields marked as "select_one" and "select_multiple" allow you to select from a list of options.
