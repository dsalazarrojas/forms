# Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Incident Report form is used to collect information about incidents that occur within the organization. It helps to gather details about the incident, including the date, time, location, and category, as well as other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Incident Details**
	* Select the **Incident Date** and **Incident Time** using the corresponding fields.
	* Enter a brief description of the incident in the **Description** field.
2. **Category and Priority**
	* Choose the category of the incident from the **Category** dropdown menu.
	* Select the **Priority** level of the incident (High, Medium, or Low).
3. **Status and Tags**
	* Set the **Status** of the incident to either **Open** or **Closed**.
	* Add any relevant **Tags** to the incident.
4. **Contacts and Attachments**
	* Enter the contact information for the person who reported the incident (Reporter) and the respondent (Respondent).
	* Choose whether or not to attach any files to the incident report.
5. **Additional Details**
	* Add any additional information about the incident in the **Additional Info** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date**: The date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time**: The time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location**: The location where the incident occurred.
* **Description**: A brief description of what happened during the incident.
* **Category**: The category of the incident (e.g., Equipment failure, Employee issue, etc.).
* **Priority**: The level of priority for the incident (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Status**: The status of the incident (Open or Closed).
* **Tags**: Any relevant tags or labels for the incident.
* **Reporter Contact**: The contact information for the person who reported the incident.
* **Respondent Contact**: The contact information for the person who responded to the incident.
* **Attachments**: Whether or not to attach any files to the incident report.
* **Additional Info**: Any additional details about the incident.
* **Reporter Email**: The email address of the person who reported the incident.
* **Respondent Email**: The email address of the person who responded to the incident.
* **Reporter Phone**: The phone number of the person who reported the incident.
* **Respondent Phone**: The phone number of the person who responded to the incident.
* **Reporter Address**: The address of the person who reported the incident.
* **Respondent Address**: The address of the person who responded to the incident.
* **Reporter Job Title**: The job title of the person who reported the incident.

By following this guide, you'll be able to effectively complete the Incident Report form and collect the necessary information about the incident.
