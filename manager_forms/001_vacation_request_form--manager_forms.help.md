# Vacation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vacation Request Form is used by employees to request time off for vacation, sick leave, or other personal reasons. This form helps managers and HR to process and approve or deny vacation requests in a standardized and efficient way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee Name and other required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Enter the Start Date and End Date of your vacation period.
3. Indicate the duration of your vacation in days.
4. Optionally, enter the Return Date if you plan to return to work before the end of your scheduled leave.
5. Provide a brief Reason for your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID card.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to start your vacation.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to return from your vacation.
* **Duration** (`duration`, number, optional): If you want to specify a fixed number of days, enter that here. For example, if you want 5 days off, enter 5.
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, optional): If you plan to return to work before the end of your scheduled leave, enter the date.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation for your vacation request, including any relevant details or context.
