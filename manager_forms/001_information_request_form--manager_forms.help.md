# Information Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Information Request Form is a tool used to collect personal and contact information from users, as well as details about their specific request or concern. This form helps management or administrative teams to gather necessary information for their tasks and operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:
- Fill out the form by entering your First Name.
- Enter your Last Name.
- Provide your Email address.
- Enter your Phone number.
- Select the date you are submitting your request.
- Finally, type in a detailed Message about your request or concern.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your personal first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your personal last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your personal or work-related email address. 
* **Phone**: Enter your personal or work-related phone number. 
* **Request Date**: Select the date you are submitting this request.
* **Message**: Write a detailed message about your request or concern, including any relevant background or context information. 

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your request is processed correctly.
- Use clear and concise language when writing your message.
- Check that your email and phone number are accurate and up-to-date.
- Submit this form only once, if you need to make subsequent requests, you can contact the relevant department or team directly.
