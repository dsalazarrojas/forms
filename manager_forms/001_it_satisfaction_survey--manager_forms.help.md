# IT Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT Satisfaction Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from IT users about their experiences with IT services. This form will help us understand how we're doing and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please take a few minutes to answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. For each question, select the option that best represents your experience.
3. You can choose one or multiple options for each question.
4. If you have any comments or suggestions, please type them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Satisfaction** (`it_satisfaction_survey_page_1`, number, required: false)
	+ This question asks for your overall satisfaction level with our IT services.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, select multiple, required: false)
	+ Select one or multiple options to rate your overall experience with our IT services.
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, select one, required: false)
	+ Choose the option that best describes our service quality.
* **Resolution Time** (`resolution`, number, required: false)
	+ Please rate how quickly our resolution process is.
* **Communication** (`communication`, number, required: false)
	+ Rate how well you've been informed about the status of your issues.
* **IT Support Team** (`it_support_team`, select multiple, required: false)
	+ Choose one or multiple options to rate our support team's performance.
* **Satisfaction Score** (`it_satisfaction_score`, number, required: false)
	+ Please rate your overall satisfaction with our IT services.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false)
	+ Share any additional comments or suggestions you might have.
