# Water Conservation Volunteer Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in water conservation efforts as volunteers. It will help us understand your availability, contact details, and roles/affiliations related to water conservation. Your input will help us better organize and utilize volunteer efforts.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out this form carefully, providing accurate and clear information in each field.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand its purpose and requirements.
3. If you need help or clarification, contact us at [contact email](mailto:form@gic.mx).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Details** (`volunteer_details`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a brief description of your interest in water conservation and what motivates you to volunteer.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name, email, and phone number so we can get in touch with you about volunteering opportunities.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `text`, required: false): Indicate the days and times you are available for volunteering, e.g., "Monday and Wednesday, 9 am - 5 pm".
* **Consent** (`consent`, `note`, required: false): This field is for your signature of consent to participate in water conservation activities. Please indicate your willingness to participate in water conservation efforts.
* **Group Affiliations** (`groups`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Environmental Groups: If you're associated with a local environmental group, please select this option.
	+ School Programs: If you're part of a school program related to water conservation, select this option.
	+ Community Programs: If you're involved in community-based water conservation initiatives, choose this option.
* **Volunteer Roles** (`roles`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Conservationist: If you're an expert in water conservation, select this option.
	+ Educator: If you have experience in teaching water conservation-related topics, choose this option.
	+ Organizer: If you have experience organizing water conservation events or activities, select this option.

## Tips

* Be as accurate and specific as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We'll get back to you soon to discuss further details and potential volunteer opportunities.
