# NGO Website Testimonial Inclusion Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather testimonial content from employees to include on your website. The form will ask for personal and professional information, as well as consent on the usage of the testimonial content.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering your employee ID. This is a mandatory field and is used to identify you as an employee of the nonprofit organization.
2. Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on the website.
3. Enter your email address, which will be used to contact you regarding the publication of your testimonial.
4. Select the department you work in or worked in.
5. Enter your job title.
6. Write a testimonial about your experience with the nonprofit organization.
7. Provide publication details, such as where and when you would like your testimonial to be published.
8. Select the media types for which you give consent to publish your testimonial.
9. Indicate your consent for photo, video, and audio content.
10. Select where you would like to be published on the website, social media, and other publications.
11. Enter the name of the publication where your testimonial will be published.
12. Enter the date you would like your testimonial to be published.
13. Enter the date and time you submit this form.
14. Enter your IP address for submission tracking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_id** (employee_id, number, required): Enter your unique employee ID as per your organization's HR system.
* **name** (name, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on the website.
* **email** (email, email, required): Enter your email address, which will be used to contact you regarding the publication of your testimonial.
* **department** (department, text, optional): Select the department you work in or have worked in.
* **job_title** (job_title, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **testimonial** (testimonial, text, optional): Write a testimonial about your experience with the nonprofit organization.
* **publication_details** (publication_details, note, optional): Provide details on where and when you would like your testimonial to be published.
* **media_release** (media_release, select_one, required): Select the media types for which you give consent to publish your testimonial.
* **photo_consent** (photo_consent, select_multiple, required): Indicate your consent for photo content.
* **video_consent** (video_consent, select_multiple, required): Indicate your consent for video content.
* **audio_consent** (audio_consent, select_multiple, required): Indicate your consent for audio content.
* **testimonial_consent** (testimonial_consent, select_multiple, required): Select your consent for testimonial content.
* **publication_on_website** (publication_on_website, select_one, required): Select where you would like to be published on the website.
* **publication_on_social_media** (publication_on_social_media, select_one, required): Select where you would like to be published on social media.
* **publication_on_email** (publication_on_email, select_one, required): Select where you would like to be published on email.
* **publication_on_print_publications** (publication_on_print_publications, select_one, required): Select where you would like to be published on print publications.
* **publication_on_other_publications** (publication_on_other_publications, select_one, required): Select where you would like to be published on other publications.
* **photo_credit** (photo_credit, text, optional): Enter a credit statement for your photo.
* **publication_name** (publication_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the publication where your testimonial will be published.
* **publication_date** (publication_date, date, required): Enter the date you would like your testimonial to be published.
* **submission_date** (submission_date, date, required): Enter the date you submit this form.
* **form_submitting_user** (form_submitting_user, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on the submission date.
* **form_submitting_date** (form_submitting_date, date, optional): Enter the date and time you submit this form.
* **form_submitting_time** (form_submitting_time, time, optional): Enter the time you submit this form.
* **form_submitting_ip** (form_submitting_ip, text, optional): Enter your IP address for submission tracking.

Note: This help guide is written in a format that is easy to follow and provides clear instructions on how to complete each field. It is recommended to review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
