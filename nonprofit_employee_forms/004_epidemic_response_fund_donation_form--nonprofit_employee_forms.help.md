# epidemic_response_fund_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The epidemic response fund donation form is designed to collect information about individuals who want to contribute to the fight against epidemics by making a donation. This form will help our team track and manage donations, ensuring that we can provide timely and effective support to those affected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the required information in each field accurately and thoroughly.
*   Make sure to review each field carefully before proceeding to the next page.
*   If you need help with any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Info (Contact Info, text, optional)**: Enter your name and contact information in this field. This will help us get in touch with you if we need to discuss your donation further.
*   **Donation Amount (Donation Amount, number, optional)**: Enter the amount you're willing to donate.
*   **Donation Frequency (Donation Frequency, select one, optional)**: Choose how often you want to make this donation (e.g., monthly, quarterly, annually). This will help us schedule future donations.
*   **Contact Method (Contact Method, select multiple, optional)**: Choose how you'd like to be contacted (phone, email, mail). You can select one or more options.
*   **Frequency (Frequency, select one, optional)**: Select the frequency of your donation (weekly, monthly, quarterly, annually).
*   **Interval (Interval, number, optional)**: Enter the interval for your donation.
*   **Frequency (Frequency, select multiple, optional)**: Choose how frequently you'd like to be contacted (daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, annually).
*   **Email (Email, email, optional)**: Enter your email address for communication.
*   **Phone (Phone, text, optional)**: Enter your phone number for communication.
*   **Address (Address, select multiple, optional)**: Choose where you'd like to receive mail (home, work, other).
*   **Mail (Mail, select multiple, optional)**: Choose where you'd like to receive mail (home, work, other).
*   **Donation Type (Donation Type, select multiple, optional)**: Select the type of donation you're making (monetary, in-kind, other).
*   **Donation Date (Donation Date, date, optional)**: Enter the date of your donation.
*   **Donation Time (Donation Time, time, optional)**: Enter the time of your donation.
*   **Notes (Notes, note, optional)**: Leave any additional notes about your donation.
*   **Form Type (Form Type, select multiple, optional)**: Choose where you'd like to submit your form (online, offline, other).
*   **Contact Name (Contact Name, select multiple, optional)**: Choose how you'd like to be identified as a contact (primary, secondary, other).
*   **Form Language (Form Language, select multiple, optional)**: Select the language you'd like the form to be presented in (english, spanish, french, other).
*   **Form Version (Form Version, select multiple, optional)**: Choose the version of the form you'd like to submit (latest, previous, other).
*   **Form Submitted (Form Submitted, select multiple, optional)**: Select whether you've already submitted this form or if it's a draft (submitted, draft, other).
*   **Donation Amount Frequency (Donation Amount Frequency, number, optional)**: Enter the frequency of your donation amount.
*   **Donation Frequency Amount (Donation Frequency Amount, number, optional)**: Enter the amount of your donation.

Please ensure you complete all the required fields and answer each question carefully. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
