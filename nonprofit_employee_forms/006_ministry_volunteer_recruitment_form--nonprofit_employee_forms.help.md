# Ministry Volunteer Recruitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ministry Volunteer Recruitment Form is a tool used to gather information from potential volunteers interested in joining the ministry's activities and events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are a contact person.
2. Enter your email address in the email field.
3. Enter your phone number in the phone number field.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" for each interest that applies to you in the volunteer interest field.
5. Select a date for your availability in the availability field.
6. Choose a role that you are interested in from the volunteer role field.
7. Enter any relevant skills you have in the skills field.
8. Briefly describe your volunteer experience in the volunteer experience field.
9. Enter any additional information or comments in the message field.
10. Select "Yes" or "No" for each of the volunteer agreement fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, select_one, required: false): Check if you are a contact person for this form. If yes, you will be the primary point of contact for the ministry.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so that the ministry can contact you about volunteer opportunities.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so that the ministry can contact you about volunteer opportunities.
* **Volunteer Interest** (`volunteer_interest`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the volunteer interests that apply to you. This will help the ministry understand how you can be involved in their activities and events.
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, required: false): Select a date for when you are available to volunteer. This will help the ministry plan their activities and events.
* **Volunteer Role** (`volunteer_role`, select_one, required: false): Choose a role that you are interested in. This will help the ministry place you in the right position for their activities and events.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, required: false): List any relevant skills you have that would make you a strong candidate for volunteering.
* **Volunteer Experience** (`volunteer_experience`, text, required: false): Briefly describe your volunteer experience, including any relevant activities or events you have participated in.
* **Message** (`message`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information or comments you would like to share with the ministry.
* **Volunteer Agreement** (`volunteer_agreement`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the volunteer agreements that apply to you. This will help the ministry understand your commitment to volunteering.
