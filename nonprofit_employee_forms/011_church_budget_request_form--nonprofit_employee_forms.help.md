# Church Budget Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Budget Request Form is designed to collect information from church members or departments requesting budget approval for various expenses or projects. It helps the church's financial management team to track, categorize, and manage budget requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and title as the person submitting the budget request.
2. Select the department or ministry requesting the funds.
3. Enter a short name for the project or expense.
4. Specify the whole dollar amount requested.
5. Provide a brief explanation of how the funds will be used.
6. Choose the category that best fits the expense (Operations, Outreach, Facilities, Events, or Mission).
7. If applicable, provide the proposed timeline for the project or expense.
8. Indicate if approval is needed from a specific person or role.
9. Attach any relevant supporting documents or quotes (if applicable).
10. Confirm if finance has been consulted regarding the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester name** (`requester_name`, text, required): This field is for the person submitting the budget request. Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Department or Ministry** (`department`, text, required): Select the department or ministry responsible for the request.
* **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, required): Enter a short name for the project or expense.
* **Amount Requested** (`amount_requested`, number, required): Enter the whole dollar amount requested.
* **Purpose Description** (`purpose_description`, text, required): Explain how the funds will be used to accomplish the project or expense.
* **Budget Category** (`budget_category`, select_one, required): Choose the category that best fits the expense (Operations, Outreach, Facilities, Events, or Mission).
* **Proposed Timeline** (`proposed_timeline`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the proposed start and end dates for the project or expense.
* **Approval Needed By** (`approval_needed`, text, optional): If required, enter the name or role of the approver needed for this request.
* **Attachments Links** (`attachments`, text, optional): Attach any supporting documents or quotes, such as invoices, receipts, or quotes.
* **Finance Contact** (`finance_contact`, select_one, required): Indicate if finance has been consulted regarding this request (True or False).
* **Attachments Links** (`attachments`, text, optional): Upload supporting documents or quotes, such as invoices, receipts, or quotes.

Note: The fields marked as optional should only be filled in if necessary, as they may not apply to every request.
