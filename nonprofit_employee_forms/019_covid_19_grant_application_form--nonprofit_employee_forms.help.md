# COVID 19 Grant Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information for a COVID-19 grant application. This form will help us understand your business or organization's eligibility and impact due to the pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
Please read each section carefully and fill out the form in a step-by-step manner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Business or Organization Name** (`business_name`): Enter the name of your business or organization.
  
- **Legal Entity Type** (`legal_entity_type`): Select the type of your business or organization (Sole Proprietor, LLC, Corporation, Non-Profit, Partnership, or Other).
  
- **Business Registration Number** (`registration_number`): Enter your business registration number.
  
- **Tax ID or EIN** (`tax_id`): Enter your tax ID or EIN.
  
- **Business Address** (`business_address`): Enter your business address.
  
- **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
  
- **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your email address.
  
- **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person`): Enter the name of the contact person.
  
- **Industry Type** (`industry_type`): Select the type of industry your business or organization belongs to (Retail, Food Service, Manufacturing, Healthcare, Professional Services, or Other).
  
- **Number of Employees** (`employees_number`): Enter the number of employees your business or organization has.
  
- **Annual Revenue Before Pandemic** (`annual_revenue`): Enter the annual revenue of your business or organization before the pandemic.
  
- **Date Business Established** (`business_established`): Enter the date your business was established.
  
- **Estimated Revenue Loss** (`revenue_loss`): Enter the estimated revenue loss due to the pandemic.
  
- **Percentage of Revenue Lost** (`revenue_loss_percentage`): Enter the percentage of revenue lost due to the pandemic.
  
- **Operational Challenges** (`operational_challenges`): Select all operational challenges your business or organization has faced due to the pandemic (Closure or Reduced Operations, Supply Chain Disruption, Staff Shortage, Increased Expenses, Customer Decline, or Other).
  
- **Intended Use of Grant Funds** (`funds_usage`): Select how you intend to use the grant funds (Payroll and Wages, Rent and Utilities, Equipment and Supplies, Debt Repayment, Working Capital, or Other).
  
- **Amount Requested** (`grant_amount`): Enter the amount of grant money you are requesting.
  
- **Supporting Documents Attached** (`supporting_documents`): Indicate if you have attached any supporting documents.
  
- **I Certify** (`agreement`): Select Yes, I Certify to confirm that all information provided is accurate and true.

Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields and reviewed the form carefully before submitting.
