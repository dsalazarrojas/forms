# Medical Volunteer Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Volunteer Application form is used to gather information from potential medical volunteers who wish to participate in medical-related activities at your organization. This form helps to verify the authenticity of the medical volunteers' credentials and ensure that they are eligible to perform medical tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please enter your name in the **Name** field.
2. Enter your address in the **Address** field.
3. Provide your email address in the **Email** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Provide your medical license number in the **Medical License Number** field.
6. Enter the name of your emergency contact person in the **Emergency Contact** field.
7. Select your medical specialty from the dropdown menu in the **Specialty** field.
8. If applicable, enter the year you graduated from medical school in the **Medical School Graduation Year** field.
9. If applicable, enter the year you obtained your medical certification in the **Certification Year** field.
10. Provide your expiration date for your medical license in the **Expiration Date** field.
11. Enter your medical volunteer certification number, if available (optional).
12. If applicable, provide your emergency response status in the **Emergency Response** field.
13. Choose your primary language from the dropdown menu in the **Language** field (optional).
14. Read and agree to the **Medical Volunteer Agreement** by typing your name in the field.
15. Enter the name of the emergency contact person in the **Emergency Contact Name** field.
16. Provide the phone number of the emergency contact person in the **Emergency Contact Phone** field.
17. Enter the email address of the emergency contact person in the **Emergency Contact Email** field (optional).
18. Provide the address of the emergency contact person in the **Emergency Contact Address** field (optional).
19. If applicable, provide the expiration date of your medical license in the **Medical License Expiration Date** field.
20. If applicable, provide the expiration date of your medical license (optional).
21. Enter the start date of your volunteer service in the **Volunteer Start Date** field.
22. If you have a second medical license number, enter it in the **Medical License Number 2** field (optional).
23. Select your volunteer status from the dropdown menu in the **Medical Volunteer Status** field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Address**: Enter your physical address where you can be contacted for medical purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your work or personal email address associated with your medical license.
* **Phone**: Enter your work or personal phone number where you can be reached during emergencies.
* **Medical License Number**: Enter your official medical license number.
* **Medical License Number 2** (optional): Enter the second medical license number you may hold.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name of the person authorized to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person authorized to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person authorized to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person authorized to contact in case of an emergency (optional).
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the address of the person authorized to contact in case of an emergency (optional).
* **Specialty**: Select your primary medical specialty.
* **Medical School Graduation Year**: If applicable, enter the year you graduated from medical school.
* **Certification Year**: If applicable, enter the year you obtained your medical certification.
* **Expiration Date**: Enter the expiration date of your medical license.
* **Medical License Expiration Date 2**: If applicable, enter the expiration date of your second medical license.
* **Volunteer Start Date**: Enter the date you started your volunteer service.
* **Language**: Choose your primary language from the dropdown menu (optional).
* **Medical Volunteer Agreement**: Agree to the terms of the volunteer agreement by typing your name.
* **Emergency Response**: Select whether you are able or unable to respond to emergencies.
* **Medical License Number 3**: If applicable, enter the number associated with your third medical license.
* **Medical Volunteer Status**: Select your current volunteer status from the dropdown menu.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields marked as required are completed.
* Please provide accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
* If you have any issues or concerns about the form, please contact your organization's administration office.
