# Donor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from individuals or organizations interested in supporting the nonprofit organization through donations, volunteering, or other forms of support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Donor Information section with your name, address, and any other details requested.
2. In the Contribution section, specify how you would like to contribute (money, time, resources, etc.).
3. Enter your address in the Donor Address section to ensure that the nonprofit can contact you about your application.
4. If you have any additional contact information, provide it in the Contact Information section. This is not required, but it's helpful for us to follow up with you about your application.
5. Select your interest area from the Donor Interest section, which will help us to direct your application to the right department within the nonprofit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Information (page 1, required)**: Enter your name as it appears on official documents and your address to ensure we can contact you correctly.
* **Contribution (page 1, required)**: Specify your intended type of contribution (e.g., money, time, resources).
* **Donor Address (page 1, required)**: Enter your address to receive updates and information about your application.
* **Contact Information (page 2, optional)**: If you have a preferred method of communication (e.g., email, phone, mail), enter it here.
* **Donor Interest (page 2, required)**: Select the area where you would like to contribute (e.g., programs, events, general support). This will help us direct your application to the right department within the nonprofit.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed and specific as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We appreciate your time and interest in supporting the nonprofit, and we look forward to reviewing your application.
