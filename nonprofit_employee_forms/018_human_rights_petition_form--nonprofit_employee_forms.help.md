# Human Rights Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Human Rights Petition Form is a data collection tool designed to gather information from individuals or organizations related to human rights petitions. This form aims to gather data on various aspects of human rights, including select one or multiple options, dates, and times.

## How To Complete This Form

Before completing this form, please make sure you have the necessary information and permissions to provide. The form is designed to be easy to use and navigate, with clear instructions for each field. To complete this form:

1. Please fill out all required fields (marked as required).
2. Choose the correct options for each field from the list of options provided.
3. Make sure to enter accurate and valid information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Data**
  * **<label: Form Data</label>**: This field is a text field where you can enter any relevant information about the human rights issue or petition. This can include the name of the organization, date, or other details.
* **Name**
  * **<label: Name</label>**: This field is for your name or the name of the representative submitting the petition.
* **Email**
  * **<label: Email</label>**: This field is for your email address or the email address of the representative submitting the petition.
* **Phone**
  * **<label: Phone</label>**: This field is for your phone number or the phone number of the representative submitting the petition.
* **Organization**
  * **<label: Organization</label>**: This field is for the name of the organization submitting the petition.
* **Select One**

* **Date**
