# Pet Rescue Adoption Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pet Rescue Adoption Application Form is used by potential adopters to express their interest in adopting a pet from a pet rescue organization. Fill out this form to start the adoption process and take the first step towards providing a forever home for a deserving pet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your name and email address.
2. Share information about the pet you're interested in adopting, such as its type (cat, dog, or other).
3. Describe how you'd like to adopt, including your request for a specific pet or any special circumstances.
4. Provide your owner information, including your address and phone number.
5. List your emergency contact information and contact details.
6. Choose the date you'd like to adopt the pet.
7. Review and sign the adoption agreement, which includes terms to be agreed upon for the adoption.
8. Add any notes or comments you'd like to share with the adoption team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
Each field is explained below:

* **Contact Info**: Please enter your name and email address so we can get in touch with you about your adoption application.
* **Pet Info**: Describe the type of pet you're interested in adopting (cat, dog, or other).
* **Adoption Request**: Share with us how you'd like to adopt, including any special circumstances or preferences.
* **Owner Info**: Provide your address and phone number so we can contact you.
* **Owner Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your adoption application.
* **Owner Email**: Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you about your adoption application.
* **Emergency Contact**: List the name and contact information of someone we can contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Info**: Provide any additional information about your emergency contact, such as their relationship to you and their contact details.
* **Adoption Date**: Choose a date for the adoption, if you have one in mind.
* **Signature**: Please sign below to acknowledge that you've read and agree to the adoption agreement terms.
* **Adoption Agreement**: Review and select the terms you agree to for the adoption.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about your adoption application.
