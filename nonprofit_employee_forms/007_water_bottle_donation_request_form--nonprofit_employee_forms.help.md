# Water Bottle Donation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Water Bottle Donation Request Form" is a crucial tool for requesting water bottle donations for those in need. By filling out this form, you will be able to request water bottles and provide us with the necessary information to facilitate the donation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your donor information, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. Indicate whether you require water bottles (select "Yes" or "No").
3. Provide any additional delivery details, such as pickup date and time.
4. Specify the pickup location for the water bottles.
5. Choose the delivery method (pick up at our location or delivery at your location).
6. Enter the delivery location if you chose delivery at your location.
7. Add any notes or comments about your request.
8. Confirm whether you have a specific person assigned to receive the water bottles (select "Yes" or "No").
9. If you have a person assigned, provide their phone number and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Information** (Donor Information, text, required: false):
	+ This field is for your contact information. Please enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Water Requests** (Water Requests, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select "Yes" if you require water bottles, and "No" if you do not.
* **Delivery Details** (Delivery Details, text, required: false):
	+ If you require water bottles, enter the date and time you would like them delivered.
* **Pickup Location** (Pickup Location, text, required: false):
	+ If you are picking up the water bottles, enter the location where you would like to collect them.
* **Pickup Date Time** (Pickup Date Time, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date you would like to collect the water bottles.
* **Pickup Time** (Pickup Time, time, required: false):
	+ Enter the time you would like to collect the water bottles.
* **Delivery Method** (Delivery Method, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose whether you would like to pick up the water bottles at our location or have them delivered at your location.
* **Delivery Location** (Delivery Location, text, required: false):
	+ If you chose delivery at your location, enter the address where the water bottles should be delivered.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, required: false):
	+ Enter any additional comments or notes about your request.
* **Assigned To** (Assigned To, select_one, required: false):
	+ If you would like to have someone assigned to receive the water bottles, select "Yes".
* **Assigned To (Phone)** (Assigned To (Phone), text, required: false):
	+ Enter the phone number of the person assigned to receive the water bottles.
* **Assigned To (Email)** (Assigned To (Email), email, required: false):
	+ Enter the email address of the person assigned to receive the water bottles.

Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth and efficient donation process.
