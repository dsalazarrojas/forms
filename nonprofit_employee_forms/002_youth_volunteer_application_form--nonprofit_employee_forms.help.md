# Youth Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals interested in volunteering for a youth-focused organization. It helps us understand your contact information, volunteer availability, and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
- Fill out your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
- Enter your email address to ensure we can reach you.
- If you have a phone number with an area code, please include it.
- Fill out your address to ensure we can send you notifications and mail you important information.
- Enter your zip code for geographic location.
- If you'd like to share, enter your date of birth for identification purposes.
- Indicate the number of volunteer hours you are available to commit.
- Tell us how often you'd like to volunteer.
- Explain why you want to volunteer with us.
- Select one or more volunteer interests (events, offices, or programs).
- Choose your preferred volunteer commitment (part-time or full-time).
- Enter your available hours for volunteering.
- Select your level of education for our reference.
- Share your volunteer experience in the past.
- Provide emergency contact information, including their name, relationship, and phone number with area code.
- If necessary, mention any medical conditions, medications, or allergies.
- If applicable, share any medical emergencies that may require attention.
- Lastly, indicate any disability that may be relevant for your volunteering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name to complete the form.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Provide a valid email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, false): Include your phone number with an area code.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your current address for delivery and notification purposes.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your zip code for geographic location.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, false): Share your date of birth for identification.
* **Volunteer Hours** (`volunteer_hours`, `number`, required): Indicate the number of volunteer hours you can commit.
* **Volunteer Frequency** (`volunteer_frequency`, `number`, required): Choose how often you'd like to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Reason** (`volunteer_reason`, `text`, false): Explain why you want to volunteer with us.
* **Volunteer Interests** (`volunteer_interests`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more areas you're interested in volunteering (events, offices, or programs).
* **Volunteer Commitment** (`volunteer_commitment`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred volunteer commitment (part-time or full-time).
* **Available Hours** (`available_hours`, `time`, required): Enter your available hours for volunteering.
* **Volunteer Education** (`volunteer_education`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your level of education for our reference.
* **Volunteer Experience** (`volunteer_experience`, `text`, required): Share your volunteer experience in the past.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required): Provide the name of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, `text`, required): Specify your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, `text`, false): Include the phone number of your emergency contact with area code.
* **Emergency Address** (`emergency_address`, `text`, false): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, false): If necessary, share any medical conditions or disabilities.
* **Medication** (`medication`, `text`, false): List any medication you're currently taking.
* **Allergy** (`allergy`, `text`, false): Declare any allergies or sensitivities.
* **Medical Emergency** (`medical_emergency`, `text`, false): If necessary, share any medical emergencies that may require attention.
* **Disability** (`disability`, `text`, false): If applicable, specify any disability or special requirements.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, false): Press submit to finalize your application.
