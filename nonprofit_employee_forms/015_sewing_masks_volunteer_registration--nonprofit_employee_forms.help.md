# sewing_masks_volunteer_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the sewing masks volunteer registration form, which is used to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering their time to create sewing masks for those in need.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This field is required.
2. Enter a valid **Email** address in the "Email" field. This field is not required but recommended for internal contact purposes.
3. Enter your **Address** in the "Address" field. This field is required.
4. Enter your **City** in the "City" field. This field is required.
5. Enter your **State** in the "State" field. This field is required.
6. Enter your **Zip Code** in the "Zip Code" field. This field is required.
7. Enter a valid **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field. This field is not required but recommended for internal contact purposes.
8. Enter your **skills**, such as sewing, in the "What skills you have?" field. This field is not required.
9. Select how often you can **volunteer** in the "How often can you volunteer?" field. You can choose from "Every Day", "2-3 times a week", "1 time a week", or "Rarely".
10. Select all the times you are **available** in the "What time of day are you available?" field. You can choose from "Morning", "Afternoon", "Evening", or "Other (please specify)".
11. If you selected "Other (please specify)" in the previous field, please enter your availability in the "Other (please specify)" field. This field is not required.
12. Select all the **volunteer types** you are interested in, such as "Sewing" or "Non-sewing", in the "What type of volunteer are you?" field. This field is not required.
13. Select all the **locations** where you can volunteer, such as "Home", "Office", or "Local park", in the "Where you can volunteer?" field. This field is not required.
14. Enter your emergency contact's **Name** in the "Emergency Contact Name" field. This field is not required.
15. Enter your emergency contact's **Phone Number** in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field. This field is not required.
16. Select your relationship with your emergency contact, such as "Parent", "Spouse", "Child", "Sibling", or "Other (please specify)", in the "Relationship" field. This field is not required.
17. If you selected "Other (please specify)" in the previous field, please enter your relationship in the "Other (please specify)" field. This field is not required.
