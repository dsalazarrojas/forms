# Nonprofit Technology Training Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from nonprofit employees to record video and audio during training sessions. It's essential to fill out this form to ensure that your recordings are done with your permission.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out this form carefully and accurately.
- Please answer each question truthfully.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Training Recording Consent** (1): This is the main question that asks for your consent to record video and audio during training sessions. You can select one of the options: "Recording of video and audio," "No recording of video," "No recording of audio," "Both," or "None."
- **Name** (2): Please enter your full name as it appears on your employee identification.
- **Email** (3): Enter your email address where you can be reached for future references or questions.
- **Phone** (4): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for future references or questions.
- **Organization** (5): If you're not sure about the organization, you can leave this field blank.
- **Department** (6): If you're not sure about the department, you can leave this field blank.
- **Position** (7): Please enter your job title or position within the organization.
- **Permission to Record** (8): Select one of the options that best suits your preference for recording video and audio during training sessions.
  - "Recording of video and audio" means you permit both video and audio recording.
  - "No recording of video" means you don't want any video recording.
  - "No recording of audio" means you don't want any audio recording.
  - "Both" means you permit both audio and video recording.
  - "None" means you don't want any recording.
- **Permission to Share** (9): Select all that apply for who you're willing to share your recorded sessions with:
  - "True" means you permit sharing with any of the selected options.
  - "False" means you don't want any sharing.
- **Permission to Share with** (10): Select all that apply for who you're willing to share your recorded sessions with:
  - "Training Team" means you're okay with sharing with the training team.
  - "Supervisor" means you're okay with sharing with your supervisor.
  - "HR" means you're okay with sharing with HR.
  - "None" means you don't want sharing with anyone.
- **Signature Date** (11): This is the date you sign this consent form.
- **Signature** (12): Place your signature here, indicating that you've reviewed this form and understand its implications.
- **Signature Position** (13): If you have a title within the organization, enter it here.
- **Notes** (14): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please type them here.

## Tips
- Please review your selections carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or HR department.
