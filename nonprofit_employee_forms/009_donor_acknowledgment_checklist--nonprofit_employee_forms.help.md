# Donor Acknowledgment Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Donor Acknowledgment Checklist is used to record and acknowledge donations received by the nonprofit organization. This form is essential for tracking and verifying donations, ensuring that all donations are properly documented and accounted for.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Donation Date" field and select the date the donation was made.
2. Enter the "Acknowledgment Date" field with the date you are acknowledging the donation.
3. Enter the "Donor Name" field with the full name of the donor.
4. Enter the "Donation Amount" field with the amount of the donation.
5. Enter a message in the "Acknowledgment Message" field to express gratitude and acknowledge the donation.
6. Enter the "Email Address" field with the donor's email address.
7. Enter the "Phone Number" field with the donor's phone number.
8. Select the "Donor Relationship" field with the donor's relationship with the nonprofit organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Date**: Enter the date the donation was made.
* **Acknowledgment Date**: Enter the date you are acknowledging the donation.
* **Donor Name**: Enter the full name of the donor.
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount of the donation received.
* **Acknowledgment Message**: Enter a message to express gratitude and acknowledge the donation.
* **Email Address**: Enter the donor's email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the donor's phone number.
* **Donor Relationship**: Select the donor's relationship with the nonprofit organization.
