# disaster_supply_organizer_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The disaster supply organizer application form is intended for individuals interested in helping in disaster relief efforts by organizing supplies and resources. This form will help us gather your contact information and other necessary details to ensure you are eligible and have the necessary permissions to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Provide your Email address in the **Email** field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. If you are representing an organization, enter your **Contact Organization** name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your personal name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your surname as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Contact Organization** (`contact_organization`, text, required: false): If you are representing an organization, enter its name to help us identify you as a representative.

## Tips
* Ensure you are eligible and have the necessary permissions before submitting this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact [support email or contact info].
