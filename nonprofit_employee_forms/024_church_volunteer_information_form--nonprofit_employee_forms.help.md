# Church Volunteer Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Volunteer Information Form is a tool designed to gather information from potential volunteers. It collects personal details, contact information, and information about their desired start date, commitment level, and areas of ministry interest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Provide your email address in the **Email Address** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the **Date of Birth** field.
6. Provide your street address in the **Street Address** field.
7. Enter the city where you reside in the **City** field.
8. Enter the state or province where you reside in the **State or Province** field.
9. Enter your ZIP or postal code in the **ZIP or Postal Code** field.
10. Enter the name of your emergency contact in the **Emergency Contact Name** field.
11. Enter the phone number of your emergency contact in the **Emergency Contact Phone** field.
12. Select the relationship to your emergency contact in the **Relationship to Emergency Contact** field.
13. Choose your desired start date in the **Desired Start Date** field.
14. Select your commitment level in the **Volunteer Commitment Level** field.
15. If applicable, enter the number of hours you can commit to per week in the **Anticipated Hours per Week** field.
16. If you have any skills or talents, select them from the list in the **Skills and Talents** field.
17. If you have previous volunteer or ministry experience, enter it in the **Previous Volunteer or Ministry Experience** field.
18. Select areas of ministry interest from the list in the **Areas of Ministry Interest** field.
19. If you have a specific role or position in mind, enter it in the **Specific Role or Position Interested In** field.
20. If you'd like to provide references, enter their names, phone numbers, and relationships in the corresponding fields.
21. Finally, select whether you consent to a background check in the **Consent to Background Check** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your given name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Street Address**: Enter the street address where you reside.
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State or Province**: Enter the state or province where you reside.
* **ZIP or Postal Code**: Enter your ZIP or postal code.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship to Emergency Contact**: Select how you are related to your emergency contact (e.g., spouse, parent, etc.).
* **Desired Start Date**: Choose the date you would like to start volunteering.
* **Volunteer Commitment Level**: Select how often you are willing to volunteer (e.g., Long-term, Seasonal, Occasional, etc.).
* **Anticipated Hours per Week**: If applicable, enter the number of hours you can commit to per week.
* **Skills and Talents**: Select any skills or talents you have that would be valuable to the church.
* **Previous Volunteer or Ministry Experience**: Enter any previous volunteer or ministry experience you have.
* **Areas of Ministry Interest**: Select areas of ministry where you are interested in serving.
* **Specific Role or Position Interested In**: Enter any specific role or position you are interested in.
* **Reference Name 1**: Enter the name of your reference.
* **Reference Phone 1**: Enter the phone number of your reference.
* **Reference Relationship 1**: Enter your relationship to your reference.
* **Consent to Background Check**: Select whether you consent to a background check.
