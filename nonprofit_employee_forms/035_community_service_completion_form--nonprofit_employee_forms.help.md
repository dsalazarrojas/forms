# Community Service Completion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your volunteer work, including details about the service hours, tasks, and experiences from your service. This information helps us to track the impact of our services and evaluate their effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details in the "Volunteer Information" page.
2. Select your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY" in the "Date of Birth" field.
6. Enter your street address in the "Street Address" field.
7. Enter your city in the "City" field.
8. Enter your state in the "State" field.
9. Enter your ZIP code in the "ZIP Code" field.
10. Select the type of service you performed in the "Type of Service" field (e.g., Community Outreach, Environmental Cleanup, etc.).
11. Enter a brief description of your service in the "Service Description" field.
12. Enter the start and end dates of your service in the "Service Start Date" and "Service End Date" fields.
13. Enter the total number of hours you completed in the "Total Hours Completed" field.
14. Select the dates you worked on the "Service Schedule" field.
15. Select the completion status of your service from the options (e.g., Completed, In Progress, Partially Completed, Discontinued).
16. Select the supervisor's name in the "Supervisor Name" field.
17. Enter the supervisor's email address in the "Supervisor Email" field.
18. Enter the supervisor's phone number in the "Supervisor Phone" field (optional).
19. Select all the skills you demonstrated during your service in the "Skills Demonstrated" field.
20. Enter a reflection on your experience in the "What Did You Learn from This Experience" field.
21. Select if you would continue volunteering (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Maybe, Probably Not, Definitely Not).
22. Select if you would recommend this service to others (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Maybe, Probably No, Definitely No).
23. Enter your signature in the "Volunteer Signature" field.
24. Enter the date of your signature in the "Date of Signature" field.
25. Enter your supervisor's signature in the "Supervisor Signature" field.
26. Enter the date of your supervisor's signature in the "Supervisor Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Information**: This page provides a brief space for you to enter your personal details.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Street Address**: Enter your street address.
* **City**: Enter the city where you live.
* **State**: Enter the state where you live.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter your ZIP code.
* **Type of Service**: Select the type of service you performed (e.g., Community Outreach, Environmental Cleanup, etc.).
* **Service Description**: Provide a brief description of your service.
* **Service Start Date**: Enter the start date of your service.
* **Service End Date**: Enter the end date of your service.
* **Total Hours Completed**: Enter the total number of hours you completed during your service.
* **Service Schedule**: Select the dates you worked on.
* **Completion Status**: Select the completion status of your service (e.g., Completed, In Progress, Partially Completed, Discontinued).
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Email**: Enter the email address of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Phone**: Enter the phone number of your supervisor (optional).
* **Skills Demonstrated**: Select all the skills you demonstrated during your service.
* **Service Hours**: This field is a note field to track your service hours.
* **What Did You Learn from This Experience**: Enter a reflection on your experience.
* **Would You Continue Volunteering**: Select if you would continue volunteering (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Maybe, Probably Not, Definitely Not).
* **Would You Recommend This to Others**: Select if you would recommend this service to others (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Maybe, Probably No, Definitely No).
* **Verification**: This field is a note field for signatures.
* **Volunteer Signature**: Enter your full name as a signature.
* **Date of Signature**: Enter the date of your signature.
* **Supervisor Signature**: Enter your supervisor's full name as a signature.
* **Supervisor Date**: Enter the date of your supervisor's signature.

## Tips
* Please complete the form carefully and accurately.
* If you have any questions or need help, do not hesitate to ask.
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* This form helps us to evaluate the effectiveness of our services and track your volunteer work.
* Thank you for your service, and we appreciate your completion of this form!
