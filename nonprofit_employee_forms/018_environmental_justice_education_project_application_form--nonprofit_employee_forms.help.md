# Environmental Justice Education Project Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from potential applicants for the Environmental Justice Education Project. It is an optional form that helps the project team assess and track applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User First Name in the "User First Name" field.
2. Fill in your User Last Name in the "User Last Name" field.
3. Select your school or nonprofit affiliation from the "School or Nonprofit" dropdown menu.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Select which application form you would like to submit in the "Application Form" dropdown menu.
7. Enter the date you applied in the "Date Applied" field.
8. Enter the time you applied in the "Time Applied" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User First Name** (`user_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification or business cards.
* **User Last Name** (`user_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification or business cards.
* **School or Nonprofit** (`school_or_nonprofit`, select_one, optional): Select if you are affiliated with a school or nonprofit organization. This will help us understand your background and potential relevance to our project.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Application Form** (`application_form`, select_one, optional): Select which application form you would like to submit. This will help us assess your qualifications for our project.
* **Date Applied** (`date_applied`, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted your application.
* **Time Applied** (`time_applied`, time, optional): Enter the time you submitted your application.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required information, but note that this form is optional.
* If you have any questions or issues with the form, contact us at [insert contact information].
