# Corporate Matching Gift Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information from employees about potential matching gifts that can be matched by their employer to increase the employee's gift.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your employer's information, including the company name and address.
2. Enter your employee information, including your name and contact details.
3. Provide details about the gift you are requesting to be matched, including the gift's purpose and the recipient's information.
4. Specify the matching funds information, including the matching gift amount and the gift's purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employer Information** (Page 1, Employer Information):
	+ This field is for your employer's information, which is required for verification purposes.
	+ Enter the company name and address where you work.
* **Employee Information** (Page 1, Employee Information):
	+ This field is for your personal information, which is required for verification purposes.
	+ Enter your name, title, and contact details.
* **Gift Details** (Page 2, Gift Details):
	+ This field is for providing details about the gift you are requesting to be matched.
	+ Enter the gift's purpose and the recipient's information.
* **Matching Gift Information** (Page 2, Matching Gift Information):
	+ This field is for providing additional information about the matching gift process.
	+ This field is required to ensure that the matching gift is legitimate.
* **Matching Funds** (Page 3, Matching Funds):
	+ This field is for specifying the matching funds information.
	+ Enter the matching gift amount and the gift's purpose.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the form is filled out correctly and accurately to avoid any issues with the matching gift process.
- If you are unsure about any field, consult with your HR department or the form creator.
- This form is for internal use only and should not be shared with anyone outside the organization.
- The organization reserves the right to verify the employee's identity and the gift's legitimacy before matching the gift.
