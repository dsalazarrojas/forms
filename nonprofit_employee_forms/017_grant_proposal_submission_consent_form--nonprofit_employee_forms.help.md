# Grant Proposal Submission Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information required for a grant proposal submission to the granting organization. It is intended to gather details about the project, the submitting organization, and the submitting organization's contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the project details (Grant Amount, Project Duration, Project Budget, Project Location, and Project Description).
2. Indicate whether the Granting Organization has granted or not.
3. Confirm if the Submitting Organization is submitting the proposal.
4. Provide the Submitting Organization's contact information (Submitting Organization, Submitting Organization Contact, Submitting Organization Address, Submitting Organization Email, and Submitting Organization Phone).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Grant Amount**: Enter the total amount of the grant being requested.
* **Project Duration**: Enter the duration of the project in time format (e.g., 1 month, 2 months, 3 years, etc.).
* **Project Budget**: Enter the total budget for the project.
* **Project Location**: Enter the location where the project will take place.
* **Project Description**: Provide a detailed description of the project.
* **Granting Organization**: Check 'Yes' if the Granting Organization has granted or approved the project.
* **Submitting Organization**: Check 'Yes' if the Submitting Organization is submitting the proposal.
* **Submitting Organization Contact**: Check 'Yes' if the Submitting Organization Contact is the authorized representative.
* **Submitting Organization Address**: Enter the address of the Submitting Organization.
* **Submitting Organization Email**: Enter the email of the Submitting Organization Contact.
* **Submitting Organization Phone**: Enter the phone number of the Submitting Organization Contact.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately.
- Double-check the information before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the Granting Organization's support team for clarification.
