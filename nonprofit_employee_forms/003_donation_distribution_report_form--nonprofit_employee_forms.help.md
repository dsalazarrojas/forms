# donation_distribution_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track donations made to the nonprofit organization. It provides a structured way to collect information about the donor, the amount and purpose of the donation, and the recipient of the donation. This information is essential for the organization to manage and report on donations accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Donor Information" section with details about the donor, including their name, title, email, and phone number.
2. Enter the "Date of Donation" in the format MM/DD/YY to record the date the donation was made.
3. Enter the "Amount Donated" in the currency of your choice.
4. In the "Purpose of Donation" field, briefly describe the reason or purpose of the donation.
5. Enter the "Donor Name", "Donor Title", "Recipient Name", and "Recipient Title" fields with the relevant information.
6. Enter the "Donor Email" and "Donor Phone" fields with the donor's contact information.
7. Review the form carefully to ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Information** (`donor_information`, text, required: false): This field is used to record the donor's contact information, including their name, title, email, and phone number.
* **Date of Donation** (`date_of_donation`, date, required: false): Enter the date the donation was made in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Amount Donated** (`amount_donated`, number, required: false): Enter the exact amount donated in the currency of your choice.
* **Purpose of Donation** (`purpose_of_donation`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the reason or purpose of the donation.
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, text, required: false): Enter the donor's name.
* **Donor Title** (`donor_title`, text, required: false): Enter the donor's title or profession.
* **Recipient Name** (`recipient_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the recipient of the donation.
* **Recipient Title** (`recipient_title`, text, required: false): Enter the title or profession of the recipient.
* **Donor Email** (`donor_email`, email, required: false): Enter the donor's email address.
* **Donor Phone** (`donor_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the donor's phone number.

Note: This guide is intended to be used as a quick reference for employees who are not familiar with the form. It is recommended to review the form carefully before filling it out to ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
