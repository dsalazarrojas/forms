# Food Bank Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Bank Registration Form is a document used for non-profit employees to register themselves with a food bank. This form is used to gather information about the individual registering, including their name, email, and phone number.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your last name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field. Make sure to format it as "example@example.com".
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required: false): Enter the first name you wish to be known by in the food bank. This can be a shortened version of your name if you prefer.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required: false): Enter the last name you wish to be known by in the food bank. This can be a shortened version of your last name if you prefer.
* **Email** (Email, email, required: false): Enter your email address. This will be used to contact you about food bank matters. Please use a valid format such as "example@example.com".
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required: false): Enter your phone number. This is optional but can be used as an alternative contact method.

## Tips
* Make sure to double check your email address for accuracy before submitting the form. This will ensure that you receive important updates about the food bank.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact the non-profit's help desk for assistance.
