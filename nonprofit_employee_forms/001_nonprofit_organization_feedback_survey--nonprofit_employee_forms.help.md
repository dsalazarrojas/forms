# Nonprofit Organization Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Nonprofit Organization Feedback Survey form is designed to collect feedback from nonprofit organizations on their experience with our services. This form helps us understand their needs and areas of improvement to provide better support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Please select the "Nonprofit Organization Feedback Survey Form" option on the left-hand side of the page.
* Enter your **Name** in the "Name" field.
* Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field.
* Provide your **Feedback** in the text box below "Feedback".
* Rate our **Rating (1-5)** services.
* Select "Submit" to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it is spelled on your business card or identification.
* **Email** (`Email`, text, required/optional): Enter your official email address.
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, text, required/optional): Describe in a few sentences what you think we could improve, if anything.
* **Rating (1-5)** (`Rating`, number, required/optional): Rate our services from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, text, required/optional): This field will be automatically populated with the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required/optional): This field will be automatically populated with the current date.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Submit" to send your feedback for processing.
* **Cancel** (`Cancel`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Cancel" to discard your feedback.

## Tips
* Please answer all required fields to ensure your feedback is complete and accurate.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [your contact email or phone number].
* We appreciate your feedback and look forward to enhancing our services based on your input.</thinking>
