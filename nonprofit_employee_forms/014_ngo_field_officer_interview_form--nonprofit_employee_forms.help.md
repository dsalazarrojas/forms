# NGO Field Officer Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NGO Field Officer Interview Form is a data collection tool used by the NGO to gather information about your personal and professional details, as well as job postings for which you may be applying or supervising.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Contact Info (Page 1).
2. Select your Job Title (Page 2).
3. For each Job Rep Title, select all the relevant options that apply to your work (Pages 3-6).
4. Briefly describe your Job Description (Page 7).
5. Outline your Job Requirements (Page 8).
6. Enter your Interview Duration (Page 9).
7. Enter the datetime of your interview (Page 10).
8. Enter your desired Salary (Page 11).
9. Select your Benefits (Page 12).
10. Enter the Job Posted On date (Page 13).
11. Enter the Job Posted By (Page 14).
12. Enter the Job Posted Time (Page 15).
13. Enter the Job Closing On date (Page 16).
14. Select your Job Openning options (Page 17).
15. Select your Job Closing options (Page 18).
16. Select your Job Schedule options (Page 19).
17. Select your Job Status options (Page 20).
18. Select your Job Locations options (Page 21).
19. Select your Work Schedule options (Page 22).
20. Enter your Work Hour Duration (Page 23).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info** (Page 1): This is where you can enter your contact details such as your name, email, and other relevant information.
* **Job Title** (Page 2): Select your job title from the options provided.
* **Job Rep 1 Title** (Page 3): Select all the relevant job rep titles that apply to your work.
* **Job Rep 2 Title** (Page 4): Select all the relevant job rep titles that apply to your work.
* **Job Rep 3 Title** (Page 5): Select all the relevant job rep titles that apply to your work.
* **Job Rep 4 Title** (Page 6): Select all the relevant job rep titles that apply to your work.
* **Job Description** (Page 7): Briefly describe your job description.
* **Job Requirements** (Page 8): Outline your job requirements.
* **Interview Duration** (Page 9): Enter the duration of your interview.
* **Interview Datetime** (Page 10): Enter the date and time of your interview.
* **Salary** (Page 11): Enter your desired salary.
* **Benefits** (Page 12): Select all the benefits you are entitled to.
* **Job Posted On** (Page 13): Enter the date when the job was posted.
* **Job Posted By** (Page 14): Enter the person who posted the job.
* **Job Posted Time** (Page 15): Enter the time when the job was posted.
* **Job Closing On** (Page 16): Enter the date when the job will close.
* **Job Openning** (Page 17): Select all the options that apply to your job openning.
* **Job Closing** (Page 18): Select all the options that apply to your job closing.
* **Job Schedule** (Page 19): Select all the options that apply to your job schedule.
* **Job Status** (Page 20): Select all the options that apply to your job status.
* **Job Locations** (Page 21): Select all the locations where you will be working.
* **Work Schedule** (Page 22): Select all the options that apply to your work schedule.
* **Work Hour Duration** (Page 23): Enter the duration of your work hours.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly to ensure that we can better understand your needs and qualifications for the position.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification before proceeding.
* Make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form.
