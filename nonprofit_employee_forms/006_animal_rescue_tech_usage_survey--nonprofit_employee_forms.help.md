# Animal Rescue Tech Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on how animal rescue organizations use technology in their operations.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please fill out the form by providing the required information for each field.
* Make sure to select all relevant options for each field.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can skip it or provide additional comments in the "Additional Comments or Suggestions" field.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (Page 1)
  * (`organization_name`, text, required)
  * This field asks for the name of your animal rescue organization.
* **Your Name** (Page 1)
  * (`respondent_name`, text, required)
  * This field asks for your full name.
* **Your Role** (Page 1)
  * (`respondent_role`, select_one, required)
  * This field asks for your position within the organization. Please select one of the provided options.
* **Email Address** (Page 1)
  * (`email`, email, required)
  * This field asks for your contact email.
* **Organization Size** (Page 1)
  * (`organization_size`, select_one, required)
  * This field asks for the number of animals served annually. Please select one of the provided options.
* **What Software Tools Do You Currently Use?** (Page 1)
  * (`current_software`, select_multiple, required)
  * This field asks you to select all the software tools your organization currently uses. Please choose from the provided options.
* **Satisfaction with Current Technology** (Page 1)
  * (`software_satisfaction`, select_one, required)
  * This field asks for your overall satisfaction level with the current technology used in your organization. Please select one of the provided options.
* **What Technology Challenges Do You Face?** (Page 1)
  * (`tech_challenges`, select_multiple, required)
  * This field asks you to select all the technology challenges your organization faces. Please choose from the provided options.
* **How Do You Currently Manage Animal Data?** (Page 1)
  * (`data_management`, select_one, required)
  * This field asks for your primary method of managing animal data. Please select one of the provided options.
* **How Do You Track Adoptions?** (Page 1)
  * (`adoption_tracking`, select_one, required)
  * This field asks for your method of tracking adoptions. Please select one of the provided options.
* **How Do You Coordinate Volunteers?** (Page 1)
  * (`volunteer_coordination`, select_one, required)
  * This field asks for your method of coordinating volunteers. Please select one of the provided options.
* **How Do You Manage Donor Information?** (Page 1)
  * (`donor_management`, select_one, required)
  * This field asks for your method of managing donor information. Please select one of the provided options.
* **Annual Technology Budget** (Page 2)
  * (`tech_budget`, select_one, optional)
  * This field asks for your approximate annual budget spent on technology. Please select one of the provided options, or leave blank if not applicable.
* **What Features Would Most Benefit Your Organization?** (Page 2)
  * (`desired_features`, select_multiple, required)
  * This field asks you to select the top features that would most benefit your organization. Please choose from the provided options.
* **What Technology Training Would Be Helpful?** (Page 2)
  * (`training_needs`, select_multiple, optional)
  * This field asks you to select the technology training that would be helpful for your organization. Please choose from the provided options, or leave blank if not applicable.
* **If Adopting New Technology, What Is Your Timeline?** (Page 2)
  * (`implementation_timeline`, select_one, required)
  * This field asks for your timeline for adopting new technology. Please select one of the provided options.
* **Who Makes Technology Decisions?** (Page 2)
  * (`decision_makers`, select_one, required)
  * This field asks for the person or group that makes technology decisions in your organization. Please select one of the provided options.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions** (Page 2)
  * (`additional_comments`, text, optional)
  * This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and thoroughly to provide valuable feedback.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* This form is designed to be completed in approximately 10-15 minutes, but you can take as much time as needed to provide accurate information.
