## Nonprofit Advocacy Strategy Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nonprofit Advocacy Strategy Feedback Evaluation Form is a tool for your organization to collect feedback from employees about their experience with the current advocacy strategy. This feedback will help inform future changes and improvements to the strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the role that best describes your position within the organization from the dropdown menu.
2. Rate the current advocacy strategy using the provided options (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
3. Describe the biggest challenges you are facing with the current advocacy strategy.
4. Provide any suggestions you have for improving the current advocacy strategy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role in the organization?** (Executive Director, Program Director, Development Director, Communications Director, Other): Please select the role that best describes your position within the organization.
* **How do you rate the current advocacy strategy?** (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor): Rate the current strategy based on your experience. Select all that apply if you have a mixed opinion.
* **What are the biggest challenges you're facing with the current advocacy strategy?** (Text field): Describe in detail the challenges you are facing. This will help us understand the difficulties you're experiencing with the current strategy.
* **What suggestions do you have for improving the current advocacy strategy?** (Text field): Share any ideas you have for improving the strategy. This will help us create a more effective and impactful advocacy strategy for the organization.
