# Vote For Courageous Women - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Vote For Courageous Women" form is designed to collect votes on individuals who embody courage. Users are asked to select from a list of pre-defined options or provide a free-form text entry for other individuals not listed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have filled out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your name in the "first_name" field.
3. Enter your name in the "last_name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
5. In the "story" field, enter any additional comments or information about your choice.
6. Select your answer to the question "vote" and "vote_for" as applicable.
7. If you do not see your preferred individual in the list, you can enter their name in the "note" field.
8. Select your preferred tool in the "assigned_tool" field.
9. Finally, choose a file format for the output from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **email** (Text, Required): Enter your email address.
* **story** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your choice.
* **vote** (Select One, Required): Select "Yes" if you would vote for the individual(s).
* **vote_for** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select "Yes" for individuals you would vote for.
* **note** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of an individual not listed but whom you would vote for.
* **assigned_tool** (Select One, Required): Select the tool assigned to this vote, which is likely a specific individual.
* **output_file** (Select One, Required): Choose a file format for the output, possibly indicating how the results will be displayed.
* **date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of your vote.
* **time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of your vote.

## Tips

* Be sure to review and confirm your selections before submitting the form.
* If an individual you would vote for is not listed, type their name in the "note" field.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact your administrator for clarification.
