# Top Refugee Support Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Top Refugee Support Nomination Form is a crucial tool for recognizing and rewarding employees who have made significant contributions to supporting refugees. This form allows you to nominate employees who deserve to be recognized for their dedication and hard work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Organization Name: This is the name of the organization where the employee works.
2. Enter the Employee Name: This is the name of the employee you are nominating.
3. Enter the Employee Phone: This is the contact phone number of the employee.
4. Enter the Employee Email: This is the contact email of the employee.
5. Enter the Employee Position: This is the current position of the employee within the organization.
6. Nomination Date: Enter the date of the nomination.
7. Nomination Category: Select one or more categories from the dropdown menu that best describe the employee's support.
8. Organization Description: Enter a brief description of the organization.
9. Employee Award Ems: If you have any additional comments, suggestions, or recommendations for the employee's award, enter them here.
10. Nomination Reason: Enter a brief explanation for why the employee deserves recognition.
11. Employee Award Type: Select the type of award that the employee should receive.
12. Nomination Date Range: If applicable, enter the date range of the nominated actions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name (text)**: Enter the name of the organization where the employee works.
* **Employee Name (text)**: Enter the name of the employee you are nominating.
* **Employee Phone (text)**: Enter the contact phone number of the employee.
* **Employee Email (email)**: Enter the contact email of the employee.
* **Employee Position (text)**: Enter the current position of the employee within the organization.
* **Nomination Date (date)**: Enter the date of the nomination.
* **Nomination Category (select_multiple)**: Select one or more categories from the dropdown menu that best describe the employee's support.
* **Organization Description (text)**: Enter a brief description of the organization.
* **Employee Award Ems (text)**: If you have any additional comments, suggestions, or recommendations for the employee's award, enter them here.
* **Nomination Reason (text)**: Enter a brief explanation for why the employee deserves recognition.
* **Employee Award Type (select_one)**: Select the type of award that the employee should receive.
* **Nomination Date Range (time)**: If applicable, enter the date range of the nominated actions.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have the necessary information before completing this form.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.
* If you need help or have questions, contact your supervisor or HR representative.
