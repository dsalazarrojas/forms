# international_aid_volunteer_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The international aid volunteer application form is used to collect information from volunteers who are interested in participating in international aid work. This form helps to gather essential details about the volunteer, including contact information and volunteer specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering the organization's name and any relevant contact information. This includes:
   * **Organization Name**: This is the name of the organization you represent or affiliate with.
   * **Contact Person**: This is the person who will handle any follow-up communication.
   * **Contact Email**: This is the email address of the contact person.
   * **Contact Phone**: This is the phone number of the contact person.
2. Provide the volunteer's details:
   * **Volunteer Name**: This is the name of the volunteer who will be participating in the international aid work.
   * **Role**: This is the specific role the volunteer will be performing during the international aid work.
3. Specify the volunteer's availability:
   * **Availability Start**: This is the date when the volunteer is available to start working on the international aid project.
   * **Duration**: This is the length of time the volunteer is available to work on the international aid project.
4. Add any additional details about the volunteer:
   * **Volunteer Details**: This is an optional field where you can provide any further information about the volunteer, such as their skills or experience.
