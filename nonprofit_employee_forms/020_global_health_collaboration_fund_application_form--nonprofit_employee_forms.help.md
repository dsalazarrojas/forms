# Global Health Collaboration Fund Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from non-profit organizations applying for the Global Health Collaboration Fund.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your organization's details.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled in.
3. Double-check your contact information and organizational details for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resource** (`resource`): Enter a brief description of your organization's resource or service.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your organization's name.
* **Organization** (`organization minimalist`): Enter your organization's name (again, for consistency).
* **Website** (`website`): Enter your organization's website URL.
* **Contact email** (`contact email`): Enter the email address of the person responsible for your application.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your organization's address.
* **City** (`city`): Enter the city where your organization is located.
* **Province** (`province`): Enter the province where your organization is located.
* **Postal code** (`postal code`): Enter your organization's postal code.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your organization's phone number.
* **Fax** (`fax`): Enter your organization's fax number.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter an email address associated with your organization.
* **Website 2** (`website 2`): Enter an additional website URL associated with your organization (if applicable).
* **Email 2** (`email 2`): Enter an additional email address associated with your organization (if applicable).
* **Phone 2** (`phone 2`): Enter an additional phone number associated with your organization (if applicable).
* **Fax 2** (`fax 2`): Enter an additional fax number associated with your organization (if applicable).
* **Name 2** (`name 2`): Enter an additional name associated with your organization (if applicable).
* **Organization 2** (`organization 2`): Enter an additional organization name associated with your organization (if applicable).
* **Resource 2** (`resource 2`): Enter an additional resource or service description associated with your organization (if applicable).
* **Province 2** (`province 2`): Enter an additional province where your organization is located (if applicable).
* **City 2** (`city 2`): Enter an additional city where your organization is located (if applicable).
* **Address 2** (`address 2`): Enter an additional address associated with your organization (if applicable).
* **Contact email 2** (`contact email 2`): Enter an additional contact email address associated with your organization (if applicable).
* **Contact phone 2** (`contact phone 2`): Enter an additional contact phone number associated with your organization (if applicable).
* **Contact fax 2** (`contact fax 2`): Enter an additional contact fax number associated with your organization (if applicable).

Note: If you have any questions or concerns while filling out this form, please contact us at [support email].
