# NGO Policy Advisor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NGO Policy Advisor Application Form is designed to collect essential information from applicants seeking to work as an advisor within a Non-Governmental Organization (NGO). This form will guide you through the necessary steps to provide all required details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in each field with accurate and relevant information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed, marked with "(Required)" in the field's label.
3. Attach any necessary documents or certificates as per the "Attachments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form**: This field is the starting point for your application. Please introduce yourself briefly, stating your name, contact information, and a short statement explaining your interest in the NGO role.
* **Personal Details**: In this field, please provide your personal details such as address, email, and phone number. This information will help the organization contact you for further discussions or interviews.
* **Employment Details**: Here, you'll list your current or previous employment status, including job titles and duration of employment. This will give the organization an idea of your work experience.
* **Work Experience**: In this field, describe your relevant work experience, including job titles, company names, and employment dates. This will help the organization evaluate your expertise.
* **Education Details**: Provide information about your educational background, including degrees earned, institutions attended, and relevant courses. This will help assess your qualifications.
* **Professional Certifications**: If you hold any professional certifications, list them here, along with the issuing institution and dates. This will demonstrate your expertise in your field.
* **Job Applications**: List any previous job applications you have made, including the company name, position applied for, and outcome. This will help the organization understand your application history.
* **References**: Provide professional references, including their name, title, company, and contact information. This will help the organization verify your work experience and qualifications.
* **Attachments**: Attach any relevant documents, such as certificates, diplomas, or letters of recommendation, to support your application.
* **Submission**: Once you have filled in all fields, submit your application for review. Please ensure that all required fields are completed to avoid rejection.

## Tips
* Ensure that all attachments are in a readable format and easily accessible.
* Please proofread your application carefully before submission to avoid any errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.
