# Nonprofit Fundraising Specialist Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nonprofit Fundraising Specialist Application Form is designed for individuals seeking to fill a fundraising position within non-profit organizations. This form will guide you through the necessary steps to submit your application, ensuring that all required information is accurately and comprehensively provided.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address.
2. Next, provide your Phone number for contact purposes.
3. Enter the name of the Non-Profit Organization you are applying to.
4. Select your current Job Title from the given options or choose 'Other' if not listed.
5. Enter your Years of Experience and Salary Requirements.
6. Briefly describe your Job Duties and the date you are Available to Start.
7. Select your preferred Work Location and type of work you are looking for (full-time/part-time).
8. Enter the address of your Non-Profit Organization.
9. Provide information on your Non-Profit's City, State, and Zip Code.
10. Specify your Non-Profit's Country.
11. Upload any required documents (e.g., CV, Cover Letter, Resume).
12. Add any Additional Comments regarding your application.
13. Finally, confirm that you have uploaded the necessary documents and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification or documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification or documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you regularly check for notifications.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Non-Profit Name**: Enter the name of the non-profit organization you are applying to.
* **Job Title**: Select your current job title from the options provided or choose 'Other' if not listed.
* **Years of Experience**: Enter the number of years you have experience in fundraising or a related field.
* **Salary Requirements**: Enter your expected salary range for the position.
* **Job Duties**: Briefly describe your current or past job duties related to fundraising.
* **Available to Start**: Enter the date you are available to start work.
* **Available Date**: Enter the date you are available to start in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Work Location**: Select your preferred work location from the options provided.
* **Work Type**: Select whether you are looking for full-time or part-time work.
* **Non-Profit Address**: Enter the address of your non-profit organization.
* **Non-Profit City**: Enter the city of your non-profit organization.
* **Non-Profit State**: Enter the state of your non-profit organization.
* **Non-Profit Zip**: Enter the zip code of your non-profit organization.
* **Non-Profit Country**: Enter the country of your non-profit organization.
* **Uploaded Documents**: Upload any necessary documents such as a CV, Cover Letter, or Resume.
* **Uploaded CV**: Upload your Curriculum Vitae (CV) if applicable.
* **Uploaded Cover Letter**: Upload a Cover Letter if applicable.
* **Uploaded Resume**: Upload a Resume if applicable.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about your application.
* **Submit**: Review and confirm that the information provided is accurate, that you understand the uploaded documents may be reviewed by multiple people, and that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Confirm Upload**: Confirm that you have uploaded the required documents.

Note: This help guide is based on the provided YAML fields and explanations. Please ensure to review and confirm that all information is up-to-date and accurate before proceeding with the application process.
