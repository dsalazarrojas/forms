# City Wide Chapter Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The City Wide Chapter Registration form is used to register local chapters or groups with the organization. This form collects information about your chapter/group, including chapter name, parent organization, contact person, and other relevant details. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide the required information for each field.
3. Review your submission before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Chapter name** (Chapter name, text, required): Enter the name of your local chapter or group.
* **Parent organization** (Parent organization, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the parent organization your chapter is affiliated with.
* **Contact person** (Contact person, text, required): Enter the name of the person who will be the primary contact for your chapter.
* **Contact email** (Contact email, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person for onboarding communications.
* **Number of members** (Number of members, number, required): Estimate the number of members in your chapter.
* **City areas served** (City areas served, text, optional): List the neighborhoods or wards served by your chapter.
* **Chapter goals and activities** (Chapter goals and activities, text, optional): Describe the key planned activities of your chapter.
* **Compliance with organization policies** (Compliance with organization policies, select_one, required): Select "Agree" to confirm compliance with the organization's policies.
* **Support needed from central office** (Support needed from central office, text, optional): If necessary, list any support needed from the central office for your chapter.
* **Submission date** (Submission date, date, required): Use the current date for the submission date.

## Tips
- Please review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.
- If you need help or have questions, contact the organization for assistance.
- This form is for local chapter or group registration only; other types of registration may require additional or different forms.
