# Volunteer Exit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Exit Survey is a form designed to collect information from volunteers who are leaving the organization. This survey aims to understand the reasons behind their departure and gather feedback to improve the volunteer experience in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have the necessary permissions to complete it.
2. Select the reason for leaving from the options provided (if applicable).
3. If you have any specific areas of improvement, type them in the "Area of Improvement" field.
4. If you have any comments or feedback, type them in the "Comments" field.
5. Enter your email address and phone number (if applicable).
6. Select your date and time of departure.
7. Review your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Exit Survey** (`text`, required/optional): This is the title of the form, but you don't need to type anything here.
* **Reason for Leaving** (`select_one`, required/optional): Check the box to indicate if you are leaving due to any of the reasons provided. If you choose "Yes", you will be able to select one or more reasons.
* **Area of Improvement** (`text`, required/optional): If you have any specific areas where the organization could improve, please type them here.
* **Open Endorsement** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Check the boxes to indicate which open areas you would like to endorse as part of the volunteer experience.
* **Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with any follow-up questions or to say thank you for your feedback.
* **Phone Number** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select one of the provided phone numbers to enter your contact number.
* **Date of Departure** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date you will be leaving the organization.
* **Time of Departure** (`time`, required/optional): Enter the time you will be leaving the organization.
* **Comments** (`text`, required/optional): Type any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience as a volunteer.
