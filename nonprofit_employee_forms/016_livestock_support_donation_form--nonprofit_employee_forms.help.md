# Livestock Support Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Livestock Support Donation Form is designed to collect information from donors who wish to make a donation to support livestock-related causes or initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Provide your donor information in the "Donor Information" section.
2. Enter the amount of your donation in the "Donation Amount" field.
3. Describe the purpose of your donation in the "Purpose Of Donation" field.
4. Select a contact method from the "Contact Method" dropdown menu.
5. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field, if applicable.
6. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field, if applicable.
7. Optionally, provide any additional comments or message in the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Information (Donor Information)** (`donor_info`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your name and contact information, such as your name, title, and/or organization.
* **Donation Amount (donation_amount**, number, optional): This field is where you can enter the amount of your donation. Please note that this form does not support fractional values or negative numbers.
* **Purpose Of Donation (purpose_of_donation**, text, optional): This field is where you can describe the specific reason or purpose behind your donation.
* **Contact Method (contact_method, select_one, optional): Select how you would like to be contacted for follow-up or communication. You can choose from one of the options provided in the dropdown menu.
* **Contact Phone (contact_phone, text, optional): If you selected "Phone" as your contact method, enter your contact phone number here.
* **Contact Email (contact_email, email, optional): If you selected "Email" as your contact method, enter your contact email address here.
* **Message (message, note, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional comments or messages to accompany your donation.
