# Volunteer Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Verification Form is a tool used to collect accurate and comprehensive information from volunteers about their employment status, past experiences, and personal details. This form is essential for verifying a volunteer's employment status with their current or past employers, ensuring that we have a clear understanding of their work history and can effectively manage our volunteer program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your **Email** address and **Phone** number for contact purposes.
3. Select your **Job Title** and provide the **Start Date** and **End Date** of your employment with the organisation.
4. Enter the **Supervisor Name**, **Supervisor Email**, and **Supervisor Phone** numbers for verification purposes.
5. Provide a brief explanation for **Why did you leave this organisation?**.
6. Select the **Organisation** you were or are employed with from the dropdown menu.
7. Enter the **Organisation Name** (if different from the selected organisation).
8. Provide your **Position in organisation**.
9. Enter your **Reference** (if any).
10. Select the **Organisation Reference** (if required).
11. Choose the **Organisation Type** from the dropdown menu.
12. If your organisation type is not listed, provide an alternative in the **Other** field.
13. If employed, select **Yes** or **No** to confirm your employment status in the **Are you employed?** field.
14. If employed, enter the name of your employer in the **Employed by** field.
15. Enter the number of **Hours Worked** (if applicable).
16. Confirm that you **Agree** to the terms of use.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title during your employment with the organisation.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you started working with the organisation.
* **End Date**: Enter the date you ended your employment with the organisation.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of your supervisor during your employment with the organisation.
* **Supervisor Email**: Enter the email of your supervisor during your employment with the organisation.
* **Supervisor Phone**: Enter the phone number of your supervisor during your employment with the organisation.
* **Supervisor Job Title**: Enter the job title of your supervisor during your employment with the organisation.
* **Why did you leave this organisation?**: Provide a brief explanation for your departure from the organisation.
* **Organisation**: Select the organisation you were or are employed with from the dropdown menu.
* **Organisation Name**: Enter the name of the organisation (if different from the selected organisation).
* **Position in organisation**: Provide your position or role within the organisation.
* **Reference**: Enter any reference number or code (if required).
* **Organisation Reference**: Select the reference (if required).
* **Organisation Type**: Choose the type of organisation from the dropdown menu.
* **Other**: If your organisation type is not listed, provide an alternative in this field.
* **Are you employed?**: Select **Yes** or **No** to confirm your employment status.
* **Employed by**: Enter the name of your employer (if employed).
* **Hours Worked**: Enter the number of hours you worked during your employment (if applicable).
* **Employed by other**: Enter the name of your employer if different from the organisation selected.
* **I agree to the terms of use**: Select **Agree** to confirm that you have read and understood the terms of use.
