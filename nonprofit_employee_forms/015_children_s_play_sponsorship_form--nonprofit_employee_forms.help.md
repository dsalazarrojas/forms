# Children Play Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Children Play Sponsorship Form is a document used by a non-profit organization to gather information from sponsors or organizations that wish to support children's activities. The form is crucial in ensuring that sponsors are aware of the terms and conditions of their sponsorship and agree to the terms of the sponsorship.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the requested information.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it to the non-profit organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Sponsor Name (1)
* **Sponsor Name** (`sponsor_name`, text, required): Provide the individual or organization name that is sponsoring the children's activities.

### Sponsor Email (2)
* **Sponsor Email** (`sponsor_email`, email, required): Enter the contact email for sponsorship correspondence. This will be used by the non-profit organization to communicate with the sponsor.

### Sponsorship Level (3)
* **Sponsorship Level** (`sponsorship_level`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following sponsorship tiers:
	+ Bronze
	+ Silver
	+ Gold
	+ Platinum

### Amount Committed (4)
* **Amount Committed** (`amount_committed`, number, required): Enter the monetary value of the sponsorship.

### Billing Address (5)
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, optional): Provide the address for invoicing purposes. This is optional, but it helps the non-profit organization to send the sponsor invoices or receipts.

### Sponsor Logo Provided (6)
* **Sponsor Logo Provided** (`logo_provided`, select_one, optional): Indicate if a logo file will be provided. This can be useful if the sponsor wants to be recognized by the non-profit organization.

### Recognition Preferences (7)
* **Recognition Preferences** (`recognition_preferences`, text, optional): Specify how the sponsor wishes to be credited. This is a free text field where the sponsor can provide any additional information.

### Contact Phone (8)
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number for sponsorship coordination. This will be used by the non-profit organization to contact the sponsor.

### Agree to Sponsorship Terms (9)
* **Agree to Sponsorship Terms** (`agree_terms`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the sponsorship. Choose "I agree" to proceed with the sponsorship.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form carefully and accurately to avoid delays in processing your sponsorship. 
- Review the terms and conditions of the sponsorship carefully before agreeing to them. 
- Contact the non-profit organization if you have any questions or concerns about the form or sponsorship.
