# small_business_fund_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit a request for a small business grant or funding from a non-profit organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields and provide as much information as possible in the optional fields.
2. Review your submission carefully before submitting.
3. Submit the form by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Information** (`donor_info`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name and contact information.
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it should appear in our records.
* **Donor Email** (`donor_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your business or organization email address.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your small business name.
* **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your business phone number.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the amount of the donation you are requesting.
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select the frequency of your donation (e.g. Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly).
* **Gift Frequency** (`gift_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select the frequency of your gift (e.g. Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly).
* **Gift Note** (`gift_note`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes about your gift.
* **Donor Address** (`donor_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your business or organization address.
* **Gift Amount** (`gift_amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the amount of your gift.
* **Gift Frequency Note** (`gift_frequency_note`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional notes about your gift frequency.
* **Donor Notes** (`donor_notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes about you as a donor.
* **Business Notes** (`business_notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes about your business.
* **Donation Frequency Note** (`donation_frequency_note`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional notes about your donation frequency.
* **Business Info** (`business_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about your business.
* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments about your form submission.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required: false): This field is duplicated. Please ignore.
* **Submission Note** (`submission_note`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments about your submission.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select the status of your submission (e.g. Draft, Awaiting Review, Approved, Rejected).
* **Submission Status Note** (`submission_status_note`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments about your submission status.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it should appear on our records.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, `time`, required: false): This field is duplicated. Please ignore.

Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out completely.
* Double-check your submission for any errors before clicking the "Submit" button.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
