# Refugee Childcare Worker Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Refugee Childcare Worker Application Form is designed to collect personal and professional information from applicants to help our organization assess their qualifications and suitability for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review each section carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
* Enter your information in the corresponding fields, using the format requested (e.g., text, select multiple, or email).
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
* Submit the form once you have completed all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Experience** (`experience`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe your relevant work experience, including any previous roles or positions held.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the times of the year you are available to work, using the options:
	+ Option 1: Monday to Friday (morning/afternoon shifts)
	+ Option 2: Weekends and holidays
	+ Option 3: Any time, flexible schedule
* **Language Speaking Languages** (`language_speaking_languages`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the languages you speak fluently, including:
	+ English
	+ Spanish
	+ French
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select your consent for background checks and other screenings.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
* We appreciate your time and consideration in completing this form.
