# Volunteer Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from volunteers who are leaving the organization. It is essential to have accurate and up-to-date information about each volunteer's status to maintain a precise record and facilitate a smooth transition.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the First Name field with your legal name as it appears on your identification.
2. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have been a prophet for the organization.
3. Enter your job title as it appears on your organizational records.
4. Provide a reason for leaving, if any. This will help us understand your decision and help us improve our services.
5. Enter the date of your last day of service with the organization.
6. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are affiliated with other organizations. If you are, please specify in the "Other Organizations" field.
7. Select the organizations you are affiliated with, if any.
8. Indicate if you have any terminologies or agreements with the organization. If you do, please specify in the "Other Term" field.
9. Select the terminologies or agreements you have with the organization, if any.
10. Indicate if you have any skills or qualifications that are relevant to your volunteer work. If you do, please specify in the "Other Skills" field.
11. List any other skills or qualifications you may have.
12. If you are affiliated with other organizations, please list them in the "Other Organizations" field.
13. Specify the date of termination of your affiliation with the organization.
14. Take note of any additional comments or details about your termination.
