# Donor Information Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Information Intake Form is designed to collect contact information and donation details from individuals who wish to contribute to the nonprofit organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your last name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your contact number in the Contact Number field.
5. Enter the amount of the gift in the Gift Amount field.
6. Select the date of the donation in the Donation Date field.
7. Provide any additional comments or notes in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (1)**: This is the first name of the donor. It is a text field where you can enter up to 32 characters.
* **Last Name (2)**: This is the last name of the donor. It is a text field where you can enter up to 32 characters.
* **Email (3)**: This is the email address of the donor. It is an email field where you can enter a valid email address.
* **Contact Number (4)**: This is the contact number of the donor. It is a text field where you can enter up to 16 characters.
* **Gift Amount (5)**: This is the amount of the gift made by the donor. It is a numeric field where you can enter a whole number.
* **Donation Date (6)**: This is the date of the donation. It is a date field where you can select a date using the calendar widget.
* **Notes (7)**: This is an optional field for any additional comments or notes from the donor. It is a text field with no character limit.
