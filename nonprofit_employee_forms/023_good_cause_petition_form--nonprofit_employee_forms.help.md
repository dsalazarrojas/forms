# Good Cause Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from employees of non-profit organizations. It's meant to gather details about their role, feedback, and ideas related to their work within the organization. The form helps in creating an internal database of employee contributions and feedback.

2.  **Description** (Page 1)

4.  **Reason** (Page 1)

9.  **Submission Time** (Page 1)
