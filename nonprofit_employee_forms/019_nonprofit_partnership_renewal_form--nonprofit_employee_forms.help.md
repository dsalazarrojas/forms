# Nonprofit Partnership Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nonprofit Partnership Renewal Form is designed to help non-profit organizations and their partners track and renew their existing partnerships. This form collects essential information about non-profit organizations, their partners, and the partnership itself, ensuring that all necessary details are up-to-date and easily accessible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the Nonprofit Name and Address.
2.  Enter the Partner Name and Address.
3.  Select the Nonprofit Type (Nonprofit Organization, Foundation, or Other).
4.  Select the Partnership Type (Full Partnership, Limited Partnership, or Other).
5.  Enter the Renewal Date.
6.  Enter the Partnership Start Date.
7.  Enter the Partnership End Date (if applicable).
8.  Select the Status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
9.  Optionally, enter the Partner Contact Information (Email, Phone, and Partner Title).
10. Enter any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Nonprofit Name** (`non_profit_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the non-profit organization.
*   **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the non-profit organization's address.
*   **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person for the non-profit organization.
*   **Email** (`email`, text, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
*   **Date Renewed** (`date_renewed`, date, required): Enter the date the partnership was last renewed.
*   **Date Reviewed** (`date_reviewed`, date, optional): Enter the date the partnership was last reviewed.
*   **Status** (`status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the partnership (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
*   **Nonprofit Type** (`non_profit_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of non-profit organization (Nonprofit Organization, Foundation, or Other).
*   **Nonprofit Category** (`non_profit_category`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the non-profit category.
*   **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the partner organization.
*   **Partner Title** (`partner_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the partner contact person.
*   **Partner Email** (`partner_email`, text, optional): Enter the email address of the partner contact person.
*   **Partner Phone** (`partner_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the partner contact person.
*   **Renewal Date** (`renewal_date`, date, required): Enter the date the partnership will be renewed.
*   **Partner Website** (`partner_website`, text, optional): Enter the website of the partner organization.
*   **Partnership Type** (`partnership_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of partnership (Full Partnership, Limited Partnership, or Other).
*   **Partnership Start Date** (`partnership_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the partnership.
*   **Partnership End Date** (`partnership_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the partnership (if applicable).
*   **Nonprofit Website** (`non_profit_website`, text, optional): Enter the website of the non-profit organization.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the partnership.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the partnership.
*   **Nonprofit Address** (`non_profit_address`, text, required): Enter the non-profit organization's address.
*   **Nonprofit Additional Address** (`non_profit_address_two`, text, optional): Enter any additional addresses for the non-profit organization.
*   **Partnership Renewal Date** (`renewal_date`, date, required): Enter the date the partnership will be renewed.
