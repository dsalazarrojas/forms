# DSWD Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The DSWD Form Template is used to record and track services provided to clients by non-profit organizations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Service Type** from the list provided. This will help identify the type of service provided to the client.
2. Enter the **Case Number** of the client, if applicable.
3. Enter the **Case Worker** information, if applicable.
4. Enter the **Social Services Provider** information, if applicable.
5. Enter the **Client Name** in the field provided.
6. Enter the **Date of Service**, which is the date the service was provided.
7. Enter the **Time of Service**, which is the time the service was provided.
8. Enter the **Location of Service**, which is the location where the service was provided.
9. Enter any additional **Notes** about the service provided.
10. If available, enter the **Email** and **Phone** of the client.
11. If available, enter the **Address** of the client.
12. If available, enter the **City** of the client.
13. If available, enter the **State** of the client.
14. If available, enter the **Zip** of the client.
15. If available, enter the **Case Manager** information, including their email and phone number.
16. If available, enter the **Service Provider** information, including their email and phone number.
17. Finally, select the **Case Review** status and enter any additional details in the **Other Service** field, if applicable.
