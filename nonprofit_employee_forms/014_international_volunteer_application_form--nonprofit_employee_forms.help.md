# International Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from volunteers who are interested in participating in our non-profit activities. Please fill out this form completely and accurately to help us better understand your background, skills, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select "Next" to begin filling out the form.
2. Fill out the form fields with the required information. Some fields are marked as "required", which means they are necessary for us to process your application.
3. If you need help with any field, you can use the hints provided to guide you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it so we can contact you.
* **Country** (Select One, Required): Select your country of origin.
* **Role** (Select Multiple, Required): Please select the role you wish to volunteer for (e.g., Volunteer, Staff, Director).
* **Availability** (Date and Time, Required): Please select your availability for volunteering.
* **Language** (Select Multiple, Optional): If you are fluent in more than one language, please select them.
* **Skills** (Text, Optional): If you have specific skills or experience, please describe them.
* **Experience** (Number, Optional): If you have previous experience in a related field, please enter the number of years.
* **Resume Link** (Text, Optional): If you have a resume or CV, please upload or link to it.
* **Portfolio Link** (Text, Optional): If you have a portfolio of your work, please upload or link to it.
* **Background Check** (Select One, Required): Please indicate if you are willing to undergo a background check.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Text, Optional): If you have an emergency contact person, please enter their name.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Text, Optional): If you have an emergency contact person, please describe their relationship to you.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Text, Optional): If you have an emergency contact person, please enter their phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (Email, Optional): If you have an emergency contact person, please enter their email address.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Extension** (Text, Optional): If you have an emergency contact person, please enter their phone extension (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Extension** (Text, Optional): If you have an emergency contact person, please describe their relationship to you (if not specified).
* **Emergency Contact Email Extension** (Text, Optional): If you have an emergency contact person, please enter their email address (if not specified).
* **Volunteer Start Date** (Date, Optional): If you have a preferred start date for volunteering, please enter it.
* **Volunteer End Date** (Date, Optional): If you have a preferred end date for volunteering, please enter it.
* **Volunteer Status** (Select One, Required): Please select your current volunteer status (e.g., Active, Inactive).
* **Volunteer Notes** (Note, Optional): If you have any additional information about your volunteering experience, please enter it.
* **Submitted By** (Text, Required): Please enter your name as submitted by.

Note: Some fields may not be applicable to you, and you can leave them blank.
