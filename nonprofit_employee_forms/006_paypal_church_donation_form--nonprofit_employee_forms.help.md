# paypal_church_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The paypal_church_donation_form is used to collect information from users when making a donation to a church through the PayPal system. This form is used to record and track donation data for the church's financial records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the form to access the fields.
2. Fill out each field with the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Info** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ This field is for any additional information regarding the donation.
* **Amount** (`2`, `number`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter the amount of the donation.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`4`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Submit** (`5`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ Click this button to submit the form and complete the donation process.
* **Error Message** (`6`, `note`, required: `false`):
	+ This field is not intended for user input, but rather is used to display error messages if any issues occur during the form submission process.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are authorized to make a donation on behalf of yourself or your organization.
* If you encounter any issues while filling out the form, please check the "Error Message" field for more information.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact the church administration for assistance.
