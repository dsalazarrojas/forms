# Volunteer Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from volunteers about their activities and engagement with your organization. This survey aims to understand the volunteer's experience, satisfaction, and feedback on their involvement. It helps us to identify areas of improvement and better support our volunteers in their endeavors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, please follow these steps:

1. Begin on the first page, where you will read the introduction to the survey. This page provides background information and context for the survey questions.
2. On the second page, please provide your volunteer information, such as your name and contact details. Please fill out this section completely and accurately.
3. On the third page, select your volunteer status from the list provided (Active or Inactive).
4. If you have volunteered with us before, please provide information about the number of hours you spent volunteering with our organization on the fourth page.
5. On the fifth page, report your volunteer frequency, which is the number of times you have volunteered with us in the past year.
6. On the sixth page, select your answer to the feedback question. Please choose 'Yes' if you have had a positive experience, and 'No' if you have not.
7. On the seventh page, provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about your experience as a volunteer with our organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, required: no): This is the introduction page that provides background information and context for the survey questions.
* **Volunteer Information** (`volunteer_information`, `number`, required: no): Please fill out your name, email address, and other relevant contact details.
* **Volunteer Status** (`volunteer_status`, `select_one`, required: no): Indicate your current volunteer status (Active or Inactive).
* **Volunteer Hours** (`volunteer_hours`, `number`, required: no): If you have volunteered with us before, please report the number of hours you have spent volunteering with our organization.
* **Volunteer Frequency** (`volunteer_frequency`, `number`, required: no): Report the number of times you have volunteered with us in the past year.
* **Volunteer Feedback** (`volunteer_feedback`, `select_multiple`, required: no): Select your answer to the feedback question. Please choose 'Yes' if you have had a positive experience, and 'No' if you have not.
* **Additional Comments** (`volunteer_comments`, `text`, required: no): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about your experience as a volunteer with our organization.
