# Alumni Award Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Alumni Award Nomination Form is used to nominate individuals for recognition and distinction within the university community. It's essential to ensure the information is accurate and complete to avoid duplication and misrepresentation. Please review the fields below to ensure you've considered all relevant aspects of the nominee's qualifications before submitting.

## How To Complete This Form

This form has multiple sections and fields that require information from both the nominator and the nominee. Please follow these steps:

1.  **Nominee Information**: Provide the full name of the person being nominated. This is a required field as it's crucial for identifying the nominee accurately.
2.  **Nominee Email**: If you know the nominee's email, please provide it. This helps us contact the nominee with any updates or requests for additional information.
3.  **Your Information**: Enter your full name as the nominator. This is a required field to ensure we can contact you with any updates or questions.
4.  **Relationship to Nominee**: Describe your relationship to the nominee, e.g., colleague, classmate, mentor, etc.
5.  **Award Category**: Choose the most suitable category for the nomination, such as Distinguished Alumni, Young Alumni, or Community Service.
6.  **Reason for Nomination**: Describe the nominee's achievements and the impact they've made. This section helps us understand their qualifications and accomplishments.
7.  **Supporting Documents**: If available, provide a link to any supporting documents like resumes or references. This can help us assess the nominee's qualifications.
8.  **Confirmation**: Confirm that the provided information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nominee name**: This is a required field where you'll provide the full name of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee email**: If known, enter the nominee's email address to help us contact them.
* **Nominator name**: This is a required field where you'll enter your full name as the nominator.
* **Relationship to nominee**: Describe your connection to the person being nominated (e.g., colleague, classmate, etc.).
* **Award category**: Choose the most fitting category for the nominee's achievements. Options include Distinguished Alumni, Young Alumni, and Community Service.
* **Reason for nomination**: Describe the nominee's achievements and the impact they've made.
* **Supporting documents**: If available, provide a link to any supporting documents like resumes or references.
* **Consent**: Confirm that the provided information is accurate by checking this box.

Note: The form requires confirmation to ensure the accuracy and authenticity of the submitted information.
