# Alumni Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Alumni Engagement Survey is a form designed to gather information from alumni about their engagement with the institution. This form will help us understand your level of interest in attending events, participating in volunteering, and provide feedback on our services. The collected data will help us improve the experience of alumni by getting your suggestions and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each section carefully and fill in the required fields. You can skip sections that are not applicable to you. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents. (Required)

### Last Name
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents. (Required)

### Email Address
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address that we can use to communicate with you. (Required)

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached during working hours. (Optional)

### Graduation Year
* **Graduation Year**: Enter the year you graduated from the institution. (Required)

### Degree Obtained
* **Degree Obtained**: Select the type of degree you obtained from the institution. (Required)
    - Associate Degree
    - Bachelor's Degree
    - Master's Degree
    - Doctorate
    - Certificate Program
    - Other

### Major/Field of Study
* **Major/Field of Study**: Enter the major or field of study you were pursuing when you were a student. (Required)

### How often do you attend alumni events
* **How often do you attend alumni events**: Indicate the frequency of your attendance to alumni events.
    - Never
    - Rarely (once a year or less)
    - Occasionally (2-3 times a year)
    - Frequently (4-6 times a year)
    - Very Often (more than 6 times a year)

### Which types of events interest you most
* **Which types of events interest you most**: Select all the types of events that interest you. 
    - Networking Events
    - Homecoming
    - Professional Development Workshops
    - Sports Events
    - Cultural Performances
    - Reunions
    - Volunteer Opportunities
    - Webinars

### Have you made donations to the institution
* **Have you made donations to the institution**: Indicate your donation history with the institution. 
    - Yes - Regularly
    - Yes - Occasionally
    - No - Never
    - Prefer not to say

### Are you interested in volunteering
* **Are you interested in volunteering**: Indicate your interest in volunteering with the institution. 
    - Yes - Very Interested
    - Maybe - Tell me more
    - No - Not at this time

### Would you be willing to mentor current students
* **Would you be willing to mentor current students**: Indicate your willingness to mentor current students. 
    - Yes - Definitely
    - Maybe - Depends on time commitment
    - No - Not able to

### Current Employment Status
* **Current Employment Status**: Indicate your current employment status. 
    - Employed Full-time
    - Employed Part-time
    - Self-employed
    - Unemployed - Seeking
    - Unemployed - Not Seeking
    - Retired
    - Student

### Employer Name
* **Employer Name**: Enter the name of your current or past employer. (Optional)

### Job Title
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title at your current or past employer. (Optional)

### Industry
* **Industry**: Select the industry you are currently or previously working in. 
    - Technology
    - Healthcare
    - Education
    - Finance
    - Manufacturing
    - Retail
    - Government
    - Nonprofit
    - Other

### How would you prefer to receive communications
* **How would you prefer to receive communications**: Select the methods you would prefer to receive communications from the institution. 
    - Email
    - Phone Calls
    - Text Messages
    - Social Media
    - Postal Mail
    - Mobile App Notifications

### How satisfied are you with alumni services
* **How satisfied are you with alumni services**: Indicate your level of satisfaction with our alumni services. 
    - 1 - Very Dissatisfied
    - 2 - Dissatisfied
    - 3 - Neutral
    - 4 - Satisfied
    - 5 - Very Satisfied

### What improvements would you suggest for alumni programs
* **What improvements would you suggest for alumni programs**: Provide any suggestions or feedback for us to improve our services. (Optional)

### Additional Comments or Questions
* **Additional Comments or Questions**: If you have any other comments or questions, please enter them here. (Optional)

## Tips
* Please answer all the required fields to help us get a clear picture of your engagement with the institution.
* Be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
