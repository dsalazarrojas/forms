# corporate_social_responsibility_network_membership_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The corporate social responsibility network membership form is designed to collect information about employees and their companies, helping to build a network of CSR initiatives and promote collaboration among nonprofit employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select your role in the "Role" field from the dropdown options.
6. Enter your company name in the "Company" field.
7. Enter your department or team in the "Department" field.
8. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
9. Enter a brief description of your job in the "Job Description" field.
10. Enter your location in the "Location" field.
11. Select the type of your company (Public, Private, or Non Profit) in the "Company Type" field.
12. Enter your industry in the "Industry" field.
13. Enter your company website in the "Website" field.
14. Enter a description of your company in the "Description" field.
15. Select a date in the "Date" field (if necessary).
16. Select a time in the "Time" field (if necessary).
17. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Additional Comments" field.
18. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (Text, Required: false): Enter your given name.
- **Last Name** (Text, Required: false): Enter your surname.
- **Email** (Email, Required: false): Enter your email address.
- **Phone Number** (Text, Required: false): Enter your phone number.
- **Role** (Select Multiple, Required: true): Select one or more roles that apply to you, such as CEO/President, Director, Manager, or Employee.
- **Company** (Text, Required: false): Enter your company's name.
- **Department** (Text, Required: false): Enter your department or team.
- **Job Title** (Text, Required: false): Enter your job title.
- **Job Description** (Text, Required: false): Enter a brief description of your job.
- **Location** (Text, Required: false): Enter your location.
- **Company Type** (Select One, Required: true): Select the type of your company (Public, Private, or Non Profit).
- **Industry** (Text, Required: false): Enter your industry.
- **Website** (Text, Required: false): Enter your company's website.
- **Description** (Text, Required: false): Enter a description of your company.
- **Date** (Date, Required: false): Select a date (if necessary).
- **Time** (Time, Required: false): Select a time (if necessary).
- **Additional Comments** (Note, Required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes.
- **Confirm Email** (Email, Required: false): Confirm your email address.

Note: All fields are not required, but the "Role" and "Company Type" fields are required. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to help build a strong CSR network.
