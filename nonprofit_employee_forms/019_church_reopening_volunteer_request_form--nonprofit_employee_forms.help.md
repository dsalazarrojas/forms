# Church Reopening Volunteer Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Reopening Volunteer Request Form is a comprehensive guide for potential volunteers to provide their details and preferences for assisting with church activities. This form helps the church to assess the individual's suitability for various roles and ensure a smooth reopening process.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all the necessary information and answers ready before starting the form.
2.  Fill in your Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Home Address, and Date of Birth in the respective fields.
3.  Select your affiliation with the church: Yes, active member; Yes, but not active; No, but interested; or No, just helping.
4.  If applicable, select how long you have been a member of the church.
5.  Choose areas where you would like to volunteer, such as cleaning and sanitizing, ushering, registration, technical support, childcare, food service, outdoor setup, and more.
6.  Indicate your availability to volunteer, choosing from Sunday morning, Sunday evening, Wednesday evening, Saturday, weekdays, or flexible options.
7.  Select the number of hours you can volunteer per week, considering 1-2 hours, 3-5 hours, 6-10 hours, or more than 10 hours or as needed.
8.  Confirm your willingness to complete health screenings, temperature checks, and health questions.
9.  Choose whether you are comfortable wearing Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) such as masks, gloves, etc.
10.  Confirm if you can enforce social distancing measures.
11.  Indicate if you are in a high-risk health category according to CDC guidelines.
12.  Confirm if anyone in your household has been ill in the past 14 days.
13.  Select the type of training you need, if any.
14.  Describe any relevant volunteer experience you have.
15.  Confirm your willingness to complete background checks, if required.
16.  Indicate when you can start volunteering, selecting a date.
17.  Share your motivation for volunteering.
18.  Provide any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first and last name.
*   **Email Address** (2, email, required): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you.
*   **Phone Number** (3, text, required): Enter your best contact phone number.
*   **Home Address** (4, text, required): Enter your street, city, state, and zip.
*   **Date of Birth** (5, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
*   **Are You a Church Member** (6, select_one, required): Select your affiliation with the church:
    *   Yes, active member (if you are an active member of the church).
    *   Yes, but not active (if you are a member but not actively involved).
    *   No, but interested (if you are not a member but interested in volunteering).
    *   No, just helping (if you are not a member but willing to help).
*   **How Long Have You Been a Member** (7, select_one, optional): If you are a member, select how long you have been a member:
    *   Less than 1 year.
    *   1-3 years.
    *   3-5 years.
    *   5-10 years.
    *   More than 10 years.
    *   Not a member.
*   **Which Areas Would You Like to Help** (8, select_multiple, required): Select all areas where you would like to volunteer:
    *   Cleaning and Sanitizing.
    *   Ushering.
    *   Registration/Check-in.
    *   Technical Support.
    *   Childcare.
    *   Food Service.
    *   Outdoor Setup.
    *   Prayer Ministry.
    *   Hospitality.
*   **When Are You Available** (9, select_multiple, required): Indicate when you are available to volunteer:
    *   Sunday morning.
    *   Sunday evening.
    *   Wednesday evening.
    *   Saturday.
    *   Weekdays.
    *   Flexible.
*   **How Many Hours Per Week Can You Volunteer** (10, select_one, required): Select the number of hours you can volunteer per week:
    *   1-2 hours.
    *   3-5 hours.
    *   6-10 hours.
    *   More than 10 hours.
    *   As needed.
*   **Are You Willing to Complete Health Screening** (11, select_one, required): Confirm your willingness to complete health screenings:
    *   True.
    *   False.
*   **Are You Comfortable Wearing PPE** (12, select_one, required): Confirm your comfort with wearing Personal Protective Equipment:
    *   Yes, always.
    *   Yes, when required.
    *   No, cannot wear PPE.
*   **Can You Help Enforce Social Distancing** (13, select_one, required): Confirm your ability to enforce social distancing measures:
    *   True.
    *   False.
*   **Are You in a High-Risk Health Category** (14, select_one, required): Indicate if you are in a high-risk health category:
    *   True.
    *   False.
    *   Prefer not to say.
*   **Has Anyone in Your Household Been Ill** (15, select_one, required): Confirm if anyone in your household has been ill:
    *   True.
    *   False.
    *   Not applicable.
*   **Training Needed** (16, select_multiple, required): Select the type of training you may require:
    *   Safety protocols.
    *   Cleaning procedures.
    *   Customer service.
    *   Technical training.
    *   Childcare protocols.
    *   No training needed.
*   **Do You Have Relevant Volunteer Experience** (17, text, optional): Describe any relevant volunteer experience.
*   **Are You Willing to Complete Background Check** (18, select_one, required): Confirm your willingness to complete background checks:
    *   True.
    *   False.
*   **When Can You Start Volunteering** (19, date, required): Select the date you can start volunteering.
*   **Why Do You Want to Volunteer** (20, text, required): Share your motivation for volunteering.
*   **Any Additional Comments or Questions** (21, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or questions you may have.
