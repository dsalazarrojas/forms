# Donor Thank You Letter Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Thank You Letter Request Form is designed to collect information from donors to create a personalized thank you letter. This letter is a way to express gratitude to donors for their support and contributions to your organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information, which includes the Doner Name, Request Date, and Request Type.
2. If you want to receive the letter via email or mail, select the desired delivery method.
3. Enter the delivery address, city, state, zip, and country if you choose to receive the letter via mail.
4. Optionally, you can also add a message to be included in the letter and select the type of letter you would like to receive (appreciation, milestone, or birthday).
5. If you have an email or phone number for the donor, you can include it in case we need to contact them.
6. Finally, add any additional message for the donor, such as a special occasion or reason for the donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Doner Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the donor who made the donation.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the donation was made.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you would like to receive the letter (email or mail).
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address where the letter should be delivered if you choose mail.
* **Delivery City** (`delivery_city`, `text`, optional): Enter the city of the delivery address.
* **Delivery State** (`delivery_state`, `text`, optional): Enter the state of the delivery address.
* **Delivery Zip** (`delivery_zip`, `text`, optional): Enter the zip code of the delivery address.
* **Delivery Country** (`delivery_country`, `text`, optional): Enter the country of the delivery address.
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, optional): Enter a message to be included in the letter.
* **Request Type** (`request_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the type of letter you would like to receive (appreciation, milestone, or birthday).
* **Doner Email** (`doner_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the donor.
* **Doner Phone** (`doner_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the donor.
* **Doner Message** (`doner_message`, `text`, optional): Enter a message for the donor, such as a special occasion or reason for the donation.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure the letter is generated correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us and we will be happy to assist.
