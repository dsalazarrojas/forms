# Produce Prescription Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Produce Prescription Program aims to provide fresh produce to individuals in need, while also gathering information about their medical conditions and produce requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Date of Birth
	* Phone Number
	* Email Address
2. Provide your medical history and produce requirements:
	* Select the produce items you need (Broccoli, Carrots, Cucumber, Kale, Mixed Greens)
	* Enter the quantity of each produce item you require
3. Enter your prescription details:
	* Prescription Start Date
	* Prescription End Date
	* Physician Name
	* Physician Contact
	* Physician Phone
4. Enter your clinic details:
	* Clinic Name
	* Clinic Address
	* Clinic Phone
	* Clinic Email
5. Confirm your patient information:
	* Patient Name
	* Patient Age
	* Patient Address
	* Medical Conditions
6. Confirm your consent and provide any additional comments:
	* Consent
	* Comments
7. Sign the form to confirm your submission

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (3, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Phone Number** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email Address** (5, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Medical History** (6, text, required): Describe your medical conditions and history.
* **Produce Item** (7, select_multiple, required): Select the produce items you require (Broccoli, Carrots, Cucumber, Kale, Mixed Greens).
* **Quantity** (8, number, required): Enter the quantity of each produce item you require.
* **Prescription Start Date** (9, date, required): Enter the start date of your prescription.
* **Prescription End Date** (10, date, required): Enter the end date of your prescription.
* **Physician Name** (11, text, required): Enter the name of your physician.
* **Physician Contact** (12, text, required): Enter your physician's contact information.
* **Physician Phone** (13, text, required): Enter your physician's phone number.
* **Clinic Name** (14, text, required): Enter the name of your clinic.
* **Clinic Address** (15, text, required): Enter the address of your clinic.
* **Clinic Phone** (16, text, required): Enter the phone number of your clinic.
* **Clinic Email** (17, email, required): Enter the email address of your clinic.
* **Patient Name** (18, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Patient Age** (19, number, required): Enter your age.
* **Patient Address** (20, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Medical Conditions** (21, text, required): Describe your medical conditions.
* **Consent** (22, text, required): Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions of the program.
* **Signature** (23, note, required): Sign the form to confirm your submission.
* **Patient's Date of Birth** (24, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Clinic Phone** (25, text, required): Enter your clinic's phone number.
* **Assigned User** (26, text, required): Enter the name of the assigned user.
* **Assigned User Phone** (27, text, required): Enter the phone number of the assigned user.
* **Assigned User Email** (28, email, required): Enter the email address of the assigned user.
* **Comments** (29, text, required): Enter any additional comments or information about your prescription.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information about your medical conditions and produce requirements.
* Select the correct produce items and quantities you need.
* Enter your prescription dates accurately.
* Double-check your contact and address information.
* Sign the form to confirm your submission.
