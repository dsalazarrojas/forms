# NGO Data Sharing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The NGO Data Sharing Request Form is used to collect information from requestors (individuals or organizations) who are requesting data from the sharing organization. The form helps to ensure that the requestors' needs and agreements are properly documented and recorded, and that the sharing organization's terms and conditions are met.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the purpose of your data request in the "Purpose" field.
2. Describe the intended usage of the data in the "Usage" field.
3. Check the "Policy Agreement" field to acknowledge that you have reviewed and agreed to the sharing organization's policies.
4. Provide your name, email, phone number, and organization in the "Requestor Name", "Requestor Email", "Requestor Phone", and "Requestor Organization" fields.
5. Specify your position within your organization in the "Requestor Position" field.
6. Enter your date of birth in the "Requestor Date of Birth" field.
7. Identify the organization requesting data in the "Sharing Organization" field.
8. Specify the department or contact person within the sharing organization in the "Sharing Department" field.
9. Provide the email and phone number of the contact person in the "Sharing Contact Email" and "Sharing Contact Phone" fields.
10. Select the purpose for which the data will be used (Non Profit, Business, Other, Government, Academic, or Personal) in the "Shared For" field.
11. Enter the date the data request was made in the "Data Request Date" field.
12. Check the "Requestor Agreement" field to acknowledge that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms and conditions.
13. Check the "Sharing Agreement" field to acknowledge that you have reviewed and agreed to the sharing organization's terms and conditions.
14. Check the "Terms and Conditions" field to acknowledge that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms and conditions.
15. Finally, sign and date the "Requestor Signature" field to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Purpose**: A brief description of the purpose of your data request.
* **Usage**: A description of how you intend to use the data.
* **Policy Agreement**: A check box indicating that you have reviewed and agreed to the sharing organization's policies.
* **Requestor Name**: Your name as the individual or representative of the organization requesting data.
* **Requestor Email**: Your email address.
* **Requestor Phone**: Your phone number.
* **Requestor Organization**: The name of your organization.
* **Requestor Position**: Your position or role within your organization.
* **Requestor Date of Birth**: Your date of birth.
* **Sharing Organization**: The name of the organization sharing data.
* **Sharing Department**: The department or contact person within the sharing organization.
* **Sharing Contact Email**: The email address of the contact person.
* **Sharing Contact Phone**: The phone number of the contact person.
* **Shared For**: Select the purpose for which the data will be used.
* **Data Request Date**: The date the data request was made.
* **Requestor Agreement**: A check box indicating that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Sharing Agreement**: A check box indicating that you have reviewed and agreed to the sharing organization's terms and conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions**: A check box indicating that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Requestor Signature**: A check box indicating that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms and conditions.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Provide all required information.
* Ensure that you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the sharing organization before submitting the form.
* Submit the form only if you are authorized to request data on behalf of your organization.
