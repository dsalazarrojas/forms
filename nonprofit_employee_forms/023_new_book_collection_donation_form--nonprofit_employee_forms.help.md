# new_book_collection_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new_book_collection_donation_form is designed to collect information from users for new book collections.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the fields in the "User Input" page.
2. Select the type of book(s) you are donating in the "Book Type" field.
3. Indicate the condition of the book(s) in the "Book Condition" field.
4. Enter the quantity of books being donated in the "Book Quantity" field.
5. Select the date of the donation in the "Donation Date" field.
6. Add any additional notes in the "Note" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "User Email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "User Phone" field.
9. Select your category as either "individual" or "organization" in the "User Category" field.
10. Fill out the "Output File" field with any additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (1): This is the main entry page where you can enter any additional information.
* **Book Type** (2): Select the type of book(s) you are donating (fiction, nonfiction, children books, etc.).
* **Book Condition** (3): Indicate the condition of the book(s) being donated (new, used, good, fair, poor, very poor).
* **Book Quantity** (4): Enter the quantity of books being donated.
* **Donation Date** (5): Select the date of the donation.
* **Note** (6): Add any additional notes about the donation.
* **User Email** (7): Enter your email address.
* **User Phone** (8): Enter your phone number.
* **User Category** (9): Select your category as either "individual" or "organization".
* **Output File** (10): Fill out this field with any additional information.
* **Assigned Tool** (11): Enter the tool being used for this form.
* **Description** (12): Enter a description of the form.
* **Category** (13): Enter a category for this form.
* **Form IDs** (14): Enter the IDs for this form.
* **Category Name** (15): Enter the name of the category.
* **Category Description** (16): Enter a description of the category.
* **Output File 2** (17): Enter additional information for this field.
* **Form Version** (18): Enter the version of the form.
* **Form Title** (19): Enter the title of the form.
* **Category Title** (20): Enter the title of the category.
* **Category 2** (21): Enter a category 2.
* **Assigned Tool 2** (22): Enter the tool being used for this form 2.
* **Description 2** (23): Enter a description 2.
* **Form IDs 2** (24): Enter the IDs for this form 2.
* **Created By** (25): Enter the person who created this form.
