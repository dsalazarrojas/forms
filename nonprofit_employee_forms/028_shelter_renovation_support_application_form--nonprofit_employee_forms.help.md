# Shelter Renovation Support Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shelter Renovation Support Application Form is used to gather necessary information from applicants seeking support for shelter renovations. It is designed to help shelter staff assess and manage renovation requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Fill in your name and submitter's name in the respective fields.
2.  Specify the shelter's name, location, and type.
3.  Provide a brief description of the shelter and the purpose of the renovation request.
4.  Enter the date and time of visit (if applicable).
5.  List the prophets of visit (if applicable).
6.  Enter your contact information (including phone number and email).
7.  Provide additional information about the renovation request (if necessary).
8.  Enter the shelter's address, phone number, and email (if applicable).
9.  Select the submitter's organization and the submitted to person.
10.  Finally, review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shelter Renovation Request** (`shelter_renovation_request`): A brief description of the renovation request, including the purpose and goals of the project.
* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`): The name of the person submitting the application.
* **Shelter Name** (`shelter_name`): The name of the shelter where the renovation is requested.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`): The location where the renovation will take place (if not specified by shelter name).
* **Budget Requested** (`budget_requested`): The total budget requested for the renovation project.
* **Shelter Type** (`shelter_type`): The type of shelter where the renovation is requested (Small, Large, or Both).
* **Shelter Description** (`shelter_description`): A brief description of the shelter.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`): The date of the visit when the renovation will occur (if applicable).
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`): The time of the visit when the renovation will occur (if applicable).
* **Prophets of Visit** (`prophets_of_visit`): A list of prophets that will be visiting the shelter during this time (if applicable).
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`): Your contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): The email address of the shelter.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`): Any additional information you would like to provide about the renovation request.
* **Shelter Address** (`shelter_address`): The address of the shelter.
* **Shelter Phone** (`shelter_phone`): The phone number of the shelter.
* **Shelter Email** (`shelter_email`): The email address of the shelter.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`): Your name.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`): Your phone number.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`): Your email address.
* **Submitter Location** (`submitter_location`): Your location.
* **Submitter Organization** (`submitter_organization`): The name of your organization.
* **Submitter Notes** (`submitter_notes`): Any additional notes you would like to provide about the application.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): The person or organization submitting the application.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`): The person or organization the application is being submitted to.
* **Submitter Organization 2** (`submitter_organization_2`): The name of the second organization associated with the submitter.

Note: Please ensure you have the necessary permissions and authorization to submit this application, and that the information provided is accurate and complete.
