# Senior Outreach Volunteer Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Senior Outreach Volunteer Registration form is a crucial tool used to collect information from senior citizens about their volunteer experience and availability. This form helps the nonprofit organization to match their skills and interests with volunteer opportunities that fit their schedule and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the corresponding field. This field is required.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the corresponding field. This field is also required.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the corresponding field. This field is required and should be a valid email address.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the corresponding field. This field is required.
5. Select your **Availability** date(s) when you are available to volunteer. This field is required and should be in the format (YYYY-MM-DD).
6. Choose your **Interest Areas** by selecting one or more from the list (Outreach, Senior Services, or Other). This field is required.
7. If you have any **Additional Comments**, please enter them in the corresponding field. This field is not required but is useful for the nonprofit organization to better understand your volunteer experience and interests.
8. Finally, sign the **Signature** field to confirm your information. This field is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name in this field.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name in this field.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Availability** (`availability`, `date`, required): Select the date(s) when you are available to volunteer.
- **Interest Areas** (`interest_areas`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose one or more areas of interest from the list (Outreach, Senior Services, or Other).
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information you would like to share about your volunteer experience or interests.
- **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, required): Sign this field to confirm your information.
- **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): If applicable, enter the date in the format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): If applicable, enter the time in the format (HH:MM).
- **Volunteer Type** (`volunteer_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose your volunteer type from the list (Regular, One-time, or Ongoing).
