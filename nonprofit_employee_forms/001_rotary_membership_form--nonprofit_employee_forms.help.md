# Rotary Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rotary Membership Form is a user-friendly form for signing up as a member of a Rotary club.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your personal details: First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, Address, and City.
2. Select your Rotary Club from the list of available options.
3. Choose your Membership Type (Annual, Bi-Annual, or Lifetime).
4. If required, enter your Join Date and Renewal Date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address** (Address, text, optional): Enter your mailing address.
* **City** (City, text, optional): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (State, text, optional): Enter the state where you reside.
* **Zip Code** (Zip Code, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Rotary Club** (Rotary Club, select_one, required): Select the Rotary club where you wish to join. This is a required field.
* **Membership Type** (Membership Type, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of membership you prefer. Select one or more from the following options: Annual, Bi-Annual, or Lifetime.
* **Join Date** (Join Date, date, optional): If you're a new member, enter the date you joined. If you've already joined, this field will be ignored.
* **Renewal Date** (Renewal Date, date, optional): If you're a renewing member, enter the date you'd like to renew your membership. This field will be ignored for new members.

## Tips
* Double-check your details before submitting the form.
* Make sure to select the correct Rotary club and membership type.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact the relevant Rotary club or a member of their team.
