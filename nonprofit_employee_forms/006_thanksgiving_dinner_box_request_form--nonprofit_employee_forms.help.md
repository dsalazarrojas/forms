# Thanksgiving Dinner Box Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Thanksgiving Dinner Box is a program designed to provide families in need with a free, pre-cooked, and pre-packed dinner box for Thanksgiving. This form is for requesting a dinner box for a family member or for yourself.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure that your request is processed correctly, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your First Name.
3. Enter your Last Name.
4. Provide your address.
5. Enter your email address (for communication purposes).
6. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
7. Select the number of family members you are requesting the dinner box for.
8. Choose your preferred foods for the dinner box (turkey, mashed potatoes, stuffing, pumpkin pie, or other).
9. Enter the date you would like to pick up the dinner box.
10. Enter the time you would like to pick up the dinner box.
11. Add any additional comments or notes about your request in the "Notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name or the first name of the person you're requesting the dinner box for.
* **Last Name**: Enter the last name of the person you're requesting the dinner box for.
* **Address**: Enter the address where you would like to receive the dinner box.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your request.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (if applicable) for us to contact you.
* **How many family members**: Select the number of family members you are requesting the dinner box for. Please choose from the options: Family of 1, Family of 2, Family of 3, Family of 4, or Family of 5 or more.
* **Food preference**: Choose the foods you would like to include in the dinner box. Select all that apply from: Turkey, Mashed potatoes, Stuffing, Pumpkin pie, or Other.
* **Date**: Select the date you would like to pick up the dinner box.
* **Time**: Select the time you would like to pick up the dinner box.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about your request in the "Notes" section.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are eligible for the program and haven't already submitted a request.
* If you have any special requests or concerns, please include them in the "Notes" section.
* You can request a dinner box for yourself or on behalf of a family member.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact us.
* We will review your request and contact you shortly after submission.
