# stripe_animal_shelter_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals who want to make a donation to the local animal shelter. It helps non-profit employees to efficiently process and categorize donations based on the frequency and amount donated.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as the donator in the "Donator Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Donator Email" field.
3. Enter the amount of your donation in the "Donator Donation Amount" field.
4. Select how often you would like to donate from the "Donation Frequency" options (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
5. If you have any special requests for your donation, type them in the "Special Donation Requests" field.
6. (Optional) Enter your phone number in the "Donator Phone" field for easy contact.
7. Leave any additional comments in the "Donator Comment" field.
8. Select the non-profit employees you would like to receive your donation in the "Non Profit Employees" field (John, Jane, or Joe).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donator Name**: Enter your full name as the person making the donation.
* **Donator Email**: Enter your email address for updates and notifications.
* **Donator Donation Amount**: Enter the amount of money you would like to donate (a number).
* **Donation Frequency**: Select the frequency at which you would like to donate from the options provided (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Special Donation Requests**: If you have specific requests for your donation, type them here.
* **Donator Phone**: (Optional) Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Donator Comment**: Leave any additional comments about your donation.
* **Non Profit Employees**: Select the non-profit employees you would like to receive your donation from the options provided (John, Jane, or Joe).
