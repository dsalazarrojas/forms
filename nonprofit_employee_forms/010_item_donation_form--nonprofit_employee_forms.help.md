# Item Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from donors about the items they wish to donate to the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Donations Details section with a brief description of the items you are donating.
2. Provide your contact information in the Donor Information section, including your name and any relevant contact details.
3. Enter your contact information in the Contact Info section, including your name, email address, and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donations Details (Required: No)**:
	+ This field is where you will describe the items you are donating. Please include a brief explanation of the items, such as the type of item, quantity, and condition.
* **Donor Information (Required: No)**:
	+ This field is where you will provide your contact information as the donor. Please include your name and any other relevant contact details.
* **Contact Info (Required: No)**:
	+ This field is where you can enter your contact information in case we need to follow up with you about your donation.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure your donation can be processed efficiently.
* If you have any questions or need help with the form, you can contact us at form@gic.mx.
