# Congregation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from congregation members on various aspects of the church's services, programs, and overall experience.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Select the relevant options for each question, taking into account your personal experiences and preferences.
3. Provide clear and concise answers to open-ended questions, such as "Prayer Request Details" and "Additional Comments or Suggestions".
4. If you have any prayer requests, please specify them in the "Prayer Requests" field.
5. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them in the "Additional Comments or Suggestions" field.
