# 1023 EZ Eligibility Worksheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 1023 EZ Eligibility Worksheet is a document used to determine whether an organization meets the requirements for tax-exempt status under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code. It is completed by the organization's representative and is a key component in the determination of tax-exempt status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully and answer questions honestly and accurately.
* Provide detailed information about your organization, including its mission, activities, and governance structure.
* Check all that apply when selecting multiple activities.
* Enter dates in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* Make sure to sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full legal name of the organization (id: 2)**: This is the legal name of your organization as it appears on your incorporation documents.
* **Employer Identification Number (EIN) (id: 3)**: This is your organization's nine-digit federal tax ID number.
* **Date of incorporation (id: 4)**: Enter the date your organization was incorporated.
* **Type of organization (id: 5)**: Choose the type of organization you are (e.g. Corporation, Unincorporated Association, Trust, Limited Liability Company (LLC)).
* **Brief description of the organization's mission (id: 6)**: Describe the primary purpose of your organization in one sentence.
* **Estimated annual gross receipts (id: 7)**: Enter your projected annual gross receipts for the next four years.
* **Total assets as of today (id: 8)**: Enter the current value of all your organization's assets.
* **Primary charitable activities (id: 9)**: Check all the activities that apply to your organization.
* **Does the organization participate in political campaign activity? (id: 10)**: Check yes or no if your organization participates in political campaign activities.
* **Does the organization engage in substantial lobbying activity? (id: 11)**: Check yes or no if your organization engages in substantial lobbying activities.
* **Approximate number of employees (id: 12)**: Enter the number of full-time equivalent staff.
* **Number of board members (id: 13)**: Enter the number of members of your governing body.
* **Organization website URL (id: 14)**: If you have a website, enter the URL.
* **Principal office address (id: 15)**: Enter your organization's street address, city, state, and ZIP.
* **Was the organization formed primarily for charitable purposes? (id: 16)**: Check yes or no if your organization was formed primarily for charitable purposes.
* **Does the organization have governance documents? (id: 17)**: Check yes or no if your organization has articles of incorporation and bylaws.
* **Is the organization a private foundation? (id: 18)**: Check yes or no if your organization is a private foundation.
* **Is the organization exempt under section 501(c)(other than c(3))? (id: 19)**: Check yes or no if your organization is exempt under section 501(c)(other than c(3)).
* **Additional notes regarding eligibility (id: 20)**: Enter any special circumstances or clarifications about your organization's eligibility.
* **Name of person completing this worksheet (id: 21)**: Enter your name.
* **Email address of person completing this worksheet (id: 22)**: Enter your email address.
* **Title of person completing this worksheet (id: 23)**: Enter your title.
* **Date this worksheet was completed (id: 24)**: Enter the date you completed this form.
* **Additional comments or recommendations (id: 25)**: Enter any additional comments or recommendations.
