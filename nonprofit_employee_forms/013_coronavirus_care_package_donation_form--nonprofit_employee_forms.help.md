# coronavirus_care_package_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The coronavirus_care_package_donation_form is designed to collect information from individuals willing to donate care packages during the coronavirus pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields: Donor Name, Address, City, State, Zip Code, and Package Value.
2. Provide your contact information: Phone and Email.
3. Describe your care package details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your complete address where you would like the care packages to be delivered.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where you would like the care packages to be delivered.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter your state or province where you would like the care packages to be delivered.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number, required): Enter your zip code where you would like the care packages to be delivered.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter a contact phone number in case we need to get in touch with you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address in case we need to contact you about your care package.
* **Care Package Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): Briefly describe what kind of care package you are offering (e.g., food, toys, clothing).
* **Package Value** (`package_value`, number, optional): Indicate the value of the care package you are offering (e.g., monetary value or items included).

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Be honest and accurate with your information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
