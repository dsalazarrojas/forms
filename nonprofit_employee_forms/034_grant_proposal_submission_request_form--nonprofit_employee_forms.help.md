# Grant Proposal Submission Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grant Proposal Submission Request Form is used to collect information from non-profit organizations submitting grant proposals to our organization. This form helps us understand the scope of the proposal, the non-profit's background, and the grant's status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the grant proposal in the **Grant Proposal** field (Field 1).
2. Fill in the non-profit information in the **Nonprofit Info** field (Field 2).
3. Enter the grant amount in the **Grant Amount** field (Field 3).
4. Specify the grant terms in the **Grant Terms** field (Field 4).
5. Identify the contact person in the **Contact Person** field (Field 5).
6. Enter the grant start and end dates in the **Grant Start Date** (Field 6) and **Grant End Date** (Field 7) fields, respectively.
7. Select the grant status from the dropdown menu in the **Grant Status** field (Field 8).
8. Provide any additional comments about the grant in the **Grant Comments** field (Field 9).
9. Enter the non-profit name in the **Nonprofit Name** field (Field 10).
10. Enter your email address in the **Email** field (Field 11).
11. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field (Field 12).
12. Describe your project in the **Project Description** field (Field 13).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Grant Proposal** (Field 1)
	+ **Type:** Text
	+ **Required:** False
	+ **Hint:** Briefly describe the grant proposal in this field.
* **Nonprofit Info** (Field 2)
	+ **Type:** Text
	+ **Required:** False
	+ **Hint:** Provide information about the non-profit organization, such as their name and contact details.
* **Grant Amount** (Field 3)
	+ **Type:** Number
	+ **Required:** False
	+ **Hint:** Enter the amount of the grant being requested.
* **Grant Terms** (Field 4)
	+ **Type:** Text
	+ **Required:** False
	+ **Hint:** Specify any specific terms or conditions of the grant, such as payment schedules or deliverables.
* **Contact Person** (Field 5)
	+ **Type:** Text
	+ **Required:** False
	+ **Hint:** Identify the person responsible for managing the grant, including their name and contact information.
* **Grant Start Date** (Field 6)
	+ **Type:** Date
	+ **Required:** False
	+ **Hint:** Enter the date the grant is expected to start.
* **Grant End Date** (Field 7)
	+ **Type:** Date
	+ **Required:** False
	+ **Hint:** Enter the date the grant is expected to end.
* **Grant Status** (Field 8)
	+ **Type:** Select One
	+ **Required:** False
	+ **Hint:** Select the current status of the grant, such as In Progress, Pending, or Complete.
* **Grant Comments** (Field 9)
	+ **Type:** Note
	+ **Required:** False
	+ **Hint:** Provide any additional comments or feedback about the grant.
* **Nonprofit Name** (Field 10)
	+ **Type:** Text
	+ **Required:** False
	+ **Hint:** Enter the non-profit organization's name.
* **Email** (Field 11)
	+ **Type:** Email
	+ **Required:** False
	+ **Hint:** Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Field 12)
	+ **Type:** Text
	+ **Required:** False
	+ **Hint:** Enter your phone number.
* **Project Description** (Field 13)
	+ **Type:** Text
	+ **Required:** False
	+ **Hint:** Describe your project or grant in this field.
