# Volunteer Candidate Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from potential volunteers for the nonprofit organization. It helps the organization understand the volunteer's demographic data and areas of interest, making it easier to match volunteers with suitable opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter a valid Email address that you check regularly.
3. Provide your Phone number for contact purposes.
4. Select your preferred Volunteer Type from the options provided.
5. Choose all the Areas of Interest that apply to you.
6. If applicable, provide a specific Date and Time for any upcoming events you are able to volunteer for.
7. Add any additional Notes if you have any specific skills or experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (`first_name`, text, optional)
Enter your first name as it is commonly used.

### Last Name (`last_name`, text, optional)
Enter your last name as it is commonly used.

### Email (`email`, email, optional)
Enter a valid email address that you regularly check. This will be used to communicate with you about volunteer opportunities.

### Phone (`phone`, text, optional)
Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### Volunteer Type (`select_one`, select, optional)
Select your preferred Volunteer Type from the options provided:
* Volunteer Leader
* Volunteer Coordinator
* Other (please specify in the Notes field)

### Areas of Interest (`select_multiple`, select, optional)
Choose all the areas of interest that apply to you:
* Volunteer Coord
* Event Planning
* Fundraising
* Marketing

### Date (`date`, date, optional)
If you have a specific date in mind for an event, enter it here.

### Time (`time`, time, optional)
If you have a specific time in mind for an event, enter it here.

### Note (`note`, note, optional)
Add any additional information about your skills or experience that you think would be helpful for us to know.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate information to ensure a smooth volunteer experience.
* If you have any questions or need help with the form, don't hesitate to contact us.
