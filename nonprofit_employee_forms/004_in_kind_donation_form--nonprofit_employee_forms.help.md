# in_kind_donation_form - Help Guide
=====================================

## Purpose
The in-kind donation form is used to record and track non-monetary contributions made by individuals or organizations to support a nonprofit organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of donation made (goods, services, or volunteer time).
2. Enter the description of the goods or services donated.
3. Enter the recipient's name and email address (if applicable).
4. Enter the donor's name and email address (if applicable).
5. Specify the total value of the donation.
6. Provide a brief description of the donation.
7. Enter the date and time of the donation (if applicable).
8. Enter any additional information about the donor's relationship to the nonprofit organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Donation Type
* **Donation Type** (`Donation Type`, select_one): Select the type of donation made (goods, services, or volunteer time).

### Goods Donated
* **Goods Donated** (`goods_donated`, text): Enter a description of the goods donated.

### Services Donated
* **Services Donated** (`service_donated`, text): Enter a description of the services donated.

### Volunteer Time Donated
* **Volunteer Time Donated** (`volunteer_time_donated`, text): Enter the amount of time donated (e.g., hours or days).

### Recipient Name
* **Recipient Name** (`recipient_name`, text): Enter the name of the recipient organization or individual.

### Recipient Email
* **Recipient Email** (`recipient_email`, email): Enter the recipient's email address (if applicable).

### Recipient Phone
* **Recipient Phone** (`recipient_phone`, text): Enter the recipient's phone number (if applicable).

### Amount Donated
* **Amount Donated** (`amount_donated`, number): Enter the total value of the donation.

### Description
* **Description** (`description`, text): Provide a brief description of the donation.

### Donor Name
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, text): Enter the name of the donor.

### Donor Email
* **Donor Email** (`donor_email`, email): Enter the donor's email address (if applicable).

### Donor Phone
* **Donor Phone** (`donor_phone`, text): Enter the donor's phone number (if applicable).

### Total Donated
* **Total Donated** (`total_donated`, text): Enter the total value of the donation.

### Date
* **Date** (`date`, date): Enter the date the donation was made.

### Time
* **Time** (`time`, time): Enter the time the donation was made.

### Form Submission ID
* **Form Submission ID** (`form_submission_id`, text): Enter a unique identifier for the form submission.

### Nonprofit Organization
* **Nonprofit Organization** (`nonprofit_organization`, text): Enter the name of the nonprofit organization.

### Staff Member Name
* **Staff Member Name** (`staff_member_name`, text): Enter the name of the staff member who submitted the form.

### Donor Relationship
* **Donor Relationship** (`donor_relationship`, select_one): Select the type of relationship between the donor and the nonprofit organization (friend, family member, coworker, volunteer, donor, or other).

### Donor Notes
* **Donor Notes** (`donor_notes`, text): Enter any additional information about the donor.

### Nonprofit Address
* **Nonprofit Address** (`nonprofit_address`, text): Enter the address of the nonprofit organization.

### Recipient Country
* **Recipient Country** (`recipient_country`, text): Enter the country where the recipient organization is located.

### Recipient Zip
* **Recipient Zip** (`recipient_zip`, text): Enter the zip code of the recipient's location.

### Donor Zip
* **Donor Zip** (`donor_zip`, text): Enter the zip code of the donor's location.

### Form Submitter
* **Form Submitter** (`form_submitted_by`, text): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.

Note: The fields marked as required are mandatory and must be filled out for the form to be complete.
