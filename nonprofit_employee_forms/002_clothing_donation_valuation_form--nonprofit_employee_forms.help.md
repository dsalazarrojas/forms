# Clothing Donation Valuation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Clothing Donation Valuation Form is designed to facilitate the accurate and efficient valuation and reporting of clothing donations made by donors to the nonprofit organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
2.  Provide your email address for communication and follow-up purposes.
3.  If desired, enter your phone number for alternate contact method.
4.  Select the main category of the items donated.
5.  Count and enter the total number of items donated.
6.  Select the overall condition of the items donated.
7.  Estimate the fair market value of the donated items.
8.  If applicable, indicate if there are any high-value items over $50 and describe them.
9.  If applicable, describe any damage, stains, or issues with the items.
10. Select your preferred payment method.
11. Indicate if you need tax documentation.
12. Finally, enter any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Donor Full Name** (`donor_name`, text, required)
Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.

### **Donor Email Address** (`donor_contact_email`, email, required)
Provide your email address for communication and follow-up purposes.

### **Donor Phone Number** (`donor_phone`, text, optional)
Enter your phone number for alternate contact method.

### **Date of Donation** (`donation_date`, date, required)
Enter the date you made the donation.

### **Primary Clothing Category** (`clothing_category`, select_multiple, required)
Select the main category of the items donated.

### **Total Number of Items** (`total_items`, number, required)
Count and enter the total number of items donated.

### **Overall Condition** (`condition_assessment`, select_one, required)
Select the overall condition of the items from new to mixed conditions.

### **Estimated Value** (`estimated_value`, number, required)
Estimate the fair market value of the donated items.

### **Designer Brands** (`designer_brands`, text, optional)
If the items include designer or premium brands, mention them here.

### **High-Value Items** (`high_value_items`, select_one, optional)
Indicate if there are any high-value items over $50.

### **Description of High-Value Items** (`high_value_description`, text, optional)
If there are high-value items, describe them here.

### **Damage or Stains** (`damage_or_stains`, text, optional)
Describe any issues with the items, such as damage or stains.

### **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required)
Select your preferred payment method.

### **Need Tax Documentation** (`tax_documentation`, select_one, optional)
Indicate if you need tax documentation.

### **Additional Notes or Comments** (`additional_notes`, text, optional)
Enter any additional comments or notes you have.

## Tips
*   Please provide as much detail as possible about the items donated.
*   Ensure the condition of the items is accurately described.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
*   The information provided will be used for tax purposes and to track your donation accurately.
