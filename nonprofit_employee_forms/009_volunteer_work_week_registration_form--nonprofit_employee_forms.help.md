# Volunteer Work Week Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register your availability and commitment for volunteer work weeks. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form accurately to ensure we can best utilize your skills and expertise during our volunteer work weeks.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by reviewing the form's fields below.
- Fill out each field according to your actual availability and preferences.
- Ensure all required fields are completed to avoid any errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Volunteer Work Week Registration Form**: This is the title of the form and is not a field to fill.
- **Volunteer Work Week Registration Form**: This field is not actually a field to fill; it's the title of the form.
- **Volunteer Work Week Ending Date**: Enter the date you plan to end your volunteer work week. Please use the date format: MM/DD/YYYY. For example, 07/01/2025.
- **Volunteer Work Week Starting Date**: Enter the date you plan to start your volunteer work week. Please use the date format: MM/DD/YYYY. For example, 06/15/2025.
- **Volunteer Work Week Days**: Select the days of the week you are available to work. You can select multiple days, separated by commas. For example, Monday, Thursday.
- **Volunteer Work Week Ending Time**: Enter the time you plan to end your volunteer work week. Please use the 12-hour clock format: 08:00, 12:00, 13:00, 14:00, etc.
- **Volunteer Work Week Starting Time**: Enter the time you plan to start your volunteer work week. Please use the 12-hour clock format: 08:00, 12:00, 13:00, 14:00, etc.
 
## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your dates and times before submission.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
- Submission of this form is a commitment. Please ensure accuracy for smooth volunteer work week planning.
