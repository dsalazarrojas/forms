# racial_equality_petition_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback, concerns, or suggestions from individuals or organizations about racial equality issues. It will help us understand the community's thoughts and opinions on this sensitive topic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling the form.
2. On the first page, you will be asked to provide some basic contact information, which is necessary for us to get in touch with you if we need to follow up on your concerns or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (`name`, `text`, required): This is where you can provide your name to be identified as a contributor to this issue. Please make sure to spell it correctly.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you to discuss your concerns or suggestions.
* **address** (`address`, `text`, required): Provide your address to help us get in touch with you, if needed.
* **city** (`city`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to specify the city you reside in or are associated with, please enter it here.
* **state** (`state`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to specify the state you are from, please enter it here.
* **zip_code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your zip code to help us get in touch with you, if needed.
* **country** (`country`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to specify the country you are from, please enter it here.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter a valid phone number where we can contact you for further discussion.
* **message** (`message`, `text`, optional): Here's the space to share your thoughts, concerns, or suggestions on racial equality. Please be clear and concise in your writing.

## Tips
- Please keep your message clear and concise, as it will be easier to review and respond to.
- If you have any attachments or supporting documents, please attach them separately, as we cannot accept files larger than the allowed size.
