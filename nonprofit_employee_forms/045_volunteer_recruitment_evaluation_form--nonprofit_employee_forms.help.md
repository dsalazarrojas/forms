# Volunteer Recruitment Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential volunteers about their background, skills, and availability for a nonprofit organization. The form will help the organization assess whether the individual is a suitable candidate for volunteer work and determine the best fit for available positions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your information in the Volunteer Information section, including any details that will help us get to know you better.
2. Provide your contact details in the Contact Details section, including a phone number and email address so we can reach out to you.
3. Share your skills and interests in the Skills and Interests section, so we can match you with opportunities that align with your passions.
4. Indicate your availability in the Availability section, including your preferred schedule and any time zones you operate in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Volunteer Information (<span style="font-weight: bold;">Volunteer Information</span>)
* **Volunteer Information** (<span style="font-weight: bold;">text</span>, <span style="font-weight: bold;">optional</span>): This section is where you can provide any additional information about yourself that you think would be helpful for us to know. This could include your previous volunteer experience, relevant skills, or any other details that you feel are relevant to your application.

### Contact Details (<span style="font-weight: bold;">Contact Details</span>)
* **Contact Details** (<span style="font-weight: bold;">text</span>, <span style="font-weight: bold;">optional</span>): In this section, please provide your phone number and email address so we can contact you to discuss further.

### Skills and Interests (<span style="font-weight: bold;">Skills and Interests</span>)
* **Skills and Interests** (<span style="font-weight: bold;">text</span>, <span style="font-weight: bold;">optional</span>): Share with us any skills or interests that you have that might be relevant to volunteer work. This could include languages, technical skills, or hobbies that you are passionate about.

### Availability (<span style="font-weight: bold;">Availability</span>)
* **Availability** (<span style="font-weight: bold;">text</span>, <span style="font-weight: bold;">optional</span>): Indicate your available time slots and any time zones you operate in, so we can schedule you for volunteer work that fits your schedule.

## Tips
- Please be honest and thorough in your responses.
- If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, feel free to reach out to us.
- We will review your application and get back to you soon to discuss further.
