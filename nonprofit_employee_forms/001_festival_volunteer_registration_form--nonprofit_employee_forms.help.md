# Festival Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a registration form for prospective volunteers to sign up for festival events. It collects personal details, availability, and event-specific information to help us prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your personal details such as first name and last name in the respective text fields.
2. Enter your email address in the email field.
3. Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
4. Indicate your available slots by selecting from the dropdown options (Yes or No).
5. Check the interest areas you're interested in by selecting from the dropdown options (Yes or No).
6. If you have any special talents, enter them in the text field.
7. Select the type of volunteer role you'd like to take on by selecting from the dropdown options (Yes or No).
8. Enter the name of the event you're interested in helping with.
9. Enter the date of the event (MM/DD/YYYY).
10. Choose the time of the event (HH:MM:SS).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name**: Enter your first name.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
- **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
- **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Available Slots**: Choose when you are available to volunteer.
  * "Yes" indicates you are available during the selected time period.
  * "No" indicates you are not available during this time period.
- **Interest Areas**: Choose which areas you have interest in volunteering for.
  * "Yes" indicates you have an interest in this area.
  * "No" indicates you do not have an interest in this area.
- **Special Talents**: If you have any special talents or skills, enter them here.
- **Volunteer Type**: Choose the type of volunteer role you'd like to take on.
  * "Yes" indicates you'd like to take on this role.
  * "No" indicates you do not want to take on this role.
- **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you're interested in helping with.
- **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event (MM/DD/YYYY).
- **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event (HH:MM:SS).
