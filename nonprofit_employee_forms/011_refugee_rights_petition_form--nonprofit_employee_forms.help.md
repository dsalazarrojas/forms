# Refugee Rights Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Refugee Rights Petition Form is designed to collect information from individuals who support the rights of refugees. This information will be used to advocate for the rights of refugees in the community. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the User Information section with your details.
2. Provide your contact information in the Contact Information section.
3. In the Petition Reason section, explain why you support the rights of refugees.
4. Optional: provide your email address and phone number if you want to be contacted for further information.
5. If you have any notes or comments, you can add them in the Notes section.
6. Finally, submit your form by signing and confirming that you have filled it out accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information**: Enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant personal details. This information will help us understand who is supporting the rights of refugees.
* **Contact Information**: Provide your contact details, such as your phone number and email address. This will allow us to get in touch with you for further information or updates.
* **Address**: Enter your current address. This will help us understand where you are located in relation to the refugee support initiative.
* **Petition Reason**: Explain in a few sentences why you support the rights of refugees. This section is essential for us to understand your reasons for supporting the initiative.
* **Email**: Optional: If you have a valid email address, please provide it. This will allow us to send you updates or further information about the initiative.
* **Phone**: Optional: If you have a valid phone number, please provide it. This will allow us to contact you for further information.
* **Notes**: If you have any additional comments or notes, you can add them here. This is a space for you to provide any additional information that may be relevant to your support for the rights of refugees.
* **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit your form.
* **Confirm**: Confirm that you have accurately filled out your form by checking the box below.
* **Signature**: Sign your name to confirm that you have filled out the form accurately and truthfully. 
* **Petition Reason**: This section should explain why you want to support the rights of refugees, and it will be used to understand your stance on the issue.
