# Baby Supplies Distribution Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information from organizations or individuals who are involved in distributing baby supplies to families. This data will help us better understand their needs and resources, and potentially identify areas where we can provide support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as they apply to your organization or situation. You can choose to leave fields blank if they do not apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent name**: Your name or the name of the person answering this form.
* **Organization**: If applicable, your non-profit or community group name.
* **Distribution area**: The city or neighborhood where you are distributing baby supplies.
* **Items distributed**: Select all the baby supplies you are currently distributing. You can select one or multiple items.
	+ Diapers
	+ Wipes
	+ Formula
	+ Clothing
	+ Toys
* **Families served weekly**: An estimate of the number of families you serve each week. (If unsure, leave blank)
* **Most critical unmet needs**: Briefly describe any unmet needs related to baby supplies distribution. (If none, leave blank)
* **Storage capacity for supplies**: A description of the storage capacity you have for baby supplies (e.g., cubic feet or a specific description).
* **Volunteer support needed**: If you need help with specific tasks, describe them here.
* **Interest in partnership with donors**: Check "True" if you are interested in partnering with donors to support your distribution efforts.
* **Contact information**: Provide an email or phone number for follow-up communication. (If unsure, leave blank)
