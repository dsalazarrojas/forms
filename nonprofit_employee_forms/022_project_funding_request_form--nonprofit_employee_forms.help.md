# Project Funding Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Project Funding Request Form is used by nonprofit employees to request funding for their projects. This form helps the organization gather information about the project, including its details, funding source, and contact information of the submitter and the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your project details, including project title, description, and funding amount.
2. Select the funding source for your project (Government Agency, Private Foundation, or Corporate Sponsor).
3. Choose the project category (Community Development, Economic Development, Health and Wellness, Education, Environment, Technology, Arts and Culture, Nonprofit Capacity Building).
4. Enter the project manager's name and project status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
5. Select whether the project has a project lead (Yes or No).
6. Enter your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
7. Enter the organization's information, including the name, address, city, state, and country.
8. Add any additional submitter notes or comments.
9. Finally, select the submit date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization** (`project_category`): Select the category of your project from the provided options.
* **Description** (`description`): Please describe your project in a few sentences.
* **Funding Amount** (`funding_amount`): Enter the amount of funding requested for your project.
* **Budget** (`budget`): Please enter the project's budget.
* **Funding Source** (`funding_source`): Select the source of funding for your project (Government Agency, Private Foundation, or Corporate Sponsor).
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`): Enter the start date of your project.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`): Enter the end date of your project.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`): Select the current status of your project (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Project Type** (`project_type`): Select the type of your project from the provided options.
* **Project Manager** (`project_manager`): Enter the name of the project manager.
* **Project Lead** (`project_lead`): Select whether the project has a project lead (Yes or No).
* **Submitter** (`submitter`): Enter your name.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`): Enter the date you're submitting the form.
* **Submit Time** (`submit_time`): Select the time you're submitting the form.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`): Enter the name of the organization.
* **Organization Address** (`organization_address`): Enter the address of the organization.
* **Organization City** (`organization_city`): Enter the city of the organization.
* **Organization State** (`organization_state`): Enter the state of the organization.
* **Organization Country** (`organization_country`): Enter the country of the organization.
* **Submitter Notes** (`submitter_notes`): Enter any additional information about the form submission.
* **Submitter Comment** (`submitter_comment`): Enter any additional comments about the form submission.

Note: Some fields are optional. Make sure to fill in only the required fields as specified above.
