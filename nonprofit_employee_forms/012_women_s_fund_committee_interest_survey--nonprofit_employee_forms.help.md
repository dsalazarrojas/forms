# Women's Fund Committee Interest Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help the Women's Fund Committee assess your interests and suitability for participation on the committee. Please answer the questions honestly and provide as much detail as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all questions thoroughly.
2. Enter your information in the corresponding fields.
3. Submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1): Enter your first name as it is spelled.
* **Last Name** (2): Enter your last name as it is spelled.
* **Email** (3): Enter your valid email address that will be used for communication.
* **Address** (4): Enter your current address or the address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (5): Enter your phone number that will be used for communication.
* **Organization** (6): Enter your current or former organization you are associated with.
* **Role** (7): Enter your role or position in your organization.
* **Committee Interest** (8): Briefly describe your interest in the Women's Fund Committee and what you can bring to the group.
* **Volunteer Hours** (9): Enter the number of hours you are willing and able to commit to volunteering per week.
* **Availability** (10): Enter your availability for meetings and events, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Preferred Commits** (11): Suggest specific tasks or activities you would like to be involved in as a committee member.
* **Committee Commitment** (12): Briefly describe your level of commitment to the Women's Fund Committee.
* **Skills** (13): List any skills or expertise you have that may be useful to the committee.
* **Committee Suggestions** (14): Suggest any other areas you would like to see the Women's Fund Committee focus on or activities you would like to implement.
* **Other Committee Suggestions** (15): Suggest any other ideas or initiatives you would like to propose for the committee.
* **Committee Suggestions Other** (16): Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have for the Women's Fund Committee.
