# Nonprofit Leadership Dinner RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nonprofit Leadership Dinner RSVP form is designed to collect information about your attendance and preferences for a leadership dinner event hosted by a nonprofit organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your guest information, including your name and contact details.
2. Confirm your attendance and dietary preferences.
3. Specify the number of guests attending with you.
4. Choose the correct event date and time.
5. Select the event location.
6. Respond with your RSVP status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Guest Info**: Enter your name and any other contact details you'd like to share.
- **Guest Name**: Enter the name(s) of the guest(s) attending with you.
- **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number for further contact or updates.
- **Dietary Preferences**:
  - If you have dietary restrictions, select one of the options:
    - Vegetarian
    - Non-vegetarian
    - Omnivore
    - None (if you don't have any restrictions)
  - If you have a different preference, provide it in the "Other Dietary Preference" field.
- **Guests Attending**: Indicate the number of guests attending with you, or select "Other" to specify.
- **Other Guests**: If you select "Other," enter the names and contact details of your guests.
- **Event Date**: Choose the date of the event from the provided options.
- **Event Time**: Select the time of the event from the provided options.
- **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
- **RSVP**: Confirm your attendance by selecting "True" or decline by selecting "False."

## Tips
- Ensure you provide accurate and complete information to help us plan the event effectively.
- If you have any special requests or dietary needs, please let us know in the "Other Dietary Preference" field.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact the event organizers for assistance.
