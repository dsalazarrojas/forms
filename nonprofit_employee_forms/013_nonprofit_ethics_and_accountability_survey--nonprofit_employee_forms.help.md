# Nonprofit Ethics And Accountability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nonprofit Ethics And Accountability Survey is a tool used to assess a nonprofit organization's commitment to ethics and accountability. This survey is designed to help the organization understand its strengths and weaknesses in these areas and identify opportunities for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2.  Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as needed.
3.  For select fields, choose the options that best match your understanding and experiences.
4.  For text fields, provide clear and concise answers.
5.  If you're unsure about any question, seek clarification from a supervisor or peer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Nonprofit Organizational Values** (`nonprofit_organizational_values`, text, required): This field is where you can describe your nonprofit organization's core values that guide its actions and decisions. Please provide a brief overview of your organization's values and their importance in the workplace.
*   **Ethics Principles** (`ethics_principles`, select_one, required): Select the principle that best describes your organization's approach to ethics, such as Principle A, Principle B, or Principle C.
*   **Accountability Measures** (`accountability_measures`, select_multiple, required): Choose the measures that your organization takes to ensure accountability, such as Measure X, Measure Y, or Measure Z.
*   **Transparency and Disclosure** (`transparency_and_disclosure`, text, required): In this field, please provide details on your organization's transparency and disclosure policies, including how you ensure that stakeholders have access to accurate and timely information.
*   **Stakeholder Expectations** (`stakeholder_expectations`, text, optional): Describe the expectations you have from your stakeholders, such as funders, grantees, or community members.
*   **Organizational Accountability** (`organizational_accountability`, text, optional): Explain the measures your organization takes to hold itself accountable, including how you monitor and evaluate progress towards your goals and objectives.

## Tips

*   Take your time to complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
*   Be honest and transparent in your answers.
*   If you're unsure about any question, don't hesitate to seek guidance.
*   Review the form periodically to track your organization's progress over time.
*   Use this form as a tool to facilitate self-reflection and improvement within the organization.
