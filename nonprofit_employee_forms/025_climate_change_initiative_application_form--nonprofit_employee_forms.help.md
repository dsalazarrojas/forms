# Climate Change Initiative Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Climate Change Initiative Application Form is designed to help organizations apply for funding to support their climate change-related projects. This form is intended for organizations that wish to collaborate with us on climate change initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your organization's information, including its name, type, and tax ID or registration number.
2. Provide a detailed description of your project, including its title, description, and expected outcomes and metrics.
3. Specify the project's start and end dates, as well as its total budget.
4. Select the primary climate focus area of your project.
5. Identify the target population or communities that will benefit from your project.
6. Choose the geographic scope of your project.
7. Specify the type of support you are requesting, such as financial, technical, or training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (Required, Text): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Organization Type** (Required, Select One):
	+ Nonprofit
	+ Government
	+ Academic
	+ Business
	+ Community Group
	+ Other
* **Tax ID or Registration Number** (Required, Text): Enter your organization's tax ID or registration number.
* **Organization Website** (Optional, Text): Enter your organization's website URL.
* **Contact Person** (Required, Text): Enter the name of the person responsible for this project.
* **Email Address** (Required, Email): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone Number** (Required, Text): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Project Title** (Required, Text): Enter a brief title of your project.
* **Detailed Project Description** (Required, Text): Describe your project in detail, answering the question "What is your climate initiative about?"
* **Primary Climate Focus Area** (Required, Select One):
	+ Mitigation
	+ Adaptation
	+ Resilience
	+ Advocacy
	+ Education
	+ Technology
	+ Other
* **Target Population or Communities** (Required, Text): Identify the population or communities that will benefit from your project.
* **Geographic Scope** (Required, Select One):
	+ Local
	+ Regional
	+ National
	+ International
* **Expected Outcomes and Metrics** (Required, Text): Specify measurable goals and outcomes for your project.
* **Project Start Date** (Required, Date): Enter the start date of your project.
* **Project End Date** (Required, Date): Enter the end date of your project.
* **Total Project Budget** (Required, Number): Enter the total budget for your project.
* **Currency** (Optional, Text): If your budget is in a different currency, please specify it here.
* **Funding Amount Requested** (Required, Number): Enter the amount of funding you are requesting.
* **Type of Support Needed** (Optional, Select Multiple):
	+ Financial
	+ Technical
	+ Training
	+ Networking
	+ Marketing
	+ Other

Please note that all fields marked as "Required" are mandatory and must be filled in.
