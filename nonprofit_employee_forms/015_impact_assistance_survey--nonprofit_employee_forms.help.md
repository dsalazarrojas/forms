# impact_assistance_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from participants about the impact of a program or aid provided by the organization. The goal is to gather information on participant's satisfaction and rating of the program or aid.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief participant feedback about the program or aid.
2. Rate the outcome of the program or aid using the "Aid Outcome" and "Participant Outcome" questions.
3. Rate your satisfaction with the program or aid using the "Participant Satisfaction" and "Program Rating" questions.
4. Provide a rating and comments about the aid outcome.
5. Rate the participant outcome and provide comments.
6. Rate the non-participant outcome.
7. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Feedback**: A brief description of your experience with the program or aid.
  (Type: Text, Required: False)
- **Aid Outcome**: Rate the outcome of the aid provided by the program.
  (Type: Select One, Options: Positive, Negative, Neutral, Required: False)
- **Program Outcome**: Rate the outcome of the program.
  (Type: Select Multiple, Options: Positive, Negative, Neutral, Required: False)
- **Participant Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with the program or aid.
  (Type: Number, Required: False)
- **Participant Satisfaction Rating**: Rate your satisfaction with the program or aid.
  (Type: Select One, Options: High, Medium, Low, Required: False)
- **Participant Satisfaction Rating Range**: A scale to rate your satisfaction with the program or aid.
  (Type: Number, Required: False)
- **Program Rating**: Rate the program.
  (Type: Number, Required: False)
- **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the program or aid.
  (Type: Select One, Options: High, Low, Required: False)
- **Participant Recommendation**: Recommend the program or aid to others.
  (Type: Select Multiple, Options: True, False, Required: False)
- **Participant Comments**: Any additional comments about the program or aid.
  (Type: Text, Required: False)
- **Aid Outcome Rating**: Rate the outcome of the aid.
  (Type: Select One, Options: High, Low, Required: False)
- **Aid Outcome Rating Range**: A scale to rate the outcome of the aid.
  (Type: Number, Required: False)
- **Aid Outcome Comments**: Any comments about the outcome of the aid.
  (Type: Text, Required: False)
- **Participant Outcome**: Rate the outcome for the participant.
  (Type: Select One, Options: Positive, Negative, Neutral, Required: False)
- **Participant Outcome Rating**: Rate the outcome for the participant.
  (Type: Select One, Options: High, Low, Required: False)
- **Participant Outcome Rating Range**: A scale to rate the outcome for the participant.
  (Type: Number, Required: False)
- **Participant Outcome Comments**: Any comments about the outcome for the participant.
  (Type: Text, Required: False)
- **Non-Participant Outcome**: Rate the outcome for non-participant.
  (Type: Select One, Options: Positive, Negative, Neutral, Required: False)
- **Non-Participant Outcome Rating**: Rate the outcome for non-participant.
  (Type: Select One, Options: High, Low, Required: False)
- **Non-Participant Outcome Rating Range**: A scale to rate the outcome for non-participant.
  (Type: Number, Required: False)
- **Non-Participant Outcome Comments**: Any comments about the outcome for non-participant.
  (Type: Text, Required: False)

## Tips
- Please answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
- If you are unsure about any question, you can skip it.
- If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the "Participant Comments" field.
