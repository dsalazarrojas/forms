# Car Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from donors who want to donate their vehicles to the nonprofit organization. It helps us process the donation efficiently and ensures a smooth transaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name, email address, and phone number in the "Donor Information" section.
2. Provide your home address in the "Home Address" field.
3. Describe your vehicle in the "Car Make and Model" field.
4. Enter the year of manufacture and Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) in the corresponding fields.
5. Select the condition of your vehicle from the options provided.
6. Enter the current mileage on your vehicle.
7. Choose the type of donation you're making (Full Donation, Partial Donation, or Other).
8. Estimate the value of your donation (if you're aware of it).
9. Specify the purpose of your donation.
10. Read and agree to the terms of service.
11. Sign with your name to confirm the donation.
12. Add any additional comments or requests in the "Additional Comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Donor Information
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as you'd like it to appear on the donation receipt.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for donation receipt delivery.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for further discussion on the donation details.
* **Home Address**: Enter your mailing address for the donation process.
* **Car Make and Model**: Describe your vehicle, including the make and model.
* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of manufacture of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)**: Enter the 17-character VIN code of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Condition**: Select the condition of your vehicle from the options provided.
* **Vehicle Odometer Reading**: Enter the current mileage on your vehicle.
* **Type of Donation**: Choose the type of donation you're making (Full Donation, Partial Donation, or Other).
* **Estimated Donation Value**: Enter the estimated value of your vehicle, if you know it.
* **Purpose of Donation**: Specify the purpose of your donation.
* **I acknowledge the donation terms**: Choose "Yes, I agree" to confirm you understand the terms of service.
* **Donor's Signature**: Type your name for donation confirmation.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or requests related to the donation.
