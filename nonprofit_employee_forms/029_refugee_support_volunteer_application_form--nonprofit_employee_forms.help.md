# Refugee Support Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from potential volunteers who are interested in supporting refugee families. The purpose of this form is to help the organization to assess the volunteer's qualifications and fit for various volunteer positions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the Volunteer Information section with your name, contact preferences (e.g. email, phone number), and availability.
2. Choose your volunteer shifts (e.g. morning, afternoon, evening).
3. Select your skills that you are proficient in (e.g. language, translation, interpretation, communication).
4. Choose your volunteer role (e.g. receptionist, translator, interpreter).
5. Indicate your level of English language proficiency (e.g. native, fluent, basic).
6. If applicable, provide information about your volunteer experience, education, and references.
7. Review and agree to the terms and conditions of volunteering.
