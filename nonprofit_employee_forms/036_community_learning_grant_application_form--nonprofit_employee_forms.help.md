# Community Learning Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for your organization to apply for a community learning grant. We use this form to collect information about your organization and the grant details so we can review your application.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form instructions and guidelines carefully before starting.
- Ensure you have all necessary information available, including the full legal name of your organization, tax ID or EIN, primary contact person, and contact email address.
- Answer each question thoroughly, providing detailed and accurate information about your organization and grant proposal.
- Review and edit your form before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Legal Name of Organization (required)**: This is the full name of your organization as it is listed in government records.
* **Federal Tax ID or EIN (required)**: This is the number assigned to your organization by the Internal Revenue Service (IRS).
* **Primary Project Contact Person (required)**: This is the person primarily responsible for this grant application.
* **Contact Email Address (required)**: This is the email address of the contact person where we can reach you for further information.
* **Title of the Community Learning Program (required)**: This is the title of the program you are proposing for funding.
* **Total Grant Amount Requested (required)**: This is the total amount of money you are requesting for the grant.
* **Total Program Budget (required)**: This is the total budget for the program, including other funding sources.
* **Primary Target Audience (required)**: Choose the most relevant target audience for your program (K-12 Students, Adults (Career Development), Senior Citizens, Professional Development, Under-served or Vulnerable Communities).
* **Comprehensive Description of Learning Program (required)**: This is a detailed description of the learning program you are proposing, including what will be taught and how.
* **Expected Learning Outcomes (required)**: This is the list of skills or knowledge that participants are expected to gain from the program.
* **Impact Measurement and Evaluation Strategy (required)**: This is how you plan to track the success of the program.
* **Authorized Representative Signature (required)**: This is the signature of the person authorized to sign on behalf of your organization.
* **Date of Grant Application (required)**: This is the date you submit this form.

Note: The date of submission will be automatically filled in once you submit the form.
