# Contact Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from individuals and organizations. The purpose of this form is to gather data for communication and potential partnership purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. This information will be used as the primary contact method.
3. (Optional) Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Enter the name of your organization or nonprofit in the "Organization Name" field.
5. Enter your current job title or position in the "Job Title" field.
6. Enter your mailing address in the "Mailing Address" field.
7. Enter your city in the "City" field.
8. Enter your state or province in the "State or Province" field.
9. Enter your ZIP or postal code in the "ZIP or Postal Code" field.
10. Select your country of residence from the "Country" dropdown list.
11. Choose your preferred contact method from the "Preferred Contact Method" dropdown list.
12. Select the purpose of your contact information from the "Purpose of Contact Information" dropdown list.
13. If desired, add any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.
14. Check the box to consent to receive communications from this organization in the "Consent to Receive Communications" field.
15. Date of submission will be automatically filled in the "Date Submitted" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): We will use this to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Include area code.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, optional): Name of your nonprofit or organization.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Your current position or role.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, `text`, optional): Street address, P.O. box, or physical address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): null
* **State or Province** (`state_province`, `text`, required): null
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`zip_postal_code`, `text`, required): null
* **Country** (`country`, `select_one`, required): Select your country of residence.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`contact_preference`, `select_one`, required): How would you like us to contact you?
* **Purpose of Contact Information** (`purpose_of_contact`, `select_one`, required): Why are you providing your contact information?
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Any other information you would like to share.
* **Consent to Receive Communications** (`consent_to_communicate`, `select_one`, required): Check to agree to receive communications from this organization.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, `date`, required): Date of submission will be automatically filled.
