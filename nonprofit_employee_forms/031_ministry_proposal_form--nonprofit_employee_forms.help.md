# Ministry Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ministry Proposal Form is a tool used to provide a clear and organized framework for submitting proposals for new ministries to be implemented within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the details about the ministry you are proposing, including its title.
2. Enter the name of the ministry.
3. Provide a brief description of the ministry.
4. Identify the goal of the ministry.
5. Specify the target audience for the ministry.
6. Enter the budget for the ministry (optional).
7. Enter the funding information for the ministry (optional).
8. Enter the timeline for the ministry, including the start and end dates.
9. Specify the duration of the ministry.
10. Select the current status of the ministry.
11. Enter the location of the ministry (optional).
12. Enter the address of the ministry (optional).
13. Enter the contact information for the ministry, including name, email, and phone number (optional).
14. Enter the extension of the contact person (optional).
15. Enter the location of the contact person (optional).
16. Upload any additional file or documentation required for the ministry (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Ministry Proposal Details**: Enter a brief description of the ministry you are proposing.
* **Ministry Name**: Enter the name of the ministry.
* **Ministry Description**: Provide a brief description of the ministry.
* **Ministry Goal**: Enter the goal of the ministry.
* **Target Audience**: Specify the target audience for the ministry.
* **Target Audience 1**: This field is likely intended to be an additional field for specifying the target audience.
* **Ministry Budget**: Enter the budget for the ministry.
* **Ministry Funding**: Enter the funding information for the ministry.
* **Ministry Timeline**: Select the start and end dates for the ministry.
* **Ministry Duration**: Enter the duration of the ministry.
* **Ministry Status**: Select the current status of the ministry.
* **Ministry Location**: Enter the location of the ministry.
* **Ministry Address**: Enter the address of the ministry.
* **Ministry Contact**: Enter the contact information for the ministry.
* **Ministry Contact Email**: Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Ministry Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Ministry Contact Extension**: Enter the extension of the contact person.
* **Ministry Contact Location**: Enter the location of the contact person.
* **Min 21**: This field appears to be a placeholder for additional information.
* **File Upload**: Upload any additional file or documentation required for the ministry.
