# Ministry Partnership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ministry Partnership Form is used to document and track partnerships between the ministry and external organizations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Ministry Partnership Form Organization**: Enter the name of the partner's organization.
* **Ministry Partnership Form Name**: Enter the name of the partner's name.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the partnership.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the partnership.
* **Email**: Enter the partner's email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the partner's phone number.
* **Relationship**: Select the type of relationship between the ministry and partner (Partner, Collaborator, Donor).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the partnership (optional).
* **Agreement Date**: Enter the date of the agreement.
* **Agreement Time**: Enter the time of the agreement.
* **Agreement Location**: Enter the location of the agreement.
* **Agreement Details**: Enter any additional details about the agreement.
* **Agreement Type**: Select the type of agreement (General Agreement, Exclusive Agreement).
* **Agreement Status**: Select the status of the agreement (Active, Inactive, Pending).
* **Agreement Terms**: Enter any additional terms or conditions of the agreement.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Enter any additional terms and conditions of the partnership.
* **Termination Date**: Enter the date of termination (if applicable).
* **Termination Reason**: Enter the reason for termination (if applicable).
* **Termination Agreement**: Enter any agreement or agreement details related to termination (if applicable).
* **Renewal Date**: Enter the date of renewal (if applicable).
* **Renewal Agreement**: Enter any agreement or agreement details related to renewal (if applicable).
* **Agreement End Date**: Enter the date the agreement ends (if applicable).
* **Agreement End Status**: Select the status of the agreement end (Active, Inactive).
* **Review Date**: Enter the date for review and evaluation (if applicable).
