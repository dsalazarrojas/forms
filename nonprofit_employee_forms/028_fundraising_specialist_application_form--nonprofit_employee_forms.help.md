# Fundraising Specialist Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Fundraising Specialist Application Form is designed to collect information from interested individuals about their background, skills, and experience related to fundraising. This form will help our organization assess each candidate's suitability for a fundraising role.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out each section thoroughly**: Please answer each question as accurately and completely as possible.
2. **Be honest**: Only answer questions truthfully, and do not provide false or misleading information.
3. **Use clear and concise language**: Type your answers clearly and in complete sentences.
4. **Attach your resume and cover letter**: Upload your resume and a brief cover letter explaining why you are the best fit for this role.
