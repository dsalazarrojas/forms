# Social Impact Mentor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals to apply to become a social impact mentor in a nonprofit organization. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the applicant's personal details, mentorship goals and commitment, and availability for mentoring.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your first name, last name, and email address in the required fields.
2. Enter your phone number and position in the organization you are associated with.
3. Provide your years of experience in the field you are applying to mentor in.
4. Describe your educational background in the "Education" field.
5. Enter the name of the organization you are associated with.
6. State your mentorship goals and what you hope to achieve as a mentor.
7. Indicate your availability for mentoring, including any available hours per week and days per month.
8. Confirm your commitment to the mentorship program, specifying the number of hours you are willing to commit.
9. Select a start date for your anticipated start as a mentor.
10. Finally, provide any additional notes about your availability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your first name, which is a required field.
  - **Last Name**: Enter your last name, which is also a required field.
  - **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you, which is a required field.
  - **Phone**: Enter your phone number, which will help us contact you, but is not the only way we communicate. This field is required.
  - **Position**: Enter your position within your organization, which will help us understand your role and responsibilities. This field is required.
  - **Years of Experience**: Enter the number of years you have spent in your field, which is required to understand your level of expertise. This field is required.
  - **Education**: Describe your educational background, including any relevant certifications or degrees. This field is required.
  - **Organization**: Enter the name of the organization you are associated with, which is required to understand your connection to the nonprofit sector. This field is required.
  - **Mentorship Goals**: State your goals for the mentorship program, including what you hope to achieve and why. This field is required.
  - **Availability**: Specify how many hours per week and days per month you are able to dedicate to mentoring. This field is required.
  - **Mentorship Commitment**: Confirm the number of hours you are willing to commit to the mentorship program. This field is required.
  - **Available Hours**: Confirm your availability per week, which is a required field used to match you with mentees.
  - **Anticipated Start Date**: Select a start date for your anticipated start as a mentor, which will help us schedule your mentorship sessions. This field is required.
  - **Availability Notes**: Provide any additional notes about your availability, which is a required field to ensure we can accommodate your schedule.
- **Availability**: Note that this field is asking for a general availability, not a specific schedule.
- **Available Hours**: This field is asking for the number of hours you are available to mentor each week. 
  - **Anticipated Start Date**: This field is asking for your earliest start date for mentoring, not necessarily the first date you can start.
