# Girl Scout Uniform Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from Girl Scouts to fulfill their uniform orders. Please complete the form to help us deliver your uniforms efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your name and contact information in the required fields.
2. Choose your delivery date and time preferences.
3. Select the page you would like to receive your uniform on.
4. Pick your preferred selection option.
5. Enter your Date of Birth.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Girl Scout Uniform Order Form** (`form_title`, text, not required): This field is the title of the form, and you can ignore it as it's only for reference.
* **Full Name** (`form_name`, text, not required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Email** (`form_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address for us to contact you about your order.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`form_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you about your order.
* **Delivery Date** (`form_delivery_date`, date, not required): Choose the date you prefer to receive your uniform.
* **Delivery Time** (`form_delivery_time`, time, not required): Select the time you prefer to receive your uniform.
* **Select a Page** (`form_page`, select_one, not required): Choose the page you would like to receive your uniform on.
* **Select** (`form_select`, select_one, not required): Pick one of the available options for your uniform.
* **Date of Birth** (`form_date`, date, not required): Enter your Date of Birth for identification purposes.

## Tips
- Please enter accurate and complete information for a successful order fulfillment.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
- We will contact you with any updates about your order.
