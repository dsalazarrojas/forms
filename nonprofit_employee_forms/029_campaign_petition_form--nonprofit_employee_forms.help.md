# Campaign Petition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information about a petition and its campaign details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about your petition.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed.
3. Select the correct options for each field as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Petitioner First Name** (`petitioner_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Petitioner Last Name** (`petitioner_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Street Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your street address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter your city or town.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Country** (`country`, select_one, required): Select your country of residence from the options provided.
* **Petition Title** (`petition_title`, text, required): Enter a name for your petition.
* **Petition Category** (`petition_category`, select_one, required): Select a category for your petition from the options provided.
* **Who Is the Target of This Petition** (`target_audience`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable targets for your petition.
* **Petition Summary** (`petition_summary`, text, required): Briefly describe your petition in 2-3 sentences.
* **Tell the Full Story** (`petition_story`, text, required): Explain why this issue is important and how it affects you.
* **What Is Your Specific Demand or Request** (`petition_demand`, text, required): Be specific about what you are asking for.
* **Signature Goal** (`petition_goal`, select_one, required): Select the target number of signatures for your petition.
* **Deadline for Signatures** (`petition_deadline`, date, optional): Enter the target date for collecting signatures.
* **What Is Your Personal Connection to This Issue** (`personal_connection`, text, required): Explain your personal connection to this issue.
* **Are Any Organizations Supporting This Petition** (`supporting_organizations`, text, optional): List any organizations supporting your petition.
* **Has This Issue Received Media Coverage** (`media_coverage`, select_one, optional): Indicate if the issue has received media coverage.
* **What Actions Have You Already Taken** (`previous_actions`, text, optional): Describe any previous efforts you've made to address this issue.
* **What Actions Do You Plan to Take** (`planned_actions`, text, optional): Outline future plans to address this issue.
* **Do You Have an Image for Your Petition** (`petition_image`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you have an image for your petition.
* **How Do You Plan to Share This Petition** (`share_plan`, select_multiple, required): Select all methods you plan to use to share your petition.
* **I Accept the Terms of Service** (`terms_accepted`, select_one, required): Acknowledge that you accept the terms of service.
* **Your Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Type your full name.
* **Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date you signed.
