# Donation Refund Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donation Refund Claim Form is used to claim a refund for a donation that should not have been made or for which a credit or adjustment is needed.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your User Name as it appears on your donation receipt or confirmation email.
2. Select the date of your donation from the calendar view.
3. Enter the exact amount of the donation you would like to claim a refund for.
4. Select the reason for your refund from the pre-populated options or choose the "Other" option to provide a custom reason for your refund.
5. Enter any additional information you'd like to provide about your reason for the refund in the Description field.
6. Enter your contact email address so we can get in touch with you.
7. Enter your contact phone number (optional, but recommended for faster communication).
8. Enter your contact address (this will help us verify your identity).
9. If you selected the "Other" option for the reason for your refund, enter the specific reason in the Select Other Reason field.
10. Upload any supporting documentation or evidence that may be relevant to your refund claim.
11. Click the Submit button to send your refund claim to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your donation receipt or confirmation email.
  * This field is required to ensure we have the correct identification information for you.
- **Donation Date**: Enter the date of your donation.
  * This field is required to ensure we have a record of your donation date.
- **Donation Amount**: Enter the exact amount of the donation you would like to claim a refund for.
  * This field is required to ensure we have accurate information about your donation amount.
- **Reason for Refund**: Select a reason for your refund from the pre-populated options or choose the "Other" option.
  * If you select "Other", you will need to provide additional information in the Select Other Reason field.
- **Reason for Refund Options**:  
  * **No longer need**: You are requesting a refund because you no longer need the donation or the funds were not used as intended.
  * **Duplicate Payment**: You made a mistake and made a double payment.
  * **Error in Donation**: Your donation was made in error (e.g., incorrect payment method, incorrect amount).
  * **Other**: You can select this option and provide a custom reason for your refund in the Select Other Reason field.
- **Select Other Reason**: Provide a specific reason for your refund if you selected the "Other" option.
  * This field is not required, but you will need to enter a reason if you selected "Other".
- **Description**: Enter any additional information you have about your reason for the refund.
  * This field is not required, but it's a good place to provide more context about your refund request.
- **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
  * This field is required to ensure we can contact you to follow up on your refund request.
- **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for faster communication.
  * This field is optional, but recommended.
- **Contact Address**: Enter your address to verify your identity.
  * This field is required to ensure we can verify your identity.
- **Uploaded Document**: If you have a supporting document, such as a receipt or proof of payment, upload it here.
  * This field is optional, but recommended.
- **Submit**: Click this button to send your refund claim to us.
  * This field is required to submit your form.
