# Membership Dues Renewal - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Membership Dues Renewal form is used to collect information from members for renewing their membership dues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your membership information in Section 1: Membership Dues Renewal Group 1.
2. Provide your membership details in Section 2:
	* Enter your Membership ID.
	* Enter your Member Name.
	* Enter your Email address.
	* Enter your Phone number.
	* Select your current Membership Status.
3. Choose your payment method and amount in Section 3:
	* Select the payment method you would like to use.
	* Enter the payment amount.
4. Add any additional comments in Section 4.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Membership Dues Renewal Group 1** (`membership_dues_renewal_page`, text, not required): This is the first section of the form where you can enter any additional information about your membership.
* **Membership ID** (`membership_dues_renewal_membership_id`, select_one, not required): Enter your membership ID to identify yourself as a member.
* **Member Name** (`membership_dues_renewal_member_name`, text, not required): Enter your name as a member.
* **Email** (`membership_dues_renewal_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`membership_dues_renewal_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Membership Status** (`membership_dues_renewal_membership_status`, select_one, not required): Select your current membership status.
* **Payment Method** (`membership_dues_renewal_payment_method`, select_one, not required): Choose the payment method you would like to use for renewal.
* **Payment Amount** (`membership_dues_renewal_payment_amount`, number, not required): Enter the payment amount.
* **Comments** (`membership_dues_renewal_comments`, note, not required): Add any additional comments or notes about your membership renewal.
* **Submit** (`membership_dues_renewal_submit`, text, not required): Click this button to submit your membership renewal information.
