# Board Member Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Board Member Acknowledgment Form is a crucial document used by the nonprofit organization to gather acknowledgment from its Board Members regarding their roles, responsibilities, and potential conflicts of interest. This form ensures that all Board Members have reviewed and acknowledged their duties and have not misrepresented their interests, maintaining transparency and accountability within the Board.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full legal name in the "Board member name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email address" field for organization records.
3. Confirm that you have reviewed your role responsibilities by selecting "True" in the "Responsibilities reviewed" field.
4. If applicable, disclose any conflicts of interest by selecting "True" in the "Conflict of interest disclosed" field.
5. Sign your name in the "Signature name" field, typing your full name to acknowledge.
6. Enter the date of acknowledgment in the "Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Board member name**: Your full legal name as a Board Member of the nonprofit organization.
* **Email address**: Your email address for organization records. Please ensure that this is the email address you use for official communication.
* **Responsibilities reviewed**: Confirm that you have reviewed and understood your role responsibilities as a Board Member. Select "True" if you have reviewed and acknowledged your responsibilities.
* **Conflict of interest disclosed**: If you have any conflicts of interest, please select "True" to disclose them. This is a crucial step to ensure transparency and accountability.
* **Signature name**: Type your full name to acknowledge your acknowledgment of duties and potential conflicts of interest.
* **Date**: Enter the date you are acknowledging your duties and potential conflicts of interest.
