# Donation Pick Up Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a simple and efficient way for non-profit staff members to request the pickup of donations that they have collected. This form helps to streamline the process of arranging for the pickup of donations from non-profits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you would like to donate.
2. Enter the donor's first name.
3. Enter the donor's last name.
4. Enter the donor's email address.
5. Enter the donor's phone number.
6. Select a date for the pickup.
7. Select a time for the pickup.
8. Enter the location where the donation will be picked up.
9. Enter any additional notes or comments about the pickup.
10. Enter the name of the non-profit organization.
11. Enter the address of the non-profit organization.
12. Enter the phone number of the non-profit organization.
13. Enter the email address of the non-profit organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What would you like to donate?**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you would like to donate. 
* **Donor First Name**: Enter the first name of the donor.
* **Donor Last Name**: Enter the last name of the donor.
* **Donor Email**: Enter the email address of the donor.
* **Donor Phone**: Enter the phone number of the donor.
* **Pickup Date**: Select a date for the pickup. This will be the date when the donation will be collected.
* **Pickup Time**: Select a time for the pickup. This will be the time when the donation will be collected.
* **Pickup Location**: Enter the location where the donation will be picked up. This will be the address or location where the staff member will be collecting the donation.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the pickup. This can be any additional information that is relevant to the pickup.
* **Non Profit Name**: Enter the name of the non-profit organization.
* **Non Profit Address**: Enter the address of the non-profit organization.
* **Non Profit Phone**: Enter the phone number of the non-profit organization.
* **Non Profit Email**: Enter the email address of the non-profit organization.
