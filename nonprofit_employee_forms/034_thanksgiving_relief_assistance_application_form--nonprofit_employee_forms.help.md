# Thanksgiving Relief Assistance Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals or families seeking assistance with Thanksgiving-related expenses. It is used to gather necessary contact and identifying information to assess eligibility and provide aid.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you are eligible to apply for Thanksgiving Relief Assistance. This form is for individuals or families who need help with food, clothing, or other essential expenses related to the Thanksgiving holiday.
2. Click on the "Start" button to begin filling out the form.
3. Fill out the "Contact Info" field with your name and address (if you wish to share this information).
4. Provide your name as it appears on your identification, and your current address or the address you would like to receive any aid.
5. Enter your contact phone number to ensure we can reach you if further information is needed.
6. Enter your email address, if you prefer to be contacted via email or have additional information regarding your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info**: If you wish to share your contact information to facilitate communication regarding your application, please enter your name and address here.
* **Name**: Please provide your name as it appears on your identification (e.g. driver's license, passport).
* **Address**: Enter your current address or the address you would like to receive any aid.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can easily contact you.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to receive updates or additional information regarding your application.

## Tips

* Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately to avoid any delays or issues with your application.
* If you need assistance with any field, do not hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your honesty and accuracy when filling out this form.
