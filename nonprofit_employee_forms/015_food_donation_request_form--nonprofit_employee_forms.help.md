# Food Donation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Donation Request Form is used to gather information from nonprofit organizations requesting food donations from suppliers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Food Donation Request Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Company Name** field with your organization's name.
2. Enter your **Contact Name** in the **Contact Name** field.
3. Provide your **Contact Email** address in the **Contact Email** field.
4. Enter your **Company Address** in the **Company Address** field.
5. Enter your **Phone Number** in the **Phone Number** field.
6. Select the type of food you are requesting in the **Food Type** field. You can select one or more options from the list.
7. Enter the quantity of food you are requesting in the **Quantity** field.
8. Choose a date for the desired **Delivery Date**.
9. If you have any additional comments or notes, enter them in the **Message** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (Text): Enter your organization's name.
* **Contact Name** (Text): Enter the name of the person completing this form.
* **Contact Email** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **Company Address** (Text): Enter your organization's address.
* **Phone Number** (Text): Enter your phone number.
* **Food Type** (Select Multiple): Select one or more types of food you are requesting. Options: Fruits, Vegetables, Grains, Dairy, Meat, Canned Goods.
* **Quantity** (Number): Enter the quantity of food you are requesting.
* **Delivery Date** (Date): Choose a date for the desired delivery.
* **Message** (Note): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Company Name (2)** (Text): This field seems to be a duplicate of the **Company Name** field. Please fill it in if required, but be aware that it's redundant with the previous field.
