# Military Families Fund Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Military Families Fund Donation Form is designed to collect information from donors to support the Military Families Fund, a charitable organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the donation amount you wish to contribute.
2. Choose the frequency of your donation (Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
3. Provide your name as the donor.
4. Enter your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount you wish to donate to the Military Families Fund. This field is not required, but entering a value will allow the fund to receive your donation.
  - **Type:** A number field where you can input your donation amount.
  - **Requirements:** This field is not required to be filled in, but a value will enable the donation to be processed.
- **Frequency**: Choose how often you want to make your donation (Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
  - **Type:** Select Multiple field that allows you to choose one or multiple options.
  - **Requirements:** This field is not required to be filled in, and you can choose one or more options.
- **Donor Name**: Enter your name as the donor.
  - **Type:** Text field for you to provide your name.
  - **Requirements:** This field is not required to be filled in.
- **Donor Email**: Enter your email address.
  - **Type:** Email field for you to provide your email.
  - **Requirements:** This field is not required to be filled in, but providing a valid email will enable the fund to contact you.
