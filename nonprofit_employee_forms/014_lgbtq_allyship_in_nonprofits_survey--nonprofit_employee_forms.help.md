# Lgbtq_allyship_in_nonprofits_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lgbtq_allyship_in_nonprofits_survey form is designed to gather information from nonprofit employees about their experiences, perceptions, and suggestions related to LGBTQ+ allyship within the organization. The form helps identify areas of strength and improvement in the current allyship culture and informs the development of strategies to promote allyship.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the category of the form (Nonprofit Employee Forms or Other).
2. Provide a brief description of your experience with LGBTQ+ allyship in the organization.
3. Answer the questions about your involvement in LGBTQ+ allyship trainings, meetings, and events.
4. Rate the current state of LGBTQ+ allyship in the organization.
5. Share any barriers or challenges you experience when promoting LGBTQ+ allyship.
6. Report any negative consequences you've encountered as a result of promoting LGBTQ+ allyship.
7. Specify the resources you would like to have more access to or training on.
8. Suggest ways to improve LGBTQ+ allyship in the organization.
9. Provide feedback on the importance of promoting LGBTQ+ allyship.
10. Share your thoughts on how to improve our Allyship efforts.
11. Specify your role in the organization.
12. Report any positive changes you've seen as a result of our efforts.
13. Identify the most important factors for improving Allyship efforts.
14. Express how important it is for you to see positive changes in our Allyship efforts.
15. Share any other thoughts or comments you have.
16. Specify how long you've been a member of the nonprofit.
17. Indicate whether you want to receive updates on our Allyship efforts.

## Tips
* Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* If you experience any issues or need help completing the form, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
* All feedback and comments are kept confidential and will be used to improve the organization's allyship efforts. 

Note: Please keep your responses brief and to the point. Your input will help us create a more welcoming and inclusive environment for everyone. Thank you for taking the time to complete this form.
