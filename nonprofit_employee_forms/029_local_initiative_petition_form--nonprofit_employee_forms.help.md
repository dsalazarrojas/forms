# local_initiative_petition_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about local initiatives and petitions, allowing individuals to express their support and gather signatures from their community. It helps local communities to voice their opinions and gather support for a specific cause or issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your local initiative or petition.
2. Provide a brief description of your initiative.
3. You may optionally enter the purpose of your initiative.
4. If required, enter the number of signatures you are collecting and the date they were collected.
5. Select the type of petition you are submitting.
6. Enter your contact information.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Optionally, enter your address.
9. Optionally, select your country (if outside of the United States, Canada, or Mexico).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Petition Name** (petition_name, text, required)
	+ Enter a clear and concise name for your local initiative or petition.
* **description** (description, note, required)
	+ Provide a brief description of your local initiative or petition.
* **purpose** (purpose, text, optional)
	+ If applicable, enter the purpose or goal of your local initiative or petition.
* **Number of Signatures** (signature_count, number, optional)
	+ Enter the total number of signatures collected for your local initiative or petition.
* **Date of Signature** (signature_date, date, optional)
	+ Enter the date the signatures were collected.
* **Petition Type** (petition_type, select_one, optional)
	+ Select the type of petition you are submitting (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Contact** (contact, text, optional)
	+ Enter your contact information.
* **Email** (email, email, optional)
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Address** (address, note, optional)
	+ Enter your address.
* **zip_code** (zip_code, number, optional)
	+ Enter your zip code.
* **City** (city, text, optional)
	+ Enter the city associated with your address.
* **State** (state, text, optional)
	+ Enter the state associated with your address.
* **Country** (country, select_one, optional)
	+ Select your country (United States, Canada, or Mexico).
