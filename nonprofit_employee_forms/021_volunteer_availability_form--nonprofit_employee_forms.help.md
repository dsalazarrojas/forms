# Volunteer Availability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from volunteers about their availability to work. The purpose of this form is to help volunteers share their availability so that the organization can better manage volunteer shifts and schedules.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Volunteer Form Data**: This field is not required, but it's an optional description of the volunteer's data.
2. **Volunteer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
3. **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
4. **Email Address**: Your email address where you can be contacted.
5. **Availability Date Range**: Select the dates you are available to work.
6. **Availability Days of Week**: Select the days of the week you are available to work. (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday)
7. **Availability Time Slots**: Select the time slots you are available to work. (morning, afternoon, evening)
8. **Availability Time Slots Duration**: Please enter the duration of your time slots. For example, 2 hours or 3 hours.
9. **Hours Per Week**: The number of hours you are willing to work per week.
10. **Availability Time Slots Start Time**: Please enter the start time of your time slots. For example, 09:00 or 12:00.
11. **Availability Time Slots End Time**: Please enter the end time of your time slots. For example, 12:00 or 15:00.
12. **Additional Comments**: Any additional comments or information about your availability.
