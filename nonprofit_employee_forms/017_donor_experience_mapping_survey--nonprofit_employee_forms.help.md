# Donor Experience Mapping Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather your feedback and preferences for communication and feedback. Your input will help us tailor our services to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your preferred method of communication (phone, email, or mail).
2. Choose how often you would like to be contacted (e.g., never, less than once a week, almost every day).
3. Select the type of feedback you would like to give (general, specific, personal).
4. Choose the topics you would like to give feedback on (donor experience, donation, communication).
5. Select your preferred method of communication for feedback (phone, email, or mail).
6. Provide open-ended feedback on the donor experience, challenges, and suggestions for improvement.
7. Add any additional comments or suggestions.
