# donation_request_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The donation request communication form is used to collect information about a donation request for communication and record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide a clear and concise title** for the donation request form.
2. **Enter the donation date** in the format of DD/MM/YYYY.
3. **Describe the donation request** in the field provided.
4. **Enter the name of the donor** who made the donation request.
5. **Specify the donation amount**, which should include any applicable details about the donation amount.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **donation_request_form** (`donation_request_form`, text, required/optional): This is the title field for the donation request. Please enter a brief but descriptive title for the form.
* **donation_date** (`donation_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the donation request was made in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **donation_description** (`donation_description`, text, required/optional): Provide a clear and detailed description of the donation request in this field.
* **donor_name** (`donor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the donor who made the donation request.
* **donation_amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount of the donation, including any relevant details about the amount.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with a team member or the form creator for clarification.
- This form is for internal use only, please do not share sensitive information without permission.
