# Church Conference Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2. For select multiple fields, select all applicable options.
3. For select one fields, choose only one option.
4. Enter your full name as it should appear on your badge.
5. Enter your email address to receive confirmation and updates.
6. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
7. Enter your church or organization name and address.
8. Select your primary role in the church.
9. Choose the days you plan to attend.
10. Select your top 3 workshop preferences.
11. Choose your meal plan.
12. If necessary, list any dietary restrictions or allergies.
13. Indicate if you need accommodation recommendations or have your own arrangements.
14. Enter the number of people from your church attending.
15. Select your conference t-shirt size.
16. Choose how you will pay your registration fee.
17. If necessary, indicate if you need special accommodations (e.g., accessibility, childcare).
18. Choose if you need transportation from the airport.
19. Indicate your interest in networking sessions.
20. Enter any additional comments or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (*): Enter your full name as it should appear on your badge.
* **Email Address** (*): Enter your email address to receive confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (*): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Church or Organization Name** (*): Enter your church or organization name.
* **Church Address** (*): Enter your church or organization address.
* **Your Role in the Church** (*): Select your primary role in the church.
* **Which Days Will You Attend** (*): Select all days you plan to attend.
* **Workshop Preferences** (*): Select your top 3 workshop preferences.
* **Meal Plan Selection** (*): Choose your meal plan (full meal plan, lunch only, dinner only, or no meals needed).
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**: If necessary, list any dietary restrictions or allergies.
* **Do You Need Accommodation Recommendations**: Indicate if you need accommodation recommendations or have your own arrangements.
* **How Many People from Your Church Will Attend** (*): Enter the number of people from your church attending, including yourself.
* **Conference T-Shirt Size** (*): Select your conference t-shirt size.
* **Registration Fee Payment** (*): Choose how you will pay your registration fee.
* **Any Special Accommodations Needed** : If necessary, indicate if you need special accommodations (e.g., accessibility, childcare).
* **Do You Need Transportation from Airport**: Choose if you need transportation from the airport.
* **Are You Interested in Networking Sessions**: Indicate your interest in networking sessions.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** : Enter any additional comments or questions.
