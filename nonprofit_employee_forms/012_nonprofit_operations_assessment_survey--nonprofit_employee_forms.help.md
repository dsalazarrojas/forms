# Nonprofit Operations Assessment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Nonprofit Operations Assessment Survey is a set of questions designed to help us understand your nonprofit organization's current operations and assess your progress towards meeting our requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2.  Enter your responses in the space provided for each question.
3.  Submit your completed form when you have answered all the questions.
