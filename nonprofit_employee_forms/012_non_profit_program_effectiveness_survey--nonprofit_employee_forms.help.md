# non_profit_program_effectiveness_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The non_profit_program_effectiveness_survey is a form designed to collect information on the effectiveness of non-profit programs. It is intended for non-profit program staff and administrators to provide insights on program impact and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read through the entire form to understand the questions and fields.
2. Answer each question carefully and accurately to provide a comprehensive picture of your program's effectiveness.
3. Ensure that you complete all required fields and provide relevant information.
4. Review your answers and make any necessary corrections before submitting the form.
