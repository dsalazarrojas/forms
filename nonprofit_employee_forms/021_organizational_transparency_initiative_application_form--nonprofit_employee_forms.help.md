# Organizational Transparency Initiative Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organizational Transparency Initiative Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants to the Organizational Transparency Initiative. This form helps the program administrators to evaluate the applicant's organization and ensure it meets the necessary criteria for participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and the name of your organization.
2. Enter your contact email address and phone number.
3. Provide your organization's street address, city, and state.
4. Enter your zip code.
5. Select the type of organization you are applying as (Public Institution, Nonprofit, or Company).
6. Choose the programs your organization would like to apply for (Transparency, Accountability, or Sustainability).
7. Click on "Submit" to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required: false): Enter your organization's full name as it appears on your business documents.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter your official email address associated with your organization.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your official phone number associated with your organization.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, text, required: false): Enter your organization's street address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required: false): Enter the city where your organization is located.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, required: false): Select the state where your organization is located.
	+ Options: California, Florida, Texas
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required: false): Enter your organization's zip code.
* **Application Date** (`date_application`, date, required: false): Enter the date you are applying for the program.
* **Application Time** (`time_application`, time, required: false): Enter the time you would like to apply for the program.
* **Application Type** (`application_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of organization you are applying as (Public Institution, Nonprofit, or Company).
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the programs you would like to apply for (Transparency, Accountability, or Sustainability).
	+ Options: Transparency, Accountability, Sustainability

# Note: Please ensure to fill in all required fields to avoid incomplete application submissions. If you have any issues or questions, contact us at [contact email address].
