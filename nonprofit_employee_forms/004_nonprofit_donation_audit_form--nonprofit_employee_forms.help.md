# nonprofit_donation_audit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The nonprofit_donation_audit_form is used to record and verify non-profit donations for auditing and reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the donation date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
2. Enter the donation amount received.
3. Provide the name of the donor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Date** (required): The date the donation was received. Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Donation Amount** (required): The amount of the donation received. This field is for financial reporting purposes.
* **Donor Name** (required): The name of the individual or organization making the donation. Please ensure to spell their name correctly.

## Tips
- Double-check the accuracy of the information provided before submitting the form.
- Ensure that the form is filled out completely and correctly to avoid errors and delays in processing.
- If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please reach out to the relevant authorities for clarification.
