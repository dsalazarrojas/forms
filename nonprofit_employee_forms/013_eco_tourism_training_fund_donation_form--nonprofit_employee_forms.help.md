# eco_tourism_training_fund_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The eco_tourism_training_fund_donation_form is designed to collect information from potential donors about their intention to donate to the nonprofit's eco-tourism training fund. The form is used to gather details about the donor's information, donation details, payment information, and non-profit information to ensure smooth and secure processing of the donation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **donor_info** field with your name and any other relevant contact details.
2. Provide detailed information about your **donation_details** in the designated field.
3. In the **payment_info** field, select one of the options:
	* 'Yes' to indicate you are ready to proceed with the payment.
	* 'No' to indicate that you are not ready to make a payment at this time.
4. If you are ready to proceed with payment, please provide the non-profit **non_profit_info** field with any relevant information about your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **donor_info**
• **Donor Information** (`donor_info`, text, required/optional): Fill in your name and any other contact details that you feel are necessary for our records.
### **donation_details**
• **Donation Details** (`donation_details`, text, required/optional): Provide detailed information about your donation, including the amount, any specific causes or projects within the eco-tourism training fund that you are interested in supporting.
### **payment_info**
• **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, select_multiple, required): Select one of the options:
	+ 'Yes' to confirm that you are ready to proceed with the payment.
	+ 'No' to indicate that you are not ready to make a payment at this time.
### **non_profit_info**
• **Non-Profit Information** (`non_profit_info`, text, required/optional): If you have selected 'Yes' in the **payment_info** field, please provide any relevant information about your donation to help us process it smoothly.
 
## Tips
- Please fill in all the required fields to avoid any issues with your donation processing.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at your earliest convenience.
