# Emergency Rental Assistance Program Eligibility Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Emergency Rental Assistance Program (ERAP) provides financial assistance to households in need of emergency rental assistance. This form helps to determine if a household is eligible for this program based on their income, expenses, rental history, and other factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the Non Profit Form Data section with information about your household, including its size and income. Please ensure that the information is accurate and up-to-date.
2.  In the Contact Information section, enter your contact details so that we can reach out to you with further instructions or updates.
3.  Answer the Emergency Rental Assistance Program Eligibility question, indicating whether you are eligible or not.
4.  If eligible, fill in the Emergency Rental Program Services section with the services you are requesting.
5.  In the Household Size and Household Income and Expenses sections, enter the number of members in your household and their respective income and expenses.
6.  In the Rental History and Rental Program Services sections, indicate whether you have had previous rental agreements and which services you are requesting.
7.  Finally, fill in the Household Member 1, 2, 3, and 4 sections with information about each household member. Please include their name, date of birth, and ID number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
