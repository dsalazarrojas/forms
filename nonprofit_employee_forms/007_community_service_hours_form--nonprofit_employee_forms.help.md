# Community Service Hours Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track the hours spent on community service activities by volunteers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name in the "Participant full name" field.
2. If you have a participant ID or reference number, enter it in the "Participant ID or reference" field. This is helpful for identification purposes.
3. Enter the date of the volunteer activity in the "Service date" field.
4. Enter the start time of the activity in the "Start time" field.
5. Enter the end time of the activity in the "End time" field.
6. Enter the total hours completed in the "Total hours completed" field. You can use decimal points for accuracy.
7. Provide a brief description of the tasks performed during the activity in the "Activity description" field.
8. If you are a supervisor, sign in the "Supervisor name for verification" field to verify the hours.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant full name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as per the legal documents.
* **Participant ID or reference** (`participant_id`, `text`, optional): If you have a school or case number, enter it for identification purposes.
* **Service date** (`service_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the volunteer activity.
* **Start time** (`start_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the start time of the activity.
* **End time** (`end_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the end time of the activity.
* **Total hours completed** (`total_hours`, `number`, required): Enter the total hours completed in decimal format.
* **Activity description** (`activity_description`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief summary of tasks performed during the activity.
* **Supervisor name for verification** (`supervisor_signature`, `text`, optional): If you are a supervisor, sign here to verify the hours.
