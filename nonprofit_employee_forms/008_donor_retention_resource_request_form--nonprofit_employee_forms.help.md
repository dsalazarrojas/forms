# Donor Retention Resource Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Retention Resource Request Form is a tool designed to help donors request various resources from the nonprofit organization. This form collects information about the donor's needs, allowing the nonprofit to allocate resources effectively and respond to the requests in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your Name in the "Name" field.
2.  Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
3.  Specify the type of resource you are requesting in the "Type of Resource" field.
4.  Choose your preferred contact method in the "Contact Preference" field.
5.  Enter the specific Resource Request you have in mind in the "Resource Request" field (optional).
6.  Select the date and time you need the resource by filling in the "Date" and "Time" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Name (1)
*   **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents or business cards.

### Email (2)
*   **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.

### Resource Requested (3)
*   **Resource Requested**: Describe the specific resource you are requesting, such as financial support, volunteer opportunities, or expert advice.

### Type of Resource (6)
*   **Type of Resource**: Select the type of resource you are requesting, such as Financial Support, Volunteer Support, or Expert Advice.

### Contact Preference (7)
*   **Contact Preference**: Choose how you would like to be contacted, such as Phone, Email, or Mail.

### Date (9)
*   **Date**: Select the date you need the resource by.

### Time (10)
*   **Time**: Select the time you need the resource by.

### Submit (11)
*   **Submit**: Once you have completed the form, select the "Submit Request" option to send your request to the nonprofit.
