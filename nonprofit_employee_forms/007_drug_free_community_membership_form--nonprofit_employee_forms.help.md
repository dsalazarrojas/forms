# Drug Free Community Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help organizations seeking to join our Drug Free Community become official members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the required fields and any optional fields that you feel are relevant to your coalition. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **coalition_name** (`coalition_name`, text, required): Fill in the name of your coalition.
* **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, optional): Provide the first name of the contact person.
* **Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, required): Provide the last name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): This must be a valid email address. This field will be used to contact you for membership updates.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Provide the phone number of the contact person.
* **Member First Name** (`member_first_name`, text, required): Fill in the first name of the member joining the coalition.
* **Member Last Name** (`member_last_name`, text, required): Fill in the last name of the member joining the coalition.
* **Member Email** (`member_email`, email, required): This must be a valid email address. This field will be used to contact the member for membership updates.
* **Member Commitments** (`member_commitments`, text, required): Briefly describe the member's commitments to the coalition.
* **Coalition Address** (`coalition_address`, text, required): Provide the address of the coalition.
* **Coalition Website** (`coalition_website`, text, optional): Provide the website of the coalition. 
* **Coalition Facebook** (`coalition_facebook`, text, optional): Provide the Facebook account of the coalition (if applicable).
* **Coalition Twitter** (`coalition_twitter`, text, optional): Provide the Twitter account of the coalition (if applicable).
* **Coalition Instagram** (`coalition_instagram`, text, optional): Provide the Instagram account of the coalition (if applicable).
* **Coalition LinkedIn** (`coalition_linkedin`, text, optional): Provide the LinkedIn account of the coalition (if applicable).
* **Coalition YouTube** (`coalition_youtube`, text, optional): Provide the YouTube account of the coalition (if applicable).
* **Coalition Purpose** (`coalition_purpose`, text, optional): Briefly describe the purpose of the coalition.
* **Coalition Mission** (`coalition_mission`, text, optional): Briefly describe the mission of the coalition.
* **Coalition Vision** (`coalition_vision`, text, optional): Briefly describe the vision of the coalition.
* **Coalition Core Values** (`coalition_core_values`, text, optional): List the core values of the coalition.
* **Coalition Leadership Team** (`coalition_leadership_team`, text, optional): Provide the names of the leaders in the coalition.
* **Coalition Committee Members** (`coalition_committee_members`, select multiple, required): Select all the committee members that apply from the dropdown menu.
* **Member Dues** (`member_dues`, number, optional): If applicable, fill in the member dues for the member.
* **Member Status** (`member_status`, select one, required): Select the correct status of the member from the dropdown menu:
	+ Active member
	+ Lapsed member
	+ Onboarding member

## Tips
This form is used to collect accurate and complete information. Make sure to fill it in carefully to ensure your membership application is processed smoothly.
