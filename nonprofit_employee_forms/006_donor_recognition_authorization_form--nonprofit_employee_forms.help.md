# Donor Recognition Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a donor's preferences for recognition, including whether or not they want to be publicly recognized, and to collect contact information and other details that will be used to acknowledge and thank donors for their support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Decide on recognition preferences:
	* Choose from the options for whether or not to be publicly recognized.
2. Fill out contact information:
	* Enter your name in the text field.
3. Add your email address:
	* Enter your email address in the required format (username@domain.com).
4. Provide contact information (optional):
	* Enter any additional contact information in the text field.
5. Add social media handle (optional):
	* Enter your social media handle if you have one.
6. Choose private recognition preferences:
	* Select whether or not to be privately recognized.
7. Choose between public and private recognition:
	* Select whether you want to be recognized in both public and private settings.
8. Choose no recognition:
	* Select if you do not want to be recognized at all.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Recognition Preferences:** (Select multiple options)
	+ Choose whether or not to be publicly recognized.
* **Name:** (Text)
	+ Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email:** (Email)
	+ Enter your email address in the required format (username@domain.com).
* **Contact Info:** (Text)
	+ Enter any additional contact information, such as a phone number or address.
* **Social Media Handle:** (Text)
	+ Enter your social media handle if you have one.
* **Public Recognition:** (Select one)
	+ Choose whether or not to be publicly recognized.
* **Private Recognition:** (Select one)
	+ Choose whether or not to be privately recognized.
* **Both:** (Select one)
	+ Choose whether or not to be recognized in both public and private settings.
* **No Recognition:** (Select one)
	+ Choose if you do not want to be recognized at all.
