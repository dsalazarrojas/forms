# life_member_reaffirmation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Life Member Reaffirmation Form is used to collect and update information about existing life members of our organization. This form is essential for us to keep our records up-to-date and ensure that our members are informed and involved in our activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your first name.
2. Provide your last name.
3. Enter your date of birth.
4. Enter your phone number (if you wish to receive updates from us).
5. Provide your email address (if you wish to receive updates from us).
6. Enter your street address.
7. Enter your city.
8. Enter your state.
9. Enter your zip code.
10. Please provide how you heard about us.
11. Please tell us how we followed up with you.
12. Please tell us why you chose to be a part of our organization.
13. Select all the services you have used from the list below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (First name)**: Your first name is required.
* **Last Name (Last name)**: Your last name is required.
* **Date of Birth (Date of birth)**: Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone (phone)**: This is an optional field, enter your phone number to receive updates from us.
* **Email (email)**: This is an optional field, enter your email address to receive updates from us.
* **Street Address (street address)**: Enter your physical address.
* **City (city)**: Enter the city where you live.
* **State (state)**: Enter the state where you live.
* **Zip Code (zip code)**: Enter your zip code.
* **How did you hear (how did you hear)**: Tell us how you found out about our organization.
* **How did we follow up (how did we follow up)**: Describe the last time we followed up with you.
* **Why did you choose us (why did you choose us)**: Please explain why you joined our organization.
* **What services did you use (what services did you use)**: Select all the services you have used from the list below.
