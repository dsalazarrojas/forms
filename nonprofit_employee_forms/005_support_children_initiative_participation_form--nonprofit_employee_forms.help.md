# Support Children Initiative Participation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect participation information from volunteers and supporters who want to contribute to the Support Children Initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant category for your organization (Nonprofit Employee Forms, School Forms, or Community Organization Forms).
2. Enter a brief description of your participation in the Support Children Initiative.
3. Provide your name and email address as a volunteer.
4. Provide the name and email address of the supporter you are working with.
5. Enter the name of the initiative you are supporting.
6. Provide a brief description of the initiative.
7. Schedule a follow-up time if needed.
8. Upload any relevant output files.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`Nonprofit Employee Forms, School Forms, Community Organization Forms`): Select the category that best describes your organization.
* **Description** (`Description`): Briefly describe your participation in the Support Children Initiative.
* **Volunteer Name** (`Volunteer Name`): Enter your name as a volunteer.
* **Volunteer Email** (`Volunteer Email`): Enter your email address as a volunteer.
* **Supporter Name** (`Supporter Name`): Enter the name of the supporter you are working with.
* **Supporter Email** (`Supporter Email`): Enter the email address of the supporter you are working with.
* **Initiative Name** (`Initiative Name`): Enter the name of the initiative you are supporting.
* **Initiative Details** (`Initiative Details`): Provide a brief description of the initiative.
* **Follow Up** (`Follow Up`): Schedule a follow-up time if needed.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`): Enter any assigned tool.
* **Output File** (`Output File`): Upload any relevant output files.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Be as detailed as possible when describing your initiative and participation.
- Use a valid email address for both your volunteer and supporter information.
- Upload any relevant files to support your initiative.
