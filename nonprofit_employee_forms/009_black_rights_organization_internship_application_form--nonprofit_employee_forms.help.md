# Black Rights Organization Internship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals to apply for an internship with the Black Rights Organization. Please submit your application if you are interested in gaining valuable experience and contributing to our mission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Applicant Name**, which should be your full name as it appears on your identification.
2. Provide a valid **Email Address** that we can use to contact you for updates on your application.
3. If available, fill in your **Phone Number** so we can reach you for further discussion.
4. If applicable, specify your **University or Employer**, which is the institution or workplace you are currently affiliated with.
5. List your **Program of Study**, which is the major or field of study you are pursuing or have pursued.
6. Enter the **Availability Dates** you are available to start the internship.
7. Summarize your **Interests and Skills**, which are the relevant skills you have that make you a great fit for this internship.
8. In the **Motivation Statement**, explain why you are interested in this internship and how you think you can contribute to our organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): This is your full name as it appears on your identification. Please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Please provide a valid email address that we can use to contact you for updates on your application.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a contact number that you would like us to reach you on, please provide it. This is optional but recommended for further discussion.
* **University or Employer** (`university_or_employer`, text, optional): If you are currently affiliated with an institution or workplace, please specify it.
* **Program of Study** (`program_of_study`, text, optional): This is the major or field of study you are pursuing or have pursued.
* **Availability Dates** (`availability_dates`, text, required): Please enter the date range you are available to start the internship.
* **Interests and Skills** (`interests_and_skills`, text, required): Summarize your relevant skills and interests that make you a great fit for this internship.
* **Motivation Statement** (`motivation_statement`, text, required): Explain why you are interested in this internship and how you think you can contribute to our organization.
* **References** (`references`, text, optional): If you have two professional references, please provide their contact information.
