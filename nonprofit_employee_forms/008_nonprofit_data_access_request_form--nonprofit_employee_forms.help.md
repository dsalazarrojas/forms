# Nonprofit Data Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for nonprofit employees to request access to sensitive data. By filling out this form, you can provide the necessary information to support your access request.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately with your information.
- Select the appropriate access level that aligns with your role and requirements.
- If applicable, provide the date and time you need access to the data.
- Add any additional notes or comments to support your access request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documentation or ID.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documentation or ID.
* **Email Address**: Enter your official email address associated with your nonprofit organization.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title within the nonprofit organization.
* **Department**: Enter the department within the nonprofit that you belong to.
* **Role**: Describe your role within the nonprofit organization.
* **Access Request**: Briefly explain the reason for your access request and what type of data you need to access.
* **Access Level**: Select the level of access you require:
	+ Level 1: Limited access to specific data or systems.
	+ Level 2: Standard access to most data and systems.
	+ Level 3: Full access to all sensitive data and systems.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/dd/yyyy.
* **Date of Access Request**: Enter the date you need access to the data.
* **Time of Access Request**: Enter the time you need access to the data (if applicable).
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes to support your access request.
* **Submit**: Click to submit your access request.
