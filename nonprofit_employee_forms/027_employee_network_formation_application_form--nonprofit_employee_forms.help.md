# Employee Network Formation Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Network Formation Application Form is used to capture information about new employees in your organization. This form helps to gather essential details such as name, job title, department, and contact information to create a comprehensive employee profile. It's crucial to fill out the form accurately to ensure the correct data is recorded for employee directory and other related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
5. Select the department you belong to in the "Department" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Confirm your job title in the "Job Title Confirmation" field.
9. Confirm your department in the "Department Confirmation" field.
10. Confirm your date of birth in the "Date of Birth Confirmation" field.
11. Confirm your first name in the "First Name Confirmation" field.
12. Confirm your last name in the "Last Name Confirmation" field.
13. Confirm your job title again in the "Job Title Confirmation 2" field.
14. Confirm your department again in the "Department Confirmation" field.
15. Confirm your department again in the "Department 2" field.
16. Confirm your department again in the "Department Confirmation 2" field.
17. Enter the date for the current date in the "Date" field.
18. Enter the time for the current time in the "Time" field.
19. Enter any additional notes in the "Note" field.
20. Confirm your email address in the "Email Confirmation" field.
21. Confirm your phone number in the "Phone Confirmation" field.
22. Confirm your job title again in the "Job Title Confirmation 3" field.
23. Confirm your department again in the "Department Confirmation 3" field.
24. Confirm your department again in the "Department Confirmation 4" field.
25. Confirm your date of birth again in the "Date Confirmation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (Date): Provide your date of birth in the format of mm/dd/yyyy or dd/mm/yyyy (month/day/year or day/month/year).
* **Job Title** (Text): Enter your job title in the organization.
* **Department** (Text): Select the department you belong to.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address in the format of name@domain.com.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
* **Job Title Confirmation** (Select One): Confirm your job title by selecting one of the options.
* **Department Confirmation** (Select Multiple): Confirm your department by selecting one or more of the options.
* **Date of Birth Confirmation** (Date): Confirm your date of birth.
* **First Name Confirmation** (Text): Confirm your first name.
* **Last Name Confirmation** (Text): Confirm your last name.
* **Job Title Confirmation 2** (Text): Confirm your job title again.
* **Department Confirmation** (Text): Confirm your department again.
* **Department 2** (Text): Enter a duplicate of your department.
* **Department Confirmation 2** (Text): Confirm your department again.
* **Department Confirmation 3** (Text): Confirm your department again.
* **Date** (Date): Enter the current date in the format of mm/dd/yyyy or dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Time** (Time): Enter the current time in the format of 12h or 24h.
* **Note** (Note): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Email Confirmation** (Email): Confirm your email address.
* **Phone Confirmation** (Text): Confirm your phone number.
* **Job Title Confirmation 3** (Text): Confirm your job title again.
* **Department Confirmation 4** (Text): Confirm your department again.
* **Date Confirmation** (Date): Confirm your date of birth.
