# Vacation Bible School Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vacation Bible School Volunteer Application Form is designed to help us understand your qualifications, availability, and preferences to best match you with volunteer opportunities that suit your skills and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in a clear and concise manner.
2. Make sure to enter accurate information for each field.
3. If you have any difficulties or questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Information (1)**: Enter a brief description of your background and experience as a volunteer.
* **Name (2)**: Enter your full name as it should be displayed on our records.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your work or personal email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone (4)**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Address (5)**: Enter your full address for our records.
* **Role (6)**: Select one or more roles you are interested in or have experience in (Volunteer Coordinator, Teacher, Helper).
* **Volunteer Experience (7)**: Describe your previous volunteer experience and why you are interested in volunteering with us.
* **Availability (8)**: Select the days you are available to volunteer. Please note that we may schedule you for any day that fits the program needs and volunteer availability.
* **Days Available (9)**: Select all the days you are available to work. Please note that we will do our best to schedule you on your preferred days.
* **Start Date (10)**: Enter the date you wish to start volunteering.
* **End Date (11)**: Enter the date you plan to stop volunteering.
* **Hours Available (12)**: Select the hours you are available to work. Please note that we may schedule you for any hours that fit the program needs and volunteer availability.
* **Comments (13)**: Enter any additional comments or information about your volunteer experience or availability.
* **Signature (14)**: Sign your name to confirm your application.
* **Date (15)**: Enter the date you sign this application.
* **Role 2 (16)**: Select one or more additional roles you are interested in or have experience in.
* **Role2 (17)**: Enter any additional comments or information about your role 2.
* **Role3 (18)**: Select one or more additional roles you are interested in or have experience in.
* **Role4 (19)**: Enter any additional comments or information about your role 3.
* **Role5 (20)**: Select one or more additional roles you are interested in or have experience in.
* **Role6 (21)**: Enter any additional comments or information about your role 5.
* **Role7 (22)**: Select one or more additional roles you are interested in or have experience in.
* **Role8 (23)**: Enter any additional comments or information about your role 7.
* **Role9 (24)**: Select one or more additional roles you are interested in or have experience in.
* **Role10 (25)**: Enter any additional comments or information about your role 9.

Please note that you can skip fields 17, 19, 20, 22, 23, 24, and 25 if you are not interested in any additional roles.
