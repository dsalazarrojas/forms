# Nonprofit Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through completing the Nonprofit Event Registration Form. Please read carefully to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of the event in the "Nonprofit Event Info" field.
2. Enter the attendee's information in the "Attendee Info" field.
3. Enter payment details in the "Payment Info" field.
4. Select relevant details for the attendee in the "Registration Info" field.
5. Enter the attendee's contact information in the "Email", "Phone", "Attendee Name", "Attendee Email", and "Attendee Phone" fields.
6. Choose the correct event date and time in the "Event Date" and "Event Time" fields.
7. Select the correct event start and end times in the "Event Start Time" and "Event End Time" fields.
8. Choose the attendee's type in the "Attendee Type" field.
9. Select the attendee's role in the "Attendee Role" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nonprofit Event Info**: Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Attendee Info**: Enter the attendee's information.
* **Payment Info**: Enter payment details.
* **Registration Info**: Select relevant details for the attendee, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Email**: Enter the attendee's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the attendee's phone number.
* **Attendee Name**: Enter the attendee's name.
* **Attendee Email**: Enter the attendee's email address.
* **Attendee Phone**: Enter the attendee's phone number.
* **Event Date**: Choose the event date.
* **Event Time**: Choose the event time.
* **Event Start Time**: Choose the event start time.
* **Event End Time**: Choose the event end time.
* **Attendee Type**: Choose the attendee's type, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Attendee Role**: Select the attendee's role, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
