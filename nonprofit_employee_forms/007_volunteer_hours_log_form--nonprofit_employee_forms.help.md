The purpose of the "Volunteer Hours Log Form" is to collect information about volunteer hours worked by individuals. This form is used to track volunteer hours for internal record-keeping and reporting purposes. To ensure accurate and reliable data, please ensure that only one form is submitted per volunteer per day.

Before starting, please note that this form is intended for single volunteer hours entries. If you need to log multiple volunteer hours for a single event, please create a separate entry for each day or session.

To complete this form, follow the steps below.

## Volunteer Hours Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record volunteer hours worked by individuals. It helps us track and report volunteer hours accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required and optional fields as instructed below.
2. Ensure all fields are filled out correctly to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Information** (`volunteer_info`, text, optional): Please enter the name and contact information of the volunteer who worked the hours.
* **Event Information** (`event_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the event where the volunteer worked hours.
* **Hours Worked** (`hours_info`, text, optional): Record the number of hours worked by the volunteer on that specific day.
* **Additional Event Information** (`event_info_2`, text, optional): If needed, provide any additional context or notes about the event.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Record any other relevant details about the volunteer hours worked.
* **Hours Worked Summary** (`hours_info_2`, text, optional): Summarize the total hours worked by the volunteer on that day.
* **Hours Worked Summary 2** (`hours_info_3`, text, optional): If you need to record multiple days or sessions, please create a separate entry for each day or session.
* **Total Hours Worked** (`hours_info_4`, text, optional): Please enter the total hours worked by the volunteer.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit the form and complete the entry.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled out correctly to avoid errors.
