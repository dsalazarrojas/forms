# Thanksgiving Food Basket Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Thanksgiving Food Basket Registration Form is used by a nonprofit organization to register individuals for a Thanksgiving food basket program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your or the individual's information.
2. Select the correct options for "Interested In" and "Assigned To" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter the first name of the individual you are registering.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter the last name of the individual you are registering.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter the email address of the individual you are registering. This will be used to contact the registrant if needed.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the individual you are registering, if available.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter the date of birth of the individual you are registering. This is to ensure accurate delivery and distribution of the food baskets.
* **Zip Code** (text, optional): Enter the zip code of the individual you are registering. This will help the nonprofit organization determine the correct delivery area.
* **Address** (text, optional): Enter the address of the individual you are registering. This will help the nonprofit organization determine the correct delivery location.
* **Interested In** (select_one, required): Select the option that best describes the individual's interest in the food basket program.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional information about the individual you are registering, if necessary.
* **Assigned To** (select_multiple, required): Select the person or organization to be assigned to the individual you are registering.
* **Form ID** (number, optional): Enter a unique ID for the form, if necessary. (Note: This field is for internal use only.)
