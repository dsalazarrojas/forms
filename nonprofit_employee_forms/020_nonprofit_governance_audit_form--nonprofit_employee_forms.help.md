# Nonprofit Governance Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The nonprofit governance audit form is a tool used to evaluate the governance practices and structures of a nonprofit organization. It is a critical process to ensure the organization's governance is functioning effectively and in accordance with best practices.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Organization Info**
	* Fill in the name of the organization.
2. **Board Member Details**
	* Select all board members and their titles.
3. **Meeting History**
	* Enter the date of the last board meeting.
4. **Committee Roles**
	* Select the roles and responsibilities of each committee.
5. **Policies And Procedures**
	* Describe the organization's policies and procedures.
6. **Audit Findings**
	* Describe the reviewer's findings and observations.
7. **Improvement Opportunities**
	* List all opportunities for improvement.
8. **Next Steps**
	* Describe the steps to be taken to address the improvement opportunities.
9. **Review Date**
	* Enter the date of the review.
10. **Reviewer Name**
	* Enter the name of the reviewer.
11. **Reviewer Title**
	* Enter the title of the reviewer.
12. **Reviewer Organization**
	* Enter the name of the reviewer's organization.
13. **Reviewer Signature**
	* Enter a signature of the reviewer.
14. **Reviewer Email**
	* Enter the email of the reviewer.
15. **Reviewer Phone**
	* Enter the phone number of the reviewer.
16. **Submitter Name**
	* Enter the name of the submitter.
17. **Submitter Organization**
	* Enter the name of the submitter's organization.
18. **Submitter Title**
	* Enter the title of the submitter.
19. **Submitter Department**
	* Enter the department of the submitter.
20. **Submitter Job Title**
	* Enter the job title of the submitter.
21. **Submitter Email**
	* Enter the email of the submitter.
22. **Submitter Phone**
	* Enter the phone number of the submitter.
23. **Submitter Signature**
	* Enter a signature of the submitter.
24. **Submitter Comments**
	* Enter any comments from the submitter.
25. **Submitter Reviewer Comments**
	* Enter any comments from the submitter to the reviewer.
