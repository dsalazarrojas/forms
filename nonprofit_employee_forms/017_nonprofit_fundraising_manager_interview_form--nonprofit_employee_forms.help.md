# Nonprofit Fundraising Manager Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from nonprofit fundraising managers to assess their skills, experience, and leadership style. The data collected will be used to identify areas of strength and improvement for the Nonprofit Fundraising Manager role.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your Name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Describe your Work Experience in the "Work Experience" field.
5. Share your Non Profit Organizational Chart in the "Non Profit Organizational Chart" field.
6. Select your Leadership Style from the options provided in the "Leadership Style" field.
7. Enter your Work History in the "Work History" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on your certificate or in any related documents.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address where you would like to be contacted for any follow-up questions or communications.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for any further discussions.
* **Work Experience** (number, required/optional): Describe your work experience in the nonprofit sector, including job titles, duration of employment, and relevant skills gained.
* **Non Profit Organizational Chart** (text, required/optional): Share a copy of your non-profit organization's chart or describe your organizational structure.
* **Leadership Style** (select_one, required/optional): Choose from the three leadership styles listed:
	+ Leadership Style: Describe a traditional, hierarchical leadership approach.
	+ Collaborative: Emphasize teamwork and open communication among team members.
	+ Authoritative: Show a strong, decisive leadership style.
	+ Visionary: Focus on innovative and forward-thinking leadership.
* **Work History** (text, required/optional): Describe your work history in the nonprofit sector, including job titles, dates of employment, and achievements.
* **Leadership Style** is a required field, and selecting only one option may not fully capture your leadership style as a nonprofit professional. However, this selection will serve as a starting point for your discussion and assessment.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your information is complete and accurate.
* Be honest and thorough in your answers, as they will be used for assessment and certification purposes.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for clarification.
