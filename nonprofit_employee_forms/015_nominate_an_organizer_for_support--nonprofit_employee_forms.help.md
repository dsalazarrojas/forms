# Nominate An Organizer For Support - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to nominate individuals or organizations that require support. It is a way to help those in need and provide resources and assistance to them.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form once for each individual or organization you wish to nominate for support.
2. Enter the first name of the individual or organization being nominated.
3. Enter the last name of the individual or organization being nominated.
4. Enter the email address of the individual or organization being nominated.
5. Select the role of the individual or organization being nominated.
6. Enter the location of the individual or organization being nominated.
7. Enter the phone number of the individual or organization being nominated.
8. Enter the reason why you are nominating this individual or organization.
9. Enter the name of the supporting organization.
10. Select the support level needed by the individual or organization.
11. Select the frequency of support needed by the individual or organization.
12. Enter the start date of support needed by the individual or organization.
13. Enter the end date of support needed by the individual or organization.
14. Enter any additional notes about the individual or organization.
15. Enter the name of the supporting organization.
16. Select the tool assigned to support the individual or organization.
17. Enter the name of the person submitting the nomination.
18. Enter the date of submission.
19. Enter the name of the person submitting the nomination.
20. Enter the category of the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter the first name of the individual or organization being nominated.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter the last name of the individual or organization being nominated.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter the email address of the individual or organization being nominated.
* **Role** (4, text, required): Select the role of the individual or organization being nominated.
* **Location** (5, text, required): Enter the location of the individual or organization being nominated.
* **Phone** (6, text, required): Enter the phone number of the individual or organization being nominated.
* **Reason** (7, text, required): Enter the reason why you are nominating this individual or organization.
* **Supporting Organization** (8, text, required): Enter the name of the supporting organization.
* **Nominee First Name** (9, text, required): Enter the first name of the individual being nominated.
* **Nominee Last Name** (10, text, required): Enter the last name of the individual being nominated.
* **Nominee Email** (11, email, required): Enter the email address of the individual being nominated.
* **Nominee Phone** (12, text, required): Enter the phone number of the individual being nominated.
* **Nominee Role** (13, text, required): Select the role of the individual being nominated.
* **Nominee Location** (14, text, required): Enter the location of the individual being nominated.
* **Support Level** (15, select_multiple, required): Select the level of support needed by the individual or organization.
* **Support Frequency** (16, select_multiple, required): Select the frequency of support needed by the individual or organization.
* **Support Start Date** (17, date, required): Enter the start date of support needed by the individual or organization.
* **Support End Date** (18, date, required): Enter the end date of support needed by the individual or organization.
* **Note** (19, note, required): Enter any additional notes about the individual or organization.
* **Assigned Tool** (20, text, required): Select the tool assigned to support the individual or organization.
* **Submitted By** (21, text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Submission Date** (22, date, required): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submitter** (23, text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Category** (24, text, required): Enter the category of the nomination.
