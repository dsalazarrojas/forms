**Disaster Relief Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Disaster Relief Volunteer Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering for disaster relief efforts. This form helps to match volunteers with suitable opportunities and ensures that essential contact information is available in case of emergencies.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all fields marked as "required" as they are essential to complete the application.
2. If you are applying for multiple volunteers, please submit separate applications for each individual.
3. Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Information** (`Volunteer Information`, `text`, required): Please provide a brief description of your name, age, and any other relevant details about yourself as a volunteer.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required): Enter your name, address, phone number, and email address.
* **What is your volunteer experience?** (`volunteer_experience`, `text`, required): Share your past experiences related to volunteering, including any relevant skills or training received.
* **Skills** (`skills`, `text`, required): Describe your skills that would be valuable to us, such as first aid, leadership, or communication skills.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `text`, required): Provide your availability for volunteering, including your preferred dates and times.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `select_one`, required): Specify your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, `text`, required): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Address** (`emergency_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`tnc_agreement`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of volunteering.

**Tips**

* Be honest and accurate in providing your information.
* Ensure that your emergency contact information is up-to-date and accurate.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
