# pet_rescue_volunteer_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from potential volunteers for pet rescue services. It is essential to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth volunteer relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by entering your first name in the "First name" field.
- Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
- Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
- Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
- Enter your date of birth in the "Date of birth" field in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
- Enter your address in the "Address" field.
- Select your volunteer role from the options provided in the "Volunteer role" field.
- Choose the date you would like to start volunteering in the "Volunteer start date" field.
- Choose the date you would like to end volunteering in the "Volunteer end date" field.
- Choose how you would like to submit your application, either "Online" or "In person".
- Check the "Timesheet integration" field if you would like to integrate with our timesheet system.
- Enter the name of an emergency contact person in the "Emergency contact name" field.
- Specify the relationship with the emergency contact person in the "Emergency contact relationship" field.
- Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person in the "Emergency contact phone" field.
- Enter the address of the emergency contact person in the "Emergency contact address" field.
- Enter the email address of the emergency contact person in the "Emergency contact email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name here.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number here.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your address here.
* **Volunteer role** (`volunteer_role`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose your volunteer role from the options provided.
* **Volunteer start date** (`volunteer_start_date`, `date`, required/optional): Choose the date you would like to start volunteering.
* **Volunteer end date** (`volunteer_end_date`, `date`, required/optional): Choose the date you would like to end volunteering.
* **Application submission** (`application_submission`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose how you would like to submit your application, either "Online" or "In person".
* **Timesheet integration** (`timesheet_integration`, `select_one`, required/optional): Check this box if you would like to integrate with our timesheet system.
* **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required/optional): Specify the relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact address** (`emergency_contact_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact email** (`emergency_contact_email`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the pet rescue organization directly.
- Ensure you provide a valid email address and phone number for emergency contact.
