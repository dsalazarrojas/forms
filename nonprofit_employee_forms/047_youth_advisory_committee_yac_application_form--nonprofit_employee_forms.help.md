# Youth Advisory Committee (YAC) Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Advisory Committee (YAC) Application Form is designed to collect information from youth members who wish to join the YAC. This form is intended for youth who are interested in participating in the YAC and have been nominated by a parent or guardian. The form is used to verify the applicant's eligibility and gather necessary contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your name and contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
2. Indicate if you have attended any YAC meetings in the "YAC Meetings" section.
3. Select your availability for YAC meetings in the "YAC Meetings" section.
4. Choose the YAC committee roles you are interested in taking on in the "YAC Committee Roles" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required): Please enter your name, address, phone number, and email address in this field. This is the primary contact information we will use to reach you.

### 2. YAC Meetings
* **YAC Meetings** (`yac_meetings`, text, required): Please indicate if you have attended any YAC meetings. This helps us understand your level of engagement with the committee.

### 3. YAC Meetings 2
* **YAC Meetings 2** (`yac_meetings_2`, text, required): Repeat the same question as above, as this field is intended to gather additional information about your YAC meeting attendance.

### 4. YAC Committee Roles
* **YAC Committee Roles** (`yac_committee_roles`, select_multiple, required): Select all the committee roles you are interested in taking on. This could include:
	+ Yes: You are interested in taking on this role.
	+ No: You are not interested in taking on this role.
