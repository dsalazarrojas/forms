# Community Outreach Coordinator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Outreach Coordinator Application Form is used to gather information from potential candidates applying for a Community Outreach Coordinator position within our nonprofit organization. This form helps us understand your personal and professional background, experience, and availability to determine the best fit for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal contact information, including your first and last name, email address, and phone number.
2. Enter your street address, city, and state for our records.
3. Enter your ZIP code to ensure we can contact you.
4. In the "Experience and Qualifications" section, describe your relevant background and experience in community outreach coordination.
5. Select your level of education and relevant skills required for the position.
6. If applicable, list any previous organizations you've worked with.
7. Choose your availability type (full-time, part-time, weekends only, evenings only, or flexible).
8. Select a start date you are available to begin working.
9. Indicate if you have reliable transportation.
10. Provide professional references, including their name, title, and contact information.
11. Finally, add any additional comments about your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your work or personal email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Include your area code in the phone number (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **Street Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your street address where you can be contacted or reached during working hours.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where you reside or work.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter the state where you reside or work.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your postal code or ZIP+4 for precise location.
* **Experience and Qualifications** (`experience_section`, `note`, required): Describe your background and experience in community outreach coordination, including any relevant events, projects, or certifications.
* **Years of Community Outreach Experience** (`years_experience`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you've had experience in community outreach coordination.
* **Highest Level of Education** (`education_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose your highest level of education from the options provided.
* **Relevant Skills** (`relevant_skills`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the skills you have that are relevant to the position.
* **Previous Organizations Worked With** (`previous_organizations`, `text`, optional): List any nonprofit or community organizations you've worked with in the past.
* **Availability** (`availability_section`, `note`, optional): Describe your schedule preferences for the position.
* **Availability Type** (`availability_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you are available to work (full-time, part-time, weekends only, evenings only, or flexible).
* **Available Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Select a start date you are available to begin working.
* **Do you have reliable transportation?** (`transportation`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you have reliable transportation to travel to work or events.
* **References** (`references_section`, `note`, optional): Provide professional references, including their name, title, and contact information.
* **Reference 1 Name** (`reference_1_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your first professional reference.
* **Reference 1 Contact Information** (`reference_1_contact`, `text`, required): Enter your first professional reference's contact information (email or phone).
* **Reference 2 Name** (`reference_2_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your second professional reference (if applicable).
* **Reference 2 Contact Information** (`reference_2_contact`, `text`, optional): Enter your second professional reference's contact information (email or phone).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Share any additional information about your application or qualifications.
