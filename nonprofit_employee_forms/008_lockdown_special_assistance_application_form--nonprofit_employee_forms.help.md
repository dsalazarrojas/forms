# Lockdown Special Assistance Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals in need of special assistance during the lockdown gather and submit their personal and contact information, as well as details about special needs and emergency services required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information (Page 1).
2. Provide your address and other contact information (Page 2).
3. Describe any special needs you may have (Page 3).
4. List the emergency contact person and their information (Page 4).
5. Share your contact information (Page 5).
6. Provide details about your family situation (Page 6).
7. Report your monthly income (Page 7).
8. Select any emergency services you require (Page 8).
9. Indicate your current housing status (Page 9).
10. Provide any additional special needs details (Page 10).
11. Enter the emergency contact person's information (Page 11).
12. Add any additional comments or notes (Page 12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Personal Info** (`personal_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other personal details relevant to your situation.
- **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your address for contact and delivery purposes.
- **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, text, required: false): Describe any special needs you may have, such as medical conditions or equipment requirements.
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information, such as phone number and email.
- **Family Info** (`family_info`, text, required: false): Describe your family situation, such as living with others or dependent family members.
- **Income** (`income`, number, required: false): Report your monthly income to help assess your financial situation.
- **Emergency Services** (`emergency_services`, select_one, required: false): Choose any emergency services you require, such as food or medical services.
- **Housing Status** (`housing_status`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate your current housing status, such as living with family or in temporary accommodation.
- **Special Needs Details** (`special_needs_details`, text, required: false): Provide any additional details about your special needs.
- **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter the emergency contact person's contact information, such as phone number and email.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about your situation.
