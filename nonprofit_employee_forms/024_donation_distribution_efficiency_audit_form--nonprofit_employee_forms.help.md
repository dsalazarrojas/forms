# Donation Distribution Efficiency Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the efficiency of the donation distribution process within a nonprofit organization. The purpose of this form is to identify areas of improvement and potential bottlenecks in the distribution process, which can help the nonprofit optimize its resources and better serve its donors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Review the form carefully and ensure you have access to the necessary information before filling it out.
*   Answer each question as accurately and completely as possible, based on your experience and knowledge of the donation distribution process.
*   If you are unsure about any question or option, please ask for clarification or consult with your colleagues or superiors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Donor Programs** (`donor_programs`, text, optional): Please enter a list of donor programs that are currently in place for donation distribution. For example, programs like "Program A", "Program B", "Program C", etc.
*   **Distribution Channels** (`distribution_channels`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the channels through which donations are distributed, such as "Channel A", "Channel B", "Channel C", etc.
*   **Distribution Periods** (`distribution_periods`, date, optional): Enter the period during which donations were distributed. Please use the format "YYYY-MM-DD" to ensure accurate date input.
*   **Distribution Frequency** (`distribution_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose the frequency at which donations are distributed, such as "Daily", "Weekly", "Monthly", "Quarterly", "Yearly".
*   **Distribution Method** (`distribution_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select all methods used for distribution, such as "Method A", "Method B", "Method C", etc.
*   **Distribution Value** (`distribution_value`, number, optional): Enter the total value of the donations distributed during the specified period. This can be a numerical value, such as "100".
*   **Distribution Location** (`distribution_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where donations are distributed. Please be as specific as possible, such as "Warehouse 1", "Location A", etc.

## Tips
*   If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or consult with your colleagues or superiors.
*   Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form, and ensure that you have the necessary information before submitting the form.
*   Review the form carefully to ensure that you have answered all the questions correctly.
*   If you need help or have further questions, please do not hesitate to reach out.
