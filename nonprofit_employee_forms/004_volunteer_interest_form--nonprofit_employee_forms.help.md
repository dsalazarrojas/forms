# volunteer_interest_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Volunteer Interest Form" is designed to help the organization understand your interests, skills, and availability for volunteering. This form will help us match you with suitable volunteer opportunities within our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and accurately.
2. Select your preferred volunteer role(s) from the list.
3. Choose your preferred frequency of volunteering.
4. Enter your availability for the volunteer shift.
5. Provide your contact information and any additional details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Role** (`volunteer_interest_form_1`): This question asks about your interest in volunteer roles within the organization. Choose one or more of the options to indicate your preferred role(s).
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Volunteer Interest Form 1 2** (`volunteer_interest_form_1_2`): This is a placeholder question and does not need to be filled.
* **Frequency of Volunteering** (`volunteer_interest_form_2`): This question asks about how often you would like to volunteer. Please select your preferred frequency from the available options.
* **Volunteer Shift** (`volunteer_interest_form_5`): This question asks about your availability for volunteer shifts. Enter your preferred shift in the format 'HH:MM - HH:MM'.
* **Volunteer Availability** (`volunteer_interest_form_6`): This question asks about your availability for the volunteer shifts. Enter your availability in the format 'HH:MM - HH:MM'.
* **Volunteer Interest Form 4** (`volunteer_interest_form_7`): This is a placeholder question and does not need to be filled.
* **Time of Day** (`volunteer_interest_form_8_2`): This question asks about your preferred time of day for volunteering. Choose one of the available options.
	+ Options: 'None', 'morning', 'afternoon', 'evening'
* **Volunteer Duration** (`volunteer_interest_form_9`): This question asks about how long you can commit to volunteering. Please enter the duration in the format 'HH:MM - HH:MM'.
* **Volunteer Start Date** (`volunteer_interest_form_10`): This question asks for the earliest date you can start volunteering. Enter the date in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **Volunteer End Date** (`volunteer_interest_form_11`): This question asks for the latest date you can end volunteering. Enter the date in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **Volunteer Interest Form 8** (`volunteer_interest_form_12`): This is a placeholder question and does not need to be filled.
* **Volunteer Interest Form 8 2** (`volunteer_interest_form_8_2`): This is another placeholder question and does not need to be filled.
* **Volunteer Interest Form 10** (`volunteer_interest_form_10`): This is another placeholder question and does not need to be filled.
* **First Name** (`volunteer_interest_form_16`): This question asks for your first name.
* **Last Name** (`volunteer_interest_form_17`): This question asks for your last name.
* **Email** (`volunteer_interest_form_18`): This question asks for your email address.
* **Phone** (`volunteer_interest_form_19`): This question asks for your phone number.
* **Organization Affiliation** (`volunteer_interest_form_20`): This question asks about your affiliation with any organization.
* **Organization** (`volunteer_interest_form_21`): This question asks for the name of your organization.
* **Address** (`volunteer_interest_form_22`): This question asks for your address.
* **Zip** (`volunteer_interest_form_23`): This question asks for your zip code.
* **Additional Information** (`volunteer_interest_form_24`): This question allows you to provide any additional information about yourself.
* **Volunteer Interest Statement** (`volunteer_interest_form_25`): This question asks you to state your interest in volunteering.
