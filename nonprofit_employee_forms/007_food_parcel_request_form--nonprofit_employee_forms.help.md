# Food Parcel Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Parcel Request Form is used to request a food parcel, which is a shipment of non-perishable food items. This form is used to provide necessary information for creating and managing food parcels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Choose the type of food parcel you need, either non-perishable, perishable, or both.
3. Specify the quantity of food parcel you need.
4. Enter a brief description of the food parcel you need.
5. Enter the address where the food parcel should be delivered.
6. Choose the date and time when you need the food parcel.
7. Enter any additional message about the food parcel.
8. If applicable, provide the name and contact information of the person who should receive the food parcel.
9. Optionally, enter any notes about the food parcel.
10. Choose the status of the food parcel, such as pending, completed, or canceled.
11. Enter the date and time when the food parcel was created.
12. Enter the date and time when the food parcel was last updated.
13. Optionally, enter the name of the person who created the food parcel.
14. Optionally, enter the name of the person who last updated the food parcel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Food Parcel Request Form** (Page 1)
  - **Contact Name** (Required): Enter your name to help us identify you as the requester.
* **Contact Email** (Optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your food parcel request.
* **Contact Phone** (Optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your food parcel request.
* **Food Parcel Type** (Optional): Choose the type of food parcel you need, either non-perishable, perishable, or both.
* **Food Parcel Quantity** (Optional): Enter the quantity of food parcel you need.
* **Food Parcel Description** (Optional): Briefly describe the food parcel you need.
* **Food Parcel Address** (Optional): Enter the address where the food parcel should be delivered.
* **Food Parcel Date** (Optional): Choose the date when you need the food parcel.
* **Food Parcel Time** (Optional): Choose the time when you need the food parcel.
* **Food Parcel Message** (Optional): Enter any additional message about the food parcel.
* **Food Parcel Recipient Name** (Optional): If the food parcel is for someone else, enter their name.
* **Food Parcel Recipient Email** (Optional): Enter the recipient's email address.
* **Food Parcel Recipient Phone** (Optional): Enter the recipient's phone number.
* **Food Parcel Notes** (Optional): Enter any additional notes about the food parcel.
* **Food Parcel Status** (Optional): Choose the status of the food parcel, such as pending, completed, or canceled.
* **Food Parcel Create Date** (Optional): Automatically populated with the date and time when the food parcel was created.
* **Food Parcel Create Time** (Optional): Automatically populated with the time when the food parcel was created.
* **Food Parcel Update Date** (Optional): Automatically populated with the date and time when the food parcel was last updated.
* **Food Parcel Update Time** (Optional): Automatically populated with the time when the food parcel was last updated.
* **Food Parcel Creator Name** (Optional): Automatically populated with the name of the person who created the food parcel.
* **Food Parcel Updated By** (Optional): Automatically populated with the name of the person who last updated the food parcel.
