# Food Drive Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Drive Form is used to collect information for non-profit food drives, helping to sort and distribute essential items to those in need.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully review the form before starting.
- Fill out each field in the following sections: "Food", "Non-Food", and "Contact Information".
- Ensure all fields are filled in with accurate and relevant information.
- Review all fields before submitting for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Food**:
  • **Food** (`food_drive_form_1`, text, required): Enter the type of food collected (e.g., canned goods, baked goods, etc.).
- **Toiletries** (`toiletries`, select_multiple, required): Select the types of toiletries collected (e.g., toothpaste, soap, etc.).
  - **Yes** and **No** options are provided to select whether the item was collected or not.
- **Baby** (`baby_items`, select_one, required): Select one of the baby items collected (e.g., Baby food, Baby wipes, etc.).
  - Options include Baby food, Baby wipes, Diapers, Formula, Pacifiers, and Pacifier refills.
- **Non-Food** (`non_food_drives`, select_multiple, required): Select the types of non-food items collected (e.g., toiletries, personal care items, etc.).
  - **Yes** and **No** options are provided to select whether the item was collected or not.
- **Personal Care** (`non_food_drives_1`, select_multiple, required): Select the types of personal care items collected (e.g., toiletries, etc.).
  - **Yes** and **No** options are provided to select whether the item was collected or not.
- **Other** (`other_items`, select_multiple, required): Select the other essential items collected (e.g., toiletries, etc.).
  - **Yes** and **No** options are provided to select whether the item was collected or not.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the collection event or when the food drive will take place.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of the collection event or when the food drive will take place.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person or organization.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person or organization.
- **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the address of the collection site or location of the food drive.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields before submitting for accuracy and completeness.
- Use the correct format for date and time fields to ensure they are readable and usable.
