# Fundraising Campaign Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fundraising Campaign Communication Form is used to collect and track information about fundraising campaigns, enabling staff members to effectively communicate with donors and manage campaign data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information for your fundraising campaign.
2. Review each field to ensure that you have provided accurate and complete information.
3. Click submit to save your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Name** (Donor Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the donor associated with this fundraising campaign.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the donor.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the donor.
* **Donor Address** (Donor Address, text, optional): Enter the address of the donor.
* **Fundraising Campaign Goal** (Fundraising Campaign Goal, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the campaign's objective.
* **Campaign Start Date** (Campaign Start Date, date, optional): Select the date the campaign started.
* **Campaign End Date** (Campaign End Date, date, optional): Select the date the campaign ended.
* **Campaign Target** (Campaign Target, number, optional): Enter the target amount of the campaign.
* **Campaign Budget** (Campaign Budget, number, optional): Enter the budget for the campaign.
* **Campaign Donors** (Campaign Donors, number, optional): Enter the number of donors for the campaign.
* **Campaign Status** (Campaign Status, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the campaign (Active, Inactive, or Completed).
* **Donor Message** (Donor Message, text, optional): Enter a message from the donor.
* **Assigned User** (Assigned User, select_one, optional): Select if the campaign is assigned to a specific user.
* **Assigned Tool** (Assigned Tool, select_one, optional): Select if the campaign has a specific tool assigned to it.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete campaign data.
- Double-check the campaign status and assigned user or tool to ensure accuracy.
- Save your changes by clicking submit after completing the form.
