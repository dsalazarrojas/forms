The purpose of this form is to gather RSVP information from individuals or groups who wish to attend or decline the Sponsor Appreciation Dinner event hosted by our organization. The form collects basic contact information (name, organization, email, and phone number) and specific details related to event attendance and preferences (dinner date, attending, number of guests, and dietary needs). This information will help the event organizers prepare for the event and ensure an enjoyable experience for all attendees.

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

## How to Complete This Form

1.  Enter your **Form Name**, which will help us identify the response to the invitation.
2.  Provide your **Guest Name**, which should match the person who will be attending the dinner.
3.  Enter the **Organization** you represent, if applicable.
4.  Provide your **Contact Email**, which will be used for communication related to the event.
5.  Enter your **Contact Phone**, which will be used for last-minute updates or changes.
6.  Select the **Dinner Date** you plan to attend.
7.  Choose whether you are **Attending** (yes/no), **Maybe**, or **Cannot Attend**.
8.  Indicate the **Number of Guests** you plan to bring along.
9.  Select your **Dietary Needs** for the dinner event. Options include **Vegan**, **Vegetarian**, **Gluten-free**, or **None**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Form Name** (`form_name`, text, optional): Enter a brief name for this RSVP response.
*  **Guest Name** (`name`, text, optional): Provide the name of the person attending the dinner.
*  **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): If you represent an organization, enter its name here.
*  **Contact Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for event-related communication.
*  **Contact Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for last-minute updates or changes.
*  **Dinner Date** (`rsvp_date`, date, optional): Select the date you plan to attend the dinner event.
*  **Are you attending?** (`attending`, select_one, optional): Choose to indicate your attendance status (Attending, Cannot Attend, Maybe).
*  **Number of Guests** (`guests`, number, optional): Indicate how many people will be attending with you.
*  **Dietary Needs** (`dietary_needs`, select_multiple, optional): Select the dietary preferences you require for the dinner event (Vegan, Vegetarian, Gluten-free, None). 

## Tips

- Please respond with accurate information about your attendance and any special dietary needs.
- Ensure the number of guests you enter aligns with the actual number of people who will be attending with you.
- If you have any last-minute updates or changes, use the contact phone or email provided.
- If you are unsure about the event date, you can choose "Maybe" to express your uncertainty.
- All fields are optional but are necessary for proper event organization and enjoyment for all attendees.
