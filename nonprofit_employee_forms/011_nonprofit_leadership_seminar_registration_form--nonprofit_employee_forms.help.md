# Nonprofit Leadership Seminar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for a Nonprofit Leadership Seminar. It is used to gather information from users to ensure they have the correct details recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
- Fill in your First Name in the First Name field.
- Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
- Provide your Email address in the Email field (make sure it is in the correct format).
- Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
- Select a Date for the seminar on the Date field.
- Choose a Time for the seminar on the Time field.
- Enter the name of your Nonprofit organization in the Nonprofit field.
- Select the type of seminar you wish to attend from the Seminar Type field (Nonprofit Leadership, Leadership Development, or Fundraising and Development).
- Fill in the Registration Date and Registration Time fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., [name]@example.com).
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number in the correct format (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **Date** (Date, date, required/optional): Select the date you wish to attend the seminar on.
* **Time** (Time, time, required/optional): Choose the time of day you wish to attend the seminar.
* **Nonprofit** (Nonprofit, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your nonprofit organization.
* **Seminar Type** (Seminar Type, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of seminar you wish to attend (Nonprofit Leadership, Leadership Development, or Fundraising and Development).
* **Registration Date** (Registration Date, date, required/optional): Fill in the date of your seminar registration.
* **Registration Time** (Registration Time, time, required/optional): Choose the time of day you registered for the seminar.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your details before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the seminar organizers for help.
* Ensure that you have filled in all the required fields correctly.
