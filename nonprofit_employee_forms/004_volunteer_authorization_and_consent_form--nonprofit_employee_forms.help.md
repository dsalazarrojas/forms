# volunteer-authorization-and-consent-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The volunteer-authorization-and-consent-form is used to gather information from volunteers, ensuring they are aware of their role, responsibilities, and commitment to the organization they are helping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Volunteer's Name in the "Volunteer Name" field. This is the name of the person who will be working with the organization.
2. Enter the Volunteer's Email in the "Email" field. This is the contact information for the volunteer.
3. Enter the Volunteer's Phone Number in the "Phone" field. This is the best way to reach the volunteer for any urgent matters.
4. Enter the Organization the Volunteer is associated with in the "Organization" field. This is the group or entity the volunteer is representing.
5. Enter the Role of the Volunteer in the "Role" field. This is the specific position or function the volunteer will be performing.
6. Select the Consent Date and Time in the "Consent Date" and "Consent Time" fields. These fields should match the date and time the volunteer gives their consent.
7. Select the End Date and Time in the "End Date" and "End Time" fields, if applicable. These fields are for when the volunteer's consent will end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Name** (`volunteer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the volunteer as it appears on their ID or documentation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the volunteer.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the volunteer.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required): Enter the name of the organization the volunteer is representing.
* **Role** (`role`, text, required): Enter the specific role or position the volunteer will be performing.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which the volunteer gives their consent.
* **Consent Time** (`consent_time`, time, required): Enter the time of day the volunteer gives their consent.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the volunteer's consent ends.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of day on which the volunteer's consent ends.
