# volunteer_opportunity_enrollment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by nonprofit organizations to collect information from potential volunteers. It helps to gather essential details about the volunteer, including their contact information, availability, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2.  Ensure that all fields are filled in correctly to avoid errors and inaccuracies.
3.  Review the information before submitting it to the nonprofit organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name**: A field to enter your first name. This will be used as the primary identifier for your records.
*   **Last Name**: A field to enter your last name. This will be used to identify you as a volunteer.
*   **Email**: A field to enter your email address. This will be used for communication and verification purposes.
*   **Phone**: A field to enter your phone number. This will be used for contacting you regarding volunteer opportunities.
*   **Date of Birth**: A field to enter your date of birth. This will be used to verify your eligibility for volunteer opportunities.
*   **Volunteer Start Date**: A field to select the date you wish to start volunteering. This will help the nonprofit organization schedule volunteer opportunities accordingly.
*   **Volunteer End Date**: A field to select the date you wish to end your volunteer services. This will help the nonprofit organization plan for future volunteer opportunities.
*   **Volunteer Hours**: A field to enter the number of volunteer hours you can commit to per week. This will help the nonprofit organization allocate volunteer opportunities based on your availability.
*   **Volunteer Frequency**: A field to select how often you can volunteer per week. Options include:
    *   Option 1
    *   Option 2
    *   Option 3
*   **Volunteer Type**: A field to select the type of volunteer work you are interested in. Options include:
    *   Option 1
    *   Option 2
    *   Option 3
*   **Volunteer Notes**: A field to enter any additional comments or information about your volunteer experience or preferences.
*   **Volunteer Agreed**: A field to confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions of the volunteer opportunity. Options include:
    *   Option 1
    *   Option 2
    *   Option 3

## Tips

*   Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and consistently.
*   Review the information carefully before submitting it to the nonprofit organization.
*   The nonprofit organization will use this information to schedule volunteer opportunities and communicate with you accordingly.
