# Donation Drive Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donation Drive Approval Form is used to document the details of a donation drive for a nonprofit organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the **Donation Drive Form** section with the details of the donation drive.
2. Select the **Non Profit Organization** that the donation drive is associated with.
3. Choose the **Donation Drive Date** and **Donation Drive Time** for the drive.
4. Enter the **Donation Drive Amount** collected during the drive.
5. Add any additional **Donation Drive Notes** about the drive.
6. If applicable, select the **Assigned Nonprofit User** who is responsible for the drive.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Drive Form**: This section is used to capture the details of the donation drive. Enter the name and description of the drive.
* **Non Profit Organization**: This field allows you to select the nonprofit organization that the donation drive is associated with. Please choose the correct organization from the dropdown list.
* **Donation Drive Date**: This field is used to select the date of the donation drive. Please enter the correct date of the drive.
* **Donation Drive Time**: This field is used to select the time of the donation drive. Please enter the correct time of the drive.
* **Donation Drive Amount**: This field is used to enter the total amount collected during the donation drive.
* **Donation Drive Notes**: This field is for any additional notes or comments about the donation drive.
* **Assigned Nonprofit User**: If an organization has assigned a specific user for the drive, select that user from the dropdown list. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
