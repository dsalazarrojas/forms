# Back To Church Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Back To Church Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals who are interested in returning to the church. This form helps us track attendance, identify interests, and facilitate communication, allowing us to better serve our community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Enter your full name in the "Attendee name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Contact email" field (if you have one).
3. Fill out your phone number (optional).
4. Let us know if you are interested in volunteering by selecting "Yes" or "No".
5. Select the specific areas of interest for you or your family (if applicable).
6. Indicate if you are a new or returning attendee.
7. If you wish, provide your address for any updates or communications.
8. Share any prayer requests or needs with us.
9. Confirm that you are comfortable with being contacted by the church by selecting "Yes" or "No".
10. Lastly, select a date for your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee name** (`attendee_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Your email address for church communications and updates.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Provide your phone number for any additional contact or updates.
* **Interested in volunteering** (`interested_in_volunteering`, `select_one`, required): Check "Yes" if you are interested in volunteering for events or programs.
* **Areas of interest** (`areas_of_interest`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the ministries or programs that interest you or your family.
* **Previous attendance** (`previous_attendance`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you are a new or returning attendee.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address for any updates or communications.
* **Prayer request** (`prayer_request`, `text`, optional): Share any prayer requests or needs with us.
* **Consent to contact** (`consent_to_contact`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you are okay with being contacted by the church.
* **Registration date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of your registration.

Note: If you need help or have any questions, feel free to contact us. We are here to assist you in any way we can.
