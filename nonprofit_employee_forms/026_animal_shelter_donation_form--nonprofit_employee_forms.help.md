# Animal Shelter Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to process donations to the animal shelter. It is essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure that your donation is processed correctly and to receive a tax receipt for your contribution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name and email address in the "Donor Full Name" and "Email Address" fields, respectively.
2. Enter the amount of your donation in the "Donation Amount - USD" field.
3. Select the frequency of your donation from the "Donation Frequency" dropdown menu.
4. Choose your preferred payment method from the "Preferred Payment Method" dropdown menu.
5. Indicate where you would like your donation to be allocated from the "Dedicated To" dropdown menu.
6. If you would like to make a tribute gift, enter the name of the person or pet it is in honor or memory of.
7. If your employer matches charitable donations, select "True" from the "Matching Gift from Employer" dropdown menu.
8. Choose whether to subscribe to the shelter's newsletter.
9. If you would like to receive a tax receipt by mail, enter your mailing address in the "Mailing Address for Tax Receipt" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Full Name (1)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address (2)**: Enter your email address where you would like to receive updates and information from the shelter.
* **Donation Amount - USD (3)**: Enter the amount you wish to donate in US dollars.
* **Donation Frequency (4)**: Select how often you would like to make this donation:
	+ One-time Gift: A single donation
	+ Monthly Recurring: A monthly donation
	+ Quarterly Recurring: A quarterly donation
	+ Annual Recurring: An annual donation
* **Preferred Payment Method (5)**: Choose how you would like to pay for your donation:
	+ Credit Card: Donate using your credit card
	+ PayPal: Donate using PayPal
	+ Stripe: Donate using Stripe
	+ Bank Transfer: Donate by bank transfer
	+ Check by Mail: Donate by check sent by mail
* **Dedicated To (6)**: Select where you would like your donation to be used:
	+ General Shelter Operations: Support the daily operations of the shelter
	+ Critical Medical Fund: Support medical care for animals in the shelter
	+ Food and Essential Supplies: Provide food and essential supplies for the animals
	+ Building Expansion and Repairs: Support the shelter's infrastructure
	+ Education and Outreach: Support educational programs and community outreach
* **In Honor or Memory Of (7)**: Enter the name of the person or pet this gift is in honor or memory of.
* **Matching Gift from Employer (8)**: Select if your employer matches charitable donations:
	+ True: Your donation will be matched by your employer
	+ False: Your donation will not be matched by your employer
	+ I am not sure: You are unsure if your employer matches charitable donations
* **Subscribe to our Newsletter (9)**: Choose whether to receive updates from the shelter:
	+ Yes - please sign me up: You would like to receive updates
	+ No - thanks: You do not want to receive updates
* **Mailing Address for Tax Receipt (10)**: Enter your address if you would like to receive a tax receipt by mail.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately to ensure proper processing of your donation.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the shelter for clarification.
* A tax receipt will be sent to you if you provide a mailing address for tax purposes.
