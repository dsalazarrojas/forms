# Hair Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about hair donations for a non-profit organization or charity. It will likely be used to record the details of the donors and their contributions, which can help the organization manage and track hair donations efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   **Fill out the form sections in order**. Start with the "Donation Form" section.
*   **Provide the required information** for each field. Some fields are not required, but it's best to fill out all fields for thorough documentation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Donation Form (Page 1)**:
    *   **Donor Information (Field 2)**:
        +   This field is for collecting basic contact information about the person making the donation, which includes their name and any other relevant details.

    *   **Contact Details (Field 3)**:
        +   This field is meant to collect more in-depth contact information from the donor for communication and follow-up purposes.

    *   **Donation Details (Field 4)**:
        +   This field will likely contain specific information about the hair donation, such as the quantity or condition of the hair being donated.

    *   **Medical Certificate (Field 5)**:
        +   This field might be used to attach any medical certification that supports the donation process, especially relevant in contexts where hair quality or quantity is critical. However, given its non-required status, it may not be applicable in all scenarios.

    *   **Hair Sample (Field 6)**:
        +   This field is likely meant for attaching any physical samples or descriptions of the hair being donated, such as photos or notes about its quality or condition.

## Tips

*   Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information for all fields.
*   If you're unsure about any field, refer to the instructions above or contact the form administrators for clarification.
*   This form is designed to be filled out digitally, so use the online interface provided.
*   If you're printing or sharing this form, ensure that it's for informational purposes and not to copy or duplicate the form without permission.
*   The information collected from this form will be used for non-profit purposes only, and any data shared is kept confidential and secure in accordance with data protection policies.
