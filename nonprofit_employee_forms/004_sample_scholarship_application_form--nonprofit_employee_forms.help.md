# Sample Scholarship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect application information from potential scholarship recipients.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, please read this guide carefully. Each section has a specific purpose, and providing accurate information will increase your chances of being considered for the scholarship. If you have any questions or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form** (`application_form`, text, optional): This section is for any additional information you would like to provide about your application. You can write a brief introduction or a statement of purpose.
* **Scholarship Details** (`scholarship_details`, text, optional): This section is for any additional details about the scholarship you are applying for. You can provide information about the scholarship's requirements, rules, or any other relevant information.
* **Applicant Info** (`applicant_info`, text, optional): This section is for your basic contact information, such as your name, email, and phone number.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): This section is for your basic contact information, such as your address, phone number, and email.
* **Education History** (`education_history`, text, optional): This section is for your academic history, including your school name, degree, and graduation date.
* **Employment History** (`employment_history`, text, optional): This section is for your employment history, including your current and previous employers, job title, and duration of employment.
* **Financial Info** (`financial_info`, text, optional): This section is for your financial information, including your income, expenses, and any other relevant financial details.
* **References** (`references`, text, optional): This section is for your professional or academic references, including their names, titles, and contact information.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): This section is for any additional information you would like to provide about yourself or your application.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, text, optional): This section is for you to agree to the terms and conditions of the scholarship program.
* **Review Process** (`review_process`, text, optional): This section is for any comments or feedback about the review process.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and truthful information to increase your chances of being considered for the scholarship.
* Do not leave any section blank if it is required.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
