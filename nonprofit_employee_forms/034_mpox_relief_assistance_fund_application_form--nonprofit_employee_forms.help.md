# mpox_relief_assistance_fund_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals affected by the mpox relief program apply for assistance. It is meant to be completed by individuals who have experienced mpox-related medical expenses, lost wages, or other costs and need financial support.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including phone number and email address.
2. Provide details about your medical expenses, including any medical expenses related to mpox.
3. Report any lost wages due to mpox-related reasons.
4. Describe any other costs related to mpox, such as transportation or childcare costs.
5. Indicate your financial support needs.
6. Provide details about your supporting document, if applicable.

## Tips

* Please provide as much detail as possible to ensure that your application is processed efficiently.
* If you are seeking assistance for multiple family members, you may need to complete the form multiple times.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to our team for assistance.
