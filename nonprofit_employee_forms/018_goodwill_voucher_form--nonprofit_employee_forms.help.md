# Goodwill Voucher Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Goodwill Voucher Form is used to collect information from employees who are requesting a Goodwill voucher for a specific reason.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name in the "employee_name" field.
2. Enter your organization name in the "organization_name" field.
3. Enter your employee ID in the "employee_id" field.
4. Specify the amount you are requesting in the "amount_requested" field.
5. Describe the reason for your voucher request in the "reason_for_voucher" field.
6. Select the status of your request in the "voucher_status" field.
7. Enter the date and time of your request in the "voucher_date" and "voucher_time" fields.
8. Provide any additional information or comments in the "notes" field.

* **Status of Request** (select_one, voucher_status, required): The current status of your request, which can be "pending", "approved", or "denied".

* **Additional Notes** (note, notes, required): Any additional comments or information related to your request.

* **City** (text, city, required): The city of your work location.
