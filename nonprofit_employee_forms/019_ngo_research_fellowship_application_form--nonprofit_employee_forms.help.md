# NGO Research Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to support nonprofit employees in applying for research fellowships. The form is used to collect relevant information about the applicant's proposal, research interests, and other necessary details for evaluation and review.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the proposal title and details in the text fields.
2. Select the correct options for candidate CV, research program details, research areas, research methods, expected outcomes, and selected location.
3. Enter the candidate's name, email, and phone number accurately.
4. Select the correct research fellowship duration in the date field.
5. Select the correct proposal deadline and research program start date in the date fields.
6. Select the correct research fellowship status and nonprofit employee form status in the dropdown menus.
7. Select the correct application submission status in the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Nonprofit Employee Form** (1): Fill in this field with the necessary information about your employment with the nonprofit.
*   **Proposal Title** (2): Enter a clear and concise title of your research proposal.
*   **Proposal Details** (3): Explain the details of your research proposal, including the background, objectives, and expected outcomes.
*   **Candidate CV** (4): Attach a copy of your CV, which should include your relevant work experience and education.
*   **Candidate Details** (5): Fill in this field with any additional information about yourself that may be relevant to your application.
*   **Research Program Details** (6): Explain the research program that you are applying for, including its goals and objectives.
*   **Selected Research Areas** (7): Select all the research areas that are relevant to your proposal.
*   **Selected Research Methods** (8): Select all the research methods that you will use in your research.
*   **Expected Outcomes** (9): Describe the expected outcomes of your research proposal.
*   **Candidate Name** (10): Fill in your full name.
*   **Email** (11): Enter your email address accurately.
*   **Phone** (12): Enter your phone number accurately.
*   **Research Fellowship Duration** (13): Enter the duration of the research fellowship in months.
*   **Proposal Deadline** (14): Enter the deadline for submitting the proposal in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Research Program Start Date** (15): Enter the start date of the research program in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Research Fellowship End Date** (16): Enter the end date of the research fellowship in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Selected Location** (17): Select the location where you will conduct your research.
*   **Research Fellowship Status** (18): Select the current status of your research fellowship application.
*   **Nonprofit Employee Form Status** (19): Select the status of your nonprofit employee form.
*   **Application Submission Status** (20): Select the status of your application submission.
*   **Research Fellowship Application Status** (21): Select the status of your research fellowship application.
