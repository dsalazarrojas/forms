# thank_you_gift_acknowledgment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for acknowledging and recording gifts made to a nonprofit organization. It helps the nonprofit track and document gift information, including the giver's details, gift description, and recipient's details.

The form is designed to be completed by both the giver and the recipient of the gift. The information collected will help the nonprofit in their record-keeping and reporting requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form in its entirety, providing accurate and truthful information.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Recipient Details** (`Recipient Details`, `text`, required: false): This section is for the recipient to provide their details, such as name and title.
*   **Gift Details** (`Gift Details`, `text`, required: false): This section is for the giver to describe the gift, including its description, value, or any other relevant details.
*   **Giver Details** (`Giver Details`, `text`, required: false): This section is for the giver to provide their details, such as name, address, and contact information.
*   **Delivery Date** (`Delivery Date`, `date`, required: false): This section is for the giver to provide the date the gift was delivered.
*   **Giver Signature** (`Giver Signature`, `note`, required: false): This section is for the giver to sign and date their acknowledgement of the gift.
*   **Recipient Signature** (`Recipient Signature`, `note`, required: false): This section is for the recipient to sign and date their acknowledgement of the gift.
*   **Gift Receipt** (`Gift Receipt`, `text`, required: false): This section is for the giver to provide a receipt of the gift, including the gift description, value, and any other relevant information.

## Tips

*   Make sure to complete the form accurately and honestly.
*   If you are unsure about any part of the form, contact the organization providing the gift for guidance.
*   Keep the form for your records and reference it as needed.
*   If you have any questions or need assistance, contact form@gic.mx.
