# Donor Outreach Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Outreach Checklist is a form used by nonprofit employees to record and track their outreach efforts and follow-up activities with donors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the donor's name in the "Donor Name" field.
2. Enter the donor's phone number in the "Donor Phone Number" field.
3. Check the boxes for any outreach efforts made, such as "Yes" or "No" for "Category 1" to "Category 10".
4. Select the follow-up method and frequency for the donor, such as "Active" or "Inactive" for "Follow-up Status".
5. Enter any follow-up notes and comments in the "Follow-up Notes" field.
6. Select the follow-up result, such as "Yes" or "No" for "Follow-up Result".
7. Finally, review and submit the form.
