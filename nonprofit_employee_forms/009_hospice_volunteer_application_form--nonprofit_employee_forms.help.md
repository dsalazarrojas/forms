# Hospice Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering at a hospice organization.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the application form on page 1 with your name and contact information.
- You may add any additional information you feel is relevant on page 2.
- Review and confirm your application on page 3.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Application Form** (`application_form`, text, required): Please provide your name, contact information, and a brief description of why you are interested in volunteering at the hospice.
  - This will help us understand who you are and what motivates you to volunteer with us.
  - Make sure to include your name and contact information so we can reach out to you for further discussion.
- **Additional Information** (Additional Information, note, optional): This is your chance to provide any extra details about yourself that might be helpful for us to know.
  - This could include any relevant work experience, skills, or interests that make you a strong candidate for volunteering with us.
  - Keep in mind that this is an optional field, so feel free to skip if you don't have anything to add.
- **Review Page** (Review Page, text, not required): Please review your application to ensure it's accurate and complete.
  - This is your last chance to make any corrections or additions before submitting your application.
  - Take a moment to review all your answers carefully before clicking submit.

## Tips
- Please make sure to proofread your application before submitting it.
- If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
- We appreciate your interest in volunteering with us and look forward to reviewing your application!
