# Environmental Restoration Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for an Environmental Restoration Grant Application.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before starting your application.
2. Ensure you have all the necessary supporting documents and information ready.
3. Fill out each section according to the guidelines provided.
4. Submit your application once you have completed all the sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details** (`project_details`, `text`, optional):
	+ Please provide a brief description of your project.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, `number`, optional):
	+ Provide the budget details for your project.
* **Project Timeline** (`project_timeline`, `date`, optional):
	+ Enter the project timeline.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, `text`, optional):
	+ Upload any supporting documents related to your project.
* **Nonprofit Employees** (`nonprofit_employees`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ List all nonprofit employees involved in the project.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter the location of the project.
* **Budget Allocation** (`budget_allocation`, `number`, optional):
	+ Provide the budget allocation details.
* **Grant Amount** (`grant_amount`, `number`, optional):
	+ Enter the grant amount you are requesting.
* **Grant Terms** (`grant_terms`, `text`, optional):
	+ Provide any additional grant terms or conditions.
* **Supporting Document 1** (`supporting_doc_1`, `text`, optional):
	+ Upload the first supporting document.
* **Supporting Document 2** (`supporting_doc_2`, `text`, optional):
	+ Upload the second supporting document.
* **Supporting Document 3** (`supporting_doc_3`, `text`, optional):
	+ Upload the third supporting document.
* **Supporting Document 4** (`supporting_doc_4`, `text`, optional):
	+ Upload the fourth supporting document.
* **Supporting Document 5** (`supporting_doc_5`, `text`, optional):
	+ Upload the fifth supporting document.
* **Date Submitter Signed** (`date_submitted`, `date`, optional):
	+ Enter the date you signed the submission.
* **Submitter Signature** (`submitter_signature`, `text`, optional):
	+ Sign your submission.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your name.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, `email`, optional):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Job Title** (`submitter_job_title`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your job title.
* **Grant Agreement** (`grant_agreement`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Choose whether you have agreed to grant terms.
* **Agreed to Terms** (`grant_terms_agreed`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Choose whether you have agreed to terms.
* **Project Details 1** (`project_details_1`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter additional project details.
* **Project Details 2** (`project_details_2`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter more project details.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have all the necessary information and supporting documents ready before filling out this form.
* Review each field carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.
* Submit your application once you have completed all the sections.
