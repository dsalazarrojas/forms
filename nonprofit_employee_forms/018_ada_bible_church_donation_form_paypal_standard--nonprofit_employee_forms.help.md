## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Donor information
* **Donor information** (`donor_information`, `note`, `false`): This field is for any additional information or comments that the donor would like to include with their donation.

### Donor name
* **Donor name** (`donor_name`, `text`, `true`): Please enter the full name of the donor for receipt purposes.

### Contact email
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, `true`): Enter the donor's contact email address for donation receipt and further communication.

### Donation amount
* **Donation amount** (`donation_amount`, `number`, `true`): Enter the amount of the donation in US dollars.

### Donation designation
* **Donation designation** (`donation_designation`, `text`, `false`): If the donor has a specific purpose for their donation, such as the missions or building fund, they can enter it here.

### Payment method
* **Payment method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, `true`): Select the payment method you would like to use for this donation. Currently, PayPal, credit card, and bank transfer are available.

### Set up recurring gift
* **Set up recurring gift** (`recurring_gift`, `select_one`, `true`): If you would like to set up a recurring gift, choose the frequency: one-time, weekly, monthly, or annually.

### Address for receipt
* **Address for tax receipt** (`address_for_receipt`, `text`, `false`): If you would like to receive a tax-deductible receipt, please provide your address here.

### Make gift anonymous
* **Make gift anonymous** (`anonymous_gift`, `select_one`, `true`): If you would like to make this gift anonymous, please select this option.

### Additional notes
* **Additional notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, `false`): If you have any additional comments or instructions for the church, please enter them here.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Use a valid email address for the contact email field.
* Enter a valid US dollar amount for the donation amount field.
* Select a valid payment method and frequency for recurring gifts.
* If making a gift anonymous, ensure you are comfortable with not having your name published.
* Review all fields carefully before submitting the form to ensure accurate and complete information.
