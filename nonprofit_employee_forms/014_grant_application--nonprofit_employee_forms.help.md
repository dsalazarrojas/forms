# Grant Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grant Application form is used to collect information for non-profit organizations to apply for grants. It includes fields that cover essential details such as organization name, grant type, budget period, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Ensure that the organization name is correctly spelled and matches the non-profit registration number (if applicable).
3. Choose the correct grant type and budget period.
4. Provide detailed contact information for the organization.
5. Complete the grant amount, start date, and end date for the requested grant.
6. Describe the purpose of the grant and provide a detailed budget description.
7. Attach any necessary documents or files to support the grant application.
8. If applicable, provide the non-profit certification number and registration number.
9. Confirm the grantor's contact information and address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, required/required): Enter the name of your non-profit organization.
* **Grant Type** (`grant_type`, required/required): Choose the type of grant you are applying for.
* **Budget Period** (`budget_period`, required/required): Select the budget period for the grant.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, required/required): Enter your contact name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, required/required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, required/required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Grant Amount** (`grant_amount`, required/required): Enter the amount of the grant you are requesting.
* **Grant Start Date** (`grant_start_date`, required/required): Enter the start date of the grant.
* **Grant End Date** (`grant_end_date`, required/required): Enter the end date of the grant.
* **Purpose of Grant** (`purpose_of_grant`, required/required): Describe the purpose of the grant in a few words.
* **Budget Description** (`budget_description`, required/required): Provide a detailed description of your budget.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, optional/required): Provide any additional comments or information that you feel is relevant to the grant application.
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, optional/optional): Attach any supporting documents or files to your grant application.
* **Grant Attachments** (`grant_application_attachments`, optional/optional): Attach any additional documents or files that support your grant application.
* **Non-Profit Certification** (`non_profit_certification`, optional/optional): If your organization is certified as a non-profit, select 'Yes'.
* **Non-Profit Registration Number** (`non_profit_registration_number`, optional/optional): If your organization is registered as a non-profit, enter the registration number.
* **Grantor Institution** (`grantor_institution`, optional/optional): Enter the name of the institution granting the grant.
* **Grantor Contact** (`grantor_contact`, optional/optional): Enter the name of the contact person for the grantor.
* **Grantor Address** (`grantor_address`, optional/optional): Enter the address of the grantor.
* **Grantor Phone** (`grantor_phone`, optional/optional): Enter the phone number of the grantor.
* **Grantor Email** (`grantor_email`, optional/optional): Enter the email address of the grantor.
* **Grantor Fax** (`grantor_fax`, optional/optional): Enter the fax number of the grantor.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, optional/optional): Enter the date for the grant review.
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, optional/optional): Attach any additional documents or files to support the grant application.
