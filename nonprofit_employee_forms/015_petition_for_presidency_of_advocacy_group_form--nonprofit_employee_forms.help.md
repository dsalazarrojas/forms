# Petition For Presidency Of Advocacy Group Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Petition For Presidency Of Advocacy Group Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in taking on a leadership role within an advocacy group.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Main Information" page to provide your basic details.
2. On the "Applicant Name" page, enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
3. On the "Contact Information" page, enter your email address and any other contact details you wish to provide.
4. On the "Reason for Applying" page, explain in a brief statement why you are interested in becoming the president of the advocacy group.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Information** (`Main Information`, text, required/optional): This page is where you can enter your basic information, such as your name, title, or role within the group.
* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address and any other contact details you wish to provide. This information will help us contact you if needed.
* **Reason for Applying** (`reason_for_applying`, text, required/optional): In this field, explain in a few sentences why you are interested in becoming the president of the advocacy group.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure your application is complete.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
