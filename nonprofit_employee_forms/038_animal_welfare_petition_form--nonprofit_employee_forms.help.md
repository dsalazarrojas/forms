# Animal Welfare Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Welfare Petition Form is used to collect signatures and information from concerned individuals who support animal welfare causes. This form helps raise awareness and gathers support to drive legislative change for animal welfare issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Petition Title or Subject**, which is the brief description of the issue you support.
2. Provide your **Full Name** and **Email Address** so we can contact you for updates and follow-up.
3. Enter your **City and State or Province** and **Country** so we can track where the support is coming from.
4. Choose your **Professional Affiliation** from the list provided, if applicable.
5. Select whether you want to be **contacted for Updates** and if you want your **Signature to be shared Publicly**.
6. If you have a specific **Reason for Signing**, please provide a brief explanation.
7. If you are a professional with expertise in this area, you can provide additional **Support for Specific Legislative Change**.
8. Finally, enter the **Date Signed** to show when you signed the petition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Petition Title or Subject**: This is a brief description of the issue you support. Be specific and clear in your title.
* **Signatory Full Name**: This is your full name, which will be displayed when you share your signature publicly or when we contact you for updates.
* **Email Address**: This is your email address, which will be used to contact you for updates and follow-up.
* **City and State or Province**: This is the city and state or province where you live. Please enter your city and state or province as it appears on your address.
* **Country**: This is the country where you live. Please select your country from the list provided.
* **Professional Affiliation**: This is your professional affiliation, if any. Choose one of the provided options that best describes your role.
* **Will you be contacted for Updates**: Select "Yes" if you want us to contact you for updates, and "No" if you do not want us to contact you.
* **Share Signature Publicly**: Select "Yes" if you want your signature to be displayed publicly, and "No" if you want to keep it private.
* **Reason for Signing**: This is an optional field, but if you have a specific reason for signing the petition, please provide a brief explanation.
* **Support for Specific Legislative Change**: If you are a professional with expertise in this area, you can provide additional support for specific legislative changes.
* **Date Signed**: This is a required field, and you must enter the date when you signed the petition.

## Tips
Please fill out the form carefully and accurately. If you need help or have questions, contact us at form@gic.mx.
