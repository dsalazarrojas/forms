# Church Background Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Background Check Form is a document used to verify the identity and background of individuals applying for various roles within the church. It's essential to complete this form accurately and truthfully to ensure a thorough background check can be conducted.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Begin with the Personal Identification section, which includes the Full Legal Name, Date of Birth, and Social Security Number. Ensure that you enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Move on to the Residence History section, where you'll list your current and previous addresses for the last 7 years. Be sure to include the city, state, and dates of residency for each address.
3. In the Criminal History Disclosure section, answer the question about having been convicted of a crime (other than minor traffic violations). If you have been convicted, provide details about the offense, including the date, location, and nature of the crime.
4. Finally, review the authorization and acknowledgement sections, where you'll confirm your consent to the background check and acknowledge receipt of the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) summary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Legal Name** (`full_legal_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents, including your middle name if applicable.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Provide your date of birth for identity verification purposes.
* **Social Security Number** (`ssn`, text, required): Securely enter your Social Security Number for background screening.
* **Current Residential Address** (`current_address`, text, required): Enter your current address, including city and state.
* **How long have you lived at this address?** (`length_of_residence`, text, required): Describe how long you've lived at your current address.
* **List previous addresses for the last 7 years** (`previous_addresses`, text, required): Provide a list of previous addresses, including city, state, and dates of residency.
* **Have you ever been convicted of a crime?** (`criminal_conviction_disclosure`, select_one, required): Answer truthfully about any past convictions (excluding minor traffic violations).
* **If yes, please provide details** (`conviction_details`, text, required): If you've been convicted, enter the date, location, and nature of the crime.
* **I authorize the church to conduct a background check** (`authorization_consent`, select_one, required): Confirm your consent for the background check.
* **I acknowledge receipt of the FCRA summary** (`fair_credit_reporting_act_ack`, select_one, required): Acknowledge the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) summary provided.
* **Electronic Signature (Full Name)** (`applicant_signature_name`, text, required): Enter your electronic signature, including your full name.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Date and time of signing.

Note: Remember to sign the form digitally and date it accurately.
