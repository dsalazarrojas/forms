# COVID 19 2020 Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The COVID 19 2020 Volunteer Registration Form is designed to help individuals register their interest in volunteering with the organization's response to the pandemic. By completing this form, you will be able to indicate your availability, areas of interest, and provide necessary contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Personal Information**: Fill out the following fields with your personal details:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email Address (we will use this to contact you)
	* Phone Number (your primary contact number)
2. **Volunteer Interest**: Select the volunteer role(s) you are interested in:
	* Medical Support
	* Administrative Support
	* Community Outreach
	* Data Management
	* Other (please specify in the field below)
3. **Availability**: Choose the days you are available to volunteer:
	* Monday
	* Tuesday
	* Wednesday
	* Thursday
	* Friday
	* Saturday
	* Sunday
4. **Medical Background**: If you have a medical or healthcare background, please select "True". This information will help with volunteer placement.
5. **Vaccination Status**: Select your current vaccination status:
	* Fully Vaccinated
	* Partially Vaccinated
	* Not Vaccinated (Note: This is a required field for volunteer placement)
6. **Emergency Contact**: Provide contact information for an emergency contact:
	* Name
	* Phone Number
7. **Agreement**: Agree to follow all COVID-19 safety protocols:
	* I Agree
	* I Do Not Agree (Note: This is a required field)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name.
* **Last Name**: Your last name.
* **Email Address**: We will use this to contact you about volunteer opportunities and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Your primary contact number.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth.
* **Street Address**: Your street address.
* **City**: Your city.
* **State**: Your state.
* **Zip Code**: Your zip code.
* **Volunteer Role Interest**: Select the volunteer role(s) that interest you. Please see the options below for choices.
* **Days Available to Volunteer**: Choose the days you are available to volunteer.
* **Do you have prior volunteer experience**: If you have experience volunteering, please select "True".
* **Do you have medical or healthcare background**: If you have a medical or healthcare background, please select "True".
* **Vaccination Status**: Please select your current vaccination status.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Phone number of the emergency contact.
* **I agree to follow all COVID-19 safety protocols**: Please select "I Agree".

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields marked as "required" are completed correctly.
* For medical or healthcare background, please ensure that you provide accurate information to ensure proper volunteer placement.
* The organization will use your email address to contact you, so please ensure it is up-to-date and correct.
* If you have any issues or concerns while filling out the form, please contact the organization's administrators.
