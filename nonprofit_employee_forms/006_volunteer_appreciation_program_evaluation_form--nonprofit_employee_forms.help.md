# Volunteer Appreciation Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the effectiveness of our volunteer appreciation program. It gathers information about volunteers' experiences, feedback, and suggestions for recognition methods, as well as their roles and departments within the organization. The form is used to identify areas of improvement and to provide valuable insights for future program development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the volunteer's name in the "Volunteer Name" field.
2.  Input the volunteer's email address in the "email" field (optional).
3.  Select the volunteer's role within the organization from the "Volunteer Role" dropdown menu.
4.  Enter the department the volunteer is working with.
5.  In the "Feedback" field, provide a brief description of your experience with the volunteer.
6.  Select the frequency at which you appreciate the volunteer's work (Always, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never).
7.  Choose the methods of recognition you would like to use for the volunteer (Publicly, Privately, or Both).
8.  If you have any additional suggestions, enter them in the "Suggestion" field (optional).
9.  Provide any comments about the volunteer's work in the "Comments" field (optional).
10.  Enter the user responsible for assigning recognition methods in the "Assigned User" field.
11.  Choose a date and time to schedule the appreciation event (format: month/day/year, 00:00).
12.  Finally, select how you would like to proceed with the form ("Submit" to submit the form, "Save and Continue" to save and return to the form later, or "Save and Edit" to save and make changes to the form).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Volunteer Name (1)** (`volunteer_name`, `text`, required): This is the field where you will enter the volunteer's name.
*   **Email (2)** (`email`, `email`, optional): This is the field where you will input the volunteer's email address, if available.
*   **Volunteer Role (3)** (`role`, `select_one`, required): This is the dropdown menu where you will select the volunteer's role within the organization (Volunteer, Non-Board Member, Staff Member, or Other).
*   **Department (4)** (`department`, `text`, required): This is the field where you will enter the department the volunteer is working with.
*   **Feedback (5)** (`feedback`, `text`, required): In this field, provide a brief description of your experience with the volunteer.
*   **Appreciation Frequency (6)** (`appreciation_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose how often you appreciate the volunteer's work (Always, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never).
*   **Recognition Methods (7)** (`recognition_methods`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the methods of recognition you would like to use for the volunteer (Publicly, Privately, or Both).
*   **Suggestion (8)** (`suggestion`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional suggestions for the volunteer.
*   **Comments (9)** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any comments about the volunteer's work.
*   **Assigned User (10)** (`assigned_user`, `text`, required): Enter the user responsible for assigning recognition methods.
*   **Date and Time (11)** (`date_time`, `date`, required): Choose a date and time to schedule the appreciation event (format: month/day/year, 00:00).
*   **Submit (12)** (`submit`, `select_one`, required): Select how you would like to proceed with the form ("Submit" to submit the form, "Save and Continue" to save and return to the form later, or "Save and Edit" to save and make changes to the form).

Note: Fields marked as "required" must be filled out to proceed with the form.
