# Mpox Engagement Fund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from nonprofit employees to allocate funds for public health responses, education, and prevention related to Mpox.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information.
2. Ensure that you are submitting for a legitimate Mpox-related need.
3. Review your submission carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Nonprofit Employees
* **Nonprofit Employees** (`nonprofit_employees`, text, optional): Enter the number of nonprofit employees involved in the Mpox response.

### Prevention Funds
* **Prevention Funds** (`prevention_funds`, number, optional): Enter the funds required for prevention measures.

### Education Funds
* **Education Funds** (`education_funds`, number, optional): Enter the funds needed for education and awareness campaigns.

### Public Health Response Funds
* **Public Health Response Funds** (`response_funds`, number, optional): Enter the funds required for public health response.

### Contact Name
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.

### Contact Email
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email. Please use a valid email address.

### Phone Numbers
* **Phone Numbers** (`phone_numbers`, text, optional): Enter phone numbers separated by commas.

### Funding Request
* **Funding Request** (`funding_request`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your funding request.

### Assigned Tool
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool assigned to support your Mpox response efforts, either Chatjimmy or Other.

### Category
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the category of your submission, either Nonprofit Employee Forms or Mpox Engagement Forms.

**Tips:**

* Ensure that your submission is accurate and detailed.
* Review your submission carefully before submitting.
* If you have any doubts, please contact the relevant authorities.
