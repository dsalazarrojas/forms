# Volunteer Hours Donation Record Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record and track volunteer hours donated to the organization. It helps to accurately document the time spent by volunteers on various activities, ensuring that their contributions are acknowledged and valued.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Volunteer Hours Form" page.
2. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Volunteer ID**: Enter the unique identifier assigned to the volunteer.
	* **Hours Donated**: Type the total number of hours donated.
	* **Date Donated**: Enter the date on which the hours were donated.
	* **Time Donated**: Select the time of day when the hours were donated.
3. Optionally, enter the **Volunteer Name**, if it is not already provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer ID** (`volunteer_id`, number, required): This is the unique identifier assigned to the volunteer. Please enter the actual ID number as provided by the volunteer management system or database.
* **Volunteer Name** (`volunteer_name`, text, optional): If you're filling out a form for a volunteer who is not already in our system, please enter their name as it appears on their ID or on our volunteer database.
* **Hours Donated** (`hours_donated`, number, required): Type the total number of hours spent on the activity. This should be a whole number (e.g., 5, 10, 20, etc.).
* **Date Donated** (`date_donated`, date, required): Enter the date on which the hours were donated. Use the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time Donated** (`time_donated`, time, required): Select the time of day when the hours were donated. This is used to record the time within the day when the volunteer worked.
