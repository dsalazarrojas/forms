# rehabilitation_center_fund_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from donors for the rehabilitation center's fund. This form is used to gather contact details, donation amount, frequency, and special circumstances or notes from the donors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information in the "Contact Information" page.
2. Enter the amount you wish to donate in the "Donor Amount" field.
3. Select your preferred donation frequency in the "Donation Frequency" dropdown menu.
4. If you have any special circumstances or requests related to your donation, select them in the "Special Donation" multiple-choice field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
7. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (contact_information)** (`text`, **Required**): This page is used to collect your contact information, including your name and any other details we need to reach out to you.
* **Donor Amount (donor_amount)** (`Number`, **Optional**): Enter the amount you wish to donate to the rehabilitation center's fund. Please enter the amount without any symbols or decimal points.
* **Donation Frequency (donation_frequency)** (`Select One`, **Required**): Select how frequently you would like to donate to the rehabilitation center's fund. Please choose one of the options provided.
* **Special Donation (special_donation)** (`Select Multiple`, **Optional**): If you have any special circumstances or requests related to your donation, select them from the options provided.
* **Email (email)** (`Email`, **Required**): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone (phone)** (`Text`, **Optional**): If you have a phone number, enter it so we can reach out to you.
* **Notes (notes)** (`Note`, **Optional**): Provide any additional notes or comments related to your donation.
