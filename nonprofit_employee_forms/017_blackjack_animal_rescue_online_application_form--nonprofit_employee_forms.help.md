# Blackjack Animal Rescue Online Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals interested in adopting a pet from Blackjack Animal Rescue. The form will help us understand your needs, preferences, and qualifications to ensure the best match between you and a pet that fits your lifestyle.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure a successful adoption, please carefully fill out this online application form. Each section will guide you through the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Animal Adoption Application (Page 1)**: This page provides an introduction to the form and thanks you for your interest in adopting from Blackjack Animal Rescue.
* **Full Name (Required)**: Enter your complete legal name.
* **Email Address (Required)**: Provide your primary contact email address for communication.
* **Phone Number (Required)**: Enter your best contact phone number.
* **Street Address (Required)**: Enter your full street address.
* **City (Required)**: Enter your city name.
* **State (Required)**: Enter your state name.
* **ZIP Code (Required)**: Enter your postal code.
* **Housing Type (Required)**: Select the type of home you have (House - Owned, House - Rented, Apartment, Condo, Townhouse, or Farm).
* **Yard Information (Required)**: Choose whether you have a yard (Large Fenced Yard, Small Fenced Yard, Unfenced Yard, No Yard, or Not Applicable).
* **Landlord Approval (Required)**: If you rent, confirm if your landlord approves of pets.
* **Current Pets (Required)**: Indicate if you currently have pets.
* **Pet Details (Optional)**: List any current pets with breed and age if applicable.
* **Pet Experience (Required)**: Describe your experience with animals.
* **Animal Preference (Required)**: Choose the type of animal you are interested in (Dog, Cat, or Either).
* **Age Preference (Required)**: Select the preferred age of the animal (Puppy or Kitten, Young 1-3 years, Adult 4-7 years, Senior 8 plus years, or No Preference).
* **Size Preference (Optional)**: Select the preferred size of the dog (Small under 20 lbs, Medium 20-50 lbs, Large 50-80 lbs, Extra Large over 80 lbs, or No Preference).
* **Activity Level (Required)**: Choose how active you are (Very Active, Moderately Active, or Mostly Sedentary).
* **Work Schedule (Required)**: Select how many hours you are away from home daily (Home Most of Day, Away 4-6 Hours, Away 7-9 Hours, Away 10 plus Hours).
* **Children in Home (Required)**: Indicate if you have children.
* **Children Ages (Optional)**: List the ages of your children if applicable.
* **Veterinarian Reference (Optional)**: Provide the name and contact of your veterinarian if applicable.
* **Personal Reference (Required)**: Enter the name and contact of a personal reference.
* **Why Do You Want to Adopt (Required)**: Explain why you want to adopt a pet from Blackjack Animal Rescue.
* **Additional Comments (Optional)**: Share any additional information you think would be helpful for us to know.
