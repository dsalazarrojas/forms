# Community Service Project Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Service Project Voting Form is a tool for community members and staff to vote for their preferred community service projects. This form ensures that each vote is unique and counts towards the project selection process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name as you would like to be listed on the project selection list.
2.  Enter your email address for verification purposes.
3.  Select your top choice projects from the list provided.
4.  Provide a brief explanation (optional) for your selection.
5.  Check if you would like to submit your vote anonymously.
6.  Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Voter full name** (`voter_name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name as you would like to be listed on the project selection list.
* **Voter email** (`voter_email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address for verification purposes. This will help us to contact you if needed.
* **Projects to vote on** (`project_list`, Select Multiple, Required): Select your top choice projects from the list provided.
* **Top choice project** (`top_choice`, Select One, Required): Select your single top preference among the projects.
* **Rationale for selection** (`rationale`, Text, Optional): Provide a brief explanation for your selection, if desired.
* **Affiliation or role** (`affiliation`, Text, Optional): Enter your affiliation or role (e.g., Volunteer, staff, resident, other).
* **Submit vote anonymously** (`anonymous_vote`, Select One, Optional): Choose Yes to hide your identity, if you prefer to vote anonymously.
