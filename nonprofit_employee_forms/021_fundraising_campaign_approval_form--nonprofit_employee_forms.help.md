# Fundraising Campaign Approval Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fundraising Campaign Approval Form is used by administrators to review and approve or reject proposals for new fundraising campaigns. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go to the first page and fill in your name and any other required information.
2. Move to the second page and select the correct campaign details from the dropdown menu. This will help the administrator to quickly identify the specific campaign you are requesting approval for.
3. Enter the name of the campaign on the third page.
4. Enter a brief description of the campaign on the fourth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page** (first_page, text, optional): This page is where you can add any additional information that may be relevant to the campaign. This could be your name, contact details, or any other relevant contact information.
* **Campaign Details** (campaign_details, select_one, required): This field allows you to select the specific campaign you are requesting approval for. Please ensure you select the correct campaign details to avoid delays in processing your request.
* **Campaign Name** (campaign_name, text, optional): This field is used to add a name to the campaign. This will help to identify the campaign and make it easier for the administrator to track.
* **Campaign Description** (campaign_description, text, optional): This field is used to add a brief description of the campaign. This will help the administrator to understand the purpose and goals of the campaign.
