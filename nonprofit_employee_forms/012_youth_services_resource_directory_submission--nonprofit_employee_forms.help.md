# Youth Services Resource Directory Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Services Resource Directory Submission form is a tool for organizations to submit their services to the Youth Services Resource Directory. The directory aims to provide a comprehensive list of services that cater to the needs of youth, making it easier for them to access resources and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your organization's information, including its name, address, and contact details.
2. Describe the services you offer, including their location and any specific details about them.
3. Specify the target audience for your services and the services you offer.
4. Add any additional information, such as service start and end dates, to help others understand your services better.
5. Finally, submit your form and wait for it to be reviewed and approved by the directory administrators.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter your organization's name as it appears on your business cards and other official documents.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your organization's address, including the street address, apartment number, and city.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number, optional): Enter your organization's zip code.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city or town name.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter your state or province name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your organization's email address, where you can be reached for inquiries and communications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your organization's phone number.
* **Website** (`website`, text, optional): Enter your organization's website URL.
* **Service Description** (`service_description`, text, optional): Describe the services you offer in detail, including their benefits and unique features.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, text, optional): Specify the age group or demographic that your services cater to.
* **Services Offered** (`services_offered`, text, optional): List the services you offer, including any special services or programs.
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your services, such as their start and end dates.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who will be handling inquiries and communications.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, text, optional): Specify the type of service you offer, such as counseling or education.
* **Service Location** (`service_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where your services are offered.
* **Service Start Date** (`service_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your services.
* **Service End Date** (`service_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your services.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your services.
* **Tags** (`tags`, select_multiple, optional): Select the tags that best describe your services.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you are submitting your services on behalf of someone else.
