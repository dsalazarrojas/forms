# Donor Data Usage Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Data Usage Acknowledgement Form is a mandatory form designed to capture an individual's consent for the use and handling of their personal data. This form ensures that donors understand and agree to the usage of their data for specific purposes, such as tracking donations and fund management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply fill in the required fields and follow these steps:
1. Enter your **First Name** in the corresponding field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the corresponding field.
3. Provide your **Email** address for communication purposes.
4. Enter your **Phone** number, if applicable.
5. Select your **Date of Birth** from the calendar picker.
6. Enter your **Social Security Number** (if provided).
7. Indicate whether you have made a **Donor Donation** to one or more funds, selecting the relevant option(s).
8. If you have made a **Donor Donation**, select the date it was made.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your given name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your preferred email address where you can be reached for communication regarding your donation or other related matters.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you want us to contact you regarding your donation or other related matters (optional).
* **Date of Birth**: Select your date of birth from the calendar picker to verify your identity.
* **Social Security Number**: If you have provided your Social Security Number for tax purposes, enter it here (if applicable).
* **Donor Donation Fund**: Select the fund(s) to which you have made a donation. Choose 'Yes' if you have made a donation to this fund.
* **Donor Donation Date**: If you have selected 'Yes' for the **Donor Donation Fund** field, enter the date of your donation.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have filled in all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* This form is for the purpose of confirming your consent to the use and handling of your personal data.
