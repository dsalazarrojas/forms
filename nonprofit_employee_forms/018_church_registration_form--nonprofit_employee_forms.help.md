# Church Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from individuals who want to register with our church. It collects contact and demographic information, as well as details about their attendance and interests. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that we can effectively reach out to you and keep you informed about our church activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each field carefully, making sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. You can save your progress as you fill out the form, but please note that you will need to submit the form in its entirety before it can be processed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): This field is where you enter your first name. Please use your full first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): This field is for your last name. Again, please use your full last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): This is where you provide your valid email address that we can use to contact you. Please make sure it is up to date and correct.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): This is your contact phone number where we can reach you. Please include the area code.
* **Street Address** (`address`, text, required): This is the physical address where you can be contacted or reached at. Please include the street number and name.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the name of the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the abbreviation for the state where you live (e.g., CA, NY, FL, etc.).
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): This field is for your date of birth. Please use the mm/dd/yyyy format (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Marital Status** (`marital_status`, select_one, required): Choose your current marital status from the provided options.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, text, optional): This field is for your occupation or profession. It is not required but can be useful for our records.
* **How Often Do You Attend Church** (`church_attendance`, select_one, required): Choose how often you attend church from the provided options.
* **Have You Been Baptized** (`baptism_status`, select_one, required): Indicate your baptism status from the options provided.
* **What Are You Interested In** (`interested_in`, select_multiple, required): Select all the activities you're interested in from the provided list. You can select multiple options.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_heard`, select_one, required): Choose how you heard about the church from the provided options.
* **Do You Have a Prayer Request** (`prayer_request`, text, optional): If you have a prayer request, please share it in this field. You can write as much or as little as you want.
* **Any Special Needs or Accommodations** (`special_needs`, text, optional): If you have any special needs or requirements, please let us know here.
* **Would You Like to Receive Our Newsletter** (`newsletter_signup`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to receive our newsletter from the provided options.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, please write them here. This is your chance to let us know anything else we might need to know.
