# Nonprofit Membership Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nonprofit Membership Form Template is designed to collect and update information for nonprofit employees. It helps track and manage membership details efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Provide your Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. Select your Non Profit affiliation (Non Profit, For Profit, or Not For Profit) in the Non Profit field.
6. Choose your Member Type (Member, Non-Member, or Staff) in the Member Type field.
7. Enter your Address in the Address field.
8. Enter your City in the City field.
9. Enter your State in the State field.
10. Enter your Zip code in the Zip field.
11. Select your Country of residence in the Country field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or official documents.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or official documents.
* **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address associated with your nonprofit account.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Non Profit** (Non Profit, select_one, required/optional): Select your nonprofit organization type (Non Profit, For Profit, or Not For Profit).
* **Member Type** (Member Type, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your member type (Member, Non-Member, or Staff).
* **Address** (Address, text, required/optional): Enter your current address.
* **City** (City, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the city where you reside.
* **State** (State, text, required/optional): Enter the state where you reside.
* **Zip** (Zip, text, required/optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Country** (Country, text, required/optional): Select your country of residence.

## Tips
* Use the same format as your government-issued ID for your first and last names.
* Ensure your email and phone number are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are a member, select Member. If you are not a member, choose Non-Member or Staff.
* If your nonprofit organization has multiple branches, select Non Profit accordingly.
* Use a valid address, city, state, and zip code to ensure accurate mailing and communication purposes.
* Select your country of residence accurately for proper mailing and communication.
