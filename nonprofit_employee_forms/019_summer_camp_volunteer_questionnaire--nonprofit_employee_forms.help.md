# Summer Camp Volunteer Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect volunteer information for a summer camp, including personal details, availability, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Indicate your volunteer start and end dates.
3. Choose your preferred shifts (Morning, Afternoon, Night).
4. Specify your availability for volunteering.
5. Select your volunteer frequency (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
6. Choose your volunteer status (Active, Inactive).
7. Provide any additional comments or information about your volunteer status and experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Name** (`volunteer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like to be known as at the summer camp.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your personal email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for quick contact and emergencies.
* **Volunteer Position** (`volunteer_position`, text, optional): Indicate your position or role at the summer camp (e.g., leader, counselor, etc.).
* **Volunteer Start Date** (`volunteer_start_date`, date, optional): Enter your start date of volunteering at the summer camp.
* **Volunteer End Date** (`volunteer_end_date`, date, optional): Enter your end date of volunteering at the summer camp.
* **Shift Preferences** (`shift_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred shifts (Morning, Afternoon, Night).
* **Volunteer Availability** (`volunteer_availability`, time, optional): Specify your availability for volunteering (e.g., full-time, part-time, flexible).
* **Volunteer Frequency** (`volunteer_frequency`, select_one, optional): Indicate how frequently you would like to volunteer (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Volunteer Status** (`volunteer_status`, select_one, optional): Choose your current volunteer status (Active, Inactive).
* **Volunteer Comments** (`volunteer_comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your volunteer experience.
