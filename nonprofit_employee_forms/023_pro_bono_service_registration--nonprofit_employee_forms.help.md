# Pro Bono Service Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pro Bono Service Registration form is designed to collect information about the services offered by an organization. It will help us understand the services provided, the organization's details, and other relevant information to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the organization from which the service is requested.
2. Enter the name of the service you are registering.
3. Provide your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Choose the service type from the options provided.
6. Select a date for the service request.
7. Choose a time for the service request.
8. Enter a follow-up date for the service.
9. Enter the follow-up time.
10. Provide a brief description of the service.
11. Choose which tool(s) will be used for the service.
12. Select the status of the service.
13. Choose the category of the service.
14. Select any subcategories that apply to the service.
15. Add any relevant service tags.
16. (Optional) Add any location information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization**: Enter the name of the organization that offers the service.
* **Name**: Enter the name of the service you are registering.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Service Type**: Choose the type of service you are offering.
* **Service Request Date**: Select a date for the service request.
* **Service Request Time**: Choose a time for the service request.
* **Follow-up Date**: Enter a follow-up date for the service.
* **Follow-up Time**: Enter the follow-up time for the service.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the service you are offering.
* **Assigned Tool**: Choose which tool(s) will be used for the service.
* **Status**: Choose the status of the service (Active or Inactive).
* **Category**: Choose the category of the service.
* **Subcategory**: Choose any subcategory that apply to the service.
* **Service Tags**: Enter any relevant service tags.
* **Location**: (Optional) Enter any location information.
* **Tags 2**, **Tags 3**, **Tags 4**: These fields are for optional additional tags. You can enter any relevant information for each.
