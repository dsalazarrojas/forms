# Employee Empowerment Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Empowerment Evaluation Survey is a tool used to gather feedback from nonprofit employees on their experience and perceptions related to their role and work environment. This survey will help the organization to identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions to foster a positive and productive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please answer each question with your honest thoughts and feelings.
2. Be as specific and detailed as possible in your answers.
3. Take your time to complete the form, and don't hesitate to ask if you need any assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_id (Number)**: Please enter your employee ID number as per your organization's employee database.
* **question1 (Text)**: How do you feel about your current role and responsibilities? Please describe your experiences and challenges.
* **question2 (Text)**: What are some areas where you feel you have opportunities for growth and development?
* **question3 (Text)**: Can you think of any suggestions to improve our organization's policies and procedures?
* **question4 (Text)**: Do you feel that your contributions and ideas are valued and recognized within the organization?
* **question5 (Text)**: Have you ever felt undervalued or unsupported in your role? If so, describe the situation.
* **question6 (Text)**: Can you describe a situation where you felt empowered and confident in your role?
* **question7 (Text)**: What are some suggestions for increasing employee autonomy and self-determination within the organization?
* **question8 (Text)**: Do you have a clear understanding of your role's objectives and expectations?
* **question9 (Text)**: Can you describe a situation where you felt micromanaged or over-controlled in your role?
* **question10 (Text)**: How do you rate the support and resources provided by the organization?
* **question11 (Text)**: Can you suggest any new tools or resources that would improve your work productivity?
* **question12 (Text)**: What do you think are the biggest challenges facing the organization today?
* **question13 (Text)**: Do you feel that your manager or supervisor provides sufficient feedback and guidance?
* **question14 (Text)**: Can you think of any ways to improve communication within the organization?
* **question15 (Text)**: Have you ever felt pressure to conform to certain expectations or norms within the organization?
* **question16 (Text)**: Can you describe a situation where you were able to make a positive impact or contribution to the organization?
* **question17 (Text)**: What are some suggestions for increasing employee engagement and motivation within the organization?
* **question18 (Text)**: Do you feel that your work is aligned with the organization's mission and values?
* **question19 (Text)**: Can you suggest any changes to the organization's leadership structure or decision-making processes?
* **question20 (Text)**: Have you ever felt that your role was not aligned with your skills and abilities?
* **question21 (Text)**: Can you think of any ways to improve the organization's performance management process?
* **question22 (Text)**: Do you feel that your work is evaluated fairly and consistently?
* **question23 (Text)**: Can you describe a situation where you felt proud of your work or accomplishment?
* **question24 (Text)**: What are some suggestions for increasing employee well-being and work-life balance within the organization?
* **question25 (Text)**: Is there anything else you would like to share or suggest for improving the organization?
