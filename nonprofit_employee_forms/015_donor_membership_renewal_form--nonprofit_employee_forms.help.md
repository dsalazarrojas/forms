# Donor Membership Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide donors through the process of renewing their membership, including selecting a renewal option, entering contact information, and submitting their renewal request. 

## How to Complete This Form
To start, click on a blank form to begin the process. The form has multiple pages, each with a series of fields asking for different types of information. Please complete each page carefully. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name, as it appears on your membership card or identification. 
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name, as it appears on your membership card or identification. 
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address, where you can be contacted for membership updates and news. 
* **Membership ID** (`membership_id`, number, required/optional): Enter your Membership ID, which can be found on your membership card or in your membership account information. 
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, date, required/optional): Select your current membership expiration date from the calendar picker. 
* **Renewal Option** (`renewal_option`, select_one, required/optional): Choose one of the provided renewal options for your membership. 
* **Address 1** (`address1`, text, required/optional): Enter the first line of your address, as it is listed on your membership information. 
* **Address 2** (`address2`, text, required/optional): Enter the second line of your address, as it is listed on your membership information. 
* **City** (`city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city where your address is located. 
* **State** (`state`, text, required/optional): Enter the state or province where your address is located. 
* **ZIP** (`zip`, text, required/optional): Enter the zip or postal code of your address. 
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number, where we can reach you for membership updates. 
* **Renewal Date** (`renewal_date`, date, required/optional): Select the new expiration date for your membership. 
* **Reason for Renewal** (`reason`, text, required/optional): Explain the reason for your membership renewal, if applicable. 
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required/optional): Please enter a signature to confirm your information is accurate. 
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" to submit the form and complete the membership renewal process. 
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" to cancel the form and start over. 
* **Error** (`error`, select_multiple, required/optional): If there are any errors on the form, select "Yes" to display the error message. 
* **Save** (`save`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" to save your progress on the form and return later. 
* **Reset** (`reset`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" to clear all fields and start over. 
* **Back** (`back`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" to go back to the previous page. 
* **Next** (`next`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" to proceed to the next page. 
* **Previous** (`previous`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" to go back to the previous page. 
* **Submit Button** (`submit_button`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" to submit the form once you have completed all the fields. 
* **Cancel Button** (`cancel_button`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" to cancel the form and start over.
