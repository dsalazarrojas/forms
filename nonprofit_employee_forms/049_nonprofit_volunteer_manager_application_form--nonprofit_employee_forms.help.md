# Nonprofit Volunteer Manager Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals interested in applying to be a volunteer manager for the nonprofit organization. Please fill out the form below to submit your application.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by carefully reading the form header section, which provides an overview of the form's purpose.
- Fill out the required and optional fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional: false): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
- **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required/optional: false): Select the role you are applying for: Volunteer, Manager, Staff, or Other.
- **Availability** (`availability`, `time`, required/optional: false): Select your available days and times to volunteer.
- **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required/optional: false): Enter the date you would like to start volunteering.
- **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required/optional: false): Enter the date you would like to end your volunteering commitment.
- **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required/optional: false): Enter any additional information or comments about your application.
