# Charitable Project Integrity Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Charitable Project Integrity Audit Form is designed to assess a charitable project's compliance with regulations, financial accuracy, and documentation status. It will help auditors and project managers identify areas of improvement and ensure that projects are running smoothly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Section 1: Auditor Information**
	* Enter your full name as the auditor conducting the audit.
	* Select the date of the audit from the calendar.
2. **Section 2: Project Details**
	* Enter the name of the charitable project being audited.
	* Enter the name of the project manager responsible for the project.
3. **Section 3: Compliance and Integrity Review**
	* Select the current status of the project from the options provided (Planning, Active, On Hold, Completed, or Cancelled).
	* Assess the overall compliance level of the project, considering adherence to nonprofit regulations. Choose from the options (Fully Compliant, Mostly Compliant, Partially Compliant, or Non-Compliant).
	* Evaluate the financial data accuracy, considering if the records are consistent with actual spending. Choose from the options (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
	* Check the status of required legal and project documents, selecting from the options (Complete, Incomplete - Minor Missing, or Incomplete - Major Missing).
4. **Section 4: Recommendations and Follow-up**
	* Describe any discrepancies or irregularities found during the audit.
	* Provide steps that must be taken to address the audit findings.
	* Schedule a follow-up audit, if necessary, by selecting a date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Auditor Full Name** (`auditor_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the auditor conducting the audit.
* **Date of Audit** (`audit_date`, date, required): Select the date of the audit from the calendar.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the charitable project being audited.
* **Project Manager** (`project_manager`, text, required): Enter the name of the project manager responsible for the project.
* **Current Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the project from the options provided.
* **Overall Compliance Level** (`compliance_level`, select_one, required): Assess the overall compliance level of the project, considering adherence to nonprofit regulations.
* **Financial Data Accuracy** (`financial_accuracy`, select_one, required): Evaluate the financial data accuracy, considering if the records are consistent with actual spending.
* **Documentation Completeness** (`documentation_status`, select_one, required): Check the status of required legal and project documents.
* **Discrepancies or Irregularities Found** (`discrepancies_found`, text, required): Describe any issues identified during the audit.
* **Required Corrective Actions** (`corrective_actions`, text, required): Provide steps that must be taken to address the audit findings.
* **Scheduled Date for Follow-up Audit** (`next_audit_date`, date, required): Schedule a follow-up audit, if necessary.
* **Auditor Final Comments** (`final_comments`, text, required): Enter any final comments or recommendations from the auditor.
