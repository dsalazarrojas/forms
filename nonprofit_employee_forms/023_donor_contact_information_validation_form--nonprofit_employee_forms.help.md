# Donor Contact Information Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to validate and record donor contact information for non-profit organizations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Donor Information Form on page 1 with the relevant details.
2. Provide your phone number on the second page.
3. Enter your email address on the third page.
4. Enter your physical address on the fourth page.
5. Select your city on the fifth page.
6. Enter your zip code on the sixth page.
7. Choose your country of residence on the seventh page.
8. Select your state of residence on the eighth page.
9. Enter your date of birth on the ninth page.
10. If applicable, enter the time of death on the tenth page.
11. If applicable, provide any additional information about your non-profit organization on the eleventh page.
12. Add any relevant notes or comments on the final page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Information Form** (`donor_information_form`, text, optional): This field is a text input where you can provide any additional information about the donor.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_number`, number, optional): Enter your phone number here.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address here.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your physical address.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Select your city of residence.
* **Zip** (`zip`, number, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Choose your country of residence.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Select your state of residence.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Time of Death** (`time_of_death`, time, optional): If applicable, enter the time of death.
* **NonProfit** (`non_profit`, text, optional): If applicable, provide any additional information about your non-profit organization.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any relevant notes or comments about yourself or your non-profit organization.

## Tips
This form is designed to be completed once to ensure accurate and up-to-date information. Please make sure to fill out the form carefully and thoroughly. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your non-profit organization's administration.
