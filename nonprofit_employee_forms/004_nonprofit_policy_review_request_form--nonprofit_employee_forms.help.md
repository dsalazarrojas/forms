# Nonprofit Policy Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to request a review of a nonprofit organization's policy documents. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the policy review request, including the types of reviews required, urgency levels, and contact information for the review requester. This information will help the review committee to process the request efficiently and ensure that all necessary information is collected.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the types of reviews you are requesting (e.g., "Yes" for review and approval, "No" for no review).
2.  Choose the urgency level for your review request (e.g., "High" for urgent, "Low" for non-urgent).
3.  Provide your information as the review requester (e.g., name, title, email, phone number, department, and position).
4.  Enter the date and time you would like to have the review completed.
5.  Set the deadline for the review (if applicable).
6.  Select the type of review you are requesting (e.g., "Yes" for review and approval, "No" for no review).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Policy Documents** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to provide a brief description of the policy documents you are requesting to be reviewed.
*   **Review Types** (`2`, `select_one`, required: false): This field allows you to select the types of reviews you are requesting, such as "Yes" for review and approval, or "No" for no review.
*   **Urgency Levels** (`3`, `select_one`, required: false): This field enables you to choose the urgency level for your review request, such as "High" for urgent, "Low" for non-urgent.
*   **Review Requester Info** (`4`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you can provide any additional information about your review request.
*   **Review Requester Name** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name as the review requester.
*   **Review Requester Title** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your title as the review requester.
*   **Review Requester Email** (`7`, `email`, required: false): Please enter your email address as the review requester.
*   **Review Requester Phone** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your phone number as the review requester.
*   **Review Requester Department** (`9`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the department or team you belong to as the review requester.
*   **Review Requester Position** (`10`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your position as the review requester.
*   **Review Date** (`11`, `date`, required: false): Please enter the date you would like to have the review completed.
*   **Review Time** (`12`, `time`, required: false): Please enter the time you would like to have the review completed.
*   **Review Deadline** (`13`, `date`, required: false): Please enter the deadline for the review (if applicable).
*   **Review Type** (`14`, `select_one`, required: false): This field allows you to select the type of review you are requesting, such as "Yes" for review and approval, "No" for no review.
*   **Policy Document** (`15`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to attach a policy document to your review request.
