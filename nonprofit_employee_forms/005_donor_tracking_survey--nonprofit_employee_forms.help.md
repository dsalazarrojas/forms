# Donor Tracking Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Donor Tracking Survey form, explaining each field and how to complete it correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form with the requested information for each field.
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
- If you need help or clarification on any field, don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Information** (Page 1, required: false)
    The Donor Information field is a general description of the form. This field is not required.
* **First Name** (Page 1, required: false)
    Please enter the donor's first name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Last Name** (Page 1, required: true)
    Enter the donor's last name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Email** (Page 1, required: true)
    Enter the donor's email address as it appears in their email communications with our organization.
* **Phone** (Page 1, required: true)
    Enter the donor's phone number as it appears in their contact information.
* **Donor Frequency** (Page 1, required: false)
    Select how often the donor has donated to our organization. Choose from:
    * Monthly
    * Quarterly
    * Annually
    * Never
* **First Interaction** (Page 1, required: false)
    Select how the donor first interacted with our organization. Choose from:
    * First time donor
    * Referred
    * Social media
    * Online ad
    * Word of mouth
    * Other
* **Donor Value** (Page 1, required: true)
    Enter the total value of the donor's contributions to our organization.
* **Survey Comments** (Page 1, required: false)
    Enter any additional comments or information about the donor's interactions with our organization.
