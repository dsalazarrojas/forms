# Fundraiser Compliance Training Sign Off - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide is to assist users in understanding and completing the Fundraiser Compliance Training Sign Off form. The form is used to collect information and confirm the completion of fundraiser compliance training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in the required information for each field. You can add or remove fields as needed.
- Ensure you fill out all fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fundraiser Compliance Training Sign Off Form** (`id: 1`): This is the title of the form.
* **Staff or Volunteer** (`id: 2`): Select "Nonprofit Employee" if you are an employee of the nonprofit organization, or "Volunteer" if you are a volunteer.
* **Date Completed Training** (`id: 3`): Enter the date you completed the fundraiser compliance training.
* **Confirm Completed Training** (`id: 4`): Confirm that you completed the training by selecting "True".
* **Signatures** (`id: 5`): Select the signatures of staff and volunteers who have confirmed completion of the fundraiser compliance training.
* **Confirm Signatures** (`id: 6`): Confirm that you received the signatures from staff and volunteers.
* **Confirmation Statement** (`id: 7`): Enter a statement confirming the completion of the fundraiser compliance training.
* **Confirm Confirmation Statement** (`id: 8`): Confirm that the statement is correct.
* **Date Training Was Completed** (`id: 9`): Enter the date the training was completed.
* **Confirm Training Completed** (`id: 10`): Confirm that the training was completed.
* **Date Training Was Received** (`id: 11`): Enter the date the training was received.
* **Confirm Training Received** (`id: 12`): Confirm that the training was received.
* **Signatures Received** (`id: 13`): Select the signatures of staff and volunteers who have confirmed completion of the fundraiser compliance training.

## Tips
- Ensure you complete all fields before submitting the form.
- Confirm your completion and confirmations carefully to avoid errors.
- This form is used for internal tracking and compliance purposes, ensure you fill it out accurately.
