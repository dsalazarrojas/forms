# Online Petition Signing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to create online petitions that can be used to raise support, awareness, or collect data from non-profit employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Online Petition Signing Form** (1): This is the title of the petition.
	* **Description** (3): Provide a brief description of the petition.
	* **Form ID** (16): Select the ID of the petition.
	* **Name** (6): Enter your name.
	* **Organization** (7): Enter your organization's name.
	* **Role** (8): Enter your role in the organization.
	* **Email Address** (5): Enter your email address.
	* **Signature** (15): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your support.
2. Fill in the optional fields:
	* **Category** (2): Select the category of the petition (Yes/No).
	* **Form Target** (25): Select the target of the petition (Academic/Non-Profit/Other).
	* **Department** (9): Enter the department you belong to.
	* **Phone** (10): Enter your phone number.
	* **Date** (11): Enter the date.
	* **Time** (12): Enter the time.
	* **Location** (13): Enter the location.
	* **Comments** (14): Provide any additional comments.
	* **Assigned Tool** (17): Enter the tool assigned to you.
	* **Output File** (18): Enter the output file.
	* **Nonprofit Employee Forms** (19): Enter the name of the form used in your non-profit organization.
	* **Online Petition Signing Form** (20): Enter the name of the online petition signing form.
	* **Raise Support** (21): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you want to raise support.
	* **Raise Awareness** (22): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you want to raise awareness.
	* **Collect Data** (23): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you want to collect data.
	* **Influence Decision-Making** (24): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you want to influence decision-making.
