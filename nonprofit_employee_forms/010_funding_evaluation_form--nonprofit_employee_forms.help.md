# Funding Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Funding Evaluation Form is a tool used to gather information about a funding request from a nonprofit organization. This form helps evaluate the request to determine if it meets the necessary criteria for funding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a clear and concise title for your funding request in the "Funding Request" field.
2. Provide the name of your organization in the "Organization Name" field.
3. Enter the grant amount you are requesting in the "Grant Amount" field.
4. Select the evaluation criteria that your funding request meets in the "Evaluation Criteria" field.
5. Choose a date for the evaluation in the "Evaluation Date" field.
6. Select the time for the evaluation in the "Evaluation Time" field.
7. Add any additional notes about the evaluation in the "Evaluation Note" field.
8. Provide the name and contact information of the contact person in the "Contact Person" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Funding Request** (`funding_request`, text, required): Enter a clear and concise title for your funding request.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Provide the name of your organization.
* **Grant Amount** (`grant_amount`, text, required): Enter the grant amount you are requesting.
* **Evaluation Criteria** (`evaluation_criteria`, select_one, required): Select the evaluation criteria that your funding request meets. The options are:
	+ Impact: The funding request should have a significant impact on the community or society.
	+ Efficiency: The funding request should be efficiently managed and implemented.
	+ Effectiveness: The funding request should be effective in achieving its goals.
	+ Feasibility: The funding request should be feasible and realistic in its goals and expectations.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Choose a date for the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, required): Select a time for the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Note** (`evaluation_note`, note, required): Add any additional notes about the evaluation.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Provide the name and contact information of the contact person.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure that your request is thoroughly evaluated.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or need help with this form, contact your designated contact person.

This guide should provide you with clear and concise instructions on how to complete the Funding Evaluation Form. If you have any further questions or need additional clarification, don't hesitate to reach out.
