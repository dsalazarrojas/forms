# Vehicle Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from donors or individuals making a vehicle donation to the non-profit organization. The gathered data will be used to process and manage the donation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are marked as "required" in the form.
2. Enter the donor's and donator's information in the respective sections.
3. Provide details about the vehicle donation type and any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, required/optional): Enter the vehicle's year.
* **Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, required/optional): Enter the vehicle's make.
* **Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, required/optional): Enter the vehicle's model.
* **Year Made** (`vehicle_year_made`, number, required/optional): Enter the year the vehicle was made.
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the donor's name.
* **Phone** (`donor_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the donor's phone number.
* **Email** (`donor_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the donor's email address.
* **Street** (`donor_street`, text, required/optional): Enter the donor's street address.
* **City** (`donor_city`, text, required/optional): Enter the donor's city.
* **State** (`donor_state`, select_one, required): Select the donor's state (CA, FL, TX).
* **Zip** (`donor_zip`, number, required/optional): Enter the donor's zip code.
* **Country** (`donor_country`, select_one, required): Select the donor's country (US, Canada, Mexico).
* **Donator Name** (`donator_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the donator's name.
* **Address** (`donator_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the donator's address.
* **Donator City** (`donator_city`, text, required/optional): Enter the donator's city.
* **Donator State** (`donator_state`, select_one, required): Select the donator's state (CA, FL, TX).
* **Donator Zip** (`donator_zip`, number, required): Enter the donator's zip code.
* **Donator Country** (`donator_country`, select_one, required): Select the donator's country (US, Canada, Mexico).
* **Donator Phone** (`donator_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the donator's phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`donor_dob`, date, required/optional): Enter the donor's date of birth.
* **Social Security Number** (`donor_ssn`, text, required/optional): Enter the donor's social security number.
* **Vehicle Donation Type** (`vehicle_donation_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of vehicle donation (Cash, Trade, Other).
* **Vehicle Donation Notes** (`vehicle_donation_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the vehicle donation.
* **Other (Please provide details)** (`vehicle_donation_other`, text, required/optional): Enter any other details about the vehicle donation.

## Tips

- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Use the correct format for the date of birth.
- If "Other" is selected for the "Vehicle Donation Type", provide a clear description of the donation.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the non-profit organization for assistance.
