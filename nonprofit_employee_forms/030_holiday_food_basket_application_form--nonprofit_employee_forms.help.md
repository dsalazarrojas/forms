# Holiday Food Basket Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Holiday Food Basket Application Form is used to collect information from non-profit organizations to help us better understand their needs and provide support for holiday food basket distributions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Review the optional fields for any additional details you'd like to provide.
3. Make sure to select the correct options for the dropdown fields.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your application.
5. Return the completed form to us by email or mail.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Holiday Food Basket Application
* **Holiday Food Basket Application** (`holiday_food_basket`, text, required): This is the primary field where you can describe your non-profit organization and its needs.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your name as the contact person for your organization.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your organization's email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, number, required): Enter your organization's phone number for us to reach you.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your organization's address, if you'd like to provide it for our records.
* **Holiday Date** (`holiday_date`, date, required): Select the date you are requesting a holiday food basket distribution for.
* **Non-Profit Name** (`non_profit_name`, text, required): Enter your non-profit organization's name.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the person we should contact for further information.
* **Holiday Basket** (`holiday_basket`, number, optional): If you'd like to request a specific number of holiday food baskets.
* **Non-Profit Address** (`non_profit_address`, text, required): Enter your non-profit organization's address.
* **Non-Profit Phone** (`non_profit_phone`, number, required): Enter your non-profit organization's phone number.
* **Non-Profit Email** (`non_profit_email`, email, optional): Enter your non-profit organization's email address, if you'd like to provide it.
* **Non-Profit Website** (`non_profit_website`, text, optional): Enter your non-profit organization's website.
* **Charity Type** (`charity_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of charity your organization is:
	+ Food Bank
	+ Food Pantry
	+ Soup Kitchen
	+ Homeless Shelter
	+ Other
* **Holiday Food Basket Distribution** (`holiday_food_basket_distribution`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you'd like to distribute the food baskets to:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Not sure
* **Holiday Food Basket Recipient** (`holiday_food_basket_recipient`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you'd like to receive the food baskets:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Holiday Gift Donations** (`holiday_gift_donations`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you'd like to receive holiday gift donations:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Holiday Food Basket Recipients** (`holiday_food_basket_recipients`, select_multiple, required): Indicate the number of people you'd like to receive holiday food baskets:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Food Basket Request** (`food_basket_request`, text, required): Describe your specific holiday food basket request.
* **Holiday Food Basket Request** (`holiday_food_basket_request`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your holiday food basket request.
* **Holiday Food Basket Comments** (`holiday_food_basket_comments`, text, optional): Leave any comments or additional information about your holiday food basket request.
* **Holiday Food Basket Notes** (`holiday_food_basket_notes`, text, optional): Leave any additional notes about your holiday food basket request.
* **Holiday Food Basket Other Notes** (`holiday_food_basket_other_notes`, text, optional): Leave any other additional notes about your holiday food basket request.
* **Holiday Food Basket Signature** (`holiday_food_basket_signature`, text, optional): Sign and date this form to confirm your application.
* **Holiday Food Basket Agreement** (`holiday_food_basket_agreement`, note, optional): This is a note field, but please sign and date this form to confirm your agreement with our terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Be as specific as possible with your requests.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at <your email address>.
