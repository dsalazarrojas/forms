# donate_for_nature - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "donate_for_nature" form is a simple data collection tool used for internal purposes to track and record donations made to the organization's "nature" cause or initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your **Phone** in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter the **Amount Donated** in the "Amount Donated" field.
5. Choose your **Donation Frequency** from the options provided in the "Donation Frequency" field.
6. If not one of the provided options fit, describe your **Frequency Other** in the "Frequency Other" field.
7. Select one or more **Payment Method** from the options provided in the "Payment Method" field.
8. Leave any additional **Notes** regarding your donation in the "Notes" field.
9. If the donation is assigned to a specific user, choose a staff member's name from the options in the "Assigned User" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1st Field): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (2nd Field): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (3rd Field): Enter your phone number.
* **Amount Donated** (4th Field): Enter the amount of money you are donating.
* **Donation Frequency** (5th Field): Select how often you plan on donating:
	+ **One-time**: A single donation.
	+ **Monthly**: Once a month.
	+ **Quarterly**: Four times a year.
	+ **Semi-Annually**: Twice a year.
	+ **Annually**: Once a year.
* **Frequency Other** (6th Field): If one of the above options do not fit your donation schedule, describe it here.
* **Payment Method** (7th Field): Choose how you plan on paying for your donation:
	+ **Credit Card**: Use a credit card.
	+ **Pay Pal**: Use PayPal.
	+ **Bank Transfer**: Use a bank transfer.
* **Notes** (8th Field): Any additional information about your donation is welcome here.
* **Assigned User** (9th Field): If this donation is for a specific user, choose their name from the list, otherwise choose "Other".
