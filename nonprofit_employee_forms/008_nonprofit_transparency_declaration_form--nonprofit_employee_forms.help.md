# nonprofit_transparency_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nonprofit Transparency Declaration Form is used to collect information about a nonprofit organization's financial and operational activities, structure, and governance. This form is intended to provide transparency and accountability for the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about your organization.
2. Select the correct options for each field from the provided lists.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nonprofit Transparency Declaration Form** (1, text): This is the title of the form. Enter the name of the form as it will be displayed.
* **Form Header** (2, text): This field is for entering the name of the form as it will be displayed. It is a text field where you can enter any text.
* **Organization Name** (3, text): Enter the name of your nonprofit organization.
* **Legal Name** (4, text): Enter the legal name of your organization as registered with the state and federal authorities.
* **Address** (5, text): Enter the address of your organization's main office or headquarters.
* **Contact Person** (6, text): Enter the name of the person who can be contacted for more information.
* **Email** (7, email): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Executive Director** (8, text): Enter the name of the executive director or the highest authority figure of your organization.
* **Financial Reporting Officer** (9, text): Enter the name of the financial reporting officer or the person responsible for preparing the financial reports.
* **Financial Reporting Period** (10, date): Enter the date from which the financial reports start.
* **Financial Reporting Frequency** (11, select_multiple): Select from the options how frequently your organization reports its financial activities.
* **Asset Types** (12, select_multiple): Select the types of assets your organization owns.
* **Asset Amount** (13, number): Enter the current amount of assets.
* **Liability Types** (14, select_multiple): Select the types of liabilities your organization has.
* **Liability Amount** (15, number): Enter the current amount of liabilities.
* **Equity Types** (16, select_multiple): Select the types of equity your organization has.
* **Equity Amount** (17, number): Enter the current amount of equity.
* **Governance Structure** (18, select_one): Select the governance structure of your organization.
* **Board of Directors** (19, select_multiple): Select the members of the board of directors.

## Tips
* Double-check the accuracy and completeness of your answers before submitting the form.
* Ensure that the information provided is up-to-date and reflects the current status of your organization.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the designated contact person for assistance.
