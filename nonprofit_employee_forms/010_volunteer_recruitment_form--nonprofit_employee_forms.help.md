# Volunteer Recruitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about yourself as a potential volunteer. Please fill it out carefully to help us understand your availability and preferences for volunteer opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Scroll through each page of the form carefully.
2. Fill in the required information in each field.
3. Select all that apply for your available days of the week and preferred times for volunteering.
4. Make sure to save your progress and review the information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Name (`volunteer_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on our communication.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): If you're currently or have previously worked with an organization, please specify it for our records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your primary email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you prefer communication via phone, please provide your phone number.
* **Available Days** (`available_days`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the days of the week you are available to volunteer.
	+ **Monday** (`available_days_0`, select_one, optional): Are you available on Mondays?
	+ **Tuesday** (`available_days_1`, select_one, optional): Are you available on Tuesdays?
	+ **Wednesday** (`available_days_2`, select_one, optional): Are you available on Wednesdays?
	+ **Thursday** (`available_days_3`, select_one, optional): Are you available on Thursdays?
	+ **Friday** (`available_days_4`, select_one, optional): Are you available on Fridays?
	+ **Saturday** (`available_days_5`, select_one, optional): Are you available on Saturdays?
	+ **Sunday** (`available_days_6`, select_one, optional): Are you available on Sundays?
* **Time Preference** (`time_prefernce`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the times of the day you are available to volunteer.
	+ **Morning** (`time_prefernce_0`, select_one, optional): Are you available in the morning?
	+ **Afternoon** (`time_prefernce_1`, select_one, optional): Are you available in the afternoon?
	+ **Evening** (`time_prefernce_2`, select_one, optional): Are you available in the evening?
