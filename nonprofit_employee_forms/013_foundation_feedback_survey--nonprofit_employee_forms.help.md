# foundation_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The foundation_feedback_survey form is designed to collect feedback from nonprofit organization employees to gauge the quality of service provided by the foundation and suggest areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the introduction page to understand the purpose of the form.
2. Select the category of feedback you'd like to provide (if applicable).
3. Fill in your rating for the quality of service provided by the foundation (question 1).
4. Provide suggestions on what the foundation can do better next time (question 2).
5. Identify the foundation employee who provided you with service (question 3).
6. Provide contact information to reach out to you if needed (category, contact_info, email, phone, and date).
7. Add any additional comments or details in the email field (email).
8. Enter any further details or comments in the phone field (phone).
9. Fill in the date of the feedback (date).
10. Enter the time of the feedback (time).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Foundation Feedback Survey** (`introduction`, `note`, not required): This is the introduction page of the form, providing an overview of the feedback survey.
* **What category of feedback would you like to provide?** (`category`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select one or more categories of feedback you'd like to provide, such as "Service Quality" or "Communication."
* **How would you rate the quality of service provided by the foundation?** (`question1`, `number`, not required): Rate the quality of service provided by the foundation using a number (e.g., 1-10).
* **What would you like the foundation to do better next time?** (`question2`, `text`, not required): Provide suggestions on what the foundation can do better next time.
* **Foundation Employee** (`question3`, `text`, not required): Identify the foundation employee who provided you with service.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `select_one`, not required): Select the category of contact information you'd like to provide.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address in the format "johndoe@example.com."
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number in the format "123-456-7890."
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of the feedback in the format "2022-02-15."
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, not required): Enter the time of the feedback in the format "14:30."

## Tips
* Be specific and clear when providing feedback.
* Ensure that the feedback is relevant and not duplicate.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to the foundation staff for assistance.
