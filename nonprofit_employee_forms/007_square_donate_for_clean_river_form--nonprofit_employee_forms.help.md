# Square Donate For Clean River Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Square Donate For Clean River Form is a tool to collect donations and track information from individuals who want to contribute to a non-profit organization's mission of cleaning rivers. This form is used to gather details about the donor, including their name, contact information, and donation preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name, amount donated, frequency of donation, and method of donation.
2. Select the category of donation (e.g., Nonprofit Employee Forms or Other).
3. Optionally provide additional contact information, such as email, phone, and address.
4. If applicable, provide a purpose or description for your donation.
5. You can also add any additional notes or comments about your donation.
6. Finally, select the assigned tools or resources you are interested in using.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it will be displayed on the donor's card.
* **Amount Donated** (`amount_donated`, `number`, required): Specify the amount you would like to donate.
* **Frequency Donated** (`frequency_donated`, `select_one`, required): Choose how often you would like to donate: Monthly, Biannually, Quarterly, or Annually.
* **Method Donated** (`method_donated`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more methods for your donation: Check, Bank, or Credit Card.
* **Sponsor** (`sponsor`, `text`, optional): Enter any sponsorship or affiliation information.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address for contact purposes.
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, `text`, optional): Enter a purpose or description for your donation.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your donation.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, optional): Select the assigned tools or resources you are interested in using.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required): Choose the category of donation: Nonprofit Employee Forms or Other.
* **Category 2** (`category_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more categories for your donation.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, optional): Enter a description or purpose for your donation.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the output file for your donation.
* **Assigned Tool 2** (`assigned_tool_2`, `text`, optional): Select the assigned tools or resources you are interested in using.
* **Form Id 2** (`form_id_2`, `text`, optional): Enter the form ID for your donation.
* **Purpose 2** (`purpose_2`, `text`, optional): Enter a purpose or description for your donation.
* **User Name 2** (`user_name_2`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as it will be displayed on the donor's card.

## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields to ensure accurate tracking of your donation.
* You can select multiple methods for your donation.
* You can also add additional contact information to ensure easy communication.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
