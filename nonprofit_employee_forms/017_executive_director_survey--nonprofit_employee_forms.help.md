# Executive Director Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Executive Director Survey is designed to collect feedback and insights from non-profit executive directors, providing a comprehensive understanding of their leadership style, management approach, and other aspects that impact organizational performance. This survey will help non-profits gather valuable information to improve leadership development, communication, and overall organizational effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form with as much detail as possible.
2. Ensure that you answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the provided text fields to express them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of your non-profit organization.
* **Years of Service** (`years_of_service`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have served as an executive director.
* **Leadership Style** (`leadership_style`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following leadership styles:
	+ Option 1: Collaborative
	+ Option 2: Autocratic
	+ Option 3: Participatory
* **Communication Approach** (`communication_approach`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following communication approaches:
	+ Option 1: Formal
	+ Option 2: Informal
* **Management Approach** (`management_approach`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following management approaches:
	+ Option 1: Hierarchical
	+ Option 2: Flat
* **Employee Satisfaction** (`employee_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following employee satisfaction levels:
	+ Option 1: High
	+ Option 2: Medium
	+ Option 3: Low
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following job satisfaction levels:
	+ Option 1: High
	+ Option 2: Medium
* **Team Satisfaction** (`team_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following team satisfaction levels:
	+ Option 1: High
	+ Option 2: Medium
	+ Option 3: Low
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following communication channels:
	+ Option 1: Email
	+ Option 2: Phone
	+ Option 3: In-person
* **Decision-Making Process** (`decision_making_process`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following decision-making processes:
	+ Option 1: Consensus-based
	+ Option 2: Top-down
* **Leadership Development** (`leadership_development`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following leadership development areas:
	+ Option 1: Communication
	+ Option 2: Strategic planning
	+ Option 3: Conflict resolution
* **Job Performance** (`job_performance`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following job performance levels:
	+ Option 1: High
	+ Option 2: Medium
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Executive Summary** (`executive_summary`, text, optional): Summarize your thoughts and key takeaways from this survey.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more areas for improvement:
	+ Option 1: Communication
	+ Option 2: Leadership development
	+ Option 3: Strategic planning
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions for improvement or future development.
* **Challenges Faced** (`challenges_faced`, text, optional): Share any challenges you've faced as an executive director.
* **Executive Director Name** (`executive_director_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the executive director.
* **Executive Director Email** (`executive_director_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address as the executive director.
* **Executive Director Phone** (`executive_director_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number as the executive director.
* **Executive Director Position** (`executive_director_position`, text, optional): Enter your position as the executive director.
* **Executive Director Start Date** (`executive_director_start_date`, date, optional): Enter your start date as the executive director.
* **Executive Director End Date** (`executive_director_end_date`, date, optional): Enter your end date as the executive director.
