# Donor Experience Internship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Experience Internship Application form is used to gather information about potential candidates for the Donor Experience Internship. This form is designed to assess whether a candidate has the right background and availability for the internship.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether you have experience as a donor.
2.  Enter your name and email address to contact you.
3.  Provide your phone number so that we can reach out to you if needed.
4.  Select your level of education from the dropdown menu.
5.  Choose the options that best describe your education background.
6.  Select the date and time you are available to start and end the internship.
7.  Indicate the duration of the internship you are available for in weeks or months.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Experience Form**: This question is designed to gauge your experience as a donor. Please answer "Yes" if you have previous experience in the non-profit sector, especially in donor relations.
* **Candidate Name**: This is the space to provide your name. Please enter your full name as it will appear in our records.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address so that we can contact you for further information or to confirm your application.
* **Phone**: This is your contact number. We might reach out to you for additional questions or clarification. Make sure to provide a number that we can easily reach you at.
* **Experience**: Select the level of education you have completed:
	+ **High School Diploma**: You have completed high school and may not have further education.
	+ **Bachelor's Degree**: You have completed a four-year undergraduate degree.
	+ **Master's Degree**: You have completed a postgraduate degree.
	+ **PhD**: You have completed a doctoral degree.
* **Education**: Select the options that apply to your education background. This will help us to better assess your fit for the internship.
* **Available Start Date**: Select the date you are available to start the internship. This will help us to plan ahead and coordinate logistics.
* **Available End Date**: Select the date you are available to end the internship. This will help us to plan ahead and coordinate logistics.
* **Available Duration**: Indicate the number of weeks or months you are available to commit to the internship. This will help us to determine your fit for the position.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and thoroughly to the best of your ability.
* If you have questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We appreciate your interest in the Donor Experience Internship, and we look forward to reviewing your application.
