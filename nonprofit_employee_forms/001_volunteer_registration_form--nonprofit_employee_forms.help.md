# Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Registration Form is designed to collect information from volunteers who will be participating in an event. This form helps to track and manage volunteer hours, event details, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out your information, including your name, email address, phone number, and address.
2. Select the event you will be participating in by entering the Event ID.
3. Specify the project you will be working on by entering the Project ID.
4. Enter the number of volunteer hours you will be working.
5. Select your status as an active, inactive, or pending volunteer.
6. Provide any additional notes about your involvement in the event.
7. Enter the start and end times of the event.
8. Enter the location of the event.
9. Enter the organization, department, and position you will be working under.
10. Enter the name and contact information of your supervisor.
11. Enter the name and email address of your volunteer manager.
12. Enter a reference or any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Name** (text): This field is used to enter your name as a volunteer. Please enter your name as it should appear on any official documents or communications.
* **Event ID** (number): This field is used to select the event you will be participating in. Please enter the ID number of the event for which you are volunteering.
* **Event Date** (date): This field is used to enter the date of the event. Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Volunteer Email** (email): This field is used to enter your email address. Please enter your email address as it should appear on any official communications.
* **Volunteer Phone** (text): This field is used to enter your phone number. Please enter your phone number as it should appear on any official communications.
* **Volunteer Address** (text): This field is used to enter your home or work address. Please enter your address as it should appear on any official documents or communications.
* **Project ID** (number): This field is used to select the project you will be working on. Please enter the ID number of the project for which you are volunteering.
* **Project Name** (text): This field is used to enter the name of the project you will be working on. Please enter the name of the project as it should appear on any official documents or communications.
* **Volunteer Hours** (number): This field is used to enter the number of hours you will be working. Please enter the number of hours as a numerical value.
* **Volunteer Start Date** (date): This field is used to enter the date you will start volunteering. Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Volunteer End Date** (date): This field is used to enter the date you will finish volunteering. Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Volunteer Status** (select multiple): This field is used to select your status as an active, inactive, or pending volunteer. Please select the status that best describes your involvement.
* **Volunteer Notes** (note): This field is used to enter any additional comments or notes about your involvement in the event.
* **Event Start Time** (time): This field is used to enter the start time of the event. Please enter the time in the format HH:MM.
* **Event End Time** (time): This field is used to enter the end time of the event. Please enter the time in the format HH:MM.
* **Event Location** (text): This field is used to enter the location of the event. Please enter the location as it should appear on any official documents or communications.
* **Organization** (text): This field is used to enter the name of the organization you are working under. Please enter the name of the organization as it should appear on any official documents or communications.
* **Department** (text): This field is used to enter the department you are working under. Please enter the name of the department as it should appear on any official documents or communications.
* **Position** (text): This field is used to enter your position or role within the department. Please enter the position as it should appear on any official documents or communications.
* **Supervisor** (text): This field is used to enter the name of your supervisor. Please enter the name of your supervisor as it should appear on any official documents or communications.
* **Supervisor Email** (email): This field is used to enter the email address of your supervisor. Please enter the email address of your supervisor as it should appear on any official communications.
* **Supervisor Phone** (text): This field is used to enter the phone number of your supervisor. Please enter the phone number of your supervisor as it should appear on any official communications.
* **Volunteer Manager** (text): This field is used to enter the name of your volunteer manager. Please enter the name of your volunteer manager as it should appear on any official documents or communications.
* **Volunteer Reference** (text): This field is used to enter any additional comments or references about your involvement in the event. Please enter any additional comments as it should appear on any official documents or communications.
