# emergency_distribution_sign_up - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Distribution Sign Up Form is used to register individuals and households in need of emergency assistance. This form helps gather essential information about the household and their needs, ensuring that the correct support is provided in a timely and effective manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct household size from the options provided.
2. Choose the emergency assistance needs of your household.
3. Identify the emergency distribution needs of your household.
4. If applicable, provide emergency contact details.
5. Select the emergency distribution date and time.
6. Add any emergency contact notes (if applicable).
7. Select your emergency distribution location.
8. Add any distribution notes.
9. Confirm your emergency distribution agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Emergency Distribution Sign Up Form**
	* `emergency_distribution_sign_up_page_1`: Enter the first page of the form.
### 2. **Contact Details**
	* `contact_details`: Enter your contact information.
### 3. **First Name**
	* `contact_first_name`: Enter the first name of the person filling out the form.
### 4. **Last Name**
	* `contact_last_name`: Enter the last name of the person filling out the form.
### 5. **Email**
	* `contact_email`: Enter your email address.
### 6. **Phone**
	* `contact_phone`: Enter your phone number.
### 7. **Household Size**
	* `household_size`: Select the number of people living in your household.
### 8. **Household Needs**
	* `household_needs`: Choose the emergency assistance needs of your household.
### 9. **Emergency Assistance Needs**
	* `emergency_assistance_needs`: Select the emergency assistance needs of your household.
### 10. **Emergency Distribution Date**
	* `emergency_distribution_date`: Select the date for the emergency distribution.
### 11. **Emergency Distribution Time**
	* `emergency_distribution_time`: Select the time for the emergency distribution.
### 12. **Emergency Contact Details**
	* `emergency_contact_details`: Enter any additional emergency contact details.
### 13. **Emergency Contact First Name**
	* `emergency_contact_first_name`: Enter the first name of the emergency contact.
### 14. **Emergency Contact Last Name**
	* `emergency_contact_last_name`: Enter the last name of the emergency contact.
### 15. **Emergency Contact Phone**
	* `emergency_contact_phone`: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
### 16. **Emergency Contact Email**
	* `emergency_contact_email`: Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
### 17. **Emergency Contact Relationship**
	* `emergency_contact_relationship`: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
### 18. **Emergency Contact Address**
	* `emergency_contact_address`: Enter the address of the emergency contact.
### 19. **Emergency Distribution Location**
	* `emergency_distribution_location`: Select the location for the emergency distribution.
### 20. **Emergency Distribution Notes**
	* `emergency_distribution_notes`: Add any additional notes for the emergency distribution.
### 21. **Emergency Contact Notes**
	* `emergency_contact_notes`: Add any additional notes for the emergency contact.
### 22. **Emergency Distribution Other Needs**
	* `emergency_distribution_other_needs`: Enter any other emergency distribution needs.
### 23. **Emergency Distribution Other Details**
	* `emergency_distribution_other_details`: Enter any other emergency distribution details.
### 24. **Emergency Distribution Signature**
	* `emergency_distribution_signature`: Sign your name to confirm the emergency distribution.
### 25. **Emergency Distribution Agreement**
	* `emergency_distribution_agreement`: Select "True" to confirm that the emergency distribution agreement has been read and understood.
 
## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure effective emergency assistance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for support.
