## local_impact_grant_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Local Impact Grant Application Form is a tool to help non-profit organizations apply for funding to support local initiatives and projects that have a positive impact on their community. This form helps organizations provide detailed information about their programs, their goals, and their budget to ensure that their application is complete and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required information in the first page.
2. Provide a clear and concise description of your program and its impact in the Impact Statement field.
3. List the key budget items and estimated costs in the Budget Summary field.
4. Attach any additional documents or attachments that support your application in the Supporting Documents field.
5. Review and edit your application carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name**: Your organization's name and contact person's name. Please enter your organization's name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Organization Name**: The name of your non-profit organization. Please enter the full name of your organization.
* **Contact Information**: Your organization's contact information, including name, email, and phone number. Please ensure this information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Program Name**: The name of the program or project you are applying for funding. Please enter a clear and concise name.
* **Funding Amount**: The total amount of funding you are requesting. Please enter a numerical value. This field is optional, but it will help the review committee determine if the requested amount is reasonable.
* **Impact Statement**: Describe the impact of your program or project on your community. Please be concise and provide specific examples.
* **Budget Summary**: List the key budget items and estimated costs for your program or project. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Supporting Documents**: Attach any additional documents or attachments that support your application, such as letters of support, resumes, or financial statements.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your application is complete and accurate to avoid delays in the review process.
* Attach all required documents and attachments in a clear and organized manner.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
* Review and edit your application carefully before submitting it.
* You can save your application and revisit it later if you need to make changes.
