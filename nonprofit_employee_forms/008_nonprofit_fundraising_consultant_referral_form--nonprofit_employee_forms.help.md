# Nonprofit Fundraising Consultant Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nonprofit Fundraising Consultant Referral Form is a tool used to refer potential new clients to our nonprofit fundraising consulting services. This form is designed to collect relevant information from the referrer and the referred individual, and to streamline the referral process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields as follows:

* Follow the steps below to refer a new potential client to our nonprofit fundraising consulting services:
1. Fill in your Non Profit Organization field with the name of the organization you are referring.
2. Enter your contact information in the Contact Number, Contact Name, and Email fields.
3. Provide a brief description of the reason for the referral in the Referral Reason field.
4. Enter the name of the consultant you are referring in the Consultant Name field.
5. Enter the consultant's title in the Consultant Title field.
6. Provide the consultant's phone number in the Consultant Phone field.
7. Enter the consultant's email address in the Consultant Email field.
8. Enter a brief description of the nonprofit organization in the Non Profit Description field.
9. Add any additional notes about the referral in the Consultant Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Non Profit Organization**: Enter the name of the nonprofit organization you are referring.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you to discuss the referral.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name so we can contact you to discuss the referral.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you to discuss the referral.
* **Referral Reason**: Explain why you are referring this potential client to our nonprofit fundraising consulting services.
* **Consultant Name**: Enter the name of the consultant you are referring.
* **Consultant Title**: Enter the title of the consultant you are referring.
* **Consultant Phone**: Provide the consultant's phone number so we can contact them to discuss the referral.
* **Consultant Email**: Enter the consultant's email address so we can contact them to discuss the referral.
* **Non Profit Description**: Enter a brief description of the nonprofit organization being referred.
* **Consultant Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the referral.
