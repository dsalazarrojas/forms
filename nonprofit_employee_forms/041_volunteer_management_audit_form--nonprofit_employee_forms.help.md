# Volunteer Management Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from nonprofit employees about their experiences and suggestions for improving volunteer management practices within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to provide specific examples or details where possible.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please leave it blank or contact the relevant department or supervisor for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Programs** (`volunteer_programs`, `text`, optional): This field is intended to capture a list of volunteer programs you are involved in or have been involved in. Please provide a list of programs, separated by commas.
* **Program Areas** (`program_areas`, `select_one`, required): This field asks you to select one or more program areas you are involved in. Please choose the options that best fit your role or responsibilities.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): This field is for providing general feedback or comments about your experiences with volunteer management.
* **Experience** (`experience`, `text`, optional): This field is intended to capture any notable experiences or achievements you have had in your role.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, optional): This field is for providing suggestions or ideas for improving volunteer management practices within the organization.
* **Positive Experience** (`positive_experience`, `number`, optional): This field asks you to rate your positive experiences with the programs. Please use a numerical value (e.g. 1-5) to indicate the level of satisfaction.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, `text`, optional): This field is for highlighting any areas where you believe the organization could improve in terms of volunteer management.

## Tips
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant department or supervisor.
* Please be honest and clear in your answers, and try to provide specific examples or details where possible.
* If you are unsure about any field, please leave it blank or contact the relevant department or supervisor for clarification.
