# Covid 19 Pandemic Assistance Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Covid 19 Pandemic Assistance Application Form is designed to collect information about individuals who are seeking help during the pandemic. Please fill out this form with accurate and complete information to ensure your application is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what information is required.
2. Fill out each section with accurate and complete information.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your full name, address, and phone number to allow us to contact you about your application.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your name as it is officially registered or commonly known.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false):
	+ Enter your valid email address to receive updates about your application.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your phone number to enable us to contact you directly.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your current address for delivery purposes or other relevant information.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your job title as it appears on your official documents or resume.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required: false):
	+ Briefly describe your job responsibilities and experience.
* **Education** (`education`, text, required: false):
	+ List your educational qualifications and relevant courses.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe your previous work experience, including job titles and duration.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required: false):
	+ Attach a copy of your resume to provide more details about your qualifications.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, required: false):
	+ List your relevant skills, including language proficiency, tools, or software knowledge.
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter any relevant certifications or licenses you hold.
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, required: false):
	+ Choose a date when you are available to start work.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date when you can start work.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date when you expect to end work.
* **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter the number of hours you are willing to work per week.
* **Hourly Rate** (`hourly_rate`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter your expected hourly rate of pay.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are accurate and complete before submission.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact us for assistance.
* We will review your application and respond with further instructions or requests for additional information.
