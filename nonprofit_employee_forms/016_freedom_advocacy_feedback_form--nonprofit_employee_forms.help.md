# Freedom Advocacy Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Freedom Advocacy Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from employees on their experiences with the Freedom Advocacy program. The form helps the Freedom Advocacy team understand how the program is performing and identify areas for improvement. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and provide your honest feedback to help us better serve you and the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name.
2. If you have a contact email, please enter it in the Email field. This will help us get in touch with you for follow-up questions or to provide updates.
3. If you have a phone number, please enter it in the Phone field.
4. Select a Date from the calendar or enter a custom date.
5. In the Message to Prophets field, please share any thoughts, comments, or suggestions you have about the program.
6. In the Perceived Impact field, select 'Yes' if you feel that the program has had a positive impact, 'No' if you feel it has had a negative impact, or 'Unsure' if you're not sure.
7. In the Future Outreach field, select 'Yes' if you know of any future outreach opportunities for the program, 'No' if you don't know of any, or 'Unsure' if you're not sure.
8. In the Measured Impact field, select 'Yes' if you feel that the program's goals have been met, 'No' if you feel they haven't been met, or 'Unsure' if you're not sure.
9. Use the Rating field to rate the program's effectiveness (Good, Fair, or Bad).
10. In the Comments field, please share any additional comments or suggestions you have for the program.
11. Click the Submit button to send your feedback.
