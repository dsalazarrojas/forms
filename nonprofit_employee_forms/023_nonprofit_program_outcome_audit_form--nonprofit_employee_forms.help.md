# Nonprofit Program Outcome Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nonprofit Program Outcome Audit Form is a crucial tool for non-profit organizations to evaluate the effectiveness of their programs and identify areas for improvement. This form helps to collect and document information about program results, outcomes, and progress towards goals, allowing organizations to make data-driven decisions and optimize their programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant program results (page 1) to audit.
2. Choose the applicable outcomes (page 2) for each selected program result.
3. Rate the outcome (page 3) using the provided rating system.
4. Indicate how frequently the outcome is rated (page 4).
5. Provide an improvement plan (page 5) for any low-rated outcomes.
6. Outline the action plan (page 6) to implement the improvement plan.
7. Describe the next steps (page 7) to take to achieve the outcome.
8. Add any additional notes (page 8) about the program or outcome.
9. Select who submitted the form (page 9) for accountability and record-keeping purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Results (Program Results)**: This page allows you to select the relevant program results to audit. Please choose the programs you want to evaluate.
* **Outcomes (Outcomes)**: Select the outcomes associated with the selected program results. This will help you to focus on the specific goals and objectives of your program.
* **Outcome Ratings (Outcome Ratings)**: Rate the outcome using the provided rating system. This will help you to assess the progress towards your goal.
* **Rating Frequency (Rating Frequency)**: Indicate how frequently the outcome is rated. This will help you to identify any patterns or trends.
* **Improvement Plan (Improvement Plan)**: Provide an improvement plan for any low-rated outcomes. This is an opportunity to think critically about how to address areas of improvement.
* **Action Plan (Action Plan)**: Outline the action plan to implement the improvement plan. This will help you to create a roadmap for achieving your goals.
* **Next Steps (Next Steps)**: Describe the next steps to take to achieve the outcome. This will help you to stay focused on progress.
* **Notes (Notes)**: Add any additional notes about the program or outcome. This is a space to capture any relevant information.
* **Submitted By (Submitted By)**: Select who submitted the form for accountability and record-keeping purposes. This will help to track who is responsible for the program's progress.
