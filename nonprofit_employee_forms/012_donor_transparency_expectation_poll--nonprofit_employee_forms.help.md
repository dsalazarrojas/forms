# Donor Transparency Expectation Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Transparency Expectation Poll is a tool designed to understand the expectations and needs of donors in various areas related to trust, communication, and accountability. This form will help in identifying trends, frequencies, and levels of expectations to inform and improve the donor relations strategy.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read each question carefully and provide your honest answers.
2.  Fill out the form with your expectations and needs regarding donor relations.
3.  Provide specific answers for each page of the form, as indicated by the page ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Donor Expectations** (`1`, `text`, required: false):
    This question asks about your expectations from the organization in terms of your donor relations. Be as specific as possible.
*   **Transparency Expectations** (`2`, `text`, required: false):
    Please rate your expectations from the organization regarding transparency.
*   **Trust Benchmarking** (`3`, `text`, required: false):
    Describe your current level of trust with the organization.
*   **Accountability Expectations** (`4`, `text`, required: false):
    Rate your expectations for accountability from the organization.
*   **Communication Channels** (`5`, `text`, required: false):
    Indicate your preferred channels of communication for donor relations.
*   **Feedback Mechanisms** (`6`, `text`, required: false):
    Describe your expected feedback mechanisms for donor relations.
*   **Trust Levels** (`7`, `number`, required: false):
    Rate your trust levels with the organization.
*   **Trust Trends** (`8`, `date`, required: false):
    This question is not applicable as it is not clear from the form description what this field is intended for. Please disregard.
*   **Feedback Frequency** (`9`, `text`, required: false):
    Rate your expectations for feedback frequency.
*   **Communication Frequency** (`10`, `text`, required: false):
    Indicate your expected communication frequency from the organization.
*   **Donor Expectations (2)** (`11`, `select_one`, required: false):
    Rate your expectations from the organization regarding your donor relations.
*   **Transparency Expectations 2** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
    Please rate your expectations from the organization regarding transparency.
*   **Communication Channels 1** (`13`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
    Indicate your preferred channels of communication for donor relations.
*   **Communication Channels 2** (`14`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
    Please rate your expected channels for donor relations communication.
*   **Accountability Expectations 2** (`15`, `select_one`, required: false):
    Rate your expectations from the organization regarding accountability.
