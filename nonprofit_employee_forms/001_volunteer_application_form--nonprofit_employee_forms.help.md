# Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Application Form is designed to collect information about an individual's volunteer availability, preferences, and qualifications. This information will be used to match them with suitable volunteer opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Select your volunteer groups from the "volunteer group" field.
5. Enter your available dates in the "available dates" field.
6. Enter your available time in the "available time" field.
7. Select your volunteer shifts from the "volunteer shifts" fields.
8. Select your volunteer type from the "volunteer type" field.
9. Enter your volunteer level of commitment in the "volunteer level" field.
10. Enter your available volunteer hours in the "volunteer hours" field.
11. Enter your volunteer dedication statement in the "volunteer dedication" field.
12. Enter your volunteer interests in the "volunteer interests" field.
13. Enter your available skills in the "volunteer skills" field.
14. Enter any special equipment you need for volunteering in the "volunteer equipment" field.
15. Select whether you have a volunteer vehicle from the "volunteer vehicle" field.
16. Enter your vehicle make, model, and year in the "volunteer vehicle make", "volunteer vehicle model", and "volunteer vehicle year" fields respectively.
17. Enter your vehicle license plate number in the "volunteer vehicle plate" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Volunteer Group** (`volunteer_group`, select_multiple, optional): Select the groups you are interested in volunteering with. This can be multiple groups.
* **Available Dates** (`available_dates`, date, optional): Enter the dates you are available to volunteer.
* **Available Time** (`available_time`, time, optional): Enter the times you are available to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Shifts** (`volunteer_shifts`, select_multiple, optional): Select the shifts you are available to volunteer. This can be multiple shifts.
* **Volunteer Shifts 2** (`volunteer_shifts_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select additional shifts you are available to volunteer. This can be multiple shifts.
* **Volunteer Type** (`volunteer_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of volunteer work you are interested in.
* **Volunteer Level** (`volunteer_level`, number, optional): Enter your level of volunteer commitment.
* **Volunteer Hours** (`volunteer_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you are available to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Dedications** (`volunteer_dedication`, text, optional): Enter a statement about your volunteer dedication.
* **Volunteer Interests** (`volunteer_interests`, text, optional): Enter your volunteer interests.
* **Volunteer Skills** (`volunteer_skills`, text, optional): Enter any special skills you have that would be useful for volunteering.
* **Volunteer Equipment** (`volunteer_equipment`, text, optional): Enter any special equipment you need to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Vehicle** (`volunteer_vehicle`, select_one, optional): Select whether you have a volunteer vehicle.
* **Volunteer Vehicle Make** (`volunteer_vehicle_make`, text, optional): Enter the make of your volunteer vehicle.
* **Volunteer Vehicle Model** (`volunteer_vehicle_model`, text, optional): Enter the model of your volunteer vehicle.
* **Volunteer Vehicle Year** (`volunteer_vehicle_year`, number, optional): Enter the year of your volunteer vehicle.
* **Volunteer Vehicle Plate** (`volunteer_vehicle_plate`, text, optional): Enter the license plate number of your volunteer vehicle.
