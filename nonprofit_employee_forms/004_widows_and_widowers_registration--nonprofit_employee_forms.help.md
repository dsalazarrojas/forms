# Widows And Widowers Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Widows And Widowers Registration form is designed to collect information from individuals who are widows or widowers. This form will help us to keep your contact information and professional details up to date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. If you are associated with an organization, enter the name of the organization in the "organization" field.
6. If you have a job title, enter it in the "job_title" field.
7. Enter the name of your department in the "department" field.
8. If you have a physical address, enter it in the "address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address associated with your personal account.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Organization**: If you are affiliated with an organization, enter its name.
* **Job Title**: If you have a job title, enter it here.
* **Department**: Enter the name of your department.
* **Address**: If you have a physical address, enter it here.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are registering as a widow or widower and not as an organization or other type of entity.
* If you are unsure, please contact our support team for assistance.
* Fill in all required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
* Be as accurate as possible when filling in your contact information and professional details.
