# Community Outreach Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your personal details, volunteer interests, availability, and background to help us determine the best fit for your volunteer position. We value your honesty and accuracy in filling out this form.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out each field carefully and accurately.
- Make sure to save your progress as you go along, as you will not be able to save your completed form.
- If you encounter any issues or have questions, please contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or documentation.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or documentation.
- **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you for further information.
- **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter a valid phone number where we can contact you for further information.
- **Street Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your street address for our records.
- **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the name of the city where you reside.
- **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state where you reside.
- **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your ZIP code for our records.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
- **Volunteer Interests** (`volunteer_interests`, note, not required): Share with us what areas interest you and why.
- **Areas of Interest** (`interest_areas`, select_multiple, required): Select all areas that apply from the list provided.
- **Skills and Talents** (`skills`, text, not required): Tell us about any skills or talents that you can contribute to our organization.
- **Languages Spoken** (`languages`, text, not required): If you speak a language besides English, please let us know.
- **Availability** (`availability`, note, not required): Share with us your available times and dates for volunteering.
- **Available Days** (`availability_days`, select_multiple, required): Select all days that you are available to volunteer.
- **Available Times** (`availability_time`, select_multiple, required): Select all times that you are available to volunteer.
- **Hours Available Per Week** (`hours_per_week`, select_one, required): Choose the number of hours you can commit per week.
- **Available Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are available to start volunteering.
- **Background Information** (`background`, note, not required): Tell us more about yourself.
- **Previous Volunteer Experience** (`volunteer_experience`, text, not required): Share with us about any previous volunteer work you have done.
- **Why Do You Want to Volunteer** (`motivation`, text, required): Share with us your motivation for volunteering with us.
- **Willing to Undergo Background Check** (`background_check`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you are willing to undergo a background check for certain positions.
- **Reliable Transportation** (`transportation`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have reliable transportation to our location.
- **Provide One Reference Name and Contact** (`references`, text, required): Provide a name and contact information for one person who can vouch for your character and abilities.
