# donation_form_paypal_checkout - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect donations for a nonprofit organization using PayPal as the payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Donation Form PayPal Checkout**: Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your donation is successfully processed.
2. Ensure that you have a PayPal account or are willing to create one, as you will be redirected to the PayPal website for payment confirmation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Form PayPal Checkout (1)**: This field is the title of the form. Please type your answer here.
* **Amount (2)**: Enter the amount you would like to donate. Please enter a numerical value without any currency symbols.
* **Currency (3)**: Select the currency of the donation. Please choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the applicable currency.
* **Shipping Address (4)**: If you would like to receive a receipt or other communication from us, please provide your shipping address.
* **Email (5)**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you regarding your donation.
* **Phone (6)**: Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted regarding your donation.
* **Donation Frequency (7)**: Check 'Yes' if you intend to make this donation regularly or 'No' otherwise.
* **Donation Date (8)**: Enter the date you would like to make this donation.
* **Donation Time (9)**: Enter the time you would like to make this donation.
* **Comments (10)**: This is an optional field where you can provide any additional comments or notes about your donation.
* **Non-Field Label (11)**: This is a free-form field where you can enter any other information not captured by the other fields.
* **Non-Field Note (12)**: This is an optional note field where you can provide any other information that doesn't fit in the other fields.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you enter accurate and complete information in all fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [your email/phone number].
* We appreciate your donation to our nonprofit organization!
