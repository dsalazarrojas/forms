# Social Justice Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to express their support for a social justice issue. The goal is to allow people to sign in and share their stance on the issue, and to provide a message for why they support it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter the name of the non-profit organization you support, if applicable.
4. Describe the social issue you are petitioning for in the "Issue" field.
5. Write a message expressing your support for the issue in the "Petition Message" field.
6. If you'd like to show support for other signers, you can list them in the "Signers" field, but it is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, Text, Required): Enter your real name as it appears on your official documents. This field helps us understand who is signing the petition.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address. This will help us contact you if we need more information.
* **Non Profit Organisation** (`non_profit_organisation`, Text, Optional): If you are representing a non-profit organization, enter its name here.
* **Issue** (`issue`, Text, Required): Describe the social justice issue you support. Be specific and clear about what you stand for.
* **Petition Message** (`petition_message`, Text, Required): Write a brief message expressing your support for the issue. This can be as short or as long as you like.
* **Signers** (`signers`, Text, Optional): If you'd like to show support for other signers, list their names here. This is optional and helps to create a sense of community.
