# Vineyard Barrels Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vineyard Barrels Requisition Form is a purchase order request form designed to collect necessary information for the delivery of barrels to the vineyard. This form is intended for users who want to place an order for barrels and need to provide specific details for the delivery process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the "User Input" field with your details.
2. Choose your preferred "Delivery Timing" from the options provided.
3. Select all necessary "Inventory Needs" from the list.
4. Select all necessary internal "Internal Approvals" from the list.
5. Choose your preferred "Delivery Frequency" from the options provided.
6. Select your preferred "Delivery Method" from the options provided.
7. Enter the "Date Needed" for delivery.
8. Enter the "Time Needed" for delivery.
