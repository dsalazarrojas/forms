# Luxury Hotel Food Supply Order Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to request food supplies for the luxury hotel. It helps the hotel gather all necessary details for each order, ensuring that the right supplies are ordered from the right vendors.
## How To Complete This Form
- Read through each section carefully and fill in all required fields.
- Enter the contact name and email address of the person submitting the form.
- Select the delivery date for the order.
- Provide the delivery date and time (if applicable) for the order.
- Choose which items are required for the order (select 'Yes' for needed items and 'No' for not needed).
- Add any additional approval information or notes about the order.

* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, Text, Required): Enter your name or the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address or the email address of the person submitting the form. This field is optional but recommended for contact purposes.
