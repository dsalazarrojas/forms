# Purchase Price Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from customers or clients who need to request a purchase price for a specific item(s) or service. It helps the purchasing team to quickly and efficiently respond to their inquiry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the contact information section with your name, email address, and phone number so that our team can reach out to you.
2. Provide details about the customer you are representing (if applicable).
3. List the items or services you would like to inquire about and specify the quantities needed.
4. Choose how you would like to be contacted (phone, email, or text message).
5. If you have any specific requirements or requests regarding the purchase, please include them in the customer notes section.
6. Select your preferred quote delivery method (autoresponder email or thank you page).
7. If necessary, specify a quote date for the requested purchase.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name, email address, and phone number so that our team can contact you.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, `text`, required: false): If you are representing a company or organization, please provide their name and contact information.
* **Items Purchased** (`items_purchased`, `text`, required: false): List the item(s) you would like to purchase, including quantities if applicable.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how you would like to be contacted (phone, email, or text message).
* **Price Request** (`price_request`, `number`, required: false): Enter the specific price you are looking for, or leave it blank for the team to provide a price quote.
* **Purchase Terms** (`purchase_terms`, `select_multiple`, required: false): If applicable, select the purchase terms that apply to your request (Immediate Payment, Payment Upon Receipt, Payment Plan).
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, `note`, required: false): Include any specific requirements or requests for the purchase.
* **Quote Delivery** (`quote_delivery`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how you would like to receive the quote (autoresponder email or thank you page).
* **Quote Date** (`quote_date`, `date`, required: false): If necessary, specify the date you would like to receive the quote.

## Tips
* Only submit one form for each unique purchase price request to avoid duplication.
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately to avoid delays in processing your request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact our purchasing team directly.
