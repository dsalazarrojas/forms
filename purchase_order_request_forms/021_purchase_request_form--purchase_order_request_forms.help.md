# Purchase Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a purchase request for the company, providing essential details about the customer, manager, and items required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Ensure you have all the necessary information about the customer, including their email, phone number, and address.
* Select the correct category for the purchase request (e.g., Request Forms, Purchase Forms, or Other).
* Indicate whether the customer has agreed to the purchase request (Yes/No).
* Enter the customer's email address and phone number in the corresponding fields.
* Select the customer's city and state from the provided options.
* Specify the items required for the purchase (Yes/No).
* Enter the quantity of items requested.
* Input the price of the item(s) requested.
* Select the date and time of the request.
* Specify whether the department is involved in the purchase request (Yes/No).
* Indicate whether the department manager is aware of the request (Yes/No).
* Enter the department manager's phone number and email address.
* Select the manager's city and state from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Category** (select_one): Select the correct category for the purchase request from the options provided.
  * Options: Request Forms, Purchase Forms, Other
  * Required: No
- **Customer** (select_one): Indicate whether the customer has agreed to the purchase request.
  * Options: Yes, No
  * Required: No
- **Customer Email** (email): Enter the customer's email address.
  * Required: No
- **Customer Phone** (text): Enter the customer's phone number.
  * Required: No
- **Customer Address** (select_multiple): Specify whether the customer has an address.
  * Options: Yes, No
  * Required: No
- **Customer City** (select_one): Select the customer's city from the options provided.
  * Options: New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Boston, Dallas, San Francisco
  * Required: No
- **Customer State** (select_one): Select the customer's state from the options provided.
  * Options: California, New York, Texas, Illinois, Florida, Michigan
  * Required: No
- **Customer Zip** (select_one): Specify whether the customer has a zip code.
  * Options: Yes, No
  * Required: No
- **Items** (select_multiple): Specify whether items are required for the purchase request.
  * Options: Yes, No
  * Required: No
- **Item Quantity** (select_one): Enter the quantity of items requested.
  * Options: Yes, No
  * Required: No
- **Item Price** (number): Input the price of the item(s) requested.
  * Required: No
- **Request Date** (date): Select the date of the request.
  * Required: No
- **Request Time** (time): Select the time of the request.
  * Required: No
- **Department** (select_one): Specify whether the department is involved in the purchase request.
  * Options: Yes, No
  * Required: No
- **Department Manager** (select_one): Indicate whether the department manager is aware of the request.
  * Options: Yes, No
  * Required: No
- **Manager Phone** (text): Enter the department manager's phone number.
  * Required: No
- **Manager Email** (email): Enter the department manager's email address.
  * Required: No
- **Manager Address** (select_multiple): Specify whether the department manager has an address.
  * Options: Yes, No
  * Required: No
- **Manager City** (select_one): Select the department manager's city from the options provided.
  * Options: New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Boston, Dallas, San Francisco
  * Required: No
- **Manager State** (select_one): Select the department manager's state from the options provided.
  * Options: California, New York, Texas, Illinois, Florida, Michigan
  * Required: No
- **Manager Zip** (select_one): Specify whether the department manager has a zip code.
  * Options: Yes, No
  * Required: No

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely to avoid any delays or issues with the purchase request.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or department manager for guidance.
* Review your form for any errors before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the support team.
