# supplier_comparison_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather and compare information about different suppliers, including their name, quote date, notes, price, contact name, email, phone number, quote status, and any additional note.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Supplier Name** field with the name of the supplier you are evaluating.
2. Select the **Quote Date** field and choose the date of the quote from the calendar picker or enter it manually.
3. Add any additional **Supplier Notes** about the supplier in the text field.
4. Enter the **Quote Price** if available or leave blank if not.
5. Enter the **Contact Name** of the supplier.
6. Enter the **Email** of the contact person.
7. Enter the **Phone** number of the contact person.
8. Choose the **Quote Status** from the dropdown list (Active, Inactive, or Expired).
9. Optionally add any **Note** about the supplier.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Name** (Supplier Name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the supplier you are evaluating.
* **Quote Date** (Quote Date, date, required: false): Select the date of the quote or enter it manually.
* **Supplier Notes** (Supplier Notes, text, required: false): Add any additional notes about the supplier.
* **Quote Price** (Quote Price, number, required: false): Enter the quote price if available or leave blank if not.
* **Contact Name** (Contact Name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Email** (Email, email, required: false): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Quote Status** (Quote Status, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the quote status from the dropdown list (Active, Inactive, or Expired).
* **Note** (Note, note, required: false): Add any additional note about the supplier.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate comparison.
* If you have any questions, please refer to this help guide for further assistance.
* If a field has a dropdown list, select the most appropriate option for the supplier.
