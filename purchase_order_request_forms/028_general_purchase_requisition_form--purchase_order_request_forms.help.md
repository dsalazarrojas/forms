# General Purchase Requisition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The General Purchase Requisition Form is used for requesting purchases from the company. Fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure that your request is processed promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are filled in.
2. Enter your employee details, including name and email.
3. Enter the purchase date.
4. Provide a detailed description of the item being requested.
5. Enter the quantity and unit price of the item.
6. State the reason for your purchase request.
7. Indicate the department where the item will be used.
8. Name the manager who will be using the item.
9. Enter the manager's email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Employee Information

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID badge or personnel file.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, required): Enter your work email address to enable communication and approval processes.
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, required): Enter the date you need the item by.
* **Item Name** (`item_name`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the item you are requesting.
* **Item Description** (`item_description`, text, required): Elaborate on the item you are requesting, including any relevant details or specifications.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the quantity of the item you need.
* **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, number, required): Enter the unit price of the item.
* **Total** (`total`, number, required): Calculate the total cost based on the quantity and unit price.
* **Purchase Reason** (`purchase_reason`, text, required): Provide a clear explanation of why you need the item.
* **Department** (`employee_department`, text, required): Indicate the department where the item will be used.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Identify the manager who will be using the item.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required): Enter the manager's email address to enable further communication.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your entries before submission to prevent errors.
* Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
* If you have any additional information or attachments regarding your purchase request, you may include them in the form's comments section or attach supporting documents.
* Review your submission carefully to ensure it meets your needs and departmental requirements.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant department or manager for assistance.
