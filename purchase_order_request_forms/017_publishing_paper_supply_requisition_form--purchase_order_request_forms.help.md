# Publishing Paper Supply Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about publishing paper supply requisition requests. It is intended to be filled out by staff members or publishing departments to request supplies for publishing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the form details on **Page 1**.
2. Enter the publishing information on **Page 2**.
3. Specify the order details on **Page 3**.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Details** (`page_1`, `text`, not required): Enter a brief description of the purpose of this request.
* **Publishing Information** (`page_2`, `text`, not required): Enter any relevant publishing information that may be required for the supply request, such as deadlines, publication dates, or relevant contacts.
* **Order Details** (`page_3`, `text`, not required): Specify the type and quantity of paper needed, including any specific requirements or special instructions.
