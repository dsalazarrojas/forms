# Municipal Office Machinery Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Municipal Office Machinery Requisition Form is a tool for municipal office employees to submit requests for equipment, machinery, or vehicles for their departments. This form helps ensure that requests are properly documented and reviewed by the relevant authorities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the department that the requested equipment is for (e.g., Department of Finance, Department of Public Works, etc.).
2. Choose the type of equipment (e.g., Office Equipment, Machinery, or Vehicles).
3. Enter the equipment model (e.g., the specific model of the machine).
4. Specify the quantity of the equipment needed.
5. Enter the price per unit of the equipment.
6. Calculate the total cost of the requested equipment.
7. Review and submit the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Department** (`department`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the department that the requested equipment is for.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of equipment (e.g., Office Equipment, Machinery, or Vehicles).
* **Requisition Date** (`requisition_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the request (optional).
* **Equipment Model** (`municipal_office_machinery_requisition_form_5`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the specific model of the equipment (optional).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, required/optional): Specify the number of units of the equipment needed.
* **Price per Unit** (`price_per_unit`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the price per unit of the equipment.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, `number`, required/optional): Calculate the total cost of the requested equipment (optional).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all relevant fields accurately and completely.
* If you are requesting equipment for a specific project, be sure to include any relevant project details in the Equipment Model field.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your department head or the municipal office administration for assistance.
