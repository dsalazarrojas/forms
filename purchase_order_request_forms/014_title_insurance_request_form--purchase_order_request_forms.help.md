# Title Insurance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers who are requesting title insurance. It is essential to fill it out accurately to ensure that your request is processed efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** accurately.
2. Enter your **Email** address to ensure that we can contact you for further information.
3. Select your **Property Type** from the options provided (residential or commercial).
4. Enter your **Order Date** in the correct format (date).
5. Choose your preferred **Shipment Method** from the options provided (mail, email, or fax).
6. Select your desired **Document Type** from the options provided (policy, title, or escrow).
7. Provide any additional **Order Comments** you have.
8. If applicable, fill in your **Client Name**, **Client Phone**, **Agent Name**, **Agent Phone**, **Company Address**, **Client Address**, and **Shipment Instructions** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name here.
* **Email**: Enter your email address here to ensure we can contact you for further information.
* **Company**: Enter your company name or information here.
* **Property Type**: Select one of the two options provided (residential or commercial).
* **Order Date**: Enter the date for the order in the correct format (date).
* **Shipment Method**: Choose how you would like to receive the document (mail, email, or fax).
* **Document Type**: Select the type of document you are requesting (policy, title, or escrow).
* **Order Comments**: Add any additional comments you have about your order.
* **Client Name**: If you are ordering on behalf of a client, enter their name here.
* **Client Phone**: If you are ordering on behalf of a client, enter their phone number here.
* **Agent Name**: Enter the name of the agent or representative.
* **Agent Phone**: Enter the phone number of the agent or representative.
* **Company Address 1**, **Company Address 2**, **Company City**, **Company State**, **Company Zip**, **Company Country**: Enter your company's address here.
* **Client Address 1**, **Client Address 2**, **Client City**, **Client State**, **Client Zip**: Enter your client's address here.
* **Shipment Instructions**: Provide any special instructions for the shipment of the document.
