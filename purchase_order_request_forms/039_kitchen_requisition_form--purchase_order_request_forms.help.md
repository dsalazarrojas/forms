# Kitchen Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The kitchen requisition form is a tool used to document kitchen equipment or supplies requests from users. It provides the necessary information for the kitchen team to process and fulfill the requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the "Requisitioner Information" field.
2. List the items you are requesting in the "List of Items" field.
3. Specify the quantity of each item you need in the "Quantity" field.
4. Provide the unit of measurement for each item in the "Unit" field.
5. Select the status of your request (e.g., urgent, non-urgent) in the "Status" field.
6. Add any additional comments or notes about your request in the "Notes" field.
7. Provide any additional information that may be relevant to your request in the "Additional Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Requisitioner Information 
* **Requisitioner Information** (`Requirer Information`, `text`, required): Enter your full name and contact information so the kitchen team can efficiently process and communicate with you about your request.

* **List of Items**
* **Quantity**
* **Unit**
* **Status**
* **Notes**
* **Additional Info**
All the other fields are text fields that require you to provide a description of the item, quantity, unit, status, and any additional comments or information. 

### Tips
* Please provide accurate and clear information to help the kitchen team efficiently process your request.
* If you need to add or remove items from your request, please create a new form and include the updated information.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact the kitchen team. 

Note: The form is for single requisition, if you need to create a new request, please use a new form.
