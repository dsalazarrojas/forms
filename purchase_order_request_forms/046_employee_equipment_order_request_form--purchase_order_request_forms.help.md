# Employee Equipment Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Equipment Order Request Form is a tool for employees to submit their equipment requests for work-related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information in the first field: "UserInfo".
2. Provide an overview of the project you are working on in the second field: "ProjectInfo".
3. Specify the equipment you need in the third field: "EquipmentRequest".
4. Add any additional order details in the fourth field: "OrderDetails".
5. Finally, click on the "Submit" button to send your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Information (1)
* **UserName** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your company ID badge or employee database.

### Project Information (2)
* **Project Name** (`project_info`, text, required): Briefly describe the project you are working on, including its name and relevant details.

### Equipment Request (3)
* **Equipment Needed** (`equipment_request`, text, required): Specify the equipment you are requesting, including the type, model, and quantity if applicable.

### Order Details (4)
* **Additional Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): If there is any additional information you want to provide about your order, such as installation requirements or special requests, please add it here.

### Approval (5)
* **Approval Comments** (`approval`, text, required): Please provide any comments or explanations about your order request, such as any alternative options you have considered or any relevant project milestones.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or equipment administrator.
* This form is only for work-related equipment requests. Personal equipment requests should be directed to your supervisor or department manager.
