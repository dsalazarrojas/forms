# Spa Skincare Product Order Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect order and payment details from customers for spa skincare products.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Order Details** section with any necessary information about your order.
2. Provide your **Shipping Information** in the **Shipping Information** section.
3. Share your **Customer Information** in the **Customer Information** section.
4. Add any additional **Order Notes** in the **Order Notes** section.
5. Enter your **Email** and **Phone** for contact purposes.
6. Select the **Delivery Date** and **Delivery Time** for your order.
7. For **Select Product**, choose the product you are interested in from the provided options.
8. If you have multiple products to select, use **Select Products**.
9. Choose your preferred **Payment Method** and enter any additional details in **Other Payment Method** if necessary.
10. Specify your **Payment Terms**.
11. Enter your **Order ID** (if applicable).
12. Add any **Customer Notes**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (Order Details): Please provide any specific details about your order.
* **Shipping Information** (Shipping Information): Enter your shipping address.
* **Customer Information** (Customer Information): Provide your name and contact information.
* **Order Notes** (Order Notes): Add any additional comments about your order.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address for contact.
* **Phone** (Phone): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Delivery Date** (Delivery Date): Select the date you would like your order delivered.
* **Delivery Time** (Delivery Time): Select the time you would like your order delivered.
* **Select Product** (Select Product): Choose the product you are interested in from the provided options.
* **Select Products** (Select Products): If you are interested in multiple products, select them here.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method): Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Other Payment Method** (Other Payment Method): If your preferred method is not listed, enter it here.
* **Payment Terms** (Payment Terms): Specify your payment terms.
* **Order ID** (Order ID): Enter your order ID (if applicable).
* **Customer Notes** (Customer Notes): Add any additional comments about your order.

## Tips

* Please fill in the form accurately and completely to ensure proper order processing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the spa business.
* Make sure to double-check your order details for accuracy before submitting the form. 

This guide aims to help users navigate and complete the form accurately while ensuring that the spa skincare business can process their orders efficiently.
