# Basic Medical Needs Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Basic Medical Needs Request Form is used to collect information for basic medical needs requests from requesting organizations. This form helps to streamline the request process and ensure that medical needs are met in a timely and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the requesting organization's information in the "Organization name", "Department or unit", "Contact person name", "Contact email", and "Contact phone" fields.
2. Enter the "Request date" and "Date needed by" dates in the respective fields.
3. Select the type of medical equipment needed from the "Type of medical equipment needed" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the "Specific items or brands needed", "Quantity needed", and "Unit of measurement" fields.
5. Select the "Medical urgency level" and "Sterilization requirements" options from the respective dropdown menus.
6. Enter the "Estimated cost" and "Budget code or cost center" fields (if applicable).
7. Fill in the "Manager/Approver name" field.
8. Enter any "Special delivery or handling instructions" and "Justification for this request" (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required): The name of the organization requesting the medical needs.
* **Department or unit** (`department`, `text`, required): The department or unit within the organization requesting the medical needs.
* **Contact person name** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): The name of the contact person for the requesting organization.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): The email address of the contact person.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): The phone number of the contact person.
* **Request date** (`request_date`, `date`, required): The date the request is being made.
* **Date needed by** (`needed_by_date`, `date`, required): The date the medical equipment is needed by.
* **Type of medical equipment needed** (`medical_equipment_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of medical equipment needed from the dropdown menu.
* **Specific items or brands needed** (`specific_items`, `text`, required): Enter the specific items or brands of medical equipment needed.
* **Quantity needed** (`quantity_needed`, `number`, required): Enter the quantity of medical equipment needed.
* **Unit of measurement** (`unit_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the unit of measurement for the quantity needed.
* **Patient population (approximate number)** (`patient_population`, `number`, optional): Enter the approximate number of patients that will be served by the requested medical equipment.
* **Medical urgency level** (`medical_urgency`, `select_one`, required): Select the medical urgency level for the request.
* **Sterilization requirements** (`sterilization_requirements`, `select_one`, optional): Select the sterilization requirements for the requested medical equipment.
* **Estimated cost** (`estimated_cost`, `number`, optional): Enter the estimated cost of the requested medical equipment.
* **Budget code or cost center** (`budget_code`, `text`, optional): Enter the budget code or cost center for the estimated cost.
* **Manager/Approver name** (`approver_name`, `text`, required): The name of the manager or approver for the request.
* **Special delivery or handling instructions** (`special_instructions`, `text`, optional): Enter any special delivery or handling instructions for the requested medical equipment.
* **Justification for this request** (`justification`, `text`, optional): Enter a justification for the request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have any special delivery or handling instructions, please enter them in the "Special delivery or handling instructions" field.
* If you have any justification for this request, please enter it in the "Justification for this request" field.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, please do not hesitate to contact us.
