## Material Requisition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Material Requisition Form is used to request materials or equipment from the supplier for purchase or use in the office. This form is required for all material or equipment requests, ensuring that all necessary information is captured for processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the equipment ordered from the list of options provided (e.g., Printer, Computer, Headphones).
2. Fill in the supplier's name and contact information, including phone number.
3. Optionally, enter the date for the material requisition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment Ordered**: <code>Printer</code>, <code>Computer</code>, or <code>Headphones</code> - Select the type of equipment or material you are requesting from the list provided.
* **Supplier Name**: Enter the name of the supplier from whom you are ordering the equipment or material.
* **Supplier Phone**: Enter the phone number of the supplier for contact purposes.
* **Material Requisition Date**: Optionally, enter the date for the material requisition (format: YYYY-MM-DD). 

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed.
* Double-check the supplier's contact information for accuracy.
* Submit the form for review and processing.

Note: This form is designed to guide the user through the steps to request materials or equipment. Ensure that all steps are followed carefully to avoid errors in the requisition process.
