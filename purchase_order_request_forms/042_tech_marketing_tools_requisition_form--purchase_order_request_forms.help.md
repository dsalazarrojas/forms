# Tech Marketing Tools Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for a "Tech Marketing Tools Requisition Form" used by the marketing department to request technical tools and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the "Marketing Tools Form" page, which includes fields for "Tool Name", "Category", "Description", "Cost", "Approved By", "Approver Email", "Approver Name", "Requester Name", "Requester Email", and "Requester Title".
2. Fill out the "Tech Request Details" page, which includes fields for "Request Date", "Request Time", "Department Request", "Manager Request", "Requester Manager", "Requester Team", "Team Lead", "Status", "Reviewer", "Reviewed", and "Comments".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tool Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the marketing tool you are requesting.
* **Category** (select_multiple, required): Select all applicable categories for this tool.
* **Description** (text, required): Briefly describe the tool and its purpose.
* **Cost** (text, required): Enter the estimated cost of the tool.
* **Approved By** (email, required): Enter the email of the person approving this request.
* **Approver Email** (email, required): Enter the email of the person approving this request.
* **Approver Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the person approving this request.
* **Requester Name** (text, required): Enter your name.
* **Requester Email** (email, required): Enter your email.
* **Requester Title** (text, required): Enter your title.
* **Request Date** (date, required): Enter the date you are requesting this tool.
* **Request Time** (time, required): Enter the time you are requesting this tool.
* **Department Request** (text, required): Enter the department requesting this tool.
* **Manager Request** (text, required): Enter the manager of this request.
* **Requester Manager** (text, required): Enter the manager of the requester.
* **Requester Team** (text, required): Enter the team of the requester.
* **Team Lead** (text, required): Enter the team lead of the requester.
* **Status** (select_one, required): Select the status of this request.
* **Reviewer** (text, required): Enter the reviewer of this request.
* **Reviewed** (text, required): Enter the reviewed date of this request.
* **Comments** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy.
* Use the correct category and status for your request. 
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor.
