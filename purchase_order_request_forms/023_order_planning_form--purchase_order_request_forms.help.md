# Order Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Order Planning Form is a tool for planning and coordinating purchase orders within the organization. It helps you gather necessary information to ensure smooth and efficient order execution.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1.  Complete the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2.  For each field, provide accurate and detailed information as per the field label.
3.  Review and update the form regularly to reflect changes in the order status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **order_planning_form** (`1`, text, optional): A brief description of the order.
*   **order_details** (`2`, text, optional): A detailed description of the order, including the ordered item(s).
*   **delivery_info** (`3`, text, optional): Information about delivery, such as delivery date and time.
*   **submitter_info** (`4`, text, optional): Information about the person or department submitting the order.
*   **contact_info** (`5`, text, optional): Contact information for the person or department submitting the order.
*   **purchase_request_details** (`6`, text, optional): Detailed information about the purchase request.
*   **department_approval** (`7`, select_one, optional): Check if department approval is required for the order.
*   **purchase_requester_approval** (`8`, select_one, optional): Check if purchase requestor approval is required for the order.
*   **purchase_requester** (`9`, text, optional): Information about the person or department requesting the purchase.
*   **contact_name** (`10`, text, optional): Name of the contact person or department.
*   **contact_phone** (`11`, text, optional): Phone number of the contact person or department.
*   **contact_email** (`12`, email, optional): Email address of the contact person or department.
*   **delivery_date** (`13`, date, optional): The planned delivery date for the order.
*   **delivery_time** (`14`, time, optional): The planned delivery time for the order.
*   **delivery_note** (`15`, note, optional): Additional information about delivery.
*   **order_notes** (`16`, note, optional): Notes about the order.
*   **purchase_requester_comments** (`17`, note, optional): Comments from the purchase requestor.
*   **department_comments** (`18`, note, optional): Comments from the department.
*   **purchase_requester_comments** (`19`, note, optional): Comments from the purchase requestor.
*   **approval_notes** (`20`, note, optional): Notes about the approval process.
*   **purchase_requester_signature** (`21`, text, optional): Signature of the purchase requestor.
*   **department_signature** (`22`, text, optional): Signature of the department.
*   **purchase_requester_approval_status** (`23`, select_one, optional): Status of the purchase requestor's approval.
*   **department_approval_status** (`24`, select_one, optional): Status of the department's approval.
*   **purchase_requester_notes** (`25`, note, optional): Additional notes from the purchase requestor.
