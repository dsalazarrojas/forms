# programming_equipment_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request programming equipment for work-related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information on each page, and make sure to complete all fields as necessary.
2. Review the fields below for further instructions on what information is required for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Purchase Order Request** (`purchase_order_request`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the equipment you need, such as the type of equipment and its intended use.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, select one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you need the equipment for work-related purposes, 'No' otherwise.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of equipment you need.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the equipment you need, including any specific features or requirements.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address so that we can reach you for further information.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number so that we can reach you for further information.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the tool or equipment you are assigning to, if applicable.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, optional): Enter the person or team you are assigning the equipment to, if applicable.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field to ensure efficient processing of your request.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at your contact email or phone number.
