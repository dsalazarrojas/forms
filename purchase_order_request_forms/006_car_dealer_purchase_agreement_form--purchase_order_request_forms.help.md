# Car Dealer Purchase Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Car Dealer Purchase Agreement Form is a document used to outline the terms and conditions of a vehicle purchase between a buyer and a dealership. It provides a clear and comprehensive overview of the vehicle's details, financial terms, and warranty information. This form is essential for ensuring a smooth and transparent transaction process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the dealership's name in the "Dealership Name" field.
2.  Enter your full name in the "Buyer Full Name" field.
3.  Enter your email address in the "Buyer Email Address" field.
4.  Enter your primary contact phone number in the "Buyer Phone Number" field.
5.  For the vehicle details, enter the "Vehicle Make" and "Vehicle Model" fields with the correct information.
6.  Enter the vehicle's "Year of Manufacture" and "Odometer Reading" fields.
7.  Enter the "Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)" with 17 characters.
8.  In the "Financial Terms" section, enter the "Total Purchase Price", "Down Payment Amount", and select the "Method of Payment/Financing" accordingly.
9.  If applicable, enter the "Trade-in Allowance" value.
10. Select the "Warranty Inclusion" from the provided options.
11.  Enter the date of sale in the "Date of Sale" field.
12.  Finally, enter your full name in the "Buyer Digital Signature" field and the dealership's representative name in the "Dealer Representative Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Dealership Name (Dealership Name, text, required)**: Enter the name of the selling dealership.
*   **Buyer Full Name (Buyer Full Name, text, required)**: Enter your full name as the buyer.
*   **Buyer Email Address (Buyer Email Address, email, required)**: Enter your email address for digital communication.
*   **Buyer Phone Number (Buyer Phone Number, text, required)**: Enter your primary contact phone number.
*   **Vehicle Make (Vehicle Make, text, required)**: Enter the make of the vehicle, e.g., Toyota, Ford, BMW.
*   **Vehicle Model (Vehicle Model, text, required)**: Enter the model of the vehicle, e.g., Camry, F-150, 3 Series.
*   **Year of Manufacture (Vehicle Year, number, required)**: Enter the year the vehicle was manufactured.
*   **Vehicle Identification Number (VIN, text, required)**: Enter the 17-character unique identifier of the vehicle.
*   **Odometer Reading (Odometer Reading, number, required)**: Enter the current mileage of the vehicle.
*   **Total Purchase Price (Purchase Price, number, required)**: Enter the final price of the vehicle including fees.
*   **Down Payment Amount (Down Payment, number, required)**: Enter the amount paid at the time of signing.
*   **Method of Payment/Financing (Method of Payment/Financing, select_one, required)**: Select how the remaining balance is covered.
*   **Trade-in Allowance (Trade-in Allowance, number, optional)**: Enter the value of the trade-in vehicle if applicable.
*   **Date of Sale (Agreement Date, date, required)**: Enter the date of the sale.
*   **Warranty Inclusion (Warranty Inclusion, select_one, required)**: Select the type of warranty provided.
*   **Buyer Digital Signature (Buyer Digital Signature, text, required)**: Enter your full name to sign electronically.
*   **Dealer Representative Name (Dealer Representative Name, text, required)**: Enter the name of the dealership's representative handling the sale.
