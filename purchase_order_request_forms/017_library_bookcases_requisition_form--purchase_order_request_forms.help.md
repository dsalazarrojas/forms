# Library Bookcases Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Library Bookcases Requisition Form is used to request library bookcases for departments or teams. This form helps us understand your specific needs and ensure efficient delivery of bookcases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information.
2. Enter your name, last name, email, phone number, and library name.
3. Indicate the item quantity needed.
4. Choose a delivery date and time that suits you.
5. Add any additional notes if required.
6. Provide your shipping address and department information.
7. Select the type of library case you require.
8. You can add multiple library case types if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`user_input_1`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name_1`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name here.
* **Email Address** (`email_1`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_1`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Library Name** (`library_1`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the library you are requesting the bookcases for.
* **Item Quantity** (`item_1`, number, required/optional): Enter the quantity of library bookcases you need.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date_1`, date, required/optional): Choose a date for delivery of the bookcases.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time_1`, time, required/optional): Choose a time for delivery of the bookcases.
* **Notes** (`notes_1`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your request.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address_1`, text, required/optional): Enter the shipping address where the bookcases will be delivered.
* **Department** (`department_1`, text, required/optional): Enter the department name where the bookcases will be used.
* **Library Case Type** (`library_case_1`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of library case you require (1-3, 4-6, 7-12, 13-20, or Other).
* **Second Name** (`user_input_2`, text, required/optional): Enter your second name here.
* **Library Case Type 1** (`library_case_type_1`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select multiple library case types (1-3, 4-6, 7-12, 13-20, or Other).
* **Third Name** (`user_input_3`, text, required/optional): Enter your third name here.
* **Item 2** (`item_2`, number, required/optional): Enter the quantity of the second item needed.
* **Delivery Date 2** (`delivery_date_2`, date, required/optional): Choose a date for delivery of the second item.
* **Delivery Time 2** (`delivery_time_2`, time, required/optional): Choose a time for delivery of the second item.
* **Notes 2** (`notes_2`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the second item.
* **Shipping Address 2** (`shipping_address_2`, text, required/optional): Enter the shipping address where the second item will be delivered.
* **Department 2** (`department_2`, text, required/optional): Enter the department name where the second item will be used.
* **Library Case 2** (`library_case_2`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of library case you require for the second item (1-3, 4-6, 7-12, 13-20, or Other).
* **Fourth Name** (`user_input_4`, text, required/optional): Enter your fourth name here.
