# Training Funds Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Training Funds Request Form** is a form used to request training funds for employees. This form helps employees submit their training needs and expenses to the management for approval. It is used to track and manage employee training requests within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a valid reason for requesting training funds, and that you have not already submitted a similar request.
2. Enter your employee name in the **employee_name** field.
3. Enter your employee ID in the **employee_id** field.
4. Enter your manager's name in the **manager_name** field.
5. Select the date of the request for the training funds in the **request_date** field.
6. Enter the amount of funds requested for the training in the **fund_amount** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_name**: This field is used to enter your name as an employee requesting the training funds.
* **employee_id**: This field is used to enter your employee ID for identification purposes.
* **manager_name**: This field is used to enter your manager's name for approval and coordination.
* **request_date**: This field is used to enter the date of the request for the training funds. Please use the date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **fund_amount**: This field is used to enter the amount of funds requested for the training. The entered amount will be used for budgeting and accounting purposes.

## Tips

- Be sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth approval process.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your manager or the finance department for assistance.
- Ensure that you have all necessary documentation and receipts for the requested training expenses.
