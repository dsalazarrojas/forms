# Library Expansion Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to request library expansions, which are used to purchase new materials or add new items to the library's collection.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the branch name, address, city, state, and country where the library expansion will be delivered.
2. Select the type of material you need (Books, Audio-visual, Software, Database, or Other).
3. Enter the quantity of the material you need.
4. Choose a desired delivery date for the material.
5. Choose a desired delivery time for the material.
6. Enter your email address and phone number so we can contact you about the expansion request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Library Expansion** (`form_submission`, note): A note section to provide any additional information about the library expansion request.
* **Branch Name** (`branch_name`, text): Enter the name of your branch where the library expansion will be delivered.
* **Branch Address** (`branch_address`, text): Enter the address of your branch where the library expansion will be delivered.
* **Branch City** (`branch_city`, text): Enter the city of your branch where the library expansion will be delivered.
* **Branch State** (`branch_state`, text): Enter the state of your branch where the library expansion will be delivered.
* **Branch Country** (`branch_country`, text): Enter the country of your branch where the library expansion will be delivered.
* **Budget Request** (`budget_request`, text): Enter any budget constraints or requirements for the library expansion request.
* **Material Type** (`material_type`, select_one): Select the type of material you need (Books, Audio-visual, Software, Database, or Other).
* **Quantity Request** (`quantity_request`, number): Enter the quantity of the material you need.
* **Desired Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date): Choose a desired delivery date for the material.
* **Desired Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time): Choose a desired delivery time for the material.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address so we can contact you about the expansion request.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about the expansion request.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for the branch details.
* Be specific about the type and quantity of material you need.
* Choose a realistic delivery date and time for the material.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
