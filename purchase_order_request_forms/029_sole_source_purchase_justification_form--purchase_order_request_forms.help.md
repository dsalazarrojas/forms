# Sole Source Purchase Justification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sole Source Purchase Justification Form is used to document the justification and details of a purchase that can only be sourced from a single vendor or supplier. This form ensures that the purchase process follows company policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have permission to submit a sole source purchase request.
2. Review company policies and procedures for sole source purchases.
3. Provide detailed information about the purchase request.
4. Select the type of purchase (Sole Source, Best Value, or Other).
5. Provide justification for the purchase.
6. Enter vendor information.
7. Enter contract dates and value (if applicable).
8. Select the procurement officer responsible for the purchase.
9. Select the department head(s) involved in the purchase.
10. Enter the request and review dates (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Purchase Request Details** (`purchase_request_details`): A text field where you can enter detailed information about the purchase request.
* **purchase_type** (`purchase_type`): A select field to choose the type of purchase (Sole Source, Best Value, or Other). This ensures that the purchase process follows company policies and procedures.
* **justification** (`justification`): A note field where you can enter the justification for the purchase.
* **vendor_name** (`vendor_name`): A text field where you can enter the vendor's name.
* **contract_date** (`contract_date`): A date field where you can enter the contract date (if applicable).
* **contract_value** (`contract_value`): A number field where you can enter the contract value (if applicable).
* **procurement_officer** (`procurement_officer`): A select field to choose the procurement officer responsible for the purchase.
* **department_head** (`department_head`): A select multiple field to choose the department head(s) involved in the purchase.
* **request_date** (`request_date`): A date field where you can enter the request date (if applicable).
* **review_date** (`review_date`): A date field where you can enter the review date (if applicable).

## Tips

* Review company policies and procedures for sole source purchases before submitting this form.
* Ensure that the justification for the purchase is clear and concise.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid delays in the purchase process.
