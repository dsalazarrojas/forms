# Theme Park Souvenirs Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request theme park souvenirs for events, promotions, or guest services. It is designed to gather information about the request, including event type, souvenir items, quantity, cost, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of event or service you are requesting souvenirs for (Event, Promotion, or Guest Service).
2. Choose the souvenir items you are interested in (T-Shirt, Hat, Pin, Mug, or Postcard).
3. Enter the quantity of each souvenir item you are requesting.
4. Enter the cost of the souvenirs.
5. Select the approval status of your request (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
6. Enter the date and time of your request.
7. Enter any additional information you would like to provide about your request.
8. Enter your name and contact information (email and phone number).
