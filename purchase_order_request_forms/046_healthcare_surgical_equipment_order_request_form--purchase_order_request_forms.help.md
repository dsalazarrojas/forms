# health_care_surgical_equipment_order_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture essential information for requesting health care surgical equipment. It is used by hospital staff to gather the necessary details for the processing and delivery of the equipment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information as accurately as possible.
2. Ensure that all fields are filled before submitting the form.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to your department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): This field is used to provide a detailed description of the order, including any additional comments or instructions.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the order is required or requested.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if the order is for an urgent or emergency request.
	+ Select 'No' otherwise.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select 'Active' if the order is active and needs to be fulfilled.
	+ Select 'Inactive' if the order is inactive or cancelled.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department or team responsible for handling the equipment request.
* **Equipment Requested** (`equipment_requested`, text, optional): Briefly describe the type of equipment being requested.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name and title of the person responsible for the order.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required information is filled out accurately and completely.
* Double-check the form for any errors before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your department for assistance.
