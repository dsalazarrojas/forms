# Bank Purchase Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bank Purchase Authorization Form is used to request approval for purchases, ensuring that all necessary information is provided and that the required approvals are in place.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete the **Requestor Information** section with your full name and department/ cost center.
2. Fill in the **Requestor Name** field with your name as it appears on your employee ID badge.
3. Select the correct department/ cost center from the **Department / Cost Center** dropdown menu.
4. Enter the date you are requesting this purchase.
5. In the **Purchase Details** section, fill in the description of the items or services requested.
6. Enter the estimated total cost, including tax and shipping.
7. Select the **Urgency Level** of the purchase (Routine, Important, or Critical / Emergency).
8. Enter the name of the proposed vendor.
9. (Optional) If available, provide a link to the vendor's website or quote.
10. In the **Budget and Justification** section, select whether the purchase is within the current department budget.
11. Provide a brief business justification for the purchase.
12. In the **Approval Routing** section, enter the name of the direct manager authorized to approve this request.
13. (Optional) Enter any comments or notes for the reviewer.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor Name**: The full name of the person initiating this request.
* **Department / Cost Center**: The department or cost center responsible for this purchase.
* **Date of Request**: The date the purchase is requested.
* **Description of Items or Services**: A detailed description of what is being purchased, including quantities and model numbers (if applicable).
* **Estimated Total Cost**: The estimated total cost of the purchase, including tax and shipping.
* **Urgency Level**: The level of urgency for this purchase (Routine, Important, or Critical / Emergency).
* **Vendor Name**: The name of the proposed vendor.
* **Vendor Quote or Website Link**: (Optional) A link to the vendor's website or quote.
* **Is this purchase within the current department budget?**: Select Yes if the purchase is within the current budget; No if it is not.
* **Business Justification**: A brief explanation of why this purchase is necessary for bank operations.
* **Direct Manager Name**: The name of the person authorized to approve this request.
* **Approval Status**: For internal use only, tracks the status of the approval process.
* **Reviewer Comments**: (Optional) Any comments or notes for the reviewer.

Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly, as this form is a critical part of the bank's procurement process.
