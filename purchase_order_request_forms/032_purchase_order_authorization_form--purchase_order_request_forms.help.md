# purchase_order_authorization_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Purchase Order Authorization Form is used to verify and approve existing purchase orders in the system. This form helps ensure that all necessary information is captured before proceeding with the purchase.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the purchase order details provided on the form.
2. Select the authorization level for the purchase order (High or Low).
3. Enter the name of the person who approved the purchase order.
4. Enter the authorized amount for the purchase order.
5. Submit the form to complete the authorization process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Purchase Order** (purchase_order_form): Enter the title of the purchase order you are attempting to authorize.
* **Date** (purchase_order_date): Enter the date when the purchase order was created.
* **Authorization Level** (authorization_level): Choose the level of authorization for the purchase order. Options are High or Low.
* **Approved By** (approved_by): Enter the name of the person who approved the purchase order.
* **Authorized Amount** (authorized_amount): Enter the amount authorized for the purchase order.

## Tips
* Ensure you have already created a purchase order in the system before using this form.
* Use this form for authorization purposes only; it is not a comprehensive purchase order creation tool.
* Review the purchase order details carefully before completing this form to ensure accuracy.
