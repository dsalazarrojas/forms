# department_purchase_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Department Purchase Request Form is designed to collect information for a purchase request from a department. This form will be reviewed and approved by the department head or approver to ensure that all necessary information is collected and that purchases are made in accordance with company policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your department from the available options in the "department" field.
2. Describe the item you wish to purchase in the "item_wish_to_buy" field.
3. Provide a brief reason for the purchase in the "reason_for_purchase" field.
4. Enter the name of the store where the item can be purchased in the "store" field.
5. Enter your name and contact information in the "purchase_requester" field.
6. Enter the name and contact information of the approver in the "approver" field.
7. Enter the email address of the approver in the "approver_email" field.
8. Enter the phone number of the approver in the "approver_phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **department**: A text field where you can select your department from a list of options.
* **item_wish_to_buy**: A text field where you can describe the item you wish to purchase.
* **reason_for_purchase**: A text field where you can provide a brief reason for the purchase.
* **store**: A text field where you can enter the name of the store where the item can be purchased.
* **purchase_requester**: A text field where you can enter your name and contact information.
* **approver**: A text field where you can enter the name and contact information of the approver.
* **approver_email**: An email field where you can enter the email address of the approver.
* **approver_phone**: A text field where you can enter the phone number of the approver.
* **department_head_note**: A note field where the department head or approver can add any additional notes or comments.
