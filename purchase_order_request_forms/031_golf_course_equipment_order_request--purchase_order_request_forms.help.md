# Golf Course Equipment Order Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request equipment for a golf course. It is a crucial tool for internal communication and order management between the staff members.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Select the Pro Shop
- **Pro Shop**: Select the Pro Shop where the equipment will be picked up from.
  - Choose from: Turfco, Tee Top Golf, Duffers Golf

### Step 2: Fill in the Order Details
- **Equipment Needed**: Describe the equipment you require.
- **Pickup Date**: Enter the date you would like to pick up the equipment.
  - Format: YYYY-MM-DD

### Step 3: Contact Information
- **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the person requesting the equipment.
- **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person requesting the equipment.
- **Email**: Enter the email address of the person requesting the equipment.

### Step 4: Delivery Information
- **Pickup Address**: Enter the address where the equipment will be delivered.
- **Delivery Date**: Enter the date you would like to receive the equipment.
  - Format: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Delivery Time**: Enter the time you would like to receive the equipment.
  - Format: 24-hour format

### Step 5: Order Status and Assignment
- **Assigned To**: The form will automatically be assigned to the selected course manager.
- **Assigned By**: Enter the person assigning the order.
- **Order Date**: Enter the date the order was created.
- **Status**: Leave this field blank if you are not sure of the status.

### Step 6: Additional Information
- **Notes**: Add any additional information about the order.
- **Notes (2)**: Add any delivery notes about the order.

## Tips
- Ensure to fill out the form with accurate information to avoid delays in processing the order.
- Double-check the form before submitting to avoid mistakes.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the course manager for assistance.
