# Souvenir Booklet Advertising Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a souvenir booklet from the company. The form asks for various details to process the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the required information. If a field is required, it is marked with an asterisk (\*). 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your contact name? (\*):** Enter your name as you want it to appear on the souvenir booklet.
* **What is your contact email address? (\*):** Enter your email address as you want it to appear on the souvenir booklet.
* **What is the size of the souvenir you want to order?:** Choose the size of the souvenir booklet you want to order.
* **Confirm your contact details?:** Repeat your contact information back to us to ensure accuracy.
* **What is your payment method?:** Choose how you want to make the payment for the souvenir booklet.
* **Confirm your payment method?:** Repeat your payment method back to us to ensure accuracy.
* **Is your address correct?:** Check the accuracy of your address for delivery purposes.
* **Confirm your address?**: Repeat your address back to us to ensure accuracy.
* **Confirm payment details?**: Repeat your payment details back to us to ensure accuracy.
* **Confirm confirm details?**: This is a redundant field and is likely an error.
* **Confirm Confirm Details 2?**: This is another redundant field and likely an error.
* **Confirm address details?**: This is a redundant field and likely an error.
* **Confirm Address Details 2?**: This is another redundant field and likely an error.
* **Confirm contact details?**: Repeat your contact details back to us to ensure accuracy, but this is likely redundant and may not be necessary.
* **Confirm Details 2?**: This is a redundant field and likely an error.
* **Confirm Details 3?**: This is another redundant field and likely an error.
* **Confirm Details 4?**: This is another redundant field and likely an error.
* **Confirm Details 5?**: This is another redundant field and likely an error.

Note: The fields marked as "Confirm" may be redundant and not actually required. Please disregard them for the purpose of this form submission. 

**Tips**: Double-check all fields before submitting the form.
