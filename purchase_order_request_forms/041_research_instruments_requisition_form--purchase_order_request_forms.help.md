# Research Instruments Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research Instruments Requisition Form is designed to gather detailed information about research instruments and related costs, vendors, and other essential details for the efficient processing and approval of purchase orders. It ensures that all required information is collected accurately and consistently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the instrument you are requesting by choosing the correct option from the provided list.
2.  Choose the quantity of the instrument you need.
3.  Enter the date of purchase, which is the date you plan to acquire the instrument.
4.  Provide your contact information, including your name, department name, email, and contact number.
5.  Enter the department location and building where you are based.
6.  If applicable, select the department level where you are working (e.g., Ground Floor, First Floor, etc.).
7.  Enter the cost of the research instrument.
8.  Briefly describe the research instrument you are requesting.
9.  If necessary, select the status of the instrument (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
10.  Enter the name and contact information of the department head or person responsible.
11.  Enter the date of approval for the purchase order.
12.  Enter the name of the person who approved the request.
13.  Enter the purchase order number if it exists.
14.  Enter the date of purchase if it already exists.
15.  Provide the name and contact information of the vendor.
16.  Enter the vendor's phone number.
17.  Enter the vendor's address.
18.  Enter the email address of the vendor.
19.  Enter a remark or comment about your request, if any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **select_instrument**: Select the research instrument you are requesting from the provided list to ensure that we understand which instrument you need.
* **select_quantity**: Enter the quantity of the instrument you require, ensuring that we know how many units you need for your research.
* **date_of_purchase**: Enter the date you plan to acquire or purchase the instrument.
* **research_coordinator**: Enter your name in this field, ensuring that we know who made this request.
* **department_name**: Enter your department name to help us understand your affiliation and contact information.
* **research_email**: Enter your email address so we can reach you regarding updates or further instructions.
* **contact_number**: Enter your contact number to facilitate communication about the requisitioning process.
* **research_department_location**: Enter your department location, which helps us understand where you are based.
* **department_level**: If applicable, select the department level where you are working.
* **research_instrument_cost**: Enter the cost of the research instrument you are requesting.
* **instrument_description**: Briefly describe the research instrument you are requesting, which helps us understand its purpose and specifications.
* **instrument_status**: If necessary, select the status of the instrument (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
* **department_head**: Enter the name and contact information of the department head or person responsible.
* **approval_date**: Enter the date of approval for the purchase order.
* **approval_by**: Enter the name of the person who approved the request.
* **purchase_order_number**: If it exists, enter the purchase order number.
* **purchase_date**: If it exists, enter the date of purchase.
* **vendor**: Enter the name of the vendor providing the research instrument.
* **vendor_phone**: Enter the vendor’s phone number.
* **vendor_address**: Enter the address of the vendor.
* **vendor_email**: Enter the email address of the vendor.
* **vendor_contact**: Enter the contact person or contact information of the vendor.
* **remarks**: Enter any remarks or comments about your request, if any.

This guide should help you understand the purpose and the process of completing the Research Instruments Requisition Form. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please do not hesitate to contact us.
