# Home Appliance Stock Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a request form for home appliance stock orders.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact information section with your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the type of appliance you're interested in purchasing.
3. Provide the quantity of the appliance you need.
4. Choose a delivery date and time that suits you best.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
6. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **home_appliance_stock_order_form** (Text Field)
	+ This is the main form title.
* **delivery_details** (Text Field)
	+ Please provide any additional delivery details here, if applicable.
* **contact_info** (Text Field)
	+ This is where you can provide your contact information.
* **contact_name** (Text Field)
	+ Enter your name here.
* **contact_email** (Text Field)
	+ Enter your email address here.
* **contact_email_1** (Text Field)
	+ Enter your email address here (if different from above).
* **contact_address** (Text Field)
	+ Enter your address here.
* **contact_number** (Text Field)
	+ Enter your phone number here.
* **contact_number_1** (Text Field)
	+ Enter your phone number here (if different from above).
* **home_appliance_type** (Select Multiple Field)
	+ Choose the type of appliance you're interested in purchasing (e.g., Appliance Type 1, Appliance Type 2, Appliance Type 3).
* **quantity** (Number Field)
	+ Enter the quantity of the appliance you need.
* **delivery_date** (Date Field)
	+ Choose a delivery date for your appliance.
* **delivery_time** (Time Field)
	+ Choose a delivery time that suits you best.
* **note** (Note Field)
	+ Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **email** (Email Field)
	+ Enter your email address here.
* **phone** (Text Field)
	+ Enter your phone number here.
* **address** (Text Field)
	+ Enter your address here.
* **address_1** (Text Field)
	+ Enter your address here (if different from above).
* **address_2** (Text Field)
	+ Enter your address here (if different from above).
* **address_3** (Text Field)
	+ Enter your address here (if different from above).
* **city** (Text Field)
	+ Enter the city where you'd like your appliance delivered.

## Tips
Before submitting the form, please double-check that you've filled out all the required fields accurately to ensure that your order is processed correctly.
