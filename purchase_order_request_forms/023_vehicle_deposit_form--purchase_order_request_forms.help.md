# vehicle_deposit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a vehicle deposit request, which includes information about the vehicle being purchased and the deposit amount.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Ensure that all fields are accurate and complete.
3. Submit the form for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **vehicle_make**: Enter the make of the vehicle being purchased (e.g., Toyota, Ford, Honda, etc.).
* **vehicle_model**: Enter the model of the vehicle being purchased (e.g., Camry, Mustang, Civic, etc.).
* **purchase_date**: Enter the date the vehicle was purchased or the date the deposit is being requested for (e.g., 2022-01-01).
* **deposit_amount**: Enter the deposit amount being requested (e.g., $1000).
