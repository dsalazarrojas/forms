# Maritime Ship Parts Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for suppliers to submit their order requests for maritime ship parts. It collects essential information about the order, supplier, and delivery details.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and fill in the required fields.
2.  Ensure you enter accurate and complete information about the order, supplier, and delivery details.
3.  Review and submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Order Number** (`order_number`, `text`, required): Enter the unique order number assigned by your organization for this order.
*   **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the supplier submitting this order request.
*   **Supplier Contact** (`supplier_contact`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact information of the supplier submitting this order request.
*   **Purchase Order Date** (`purchase_order_date`, `text`, optional): Enter the date of the purchase order (if applicable).
*   **Purchase Order Reference** (`purchase_order_reference`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant purchase order reference information (if applicable).
*   **Purchase Order ID** (`purchase_order_id`, `text`, required): Enter the unique ID of the purchase order.
*   **Vessel Details** (`vessel_details`, `text`, required): Enter accurate and complete details about the vessel related to this order request.
*   **Order Request Date** (`order_request_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you need to fulfill the order by.
*   **Order Request Time** (`order_request_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you need to fulfill the order by (if applicable).
*   **Ship To** (`ship_to`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and address of the location you want the order shipped to.
*   **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your billing address for this order.
*   **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the delivery address of the order recipient.
*   **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant payment terms or conditions for this order.
*   **Order Total** (`order_total`, `text`, optional): Enter the total cost of the order.
*   **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, `text`, optional): Choose the shipping method for this order (if applicable).
*   **Shipping Service** (`shipping_service`, `text`, optional): Choose the shipping service provider (if applicable).
*   **Shipping Speed** (`shipping_speed`, `text`, optional): Choose the shipping speed (if applicable).
*   **Order Status** (`order_status`, `text`, optional): Enter the current status of the order.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments or notes about this order request.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes or information about this order request.

Please review each field carefully and enter accurate and complete information to ensure your order request is successful.
