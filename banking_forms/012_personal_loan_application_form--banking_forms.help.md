# ประก prophets form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers seeking loan services. It includes fields for personal details, loan specifics, and other relevant data to help loan officers assess loan applications and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
3. Specify the loan amount you are seeking.
4. Choose the loan term you are interested in (Six months, One year, or Two years).
5. Select the loan rate you prefer (6.0, 8.0, or 10.0).
6. Enter your date of birth and time of birth (if applicable).
7. Add any additional notes or comments about your loan application.
8. If applicable, select a category for your loan application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the amount you are seeking as a loan.
* **Loan Term** (`loan_term`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the loan term you are interested in (Six months, One year, or Two years).
* **Loan Rate** (`loan_rate`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the loan rate you prefer (6.0, 8.0, or 10.0).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your date of birth (if applicable).
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, `time`, required/optional): Enter your time of birth (if applicable).
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your loan application.
* **Category** (`category`, `text`, required/optional): If applicable, select a category for your loan application.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the tool you will be using for this loan application.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for a successful loan application.
* If you have any special requirements or considerations, please include them in the `Note` field.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask a loan officer for assistance.
* This form is intended to help us process your loan application efficiently, so please fill it out thoroughly and accurately.
