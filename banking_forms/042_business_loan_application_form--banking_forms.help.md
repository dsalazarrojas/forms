# Business Loan Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for submitting a loan application to request financial assistance for business-related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting the application process, please ensure you have all the necessary information ready. The form is divided into sections, and each section has specific fields that require your attention. Please fill out the form in the following order:

1.  Enter your name and contact email address.
2.  Provide the name of your business (if applicable).
3.  Fill in the loan amount and purpose.
4.  Outline the planned use of funds.
5.  (Optional) If you wish to disclose your annual revenue and years in business, please provide the relevant information.
6.  Consent to a credit check by selecting the correct option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Business name** (`business_name`, `text`, **optional**): If you are applying on behalf of a business, enter the business name.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, **required**): Provide your valid email address for loan correspondence.
* **Loan amount requested** (`loan_amount_requested`, `number`, **required**): Enter the total loan amount you are requesting.
* **Loan purpose** (`loan_purpose`, `text`, **required**): Briefly outline the purpose of the loan funds.
* **Project details** (`project_details`, `text`, **optional**): Provide a detailed description of how you plan to use the loan funds.
* **Annual revenue** (`annual_revenue`, `number`, **optional**): If applicable, enter your approximate annual revenue.
* **Years in business** (`years_in_business`, `number`, **optional**): If applicable, enter the number of years your business has been in operation.
* **Agree to credit check** (`agree_credit_check`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the correct option to indicate your consent for a credit check.
