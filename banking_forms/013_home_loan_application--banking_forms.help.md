# Home Loan Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to apply for a home loan. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to increase the chances of a successful loan application.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required and optional fields as instructed.
2. Double-check your input for accuracy.
3. Submit your application once all fields are complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your current address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, optional): Enter the loan amount you are applying for.
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, text, optional): Describe the purpose of your loan application.
* **Credit Score** (`credit_score`, number, optional): Enter your current credit score.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, optional): Select your employment status (Employed or Unemployed).
* **Employment Length** (`employment_length`, number, optional): Enter the length of your current employment.
* **Income** (`income`, number, optional): Enter your annual income.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_one, optional): Select your employment type (Salaried or Self-Employed).
* **Employment Date** (`employment_date`, date, optional): Enter your employment start date.
* **Property Value** (`property_value`, number, optional): Enter the value of the property you are applying for.
* **Down Payment** (`down_payment`, number, optional): Enter the down payment amount.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of property (Single Family, Multi-Family, or Condo).
* **Credit Score 2** (`credit_score_2`, number, optional): Enter your second credit score (if applicable).
* **Loan Term** (`loan_term`, select_one, optional): Select the loan term (12, 24, 36, or 48 months).
* **Interest Rate** (`interest_rate`, number, optional): Enter the interest rate for your loan.
* **Loan Status** (`loan_status`, select_one, optional): Select the loan status (Pending, Approved, Denied, or Paid Off).
* **Property Type 2** (`property_type_2`, select_one, optional): Select the type of property 2 (Single Family, Multi-Family, or Condo).
* **Loan Amount 2** (`loan_amount_2`, number, optional): Enter the loan amount 2.
* **Employment Length 2** (`employment_length_2`, number, optional): Enter the length of your current employment 2.
* **Interest Rate 2** (`interest_rate_2`, number, optional): Enter the interest rate 2.
* **Property Value 2** (`property_value_2`, number, optional): Enter the value of the property 2.
* **Loan Term 2** (`loan_term_2`, select_one, optional): Select the loan term 2 (12, 24, 36, or 48 months).

Note: Some fields are not required, you may skip them if not applicable to your situation.
