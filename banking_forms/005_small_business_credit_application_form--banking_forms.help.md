# Small Business Credit Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your small business to help us assess your creditworthiness and provide a loan with the best possible terms.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each page completely, ensuring all required fields are filled out.
2. If a field is not applicable to your business, please skip it and move on.
3. If you're unsure about any field, please ask your loan officer for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Details** (`business_details`, `text`, required: false): This page is a general introduction to your business. Please provide a brief description of your business, including its nature, size, and main activities.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Fill out your business's address. This will help us locate you on our records and verify your business's existence.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Provide your name and contact information.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address where we can reach you for further communication.
* **Business Description** (`business_description`, `text`, required: false): Describe your business in detail, including its products or services offered and target market.
* **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, `text`, required: false): Provide your business's phone number for further communication.
* **Business Website** (`business_website`, `text`, required: false): If your business has an online presence, please provide its website URL.
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, `number`, required: false): Indicate the amount of money you're requesting for the loan.
* **Loan Term** (`loan_term`, `number`, required: false): Choose the term for your loan.
* **Credit Score** (`credit_score`, `number`, required: false): If you have an existing credit score, please provide it.
* **Select One** (`select_one_option`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the best option that describes your business's financial situation. Please refer to the options provided.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple_option`, `select_multiple`, required: false): If applicable, select multiple options that describe your business's financial situation.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Fill out the date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Fill out the time in the format 12:00 PM / 13:00.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Write any additional comments or notes about your business.
* **Select Multiple2** (`select_multiple_option2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): If applicable, select multiple options that describe your business's financial situation.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Confirm your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Provide your phone number for further communication.
* **Website** (`website`, `text`, required: false): If your business has a website, please provide its URL.
* **Credit Score2** (`credit_score2`, `number`, required: false): If you have an existing credit score, please provide it.
* **Select One2** (`select_one_option2`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the best option that describes your business's financial situation.
* **Select Multiple3** (`select_multiple_option2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): If applicable, select multiple options that describe your business's financial situation.
* **Date2** (`date2`, `date`, required: false): Fill out the date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time2** (`time2`, `time`, required: false): Fill out the time in the format 12:00 PM / 13:00.
* **Note2** (`note2`, `note`, required: false): Write any additional comments or notes about your business.
