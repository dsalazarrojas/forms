# Credit Card Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information required to process credit card transactions. Please fill out this form to provide authorization to process your credit card for future transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the first name and last name fields with your name as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter your address in the Address field.
3. Enter your zip code in the Zip code field.
4. Select your credit card type from the Card Type dropdown list.
5. Enter the authorization date for the card in the Authorization Date field.
6. Enter the CVV (Card Verification Value) code in the Cvv field.
7. Select Expire date of the card from the Expire date field.
8. Enter your customer ID if you have one.
9. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
10. Enter your email address in the Email field.
11. If you have any other credit card information (CVV2, Zip code2, Address2, Last 4 digits, Expire date2, Cvv3), please fill them out as well.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last name**: Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Address**: Please enter your full address.
* **Zip code**: Please enter your zip code.
* **Card Type**: Select the type of credit card you have (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, etc.).
* **Authorization Date**: Enter the authorization date for your credit card.
* **Cvv**: Enter the CVV code on the back of your credit card.
* **Expire date**: Select whether the card expire date is Yes or No.
* **Customer ID**: If you have a customer ID, please enter it here.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Cvv2**, **Zip code2**, **Address2**, **Last 4 digits**, **Expire date2**, **Cvv3**: These fields are optional and only required if you have more than one credit card. Please fill them out accordingly.
* **Expire date2**: Select whether the second expire date is Yes or No.
* **Cvv3**: Enter the third CVV code (if available).
* **Zip code2**: Enter the second zip code (if available).
* **Address2**: Enter the second address (if available).
* **Last 4 digits**: Enter the last 4 digits of your credit card number (if available).
