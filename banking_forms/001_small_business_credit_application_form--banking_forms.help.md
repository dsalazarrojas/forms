# Small Business Credit Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Small Business Credit Application Form is designed to gather information from small businesses to facilitate the credit application process. This form is used to collect business and contact information, financial data, and other relevant details to assess the creditworthiness of the applicant business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions accurately and thoroughly. Ensure that all required fields are filled out, and provide as much detail as possible for the optional fields. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please contact us for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Business Information
* **Business Title** (`business_title`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to provide a name for the business.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to provide information about the business's contact details.
* **Financial Information** (`financial_info`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to provide financial information about the business.
* **Business Location** (`business_location`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to provide the business location.
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, `number`, required/optional): This field is used to indicate the loan amount requested.
* **Loan Term** (`loan_term`, `number`, required/optional): This field is used to indicate the loan term.
* **Collateral** (`collateral`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to provide information about any collateral.
* **Credit History** (`credit_history`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select from: Excellent, Good, Fair.
* **Business Ownership** (`business_ownership`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Please select from: Sole Proprietor, Partnership, Corporation.
* **Business Structure** (`business_structure`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select from: Sole Proprietorship, Single Member LLC, Multi-Member LLC.
* **Business Activities** (`business_activities`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to provide information about the business activities.
* **Business Products** (`business_products`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to provide information about the business products.
* **Business Description** (`business_description`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to provide a brief description of the business.
* **Business History** (`business_history`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to provide information about the business's history.
* **Tax ID** (`tax_id`, `number`, required/optional): This field is used to provide the tax ID number.
* **Business License** (`business_license`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select from: No License, Valid License, Expired License.
* **Loan Status** (`loan_status`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Please select from: Pending Approval, Approved, Denied.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to provide any additional information.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, `date`, required/optional): This field is used to provide the date the form was submitted.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, `time`, required/optional): This field is used to provide the time the form was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `email`, required/optional): This field is used to provide the email of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select from: Loan Officer, Credit Manager.
* **Submitted Comment** (`submitted_comment`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to provide any comments from the person submitting the form.
* **Reviewer Comment** (`reviewer_comment`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to provide comments from the reviewer.
* **Review Result** (`review_result`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select from: Approved Loan, Rejected Loan.
