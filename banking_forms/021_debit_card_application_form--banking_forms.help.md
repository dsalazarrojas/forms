# Debit Card Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Debit Card Application Form is designed to collect necessary information from applicants for a Debit Card. Please fill out the form accurately and honestly to ensure a smooth processing of your application.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your First Name: This is the name that appears on your credit card.
2. Enter your Last Name: This is the name that appears on your credit card.
3. Enter your Email: This is your personal email address.
4. Enter your Phone Number: This is your personal phone number.
5. Enter your Address: This is your home or work address where you can be reached.
6. Enter your Card Number: This is the credit card number you wish to link to your debit card.
7. Enter your Expiration Date: This is the expiration date of your credit card.
8. Confirm your CVV: This is the 3-digit security code on the back of your credit card.
9. Confirm your CVV: This is to ensure your CVV is accurate. Please select the same option as in the previous field.
10. Add your Signature: This is your digital signature.
11. IP Address: This is your current IP address.
12. User Agent: This is your browser type.
13. Date and Time: This is the date and time you completed this form.
14. Host: This is your computer's hostname or domain name.
15. OS: This is your operating system.
16. Browser: This is your browser type.
17. Referrer URL: This is the URL of the webpage that referred you to this form.
18. User Agent: This is your browser's user agent.
19. IP Address: This is your IP address.
20. Country: This is your country of origin.
21. City: This is your city of origin.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your debit card.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your debit card.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Address** (Text, Optional): Please enter your address. This is optional if you have provided a phone number or email.
* **Card Number** (Text, Required): Please enter the number of your debit card.
* **Expiration Date** (Date, Required): Please enter the expiration date of your debit card.
* **CVV** (Select One, Required): Please select your CVV number from the options provided.
* **Confirm CVV** (Select One, Required): Please confirm your CVV number as it is entered in the previous field.
* **Submitter Signature** (Note, Required): This is a digital signature that confirms you are the person completing this form.
* **Submitter IP** (Note, Required): This is the IP address of the device you are using to complete this form.
* **Submitter User Agent** (Note, Required): This is the type of browser and its version you are using to complete this form.
* **Submitter Date** (Date, Required): This is the date and time you completed this form.
* **Submitter Host** (Note, Required): This is the hostname or domain name of your computer.
* **Submitter OS** (Note, Required): This is your operating system.
* **Submitter Browser** (Note, Required): This is the type and version of your browser.
* **Submitter URL** (Note, Required): This is the URL of the webpage that referred you to this form.
* **Submitter Referrer** (Note, Required): This is the URL that referred you to this form.
* **Submitter Query String** (Note, Required): This is the data passed to the server through your browser.
* **Submitter Country** (Note, Required): This is your country of origin.
* **Submitter City** (Note, Required): This is your city of origin.
