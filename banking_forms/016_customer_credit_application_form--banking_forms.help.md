# customer_credit_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer_credit_application_form is used for customer credit applications. Customers who have been approved by a credit representative will need to fill out this form to provide required information for their credit application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your customer information, including your name and contact details.
2. Provide your employment information, such as your job title and work history.
3. Share your education background, including your highest level of education completed.
4. If applicable, enter your current credit score.
5. Report your monthly income.
6. List your assets, including any valuable items you own.
7. If you have had a credit check, enter the details.
8. Review and submit your form once you have completed all the sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required/optional): Fill out your name and contact details, such as phone number and email.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact details, including phone number and email.
* **Employment Information** (`employment_info`, text, required/optional): Provide your job title and work history, including your current and previous workplaces.
* **Education** (`education`, text, required/optional): Share your highest level of education completed.
* **Credit Score** (`credit_score`, text, required/optional): If applicable, enter your current credit score.
* **Income** (`income`, text, required/optional): Report your monthly income.
* **Assets** (`assets`, text, required/optional): List your valuable items, such as properties, stocks, or bonds.
* **Credit Check** (`credit_check`, text, required/optional): If you have had a credit check, enter the details.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): Review and submit your form once you have completed all the sections.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, text, required/optional): Confirm that you have reviewed and submitted your form correctly.
