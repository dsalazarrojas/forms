# identity_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify employee identity for banking purposes. It's likely used by banks to gather employee information for regulatory compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form with your personal information as requested.
2. Provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`Employee Name`, text, **required**): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Employee SSN** (`employee_ssn`, number, **required**): Enter your Social Security Number (SSN) as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Employee Address** (`Employee Address`, text, **required**): Enter your current address as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Employee Phone** (`Employee Phone`, text, **required**): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Manager Name** (`Manager Name`, text, **optional**): If applicable, enter your manager's name for verification purposes.
* **Manager SSN** (`manager_ssn`, number, **optional**): If you have a manager, please enter their Social Security Number (SSN) as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Manager Address** (`Manager Address`, text, **optional**): If you have a manager, enter their current address as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Manager Phone** (`Manager Phone`, text, **optional**): If you have a manager, enter their phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Be accurate and complete when filling out this form to avoid delays in the verification process.
* Double-check each field for correctness before submission.
