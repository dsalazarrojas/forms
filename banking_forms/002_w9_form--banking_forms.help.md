# W9 Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The W9 Form is a tax withholding form used for reporting employee tax payments. It's intended for employees who have received non-employee compensation, such as freelance work or independent contractor work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct option from the "type" field.
2. Fill in the name of the form as it's known to the payer.
3. Enter the full address of the payer.
4. Enter the city of the payer.
5. Enter the state of the payer.
6. Enter the zip code of the payer.
7. Select the type of form being completed.
8. Select the type of entity as "individual" or "business".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Type (`form_1_type`, select_one, required)**: 
  Select the type of form being completed from the provided options. Typically, "W-2" is for employees, "W-4" is for employees who want to elect to be treated as self-employed, and "W-8" is for foreign employees or non-US citizens.

* **Payer Name (`form_1_name`, text, required)**:
  Enter the full name of the payer.

* **Business Name (`form_1_business_name`, text, required)**:
  Enter the full name of the business or company paying the compensation.

* **Address (`form_1_address`, text, required)**:
  Enter the full address of the payer.

* **City (`form_1_city`, text, required)**:
  Enter the city of the payer.

* **State (`form_1_state`, text, required)**:
  Enter the state of the payer.

* **Zip (`form_1_zip`, number, required)**:
  Enter the zip code of the payer.

* **Payer Type (`form_1_payer`, select_one, required)**:
  Select "individual" for a person paying the compensation or "business" for a company paying the compensation.

## Tips
This form is for informational purposes only. Please note that some fields may be mandatory depending on the selected options.
If you're not sure about any field, contact your HR representative or the person who requested the form for guidance.
