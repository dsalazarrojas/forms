# Webinar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects registration information from attendees of a webinar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the first field. This is the name that will be displayed on the certificate of participation after the webinar.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second field. This is the surname that will be displayed on the certificate of participation after the webinar.
3. Enter your **Email Address** in the third field. This will be used to send you notifications about the webinar, including reminders and follow-up materials.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the fourth field. This is the number that will be used to contact you in case of last-minute changes or updates.
5. Enter your **Company Name** in the fifth field. This is the name of the company you are representing, or the name of the organization you are affiliated with.
6. Select your **Role** from the dropdown menu in the sixth field. This indicates your position within your company or organization.
7. Enter the **Date** you plan to attend the webinar in the seventh field. Please use the format MM/DD/YYYY to ensure accurate date parsing.
8. Select the **Time** of the webinar you plan to attend in the eighth field. Please use the 24-hour format (HH:MM) to ensure accurate time parsing.
9. Enter any additional **Note** or comments about your registration in the ninth field. This can be useful for our team to better understand your specific requirements or preferences.
10. Enter the **Webinar ID** in the tenth and final field. This is a unique identifier associated with this webinar, which will help us track your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Your name that will be displayed on the certificate of participation after the webinar.
	* Label: First Name
	* Type: Text
	* Required: Optional
	* Hint: Enter your first name (e.g., John)
- **Last Name**: Your surname that will be displayed on the certificate of participation after the webinar.
	* Label: Last Name
	* Type: Text
	* Required: Optional
	* Hint: Enter your last name (e.g., Doe)
- **Email Address**: The email address we will use to send you notifications about the webinar.
	* Label: Email
	* Type: Email
	* Required: Optional
	* Hint: Enter your email address (e.g., johndoe@example.com)
- **Phone Number**: The phone number we will use to contact you in case of last-minute changes or updates.
	* Label: Phone Number
	* Type: Text
	* Required: Optional
	* Hint: Enter your phone number (e.g., 123-456-7890)
- **Company**: The name of the company you are representing, or the name of the organization you are affiliated with.
	* Label: Company
	* Type: Text
	* Required: Optional
	* Hint: Enter your company name (e.g., Example Inc.)
- **Role**: Your position within your company or organization.
	* Label: Role
	* Type: Select Multiple
	* Required: Optional
	* Options: CEO, Manager, Intern, Director
	* Hint: Select your role within your company (e.g., CEO, Manager, etc.)
- **Date**: The date you plan to attend the webinar.
	* Label: Date
	* Type: Date
	* Required: Optional
	* Hint: Enter the date in MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/2024)
- **Time**: The time of the webinar you plan to attend.
	* Label: Time
	* Type: Time
	* Required: Optional
	* Hint: Enter the time in 24-hour format (HH:MM) (e.g., 12:00)
- **Note**: Any additional comments or preferences.
