# Tax Preparer Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Preparer Agreement Form is used to collect information and agreements from tax preparers, which are individuals or companies that help taxpayers with their tax-related tasks. This form serves as a document to verify the agreements and understandings between the tax office and the tax preparer regarding the tax preparation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the tax preparer's details**: Enter the tax preparer's identifying information, including their name and tax preparer ID.
2. **Provide client information**: Enter the client's identifying information, including their name and client ID.
3. **Specify tax year**: Enter the tax year for which the tax preparation is being done.
4. **Agree on terms**: Enter the agreement term and details of the agreement.
5. **Acknowledge agreements**: Both the tax preparer and client must acknowledge their understanding of the agreements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **tax_preparer_id (1)**: Enter the unique identifier assigned to the tax preparer by the tax office.
* **client_id (2)**: Enter the unique identifier assigned to the client by the tax office.
* **tax_year (3)**: Enter the tax year for which the tax preparation is being done (e.g., 2020).
* **agreement_term (4)**: Enter the agreed-upon term of the agreement.
* **agreement_details (5)**: Enter a brief description of the agreement terms.
* **agreement_terms (6)**: Enter the detailed terms of the agreement.
* **client_acknowledgement (7)**: The client must enter their acknowledgement of the agreement terms (1 for acknowledgement).
* **preparer_acknowledgement (8)**: The tax preparer must enter their acknowledgement of the agreement terms (1 for acknowledgement).
* **dispute_resolution (9)**: Enter a description of the process for resolving any disputes that may arise during the tax preparation process.

## Tips

* Ensure that both the tax preparer and client have a clear understanding of the agreement terms.
* Review all fields carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
