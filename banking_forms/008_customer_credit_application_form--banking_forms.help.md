# Customer Credit Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a customer credit application form used to assess the creditworthiness of a potential customer for banking services. Please provide accurate and complete information to increase the chances of getting an accurate credit score.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your personal details.
2. Select your employment status.
3. Provide your employment history details.
4. Enter your income details.
5. Enter your address details.
6. Verify your contact information.
7. Submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Customer Name**: <customer_name> (`Text`, **Required**: No): This field is used to enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Account Type**: <account_type> (`Select One`, **Required**: No): Please select the type of account you are applying for.
- **Credit Score**: <credit_score> (`Number`, **Required**: No): Please enter your current credit score.
- **Income**: <income> (`Number`, **Required**: No): Enter your annual income.
- **Employment Status**: <employment_status> (`Select One`, **Required**: No): Please select your current employment status.
- **Occupation**: <occupation> (`Text`, **Required**: No): Enter your current occupation.
- **Employment Start Date**: <employment_start_date> (`Date`, **Required**: No): Enter the date you started your current employment.
- **Employment End Date**: <employment_end_date> (`Date`, **Required**: No): Enter the date you ended your current employment.
- **Credit History**: <credit_history> (`Text`, **Required**: No): Please enter any relevant credit history information.
- **Address**: <address> (`Text`, **Required**: No): Enter your current address.
- **City**: <city> (`Text`, **Required**: No): Enter the city where you reside.
- **State**: <state> (`Text`, **Required**: No): Enter the state where you reside.
- **Zip Code**: <zip_code> (`Text`, **Required**: No): Enter your zip code.
- **Email**: <email> (`Email`, **Required**: No): Please enter your email address.
- **Phone**: <phone> (`Text`, **Required**: No): Enter your contact phone number.
- **Social Security Number**: <social_security_number> (`Text`, **Required**: No): Please enter your social security number.
- **Driver's License**: <driver_license> (`Text`, **Required**: No): Please enter your driver's license number.
- **Credit Score Note**: <credit_score_note> (`Text`, **Required**: No): Enter any additional credit score information.
- **Employment Length**: <employment_length> (`Number`, **Required**: No): Enter your length of employment in years.
- **Employment Length Unit**: <employment_length_unit> (`Select One`, **Required**: No): Please select the unit of employment length.
- **Annual Income**: <annual_income> (`Number`, **Required**: No): Enter your annual income.
- **Monthly Income**: <monthly_income> (`Number`, **Required**: No): Enter your monthly income.
- **Bank Account Number**: <bank_account_number> (`Text`, **Required**: No): Please enter your bank account number.
- **Routing Number**: <routing_number> (`Text`, **Required**: No): Please enter your routing number.
