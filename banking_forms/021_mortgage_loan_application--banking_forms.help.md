# Mortgage Loan Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information for a mortgage loan application.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide loan details such as loan amount, loan term, and loan rate.
3. Enter property information, including property address.
4. Fill in employment information and income verification.
5. Select the loan officer.
6. Provide borrower and co-borrower information if applicable.
7. Choose the payment frequency and payment amount.
8. Enter loan date and loan time.
9. Add any additional notes or comments.
10. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `text`, required): Please fill in your name, email, and phone number.
* **Loan Details** (`Loan Details`, `text`, required): Please enter loan details such as loan amount, loan term, and loan rate.
* **Property Information** (`Property Information`, `text`, required): Please enter property address and other relevant details.
* **Employment Information** (`Employment Information`, `text`, required): Please provide employment details, including job title and employer.
* **Income Verification** (`Income Verification`, `text`, required): Please provide income verification details.
* **Loan Officer** (`Loan Officer`, `select_one`, optional): Please select the loan officer who will be handling your loan application.
* **Borrower Information** (`Borrower Information`, `text`, required): Please fill in borrower's name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Co-Borrower Information** (`Co-Borrower Information`, `text`, optional): Please fill in co-borrower's name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Property Address** (`Property Address`, `text`, required): Please enter property address.
* **Collateral Information** (`Collateral Information`, `text`, required): Please provide collateral details.
* **Loan Amount** (`Loan Amount`, `number`, required): Please enter loan amount.
* **Loan Term** (`Loan Term`, `number`, required): Please enter loan term.
* **Loan Rate** (`Loan Rate`, `number`, required): Please enter loan rate.
* **Payment Frequency** (`Payment Frequency`, `select_one`, required): Please choose the payment frequency (e.g., monthly, quarterly).
* **Payment Amount** (`Payment Amount`, `number`, required): Please enter payment amount.
* **Loan Date** (`Loan Date`, `date`, required): Please enter loan date.
* **Loan Time** (`Loan Time`, `time`, required): Please enter loan time.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, optional): Please add any additional notes or comments.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): Please enter your phone number.
