# Tax Preparer Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for tax preparers to collect and document information from clients, ensuring a clear understanding of their relationship and the tax return being prepared.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the form completely and accurately.
- Ensure you have permission from your client to collect and use their information.
- Review the form carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tax Preparer Agreement (1)** (`tax_preparer_agreement_form`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief statement agreeing to work with the client and prepare their tax return.
* **Client (2)** (`client`, text, required): Enter the client's name and any relevant contact information.
* **Preparer (3)** (`preparer`, text, required): Enter your name and any relevant contact information.
* **Date (4)** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date you agree to work with the client.
* **Tax Year (5)** (`tax_year`, text, required): Enter the year for which the tax return is being prepared.
* **Tax Return Type (6)** (`tax_return_type`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable tax return types, such as 'Yes' for individual returns or 'No' for business returns.
* **Services (7)** (`services`, text, optional): If necessary, describe the specific services you will provide to the client.
* **Signature (8)** (`signature`, text, optional): If required, sign and date your agreement with the client.

## Tips
- Be mindful of data security and client confidentiality.
- Ensure accuracy and completeness of information collected.
- Review and revise as necessary.
