# customer_bill_pay - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer_bill_pay form is used to collect the customer's contact information for billing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the customer_bill_pay form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's **Customer Name** in the first field.
2. Provide the customer's **Billing Address** in the second field.
3. Enter the customer's email address in the **Email** field.
4. Input the customer's **Phone** number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's name.
  - Please ensure to enter the customer's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
  - This field is not currently required to submit the form, but it's best to ensure accurate records.
- **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's billing address.
  - Use a format suitable for billing purposes, such as street, state, and zip code.
  - This field is not currently required to submit the form, but it's best to ensure accurate records.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the customer's email address.
  - This should match the email address the customer uses for correspondence with your institution.
  - This field is not currently required to submit the form, but it's best to ensure accurate communication.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
  - Use a format suitable for phone numbers, such as XXX-XXX-XXXX or +X-XXX-XXXX.
  - This field is not currently required to submit the form, but it's best to ensure accurate contact information.

## Tips
- If any of the fields are left blank, the form can still be submitted, but please make sure to fill in as much information as possible.
- If you are unsure about any field, please consult your customer support team for assistance.
