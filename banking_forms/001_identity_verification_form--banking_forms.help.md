# Identity Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Identity Verification Form is designed to collect information for identity verification purposes. This form will help us verify the identity of employees and ensure that the data provided is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields. While the form is not mandatory, it's essential to provide as much information as possible to ensure accurate verification.
2. Ensure that the employee's name, address, date of birth, and employment status are accurate and up-to-date.
3. Enter the employee's email and phone number if available.
4. If the employee has an Employee ID, please enter it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Name (`employee_name`, text, optional)
* The employee's full name as it appears on their identification documents.

### Home Address (`address`, text, optional)
* The employee's current home address as it appears on their identification documents.

### Date of Birth (`birth_date`, date, optional)
* The employee's date of birth as it appears on their identification documents.

### Email (`email`, email, optional)
* The employee's email address as it appears on their identification documents or the email they use for official communication.

### Phone Number (`phone`, text, optional)
* The employee's phone number as it appears on their identification documents.

### Employee ID (`employee_id`, number, optional)
* The employee's ID number as assigned by the organization or government agency.

### Employment Status (`employment_status`, select_multiple, optional)
* Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive

### Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date to avoid any discrepancies or errors in the verification process.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities or the organization's HR department for assistance.
