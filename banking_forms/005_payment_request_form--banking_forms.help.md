# Payment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a payment from another party for a specified amount and method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of payment you are requesting (cash, check, or bank transfer).
2. Enter the date of the debt (the date when the payment is due).
3. Enter the amount of the payment.
4. Choose the method of payment (cash, check, or bank transfer).
5. Enter the account number of the payer's account (if applicable).
6. Provide a brief description of the payment request, including any additional information about the payee (e.g. name, address).
7. Enter any additional comments or details about the payment request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Request Form Fields (1)**: Enter a brief description of your payment request.
* **Payment Type (2)**: Select the type of payment you are requesting (cash, check, or bank transfer).
* **Debt Date (3)**: Enter the date when the payment is due.
* **Payment Amount (4)**: Enter the amount of the payment.
* **Payment Method (5)**: Choose the method of payment (cash, check, or bank transfer).
* **Account Number (6)**: Enter the account number of the payer's account (if applicable).
* **Payee Information (7)**: Provide a brief description of the payee, including their name and address (if applicable).
* **Notes (8)**: Enter any additional comments or details about the payment request.

## Tips
* Ensure you understand your responsibilities and obligations regarding payment requests before submitting this form.
* Only submit this form for legitimate payment requests.
* Be prepared to provide additional information to support your payment request if required.
