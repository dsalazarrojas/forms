# credit_application_for_business_account_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The credit application form is used to create a credit application for a business account. This form helps the financial institution to understand the business's details and make an informed decision about the credit application.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form with all required information.
2.  Ensure that you've answered all the questions accurately.
3.  Review the form before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Business Name** (`Business Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your business.
*  **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter the contact number of your business.
*  **Business Address** (`business_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of your business.
*  **Account Holder** (`account_holder`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the account holder.
*  **Account Holder Title** (`title`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the title of the account holder (e.g., Mr, Mrs, Dr).
*  **Business Type** (`business_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of your business (e.g., Limited, Sole, Partnership).
*  **Business Status** (`business_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your business (e.g., Active, Suspended, Closed).
*  **Business Description** (`business_description`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of your business.
*  **Financial Institution** (`financial_institution`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the financial institution where the credit application is being made (e.g., Bank, Credit Union, Savings and Loan).
*  **Account Manager** (`account_manager`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the account manager.
*  **Account Manager Title** (`title`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the title of the account manager (e.g., Mr, Mrs, Dr).
*  **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of your business.
*  **Contact Number 2** (`contact_number_2`, `text`, optional): Enter the secondary contact number of your business.
*  **Business Fax** (`business_fax`, `text`, optional): Enter the fax number of your business.
*  **Business Website** (`business_website`, `text`, optional): Enter the website of your business.
*  **Business Address 2** (`business_address_2`, `text`, optional): Enter the secondary address of your business.
*  **Business Notes** (`business_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes about your business.
*  **Credit Limit** (`credit_limit`, `number`, optional): Enter the credit limit for the business account.
*  **Credit Amount** (`credit_amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the credit amount for the business account.
*  **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment terms for the business account (e.g., 30, Net 30, Cash).
*  **Payment Terms 2** (`payment_terms_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select additional payment terms for the business account (e.g., Net 30, Cash, 60).
*  **Credit Application Date** (`credit_application_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the credit application.
*  **Business License** (`business_license`, `text`, optional): Enter the business license number or ID.
*  **Business License State** (`business_license_state`, `text`, optional): Enter the state where the business license is issued.
*  **Credit Agreed** (`credit_agreed`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you agree to the credit terms (e.g., True, False).
