# credit_application_for_business_account_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create and manage business accounts for business credit applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your account information, including your name, address, and contact details.
2. Select whether you are a business entity or not.
3. Choose the type of business entity you are.
4. Input your credit limit and credit term information.
5. Select your payment term and payment term ID.
6. Choose your annual and monthly fees.
7. Input your minimum renewal fees.
8. Enter your account number.
9. Provide your contact information, including email and phone number.
10. Input your credit score.
11. Select your account status.
12. Input your account status date.
13. Add any additional notes, if necessary.
14. Review your form and submit it to complete your business account application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Account Information (1)**: Enter your account information, including your name, address, contact details, and other relevant details. This information will be used to create or manage your business account.
* **Owner (2)**: Select whether you are a business owner or not. If you are a business owner, select 'Yes'. Otherwise, select 'No'.
* **Business Entity (3)**: Choose the type of business entity you are. This will help us understand your business structure and requirements.
* **Business Entity Type (4)**: Select the type of business entity you are. This will help us understand your business structure and requirements.
* **Business Entity Type ID (5)**: Input your business entity type ID. This is a unique identifier for your business entity.
* **Tax ID Number (6)**: Input your tax ID number. This is used for tax purposes and is required for business accounts.
* **Tax ID Type (7)**: Select whether you have a tax ID or not. If you do, select 'Yes'. Otherwise, select 'No'.
* **Account Type (8)**: Select the type of account you are applying for. This will help us understand your account requirements.
* **Credit Limit (9)**: Input your credit limit. This is the maximum amount of credit you are applying for.
* **Credit Term (10)**: Select the term of your credit. This will help us understand your credit requirements.
* **Payment Term (11)**: Select your payment term. This will help us understand your payment requirements.
* **Payment Term ID (12)**: Input your payment term ID. This is a unique identifier for your payment term.
* **Annual Fee (13)**: Input your annual fee. This is the annual fee associated with your account.
* **Monthly Fee (14)**: Input your monthly fee. This is the monthly fee associated with your account.
* **Minimum Renewal Fees (15)**: Input your minimum renewal fees. This is the minimum amount of fees associated with renewing your account.
* **Account Number (16)**: Enter your account number. This is a unique identifier for your account.
* **Contact Information (17)**: Provide your contact information, including your name, address, email, and phone number.
* **Contact E-mail (18)**: Enter your contact email. This will be used to contact you regarding your account.
* **Contact Phone (19)**: Enter your contact phone number. This will be used to contact you regarding your account.
* **Contact Address (20)**: Enter your contact address. This will be used to contact you regarding your account.
* **Credit Score (21)**: Input your credit score. This will help us understand your creditworthiness.
* **Account Status (22)**: Select the status of your account. This will help us understand your account requirements.
* **Status Date (23)**: Input the date of your account status. This is the date when your account status was last updated.
* **Note (24)**: Add any additional notes regarding your account. This is optional but will help us understand your account requirements.
* **Submit (25)**: Once you have completed all the required fields, click "Submit" to complete your business account application.

Note: Some fields may not be required for all business accounts. Please ensure to fill out the required fields according to your specific requirements.
