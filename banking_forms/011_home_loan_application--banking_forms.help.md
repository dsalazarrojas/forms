# Home Loan Application - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Home Loan Application form is designed to collect information for processing home loan requests. This form helps our institution evaluate and approve loan applications efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

Before starting, ensure all required fields are filled accurately and truthfully. This form is divided into several sections, each with specific fields. Complete each section carefully, and you'll be on your way to getting approved for your home loan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, required: false): Enter your name and other contact details to identify you and contact you regarding the loan process.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): This field is intended for you to provide any additional contact information, such as phone number or email address.
* **Employment** (`employment`, text, required: false): Enter your employment status, whether active or inactive, to help us assess your income and creditworthiness.
* **Income** (`income`, number, required: false): Provide your annual income to help us evaluate your loan eligibility.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, options: Active, Inactive, required: false): Select your employment status to help us assess your creditworthiness.
* **Employment Length** (`employment_length`, number, required: false): Enter the length of time you've been employed to provide more context about your employment status.
* **Annual Income** (`annual_income`, number, required: false): Re-enter your annual income to confirm your financial status.
* **Credit Score** (`credit_score`, number, required: false): Enter your credit score for a more accurate assessment.
* **Credit History** (`credit_history`, text, required: false): Provide any relevant details about your credit history, such as outstanding debts or credit inquiries.
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, required: false): Specify the loan amount you are applying for to help us finalize the loan details.
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, select_one, options: Yes, No, required: false): Select the purpose of the loan, whether for buying a house or other reasons.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, options: Yes, No, required: false): Check if you're applying for a new property or refinancing an existing one.
* **Property Value** (`property_value`, number, required: false): Enter the value of the property you're applying for to help us calculate loan amounts.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required: false): Provide the address of the property you're applying for.
* **Loan Terms** (`loan_terms`, select_multiple, options: Yes, No, required: false): Select the loan terms that apply to your application, such as payment schedule and interest rate.
* **Loan Term** (`loan_term`, select_multiple, options: Yes, No, required: false): Choose the loan term, which is the duration of the loan.
* **Annual Interest Rate** (`annual_interest_rate`, number, required: false): Provide the annual interest rate for the loan.
* **Interest Rate Type** (`interest_rate_type`, select_one, options: Good, Fair, required: false): Choose the interest rate type, either good or fair.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, select_multiple, options: Yes, No, required: false): Select the payment frequency for the loan, such as weekly or monthly payments.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required: false): Enter the payment date for the loan.

Please complete each field accurately and truthfully to ensure the best possible outcome for your loan application.
