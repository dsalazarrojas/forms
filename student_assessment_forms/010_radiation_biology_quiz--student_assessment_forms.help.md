# Radiation Biology Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Radiation Biology Quiz is a form designed to assess students' knowledge on radiation biology. It is intended to gauge their understanding of various aspects of radiation, including radioactivity, radiation dosimetry, and the biological effects of radiation. This quiz is a tool to evaluate students' comprehension and identify areas where they may need further instruction.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Start by reading each question carefully and understanding what is being asked.

### Step 2: For questions that are multiple-choice, select the most appropriate answer from the provided options.

### Step 3: For questions that require a written answer, provide your best response in the provided text field.

### Step 4: Score each question based on your answer. For multiple-choice questions, score 1 if your answer is correct and 0 if not. For written questions, score 1 if your answer is correct and 0 otherwise.
