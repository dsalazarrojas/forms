# High School Student Skills Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The High School Student Skills Assessment Questionnaire is designed to evaluate a student's skills and interests to provide a comprehensive understanding of their strengths and weaknesses. This form will help educators and counselors tailor their guidance and support to better serve the students' needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please select the relevant pages and fill in the required fields for each page. You can refer to the field-by-field explanation below for more details on each field.
- Be as accurate as possible when answering each question.
- Don't hesitate to ask for help or clarification if you're unsure about any part of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Information** (`student_info`, `text`, required/optional): This section is meant for students to enter their basic details like their name and student ID. Please fill this section to provide your personal details.
- **Academic Readiness** (`academic_readiness`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for students to express their academic readiness. Be honest about your current academic standing, and describe your strengths and weaknesses.
- **Career Readiness** (`career_readiness`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your career aspirations and interests in this section. Be specific about your desired profession or industry.
- **Career Interests** (`career_interests`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Choose from the options provided to indicate your career interests. Select all that apply.
  - Computers
  - Engineering
  - Arts
  - Sciences
  - Business
  - Social Science
- **Academic Background** (`academic_background`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Indicate your current academic background. Select all that apply.
  - 9th to 10th grade
  - 11th grade
  - 12th grade
- **Academic Goals** (`academic_goals`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Select your long-term academic goals.
  - College
  - University
  - Community College
  - Vocational school
- **Skills 1-10** (`skills_1`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for you to describe your skills and strengths. Be specific and brief.
- **Additional Skills** (`additional_skills`, `text`, required/optional): List any other skills or qualifications you have not mentioned earlier.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required/optional): If you have anything you'd like to add or share, use this section.
- **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, `text`, required/optional): Please sign this section to indicate that you've completed the form accurately.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer each question truthfully and to the best of your abilities.
- If you're unsure about any question, consult with your teacher or counselor for guidance.
- The form's structure and content may be subject to change as per your institution's policies and preferences.
