# Node.js Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess a student's knowledge, skills, and experience in Node.js, including their problem-solving skills, technical skills, work experience, and personal qualities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and accurately, based on your knowledge and experience.
2. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
3. If you have any comments or feedback, please provide them at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Knowledge** (Text, Optional): This question is designed to test your general knowledge in Node.js, including your understanding of its fundamentals, syntax, and ecosystem.
* **Problem Solving Skills** (Text, Optional): Describe how you approach problems and how you would solve a Node.js-related problem.
* **Node Js Skills** (Select Multiple, Required): Select all that apply from Node.js fundamentals, Microservice architecture, and Database modeling.
* **Confidence Levels** (Select One, Required): Choose your confidence level in Node.js, from High, Medium, to Low.
* **Node.js Experience** (Number, Optional): Enter your experience level in Node.js, from 0 to a large number.
* **Relevant Work Experience** (Text, Optional): Describe your relevant work experience, including any projects or work related to Node.js.
* **Technical Skills** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all that apply from JavaScript, HTML, CSS, and Python.
* **Education Background** (Text, Optional): Describe your educational background, including any courses or degrees related to Node.js.
* **Certifications and Licenses** (Text, Optional): List any certifications or licenses you have acquired in Node.js or related fields.
* **Personal Project Experience** (Text, Optional): Describe your personal project experience, including any projects you have completed in Node.js.
* **Work Experience** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all that apply from Software Development, IT, Project Management, and Data Science.
* **Technical Insights** (Text, Optional): Provide any technical insights or knowledge you have gained from your experience with Node.js.
* **Communication Skills** (Select One, Optional): Choose your level of communication skills, from Excellent, Good, to Poor.
* **Team Management** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all that apply from Team lead, Team member, Project lead, and Project manager.
* **Adaptability** (Select One, Optional): Choose your level of adaptability, from Excellent, Good, to Poor.
* **Creativity** (Select One, Optional): Choose your level of creativity, from Excellent, Good, to Poor.
* **Attention to detail** (Select One, Optional): Choose your level of attention to detail, from Excellent, Good, to Poor.
* **Problem Solving Speed** (Select One, Optional): Choose your level of problem-solving speed, from Excellent, Good, to Poor.
* **Work Environment** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all that apply from Office, Home office, Virtual, and Remote.
* **Work Hours** (Number, Optional): Enter your typical work hours per week.
* **Work Breaks** (Number, Optional): Enter your typical work breaks per day.
* **Salary Range** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all that apply from Low, Medium, and High.
* **Job Satisfaction** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all that apply from High, Medium, and Low.
* **Feedback** (Text, Optional): Provide any feedback on how to improve the form or questions.
* **Comments** (Text, Optional): Provide any comments or thoughts on the form or assessment process.
