# teacher_aide_conduct_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The teacher aide conduct assessment form is designed to help teachers evaluate and provide feedback to students on their overall performance, strengths, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
*   Review each page carefully, as some fields are required and others are optional.
*   Be honest and objective in your assessment, as this will help the student receive a fair and accurate evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Overall Performance** (`required`, `number`): This field is used to rate the student's overall performance. Please use a number between 1 and 10 to indicate your assessment.
*   **Conduct** (`required`, `number`): This field is used to rate the student's conduct. Please use a number between 1 and 10 to indicate your assessment.
*   **Strengths** (`optional`, `select_one`): List any strengths you have observed in the student, using the options provided.
*   **Areas for Improvement** (`required`, `select_multiple`): List any areas where the student needs improvement, using the options provided.
*   **Overall Performance Rating** (`optional`, `number`): This field is optional and allows you to rate the student's overall performance on a scale of 1-10.
*   **Teacher Comments** (`optional`, `text`): Use this field to provide any additional comments or feedback you have for the student.
*   **Student Comments** (`optional`, `text`): This field is for the student to provide any comments or feedback to the teacher.
*   **Date Completed** (`required`, `date`): This field is used to record the date the assessment was completed.
*   **Date Started** (`required`, `date`): This field is used to record the date the assessment was started.
*   **Date Due Date** (`required`, `date`): This field is used to record the due date for the assessment.
*   **Date Return** (`optional`, `date`): This field is optional and allows you to record the date the assessment was returned.
*   **Form Version** (`required`, `select_multiple`): This field is used to select which version of the form is being completed.
*   **User Rating** (`optional`, `text`): This field is optional and allows you to provide your personal rating or comments about the form.
*   **Teacher Name** (`required`, `text`): This field is used to record the name of the teacher completing the form.
*   **Student Name** (`required`, `text`): This field is used to record the name of the student being assessed.
*   **Student ID** (`required`, `number`): This field is used to record the student's ID number.
*   **Class Name** (`required`, `text`): This field is used to record the name of the class the student is in.
*   **School Name** (`required`, `text`): This field is used to record the name of the school the student attends.
*   **Teacher Email** (`required`, `email`): This field is used to record the email address of the teacher completing the form.
