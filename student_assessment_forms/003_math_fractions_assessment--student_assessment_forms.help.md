# Math Fractions Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Math Fractions Assessment is a form designed to evaluate students' understanding of fractions, specifically their concept of equivalent fractions, operations, and their relationship with each other. This assessment aims to determine students' knowledge and skills in understanding fractions and apply them in various mathematical operations.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Read and understand the introduction page, which will provide a brief background on fractions.
2. Answer the question "What is a fraction?" and provide a short description of your understanding.
3. Indicate whether you are familiar with equivalent fractions or not.
4. Identify which fractions are equivalent to others, selecting all that apply.
5. Describe your understanding of fraction operations (addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division).
6. Select the types of equivalent fractions operations you are familiar with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`intro_page`, `text`, required: false): This is the introductory page, providing background information on fractions. Read it carefully to understand the concept and its importance.
* **What is a fraction?** (`what_is_a_fraction`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a short description of your understanding of what a fraction is.
* **Equivalent Fractions** (`equivalent_fractions`, `select_one`, required: false): Please choose whether you are familiar with equivalent fractions or not. If you are not sure, you can choose the 'No' option.
* **What are Equivalent Fractions?** (`what_are_equivalent_fractions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the types of equivalent fractions you know, as per the options provided.
* **Fraction Operations** (`fraction_operation`, `text`, required: false): Please describe your understanding of fraction operations (addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division).
* **Equivalent Fractions Operations** (`equivalent_fractions_operation`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the types of equivalent fractions operations you are familiar with.
## Tips
* Please answer each question truthfully and to the best of your abilities.
* If you are unsure of any question, select the option that best reflects your current knowledge.
* Take your time to review each question carefully before proceeding to the next.
