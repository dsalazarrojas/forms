# Physics Exam Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide a quick and efficient way for students to assess their readiness for a physics exam. It helps teachers to gather information on the student's confirmation of exam readiness, materials reviewed, rules acknowledged, special requirements reviewed, physics exam preparation checklist, physics exam instructions, teacher comments, teacher name, school name, department, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Confirm whether you are ready for the exam by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Confirm Exam Readiness" field.
2. Check the box next to each relevant item in the "Materials Reviewed" field to indicate which materials you have reviewed.
3. Choose "Yes" or "No" for the "Rules Acknowledged" field.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" for each item in the "Special Requirements Reviewed" field.
5. Enter a brief description of your exam preparation in the "Physics Exam Preparation Checklist" field.
6. Read and write any necessary notes in the "Physics Exam Instructions" field.
7. Add any comments for the teacher in the "Teacher Comments" field.
8. Enter your name in the "Teacher Name" field.
9. Enter your school's name in the "School Name" field.
10. Enter your department's name in the "Department" field.
11. Enter your school's name in the "School" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Confirm Exam Readiness** (`confirm_exam_readiness`, select_one, required: false): Check if you are ready for the exam by selecting "Yes" or "No". This will help the teacher assess your preparation.
* **Materials Reviewed** (`materials_reviewed`, select_multiple, required: false): Check the boxes of the materials you have reviewed for the exam.
* **Rules Acknowledged** (`rules_acknowledged`, select_one, required: false): Confirm if you have read and understood the rules.
* **Special Requirements Reviewed** (`special_requirements_reviewed`, select_multiple, required: false): Check the boxes of the special requirements you have reviewed.
* **Physics Exam Preparation Checklist** (`physics_exam_preparation_checklist`, text, required: false): Write a brief description of your exam preparation.
* **Physics Exam Instructions** (`physics_exam_instructions`, text, required: false): Read and write any necessary notes for the exam.
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, text, required: false): Add any comments for the teacher.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required: false): Enter your school's name.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): Enter your department's name.
* **School** (`school`, text, required: false): Re-enter your school's name (this may seem repetitive, but it's a required field in the input schema).
