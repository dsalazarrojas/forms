# STEAM Learning Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The STEAM Learning Assessment form is used to gauge a student's interests and confidence levels in various STEAM fields, including Science, Technology, Art, and Music.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant options for each field. You can choose from "Yes" or "No" for the Student Interest and Experience fields, and enter a number between 0 and 100 for the confidence fields. You do not need to fill out all fields, but filling them out will help provide a more accurate assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Interest** (`student_interest`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all the STEAM fields that you are interested in.
* **Math Confidence** (`math_confidence`, number, required: false): Rate your confidence level in math on a scale from 0 to 100, where 0 means "not confident at all" and 100 means "very confident".
* **Science Confidence** (`science_confidence`, number, required: false): Rate your confidence level in science on a scale from 0 to 100, where 0 means "not confident at all" and 100 means "very confident".
* **Tech Confidence** (`tech_confidence`, number, required: false): Rate your confidence level in technology on a scale from 0 to 100, where 0 means "not confident at all" and 100 means "very confident".
* **Art Confidence** (`art_confidence`, number, required: false): Rate your confidence level in art on a scale from 0 to 100, where 0 means "not confident at all" and 100 means "very confident".
* **Music Confidence** (`music_confidence`, number, required: false): Rate your confidence level in music on a scale from 0 to 100, where 0 means "not confident at all" and 100 means "very confident".
* **Experience 1** (`experience_1`, select_one, required: false): Have you had any experience in one of the STEAM fields mentioned above? If so, select "Yes".
* **Experience 2** (`experience_2`, select_one, required: false): Have you had any other experience in one of the STEAM fields mentioned above? If so, select "Yes".

## Tips
* Fill out all fields that apply to you.
* Rate your confidence levels honestly, as they will be used for assessment purposes.
* If you are unsure about a field, try to rate it based on how you think you would perform if you were to work on a related task.
