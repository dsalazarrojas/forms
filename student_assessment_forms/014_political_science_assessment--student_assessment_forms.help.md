# Political Science Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about political science topics. It is not a mandatory form, so you can fill it in as much or as little as you need to answer the questions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the fields as needed. You can skip any field that does not apply to your situation. The form is divided into several pages, but each page only has one field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Political Science Assessment Form 1**: This is a text field where you can provide your thoughts or answers to the political science question on Page 1.
* **Political Science**: This select_one field allows you to choose one of the provided options related to political science.
* **Political Science 2**: This select_multiple field allows you to choose multiple options related to political science.
* **Political Science 3**: This number field is meant to collect a numerical value related to political science.
* **Political Science 4**: This is another number field for collecting a numerical value.
* **Political Science 5**: This date field is used to select a date related to political science.
* **Political Science 6**: This time field is used to select a time of day related to political science.
* **Political Science 7**: This note field allows you to provide additional information about political science.
* **Political Science 8**: This email field is for collecting an email address related to political science.
* (The following fields are repeated with no specific purpose)

Please note that most fields are simply labeled as "Political Science" without any additional description. If you have any questions about a specific field, you can contact the form's creator or an expert in the field.
