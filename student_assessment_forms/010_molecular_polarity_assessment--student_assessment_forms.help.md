# Molecular Polarity Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Molecular Polarity Assessment form is used to assess the polarity of molecules and gather information on various fields such as student's name, email, phone number, and submission time. It's essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that the submitted data is valid and useful for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Molecular Polarity Assessment form, follow these steps:

* Select the type of molecule you are assessing (molecular_polarity_assessment)
* Select the type of nonpolar molecules you are dealing with (non_polar_molecules)
* Select the type of polar molecules you are dealing with (polar_molecules)
* Enter your name (student_name)
* Enter your student ID number (student_id)
* Check if you have completed the form (form_completed)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Molecular Polarity Assessment** (molecular_polarity_assessment): Select the type of molecule you are assessing.
* **Nonpolar Molecules** (non_polar_molecules): Select all nonpolar molecules that apply to your case.
* **Polar Molecules** (polar_molecules): Select all polar molecules that apply to your case.
* **First Answer** (first_answer): This field is currently empty and not being used in the form. Please disregard.
* **Second Answer** (second_answer): This field allows you to choose between three options (option one, option two, and option three). Please select one of these options.
* **Nonpolar Molecule Answer** (non_polar_molecule_answer): Select all nonpolar molecules that apply to your case.
* **Polar Molecule Answer** (polar_molecule_answer): Select all polar molecules that apply to your case.
* **Student Name** (student_name): Enter your name.
* **Student ID** (student_id): Enter your student ID number.
* **Teacher Comments** (teacher_comments): This field is not required. You can enter any comments or information you want to share with the teacher.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (phone): Enter your phone number.
* **Student Email** (student_email): This field is not required. You can enter your email address if you want to.
* **Student Phone** (student_phone): Enter your phone number.
* **Teacher Name** (teacher_name): This field is not required. You can enter the teacher's name if you want to.
* **Teacher Email** (teacher_email): This field is not required. You can enter the teacher's email address if you want to.
* **Teacher Phone** (teacher_phone): This field is not required. You can enter the teacher's phone number if you want to.
* **Class Name** (class_name): This field is not required. You can enter the class name if you want to.
* **Class Number** (class_number): This field is not required. You can enter the class number if you want to.
* **Submission Date** (submission_date): Enter the date you are submitting the form.
* **Submission Time** (submission_time): Enter the time you are submitting the form.
* **Submission Hour** (submission_hour): Enter the hour you are submitting the form.
* **Submission Minute** (submission_minute): Enter the minute you are submitting the form.
* **Submission Second** (submission_second): Enter the second you are submitting the form.
* **Form Completed** (form_completed): Select whether you have completed the form.
