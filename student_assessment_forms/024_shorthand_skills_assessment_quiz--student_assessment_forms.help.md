# shorthand_skills_assessment_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to assess an individual's shorthand skills, specifically their proficiency in various speeds of shorthand writing, such as 25, 40, 50, 75, 100, and 150 words per minute.

## How To Complete This Form

* Read each field carefully and answer the questions honestly. You can skip questions that do not apply to your skills.
* Enter your answers in the corresponding boxes provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title** (`title`, `text`, **required**): This is the title of the assessment. Please enter your name or title for this evaluation.
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, **optional**): This is an introduction or explanation for the assessment. Please provide any background information that may be helpful for the evaluator.
* **Shorthand Proficiency** (`shorthand_proficiency`, `number`, **optional**): Rate your own shorthand proficiency in wpm (words per minute).
* **Transcription Ability** (`transcription_ability`, `number`, **optional**): Rate your own ability to transcribe shorthand in wpm (words per minute).
* **Real World Usage** (`real_world_usage`, `select_one`, **optional**): Indicate how often you use shorthand in real-world situations. Choose one of the options: shorthand in 50 wpm, shorthand in 75 wpm, shorthand in 100 wpm, shorthand in 150 wpm.
* **Shorthand in 25 wpm** (`shorthand_in_25_wpm`, `number`, **optional**): Rate your proficiency in 25 wpm shorthand.
* **Shorthand in 40 wpm** (`shorthand_in_40_wpm`, `number`, **optional**): Rate your proficiency in 40 wpm shorthand.
* **Shorthand in 50 wpm** (`shorthand_in_50_wpm`, `number`, **optional**): Rate your proficiency in 50 wpm shorthand.
* **Shorthand in 75 wpm** (`shorthand_in_75_wpm`, `number`, **optional**): Rate your proficiency in 75 wpm shorthand.
* **Shorthand in 100 wpm** (`shorthand_in_100_wpm`, `number`, **optional**): Rate your proficiency in 100 wpm shorthand.
* **Shorthand in 150 wpm** (`shorthand_in_150_wpm`, `number`, **optional**): Rate your proficiency in 150 wpm shorthand.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate with your ratings. This assessment is for evaluation purposes only.
* If you have any difficulty or questions, contact the evaluator for assistance.
