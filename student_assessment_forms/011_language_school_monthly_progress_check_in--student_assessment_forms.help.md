# Language School Monthly Progress Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for language school staff to track student progress on a monthly basis. It collects information on student's current status, program participation, and submission status, enabling staff to monitor and address any issues promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's ID to identify the student.
2. Describe the student's current progress in the "student_progress" field.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the student is currently participating in a program.
4. Choose multiple programs the student is participating in using the "program_type" field.
5. Enter the date of the progress check-in.
6. Indicate if the submission is submitted by the student or staff using the "submitted_by" field.
7. Enter the date of submission.
8. Enter the time of submission.
9. Add any comments regarding the student's progress.
10. Enter the student's email address and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID (student_id)**: Enter the student's unique ID number.
* **Student Progress (student_progress)**: Describe the student's current progress, including any achievements or challenges faced.
* **Program Participation (program_id)**: Indicate if the student is currently participating in a program. Select "Yes" for yes, "No" for no.
* **Program Type (program_type)**: Select multiple programs the student is participating in.
* **Date (date)**: Enter the date of the progress check-in.
* **Submitted By (submitted_by)**: Indicate who submitted the progress check-in (Student or Staff). Select "Yes" for submitted by student, "No" for submitted by staff.
* **Submission Date (submission_date)**: Enter the date the progress check-in was submitted.
* **Submission Time (submitted_at)**: Enter the time of submission.
* **Comments (comments)**: Add any comments or notes regarding the student's progress.
* **Email (email)**: Enter the student's email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter the student's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Assigned Tool (assigned_tool)**: Indicate if the student is using a specific tool or resource. Select "Yes" for yes, "No" for no.
