# Graphing Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graphing Skills Assessment form is designed to evaluate students' graphing skills, which is a crucial part of mathematics and problem-solving. This form helps to assess students' current proficiency level, challenges they face, and their rating of graphing skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student name in the "Enter Student Name" field.
2. Enter your student email in the "Student Email" field.
3. Select your graphing skill level from the options: Graphing, Calculus, Algebra, Geometry, or Trigonometry.
4. Choose the tools you used for graphing from the available options: Graphing Calculator, Graphing Software, Online Graphing Tools, or None.
5. Describe any challenges you faced while graphing in the "What challenges faced" field.
6. Choose your current proficiency level in graphing from the options: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert.
7. Rate your graphing skills from 1 to 10.
8. Provide any additional comments about your graphing skills or experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Student Name**: This field is used to record the student's name. Please enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you would like it to be known.
* **Student Email**: Enter your student email address.
* **Graphing Skill**: This field asks about your proficiency in graphing. Please select the area of graphing you are most proficient in.
* **Which tool(s) used**: If you have used any tools for graphing, select all that apply. This can include graphing calculators, software, or online tools.
* **What challenges faced**: If you faced any difficulties while graphing, describe them here. This can include issues with specific tools, concepts, or processes.
* **What is their current proficiency level**: Choose your current level of skill in graphing from the options provided.
* **Rating of Graphing Skills**: Rate your graphing skills from 1 to 10. This is a subjective measure of your confidence in your graphing abilities.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your graphing skills or experience.
