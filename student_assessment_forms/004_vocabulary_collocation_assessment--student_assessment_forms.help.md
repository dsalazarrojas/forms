# Vocabulary Collocation Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the vocabulary collocations presented to learners. Please complete the form to provide your assessment of the collocations.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form in the following steps:
  1. Read through the collocation carefully and make sure you understand its meaning and context.
  2. For each field, provide a response based on your understanding of the collocation. You can type in the space provided or choose a number that best describes your response.
  3. When you are finished, click "Submit" to send your response to the system.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **learner_response**: This is a text field where you can provide any notes, comments, or observations about the collocation. This field is optional.
* **teacher_comments**: This field is for you to add any comments about the collocation that you think the teacher should see. This field is optional.
* **collocation_correctness**: This field is where you rate how correct or incorrect you think the collocation is. Choose a number from 1 to 5 to indicate the level of correctness.
* **collocation_usefulness**: This field is where you rate the usefulness of the collocation in a practical context. Choose a number from 1 to 5 to indicate the level of usefulness.
* **collocation_frequency**: This field is where you indicate how often you have seen or heard the collocation in your experience or in the materials you have studied. Choose a number from 1 to 5 to indicate the frequency of use.
* **collocation_familiarity**: This field is where you rate your level of familiarity with the collocation. Choose a number from 1 to 5 to indicate the level of familiarity.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide a clear and concise response for each field.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification or assistance from the teacher.
- Remember to be honest and objective in your assessment, and provide constructive feedback that will help refine the vocabulary collocation materials.
