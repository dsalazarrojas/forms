# Learning Progress Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Learning Progress Assessment" form is a tool used to track and document the progress of students in various aspects, including learning progress, attendance, and performance. It helps teachers and parents to monitor the student's advancement, identify areas that need improvement, and make informed decisions about their education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant page for the assessment.
2. Fill in the required fields, such as Subject Area, Assessment Date, and Student Name.
3. Provide information on the student's current progress, attendance, and performance.
4. Add any necessary comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First page:**
	+ **Subject Area** (`subject_area`, text, optional): Enter the subject area of the assessment, e.g., math, reading, or science.
	+ **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Student Information** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's name.
	+ **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, number, optional): Enter the student's grade level.
	+ **Student Score** (`student_score`, number, optional): Enter the student's score or progress level.
* **Contact Information**:
	+ **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, optional): Enter the parent's email address.
	+ **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, optional): Enter the teacher's email address.
	+ **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter the student's phone number.
	+ **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, text, optional): Enter the teacher's phone number.
* **Assessment Status** (`assessment_status`, select_one, optional): Choose from:
	+ Completed
	+ Ongoing
	+ In Progress
* **Assessment Comments** (`assessment_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or feedback about the assessment.
* **Parent Comments** (`parent_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or feedback from the parent.
* **Student Attendance** (`student_attendance`, select_multiple, optional): Choose from:
	+ Regular
	+ Absent
	+ Late
	+ Unexcused Absence
* **Student Performance** (`student_performance`, select_multiple, optional): Choose from:
	+ Excellent
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Needs Improvement
	+ Unsatisfactory
* **Student Progress** (`student_progress`, select_multiple, optional): Choose from:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Parent Attendance** (`parent_progress`, select_multiple, optional): Choose from:
	+ Regular
	+ Absent
	+ Late
	+ Unexcused Absence
* **Parent Progress** (`parent_progress`, select_multiple, optional): Choose from:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* Review and update the form regularly to reflect changes in the student's progress.
* Use this form to track and document the student's progress throughout the academic year.
* Use the assessment status and comments to make informed decisions about the student's education.
