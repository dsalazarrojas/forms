# Instrument Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Instrument Knowledge Assessment form is designed to evaluate an individual's knowledge and skills related to musical instruments and music theory.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through each section and fill in the required information as prompted. Please ensure that you answer each question carefully and accurately, as the form is designed to provide a comprehensive assessment of your knowledge and skills.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Instrument Experience** (`instrument_experience`, text, optional): This section is designed to capture your experience with musical instruments. Describe your experience with various instruments, including the types of instruments you play and for how long you have been playing.
* **Theory Knowledge** (`theory_knowledge`, text, optional): This section is intended to assess your knowledge of music theory. Please provide information on the basics of music theory, such as chord progressions, scales, and key signatures.
* **Music Reading** (`music_reading`, text, optional): This section is focused on your ability to read music. Describe your skills and experience in reading sheet music, tablature, or other music notation systems.
* **Ear Training** (`ear_training`, text, optional): This section is designed to evaluate your ability to recognize and reproduce musical melodies, harmonies, and other musical elements through ear training exercises.
* **Technique Training** (`technique_training`, text, optional): This section is intended to assess your skills in various musical techniques, such as playing specific instruments, singing, or other music-related skills.
* **Practice Time** (`practice_time`, number, optional): This section is designed to gauge your regular practice time. Please provide the average number of hours you spend practicing each week.
* **Music Theory** (`music_theory`, text, optional): This section is focused on your knowledge and application of music theory concepts, such as chord progressions, scales, and key signatures.
* **Performance Skills** (`performance_skills`, text, optional): This section is intended to evaluate your performance skills, including your ability to play musical pieces, improvise, or perform in ensembles.
* **Music Reading Skills** (`music_reading_skills`, text, optional): This section is designed to assess your skills in reading and applying music notation, tablature, or other musical notation systems.
* **Practice Performance** (`practice_performance`, text, optional): This section is intended to evaluate your ability to perform musical pieces with proper technique and expression.

## Tips
Remember to answer each question carefully and accurately, as this form is designed to provide a comprehensive assessment of your knowledge and skills. If you are unsure about any question, please feel free to ask for clarification or guidance.
