# Student Agency And Empowerment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Agency And Empowerment Survey aims to assess the degree of agency and empowerment experienced by students in a school setting. This survey is designed to evaluate the level of autonomy and support provided to students, allowing stakeholders to identify areas for improvement and develop targeted strategies to enhance student agency.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Take a few minutes to review each question carefully.
2. Answer each question based on your personal experience and observations within the school setting.
3. Select all that apply or type in your response.
4. Submit your response to complete the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Feedback** (`student_feedback`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your overall impression of the student experience in the classroom or school.
* **Teacher Support** (`teacher_support`, select_one, optional): How do you feel about the level of support provided by teachers in the classroom?
	+ 'Yes': Teachers are highly supportive and responsive to student needs.
	+ 'No': Teachers are not very supportive or responsive.
* **Classroom Voice** (`voice`, text, optional): Can students express their thoughts and ideas freely in the classroom?
* **Classroom Choice** (`choice`, select_multiple, optional): How much freedom do students have to make choices about their own learning?
	+ 'Yes': Students have a lot of freedom to make choices.
	+ 'No': Students have very little freedom to make choices.
* **Student Confidence** (`confidence`, text, optional): How confident do you feel students are in their abilities and decisions?
* **School Culture** (`school_culture`, text, optional): Can students feel welcome and supported in the school culture?
* **Classroom Environment** (`classroom_environment`, select_multiple, optional): Can students feel free to learn and make mistakes in the classroom?
	+ 'Yes': Students feel free to learn and make mistakes.
	+ 'No': Students do not feel free to learn and make mistakes.
* **Teacher Training** (`teacher_training`, select_one, optional): Do teachers receive training on supporting student agency and empowerment?
	+ 'Yes': Teachers receive regular training on supporting student agency and empowerment.
	+ 'No': Teachers do not receive regular training on supporting student agency and empowerment.
* **School Resources** (`school_resources`, text, optional): What resources are available to support student agency and empowerment in the school?
* **Student Services** (`student_services`, text, optional): What services are available to support students in developing their agency and empowerment?
* **Parent Involvement** (`parent_involvement`, text, optional): How involved are parents in supporting student agency and empowerment?
* **Classroom Voice** (`voice`, text, optional): Can students express their thoughts and ideas freely in the classroom?
