# Equation Solving Worksheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equation Solving Worksheet form is designed to collect information from students about their equation solving work and allow teachers to review and provide feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **Student Name** field with your name.
2. In the **Equation to Solve** field, enter the equation you were trying to solve.
3. In the **Solve Equation** field, explain your thought process or solution.
4. If applicable, upload your work in the **Work Upload** field.
5. Add any additional comments in the **Additional Comments** field.
6. Input your Student ID in the **Student ID** field.
7. Select your class in the **Student Class** field.
8. Input your teacher's name in the **Teacher Name** field.
9. Input your teacher's email address in the **Teacher Email** field.
10. Input your school name in the **School Name** field.
11. Input your school name in the **Teacher School** field.
12. Input your student's grade level in the **Student Grade** field.
13. Click the **Submit** button to complete the form.

* **Equation Solving Worksheet** (`equation_solving_worksheet`, text, optional): This is a text area where you can provide your work in solving the equation.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): This field is for you to input your name.
* **Equation to Solve** (`equation_to_solve`, text, optional): This field is for you to specify the equation you were trying to solve.
* **Solve Equation** (`solve_equation`, text, optional): This field is for you to explain your thought process or solution to the given equation.
