# Equation Solving Worksheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equation Solving Worksheet form is designed to collect information from students about their equation solving work and allow teachers to review and provide feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **Student Name** field with your name.
2. In the **Equation to Solve** field, enter the equation you were trying to solve.
3. In the **Solve Equation** field, explain your thought process or solution.
4. If applicable, upload your work in the **Work Upload** field.
5. Add any additional comments in the **Additional Comments** field.
6. Input your Student ID in the **Student ID** field.
7. Select your class in the **Student Class** field.
8. Input your teacher's name in the **Teacher Name** field.
9. Input your teacher's email address in the **Teacher Email** field.
10. Input your school name in the **School Name** field.
11. Input your school name in the **Teacher School** field.
12. Input your student's grade level in the **Student Grade** field.
13. Click the **Submit** button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equation Solving Worksheet** (`equation_solving_worksheet`, text, optional): This is a text area where you can provide your work in solving the equation.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): This field is for you to input your name.
* **Equation to Solve** (`equation_to_solve`, text, optional): This field is for you to specify the equation you were trying to solve.
* **Solve Equation** (`solve_equation`, text, optional): This field is for you to explain your thought process or solution to the given equation.
* **Work Upload** (`work_upload`, text, optional): This field allows you to upload your work related to the equation.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required): This field is for you to input your ID number.
* **Student Class** (`student_class`, text, optional): This field is for you to specify your class.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): This field is for you to input your teacher's name.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, optional): This is your teacher's email address.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): This field is for you to input your school name.
* **Teacher School** (`teacher_school`, text, optional): This field is for you to input your school name.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, number, optional): This field is for you to input your grade level.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): This field is for you to submit your work for review.
* **Student Subject** (`student_subject`, text, optional): This field is for you to input your subject area.
* **Work URL** (`work_url`, text, optional): This field is for you to input a link to your work.
* **Work File Name** (`work_file_name`, text, optional): This field is for you to input a name for your work file.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, optional): This field is for you to input the date of submission.
* **Time Spent** (`time_spent`, time, optional): This field is for you to input the time spent on the work.
* **Teacher Subject** (`teacher_subject`, text, optional): This field is for you to input your subject area.
* **Teacher Email Hint** (`teacher_email_hint`, text, optional): This field is for you to input a hint about your email address.
* **Teacher School Hint** (`teacher_school_hint`, text, optional): This field is for you to input a hint about your school.
* **Teacher Class Hint** (`teacher_class_hint`, text, optional): This field is for you to input a hint about your class.
* **Student School Hint** (`student_school_hint`, text, optional): This field is for you to input a hint about your school.
