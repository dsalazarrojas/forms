# Student Led Education Reform Project Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Led Education Reform Project Application Form is a tool designed to collect information from students about their project proposals. This form helps project administrators track and review proposals efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely, ensuring all fields are accurate and relevant to your project proposal.
- Select the correct category for your project proposal.
- Enter a brief description of your project proposal.
- Enter your project proposal title.
- Describe your project goals.
- Describe your expected project outcomes.
- Enter your project start and end dates.
- Enter your project location.
- Enter your project funding information.
- Enter the number of students, teachers, or other individuals involved in your project.
- Enter the duration of your project.
- Enter your name and submission date.
- Enter any assigned tools or tools you would like to be assigned.
- Enter any additional form IDs that are relevant to your project.
