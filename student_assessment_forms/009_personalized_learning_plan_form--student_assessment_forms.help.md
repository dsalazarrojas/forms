# Personalized Learning Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personalized Learning Plan Form is used to collect information about a student's learning preferences, goals, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Student Goals**: Describe your short-term and long-term goals for your learning journey.
2. Choose your **Learning Styles**: Select the learning style that best suits you from the options provided. You can choose one or multiple options.
3. **Skill Levels**: Enter your current skill level for each of the skills listed. This will help us understand your strengths and weaknesses.
4. **Availability**: If you have any conflicts or limitations that may affect your availability, please enter the dates when you are available to learn.
5. **Drag and Drop**: If you have any specific requirements or expectations for your learning environment, please select them from the options provided.
6. **Submission**: Choose how you prefer to submit your work, either Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
7. **Preferred Learning Style 1-4**: Choose your preferred learning style for each of the options provided.
