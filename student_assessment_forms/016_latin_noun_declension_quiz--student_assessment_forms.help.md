# Latin Noun Declension Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Latin Noun Declension Quiz form is designed to assess the Latin Noun Declension skills of students. It aims to evaluate their knowledge of Latin noun cases, endings, and meanings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Student Name
	* Student Email
2. Choose the correct answers to the quiz questions.
3. Provide any additional comments or remarks in the Submission Comments section.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name** (Student Name, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
- **Student Email** (Student Email, email, required): Enter your email address that you check regularly.
- **Latin Noun Declension Quiz** (Latin Noun Declension Quiz, text, optional): This field is not required but can be used for additional comments or questions.
- **Noun Cases 1** (Noun Cases 1, select_one, optional): Choose the correct case for the Latin noun (Masculine, Feminine, Neuter).
- **Noun Cases 2** (Noun Cases 2, select_multiple, optional): Select all the correct cases for the Latin noun (1st, 2nd, 3rd).
- **Noun Endings 1** (Noun Endings 1, select_one, optional): Choose the correct ending for the Latin noun (-a, -um, -us).
- **Noun Endings 2** (Noun Endings 2, select_multiple, optional): Select all the correct endings for the Latin noun (-a, -um, -is).
- **Noun Meanings 1** (Noun Meanings 1, select_one, optional): Choose the correct meaning for the Latin noun (Masculine, Feminine, Neuter).
- **Quiz Questions 2** (Quiz Questions 2, text, optional): This field is not required but can be used for additional comments or questions.
- **Submission Comments** (Submission Comments, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or remarks about your submission.

## Tips
* Please answer all the questions honestly and accurately.
* Use the correct format for your answers.
* If you have any questions or doubts, don't hesitate to ask.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting.
