# Ionization And Electronegativity Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ionization And Electronegativity Assessment form is used to collect information on students' ionization and electronegativity trends, periods, frequencies, and additional contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure that you are filling it out for the correct purpose.
2. For each field, provide the requested information accurately and thoroughly.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the relevant authorities for clarification.

* **Ionization And Electronegativity Assessment Frequency** (`ionization_and_electronegativity_assessment_frequency`, time, required/optional): In this field, please enter the frequency of your assessment. This field is intended for numerical input, such as "daily", "weekly", or "monthly".
* **Ionization And Electronegativity Assessment Note** (`ionization_and_electronegativity_assessment_note`, note, required/optional): This field is for any additional comments or notes related to your assessment.
