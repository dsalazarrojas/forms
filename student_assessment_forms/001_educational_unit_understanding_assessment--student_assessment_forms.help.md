# Educational Unit Understanding Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help educators evaluate the effectiveness of an educational unit and gather feedback from students about their learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant answers for each lesson and topic based on your understanding.
2. Provide feedback about your learning experience for each lesson.
3. Ensure you have answered all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lesson 1** (`lesson_1`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to provide a brief summary or reflection about Lesson 1. Please type in your thoughts, insights, or comments about the lesson.
* **Unit Topics 1** (`unit_topics_1`, select_one, required/optional): Choose one option that best describes your understanding of the unit topics covered in Lesson 1.
* **Student Feedback 1** (`student_feedback_1`, text, required/optional): Share your feedback about Lesson 1, including any positive or negative experiences you had during the lesson.
* **Lesson 2** (`lesson_2`, date, required/optional): If you have a specific date in mind for Lesson 2, please enter it here. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Student Feedback 2** (`student_feedback_2`, text, required/optional): Provide feedback about Lesson 2, including any thoughts, insights, or suggestions you have about the lesson.
* **Unit Topics 2** (`unit_topics_2`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose all relevant options that best describe your understanding of the unit topics covered in Lesson 2.
* **Lesson 3** (`lesson_3`, time, required/optional): If you have a specific time in mind for Lesson 3, please enter it here. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Student Feedback 3** (`student_feedback_3`, text, required/optional): Share your feedback about Lesson 3, including any positive or negative experiences you had during the lesson.
* **Unit Topics 3** (`unit_topics_3`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose all relevant options that best describe your understanding of the unit topics covered in Lesson 3.
* **Lesson 4** (`lesson_4`, select_one, required/optional): Choose one option that best describes your understanding of Lesson 4.
* **Student Feedback 4** (`student_feedback_4`, text, required/optional): Provide feedback about Lesson 4, including any thoughts, insights, or suggestions you have about the lesson.

## Tips
* Please answer all required fields to ensure accurate assessment.
* Be as concise and detailed as possible when providing feedback.
* You can modify the form to suit your specific needs and requirements.
