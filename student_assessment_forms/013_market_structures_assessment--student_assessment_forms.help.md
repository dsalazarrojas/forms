# Market Structures Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Market Structures Assessment form is designed to collect information from students about their market structures assessment. This form aims to gather data on students' performance, progress, and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Name** (Field 1).
2. Enter your **School Name** (Field 2).
3. Provide your **Class Name** (Field 3).
4. Answer **Question 1** (Field 4) and **Question 2** (Field 5).
5. Respond to **Question 3** (Field 6).
6. Enter the **Submission Date** (Field 7) in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
7. Enter the **Submission Time** (Field 8) in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
8. Provide any additional **Submission Comments** (Field 9) you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **School Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your school name, if applicable.
* **Class Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your class name, if applicable.
* **Question 1** (Text, Optional): Answer the first question as instructed.
* **Question 2** (Text, Optional): Answer the second question as instructed.
* **Question 3** (Text, Optional): Answer the third question as instructed.
* **Submission Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you're submitting this form in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Submission Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time you're submitting this form in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Submission Comments** (Note, Optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share.

## Tips
* Please ensure you answer all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your submission date and time for accuracy.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact form@gic.mx.
