# Final Study Guide Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Final Study Guide Quiz is a form used to gather feedback from students on their understanding of a course material and their overall learning experience. This feedback will help instructors improve the course content and teaching style for future semesters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a valid student ID number.
2. Answer the questions on each page as honestly and thoroughly as possible.
3. Use the provided options for the "Confidence Level" question to indicate your level of confidence in your understanding of the course material.
4. Use the "Feedback" field to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): Enter your student ID number, if applicable.
* **Prophets** (`prophets`, text, optional): Write a short description of the most important concepts or topics you have learned from the course.
* **Confidence Level** (`confidence_level`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options that best describes your confidence level in understanding the course material:
	+ High: I am very confident in my understanding of the course material.
	+ Medium: I have some understanding, but there are still some areas where I am unsure or need improvement.
	+ Low: I am not confident in my understanding of the course material.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have on the course or instructor.
