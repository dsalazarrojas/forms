# Facial Vocabulary Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facial Vocabulary Assessment form is designed to help students evaluate their ability to identify and label different facial expressions, which are an essential aspect of communication and social skills. The form will help students assess their knowledge of facial vocabulary and provide them with the opportunity to reflect on their understanding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the introductory questions to understand the purpose and format of the form.
2. Select all the faces that you are comfortable labeling.
3. Label each face with the correct name of the expression.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facial Expression 1** (`Left`, `text`, `false`):
	+ This field asks you to label the left side of your face. Please type the name of the expression that you commonly associate with the left side of your face.
* **Facial Expression 2** (`Right`, `text`, `false`):
	+ This field asks you to label the right side of your face. Please type the name of the expression that you commonly associate with the right side of your face.
* **Facial Expression 3** (`Top`, `text`, `false`):
	+ This field asks you to label the top part of your face. Please type the name of the expression that you commonly associate with the top part of your face.
* **Facial Expression 4** (`Bottom`, `text`, `false`):
	+ This field asks you to label the bottom part of your face. Please type the name of the expression that you commonly associate with the bottom part of your face.
* **Facial Expression 5** (`Back`, `text`, `false`):
	+ This field asks you to label the back of your face. Please type the name of the expression that you commonly associate with the back of your face.
* **Facial Expression 6** (`Face6`, `text`, `false`):
	+ This field is a generic field for any other facial expression that is not listed above. Please type the name of the expression that you commonly associate with this part of your face.
* **Facial Expression 7** (`Face7`, `text`, `false`):
	+ This field is a generic field for any other facial expression that is not listed above. Please type the name of the expression that you commonly associate with this part of your face.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when labeling each facial expression.
* Use the names of expressions that you commonly use in your daily life.
* If you are unsure of an expression, you can type "don't know" or "I'm not sure".
* You can use this form as many times as you need to assess your facial vocabulary.
