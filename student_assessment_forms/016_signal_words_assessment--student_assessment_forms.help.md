# Signal Words Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate students' understanding of signal words and their ability to apply this understanding in reading passages.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer according to your knowledge.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best answers the question.
3. For text questions, provide a clear and concise answer that demonstrates your understanding of signal words.
4. Be sure to complete all questions, even if you are unsure of the answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: intro_page (signal_words_assessment)
* **signal_words_assessment** (Text, Optional): This is the title of the form, which is also the name of the assessment itself.

### Page 2: signal_words_page (what_do_students_do_when_they_encounter)
* **what_do_students_do_when_they_encounter** (Select Multiple, Optional): This question asks students to identify what they do when they encounter signal words in a reading passage. Select all options that apply to the correct answer.

### Page 3: reading_transitions_page (reading_transitions)
* **reading_transitions** (Text, Optional): This is a text question where students are asked to provide an example of a signal word that indicates a transition in a reading passage. This question is designed to assess students' ability to provide a clear and concise example.

### Page 4: reading_transitions_page (reading_transitions)
* **reading_transitions** (Select One, Optional): This question asks students to select a type of transition that is commonly found in reading passages. This question is designed to assess students' ability to recognize and identify signal words that indicate a transition.

### Page 5: reading_transitions_page (reading_transitions)
