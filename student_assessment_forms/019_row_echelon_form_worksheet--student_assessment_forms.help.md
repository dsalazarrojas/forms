# Row Echelon Form Worksheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on students' understanding of row echelon forms, a fundamental concept in linear algebra. The form is divided into two sections: one for the student to complete and another for the teacher to provide feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Complete the form as instructed below.
2. Review your work carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **row_echelon_form_worksheet_form** (`row_echelon_form_worksheet_form`, text, required): Enter your answer to the question about the given matrix being in row echelon form.
* **Step Number** (`step_number`, number, required): Enter the step number of the process you used to determine that the matrix is in row echelon form.
* **matrix** (`matrix`, select_multiple, optional): Check the boxes to indicate which row operations you performed on the matrix. You can select multiple options.
	+ 'Yes' indicates that you performed the row operation.
	+ 'No' indicates that you did not perform the row operation.
* **reduced matrix** (`reduced_matrix`, select_one, required): Choose the option to indicate whether the given matrix is in row echelon form.
	+ 'Yes' indicates that the matrix is in row echelon form.
	+ 'No' indicates that the matrix is not in row echelon form.
* **Row Echelon Form Worksheet Comments** (`row_echelon_form_worksheet_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or thoughts you have about the matrix and your answer.
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback you want to provide to the teacher.
* **Student Response** (`student_response`, text, optional): Enter your response or answer to the question.
* **Teacher Response** (`teacher_response`, text, optional): The teacher will enter their response or feedback in this field. 

## Tips
- Please read the instructions carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
- Enter your answer in the required fields as instructed.
- Use clear and concise language when entering your comments or feedback. 

Note: The fields that have `required: true` are necessary to complete the form, while the others are optional and may be used for additional comments or feedback.
