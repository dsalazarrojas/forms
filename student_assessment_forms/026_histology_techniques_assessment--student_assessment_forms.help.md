# Histology Techniques Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess a student's knowledge and skills in histology techniques. It is used to evaluate a student's proficiency in histology procedures and identify areas where additional training is needed. This form will help the instructor to provide targeted feedback and support to improve the student's skills.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the required information, including general information, training history, and other fields as necessary.
* Select the relevant options for each field from the provided list.
* Provide any additional comments or information that you think will help the instructor understand your skills and needs.
* Review and submit the form for feedback and evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`1`, text, required): Please enter your name and any relevant contact information.
* **Training History** (`2`, select_one, required): Choose the relevant options that describe your prior training in histology techniques:
	+ Option 1: Completed course or program in histology techniques.
	+ Option 2: Completed other relevant medical or science coursework.
	+ Option 3: Other relevant training or experience.
* **Experience** (`3`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant options that describe your experience with histology techniques:
	+ Option 1: Laboratory setting.
	+ Option 2: Clinical setting.
	+ Option 3: Other relevant experience.
* **Proficiency** (`4`, select_one, required): Choose the level of proficiency you have achieved in histology techniques:
	+ Option 1: Basic level of proficiency.
	+ Option 2: Intermediate level of proficiency.
	+ Option 3: Advanced level of proficiency.
* **Lab Practices** (`5`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant options that describe your experiences in lab practices:
	+ Option 1: Handling and preparing specimens.
	+ Option 2: Using microscopes and other laboratory equipment.
	+ Option 3: Other relevant lab practices.
* **Training Needs** (`6`, select_one, required): Choose the areas where you need additional training or review in histology techniques:
	+ Option 1: Techniques and procedures.
	+ Option 2: Microscopy and imaging.
	+ Option 3: Other relevant areas.
* **Additional Comments** (`7`, note, required): Use this field to provide any additional comments or information that will help the instructor understand your skills and needs.
* **Overall Rating** (`8`, select_one, required): Choose the overall rating that describes your proficiency in histology techniques:
	+ Option 1: Novice.
	+ Option 2: Intermediate.
	+ Option 3: Advanced.
* **Assessment Status** (`9`, select_one, required): Choose the assessment status:
	+ Option 1: Complete.
	+ Option 2: Incomplete.
* **Evaluation** (`10`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant options that describe your evaluation:
	+ Option 1: Excellent.
	+ Option 2: Satisfactory.
	+ Option 3: Needs improvement.
* **Additional Information** (`11`, note, required): Use this field to provide any additional comments or information that will help the instructor understand your skills and needs.
* **Email** (`12`, email, required): Enter your email address so that the instructor can contact you for further discussion.
* **Phone Number** (`13`, text, required): Enter your phone number so that the instructor can contact you for further discussion.
* **Signature** (`14`, text, required): Sign your name, indicating that you have completed and submitted this form truthfully.
* **Date** (`15`, date, required): Enter the date you completed this form.
* **Time** (`16`, time, required): Enter the time you completed this form.
* **Location** (`17`, select_one, required): Choose the location where you completed this form:
	+ Option 1: On-campus.
	+ Option 2: Off-campus.
* **Lab Name** (`18`, text, optional): Enter the name of the lab where you worked or studied histology techniques.
* **Course Name** (`19`, text, optional): Enter the name of the course where you learned histology techniques.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions and selecting options.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are complete.
* If you are unsure about any of the options or questions, ask the instructor for guidance.
