# Phonetics Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Phonetics Assessment Survey is designed to evaluate students' language skills in phonetics, a field of study that focuses on the sounds of spoken languages.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by gathering all the necessary information and resources, such as the student's language background and audio samples.
2. Review each page carefully and answer all questions accurately to the best of your ability.
3. For questions with multiple options, select the most appropriate response from the list.
4. Use the text fields to provide any additional comments or observations.
5. Make sure to complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Info (student_info)** (`Text`, Required): Enter the student's name, ID, or any other identifying information.
* **Language Background (language_background)** (`Text`, Optional): Describe the student's language background, including their native language and any other relevant language skills.
* **Audio Samples (audio_samples)** (`Select Multiple`, Optional): Indicate whether the student has audio samples available for evaluation.
* **Pronunciation Self-Rating (pronunciation_self_rating)** (`Select One`, Optional): Rate the student's pronunciation from Excellent to Poor.
* **Comments 1-8 (comments_1-8)** (`Text`, Optional): Provide any additional comments or observations for each of the eight pronunciation samples.
* **Language Proficiency Scale (language_proficiency_scale)** (`Select One`, Optional): Select the student's language proficiency level from Native Speaker to None.
* **Language Proficiency Scale 2 (language_proficiency_scale_2)** (`Select One`, Optional): Select the student's language proficiency level from Native Speaker to None.
* **Language Proficiency Scale 3 (language_proficiency_scale_3)** (`Select One`, Optional): Select the student's language proficiency level from Native Speaker to None.
* **Pronunciation Sample 1-8 (pronunciation_sample_audio_1-8)** (`Text`, Optional): Enter any additional comments or observations for each of the eight pronunciation samples.
* **Comments 1-8 (comments_1-8)** (`Text`, Optional): Enter any additional comments or observations for each of the eight comments fields.
