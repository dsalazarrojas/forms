# Graduate Career Readiness Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graduate Career Readiness Assessment is a form designed to help students and recent graduates gauge their current career readiness. It's a self-assessment tool to help individuals identify their strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement in preparation for their future career.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your current employment status from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter your expected graduation date or completion date in the date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
3. (Optional) Select up to two options for what you're doing to prepare for your career.
4. (Optional) Select up to three options for what you're most interested in about your career.
5. (Optional) Select up to three options for what kind of work environment you're interested in.
6. (Optional) Select up to three options for what industry you're interested in.
7. Enter your ideal career role or job title.
8. (Optional) Enter up to two options for what you're most looking forward to about your future career.
9. (Optional) Enter up to two options for what you're most concerned about your future career.
10. (Optional) Select up to three options for what support or resources you need to be successful in your career.
11. (Optional) Select up to three options for what you're expecting from your future career.
12. (Optional) Select up to three options for what motivates your career goals.
13. (Optional) Enter the field of study or work you're currently engaged in.
14. (Optional) Select whether you're considering starting a side-hustle or business.
15. (Optional) Enter the industry you think you'll be working in.
16. (Optional) Enter what you're expecting from your employer.
17. (Optional) Enter how you plan to use the assessment results.
18. (Optional) Enter how you see yourself using the assessment results in future career decisions.
19. (Optional) Enter how long you expect your first job to last.
20. (Optional) Enter how long you expect to work in your first job.
21. (Optional) Enter how long you expect to spend on education after graduation.
22. (Optional) Enter your expected starting salary range.
23. (Optional) Select whether you're expecting any student loan debt.
24. (Optional) Enter what you plan to do to manage your student loan debt.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your current employment status**: This question helps us understand your current situation and how it may impact your career goals.
* **What is your expected graduation date or completion date**: This question helps us understand your timeline for entering the workforce and how it may influence your career choices.
* **What type of job or career are you preparing for**: This question helps us understand your career aspirations and what you hope to achieve in your future career.
* **What are you doing to prepare for your career**: This question helps us understand your current activities and efforts to prepare for your future career. Select all that apply.
	+ **Seeking out job opportunities**: This means actively looking for job openings and applying to positions that match your skills and interests.
	+ **Taking relevant coursework**: This means taking courses or classes that help you develop skills and knowledge related to your desired career.
	+ **Participating in career-related projects**: This means engaging in projects or activities that demonstrate your skills and experience in your desired field.
	+ **Building a professional network**: This means connecting with professionals and industry leaders to learn from them and expand your network.
	+ **Creating a personal brand online**: This means creating a professional online presence, such as a website or social media profiles, to promote yourself and your skills.
	+ **Other**: If none of the above options apply, enter your own answer.
* **What are you most interested in about your career**: This question helps us understand what motivates and motivates you in your career. Select all that apply.
	+ **Work-life balance**: This means finding a career that allows you to balance your work and personal life effectively.
	+ **Salary and benefits**: This means being motivated by financial rewards and benefits.
	+ **Job security**: This means seeking a career that offers stability and predictability.
	+ **Professional development**: This means seeking a career that offers opportunities for growth and development.
	+ **Personal fulfillment**: This means seeking a career that aligns with your values and provides a sense of purpose.
	+ **Other**: If none of the above options apply, enter your own answer.
* **What kind of work environment are you interested in**: This question helps us understand your preferences for your work environment. Select all that apply.
	+ **Office work**: This means working in a traditional office setting.
	+ **Remote work**: This means working from home or remotely.
	+ **Freelance or consulting**: This means working independently or on a project basis.
	+ **Entrepreneurship**: This means starting your own business.
	+ **Other**: If none of the above options apply, enter your own answer.
* **What kind of industry are you interested in**: This question helps us understand your career aspirations. Select all that apply.
	+ **Tech**: This means working in technology-related industries.
	+ **Arts and humanities**: This means working in creative or arts-related industries.
	+ **Business**: This means working in business-related industries.
	+ **Education and research**: This means working in academic or research-related fields.
	+ **Health and wellness**: This means working in healthcare or wellness-related industries.
	+ **Other**: If none of the above options apply, enter your own answer.
* **What is your ideal career role or job title**: This question helps us understand your career aspirations.
* **What are you most looking forward to about your future career**: This question helps us understand your motivations and expectations. Enter up to two options.
* **What are you most concerned about your future career**: This question helps us understand your concerns and anxieties. Enter up to two options.
* **What support or resources do you need to be successful in your career**: This question helps us understand your needs and expectations. Select all that apply.
	+ **Career counseling**: This means seeking guidance and advice from career professionals.
	+ **Professional networking**: This means connecting with industry leaders and professionals.
	+ **Mentorship**: This means seeking guidance and mentorship from experienced professionals.
	+ **Financial assistance**: This means seeking financial support or resources.
	+ **Other**: If none of the above options apply, enter your own answer.
* **What are you expecting from your future career**: This question helps us understand your expectations and aspirations. Select all that apply.
	+ **Job satisfaction**: This means finding a career that brings you joy and fulfillment.
	+ **Financial stability**: This means finding a career that provides financial security.
	+ **Personal fulfillment**: This means finding a career that aligns with your values and provides a sense of purpose.
	+ **Career growth**: This means finding a career that offers opportunities for growth and development.
	+ **Other**: If none of the above options apply, enter your own answer.
* **How would you describe your career goals**: This question helps us understand your goals and aspirations. Select all that apply.
	+ **Short-term career goals**: This means career goals that can be achieved in the short-term.
	+ **Long-term career goals**: This means career goals that can be achieved in the long-term.
	+ **Career goals for personal fulfillment**: This means career goals that align with your personal values and purpose.
	+ **Other**: If none of the above options apply, enter your own answer.
* **What are you currently studying or working towards**: This question helps us understand your current focus and goals. Enter your current field of study or work.
* **Are you considering starting a side-hustle or business**: This question helps us understand your entrepreneurial aspirations. Select 'True' if you're considering starting a side-hustle or business.
* **What industry do you think you'll be working in**: This question helps us understand your career aspirations. Enter the industry you think you'll be working in.
* **What are you expecting from your employer**: This question helps us understand your expectations and needs. Enter your expectations.
* **How do you plan to use this assessment results**: This question helps us understand how you plan to use the assessment results. Enter your plans.
* **How do you see yourself using this assessment results in future career decisions**: This question helps us understand how you plan to apply the assessment results in future career decisions. Enter your plans.
* **How long do you expect your first job to last**: This question helps us understand your career goals and expectations. Enter how long you expect your first job to last.
* **How long do you expect to work in your first job**: This question helps us understand your career goals and expectations. Enter how long you expect to work in your first job.
* **How long do you expect to spend on education after graduation**: This question helps us understand your educational goals and expectations. Enter how long you expect to spend on education after graduation.
* **What is your expected starting salary range**: This question helps us understand your financial goals and expectations. Enter your expected starting salary range.
* **Are you expecting any student loan debt**: This question helps us understand your financial situation. Select 'True' if you expect to have student loan debt.
* **What do you plan to do to manage your student loan debt**: This question helps us understand your plans for managing student loan debt. Enter your plans.
