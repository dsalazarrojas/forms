# Semantic Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Semantic Knowledge Assessment form is used to evaluate the semantic knowledge of students. It is designed to determine the level of understanding that students have on a specific topic or subject.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start by reviewing the questions and answering them accordingly.
2. Begin with the introduction, where you can provide a brief description of the topic or subject.
3. Select the correct question type for each question.
4. Indicate the level of understanding you possess on the question.
5. Type your answer to each question in the text field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**:
	+ (1) A text field where you can provide a brief description of the topic or subject being assessed.
* **Question Type**:
	+ (2) A select-one field with two options: 'Yes' or 'No'. This field is used to determine whether you have the required knowledge for the question.
* **Question Level**:
	+ (3) A select-multiple field where you can select from 'High', 'Low', or both. This field is used to indicate the level of understanding you possess on the question.
* **Question Text**:
	+ (4) A text field where you can provide your answer to the question.

## Tips

* Please answer each question accurately and honestly.
* Be concise in your answers, but provide enough detail to demonstrate your understanding.
* If you are unsure about any question, please select 'No' for question type and 'Low' for question level.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact your instructor or support staff.
