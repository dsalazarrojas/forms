# Database Design Concepts Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your understanding of database design concepts. It will ask you questions related to the importance of theory questions, your experience with database design scenarios, and your ability to apply these concepts to real-world situations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please review the provided material on database design concepts.
2. Answer the questions honestly, based on your understanding of the material.
3. Use the options provided for each question to select your response.
4. For the scenario questions, choose all options that apply.
5. In the conclusion section, provide a brief summary of your thoughts on the material.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This section is a note section for any additional information you'd like to share with the instructor.
* **Theory Questions**: This question asks whether you think theory questions are important in database design. Please choose 'Yes' if you believe they are essential.
* **Scenario Questions**: In this question, please choose all options that apply if you have experience with database design scenarios.
* **Database Design Scenario**: This is a text field where you can describe a real-world database design scenario that you have experience with.
* **Conclusion**: This section is for you to provide a brief summary of your thoughts on the material.
* **Student Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your student ID.
* **Student ID**: This is a numerical field where you enter your student ID number.

## Tips
- Be honest in your answers, and provide clear explanations for your choices.
- Use the provided options for the scenario questions to ensure your answer is clear and understandable.
- For the database design scenario, provide as much detail as possible about your experience.
- If you are unsure of an answer, it's okay, but be sure to explain your reasoning.
