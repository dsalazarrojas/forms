# Student Fear And Learning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Fear And Learning Survey is a tool designed to gather information from students about their fears and learning experiences in various subjects, as well as their perception of support from teachers and parents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your school year: Please select the current school year.
2. Enter your school name: Please type in the name of your school.
3. Enter your teacher's name: This is the name of the teacher who is giving you this survey.
4. Select your grade: Please select your grade level or the level you feel comfortable with.
5. Select your subject: Please choose the subject you are currently learning (e.g. math, reading, writing, etc.).
6. Select your fear level: Choose how much fear you feel about learning this subject.
7. Select your anxiety level: Choose how much anxiety you feel about learning this subject.
8. Select your coping strategies: Choose how often you use coping strategies for dealing with fear or anxiety.
9. Select teacher support: Choose how much support you receive from your teacher.
10. Select parent support: Choose how much support you receive from your parents or guardians.
11. Describe your class environment: Type in the current environment of your class, such as online, in-person, or hybrid.
12. Describe any additional comments or concerns: If you have any additional thoughts or concerns, please type them in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Year** (`school_year`, text, required): Please enter the current school year.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of your school.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): This is the name of your teacher who is giving you this survey.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, text, optional): Please select your grade level or the level you feel comfortable with.
* **Student Subject** (`student_subject`, text, optional): Please choose the subject you are currently learning (e.g. math, reading, writing, etc.).
* **Student Fear Level** (`student_fear_level`, select_one, required): Please choose how much fear you feel about learning this subject.
	+ Low: You feel very little fear about learning this subject.
	+ Moderate: You feel some fear about learning this subject.
	+ High: You feel a lot of fear about learning this subject.
* **Student Anxiety Level** (`student_anxiety_level`, select_one, required): Please choose how much anxiety you feel about learning this subject.
	+ Not at all: You feel no anxiety about learning this subject.
	+ Somewhat: You feel some anxiety about learning this subject.
	+ A lot: You feel a lot of anxiety about learning this subject.
* **Student Coping Strategies** (`student_coping_strategies`, select_one, required): Please choose how often you use coping strategies for dealing with fear or anxiety.
	+ Not at all: You rarely or never use coping strategies.
	+ Somewhat: You use coping strategies sometimes.
	+ A lot: You use coping strategies often.
* **Teacher Support** (`teacher_support`, select_one, required): Please choose how much support you receive from your teacher.
	+ Not at all: You receive very little support from your teacher.
	+ Somewhat: You receive some support from your teacher.
	+ A lot: You receive a lot of support from your teacher.
* **Parent Support** (`parent_support`, select_one, required): Please choose how much support you receive from your parents or guardians.
	+ Not at all: You receive very little support from your parents or guardians.
	+ Somewhat: You receive some support from your parents or guardians.
	+ A lot: You receive a lot of support from your parents or guardians.
* **Class Environment** (`class_environment`, text, optional): Please describe the current environment of your class, such as online, in-person, or hybrid.
* **Teacher Training** (`teacher_training`, text, optional): Please describe any teacher training or professional development experiences that may be relevant.
* **Student Fears and Anxieties** (`student_fears_and_anxieties`, text, optional): If you have any fears or anxieties that you would like to share, please type them in.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional thoughts or concerns, please type them in.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Please answer the questions based on your current experience and feelings.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask your teacher for help.
* This form is confidential and anonymous, so you can feel safe sharing your thoughts and feelings with us.
