# Human Learning Assessment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Human Learning Assessment Quiz is a tool to evaluate a student's knowledge and understanding of a subject or topic. It is a series of questions designed to gauge a student's understanding of the material and identify areas where they need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on each question to select your answer from the options provided.
2. Make sure to answer all required questions (question 1, question 2, question 3).
3. For questions that are not marked as "required", you can choose any of the provided options.
4. Once you have completed all the questions, click the "Submit" button to submit your answers.
