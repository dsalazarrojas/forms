# Student Media Network Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Media Network Feedback form is designed to gather student feedback on their overall satisfaction and content preferences across different media platforms. This form helps the media network to understand student opinions and preferences, enabling them to refine their content and services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions about your overall satisfaction with the Student Media Network.
2. Indicate your preferences for content on different media platforms such as newspapers, radio, TV, podcasts, and digital channels.
3. Provide additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
4. Enter your email address and phone number (optional).
5. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction** (`student_media_network_satisfaction`, Number): This field asks for your overall satisfaction with the Student Media Network.
* **Newspaper** (`newspaper_preferences`, Select Multiple): Please select the types of content you'd like to see more of in our newspaper. You can choose one or more options.
* **Radio** (`radio_preferences`, Select Multiple): Select the types of content you'd like to see more of on our radio.
* **TV** (`tv_preferences`, Select Multiple): Choose the types of content you'd like to see more of on our TV.
* **Podcasts** (`podcast_preferences`, Select Multiple): Select the types of content you'd like to see more of on our podcasts.
* **Digital Channel** (`digital_channel_preferences`, Select Multiple): Choose the types of content you'd like to see more of on our digital channels.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, Text): We value your feedback and suggestions! Please provide any additional comments or ideas you think could improve our services.
* **Email** (`email`, Email): Enter your email address for us to contact you (optional).
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Note): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you'd like to make.
* **Content Preferences** (`newspaper_content`, Select Multiple, `radio_content`, Select Multiple, `tv_content`, Select Multiple, `podcast_content`, Select Multiple, `digital_channel_content`, Select Multiple, `email_content`, Select Multiple): Choose the types of content you'd like to see more of on each platform.
* **Submit** (`submit`, Text): Click this button to send your feedback.

## Tips

* You can select multiple options for each media platform in the "Content Preferences" fields.
* You can provide additional comments or suggestions in the "Suggestions" field.
* You can enter your email address and phone number if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up or further discussion.
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback and suggestions.
