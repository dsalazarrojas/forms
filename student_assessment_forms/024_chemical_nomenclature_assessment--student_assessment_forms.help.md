# Chemical Nomenclature Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess students' knowledge of chemical nomenclature. It consists of 18 questions that test your understanding of chemical names and formulas.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question with the correct name or formula of the given chemical compound.
2. For questions with a dropdown list of options, select the best answer from the list.
3. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field to identify yourself for this assessment.
4. Enter your student ID in the "Student ID" field for tracking purposes.
