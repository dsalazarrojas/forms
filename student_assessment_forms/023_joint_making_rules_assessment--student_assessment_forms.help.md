# Joint Making Rules Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the quality and importance of joints in a joint-making process. It is used to evaluate and document the performance of joints in a specific context or project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately, taking into account the type of joint and its properties.
2. Choose the correct option from the list for each question.
3. Fill out all required fields.
4. Make sure to review your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
