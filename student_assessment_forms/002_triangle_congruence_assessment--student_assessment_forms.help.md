# Triangle Congruence Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Triangle Congruence Assessment is a form used to assess a student's understanding of triangle congruence. This form is designed to help students demonstrate their knowledge of triangle congruence concepts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your student response**: Please provide a brief statement or description of your understanding of triangle congruence.
2. **Select your student ID**: Choose the correct student ID from the dropdown menu.
3. **Rate your congruence criterion 1**: Answer the first congruence criterion question using a number scale.
4. **Rate your congruence criterion 2**: Answer the second congruence criterion question using either option 4 or 5.
5. **Rate your congruence criterion 3**: Answer the third congruence criterion question using a number scale.
6. **Rate your congruence criterion 4**: Answer the fourth congruence criterion question using either option 6 or 7.
7. **Rate your congruence criterion 5**: Answer the fifth congruence criterion question using option 8 or 9.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Response** (text, required): This field is a text box where you can enter a brief statement or description of your understanding of triangle congruence.
* **Student ID** (select one, required): Select the correct student ID that corresponds to your identity.
* **Congruence Criterion 1** (number, required): Rate your understanding of the first congruence criterion using a number scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Congruence Criterion 2** (select one, required): Choose the correct option that describes your understanding of the second congruence criterion.
* **Congruence Criterion 3** (number, required): Rate your understanding of the third congruence criterion using a number scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Congruence Criterion 4** (select one, required): Choose the correct option that describes your understanding of the fourth congruence criterion.
* **Congruence Criterion 5** (select one, required): Choose the correct option that describes your understanding of the fifth congruence criterion.
* **Date Submitted** (date, optional): If you want to record the date and time you submitted this form.
* **Time Submitted** (time, optional): If you want to record the time you submitted this form.
* **Note** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your understanding of triangle congruence.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate in your responses.
* Make sure to answer all required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your instructor or mentor for help.
