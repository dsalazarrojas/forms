# Educational Environment Assessment Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students on the educational environment of the classroom, providing an opportunity for improvement and growth.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page and ensure you are in the correct page for your feedback.
2. For each page, select the options that best describe your experience or choose "N/A" if the option does not apply.
3. For fields that ask for text responses, provide a clear and concise answer.
4. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Class Resources** (`class_resources`, text, optional): Please describe the quality and availability of class resources (e.g., textbooks, technology, equipment, etc.).
- **Class Safety** (`class_safety`, select_one, optional): Select the option that best describes your experience with the class safety (e.g., safe, moderately safe, not safe).
- **Teaching Quality** (`teaching_quality`, select_multiple, optional): Select all options that best describe the quality of teaching in the class (e.g., engaging, clear, challenging, etc.).
- **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, time, optional): Please rate your overall satisfaction with the class from 0 to 100.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions you have for the class.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address for follow-up communication.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
- **Student Feedback** (`student_feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about the class.

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate in your responses.
- Ensure that all fields are completed before submitting your response.
- If you have any additional feedback or comments, please include them in the "Additional Comments" field.
