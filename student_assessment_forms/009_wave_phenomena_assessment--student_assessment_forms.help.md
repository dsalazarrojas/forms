# Wave Phenomena Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wave Phenomena Assessment form is used to evaluate students' understanding of wave phenomena, providing instructors with a means to monitor student progress and assess their grasp of wave-related concepts.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, ensure you have completed a lesson on wave phenomena and have a basic understanding of the topics covered. This form is meant to be a practical assessment, so apply the concepts you've learned to the provided fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (1)**: Enter your name as it appears on your official student record.
* **Wave Type (2)**: Select the type of wave you're referring to, such as Mechanical Waves, Electromagnetic Waves, or Sound Waves.
* **Wave Speed (3)**: Enter the speed of the wave you've identified, using numbers only (no text).
* **Wave Frequency (4)**: Enter the frequency of the wave you've identified, using numbers only (no text).
* **Real-Life Example (5)**: Describe a real-life example where you have applied the knowledge of wave phenomena, such as a personal experience or a historical event related to waves.
* **Wave Properties (6)**: Describe the properties of the wave you've studied, such as its characteristics or behaviors.
* **Student Assessment (7)**: Write a brief assessment of what you've learned from the lesson and how you can apply it in the future.
* **Wave Equations (8)**: Write any equations or mathematical concepts you've learned from the lesson that relate to wave phenomena.
* **Date Completed (9)**: Enter the date you completed the lesson and submitted this form (if applicable).
* **Time Completed (10)**: Enter the time you completed the lesson and submitted this form (if applicable).
* **Assigned Tool (11)**: If applicable, select the tool used to complete this form, such as Chatjimmy.
* **Email (12)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes (if applicable).
* **Phone (13)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes (if applicable).
