# final_exam_preparation_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Final Exam Preparation Checklist is designed to help students assess their progress, prepare for exams, and identify areas of improvement. This form is used to track a student's study progress, exam dates, confidence levels, revision planning, and study goals, providing a comprehensive overview of their exam preparation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each field honestly and thoroughly, as it will help you get a true understanding of your exam preparation progress.
2. Make sure to fill out each field, but note that some fields are optional and do not require input.
3. Select the correct options for the "Confidence Levels" field, as it will help you identify areas where you need to focus your efforts.
4. Write a detailed answer for the "Revision Planning" field, as it will help you create a plan to tackle any areas where you feel you need improvement.
5. Clearly state your study goals in the "Study Goals" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Study Progress** (`1`, `number`, required: `false`): This field is used to track your progress in studying and completing tasks. Please enter the number that best reflects your current progress, with 1 being the least progressed and 10 being the most progressed.
* **Exam Dates** (`2`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the dates of any upcoming exams. This will help you stay on track and ensure you are prepared for any exams.
* **Confidence Levels** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Please select the levels that best describe your confidence in each subject or topic. Choose from:
	+ High: You feel confident and prepared for the exam.
	+ Medium: You have some knowledge, but may need more review or practice.
	+ Low: You are unsure or have little knowledge of the subject or topic.
* **Revision Planning** (`4`, `note`, required: `false`): This field is for you to write a note about your revision plan. This will help identify areas where you need to focus on and create a plan to improve.
* **Study Goals** (`5`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your short-term and long-term study goals, including what you want to achieve and any specific objectives you have in mind.
