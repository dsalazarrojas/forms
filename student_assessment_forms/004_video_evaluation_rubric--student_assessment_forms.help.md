# Video Evaluation Rubric - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Video Evaluation Rubric is a form designed to assess the quality and impact of student-submitted videos. It provides a framework for evaluating video submissions, helping to identify strengths and weaknesses in a structured and objective manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the video from the list provided (Student Performance, Peer-to-Peer Review, or Peer-to-Peer Self-Review).
2. Enter the scores for each of the following criteria: Creativity, Originality, Relevance, Effectiveness, and Impact.
3. Enter the Overall Score, which is the sum of the scores for the above criteria.
4. Review and submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Video Evaluation Rubric** (`video_evaluation_rubric`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the video being evaluated.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Choose the category of the video from the list (Student Performance, Peer-to-Peer Review, or Peer-to-Peer Self-Review).
* **Creativity** (`creativity`, number, optional): Assess the video's creativity, with higher scores indicating more innovative and original ideas.
* **Originality** (`originality`, number, optional): Evaluate the video's originality, with higher scores indicating more novel and unique approaches.
* **Relevance** (`relevance`, number, optional): Assess the video's relevance to the topic or prompt, with higher scores indicating more effective application.
* **Effectiveness** (`effectiveness`, number, optional): Evaluate the video's effectiveness in communicating its message, with higher scores indicating clearer and more persuasive communication.
* **Impact** (`impact`, number, optional): Assess the video's potential impact, with higher scores indicating more significant and lasting effects.
* **Overall Score** (`overall_score`, number, optional): Enter the sum of the scores for the above criteria, providing an overall assessment of the video's quality.

## Tips
- Be objective and fair in your evaluation, using the rubric as a guide.
- Use the scores to provide constructive feedback to the student.
- Review and revise your scores as needed, ensuring consistency and accuracy.
- Ensure that you have filled out all required fields before submitting your feedback.
