# Poetic Devices Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to test your knowledge of poetic devices, a fundamental part of literary analysis and creative writing. The form assesses your understanding of various devices used in poetry to create a specific effect or atmosphere.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each section carefully and answer every question based on your knowledge.
2. Select or enter the correct answer for each question.
3. Make sure to complete every required field.
