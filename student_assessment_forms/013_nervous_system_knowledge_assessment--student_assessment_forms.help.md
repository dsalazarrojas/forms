# Nervous System Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for students to assess their knowledge of the nervous system, its components, and its functions. The purpose of this form is to evaluate students' understanding of the nervous system, identify areas of knowledge gaps, and provide feedback for further learning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Fill in the blanks with the required information.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nervous System Knowledge Assessment** (`nervous_system_knowledge_assessment`, `text`, required): This field requires you to provide a short description of your current knowledge level of the nervous system.
* **Nervous System Functions** (`nervous_system_functions`, `select_one`, required): This field asks you to confirm if you are familiar with the main functions of the nervous system. Please select "Yes" if you are familiar with its functions.
* **What Are The Functions Of The Nervous System** (`what_are_the_functions_of_the_nervous_system`, `text`, required): This field is an open question where you can describe the main functions of the nervous system.
* **Nervous System Divisions** (`nervous_system_divisions`, `select_multiple`, required): This field asks you to confirm if you are familiar with the different divisions of the nervous system. Please select all that apply.
* **Nervous System Components** (`nervous_system_components`, `text`, required): This field requires you to describe the main components of the nervous system.
* **Nervous System Organ** (`nervous_system_organ`, `date`, required): This field is used to record the date when you first learned about the nervous system organ.
* **Nervous System Dysfunctions** (`nervous_system_dysfunctions`, `text`, required): This field is an open question where you can describe any known nervous system dysfunctions.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `select_one`, required): This field asks you to confirm if you have provided feedback in the past. Please select "Yes" if you have.
* **Nervous System Knowledge Assessment** (`nervous_system_knowledge_assessment`, `note`, required): This field is used to record your overall knowledge level of the nervous system.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, `date`, required): This field is used to record the date when you completed this form.
* **Time Completed** (`time_completed`, `time`, required): This field is used to record the time when you completed this form.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `email`, required): This field is used to record your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): This field is used to record your phone number.
* **Nervous System Knowledge Assessment 2** (`nervous_system_knowledge_assessment_2`, `note`, required): This field is used to record your overall knowledge level of the nervous system.
