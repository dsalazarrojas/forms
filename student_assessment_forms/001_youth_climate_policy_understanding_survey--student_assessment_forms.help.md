# Youth Climate Policy Understanding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your understanding of climate policies and their impacts on society.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Take a few minutes to carefully read through each question.
2. Answer each question truthfully based on your current knowledge and opinions.
3. Select the options that best match your thoughts and feelings.
4. For text fields, provide as much detail as possible in your answers.
5. If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
6. Once you've completed all the questions, review your answers for accuracy and completeness.
7. Finally, submit your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Questions**: 
	+ Please answer the questions to the best of your ability and provide any relevant information you feel is important to share.
* **Climate Knowledge** 
	+ Please select 'Yes' if you feel you have a good understanding of the following climate-related topics:
* **Climate Actions**: 
	+ Please select 'Yes' if you feel you are actively working to address climate-related issues.
* **Climate Impacts**: 
	+ Please describe any climate-related impacts you are concerned about or have experienced.
* **Policy Awareness** 
	+ Please select 'Yes' if you feel you are aware of current climate policies and initiatives.
* **Policy Support** 
	+ Please select 'Yes' if you support current climate policies and initiatives.
* **Policy Participation** 
	+ Please select 'Yes' if you participate in climate-related activities or events.
* **Climate Emotions** 
	+ Please select 'Yes' if you feel emotions such as anxiety, fear, or concern about climate-related issues.
* **Policy Opinions** 
	+ Please provide your opinions about climate policies and their effectiveness.
* **Policy Recommendations**

* **Other Comments**

* **Climate Knowledge Important**

* **Demographic Information**

* **Contact Information**

* **Other Comments**
