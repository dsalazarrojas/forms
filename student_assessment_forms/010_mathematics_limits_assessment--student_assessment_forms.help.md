# Mathematics Limits Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Mathematics Limits Assessment form is designed to gather information about a user's confidence in their mathematical limits, allowing educators to evaluate their understanding of mathematical concepts.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly to provide a comprehensive assessment of your mathematical limits.
2. Select the option that best describes your confidence level for each question.
3. Review your responses for accuracy before submitting the form.
4. If a question is not applicable or you are unsure, please provide a brief explanation in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, note, optional): This is a general introduction to the form, providing context for the questions that follow.
* **Questions** (`questions`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all that apply for each question.
	+ **Questions 1-20** (`questions_part_1`, `questions_part_2`, ..., `questions_part_20`, text, optional): Write your response to each question in the text field. These questions are designed to assess your mathematical limits and understanding.
* **Submission** (`submission`, note, optional): This is a general note for you to provide any additional comments or explanations for your responses.
* **Gauging Confidence** (`gauging_confidence`, select_one, optional): Select the option that best describes your confidence level for this assessment.
	+ A lot of confidence
	+ A moderate amount of confidence
	+ A little confidence
	+ No confidence
