# Renaissance And Reformation Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the reformation and its impact on European society. It asks users to provide their thoughts on the major events, causes, and consequences of the Reformation, as well as their views on its influence on art and music. This form is meant to gather feedback and opinions from users on the Reformation, and its completion is optional.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the form title, which will display as the heading of the form.
2. Answer the four text questions about the Reformation and its impact. You can provide as much or as little information as you like.
3. Select from the options provided for the "Reformation Influence" question. This question is multiple-choice, and you can only select one option.
4. Select multiple options for the "Reformation Impact" question. You can select as many or as few options as you like.
5. Enter a date for the "Reformation Date" question if you know it.
6. Enter a time for the "Reformation Time" question if you know it.
7. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Reformation Note" field.
8. Enter your email address for the "Reformation Email" field, if you want to receive follow-up information.
9. Finally, enter your phone number in the "Reformation Phone" field, if you want to be contacted by phone.
