# Cubic Functions Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cubic Functions Assessment is designed to evaluate a student's and teacher's understanding of cubic functions, including their ability to identify key characteristics and provide feedback on student work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the introduction section, which provides a brief overview of the concept of cubic functions.
2. Answer the mixed numerical and true/false questions, which assess your understanding of the relationship between numerical values and true/false statements.
3. Answer the mixed multiple choice questions, which test your ability to select the correct options from a list.
4. Answer the mixed open-ended questions, which require you to provide a detailed answer.
5. Upload files as required.
6. Rate your confidence in your answers.
7. If you are a teacher, provide feedback on the student's work.
