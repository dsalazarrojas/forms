# Human Development Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you evaluate your overall well-being and identify areas for growth and improvement. It will ask you to rate your well-being, select areas where you'd like to grow, and provide your personal and well-being goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Rate your overall well-being by selecting from the options provided.
2. Select all areas where you'd like to grow and improve.
3. Enter your personal and well-being goals, if applicable.
4. Enter your contact information: email, phone number, and date and time of submission.
5. Add any additional comments or notes.
6. Finally, review your submission to ensure everything is complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall well-being**: How do you rate your overall well-being? Select one of the three options: High, Medium, or Low.
* **Growth areas**: What are your growth areas? Select all areas where you'd like to improve, such as Emotional Stability, Self-awareness, Social Skills, Physical Health, and Mental Health.
* **Well-being goals**: What are your well-being goals? Enter a brief description of your goals, if you have any.
* **Personal goals**: What are your personal goals? Enter a brief description of your goals.
* **Overall rating**: How would you rate your overall well-being? Select one of the three options: Very Satisfied, Neutral, or Dissatisfied.
* **Comments**: Any additional comments or feedback?
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Time**: Enter the time of submission.
* **Notes**: Any additional comments or notes.
* **Submitted by**: Enter your name.
* **Submitted at**: Date and time of submission.
* **Submitted time**: Time of submission.
* **Submitted at 2**: Date of submission.
* **Submitted Time 2**: Time of submission.
* **Submitted at 3**: Date of submission.
* **Submitted Time 3**: Time of submission.

Note: All fields marked as "required" are essential to complete the form, while others are optional.
