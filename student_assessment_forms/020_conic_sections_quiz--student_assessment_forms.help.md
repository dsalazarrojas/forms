# Conic Sections Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess students' understanding of conic sections. You will be presented with a series of questions about the properties, equations, and characteristics of conic sections.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student's name and ID number.
2. Select the correct answer for each question.
3. Enter the numerical value or coordinate for questions that require it.
4. Rate your confidence level in the material after completing the quiz.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (student_name)** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID card.
* **Student ID (student_id)** (`student_id`, `text`, required): Enter your student ID number.
* **Class Period (class_period)** (`class_period`, `select_one`, required): Choose your class period or section.
* **Quiz Date (quiz_date)** (`quiz_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the quiz in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Question 1 - What is the standard form equation of a circle (question_1)** (`question_1`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct equation for a circle.
	+ The correct answer is the equation (x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2, where h and k are the coordinates of the center of the circle and r is the radius.
* **Question 2 - Which conic section has eccentricity equal to 0 (question_2)** (`question_2`, `select_one`, required): Choose the conic section with an eccentricity of 0.
	+ The correct answer is a circle.
* **Question 3 - What is the eccentricity of a parabola (question_3)** (`question_3`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct value of the eccentricity of a parabola.
	+ The correct answer is 0.
* **Question 4 - The equation x^2/a^2 + y^2/b^2 = 1 represents which conic (question_4)** (`question_4`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct conic section represented by the equation.
	+ The correct answer is an ellipse.
* **Question 5 - What are the two fixed points of an ellipse (question_5)** (`question_5`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct term for the fixed points of an ellipse.
	+ The correct answer is foci.
* **Question 6 - Which conic section is formed when a plane is parallel to the generator of the cone (question_6)** (`question_6`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct conic section formed by a plane parallel to the generator of a cone.
	+ The correct answer is a circle.
* **Question 7 - What is the standard form of a hyperbola opening left and right (question_7)** (`question_7`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct equation for a hyperbola opening left and right.
	+ The correct answer is x^2/a^2 - y^2/b^2 = 1.
* **Question 8 - The distance from the center to a vertex of an ellipse is called the (question_8)** (`question_8`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct term for the distance from the center to a vertex of an ellipse.
	+ The correct answer is semi-major axis.
* **Question 9 - What is the discriminant condition for a hyperbola in general conic form (question_9)** (`question_9`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct condition for a hyperbola in general conic form.
	+ The correct answer is B^2 - 4AC greater than 0.
* **Question 10 - Find the center of the circle (x - 3)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = 16 (question_10)** (`question_10`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct coordinates for the center of the circle.
	+ The correct answer is (3, -2).
* **Question 11 - What is the radius of the circle x^2 + y^2 = 25 (question_11)** (`question_11`, `number`, required): Enter the numerical value of the radius of the circle.
	+ The correct value is 5.
* **Question 12 - The asymptotes of a hyperbola x^2/a^2 - y^2/b^2 = 1 are given by (question_12)** (`question_12`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct equations for the asymptotes of a hyperbola.
	+ The correct answer is y = plus or minus (a/b)x.
* **Question 13 - Which conic section has one focus and one directrix (question_13)** (`question_13`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct conic section with one focus and one directrix.
	+ The correct answer is a parabola.
* **Question 14 - For an ellipse, the relationship between a, b, and c is (question_14)** (`question_14`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct relationship between a, b, and c for an ellipse.
	+ The correct answer is c^2 = a^2 + b^2.
* **Question 15 - What type of conic is represented by y = 2x^2 - 4x + 1 (question_15)** (`question_15`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct type of conic represented by the equation.
	+ The correct answer is a parabola.
* **Question 16 - The latus rectum of a parabola is also known as the (question_16)** (`question_16`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct term for the latus rectum of a parabola.
	+ The correct answer is directrix.
* **Question 17 - If the eccentricity of an ellipse is 0.5, the ellipse is (question_17)** (`question_17`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct description of the ellipse with an eccentricity of 0.5.
	+ The correct answer is nearly circular.
* **Question 18 - Write the focus of the parabola y^2 = 12x (question_18)** (`question_18`, `text`, required): Enter the coordinates of the focus of the parabola.
	+ Enter the coordinates (0, 6).
* **Question 19 - The graph of xy = 1 is which type of conic section (question_19)** (`question_19`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct type of conic section for the graph of xy = 1.
	+ The correct answer is a hyperbola.
* **Question 20 - Calculate the area of a circle with radius 5 units (question_20)** (`question_20`, `text`, required): Enter the area of the circle with a radius of 5 units.
	+ Enter the answer 39.25 or pi r^2.
* **Your Confidence Level (confidence_level)** (`confidence_level`, `select_one`, required): Rate your confidence level in the material.
	+ Select very confident, somewhat confident, or not confident.
