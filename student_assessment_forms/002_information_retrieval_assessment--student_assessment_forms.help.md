# Information Retreival Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Information Retrieval Assessment form is designed to evaluate students' ability to retrieve information from search engines. It is used to collect feedback on their search performance and provide insights for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with your name, search question, explanation, confidence level, search result, search result details, submit, search engine, confidence rating, date submitted, time submitted, form submitted by, email, phone, and other comments.
2. You can use the "Other Search Engine" field if you did not use one of the listed search engines.
3. Select the search engine used from the provided options.
4. Rate your confidence level in the search result from "High", "Medium", "Low", or select multiple levels.
5. Enter any additional comments or feedback about the search result.
6. Fill in your email address and phone number.
7. Select "Submit" to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief introduction to your search question or topic.
* **Student Name**: This is your name for identification purposes.
* **Search Question**: Enter the question or topic you searched for.
* **Explanation**: Explain how you obtained your search result.
* **Confidence**: Rate your confidence level in the search result from "High", "Medium", "Low".
* **Search Result**: Enter the actual search result you obtained.
* **Search Result Details**: Provide additional details about your search result.
* **Submit**: Confirm that you have completed the form.
* **Other Search Engine**: If you used a different search engine, enter the name of the engine and any additional details.
* **Other Search Engine Details**: Enter any additional comments or details about the search engine used.
* **Search Engine**: Select the search engine you used from the options provided.
* **Confidence Rating**: Rate your confidence level in the search result from "High", "Medium", "Low".
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional feedback or comments about the search result.
* **Date Submitted**: Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Time Submitted**: Enter the time you submitted the form.
* **Form Submitted By**: Enter your name or title for identification purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Other Comments**: Enter any other comments or feedback about the search result.
* **Submitted By**: Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Search Engine Version**: This field is for internal use only.
