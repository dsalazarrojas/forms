# Student Choreography Progress Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Choreography Progress Assessment Form is designed to evaluate a student's progress in choreography skills, collecting feedback from students, teachers, and parents on aspects such as technique, artistry, musicality, and performance standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form carefully and ensure you have the relevant information ready.
2. Fill in the required information for each field, including:
	* Student Name (page 1)
	* Student Level (page 1)
	* Submission Date (page 1)
	* Submission Time (page 1)
3. For each field, provide detailed feedback and observations as requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Student Choreography Progress Assessment Form
* **Student Choreography Progress Assessment Form** (`student_choreography_progress_assessment_form`, text, required): This is the title of the form, which should be filled in to identify the specific form being used.
* **Student Level** (`student_level`, select_one, required): Choose the student's level of proficiency, either High or Low.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name who is being assessed.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date the assessment was submitted.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required): Enter the time the assessment was submitted.
* **Dance Style** (`dance_style`, select_multiple, optional): Check all dance styles the student has experience with (if applicable).
* **Technique Observations** (`technique_observations`, text, optional): Provide detailed feedback on the student's technique.
* **Artistry Observations** (`artistry_observations`, text, optional): Provide feedback on the student's artistry.
* **Musicality Observations** (`musicality_observations`, text, optional): Provide feedback on the student's musicality.
* **Performance Standards** (`performance_standards`, text, optional): Provide feedback on the student's performance standards.
* **Student Feedback** (`student_feedback`, text, optional): Enter any feedback or comments from the student.
* **Teacher Feedback** (`teacher_feedback`, text, optional): Enter any feedback or comments from the teacher.
* **Parent Feedback** (`parent_feedback`, text, optional): Enter any feedback or comments from the parent.

## Tips
* Be specific and detailed when providing feedback and observations.
* Use clear and concise language when writing your comments.
* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
