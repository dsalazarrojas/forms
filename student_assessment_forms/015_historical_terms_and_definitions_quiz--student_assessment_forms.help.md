# Historical Terms And Definitions Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to create a historical quiz for educational purposes, allowing users to test their knowledge of historical terms and definitions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the title of the quiz.
2. Answer the questions on the first page, selecting the correct options for each.
3. Fill out the historical period and date fields.
4. Enter a score out of 10 for each question.
5. Add any additional notes if necessary.
6. Enter the submission date and time.
7. Provide your email address and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Quiz Form** (`quiz_form`, text, optional): Enter the title of the quiz, which will be displayed at the top of the assessment.
* **What is this word?** (`history_quiz_question`, select_one, optional): This field allows you to select the correct definition or term for the given historical word or phrase.
* **Historical Period** (`historical_period`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the historical period that corresponds to the question or content being assessed.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date associated with the historical event or period.
* **Score** (`score`, number, optional): Enter a score out of 10 for each question, reflecting your assessment of the response.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the assessment.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of submission.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
