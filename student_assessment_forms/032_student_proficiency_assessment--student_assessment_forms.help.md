# Student Proficiency Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess a student's current level of proficiency in a specific subject or skill, identify areas for improvement, and provide guidance for further instruction and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer each question or prompt in the designated fields, providing as much detail as possible.
* For the "Student Performance" fields, enter a numerical value indicating the student's current level of performance, on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* For the "Feedback" field, select one of the provided options to indicate the level of feedback you want to give to the student.
* For the "Improvement Plan" field, enter a brief note with suggestions for improvement.
* For the "Date" fields, select the date of the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Proficiency Assessment 1** (`student_proficiency_assessment_1`, `time`, optional): This field is where you will enter the date and time of the assessment.
* **Student Performance 1** (`student_performance_1`, `number`, optional): Enter the student's current level of performance in the subject or skill being assessed, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Feedback 1** (`feedback_1`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the options to provide feedback to the student.
	+ 1: The student is meeting the expected standards.
	+ 2: The student is below the expected standards.
	+ 3: The student has some areas of strength, but needs improvement in other areas.
* **Student Performance 2** (`student_performance_2`, `number`, optional): Enter the student's current level of performance in the subject or skill being assessed, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Improvement Plan 1** (`improvement_plan_1`, `note`, optional): Enter a brief note with suggestions for improvement for the student.
* **Student Proficiency Assessment 2** (`student_proficiency_assessment_2`, `date`, optional): This field is where you will select the date of the assessment.
* **Student Progress 1** (`student_progress_1`, `number`, optional): Enter the student's progress in the subject or skill being assessed, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Student Proficiency Assessment 3** (`student_proficiency_assessment_3`, `text`, optional): This field is for any additional comments or feedback about the student's proficiency level.

## Tips
* Be sure to provide clear and constructive feedback to the student, using the provided options for Feedback 1.
* Use the Improvement Plan 1 field to suggest specific steps for improvement.
* Use the Student Progress 1 field to track the student's progress over time.
