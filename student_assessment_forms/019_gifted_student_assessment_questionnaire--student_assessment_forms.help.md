# Gifted Student Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gifted Student Assessment Questionnaire is used to collect demographic information from students to identify their giftedness and provide targeted support. The data collected will help inform educational programming and resource allocation decisions at the district or school level.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete the form carefully and accurately. If you are unsure about a particular question, please ask your teacher or school administrator for guidance.
2. Please answer all required questions. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. If you have any accommodations or disabilities that you think would be helpful for your teacher or school to know, please include this information in the "Accommodations" field.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your teacher or school administrator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What is your name? (<name>)

*   Your name is the name you would like to be known by.
*   Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
*   For example: Jane Doe

### 2. What is your title?

*   Your title is the honorific or prefix associated with your name, such as Mr., Mrs., or Ms.
*   Please enter your title as it appears on your official documents.
*   For example: Mr. or Dr.

### 3. Student's grade level?

*   This question is asking for your current grade level in school.
*   Please enter the number of the grade you are currently in (e.g. 9 for 9th grade).
*   For example: 9

### 4. What is your ethnicity?

*   This question is asking for your racial or ethnic group identity.
*   Please select all that apply:
	+ Asian
	+ African American
	+ Hispanic or Latino
	+ Native American
	+ White
*   For example: Asian and Hispanic or Latino

### 5. What language do you use at home?

*   This question is asking for the language you use most frequently at home.
*   Please select one of the following options:
	+ English
	+ Spanish
	+ French
	+ Chinese
	+ Other
*   For example: English

### 6. Do you have any disability status?

*   This question is asking for your current disability status.
*   Please select all that apply:
	+ True
	+ False
*   For example: True

### 7. What are your accommodation needs?

*   This question is asking for any accommodations you may need to access educational resources.
*   Please enter any information about accommodations you may need in the text field below.
*   For example: Extra time to complete assignments

### 8. Who is your primary caregiver?

*   This question is asking for the person responsible for your daily care and well-being.
*   Please enter the name and relationship of your primary caregiver.
*   For example: Mother

### 9. Who is your teacher?

*   This question is asking for your current teacher's name.
*   Please enter the name of your current teacher.
*   For example: Ms. Johnson

### 10. What language do you speak?

*   This question is asking for the language you speak most frequently.
*   Please select one of the following options:
	+ English
	+ Spanish
	+ French
	+ Chinese
	+ Other
*   For example: English
