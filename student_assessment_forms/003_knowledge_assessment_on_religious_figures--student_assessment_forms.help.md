# Knowledge Assessment On Religious Figures - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and assess students' knowledge of religious figures, helping educators evaluate their understanding and identify areas where they may need additional support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `full_name` field with your name as it appears on your record.
2. Answer the `question` field with your response to the assessment question.
3. Select the correct answer from the `option_a`, `option_b`, `option_c`, etc. fields, depending on the specific question.
4. Optionally, provide a `date` and `time` for the assessment.
5. If necessary, enter a `note` to provide additional context or information.
6. Enter your email and phone number (optional) to allow us to contact you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **full_name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your record.
* **question** (`question`, `text`, optional): Answer the assessment question to the best of your ability.
* **answer** (`answer`, `text`, optional): Enter your response to the assessment question.
* **option_a** (`option_a`, `select_one`, optional): Select the correct answer from the options provided.
* **option_b** (`option_b`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all correct answers from the options provided.
* **date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the assessment (if applicable).
* **time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the assessment (if applicable).
* **note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional context or information (if necessary).
* **email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you.
* **phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you.
* **option_c**, **option_d**, **option_e**, **option_f**, **option_g**, **option_h**, **option_i**, **option_j**, **option_k**, **option_l**, **option_m**, **option_n**, **option_o**, **option_p**, **option_q** (`option_c`, `select_one`, optional): Select the correct answer from the options provided for each question.
