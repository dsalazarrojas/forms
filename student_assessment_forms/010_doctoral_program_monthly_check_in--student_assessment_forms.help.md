# Doctoral Program Monthly Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a structured and consistent way for doctoral students to report their progress, challenges, and activities on a monthly basis. This will help in tracking and monitoring progress, identifying areas of improvement, and facilitating the advisor-student communication and support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and understand the questions and fields provided.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Provide detailed answers where applicable.
4. Review your answers before submitting for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Monthly Progress** (`monthly_progress`, text, required: false): Describe your monthly progress in your doctoral program, including any notable achievements, milestones, or challenges you have faced.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, text, required: false): List any challenges or difficulties you have faced during the month, including any obstacles or hindrances that impacted your progress.
* **Goals** (`goals`, text, required: false): Outline your short-term and long-term goals for the next month, including any specific targets or objectives you aim to achieve.
* **Research Activities** (`research_activities`, text, required: false): Describe any research-related activities you have undertaken during the month, including any experiments, data collections, or literature reviews.
* **Goals And Objectives** (`goals_and_objectives`, text, required: false): Outline any specific goals and objectives you have set for yourself, including any research questions or hypotheses you aim to explore.
* **Research Goals** (`research_goals`, text, required: false): Outline any research goals or objectives you have set for yourself, including any specific research questions or hypotheses you aim to investigate.
* **Research Areas Of Focus** (`research_areas_of_focus`, text, required: false): Describe the research areas or topics you are currently focusing on, including any specific themes or areas of interest.
* **Challenges And Roadblocks** (`challenges_and_roadblocks`, text, required: false): List any challenges or roadblocks you have faced in your research, including any obstacles or hindrances that impacted your progress.
* **Communication With Advisor** (`communication_with_advisor`, text, required: false): Describe your communication with your advisor, including any meetings, discussions, or correspondence.
* **Communication With Colleagues** (`communication_with_colleagues`, text, required: false): Describe your communication with your colleagues, including any collaborations, discussions, or team meetings.
* **Research Support Resources** (`research_support_resources`, text, required: false): List any research support resources you have accessed, including any grants, funding, or research services.
* **Research Support Network** (`research_support_network`, text, required: false): Describe any research support network you have established, including any mentorship, peer review, or research groups.
* **Travel Plans** (`travel_plans`, text, required: false): Outline any travel plans you have for the next month, including any conferences, workshops, or research-related trips.
* **Research Grants** (`research_grants`, text, required: false): Describe any research grants or funding you have received, including any specific funding sources or amounts.
* **Research Awards** (`research_awards`, text, required: false): List any research awards or recognition you have received, including any specific awards or honors.
* **Research Publications** (`research_publications`, text, required: false): Describe any research publications you have authored or co-authored, including any specific journals, conferences, or books.
* **Peer-Reviewed Publications** (`peer_reviewed_publications`, text, required: false): Describe any peer-reviewed publications you have authored or co-authored, including any specific journals or conferences.
* **Presentations And Papers Delivered** (`presentations_and_papers_delivered`, text, required: false): Outline any presentations or papers you have delivered, including any specific talks, posters, or workshops.
* **Conferences Attended** (`conferences_attended`, text, required: false): Describe any conferences or workshops you have attended, including any specific events or venues.
* **Research Outreach Activities** (`research_outreach_activities`, text, required: false): List any research outreach activities you have undertaken, including any public engagement, community service, or knowledge transfer.
* **Community Engagement** (`community_engagement`, text, required: false): Describe any community engagement activities you have undertaken, including any public speaking, volunteer work, or community service.
* **Research Support Services** (`research_support_services`, text, required: false): List any research support services you have accessed, including any specific services or resources.
* **Research Colleagues And Collaborators** (`research_colleagues_and_collaborators`, text, required: false): Describe any research colleagues or collaborators you have worked with, including any specific researchers, institutions, or projects.
* **Research Collaborators** (`research_collaborators`, text, required: false): List any research collaborators you have worked with, including any specific researchers, institutions, or projects.
* **Other** (`other`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information that does not fit in any of the above categories.
