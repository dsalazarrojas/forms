# Creative Assessment Research Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative Assessment Research Application Form is designed to collect information from students about their research interests, backgrounds, and contact details. This form helps us assess their suitability for a research opportunity and ensures we can follow up with them effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `Research Project` field with a brief description of your research project.
2. Provide your `Background Information`, including any relevant experience or skills you've gained in the field.
3. Indicate your `Research Interests` by selecting the relevant options (e.g., Yes or No).
4. Enter your `Contact Details`, including your name, email address, phone number, and other relevant contact information.
5. Choose your preferred `Contact Preference` (e.g., Yes or No).
6. Confirm your consent to be contacted by selecting Yes or No in the `Consent` field.
7. You can optionally upload a file to support your application.
8. If required, provide an `Application Form` with additional information.
9. (Optional) Enter the date and time of your consent in the `Consent Date` and `Consent Time` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Project** (`research_project`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your research project, highlighting your research interests and goals.
* **Background Information** (`background_information`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of your relevant experience, skills, or education in the field.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): Enter your name, email address, and phone number to ensure we can contact you efficiently.
* **Research Interests** (`research_interests`, select_multiple, required): Select the research areas that interest you, helping us understand your focus.
* **Contact Preference** (`contact_preference`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone, or in-person).
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address for us to reach out to you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Provide your phone number for additional contact.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Confirm your consent to be contacted by us.
* **Upload File** (`upload_file`, note, optional): You can upload a supporting file to supplement your application.
* **Application Form** (`application_form`, text, optional): If required, fill out this additional form with more information.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you gave your consent.
* **Consent Time** (`consent_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your consent.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* For fields with select options, choose the relevant selections that best represent your interests and preferences.
* If uploading a file, ensure it's relevant and supports your application.
* If an additional `Application Form` is required, complete it according to the provided instructions.
