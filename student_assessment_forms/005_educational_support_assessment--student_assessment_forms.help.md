# Educational Support Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Educational Support Assessment form is used to gather information about a student's educational support needs. This information will be used to provide targeted support services to the student.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
3. Select all the support needs that the student requires in the "Support Needs" field.
4. Enter the school name in the "School Name" field.
5. Enter the student's contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
6. Enter the student's contact email (if available) in the "Contact Email" field.
7. Enter the date of the last contact with the student in the "Last Contact Date" field.
8. Enter the time of the last contact with the student in the "Last Contact Time" field.
9. Enter the student's support goal in the "Support Goal" field.
10. Select the student's status from the options provided in the "Student Status" field.
11. Select all the subjects the student is studying in the "Student Subjects" field.
12. Enter any additional notes about the student in the "Note" field.
13. Enter the assigned tool for the student in the "Assigned Tool" field.
14. Enter the student's language(s) in the "Student Language" field.
15. Enter the student's English language proficiency in the "English Language" field.
16. Enter the student's native language in the "Native Language" field.
17. Enter any other languages the student speaks in the "Other Languages" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (text, required): Enter the student's name.
* **Support Needs** (select multiple, required): Select all the support needs that the student requires.
* **School Name** (text, required): Enter the school name.
* **Contact Phone** (text, required): Enter the student's contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (email, optional): Enter the student's contact email (if available).
* **Last Contact Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the last contact with the student.
* **Last Contact Time** (time, required): Enter the time of the last contact with the student.
* **Support Goal** (text, required): Enter the student's support goal.
* **Student Status** (select one, required): Select the student's status from the options provided.
* **Student Grade** (select multiple, required): Select all the subjects the student is studying.
* **Student Year** (select multiple, required): Select all the years the student is in.
* **Support Plan** (text, required): Enter any additional notes about the student.
* **Follow Up** (text, optional): Enter any follow-up plans for the student.
* **School Year** (number, optional): Enter the school year (if available).
* **Teacher Name** (text, required): Enter the student's teacher's name.
* **Student Occupation** (text, optional): Enter the student's occupation.
* **Parent/Guardian** (text, optional): Enter the student's parent/guardian's contact information.
* **Contact Address** (text, required): Enter the student's contact address.
* **Student Subjects** (text, optional): Select all the subjects the student is studying.
* **Note** (text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the student.
* **Assigned Tool** (text, optional): Enter the assigned tool for the student.
* **Student Language** (text, optional): Enter the student's language(s).
* **English Language** (text, optional): Enter the student's English language proficiency.
* **Native Language** (text, optional): Enter the student's native language.
* **Other Languages** (text, optional): Enter any other languages the student speaks.
