# Distance Between Two Points Worksheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you complete the Distance Between Two Points Worksheet, a form used to calculate the distance between two points.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the two points between which you want to calculate the distance by choosing "Point A" or "Point B" from the "Points A" dropdown menu.
2.  Enter the x and y coordinates of point A and point B in the respective fields.
3.  Choose the units you want to use for the distance calculation from the "units" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Points A** (distance_between_two_points_worksheet_points_a, dropdown, required): Choose the first point between which you want to calculate the distance.
* **x1** (distance_between_two_points_worksheet_x1, text, required): Enter the x-coordinate of point A.
* **y1** (distance_between_two_points_worksheet_y1, text, required): Enter the y-coordinate of point A.
* **x2** (distance_between_two_points_worksheet_x2, text, required): Enter the x-coordinate of point B.
* **y2** (distance_between_two_points_worksheet_y2, text, required): Enter the y-coordinate of point B.
* **Distance** (distance_between_two_points_worksheet_distance, number, required): The calculated distance between the two points.
* **Units** (distance_between_two_points_worksheet_units, text, required): Enter the units to use for the distance calculation. This field is optional but may be required depending on the form's requirements.
* **Units** (distance_between_two_points_worksheet_units_choice, select_one, required): Choose the units to use for the distance calculation, which may be one of the following: miles, kilometers, feet, or centimeters.

## Tips
