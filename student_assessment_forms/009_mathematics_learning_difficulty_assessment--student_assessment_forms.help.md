# Mathematics Learning Difficulty Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mathematics Learning Difficulty Assessment is a tool to identify and address students' mathematics learning difficulties. By gathering information about students' experiences and progress, this assessment helps teachers and educators better understand individual students' strengths and challenges in mathematics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the requested information about your mathematics learning experiences. Please answer each question honestly, as the information you provide will help us better understand your needs and provide more effective support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information**: This field is not required but provides a way to capture any additional information about the student.
* **Math Challenge**: Please select whether you have faced any math challenges (Yes or No).
* **Other Math Challenges**: If you have faced other math challenges not listed, please describe them in this field.
* **Other Math Challenges (text)**: This field is for you to expand on your previous answer, if needed.
* **Student Support**: Please select any student support you have received (Yes, No).
* **Teacher Support**: Please select any teacher support you have received (Yes, No).
* **Other Teacher Support**: If you have received other teacher support not listed, please describe them in this field.
* **Math Challenge Date**: Please enter the date you faced a math challenge.
* **Math Challenge Time**: Please enter the time you spent on a math challenge.
* **Student Assessment**: Please rate your current mathematics assessment (on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is unsatisfactory and 5 is excellent).
* **Teacher Assessment**: Please rate your teacher's current assessment of your mathematics progress (on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is unsatisfactory and 5 is excellent).
* **Student Progress**: Please rate your current progress in mathematics (on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is unsatisfactory and 5 is excellent).
* **Teacher Progress**: Please rate your teacher's assessment of your progress in mathematics (on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is unsatisfactory and 5 is excellent).
* **Student Outcomes**: Please select your current student outcomes (Satisfactory, Marginal, or Unsatisfactory).

Note: Please be honest when answering these questions, as the information provided will help us better understand your needs and provide targeted support.
