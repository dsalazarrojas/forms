# Play Comprehension Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students after they have watched or experienced a play. It will help educators or producers gain insights into how their play was received, including the title and type of play, the audience's reaction, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with your honest and thoughtful feedback.
2.  Be as detailed as possible in your answers.
3.  Make sure to select the correct options for each field as per the labels.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Play Title** (`play_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the play you have watched or experienced.
* **Play Type** (`play_type`, text, required): Identify the genre, category, or type of the play (e.g. drama, comedy, etc.).
* **Audience Reaction** (`audience_reaction`, text, required): Describe how you reacted to the play. Be as detailed as possible.
* **How did the play help you use your imagination?** (`play_imagination`, text, required): Share how the play helped you use your imagination.
* **How did the play make you feel?** (`play_emotion`, text, optional): Describe how the play made you feel.
* **How much did you like the play?** (`play_liking`, number, required): Rate your liking for the play on a scale.
* **Would you recommend this play?** (`play_recommend`, select one, required): Indicate if you would recommend this play to others.
* **How much did the play help you use your imagination?** (`play_imagination_liking`, select multiple, required): Choose from options how much the play helped you use your imagination.
* **How much did the play make you feel?** (`play_emotion_liking`, select multiple, required): Choose from options how much the play made you feel.
* **What suggestions do you have for the play?** (`play_suggestion`, text, optional): Share any suggestions you have for the play.
* **Comments** (`play_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the play.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required): Enter your student ID number.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter your teacher's name.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, required): Enter your teacher's email address.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, text, optional): Enter your teacher's phone number.
* **Teacher Notes** (`teacher_notes`, note, optional): Add any notes you have for your teacher.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of submission.
* **Submission Method** (`submission_method`, select one, optional): Choose how the play was submitted to you (e.g. in-person, video, online).
* **Submission Location** (`submission_location`, select multiple, optional): Choose where the play was submitted to you (e.g. theater, school, home).
* **Submission Device** (`submission_device`, select multiple, optional): Choose what device was used to submit the play (e.g. desktop, tablet, mobile).
* **Submission Browser** (`submission_browser`, select multiple, optional): Choose what browser was used to submit the play (e.g. Chrome, Safari, Firefox).

The fields marked as "required" must be filled out before submission. The form is designed to be filled out in a way that makes it easy to understand and provide clear and concise feedback.
