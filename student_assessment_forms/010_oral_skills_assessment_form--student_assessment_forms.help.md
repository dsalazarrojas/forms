# Oral Skills Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to assess a student's oral skills, including pronunciation, fluency, interaction, and vocabulary.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions and instructions to understand what is being asked.
2. Answer each question thoroughly and honestly, using the provided answer options as a guide.
3. For each field, provide a clear and concise response that addresses the question being asked.
4. If a field is not required, you may leave it blank or skip it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pronunciation** (Pronunciation, text, optional): This field is used to evaluate the student's ability to pronounce words correctly. Provide a brief description of the student's pronunciation.
* **Fluency** (Fluency, text, optional): This field is used to assess the student's ability to speak smoothly and coherently. Describe the student's ability to convey ideas and information effectively.
* **Interaction** (Interaction, select_one, optional): This field is used to evaluate the student's interaction skills, such as their ability to engage in conversation. Choose one of the provided options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Interaction 2** (Interaction 2, select_one, optional): This field is similar to Interaction, where you evaluate the student's interaction skills again, focusing on a different aspect.
* **Comprehension** (Comprehension, text, optional): This field is used to assess the student's ability to understand spoken language. Provide a brief description of the student's comprehension skills.
* **Vocabulary** (Vocabulary, text, optional): This field is used to evaluate the student's vocabulary skills, such as their ability to use correct words and expressions. Describe the student's vocabulary skills.
* **Note** (Note, note, optional): This field is a free-text field where you can add any additional comments or notes about the student's oral skills.
* **Vocabulary_2** (Vocabulary_2, text, optional): This field is similar to Vocabulary, where you evaluate the student's vocabulary skills again, focusing on a different aspect.
* **Vocabulary_3** (Vocabulary_3, text, optional): This field is similar to Vocabulary 2, where you evaluate the student's vocabulary skills again, focusing on a different aspect.
* **Extra question** (Extra question, text, optional): This field is a free-text field where you can ask any additional questions or provide any extra comments about the student's oral skills.

## Tips

* Be specific and concrete when providing feedback on each field.
* Use the provided answer options as a guide, but feel free to add any additional comments or suggestions.
* If a field is not required, prioritize providing feedback on the fields that are most relevant to the student's oral skills.
