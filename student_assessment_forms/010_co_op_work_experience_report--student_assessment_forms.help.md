# Co Op Work Experience Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Co Op Work Experience Report is a form used to collect information from students about their work experience during their co-op terms. This form helps track student performance, skills gained, and feedback from supervisors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your full name as it appears on your university or college records.
2. Enter your university or college ID number.
3. Provide your email address associated with your educational institution.
4. Enter your contact phone number.
5. Select your current program or major of study.
6. Select the year you are currently in (e.g., First Year, Second Year, Third Year, etc.).
7. Enter the name of the company or organization where you worked.
8. Enter the full name of your direct supervisor.
9. Enter your supervisor's contact email address.
10. Enter your supervisor's contact phone number (optional).
11. Select your official job title.
12. Enter the department you worked in (optional).
13. Enter the start date of your co-op term.
14. Enter the end date of your co-op term.
15. Select the average number of hours you worked per week.
16. Enter the total hours you worked during the co-op term.
17. Describe your primary job responsibilities.
18. List the new skills you developed during the co-op term.
19. List any classroom skills you applied during the co-op term (optional).
20. Describe major projects you worked on during the co-op term (optional).
21. Rate the support from your supervisor (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
22. Rate the work environment (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
23. Rate the overall learning experience (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
24. Describe any challenges you faced during the co-op term (optional).
25. List any key accomplishments you are proud of.
26. Explain how this experience impacted your career goals (optional).
27. Select if you would return to this employer (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Not Sure, Probably No, or Definitely No).
28. Select if you would recommend this co-op experience to other students (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Not Sure, Probably No, or Definitely No).
29. Provide feedback for the school coordinator (optional).
30. Add any additional comments or reflections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Your full legal name as it appears on your university or college records.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id`, `text`, required): Your university or college ID number.
* **School Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Your educational institution email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Your contact phone number.
* **Program or Major** (`program_name`, `text`, required): Your field of study.
* **Year of Study** (`year_of_study`, `select_one`, required): Select the year you are currently in (e.g., First Year, Second Year, Third Year, etc.).
* **Employer or Company Name** (`employer_name`, `text`, required): Name of organization where you worked.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, `text`, required): Full name of your direct supervisor.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, `email`, required): Contact email for your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, `text`, optional): Contact phone for your supervisor.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Your official title at the company.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional): Which department did you work in.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): When did your co-op begin.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required): When did your co-op end.
* **Hours Per Week** (`hours_per_week`, `select_one`, required): Select the average weekly hours you worked.
* **Total Hours Completed** (`total_hours`, `number`, required): Enter the total hours you worked during the co-op term.
* **Main Job Responsibilities** (`job_responsibilities`, `text`, required): Describe your primary duties.
* **New Skills Learned** (`skills_learned`, `text`, required): List the new skills you developed.
* **Skills Applied from School** (`skills_applied`, `text`, optional): List any classroom skills you applied during the co-op term.
* **Key Projects Completed** (`project_description`, `text`, optional): Describe major projects you worked on.
* **Supervisor Support Rating** (`supervisor_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the support from your supervisor (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Work Environment Rating** (`work_environment_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the workplace environment (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Learning Experience Rating** (`learning_experience_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the overall learning value (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Challenges Faced** (`challenges_faced`, `text`, optional): Describe any difficulties you encountered.
* **Key Accomplishments** (`accomplishments`, `text`, optional): List any key accomplishments you are proud of.
* **How Did This Impact Career Goals** (`career_goals_impact`, `text`, optional): Explain how this experience impacted your career goals.
* **Would You Return** (`would_return`, `select_one`, required): Select if you would return to this employer (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Not Sure, Probably No, or Definitely No).
* **Recommend to Others** (`recommend_to_others`, `select_one`, required): Select if you would recommend this co-op experience to other students (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Not Sure, Probably No, or Definitely No).
* **Feedback for School Coordinator** (`school_coordinator_feedback`, `text`, optional): Provide feedback for your academic advisor.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any other reflections or feedback.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are not duplicating information already provided in other assessments or reports.
* Be honest and accurate when providing feedback and ratings.
* Describe any challenges or difficulties you faced clearly.
* List any key accomplishments or skills gained.
* Select the most accurate rating for each field.
* Provide any additional comments or reflections that may be helpful for the school or future students.
