# Unit Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help students assess their knowledge on a unit of study. The goal is to gather feedback from students on their understanding of the unit material.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question based on your knowledge of the unit.
2. For each question, rate your confidence level using the following scale:
	* Not at all confident
	* A little confident
	* Somewhat confident
	* Confident
	* Highly confident
3. If you are unsure of the answer, please answer based on your best guess.
4. You can add any additional notes or comments in the "Submission Notes" field.
