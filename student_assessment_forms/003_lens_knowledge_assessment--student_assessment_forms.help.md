# Lens Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lens Knowledge Assessment form is designed to evaluate a student's understanding of lens concepts and their practical applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "General Information" page, which provides a brief overview of the assessment.
2. Move on to the "Confidence Level" page, where you will select your level of confidence regarding the understanding of lens concepts.
3. On the "Real-Life Applications" page, choose the types of applications where lenses are used.
4. Finally, on the "Optical Lenses" page, provide a brief description of a specific type of lens.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (page 1): This section provides an introduction to the assessment and is not a required field.
* **Confidence Level** (page 2, label: confidence Level): This field allows you to indicate your level of confidence regarding the understanding of lens concepts. Choose one of the two options: High or Low.
* **Real-Life Applications** (page 3, label: Real-Life Applications): This field asks you to identify the types of applications where lenses are used. Select all that apply from the list.
* **Optical Lenses** (page 4): This is a text field where you can describe a specific type of lens. Be as brief and clear as possible in your description. 

## Tips
- Be honest in your assessment and answer the questions to the best of your abilities.
- Make sure to select all applicable real-life applications of lenses.
- Keep the description of the optical lens brief and concise.
