# Final Assessment For Drawing Course - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluate the performance of students in the Final Drawing Course. It is intended to assess the student's artwork, reflection, and overall performance in the course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you are familiar with the content and instructions.
2. For each field, enter your answer or upload your artwork as required.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields, if any.
4. Submit your form once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Reflections** (`student_reflections`, note, optional): This field is for students to reflect on their learning experience, thoughts, and feelings about the course. Please provide a written response to this field.
* **Instructor Evaluation** (`instructor_evaluation`, select_one, optional): In this field, the instructor will select their evaluation of your artwork from the options: Excellent, Good, Poor.
* **Uploaded Artwork** (`uploaded_artwork`, text, optional): If you have uploaded an artwork that you would like to submit as part of your final assessment, this is the field for it.
* **Final Assessment** (`final_assessment`, select_multiple, optional): The instructor will select multiple evaluation criteria for your final artwork from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Student Comments** (`student_comments`, text, optional): This field is for students to provide comments or feedback about their experience in the course.
* **Instructor Comments** (`instructor_comments`, text, optional): The instructor will add comments about your performance in the course.
