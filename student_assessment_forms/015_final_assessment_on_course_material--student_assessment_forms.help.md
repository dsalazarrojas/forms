# Final Assessment On Course Material - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Final Assessment On Course Material form is designed to gather feedback from students after they have completed a course. This form helps us understand students' perceptions of the course's overall quality, their takeaways, and their ratings of the course material. Please complete this form to provide valuable insights that will help us improve our courses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reading through the form to understand the questions and fields.
2. Answer each field according to your honest assessment of the course.
3. Fill in all the fields, as each one contributes to a complete understanding of your experience with the course.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Overview** (`course_overview`, text, optional): Describe the overall structure and content of the course. This field is for you to provide a brief summary of what the course covered.
* **Key Takeaways** (`key_takeaways`, text, optional): Write down the most important points you took away from the course. This will help us understand what you found valuable and what you can apply in your daily life.
* **Course Effectiveness** (`course_effectiveness`, number, optional): Rate the course on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not effective" and 5 is "very effective". This is subjective and reflects your assessment of how well the course met your needs.
* **Assessment Questions** (`assessment_questions`, select multiple, optional): Select the questions that best reflect your assessment of the course. We use this to gauge your overall satisfaction and what aspects of the course need improvement.
* **Comments** (`course_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions about the course, use this field to provide them. We value any feedback that can help us improve our courses.
* **Course Rating** (`course_rating`, select one, optional): Rate the course on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "very poor" and 5 is "excellent". This rating is used to get an overall sense of how well the course met your expectations.
* **E-mail** (`email`, email, optional): If you want to be contacted for further discussion or questions, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you want to be contacted for further discussion or questions, please provide your phone number.

## Tips
- Please answer each field honestly and to the best of your ability.
- If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to us and we'll be happy to help.
- If you want to be contacted for further discussion or questions, please make sure to fill in your email or phone number.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them in the "Comments" field.
