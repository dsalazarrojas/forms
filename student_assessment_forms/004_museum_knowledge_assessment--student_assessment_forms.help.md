# Museum Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Museum Knowledge Assessment form is designed to test a user's knowledge and understanding of a museum visit. This form will help museum staff evaluate the effectiveness of their educational program and assess the engagement of museum visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all required questions (indicated by *) to the best of your ability.
2. Enter your user ID or other unique identifier in the User Input field for tracking purposes.
3. Select the correct answer from the Quiz Questions section.
4. Enter your response to the User Response question, if applicable.
5. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number, if provided.
6. Provide the date and time of your visit, if known.

**User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter a unique identifier or code that can be used to track your responses. This field is not required but will help museum staff keep track of your responses.
