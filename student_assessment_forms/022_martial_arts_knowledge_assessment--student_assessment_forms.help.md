# Martial Arts Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The martial arts knowledge assessment form is a self-assessment tool designed to help martial arts students, instructors, and practitioners evaluate their understanding of various aspects of martial arts training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions honestly and accurately based on your knowledge and experience.
2. Choose the correct options for each question, or type your answer if a text field is required.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Dojos (1)
* **Dojos**: This field asks for the name of the dojo where you typically train or practice martial arts. If you train in multiple dojos, list each one separately.
### Instructors (2)
* **Instructors**: This field asks you to select the instructors who have taught or currently teach you. Choose from the options: 1st Dan, 2nd Dan, 3rd Dan, 4th Dan, or 5th Dan.
### Training Programs (3)
* **Training Programs**: This field is for you to describe your current or typical training program(s). Be as detailed as possible.
### Students (4)
* **Students**: This field asks you to select the level of students you typically train with. Choose from: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
### Rank (5)
* **Rank**: This field asks for your current rank in martial arts. If you don't have a rank, or if you're unsure, leave this field blank.
### Martial Arts Concepts (6)
* **Martial Arts Concepts**: This field is for you to describe your understanding of basic martial arts concepts, such as techniques, philosophies, or history. Be as detailed as possible.
### Better Class Placement (7)
* **Better Class Placement**: This field asks you to indicate whether you think you could benefit from more challenging or easier classes. Choose: True or False.
### Instruction Planning (8)
* **Instruction Planning**: This field asks you to indicate whether you would like to receive more guidance on instruction planning or if you're able to plan your own classes. Choose: True or False.
### Experience (9)
* **Experience**: This field is for you to describe your experience in martial arts, including any specific experiences that have helped you grow as a martial artist.
### Years of Experience (10)
* **Years of Experience**: This field asks for the number of years you've been training in martial arts.

### Tips
* Please be honest and accurate in your answers.
* Take your time and review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about an answer, don't hesitate to ask your instructor or seek guidance.
