# Proportional Detector Understanding Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from instructors or assessors on their scoring practices and confidence levels for different score ranges.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions and provide any relevant information or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the Proportional Detector Understanding Quiz** (`intro_question`): This is a text field where you can provide a brief description of the proportional detector understanding quiz.
* **How many points do you award for a 5 point score** (`score_5_point`): Please enter the number of points you award for a score of 5 points. For example, if you award 1 point for every 5 points scored.
* **Confidence for 5 point score** (`confidence_5_point`): Please describe your confidence level for scoring 5 points. For example, "High" or "Medium".
* **How many points do you award for a 10 point score** (`score_10_point`): Please enter the number of points you award for a score of 10 points.
* **Confidence for 10 point score** (`confidence_10_point`): Please describe your confidence level for scoring 10 points.
* **How many points do you award for a 20 point score** (`score_20_point`): Please enter the number of points you award for a score of 20 points.
* **Confidence for 20 point score** (`confidence_20_point`): Please describe your confidence level for scoring 20 points.
* **How many points do you award for a 30 point score** (`score_30_point`): Please enter the number of points you award for a score of 30 points.
* **Confidence for 30 point score** (`confidence_30_point`): Please describe your confidence level for scoring 30 points.
* **How many points do you award for a 40 point score** (`score_40_point`): Please enter the number of points you award for a score of 40 points.
* **Confidence for 40 point score** (`confidence_40_point`): Please describe your confidence level for scoring 40 points.
* **Submission comments** (`submission_comments`): This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback on the submission.

Note: Please provide concrete, specific answers for each field, and use the provided examples as guidance. If you're unsure about a field, you can describe your thought process or reasoning behind your answer.
