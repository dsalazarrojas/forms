# Aviation Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Aviation Knowledge Assessment form is a tool designed to help students evaluate their knowledge and skills in various aviation-related areas. It is intended to be completed by students to track their progress and identify areas where they need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer every question to the best of your ability.
2. For select_one fields, choose the rating that best describes your confidence level or rating.
3. For select_multiple fields, choose all the categories that apply.
4. For text fields, provide a brief answer to the question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name (1)**: Your complete name as it appears on your student ID.
* **Student ID Number (2)**: Your student identification number.
* **Email Address (3)**: Your school or personal email address.
* **Course Name (4)**: Select the course you are currently taking (e.g. Private Pilot Ground School).
* **Instructor Name (5)**: The name of your current instructor.
* **Assessment Date (6)**: The date you are completing this assessment.
* **Assessment Type (7)**: Choose the type of assessment you are completing (e.g. Pre-test, Post-test).
* **Total Flight Hours (8)**: Your current total flight hours (optional).
* **Current Pilot Certificate (9)**: Select your current pilot certification level (e.g. Private Pilot).
* **Aircraft Category Ratings (10)**: Select all categories of aircraft you are rated for (e.g. Single-engine land, Multi-engine sea).
* **Federal Aviation Regulations Rating (11)**: Rate your knowledge of Federal Aviation Regulations.
* **Aircraft Systems Knowledge Rating (12)**: Rate your knowledge of aircraft systems.
* **Weather Knowledge Rating (13)**: Rate your knowledge of meteorology.
* **Navigation Knowledge Rating (14)**: Rate your knowledge of navigation.
* **Performance and Limitations Rating (15)**: Rate your knowledge in this area.
* **Emergency Procedures Rating (16)**: Rate your knowledge of emergency procedures.
* **Hours Studied Per Week (17)**: Average weekly study time (optional).
* **Preferred Learning Style (18)**: Choose how you learn best (e.g. Visual, Auditory).
* **Overall Confidence Level (19)**: Choose how confident you feel about the material.
* **Areas Needing Improvement (20)**: Briefly describe areas where you need improvement.
* **Additional Resources Needed (21)**: Suggest any additional resources that would help you learn.
* **Instructor Feedback Request (22)**: Choose whether you would like instructor feedback.
* **Aviation Goals (23)**: Briefly describe your aviation career goals.

Note: This guide is not a formal evaluation tool, but rather a tool to support students in their learning journey.
