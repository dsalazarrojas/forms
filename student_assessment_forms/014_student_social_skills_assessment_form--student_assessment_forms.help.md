**Student Social Skills Assessment Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Student Social Skills Assessment Form is a tool to evaluate students' social skills, such as communication, teamwork, and conflict resolution, to help teachers assess their strengths and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
3. Select the school year of the student in the "School Year" field.
4. Enter a unique student ID number in the "Student ID" field.
5. Select the student's class or group in the "Student Class" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (student_name)**: Enter the student's full name as it appears on their school records.
* **Teacher Name (teacher_name)**: Enter the name of the teacher who is completing this assessment.
* **School Year (school_year)**: Select the current school year of the student (e.g., 9, 10, 11, etc.).
* **Student ID (student_id)**: Enter a unique student ID number as assigned by the school.
* **Student Class (student_class)**: Select the student's class or group (e.g., 6th, 7th, 8th, etc.). 

## Tips

* Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
* If you have any questions or concerns while filling out this form, please contact your school administration.

Note: This form is designed for internal use only and should not be shared with anyone outside of the school.
