# University Honors Program Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Honors Program Assessment is a form designed to evaluate students' progress and experiences in the honors program. This form helps the honors program directors and staff to assess students' performance, identify areas of improvement, and provide guidance for future progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Provide Student Feedback**: In the "Student Feedback" section, please provide a brief description of your experience in the honors program. This can include any notable achievements, challenges you've faced, and suggestions for improvement.
2. **Share Honors Experience**: In the "Honors Experience" section, please describe your experiences, achievements, and growth within the honors program. This will help us understand your overall experience and how the program has impacted you.
3. **Evaluate Impact**: In the "Impact Evaluation" section, please provide a specific date for when you completed a project, task, or activity that demonstrates your impact on the program. This will help us assess your progress and growth.
4. **Add Additional Comments**: In the "Additional Comments" section, please provide any additional information or feedback you'd like to share about your experience in the honors program.
5. **Enter Email and Phone Number**: In the "Email" and "Phone" sections, please enter your valid email address and phone number, respectively. This will help us contact you for further discussion or clarification.
6. **Assign Tool**: In the "Assigned Tool" section, please select the tool that was assigned to you for the project or activity. This can be either "Chatjimmy" or "Other" depending on the assigned tool.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Feedback (text, optional)
* **Student Feedback**: A brief description of your experience in the honors program. 

### Honors Experience (text, optional)
* **Honors Experience**: Describe your experiences, achievements, and growth within the honors program. 

### Impact Evaluation (date, optional)
* **Impact Evaluation**: A specific date for when you completed a project, task, or activity that demonstrates your impact on the program. 

### Additional Comments (text, optional)
* **Additional Comments**: Any additional information or feedback you'd like to share about your experience in the honors program. 

### Email (email, optional)
* **Email**: Your valid email address. 

### Phone (text, optional)
* **Phone**: Your valid phone number. 

### Assigned Tool (select_one, optional)
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool that was assigned to you for the project or activity, either "Chatjimmy" or "Other".
