# Empathy And Respect Self Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Empathy And Respect Self Assessment is a form designed to help you reflect on your ability to understand and respond to others in different situations. This form is intended to be a self-assessment tool to help you evaluate your emotional intelligence and behavior in workplace or academic settings.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please read each question carefully and choose the best answer that reflects your feelings and actions in the described situations.
2. Select the options that best match your thoughts and behaviors.
3. You can skip questions that do not apply to you or your situation.
4. Take your time to answer each question truthfully, and don't worry if you're unsure about some questions.
5. Once you've completed the form, review your answers to gain insights into your areas for improvement.
