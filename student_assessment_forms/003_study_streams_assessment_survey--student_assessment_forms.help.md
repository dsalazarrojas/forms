# Study Streams Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the study streams process, which is a crucial assessment tool for evaluating the effectiveness and quality of study streams. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select all the study streams that apply to you.
2. Choose the method of study that you use most frequently.
3. Choose the teaching method you prefer.
4. Choose the format of the study streams that suits your needs best.
5. Provide any additional feedback you may have.
6. If you have any comments or suggestions, please type them in the "Feedback" field.
7. If you have any extra comments or feedback not mentioned above, please type them in the "Additional Feedback" field.
8. Finally, type your name and title in the "Assigned To" field.
