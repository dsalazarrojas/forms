# Anatomical Planes Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Anatomical Planes Assessment form is designed to evaluate a student's knowledge of the anatomical planes of the body. It helps teachers assess the student's understanding of the subject matter and provide feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student's full name in the "Student name" field.
2. If you have a student ID number, enter it in the "Student ID" field. This is optional.
3. Answer the questions "Identify the plane that divides the body into left and right" and "Which plane is also called frontal" by selecting the correct option from the dropdown menu.
4. Rate your confidence in your answer from 1 to 5 in the "Confidence rating" field.
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Instructor notes" field.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you agree to have your results recorded.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): Enter your institutional ID number if applicable.
* **Identify the plane that divides the body into left and right** (`question_1`, select_one, required): Choose the plane that correctly divides the body into left and right regions.
* **Which plane is also called frontal** (`question_2`, select_one, required): Choose the plane that is also known as frontal.
* **Confidence rating** (`confidence_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate your confidence in your answer from 1 to 5.
* **Instructor notes** (`instructor_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **I agree to have my results recorded** (`consent`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you agree to have your results recorded.
