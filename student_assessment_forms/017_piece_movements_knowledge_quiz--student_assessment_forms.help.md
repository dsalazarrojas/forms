# piece_movements_knowledge_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "piece_movements_knowledge_quiz" form is designed to collect data about chess piece movements for educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form sections carefully and enter the corresponding information for each field.
2. Each section is a separate part of the quiz, and you will be asked a question related to a specific piece movement.
3. Use your knowledge of chess pieces' movements to answer each question correctly.
4. Enter the required information for each question, and submit your answers to review them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **chess_piece_moves**: This field is not applicable in this form as it is the title of the form. Please, focus on the other fields.
* **chess_piece_name** (piece_name): Enter the name of the chess piece you are being asked about.
* **chess_piece_moved_by** (piece_moved_by): Enter the piece that moved from the starting position.
* **chess_board_position** (board_position): Enter the starting position of the piece on the chessboard.
* **chess_piece_moved_to** (piece_moved_to): Enter the position of the piece after it moved.
* **time_moved** (time_moved): Enter the amount of time the piece took to move.
* **chess_piece_moved_from** (piece_moved_from): Enter the starting position of the piece.
* **chess_board_size** (board_size): Enter the size of the chessboard (e.g., 8x8).
* **chess_clock** (clock): Enter the time when the piece moved.
* **chess_piece_moved_to_square** (piece_moved_to_square): Enter the ending position of the piece on the chessboard.
* **chess_board_state** (board_state): This field is not necessary for the quiz.
* **chess_clockwise** (clockwise): Enter if the piece moved in a clockwise direction.
* **chess_piece_moved_alongsquare** (piece_moved_alongsquare): Enter if the piece moved along a square or diagonally.

Note: The provided information and answers will be used solely for the purpose of this quiz, and they do not affect any real-world outcomes or consequences.
