# Geotechnical Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Geotechnical Knowledge Assessment form is designed to help instructors or assessors evaluate the geotechnical knowledge of students working on projects. This form is meant to gather essential information about the student's background, experience, skills, and education to provide a comprehensive assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your education details in the "education" field.
2. Describe your experience in the "experience" field.
3. List your technical skills in the "technical skills" field.
4. Select your university from the list of options provided in the "university" field.
5. Choose the relevant training provider(s) for your work in the "training provider" field.
6. Select the engineering firm you are working with in the "engineering firm" field.
7. Enter your Form ID number in the "Form ID" field.
8. Provide a brief description of your work in the "description" field.
9. List the tool assigned to you in the "assigned tool" field.
10. Upload your output file in the "output file" field.
11. Add any relevant notes in the "notes" field.
12. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
13. Provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
14. Enter the date of your assessment in the "date" field.
15. Enter the time of your assessment in the "time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **education**: Please enter your educational background, including any relevant degrees or certifications.
* **experience**: Briefly describe your relevant work experience.
* **technical skills**: List your relevant technical skills.
* **university**: Select the university where you studied geotechnics.
* **training provider**: Choose any relevant training providers for your work.
* **engineering firm**: Select the engineering firm you are working with.
* **Form ID**: Enter the unique ID provided by your instructor or assessor.
* **description**: Provide a brief description of your work or project.
* **assigned tool**: List the tool assigned to you for your work.
* **output file**: Upload your completed work or output file.
* **notes**: Add any relevant notes or comments.
* **email**: Enter your email address for communication.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number for contact.
* **date**: Enter the date of your assessment.
* **time**: Enter the time of your assessment.
