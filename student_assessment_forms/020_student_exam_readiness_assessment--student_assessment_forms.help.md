# Student Exam Readiness Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Exam Readiness Assessment form is used to evaluate a student's readiness for an exam. This form helps educators assess whether a student is prepared for an exam and identify areas where they may need extra help.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's ID to identify who is being assessed.
2. Enter the student's name and email address.
3. Choose the exam date and time.
4. Select whether the student has type (e.g., "Yes" or "No") and the type of exam they will be taking.
5. Choose whether the student has difficulty with certain subjects (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
6. Select whether the student needs help with certain subjects (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
7. Enter any additional comments about the student's readiness.
8. Enter the name of the person submitting the assessment.
9. Enter the date and time the form was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID** (`student_id`, Number, Optional): Enter the student's ID number for easy identification.
* **Name** (`name`, Text, Optional): Enter the student's name.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Exam Date** (`exam_date`, Date, Optional): Choose the date of the exam.
* **Exam Type** (`exam_type`, Select One, Optional): Select whether the student has type (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Subject** (`subject`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose whether the student has difficulty with certain subjects (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Difficulty** (`difficulty`, Select One, Optional): Select whether the student has difficulty with certain subjects (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Needs Help** (`need_help`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select whether the student needs help with certain subjects (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments about the student's readiness.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the assessment.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, Date, Optional): Enter the date and time the form was submitted.
