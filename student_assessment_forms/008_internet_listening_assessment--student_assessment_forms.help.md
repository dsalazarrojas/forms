# Internet Listening Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess a student's online habits, skills, and goals related to the internet. It aims to collect data on a student's online presence, including their online habits, internet skills, and goals, to help educators and parents understand their internet usage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and understand the context.
2. Select the correct answer for each question based on your online habits and goals.
3. If a question does not apply to you, please select the option that says "No."
4. Provide the required information for each question.
5. Take breaks if needed, and it is recommended to take approximately [insert time] to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Online Habits** (`online_habits`, `select_multiple`, required: true)
Please select all the online habits that apply to you.

*   Yes/No: Select "Yes" for each online habit that you frequently do, and "No" for each that you do not.

### 2. **Internet Skills** (`internet_skills`, `select_one`, required: true)
Please select the internet skills that you possess.

*   Yes/No: Select "Yes" for each internet skill that you are familiar with, and "No" for each that you are not.

### 3. **Challenges** (`challenges`, `text`, required: false)
Please describe any challenges you face while using the internet.

*   This question is free-text, so you can write your answer in the space provided.

### 4. **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false)
Please provide any additional comments or information about your online habits and goals.

*   This question is free-text, so you can write your answer in the space provided.

### 5. **Internet Time Spent** (`internet_time_spent`, `number`, required: true)
Please enter the total amount of time you spend on the internet per day.

*   Please enter a number that represents the total time you spend on the internet per day.

### 6. **Average Time Per Day** (`average_time_per_day`, `number`, required: true)
Please enter the average amount of time you spend on the internet per day.

*   Please enter a number that represents the average time you spend on the internet per day.

### 7. **Online Communities** (`online_communities`, `select_multiple`, required: true)
Please select all the online communities you are a part of.

*   Yes/No: Select "Yes" for each online community that you are a part of, and "No" for each that you are not.

### 8. **Online Sources** (`online_sources`, `select_multiple`, required: true)
Please select all the online sources you use.

*   Yes/No: Select "Yes" for each online source that you use, and "No" for each that you do not.

### 9. **Internet Goals** (`internet_goals`, `select_one`, required: true)
Please select the internet goal that best describes your goals.

*   Yes/No: Select "Yes" for each internet goal that describes your goals, and "No" for each that does not.

### 10-13. **Other Internet Goals** (`internet_goals_other`, `select_one`, required: false)
These questions are for other internet goals that do not fit in the previous categories. Please select the option that applies.

*   Yes/No: Select "Yes" for each other internet goal that describes your goals, and "No" for each that does not.

### 14-20. **Other Internet Goals 2-15** (`internet_goals_other_text`, `text`, required: false)
Please describe other internet goals that are not listed.

*   This question is free-text, so you can write your answer in the space provided.

## Tips

*   Please take your time to read each question carefully and understand the context.
*   If you are unsure or do not understand any question, please ask for help.
*   Please select the correct answer based on your online habits and goals.
*   This form is intended to help you reflect on your online habits and goals, so take your time and do not rush through it.
