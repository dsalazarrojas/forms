# Concept Review Crossword Puzzle - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and assessment from students on the concept review crossword puzzle activity. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and help us improve our teaching materials.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your submission details, including your unique submission identifier, date of submission, and student identification number.
2. Provide your full name and email address for contact purposes.
3. Enter the name of the course and course code (if applicable).
4. Select the topic of the puzzle and rate its difficulty level.
5. Enter the time it took you to complete the puzzle in minutes.
6. Provide your answers to the crossword puzzle questions, separated by commas.
7. Indicate your level of confidence in your answers.
8. Select the concepts that were covered in the puzzle.
9. Rate the helpfulness of the puzzle for learning.
10. Indicate whether you enjoyed the activity.
11. State whether you would recommend the activity to others.
12. Provide any suggestions for improvement.
13. Add any additional comments or feedback.
14. Certify that you completed the puzzle independently (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submission ID** (1, text, required): Enter your unique submission identifier.
* **Submission Date** (2, date, required): Enter the date you submitted the puzzle.
* **Student Name** (3, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Student ID** (4, text, required): Enter your student identification number.
* **Email Address** (5, email, required): Enter your student email address.
* **Course Name** (6, text, required): Enter the name of the course.
* **Course Code** (7, text, optional): Enter the course code (if applicable).
* **Instructor Name** (8, text, required): Enter the name of your instructor.
* **Puzzle Topic** (9, text, required): Enter the topic of the puzzle.
* **Puzzle Difficulty** (10, select_one, required): Rate the difficulty level of the puzzle (Easy, Medium, Hard, Very Hard).
* **Completion Time** (11, number, required): Enter the time it took you to complete the puzzle in minutes.
* **Across Answers** (12, text, required): Enter all your answers to the across questions, separated by commas.
* **Down Answers** (13, text, required): Enter all your answers to the down questions, separated by commas.
* **Confidence Level** (14, select_one, required): Indicate your level of confidence in your answers (5 - Very Confident, 4 - Confident, 3 - Somewhat Confident, 2 - Not Very Confident, 1 - Not Confident).
* **Concepts Reviewed** (15, select_multiple, required): Select all concepts covered in the puzzle (Terminology, Definitions, Processes, Formulas, Theories, Historical Facts, Applications, Other).
* **Helpfulness Rating** (16, select_one, required): Rate how helpful the puzzle was for learning (5 - Very Helpful, 4 - Helpful, 3 - Somewhat Helpful, 2 - Not Very Helpful, 1 - Not Helpful).
* **Enjoyed Activity** (17, select_one, required): Indicate whether you enjoyed the activity (Yes, Very Much, Yes, Somewhat, Neutral, No, Not Really, No, Not at All).
* **Would Recommend** (18, select_one, required): State whether you would recommend the activity to others (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Neutral, Probably No, Definitely No).
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (19, text, optional): Provide any suggestions for improvement.
* **Additional Comments** (20, text, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback.
* **Academic Integrity** (21, select_one, required): Certify that you completed the puzzle independently (Yes, I Certify, No, I Had Help).
