# Study Environment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students about their current study environment. The goal is to gather information about what students like and dislike about their study space, their satisfaction, and available resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the provided fields, making sure to choose one or more options for the relevant questions.
2. For questions with a select_multiple type, you can choose multiple options.
3. For questions with a select_one type, choose only one option.
4. If you have comments about your study environment, you can type them in the Comments field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Study settings** (`study_environment`, text, optional): This field is where you can describe your current study environment, including any settings or conditions that affect your learning.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options to express your level of satisfaction with your current study environment.
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Resources** (`resources`, select_multiple, optional): Check any additional resources that you have access to in your study environment.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Learning conditions** (`learning_conditions`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options to describe your current learning conditions.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Resources** (`resources`, select_multiple, optional): Check any additional resources that you have access to in your study environment.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): If you have any comments or suggestions about your study environment, type them in this field.
* **Additional resources** (`additional_resources`, select_multiple, optional): Check any additional resources that you have access to in your study environment, such as computers, printers, or internet access.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **What do you like about your current study environment?** (`study_settings_2`, select_multiple, optional): Choose any aspects that you like about your current study environment.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **What do you dislike about your current study environment?** (`satisfaction_2`, select_multiple, optional): Choose any aspects that you dislike about your current study environment.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **How satisfied are you with your current study environment?** (`satisfaction_3`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options to express your level of satisfaction with your current study environment.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **How would you describe your current study environment?** (`learning_conditions_2`, select_multiple, optional): Choose any aspects that you would describe as your current study environment.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Your current study environment is** (`study_environment_2`, select_multiple, optional): Check any aspects that you believe describe your current study environment.
	+ Yes
	+ No

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and thoroughly to provide valuable feedback.
* You can provide additional comments or suggestions in the Comments field at the end of the form.
* Your feedback is anonymous and will be used to improve the study environment.
