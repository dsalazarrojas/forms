# Ethics Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ethics Knowledge Assessment form is used to evaluate a participant's knowledge of ethics, specifically their ability to answer a series of questions related to ethical dilemmas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Participant Email" field (optional).
3. Answer the questions posed by the assessment, selecting the correct response(s) for each question.
4. Complete all fields as required by the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it would appear on your official identification.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): If you wish to be contacted for any reason, please enter your email address. This field is not required for participation.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, optional): The date of the assessment. This field is optional and only necessary if you need to document the time and date of the assessment.
* **Assessment Time** (`assessment_time`, time, optional): The time of the assessment. This field is also optional and not required for participation.

## Tips

* Please answer each question to the best of your ability, and do not worry about the time it takes to complete the assessment.
* If you are unsure of a question, please enter a response that indicates your uncertainty (e.g. "I am unsure", "I do not know", etc.).
* If you need to add any additional information or comments, please use the "Question 7" field for notes.
