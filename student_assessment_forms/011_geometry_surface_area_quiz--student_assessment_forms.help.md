# Geometry Surface Area Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be a tool for students to demonstrate their understanding of surface area concepts in geometry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct answers to the quiz questions by choosing the appropriate options in the **quiz_questions** field.
2. For the **calculate_surface_area** field, enter the correct surface area of the shape using the formula provided. For example, if the shape is a square with side length 1, the surface area would be 1^2 = 1.
3. Choose the correct score from the **quiz_scores** field, indicating the number of questions answered correctly.
4. Provide feedback on the quiz in the **feedback** field.
5. Enter your student ID in the **student_id** field.
6. Enter your name in the **student_name** field.
7. Enter your teacher's name in the **teacher_name** field.
8. Enter your teacher's email address in the **teacher_email** field.
9. Enter any additional notes from your teacher in the **teacher_notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **surface_area_concepts_quiz**: This field is a note field where you can provide additional context or comments about the quiz.
* **quiz_questions**: This field contains multiple-choice questions about the surface area of various shapes. Select all that apply.
* **calculate_surface_area**: This field allows you to calculate the surface area of a shape. For example, if you have a square with side length 1, the surface area would be 1^2 = 1.
* **quiz_scores**: This field allows you to select the number of questions you answered correctly.
* **feedback**: This field is for providing feedback on the quiz.
* **student_id**: This field is for entering your student ID number.
* **student_name**: This field is for entering your name.
* **teacher_name**: This field is for entering your teacher's name.
* **teacher_email**: This field is for entering your teacher's email address.
* **teacher_notes**: This field is for entering any additional notes from your teacher.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields, indicated by the "required" label in each field.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, you can consult with your teacher or a classmate for assistance.
