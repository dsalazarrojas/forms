# Professional Award Recipients Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from individuals who have been nominated for a professional award. It helps to evaluate the nominee's performance and gather supporting statements. Your input will contribute to the decision-making process, and we appreciate your participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the first field.
2. Check the box to indicate that you agree to participate in this voting process.
3. Enter your **Occupation** in the corresponding field.
4. Describe your **Role in the Award-Nominated Field**.
5. Provide the number of years you have known the nominee in the **How long have you known the nominee?** field.
6. You can optionally rate the nominee's performance in the **Rate the nominee's performance** field.
7. If applicable, provide a **Why would you recommend this nominee for the award?** statement in the optional field.
8. If you are affiliated with the nominee's work or organization, check the corresponding box.
9. Choose your **Relation to the Nominee** from the list of options.
10. Enter your **Email Address** to facilitate communication with you after the voting process.
11. If you want to be notified about the award winner(s), check the box.
12. Optionally, provide a **Supporting Statement** for the nominee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Agree to participate in this voting process?**: Check this box if you are willing to participate and submit your vote.
* **Occupation**: Enter your current or previous occupation related to the nominee's field.
* **Role in the award-nominated field**: Describe your position or title in the nominee's organization or industry.
* **How long have you known the nominee?**: Enter the number of years you have known the nominee.
* **Rate the nominee's performance**:
	+ **Outstanding**: Exceptional work or results.
	+ **Excellent**: Very good performance or results.
	+ **Good**: Average or acceptable performance or results.
	+ **Fair**: Below-average performance or results.
	+ **Not Impressive**: Unsatisfactory performance or results.
* **Why would you recommend this nominee for the award?**: Provide a brief statement explaining why you recommend the nominee.
* **Are you affiliated with the nominee's work or organization?**: Check this box if you have a relationship with the nominee's work or organization.
* **Relation to the nominee**: Choose how you are related to the nominee:
	+ **Friend**: Personal acquaintance.
	+ **Colleague**: Work colleague.
	+ **Family Member**: Family member or relative.
	+ **Professional Association**: Member of the same professional association.
	+ **Mentor**: Mentoring relationship.
	+ **No Relation**: No personal or professional connection.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Would you like to be notified about the award winner(s)?**: Check this box if you want to be informed about the award winner(s).
* **Would you like to provide a supporting statement for this nominee?**: Check this box if you want to provide additional information about the nominee.

Note: All fields marked as "required" are mandatory. Optional fields are denoted with an asterisk (\*).
