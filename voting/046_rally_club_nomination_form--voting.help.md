# Rally Club Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rally Club Nomination Form is used to gather information about individuals who deserve recognition or awards within the organization or community. This form helps the nominators to provide detailed information about the nominees' contributions, achievements, and reasons for the nomination.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Nomination Form**: This is the initial section that asks for the basic information about the nomination. Please fill in the relevant details, if any.
2. **Nominee**: Select one or more of the options from the dropdown menu to indicate the nominee.
3. **Nominee Name**: Enter the name of the person being nominated.
4. **Role Name**: Choose the role or position held by the nominee.
5. **Reason for Nomination**: Provide a clear and concise explanation for why you are nominating this person.
6. **Award Type**: Select the type of award or recognition the nominee is being considered for.
7. **Nomination Date**: Enter the date of the nomination (if applicable).
8. **Nomination Time**: Enter the time of the nomination (if applicable).
9. **Nomination Period**: Choose the period or timeframe of the nomination (if applicable).
10. **Nominee Email**: Enter the nominee's email address (if applicable).
11. **Nominee Phone**: Enter the nominee's phone number (if applicable).
12. **Nominator Name**: Enter your name as the nominator.
13. **Nominator Email**: Enter your email address as the nominator.
14. **Nominator Phone**: Enter your phone number as the nominator.
15. **Nominator Note**: Add any additional information or comments from the nominator.
16. **Submitted by**: Enter your name as the person submitting the form.
17. **Submitted**: Enter the date and time of submission (if applicable).
18. **Submitted Period**: Choose the period or timeframe of submission (if applicable).
19. **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or reasons for the submission.
20. **Submitter**: Select the person submitting the form.
21. **Submitter Email**: Enter the submitter's email address (if applicable).
22. **Submitter Phone**: Enter the submitter's phone number (if applicable).
23. **Submitter Note**: Add any additional information or comments from the submitter.
24. **Submitter Comments**: Provide any additional comments or reasons for the submission.
