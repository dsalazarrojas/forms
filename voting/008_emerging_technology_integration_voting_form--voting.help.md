# Emerging Technology Integration Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on emerging technologies that can be integrated into existing systems. It will help us understand your thoughts on the priority and feasibility of integrating these technologies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter a name for the project you are voting on in the **project_name** field.
2. Select a priority for the project in the **vote_priority** field (High, Medium, Low).
3. Choose the integration methods that best align with your project's needs in the **integration_method** field.
4. Enter your email address in the **email** field.
5. Select a date and time for when you think the project should be implemented in the **date** and **time** fields.
6. Select the assigned tools that you think are best for the project in the **assigned_tool** field (optional).
7. Choose the categories that are relevant to your project in the **category**, **category_1**, **category_2**, **category_3**, and **category_4** fields.
8. Provide any additional comments or notes about your vote in the **comments** field.
9. Summarize your thoughts on the project in the **description** fields (optional).
10. Finally, submit the form to provide your vote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **project_name**: A brief name for the project you are voting on.
* **vote_priority**: The priority level for the project, with options: High, Medium, Low.
* **integration_method**: Select the integration methods that best align with your project's needs.
* **email**: Enter your email address for contact and follow-up purposes.
* **date**: The date when you think the project should be implemented.
* **time**: The time when you think the project should be implemented.
* **category**: Choose the category that best fits your project.
* **category_1**, **category_2**, **category_3**, **category_4**: Select additional categories that are relevant to your project.
* **comments**: Any additional comments or notes about your vote.
* **description**: Summarize your thoughts on the project in a few sentences.
* **description_1**, **description_2**, **description_3**, **description_4**, **description_5**, **description_6**, **description_7**, **description_8**: Provide additional details about your project.
* **assigned_tool**: Select the assigned tools that best fit your project's needs (optional).
* **assigned_tool_1**: Select the assigned tools that best fit your project's needs (optional).

Note: Fields **description_1** to **description_8** and **assigned_tool_1** are duplicates of the original **description** and **assigned_tool** fields and are not required. They are provided for additional information, but not mandatory.
