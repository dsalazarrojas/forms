# Voting Closure Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Voting Closure Survey is designed to gather feedback from participants about their experience with the voting process and the roles involved. This survey aims to understand what worked well and what could be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each field in the text boxes provided.
2. Make sure to provide honest and constructive feedback.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### role_feedback
• **Role Feedback** (Text, Optional): Describe your role's involvement in the voting process. What was your experience like? What worked well, and what didn't?

### fairness_feedback
• **Fairness Feedback** (Text, Optional): Were you satisfied with the fairness of the voting process? If not, please explain why.

### organization_feedback
• **Organization Feedback** (Text, Optional): How would you rate your experience with the organization's support during the voting process?

### voting_process
• **Voting Process** (Text, Optional): What did you think about the voting process? Were there any issues or improvements you would suggest?

### fairness_and_roles
• **Fairness and Roles** (Text, Optional): How did you feel about the fairness and roles involved in the voting process? Were there any issues or concerns you would like to raise?
