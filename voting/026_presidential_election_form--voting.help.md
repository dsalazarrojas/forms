# Presidential Election Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Presidential Election Form is designed to collect votes and demographic information from users. It is a one-time submission form, and users are expected to fill it out only once to have their vote counted.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field. This field is a text field where you can type your first name.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field. This field is a text field where you can type your last name.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This field is an email field where you can type a valid email address.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field. This field is a text field where you can type your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`presidential_election_form_1`, text, optional): Please enter your first name to help us identify you uniquely.
* **Last Name** (`presidential_election_form_2`, text, optional): Please enter your last name to help us identify you uniquely.
* **Email** (`presidential_election_form_3`, email, optional): Please enter a valid email address where you would like to be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (`presidential_election_form_4`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number where you can be reached.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, if any.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* This form is meant for one-time submissions only; if you have already submitted your vote, there's no need to submit again.
