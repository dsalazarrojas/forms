# Teacher Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Nomination Form is used to gather information about a teacher being nominated for recognition or award. This form is intended to be completed only once per teacher, and the information collected will be used to evaluate and consider the teacher for the nomination.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Nominee Name**, which is the name of the teacher being nominated.
2.  Enter the **Nominee Email**, which is the email address of the nominee.
3.  Enter the **Teacher Name**, which is the name of the teacher being nominated.
4.  Enter the **Teacher Department**, which is the department of the teacher being nominated.
5.  Enter the **Reason for Nomination**, which is a brief explanation of why the teacher is being nominated.
6.  Enter the **Nomination Message**, which is a statement to support the nomination.
7.  Select the **Category**, which is the group or category the teacher belongs to (A, B, or C).
8.  Enter your **Submitter Email**, which is your email address.
9.  Enter your **Submitter Name**, which is your name.
10. Enter your **Submitter Phone**, which is your phone number.
11. Enter your **Submitter Institution**, which is the name of your institution.
12. Enter your **Submitter Department**, which is your department.
13. Optionally, add any **Additional Info**, which is a field for any extra information you'd like to provide.
14. Enter the **Submission Date**, which is the date the nomination was submitted.
15. Enter the **Submission Time**, which is the time the nomination was submitted.
16. (Optional) Enter the **Tool ID**, **Tool Name**, **Tool Description**, and **Tool URL**, which are fields for providing additional information about any tools or resources used by the teacher.
