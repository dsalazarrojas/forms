# Teacher Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Nomination Form is used to gather information about a teacher being nominated for recognition or award. This form is intended to be completed only once per teacher, and the information collected will be used to evaluate and consider the teacher for the nomination.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Nominee Name**, which is the name of the teacher being nominated.
2.  Enter the **Nominee Email**, which is the email address of the nominee.
3.  Enter the **Teacher Name**, which is the name of the teacher being nominated.
4.  Enter the **Teacher Department**, which is the department of the teacher being nominated.
5.  Enter the **Reason for Nomination**, which is a brief explanation of why the teacher is being nominated.
6.  Enter the **Nomination Message**, which is a statement to support the nomination.
7.  Select the **Category**, which is the group or category the teacher belongs to (A, B, or C).
8.  Enter your **Submitter Email**, which is your email address.
9.  Enter your **Submitter Name**, which is your name.
10. Enter your **Submitter Phone**, which is your phone number.
11. Enter your **Submitter Institution**, which is the name of your institution.
12. Enter your **Submitter Department**, which is your department.
13. Optionally, add any **Additional Info**, which is a field for any extra information you'd like to provide.
14. Enter the **Submission Date**, which is the date the nomination was submitted.
15. Enter the **Submission Time**, which is the time the nomination was submitted.
16. (Optional) Enter the **Tool ID**, **Tool Name**, **Tool Description**, and **Tool URL**, which are fields for providing additional information about any tools or resources used by the teacher.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Nominee Name** (`<nominee_name>`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the teacher being nominated.
*   **Nominee Email** (`<email>`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the nominee.
*   **Teacher Name** (`<teacher_name>`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the teacher being nominated.
*   **Teacher Department** (`<teacher_department>`, `text`, required): Enter the department of the teacher being nominated.
*   **Reason for Nomination** (`<reason_for_nomination>`, `text`, required): Enter a brief explanation of why the teacher is being nominated.
*   **Nomination Message** (`<nomination_message>`, `text`, required): Enter a statement to support the nomination.
*   **Category** (`<category>`, `select_one`, required): Select the category or group the teacher belongs to (A, B, or C).
*   **Submitter Email** (`<submitter_email>`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
*   **Submitter Name** (`<submitter_name>`, `text`, required): Enter your name.
*   **Submitter Phone** (`<submitter_phone>`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
*   **Submitter Institution** (`<submitter_institution>`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your institution.
*   **Submitter Department** (`<submitter_department>`, `text`, required): Enter your department.
*   **Additional Info** (`<additional_info>`, `text`, optional): This field is for any extra information you'd like to provide.
*   **Submission Date** (`<submit_date>`, `date`, required): Enter the date the nomination was submitted.
*   **Submission Time** (`<submit_time>`, `time`, required): Enter the time the nomination was submitted.
*   **Tool ID** (`<tool_id>`, `number`, optional): This field is not required and is likely for internal use.
*   **Tool Name** (`<tool_name>`, `text`, optional): This field is not required and is likely for internal use.
*   **Tool Description** (`<tool_description>`, `text`, optional): This field is not required and is likely for internal use.
*   **Tool URL** (`<tool_url>`, `text`, optional): This field is not required and is likely for internal use.
