# Song Relay Competition Results - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and display the results of a song competition, including the overall score and ranking, as well as individual song scores.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Competition Score**, which represents the overall score of the competition.
2. Enter the **Judge Name**, which is the name of the person who provided the score.
3. Enter the **Ranking Position**, which is the ranking of the song in the competition.
4. Enter the **Song ID**, which is the unique identifier of the song.
5. Enter the **Song Title**, which is the title of the song.
6. Enter the scores given by the **Score 1**, **Score 2**, and **Score 3**, which represent the scores given by multiple judges or jurors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Competition Score**: A number that represents the overall score of the competition.
* **Judge Name**: The name of the person who provided the score.
* **Ranking Position**: A number that represents the ranking of the song in the competition.
* **Song ID**: A unique identifier of the song.
* **Song Title**: The title of the song.
* **Score 1**: A number that represents the score given by the first judge or juror.
* **Score 2**: A number that represents the score given by the second judge or juror.
* **Score 3**: A number that represents the score given by the third judge or juror.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete results.
* If you are a judge or juror, make sure to enter your score accurately and honestly.
* If you are a participant, make sure to enter your song's title and ID accurately.
