# Strike Vote Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data from strike participants and understand their opinions on proposed actions and their experience with the strike.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Select all the options that apply to you regarding your vote on proposed actions.
- If you have any affiliation with the strike, such as your union or organization, enter your affiliation in the corresponding field.
- Provide any feedback you have about your experience with the strike.
- If you have proposed actions that you think should be taken during the strike, select them in the "Proposed Actions" field.
- Enter the duration of your participation in the strike.
- If you have a contact email address, enter it in the "Email" field.
- Select a time and date that you participated in the strike, this will help us to get a better understanding of when the strike took place.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vote** (`Yes/No`, required): This field allows you to indicate your stance on the proposed actions. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Affiliation** (`text`, optional): If you have any affiliation with the strike, enter it here. This could be your union, organization, or any other relevant information.
* **Feedback** (`note`, optional): Provide any feedback you have about your experience with the strike.
* **Proposed Actions** (`Yes/No`, optional): If you have proposed actions that you think should be taken during the strike, select them here.
* **Participation** (`number`, optional): Enter the duration of your participation in the strike.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): If you have a contact email address, enter it here.
* **Time** (`time`, optional): Select a time that you participated in the strike.
* **Date** (`date`, optional): Select a date that you participated in the strike.

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- Make sure to select all the options that apply to you regarding your vote on proposed actions.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, contact the form administrator.
