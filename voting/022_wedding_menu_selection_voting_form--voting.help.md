# Wedding Menu Selection Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect important information from wedding guests to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your RSVP (RSVP) number to indicate whether you will be attending the wedding.
2. Fill in your Guest Name to let us know who you are.
3. Provide your Phone number in case we need to contact you.
4. Share your Email address for further communication.
5. Select your preferred Meal Choice from the options provided. Please note that this is a required field.
6. If desired, you can specify your RSVP Date and RSVP Time.
7. Add any Comments about your preferences or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **RSVP** (`RSVP`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your RSVP number to let us know if you will be attending the wedding.
* **Guest Name** (`Guest Name`, `text`, required/optional): Please fill in your name so we can personalize the wedding experience for you.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required/optional): If we need to reach you, provide your contact number.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): Share your email address for further communication and updates.
* **Meal Choice** (`Meal Choice`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of meal you would like to enjoy during the wedding.
	+ Please note that the options are: Wedding Cake, Salad, Seafood, or Beef.
* **RSVP Date** (`RSVP Date`, `date`, required/optional): If desired, specify the date you will be attending the event.
* **RSVP Time** (`RSVP Time`, `time`, required/optional): If desired, let us know the time you will arrive at the event.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `note`, required/optional): Add any comments or special requests you might have.

## Tips
Please double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form, especially the RSVP number and meal choice. This will help us ensure that we have correct headcount and catering arrangements in place.
