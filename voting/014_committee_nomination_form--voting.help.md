# Committee Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect nominations for potential committee members. It is essential that the nominator provides accurate and relevant information about the nominee to support their eligibility and suitability for the position.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Nomination**: If you are nominating someone else for a committee position, fill out this form with the nominee's details.
2. **Nominee Information**: Fill in the nominee's full name, current title or position, email address, and phone number.
3. **Committee and Role**: Select the committee the nominee is being nominated for and choose the targeted role.
4. **Qualifications and Experience**: Write a statement explaining why the nominee is a good fit for the position and provide any relevant past experience.
5. **Consent**: Confirm that the nominee has consented to the nomination.
6. **Verification**: If you have a seconder (a second person who supports the nomination), enter their name. This is optional.
7. **Confirmation**: Check that the information is accurate, and then confirm that you have checked the provided information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nominator Name**: The full name of the person filling out this form.
* **Nominee Full Name**: The full name of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee Email Address**: The nominee's email address.
* **Nominee Phone Number**: The nominee's phone number.
* **Nominee Member ID Number**: The member ID number of the nominee (if applicable).
* **Committee Name for Nomination**: Select the committee the nominee is being nominated for.
* **Targeted Role**: Select the specific role within that committee that the nominee is being nominated for.
* **Nominee Consent**: Confirm that the nominee has consented to the nomination.
* **Statement of Qualifications**: Write a statement explaining why the nominee is a good fit for the position.
* **Previous Relevant Experience**: Provide any relevant past experience the nominee may have.
* **Has the Nominee Consented**: Confirm that the nominee has agreed to be nominated.
* **Seconder's Name (Optional)**: Enter the name of the person who supports this nomination.
* **Nomination Date**: Enter the date of the nomination.
* **Internal Reference Code**: Enter a reference code for tracking purposes.
