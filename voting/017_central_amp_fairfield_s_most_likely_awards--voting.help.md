# Central - Fairfield's Most Likely Awards - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to nominate students for various awards in different categories.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with the required information.
2. Select the branch you belong to from the options in the "Your School - Branch" field.
3. Nominate one student per award category.
4. Confirm that you have finished your nominations.
5. Fill in the current date in the "Date of Voting" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Voter Name** (`voter_name`, Text, Optional): This field allows you to enter your name. Your name will not be shared publicly.
* **Your School - Branch** (`voter_school_branch`, Select One, Required): Select the branch you belong to from the options provided.
* **Most Likely to Succeed** (`most_likely_succeed`, Text, Required): Nominate one student.
* **Most Likely to Win a Nobel Prize** (`most_likely_nobel_prize`, Text, Required): Nominate one student.
* **Most Likely to Become a Famous Artist** (`most_likely_famous_artist`, Text, Required): Nominate one student.
* **Most Likely to Be on Broadway** (`most_likely_broadway`, Text, Required): Nominate one student.
* **Most Likely to Brighten Someone's Day** (`most_likely_brighten_day`, Text, Required): Nominate one student.
* **Most Likely to Become President** (`most_likely_president`, Text, Required): Nominate one student.
* **Most Likely to Travel the World** (`most_likely_travel_world`, Text, Required): Nominate one student.
* **Most Likely to Start a Successful Startup** (`most_likely_startup_founder`, Text, Required): Nominate one student.
* **Confirmation of Votes** (`confirmation_of_votes`, Select One, Required): Confirm that you have finished your nominations.
* **Date of Voting** (`date_of_voting`, Date, Required): Fill in the current date.
