# Homecoming Court Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Homecoming Court Nomination Form is used to submit nominations for the homecoming court. This form is designed to collect essential information about the nominee, including their grade level, name, and teacher's contact details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to select the correct grade level for the nominee.
3. Enter the nominee's name and your own name and contact information as the nominator.
4. Provide your teacher's name and contact information if you are a teacher.
5. Select the teacher's subject area or other relevant details about your teacher.
6. Provide any additional comments or information about the nominee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Homecoming Nomination Form**: This is the main section of the form where you can enter your nomination. Please make sure to fill it out completely and accurately.
* **Student Nominee Name**: Enter the name of the student you are nominating. This is a required field.
* **Grade Level**: Select the grade level of the student you are nominating. This will help the homecoming committee verify their eligibility. 
* **Nominator's Name**: Enter your name as the nominator. This is a required field.
* **Teacher's Name**: Enter the name of the student's teacher. This is a required field.
* **Teacher's Email**: Enter the teacher's email address. This is an optional field.
* **Teacher's Phone**: Enter the teacher's phone number. This is an optional field.
* **Student's Name**: Enter the name of the student you are nominating. This is a required field, and it should match the student's name entered above.
* **Student's Grade Level**: Select the grade level of the student you are nominating. This will help the homecoming committee verify their eligibility. 
*   **Teacher's Subject Area**: If the student is a teacher or has a specific subject area of interest, enter it here. This is an optional field.
*   **Teacher's Department**: Enter the teacher's department or school. This is an optional field.
*   **Other Teacher's Area of Interest**: Provide any additional information about the teacher's area of interest. This is an optional field.
*   **Other Teacher's Name**: If the student has an other teacher's contact information, enter it here. This is an optional field.
*   **Other Teacher's Interest**: Provide any additional information about the other teacher's interest. This is an optional field.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you are only nominating students who are eligible for the homecoming court.
*   Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date contact information for the nominator and teacher.
*   If you are unsure about the nomination process or have any questions, please contact the homecoming committee or a staff member for guidance.
