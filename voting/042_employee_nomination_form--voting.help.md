# Employee Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to nominate a colleague or team member for recognition and appreciation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the first name of the person you are nominating.
2. Enter the last name of the person you are nominating.
3. Indicate whether the person being nominated is in the same department as you or not.
4. Indicate whether the person being nominated is in a role that is directly above or below them in your organizational chart.
5. Select multiple job titles held by the person being nominated (e.g., Team Lead, Project Manager, etc.).
6. Enter the name of the person who would be the best contact for the person being nominated.
7. Add any additional comments or reasons for the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: Enter the first name of the person you are nominating.
* **Last Name (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: Enter the last name of the person you are nominating.
* **Role (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: Indicate whether the person being nominated is in a role that is directly above or below them in your organizational chart.
* **Department (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: Indicate whether the person being nominated is in the same department as you or not.
* **Job Title (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: Select multiple job titles held by the person being nominated (e.g., Team Lead, Project Manager, etc.).
* **Manager Name (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: Enter the name of the person who would be the best contact for the person being nominated.
* **Nomination Form Comments (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: Add any additional comments or reasons for the nomination.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the person being nominated is currently or previously employed by the organization.
- Please ensure that the nomination is based on genuine appreciation or recognition of their work performance.
- Please avoid submitting duplicate nominations for the same person.
