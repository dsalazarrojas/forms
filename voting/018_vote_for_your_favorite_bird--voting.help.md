# Vote For Your Favorite Bird - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user feedback and suggestions for the "Vote For Your Favorite Bird" system. The form has been created to gather information about users' favorite bird species, which can be used to improve the system's features and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your favorite bird species from the list.
2. Enter your name.
3. Provide your email address.
4. (Optional) Enter your phone number.
5. Type any additional message you'd like to share with us.
6. If your favorite bird is not listed, provide the name of the bird in the "Other" field.
7. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Favorite Bird** (`favorite_bird`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose one or more of the listed bird species that you enjoy. You can select multiple birds by holding down Ctrl key (PC) or Command key (Mac).
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: true): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: true): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Message** (`message`, `note`, required: false): Share any additional thoughts or comments about your favorite bird.
* **Other (if not listed)** (`favorite_bird_other`, `text`, required: false): If your favorite bird is not in the list, enter its name here.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: true): Click this button to send your feedback to us.
