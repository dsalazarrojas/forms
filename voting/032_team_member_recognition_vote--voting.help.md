# Team Member Recognition Vote - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Team Member Recognition Vote form is used to gather feedback and recognition from team members about their peers who have demonstrated exceptional work or behavior. This form helps to acknowledge and appreciate team members' contributions, fostering a positive and supportive team environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the team member's name who is being recognized.
2. Enter the team member's name of the team they belong to.
3. Briefly describe the reason for the recognition.
4. Rate the impact of the team member's actions on the team or project.
5. Enter the manager's name and email (if applicable).
6. Enter the team member's email and phone number (if applicable).
7. Select the date and time of the recognition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Member Name** (`team_member_name`): Enter the name of the team member being recognized.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`): Enter the name of the team the team member belongs to.
* **Nomination Reason** (`nomination_reason`): Briefly describe the reason for the recognition.
* **Nomination Rating** (`nomination_rating`): Rate the impact of the team member's actions on the team or project (using a number value).
* **Nomination Impact** (`nomination_impact`): Rate the impact of the team member's actions on the team or project (using a number value).
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`): Enter the name of the team member's manager (if applicable).
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`): Enter the email address of the team member's manager (if applicable).
* **Team Member Email** (`team_member_email`): Enter the email address of the team member being recognized (if applicable).
* **Team Member Phone** (`team_member_phone`): Enter the phone number of the team member being recognized (if applicable).
* **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`): Select the date of the recognition.
* **Nomination Time** (`nomination_time`): Select the time of the recognition.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Click the "Submit" button to complete the form and submit your nomination for recognition.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* If you are recognizing a team member's actions from a previous date, select the corresponding date for the nomination.
* If you are entering a phone number, use the standard phone number format (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the team member or their manager before submitting the form.
