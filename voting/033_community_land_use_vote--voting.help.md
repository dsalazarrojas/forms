# Community Land Use Vote - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Land Use Vote is a crucial process for residents and property owners to express their opinions on proposed land use changes in the community. This form aims to collect feedback on three distinct proposals: A, B, and C, to ensure that the community's voice is heard in the decision-making process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Resident Full Name** field with your full name as a resident or property owner in the community.
2. Enter your **Residential or Business Address** in the community in the **Residential or Business Address** field.
3. Select your **Residency Status** from the options provided (Residential Homeowner, Residential Renter, Local Business Owner, or Commercial Property Owner).
4. For each of the three land use proposals (A, B, and C), select your opinion using the radio buttons (In Favor, Opposed, or Neutral/No Opinion).
5. Provide your level of concern for **Environmental Sustainability** by selecting the appropriate option (Extremely High Priority, High Priority, Moderate Priority, or Low Priority).
6. If you have any comments or suggestions regarding the proposed changes, you can add them in the **Detailed Comments** field.
7. If you have a preferred alternative use for the vacant lot on Main Street, you can provide it in the **Preferred Alternative Use for Vacant Lot on Main Street** field.
8. Select the **Date of Vote Submission** using the date picker.
9. Finally, confirm your **Verification Acknowledgement** by selecting "I Certify" if you are a resident or property owner eligible to vote. If you are not, choose "Other".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resident Full Name**: Your full name as a resident or property owner in the community.
	+ This is a required field to ensure that only eligible individuals vote.
* **Residential or Business Address**: Enter your address in the community where you reside or own property.
	+ This is a required field to confirm your residency or property ownership.
* **Residency Status**: Select the one that primarily applies to you.
	+ This will help in determining your eligibility to vote and understand your perspective on the proposed changes.
* **Land Use Proposal A - Creation of New Community Park**: Express your opinion on the proposal to create a new community park.
	+ Select In Favor, Opposed, or Neutral/No Opinion.
* **Land Use Proposal B - Mixed-use Housing and Retail Development**: Express your opinion on the proposal to rezone the Industrial District for mixed-use housing and retail development.
	+ Select In Favor, Opposed, or Neutral/No Opinion.
* **Land Use Proposal C - Public Library Expansion and Tech Hub**: Express your opinion on the proposal to expand the public library and create a tech hub.
	+ Select In Favor, Opposed, or Neutral/No Opinion.
* **Detailed Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have regarding the proposed changes.
	+ This is an optional field for you to share your thoughts.
* **Environmental Sustainability Importance**: Indicate the level of priority you give to environmental sustainability.
	+ Select Extremely High, High, Moderate, or Low.
* **Preferred Alternative Use for Vacant Lot on Main Street**: Suggest an alternative use for the vacant lot on Main Street.
	+ This is an optional field for you to share your idea.
* **Date of Vote Submission**: Select the date when you submit your vote.
	+ This is a required field to track the submission date.
* **I Certify**: Confirm your residency or property ownership eligibility to vote.
	+ Select I Certify if you are eligible. Otherwise, choose Other.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are a resident or property owner eligible to vote before submitting your vote.
* Each proposal will only be displayed once.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, feel free to reach out to the community administration.
* Your input is valuable, and your opinions will contribute to informed decision-making in the community.
