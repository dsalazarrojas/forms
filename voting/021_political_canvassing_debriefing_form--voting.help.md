# political_canvassing_debriefing_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from canvassers after they have completed a political canvassing session. The purpose of this form is to ensure that the data collected is accurate and consistent across different sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields, which are marked as required.
2. Enter the canvasser's id number in the `canvasser id` field.
3. Describe the person you canvassed, including their name and any other relevant details, in the `canvassed person` field.
4. Indicate the type of conversation you had with the person in the `conversation type` field. You can select from the options 'Yes' or 'No'.
5. Provide more details about the conversation in the `conversation details` field.
6. Enter the location where the conversation took place in the `location` field.
7. Add any additional notes about the conversation in the `notes` field.
8. If you have the person's phone number, enter it in the `phone number` field.
9. If you have the person's email, enter it in the `email` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **canvasser id** (`number`, required): Enter the id number of the canvasser who conducted the conversation.
* **canvassed person** (`text`, required): Describe the person you spoke to, including their name and any other relevant details.
* **conversation type** (`select_one`, required): Indicate the type of conversation you had, which can be either 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **conversation details** (`text`, optional): Provide more details about the conversation, such as what was discussed or how the conversation went.
* **location** (`text`, optional): Enter the location where the conversation took place.
* **notes** (`note`, optional): Add any additional notes about the conversation.
* **phone number** (`text`, optional): If you have the person's phone number, enter it here.
* **email** (`email`, optional): If you have the person's email, enter it here.
