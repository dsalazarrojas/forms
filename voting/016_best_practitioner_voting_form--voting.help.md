# Best Practitioner Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Best Practitioner Voting Form is designed to gather information from voters about their experiences with practitioners in their field. This form helps to identify exceptional practitioners who deserve recognition and rewards.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter your full name and email address in the required fields.
*   Enter the full name, title, and organization of the practitioner you are nominating.
*   Select the category for the nominated practitioner (e.g., Medical Practitioner, Legal Practitioner).
*   Rate the practitioner's level of expertise, quality of care, communication skills, professionalism, and treatment outcomes.
*   Provide the number of years the practitioner has been practicing.
*   Indicate how long you have been their patient or client.
*   Rate how well you know the practitioner.
*   Provide a reason for nominating the practitioner and a success story (optional).
*   Indicate if you would recommend the practitioner to others.
*   Add any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Email Address**: Enter the email address we will use to contact you with confirmation and updates.
*   **Your Organization**: Enter the name of your company or institution (optional).
*   **Nominated Practitioner Full Name**: Enter the full name of the practitioner you are nominating.
*   **Practitioner Title or Designation**: Enter the practitioner's professional title or designation.
*   **Practitioner Organization**: Enter the name of the organization where the practitioner works.
*   **Practice Location**: Enter the city and state where the practitioner practices.
*   **Practice Category**: Select the category for the nominated practitioner (e.g., Medical Practitioner, Legal Practitioner).
*   **Years of Practice**: Select the number of years the practitioner has been practicing.
*   **Relationship Duration**: Select how long you have been their patient or client.
*   **Professional Expertise Rating**: Rate the practitioner's level of expertise.
*   **Quality of Care Rating**: Rate the quality of care provided by the practitioner.
*   **Communication Skills Rating**: Rate the practitioner's communication skills.
*   **Professionalism Rating**: Rate the practitioner's professionalism.
*   **Treatment Outcomes Rating**: Rate the effectiveness of the practitioner's treatments.
*   **Standout Qualities**: Select the qualities that make this practitioner exceptional (e.g., Deep Expertise, Compassionate Care).
*   **Nomination Reason**: Explain why you are nominating this practitioner.
*   **Success Story**: Describe a specific instance where the practitioner made a positive impact (optional).
*   **Would You Recommend**: Indicate if you would recommend this practitioner to others.
*   **Additional Comments**: Add any additional feedback or comments about the practitioner.

Note that some fields are required and should be filled out accordingly. Please make sure to answer honestly and accurately to help identify exceptional practitioners.
