# Employee Of The Month Ballot Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit nominations for the Employee of the Month award. The form allows users to provide the name of the employee being nominated, along with the reason for the nomination, and the nominator's details. The nomination process helps to identify and recognize outstanding employees who have made significant contributions to the team or organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the employee you are nominating.
2. Provide a brief title or description of the nomination reason.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are willing to provide more information about your nomination.
4. If you selected "Yes", provide your name and title as the nominator.
5. Enter your email address so we can contact you for more information (if needed).
6. Enter your phone number so we can contact you (if needed).
7. Add any additional comments or reasons why the employee deserves the award.
8. Enter a title for the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee of the month name** (`employee_of_the_month_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee you are nominating.
* **Nomination reason** (`nomination_reason`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the reason for the nomination.
* **Nomination reason** (`employee_of_the_month_title`, text, optional): Enter a title for the nomination (e.g., "Outstanding Service", "Exceptional Leadership", etc.).
* **Nomination reason** (`nomination_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the nomination (if applicable).
* **Nominator information** (`nominator_names`, text, optional): If you selected "Yes" in the previous field, enter your name as the nominator.
* **Nominator information** (`nominator_title`, text, optional): Enter your title as the nominator.
* **Nominator information** (`nominator_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for more information (if needed).
* **Nominator information** (`nominator_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you (if needed).
* **Nominator comments** (`nominator_comment`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or reasons why the employee deserves the award.
* **Nominator information** (`employee_of_the_month_title`, text, optional): Enter a title for the nomination (e.g., "Outstanding Service", "Exceptional Leadership", etc.).
