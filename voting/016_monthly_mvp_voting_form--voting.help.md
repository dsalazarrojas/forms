# monthly_mvp_voting_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for monthly MVP voting, where team members can vote for a nominee. To participate, select "Yes" or "No" for the relevant fields and provide any additional information required.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Yes" or "No" for each field as applicable.
2. Fill in the nominee's details such as name, email, job title, and contribution.
3. Provide a reason for the nomination.
4. Enter any additional comments.
5. Click the "Submit" button to submit your vote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Choose Mvp** (1, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" to indicate that you voted for the nominee.
* **Nominee Name** (2, text, optional): Enter the nominee's name.
* **Email** (3, email, optional): Enter the nominee's email.
* **Nominee Job Title** (4, text, optional): Enter the nominee's job title.
* **Nominee Contribution** (5, text, optional): Describe the nominee's contribution.
* **Reason For Nomination** (6, text, optional): Explain the reason for the nomination.
* **Team Members Voted For** (7, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if you voted for the nominee on your team.
* **Submit** (8, text, optional): Enter your vote.
* **Team Name** (9, text, optional): Enter your team's name.
* **Team Email** (10, email, optional): Enter your team's email.
* **Month** (11, date, optional): Select the month of the nomination.
* **Year** (12, number, optional): Select the year of the nomination.
* **Time of Day** (13, time, optional): Select the time of day of the nomination.
* **Note** (14, note, optional): Enter any additional notes.
* **Phone** (15, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Vote** (16, text, optional): Enter your vote.
* **Vote For** (17, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" to indicate that you voted for the nominee.
* **Comments** (18, text, optional): Enter any comments about the nomination.
* **Submit Button** (19, text, optional): This is the submit button, click to submit your vote.
* **Team Lead** (20, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" to indicate that the nominee is a team lead.
* **Nominee** (21, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" to indicate that the nominee is on your team.
* **Submit** (22, text, optional): Enter your vote.
* **Team** (23, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" to indicate that the nominee is on your team.
* **MVP Form Submit** (24, text, optional): This is the submit button, click to submit your vote.
* **Note** (25, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the submission.

Note: This help guide is a general explanation and may not cover every possible scenario or edge case. If you have questions or need further clarification, please contact the relevant authorities.
