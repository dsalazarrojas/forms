# Straw Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose

This straw poll is designed to gather information on the level of education and other demographic information from participants. The form has 25 fields, divided into two sections: "Main Subject" and "Education Levels".

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Start by selecting the "Main Subject" field.** This field is a text input field where you can enter any topic or issue you wish to discuss or vote on.
2. **Enter your main subject in the text field.** Please keep it concise and clear.
3. **Choose your level of education**. Select one or more options from the list below. Note that some fields are optional.
4. **If you have a different level of education, please specify it in the "Education Levels" section.** This is where you can enter any additional education levels that are not listed in the "Education Levels" section.
5. **Choose how to filter the results**. Select one or more options from the list below.
6. **If you have a different way to filter the results, please specify it in the "Filter by Education" section.** This is where you can enter any additional ways to filter the results.
7. **Provide any additional notes or comments.** This is an optional field where you can add any additional information you feel is relevant.
8. **Enter your email address.** This is optional, but it is recommended in case you need to be contacted for further information.
9. **Enter your phone number.** This is optional, but it is recommended in case you need to be contacted for further information.
10. **Review and submit the form.** Once you have completed all the fields, review your information and submit the form.
