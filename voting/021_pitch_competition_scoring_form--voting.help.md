# Pitch Competition Scoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record each judge's scores and feedback for each team in a pitch competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about each team and the competition.
2. Fill out each field with the corresponding information for the selected team.
3. Enter the scores and feedback from each judge for the team.
4. Review your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Score**: A numerical score for the team, if required (optional, default: 0).
* **Team Name**: The name of the team being evaluated.
* **Score 1**: A numerical score for the first evaluation criteria (optional).
* **Score 2**: A numerical score for the second evaluation criteria (optional).
* **Score 3**: A numerical score for the third evaluation criteria (optional).
* **Team Score Total**: The total score for the team (calculated internally, not user-entered).
* **Judge Name**: The name of the judge who entered the score and feedback.
* **Judge Note**: A note from the judge about their evaluation (optional).
* **Judge Email**: The email address of the judge (optional).
* **Judge Phone**: The phone number of the judge (optional).
* **Judge Score**: A numerical score from the judge (optional).
* **Judge Name**, **Judge Note**, **Judge Email**, and **Judge Phone** can be left empty if the judge did not provide a score or feedback.
