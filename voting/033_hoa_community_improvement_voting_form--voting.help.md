# hoa_community_improvement_voting_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to collect feedback and suggestions from community members on potential community improvement projects.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, select up to 1 project from the provided options.
- Choose the priority level for your selected project.
- Provide a brief description of your selected project.
- Confirm whether or not you support your selected project.
- Confirm your email address.
- Provide your name.
- (Optional) Provide additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Select a project** (`project_selection`, select_multiple, required): Select up to 1 project from the options provided.
  - This field is required and is the first field on the form.
- **Choose the priority level** (`priority_selection`, select_one, required): Choose a priority level for your selected project. Priority levels are High, Medium, and Low.
- **Describe your selected project** (`project_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your selected project.
  - This field is required and should provide a clear and concise description of your project.
- **Do you support the selected project?** (`vote_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm whether or not you support your selected project.
  - This field is required and has three options: True, False, and Abstain.
- **Confirm your email address** (`email_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm your email address.
  - This field is required and has three options: True, False.
- **Provide your name** (`name_selection`, text, required): Enter your name.
  - This field is required and should be a valid name.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (optional).
  - This field is optional and not required.
- **Confirm Submission** (`confirm_submission`, text, required): Enter a confirmation of your submission (e.g. "I confirm that the above information is correct").
  - This field is required and should provide a confirmation of your submission.
- **Confirm Email Address** (`confirm_email_address`, text, required): Enter a confirmation of your email address.
  - This field is required and should be a re-entry of your email address.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information you would like to provide (optional).
  - This field is optional and not required.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have (optional).
  - This field is optional and not required.
- **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, select_one, required): Confirm that your email address is correct.
  - This field is required and has two options: True, False.
