# Most Active Member Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Most Active Member Voting Form is designed to allow team members to vote on which members of the organization are the most active or effective in their roles, providing valuable feedback for the team or leadership to recognize and reward their efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Most Active Member Voting Form** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a title for your vote.
2. **Category** (`voting`, select_one, optional): Select the category that best describes the member being voted for (e.g., Most Active, Most Effective, Most Valuable).
3. **Description** (`the_most_active_member_voting_form`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the member's activity or effectiveness, including specific examples and reasons why they stand out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Most Active Member Voting Form**: Enter a short title for your vote, describing the member you are voting for.
* **Category**: Select the category that best describes the member being voted for, such as their level of activity, effectiveness, or contribution to the team. This will help in filtering and grouping votes.
* **Description**: Briefly describe the member's activity or effectiveness, including specific examples and reasons why they stand out. This will provide more context to the vote, allowing for more accurate evaluation.

## Tips
* Only vote for members with whom you have direct experience working or collaborating.
* Be honest and objective in your assessment, focusing on the member's actual performance and impact.
* Keep your description concise and focused on specific examples to make your vote count.
* If a member has consistently demonstrated exceptional performance, consider providing supporting evidence or references to back your vote.
