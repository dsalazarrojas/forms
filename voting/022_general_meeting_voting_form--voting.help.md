# General Meeting Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect voting data for a general meeting. It consists of multiple pages, allowing you to record your opinion on various topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Navigate through each page by clicking on the "Next" button.
2. On each page, fill in the required information in the designated fields.
3. You can skip any pages that are not relevant to your needs.
4. Submit your completed form when you're finished.

- **Meeting Voting Form Page 1**: This page is the starting point for the form.

## Tips
