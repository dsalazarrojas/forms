# Therapist Of The Year Voting Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect votes for the "Therapist Of The Year" award. This form is designed to ensure that each voter provides a valid reason for their vote and can only vote for one therapist per submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the instructions below:

1. Please fill out each page carefully and accurately.
2. Ensure that you only vote for a single therapist per submission.
3. Provide a valid reason for your vote in the voting justification field.
4. If you need to provide additional justification for your vote, you may include more text in the voting justification field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Username**: This is the name that will be associated with your vote. Please enter your username carefully, as this will be used to track your vote.
* **Name of Therapist**: This is the name of the therapist you are voting for. Please enter the name of the therapist accurately.
* **Voting Justification**: This field is used to provide a reason for your vote. Please be as detailed and accurate as possible.
* **Vote For Therapist**: This is a multiple-choice question where you must select "Yes" if you think the therapist is deserving of the award. You may select "No" if you do not think the therapist is deserving of the award.
* **Email**: This is your email address, which will be used to contact you if needed. Please ensure that you enter a valid email address.
* **Phone**: This is your phone number, which will be used to contact you if needed. Please ensure that you enter a valid phone number.
* **Vote For Therapist (Multi-Select)**: This is another multiple-choice question where you must select "Yes" for each therapist you are voting for. You may select "No" for each therapist you do not think is deserving of the award.
* **Date of Voting**: This field is used to record the date when you voted.
* **Time of Voting**: This field is used to record the time when you voted.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you only vote for one therapist per submission.
* Please be as honest and accurate as possible when providing your justification for your vote.
* If you need to provide additional justification for your vote, you may include more text in the voting justification field.
* The form has multiple pages, so please fill out each page carefully and accurately.
* The voting process is anonymous and will not be tracked back to you.
