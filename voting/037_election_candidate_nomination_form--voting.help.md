# Election Candidate Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Election Candidate Nomination Form is a tool used to submit nominations for election candidates. It is intended to gather essential information about the candidate and the reason for nomination, as well as contact details for verification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the nomination form, follow these steps:

1. Select the candidate you wish to nominate from the list of options provided.
2. Provide a reason for nominating this candidate.
3. Enter the contact person's name and any additional contact information.
4. Enter the submission date and time (optional).
5. Attach any additional attachments or supporting documents (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Election Candidate Form** (`<select one>`):
  Choose a candidate from the list to nominate.
* **Nomination Reason** (`<note>`):
  Enter a brief description of why you are nominating this candidate.
* **Contact Person 1** (`<text>`):
  Enter the name of the person nominating the candidate.
* **Contact Info 1** (`<text>`):
  Enter the contact details for the person nominating the candidate.
* **Contact Info 2** (`<text>`):
  Enter any additional contact information (e.g., phone number, email).
* **Submission Date** (`<date>`):
  Enter the date the nomination was submitted (optional).
* **Submission Time** (`<time>`):
  Enter the time the nomination was submitted (optional).
* **Nomination Form Attachments** (`<text>`):
  Attach any supporting documents or files to the nomination (optional).
