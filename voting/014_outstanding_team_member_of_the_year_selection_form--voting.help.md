# Outstanding Team Member Of The Year Selection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect nominations for the Outstanding Team Member Of The Year award. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the team member being nominated, including their name, role, and qualifications for the award. This information will be used to evaluate and select the team member who will receive the award.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Name" field with the name of the team member being nominated.
2. Enter the email address of the team member being nominated in the "Email" field.
3. Select the team member's role from the "Role" dropdown menu.
4. Choose the team member you are nominating from the "Team Member" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the year the team member qualified for the award in the "Year Qualified" field.
6. Cast your vote by selecting the time you wish to allocate for this nomination.
7. Add any additional comments or reasons for your nomination in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination Form**: This is the main form section where you will enter the information about the team member being nominated.
* **Name**: Enter the name of the team member being nominated.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the team member being nominated. This will help us contact the team member if needed.
* **Role**: Select the team member's role in the team, such as Team Leader, Project Manager, or Developer.
* **Team Member**: Choose the team member you are nominating. Select the team member that has worked with you or is part of the same team.
* **Year Qualified**: Enter the year the team member qualified for the award. Typically, this would be the year they started working on their current role or project.
* **Vote**: Cast your vote by allocating the time you wish to allocate for this nomination. This will help determine how many points the team member receives.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or reasons why the team member deserves the award. This could include their achievements, contributions, or challenges they overcame.
