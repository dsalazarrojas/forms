# Best Booth Award Voting - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and ratings from attendees about their experience at the event. The form is divided into three main sections: "Voter Registration", "Vote for Best Booth", and "Evaluation Criteria". 

## How To Complete This Form

### 1. Voter Registration
To start, please fill out the "Voter Registration" section with your information. This includes:
* **Full Name**: Your full name as it appears on your event badge or ID card.
* **Email Address**: Your email address, which we will use to send you updates about next year's event.
* **Ticket Number**: Your event ticket number, located on your event badge or confirmation email.

### 2. Vote for Best Booth
Next, please select your favorite booth by rating the "Booth Number" field. You can find the booth number on your event badge or confirmation email. Then, describe what you liked most about this booth by filling out the "Company or Booth Name" field.

### 3. Evaluation Criteria
Now, rate each of the evaluation criteria below on a scale from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent). Please be honest and provide feedback that will help us improve future events.

* **Visual Appeal and Design**: How well was the booth visually appealing and designed?
* **Staff Friendliness and Engagement**: How friendly and engaging were the staff at the booth?
* **Information and Content Quality**: How good was the information and content provided at the booth?
* **Interactivity and Engagement Tools**: Were the engagement tools and activities provided at the booth engaging and effective?
* **Originality and Innovation**: How original and innovative was the booth design and content?

### 4. Confirmation
Finally, please confirm your marketing consent by selecting "Yes - I would like to stay informed" if you would like to receive updates about next year's event. Then, select a date for submission.

**Tips**

* Please be honest and provide feedback that will help us improve future events.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, please feel free to use the "One thing the exhibitor could improve" field.
* If you would like to stay informed about next year's event, please select "Yes - I would like to stay informed" in the "Consent for Future Event Marketing" field.
