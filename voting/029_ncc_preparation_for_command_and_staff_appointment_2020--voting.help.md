## Purpose

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Start by selecting the type of appointment you are making from the options provided in the "Command Staff Type" field.
* Enter the name of the command staff member you are appointing in the "Command Staff Name" field.
* Choose the date and time of the appointment by using the date and time picker in the "Date of Appointment" field.
