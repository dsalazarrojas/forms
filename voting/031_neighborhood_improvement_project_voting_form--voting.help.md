# Neighborhood Improvement Project Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Neighborhood Improvement Project Voting Form is a tool used to gather feedback and suggestions from community members on neighborhood improvement projects. This form helps local organizations or governments understand what projects are needed or desired in the community and how to prioritize them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the first page with your basic information.
2. On the second page, provide more details about the neighborhood improvement project you are voting for.
3. On the third page, provide your contact information so we can get back to you with any questions or updates.
4. If you have any additional comments or thoughts, you can provide them on the fourth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Page 1: Neighborhood Improvement Project Voting Form** (`neighborhood_improvement_project_voting_form_page_1`, text, required)
Enter your name and any other contact information you'd like to share.
### **Page 2: Project Details** (`neighborhood_improvement_project_voting_form_page_2`, text, required)
Describe the neighborhood improvement project you are voting for. Provide as much detail as you'd like, but be clear and concise so we can understand your request.
### **Page 3: Contact Information** (`neighborhood_improvement_project_voting_form_page_3`, text, required)
Provide your name, email address, and any other contact information so we can get back to you with any questions or updates.
### **Page 4: Additional Comments** (`neighborhood_improvement_project_voting_form_page_4`, text, required)
If you have any additional comments or thoughts, you can provide them here.
## Tips
* Please provide clear and concise information for each field to help us understand your request.
* You can vote on multiple projects, but you'll need to fill out the form multiple times with a different project name each time.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask, and we'll do our best to clarify the information.
