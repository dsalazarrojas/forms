# Senior Superlatives Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Senior Superlatives form is designed to collect feedback, preferences, and information about senior individuals who have mentored or guided students. This form is intended for individuals who have had positive interactions with a senior and want to acknowledge their contributions. It helps to provide valuable insights and recommendations to the Senior Superlatives, which can be used for future mentorship programs and recognition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and the senior's name you are submitting feedback about.
2. Select your relationship with the senior (e.g., Friend, Mentor, Colleague, Classmate, Other).
3. Describe your feelings about the senior.
4. Provide any additional feedback or comments you have about the senior.
5. Choose your preferences for the senior (e.g., yes or no).
6. Share your demographic information (if any).
7. Describe your experiences with the senior (if any).
8. Recommend other seniors for recognition (if any).
9. Confirm that you are ready to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name?** (text, required): Please provide your full name so that we can contact you for any further questions or clarification.
* **What is the Senior Superlatives' name?** (text, required): Provide the name of the senior you are submitting feedback about.
* **What is your relationship to the Senior Superlatives?** (select multiple, required): Choose how you are related to the senior (e.g., Friend, Mentor, Colleague, Classmate, Other).
* **How do you feel about the Senior Superlatives?** (text, optional): Share your feelings or experiences about the senior.
* **Additional feedback or comments about the Senior Superlatives?** (note, optional): Provide any additional thoughts or comments about the senior.
* **What are your preferences for the Senior Superlatives?** (select multiple, optional): Choose your preferences for the senior (e.g., yes or no).
* **What is your demographic information?** (date, optional): If you have demographic information (e.g., year of graduation), please provide it.
* **Can you describe your senior experiences?** (text, optional): Share any experiences or stories about the senior.
* **Who do you recommend for Senior Superlatives?** (select one, optional): Suggest another senior for recognition (or choose "Other" for a specific senior not listed).
* **Are you ready to submit?** (radio, required): Confirm that you are ready to submit the form.
