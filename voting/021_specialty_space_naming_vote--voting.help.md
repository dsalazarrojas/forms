# Specialty Space Naming Vote - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Specialty Space Naming Vote form is used to collect feedback and ratings on the specialty space naming options provided. This form is part of the decision-making process to choose the best name for a particular specialty space.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, select the options that you prefer for the specialty space name from the available options.
2. You can select only one or multiple options, depending on your preferences.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, you can provide them in the comments field.
4. Once you have selected all the options and added comments, click "Submit" to submit your vote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **specialty space naming vote**: 
  This is a select multiple field where you can choose multiple options for the specialty space name. Choose the options that you prefer the most.
* **name vote 1**: 
  Enter the first name of your preference for the specialty space. This can be a single word or a phrase.
* **name vote 2**: 
  Enter the second name of your preference for the specialty space. This can be a single word or a phrase.
* **name vote 3**: 
  Enter the third name of your preference for the specialty space. This can be a single word or a phrase.
* **ranking 1**: 
  Assign a ranking of 1 to the name that you prefer the most.
* **ranking 2**: 
  Assign a ranking of 2 to the name that you prefer the second most.
* **ranking 3**: 
  Assign a ranking of 3 to the name that you prefer the third most.
* **comments**: 
  Add any additional comments or suggestions you have about the specialty space naming options.

## Tips
* Make sure to select only the options that you prefer, and not what you think others may prefer.
* Use the rankings to give more weight to the names you prefer more.
* The comments field is there for you to provide any additional context or suggestions. 

Please note that the form requires no technical skills or knowledge to be completed. Just select the options and provide comments as per your preferences. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please ask your team member or project lead.
