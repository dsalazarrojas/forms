# Favorite Poster Voting - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form allows users to cast their votes for their favorite poster. The purpose of this form is to collect data for analysis or voting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your first name in the text field labeled "First Name".
* Enter your last name in the text field labeled "Last Name".
* Select "Yes" or "No" for each category of your favorite poster from the corresponding select multiple fields.
* Enter a valid email address in the email field labeled "Email".
* Optionally, enter your phone number, date, and time if desired.
* Enter any additional comments in the text area labeled "Additional Comments".
* Submit the form to cast your vote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`form_1`, `text`, required): Enter your first name here.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`form_2`, `text`, required): Enter your last name here.

### Favorite Poster
* **Favorite Poster** (`form_3`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" for your favorite poster.

### Email
* **Email** (`form_4`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address here.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`form_5`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number, if desired.

### Date
* **Date** (`form_6`, `date`, optional): Enter the date, if desired.

### Time
* **Time** (`form_7`, `time`, optional): Enter the time, if desired.

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`form_8`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments, if desired.

### First
* **First** (`form_9`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select "Yes" for your favorite poster in this category.

### Second
* **Second** (`form_10`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select "Yes" for your favorite poster in this category.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter a valid email address in the "Email" field.
* Ensure that you have selected "Yes" or "No" for each category in the "Favorite Poster" fields.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact your administrator for assistance.
