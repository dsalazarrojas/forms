# Voting Assistance Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report a voter's absence from voting, allowing the relevant authorities to verify the voter's identity and accurately record their absence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Ensure you provide a valid reason for the voter's absence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **voting_assistance_absence_form**: This is the title of the form, which you should leave blank as it is a descriptive field.
* **voter_email**: This field is for the voter's email address. Please ensure you enter a valid email address that belongs to the voter.
* **voter_phone**: This field is for the voter's phone number. If the voter has a phone number, please enter it for contact purposes.
* **voter_address**: This field is for the voter's address. Please enter the address where the voter is currently residing.
* **reason_for_absence**: This field is for the reason why the voter is absent. Please enter a clear and concise description of the reason.
* **expected_return_date**: This field is for the expected date of the voter's return. Please enter the date when the voter is expected to return, if known.
