# Best Mentor Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Best Mentor Voting Form is designed to identify and recognize outstanding mentors in various categories. This form helps the community to acknowledge and appreciate the contributions of these mentors, and to provide guidance on how to improve the mentorship experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and email address to ensure that you can receive confirmation of your submission.
2. Enter the name of the mentor you are nominating, including their title or position.
3. Provide the name of the mentor's organization or institution.
4. Select the category of mentorship provided by the mentor (e.g., academic, career, business, etc.).
5. Rate the duration of the mentorship and your relationship duration with the mentor.
6. Rate the quality of guidance, availability, knowledge, inspiration, and other relevant qualities of the mentor.
7. Share a specific instance where the mentor's guidance made a significant difference.
8. Provide additional comments or reasons why the mentor deserves recognition.
9. Finally, indicate whether you would recommend this mentor to others seeking guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Your Name** (1): Enter your full name to confirm your identity and ensure that you can receive confirmation of your submission.
  • Type: text
  • Required: true

- **Email Address** (2): Enter the email address where you would like to receive confirmation of your submission.
  • Type: email
  • Required: true

- **Organization or Institution** (3): Enter the name of the organization or institution where the mentor works or volunteers.
  • Type: text
  • Required: false

- **Nominated Mentor Full Name** (4): Enter the full name of the mentor you are nominating.
  • Type: text
  • Required: true

- **Mentor Title or Position** (5): Enter the title or position of the mentor.
  • Type: text
  • Required: true

- **Mentor Organization** (6): Enter the name of the mentor's organization or institution.
  • Type: text
  • Required: true

- **Mentorship Category** (7): Select the category of mentorship provided by the mentor.
  • Type: select_one
  • Required: true

- **Duration of Mentorship** (8): Rate the duration of the mentorship.
  • Type: select_one
  • Required: true

- **Relationship Duration** (9): Rate the duration of your relationship with the mentor.
  • Type: select_one
  • Required: true

- **Quality of Guidance Rating** (10): Rate the quality of guidance provided by the mentor.
  • Type: select_one
  • Required: true

- **Availability Rating** (11): Rate the availability of the mentor when you need them.
  • Type: select_one
  • Required: true

- **Knowledge and Expertise Rating** (12): Rate the mentor's level of knowledge in their field.
  • Type: select_one
  • Required: true

- **Inspiration and Motivation Rating** (13): Rate how well the mentor inspires and motivates you.
  • Type: select_one
  • Required: true

- **Describe Their Impact** (14): Share a specific instance where the mentor's guidance made a difference.
  • Type: text
  • Required: true

- **Standout Qualities** (15): Select the standout qualities that make this mentor exceptional.
  • Type: select_multiple
  • Required: true

- **Share a Success Story** (16): Share a specific instance where the mentor's guidance made a difference.
  • Type: text
  • Required: false

- **Would You Recommend This Mentor** (17): Indicate whether you would recommend this mentor to others seeking guidance.
  • Type: select_one
  • Required: true

- **Additional Comments** (18): Provide any other reasons why this mentor deserves recognition.
  • Type: text
  • Required: false
