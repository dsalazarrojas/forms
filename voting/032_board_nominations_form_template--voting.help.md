# Board Nominations Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Board Nominations Form Template is designed to gather all the necessary details for a board position nomination. Please use this template to submit a nomination for a board member.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Nominee's name, role, and qualifications in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter the Nominator's name and email address for confirmation purposes.
3. Confirm that the nominee has given their consent for the nomination.
4. Attach any supporting documents if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Nominee name**: Enter the full name of the person being nominated.
- **Nominee contact email**: Enter the email address of the person being nominated.
- **Nominator name**: Enter the name of the person submitting the nomination.
- **Role nominated for**: Enter the specific position on the board that the nominee is being nominated for.
- **Qualifications summary**: Briefly explain why the nominee is qualified for the position.
- **Consent obtained from nominee**: Confirm that the nominee has given their consent for the nomination. Choose 'True' if consent has been obtained, and 'False' otherwise.
- **Supporting documents description**: Provide a brief description of any attached documents that support the nomination.
