# Workplace Committee Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Committee Voting Form is a tool for committee members to cast their votes on various topics relevant to the workplace. This form is designed to collect their input and opinions to inform decisions made by the committee.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully to understand the questions and topics presented.
* Make sure you are an authorized committee member to complete this form.
* Answer each question accurately and honestly based on your knowledge and experience.
* For the "Yes/No" question on page 5, select "Yes" if you agree with the topic, and "No" if you do not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **workplace_committee_voting_form_page_1** (`page_1`, note, required: false):
    This page is for general comments and notes about the workplace.
* **workplace_committee_voting_form_page_2** (`page_2`, text, required: false):
    This field is for any additional information or comments about the topics presented.
* **workplace_committee_voting_form_page_3** (`page_3`, email, required: false):
    This field is for your email address, which will be used to contact you in case we need to follow up on your comments.
* **workplace_committee_voting_form_page_4** (`page_4`, number, required: false):
    This field is for any numerical value or rating you want to provide, such as a score or a percentage.
* **workplace_committee_voting_form_page_5** (`page_5`, select_multiple, required: false):
    This question asks if you agree or disagree with a particular topic. Select "Yes" if you agree, and "No" if you do not.
