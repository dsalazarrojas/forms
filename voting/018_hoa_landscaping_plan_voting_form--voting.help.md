# HOA Landscaping Plan Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HOA Landscaping Plan Voting Form is a tool for residents to provide feedback and opinions on proposed landscaping plans for the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the proposed landscaping options and select your preferred choice(s) from the dropdown menu.
2. Decide whether you support or oppose the proposed plan and choose your answer from the dropdown menu.
3. If you have any thoughts or concerns about the proposed plan, please provide your comments in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Proposed Options** (`proposed_options`, select multiple, required: false): Select all or some of the proposed options for the landscaping plan. You can choose one, two, or multiple options.
* **Resident Votes** (`resident_votes`, select multiple, required: false): Choose whether you support or oppose the proposed plan. You can select one or both options.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): If you have any thoughts or concerns about the proposed plan, please provide them in this text field.
* **HOA Landscaping Plan Voting Form** (`title`, text, required: false): This is just a placeholder field with the name of the form. Please ignore this field.
