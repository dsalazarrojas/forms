# Patron Saint Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patron Saint Selection Survey is a form designed to collect information from users regarding their selection of a patron saint and additional relevant details. This form is used to gather data for [internal purposes, e.g., for church records or research]. Please ensure the information provided is accurate and complete.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the patron saint from the list provided.
2.  Provide the reason for choosing the patron saint (optional).
3.  Enter the name of the church associated with the selection (optional).
4.  Add any other relevant information (optional).
5.  Select the submission date (required).
6.  Enter your name or submitter information (required).
7.  Add any additional church information (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Select a Patron Saint** (`Select a Patron Saint`, `Select One`, required): Select the patron saint that has a significant meaning or connection for you. Please choose one from the provided list (Saint John the Baptist, Saint Mary, Saint Paul, Saint Michael, Saint Peter, Saint Francis of Assisi).
  - This field is required as it is an essential part of the selection process.

- **Why did you choose this patron saint?** (`Reason for Choice`, `Text`, optional): If you have a special reason for choosing this patron saint, please provide it here.
  - This field is optional and is used to gather additional context about the selection.

- **Church Name** (`Church Name`, `Text`, optional): If you are representing a church, please enter the name of the church associated with your selection.
  - This field is optional and allows users to link their selection to a specific church entity.

- **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `Text`, optional): Add any additional contact information relevant to your selection.
  - This field is optional and is used for internal purposes such as follow-up or correspondence.

- **Other Information** (`Other Information`, `Text`, optional): Add any other relevant details about your selection.
  - This field is optional and is used to provide additional context or information not captured in other fields.

- **Submission Date** (`Submission Date`, `Date`, required): Select the date of submission.
  - This field is required to track the date of submission.

- **Submitter** (`Submitter`, `Text`, required): Enter your name or submitter information.
  - This field is required to attribute the submission to the user.

- **Church Information** (`Church Information`, `Text`, optional): Add any additional church-related information not captured in other fields.
  - This field is optional and used for internal purposes such as record-keeping.
