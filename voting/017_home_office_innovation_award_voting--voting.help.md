# Home Office Innovation Award Voting - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Office Innovation Award is a recognition program that celebrates innovative ideas and projects that have positively impacted the work and lives of our team members. This form allows you to cast your vote for the innovation award, which will help us identify and recognize the best ideas and projects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Organization" field and enter your organization's name.
2. In the "Question" field, briefly describe the innovation that you would like to nominate.
3. For each choice field (e.g. Choice 1, Choice 2, etc.), select the innovation that you think best represents the innovation award. If none of the provided choices match your idea, you can select "Other" and describe your choice in the "Other" field.
4. In the "Comment" field, provide any additional comments or feedback about the innovation.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field to receive a confirmation of your vote.
6. Click the "Submit" button to submit your vote.
