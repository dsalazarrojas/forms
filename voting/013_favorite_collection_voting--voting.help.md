# Favorite Collection Voting - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather user feedback on their favorite collections. Completing this form will help us understand which collections are most popular among users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm if your collection is a favorite.
2. Select multiple options to vote for which of your collections you like most.
3. Input the number that represents the degree of your enthusiasm for your favorite collection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Select One** (`Select One`, optional): This field allows you to confirm whether your collection is a favorite or not. Select "Yes" if your collection is a favorite, or "No" otherwise.
* **Vote For** (`Vote For`, optional, multiple selections allowed): This field allows you to select multiple collections that you like. Select all collections that apply.
* **Favorite Collection** (`Favorite Collection`, optional): This field is intended for you to input the number that represents the degree of your enthusiasm for your favorite collection. For example, if you have 5 collections that you like, input 5. If you have 10, input 10. If you have no collections that you like, input 0. 

## Tips
* Be honest in your responses. Your feedback is important to us.
