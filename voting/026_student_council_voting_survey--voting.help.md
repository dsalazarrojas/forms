# student_council_voting_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for students to vote for candidates in student council elections, ensuring that each student has a voice in electing their representatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the list of candidates carefully and select the candidates you wish to vote for from each of the select multiple fields.
2. Ensure that you have not already completed this form to avoid duplicate entries.
3. Fill out the form by selecting your choices and providing any additional information required (if any).
4. Submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate 1** (`candidate_1`, select_multiple, required): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to vote for or against this candidate.
* **Candidate 2** (`candidate_2`, select_multiple, required): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to vote for or against this candidate.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): If applicable, enter your age to help us better understand our voter demographics.
* **Vote Count** (`vote_count`, number, optional): Enter the number of votes you have cast for the current election cycle.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you have one associated with your student profile.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you have one associated with your student profile.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or remarks about the election.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select your preferred date for voting (if applicable).
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select your preferred time for voting (if applicable).
* **Candidate 3** (`candidate_3`, select_multiple, optional): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to vote for or against this candidate.
* **Candidate 4** (`candidate_4`, select_multiple, optional): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to vote for or against this candidate.

## Tips
* Always ensure that you have not already completed this form before casting another vote.
* Review your selections carefully before submitting your form.
