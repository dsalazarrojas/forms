# Voter Registration Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect consent from individuals who wish to participate in the voting process. It is a one-time registration process that verifies your identity and ensures you are a genuine voter.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select your response to the question "Voter Registration Consent Form" on page 1 by clicking on one of the options "Yes" or "No".
2.  Enter your name, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields on page 2.
3.  Enter your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY" in the "Date of Birth" field on page 3.
4.  Enter your address in the "Address" field on page 4.
5.  Enter your city in the "City" field on page 5.
6.  Select your state of residence from the options provided in the "State" field on page 6.
7.  Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field on page 7.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Voter Registration Consent Form** (`page 1`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question confirms your agreement to participate in the voting process. Select "Yes" if you consent to participate and "No" if you do not.
* **Name** (`page 2`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for your first and last name.
* **Email** (`page 2`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`page 2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you in case of any issue.
* **Date of Birth** (`page 3`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY" to verify your age eligibility.
* **Address** (`page 4`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full address where you reside.
* **City** (`page 4`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your city of residence.
* **State** (`page 6`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your state of residence from the provided options.
* **Zip** (`page 7`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your zip code that we can use to verify your address.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all the fields are filled accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us at your provided email or phone number.
* If you have already completed this form, you will not be able to complete it again. Please do not attempt to register multiple times.
