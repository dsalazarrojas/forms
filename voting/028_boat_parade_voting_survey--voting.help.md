# Boat Parade Voting Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and ratings from spectators and participants of the boat parade event. Your input will help event organizers understand what made the parade enjoyable and what can be improved upon for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your name (optional) and email (optional) to help us contact you if needed.
2. Select your voter type (Spectator, Participant, Judge, or Event Staff).
3. Choose your favorite boat in each category (Best Decorated, Best Classic, Best Family, Best Crew Costume, People's Choice, Most Creative).
4. Rate the overall parade and select your favorite boat overall.
5. Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
6. Let us know if you will attend next year's parade.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Voter Name** (1): Your name (optional). If you leave this blank, we'll not be able to contact you for any questions or concerns.
* **Voter Email** (2): Your email address (optional). If you provide an email, we'll use it to contact you for any questions or concerns.
* **Voter Type** (3): Select Spectator, Participant, Judge, or Event Staff to indicate your role in the parade. This is a required field.
* **Viewing Location** (4): Choose where you watched the parade from: Shoreline - North, Shoreline - South, On Another Boat, VIP Stand, Online Stream, or Other.
* **Best Decorated Boat** (5): Select your favorite decorated boat from the options.
* **Best Classic Boat** (6): Select your favorite classic boat from the options.
* **Best Family Boat** (7): Select your favorite family boat from the options.
* **Best Crew Costume** (8): Select your favorite crew costume from the options.
* **People's Choice Award** (9): Select your favorite boat overall.
* **Most Creative Entry** (10): Select your most creative favorite boat from the options.
* **Overall Favorite Boat** (11): Write in your favorite boat in the text box.
* **Overall Parade Rating** (12): Rate the parade on a scale of 1 to 5, where 5 is Excellent and 1 is Poor.
* **Best Part of the Parade** (13): Write in what you enjoyed most about the parade.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (14): Write any suggestions you have to improve the parade.
* **Will You Attend Next Year?** (15): Select whether you will definitely attend, probably attend, maybe attend, probably not attend, or definitely not attend.
* **Additional Comments** (16): Write any additional comments or feedback you have.

Please take a few minutes to fill out this form and help us make the boat parade even better!
