# Readers Choice Awards Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to cast votes in the Readers Choice Awards. Please use it to select the categories, books, authors, dates, times, notes, emails, or phone numbers you want to participate with.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each page and select the categories that apply to you.
2.  Make sure to answer all questions and provide relevant information on each page.
3.  If you need help with a specific field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Select Category** (`Select Category`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is used to select a category for your vote. Choose one option from the dropdown menu that best fits your choice. The available options are "Yes" and "No".
*   **Select Book** (`Select Book`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field is used to select one or more books for your vote. You can choose multiple books from the dropdown menu.
*   **Select Author** (`Select Author`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is used to select an author for your vote. Choose one option from the dropdown menu that best fits your choice. The available options are "Yes" and "No".
*   **Select Date** (`Select Date`, `date`, required: false): This field is used to enter a date for your vote. The format should be mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **Select Time** (`Select Time`, `time`, required: false): This field is used to enter a time for your vote. The format should be hh:mm.
*   **Note** (`Note`, `note`, required: false): This field is used to provide any additional information related to your vote. You can type in any text you think is relevant.
*   **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): This field is used to enter your email address. Please make sure to enter a valid email address.
*   **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to enter your phone number. Please make sure to enter a valid phone number.
