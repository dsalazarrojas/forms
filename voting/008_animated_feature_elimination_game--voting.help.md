# Animated Feature Elimination Game - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animated Feature Elimination Game is a voting game that allows participants to eliminate features one by one until only one remains. The game aims to determine the most popular animated feature among the six provided options: Hand-drawn Animation, CGI 3D Animation, Stop-Motion Animation, Motion Capture Technology, Traditional Cel Animation, and Digital Vector Animation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your participant information, including your name and email address.
2.  Select your age group to help us understand your demographics.
3.  Read the game rules and instructions on how the elimination game works.
4.  In each round, vote to eliminate one feature that you like the least.
5.  Continue voting until only one feature remains.
6.  Select your favorite animated feature among the remaining options.
7.  Provide feedback on your experience with the game.
8.  Share your anonymized results on social media (optional).
