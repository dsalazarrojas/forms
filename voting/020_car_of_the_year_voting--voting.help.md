# Car Of The Year Voting - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Car Of The Year Voting" form is designed to gather information from users about their opinions and preferences regarding various vehicles in different categories. This form will help decide which vehicles are the best of the year and provide valuable insights to car manufacturers and enthusiasts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Start Voting" button to begin.
2. Fill in your name, email address (optional), and location (optional).
3. Select your age group from the provided options.
4. Indicate if you are an automotive enthusiast.
5. Choose your top pick in each of the vehicle categories (Car, SUV, Truck, Electric Vehicle, Sports Car, Luxury Vehicle, and Best Value Vehicle).
6. Select your preferred fuel type.
7. Indicate when you plan to buy your next vehicle.
8. Choose your budget range.
9. Select your favorite brand.
10. Provide any additional comments or feedback.
11. Rate your overall impression of this year's models.
12. Identify the most standout feature of this year's vehicles.
13. Mention any disappointment.
14. Add any additional comments.
15. Confirm your vote by clicking on the "Submit" button.
