# Christmas Party Theme Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Christmas Party Theme Voting Form is designed to collect feedback and preferences from employees for this year's party. It's a fun and interactive way to get everyone's thoughts on themes, activities, drinks, music, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your name and department.
2. Cast your vote for the primary theme you prefer.
3. Select up to two secondary theme choices if the primary one isn't selected.
4. Choose your favorite activities from the list.
5. Pick your preferred signature cocktail or mocktail style.
6. Select your preferred music genres.
7. If you have an idea for a theme not listed, feel free to suggest it.
8. Share your favorite part of last year's party (optional).
9. Indicate your likelihood of attending the party on a Friday night.
10. Click submit to send your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID or email.
* **Department**: Select your department to track participation by team.
* **-- Cast Your Vote --**: This note section is for your attention, but it does not require a response.
* **Which theme do you prefer for this year's party?**: Choose one from the list of themes provided.
* **Second Choice (if primary is not selected)**: If you prefer a different theme than your primary choice, select it here.
* **Which activity would you enjoy most?**: Choose up to two activities from the list.
* **Preferred Signature Cocktail/Mocktail Style**: Select your preferred style of drink.
* **Preferred Music Genres**: Choose up to two music genres from the list.
* **Have a theme idea not listed?**: If you have a different theme idea, type it in here.
* **What was your favorite part of last year's party?**: Share your favorite moment from last year's party (optional).
* **How likely are you to attend on a Friday night?**: Select how likely you are to attend the party on a Friday night.

## Tips
- Be honest and have fun with it!
- If you have any other suggestions or ideas, don't hesitate to share them.
- Remember, your input will help shape this year's party into something special!
