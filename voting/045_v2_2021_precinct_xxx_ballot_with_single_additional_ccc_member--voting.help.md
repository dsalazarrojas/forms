## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Precinct Xxx ID** (`precinct_xxx_id`, number, required/optional: optional): Enter the precinct ID number.
* **Additional CCC ID** (`additional_ccc_id`, number, required/optional: optional): Enter the additional CCC member's ID number.
* **Additional CCC Name** (`additional_ccc_name`, text, required/optional: optional): Enter the additional CCC member's name.
* **Additional CCC Party** (`additional_ccc_party`, select_one, required/optional: optional): Select the additional CCC member's party affiliation (e.g., Democratic, Republican, Independent, Other).
* **Additional CCC Address** (`additional_ccc_address`, text, required/optional: optional): Enter the additional CCC member's address.
* **Additional CCC Email** (`additional_ccc_email`, email, required/optional: optional): Enter the additional CCC member's email address.
* **Additional CCC Phone** (`additional_ccc_phone`, text, required/optional: optional): Enter the additional CCC member's phone number.

## Tips
