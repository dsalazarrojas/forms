# Sports Tournament Location Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sports Tournament Location Voting Form is designed to collect user input on potential venues for a sports tournament. This form helps organizers gather information about different locations, their capacity, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are submitting a new location not already listed in the form.
2. Select the type of location (e.g., stadium, arena, etc.) from the text field.
3. Enter the name of the location in the text field.
4. Provide the venue capacity (number) in the corresponding field.
5. Enter the address of the location in the text field.
6. Enter the contact phone number of the location in the text field.
7. Enter the contact email of the location in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Location Type** (`location_type`, text, optional): Select the type of location (e.g., stadium, arena, etc.) that best describes the venue where the tournament will take place.
- **Location Name** (`location_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the location in a way that will help identify it clearly.
- **Venue Capacity** (`venue_capacity`, number, optional): Provide the maximum number of participants or attendees the venue can accommodate.
- **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the physical address of the location.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Provide the phone number of the contact person or organization responsible for the location.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person or organization responsible for the location.

## Tips
- Double-check the provided information for accuracy.
- Be concise and clear in your responses.
- Ensure you're submitting a new location not already listed in the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, ask for clarification before submitting.
