# Gratitude And Recognition Voting - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to facilitate a process where users can submit nominations and recognitions for individuals or groups they wish to appreciate. This form will ask users to provide details about the person or group being recognized, including why they want to recognize them and the award they think they deserve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Nominations**: Provide the name of the person or group you want to recognize in this field.
2.  **Who Do You Want To Recognize?**: Select all the individuals you wish to recognize from the dropdown menu.
3.  **Why Do You Want To Recognize Them?**: Provide a short explanation for each selected individual on why you want to recognize them. This field allows you to express your appreciation and gratitude for their actions or contributions.
4.  **Comments**: Add any additional comments or reasons for your nomination, if needed.
5.  **Award**: Choose an award that you think best suits the recognized individual or group.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nominations**: This is the field where you will input the name of the person or group you wish to recognize. You can type any name or names that you believe deserve appreciation.
*  **Who Do You Want To Recognize?**: This field is a dropdown menu where you can select one or multiple individuals who you want to give recognition to. Please choose only those who are being considered for recognition.
*  **Why Do You Want To Recognize Them?**: Here, you can provide a brief description of why you want to recognize the selected individual(s) in the previous field. Be as specific as possible to show your appreciation and gratitude.
*   **Comments**: This is an optional field where you can add any additional comments or reasons for your nomination. You can use this field to provide more context or details about your choice.
*  **Award**: This dropdown menu is where you can choose the best award that suits the recognized individual or group. Please select the award that you think best represents their achievement or contribution.
