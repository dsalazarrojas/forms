# ugly_christmas_sweater_contest_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participants' votes on the best ugly Christmas sweater in a contest. Users will be asked to provide their votes on the best ugly sweater based on different criteria.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in the form with the required information.
- Select the best ugly sweater from the provided options for each category.
- Upload a photo of your own ugly sweater (if possible).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Voting** (`voting`, text, required): Enter your name to participate in the contest.
- **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more categories from the provided options to indicate the type of ugly sweater you are voting for. Options are:
  - Sweater Color
  - Material
- **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name to associate with your vote.
- **Best of Show** (`best_of_show`, date, required): Choose a date when the participant's ugly sweater was best displayed. Note: This field is not related to a specific sweater but rather when the participant's sweater was worn or displayed.
- **Best of Party** (`best_of_party`, text, required): Enter a brief description of why your sweater was the best at a party or event.
- **Photo** (`photo`, text, required): Upload a photo of your ugly sweater.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Best of Sweater** (`best_of_sweater`, select_one, required): Select the best ugly sweater from the provided options.
- **Best of Sweater Color** (`best_of_sweater_color`, select_one, required): Select the color of the best ugly sweater from the provided options.
- **Best of Sweater Material** (`best_of_sweater_material`, select_one, required): Select the material of the best ugly sweater from the provided options.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields for a valid entry.
- If you are unable to upload a photo, please provide a brief description of your ugly sweater.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact us at your@email.com.
