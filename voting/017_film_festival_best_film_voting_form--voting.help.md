# Film Festival Best Film Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for festival attendees to cast their votes for the best film of the festival. We value your opinion and would like to know which films you enjoyed the most.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read through the options for your top choice for "Best Film".
2. Choose your favorite film from the list.
3. You can also provide feedback about your experience at the festival in the "Audience Feedback" section.
4. If you'd like to be kept in the loop for future festivals, please enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. If you'd like to share your feedback and email with the festival team, you can enter it in the "Audience Feedback Email" field.
6. Finally, if you'd like to provide more information about a specific film, you can do so in the "Film Title" field.
7. Enter the director's name in the "Director" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Best Film** (`best_film`, select_one, optional): Choose your top pick for the best film in the festival.
* **Audience Feedback** (`audience_feedback`, note, optional): Share your thoughts and feelings about your experience at the festival. This is an open-ended field, so feel free to express yourself!
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you'd like to be kept in the loop for future festivals, please enter your email address here.
* **Audience Feedback Email** (`audience_feedback_email`, email, optional): If you'd like to share your feedback and email with the festival team, enter it here.
* **Film Title** (`film_title`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide more information about a specific film, enter its title here.
* **Director** (`director`, text, optional): Enter the director's name of the film you'd like to comment on.

## Tips
* Please make sure to submit your form only once per person.
* If you have any trouble with the form, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We'll use your feedback to improve future festival experiences!
