# Company Slogan Voting - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on potential slogans for a company. You will be presented with five different slogans and asked to rate them on a scale from "Love it" to "Hate it". You will also be asked to select your favorite slogan and provide a reason for your choice. Additionally, you will be asked to suggest any alternative slogans and provide feedback on what makes a great company slogan in your opinion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name and email address in the "Full Name" and "Email Address" fields.
2. Choose your relationship to the company by selecting "Employee", "Customer", "Partner", "Vendor", or "Other" from the "I am a" dropdown menu.
3. If you are an employee, you can choose your department from the available options.
4. Review each of the five slogans and rate them on a scale from "Love it" to "Hate it" in the corresponding "Slogan X - Rating" fields (where X is the slogan letter, e.g., "Slogan A - Building Tomorrow Together").
5. In the "Which slogan is your favorite?" field, select the slogan that you like best.
6. Provide a reason for your favorite slogan selection in the "Why did you choose this slogan?" field.
7. Optional: In the "Least favorite slogan" field, select the slogan that you like least. If you don't have a strong opinion, select "No strong opinion".
8. Optional: In the "Why is this your least favorite slogan?" field, provide your reasoning.
9. Optional: If you have any feedback or suggestions for alternative slogans, provide them in the "Do you have any slogan suggestions?" field.
10. Optional: In the "What makes a great company slogan in your opinion?" field, provide your thoughts.
11. Optional: If you have any additional comments, you can enter them in the "Any additional comments?" field.
12. Finally, select the current date from the "Vote Date" field.

* **Department** (4): If you are an employee, select your department.
* **Slogan A - Rating** (6): Rate this slogan on a scale from "Love it" to "Hate it".
* **Slogan B - Rating** (7): Rate this slogan on a scale from "Love it" to "Hate it".
* **Slogan C - Rating** (8): Rate this slogan on a scale from "Love it" to "Hate it".

* **Slogan E - Rating** (10): Rate this slogan on a scale from "Love it" to "Hate it".
* **Which slogan is your favorite?** (12): Select the slogan that you like best.
* **Why did you choose this slogan?** (13): Provide a reason for your favorite slogan selection.
* **Least favorite slogan** (14): Select the slogan that you like least. If you don't have a strong opinion, select "No strong opinion".
* **Why is this your least favorite slogan?** (15): Provide your reasoning.
* **Do you have any slogan suggestions?** (22): If you have any feedback or suggestions for alternative slogans, provide them here.
