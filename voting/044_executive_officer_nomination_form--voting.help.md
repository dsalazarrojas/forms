# executive_officer_nomination_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Executive Officer Nomination Form is used to nominate individuals to become executive officers in various organizations. This form collects information about the nominee, the nominator (voter), and any additional message from the voter. The purpose of this form is to facilitate the executive officer nomination process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the necessary information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Executive Officer Nomination Form
* **Executive Officer Nomination Form** (`executive_officer_nomination_form`, note, required: false): This is the title of the form, providing a clear label for the purpose of the form.
* **Nominee Name** (`nominee_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the nominee being nominated.
* **Nominee Title** (`nominee_title`, text, required: false): Enter the title or position of the nominee.
* **Nominee Email** (`nominee_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the nominee. Please ensure the email address is valid and correctly formatted.
* **Nominee Phone** (`nominee_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the nominee.
* **Voter Name** (`voter_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as the voter.
* **Voter Email** (`voter_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address as the voter. Please ensure the email address is valid and correctly formatted.
* **Voter Phone** (`voter_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number as the voter.
* **Nominee Voter Message** (`nominee_voter_message`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information or message from the voter to the nominee.

## Tips
- Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
- If the nominee has no email address or phone number, you can leave those fields blank.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your administrator or help desk for assistance.
