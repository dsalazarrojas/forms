# Member Selection Ranking Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Member Selection Ranking Survey is a form used to collect information from members on their participation status and other relevant details. It helps the organization to track and monitor member contributions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Select Members" option to choose from the list of members.
2. Select up to 5 members you are interested in ranking. If you do not wish to select members, please leave this field blank.
3. Rank the selected members by choosing one of the options (A, B, C, D, or E).
4. Enter your name as it appears on official records.
5. Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for further information.
6. Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for further information.
7. Provide any additional comments about your selection or ranking.
8. Enter the date and time you are submitting this form.
9. Choose the submission status of your selection (New, In Progress, or Completed).
10. Optionally, you may enter an ID number for tracking purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Members** (`id: 1`, Select, optional): Please select up to 5 members you are interested in ranking.
* **Rank** (`id: 2`, Select One, required): Rank the selected members by choosing one of the options (A, B, C, D, or E).
* **Name** (`id: 3`, Text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on official records.
* **Email** (`id: 4`, Email, optional): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for further information.
* **Phone** (`id: 5`, Text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for further information.
* **Comment** (`id: 6`, Note, optional): Provide any additional comments about your selection or ranking.
* **Submission Date** (`id: 7`, Date, required): Enter the date and time you are submitting this form.
* **Submission Time** (`id: 8`, Time, optional): Enter the time you are submitting this form.
* **Submission Status** (`id: 9`, Select One, required): Choose the submission status of your selection (New, In Progress, or Completed).
* **Submission ID** (`id: 10`, Text, optional): Optionally, enter an ID number for tracking purposes.
