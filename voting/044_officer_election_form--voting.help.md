# Officer Election Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Officer Election Form" is used to collect information about candidates for an election within an organization or institution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your general information on page 1.
2. Move on to fill in the title and position you are applying for on page 2.
3. Provide your term start and end dates on page 3.
4. Specify who is issuing the vote and any additional comments on page 4.
5. Enter your contact information on page 5.
6. Confirm your submission details on page 6.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Informations** (`general_informations`, text, required/optional): This field is for general information about yourself or the entity you represent. Please fill it out with any relevant details you think would be helpful for the election process.
* **Title** (`title`, text, required/optional): Enter your title or the title of the entity you represent.
* **Position** (`position`, text, required/optional): Specify the position you are applying for or the position you represent.
* **Term Start** (`term_start`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of your term or the term start date of the entity you represent.
* **Term End** (`term_end`, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of your term or the term end date of the entity you represent.
* **Vote Issued By** (`vote_issued_by`, select_one, required/optional): Choose who is issuing the vote. Options include President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer, and Other.
* **Vote Issued By Other** (`vote_issued_by_other`, text, required/optional): Provide details if the vote is issued by someone other than listed above.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact information or the contact information of the entity you represent.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email or the email of the entity you represent.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number or the phone number of the entity you represent.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, required/optional): Provide your address or the address of the entity you represent.
* **Contact Note** (`contact_note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your contact information.
* **Vote Issued By Other Note** (`vote_issued_by_other_note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about who is issuing the vote.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required/optional): Confirm that the submission is true or not. Options include True, False, Not Applicable, Other, Not Provided, and Undecided.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required/optional): Choose who submitted the form.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date and time of submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of submission.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, required/optional): Enter your name or the name of the entity you represent.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email or the email of the entity you represent.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number or the phone number of the entity you represent.
* **Submitter Address** (`submitter_address`, text, required/optional): Provide your address or the address of the entity you represent.
* **Submitter Note** (`submitter_note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about who submitted the form.
* **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, text, required/optional): Enter the department of the entity you represent.
* **Submitter Job Title** (`submitter_job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your job title or the job title of the entity you represent.
* **Submitter Designation** (`submitter_designation`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional designations about who submitted the form.
