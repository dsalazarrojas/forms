# pumpkin_carving_contest_voting_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form allows users to participate in a pumpkin carving contest by casting votes for different categories and entries. Users can submit their ratings and comments for each entry, as well as provide additional information such as entry images and URLs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the category and entry you wish to vote for, and fill in the required fields. You can also add comments and ratings for each entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Voting Category** (`1`, `select_one`, required: false):
  * Select the category for which you want to vote.
* **Pumpkin Entry Name** (`2`, `text`, required: false):
  * Enter the name of the pumpkin entry you want to vote for.
* **Description** (`3`, `text`, required: false):
  * Enter a brief description of the pumpkin entry.
* **Category** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false):
  * Select the category you want to vote under.
* **Photo URL** (`5`, `text`, required: false):
  * Enter the URL of the photo of the pumpkin entry.
* **Rating** (`6`, `number`, required: false):
  * Enter a number rating (e.g. 1-10) for the pumpkin entry.
* **Comments** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
  * Select multiple comments you want to make for the pumpkin entry.
* **Submit** (`8`, `note`, required: false):
  * Note: This is the submit button, click it when you're finished filling out the form.
* **Email** (`9`, `email`, required: false):
  * Enter your email address for voting purposes.
* **Phone** (`10`, `text`, required: false):
  * Enter your phone number for voting purposes.
* **Submit Date** (`11`, `date`, required: false):
  * Enter the date you are submitting your vote.
* **Submit Time** (`12`, `time`, required: false):
  * Enter the time you are submitting your vote.
* **Rating Scale** (`13`, `select_one`, required: false):
  * Select the rating scale you wish to use (1-3, 4-6, 7-10).
* **Rating Comment** (`14`, `text`, required: false):
  * Enter a comment for your rating.
* **Entry ID** (`15`, `text`, required: false):
  * Enter the ID of the entry you are voting for.
* **User Name** (`16`, `text`, required: false):
  * Enter your name for voting purposes.
* **User Email** (`17`, `email`, required: false):
  * Enter your email address for voting purposes.
* **User Phone** (`18`, `text`, required: false):
  * Enter your phone number for voting purposes.
* **Rating Scale Note** (`19`, `note`, required: false):
  * Note: This is a note for the rating scale, enter any additional information.
* **Submit Note** (`20`, `note`, required: false):
  * Note: This is a note for submission.
* **Entry Image** (`21`, `text`, required: false):
  * Enter the image URL for the entry.
* **Image URL** (`22`, `text`, required: false):
  * Enter the URL of the image for the entry.
* **Rating Image** (`23`, `text`, required: false):
  * Enter the URL of the rating image.
* **Comments Image** (`24`, `text`, required: false):
  * Enter the URL of the comments image.
* **Category Image** (`25`, `text`, required: false):
  * Enter the URL of the category image.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting your vote.
* Use the correct format for dates and times (e.g. MM/DD/YYYY HH:MM:SS).
* Use the correct format for URLs (e.g. http://example.com/image.jpg).
