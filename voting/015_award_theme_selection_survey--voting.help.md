# Award Theme Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form aims to collect feedback and preferences from event attendees to ensure that the awards event meets their expectations and needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions carefully and honestly to provide accurate information about your preferences and expectations.
2. Fill out the form in a way that reflects your true opinions and feelings.
3. You can select one option from each dropdown menu or write in the text fields if you have more to express.
4. Make sure to answer all required fields (marked as "required") to help us better understand your needs.
