# Employee Of The Month Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee of the Month award is a recognition given to an employee who demonstrates exceptional performance and behavior. This form is used to collect nominations from team members to identify the winner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the list of nominated employees and select those who you believe have demonstrated exceptional performance and behavior.
2. Enter the name of the employee or employees you would like to nominate.
3. Vote count is not required for nomination.
4. Optionally provide a brief description of why you nominated the employee(s).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select The Winning Employee** (`Select The Winning Employee`, Select Multiple, false): Choose up to 2 employees who you believe have demonstrated exceptional performance and behavior. You can select multiple employees if you believe there are multiple winners.
* **Name** (`Name`, Text, false): Enter the name of the employee or employees you would like to nominate.
* **Vote Count** (`Vote Count`, Number, false): This field is optional and not required for nomination.
* **Description** (`Description`, Text, false): Provide a brief description of why you nominated the employee(s). This can be a short paragraph or a few sentences explaining their exceptional performance and behavior.
