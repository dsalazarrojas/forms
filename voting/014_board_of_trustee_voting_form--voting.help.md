# Board Of Trustee Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for the official voting process of a Board of Trustees, used to determine the outcome of various resolutions and decisions. It collects votes and comments from all Trustees and ensures the confidentiality and integrity of the voting process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1.  Read the relevant information about the resolutions and decisions before the meeting.
2. For each resolution, select your vote (In Favor, Opposed, or Abstain) from the provided options.
3. Enter your comments or feedback about the resolution, if you want to provide additional information.
4. Finally, sign your name and date and time of the vote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trustee Full Name**: 
  This field is required and should be filled in with your complete name, as a Trustee of the organization. 
  Please ensure that this is your official title, and not a nickname.
* **Date of Meeting**: 
  This field is a date picker that allows you to select the date of the meeting. 
  Ensure it matches the actual date of the meeting you are voting for.
* **Approval of Annual Budget for Fiscal Year 2026**: 
  This field is a selection field where you have to select In Favor, Opposed, or Abstain. 
  Please choose the option that reflects your vote regarding the resolution.
* **Comments on Resolution 1**: 
  This field is for providing optional feedback about Resolution 1. 
  You can enter any comments or thoughts you have about the resolution, and they will be considered as part of the voting process.
* **Adoption of the Revised Ethics and Conflict of Interest Policy**: 
  This field is a selection field where you have to select In Favor, Opposed, or Abstain. 
  Please choose the option that reflects your vote regarding the resolution.
* **Appointment of External Auditor for the upcoming year**: 
  This field is a selection field where you have to select In Favor, Opposed, or Abstain. 
  Please choose the option that reflects your vote regarding the resolution.
* **Trustee Signature - Type Full Name**: 
  This field is required and should be filled in with your complete name, as a Trustee of the organization. 
  Please ensure that this is your official title, and not a nickname.
* **Date and Time of Vote**: 
  This field is a date picker that allows you to select the date and time you have voted. 
  Ensure it matches the actual time you have voted.

## Tips
* Please fill in the form carefully and accurately, as your vote will reflect your official stance on the resolutions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the organization's administration.
* Make sure to sign your name correctly, as this is a formal document.
