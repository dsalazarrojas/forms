# Umbrella Design Voting Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and ratings on different design concepts. It is intended to gather input from users on their preferences and opinions on various design options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the design concept(s) that you prefer from the "Umbrella Concepts" field.
2. Rate each design concept by selecting a number from 1 to 5 in the corresponding "Ratings" fields.
3. Provide any additional feedback or comments in the "Feedback" field.
4. Select whether or not you agree with the design concept by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Votes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Umbrella Concepts (Select Multiple)**: Choose one or more of the design concepts that you prefer. The options are:
	+ Umbrella 1
	+ Umbrella 2
	+ Umbrella 3
* **Ratings**: Rate each design concept by selecting a number from 1 to 5. The more you like a design concept, the higher the number you should select.
* **Feedback (Text)**: Provide any additional feedback or comments on the designs.
* **Votes (Select One)**: Select "True" if you agree with the design concept, or "False" if you do not.
* **Rating 1, Rating 2, Rating 3, Rating 4, Rating 5 (Number)**: Rate each design concept by selecting a number from 1 to 5. The more you like a design concept, the higher the number you should select.
* **Total**: This field will automatically calculate the total number of preferences selected.
* **Rating Average**: This field will automatically calculate the average rating of all design concepts selected.
* **Rating Standard Deviation**: This field will automatically calculate the standard deviation of all ratings.
* **Design Preference (Select One)**: Select your preferred design color: Blue, Red, or Green.
* **Design Preference Count**: This field will automatically calculate the number of preferences selected.
* **Rating Standard Deviation (Number)**: This field will automatically calculate the standard deviation of all ratings.
* **Design Preference Average (Number)**: This field will automatically calculate the average preference count.

Tips:

* Please be honest and sincere in your ratings and feedback.
* You can select more than one design concept in the "Umbrella Concepts" field.
* You can only select one design concept in the "Design Preference" field.
* The ratings and feedback fields are not required, but they are strongly encouraged.
