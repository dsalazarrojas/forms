# Team Member Of The Month Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to recognize and appreciate team members who have demonstrated exceptional skills, work ethic, or teamwork within the team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select "Yes" or "No" for each of the questions regarding team members.
2. Choose the type of vote you wish to submit (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
3. Optionally, provide comments for the selected member.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Team Member Of The Month
* **Team Member Of The Month** (team_member_of_the_month_voting_form): A list of team members who you would like to see as Team Member Of The Month. Select "Yes" or "No" for each team member.

### 2. Select Member
* **Select Member** (select_member): Another list of team members you can choose from. Like the previous option, select "Yes" or "No" for each team member.

### 3. Vote Type
* **Vote Type** (vote_type): Choose the type of vote you want to submit (e.g., "Yes" or "No").

### 4. Submit Vote
* **Submit Vote** (submit_vote): This option determines whether you want to submit your vote or not (e.g., "Yes" or "No").

### 5. Member Of The Month
* **Member Of The Month** (member_of_the_month): Select "Yes" or "No" for the team member you wish to see as Team Member Of The Month.

### 6. Comments
* **Comments** (comments): Provide optional comments for the selected team member, explaining why you chose them. This field is not required, so you can leave it blank if you do not have any comments.

## Tips
- Review the team members carefully before making your selection.
- Avoid submitting duplicate or repetitive selections.
- Use the "Comments" field to provide feedback or suggestions for the selected team member.
