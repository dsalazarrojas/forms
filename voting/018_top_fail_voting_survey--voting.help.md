# top_fail_voting_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback and ratings from teams on a particular project or initiative. It is intended to be filled out by team members to provide their input on whether the project has failed or not, and if so, to provide a rating of the severity of the failure. The form is meant to be completed once per team member.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions carefully and answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select the teams that apply to your feedback in the "Team Select" field.
3. Provide a brief input on what went wrong in the "Fail Input" field.
4. Rate the severity of the failure in the "Severity Rating" field.
5. Provide additional explanation or comments in the "Explanation" field.
6. Give feedback on whether the audience was affected by the failure in the "Audience Feedback" field.
7. Answer the question of whether the project has failed or not in the "Vote Input" field.
8. If applicable, provide the time of the failure in the "Vote" field.
9. Finally, submit the form by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Select** (`team_select`, select_multiple, optional): Select all teams that apply to your feedback. This will help us understand which teams were impacted by the failure.
* **Fail Input** (`fail_input`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of what went wrong. Be as specific as possible.
* **Severity Rating** (`severity_rating`, number, optional): Rate the severity of the failure on a scale (e.g., 1-10). This will help us understand the magnitude of the failure.
* **Explanation** (`explanation`, text, optional): Provide additional context or explanation about what happened.
* **Audience Feedback** (`audience_feedback`, text, optional): Share your feedback on whether the audience was affected by the failure.
* **Vote Input** (`vote_input`, select_one, optional): Answer the question of whether the project has failed or not.
* **Vote** (`vote`, time, optional): If applicable, provide the time of the failure.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your feedback and complete the form.
