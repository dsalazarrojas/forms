# Simple Voting Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Simple Voting Form Template is a basic form for collecting user input on a given subject, suitable for casual polls or surveys. It is intended to gather users' opinions, preferences, or thoughts on a particular issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **User Name** in the provided text field. This is the field where you will enter your name or alias to identify yourself.
2. Select one or multiple options from the **Vote Option** field. This is where you will choose the options provided.
3. Enter the number of **Vote Count** you want to cast for your selected option(s). This will help us understand the magnitude of your preference.
4. If applicable, select a specific **Vote For** date when you would like your vote to count towards.
5. Select a specific time for **Vote Time** when you would like your vote to be counted.
6. If you have any additional comments or thoughts, you may add them in the **Comment** field.
7. Optionally, you may provide your **Email** and **Phone** for follow-up or notification purposes.
8. You may also add any additional **Note** that you feel is relevant to your input.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your name or alias to identify yourself.
* **Vote Option** (`vote_option`, select_multiple, required): Select one or multiple options for your preference.
* **Vote Count** (`vote_count`, number, optional): Enter the number of votes you want to cast for your selected option.
* **Vote For** (`vote_for`, date, optional): If applicable, select a date for your vote to count towards.
* **Vote Time** (`vote_time`, time, optional): Select a time for your vote to be counted.
* **Comment** (`comment`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Optionally, enter your email for follow-up or notification purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Optionally, enter your phone number for follow-up or notification purposes.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes that are relevant to your input.
