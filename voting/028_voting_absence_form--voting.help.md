# voting-absence-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The voting-absence-form is designed for voting absentee in an election or similar voting process. It gathers information about the absentee voter, including their name, contact information, and reason for absence.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2. Provide accurate and truthful information in each field.
3. Review your answers before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Voting Absence Form** (`page_1`, text, required): Please enter a title or description for the form, if necessary.
 
- **Individual** (`individual`, text, false): Enter your name or the name of the person voting absentee.
 
- **Reason for Absence** (`reason_for_absence`, text, required): Explain why you are voting absentee, e.g., illness, out of town, or work schedule conflict.
 
- **Absentee Name** (`absentee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person voting on your behalf.
 
- **Relationship** (`relationship`, text, required): Describe your relationship to the absentee voter.
 
- **Absentee Contact** (`absentee_contact`, text, required): Provide a contact method (e.g., phone or email) for the absentee voter.
 
- **Election Day** (`election_day`, date, required): Enter the date of the election.
 
- **Election Time** (`election_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the election.
 
- **Election Date Verified** (`election_date_verified`, date, false): This field is optional and likely used for verification purposes only.
 
- **Election Time Verified** (`election_time_verified`, time, false): This field is optional and likely used for verification purposes only.
 
- **Voter Verified** (`voter_verified`, text, false): This field is optional and likely used for verification purposes only.
 
- **Reason for Ineligibility** (`reason_for_ineligibility`, text, false): Explain why the person is not eligible to vote, if applicable.
 
- **Reason for Absentee** (`reason_for_absentee`, text, false): Provide an explanation for why the person is voting absentee, if applicable.
 
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, false): Enter any additional comments or explanations.
 
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, false): Enter any additional comments or explanations.
 
- **Additional Comments 2** (`additional_comments_2`, text, false): Enter any additional comments or explanations.
 
- **Comments Verified** (`comments_verified`, text, false): This field is optional and likely used for verification purposes only.
 
- **Comments Verified 2** (`comments_verified_2`, text, false): This field is optional and likely used for verification purposes only.
 
- **Voter Verified 2** (`voter_verified_2`, text, false): This field is optional and likely used for verification purposes only.
 
- **Election Office** (`election_office`, text, false): Enter the name of the election office or official.
 
- **Election Officer Verified** (`election_officer_verified`, text, false): This field is optional and likely used for verification purposes only.
 
- **Absentee Office** (`absentee_office`, text, false): Enter the name of the absentee office or official.
 
- **Absentee Office 2** (`absentee_office_2`, text, false): Enter the name of the absentee office or official.
 
- **Voting Location** (`voting_location`, text, false): Enter the location where the absentee vote will be cast.
 
- **Location** (`location`, text, false): Enter the location of the voter or absentee voter.
