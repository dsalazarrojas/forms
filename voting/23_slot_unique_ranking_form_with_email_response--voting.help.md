# 23 Slot Unique Ranking Form With Email Response - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data from users about their experience and satisfaction with the 23 Slot Unique Ranking Form With Email Response. It is intended to be used for gathering feedback and ratings on this specific form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email address" field. A confirmation will be sent to this address.
3. Select the date of your experience with the form in the "Date" field.
4. Rank the 23 items from 1 (highest priority) to 23 (lowest priority) in the corresponding fields (e.g. Rank for Item 1, Rank for Item 2, etc.).
5. Select your overall satisfaction level with the form in the "How satisfied are you with 23 Slot Unique Ranking Form With Email Response?" field.
6. Choose the aspects of the form that you found easy to use or not in the "Which aspects applied to your experience with 23 Slot Unique Ranking Form With Email Response?" field (select all that apply).
7. If you have any additional comments or feedback, enter them in the "Additional comments or notes" field.

- **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name in this field.
- **Email address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address in this field. A confirmation will be sent to this address.
- **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Select the date of your experience with the form.
- **Rank for Item 1** (`item_1_rank`, `integer`, required): Enter a unique number between 1 and 23 to rank Item 1.
- **Rank for Item 2** (`item_2_rank`, `integer`, required): Enter a unique number between 1 and 23 to rank Item 2.
- **...** (repeat for each item)
- **How satisfied are you with 23 Slot Unique Ranking Form With Email Response?** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Select your overall satisfaction level with the form.
- **Which aspects applied to your experience with 23 Slot Unique Ranking Form With Email Response?** (`experience_aspects`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the aspects of the form that you found easy to use or not (select all that apply).
- **Additional comments or notes** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required): Enter any additional comments or feedback you might have.
- **Overall rating** (`overall_rating`, `integer`, required): Enter a number between 1 and 10 to rate your overall experience with the form.
- **What would you improve or change about 23 Slot Unique Ranking Form With Email Response?** (`open_feedback`, `text`, optional): Enter any feedback you might have on how to improve the form.

## Tips
