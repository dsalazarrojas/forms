# Rotary Representative Election Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rotary Representative Election Survey is a form used for nominating and voting for representatives. It consists of several sections with fields that guide you through the nomination and voting process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the Nomination Form (page 1):
	* Enter your nomination in the text field (Nomination Form).
2. Select your preferred voting options (page 2):
	* Choose your "Yes" or "No" for each option in the Voting Form section.
3. Select a candidate from the Candidate List (page 3):
	* Choose the candidate you would like to vote for from the dropdown list.
4. Provide candidate and nominator information (page 4):
	* Enter relevant candidate and nominator information in the text fields.
5. Enter nominator contact information (page 5):
	* Enter your name, email, and website (if applicable) for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Nomination Form (Page 1)
**Nomination Form (Nomination Form)** (`Nomination Form`, `text`, required: false): Enter your nomination in this text field.

### Voting Form (Page 2)
**Voting Form (Voting Form)** (`Voting Form`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose your "Yes" or "No" for each option.

### Candidate List (Page 3)
**Candidate List (Candidate List)** (`Candidate List`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the candidate you would like to vote for from the dropdown list.

### Candidate Info (Page 4)
**Candidate Info (Candidate Info)** (`Candidate Info`, `text`, required: false): Enter relevant candidate information in this text field.

### Nominator Info (Page 4)
**Nominator Info (Nominator Info)** (`Nominator Info`, `text`, required: false): Enter relevant nominator information in this text field.

### Nominator (Page 5)
**Nominator (Nominator)** (`Nominator`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name for contact purposes.

### Email (Page 5)
**Email (Email)** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.

### Website (Page 5)
**Website (Website)** (`Website`, `text`, required: false): Enter your website (if applicable) for additional contact information.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your nomination is valid.
* Double-check your selections before submitting your vote.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date candidate and nominator information.
* Use a valid email address and website (if applicable) for contact purposes.
