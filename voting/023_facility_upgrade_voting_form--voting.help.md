# Facility Upgrade Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The facility upgrade voting form is used to gather input from staff regarding facility upgrades. This form helps the management team make informed decisions on which facilities to prioritize for upgrades.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Click on the "Facility Upgrade Voting Form" page.
*   Review the feedback questions provided.
*   Provide feedback on the facilities that need upgrades by selecting the priorities.
*   Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Facility Upgrade Voting Form** (`facility_upgrade_voting_form`, `note`, required/optional): This section provides the title of the form.
*   **Feedback** (`facility_upgrade_voting_feedback`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select "Yes" to indicate that you have feedback on this form.
*   **What is your feedback on this form?** (`facility_upgrade_voting_feedback`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your feedback on the form.
*   **Email** (`facility_upgrade_voting_feedback_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address if you want to receive updates on the facilities that have been upgraded.
*   **Phone** (`facility_upgrade_voting_feedback_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number if you want to receive updates on the facilities that have been upgraded.
*   **Priority 1** (`facility_upgrade_voting_priority`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the priority for the first facility that needs an upgrade.
*   **Priority 2** (`facility_upgrade_voting_priority_2`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the priority for the second facility that needs an upgrade.
*   **Priority 3** (`facility_upgrade_voting_priority_3`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the priority for the third facility that needs an upgrade.
*   **Priority 4** (`facility_upgrade_voting_priority_4`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the priority for the fourth facility that needs an upgrade.
*   **Priority 5** (`facility_upgrade_voting_priority_5`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the priority for the fifth facility that needs an upgrade.

## Tips

*   If you have feedback on the form, please provide it in the "Feedback" section.
*   If you want to receive updates on the facilities that have been upgraded, please enter your email address in the "Email" section.
*   If you want to receive updates on the facilities that have been upgraded, please enter your phone number in the "Phone" section.
*   Prioritize the facilities based on their importance to you.
*   Select the correct priority for each facility that needs an upgrade.
