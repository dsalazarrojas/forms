# Precision Tool Calibration Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Precision Tool Calibration Order Form is a critical document used to request calibration of tools and equipment in the laboratory or workshop. This form ensures that tools are properly maintained and serviced to guarantee accurate results and extend their lifespan.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields (marked as 'True' in the form).
2. Select the relevant tool type from the options provided.
3. Enter the tool serial number and description accurately.
4. If applicable, enter the lab testing equipment used.
5. Select the service date and time for the calibration.
6. Enter your name and signature as the technician who will perform the calibration.
7. Enter the name and contact information of the person who will be assigned to the tool.
8. Enter the date and time you submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **tool_type**: Select the type of tool that needs calibration (1, 2, or 3).
* **tool_serial_number**: Enter the serial number of the tool to be calibrated.
* **tool_description**: Provide a brief description of the tool to be calibrated.
* **lab_testing_equipment**: If applicable, enter the lab testing equipment used (optional).
* **calib_testing_equipment**: If applicable, enter the calibration testing equipment used (optional).
* **service_date**: Select the date and time for the calibration service.
* **service_time**: Select the time for the calibration service.
* **technician_signature**: Enter your name and signature as the technician performing the calibration.
* **technician**: Enter the name of the technician assigned to the tool.
* **customer_signature**: Enter your name and signature as the customer.
* **calib_report**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the calibration process (optional).
* **order_notes**: Any additional comments or notes about the order (optional).
* **form_submitted**: Enter the form title.
* **date_submitted**: Enter the date and time you submitted the form.
* **submitted_by**: Enter your name.
* **submitted_from**: Enter the device or platform from which you submitted the form.
* **submitted_ip**: Enter the IP address of the device or platform from which you submitted the form.
* **assigned_to**: Enter the name of the person assigned to the tool.
* **assigned_tool**: Enter the tool ID.
* **assigned_to_name**: Enter the name of the person assigned to the tool (optional).
* **assigned_to_email**: Enter the email of the person assigned to the tool.
* **assigned_to_phone**: Enter the phone number of the person assigned to the tool.
* **submitted_at**: Enter the date and time you submitted the form.
* **tool_id**: Enter the tool ID.
* **assigned_to_name**: Enter the name of the person assigned to the tool (optional).
* **assigned_to_email**: Enter the email of the person assigned to the tool.
* **assigned_to_phone**: Enter the phone number of the person assigned to the tool.
