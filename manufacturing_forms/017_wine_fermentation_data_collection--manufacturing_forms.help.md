# Wine Fermentation Data Collection - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect data on wine fermentation processes. The data collected will be used to track and analyze wine production data.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you have the necessary information.
2. Complete each required field to ensure accurate tracking and analysis of wine fermentation processes.
3. Select the correct wine type from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter the correct fermentation time in the date field.
5. Enter the correct temperature range in the range field.
6. Select multiple yeast types as required.
7. Enter the correct tank size and tank volume.
8. Enter the correct sugar content in the number field.
9. Enter the correct fermentation duration.
10. Enter the correct fermentation time in the date field.
11. Enter the correct wine yield in the number field.
12. Add any additional notes about the fermentation process.
13. If necessary, add notes about wine solids and pH target and actual values.
14. Add any additional notes for further reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **introduction** (1, note, required: false): This is the beginning of the form where you can briefly introduce the fermentation process.
* **wine-type** (2, select_one, required: false): Select the type of wine being produced, such as Apple, Chardonnay, Merlot, etc.
* **fermentation-time** (3, date, required: false): Enter the date of the fermentation process.
* **temperature-range** (4, number, required: false): Enter the temperature range used during fermentation.
* **yeast-type** (6, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the type of yeast used in the fermentation process, such as Active Dry Yeast, Baker's Yeast, etc.
* **tank-size** (7, number, required: false): Enter the size of the tank used.
* **tank-volume** (8, number, required: false): Enter the volume of the tank used.
* **sugar-contents** (9, number, required: false): Enter the sugar content of the wine.
* **fermentation-duration** (10, number, required: false): Enter the duration of the fermentation process.
* **fermentation-time** (11, date, required: false): Enter the start and end date of the fermentation process.
* **wine-yield** (12, number, required: false): Enter the yield of the wine produced.
* **notes** (13, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the fermentation process.
* **wine-solids** (14, number, required: false): Enter the wine solids, if required.
* **ph-target** (15, number, required: false): Enter the pH target for the fermentation process.
* **ph-actual** (16, number, required: false): Enter the actual pH value of the fermentation process.
* **notes_2** (17, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the fermentation process.
* **notes_3** (18, note, required: false): Add any additional notes for further reference.
* **notes_4** (19, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the fermentation process.
* **notes_5** (20, note, required: false): Add any final notes about the fermentation process.
