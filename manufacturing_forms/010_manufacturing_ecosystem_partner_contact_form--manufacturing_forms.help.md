# Manufacturing Ecosystem Partner Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and update contact information and details about existing manufacturing partners in the ecosystem. If you're a partner looking to provide more information or an existing partner updating their details, please use this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) or labeled as "required".
2. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Form" section.
3. Provide general information about your company in the "General Information" section.
4. Enter your address in the "Address" section.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" section.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" section.
7. Choose your manufacturing type in the "Manufacturing Type" section.
8. Enter your production capacity in the "Production Capacity" section.
9. Describe your production frequency in the "Production Frequency" section.
10. Choose the types of products you manufacture in the "Product Type" section.
11. Enter your company name in the "Company Name" section.
12. If you have an alternative company name, enter it in the "Company Name Other" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your company's official name.
* **Contact Form** (`contact_form`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact information, including your name, title, and company affiliation.
* **General Information** (`general_info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your company and its services.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your company's physical address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your company's email address.
* **Manufacturing Type** (`manufacturing_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of manufacturing your company offers, such as Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **Production Capacity** (`production_capacity`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your company's production capacity, in a numerical format.
* **Production Frequency** (`production_frequency`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your production frequency, e.g., daily, weekly, monthly.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the types of products your company manufactures, such as Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **Company Name Other** (`company_name_other`, `text`, required/optional): If you have an alternative company name, enter it here.
