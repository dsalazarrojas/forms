# Heat Exchanger Assembly Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track orders for heat exchanger assemblies. It captures essential details required for production, including part type, quantity, and material specifications. By completing this form accurately, you'll help ensure that orders are fulfilled correctly and promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reviewing the form and ensuring you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*).
3. For fields with multiple options, select the relevant options that apply to your order.
4. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user_input** (Text, Optional): Enter any relevant additional information about the order.
* **part_type** (Select One, Optional): Indicate if the part is a "Yes" or "No" type.
* **quantity** (Number, Optional): Enter the number of units required.
* **weight** (Number, Optional): Enter the total weight of the parts required.
* **heat_transfer_method** (Select Multiple, Optional): Check "Yes" if the heat transfer method is applied; "No" otherwise.
* **material** (Select Multiple, Optional): Check "Yes" if the material is required; "No" otherwise.
* **notes** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or remarks about the order.
* **submitted_by** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address to be associated with the order.
* **submitted_to** (Select One, Optional): Indicate who or which department submitted the order ("Yes" or "No").
* **submitted_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the order was submitted.
* **heat_exchanger_model** (Select One, Optional): Indicate if the heat exchanger model is required ("Yes" or "No").
* **heat_exchanger_serial_number** (Text, Optional): Enter the heat exchanger serial number (if applicable).
