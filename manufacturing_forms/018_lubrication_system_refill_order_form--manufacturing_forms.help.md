# Lubrication System Refill Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lubrication System Refill Order Form is used to submit refill orders for the lubrication system. This form gathers necessary information from the submitter to ensure the correct materials are ordered and delivered to the correct location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (marked as "true" in the form) as accurately as possible.
2. Review each field to ensure that you have filled out the correct information.
3. Once you have completed all required fields, click the "Submit" button to send the form to the designated personnel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. System ID
* **System ID** (`system_id`, text, required): Enter the ID of the lubrication system that needs to be refilled.

### 2. Part ID
* **Part ID** (`part_id`, text, required): Enter the ID of the part that needs to be refilled.

### 3. Quantity
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the quantity of the part that needs to be refilled.

### 4. Submitter Information
* **Submitter Information** (`submitter_info`, note, required): Please fill in any relevant information about the submitter, such as their role or department.

### 5. Name
* **Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the submitter.

### 6. Email
* **Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required): Enter your email address as the submitter.

### 7. Phone
* **Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number as the submitter.

### 8. Department
* **Department** (`submitter_department`, select_one, required): Select the department you belong to (e.g. Sales and Marketing, Engineering, etc.).

### 9. Location
* **Location** (`submitter_location`, select_multiple, required): Select all locations where the part will be used (e.g. 'Yes', 'No').

### 10. Manager
* **Manager** (`submitter_manager`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if you have permission from a manager to submit this refill order (e.g. 'Yes', 'No').

### 11. Comments
* **Comments** (`submitter_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the refill order.

### 12. Signature
* **Signature** (`submitter_signature`, date, optional): Date and time of the signature of the submitter.

### 13. Date
* **Date** (`submitter_date`, date, required): Enter the date you want the part to be refilled.

### 14. Time
* **Time** (`submitter_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you want the part to be refilled.

### 15. IP
* **IP** (`submitter_ip`, text, optional): Enter your IP address as the submitter.

### 16. City
* **City** (`submitter_city`, text, optional): Enter your city as the submitter.

### 17. Country
* **Country** (`submitter_country`, text, optional): Enter your country as the submitter.

### 18. State
* **State** (`submitter_state`, text, optional): Enter your state as the submitter.

### 19. Zip
* **Zip** (`submitter_zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code as the submitter.

### 20. Refill Frequency
* **Refill Frequency** (`submitter_refill_frequency`, select_one, required): Indicate how frequently you would like the part to be refilled (e.g. 'Yes', 'No').

### 21. Refill Date
* **Refill Date** (`submitter_refill_date`, date, required): Enter the date you want the part to be refilled by.

### 22. Refill Frequency
* **Refill Frequency** (`submitter_refill_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Indicate how frequently you would like the part to be refilled (e.g. 'Yes', 'No').

### 23. Refill Time
* **Refill Time** (`submitter_refill_time`, time, required): Enter the time you want the part to be refilled by.

### 24. Notes
* **Notes** (`submitter_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the refill order.

Please ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately and completely before submitting the form. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the designated personnel for assistance.
