# Manufacturing Risk Assessment Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect data for a Manufacturing Risk Assessment Workshop Registration. This data will help the organizers of the workshop to tailor the content and schedule according to the needs and interests of the participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Select your session choices by checking the boxes for the relevant options.
- Provide your attendee details in the text field.
- Select your risk level from the options provided.
- Choose your session type.
- Enter the date and time for the workshop (if applicable).
- Add any additional notes if needed.
- Select your additional session choices.
- Choose your training provider(s).
- Select your training type.
- Identify risk factors relevant to you.
- Select the workshop date and time (if applicable).
- Choose your attendees.
- Select the workshop type.
- Add risk comments if needed.
- Add additional training provider(s).
- Add workshop comments if needed.
- Identify additional risk factors.
- Add additional attendees.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Session Choices** (Select Multiple): Choose the sessions you are interested in attending by checking the boxes for the relevant options.
  - Option 1: Select this to attend the first session.
  - Option 2: Select this to attend the second session.
  - Option 3: Select this to attend the third session.
- **Attendee Details** (Text): Provide your name, company, and job title to help us get a better understanding of your background and interests.
- **Risk Level** (Select One): Select your risk level as:
  - Low: You have minimal risk exposure.
  - Medium: You have moderate risk exposure.
  - High: You have significant risk exposure.
- **Session Type** (Select One): Choose the type of session that suits your needs.
- **Date** (Date): Enter the date you would like to attend the workshop.
- **Time** (Time): Enter the time you would like to attend the workshop.
- **Note** (Note): Add any additional comments or notes about your interests or requirements.
- **Additional Session Choices** (Select Multiple): Choose any additional sessions you would like to attend.
- **Training Provider** (Select Multiple): Select the training provider(s) you would like to attend.
- **Training Type** (Select One): Choose the type of training you would like to receive.
- **Risk Factors** (Select Multiple): Identify the risk factors relevant to you.
- **Workshop Date** (Date): Enter the date of the workshop.
- **Workshop Time** (Time): Enter the time of the workshop.
- **Attendees** (Select Multiple): Select the attendees you would like to attend with.
- **Workshop Type** (Select One): Choose the type of workshop that suits your needs.
- **Risk Comments** (Note): Add any risk comments or concerns.
- **Additional Attendees** (Select Multiple): Identify any additional attendees you would like to invite.
- **Training Location** (Select One): Choose the location of the training.
- **Risk Comments** (Note): Add any additional risk comments or concerns.
- **Additional Training Provider** (Select Multiple): Select any additional training provider(s).
- **Workshop Comments** (Note): Add any comments about the workshop.
- **Additional Risk Factors** (Select Multiple): Identify any additional risk factors.
- **Additional Attendees** (Select Multiple): Identify any additional attendees you would like to invite.
- **Additional Training Type** (Select One): Choose the additional type of training you would like to receive.
- **Additional Risk Comments** (Note): Add any additional risk comments or concerns.
