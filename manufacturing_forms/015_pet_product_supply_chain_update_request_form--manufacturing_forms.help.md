**Pet Product Supply Chain Update Request Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose

This form is used to update the supply chain information for pet products. It is essential for accurate and up-to-date inventory management.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please make sure you only fill out this form for genuine updates**. If you are unsure about the purpose or requirement of this form, please contact your supervisor or team lead for clarification.
2.  **Complete all required fields**, marked as **<Required>**. These fields are essential for processing the update request.
3.  **Be as detailed as possible** when filling out the form. This will help ensure that the update is processed accurately and efficiently.
4.  **Double-check your information** before submitting the form to ensure it is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Pet Product Update**: Enter a brief description of the pet product update, including any relevant details about the product.
    *   **Type:** Text field.
*   **Shipment Details**: Enter your shipment details, including any relevant information about the shipment.
    *   **Type:** Text field.
*   **Product Information**: Enter the product information, including any relevant details about the product.
    *   **Type:** Text field.
*   **Inventory Update**: Enter any inventory updates, including any changes in quantity or product status.
    *   **Type:** Text field.
*   **Shipment Date**: Enter the date of the shipment.
    *   **Type:** Date field.
*   **Shipment Time**: Enter the time of the shipment.
    *   **Type:** Time field.
*   **Shipment Status**: Select the status of the shipment from the options provided.
    *   **Type:** Select-one field with options: Pending, Shipped, Delivered.
*   **Shipment Location**: Enter the location of the shipment.
    *   **Type:** Text field.
*   **Update Reason**: Enter the reason for the update.
    *   **Type:** Text field.
*   **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the shipment.
    *   **Type:** Note field.
*   **Contact Email**: Enter the contact email of the person responsible for the shipment.
    *   **Type:** Email field.
*   **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the person responsible for the shipment.
    *   **Type:** Text field.
*   **Assigned Tool**: Enter any assigned tool or equipment used for the shipment.
    *   **Type:** Text field. 

## Tips

*   Always double-check your information before submitting the form.
*   Use a separate update form for each unique shipment update.
*   Make sure to include all relevant details to ensure accurate update processing.
