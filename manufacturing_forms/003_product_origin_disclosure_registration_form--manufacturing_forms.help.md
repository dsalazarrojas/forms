# Product Origin Disclosure Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Origin Disclosure Registration Form is designed to collect detailed information about the origin of a product, which is essential for various industries, especially those regulated, to ensure product authenticity and compliance with relevant laws and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Company Name** of the product originator.
2.  Enter the **Company Address** where the product was manufactured or originated.
3.  Provide a **Product Origin Disclosure** statement, describing the origin of the product.
4.  Enter a detailed **Origin Description** that explains the origin of the product.
5.  Enter the **Origin Location**, including the city, state, and region.
6.  Enter the **Origin Country** where the product was manufactured or originated.
7.  Enter the **Origin Region**.
8.  Enter the **Origin City**.
9.  Enter the **Origin Postal Code**.
10. Enter the **Origin State**.
11. Enter the **Origin Road**.
12. Enter a **Origin Landmark**, if any.
13. Enter the **Origin Geolocation**, if available.
14. Enter the **Origin Coordinates** (longitude and latitude).
15. Enter the **Origin Timezone** (if applicable).
16. Enter the **Origin Elevation** (if applicable).
17. Enter the **Origin Longitude**.
18. Enter the **Origin Latitude**.
19. If applicable, select the **Origin Elevation Unit** and **Origin Longitude Unit** and **Origin Latitude Unit** for the **Origin Elevation**, **Origin Longitude**, and **Origin Latitude** fields respectively.
20. Enter the **Origin Altitude**, if applicable.
21. Select the **Origin Altitude Unit** for the **Origin Altitude** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`product_origin_disclosure_registration_form`, text, required): Enter the name of the company that is registering the origin of the product.
* **Company Address** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the address of the company that is registering the origin of the product.
* **Product Origin Disclosure** (`product_origin_disclosure`, text, required): Provide a statement describing the origin of the product.
* **Origin Description** (`origin_description`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the origin of the product.
* **Origin Location** (`origin_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the product origin (city, state, region).
* **Origin Country** (`origin_country`, text, required): Enter the country of the product origin.
* **Origin Region** (`origin_region`, text, required): Enter the region of the product origin.
* **Origin City** (`origin_city`, text, required): Enter the city where the product was manufactured or originated.
* **Origin Postal Code** (`origin_postal_code`, text, required): Enter the postal code of the product origin.
* **Origin State** (`origin_state`, text, required): Enter the state of the product origin.
* **Origin Road** (`origin_road`, text, required): Enter the street or road where the product was manufactured or originated.
* **Origin Landmark** (`origin_landmark`, text, required): If applicable, enter a landmark near the origin location.
* **Origin Geolocation** (`origin_geolocation`, text, required): If available, enter the geolocation of the origin.
* **Origin Coordinates** (`origin_coordinates`, text, required): Enter the coordinates of the origin in longitude and latitude.
* **Origin Timezone** (`origin_timezone`, text, required): Enter the timezone of the origin.
* **Origin Elevation** (`origin_elevation`, text, required): If applicable, enter the elevation of the origin.
* **Origin Longitude** (`origin_longitude`, text, required): Enter the longitude of the origin.
* **Origin Latitude** (`origin_latitude`, text, required): Enter the latitude of the origin.
* **Origin Elevation Unit** (`origin_elevation_unit`, text, required): Select the unit of measurement for the **Origin Elevation** field, if applicable.
* **Origin Longitude Unit** (`origin_longitude_unit`, text, required): Select the unit of measurement for the **Origin Longitude** field, if applicable.
* **Origin Latitude Unit** (`origin_latitude_unit`, text, required): Select the unit of measurement for the **Origin Latitude** field, if applicable.
* **Origin Altitude** (`origin_altitude`, text, required): If applicable, enter the altitude of the origin.
* **Origin Altitude Unit** (`origin_altitude_unit`, text, required): Select the unit of measurement for the **Origin Altitude** field, if applicable.
* **Origin Geoid** (`origin_geoid`, text, required): Enter the geoid of the origin, if available.
* **Origin Coordinates** (`origin_coordinates`, text, required): If applicable, enter the coordinates of the origin in geoid and longitude and latitude.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely to avoid errors.
* Double-check your entries to ensure that they align with the actual origin of the product.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities or seek clarification from the product originator.
