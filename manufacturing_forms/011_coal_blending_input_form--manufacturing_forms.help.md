# Coal Blending Input Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coal Blending Input Form is designed to capture the essential details of a coal blending operation. This form is used to input information about the blending run, including the date, operators, coal sources, blending parameters, blending volume, quality assessment, and storage and handling details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Blending Run Date** in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
2. Enter the **Run Number** as a unique identifier for this blending run.
3. Select the **Operator Name** of the person conducting the blend.
4. Choose the **Shift** during which the operation is conducted (Day, Evening, or Night).
5. Enter the **Primary Coal Source** as the name or ID of the first coal source.
6. Enter the **Primary Coal Percentage** as a percentage by weight.
7. If applicable, enter the **Secondary Coal Source** and **Secondary Coal Percentage**.
8. Enter the **Tertiary Coal Source** and **Tertiary Coal Percentage**, if necessary.
9. Set the **Target Ash Content**, **Target Sulfur Content**, and **Target Moisture Content** as desired percentages.
10. Set the **Target BTU Value**, if applicable.
11. Enter the **Total Blend Volume** as the total weight of the final blend.
12. Set the **Target Fineness** as the desired coal fineness specification.
13. Perform an **Initial Quality Check** by selecting 'Yes', 'No', or 'Pending' to indicate if the initial quality check has passed or not.
14. Enter any **Quality Assessment Notes**, if necessary.
15. Select the **Storage Location** where the blend will be stored.
16. Choose the **Current Weather Conditions** (Clear, Cloudy, Rainy, Windy, or Other) as important for coal handling.
17. Click submit to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Blending Run Date**: Enter the date of the blending operation.
* **Run Number**: A unique identifier for this blending run.
* **Operator Name**: Name of the person conducting the blend.
* **Shift**: The time of day the blending operation is conducted.
* **Primary Coal Source**: Name or ID of the first coal source.
* **Primary Coal Percentage**: Percentage by weight of the primary coal source.
* **Secondary Coal Source**: Name or ID of the second coal source (if applicable).
* **Secondary Coal Percentage**: Percentage by weight of the second coal source (if applicable).
* **Tertiary Coal Source**: Name or ID of the third coal source (if applicable).
* **Tertiary Coal Percentage**: Percentage by weight of the tertiary coal source (if applicable).
* **Target Ash Content**: Desired ash content in the final blend.
* **Target Sulfur Content**: Desired sulfur content in the final blend.
* **Target Moisture Content**: Desired moisture level in the final blend.
* **Target BTU Value**: Desired calorific value in the final blend.
* **Total Blend Volume**: Total weight of the final blend.
* **Target Fineness**: Desired coal fineness specification.
* **Initial Quality Check**: Select 'Yes', 'No', or 'Pending' to indicate if the initial quality check has passed or not.
* **Quality Assessment Notes**: Any observations or concerns regarding the quality of the blend.
* **Storage Location**: Where the blend will be stored.
* **Current Weather Conditions**: Important for coal handling, choose from Clear, Cloudy, Rainy, Windy, or Other.
