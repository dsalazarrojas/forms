# pharmaceutical_packaging_requisition_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to capture essential information for pharmaceutical packaging requisitions, ensuring accurate and efficient processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the product ID field with the specific product ID assigned to the product being packaged.
2. Enter the packaging ID associated with the packaging request.
3. Provide the name of the product being packaged.
4. Indicate whether the product requires packaging or not by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the packaging type field.
5. Enter the size of the packaging required for the product.
6. Specify the quantity of the packaging needed.
7. Enter the type of packaging material required for the product.
8. Enter the date the packaging was requested.
9. Enter the date the packaging was produced.
10. Enter the time the packaging was produced.
11. Indicate the location where the packaging was produced.
12. Choose the status of the packaging (Active or Inactive).
13. Add any relevant comments about the packaging request.
14. Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
15. Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
16. Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
17. Enter the department of the person submitting the form.
18. Enter the job title of the person submitting the form.
19. Enter the position of the person submitting the form.
20. Enter the designation of the person submitting the form.
21. Indicate whether packaging supervisors are involved ('Yes' or 'No') and select all applicable options.
22. Indicate whether production supervisors are involved ('Yes' or 'No') and select all applicable options.
23. Indicate whether an approver is involved ('Yes' or 'No') and select all applicable options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product ID** (`1`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the specific product ID assigned to the product being packaged.
* **Packaging ID** (`2`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the packaging ID associated with the packaging request.
* **Product Name** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the name of the product being packaged.
* **Packaging Type** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate whether the product requires packaging or not by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Packaging Size** (`5`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the size of the packaging required for the product.
* **Quantity** (`6`, `number`, required/optional): Specify the quantity of the packaging needed.
* **Packaging Material** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the type of packaging material required for the product.
* **Requested Date** (`8`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date the packaging was requested.
* **Production Date** (`9`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date the packaging was produced.
* **Production Time** (`10`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time the packaging was produced.
* **Packaging Location** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): Indicate the location where the packaging was produced.
* **Packaging Status** (`12`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the status of the packaging (Active or Inactive).
* **Comments** (`13`, `note`, required/optional): Add any relevant comments about the packaging request.
* **Email** (`14`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Phone** (`15`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter's Email** (`16`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter's Phone** (`17`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter's Name** (`18`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter's Department** (`19`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the department of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter's Job Title** (`20`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the job title of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter's Position** (`21`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the position of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter's Designation** (`22`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the designation of the person submitting the form.
* **Packaging Supervisors** (`23`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate whether packaging supervisors are involved ('Yes' or 'No') and select all applicable options.
* **Production Supervisors** (`24`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate whether production supervisors are involved ('Yes' or 'No') and select all applicable options.
* **Approver** (`25`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate whether an approver is involved ('Yes' or 'No') and select all applicable options.
