# Raw Material Variance Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Raw Material Variance Report Form is used to document and track variances in raw materials, ensuring that they are properly recorded and addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the root cause of the variance.
2. Enter the amount of the variance.
3. Describe any corrective action taken to address the variance.
4. Enter the date and time of the variance.
5. Enter any additional notes about the variance.
6. If applicable, enter your email address and phone number for follow-up communication.
7. Indicate if the production team was involved in the variance.
8. Select the members of the production team that were involved in the addressing the variance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Root Cause** (`root_cause`, text, required/optional): Enter a concise description of the root cause of the variance.
* **Variance** (`variance`, number, required/optional): Enter the quantity or amount of the variance.
* **Corrective Action** (`corrective_action`, text, required/optional): Describe any corrective action taken to address the variance.
* **Variance Date** (`variance_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date on which the variance occurred.
* **Variance Time** (`variance_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time at which the variance occurred.
* **Variance Note** (`variance_note`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the variance.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Production Team Involved** (`production_team`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check if any members of the production team were involved in addressing the variance.
* **Production Team Members** (`production_team_members`, select_one, required/optional): Select the specific members of the production team that were involved in addressing the variance.
