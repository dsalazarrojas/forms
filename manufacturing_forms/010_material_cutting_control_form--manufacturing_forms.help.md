# Material Cutting Control Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about material cutting processes, ensuring accurate tracking and management of production processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are completing the form for material cutting activities only.
2. Fill in the "Material Cutting Details" section with relevant information about the material being cut.
3. Indicate the quality checks performed on the material in the "Quality Checks" section.
4. Provide the status of inspection sign-offs in the "Inspection Sign-offs" section.
5. Document any production issues in the "Production Issues" section.
6. Attach photos and videos related to the material cutting process in the "Photos" and "Videos" sections.
7. Update the production status in the "Production Status" section and provide any additional notes in the "Production Status Notes" section.
8. Record production comments and cutting notes in the "Production Comments" and "Production Cutting Notes" sections.
9. Verify the production quality certification status in the "Production Quality Certified" section.
10. Set the production cutting status in the "Production Cutting Status" section.
11. Document the production re-work order and date in the "Production Re-work Order" and "Production Re-work Date" sections (if applicable).
12. Record the material receiving date in the "Material Receiving Date" section.
13. Record the material inspection result in the "Material Inspection Result" section.
14. Record the quality inspection result in the "Quality Inspection Result" section.
15. Update the material receiving status in the "Material Receiving Status" section.
16. Set the material cutting status in the "Material Cutting Status" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Material Cutting Details (1)** (`text`, required): Enter a brief description of the material being cut.
- **Quality Checks (2)** (`text`, required): Document the quality checks performed on the material.
- **Inspection Sign-offs (3)** (`select_one`, optional): Indicate whether inspection sign-offs have been performed (`Yes` or `No`).
- **Production Issues (4)** (`text`, optional): Report any production issues.
- **Photos (5)** (`note`, optional): Attach photos related to the material cutting process.
- **Videos (6)** (`note`, optional): Attach videos related to the material cutting process.
- **Production Status (7)** (`select_multiple`, required): Select the current production status (`In Progress`, `Complete`, `On Hold`, or `Aborted`).
- **Production Status Notes (8)** (`text`, optional): Provide any additional notes related to the production status.
- **Production Comments (9)** (`text`, optional): Record production comments.
- **Production Cutting Notes (10)** (`text`, optional): Record cutting notes.
- **Production Quality Certified (11)** (`select_one`, required): Verify the production quality certification status (`True` or `False`).
- **Production Cutting Status (12)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Set the production cutting status (`Complete`, `Partial`, `Re-cut`, or `Pending`).
- **Production Re-work Order (13)** (`number`, optional): Document the production re-work order (if applicable).
- **Production Re-work Date (14)** (`date`, optional): Record the production re-work date (if applicable).
- **Material Receiving Date (15)** (`date`, optional): Record the material receiving date.
- **Material Inspection Result (16)** (`select_one`, optional): Record the material inspection result (`Pass` or `Fail`).
- **Quality Inspection Result (17)** (`select_one`, optional): Record the quality inspection result (`Pass` or `Fail`).
- **Material Receiving Status (18)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Set the material receiving status (`Complete`, `Pending`, or `In Progress`).
- **Material Cutting Status (19)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Set the material cutting status (`Complete`, `Pending`, or `In Progress`).
