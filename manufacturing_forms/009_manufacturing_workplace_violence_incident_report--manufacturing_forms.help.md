# manufacturing_workplace_violence_incident_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents of workplace violence and gather relevant details to review and resolve the incident.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the details of the incident in the corresponding fields. If you are unsure about any information, please consult the relevant personnel or supervisor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`): Describe the incident in as much detail as possible. This includes what happened, including any relevant circumstances, and any witnesses.
* **Location** (`location`): Enter the location where the incident occurred, including the floor/department/room number.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Summary** (`incident_summary`): Briefly describe the incident, including any relevant details.
* **Incident Cause** (`incident_cause`): Describe the root cause of the incident, if known.
* **Incidents Injured** (`incident_injuries`): Enter the number of people injured in the incident, if any.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`): Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any actions taken or witnesses involved.
* **Incident Investigation** (`incident_investigation`): Describe any actions taken to investigate the incident, including any interviews or investigations conducted.
* **Incident Resolution** (`incident_resolution`): Describe the resolution of the incident, including any actions taken or outcomes.
* **Incident Review** (`incident_review`): Enter any additional comments or observations about the incident.
