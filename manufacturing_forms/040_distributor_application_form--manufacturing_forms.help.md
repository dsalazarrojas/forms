# Distributor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Distributor Application Form is used to collect information from potential distributors to evaluate their application and process it efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields, marked as "required" on the form.
2. Enter your first name in the First Name field.
3. Enter your last name in the Last Name field.
4. Enter your company name in the Company Name field.
5. Enter your address in the Address field.
6. Enter your contact number in the Contact Number field.
7. Enter your email address in the Email field.
8. Select your distributor category from the options provided in the Distributor Category field.
9. Select your territory from the options provided in the Territory field.
10. Select all applicable product categories from the options provided in the Product Categories field.
11. Enter the start date of your distributorship in the Start Date field.
12. Enter the end date of your distributorship in the End Date field.
13. (Optional) Add any additional notes about your application in the Note field.
14. Select the agreements that apply to your distributorship from the options provided in the Agreement field.
15. Enter the name of the sales representative assigned to your account in the Sales Rep Name field.
16. Enter the email address of the sales representative in the Sales Rep Email field.
17. Enter the contact number of the sales representative in the Sales Rep Number field.
18. Enter the name of the distributor manager in the Distributor Manager Name field.
19. Enter the email address of the distributor manager in the Distributor Manager Email field.
20. Enter the contact number of the distributor manager in the Distributor Manager Number field.
21. Confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms of the distributorship in the Terms Agreed field.
22. Enter the date you submitted your application in the Date Submitted field.
23. (Optional) Enter any comments or additional information about your application in the Comments field.
24. Select the status of your distributor account from the options provided in the Status field.
