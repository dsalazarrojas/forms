# Process Failure Detection Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Process Failure Detection Report Form is designed to collect information about process failures in a manufacturing setting. This form helps the team identify and address process failures quickly and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the severity level of the failure and select the correct option:
	* Critical: The failure has a significant impact on product quality, production, or safety.
	* Major: The failure has a moderate impact on product quality, production, or safety.
	* Minor: The failure has a minor impact on product quality, production, or safety.
2. Provide detailed information about the process failure in the "Process Failure Details" field.
3. If necessary, upload evidence or attachments to support your report using the "Upload" fields.
