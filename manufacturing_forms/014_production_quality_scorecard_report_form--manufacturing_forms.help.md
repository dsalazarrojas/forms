# Production Quality Scorecard Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Production Quality Scorecard Report Form is a tool used to document and evaluate the quality of products during production. It helps in tracking and analyzing production data over time to identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the Product Name**: Type in the name of the product being produced.
2. **Enter the Production Date**: Select the date of production using the date picker.
3. **Enter Shift Information**: Enter the shift information in the free-form text field.
4. **Enter Inspector's Name**: Enter the name of the inspector who conducted the quality control.
5. **Enter Product ID**: Enter the numerical ID of the product.
6. **Enter Quality Rating**: Enter the overall quality rating of the product (1-10).
7. **Select Criteria 1**: Choose the rating for the first criteria from the multiple-choice options.
8. **Enter Criteria 1 Rating**: Enter the numerical rating for the first criteria.
9. **Select Criteria 2**: Choose the rating for the second criteria from the multiple-choice options.
10. **Enter Criteria 2 Rating**: Enter the numerical rating for the second criteria.
11. **Select Criteria 3**: Choose the rating for the third criteria from the multiple-choice options.
12. **Enter Criteria 3 Rating**: Enter the numerical rating for the third criteria.
13. **Add Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the product or production process.
14. **Add Photos**: Take photos of the product or production line and enter them in the free-form text fields.
15. **Enter Assessment Date**: Select the date of assessment using the date picker.
16. **Enter Final Score**: Enter the final numerical score.
17. **Add Comments 2**: Enter any additional comments about the assessment.
