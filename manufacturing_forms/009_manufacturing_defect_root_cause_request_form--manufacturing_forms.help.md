# Manufacturing Defect Root Cause Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request and track the root cause of a manufacturing defect. This form helps in identifying and addressing potential issues that may affect production quality and efficiency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form with the required information about the manufacturing defect you are reporting.
*   Ensure that you select the correct options for each field, as they are designed to guide you through the process.
*   Provide accurate and detailed information, especially for fields like "Description," "Root Cause," and "Corrective Action," as this will help in understanding the issue and resolving it effectively.
*   Check the "Status" field to update the form's status accordingly, as this will reflect the progress of your request.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all relevant information is included.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Manufacturing Defect Request
*   **Manufacturing Defect Request**: Select "Yes" if you are requesting to report a manufacturing defect. This field helps in tracking the number of defects reported.

### Defect Type
*   **Defect Type**: Select the type of defect you are experiencing. Although not all fields are required, it's essential to select the correct type to help in further investigation.

### Date of Occurrence
*   **Date of Occurrence**: Enter the date when the manufacturing defect occurred. This helps in tracking the timeline of the issue.

### Time of Occurrence
*   **Time of Occurrence**: Enter the time when the manufacturing defect occurred. This field is useful for understanding the timing of the issue.

### Department
*   **Department**: Select the department involved in the manufacturing process that encountered the defect. This field will help in assigning resources and tracking progress.

### Team
*   **Team**: Select the team responsible for the manufacturing process that encountered the defect. This helps in coordinating efforts and tracking accountability.

### Description
*   **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the defect. This is a crucial field as it will help in understanding the defect's characteristics and severity.

### Root Cause
*   **Root Cause**: Enter the root cause of the defect. This field is essential for identifying the underlying issue and preventing future occurrences.

### Corrective Action
*   **Corrective Action**: Document any actions taken to correct or mitigate the defect. This helps in tracking progress and ensuring that the issue is addressed.

### Preventive Measures
*   **Preventive Measures**: Record any measures taken to prevent similar defects in the future. This field is vital for long-term solutions and process improvements.

### Responsible Person
*   **Responsible Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the defect. This helps in assigning accountability and tracking progress.

### Assigned Date
*   **Assigned Date**: Enter the date when the defect was assigned to someone. This field is useful for tracking deadlines and progress.

### Status
*   **Status**: Update the form's status to reflect the current state of the request. This helps in tracking progress and ensuring that the issue is resolved.

### Tips
*   Always ensure that the form is filled out accurately and thoroughly to avoid delays or misallocated resources.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all necessary information is included.
*   Check for similar requests to avoid duplicate efforts.
*   Update the form regularly to track progress and ensure that the issue is resolved efficiently.
