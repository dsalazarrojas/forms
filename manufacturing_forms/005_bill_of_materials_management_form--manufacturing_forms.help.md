# Bill Of Materials Management Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bill Of Materials Management Form is a tool used to store and track product information, including product details, costs, and production lead times.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, select the appropriate product details section.
2.  Fill in the required fields:
   *   **Product name**: Enter the official product or assembly name.
   *   **Product SKU**: Enter the internal stock keeping unit for the product.
3.  If necessary, add additional information:
   *   **Revision number**: Enter the BOM revision or version number.
   *   **Component list summary**: Provide a brief list of primary components and quantities.
4.  Enter estimated costs and lead times:
   *   **Total cost estimate**: Enter the estimated cost in local currency.
   *   **Lead time days**: Enter the expected procurement lead time in days.
5.  Add supplier and critical component details:
   *   **Supplier name**: Enter the primary supplier for key components.
   *   **Critical components**: List components that require special handling.
6.  Add any additional notes or change history:
   *   **Notes or change history**: Enter any additional information for production teams.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Product name (Product name, text, required)
Enter the official product or assembly name.

### 2. Product SKU (Product sku, text, required)
Enter the internal stock keeping unit for the product.

### 3. Revision number (Revision number, text, optional)
Enter the BOM revision or version number.

### 4. Component list summary (Component list summary, text, required)
Provide a brief list of primary components and quantities.

### 5. Total cost estimate (Total cost estimate, number, optional)
Enter the estimated cost in local currency.

### 6. Lead time days (Lead time days, number, required)
Enter the expected procurement lead time in days.

### 7. Supplier name (Supplier name, text, optional)
Enter the primary supplier for key components.

### 8. Critical components (Critical components, text, optional)
List components that require special handling.

### 9. Notes or change history (Notes or change history, text, optional)
Enter any additional information for production teams.

## Tips
*   Ensure that all required fields are filled out to ensure accurate product data.
*   Use the revision number to track changes to the BOM.
*   List critical components to prioritize procurement and production planning.
*   Update existing entries instead of creating duplicates to maintain data consistency.
