# machine Retrofit program contact form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The machine Retrofit program contact form is designed to collect contact information and details about a retrofit project from end-users. This form is used to establish communication and gather essential information about the project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your User Information, including your name and other relevant details.
2. Enter your Contact Information, which includes your phone number and email address.
3. Fill in the Retrofit Information field with details about the project.
4. If you have any additional notes, you can provide them in the Additional Notes field.
5. Finally, enter your phone number in the Phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): This field is used to provide your name and other relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): This field is used to enter your phone number and email address.
* **Retrofit Information** (`retrofit_info`, text, optional): This field is used to provide details about the retrofit project.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): This field is used to provide any additional notes or comments about the project.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is used to enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out this form, do not hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your cooperation and look forward to establishing communication with you through this form.
