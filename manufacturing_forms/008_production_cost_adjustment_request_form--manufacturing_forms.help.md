# Production Cost Adjustment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Production Cost Adjustment Request Form is designed to support the production team in adjusting production costs for specific products. This form is used to request a cost adjustment for a production product and is an essential tool for the production team to manage cost adjustments.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the base product to which you want to apply the cost adjustment.
2. Choose the type of adjustment you are requesting (Item or Process).
3. Enter the date of the adjustment.
4. Provide a detailed description of the reason for the adjustment.
5. Enter the proposed cost change for the adjustment.
6. Enter the justification for the cost change.
7. Select the status of the adjustment (Approved, Rejected, In Progress, or Pending).
8. Enter any comments about the adjustment.
9. Enter the name of the person who approved the adjustment.
10. Enter the date when the adjustment was approved.
11. Select the status of the adjustment (Open or Closed).
12. Enter the date when the adjustment was closed.
13. Enter the name of the person who closed the adjustment.
14. Enter the original adjustment value.
15. Enter the new adjustment value.
16. Calculate the adjustment value delta.
17. Calculate the adjustment value percentage change.
18. Enter any comments about the adjustment.
19. Enter the title of the person who approved the adjustment.
20. Enter the person who created the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Base Product** (`base_product`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the base product to which you want to apply the cost adjustment.
* **Adjustment Type** (`adjustment_type`, Select One, Required): Choose the type of adjustment you are requesting (Item or Process).
* **Adjustment Date** (`adjustment_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date of the adjustment.
* **Adjustment Description** (`adjustment_description`, Text, Required): Provide a detailed description of the reason for the adjustment.
* **Proposed Cost Change** (`proposed_cost_change`, Number, Required): Enter the proposed cost change for the adjustment.
* **Justification for Change** (`justification_for_change`, Text, Required): Enter the justification for the cost change.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, Select One, Required): Select the status of the adjustment (Approved, Rejected, In Progress, or Pending).
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Required): Enter any comments about the adjustment.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the person who approved the adjustment.
* **Approved Date** (`approved_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date when the adjustment was approved.
* **Adjustment Status** (`adjustment_status`, Select One, Required): Select the status of the adjustment (Open or Closed).
* **Closed Date** (`closed_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date when the adjustment was closed.
* **Closed By** (`closed_by`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the person who closed the adjustment.
* **Adjustment Value Original** (`adjustment_value_original`, Number, Required): Enter the original adjustment value.
* **Adjustment Value New** (`adjustment_value_new`, Number, Required): Enter the new adjustment value.
* **Adjustment Value Delta** (`adjustment_value_delta`, Number, Required): Calculate the adjustment value delta.
* **Adjustment Value Percentage** (`adjustment_value_percentage`, Number, Required): Calculate the adjustment value percentage change.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Required): Enter any comments about the adjustment.
* **Approved By Title** (`approved_by_title`, Text, Required): Enter the title of the person who approved the adjustment.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, Text, Required): Enter the person who created the form.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide detailed and accurate information in each field.
* Ensure that the adjustment is not a duplicate request.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.
