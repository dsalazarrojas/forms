# Factory Material Flow Study Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Material Flow Study Request Form is designed to collect information for a factory material flow study. This study aims to analyze and optimize the flow of materials within a factory, ensuring efficient and effective production processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide detailed information about the factory, including the facility name and location.
2.  Clearly state the objective of the study, explaining what you hope to achieve through the analysis.
3.  Enter the start and end dates for the study period.
4.  Select the type of study you require from the options provided:
	*   Flow Analysis: This type of study focuses on optimizing the flow of materials within the factory.
	*   Production Planning: This type of study involves analyzing and optimizing production planning and scheduling.
	*   Quality Control: This type of study focuses on analyzing and optimizing quality control processes within the factory.
5.  If you have a preferred study start date, you can provide it. Otherwise, the default start date will be used.
6.  Provide any additional information that will help the study team better understand your needs.
7.  Enter your contact number so that we can reach out to you for further clarification or updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Factory Information** (`factory_info`, `text`, required): Enter a detailed description of the factory, including its name and location.
*   **Objective** (`objective`, `text`, required): Clearly state the objective of the study, explaining what you hope to achieve through the analysis.
*   **Study Dates** (`study_dates`, `date`, required): Enter the start and end dates for the study period.
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date for the study period.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the end date for the study period.
*   **Material Flow Study Request** (`material_flow_study_request`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the type of study you require:
	+ Flow Analysis: This type of study focuses on optimizing the flow of materials within the factory.
	+ Production Planning: This type of study involves analyzing and optimizing production planning and scheduling.
	+ Quality Control: This type of study focuses on analyzing and optimizing quality control processes within the factory.
*   **Preferred Study Date** (`preferred_study_date`, `date`, optional): If you have a preferred study start date, you can provide it. If not, the default start date will be used.
*   **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information that will help the study team better understand your needs.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact number so that we can reach out to you for further clarification or updates.

Note: This form and the guide are intended to be used as-is, but you are free to modify and use them as you see fit, just give credit.
