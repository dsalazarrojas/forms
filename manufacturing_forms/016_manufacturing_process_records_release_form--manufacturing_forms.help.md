# manufacturing_process_records_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The manufacturing process records release form is used to record the release of a manufacturing process. This form captures relevant information about the release, including the company name, release date, manufacturing process, product, employee, reason for release, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the company name in the Company Name field.
2. Enter the release date in the Release Date field.
3. Select the manufacturing process from the Manufacturing Process field.
4. Select the product from the Product field.
5. Select the employee who is submitting the form from the Employee field.
6. Enter the reason for release in the Reason for Release field.
7. (Optional) Enter any additional comments in the Notes field.
8. (Optional) Enter the employee's email and phone number in the Email and Phone fields.
9. Select the status from the Status field.
10. (Optional) Attach any relevant files or documents in the Attachment field.
11. Enter any additional comments or remarks in the Additional Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company that is releasing the manufacturing process.
* **Release Date**: Enter the date of the release.
* **Manufacturing Process**: Select the manufacturing process being released.
* **Product**: Select the product being released.
* **Employee**: Select the employee who is submitting the form.
* **Reason for Release**: Enter the reason for releasing the manufacturing process.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or remarks about the release.
* **Email**: Enter the employee's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Status**: Select the status of the manufacturing process (Active or Inactive).
* **Attachment**: Attach any relevant files or documents to the release.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or remarks about the release.
* **Submitted by**: Select the employee who submitted the form.
* **Submitted Date**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or remarks about the release.
* **Submission Information**: This field is not applicable in this form.
