# Bioburden Monitoring Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and track bioburden monitoring data in manufacturing facilities to ensure the quality and safety of products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter facility details, including name and ID.
*   Select the department or area where the sample was collected.
*   Specify the cleanroom classification of the area.
*   Enter the sample collection details, including date and time.
*   Provide the sample ID and type.
*   Enter the location where the sample was taken.
*   Enter the test method used.
*   Enter the incubation temperature and time.
*   Enter the bioburden count and action limit.
*   Enter the test results and result status.
*   Identify any organisms found.
*   Provide any corrective actions taken if out of specification.
*   Specify if corrective action is required.
*   Enter the reviewer's details, including name and review date.
*   Enter the review outcome.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Facility Information** (`facility_name`, `facility_id`, required): Enter the name and ID of the facility.
*   **Department** (`department`, `location_id`, required): Enter the department or area where the sample was collected.
*   **Cleanroom Classification** (`cleanroom_classification`, `required`): Select the cleanroom classification of the area.
*   **Sample Date** (`sample_date`, `required`): Enter the date the sample was collected.
*   **Sample Time** (`sample_time`, `required`): Enter the time the sample was collected.
*   **Sample ID** (`sample_id`, `required`): Enter a unique ID for the sample.
*   **Sample Type** (`sample_type`, `required`): Select the type of sample collected.
*   **Sampling Location** (`sampling_location`, `required`): Enter the location where the sample was taken.
*   **Location ID** (`location_id`, `required`): Enter a location ID.
*   **Collector Name** (`collector_name`, `required`): Enter the name of the person who collected the sample.
*   **Collector ID** (`collector_id`, `required`): Enter the employee ID of the collector.
*   **Test Method** (`test_method`, `required`): Select the method used for testing.
*   **Incubation Temperature** (`incubation_temperature`, `required`): Enter the incubation temperature in Celsius.
*   **Incubation Time** (`incubation_time`, `required`): Enter the incubation time in hours.
*   **Bioburden Count** (`bioburden_count`, `required`): Enter the bioburden count.
*   **Action Limit** (`action_limit`, `required`): Enter the action limit.
*   **Result Status** (`result_status`, `required`): Select the result status.
*   **Organisms Identified** (`organisms_identified`, `required`): Enter any organisms found.
*   **Corrective Actions** (`corrective_actions`, `required`): Enter any corrective actions taken.
*   **Corrective Action Required** (`corrective_action_required`, `required`): Select if corrective action is required.
*   **Corrective Action Description** (`corrective_action_description`, `required`): Enter a description of the corrective action.
*   **Review Information** (`review_information`, `required`): Enter reviewer details.
*   **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, `required`): Enter the name of the QA reviewer.
*   **Review Date** (`review_date`, `required`): Enter the date of the review.
*   **Review Status** (`review_status`, `required`): Select the review outcome.
*   **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `required`): Enter any other observations.
