# Manufacturing Quality Assurance Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from candidates applying for a Manufacturing Quality Assurance job. It gathers data on the candidate's background, contact details, and work experience to assess their suitability for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Candidate Information section with your name, email address, and other relevant details.
2. Provide your contact details, including your address, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
3. Answer the Additional Contact Info questions, selecting the appropriate options for your situation.
4. Upload your resume to demonstrate your qualifications and experience.
5. Share your work history, highlighting your relevant job experience and skills.
6. Describe your job satisfactions, providing specific examples or comments.
7. Answer the second Job Satisfactions question, providing additional details if needed.
8. List your references, including their names, job titles, and contact information.
9. Add any additional references, if applicable.
10. Finalize by reviewing and submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Information** (`candidate_info`, text, required: false): Fill in your name, email address, and any other relevant details to help us get in touch with you.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Provide your address, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Additional Contact Info** (`contact_details_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Select whether you are a local resident, have a driver's license, or have any other relevant details.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required: false): Upload your resume to demonstrate your qualifications and experience.
* **Work History** (`work_history`, text, required: false): Share your work history, highlighting your relevant job experience and skills.
* **Job Satisfactions** (`job_satisfactions`, text, required: false): Describe your job satisfactions, providing specific examples or comments.
* **Job Satisfactions 2** (`job_satisfactions_2`, text, required: false): Answer this question to provide additional details on your job satisfactions.
* **References** (`references`, text, required: false): List your references, including their names, job titles, and contact information.
* **References 2** (`references_2`, text, required: false): Add any additional references if applicable.
* **References 3** (`references_3`, text, required: false): Include any other relevant references.

Please note that some fields may be optional, but providing accurate and complete information will help us assess your application more efficiently.
