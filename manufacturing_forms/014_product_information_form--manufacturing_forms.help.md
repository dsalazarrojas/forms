# Product Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather product information for the manufacturing process. This form is used to collect and document essential details about products, ensuring accuracy and consistency in our records.

## How To Complete This Form
To get started, carefully review the form sections and their respective fields. The form is divided into the following sections:
* Product Details
* Product Images
* Description
* Price
* Manufacturer
* Weight
* Weight Unit
* Category
* Tags
* Note

For each section, follow these steps:
1. Review the section label and understand what information it requires.
2. Fill in the necessary fields with accurate and relevant data.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled in, if applicable.
4. If unsure about any field or section, refer to the field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Details** (`product_details`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the product. This field is optional, but it helps to provide context about the product.
* **Product Images** (`product_images`, select_multiple, not required): Select all applicable images related to the product. You can choose from the options provided.
* **Description** (`product_description`, text, not required): Enter a detailed description of the product. This field is optional but helps to provide a better understanding of the product.
* **Price** (`product_price`, number, not required): Enter the price of the product. This field is optional but helps in pricing and cost calculations.
* **Manufacturer** (`manufacturer`, select_one, not required): Choose whether the product is manufactured or not. You can choose from the options provided.
* **Weight** (`product_weight`, number, not required): Enter the weight of the product. This field is optional but helps in inventory management and logistics.
* **Weight Unit** (`product_weight_unit`, select_one, not required): Choose the unit of weight for the product, such as kg or lbs.
* **Category** (`product_category`, select_multiple, not required): Select all applicable categories for the product. You can choose from the options provided.
* **Tags** (`product_tags`, select_multiple, not required): Select all applicable tags for the product. You can choose from the options provided.
* **Note** (`product_note`, note, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments about the product. This field is optional but helps in providing extra context.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* If unsure about any field or section, refer back to the section label and this help guide for guidance.
* This form is designed to be as straightforward as possible, so if you're still unsure, don't hesitate to ask for help or clarification.
