# mixer_inspection_checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The mixer inspection checklist is used to provide a comprehensive overview of the mixer's status, maintenance schedule, and safety equipment availability.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the mixer's equipment status and select the correct option from the dropdown menu.
2.  Enter the mixer's model number in the text field.
3.  Enter the mixer's mixing capacity in the text field.
4.  List all safety equipment required on-site in the safety equipment field, choosing from the available options or providing a custom entry in the text field.
5.  Select the date for the next maintenance scheduled from the calendar.
6.  Check if the next maintenance is due using the dropdown menu.
7.  Confirm if maintenance has been completed for the mixer using the dropdown menu.
8.  Enter any comments or additional information in the comments field.
9.  Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the mixer.
10.  Enter the contact phone number of the contact person.
11.  Enter the contact email of the contact person.
12.  Select if the form was submitted by a person or team member using the dropdown menu.
13.  Select the date the form was submitted using the calendar.
14.  Enter any additional notes in the notes field.
15.  Sign and date the form with the signature field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Status (Active/Inactive)**: Select the current status of the mixer's equipment.
* **Mixer Model**: Enter the mixer's model number for identification and tracking.
* **Mixing Capacity**: Enter the mixer's capacity for reference and record-keeping purposes.
* **Safety Equipment**: List all safety equipment required on-site for the mixer's operation.
* **Maintenance Scheduling**: Select the date for the next scheduled maintenance.
* **Next Maintenance Due**: Confirm if the next maintenance is due.
* **Maintenance Completed**: Confirm if maintenance has been completed for the mixer.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or additional information about the mixer.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the mixer.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact email of the contact person.
* **Form Submitted By**: Select if the form was submitted by a person or team member.
* **Date Submitted**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the mixer.
* **Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm completion and submission.

Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure that all necessary information is recorded for the mixer.
