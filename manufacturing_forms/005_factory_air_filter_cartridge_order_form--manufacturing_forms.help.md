# factory air filter cartridge order form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer information and order details for air filter cartridges. The purpose of this form is to facilitate the processing of customer orders, ensuring accurate and timely delivery of the ordered products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure that all required fields are filled accurately and correctly.
* Review each field explanation for a detailed description of what each field represents.
* Save your form regularly to avoid losing your progress.
* If unsure about any field, refer to the field explanation or contact the factory representative for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details**: 
  • This field is for customer information.
  • Enter the customer's name, address, and other relevant contact details.
  • This field is optional.
* **Order Details**: 
  • This field is for the order information.
  • Enter the details of the ordered product, including the quantity and other relevant details.
  • This field is optional.
* **Filter Selection**: 
  • This field is to select whether or not the customer has ordered a filter.
  • Select 'Yes' if the customer has ordered a filter and 'No' otherwise.
  • This field is optional.
* **Date of Delivery**: 
  • This field is to select the date of delivery.
  • Enter the date when the customer wants to receive the ordered products.
  • This field is optional.
* **Time of Delivery**: 
  • This field is to select the time of delivery.
  • Enter the time when the customer wants to receive the ordered products.
  • This field is optional.
* **Order Total**: 
  • This field is for the total cost of the order.
  • Enter the total cost of the ordered products.
  • This field is optional.
* **Shipping Cost**: 
  • This field is for the shipping cost of the order.
  • Enter the shipping cost for the ordered products.
  • This field is optional.
* **Customer Notes**: 
  • This field is for any additional notes or comments from the customer.
  • Enter any relevant notes or comments about the order.
  • This field is optional.
