# Manufacturing Line Supervisor Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and scores from a manufacturing line supervisor for a candidate's performance in a manufacturing line experience. This information will be used by HR or management to evaluate and track candidate performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your score for the candidate's manufacturing line experience.
2. Enter the candidate's name.
3. Enter the candidate's phone number.
4. Provide any additional comments about the candidate's performance.
5. Provide any additional comments about your score.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **manufacturing_line_supervisor_score** (number): Enter a score out of 10 for the candidate's manufacturing line experience. This score represents your overall assessment of the candidate's performance.
* **candidate_name** (text): Enter the name of the candidate being evaluated.
* **candidate_phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the candidate.
* **manufacturing_line_supervisor_comments** (text): Enter any additional comments about the candidate's performance.
* **manufacturing_line_supervisor_score_comments** (text): Enter any additional comments about your score, explaining why you gave the score you did.
