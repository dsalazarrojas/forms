# Membrane Switch Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for quote requests of membrane switches. Please complete the form below to provide your requirements for a quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on each tab to navigate through the different sections.
2. Enter the required information in each field.
3. Submit the form once all necessary information is provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Quote Request** (`quote_request_page`, Text, Optional)
Provide a brief description of the desired membrane switch.

### **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, Text, Optional)
Enter the name and contact information of the customer.

### **Membrane Switch Information** (`switch_info`, Text, Optional)
Enter details about the membrane switch you are requesting a quote for, including specifications and any other relevant information.

### **Quote Information** (`quote_info`, Text, Optional)
Enter any specific quote requirements you have.

### **Shipping Information** (`shipping_info`, Text, Optional)
Provide shipping details, if applicable.

### **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, Text, Optional)
Enter your contact information so we can get back to you.

### **Quote Request Information** (`quote_request_info`, Text, Optional)
Provide any additional information about your quote request.

### **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, Text, Optional)
Provide payment details, if applicable.

### **Submit Quote Request** (`submit_request`, Text, Optional)
Click this button to submit your quote request once all fields are filled out.

### **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional)
Enter your email address so we can get back to you.

Note: All fields are optional unless otherwise specified. Please fill out only the fields necessary to provide your requirements for a quote.
