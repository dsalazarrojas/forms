# Manufacturing Workforce Alliance Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect relevant information from manufacturing workforce alliance partners. It will help to establish a formal partnership between the manufacturing entity and the registered partner. The goal is to gather accurate and up-to-date information from each partner.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you provide your real name as the partner name.
3. Enter a valid email address that you have access to.
4. Fill out the contact number with your primary contact number.
5. Select relevant interest areas from the list.
6. Choose a correct partner type from the list.
7. Provide the location of your business or organization.
8. (Optional) Add a message to introduce yourself or your organization.
9. (Optional) Upload your company logo.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, required): Enter your company or organization's name that you would like to register as a manufacturing workforce alliance partner.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you have access to. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, required): Enter your primary contact number that we can use to reach you.
* **Interest Areas** (`interest_areas`, select_multiple, required): Select all the areas that you are interested in, from the provided list.
* **Partner Type** (`partner_type`, select_one, required): Choose the correct partner type from the provided list.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location of your business or organization.
* **Message** (`message`, note, optional): (Optional) Write a brief introduction about your company or organization.
* **Partner Logo** (`partner_logo`, text, optional): (Optional) Upload your company logo for recognition purposes.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have read and understood the purpose of this form before proceeding.
* Double-check the form for any errors before submission.
* Make sure that you have provided accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
