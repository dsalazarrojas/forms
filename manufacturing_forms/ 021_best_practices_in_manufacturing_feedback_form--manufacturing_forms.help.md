# Best Practices In Manufacturing Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather feedback on best practices in manufacturing from customers. It helps to identify areas of improvement and ensure that the manufacturing process is running smoothly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your personal details, including your full name and email address.
2. Choose the date that best applies to your feedback.
3. Select the primary option that best describes your experience.
4. If applicable, provide a whole number for quantity or rating.
5. Add any additional details or comments.
6. Select any practices that you agree or disagree with.
7. If applicable, enter the estimated value in decimal format.
8. Enter a preferred time if scheduling is required.
9. Provide any final comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal full name.
* **Email address** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for contact.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, optional): Include your phone number, including the country code if outside your local region.
* **Date** (`reference_date`, date, required): Select the date that best applies to your feedback.
* **Primary selection** (`primary_selection`, select_one, required): Choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **Quantity or rating** (`quantity_or_rating`, number, optional): Provide a whole number for quantity or rating.
* **Additional details** (`additional_details`, text, optional): Add any additional relevant information.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_multiple, required): Select any practices that you agree or disagree with.
* **Estimated value** (`estimated_value`, number, optional): Enter the estimated value in decimal format.
* **Preferred time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): Enter a time if scheduling is required.
* **Final comments or notes** (`final_comments`, text, optional): Provide any final comments or notes.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Provide clear and concise information for each field.
* Use decimal format for estimated value.
* Select only the relevant options for primary selection and agreement.
