# Factory Equipment Documentation Kit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers or clients for a Factory Equipment Documentation Kit order. It will guide you through the necessary steps to provide accurate and complete details for your order.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out the form carefully, following the instructions provided for each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): This field is where you should enter your company's name for the order.
* **Equipment Ordered** (`equipment_ordered`, select_one, optional): Select the type of equipment you are ordering from the options provided.
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, optional): Enter any special delivery instructions or requirements you may have.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to receive the order.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter your shipping address for the ordered equipment.
* **Shipping Phone** (`shipping_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for the shipping address.
* **Shipping Email** (`shipping_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for the shipping address.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's name for the order.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or information you'd like to include with the order.
* **Delivery Instructions** (`delivery_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any special delivery instructions, such as specific delivery times or locations.

## Tips
- Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant personnel for assistance.
- Ensure you have all necessary permissions and approvals before completing the form.
