# juice_production_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and information from users about juice production. It helps the production team understand what works well and what can be improved, allowing them to make data-driven decisions to enhance the juice production process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out each field carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. If you're unsure about a particular field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation below.
3. Submit the form when you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_page** (Page 1, Text, Required: False): Please provide your name and title or position in the production team.
* **feedback** (Page 1, Text, Required: False): Share any general feedback or comments about the juice production process.
* **rating** (Page 1, Number, Required: False): Rate the quality of the juice production process on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **select_one** (Page 1, Select One, Required: False): Choose one of the following options:
	+ apple
	+ banana
	+ orange
* **select_multiple** (Page 1, Select Multiple, Required: False): Select all that apply:
	+ A
	+ B
	+ C
* **date** (Page 1, Date, Required: False): Enter the date of the juice production batch you're providing feedback on.
* **time** (Page 1, Time, Required: False): Enter the time of the juice production batch you're providing feedback on.
* **email** (Page 1, Email, Required: False): Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
* **phone_number** (Page 1, Text, Required: False): Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.

## Tips

* Be specific and detailed in your feedback to help the production team improve the process.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the feedback field.
* If you're not sure about the options in select_one or select_multiple, refer to the production schedule or production notes for guidance.
