# prototype_inspection_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and document essential information during the inspection of prototypes in a manufacturing process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you have all necessary information and approvals before proceeding.
2. Enter the prototype name and date inspected in the corresponding fields.
3. Indicate the person who inspected the prototype.
4. Provide notes on the inspection, including any issues found and corrective actions taken or recommended.
5. Determine the outcome of the inspection and select the corresponding status.
6. Enter the production status and location, if applicable.
7. Record the production date and any production comments.
8. Identify any production issues and provide their description.
9. Summarize the production outcome and provide any additional comments.
10. Enter any review comments and date.
11. Identify the person who reviewed the prototype.
12. Complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that all necessary information is collected and documented.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Prototype Name**: Enter the name of the prototype being inspected.
	+ Example: "Sample Prototype"
* **Date Inspected**: Enter the date the prototype was inspected.
	+ Format: MM/DD/YYYY
	+ Example: 01/01/2020
* **Inspected By**: Enter the person who inspected the prototype.
	+ Example: John Doe
* **Inspection Notes**: Provide notes on the inspection, including any issues found and corrective actions taken or recommended.
	+ Example: "The prototype had some minor issues with the design but overall it met all required specifications."
* **Outcome**: Select the outcome of the inspection (Approved, Not Approved, On Hold, Rejected).
	+ Example: Approved
* **Issues Found**: Indicate if any issues were found during the inspection.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **Issue Description**: Describe any issues found during the inspection.
	+ Example: "The design had a minor defect that needs to be addressed."
* **Corrective Actions**: Provide any corrective actions taken or recommended.
	+ Example: "The design team will review and revise the prototype to fix the issue."
* **Action Status**: Enter the status of the corrective actions (in progress, completed, etc.).
	+ Example: In Progress
* **Next Review Due**: Enter the date of the next review.
	+ Format: MM/DD/YYYY
	+ Example: 01/15/2020
* **Review Frequency**: Enter the review frequency (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).
	+ Example: bi-weekly
* **Responsible Person**: Enter the person responsible for the next review.
	+ Example: Jane Doe
* **Review Notes**: Provide notes on the review.
	+ Example: "The prototype has been revised to address the issues found."
* **Prototype Status**: Select the current status of the prototype (Approved, Not Approved, On Hold, Rejected).
	+ Options: Approved, Not Approved, On Hold, Rejected
* **Production Status**: Select the current production status (In Design, In Progress, In Production, Ready for Production, In Review, Not Ready for Production).
	+ Options: In Design, In Progress, In Production, Ready for Production, In Review, Not Ready for Production
* **Production Location**: Enter the location of production.
	+ Example: Factory X
* **Production Date**: Enter the date of production.
	+ Format: MM/DD/YYYY
	+ Example: 01/10/2020
* **Production Comments**: Provide any comments on production.
	+ Example: "The production process was completed with minimal issues."
* **Production Issues**: Describe any production issues.
	+ Example: "The production team had some challenges with the assembly process."
* **Production Outcome**: Summarize the outcome of production.
	+ Example: "The production process was successful."
* **Outcome Comments**: Provide any additional comments on the outcome.
	+ Example: "The prototype met all required specifications."
* **Review Comments**: Provide any comments on the review.
	+ Example: "The review process was thorough and efficient."
* **Review Date**: Enter the date of the review.
	+ Format: MM/DD/YYYY
	+ Example: 01/20/2020
* **Review Comments By**: Enter the person who reviewed the prototype.
	+ Example: Jane Doe
* **Production Location**: Enter the location of production.
	+ Example: Factory X
