# Maintenance Audit For Operators - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Maintenance Audit For Operators" form is designed to gather essential information about maintenance activities performed by operators. This includes details about equipment type, location, and status. The form helps standardize the process of documenting and tracking maintenance tasks, ensuring consistency and accuracy in the reporting process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have a clear understanding of the maintenance activity you're reporting. Review the equipment type and status (Active or Inactive) in the "Equipment Type" and "Equipment Status" fields.
2. Enter the location where the maintenance activity was performed in the "Location" field.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Equipment Type," "Section," "Area," "Room," and "Department" as they apply to the maintenance activity.
4. Enter the date and time of completion in the "Date Completed" and "Time Completed" fields.
5. Enter the name of the operator who completed the maintenance activity in the "Operator" field.
6. Enter any additional comments or notes about the maintenance activity in the "Comments" field (if applicable).
7. Review and ensure accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page**: This is the initial page of the form, where you'll enter the general information about the maintenance activity.
	+ **Location (ID: 3)**: Enter the specific location where the maintenance activity was performed.
* **Equipment Type (ID: 2)**: Indicate if the equipment was active or inactive during the maintenance activity.
* **Date Completed (ID: 5)**: Enter the date in MM/DD/YY format when the maintenance activity was completed.
* **Time Completed (ID: 6)**: Enter the time in 12-hour format (e.g., 02:30 PM).
* **Operator (ID: 7)**: Enter the name of the operator who performed the maintenance activity.
* **Comments (ID: 8)**: Add any additional comments or observations about the maintenance activity (if applicable).
* **Equipment Status (ID: 9)**: Indicate the status of the equipment (Active or Inactive).
* **Completed By (ID: 10)**: Enter the email address of the operator who completed the maintenance activity.
* **Date Created (ID: 11)**: This field is not editable and will automatically display the date the form was created.
* **Date Modified (ID: 12)**: This field is not editable and will automatically display the last modified date of the form.
* **Equipment (ID: 13)**: Indicate if the equipment was active or inactive during the maintenance activity.
* **User Name (ID: 14)**: Enter your name for the record of who initiated the form.
* **User Email (ID: 15)**: Enter your email address for the record of who initiated the form.
* **Department (ID: 16)**: Indicate the department related to the maintenance activity (e.g., engineering, operations, etc.).
* **Section (ID: 17)**: Indicate the section related to the maintenance activity.
* **Area (ID: 18)**: Indicate the area where the maintenance activity took place.
* **Room (ID: 19)**: Indicate the room where the maintenance activity was performed.
* **Equipment Type (ID: 20)**: Indicate if the equipment was active or inactive during the maintenance activity.
* **Notes (ID: 21)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the maintenance activity (if applicable).
* **Status (ID: 22)**: Indicate the status of the form submission (Active or Inactive).

Please review this guide carefully and use it as a reference when completing the form. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the maintenance team for assistance.
