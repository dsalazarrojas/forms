# Manufacturing Machinery Reliability Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manufacturing Machinery Reliability Audit Form is a tool for production teams to assess the condition and maintenance history of manufacturing machinery. This form helps identify potential issues and prioritize maintenance tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and gather the necessary information.
2. Answer each question accurately and thoroughly.
3. Ensure you have permission to access and collect data on the specified machinery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info** (`general_info`, text, optional): Provide a brief overview of the machine and its purpose in the production process.
* **Machine Info** (`machine_info`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the machine, including its model, serial number, and any relevant specifications.
* **Condition** (`condition`, text, optional): Describe the current state of the machine, including any visible signs of wear or damage.
* **Maintenance** (`maintenance`, text, optional): Record any recent maintenance or repairs performed on the machine.
* **Issues** (`issues`, text, optional): List any known or potential issues with the machine that may impact its performance or reliability.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out this form.
* Use a standard format for consistency.
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure ongoing assessment and maintenance of manufacturing machinery.
