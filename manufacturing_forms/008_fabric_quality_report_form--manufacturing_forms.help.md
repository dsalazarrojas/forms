# Fabric Quality Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about fabric quality, including supplier details, batch information, inspection data, fabric specifications, quality control, and comments.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter supplier information on the first page.
2. Provide batch details.
3. Enter inspection data.
4. Enter fabric information.
5. Report quality control issues or defects.
6. Add comments or additional notes.
7. Repeat the process for subsequent pages, if any.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Information** (Page 1): Enter the name, contact details, or any other relevant information about the supplier.
* **Batch Information** (Page 1): Enter details about the batch, such as its ID, production date, or any other relevant information.
* **Inspection Data** (Page 1): Enter the date and time of inspection or any other relevant data related to the inspection process.
* **Fabric Information** (Page 1): Enter details about the fabric, such as its type, material, or any other relevant information.
* **Quality Control** (Page 1): Enter any quality control issues or concerns found during inspection.
* **Defects** (Page 1): Report any defects or defects observed during inspection.
* **Comments** (Page 1): Enter any additional comments or notes about the fabric quality.
* **First Page 2** (Page 2): This field is identical to the first page, and its purpose is to collect the same information as the first page.
