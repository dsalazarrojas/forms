# Manufacturing Logistics Company Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about manufacturing logistics companies that you think may be a good fit for our business. Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to help us evaluate the referred company's potential partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your personal details, including your first and last name.
2. Enter your valid email address.
3. Provide your job title.
4. If you are referring a company, please enter the referred company's name.
5. Enter the reason why you are referring this company.
6. Enter the contact information of the referred company's representative.
7. Enter the referred company's address.
8. Enter the referred company's website, if applicable.
9. Enter the referred company's phone number, if applicable.
10. Enter the referred company's email address, if applicable.
11. Provide any additional notes about the referred company.
12. Click the "Submit" button to send your referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Job Title**: Please enter your current job title.
* **Company**: Enter the name of your current company.
* **Referral Reason**: Explain why you are referring this company. Be specific and honest about your connection to the referred company.
* **Referred Company**: Enter the name of the company you are referring.
* **Referred Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of the referred company's representative.
* **Referred Company Address**: Enter the address of the referred company.
* **Referred Company Website**: If the referred company has a website, enter the URL.
* **Referred Company Phone**: Enter the phone number of the referred company.
* **Referred Company Email**: Enter the email address of the referred company.
* **Referred Company Notes**: Enter any additional information about the referred company.
* **Submit**: Click this button to send your referral.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and clearance to refer a company.
* Be honest and specific about your connection to the referred company.
* Only submit forms for companies that align with our company's core values and mission.
