# Warehouse Layout Redesign Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warehouse Layout Redesign Application Form is used to collect information from warehouse management on the current state of their warehouse layout, including any issues they're experiencing and their goals for a new layout. This form will help the design team understand the warehouse's needs and preferences for the new layout.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your warehouse's unique ID in the "Warehouse ID" field.
2. Describe the address of your warehouse in the "Address" field.
3. Identify any layout issues you're experiencing in the "Layout Issues" field. You can select multiple issues that apply to you.
4. Specify your goals for the new warehouse layout in the "Layout Goals" field.
5. Describe any required improvements for the new layout in the "Required Improvements" field.
6. If you have any files or attachments related to your warehouse's layout, upload them in the "File Uploads" field (optional).
7. Review and submit your form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Warehouse ID** (`warehouse_id`, `number`, required): Enter your warehouse's unique ID, a numeric value that helps us identify your warehouse's location and operations.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Describe the physical address of your warehouse, including the street name, number, city, state, and zip code.
* **Layout Issues** (`layout_issues`, `select_multiple`, required): Identify any issues you're experiencing with your current warehouse layout. You can select multiple issues that apply to you.
* **Layout Goals** (`layout_goals`, `text`, optional): Specify your goals for the new warehouse layout. This could be related to increased efficiency, better organization, or improved safety.
* **Required Improvements** (`required_improvements`, `text`, required): Describe any required improvements you need for the new layout.
* **File Uploads** (`file_uploads`, `text`, optional): If you have any relevant files or attachments related to your warehouse's layout, such as diagrams or photos, upload them here.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional): Review and submit your form when finished.
