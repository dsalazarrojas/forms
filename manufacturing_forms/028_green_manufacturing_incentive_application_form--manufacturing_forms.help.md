# Green Manufacturing Incentive Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Green Manufacturing Incentive Application Form is designed to help manufacturers apply for incentives to reduce their environmental impact and improve their sustainability practices.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, please fill out the required fields with your company's information.
2. Select the type of incentive you are applying for from the options provided.
3. Provide the date and time of submission.
4. Describe the manufacturing process and production capacity of your company.
5. Choose the green practices your company is implementing or has implemented.
6. If applicable, upload any supporting files or certificates.
7. Add any additional comments or notes about your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's name as it appears on your business license or registration.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your company's email address.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name and title of the person submitting the application.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your company's address.
* **Incentive** (`incentive`, text, optional): Briefly describe the type of incentive you are applying for.
* **Type of Incentive** (`type_of_incentive`, select_one, required): Select the specific type of incentive you are applying for from the provided options.
* **Date/Time of Submission** (`date_time_of_submission`, date, required): Enter the date and time of submission.
* **Manufacturing Process** (`manufacturing_process`, text, optional): Describe the manufacturing process used by your company.
* **Production Capacity** (`production_capacity`, number, optional): Enter your company's production capacity.
* **Green Practices** (`green_practices`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the green practices your company is implementing or has implemented.
* **Certification** (`certification`, text, optional): Enter any relevant certification or certification number.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your application.
* **Upload File** (`upload_file`, text, optional): Upload any supporting files or documentation.
* **Upload Certificate** (`upload_certificate`, text, optional): Upload any relevant certificates or certification documents.
* **Uploaded Certificate** (`uploaded_certificate`, text, optional): Display the uploaded certificate.

Note: Please ensure that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions before submitting this form.
