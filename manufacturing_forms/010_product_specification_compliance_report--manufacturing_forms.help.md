# Product Specification Compliance Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Specification Compliance Report form is used to track the status of product specifications compliance. This form captures information from various parties involved in the production process, including customers and suppliers, to ensure accurate and timely reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Customer ID.
2. Enter the Specification ID associated with the product.
3. Select the Inspection Date of the product.
4. Choose the Compliance Status of the product (Compliance or Non-Compliance).
5. If applicable, enter Corrective Actions taken or not taken.
6. Enter any additional comments from the supplier.
7. Enter any comments from the Quality Assurance department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, Number, Not Required): Enter your company's or customer's ID.
* **Specification ID** (`specification_id`, Number, Not Required): Enter the unique identifier associated with the product specification.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, Date, Not Required): Select the date of the product inspection.
* **Compliance Status** (`compliance_status`, Select One, Required): Choose whether the product is in compliance or non-compliance with the specification.
* **Corrective Actions** (`corrective_actions`, Select Multiple, Not Required): Check if corrective actions were taken or not.
* **Supplier Comments** (`supplier_comments`, Text, Not Required): Enter any comments from the supplier.
* **QA Comments** (`qa_comments`, Text, Not Required): Enter any comments from the Quality Assurance department.

**Tips**

* Make sure to select the correct Compliance Status.
* Enter any relevant comments to provide additional context.
* If corrective actions were taken, select the corresponding option.
