# Industrial Process Improvement Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about industrial process improvements. Please use this form to report any process improvements you wish to address in your manufacturing process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Industrial Process Improvement Report**: In this section, provide a brief description of the process improvement area you are looking to address.
2. **Category**: Select the category that best describes the type of process improvement you are reporting (Manufacturing or Other).
3. **Description**: Briefly describe the process improvement area(s) you are looking to address.
4. **Current Process Name**: Enter the current name of the process.
5. **Current Process Description**: Describe the current process in detail.
6. **Improvement Area**: Select the type of process improvement area you are looking to address (Manufacturing Equipment, Manufacturing Process, or Other).
7. **Current Process Score**: Rate the current process on a scale of 1-5.
8. **Improvement Area Score**: Rate the improvement area on a scale of 1-5.
9. **Improvement Area Description**: Describe the improvement area(s) you are looking to address.
10. **Process Improvement Plan**: Select the method you plan to use to improve the process (Root Cause Analysis, 8Ds Method, or Other).
11. **Improvement Area Score Overall**: Rate the improvement area overall on a scale of 1-5.
12. **Date of Last Update**: Enter the date of the last update to the process.
13. **Time of Last Update**: Enter the time of the last update to the process.
14. **Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments about the process improvement area.
15. **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your report.
16. **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your report.
