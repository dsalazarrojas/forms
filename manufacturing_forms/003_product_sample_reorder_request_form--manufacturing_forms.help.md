# Product Sample Reorder Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a product sample for testing or evaluation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the product you are interested in by choosing an option in the "Product Sample" field.
2. Choose the type of sample you need by selecting an option in the "Sample Type" field.
3. Specify the quantity of the sample you need in the "Quantity" field.
4. Provide a reason for reordering the sample in the "Reorder Reason" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (this is optional).
7. Optionally, enter the date you would like to receive the sample by in the "Order Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Details** (text, optional): This field is where you should provide any additional information about the product you are requesting a sample of.
* **Product Sample** (select_one, optional): Choose the product you are interested in from the list of available options.
* **Sample Type** (select_one, optional): Select the type of sample you need (Standard, Express, or Premium).
* **Quantity** (number, optional): Enter the quantity of the sample you need.
* **Reorder Reason** (note, optional): Provide a reason for reordering the sample.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address where we can send the sample to you.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number (this is not required, but we may call you if we need more information).
* **Order Date** (date, optional): If you would like to receive the sample by a specific date, please enter it here.

## Tips
* Only submit this form once for each product sample you need.
* If you have already submitted a request for the same product sample, do not resubmit.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (if any) accurately and completely.
* If you have any issues or need help with the form, contact us at your preferred contact method.
