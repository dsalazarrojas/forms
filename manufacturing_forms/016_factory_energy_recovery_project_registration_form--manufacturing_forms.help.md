# Factory Energy Recovery Project Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a Factory Energy Recovery Project. It's intended for use within the manufacturing industry to register projects related to energy recovery and efficiency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling in the project name and customer name fields, which are required for identifying the project and the associated customer.
2.  Enter the customer's email address in the customer email field, which will be used for communication and contact purposes.
3.  Review and verify the information provided to ensure it's accurate and complete.
4.  Once filled out, submit the form to register the Factory Energy Recovery Project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Factory Energy Recovery Project Registration** (page 1, **factory_energy_recovery_project_registration**): This is a note field where you can provide any additional information about the project. It's not mandatory, so feel free to add any relevant details about the project.
* **Project Name** (page 2, **project_name**): Enter the name of your project, such as "Energy Recovery Initiative" or "Efficient Production Line".
* **Customer Name** (page 2, **customer_name**): Type in the name of the customer or organization associated with the project.
* **Customer Email** (page 2, **customer_email**): Enter the email address of the customer or organization where they can be contacted for further information or questions.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure successful project registration.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, please reach out to our team.
- Only submit one form per project to avoid duplication of efforts and maintain accurate records.
