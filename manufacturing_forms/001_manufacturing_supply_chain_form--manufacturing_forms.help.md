# Manufacturing Supply Chain Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form collects information from manufacturers about their supply chain activities. This information can be used to monitor and analyze the supply chain processes and optimize them for better efficiency and quality.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information.
2.  Provide clear and concise answers to the questions.
3.  Make sure to proofread your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Manufacturer Info** (`manufacturer_info`, text, optional): Enter the name and other basic details about the manufacturing company.
*   **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter the contact information of the person responsible for the supply chain activities.
*   **Production Info** (`production_info`, text, optional): Enter details about the production activities, such as the type of product, production quantity, and production date.
*   **Shipping Info** (`shipping_info`, text, optional): Enter details about the shipping activities, such as shipping dates, shipment quantity, and shipping destinations.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in the form accurately and completely.
*   If you are unsure about any field, consult with your company's supply chain manager or the person responsible for the activities.
*   You can submit the form multiple times for different supply chain activities or production batches.
