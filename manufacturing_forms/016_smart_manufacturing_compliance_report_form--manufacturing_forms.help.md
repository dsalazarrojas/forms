# Smart Manufacturing Compliance Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help manufacturing teams record and track compliance incidents, including non-compliance incidents, corrective actions, and follow-up frequencies. It ensures that teams are able to effectively report and manage compliance issues, improving overall manufacturing quality and efficiency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the last inspection.
	* **Production Location**: Enter the production location where the incident occurred.
	* **Non-Compliance Incident**: Describe the non-compliance incident.
	* **Corrective Action**: Describe the corrective action taken.
	* **Action Effective Date**: Enter the date the corrective action was implemented.
	* **Assigned To**: Enter the person responsible for the action.
	* **Inspection Area**: Enter the area where the inspection was conducted.
	* **Inspection Item**: Enter the item inspected.
	* **Observation**: Enter any observations made during the inspection.
	* **Action Status**: Select the status of the corrective action (Corrective action implemented, Corrective action in progress, No action taken, or No response from team).
	* **Action Item Status**: Select the status of the action item (Not Started, In Progress, Completed, or Not Applicable).
	* **Follow-up Frequency**: Select the frequency of follow-up activities (e.g., weekly, monthly, etc.).
	* **Next Follow-up Date**: Enter the date of the next follow-up activity.
	* **Follow-up Status**: Select the status of the follow-up (Scheduled, In Progress, Completed, or N/A).
	* **Follow-up Frequency 2**: Select the frequency of follow-up activities for this specific follow-up (e.g., weekly, monthly, etc.).
	* **Follow-up Status 2**: Select the status of the follow-up (Scheduled, In Progress, Completed, or N/A).
	* **Follow-up Frequency 2 Next Follow-up Date**: Enter the date of the next follow-up activity.

Optional fields:
	* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or observations.
	* **Attachments**: Upload any relevant attachments or documents.
