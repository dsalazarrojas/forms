# Manufacturing Long Term Sick Leave Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manufacturing Long Term Sick Leave Request form is used to submit a request for extended sick leave from work due to illness or injury. This form provides a structured process for employees to submit their requests, allowing for easy tracking and management of absences.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your *User ID*, which is your unique identifier at the organization.
2.  Enter the *Start Date* and *End Date* of your leave period.
3.  Provide a brief *Reason* for your leave request.
4.  Specify the total *Number of Days Required* for your leave.
5.  Select your *Department* from the provided options.
6.  Enter your *Submitter Name* and *Submitter Email* for contact information.
7.  Choose the *Approver Name* responsible for reviewing your request.
8.  Enter your *Approver Email* for contact information.
9.  Add any *Comments* you wish to include with your request.
10.  If necessary, attach a supporting *Attachment*.
11.  Once you have completed all fields, click the submission button to send the form.
