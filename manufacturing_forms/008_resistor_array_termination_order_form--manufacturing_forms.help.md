This form is used to collect information from customers for resistor array termination orders. The sales, engineering, or production teams use this form to take orders from customers and ensure accurate communication and fulfillment of their needs.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the `customer_name` field.
2. Enter the customer's email in the `customer_email` field.
3. Enter the customer's phone number in the `customer_phone` field.
4. Select the date of termination in the `termination_date` field.
5. Select the time of termination in the `termination_time` field.
6. Enter any additional termination notes in the `termination_notes` field.
