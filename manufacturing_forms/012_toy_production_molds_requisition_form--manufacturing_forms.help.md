# Toy Production Molds Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request production molds for toys. It is used to gather information about the manufacturing company, contact person, and production details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information such as manufacturing company, contact person, and phone number.
2. Enter the production date and production time.
3. Select the level of urgency for the production (urgent or non-urgent).
4. Add a description of the tooling required.
5. Enter the quantity of tooling needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Toy Production Molds Requisition** (`toy_production_molds_requisition_form`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the toy production molds required.
* **Manufacturing Company** (`manufacturing_company`, text, not required): Enter the name of the company that will be producing the toys.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, not required): Enter the name of the person who will be responsible for contacting you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, not required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, not required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Production Date** (`production_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the production.
* **Production Time** (`production_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the production.
* **Urgent Need** (`urgent_need`, select_one, not required): Select "Urgent" if the production is needed urgently, "Non-Urgent" otherwise.
* **Tooling Description** (`tooling_description`, note, not required): Enter a description of the tooling required.
* **Tooling Quantity** (`tooling_quantity`, number, not required): Enter the quantity of tooling needed.
* **Tooling Status** (`tooling_status`, select_one, not required): Select the status of the tooling (in progress, approved, or cancelled).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields (Manufacturing Company, Contact Person, and Production Date).
* If you are unsure about the level of urgency, select "Non-Urgent" as a default.
* Enter accurate and detailed information about the tooling required and needed.
* If you need to cancel or update the production, select the corresponding status in the "Tooling Status" field.
