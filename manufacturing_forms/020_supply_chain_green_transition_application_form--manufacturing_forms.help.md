# Supply Chain Green Transition Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Supply Chain Green Transition Application Form is designed to help companies demonstrate their commitment to sustainability and reduce their environmental impact.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Ensure that all fields have the correct format and content.
3. Review each section carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, not required): 
Enter the name of your company.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, not required): 
Enter the name of the person filling out the form.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `text`, not required): 
Enter the email address of the person filling out the form.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, not required): 
Enter the phone number of the person filling out the form.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, `text`, not required): 
Enter the address of your company.
* **Company Type** (`company_type`, `text`, not required): 
Select the type of company you represent (e.g., manufacturer, retailer, etc.).
* **Industry** (`industry`, `text`, not required): 
Select the industry your company belongs to.
* **Company Description** (`company_description`, `text`, not required): 
Provide a brief description of your company.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, not required): 
Enter the location of your company.
* **Green Initiatives Description** (`green_initiatives_description`, `text`, not required): 
Describe the sustainability initiatives your company is involved in.
* **Green Initiatives** (`green_initiatives`, `text`, not required): 
List the sustainability initiatives your company is involved in.
* **Certification Description** (`certification_description`, `text`, not required): 
Describe the certification your company is applying for.
* **Certification** (`certification`, `text`, not required): 
Select the certification your company is applying for.
* **Certification Status** (`certification_status`, `text`, not required): 
Provide the status of your certification.
* **Action Plan Description** (`action_plan_description`, `text`, not required): 
Describe the action plan your company is proposing.
* **Action Plan** (`action_plan`, `text`, not required): 
Outline the steps your company plans to take for a green transition.
* **Timeline** (`timeline`, `text`, not required): 
Provide a timeline for your company's green transition.
* **Submission** (`submission`, `text`, not required): 
Provide a submission or a summary of your application.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, not required): 
Add any additional comments or notes to your application.
