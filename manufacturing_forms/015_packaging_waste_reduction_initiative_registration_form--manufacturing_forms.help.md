# Packaging Waste Reduction Initiative Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Packaging Waste Reduction Initiative Registration Form is designed to gather information from organizations interested in participating in the initiative. Please fill out this form carefully and honestly to ensure accurate representation of your organization.
## How To Complete This Form
1. **Answer the questions honestly**: Provide accurate information about your organization's participation in the initiative.
2. **Select the correct options**: Choose the options that best represent your organization's situation.
3. **Fill out all required fields**: Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
4. **Upload any necessary documents**: If required, upload the necessary documents to support your application.
5. **Review and submit**: Review the form carefully before submitting it. If you need to cancel or submit your form, select the corresponding option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user**: Select 'Yes' if your organization is interested in participating in the Packaging Waste Reduction Initiative. (Required)
* **organization name**: Enter the name of your organization. (Required)
* **contact name**: Enter the name of the contact person for your organization. (Optional)
* **contact job title**: Enter the job title of the contact person. (Optional)
* **email**: Enter the email address of the contact person. (Optional)
* **phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person. (Optional)
* **address**: Enter the physical address of your organization. (Optional)
* **country**: Select 'Yes' if your organization operates in multiple countries. (Optional)
* **state**: Select 'Yes' if your organization operates in multiple states. (Optional)
* **city**: Enter the city where your organization is located. (Optional)
* **zip**: Enter the zip code of your organization's location. (Optional)
* **company**: Enter the name of your company. (Optional)
* **product**: Enter the product or product line of your organization. (Optional)
* **quantity**: Enter the expected reduction in waste. (Optional)
* **prophets reduction**: This field is not clearly labeled. Please fill this out as "prophets reduction" is likely a typo or mislabeled. (Optional)
* **target date**: Enter the target date for reducing waste. (Optional)
* **target time**: Enter the target time for reducing waste. (Optional)
* **note**: Enter any additional information about your organization's participation. (Optional)
* **signature**: Provide a signature to confirm your identity. (Optional)
* **file**: Upload any supporting documents. (Optional)
* **upload file**: This field is not clearly labeled. Please fill this out as "upload file" is likely a typo or mislabeled. (Optional)
* **confirm upload**: Select 'Yes' if you have uploaded a file. (Optional)
* **submit**: Select 'Yes' to submit the form. (Required)
* **cancel**: Select 'Yes' to cancel the form submission. (Required)

Note: The optional fields are not required for form submission, but they can provide additional context and information to the initiative's administrators.
