# inventory_management_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The inventory_management_form is a general-purpose form for tracking and managing inventory data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the form in the "Form Title" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the form's purpose in the "Form Description" field.
3. Select the category of the form from the options provided in the "Form Category" field.
4. Enter any additional description in the "Description" field.
5. Enter the date and time of the form's creation in the "Date" and "Time" fields, respectively.
6. Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the form in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, not required): Enter a brief title for the form.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, not required): Provide a brief description of the form's purpose.
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, select_one, not required): Select the category of the form from the options provided (Manufacturing or Other).
* **Description** (`form_description`, text, not required): Enter any additional description of the form.
* **Date** (`form_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the form's creation.
* **Time** (`form_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the form's creation.
* **Phone** (`form_phone`, text, not required): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the form.

## Tips
* Fill out all required fields to ensure that the data is complete and accurate.
* If you are unsure about a particular field, feel free to ask for clarification.
* The form category is not required, but selecting the correct category will help with organization and searching.
