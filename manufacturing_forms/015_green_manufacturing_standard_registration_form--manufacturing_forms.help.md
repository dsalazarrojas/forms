# Green Manufacturing Standard Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Green Manufacturing Standard Registration Form is designed for companies to declare their commitment to sustainable practices and register for the Green Manufacturing Standard. This form helps companies to provide information about their environmental impact and ensure transparency and accountability in the manufacturing industry.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields:
	* Name: Enter the official name of your company as it appears on your business registration.
	* Address: Enter your company's business address.
	* Energy Consumption (kWh): Enter your company's energy consumption in kilowatt-hours (kWh).
	* Water Consumption (m3): Enter your company's water consumption in cubic meters (m3).
	* Waste to Landfill (kg): Enter your company's waste to landfill in kilograms (kg).
	* Greenhouse Gas Emissions (tonnes): Enter your company's greenhouse gas emissions in tonnes.
2. Confirm your certification status for:
	* Confirm Greenhouse Gas Emissions: Select the correct status for your company's greenhouse gas emissions certification.
	* Confirm Sustainability Reporting: Select the correct status for your company's sustainability reporting certification.
	* Confirm Environmental Impact Assessment: Select the correct status for your company's environmental impact assessment certification.
3. (Optional) Add any additional certifications or notes about your company's sustainability practices.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (Required)** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter the official name of your company as it appears on your business registration.
* **Address (Required)** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your company's business address.
* **Energy Consumption (kWh) (Required)** (`energy_consumption_kwh`, `number`, required): Enter your company's energy consumption in kilowatt-hours (kWh).
* **Water Consumption (m3) (Required)** (`water_consumption_m3`, `number`, required): Enter your company's water consumption in cubic meters (m3).
* **Waste to Landfill (kg) (Required)** (`waste_to_landfill_kg`, `number`, required): Enter your company's waste to landfill in kilograms (kg).
* **Greenhouse Gas Emissions (tonnes) (Required)** (`greenhouse_gas_emissions_tonnes`, `number`, required): Enter your company's greenhouse gas emissions in tonnes.
* **Confirm Greenhouse Gas Emissions (Required)** (`confirm_greenhouse_gas_emissions`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct status for your company's greenhouse gas emissions certification.
* **Confirm Sustainability Reporting (Required)** (`confirm_sustainability_reporting`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct status for your company's sustainability reporting certification.
* **Confirm Environmental Impact Assessment (Required)** (`confirm_environmental_impact_assessment`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct status for your company's environmental impact assessment certification.
* **Certifications (Optional)** (`certifications`, `text`, optional): Add any additional certifications or notes about your company's sustainability practices.
* **Sustainability Reporting (Optional)** (`sustainability_reporting`, `text`, optional): Add any additional information about your company's sustainability reporting practices.
