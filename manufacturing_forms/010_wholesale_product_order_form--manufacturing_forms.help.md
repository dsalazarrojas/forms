# Wholesale Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about wholesale product orders from customers. It helps us to process orders efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Customer Name, Product Name, Order Date, and Order Time fields with the relevant information.
2. Enter the Contact Person's name and the Customer Address.
3. Provide the Order Details and any additional comments.
4. Choose the Payment Method and select the customer's ID if they have one.
5. Enter the Product ID of the product being ordered.
6. Fill in the Order Number and any Order Comments.
7. Enter the Payment Status and Customer Comments.
8. Finally, fill in the Shipping Address, Shipping City, and Shipping Postal Code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter the full name of the customer.
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product being ordered.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time**: Enter the time of the order.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Customer Address**: Enter the customer's address.
* **Order Details**: Enter any additional details about the order.
* **Notes**: Enter any notes or comments about the order.
* **Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Product Price**: Enter the price of the product being ordered.
* **Quantity Ordered**: Enter the quantity of the product ordered.
* **Order Total**: Enter the total price of the order.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method used (Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, or Other).
* **Customer ID**: Select one or more of the customer's IDs.
* **Sales Status**: Select the status of the order (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
* **Order Status**: Select the status of the order (New, In Progress, Completed, or Canceled).
* **Product ID**: Select the ID of the product being ordered.
* **Order Number**: Enter the order number.
* **Order Comments**: Enter any comments about the order.
* **Payment Status**: Select the status of the payment (Paid, Partially Paid, or Unpaid).
* **Customer Comments**: Enter any comments from the customer.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the shipping address.
* **Shipping City**: Enter the city of the shipping address.
* **Shipping Province**: Enter the province of the shipping address.
* **Shipping Postal Code**: Enter the postal code of the shipping address.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate order processing.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask your contact person for clarification.
* This form is for wholesale product orders only; if you're ordering for personal use, please use the personal order form.
