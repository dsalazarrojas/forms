# Drying Process Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to document the drying process for a production batch. It helps production technitians to track and report the progress of the drying process, including any issues that may arise during the process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure that you have all the necessary information about the production batch and drying cycle before filling out this form.
2. Fill in the required fields, including drying cycle ID, batch ID, and start and end times.
3. Record the dryer ID and drying temperature.
4. Note the moisture level at the start and end of the drying process.
5. Enter the total drying time.
6. Enter any observations or notes about the drying process.
7. Identify the production technitian who performed the drying process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Drying Cycle ID** (`drying_cycle_id`, number): Enter the ID of the drying cycle that this form is associated with.
* **Batch ID** (`batch_id`, number): Enter the ID of the production batch being processed.
* **Dryer ID** (`dryer_id`, number): Enter the ID of the dryer used in the drying process.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time): Enter the start time of the drying process.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time): Enter the end time of the drying process.
* **Drying Temperature** (`drying_temperature`, number): Enter the temperature at which the drying process was performed.
* **Moisture Level** (`moisture_level`, number): Enter the moisture level at the start and end of the drying process.
* **Drying Time** (`drying_time`, number): Enter the total time taken for the drying process.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note): Enter any relevant notes or observations about the drying process.
* **Observations** (`observations`, note): Enter any issues or concerns that arose during the drying process.
* **Production Technitian** (`production_technitian`, text): Enter the name of the production technitian who performed the drying process.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure accurate tracking and reporting of the drying process.
* Review and revise your entries as necessary to reflect any changes in the drying process.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please contact your supervisor or production management.
