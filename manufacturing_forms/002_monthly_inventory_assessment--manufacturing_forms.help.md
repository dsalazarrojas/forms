# Monthly Inventory Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Monthly Inventory Assessment" form is a tool for manufacturers to track and assess their stock counts and issues on a monthly basis. It helps them to monitor their inventory levels, identify any discrepancies or issues, and maintain accurate records.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your company's current stock counts and any recent issues that have arisen.
2. Enter your company's location where you are currently stocking your inventory.
3. Record any stock issues that have occurred, including dates and notes.
4. Provide your email address and phone number in case of any further contact or communication.
5. Enter the stock value and issue stock count for each item.
6. Record the issue stock location and stock value for each item.
7. Provide any additional notes or comments regarding the stock issues.
8. Review and submit the form for review and analysis.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter your company's location where you are currently stocking your inventory. This will help us understand where you are stocking your items.
* **Stock Count** (`stock_count`, number, required: false): Record the current stock count for your inventory. This includes the quantity of items you have in stock.
* **Stock Issue** (`stock_issue`, text, required: false): Enter any recent stock issues that have occurred, including notes on what happened.
* **Stock Location** (`stock_location`, text, required: false): Record where the stock issue occurred, such as a specific location within your warehouse.
* **Stock Value** (`stock_value`, number, required: false): Record the value of the stock that was affected by the issue.
* **Stock Date** (`stock_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the stock issue occurred.
* **Stock Note** (`stock_note`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments regarding the stock issue.
* **Stock Email** (`stock_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address in case we need to contact you for further information.
* **Stock Phone** (`stock_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you for further information.
* **Stock Issue Location** (`stock_issue_location`, text, required: false): Record the location where the stock issue occurred.
* **Stock Issue Date** (`stock_issue_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the stock issue occurred.
* **Stock Issue Note** (`stock_issue_note`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments regarding the stock issue.
* **Stock Issue Phone** (`stock_issue_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you for further information.
* **Stock Issue Email** (`stock_issue_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address in case we need to contact you for further information.
* **Stock Issue Stock Count** (`stock_issue_stock_count`, number, required: false): Enter the stock count affected by the issue.
* **Stock Issue Stock Location** (`stock_issue_stock_location`, text, required: false): Record the location where the stock issue occurred.
* **Stock Issue Stock Value** (`stock_issue_stock_value`, number, required: false): Enter the value of the stock affected by the issue.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate tracking and assessment.
* Use clear and concise notes to describe stock issues.
* Review and submit the form regularly to stay on top of your inventory management.
