# Factory Workforce Wellness Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Workforce Wellness Contact Form is a tool for factory employees to report their wellbeing-related issues or concerns to HR staff. This form helps HR to understand the nature of the problems and provide support and assistance where possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select your department from the "Department" dropdown menu.
5. Choose the type(s) of issue you are experiencing from the "Issue Type" dropdown menu.
6. Describe the issue in the "Description" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Select the date you are reporting the issue in the "Date" field.
9. Select the time you are reporting the issue in the "Time" field.
10. Click the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`text`, **Required**: no): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`text`, **Required**: no): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, **Required**: no): Enter your email address.
* **Department** (`select_one`, **Required**: no): Select your department from the list (HR, Operations, Engineering).
* **Issue Type** (`select_multiple`, **Required**: no): Select the type(s) of issue you are experiencing from the list (Health, Safety, Wellness).
* **Description** (`text`, **Required**: no): Describe the issue you are experiencing.
* **Phone** (`text`, **Required**: no): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, **Required**: no): Select the date you are reporting the issue.
* **Time** (`time`, **Required**: no): Select the time you are reporting the issue.
* **Submit** (`note`, **Required**: no): Click the "Submit" button to send the form.
