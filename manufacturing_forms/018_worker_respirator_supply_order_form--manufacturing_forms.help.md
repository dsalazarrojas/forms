# Worker Respiator Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the order details for respiators. It is intended for internal use within the organization to track worker respiator supply orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Enter your Email address, which will be used for contact purposes.
3. Provide your Phone number for efficient communication.
4. Indicate if you have a Respiator Type, and if so, select "Yes" to proceed.
5. If applicable, specify the number of respiators you need ("Quantity").
6. Enter the Date and Time you would like to pick up your respiators.
7. Provide any additional Notes if required.
8. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
  - This field is used to identify you within the system.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
  - This field is used to identify you within the system.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
  - This field is used for contact purposes.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
  - This field is used for efficient communication.
- **Respiator Type** (`respirator_type`, select_one, required/optional): Check "Yes" if you have a respiator type.
  - This field is used to determine your respiator type.
- **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required/optional): If you selected "Yes" for Respiator Type, enter the number of respiators you need.
  - This field is used to track the quantity of respiators ordered.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to pick up your respiators.
  - This field is used to schedule the pickup date.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you would like to pick up your respiators.
  - This field is used to schedule the pickup time.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional information about your order if required.
  - This field is used to store any additional notes about your order.
- **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): Click this button to complete your order.
  - This button submits your order for processing.
