# Industrial Workflow Architect Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for Industrial Workflow Architects to contact us with various requests, questions, or feedback. It helps us understand their needs and provide the most suitable support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form completely and accurately, including all required fields (marked with `*`).
2. Enter your first name, last name, and email address to ensure we can contact you efficiently.
3. If you're reaching out from a company, provide your company name and website for context.
4. Describe your workflow inquiry, support request, or feedback in the field provided.
5. Choose how you would like us to contact you (email, phone, or both).
6. If necessary, add any additional comments or notes to support your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, `*`): Enter your first name for easier identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, `*`): Enter your last name for a complete name reference.
* **Email** (`email`, email, `*`): Enter your work or personal email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, `*`): Enter your phone number for direct contact.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your company name for context.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your company website for reference.
* **Workflow** (`workflow`, text, optional): Describe your current workflow or the specific workflow you're inquiring about.
* **Process** (`process`, text, optional): Explain the specific process related to your inquiry.
* **Support Team** (`support_team`, text, optional): Identify the support team you're working with.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_multiple, `*`): Choose how you'd like us to contact you (email, phone, or both).
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Add a detailed description of your inquiry, request, or feedback.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes to support your inquiry.
