# material_trace_compliance_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The material trace compliance registration form is used to collect information from suppliers and manufacturers for material trace compliance purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

This form has three sections:

1. **Supplier Details**:
	* Please enter your company name and address.
	* Provide your contact person's name and title.
	* Enter your supplier ID or registration number.
2. **Material Trace Data**:
	* Enter the material name or description.
	* Provide the material type (e.g., chemical, alloy, etc.).
	* Enter the source country or region.
3. **Compliance Documents**:
	* Upload or attach any relevant compliance documents (e.g., certifications, permits, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Details** (`supplier_details`):
	+ **Company Name**: Enter your company name as it appears on your business registration.
	+ **Address**: Enter your business address where the material is sourced or processed.
	+ **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the material sourcing or processing.
	+ **Title**: Enter the title of the person (e.g., Purchasing Manager, Production Manager).
	+ **Supplier ID or Registration Number**: Enter your supplier ID or registration number (if applicable).
* **Material Trace Data** (`material_trace_data`):
	+ **Material Name or Description**: Enter the name or description of the material being sourced or processed.
	+ **Material Type**: Enter the type of material (e.g., chemical, alloy, etc.).
	+ **Source Country or Region**: Enter the country or region where the material is sourced or processed.
* **Compliance Documents** (`compliance_documents`):
	+ **Compliance Documents**: Upload or attach any relevant compliance documents (e.g., certifications, permits, etc.).

## Tips

* Ensure accuracy and completeness in filling out this form to avoid any errors or discrepancies.
* Attach any supporting documents in a clear and legible format.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact [insert contact information] for assistance.
