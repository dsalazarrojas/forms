# Fencing Permit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for a fencing permit application. This information is used to facilitate the processing and approval of the permit application.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your name and business name (if applicable) in the first two fields.
2. Select the type of permit you are applying for from the list in the "Permit Type" field.
3. Enter the address of the property where the permit will be applied for.
4. If you have any additional information to provide about your permit application, you can enter it in the "Additional Info" field.
5. Enter your phone number and email address for contact purposes.
6. Sign your name and date in the "Signature" field, indicating your agreement to the permit terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (User First Name, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (User Last Name, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Business Name** (Business Name, text, required/optional): Enter your business name (if applicable).
* **Permit Type** (Permit Type, select one, required): Select the type of permit you are applying for.
* **Permit Address** (Permit Address, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the property where the permit will be applied for.
* **Phone** (User Phone, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (User Email, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Additional Info** (Permit Type Additional Info, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information you feel is relevant to your permit application.
* **Signature** (Signature, note, required/optional): Sign your name and date, indicating your agreement to the permit terms and conditions.
