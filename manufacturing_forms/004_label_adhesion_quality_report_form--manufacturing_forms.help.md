# label_adhesion_quality_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and report on the quality of labels used in a manufacturing process. It helps evaluate the adhesion of labels, which is a critical aspect of product quality and efficiency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Review the product and its label to determine the label's adhesion quality.
2. Choose the correct options for "label_type" based on the observed adhesion (Yes/No).
3. Enter the label width in inches or centimeters.
4. Select the unit of measurement for the label width (inches/centimeters).
5. Provide an adhesion score for the label (poor/good/excellent).
6. Enter any comments or observations about the label's adhesion.
7. If applicable, upload an image of the label to provide visual evidence of its adhesion.
8. If necessary, enter a URL to a reference image or external evidence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **product_id** (Number): Enter the ID number of the product being evaluated.
* **label_type** (Select One): Choose "Yes" if the label's adhesion is satisfactory, and "No" if it's not.
* **label_width** (Number): Measure and enter the label's width in inches or centimeters.
* **label_width_units** (Select One): Select the unit of measurement for the label width (inches/centimeters).
* **adhesion_score** (Number): Enter an adhesion score for the label (poor, good, or excellent).
* **comments** (Text): Provide any comments or observations about the label's adhesion.
* **label_adhesion_score** (Select One): Choose the overall adhesion score for the label (poor, good, or excellent).
* **label_adhesion_image** (Text): Upload an image of the label to provide visual evidence of its adhesion.
* **label_adhesion_image_url** (Text): Enter a URL to a reference image or external evidence.
* **label_adhesion_comment** (Text): Enter any comments or observations about the label's adhesion.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when entering values for label width and adhesion score.
* If you're unsure about the label's adhesion, choose "No" for "label_type" and provide a clear explanation in the comments field.
* Use the image upload feature to provide clear visual evidence of the label's adhesion.
* Ensure that the form is filled out accurately and consistently to maintain data integrity.
