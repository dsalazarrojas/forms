# Supply Chain Operations Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supply Chain Operations Assessment is a form designed to evaluate the performance and state of a company's supply chain operations. This form helps to identify areas of strength and weakness, providing valuable insights for improvement and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the overall performance of your supply chain operations and select the answer that best describes your current state (Yes, No, or Other).
2. For each of the subsequent fields, select the appropriate option or enter your response as per the field type (text, email, etc.).
3. Provide any additional comments or explanations for your responses, if necessary.
4. Review your submission status and overall comments fields to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall** (id: 2, type: number): This field asks for a numerical assessment of your overall performance, where 'Yes' indicates a satisfactory performance, and 'No' indicates a poor performance.
* **Quality** (id: 3, type: select_one): Choose 'Yes' for a strong quality performance, 'No' for a weak performance, or 'Other' for a performance that doesn't fit into the provided options.
* **Team** (id: 5, type: select_multiple): Indicate the level of support your team is providing, 'Yes' for satisfactory support, 'No' for inadequate support, or 'Other' for a performance that doesn't fit into the provided options.
* **Email** (id: 7, type: email): Enter the email address associated with this submission.
* **Submission** (id: 9, type: select_one): Indicate whether this submission is 'Yes' for complete, 'No' for incomplete, or 'Other' for a submission that doesn't fit into the provided options.
* **Submission Status** (id: 12, type: select_one): Choose the status of your submission, 'Active' for active, 'Inactive' for inactive, or 'Other' for a status that doesn't fit into the provided options.

Note: Fields with type 'select_multiple' (Main Page, Team) and 'select_one' (Quality, Submission, Submission Status) require selecting the most appropriate option from the provided choices. Fields with type 'text' (Comments, Overall Comments, Additional Comments) require entering a written response. Fields with type 'email' (Email) require entering a valid email address.
