# Food Production Process Flow Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record the steps involved in the food production process, including production process, date, and other relevant details. It helps in standardizing the production process and ensuring that all necessary steps are taken into account.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the production process details in the "Production Process" field.
2. Provide the production date and time in the "Production Date" and "Production Time" fields, respectively.
3. Enter the product name and description in the "Product Name" and "Product Description" fields.
4. Specify the production location, production site, production line, and production equipment used in the "Production Location", "Production Site", "Production Line", and "Production Equipment" fields, respectively.
5. Identify the production supervisor and production flow in the "Production Supervisor" and "Production Flow" fields.
6. Enumerate the production steps taken in the "Production Steps" field.
7. Update the production status and comments in the "Production Status" and "Production Comments" fields, respectively.
8. Review and finalize the production details in the "Production Review" field.
9. Record the production approval in the "Production Approval" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Production Process** (`production_process`, `text`, required/optional): A brief description of the production process used.
* **Production Date** (`production_date`, `date`, required/optional): The date on which the production took place.
* **Production Time** (`production_time`, `time`, required/optional): The time of production.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, required/optional): The name of the product being produced.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, `text`, required/optional): A detailed description of the product.
* **Production Location** (`production_location`, `text`, required/optional): The location where production took place.
* **Production Site** (`production_site`, `text`, required/optional): The site where production occurred.
* **Production Line** (`production_line`, `text`, required/optional): The production line used.
* **Production Equipment** (`production_equipment`, `text`, required/optional): The equipment used during production.
* **Production Supervisor** (`production_supervisor`, `text`, required/optional): The supervisor overseeing production.
* **Production Flow** (`production_flow`, `text`, required/optional): A description of the production flow.
* **Production Steps** (`production_steps`, `text`, required/optional): A list of production steps taken.
* **Production Status** (`production_status`, `text`, required/optional): The current production status.
* **Production Comments** (`production_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Any additional comments about the production process.
* **Production Review** (`production_review`, `text`, required/optional): A review of the production process.
* **Production Approval** (`production_approval`, `text`, required/optional): The approval status of the production process.
