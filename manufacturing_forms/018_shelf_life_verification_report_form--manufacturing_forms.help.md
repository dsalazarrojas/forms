# shelf_life_verification_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify the shelf life of a batch of products or materials. It captures and documents key information about the batch, including its current status, production dates, and inspection results.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the batch information in the "Batch Info" field.
2. Select the relevant shelf life dates from the "Shelf Life Dates" field.
3. Choose the inspection result from the "Inspection Result" field.
4. Provide any evidence or documentation attached to the batch in the "Evidence Attached" field.
5. Enter the date of inspection in the "Date Inspected" field.
6. Enter the batch number in the "Batch Number" field.
7. Enter the batch ID in the "Batch Id" field.
8. Enter the production date in the "Production Date" field.
9. Enter the manufacturing date in the "Manufacturing Date" field.
10. Select the shelf life verified status from the "Shelf Life Verified" field.
11. Choose the batch status from the "Batch Status" field.
12. Enter any additional notes about the batch in the "Notes" field.
13. Select the production status from the "Production Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Batch Info**: This field captures the key information about the batch, such as the batch number and ID.
* **Shelf Life Dates**: This field allows you to select the shelf life dates for the batch.
* **Inspection Result**: This field asks you to confirm the result of the inspection.
* **Evidence Attached**: If there are any documents or evidence attached to the batch, you can add them here.
* **Date Inspected**: Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Batch Number**: Enter the batch number.
* **Batch Id**: Enter the batch ID.
* **Production Date**: Enter the production date.
* **Manufacturing Date**: Enter the manufacturing date.
* **Shelf Life Verified**: Select whether the shelf life has been verified.
* **Batch Status**: Choose the batch status.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the batch.
* **Production Status**: Select the production status.

## Tips

* Ensure you have all necessary information about the batch before completing this form.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy.
* Save a copy of the form for future reference.
