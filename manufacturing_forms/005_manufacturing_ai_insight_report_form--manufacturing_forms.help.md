# Manufacturing AI Insight Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather insights and information about manufacturing projects. It will likely be used by project leads or project managers to report on the status, challenges, and outcomes of their projects.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter project details, including the project status and start and end dates.
2. Select the relevant AI model used for the project.
3. Report any significant impacts or challenges encountered during the project.
4. Enter project lead contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
5. Provide a brief description of the project's insights and outcomes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details** (`project_details`): Enter a brief description of the project, including the project status.
* **AI Model** (`ai_model`): Select the AI model used for the project. This will help us understand which model was used to achieve the project's goals.
* **Impacts** (`impacts`): Report any significant impacts or challenges encountered during the project. This will help us identify areas for improvement and best practices.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`): Enter any challenges that were faced during the project.
* **Project Lead** (`project_lead`): Enter the name of the project lead or manager.
* **Project Lead Email** (`project_lead_email`): Enter the email address of the project lead or manager.
* **Project Lead Phone** (`project_lead_phone`): Enter the phone number of the project lead or manager.
* **Project Lead Department** (`project_lead_department`): Enter the department of the project lead or manager.
* **Project Lead Title** (`project_lead_title`): Enter the title of the project lead or manager.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`): Select the current status of the project. This will help us track project progress.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`): Enter the date when the project started.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`): Enter the date when the project ended.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`): Enter the project budget.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`): Enter the location where the project was conducted.
* **Insights** (`insights`): Enter a brief description of the project's insights and outcomes. This will help us understand what was achieved and what worked well.
