# softgel_capsule_quality_inspection_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for quality inspection of softgel capsules during production. It captures important details such as capsule color, shape, size, and packaging. This data helps ensure that the production process meets quality standards and identifies any defects or issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Batch ID of the capsule production batch being inspected.
2. Select the capsule color from the options provided.
3. Choose the capsule shape from the list of options.
4. Select the inspection result for the capsule (Pass, Fail, or Reject).
5. Enter the Batch Number, which is a unique identifier for the batch.
6. Choose the Production Shift during which the capsule was produced.
7. Choose the Inspection Shift during which the inspection was performed.
8. Select the Production Line where the capsule was produced.
9. Enter the Production Date of the capsule.
10. Enter the Production Time of the capsule.
11. Enter the Shift Time of the inspection.
12. Enter the Start Time of the production shift.
13. Enter the End Time of the production shift.
14. Enter the Inspection Time of the inspection.
15. Enter the Production Duration of the capsule production process.
16. Select whether Quality Control was performed on the capsule (True or False).
17. Choose the Production Status of the capsule (Active or Inactive).
18. Enter the Capsule Weight.
19. Enter the Capsule Size.
20. Enter the Capsule Diameter.
21. Select the Capsule Color Consistency (Uniform or Inconsistent).
22. Select the Capsule Shape Consistency (Uniform or Inconsistent).
23. Select the Capsule Texture (Smooth or Rough).
24. Select the Capsule Packaging (Proper or Improper).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Batch ID** (`Batch ID`, `number`, required: false): Enter a unique identifier for the batch of capsules being inspected.
* **Capsule Color** (`capsule_color`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the color of the capsule from the options provided (Black, Brown, Green, Red).
* **Capsule Shape** (`capsule_shape`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the shape of the capsule from the options provided (Sphere, Oval, Elliptical).
* **Inspection Result** (`inspection_result`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the result of the inspection (Pass, Fail, Reject).
* **Batch Number** (`batch_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter a unique identifier for the batch.
* **Production Shift** (`production_shift`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the shift during which the capsule was produced (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Inspection Shift** (`inspection_shift`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the shift during which the inspection was performed (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Production Line** (`production_line`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the line where the capsule was produced (Line 1, Line 2, Line 3).
* **Production Date** (`production_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the capsule was produced.
* **Production Time** (`production_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time the capsule was produced.
* **Shift Time** (`shift_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Start Time** (`production_shift_start_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the start time of the production shift.
* **End Time** (`production_shift_end_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the end time of the production shift.
* **Inspection Time** (`inspection_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Production Duration** (`production_duration`, `number`, required: false): Enter the duration of the production process.
* **Inspection Result Time** (`inspection_result_time`, `time`, required: false): Not used in this form.
* **Quality Control** (`quality_control`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether Quality Control was performed (True or False).
* **Production Status** (`production_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the status of the capsule production (Active or Inactive).
* **Capsule Weight** (`capsule_weight`, `number`, required: false): Enter the weight of the capsule.
* **Capsule Size** (`capsule_size`, `number`, required: false): Enter the size of the capsule.
* **Capsule Diameter** (`capsule_diameter`, `number`, required: false): Enter the diameter of the capsule.
* **Capsule Color Consistency** (`capsule_color_consistency`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the color consistency of the capsule (Uniform or Inconsistent).
* **Capsule Shape Consistency** (`capsule_shape_consistency`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the shape consistency of the capsule (Uniform or Inconsistent).
* **Capsule Texture** (`capsule_texture`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the texture of the capsule (Smooth or Rough).
* **Capsule Packaging** (`capsule_packaging`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the packaging of the capsule (Proper or Improper).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely for each capsule being inspected.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the relevant production documentation or contact the production team for assistance.
* This form is designed to ensure consistency and quality in the production process.
