# Pharmaceutical Production Line Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pharmaceutical Production Line Inspection Form is designed to collect information about the inspection of pharmaceutical production lines. The form is used to identify any issues or discrepancies found during the inspection and document corrective actions taken. This form aims to ensure the production line's equipment, cleanliness, and overall quality meet the necessary standards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete the form with your name and role.
2. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the relevant questions regarding equipment condition and cleanliness.
3. Describe any production area walk-throughs or observations in the "Area Inspection Findings" field.
4. Document any corrective actions taken.
5. Complete the "Audit Date", "Audit Time", and "Auditor" fields.
6. Fill in the "Audit Comments" if necessary.
7. Complete the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Manufacturing Area** (`manufacturing_area`, text, not required): Enter the name of the production line being inspected.
* **Inspector** (`inspector`, select_one, not required): Answer "Yes" if you are the inspector, otherwise answer "No".
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the audit in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Production Line** (`production_line`, select_one, not required): Answer "Yes" if the production line is being inspected.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of the audit in the format "HH:MM:SS".
* **Equipment Condition** (`equipment_condition`, select_multiple, not required): Answer "Yes" for each piece of equipment that is in good condition.
* **Equipment Cleanliness** (`equipment_cleanliness`, select_multiple, not required): Answer "Yes" for each piece of equipment that is clean.
* **Production Area Walkthrough** (`production_area_walkthrough`, text, not required): Describe any walk-throughs or observations.
* **Area Inspection Findings** (`area_inspection_findings`, text, required): Describe any findings or issues found during the walk-through.
* **Corrective Actions Taken** (`corrective_actions_taken`, select_multiple, required): Answer "Yes" for each corrective action taken.
* **Follow-up Actions** (`follow-up_actions`, select_multiple, not required): Answer "Yes" for each follow-up action taken.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, not required): Enter any additional comments or information.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Production Supervisor Name** (`production_supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the production supervisor's name.
* **Production Supervisor Signature** (`production_supervisor_signature`, text, required): Enter the production supervisor's signature.
* **Production Supervisor Title** (`production_supervisor_title`, text, required): Enter the production supervisor's title.
* **Quality Supervisor Name** (`quality_supervisor_name`, text, not required): Enter the quality supervisor's name.
* **Quality Supervisor Signature** (`quality_supervisor_signature`, text, not required): Enter the quality supervisor's signature.
* **Quality Supervisor Title** (`quality_supervisor_title`, text, not required): Enter the quality supervisor's title.
* **Audit Date** (`audit_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the audit in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Audit Time** (`audit_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the audit in the format "HH:MM:SS".
* **Auditor** (`auditor`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Audit Comments** (`audit_comments`, text, not required): Enter any comments or observations.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): Enter the assigned tool.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are completed.
- Review the form carefully before submission.
- Ensure that all dates and times are in the correct formats.
- If you are unsure about any field or section, do not hesitate to ask for help.
