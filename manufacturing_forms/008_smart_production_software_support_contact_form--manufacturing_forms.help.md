# Smart Production Software Support Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Production Software Support Contact Form is a support channel for users to report issues, provide feedback, or seek assistance with their smart production software. This form helps the support team gather necessary information to troubleshoot and resolve the issue efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Form Details**: Please provide a brief description of the issue or your inquiry. This field helps us understand the context of your concern.
2.  **Contact Method**: Select whether you would like to be contacted via phone, email, or not contacted at all.
3.  **Email**: Enter your email address to receive further instructions or follow-up communication. This is an optional field, but it's recommended for efficient communication.
4.  **Phone Number**: If you selected to be contacted via phone, provide your phone number. Please format it as follows: Country Code - Area Code - Line Number (e.g., +1 123 456 7890).
5.  **Issue Description**: Describe the issue or problem you are experiencing in as much detail as possible. Be sure to include screenshots or relevant information that will assist the support team.
6.  **Software Version**: If you're experiencing an issue with a specific version of our software, please specify the version number for our reference.
7.  **Operating System**: If you're using the software on a specific operating system, please let us know so we can tailor our support accordingly.
8.  **Screenshot** (Attachment): If you're experiencing a visual issue, please attach a screenshot that illustrates the problem. This will help the support team better understand your concern.
9.  **Attachment**: You can attach any relevant files or documents related to the issue. Please ensure they are in a format that our support team can easily review (e.g., PDF, JPEG, or PNG).
10. **Submit**: Once you've filled out the form, submit it for our support team to review and assist you.
