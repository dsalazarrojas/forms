# Manufacturing Process Efficiency Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your manufacturing process, its efficiency, and potential areas for improvement. It helps identify bottlenecks and suggests next steps for enhancement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the type of production process you primarily use (Continuous, Batch, or Other).
2. Enter the time it takes to complete your production process.
3. Provide your production capacity.
4. Indicate your production volume per day.
5. Identify your main bottlenecks (Quality control, Production time, Equipment reliability, or Other).
6. (Optional) Suggest key process improvements.
7. Rate your process efficiency on a scale of 1-5.
7. Suggest suggestions for improving the process.
8. Outline your next steps for implementing improvements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your primary production process?** (`production_process`, `select_one`, required): Please select the type of production process your team primarily uses (Continuous, Batch, or Other).
* **How much time does it take to complete this process?** (`production_time`, `number`, required): Enter the time it takes to finish your production process.
* **What is your production capacity?** (`production_capacity`, `number`, required): Enter your production capacity.
* **What is your production volume per day?** (`production_volume`, `number`, required): Enter your daily production volume.
* **What are your main bottlenecks?** (`bottleneck`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate your main bottlenecks (Quality control, Production time, Equipment reliability, or Other).
* **What are some key process improvements that we could make?** (`process_efficiency`, `text`, optional): Suggest key process improvements if you have any.
* **On a scale of 1-5, how would you rate your process efficiency?** (`process_efficiency_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate your process efficiency on a scale of 1-5.
* **What suggestions do you have for improving the process?** (`suggestions`, `text`, optional): Suggest any ideas you have for improving the process.
* **What are your next steps for implementing improvements?** (`next_steps`, `text`, optional): Outline your next steps for implementing improvements.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough when answering these questions.
- Provide as much detail as possible.
- Contact relevant personnel or departments if you're unsure about any question or need further clarifications.
