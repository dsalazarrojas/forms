# Factory Calibration Toolkit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Calibration Toolkit Order Form is designed to collect information for ordering factory calibration tools. This form will help you provide accurate and complete information for your order.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Order Details** field with your selected option.
2. Select the **tool selection** fields for the tools you need, making sure to choose at least one option.
3. Enter the **delivery information** in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Fill in the **Contact Information** field with your name and contact details.
5. Enter your **email address** in the format [email@example.com](mailto:email@example.com).
6. If necessary, add any **Notes** you may have.
7. Assign the order to a **User** by entering their name.
8. Select the **Tool** you are ordering.
9. Choose the **Tool Type** you need.
10. Select the **Tool Condition** (if applicable).
11. Choose the **Tool Status**.
12. Enter the name of the person who will **Assign** the tool.
13. Enter the **Tool Number**, **Tool Model**, and **Tool Serial Number**.
14. Choose the **Tool Warranty**.
15. If applicable, choose the **Tool Return** option.
16. Add any **Tool Comments** you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details**: Select the type of order you are placing (e.g., "Calibration", "Repair", "Other").
* **Tool Selection**: Choose the tools you need for your order.
* **Delivery Information**: Enter the date when you would like to receive your tools (format: MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Contact Information**: Enter your name and contact details.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address (e.g., [email@example.com](mailto:email@example.com)).
* **Notes**: Any additional comments or information about your order.
* **Assigned User**: Enter the name of the user who will be assigned to the order.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool being assigned to.
* **Assigned Tool Type**: Choose the type of tool being assigned.
* **Assigned Tool Condition**: Choose the condition of the tool being assigned (if applicable).
* **Assigned Tool Status**: Choose the status of the tool being assigned.
* **Assigned By**: Enter the name of the person assigning the tool.
* **Tool Number**: Enter the tool number (e.g., #1234).
* **Tool Model**: Enter the tool model (e.g., "ABC-123").
* **Tool Serial Number**: Enter the tool serial number (e.g., "ABC-123-456").
* **Tool Warranty**: Choose the tool warranty (e.g., "Standard", "Premium").
* **Tool Return**: Choose whether you want to return the tool (if applicable).
* **Tool Comments**: Add any additional comments about the tool.
