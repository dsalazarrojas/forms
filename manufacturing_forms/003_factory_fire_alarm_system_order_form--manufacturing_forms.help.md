# Factory Fire Alarm System Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information for the installation of a fire alarm system in a factory setting. It helps ensure that your order is processed accurately and promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Enter your factory site name.
2. Add your contact email address.
3. Enter your contact phone number.
4. (Optional) Provide your name for our records.
5. Select the type of equipment you need.
6. Enter the number of systems you need.
7. Enter your site address for installation purposes.
8. (Optional) Provide additional address information if needed.
9. Check if you require installation service.
10. Add any additional comments or instructions regarding your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Name** (`Site Name`, text, required): Enter the name of your factory site where the fire alarm system will be installed.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address so we can reach you regarding any updates or queries about your order.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Provide your contact phone number for easy communication.
* **Name** (`Name`, text, optional): Enter your name for our records, but it's not required.
* **Equipment Type** (`Equipment Type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of equipment you need, including Analog, Addressable, Hybrid, Wireless, or None.
* **Quantity** (`Quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of systems you need.
* **Site Address 1** (`Site Address 1`, text, required): Enter the address of your site for installation purposes.
* **Site Address 2** (`Site Address 2`, text, optional): Provide additional address information if needed.
* **Site Address 3** (`Site Address 3`, text, optional): Additional address information, if needed.
* **Installation Service** (`Installation Service`, select_one, optional): Check if you require installation service.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, note, optional): Use this field for any extra information or instructions regarding your order.

## Tips
- Double-check all required fields before submission.
- Use the correct equipment type and quantity to ensure accurate installation.
- Provide all necessary address information for installation purposes.
- Add any additional comments to ensure we can meet your specific needs.
- Review your submission carefully before submitting the form.
