# Factory Cleanliness Audit Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Cleanliness Audit Report is designed to evaluate the cleanliness of a factory and identify areas that require improvement. This report helps factory management make informed decisions about maintaining a clean and healthy work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the General Information section with the relevant details about the factory, such as its name and ID.
2.  Select the areas that you inspected during the audit in the Areas Checked section.
3.  Provide a brief description of your comments in the Comments section.
4.  Choose the cleanliness scale that best represents your rating in the Cleanliness Scale section.
5.  Enter the cleanliness rating score for the selected area in the Cleanliness Rating Score section.
6.  Enter the date of the inspection in the Date Inspected section.
7.  Enter the name of the inspector in the Inspector section.
8.  Enter the facility ID in the Facility ID section.
9.  Enter the equipment location in the Equipment Location section.
10.  Enter the equipment serial number in the Equipment Serial Number section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **General Information (General Information)** (`text`, required/optional): This section is for providing general information about the factory, such as its name and ID.
*   **Areas Checked (Areas Checked)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select all areas that were inspected during the audit.
*   **Comments (Comments)** (`note`, optional): Provide any comments or observations about the areas inspected.
*   **Cleanliness Scale (Cleanliness Scale)** (`select_one`, optional): Choose the cleanliness scale that best represents your rating.
*   **Cleanliness Rating Score (Cleanliness Rating Score)** (`number`, optional): Enter the cleanliness rating score for the selected area.
*   **Date Inspected (Date Inspected)** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of the inspection.
*   **Inspector (Inspector)** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the inspector.
*   **Facility ID (Facility ID)** (`text`, optional): Enter the facility ID.
*   **Equipment Location (Equipment Location)** (`text`, optional): Enter the equipment location.
*   **Equipment Serial Number (Equipment Serial Number)** (`text`, optional): Enter the equipment serial number.

## Tips
- Be as accurate and detailed as possible when answering the questions.
- Ensure that you have inspected all areas of the factory before submitting the form.
- Use the cleanliness scale to rate the areas inspected honestly and fairly.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact the factory management or the person in charge of maintenance.
