# Inventory Transfer Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Inventory Transfer Form is used to document the transfer of inventory between members of the team. It helps in tracking the movement of items within the inventory, enabling accurate reporting and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information:
	* Make sure to select a valid "From Member" and "To Member".
	* Enter the quantity of items being transferred.
	* Choose the transfer date and time.
	* Optionally, add any notes about the transfer.
2. Review the form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Transfer Inventory** (Page 1, Label: Transfer Inventory): A brief description of the transfer.
* **From Member** (Page 1, Label: From Member, Required): Select the member who is transferring the inventory.
* **To Member** (Page 1, Label: To Member, Required): Select the member who is receiving the inventory.
* **Quantity** (Page 1, Label: Quantity, Required): Enter the number of items being transferred.
* **Notes** (Page 1, Label: Notes, Optional): Add any additional comments or details about the transfer.
* **Transfer Date** (Page 2, Label: Transfer Date, Required): Enter the date when the transfer took place.
* **Transfer Time** (Page 2, Label: Transfer Time, Required): Enter the time when the transfer took place.
* **Transferred By** (Page 2, Label: Transferred By, Optional): If the transfer was performed by someone else, enter their name.
* **Transferor** (Page 2, Label: Transferor, Optional): If applicable, enter the name of the person performing the transfer.
* **Reviewer** (Page 2, Label: Reviewer, Optional): If you want to assign a reviewer for this transfer, enter their name.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate tracking.
* Use the "Notes" field to add any additional context or details about the transfer.
* If you are unsure about any information, consult with the relevant team member or supervisor.
