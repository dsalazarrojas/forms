# Production Site Size Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a production site's size and capacity. The data collected will help assess the site's production capacity, expansion planning status, and other relevant metrics. Fill out this form to provide accurate and detailed information about your production site.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the Facility Type of your production site from the options provided.
2. Choose the Location of your production site from the options provided.
3. If applicable, enter the Floor Area of your production site in square feet.
4. Enter the Length and Width of your production site in feet.
5. Enter the Height of your production site in feet.
6. Enter the Production Capacity of your production site in the units you've selected (Tons, Pieces, or Units).
7. Select the Production Capacity Unit from the options provided.
8. Enter the Site Area of your production site in the units you've selected (Square Feet or Square Meters).
9. Select the Site Area Unit from the options provided.
10. Enter the Building Area of your production site in the units you've selected (Square Feet or Square Meters).
11. Select the Building Area Unit from the options provided.
12. Enter the Production Area of your production site in the units you've selected (Square Feet or Square Meters).
13. Select the Production Area Unit from the options provided.
14. Enter the Production Capacity Factor of your production site as a percentage.
15. Select the Production Capacity Factor Unit from the options provided.
16. Enter the Max Production Capacity of your production site in the units you've selected (Units, Tons, or Pieces).
17. Enter the Max Production Capacity Factor of your production site as a percentage.
18. Enter the Site Assessment Date of your production site.
19. Enter the Site Assessment Time of your production site.
20. Enter the Production Capacity 2 of your production site in the units you've selected (Units, Tons, or Pieces).
21. Select the Production Capacity Unit 2 from the options provided.
22. Enter the Production Capacity Factor 2 of your production site as a percentage.
23. Enter the Production Area 2 of your production site in the units you've selected (Square Feet or Square Meters).
24. Select the Expansion Planning Status of your production site from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Type** (`facility_type`, select_one): Choose the type of facility you are working with (Small, Medium, or Large).
* **Location** (`location`, select_multiple): Select the location of your production site (Chicago, New York, or Los Angeles).
* **Floor Area** (`floor_area`, number): Enter the floor area of your production site in square feet if applicable.
* **Length** (`length`, number): Enter the length of your production site in feet.
* **Width** (`width`, number): Enter the width of your production site in feet.
* **Height** (`height`, number): Enter the height of your production site in feet.
* **Production Capacity** (`production_capacity`, number): Enter the production capacity of your production site in the selected units (Tons, Pieces, or Units).
* **Production Capacity Unit** (`production_capacity_unit`, select_one): Select the unit of production capacity (Tons, Pieces, or Units).
* **Site Area** (`site_area`, number): Enter the site area of your production site in the selected units (Square Feet or Square Meters).
* **Site Area Unit** (`site_area_unit`, select_one): Select the unit of site area (Square Feet or Square Meters).
* **Building Area** (`building_area`, number): Enter the building area of your production site in the selected units (Square Feet or Square Meters).
* **Building Area Unit** (`building_area_unit`, select_one): Select the unit of building area (Square Feet or Square Meters).
* **Production Area** (`production_area`, number): Enter the production area of your production site in the selected units (Square Feet or Square Meters).
* **Production Area Unit** (`production_area_unit`, select_one): Select the unit of production area (Square Feet or Square Meters).
* **Production Capacity Factor** (`production_capacity_factor`, number): Enter the production capacity factor of your production site as a percentage.
* **Production Capacity Factor Unit** (`production_capacity_factor_unit`, select_one): Select the unit of production capacity factor (Percentage or Units).
* **Max Production Capacity** (`max_production_capacity`, number): Enter the max production capacity of your production site in the selected units (Units, Tons, or Pieces).
* **Max Production Capacity Factor** (`production_capacity_factor_max`, number): Enter the max production capacity factor of your production site as a percentage.
* **Site Assessment Date** (`site_assessment_date`, date): Enter the site assessment date of your production site.
* **Site Assessment Time** (`site_assessment_time`, time): Enter the site assessment time of your production site.
* **Production Capacity 2** (`production_capacity_2`, number): Enter the production capacity 2 of your production site in the selected units (Units, Tons, or Pieces).
* **Production Capacity Unit 2** (`production_capacity_unit_2`, select_one): Select the unit of production capacity 2 (Units, Tons, or Pieces).
* **Production Capacity Factor 2** (`production_capacity_factor_2`, number): Enter the production capacity factor 2 of your production site as a percentage.
* **Production Area 2** (`production_area_2`, number): Enter the production area 2 of your production site in the selected units (Square Feet or Square Meters).
* **Expansion Planning Status** (`expansion_planning_status`, select_multiple): Select the expansion planning status of your production site (Planning, Approved, In Progress, or Completed).
