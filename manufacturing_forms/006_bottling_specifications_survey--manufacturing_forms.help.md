The form titled "Bottling Specifications Survey" is designed to capture critical information from respondents, including their company details and specific requirements for bottling. The purpose of this form is to collect standardized data from respondents to facilitate accurate and efficient production processes in the manufacturing industry.

## Purpose

This form aims to gather essential specifications from respondents, enabling the production team to tailor their bottling processes accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

- Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
- Make sure to select the correct bottle type and cap type from the provided options.
- Enter the contact email of the primary technical contact person.
- Ensure the volume milliliters and minimum order quantity fields are populated with numerical values.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Respondent Company (Respondent company, text, required)**
Respondent company is the name of the company submitting the specifications.

### 2. **Contact Person (Contact person, text, required)**
The primary technical contact person for the company submitting the specifications.

### 3. **Contact Email (Contact email, email, required)**
Enter the email address of the primary technical contact person for any further questions or clarifications.

### 4. **Bottle Type (Bottle type, select_one, required)**
Select the preferred bottle type: Glass, PET plastic, HDPE, or Aluminum.

### 5. **Volume Milliliters (Volume milliliters, number, required)**
Enter the standard fill volume for the production run.

### 6. **Cap Type (Cap type, select_one, required)**
Select the closure type: Screw cap, Flip top, Cork, or Child resistant.

### 7. **Labeling Requirements (Labeling requirements, text, optional)**
Enter any specific size materials and placement notes for labeling.

### 8. **Minimum Order Quantity (Minimum order quantity, number, optional)**
Enter the minimum units required for the production run.

## Tips

- Double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before submission.
- If unsure about any field, refer to your production team or the provided guidelines.
- Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
