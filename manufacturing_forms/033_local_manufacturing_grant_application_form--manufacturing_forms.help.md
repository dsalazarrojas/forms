# Local Manufacturing Grant Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Local Manufacturing Grant Application Form is designed to help local manufacturers apply for grants that support their business growth and development. The form collects information about your project, including its budget, contact details, and manufacturing information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Application Details**: Start by providing your basic contact information and project summary in the Application Details section.
2. **Project Contact Information**: Enter the contact details of the person or company responsible for the project.
3. **Budget**: Enter the budget for your project.
4. **Documents**: Upload any additional documents related to your project.
5. **Manufacturing Information**: Choose the type of grant you are applying for (manufacturing or non-manufacturing) and select the type of manufacturing (local or regional).
6. **Location**: Select the location where your project will be manufactured.
7. **Sector**: Choose the sector of your industry (aerospace, automotive, or consumer electronics).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Details (applicant_details)**: Enter your name, title, and contact information (phone number and email).
* **Project Summary (project_summary)**: Briefly describe your project, including its objectives and goals.
* **Budget (budget)**: Enter the budget for your project.
* **Documents (documents)**: Upload any additional documents related to your project, such as project proposals, resumes, or other supporting materials.
* **Contact Information (applicant_contacts)**: Enter the name and contact information of the person or company responsible for the project.
* **Project Contact Information (project_contacts)**: Enter the name and contact information of the project's lead contact.
* **Phone Number (applicant_phone)**: Enter the phone number for the applicant.
* **Project Phone Number (project_phone)**: Enter the phone number for the project.
* **Email (applicant_email)**: Enter the email address for the applicant.
* **Project Email (project_email)**: Enter the email address for the project.
* **Project Budget (project_budget)**: Enter the budget for the project.
* **Project Documents (project_documents)**: Upload any additional documents related to the project.
* **Applicant Documents (applicant_document)**: Upload any additional documents related to the applicant.
* **Grant Type (manufacturing_grant)**: Choose whether your grant is for manufacturing or non-manufacturing.
* **Manufacturing Type (manufacturing_type)**: Choose the type of manufacturing (local or regional).
* **Manufacturing Location (manufacturing_location)**: Select the location where your project will be manufactured.
* **Manufacturing Sector (manufacturing_sector)**: Choose the sector of your industry (aerospace, automotive, or consumer electronics).

## Tips

* Ensure you have all required documents and information before submitting the form.
* Double-check your contact and project details for accuracy.
* Be concise and clear in your project description.
* Attach any supporting materials that demonstrate your project's feasibility and potential impact.
