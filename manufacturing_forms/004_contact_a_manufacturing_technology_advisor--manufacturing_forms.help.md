# Contact A Manufacturing Technology Advisor - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to help manufacturing technology advisors provide expert guidance and support to potential clients. It's designed to collect essential information about the client and their project needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Please enter your company name and select the facility type for your project.
2. Enter your name and contact email address.
3. Provide your phone number (optional) and a brief description of your technical challenge or opportunity.
4. Choose the estimated project duration in months.
5. Select the approximate project budget range.
6. Describe your desired outcome for this engagement.
7. Specify the best time for the advisor to contact you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter your company's full legal name.
* **Contact name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person making the request.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the best email address for follow-up communication.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your direct phone number (optional).
* **Facility type** (`facility_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the primary environment for your project from the options provided:
	+ Small batch workshop
	+ Automated production line
	+ Prototype lab
	+ Contract manufacturing
* **Technology need description** (`technology_need`, `text`, required): Describe the technical challenge or opportunity you're seeking to address.
* **Estimated project timeline in months** (`estimated_project_timeline`, `number`, optional): Enter the expected duration of the project in months.
* **Project budget range** (`project_budget`, `select_one`, optional): Select the approximate budget range for the advisory work from the options provided:
	+ Under 10k
	+ 10k to 50k
	+ 50k to 200k
	+ Over 200k
* **Desired outcome** (`desired_outcome`, `text`, required): Describe what success looks like for this engagement.
* **Best time for contact** (`best_time_for_contact`, `text`, optional): Specify the preferred days or times for follow-up communication.

## Tips
* Ensure you've provided accurate and clear information to help the advisor understand your project requirements.
* Be prepared to answer additional questions the advisor may have to better understand your project needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the manufacturing technology advisor directly.
