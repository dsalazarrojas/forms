# industrial_control_panel_assembly_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The `industrial_control_panel_assembly_order_form` is designed to gather information from customers or clients for the creation, assembly, or maintenance of industrial control panels. This form helps to ensure accurate order placement and delivery details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your order information, including the type of control panel you need.
2. Provide delivery details such as address, delivery date, and time.
3. Add any custom options or special requests you may have.
4. Double-check your contact information and email address for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Control Panel Order Form**: Enter the type of control panel you are ordering.
* **Delivery Details**: Enter the delivery address, delivery date, and time you prefer.
* **Control Panel Configuration**: Describe the specific configuration of the control panel you need.
* **Delivery Information**: Add any additional delivery instructions or notes.
* **Custom Options**: Enter any custom requirements or special requests.
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the expected delivery date for the control panel.
* **Delivery Time**: Enter the time when you would like the control panel delivered.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter your shipping address where the control panel will be delivered.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact information so we can reach you.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for further correspondence.
* **Order Notes**: Add any additional information about your order.

## Tips
* Double-check your form information for accuracy before submitting.
* Contact us if you have any questions or need further assistance.
* Please allow for adequate delivery time, as rush orders may incur extra fees.
* Ensure your contact information and email are up-to-date in case we need to contact you.
