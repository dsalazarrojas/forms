# Component Order Form For Manufacturers - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for companies to submit orders for components to manufacturers. It requires essential details such as the purchaser's name, contact email, and delivery address. Companies can also provide optional information like the unit price and special instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing the order details in the "Order Information" section.
2. Enter the Purchaser's name and contact email in the "Purchaser name" and "Contact email" fields, respectively.
3. Enter the component part number and quantity in the "Component part number" and "Quantity requested" fields.
4. If known, provide the unit price and preferred delivery date in the "Unit price" and "Requested delivery date" fields, respectively.
5. Enter the full shipping address in the "Delivery address" field.
6. If necessary, add any special instructions in the "Special instructions" field.
7. Review the form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Order Information --**: This section provides a note to explain the purpose of the form and its contents.
* **Purchaser name**: Enter the name of the company or person placing the order.
* **Contact email**: Enter the email address used for order confirmation.
* **Component part number**: Enter the manufacturer's part number for the ordered component.
* **Quantity requested**: Enter the whole number quantity of the ordered component.
* **Unit price**: If known, enter the price per unit of the ordered component.
* **Delivery address**: Enter the full shipping address for the order delivery.
* **Requested delivery date**: If desired, enter the preferred delivery date.
* **Special instructions**: Enter any special instructions for the order, such as packaging or handling notes.
