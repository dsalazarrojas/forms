# Dough Room Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dough Room Monitoring Form is designed to collect and record information about the current state of the dough room. This form will help in monitoring and maintaining the dough room's optimal conditions, including temperature, humidity, and pressure.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the questions and sections.
2. Answer each question in a logical and sequential manner.
3. Be accurate and thorough in your responses, as this information will help in monitoring and maintaining the dough room's optimal conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information
* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the current state of the dough room, including any notable issues or concerns.

### Room Conditions
* **Room Conditions** (`room_conditions`, text, optional): Describe the overall conditions of the dough room, including any issues or anomalies detected.

### Environmental Conditions
* **Temperature** (`temperature`, number, optional): Enter the current temperature reading in the dough room.
* **Humidity** (`humidity`, number, optional): Enter the current humidity reading in the dough room.
* **Pressure** (`pressure`, number, optional): Enter the current pressure reading in the dough room.

### Status and Limitations
* **Temperature Status** (`temperature_status`, select_one, optional): Select "Active" if the temperature is within the allowed limits, "Inactive" if it's not.
* **Humidity Status** (`humidity_status`, select_one, optional): Select "Active" if the humidity is within the allowed limits, "Inactive" if it's not.
* **Pressure Status** (`pressure_status`, select_one, optional): Select "Active" if the pressure is within the allowed limits, "Inactive" if it's not.
* **Temperature Limit** (`temperature_limit`, number, optional): Enter the allowed temperature limit for the dough room.
* **Humidity Limit** (`humidity_limit`, number, optional): Enter the allowed humidity limit for the dough room.
* **Pressure Limit** (`pressure_limit`, number, optional): Enter the allowed pressure limit for the dough room.

### Additional Information
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the current state of the dough room.
* **Notes 1** (`notes_1`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the dough room.
* **Notes 2** (`notes_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the dough room.
* **Notes 3** (`notes_3`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the dough room.
* **Notes 4** (`notes_4`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the dough room.

### Submission Information
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_multiple, optional): Select who is currently assigned to the dough room.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_multiple, optional): Select who submitted this form.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, select_multiple, optional): Select when this form was submitted.

Note: Some fields may not be required, but please ensure that all relevant information is provided to maintain an accurate and complete record of the dough room's state.
