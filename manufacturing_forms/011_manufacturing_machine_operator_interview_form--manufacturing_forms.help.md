# Manufacturing Machine Operator Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from manufacturing machine operators for the purpose of maintaining a database of candidate information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the candidate information section, including the candidate's name, contact phone number, and email address.
2. Select the candidate's work experience in years and months.
3. Choose the machine types the candidate has experience with.
4. Provide a brief description of the candidate's qualifications.
5. Select the candidate's availability for work.
6. Choose the location of the interview.
7. Select the date and time of the interview.
8. Provide any additional interview notes.
9. If necessary, provide reference checks and emergency contact information.
10. Finally, select the candidate's status and assigned tool.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name** (id: 1, text, required): Enter the name of the candidate.
* **Contact Phone** (id: 2, text, optional): Enter the candidate's contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (id: 3, email, optional): Enter the candidate's email address.
* **Work Experience** (id: 4, select_multiple, required): Select the number of years and months the candidate has work experience with.
* **Machine Types** (id: 5, select_one, required): Choose the machine types the candidate has experience with.
* **Qualifications** (id: 6, text, required): Provide a brief description of the candidate's qualifications.
* **Work Availability** (id: 7, date, optional): Enter the candidate's availability for work.
* **Work Availability End** (id: 8, date, optional): Enter the end date of the candidate's availability for work.
* **Interview Notes** (id: 9, note, optional): Provide any additional interview notes.
* **Interview Location** (id: 10, select_one, required): Choose the location of the interview.
* **Interview Date** (id: 11, date, required): Select the date of the interview.
* **Interview Time** (id: 12, time, required): Select the time of the interview.
* **Reference Check** (id: 13, text, optional): Provide any reference checks or additional information.
* **Candidate References** (id: 14, text, optional): Provide any additional reference information.
* **Emergency Contact** (id: 15, text, optional): Enter the contact information for the candidate's emergency contact.
* **Candidate Status** (id: 16, select_one, required): Choose the candidate's status (Active or Inactive).
* **Assigned Tool** (id: 17, select_one, required): Choose the assigned tool for the candidate.
* **Submission Date** (id: 18, date, optional): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Created By** (id: 19, text, required): Enter the user who created the form.
* **Updated By** (id: 20, text, optional): Enter the user who last updated the form.
* **Updated Date** (id: 21, date, optional): Enter the date the form was last updated.
* **Deleted By** (id: 22, text, optional): Enter the user who deleted the form.
* **Deleted Date** (id: 23, date, optional): Enter the date the form was deleted.
* **Deleted Reason** (id: 24, text, optional): Enter the reason the form was deleted.
* **Notes** (id: 25, note, optional): Enter any additional notes.
