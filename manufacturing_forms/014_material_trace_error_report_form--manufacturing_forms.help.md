# Material Trace Error Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Material Trace Error Report Form is used to document and report errors that occur during the material trace process. This form helps to identify the cause of the error, the actions taken to address it, and the status of the batch that was affected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the batch number of the affected batch.
2. Indicate whether the error occurred during production shift or not.
3. Provide a description of the error that occurred.
4. Enter the material ID, name, and type.
5. Enter the quantity and unit price of the material that was affected.
6. Choose the status of the batch (e.g., "Batch Status 1", "Batch Status 2", "Batch Status 3").
7. Enter the date and time of the batch status update.
8. Enter the name of the person who reported the error.
9. Choose the date and time of when the error was reported.
10. Enter any comments or notes about the error.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Batch Number**: Enter the batch number of the affected batch.
* **Production Shift**: Indicate whether the error occurred during production shift or not. This will help track when the error occurred.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the error occurred.
* **Error Description**: Provide a detailed description of the error that occurred.
* **Material ID**: Enter the ID of the material that was affected.
* **Material Name**: Enter the name of the material that was affected.
* **Material Type**: Enter the type of material that was affected.
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of material that was affected.
* **Unit Price**: Enter the unit price of the material that was affected.
* **Unit of Measurement**: Enter the unit of measurement of the material (e.g., kilograms, liters, etc.).
* **Error Date**: Enter the date when the error was detected.
* **Error Time**: Enter the time when the error was detected.
* **Cause of Error**: Enter the cause of the error.
* **Action Taken**: Enter the actions taken to address the error.
* **Action Taken By**: Enter the name of the person who took action to address the error.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the error.
* **Reported By**: Enter the name of the person who reported the error.
* **Reported Date**: Enter the date when the error was reported.
* **Reported Time**: Enter the time when the error was reported.
* **Batch Status**: Choose the status of the batch (e.g., "Batch Status 1", "Batch Status 2", "Batch Status 3").
* **Batch Status Date**: Enter the date when the batch status was updated.
* **Batch Status Time**: Enter the time when the batch status was updated.
* **Reported By Who**: Enter the name of the person who reported the error.
* **Reported Date**: Enter the date when the error was reported.
* **Reported Time**: Enter the time when the error was reported.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the error.

Note: The fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be filled out. The other fields are optional and can be filled out as needed.
