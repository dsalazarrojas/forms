# Validation Of Quantity And Quality Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and verify the quantity and quality of items received from suppliers or manufacturers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Item Details** (`item_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the item received.
* **Quantity Received** (`quantity_received`, number, optional): Record the actual amount of items received from the supplier or manufacturer.
* **Quantity Expected** (`quantity_expected`, number, optional): Enter the amount of items that was expected to be received.
* **Quantity Difference** (`quantity_diff`, number, optional): The difference between the quantity received and the quantity expected.
* **Quality Rating** (`quality_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the quality of the received item using the quality rating scale (4, 4.5, or 5).
* **Quality Rating Notes** (`quality_rating_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional quality rating notes.
* **Date Received** (`date_received`, date, optional): Record the date the item was received.
* **Time Received** (`time_received`, time, optional): Record the time the item was received.
* **Received By** (`received_by`, text, optional): Record the name of the person who received the item.
* **Supporting Evidence** (`supporting_evidence`, text, optional): Attach any supporting evidence, such as receipts or documents, that can be used to verify the quantity and quality of the received item.
* **Quantity Received Unit** (`quantity_received_unit`, text, optional): Record the unit of measurement for the quantity received.
* **Quantity Expected Unit** (`quantity_expected_unit`, text, optional): Record the unit of measurement for the quantity expected.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the item.
* **Category** (`category`, text, optional): Record the category of the item.
* **Item Type** (`item_type`, text, optional): Record the type of the item.
* **Subcategory** (`item_subcategory`, text, optional): Record the subcategory of the item.
* **Serial Number** (`serial_number`, text, optional): Record the serial number of the item.
* **Production Date** (`production_date`, date, optional): Record the production date of the item.
* **Production Time** (`production_time`, time, optional): Record the production time of the item.
* **Received Date** (`received_date`, date, optional): Record the date the item was received.
* **Received Time** (`received_time`, time, optional): Record the time the item was received.
* **Vendor** (`vendor`, text, optional): Record the name of the vendor who supplied the item.
* **Quantity Verified** (`quantity_verified`, select_multiple, optional): Check if the quantity received is verified.
* **Verified By** (`verified_by`, text, optional): Record the name of the person who verified the quantity.
* **Serial Number Verified** (`serial_number_verified`, text, optional): Check if the serial number is verified.
