# Supply Forecast Request Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supply Forecast Request Contact Form is a contact form for users to request supply forecasting. This form is designed to gather contact and preference information from users to help the internal team better respond to their supply forecasting needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your contact name on the "Contact Name" field.
2. Enter your contact email address on the "Email" field.
3. Enter your contact phone number on the "Phone" field.
4. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
5. Select the preferred manufacturing form from the "Manufacturing Form" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Name
* **Contact Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person requesting supply forecasting.

### Email
* **Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.

### Note
* **Note** (`contact_note`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments regarding your supply forecasting request.

### Manufacturing Form 1
* **Manufacturing Form 1** (`manufacturing_form_1`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the preferred manufacturing form.

### Manufacturing Form 2
* **Manufacturing Form 2** (`manufacturing_form_2`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the preferred manufacturing form 2.

### Manufacturing Form 3
* **Manufacturing Form 3** (`manufacturing_form_3`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the preferred manufacturing form 3.

### Manufacturing Form 4
* **Manufacturing Form 4** (`manufacturing_form_4`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the preferred manufacturing form 4.

## Tips
- Please be clear and concise when filling out this form to ensure the internal team can best assist with your supply forecasting needs.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the internal team after submitting the form.
