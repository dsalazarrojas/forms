# Ingredient Contaminant Alert Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ingredient Contaminant Alert Report Form is used to document and track any contaminants found in ingredients used during manufacturing processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Choose the correct contaminant type from the options provided.
3. Select all relevant locations where the contaminant was detected.
4. Attach any relevant photos of the contaminant.
5. Enter the name and contact information of the person who detected the contaminant.
6. Enter the date and time the contaminant was detected.
7. Provide any additional notes or comments about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **ingredient** (text)
	+ This field is where you will enter the name of the ingredient that was found to be contaminated.
* **contaminant_type** (select one)
	+ Choose the type of contaminant that was detected from the options provided.
* **severity** (select one)
	+ Select the severity level of the contaminant, such as minor, moderate, or severe.
* **location** (select multiple)
	+ Choose all locations where the contaminant was detected.
* **photos** (select multiple)
	+ Attach any photos of the contaminant as evidence.
* **assigned_to** (text)
	+ Enter the name and contact information of the person or team that will be handling the incident.
* **submitted_by** (text)
	+ Enter your name and contact information.
* **submitted_at** (date)
	+ Enter the date and time the contaminant was detected.
* **submitted_by_phone** (text)
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **note** (note)
	+ Add any additional comments or notes about the incident.

## Tips

* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please ask for assistance from the relevant team or supervisor.
* Ensure that you're using the correct format for dates (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* If you're attaching photos, please make sure they are clear and legible.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the relevant team.
