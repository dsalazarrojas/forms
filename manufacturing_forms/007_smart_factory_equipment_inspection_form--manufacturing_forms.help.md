# Smart Factory Equipment Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Factory Equipment Inspection Form is designed to evaluate and document the condition of various equipment in a manufacturing facility, including air compressors, belts, gears, motors, pumps, and valves. This form helps inspectors assess the equipment's condition, identify any maintenance needs, and record their findings and comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select all the equipment types that you are inspecting, using the options provided in the "Equipment Inspected" field.
2. Choose the condition of the selected equipment using the "Condition" field.
3. Determine if maintenance is needed, and select the relevant options from the "Maintenance Needed" field.
4. Enter the next scheduled inspection date for the equipment in the "Next Inspection Date" field.
5. Provide any comments or notes about the equipment's condition in the "Inspector Comments" field.
6. Record the equipment's condition history, including any previous inspections, maintenance, or repairs, in the "Equipment Condition History" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Inspected (select_multiple)**: Select all the equipment types you are inspecting, such as Air Compressor, Belt, Gear, Motor, Pump, or Valve.
* **Condition (select_one)**: Choose the condition of the selected equipment using one of the three options: Excellent, Fair, or Poor.
* **Maintenance Needed (select_multiple)**: Determine if any maintenance is required, selecting either Replace, Repair, or None.
* **Next Inspection Date (date)**: Enter the date of the next scheduled inspection for the equipment.
* **Inspector Comments (note)**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the equipment's condition.
* **Equipment Condition History (note)**: Record the equipment's condition history, including any previous inspections, maintenance, or repairs.
