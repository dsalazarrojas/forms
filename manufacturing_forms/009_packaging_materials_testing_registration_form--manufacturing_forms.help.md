# Packaging Materials Testing Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Packaging Materials Testing Registration Form is intended for customers to provide their contact and customer information in order to register their packaging materials for testing. The form is used to gather necessary details for the testing process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Please ensure you have all necessary information about your packaging materials and the customer who will be handling the testing process.
* Fill in the required fields (customer_email, contact_name) and any other relevant fields you'd like to provide.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, Email, required): This field is for the customer's email address. Please enter a valid email address to ensure we can reach you and your customer for any further communication.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, Text, required): This field is for the name of the contact person for the customer.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, Text, optional): This field is an open text field where you can provide any relevant information about your customer.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, Text, optional): This field is an open text field where you can provide any relevant information about the contact person.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, Text, optional): This field is for the customer's phone number.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, Text, optional): This field is for the contact person's title or position.
* **Test Page** (`test_page`, Text, optional): This field is for any additional comments or information about the testing process.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date to avoid delays in the testing process.
* If you are unsure about any field or have any questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
