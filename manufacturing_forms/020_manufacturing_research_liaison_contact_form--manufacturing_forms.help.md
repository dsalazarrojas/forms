# Manufacturing Research Liaison Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from users regarding manufacturing research liaison contacts. It serves as a platform for users to provide essential details about their manufacturing research liaison contacts, which will be used for further investigation and communication.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Navigate through the form pages to select the relevant sections that apply to your research liaison contact.
2.  Enter your contact name in the designated field (page 2).
3.  Enter the email address of your contact (page 3).
4.  Provide the phone number of your contact (page 4).
5.  Review the additional pages for other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name** (`page_2`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the contact person or company.
*   **Email** (`page_3_1`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the contact person or company.
*   **Phone** (`page_4`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person or company.
*   **Page 3, Page 5, Page 6, Page 7, Page 8, Page 9, Page 10, Page 11, Page 12, Page 13, Page 14, Page 15, Page 16, Page 17, Page 18, Page 19, Page 20, Page 21, Page 22** (`page_3`, `page_5`, `page_6`, `page_7`, `page_8`, `page_9`, `page_10`, `page_11`, `page_12`, `page_13`, `page_14`, `page_15`, `page_16`, `page_17`, `page_18`, `page_19`, `page_20`, `page_21`, `page_22`, `note`, required): Enter any additional relevant information about the contact person or company.
