# Batch Rejection Summary Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Batch Rejection Summary Report Form is a crucial tool for manufacturers to record and document the details of batches of products that have been rejected during the production process. This form is designed to gather essential information about the batch, including production date, total units, rejection rate, and other specific reasons for rejection.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the form to start filling it out.
2.  Enter the **Batch ID**, which is the unique identifier for the rejected batch.
3.  Select the **Batch Production Date** from the calendar.
4.  Enter the **Total Units in Batch**, which is the number of units that were part of the batch.
5.  Enter the **Units Rejected**, which is the number of units rejected from the batch.
6.  Enter the **Rejection Rate (%)**, which is the percentage of units rejected.
7.  Select the **Manufacturing Department** where the batch was produced.
8.  Identify the **Primary Defect Type**, which is the main reason for the rejection of the batch.
9.  Enter a detailed description of the **Defect Description** to explain the specific issues found.
10.  Provide a thorough analysis of the **Root Cause**, which led to the rejection of the batch.
11.  Indicate the **Severity Level** of the issue, which can be Critical, High, Medium, or Low.
12.  Enter the **Corrective Action Taken**, which describes the steps being implemented to address the issue.
13.  Provide **Preventive Measures**, which outlines the measures to be taken to prevent this issue in future batches.
14.  Indicate if a **Follow-up Inspection** is required, and if so, select the date.
15.  Add any **Additional Notes**, which can be useful supplementary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Batch ID** (id: 2, required): Enter the unique identifier for the batch. This will help in referencing the batch during analysis and tracking.
*   **Batch Production Date** (id: 3, required): Select the date when the batch was produced. This date helps in tracking the production timeline.
*   **Total Units in Batch** (id: 4, required): Enter the total number of units that were part of the batch. This information is crucial for calculating the rejection rate.
*   **Units Rejected** (id: 5, required): Enter the number of units that were rejected from the batch. This number is used in calculating the rejection rate.
*   **Rejection Rate (%)** (id: 6, required): Calculate and enter the rejection rate based on the total units and units rejected. This helps in assessing the quality of the production process.
*   **Manufacturing Department** (id: 10, required): Select the department involved in the production of the batch. This information can aid in identifying production-related issues.
*   **Primary Defect Type** (id: 12, required): Select the main reason for the rejection of the batch. This helps in pinpointing the root cause of the issue.
*   **Defect Description** (id: 13, required): Describe the specific defects found in the batch. This provides clear and detailed information about the issues.
*   **Root Cause** (id: 14, required): Analyze and document the root cause of the rejection of the batch. This helps in implementing effective corrective actions.
*   **Severity Level** (id: 15, required): Select the level of severity for the defect. This helps in prioritizing corrective actions based on the severity.
*   **Corrective Action Taken** (id: 16, required): Describe the steps taken to address the issue. This ensures that appropriate actions are taken to rectify the problem.
*   **Preventive Measures** (id: 18, required): Outline the measures to be taken to prevent this issue in future batches. This aids in improving the overall quality of the production process.
*   **Follow-up Inspection Required** (id: 19, required): Indicate if a follow-up inspection is required. If so, select the date for the follow-up inspection.
*   **Follow-up Inspection Date** (id: 20, optional): Select the date for the follow-up inspection, if required.
*   **Additional Notes** (id: 21, optional): Add any supplementary information that may be useful for further analysis or future reference.

Tips:

*   Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
*   Be specific when describing defects and their causes to aid in clear analysis and corrective actions.
*   Use clear and concise language throughout the form to avoid confusion.
*   Review and revise the form regularly to ensure that it remains up-to-date and relevant to your specific needs.
