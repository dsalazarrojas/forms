# Smart Labeling Technology Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants for smart labeling technology services or products. It includes fields for personal and business contact details, as well as specific requirements for the product or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in each field accurately and thoroughly.
2. Make sure to include all necessary details as requested.
3. Review your submission before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Form** (`application_form`, Text, Not required): This field is for you to provide a brief description of your application.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, Text, Not required): Please enter your contact information, including name, phone number, and email.
* **Business Details** (`business_details`, Text, Not required): If you are applying on behalf of a business, provide your company's name, address, and relevant contact information.
* **Address Details** (`address_details`, Text, Not required): Enter your physical address for us to contact you if needed.
* **Product Details** (`product_details`, Text, Not required): Describe the product or service you are interested in or applying for.
* **Product Labeling Preferences** (`product_labeling_preferences`, Text, Not required): Specify any specific labeling requirements or preferences for the product or service.
* **Application Terms** (`application_terms`, Text, Not required): This field is for you to acknowledge that you have read and agree to the application terms.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to avoid any issues with your application.
* Double-check your submission for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* If you need help with any part of the form, please reach out to us for assistance.
