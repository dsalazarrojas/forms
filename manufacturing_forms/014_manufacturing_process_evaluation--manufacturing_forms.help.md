# Manufacturing Process Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate and document the manufacturing process, ensuring that it is compliant with safety and quality standards. This form is used by manufacturing staff to review and improve the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and fill out the form according to the instructions provided.
2. Ensure that you answer each question or select relevant options for your process.
3. Make sure to save your progress and submit the form for review and evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Manufacturing Process Steps** (`manufacturing_process_steps`, text, required): This section is for describing the current manufacturing process. Please enter a brief description of the process, including any relevant details such as equipment used and materials handled.
* **Step 1-1** (`step_1_1`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options for this step of the process.
* **Step 1-2** (`step_1_2`, select_one, required): Select one option that best describes this step of the process.
* **Safety Compliance** (`safety_compliance`, text, required): Describe any safety compliance measures taken during the manufacturing process.
* **Quality Checks** (`quality_checks`, text, required): Describe any quality checks performed during the process.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date the process was last updated.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time the process was last updated.
* **Step 2-1** (`step_2_1`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options for this step of the process.
* **Step 2-2** (`step_2_2`, select_one, required): Select one option that best describes this step of the process.
* **Step 3** (`step_3`, text, required): Describe this step of the process, including any relevant details such as equipment used and materials handled.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it for evaluation.
* If you are unsure of any field or option, please ask for clarification from your supervisor or the manufacturing department.
* This form is intended to be a living document and will be reviewed and updated regularly. Your input and feedback are valuable in ensuring the accuracy and effectiveness of the process.
