# Smart Sensor Calibration Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Smart Sensor Calibration Report Form is used to collect data related to the calibration process of smart sensors. This form helps in maintaining accurate records of sensor ID, location, and calibration settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the "sensor_details" page**: Enter the sensor's ID and location where it is installed.
2. **Fill out the "calibration_details" page**: Record the calibration date, time, and settings used for each sensor.
3. **Fill out the "submission_details" page**: Provide any additional information related to the submission of the data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sensor Details (<sensor_details>):** 
  * **Sensor ID**: Enter the unique identifier of the sensor.
  * **Location**: Enter the location where the sensor is installed.
* **Calibration Details (<calibration_details>):** 
  * **Calibration Date and Time**: Record the date and time when the sensor was calibrated.
  * **Calibration Settings**: Enter any additional information about the calibration settings used.
* **Submission Details (<submission_details>):** 
  * **Additional Information**: Provide any notes or comments about the submission of the data.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all information is correct and complete.
- If you have any questions or need assistance with this form, contact your supervisor or the relevant department.
