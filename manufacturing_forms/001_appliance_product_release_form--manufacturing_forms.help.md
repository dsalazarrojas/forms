# Appliance Product Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the release of a new appliance product, ensuring that all relevant information is captured to support internal tracking and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review and complete each field carefully to ensure accurate and consistent data.
3. Use the provided hints as guidelines for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Release ID 
* Release ID (text, required): Enter the internal tracking number for this release.

### 2. Product Model 
* Product model (text, required): Enter the model number or name of the appliance.

### 3. Production Batch 
* Production batch (text, required): Enter the batch or lot identifier for this release.

### 4. Release Date 
* Release date (date, required): Enter the date approved for release.

### 5. Quality Inspector 
* Quality inspector name (text, required): Enter the name of the person who performed the final QA.

### 6. Quality Status 
* Quality status (select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Passed
	+ Failed
	+ Conditional

### 7. Corrective Actions 
* Corrective actions required (text, optional): If the quality status is Failed or Conditional, enter the required actions.

### 8. Distribution Center 
* Distribution center (select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Warehouse A
	+ Warehouse B
	+ Regional Hub

### 9. Handoff Contact 
* Handoff contact (text, required): Enter the name of the logistics contact for this release.

### 10. Additional Notes 
* Additional notes (text, optional): Enter any other relevant information.

**Tips**

* Make sure to review and complete each field carefully to ensure accurate and consistent data.
* Use the provided hints as guidelines for each field.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or quality control team for clarification.
* This form is designed to support internal tracking and communication, so ensure that it is filled out accurately and thoroughly.
