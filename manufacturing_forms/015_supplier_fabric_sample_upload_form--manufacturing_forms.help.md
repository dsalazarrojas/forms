# supplier_fabric_sample_upload_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The supplier fabric sample upload form is a tool designed to collect and organize fabric sample data from suppliers. This form aims to make it easier for suppliers to provide accurate and comprehensive information about their fabric samples.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the supplier fabric sample details in the text field.
2.  Select and upload relevant files related to the fabric sample.
3.  Choose the relevant fabric options from the list.
4.  Provide a description of the fabric sample.
5.  Enter the weight of the fabric sample.
6.  Specify the unit of measurement for the fabric sample weight.
7.  Enter the color of the fabric sample.
8.  Enter the length of the fabric sample.
9.  Enter the width of the fabric sample.
10.  Add any relevant notes about the fabric sample.
11.  Enter the email address of the contact person.
12.  Enter the phone number of the contact person.
13.  Select the date of the fabric sample submission.
14.  Select the time of the fabric sample submission.
15.  Select the time zone of the fabric sample submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Supplier Fabric Sample Details** (`supplier_fabric_sample_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the fabric sample.
*   **Fabric Sample Files** (`fabric_sample_files`, text, optional): Upload relevant files related to the fabric sample, such as images or documents.
*   **Fabric Sample Options** (`fabric_sample_options`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the relevant fabric options from the list, such as Fabric A, Fabric B, or Fabric C.
*   **Fabric Sample Description** (`fabric_sample_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the fabric sample.
*   **Fabric Sample Weight** (`fabric_sample_weight`, number, optional): Enter the weight of the fabric sample.
*   **Fabric Sample Unit** (`fabric_sample_unit`, text, optional): Specify the unit of measurement for the fabric sample weight, such as grams or pounds.
*   **Fabric Sample Color** (`fabric_sample_color`, text, optional): Enter the color of the fabric sample.
*   **Fabric Sample Length** (`fabric_sample_length`, number, optional): Enter the length of the fabric sample.
*   **Fabric Sample Width** (`fabric_sample_width`, number, optional): Enter the width of the fabric sample.
*   **Fabric Sample Notes** (`fabric_sample_notes`, note, optional): Add any relevant notes about the fabric sample.
*   **Fabric Sample Email** (`fabric_sample_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
*   **Fabric Sample Phone** (`fabric_sample_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
*   **Fabric Sample Date** (`fabric_sample_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the fabric sample submission.
*   **Fabric Sample Time** (`fabric_sample_time`, time, optional): Select the time of the fabric sample submission.
*   **Fabric Sample Time Zone** (`fabric_sample_timezone`, text, optional): Select the time zone of the fabric sample submission.
