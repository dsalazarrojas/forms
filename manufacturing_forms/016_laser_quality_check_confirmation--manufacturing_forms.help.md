# Laser Quality Check Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laser Quality Check Confirmation form is used to verify the quality of laser equipment used in the manufacturing process. This form ensures that all equipment is functioning properly and meets the required standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's first name in the "first-name" field.
2. Select the department responsible for the laser equipment.
3. Enter the location where the equipment is used.
4. Enter the date and time of the laser check.
5. Enter the equipment serial number.
6. Enter any additional notes about the quality check.
7. Confirm whether the laser equipment passes the quality check (True or False).
8. (Optional) Enter the follow-up date and time if the laser check is not passed.
9. (Optional) Enter a note about the follow-up date.
10. Enter the employee's email for record-keeping purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first-name** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the employee who performed the laser quality check.
* **department** (text, required: false): Select the department responsible for the laser equipment.
* **location** (text, required: false): Enter the location where the laser equipment is used.
* **date** (date, required: false): Enter the date of the laser quality check.
* **time** (time, required: false): Enter the time of the laser quality check.
* **equipment-serial-number** (text, required: false): Enter the serial number of the laser equipment.
* **notes** (note, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the quality check.
* **laser-check-pass** (select_one, required: true): Confirm whether the laser equipment passes or fails the quality check.
* **follow-up-date** (date, required: false): Enter the follow-up date if the laser check is not passed.
* **follow-up-time** (time, required: false): Enter the follow-up time if the laser check is not passed.
* **follow-up-date-note** (text, required: false): Enter a note about the follow-up date.
* **user-email** (email, required: false): Enter the employee's email for record-keeping purposes.
