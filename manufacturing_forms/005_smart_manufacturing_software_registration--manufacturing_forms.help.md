# Smart Manufacturing Software Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Manufacturing Software Registration form is designed to collect essential information about companies and their manufacturing presence on our platform. By providing accurate and complete data, you'll help us better serve you and other users.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please review each section carefully and fill out the required information accurately.
2. Ensure that you have permission to register your company on our platform.
3. Verify that your company details are up to date and consistent with your public records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Details**: Enter the name and other essential details about your company. 
  (Required: No)
  This field is for providing your company's name and a brief description if available.
* **User Details**: Enter your name, job title, and contact information. 
  (Required: No)
  This field is for providing the name and contact information of the primary user or contact person for your company.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your contact's email address, phone number, and any other relevant contact information. 
  (Required: No)
  This field is for providing a way to get in touch with you or your company's representative.
* **Process Details**: Enter any additional information about your manufacturing process or software/service offerings. 
  (Required: No)
  This field is for providing any further context or details about your company's products or services.
* **Company Information**: Enter any additional information about your company. 
  (Required: No)
  This field is for providing any other relevant details about your company that might be helpful.
* **Lead Source**: Select the source where you heard about us. 
  (Required: No)
  This field helps us understand how you found out about our platform.
* **Company Type**: Select the type of company you represent. 
  (Required: No)
  This field is for classifying your company's type.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. 
  (Required: No)
  This field is for ensuring we have the correct email address on file.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number. 
  (Required: No)
  This field is for providing a way to contact you or your company's representative.
* **Address**: Enter your company's address. 
  (Required: No)
  This field is for providing your company's address for records and contact purposes.
* **Website**: Enter your company's website. 
  (Required: No)
  This field is for providing your company's website for further research or information.
* **Facebook**: Enter your company's Facebook page. 
  (Required: No)
  This field is for providing your company's Facebook page for further research or information.
* **Twitter**: Enter your company's Twitter page. 
  (Required: No)
  This field is for providing your company's Twitter page for further research or information.
* **Instagram**: Enter your company's Instagram page. 
  (Required: No)
  This field is for providing your company's Instagram page for further research or information.
* **LinkedIn**: Enter your company's LinkedIn page. 
  (Required: No)
  This field is for providing your company's LinkedIn page for further research or information.

## Tips
- Ensure that all information is accurate and up to date.
- Review the form carefully before submission.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
