Production Waste Tracking Report Form - Help Guide
==============================

### Purpose

The Production Waste Tracking Report Form is used to record and track production waste occurrences on the production lines in the facility. This form helps to identify areas that need improvement and optimize production processes to minimize waste generation.

### How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the production line where the waste occurred.
2. Choose the shift during which the waste occurred.
3. Select the date of the production.
4. Enter the time of day when the waste occurred.
5. Choose the type(s) of waste generated.
6. Enter the weight of the waste.
7. Select the unit of measurement for the waste.
8. Choose the waste category (e.g., correctible, incidental, or non-reportable).
9. Enter any additional remarks or comments.
10. Select the submitted by field (not required).
11. Enter the submitted date (not required).
12. Enter any production lead information (not required).
13. Enter the manager's name (not required).
14. Enter the department (not required).
15. Enter the team (not required).
16. Enter the location (not required).
17. Add any attachments (e.g., photos, videos, or documents).
18. Enter the total weight of the waste (not required).
19. Enter any comments or notes.
20. Enter the created by field (not required).
21. Enter the updated by field (not required).
22. Enter the updated date (not required).
23. Enter the deleted by field (not required).

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Production Line** (`production_line`, `select_one`): Select the production line where the waste occurred.
* **Shift** (`shift`, `date`): Choose the shift during which the waste occurred.
* **Production Date** (`production_date`, `date`): Enter the date of the production.
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, `time`): Select the time of day when the waste occurred.
* **Type of Waste** (`type_of_waste`, `select_multiple`): Choose the type(s) of waste generated.
* **Weight of Waste** (`weight_of_waste`, `number`): Enter the weight of the waste.
* **Unit of Measurement** (`unit_of_measurement`, `select_one`): Select the unit of measurement for the waste (e.g., kg, lbs, tons).
* **Waste Category** (`waste_category`, `select_one`): Choose the waste category (e.g., correctible, incidental, or non-reportable).
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, `note`): Enter any additional remarks or comments.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`): Enter the name of the person who submitted the report (not required).
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, `date`): Enter the date the report was submitted (not required).
* **Production Lead** (`production_lead`, `text`): Enter any production lead information (not required).
* **Manager** (`manager`, `text`): Enter the name of the production manager (not required).
* **Department** (`department`, `text`): Enter the name of the department (not required).
* **Team** (`team`, `text`): Enter the team name (not required).
* **Location** (`location`, `text`): Enter the location where the waste occurred (not required).
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, `select_multiple`): Add any attachments, such as photos or videos, to support the report.
* **Total Weight** (`total_weight`, `number`): Enter the total weight of the waste (not required).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`): Enter any comments or notes about the waste.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, `text`): Enter the name of the person who created the report (not required).
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, `text`): Enter the name of the person who updated the report (not required).
* **Updated On** (`updated_on`, `date`): Enter the date the report was updated (not required).
* **Deleted By** (`deleted_by`, `text`): Enter the name of the person who deleted the report (not required).

### Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields, but be aware that some fields are optional.
* Select only one production line and one shift.
* Use the correct unit of measurement for the waste.
* Use the attachments feature to add supporting evidence.
* Make sure to enter the correct type of waste generated.
* Enter the correct weight and total weight of the waste.
* Enter any additional remarks or comments.
* Be mindful of the waste category and production lead information.
* Make sure to enter the correct submitted date and created by fields.
