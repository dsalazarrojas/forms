# Metal Fabrication Sample Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to place an order for metal fabrication samples. Please fill out the form with your contact information, sample requirements, and order details to submit your order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your contact information in the "User Info" section.
2. In the "Sample Request" field, provide a brief description of the sample you need.
3. Select the type of material you need from the "Material Selection" dropdown list.
4. Choose a fabrication method from the "Fabrication Method" dropdown list.
5. Enter the quantity and unit cost of the sample in the "Quantity" and "Unit Cost" fields, respectively.
6. Select a delivery method from the "Delivery Method" dropdown list.
7. If necessary, provide special instructions in the "Special Instructions" field.
8. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
9. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (1, text, required): Fill out your name, company name, and other contact information in this field.
* **Sample Request** (2, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the sample you need. This field is not required.
* **Material Selection** (3, select_multiple, required): Select the type of material you need for your sample.
* **Fabrication Method** (4, select_one, required): Choose the method of fabrication you prefer for your sample.
* **Quantity** (5, number, required): Enter the quantity of the sample you need.
* **Unit Cost** (6, number, required): Enter the unit cost of the sample.
* **Total Cost** (7, number, required): The total cost of the sample will be calculated based on the quantity and unit cost.
* **Delivery Method** (8, select_one, required): Select the method of delivery you prefer for your sample.
* **Special Instructions** (9, text, optional): Provide any special instructions for your sample order.
* **Contact Number** (12, text, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Email Address** (13, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Notes** (14, note, optional): Provide any additional notes for your order.
* **Review Status** (17, select_one, required): Select the status of your review.
* **Review Date** (18, date, optional): Enter the date of your review.

* **Review Time 2** (23, time, optional): Enter the time of your second review.
* **User Comments** (24, text, optional): Provide any comments for your order.
* **User Notes** (25, note, optional): Provide any additional notes for your order.
