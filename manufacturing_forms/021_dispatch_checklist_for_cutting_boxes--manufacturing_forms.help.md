# Dispatch Checklist For Cutting Boxes - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and verify the dispatch of cut boxes, ensuring that each box is properly signed off and documented in the system. This includes confirming that the box has been properly cut and prepared for dispatch.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form and ensure all necessary information is available.
2. Select the "is_cut_box_confirmed" field and choose "confirmed" if the box has been properly cut and prepared.
3. Enter any relevant signatures or comments in the "capture_signatures" field.
4. Enter any additional dispatch notes in the "cut_box_dispatch_notes" field.
5. Select the date and time of dispatch from the "cut_box_dispatch_date" and "cut_box_dispatch_time" fields.
6. Enter the name of the person or team responsible for dispatching the box in the "cut_box_dispatched_by" field.
7. Confirm that the box has been properly signed off by checking the "cut_box_dispatched_at" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **cut_box (1)**: Enter the number of the cut box being dispatched.
* **is_cut_box_confirmed (2)**: Confirm that the box has been properly cut and prepared for dispatch. Select "confirmed" if true, "not confirmed" if not.
* **capture_signatures (3)**: Enter any signatures or comments related to the box.
* **cut_box_dispatch_notes (4)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the dispatch process.
* **cut_box_dispatch_date (5)**: Select the date when the box was dispatched.
* **cut_box_dispatch_time (6)**: Select the time when the box was dispatched.
* **capture_cut_box_signatures (7)**: Enter any additional signatures or comments related to the box's dispatch.
* **cut_box_dispatched_at (8)**: This field is automatically populated when the box is confirmed dispatched. 
* **cut_box_dispatched_by (9)**: Enter the name of the person or team responsible for dispatching the box.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to maintain accurate records.
* If the "is_cut_box_confirmed" field is selected as "confirmed", the "cut_box_dispatched_at" field will be automatically populated.
* Use the "cut_box_dispatch_notes" field for any additional notes or comments about the dispatch process.
