# Facility And Production Capabilities Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey is designed to collect comprehensive data about a facility's production capabilities, including contact information, production capacity, and operational hours. It is essential to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure seamless communication and collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Facility Details**: Please complete the following fields with your facility's information:
	* **Facility Description**: A brief description of your facility.
	* **Facility Address**: Enter your facility's physical address.
	* **Facility City**: Enter the city where your facility is located.
	* **Facility Zip**: Enter your facility's postal/zip code.
	* **Facility Country**: Enter the country where your facility is located.
	* **Facility Phone**: Enter your facility's phone number.
	* **Facility Email**: Enter your facility's email address.
	* **Facility Website**: Enter your facility's website URL (optional).
2. **Contact Information**: Complete the following fields with your contact information:
	* **Contact Name**: Enter your name.
	* **Contact Title**: Enter your title/position.
	* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address.
	* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number.
	* **Contact Fax**: Enter your fax number.
	* **Contact Address**: Enter your address.
	* **Contact City**: Enter the city where you are located.
	* **Contact Region**: Enter your region.
	* **Contact Postal Code**: Enter your postal/zip code.
3. **Production Capacity**: Answer the following questions about your facility's production capacity:
	* **Capacity Survey**: Briefly describe your facility's current production capacity.
	* **Production Capacity**: Describe your facility's current production capacity.
	* **Facility Capabilities**: Describe your facility's production capabilities.
	* **Production Capacity Note**: Share any additional notes or information about your facility's production capacity.
4. **Operational Hours and Contact Info**: Complete the following fields with your operational hours and contact information:
	* **Production Hours**: Describe your facility's production hours of operation.
	* **Production Days**: Describe the days of operation of your facility.
	* **Contact Notes**: Add any additional notes or information about your facility's contact.

Please ensure you complete this form accurately and thoroughly to provide valuable insights about your facility's capabilities and operational hours. If you have any questions or need clarification, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
