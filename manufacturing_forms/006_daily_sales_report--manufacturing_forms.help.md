# daily_sales_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The daily sales report form is used to track sales information on a daily basis, including sales date, customer, product, sales amount, and other relevant details. This form is meant to be completed by sales representatives to provide accurate and timely data for sales management.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the date of the sales event from the calendar.
* Choose whether the sales event included a customer or not.
* Indicate which product(s) were included in the sale.
* Enter the amount of sales made on the day.
* Add any additional notes about the sale.
* Specify the time the sale was submitted, if applicable.
* Enter the contact information of the sales representative.
* Select whether a sales manager was involved in the sale or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sales Date** (`sales_date`, `date`, optional): Select the date of the sales event from the calendar.
* **Customer** (`customer`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether the sales event included a customer or not.
* **Product** (`product`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate which product(s) were included in the sale.
* **Sales Amount** (`sales_amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount of sales made on the day.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about the sale.
* **Submitted** (`submitted`, `time`, optional): Specify the time the sale was submitted, if applicable.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter the contact information of the sales representative in the format "example@example.com".
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact information of the sales representative in the format "123-456-7890".
* **Sales Manager** (`sales_manager`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether a sales manager was involved in the sale or not.
## Tips
* Make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure timely and accurate sales data.
* If a sales manager was involved, be sure to include their contact information in the "Email" field.
* If the sale included a customer, consider including additional details about the customer in the "Notes" field.
