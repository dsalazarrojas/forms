# Packaging Prototype Sample Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Packaging Prototype Sample Request Form is a streamlined tool designed to facilitate the collection of packaging prototype samples from packaging providers. This form is intended for use by packaging professionals and researchers who need to request samples for testing, evaluation, or development purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form to understand the fields and their purposes.
2. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
3. Provide detailed request specifics in the "Request Details" field.
4. Specify packaging information in the "Packaging Info" field.
5. Review your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information to ensure that we can get in touch with you regarding your request.
* **Request Details** (`request_details`, text, optional): Describe the specific requirements and expectations for the prototype sample you are requesting, including any technical specifications or deadlines.
* **Packaging Info** (`packaging_info`, text, optional): Provide information about the packaging you are requesting, such as the material, size, or type.

## Tips
- Be clear and concise when describing your request to ensure that the packaging provider understands your requirements.
- Double-check your submission before clicking the "Submit" button to avoid errors.
