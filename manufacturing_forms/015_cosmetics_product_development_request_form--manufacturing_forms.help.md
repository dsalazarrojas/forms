# cosmetics_product_development_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users for the development of new cosmetic products. It collects specifications, features, and other relevant details about the product being requested.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Please provide the new product's specification in the "New Product Specification" field.
2. Choose the product type from the options in the "Product Type" field. You can select multiple options if applicable.
3. Select the packaging type for the product from the options in the "Packaging Type" field.
4. Enter the product's name in the "Product Name" field.
5. Describe the product in the "Product Description" field.
6. List the product's features in the "Product Features" field.
7. Specify the target market for the product in the "Target Market" field.
8. Select the current development status of the product from the options in the "Development Status" field.
9. Add any additional notes about the product in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Product Specification**: Please enter the new product's technical specifications, such as its composition, packaging, and other relevant details.
* **Product Type**: Select the category or type of product you are requesting, such as skin care, hair care, or nail care. You can select multiple options if applicable.
* **Packaging Type**: Select the type of packaging for the product, such as bottle, tube, or box.
* **Product Name**: Enter a name for the product that reflects its purpose and characteristics.
* **Product Description**: Describe the product's features, benefits, and unique selling points.
* **Product Features**: List the product's features, such as its benefits, advantages, and special ingredients.
* **Target Market**: Specify the target audience or market for the product, such as age group, skin type, or hair type.
* **Development Status**: Select the current status of the product development, such as "In Progress", "Completed", or "Pending".
* **Notes**: Add any additional information or comments about the product that may be helpful for the development team.

## Tips
Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to facilitate the development process. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the development team.
