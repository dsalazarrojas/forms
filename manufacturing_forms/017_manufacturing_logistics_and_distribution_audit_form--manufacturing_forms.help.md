# Manufacturing Logistics And Distribution Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form serves to assess the current state of logistics and distribution processes at a given site, including distribution methods, facility conditions, site personnel, and related documentation. Its purpose is to identify areas of improvement and ensure regulatory compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Site Identification
1. Enter the **Site Id** (id 1), a unique identifier for your site.
2. Fill in the **site name** (id 2) if it is not already populated.

### Step 2: Distribution and Facility Information
1. Enter the **facility id** (id 3), which is also a unique identifier for your facility.
2. Fill in the **facility address** (id 4) in a format that can be easily read and referenced.
3. Select the **distribution method** (id 5) that is currently used at your site.
4. Choose the **site status** (id 10) that accurately reflects the current operational state of your facility.

### Step 3: Audit Details
1. Enter the **audit date** (id 6) and **audit time** (id 7) for the audit you are conducting.
2. In the **findings** (id 8) field, provide a detailed description of any observed issues or concerns during the audit.
3. If necessary, list **corrective actions** (id 9) to address any issues identified during the audit.

### Step 4: Personnel
1. Identify **site employees** (id 17) and **site managers** (id 18) who are directly involved with the site or facilities.
2. List any **site supervisors** (id 19), who may be relevant to the audit.
3. Note the **site lead** (id 20), the primary contact person for the site.
4. Document the **site engineer** (id 21), who may have specific expertise related to the facilities and equipment.
5. Document any **site security** (id 22) personnel or concerns.
6. Document any **site insurance** (id 23) details or requirements.
7. Document any **site vehicles** (id 24) used for site operations.
8. Document any **site equipment** (id 25), including its condition and any necessary maintenance.

### Step 5: Submission
1. Select the **submitted by** (id 16) individual or team that conducted the audit.
2. Select the **assigned to** (id 14) individual or team that will follow up on the audit results.
3. Select the **assigned by** (id 15) individual or team that assigned the audit.
4. Enter your **email** (id 12) and **phone** (id 13) contact information for follow-up or communication.

## Tips
* Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
* Be as detailed as possible in your descriptions and observations.
* Ensure that all personnel and facilities are correctly identified and referenced.
* This form is intended to facilitate improvement and compliance; use it as a tool for positive change within your site.
* Store the form and its contents securely to ensure confidentiality and compliance with company policies.
* Consider sharing the form with relevant personnel or stakeholders for input or feedback.
