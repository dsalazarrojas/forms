# Manufacturing Quality Control Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on the quality of products or services in the manufacturing process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about the manufacturing process, including the production site, equipment, and personnel involved.
2. Assess the quality of products or services using the provided fields.
3. Track any issues or defects found during the quality assessment.
4. Enter production data, such as quantities produced and production dates.
5. Record quality metrics, including defect rates and other relevant data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (General Information, text, optional): This field is for providing basic information about the manufacturing process, such as the production site, equipment, and personnel involved.
* **Quality Assessment** (Quality Assessment, text, optional): Use this field to evaluate the quality of products or services, including any defects or issues found during the assessment.
* **Issue Tracking** (Issue Tracking, text, optional): Record any issues or defects found during the quality assessment.
* **Production Data** (Production Data, text, optional): Enter production data, such as quantities produced and production dates.
* **Quality Metrics** (Quality Metrics, text, optional): Record quality metrics, including defect rates and other relevant data.

## Tips
* Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out this form to ensure accurate quality assessment and tracking.
* If unsure about any section or question, please consult with your supervisor or a quality control expert.
* Review and update this form regularly to monitor and improve the quality of products or services in the manufacturing process.
