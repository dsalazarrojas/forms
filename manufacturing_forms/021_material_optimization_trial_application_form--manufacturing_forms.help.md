# Material Optimization Trial Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Material Optimization Trial Application Form is a tool to help users optimize their materials for manufacturing processes. The form collects information about whether users want to apply for a material optimization trial, their selection of options, and details about their manufacturing process and trial request date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form instructions carefully.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you want to apply for a material optimization trial.
3. Select one or multiple options for your response.
4. Provide details about your manufacturing process in the text field.
5. Choose a trial request date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Material Optimization Trial (`form_1`)
* Select "Yes" if you want to apply for a material optimization trial. This will indicate your interest in optimizing your materials for manufacturing.

### Select Multiple (`form_2`)
* Choose one or multiple options to answer this question, as you may have multiple reasons for applying for the material optimization trial.

### Manufacturing Process (`form_3`)
* Describe your manufacturing process in as much detail as possible. This will help us understand your specific needs and optimize materials accordingly.

### Trial Request Date (`form_4`)
* Choose a date for when you would like to start the trial. This will help us schedule the trial accordingly.
