# Component Thermal Shield Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect order specifications and details from buyers for thermal shields.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the buyer's name in the "Buyer name" field.
2. Provide the buyer's contact email for order acknowledgment.
3. Enter the thermal shield's model identifier in the "Shield model" field.
4. Specify the material grade and finish of the thermal shield in the "Material specification" field.
5. Enter the dimensions of the thermal shield (if relevant) in the "Dimensions" field.
6. Enter the number of units required in the "Quantity" field.
7. Provide the delivery timeline and special handling notes in the "Delivery requirements" field (optional).
8. Enter any purchase order number for reference (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Buyer name** (`buyer_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the individual or company placing the order.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, **required**): Enter the email address of the buyer for order acknowledgment.
* **Shield model** (`shield_model`, text, **required**): Enter the model identifier for the thermal shield being ordered.
* **Dimensions** (`dimensions`, text, **optional**): Enter the length, width, and height of the thermal shield (if applicable).
* **Material specification** (`material_specification`, text, **required**): Enter the material grade and finish of the thermal shield.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, **required**): Enter the number of units of the thermal shield being ordered.
* **Delivery requirements** (`delivery_requirements`, text, **optional**): Provide any special delivery instructions or timeline.
* **Purchase order number** (`purchase_order_number`, text, **optional**): Enter any relevant purchase order number for reference.
* **Note: All fields are case sensitive, and please ensure accuracy in filling the form.**

**Tips**
* Double-check that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to our team for support.
