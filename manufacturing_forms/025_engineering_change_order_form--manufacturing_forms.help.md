> # Engineering Change Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for a change order request, which is a formal request to alter or modify a product or service.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Customer Info** field with the relevant customer information.
2. Enter the **Product Id** of the product or service to be modified.
3. Write a brief description of the change request in the **Description** field.
4. Select the **Change Type** from the options provided (Modification, Revision, or Update).
5. Enter your name and contact information as the **Change Requestor** in the respective fields.
6. Add any additional comments or comments from the **Reviewer** and **Approver** in the respective fields.
7. Set the **Review Date** and **Approved Date** fields as required.
8. Choose the **Change Status** from the options provided (In Progress, Approved, or Rejected).
9. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info**: Enter the customer's name and contact information.
* **Product Id**: Enter the product id to be modified.
* **Description**: Briefly describe the change request.
* **Impact Date**: Enter the date when the change will take effect.
* **Impact Time**: Enter the time when the change will take effect.
* **Change Description**: Describe the change in detail.
* **Impact User**: Enter the user impacted by the change.
* **Change Type**: Select the type of change requested (Modification, Revision, or Update).
* **Change Requestor**: Enter your name and contact information.
* **Change Requestor Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Change Requestor Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Change Requestor Department**: Enter your department.
* **Approver Info**: Enter the approver's name and department.
* **Approver Email**: Enter the approver's email address.
* **Approver Phone**: Enter the approver's phone number.
* **Approver Department**: Enter the approver's department.
* **Review Date**: Set the date for the review of the change request.
* **Change Status**: Choose the status of the change request (In Progress, Approved, or Rejected).
* **Change Status Comment**: Add any comments for the change status.
* **Reviewer Comments**: Add any comments from the reviewer.
* **Approver Comments**: Add any comments from the approver.
* **Revision Date**: Set the date for the revision of the change request.
* **Approved By**: Enter the name of the approver.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments.
