# Machine Performance Benchmark Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Machine Performance Benchmark Registration form is a platform for registering machines or vehicles for performance testing. This form collects various details about the machine, including its specifications, fuel efficiency, and testing information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the machine's name and model.
2. Provide the year of manufacture and engine power (HP) of the machine.
3. Enter the maximum speed in miles per hour (mph) and kilometers per hour (km/h).
4. Enter the torque (RPM) and fuel efficiency (gpmg) of the machine.
5. Select the fuel type and drive type of the machine.
6. Choose the transmission type.
7. Enter the vehicle weight, length, width, and height.
8. Select the machine type (car, truck, or other).
9. Specify the machine's condition (new, used, refurbished, or other).
10. Enter the owner's contact information.
11. Provide the machine's location.
12. Enter the tester's contact email and phone number.
13. Optionally, enter the date and time of the test.
14. Enter the test result.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Machine Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the machine or vehicle being registered.
* **Machine Model** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter the model of the machine or vehicle.
* **Machine Year Of Manufacture** (`3`, `number`, required): Enter the year the machine was manufactured.
* **Engine Power (HP)** (`4`, `number`, required): Enter the engine power of the machine in horsepower.
* **Max Speed (mph)** (`5`, `number`, required): Enter the maximum speed of the machine in miles per hour.
* **Max Speed (km/h)** (`6`, `number`, required): Enter the maximum speed of the machine in kilometers per hour.
* **Torque (RPM)** (`7`, `number`, required): Enter the torque of the machine in revolutions per minute.
* **Fuel Efficiency (gpmg)** (`8`, `number`, required): Enter the fuel efficiency of the machine.
* **Fuel Type** (`9`, `text`, required): Select the type of fuel the machine uses.
* **Drive Type** (`10`, `text`, required): Select the drive type of the machine.
* **Transmission** (`11`, `select_one`, required): Select the transmission type (automatic, manual, semi-automatic, or other).
* **Vehicle Weight (lbs)** (`12`, `number`, required): Enter the weight of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Length (ft)** (`13`, `number`, required): Enter the length of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Width (ft)** (`14`, `number`, required): Enter the width of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Height (ft)** (`15`, `number`, required): Enter the height of the vehicle.
* **Machine Type** (`16`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the type of machine (car, truck, or other).
* **Machine Condition** (`17`, `select_multiple`, required): Specify the condition of the machine (new, used, refurbished, or other).
* **Owner** (`18`, `text`, required): Enter the owner's name.
* **Location** (`19`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the machine.
* **Contact E-mail** (`20`, `email`, required): Enter the tester's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`21`, `text`, required): Enter the tester's phone number.
* **Date Tested** (`22`, `date`, optional): Optionally, enter the date the machine was tested.
* **Time Tested** (`23`, `time`, optional): Optionally, enter the time the machine was tested.
* **Test Result** (`24`, `note`, optional): Optionally, enter the test result.
