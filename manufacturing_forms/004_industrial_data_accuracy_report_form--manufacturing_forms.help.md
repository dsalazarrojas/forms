It's used to report on errors, discrepancies, or issues that affect the accuracy of the industrial data. This form is likely used in a data collection or quality control process to gather details about what went wrong, how severe it was, and what actions were taken to correct it.

Before filling out this form, please ensure you're only completing one instance per discrepancy. If multiple discrepancies occurred, fill out multiple forms, one for each issue. This form should not be used to report on routine or expected variations in data.

## industrial_data_accuracy_report_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to report discrepancies or errors found during industrial data collection. It helps to track and investigate issues affecting data accuracy.

## How To Complete This Form

- Start by filling out the form once you've identified a discrepancy or error in the industrial data.
- Answer each field carefully and accurately to provide as much detail as possible.
- You can skip fields that are not relevant to the specific incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Discrepancy** (`discrepancy`, text, required/optional): Describe the nature of the discrepancy or error you've found. Please provide as much detail as possible.
* **Severity** (`severity`, select_one, required/optional): Rate the severity of the discrepancy or error. Choose from the available options: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Evidence** (`evidence`, text, required/optional): Provide any supporting evidence for the discrepancy or error. This could include photos, videos, or notes about what you observed.
* **Corrective Actions** (`corrective_actions`, text, required/optional): Describe the actions you took or plan to take to correct the discrepancy or error.
* **Severity Rating** (`severity_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate the severity of the discrepancy or error on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being minor and 10 being critical.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the discrepancy or error occurred.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time when the discrepancy or error occurred.
* **Evidence Description** (`evidence_description`, text, required/optional): Describe the evidence for the discrepancy or error in more detail, if applicable.

## Tips

- Be as specific as possible when describing the discrepancy or error.
- Use the available options for Severity to ensure consistency in rating severity.
- Take care when entering dates and times to ensure accuracy.
- If you're unsure about any field, ask for clarification or guidance from the relevant team.
