# Casting Progress Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Casting Progress Report Form is used to report on the progress of a casting operation, providing a concise overview of key milestones and quality status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form for each progress update of a single casting operation.
2. Enter the report date to record the date of the progress update.
3. Enter the production shift identifier to link the progress update to the corresponding shift.
4. Enter the casting batch or mold ID to identify the specific batch or mold being worked on.
5. Enter the name of the person reporting the progress update.
6. Enter the total number of units produced.
7. Check the final quality status, indicating whether the castings are acceptable, require rework, or are scrap.
8. List any attachments or storage locations for reference materials.
9. Provide any additional comments from the supervisor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Date (1)**: `report_date`
	+ Date of this progress update.
* **Shift (2)**: `shift`
	+ Production shift identifier.
* **Batch Number (3)**: `batch_number`
	+ Casting batch or mold ID.
* **Operator Name (4)**: `operator_name`
	+ Name of the person reporting.
* **Quantity Cast (5)**: `quantity_cast`
	+ Number of units produced.
* **Defects Identified (6)**: `defects_identified`
	+ Brief description of any defects identified during the progress update.
* **Corrective Actions Taken (7)**: `corrective_actions`
	+ Steps taken to resolve issues.
* **Quality Status (8)**: `quality_status`
	+ Final quality assessment: Acceptable, Rework required, or Scrap.
* **Attachments and Photos (9)**: `attachments`
	+ List filenames or storage locations for reference materials.
* **Supervisor Comments (10)**: `supervisor_comments`
	+ Additional notes from the supervisor.

## Tips

* Be sure to fill out each field accurately and completely.
* Review and update the form for each progress update of a single casting operation.
* Attach any relevant documents or photos to support your report.
* Use the quality status field to communicate the current state of the product.
