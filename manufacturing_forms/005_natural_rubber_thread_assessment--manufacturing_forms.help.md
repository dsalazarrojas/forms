# Natural Rubber Thread Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Natural Rubber Thread Assessment form is used to evaluate the quality of a natural rubber thread sample. It is a critical component of the quality control process to ensure that the thread meets the required standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Sample Received field with a brief description of the sample you are evaluating.
2. Enter the Sample ID to uniquely identify the thread sample.
3. Assess the thread's quality by selecting one of the options (None, Wrinkling, Puckering, or Cracking) for each of the three defect types.
4. Write a note in the Signed Evaluation field to provide any additional information or comments about the thread's quality.
5. Sign the Signature field to confirm that you have completed the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sample Received**: Enter a brief description of the sample you are evaluating.
* **Sample ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the thread sample.
* **Rating 1**: Rate the thread's quality on a scale from 1 to 100, where 1 is the lowest and 100 is the highest. 
* **Rating 2**: Rate the thread's quality on a scale from 1 to 100, where 1 is the lowest and 100 is the highest.
* **Rating 3**: Rate the thread's quality on a scale from 1 to 100, where 1 is the lowest and 100 is the highest.
* **Defect 1**: Select one of the following defects:
	+ None: No defects were observed.
	+ Wrinkling: The thread has a wrinkling defect.
	+ Puckering: The thread has a pukering defect.
	+ Cracking: The thread has a cracking defect.
* **Defect 2**: Select one of the following defects:
	+ None: No defects were observed.
	+ Wrinkling: The thread has a wrinkling defect.
	+ Puckering: The thread has a pukering defect.
	+ Cracking: The thread has a cracking defect.
* **Defect 3**: Select one of the following defects:
	+ None: No defects were observed.
	+ Wrinkling: The thread has a wrinkling defect.
	+ Puckering: The thread has a pukering defect.
	+ Cracking: The thread has a cracking defect.
* **Signed Evaluation**: Write a brief note about any additional comments or information about the thread's quality.
* **Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm that you have completed the assessment.
