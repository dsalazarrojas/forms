# Surface Finish Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the surface finish of a manufacturing process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions and familiarize yourself with the form sections and fields.
2. Review the specific criteria for the surface finish rating in each section.
3. Answer each field according to the criteria and your personal observation.
4. Provide any additional comments or information about the surface finish in the "comments" field.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **surface_finish_check_1** (Text, Optional): Please rate the surface finish based on the criteria specified on the first page. You can type or select the corresponding rating from the options below.
* **surface_finish_check_2** (Text, Optional): Please rate the surface finish based on the criteria specified on the second page. You can type or select the corresponding rating from the options below.
* **defects** (Text, Optional): Please describe any defects or issues observed with the surface finish.
* **comments** (Text, Optional): Please provide any additional comments or information about the surface finish.
* **inspection_outcomes** (Text, Optional): Please describe the results of the inspection, including any issues or concerns identified.

## Tips
* Please answer each field honestly and accurately based on your observation.
* If you are unsure about a specific field, please consult with the production team or quality control specialist.
* Please keep the comments section concise and focused on the specific issues or concerns.
