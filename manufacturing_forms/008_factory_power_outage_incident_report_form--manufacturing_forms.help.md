# Factory Power Outage Incident Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Factory Power Outage Incident Report Form is used to report and document factory power outages. This form helps the factory management to gather essential information about the incident, including its date, location, and equipment affected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the incident date of the power outage.
2. Provide the location where the power outage occurred.
3. Check the incident type to indicate whether it was a planned or unplanned event.
4. Select the equipment that was affected by the power outage.
5. Enter the start and end times of the power outage.
6. If available, provide the duration of the power outage.
7. Enter your contact information (name, phone number, and email) so that we can contact you for more information.
8. Add any comments or observations about the incident.
9. Optionally, provide comments or feedback to the person assigned to the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **incident_date** (Date)
* This field is used to enter the date the power outage occurred.
* Please use the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).

### 2. **incident_location** (Text)
* Enter the location where the power outage occurred, e.g., production line, department, or specific equipment.

### 3. **incident_type** (Select)
* Choose whether the incident was a:
	+ Planned event (e.g., scheduled maintenance)
	+ Unplanned event (e.g., unexpected equipment failure)

### 4. **affected_equipment** (Select Multiple)
* Choose which equipment was affected by the power outage:
	+ Yes (affected equipment)
	+ No (not affected equipment)

### 5. **start_time** (Time)
* Enter the start time of the power outage in 24-hour format (HH:MM).

### 6. **end_time** (Time)
* Enter the end time of the power outage in 24-hour format (HH:MM).

### 7. **duration** (Number)
* If known, enter the duration of the power outage.

### 8. **reporter** (Text)
* Enter your name.

### 9. **phone** (Text)
* Enter your phone number.

### 10. **email** (Email)
* Enter your email address.

### 11. **comments** (Note)
* Add any comments or observations about the incident.

### 12. **assigned** (Text)
* This field is currently not used.

### 13. **comments_to** (Text)
* Optionally, provide comments or feedback to the person assigned to the incident.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and detailed information to ensure the incident is reported correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask a supervisor or colleague for guidance.
* This form should only be used for reporting new incidents. If you have already reported an incident, please do not fill out this form again.
