# Production Request Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help production teams gather and record necessary information for production requests, ensuring timely and accurate completion of production tasks.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Main Page**: This section provides an overview of the production request.
   * **Job Request**: Select whether the job is a 'Yes' or 'No' request.
   * **Job Requester**: Enter the name or identifier of the person or team requesting the production.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Request Date**: Enter the date of the job request.
* **Job Request Time**: Enter the time of the job request.
* **Job Description**: Provide a brief description of the job or production requirement.
* **Production Operator**: Enter the name of the production operator assigned to this job.
* **Production Machine**: Enter the machine used for production.
* **Production Date**: Enter the date of the production.
* **Production Time**: Enter the time of the production.
* **Production Status**: Choose the current status of production: 'In Progress', 'Complete', or 'Cancelled'.
* **Production Notes**: Enter any important production notes.
* **Production Attachments**: Add any relevant production attachments, if necessary.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out correctly and accurately.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid missing important information.
* Update production status as needed to reflect the current production status.
