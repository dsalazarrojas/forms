# factory-co2-emission-report-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The factory-co2-emission-report-form is a tool used by the factory to report on their CO2 emissions during a specific reporting period. The data collected from this form helps us to understand the factory's impact on the environment and identify potential areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the 'Factory details' page and enter the necessary information about the factory, such as its name, location, and other relevant details.
2.  Select the 'Reporting period' for which the emissions data is being reported.
3.  Enter the start and end dates of the reporting period.
4.  Provide the total emissions for the reporting period.
5.  Enter the details about the factory's emission standards.
6.  Outline any action plan the factory has to reduce their emissions.
7.  Enter the details about the action plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Factory details** (factory_details, text, optional): Enter the details about the factory, such as its name, location, and other relevant information.
- **Reporting period** (reporting_period, select_one, required): Select the specific reporting period for which the emissions data is being reported.
  - 1st reporting period
  - 2nd reporting period
  - 3rd reporting period
- **Reporting start date** (reporting_start_date, date, optional): Enter the date of start of the reporting period.
- **Reporting end date** (reporting_end_date, date, optional): Enter the date of end of the reporting period.
- **Emission totals** (emission_totals, number, optional): Enter the total emissions for the reporting period.
- **Emission standards** (emission_standards, text, optional): Enter the details about the factory's emission standards.
- **Action plan** (action_plan, text, optional): Outline any action plan the factory has to reduce their emissions.
- **Action plan details** (action_plan_details, text, optional): Enter the details about the action plan. 

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct reporting period and enter accurate start and end dates for the reporting period.
- Enter the correct total emissions for the reporting period.
- Enter the details about the factory's emission standards.
- Outline a clear and specific action plan to reduce emissions.
