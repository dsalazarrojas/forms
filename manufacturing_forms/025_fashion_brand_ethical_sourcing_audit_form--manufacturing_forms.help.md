# Fashion Brand Ethical Sourcing Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the ethical and sourcing practices of a fashion brand. It assesses various aspects of the brand's supplier selection, production processes, material sourcing, labor laws, environmental impacts, chemical substances, and certifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions based on your brand's actual practices and policies.
2. Provide clear and concise answers for each question.
3. Choose the relevant options for each question from the provided dropdown menus.
4. Provide additional comments or explanations if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier**: Please enter the name of your current supplier. 
* **Production Processes**: Select all the production processes your brand uses, such as Manufacturing, Sub-Contracting, Contracting, or Private Label. 
* **Material Sourcing**: Describe your current material sourcing practices. 
* **Ethical Practices**: Please provide any relevant information about your brand's ethical practices. (Type in a free-text area)
* **Labor Laws**: Choose the labor laws that apply to your brand, including Labor Standards Act, Fair Labor Association, ILO, National Labor Relations Act, or Local Labor Law. 
* **Environmental Impacts**: Select all the environmental impacts that apply to your brand, such as Yes or No. 
* **Chemical Substances**: Choose the chemical substances used in your production, such as Yes or No. 
* **Certification**: Select the certifications held by your brand, including GOTS, BSCI, WRAP, Amfori, Sedex, Fairtrade, or SA8000. 
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or explanations about your brand's practices.

## Tips
* Please answer based on your brand's actual practices and policies.
* Provide clear and concise answers for each question.
* If unsure, refer to the provided options or seek clarification from your team.
* This form is a self-assessment tool and should not be used as a substitute for external audits or certifications.
