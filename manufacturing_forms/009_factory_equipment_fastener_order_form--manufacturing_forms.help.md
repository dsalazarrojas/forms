# Factory Equipment Fastener Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the ordering process for factory equipment fasteners. It collects essential information to track the quantity and delivery details of fasteners ordered for factory equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information. The form has multiple sections, but not all fields are required.
2. Ensure you have all the necessary details before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fastener Order Form** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This is the main text field where you can enter any relevant comments or notes about the ordering process.
* **Equipment ID** (`2`, `number`, required: false): Enter the unique ID number associated with the equipment being fastened.
* **Fastener ID** (`3`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the specific type of fastener being ordered from the available options (A, B, C).
* **Quantity Ordered** (`4`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of fasteners being ordered for the equipment.
* **Quantity Received** (`5`, `number`, required: false): This field is for recording the quantity of fasteners actually received after delivery. It should be updated once the fasteners are received.
* **Delivery Date** (`6`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the fasteners are expected to be delivered.
* **Delivery Time** (`7`, `time`, required: false): Enter the specific time of day the fasteners are expected to be delivered.

## Tips
* Be accurate and precise when filling out the form to ensure correct tracking and inventory management.
* Review the form before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out.
* Update the quantity received field once the delivery is confirmed.
