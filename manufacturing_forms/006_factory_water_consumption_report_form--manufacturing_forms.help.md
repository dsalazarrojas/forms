# Factory Water Consumption Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Water Consumption Report Form is used to collect and track data on the factory's water usage. This information is crucial for factory management to make informed decisions about resource allocation and efficiency.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant options for "Water Usage" to indicate whether the factory used water during the reporting period.
2. Enter the date of the reporting period in the "Date" field.
3. Fill in the "Total Consumption" field with the total amount of water used during the period.
4. Choose the source of the water used from the options provided in the "Water Source" field.
5. Enter the quantity of water used in the "Quantity" field.
6. Rate the quality of the water used by selecting "Good" or "Fair" in the "Rate" field.
7. Enter any additional comments or notes about the water usage in the "Comments" field.
8. Fill in your email address in the "Form Submitter" field.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Water Usage** (`Water Usage`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" to indicate that the factory used water during the reporting period.
* **Date** (`factory_water_usage_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the reporting period in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Total Consumption** (`total_consumption`, number, required/optional): Enter the total amount of water used during the period.
* **Water Source** (`water_source`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the source of the water used from the options provided.
* **Quantity** (`factory_water_usage_quantity`, text, required/optional): Enter the quantity of water used.
* **Rate** (`factory_water_usage_rate`, select_one, required/optional): Rate the quality of the water used by selecting "Good" or "Fair".
* **Total Water Cost** (`total_water_cost`, number, required/optional): Not applicable for this form.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the water usage.
* **Form Submitter** (`form_submitter`, email, required/optional): Fill in your email address.
* **Phone** (`form_submitter_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.

Note: The "Total Water Cost" field is not applicable for this form as it is not a required field and has no options.
