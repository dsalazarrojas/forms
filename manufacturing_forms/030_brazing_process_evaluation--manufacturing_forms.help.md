# Brazing Process Evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Brazing Process Evaluation form is used to evaluate the brazing process in manufacturing, ensuring that the process is executed correctly and efficiently. This form captures essential information about the brazing process, including the operator's details, evaluation date and time, and various parameters of the brazing process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Operator Name and ID fields with your full name and employee or contractor ID.
2. Select the Evaluation Date and Time fields using the calendar or clock functions available.
3. Enter the Work Station and Part Number fields with the relevant information.
4. Choose the Brazing Method and Filler Material from the available options.
5. Select the Base Material that is being brazed.
6. Select the Flux Type used in the process.
7. Enter the Preheat Temperature, Brazing Temperature, and Heating Time fields with the relevant information.
8. Choose the Joint Design and Visual Inspection Result fields from the available options.
9. Rate the Joint Fill Quality and Surface Finish using the available options.
10. Describe any defects found in the Defects Found field.
11. Select the Non-Destructive Testing results, if applicable.
12. Choose the Overall Quality Status field based on the evaluation results.
13. Select the Process Parameters Met field based on whether all parameters were within specification.
14. If applicable, describe any corrective actions needed in the Corrective Actions field.
15. Enter the name of the quality inspector and sign off with your signature in the Inspector Name and Inspector Signature fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Operator Name**: Enter your full name. This field is required to identify you as the evaluator.
* **Operator ID**: Enter your employee or contractor ID. This field is required to track your association with the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Date**: Use the calendar function to select the date of evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time**: Use the clock function to select the time of evaluation.
* **Work Station**: Enter the station or location number where the brazing process was performed.
* **Part Number**: Enter the part or product being brazed.
* **Batch Number**: Enter the batch or lot number.
* **Brazing Method**: Select the type of brazing process used (e.g., Torch, Furnace, Induction, etc.).
* **Filler Material**: Choose the type of filler material used (e.g., Silver Alloy, Copper Alloy, etc.).
* **Base Material**: Select the material being brazed (e.g., Steel, Stainless Steel, etc.).
* **Flux Type**: Enter the type of flux used in the process.
* **Preheat Temperature**: Enter the temperature in degrees used for preheating.
* **Brazing Temperature**: Enter the actual brazing temperature.
* **Heating Time**: Enter the time in minutes used for brazing.
* **Joint Design**: Select the type of joint configuration (e.g., Lap, Butt, T-Joint, etc.).
* **Visual Inspection Result**: Choose the result of the visual inspection (e.g., Pass, Fail, Rework Required).
* **Joint Fill Quality**: Rate the joint fill quality using the available options (e.g., Excellent, Good, Acceptable, Poor).
* **Surface Finish**: Rate the quality of surface finish using the available options (e.g., Excellent, Good, Acceptable, Poor).
* **Defects Found**: Describe any defects found during the brazing process.
* **Non-Destructive Testing**: Select whether Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) was performed.
* **NDT Results**: Select the results of NDT if performed (e.g., Pass, Fail, Not Performed).
* **Overall Quality Status**: Choose the final quality status based on the evaluation results (e.g., Approved, Approved with Notes, Rework Required, Rejected).
* **Process Parameters Met**: Select whether all process parameters were within specification.
* **Corrective Actions**: Describe any corrective actions needed if the process parameters were not met.
* **Inspector Name**: Enter the name of the quality inspector.
* **Inspector Signature**: Sign off with your name as the quality inspector.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to ensure a comprehensive evaluation of the brazing process.
* If any defects were found, describe them clearly and concisely in the Defects Found field.
* If Non-Destructive Testing was performed, select the relevant results in the NDT Results field.
* Review the Overall Quality Status field carefully to ensure that it accurately reflects the final quality determination.
* Sign off with your name and title in the Inspector Signature field to confirm that you have evaluated the brazing process.
