# Athletic Gear Manufacturer Partnership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from manufacturers interested in partnering with us. It's essential to fill it out accurately and completely to showcase your company's background, products, and partnership objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Begin by providing your company's background information, including your manufacturer name, corporate headquarters address, and website URL.
2.  Next, select the primary product categories that your company offers.
3.  Indicate the type of partnership you're seeking.
4.  Describe your target demographic, the unique selling proposition (USP) of your products, and provide your annual production capacity.
5.  If applicable, list any quality and ethical certifications held by your company.
6.  Confirm whether you have a formal ethical sourcing commitment in place.
7.  Specify the average lead time for custom orders.
8.  Finally, provide information about your marketing budget, social media reach, and current sponsorships.
9.  Complete the contact details, including the primary partnership contact name, job title, email address, and phone number.
10.  Summarize your proposed partnership in a concise and compelling manner.
11.  Certify that the information provided is accurate and complete.
12.  Finally, enter the date of application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Manufacturer Name (2)**: This is the name of your company, which should match the name on your website and marketing materials.
*   **Corporate Headquarters Address (3)**: Enter the physical address of your company's headquarters.
*   **Website URL (4)**: Provide the URL of your company's website, which should be a live link.
*   **Year Established (5)**: Enter the year your company was founded.
*   **Primary Product Categories (6)**: Select all that apply from the listed categories: Athletic Footwear, High-Performance Apparel, Protective Gear and Safety, Sports Equipment (Hard Goods), and Training Accessories.
*   **Type of Partnership Sought (8)**: Choose from the options: Official Team / Program Supplier, Retail Distribution and Wholesale, Co-Branded Design Collaboration, Material Research and Field Testing, and Strategic Sponsorship.
*   **Target Demographic (9)**: Describe the demographic you're targeting with your products, e.g., elite professional athletes, youth academies, fitness enthusiasts.
*   **Unique Selling Proposition (USP) of your Products (10)**: Explain what differentiates your products from competitors.
*   **Annual Production Capacity - Total Units (12)**: Enter your company's annual production capacity.
*   **Quality and Ethical Certifications Held (13)**: Select all that apply from the listed certifications: ISO 9001, OEKO-TEX Standard 100, Fair Trade Certified, BlueSign System Partner, WRAP Certification.
*   **Formal Ethical Sourcing Commitment (14)**: Confirm whether you have a formal commitment in place.
*   **Average Lead Time for Custom Orders (15)**: Specify the average time for custom orders in weeks.
*   **Current Major Sponsorships or High-Profile Partners (17)**: List any current major sponsorships or high-profile partners.
*   **Annual Marketing Budget for Partnerships (18)**: Estimated in USD.
*   **Total Social Media Reach - Combined Followers (19)**: Approximate count.
*   **Primary Partnership Contact Name (21)**: Enter the name of your primary partnership contact.
*   **Contact Job Title (22)**: Enter your contact's job title.
*   **Contact Email Address (23)**: Enter your contact email address.
*   **Contact Phone Number (24)**: Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Executive Summary of Partnership Proposal (25)**: Briefly describe the proposed collaboration.
*   **I Certify that all information provided is accurate and complete (27)**: Select from the options: I certify, Other.
*   **Date of Application (28)**: Enter the date of application.

Please note that some fields may not be applicable to your company, and you're free to fill them out as needed. If you have any questions or need clarification, don't hesitate to reach out to us for support.
