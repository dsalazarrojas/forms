# Manufacturing Worker Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manufacturing Worker Employment Form is used to collect information for manufacturing workers.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please fill out each field accurately and completely to ensure that your information is up to date and correct.
* If you're unsure about any field, please consult with your supervisor or HR representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info**: Please enter your contact information, including your email and phone number.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **Personal Details**: Enter your personal details, such as your full name and any other relevant information.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **Employment History**: Briefly describe your previous work experience in the manufacturing industry.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **Employment Status**: Indicate your current employment status, such as whether you're currently employed or unemployed.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **Education**: Enter the name of your educational institution and your level of education (e.g., High School, College, University).
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **References**: List any relevant references or professionals who can vouch for your work.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **Job Applications**: Enter the number of job applications you've made in the past year.
	+ Type: Number
	+ Required: No
* **Job Match Status**: Select whether you've been matched with a job opportunity.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: No
* **Job Match Comment**: Add any additional comments about the job match.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **Education Level**: Enter your level of education, such as High School, College, University, etc.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **Education Institution**: Enter the name of your educational institution.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **Job Applications Count**: Enter the number of job applications you've made in the past year.
	+ Type: Number
	+ Required: No
* **Job Start Date**: Enter the date you started your current or previous job.
	+ Type: Date
	+ Required: No
* **Job End Date**: Enter the date your current or previous job ended.
	+ Type: Date
	+ Required: No
* **Job Match Date**: Enter the date you were matched with a job opportunity.
	+ Type: Date
	+ Required: No
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you submit this form.
	+ Type: Date
	+ Required: No
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time you submit this form.
	+ Type: Time
	+ Required: No
* **Shift Preferences**: Select your preferred shifts (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening).
	+ Type: Select Multiple
	+ Required: No
* **Job Match Status**: Select the status of your job match.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: No
* **Job Match Comment**: Add any additional comments about your job match.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
	+ Type: Email
	+ Required: No
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No

Note: The form is not required to be filled out completely, but it's recommended to fill out as much information as possible to get a better match.
