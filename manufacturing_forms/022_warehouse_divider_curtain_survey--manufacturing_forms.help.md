# Warehouse Divider Curtain Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about warehouse divider curtains, specifically their type, dimensions, performance, and personnel involved in their installation, maintenance, and inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields marked with an exclamation mark.
2. Select the appropriate type of divider curtain from the options provided under "divider Curtain Type".
3. Enter the dimensions of the divider curtain (number of divider curtains and curtain width).
4. Rate the performance of the divider curtain.
5. Select the personnel responsible for the installation, maintenance, and inspection of the divider curtain.
6. Select the sections where the divider curtain is installed.
7. Ensure that all fields are accurately and completely filled in before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **divider Curtain Type** (`divider_curtain_type`, required, select_one): This field is for selecting the type of divider curtain to be used, which can be 3-Pin, 4-Pin, or 6-Pin.
* **Number of divider curtains** (`number_of_divider_curtains`, required, number): Enter the number of divider curtains to be installed.
* **Curtain height** (`curtain_height`, optional, number): This field is for entering the height of the divider curtain.
* **Curtain width** (`curtain_width`, required, number): Enter the width of the divider curtain.
* **divider curtain performance** (`divider_curtain_performance`, required, select_multiple): Rate the performance of the divider curtain, which can be Very Good, Good, Fair, or Not Good.
* **comments** (`comments`, optional, note): Enter any additional comments about the divider curtain.
* **date** (`date`, optional, date): Enter the date when the survey was completed.
* **time** (`time`, optional, time): Enter the time when the survey was completed.

* **phone** (`phone`, optional, text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
