# Raw Material Utilization Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on the utilization of raw materials in the manufacturing process. It helps to monitor and analyze the usage of materials, identify areas for improvement, and inform decision-making on raw material procurement and management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the "Manufacturing Team" question with either "Yes" or "No" to indicate if your team is responsible for manufacturing.
2. Enter the raw material used in the "Raw Material Used" field.
3. Select the "Material Type" to indicate if the material is used or not.
4. Enter the wastage percentage in the "Wastage Percentage" field, with the '%' symbol.
5. Describe the production details in the "Production Details" field.
6. Select a date for the report in the "Date" field using the YYYY-MM-DD format.
7. Enter the time of the report in the "Time" field using the HH:MM format.
8. Describe the manufacturing process in the "Manufacturing Process" field.
9. Enter any remarks or comments in the "Remarks" field.
10. Finally, confirm that the form is accurate and complete by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the "Raw Material Utilization Report Form" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Manufacturing Team**: Select "Yes" if you are the manufacturing team responsible for the production process.
* **Raw Material Used**: Enter the raw material used during the production process.
* **Material Type**: Select "Yes" if the material is used, and "No" if not.
* **Wastage Percentage**: Enter the percentage of wastage, with the '%' symbol.
* **Production Details**: Describe the production details, including any relevant information about the production process.
* **Date**: Select a date for the report in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time**: Select a time for the report in the HH:MM format.
* **Manufacturing Process**: Describe the manufacturing process used.
* **Remarks**: Enter any additional comments or remarks about the report.
* **Raw Material Utilization Report Form**: Confirm that the form is accurate and complete by selecting "Yes" or "No".
