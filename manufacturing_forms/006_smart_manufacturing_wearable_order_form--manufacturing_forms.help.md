# Smart Manufacturing Wearable Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for ordering smart manufacturing wearables from our company. Please fill out this form to provide us with the necessary details about your company and delivery requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your company details, including any relevant information about your business.
2. Enter your delivery information, including any specific delivery dates or times.
3. Select the wearables you are interested in ordering from the options provided.
4. Add any additional company information if necessary.
5. Provide a delivery date and time.
6. Enter your contact phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company Details** (`company_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your company.
- **Delivery Info** (`delivery_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the delivery address or any specific instructions for delivering your order.
- **Wearable Selections** (`wearable_selections`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the wearables you would like to order from the options provided. This field allows you to choose multiple wearables.
- **Additional Company Info** (`company_details_2`, `number`, required: false): If you have any additional information about your company, please enter it here.
- **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you would like to receive your order.
- **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you would like to receive your order.
- **Contact Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact phone number for follow-up and communication purposes.

## Tips
Please make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid any delays or errors in processing your order. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
