# Fermentation Tank Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track information about fermentation tanks in a manufacturing process. It is designed to help inspectors gather and report data about tank conditions, temperatures, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the tank ID, tank name, and tank type fields to identify the tank being inspected.
2. Enter the inspection date and time.
3. Record the last cleaned date and the next scheduled cleaning date.
4. Record the tank's status (Active, Inactive, or Offline).
5. Enter the temperature reading and PH reading.
6. Record the volume level and tank capacity.
7. Report the last inspection result (Pass, Fail, or N/A).
8. Record who last inspected the tank and when.
9. Enter the next scheduled inspection date.
10. Record the tank's condition (Good, Poor, or N/A).
10. Add any additional comments or notes.
11. Record the tank's size, depth, width, and length.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tank ID** (1, number, required): Enter a unique identifier for the tank being inspected.
* **Tank Name** (2, text, required): Enter the name of the tank being inspected.
* **Tank Type** (3, select_one, required): Select the type of tank being inspected (Fermentation, Aging, or Storage).
* **Inspection Date** (4, date, required): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Last Cleaned** (5, date, required): Enter the date the tank was last cleaned.
* **Next Cleaning Due** (6, date, required): Enter the date the tank is next due for cleaning.
* **Comments** (7, note, required): Add any comments or notes about the tank's condition.
* **Assigned To** (8, text, required): Enter the name of the person assigned to inspect the tank.
* **Tank Status** (9, select_one, required): Select the status of the tank (Active, Inactive, or Offline).
* **Temperature Reading** (10, number, required): Enter the tank's temperature reading.
* **PH Reading** (11, number, required): Enter the tank's PH reading.
* **Volume Level** (12, text, required): Enter the tank's volume level.
* **Tank Capacity** (13, number, required): Enter the tank's capacity.
* **Last Inspection Result** (14, select_one, required): Report the result of the last inspection (Pass, Fail, or N/A).
* **Last Inspection By** (15, text, required): Enter the name of the person who last inspected the tank.
* **Next Inspection Due** (16, date, required): Enter the date the tank is next due for inspection.
* **Last Cleaning Date** (17, date, required): Enter the date the tank was last cleaned.
* **Next Cleaning Due** (18, date, required): Enter the date the tank is next due for cleaning.
* **Assigned By** (19, text, required): Enter the name of the person who assigned the inspection.
* **Tank Condition** (20, select_one, required): Report the condition of the tank (Good, Poor, or N/A).
* **Notes** (21, note, required): Add any additional comments or notes about the tank's condition.
* **Tank Size** (22, number, required): Enter the tank's size.
* **Tank Depth** (23, number, required): Enter the tank's depth.
* **Tank Width** (24, number, required): Enter the tank's width.
* **Tank Length** (25, number, required): Enter the tank's length.
