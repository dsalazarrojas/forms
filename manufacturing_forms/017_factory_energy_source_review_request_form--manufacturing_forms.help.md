# Factory Energy Source Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Energy Source Review Request Form is used to collect information about the current and planned energy usage and sources of the factory. This form helps factory management to track and analyze energy usage and make informed decisions about energy efficiency and sustainability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the General Information page with your factory's basic details.
2. Answer the questions on the Current Energy Use page, including your current energy usage in numbers.
3. Describe the planned changes to your energy sources on the Planned Changes page.
4. Choose your sustainability initiatives on the Sustainability Initiatives page.
5. Select your energy source on the Energy Source page.
6. Choose your energy type on the Energy Type page.
7. Select your energy source purchased on the Energy Source Purchased page.
8. Select your energy source on-site on the Energy Source On-Site page.
9. Add any additional comments on the Notes page.
10. Assign a user to review this form on the Assigned User page.
11. Select the user who submitted this form on the Submitter page.
12. Enter the date when you submitted this form on the Date Submitted page.
13. Enter the date of the last update on the Date Last Update page.
14. Select the user who last updated this form on the Updated By page.
15. Enter the date of the last update on the Updated Date page.
16. Select the form name on the Form Name page.
17. Enter your factory's energy usage in numbers on the Factory Energy Use page.
18. Select your factory's energy source on the Factory Energy Source page.
19. Choose your factory's sustainability initiatives on the Sustainability Initiatives page.
