# Equipment Retrofitting Initiative Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register equipment for potential retrofitting or upgrading. By answering the questions in this form, users can help identify which equipment can benefit from retrofitting and which cannot.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form fields carefully to ensure you are answering the questions accurately.
2. Select the correct answer for each field based on your knowledge of the equipment.
3. Complete all required fields to ensure that your registration is processed correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment details** (`equipment_details`, select_one, required: false): Answer "Yes" if the equipment is currently in use or has a high likelihood of being used in the near future. Answer "No" if the equipment is not in use or unlikely to be used in the near future.
* **Equipment model** (`equipment_model`, text, required: false): Enter the model number or name of the equipment you are registering.
* **Retrofitting process** (`retrofitting_process`, select_one, required: false): Answer "Yes" if retrofitting is necessary for the equipment to continue functioning properly. Answer "No" if no retrofitting is required.
* **Equipment description** (`equipment_description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the equipment, including its current state and any relevant details that may be useful for the retrofitting process.
* **Retrofitting initiatives** (`retrofitting_initiatives`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all initiatives that apply to the equipment you are registering. These initiatives represent different areas of focus for the retrofitting process, such as performance improvement, cost reduction, or environmental impact. 

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields accurately to ensure that your registration is processed correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant team or department for guidance.
* This form is a crucial step in the equipment retrofitting process, and your input will help inform decisions about which equipment to prioritize for modification or upgrade.
