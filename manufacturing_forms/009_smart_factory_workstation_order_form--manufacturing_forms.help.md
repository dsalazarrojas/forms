# Smart Factory Workstation Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture the necessary information for ordering workstation equipment in the Smart Factory. It guides users through the process of requesting and ordering workstations and associated equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of workstation you are requesting (Workstation 1, Workstation 2, or Workstation 3) in the "Workstation Request" field.
2. Choose the specific workstation model you require from the "Workstation Model" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the serial number of the workstation in the "Workstation Serial" field.
4. Enter the location where the workstation will be installed in the "Workstation Location" field.
5. Select the installation date for the workstation in the "Installation Date" field.
6. Choose the installation time for the workstation in the "Installation Time" field.
7. Enter any additional comments or notes for the workstation installation in the "Comments" field.
8. Provide your contact information, including your email, phone number, and company name.
9. Select the department and company where the workstation will be used.
10. Finally, submit the form to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workstation Request** (`<name>`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of workstation you are requesting.
* **Request Type** (`<name>`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select any additional requests or features you require for the workstation.
* **Workstation Location** (`<name>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the location where the workstation will be installed.
* **Installation Date** (`<name>`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when the workstation will be installed.
* **Installation Time** (`<name>`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time when the workstation will be installed.
* **Comments** (`<name>`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes for the workstation installation.
* **Email** (`<name>`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`<name>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Company** (`<name>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **Department** (`<name>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the department where the workstation will be used.
* **Contact** (`<name>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name and title.
* **Workstation Model** (`<name>`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the specific workstation model you require.
* **Workstation Serial** (`<name>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the serial number of the workstation.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure you have the necessary permissions to place workstation orders.
- If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to factory management.
