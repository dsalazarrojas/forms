# Manufacturing Process Partner Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for potential partners in the manufacturing industry to provide their information and initiate or continue a business relationship. It's essential to ensure that the form is filled out accurately and completely to facilitate efficient processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields marked as "required".
2. Enter your partner's name in the "Partner Name" field.
3. Provide the contact person's name and details in the "Contact Person" field.
4. Enter your business name in the "Business Name" field.
5. Enter your manufacturing process in the "Manufacturing Process" field (optional).
6. Fill out the "Address" field with your location.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
8. Enter your email in the "Email" field.
9. Provide the date and time of your last contact with the partner in the "Date of Last Contact" and "Time of Last Contact" fields.
10. Select your preferred communication method from the options in the "Preferred Communication Method" field.
11. Choose your manufacturing capabilities from the list in the "Manufacturing Capabilities" field.
12. Add any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, required): Enter the name of the partner company or organization.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, required): Enter the name and details of the contact person.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, required): Enter the name of your business or organization.
* **Manufacturing Process** (`manufacturing_process`, optional): If applicable, describe the manufacturing process you offer.
* **Address** (`address`, required): Enter your business or organization's address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Date of Last Contact** (`date_of_last_contact`, required): Enter the date and time of your last contact with the partner.
* **Time of Last Contact** (`time_of_last_contact`, required): Enter the time of your last contact with the partner.
* **Preferred Communication Method** (`preferred_communication_method`, required): Choose how you would like to communicate with the partner (Phone, Email, Chat, Meeting, or Email and Phone).
* **Manufacturing Capabilities** (`manufacturing_capabilities`, required): Select all applicable manufacturing capabilities from the list (CNC Machining, 3D Printing, CNC Routing, Laser Cutting, Welding, Assembly, Surface Finishing, Other).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your business or partnership.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Be specific and clear when describing your manufacturing process and capabilities.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the form's guidelines above.
* If you have any further questions or need assistance, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
