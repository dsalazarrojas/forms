# Product Acceptance Testing Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Acceptance Testing Report Form is used to record the testing results and decisions made during the product testing process. This form helps in capturing the testing outcomes, issues encountered, and the final decision to accept or reject the product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `test_criteria` field with a brief description of the testing criteria used.
2. Provide a detailed description of the `test_results` field, including any positive or negative findings.
3. If any issues were encountered during testing, document them in the `issues_encountered` field.
4. Select the `final_decision` field to indicate whether the product meets the testing criteria (Accept), does not meet the criteria (Reject), or is not applicable (Not Applicable).
5. Enter the date and time the testing was completed in the `date_completed` and `time_completed` fields, respectively.
6. If any comments or notes are relevant to the testing process, add them in the `comments` field.
7. If any issues were noted during the testing process, provide a detailed description in the `issue_description` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **test_criteria** (Text): A brief description of the testing criteria used.
  - This field is required to ensure that the testing process is aligned with the product requirements.
- **test_results** (Text): A detailed description of the testing results, including any positive or negative findings.
  - This field helps in capturing the overall outcome of the testing process.
- **issues_encountered** (Text): A description of any issues encountered during the testing process.
  - This field is useful for identifying and addressing potential product defects.
- **final_decision** (Select One): Select whether the product meets the testing criteria (Accept), does not meet the criteria (Reject), or is not applicable (Not Applicable).
  - This field is crucial in determining the product's readiness for release or further development.
- **date_completed** (Date): The date the testing was completed.
  - This field is essential for tracking the testing schedule and progress.
- **time_completed** (Time): The time the testing was completed.
  - This field is useful for recording the exact time the testing was finished.
- **comments** (Note): Any additional comments or notes relevant to the testing process.
  - This field is useful for capturing any additional information that may not be included in the other fields.
- **issue_description** (Text): A detailed description of any issues encountered during the testing process.
  - This field is useful for documenting and addressing product defects.
## Tips

* Ensure that the testing criteria and results are clearly documented to facilitate accurate decision-making.
* Be thorough in describing any issues encountered to ensure that they are properly addressed.
* Select the `final_decision` field carefully, as it directly affects the product's readiness for release or further development.
