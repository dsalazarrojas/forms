# Manufacturing Line Shutdown Notice Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a shutdown of a manufacturing line, allowing production to be temporarily halted for maintenance, upgrades, or other reasons. It helps in keeping all parties informed about the planned downtime and facilitating a smooth transition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Manufacturing Line Shutdown Request** field with a brief description of the reason for the shutdown.
2. Enter the **Start Time** and **End Time** of the shutdown.
3. Select the relevant **Manufacturing Line** from the dropdown list.
4. Choose the **Manager Signature** and enter their email address.
5. Optionally, add any **Comments** or **Notes** to provide further context.
6. Enter the **Manager Email** and **Manager Phone** for contact information.
7. Choose the **Approver** and enter their email address.
8. Optionally, enter the **Approver Phone**.
9. Check the **Manager Note** field to indicate if the manager has taken any note.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Manufacturing Line Shutdown Request**: Briefly describe the reason for the shutdown.
* **Start Time**: Enter the time when the manufacturing line will be shut down. Format is `HH:MM:SS` and use 24-hour time format.
* **End Time**: Enter the time when the manufacturing line will be back online. Format is `HH:MM:SS` and use 24-hour time format.
* **Reason**: Explain the reason for the shutdown in a text form.
* **Manufacturing Line**: Select the specific manufacturing line to be shut down from the dropdown list.
* **Manager Signature**: Choose the manager responsible for the shutdown.
* **Manager Email**: Enter the email address of the manager responsible for the shutdown.
* **Manager Phone**: Enter the phone number of the manager responsible for the shutdown.
* **Manager Note**: Check if the manager has taken any note.
* **Approver**: Choose the approver for the shutdown.
* **Approver Email**: Enter the email address of the approver.
* **Approver Phone**: Enter the phone number of the approver.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the shutdown.
