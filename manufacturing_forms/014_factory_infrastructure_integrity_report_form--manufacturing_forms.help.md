# Factory Infrastructure Integrity Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Factory Infrastructure Integrity Report Form is used to report on the current state of a factory's infrastructure. This form is meant to be completed by users who have access to the factory's equipment and facilities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user input in the "User Input" field. This is a free-text field where you can provide any additional information about the factory's infrastructure.
2. Select the date and time of the inspection from the "Date and Time" field.
3. Enter the unique ID of the factory in the "Factory ID" field. This is a required field.
4. Enter the ID of the equipment being inspected in the "Equipment ID" field. This is a required field.
5. Select the current condition of the equipment in the "Equipment Condition" field. The options are "Good", "Fair", and "Poor".
6. Enter the date of the inspection in the "Inspection Date" field. This is a required field.
7. If necessary, enter any additional remarks about the equipment's condition in the "Remarks" field.
8. Enter your name as the person submitting the form in the "Submitted By" field. This is a required field.
9. Provide a signature to confirm that the form is being submitted accurately and truthfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input (User Input, text, optional):** Enter any additional information about the factory's infrastructure.
* **Date and Time (Date and Time, date, optional):** The date and time of the inspection.
* **Factory ID (Factory ID, number, required):** The unique ID of the factory.
* **Equipment ID (Equipment ID, number, required):** The ID of the equipment being inspected.
* **Location (Location, text, optional):** The location where the equipment is situated.
* **Equipment Condition (Equipment Condition, select one, required):** The current condition of the equipment. Choose from "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Inspection Date (Inspection Date, date, required):** The date of the inspection.
* **Remarks (Remarks, text, optional):** Any additional comments about the equipment's condition.
* **Submitted By (Submitted By, text, required):** The name of the person submitting the form.
* **Signature (Signature, text, required):** A digital signature to confirm the form is being submitted accurately and truthfully.
