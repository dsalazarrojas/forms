# Textile Quality Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Textile Quality Inspection Form is used to document the results of textile product inspections. The form captures information about the supplier, product, inspection results, and quality rating.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure that the supplier's name and address are filled in fields 1 and 2.
2. Enter the product type (field 3) and description (field 4) accurately.
3. Enter the material type (field 5), color code (field 6), size label (field 7), and color sample (field 8) as per the product information.
4. Document any defects observed during the inspection in fields 9 and 10.
5. Describe the actions required and taken to address the defects in fields 11 and 12.
6. Evaluate the quality rating of the product in field 14.
7. Record the completion date and time of the inspection in fields 15 and 16.
8. Identify your name and title in field 17.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Name** (field 1): Enter the name of the supplier of the textile product.
* **Supplier Address** (field 2): Enter the address of the supplier.
* **Product Type** (field 3): Enter the type of textile product (e.g., fabric, yarn, etc.).
* **Product Description** (field 4): Enter a brief description of the product being inspected.
* **Material Type** (field 5): Enter the type of material used to make the product.
* **Color Code** (field 6): Enter the color code of the product (e.g., Pantone number, etc.).
* **Size Label** (field 7): Enter the size label of the product (e.g., S, M, L, etc.).
* **Color Sample** (field 8): Enter any relevant information about the color sample provided with the product.
* **Defects Observed** (field 9): List any defects or imperfections observed during the inspection.
* **Defects Description** (field 10): Provide a detailed description of each defect.
* **Action Required** (field 11): Describe the actions required to address the defects.
* **Action Taken** (field 12): Describe the actions taken to address the defects.
* **Next Action Plan** (field 13): Plan the next steps to be taken to further improve quality.
* **Quality Rating** (field 14): Evaluate the quality of the product based on the inspection results (scale of 1-10).
* **Date Completed** (field 15): Enter the date on which the inspection was completed.
* **Time Completed** (field 16): Enter the time on which the inspection was completed.
* **Inspector Name** (field 17): Enter your name as the inspector.
