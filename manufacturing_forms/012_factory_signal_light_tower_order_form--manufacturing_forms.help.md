# Factory Signal Light Tower Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for placing an order for a factory signal light tower. You will be asked to provide contact details, tower specifics, and order information to process your request.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Please make sure you are logged in** to access this form.
- **Fill in the form completely and accurately**. If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for clarification.
- Click the "Submit" button to send the form. Our team will review and process your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (1): Enter your name so we can address correspondence to you.
* **Email Address** (2): Enter your email address so we can keep you informed about your order.
* **Tower Type** (3): Select the type of tower you are interested in purchasing (Yes or No).
* **Tower Size** (4): Select the size of the tower you are interested in purchasing. Please note that size options are limited to what we can produce.
* **Material Type** (5): Select the materials you are interested in using for the tower (e.g. stainless steel or aluminum). Please note that material options are limited to what we can produce.
* **Color** (6): Select the color of the tower you are interested in purchasing (Yes or No).
* **Delivery Date** (7): Enter the date you would like to receive your order by. Please note that delivery dates are not guaranteed and may vary depending on our production schedule.
* **Delivery Time** (8): Enter the time of day you would like to receive your order (optional).
* **Special Instructions** (9): If you have any special requests or requirements for your order, enter them here.
* **Order Quantity** (10): Enter the number of towers you would like to purchase. Please make sure this aligns with your actual needs.
* **Order Date** (11): Enter the date you would like to receive your order by. Please note that this will not affect the order processing, but rather provide an additional reference for your records.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for clarification.
- Our team will review and process your order once submitted. If we have any questions or need additional information, we will contact you via your provided email address.
