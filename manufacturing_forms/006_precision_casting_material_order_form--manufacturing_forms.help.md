# precision_casting_material_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "precision_casting_material_order_form" form is designed to collect information about customer orders for precision casting materials. This form aims to gather essential details to ensure accurate and timely deliveries.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you have all the required information.
2. Enter your name and email address in the "customer_info" field.
3. Provide details about the order you would like to place in the "delivery_details" field.
4. Enter the date you would like to receive the delivery in the "delivery_date" field.
5. Select the material you would like to order from the "material_selection" field.
6. Enter the date you would like to place the order in the "order_date" field.
7. Describe your production plan and any specific requirements in the "production_plan" field.
8. Leave any additional comments or notes about your order in the "comments" field.
9. Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the order in the "contact_person" field.
10. Enter the email address of the contact person in the "contact_email" field.
11. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the "contact_phone" field.
12. Provide the delivery address in the "delivery_address" field.
13. Enter the city for the delivery address in the "delivery_city" field.
14. Enter the zip code for the delivery address in the "delivery_zip" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_info** (`customer_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and email address here.
* **delivery_details** (`delivery_details`, text, required/optional): Describe the order you would like to place, including any specific requirements or instructions.
* **delivery_date** (`delivery_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to receive the delivery.
* **material_selection** (`material_selection`, select_one, required/optional): Select the material you would like to order from the available options.
* **order_date** (`order_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to place the order.
* **production_plan** (`production_plan`, text, required/optional): Describe your production plan and any specific requirements.
* **comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Leave any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **contact_person** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the order.
* **contact_email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **contact_phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **delivery_address** (`delivery_address`, text, required/optional): Provide the delivery address.
* **delivery_city** (`delivery_city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city for the delivery address.
* **delivery_zip** (`delivery_zip`, text, required/optional): Enter the zip code for the delivery address.

This form requires attention to detail, so please fill it out carefully and accurately. If you have any questions, feel free to contact us.

Please note that some fields may be optional, but we recommend filling out as much information as possible to ensure the smooth execution of your order.
