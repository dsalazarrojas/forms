# Metals Quality Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Metals Quality Assessment Survey is a form used to collect information related to metals quality assessment for samples. This form is used to gather details about each sample, including its quality assessment, condition, shape, surface, material, location, and result.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. For select fields, choose one or more options that apply to the sample.
3. Enter any additional comments or notes in the note field.
4. Upload any relevant images for the sample.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sample Type** (`sample_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of sample (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Sample Condition** (`sample_condition`, select_multiple, required): Choose all applicable conditions for the sample (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Sample Shape** (`sample_shape`, select_one, required): Choose the shape of the sample (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Sample Surface** (`sample_surface`, select_multiple, required): Choose all applicable surfaces for the sample (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Sample Material** (`sample_material`, select_multiple, required): Choose all applicable materials for the sample (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Sample Location** (`sample_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the sample was collected.
* **Sample Status** (`sample_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of the sample (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Sample Result** (`sample_result`, number, required): Enter the result of the quality assessment.
* **Sample Result Status** (`sample_result_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of the sample result (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Sample Result Comments** (`sample_result_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments or notes about the sample result.
* **Sample Sample Number** (`sample_number`, text, required): Enter the sample number.
* **Sample Date** (`sample_date`, date, required): Enter the date the sample was collected.
* **Sample Weight** (`sample_weight`, number, optional): Enter the weight of the sample.
* **Sample Image** (`sample_image`, text, optional): Upload an image of the sample.
* **Sample Email** (`sample_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person who collected the sample.
* **Sample Phone** (`sample_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person who collected the sample.
* **Sample Comments** (`sample_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the sample.
* **Sample Result Image** (`sample_result_image`, text, optional): Upload an image of the sample result.
* **Sample Result Phone** (`sample_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person who collected the sample result.
* **Sample Result Email** (`sample_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person who collected the sample result.
* **Sample Result Notes** (`sample_result_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the sample result.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Be as detailed as possible when entering information about the sample.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or the sample collector for guidance.
