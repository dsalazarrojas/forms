# project_change_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The project_change_order_form is designed to capture and document project changes, including project details, change requests, and status updates. It helps project team members and stakeholders communicate and track project changes effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the project_change_details field with a brief description of the project change.
2. In the change_details field, provide a detailed explanation of the change request.
3. Add any relevant documents to the documents field for easy reference.
4. Enter your electronic signature in the e_signature field.
5. Select the project_change_date of the change.
6. Choose the status of the change request from the request_status field.
7. Select the person or people who requested the change from the requested_by field.
8. Enter the date when the change request was made in the change_request_date field.
9. Provide the name and contact information of the person who requested the change in the requestor field.
10. Choose the status of the project from the project_status field.
11. Enter the email address of the person who requested the change in the requestor_email field.
12. Select the date when the project status was last updated in the project_status_date field.
13. Enter the phone number of the person who requested the change in the requestor_phone field.
14. Provide the phone number of the person who requested the change in the change_requester_phone field.
15. Finally, select the status of the project_change_order_form from the project_change_order_form_status field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **project_change_details** (`project_change_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the project change.
* **change_details** (`change_details`, text, optional): Provide a detailed explanation of the change request.
* **documents** (`documents`, text, optional): Add any relevant documents to the form for easy reference.
* **e_signature** (`e_signature`, text, optional): Enter your electronic signature in this field.
* **project_change_date** (`project_change_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the project change.
* **change_request** (`change_request`, text, optional): This field is for additional comments or notes about the change request.
* **request_status** (`request_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of the change request from the provided options.
* **requested_by** (`requested_by`, select_multiple, optional): Select the person or people who requested the change.
* **change_request_date** (`change_request_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the change request was made.
* **requestor** (`requestor`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of the person who requested the change.
* **change_requester** (`change_requester`, text, optional): This field is for additional comments or notes about the person who requested the change.
* **project_status** (`project_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of the project from the provided options.
* **requestor_email** (`requestor_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person who requested the change.
* **project_status_date** (`project_status_date`, date, optional): Select the date when the project status was last updated.
* **requestor_phone** (`requestor_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person who requested the change.
* **change_requester_phone** (`change_requester_phone`, text, optional): This field is for additional comments or notes about the person who requested the change.
* **project_change_order_form_status** (`project_change_order_form_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of the project_change_order_form from the provided options.

Note: Some fields are marked as optional, indicating that they are not required but can be filled in for additional context and information. Please fill in only the necessary fields based on your specific needs and requirements.
