# Manufacturer Customer Support Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide a channel for customers to contact the manufacturer's customer support team. It is used to submit queries, issues, or concerns that require support from the manufacturer's team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information (Contact Information field) so that our support team can reach out to you.
2. Specify the product that you are inquiring about (Product Information field).
3. Describe your support request (Support Request field).
4. If you want to be contacted by email, please enter your email address in the Email field.
5. If you prefer to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number in the Phone field.
6. Provide a detailed description of your issue or concern (Description field).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information**: <i class="fas fa-building"></i> (text)
	+ This field is where you enter your name and contact details so that our support team can reach out to you.
* **Product Information**: <i class="fas fa-box"></i> (text)
	+ In this field, please specify the product that you are inquiring about. This will help our support team to quickly identify the product and resolve your query.
* **Support Request**: <i class="fas fa-exclamation-circle"></i> (text)
	+ This field is where you describe your support request, issue, or concern in as much detail as possible.
* **Email**: <i class="fas fa-envelope"></i> (email)
	+ If you want to be contacted by email, please enter your email address here.
* **Phone**: <i class="fas fa-phone"></i> (text)
	+ If you prefer to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number here.
* **Description**: <i class="fas fa-file-alt"></i> (text)
	+ In this field, please provide a detailed description of your issue or concern. This will help our support team to quickly understand the problem and provide an efficient resolution.

## Tips
* Please fill in the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your support request is resolved efficiently.
* If you have any attachments or documents related to your support request, please attach them to the form.
* If you have any additional information or context that may help our support team in resolving your issue, please provide it in the description field.
* Our support team will contact you within 24-48 hours to discuss your support request.
