# Material Storage Condition Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to review and assess the condition of stored materials, ensuring that they are properly stored and maintained. The goal is to track and monitor the status of material storage, making it easier to identify any issues or concerns that may arise.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select the submitter**: Choose who is submitting the review request, which can be the lab, warehouse, manufacturing, or user.
2. **Enter the email of the submitter**: Provide the email address of the person submitting the review request.
3. **Choose the facility**: Select the location where the material is stored, which can be the lab, warehouse, or manufacturing department.
4. **Enter the location within the facility**: Provide the specific location within the selected facility where the material is stored.
5. **Select the storage type**: Choose the type of storage for the material, which can be refrigerated, ambient, or freezer storage.
6. **Assess the storage condition**: Evaluate the condition of the stored material by selecting good, fair, or poor.
7. **Determine the urgency level**: Choose the urgency level of the review, which can be low, medium, or high.
8. **Provide supporting documents**: Add any relevant documents or information that may support the review request.
9. **Enter comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the material storage condition.
10. **Select the reviewer**: Choose who will be reviewing the material, which can be the lab, warehouse, or manufacturing department.
11. **Enter the reviewer's email**: Provide the email address of the reviewer.
12. **Enter reviewer comments**: Add any comments or notes from the reviewer.
13. **Schedule the review date and time**: Set the date and time for the review.
14. **Set the review status**: Select the status of the review, which can be not started, in progress, or completed.
15. **Enter the reviewer's name**: Provide the name of the reviewer.
16. **Enter the reviewer's location**: Provide the location of the reviewer.
17. **Enter the storage capacity**: Provide the storage capacity of the material.
18. **Enter the time spent reviewing**: Enter the time spent reviewing the material.
19. **Enter the time spent reviewing out**: Enter the time spent reviewing out.
20. **Enter the total review time**: Enter the total time spent reviewing the material.
21. **Enter the reviewer's phone number**: Provide the phone number of the reviewer.

* **Reviewer Email**: Provide the email address of the reviewer. (Optional)

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, providing all required information.
* Check the form for any errors or missing fields before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the form's instructions or contact the relevant department for clarification.
* Ensure that you are not duplicating an existing review, as it will not be necessary and may cause confusion.
