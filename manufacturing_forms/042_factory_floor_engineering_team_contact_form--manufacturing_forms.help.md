# Factory Floor Engineering Team Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by the Factory Floor Engineering Team to collect and manage information from team members, including their first name, last name, email, phone number, issue or concern, priority, category, assigned tool, assigned group, description, created date, and updated date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Fill in your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email** field. Please use a valid email address that you can be reached at.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Describe the issue or concern you have in the **Issue** field.
6. Select your **Priority** level, which can be either **Yes** or **No**.
7. Select your **Category**, which can be either **Yes** or **No**.
8. Select if a tool is **Yes** or **No** for the **Assigned Tool** field.
9. Select one or more groups from the list for the **Assigned Group** field.
10. Provide a detailed **Description** of your issue or concern.
11. Select the date when the issue was **Created**.
12. Select the date when the issue was **Updated**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that you can be reached at.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Issue** (text, required/optional): Describe the issue or concern you have.
* **Priority** (select one, required/optional): Select **Yes** or **No** for the priority level.
* **Category** (select multiple, required/optional): Select **Yes** or **No** for the category.
* **Assigned Tool** (select one, required/optional): Select **Yes** or **No** to indicate if a tool is assigned to you.
* **Assigned Group** (select multiple, required/optional): Select one or more groups from the list.
* **Description** (text, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of your issue or concern.
* **Created** (date, required/optional): Select the date when the issue was created.
* **Updated** (date, required/optional): Select the date when the issue was updated.
