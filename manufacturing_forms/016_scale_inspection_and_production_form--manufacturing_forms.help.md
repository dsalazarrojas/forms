# Scale Inspection And Production Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scale Inspection and Production Form is used to document and track the production process of scales, measuring machines, and weighing systems. It captures essential information about each production run, including production date, status, quantity, and any issues encountered during inspection. This form helps ensure that production is accurately recorded and that any problems are identified and addressed in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Equipment Type" field to specify the type of equipment being inspected (e.g., Scales, Measuring Machines, or Weighing Systems).
2. Enter the "Production Date" in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Select the "Production Status" to indicate the current production status (In Progress, Approved, or Reopened).
4. Fill in the "Issues Identified" field with any problems found during inspection.
5. Provide a detailed description of the issue in the "Issue Description" field.
6. Enter the action taken to address the issue in the "Action Taken" field.
7. Select the "Approved By" field to designate the person who approved the production.
8. Enter the "Production Quantity" and "Total Quantity" fields to record the number of units produced.
9. Write notes about the production in the "Production Notes" field.
10. Fill in the "Email Address", "Phone Number", "Company", "Location", "Serial Number", and "Equipment ID" fields to identify the contact information and equipment details.
11. Enter the "Inspection ID" field to record the identification number for the inspection.
12. If applicable, enter the "Date of Purchase" and "Date Last Inspected" fields to record the purchase date and last inspected date.
13. Select the "Inspection Status" field to indicate the current inspection status.
14. If applicable, enter the "Next Scheduled Inspection Date" field to schedule the next inspection.
15. Fill in the "Last Inspected By" and "Last Inspected By Signature" fields to record the person who conducted the last inspection.
