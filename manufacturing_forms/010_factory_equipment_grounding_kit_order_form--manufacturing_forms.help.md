# Factory Equipment Grounding Kit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Factory Equipment Grounding Kit Order Form" is used by customers to order the necessary Grounding Kit for their factory equipment, ensuring safe operation and protection from electrical shocks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your customer information on **page 1**.
2. Enter your shipment details on **page 2**.
3. Enter your order details on **page 3**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Information (Page 1)
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Please enter your name, company name, address, and any other relevant information about yourself or your company.

### Shipment Details (Page 2)
* **Shipment Details** (`shipment_details`, text, optional): Enter the details about your shipment, including the shipping method and any special instructions.

### Order Details (Page 3)
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): Enter the specifics about the Grounding Kit you are ordering, including the type and quantity needed.
