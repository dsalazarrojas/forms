# Packaging Prototype Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Packaging Prototype Program application form is designed to help companies submit their project proposals, allowing them to access the program's support and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out all required fields (marked with *) in the form. 
2.  Ensure that your company and project information is accurate and up-to-date.
3.  Attach any required supporting documents or files to the form.
4.  Review your form carefully before submitting it to the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Description (1)** (*): A short description of your project and its goals.
* **Company (2)** (*): Your company's name and address.
* **Application (3)**: An optional description of how your project aligns with the program's goals and objectives.
* **Project Design Files (4)** (*): Upload any project design files that are relevant to your application.
* **Contact Person (5)** (*): The person responsible for the project, including their name and title.
* **Contact Email (6)** (*): The email address of the contact person.
* **Phone (7)**: The phone number of the contact person.
* **Company Website (8)**: The URL of your company's website.
* **Company Address (9)** (*): The address of your company.
* **Project Budget (10)** (*): The total budget for your project.
* **Project Timeframe (11)** (*): The expected start and end dates for your project.
* **Project Start Date (12)** (*): The start date of your project.
* **Project End Date (13)** (*): The end date of your project.
* **Project Status (14)** (*): Select the current status of your project (Active, In Progress, Completed, or Cancelled).
* **Project Status Reason (15)**: Provide a brief explanation for the project status.
* **Project Notes (16)**: Any additional comments or notes about your project.
* **Project Comments (17)**: Any additional comments about your project.
* **Project Files (18)**: Upload any additional files that are relevant to your project.
* **Project Images (19)**: Upload any images that are relevant to your project.
* **Project Audio (20)**: Upload any audio files that are relevant to your project.
* **Project Video (21)**: Upload any video files that are relevant to your project.
* **Project Docs (22)**: Upload any documents that are relevant to your project.
* **Project Files 2 (23)**: Upload any additional files that are relevant to your project.
* **Project Files 3 (24)**: Upload any additional files that are relevant to your project.
* **Project Files 4 (25)**: Upload any additional files that are relevant to your project.
