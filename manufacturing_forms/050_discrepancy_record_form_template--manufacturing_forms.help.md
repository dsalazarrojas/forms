# Discrepancy Record Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Discrepancy Record Form Template is designed for recording discrepancies in the manufacturing process. This form helps identify and document issues that occur during production, allowing for prompt attention and resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the page where the discrepancy occurred (1) and fill out any relevant information.
2. Provide a clear and detailed description of the discrepancy (2).
3. Select the date (3) and time (4) of the discrepancy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Discrepancy Record** (`discrepancy_record`, text, not required):
Enter the page where the discrepancy occurred. This helps identify the specific area where the issue was found.

*   This field is not required, but it can be helpful for tracking and referencing.

### 2. **Discrepancy Description** (`discrepancy_description`, text, not required):
Give a clear and detailed description of the discrepancy. This is the most important field, as it helps identify the issue.

*   Be as specific as possible about what went wrong.

### 3. **Discrepancy Date** (`discrepancy_date`, date, not required):
Enter the date of the discrepancy.

*   This field is useful for tracking progress and history.

### 4. **Discrepancy Time** (`discrepancy_time`, time, not required):
Enter the time of the discrepancy.

*   This field is useful for identifying exact timing and scheduling.

### 5. **Discrepancy Date Time** (`discrepancy_date_time`, text, not required):
Enter the date and time of the discrepancy, if desired.

*   This field is optional but can be helpful in certain situations.

### 6. **Discrepancy Date Range** (`discrepancy_date_range`, text, not required):
Enter a range of dates for the discrepancy, if desired.

*   This field is also optional but can be useful in specific cases.

## Tips
*   Be thorough and accurate when filling out this form.
*   Use clear and concise language in the discrepancy description.
*   If you're unsure about any field, refer to the specific guidance above.
*   Remember that this form is designed for reporting discrepancies in the manufacturing process, so try to focus on that context.
