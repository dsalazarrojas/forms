# Warranty Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register warranty details for a product, ensuring that customers can access support and benefits associated with their purchase.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the following fields with your relevant information:
	* First name: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
	* Last name: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
	* Email: Enter your valid email address.
	* Phone: Enter your primary phone number.
	* Street address: Enter the address associated with your purchase.
	* City: Enter the city associated with your address.
	* State: Enter the state associated with your address.
	* Zip code: Enter the zip code associated with your address.
	* Date of purchase: Enter the date you purchased the product.
	* Purchase order number: Enter the purchase order number for your product.
	* Product model number: Enter the product model number for your product.
	* Serial number: Enter the product serial number.
	* Warranty start date: Enter the start date of your warranty period.
	* Warranty end date: Enter the end date of your warranty period.
	* Customer message: Enter any additional comments about your purchase.
	* Customer name: Enter the name of the customer.
	* Customer email: Enter the customer's email address.
	* Customer phone: Enter the customer's phone number.
	* Customer address: Enter the customer's address.
	* Customer city: Enter the city associated with the customer's address.
	* Customer state: Enter the state associated with the customer's address.
	* Customer zip code: Enter the zip code associated with the customer's address.
	* Customer country: Enter the country associated with the customer's address.
	* Assign to: Select the person to whom you would like the warranty to be assigned.
	* Notes: Enter any additional notes about your purchase.
