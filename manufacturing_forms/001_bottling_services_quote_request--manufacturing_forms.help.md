# Bottling Services Quote Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a quote for bottling services. It gathers essential information about your company and product to provide a detailed quote for your project. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure accurate pricing and project timelines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company information, including your full name, contact person, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide the name of the product you need to be bottled, its type, and the estimated monthly volume.
3. Choose the bottle material, cap/closure type, and whether custom labeling is required.
4. Select the desired project start date and required delivery date.
5. Specify your budget range for the project.
6. Check any additional services you may need.
7. Enter any special requirements or notes you have for the project.
8. Finally, let us know how you heard about us (e.g., referral, internet search, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter your company's legal name.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the primary contact person.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your company's primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your company's business phone number.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the product to be bottled.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the category or classification of the product (e.g., Beverage, Personal Care, etc.).
* **Bottle Size (mL)** (`bottle_size`, `number`, required): Enter the volume capacity needed for the bottles.
* **Estimated Monthly Volume** (`estimated_volume`, `number`, required): Enter the approximate number of bottles needed per month.
* **Bottle Material** (`bottle_material`, `select_one`, required): Choose the material for the bottle (e.g., Glass, Plastic (PET), etc.).
* **Cap/Closure Type** (`cap_type`, `text`, optional): Enter the type of cap or closure needed (e.g., Screw cap, pump dispenser, etc.).
* **Custom Labeling Required** (`labeling_required`, `select_one`, required): Check if custom labeling is required for the product.
* **Desired Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, `date`, required): Choose the desired start date for the project.
* **Required Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date when you need the finished products.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, required): Choose your approximate budget for the project (e.g., Under 5000, 5000-15000, etc.).
* **Additional Services Needed** (`additional_services`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check all services you may need for the project (e.g., Filling only, Labeling, etc.).
* **Special Requirements or Notes** (`special_requirements`, `text`, optional): Enter any specific concerns or requests you may have for the project.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`company_history`, `select_one`, optional): Let us know how you heard about us (e.g., Referral, internet search, etc.).
