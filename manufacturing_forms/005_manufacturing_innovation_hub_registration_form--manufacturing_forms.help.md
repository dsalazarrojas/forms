# Manufacturing Innovation Hub Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manufacturing Innovation Hub Registration Form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in participating in the Manufacturing Innovation Hub's activities and events. This form is necessary to ensure that users can be properly registered and connected with other participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out all required fields: email, phone, date, time, manufacturing_innovation_hub_registration_form_submit, and manufacturing_innovation_hub_registration_form_cancel.
2. Select the relevant option for user_info from the available options.
3. Choose your preferred submission options for manufacturing_innovation_hub_registration_form_submit.
4. If you prefer, select the cancel option for manufacturing_innovation_hub_registration_form_cancel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info**: This field is a select box asking you to choose how you are associated with the Manufacturing Innovation Hub. Please select the option that best describes your relationship.
* **Email**: A required text field for entering your email address.
* **Phone**: A required text field for entering your phone number.
* **Note**: This is an optional text field for providing any additional information you wish to share with the Manufacturing Innovation Hub.
* **Date**: A required date field for selecting the date you wish to participate in the hub's activities.
* **Time**: A required time field for selecting the time you wish to participate in the hub's activities.
* **Manufacturing Innovation Hub Registration Form Submit**: This field is a required select box asking you to choose how you wish to submit your registration. Please select one or more of the options provided.
* **Manufacturing Innovation Hub Registration Form Cancel**: This field is an optional select box asking you to choose how you wish to cancel your registration. Please select one or more of the options provided.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to avoid any errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the Manufacturing Innovation Hub team.
* Once you have completed and submitted this form, you will be registered with the Manufacturing Innovation Hub.
* The Manufacturing Innovation Hub will use your information to facilitate your participation in our activities and events.
