# Factory Energy Usage Overview Report - Help Guide
## Purpose

This report provides an overview of a factory's energy usage and associated metrics.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the facility name and select the facility's energy source.
2. Provide the total energy consumption, production capacity, energy efficiency, energy intensity, carbon footprint, and other metrics.
3. Enter the facility's location and provide any additional comments or notes about the facility.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name** (Facility Name, text, required): Enter the name of the factory.
* **Energy Source** (Energy Source, select_one, required): Select the energy source used by the factory. Options include Coal, Natural Gas, Nuclear, Oil, Renewable, and Other.
* **Total Energy Consumption** (Total Energy Consumption, number, required): Enter the total energy consumed by the factory.
* **Production Capacity** (Production Capacity, text, required): Enter the production capacity of the factory.
* **Energy Efficiency** (Energy Efficiency, number, required): Enter the energy efficiency of the factory.
* **Energy Intensity** (Energy Intensity, number, required): Enter the energy intensity of the factory.
* **Carbon Footprint** (Carbon Footprint, number, required): Enter the carbon footprint of the factory.
* **Total CO2 Emissions** (Total CO2 Emissions, number, required): Enter the total CO2 emissions produced by the factory.
* **Total Water Consumption** (Total Water Consumption, number, required): Enter the total water consumed by the factory.
* **Total Waste Production** (Total Waste Production, number, optional): Enter the total waste produced by the factory (not required).
* **Energy Cost** (Energy Cost, number, optional): Enter the energy cost of the factory.
* **Labor Cost** (Labor Cost, number, optional): Enter the labor cost of the factory.
* **Total Electricity Consumption** (Total Electricity Consumption, number, optional): Enter the total electricity consumed by the factory.
* **Total Gas Consumption** (Total Gas Consumption, number, optional): Enter the total gas consumed by the factory.
* **Total Oil Consumption** (Total Oil Consumption, number, optional): Enter the total oil consumed by the factory.
* **CO2 Emissions/Unit** (CO2 Emissions/Unit, number, optional): Enter the CO2 emissions per unit produced by the factory.
* **Water Cost** (Water Cost, number, optional): Enter the water cost of the factory.
* **Waste Disposal Cost** (Waste Disposal Cost, number, optional): Enter the waste disposal cost of the factory.
* **Total Energy Cost** (Total Energy Cost, number, optional): Enter the total energy cost of the factory.
* **Energy Cost/Unit** (Energy Cost/Unit, number, optional): Enter the energy cost per unit produced by the factory.
* **Facility Notes** (Facility Notes, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the factory.
* **Total Waste Disposal** (Total Waste Disposal, number, optional): Enter the total waste disposed by the factory.
* **Facility Location** (Facility Location, text, required): Enter the location of the factory.
* **Total Carbon Footprint** (Total Carbon Footprint, number, optional): Enter the total carbon footprint of the factory.
* **Production Capacity Percentage** (Production Capacity Percentage, number, optional): Enter the production capacity percentage of the factory.
