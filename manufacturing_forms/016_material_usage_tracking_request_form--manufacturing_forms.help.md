# Material Usage Tracking Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track requests for material usage, helping you keep accurate records of your material usage and ensure that materials are used correctly across your organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your request with the required details, including material name, supplier information, quantity needed, and other relevant remarks.
2. Select a status for the request from the options provided.
3. Review and submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Request Form (`request_form`)

*   `Request Form`: Fill in your request with the required details, including material name, supplier information, quantity needed, and other relevant remarks.

### Material Name (`material_name`)

*   `Material Name`: Enter the name of the material you are requesting.

### Supplier

*   `Supplier`: Enter the name of the supplier providing the material.

### Quantity Requested (`quantity_requested`)

*   `Quantity Requested`: Enter the amount of material you need.

### Unit of Measure

*   `Unit of Measure`: Select the unit of measure for the quantity requested.

### Date Requested

*   `Date Requested`: Select the date you need the material by.

### Remarks

*   `Remarks`: Provide any additional comments or information about your material request.

### Status

*   `Status`: Select whether the request is Active or Inactive.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure that your request is processed correctly.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification from your supervisor or department manager.
- This form is for tracking purpose only and does not override any existing material tracking processes or systems.
