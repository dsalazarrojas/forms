# Material Movement Audit Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Material Movement Audit Report Form is used to document and track the movement of materials or goods within or between warehouses, factories, or shifts. This form helps in maintaining transparency and accountability within the organization and ensures accurate recording of material movements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Transfer Date** (1) using the date picker provided. This is the date on which the material movement occurred.
2. Select the **Material Type** (2) from the dropdown list. This indicates the type of material being transferred (e.g., Metal, Plastic, Glass, etc.).
3. Enter the **Transfer Reason** (3). This field should describe the reason for the material transfer (e.g., production, maintenance, repair, etc.).
4. Select the **Warehouse ID** (4) and **Factory ID** (5) of the locations involved in the material transfer.
5. Enter the **Email** (7) of the person responsible for the material transfer.
6. Select the **Assigned User** (9) who was assigned to handle the material transfer.
7. Enter the **Transfer ID** (10) assigned by the system or the transfer identifier.
8. Enter the **Factory Location** (11) and **Warehouse Location** (12) if applicable. These fields are optional but help in tracking the movement of materials within specific locations.
9. Enter the **Transfer Date In Shift** (13) using the date picker provided. This is the date on which the material transfer started.
10. Select the **Shift Start Time** (14) and **Shift End Time** (15) using the time picker. These indicate the time period during which the material transfer occurred.
11. Enter the **Quantity** (16) of the material transferred.
12. Enter any additional **Remarks** (17-23) that are relevant to the material transfer. These fields are optional and can be used to record any specific details or notes regarding the transfer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Transfer Date** (1): The date on which the material movement occurred.
* **Material Type** (2): The type of material being transferred (e.g., Metal, Plastic, Glass, etc.).
* **Transfer Reason** (3): The reason for the material transfer (e.g., production, maintenance, repair, etc.).
* **Warehouse ID** (4): The identifier of the warehouse involved in the material transfer.
* **Factory ID** (5): The identifier of the factory involved in the material transfer.
* **Email** (7): The email address of the person responsible for the material transfer.
* **Assigned User** (9): The person assigned to handle the material transfer.
* **Transfer ID** (10): The transfer identifier assigned by the system or the transfer identifier.
* **Factory Location** (11): The location where the factory is located.
* **Warehouse Location** (12): The location of the warehouse involved in the material transfer.
* **Transfer Date In Shift** (13): The date on which the material transfer started.
* **Shift Start Time** (14): The start time of the shift during which the material transfer occurred.
* **Shift End Time** (15): The end time of the shift during which the material transfer occurred.
* **Quantity** (16): The amount of material transferred.
* **Remarks** (17-23): Optional fields to record any additional details or notes regarding the material transfer.
