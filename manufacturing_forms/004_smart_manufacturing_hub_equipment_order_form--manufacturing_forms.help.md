**Smart Manufacturing Hub Equipment Order Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Smart Manufacturing Hub Equipment Order Form is used to collect information about equipment orders for the Smart Manufacturing Hub. This form ensures that order details are accurate and up-to-date, enabling efficient processing and communication with customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Enter user information in the "User Info" section.
2.  Complete the "Order Details" section with order specifics.
3.  Enter shipping details in the "Shipping Info" section.
4.  Provide contact information for the customer in the "Contact Info" section.
5.  Review the order details in the "Order Review" section.
6.  Select a payment method (Stripe, PayPal, or Bank Transfer) in the "Payment Info" section.
7.  Add any additional comments about the order in the "Order Comments" section.
8.  Enter the equipment name, serial number, purchase date, and total price in the "Equipment Details" and "Payment" sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Info** (User Information, Text, Optional): This section is used to capture the customer's or buyer's details. Enter your name, contact number, and other relevant information.
*   **Order Details** (Order Details, Text, Optional): Describe the equipment you are ordering, including any specific features, models, or quantities.
*   **Shipping Info** (Shipping Information, Text, Optional): Enter the shipping details, including the shipping address, shipping method, and any other relevant information.
*   **Contact Info** (Contact Information, Text, Optional): Provide the customer's contact details, including email, phone number, or any other contact method.
*   **Order Review** (Order Review, Text, Optional): Review and confirm the order details before proceeding.
*   **Payment Info** (Payment Information, Text, Optional, Select One): Choose your payment method (Stripe, PayPal, or Bank Transfer) and enter the related information.
*   **Order Comments** (Order Comments, Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or instructions about the order.
*   **Equipment Details** (Equipment Details, Text, Optional): Enter the equipment name, serial number, and purchase date.
*   **Equipment Name** (Equipment Name, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the equipment being ordered.
*   **Serial Number** (Serial Number, Text, Optional): Enter the serial number of the ordered equipment.
*   **Purchase Date** (Purchase Date, Date, Optional): Enter the date of purchase for the ordered equipment.
*   **Price** (Price, Text, Optional): Enter the price of the equipment.
*   **Total Price** (Total Price, Number, Optional): Enter the total price of the ordered equipment.
*   **Payment Method** (Payment Method, Select One, Optional): Select the payment method (Stripe, PayPal, or Bank Transfer) to be used for the order.
*   **Order Comments** (Order Comments, Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or instructions about the order.
