# Product Repackaging Compliance Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a product that has undergone repackaging. It's essential to fill out this form to document the compliance of repackaging activities for regulatory and quality control purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Enter the product ID.
2.  Enter the product name.
3.  Enter the date the repackaging activity started.
4.  Enter the date the product was packaged.
5.  Enter the location where the product was packaged.
6.  Enter the facility ID.
7.  Enter the facility name.
8.  Enter the production line ID.
9.  Enter the production line name.
10.  Enter the quantity of the product that was repackaged.
11.  Select the repackaging unit (e.g., case, carton, etc.).
12.  Select the repackaging method (e.g., manual reprocessing, etc.).
13.  Enter the location where the product is stored.
14.  Record any inspection results (e.g., passed, failed).
15.  Add any comments about the repackaging process or product quality.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **product_id** (Required): Enter the unique product identifier, usually a serial number.
* **product name** (Optional): Enter the name of the product being repackaged.
* **repackaging activity start date** (Optional): Enter the date when repackaging started.
* **packaging date** (Optional): Enter the date when the product was originally packaged.
* **packaging location** (Optional): Enter the location where the product was originally packaged.
* **facility id** (Required): Enter the unique facility identifier, usually a number.
* **facility name** (Optional): Enter the name of the facility where the product was repackaged.
* **production line id** (Required): Enter the unique production line identifier, usually a number.
* **production line name** (Optional): Enter the name of the production line where the product was repackaged.
* **repackaging quantity** (Required): Enter the quantity of the product that was repackaged.
* **repackaging unit** (Required): Select the unit in which the product was repackaged (e.g., case, carton, etc.).
* **repackaging method** (Optional): Select the method used for repackaging (e.g., manual reprocessing, etc.).
* **storage location** (Optional): Enter the location where the repackaged product is stored.
* **inspection result** (Optional): Record any inspection results, usually "passed" or "failed".
* **comments** (Optional): Add any comments about the repackaging process or product quality.

Tips:

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the form is filled out correctly.
* Review the form before submission to ensure all required fields are filled out.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or quality control team for assistance.
