# Industrial Data Exchange Platform Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for companies interested in becoming registered members of the Industrial Data Exchange Platform. By completing this form, you will be able to access the platform's services and exchange data with other companies in your industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: `company_description`, `industry`, `terms_conditions`.
2. Provide optional fields such as `onboarding_details`, `company_details`, `contact_person`, `phone_number`, `email`, `address`, `product_or_service`, `company_representative`, `contact_role`, `role_in_platform`, `company_size`, `employee_count`, `revenue`, `company_website`, `social_media_links`, `platform_access_agreement`, `document_upload`.
3. Select the correct options for `industry`, `company_size`, and `terms_conditions`.
4. Click the `Submit` button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Onboarding Details** (`onboarding_details`, `text`, optional): This field is for any additional information you want to provide about your company's onboarding process.
* **Company Details** (`company_details`, `text`, optional): This field is for any additional information you want to provide about your company.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, optional): This field is for the name and contact information of the person filling out this form.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): This field is for your company's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): This field is for your company's email address.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): This field is for your company's address.
* **Industry** (`industry`, `select_one`, optional): Select the industry your company operates in.
* **Company Description** (`company_description`, `text`, optional): This field is for a brief description of your company.
* **Product or Service** (`product_or_service`, `text`, optional): This field is for any products or services your company offers.
* **Company Representative** (`company_representative`, `text`, optional): This field is for the name and contact information of the person filling out this form.
* **Contact Role** (`contact_role`, `text`, optional): This field is for the role of the contact person in your company.
* **Role in Platform** (`role_in_platform`, `text`, optional): This field is for your role in the Industrial Data Exchange Platform.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, `select_one`, optional): Select the size of your company.
* **Employee Count** (`employee_count`, `number`, optional): This field is for the number of employees in your company.
* **Revenue** (`revenue`, `number`, optional): This field is for your company's revenue.
* **Website** (`company_website`, `text`, optional): This field is for your company's website.
* **Social Media Links** (`social_media_links`, `text`, optional): This field is for any social media links related to your company.
* **Platform Access Agreement** (`platform_access_agreement`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the agreement type for platform access.
* **Document Upload** (`document_upload`, `text`, optional): This field is for uploading any relevant documents.
* **Submit** (`submit_form`, `select_one`, optional): Select the Submit option to submit the form.
* **Terms Conditions** (`terms_conditions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the option indicating you have read and understand the terms and conditions of the platform.
