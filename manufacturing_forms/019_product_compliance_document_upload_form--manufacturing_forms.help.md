# Product Compliance Document Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by users to upload and manage compliance documents for products manufactured by their company. The form ensures that all necessary compliance documents are uploaded and stored correctly to meet regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Document Type** from the provided options (Compliance Documents, Product Data Sheets, Certificates).
2. Enter your **User Email** address, which will receive notifications about the uploaded document.
3. Upload the **File Upload** containing the document.
4. Optionally, provide additional **Document Date**, **Document Number**, and **Document Version** information.
5. Provide any additional **Document Comments** about the document.
6. If necessary, upload additional **Supporting Files**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Email** (`user_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address that will receive notifications about the uploaded document.
* **Document Type** (`document_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of compliance document you are uploading (e.g. Compliance Documents, Product Data Sheets, Certificates).
* **File Upload** (`file_upload`, `text`, required): Upload the compliance document file.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your company that manufactured the product.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the product being manufactured.
* **Document Date** (`document_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the document was issued or updated.
* **Document Number** (`document_number`, `number`, optional): Enter the unique identifier assigned to the document.
* **Document Version** (`document_version`, `number`, optional): Enter the version of the document.
* **Document Comments** (`document_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments about the document.
* **Supporting Files** (`supporting_files`, `text`, optional): Upload any additional files or information that support the document.
