# printed_material_quality_audit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "printed_material_quality_audit_form" is used for auditing the quality of printed materials.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the "Print Quality Checks" page to begin the audit.
2. Fill out the project ID, print date, print time, material ID, print location, and other relevant details.
3. Move on to the "Supplier Information" page.
4. Fill out the supplier name, supplier contact, supplier email, supplier phone, supplier address, print shop name, print shop contact, print shop email, print shop phone, print shop address, and other relevant details.
5. Enter the form submitted by, form submitted on, and other relevant information.
6. Submit the form after filling out all the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Print Quality Checks**: This page captures information about the print quality issues found during the audit.
* **Project ID**: Enter the project ID to identify the specific project being audited.
*  **Print Date**: Enter the date when the print was done.
*  **Print Time**: Enter the time when the print was done.
*  **Material ID**: Enter the ID of the printed material.
*  **Print Location**: Enter the location where the print was done.
*  **Print Color**: Enter the color of the printed material.
*  **Issue Found**: Enter any issues found with the print quality.
*  **Issue Description**: Enter a detailed description of the issue found.
* **Supplier Information**: This page captures information about the supplier and print shop involved in the printing process.
*  **Supplier Name**: Enter the name of the supplier.
*  **Supplier Contact**: Enter the contact information of the supplier.
*  **Supplier Email**: Enter the email of the supplier.
*  **Supplier Phone**: Enter the phone number of the supplier.
*  **Supplier Address**: Enter the address of the supplier.
*  **Print Shop Name**: Enter the name of the print shop.
*  **Print Shop Contact**: Enter the contact information of the print shop.
*  **Print Shop Email**: Enter the email of the print shop.
*  **Print Shop Phone**: Enter the phone number of the print shop.
*  **Print Shop Address**: Enter the address of the print shop.
*  **Form Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
*  **Form Submitted On**: Enter the date when the form was submitted.
*  **Form Submitted By User**: Enter the name of the user who submitted the form.
*  **Form Submitted By Email**: Enter the email of the user who submitted the form.
*  **Form Submitted By Phone**: Enter the phone number of the user who submitted the form.
*  **Form Submitted By Address**: Enter the address of the user who submitted the form.

Note: The fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be filled out. The others are optional and can be filled out if necessary.
