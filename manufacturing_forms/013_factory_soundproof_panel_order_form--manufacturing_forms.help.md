# Factory Soundproof Panel Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for soundproof panel orders, allowing customers to provide details about the panel, their contact information, and additional notes for the order. The form helps ensure accurate and efficient processing of orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the department responsible for the order (Marketing, Production, or Sales).
3. Choose the order status (Pending, In Progress, Completed, or Cancelled).
4. Choose the delivery method (Pickup, Delivery, or Freight).
5. Enter the order number.
6. Select the department manager (if applicable).
7. Enter the employee name, email, and phone number responsible for the order.
8. Enter the delivery date and time.
9. Enter any additional delivery notes.
10. Enter any installation notes.
11. Enter any payment notes.
12. Enter any customer notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (Customer ID, text, not required): Enter your name, email, and phone number for contact purposes.
* **Panel Info** (Panel Info, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the soundproof panel.
* **Delivery Info** (Delivery Info, text, not required): Enter any relevant delivery information.
* **Installation Info** (Installation Info, text, not required): Enter any relevant installation information.
* **Order Notes** (Order Notes, text, not required): Add any additional notes about the order.
* **Order Status** (Order Status, select_one, required): Select the current status of the order (Pending, In Progress, Completed, or Cancelled).
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, not required): Enter any additional contact information.
* **Payment Info** (Payment Info, text, not required): Enter any payment details.
* **Delivery Method** (Delivery Method, select_multiple, required): Choose the delivery method (Pickup, Delivery, or Freight).
* **Delivery Date** (Delivery Date, date, not required): Enter the date of the delivery.
* **Delivery Time** (Delivery Time, time, not required): Enter the time of the delivery.
* **Installation Time** (Installation Time, time, not required): Enter the time of the installation.
* **Notes** (Notes, text, not required): Enter any additional notes about the order.
* **Order Number** (Order Number, number, required): Enter the unique order number.
* **Customer ID** (Customer ID, number, not required): Enter any customer identification number.
* **Employee ID** (Employee ID, number, not required): Enter any employee identification number.
* **Department** (Department, select_one, required): Select the department responsible for the order (Marketing, Production, or Sales).
* **Department Manager** (Department Manager, select_one, not required): Select the department manager.
* **Employee Name** (Employee Name, text, not required): Enter the name of the employee responsible for the order.
* **Employee Email** (Employee Email, email, not required): Enter the email of the employee responsible for the order.
* **Employee Phone** (Employee Phone, text, not required): Enter the phone number of the employee responsible for the order.
* **Delivery Note** (Delivery Note, text, not required): Enter any delivery notes.
* **Installation Note** (Installation Note, text, not required): Enter any installation notes.
* **Payment Note** (Payment Note, text, not required): Enter any payment notes.
* **Customer Note** (Customer Note, text, not required): Enter any customer notes.
