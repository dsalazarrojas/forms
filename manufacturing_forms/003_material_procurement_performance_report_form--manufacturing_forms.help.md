# Material Procurement Performance Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Material Procurement Performance Report Form is designed to collect information about material procurement performance from suppliers. This form helps assess and evaluate the performance of suppliers based on your experiences and interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the supplier name and location for the supplier you are reporting on.
2.  Provide details about the material sourced from the supplier.
3.  Enter the total value of purchases made from the supplier.
4.  Indicate if you made purchases from the supplier more than once.
5.  Select the material type(s) sourced from the supplier.
6.  Describe the procurement process used.
7.  Rate the supplier's quality performance.
8.  Rate the communication effectiveness of the supplier.
9.  Indicate if the time taken to resolve issues with the supplier was satisfactory.
10.  Rate your overall satisfaction with the supplier.
11.  Provide any additional comments about your experience with the supplier (optional).
12.  If you have a second supplier you'd like to report on, repeat the process from step 1-11.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Name (1)**: Enter the name of the supplier.
* **Supplier Location (2)**: Enter the location of the supplier.
* **Material Sourced (3)**: Enter the number of materials sourced from the supplier.
* **Total Value of Purchases (4)**: Enter the total value of purchases made from the supplier.
* **Purchase Frequency (5)**: Check if you've made purchases from the supplier more than once.
* **Material Type (6)**: Select the type(s) of material sourced from the supplier.
* **Procurement Process (7)**: Describe the process used to procure material from the supplier.
* **Supplier Quality Rating (8)**: Rate the supplier's quality performance (Good or Poor).
* **Communication Effectiveness (9)**: Rate the effectiveness of the supplier's communication (Yes or No).
* **Time Taken to Resolve Issues (10)**: Indicate if the time taken to resolve issues with the supplier was satisfactory (Yes or No).
* **Overall Satisfaction (11)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the supplier (Satisfied or Dissatisfied).
* **Comments (12)**: Provide any additional comments about your experience with the supplier.
* **Supplier Name 2 (13)**: Enter the name of a second supplier (if applicable).
* **Supplier Location 2 (14)**: Enter the location of the second supplier (if applicable).
* **Material Sourced 2 (15)**: Enter the number of materials sourced from the second supplier (if applicable).
* **Total Value of Purchases 2 (16)**: Enter the total value of purchases made from the second supplier (if applicable).
* **Purchase Frequency 2 (17)**: Indicate if you've made purchases from the second supplier more than once (if applicable).
* **Material Type 2 (18)**: Select the type(s) of material sourced from the second supplier (if applicable).
* **Procurement Process 2 (19)**: Describe the process used to procure material from the second supplier (if applicable).
* **Supplier Quality Rating 2 (20)**: Rate the quality performance of the second supplier (if applicable).
* **Communication Effectiveness 2 (21)**: Rate the effectiveness of the communication of the second supplier (if applicable).
* **Time Taken to Resolve Issues 2 (22)**: Indicate if the time taken to resolve issues with the second supplier was satisfactory (if applicable).
* **Overall Satisfaction 2 (23)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the second supplier (if applicable).
* **Comments 2 (24)**: Provide any additional comments about your experience with the second supplier (if applicable).
* **Submission Date (25)**: Enter the date of submission (if applicable).
