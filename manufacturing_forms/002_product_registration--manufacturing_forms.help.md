# Product Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Registration form is a critical tool for ensuring accurate and up-to-date records of all products in the inventory. It helps maintain a centralized database of product information, customer details, and order data.

## How to Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the product details, including product serial number, in the corresponding field.
2. Provide customer contact information, including email, phone, and address.
3. Fill in shipping details, including city, state, and country.
4. Enter payment information, selecting the payment method used.
5. Upload or provide a product image (optional).
6. Enter order information, including order number, customer ID, and order date.
7. Complete any additional fields as required.
