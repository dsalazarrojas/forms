# School District Technology Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School District Technology Audit Form is designed to collect information about the current technology infrastructure and status of school districts. This includes details about technology upgrades, contacts, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Select the correct "Technology Overview" option that best describes your school district's current technology status.
2. Choose "Upgraded" if your school district has a functioning technology upgrade currently in place.
3. If your school district doesn't have a technology upgrade, choose "Planned" if it's in the pipeline.
4. Select "Not upgraded" if your school district hasn't upgraded its technology recently.
5. Choose "Unknown" if you're not sure about the current technology status.
6. If you've upgraded your technology recently, enter the date of the last upgrade in the "Last Upgrade" field.
7. If you're planning a future upgrade, enter the date of the next planned upgrade in the "Next Upgrade" field.
8. In the "Comments" field, provide any additional information about your school district's technology infrastructure.
9. Check the "Technology Contact" field to confirm if you have a designated technology contact person.
10. Enter the contact's email address in the "Email" field, if applicable.
11. Enter the contact's phone number in the "Phone" field, if applicable.
12. Enter your school district's name in the "School District" field.
13. In the "School District Information" field, provide any additional information about your school district.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Technology Overview** (`Technology Type`, select_one): This field determines the current technology status of your school district.
* **Current Status** (`select_one`): Choose the status of your school district's technology infrastructure.
* **Last Upgrade** (`date`): Enter the date of the last technology upgrade if applicable.
* **Next Upgrade** (`date`): Enter the date of the next planned technology upgrade if applicable.
* **Comments** (`text`): Provide any additional comments or information about your school district's technology infrastructure.
* **Technology Contact** (`select_one`): Confirm if you have a designated technology contact person.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the contact's email address, if applicable.
* **Phone** (`text`): Enter the contact's phone number, if applicable.
* **School District** (`text`): Enter your school district's name.
* **School District Information** (`text`): Provide any additional information about your school district.

## Tips

* Please ensure the accuracy of the information provided to help the school district make informed decisions.
* If you're unsure about any field, it's okay to ask for clarification or guidance.
