# School Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Absence Form is used to report a student's absence from school. It is a record-keeping tool for school administrators to track and manage student absences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the student's name as it appears on their official school record.
2. Provide the parent's name, phone number, and email address for contact purposes.
3. Enter the start and end dates of the student's absence, if applicable.
4. Enter the reason for the student's absence.
5. Enter the student's phone number, if applicable.
6. Enter the parent's phone number, if applicable.
7. Enter the parent's email address, if applicable.
8. Enter the school's name where the student will be returning to, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name as it appears on their official school record.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the parent or guardian associated with the student.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date the student was absent from school.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date the student returned to school.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, required): Enter a brief explanation for the student's absence.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter the student's phone number, if applicable.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, optional): Enter the parent's phone number, if applicable.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, optional): Enter the parent's email address, if applicable.
* **Student Absence Date** (`student_absence_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the student was absent, if different from the start date.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school the student will return to, if different from the student's current school.
 
## Tips
* Ensure that you provide accurate and clear information to support the student's absence.
* If the student was absent for multiple days, enter the start and end dates accordingly.
* If the student will be returning to a different school, enter the school's name for record-keeping purposes.
* If you are unsure about any information, contact the school administrator for assistance.
