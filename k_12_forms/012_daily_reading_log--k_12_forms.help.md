# daily_reading_log - Help Guide

## Purpose
The daily reading log form is a tool to track and document your reading activities. It helps you keep a record of the time spent reading, the status of your reading, and any notes or comments related to your reading sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Reading Log" page and enter a brief description of your reading activity in the "Reading Log" field.
2. If applicable, set your reading goals in the "Reading Goal" field. This includes frequency, duration, start time, end time, and status goals.
3. Record the start and end times of your reading sessions in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields, respectively.
4. Select the status of your reading activity in the "Status" field.
5. Add any comments or notes about your reading session in the "Comments" field.
6. If you have any additional notes, add them in the "Reading Notes" field.
7. If you have any reading goals that you want to set for the future, add them in the "Reading Frequency Goal", "Reading Duration Goal", "Start Time Goal", "End Time Goal", and "Status Goal" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reading Log** (Reading Log, note, **Required**)
	+ A brief description of your reading activity.
* **Reading Goal** (Reading Goal, text, **Optional**)
	+ A description of the reading goals you have set for yourself.
* **Reflections** (Reflections, text, **Optional**)
	+ Any thoughts, insights, or comments related to your reading activity.
* **Reading Frequency** (Reading Frequency, number, **Required**)
	+ The frequency of reading activities you plan to engage in.
* **Reading Duration** (Reading Duration, time, **Required**)
	+ The duration of each reading session.
* **Start Time** (Start Time, time, **Required**)
	+ The time you started reading.
* **End Time** (End Time, time, **Required**)
	+ The time you finished reading.
* **Date** (Date, date, **Optional**)
	+ The date of your reading session.
* **Status** (Status, select_one, **Optional**)
	+ The status of your reading activity.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, **Optional**)
	+ Any additional comments about your reading session.
* **Reading Notes** (Reading Notes, text, **Optional**)
	+ Additional notes about your reading activity.
* **Reading Frequency Goal** (Reading Frequency Goal, text, **Optional**)
	+ A description of your reading frequency goal.
* **Reading Duration Goal** (Reading Duration Goal, text, **Optional**)
	+ A description of your reading duration goal.
* **Start Time Goal** (Start Time Goal, text, **Optional**)
	+ A description of your start time goal.
* **End Time Goal** (End Time Goal, text, **Optional**)
	+ A description of your end time goal.
* **Status Goal** (Status Goal, text, **Optional**)
	+ A description of your status goal.
* **Assigned to** (Assigned to, select_one, **Optional**)
	+ Who the reading activity is assigned to.
* **Reading Frequency Goal Value** (Reading Frequency Goal Value, number, **Optional**)
	+ The value of your reading frequency goal.
* **Reading Duration Goal Value** (Reading Duration Goal Value, number, **Optional**)
	+ The value of your reading duration goal.
* **Start Time Goal Value** (Start Time Goal Value, time, **Optional**)
	+ The value of your start time goal.
* **End Time Goal Value** (End Time Goal Value, time, **Optional**)
	+ The value of your end time goal.
* **Reading Status Goal** (Reading Status Goal, text, **Optional**)
	+ A description of your status goal.
* **Reading Notes Goal** (Reading Notes Goal, text, **Optional**)
	+ Additional notes about your reading goal.
* **Reading Goal Value** (Reading Goal Value, text, **Optional**)
	+ A description of your reading goal.
* **Reading Date Goal Value** (Reading Date Goal Value, date, **Optional**)
