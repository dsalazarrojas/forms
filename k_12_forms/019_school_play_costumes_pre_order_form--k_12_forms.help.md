# School Play Costumes Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to pre-order costumes for a school play or event. It collects relevant information to ensure accurate and timely delivery of costumes for participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the school play or event in the "Title" field.
2. Select the relevant category for this pre-order from the "Category" dropdown menu.
3. Add any additional comments or notes about the pre-order in the "Description" field.
4. Choose the delivery preference (before, during, after) from the "Delivery Preference" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the date for delivery in the "Delivery Date" field.
6. Enter the time for delivery in the "Delivery Time" field (optional).
7. Select the payment method (online, cash, check) for the pre-order from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
8. Enter the amount to be paid in the "Payment Amount" field.
9. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field (optional).
10. Enter the email address of the person making the pre-order in the "Email" field.
11. Enter the phone number of the person making the pre-order in the "Phone" field.
12. Click the "Submit" button to complete the pre-order.
