# Vocabulary Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect students' vocabulary quiz answers and demographic information for further analysis or communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the three vocabulary quiz questions on pages 1-3.
2. Provide your score on page 4.
3. Enter your email address on page 5.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it on page 6 (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_question** (Student Answer 1, Text, Required: No): Enter your answer to the first vocabulary quiz question.
* **second_question** (Student Answer 2, Text, Required: No): Enter your answer to the second vocabulary quiz question.
* **third_question** (Student Answer 3, Text, Required: No): Enter your answer to the third vocabulary quiz question.
* **score** (Score, Number, Required: Yes): Enter the score you received on the vocabulary quiz.
* **email** (Email, Email, Required: Yes): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (Phone Number, Text, Required: No): If you have a phone number, enter it here (optional).

## Tips

* Please ensure that your email address is entered correctly, as this will be used for further communication.
* If you are unsure about any of the answers, you can ask your instructor or the course administrator for clarification.
* You can leave the phone number field blank if you do not have a phone number.
