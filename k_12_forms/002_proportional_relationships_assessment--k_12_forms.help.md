# Proportional Relationships Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Proportional Relationships Assessment form is designed to evaluate a student's understanding of proportional relationships. It gathers information on their answers to questions, score, and comments from the teacher.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all that apply from the "User Inputs" question.
2. Select your assessment of the student's answer to the teacher's question.
3. Enter the submission date and time of the assessment.
4. Enter the student's name.
5. Enter the time taken to complete the assessment.
6. Enter the duration of the assessment.
7. Enter the student's ratio scores 1, 2, 3, and 4.
8. Enter the overall score.
9. Enter any comments you have for the student.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Inputs** (`user_inputs`, select_multiple, Required/Optional): This is a multiple-choice question where you select all that apply from the given options.
* **Teacher Assessment** (`teacher_assessment`, select_one, Required): This is a question where you select your assessment of the student's answer.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, Required): This is the date and time when the assessment was submitted.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, Required): This is the student's name.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, Required): This is the time when the assessment was submitted.
* **Submission Duration** (`submission_duration`, number, Required): This is the duration of the assessment.
* **Ratio Score 1** (`ratio_score_1`, number, Required): This is the score for the first ratio question.
* **Ratio Score 2** (`ratio_score_2`, number, Required): This is the score for the second ratio question.
* **Ratio Score 3** (`ratio_score_3`, number, Required): This is the score for the third ratio question.
* **Ratio Score 4** (`ratio_score_4`, number, Required): This is the score for the fourth ratio question.
* **Score** (`score`, number, Required): This is the overall score.
* **Comment** (`comment`, text, Optional): This is a free text field for any comments you have for the student.
