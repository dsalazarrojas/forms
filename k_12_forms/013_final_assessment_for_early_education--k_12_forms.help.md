# Final Assessment For Early Education - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Final Assessment for Early Education form is designed to provide teachers with a structured way to evaluate students' performance, identify areas of improvement, and offer suggestions for further learning opportunities. This form helps to gather essential feedback that supports student growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Overall Rating: Please provide an overall rating of the student's performance out of 100.
2.  Teacher Comments: Please add any comments or observations you have about the student's strengths and weaknesses.
3.  Overall Recommendations: Provide any overall suggestions or recommendations for future learning opportunities.
4.  Rating 1: Please rate the student on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Needs Improvement" and 5 is "Excellent".
5.  Comments 1: Add any comments or observations related to this rating.
6.  Rating 2: Repeat the same rating process as Rating 1.
7.  Comments 2: Add any additional comments or observations related to this rating.
8.  Teacher Recommendation 1: Please provide a specific suggestion or recommendation for the student.
9.  Teacher Recommendation 2: Repeat the same process as Teacher Recommendation 1.
10.  Teacher Recommendation 3: Repeat the same process as Teacher Recommendation 1.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, required): This field is used to rate the student's overall performance out of 100.
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, text, optional): Use this field to add any comments or observations you have about the student's strengths and weaknesses.
* **Overall Recommendations** (`overall_recommendations`, note, required): Provide any overall suggestions or recommendations for future learning opportunities.
* **Rating 1** (`rating_1`, number, optional): Rate the student on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Needs Improvement" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **Comments 1** (`comments_1`, text, optional): Add any comments or observations related to this rating.
* **Rating 2** (`rating_2`, number, optional): Repeat the same rating process as Rating 1.
* **Comments 2** (`comments_2`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or observations related to this rating.
* **Teacher Recommendation 1** (`teacher_recommendation_1`, text, optional): Please provide a specific suggestion or recommendation for the student.
* **Teacher Recommendation 2** (`teacher_recommendation_2`, text, optional): Repeat the same process as Teacher Recommendation 1.
* **Teacher Recommendation 3** (`teacher_recommendation_3`, text, optional): Repeat the same process as Teacher Recommendation 1.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when providing comments and recommendations.
* Use clear and concise language to ensure the student can understand the feedback.
* Focus on providing actionable advice for improvement, rather than general comments.
* Review and revise your responses before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and clarity.
* Please fill out all required fields, as they are essential for providing a complete assessment.
* If a student has multiple areas of improvement, provide separate recommendations for each area.
