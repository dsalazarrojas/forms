# Kodesh Studies Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kodesh Studies Participation Form is a comprehensive collection of fields designed to gather information from students participating in Kodesh studies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **School Name** in the designated field.
2. Fill in your **Student Name** accurately.
3. Provide your valid **Email** address.
4. If applicable, enter your **Phone** number.
5. Select your **School ID** status.
6. Indicate your **Grade** level of participation.
7. Select your **Subject** of interest.
8. Enter any additional **Notes** about your participation.
9. Choose your **Availability** for further studies.
10. Select the **Needs** you require for participation.
11. Indicate your **Participation Level**.
12. Choose your **Program Type**.
13. Enter your unique **Student ID** (optional).
14. Select your **School Type**.
15. Select your **Location**.
16. Indicate whether you have a **Teacher**.
17. Identify your **School Admin** status.
18. Indicate your **School Principal** status.
19. Choose your **Program Director** status.
20. Select your **Community Program** status.
21. Choose your **Program Type 2**.
22. Select your **Student Level**.
23. Enter your **Teacher Name** (optional).
24. Enter your **Teacher Email** (optional).
25. Submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school where you are participating in Kodesh studies.
* **Student Name**: Provide your full name as a student.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address associated with your school or program.
* **Phone**: If applicable, enter your phone number for further contact.
* **School ID**: Indicate whether you have a school ID or not.
* **Grade**: Select your current grade level in the program.
* **Subject**: Choose the subject area you are interested in.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your participation.
* **Availability**: Choose a preferred availability for further studies.
* **Needs**: Select the needs you require for participation in Kodesh studies.
* **Participation Level**: Indicate your current level of participation.
* **Program Type**: Choose the type of program you are interested in.
* **Student ID**: Enter a unique ID associated with your student profile (optional).
* **School Type**: Select the type of school you attend.
* **Location**: Choose your location within the school or program.
* **Teacher**: Identify whether you have a teacher associated with your program.
* **School Admin**: Indicate your status as a school admin.
* **School Principal**: Identify your status as a school principal.
* **Program Director**: Choose your status as a program director.
* **Community Program**: Select your status within the community program.
* **Program Type 2**: Choose your program type 2.
* **Student Level**: Select your student level within the program.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter your teacher's name (optional).
* **Teacher Email**: Enter your teacher's email (optional).
