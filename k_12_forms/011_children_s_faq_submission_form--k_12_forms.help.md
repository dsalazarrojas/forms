# Children FAQ Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Children FAQ Submission Form is a tool for students, parents, teachers, and staff members to submit questions related to various topics in a school setting. The form helps the school community to gather, organize, and share knowledge on a wide range of subjects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  **Submitter Name:** Enter your name as the student or parent submitting the question.
2.  **Submitter Role:** Select your role within the school community (Student, Parent, Teacher, or Staff).
3.  **School Name:** If applicable, enter the name of your school or program.
4.  **Question Topic:** Provide the topic or category of the question being asked.
5.  **Question:** Clearly describe the question or problem you are facing.
6.  **Consent to Publish Question:** Check the box to consent to the question being published in the FAQ.
7.  **Contact Email:** Enter your email for follow-up communication if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submitter Name (id: 1)**: Enter your name as the student or parent submitting the question.
* **Submitter Role (id: 2)**: Select your role within the school community to help us understand who is submitting the question.
* **School Name (id: 3)**: If you are affiliated with a specific school or program, please enter the name here (optional).
* **Question Topic (id: 4)**: Choose a category or topic for your question to help us better understand it.
* **Question (id: 5)**: Provide a clear and concise description of the question or problem you are facing.
* **Suggested Answer (id: 6)**: If you have an idea or resource that answers the question, feel free to provide it (optional).
* **Consent to Publish Question (id: 7)**: Check this box to allow the question to be published in the FAQ (required).
* **Contact Email (id: 8)**: Enter your email address for follow-up communication (optional).
