# School Lunch Menu Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students about the school lunch menu. It is part of a voting system that helps school administrators to determine which menu items are most popular among students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "School Lunch Form" page and start filling out the form as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### School Lunch Form (<name>, <type>, required/optional)
The School Lunch Form is a text field where you can enter your thoughts about the school lunch menu. It's a free-form field, so you can write as much or as little as you like.

### School Lunch Menu (<name>, <type>, required/optional)
The School Lunch Menu field is another text field where you can list the menu items you've chosen for lunch. You can enter one or multiple menu items, separated by commas.

### School Lunch Rating (<name>, <type>, required/optional)
The School Lunch Rating field is a dropdown menu with five options: 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5. Please select the rating that best reflects your opinion of the lunch menu. 

### School Lunch Feedback (<name>, <type>, required/optional)
The School Lunch Feedback field is a free-form field for any additional feedback or thoughts you have about the lunch menu. You can use this space to suggest new menu items or provide other comments.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions as truthfully as possible to help school administrators make informed decisions about the lunch menu.
* You can select only one rating for each menu item.
* If you have any additional feedback, please use the "School Lunch Feedback" field.
