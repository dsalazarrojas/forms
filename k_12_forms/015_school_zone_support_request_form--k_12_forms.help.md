# School Zone Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Zone Support Request Form is used to report incidents, request assistance, and provide information to the school zone support team. This form is designed to collect relevant details about the incident, the student involved, and the support required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student ID number to identify the student involved in the incident.
2. Provide the parent's phone number so that we can contact them for further information.
3. Enter the student's email address for communication purposes.
4. Enter the date of the incident in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
5. Select the school zone location where the incident occurred.
6. Describe the incident in detail, including any relevant information about what happened.
7. Specify the assistance required, such as repair, replacement, or other.
8. Enter your name and contact information so that we can reach out to you for further information.
9. Add any additional notes or comments about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Zone Information**: This section is used to provide general information about the school zone where the incident occurred.
* **Student Id**: Enter the student ID number to identify the student involved in the incident.
* **Parent Phone**: Provide the parent's phone number so that we can contact them for further information.
* **Email**: Enter the student's email address for communication purposes.
* **Date Of Incident**: Enter the date of the incident in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`, e.g., `2022-05-23`.
* **School Zone Location**: Select the school zone location where the incident occurred from the list provided.
* **Incident Description**: Describe the incident in detail, including any relevant information about what happened.
* **Assistance Requested**: Specify the assistance required, such as repair, replacement, or other.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name and title for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number so that we can reach out to you for further information.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Additional Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the incident.
