# Public School Parent/Student Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public School Parent/Student Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information from parents and students about their experiences in public schools. This form helps the school administration understand their students' and parents' needs, providing valuable feedback for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately. Ensure you provide your name, email, phone number, and other required information.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and provide as much detail as possible in the open-ended fields.
3. Review your submission before submitting the form to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### School Name (Page 1)
* **School Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the public school your child attends.

### Student Name (Page 1)
* **Student Name** (text, required): Enter your child's name.

### Parent Name (Page 1)
* **Parent Name** (text, not required): If you are a parent, enter your name. If you are a student, leave this field blank.

### Email (Page 2)
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your valid email address.

### Phone (Page 2)
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.

### Feedback (Page 2)
* **Feedback** (text, required): Share any feedback or comments about your school experience.

### Suggestions (Page 3)
* **Suggestions** (text, not required): If you have any suggestions for improving the school, share them here.

### Comments (Page 3)
* **Comments** (text, not required): Add any additional comments or thoughts about your school experience.

### School Year (Page 3)
* **School Year** (number, required): Enter the current school year of your child.

### Student Age (Page 4)
* **Student Age** (number, required): Enter your child's age.

### Parent Email (Page 4)
* **Parent Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.

### Parent Phone (Page 4)
* **Parent Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible in the open-ended fields.
* Ensure you have filled out all required fields.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the school administration for assistance.
