# junior_high_electives_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Junior High Electives Survey" form is designed to gather information from junior high students to better understand their interests and needs regarding electives.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the form to ensure you understand what it's asking for.
2. Select "Student Preferences" if you have any special requests or requirements.
3. Choose from the options in "Past Courses" if you have taken courses in middle school, junior high, or high school.
4. Select any applicable electives from "Elective Preferences" that you are interested in.
5. If you have other electives or activities not listed, enter them in "Other Electives."
6. If you have a special request, select "Special Requests" and choose your preference.
7. If you want to request a specific course, enter the course name in "Course Request."
8. Provide a reason for your request in "Request Reason."
9. Enter the date you would like to have your request considered in "Request Date."
10. Select the time of year you would like to have your request considered in "Request Time."
11. Add any additional comments regarding your request in "Request Comment."
12. Finally, select the status of your request in "Elective Request Status."

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Preferences** (`Student Preferences`, text, optional): Please enter any special requests or requirements you have.
* **Past Courses** (`Past Courses`, select_multiple, optional): List courses previously taken in middle school, junior high, or high school.
* **Special Requests** (`Special Requests`, select_one, optional): Select your preference:
	+ Gifted and Talented
	+ Special Needs
	+ None
* **Elective Preferences** (`Elective Preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Art
	+ Drama
	+ Foreign Language
	+ Music
	+ Physical Education
	+ Robotics
	+ STEM
* **Other Electives** (`Other Electives`, text, optional): List other electives or activities not listed above.
* **Elective Request** (`Elective Request`, text, optional): Enter any additional electives or activities you would like to request.
* **Course Request** (`Course Request`, text, optional): Enter a course name.
* **Request Reason** (`Request Reason`, text, optional): Provide a reason for your request.
* **Request Date** (`Request Date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to have your request considered.
* **Request Time** (`Request Time`, time, optional): Select the time of year you would like to have your request considered.
* **Request Comment** (`Request Comment`, text, optional): Add any additional comments regarding your request.
* **Elective Request Status** (`Elective Request Status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your request:
	+ Under Review
	+ Approved
	+ Denied
	+ Hold
