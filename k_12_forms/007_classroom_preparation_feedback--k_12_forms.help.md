**Classroom Preparation Feedback - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate the classroom preparation for an upcoming educational session. It is used to assess the overall condition and readiness of a classroom for teaching and learning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1.  Enter the name or number of the classroom being evaluated.
2.  Enter the name of the person conducting the evaluation.
3.  Enter the date of the classroom preparation check.
4.  Select the grade level assigned to this classroom.
5.  Enter the name of the teacher assigned to this classroom.
6.  Rate the condition of the desks, chairs, and tables.
7.  Rate the overall cleanliness of the classroom.
8.  Assess the availability and organization of necessary classroom supplies.
9.  Rate the setup and functionality of classroom technology.
10. Assess the availability of safety equipment and supplies.
11. Rate the sufficiency and functionality of classroom lighting.
12. Rate the comfort level of the classroom temperature.
13. Rate the availability and organization of whiteboards, projectors, and teaching aids.
14. List any issues that need to be addressed in the classroom.
15. Rate the urgency of the identified issues.
16. Make recommendations for improving classroom readiness (optional).
17. Determine if the classroom is ready for instruction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Classroom Number or Name**: Enter the name or number of the classroom being evaluated.
* **Evaluator Name**: Enter the name of the person conducting the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date of the classroom preparation check.
* **Grade Level**: Select the grade level assigned to this classroom.
* **Teacher Assigned**: Enter the name of the teacher assigned to this classroom.
* **Furniture Condition**: Rate the condition of the desks, chairs, and tables.
* **Cleanliness Rating**: Rate the overall cleanliness of the classroom.
* **Supplies Availability**: Assess the availability and organization of necessary classroom supplies.
* **Technology Readiness**: Rate the setup and functionality of classroom technology.
* **Safety Equipment Check**: Assess the availability of safety equipment and supplies.
* **Lighting Adequacy**: Rate the sufficiency and functionality of classroom lighting.
* **Temperature Comfort**: Rate the comfort level of the classroom temperature.
* **Instructional Materials**: Rate the availability and organization of whiteboards, projectors, and teaching aids.
* **Issues Identified**: List any issues that need to be addressed in the classroom.
* **Priority Level**: Rate the urgency of the identified issues.
* **Improvement Suggestions**: Make recommendations for improving classroom readiness (optional).
* **Is Classroom Ready for Use**: Determine if the classroom is ready for instruction.

## Tips

* Be specific when rating the condition of the furniture, cleanliness, and other aspects of the classroom.
* Use the options provided to rate the condition of the classroom.
* Make sure to provide clear and accurate information about any issues that need to be addressed.
* Consider the teacher's needs and the needs of the students when making improvement suggestions.
* Determine if the classroom is ready for instruction based on the ratings and information provided.
