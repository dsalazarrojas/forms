# Student Leadership Project Funding Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Leadership Project Funding Request Form is used to request funding for student leadership projects that aim to promote student engagement, creativity, and development. This form is designed to gather essential information about the project, including its title, description, budget, and key contacts. It is crucial to fill out this form accurately to ensure that your project is properly reviewed and considered for funding.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Read carefully**: Before starting to fill out the form, review the entire form to understand what information is required and why.
2. **Fill out required fields**: Ensure that all required fields (marked as 'required: true') are completed accurately and comprehensively.
3. **Be specific and clear**: Use specific and clear answers to help reviewers understand your project and its needs.
4. **Attach necessary documents**: Depending on the specific requirements for your project, you may need to attach additional documents, such as project proposals or budget breakdowns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Title** (`project_title`): A brief title of your project that summarizes the key aspects of your project. This should be clear and concise.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`): A detailed description of your project, including its objectives, goals, and expected outcomes. This should be thorough and well-written.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`): A financial plan for your project, including how you plan to spend the allocated funds. This should be realistic and justified.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`): The location where your project will be held, including school or community setting. This is an important factor in determining feasibility and logistics.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`): A specific date when your project is expected to start.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`): A specific date when your project is expected to end.
* **Project Justification** (`project_justification`): A clear explanation of why your project is important and why it deserves funding. This should be well-argued and persuasive.
* **School District** (`school_district`): An indicator of whether your school district has approved or not approved your project. Be honest and accurate in your response.
* **School Principal** (`school_principal`): The name of the school principal involved in your project. This information will be useful for future contact and follow-up purposes.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`): The name of the teacher leading your project. This ensures that we can reach out to the right person.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`): The email address of the teacher leading your project. This allows for easy communication and updates.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`): The phone number of the teacher leading your project. This ensures that we can reach out to them if needed.
* **Project Lead** (`project_lead`): The name of the person leading your project. This should be the person responsible for project implementation and follow-up.
* **Project Lead Email** (`project_lead_email`): The email address of the project lead. This allows for easy communication and updates.
* **Project Lead Phone** (`project_lead_phone`): The phone number of the project lead. This ensures that we can reach out to them if needed.
* **Student Leadership Project** (`student_leadership_project`): A brief description of the student leadership project, including its purpose and goals.
* **Student Leadership Project ID** (`student_leadership_project_id`): A unique identifier for your project. This should be a number or code assigned to your project.
* **School Year** (`school_year`): The school year in which your project is being implemented.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`): The grade level that the project is targeting. This includes the specific grades (e.g., 9-12) that your project will cater to.
* **Student Count** (`student_count`): The number of students expected to participate in or be affected by your project.
* **Project Ideas** (`project_ideas`): A clear and concise explanation of your project ideas, including its objectives and expected outcomes.
* **Budget Allocation** (`budget_allocation`): A detailed breakdown of how your budget will be spent, including on what, why, and how much.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`): The current status of your project, which can be in progress, complete, pending, or on hold.
* **Total Budget Amount** (`total_budget_amount`): The total amount of funds requested for your project.
* **Project Start Time** (`project_start_time`): The specific time of day when your project is expected to start.

## Tips

* Double-check all required fields before submitting the form.
* Ensure that your answers are clear, concise, and well-written.
* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* Attach additional documents or resources that support your project proposal.
* Follow up with the form reviewer to discuss any questions or concerns they may have.
