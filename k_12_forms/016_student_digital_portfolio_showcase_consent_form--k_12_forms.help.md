# Student Digital Portfolio Showcase Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Digital Portfolio Showcase Consent Form is used to obtain consent from parents/guardians to showcase a student's digital portfolio. This form helps to ensure that the student's work is shared with the right people.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's ID number.
2. Provide the parent's name and email address.
3. Enter the parent's phone number.
4. Choose the correct showcase option(s) for the student's portfolio (e.g., Portfolio Only, Digital Showcase, Both).
5. Read and acknowledge the terms and conditions of the showcase.
6. Select 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm the student's consent to showcase their work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required): Enter the student's ID number as assigned by the school or institution.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter the parent's name.
* **Parent Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the parent's email address.
* **Parent Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the parent's phone number.
* **Showcase Options** (`showcase`, select multiple, required): Select the option(s) to showcase the student's portfolio (e.g., Portfolio Only, Digital Showcase, Both).
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms`, note, required): Read and acknowledge the terms and conditions of the showcase before proceeding.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select one, required): Select 'Yes' to confirm the student's consent to showcase their work, or 'No' to decline.
