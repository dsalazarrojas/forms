# Civil War Map Worksheet - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help students analyze and learn from a historical map related to the American Civil War. It guides the user through key questions and fields to collect information about the map, its creator, its purpose, and the student's insights and thoughts after studying it.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and understand what each field is asking you to do.
2. Answer each field based on your observations and thoughts about the map.
3. Be as specific as possible when answering each question.
4. Take your time to think carefully about each field before moving on to the next.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first and last name as you would like it to appear on this form.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your current grade level, which will help us understand your level of understanding and analysis.
* **Class or Course Name** (`class_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your class or course, which will help us understand your context and assignment.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your teacher or instructor, which will help us understand who assigned this activity.
* **Assignment Date** (`assignment_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when this activity was assigned, which will help us understand your timing and preparation.
* **Title of Map Being Analyzed** (`map_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the map you are analyzing, which will help us understand the focus of your activity.
* **Date of the Map** (`map_date`, `text`, optional): Enter the date when the map was created, which will give us more information about the map's historical context.
* **Who Created the Map** (`map_creator`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person or organization that created the map, which will give us more information about the map's creation.
* **What Was the Purpose of This Map** (`map_purpose`, `select_one`, required): Select the reason why the map was created, which will help us understand its intended use and significance.
* **What Geographic Area Does the Map Show** (`geographic_area`, `text`, required): Describe the region or area covered by the map, which will help us understand its scope and focus.
* **Which States or Territories Are Shown** (`states_shown`, `text`, required): List the states or territories visible on the map, which will help us understand its coverage and accuracy.
* **What Symbols Are Used on the Map** (`map_symbols`, `text`, required): Describe the symbols, colors, or other markings used on the map, which will help us understand its visual representation.
* **Are Battle Locations Marked** (`battle_locations`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the map shows battle locations, which will help us understand its focus and detail.
* **List Any Battles or Locations You Recognize** (`battles_named`, `text`, optional): Enter any battles or locations you recognize on the map, which will help us understand your familiarity with the map's content.
* **Are Troop Movements Shown** (`troop_movements`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the map shows troop movements, which will help us understand its dynamic and action.
* **Does the Map Show Union Territory, Confederate Territory, or Both** (`north_south`, `select_one`, required): Select what the map depicts, which will help us understand its perspective and bias.
* **How Accurate Do You Think This Map Is** (`map_accuracy`, `select_one`, required): Select your assessment of the map's accuracy, which will help us understand your critical thinking.
* **Does the Map Show Bias Toward One Side** (`map_bias`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you think the map shows bias toward one side, which will help us understand its perspective and accuracy.
* **What Evidence Suggests Bias** (`bias_evidence`, `text`, optional): Enter any evidence you find that suggests bias, which will help us understand your reasoning.
* **What Historical Event or Period Does This Map Relate To** (`historical_context`, `text`, required): Enter the historical event or period the map relates to, which will help us understand its context and significance.
* **What Did You Learn From Analyzing This Map** (`map_insights`, `text`, required): Enter any new information or insights you gained from analyzing the map, which will help us understand your learning and growth.
* **What Questions Do You Still Have** (`questions_remaining`, `text`, optional): Enter any questions or concerns you still have after analyzing the map, which will help us understand your ongoing inquiry.
* **How Difficult Was This Worksheet** (`difficulty_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your self-assessment of the difficulty level of this activity, which will help us understand your experience and feedback.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any other thoughts or comments you have about the map or activity, which will help us understand your overall experience.

## Tips

* Be thorough and careful when answering each field, as it will help us understand your analysis and insights.
* Use specific examples and descriptions when answering fields that ask for details.
* Take your time to think carefully about each field before moving on to the next.
* If you are unsure about a field, try to answer based on your best knowledge and judgment.
* If you have any questions or concerns, use the "What Questions Do You Still Have" field to share them with us.
