# School Curriculum Compliance Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Curriculum Compliance Audit Form is a tool used to assess and document the compliance status of a school's curriculum.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the school's curriculum and relevant policies to ensure accuracy and completeness of the information provided.
2.  Fill in the required fields (marked as `required`) with the relevant information.
3.  For fields that are not required, provide any available information or notes that may be relevant to the audit.
4.  Attach any relevant curriculum documents or supporting evidence as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **School Curriculum Audit Form** (`school_curriculum_audit_form`): This field is the main section for the user to provide a narrative overview of the school's curriculum.
    *   **Curriculum Review** (`school_curriculum_review`): Record the date the curriculum was last reviewed.
    *   **Curriculum Audit Notes** (`school_curriculum_audit_notes`): Provide notes and observations about the curriculum audit.
    *   **School District ID** (`school_district_id`): Enter the school district's ID number.
    *   **District Address** (`school_district_address`): Enter the address of the school district.
    *   **School Name** (`school_name`): Enter the name of the school.
    *   **Auditor Name** (`auditor_name`): Enter the name of the auditor or reviewer.
    *   **Auditor Email** (`auditor_email`): Enter the email address of the auditor or reviewer.
    *   **Auditor Phone** (`auditor_phone`): Enter the phone number of the auditor or reviewer.
    *   **Curriculum Document** (`curriculum_document_upload`): Attach relevant curriculum documents or supporting evidence.

## Tips

*   Make sure to double-check the provided information for accuracy and completeness.
*   If unsure, refer to school policies or seek clarification from school administrators.
*   Ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.
*   Attach relevant documents or evidence as needed.
*   Review and revise the form as necessary to ensure it is up-to-date and accurate.
