# open_ended_student_response_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect open-ended responses from students about their experiences, thoughts, and feelings. It is meant to be used for formative assessments or to collect feedback from students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and student ID if provided.
2. Answer the open-ended questions to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as you like.
3. If you prefer, you can also leave the contact information fields (email and phone) blank, as they are optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be recorded.
* **Student ID** (`Student ID`, number, optional): Enter your student ID number if you have one.
* **What is your favorite subject?** (`What is your favorite subject?`, text, optional): Share with us what subject you enjoy learning the most.
* **What do you like to do when you're not in school?** (`What do you like to do when you're not in school?`, text, optional): Tell us about a favorite hobby or activity you enjoy outside of school.
* **What's the most interesting thing you've learned this week?** (`What's the most interesting thing you've learned this week?`, text, optional): Share a recent discovery or skill you've learned.
* **What's something you've accomplished that you're proud of?** (`What's something you've accomplished that you're proud of?`, text, optional): Share a recent achievement that you're proud of.
* **What's the one thing you'd like to change about school?** (`What's the one thing you'd like to change about school?`, text, optional): Share a suggestion or criticism you have about your school experience.
* **Do you like your teacher?** (`Do you like your teacher?`, select one, optional): Select "True", "False", or "Maybe".
* **Do you like your school?** (`Do you like your school?`, select one, optional): Select "True", "False", or "Maybe".
* **Date** (`Date`, date, optional): Enter the date (in any format you like).
* **Time** (`Time`, time, optional): Enter the time (in any format you like).
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if you'd like to be contacted).
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted).
* **Note** (`Note`, note, optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments you have.
