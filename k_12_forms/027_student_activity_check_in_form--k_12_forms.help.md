# Student Activity Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to check in students attending events. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the student's name under "student_name".
2. Enter the name of the event in the "event_name" field.
3. Select the date of the event in the "date" field.
4. Enter the time of the event in the "time" field (if required for the event).
5. Enter the number of students attending in the "student_count" field.
6. Enter the name of the staff member overseeing the event in the "staff_name" field.
7. Select the student's status as a part of a group ("Yes" or "No") in the "student_ids" field.
8. Enter any additional notes regarding the student's attendance in the "notes" field (if necessary).
9. Enter the email of the staff member overseeing the event in the "staff_email" field.
10. Enter the phone number of the staff member overseeing the event in the "staff_phone" field.
11. Select the staff member's status as part of a group ("Yes" or "No") in the "staff_ids" field.
12. Enter any comments about the event in the "comments" field (if necessary).
13. Enter the staff member's notes regarding the event in the "staff_notes" field (if necessary).
14. Enter the name of the staff member overseeing the event in the "staff_name2" field (if necessary).
15. Enter the number of staff members attending the event in the "staff_count" field (if necessary).
16. Select the student's status as part of a group ("Yes" or "No") in the "student_ids2" field.
17. Select the staff member's status as part of a group ("Yes" or "No") in the "staff_ids2" field.
18. Enter the unique identifier for the event in the "event_id" field (if necessary).
19. Enter the unique identifier for the staff member overseeing the event in the "staff_id" field (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_name**: Enter the name of the student attending the event.
* **event_name**: Enter the name of the event the student is attending.
* **date**: Select the date of the event.
* **time**: Enter the time of the event (if required).
* **student_count**: Enter the number of students attending the event.
* **staff_name**: Enter the name of the staff member overseeing the event.
* **student_ids**: Select the student's status as part of a group ("Yes" or "No").
* **notes**: Enter any additional notes regarding the student's attendance.
* **staff_email**: Enter the email of the staff member overseeing the event.
* **staff_phone**: Enter the phone number of the staff member overseeing the event.
* **staff_ids**: Select the staff member's status as part of a group ("Yes" or "No").
* **comments**: Enter any comments about the event.
* **staff_notes**: Enter the staff member's notes regarding the event.
* **staff_name2**: Enter the name of the staff member overseeing the event (if necessary).
* **staff_count**: Enter the number of staff members attending the event (if necessary).
* **student_ids2**: Select the student's status as part of a group ("Yes" or "No").
* **staff_ids2**: Select the staff member's status as part of a group ("Yes" or "No").
* **event_id**: Enter the unique identifier for the event (if necessary).
* **staff_id**: Enter the unique identifier for the staff member overseeing the event (if necessary).
