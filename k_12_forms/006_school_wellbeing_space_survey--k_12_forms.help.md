# School Wellbeing Space Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Wellbeing Space Survey is designed to collect feedback and data on student well-being in a school setting, specifically on the physical spaces within the school where students typically spend their time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question about which type of space you usually spend time in (e.g., Outdoor spaces, Classrooms, Hallways).
2. Indicate how frequently you use the space (Very frequently, Somewhat frequently, Rarely).
3. Provide feedback on what can be improved on in the space (e.g., lighting, seating, temperature).
4. Check if you participate in any support programs offered by the school (True or False).
5. Leave any additional comments or thoughts about the space.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wellbeing spaces** (`wellbeing_spaces`, `select_one`): Choose the type of space where you usually spend time, such as Outdoor spaces, Classrooms, or Hallways.
* **How do you usually use the space?** (`space_usage`, `select_multiple`): Indicate how frequently you use the space, such as Very frequently, Somewhat frequently, or Rarely.
* **What can we improve on?** (`feedback`, `text`): Provide feedback on what can be improved in the space, such as lighting, seating, or temperature.
* **Do you participate in any support programs?** (`support_programs`, `select_one`): Check if you participate in any support programs offered by the school, such as counseling or mentorship programs.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`): Leave any additional comments or thoughts about the space.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`): Enter your email address (optional).
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`): Enter your phone number (optional).
