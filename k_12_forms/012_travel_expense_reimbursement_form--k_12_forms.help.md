# travel_expense_reimbursement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The travel expense reimbursement form is for employees to request reimbursement for business-related travel expenses. Only submit this form for travel that is approved by your manager and not for non-work-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form

### Completing the form

*   Start by selecting the "Yes" or "No" option to indicate whether you have already submitted a travel expense report for this trip.
*   Enter the travel dates for the trip, using the date picker provided in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
*   Please describe the destination of your business travel, e.g., city, country, etc.
*   Enter the total amount of expenses you would like to be reimbursed for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **travel_request** (`title`,  `select_multiple`, Required/Optional: Optional)
    +   Select "Yes" if you have already submitted a travel expense report for this trip, otherwise select "No".
*   **travel_dates** (`title`,  `date`, Required/Optional: Optional)
    +   Enter the start and end dates of your trip in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
*   **travel_destination** (`title`,  `text`, Required/Optional: Optional)
    +   Describe the destination of your business travel, e.g., city, country, etc.
*   **expense_amount** (`title`,  `number`, Required/Optional: Optional)
    +   Enter the total amount of expenses you would like to be reimbursed for.
