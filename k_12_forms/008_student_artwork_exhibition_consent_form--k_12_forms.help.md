# Student Artwork Exhibition Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from parents/guardians for their child's artwork to be displayed in a student exhibition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Select the relevant options for the "Parent Permission" field, indicating whether or not the parent/guardian gives their permission for the artwork to be displayed.
2. Enter the student's artwork title in the "Student Artwork Title" field.
3. Enter a brief description of the artwork in the "Student Artwork Description" field.
4. Select the relevant media type for the artwork in the "Artwork Media Type" field.
5. Select the relevant medium for the artwork in the "Artwork Medium" field.
6. Select the dimensions of the artwork in the "Artwork Dimensions" field.
7. Enter your signature and comments in the "Parent Signature" and "Parent Comments" fields, respectively.
8. Enter your signature and comments in the "Teacher Signature" and "Teacher Comments" fields, respectively.
9. Enter your signature and comments in the "School Signature" and "School Comments" fields, respectively.
10. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
11. Select the student's grade level in the "Student Grade" field.
12. Select the student's class in the "Student Class" field.
13. Enter the student's date of birth in the "Student Date of Birth" field.
14. Enter the student's contact information in the "Student Contact Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Permission** (`Parent Permission`, Select, Required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether or not the parent/guardian gives their permission for the artwork to be displayed.
* **Student Artwork Title** (`Student Artwork Title`, Text, Required): Enter a brief title for the student's artwork.
* **Student Artwork Description** (`Student Artwork Description`, Text, Required): Enter a brief description of the artwork.
* **Artwork Media Type** (`Artwork Media Type`, Select, Required): Select the type of media used in the artwork.
* **Artwork Medium** (`Artwork Medium`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the medium(s) used in the artwork.
* **Artwork Dimensions** (`Artwork Dimensions`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the dimensions of the artwork.
* **Parent Signature** (`Parent Signature`, Text, Required): Enter your signature as the parent/guardian.
* **Parent Comments** (`Parent Comments`, Text, Required): Enter any comments or notes from the parent/guardian.
* **Teacher Signature** (`Teacher Signature`, Text, Required): Enter your signature as the teacher.
* **Teacher Comments** (`Teacher Comments`, Text, Required): Enter any comments or notes from the teacher.
* **School Signature** (`School Signature`, Text, Required): Enter your signature as the school representative.
* **School Comments** (`School Comments`, Text, Required): Enter any comments or notes from the school representative.
* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, Text, Required): Enter the student's name.
* **Student Grade** (`Student Grade`, Select, Required): Select the student's grade level.
* **Student Class** (`Student Class`, Select, Required): Select the student's class.
* **Student Date of Birth** (`Student Date of Birth`, Date, Required): Enter the student's date of birth.
* **Student Contact Info** (`Student Contact Info`, Text, Required): Enter the student's contact information.

Note: All fields marked as "Required" must be filled in order to complete the form.
