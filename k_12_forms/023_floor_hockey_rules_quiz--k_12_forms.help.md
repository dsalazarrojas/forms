# Floor Hockey Rules Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from floor hockey players and coaches to ensure a safe and enjoyable game environment. It will help you provide your input on the rules, safety measures, and game details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (indicated by **required**)
2. Select the correct options for each multiple choice question (e.g., Rule Check, Game Safety Check, Coach Safety Check)
3. Provide accurate and complete information in the text fields (e.g., Game Info, Player Info, Coach Info, Additional Comments)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Game Info** (`game_info`, text, required): This field is for providing general information about the game, such as the game's objective, rules, or any unique features.
* **Player Info** (`player_info`, text, required): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Rule Check** (`rule_check`, select_one, required): Choose the correct answer to the question about the game's rules. This field has three options: Label 1, Label 2, and Label 3.
* **Safety Check** (`safety_check`, text, required): Answer the safety-related question in this field. This is an opportunity to report any safety concerns or incidents that occurred during the game.
* **Coach Safety Check** (`coach_safety_check`, select_multiple, required): Select all options that apply regarding coach safety during the game. This field has three options: Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3.
* **Practice Info** (`practice_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the game's practice schedule or setup.
* **Game Safety Check** (`game_safety_check`, select_one, required): Choose the correct answer to the question about game safety.
* **Coach Info** (`coach_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about the coach, such as their experience or qualifications.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any further comments or suggestions about the game or its administration.

## Tips
- Please answer all required fields accurately and completely.
- For select fields, choose the best option that fits your situation.
- If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to ask the game organizers or administrators.
