# Summer School Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer School Application Form is a document used by students to apply for a summer school program. The form collects information necessary for the application process, including personal details, payment information, and application dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the field marked "First Name."
2. Enter your last name in the field marked "Last Name."
3. Enter your email address in the field marked "Email."
4. Select the payment method you wish to use for tuition payments in the field marked "Tuition Method" (you may choose between "Square" and "PayPal"). This is a required field.
5. If paying by Square, enter the tuition amount in the field marked "Tuition Amount."
6. Choose your preferred application date in the field marked "Application Date" (this is not a required field).
7. Enter the time you would like to apply in the field marked "Application Time" (this is not a required field).
8. Enter any additional notes or comments you may have in the field marked "Note" (this is not a required field).
9. If applicable, enter the application fee amount in the field marked "Application Fee Amount" (this is not a required field).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name to help us get to know you better.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name to help us confirm your identity.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your application.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Tuition Method** (`tuition_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose between "Square" and "PayPal" for your tuition payments.
* **Tuition Amount** (`tuition_amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount you're paying for tuition, if paying by Square.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Choose a date for your application.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Choose a time for your application.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Enter any notes or comments you may have about your application.
* **Application Fee** (`application_fee`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information about your application fee.
* **Application Fee Amount** (`application_fee_amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount of the application fee.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose between "Square" and "PayPal" for your application fee payments.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, `date`, optional): Choose a date for your application.
* **Application Time** (`application_time`, `time`, optional): Choose a time for your application.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the student applying.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information in all fields.
* Review your application before submitting to ensure it's correct.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
