# Homework Assistance Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students registering for homework assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the 'Student Details' field with your name and any other relevant student information.
2. If desired, provide the 'Subjects and Schedule' field with the subjects you are working on and your schedule.
3. Fill out the 'Contact Information' field with your contact details, if you would like to be contacted for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Details
* **Student Details** (`Student Details`, `text`, required): Enter your name, ID number, or any other relevant student information.

### Subjects and Schedule
* **Subjects and Schedule** (`Subjects and Schedule`, `text`, optional): If you want to specify the subjects you are working on, please list them here. You can also indicate your schedule, such as your class schedule or the time you usually study.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required): Enter your email address, phone number, or other contact information so we can reach out to you.
