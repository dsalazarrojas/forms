# Leave Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to process leave requests from employees. Fill in your information to submit a leave request, which will then be reviewed and approved or denied by the relevant department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Employee Name field with your employee identification information.
2. Choose a valid Leave Start Date for your leave.
3. Choose a valid Leave End Date for your leave.
4. Provide a clear Reason For Leave to explain your absence.
5. Enter your Employee ID for your employee records.
6. Provide your Contact Email and Contact Phone to enable easy communication with you.
7. Select your Department from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Name
• **Employee Name** (employee_name, text, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your employee badge or HR records.

### Leave Start Date
• **Leave Start Date** (leave_start_date, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to start your leave in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

### Leave End Date
• **Leave End Date** (leave_end_date, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to end your leave in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

### Reason For Leave
• **Reason For Leave** (reason_for_leave, text, optional): Provide a clear and concise reason for your leave, so your department can understand your needs.

### Employee ID
• **Employee ID** (employee_id, number, optional): Enter your unique employee identification number.

### Contact Email
• **Contact Email** (contact_email, email, optional): Enter your work email to enable easy communication with you.

### Contact Phone
• **Contact Phone** (contact_phone, text, optional): Enter your work phone number for further communication.

### Department
• **Department** (department, text, optional): Select your department from the provided options to ensure your leave is approved by the right team.
