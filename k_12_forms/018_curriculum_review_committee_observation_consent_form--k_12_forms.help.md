# Curriculum Review Committee Observation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document observations made by teachers or observers during classroom activities and to ensure that all parties involved in the curriculum review process are aware of and consent to the observation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the "first name" field with the name of the person being observed.
2. Fill in the "last name" field with the last name of the person being observed.
3. Enter the name of the teacher or observer in the "teacher name" field.
4. Enter the date of the observation in the "date observed" field.
5. Enter the time of the observation in the "time observed" field.
6. Enter the name of the school where the observation took place in the "school name" field.
7. Enter the name of the classroom where the observation took place in the "classroom name" field.
8. Enter the grade level of the students being observed in the "grade level" field.
9. Enter the name of the person or organization conducting the observation in the "observed by" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (text, optional): This field collects the first name of the person being observed.
* **Last name** (text, optional): This field collects the last name of the person being observed.
* **Teacher name** (text, optional): This field collects the name of the teacher or observer.
* **Date Observed** (date, optional): This field collects the date when the observation took place.
* **Time Observed** (time, optional): This field collects the time when the observation took place.
* **School name** (text, optional): This field collects the name of the school where the observation occurred.
* **Classroom name** (text, optional): This field collects the name of the classroom where the observation took place.
* **Grade Level** (number, optional): This field collects the grade level of the students being observed. Note: This field should be filled in with the numerical value, e.g., 9 for 9th grade.
* **Observed by** (text, optional): This field collects the name of the person or organization conducting the observation.
