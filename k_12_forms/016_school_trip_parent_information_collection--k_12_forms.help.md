# school_trip_parent_information_collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from parents of students who are going on a school trip. The purpose of this form is to gather details about each student and their parent, including contact information and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Student Name** in the first field.
2. Enter the **Student Grade** in the second field.
3. Enter the **Date of Birth** in the format mm/dd/yyyy in the third field.
4. Enter the **Parent Name** in the fourth field.
5. Enter the **Parent Email** in the fifth field.
6. Enter the **Parent Phone** in the sixth field.
7. Enter the **Teacher Name** in the seventh field.
8. Enter the **Student Address** in the eighth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the student.
* **Student Grade** (number, required: false): Enter the grade of the student.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required: false): Enter the date of birth of the student in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Parent Name** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the parent.
* **Parent Email** (email, required: false): Enter the email address of the parent.
* **Parent Phone** (text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the parent.
* **Teacher Name** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the teacher accompanying the student on the trip.
* **Student Address** (text, required: false): Enter the address of the student.
