# Student Baptism Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from students who have expressed interest in baptism, including personal details and preferences for the baptism ceremony.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Date of Birth on the first page.
2. Select your Faith Background on the second page.
3. Indicate your interest in baptism and your parents' consent on the third page.
4. Enter your contact number, address, city, and church name on the next two pages.
5. Provide details for the pastor's contact information, baptism date, time, location, and type on the next four pages.
6. Add any comments or additional notes about your baptism interest on the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification or passport.
* **Last Name** (Text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification or passport.
* **Email** (Email, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted about the baptism ceremony.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Faith Background** (Select Multiple, optional): Select all the faith backgrounds that apply to you.
* **Interested In Baptism** (Select One, required): Indicate whether you are interested in undergoing baptism.
* **Parents Consent** (Select One, required): Indicate whether your parents have given consent for your baptism.
* **Contact Number** (Text, optional): Enter your contact number so we can reach out to you about the baptism ceremony.
* **Address** (Text, required): Enter your address so we can send you information about the baptism ceremony.
* **City** (Text, required): Enter the city where you would like to have the baptism ceremony.
* **Church Name** (Text, required): Enter the name of the church where you would like to have the baptism ceremony.
* **Church Address** (Text, required): Enter the address of the church where you would like to have the baptism ceremony.
* **Pastor's Name** (Text, required): Enter the name of the pastor who will perform the baptism ceremony.
* **Pastor's Phone** (Text, required): Enter the phone number of the pastor who will perform the baptism ceremony.
* **Baptism Date** (Date, required): Enter the date for the baptism ceremony in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Baptism Time** (Time, required): Enter the time for the baptism ceremony in the format HH:MM.
* **Baptism Location** (Select One, required): Indicate whether the baptism ceremony will be held at the church or at a different location.
* **Baptism Type** (Select One, required): Indicate the type of baptism you are interested in.
* **Baptised At** (Select One, required): Indicate whether you will be baptised alone or with a group.
* **Follow-up Info** (Text, required): Enter any follow-up information you would like to provide about your baptism interest.
* **Baptism Info** (Text, required): Enter any additional information about your baptism interest.
* **Comments** (Text, required): Enter any comments or questions you have about your baptism interest.
* **Student's Baptism Interest Form 1** (Text, optional): Enter any additional information about your baptism interest.
* **Student's Baptism Interest Form 2** (Text, optional): Enter any additional information about your baptism interest.
* **Student's Baptism Interest Form 3** (Text, optional): Enter any additional information about your baptism interest.
