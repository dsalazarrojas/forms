# Kindergarten Readiness Skills Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the readiness of a preschooler for formal kindergarten education. It covers various skills and abilities that a child should have developed before entering kindergarten. The assessment will help educators and caregivers understand the strengths and weaknesses of the child and make necessary adjustments to support their development.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each question carefully and answer according to the instructions provided.
- For multiple-choice questions, select the most accurate answer from the options provided.
- For open-ended questions, provide a brief but detailed response.
- Make sure to answer all questions, even if you're unsure about some.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Preschooler Info** (`preschooler_info`, text, optional): This is a text field where you can provide any additional information about the preschooler, such as their name, birthdate, or any relevant medical conditions.

- **Social Skills** (`social_skills`, number): This question asks you to rate the preschooler's social skills on a scale of 1-5. Please consider how well the child interacts with others, shares toys and materials, takes turns, and shows empathy towards others.

- **Language Skills** (`language_skills`, text, required): Please describe the preschooler's language skills, including their ability to speak, write, and communicate effectively.

- **Fine Motor Skills** (`fine_motor_skills`, number): Rate the preschooler's fine motor skills on a scale of 1-5, considering their ability to use scissors, draw, and write.

- **Gross Motor Skills** (`gross_motor_skills`, select_one, required): Select one of the options provided: Riding a bike, Dancing, Jumping, or Playing ball. This question assesses the preschooler's gross motor skills, such as their ability to balance, move with coordination, and engage in physical activities.

- **Emotional Skills** (`emotional_skills`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options from the list: Calm, Angry, Happy, Sad, Frustrated. This question evaluates the preschooler's emotional intelligence, including their ability to manage their emotions, empathize with others, and regulate their behavior.

- **Math Skills** (`math_skills`, number, required): Rate the preschooler's math skills on a scale of 1-5, considering their understanding of numbers, counting, and basic math concepts.

- **Reading Skills** (`reading_skills`, select_one, required): Select the most accurate option from the list: Reading, Non-Reader. This question checks the preschooler's reading abilities, including their understanding of books and written materials.

- **Writing Skills** (`writing_skills`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options from the list: Writing, Non-Writer. This question assesses the preschooler's writing abilities, including their ability to write their name, write simple sentences, and create stories.

- **Science Skills** (`science_skills`, number, optional): Rate the preschooler's science skills on a scale of 1-5, considering their understanding of the natural world, scientific concepts, and experimentation.

- **Social-Emotional Skills** (`social_emotional_skills`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options from the list: Self-Aware, Self-Regulation, Social Skills. This question evaluates the preschooler's social-emotional intelligence, including their ability to understand self-awareness, self-regulate, and interact with others.

- **Physical Skills** (`physical_skills`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options from the list: Dancing, Jumping, Running. This question assesses the preschooler's physical abilities, including their coordination, balance, and overall physical fitness.

- **Art-Creativity Skills** (`art_creativity_skills`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable options from the list: Artistic, Musical, Dramatic. This question evaluates the preschooler's artistic abilities, including their creativity, imagination, and performance skills.

- **Self-Concept Skills** (`self_concept_skills`, text, required): Please describe the preschooler's self-concept skills, including their understanding of their own identity, self-esteem, and self-worth.

- **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options from the list: Verbal, Nonverbal. This question assesses the preschooler's communication abilities, including their verbal and nonverbal skills, such as speaking, listening, and interacting with others.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all questions honestly and accurately.
- If unsure about any question, consider consulting with a teacher or caregiver.
- Take your time to complete the form, and do not rush through it.
