# Monthly Task Checklist For Children - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to track the progress and challenges faced by children in various tasks or activities over a month. It helps educators or childcare providers to monitor the children's efforts and challenges in order to provide support and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the list of tasks on the form and identify the task number that you are reporting on.
2. Enter the effort or score for the task (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).
3. Enter the challenges faced by the child during the task (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).
4. Provide feedback from the guardian or caregiver on the child's progress and challenges.
5. Review the progress reviews section and select the most relevant option (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, etc.).
6. Finally, submit the form by selecting the corresponding option for each task.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`monthly_task_checklist`, `number`, required: false): This is the title of the form, which is displayed at the top of the page.
* **Effort** (`effort`, `number`, required: false): Enter the effort or score for the task. For example, if the child completed the task easily, you can enter 1. If the child faced significant challenges, you can enter 3.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, `number`, required: false): Enter the challenges faced by the child during the task. For example, if the child had trouble with reading, you can enter 1.
* **Guardian Feedback** (`guardian_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide feedback from the guardian or caregiver on the child's progress and challenges.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required: false): This is the space for the guardian or caregiver to sign off on the form.
* **Progress Reviews** (`progress_reviews`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the most relevant option for each task.
* **Task 1-6** (`task_1`, `task_2`, `task_3`, `task_4`, `task_5`, `task_6`, `number`, required: false): These are the specific tasks or activities that the child is working on. Enter the effort or score for each task.
* **Effort 1-6** (`effort_1`, `effort_2`, `effort_3`, `effort_4`, `effort_5`, `effort_6`, `number`, required: false): Enter the effort or score for each task.
* **Challenges 1-6** (`challenges_1`, `challenges_2`, `challenges_3`, `challenges_4`, `challenges_5`, `challenges_6`, `number`, required: false): Enter the challenges faced by the child during each task.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the option to submit the form. You can choose 'Yes' or 'No'.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form for each task accurately and honestly.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that you have provided all necessary information.
* If you need help or clarification on any of the fields, contact your supervisor or support team.
