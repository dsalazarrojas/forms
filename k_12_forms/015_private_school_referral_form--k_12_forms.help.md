# private_school_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The private school referral form is used to collect and store information about students referred to a private school. This information helps the school to identify potential students and assess their eligibility for enrollment or other school-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the student's details.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information about the student.
3. Review your submission before submitting it to the school.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School ID** (`1`): Enter the student's school ID, if available.
* **Student Name** (`2`): Enter the student's name as per the provided hint.
* **Grade Level** (`3`): Select the student's current grade level from the provided options.
* **Grade Range** (`4`): Select the student's grade range from the provided options.
* **School Name** (`5`): Enter the name of the student's school.
* **Student Email** (`6`): Enter the student's email address.
* **School Email** (`7`): Enter the email address of the school or contact person.
* **Student Phone** (`8`): Enter the student's phone number.
* **School Phone** (`9`): Enter the phone number of the school or contact person.
* **Referrer Name** (`10`): Enter the name of the person referring the student to the school.
* **Referrer Email** (`11`): Enter the email address of the referrer.
* **Referrer Phone** (`12`): Enter the phone number of the referrer.
* **Student Address** (`13`): Enter the student's permanent address.
* **School Address** (`14`): Enter the address of the school.
* **Student City** (`15`): Enter the city of the student's residence.
* **Student State** (`16`): Select the state where the student resides from the provided options.
* **Student Zip** (`17`): Enter the student's zip code.
* **School Zip** (`18`): Enter the school's zip code.
* **Date of Birth** (`19`): Enter the student's date of birth.
* **Date of Interest** (`20`): Enter the date when the student is interested in joining the school.
* **Student Gender** (`22`): Select the student's gender from the provided options.
* **Student Race** (`23`): Select all applicable races for the student from the provided options.
* **Student English Proficiency** (`24`): Select the student's English proficiency from the provided options.
* **School Comments** (`25`): Enter any additional comments about the student.
