# Student Transportation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Transportation Form is designed to collect essential student and guardian information for transportation purposes, ensuring efficient and safe transportation services are provided to students.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form accurately**: Provide accurate and up-to-date information for the student and guardian fields.
2. **Use real email addresses**: Enter valid email addresses for both student and guardian email fields.
3. **Select the correct mode of transportation**: Choose the mode of transportation that applies to the student.
4. **Enter dates of birth**: Ensure dates of birth are entered in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
5. **Enter addresses correctly**: Enter the correct address, city, and zip code for both student and school fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the student.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the guardian or parent responsible for the student.
* **Guardian Email** (`guardian_email`, email, required): Enter the guardian's email address.
* **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, text, required): Enter the guardian's phone number.
* **Student Date of Birth** (`student_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the student's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Guardian Date of Birth** (`guardian_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the guardian's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, required): Enter the student's home address.
* **Student City** (`student_city`, text, required): Enter the city where the student resides.
* **Student State** (`student_state`, select_one, required): Select the state where the student resides.
* **Student Zip** (`student_zip`, number, required): Enter the student's zip code.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, select_multiple, optional): Select the student's grade level.
* **Mode of Transportation** (`mode_of_transportation`, select_one, optional): Choose the mode of transportation used for the student.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school the student attends.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, required): Enter the school's email address.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, required): Enter the school's phone number.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the teacher responsible for the student.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, required): Enter the teacher's email address.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, text, required): Enter the teacher's phone number.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, required): Enter the school's address.
* **School City** (`school_city`, text, required): Enter the city where the school is located.
* **School State** (`school_state`, select_one, required): Select the state where the school is located.
* **School Zip** (`school_zip`, number, required): Enter the school's zip code.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the student or school.
