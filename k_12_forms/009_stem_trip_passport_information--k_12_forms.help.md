# stem_trip_passport_information - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "stem_trip_passport_information" form is designed to collect critical information from travelers and trip participants. This form is meant to be used for organizational purposes to streamline the collection of data necessary for trip management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in a clear and concise manner.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed.
3. Review the form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Passport Details (Page 1, Field 1)
* **Passport Details** (`Passport Details`, `text`, required/optional: False): This field is for collecting passport details. Please enter your passport number, country of issue, and any other relevant information.

### Participant Contact (Page 1, Field 2)
* **Participant Contact** (`Participant Contact`, `text`, required/optional: False): This field is for providing contact information of the participant. Enter the participant's name, phone number, and email address to keep them in touch.

### Emergency Contact (Page 1, Field 3)
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, `text`, required/optional: False): In the event of an emergency, enter the contact information of a person who should be contacted.

### Participant Emergency (Page 1, Field 4)
* **Participant Emergency** (`Participant Emergency`, `text`, required/optional: False): If you have any medical conditions or other critical information, please provide it here to ensure proper emergency response.

## Tips
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* Double-check for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact our team.
