# Student Attendance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Attendance Form is a simple form for recording student attendance information. It is intended to be completed by a student, teacher, or other authorized personnel to provide an overview of the student's attendance status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the student's name in the "student_name" field.
2. Enter the name of the class the student is attending in the "class_name" field.
3. Enter the date of the attendance in the "date" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Select the time of the attendance in the "time" field in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
5. Choose the attendance status (e.g., Absent, Late, Leave, or Present) in the "status" field.
6. Add any additional comments or notes about the student's attendance in the "note" field.
7. Enter the student's email address in the "email" field.
8. Enter the student's phone number in the "phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the student who attended the class.
* **class_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the class the student attended.
* **date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the attendance in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of the attendance in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **status** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the attendance status (e.g., Absent, Late, Leave, or Present).
* **note** (Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the student's attendance.
* **email** (Email, Optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the student's phone number.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields, even if they are optional.
- Double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
- If the student is absent or late, provide a reason for the status in the "note" field.
- If the student has any other contact information, make sure to include it in the "email" and "phone" fields.
