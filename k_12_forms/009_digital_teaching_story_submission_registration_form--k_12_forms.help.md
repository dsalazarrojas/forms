# Digital Teaching Story Submission Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for teachers or educators to submit their digital teaching stories, which will be reviewed and potentially used to develop the platform further.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Teaching Story ID**, a unique identifier for your submission.
2. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** as they appear on your official records.
3. Enter your **Email** address associated with your school or institution (if applicable).
4. Indicate whether you teach at a **School** that uses this platform.
5. Choose the **Grade Level** of your students (High or Low).
6. Select the **Subject** you teach (Yes or No).
7. Write a brief description of your **Teaching Story**, detailing your experience and the story behind it.
8. Enter the **Submission Date** and **Submission Time** when you completed this form.
9. Indicate who you **Submitted To**, i.e., the intended audience for your submission.
10. Choose the **Submission Status** of your story (Active or Inactive).
11. Select the **Teaching Story Type** (Yes or No).
12. Enter the **School Email**, **School Phone**, and **School Address** associated with your school (if applicable).
13. Indicate whether you have a **School** (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teaching Story ID**: A unique identifier for your submission.
* **First Name**: Your first name as recorded on your official records.
* **Last Name**: Your last name as recorded on your official records.
* **Email**: Your email address associated with your school or institution (if applicable).
* **School**: Indicate whether you teach at a school using this platform (Yes or No).
* **Grade Level**: The grade level of your students (High or Low).
* **Subject**: The subject you teach (Yes or No).
* **Teaching Story**: A brief description of your teaching story.
* **Submission Date**: The date you submitted this form.
* **Submission Time**: The time you submitted this form.
* **Submitted To**: The intended audience for your submission.
* **Submission Status**: The status of your submission (Active or Inactive).
* **Teaching Story Type**: The type of your teaching story (Yes or No).
* **School Email**: Your school's email address (if applicable).
* **School Phone**: Your school's phone number (if applicable).
* **School Address**: The address of your school (if applicable).
* **School**: Indicate whether your school is associated with this platform (Yes or No).

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you've already submitted a story previously, do not fill out this form again.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the platform administrators.
* This form will not be used for non-deduplicative purposes, so your information will not be shared or used without your consent.
