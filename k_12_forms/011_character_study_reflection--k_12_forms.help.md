# Character Study Reflection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you analyze a character from a book or story by exploring their traits, motivations, and impact on the story.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by providing your name and grade level to ensure the correct context for your reflection.
2. Select the title of the book or story featuring the character you'd like to analyze.
3. Enter the name of the author who wrote the book or story.
4. Identify the main character you'll be focusing on.
5. Choose the role of the character within the story (e.g., protagonist, antagonist, supporting character, minor character, or narrator).
6. List three personality traits of the character.
7. Describe the motivations that drive the character's actions.
8. Identify the main challenge or problem faced by the character.
9. Describe how the character responds to challenges.
10. Decide whether the character undergoes positive or negative change throughout the story.
11. Optionally, share a quote that resonates with you about or from the character, along with the page number.
12. Indicate how closely you relate to this character.
13. Share any lessons you learned from the character.
14. Rate the character overall based on your opinion.
15. Decide whether you would recommend this character to others.
16. If you have more thoughts or reflections, use the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Your full name to provide context for your reflection.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required): Your current grade level to ensure the correct context.
* **Book Title** (`book_title`, text, required): The title of the book or story featuring the character.
* **Author Name** (`author_name`, text, required): The name of the author who wrote the book or story.
* **Character You Studied** (`character_name`, text, required): The name of the main character you're analyzing.
* **What role does this character play** (`character_role`, select_one, required): Choose the role of the character within the story (e.g., protagonist, antagonist, etc.).
* **Describe three character traits** (`character_traits`, text, required): List three personality characteristics of the character.
* **What motivates this character** (`character_motivation`, text, required): Describe the motivations that drive the character's actions.
* **What challenge does the character face** (`character_challenge`, text, required): Identify the main conflict or problem faced by the character.
* **How does the character respond to challenges** (`character_response`, text, required): Describe the character's reaction and choices in response to challenges.
* **Does the character change throughout the story** (`character_change`, select_one, required): Decide whether the character undergoes positive or negative change.
* **Share a quote about or from the character** (`favorite_quote`, text, optional): Optionally, share a quote that resonates with you about or from the character, along with the page number.
* **Can you relate to this character** (`character_connection`, select_one, required): Indicate how closely you relate to this character.
* **What lesson did you learn from this character** (`lesson_learned`, text, required): Share any lessons you learned from analyzing the character.
* **Rate this character** (`character_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the character based on your overall opinion.
* **Would you recommend this character to others** (`recommend_character`, select_one, required): Decide whether you would recommend this character to others.
* **Additional Thoughts or Reflections** (`additional_thoughts`, text, optional): Share any additional insights or thoughts you have about the character.

This guide will walk you through each field of the form, making it easier for users to complete the "Character Study Reflection" form accurately and effectively.
