# out_of_district_transfer_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The out_of_district_transfer_application_form is used by schools to process student information and transfer requests from out-of-district students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's information (Section 1: Student Information).
2. Enter the parent's information (Section 2: Parent Information).
3. Provide the student's address (Section 3: Student Address).
4. Enter the name of the student's current school (Section 4: School Name).
5. Explain the reason for the transfer request (Section 5: Reason For Transfer).
6. Provide the student's current school information (Section 6: Current School Info).
7. Select the student's grade level (Section 7: Grade Level).
8. Select the subject areas of interest (Section 8: Subject Areas of Interest).
9. Choose the preferred start and end dates for the transfer (Section 9: Preferred Start Date and 10: Preferred End Date).
10. Sign the form to acknowledge the transfer request (Section 11: Parent Signature).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (Section 1, Student Info): Enter the student's name, date of birth, and other relevant student details.
* **Parent Information** (Section 2, Parent Info): Enter the parent's name and contact information.
* **Student Address** (Section 3): Provide the student's current address.
* **School Name** (Section 4): Enter the name of the student's current school.
* **Reason For Transfer** (Section 5): Explain why the student is requesting to transfer to another district.
* **Current School Info** (Section 6): Provide any additional information about the student's current school.
* **Grade Level** (Section 7, Grade Level): Select the student's grade level from the options: 9th, 10th, or 11th.
* **Subject Areas of Interest** (Section 8, Subject Areas of Interest): Select all the subject areas the student is interested in (multiple selection allowed).
* **Preferred Start Date** (Section 9, Preferred Start Date): Choose the preferred date for the student's start at the new school.
* **Preferred End Date** (Section 10, Preferred End Date): Choose the preferred date for the student's end at the new school.
* **Parent Signature** (Section 11, Parent Signature): Sign to acknowledge the transfer request.
