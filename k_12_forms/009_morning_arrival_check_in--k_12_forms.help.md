# Morning Arrival Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Morning Arrival Check In form is used to record and report morning arrival information of students, faculty, or staff. It helps to track attendance, health status, and other relevant details for administrative or monitoring purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, please follow these steps:

1.  Check the box next to "Morning Arrival Status" to report your morning arrival status.
2.  Enter your "Health Status" to report your current health status.
3.  Provide your "Student Id" if applicable.
4.  Enter your "Morning Arrival Time" and "Morning Arrival Date" to report your morning arrival details.
5.  If applicable, enter "Notes" for any additional comments or information.
6.  Select "Morning Arrival Check In Form Request" to confirm that you have submitted the form.
7.  Ensure that "Email Address" is entered correctly, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Morning Arrival Status** (`morning_arrival_status`, required): Report your morning arrival status as "On Time", "Late", or "Unknown".
*   **Health Status** (`health_status`, optional): Choose your current health status from "Good", "Sick", or "Out".
*   **Student Id** (`student_id`, optional): Enter your student ID number if you are a student.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, optional): Provide any additional comments or information.
*   **Morning Arrival Check In Form Request** (`morning_arrival_check_in_form_request`, optional): Select "True" to confirm that you have submitted the form.
*   **Morning Arrival Time** (`morning_arrival_time`, optional): Enter your morning arrival time.
*   **Morning Arrival Date** (`morning_arrival_date`, optional): Enter your morning arrival date.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that all required fields are filled accurately and completely.
*   If you experience any issues with the form, contact the system administrator for assistance.
*   This form is designed to be a single instance per user per day, so avoid submitting multiple forms for the same day.
