# Parental Consent Form Upload - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from parents or guardians for their child's participation in a program, event, or activity. It is an essential document that records the child's and the organization's understanding of the program's terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your consent for your child's participation.
*   Sign your name in the "Signature" field to confirm your agreement.
*   Upload any relevant files related to your child's participation, such as medical information or emergency contact details.
*   Enter the date and time of submission.
*   Provide your email address so we can contact you if needed.
*   Enter your phone number in case we need to reach you quickly.
*   Add any additional notes or comments related to your child's participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Parental Consent** (select\_one, `parent_consent`): Select "Yes" if you consent to your child's participation, or "No" if you do not.
*   **Signature** (text, `signature`): Please sign your name to confirm your agreement. This is a required field.
*   **File Upload** (text, `file_upload`): You can upload any relevant files related to your child's participation. This is an optional field.
*   **Date** (date, `date`): Enter the date of submission.
*   **Time** (time, `time`): Enter the time of submission.
*   **Email** (email, `email`): Provide your email address so we can contact you if needed.
*   **Phone** (text, `phone`): Enter your phone number in case we need to reach you quickly.
*   **Note** (note, `note`): Add any additional notes or comments related to your child's participation.
