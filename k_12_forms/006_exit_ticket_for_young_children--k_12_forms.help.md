# Exit Ticket For Young Children - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a document that serves as proof of a young child's participation in a lesson or activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure the child understands the purpose and significance of the form.
2. Fill in the "Lesson Name" field with the name of the lesson or activity the child participated in.
3. Fill in the "Teacher Name" field with your name.
4. Fill in the "Student Name" field with the child's name.
5. Answer the "Subject" question with "Yes" or "No", indicating whether the child has completed the lesson or activity.
6. If "Student Grade" is relevant, fill in "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the child has met the grade requirements.
7. Fill in the "Teacher Comments" field with any observations or feedback you have for the child.
8. Have the child fill in the "Student Comments" field with their own thoughts or feelings about the lesson or activity.
9. If you are a teacher, sign the "Teacher Signature" field with your name.
10. If the child is old enough to sign, have them sign the "Student Signature" field with their name.
11. Fill in the "Date Submitted" field with the date the form was completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lesson Name (1)**: The name of the lesson or activity the child participated in.
* **Teacher Name (2)**: Your name as the teacher.
* **Student Name (3)**: The child's name, as recorded on their student record.
* **Subject (4)**: A checkbox indicating whether the child has completed the lesson or activity. Choose "Yes" if they have, "No" if not.
* **Student Grade (5)**: If applicable, choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the child has met the grade requirements.
* **Teacher Comments (6)**: A text field for you to record any observations or feedback you have for the child.
* **Student Comments (7)**: A text field for the child to record their own thoughts or feelings about the lesson or activity.
* **Student Signature (8)**: The child's name signed with their own hand, indicating their understanding of the form.
* **Teacher Signature (9)**: Your name signed with your own hand, indicating your verification of the form completion.
* **Date Submitted (10)**: The date the form was completed.

## Tips

* Always ensure the child understands the purpose and significance of the form before completing it.
* Use clear and child-friendly language in the "Teacher Comments" and "Student Comments" fields.
* Have the child sign the "Student Signature" field only if they are old enough to write their name legibly. 
* Keep the "Date Submitted" field up-to-date to ensure accurate record-keeping.
