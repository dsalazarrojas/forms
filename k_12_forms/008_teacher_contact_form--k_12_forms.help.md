# Teacher Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for parents or guardians to provide contact information and ask questions or concerns to a school or educational institution. It allows you to reach out to teachers or educators for support, guidance, or clarification on various matters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out your name and contact information in the "Parent Information" field.
* Write your question or concern in the "question" field.
* Optionally, provide your email address in the "email" field for further communication.
* Leave a response for the teacher or educator in the "teacher_response" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Information (Parent Information)** (`text`, required/optional): This field is where you can provide your name and contact details for the teacher or educator to reach out to you.
* **question (question)** (`text`, required/optional): This field is where you can write your query or concern for the teacher or educator to respond to.
* **teacher_response (teacher_response)** (`note`, required/optional): This field is where the teacher or educator can respond to your query or concern.
* **email (email)** (`email`, required/optional): This field is where you can provide your email address for the teacher or educator to contact you back.
