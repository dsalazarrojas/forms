# IEP Scheduling Notes - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect notes and information related to Individualized Education Program (IEP) meetings. It helps gather relevant details about the meeting, including the date, attendees, meeting purpose, summary, follow-up actions, next steps, and next meeting date.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Meeting Date" field and select the date of the IEP meeting.
2. In the "Select Attendees" field, choose all the attendees that will be participating in the meeting. You can select multiple attendees from the provided options.
3. In the "Meeting Purpose" field, briefly describe the purpose of the meeting.
4. In the "Summary Notes" field, provide a detailed summary of the meeting.
5. In the "Follow-up Actions" field, list any follow-up actions that will be taken after the meeting.
6. In the "Next Steps" field, describe any next steps or deadlines for the meeting.
7. In the "Next Meeting" field, select the date of the next IEP meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Date (id: 1)**: Select the date of the IEP meeting you are planning or have attended. This field is not required, but it helps to track the meeting schedule.
* **Select Attendees (id: 2)**: Choose all the attendees that will be participating in the meeting. The provided options include John Doe, Jane Doe, and Bob Smith.
* **Meeting Purpose (id: 3)**: Briefly describe the purpose of the meeting. This field helps to clarify the meeting's objectives and goals.
* **Summary Notes (id: 4)**: Provide a detailed summary of the meeting. This field is useful for capturing key points and outcomes.
* **Follow-up Actions (id: 5)**: List any follow-up actions that will be taken after the meeting. This helps to track and ensure tasks are completed.
* **Next Steps (id: 6)**: Describe any next steps or deadlines for the meeting. This field is useful for establishing clear expectations and timelines.
* **Next Meeting (id: 7)**: Select the date of the next IEP meeting. This field helps to schedule and plan for future meetings.
