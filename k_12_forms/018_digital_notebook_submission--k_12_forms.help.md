# Digital Notebook Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Digital Notebook Submission form is used to collect and record information on student assignments, grades, and other related details from students, teachers, and schools.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form successfully, please follow these steps:

1. Make sure you are in the correct context and have access to the form.
2. Select the correct options for each field, as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title:** (Digital Notebook Submission Form)
* **Grade Level:** (grade_level) - The grade level of the student. This is a required field.
* **Student Name:** (student_name) - The student's name. This is an optional field.
* **Assignment Name:** (assignment_name) - The name of the assignment. You can select one or multiple options from the dropdown menu. This is a required field.
* **Assignment Date:** (assignment_date) - The date of the assignment. This field is optional.
* **Submission Comments:** (submission_comments) - Any comments or feedback from the student. This is an optional field.
* **Submission File:** (submission_file) - A file attachment for the submission. This is optional.
* **Teacher Name:** (teacher_name) - The teacher's name. This is a required field.
* **Teacher Email:** (teacher_email) - The teacher's email address. This is a required field.
* **Teacher Phone:** (teacher_phone) - The teacher's phone number. This is a required field.
* **School Name:** (school_name) - The name of the school. This is an optional field.
* **Student Phone:** (student_phone) - The student's phone number. This is optional.
* **Submission Time:** (submission_time) - The time of the submission. This field is optional.
* **Teacher Comments:** (teacher_comments) - Comments or feedback from the teacher. This is an optional field.
* **Student Comments:** (student_comments) - Comments or feedback from the student. This is an optional field.
* **Grade:** (grade) - The grade of the student for the assignment. This is optional.
* **Submission Status:** (submission_status) - The status of the submission. This is optional.
* **Submission Note:** (submission_note) - Additional information about the submission. This is optional.
* **School Address:** (school_address) - The address of the school. This is optional.
* **School Phone:** (school_phone) - The phone number of the school. This is optional.
* **School Email:** (school_email) - The email address of the school. This is optional.
* **Teacher Notes:** (teacher_notes) - Notes from the teacher. This is optional.
* **Student Notes:** (student_notes) - Notes from the student. This is optional.
* **Submission Date:** (submission_date) - The date of the submission. This field is optional.

Please make sure to review each field carefully and fill out the form accurately for successful submission. If you have any questions or issues, please contact your administrator or help desk for assistance.
