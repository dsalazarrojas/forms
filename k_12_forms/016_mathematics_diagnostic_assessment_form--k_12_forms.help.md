# mathematics_diagnostic_assessment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Mathematics Diagnostic Assessment Form is a tool for teachers to assess students' math skills and backgrounds. It is designed to identify areas where students may need additional support or review. By filling out this form, teachers can get an overview of a student's math abilities and plan future instruction accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to select the correct grade level for your math background.
3. Enter your student's ID number.
4. Enter your student's name.
5. Enter your scores for each math concept (if applicable).
6. Enter any additional comments or feedback for the teacher.
7. Enter your background information.
8. Select the submit option to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`intro`, `text`, optional): Please provide a brief introduction or background information about the student.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, `number`, required): Enter the student's ID number to identify them.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `select_one`, optional): Select the grade level that best describes your math background.
* **Score 1** (`score_1`, `number`, optional): Enter your score for this math concept.
* **Score 2** (`score_2`, `number`, optional): Enter your score for this math concept.
* **Score 3** (`score_3`, `number`, optional): Enter your score for this math concept.
* **Score 4** (`score_4`, `number`, optional): Enter your score for this math concept.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback for the teacher.
* **Math Background** (`math_background`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information about your math background.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter your student's name.
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback for the teacher.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address (if desired).
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number (if desired).
* **Submit** (`submit`, `select_one`, required): Select the "Submit" option to complete the form.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
