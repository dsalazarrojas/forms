# read_a_thon_participation_form--k_12_forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant information for the Read-a-thon program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "School Name" field with your school's name.
2. Enter your school's email address in the "School Email" field.
3. Enter your student's name in the "Student Name" field.
4. Enter your student's birthdate in the "Student Birthdate" field.
5. Select your student's grade level from the options in the "Student Grade" field.
6. Indicate whether or not your student has specific reading goals by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Student Goal" field.
7. Enter your student's desired reading level in the "Student Reading Level" field.
8. Select a goal date for your student's reading goals.
9. Enter your school's phone number in the "School Phone" field.
10. Enter your school's address in the "School Address" field.
11. Indicate your student's permission to participate by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Student Permission" field.
12. Enter the date of your student's permission to participate.
13. Enter your school's contact person's name and title in the "School Contact" field.
14. Enter your student's contact person's name and title in the "Student Contact" field.
15. Enter any additional notes or comments about your school in the "School Notes" field.
16. Enter any additional notes or comments about your student in the "Student Notes" field.
17. Enter the name of your school's designated organizer in the "School Designated Organizer" field.
18. Enter the name of your student's designated organizer in the "Student Designated Organizer" field.
19. Enter the name of your school's designated coordinator in the "School Designated Coordinator" field.
20. Enter the name of your student's designated coordinator in the "Student Designated Coordinator" field.
21. Enter the name of your school's administrator in the "School Admin" field.
22. Enter the name of your student's administrator in the "Student Admin" field.
23. Enter the name of your school's sponsor in the "School Sponsor" field.
24. Enter the name of your student's sponsor in the "Student Sponsor" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (Text, Required): Enter your school's name.
* **School Email** (Email, Required): Enter your school's email address.
* **Student Name** (Text, Required): Enter your student's name.
* **Student Birthdate** (Date, Required): Enter your student's birthdate.
* **Student Grade** (Select One, Required): Select your student's grade level.
* **Student Goal** (Select Multiple, Required): Indicate whether or not your student has specific reading goals.
* **Student Reading Level** (Select One, Required): Select your student's desired reading level.
* **Goal Date** (Date, Required): Select a goal date for your student's reading goals.
* **School Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your school's phone number.
* **School Address** (Text, Required): Enter your school's address.
* **Student Permission** (Select One, Required): Indicate your student's permission to participate.
* **Permission Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date of your student's permission to participate.
* **School Contact** (Text, Required): Enter your school's contact person's name and title.
* **Student Contact** (Text, Required): Enter your student's contact person's name and title.
* **School Notes** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your school.
* **Student Notes** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your student.
* **School Designated Organizer** (Text, Required): Enter the name of your school's designated organizer.
* **Student Designated Organizer** (Text, Required): Enter the name of your student's designated organizer.
* **School Designated Coordinator** (Text, Required): Enter the name of your school's designated coordinator.
* **Student Designated Coordinator** (Text, Required): Enter the name of your student's designated coordinator.
* **School Admin** (Text, Required): Enter the name of your school's administrator.
* **Student Admin** (Text, Required): Enter the name of your student's administrator.
* **School Sponsor** (Text, Required): Enter the name of your school's sponsor.
