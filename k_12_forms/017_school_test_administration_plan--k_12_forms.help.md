# School Test Administration Plan - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Test Administration Plan form is used for planning and organizing information related to school testing, including exam dates, test locations, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the exam date for the test.
2. Enter the name of the test.
3. Enter the name of the school.
4. Enter the contact information of the test coordinator.
5. Select the accommodation requests for the test.
6. Select the accommodation types for the test.
7. Enter the contact information of the school.
8. Enter the special instructions for the test.
9. Enter the contact information of the test proctors.
10. Enter the other information for the test.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exam Date** (`exam_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the exam.
* **Test Name** (`test_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the test.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school.
* **Test Coordinator** (`test_coordinator`, text, required): Enter the contact information of the test coordinator.
* **Accommodation Requests** (`accommodation_requests`, select_multiple, optional): Select the accommodation requests for the test.
* **Accommodation Types** (`accommodation_types`, select_one, optional): Select the accommodation types for the test.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter the contact information of the school.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, note, optional): Enter the special instructions for the test.
* **Test Proctors** (`test_proctors`, text, optional): Enter the contact information of the test proctors.
* **Other Info** (`other_info`, text, optional): Enter other information related to the test.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* Review the form for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the test coordinator.
