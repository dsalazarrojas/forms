# Student Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Information Collection Form is designed to collect essential details about students, including contact information, academic performance, and behavioral data. This form helps administrators and educators to track student progress, create profiles, and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your student's name in the **Student Name** field.
2. Provide your student's date of birth in the **Date of Birth** field.
3. Select your student's grade level from the options provided in the **Grade Level** field.
4. Enter your student's email address in the **Email** field.
5. Provide your student's phone number in the **Phone** field.
6. Enter your student's address in the **Address** field.
7. Enter your student's parent's name in the **Parent Name** field.
8. Add any additional notes or comments about your student in the **Notes** field.
9. Indicate if your student will have any absences from school in the **Absences** field.
10. Enter any comments or feedback from teachers in the **Comments** field.
11. Select your student's grade level in English, Math, and Science subjects from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's full name.
* **Date of Birth** (`student_dob`, date, required): Enter the student's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, select_one, required): Select the student's grade level (3, 4, 5, or 6).
* **Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter the student's email address.
* **Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Address** (`student_address`, text, required): Enter the student's address.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter the parent's name.
* **Notes** (`student_notes`, note, required): Add any additional comments or notes about the student.
* **Absences** (`student_absences`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if the student will have any absences from school.
* **Comments** (`teacher_comments`, note, required): Enter any comments or feedback from teachers.
* **English Grade** (`student_grade_level_english`, select_one, required): Select the student's grade level in English subject.
* **Math Grade** (`student_grade_level_math`, select_one, required): Select the student's grade level in Math subject.
* **Science Grade** (`student_grade_level_science`, select_one, required): Select the student's grade level in Science subject.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact your school administrator.
* Ensure you save the form after completion for future reference.
* If you need to update any information, you can always revisit this form to make changes.
