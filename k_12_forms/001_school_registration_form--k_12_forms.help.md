# School Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for students to register to a school and provide relevant details for administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name and email address accurately.
2. Enter your phone number if necessary.
3. Enter your course ID (a numerical value).
4. Select your grade level from the available options.
5. Enter your student ID (if applicable).
6. Provide your class name.
7. Enter your parent's name (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **Course ID** (`course_id`, number, required/optional): Enter a unique numerical value representing your course or program.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required/optional): Select your current grade level from the options available.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required/optional): Enter your student ID if you have one.
* **Class Name** (`class_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your class or group.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your parent or guardian.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any difficulties or questions, contact the school administration for assistance.
