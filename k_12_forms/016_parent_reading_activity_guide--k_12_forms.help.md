# parent_reading_activity_guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "parent_reading_activity_guide" form is designed to collect information about parents' and caregivers' education, reading frequency, and other relevant details. This form is used to gather data for educational research and development purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the relevant options for each field from the available choices.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled in completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name (Parent Name)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **School Grade (School Grade)** (`number`, required/optional): Indicate the grade level of the school attended by the child.
* **Reading Frequency (Reading Frequency)** (`number`, required/optional): Report how often the parent or caregiver reads to the child (e.g., rarely, sometimes, often, always).
* **Parent Education (Parent Education)** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select the highest level of education completed by the parent or guardian.
	+ High school or less
	+ Some college
	+ Bachelor's degree
	+ Master's degree
	+ Doctoral degree
* **Caregiver Name (Caregiver Name)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the caregiver or individual responsible for the child's care.
* **Caregiver Education (Caregiver Education)** (`select_one`, required): Select the highest level of education completed by the caregiver or individual.
	+ High school or less
	+ Some college
	+ Bachelor's degree
	+ Master's degree
	+ Doctoral degree
* **Caregiver Work Experience (Caregiver Work Experience)** (`number`, required/optional): Report the number of years of work experience the caregiver or individual has.
* **Reading Frequency Caregiver (Reading Frequency Caregiver)** (`number`, required/optional): Report how often the caregiver or individual reads to the child (e.g., rarely, sometimes, often, always).
* **Parent Reading Frequency (Parent Reading Frequency)** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select how often the parent reads to the child.
	+ Rarely
	+ Sometimes
	+ Often
	+ Always
* **Feedback (Feedback)** (`note`, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the form or the collected data.
* **Additional Comments (Additional Comments)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the parent or caregiver.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the parent or caregiver (if applicable).
* **Phone (Phone)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the parent or caregiver (if applicable).

Note: Asterisk (*) denotes required fields.
