# Elementary School Entrance Exam Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Elementary School Entrance Exam Quiz form is designed to collect information from students and their parents or guardians to assess their readiness for elementary education. This information will be used to place students in the most suitable grade level and educational environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* First Name: Enter your child's first name.
	* Last Name: Enter your child's last name.
	* Date of Birth: Enter your child's date of birth (mm/dd/yyyy).
2. Provide contact information:
	* Email: Enter your email address.
	* Parent/Guardian Name: Enter your name.
	* Parent/Guardian Email: Enter your email address.
	* Parent/Guardian Phone: Enter your phone number.
3. Select your child's details:
	* Student Gender: Choose your child's gender (male, female, or other).
	* Student Grade: Choose the grade level your child is currently in or will be entering (K, 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th, 6th, 7th, 8th, 9th, 10th, 11th, or 12th).
	* Student Language: Select your child's primary language spoken at home.
4. Provide school preferences:
	* School Name: Enter the name of the school your child would like to attend.
	* School Location: Enter the location of the school your child would like to attend.
5. Special needs and other requirements:
	* Student Special Needs: Indicate if your child has any special needs or requirements.
	* Student Language: Select any additional languages your child speaks.
6. Review and submit:
	* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
