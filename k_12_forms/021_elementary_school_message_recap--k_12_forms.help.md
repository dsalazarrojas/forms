# Elementary School Message Recap - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Elementary School Message Recap form is designed to record and document communication between teachers and other stakeholders, such as parents or staff members. This form helps administrators and staff members to track and review important messages and attendance details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the teacher's notes regarding the message or conversation in the "Teacher Notes" field.
2. Select the subject of the message in the "Subject" field.
3. Type or copy and paste the message itself in the "Message" field.
4. Specify to whom the message is addressed in the "To Whom" field.
5. Enter the date the message was sent or received in the "Date" field.
6. Enter the time the message was sent or received in the "Time" field (optional).
7. Select "Yes" or "No" for the student's attendance in the "Student Attendance" field.
8. Select "Yes" or "No" for the parent's attendance in the "Parent Attendance" field.
9. Select "Yes" or "No" for the staff member's attendance in the "Staff Attendance" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Teacher Notes** (`teacher_notes`): Enter the notes or comments from the teacher regarding the message.
* **Subject** (`subject`): Select the subject of the message or conversation.
* **Message** (`message`): Type or copy and paste the message or conversation details.
* **To Whom** (`to_who`): Specify to whom the message is addressed (e.g., parent, student).
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date the message was sent or received (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time the message was sent or received (HH:MM format, optional).
* **Staff Notes** (`staff_notes`): Enter any additional comments or notes from the staff member.
* **Student Attendance** (`student_attendance`): Select "Yes" if the student was present during the message exchange, "No" otherwise.
* **Parent Attendance** (`parent_attendance`): Select "Yes" if the parent was present during the message exchange, "No" otherwise.
* **Staff Attendance** (`staff_attendance`): Select "Yes" if the staff member was present during the message exchange, "No" otherwise.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields to ensure accurate tracking and documentation.
* Use the "Staff Notes" field for any additional comments or information from the staff member.
* Use "Yes" or "No" for attendance fields to indicate presence or absence during the message exchange.
