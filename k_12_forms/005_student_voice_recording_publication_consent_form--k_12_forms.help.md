# Student Voice Recording Publication Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Student Voice Recording Publication Consent Form" is a document used to obtain consent from parents or guardians for the publication of their child's voice recordings. This form is essential for ensuring the privacy and confidentiality of students' voice recordings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the parent school information in the "Parent School Information" field.
2. Provide the parent or guardian's name and contact information in the "Parent/Guardian Information" field.
3. Select the "Publication Consent" field to indicate whether you agree or disagree to the publication of your child's voice recordings.
4. If you choose to agree, provide any additional comments or notes about the publication consent in the "Publication Consent Notes" field.
5. Enter your child's ID number in the "Student ID Number" field.
6. Enter your child's name in the "Student Name" field.
7. Describe the purpose of the publication in the "Publication Purpose" field.
8. If applicable, provide a student image in the "Student Image" field.
9. Enter the publication date in the "Publication Date" field.
10. If applicable, provide a student audio recording in the "Student Audio" field.
11. Enter the publication location in the "Publication Location" field.
12. Enter your contact number in the "Parent/Guardian Contact Number" field.
13. Enter your email address in the "Parent/Guardian Email" field.
14. Select the "Publication Agreement" field to indicate whether you agree or disagree to the terms and conditions.
15. If you choose to agree, provide any additional comments or notes about the publication agreement in the "Publication Agreement Notes" field.
16. Sign the form in the "Parent/Guardian Signature" field.
17. Enter the date you signed the form in the "Date Signed" field.
18. Enter the school name in the "School Name" field.
19. Enter the school email address in the "School Email" field.
20. Enter the school address in the "School Address" field.
21. Enter the school phone number in the "School Phone Number" field.
22. Enter the school principal's name in the "School Principal's Name" field.
23. Enter the school principal's email address in the "School Principal's Email" field.
