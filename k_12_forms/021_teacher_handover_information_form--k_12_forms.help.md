# Teacher Handover Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects information from teachers during the handover process, allowing administrators to gather essential details for a smooth transition.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please fill out all relevant fields**: Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information to facilitate an efficient handover process.
2. **Select the correct answers**: Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for fields where applicable, and select the correct options from the dropdown menus.
3. **Enter text**: Type in the required text fields, such as teacher name, department name, and school name.
4. **Provide dates and times**: Enter the date and time of transition using the date and time fields.
5. **Add resource links**: If applicable, provide links to relevant resources.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **teacher_notes**: Brief notes from the teacher, describing the current state of the class, projects, or other relevant information.
* **outstanding_tasks**: Outstanding tasks or issues that need to be addressed during the handover process. Select 'Yes' if there are outstanding tasks, and 'No' otherwise.
* **class_resources**: Indicate whether there are class resources available for the next teacher. Select 'Yes' if resources are available, and 'No' otherwise.
* **student_info**: This field is currently not used in the form. You can use it for any additional information about the students.
* **teacher_comments**: Leave comments or feedback for the next teacher, such as best practices or areas of improvement.
* **teacher_name**: Enter your name as the teacher who is handing over the class.
* **department_name**: Enter the name of your department.
* **school_name**: Enter the name of your school.
* **date_of_transition**: Enter the date of the transition.
* **time_of_transition**: Enter the time of the transition.
* **resource_links**: If applicable, provide links to relevant resources, such as lesson plans or documents. Select 'Yes' if resources are available, and 'No' otherwise.
