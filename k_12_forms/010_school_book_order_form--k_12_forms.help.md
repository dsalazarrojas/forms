# School Book Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Book Order Form is used to collect information about school book orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the student's name.
2. Select the grade level of the student.
3. Enter the school name.
4. Enter the student's email address.
5. Select the payment gateway (e.g., Square, PayPal, Authorize.net).
6. Confirm the payment details.
7. Select the order date and time.
8. Finally, submit or confirm the form, depending on the status of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name (1)**: Enter the student's name as it appears on their student ID or in the school's records.
* **Grade Level (2)**: Select the student's grade level from the options provided.
* **School Name (3)**: Enter the name of the school where the student attends.
* **Email (4)**: Enter the student's email address.
* **Payment Gateway (5)**: Select the payment method to be used for the order (e.g., Square, PayPal, Authorize.net).
	+ Only select one option.
* **Selected Items (6)**: This field is for additional information about the order. Enter details as needed.
* **Confirm Payment Details (7)**: Confirm that the payment details are accurate.
* **Order Date (8)**: Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time (9)**: Enter the time of the order.
* **Submit (10)**: Submit the form if all information is correct and complete. Otherwise, select "No" and make the necessary corrections.
* **Confirm (11)**: Confirm that all information is correct and complete before confirming the order.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all information is accurate and complete before submitting the form.
* Only select one payment gateway option.
* Confirm that the payment details are correct before confirming the order.
* If the form is not complete or accurate, select "No" for "Submit" and make the necessary corrections before resubmitting.
