# Student Voice In Learning Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Student Voice In Learning Forum Registration" form is designed to gather information from students who are interested in participating in online forums related to learning. This form helps us better understand the needs, interests, and preferences of our students to provide more effective and relevant support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the required information before starting.
2. Select all that apply for "Sign Ups" (page 1) to indicate your interest in joining the forum.
3. Choose your interests (page 2) that you would like to focus on or participate in, such as "Yes" or "No".
4. Answer "Yes" or "No" to questions (page 3) about your interests, if applicable.
5. Provide your school name, name, student number, and grade level (page 4-5) to help us understand your background.
6. Enter your contact information (page 6) so we can reach out to you with more information or for further assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sign Ups** (`sign_ups`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all that apply to indicate your interest in joining the forum.
* **Interests** (`interests`, select_one, required: false): Choose the interests you would like to focus on or participate in. Select "Yes" for an interest that applies to you and "No" if it doesn't.
* **Questions** (`questions`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is not applicable as it is currently not in use.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required: false): Enter your school name to help us understand your affiliation.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Student Number** (`student_number`, text, required: false): This field is currently not in use.
* **Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, select_one, required: false): Choose your grade level from High or Low.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information so we can reach out to you for further assistance.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required: false): This field is currently not in use.

Note: Some fields are not currently being used in the form but are still included in the help guide for future reference.
