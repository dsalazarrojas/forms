# School Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate parents ordering school supplies for their children. By submitting this form, parents can provide the necessary information about their child's grade level, school, and preferred order date and time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as the parent.
2. Enter the name of the school your child attends.
3. Select your child's current grade level from the dropdown menu.
4. Optionally, choose a specific date and time for the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Parent Name (<parent_name>)
* A field where you can enter your name as the parent.

### School (<school_name>)
* A field where you can type in the name of the school your child attends.

### Grade (<grade_level>)
* Select your child's current grade level from the dropdown menu:
	+ First Grade
	+ Second Grade
	+ Third Grade
	+ Fourth Grade
	+ Fifth Grade
	+ Sixth Grade
	+ Seventh Grade
	+ Eighth Grade

### Order Date (<order_date>)
* If you would like to schedule a specific date for your order, you can fill in the date here.

### Order Time (<order_time>)
* If you would like to schedule a specific time for your order, you can fill in the time here.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and approvals before submitting this form.
