# Student Teacher Co Design Study Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and consent from students, parents, and teachers for a co-design study.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field and provide accurate information.
2. Select the relevant options for each field.
3. Fill in the required fields with your details.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (Page 1): This field is used to capture general information about the student, parent, or teacher.
* **Parent Consent** (Page 2): Select the correct option to indicate the parent's consent status. Options:
	+ Option 1: Parent has given consent.
	+ Option 2: Parent has not given consent.
* **Teacher Consent** (Page 3): Select the correct option to indicate the teacher's consent status. Options:
	+ Option 1: Teacher has given consent.
	+ Option 2: Teacher has not given consent.
* **Student Consent** (Page 4): Select the options that apply to the student's participation. Options:
	+ Option 1: Student is participating.
	+ Option 2: Student is not participating.
* **Contact Information** (Page 5): Select the correct option to provide contact information for the student, parent, or teacher. Options:
	+ Option 1: Contact information is provided.
	+ Option 2: No contact information is provided.
* **Study Duration** (Page 6): Enter the duration of the study.
* **Study Schedule** (Page 7): Enter the schedule of the study.
* **Study Location** (Page 8): Enter the location of the study.
* **Research Question** (Page 9): Enter a brief description of the research question.
* **Study Design** (Page 10): Enter a brief description of the study design.
* **Consent Status** (Page 11): Select the correct option to indicate the consent status. Options:
	+ Option 1: Consent is given.
	+ Option 2: Consent is not given.
* **Study Termination** (Page 12): Enter the date of study termination.
* **Student Notes** (Page 13): Enter any additional notes from the student.
* **Teacher Notes** (Page 14): Enter any additional notes from the teacher.
* **Parent Notes** (Page 15): Enter any additional notes from the parent.
* **Consent Date** (Page 16): Enter the date of consent.
* **Study Status** (Page 17): Select the correct option to indicate the study status. Options:
	+ Option 1: Study is active.
	+ Option 2: Study is inactive.
* **Study Start Date** (Page 18): Enter the start date of the study.
* **Study End Date** (Page 19): Enter the end date of the study.
* **Study Duration Remaining** (Page 20): Enter the remaining duration of the study.
* **Study Remaining Schedule** (Page 21): Select the correct option to indicate the remaining schedule. Options:
	+ Option 1: Schedule is remaining.
	+ Option 2: Schedule is not remaining.
* **Contact Person** (Page 22): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (Page 23): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (Page 24): Enter the phone number of the contact person.

## Tips

* Be accurate and honest when filling out this form.
* Double-check your responses before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the contact person listed above.
