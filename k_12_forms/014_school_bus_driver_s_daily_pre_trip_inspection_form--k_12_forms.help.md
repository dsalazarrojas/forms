# School Bus Drivers Daily Pre Trip Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used by school bus drivers to report the status of their vehicle and identify any potential issues before a trip. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to maintain the highest standards of safety and efficiency for students and school districts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the vehicle's status, selecting from the options provided (Ready for use or Not ready for use).
2. Enter the vehicle's year.
3. Record the vehicle's license number.
4. Enter the inspection date.
5. Enter the route number (if applicable).
6. Enter your name and driver's license number.
7. Record the bus number.
8. Identify the type of bus you are operating (e.g., regular, special needs).
9. Assess the vehicle's condition, selecting from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
10. Select any equipment present (e.g., air compressor, fire extinguisher, first aid kit).
11. Sign the driver signature section to acknowledge the accuracy of the form.
12. Sign the inspector signature section (if applicable).
13. Enter any vehicle defects or issues.
14. Add any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Status (Vehicle Status)** (`vehicle_status`, select_multiple, required: false): Select whether the vehicle is ready for use or not ready for use.
* **Vehicle Year (Vehicle Year)** (`vehicle_year`, number, required: false): Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle License (Vehicle License)** (`vehicle_license`, text, required: false): Record the vehicle's license number.
* **Inspection Date (Inspection Date)** (`inspection_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Route Number (Route Number)** (`route_number`, text, required: false): Enter the route number (if applicable).
* **Driver Name (Driver Name)** (`driver_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Driver License (Driver License)** (`driver_license`, text, required: false): Record your driver's license number.
* **Bus Number (Bus Number)** (`bus_number`, text, required: false): Record the bus number.
* **Bus Type (Bus Type)** (`bus_type`, text, required: false): Identify the type of bus you are operating (e.g., regular, special needs).
* **Vehicle Condition (Vehicle Condition)** (`vehicle_condition`, select_one, required: false): Assess the vehicle's condition, selecting from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Vehicle Equipment (Vehicle Equipment)** (`vehicle_equipment`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any equipment present in the vehicle (e.g., air compressor, fire extinguisher).
* **Driver Signature (Driver Signature)** (`driver_signature`, note, required: false): Sign to acknowledge the accuracy of the form.
* **Inspector Signature (Inspector Signature)** (`inspector_signature`, note, required: false): Sign if you are an inspector (if applicable).
* **Vehicle Defect (Vehicle Defect)** (`vehicle_defect`, text, required: false): Enter any vehicle defects or issues.
* **Comments (Comments)** (`comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes.

This guide will help you fill out the School Bus Drivers Daily Pre-Trip Inspection Form accurately and effectively. Always review each field carefully and truthfully to ensure the highest standards of safety and efficiency for students and school districts.
