# Discipline Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Discipline Referral Form is used by teachers to document incidents of misbehavior that occur within the school. This form helps to track student behavior, keep a record of incidents, and provide a clear understanding of student actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant information for each field and type or select accordingly. Make sure to fill out one form per incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student** (Select One):
	+ This field indicates the student involved in the incident. Choose the student's status as either "Student" or "Non-Student" to indicate if they are a student, a staff member, or someone else.
* **Reason** (Select One):
	+ This field indicates the reason for the incident. Choose one of the provided options: "Bullying", "Fighting", or "Other".
* **Description** (Text):
	+ In this field, provide a brief description of the incident. Include as much detail as possible to help the school administration understand what happened.
* **Actions** (Select Multiple):
	+ This field indicates what actions were taken as a result of the incident. Choose from the options: "Verbal Warning", "Written Warning", "Detention", "Parent Contact", "Suspended", "Removed".
* **Date** (Date):
	+ This field indicates the date of the incident. Enter the date in the correct format.
* **Time** (Time):
	+ This field indicates the time of the incident. Enter the time in the correct format.
* **Comments** (Note):
	+ In this field, provide any additional comments or context related to the incident.
* **Signature** (Text):
	+ This field is for the teacher's signature, indicating that they have filled out this form accurately and truthfully.
* **Student Signature** (Text):
	+ This field is for the student's signature, if applicable. If the student is not able to sign, leave this field blank.
* **Teacher Signature** (Text):
	+ This field is for the teacher's signature, indicating that they have filled out this form accurately and truthfully.
* **Teacher Name** (Text):
	+ This field is for the teacher's name, for reference.
* **Grade** (Select One):
	+ This field indicates the grade level of the student involved in the incident. Choose from the options: "9th Grade", "10th Grade", "11th Grade", "12th Grade".
* **Subject** (Text):
	+ This field is for the subject or class in which the incident occurred.
* **Student Name** (Text):
	+ This field is for the student's name, if applicable.
* **Teacher Name** (Text):
	+ This field is for the teacher's name.
* **Teacher Email** (Text):
	+ This field is for the teacher's email address.
* **Teacher Phone** (Text):
	+ This field is for the teacher's phone number.
* **Student Phone** (Text):
	+ This field is for the student's phone number.
* **School** (Text):
	+ This field is for the name of the school.
* **Classroom** (Text):
	+ This field is for the name of the classroom.
* **Teacher Address** (Text):
	+ This field is for the teacher's address.
* **Date of Incident** (Date):
	+ This field indicates the date of the incident.
* **Time of Incident** (Time):
	+ This field indicates the time of the incident.
* **Actions Taken** (Text):
	+ This field is for any actions taken as a result of the incident.

Please fill out this form accurately and truthfully. If you have any questions or concerns, consult with your school administration.
