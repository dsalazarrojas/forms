# Student Virtual Profile Display Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Virtual Profile Display Consent Form is designed to collect student consent to display their virtual profile information. This form ensures that students are aware and approve the display of their virtual profile information, and it's part of the school's or organization's onboarding process.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your form title in both English and Spanish.
2.  Choose a category for your virtual profile information in English and Spanish.
3.  Enter a description in both English and Spanish.
4.  If necessary, add a hint for each section in both English and Spanish.
5.  Fill out the required fields as indicated by the "Form Title English Required", "Category English Required", and "Description English Required" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Category (K-12 Forms / Other)**: Select the type of virtual profile information you want to display.
*   **Description (English/Spanish)**: Enter a description in the chosen language to further explain the virtual profile information.
*   **Category English (English/Spanish)**: Choose the correct language setting for the virtual profile information.
*   **Form Title (English/Spanish)**: Enter the title for your virtual profile display form in the corresponding language.
*   **Category English Hint (English/Spanish)**: Provide a hint for the category in the selected language.
*   **Category Spanish Hint (English/Spanish)**: Enter a hint for the category in the chosen language.
*   **Form Title English Hint (English/Spanish)**: Enter a hint for the form title in the corresponding language.
*   **Form Title Spanish Hint (English/Spanish)**: Enter a hint for the form title in the chosen language.
*   **Form Title English Place (English/Spanish)**: Enter a placeholder for the form title in the corresponding language.
*   **Form Title Spanish Place (English/Spanish)**: Enter a placeholder for the form title in the selected language.
*   **Category English Pl (English/Spanish)**: Enter a placeholder for the category in the corresponding language.
*   **Category Spanish Pl (English/Spanish)**: Enter a placeholder for the category in the selected language.
*   **Description English Pla (English/Spanish)**: Enter a placeholder for the description in the corresponding language.
*   **Description Spanish Pla (English/Spanish)**: Enter a placeholder for the description in the selected language.
*   **Form Title English Required (English/Spanish)**: Enter the required field for the form title in English.
*   **Form Title Spanish Required (English/Spanish)**: Enter the required field for the form title in Spanish.
*   **Category English Required (English/Spanish)**: Enter the required field for the category in English.
*   **Description English Required (English/Spanish)**: Enter the required field for the description in English.

Note that the fields marked as "Required" should be completed.
