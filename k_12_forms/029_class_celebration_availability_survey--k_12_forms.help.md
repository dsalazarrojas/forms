# Class Celebration Availability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from parents and guardians about their child's availability and preferences for a class celebration. It will help the class organizers plan and prepare for the celebration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the student and parent information fields (Student Name, Parent Name, and Parent Email Address).
2. Indicate your child's availability for the celebration (Celebration Date).
3. Choose your preferred celebration dates (Preferred Celebration Dates).
4. Select your activity preferences (Activity Preferences).
5. If you are willing to help with planning and organization (Willing to Volunteer).
6. Select how you would like to contribute (How Would you Like to Help).
7. If you have dietary restrictions or allergies, please let us know (Dietary Restrictions).
8. Confirm your willingness to bring snacks or drinks (Willing to Bring).
9. If you have any special requests or suggestions, please let us know (Special Requests).
10. Provide any additional comments or concerns (Additional Comments).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, **required**): Fill out your child's name.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, **required**): Fill out your name or the name of the person completing this form on your behalf.
* **Parent Email Address** (`parent_email`, **required**): Enter your email address so we can contact you for any further information.
* **Parent Phone Number** (`parent_phone`, **optional**): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Celebration Date** (`celebration_date`, **required**): Indicate whether your child can attend the class celebration.
* **Preferred Celebration Dates** (`preferred_dates`, **optional**): Select all dates that work for you and your child.
* **Activity Preferences** (`activity_preferences`, **optional**): Choose the activities you and your child would like to participate in.
* **Willing to Volunteer** (`willing_to_volunteer`, **required**): If you are able and willing, let us know how you can help.
* **How Would you Like to Help** (`volunteer_role`, **optional**): Select how you would like to contribute to the celebration.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, **optional**): If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, please let us know.
* **Willing to Bring** (`willing_to_bring`, **required**): Let us know if you are able and willing to contribute snacks or drinks.
* **What would you Like to Bring** (`contribution_idea`, **optional**): If you are bringing snacks or drinks, indicate what you plan to bring.
* **Budget Contribution** (`budget_contribution`, **optional**): If you are willing to contribute to the celebration budget, select the amount.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, **optional**): If you have any special requests or suggestions, please let us know.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, **optional**): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please let us know.
