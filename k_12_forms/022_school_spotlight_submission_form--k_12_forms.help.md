# School Spotlight Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your school for our internal purposes. Please fill in the necessary fields to help us showcase your school's details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the fields as follows:

- Fill out the School Name field with your school's official name.
- Enter your school's email address in the School Email field.
- Provide your school's phone number in the School Phone field.
- Enter your school's website URL in the School Website field.
- Fill out the School Category field with the relevant category for your school (e.g. Elementary, High School, University).
- If your school has a Facebook page, Twitter, Instagram, or YouTube presence, fill in the respective fields. You can also list other social media platforms in the Other Social field.
- Enter your school's physical address in the School Address field.
- Fill out the School City and School State fields with your school's location.
- Enter your school's zip code in the School Zip field.
- Write a brief description of your school in the School Description field.
- You can also add relevant tags, images, videos, and audio content to further represent your school.
