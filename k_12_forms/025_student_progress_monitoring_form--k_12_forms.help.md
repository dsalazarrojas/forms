# Student Progress Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Progress Monitoring Form is designed to help educators track and monitor student progress towards academic goals.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Enter Student Information
- Please fill out the form for each student individually.
- If a student's progress has not been checked recently, fill out the form with the current date to update the record.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Progress Monitoring** (`student_progress_monitoring_form`, `text`, **optional**): 
  This field is where you provide a brief description of the student's progress, highlighting their strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, **optional**): 
  Enter the student's name to associate this progress report with the correct student.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, `number`, **optional**): 
  Enter the student's ID number to track progress within the student's ID system.
* **Date of Last Progress Check** (`date_of_last_progress_check`, `date`, **optional**): 
  Enter the date of the last progress check to maintain an accurate record of when the student was last monitored.
* **Student Progress Goals** (`student_progress_goals`, `text`, **optional**): 
  Describe the student's goals and objectives, including specific tasks or areas to focus on for future progress.

## Tips
- Review and update this form regularly to ensure accurate and up-to-date progress tracking.
- Use this form to inform and guide future progress assessments.
- Consider sharing these progress reports with other relevant educators or support staff.
