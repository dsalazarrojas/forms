# Student Representative Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Representative Nomination Form is used to collect information for student nominations. This process aims to identify students who are suitable representatives for their classes or course clubs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the student's name in the "student_name" field.
2. Enter the student's ID number in the "student_id" field.
3. Select the relevant class or course club from the "class_course_club" field.
4. Provide a brief reason for the nomination in the "reason_for_nomination" field.
5. Choose a date for the nomination, if required.
6. Enter your name and other relevant details in the "nominator_name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **student_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the student you are nominating.
* **student_id** (number, optional): Enter the student's ID number, if available.
* **class_course_club** (select multiple, optional): Select the relevant class or course club the student represents. Examples: Class A, Class B, Class C, Class D.
* **reason_for_nomination** (text, optional): Provide a brief reason for the nomination, e.g., leadership qualities, academic achievements, etc.
* **nomination_date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the nomination, if required.
* **nominator_name** (text, optional): Enter your name and other relevant details, e.g., title, department, etc.

## Tips
- Please ensure the information you enter is accurate and complete.
- If a field is marked as optional, provide as much detail as you can.
- Review your submission before submitting to ensure it meets the required fields and information.
