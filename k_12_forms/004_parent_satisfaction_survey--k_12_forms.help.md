# Parent Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from parents on their satisfaction with the school. It will help us understand their experiences and opinions, which will inform our efforts to improve the school's services and overall quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*). You can leave the optional fields blank.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure that you are comfortable with your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Satisfaction Survey**: This is the main survey question where you can express your overall satisfaction with the school. Please answer using plain text.
* **How satisfied are you with the school?**: This question is a numerical rating scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Very dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very satisfied". Please select the number that best represents your level of satisfaction.
* **School Name**: This is the name of the school where your child is enrolled. Please enter the full name of the school.
* **Teacher Name**: This is the name of your child's teacher(s). Please enter the full name of the teacher.
