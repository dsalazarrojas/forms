# Youth Literacy Program Guardian Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Literacy Program Guardian Consent Form is a form used to collect contact information from guardians and emergency contacts for students participating in the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about your relationship with the student (guardian_info)
2. Provide emergency contact information (emergency_contact_info)
3. Specify the type of emergency contact (emergency_contact_type)
4. Enter the name of the emergency contact (emergency_contact_name)
5. Enter the phone number of the emergency contact (emergency_contact_phone)
6. Enter the address of the emergency contact (emergency_contact_address)
7. Specify the relationship between the student and emergency contact (emergency_contact_relationship)
8. If "Other" is selected as the emergency contact type, provide additional information in the "emergency_contact_relationship_other" field
9. Enter any additional information about the student's school (school_info)
10. Enter the name of the student's school (school_name)
11. Enter the phone number of the student's school (school_phone)
12. Enter the email address of the student's school (school_email)
13. Provide additional information about the program (program_info)
14. Enter the name of the program (program_name)
15. Enter the start and end dates of the program (program_start_date and program_end_date)
16. Enter the location of the program (program_location)
17. Enter any additional notes about the program (program_notes)
18. Enter any additional information about the student (student_info)
19. Enter the student's name (student_name)
20. Enter the student's age (student_age)
21. Enter the student's email address (student_email)
22. Enter the student's phone number (student_phone)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **guardian_info**: This is the field where you provide general information about your relationship with the student.
* **emergency_contact_info**: This is the field where you provide the contact information for the emergency contact.
* **emergency_contact_type**: Select the type of emergency contact: Emergency Contact, Other Contact, or Other (Specify).
* **emergency_contact_name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **emergency_contact_phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **emergency_contact_address**: Enter the address of the emergency contact.
* **emergency_contact_relationship**: Specify the relationship between the student and emergency contact. Options include: Biological parent, Foster parent, Guardian, or Other.
* **emergency_contact_relationship_other**: If "Other" is selected as the emergency contact type, provide additional information about the relationship.
* **school_info**: This is the field where you provide additional information about the student's school.
* **school_name**: Enter the name of the student's school.
* **school_phone**: Enter the phone number of the student's school.
* **school_email**: Enter the email address of the student's school.
* **program_info**: This is the field where you provide additional information about the program.
* **program_name**: Enter the name of the program.
* **program_start_date**: Enter the start date of the program.
* **program_end_date**: Enter the end date of the program.
* **program_location**: Enter the location of the program.
* **program_notes**: Enter any additional notes about the program.
* **student_info**: This is the field where you provide additional information about the student.
* **student_name**: Enter the student's name.
* **student_age**: Enter the student's age.
* **student_email**: Enter the student's email address.
* **student_phone**: Enter the student's phone number.
