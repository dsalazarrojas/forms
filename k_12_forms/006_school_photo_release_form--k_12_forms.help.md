# School Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Photo Release Form is used to obtain parental consent for the use of photos of their child for educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the name of your child in the "Child Name" field.
2.  Enter your name in the "Parent Name" field.
3.  Select the intended use of the photo in the "Photo Use" field. You can choose from "School Website", "Social Media", "School Yearbook", or "School Calendar".
4.  Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
5.  Sign your name in the "Parent Signature" field.
6.  Enter the date and time you signed the form in the "Date Signed" and "Time Signed" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Child Name** (`child_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your child.
*   **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name.
*   **Photo Use** (`photo_use`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the intended use of the photo.
	+   "School Website": The photo will be used on the school's official website.
	+   "Social Media": The photo will be used on the school's social media channels.
	+   "School Yearbook": The photo will be used in the school yearbook.
	+   "School Calendar": The photo will be used on the school calendar.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
*   **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, `text`, required: false): Sign your name to confirm your consent.
*   **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you signed the form.
*   **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you signed the form.

## Tips

*   Ensure you enter accurate and up-to-date information in all fields.
*   Sign the form with your actual signature to confirm your consent.
*   Keep a record of the signed form for your personal reference.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the school administration for assistance.
