# Student Collaboration Space Access Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students to request access to a collaboration space. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **User Information**: Enter your name, email, and any other relevant information about yourself.
2. **Request Timing**: Choose the date and time you need access to the space.
3. **Request Purpose**: Describe the reason you need access to the space.
4. **Agreement to Space Rules**: Check that you agree to follow the space's rules.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Information** (User Information, text, required): Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
- **Request Timing** (Request Timing, date, required): Choose the date and time you need access to the space.
- **Request Purpose** (Request Purpose, text, required): Describe the reason you need access to the space.
- **Agreement to Space Rules** (Agreement to Space Rules, text, required): Check that you agree to follow the space's rules.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and relevant information in each field.
- Be specific about the reason for your request and your availability to access the space.
- Remember to check your availability before submitting the form.
