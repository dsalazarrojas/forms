# Curriculum Equity Review Board Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used by students, educators, or school administrators to apply to the Curriculum Equity Review Board (CERB) for review and consideration. The CERB is a board that reviews curriculum content to ensure it is equitable and inclusive of all students. If you are an educator or student looking to propose changes to the curriculum, this form is where you should start.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the "Applicant Details" section.
2. Provide any additional referral information in the "Other Referral Information" section (if applicable).
3. Add a submit comment in the "Submit Comment" section (if applicable).
4. Provide any relevant information about the review board in the "Review Board" section (if applicable).
5. Click "Submit Application" to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Details** (`applicant_details`, text, **required**): Please fill in your personal details such as your name, contact information, and any other relevant personal details.
* **Other Referral Information** (`other_referral_info`, text, **optional**): If you are applying on behalf of someone else, please provide their name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Submit Comment** (`submit_comment`, text, **optional**): Provide a brief comment about your application, such as why you are submitting this application or what you hope to achieve through the CERB.
* **Review Board** (`review_board`, text, **optional**): Provide any relevant information about the review board, such as its purpose, goals, or membership.
* **Submit Application** (`submit_application`, text, **optional**): Click this button to submit your application for review.
