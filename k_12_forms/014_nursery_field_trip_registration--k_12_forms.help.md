# Nursery Field Trip Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nursery Field Trip Registration form is designed to collect information from students and their guardians to facilitate a smooth and enjoyable field trip experience. The form aims to gather essential details about the trip, student, and guardian, ensuring that all necessary information is captured to make the trip a success.

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.

3. In the **Student Details** section, fill in the form with the following:
   * **Student Details** (`student_details`, text, required: false): Enter your name and any relevant information about yourself.
4. In the **Guardian Details** section, fill in the form with the following:
   * **Guardian Details** (`guardian_details`, text, required: false): Enter your name and any relevant information about yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trip Details** (`trip_details`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the trip, including the date, time, and location.
* **Student Details** (`student_details`, text, required: false): Enter the student's name and any other relevant information.
* **Guardian Details** (`guardian_details`, text, required: false): Enter the guardian's name and any other relevant information.
