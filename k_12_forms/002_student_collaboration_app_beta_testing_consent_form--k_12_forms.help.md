# Student Collaboration App Beta Testing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant to be filled out by students who wish to participate in the beta testing of the Student Collaboration App. It will help us understand your preferences and boundaries for collaboration in the app.

## How To Complete This Form

- Fill out the form with your own information if you are the student.
- If you are a parent/guardian, fill out the form with your child's information.
- Make sure to fill in all required fields (marked as "required").
- You can leave optional fields blank if you do not have an answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): Enter your student ID number as provided by your school.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name, as it appears on your school records.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): If you are a parent/guardian, enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your teacher/educator who will be involved in the collaboration.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, select_one, optional): Choose your current grade level from the options provided:
	+ 9th
	+ 10th
	+ 11th
	+ 12th
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are giving consent for your child to participate in the beta testing.
* **Consent Time** (`consent_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you are giving consent for your child to participate in the beta testing.
* **Consent Given** (`consented`, note, optional): This field will be checked by the system administrator. If you check "Yes", your child will be added to the beta testing group. If you check "No", your child will not be added.
* **Student Collaboration Consent** (`student_collaboration_consent`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or both of the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Other (please specify)** (`student_collaboration_consent_other`, text, optional): If you chose "Other" above, please specify your reasons why you cannot participate in the beta testing.
