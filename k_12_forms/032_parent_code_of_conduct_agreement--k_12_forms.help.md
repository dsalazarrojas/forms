# Parent Code Of Conduct Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information from parents or guardians to confirm their child's participation in an activity or program. By filling out this form, parents or guardians acknowledge and agree to the code of conduct guidelines for their child.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the parent or guardian.
2. Enter your name as the guardian (if applicable).
3. Enter your email address (optional).
4. Enter your phone number (optional).
5. Enter your child's name.
6. Enter your child's age.
7. Select your child's grade level.
8. Sign the form as the parent to confirm your agreement to the code of conduct guidelines.
9. Sign the form as the guardian (if applicable) to confirm their agreement to the code of conduct guidelines.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the parent or guardian.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name if you are a guardian (if you are not a parent).
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address (if you wish to provide it).
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number (if you wish to provide it).
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter your child's name.
* **Student Age** (`student_age`, `number`, required): Enter your child's age.
* **Student Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your child's grade level from the available options.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, `note`, required): Sign this field to confirm your agreement to the code of conduct guidelines.
* **Guardian Signature** (`guardian_signature`, `note`, required): Sign this field if you are a guardian, confirming your agreement to the code of conduct guidelines.
