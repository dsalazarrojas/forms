# School Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Application Form is designed to collect information from students and parents/guardians to facilitate the school administration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the student's personal information (Student Name, Parent Name, Date of Birth, Contact Number, Email Address, and Student Address).
2. Choose the student's grade level and school type (Regular, International, or Exchange).
3. Select the student's enrollment status (New, Returning, or Transfer).
4. Provide the school's details (School Name, School Address, School Phone Number, and School Email Address).
5. Specify the student's enrollment and start dates, as well as their end dates.
6. Choose the student's additional school type (Daycare, Elementary, High School, or Middle School).
7. Attach any required student documents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name.
* **Parent Name**: Enter the parent's name.
* **Grade Level**: Select the student's grade level (e.g., 9-12).
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the student's date of birth (mm/dd/yyyy format).
* **Contact Number**: Enter the parent's contact number.
* **Email Address**: Enter the parent's email address.
* **Student Address**: Enter the student's address.
* **Parent Permission**: Choose the parent's permission level (Parent, Other Relative, or No Permission Given).
* **Student Permission**: Select the student's permission level (Parent, Other Relative, or School).
* **School Name**: Enter the school's name.
* **School Address**: Enter the school's address.
* **School Phone Number**: Enter the school's phone number.
* **School Email Address**: Enter the school's email address.
* **School Administrative Information**: Enter any relevant school administration information.
* **Student Document Upload**: Attach any required student documents.
* **Student Document**: Attach any additional documents.
* **School Application Form Type**: Choose the type of school application (Regular, International, or Exchange).
* **School Application Form Sub Type**: Select the sub-type of school application (Daycare, Elementary, High School, or Middle School).
* **Student Enrollment Status**: Select the student's enrollment status (New, Returning, or Transfer).
* **Student Enrollment Date**: Enter the date of the student's enrollment.
* **Student Start Date**: Enter the date the student started at the school.
* **School Start Date**: Enter the date the school started.
* **Student End Date**: Enter the date the student's enrollment ended.
* **School End Date**: Enter the date the school ended.
* **Student Additional Notes**: Enter any additional information about the student.
* **School Application Form Type**: Choose the type of school application (Regular, International, or Exchange).
* **School Application Form Sub Type**: Select the sub-type of school application (Daycare, Elementary, High School, or Middle School).
