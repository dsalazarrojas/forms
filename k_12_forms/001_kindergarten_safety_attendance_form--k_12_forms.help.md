# Kindergarten Safety Attendance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to provide a quick and efficient way to report your child's attendance, including their pickup and dropoff times, medical conditions, and allergies. This information helps us ensure your child's safety and well-being while in our care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about your child.
2. Review each section carefully and ensure that you provide all necessary details.
3. If you have any medical conditions or allergies listed, please provide a brief explanation of each in the corresponding text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`): Enter a brief title for your child, e.g., "John Doe, Kindergarten Student".
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`): Enter the time you will pick up your child from our care.
* **Pickup Location** (`pickup_location`): Enter the location where you will be picking up your child.
* **Dropoff Time** (`dropoff_time`): Enter the time you will drop off your child to our care.
* **Dropoff Location** (`dropoff_location`): Enter the location where your child will be dropped off.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your child's identification card or government-issued ID.
* **Parent Relationship** (`parent_relationship`): Select your relationship to the child (e.g., Mother, Father, Guardian).
* **Child Name** (`child_name`): Enter your child's full name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter your child's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter your phone number or other contact information.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`): Enter any additional information about your child's care or needs.
* **Is Medical Condition** (`is_medical_condition`): Select yes if your child has a medical condition that requires attention.
* **Medical Condition Details** (`medical_condition_details`): Enter a brief explanation of your child's medical condition.
* **Allergy 1** (`allergy_1`): Select any allergies your child may have.
* **Allergy 1 Details** (`allergy_1_details`): Enter a brief explanation of your child's allergy 1.
* **Allergy 2** (`allergy_2`): Select any allergies your child may have.
* **Allergy 2 Details** (`allergy_2_details`): Enter a brief explanation of your child's allergy 2.
* **Allergy 3** (`allergy_3`): Select any allergies your child may have.
* **Allergy 3 Details** (`allergy_3_details`): Enter a brief explanation of your child's allergy 3.
* **Allergy 4** (`allergy_4`): Select any allergies your child may have.
* **Allergy 4 Details** (`allergy_4_details`): Enter a brief explanation of your child's allergy 4.
* **Allergy 5** (`allergy_5`): Select any allergies your child may have.
* **Allergy 5 Details** (`allergy_5_details`): Enter a brief explanation of your child's allergy 5.
* **Medical Allergies** (`medical_allergies`): Select any medical allergies your child may have.
* **Medical Allergies Details** (`medical_allergies_details`): Enter a brief explanation of your child's medical allergies.
