## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information and documentation from students applying to the high school gifted program. It includes fields for student acceptance reasons, program terms and conditions, submission date, submission time, submission email, submission phone number, a status note, and an optional upload of supporting documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read and carefully review the form to understand the purpose of each field.
* Fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
* Provide detailed information in all fields, especially in fields with a 'hint' that provides additional guidance.
* Use the correct format for date and time fields (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY for dates and HH:MM AM/PM for times).
* Upload any required or recommended supporting documents.
* Double-check your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Acceptance Reasons (student_acceptance_reasons)** (`Student Acceptance Reasons`, note, not required): Explain why the student is accepting the high school gifted program. Use this field to provide any relevant reasons or details.
* **Program Terms and Conditions (program_terms_and_conditions)** (`Program Terms and Conditions`, note, not required): Read and acknowledge the terms and conditions of the program. This field is for your reference only.
* **Submission Date (submission_date)** (`Submission Date`, date, not required): Enter the date you are submitting the application.
* **Submission Time (submission_time)** (`Submission Time`, time, not required): Enter the time you are submitting the application.
* **Submission Email (submission_email)** (`Submission Email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Submission Phone (submission_phone)** (`Submission Phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Submission Status Note (submission_status_note)** (`Submission Status Note`, note, not required): Enter any additional information or comments about your submission.
* **Upload Document (upload_document)** (`Upload Document`, text, not required): Upload any supporting documentation required for the application.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your submission for any errors or omissions before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, reach out to the program administrators for guidance.
