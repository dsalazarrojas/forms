# Linear Inequalities Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess users' knowledge about linear inequalities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student name in the "student_name" field.
2. Answer the question in the "answer1" field by selecting one of the available options.
3. Select all relevant answers for the question in the "answer2" field.
4. Type your answer to the open-ended question in the "answer3" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "date" field in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
6. Enter your time of completion in the "time" field in the format of HH:MM:SS.
7. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Please fill in your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Answer 1** (`answer1`, select_one, required): Select one of the available options to answer the question.
* **Answer 2** (`answer2`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant answers for the question.
* **Answer 3** (`answer3`, text, optional): Type your answer to the open-ended question.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter your time of completion in the format of HH:MM:SS.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
