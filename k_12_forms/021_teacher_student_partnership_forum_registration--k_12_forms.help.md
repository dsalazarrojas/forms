# Teacher Student Partnership Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Student Partnership Forum Registration form is used to register participants for a Teacher Student Partnership Forum. This form is required for all three roles: Teacher, Student, and Administrator.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Select the correct role from the dropdown menu.
3. Fill out the event details (event, date, time, location, and notes).
4. Enter your email and phone number.
5. Choose the assigned tool (Chat, Forum, or Video Conference).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a teacher.
- **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter your school's name.
- **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Select your role as a participant (Teacher, Student, or Administrator).
- **Institution** (`institution`, text, required): Enter your institution's name (for example, a school or university).
- **Expectations** (`expectations`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your expectations for the partnership.
- **Event** (`event`, text, required): Enter the name of the event you will be participating in.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of the event.
- **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location of the event.
- **Notes** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the event.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Choose the tool assigned to you for the event (Chat, Forum, or Video Conference).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure your registration is complete.
- Be as detailed as possible in the event details and expectations fields.
- Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
