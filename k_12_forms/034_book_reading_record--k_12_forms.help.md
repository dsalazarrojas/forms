# Book Reading Record - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for recording your reading records of books you have read.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your reading information.
2. Enter your name, grade, and teacher name (optional).
3. Fill in the book title and author.
4. Select the book genre.
5. Select your reading level.
6. Enter the number of pages and time spent reading (optional).
7. Choose the start and finish dates of reading.
8. Rate the book (required).
9. Choose your favorite part and character (optional).
10. Describe why you liked your favorite character.
11. Summarize the book in 3-5 sentences.
12. What did you learn from the book? (optional).
13. Would you recommend the book to friends? (required).
14. List any other books that remind you of this one (optional).
15. List any new words you learned (optional).
16. Sign and date the form with your parent or guardian's signature.
17. Record the date and time of completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Full Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required): Select your grade level.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter your teacher's name.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your school.
* **Book Title** (`book_title`, text, required): Enter the full title of the book.
* **Book Author** (`book_author`, text, required): Enter the author's name.
* **Book Genre** (`book_genre`, select_one, required): Select the book genre.
* **Reading Level** (`reading_level`, select_one, optional): Select your reading level.
* **Number of Pages** (`page_count`, number, optional): Enter the total number of pages.
* **Date Started Reading** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you started reading.
* **Date Finished Reading** (`finish_date`, date, required): Enter the date you finished reading.
* **Book Rating** (`book_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the book.
* **Favorite Part** (`favorite_part`, text, optional): Describe your favorite part of the book.
* **Favorite Character** (`favorite_character`, text, optional): Enter the name of your favorite character.
* **Describe Favorite Character** (`character_description`, text, optional): Enter why you liked your favorite character.
* **Brief Summary** (`book_summary`, text, required): Summarize the book in 3-5 sentences.
* **What Did You Learn** (`what_learned`, text, optional): Enter anything you learned from the book.
* **Would Recommend** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Would you recommend the book to friends.
* **Similar Books** (`similar_books`, text, optional): Enter any other books that remind you of this one.
* **New Words Learned** (`new_words_learned`, text, optional): Enter any new vocabulary you learned.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature** (`parent_signature`, text, optional): Sign and date the form with your parent or guardian.
* **Record Date** (`record_date`, date, required): Enter the date of completion.

Note: This form is not a required form, but it is a useful tool for students to reflect on their reading habits and provide feedback on the books they have read.
