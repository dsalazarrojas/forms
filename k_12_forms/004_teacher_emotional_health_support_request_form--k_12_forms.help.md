# teacher_emotional_health_support_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for teachers to submit requests for emotional health support. It helps the school administration or HR department understand their needs and provide necessary assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your teacher name and email address.
2. Provide the name of your school.
3. Specify your department or team.
4. Choose your grade level.
5. Describe the issue you are experiencing.
6. Indicate if you need support with 'Yes' or 'No' (you can select 'Yes' if you need emotional health support, and 'No' otherwise).
7. If you are requesting support, you can also provide a brief description of what kind of support you need.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required: false)
Enter your full name as it appears on your teacher ID badge.

### 2. **Email** (`email`, email, required: false)
Enter your school-issued email address.

### 3. **School** (`school`, text, required: false)
Enter the name of your school.

### 4. **Department** (`department`, text, required: false)
Enter the name of your department or team.

### 5. **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, text, required: false)
Enter your grade level (e.g., elementary, middle school, high school).

### 6. **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, required: false)
Provide a brief description of the issue you are experiencing.

### 7. **Support Requests** (`support_requests`, select_multiple, required: false)
Choose 'Yes' if you need emotional health support, 'No' otherwise.

### 8. **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required: false)
If you are using this form as part of your school's internal processes, this field is likely for internal tracking purposes. You can leave this field blank.

### 9. **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, required: false)
If you are filling out this form as part of your school's internal processes, this field is likely for tracking the date you submitted the form. You can leave this field blank.

### 10. **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time, required: false)
If you are filling out this form as part of your school's internal processes, this field is likely for tracking the time you submitted the form. You can leave this field blank.

## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields.
* If you need to request support, select 'Yes' under **Support Requests**.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult with your school administration or HR department for guidance.
* Please be as detailed as possible when describing your issue and any support requests.
