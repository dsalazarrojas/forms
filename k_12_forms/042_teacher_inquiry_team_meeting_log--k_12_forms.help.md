# Teacher Inquiry Team Meeting Log - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Teacher Inquiry Team Meeting Log form is a tool for teachers to report on the outcomes and next steps of team meetings. The form helps the Teacher Inquiry Team to track and analyze meeting data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant team members who attended the meeting by choosing their names from the list.
2. Fill in the meeting date and time in the date and time field.
3. Enter a brief description of the meeting outcome in the "Meeting Outcome" field.
4. Describe the next steps to be taken after the meeting in the "Next Steps" field.
5. If the meeting had an outcome that is not listed in the "Meeting Outcome" field, select "Other" and provide a brief description in the "Meeting Outcome (Other)" field.
6. If there are any additional next steps or notes that do not fit in the "Next Steps" field, enter them in the "Next Steps (Other)" field.
7. Finally, enter any additional information that you would like to share with the team in the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Outcome** (`Meeting Outcome`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the meeting outcome.
* **Next Steps** (`Next Steps`, text, required): Describe the next steps to be taken after the meeting.
* **Meeting Date** (`Meeting Date`, date, required): Enter the date of the meeting.
* **Meeting Time** (`Meeting Time`, time, required): Enter the time of the meeting.
* **Team Members** (`Team Members`, select_multiple, required): Select the team members who attended the meeting by choosing their names from the list.
* **Meeting Location** (`Meeting Location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the meeting, if applicable.
* **Meeting Outcome (Other)** (`Meeting Outcome (Other)`, text, optional): If the meeting outcome is not listed in the "Meeting Outcome" field, enter a brief description here.
* **Meeting Outcome Notes** (`Meeting Outcome Notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes on the meeting outcome.
* **Next Steps Notes** (`Next Steps Notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes on the next steps.
* **Next Steps (Other)** (`Next Steps (Other)`, text, optional): If there are any additional next steps or notes that do not fit in the "Next Steps" field, enter them here.
* **Meeting Date and Time** (`Meeting Date and Time`, date, required): Enter the date and time of the meeting.
* **Meeting Duration** (`Meeting Duration`, text, optional): Enter the length of the meeting, if applicable.
* **Meeting Outcome (Other) Notes** (`Meeting Outcome (Other) Notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes on the meeting outcome.
* **Team Leadership** (`Team Leadership`, select_multiple, required): Select the team leaders who attended the meeting by choosing their names from the list.
* **Additional Information** (`Additional Information`, text, optional): Enter any additional information that you would like to share with the team.
