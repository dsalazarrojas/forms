# Teacher Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Teacher Satisfaction Survey is designed to collect feedback from teachers on their teaching experience, gathering their thoughts and opinions to understand their satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields: First name, Last name, Email, and Department (if applicable).
2. Provide your school name (if you have access to it).
3. Answer the four questions: Question 1, Question 2, Question 3, and Question 4.
4. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
5. Once you have filled out all the required and optional fields, click "Submit" to submit your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): This is the first name of the teacher.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): This is the last name of the teacher.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This is the email of the teacher. Please provide a valid email address.
* **School name** (`school_name`, text, optional): This is the name of the school where the teacher works. If you don't have access to it, you can leave it blank.
* **Department** (`department_name`, select_one, optional): This field allows you to select your department from the available options: Math, English, Science, History, or Art.
* **Question 1**, **Question 2**, **Question 3**, **Question 4** (`question_1`, `question_2`, `question_3`, `question_4`, text, optional): Please answer each question briefly, providing your thoughts and opinions.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): This is the field for any additional comments or notes you may want to add. 

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly, providing your true thoughts and opinions.
* Be concise in your answers, keeping them brief and to the point.
* Use a valid email address in the "Email" field.
* If you have access to your school name, please provide it for accurate tracking.
