# Preschool Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential details for preschool enrollment. It helps to gather information from parents or guardians to assign a student to a classroom and a teacher.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Student Name field with the name of the student to be enrolled.
2. Enter the Parent Name field with the name of the parent or guardian of the student.
3. Provide the Date of Birth field with the student's date of birth.
4. Enter the Contact Number field with the contact number for the parent or guardian.
5. Enter the Email field with the email address of the parent or guardian.
6. Enter the School ID field with the ID of the school the student is enrolling in.
7. If applicable, enter the Teacher Name field with the name of the teacher the student will be assigned to.
8. Select one or more Classrooms from the Classroom field to which the student will be assigned.
9. Enter the Classroom Name field with the name of the classroom the student will be assigned to.
10. If necessary, provide additional Notes for the teacher in the Notes for Teacher field.
11. Enter the Teacher Email field with the email address of the teacher assigned to the student.
12. Enter the Student Email field with the email address of the student.
13. Enter the Teacher Contact field with the contact information of the teacher assigned to the student.
14. Enter the Teacher Phone field with the phone number of the teacher assigned to the student.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Preschool Enrollment Form** (Text, Not Required): This is the title of the form.
* **Student Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the student to be enrolled.
* **Parent Name** (Text, Not Required): Enter the name of the parent or guardian of the student.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Not Required): Enter the date of birth of the student.
* **Contact Number** (Text, Not Required): Enter the contact number of the parent or guardian.
* **Email** (Email, Not Required): Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
* **School ID** (Number, Not Required): Enter the ID of the school the student is enrolling in.
* **Teacher Name** (Text, Not Required): If applicable, enter the name of the teacher the student will be assigned to.
* **Classroom** (Select Multiple, Not Required): Select one or more classrooms to which the student will be assigned.
* **Classroom Name** (Text, Not Required): This is the name of the classroom the student will be assigned to.
* **Notes** (Note, Not Required): Enter any additional notes for the student's enrollment.
* **Notes for Teacher** (Text, Not Required): Enter any additional notes for the teacher assigned to the student.
* **Teacher Email** (Email, Not Required): Enter the email address of the teacher assigned to the student.
* **Student Email** (Email, Not Required): Enter the email address of the student.
* **Teacher Contact** (Text, Not Required): Enter the contact information of the teacher assigned to the student.
* **Teacher Phone** (Text, Not Required): Enter the phone number of the teacher assigned to the student.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely to avoid discrepancies.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to prevent errors.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the school's administrative personnel for assistance.
