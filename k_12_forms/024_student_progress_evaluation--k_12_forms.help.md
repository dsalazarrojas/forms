# Student Progress Evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from teachers or educators about a student's progress, allowing for the assessment of their current level of understanding, behavior, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please select the relevant information for each field.
- Fill in the fields as instructed by the form labels.
- Make sure to fill in all required fields, which are marked as `required: true`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Teacher** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the teacher or educator filling out the form.
- **Student** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the student being evaluated.
- **Date Last Observed** (`date_last_observed`, date, optional): Enter the date when the student's progress was last observed.
- **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): Enter the student's ID number or any other unique identifier.
- **Progress Notes** (`progress_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes about the student's progress, including strengths and weaknesses.
- **Grade** (`grade`, select_one, optional): Select the student's current grade level from the options 9th, 10th, 11th, or 12th.
- **Student Grade Scale** (`student_grade_scale`, select_one, optional): Select the student's performance on a scale from 1-5, 1-3, or 1-2.
- **Behavior Notes** (`behavior_notes`, text, optional): Enter any observations about the student's behavior, including areas of improvement.
- **Parent Concerns** (`parent_concerns`, text, optional): Enter any concerns or issues that have been raised by the student's parents or guardians.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the student.
- **Student Comments** (`student_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback from the student.
- **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, optional): Enter the date when the form was completed.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields, even if it's just a placeholder value.
- If a field is not required, you can leave it blank or enter a placeholder value.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact form@gic.mx.
