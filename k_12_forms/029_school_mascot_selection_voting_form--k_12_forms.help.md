# School Mascot Selection Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect opinions on which school mascot is preferred. Please submit your favorite mascot options and any additional information to help us understand your opinion better.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the school mascot you prefer from the options listed.
2. If you have a preferred category for the mascot, please select it from the options provided.
3. You can add any additional information or notes that you think would be helpful for us to understand your opinion.
4. If you want to be reached for more information, please provide your email or phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Mascot Selection 1** (`Which mascot is your favorite`, `select_one`, optional): 
    This field allows you to select a single mascot that you prefer for the school.
* **School Mascot Selection 2** (`Which mascot is your favorite`, `select_multiple`, optional): 
    You can select multiple mascots that you prefer for the school. Selecting multiple mascots will give us a better understanding of your diverse opinions.
* **School Mascot Date** (`When will you have your next game`, `date`, optional): 
    We are interested in knowing when your team's next game will be. This information will help us understand how the game schedule affects your opinion.
* **Next Game Time** (`Next game time`, `time`, optional): 
    Please specify the time when your team's next game will take place. This information will help us understand the best time for the game.
* **Additional Notes** (`Additional notes`, `note`, optional): 
    Add any additional information that you think would be helpful for us to understand your opinion better.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): 
    If you want to be contacted for more information, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): 
    If you want to be contacted for more information, please provide your phone number.
* **School Mascot Title** (`School Mascot Title`, `text`, optional): 
    You can add any relevant title for the mascot you selected.
* **School Mascot Description** (`School Mascot Description`, `text`, optional): 
    Add any additional description about the mascot you selected.
* **Category** (`Category`, `text`, optional): 
    Select a relevant category for the mascot you selected.
* **Category Options** (`Category Options`, `select_one`, optional): 
    Select an option from the list of categories provided for your selected mascot.
