# Leadership Assessment For Early Education - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leadership Assessment For Early Education form is designed to gather information from school leaders and educators about their leadership skills, experience, and submission status. This form helps assess their leadership qualities and provides a platform for submission status updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name in the "What is your name" field.
2. Enter your school's name in the "What is your school name" field.
3. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the "What is your role" question.
4. Select the leadership skills you possess from the "Which leadership skills do you have?" drop-down menu.
5. Enter your years of experience in the "How many years of experience" field.
6. Enter your leadership score, if applicable.
7. Set your submission date and time using the "Submission date" and "Submission time" fields.
8. Enter your email address in the "E-mail" field.
9. Enter your contact number in the "Contact number" field.
10. Add any additional comments in the "Additional comments" field.
11. Provide any submission comments in the "Submission comments" field.
12. Choose the submission status from the "Submission status" drop-down menu.
13. (Optional) If your submission status is not listed, provide an explanation in the "Submission status other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What is your name
* **What is your name**: Your name will be used to identify you as a school leader or educator.
* Required field.

### 2. What is your school name
* **What is your school name**: Enter the name of your school for reference and identification.
* Optional field.

### 3. What is your role
* **What is your role**: Check "Yes" if you are a school leader or educator, "No" otherwise.
* Optional field.

### 4. Which leadership skills do you have?
* **Which leadership skills do you have?**: Select the leadership skills you possess, such as "Yes" for "Yes, I have this skill".
* Optional field.

### 5. How many years of experience
* **How many years of experience**: Enter the number of years you have been in leadership roles.
* Required field.

### 6. What is your leadership score
* **What is your leadership score**: Enter a numerical value representing your leadership abilities.
* Optional field.

### 7. Submission date
* **Submission date**: Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* Required field.

### 8. Submission time
* **Submission time**: Enter the time of submission.
* Optional field.

### 9. E-mail
* **E-mail**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* Optional field.

### 10. Contact number
* **Contact number**: Enter your contact number.
* Optional field.

### 11. Additional comments
* **Additional comments**: Enter any additional comments or information you wish to provide.
* Optional field.

### 12. Submission comments
* **Submission comments**: Enter any comments regarding your submission.
* Optional field.

### 13. Submission status
* **Submission status**: Choose the status of your submission from the provided options, or select "Submission status other" if not listed.
* Required field.

### 14. Submission status other
* **Submission status other**: If "Submission status other" is selected, enter a brief explanation of your submission status.
* Optional field.
