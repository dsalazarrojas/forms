# school_website_image_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain consent from parents or guardians to publish images, videos, or audio recordings of students on a school website.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your response to "Consent" by choosing "Yes" if you agree to publish your child's image or video on the school website, or "No" if you do not agree.
2. Enter your name as the parent or guardian.
3. Enter your school email address.
4. Provide your school contact information.
5. Enter your child's name as it will appear on the school website.
6. If you have chosen to publish your child's image or video on the school website, you will be asked to provide a digital signature.
7. Upload an optional image or video file to accompany your child's image or video on the school website.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Consent** (`Consent`, select_multiple, required): 
This field asks you to indicate whether you agree to publish your child's image or video on the school website. Select "Yes" if you agree, "No" if you do not, or "Not sure" if you are unsure.

### 2. **Restrictions** (`Restrictions`, text, optional):
This field is for any additional restrictions or concerns you have about publishing your child's image or video on the school website. If you don't have any restrictions, you can leave this field blank.

### 3. **e-signature** (`e-signature`, text, required):
If you have chosen to publish your child's image or video on the school website, you will be asked to provide a digital signature here. This will serve as proof of your consent.

### 4. **Parent's Name** (`Parent's Name`, text, required):
Enter your name as the parent or guardian.

### 5. **School Email** (`School Email`, email, required):
Enter your school email address.

### 6. **School Contact Info** (`School Contact Info`, text, required):
Provide your school's contact information.

### 7. **Parent's Name on School Website** (`Parent's Name on School Website`, text, required):
Enter your child's name as it will appear on the school website.

### 8. **School Website Image Consent Image** (`School Website Image Consent Image`, text, required):
If you have chosen to publish your child's image or video on the school website, you will be asked to provide an optional image or video file here.

## Tips
* Make sure to carefully review each field and ensure you have filled out all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can ask your school's administration for clarification.
* Please note that this form is designed to be completed in one sitting, so make sure to have all necessary information available before starting the form.
