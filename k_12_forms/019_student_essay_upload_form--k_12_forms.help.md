# student_essay_upload_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "student_essay_upload_form" is designed to collect student essays online, as part of the "K-12 Forms" category.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the form (Field 1: <u>form_title</u>).
2. Select the category "K-12 Forms" (Field 2: <u>category</u>).
3. Optionally, provide a brief description of the form (Field 3: <u>description</u>).
4. Upload a file, if needed (Field 4: <u>file_upload</u>).
5. Enter the instructor's name (Field 5: <u>instructor_name</u>).
6. Enter the instructor's email (Field 6: <u>instructor_email</u>).
7. Enter the student ID (Field 7: <u>student_id</u>).
8. Set submission tracking information (Field 8: <u>submission_tracking</u>, Field 9: <u>form_submission</u>, Field 10: <u>upload_button</u>, and Field 11: <u>submission_tracking_2</u>).
9. Set form submission information (Field 12: <u>form_submission_2</u>, Field 13: <u>upload_button_2</u>, and Field 14: <u>instructor_name_2</u>).
10. Set submission tracking information (Field 15: <u>submission_tracking_3</u>).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Form Title** (<u>form_title</u>): A brief and descriptive title of the student essay upload form. Please enter a clear and concise title that accurately reflects the purpose of the form.
*   **Category** (<u>category</u>): Select "K-12 Forms" to categorize this form.
*   **Description** (<u>description</u>): A brief description of the form is optional, but it helps to provide context and clarity.
*   **Upload File** (<u>file_upload</u>): If needed, upload a relevant file to accompany the student essay.
*   **Instructor Name** (<u>instructor_name</u>): Enter the name of the instructor associated with this form.
*   **Instructor Email** (<u>instructor_email</u>): Enter the email of the instructor associated with this form.
*   **Student ID** (<u>student_id</u>): Enter the student ID number.
*   **Submission Tracking** (<u>submission_tracking</u>): Track the submission of the student essay.
*   **Form Submission** (<u>form_submission</u>): Set the time of form submission.
*   **Upload Essay** (<u>upload_button</u>): Upload the student essay.
