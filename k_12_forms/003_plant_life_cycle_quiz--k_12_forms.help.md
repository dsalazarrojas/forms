# plant_life_cycle_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The plant_life_cycle_quiz is designed to evaluate knowledge on the life cycle of plants, covering topics such as seed planting, photosynthesis, and plant growth stages.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, read the instructions and understand the questions carefully.
2.  Answer the questions in the corresponding fields. For text fields, provide a clear and concise response.
3.  For select-one and select-multiple fields, choose the correct option from the list.
4.  Make sure to complete all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **How do plants grow from seed** (`seed_planting`): Describe the process of plant growth from seed.
    This field is a text field, where you need to provide a short, clear, and concise response.
*   **What is photosynthesis** (`photosynthesis`): Define the process of photosynthesis in plants.
    This field is also a text field, where you need to provide a short, clear, and concise response.
*   **Types of plants** (`plant_types`): Select one of the following types of plants:
    *   Pineapple
    *   Sunflower
    *   Daisy
    *   Cactus
    *   Apple
    *   Carrot
    *   Radish
    *   Potato
    This field is a select-one field. Choose the type of plant that best suits your response.
*   **Life cycle questions** (`life_cycle_questions`): Select all applicable life cycle stages of a plant:
    *   seed
    *   sprout
    *   adult
    *   seedling
    *   mature
    This field is a select-multiple field. Choose all life cycle stages that apply to your response.
*   **What is the plant life cycle** (`plant_life_cycle`): Provide a brief description of the plant life cycle.
    This field is a note field, where you need to provide a short, clear response.
*   **Growth stage of the plant** (`plant_growth_stage`): Select the growth stage of the plant:
    *   Seed
    *   Sprout
    *   Sapling
    *   Mature
    *   Adult
    This field is a select-one field. Choose the growth stage that best describes the plant's growth.
*   **How many days to maturity** (`days_to_maturity`): Enter the number of days it takes for the plant to mature.
    This field is a number field. Enter a numerical value.
*   **Quiz completion time** (`quiz_completion_time`): Record the time it took to complete the quiz.
    This field is a time field. Enter the time it took to complete the quiz.
*   **Comments about the quiz** (`educator_comments`): Provide any comments or feedback about the quiz.
    This field is a text field. Write any comments or suggestions you have about the quiz.
*   **Educator name** (`educator_name`): Enter your name or the name of the educator who administered the quiz.
    This field is a text field. Enter your name or the name of the educator.

## Tips

*   Make sure to complete all required fields.
*   Use clear and concise language in text fields.
*   Be accurate when selecting options in select-one and select-multiple fields.
*   If you need help or have questions, refer to the instructions or seek assistance from the educator.
