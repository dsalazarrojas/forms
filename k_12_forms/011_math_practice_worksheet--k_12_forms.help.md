# Math Practice Worksheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help students practice their math skills in a controlled environment. It's intended for students of various grade levels, allowing teachers to assess their progress and understanding of math concepts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your student name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Select your grade level from the "Grade Level" dropdown menu.
3. Indicate the math topic you are practicing in the "Math Topic" field.
4. Rate your confidence in the problem in the "Confidence Rating" field.
5. Sign your name as the student in the "Student Signature" field.
6. Sign your name as the teacher in the "Teacher Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your report card or identification.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, false): Select the grade level you are currently in, e.g. 1st, 2nd, 3rd, etc.
* **Math Topic** (`math_topic`, text, required): Describe the math concept or topic you are practicing, e.g. algebra, geometry, etc.
* **Confidence Rating** (`confidence_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your confidence in the problem, from 1 to 6 where 1 is "not confident" and 6 is "very confident".
* **Written Work** (`written_work`, note, false): This field is optional, where you can describe your problem-solving process or any challenges you faced.
* **File Uploads** (`file_uploads`, text, false): You may upload relevant files that support your work, e.g. diagrams or equations.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, false): Leave any additional comments or thoughts you'd like to share about your experience.
* **Math File Uploads** (`math_file_uploads`, text, false): Upload any relevant math-related files, e.g. worksheets or handouts.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, text, required): Sign your name as the student.
* **Teacher Signature** (`teacher_signature`, text, required): Sign your name as the teacher.

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct grade level to ensure accurate assessment.
- Be honest when rating your confidence in the problem, as this helps teachers understand your thought process.
- Keep your "Additional Comments" field concise, as it's meant to complement your work, not replace it.
- Ensure accurate spelling and formatting in the "Student Signature" and "Teacher Signature" fields to ensure verification.
- Upload relevant files that support your work and are easily accessible for teachers to review.
