# Student Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this Student Contact Form is to collect essential information from students' parents or guardians for administrative and communication purposes in a school setting.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name in the **Student Name** field (field 1).
2. Provide the **Parent Name** (field 2).
3. Enter the **Parent Phone** number (field 3) if applicable.
4. Select the **Relationship to Student** (field 4) - select "Parent", "Guardian", or other if necessary.
5. Enter the **Contact Address** (field 5) if applicable.
6. Provide the **Email** (field 6) for both the student and the parent.
7. Select the **Date of Birth** (field 7) of the student.
8. Choose the grade level of the student from the **Student Grade Level** dropdown options (field 12).
9. Enter the **School Name** (field 13).
10. Enter the **School Address** (field 14).
11. Enter the **School Email** (field 15).
12. Enter the **School Phone** (field 16).
13. Enter the **Parent Email** (field 10).
14. Enter the **Student ID** (field 11).
15. Select the **Teacher Name** (field 19), **Teacher Email** (field 20), and **Teacher Phone** (field 21) if applicable.
16. Enter the **School Website** (field 17) and **Teacher Website** (field 22) if applicable.
17. Enter the **School Principal** (field 18) and **Teacher Principal** (field 23) if applicable.
18. Enter the **Parent Occupation** (field 24) and **Parent Income** (field 25) if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Student Name (1)**: Enter the student's full name.
- **Parent Name (2)**: Enter the parent's or guardian's name.
- **Parent Phone (3)**: Enter the parent's phone number if applicable.
- **Relationship to Student (4)**: Select the relationship between the parent/guardian and the student.
- **Contact Address (5)**: Enter the address where the parent/guardian can be contacted.
- **Email (6)**: Enter the parent's/guardian's email address.
- **Date of Birth (7)**: Select the student's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Student Grade Level (12)**: Select the student's grade level from the dropdown options.
- **School Name (13)**: Enter the name of the student's school.
- **School Address (14)**: Enter the address of the student's school.
- **School Email (15)**: Enter the school's email address.
- **School Phone (16)**: Enter the school's phone number.
- **School Website (17)**: Enter the school's website URL if applicable.
- **School Principal (18)**: Enter the name of the school's principal if applicable.
- **Teacher Name (19)**: Enter the name of the teacher if applicable.
- **Teacher Email (20)**: Enter the teacher's email address if applicable.
- **Teacher Phone (21)**: Enter the teacher's phone number if applicable.
- **Teacher Website (22)**: Enter the teacher's website URL if applicable.
- **Teacher Principal (23)**: Enter the name of the teacher's principal if applicable.
- **Parent Occupation (24)**: Enter the parent's/guardian's occupation if applicable.
- **Parent Income (25)**: Enter the parent's/guardian's income if applicable.
- **Parent Email (10)**: Enter the parent's/guardian's email address.

Please ensure that you fill out this form accurately and provide all the necessary information to avoid any potential delays or issues.
