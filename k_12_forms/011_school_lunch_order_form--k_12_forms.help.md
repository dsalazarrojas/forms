# School Lunch Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about meal preferences for the school lunch program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the day of the week for which you would like to order a meal.
2. Choose your preferred lunch option from the available choices.
3. Select the main course option you would like to have with your lunch.
4. Choose your preferred fruit and juice options.
5. Repeat steps 2-4 for each day of the week.
