# Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback from students on their evaluation of a course they have taken. This feedback is essential for instructors to understand what they are doing well and what they can improve on for future courses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name and grade level (if applicable).
2. Provide the title of the course you are evaluating.
3. Choose the correct semester you took the course.
4. Rate your overall experience with the course using the number rating provided.
5. Provide any comments or suggestions you have for improving the course.
6. Let us know if you would recommend the course to others.
7. If you have any suggestions or comments, please include them in the text field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, text, required): This is the field where you enter your name.
* **Grade Level** (`Grade Level`, select_one, optional): This is the field where you select your current grade level (e.g., 9th-10th, 11th-12th).
* **Course Title** (`Course Title`, text, optional): This is the field where you enter the title of the course you took.
* **Semester** (`Semester`, select_one, optional): This is the field where you select the semester you took the course (e.g., Fall, Spring, Summer).
* **Overall Rating** (`Overall Rating`, number, optional): This is the field where you rate your overall experience with the course (1-5).
* **Comment** (`Comment`, text, optional): This is the field where you can provide any comments or suggestions you have for improving the course.
* **Recommendation** (`Would you recommend this course?`, select_one, optional): This is the field where you let us know if you would recommend the course to others.
* **Suggestions** (`What could we do to improve the course?`, text, optional): This is the field where you can provide any suggestions or comments for improving the course.
* **Rating Comments** (`Comments on rating`, text, optional): This is the field where you can provide any comments or suggestions regarding your rating.
* **Would you recommend this course?** (`Would you recommend this course?`, select_one, optional): This is the field where you let us know if you would recommend the course to others.
