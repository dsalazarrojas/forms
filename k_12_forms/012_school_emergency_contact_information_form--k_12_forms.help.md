# School Emergency Contact Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Emergency Contact Information Form is used to gather contact information for students' emergency contacts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Indicate your relationship with the student by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Relationship" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your home address in the "Address" field.
7. Enter the city where you reside in the "City" field.
8. Enter the state where you reside in the "State" field.
9. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
10. Enter the name of the school your student attends in the "School Name" field.
11. Enter the program your student is in in the "School Program" field.
12. Indicate your emergency contact's relationship with your student by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Program Type" field.
13. Enter the emergency contact's first name in the "Emergency Contact First Name" field (if applicable).
14. Enter the emergency contact's last name in the "Emergency Contact Last Name" field (if applicable).
15. Enter the emergency contact's phone number in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
16. Enter the emergency contact's email address in the "Emergency Contact Email" field.
17. Enter the emergency contact's home address in the "Emergency Contact Address" field.
18. Enter the city where the emergency contact resides in the "Emergency Contact City" field.
19. Enter the state where the emergency contact resides in the "Emergency Contact State" field.
20. Enter the zip code where the emergency contact resides in the "Emergency Contact Zip" field.
21. Indicate the emergency contact's relationship with your student by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Program Type" field.
22. If you have any other contact information you would like to provide, enter it in the "Other Contact Information" field (if applicable).
23. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Relationship** (`relationship`, select_one, required): Indicate your relationship with the student by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your home address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state where you reside.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school your student attends.
* **School Program** (`school_program`, text, required): Enter the program your student is in.
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_multiple, required): Indicate your student's program type by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (`emergency_contact_first_name`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's first name (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (`emergency_contact_last_name`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's last name (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's relationship with your student.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, required): Enter the emergency contact's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's home address.
* **Emergency Contact City** (`emergency_contact_city`, text, required): Enter the city where the emergency contact resides.
* **Emergency Contact State** (`emergency_contact_state`, text, required): Enter the state where the emergency contact resides.
* **Emergency Contact Zip** (`emergency_contact_zip`, text, required): Enter the zip code where the emergency contact resides.
* **Emergency Contact Program** (`emergency_contact_program`, text, required): Enter the program your emergency contact is in.
* **Emergency Contact Program Type** (`emergency_contact_program_type`, select_multiple, required): Indicate your emergency contact's program type by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Other Contact Information** (`other_contact`, text, required): Enter any other contact information you would like to provide (if applicable).
* **Submit** (`submit`, note, required): Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.
