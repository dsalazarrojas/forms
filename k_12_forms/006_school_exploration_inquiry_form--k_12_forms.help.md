# School Exploration Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for prospective families to inquire about schools in the K-12 education category. It is intended to gather information for school exploration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Prospective Family Contact Information** on the first page.
2. On the second page, select the **School of Interest** from the list of available options.
3. Enter the **Student Name**, **Grade Level and Date of Birth** (formatted as Grade Level and Date of Birth, e.g., 3rd, 12/02/2000), and select the student's **Student Interests** (e.g., Art, Athletics, Band, etc.).
4. Choose the desired **School Visit Preferences** (e.g., True, False, or Undecided) and **Parent Preferences** (e.g., In-person, Phone, Email, or Online).
5. Select the preferred **Contact Preferences** (e.g., In-person, Phone, Email, or Online).
6. Indicate whether you are familiar with the **Admissions Process** (e.g., True, False, or Undecided).
7. Finally, provide any additional **Next Steps** (optional) and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Prospective Family Contact Information (Page 1)**: This page is where you can enter your name, phone number, and email address. Enter your name, phone number, and email address as accurately as possible.
* **School Information (Page 2)**: Select the school that you are interested in by choosing an option from the list (e.g., School of Interest).
* **Student Name (Page 2)**: Enter the name of the student who will be attending the school.
* **Grade Level and Date of Birth (Page 2)**: Enter the student's grade level and date of birth in the format specified (e.g., 3rd, 12/02/2000).
* **Student Interests (Page 2)**: Select all the interests that apply to the student (e.g., Art, Athletics, Band, etc.).
* **School Visit Preferences (Page 2)**: Choose whether you would prefer to visit the school in-person, by phone, by email, or online.
* **Parent Preferences (Page 2)**: Choose how you would prefer to be contacted (e.g., In-person, Phone, Email, or Online).
* **Contact Preferences (Page 2)**: Choose the methods by which you would like to be contacted (e.g., In-person, Phone, Email, or Online).
* **Admissions Process (Page 2)**: Indicate whether you understand the admissions process or are undecided.
* **Next Steps (Page 2)**: Provide any additional information or next steps you would like to take after submitting this form (optional).
