# High School Tutoring Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record high school students' attendance during tutoring sessions. It captures essential details such as student name, ID, session date, time, location, and other relevant information to track attendance, absence reasons, and notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Provide the student's ID number in the "Student ID" field.
3. Select the session date in the "Session Date" field.
4. Enter the session time in the "Session Time" field.
5. If the session was held at a specific location, enter it in the "Session Location" field. (This field is optional.)
6. Check the "Student Tutor Ratio" field to indicate if the student had a student tutor present during the session. (Select "Yes" if the student was accompanied by a tutor, "No" if they were not, or select "Other" if applicable.)
7. In the "Student Attendance Status" field, select "attended" if the student attended the session, "not attended" if they did not, or "unknown" if the status is uncertain.
8. If the session started at a specific time, enter it in the "Session Start" field.
9. If the session ended at a specific time, enter it in the "Session End" field.
10. In the "Session Absence Reason" field, provide a brief reason for the student's absence from the session. (This field is optional.)
11. Enter the student's grade level in the "Student Grade" field.
12. In the "Session Start Time" field, enter the start time of the session if applicable.
13. In the "Session End Time" field, enter the end time of the session if applicable.
14. In the "Session Notes" field, provide any additional comments or notes about the session.
15. In the "Student Attendance Status2" field, select "attended" if the student attended the session, "not attended" if they did not, or "unknown" if the status is uncertain. (This field is optional and duplicate of "Student Attendance Status.")
16. In the "Student Absence Reason2" field, provide a brief reason for the student's absence from the session. (This field is optional and duplicate of "Session Absence Reason.")
17. In the "Session Absence Reason3" field, provide a brief reason for the session's absence. (This field is optional and duplicate of "Session Absence Reason2.")
18. In the "Session Absence Reason4" field, provide a brief reason for the session's absence. (This field is optional and duplicate of "Session Absence Reason3.")
