# Classroom Rules Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Classroom Rules Agreement Form is a document that helps establish a clear understanding of the classroom rules and expectations between students and parents. It's used to gather acknowledgments and information from both students and parents, promoting a positive and productive learning environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Complete Student Information**:
   - Fill in your student's name, ID number, grade level, and teacher's name.
   - Make sure to enter accurate information in all fields marked as "required."

2. **Acknowledge Classroom Rules**:
   - Review the list of classroom rules and select "I Agree" for each rule that you understand and will follow.
   - Be sure to acknowledge all rules, as they are an essential part of the classroom environment.

3. **Parent or Guardian Section**:
   - If you're a parent or guardian, fill in your name, email, and phone number.
   - Review the classroom rules with your child and select your acknowledgment options for each rule.
   - Commit to supporting the classroom rules and communicate with the teacher.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter your full name as a student.
* **Student ID Number**: Enter your student ID number for identification purposes.
* **Grade Level**: Select your current grade level (e.g., Kindergarten, Grade 1, Grade 2, etc.).
* **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of your classroom teacher.
* **Classroom Number**: Enter the room number or classroom identifier.
* **School Year**: Enter the academic year (e.g., 2023-2024).
* **I will respect others and their property**: Acknowledge that you will respect others and their property.
* **I will follow directions the first time they are given**: Acknowledge that you will follow directions immediately.
* **I will raise my hand and wait to be called on before speaking**: Confirm that you will raise your hand before speaking.
* **I will stay in my seat unless given permission to move**: Agree to stay seated unless given permission to move.
* **I will complete and turn in all assignments on time**: Acknowledge that you will finish and hand in work on schedule.
* **I will not bully, harass, or intimidate others**: Agree to maintain a positive, respectful atmosphere.
* **I will help keep the classroom clean and organized**: Commit to contributing to a clean and organized classroom environment.
* **I will use technology and devices responsibly and only for learning**: Understand the proper use of technology and devices in the classroom.
* **I will arrive to class on time and prepared to learn**: Arrive on time and be prepared for lessons.
* **I will do my own work and not cheat or plagiarize**: Commit to completing original work without cheating or plagiarizing.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: Enter your full name as a parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Have you reviewed the classroom rules with your child?**: If applicable, select "Yes - We have reviewed and understand" or "Yes - I have reviewed (student reviewed independently)". If not, choose "No - Need to schedule a conference."
* **I commit to supporting these rules and communicating with the teacher**: Confirm your commitment to supporting the classroom rules and communicating with the teacher.
* **Additional Concerns or Comments**: If you have any concerns or comments, enter them here.
* **Student Signature (Type Full Name)**: Enter your student's electronic signature.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature (Type Full Name)**: Enter your electronic signature.
* **Date of Agreement**: Enter the date when the rules were agreed upon.

## Tips
* Make sure to review each field carefully and accurately before submitting.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, don't hesitate to ask your teacher for clarification.
* This form is an essential part of the classroom environment, and it's crucial to take the time to complete it thoroughly.
* If you need to make any changes or have further questions, please reach out to your teacher or school administration.
