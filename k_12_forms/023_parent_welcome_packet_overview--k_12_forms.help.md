# Parent Welcome Packet Overview - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Parent Welcome Packet Overview" form is used to gather information from parents or guardians about their children, such as school details and personal preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully and fill it out completely to ensure you provide all necessary information.
- If you are unsure about any field, ask a school representative for clarification.
- Submit the completed form to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### School Name (school_name)
* **school_name**: This field is for you to enter your child's school name.
* **Type**: Text field.
* **Required**: No.

### Contact Preferences (contact_preferences)
* **contact_preferences**: This field allows you to select how you would like to be contacted by the school.
* **Type**: Select multiple options.
* **Options**: 
  * Option 1
  * Option 2
* **Required**: No.

### Question/Comment (question)
* **question**: This field is for you to write any additional comments or questions you have about your child's school experience.
* **Type**: Text field.
* **Required**: No.
