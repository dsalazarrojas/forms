# Sight Word Assessment Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to assess students' knowledge of sight words. It is meant to gauge their understanding of sight words that they have learned through prior instruction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the correct student information (grade level, name, etc.)
2. Select the sight words that you have been taught.
3. Submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student-info (student_info_page)**: Choose the appropriate student information from the provided options to associate with your assessment. This will help the teacher or administrator to identify which student's assessment results to display.
* **Assessment (assessment_page)**: Select all the sight words that you have been taught. Each option is a sight word that may appear on the assessment.
* **Results (results_page)**: This field is not applicable as it's a date field, but it's here for form structure purposes.
* **Additional-info (additional_info_page)**: Provide any additional comments or information that you would like to add to your assessment.
* **Submit (submit_page)**: Enter your name or other identifier as a final step before submitting your assessment.

Note that this form has two submit fields, but only the last one is required for actual submission. The first is likely there for form structure purposes.
