# Field Trip Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Trip Survey form is used to collect information about field trips for students and teachers. This form helps us to monitor and record trip details accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the field trip is taking place.
2. Fill in the "Trip Date" field with the date of the field trip (if applicable).
3. Enter the "Trip Time" for the field trip (if applicable).
4. Type in the "Trip Location" field with the location of the trip.
5. Enter the name of the "Trip Organization" (if any).
6. For "Student Attendance" and "Teacher Attendance", select the number of students and teachers attending the trip.
7. Input the time of "Bus Arrival".
8. Enter the time of "Bus Departure".
9. Enter the name of the "Bus Driver".
10. Type in the "Bus License" if necessary.
11. Enter the "Bus Vehicle ID" (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Yes/No** (1): Indicates whether the field trip is taking place.
* **Trip Date** (2): The date of the field trip.
* **Trip Time** (3): The time of the trip.
* **Trip Location** (4): The location of the trip.
* **Trip Organization** (5): The name of the organization leading the trip (if any).
* **Student Attendance** (6): Number of students attending the trip.
* **Teacher Attendance** (7): Number of teachers attending the trip.
* **Bus Arrival** (8): Time of bus arrival.
* **Bus Departure** (9): Time of bus departure.
* **Bus Driver Name** (10): Name of the bus driver.
* **Bus License** (11): License number or ID of the bus vehicle (if necessary).
* **Bus Vehicle ID** (12): Vehicle ID of the bus (if necessary).

## Tips
- Please fill out all relevant fields.
- If the trip is not taking place, please fill out only the "Yes" field.
- Be accurate and thorough in entering the bus-related information.
