# School Transport Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about students' transportation to school. It is intended for students or school administrators to provide details about their school's transportation services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form completely and accurately.
2. Enter the bus route number.
3. Provide the name of the bus stop you take.
4. Enter the ID number of your bus route.
5. Enter the name of the school you attend.
6. Enter the address of the school you attend.
7. Enter the city of the school you attend.
8. Enter the state of the school you attend.
9. Enter the post office code of the school you attend.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Transport Survey** (school_transport_survey, text, optional): This section is where you can describe your daily school transport experience.
* **Bus Route** (bus_route, number, optional): Enter the number of the bus route you take to get to school.
* **Bus Stop** (bus_stop, text, optional): This is the stop where your bus drops you off. Please provide the name or number of the stop.
* **Bus Route ID** (bus_route_id, number, optional): Enter the ID number of your bus route.
* **School Name** (school_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the school you attend.
* **School Address** (school_address, text, optional): Enter the address of the school you attend.
* **School City** (school_city, text, optional): Enter the city of the school you attend.
* **School State** (school_state, text, optional): Enter the state of the school you attend.
* **School Postal Code** (school_postal_code, text, optional): Enter the post office code of the school you attend.

## Tips
* Double-check that all fields are completed before submission.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your school's administration for clarification.
* Remember to keep your input accurate to help improve school's transportation services.
