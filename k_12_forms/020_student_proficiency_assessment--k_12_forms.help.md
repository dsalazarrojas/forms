# Student Proficiency Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess a student's proficiency in various subjects, allowing educators to evaluate their skills and knowledge in areas such as English Language Arts, Science, Mathematics, Social Studies, Physical Education, Health, and Foreign Language. The form is used to gather information on student proficiency, which can help inform instruction and support student growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the subject areas where you are assessing the student's proficiency.
2. Select whether the student is proficient or not for each subject area.
3. Rate the student's performance on a scale of 1 to 5, if applicable.
4. Provide feedback on the student's overall performance and areas for improvement.
5. Check if there are any improvement plans in place for the student.
6. Enter the date the assessment was completed.
7. Record the name of the person who completed the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Student Proficiency** (`student_proficiency`, select_multiple, Optional)
Select the subject areas where the student is currently proficient.

*   Choose one or more of the following options: English Language Arts, Science, Mathematics, Social Studies, Physical Education, Health, or Foreign Language.

### **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, Optional)
Enter the student's name.

### **Subject Area** (`subject_area`, select_one, Optional)
Select the subject area you are assessing.

*   Choose one of the following options: English Language Arts, Science, Mathematics, Social Studies, Physical Education, Health, or Foreign Language.

### **Skill Assessed** (`skill_assessed`, select_multiple, Optional)
Select whether the student was assessed on the following skills:

*   Yes for skills that were assessed.
*   No for skills that were not assessed.

### **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, select_one, Optional)
Choose a rating from 1 to 5 to evaluate the student's performance.

*   1 = Needs significant improvement
*   2 = Needs some improvement
*   3 = Meets expectations
*   4 = Exceeds expectations
*   5 = Exceeds expectations significantly

### **Overall Performance** (`overall_performance`, number, Optional)
Enter the student's overall performance rating.

*   Enter a number from 1 to 5.

### **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, Optional)
Provide feedback on the student's overall performance.

### **Improvement Plans** (`improvement_plans`, select_multiple, Optional)
Select whether improvement plans are in place for the student.

*   Yes for students with improvement plans.
*   No for students without improvement plans.

### **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, Optional)
Enter the date the assessment was completed.

### **Recorded By** (`recorded_by`, text, Optional)
Enter the name of the person who completed the assessment.
