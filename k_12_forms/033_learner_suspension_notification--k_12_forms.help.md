## Learner Suspension Notification - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Learner Suspension Notification" form is used to notify learners of their suspension status and gather information about their suspension period.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the relevant fields as required.
2. Enter your suspension period in the "Suspension Periods" field.
3. Enter the start date of your suspension in the "Suspension Start Date" field.
4. Enter the end date of your suspension in the "Suspension End Date" field.
5. Provide a clear reason for your suspension in the "Reason for Suspension" field.
6. Select whether you would like to receive notifications to your parents in the "Notification to Parents" field.
7. Choose the follow-up steps for your suspension in the "Follow-up Steps" field.
8. Select the method for follow-up steps in the "Follow-up Method" field.
9. Enter the date for follow-up steps in the "Follow-up Date" field.
10. Provide any relevant information for suspension resolution in the "Suspension Resolution" field.
11. Enter your school's name and contact information in the "School Name", "School Phone", and "School Email" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Suspension Periods**: Enter the length of time you are suspended from school. 
* **Suspension Start Date**: Enter the date your suspension started. 
* **Suspension End Date**: Enter the date your suspension will end. 
* **Reason for Suspension**: Provide a clear and brief explanation of why you are being suspended. 
* **Notification to Parents**: Select "Yes" if you want to receive notifications to your parents, otherwise select "No". 
* **Follow-up Steps**: Select "Yes" if you are required to take follow-up steps, otherwise select "No". 
* **Follow-up Method**: Select the method you would like to use for follow-up steps. 
* **Follow-up Date**: Enter the date for follow-up steps. 
* **Suspension Resolution**: Provide any relevant information about your suspension resolution. 
* **School Name**: Enter your school's name. 
* **School Phone**: Enter your school's phone number. 
* **School Email**: Enter your school's email address.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your school administration for guidance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your school's administration.
