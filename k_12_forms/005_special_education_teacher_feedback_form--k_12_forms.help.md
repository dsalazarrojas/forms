# Special Education Teacher Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on special education teachers from their students to help improve teacher performance and teaching style.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, select an answer for each question by choosing one of the provided options. You do not need to complete all questions, as they are optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Performance** (Overall Performance, Select One, Required: False)
	+ This question asks how you rate the overall performance of your special education teacher. Select one of the three options: Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Teacher Support** (Teacher Support, Select One, Required: False)
	+ This question asks how often your teacher provides support and guidance when you need it. Choose from Always, Often, or Rarely.
* **Student Performance** (Student Performance, Select One, Required: False)
	+ This question asks how you rate your own performance and progress in the class. Select one of the three options: Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Teacher Attendance** (Teacher Attendance, Select One, Required: False)
	+ This question asks how often your teacher attends classes regularly. Choose from Always, Often, or Rarely.
* **Communication** (Communication, Select One, Required: False)
	+ This question asks how you rate the communication between you and your teacher. Select one of the three options: Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Overall Rating** (Overall Rating, Select One, Required: False)
	+ This question asks for your overall rating of your special education teacher. Choose from Excellent, Good, Average, or Poor.
* **Teacher Feedback** (Teacher Feedback, Select One, Required: False)
	+ This question asks how often your teacher asks for your feedback and opinions. Select from Always, Often, or Rarely.
* **Ask Questions** (Ask Questions, Select One, Required: False)
	+ This question asks how often your teacher provides opportunities for you to ask questions. Choose from Always, Often, or Rarely.
