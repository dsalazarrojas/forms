# Vision Screening Results Log - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to record and document the results of a vision screening performed on students. The form captures key information about the screening, including the school identifier, screening date, student ID, and results.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the school from the list to associate the screening with the correct school.
2. Enter the date the screening was performed.
3. Enter the student ID to identify the student being screened.
4. Choose the result of the screening from the options provided.
5. Provide any additional details about the result.
6. Enter any follow-up recommendations or notes.
7. (Optional) Select the follow-up status and schedule.
8. Enter any outcome comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Id (1)**: Enter the ID of the school where the screening was performed.
* **Screening Date (2)**: Enter the date the screening was performed.
* **Student Id (3)**: Enter the ID of the student being screened.
* **Eye Examined By (4)**: Enter the name of the person who performed the screening.
* **Result Type (5)**: Choose the result of the screening from the options provided.
* **Result Details (6)**: Enter any additional details about the result.
* **Follow Up Recommendations (7)**: Enter any follow-up recommendations or notes.
* **Notes (8)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the screening.
* **Follow Up Scheduled (9)**: (Optional) Select the follow-up status and schedule.
* **Follow Up Date (10)**: (Optional) Enter the date of the scheduled follow-up.
* **Outcome Likely (11)**: Choose the outcome likely from the options provided.
* **Outcome Unlikely (12)**: Choose the outcome unlikely from the options provided.
* **Outcome Unknown (13)**: Choose the outcome unknown from the options provided.
* **Outcome Pending (14)**: Choose the outcome pending from the options provided.
* **Outcome Don't Know (15)**: Choose the outcome don't know from the options provided.
* **Outcome Scheduled (16)**: Choose the outcome scheduled from the options provided.
* **Outcome Refused (17)**: Choose the outcome refused from the options provided.
* **Follow Up Status (18)**: (Optional) Select the follow-up status from the options provided.
* **Follow Up Last Updated (19)**: (Optional) Enter the date of the last follow-up update.
* **Outcome Comments (20)**: Enter any comments about the outcome.

Note: The form fields marked as 'Optional' are only displayed when the corresponding options are selected.
