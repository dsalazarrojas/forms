# Online Learning Reflection Story Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online learning reflection story consent form is used to collect user reflections on their online learning experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your title for the reflection in the "Online Learning Reflection Story Consent Form" field.
2. Provide a brief description of your reflection in the "Description" field.
3. Select the category for your reflection from the "Category" dropdown menu.
4. If you have a file related to your reflection, you can upload it in the "Upload File" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Click the "Submit" button to save your reflection.
