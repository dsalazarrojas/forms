# Restaurant Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Restaurant Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback on your dining experience at this restaurant. Your input will help us improve our service and food offerings to better serve you and future customers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question based on your recent dining experience at this restaurant.
2. Use the scale provided for each question to rate your experience (1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest).
3. If a question does not apply to your experience, please mark the corresponding box with an "N/A" (Not Applicable).
4. Use the "Additional Comments" section to share any additional thoughts or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Food Quality (1)**: How would you rate the quality of the food you ordered?
	+ Scale: 1-5
* **Overall Service Quality (2)**: How would you rate the overall service quality you received during your visit?
	+ Scale: 1-5
* **Cleanliness (3)**: How would you rate the cleanliness of the restaurant facilities and surroundings?
	+ Scale: 1-5
* **Order Accuracy (4)**: How accurate was the order taken by the server?
	+ Scale: 1-5
* **Speed of Service (5)**: How would you rate the speed of service you received during your visit?
	+ Scale: 1-5
* **Value for Money (6)**: How would you rate the value for money you received during your visit?
	+ Scale: 1-5
* **Atmosphere (7)**: How would you rate the atmosphere of the restaurant?
	+ Scale: 1-5
* **Parking (8)**: How would you rate the parking facilities and convenience?
	+ Scale: 1-5
* **Staff Friendliness (9)**: How would you rate the friendliness of the staff?
	+ Scale: 1-5
* **Food Quality 2 (10)**: How would you rate the quality of the food you ordered (this question is similar to the first, but if you have additional feedback, please use this space)?
	+ Scale: 1-5

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when answering these questions.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments" section at the end of the form.
* If you have already submitted feedback or evaluations for this restaurant, please do not submit duplicate entries.
