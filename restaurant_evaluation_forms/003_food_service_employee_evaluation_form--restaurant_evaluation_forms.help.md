# Food Service Employee Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Service Employee Evaluation Form is a tool used to assess the performance of food service employees. It helps managers or supervisors to evaluate employees' overall performance, rating, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's name in the "employee_name" field.
2. Specify the employee's position in the "position" field.
3. Enter the date of the evaluation in the "evaluation_date" field (optional).
4. Rate the employee's performance in the "rating" field using the provided options (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor).
5. Enter any comments or feedback in the "comment" field.
6. Enter the employee's contact information (email and phone number).
7. Enter your own contact information as the supervisor (name, email, and phone number).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (employee_name, text, required): 
Enter the name of the employee being evaluated.
* **Position** (position, text, required): 
Enter the employee's job title or position.
* **Evaluation Date** (evaluation_date, date, optional): 
Enter the date of the evaluation, if applicable.
* **Rating** (rating, select_one, required): 
Choose one of the provided rating options (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor) to assess the employee's performance.
* **Comment** (comment, note, optional): 
Enter any comments or feedback for the employee.
* **Email** (email, email, required): 
Enter the employee's email address.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): 
Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Supervisor Name** (supervisor_name, text, required): 
Enter your own name as the supervisor.
* **Supervisor Email** (supervisor_email, email, required): 
Enter your email address as the supervisor.
* **Supervisor Phone** (supervisor_phone, text, required): 
Enter your phone number as the supervisor.
