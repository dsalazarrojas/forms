# Food Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess a restaurant's food safety protocols and ensure that they are in compliance with industry standards.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form in sections, one section at a time.
- Ensure that you answer every question to the best of your ability.
- Do not skip any questions, as every answer is necessary for an accurate evaluation.
- Use this form for the first time you are evaluating this restaurant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Page (Page 1)** (`title`, `text`, Not Required): This section provides an introduction to the form and serves as an informational page.
- **Food Handling (Page 2)** (`food_handling`, `text`, Not Required): This section asks questions about the food handling practices at the restaurant, such as how food is prepared, stored, and handled.
  - For example, are utensils and equipment cleaned and sanitized regularly?
  - Are food handlers wearing gloves and hairnets?
- **Food Storage (Page 3)** (`food_storage`, `text`, Not Required): This section inquires about the restaurant's food storage procedures, such as the storage of raw and cooked food.
  - For instance, are raw and cooked foods stored separately and at the correct temperatures?
- **Temperature Control (Page 4)** (`temperature_control`, `text`, Not Required): This section examines the restaurant's temperature control measures, such as refrigerator and freezer temperatures.
  - Are refrigerators and freezers at the correct temperatures?
  - Are temperature records kept?
- **First Page 2 (Page 5)** (`first_page_2`, `text`, Not Required): This section is another informational page. 

## Tips
- Please answer every question carefully and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any question, try to answer based on your best judgment or observation.
- This form is for internal use only. Please do not share your answers with anyone not authorized to access them.
- Please fill out the form honestly and accurately to ensure that the restaurant is evaluated fairly.
