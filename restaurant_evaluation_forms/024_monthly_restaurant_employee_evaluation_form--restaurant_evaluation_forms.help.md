This form is designed for restaurant staff members to evaluate their colleagues' performance on a monthly basis. The evaluation is an essential process for restaurants to monitor and improve employee performance, address any issues, and provide feedback for growth. This form will be used to collect information from restaurant managers, supervisors, or other staff members. Please note that this form is a standard template and might need adjustments according to your restaurant's specific policies and procedures.

# monthly_restaurant_employee_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The monthly restaurant employee evaluation form is a tool for restaurant staff members to evaluate and provide feedback on their colleagues' performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "employee name" field.
2. Select the employee's department from the "department" dropdown menu (if applicable).
3. Enter the employee's title in the "employee title" field.
4. Write any comments on the employee's performance in the "performance comments" field.
5. Identify areas where the employee needs improvement in the "areas for improvement" dropdown menu (if applicable).
6. Provide an overall performance rating for the employee in the "overall performance rating" dropdown menu.
7. If desired, include any comments from the employee in the "employee comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee name** (employee name, text, required): Enter the name of the employee being evaluated.
* **department** (department, select_one, required: false): Select the department the employee works in (if applicable).
* **employee title** (employee title, text, required): Enter the employee's job title or position.
* **performance comments** (performance comments, note, required: false): Write any comments or observations about the employee's performance.
* **areas for improvement** (areas for improvement, select_multiple, required: false): Identify areas where the employee needs improvement (select multiple options).
* **overall performance rating** (overall performance rating, select_one, required): Rate the employee's overall performance as Outstanding, Needs Improvement, Satisfactory, or Unsatisfactory.
* **employee comments** (employee comments, text, required: false): Include any comments or feedback from the employee (if applicable).

## Tips
* Be specific and concrete when providing feedback.
* Focus on areas of improvement and suggestions for growth.
* Consider using the dropdown menus to make your evaluation more efficient.
* Proofread your responses to ensure accuracy and clarity.
