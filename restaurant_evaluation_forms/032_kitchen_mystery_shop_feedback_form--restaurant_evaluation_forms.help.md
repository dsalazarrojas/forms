## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on your recent visit to our restaurant. Your input is invaluable to us, as it helps us understand what we're doing well, what we're doing wrong, and how we can improve to provide the best possible dining experience for you and our other customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
2. Select all applicable options for each question.
3. Provide as much detail as you're comfortable sharing in the customer_comments field.
4. Ensure that you've reviewed each question carefully before submitting your feedback.
