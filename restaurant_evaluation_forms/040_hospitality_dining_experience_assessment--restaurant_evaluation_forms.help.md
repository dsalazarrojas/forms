# Hospitality Dining Experience Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their dining experience at our restaurant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Select your satisfaction rating in the "satisfaction_rating" field.
6. Indicate your intent to recommend the restaurant to others in the "recommendation_intent" field.
7. Enter any additional comments you'd like to share in the "comments" field (optional).
8. Enter the name of the restaurant you visited in the "restaurant_name" field.
9. Enter the date you visited the restaurant in the "visited_date" field.
10. Enter the duration of your visit in the minutes in the "visit_duration" field.
11. Enter the time of your visit in the 24-hour clock format in the "visited_time" field.
12. Select your level of satisfaction with the service quality in the "satisfaction_level" field.
13. Select the quality of the service you experienced during your visit in the "service_quality" field.
14. Select the quality of the food you had during your visit in the "food_quality" field.
15. Select the price range of the meal in the "price_range" field.
16. Indicate if you visited the restaurant with others in the "visited_with" field.
17. Select if you visited the restaurant during this visit in the "visited_frequency" field.
18. Select the purpose of your visit in the "visit_purpose" field.
19. Indicate if you recommended the restaurant to others in the "recommended_to" field.
20. Enter your reason for recommending or not recommending the restaurant in the "recommend_reason" field (optional).
21. Indicate if you visited the restaurant on a previous visit in the "visited_previous_visit" field.
22. Indicate if you visited the restaurant on the next visit or not in the "visited_next_visit" field.
23. Select if this was a revisit to the restaurant in the "visited_previous_visit" field.
24. Indicate if you visited the restaurant before in the "visited_before" field.
25. Select if you've ever visited the restaurant in the "visited_ever" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name (text, required)**: Enter your first name.
* **last_name (text, required)**: Enter your last name.
* **email (email, required)**: Enter your email address.
* **phone (text, required)**: Enter your phone number.
* **satisfaction rating (number, required)**: Select your level of satisfaction with the service, food, and overall experience.
* **intent to recommend (text, required)**: Indicate if you would recommend this restaurant to others.
* **comments (note, optional)**: Enter any additional comments about your visit.
* **restaurant name (text, required)**: Enter the name of the restaurant you visited.
* **visited date (date, required)**: Enter the date of your visit in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **visit duration (number, required)**: Enter the duration of your visit in minutes.
* **visited time (time, required)**: Enter the time of your visit in the 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
* **satisfaction level (select_one, required)**: Select your level of satisfaction with the service.
* **service quality (select_multiple, required)**: Select the quality of service you experienced during your visit.
* **food quality (select_multiple, required)**: Select the quality of food you had during your visit.
* **price range (select_multiple, required)**: Select the price range of the meal.
* **visited with (select_multiple, required)**: Indicate if you visited the restaurant with others.
* **visited frequency (select_one, required)**: Select if you visited the restaurant during this visit.
* **visit purpose (select_multiple, required)**: Select the purpose of your visit.
* **recommended to (select_multiple, required)**: Indicate if you recommended the restaurant to others.
* **reason to recommend (text, optional)**: Enter your reason for recommending or not recommending the restaurant.
* **visited previous visit (select_one, required)**: Indicate if you visited the restaurant on a previous visit.
* **visited next visit (select_one, required)**: Indicate if you visited the restaurant on the next visit.
* **visited previous visit (select_one, required)**: Select if this was a revisit to the restaurant.
* **visited before (select_one, required)**: Indicate if you visited the restaurant before.
* **visited ever (select_one, required)**: Select if you've ever visited the restaurant.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
- Enter accurate and complete information to help us improve our services.
- If you have any feedback or suggestions, please enter them in the "comments" field.
- Thank you for taking the time to share your experience with us!
