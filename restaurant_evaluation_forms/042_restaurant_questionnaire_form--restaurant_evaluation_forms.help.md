# Restaurant Questionnaire Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your visit to a restaurant, including ratings and comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply click on each page to access the corresponding section of the form. Each section contains specific fields to fill in, which are explained below.

- **General Information** (`text`, required: false): Enter any general information you'd like to share about your visit.
- **Rate Your Visit** (`number`, required: false): Provide a numerical rating of your visit, on a scale that we can use to better understand your experience.
- **About The Restaurant** (`text`, required: false): Share your thoughts about the restaurant.
- **Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your name, if you'd like to be associated with your comments.
- **Address** (`text`, required: false): Enter the address of the restaurant.
- **Phone** (`text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the restaurant.
- **Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the restaurant.
- **Website** (`text`, required: false): Enter the website of the restaurant.
- **Description** (`text`, required: false): Describe your experience at the restaurant.
- **Hours** (`text`, required: false): Enter the operating hours of the restaurant.
