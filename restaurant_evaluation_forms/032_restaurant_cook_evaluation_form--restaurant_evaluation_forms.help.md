# Restaurant Cook Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Cook Evaluation Form is a tool used to collect feedback and assessments on the performance of a restaurant cook. It's essential for restaurants to improve their food quality, service, and overall dining experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form's instructions and guidelines to understand the evaluation process.
2. Ensure you are familiar with the cook's performance during the interaction.
3. Fill out the form with your honest feedback and assessment.
4. Select the relevant options for the evaluation type, status, and other fields as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Restaurant Name** (`restaurant_name`, text, optional): Please enter the restaurant's name where you interacted with the cook.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Please enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Score** (`evaluation_score`, number, optional): Please rate the cook's performance out of 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about the cook's performance.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, note, optional): Please provide any comments or feedback about the cook's performance.
