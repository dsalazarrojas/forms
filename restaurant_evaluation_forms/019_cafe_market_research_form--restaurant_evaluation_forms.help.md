# Cafe Market Research Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather insights about your experiences and preferences regarding our cafe. Your input will help us understand what we do well and where we can improve. Please take a few minutes to answer the questions honestly, and don't hesitate to provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all questions honestly, and try to provide specific details whenever possible.
2. Select only one option for questions that ask for a single selection.
3. Select multiple options for questions that allow multiple selections (e.g., favorite menu items, marketing channels).
4. Use the "None" option when it applies to your situation.
5. If you have any additional suggestions or comments, use the "Additional Suggestions" field.
