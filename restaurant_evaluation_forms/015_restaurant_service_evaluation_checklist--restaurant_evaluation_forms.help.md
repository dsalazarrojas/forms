# Restaurant Service Evaluation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers on their dining experience at the restaurant, allowing the establishment to improve their service and quality of food and drinks.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each page carefully and answer the questions based on your recent visit to the restaurant.
2. Please provide specific examples or details when possible to help the restaurant understand your feedback.
3. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Page** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This is the title page of the form, which serves as an introduction to the rest of the form.
* **Staff Friendliness** (`2`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to ask if you found the staff friendly and welcoming during your visit. Please answer with a brief explanation of your experience.
* **Food And Beverage** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Describe the food and drinks you ordered, and how you liked or disliked them.
* **Food And Beverage Review** (`4`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to rate the quality of the food and drinks served, providing specific reasons for your rating.
* **Drink Service** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Share your experience with the drink service, including how quickly you were served and if your drinks were delivered promptly.
* **Drink** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Describe the drink you ordered and how you liked or disliked it.
* **Staff Efficiency** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Answer with "Yes" if the staff was efficient in serving you, "No" if they were not, or "Other" if you have a different experience.
* **Food Preparation Time** (`8`, `number`, required: false): Estimate the time it took for your food to be prepared, from ordering to serving.
* **Staff Attentiveness** (`9`, `text`, required: false): Share your experience with the staff's attentiveness, including if they checked in on you regularly.
* **Staffpage4** (`10`, `text`, required: false): Please note that this field is intended to capture any other comments or feedback on the staff's behavior or service.
* **Food Quality** (`11`, `text`, required: false): Describe the quality of the food you ordered, highlighting any strengths or weaknesses.
* **Food Quality Review** (`12`, `text`, required: false): Rate the quality of the food, providing specific reasons for your rating.
* **Staffpage5** (`13`, `text`, required: false): This field is intended to capture any feedback on the staff's overall behavior or service.
* **Staffpage6** (`14`, `select_one`, required: false): Answer with "Yes" if the staff was available to attend to your needs, "No" if they were not, or "Other" if you have a different experience.
* **Foodservicepage2** (`15`, `text`, required: false): Share your experience with the food service, including how well it met your expectations.
* **Staffpage7** (`16`, `text`, required: false): Please note that this field is intended to capture any other comments or feedback on the staff's service.
* **Staffpage8** (`17`, `text`, required: false): This field is intended to capture any other comments or feedback on the staff's behavior or service.
* **Foodqualitypage2** (`18`, `text`, required: false): Describe the quality of the food you ordered, including any strengths or weaknesses.
* **Foodqualityrev2** (`19`, `text`, required: false): Rate the quality of the food, providing specific reasons for your rating.
* **Staff** (`20`, `text`, required: false): This field is intended to capture any other comments or feedback on the staff's behavior or service.

Please submit your feedback and help the restaurant improve their service!
