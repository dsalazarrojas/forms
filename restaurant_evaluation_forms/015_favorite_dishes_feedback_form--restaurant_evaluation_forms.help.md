# Favorite Dishes Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Favorite Dishes Feedback Form is a tool used to collect feedback from customers about their favorite dishes at a restaurant. It helps the restaurant to understand what customers like and dislike about their menu items.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your rating of your overall experience by choosing from the rating options below.
2. If you have a favorite dish that you would like to provide feedback on, please enter its name in the "dish" field.
3. If you have ordered the dish that you are providing feedback on, enter a rating from 1 to 5 to rate the dish's quality.
4. Select the menu category of the dish you ordered, e.g. appetizer, entree, or dessert.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Rating** (`your rating`, select_one, required): Please choose how you would rate your overall experience at the restaurant from 1 to 5.
* **Dish Name** (`dish`, text, optional): Enter the name of the dish you ordered or a dish that you would recommend.
* **Dish Rating** (`dish rating`, number, optional): If you have ordered the dish, please rate its quality from 1 to 5.
* **Menu Category** (`menu category`, select_multiple, optional): Select the category that the dish you ordered belongs to, e.g. appetizer, entree, or dessert.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide specific details about your experience.
* If you have any specific feedback or suggestions, please provide them in the additional notes field.
* If you have ordered multiple dishes, please complete this form once for each dish.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
