# Restaurant Dining Experience Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Dining Experience Check In Form is designed to help the restaurant provide an exceptional experience tailored to your preferences and needs. Please take a few minutes to complete this form to ensure a smooth and enjoyable dining experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your guest preferences, including any dietary restrictions or special requests.
2. Provide your contact information, including phone number and email address.
3. Choose your check-in time and date for your visit.
4. Enter your server's name or a note for the staff to know who will be serving you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Preferences** (`guest_preferences`, select_one, required: false): Select from the options provided to indicate your preferences for the dining experience, such as vegetarian or gluten-free options.
* **Contact Information** (`guest_contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information, such as a phone number or address.
* **Phone Number** (`guest_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Email Address** (`guest_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can keep you informed of any updates or promotions.
* **Check In Time** (`guest_checkin_time`, time, required: false): Select your desired check-in time for your visit.
* **Check In Date** (`guest_checkin_date`, date, required: false): Select your desired check-in date for your visit.
* **Server/Server Note** (`guest_checkin_server`, text, required: false): Enter the server's name or a note that will help the staff know who will be serving you.

## Tips
- Please complete the form with accurate information to ensure our staff can tailor their services to your needs.
- If you have any questions or need assistance with the form, don't hesitate to ask our staff.
- Duplicate submissions will be discarded, so please ensure you only submit the form once per visit.
