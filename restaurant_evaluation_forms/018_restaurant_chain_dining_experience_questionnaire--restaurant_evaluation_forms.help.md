# Restaurant Chain Dining Experience Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the dining experience of a customer in a restaurant chain. It is used to collect data on the quality of food, service, and overall experience, as well as any recommendations or additional comments the customer may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your title in the "Restaurant Chain Dining Experience Questionnaire" field.
2. Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-4.
3. Choose your rating for the quality of service, food, and value for money.
4. Indicate if you will visit the restaurant again.
5. Provide any recommendations or additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (optional).
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Restaurant Chain Dining Experience Questionnaire** (`form_title`, text): Enter a brief title for your review.
- **Overall Experience** (`dining_experience`, number): Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-4.
  - 1: Poor
  - 2: Fair
  - 3: Good
  - 4: Excellent
- **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, select_one): Rate the quality of service you received.
  - Excellent
  - Good
  - Fair
  - Poor
- **Food Quality** (`food_quality`, select_one): Rate the quality of the food you received.
  - Excellent
  - Good
  - Fair
  - Poor
- **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, select_one): Rate the value for money you received.
  - Excellent
  - Good
  - Fair
  - Poor
- **Will Visit Again** (`will_visit_again`, select_one): Indicate if you will visit the restaurant again.
  - True
  - False
  - Undecided
- **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, note): Provide any recommendations for the restaurant.
- **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address (optional).
- **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number (optional).
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
