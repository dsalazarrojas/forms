# Restaurant Employee Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to evaluate the performance of a restaurant employee. The form captures the supervisor's rating and comments on the employee's performance, along with other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please ensure you have the required permissions and access to complete this form**.
2. To complete the form, fill out the required fields with the relevant information about the employee.
3. Click "Submit" to save your progress.
4. You can return to the form later to review and update your progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supervisor Name**: <input type="text" id="1">
	+ The name of the supervisor who is evaluating the employee.
* **Restaurant Name**: <input type="text" id="2">
	+ The name of the restaurant where the employee works.
* **Employee Name**: <input type="text" id="3">
	+ The name of the employee being evaluated.
* **Rating**: <input type="number" id="4">
	+ A numerical rating of the employee's performance.
* **Comments**: <input type="text" id="5">
	+ Any additional comments or feedback for the employee.
* **Evaluation Date**: <input type="date" id="6">
	+ The date the form was completed.
* **Evaluation Time**: <input type="time" id="7">
	+ The time the form was completed.
* **Supervisor Signature**: <input type="note" id="8">
	+ The supervisor's signature indicating completion of the form.
* **Employee Signature**: <input type="note" id="9">
	+ The employee's signature indicating acknowledgement of the form.
* **Evaluation Form Completion Status**: <input type="select_one" id="10">
	+ The status of the form completion (Completed, In Progress, Pending).
* **Evaluation Form Completion Date**: <input type="date" id="11">
	+ The date the form was completed.
* **Restaurant Evaluation Form Completion Status**: <input type="select_one" id="12">
	+ The status of the restaurant's evaluation form completion (Completed, In Progress, Pending).
* **Restaurant Evaluation Form Completion Date**: <input type="date" id="13">
	+ The date the restaurant's evaluation form was completed.
* **Restaurant Evaluation Form Completion Reason**: <input type="select_multiple" id="14">
	+ The reason for the restaurant's evaluation form completion (Completed on time, Completed on time but with minor errors, Completed with significant errors, Not yet completed).
* **Restaurant Evaluation Form Completion Notes**: <input type="text" id="15">
	+ Any additional notes for the restaurant's evaluation form completion.
* **Restaurant Evaluation Form Completion Date Awaiting Feedback**: <input type="date" id="16">
	+ The date the restaurant's evaluation form is awaiting feedback.
* **Restaurant Evaluation Form Completion Date Awaiting Approval**: <input type="date" id="17">
	+ The date the restaurant's evaluation form is awaiting approval.
* **Restaurant Evaluation Form Completion Status Awaiting Approval**: <input type="select_one" id="18">
	+ The status of the restaurant's evaluation form completion awaiting approval (Completed, Awaiting Approval).
* **Restaurant Evaluation Form Completion Date Awaiting Review**: <input type="date" id="19">
	+ The date the restaurant's evaluation form is awaiting review.
* **Restaurant Evaluation Form Completion Status Awaiting Review**: <input type="select_one" id="20">
	+ The status of the restaurant's evaluation form completion awaiting review (Completed, Awaiting Review).
* **Restaurant Evaluation Form Completion Date Awaiting Approval and Review**: <input type="date" id="21">
	+ The date the restaurant's evaluation form is awaiting approval and review.
* **Restaurant Evaluation Form Completion Status Awaiting Approval and Review**: <input type="select_one" id="22">
	+ The status of the restaurant's evaluation form completion awaiting approval and review (Completed, Awaiting Approval and Review).
* **Restaurant Evaluation Form Completion Date Awaiting Review and Approval**: <input type="date" id="23">
	+ The date the restaurant's evaluation form is awaiting review and approval.
* **Restaurant Evaluation Form Completion Status Awaiting Review and Approval**: <input type="select_one" id="24">
	+ The status of the restaurant's evaluation form completion awaiting review and approval (Completed, Awaiting Review and Approval).
* **Evaluation Form Completion Date**: <input type="date" id="25">
	+ The date the evaluation form was completed.

**Tips**

* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Review and update your progress regularly.
* You can save your progress and come back later to finish or update the form.
* If you are having trouble with the form, contact your supervisor or support team for assistance.
