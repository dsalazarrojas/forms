# Kitchen Staff Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance of kitchen staff in a restaurant. It is meant to gather feedback from supervisors on staff members' work habits, skills, and overall performance. The form is designed to be completed by supervisors to provide constructive feedback and help kitchen staff improve their work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form and understand the purpose and questions being asked.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing specific examples and details.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) in the label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supervisor Feedback** (`supervisor_feedback`, text, optional): This field is for the supervisor to provide their overall feedback on the staff member's performance. Please type your feedback in this text field.
* **Rating Score 1** (`rating_score_1`, number, optional): This field is for rating the staff member's performance on a scale from 1 to 10. Please rate their performance and provide your reasoning.
* **Rating Scale 1** (`rating_scale_1`, select_one, required): This field is for selecting a rating scale for the staff member's performance. Please choose one of the provided options:
	+ Label 1
	+ Label 2
	+ Label 3
* **Label 1** (`rating_scale_1_label_1`, text, optional): This field is for providing a description for the Label 1 option. Please type a brief description.
* **Label 2** (`rating_scale_1_label_2`, text, optional): This field is for providing a description for the Label 2 option. Please type a brief description.
* **Label 3** (`rating_scale_1_label_3`, text, optional): This field is for providing a description for the Label 3 option. Please type a brief description.
* **Rating Score 2** (`rating_score_2`, number, optional): This field is for rating the staff member's performance on another scale from 1 to 10. Please rate their performance and provide your reasoning.

## Tips

* Please be honest and constructive in your feedback.
* Use specific examples to support your ratings and feedback.
* Complete all required fields to ensure thorough evaluation.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
