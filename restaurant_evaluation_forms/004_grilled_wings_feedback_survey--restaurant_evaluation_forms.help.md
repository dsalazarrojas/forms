# Grilled Wings Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather your honest feedback on our grilled wings. Your input will help us understand what you like and dislike about our food and service, and help us make improvements to create a better dining experience for you and our other customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions that apply to you.
2. Rate each section with a number (1-5) based on your experience.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please type them in the "Additional comments" section.
4. Click "Submit" to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the survey** (Page 1):
	+ This section welcomes you to the survey and sets the tone for the rest of the form.
* **How would you rate the sauce?** (Page 2):
	+ Please rate how much you enjoyed the sauce of our grilled wings on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not good at all" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **How would you rate the flavor?** (Page 3):
	+ Rate the flavor of our grilled wings on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not good at all" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **How would you rate the presentation?** (Page 4):
	+ Rate the presentation of our grilled wings on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not good at all" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **How would you rate the service?** (Page 5):
	+ Rate the service provided by our staff on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not good at all" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **What items do you recommend we offer next?** (Page 6):
	+ Type in any new item you think we should offer in addition to our current grilled wings menu.
* **Additional comments** (Page 7):
	+ If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please type them here.

## Tips
* Please be as honest and thorough as possible with your feedback.
* We value your opinions and will use them to improve our menu and customer experience.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey!
