# restaurant_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer feedback and complaints about a visit to a restaurant. It is meant to help the establishment improve its services and provide a better experience for future customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as you would like to be contacted.
2. Enter the name of the restaurant you visited.
3. Select the date and time you visited the restaurant.
4. Choose the reason for your visit.
5. If you had contact with an employee, please enter their name.
6. Provide a brief description of your experience.
7. If you wish to be contacted, please enter your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (text): This is the name of the person submitting the complaint. Please enter your name as you would like to be contacted.
* **Restaurant Name** (text): This is the name of the restaurant where you visited. Please enter the exact name as it appears on their sign or marketing materials.
* **Date of Visit** (date): Select the date you visited the restaurant. This will help us to identify which visit this complaint is related to.
* **Time of Visit** (time): Select the time you arrived at the restaurant. This will help us to identify the timing of your visit.
* **Reason for Visit** (text): Choose the reason you visited the restaurant (e.g., dinner, lunch, special occasion).
* **Employee Name** (text): If you had contact with an employee, please enter their name. This will help us to identify who you spoke with.
* **Description** (note): Please provide a brief description of your experience at the restaurant. Be as detailed as possible, but keep it concise.
* **Email** (email): If you would like to be contacted about your complaint, please enter your email address. This will allow us to follow up with you directly.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when describing your experience.
* If you are having trouble selecting the date or time, refer to the restaurant's website or social media for their business hours.
* If you have already contacted the restaurant about this issue, please let us know and we will follow up with you directly.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help the restaurant to improve its services.
