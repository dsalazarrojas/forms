# restaurant_opening_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The restaurant_opening_evaluation_form is designed to collect information about a restaurant at the time of its opening or evaluation. This form helps restaurant owners, managers, or evaluators to provide a comprehensive view of the restaurant's offerings and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the restaurant's address in the `restaurant_address` field.
2. Enter the name of the restaurant in the `restaurant_name` field.
3. Specify the restaurant's operating hours in the `restaurant_hours` field.
4. Select the services offered by the restaurant from the `restaurant_services` field.
5. Enter the restaurant's pricing information in the `restaurant_price` field.
6. Provide the phone number of the restaurant in the `restaurant_phone` field.
7. Enter the email address of the restaurant in the `restaurant_email` field.
8. Enter the website of the restaurant in the `restaurant_website` field.
9. Rate the restaurant's quality of service in the `restaurant_rating` field.
10. Provide any additional feedback about the restaurant in the `restaurant_feedback` field.
11. Add any additional notes or comments about the restaurant in the `restaurant_notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **restaurant_address**: Enter the physical address of the restaurant.
* **restaurant_name**: Enter the name of the restaurant.
* **restaurant_hours**: Enter the restaurant's operating hours (e.g., Monday to Friday, 11am-7pm).
* **restaurant_services**: Select the services offered by the restaurant from the provided options (e.g., food, drinks, takeout).
* **restaurant_price**: Enter the average price range of the restaurant's dishes.
* **restaurant_phone**: Enter the phone number of the restaurant.
* **restaurant_email**: Enter the email address of the restaurant.
* **restaurant_website**: Enter the website of the restaurant.
* **restaurant_rating**: Rate the restaurant's quality of service on a scale of 1-5.
* **restaurant_feedback**: Provide any feedback or comments about the restaurant.
* **restaurant_notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the restaurant.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
* Use clear and concise language to describe the restaurant's services and offerings.
* If you are unsure about a particular field, refer to the provided instructions or contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
