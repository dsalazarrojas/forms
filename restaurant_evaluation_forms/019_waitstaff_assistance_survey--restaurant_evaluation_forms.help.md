# Waitstaff Assistance Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from waitstaff on their experiences with assistance provided during their shifts. It helps to ensure that waitstaff are meeting the expectations of providing friendly and responsive service, resolving issues as they occur, and following up on concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select whether you experienced waitstaff assistance during your last shift (survey_start)**: Choose 'Yes' if you received assistance, or 'No' if you didn't.
2. **Rate the waitstaff friendliness (friendly)**: Answer with a number, where 1 is 'Not Friendly' and 10 is 'Extremely Friendly'.
3. **Assess how responsive the waitstaff were (responsive)**: Rate their responsiveness using the same scale as in step 2.
4. **Report if any issues were resolved (issue_resolution)**: Select 'Yes' if an issue was resolved, or 'No' if not.
5. **Describe any issues that occurred (issue_desc)**: Provide a brief description of any issues that arose during your shift.
6. **Date and time the issue occurred (issue_date)**: Enter the date and time when the issue occurred.
7. **Report the time of the issue resolution (issue_time)**: Enter the time when the issue was resolved, if applicable.
8. **Choose how you would like to be followed up on (follow_up)**: Select 'Option 1', 'Option 2', or 'Option 3' to describe your preferred follow-up method.
9. **Provide details on follow-up (follow_up_details)**: If you selected a follow-up method, describe the details in this field.
10. **Select how you contacted waitstaff to address concerns (contact_method)**: Choose 'Option 1', 'Option 2', or 'Option 3' to describe the method you used to contact waitstaff.
11. **Describe how you contacted waitstaff (contact_method_details)**: If you selected a contact method, provide more details on how you contacted them.
12. **Enter your name (contact_name)**: This is required to ensure that we can follow up with you.
13. **End of Survey (survey_end)**: Confirm whether this is the end of your survey or if further issues arose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **waitstaff_survey_form**: This field is not a data entry field and serves as the beginning of the form where you indicate whether you received assistance or not.
* **friendly**: Rate how friendly you found the waitstaff to be, from 1 (Not Friendly) to 10 (Extremely Friendly).
* **responsive**: Rate the waitstaff responsiveness on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **issue_resolution**: Report if an issue was resolved or not.
* **issue_desc**: Describe any issues that occurred during your shift.
* **issue_date**: Enter the date when the issue occurred.
* **issue_time**: Report the time when the issue occurred, if applicable.
* **follow_up**: Choose your preferred method for further follow-up.
* **follow_up_details**: Describe the details of your preferred follow-up method.
* **contact_method**: Select the method used to contact waitstaff to address concerns.
* **contact_method_details**: Describe how you contacted waitstaff if you selected a contact method.
* **contact_name**: Enter your name for follow-up purposes.
* **waitstaff_assistance_survey**: This field is not a data entry field, serving as a divider for the survey end confirmation.
* **survey_end**: Confirm whether you've completed the survey. If not, you will be prompted to continue filling it out.

Note: This guide assumes that users are familiar with standard form conventions, such as selecting radio buttons and entering text fields. If a field is optional, it is not explicitly marked as so; instead, the user's understanding of its requirement is based on the form's structure and purpose.
