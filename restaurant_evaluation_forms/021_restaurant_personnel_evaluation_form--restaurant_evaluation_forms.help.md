# Restaurant Personnel Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Personnel Evaluation Form is a tool used by restaurant management to assess the performance of restaurant personnel. It's used to evaluate staff skills, attendance, and work ethic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the restaurant personnel you're evaluating and fill in their name in the "manager_name" field.
2. Choose the employee's job title in the "job_title" field.
3. Enter the date and time of the last evaluation in the "date_last_evaluation" and "time_last_evaluation" fields, respectively.
4. Rate the employee's overall performance on a scale of 1-10 in the "overall_rating" field.
5. Rate the employee's performance in the following areas:
	* Performance: "performance_rating"
	* Communication: "communication_rating"
	* Attendance: "attendance_rating"
	* Punctuality: "punctuality_rating"
	* Teamwork: "teamwork_rating"
	* Leadership: "leadership_rating"
6. Enter any overall comments about the employee in the "overall_comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Restaurant Personnel Evaluation Form**: This is a label to identify the form.
* **manager_name** (`Text`, required): This is the name of the manager evaluating the staff.
* **employee_name** (`Text`, required): This is the name of the staff member being evaluated.
* **job_title** (`Text`, required): This is the job title of the staff member being evaluated.
* **date_last_evaluation** (`Date`, required): This is the date of the last evaluation.
* **time_last_evaluation** (`Time`, required): This is the time of the last evaluation.
* **overall_rating** (`Number`, required): This is the overall performance rating on a scale of 1-10.
* **performance_rating** (`Number`, required): This is the performance rating on a scale of 1-10.
* **communication_rating** (`Number`, required): This is the communication rating on a scale of 1-10.
* **attendance_rating** (`Number`, required): This is the attendance rating on a scale of 1-10.
* **punctuality_rating** (`Number`, required): This is the punctuality rating on a scale of 1-10.
* **teamwork_rating** (`Number`, required): This is the teamwork rating on a scale of 1-10.
* **leadership_rating** (`Number`, required): This is the leadership rating on a scale of 1-10.
* **overall_comments** (`Note`, required): This is a space to enter any overall comments about the staff member.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Rate performance on a scale of 1-10.
* Enter comments in the "overall_comments" field.
* Review and edit your answers before submitting the form.
