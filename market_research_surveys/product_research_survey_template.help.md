# product_research_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather user opinions and feedback on products, aiding in market research and product development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and respond to each question honestly.
2. Answer questions with the options provided or add any additional comments if needed.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select all applicable options.
4. Provide thoughtful and concrete feedback in text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How satisfied are you with our product overall?** (`Very satisfied`, `Somewhat satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`): This question asks for your overall satisfaction level with the product.
* **Which aspects most influenced your opinion of the product? (select all that apply)** (`Design`, `Price`, `Performance`, `Features`, `Support`, `Ease of use`, `Quality`): Choose all relevant factors that have impacted your opinion on the product.
* **How likely are you to recommend this product to others?** (`Very likely`, `Likely`, `Neutral`, `Unlikely`, `Very unlikely`): Indicate how likely you would recommend the product to others.
* **What is the primary reason you chose our product?** (`Features`, `Price`, `Brand reputation`, `Recommendation`, `Ease of use`, `Other`): Select the main reason you chose the product.
* **What would you most improve or change about the product?** (text): Share your thoughts on areas for improvement or changes you would suggest.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback regarding the product?** (text): Provide any additional thoughts or suggestions on the product.

## Tips
- Answer questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
- Select all applicable options for multiple-choice questions.
- Provide concrete and specific feedback in text fields.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
