# Nonviolent Communication Training Fund Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nonviolent Communication Training Fund Donation Form is designed to collect donations for the Nonviolent Communication (NVC) Training program. This form helps us understand your willingness to support the program financially, allowing us to provide more people with the knowledge and skills of NVC.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your details as a donor in the "Donor Details" section.
2.  Enter your payment information in the "Payment Information" section.
3.  Choose how you want to make the payment in the "Pay With" section.
4.  Enter the amount you want to donate in the "Donation Amount" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Details** (donator_details):
	+ This is where you provide your name, contact information, and any other details you'd like to share about yourself as a donor.
	+ Please fill in your name, email address, and any other relevant details.
* **Payment Information** (payment_info):
	+ This section is where you'll provide your payment information to process the donation.
	+ Please enter your credit card number, expiration date, and any other required payment information.
* **Pay With** (payment_method):
	+ This is where you choose how you'd like to pay for your donation (e.g., Card or Bank Transfer).
	+ Select the payment method that best suits your needs.
* **Donation Amount** (donation_amount):
	+ Here, you'll enter the amount you'd like to donate to the NVC Training program.
	+ Please use a numerical value (e.g., 10, 20, 50, or any other amount you choose). 

## Tips
- Make sure to review your details and payment information carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
- We appreciate your support for the NVC Training program and look forward to providing you with valuable resources and knowledge!
