**Exotic Fruit Subscription Signup - Help Guide**
=====================================================

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who wish to sign up for an exotic fruit subscription service. This information will be used to set up a regular delivery of exotic fruits at the user's preferred frequency and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
---------------------------

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select a payment method**: Choose from the options of Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
2. Fill out the **delivery address** field: Enter your address where you would like to receive the exotic fruits.
3. **Choose your delivery frequency**: Select from the options of Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly.
4. **Choose your payment frequency**: Select from the options of One Time Payment or Recurring Payment.
5. **Set the delivery date**: Enter the date you would like to start receiving the exotic fruits.
6. **Set the delivery time**: Choose the time of day you would like to receive your delivery.
7. **Add a delivery note** (optional): Leave a message or note for our delivery staff to include with your exotic fruit delivery.
8. **Choose the number of exotic fruits per box** (optional): Enter the quantity of fruits you would like to receive per delivery.
9. **Enter your delivery zip code**: Enter the zip code of the address where you would like to receive your exotic fruits.
10. **Enter your email address**: Enter your email address for our delivery team to contact you.
11. **Enter your phone number**: Enter your phone number for our delivery team to contact you.
12. **Add any additional notes** (optional): Leave any additional comments or requests for our delivery team to consider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
---------------------------

* **Signup form**: This is the main form where you can input your general information.
* **payment_method**: Choose from the options of Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer. This will affect the method used to process your payment for the exotic fruit subscription.
* **delivery_address**: Enter your address where you would like to receive the exotic fruits.
* **delivery_frequency**: Choose from the options of Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly. This will affect the frequency of your exotic fruit deliveries.
* **payment_frequency**: Choose from the options of One Time Payment or Recurring Payment. This will affect the payment method for your subscription.
* **delivery_day**: Enter the date you would like to start receiving the exotic fruits.
* **delivery_time**: Choose the time of day you would like to receive your delivery.
* **delivery_note**: Leave a message or note for our delivery staff to include with your exotic fruit delivery.
* **fruit_box**: Enter the quantity of exotic fruits you would like to receive per delivery.
* **delivery_zip**: Enter the zip code of the address where you would like to receive your exotic fruits.
* **email**: Enter your email address for our delivery team to contact you.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number for our delivery team to contact you.
* **notes**: Leave any additional comments or requests for our delivery team to consider.

**Tips**

* Choose a payment method that is convenient for you.
* Select the delivery frequency that best suits your needs.
