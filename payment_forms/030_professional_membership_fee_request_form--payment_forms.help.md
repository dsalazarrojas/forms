# Professional Membership Fee Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Professional Membership Fee Request Form is used by organizations to request a professional membership fee from the organization. This form is designed to collect information about the organization and their request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form submission page with your organization's details.
2. Enter your organization's name and address on the corresponding fields.
3. Select the type of fee you are requesting (e.g., Annual, Quarterly, Monthly).
4. Enter the amount of the fee.
5. Provide a brief description of your request.
6. Enter your contact email and phone number.
7. Enter the date of the form submission.
8. Optional: provide any additional notes or information about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Submission**: Enter a brief title for your request.
* **Organization Name**: Enter your organization's full name.
* **Organization Address**: Enter your organization's address in the format you prefer.
* **Fee Type**: Select the type of fee you are requesting (e.g., Annual, Quarterly, Monthly).
* **Amount**: Enter the amount of the fee you are requesting.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of your request.
* **Requested By**: Enter the name of the person submitting the request (usually the organization's representative).
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the request.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the request.
* **Form Submission Date**: Enter the date the form was submitted (usually automatically populated).
* **Time**: Enter the time the form was submitted (usually automatically populated).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information or comments about your request.
