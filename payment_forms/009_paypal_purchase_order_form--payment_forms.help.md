# PayPal Purchase Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information for a PayPal purchase order. It includes fields for the customer's personal details, such as name, address, and email, as well as information about the purchase, including total items, unit price, and shipping cost.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Fill in your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Fill in your address in the "Address" field.
5. Fill in your city in the "City" field.
6. Select your state from the "State" field.
7. Fill in your zip code in the "Zip" field.
8. Select your country from the "Country" field.
9. Fill in the total number of items you are purchasing in the "Total items" field.
10. Fill in the unit price of each item in the "Unit price" field.
11. Fill in the shipping cost in the "Shipping cost" field.
12. Select your payment method from the "Payment method" field.
13. Fill in any additional payment method options in the "Payment method options" field.
14. Fill in your PayPal email in the "PayPal email" field.
15. Fill in your PayPal password in the "PayPal password" field.
16. Fill in your PayPal token in the "PayPal token" field.
17. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm email" field.
18. Confirm your password in the "Confirm password" field.
19. Confirm your PayPal email in the "Confirm PayPal email" field.
20. Confirm your PayPal password in the "Confirm PayPal password" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Fill in your first name.
* **Last name**: Fill in your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State**: Select your state of residence.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Country**: Select your country of residence.
* **Total items**: Enter the total number of items you are purchasing.
* **Unit price**: Enter the price of each item.
* **Shipping cost**: Enter the shipping cost for the purchase.
* **Payment method**: Select your preferred payment method.
* **Payment method options**: Enter any additional payment method options.
* **PayPal email**: Enter your PayPal email address.
* **PayPal password**: Enter your PayPal password.
* **PayPal token**: Enter your PayPal token.
* **Confirm email**: Confirm your email address.
* **Confirm password**: Confirm your password.
* **Confirm PayPal email**: Confirm your PayPal email address.
* **Confirm PayPal password**: Confirm your PayPal password.
* **Confirm total**: Confirm the total number of items.
* **Confirm ship cost**: Confirm the shipping cost.
* **Confirm payment method**: Confirm your payment method.
* **Confirm payment method options**: Confirm any additional payment method options.
