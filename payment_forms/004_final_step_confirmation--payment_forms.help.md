# Final Step Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The final step confirmation form is used to verify the user's details before proceeding with the payment process. This form ensures that the user's identity and payment details are accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please ensure that this is the same email address you used for your account or payment method.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select a date in the "Date" field.
6. Select a time in the "Time" field.
7. Confirm the date and time you entered in the "Confirm Date" and "Confirm Time" fields. Ensure that these match the values entered in steps 5 and 6.
8. Confirm your phone number in the "Confirm Phone Number" field. Ensure that this matches the value entered in step 4.
9. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm Email" field. Ensure that this matches the value entered in step 3.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, required: false): Enter your first name, as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required: false): Enter your last name, as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email** (Email, email, required: false): Enter the email address associated with your account or payment method.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required: false): Enter your phone number as it appears on your government-issued ID or payment method.
* **Date** (Date, date, required: false): Select the date you would like to make the payment on.
* **Time** (Time, time, required: false): Select the time you would like to make the payment on.
* **Confirm Date** (Confirm Date, date, required: false): Confirm the date you selected in the "Date" field.
* **Confirm Time** (Confirm Time, time, required: false): Confirm the time you selected in the "Time" field.
* **Confirm Phone Number** (Confirm Phone Number, text, required: false): Confirm your phone number.
* **Confirm Email** (Confirm Email, email, required: false): Confirm your email address.

## Tips
* Double-check the fields to ensure that they match your identity and payment details.
* Ensure that the "Confirm" fields match the corresponding fields in this form.
* If you encounter any issues or doubts, contact our support team for assistance.
