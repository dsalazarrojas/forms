# Product Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Pre Order Form is a tool for customers to submit their pre-order requests and provide shipping and payment details for products they are interested in purchasing before they are available for actual purchase.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the pre-order request process.
2. Select the shipping method you prefer from the options provided.
3. Enter the shipping rate (if applicable).
4. Select a shipping date and time (if applicable).
5. Enter your customer email address.
6. Select the payment method you prefer from the options provided.
7. Enter any additional order details in the "Order details" field.
8. Enter the name of the product you wish to pre-order.
9. Enter the quantity of the product you wish to pre-order.
10. Enter the price of the product you wish to pre-order.
11. Calculate the total amount by multiplying the price by the quantity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Shipping method** (shipping_method, select_multiple, required: false): Select the shipping method you prefer from the options provided.
- **Shipping rate** (shipping_rate, number, required: false): Enter the shipping rate (if applicable).
- **Shipping date** (shipping_date, date, required: false): Select the shipping date you prefer (if applicable).
- **Shipping time** (shipping_time, time, required: false): Select the shipping time you prefer (if applicable).
- **Customer email** (customer_email, email, required: false): Enter your customer email address.
- **Payment method** (payment_method, select_one, required: false): Select the payment method you prefer from the options provided.
- **Order details** (order_details, note, required: false): Enter any additional order details.
- **Product name** (product_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the product you wish to pre-order.
- **Quantity** (quantity, number, required: false): Enter the quantity of the product you wish to pre-order.
- **Price** (price, number, required: false): Enter the price of the product you wish to pre-order.
- **Total** (total, number, required: false): Calculate the total amount by multiplying the price by the quantity.
