# Fee Payment Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather fee payment information from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select a payment method from the options provided.
2.  Enter the date you made the payment.
3.  Enter the fee amount you paid.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method you used to make the payment. You may select from the following options: Cash, Cheque, Bank Transfer, Online Payment, or Others.
*   **Fee Date**: Enter the date you made the payment. The format should be the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
*   **Fee Amount**: Enter the amount you paid. This should be a numerical value. Ensure it matches the amount you paid.

## Tips
*   If you have any issues or concerns about the payment process, please contact your administrator for assistance.
*   Ensure the date and amount fields are accurate and consistent with your records.
