# Custom Sticker Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Custom Sticker Order Form is designed to allow customers to create custom sticker orders by specifying their design preferences, quantity, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select the page with the label "custom_sticker_order_form_page_1" from the provided pages.
2. Fill out the form fields as described in the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Custom Sticker Order Form Page 1**: This is the first page of the form where you can select your design preferences.
- **Design Preferences**: Select one or more options that best describe your design preferences. You can choose from the following options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
- **Quantity**: Enter the number of stickers you would like to order. You can choose any whole number.
- **Sticker Size**: Choose the size of the sticker you would like to have, you can select from Small, Medium, or Large.
- **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it should appear on the stickers.
- **Email**: Enter your email address to contact you about the order.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number to contact you about the order.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to avoid delays in the order processing.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at your email or phone number provided above.
- If you would like to add more pages to your order, please let us know and we will guide you through the process.
