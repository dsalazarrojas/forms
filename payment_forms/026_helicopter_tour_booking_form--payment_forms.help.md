# helicopter_tour_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The helicopter_tour_booking_form is designed to collect user information for a helicopter tour booking system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **User Details**:
	* Provide your name and any other relevant user information.
2. Enter your **Tour Details**:
	* Provide the tour information, including any relevant details.
3. Fill out your **Payment Details**:
	* Enter your payment information for the tour.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details** (`id: 1`, `label: User Details`):
	+ This page is used to collect information about the user.
	+ Required: No
* **Tour Details** (`id: 2`, `label: Tour Details`):
	+ This page is used to collect information about the tour being booked.
	+ Required: No
* **Payment Details** (`id: 3`, `label: Payment Details`):
	+ This page is used to collect payment information for the tour.
	+ Required: No
