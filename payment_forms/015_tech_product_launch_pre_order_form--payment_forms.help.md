# Tech Product Launch Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers pre-order a new tech product launch. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide your product preferences, shipping details, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page of the form.
2. Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
3. Click "Submit" to send your pre-order information to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Pre Order Info
* **Product Preference** (`tech_product_launch_pre_order_form_page_1`, text, required): Please select the tech product you are interested in pre-ordering. This will help us understand your preferences and provide you with updates on the product's launch status.
* **Other Product Preferences** (`tech_product_launch_pre_order_form_page_1`, text, optional): If you have any other products in mind, please let us know in the text field below.

### Page 2: Shipping Info
* **Shipping Address** (`tech_product_launch_pre_order_form_page_2`, text, required): Enter your shipping address to ensure timely and correct delivery of your pre-ordered product.
* **Shipping Method** (`tech_product_launch_pre_order_form_page_2`, text, required): Select the shipping method you prefer for your order (e.g., standard shipping, express shipping, etc.).

### Page 3: Payment Info
* **Payment Method** (`tech_product_launch_pre_order_form_page_3`, text, required): Choose the payment method you prefer for this pre-order (e.g., credit card, PayPal, bank transfer, etc.).
