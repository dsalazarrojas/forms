# Sneaker Limited Edition Pre Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for customers to pre-order a limited edition sneaker. It collects customer information for order processing and shipping purposes. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure a smooth and efficient ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information, marked as "required" on the form.
2. Make sure to provide a valid email address and phone number for contact and shipping purposes.
3. For payment method, select the method you prefer from the available options.
4. Enter your shipping and billing addresses, including postal code (ZIP code), city, state, and country.
5. Fill out any additional order notes or shipping notes if required.
6. Review your order carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)**: Please enter your first name as it should be displayed on your order.
* **Last Name (2)**: Please enter your last name as it should be displayed on your order.
* **Email (3)**: A valid email address is required for contact and order confirmation purposes.
* **Phone (4)**: A valid phone number is required for contact and shipping purposes.
* **Delivery Address (5)**: Enter your delivery address, including the postal code (ZIP code).
* **ZIP/Postal Code (6)**: Enter the postal code for your delivery address.
* **Quantity (7)**: Please enter the quantity of limited edition sneakers you wish to order.
* **Order Notes (8)**: Enter any additional notes about your order if required.
* **Payment Method (9)**: Select the payment method you prefer from the available options.
* **Payment Method Details (10)**: Enter any additional payment details if required.
* **Card Number (11)**: If payment method is credit card, enter the card number.
* **Expiration Date (12)**: If payment method is credit card, enter the expiration date.
* **CVV/CVV (13)**: If payment method is credit card, enter the CVV.
* **Shipping Address (14)**: Enter your shipping address, including the postal code (ZIP code).
* **Shipping Postal Code (15)**: Enter the postal code for your shipping address.
* **City (16)**: Enter the city for your shipping address.
* **State (17)**: Enter the state for your shipping address.
* **Country (18)**: Enter the country for your shipping address.
* **Shipping Phone (19)**: A valid phone number is required for shipping purposes.
* **Shipping Email (20)**: A valid email address is required for shipping purposes.
* **Shipping Notes (21)**: Enter any additional shipping notes if required.
* **Billing Address (22)**: Enter your billing address, including the postal code (ZIP code).
* **Billing Postal Code (23)**: Enter the postal code for your billing address.
* **Billing City (24)**: Enter the city for your billing address.
* **Billing State (25)**: Enter the state for your billing address.
