# Virtual Workout Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Workout Payment Form is used to collect payment information and workout preferences from clients and coaches for virtual workout sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Payment Information** page with the relevant details.
	* Enter your **Amount** to pay for the virtual workout session.
	* Select your preferred **Payment Method** (Bank Transfer, PayPal, or Stripe).
2. Provide your **Client Information**:
	* Enter your **Client Name** for identification purposes.
	* Enter your **Client Email** for contact and payment verification.
	* Enter your **Client Phone** for communication purposes.
3. Enter **Coach Information**:
	* Enter your **Coach Name** for identification purposes.
	* Enter your **Coach Email** for contact and payment verification.
4. Schedule your virtual workout session:
	* Choose your preferred **Workout Type** (Yes or No).
	* Select the **Coaching Date** for your virtual workout session.
	* Choose the **Coaching Time** for your virtual workout session.
5. Review and update **Session Notes** for your virtual workout session.
6. Add any **Additional Notes** as needed.
7. Review and confirm your payment and session details before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Information (form_info)**:
	+ This section collects payment details for the virtual workout session.
* **Amount (payment_amount)**:
	+ Enter the amount to pay for the virtual workout session.
* **Payment Method (payment_method)**:
	+ Select your preferred payment method (Bank Transfer, PayPal, or Stripe).
* **Client Name (client_name)**:
	+ Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Client Email (client_email)**:
	+ Enter your email for contact and payment verification.
* **Client Phone (client_phone)**:
	+ Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Coach Name (coach_name)**:
	+ Enter the name of your assigned coach.
* **Coach Email (coach_email)**:
	+ Enter the coach's email for contact and payment verification.
* **Coaching Date (coaching_date)**:
	+ Choose the date for your virtual workout session.
* **Coaching Time (coaching_time)**:
	+ Choose the time for your virtual workout session.
* **Workout Type (workout_type)**:
	+ Choose whether you want to do a workout (Yes or No).
* **Session Length (session_length)**:
	+ Choose whether you want a short or long session (Yes or No).
* **Session Notes (session_notes)**:
	+ Enter any additional notes for your virtual workout session.
* **Payment Terms (payment_terms)**:
	+ Enter any special payment terms or conditions.
* **Additional Notes (notes)**:
	+ Enter any additional information about your virtual workout session.

## Tips

* Make sure to review your payment and session details carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any special requirements or requests, please enter them in the **Additional Notes** field.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact the designated support team.
