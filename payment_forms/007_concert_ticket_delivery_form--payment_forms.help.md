# Concert Ticket Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide details for concert ticket delivery. Please fill out the form below to ensure that your tickets are delivered correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out all the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
- For the "Delivery option" field, choose one of the three options provided: Email PDF, Physical mail, or Venue pickup.
- If you've chosen "Email PDF" for delivery, provide your delivery email address in the "Delivery email" field.
- If you've chosen "Physical mail" for delivery, provide your delivery address in the "Delivery address" field.
- If you've chosen "Venue pickup" for delivery, no additional fields are required.
- If you have any special handling instructions (e.g., fragile items, signature required), fill them out in the "Special handling instructions" field.
- For "Contact phone" field, provide a phone number where our courier or pick-up person can reach you.
- Fill out the "Order ID" field with the provided identifier.
- If you have any additional delivery instructions, please provide them in the "Additional delivery notes" field.
- Please read the "Delivery confirmation note" carefully for expected delivery window.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recipient name**: Enter your name receiving the ticket. This is a required field.
* **Delivery option**: Choose how you would like to receive your concert ticket. This field is required and has three options: Email PDF, Physical mail, and Venue pickup.
* **Delivery email**: If you've chosen "Email PDF", enter your email address where the ticket will be delivered. This field is not required but necessary for "Email PDF" choice.
* **Delivery address**: If you've chosen "Physical mail", enter the address where the ticket will be delivered. This field is not required but necessary for "Physical mail" choice.
* **Preferred delivery date**: Choose your desired delivery date for the ticket. This field is not required.
* **Special handling instructions**: Enter any special instructions for handling the ticket, e.g., fragile items, signature required. This field is not required.
* **Contact phone**: Enter a phone number where our courier or pickup person can reach you. This field is not required.
* **Order ID**: Enter the provided order identifier. This field is required.
* **Additional delivery notes**: Enter any additional instructions for delivery. This field is not required.
* **Delivery confirmation note**: Read this field carefully for expected delivery window. This field is not required.

Please fill out this form carefully to ensure that your concert tickets are delivered correctly. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
