# Prescription Reimbursement Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prescription Reimbursement Claim Form is designed to help patients submit claims for reimbursement for prescription medication costs. This form gathers essential information from the patient to process their claims efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Patient Information** page with your name, address, and contact details.
2. Enter your **Insurance Coverage** information, including your insurance provider and policy number.
3. Provide your **Medical Information** on the **Medical Information** page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: Enter your personal details, including your name, address, and phone number. This information is required to contact you for follow-up or to process your claim. 
* **Insurance Coverage**: Enter your insurance provider's name and your policy number. This information is required to verify your coverage and process your claim.
* **Medical Information**: Enter your medical information, including any relevant medical notes or prescriptions. This information is required to ensure your claim is accurately processed. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your claim is processed efficiently.
* Double-check your information for accuracy to avoid delays or claim denial.
