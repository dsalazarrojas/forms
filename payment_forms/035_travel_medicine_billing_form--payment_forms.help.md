# travel_medicine_billing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect travel-related medical billing and payment details from patients who have visited a medical service provider while traveling.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please scroll through all the pages and fill out all the required fields.
2. Make sure to complete the following required fields:
	* Payment Status (id: 18)
	* Medical Insurance (id: 8)
	* Billing Provider (id: 9)
	* Payment Terms Agreement (id: 23)
	* Patient Acknowledgement (id: 24)
	* Provider Signature (id: 22)
3. For the payment method section, select the applicable payment method from the options provided.
4. Fill out the payment amount if applicable.
5. In the service details section, provide any relevant medical service information.
6. In the contact information section, enter your contact details.
7. Finally, sign and date the form as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (id: 1)**: This field is to be completed with your personal details.
* **Billing Address (id: 2)**: This field is to be completed with your billing address.
* **Payment Details (id: 3)**: This field is to be completed with your payment details.
* **Service Details (id: 4)**: This field is to be completed with any relevant medical service information.
* **Payment Method (id: 5)**: Select the applicable payment method from the options provided.
* **Payment Amount (id: 6)**: This field is to be completed with your payment amount if applicable.
* **Payment Frequency (id: 7)**: Select whether you want to pay on a regular basis or not.
* **Medical Insurance (id: 8)**: Select whether you have medical insurance or not.
* **Billing Provider (id: 9)**: Select whether you are the billing provider or not.
* **Contact Information (id: 10)**: This field is to be completed with your contact details.
* **Signature (id: 11)**: This field is for your signature.
* **Date (id: 12)**: This field is for the date of the payment.
* **Time (id: 13)**: This field is for the time of the payment.
* **Travel Destination (id: 14)**: Select whether the medical service was provided while traveling or not.
* **Travel Type (id: 15)**: Select the type of travel.
* **Medical Service (id: 16)**: Select the medical service provided.
* **Medical Condition (id: 17)**: Select any relevant medical conditions.
* **Payment Status (id: 18)**: Select the status of your payment.
* **Payment Terms (id: 19)**: Select whether you agree with the payment terms.
* **Patient Signature (id: 20)**: This field is for your signature as a patient.
* **Billing Note (id: 21)**: This field is for any billing notes.
* **Provider Signature (id: 22)**: This field is for the signature of the medical provider.
* **Payment Terms Agreement (id: 23)**: Select whether you agree with the payment terms.
* **Patient Acknowledgement (id: 24)**: Select whether you acknowledge the payment terms.
* **Billing Contact (id: 25)**: This field is to be completed with the contact details of the billing provider.

Note: This form is to be completed in accordance with the medical service provider's guidelines and policies.
