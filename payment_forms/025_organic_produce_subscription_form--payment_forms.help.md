# organic_produce_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers to set up their organic produce subscription.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your name and phone number.
2. Choose your preferred delivery frequency (e.g., Yes or No).
3. Select the length of your subscription (e.g., Yes or No).
4. Choose your preferred payment method (e.g., Yes or No).
5. Enter your start and end dates for your subscription (optional).
6. Enter any additional customer information (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user_input** (text, optional): This field is for you to enter your name.
* **delivery_frequency** (select_multiple, optional): Choose how often you want your organic produce delivered: Yes or No.
* **delivery_address** (text, optional): Enter your delivery address for our team to drop off your produce.
* **subscription_length** (select_one, optional): Select the length of your subscription: Yes or No.
* **payment_method** (select_one, optional): Choose how you want to pay for your subscription: Yes or No.
* **start_date** (date, optional): Enter the start date for your subscription.
* **end_date** (date, optional): Enter the end date for your subscription.
* **customer_info** (text, optional): Enter any additional customer information you'd like to share with us.
* **customer_phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for our team to contact you.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure smooth delivery and subscription process.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Our team will be in touch with you shortly after receiving your form submission.
