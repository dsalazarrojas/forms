# Custom Accessories Subscription Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for custom accessories subscription services. It includes fields for customer information, shipping details, billing information, subscription plan, delivery frequency, payment method, and other relevant details. This form helps us understand your subscription preferences and process your requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your **Customer Information**, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. Enter your **Shipping Information**, including your address and any other shipping details.
3. Enter your **Billing Information**, including your payment method and billing cycle details.
4. Select your preferred **Subscription Plan**, which can be Month-to-Month, Yearly, or Quarterly.
5. Choose your **Delivery Frequency**, which can be Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or One-Time.
6. Select your preferred **Payment Method**, which can be Card, Bank Transfer, or Other.
7. Enter your **Email** address.
8. Enter your **Phone** number.
9. If required, enter a **Date** for your subscription to start or delivery.
10. If required, enter a **Time** for your delivery.
11. Enter a **Note** (optional) if you have any special requests or instructions.
12. If required, enter a **Delivery Date** for your subscription.
13. If required, enter a **Delivery Time** for your delivery.
14. Enter your preferred **Delivery Frequency** in text format (optional).
15. Enter your preferred **Billing Cycle** in text format (optional).
16. If required, enter the start and end dates for your **Billing Cycle**.
17. If required, enter the length of your **Billing Cycle**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant details.
- **Shipping Information** (`shipping_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your shipping address and any other shipping details.
- **Billing Information** (`billing_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your billing address and payment details.
- **Subscription Plan** (`subscription_plan`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select your preferred subscription plan, which can be Month-to-Month, Yearly, or Quarterly.
- **Delivery Frequency** (`delivery_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your preferred delivery frequency, which can be Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or One-Time.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your preferred payment method, which can be Card, Bank Transfer, or Other.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
- **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): If required, enter a date for your subscription to start or delivery.
- **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): If required, enter a time for your delivery.
- **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Enter a note with special requests or instructions.
- **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required: false): If required, enter a delivery date for your subscription.
- **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`, required: false): If required, enter a delivery time for your delivery.
- **Delivery Frequency** (`delivery_frequency_text`, `text`, required: false): Enter your preferred delivery frequency in text format (optional).
- **Billing Cycle** (`billing_cycle`, `text`, required: false): Enter your preferred billing cycle in text format (optional).
- **Billing Cycle Start** (`billing_cycle_start`, `date`, required: false): If required, enter the start date for your billing cycle.
- **Billing Cycle End** (`billing_cycle_end`, `date`, required: false): If required, enter the end date for your billing cycle.
- **Billing Cycle Length** (`billing_cycle_length`, `number`, required: false): If required, enter the length of your billing cycle.
