# Renovation Invoice Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for submitting invoices related to renovation projects. It captures essential details such as invoice date, amount, contractor information, and payment status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the correct project**: Ensure you're submitting the invoice for the correct project.
2. **Enter the invoice date**: This is the date you're submitting the invoice.
3. **Enter the invoice amount**: The total amount invoiced for the project.
4. **Enter contractor details**: Provide the contractor's name, address, phone number, and email address.
5. **Select payment status**: Choose the status of the payment (Paid or Pending).
6. **Select payment method**: Choose how the payment was made (if applicable).
7. **Provide additional notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes for the project.
