# Travel Reimbursement Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for travel expense reporting and reimbursement. It collects information about travel expenses, including travel dates, destinations, reimbursement details, and payment information. This form helps managers or administrators track and process travel expenses for the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your travel details.
2. Enter the date of your trip.
3. Specify the destination of your trip.
4. Provide the purpose of your travel.
5. Enter the total travel cost.
6. Select the payment status of your travel expenses.
7. Choose the method of payment for your reimbursement.
8. Enter any additional comments or information about your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Expenses Form**: This field is the main section of the form where you'll enter all your travel details.
* **Date Of Travel**: Enter the date of your trip in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Travel Destination**: Enter the city or location where you traveled.
* **Purpose Of Travel**: Describe the reason or purpose of your trip.
* **Travel Cost**: Enter the total cost of your trip.
* **Reimbursement Amount**: Enter the amount you're requesting for reimbursement.
* **Payment Status**:
	+ Paid: The payment has been made.
	+ Unpaid: The payment has not been made.
	+ Partially Paid: The payment has been partially made.
* **Payment Method**:
	+ Cash: Reimbursement was made in cash.
	+ Check: Reimbursement was made by check.
	+ Wire Transfer: Reimbursement was made through wire transfer.
	+ Credit Card: Reimbursement was made by credit card.
* **Submitter**: Enter your name and any additional contact information.
* **Approver**: Enter the name and contact information of the person who approved this reimbursement.
* **Payment Date**: Enter the date of the payment.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the total amount paid for this reimbursement.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the form is complete and accurate before submission.
* Attach any supporting documents, such as receipts or invoices, to your submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the administrator.
* This form is for travel expenses reporting and reimbursement only.
