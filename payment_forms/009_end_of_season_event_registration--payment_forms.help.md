# End Of Season Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "End Of Season Event Registration" form is designed to collect information from participants about their event registration status, payment details, and other relevant event specifics. This form helps event administrators or organizers track participant involvement and manage event-related tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your event details, including the event date and time.
2. Provide your participant details, such as your name and contact information.
3. Choose your payment status and method, if applicable.
4. Add any additional notes about your participation.
5. Review your information and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (Event Details, <code>text</code>, required: false): Enter a brief description of the event you are registering for.
* **Participant Details** (Participant Details, <code>text</code>, required: false): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Payment Details** (Payment Details, <code>text</code>, required: false): Enter any payment information related to your event participation, if applicable.
* **Participant Payment Terms** (Participant Payment Terms, <code>select_one</code>, required: false): Choose whether you have agreed to pay for event participation or not.
* **Participant Payment Method** (Participant Payment Method, <code>select_one</code>, required: false): Choose your preferred payment method, if you have agreed to pay.
* **Participant Contact** (Participant Contact, <code>text</code>, required: false): Enter your preferred contact method, such as email or phone number.
* **Participant Address** (Participant Address, <code>text</code>, required: false): Enter your mailing address, if applicable.
* **Notes** (Notes, <code>note</code>, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about your participation.
* **Event Date** (Event Date, <code>date</code>, required: false): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (Event Time, <code>time</code>, required: false): Enter the time of the event.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact the event administrators or organizers for assistance.
