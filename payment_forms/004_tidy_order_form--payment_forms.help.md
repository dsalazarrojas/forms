# Tidy Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tidy Order Form is designed to collect important details about a customer's order, including order date, payment method, and billing/shipping information. This form helps us understand the customer's needs and preferences to process their order efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields as instructed. Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth processing experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Date (1)** (`order_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date of the order. This helps us track the order's timeline.
* **Payment Method (2)** (`payment_method`, select_one, **optional**): Select the method of payment for the order, such as Cash, Credit Card, or Other. If you select Other, please specify the payment method in the Order Notes field.
* **Order Total (3)** (`order_total`, number, **optional**): Enter the total amount of the order.
* **Billing Address (4)** (`billing_address`, text, **optional**): Provide the billing address of the customer, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Shipping Address (5)** (`shipping_address`, text, **optional**): Enter the shipping address of the customer, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Order Notes (6)** (`order_notes`, note, **optional**): If you need to provide additional information about the order, such as special instructions or requests, enter them here.
* **Customer Email (7)** (`customer_email`, email, **optional**): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone (8)** (`customer_phone`, text, **optional**): Enter the customer's phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, if applicable.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
