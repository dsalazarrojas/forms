# Reading Material Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for a reading material order. It's essential to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the "order_form" field with the title of the reading material you're interested in.
2. Provide your contact information in the "contact_info" field, including your name and any other relevant contact details.
3. Choose your preferred payment method (Card, Bank, or PayPal) from the options provided in the "payment_method" field.
4. Select your delivery options (if applicable) in the "delivery_options" field.
5. Provide any additional comments or information about your order in the "order_notes" field.
6. Finally, submit the form to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **order_form** (`order_page`, text, required): This is the title of the reading material you're interested in ordering.
* **contact_info** (`contact_page`, text, required): Please provide your name and contact details here.
* **payment_method** (`payment_page`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred payment method (Card, Bank, or PayPal).
* **delivery_options** (`delivery_page`, text, optional): Choose your delivery options (if applicable).
* **order_form_2** (`order_page_2`, text, optional): This field is not currently being used.
* **review** (`review_page`, note, optional): This field is not currently being used.
* **contact** (`contact_info_page`, email, optional): Please provide your email address.
* **payment_confirmation** (`payment_confirmation`, date, optional): This field is not currently being used.
* **order_total** (`order_page_9`, number, optional): This field is not currently being used.
* **order_notes** (`order_page_10`, note, optional): Please provide any additional comments or information about your order.
