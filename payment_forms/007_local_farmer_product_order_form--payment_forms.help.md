# Local Farmer Product Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Local Farmer Product Order Form is a form used to place orders for local farmer products. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure timely processing and delivery of your order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information in each field. All fields marked as "true" are required.
2. Select the correct order type and status for your order.
3. Add any special requests or comments as needed.
4. Confirm your shipping preferences and any additional information you would like to receive about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name?** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as the customer placing the order.
* **What is your email address?** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so the farmer can contact you about your order.
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone`, text, required): Provide your phone number so the farmer can contact you about your order.
* **Do you have any special orders or requests?** (`customer_requests`, note, required): If you have any special requests or requirements for your order, enter them here.
* **What is the type of order you would like to place?** (`order_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of order you would like to place, such as Regular Order or Special Order.
* **What is the status of your order?** (`order_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your order, such as Placed, Pending, or Delivered.
* **Do you have any additional comments or notes about your order?** (`additional_comments`, note, required): Add any additional comments or notes about your order here.
* **Do you have any special requests for shipping?** (`shipping_requests`, select_one, required): Select if you have any special requests for shipping.
* **Do you want to receive any special information or updates about your order?** (`customer_info`, select_one, required): Select if you want to receive special information or updates about your order.
* **Do we have any other information about the customer or order?** (`other_info`, note, required): Add any other information about the customer or order here.
