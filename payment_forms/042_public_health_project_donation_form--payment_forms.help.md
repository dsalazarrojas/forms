# public_health_project_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The public_health_project_donation_form is used to collect donations for a public health project. The form is used to gather information about the donor, the donation amount, and the project being supported.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

*   Select a donation amount.
*   Choose how often you would like to donate (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
*   Enter a start date for your donation frequency.
*   Provide your name and address.
*   Enter the name of the project you would like to support.
*   Choose a payment method (Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal).
*   Confirm your donation frequency (True or False).
*   Enter the name of the organization receiving the donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount you are willing to donate to the public health project. This field is required.
  *   Enter a numerical value for the amount you wish to donate.
  
- **How often would you like to donate?**: Choose how often you would like to make donations to the public health project. This field is required.
  *   Select one or more of the following options: Once, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually.

- **When would you like to start donating?**: Select a date when you would like to begin making your donations. This field is required.
  *   Choose a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

- **What is your name?**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents. This field is required.
  *   Enter the name you wish to be associated with the donation.

- **What is your phone number?**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes. This field is required.
  *   Enter a valid phone number.

- **What is your address?**: Enter your mailing address for follow-up and communication purposes. This field is required.
  *   Enter the address where you can be reached.

- **What is the name of the project you would like to donate to?**: Enter the name of the project you wish to support. This field is required.
  *   Enter the name of the public health project you are donating to.

- **How would you like to pay for your donation?**: Choose the method you would like to use for your donation payment. This field is required.
  *   Select one of the following options: Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal.

- **How frequent are you in donating?**: Confirm your donation frequency. This field is required.
  *   Select True or False for your donation frequency.

- **What is the name of the organization receiving the donation?**: Enter the name of the organization or entity that will receive the donation. This field is required.
  *   Enter the name of the organization or entity you wish to support.
