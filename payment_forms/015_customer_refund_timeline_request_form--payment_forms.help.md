# Customer Refund Timeline Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a refund for your purchase. Please fill out the form with your information and preferences, and our team will review and process your request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Customer Refund Details" page and fill out the form with your details.
	* Enter your information in the relevant fields.
2. On the "Reason For Refund" page, select the reason for your refund.
	* Select one of the given options (Faulty product, Wrong order, Products not as described).
3. On the "Requested Refund Amount" page, enter the amount you are requesting.
	* Enter a numerical value.
4. On the "Payment Method" page, select the payment method used for the original payment.
	* Select one of the given options (Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal).
5. On the "Preferred Refund Method" page, select your preferred refund method.
	* Select one of the given options (Refund in Store Credit, Refund to original payment method).
6. On the "Refund Status" page, select the status of your refund request.
	* Select one of the given options (Processing, Approved, Rejected).
7. On the "Notes" page, enter any additional comments or information.
	* Enter your comments.
8. On the "Customer Refund Request Date" page, select the date of your refund request.
	* Select a date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details** (Page 1, Customer Refund Details)
	+ This field is required and should be filled out with your information.
	+ This field is where you will enter your details.
* **Reason For Refund** (Page 2)
	+ This field is optional, but recommended to select the reason for your refund.
	+ You can select one of the given options (Refund for a faulty product, Refund for a wrong order, Refund for products not as described).
* **Requested Refund Amount** (Page 3)
	+ This field is required and should be filled out with the amount you are requesting.
	+ Enter a numerical value.
* **Payment Method** (Page 4)
	+ This field is optional, but recommended to select the payment method used for the original payment.
	+ Select one of the given options (Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal).
* **Preferred Refund Method** (Page 5)
	+ This field is optional, but recommended to select your preferred refund method.
	+ Select one of the given options (Refund in Store Credit, Refund to original payment method).
* **Refund Status** (Page 6)
	+ This field is optional, but recommended to select the status of your refund request.
	+ Select one of the given options (Processing, Approved, Rejected).
* **Notes** (Page 7)
	+ This field is required and should be filled out with any additional comments or information.
	+ Enter your comments.
* **Customer Refund Request Date** (Page 8)
	+ This field is required and should be filled out with the date of your refund request.
	+ Select a date.
