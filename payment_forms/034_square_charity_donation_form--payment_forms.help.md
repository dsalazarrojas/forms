# square-charity-donation-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "square-charity-donation-form" is designed to collect donation information from donors for a charity, providing a platform for specifying donation details and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Donation Amount** in the specified field.
2. Specify the **Donation Purpose** of your contribution.
3. Choose whether your donation is a **One Time** or **Recurring** gift.
4. Select the **Donation Method** (Stripe, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
5. Enter your **Donor Name**, **Donor Email**, **Donor Phone**, **Donor Address**, **Donor City**, **Donor State**, and **Donor Zip**, as well as **Donor Country**.
6. Add any additional **Donation Notes** (optional).
7. Choose the **Submit** option to finalize your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount you wish to donate to the charity.
* **Donation Purpose**: Explain why you're donating to the charity.
* **Donor Name**: Enter your name as the donor.
* **Donor Email**: Enter your email address for contact.
* **Donor Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Donor Address**: Enter your physical address for contact.
* **Donor City**: Specify the city where you reside.
* **Donor State**: Specify your state or region of residence.
* **Donor Zip**: Enter your zip code for mailing purposes.
* **Donor Country**: Specify the country where you reside.
* **Donation Frequency**: Choose whether your donation is **One Time** or **Recurring**.
* **Donation Method**: Select **Stripe**, **PayPal**, or **Bank Transfer** as the method of payment.
* **Donation Notes**: Optional space to leave any additional comments or notes about your donation.
* **Submit**: Finalize your donation entry by selecting this option.
* **Custom 1, 2, and 3**: Optional custom fields with no specific explanation, possibly for internal or specific purpose use.
* **Cancel**: Option to cancel your donation entry.
