# Afterpay Fashion Accessories Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Afterpay Fashion Accessories Order Form is designed to collect information about the fashion accessory you wish to purchase. This includes your contact information, shipping and billing details, the accessory category and specific item name, and your preferences for payment and shipping methods.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the customer information sections (Fields 1-4)
2. Select the accessory category and specific item you wish to purchase (Fields 8 and 9)
3. Choose your preferred shipping method (Field 17)
4. Select Afterpay as your payment method (Field 18)
5. Accept the terms and conditions of the form (Field 19)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Full Name (1)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address (2)**: Enter a valid email address for order confirmation and communication.
* **Phone Number (3)**: Enter your phone number for delivery updates and any questions or concerns.
* **Shipping Address (4)**: Enter the complete address where you would like the accessory delivered.
* **Billing Address Same as Shipping (5)**: If your billing address is the same as your shipping address, select 'Yes'. Otherwise, select 'No' and fill out the Billing Address field.
* **Billing Address (6)**: If your billing address is different from your shipping address, enter the complete address.
* **Accessory Category (8)**: Select the type of fashion accessory you are interested in (e.g., Jewelry, Handbags, etc.).
* **Specific Item Name (9)**: Enter the name of the specific accessory you would like to purchase.
* **Brand Preference (10)**: If you have a preferred brand for the accessory, enter it here.
* **Color Preference (11)**: If you have a preferred color for the accessory, enter it here.
* **Size Specifications (12)**: If the accessory has specific size requirements, enter them here.
* **Material Preference (13)**: Select the material you prefer for the accessory (e.g., Leather, Fabric, etc.).
* **Quantity (14)**: Enter the number of this accessory you would like to order.
* **Price Range (15)**: Select your preferred price range for the accessory.
* **Occasion or Use (16)**: Enter the occasion or purpose for which you would like to use this accessory.
* **Shipping Preference (17)**: Select your preferred shipping method (e.g., Standard, Express, etc.).
* **Payment Method (18)**: Select Afterpay as your payment method for this order.
* **Terms Agreement (19)**: Select 'I Agree' to accept the store's terms and conditions.
