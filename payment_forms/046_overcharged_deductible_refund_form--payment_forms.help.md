# Overcharged Deductible Refund Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to aid customers in processing their overcharged deductible refund requests by providing a structured format for collecting and submitting necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and detailed information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.
3. Review and submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overcharged Deductible Refund Form** (`overcharged_deductible_refund_form`, text, required): Enter a brief title or description for your refund request.
* **Date of Claim** (`date_of_claim`, date, required): The date on which the claim was made.
* **Claim Number** (`claim_number`, number, required): The unique number assigned to the claim.
* **Amount Overcharged** (`amount_overcharged`, number, optional): The total amount overcharged by the insurance provider.
* **Amount Refund** (`amount_refund`, number, optional): The amount being requested for refund.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Your name as a customer.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Your email address where you can be reached.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, select_one, optional): Select the insurance provider associated with your claim.
* **Reason for Refund** (`reason_for_refund`, text, optional): A brief explanation of why you are requesting a refund.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, note, optional): Attach any supporting documents or evidence relevant to your refund request.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any further comments or details that may be useful for processing your refund.

## Tips
- Be accurate and thorough when filling out the form.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed.
- If you have supporting documents, attach them to the corresponding field for easy review.
- Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submission.
