# Surgical Ward Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Surgical Ward Donation Form is a tool for individuals to donate to a surgical ward. This form will help you collect donations for a specific purpose and keep a record of the donation details.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form with accurate and truthful information.
- Make sure to complete all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*) in the field label.
- If a field has a hint, please read it for further clarification.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation amount** (`donation_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you would like to donate. This field is optional, but please be accurate to the best of your ability.
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification. This field is optional but required for record-keeping purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted about your donation. This field is optional.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted about your donation. This field is optional.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select a payment method from the provided options (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Interac, PayPal). Please choose a valid payment method that you are familiar with.
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select the frequency of your donation from the provided options (One-Time, Monthly, Annually). Please choose one or more frequencies that apply to your donation.
* **Gift Aid** (`giftaid`, select_multiple, optional): Select if you would like to use gift aid for your donation. Please choose one or both options (Yes, No).
* **Gift Aid Amount** (`giftaid_amount`, number, optional): Enter the gift aid amount if you selected the Gift Aid option. This field is optional and only required if you have selected Gift Aid.

Note: This form is intended for general use, and the explanations are based on the provided YAML fields. However, please review the form carefully before filling it out to ensure that you understand the purpose and the requirements.
