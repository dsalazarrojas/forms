# Anniversary Gift Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Anniversary Gift Order Form is used to gather information for ordering anniversary gifts, including customer details, gift preferences, and delivery instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Provide your email address for order confirmation and updates.
3. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
4. Select the type of anniversary you are celebrating.
5. Choose the gift category that suits your preference.
6. Select a specific gift or package from the available options.
7. If applicable, provide personalization text for engraving, printing, or custom messaging.
8. Choose the gift wrapping style from the available options.
9. Enter a message to include with the gift, if desired.
10. Select the delivery type that suits your needs.
11. Specify the desired delivery date.
12. Enter the full name of the gift recipient.
13. Provide the delivery address.
14. Enter the contact number for the recipient, if necessary.
15. Provide any special delivery instructions, if required.
16. Specify the number of items to order.
17. Select the payment method you prefer.
18. Choose whether to include a gift receipt.
19. Indicate whether to receive marketing communications from us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (customer_name, text, required): Enter your full name for accurate gift delivery and communication purposes.
* **Email Address** (email, email, required): Provide your email address for order confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (phone, text, required): Enter your contact number for delivery and communication purposes.
* **Anniversary Type** (anniversary_type, select_one, required): Select the type of anniversary you are celebrating, such as a wedding or relationship anniversary.
* **Anniversary Year/Milestone** (anniversary_year, text, optional): If applicable, specify the year or milestone of the anniversary.
* **Gift Category** (gift_category, select_one, required): Choose the type of gift that suits your preference, such as jewelry or a personalized item.
* **Specific Gift Selection** (gift_selection, text, required): Describe the specific gift or package you have chosen.
* **Personalization Text** (personalization_text, text, optional): If desired, provide text for engraving, printing, or custom messaging.
* **Gift Wrap Option** (gift_wrap_option, select_one, required): Select the gift wrapping style that suits your preference.
* **Greeting Card Message** (greeting_card_message, text, optional): If desired, enter a message to include with the gift.
* **Delivery Type** (delivery_type, select_one, required): Choose the delivery type that suits your needs, such as standard shipping or express delivery.
* **Desired Delivery Date** (delivery_date, date, required): Specify the date when you would like the gift to arrive.
* **Recipient Name** (recipient_name, text, required): Enter the full name of the gift recipient.
* **Delivery Address** (recipient_address, text, required): Provide the full delivery address.
* **Recipient Phone** (recipient_phone, text, optional): Enter the contact number for the gift recipient, if necessary.
* **Special Delivery Instructions** (special_instructions, text, optional): If required, provide any specific delivery instructions.
* **Quantity** (order_quantity, number, required): Specify the number of items to order.
* **Payment Method** (payment_method, select_one, required): Select the payment method you prefer, such as credit card or PayPal.
* **Include Gift Receipt** (gift_receipt, select_one, required): Choose whether to include a gift receipt with the gift.
* **Marketing Communications** (marketing_opt_in, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you would like to receive updates on new products and offers.
