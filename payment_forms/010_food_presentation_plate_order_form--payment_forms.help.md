# Food Presentation Plate Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Food Presentation Plate Order Form" is a form used to collect information from clients for food presentation orders. This form is used by a business offering food presentation services to process orders accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the following information:
	* Client Name: Enter the name of the client or customer.
	* Client Phone: Enter the client's phone number.
	* Client Email: Enter the client's email address.
	* Order Description: Provide a brief description of the order, including any specific details or instructions.
	* Food Type: Select one or more food types from the options provided (Fruits, Vegetables, Meat).
	* Presentation Style: Choose the presentation style for the order (Plate, Bowl, Container).
	* Quantity: Enter the quantity of the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name (Text, Optional)**: Enter the name of the client or customer.
* **Client Phone (Text, Optional)**: Enter the client's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Client Email (Email, Optional)**: Enter the client's email address for communication.
* **Order Description (Text, Optional)**: Provide a brief description of the order, including any specific details or instructions.
* **Food Type (Select Multiple, Optional)**: Select one or more food types from the options provided (Fruits, Vegetables, Meat).
* **Presentation Style (Select One, Optional)**: Choose the presentation style for the order (Plate, Bowl, Container).
* **Quantity (Number, Optional)**: Enter the quantity of the order.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Use the correct format for the "Client Email" field (e.g., [name@example.com](mailto:name@example.com)).
* If you're unsure about any field, ask your sales representative for assistance.
