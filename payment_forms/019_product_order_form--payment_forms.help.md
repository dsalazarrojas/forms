# product order form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The product order form is a tool used to collect information about a customer's product order. It includes fields for order details, product selection, quantity, payment method, shipping address, product name, product price, product description, shipping date, shipping time, email, and phone number.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the order details section with any relevant information about the order.
2. Select the product(s) you would like to order using the "products" field.
3. Enter the quantity of the product(s) you would like to order.
4. Choose the payment method for the order.
5. Enter your shipping address in the "shipping address" field.
6. Enter the name of the product you are ordering.
7. Enter the price of the product being ordered.
8. Enter a brief description of the product being ordered.
9. Enter the shipping date for the order.
10. Select the shipping time for the order.
11. Enter your email address.
12. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details (id: 1)**: Fill in any relevant information about the order, such as the order number or any special instructions.
* **Products (id: 2)**: Select one or more products to include in the order. The options are "Yes" and "No". 
* **Quantity (id: 3)**: Enter the number of products you would like to order.
* **Payment Method (id: 4)**: Choose how you would like to pay for the order. The options are "Yes" and "No". This field is likely intended to ask if you are choosing the payment method, not the actual payment method.
* **Shipping Address (id: 5)**: Enter your shipping address to ensure the order is delivered correctly.
* **Product Name (id: 6)**: Enter the name of the product being ordered.
* **Product Price (id: 7)**: Enter the price of the product being ordered.
* **Product Description (id: 8)**: Enter a brief description of the product being ordered.
* **Shipping Date (id: 9)**: Enter the date you would like the order to be shipped.
* **Shipping Time (id: 10)**: Select the time you would like the order to be shipped.
* **Email (id: 11)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about the order.
* **Phone (id: 12)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about the order.

## Tips
* Please fill in the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure we have all the necessary information to process the order correctly.
* Make sure to review the form before submitting it to ensure you haven't missed any important information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us and we will be happy to assist you.
