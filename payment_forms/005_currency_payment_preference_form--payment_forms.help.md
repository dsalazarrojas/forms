# Currency Payment Preference Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to update your payment method and currency preference for future transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, navigate to the **Payment Methods** section and select one or more preferred payment methods from the options provided. 
2. Then, choose your preferred currency from the **Currency** field. Select one currency option from the dropdown list. 
3. In the **Additional Information** section, select whether you want to display additional payment information in your transactions. 
4. If applicable, provide your **Currency Code** in the corresponding field.
5. In the **Payment Options** section, select whether you want to display payment options in your transactions.
6. In the **Bank** field, enter your bank information if it's required to be displayed in transactions. 
7. In the **Contact Information** section, select whether you want to display contact information in your transactions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Methods** (select_multiple):
  * <label>Payment Methods</label> (Yes, No): This field allows you to select one or more payment methods that you prefer to use for financial transactions.
* **Customer** (text):
  * <label>Customer</label> (text): Please enter your customer name to associate with this payment preference.
* **Currency** (select_one):
  * <label>Currency</label> (select one): Choose your preferred currency for financial transactions from the available options (USD, EUR, JPY, CNY, AUD, INR, GBP, CHF, CAD, NZD, RUB, MXN).
* **Additional Information** (select_multiple):
  * <label>Additional Information</label> (Yes, No): This field determines whether you want to display additional payment information in your transactions.
* **Currency Code** (text):
  * <label>Currency Code</label> (text): If you selected a currency from the previous field, enter the corresponding currency code.
* **Payment Options** (select_multiple):
  * <label>Payment Options</label> (Yes, No): This field allows you to select whether you want to display payment options in your transactions.
* **Bank** (text):
  * <label>Bank</label> (text): Enter your bank information if required to be displayed in transactions.
* **Contact Information** (select_multiple):
  * <label>Contact Information</label> (Yes, No): This field determines whether you want to display contact information in your transactions.
