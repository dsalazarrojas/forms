# Co-Working Desk Rental Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The form is designed to gather details about your co-working desk rental requirements, including preferred workspace type, desired lease start date, and other relevant business information. It will help us understand your needs better and provide an ideal workspace for your team.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name, email address, and phone number as required contact information.
2. Choose your preferred workspace type: Hot Desk, Dedicated Desk, or Private Office.
3. Select your desired lease start date.
4. Choose your initial rental duration: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, 6 Months, or 12 Months.
5. Provide your company name and team size (if applicable).
6. Specify your industry or profession.
7. Indicate your need for 24-7 building access and a professional mailing address service.
8. Select your preferred payment method: Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal.
9. Agree to the co-working space terms of service and community guidelines.
10. Upload your digital signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Workspace Preferences** (`section_workspace_pref`, `note`, optional): This is a note field for you to provide any additional workspace preferences.
* **Type of Desk or Workspace Required** (`desk_type`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the options: Hot Desk, Dedicated Desk, or Private Office.
* **Desired Lease Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date you would like your lease to start.
* **Initial Rental Duration** (`rental_duration`, `select_one`, required): Select the duration of your rental: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, 6 Months, or 12 Months.
* **Business Details** (`section_business_details`, `note`, optional): This is a note field for you to provide any additional business details.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, optional): If you are an individual, please leave this field blank.
* **Total Number of Team Members requiring access** (`team_size`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of team members that need access to the space.
* **Industry or Profession** (`industry_profession`, `text`, required): Specify your industry or profession for our records.
* **Amenities and Access** (`section_access`, `note`, optional): This is a note field for you to provide any additional access requirements.
* **Do you require 24-7 building access?** (`access_24_7_req`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you need 24-7 access.
* **Do you need a professional mailing address service?** (`mailing_service_req`, `select_one`, optional): Select if you need a professional mailing address service.
* **Payment and Terms** (`section_payment_terms`, `note`, optional): This is a note field for you to provide any additional payment terms.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method_pref`, `select_one`, optional): Choose from Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal.
* **Agree to the co-working space terms of service and community guidelines** (`terms_service_ack`, `select_one`, required): Select to agree to the terms of service and community guidelines.
* **Applicant Digital Signature** (`digital_signature`, `text`, required): Upload your digital signature for verification purposes.
