# Mobile Accessory Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mobile Accessory Order Form is a form designed for customers to submit their mobile accessory orders. It collects payment details, delivery preferences, and customer information to process the order correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your payment details in the "payment_details" field.
2. Select your delivery preferences in the "delivery_preferences" field. You can choose from "Yes" or "No".
3. Select your customer details in the "customer_details" field. You can choose from "Yes" or "No".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Mobile Accessory Order Form** (mobile_accessory_order_form, text, required): Enter the description of the mobile accessory order you would like to submit.
- **payment_details** (payment_details, text, required): Enter your payment details such as credit card number, expiration date, and security code. Please ensure that the information you enter is accurate and up-to-date.
- **delivery_preferences** (delivery_preferences, select_multiple, required): Select your delivery preferences. If you select "Yes", you will receive the order via delivery. If you select "No", you will receive the order by mail.
- **customer_details** (customer_details, select_one, required): Select your customer details. If you select "Yes", you are a new customer. If you select "No", you are an existing customer.

## Tips
- Double-check your payment details for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure that your delivery preferences are correct to receive your order on time.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
