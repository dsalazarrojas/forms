# Road Sign Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Road Sign Order Form is used to collect information for the ordering of custom road signs. This includes details about the desired road sign, artwork, delivery details, payment method, and payment amount.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the road sign in the "Custom Road Sign" field.
2. Provide a description of the artwork in the "Artwork" field.
3. Enter the delivery details in the "Delivery Details" field.
4. Select the payment method in the "Payment Method" dropdown menu. Available options are: Cash, Check, and Credit Card.
5. Enter the payment amount in the "Payment Amount" field.
6. If desired, provide any additional payment terms in the "Payment Terms" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Custom Road Sign** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter a brief title of the road sign you would like to order.
* **Artwork** (`2`, `text`, required): Describe the artwork you would like to include on your road sign.
* **Delivery Details** (`3`, `text`, required): Provide details about the delivery address, date, and any specific delivery instructions.
* **Payment Method** (`4`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the available payment options: Cash, Check, and Credit Card.
* **Payment Amount** (`5`, `number`, required): Enter the total payment amount for the road sign.
* **Payment Terms** (`6`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional payment terms or conditions if necessary.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the title and artwork descriptions are accurate and clear to avoid any delays in processing your order.
* If you have any special delivery instructions, please provide them in the "Delivery Details" field.
* For payment amounts, please enter only numerical values without any currency symbols.
