# Billboard Traffic Service Form By Asiah Amsterdamm - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request billboard advertising services for your business. Please follow the instructions carefully to ensure your submission is completed accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your business name exactly as it appears on your legal documents.
2. Provide the name of the contact person responsible for the campaign.
3. Enter your email address for campaign communications.
4. Enter your phone number for easy contact.
5. Select your preferred billboard location.
6. Choose the start date for your advertising campaign.
7. Choose the duration of your campaign.
8. Indicate if you have existing artwork or need design services.
9. Add any special requirements or notes for your campaign.
10. Choose your preferred payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the legal name of your business.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person`, text, required): Provide the name of the contact person responsible for the campaign.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address we will use for all campaign communications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Preferred Billboard Location** (`billboard_location`, text, required): Select the specific intersection or city area for the billboard.
* **Advertising Start Date** (`campaign_start_date`, date, required): Choose the start date for your advertising campaign.
* **Campaign Duration** (`campaign_duration`, select_one, required): Choose the duration of your campaign (1 Month, 3 Months, 6 Months, 1 Year).
* **Do you have existing artwork?** (`artwork_status`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have existing artwork or need design services (Yes, No).
* **Special Requirements or Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Add any special requirements or notes for your campaign.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Wire Transfer, PayPal).
