# DVD Rental Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The DVD Rental Order Form is used to collect information about a customer's DVD rental request. This form allows customers to provide their details, select their preferred pickup or delivery option, and specify the DVD title they wish to rent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer details, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the DVD title you wish to rent from the provided options.
3. Choose whether you would like to pick up the DVD or have it delivered.
4. Specify the date and time you would like to rent the DVD.
5. Indicate the quantity of DVDs you would like to rent.
6. Provide any additional comments or instructions for your rental.
7. Review your order details before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`): Enter your full name in this field.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): Enter your valid email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **DVD Title** (`dvd_title`): Select the specific DVD title you would like to rent.
* **Pickup/Delivery** (`pickup_delivery`): Choose whether you want to pick up the DVD at the store or have it delivered to your location. The options are:
	+ Pickup: Pick up the DVD at the store.
	+ Delivery: Have the DVD delivered to your location.
* **Date** (`date`): Select the date you would like to rent the DVD. The date format is dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Time** (`time`): Select the time you would like to rent the DVD. The time format is 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`): Indicate the number of DVDs you would like to rent.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Provide any additional comments or instructions for your rental.
* **Name** (`customer_name`): Enter your name.
* **Address** (`customer_address`): Enter your address.

Note: All fields marked with `required: false` are not mandatory, but providing accurate information will help us process your request more efficiently.
