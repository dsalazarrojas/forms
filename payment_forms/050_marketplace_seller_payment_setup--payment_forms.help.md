# Marketplace Seller Payment Setup - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect payment information from marketplace sellers, allowing them to set up their payment preferences and details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the payment method you prefer: Choose the payment method that suits you best by selecting one of the options under "payment_method" (credit card, bank transfer, PayPal, or Stripe).
2. Provide your details: Fill in your seller details, including your email address, phone number, and bank account information.
3. Set your payment terms: Choose the payment terms and conditions you agree to by selecting "accepted" under "payment_terms_and_conditions_agreement".
4. Agree to the terms of service: Confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms of service by selecting "accepted" under "terms_of_service_agreement".
5. Confirm your bank details: Choose the bank details you want to use for payments by selecting one of the options under "bank_details".
6. Set your payment status: Fill in any additional comments or payment status information you wish to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **payment_method** (select_one): Choose the payment method you prefer from the list:
	+ Credit card
	+ Bank transfer
	+ PayPal
	+ Stripe
* **payment_terms_and_conditions_agreement** (select_one): Confirm that you have read and agreed to the payment terms and conditions:
	+ Accepted
	+ Not accepted
* **terms_of_service_agreement** (select_one): Confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms of service:
	+ Accepted
	+ Not accepted
* **bank_details** (select_one): Choose the bank details you want to use:
	+ Bank details 1
	+ Bank details 2
* **email_address** (email): Provide your email address.
* **phone_number** (text): Provide your phone number.
* **street_address** (text): Provide your street address.
* **city** (text): Provide your city.
* **state_province** (text): Provide your state or province.
* **postal_code** (text): Provide your postal code.
* **bank_account_number** (number): Provide your bank account number.
* **bank_account_name** (text): Provide your bank account name.
* **bank_account_routine** (select_multiple): Choose the payment routine you prefer from the list:
	+ Routine 1
	+ Routine 2
* **payment_terms_date** (date): Set the payment terms date.
* **payment_terms_time** (time): Set the payment terms time.
* **payment_terms_note** (note): Provide any additional notes about your payment terms.
* **bank_routing** (text): Provide any additional information about your bank routing.
* **routing_rules** (text): Provide any additional information about your payment routing rules.
* **payment_status** (text): Provide any additional comments or payment status information.

Note: Please ensure that you have completed all required fields before submitting the form.
