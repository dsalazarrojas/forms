# Cosmetic Subscription Box Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers regarding their subscription to a cosmetic subscription box service. This form will be used to process their order and deliver the selected products to their doorstep.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your customer information.
2. Select your preferred subscription plan (Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
3. Choose your desired products (Face Cream, Lip Balm, Mascara, or Nail Polish).
4. Enter your shipping information.
5. Select your payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
6. Enter your payment information.
7. Review your order details.
8. Submit your order and pay or save it for later.
