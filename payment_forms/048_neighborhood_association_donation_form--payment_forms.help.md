# Neighborhood Association Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for collecting donations from neighborhood association members.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount you would like to donate.
2. **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method:
	* Bank Transfer
	* Check
	* Cash
3. **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
4. **Contact Number**: Provide your contact phone number for further communication.
5. **Message**: Add any additional comments or notes about your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Amount**: This field asks for the amount you would like to donate. Please enter a numerical value in the field.
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred method of payment:
	+ **Bank Transfer**: A transfer from your bank account.
	+ **Check**: A check drawn from your account.
	+ **Cash**: A physical payment of cash.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed. This should be a valid email address.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact phone number for further communication. This should be a valid phone number.
* **Message**: This field is for any additional comments or notes about your donation. You can use this field to explain why you are making this donation or if you have any special requests.
