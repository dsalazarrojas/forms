# e_commerce_payment_processing_audit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and verify information about e-commerce payment processing activities. It is designed for auditors and reviewers to document and verify the accuracy of e-commerce payment processing activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the e-commerce payment information in the corresponding fields.
2. Choose the relevant payment workflow and security practices.
3. Rate the reliability of the payment process.
4. Select the internal review option that best describes the payment process.
5. Select the payment gateways used for the payment process.
6. Enter the audit date and time.
7. Sign the auditor signature field.
8. Enter the payment amount.
9. Select the payment method and status.
10. Enter any payment remarks if necessary.
11. Enter the reviewer information.
12. Sign the reviewer signature field.
13. Enter the review date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **E Commerce Payment Info** (text, required): Enter the e-commerce payment information.
* **Payment Workflow** (text, required): Select the payment workflow used for the payment process.
* **Security Practices** (text, optional): Describe the security practices used for the payment process.
* **Reliability Rating** (number, required): Rate the reliability of the payment process (e.g., 1-5).
* **Internal Reviews** (select_one, required): Select the internal review option that best describes the payment process.
* **Payment Gateways** (select_multiple, optional): Select the payment gateways used for the payment process.
* **Audit Date** (date, required): Enter the audit date.
* **Audit Time** (time, optional): Enter the audit time.
* **Auditor Signature** (text, required): Sign the auditor signature field.
* **Payment Amount** (number, required): Enter the payment amount.
* **Payment Method** (select_one, optional): Select the payment method used for the payment process.
* **Payment Status** (select_one, required): Select the payment status (e.g., successful, pending, failed).
* **Payment Remark** (text, optional): Enter any payment remarks if necessary.
* **Reviewer Info** (text, optional): Enter the reviewer information.
* **Reviewer Signature** (text, required): Sign the reviewer signature field.
* **Review Date** (date, required): Enter the review date.
