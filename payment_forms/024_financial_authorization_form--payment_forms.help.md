# Financial Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal and financial information for financial authorization purposes. Please fill it out if you have been asked to do so by your supervisor or the finance department.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before starting.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
3. Ensure you have the required approval and authorization before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification document.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification document.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your work phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Date of Authorization** (`date_of_authorization`, date, required): Enter the date you were authorized to access financial resources.
* **Authorization Type** (`authorization_type`, text, required): Select the type of authorization you are requesting (e.g., 'Travel', 'Project', etc.).
* **Authorization Amount** (`authorization_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you are authorized to access.
* **Authorization Date** (`authorization_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are authorized to access financial resources.
* **Authorization Time** (`authorization_time`, time, required): Enter the time you are authorized to access financial resources.

## Tips
* Ensure you have all necessary approvals and information before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or finance department for assistance.
