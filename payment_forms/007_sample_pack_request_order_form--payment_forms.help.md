# Sample Pack Request Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a sample pack from our organization. It captures essential contact and shipping information to ensure accurate and efficient processing of your sample pack request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required contact information: 
   - Enter your **Contact Name** in the field labeled "contact name".
   - Enter your company or organization's **Business Name** in the field labeled "business name".
   - Enter your **Phone Number** in the field labeled "phone number".
   - Enter your **Email** address in the field labeled "email".
2. Provide your **Sample Type** in the field labeled "sample type".
3. Specify the **Quantity** of sample packs you would like to request.
4. Enter the address for your **Ship to** location.
   - Ensure the **City** field is filled out accurately.
   - Enter your **Zip Code** in the format of XXXXX (5 digits).
   - Choose your **State** from the list of available states.
   - Select your **Country** from the list of available countries.
5. Review your entry for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Name** (Contact Name, text, required): This is where you enter your name, which will be associated with your sample pack request.
* **Business Name** (Business Name, text, optional): If you represent a company or organization, this field allows you to provide your business name.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Provide your email address for communication regarding your sample pack request.
* **Sample Type** (Sample Type, text, optional): Specify the type of sample pack you are interested in requesting.
* **Quantity** (Quantity, number, optional): Indicate the number of sample packs you would like to request.
* **Ship to** (Ship to, text, optional): Enter the full address where you would like to have your sample packs shipped to, including city and state.
* **City** (City, text, optional): Enter the city where you would like your sample packs shipped to.
* **Zip** (Zip, number, optional): Enter the 5-digit zip code where you would like your sample packs shipped to.
* **State** (State, text, optional): Choose your state from the list to ensure accurate shipping.
* **Country** (Country, text, optional): Select your country of residence for accurate shipping purposes.
