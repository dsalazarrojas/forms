# Online Course Pre Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect pre-order information for online courses. It gathers essential details to manage course enrollments, payments, and assignments. Please fill out this form to provide necessary information for course pre-ordering.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked as "Required" on the form. These fields are:
   - Course ID
   - Email
   - Course Title
   - Start Date
   - End Date
   - Enrollment Period
   - Enrollment Limit
   - Price
   - Status
   - Payment Term
   - Payment Status
   - Payment Method

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course ID** (`1`, `number`, **Required**): Enter the unique ID of the course you are pre-ordering.
* **Learner Name** (`2`, `text`, **Not Required**): If you are pre-ordering a course on behalf of someone else, enter their name here.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, **Required**): Enter the email address associated with the course pre-order.
* **Phone** (`4`, `text`, **Not Required**): Enter the phone number associated with the course pre-order.
* **Course Title** (`5`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the title of the course you are pre-ordering.
* **Start Date** (`6`, `date`, **Required**): Enter the start date of the course.
* **End Date** (`7`, `date`, **Required**): Enter the end date of the course.
* **Enrollment Period** (`8`, `number`, **Required**): Enter the enrollment period for the course.
* **Enrollment Limit** (`9`, `number`, **Required**): Enter the enrollment limit for the course.
* **Price** (`10`, `number`, **Required**): Enter the price of the course.
* **Status** (`11`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select the status of the course:
	+ Open
	+ Closed
	+ Pre-ordering
* **Payment Term** (`12`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Select the payment term for the course:
	+ One-time
	+ Recurring
	+ Other (one-time)
	+ Other (recurring)
* **Payment Status** (`13`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select the payment status for the course:
	+ Unpaid
	+ Paid
	+ Refunded
* **Payment Method** (`14`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select the payment method for the course:
	+ PayPal
	+ Stripe
	+ Bank Transfer
* **Comments** (`15`, `note`, **Not Required**): Add any additional comments for the course pre-order.
* **Assign Form** (`16`, `select_one`, **Not Required**): Select the assigned tool for this form.
* **Assign User** (`17`, `select_one`, **Not Required**): Select if this form is assigned to a user.
* **Assign Date** (`18`, `date`, **Not Required**): Enter the date the form was assigned.
* **Assign Time** (`19`, `time`, **Not Required**): Enter the time the form was assigned.
* **Comments 2** (`20`, `note`, **Not Required**): Add any additional comments 2 for the course pre-order.
* **Start Date 2** (`21`, `date`, **Not Required**): Enter the start date 2 of the course.
* **End Date 2** (`22`, `date`, **Not Required**): Enter the end date 2 of the course.
* **Price 2** (`23`, `number`, **Not Required**): Enter the price 2 of the course.
* **Status 2** (`24`, `select_one`, **Not Required**): Select the status 2 of the course:
	+ Open
	+ Closed
	+ Pre-ordering
