# Urban Farming Education Fund Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Urban Farming Education Fund Donation Form is designed to collect donations and relevant information from individuals and organizations. The form will help the non-profit or educational institution track and process donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **Name** in the provided text field.
2. Enter your **Email** in the email field. This should be a valid email address that can be used for correspondence.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** in the text field.
4. Enter the **Amount Donated** in the number field. This should be the amount you are willing to donate to the Urban Farming Education Fund.
5. Select your preferred **Payment Method** from the dropdown options.
6. If you have any additional comments, you can provide them in the **Comment** field.
7. Choose your **Organization** (if applicable). You can select from the options: Urban Farming Initiative, Local Organization, or Other.
8. Finally, click the **Submit** button to complete and send your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Name (Required)**
* The **Name** field is for your personal or organizational name, and is required for identification purposes.
### **Email (Required)**
* The **Email** field is your email address, which will be used for correspondence and updates.
### **Phone (Required)**
* The **Phone** field is your phone number, which will be used to contact you for further details if needed.
### **Amount Donated (Required)**
* The **Amount Donated** field is where you enter the amount you are willing to donate to the Urban Farming Education Fund.
### **Payment Method (Required)**
* The **Payment Method** field allows you to select your preferred payment method from the options: Cash, Check, Bank Transfer, or Credit Card.
### **Comment (Optional)**
* The **Comment** field is for any additional information you'd like to share with the non-profit or educational institution.
### **Organization (Required)**
* The **Organization** field is for identifying the organization you represent, which can be: Urban Farming Initiative, Local Organization, or Other.
### **Submit (Required)**
* The **Submit** button sends your completed donation form to the non-profit or educational institution for processing.
