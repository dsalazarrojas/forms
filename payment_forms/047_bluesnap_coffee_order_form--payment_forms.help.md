# BlueSnap Coffee Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the BlueSnap Coffee Order Form with ease.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred coffee type.
2. Choose the roast level that suits your taste.
3. Choose your grind size according to your brewing method.
4. Select the bag size that's right for you.
5. Decide on the number of bags you'd like to order.
6. Choose a flavor option: pure or flavored.
7. Select the payment method that you're comfortable with.
8. Choose the delivery frequency that suits your needs.
9. If you have any special requests, such as allergies or preferences, enter them here.
10. Confirm the order date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, **required**): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address for contact and order updates.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your phone number for additional contact.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, **required**): Enter the address where you'd like your coffee delivered.
* **City** (`delivery_city`, `text`, **required**): Enter the city where you'd like your coffee delivered.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (`delivery_zip`, `text`, `required`): Enter the ZIP or postal code of your delivery location.
* **Coffee Type** (`coffee_type`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Choose one or more coffee types: Espresso, Americano, Cappuccino, Latte, Macchiato, Mocha, Flat white, Cold brew.
* **Roast Level** (`roast_level`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the roast level that suits your taste: Light, Medium, Dark, Extra dark.
* **Bean Origin** (`bean_origin`, `select_one`, **optional**): Select the origin of the coffee beans: Colombian, Ethiopian, Brazilian, Indonesian, Kenyan, Mixed blend.
* **Grind Size** (`grind_size`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the grind size that suits your brewing method: Whole bean, Fine, Medium, Coarse.
* **Number of Bags** (`quantity_bags`, `number`, **required**): Enter the number of bags you'd like to order.
* **Bag Size (ounces)** (`bag_size`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the bag size: 8 oz, 12 oz, 1 lb, 2 lb, 5 lb.
* **Flavored or Pure?** (`flavored_option`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose whether you'd like your coffee to be pure or flavored: Pure, Vanilla, Hazelnut, Caramel, Mocha.
* **Price Per Bag** (`price_per_bag`, `number`, **optional**): Enter the price per bag (if you've already negotiated a price with us).
* **Total Order Amount** (`total_cost`, `number`, **optional**): Calculate the total cost of your order (will be displayed automatically).
* **Delivery Frequency** (`delivery_frequency`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the delivery frequency: One-time, Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the payment method: Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, **optional**): Enter any special requests or allergies you have.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, **required**): Select the date you'd like to receive your order.

Note: This form is designed to guide you through the process of ordering your coffee with ease. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
