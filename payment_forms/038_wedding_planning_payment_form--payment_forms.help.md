# Wedding Planning Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record payments and services for wedding planning. It helps vendors track payments and services rendered for each wedding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the payment method (yes/no) and whether you're making another payment (yes/no).
2. Enter the payment amount.
3. Choose the payment date (in the format MM/DD/YYYY).
4. Enter the vendor's name and contact information.
5. Select the type of service provided (yes/no).
6. Enter any additional service details.
7. Confirm the payment status (active/inactive).
8. Select any additional payment method (yes/no).
9. Choose the services provided by the vendor (yes/no).
10. Enter the service date (in the format MM/DD/YYYY).
11. Enter any notes about the payment or service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **payment_method** (select_one): Choose 'Yes' if this is a payment form, 'No' if this is a test or demonstration.
- **amount** (number): Enter the total amount paid.
- **payment_date** (date): Choose the date of the payment in MM/DD/YYYY format.
- **vendor_name** (text): Enter the name of the vendor who received the payment.
- **service_type** (select_one): Select 'Yes' if the service was rendered, 'No' if not.
- **service_details** (text): Describe any additional details about the service provided.
- **contact_info** (text): Enter the contact information for the vendor.
- **payment_status** (select_one): Confirm the payment status as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
- **payment_method_2** (select_one): Select 'Yes' if this is another payment method, 'No' if not.
- **vendor_service** (select_multiple): Choose any additional services provided by the vendor (yes/no).
- **service_date** (date): Enter the date the service was rendered in MM/DD/YYYY format.
- **notes** (text): Enter any additional notes about the payment or service.
