# Professional Networking Event Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for attendees to provide their contact information and feedback about the professional networking event. Event organizers or hosts may use this data to follow up with attendees, share event results, or gather feedback for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
2. Provide your email address where you can be reached for future communications.
3. If applicable, specify your organization or company name.
4. Share your job title or role within your organization.
5. Provide any feedback or comments about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Attendee Name**: Your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Event Attendee Email**: Your email address where you can be reached for future communications.
* **Event Attendee Organization**: If you're representing a company or organization, please specify it here.
* **Event Attendee Job Title**: Your job title or role within your organization.
* **Event Attendee Feedback**: Share your thoughts, comments, or suggestions about the event.
