# Donation Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from donors for the purpose of processing and recording their donations. It helps to ensure that all necessary details are captured accurately and consistently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contribution amount in the "Contribution Amount" field. This is the amount you wish to donate.
2. Select your preferred payment method by choosing "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu. This will indicate whether you are willing to pay for the donation.
3. In the "Payment Info" field, you can enter any additional information about your payment method. This can include your payment card number, bank account number, or other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contribution Amount (1)**: Enter the amount you wish to donate.
* **Payment Method (2)**: Select "Yes" if you are willing to pay for the donation, or "No" if you are not. This will help us determine the best payment method for your contribution.
* **Payment Info (3)**: Enter any additional information about your payment method, such as a payment card number or bank account number. This field is optional and only necessary if you selected "Yes" as your payment method.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately to avoid any issues with your donation processing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
* Review all fields carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
