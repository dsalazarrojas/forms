# Frozen Food Truck Accessory Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect order information from frozen food truck customers, allowing the sales or customer service team to gather essential details about their orders and shipping addresses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the order form with your order details.
2. Provide your shipping address for delivery purposes.
3. Choose your preferred payment method.
4. Add any additional notes or comments for our team to assist you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Form (1)**: This is the main section where you can enter your order details. Please provide any relevant information about your order, such as the items you wish to purchase.
* **Shipping Address (2)**: Enter your shipping address to ensure that your order is delivered correctly. This includes your name, street address, apartment or suite number, city, state or province, and zip or postal code.
* **Payment Method (3)**: Select your preferred payment method for the order. You can choose from a list of available payment options.
* **Notes (4)**: Use this field to add any additional comments or notes for our team to assist you. This can include special instructions or any other information that might be helpful for our team to complete your order efficiently.

## Tips
- Please double-check your order form and shipping address for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
