# Automatic Payment Authorization Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Automatic Payment Authorization Consent Form is used to collect information and consent from customers to set up automatic recurring payments. This form helps ensure that customers understand and agree to the terms of the payment, including the amount, frequency, and method of payment. 

To ensure you're filling out the form correctly, please review the instructions below and ensure you're meeting all the required fields.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, please navigate through each section and fill in the required information as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Account Holder Name** (`account_holder_name`): Enter your name as it appears on your account.
* **Email Address** (`account_email`): Enter your email address to receive invoices and payment confirmations.
* **Phone Number** (`account_phone`): Enter your phone number to contact you for any payment-related questions or issues.
* **Customer ID** (`customer_id`): This field is optional and not currently used. You may fill it out if you know the ID.
* **Authorization Date** (`date_of_authorization`): The date you're giving consent for automatic payments.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`):
	+ Choose one of the following payment methods: Bank Account, Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, ACH, or Other.
* **Bank Account Last 4 Digits** (`bank_account_last_four`): If you selected Bank Account as your payment method, enter the last 4 digits of your bank account number. 
* **Card Last 4 Digits** (`card_last_four`): If you selected Credit Card, Debit Card, or Other as your payment method, enter the last 4 digits of your card number. 
* **Recurring Payment Amount** (`billing_amount`): The amount to be charged each billing period. 
* **Billing Frequency** (`billing_frequency`):
	+ Choose one of the following frequencies: Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annual, or Annual.
* **First Billing Date** (`billing_start_date`): The date when the first payment will be made.
* **Billing End Date** (`billing_end_date`): If you want to set an end date for automatic payments, enter it here. Otherwise, leave it blank for indefinite payments.
* **Service or Product Description** (`service_description`): Describe what the payment is for. 
* **Invoice Delivery Email** (`invoice_email`): Enter the email address to which invoices should be sent.
* **I understand the payment terms and amounts** (`understand_terms`):
	+ Select 'Yes' to confirm you've read and understand the payment terms.
* **I authorize automatic recurring payments** (`authorize_payment`):
	+ Select 'I agree and authorize' to confirm you're willing to pay automatically. 
* **I understand I can cancel anytime with written notice** (`acknowledge_cancellation`):
	+ Select 'Yes, I understand' to confirm you understand your ability to cancel payments.
* **I consent to payment information being processed securely** (`acknowledge_privacy`):
	+ Select 'I consent' to confirm you understand how your payment information is processed.
* **Authorized Signature Name** (`authorized_signature_name`): Print your name to verify your consent. 
* **Consent Date** (`consent_completion_date`): The date and time you gave consent. 

Please ensure you understand and agree to all the terms before filling out this form. Your consent is necessary for automatic payments to begin.
