# Community Race Finish Line Banner Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you order custom banners for your community race or event. It will guide you through the process of providing all the necessary information for us to create and deliver your banner in time for your event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your contact and order details on the **Order Information** page.
2. Provide your first name, last name, organization or event name, email address, and phone number.
3. Choose the size and material of your banner on the **Banner Specifications** page.
4. Select the finish type and design preferences on the **Banner Design** and **Design Preferences** pages.
5. Provide shipping information and payment details on the **Shipping Information** and **Payment Method** pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Information**: This page is where you will enter your contact and order details. Please fill in your first name, last name, organization or event name, email address, and phone number.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Organization or Event Name**: Enter the name of your organization or event.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for order confirmation and any further communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of your event.
* **Banner Specifications**: Choose the size and material of your banner from the options provided.
* **Banner Size**: Select the size of your banner from the options:
	+ 8 ft x 2 ft
	+ 10 ft x 2 ft
	+ 12 ft x 3 ft
	+ 15 ft x 3 ft
	+ 20 ft x 4 ft
	+ Custom Size
* **Custom Width and Height**: If you select **Custom Size**, please enter the width and height of your banner in feet.
* **Banner Material**: Select the material of your banner from the options:
	+ 13 oz Vinyl
	+ 18 oz Vinyl
	+ Mesh
	+ Fabric
	+ Eco-Friendly
* **Finish Type**: Select the finish type of your banner from the options:
	+ Grommets Every 2 Feet
	+ Grommets Every 1 Foot
	+ Pole Pockets
	+ Hem Only
	+ No Finish
* **Banner Design**: This is a space for you to describe your banner design preferences.
* **Primary Text**: Enter the main message or title of your banner.
* **Secondary Text**: Enter any additional information you want to display on your banner.
* **Include Logo**: If you have a logo, select **Yes - Will Upload**, otherwise select **No Logo Needed**.
* **Background Color**: Select the background color of your banner from the options:
	+ White
	+ Black
	+ Blue
	+ Red
	+ Yellow
	+ Green
* **Text Color**: Select the color of the text on your banner from the options:
	+ Black
	+ White
	+ Blue
	+ Red
	+ Yellow
	+ Green
* **Do You Need Design Services**: Select whether you need our design services.
* **Will You Upload Artwork**: If you have final design artwork, select **Yes**.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of banners you need.
* **Is This a Rush Order**: If you need your banner within 3 days or 1 week, select **Yes**.
* **Shipping Information**: This page is a space for you to provide shipping details.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your shipping address for delivery purposes.
* **Shipping Method**: Select the shipping method from the options:
	+ Standard Ground
	+ 2-Day Express
	+ Overnight
	+ Will Pick Up
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method from the options:
	+ Credit Card
	+ Debit Card
	+ Purchase Order
	+ Check
	+ Invoice
* **Purchase Order Number**: If you are paying with a purchase order, enter the order number.
* **Special Instructions**: Enter any additional notes or instructions.
* **Estimated Total**: This will be confirmed after reviewing your order.
