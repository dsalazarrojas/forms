# Stripe Email Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stripe Email Subscription Form is designed to collect email addresses from users for marketing or promotional purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your email address in the "User Email" field.
2. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm Email" field by typing it exactly as you entered it in the first field.
3. Enter your name in the "User Name" field.
4. Confirm your name in the "Confirm Name" field by typing it exactly as you entered it in the previous field.
5. Review the "Reconfirm Email" fields for accuracy, but do not fill them out if you have already confirmed your email address correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Email (1)**: Enter your email address that you would like to subscribe to. This field is required to complete the form.
* **Confirm Email (2)**: Type your email address exactly as you entered it in the "User Email" field. This field is also required.
* **User Name (3)**: Enter your name as you would like to be referred to. This field is required.
* **Confirm Name (4)**: Type your name exactly as you entered it in the "User Name" field. This field is also required.
* **Reconfirm Email (11)**: This field is intended to verify that you have correctly confirmed your email address. Do not fill it out if you have already confirmed correctly. This field is required.
* **Confirm Reconfirm Email (9)**: Similar to the previous field, this one is also intended to verify your email address. Do not fill it out if you have already confirmed correctly. This field is required.
* **Reconfirm Email (2)**: This field is intended to re-verify your email address. Do not fill it out if you have already confirmed correctly. This field is required.
* **Confirm Reconfirm Email (10)**: Similar to the previous field, this one is also intended to re-verify your email address. Do not fill it out if you have already confirmed correctly. This field is required.
* **Reconfirm** (12): This field is intended to confirm your email address one more time. Do not fill it out if you have already confirmed correctly. This field is required.

Note: The multiple "reconfirm" fields are likely intended to improve form accuracy, but they might also be confusing. If you are unsure, it's best to only fill out the first two fields and confirm your email address correctly.
