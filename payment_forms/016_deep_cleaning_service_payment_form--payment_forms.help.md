# deep_cleaning_service_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "deep_cleaning_service_payment_form" is used to capture payment-related information from customers for deep cleaning services.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have previously opted in for deep cleaning services and are a registered customer.
2. Fill out each field accurately and completely.
3. Review the form for any errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, optional): Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Enter your payment method (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, cash).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more payment methods (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, cash).
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): Enter any specific payment terms or conditions.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): Enter your address for billing purposes.
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the service you are paying for.
* **Total Amount** (`total_amount`, number, optional): Enter the total amount due.
* **Transaction ID** (`transaction_id`, number, optional): Enter the transaction ID (if known).
* **Transaction Date** (`transaction_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the transaction.
* **Transaction Time** (`transaction_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the transaction.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.

## Tips
* Be sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact our support team.
* This form is only for customers who have previously opted in for deep cleaning services.
* Accurately filling out this form ensures smooth payment processing.
