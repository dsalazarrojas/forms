# PayPal Orphanage Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PayPal Orphanage Donation Form is used to collect information for a donation to an orphanage through a PayPal payment system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Enter a brief description of your donation in the "form_title" field.
- Provide a more detailed description of your donation in the "form_description" field.
- Enter the amount of your donation in the "form_amount" field. Please use numerical values only.
- Select the frequency of your donation from the "form_frequency" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_title** (text): Enter a brief description of your donation.
* **form_description** (text): Provide a more detailed description of your donation, including any specific requests or requirements.
* **form_amount** (number): Enter the amount of your donation. Please use numerical values only.
* **form_frequency** (select one): Select the frequency of your donation: One Time or Recurring.
