# Parking Fee Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parking Fee Refund Form is a form used to request a refund for parking fees that were paid incorrectly or excessively. It is used to provide information about the vehicle, parking fee date, refund amount, and other relevant details to ensure a smooth and accurate refund process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure all information is accurate.
2.  Select "Yes" or "No" for each confirm field to indicate whether the information provided is correct or not.
3.  Fill in the vehicle number plate, vehicle type, parking fee date, refund amount, reason for refund, email, phone, address, business name, and municipality as applicable.
4.  Add any additional comments if necessary.
5.  Submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parking Fee Refund Form (id: 1)**: Select "Yes" if the parking fees are eligible for refund.
* **Vehicle Number Plate (id: 2)**: Enter the vehicle number plate as per the parking ticket or receipt.
* **Vehicle Type (id: 3)**: Select the type of vehicle from the options provided (Car, Truck, Motorcycle, Bus).
* **Parking Fee Date (id: 4)**: Enter the date of the parking fee payment.
* **Refund Amount (id: 5)**: Enter the amount of refund eligible.
* **Reason for Refund (id: 6)**: Provide a brief explanation for the reason of the refund.
* **Payment Method (id: 7)**: Select the method of payment used (Credit Card, Bank Transfer).
* **Email (id: 8)**: Enter the email address associated with the refund.
* **Phone (id: 9)**: Enter the phone number associated with the refund.
* **Address (id: 10)**: Enter the address associated with the refund.
* **Business Name (id: 11)**: Enter the name of the business associated with the refund.
* **Municipality (id: 12)**: Enter the municipality associated with the refund.
* **Additional Comments (id: 13)**: Add any additional comments or details as necessary.
* **Date (id: 14)**: Enter the date of the refund.
* **Time (id: 15)**: Enter the time of the refund.
* **Confirm (id: 16)**: Confirm that the information provided is correct.
* **Confirm Email (id: 17)**: Confirm that the email address is correct.
* **Confirm Phone (id: 18)**: Confirm that the phone number is correct.
* **Confirm Address (id: 19)**: Confirm that the address is correct.
* **Confirm Municipality (id: 20)**: Confirm that the municipality is correct.
* **Confirm Vehicle Number Plate (id: 21)**: Confirm that the vehicle number plate is correct.
* **Confirm Vehicle Type (id: 22)**: Confirm that the vehicle type is correct.
* **Confirm Parking Fee Date (id: 23)**: Confirm that the parking fee date is correct.
* **Confirm Refund Amount (id: 24)**: Confirm that the refund amount is correct.
* **Confirm Reason for Refund (id: 25)**: Confirm that the reason for refund is correct.

## Tips
* Double-check all information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure all confirm fields are completed correctly to avoid delays in the refund process.
