# Single Plane Engine Intake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect orders for single plane engines with precision and accuracy. By providing clear and concise fields, you can ensure that your order is processed correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Plane Type** for your order from the available options (Aircraft 1, Aircraft 2, or Aircraft 3).
2. Enter the **Order Date** for your order using the date picker.
3. Specify the **Engine Number** by selecting multiple options (Engine 1, Engine 2, or Engine 3).
4. Enter the **Customer Name** in the text field.
5. Select the **Payment Method** for your order from the available options (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
6. Enter the total **Total Amount** for your order.
7. Provide the **Shipping Address** in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Plane Type** (`plane_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of plane for your order.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date for your order using the date picker.
* **Engine Number** (`engine_number`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple engine numbers for your order.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the customer.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select the payment method for your order.
* **Total Amount** (`total_amount`, number, optional): Enter the total amount for your order.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter the shipping address for your order.
