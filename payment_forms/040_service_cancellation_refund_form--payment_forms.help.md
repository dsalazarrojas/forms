# service_cancellation_refund_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to initiate a refund request for a cancelled service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the reason for cancellation from the "Cancellation Reason" field.
2. Enter your name and email address in the "Customer Name" and "Customer Email" fields.
3. Provide details about the cancellation reason in the "Reason Details" field.
4. Select the refund amount from the "Refund Amount" field.
5. Choose the refund method from the "Refund Method" dropdown.
6. Select the customer service agent who handled your refund request from the "Customer Service Agent" dropdown.
7. Enter any additional comments or notes about the refund request in the "Service Cancellation Refund Form Comments" field.
8. If applicable, enter the customer service agent's name, email, and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cancellation Reason**: Explain the reason for cancelling the service. (Text field)
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name. (Text field)
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address. (Email field)
* **Reason Details**: Provide more details about the cancellation reason. (Text field)
* **Refund Amount**: Enter the amount you would like to be refunded. (Number field)
* **Refund Method**: Select the method for the refund, e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", etc. (Dropdown field)
* **Customer Service Agent**: Select the customer service agent who handled your refund request. (Dropdown field)
* **Service Cancellation Refund Form Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the refund request. (Text field)
* **Customer Service Agent Name**: If applicable, enter the customer service agent's name. (Text field)
* **Customer Service Agent Email**: If applicable, enter the customer service agent's email address. (Email field)
* **Customer Service Agent Phone**: If applicable, enter the customer service agent's phone number. (Text field)
* **Customer Service Agent Designation**: If applicable, enter the customer service agent's designation. (Text field)
* **Customer Service Agent Note**: Enter any notes about the refund request. (Text field)
* **Order Details**: Provide any additional details about the order. (Text field)
* **Customer Service Agent Refund Amount**: Enter the refund amount selected by the customer service agent. (Number field)
* **Customer Service Agent Refund Method**: Select the refund method selected by the customer service agent. (Dropdown field)
* **Customer Service Agent Refund Status**: Select the refund status selected by the customer service agent. (Dropdown field)
* **Customer Service Agent Refund Date**: Enter the date of the refund selected by the customer service agent. (Date field)
* **Service Cancellation**: Enter the service cancellation details. (Text field)
* **Refund Details**: Enter any additional refund details. (Text field)

Note: The fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be filled in to complete the form.
