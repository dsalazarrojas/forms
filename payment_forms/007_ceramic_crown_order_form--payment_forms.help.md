# Ceramic Crown Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ceramic Crown Order Form is used to submit an order for ceramic dental crowns, ensuring accurate and efficient fulfillment of the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Order Date** in the first field. This is the date the crown order was submitted.
2. Provide your **Dentist Name** in the second field. This should be your full name as it appears on your license.
3. Enter the **Dental Practice Name** in the third field. This should be the name of your dental clinic or practice.
4. Enter the **Practice Address** in the fourth field. This should be your practice's full mailing address.
5. Provide your **Office Phone Number** in the fifth field. This is your practice's contact phone number.
6. Enter your **Email Address** in the sixth field. This is the email address you would like to receive order confirmations.
7. Enter your **Dental License Number** in the seventh field. This is your state dental license number.
8. Enter the **Patient Initials** in the eighth field. This should be the patient's first and last initials.
9. Enter the **Patient Date of Birth** in the ninth field. This is the patient's date of birth.
10. Select the **Patient Gender** in the tenth field. This is the patient's gender for shade matching.
11. Enter the **Tooth Number** in the eleventh field. This is the universal tooth numbering system.
12. Select the **Arch Location** in the twelfth field. This is where the crown will be placed (upper or lower arch).
13. Select the **Crown Type** in the thirteenth field. This is the type of ceramic crown (e.g., full ceramic, porcelain fused to metal, etc.).
14. Enter the **Shade Selection** in the fourteenth field. This should be the VITA shade guide selection.
15. Enter the **Stump Shade** in the fifteenth field. This is the underlying tooth shade if applicable.
16. Select the **Margin Design** in the sixteenth field. This is the type of margin required (e.g., chamfer, shoulder, etc.).
17. Select the **Occlusion Type** in the seventeenth field. This is the patient's bite classification.
18. Enter the **Preparation Date** in the eighteenth field. This is the date the tooth was prepared.
19. Select the **Delivery Method** in the nineteenth field. This is how the crown should be delivered (e.g., courier pickup, postal mail, etc.).
20. Select the **Impression Type** in the twentieth field. This is the method used for the impression (e.g., digital scan, PVS impression, etc.).
21. Select the **Payment Method** in the twenty-first field. This is the method of payment (e.g., credit card, debit card, etc.).
22. Select the **Rush Order** in the twenty-second field. This indicates if the order is a rush order.
23. Enter your **Authorization Signature** in the twenty-third field. This should be your name as a signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Date**: Date of crown order submission.
* **Dentist Name**: Full name of ordering dentist.
* **Dental Practice Name**: Name of dental clinic or practice.
* **Practice Address**: Full mailing address of dental practice.
* **Office Phone**: Contact phone number of dental practice.
* **Email Address**: Email for order confirmations.
* **Dental License Number**: State dental license number.
* **Patient Initials**: Patient first and last initials.
* **Patient Date of Birth**: Patient date of birth.
* **Patient Gender**: Patient gender for shade matching.
* **Tooth Number**: Universal tooth numbering system.
* **Arch Location**: Where the crown will be placed (upper or lower arch).
* **Crown Type**: Type of ceramic crown.
* **Shade Selection**: VITA shade guide selection.
* **Stump Shade**: Underlying tooth shade if applicable.
* **Margin Design**: Type of margin required.
* **Occlusion Type**: Patient bite classification.
* **Preparation Date**: Date tooth was prepared.
* **Delivery Method**: How crown should be delivered (e.g., courier pickup, postal mail, etc.).
* **Impression Type**: Method used for impression (e.g., digital scan, PVS impression, etc.).
* **Payment Method**: Method of payment (e.g., credit card, debit card, etc.).
* **Rush Order**: Indicates if the order is a rush order.
* **Authorization Signature**: Type dentist name as signature.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult your practice or office manager.
* Double-check the form for errors before submitting.
* If you need to make changes, please contact us to inquire about possible alternatives.
