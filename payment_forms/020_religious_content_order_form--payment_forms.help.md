# Religious Content Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Religious Content Order Form is designed to collect information about orders for religious content, such as materials or services, from customers. This form helps staff efficiently process and manage these orders.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Order Details** section with a brief description of the order.
2. Select the **Payment Information** option to confirm if payment is required or not.
3. Choose your **Delivery Preferences** from the available options.
4. Enter your **Delivery Address** if applicable.
5. Provide the **Order Total**.
6. Add any **Order Notes** if necessary.
7. Select the **Order Type**.
8. Choose the **Order Status** from the available options.
9. Enter the **Order Date** and **Order Time** if required.
10. Enter your **Email** and **Phone** contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details**: A text field to enter a brief description of the order.
* **Payment Information**: A select one field to confirm if payment is required (Yes or No).
* **Delivery Preferences**: A select multiple field to choose from available options (e.g., Yes or No).
* **Delivery Address**: A text field to enter the delivery address if applicable.
* **Order Total**: A number field to enter the total cost of the order.
* **Order Notes**: A text field for any additional comments or notes related to the order.
* **Order Type**: A select one field to choose the type of order (e.g., Yes or No).
* **Order Status**: A select multiple field to choose from available options (e.g., Active, Inactive).
* **Order Date**: A date field to enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time**: A time field to enter the time of the order.
* **Email**: An email field to enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone**: A text field to enter the customer's phone number.

## Tips

* Please ensure you have permission to use the customer's contact information.
* Double-check the order details and ensure they are accurate before submitting.
* If you have any issues or need assistance with the form, please contact our support team.
