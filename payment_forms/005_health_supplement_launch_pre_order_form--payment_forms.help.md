# Health Supplement Launch Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from customers to facilitate the pre-order of health supplements. The gathered data includes customer contact information and pre-order details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields to ensure accurate processing of your pre-order.
2. For fields not marked as required, you may choose whether or not to fill them out.
3. Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to send your pre-order details to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (customer_name, text, Required): This field is used to collect the customer's name. Please enter your full name, including your first and last name.
* **Email** (email, email, Optional): This field is used to collect the customer's email address. If you have one, please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (phone, text, Optional): This field is used to collect the customer's phone number. If you have one, please enter your phone number.
* **Order Date** (order_date, date, Optional): This field is used to select the date for your pre-order. Please choose the date you would like to pre-order your health supplements.
* **Shipping Address** (shipping_address, text, Optional): This field is used to collect the customer's shipping address. Please enter your shipping address to ensure accurate delivery of your pre-ordered health supplements.
* **Pre-Order Amount** (pre_order_amount, number, Optional): This field is used to specify the quantity of health supplements you would like to pre-order. Please enter the number of health supplements you would like to purchase.
* **Pre-Order Method** (pre_order_method, select_one, Optional): This field is used to select the pre-order method you would like to use. Please choose from the available options.
* **Payment Method** (payment_method, select_one, Optional): This field is used to select the payment method you would like to use. Please choose from the available options.
* **Notes** (notes, note, Optional): This field is used for any additional comments or special requests you may have for your pre-order. Please use this field to provide any necessary information about your pre-order.
