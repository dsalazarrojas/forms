# vip_hospitality_service_quotation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The VIP hospitality service quotation form is used to collect information from the client to create a personalized quotation for the sale of a hospitality service. This form is a critical part of the sales process, and it is essential to complete it accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Client Information** (`client_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's details, including their name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the contact preferences for the client, such as whether they prefer to be contacted via email, phone, or other means.
3. **Service Quotation** (`service_quote`, `number`, required: true): Enter the total value of the service quotation.
4. **Service Quotation Details** (`service_quote_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional details about the service quotation, such as the package details or services included.
5. **Client Requirements** (`client_requirements`, `text`, required: false): List any specific client requirements or requests.
6. **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, required: false): List any special requests or requirements for the service quotation.
7. **Quotation Status** (`quote_status`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the status of the quotation, such as 'Draft', 'Sent', or 'Approved'.
8. **Quotation Date** (`quote_date`, `date`, required: true): Enter the date of the quotation.
9. **Quotation Time** (`quote_time`, `time`, required: true): Enter the time of the quotation.
10. **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the payment terms for the quotation, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.
11. **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the payment method for the quotation, such as Option 1 or Option 2.
12. **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about the quotation.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are completed.
* Use the correct format for the date and time fields (yyyy-mm-dd and hh:mm format, respectively).
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your supervisor or manager for guidance.
