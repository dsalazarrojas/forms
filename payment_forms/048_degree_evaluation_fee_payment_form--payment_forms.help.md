# Degree Evaluation Fee Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide information for degree evaluation and payment purposes. It is used to process payments for the evaluation of a degree from a university.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your degree evaluation request in the "Degree Evaluation Request" field.
3. Attach relevant academic documents as required.
4. Choose a payment method (Bank Transfer, Credit Card, or PayPal).
5. Enter the payment amount in the "Payment Amount" field.
6. Indicate whether you have opted for online payment or not.
7. Choose the review team for your degree evaluation (School Review Team or External Review Team).
8. Add any additional comments or instructions for the review team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Degree Evaluation Request** (id: 1, text, required: false)
	+ This field is where you can enter any additional information about your degree evaluation request.
* **Academic Documents** (id: 2, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for attaching relevant academic documents, such as transcripts or diplomas, for the review team to consider.
* **Payment Method** (id: 3, select_one, required: false)
	+ Choose how you want to pay for the degree evaluation service: Bank Transfer, Credit Card, or PayPal.
* **Payment Amount** (id: 4, number, required: true)
	+ Enter the total amount you are paying for the degree evaluation service.
* **Online Payment** (id: 5, select_one, required: false)
	+ Indicate whether you have opted for online payment or not.
* **Review Team** (id: 7, select_one, required: false)
	+ Choose between the School Review Team or the External Review Team for your degree evaluation.
* **Additional Comments** (id: 8, text, required: false)
	+ Add any additional comments or instructions for the review team.
* **Submit** (id: 6, text, required: false)
	+ This button will submit your form and begin the degree evaluation process.
