# public_awareness_campaign_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information for public awareness campaign donations. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select the **Donation Amount** field and choose the amount you'd like to donate. 
   Please note that this field is required.
2. In the **Donation Frequency** field, choose how often you'd like to make your donation. 
   You can choose between **Monthly**, **One-time**, or **Other** options. 
   Please note that this field is required.
3. In the **Payment Method** field, select your preferred payment method. 
   You can choose between **card**, **PayPal**, or **Apple Pay**. 
   Please note that this field is required.
4. If you'd like to include your **Email** address, please fill it in. 
   This will allow us to keep you updated on your donation progress. 
   This field is optional.
5. If you'd like to include your **Phone** number, please fill it in. 
   This will allow us to contact you in case of any updates or issues. 
   This field is optional.
6. If you'd like to include a **Note** for your donation, please fill it in. 
   This will allow us to understand the purpose of your donation better. 
   This field is optional.
7. If you'd like to select **Flexible Frequency Options**, please choose from **daily**, **weekly**, **monthly**, **quarterly**, or **annual**. 
   This will determine how frequently you'd like to make your donation. 
   This field is optional.
8. If you'd like to set a **Recurring Donation Start Date**, please select a date. 
   This will determine when your recurring donation will start. 
   This field is optional.
9. If you'd like to set a **Recurring Donation Frequency**, please select a number. 
   This will determine how frequently your donation will recur. 
   This field is optional.
10. If you'd like to set a **Recurring Donation Period**, please select a number. 
   This will determine how long your recurring donation will last. 
   This field is optional.
11. If you'd like to set an **Recurring Donation End Date**, please select a date. 
   This will determine when your recurring donation will end. 
   This field is optional.
12. If you'd like to include a **Recurring Donation Note**, please fill it in. 
   This will allow us to understand the purpose of your recurring donation better. 
   This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required): Please fill in the amount you'd like to donate.
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often you'd like to make your donation.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required): Select your preferred payment method.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please fill in your email address for updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please fill in your phone number for contact.
* **Note** (`donation_note`, note, optional): Add a note for your donation.
* **Flexible Frequency Options** (`flexible_frequency_options`, select_multiple, optional): Choose from **daily**, **weekly**, **monthly**, **quarterly**, or **annual** for your donation frequency.
* **Recurring Donation Start Date** (`recurring_donation_start_date`, date, optional): Select a start date for your recurring donation.
* **Recurring Donation Frequency** (`recurring_donation_frequency`, number, optional): Select a frequency for your recurring donation.
* **Recurring Donation Period** (`recurring_donation_period`, number, optional): Select a period for your recurring donation.
* **Recurring Donation End Date** (`recurring_donation_end_date`, date, optional): Select an end date for your recurring donation.
* **Recurring Donation Note** (`recurring_donation_note`, note, optional): Add a note for your recurring donation.

## Tips
- Ensure you're filling out this form for the correct public awareness campaign.
- Fill out this form accurately and honestly.
- If you've already filled out this form, please avoid duplication.
