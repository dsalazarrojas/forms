# Afterpay Electronic Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Afterpay Electronic Product Order Form is used to create electronic orders for products that can be paid in installments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your full name in the "Customer Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field for order confirmation and updates.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field for delivery updates.
4. Fill in the "Shipping Address" field with your complete address for delivery.
5. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Billing Address Same as Shipping" field if your billing address is the same as your shipping address.
6. If your billing address is different from your shipping address, fill in the "Billing Address" field with the correct address.
7. Select the "Product Category" of the electronic product you're ordering from the dropdown menu.
8. Enter the brand of the electronic product in the "Product Brand" field.
9. Fill in the specific model of the product in the "Product Model" field.
10. Enter the specifications of the product (storage, RAM, etc.) in the "Specifications" field (optional).
11. Select the quantity of units you want to order in the "Quantity" field.
12. Select the condition of the product you prefer ("New", "Certified Refurbished", "Like New", "Very Good", or "Good") in the "Product Condition" field.
13. Select the warranty option you prefer from the dropdown menu in the "Warranty Selection" field (optional).
14. If you need any accessories with your product, fill in the "Accessories Required" field.
15. Select if you require setup assistance or technical support in the "Technical Support Needed" field (optional).
16. Choose your preferred shipping method from the dropdown menu in the "Shipping Preference" field.
17. Select your payment method from the dropdown menu in the "Payment Method" field.
18. Finally, select "I Agree" to the store's terms and Afterpay conditions in the "Terms Agreement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Full Name** (Customer Full Name, text, Required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, Required): Enter your email for order confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, Required): Enter your phone number for delivery updates.
* **Shipping Address** (Shipping Address, text, Required): Enter the complete address for delivery.
* **Billing Address Same as Shipping** (Billing Address Same as Shipping, select_one, Required): Is your billing address the same as your shipping address?
* **Billing Address** (Billing Address, text, Not Required): Enter the complete billing address if different from shipping.
* **-- Product Category --** (note, Not Required): A note field with a heading.
* **Product Category** (Product Category, select_one, Required): Select the category of electronic product you're ordering.
* **Product Brand** (Product Brand, text, Required): Enter the brand of the electronic product.
* **Product Model** (Product Model, text, Required): Enter the specific model of the product.
* **Specifications** (Specifications, text, Not Required): Enter desired specifications (storage, RAM, etc.) of the product.
* **Quantity** (Quantity, number, Required): Enter the quantity of units you want to order.
* **Product Condition** (Product Condition, select_one, Required): Select the condition of the product you prefer ("New", "Certified Refurbished", "Like New", "Very Good", or "Good").
* **Warranty Selection** (Warranty Selection, select_one, Not Required): Select the warranty option you prefer from the dropdown menu.
* **Accessories Required** (Accessories Required, text, Not Required): If you need any accessories with your product, fill in the field.
* **Technical Support Needed** (Technical Support Needed, select_one, Not Required): If you require setup assistance or technical support, select "Yes" or "Maybe".
* **Shipping Preference** (Shipping Preference, select_one, Required): Choose your preferred shipping method from the dropdown menu.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, Required): Select your payment method from the dropdown menu.
* **Terms Agreement** (Terms Agreement, select_one, Required): Select "I Agree" to the store's terms and Afterpay conditions.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your email and phone number for order confirmation and delivery updates.
* If you have any questions, contact our customer support team.
* After filling out the form, click submit to complete your order.
