# Art Workshop Registration Form WorldPay UK Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Workshop Registration Form is used to collect participant information and preferences for an art workshop. This form is required to be completed to register for the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. We will send a confirmation email to this address.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select the workshop type you want to attend from the "Select Workshop" field.
6. Choose your preferred workshop date from the "Preferred Workshop Date" field.
7. Select your experience level in the "Experience Level" field.
8. Choose whether you want materials to be included or not in the "Materials Package" field.
9. Enter the registration fee in the "Registration Fee" field. 
10. Enter the optional materials fee in the "Materials Fee" field.
11. Enter the total amount due in the "Total Amount (GBP)" field.
12. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" field.
13. Enter the name as it appears on your card in the "Cardholder Name" field.
14. If you have any dietary requirements or allergies, enter them in the "Dietary Requirements or Allergies" field.
15. If you require any accessibility accommodations, select the relevant option in the "Accessibility Needs" field.
16. If you have an emergency contact, enter their name and phone number in the "Emergency Contact Name" and "Emergency Contact Phone" fields.
17. Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions in the "I agree to the terms and conditions" field.
18. Choose whether you want to receive updates about future workshops in the "Receive updates about future workshops" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address. We will send a confirmation email to this address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact number for the workshop.
* **Select Workshop**: Choose the art workshop you wish to attend.
* **Preferred Workshop Date**: Select your preferred session date.
* **Experience Level**: Choose your prior art experience level.
* **Materials Package**: Select if you need materials to be provided by the workshop.
* **Registration Fee**: Enter the base fee for workshop attendance.
* **Materials Fee**: Enter the optional materials package fee.
* **Total Amount (GBP)**: Enter the sum of all fees.
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment option.
* **Cardholder Name**: Enter the name as it appears on your card.
* **Dietary Requirements or Allergies**: Enter any dietary requirements or allergies you have.
* **Accessibility Needs**: Select any accessibility accommodations you require.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **I agree to the terms and conditions**: Confirm that you agree to the workshop policies.
* **Receive updates about future workshops**: Choose whether you want to receive updates about future workshops.
* **Additional Comments or Questions**: Enter any additional information or questions you have.
