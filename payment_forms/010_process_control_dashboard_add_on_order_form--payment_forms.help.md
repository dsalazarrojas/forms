# Process Control Dashboard Add On Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect order details, delivery information, customer information, and payment details from customers who want to purchase and install a process control dashboard.

## How To Complete This Form

- Please fill out this form with the required information as prompted. The form is divided into four sections.
- Review each section carefully to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details (1)**: This section is where you enter the details of the order, such as the product or service you wish to purchase and the quantity required.
* **Delivery Details (2)**: In this section, please provide the delivery address where you would like the order to be shipped to. Ensure that you enter accurate information to facilitate smooth delivery.
* **Customer Information (3)**: Here, you will enter your customer information, including your name, contact number, and any other relevant details that we need to know for the installation process.
* **Payment Info (4)**: This is where you will enter payment information, including the payment method and any other required details. Please make sure to enter accurate payment information to avoid any issues with the order.

## Tips

* Make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, contact our support team for assistance.
* Please note that your order will be processed as soon as we receive this form.
