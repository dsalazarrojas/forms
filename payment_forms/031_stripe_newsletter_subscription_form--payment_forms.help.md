# Stripe Newsletter Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect subscription information for the Stripe Newsletter, allowing users to choose their preferred format and duration of receiving the newsletter.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on the "Submit" button at the bottom of the page to submit your subscription details.
- Fill out the required fields (marked with an asterisk \* in the field labels) completely.
- Review your submission before clicking "Submit" to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): This is where you enter your name or username. Please fill in your name to help us better serve you.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address that you'd like to use for newsletter subscriptions. We'll use this to send you the newsletter.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): If you'd like to provide your phone number, please do so here. We'll use this to contact you regarding your subscription.
- **Format** (`format`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the format of the newsletter you'd like to receive: Digital or Print. You can select one or both formats.
- **Duration** (`duration`, `select_one`, required: false): Select how long you'd like to subscribe for: 1 Month, 3 Months, or 6/12 Months. Please note that you can only choose one duration.
- **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_conditions`, `note`, required: false): Please review and agree to our Terms and Conditions before submitting your subscription. This is not a required field, but it's essential to read before proceeding.
 
 ## Tips
- Make sure to review your submission carefully before clicking "Submit" to avoid any mistakes.
- If you have any issues with your subscription or would like to unsubscribe, please contact us at your convenience.
