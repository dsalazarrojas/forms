# silent_auction_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The silent auction check out form is used to collect participant and payment information for a silent auction event. This form is used to process payments or donations from participants who have won items in the silent auction.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the payment method you would like to use for your payment.
2. Enter your participant information, including name, email, and phone number.
3. Enter your payment amount and any comments you may have about your payment.
4. Enter your donor information, including name, email, and address.
5. Enter the amount you would like to donate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Silent Auction Check Out Form**:
	+ This is the form title.
* **participant_name**:
	+ Enter your name as a participant in the silent auction.
* **participant_email**:
	+ Enter your email address.
* **participant_phone**:
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **payment_amount**:
	+ Enter the amount you would like to pay for the items you have won.
* **payment_method**:
	+ Select the payment method you would like to use. Options include Payment by check, Payment by online, and Payment by cash.
* **payment_comments**:
	+ Enter any comments you may have about your payment.
* **donor_name**:
	+ Enter the name of the donor who is giving the donation.
* **donor_email**:
	+ Enter the email address of the donor.
* **donor_phone**:
	+ Enter the phone number of the donor.
* **donor_address**:
	+ Enter the address of the donor.
* **donation_amount**:
	+ Enter the amount you would like to donate.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete all required fields before submitting the form.
* Use the payment method selected to process your payment or donation.
* If you have any issues or need to make changes to your payment or donation, contact us for assistance.
