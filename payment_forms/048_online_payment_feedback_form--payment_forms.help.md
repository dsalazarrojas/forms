# online_payment_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from customers about their experiences with our online payment method. The form is designed to be simple and easy to complete, with questions that cover various aspects of the payment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the payment method that you used for your last payment.
2. Rate your overall experience with our online payment method.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback that you have.
4. Enter your email address (if you are not a customer of our business, please leave this field blank).
5. Enter your phone number (if you are not a customer of our business, please leave this field blank).
6. Enter the date and time of your last payment.
7. Rate your satisfaction with our online payment method.
8. Indicate whether you would use our online payment method again.
9. Enter any feedback comments you have.
10. Enter the name of your business or organization.
11. Enter the email address of your business or organization.
12. Enter the phone number of your business or organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment method that you used for your last payment, such as Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
* **Payment Status** (`Payment Status`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all payment statuses that apply to your last payment, such as Paid, Pending, or Failed.
* **How would you rate your experience with our online payment method?** (`How would you rate your experience with our online payment method?`, `note`, required): Provide a short description of your experience with our online payment method.
* **Additional comments** (`Additional comments`, `note`, required): Provide any additional comments or feedback that you have about our online payment method.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address (if you are a customer of our business).
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number (if you are a customer of our business).
* **Date of last payment** (`Date of last payment`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your last payment.
* **Time of last payment** (`Time of last payment`, `time`, required): Enter the time of your last payment.
* **How satisfied are you with our online payment method?** (`How satisfied are you with our online payment method?`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of satisfaction that you have with our online payment method.
* **Would you use our online payment method again?** (`Would you use our online payment method again?`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you would use our online payment method again.
* **Rating** (`Rating`, `number`, required): Enter a rating for our online payment method.
* **Feedback Comments** (`Feedback Comments`, `note`, required): Provide any feedback comments that you have about our online payment method.
* **Business Name** (`Business Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your business or organization.
* **Business Email** (`Business Email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of your business or organization.
* **Business Phone** (`Business Phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your business or organization.
