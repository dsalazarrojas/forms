# festival_registration_form_with_eway - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for festival attendees to register for a festival event using eWay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your festival details in the **Festival Details** field.
2. Enter your attendee information in the **Attendee Information** field.
3. Fill out your registration information in the **Registration Information** field.
4. If applicable, add any additional payment information in the **Payment Information** field.
5. Upload any necessary files in the **Additional Information** field.
6. Click **Submit** to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Festival Details**: This field is where you should enter your festival details. Please provide your festival name, date, and location. This information will be used to identify your festival participation.
* **Attendee Information**: Enter your name, email, and any other relevant information about yourself. This will help us to contact you with important festival updates.
* **Registration Information**: Provide your registration information, including your festival type (e.g., artist, staff, volunteer) and any other relevant details.
* **Payment Information**: If you are registering with a payment plan, fill out this field to proceed with payment. (Note: This field is optional, but required for registration completion)
* **Additional Information**: If you have any supporting documents or files to upload, such as your ID or contract, use this field to upload them.
* **Submit**: Click this button to complete your registration and submit your application.
