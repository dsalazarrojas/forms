# ocean_coat_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your order or purchase, including product selection, payment details, and customer information. Please follow these steps to complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Ensure you have all the necessary information ready before starting to fill out the form.
*   Select your preferences for each category using the provided options.
*   Fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
*   Review your entries carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Select an Ocean Coat (product_selection)** (`Yes, No`): Select your preferred Ocean Coat.
*   **Product Details (product_details)** (`text`): Enter a brief description of the product you selected.
*   **Payment Information (payment_info)** (`Yes, No`): Specify whether you'll pay for the order (required for order placement).
*   **Delivery Information (delivery_info)** (`Yes, No`): Indicate whether you require delivery for your order (required for order placement).
*   **Order Total (order_total)** (`number`): Enter the total cost of the order (if applicable).
*   **Customer Information (customer_info)** (`Yes, No`): Provide your customer information (required for order processing).
*   **Payment Method (payment_method)** (`Yes, No`): Select the payment method (required for order processing).
*   **Payment Status (payment_status)** (`Active, Inactive`): Enter the current status of your payment (not required but useful for reference).
*   **Order Status (order_status)** (`Active, Inactive`): Enter the current status of your order (not required but useful for reference).

## Tips

*   Make sure to review all fields carefully to avoid missing any essential information.
*   If you encounter any issues or have questions, contact our support team for assistance.
