# Delayed Service Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to request a refund for a delayed service. Fill out this form to provide us with the details needed to process your refund.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Not Delivered** (`delayed_service_refund_form`, text, required: false): Enter the description of the service that was not delivered in a few sentences or a short paragraph.
* **Refund Amount** (`refund_amount`, number, required: true): Enter the amount you would like to receive as a refund. This should be a numerical value (e.g., 10.00).
* **Reason For Delayed Service** (`reason_for_delayed_service`, text, required: true): Provide a brief explanation of why the service was delayed. This will help us understand the situation better and process your refund efficiently.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: true): Enter your email address so we can contact you to confirm your refund.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): If you have a preferred phone number, enter it here so we can contact you if needed.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required: false): If you are representing a business, enter your business name here.
* **Service Description** (`service_description`, text, required: true): Provide a detailed description of the service that was delayed. This will help us process your refund correctly.
* **Order ID** (`order_id`, text, required: true): Enter the ID of the order for which you are requesting a refund.

Note: Please make sure to fill out all required fields as accurately as possible to ensure your refund is processed efficiently.
