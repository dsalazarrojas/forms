# Home Medical Device Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for ordering home medical devices. Please fill out this form to submit an order for a medical device.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your order type from the options provided.
2. Select the medical devices you wish to order.
3. Enter the quantity of each medical device.
4. Enter the price of each medical device.
5. Select your preferred payment method.
6. Enter any relevant patient information.
7. Enter any relevant provider information.
8. Add any additional notes about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Type**: Select whether you want to order a new medical device or not.
* **Medical Device**: Choose the medical devices you want to order.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of medical devices to order.
* **Price**: Enter the price of the medical devices.
* **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Patient Info**: Enter any relevant patient information.
* **Provider Info**: Enter any relevant provider information.
* **Order Notes**: Add any additional notes about your order.
