# Award Trophy Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Award Trophy Order Form is used to create and manage award and trophy orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the order type from the "Order Type" field. This will determine the type of award or trophy you are ordering.
2. Enter the order date in the "Order Date" field.
3. Enter your name or organization name in the "Customer Name" field.
4. Enter your organization name (if different from your name) in the "Organization Name" field (if required).
5. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
7. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
8. Enter your city in the "City" field.
9. Enter your state or province in the "State" field.
10. Enter your ZIP or postal code in the "ZIP/Postal Code" field.
11. Select the type of award or trophy you need from the "Trophy Type" field.
12. Select the size of the award or trophy from the "Trophy Size" field.
13. Select the material of the award or trophy from the "Trophy Material" field.
14. Select the color of the award or trophy from the "Trophy Color" field.
15. Enter the quantity of awards or trophies you need in the "Quantity" field.
16. Enter the text for the engraving on the first line in the "Engraving Line 1" field.
17. Enter the text for the engraving on the second line in the "Engraving Line 2" field (if required).
18. Enter the text for the engraving on the third line in the "Engraving Line 3" field (if required).
19. Upload any logo or design attachments in the "Logo or Design Attachment" field (if required).
20. Enter any special instructions or requirements in the "Special Instructions" field (if required).
21. Enter the date of the event or occasion in the "Event Date" field.
22. Select whether you need expedited service for this order from the "Is This a Rush Order" field.
23. Enter the unit price per award or trophy in the "Unit Price" field (if required).
24. Enter the total amount for the order in the "Total Amount" field (if required).
25. Select the payment method from the "Payment Method" field.
26. Enter the purchase order number (if applicable) in the "Purchase Order Number" field.
27. Enter the billing address (if different from the shipping address) in the "Billing Address" field (if required).
28. Accept the terms and conditions in the "I Accept Terms and Conditions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Type** (Order Type, select_one, required): Select the type of order you are placing. This will help us understand the type of award or trophy you need.
* **Order Date** (Order Date, date, required): Enter the date for the order.
* **Customer Name** (Customer Name, text, required): Enter your name or organization name.
* **Organization Name** (Organization Name, text, optional): Enter your organization name if it is different from your name.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter your email address for order confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Shipping Address** (Shipping Address, text, required): Enter the address where we will ship the order.
* **City** (City, text, required): Enter your city.
* **State** (State, text, required): Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (ZIP/Postal Code, text, required): Enter your ZIP or postal code.
* **Trophy Type** (Trophy Type, select_one, required): Select the type of award or trophy you need.
* **Trophy Size** (Trophy Size, select_one, required): Select the size of the award or trophy.
* **Trophy Material** (Trophy Material, select_one, required): Select the material of the award or trophy.
* **Trophy Color** (Trophy Color, select_one, required): Select the color of the award or trophy.
* **Quantity** (Quantity, number, required): Enter the quantity of awards or trophies you need.
* **Engraving Line 1** (Engraving Line 1, text, required): Enter the text for the engraving on the first line.
* **Engraving Line 2** (Engraving Line 2, text, optional): Enter the text for the engraving on the second line.
* **Engraving Line 3** (Engraving Line 3, text, optional): Enter the text for the engraving on the third line.
* **Logo or Design Attachment** (Logo or Design Attachment, text, optional): Upload any logo or design attachments (if required).
* **Special Instructions** (Special Instructions, text, optional): Enter any special instructions or requirements.
* **Event Date** (Event Date, date, required): Enter the date of the event or occasion.
* **Is This a Rush Order** (Is This a Rush Order, select_one, required): Select whether you need expedited service for this order.
* **Unit Price** (Unit Price, number, optional): Enter the unit price per award or trophy (if required).
* **Total Amount** (Total Amount, number, optional): Enter the total amount for the order (if required).
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, required): Select the payment method for the order.
* **Purchase Order Number** (Purchase Order Number, text, optional): Enter the purchase order number (if applicable).
* **Billing Address** (Billing Address, text, optional): Enter the billing address (if different from the shipping address).
* **I Accept Terms and Conditions** (I Accept Terms and Conditions, select_one, required): Accept the terms and conditions for the order.

## Tips
* Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
* Use the correct format for dates and addresses.
* If you need any special instructions or attachments, enter them in the corresponding fields.
* Double-check all fields before submitting the form.
