# multiple_choice_recurring_subscriptions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for recurring subscription payments. The form is intended to be completed by customers who wish to set up automatic payments with the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following fields:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **order_form** (`order_form`, text, optional): This field allows you to enter any additional information about your order. Please enter any relevant details, such as the type of subscription you are requesting.
* **payment_method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Please select the payment method you would like to use for your recurring subscription. Choose from the options provided in the dropdown menu.
* **subscription_term** (`subscription_term`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the term of your subscription. Choose from the options provided in the dropdown menu.
* **payment_cycle** (`payment_cycle`, number, optional): This field is not currently applicable. Please disregard.
* **price** (`price`, number, optional): Enter the price of your subscription. Please ensure this is accurate.
* **frequency** (`frequency`, select_one, optional): Please select how frequently you want to be charged. Choose from the options provided in the dropdown menu.
* **order_date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to start your subscription.
* **order_time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to start your subscription.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your subscription.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
 
## Tips
* Please ensure all fields that are marked as required are completed.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
* If you have already set up recurring subscriptions, please do not complete this form. Instead, contact our support team to modify your existing subscriptions.
