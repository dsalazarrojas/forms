# left_handed_golf_grip_training_aid_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for a customer's left-handed golf grip training aid order.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Customer Details** in the first field.
2. Select the **Product Selection** you wish to purchase.
3. Specify your **Shipping Preferences** in the second field.
4. Enter your **Payment Details** in the third field.
5. Review your **Order Summary** in the fourth field to ensure everything is correct.
6. Choose your preferred **Order Confirmation** option from the drop-down list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details** (Customer Details, text, optional): Enter your customer name, email, and any other relevant details to help us process your order efficiently.
* **Product Selection** (Product Selection, text, optional): Select the type or model of left-handed golf grip training aid you wish to purchase.
* **Shipping Preferences** (Shipping Preferences, text, optional): Specify your shipping address, preferred shipping method, or any special requests for shipping.
* **Payment Details** (Payment Details, text, optional): Enter your payment method (e.g., credit card, PayPal) and any other payment-related information.
* **Order Summary** (Order Summary, text, optional): Review the order details to ensure accuracy and completeness before submitting.
* **Order Confirmation** (Order Confirmation, select_one, optional): Choose how you'd like to receive your order confirmation, whether it's via email or a phone call.
