# gaming_convention_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Gaming Convention Payment Form is designed to collect necessary information to register attendees for a gaming convention. This form requires the following details: convention details, payment information, attendee specifics, and a payment method. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select "Submit" to begin filling out the form.
2. Fill out each field as instructed, following the guidelines below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gaming Convention Details** (`convention_details`): Enter the name and description of the gaming convention you are registering for.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`): Enter your payment information, including any relevant details such as payment method and any additional notes.
* **Attendee Info** (`attendee_info`): Enter the name of the attendee(s) you are registering.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`): Enter any specific payment terms or conditions, such as payment method and any other relevant details.
* **Registration Details** (`registration_details`): Enter any registration details, such as the date and time of registration.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select the payment method for your registration. Options include:
	+ Card Number
	+ CVC
	+ Expiration Date
	+ Expiration Month
	+ Expiration Year
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`): Enter the name of the attendee(s) you are registering.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter a valid email address for the attendee(s).
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter a phone number for the attendee(s), if applicable.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information for each field.
* Review your entries before submitting to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or issues with the form, contact the form administrator.
