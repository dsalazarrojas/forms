# Courier Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Courier Check Out Form is used to collect necessary information for a customer's delivery and payment details. This form is used to ensure that the delivery and payment process goes smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the payment method from the available options: Delivery, Pickup, or Online Payment.
2.  Enter the delivery date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3.  Enter the delivery time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
4.  Enter the payment amount.
5.  Provide your customer name.
6.  Enter your customer email.
7.  Enter your customer phone number.
8.  Enter the address line 1.
9.  Enter the address line 2.
10.  Select the payment status: Paid or Unpaid.
11.  Enter the order date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
12.  Enter the order time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
13.  Provide any customer notes.
14.  Enter the total payment amount.
15.  Enter the package number.
16.  Provide your signature.
17.  Select the order status: Pending, Shipped, Delivered, or Cancelled.
18.  Enter the delivery address.
19.  Enter your customer ID.
20.  Enter the payment date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
21.  Select the package status: Paid or Unpaid.
22.  Enter the delivery fee.
23.  Enter the package weight.
24.  Enter your customer zip code.
25.  Enter the order number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Method**: (id: 1, type: select_one) Select the method of payment for the delivery. Choose from Delivery, Pickup, or Online Payment.
* **Delivery Date**: (id: 2, type: date) Enter the date of delivery in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Delivery Time**: (id: 3, type: time) Enter the time of delivery in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Payment Amount**: (id: 4, type: number) Enter the amount paid for the delivery.
* **Customer Name**: (id: 5, type: text) Enter your customer's name.
* **Customer Email**: (id: 6, type: email) Enter your customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone**: (id: 7, type: text) Enter your customer's phone number.
* **Address Line 1**: (id: 8, type: text) Enter the first line of your address.
* **Address Line 2**: (id: 9, type: text) Enter the second line of your address.
* **Payment Status**: (id: 10, type: select_one) Select the status of payment. Choose from Paid or Unpaid.
* **Order Date**: (id: 11, type: date) Enter the date of the order in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Order Time**: (id: 12, type: time) Enter the time of the order in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Customer Notes**: (id: 13, type: text) Provide any notes or comments about the customer.
* **Total Payment**: (id: 14, type: number) Enter the total amount paid for the delivery.
* **Package Number**: (id: 15, type: text) Enter the package number for the delivery.
* **Signature**: (id: 16, type: note) Provide your signature for the delivery.
* **Order Status**: (id: 17, type: select_one) Select the status of the order. Choose from Pending, Shipped, Delivered, or Cancelled.
* **Delivery Address**: (id: 18, type: text) Enter the delivery address.
* **Customer ID**: (id: 19, type: text) Enter your customer's ID.
* **Payment Date**: (id: 20, type: date) Enter the date of payment in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Package Status**: (id: 21, type: select_one) Select the status of the package. Choose from Paid or Unpaid.
* **Delivery Fee**: (id: 22, type: number) Enter the fee for delivery.
* **Package Weight**: (id: 23, type: text) Enter the weight of the package.
* **Customer Zip**: (id: 24, type: text) Enter your customer's zip code.
* **Order Number**: (id: 25, type: text) Enter the order number.
