# Stablecoin Payment Information Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request payment information from stablecoin users. It's intended to collect details such as payment methods, dates, and amounts for various transactions, including subscription renewals, invoices, or other payments.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the correct payment method for your transaction (Card number, Account number, Bank transfer, or Other).
2.  Enter the payment date (mm/dd/yyyy format).
3.  Enter the payment amount (number).
4.  Specify who the payment is for (Individual or Organization).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Method (Card number, Account number, Bank transfer, or Other)**: This field determines the type of payment you are making. Please select the correct payment method to ensure accurate processing.
* **Payment Date (mm/dd/yyyy)**: Enter the date the payment is scheduled for or has been made. Format the date using the mm/dd/yyyy format (month/day/year).
* **Payment Amount (number)**: Enter the amount of the payment. This is a numerical value, so please ensure it's a valid number.
* **Payee Details (Individual or Organization)**: Specify who the payment is for, whether it's an individual or an organization. This helps ensure accurate distribution of the payment.
