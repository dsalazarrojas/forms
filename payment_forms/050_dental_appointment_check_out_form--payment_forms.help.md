# Dental Appointment Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect payment details and confirm appointment information after a dental appointment has taken place. It ensures accurate and comprehensive record-keeping for payment processing and appointment management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Confirm Appointment Details**: Enter or verify the appointment details to ensure accuracy.
2. Choose a **Payment Method**: Select a payment method used during the appointment (Visa, Mastercard, or Amex).
3. Enter **Payment Details**: Provide the payment details, including the type of payment and amount paid.
4. **Confirm Payment**: Confirm whether the payment has been made or not.
5. Confirm the **Appointment**: Confirm whether the appointment was successfully completed or not.
6. **Submit**: Review and submit the form to complete the payment process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Confirm Appointment Details (1)**: Ensure the appointment date, time, and other details are correct.
* **Patient Info (2)**: Enter the patient's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Payment Method (3)**: Select the payment method used during the appointment.
* **Payment Details (4)**: Enter the payment amount, type of payment, and other payment details.
* **Payment Amount (5)**: Enter the amount paid for the appointment.
* **Notes (6)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the appointment or payment.
* **Confirm Payment (7)**: Confirm whether the payment has been made or not.
* **Confirm Appointment (8)**: Confirm whether the appointment was successfully completed or not.
* **Review (9)**: Review the form for any errors or discrepancies before submission.
* **Rating (10)**: Enter a rating of the appointment or service provided.
* **Additional Comments (11)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the appointment or service.
* **Submit (12)**: Review and submit the form to complete the payment process.
* **Confirm Submit (13)**: Confirm that you want to submit the form.
* **Cancel (14)**: Cancel the submission process if any errors or discrepancies are found.
* **Reason (15)**: Enter the reason for cancellation or rejection.
* **Patient Name (16)**: Enter the patient's name.
* **Appointment Date (17)**: Enter the appointment date.
* **Appointment Time (18)**: Enter the appointment time.
* **Doctor Name (19)**: Enter the name of the doctor who attended the appointment.
* **Cinic Address (20)**: Enter the address of the clinic where the appointment took place.
* **Patient Phone (21)**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Email (22)**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Doctor Name 2 (23)**: Enter the name of the doctor who attended the appointment, if different from the first doctor.
* **Appointment Status (24)**: Enter the status of the appointment (e.g. "Confirmed", "Postponed", etc.).
* **Submitter (25)**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Cancel (24)**: Cancel the submission process if any errors or discrepancies are found. 
* **Cancel (25)**: Confirm that you want to cancel the submission process.
