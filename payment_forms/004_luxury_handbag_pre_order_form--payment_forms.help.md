# Luxury Handbag Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Luxury Handbag Pre-Order Form is designed to collect information from customers interested in pre-ordering a luxury handbag. This form is used to gather details such as customer contact information, order details, and payment information to ensure smooth delivery and processing of the pre-order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your desired form title, which will appear on the order confirmation page.
* Enter your Customer Name, which is required for delivery and communication.
* Provide your Customer Email, which will be used for order updates and notifications.
* Enter your Customer Phone number, which will be used for delivery coordination.
* Enter your Customer Address for delivery purposes.
* Select the City where the order will be delivered.
* Choose the State or region where the order will be delivered.
* Select the Country where the order will be delivered.
* Enter your ZIP or Postal Code for delivery purposes.
* Choose a Desired Delivery Date for your pre-ordered item.
* Choose the Desired Delivery Time for your pre-ordered item.
* Enter the Deposit Amount for your pre-ordered item.
* Select the Payment Method used for the pre-order.
* Enter any additional Payment Details if required.
* Enter the Total cost of the pre-ordered item.
* Select the Status of your pre-order (e.g., pending, confirmed, canceled).
* Enter any additional Notes about your pre-order.
* Review and agree to the Terms and Conditions of the sale.
* Review and agree to the Privacy Policy of the sale.
* Review and agree to the Refund Policy of the sale.
* If needed, enter the Refund Reasons.
* If needed, enter the Refund Amount.
* If needed, enter the Refund Date.
* If needed, enter the Refund Time.
* If needed, enter the Refund Total.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a title for your pre-order, which will appear on the order confirmation page.
* **Customer Name** (`form_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name, which will be used for delivery and communication.
* **Customer Email** (`form_email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address, which will be used for order updates and notifications.
* **Customer Phone** (`form_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, which will be used for delivery coordination.
* **Customer Address** (`form_address`, text, optional): Enter your shipping address for delivery purposes.
* **City** (`form_city`, text, optional): Select the city where the order will be delivered.
* **State** (`form_state`, select_one, optional): Choose the State or region where the order will be delivered.
* **Country** (`form_country`, select_multiple, optional): Select the Country where the order will be delivered.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (`form_zip`, text, optional): Enter your ZIP or Postal Code for delivery purposes.
* **Desired Delivery Date** (`form_delivery_date`, date, optional): Choose the date for delivery.
* **Desired Delivery Time** (`form_delivery_time`, time, optional): Choose the time for delivery.
* **Deposit Amount** (`form_deposit_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount to be deposited for the pre-ordered item.
* **Payment Method** (`form_payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select the Payment Method used for the pre-order.
* **Payment Details** (`form_payment_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional Payment Details if required.
* **Total** (`form_total`, text, optional): Enter the total cost of the pre-ordered item.
* **Status** (`form_status`, select_one, optional): Select the Status of your pre-order (e.g., pending, confirmed, canceled).
* **Note** (`form_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional Notes about your pre-order.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`form_terms_and_conditions`, note, optional): Review and agree to the Terms and Conditions of the sale.
* **Privacy Policy** (`form_privacy_policy`, note, optional): Review and agree to the Privacy Policy of the sale.
* **Refund Policy** (`form_refund_policy`, note, optional): Review and agree to the Refund Policy of the sale.
* **Refund Reasons** (`form_refund_reasons`, text, optional): If needed, enter the Refund Reasons.
* **Refund Amount** (`form_refund_amount`, number, optional): If needed, enter the Refund Amount.
* **Refund Date** (`form_refund_date`, date, optional): If needed, enter the Refund Date.
* **Refund Time** (`form_refund_time`, time, optional): If needed, enter the Refund Time.
* **Refund Total** (`form_refund_total`, number, optional): If needed, enter the Refund Total.
