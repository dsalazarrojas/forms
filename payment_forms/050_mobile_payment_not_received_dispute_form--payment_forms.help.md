# Mobile Payment Not Received Dispute Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to dispute a mobile payment that has not been received. It is essential to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that the issue is handled efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select the reason for the dispute from the options provided in the "mobile_payment_details" field. This will help identify the specific issue with the payment.
2.  Choose the relevant details from the "order_details" field, such as the order amount, date, and status, to provide context to the dispute.
3.  Indicate who the dispute should be assigned to from the "user_info" field, whether it's the customer, merchant, support, or someone else.
4.  Clearly type out the reason for the dispute in the "reason_for_dispute" field.
5.  Upload any attachments or supporting documents in the "attachment_1", "attachment_2", and "attachment_3" fields, if applicable.
6.  Enter the order ID in the "order_id" field.
7.  Enter your message to the customer in the "customer_message" field.
8.  Enter your message to the merchant in the "merchant_message" field.
9.  Enter a message to support, if necessary, in the "support_message" field.
10. Set the "dispute_date" to the date of the original dispute.
11. Set the "dispute_time" field to the time of the original dispute.
12. Select the current status of the dispute from the options in the "dispute_status" field.
13. Indicate in the "assigned_to" field who the dispute was assigned to.
14. Set the follow-up status of the dispute in the "follow_up_status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mobile payment details**: Please select the reason why the payment was not received. Choose from "Payment method not received", "Payment method wrong", or "Payment method not recognized".
* **Order details**: This section is about the order in question. Please choose relevant details to help resolve the issue. You can select from "Order amount", "Order date", "Order time", or "Order status".
* **User info**: Please select who this dispute should be assigned to. You can choose "Customer", "Merchant", "Support", or "Other".
* **Reason for dispute**: This is the main description of the dispute. Type out why the payment was not received.
* **Attachment 1, 2, and 3**: You can upload any relevant documents or images to support your dispute.
* **Order ID**: Please enter the ID of the order in dispute.
* **Customer message**: This is your message to the customer. Please be clear and concise.
* **Merchant message**: This is your message to the merchant. Please be professional and objective.
* **Support message**: If this dispute requires support intervention, type a message to explain the situation.
* **Dispute date**: Enter the date of the original dispute.
* **Dispute time**: Enter the time of the original dispute.
* **Dispute status**: Choose the current status of the dispute from the provided options.
* **Assigned to**: Indicate who the dispute was assigned to.
* **Follow-up status**: Set the follow-up status of the dispute to "No follow-up", "Follow-up sent", "Follow-up pending", or "Follow-up completed".
