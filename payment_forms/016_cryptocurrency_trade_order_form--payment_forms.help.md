# cryptocurrency_trade_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cryptocurrency_trade_order_form is used to place orders or trades for cryptocurrencies, allowing you to submit your trade details, preferences, and confirmations for processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your client details.
2. Describe the trade details.
3. Specify your order preferences.
4. Confirm the instructions and details of the trade.
5. Confirm your email address and phone number.
6. Confirm the email and phone number provided.
7. Select the status of your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, required): Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Trade Details** (`trade_details`, text, required): Describe the cryptocurrency trade you wish to make, including the type and amount of cryptocurrency, as well as any other relevant details.
* **Order Preferences** (`order_preferences`, text, required): Specify any special instructions or preferences for your trade, such as urgency or trade type.
* **Clear Instructions** (`clear_instructions`, text, required): Confirm you understand the instructions and details of the trade.
* **Confirm Order** (`confirm_order`, select, required): Confirm that you have reviewed and understood the trade details.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication and validation purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for confirmation and communication.
* **Instructions** (`instructions`, text, required): Repeat and confirm the trade instructions for clarity.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, select, required): Confirm that your email address is correct.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, select, required): Confirm that your phone number is correct.
* **Confirm Phone Number** (`confirm_phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for confirmation.
* **Confirm Email Number** (`confirm_email_number`, email, required): Enter your phone number for confirmation purposes.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select, required): Select the status of your order, such as pending or completed.
