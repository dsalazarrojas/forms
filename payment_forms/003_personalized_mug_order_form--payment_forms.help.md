# Personalized Mug Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personalized Mug Order Form is a tool used to collect customer information and order details for customized mugs. This form is designed to be completed by customers to place their orders for customized mugs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your customer name and email address in the "Customer Name" and "Customer Email" fields, respectively.
2. Provide a phone number in the "Customer Phone" field for us to contact you if needed.
3. In the "Message on Mug" field, enter a personalized message to be printed on your customized mug.
4. Select the color and size of the mug you prefer from the "Mug Color" and "Mug Size" dropdown menus.
5. Choose a payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
6. Enter any additional order notes or customer notes in the "Order Notes" and "Customer Notes" text fields.
7. Review your order details before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be printed on the mug.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address for us to contact you with order updates.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter a phone number for us to contact you if needed.
* **Message on Mug**: Enter a short message to be printed on the customized mug.
* **Mug Color**: Select the color of the mug you prefer.
* **Mug Size**: Select the size of the mug you prefer.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to pay for your customized mug (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
* **Order Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your order.
* **Customer Notes**: Enter any additional notes you would like to share with us.
* **Form Submitted**: This field is used to track when the form was submitted. (System-generated field).
* **Form Submitted Time**: This field is used to track when the form was submitted. (System-generated field).
* **Payment Status**: This field is used to track the status of your payment. (System-generated field).
* **Order Status**: This field is used to track the status of your order. (System-generated field).
* **Order Total**: This field is used to track the total cost of your order. (System-generated field).
* **Customer ID**: This field is used to track your customer ID. (System-generated field).

Note: The fields marked as system-generated are filled in automatically by the system and should not be edited by the user.
