# eye_surgery_billing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "eye_surgery_billing_form" is a template for collecting and managing billing information related to eye surgeries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the patient's information in the "Patient Information" section.
2.  Provide the patient's medical history in the "Medical History" section.
3.  Add any relevant medical history notes if necessary.
4.  Enter the patient's medical record number, insurance information, and insurance ID in the corresponding fields.
5.  Fill in the patient's billing information, including address, phone number, and email.
6.  Ensure you have filled out all required fields and submitted the form for processing.
