# homeless-shelter-improvement-donation-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for those who want to support the improvement of homeless shelters by making a donation. It will help us understand how you'd like to help and what specific improvements you're interested in supporting.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Choose if you're willing to make a one-time or recurring gift.
2. Select the type of upgrades you'd like to support (e.g., Building, Furniture, Technology).
3. Enter the amount of your donation.
4. Decide on the frequency of your donations (e.g., one-time, recurring).
5. Enter the date you'd like to make your donation (if applicable).
6. Choose the time you'd like to make your donation (if applicable).
7. Add any additional notes or comments about your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **One-time or Recurring Gift** (`homeless_shelter_improvement_donation_form`, `select_one`): Choose whether you'd like to make a one-time or recurring gift to support the homeless shelter's improvements. This will help us understand your willingness to commit to regular support.
* **Shelter Upgrades** (`shelter_upgrades`, `select_multiple`): Select from the options to choose the type of upgrade you'd like to support, such as Building, Furniture, or Technology. This will help us understand what specific improvements you'd like to contribute towards.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, `number`): Enter the amount you'd like to donate. This will help us understand how much you're willing to contribute.
* **Donation Frequency** (`frequency`, `select_one`): Choose whether you'd like to make a one-time or recurring gift. This will help us understand when you'd like to make your donation.
* **Donation Date** (`donation_date`, `date`): Enter the date you'd like to make your donation. This will help us understand when you're expecting to make your donation.
* **Donation Time** (`donation_time`, `time`): Choose the time of day you'd like to make your donation. This will help us understand your time preferences.
* **Notes** (`note`, `note`): Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to share about your donation. This will help us understand your thoughts and ideas.
