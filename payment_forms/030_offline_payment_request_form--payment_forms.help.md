# Offline Payment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The offline payment request form is used to collect information from employees for payment requests that cannot be made online. This form is designed to be used when employees need to request payment for orders, salaries, or other purposes not available online.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your User Information on page 1.
2. Provide your Order Number on page 1.
3. Select your preferred Payment Method on page 1.
4. Enter either your Order Number or Salary Reference Number on page 1.
5. Enter the Reason for your Request on page 1.
6. Select the Date of your Request on page 1.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Information** (User Information, text, required/optional): Enter your name, department, and contact information in this field.
- **Order Number** (Order Number, text, required/optional): Enter the order number for which you are requesting payment.
- **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, required/optional): Choose how you would like to receive payment, either by cash or by check.
- **Order Number or Salary Reference Number** (Order Number or Salary Reference Number, text, required/optional): Enter either your order number or salary reference number, or both if applicable.
- **Reason for Request** (Reason for Request, text, required/optional): Explain why you are requesting payment.
- **Date of Request** (Date of Request, date, required/optional): Select the date on which you are requesting payment.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and complete.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or HR department for assistance.
- This form is for offline payment requests only; online payment options are not available for these specific requests.
- Please allow sufficient time for processing and delivery of your payment.
