# stripe_animal_shelter_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donations to support a local animal shelter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
3. Select the **Donation Amount** you wish to give.
4. Choose how often you would like to **Frequency** of your donation (once, monthly, bi-weekly, weekly).
5. If you have a specific **Frequency Repeats** schedule, select from the options provided.
6. Enter any **Notes** or comments you have for your donation.
7. Choose whether you would like to **Opt out of email communication** with the shelter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name for the donation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address to receive updates and communication from the shelter.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount you wish to donate.
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, select_one, required/optional): Select how often you would like to donate (once, monthly, bi-weekly, weekly).
* **Frequency Repeats** (`frequency_repeats`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select from the options to specify how often you would like to repeat your donation (Never, 1 month, 2 months, 3 months, 4 months).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any notes or comments you have for your donation.
* **Opt out of email communication** (`email_opt_out`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you would like to opt-out of email communication with the shelter.
