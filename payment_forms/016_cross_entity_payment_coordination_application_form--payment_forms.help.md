# Cross Entity Payment Coordination Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cross Entity Payment Coordination Application Form is a tool designed to streamline payment coordination between entities. This form is used for submitting and managing payment requests, payment details, and other related information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the entities involved in the payment coordination process.
2. Enter the request date and time for the payment.
3. Specify the payment amount and method.
4. Provide a brief description of the payment request.
5. Enter contact information for the entity making the request.
6. Enter the name and address of the entity receiving the payment.
7. Select the payment status.
8. Add any payment details or reasons for rejection (if applicable).
9. Add any additional notes for the reviewer or submitter.
10. Confirm the submission date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Entity Type** (`Entity Type`, select_multiple, required): Select the entities involved in the payment coordination process. This may include Company A, Company B, or Company C.
* **Request Date** (`Request Date`, date, required): Enter the date when the payment request was made.
* **Request Time** (`Request Time`, time, required): Enter the time when the payment request was made.
* **Payment Amount** (`Payment Amount`, number, required): Specify the amount of payment being requested.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select_one, required): Choose the method of payment, such as Cash, Check, Credit/Debit Card, or Other.
* **Description** (`Description`, text, required): Briefly describe the purpose of the payment request.
* **Contact Name** (`Contact Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person for the payment request.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Entity Name** (`Entity Name`, text): Enter the name of the entity receiving the payment.
* **Entity Address** (`Entity Address`, text): Enter the address of the entity receiving the payment.
* **Entity Postal Code** (`Entity Postal Code`, text): Enter the postal code of the entity receiving the payment.
* **Payment Status** (`Payment Status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the payment, such as Pending, Approved, Rejected, or Paid.
* **Payment Details** (`Payment Details`, text): Add any additional payment details or reasons for rejection (if applicable).
* **Reason for Rejection** (`Reason for Rejection`, text): Enter the reason for rejection (if the payment was rejected).
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note): Add any additional notes for the reviewer or submitter.
* **Submission Date** (`Submission Date`, date): Enter the date when the submission was made.
* **Submission Time** (`Submission Time`, time): Enter the time when the submission was made.
* **Reviewer Name** (`Reviewer Name`, text): Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Email** (`Reviewer Email`, email): Enter the email address of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Phone** (`Reviewer Phone`, text): Enter the phone number of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Notes** (`Reviewer Notes`, note): Add any additional notes for the reviewer.
* **Submitter Name** (`Submitter Name`, text): Enter the name of the submitter.
* **Submitter Email** (`Submitter Email`, email): Enter the email address of the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone** (`Submitter Phone`, text): Enter the phone number of the submitter.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are filled in to ensure proper submission.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all information is accurate.
* If you need assistance with completing the form, please reach out to the relevant contact person for help.
