# Membership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from new members seeking to join the community. It's a required step for onboarding new members and is critical for accurate and efficient processing of their membership application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your membership number, a unique identifier assigned to you by the community.
2. Provide your name as it appears on your identification documents.
3. Enter your address to facilitate communication and delivery of community materials.
4. Enter your email address to stay connected with the community and receive important updates.
5. Provide your phone number to enable direct contact and emergency situations.
6. Indicate your occupational affiliations to help the community tailor services to your needs.
7. List any special skills you have that will aid in your membership or community involvement.
8. Select whether you're interested in participating in any membership options available (check all that apply).
9. Select whether you're interested in using the PayPal payment method for any membership-related expenses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Membership Number**: A unique identifier assigned to you by the community, used for tracking and referencing your membership.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Address**: Enter your current address to receive community materials, updates, and notifications.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address to stay connected with the community and receive important updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number for direct communication and emergency situations.
* **Occupational Affiliations**: Indicate any professional or work-related affiliations you have that may impact your membership involvement.
* **Special Skills**: List any special skills or qualifications you have that can be beneficial to the community or your role within it.
* **Membership Options**: Select any membership options available that you're interested in participating in (check all that apply).
* **PayPal Button**: Select whether you're interested in using the PayPal payment method for any membership-related expenses.
