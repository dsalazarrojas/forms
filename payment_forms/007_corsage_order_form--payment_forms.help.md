# Corsage Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers submit their corsage orders. It's used to collect order information for efficient processing and shipping.

## How To Complete This Form

To use this form, follow these steps:

- Fill out each field with your relevant information.
- Review your completed form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **customer_info**
(customer_info, text, optional) 
Enter your name and contact information for order updates and inquiries.

### 2. **delivery_info**
(delivery_info, text, optional) 
Describe the delivery details such as date, time, and location for your corsage order.

### 3. **corsage_info**
(corsage_info, text, optional)
 Specify the corsage order number, type, or any other relevant details.

### 4. **shipping_info**
(shipping_info, text, optional)
Enter shipping address and any special instructions for the delivery process.

## Tips

- Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
- In case of any issues, please contact form@gic.mx for support.
