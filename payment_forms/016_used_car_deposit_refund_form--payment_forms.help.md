# used_car_deposit_refund_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a deposit refund for a used car purchase.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **customer name** in the field labeled "customer name".
2. Enter the **deposit amount** you are requesting a refund for in the field labeled "deposit amount".
3. Provide the **refund reason** for your request in the field labeled "refund reason".
4. Enter the **vehicle ID** of the car you are requesting a refund for in the field labeled "vehicle id".
5. Enter the **deposit date** of the original deposit in the field labeled "deposit date".
6. Enter the **refund amount** you are requesting in the field labeled "refund amount".
7. Enter the **used car dealer's name** in the field labeled "used car dealer".
8. Add any additional **dealer notes** about your request in the field labeled "dealer notes".
9. Enter your **email address** in the field labeled "customer email".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **deposit amount**: Enter the exact amount you paid as a deposit for the used car.
* **refund reason**: Enter a brief description of why you are requesting a refund.
* **vehicle id**: Enter the vehicle's ID number as assigned by the dealership.
* **deposit date**: Enter the date you made the original deposit for the used car.
* **refund amount**: Enter the exact amount you are requesting as a refund.
* **used car dealer**: Enter the name of the dealership from which you purchased the used car.
* **dealer notes**: Add any additional information about your request that you think would be helpful for the dealership.
* **customer email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you with updates about your refund request.
