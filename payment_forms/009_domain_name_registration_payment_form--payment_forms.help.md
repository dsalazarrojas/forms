# Domain Name Registration Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect domain name registration information and payment details from customers. This form is part of the domain name registration process, allowing customers to provide their domain name and payment method details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your domain name in the "Enter Domain Name" field.
2. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" field.
3. If necessary, provide additional payment details in the "Payment Details" field.
4. Confirm your payment method in the "Confirm Payment" field.
5. Provide domain name details in the "Domain Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter Domain Name** (`domain_name`, text, required): Enter the domain name you want to register. Please make sure to enter it accurately, as any errors may result in your domain name not being registered correctly.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method from the available options. This will determine how you will complete the payment.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, note, optional): If you need to provide additional payment details, such as your account information or invoice number, enter them here.
* **Confirm Payment** (`confirm_payment`, select_one, required): Confirm your payment method selection. This is an important step to ensure your payment is processed correctly.
* **Domain Details** (`domain_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about your domain, such as its purpose or features. This will help our team understand your domain better.
