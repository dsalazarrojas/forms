# Regulatory Violation Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Regulatory Violation Payment Form is used to collect and document payments related to regulatory violations. It is a critical tool for tracking and managing regulatory fines and fees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have access rights to use this form.
2. Select the payment method used for the regulatory violation (e.g., Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Check).
3. Enter the date of the regulatory violation and the date of the payment.
4. Enter the amount received for the payment.
5. Provide a detailed description of the regulatory violation.
6. Select the regulatory violation type(s) that apply.
7. Enter any additional comments related to the payment.
8. Enter the payment status (Pending, Completed, Failed).
9. Enter the details of the payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Regulatory Violation Payment** (regulatory_violation_payment_form): This is the title of the form.
* **Regulatory Violation Date** (regulatory_violation_date): Enter the date of the regulatory violation.
* **Payment Method** (payment_method): Choose the payment method used for the regulatory violation payment.
	+ Choose from: Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Check
* **Amount Received** (amount_received): Enter the amount received for the payment.
* **Regulatory Violation Description** (regulatory_violation_description): Provide a detailed description of the regulatory violation.
* **Payment Status** (payment_status): Select the payment status (Pending, Completed, Failed).
	+ Choose from: Pending, Completed, Failed
* **Email Address** (email_address): Enter the email address of the person making the payment.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number): Enter the phone number of the person making the payment.
* **Payment Amount** (payment_amount): Enter the amount paid for the payment.
* **Payment Details** (payment_details): Enter any additional details related to the payment.
* **Additional Comments** (additional_comments): Provide any additional comments related to the payment (optional).
* **Regulatory Violation Type** (regulatory_violation_type): Select the regulatory violation type(s) that apply.
	+ Choose from: Type 1, Type 2, Type 3
* **Regulatory Violation ID** (regulatory_violation_id): Enter the regulatory violation ID.
* **Submitter Details** (submitter_details): Enter any details related to the person submitting the payment.
* **Submission Date** (submission_date): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Payment Date** (payment_date): Enter the date of the payment.
* **Paid Amount** (paid_amount): Enter the amount paid for the payment.
* **Regulatory Violation Notes** (regulatory_violation_notes): Enter any notes related to the regulatory violation.
* **Regulatory Violation Fines** (regulatory_violation_fines): Enter the fines related to the regulatory violation.
* **Regulatory Violation Fees** (regulatory_violation_fees): Enter the fees related to the regulatory violation.
* **Regulatory Violation Fines Total** (regulatory_violation_fines_total): Enter the total fines related to the regulatory violation.
* **Regulatory Violation Fees Total** (regulatory_violation_fees_total): Enter the total fees related to the regulatory violation.
* **Regulatory Violation Payment Method** (regulatory_violation_payment_method): Enter the payment method used for the regulatory violation payment.
* **Regulatory Violation Payment Status** (regulatory_violation_payment_status): Select the payment status (Pending, Completed, Failed).
	+ Choose from: Pending, Completed, Failed
* **Regulatory Violation Payment Date** (regulatory_violation_payment_date): Enter the date of the payment.
