# Habitat Restoration Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Habitat Restoration Donation Form is designed to collect personal and donation information from users who want to support habitat restoration efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the requested information in each field. Make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Amount Donated** (`amount_donated`, number, optional): Specify the amount of your donation.
* **Payment Type** (`payment_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the payment method you prefer: Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal.
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select how often you would like to donate: One-time, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually.
* **Donation Date** (`donation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like your donation to be processed.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information or comments about your donation.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization you are representing (if applicable).

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact [support email/phone number] for assistance.
* If you are submitting on behalf of an organization, please ensure you have permission to do so from the organization's representative.
