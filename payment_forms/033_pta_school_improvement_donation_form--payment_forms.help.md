# PTA School Improvement Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PTA School Improvement Donation Form is a form designed to accept donations from users to support school improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to make sure you have all the required information.
2. Fill in the General Information page with your basic contact details.
3. On the donation_amount page, enter the amount you wish to donate.
4. On the payment_method page, select the payment option that best suits your needs.
5. If you have any additional notes or comments, you can include them on the notes page.
6. Finally, select the option to submit your form and confirm that you want to email a copy of your form to yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): This page is where you will enter your basic contact details.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, optional): This is where you will enter the amount you wish to donate.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): This is where you will enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This is where you will enter your email address.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): This is where you will enter your contact phone number.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): This is where you will select the payment method you wish to use (e.g., bank transfer, credit card, etc.).
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, optional): This is where you can include any additional payment details.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): This is where you can include any additional comments or notes.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, optional): This is where you will select the option to submit your form.
* **Email Send** (`email_send`, select_one, optional): This is where you will select the option to email a copy of your form to yourself.
