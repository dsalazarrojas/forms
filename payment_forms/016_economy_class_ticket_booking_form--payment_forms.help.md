# Economy Class Ticket Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information for booking economy class tickets. It gathers customer details, payment information, and other essential requirements for the booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's name and contact information (Customer Name, Email, and Phone).
2. Select the ticket date.
3. Enter the passenger's name and contact information.
4. Provide the ticket price.
5. Choose a payment method (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, or PayPal).
6. Enter the card details (card number, card expiry, and cvv).
7. Select a travel agency (if required).
8. Enter flight details (flight number and travel date).
9. Fill in any additional customer notes (if required).
10. Confirm the payment method and card details for the second passenger.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter the full name of the person booking the ticket.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the email address associated with the customer's name.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the phone number for the customer's contact.
* **Ticket Date**: Select the date the ticket is required for.
* **Passenger Name**: Enter the full name of the person traveling on the ticket.
* **Passenger Email**: Enter the email address associated with the passenger's name.
* **Passenger Phone**: Enter the phone number for the passenger's contact.
* **Ticket Price**: Enter the price of the ticket.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method used for the booking (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, or PayPal).
* **Card Number**: Enter the card number for the payment method selected.
* **Card Expiry**: Enter the card expiry date.
* **Cvv**: Enter the security code (cvv) for the card.
* **Cardholder Name**: Enter the name of the cardholder.
* **Customer Address**: Enter the customer's physical address (if required).
* **Ticket Number**: Enter the ticket number or booking reference number.
* **Travel Agency**: Select the travel agency associated with the booking (if required).
* **Flight Number**: Enter the flight number or flight reference number.
* **Travel Date**: Select the date of the travel.
* **Customer Notes**: Fill in any additional customer notes (if required).
* **Payment Method 2**: Select the payment method used for the second passenger (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, or PayPal).
* **Card Number 2**: Enter the card number for the second passenger's payment method.
* **Card Expiry 2**: Enter the card expiry date for the second passenger.
* **Cvv 2**: Enter the security code (cvv) for the second passenger's card.
* **Cardholder Name 2**: Enter the name of the cardholder for the second passenger.
