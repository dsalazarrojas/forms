# Custom Toolkit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Toolkit Order Form is designed for customers to submit their custom requests for toolkits or kits. It requires various details for a successful order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Describe your custom kit needs in the **Made to Order Kit Requests** field.
2. Choose your **Payment Details** by selecting one or more options from the available choices.
3. Add any delivery instructions or notes in the **Delivery Preferences** field (if necessary).
4. Select your **Billing Details** by choosing one option from the available choices.
5. Enter your **Contact Number** for us to contact you.
6. Provide your **Email** address for order updates and communication.
7. Add any additional order information in the **Order Notes** field (if necessary).
8. Provide a custom request or message in the **Custom Request** field.
9. Choose your **Payment Method** by selecting one or more options from the available choices.
10. Finally, add any comments about your order in the **Order Comments** field (if you have any).
