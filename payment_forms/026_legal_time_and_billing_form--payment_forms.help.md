# Legal Time And Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Time And Billing Form is a tool for capturing and reporting time spent on various activities and tasks, providing a clear picture of work hours and costs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether you have payment forms (`Yes` or `No`) from the dropdown options on "Payment Forms" page 1.
2. If you have payment forms, select which ones you want to include on "Time Tracking Forms" page 2.
3. Enter the total hours worked and total billable hours (billed hours) on "Time Tracking Forms" page 3 and 4 respectively.
4. Optionally, you can enter the total cost on "Time Tracking Forms" page 5.
5. Click on "Calculate Totals" on "Time Tracking Forms" page 6 to generate the totals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Forms** (`payment_forms`, Select Multiple, Not Required): Select whether you have payment forms. This field helps in categorizing your activities and tasks.
* **Time Tracking Forms** (`time_tracking_forms`, Select Multiple, Not Required): If you have payment forms, select the relevant time tracking forms related to those forms.
* **Total Hours Worked** (`total_hours_worked`, Number, Not Required): Enter the total hours spent on activities and tasks. This helps in understanding the total time spent.
* **Total Billable Hours** (`total_billable_hours`, Number, Not Required): Enter the total billable hours, i.e., the hours that can be billed to the clients or customers. This helps in calculating the billable cost.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, Number, Not Required): Enter the total cost associated with the activities and tasks. This helps in understanding the overall cost.
* **Calculate Totals** (`calculate_totals`, Note, Not Required): Click on this field to generate the totals of hours and costs spent. This field does not require any input but triggers the calculation.
