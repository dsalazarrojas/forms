# Food Truck Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Truck Check Out Form is used by customers to finalize their orders with our food truck. This form collects essential details for order processing and payment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of your order.
* **Items** (`items`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the items ordered.
* **Subtotal** (`subtotal`, `number`, required): Enter the total cost of the order before tax.
* **Tax** (`tax`, `number`, optional): Enter any additional tax applied to the order (if applicable).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose the method of payment used for this order.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check the box if you want to save payment information for future orders.
* **Total** (`total`, `number`, optional): The total cost of the order including tax (automatically calculated).
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `select_one`, required): Mark the payment status as Active or Inactive.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about the order.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the customer's address.
* **Payment Address** (`payment_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the payment method's address.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the order.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the event.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about the order.
* **Payment Notes** (`payment_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional payment notes.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, `select_one`, required): Mark the order status as Active or Inactive.
* **Customer Status** (`customer_status`, `select_one`, optional): Mark the customer status as Active or Inactive.
* **Order ID** (`order_id`, `text`, required): Enter the unique order ID.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, `text`, optional): Enter the assigned user's name.

Note that this form requires specific information for accurate order processing. Ensure you fill out all required fields and review your answers carefully before submission.
