# VIP Family Lounge Pass Donation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The VIP Family Lounge Pass Donation form is used to collect information from donors for the VIP Family Lounge Pass. This form is used to gather details about the donor and the donation, including donor information, donation details, and recipient information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide donor information in the "Donor Info" field.
2. Enter donation details in the "Donation Info" field.
3. Add recipient information in the "Recipient Info" field.
4. Provide additional details about the donor in the "Donor Details" field.
5. Enter payment information in the "Payment Info" field, if applicable.
6. Add any additional information or comments in the "Note" field.
7. Review the "Form Submission" field for any final comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Info** (Donor Info, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the donor who is making the donation.
* **Donation Info** (Donation Info, text, optional): Provide details about the donation, including the amount and any other relevant information.
* **Recipient Info** (Recipient Info, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the recipient of the donation.
* **Donor Details** (Donor Details, text, optional): Add any additional details about the donor, such as their relationship to the recipient.
* **Payment Info** (Payment Info, text, optional): Enter payment details, including the method and amount of the donation.
* **Note** (Note, note, optional): Add any additional comments or information about the donation.
* **Form Submission** (Form Submission, text, optional): Review and finalize the form submission, ensuring all fields are complete and accurate.
