# Referee Payment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Referee Payment Request Form is designed to collect details about payments made to referees, allowing for efficient and accurate financial record-keeping. This form ensures that all necessary information is gathered in one place.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the payment request details in the "Referee Payment Request Form" field.
2. In the "Referee Name" field, enter the name of the referee who received the payment.
3. Enter the referee's email address in the "Referee Email" field.
4. Provide the referee's phone number in the "Referee Phone" field.
5. Enter the referee's address in the "Referee Address" field.
6. Specify the referee's pay rate in the "Referee Pay Rate" field. Please note that this field accepts numerical values only.
7. Select the currency of the pay rate in the "Referee Pay Rate Currency" field from the available options (AUD, USD, or EUR).
8. Enter the date of the payment in the "Referee Payment Date" field in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
9. Indicate the status of the payment in the "Referee Payment Status" field from the available options (Paid, Pending, or Declined).
10. Enter any additional notes about the payment in the "Referee Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Request** (`referee_payment_request_form`): Enter a brief description of the payment request.
* **Referee Name** (`referee_name`): Enter the name of the referee who received the payment.
* **Referee Email** (`referee_email`): Enter the email address of the referee. This is used to communicate with the referee.
* **Referee Phone** (`referee_phone`): Enter the referee's phone number for easy contact.
* **Referee Address** (`referee_address`): Enter the referee's address for reference.
* **Referee Pay Rate** (`referee_pay_rate`): Enter the referee's pay rate for the service provided.
* **Referee Pay Rate Currency** (`referee_pay_rate_currency`): Select the currency of the pay rate (AUD, USD, or EUR).
* **Referee Payment Date** (`referee_payment_date`): Enter the date of the payment in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Referee Payment Status** (`referee_payment_status`): Indicate the status of the payment (Paid, Pending, or Declined).
* **Referee Note** (`referee_note`): Enter any additional notes or comments about the payment.
