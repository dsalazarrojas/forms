# express_package_pickup_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information for express package pickup bookings. Please provide your details for a package pickup at a specified date and time.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the First Name field with your name as it appears on official documents.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Provide your Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
5. Select the Pickup Date for your package.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, Required/Optional): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, Required/Optional): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
- **Email** (`email`, email, Required/Optional): Enter your valid email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, Required/Optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, date, Required/Optional): Select the date for your package pickup.
