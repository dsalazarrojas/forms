# Cultural Integration Program Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cultural Integration Program Donation Form is designed to collect information for processing donations to the program. The form is a critical component in ensuring accurate and efficient processing of donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your email address in the Email field.
3. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
4. Select your preferred Payment Method from the options provided.
5. Enter the Donation Amount you wish to donate.
6. Enter any additional Notes about your donation in the Notes field.
7. Confirm your donation details by selecting 'True' for each of the confirm fields.
