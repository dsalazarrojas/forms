# Service Invoice Generator - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Service Invoice Generator form is designed to create a service invoice for a client, including details such as client information, service type, and payment status. This form helps to generate a professional invoice that can be used for billing and accounting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Client Information**
	* Enter the client's name in the **Client Name** field.
	* Enter the client's email address in the **Client Email** field.
	* Enter the client's phone number in the **Client Phone** field.
2. **Enter Service Details**
	* Enter the date of the service in the **Invoice Date** field.
	* Enter the time of the service in the **Invoice Time** field.
	* Select the **Service Type** from the available options.
	* Enter a brief description of the service provided in the **Service Description** field.
3. **Enter Financial Details**
	* Enter the total cost of the service in the **Total Cost** field.
	* Select the payment method from the available options in the **Payment Method** field.
	* Select the payment status from the available options in the **Payment Status** field.
4. **Additional Information**
	* Enter any additional notes or comments in the **Notes** field.
	* Enter the form submitted by information in the **Form Submitted By** field.
5. **Review and Submit**
	* Review all the fields to ensure they are accurate and complete.
	* Submit the form to generate the service invoice.
