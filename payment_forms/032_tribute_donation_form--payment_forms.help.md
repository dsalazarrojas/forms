# tribute_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tribute Donation Form is used to record a one-time or recurring donation made in tribute to a particular person or entity. It collects essential information about the donor and the gift details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Enter your donor information:
	* **Doner**: Type a name or a brief description of the person making the donation.
2. Select your gift details:
	* **Tribute Name**: Enter the name of the person or entity being honored.
	* **Gift Amount**: Enter the amount of the donation.
	* **Gift Frequency**: Choose how often you'd like to make this gift (Monthly or One-time).
3. Add a message or dedication:
	* **Dedication Message**: Optionally, add a personal message or dedication for the person or entity being honored.
4. Provide your contact information:
	* **Donor Name**: Enter your name as the person making the donation.
	* **Donor Email**: Enter your email address for contact and confirmation purposes.
	* **Donor Phone**: Optionally, enter your phone number for further contact.
5. Choose your payment method:
	* **Payment Method**: Select the method for making the donation (Credit Card or Bank Transfer).
6. Finalize your gift:
	* **Payment Details**: Enter any additional payment details, such as payment method or account numbers.
	* **Gift Frequency How Often**: Confirm how often you'd like to make this gift (Monthly or One-time).
	* **Gift Frequency When**: Enter the date for the first gift payment (Monthly or One-time).

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out all required fields (Doner, Donor Name, Donor Email, Payment Method, Gift Frequency, Gift Frequency When).
* Double-check your contact information and payment details for accuracy.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact our support team for assistance.
