# Burn Unit Expansion Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Burn Unit Expansion Donation Form is used to collect donation information from donors who wish to contribute to the burn unit expansion project.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps in the "How To Complete This Form" section above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address where you would like to receive your receipt.
* **Donation Amount (USD)**: Enter the amount you wish to contribute. Please use numerical values only.
* **Donation Frequency**: Select the frequency of your donation from the options provided.
* **Fund Allocation Preference**: Select the specific area of the expansion where you would like your donation to be allocated.
* **Is this a tribute gift**: Indicate if this gift is a tribute gift.
* **Tribute Recipient Name**: If this is a tribute gift, enter the name of the person being honored or remembered.
* **Street Address**: Enter your billing address.
* **City, State, and Zip Code**: Enter your city, state, and zip code.
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method.
* **Keep my donation anonymous**: Check this box if you would like to keep your donation anonymous.

## Tips

* Please enter all required fields for secure processing.
* Make sure to select a valid payment method to avoid processing issues.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
