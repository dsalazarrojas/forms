The form purpose is to collect donation information from donors who wish to contribute to the burn unit expansion project. This form is used to gather donor contact and demographic information, as well as details about the gift, such as the amount, frequency, and allocation preference. The form also includes questions to determine if the donation is a tribute gift, the type of tribute gift, and if the donor wishes to keep their donation anonymous.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your full name, email address, and phone number (if applicable) in the donor identification section.
2. Select the donation amount you wish to contribute.
3. Choose the frequency of your donation (one-time, monthly, quarterly, or annual).
4. Select the fund allocation preference for your donation.
5. Indicate if this is a tribute gift and choose the type of tribute gift.
6. If applicable, provide the name of the person being honored or remembered.
7. Enter your billing address and payment method preference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Donor Identification --**: This section is used to gather your contact information for tax purposes and to send your receipt.
* **Donor Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address where you would like to receive your receipt.
* **Contact Phone Number**: If you would like to be contacted by us to discuss your donation or for large gift verification, please enter your phone number.
* **Donation Amount (USD)**: Enter the amount you wish to contribute. Please use numerical values only.
* **Donation Frequency**: Select the frequency of your donation from the options provided:
	+ One-time Gift: A one-time gift is a single donation.
	+ Monthly Recurring: A monthly recurring gift is a regular donation made on a monthly basis.
	+ Quarterly Recurring: A quarterly recurring gift is a regular donation made on a quarterly basis.
	+ Annual Gift: An annual gift is a single donation made once a year.
* **Fund Allocation Preference**: Select the specific area of the expansion where you would like your donation to be allocated:
	+ General Expansion Fund: Supports the overall expansion project.
	+ Advanced Equipment Purchase: Supports the purchase of new equipment for the expansion.
	+ Patient Rehabilitation Area: Supports patient rehabilitation efforts.
	+ Family Support Center: Supports the family support center.
	+ Research and Training: Supports research and training initiatives.
* **Is this a tribute gift**: Indicate if this gift is a tribute gift.
* **Tribute Recipient Name**: If this is a tribute gift, please enter the name of the person being honored or remembered.
* **-- Billing Address --**: This section is required for card verification purposes.
* **Street Address**: Enter your billing address.
* **City, State, and Zip Code**: Enter your city, state, and zip code.
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method:
	+ Credit/Debit Card: Uses your credit or debit card to process your donation.
	+ Electronic Check (ACH): Transfers funds directly from your bank account.
	+ PayPal: Uses PayPal to process your donation.
	+ Send Check by Mail: Sends a check to our mailing address.
* **Keep my donation anonymous**: Check this box if you would like to keep your donation anonymous. If checked, your name will not appear on public donor lists.

Note: This form requires a secure payment method to process your donation.
