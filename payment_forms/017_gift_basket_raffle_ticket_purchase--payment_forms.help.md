# Gift Basket Raffle Ticket Purchase - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gift Basket Raffle Ticket Purchase form is designed to collect information from customers interested in participating in a gift basket raffle. The form helps the organization to gather the required details from customers, including their name, preferred payment method, and the number of tickets they would like to purchase. This form is typically filled out by customers before attending a raffle event or during the event itself.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Enter Your Names" field, selecting the correct option for your name.
2. Indicate the number of tickets you would like to purchase in the "How Many Tickets" field.
3. Enter the amount you would like to spend in the "Amount To Spend" field.
4. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Gift Basket Raffle Ticket Purchase Form**: Enter your name in the form to participate in the raffle.
  - **Enter Your Names**: Select your preferred name option (e.g., Option 1, Option 2).
  - **How Many Tickets**: Enter the number of tickets you would like to purchase (e.g., 1, 2, 3).
  - **Amount To Spend**: Enter the amount you are willing to spend on the gift basket raffle (e.g., $100, $500).
  - **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method (e.g., Payment method 1, Payment method 2).
