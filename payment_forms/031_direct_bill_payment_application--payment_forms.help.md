# Direct Bill Payment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Direct Bill Payment Application form is designed to collect billing information and payment terms from applicants. This form helps the applicant provide necessary details for a smooth payment process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Billing Details** page (page 1).
   This page collects essential billing information.
   
2. On the **Applicant info**, **Applicant contact**, and **Billing info** pages, provide your personal and contact details.

3. Review the **Payment terms** and **Direct bill terms** pages carefully and fill out as needed.

4. Finally, submit your form and a copy will be sent to the applicant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Billing Details** (Billing details, text, required): Enter your billing address, including your name, address, phone number, and any other relevant billing information.
* **Applicant info** (Applicant info, text, required): Enter your name and any other personal details as required.
* **Applicant contact** (Applicant contact, text, required): Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
* **Billing info** (Billing info, text, required): Enter your billing address, including your name and address.
* **Payment terms** (Payment terms, text, required): Review and fill out the payment terms as necessary.
* **Direct bill terms** (Direct bill terms, text, required): Review and fill out the direct bill terms as necessary.
* **Review and sign** (Review and sign, text, required): Carefully review the form, fill out any remaining fields, and sign the document.
* **Submit** (Submit, text, required): Submit the completed form to receive a copy.

## Tips

- Please ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting.
- Double-check the form for any errors or missing information.
- Review the payment and direct bill terms carefully before signing.
