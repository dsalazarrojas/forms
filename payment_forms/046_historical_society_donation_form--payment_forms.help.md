# Historical Society Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Historical Society Donation Form is a tool used to collect donations and track donor information for the Historical Society. Donations are crucial for supporting the society's activities and initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the amount you wish to donate in the "Donation Amount" field.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Donor Information" field.
3. Enter your name in the "Donor Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Donor Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Donor Phone" field.
6. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
7. Provide any additional comments or information in the "Donor Comment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Amount** (Number): Enter the amount you wish to donate to the Historical Society.
* **Donor Information** (Note): Provide a brief description of yourself, including any relevant details about your donation.
* **Donor Name** (Text): Enter your full name as you would like it to be associated with your donation.
* **Donor Email** (Email): Enter your email address where you can be contacted for further information.
* **Donor Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for further information.
* **Payment Method** (Select One): Choose how you would like to make your payment: Stripe, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
* **Donor Comment** (Text): Provide any additional comments or information about your donation.
