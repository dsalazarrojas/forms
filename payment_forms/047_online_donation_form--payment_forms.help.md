# Online Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Donation Form is designed to collect contact information and details about the donation from individuals wishing to make an online donation to our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2.  Enter your Phone Number.
3.  Enter the Donation Amount (this can be adjusted to your preference).
4.  Select your preferred Payment Method(s) (you can choose one or multiple methods).
5.  If you have any additional Notes or comments regarding your donation, enter them in the Notes field.
6.  Confirm your donation by selecting "True" from the Confirm Donation dropdown.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it is known to you.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it is known to you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you wish to donate. This can be adjusted to your preference.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple payment methods that you are comfortable with. This includes:
	+ **Cash**: A one-time payment made in cash.
	+ **Check**: A payment made via check, which will be mailed to our organization.
	+ **Credit Card**: A payment made using your credit card.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or notes regarding your donation, you may enter them here.
* **Confirm Donation** (`confirm_donation`, select_one, optional): To confirm your donation, select "True" from the dropdown.
