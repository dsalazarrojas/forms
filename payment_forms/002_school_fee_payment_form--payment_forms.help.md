# School Fee Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from parents for school fee payments, enabling the school to process payments efficiently and maintain accurate records.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required and optional information as instructed.
2. Ensure that you answer all the questions accurately and truthfully.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure there are no errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Fee Payment** (`school_fee_payment_form`, text, **Required**):
Provide the amount of school fee to be paid.
* **School Fee Details** (`school_fee_details`, text, **Optional**):
Enter any additional details or comments regarding the school fee payment.
* **School Supplies** (`school_supplies`, text, **Optional**):
List any school supplies that need to be purchased for your child.
* **School Swags** (`school_swags`, select_multiple, **Optional**):
Select if you would like to purchase school swags. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, **Optional**):
Enter any additional information regarding the order.
* **Order Quantity** (`order_quantity`, number, **Optional**):
Enter the quantity of the order.
* **Order Price** (`order_price`, number, **Optional**):
Enter the price of the order.
* **Parent Details** (`parent_details`, text, **Optional**):
Enter any additional comments or details regarding your payment.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, **Optional**):
Enter your name as a parent/guardian.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, **Required**):
Enter your email address.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, **Optional**):
Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the school administration for guidance.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid errors.
