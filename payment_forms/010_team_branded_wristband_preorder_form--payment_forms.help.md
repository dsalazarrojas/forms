# Team Branded Wristband Preorder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a team-branded wristband preorder. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth and successful order process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose your desired branded color for the wristband.
2. Select the size(s) you need for your order.
3. Enter the quantity of wristbands you need.
4. Enter your email address and contact phone number so we can get in touch with you about your order.
5. Provide a valid date for the event or occasion for which you're ordering the wristbands.
6. If you have any additional information or special requests for your order, please type them in the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Branded Color** (optional): Select the color of your team-branded wristband from the dropdown menu. Please choose from red, blue, or green.
* **Wristband Size** (optional): Select the size(s) you need for your order. You can choose from Small, Medium, and Large.
* **Quantity** (optional): Enter the number of wristbands you wish to order.
* **Email** (optional): Enter a valid email address so we can get in touch with you about your order.
* **Phone** (optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date** (optional): Enter the date for the event or occasion for which you're ordering the wristbands.
* **Additional Information** (optional): If you have any additional information or special requests for your order, please type them in this field.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you select the correct color and size(s) for your order.
* Enter accurate and valid information in the "Email" and "Phone" fields to ensure we can contact you about your order.
* If you have any special requests or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.

Note: Please keep in mind that all fields are optional, except for the "Branded Color" and "Quantity" fields, which are required if you choose to order something.
