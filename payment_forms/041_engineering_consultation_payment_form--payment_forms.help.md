# engineering_consultation_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture payment information for engineering consultations. It helps track payments or invoices related to engineering services, allowing users to record and manage payment data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the field labels.
2. Select multiple options for "Consultation Services" by holding down the control key (or command key on Mac) while clicking on the options.
3. Enter the "Payment Amount" as a numerical value.
4. Select the "Payment Method" and "Payment Status" from the available options.
5. Enter the "Date of Service" and "Payment Date" using the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
6. Enter a "Note" if necessary.
7. Enter a valid "E-mail" address.
8. Enter a valid "Phone" number.
9. Select the "Payment Status" and ensure it matches the current status of the payment.
10. Click "Submit" to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the service or consultation being paid for.
* **Consultation Services** (`consultation_services`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select all relevant services related to the consultation.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, Number, Optional): Enter the amount paid for the consultation services.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, Select One, Optional): Select the method of payment used (e.g., Bank Transfer, Credit Card, etc.).
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, Date, Optional): Enter the date the services were rendered.
* **Time of Service** (`time_of_service`, Time, Optional): Enter the time the services were rendered.
* **Note** (`note`, Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the payment.
* **E-mail** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the person who made the payment.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the person who made the payment.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, Select One, Optional): Select the status of the payment (e.g., Draft, Paid, Canceled).
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date the payment was made.

## Tips

* Double-check the accuracy of your input before submitting the form.
* Ensure you fill in all required fields correctly.
* Use the correct date format (YYYY-MM-DD) for dates.
* Use the correct phone number format (###-###-####).
