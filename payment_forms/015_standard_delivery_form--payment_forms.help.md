# Standard Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture customer and delivery details for standard delivery operations, ensuring all necessary information is collected for efficient and effective delivery processes.

## How To Complete This Form

- Please fill in all the required fields with accurate and complete information about the customer and delivery.
- Ensure you've checked the correct options for delivery method, status, and type, as these selections are critical for our delivery operations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, Text, Required): Enter the customer's name and contact details to identify the person or organization making the request.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, Text, Required): Provide the delivery address where the items will be sent to.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, Date, Required): Select the date you expect the delivery to be made on.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, Time, Required): Choose the time you'd like the delivery to take place at.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, Text, Required): Enter any applicable payment information, such as invoices or payment references.
* **Delivery Notes** (`delivery_notes`, Text, Required): Add any additional delivery notes or messages for the customer.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, Email, Required): Enter the customer's email address for correspondence and updates.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, Text, Required): Provide the customer's phone number for potential contact.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, Select One, Required): Select how the delivery will be made (e.g., in-person, mail, etc.).
* **Estimated Delivery Time** (`estimated_delivery_time`, Number, Required): Indicate the estimated time for delivery.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose the personnel or team member responsible for the delivery.
* **Assigned Status** (`assigned_status`, Select One, Required): Select the status of the delivery assignment (e.g., pending, in progress, etc.).
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, Text, Required): Enter the customer's name for reference and communication.
* **Customer Message** (`customer_message`, Text, Optional): If you have a message or note for the customer, include it here.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, Select One, Required): Select the method of payment used for the delivery (e.g., cash, credit card, etc.).
* **Customer Comment** (`customer_comment`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes for the customer.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, Text, Required): Have the customer sign off on the delivery.
* **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, Text, Required): Enter the customer's identification number or ID.
* **Delivery Note** (`delivery_note`, Text, Required): Add any additional delivery notes or information for the customer.
* **Delivery Status** (`delivery_status`, Select One, Required): Select the status of the delivery (e.g., pending, completed, etc.).
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, Text, Required): Enter the customer's address for delivery purposes.
* **Delivery Type** (`delivery_type`, Select One, Required): Choose the type of delivery (e.g., standard, expedited, etc.).
* **Delivery Note 2** (`delivery_note_2`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional delivery notes or information for the customer.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments for the customer.
* **Assigned At** (`assigned_at`, Time, Required): Indicate the time the delivery was assigned to a team member.
* **Customer Signature 2** (`customer_signature_2`, Text, Optional): Not applicable in this guide but could be used for future expansions.
