# Job Fair Registration Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect payment information for job seekers and employers participating in a job fair event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form fields to ensure you have all necessary information ready.
2. Fill out the form fields according to the required and optional fields marked.
3. Double-check your details before submitting the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Event Organizer Info** (text)
	* A text field to input the event organizer's information.
### 2. **Event Title** (text)
	* Enter the title of the event you are registering for.
### 3. **Job Seeker Info** (text)
	* Provide your job seeker information, including your name and contact details.
### 4. **Employer Info** (text)
	* Enter the name and contact details of your employer or organization.
### 5. **Job Fair Info** (text)
	* Enter the details of the job fair event, including the location and any other relevant information.
### 6. **Registration Type** (select one)
	* Choose whether you are registering as a job seeker or employer.
### 7. **Payment Amount** (number)
	* Enter the payment amount due for the job fair registration.
### 8. **Payment Method** (select one)
	* Choose your preferred payment method, such as cash, credit card, or bank transfer.
### 9. **Job Fair Date** (date)
	* Enter the date of the job fair event.
### 10. **Job Fair Time** (time)
	* Enter the time of the job fair event.
### 11. **Job Fair Location** (text)
	* Enter the location of the job fair event.

## Tips

* Ensure you have all necessary information ready before filling out the form.
* Double-check your details before submitting the form for processing.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the form instructions or seek assistance from the event organizers.
