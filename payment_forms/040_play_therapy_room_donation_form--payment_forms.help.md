# play_therapy_room_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by organizations to provide their donation information for the play therapy room.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the relevant information about your organization's donation. All fields are optional, so you can skip any field that does not apply to your donation.
