# trash_can_strap_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect general information, payment details, strap information, and shipping details for ordering a product that attaches to a trash can or a similar use case. Please follow this guide to complete the form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and provide the required information.
2. Fill out the **General Info** section with your name, email, and any other relevant details.
3. Enter your **Payment Info**, including the payment method and amount.
4. Specify your **Strap Info**, such as the strap size and type.
5. Provide your **Shipping Info**, including the shipping address and any additional instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Info** (`general_info`, text, required: false): This section is for providing your name, email, and any other relevant general information.
- **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, required: false): Enter your payment method and amount to complete the transaction.
- **Strap Info** (`strap_info`, text, required: false): Specify the strap size and type to ensure accurate ordering.
- **Shipping Info** (`shipping_info`, text, required: false): Enter your shipping address and any additional instructions for delivery.

## Tips
- Double-check your input for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Make sure to provide all required information for successful ordering.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team for assistance.
