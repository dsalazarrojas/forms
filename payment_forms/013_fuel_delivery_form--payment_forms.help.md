# Fuel Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fuel Delivery Form is designed to collect customer information and delivery details for fuel delivery services. It helps the station staff to log customer information, track orders, and improve customer experience.


## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out each section as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
- Start by filling out the Customer Details section.
- Move on to the Delivery Address section, providing as much detail as possible.
- Enter payment information to complete the payment process.
- Provide any additional delivery notes in the Delivery Info section.
- Finally, add any order notes or comments in the Order Notes section.


## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Details** (`Customer Details`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
  - This field is meant to capture the customer's identity and contact information for future reference.
- **Delivery Address** (`Delivery Address`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's delivery address as accurately as possible.
  - This field is used to track the delivery location and ensure accurate delivery service.
- **Payment Info** (`Payment Info`, text, required/optional): Enter payment method and any additional payment details if applicable.
  - This field is used for payment processing and tracking.
- **Delivery Info** (`Delivery Info`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional delivery information or instructions for the delivery driver.
  - This field allows the driver to know what to expect during the delivery process.
- **Order Notes** (`Order Notes`, text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes regarding the order.
  - This field helps the driver to understand any special requests or instructions for the delivery.
