# Payment Voucher Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payment Voucher Form Template is designed to capture payment voucher information from vendors, including payment method, amount, date, and status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are filling out the correct form for your company's payment system.
2. Select the relevant payment method (Bank, Cheque, Online Payment, or Other) that was used to make the payment.
3. Enter the voucher amount, date, and time in the corresponding fields.
4. Provide any additional details about the payment voucher, if necessary.
5. Review and verify that all the information is accurate and complete before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Voucher Form**: 
This is the main form where you enter payment voucher details.
* **Vendors Details**: 
Enter the details of the vendor receiving the payment.
* **Payment Method**: 
Select the payment method used (Bank, Cheque, Online Payment, or Other).
* **Voucher Amount**: 
Enter the amount of the payment voucher.
* **Voucher Date**: 
Enter the date of the payment voucher.
* **Voucher Time**: 
Enter the time of the payment voucher.
* **Voucher Note**: 
Enter any additional notes about the payment voucher.
* **Vendor Email**: 
Enter the email address of the vendor.
* **Vendor Phone**: 
Enter the phone number of the vendor.
* **Voucher Status**: 
Select the status of the payment voucher (Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid).
* **Payment Status**: 
Select the status of the payment (Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid).
* **Payment Method Details**: 
Enter any additional details about the payment method.
* **Payment Refund**: 
Enter any refund details related to the payment voucher.
* **Payment Voucher Refund**: 
Enter any refund details related to the payment voucher.
* **Payment Voucher Reimbursed Refund**: 
Enter any refund details for the reimbursed payment voucher.
* **Additional Voucher Details**: 
Enter any additional details about the payment voucher.
* **Voucher Total**: 
Enter the total amount of the payment voucher.
* **Vendor Total**: 
Enter the total amount of the payment voucher for the vendor.
* **Vendor Voucher Details**: 
Enter any details about the vendor's voucher.
* **Vendor Voucher Amount**: 
Enter the amount of the vendor's voucher.
* **Vendor Voucher Date**: 
Enter the date of the vendor's voucher.
* **Vendor Voucher Time**: 
Enter the time of the vendor's voucher.
* **Vendor Voucher Note**: 
Enter any notes about the vendor's voucher.
* **Vendor Voucher Status**: 
Select the status of the vendor's payment voucher (Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid).
