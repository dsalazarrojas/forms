# Food Truck Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Truck Order Form is a simple order form designed to capture customer information and order details for customers to place an order with a food truck.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Customer Name** field.
2. Provide your **Phone Number** and **Customer Email** fields if required.
3. Enter your **Delivery Address** if required.
4. In the **Order Notes** field, enter any specific instructions or notes about your order.
5. Select your preferred **Payment Method** from the available options.
6. Choose the **Order Status** of your order.
7. Enter the date and time of your order in the **Date of Order** and **Time of Order** fields, if required.
8. Click submit to place your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID or as you would like to be addressed.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number if required for delivery or order confirmation.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address if required for order updates or delivery notifications.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter the address where you would like your order delivered.
* **Order Notes**: Enter any special requests or instructions for your order.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you would like to use for your order. Options are:
	+ Credit Card
	+ PayPal
* **Order Status**: Select the status of your order from the available options:
	+ New
	+ Placed
	+ Shipped
	+ Delivered
	+ Cancelled
* **Date of Order**: Enter the date you would like to place your order.
* **Time of Order**: Enter the time you would like to place your order (optional, 24 hour time format).
* **Order Total**: This field is reserved for the total cost of your order, and will be populated automatically.
