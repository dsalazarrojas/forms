# Soundproofing Material Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soundproofing Material Order Form is used to gather information from customers for the purpose of placing an order for soundproofing materials. Please complete the form with the required information to ensure that your order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Customer Information**: Fill in your personal details, including your name and any other relevant information.
2. **Soundproofing Materials**: Select the soundproofing materials you wish to order. Choose multiple materials if necessary.
3. **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of each material you wish to order.
4. **Unit Price**: Enter the unit price of each material you have selected.
5. **Payment Info**: Enter any relevant payment information.
6. **Billing Address**: Enter your billing address to ensure that we can deliver your order correctly.
7. **Customer Email**: Enter your email address to allow us to contact you regarding your order.
8. **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
9. **Payment Method**: Select how you wish to pay for your order (Credit, Debit, or PayPal).
10. **Order Notes**: Enter any additional notes or instructions for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information (Text)**: Enter your name and any other relevant personal details.
* **Soundproofing Materials (Select Multiple)**: Choose one or multiple materials you wish to order.
* **Quantity (Number)**: Enter the quantity of each material you wish to order.
* **Unit Price (Number)**: Enter the unit price of each material you have selected.
* **Payment Info (Text)**: Enter any relevant payment information.
* **Billing Address (Text)**: Enter your address to ensure delivery of your order.
* **Customer Email (Email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Customer Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Payment Method (Select One)**: Choose how you wish to pay for your order.
* **Order Notes (Note)**: Enter any additional notes or instructions for your order.
