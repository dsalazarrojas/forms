# House Rent Receipt Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a house rent receipt, which is a document that confirms the payment of rent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the tenant details section with the required information.
2. Fill out the owner details section with the required information.
3. Select the correct date of rent receipt.
4. Enter the rent amount.
5. Select the payment terms that apply to the rent payment.
6. Enter the rent period.
7. Provide a signature.
8. Enter the date of issue rent (if applicable).
9. Enter the issue rent amount (if applicable).
10. Select the payment terms for issue rent (if applicable).
11. Enter the issue rent period (if applicable).
12. Add any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <table>
* **Tenant Details** (text, optional): Enter the details of the tenant, including name and contact information.
* **Owner Details** (text, optional): Enter the details of the owner, including name and contact information.
* **Date Rent Received** (date, optional): Select the date the rent was received.
* **Rent Amount** (number, optional): Enter the amount of rent paid.
* **Payment Terms** (select_multiple, optional): Select the payment terms that apply, such as Rent, Advance, or Security Deposit.
* **Rent Period** (number, optional): Enter the period of rent paid.
* **Signature** (text, optional): Provide a signature to confirm the rent payment.
* **Owner Signature** (text, optional): Provide a signature from the owner to confirm the rent payment.
* **Date Issue Rent** (date, optional): Select the date the issue rent was received.
* **Issue Rent Amount** (number, optional): Enter the amount of issue rent paid.
* **Payment Terms for Issue Rent** (select_multiple, optional): Select the payment terms that apply, such as Rent, Advance, or Security Deposit.
* **Issue Rent Period** (number, optional): Enter the period of issue rent paid.
* **Notes** (text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Use the correct date formats (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD) for date fields.
* Use the correct payment terms and rent periods for accurate calculations.
