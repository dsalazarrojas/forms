# Crowdfunding Pledge Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used by a supporter to claim their pledge amount after a crowdfunding campaign has been successful. It captures the supporter's details and the amount they pledged, and allows them to provide additional information if needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the `Amount Captured`, which is the amount that was actually collected from you.
2.  Enter the `Amount Requested`, which is the amount you originally pledged to the campaign.
3.  Fill in the `Supporter Details`, `Supporter Name`, `Supporter Email`, and `Supporter Address` fields with your relevant information.
4.  You may optionally enter a `Supporter Phone` and `Supporter Note` if you have any additional details you'd like to provide.
5.  Click "Submit" to complete the form and claim your pledge amount.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Pledge Amount**: The amount that was actually collected from you.
*   **Amount Captured**: The amount that was actually collected from the supporter.
*   **Amount Requested**: The amount you originally pledged to the campaign.
*   **Supporter Details**: A free text field for any additional information you'd like to provide.
*   **Supporter Name**: Your name as the supporter.
*   **Supporter Email**: Your email address as the supporter.
*   **Supporter Phone**: Your phone number as the supporter (optional).
*   **Supporter Address**: Your address as the supporter.
*   **Supporter Note**: A free text field for any additional notes you'd like to provide (optional).
