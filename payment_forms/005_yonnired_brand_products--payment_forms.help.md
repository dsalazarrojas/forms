# YonniRed Brand Products - Help Guide
## Purpose
The YonniRed Brand Products form is designed to collect information related to payments made by customers for products. It serves as a platform for recording and tracking payment details, including customer information, payment methods, and product pricing. This form helps in maintaining accurate records and ensures that all payment transactions are properly documented.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Customer Name** field with the name of the person making the payment.
2. Enter the **Product Price** field with the price of the product being paid for.
3. Select the **Payment Method** field from the options provided (Cash App, PayPal, or Stripe).
4. Enter the **Send Total** field with the total amount to be sent to the customer (if applicable).
5. Enter the **Receive Total** field with the total amount received from the customer (if applicable).
6. Select the **Payment Status** field with the current status of the payment (Paid, Pending, or Cancelled).
7. Enter any additional information in the **Customer Message** field if necessary.
8. If needed, select the **Send Message** field to choose who to send the message to (Customer or Shopkeeper).
9. Enter any additional notes in the **Payment Note** field.
10. Enter the **Customer Email** field with the customer's email address.
11. Enter the **Customer Phone** field with the customer's phone number.
12. Enter the payment date in the **Payment Date** field.
13. Enter the payment time in the **Payment Time** field.
14. Enter the **Product ID** field with the ID of the product being paid for (if applicable).
15. Enter the **Form ID** field with the ID of the form (if applicable).
16. Enter the **Payment Amount** field with the total payment amount.
17. Enter the **Payment User** field with the ID of the user making the payment.
18. Enter the **Customer Address** field with the customer's address.
19. Enter the **Payment Status Note** field with any additional notes on the payment status.
20. Select the **Customer Country** field from the options provided (USA or Canada).
21. Select the **Customer Province** field from the options provided (Province 1 or Province 2).
22. Enter the **Customer City** field with the customer's city.
23. Enter any additional notes in the **Payment Method Note** field.
24. Enter the customer's zip code in the **Customer Zip** field.
25. Enter any additional notes in the **Customer Notes** field.
