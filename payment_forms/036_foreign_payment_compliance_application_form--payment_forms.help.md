# Foreign Payment Compliance Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to manage and track foreign payments, ensuring that they are processed and approved according to internal regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your payment information in the `Payment Information` section.
2. Select the `Sender Information` options that apply to your payment.
3. Fill out the `Foreign Payment Details` section with relevant information.
4. Choose the date and time of approval for your payment in the `Approval Date/Time` section.
5. Select the `Payment Status` and `Payment Method` options that apply to your payment.
6. Enter any additional details or notes for your payment in the `Payment Description` and `Approval Notes` sections.
7. Select the `Foreign Country`, `Foreign Currency`, and `Foreign Bank` options that apply to your payment.
8. Enter any relevant details for the `Foreign Bank`, `Foreign Swift`, and `Foreign BIC` fields.
9. Finally, review and confirm your entries before submitting the form.
