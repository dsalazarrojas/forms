# Airline Priority Boarding Purchase Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Airline Priority Boarding Purchase Form is designed for passengers to purchase priority boarding upgrades for their flights. This guide will walk you through each field of the form, ensuring a smooth and secure experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Airline Priority Boarding Purchase Form, follow the step-by-step instructions below:

1.  Fill out the form with the required fields (marked as `required` in the form).
2.  Make sure to enter the correct information for your flight and payment details.
3.  Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the priority boarding purchase.
4.  Click the 'Submit' button to complete the purchase.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Passenger Full Name** (`Passenger Name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the full name of the passenger(s) traveling.
*   **Booking Reference (PNR)** (`Booking Reference`, `text`, `required`): Enter the booking reference number for the passenger(s) flight(s).
*   **Flight Number** (`Flight Number`, `text`, `required`): Enter the flight number associated with the booking reference.
*   **Departure Date** (`Departure Date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the departure date of the flight in the format `yyyy-mm-dd`.
*   **Contact Email Address** (`Contact Email`, `email`, `required`): Enter the email address associated with the passenger(s) contact information.
*   **-- Priority Boarding Options --** (`Priority Boarding Options`, `note`, `optional`): This section provides information about the priority boarding options available for purchase. Please review the options carefully before selecting.
*   **Priority Level Requested** (`Priority Level Requested`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the priority level desired for the flight (Standard Priority, Business Priority, or First Class Priority).
*   **Number of Passengers for Upgrade** (`Number of Passengers`, `number`, `required`): Enter the number of passengers for the upgrade.
*   **-- Payment Details --** (`Payment Details`, `note`, `optional`): This section provides information about the payment process. Please review the details carefully before proceeding.
*   **Calculated Amount ($)** (`Calculated Amount`, `number`, `required`): This field displays the calculated total amount for the priority boarding upgrade.
*   **Card Type** (`Card Type`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the type of credit card used for payment (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, or Discover).
*   **Cardholder Name** (`Cardholder Name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of the cardholder.
*   **Billing Address** (`Billing Address`, `text`, `required`): Enter the billing address associated with the credit card.
*   **I agree to the terms and conditions of priority boarding purchase** (`Agreement`, `select_one`, `required`): Review and select 'I agree' to confirm you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
*   **Send payment confirmation to my email** (`Notification Opt-in`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select 'True' to receive payment confirmation via email, or 'False' if you do not want to receive it.

## Tips

*   Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting.
*   Ensure all required fields are filled before clicking the 'Submit' button.
*   Review and agree to the terms and conditions of priority boarding purchase.
*   Use the correct formatting for the departure date (yyyy-mm-dd).
*   Verify the card type and number match the card details.
