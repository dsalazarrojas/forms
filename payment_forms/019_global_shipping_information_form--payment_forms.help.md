# Global Shipping Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Shipping Information Form is designed to collect essential shipping details for global shipping purposes, such as customs and tax compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Shipping Address field with the recipient's address.
2. Enter the Shipping City field with the city of the shipping destination.
3. Type the Shipping State field with the state or province of the shipping destination.
4. Enter the Shipping Zip field with the postal code of the shipping destination.
5. Select the Shipping Country from the dropdown list (e.g., United States, Canada, Mexico).
6. If applicable, fill out the Shipping Date field with the expected shipping date.
7. If applicable, fill out the Shipping Time field with the expected shipping time.
8. Enter the Shipping Phone field with the recipient's phone number (if available).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter the shipping address of the recipient, including the street address, building number, and apartment number (if applicable).
* **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the shipping destination.
* **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`, text, optional): Enter the state or province of the shipping destination.
* **Shipping Zip** (`shipping_zip`, number, optional): Enter the postal code of the shipping destination.
* **Shipping Country** (`shipping_country`, select_one, optional): Select the country of the shipping destination from the dropdown list (e.g., United States, Canada, Mexico).
* **Shipping Date** (`shipping_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the expected shipping date in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/15/2023).
* **Shipping Time** (`shipping_time`, time, optional): If applicable, enter the expected shipping time in the format HH:MM AM/PM (e.g., 02:30 PM).
* **Shipping Phone** (`shipping_phone`, text, optional): Enter the recipient's phone number (if available).
