# Fantasy Sports Registration Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fantasy Sports Registration Payment Form is a tool to collect payment information from fantasy sports participants. It is an essential part of the registration process, and it helps us process payments and update participant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by providing your participant name to identify yourself in the registration.
2. Please choose the league you are registering for from the drop-down list.
3. Enter your email address where you want us to send notifications and updates.
4. If you are registering on behalf of a team, provide the team's name and size.
5. Choose the payment method that suits you best, such as Credit card, PayPal, or bank transfer.
6. Select the registration type, which is League Registration, Team Registration, or Individual Registration.
7. If applicable, provide any notes about your registration.
8. Choose who submitted the registration, such as the participant, an organizer, or a referee.
9. Enter your address, city, state, and zip code for communication purposes.
10. Finally, select the payment status as 'Paid' or 'Unpaid'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name**: Enter your real name, as it will be used to identify you in the registration process.
* **League name**: Choose the name of the league you are registering for. This field is not mandatory.
* **Participant email**: Enter a valid email address where you want to receive notifications and updates.
* **Participant phone**: This field is not mandatory, but it can be used for further communication.
* **Payment method**: Choose a payment method from the drop-down list. Credit card, PayPal, or bank transfer are available options.
* **Registration type**: Select the type of registration, which is League Registration, Team Registration, or Individual Registration.
* **Team name**: If you are registering on behalf of a team, enter the team's name.
* **Team size**: Enter the number of team members if you are registering a team.
* **Participant fee**: This field is not applicable to free registrations.
* **Registration date**: Enter the date of your registration, which will be auto-filled based on the current date.
* **Payment amount**: Enter the payment amount, which will be auto-filled based on the participant fee.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information about your registration if necessary.
* **Submitted by**: Choose who submitted the registration, such as the participant, an organizer, or a referee.
* **Participant address**: Enter your address for communication purposes.
* **Participant city**: Enter your city for communication purposes.
* **Participant state**: Enter your state for communication purposes.
* **Participant zip**: Enter your zip code for communication purposes.
* **Payment status**: Select the payment status as 'Paid' or 'Unpaid'.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately and truthfully. Some fields are not mandatory, so you can skip them if not applicable. If you have any issues or doubts, feel free to contact our support team.
