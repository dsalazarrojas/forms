# Customized Corporate Gift Basket Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a customized corporate gift basket order. It will help the gift buyer provide all the necessary details to successfully complete the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the gift option that suits your needs.
2. Choose a specific gift item from the options provided.
3. Enter the date and time for the order.
4. Provide the recipient's name and any additional details about them.
5. Enter the company's name.
6. Enter a gift message for the recipient (if desired).
7. Select a payment method (credit card, PayPal, or bank transfer).
8. Enter the billing address.
9. Choose the shipping option (standard, expedited, or express).
10. Add any additional order notes or comments.
11. Enter the phone number for billing and payment purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select A Gift** (`gift_option`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of gift basket you want to order (Gift Box A, Gift Box B, or Gift Box C).
* **Select Gift Item** (`gift_item`, select_one, required: true): Choose a specific gift item (e.g., Apple, Banana, or Orange) for the gift basket.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date for the order. This will help schedule the delivery of the gift basket.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time for the order.
* **Recipient Name** (`recipient_name`, text, required: false): Enter the recipient's full name.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required: false): Enter the company's name for where the gift basket is being sent from.
* **Gift Message** (`gift_message`, text, required: false): Add a gift message for the recipient.
* **Order Amount** (`order_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: true): Select a payment method (credit card, PayPal, or bank transfer) to complete the order.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, required: false): Enter the billing address for payment and shipping purposes.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required: false): Enter the shipping address for the gift basket.
* **Select Shipping Option** (`shipping_option`, select_one, required: true): Choose the shipping option (standard, expedited, or express) for the gift basket.
* **Order Note** (`order_note`, text, required: false): Add any additional notes about the order.
* **Comment** (`order_comment`, text, required: false): Add any comments about the order.
* **Phone** (`billing_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number for billing and payment purposes.
