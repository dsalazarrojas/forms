# Gift Subscription Fulfillment Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Gift Subscription Fulfillment Order Form is a tool used to track and manage gift subscriptions. This form helps ensure accurate and efficient fulfillment of these subscriptions by collecting and storing relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Gift Subscriber Name field with the name of the person receiving the gift subscription.
2. Enter the Gift Subscription Name in the corresponding field.
3. Provide the Order Date, if known or available.
4. Select the Payment Method used for the subscription.
5. Choose the current Order Status.
6. If necessary, add any additional Notes regarding the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gift Subscriber Name** (`gift_subscriber_name`): Enter the name of the person receiving the gift subscription. This is a required field.
* **Gift Subscription Name** (`gift_subscription_name`): Enter the name of the gift subscription being processed.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`): Enter the date the order was made, if known. This date can help in tracking the order's history.
* **Fulfillment Date** (`fulfillment_date`): Enter the date the gift subscription was fulfilled, if known. This date is optional but helps in keeping up-to-date records.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`): Enter the amount paid for the gift subscription. This field is not directly used in the form but is a potential field for reporting or tracking purposes.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select how the payment was made from the options provided: Bank Transfer, Check, or PayPal. This is a required field for accurately tracking payment methods.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`): Select the current status of the order from the options provided: Unfulfilled, Fulfillment Complete, or Canceled. This is a required field for up-to-date tracking.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional comments or notes regarding the order. This field is optional but can be useful for internal reference.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`): Enter any additional information about the payment made. This field is not directly used in the form but is a potential field for reporting or tracking purposes.
