# Customer Invoice Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers related to their invoices. Please complete this form carefully to ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin with the first page, "Customer Invoice Information", and fill out all the required fields.
2. Proceed to each subsequent page, completing all the required fields.
3. Review your responses carefully on each page to ensure accuracy.
4. Once you've filled out all pages, review and finalize your submission on the "Review and Finalize" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Invoice Information (customer_invoice_checklist_form_page_1)** (`title: Customer Invoice Information`, required): This page is the first step in completing the form. Fill out the fields accurately and completely.
* **Payment Terms (customer_invoice_checklist_form_page_2)** (`title: Payment Terms`, optional): This page provides an opportunity to discuss payment terms with the customer.
* **Shipping and Taxes (customer_invoice_checklist_form_page_3)** (`title: Shipping and Taxes`, optional): Complete this page to provide further details about shipping and taxes related to the invoice.
* **Customer Contact Information (customer_invoice_checklist_form_page_4)** (`title: Customer Contact Information`, required): Provide accurate contact information for the customer.
* **Payment Method (customer_invoice_checklist_form_page_5)** (`title: Payment Method`, optional): If applicable, select the payment method used for the invoice.
* **Review and Save (customer_invoice_checklist_form_page_6)** (`title: Review and Save`, optional): Review your responses on this page and save your progress.
* **Review and Finalize (customer_invoice_checklist_form_page_7)** (`title: Review and Finalize`, required): Carefully review all your responses and finalize your submission.
* **Review and Save (customer_invoice_checklist_form_page_8)** (`title: Review and Save`, optional): Another opportunity to review and save your progress.
* **Additional Information (customer_invoice_checklist_form_page_9)** (`title: Additional Information`, optional): Provide any additional information related to the invoice.
* **Customer Invoice (customer_invoice_checklist_form_page_10)** (`title: Customer Invoice`, optional): A sample customer invoice is provided for reference.

## Tips
* Be thorough in filling out each field, as accuracy is crucial for smooth invoice processing.
* Review each page carefully before proceeding to ensure all required fields are filled out.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
