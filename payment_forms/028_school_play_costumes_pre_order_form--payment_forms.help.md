# School Play Costumes Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for school administrators or staff to collect information for a school play costume pre-order. It includes details for student preferences, delivery, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for each of the costume selection fields to choose the costumes you want.
2. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
3. Enter the parent's name in the "Parent Name" field (optional).
4. Enter the parent's contact information in the "Parent Contact Info" field (optional).
5. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Student Costume Request" field to confirm your request.
6. Enter the student's delivery address in the "Student Delivery Address" field.
7. Enter any delivery instructions in the "Delivery Instructions" field (optional).
8. Select a date and time for the order in the "Order Date" and "Order Time" fields (optional).
9. Enter the school's contact information in the "School Contact Info" field (optional).
10. Enter any order comments in the "Order Comments" field (optional).
11. Sign and confirm your order by entering your parent's signature in the "Parent Signature" field (optional).
12. Select your payment method and confirm your payment status.
13. Confirm the payment total.
