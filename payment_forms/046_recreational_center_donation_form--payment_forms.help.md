# Recreational Center Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to donate to a recreational center.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide your information on the first page, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. On the second page, select the donation amount and payment method you prefer.
3. Fill out any additional payment information required for your selected method.
4. Confirm the details of your donation on the final page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Information (Donor Information, text, required: false)**: Enter your name and other relevant information about yourself.
* **Donation Amount (Donation Amount, number, required: false)**: Enter the amount you wish to donate.
* **Payment Method (Payment Method, select_one, required: false)**: Select how you would like to make your donation: Card Number, E-check/ACH, Cash, or Other.
* **Card Number (Card Number, number, required: false)**: If you selected Card Number as your payment method, enter your card number here.
* **E-check/ACh (E-check/ACh, text, required: false)**: If you selected E-check/ACh as your payment method, enter your E-check/ACh information here.
* **Payment Date (Payment Date, date, required: false)**: If applicable, enter the date of your payment.
* **Payment Time (Payment Time, time, required: false)**: If applicable, enter the time of your payment.
* **Other (If other, please specify) (Other, text, required: false)**: If you selected Other as your payment method, describe your other payment method here.
* **Notes (Notes, note, required: false)**: Enter any additional information you would like to include with your donation.
* **Email (Email, email, required: false)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Phone, text, required: false)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Confirm Payment (Confirm Payment, select_one, required: false)**: Confirm that you have made a donation.
* **Donation Confirmation (Donation Confirmation, select_one, required: false)**: Confirm the details of your donation.
