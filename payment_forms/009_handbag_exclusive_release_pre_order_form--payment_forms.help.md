# Handbag Exclusive Release Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a way for customers to express their interest in pre-ordering a limited edition handbag. It's not a standard order form, but rather a way for us to gauge demand and interest in the product before it's released.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the Pre Order Form with your details.
2. Provide your contact information on the Contact page.
3. Select your shipping preferences on the Shipping Details page.
4. Choose your payment method on the Payment Details page.
5. Make selections for each of the Shipping Options pages.
6. Add any additional notes or comments on the Order Notes pages.
7. Select whether you'd like to pre-order the Product Image(s) on the Product Image page(s).
