# investment_withdrawal_request_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a withdrawal from an investment account. You will need to provide information about the withdrawal, including the account number, amount, and reason for withdrawal. You will also be asked to provide your contact details and any additional information about the withdrawal. Finally, you will be able to submit your review notes and comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand the required and optional fields.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for each of the fields, where applicable.
3. Enter the account number in the "account_number" field.
4. Enter the amount of the withdrawal in the "amount" field.
5. Select the date of the withdrawal from the calendar.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" for each of the options in the "time_period" and "financial_institution" fields.
7. Select "Yes" or "No" for each of the options in the "reason_for_withdrawal" field.
8. Enter any additional information in the "additional_information" field.
9. Review the "review_status" and "approval_status" fields to understand the current status of your request.
10. Enter any review comments and review notes in the "review_comments" and "review_note" fields.
11. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "reviewer" field.
12. Enter your email address and phone number in the "email" and "phone_number" fields.
13. Enter your signature in the "signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter (Yes/No)**: Check this box if you are requesting a withdrawal. If you are not requesting a withdrawal, uncheck this box.
* **Account Number**: Enter your account number to identify your investment account.
* **Amount**: Enter the amount of the withdrawal you are requesting.
* **Withdrawal Date**: Select the date of the withdrawal from the calendar.
* **Time Period**: Select "Yes" if you are requesting a withdrawal for a specific time period. Select "No" if you are not requesting a withdrawal for a specific time period.
* **Financial Institution**: Select "Yes" if you are requesting a withdrawal from a financial institution. Select "No" if you are not requesting a withdrawal from a financial institution.
* **Reason for Withdrawal**: Select "Yes" for each reason that applies to your withdrawal request.
* **Contact Details**: Enter any additional contact information that will help us process your request.
* **Submit**: Enter any additional information or comments in this field.
* **Review Status**: Review this field to understand the current status of your request (Active or Inactive).
* **Review Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback about your request.
* **Reviewer**: Select "Yes" if you want to review your request. Select "No" if you do not want to review your request.
* **Approval Status**: Review this field to understand the current approval status of your request (Active or Inactive).
* **Additional Information**: Enter any additional information about your request.
* **Review Note**: Enter any notes or comments about your request.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to contact you about your request.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number to contact you about your request.
* **Signature**: Enter your signature to confirm your request.
