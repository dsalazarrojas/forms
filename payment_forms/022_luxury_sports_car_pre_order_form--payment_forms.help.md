# Luxury Sports Car Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers interested in pre-ordering a luxury sports car. The form will guide you through the necessary steps to provide your details and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Make sure to use the correct format for email addresses.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the model of the luxury sports car you are interested in from the "What model would you like?" list.
6. Choose the color of the car from the "What color would you like?" list.
7. Enter the amount you would like to pay as a deposit in the "Deposit amount" field.
8. Select whether you would like to pay now or later from the "Would you like to pay a deposit now?" list.
9. Enter any additional comments you would like to share in the "Additional comments" field. (Optional)
10. Confirm your phone number in the "Confirm phone number" field.
11. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm email" field.
12. Confirm the deposit amount in the "Confirm deposit amount" field.
13. Select whether you would like to pay through a payment gateway in the "Payment Gateway" field.
14. Select whether you would like to use a specific payment method in the "Payment Method" field.
15. Confirm your payment method in the "Confirm Payment Method" field.
16. Confirm whether you would like to use a specific payment gateway in the "Confirm Payment Gateway" field.
17. Confirm your payment terms in the "Confirm Payment Terms" field.
