# DJ Services Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **DJ Services Billing Form** is a form for users to manage and process payments for services provided by DJ Services. This form gathers necessary information for payment processing and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **payment method** you would like to use for payment (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
2. Enter the **client name** for the service provided.
3. Provide **event details**, if necessary, for the service performed.
4. Enter the **date** of the service.
5. Enter the **time** of the service.
6. Add any additional **service details**, if applicable.
7. Enter the **payment amount** for the service.
8. Enter the **email** address of the service recipient.
9. Enter your **phone number** for contact purposes.
10. Select the **service status** (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
11. Enter the **billing address** of the service recipient.
12. Enter the **billing city** of the service recipient.
13. Select the **billing country** (United States, Canada, or Mexico).
14. Enter any additional **payment method ID**, if necessary.
15. Add any extra **service details** information, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **payment_method**: Select the method you'd like to use for payment. This can be Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
* **client_name**: Enter the name of the client you're billing.
* **event_details**: Add any additional information about the event or service performed.
* **date**: Enter the date of the service.
* **time**: Enter the time of the service.
* **service_details**: Add any extra details about the service performed.
* **payment_amount**: Enter the total amount due for the service.
* **email**: Enter the email address of the client.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **service_status**: Choose the status of the service (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
* **billing_address**: Enter the address of the client for billing purposes.
* **billing_city**: Enter the city of the client's billing address.
* **billing_country**: Choose the country of the client's billing address (United States, Canada, or Mexico).
* **payment_method_id**: If necessary, enter any additional payment method ID information.
* **service_details_2**: Add any extra details about the service performed.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Double-check your payment method and client information for accuracy.
- This form is for billing purposes only; please do not use for other transactions.
