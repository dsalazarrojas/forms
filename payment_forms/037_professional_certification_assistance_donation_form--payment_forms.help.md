# professional_certification_assistance_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to provide details about themselves and their donation to aid in professional certification. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that your certification can be processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your user details in the User Details field.
2. Provide details about your donation in the Donation Details field.
3. Enter your payment information, including donation amount.
4. Choose the frequency of your donation (if applicable).
5. Add any additional notes about your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details** (`user_details`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other details you'd like to share about yourself.
* **Donation Details** (`donation_details`, text, optional): Describe your donation, including the type of certification you're assisting with and any relevant information.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_information`, text, optional): Enter your payment information, including the amount you're donating.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Donor Email** (`donor_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your donation.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you're donating.
* **Frequency of Donations** (`donation_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select how frequently you plan to make this type of donation.
	+ Option 1: Daily
	+ Option 2: Weekly
	+ Option 3: Monthly
	+ Option 4: Annually
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your donation.
* **Other** (`donation_frequency_other`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" for Frequency of Donations, enter your selected option here.
* **Note** (`donation_frequency_note`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" for Frequency of Donations, enter a note about your other donation frequency.
