# gift_card_balance_inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to inquire about the balance of a gift card.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the card number of the gift card you are inquiring about.
2. Select the desired gift card balance from the drop-down list.
3. (Optional) Enter your email address if you want to receive the balance information via email.
4. (Optional) Enter your phone number if you want to receive a call about the gift card balance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Card Number** (`card_number`, Number, Required): Enter the card number of the gift card you are inquiring about.
* **Balance** (`gift_card_balance`, Select One, Required): Select the desired gift card balance from the list. Please select a balance that matches the current status of your gift card.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address if you want to receive the balance information via email.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number if you want to receive a call about the gift card balance.
