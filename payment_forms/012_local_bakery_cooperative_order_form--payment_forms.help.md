# Local Bakery Cooperative Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Local Bakery Cooperative Order Form is designed to collect information from customers for either pickup or delivery orders. The form helps the cooperative organize and process customer orders, ensuring accurate and timely fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to pick up your order.
2. **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you would like to pick up your order.
3. **Pickup Location** (`pickup_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location you would like to pick up your order.
4. **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to have your order delivered.
5. **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you would like to have your order delivered.
6. **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, as it will appear on your order.
7. **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication about your order.
8. **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number, which will be used for communication about your order.
9. **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional details about your order, such as specific items or quantities.
10. **Order Type** (`order_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you would like to pick up your order or have it delivered.
11. **Order Type Options** (`order_type_options`, text, required/optional): If you chose "pick up" in the previous field, provide more details about your pickup location.
12. **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or requests you have for your order.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have any special requests or preferences, include them in the "Customer Notes" field.
* If you chose to have your order delivered, please ensure that your delivery address is accurate and up-to-date.
