# promotional_banner_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The promotional_banner_order_form is designed to collect customer information and promotional banner details for processing orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Customer Info section with your name, email, and other relevant details.
2. Provide information about the promotional banner you are interested in purchasing.
3. Enter any additional order details as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info**: 
  * (`name`, `email`, `required`)
  This section captures your contact information so we can reach out to you regarding your order status.
* **Promotional Banner Info**: 
  * (`name`, `type`, `required`)
  Here, you can describe the promotional banner you are looking for. Please provide as much detail as possible to ensure we find the correct product.
* **Order Details**: 
  * (`description`, `required`)
  In this section, provide any additional details about your order, such as the date and time you need it for.

## Tips
- Please fill in all required fields to ensure your order is processed correctly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
- Double-check the form before submission to avoid errors.
- If you have any further requests or changes to your order, contact us at form@gic.mx.
