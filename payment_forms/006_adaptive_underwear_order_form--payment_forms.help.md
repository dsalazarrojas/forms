# Adaptive Underwear Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Adaptive Underwear Order Form is used to collect information for customers who are interested in purchasing adaptive underwear. This form is designed to ensure that we have all the necessary details to provide the right products and services for the customer's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out all required fields (indicated by *) with accurate and complete information.
2.  Review the options for each field carefully to select the most suitable choice for your needs.
3.  Double-check that all fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (*): Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (*): Enter a valid email address for order confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (*): Enter your contact phone number for delivery questions.
* **Shipping Address** (*): Enter your complete shipping address for delivery purposes.
* **Billing Address** (optional): If your shipping address is different from your billing address, please enter your billing address here.
* **Waist Size (inches)** (*): Measure around the narrowest part of your waist.
* **Hip Size (inches)** (*): Measure around the fullest part of your hips.
* **Underwear Style** (*): Select your preferred style of underwear.
* **Fabric Preference** (*): Choose your preferred fabric type.
* **Special Features Needed** (optional): Check any special features you require, such as extra support or antimicrobial properties.
* **Quantity** (*): Enter the number of pairs you would like to order.
* **Color Choice** (*): Select your preferred color.
* **Special Instructions** (optional): Enter any special instructions or requests you have for your order.
* **Medical Condition Requiring Adaptive Underwear** (optional): Briefly describe any medical conditions that require adaptive underwear.
* **Caregiver Information** (optional): If you are ordering for someone else, provide their details here.
* **Insurance Coverage** (optional): Check if your order is covered by insurance.
* **Payment Method** (*): Select how you would like to pay for your order.
* **Estimated Order Total** (optional): Enter the estimated amount you expect to pay.
* **Desired Delivery Date** (optional): Select the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Terms and Conditions Accepted** (*): Confirm that you have read and accepted the terms and conditions.
* **Signature Date** (*): Date and sign this form to confirm your order.
* **Customer Signature** (*): Sign your name electronically to confirm your order.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
- Double-check that all required fields are filled out correctly.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact our customer support for guidance.
