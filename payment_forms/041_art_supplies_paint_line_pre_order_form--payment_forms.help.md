# Art Supplies Paint Line Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Supplies Paint Line Pre Order Form is designed to allow customers to pre-order art supplies products online. This form captures the necessary information to complete the order, including customer contact details, product selections, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your pre-order date in the Pre Order Date field.
2. Fill in your customer information, including name, email, phone number, and shipping address.
3. Select the paint line and color set you want to order.
4. Choose your preferred tube size and number of sets to order.
5. Select any additional products you'd like to purchase.
6. Choose your pre-order tier and preferred payment method.
7. Fill in any special instructions or notes.
8. Confirm that you've read and agreed to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pre Order Date**: 
  * (id: 1, <date>): Enter the date you'd like to pre-order the art supplies.
* **Customer Name** 
  * (id: 2, <text>): Enter your full name.
* **Customer Email**
  * (id: 3, <email>): Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone**
  * (id: 4, <text>): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Shipping Address**
  * (id: 5, <text>): Enter your full shipping address.
* **City**
  * (id: 6, <text>): Enter your city of residence.
* **State**
  * (id: 7, <text>): Enter your state or province of residence.
* **Zip Code**
  * (id: 8, <text>): Enter your postal or zip code.
* **Paint Line**
  * (id: 9, <select_one>): Select the paint line you'd like to order:
    + Professional Acrylic
    + Professional Oil
    + Watercolor
    + Gouache
    + Acrylic Gouache
    + Mixed Media
    + Complete Set
* **Color Set**
  * (id: 10, <select_one>): Select the color set you'd like to order:
    + Warm Primaries Set
    + Cool Primaries Set
    + Earth Tones Set
    + Landscape Set
    + Portrait Set
    + Full Studio Set
    + Custom Selection
* **Tube Size**
  * (id: 11, <select_one>): Select your preferred tube size:
    + 15ml Tubes
    + 37ml Tubes
    + 60ml Tubes
    + 120ml Tubes
    + 200ml Tubes
    + Mixed Sizes
* **Quantity Sets**
  * (id: 12, <number>): Enter the number of sets to order.
* **Individual Colors**
  * (id: 13, <text>): If you've selected a custom color set, list the individual colors you'd like to order.

  * (id: 14, <select_multiple>): Select any additional products you'd like to purchase:

  * (id: 15, <select_one>): Select your pre-order tier:
    + Early Bird - 20% Off

    + Regular Pre-Order
    + VIP Bundle
* **Unit Price**

* **Subtotal**

* **Discount Applied**
  * (id: 18, <number>): The discount amount applied.
* **Shipping Cost**

* **Deposit Required**

* **Balance Due**
  * (id: 23, <number>): The balance due on shipment.
* **Payment Method**
  * (id: 24, <select_one>): Select how you'd like to pay:
    + Credit Card
    + Debit Card
    + PayPal
    + Bank Transfer
    + Check
* **Expected Ship Date**
  * (id: 25, <date>): Enter the expected ship date.
* **Special Instructions**
  * (id: 26, <text>): Enter any special instructions or notes.
* **Terms Accepted**

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any special requests or questions, use the special instructions field.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to our product descriptions or contact us for assistance.
