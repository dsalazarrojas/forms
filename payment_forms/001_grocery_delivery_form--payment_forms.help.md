# Grocery Delivery Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Grocery Delivery Form is designed to collect information from customers about their grocery orders. This form helps our team understand what customers are looking for in a grocery delivery service and ensures that orders are processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Fill out the form fields as follows:
	* **Order Details (Page 1)**: Enter your grocery order details, including any specific items you'd like to purchase.
	* **Customer Info (Page 2)**: Provide your contact information and any other relevant details about your delivery preferences.
	* **Payment (Page 3)**: Enter your payment method and any additional payment details.
	* **Shipping (Page 4)**: Confirm your shipping address and any delivery instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details (Order Details) (Page 1)**: This field is not required, but if you leave it blank, your order may be delayed or cancelled. Please enter a clear and specific description of your order.
* **Customer Info (Customer Info) (Page 2)**: Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information. This helps us confirm your identity and ensure that we contact you about your order.
* **Payment (Payment) (Page 3)**: Enter your chosen payment method, whether it's credit card, bank transfer, or another accepted method. Be sure to include any additional payment details.
* **Shipping (Shipping) (Page 4)**: Enter your shipping address and any special instructions for delivery, such as a preferred delivery time or special requests for your order.
