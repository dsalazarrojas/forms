# Donation Agreement Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donation Agreement Renewal Form is used to update or modify existing donation agreements, ensuring that all relevant information is up-to-date and accurate. This form should only be used for renewal purposes and not for initial agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all the fields with the current information of the donor and the agreement.
2. Select the correct agreement type from the provided options (Gift Agreement, Grant Agreement, Other).
3. Enter the agreement date (the date the agreement was initially signed) and the renewal date (the date the agreement is being renewed).
4. Fill out the donor information: name, address, email, and phone number.
5. Enter the staff contact information: name, email, and phone number.
6. Select the correct payment terms, payment status, and agreement status.
7. Add any payment terms notes or payment status notes if necessary.
8. Finally, review all the information entered for accuracy and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Donor Information**: Enter the current information of the donor, including name, address, email, and phone number.
- **Agreement Type**: Select the type of agreement that this renewal is for (Gift Agreement, Grant Agreement, or Other).
- **Agreement Date**: Enter the date the agreement was initially signed.
- **Renewal Date**: Enter the date this agreement is being renewed.
- **Agreement Status**: Select the current status of the agreement (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
- **Payment Terms**: Select the payment terms applicable to this agreement.
- **Payment Status**: Select the current payment status (Paid, Partially Paid, or Unpaid).
- **Payment Method**: Select the payment method used for this agreement (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
- **Staff Information**: Enter the current contact information of the staff member responsible (name, email, and phone number).
- **Agreement Remarks**: Add any additional remarks about the agreement.
- **Agreement Notes**: Add any additional notes about the agreement.
- **Submission Status**: Select the status of the submission (Submitted, Incomplete, or Not Submitted).
