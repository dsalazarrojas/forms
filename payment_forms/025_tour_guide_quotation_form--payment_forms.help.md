# Tour Guide Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tour Guide Quotation Form is designed to collect necessary information for tour quotations, including quotation date, tour operator details, tour type, start and end locations, travel duration, tour price, customer contact details, and any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields with the necessary information.
* Select the relevant options from the available choices, if applicable.
* Ensure that you have filled in all the required fields, as indicated by the `required` field attribute.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quotation Date (quotation_date)** (`1`, `date`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter the date of the quotation.
	+ Please use the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Tour Operator (tour_operator)** (`2`, `select_one`, required: `false`):
	+ Select one of the available options to choose your tour operator.
* **Tour Type (tour_type)** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`):
	+ Select one or more of the available options for the type of tour you are offering.
* **Start Location (start_location)** (`4`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter the start location of the tour.
* **End Location (end_location)** (`5`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter the end location of the tour.
* **Travel Duration (travel_duration)** (`6`, `time`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter the duration of the tour.
* **Tour Price (tour_price)** (`7`, `number`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter the price of the tour.
* **Customer Name (customer_name)** (`8`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter the customer's name.
* **Contact Email (contact_email)** (`9`, `email`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter the customer's contact email address.
* **Contact Phone (customer_phone)** (`10`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter the customer's contact phone number.
* **Customer Note (customer_note)** (`11`, `note`, required: `false`):
	+ Add any additional notes about the customer.
* **Agency Note (agency_note)** (`12`, `note`, required: `false`):
	+ Add any additional notes about the agency or tour operator's side.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill in all required fields to avoid any errors.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant tour operator or agency.
