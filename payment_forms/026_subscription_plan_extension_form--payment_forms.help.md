# Subscription Plan Extension Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Subscription Plan Extension Form is used to collect additional information about a customer's subscription plan. This form is essential for businesses to manage their subscription-based services and ensure accurate customer records.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required fields:
   - Customer Name
   - Customer Email
   - Customer Phone
   - Subscription ID

2. If you have any existing subscription plans that are up for renewal, you may want to fill out the Renewal Date field to indicate when the plan needs to be renewed.

3. Select the payment method your customer uses for their subscription from the list provided. Please note that some payment methods might not be applicable for all customers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the customer's name as it appears on their account.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter the customer's email address associated with their subscription.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter the customer's phone number as it appears on their account.
* **Renewal Date** (`renewal_date`, date, optional): If you have an existing subscription plan that's up for renewal, indicate when it's due for renewal. Use the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Subscription ID** (`subscription_id`, text, required): Enter the ID associated with the customer's subscription plan.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select one, optional): Select the payment method used by your customer. Please choose from the list of available payment methods, such as American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay, etc.
* **Payment Method Fields** (e.g., `payment_method_american_express`, select one, optional): If you select a specific payment method, you may need to confirm your choice by selecting the corresponding field. Please note that these fields are only visible if you select the corresponding payment method in the `Payment Method` field.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill out all the required fields to avoid incomplete submissions.
- Double-check the payment method fields to ensure you've selected the correct payment method.
- If you're unsure about any field or payment method, feel free to reach out to our support team for assistance.
