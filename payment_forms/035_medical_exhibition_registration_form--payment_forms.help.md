# Medical Exhibition Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Exhibition Registration Form is a critical document that helps us understand your exhibition details and ensures that you have the necessary information required to participate in the exhibition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the Exhibitor Details page with your company name, contact information, and other relevant details.
2. On the next page, provide your Medical License information.
3. Enter your Medical License Number.
4. Choose your PayPal Account status.
5. Select your preferred Payment Method.
6. Enter the Amount to Pay (if applicable).
7. Enter the Fee (if applicable).
8. Finally, confirm your Payment Status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exhibitor Details** (`exhibitor_details`, text, optional): Provide your company name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Medical License** (`medical_license`, text, optional): Enter your medical license number (e.g., your medical degree or certification number).
* **Medical License Number** (`medical_license_number`, text, optional): Enter your actual medical license number, if you have one.
* **PayPal Account** (`paypal_account`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you have a PayPal account, and "No" otherwise.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the payment methods you wish to use (e.g., "Yes" or "No" for each method).
* **Amount to Pay** (`amount_to_pay`, number, optional): If you are paying for your exhibition, enter the amount you wish to pay.
* **Fee** (`fee`, number, optional): Enter any fees you may need to pay for your exhibition participation.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, optional): Confirm that you have made a payment (select "Paid") or that you have not made a payment (select "Not Paid") .

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or need further clarification, please contact us.
