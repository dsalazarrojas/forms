# Down Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Down Payment Form is used to collect information about a client's down payment, including the payment amount, due date, payment method, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's information, including their name and contact details.
2. Enter the payment amount and due date.
3. Select the payment method from the options provided.
4. Add any additional notes if necessary.
5. Specify who the payment is assigned to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information**: Enter the client's name and contact details.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount of the down payment.
* **Due Date**: Enter the date by which the down payment is due.
* **Payment Method**: Select one or more payment methods to process the down payment (e.g., bank transfer, check, etc.).
* **Client Email**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Phone**: Enter the client's phone number.
* **Note**: Enter any additional notes or instructions about the payment.
* **Assigned to**: Enter the person or department responsible for processing the payment.
* **Note**: Enter any additional notes or instructions about the payment.
