# cookie_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cookie_order_form is a simple form used to collect customer information for a cookie delivery or pickup. It's not a mandatory form, so customers can submit it voluntarily.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of cookie you'd like to order by choosing one or more cookie types from the Cookie Type field.
2. Enter your name in the Name field.
3. Provide your address in the Address field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
5. Add your email address in the Email field.
6. Choose your preferred shipping option from the Shipping field.
7. Select the payment method for your order from the Payment field.
8. Enter the quantity of cookies you'd like to order in the Quantity field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Cookie Type** (Cookie Type, select_multiple, required: false):
This field allows you to choose one or more types of cookies you'd like to order. You can select multiple options by holding down the Ctrl key while clicking on each desired option.
- **Name** (Name, text, required: false):
Enter your name as you'd like it to appear on the order or on any associated documentation.
- **Address** (Address, text, required: false):
Provide your physical address where you'd like to receive the cookies or where you'll be picking them up.
- **Phone** (Phone, text, required: false):
Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
- **Email** (Email, email, required: false):
Enter a valid email address where you can receive notifications or updates about your order.
- **Shipping** (Shipping, select_one, required: false):
Choose how you'd like to receive your cookies. Options include Standard Shipping, Express Shipping, or USPS.
- **Payment** (Payment, select_one, required: false):
Select the payment method you'd like to use for your order. You can choose between Credit Card or PayPal.
- **Quantity** (Quantity, number, required: false):
Enter the number of cookies you'd like to order.

## Tips

* Please ensure you fill in all the fields as needed before submitting the form.
* Double-check your choices, especially for Shipping and Payment, to ensure you get your cookies on time and in the method you prefer.
