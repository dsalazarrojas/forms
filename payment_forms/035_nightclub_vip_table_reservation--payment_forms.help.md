# Nightclub VIP Table Reservation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users to process a Nightclub VIP table reservation. The form helps in confirming the reservation, gathering details about the VIP, and processing payment information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Confirm if you want to reserve a table by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Table Reservation Form" section.
2. If you select "Yes", fill out the "VIP Details Form" with the required information about the VIP.
3. In the "Payment Method" section, choose your preferred payment method.
4. If you have a deposit, fill in the "Deposit" field with the amount.
5. Fill out the "Payment Amount" and "Total Amount" fields with the corresponding values.
6. Review the "Terms" section to ensure you understand the terms of service.
7. Finally, confirm your payment by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Accept" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Table Reservation Form (Yes/No)**: Confirm whether you want to reserve a table.
* **VIP Details Form (Name, Email, Phone, Guest Name)**: Enter the details about the VIP, including name, email, phone number, and guest name.
* **Bottle Service Form (Unused)**: This section is currently unused and may be removed or modified in the future.
* **Payment Method (Yes/No)**: Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Deposit (Optional)**: If you have a deposit, enter the amount.
* **Payment Amount (Number)**: Enter the total payment amount.
* **Total Amount (Number)**: Enter the total amount to be paid.
* **Terms (Yes/No)**: Review and accept the terms of service.
* **Accept (Yes/No)**: Confirm your payment and acceptance of the terms.
* **Refund (Yes/No)**: This section is likely related to the refund policy, but its purpose is unclear.
* **Cancel (Yes/No)**: Confirm whether you want to cancel the reservation.
