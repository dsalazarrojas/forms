# Vendor Payment Hold Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Payment Hold Request Form is a tool used to request a payment hold on a vendor's account for various reasons, such as delays in payment or other issues.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with the requested information, and submit the form. The form consists of two pages, each containing fields that require input from you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Payment Hold Request** (`vendor_payment_hold_request_form_page_1`, text, optional): This is the main field where you can write a brief description of the reason for requesting a payment hold.
* **Invoice Information** (`vendor_details`, text, optional): Please provide information about the invoice related to the payment hold request, such as the invoice number, date, or any other relevant details.
* **Payment Delay Date** (`payment_delay_date`, date, optional): If applicable, please specify the date of the payment delay.
* **Delay Reason** (`payment_delay_reason`, text, optional): Provide a clear and concise reason for the payment delay.
* **Additional Information** (`vendor_payment_request_form_page_2`, text, optional): If there is any additional information you would like to provide about your request, please use this field.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide a clear and concise reason for the payment delay to support your request.
* Ensure that the information provided in the form is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any supporting documents or attachments, consider attaching them to the form to further support your request.
