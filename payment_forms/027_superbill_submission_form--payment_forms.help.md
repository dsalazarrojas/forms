# Superbill Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Superbill Submission Form is a simple text-based form designed to collect relevant information from patients or their representatives regarding superbill insurance claims.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Review and fill out each field as prompted.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Superbill Submission Form Page** (`superbill_submission_form_page`, text, required: false): This is the title page of the form.
* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, or any other relevant identifying information.
* **Insurance Information** (`insurance_info`, text, required: false): Provide the patient's insurance provider and policy number, or any other relevant insurance details.
* **Claim Details** (`claim_details`, text, required: false): Describe the superbill claim in detail, including the procedure, date of service, and any other relevant information.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you are submitting the claim.
 
## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you need to edit or re-submit a claim, please contact [healthcare organization/insurance company] for assistance.
