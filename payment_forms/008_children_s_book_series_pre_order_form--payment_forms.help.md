# Children Book Series Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect pre-order information for a book series. Fill out this form to place an order for the book series and provide the required details for processing the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Buyer Name** field with the name of the person placing the order.
2. Enter the **Buyer Email** address for order confirmation and any future communications.
3. Provide the **Shipping Address** for delivery.
4. Select the **Book Title** for the pre-order.
5. Choose the **Quantity** of books to pre-order.
6. If you're gifting, select the **Gift Option** as per your choice.
7. Choose the **Payment Method** for payment processing.
8. If you have any **Special Instructions**, enter them in this field.
9. Finally, confirm that you **Agree to Terms and Conditions**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Buyer Name** (`buyer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person placing the order.
* **Buyer Email** (`buyer_email`, email, required): Enter the email address for order confirmation and future communications.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Provide the street address for delivery.
* **Book Title** (`book_title`, select_one, required): Choose the title of the book you'd like to pre-order from the options.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of copies you'd like to pre-order.
* **Gift Option** (`gift_option`, select_one, optional): If this is a gift, select 'Yes include gift wrap' or 'Yes do not include price'.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to pay for the order from the options.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any delivery notes or personalized message you'd like to include with the order.
* **Agree to Terms and Conditions** (`agree_terms`, select_one, required): Confirm that you've read and agree to the pre-order policy.
