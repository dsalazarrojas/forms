# market_fee_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record market fees for events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Event Organizer field with the name of the event organizer.
2. Enter the Vendor Name in the field provided.
3. Enter the vendor's email address in the Vendor Email field.
4. Enter the vendor's phone number in the Vendor Phone field.
5. Enter the amount of the fee in the Fee Amount field.
6. Select the payment method from the Payment Method dropdown menu.
7. Enter the payment date in the Payment Date field.
8. Enter the payment time in the Payment Time field.
9. Add any additional notes in the Notes field.
10. Enter the vendor's address in the Vendor Address field.
11. Select the fee status from the Fee Status dropdown menu.
12. Select whether additional fees apply from the Additional Fees dropdown menu.
13. Enter the payment terms and conditions in the Payment Terms and Conditions field.
14. Enter any bank draft fee in the Bank Draft Fee field.
15. Enter any check fee in the Check Fee field.
16. Enter any wire transfer fee in the Wire Transfer Fee field.
17. Enter any cash fee in the Cash Fee field.
18. Enter any payment gateway fee in the Payment Gateway Fee field.
19. Enter the payment gateway fee percentage in the Payment Gateway Fee Percentage field.
20. Enter the event organizer fee in the Event Organizer Fee field.
21. Enter the event organizer fee percentage in the Event Organizer Fee Percentage field.
22. Enter the market fee in the Market Fee field.
23. Enter the market fee percentage in the Market Fee Percentage field.
24. Enter the tax rate in the Tax Rate field.
25. Enter the tax rate percentage in the Tax Rate Percentage field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Organizer**: Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Vendor Name**: Enter the name of the vendor.
* **Vendor Email**: Enter the email address of the vendor.
* **Vendor Phone**: Enter the phone number of the vendor.
* **Fee Amount**: Enter the amount of the fee.
* **Payment Method**: Select the method of payment (Bank Draft, Check, Wire Transfer, or Cash).
* **Payment Date**: Enter the date of payment.
* **Payment Time**: Enter the time of payment.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Vendor Address**: Enter the address of the vendor.
* **Fee Status**: Select the status of the fee (Paid, Partially Paid, Unpaid, Refunded, or Cancelled).
* **Additional Fees**: Select whether additional fees apply (True, False, or Not Specified).
* **Payment Terms and Conditions**: Enter any payment terms and conditions.
* **Bank Draft Fee**: Enter any bank draft fee.
* **Check Fee**: Enter any check fee.
* **Wire Transfer Fee**: Enter any wire transfer fee.
* **Cash Fee**: Enter any cash fee.
* **Payment Gateway Fee**: Enter any payment gateway fee.
* **Payment Gateway Fee Percentage**: Enter the payment gateway fee percentage.
* **Event Organizer Fee**: Enter any event organizer fee.
* **Event Organizer Fee Percentage**: Enter the event organizer fee percentage.
* **Market Fee**: Enter the market fee.
* **Market Fee Percentage**: Enter the market fee percentage.
* **Tax Rate**: Enter the tax rate.
* **Tax Rate Percentage**: Enter the tax rate percentage.
* **Payment Terms and Conditions**: Enter any payment terms and conditions.
* **Fee Status**: Select the status of the fee.
