# Spa Membership Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for customers to subscribe to a spa membership, providing essential information for processing their membership application.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your information:
	* Enter your name (optional but recommended for our records).
	* Provide your email address (required for communication and notifications).
	* Enter your phone number (optional but recommended for easy communication).
2. Choose your preferred payment method:
	* Select from one of the provided options: Stripe, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
3. Specify your membership type:
	* Choose from Basic or Premium membership options.
4. If applicable, provide the start and end dates of your membership.
5. Review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your name, which will be used for internal records and correspondence.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address to receive important updates and notifications about your membership.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Your phone number is optional but recommended for easy communication.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred payment method to complete your application.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `note`, optional): This field is for any additional payment information you'd like to provide.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your preferred membership type (Basic or Premium).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the start date of your membership (if applicable).
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the end date of your membership (if applicable).
* **Total Payment** (`total_payment`, `number`, optional): Enter the total payment amount (if applicable).

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields (email) are filled out accurately.
* For payment method and membership type, choose the options that best suit your needs.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact our support team for assistance.
