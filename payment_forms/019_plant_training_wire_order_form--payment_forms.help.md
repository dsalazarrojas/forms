# plant_training_wire_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers for plant training orders. Please fill out this form to place an order for plant training services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **customer details** section with the customer's name and contact information.
2.  Select the **payment method** you would like to use for the order.
3.  Enter the **order date** for the training order.
4.  Enter the **total amount** for the order.
5.  Add any **order comments** you would like to include with the order.
6.  Fill in the customer's **email** and **phone number** for contact purposes.
7.  Enter the customer's **address** for delivery purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Plant Training Wire Order Form (form_head)**: This is the title of the form.
* **2. customer_details (customer_info)**: Enter the customer's name and other identifying information. 
* **3. order_date**: Enter the date for the plant training order in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **4. total_amount**: Enter the total cost of the order.
* **5. payment_method (payment_method)**: Select the payment method for this order. Options are credit card, paypal, or cash.
* **6. customer_name (customer_name)**: Enter the customer's name.
* **7. order_comments (order_comments)**: Enter any additional comments or notes for the order.
* **8. customer_email (customer_email)**: Enter the customer's email.
* **9. customer_phone (customer_phone)**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **10. customer_address (customer_address)**: Enter the customer's address for delivery purposes. 

**Tips**
* Make sure to double-check the customer's information for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our customer support team for assistance.
