# payment_summary_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The payment_summary_form is a form used to gather and display key details about payments made and received by an individual or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Start by filling out the General Info section with any necessary details.
*   In the Payments Received and Payments Made sections, select all relevant options for the payments you are summarizing.
*   Enter the Date Of Last Transaction and Time Of Last Transaction fields for the date and time of the last transaction.
*   Add any additional notes or comments in the Note field.
*   Enter the email address of the individual or organization making the payment.
*   Add any phone number for additional contact information.
*   Select the Payments Status option to indicate the current status of the payment.
*   Finally, enter the Payment Amount Received.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **General Info** (`general_info`, text, optional): This field is used to capture any general information about the payment or organization.
*   **Payments Received** (`payments_received`, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant options for the payments received.
*   **Payments Made** (`payments_made`, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant options for the payments made.
*   **Date Of Last Transaction** (`date_of_last_transaction`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last transaction in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
*   **Time Of Last Transaction** (`time_of_last_transaction`, time, optional): Enter the time of the last transaction in the format HH:MM.
*   **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the payment or organization.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the individual or organization making the payment.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number for additional contact information.
*   **Payments Status** (`payments_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the payment from the available options.
*   **Payment Amount Received** (`payment_amount_received`, number, optional): Enter the amount received in the payment.
