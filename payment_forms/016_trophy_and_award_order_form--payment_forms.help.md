# Trophy And Award Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for trophy and award orders. Fill out the form with your details to place an order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the first name field.
2. Enter your last name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Provide the name of the organization you're ordering for.
5. Choose the type of award you're ordering.
6. Select the quantity of awards you want to order.
7. Enter the price for each award.
8. Provide any additional details about the award.
9. Choose how you want to order (Online, In Person, or Mail).
10. Select how you want to pay (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
11. Enter the date you want to pay.
12. Provide the award shipping details (city, state, and postal code).
13. Enter the date you want to receive the award.
14. Choose when you want to deliver the award.
15. Confirm the payment status (Paid or Unpaid).
16. Provide any additional details about the trophy.
17. Enter the price of the trophy.
18. Enter the total price of the order.
19. Enter the details about your payment method.
20. Provide additional details about the order method.
21. Enter your award shipping address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (`first_name`, Text, Optional):** Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name (`last_name`, Text, Optional):** Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email (`email`, Email, Optional):** Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Organization (`organization`, Text, Optional):** Enter the name of the organization you're ordering for.
* **Award Type (`award_type`, Text, Optional):** Choose the type of award you want to order.
* **Quantity (`quantity`, Number, Optional):** Enter the number of awards you want to order.
* **Award Price (`award_price`, Number, Optional):** Enter the price for each award.
* **Award Details (`award_details`, Text, Optional):** Provide any additional details about the award.
* **Order Method (`order_method`, Select One, Optional):** Choose how you want to order (Online, In Person, or Mail).
* **Payment Method (`payment_method`, Select One, Optional):** Select how you want to pay (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Terms (`payment_terms`, Date, Optional):** Enter the date you want to pay.
* **Award Shipping (`award_shipping`, Time, Optional):** Enter the time for award shipping.
* **Order Date (`order_date`, Date, Optional):** Enter the date you want to receive the award.
* **Delivery Date (`delivery_date`, Date, Optional):** Enter the date you want to receive the award.
* **Delivery Time (`delivery_time`, Time, Optional):** Choose when you want to deliver the award.
* **Payment Status (`payment_status`, Select One, Optional):** Confirm the payment status (Paid or Unpaid).
* **Trophy Details (`trophy_details`, Text, Optional):** Enter any additional details about the trophy.
* **Trophy Price (`trophy_price`, Number, Optional):** Enter the price of the trophy.
* **Total Price (`total_price`, Number, Optional):** Enter the total price of the order.
* **Payment Method Details (`payment_method_details`, Text, Optional):** Enter the details about your payment method.
* **Order Method Details (`order_method_details`, Text, Optional):** Enter additional details about the order method.
* **Award Shipping Address (`award_shipping_address`, Text, Optional):** Enter your award shipping address.
* **Award Shipping City (`award_shipping_city`, Text, Optional):** Enter the city for award shipping.
* **Award Shipping State (`award_shipping_state`, Text, Optional):** Enter the state for award shipping.
* **Award Shipping Postal (`award_shipping_postal`, Text, Optional):** Enter the postal code for award shipping.

Note: All fields are optional unless noted otherwise.
