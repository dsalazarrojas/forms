# video_recording_purchase_order - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for purchasing video recordings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the title of the video recording you wish to purchase.
2. Indicate if you are a client (yes or no).
3. Select the type of service you require (Audio Recording, Video Recording, or Live Streaming).
4. Enter any additional service information (this field is optional and can be used to include any other relevant details).
5. Select a schedule for your recording (if applicable).
6. Enter the recording date and time (if applicable).
7. Enter the price you are willing to pay for the recording.
8. Select the payment method you prefer (Credit Card or Bank Transfer).
9. Enter a unique order number (if applicable).
10. Add any notes about your order (if applicable).
11. Click the "Submit" button to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Video Recording Purchase Order** (1): Enter the title of the video recording you wish to purchase.
* **Client Information** (2): Check if you are a client for this order. This will help the service provider to know who to contact you about the order.
* **Service Type** (3): Select the type of service you require. You can choose from Audio Recording, Video Recording, or Live Streaming.
* **Service Information** (4): Enter any additional details that will help the service provider to complete your order. This is an optional field.
* **Schedule** (5): If you have a specific schedule in mind for your recording, select the date and time from the calendar.
* **Recording Date** (6): Enter the date and time you would like the recording to take place.
* **Price** (7): Enter the price you are willing to pay for the recording.
* **Payment Method** (8): Select the payment method you prefer. You can choose from Credit Card or Bank Transfer.
* **Order Number** (9): Enter a unique order number if you have been given one.
* **Notes** (10): Add any additional information about your order that you think is relevant. This is an optional field.
* **Submit** (11): Click this button to complete and submit your order.
