# Afterpay Household Products Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers order household products in a way that suits their needs and preferences using the Afterpay payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Customer Full Name** as it appears on your official ID.
2. Provide your **Email Address** for order confirmation and updates.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** for delivery updates.
4. Enter your **Shipping Address** for delivery purposes.
5. Select **Yes** or **No** to confirm if your **Billing Address** is the same as your shipping address.
6. If different, enter your **Billing Address** for billing purposes.
7. Choose the category of household products you're interested in by selecting from the **Product Category** options.
8. Enter the name of the specific **Specific Product Name** you want to order.
9. Specify your **Brand Preference** if you have one.
10. Enter the **Product Size or Quantity** you want to order.
11. Enter the **Quantity** of units you want to order.
12. Select your **Eco-Friendly Preference** if you have a preference.
13. If applicable, enter any **Allergy or Sensitive Requirements** you may have.
14. Specify the **Desired Features** for the product if needed.
15. Select your **Budget Range** if you have a budget in mind.
16. Choose your **Frequency of Purchase** if you have one.
17. Select your preferred **Shipping Method** for delivery.
18. Select your preferred **Payment Method**, which should be Afterpay for this form.
19. Finally, confirm that you agree to the store's **Terms and Conditions**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official ID.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for order confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for delivery updates.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the complete address for delivery purposes.
* **Billing Address (if different)**: Enter the complete address if different from your shipping address.
* **Product Category**: Choose the category of household products you're interested in from the options provided.
* **Specific Product Name**: Enter the name of the specific household product you want to order.
* **Brand Preference**: Enter your preferred brand if you have one (optional).
* **Product Size or Quantity**: Enter the size or quantity you want to order.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of units you want to order.
* **Eco-Friendly Preference**: Select **Yes** if you prefer eco-friendly or sustainable products.
* **Allergy or Sensitive Requirements**: Enter any allergy or sensitivity considerations if you have any.
* **Desired Features**: Enter any specific features you require for the product.
* **Budget Range**: Select your preferred budget range if you have one.
* **Frequency of Purchase**: Choose how often you typically purchase this type of product.
* **Shipping Preference**: Select your preferred shipping method from the options provided.
* **Payment Method**: Select **Afterpay** as your payment method.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Confirm that you agree to the store's terms and conditions.

Note: This form is designed to be filled out by customers who are using the Afterpay payment method. If you are not using Afterpay, please do not fill out this form.
