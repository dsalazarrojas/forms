# Holiday Gift Certificate Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Gift Certificate Request form is used to request a gift certificate for a colleague or team member from your company. This form helps the purchasing team process the request and ensure that the gift is delivered on time.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your company in the **Company Name** field.
2. Enter the name of the recipient in the **Recipient Name** field, following the format "Full name, e.g., John Smith".
3. Enter the amount of the gift certificate in the **Certificate Amount** field, as a numerical value (e.g., 10).
4. Choose the type of gift card you want to purchase from the **Gift Card Type** field.
5. Select a payment method from the **Payment Method** field (PayPal, Stripe, or Square).
6. Enter the date you would like the gift to be delivered on in the **Gift Delivery Date** field (e.g., 2024-02-15).
7. Enter the city, state, and zip for the shipping address in the **Shipping Address 1**, **Shipping Address 2**, and **Shipping Address 3** fields.
8. Enter the gift message in the **Gift Message** field.
9. Enter your billing name and address in the **Billing Name**, **Billing Address 1**, **Billing Address 2**, and **Billing Address 3** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Recipient Name**: Enter the full name of the recipient.
* **Certificate Amount**: Enter the amount of the gift certificate (e.g., 10).
* **Gift Card Type**: Select the type of gift card you want to purchase.
* **Payment Method**: Choose a payment method to use for the gift certificate (PayPal, Stripe, or Square).
* **Gift Delivery Date**: Select the date you would like the gift to be delivered on.
* **Shipping Address 1**, **Shipping Address 2**, and **Shipping Address 3**: Enter the city, state, and zip of the shipping address.
* **Gift Message**: Enter a message for the gift certificate.
* **Billing Name** and **Billing Address 1, 2, 3**: Enter your name and address for billing purposes.
* **Note**: You may add any additional notes or comments about the request.
* **Email**, **Phone**, **Fax**, and **Website**: Enter your contact information.
