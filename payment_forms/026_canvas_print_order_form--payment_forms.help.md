# Canvas Print Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Canvas Print Order Form is a critical step in creating a custom canvas print order. This form collects essential information about the user, canvas specifications, and image details to ensure accurate and timely production of the order.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read each section carefully and answer all questions and fields.
* Enter your full name, email address, and phone number to ensure we can contact you regarding your order.
* Select the canvas specifications that best fit your needs, including width, height, preset size, and material type.
* Choose the image file format and color profile, and describe the image details.
* Provide any special instructions or requests, such as color corrections.
* Confirm your order and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required)
Your full name is required to confirm your identity and ensure accurate communication.

### 2. **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required)
Enter your email address for contact purposes and order updates.

### 3. **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required)
Provide your phone number for contact purposes and order updates.

### 4. **Company Name (if applicable)** (`company_name`, `text`, optional)
If you are representing a company, enter your company name here.

### 5. **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, required)
Enter the address where you would like to receive your order.

### 6. **City** (`city`, `text`, required)
Enter the city for accurate delivery purposes.

### 7. **State/Province** (`state`, `text`, required)
Enter the state or province for accurate delivery purposes.

### 8. **ZIP or Postal Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required)
Enter your ZIP or postal code for accurate delivery purposes.

### 9. **Canvas Specifications** (`canvas_specifications_header`, `note`, required)
Select the canvas size, weight, material, and other specifications that fit your needs.

### 10. **Width (in inches)** (`width`, `number`, required)
Enter the width of your canvas in inches.

### 11. **Height (in inches)** (`height`, `number`, required)
Enter the height of your canvas in inches.

### 12. **Preset Size** (`preset_size`, `select_one`, optional)
Select a pre-defined canvas size that suits your needs.

### 13. **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, required)
Enter the number of prints you would like to order.

### 14. **Canvas Material** (`canvas_material`, `select_one`, required)
Select the type of canvas material that suits your needs.

### 15. **Canvas Weight** (`canvas_weight`, `select_one`, required)
Select the canvas weight that suits your needs.

### 16. **Stretching and Wrapping** (`stretching_option`, `select_one`, required)
Select the canvas stretching and wrapping option that suits your needs.

### 17. **Surface Finish** (`surface_finish`, `select_one`, required)
Select the canvas surface finish that suits your needs.

### 18. **Edge Color (for wrapped edges)** (`edge_color`, `select_one`, optional)
Select the edge color for wrapped edges, if applicable.

### 19. **Lamination** (`lamination`, `select_one`, required)
Select the lamination option that suits your needs.

### 20. **Image Information** (`image_information_header`, `note`, required)
Provide details about your image, including format and color profile.

### 21. **Image Description** (`image_description`, `text`, required)
Describe the image details, including any color corrections.

### 22. **Image Format** (`image_format`, `select_one`, required)
Select the image file format.

### 23. **Color Profile** (`color_profile`, `select_one`, optional)
Select the color profile that suits your needs.

### 24. **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions_header`, `note`, optional)
Provide any special instructions or requests, such as color corrections.

### 25. **Need Color Corrections?** (`color_corrections`, `select_one`, optional)
Indicate if you need color corrections.

### 26. **Special Requests or Notes** (`special_requests`, `text`, optional)
Provide any special requests or notes.

### 27. **I confirm this order and agree to the terms** (`order_confirmation`, `select_one`, required)
Confirm your order and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting.
* Use clear and concise answers to ensure easy production.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
