# Graduation Lei Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for a graduation lei, a traditional Hawaiian garland worn by graduates to commemorate their special day. You will be asked to provide information about the delivery of the lei, including whether you want to pickup or have it delivered, the student's name and school name, the delivery date, time, and address, as well as your payment method.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review this form carefully before starting.
2. Select the option for "Pickup or Delivery" from the dropdown list.
3. Enter the student's name in the format of "First Last" (e.g., "John Smith").
4. Enter the school name (e.g., University of California).
5. Enter the delivery date in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 12/15/2023).
6. Select the delivery method (Pickup or Delivery).
7. If applicable, enter the pickup time (e.g., 2:00 PM).
8. Enter the school or pickup address.
9. Enter your phone number.
10. Enter your email address.
11. Select the payment method (Cash, Check, Credit Card).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pickup or Delivery**: Choose whether you want to pickup your lei order at our shop or have it delivered to your school or address. Please select "Pickup" if you plan to visit our shop, or select "Delivery" if you'd like us to handle the logistics.
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name in the format of "First Last" (e.g., "John Smith").
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school where the student graduated (e.g., University of California).
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date you'd like to receive the lei in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 12/15/2023).
* **Delivery Method**: Select whether you'd like to have the lei delivered or pickup it yourself.
* **Pickup Time**: If you've selected "Pickup," enter the time you'd like to pickup your lei order (e.g., 2:00 PM).
* **Address**: Enter the school or address where you'd like to have the lei delivered or picked up.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can keep you updated on your order status.
* **Payment Method**: Select how you'd like to pay for your lei order (Cash, Check, Credit Card).

Note: Please review your information carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness of your order.
