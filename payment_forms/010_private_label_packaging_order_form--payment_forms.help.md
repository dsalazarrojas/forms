# private_label_packaging_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for ordering private label packaging for products. It collects information about the product and the packaging details to facilitate the private label packaging process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the order in the "form_title" field.
2. Provide the name of the product in the "product_name" field.
3. Enter the product description in the "product_description" field.
4. Enter the product color in the "product_color" field.
5. If you want to provide an image reference or product design idea, you can enter it in the "product_image" field.
6. Select the packaging type from the options provided in the "packaging_type" field.
7. Choose the packaging size from the options provided in the "packaging_size" field.
8. Enter the quantity of the product in the "quantity" field.
9. Enter the delivery date in the "delivery_date" field.
10. Enter the delivery time in the "delivery_time" field.
11. Enter the delivery address in the "delivery_address" field.
12. Enter the phone number in the "phone" field.
13. Enter the email in the "email" field.
14. If you want to add any additional comments, you can enter them in the "comments" field.
15. Review your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **form_title** (`title`, required): This is the title of the order.
- **product_name** (`product_name`, required): Enter the name of the product being ordered.
- **product_description** (`product_description`, required): Enter a description of the product being ordered.
- **product_color** (`product_color`, required): Enter the color of the product being ordered.
- **product_image** (`product_image`, optional): This field is optional for providing an image reference or product design idea.
- **packaging_type** (`packaging_type`, required): Select the type of packaging required.
- **packaging_size** (`packaging_size`, required): Choose the size of the packaging required.
- **quantity** (`quantity`, required): Enter the quantity of the product being ordered.
- **delivery_date** (`delivery_date`, required): Enter the delivery date for the ordered product.
- **delivery_time** (`delivery_time`, required): Enter the delivery time for the ordered product.
- **delivery_address** (`delivery_address`, required): Enter the delivery address for the product.
- **phone** (`phone`, required): Enter the phone number of the recipient.
- **email** (`email`, required): Enter the email of the recipient.
- **comments** (`comments`, optional): This field is optional for any additional comments.
- **form_submit** (`form_submit`, optional): This field is a form submit button.
- **form_reset** (`form_reset`, optional): This field is for resetting the form.
- **form_cancel** (`form_cancel`, optional): This field is for canceling the form.
- **form_back** (`form_back`, optional): This field is for going back in the form.
- **form_save** (`form_save`, optional): This field is for saving the form.
- **form_delete** (`form_delete`, optional): This field is for deleting the form.
- **form_preview** (`form_preview`, optional): This field is for previewing the form.
- **form_print** (`form_print`, optional): This field is for printing the form.
- **form_download** (`form_download`, optional): This field is for downloading the form.
- **form_help** (`form_help`, optional): This field is for accessing help for the form.
