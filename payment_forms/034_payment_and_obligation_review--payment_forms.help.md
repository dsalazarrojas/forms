# Payment And Obligation Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payment And Obligation Review form is used to collect payment and obligation details from clients to help them understand their payment obligations and to assist service providers in tracking and managing payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the payment terms and status provided.
2.  Select all payment terms that apply to your account.
3.  Enter the payment date and due date if applicable.
4.  Select the payment status (e.g., paid, outstanding).
5.  Enter the payment amount if applicable.
6.  Confirm whether you have paid or not.
7.  Add any additional notes or comments about your payment obligations.
8.  Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
9.  Review and confirm all the information entered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all payment terms that apply to your account.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the payment if applicable.
* **Due Date** (`due_date`, `date`, required): Enter the due date of the payment if applicable.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment status (e.g., paid, outstanding).
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the payment amount if applicable.
* **Paid** (`paid`, `select_one`, required): Confirm whether you have paid or not.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required): Add any additional notes or comments about your payment obligations.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, `time`, required): Enter the date of the review.
* **Payment Terms 1-14** (`payment_terms_1-14`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all payment terms that apply to your account.
