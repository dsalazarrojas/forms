# winery_vintage_collection_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The winery's vintage collection pre-order form is designed to collect order and customer information for customers interested in pre-ordering a selection of wines. This form helps the winery gather essential details to fulfill orders efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Order Details** section with the required information.
2. Provide **Delivery Details** to ensure accurate shipping and delivery.
3. Confirm **Customer Confirmation** to verify that the order is accurate.
4. Enter **Payment Details** to complete the order.
5. Add your **Customer Details** for the winery to keep records up to date.
6. Review **Order Confirmation** for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details**: Enter the details of your order, including the type of collection and any specific wines you'd like to pre-order. This section helps the winery prepare for your order.
* **Delivery Details**: Enter your delivery address and any special requests for shipping, such as a specific date or gift wrapping. This ensures the winery gets your order to you on time and in the right condition.
* **Customer Confirmation**: Double-check the information provided in the previous fields to ensure accuracy. This step helps prevent errors and saves time for the winery's staff.
* **Payment Details**: Enter your payment method and any relevant payment information. This ensures the winery receives your payment for the order.
* **Customer Details**: Provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details to help the winery keep your records up to date.
* **Order Confirmation**: Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting. This final step helps you verify that your order is correct and complete.
