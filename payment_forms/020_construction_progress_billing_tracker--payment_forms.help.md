# Construction Progress Billing Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Construction Progress Billing Tracker is a form used to track the progress and financial status of construction projects. It helps project managers and billing managers to submit billing information to clients or stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
3. Review your submission before submitting it to the client or stakeholder.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, *): Enter the name of the project being tracked.
* **Project ID or Number** (`project_id`, text, *): Enter the unique identifier or number assigned to the project.
* **Billing Period Number** (`billing_period_number`, number, *): Enter the billing period number (e.g., Period 1, Period 12).
* **Period Start Date** (`period_start_date`, date, *): Enter the start date of the current billing period.
* **Period End Date** (`period_end_date`, date, *): Enter the end date of the current billing period.
* **Original Contract Amount ($)** (`original_contract_amount`, number, *): Enter the original contract amount.
* **Net Value of Change Orders ($)** (`net_change_orders`, number, *): Enter the net value of change orders, using negative for credits.
* **Revised Total Contract Amount ($)** (`total_contract_to_date`, number, *): Enter the revised total contract amount, which includes the original contract amount and net change orders.
* **Total Work Completed and Stored to Date ($)** (`total_work_to_date`, number, *): Enter the total work completed and stored to date, including the current period.
* **Previous Work Completed and Stored to Date ($)** (`previous_work_to_date`, number, *): Enter the amount of work completed and stored from the previous billing period.
* **Total Work Completed this Period ($)** (`current_period_work`, number, *): Enter the difference between the total work completed and stored to date and the previous period.
* **Retainage Percentage (%)** (`retainage_percentage`, number, *): Enter the retainage percentage (usually 5% or 10%).
* **Total Retainage Amount to Date ($)** (`total_retainage_to_date`, number, *): Enter the total retainage amount withheld.
* **Total Earned Less Retainage ($)** (`total_earned_less_retainage`, number, *): Enter the net amount earned to date.
* **Net Amount Due this Period ($)** (`net_payment_due_period`, number, *): Enter the amount requested for the current payment.
* **Balance to Finish ($)** (`balance_to_finish`, number, *): Enter the balance to finish, which is the total contract amount less the total earned less retainage.
* **Billing Manager Name** (`billing_manager_name`, text, *): Enter the name of the billing manager submitting the form.
* **Date of Billing Submission** (`submission_date`, date, *): Enter the date of submission of the billing form.

Note: This help guide assumes that all fields are marked with an asterisk (*) to indicate that they are required. If a field is not marked with an asterisk, it is optional.
