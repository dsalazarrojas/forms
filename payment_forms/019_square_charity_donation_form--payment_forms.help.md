# Square Charity Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Square Charity Donation Form is a payment form designed for charities and non-profit organizations to collect donations from users online. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1. **Enter a Title**: Enter a title for your donation in the first field. This is a required field, please provide a clear and concise title that describes the reason for your donation.
2. **Add a Description** (Optional): If you'd like to provide more context about your donation, you can add a description in the second field. This is an optional field, but can be helpful for the charity to understand your intentions.
3. **Specify the Donation Amount**: In the third field, enter the amount you'd like to donate. Please make sure to enter a valid number.
4. **Choose a Currency**: Select the currency for your donation from the options provided (USD, EUR, JPY, CNY). This will ensure the charity receives the correct payment.
5. **Enter Email Address**: In the fifth field, enter your email address. This will be used to contact you with updates and information about your donation.
6. **Provide a Phone Number (Optional)**: If you have a phone number that you'd like to be contacted, you can enter it in the sixth field. This is optional and not required for a donation to be processed.
7. **Add Additional Notes**: If you'd like to provide additional comments or information about your donation, you can do so in the seventh field.
8. **Submit**: Finally, click the 'Submit' button to complete your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, Text, Required): A brief title for your donation.
* **Description** (`form_description`, Text, Optional): A description of your donation, if desired.
* **Amount** (`form_amount`, Number, Required): The amount you'd like to donate. Enter a valid number.
* **Currency** (`form_currency`, Select One, Required): The currency of your donation. Choose from USD, EUR, JPY, or CNY.
* **Email** (`form_email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address. This will be used for updates and information about your donation.
* **Phone** (`form_phone`, Text, Optional): If you'd like to be contacted, enter your phone number.
* **Note** (`form_note`, Note, Optional): A space to add additional comments or information about your donation.
* **Submit** (`form_submit`, Text, Required): Click this button to complete and submit your donation.
