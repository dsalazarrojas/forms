# Custom Cufflink Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Cufflink Order Form is used to collect information for a custom cufflink order. This form gathers details such as recipient preferences, cufflink type and size, shipping information, and customer contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the desired recipient for the cufflink.
2. Choose the cufflink type and size that best suits your needs.
3. Provide the shipping address and city where the cufflink will be delivered.
4. Enter the shipping state and zip code for the delivery location.
5. Enter the total price of the order (this will be calculated automatically based on the selected options).
6. Enter your customer name and email address for communication and order tracking purposes.
7. Optionally, enter your phone number for further contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recipient** (`recipient`, select multiple): Select the desired recipient for the cufflink. You can choose multiple recipients by holding the Ctrl key (or Command key on Mac) and clicking on multiple options.
* **Recipient Relationship** (`recipient_relationship`, select one): Choose the relationship between you and the recipient, such as a gift or personal use.
* **Cufflink Type** (`cufflink_type`, select one): Select the type of cufflink you desire, such as silver or gold.
* **Cufflink Size** (`cufflink_size`, select one): Choose the size of the cufflink that best fits your needs, such as small, medium, or large.
* **Cufflink Color** (`cufflink_color`, select one): Select the color of the cufflink you prefer, such as blue or red.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text): Enter the shipping address where the cufflink will be delivered.
* **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, text): Enter the city where the cufflink will be shipped.
* **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`, select one): Select the state where the cufflink will be shipped, such as Alabama or Alaska.
* **Shipping Zip** (`shipping_zip`, number): Enter the zip code for the shipping address.
* **Total Price** (`total_price`, number): This field will be automatically calculated based on your selected options.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text): Enter your customer name for order tracking and communication purposes.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email): Enter your email address for order updates and communication.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text): Optionally, enter your phone number for further contact and order updates.
