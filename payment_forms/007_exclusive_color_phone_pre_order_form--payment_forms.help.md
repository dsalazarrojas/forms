# Exclusive Color Phone Pre-Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and payment details for customers who want to pre-order exclusive color phones. The form ensures that the customer has provided necessary billing and shipping information, selected a payment method, and submitted their order. It also allows customers to input their email address for communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the Order Info section with your order details.
2. Select a payment method from the dropdown menu (Credit Card, PayPal, or Apple Pay).
3. Input your payment amount.
4. Provide your billing information in the Billing Info section.
5. Enter your shipping information in the Shipping Info section.
6. Confirm that your order has been submitted correctly by selecting "Submit Order" (if you're ready to place the order) or "Not Submit" (if you need to make changes).
7. If you want to receive updates and information about your order, input your email address in the Email field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Info**: This field allows you to enter your order details, such as phone model and color.
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method from the dropdown menu. You can choose between Credit Card, PayPal, and Apple Pay.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the payment amount for your order. This field is a number field, so please input a numeric value.
* **Billing Info**: Enter your billing information, such as name, address, and other details as required.
* **Shipping Info**: Enter your shipping information, such as shipping address and any special instructions.
* **Submit Order**: Confirm that your order has been submitted correctly. If you are ready to place the order, select "True" and click Submit. If you need to make changes, select "False".
* **Email**: Input your email address to receive updates and information about your order.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you input is accurate and complete.
* Double-check your payment amount before submitting your order.
* Make sure to input your email address correctly.
* If you need to make changes to your order, select "Not Submit" in the Submit Order field and contact our support team for assistance.
