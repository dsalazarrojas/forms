# Fitness Club Supplement Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Club Supplement Order Form is designed to collect information for the efficient ordering of supplements. This form is used by users to provide their personal details and order specifications to ensure accurate and timely delivery of the supplements ordered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. Select the date you would like to order the supplement from the Order Date field (if applicable).
5. Choose the type of supplement you would like to order from the Supplement field.
6. Enter the quantity of the supplement you would like to order in the Quantity field.
7. Select the payment method for your order from the Payment Method field (multiple options can be selected).
8. Add any additional notes about your order in the Notes field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name (text)**: Enter your first name.
- **Last Name (text)**: Enter your last name.
- **Email (email)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
- **Order Date (date)**: Select the date you would like to order the supplement (optional).
- **Order Type (text)**: Choose the type of supplement you would like to order (optional).
- **Supplement (select_one)**: Select the type of supplement you would like to order from the available options.
- **Quantity (number)**: Enter the quantity of the supplement you would like to order.
- **Pickup Date (date)**: Select the date you would like to pick up the supplement (optional).
- **Pickup Time (time)**: Select the time you would like to pick up the supplement (optional).
- **Delivery Address (text)**: Enter your delivery address (if applicable).
- **Payment Method (select_multiple)**: Select the payment method(s) you would like to use for your order.
- **Notes (note)**: Add any additional notes about your order (optional).
