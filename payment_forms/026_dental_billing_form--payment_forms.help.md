# dental_billing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect patient billing information and payment details for dental treatments. This form is used to capture essential data for processing payments and updating patient records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information on the **patient_info** field. This field is a text field where you can enter the patient's name, contact details, and other relevant information.
2. Provide billing information in the **billing_info** field. This field is also a text field where you can enter payment details such as invoice numbers, due dates, or other relevant billing information. This field is required to be filled in order to proceed with the payment.
3. Select the **payment_method** field from the dropdown options. You can choose from Cash, Credit Card, Debit Card, or Eft/Transfer as the payment method. This field is optional but recommended to be filled in for accurate payment processing.
4. Select the **payment_status** field from the dropdown options. You can choose from Paid, Pending, or Overdue as the payment status. This field is required to be filled in to determine the current payment status.
5. Enter the **total_amount** field with the total amount due for the dental treatment. This field is a numerical field where you can enter the exact amount. This field is required to be filled in to process the payment.
6. Add any additional payment notes or comments in the **payment_notes** field. This field is a text field where you can enter any relevant notes or comments about the payment. This field is optional but recommended to provide additional context.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_info** (text, required/optional): Enter the patient's name, contact details, and other relevant information.
* **billing_info** (text, required): Enter payment details such as invoice numbers, due dates, or other relevant billing information.
* **payment_method** (select_multiple, optional): Select the payment method from the dropdown options: Cash, Credit Card, Debit Card, or Eft/Transfer.
* **payment_status** (select_one, required): Select the payment status from the dropdown options: Paid, Pending, or Overdue.
* **total_amount** (number, required): Enter the exact amount due for the dental treatment.
* **payment_notes** (text, optional): Enter any additional payment notes or comments.
