# Winter Collection Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Winter Collection Pre Order Form is a tool used to gather information from customers who have pre-ordered a winter collection. It helps us update the order status and ensure that we have all necessary information to process the pre-order correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the payment method you would like to use for your pre-order.
2. Choose the delivery details that suit your needs.
3. Enter your delivery date and time.
4. Provide your shipping address, city, state, and country.
5. Enter any additional notes or instructions about your pre-order.
6. Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates.
7. Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
8. Enter your name for our records.
9. Enter any additional notes or instructions for our team.
10. Finally, select the order status of your pre-order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Form**: This field is for any additional information or special requests for your pre-order. It is not required but can be useful for our team to process your pre-order correctly.
* **Payment Method**: This field allows you to choose how you would like to pay for your pre-order. Options include Credit Card, PayPal, and Other. If you choose "Other", please provide more details in the "Payment Method Other" field.
* **Payment Method Other**: This field is only visible if you choose "Other" as your payment method. Please provide more details about your payment method in this field.
* **Delivery Details**: This field allows you to choose how you would like to receive your pre-ordered items. Options include In-store pickup, Home delivery, and Other. If you choose "Other", please provide more details in the "Delivery Details" field.
* **Delivery Date**: This field is for selecting the date when you would like to receive your pre-ordered items.
* **Delivery Time**: This field is for selecting the time when you would like to receive your pre-ordered items.
* **Shipping Address**: This field is for providing your shipping address, including the street address, city, state, and country.
* **Shipping City**: This field is for selecting the city where you would like to receive your pre-ordered items.
* **Shipping State**: This field is for selecting the state where you would like to receive your pre-ordered items.
* **Shipping Zip**: This field is for entering the postal/zip code where you would like to receive your pre-ordered items.
* **Shipping Country**: This field is for selecting the country where you would like to receive your pre-ordered items.
* **Order Status**: This field is for selecting the status of your pre-order. Options include New, Shipped, Pending, and Canceled.
* **Order Notes**: This field is for entering any additional notes or instructions for our team to process your pre-order.
* **Customer Email**: This field is for entering your email address so we can contact you with updates about your pre-order.
* **Customer Phone**: This field is for entering your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Customer Name**: This field is for entering your name for our records.
* **Customer Note**: This field is for entering any additional notes or instructions for our team.
* **Customer Shipping Name**: This field is for entering the name of the person receiving the pre-ordered items.
* **Notes**: This field is for entering any additional notes or instructions for our team.
* **Notes**: This field is for entering any additional notes or instructions for our team.
