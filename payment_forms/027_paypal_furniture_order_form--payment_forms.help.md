# Paypal Furniture Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect payment and order information from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your required and optional information.
2. Review your details carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (<id: 1>)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (<id: 2>)**: Enter your last name.
* **Address (<id: 3>)**: Enter your address.
* **City (<id: 4>)**: Enter the city of your address.
* **State (<id: 5>)**: Enter the state of your address.
* **Zip (<id: 6>)**: Enter your zip code.
* **Items ordered (<id: 7>)**: Enter the number of items you are ordering.
* **Order date (<id: 8>)**: Enter the date of your order. Please use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Payment method (<id: 9>)**: Select either PayPal or credit card as your payment method.
* **Phone (<id: 10>)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email (<id: 11>)**: Enter your email address.
* **Confirm payment (<id: 12>)**: Please confirm your payment details carefully.
* **Order total (<id: 13>)**: Enter the total cost of your order.
* **First Name (<id: 14>)**: This field is a duplicate of the first name field, please ignore it.
* **Last Name (<id: 15>)**: This field is a duplicate of the last name field, please ignore it.
