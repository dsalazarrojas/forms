# payment_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a payment from a submitter for processing transactions between two parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Ensure that you are not duplicating a request for the same transaction.
3. Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Request Form (submitter_name)** (`submitter_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Amount (payment_amount)** (`payment_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the total amount of the payment request.
* **Payment Method (payment_method)** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select the payment method you wish to use (Bank, Cash, Check).
* **Payment Terms (payment_terms)** (`payment_terms`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Choose one or more payment terms (Installment payments, Lump sum payments, Other).
* **Date of Payment (date_of_payment)** (`date_of_payment`, date, required: false): Enter the date the payment is due.
* **Time of Payment (time_of_payment)** (`time_of_payment`, time, required: false): Enter the time the payment is due.
* **Notes (notes)** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional information regarding the payment request.
* **Email (submitter_email)** (`submitter_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email for contact purposes.
* **Phone (submitter_phone)** (`submitter_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Double-check the form for accurate and complete information before submission.
* Review the submitted form carefully to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
