# Raffle Ticket Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Raffle Ticket Order Form is a form used for customers to order raffle tickets. It requests information about the customer, the type of ticket they want, the quantity, payment method, and other order details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Customer Name" and "Customer Email" fields.
2. Select the type of ticket you want to order (Single, Double, or Triple) in the "Ticket Type" field.
3. Enter the number of tickets you want to order in the "Quantity" field.
4. Choose your preferred payment method (PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Credit Card) in the "Payment Method" field.
5. Fill in the total amount for the order in the "Order Total" field.
6. Check the "Payment Status" field to reflect the status of your payment.
7. Select the status of your order (Confirmed, Pending, or Cancelled) from the "Order Status" field.
8. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
9. Finally, submit the form by filling in a confirmation code in the "Submit Form" field.
10. If you have any specific message for the customer service, you can enter it in the "Customer Note" field.
11. Enter the date and time of your order in the "Order Date" and "Order Time" fields.
12. Enter the ticket number, if any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Raffle Ticket Details**: A description of the ticket you want to order. Please leave this field blank.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as you want it to appear on the ticket.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you if there's a problem with your order.
* **Ticket Type**: Select the type of ticket you want to order (Single, Double, or Triple).
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of tickets you want to order.
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method (PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Credit Card).
* **Total Tickets**: Enter the total number of tickets you are ordering.
* **Order Total**: Enter the total amount of your order.
* **Payment Status**: This field will be updated automatically after you make a payment.
* **Order Status**: Select the status of your order (Confirmed, Pending, or Cancelled).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Submit Form**: A confirmation code to submit your order.
* **Customer Note**: If you have any message for the customer service, enter it here.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date of your order.
* **Order Time**: Enter the time of your order.
* **Ticket Number**: If you have a ticket number, enter it here.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your order details before submitting the form.
* If you have any question or issue, contact our customer service.
* You can use the "Customer Note" field to add any specific information about your order.
