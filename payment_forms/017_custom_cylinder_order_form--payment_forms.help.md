# Custom Cylinder Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for custom-made cylinders with specific dimensions, material, and features. To complete this form, you will need to provide the necessary details about your business, customer, and order requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the required fields, such as business name, customer name, and contact information (email and phone).
2. Specify the dimensions of the cylinder you require, including width and height.
3. Choose the material from the list provided (Acrylic, Aluminum, Glass, Plastic, or Steel).
4. Indicate the quantity of cylinders you need.
5. Select the payment method you prefer (Bank Transfer, Credit Card, or PayPal).
6. Add any specific features you require for your order.
7. Enter the total price you are expecting to pay.
8. Provide the date and time for the order.
9. Add any additional notes or comments about the order.
10. Finally, confirm your order details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Enter your business name for reference.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the customer associated with this order.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your company email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your company phone number.
* **Cylinders Width** (`cylinder_width`, number, optional): Specify the width of the cylinder.
* **Cylinders Height** (`cylinder_height`, number, optional): Specify the height of the cylinder.
* **Material** (`material`, select_one, optional): Choose the material for the cylinder from the list (Acrylic, Aluminum, Glass, Plastic, or Steel).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Indicate the number of cylinders you need.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select the payment method you prefer (Bank Transfer, Credit Card, or PayPal).
* **Features** (`features`, text, optional): Enter any specific features you require for your order.
* **Total Price** (`total_price`, number, optional): Enter the total price you are expecting to pay.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date for the order.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time for the order.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the order.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm your order details by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Order Confirmation** (`order_confirmation`, text, optional): Enter the order confirmation for reference.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information you provide to avoid errors in the order process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our customer support team for assistance.
