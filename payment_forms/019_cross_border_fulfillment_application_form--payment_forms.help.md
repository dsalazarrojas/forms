# cross_border_fulfillment_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect business and individual identifying information, as well as scheduling preferences from businesses seeking cross-border fulfillment services.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Enter your business name in the first field, "business_name". This is a text field where you can provide your company's name.
2.  Enter your address in the second field, "address". This is also a text field where you can provide your business's address.
3.  Enter your date of birth in the third field, "date_of_birth". This is a date field where you can select your date of birth.
4.  Enter your time preference in the fourth field, "time". This is a time field where you can select your preferred time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Business Name (Business Name, text, required: false)**: Enter your business's name.
*   **Address (Address, text, required: false)**: Enter your business's address.
*   **Date of Birth (Date of Birth, date, required: false)**: Select your date of birth.
*   **Time (Time, time, required: false)**: Select your preferred time.

## Tips
*   Be accurate and up-to-date with the information provided.
*   Review all fields carefully to avoid errors.
*   Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and consistent with your business's identity.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
