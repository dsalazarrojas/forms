# Baby Shower Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for a baby shower celebration. Please fill out the form to place an order for baby shower supplies, such as decorations, tableware, food, beverages, games, and party favors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information: Enter your name, email address, and phone number to allow us to contact you regarding your order.
2. Enter event details: Provide the date of the baby shower event and select the delivery method you prefer (e.g., home delivery, store pickup, etc.).
3. Choose your order preferences: Select the decorations, tableware, food, beverages, games, and party favors you wish to order.
4. Customize your order: Select a theme and color scheme for your baby shower.
5. Finalize your order: Enter your payment method and estimated order total.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`orderer_name`, text, required): Enter your name to allow us to contact you regarding your order.
* **Email Address** (`orderer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for order confirmation and communication.
* **Phone Number** (`orderer_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you regarding your order.
* **Baby Shower Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the baby shower event.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, required): Select how you would like to receive your order:
	+ **Home Delivery**: We will deliver to your home.
	+ **Store Pickup**: You will pick up your order at our store.
	+ **Event Venue Delivery**: We will deliver to the venue of the baby shower.
	+ **Ship to Address**: We will ship to the address you provide.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required): Enter the address where you would like to receive your order if it's not delivered to your home.
* **Decorations** (`decorations_order`, select_multiple, required): Select the decorations you would like to order:
	+ **Balloon bouquet**: Decorations for the baby shower celebration.
	+ **Welcome banner**: A banner welcoming the baby shower guests.
	+ **Table centerpieces**: Decorations for the tables.
	+ **Photo backdrop**: A backdrop for taking photos.
	+ **Streamers**: Decorations for the party area.
	+ **None**: No decorations.
* **Tableware** (`tableware_order`, select_multiple, required): Select the tableware you would like to order:
	+ **Plates, small**: Plates for appetizers or desserts.
	+ **Plates, large**: Plates for main courses.
	+ **Cups**: Cups for beverages.
	+ **Napkins**: Napkins for cleaning hands and faces.
	+ **Plastic utensils**: Utensils for eating.
	+ **Tablecloth**: A tablecloth for the tables.
	+ **Serving trays**: Trays for serving food.
	+ **None**: No tableware.
* **Food Items** (`food_order`, select_multiple, required): Select the food items you would like to order:
	+ **Cupcake set**: A set of cupcakes.
	+ **Cake**: A cake for the celebration.
	+ **Cookie platter**: A platter of cookies.
	+ **Fruit platter**: A platter of fruit.
	+ **Vegetable platter**: A platter of vegetables.
	+ **Sandwich platter**: A platter of sandwiches.
	+ **Cheese platter**: A platter of cheese and crackers.
	+ **None**: No food items.
* **Beverages** (`beverage_order`, select_multiple, required): Select the beverages you would like to order:
	+ **Punch**: A punch bowl for the celebration.
	+ **Juice boxes**: Juice boxes for the guests.
	+ **Soda**: Soda for the celebration.
	+ **Water bottles**: Water bottles for the guests.
	+ **Coffee**: Coffee for the adults.
	+ **Tea**: Tea for the adults.
	+ **None**: No beverages.
* **Games and Activities** (`games_order`, select_multiple, required): Select the games and activities you would like to order:
	+ **Baby bingo cards**: Baby bingo cards for the guests.
	+ **Baby food tasting kit**: A kit for tasting baby foods.
	+ **Diaper raffle tickets**: Diaper raffle tickets for the guests.
	+ **Advice cards**: Cards for advice and wishes for the baby.
	+ **Pen set for games**: A set of pens for the games.
	+ **Prize items**: Prize items for the games.
	+ **None**: No games and activities.
* **Party Favors** (`party_favors_order`, select_multiple, required): Select the party favors you would like to order:
	+ **Mini candles**: Mini candles for the guests.
	+ **Soap bars**: Soap bars for the guests.
	+ **Seed packets**: Seed packets for the guests.
	+ **Candy bags**: Candy bags for the guests.
	+ **Keychains**: Keychains for the guests.
	+ **Photo frames**: Photo frames for the guests.
	+ **None**: No party favors.
* **Theme Selection** (`theme_selection`, select_one, required): Select the theme for your baby shower:
	+ **Classic Baby**: A classic baby shower theme.
	+ **Rubber Ducky**: A rubber ducky theme.
	+ **Teddy Bear**: A teddy bear theme.
	+ **Safari**: A safari theme.
	+ **Storybook**: A storybook theme.
	+ **Floral**: A floral theme.
	+ **No specific theme**: No theme selected.
* **Color Preference** (`color_preference`, select_multiple, required): Select the primary colors for your baby shower:
	+ **Pink**: Pink.
	+ **Blue**: Blue.
	+ **Yellow**: Yellow.
	+ **Green**: Green.
	+ **White**: White.
	+ **Gold**: Gold.
	+ **Silver**: Silver.
	+ **Multicolor**: Multi-colored.
* **Expected Guest Count** (`guest_count`, number, required): Enter the estimated number of guests for the baby shower.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any special requests or notes for your order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select how you would like to pay:
	+ **Credit Card**: Pay with a credit card.
	+ **Debit Card**: Pay with a debit card.
	+ **PayPal**: Pay with PayPal.
	+ **Cash on Pickup**: Pay with cash when you pick up your order.
	+ **Invoice**: Pay by invoice.
* **Estimated Order Total** (`order_total`, number, optional): Enter an estimated order total (not required but recommended for budgeting).
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are placing this order.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or information for your order.

## Tips

* Please review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* We will do our best to accommodate any special requests, but please note that some items may not be available upon request.
* Estimated order total is not required but is recommended for budgeting purposes.
