# Luxury Bed Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help luxury bed customers provide their order details and payment information to complete a purchase.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer information in the "Customer Information" section.
2. Enter your order details in the "Order Details" section.
3. Provide your delivery information in the "Delivery Info" section.
4. Enter your billing information in the "Billing Info" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required: false): This is the section where you can enter your name, email, and any other customer information we might need to know.
- **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required: false): Here, you can provide more details about your order, such as the type of bed you're interested in or any special requests.
- **Delivery Info** (`delivery_info`, text, required: false): In this section, please enter the address where you'd like your luxury bed delivered.
- **Billing Info** (`billing_info`, text, required: false): Here, you can enter your billing information, such as your name, email, and payment method.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* This form is designed for one-time use, so please do not fill it out multiple times.
* You can use this form to place an order for a luxury bed.
