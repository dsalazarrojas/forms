# Online Purchase Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about an online purchase, including delivery preferences, customer details, order details, and contact information. It is intended to be filled out once for each purchase or transaction.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Make sure to select a valid payment method.
3. Enter accurate contact information.
4. Enter any additional notes if needed.
5. Click submit to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Preferences** (delivery_preferences): Enter your preferred delivery options (e.g., in-store pickup, home delivery, etc.)
* **Customer Details** (customer_details): Enter your name, address, and other relevant customer information.
* **Order Details** (order_details): Enter details about the items being purchased, such as quantity and description.
* **Contact Details** (contact_details): Enter your contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Payment Details** (payment_details): Enter payment information, including your chosen payment method (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, PayPal).
* **Delivery Time** (delivery_time): Enter the desired delivery time for your order.
* **Order Date** (order_date): Enter the date of the order.
* **Payment Method** (payment_method): Select a valid payment method from the options provided (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, PayPal).
* **Order Items** (order_items): Enter details about the items being purchased.
* **Notes** (notes): Enter any additional notes or comments about the order.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (phone): Enter your phone number.
