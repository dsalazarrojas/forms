# Afterpay Electronic Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Afterpay Electronic Product Order Form is used to place electronic product orders securely and accurately through the Afterpay payment option. It ensures that buyers provide their necessary details for order processing and receipt delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Buyer name and Email for receipt delivery.
2. Enter the Product Model or SKU.
3. Select if a Warranty is required.
4. Enter the Quantity of units to purchase.
5. Choose your preferred Shipping option.
6. Select to use Afterpay payment.
7. Enter your Delivery Address for shipping.
8. (Optional) Add any Additional Notes for installation purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Buyer name** (Buyer name, text, required): Enter your full name for order records.
* **Buyer email** (Buyer email, email, required): Enter your email for receipt delivery.
* **Product model** (Product model, text, required): Enter the product model or SKU for order processing.
* **Warranty required** (Warranty required, select_one, required): Select "True" if you want to purchase a warranty.
* **Quantity** (Quantity, number, required): Enter the number of units to purchase.
* **Shipping option** (Shipping option, select_one, required): Choose your preferred shipping speed: Standard, Express, or Overnight.
* **Pay with Afterpay** (Pay with Afterpay, select_one, required): Select "True" to use Afterpay payment.
* **Delivery address** (Delivery address, text, required): Enter your delivery address including street, city, and postal code.
* **Additional notes** (Additional notes, text, optional): Enter any necessary installation notes if required.
