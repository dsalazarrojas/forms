# Anniversary Event Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from customers for their anniversary event, ensuring that their order is accurate and meets their expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as it appears on your ID in the "Full Name" field.
2. Provide your email address to receive order confirmations in the "Email Address" field.
3. Enter your phone number, including the area code, in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Select the date of your anniversary event in the "Event Date" field.
5. Choose the type of anniversary event from the "Event Type" dropdown menu.
6. Estimate the number of guests attending in the "Expected Guest Count" field.
7. Enter the name of the event venue in the "Venue Name" field (optional).
8. Select your preferred catering package from the "Catering Package Selection" dropdown menu.
9. Choose any dietary requirements from the "Dietary Requirements" dropdown menu.
10. Select your beverage preferences from the "Beverage Package" dropdown menu.
11. Choose your decoration style from the "Decoration Package" dropdown menu (optional).
12. Enter any additional requirements or preferences in the "Special Requests" field (optional).
13. Select the preferred delivery date in the "Preferred Delivery Date" field.
14. Choose your preferred delivery time in the "Preferred Delivery Time" field (optional).
15. Select your payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
16. Enter the deposit amount required to secure the order in the "Deposit Amount" field.
17. Enter your full billing address for payment purposes in the "Billing Address" field.
17. Accept or decline the terms and conditions in the "Terms and Conditions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
* **Email Address** (email, required): We will send order confirmations to this email address.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Include area code. 
* **Event Date** (date, required): Select the date of your anniversary event.
* **Event Type** (select_one, required): Choose the type of anniversary event (Wedding Anniversary, Business Anniversary, Organization Anniversary, or Other).
* **Expected Guest Count** (number, required): Estimate the number of guests attending.
* **Venue Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the event venue.
* **Catering Package Selection** (select_one, required): Choose your preferred catering package (Basic, Standard, Premium, or Custom).
* **Dietary Requirements** (select_multiple, required): Select all applicable dietary requirements (None, Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Kosher, Halal, or Nut Allergies).
* **Beverage Package** (select_one, required): Select your beverage preferences (Non-Alcoholic Only, Beer and Wine, Full Bar, or Custom Selection).
* **Decoration Package** (select_one, optional): Choose your decoration style (No Decorations, Basic Decorations, Standard Decorations, Premium Decorations, or Custom Design).
* **Special Requests** (text, optional): Enter any additional requirements or preferences.
* **Preferred Delivery Date** (date, required): Select the preferred delivery date.
* **Preferred Delivery Time** (time, optional): Select preferred delivery time (if applicable).
* **Payment Method** (select_one, required): Choose your payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank Transfer, Check, or Invoice (Business Accounts)).
* **Deposit Amount** (number, required): Enter the required deposit amount to secure the order.
* **Billing Address** (text, required): Enter your full billing address for payment purposes.
* **Terms and Conditions** (select_one, required): Accept or decline the terms and conditions.
