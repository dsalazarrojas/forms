# photography_course_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect payment information from students for photography courses.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure timely and correct processing of your payment.
- Make sure to select the correct payment method (Yes/No) in the first field.
- Enter the course fee as a numerical value in the second field, representing the amount due.
- Enter your student name in the third field, ensuring it matches your registered student name.
- Provide your email address in the fourth field, which will be used for communication regarding payment and course updates.
- Select a payment date from the calendar (if available) or provide the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY (if not available).
- Fill out any additional fields (Field 6 and notes) as requested or required by your instructor or course administrators.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **payment_method** (Yes/No, required): Indicates whether you are paying for the course or not.
* **course_fee** (Number, required): Enter the amount you are paying for the course.
* **student_name** (Text, required): Enter your student name.
* **email** (Email, required): Enter your registered email address.
* **payment_date** (Date, required): Select or enter the payment date from the calendar (if available) or provide the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY (if not available).
* **Field 6** (Text): This field is for any additional information requested by your instructor or course administrators.
* **instructor_name** (Text, optional): This field is not required but may be used by the instructor to address specific questions or comments.
* **course_name** (Text, optional): This field is for course identification or reference.
* **notes** (Note, optional): Enter any additional comments or remarks about your payment or course participation.
