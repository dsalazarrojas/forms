# Legal Support Fund Contribution - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Support Fund Contribution form is a tool used to collect donations for the Legal Support Fund, a program that provides financial support to individuals in need of legal services. This form helps us understand the intentions of the donor and their contribution preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

- Fill in the form title: This is a text field where you can enter the name of the contribution (e.g., "John Doe's Legal Support Contribution").
- Enter your name: This is a text field where you can enter your name as the donor.
- Enter the amount: This is a numerical field where you can enter the amount you wish to contribute.
- Select the contribution frequency: Choose between "Legal Support Fund" and "General Fund" to specify how your contribution will be allocated.
- Select the payment method: Choose between "Online Payment", "In-person Payment", and "Mail Payment" to specify how you will be making the payment.
- Enter your email address (optional): This is an email field where you can enter your contact information if you wish.
- Enter your phone number (optional): This is a text field where you can enter your phone number for any follow-up communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Details** (`1`, `text`, not required): This field allows you to enter a description of your contribution.
- **Name** (`2`, `text`, not required): This field is to enter your name as the donor.
- **Amount** (`3`, `number`, not required): Enter the amount of your contribution.
- **Frequency** (`4`, `select_one`, not required): This field allows you to choose between "Legal Support Fund" and "General Fund" to specify the allocation of your contribution. Please note that "Legal Support Fund" is the main purpose of this form, and selecting this option will direct your contribution towards supporting legal services.
- **Payment Method** (`5`, `select_one`, not required): This field enables you to select how you will be making the payment ("Online Payment", "In-person Payment", or "Mail Payment").
- **Email** (`6`, `email`, not required): This is an optional field for you to enter your email address.
- **Phone Number** (`7`, `text`, not required): This is an optional field for you to enter your phone number.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any specific instructions or requests regarding your contribution, please include them in the "Form Details" field.
- If you have any questions or need further assistance, please contact us at [support email or phone number].
