# Primitive Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for processing basic orders from customers. It collects information about the customer's name, address, order date, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with the correct information.
2. Select your preferred payment method (if applicable).
3. Provide any additional comments or messages (if needed).
4. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name in this field. (Text, Optional)
* **Customer Address**: Enter the customer's address in this field. (Text, Optional)
* **Order Date**: Enter the date of the order in this format: YYYY-MM-DD. (Date, Optional)
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX. (Text, Optional)
* **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address in the format example@example.com. (Email, Optional)
* **Customer Payment**: Select the payment method used for the order (if applicable). (Select One, Optional)
	+ Options: Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, American Express, Discover
* **Total Cost**: Enter the total cost of the order. (Number, Optional)
* **Customer Message**: Enter any additional comments or messages (if needed). (Note, Optional)
* **Submit**: Confirm that the order is ready to be processed. (Select Multiple, Optional)
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method used for the order (if applicable). (Select Multiple, Optional)
	+ Options: Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Check
* **Customer City**: Enter the customer's city. (Text, Optional)
* **Customer State**: Enter the customer's state. (Text, Optional)
* **Customer Post Code**: Enter the customer's post code. (Text, Optional)

**Tips**: If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please contact our support team.
