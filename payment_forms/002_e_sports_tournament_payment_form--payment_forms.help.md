# e_sports_tournament_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e_sports_tournament_payment_form is a payment form designed to collect information from participants in an e-sports tournament. This form is used to gather details for processing payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required information as indicated by the "required" field in each section.
2. Ensure accurate information is entered for each field to avoid processing issues.
3. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_info** (`participant_info`, text, optional): Enter the participant's name, nickname, or any identifying information.
* **payment_method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select how the participant plans to make payment (cash, check, online, or other).
* **payment_date** (`payment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the payment.
* **payment_time** (`payment_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the payment.
* **participant_name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter the participant's full name.
* **participant_email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter the participant's email address.
* **participant_phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **participant_address** (`participant_address`, text, optional): Enter the participant's address or location.
* **participant_notes** (`participant_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the participant.

## Tips
* Be accurate when filling out the form to avoid payment processing issues.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the tournament organizers for clarification.
