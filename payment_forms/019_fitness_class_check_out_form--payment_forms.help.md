# Fitness Class Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Class Check Out Form is designed to collect information from students who are checking out or registering for a fitness class. This form aims to gather essential details about the students and their class registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Student Information** page with your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. Proceed to the **Class Information** page and enter the class details, such as the class name, date, and time.
3. On the **Card Information** page, enter your payment card details, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information (Page 1)** (`<name>`, `<type>`, `optional`): This page is for entering your personal details, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant information.
* **Class Information (Page 2)** (`<name>`, `<type>`, `optional`): This page is for selecting the class you are registering for or checking out. Please enter the class name, date, and time you plan to attend.
* **Card Information (Page 3)** (`<name>`, `<type>`, `optional`): This page is for entering your payment card details. Please note that this field is not currently being used for payment processing and its purpose is unclear.
