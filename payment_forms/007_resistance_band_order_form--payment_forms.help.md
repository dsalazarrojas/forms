# Resistance Band Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resistance Band Order Form is a simple form designed to gather information from customers who are interested in purchasing resistance bands. This form helps us understand their needs and preferences for shipping and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to send your order details.
2. Make sure to fill in all required fields (if any).
3. Review your information before submitting your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input Form** (`user_input_form`, text, optional): Please fill in any relevant information you'd like to share about your order. This can include any special requests or comments.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Enter your payment details, including the method of payment you'd like to use.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, optional): Fill in your shipping address and any other information required for delivery.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues or questions, please contact our support team at form@gic.mx.
