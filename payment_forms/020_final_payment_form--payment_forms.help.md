# Final Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Final Payment Form is a crucial tool for customers to provide their payment information, ensuring accurate and efficient processing of payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Final Payment Form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submission.
2. Enter the customer's information in the Customer Info section.
3. Select the desired payment method in the Payment Method section.
4. Enter the payment amount, date, and time in the Payment Details section.
5. Provide any additional notes or comments in the Note section.
6. Enter the customer's email address and phone number in the Email and Phone sections.
7. Enter the billing information in the Billing Info section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' to indicate that the payment is being made.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, optional): Enter the payment amount, date, and time.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, optional): Enter the payment amount.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the payment date.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the payment time.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the payment.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Billing Info** (`billing_info`, text, optional): Enter the billing information for the payment, such as the billing address.
