# Cloud Computing Subscription Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to renew your cloud computing subscription. Please fill out the form with your current subscription information and any necessary details for the renewal process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the required information in each field.
2. Review your answers before submitting the form.
3. Submit the form for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Subscription Account ID** (`subscription_account_id`, text, **Required**): Enter your current subscription account identifier.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, **Required**): Enter the legal business name of your company.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person`, text, **Required**): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the subscription.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, **Required**): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, **Required**): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Current Service Plan** (`current_service`, select_one, **Required**): Select your current subscription level.
	+ Basic: Basic subscription plan.
	+ Professional: Professional subscription plan.
	+ Enterprise: Enterprise subscription plan.
	+ Custom: Custom subscription plan.
* **Current Monthly Subscription Cost** (`current_monthly_cost`, number, **Required**): Enter your current monthly subscription cost.
* **Current Renewal Due Date** (`renewal_date`, date, **Required**): Enter your current renewal due date.
* **Desired Renewal Period** (`renewal_period`, select_one, **Required**): Select your desired billing cycle.
	+ Monthly: Monthly billing cycle.
	+ Quarterly: Quarterly billing cycle.
	+ Annual: Annual billing cycle.
	+ 2 Years: 2-year billing cycle.
	+ 3 Years: 3-year billing cycle.
* **Are You Changing Your Plan?** (`plan_change`, select_one, **Required**): Select whether you are changing your plan.
	+ No Change: No changes to your plan.
	+ Upgrade: Upgrade your plan.
	+ Downgrade: Downgrade your plan.
	+ Custom Modification: Custom modifications to your plan.
* **New Plan if Changing** (`new_plan`, select_one, **Required** if you selected "Upgrade" or "Downgrade", Optional otherwise): Select your new plan if you are upgrading or downgrading.
	+ Basic: Basic subscription plan.
	+ Professional: Professional subscription plan.
	+ Enterprise: Enterprise subscription plan.
	+ Custom: Custom subscription plan.
* **Estimated New Monthly Cost** (`estimated_new_cost`, number, Optional): Enter your estimated new monthly cost (if you are upgrading or downgrading).
* **Number of Users** (`number_of_users`, number, Optional): Enter the expected number of users for this renewal.
* **Storage Requirement** (`storage_requirement`, number, Optional): Enter the total storage needed in GB.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, **Required**): Select your preferred payment option.
	+ Credit Card: Credit card payment.
	+ Bank Transfer: Bank transfer payment.
	+ Check: Check payment.
	+ Purchase Order: Purchase order payment.
	+ Wire Transfer: Wire transfer payment.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, **Required**): Enter your billing address.
* **Special Requests or Negotiations** (`special_requests`, text, Optional): Enter any custom terms or requirements.
