# Afterpay Clothing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers for order registration and fulfillment using the Afterpay payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form introduction page (optional).
2. Fill in your name and email address in the "Customer" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Describe the item you are ordering in the "Item description" field.
5. Select or enter your preferred size in the "Size" field.
6. Choose your preferred color in the "Color" field, or leave it blank if not available.
7. Enter the number of units you wish to order in the "Quantity" field.
8. Select "Yes" if you wish to split your payment in the "Use Afterpay" field, or "No" if you wish to pay upfront.
9. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping address" field.
10. Provide any special instructions for fulfillment or gift options in the "Special instructions" field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer** (`-- Customer --`, `note`, not required): This is an optional introduction page that provides context for the form fields that follow.
* **Customer name** (`Customer name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name for order registration.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for order confirmation.
* **Item description** (`Item description`, `text`, required): Describe the item you are ordering, including SKU, color, and size.
* **Size** (`Size`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred size from the dropdown list or enter your own measurement.
* **Color** (`Color`, `text`, optional): Enter your preferred color choice, if available.
* **Quantity** (`Quantity`, `number`, required): Enter the number of units you wish to order.
* **Use Afterpay** (`Use Afterpay`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to split your payment, or "No" to pay upfront.
* **Shipping address** (`Shipping address`, `text`, required): Enter your shipping address, including street, city, and postal code.
* **Special instructions** (`Special instructions`, `text`, optional): Provide any special instructions for fulfillment or gift options, if necessary.
