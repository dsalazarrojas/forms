# credit_card_authorization_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare a credit card for a particular transaction. It is meant to be filled out by users who are authorizing the use of their credit card for a transaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Choose the type of card you are using (credit card or debit card).
3. Enter your Card Number.
4. Enter your Expiration Date.
5. Enter your CVV.
6. Enter your PIN (if required).
7. Enter your email address (if you have one associated with your card).
8. Enter your phone number (if you have one associated with your card).
9. Enter your Card Security Code.
10. Enter the name of your Card Issuer.
11. Enter the country where your Card was issued.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your credit card.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your credit card.
* **Card Type**: Choose the type of card you are using (credit card or debit card).
* **Card Number**: Enter your credit card number without any additional characters.
* **Expiration Date**: Enter the expiration date of your credit card.
* **CVV**: Enter your card verification value (CVV) found on the back of your credit card.
* **PIN**: If required, enter the PIN of your credit card (if you have one).
* **Email**: If you have an email associated with your credit card, enter it here.
* **Phone**: If you have a phone number associated with your credit card, enter it here.
* **Card Security Code**: Enter the security code found on your credit card.
* **Card Issuer**: Enter the name of your credit card issuer.
* **Card Country**: Enter the country where your credit card was issued.
