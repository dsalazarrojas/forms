# Museum Pass Subscription Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Museum Pass Subscription Form is designed to collect information for subscribers wishing to purchase a museum pass. This form will guide you through the necessary steps to complete the form and provide an explanation of each field to help you provide accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your subscriber information in the "Enter Subscriber Information" field.
2. Fill in your name in the "Subscriber Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your preferred payment plan in the "Payment Plan" field.
6. Choose the type of pass you wish to purchase in the "Pass Type" field.
7. Specify the quantity of passes you require in the "Quantity" field.
8. Select the start date of your subscription in the "Start Date" field.
9. Select the end date of your subscription in the "End Date" field.
10. Enter the subscription term in the "Subscription Term" field.
11. Specify the start date of your subscription term in the "Subscription Start" field.
12. Specify the end date of your subscription term in the "Subscription End" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Subscriber Information**: Enter a brief description of why you're interested in the museum pass. (text)
	+ This field is not visible in the form, but it's a required field to gather information on the subscriber's details.
* **Subscriber Name**: Enter the name of the person who will be using the museum pass. (text)
	+ This field is required to ensure that the pass is issued to the correct individual.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for communication purposes. (email)
	+ This field is required to send important updates and notifications regarding your subscription.
* **Phone**: Enter a phone number for contact purposes. (text)
	+ This field is required for any urgent communication or issues that may arise during your subscription.
* **Payment Plan**: Select whether you'd like to pay monthly or annually. (select_one)
	+ This field determines how often you'll be charged for the pass subscription.
* **Pass Type**: Choose the type of pass that suits your needs. (select_multiple)
	+ This field determines the type of pass you'll be purchasing, such as Individual, Family, or Senior Pass.
* **Quantity**: Specify how many passes you require. (number)
	+ This field ensures that you receive the correct number of passes for your needs.
* **Start Date**: Select the date you'd like your subscription to start. (date)
	+ This field determines when your subscription begins.
* **End Date**: Select the date you'd like your subscription to end. (date)
	+ This field determines when your subscription will expire.
* **Subscription Term**: Enter a brief description of your subscription term. (text)
	+ This field is optional but recommended to provide additional information about your subscription.
* **Subscription Start**: Specify the start date of your subscription term. (date)
	+ This field ensures that your subscription start date is accurately recorded.
* **Subscription End**: Specify the end date of your subscription term. (date)
	+ This field ensures that your subscription end date is accurately recorded.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have provided accurate and complete information to avoid any issues with your subscription.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us for assistance.
* The information provided will be used to process your subscription and create your museum pass.
