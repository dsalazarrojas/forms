# Data Storage Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect and store payment information for a data storage service. It gathers details such as payment method, total cost, payment date, and payment status, among other relevant fields. The form helps in processing and tracking payments efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2.  Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3.  Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field. This field is required to ensure that you can be contacted for any further information related to your payment.
4.  Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field.
5.  Enter your **Address** in the "Address" field.
6.  Select the **Payment Method** you would like to use (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, or Discover).
7.  Enter the **Total Cost** of the payment in the "Total Cost" field.
8.  Enter the **Payment Date** in the "Payment Date" field.
9.  Select the status of your **Payment Status** (Paid, Pending, or Reimbursed).
10.  Select the **Currency** you would like to use (USD, EUR, JPY, or CNY).
11.  Enter the **Payment Amount** in the "Payment Amount" field.
12.  Enter any additional **Payment Note** in the "Payment Note" field.
13.  Select if the payment is **Refundable** or **Not Refundable**.
14.  Enter the reason for **Refund Reason** in the "Refund Reason" field.
15.  Enter the **Refund Amount** in the "Refund Amount" field.
16.  Read and agree to the **Payment Terms** by selecting "Agree" in the "Agree to Terms" field.
17.  Confirm if you **Agree to Terms and Conditions** by selecting "Agree" in the "Agree to Terms and Conditions" field.
18.  Select if the **Terms and Conditions Version** is up to date ("Yes" or "No").
19.  Enter the **Terms and Conditions Link** in the "Terms and Conditions Link" field.
20.  Enter a brief description of the **Terms and Conditions** in the "Terms and Conditions Description" field.
21.  Enter the date of the **Last Updated Terms and Conditions** in the "Last Updated Terms and Conditions" field.
22.  Select if you are the **Submitted By** ("Yes" or "No").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name here.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name here.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address here.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number here.
*   **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address here.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method you would like to use (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, or Discover).
*   **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, number, required): Enter the total cost of the payment here.
*   **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, optional): Enter the payment date here.
*   **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of your payment (Paid, Pending, or Reimbursed).
*   **Currency** (`currency`, select_multiple, optional): Select the currency you would like to use (USD, EUR, JPY, or CNY).
*   **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, optional): Enter the payment amount here.
*   **Payment Note** (`payment_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional payment note here.
*   **Refundable** (`payment_refund`, select_one, optional): Select if the payment is refundable or not.
*   **Refund Reason** (`payment_refund_reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for refund here.
*   **Refund Amount** (`payment_refund_amount`, number, optional): Enter the refund amount here.
*   **Terms and Conditions Version** (`payment_terms_and_conditions_version`, select_multiple, optional): Select if the Terms and Conditions Version is up to date ("Yes" or "No").
*   **Agree to Terms** (`payment_terms_agreed`, select_one, required): Read and agree to the payment terms by selecting "Agree" here.
*   **Agree to Terms and Conditions** (`payment_terms_and_conditions_agreed`, select_one, required): Confirm if you agree to the Terms and Conditions by selecting "Agree" here.
*   **Terms and Conditions Last Updated** (`payment_terms_and_conditions_last_updated`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last updated Terms and Conditions here.
*   **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required): Select if you are the submitted by ("Yes" or "No").

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all required fields.
*   Double-check your details before submitting the form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
