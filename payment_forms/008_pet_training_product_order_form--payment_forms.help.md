# Pet Training Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for ordering pet training products. The fields are intended for internal use by the pet training company to gather necessary data for processing orders and communicating with customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `pet_trainer` field with the name of the trainer who will be handling the order.
2. Select the desired trainer from the `select_trainer` field.
3. Select the desired products from the `select_product` field.
4. Enter the date of the order in the `order_date` field.
5. Enter the time of the order in the `order_time` field.
6. Enter any additional notes about the order in the `order_notes` field.
7. Enter the customer's email address in the `customer_email` field.
8. Enter the customer's phone number in the `customer_phone` field.
9. Enter the product cost in the `product_cost` field.
10. Select the payment method in the `payment_method` field.
11. Select the payment terms in the `payment_terms` field.
12. Enter the trainer's fee in the `trainer_fee` field.
13. Select the terms for the trainer's fee in the `trainer_fee_terms` field.
14. Select the pet ID from the `select_one` pet ID field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **pet_trainer**: Enter the name of the trainer who will be handling the order. This field is not required.
* **select_trainer**: Select the trainer from the list of available trainers. This field is not required.
* **select_product**: Select the product(s) you want to order. This field is not required.
* **order_date**: Enter the date of the order. This field is not required.
* **order_time**: Enter the time of the order. This field is not required.
* **order_notes**: Enter any additional notes about the order. This field is not required.
* **customer_email**: Enter the customer's email address. This field is not required.
* **customer_phone**: Enter the customer's phone number. This field is not required.
* **product_cost**: Enter the cost of the ordered product. This field is not required.
* **payment_method**: Select the payment method used for the order. This field is not required.
* **payment_terms**: Select the payment terms used for the order. This field is not required.
* **trainer_fee**: Enter the fee of the trainer. This field is not required.
* **trainer_fee_terms**: Select the terms for the trainer's fee. This field is not required.
* **pet_id**: Select the pet ID from the list of available pet IDs. This field is not required.

Note: All fields are not required, but they are all provided for your convenience and accuracy.
