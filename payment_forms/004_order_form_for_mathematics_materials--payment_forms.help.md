# Order Form For Mathematics Materials - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for the order of mathematical materials. It is crucial to fill out this form accurately to ensure that your order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form name, which should be a unique name for your order.
2. Enter your email address so that we can contact you regarding your order.
3. Provide your phone number so that we can reach you for any further inquiries.
4. Enter your address for shipping purposes.
5. Select your preferred payment method from the options provided (Bank Transfer, PayPal, or Check).
6. Enter the amount of the order.
7. Provide any additional shipping details if necessary.
8. Enter any additional notes or comments about your order.
9. Finally, confirm the form submission date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Name
* **Form Name** (`form_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the unique name for your order, which will help us identify your request.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so that we can contact you regarding your order.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Provide your phone number for any further inquiries or updates about your order.

### Address
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your shipping address for accurate delivery of your order.

### Payment Method
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your preferred payment method from Bank Transfer, PayPal, or Check.

### Amount
* **Amount** (`amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the amount of the order.

### Shipping Details
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional shipping details if required.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about your order.

### Form Submitted
* **Form Submitted** (`form_submitted`, `date`, required: false): Confirm the date and time of form submission.
