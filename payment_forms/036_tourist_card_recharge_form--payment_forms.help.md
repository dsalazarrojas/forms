# Tourist Card Recharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to recharge a tourist's card for a specific service or privilege.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **Tourist Card Number** in the corresponding text field.
3. (Optional) Enter the **Amount** you wish to recharge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Your name as it appears on your tourist card.
* **Tourist Card Number**: The unique number assigned to your tourist card.
* **Amount**: (Optional) The amount you wish to recharge. 

## Tips
- Make sure to enter your name and tourist card number correctly to avoid any issues with your recharge.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for help from our staff.
- This form is for tourist card recharges only; if you are trying to do something else, please use the correct form.
