# Community Theater Play Development Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Theater Play Development Donation Form is designed to collect information from donors to support the development of community theater plays. By filling out this form, you will be able to specify your donation details, recognition preferences, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal and donation details.
2. Specify your recognition preferences.
3. Choose your payment method.
4. Agree to the terms and conditions of donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (1): Please enter your full name as you would like to be recognized.
* **Email Address** (2): Enter your email address for receipt and updates.
* **Phone Number** (3): Enter your phone number for contact purposes (optional).
* **Mailing Address** (4): Enter your street, city, state, and zip code for receipt and updates.
* **Donor Type** (5): Choose whether you are donating as an individual or an organization.
* **Organization Name** (6): Enter your business or foundation name if applicable.
* **Which Play Development** (7): Select the production you are supporting.
* **Donation Amount** (8): Enter the amount of your donation.
* **Donor Level** (9): Based on your donation amount, select your donor level.
* **Donation Frequency** (10): Choose whether this is a one-time or recurring donation.
* **Donation Purpose** (11): Select the specific area of interest for your donation.
* **Recognition Preference** (12): Choose how you would like to be recognized.
* **Name Display** (13): Enter how you would like your name to be displayed in recognition.
* **Payment Method** (14): Choose how you would like to pay.
* **Credit Card Type** (15): If paying by card, select your credit card type.
* **Billing Address** (16): Enter your billing address if different from mailing address.
* **Tribute Donation** (17): Check if this is a donation in honor or memory of someone.
* **Tribute Name** (18): Enter the name of the honoree if applicable.
* **Tribute Notification** (19): Check if you would like to send a notification to the honoree or family.
* **Tribute Contact** (20): Enter the contact address for the notification.
* **Employer Match** (21): Check if your employer offers matching gifts.
* **Tax Receipt** (22): Choose how you would like to receive your tax receipt.
* **Tax ID or EIN** (23): Enter your tax ID or EIN (for organizations).
* **Newsletter Signup** (24): Check if you would like to receive the theater's newsletter.
* **Volunteer Interest** (25): Check if you are interested in volunteering.
* **Additional Comments** (26): Enter any additional comments or dedications.
* **Donation Date** (27): The date of your donation.
* **Terms Agreement** (28): Agree to the terms and conditions of donation.

Please note that all fields are mandatory, and some fields may have specific instructions or requirements to ensure accurate and proper processing of your donation. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
