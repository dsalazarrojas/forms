# lease_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "lease_payment_form" is designed to process lease-related payments efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the step-by-step instructions below:

1. Fill in the tenant information (id: 1)
2. Provide payment details (id: 2)
3. Specify lease terms (id: 3)
4. Choose a payment date (id: 4) or select a time (id: 5) 
5. Enter the rent amount (id: 6)
6. Select a payment gateway (id: 7) (e.g., PayPal, Stripe, Bank transfer)
7. Add any additional notes or comments (id: 8)
8. Provide your email address (id: 9) and phone number (id: 10)
9. Specify the lease terms and conditions (id: 11, 12)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **tenant_info** (id: 1, text, required: false): Enter the name and any other relevant details about the tenant.
* **payment_details** (id: 2, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the payment details.
* **lease_terms** (id: 3, text, required: false): Enter any lease terms or conditions.
* **lease_date** (id: 4, date, required: false): Select the date of the lease.
* **lease_time** (id: 5, time, required: false): Choose a time of the lease.
* **rent_amount** (id: 6, number, required: false): Enter the rent amount.
* **payment_gateway** (id: 7, select_one, required: false): Choose the payment gateway (e.g., PayPal, Stripe, Bank transfer).
* **notes** (id: 8, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the payment.
* **email** (id: 9, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (id: 10, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **lease_terms_1** (id: 11, text, required: false): Specify the lease terms and conditions 1.
* **lease_terms_2** (id: 12, text, required: false): Specify the lease terms and conditions 2.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and efficient processing of the lease payment.
* Double-check the payment gateway selection to ensure it matches your preference.
* Review and understand the lease terms and conditions before proceeding with the payment.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date contact information to facilitate communication and payment processing. 

Note: The form fields are not explicitly labeled "required" or "optional" but based on their types and the context, we've provided explanations and instructions accordingly.
