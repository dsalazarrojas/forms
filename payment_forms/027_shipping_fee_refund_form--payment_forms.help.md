# shipping_fee_refund_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The shipping_fee_refund_form is used to request a refund for a shipping fee associated with an order. This form allows customers to provide the necessary information to initiate the refund process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the "Refund Requested" option in the "Request Refund" field.
2. Enter the amount of the refund in the "refund_amount" field.
3. Enter the date of the shipping in the "shipping_date" field.
4. Enter a brief explanation for the refund in the "reason" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Refund** (`Request Refund`, select_one, required): Select this option to indicate that you are requesting a refund for the shipping fee.
* **Refund Amount** (`refund_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of the refund you are requesting.
* **Shipping Date** (`shipping_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the shipment that the refund is related to.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, required): Provide a brief explanation for the refund request.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure the refund request is processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the refund process, contact our customer service team.
