# Park Restoration Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for park restoration donors to submit their information and donation details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields: **Donor Name** and **Amount Donated**.
* If applicable, select a **Phone Number** to allow the organization to contact you.
* Choose your preferred **Payment Method** (Card, PayPal, or Stripe).
* Indicate your **Frequency of Donations** (One-time, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
* Optionally, specify the **Donation Date** and **Donation Time** of your donation.
* Fill in your **Email** address if you want to receive updates about the park restoration project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Name** (Donor Name, text, required): Enter your name as you would like it to appear on our records.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates about the park restoration project.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, optional): Enter your phone number for the organization to contact you.
* **Amount Donated** (Amount Donated, number, required): Enter the amount you would like to donate to the park restoration project.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to make your donation (Card, PayPal, or Stripe).
* **Frequency of Donations** (Frequency of Donations, select_multiple, optional): Select how frequently you would like to make donations (One-time, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
* **Donation Date** (Donation Date, date, optional): If you have a specific date in mind for your donation, enter it here.
* **Donation Time** (Donation Time, time, optional): If you have a specific time in mind for your donation, enter it here.
