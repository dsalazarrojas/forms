# Hotel Policy And Deposit Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a template for hotel properties to gather information from guests regarding their understanding and acceptance of the hotel's policies and deposit requirements. It is used to ensure that guests are aware of and agree to the hotel's terms of service, including payment and refund policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the hotel's policy and terms of service to ensure you understand the information being presented to you.
2.  Select "I agree to the hotel's policy" or "I do not agree to the hotel's policy" for the hotel policy agreement field, depending on your understanding and acceptance of the hotel's policies.
3.  Enter your desired checkin date in the checkin_date field. This should be the date you plan to arrive at the hotel.
4.  Enter your desired checkin time in the checkin_time field. This should be the time you plan to arrive at the hotel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **hotel policy agreement** (Select One): This field asks you to confirm that you have read and understand the hotel's policies. Select "I agree to the hotel's policy" if you have reviewed the policies and are comfortable with them. If you are unsure or do not agree with any part of the policy, select "I do not agree to the hotel's policy" and discuss your concerns with the hotel staff.
*   **deposit_consent** (Text): This field is intended for additional comments or information about your deposit. However, based on the provided YAML, it seems that this field is currently not being used for any specific functionality.
*   **checkin_date** (Date): Enter the date you plan to arrive at the hotel. This is a required field for hotel staff to schedule your arrival and prepare for your stay.
*   **checkin_time** (Time): Enter the time you plan to arrive at the hotel. This field is likely used by the hotel staff to coordinate your arrival and ensure that you are greeted promptly.

## Tips
*   Be sure to review the hotel's policy and terms of service carefully before completing this form.
*   Make sure you select the correct checkin date and time to ensure smooth checkin processing.
*   If you have any questions or concerns about the hotel's policy or deposit requirements, discuss them with the hotel staff before completing this form.
