# Factory Production Counter Device Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Production Counter Device Order Form is designed to collect details about a production counter device order, including the payment method and payment details. This form will help the user efficiently complete the order process for production counter devices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to ensure you understand what information is required and what options are available.
2. Fill out the "Order Details" page (page 1) with the necessary information about the production counter device order.
3. On the "Payment Method" page (page 2), select the payment method that applies to your order (e.g., Payment by credit card, Payment by bank transfer, or Payment by cash).
4. If you selected a payment method that requires additional information, fill out the "Payment Details" page (page 3) with the required details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details (Page 1)**:
	+ The "Order Details" page is where you provide basic information about the production counter device order.
	+ Enter the required details in this field to ensure the order is processed correctly.
* **Payment Method (Page 2)**:
	+ Select the payment method that applies to your order.
	+ The options are:
		- Payment by credit card
		- Payment by bank transfer
		- Payment by cash
* **Payment Details (Page 3)**:
	+ If you selected "Payment by credit card" or "Payment by bank transfer", you will need to provide additional information in this field.
	+ Please enter the required details for the selected payment method.
	+ If you selected "Payment by cash", you can skip this page as no further information is required.

## Tips
- Double-check the selected payment method and payment details to ensure they are correct.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact the form administrator.
- Please review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
