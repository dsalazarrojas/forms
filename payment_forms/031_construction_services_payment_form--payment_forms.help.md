# Construction Services Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for submitting payment information for construction services. It ensures accuracy and completeness in payment details, making it easier for both payers and contractors to manage payments and avoid disputes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the payment information as required and accurate.
2. Ensure all fields are filled out before submission.
3. Use the provided options for payment method and payment terms selection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Date** (1): Date of payment submission.
* **Invoice Number** (2): Reference invoice number.
* **Project Name** (3): Name of construction project.
* **Project Address** (4): Site location.
* **Contractor Name** (5): Company or individual providing services.
* **Contractor Address** (6): Mailing address for payment.
* **Contractor Email** (7): Email for payment confirmation.
* **Contractor Phone** (8): Contact phone number.
* **Contractor Tax ID or SSN** (9): For tax reporting purposes.
* **Service Period Start** (10): Start date of billing period.
* **Service Period End** (11): End date of billing period.
* **Description of Services** (12): Detailed description of work performed.
* **Labor Amount** (13): Total labor charges.
* **Labor Hours** (14): Total hours worked. (Optional)
* **Materials Amount** (15): Total materials charges. (Optional)
* **Equipment Amount** (16): Equipment rental or usage charges. (Optional)
* **Subcontractor Amount** (17): Subcontractor charges if applicable. (Optional)
* **Other Charges** (18): Any additional charges. (Optional)
* **Subtotal** (19): Sum of all charges before adjustments.
* **Retainage Percentage** (20): Percentage withheld if applicable. 
* **Retainage Amount** (21): Amount being withheld. 
* **Previous Payments** (22): Total paid to date on this project. 
* **Change Order Adjustments** (23): Net change order amount. 
* **Total Due This Period** (24): Amount due for this invoice.
* **Payment Method** (25): How should payment be made (Check, Wire Transfer, ACH Transfer, Credit Card, Online Payment).
* **Payment Terms** (26): When is payment due (Due on Receipt, Net 15 Days, Net 30 Days, Net 45 Days, Net 60 Days).
* **Lien Waiver Provided** (27): Is lien waiver included.
* **Certified Payroll Provided** (28): Required for prevailing wage projects. 
* **Supporting Documents** (29): List attached documentation. 
* **Authorized By** (30): Name of person authorizing payment.
* **Authorized Signature** (31): Type full name as signature.

Note: Ensure all fields that are marked as required are filled out before submission.
