# Souvenir Booklet Advertising Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Souvenir Booklet Advertising Form is used by businesses or individuals to submit their advertising content for a souvenir booklet. The form requires basic information about the business and payment details to complete the advertising process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the necessary information about your business.
* Select the ad size that best represents your business.
* Fill out your contact details.
* Provide payment details.
* Choose your preferred payment method.
* Enter the payment amount.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Details (`business_details`, text, optional): This is a text field where you can enter a brief description of your business. Please keep it concise and within the character limit.
* **Ad Size (`ad_size`, select_one, optional): Select the ad size that best represents your business from the options: Small, Large, or Extra Large.
* **Contact Details (`contact_details`, text, optional): This field is for your business contact information. Please enter your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Payment Details (`payment_details`, text, optional): In this field, provide any relevant payment details for your advertisement.
* **Payment Method (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred payment method from the options: Card or Bank Transfer.
* **Payment Amount (`payment_amount`, number, optional): Enter the payment amount for your advertisement. Please ensure you enter a valid number.

## Tips
* Only one advertisement per business/service will be accepted.
* Advertisements will be reviewed for approval before being published in the souvenir booklet.
* Ensure you have all necessary permissions and approvals before submitting the form.
