# Personal Chef Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers about their event and booking requirements for a personal chef. The form is designed to be completed by event organizers or hosts to book a personal chef for their event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide event details such as event type and description in the "Event Details" field.
2. Enter the date of the event in the "Event Date" field using the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
3. Choose the time of the event from the time format in the "Event Time" field.
4. Enter the chef's details, including their name and contact information in the "Chef Details" field.
5. Provide the address of the event location in the "Event Address" field.
6. Enter the contact number of the event organizer or host in the "Contact Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required): Describe your event, including its type and description.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your event in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Choose the time of your event in the 24-hour format.
* **Chef Details** (`chef_details`, text, optional): Enter the chef's name and contact information.
* **Event Address** (`event_address`, text, optional): Provide the address of the event location.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the contact number of the event organizer or host.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a successful booking.
* Double-check your contact number and chef details for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
