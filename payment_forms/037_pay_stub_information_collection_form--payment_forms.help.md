# pay_stub_information_collection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect payment information from employees for the purpose of maintaining accurate and up-to-date records for employee benefits, tax compliance, and financial analysis.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field with accurate and consistent data.
2. Review the options carefully to ensure the correct selections are made.
3. Complete any fields that are required, even if they are not marked as such.
4. If a field is not applicable, mark it with 'N/A' or 'Not Applicable' to prevent errors.
5. Double-check your entries before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee as it appears on their ID or passport.
* **Pay Cycle**: Select 'Yes' if the employee is subject to a pay cycle or 'No' if they are not. 
* **Pay Period**: Select all pay periods that apply to the employee, separated by commas (e.g., Pay Period 1, Pay Period 2, etc.).
* **Date Last Pay**: Enter the date of the last payment received.
* **Time Last Pay**: Enter the time of the last payment received.
* **Gross Pay**: Enter the total gross pay received.
* **Tax Fed**: Enter the federal tax deducted from the gross pay.
* **Tax State**: Enter the state tax deducted from the gross pay.
* **Tax Local**: Enter the local tax deducted from the gross pay.
* **Net Pay**: Enter the net pay after tax deductions.
* **Benefits**: Enter any additional benefits received.
* **Insurance**: Enter any insurance information or policies relevant to the employee.
* **Address**: Enter the full address of the employee.
* **Zip**: Enter the zip code of the employee's address.
* **Pay Frequency**: Select the pay frequency, if applicable:
	+ Weekly
	+ Bi-Weekly
	+ Semi-Monthly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Annually
* **Pay Method**: Select 'Yes' if the payment is made through an electronic method (e.g., direct deposit) or 'No' if not. 
* **Pay Type**: Select 'Yes' if the payment is a type of payment that is typically subject to taxation or 'No' if not.
* **Pay Status**: Select the active or inactive status of the employee's payment account.
* **Bank Info**: Enter any relevant bank information, such as bank name or account number.
* **Account Number**: Enter the account number.
* **Routing Number**: Enter the routing number associated with the account.
* **Account Name**: Enter any other relevant account details.
