# Festival Invoice - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for creating a festival invoice for entities to be paid after a festival or event. It gathers essential information from the organization's administrative team to process payments accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the festival invoice form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the event in the **Event Name** field.
2. Enter the name of the entity being invoiced in the **Entity Name** field.
3. Enter the fee for the entity in the **Entity Fee** field.
4. Specify the total number of entities in the **Number of Entities** field.
5. Optionally, you can enter the total fee by typing it in the **Total Fee** field. If you leave this field blank, the total fee will be calculated automatically based on the other information provided.
6. Select the method of payment from the options provided in the **Payment Method** field (bank transfer, cash, or mobile transfer). Make sure to select at least one payment method to proceed.
7. Finally, click the **Submit** button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name (event_name)**: Enter the name of the event for which you're creating the invoice.
* **Entity Name (entity_name)**: Enter the name of the entity being invoiced, such as the name of an artist, performer, or vendor. This field is **required**.
* **Entity Fee (entity_fee)**: Enter the total amount of money owed to the entity, including any additional fees or taxes. This field is **required**.
* **Number of Entities (num_entities)**: Enter the total number of entities being invoiced, such as the number of artists or performers. This field is **required**.
* **Total Fee (total_fee)**: Enter the total amount of money owed to all entities if you want to override the calculated total. If left blank, the total fee will be calculated automatically. This field is **optional**.
* **Calculate Fee (calculate_fee)**: Select '0' to override the total fee calculation or '1' to use the calculated total. This field is **optional**, but if not selected, the total fee will be calculated based on the other information provided.
* **Payment Method (payment_method)**: Select at least one of the available payment options (bank transfer, cash, or mobile transfer). This field is **required**.
* **Submit (submit)**: Click this button to complete and submit the form, which will generate a festival invoice for the entity based on your input.
