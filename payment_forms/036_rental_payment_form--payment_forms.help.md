# rental_payment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The rental payment form is designed to collect information related to rent payments for residential properties. It is used to record payment details such as the method of payment, due date, amount paid, and payment status. This form is essential for maintaining accurate records and ensuring timely rent payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the rental payment form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Rental Payment Form 1** field with any relevant information.
2. Enter the **Tenant's Name** in the designated field.
3. Select the method of payment from the options provided in the **Payment Method** field.
4. Enter the **Due Date** of the payment in the date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
5. Enter the **Amount Paid** using a numerical value.
6. Select the **Payment Status** from the provided options to indicate the current status of the payment.
7. If necessary, provide any additional **Notes** about the payment.
8. Enter the **Email** address of the tenant or property owner, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rental Payment Form 1** (`rental_payment_form_1`, text, optional): Enter a brief description or reference number for the rental payment.
* **Tenant's Name** (`tenant_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the tenant or property owner.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how the rent was paid, such as Credit Card, Debit Card, or Bank Transfer.
* **Due Date** (`due_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the payment is due in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Amount Paid** (`amount_paid`, number, optional): Enter the amount paid for the rental.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the payment, which can be Paid, Overdue, or Pending.
* **Notes** (`note`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or explanations about the payment.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the tenant or property owner, if required.
