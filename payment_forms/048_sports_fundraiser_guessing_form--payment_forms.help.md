# Sports Fundraiser Guessing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Fundraiser Guessing Form is a simple form designed to help raise funds for a sports-related charity event. Individuals can predict scores, make monetary contributions, or enter their name and donation to support their favorite team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the team name that you think will win or is your favorite team.
* **Prediction** (`score_prediction`, text, required/optional): Predict the final score for that team.
* **Entry** (`entry_dollar_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount of money you would like to donate.
* **Donation** (`donation_dollar_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter a different amount that you would like to donate if you're not making an entry.
* **Entry Donation** (`entry_dollar_amount_other`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount of money you're contributing for your entry.
* **Entry Name** (`entry_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name if you're making an entry.
* **Entry Email** (`entry_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address if you're making an entry.
* **Entry Phone** (`entry_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number if you're making an entry.

## Tips
This form is designed to be simple and easy to use. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask the event organizers for help.
