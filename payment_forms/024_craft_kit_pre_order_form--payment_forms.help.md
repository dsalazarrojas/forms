# craft_kit_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect pre-order details and payment information from customers for the craft kit before the official release date. This information will be used to confirm the pre-order and provide updates to the customer on the status of their order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your pre-order details, including the payment amount and currency.
2. Select the payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Other).
3. Provide your shipping information, including shipping address, city, state, zip, and country.
4. Enter any additional shipping notes or comments.
5. Review your information for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
6. Submit the form to place your pre-order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Pre Order Details**: Enter your pre-order details, including the payment amount and currency.
  (`<type>: number`, `required: false`): Enter the total amount you are willing to pay for the craft kit.
- **Payment Info**: Enter the payment information, including payment method and amount.
  (`<type>: text`, `required: false`): Enter your payment method (e.g., Credit Card, PayPal).
- **Payment Amount**: Enter the total amount you are willing to pay for the craft kit.
  (`<type>: number`, `required: false`): Enter the payment amount in the currency selected.
- **Currency**: Select the currency for the payment amount.
  (`<type>: select_one`, `options: USD, EUR, JPY`, `required: false`): Choose the currency for the payment amount (USD, EUR, or JPY).
- **Payment Method**: Select the payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Other).
  (`<type>: select_one`, `options: Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, Other`, `required: false`): Choose the payment method.
- **Payment Status**: Select the status of the payment (Paid, Pending, or Refunded).
  (`<type>: select_one`, `options: Paid, Pending, Refunded`, `required: false`): Choose the payment status.
- **Shipping Address**: Enter your shipping address.
  (`<type>: text`, `required: false`): Enter your shipping address, including street address and postal code.
- **Shipping City**: Enter your shipping city.
  (`<type>: text`, `required: false`): Enter the city for the shipping address.
- **Shipping Country**: Enter your shipping country.
  (`<type>: text`, `required: false`): Enter the country for the shipping address.
- **Shipping Zip**: Enter your shipping zip code.
  (`<type>: text`, `required: false`): Enter the zip code for the shipping address.
- **Shipping State**: Enter your shipping state.
  (`<type>: text`, `required: false`): Enter the state for the shipping address.
- **Shipping Company**: Enter the shipping company name.
  (`<type>: text`, `required: false`): Enter the shipping company name.
- **Shipping Date**: Enter the shipping date.
  (`<type>: date`, `required: false`): Enter the expected shipping date.
- **Shipping Time**: Enter the shipping time.
  (`<type>: time`, `required: false`): Enter the expected shipping time.
- **Shipping Note**: Enter any additional shipping notes or comments.
  (`<type>: text`, `required: false`): Enter any additional shipping notes or comments.
- **Shipping Status**: Select the shipping status (Shipped, Pending, or Cancelled).
  (`<type>: select_one`, `options: Shipped, Pending, Cancelled`, `required: false`): Choose the shipping status.
- **Shipping Cost**: Enter the shipping cost.
  (`<type>: number`, `required: false`): Enter the shipping cost.
- **Customer Name**: Enter your name.
  (`<type>: text`, `required: false`): Enter your name.
- **Customer E-Mail**: Enter your email address.
  (`<type>: email`, `required: false`): Enter your email address.
- **Payment Terms**: Enter any payment terms or conditions.
  (`<type>: text`, `required: false`): Enter any payment terms or conditions.
- **Submit**: Review and submit the form to place your pre-order. 

## Tips
- Make sure to review your information for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues or questions, please contact us.
- We will contact you with updates on the status of your pre-order.
