# supplier_golf_day_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for suppliers or service providers to register or create events related to golf. The purpose of this form is to provide a basic template for users to enter information about the event, including contact details, event specifics, and tool assignments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and email address in the respective text fields.
2. Select the event type from the "Event Type" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the event date and time in the designated date and time fields.
4. Add any additional event details in the "Event Details" note field.
5. Assign a tool (e.g., employee or team member) from the "Assigned Tool" dropdown menu.
6. Optionally, enter company and contact information for the golf club.
7. Submit the form once all required fields are filled in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Company Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the company or service provider.
* **Company Address** (text, required/optional): Enter the address of the company or service provider.
* **Company Website** (text, required/optional): Enter the website of the company or service provider.
* **Event Type** (select-one, required/optional): Select the type of golf event ("Yes" or "No").
* **Event Date** (date, required/optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (time, required/optional): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Details** (note, required/optional): Add any additional details about the event.
* **Golf Club Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the golf club.
* **Golf Club Location** (text, required/optional): Enter the location of the golf club.
* **Golf Club Contact** (text, required/optional): Enter the contact information for the golf club.
* **Assigned Tool** (select-one, required): Select the tool or team member assigned to the event.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Use the "Event Details" field to add any additional information about the event.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this guide for clarification.
