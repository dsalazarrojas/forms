# Custom Recipe Book Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Custom Recipe Book Order Form is designed to collect information for custom recipe book orders. Please fill in the form with your personal contact and payment details to place your order.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your email address to receive updates and confirmation of your order.
2. Fill in your first and last name.
3. Provide your address, city, state, and zip code.
4. Enter your phone numbers (home, work, or cell).
5. Select your preferred payment method (Bank Transfer, PayPal, or Credit Card).
6. Enter any additional order notes or comments.
7. Fill in your country, region, and time zone.
8. Enter your currency and currency symbol (if applicable).
9. Confirm your order date and time.
10. Enter the total amount of your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address to receive updates and confirmation of your order.
* **customer_first_name** (text, required): Fill in your first name.
* **customer_last_name** (text, required): Fill in your last name.
* **customer_address** (text, required): Provide your address.
* **customer_city** (text, required): Enter your city.
* **customer_state** (text, optional): Enter your state.
* **customer_zip** (text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **customer_phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **customer_cell_phone** (text, optional): Enter your cell phone number.
* **customer_work_phone** (text, optional): Enter your work phone number.
* **customer_country** (text, optional): Enter your country.
* **customer_country_code** (number, optional): Enter your country code.
* **customer_region** (text, optional): Enter your region.
* **customer_language** (text, optional): Enter your language.
* **customer_time_zone** (text, optional): Enter your time zone.
* **customer_currency** (text, optional): Enter your currency.
* **customer_currency_symbol** (text, optional): Enter your currency symbol.
* **order_date** (date, required): Enter the date of your order.
* **order_time** (time, required): Enter the time of your order.
* **order_total** (number, required): Enter the total amount of your order.
* **payment_method** (select_one, optional): Select your preferred payment method (Bank Transfer, PayPal, or Credit Card).
* **payment_terms** (text, optional): Enter any payment terms or conditions.
* **order_notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional order notes or comments.
* **customer_note** (note, optional): Enter any additional customer notes or comments.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately to ensure that your order is processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
* Please note that some fields may be optional, but it's essential to fill in the required fields to ensure that your order is processed correctly.
