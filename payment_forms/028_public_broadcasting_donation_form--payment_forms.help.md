# Public Broadcasting Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The public broadcasting donation form is used to collect donations from supporters of a public broadcasting entity. This information is used to tailor communication and follow up on donations.

## How To Complete This Form
- Follow the steps below to complete the form:
 1. Fill in your **Donation Amount**. This is the amount you are willing to donate. This field is required.
 2. Enter your **Supporter Name** for your own identification purposes.
 3. Enter your **Email** address so we can contact you about your donation.
 4. Select your **Phone Number** if you have one. This is not required.
  5. If you have a message for us, you can type it in under **Message**.
  6. Choose how frequently you support us: **Donation Frequency**
  7. Select which medium you prefer for communication: **Preferred Medium**
  8. Choose the message you want us to send you: **Message to Admin**
  9. Indicate whether you want to receive reminders: **Send Reminders**
  10. Indicate whether you want to receive a thank-you email: **Send Thank You Email**
  11. Select how you would like to receive donation summaries: **Send Donation Summary**
  12. Choose whether you want to receive supporter information: **Send Supporter Info**
  13. If you have a preferred way of supporting, enter it under **Supporter Info**.
  14. Enter your **Supporter Email** and **Supporter Phone Number** if you have them.
  15. If you have a message for your supporter: **Supporter Message**.
  16. Select how frequently you support us: **Supporter Frequency**
  17. Select how you would like to receive supporter information: **Supporter Preferred Medium**
  18. Enter any other information about your payment method if it's not listed: **Payment Method Other**
  19. If you have any other payment method details, enter them here: **Payment Method Other Details**
  20. Enter any other information about your banking: **Bank Transfer Info**
  21. Enter any other information about how often you support us: **Supporter Frequency Info**

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Amount (required)**: Enter the amount you are willing to donate.
* **Supporter Name**: Enter your name for our records.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone (optional)**: Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **Message**: Enter a message for us, if you have any.
* **Donation Frequency (optional)**: Choose how frequently you support us.
* **Preferred Medium (optional)**: Choose your preferred medium of contact.
* **Message to Admin (optional)**: Choose the message you want us to send you.
* **Send Reminders (optional)**: Indicate whether you want to receive reminders.
* **Send Thank You Email (optional)**: Indicate whether you want to receive a thank-you email.
* **Send Donation Summary (optional)**: Choose how you would like to receive donation summaries.
* **Send Supporter Info (optional)**: Choose whether you want to receive supporter information.
* **Supporter Info (optional)**: Enter any other information about your supporter.
* **Supporter Email (optional)**: Enter your supporter's email address.
* **Supporter Phone (optional)**: Enter your supporter's phone number.
* **Supporter Message (optional)**: Enter a message for your supporter.
* **Supporter Frequency (optional)**: Choose how frequently you support us.
* **Supporter Preferred Medium (optional)**: Choose how you would like to receive supporter information.
* **Admin Message (optional)**: Enter any other information about your admin communication.
* **Bank Transfer Info (optional)**: Enter any other information about your banking.
* **Supporter Frequency Info (optional)**: Enter any other information about your supporter's frequency.
* **Payment Method (optional)**: Select your payment method.
* **Payment Method Other (optional)**: Enter any other information about your payment method.
* **Payment Method Other Details (optional)**: Enter any other information about your payment method details.
* **Payment Method Other (optional)**: Enter any other information about your banking.
