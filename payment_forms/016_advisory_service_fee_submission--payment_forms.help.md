# Advisory Service Fee Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit advisory service fees to our organization. The purpose of this form is to provide a clear and concise way to capture essential information related to the advisory service provided, including client contact details, service description, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's information, including their name, email, and phone number.
2. Enter the invoice number and brief description of the advisory service provided.
3. Enter the date when the service was rendered.
4. Select the payment method and enter the amount due.
5. Enter the billing address associated with the payment method.
6. Add any additional notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Information
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the client, whether it's a company or an individual.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address where the receipt will be sent.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter the client's phone number.

### Service Details
* **Invoice Number** (`invoice_number`, `text`, required): Enter the invoice number for the advisory service.
* **Service Description** (`service_description`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the advisory service provided.
* **Service Date** (`service_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the service was rendered.

### Payment Information
* **Select Payment Type** (`payment_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment method from the available options (Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer).
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address associated with the payment method.

### Additional Information
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the submission.

Note: Please make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
