# corporate_formation_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The <corporate_formation_order_form> is used for customers to order a corporate formation, providing essential details about their requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately to avoid delays or errors.
- Review all fields for correct and complete information before submitting.
- Submit the form only once to avoid duplicate orders.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company Name** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your company's name as it will appear in public documents.
- **Address** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your company's address for communication and legal purposes.
- **City** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your company's city for address purposes.
- **State** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your company's state for address purposes.
- **Zip Code** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your company's zip code for address purposes.
- **Formation Type** (`6`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of corporate formation you require: Formation of an existing corporation, Formation of a new corporation, or Formation of a non-profit.
- **Payment Method** (`7`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the payment method you prefer: Credit Card, Bank Account, or Check or Money Order.
- **Amount** (`8`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the amount for your corporate formation order.
- **Payment Date** (`9`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of your payment.
- **Payment Time** (`10`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of your payment.
- **Email** (`11`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address for communication.
- **Phone** (`12`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your company's phone number for communication.
- **Fax** (`13`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your company's fax number for communication.
- **Company Type** (`14`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of company you want to form: Corporation, LLC, or Sole Proprietorship.
- **Officers** (`15`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate if your company will have officers: Yes or No.
- **Order Date** (`16`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of your order.
- **Submitted** (`17`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate if this order has been submitted: True or False.
- **Submitted Date** (`18`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you submitted your order.
- **Comments** (`19`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any comments about your order.
- **Assigned Tool** (`20`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the tool assigned to your order.
- **Submitted By** (`21`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name, which will appear on the submitted order.
- **Submitted Date Time** (`22`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the date and time you submitted your order.
- **Submission Date Time** (`23`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the submission date and time of your order.
- **Submission Date Time** (`24`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the submission date and time of your order.
- **Payment Status** (`25`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the payment status: Payment Pending, Payment Confirmed, or Payment Denied.
