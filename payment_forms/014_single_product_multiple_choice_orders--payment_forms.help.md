# single_product_multiple_choice_orders - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to create a single-product multiple-choice order. It is designed for situations where a user needs to select a product and specify additional order details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select a Product**: Choose the product you want to order from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter **Quantity**: Type in the quantity of the product you want to order.
3. Enter **Email**: Enter your email address to help us contact you about your order.
4. Enter **Name**: Enter your name to help us with order fulfillment and contact.
5. Enter **Order Date**: Choose the date you want to order by.
6. Enter **Order Time**: Choose the time you want to order by.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **product_choice**: Select the product you want to order. This field provides a dropdown menu with the available products.
* **quantity**: Enter the number of products you want to order.
* **email**: Enter your email address to help us contact you about your order.
* **name**: Enter your name to help us with order fulfillment and contact.
* **order_date**: Choose the date you want to order by. This field accepts dates in a standard format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **order_time**: Choose the time you want to order by. This field accepts times in a standard format (HH:MM:SS).
