# designer_notebook_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The `designer_notebook_pre_order_form` is a form used for pre-ordering a designer notebook. This form collects payment and delivery information from customers. It is designed for both individual and business customers, and it allows them to provide details about their payment method, address, and order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the payment details: Select your payment method (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, or PayPal) and enter the payment amount.
2. Enter your delivery details: Provide the address where you would like your order to be delivered.
3. Review your order total: The total amount of your order will be automatically calculated based on the payment amount and other factors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Payment Details (1)**: Enter the details of your payment method, including the type (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, or PayPal) and the amount to be paid.
- **Payment Method (2)**: Select the payment method you will be using for this pre-order.
- **Payment Amount (3)**: Enter the amount of money you will be paying.
- **Delivery Date (4)**: Choose the date you would like your order to be delivered on.
- **Delivery Time (5)**: Select the time of day you would like your order to be delivered.
- **Delivery Address (6)**: Enter the address where you would like your order to be delivered.
- **Delivery City (7)**: Enter the city where the order will be delivered.
- **Delivery State (8)**: Enter the state where the order will be delivered.
- **Delivery Postal Code (9)**: Enter the postal code where the order will be delivered.
- **Order Total (10)**: This field will display the total amount of your order.
- **Payment Method 2 (11)**: Select an additional payment method if needed.
- **Payment Amount 2 (12)**: Enter the amount to be paid using the additional payment method.
- **Delivery Details (13)**: Provide any additional details for your delivery, such as special instructions.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate payment and delivery information to avoid any issues with your order.
- Review your order total carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or issues with the form, contact our support team.
