# Toy Prototype Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for toy prototypes orders. It is used to gather details about the prototype design requirements and customer information for processing the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the relevant information in the **Toy Prototype Order Form** field.
2. Select the **Yes** or **No** option in the **order_type** field to indicate if this is a new or existing order.
3. In the **Design Requirements** field, provide any specific design requirements or requests for the prototype.
4. Enter the relevant delivery information in the **Delivery Info** field.
5. Fill in the customer information in the **Customer Info** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Toy Prototype Order Form** (1): Enter a brief description of the prototype order.
* **order_type** (2): Choose whether this is a new or existing order.
* **Design Requirements** (3): Provide any specific design requirements or requests for the prototype.
* **Delivery Info** (4): Enter the delivery details, such as address and date.
* **Customer Info** (5): Fill in the customer's name, address, and contact information.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check the accuracy of the information provided.
- If any field is left blank, it may delay or affect the processing of the order.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant team for assistance.
