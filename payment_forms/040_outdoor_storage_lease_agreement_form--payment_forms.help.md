# Outdoor Storage Lease Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Outdoor Storage Lease Agreement Form is used to collect information from renters or lessees to create an outdoor storage lease agreement. This form helps to establish a legal contract between the storage facility and the renter, outlining the terms and conditions of the lease. It's essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth and secure rental experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Renter Details section with your name, address, and contact information.
2. Provide the name of the contact person responsible for the unit.
3. Enter the unit's address where you will be storing your items.
4. Enter your phone number and email address for easy communication.
5. Select the start and end dates for your lease.
6. Enter the unit number assigned to you.
7. Double-check all the entered information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Renter Details** (`renter_details`, text, required: false): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant information about yourself as the renter.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Provide the name and contact information of the person who will be the primary point of contact for this rental.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter the physical address where you will be storing your items.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for easy communication with the storage facility.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for easy communication with the storage facility.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Select the start date of your lease. This is the date when you will begin storing your items at the facility.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Select the end date of your lease. This is the date when your lease will expire, and you will need to remove your items from the facility.
* **Unit Number** (`unit_number`, number, required: false): Enter the unit number assigned to you for easy identification and access to your unit.
