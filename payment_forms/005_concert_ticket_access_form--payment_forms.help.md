# Concert Ticket Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Concert Ticket Access Form is a user-facing guide designed to collect attendee information for concert ticket access. The form helps individuals provide necessary details for smooth ticket delivery and access processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your attendee's full name as on the ticket.
2. Provide your ticket or order ID number.
3. Enter the email used to purchase the ticket.
4. Choose how you want to receive your ticket:
	* Mobile entry: Receive your ticket on your mobile device.
	* Print at home: Receive a physical ticket to print at home.
	* Will call pickup: Pick up your ticket at the specified location.
5. If you selected will call pickup, specify the ID verification method:
	* Driver license
	* Passport
	* Government ID
6. If you selected will call pickup, enter the delivery address for your ticket.
7. If applicable, specify any accessibility needs or requirements.
8. List any additional contact names and phone numbers.
9. Add any additional notes for ticket access.

- **Access type requested** (`Access type requested`, **Required**): Choose how you want to receive your ticket: Mobile entry, Print at home, or Will call pickup.
- **ID verification method** (`ID verification method`, **Optional**): If you selected will call pickup, choose one of the ID verification methods: Driver license, Passport, or Government ID.
- **Delivery address** (`Delivery address`, **Optional**): Enter the delivery address if you selected will call pickup for ticket delivery.
- **Preferred will call pickup location** (`Preferred will call pickup location`, **Optional**): If you selected will call pickup, choose the preferred pickup location.
