# Domain Leasing Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the leasing process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have understood the purpose and instructions.
2. Select the type of lease you are interested in from the first field, "Domain Leasing Application".
3. Fill in your domain provider information in the "domain_provider" field.
4. Provide details about the domain in the "domain_details" field.
5. Select your business or personal use for the domain from the "business_use" field.
6. List your available domains for lease in the "available_domain" field.
7. Specify your lease terms and conditions in the "lease_terms" field.
8. Choose how long you would like to lease the domain for in the "lease_duration" field.
9. Select whether you want to opt-in for website maintenance or not in the "website_maintenance" field.
10. Choose where you would like to sign the agreement in the "agreement_signing" field.
11. Review your selections carefully and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Domain Leasing Application** (`domain_leasing_application`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of lease you are interested in.
* **Domain Provider** (`domain_provider`, text, required: false): Provide your domain provider information.
* **Domain Details** (`domain_details`, text, required: false): Enter details about the domain you are leasing.
* **Business Use** (`business_use`, select_one, required: false): Select your business or personal use for the domain.
* **Available Domains for Lease** (`available_domain`, text, required: false): List your available domains for lease.
* **Lease Terms** (`lease_terms`, text, required: false): Specify your lease terms and conditions.
* **Lease Duration** (`lease_duration`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose how long you would like to lease the domain for (1, 3, or 6 months).
* **Website Maintenance** (`website_maintenance`, select_multiple, required: false): Select whether you want to opt-in for website maintenance or not (True, False, or None).
* **Agreement Signing** (`agreement_signing`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose where you would like to sign the agreement (By Hand, Online, or Paper).

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Ensure you have answered all required fields accurately.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact our support team for clarification.
* We will be in touch with you shortly after submitting the form to discuss your application.
