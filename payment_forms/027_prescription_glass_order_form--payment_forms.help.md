# Prescription Glass Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about your prescription glass order. Please fill in your details accurately to ensure a smooth ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select your Prescription Glasses preference from the drop-down menu.
5. Choose your Frames preference from the drop-down menu.
6. Enter your Prescription details in the "Prescription" field.
7. Enter the number of lenses you need in the "Lens" field.
8. Select the material of your glasses.
9. Select the color of your glasses.
10. Enter the power of your prescription in the "Power" field.
11. Enter the axis of your prescription in the "Axis" field.
12. Enter the add power of your prescription in the "Add Power" field.
13. Enter the add axis of your prescription in the "Add Axis" field.
14. Select your shipping preference from the drop-down menu.
15. Enter your Shipping Address.
16. Select your payment method from the drop-down menu.
17. Select your card type from the drop-down menu.
18. Enter your card number.
19. Enter your expiration date of your card in the "Expiration Date" field.
20. Select the expiration month of your card from the drop-down menu.
21. Enter the expiration year of your card.
22. Enter your Cvv.
23. Enter your Security Code.
24. Select the "Submit" button to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required)
  Enter your First Name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required)
  Enter your Last Name.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required)
  Enter your email address.
* **Prescription Glasses** (`Prescription Glasses`, select_one, required)
  Select your prescription glasses.
* **Frames** (`Frames`, select_multiple, required)
  Choose your frames preference.
* **Prescription** (`Prescription`, text, required)
  Enter your prescription details.
* **Lens** (`Lens`, number, required)
  Enter the number of lenses you need.
* **Material** (`Material`, select_one, required)
  Choose the material of your glasses.
* **Color** (`Color`, select_one, required)
  Select the color of your glasses.
* **Power** (`Power`, number, required)
  Enter the power of your prescription.
* **Axis** (`Axis`, number, required)
  Enter the axis of your prescription.
* **Add Power** (`Add Power`, number, required)
  Enter the add power of your prescription.
* **Add Axis** (`Add Axis`, number, required)
  Enter the add axis of your prescription.
* **Shipping** (`Shipping`, select_one, required)
  Choose your shipping preference.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required)
  Enter your Shipping Address.
* **Payment** (`Payment`, select_one, required)
  Select your payment method.
* **Card Type** (`Card Type`, select_one, required)
  Select your card type.
* **Card Number** (`Card Number`, text, required)
  Enter your card number.
* **Expiration Date** (`Expiration Date`, date, required)
  Enter your card expiration date.
* **Expiration Month** (`Expiration Month`, select_one, required)
  Select the expiration month of your card.
