# Curbside Pickup Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Curbside Pickup Order Form is used for customers to place orders for curbside pickup at a store. This form is designed for customers to provide their order details, including order number, customer information, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with the required information. While most of the fields are optional, it is recommended that customers provide as much information as possible to ensure accurate processing of their order.
2. Choose a payment method (Card or Cash) in the "Payment Method" field.
3. Enter your payment details, such as card type or number, in the "Payment Details" field.
4. Fill out any additional fields, including order total, customer address, order comments, store name, store phone, store address, and store hours.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Number**: A unique identifier for your order.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date of your order.
* **Pickup Time**: Select the time you would like to pick up your order.
* **Payment Method**: Select between "Card" or "Cash" for payment method.
* **Payment Details**: Enter your payment details, such as card type or number. 
* **Order Total**: The total value of your order.
* **Customer Address**: Enter your delivery address for the store to contact you.
* **Order Comments**: Enter any additional comments about your order.
* **Store Name**: Enter the name of the store where you will pick up your order.
* **Store Phone**: Enter the phone number of the store where you will pick up your order.
* **Store Address**: Enter the address of the store where you will pick up your order.
* **Store Hours**: Enter the hours of operation of the store where you will pick up your order.

Note: While the form has some optional fields, it is recommended that customers provide as much information as possible to ensure accurate processing of their order.
