# School Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Donation Form is designed to collect information from donors making a payment on behalf of a school. This form is used to track and process donations for schools, ensuring accurate and efficient record-keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Doner Information, including your name and any additional details you'd like to provide.
2. Specify the Donation Amount and select the Donation Frequency.
3. Choose your preferred Payment Method (Cash, PayPal, or Credit Card).
4. Enter your Payment Information for the chosen payment method.
5. Fill in your Donor Name, Email, Phone, and any additional details as required.
6. Select the payment Gateway (Stripe, PayPal, or Bank Transfer) and Payment Status (Paid, Pending, or Unpaid).
7. If applicable, choose your preferred Payment Method 2 (Online or Offline).
8. Fill in any additional fields as needed, such as School Donation Amount, Student Name, Student Information, and Student Phone.
