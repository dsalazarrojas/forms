# Vendor Payment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Vendor Payment Request Form" is a tool used to request payment from vendors for various services or goods provided. This form collects essential information to facilitate the payment request process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Determine if you are requesting payment from the vendor (`payment_method` field).
2.  Enter the vendor's name in the `vendor_name` field.
3.  Enter the amount due from the vendor in the `payment_amount` field.
4.  Specify the invoice date in the `invoice_date` field.
5.  Check if payment terms are agreed upon in the `payment_terms` field.
6.  Enter the due date for payment in the `due_date` field.
7.  Check if the vendor has referred you to our company in the `vendor_referral` field.
8.  Enter the date of the request in the `request_date` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **payment_method**: This field is a select multiple field asking if you are requesting payment from the vendor. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'. 
*   **vendor_name**: Enter the name of the vendor you are requesting payment from.
*   **payment_amount**: Enter the amount due from the vendor.
*   **invoice_date**: Specify the date the invoice was issued.
*   **payment_terms**: Check if payment terms are agreed upon with the vendor.
*   **due_date**: Enter the due date for payment.
*   **vendor_referral**: Check if the vendor has referred you to our company.
*   **request_date**: Enter the date of the payment request. 

## Tips
- Before submitting your request, make sure you have filled out all necessary fields.
- Double-check the entered values for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the responsible team for assistance.
