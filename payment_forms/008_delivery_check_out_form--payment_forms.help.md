# Delivery Check Out Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for the delivery process, including delivery date, shipping address, and payment details. It is intended for delivery management and organization purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the delivery date in the format MM/DD/YY (month/day/year).
2. Enter the shipping address in the format "Street Address, City, State, Zip".
3. Select the shipping city from the list of options.
4. Select the shipping state from the list of options.
5. Select the shipping country from the list of options.
6. Enter the customer name.
7. Enter the customer email address.
8. Enter the customer phone number.
9. Enter the order total.
10. Select the payment method(s) used (e.g., credit card, PayPal).
11. Enter any additional payment details or terms.
12. Enter any delivery notes or instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Date**: <deliv_date> (MM/DD/YY): Enter the delivery date of the order.
* **Shipping Address**: <shipping_address> (Street Address, City, State, Zip): Enter the street address where the order will be delivered.
* **Shipping City**: <shipping_city> (select from options): Select the city where the order will be shipped from the list of options.
* **Shipping State**: <shipping_state> (select from options): Select the state where the order will be shipped from the list of options.
* **Shipping Country**: <shipping_country> (select from options): Select the country where the order will be shipped from the list of options.
* **Customer Name**: <customer_name> (text): Enter the customer's name.
* **Email**: <email> (email): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone**: <phone> (text): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Order Total**: <order_total> (number): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Payment Method**: <payment_method> (select from options): Select the payment method used for the order (e.g., credit card, PayPal).
* **Order Notes**: <order_notes> (text): Enter any additional notes or instructions for the delivery.
* **Payment Details**: <payment_details> (text): Enter any additional payment details or terms.
* **Delivery Notes**: <delivery_notes> (text): Enter any delivery notes or instructions.

## Tips
* Use the format MM/DD/YY for the delivery date.
* Use the format "Street Address, City, State, Zip" for the shipping address.
* Make sure to select the correct payment method.
* Enter any additional payment details or terms in the "Payment Details" field. 
* Enter any delivery notes or instructions in the "Delivery Notes" field.
