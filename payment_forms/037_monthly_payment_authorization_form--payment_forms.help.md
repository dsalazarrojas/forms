# monthly_payment_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a convenient and secure way for users to authorize and schedule monthly payments. It is designed to collect necessary information to facilitate smooth and efficient payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with accurate information, as required.
2. Review and adjust as needed.
3. Submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **monthly_payment_form (text, text, false)**: 
This is the main payment authorization form, where you can input your desired payment details.

### 2. **membership_plan (select_one, select, false)**: 
Select the type of membership plan you're interested in. This will help us tailor your payment schedule accordingly.

### 3. **payment_method (select_multiple, select, false)**: 
Choose how you'd like to make your payments. You can select one or multiple methods for payment (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, or PayPal).

### 4. **payment_cycle (select_one, select, false)**: 
Determine how often you'd like to make your payments. Options include monthly, annual, or a one-time payment.

### 5. **email (email, email, false)**: 
Enter your email address to receive notifications about your payment status.

### 6. **phone (text, text, false)**: 
Input your phone number for any additional communication about your payment.

### 7. **address (text, text, false)**: 
Provide your address for any additional context or information related to your payment.

### 8. **name (text, text, false)**: 
Input your name for identification purposes.

### 9. **amount (number, number, false)**: 
Enter the amount you're willing to pay.

### 10. **start_date (date, date, false)**: 
Specify the start date of your payment schedule.

### 11. **end_date (date, date, false)**: 
Input the end date of your payment schedule.

### 12. **notes (note, note, false)**: 
Add any additional comments or notes related to your payment.

### 13. **payment_frequency (select_multiple, select, false)**: 
Choose how frequently you'd like to make your payments. Options include weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, or annually.

### 14. **bank_transfer_info (text, text, false)**: 
Provide any additional information related to your bank transfer, if applicable.

### 15. **bank_transfer_type (text, text, false)**: 
Input any specific details about your bank transfer, if applicable.
