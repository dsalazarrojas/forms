# product Delivery Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The product Delivery Form is used by customers to provide delivery information for their orders. This form allows customers to input their delivery address, phone number, and delivery date and time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter your phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
3. Enter your delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field.
4. Select the delivery date in the "Delivery Date" field.
5. Select the delivery time in the "Delivery Time" field.
6. Enter any additional delivery note in the "Delivery Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
  - This field is used to verify your identity.
- **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you regarding delivery arrangements.
  - This field is used to contact you regarding any delivery issues or updates.
- **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required): Enter the complete address where you would like your order to be delivered.
  - Please ensure the address is accurate and up-to-date.
- **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required): Select the date you would like to receive your order.
  - Please select a date when you are available to receive your order.
- **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required): Select the time you would like to receive your order.
  - Please select a time that suits your schedule.
- **Delivery Note** (`delivery_note`, note, required): Enter any special delivery instructions or requests.
  - If you have any special requirements or requests for delivery, please enter them here.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the delivery address you enter is correct and up-to-date.
* If you need to change your delivery date or time, please contact our support team in advance.
* This form must be completed for each order separately.
