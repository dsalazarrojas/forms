# Crowdfunding Reward Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from backers during a crowdfunding campaign. It is used to determine their preferred reward and shipping details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details on the "Backer Details" page.
2. Select your preferred reward tier from the "Reward Tier" dropdown menu.
3. If you have any special shipping instructions, please fill in the "Shipping Details" page.
4. Enter your email address on the "Backer Email" page.
5. Choose your preferred shipping method from the "Shipping Method" dropdown menu.
6. Finally, enter your phone number on the "Backer Phone" page (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Backer Details** (`backer_details`, text, optional): This page is used to collect your name and any other details you would like to share with the campaign creator.
* **Reward Tier** (`reward_tier`, select_one, optional): This dropdown menu allows you to choose your preferred reward tier. Select one of the options to proceed.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, optional): This page is used to provide any additional shipping instructions that the campaign creator should be aware of.
* **Backer Info** (`backer_info`, text, optional): This page is used to provide any additional information about yourself that you would like to share with the campaign creator.
* **Pledge Amount** (`pledge_amount`, number, optional): This field is used to enter the amount you are pledging.
* **Backer Email** (`backer_email`, email, optional): This page is used to enter your email address.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, select_one, optional): This dropdown menu allows you to choose your preferred shipping method.
* **Backer Phone** (`backer_phone`, text, optional): This page is used to enter your phone number (if necessary).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure a smooth campaign process.
* If you have any questions, you can contact the campaign creator for assistance.
