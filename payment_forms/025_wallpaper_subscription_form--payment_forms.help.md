# Wallpaper Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wallpaper Subscription Form is used to collect information from users to create a subscription plan for wallpaper services. This form will guide you through the process of selecting a plan that meets your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To get started, fill out each field in the form according to the instructions below.
2. Please note that each field is marked with a label that indicates what it represents.
3. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, required/optional): Please enter your email address to be contacted for subscription updates and notifications.
* **Plan** (`plan`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the plan that suits your needs. You can select multiple plans if you want to.
* **Subscription Start Date** (`subscription_start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of your subscription plan. This is the date when you want your subscription to begin.
* **Subscription End Date** (`subscription_end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of your subscription plan. This is the date when you want your subscription to end.
* **Recurring Payment** (`recurring_payment`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate whether you want recurring payments or not.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the payment method you prefer (e.g. credit card, bank transfer, etc.).
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how often you want to make payments (e.g. monthly, annually, etc.).
* **Recurring Preferences** (`recurring_preferences`, note, required/optional): Add any recurring preferences you may have (e.g. payment method, payment frequency, etc.).
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your billing address for payment processing.
* **Billing City** (`billing_city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city of your billing address.
* **Billing State** (`billing_state`, text, required/optional): Enter the state of your billing address.
* **Billing Zip** (`billing_zip`, text, required/optional): Enter the zip code of your billing address.
* **Billing Country** (`billing_country`, text, required/optional): Enter the country of your billing address.
* **Payment Gateway** (`payment_gateway`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the payment gateway to use (e.g. Stripe, PayPal, etc.).
* **Billing Status** (`billing_status`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the status of your billing account (e.g. Active, Inactive, etc.).
* **Payment Frequency Type** (`payment_frequency_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of payment frequency (e.g. Daily, Weekly, etc.).

## Tips
* Please fill out each field carefully and accurately to ensure your subscription plan is correct.
* If you have any questions or concerns, refer to the form instructions above.
* If you need further assistance, contact our support team.
