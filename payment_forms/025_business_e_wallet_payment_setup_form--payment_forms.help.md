# Business E Wallet Payment Setup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential business information and setup details for the e-wallet payment system.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure a smooth and secure payment process.
2. Enter your business name, type, tax ID or EIN, address, phone number, and contact information.
3. Select the e-wallet provider and provide details about your expected monthly processing volume, average transaction amount, and types of transactions.
4. Provide information about your current payment processor, desired setup timeline, system compatibility, and PCI compliance status.
5. Enter the name and contact information of the authorized person and the date of authorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, **Required**): Enter your legal business name.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, **Required**): Select your type of business, such as Retail, E-Commerce, Services, etc.
* **Tax ID or EIN** (`tax_id`, **Required**): Enter your federal tax ID or EIN (Employer Identification Number).
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, **Required**): Enter your street address.
* **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, **Required**): Enter your main business phone number.
* **Primary Contact Name** (`primary_contact`, **Required**): Enter the name of the authorized contact.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, **Required**): Enter the position of the primary contact.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, **Required**): Enter your business email.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, **Required**): Enter the direct phone number of the primary contact.
* **E-Wallet Provider** (`ewallet_provider`, **Required**): Select your e-wallet provider, such as Apple Pay, Google Pay, etc.
* **Expected Monthly Processing Volume** (`processing_volume`, **Required**): Enter your approximate monthly transactions.
* **Average Transaction Amount** (`average_transaction`, **Required**): Enter the average transaction amount.
* **Types of Transactions** (`transaction_types`, **Required**, Multiple Select**): Select the types of transactions you will process, such as In-Store Purchases, Online Purchases, etc.
* **Current Payment Processor** (`current_processor`, **Optional**): Enter your current payment processor, if applicable.
* **Desired Setup Timeline** (`setup_timeline`, **Required**): Select the desired setup timeline, such as Immediately, Within 1 Week, etc.
* **System Compatibility** (`system_compatibility`, **Optional**): Enter the name of your POS system, if applicable.
* **PCI Compliance Status** (`security_compliance`, **Required**): Select your PCI compliance status, such as Fully Compliant, Partial Compliance, etc.
* **Authorized By** (`authorized_signature`, **Required**): Enter the name of the authorizing officer.
* **Authorization Date** (`authorization_date`, **Required**): Enter the date of authorization.
* **System Compatibility** (`system_compatibility`, **Optional**): Enter the name of your current system compatibility.
* **Authorization Date** (`authorization_date`, **Required**): Enter the date of authorization.

Note: Fields marked as **Required** must be completed for the form to be submitted.
