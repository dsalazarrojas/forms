# University Endowment Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for donors to provide their support to the University Endowment Fund. It collects contact information, gift details, and notification preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete each page of the form one at a time, using the instructions below.
2. Enter your contact information on the first page, including your name and address.
3. Enter your phone number and gift details on the second page, including the gift amount and fund choice.
4. Select your notification preference for recurring gifts on the third page.
5. Choose your gift frequency and start and end dates (if applicable) on the fourth page.
6. Add any additional comments about your gift on the fifth page.
7. Enter your email address and read the donar agreement on the sixth page.
8. Choose your gift receipt method and any additional notification preference on the seventh and eighth pages, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Name**: This is the name of the person making the donation.
* **Donor Address**: Enter your home or office address.
* **Donor Phone**: Enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted about your donation. (Optional)
* **Gift Amount**: Enter a whole number, representing the amount of the donation.
* **Gift Fund**: Choose the fund that you would like to support. Options include Fund A, Fund B, and Fund C.
* **Recurring Gifts**: Check "True" if you would like your donation to be recurring. (Optional)
* **Gift Frequency**: Choose the frequency of your gift: Monthly or One-time.
* **Gift Start Date**: Choose when you would like your gift to start. Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Gift End Date**: Choose when you would like your gift to end. Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY. (Optional)
* **Gift Comment**: Enter any additional comments about your gift.
* **Donor E-mail**: Enter your email address.
* **Donor Agreement**: Read and agree to the terms of the University Endowment Fund. (Note: This is a required field.)
* **Gift Receipt Method**: Choose how you would like to receive your gift receipt: by Email or by Mail.
* **Gift Notification**: Check "True" if you would like to receive notifications about your gift. (Optional)
