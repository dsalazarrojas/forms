# Loan Repayment Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Loan Repayment Payment Form is used to make payments towards loan repayments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. On the first page, simply click "Next" to proceed to the next page.
3. On the second page, enter the loan repayment amount in the "Loan Repayment Amount" field. This field is required and must be filled out in order to proceed.
4. Enter the loan repayment date in the "Loan Repayment Date" field. This field is also required and must be filled out in order to proceed.
5. Once you have filled out all required fields, click "Submit" to submit your payment information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Loan Repayment Amount** (`Loan Repayment Amount`, `number`, required): Enter the amount being repaid on the loan. This is the amount you are paying towards your loan balance.
* **Loan Repayment Date** (`Loan Repayment Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the payment is being made. This date should match the date you are using for your loan repayment recordkeeping. 

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your payment is processed correctly.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to your loan representative for assistance.
