# Beverage Logistics Compliance Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Beverage Logistics Compliance Order Form is used to gather essential information for the processing and delivery of beverage products. This form ensures that we have all necessary details for compliance and delivery purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information as a buyer, including your name, title, and email address.
2. Enter your company's information, including the name, distributor license number, tax ID or EIN, and license expiration date.
3. Specify the delivery address, city, state or province, and country.
4. Choose the type of beverages being ordered (e.g., non-alcoholic, beer, wine, etc.).
5. Confirm the total order quantity and temperature control requirements.
6. Declare any hazardous materials in the order.
7. Verify your ability to comply with age restrictions and labeling requirements.
8. Confirm your export compliance status (if applicable).
9. Select your preferred payment method and provide your billing address if different from the delivery address.
10. Provide a purchase order number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Buyer Name**: Enter your full name as the authorized person placing this order.
* **Title or Position**: Enter your title or position within the company.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the business placing the order.
* **Distributor License Number**: Enter the license number issued to your company for distributing beverages.
* **License Expiration Date**: Enter the date when your license is valid until.
* **Tax ID or EIN**: Enter your business tax identification number or Employer Identification Number.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter the address where the order should be shipped.
* **City**: Enter the city where the delivery address is located.
* **State or Province**: Enter the state or province where the delivery address is located.
* **Postal Code**: Enter the postal code for the delivery address.
* **Country**: Enter the country where the delivery address is located.
* **Requested Delivery Date**: Enter the date when you need the order delivered.
* **Beverage Product Types Ordered**: Select all types of beverages that apply to your order (e.g., non-alcoholic, beer, wine, etc.).
* **Total Order Quantity**: Enter the total number of units or cases being ordered.
* **Temperature Control Required**: Select the temperature control requirements for the order (e.g., refrigerated, room temperature, frozen, etc.).
* **Does This Order Contain Hazardous Materials**: Declare if the order includes hazardous materials (e.g., alcoholic beverages, energy drinks with caffeine, etc.).
* **Age Verification Capability**: Declare your ability to verify age for restricted products.
* **All Products Have Required Labeling**: Confirm that all products have the required labeling (e.g., nutrition facts, ingredients, warnings, etc.).
* **If International - Export Compliance Status**: Declare your export compliance status if the order is for international delivery.
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, check, etc.).
* **Billing Address (if different from delivery)**: Enter your billing address if different from the delivery address.
* **Purchase Order Number (if applicable)**: Enter your internal purchase order number (if applicable).

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are completed accurately to avoid delays or errors in processing your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
* This form is for informational purposes only and will not be used for promotional or marketing activities.
