# Boat Registration Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Boat Registration Renewal Form is designed to collect information required for the renewal of your boat registration. This form is split into several sections, each focused on a specific aspect of the registration renewal process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your owner's information, including name, email, phone number, and mailing address.
2. Enter your boat details, including hull number, manufacturer, model, year of manufacture, and length.
3. Select the renewal period you wish to register for.
4. Choose the vessel use classification and type of boat you own.
5. Enter the current registration expiration date.
6. Select the payment method and enter the required details.
7. Confirm your acceptance of the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Owner Full Name
• **Owner Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
### 2. Email Address
• **Email Address**: Enter your email address for contact and notification purposes.
### 3. Phone Number
• **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact and verification purposes.
### 4. Mailing Address
• **Mailing Address**: Enter your street address for mailing and communication purposes.
### 5. City - State - Zip
• **City - State - Zip**: Enter your city, state, and zip code for address verification.
### 6. Boat Hull Number
• **Boat Hull Number**: Enter your boat's hull number.
### 7. Current Registration Number
• **Current Registration Number**: Enter your current registration number.
### 8. Boat Name
• **Boat Name**: Enter your boat's name.
### 9. Boat Manufacturer
• **Boat Manufacturer**: Enter your boat's manufacturer.
### 10. Boat Model
• **Boat Model**: Enter your boat's model.
### 11. Year of Manufacture
• **Year of Manufacture**: Enter the year your boat was manufactured.
### 12. Boat Length in Feet
• **Boat Length in Feet**: Enter the length of your boat in feet.
### 13. Type of Boat
• **Type of Boat**: Select the type of boat you own.
### 14. Engine Type
• **Engine Type**: Select the type of engine your boat has.
### 15. Current Registration Expiration Date
• **Current Registration Expiration Date**: Enter the current registration expiration date.
### 16. Renewal Period
• **Renewal Period**: Select the renewal period you wish to register for.
### 17. Vessel Use Classification
• **Vessel Use Classification**: Select the classification of use for your boat.
### 18. Moorage or Dock Location
• **Moorage or Dock Location**: Enter the location of your boat's moorage or dock.
### 19. Registration Fee
• **Registration Fee**: Enter the payment amount for the registration renewal.
### 20. Payment Method
• **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you wish to use.
### 21. Cardholder Name
• **Cardholder Name**: Enter the name of the cardholder if paying by card.
### 22. Card Number
• **Card Number**: Enter the last four digits of your card number.
### 23. Card Expiration Date
• **Card Expiration Date**: Enter the expiration date of your card in MM/YY format.
### 24. CVV Code
• **CVV Code**: Enter the three-digit security code on your card.
### 25. I Accept the Terms and Conditions
• **I Accept the Terms and Conditions**: Confirm your acceptance of the terms and conditions.
