# Zero Waste Initiative Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow individuals to make a donation to the Zero Waste Initiative. Please fill out the form with your information and preferences to support this community-driven project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your personal details:
	* First and last name
	* Email address
	* Phone number (optional)
2. Specify your donation details:
	* The amount you would like to donate
	* Whether this is a one-time or recurring donation (e.g. monthly or annually)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): This is your personal name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): This is your surname.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address to receive updates and confirmations about your donation.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number to be contacted about your donation or for any other purpose.
* **Donation Amount** (`amount`, `number`, required/optional): Specify the amount you would like to donate.
* **Donation Frequency** (`frequency`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose how often you would like to make this donation. Options include:
	+ One-time: A single, one-off donation
	+ Recurring: A recurring donation that will be processed at regular intervals
	+ Monthly: A donation processed monthly
	+ Annually: A donation processed once a year

## Tips
* Please ensure that your email address is up to date and active to receive any updates or confirmations about your donation.
* You can choose to make a one-time or recurring donation, depending on your preference.
* You can adjust the donation amount to suit your financial capabilities.
