# health_insurance_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for health insurance orders, including client details, policy status, payment terms, and other related information. 
The form is intended to capture all necessary details required for processing health insurance policies, ensuring accurate and efficient handling of client data.
</thinking>

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Enter your Client Name.
2. Provide your Date of Birth.
3. Enter your Phone Number.
4. Provide your Email Address.
5. Select your Health Insurance Plan.
6. Indicate if you have a Policy Holder.
7. Choose your Policy Status.
8. Enter your Address.
9. Select the Application Date.
10. Enter your Policy Number.
11. Specify your Payment Terms.
12. Provide your Insurance Provider.
13. Indicate the Application Status.
14. Enter your Coverage Start Date.
15. Enter your Coverage End Date.
16. Select the Payment Method.
17. Select your Payment Status.
18. Enter your Payment Frequency.
19. Enter the Payment Amount.
20. Select your Payment Term.
21. Specify your Policy Holder Relationship.
22. Enter the Coverage Start Date 2.
23. Enter the Coverage End Date 2.
24. Select your Payment Status 2.
25. Enter your Client Address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (1, text, required: false): Enter the name of the client.
* **Date of Birth** (2, date, required: false): Enter the date of birth of the client.
* **Phone Number** (3, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the client.
* **Email Address** (4, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the client.
* **Health Insurance Plan** (5, select_one, required: false): Select the type of health insurance plan you are applying for.
* **Policy Holder** (6, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate if you have a policy holder.
* **Policy Status** (7, select_one, required: false): Choose the status of your policy.
* **Address** (8, text, required: false): Enter the address of the client.
* **Application Date** (9, date, required: false): Select the application date.
* **Policy Number** (10, text, required: false): Enter the policy number.
* **Payment Terms** (11, text, required: false): Specify your payment terms.
* **Insurance Provider** (12, text, required: false): Provide the name of the insurance provider.
* **Application Status** (13, select_one, required: false): Indicate the status of your application.
* **Coverage Start Date** (14, date, required: false): Enter the start date of your coverage.
* **Coverage End Date** (15, date, required: false): Enter the end date of your coverage.
* **Payment Method** (16, select_one, required: false): Choose the payment method.
* **Payment Status** (17, select_one, required: false): Select your payment status.
* **Payment Frequency** (18, text, required: false): Enter the payment frequency.
* **Payment Amount** (19, number, required: false): Enter the payment amount.
* **Payment Term** (20, select_one, required: false): Select the payment term.
* **Policy Holder Relationship** (21, select_one, required: false): Specify your relationship to the policy holder.
* **Coverage Start Date 2** (22, date, required: false): Enter the start date of your coverage 2.
* **Coverage End Date 2** (23, date, required: false): Enter the end date of your coverage 2.
* **Payment Status 2** (24, select_one, required: false): Select your payment status 2.
* **Client Address** (25, text, required: false): Enter the address of the client.
