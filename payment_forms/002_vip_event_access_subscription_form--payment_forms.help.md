# vip_event_access_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vip_event_access_subscription_form is used to capture information from event organizers and participants about their willingness to pay for accessing a VIP event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Determine whether the event has a payment requirement by answering the "payment_type" question.
2.  If the event has a payment requirement, fill out the necessary fields with your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **payment_type** (select_one, required: false):
    *   This field asks whether the event has a payment requirement.
    *   Select "Yes" if the event requires payment, and "No" if it does not.
*   **name** (text, required: false):
    *   Enter your name as the event organizer or participant.
*   **email** (email, required: false):
    *   Enter your email address.
*   **phone** (text, required: false):
    *   Enter your phone number.
*   **event_start_date** (date, required: false):
    *   Enter the date and time of the event.
*   **note** (note, required: false):
    *   This field is for any additional information or notes about the event.
*   **event_organizer_name** (text, required: false):
    *   Enter the name of the event organizer.
*   **payment_amount** (number, required: false):
    *   Enter the payment amount for accessing the VIP event.

## Tips
*   Please be accurate when filling out the form to ensure that we have the correct information for the event.
*   If you have already filled out a similar form, you will not be able to fill out this form again to avoid duplication.
