# BlueSnap Cake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers order a cake for a specific occasion. It captures essential information such as customer details, cake preferences, and payment method to ensure accurate order processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of occasion for which you're ordering the cake (e.g., Birthday, Wedding, etc.).
2. Enter your full name as it appears on your identification or passport.
3. Provide your email address so we can get in touch with you for delivery and payment confirmation.
4. Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
5. Enter your delivery address for us to deliver the cake on the chosen date.
6. Choose the number of servings for your cake.
7. Select the flavor of cake you prefer (e.g., Vanilla, Chocolate, etc.).
8. Choose the type of frosting you prefer (e.g., Buttercream, Cream cheese, etc.).
9. Enter the flavor of frosting you prefer (e.g., plain, strawberry, etc.).
10. If applicable, describe any special design or theme you'd like for your cake.
11. If you have any dietary restrictions (e.g., gluten-free, vegan, etc.), let us know.
12. Enter the price per serving for your cake.
13. Enter the total cost of your order.
14. Enter the deposit amount for your order.
15. Select your preferred payment method (e.g., Credit Card, Debit Card, etc.).
16. If you have any special instructions or requests, enter them here.
17. Finally, enter the date you'd like to place your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Order Your Cake --** (`1`, `note`, not required): A welcome note to get you started.
* **Full Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification or passport.
* **Email Address** (`3`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
* **Delivery Address** (`5`, `text`, required): Enter the delivery address where you'd like to receive your cake.
* **Occasion** (`6`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of occasion for which you're ordering the cake (e.g., Birthday, Wedding, etc.).
* **Delivery Date** (`9`, `date`, required): Enter the date you'd like to receive your cake.
* **Number of Servings** (`8`, `select_one`, required): Choose the number of servings for your cake.
* **Cake Flavor** (`9`, `select_one`, required): Select the flavor of cake you prefer (e.g., Vanilla, Chocolate, etc.).
* **Frosting Type** (`10`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of frosting you prefer (e.g., Buttercream, Cream cheese, etc.).
* **Frosting Flavor** (`11`, `text`, required): Enter the flavor of frosting you prefer (e.g., plain, strawberry, etc.).
* **Design/Theme** (`12`, `text`, not required): If you have any special design or theme in mind, describe it here.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`13`, `text`, not required): If you have any dietary restrictions (e.g., gluten-free, vegan, etc.), let us know.
* **Price Per Serving** (`14`, `number`, not required): Enter the price per serving for your cake.
* **Total Cost** (`15`, `number`, not required): Enter the total cost for your order.
* **Deposit Amount** (`16`, `number`, not required): Enter the deposit amount for your order.
* **Payment Method** (`17`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred payment method (e.g., Credit Card, Debit Card, etc.).
* **Special Instructions** (`18`, `text`, not required): If you have any special instructions or requests, enter them here.
* **Order Placement Date** (`19`, `date`, required): Enter the date you'd like to place your order.
