# Gingerbread House Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact and payment information from attendees for the Gingerbread House Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with your relevant information.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and complete details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Fill in your name and email address to help us get in touch with you about the workshop.

### 2. Contact Information (Duplicate)
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): If you want to register another person or have additional contact details, fill in this field.

### 3. Workshop Information
* **Workshop Information** (`workshop_information`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your attendance or preferences for the workshop.

### 4. Payment Information
* **Payment Information** (`payment_information`, text, optional): This field is currently not used for payment collection. Please fill in any additional comments or information for payment purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at <contact email>.
* We look forward to seeing you at the Gingerbread House Workshop!
