## Bank Account Authorization Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients or account holders to authorize payments from their bank account. It's used for recurring and one-time payments, as well as for variable amount payments within specified limits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, you'll need to provide the following information:

1. Client / Company Name: This is the legal name of the account holder.
2. Email Address: This is the email address that will be used for payment notifications and receipts.
3. Phone Number: This is the phone number of the account holder.
4. Name of Financial Institution: This is the name of the bank or financial institution where the account is held.
5. 9-Digit Routing Number (ABA): This is the 9-digit routing number found at the bottom left of the check.
6. Account Number: This is the account number found to the right of the routing number.
7. Bank Account Type: This can be Checking, Savings, Business Checking, or Business Savings.
8. Type of Payment Authorization: This is the type of payment authorization required, which can be Recurring Payments (Automatic), One-Time Payment, or Variable Amount (Within Limits).
9. Payment Frequency: If you've chosen Recurring Payments, select the payment frequency (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
10. Payment Amount or Maximum Limit: Specify the exact amount or the maximum limit for variable payments.
11. Authorization Effective Date: This is the date when payments should begin.
12. I Authorize ACH Debits: Confirm that you authorize the business to initiate ACH debit entries to your account.
13. I Understand UnAuthorized Debites: Confirm that you understand you have the right to dispute unauthorized debits.
14. Cancellation Notice Requirement: Confirm that you understand you must provide written notice 10 days before cancellation.
15. Date of Consent: This is the date of submission of the consent form.
