# Fundraiser For Elementary School Pta - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information and details for a fundraising campaign for an elementary school Parent-Teacher Association (PTA). The purpose of this form is to collect essential information from users, including contact details, fundraiser details, donation information, and any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as you see fit.
2. If you're providing contact information, ensure it's up-to-date and accurate.
3. Enter any relevant details about the fundraiser, such as its purpose and goals.
4. Provide any additional donation information, such as the amount and frequency of donations.
5. Add any relevant notes or comments about the fundraiser.
6. Review and confirm the provided information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **base_info**: A general text field for entering basic information about the fundraiser.
* **contact_info**: Enter your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Fundraiser Info**: Enter details about the fundraiser, such as its purpose and goals.
* **Donation Info**: Enter any additional details about donations, such as the amount and frequency.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the fundraiser.
* **Confirm Info**: Confirm the provided information before submitting the form.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
- Double-check your contact information for any errors.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
