# Credit Card Dispute Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you file a credit card dispute claim with your bank or credit card issuer. It's essential to provide accurate and detailed information to facilitate a smooth resolution process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select your credit card type from the "Payment method" dropdown.
2. Enter your billing address accurately to ensure we can identify you correctly.
3. Choose the date you became aware of the dispute issue.
4. Provide the exact amount disputed.
5. Explain the dispute in detail in the "Dispute description" field.
6. If relevant, upload any supporting documents to help us process your claim.
7. Include any relevant merchant information to help us verify the transaction.
8. Sign and date the form to acknowledge your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment method** (select_one, required): Select the type of credit card you are disputing. Choose one of the available options (e.g., VISA, Mastercard, American Express, or Discover).
* **Billing address** (text, required): Enter your complete billing address as it appears on your credit card statement.
* **Date of dispute** (date, required): Specify the exact date when you first noticed the issue with your credit card payment.
* **Amount of dispute** (number, required): Enter the exact amount of the disputed transaction.
* **Dispute description** (text, required): Describe the dispute in detail, including any relevant dates and transactions.
* **Supporting documents** (note, optional): Attach any supporting documents or evidence that can help resolve the dispute.
* **Merchant information** (text, required): Provide any relevant merchant contact information or details to help us verify the transaction.
* **Customer signature** (text, required): Sign the form to acknowledge your claim and confirm you are the account holder.
* **Employee signature** (text, optional): If applicable, sign the form as an employee or representative of the merchant.
* **Dispute resolution** (note, optional): Use this field to provide any additional information or comments about the dispute resolution process.
