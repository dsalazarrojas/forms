# Tasting Experience Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant information for tastings, including participant name, email, and phone number. Participants can also provide additional details such as participant notes and comments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your participant name.
2. Enter your participant email address.
3. Enter your participant phone number.
4. Select the participant type of your tasting experience (e.g., Tasting Experience, Private Tasting, Virtual Tasting).
5. Select the payment method used for your purchase (e.g., Credit Card, PayPal, Stripe).
6. If applicable, enter your payment card number.
7. Provide any additional participant notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Name
* Participant Name: **participant_name** (Text) - Your name as a participant in the tasting experience.

### Participant Email
* Participant Email: **participant_email** (Email) - Your email address where we can contact you.

### Participant Phone
* Participant Phone: **participant_phone** (Text) - Your phone number where we can reach you.

### Participant Date
* Participant Date: **participant_date** (Date) - Not required for this field, but may be used in conjunction with participant_time for scheduling purposes.

### Participant Time
* Participant Time: **participant_time** (Time) - Not required for this field.

### Participant Tasting Time
* Participant Tasting Time: **participant_tasting_time** (Time) - Not required for this field.

### Participant Experience Type
* Participant Experience Type: **participant_experience_type** (Select One) - The type of tasting experience you are registering for (e.g., Tasting Experience, Private Tasting, Virtual Tasting).

### Payment Method
* Payment Method: **payment_method** (Select Multiple) - The payment method used for your purchase (e.g., Credit Card, PayPal, Stripe).

### Payment Card Number
* Payment Card Number: **payment_card** (Text) - Only required if payment method is credit card.

### Participant Notes
* Participant Notes: **participant_note** (Note) - Additional details about your participant experience.

### Participant Tasting Notes
* Participant Tasting Notes: **participant_tasting_notes** (Note) - Additional details about your tasting experience.

### Participant Experience Notes
* Participant Experience Notes: **participant_experience_notes** (Note) - Additional details about your overall experience.

### Participant Additional Comments
* Participant Additional Comments: **participant_additional_comments** (Note) - Additional comments or feedback about your experience.

### Participant Tasting Preferences
* Participant Tasting Preferences: **participant_tasting_preferences** (Note) - Your preferences for the tasting experience.

### Participant Dietary Restrictions
* Participant Dietary Restrictions: **participant_dietary_restrictions** (Note) - Any dietary restrictions you may have.

### Participant Experience Preferences
* Participant Experience Preferences: **participant_experience_preferences** (Note) - Additional details about your experience preferences.
