# Payment Receipt - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to record and verify payments made to an individual or organization. This will help ensure accurate and organized payment records, providing a clear financial transaction history.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all the required fields (marked as `required`) as they are essential for accurate payment records.
2. Enter the Customer Name, which is the recipient of the payment.
3. Enter the Date of the payment.
4. Enter the amount paid, also known as the Payment Amount.
5. Enter the unique Receipt Number assigned to this payment.
6. Provide a Reason for Payment, which can be a brief description of the payment's purpose.
7. Enter the Receiver Name if the payment was made to an individual.
8. Select the method of payment used (Cash, Bank Transfer, Credit Card, or Other).
9. Add any additional Notes about the payment.
10. Enter the Email Address of the recipient (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the person or organization receiving the payment.
* **Date** (date, required): Enter the date the payment was made.
* **Payment Amount** (number, required): Enter the total amount paid.
* **Receipt Number** (number, required): Enter a unique identifier for this payment record.
* **Reason for Payment** (text, optional): Provide a brief description of the payment's purpose.
* **Receiver Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the individual or organization receiving the payment.
* **Payment Method** (select_one, optional): Select the method used for the payment (Cash, Bank Transfer, Credit Card, or Other).
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or details about the payment.
* **Email Address** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the recipient (if applicable).

## Tips
* Always ensure that all required fields are filled out for accurate payment records.
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the "Reason for Payment" and "Notes" fields to provide a clear understanding of the payment's purpose.
* Double-check the "Payment Amount" and "Receipt Number" to ensure accuracy.
* If the "Payment Method" is "Other", provide a brief explanation of the method used.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all necessary information is included.
