# Executive Training Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Executive Training Check Out Form is designed to collect information from participants about their attendance and payment details for executive training sessions. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure the correct processing of your payment and attendance records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the payment method and amount according to your chosen payment option.
3. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name (participant_name)**: Enter your full name as it should be listed on your official documents.
* **Participant Email (participant_email)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Participant Phone (participant_phone)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Participant Address (participant_address)**: Enter your address where you can be reached.
* **Participant City (participant_city)**: Enter your city or town where you are located.
* **Participant State (participant_state)**: Enter your state or province where you are located.
* **Participant Zip (participant_zip)**: Enter your zip code or postal code where you are located.
* **Training Date (training_date)**: Enter the date of the executive training session you attended.
* **Training Time (training_time)**: Enter the time of the executive training session you attended.
* **Payment Method (payment_method)**: Select the payment method you would like to use for this transaction.
* **Payment Amount (payment_amount)**: Enter the total payment amount for this transaction.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out to avoid errors.
* If you experience any issues while filling out the form, contact the support team for assistance.
