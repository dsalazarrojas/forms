# gourmet_food_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers who want to place an order for gourmet food. Please fill out the form below to provide us with the necessary information to process your order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the "Order Details" page and fill in the order details required.
2. Next, go to the "Customer Info" page and provide your name, email address, and any other relevant details about yourself.
3. On the "Payment Info" page, enter your payment details so we can process your order securely.
4. Finally, review and confirm your order on the "Order Confirmation" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Order Details
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): 
    Provide the type of food you're interested in ordering, and any other details about the order, such as quantity or special requests.
    
### Customer Info
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): 
    This is a field to enter your name, email, and any other relevant details about yourself so we can get in touch with you about your order.
    
### Payment Info
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, optional): 
    Enter your payment method information, such as card details or other payment options you have chosen.
    
### Order Confirmation
* **Order Confirmation** (`order_confirmation`, text, optional): 
    Review and confirm your order to make sure everything is correct and complete before submitting the form.
