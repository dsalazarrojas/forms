# Collections Management Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to manage and track collections data for customers with outstanding invoices. It provides a structured and organized way to gather information about a customer's account, including their contact history, payment behavior, and dispute status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), with the relevant customer information.
2. Select the appropriate payment terms and credit status from the dropdown menus.
3. Enter any additional notes or comments in the text fields.
4. Review and update the form regularly to reflect changes in the collection status and follow-up dates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Account Manager Name** (\*: text): Enter the name of the account manager assigned to this customer.
- **Account Manager Email** (\*: email): Enter the email address of the account manager.
- **Management Date** (\*: date): Enter the date the collection process began.
- **Customer Name** (\*): Enter the name of the customer.
- **Customer ID**: Enter the customer's account number.
- **Customer Type** (\*): Select the type of customer (Individual, Small Business, Corporation, Government Entity, Non-Profit).
- **Customer Address**: Enter the customer's current address.
- **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's contact number.
- **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
- **Invoice Numbers**: Enter all outstanding invoices for this customer.
- **Invoice Dates**: Enter the date range for the outstanding invoices.
- **Total Amount Due**: Enter the combined balance of all outstanding invoices.
- **Amount Past Due**: Enter the amount that is past due.
- **Amount Current**: Enter the amount not yet due.
- **Oldest Invoice Date**: Enter the date of the oldest outstanding invoice.
- **Original Payment Terms**: Select the agreed-upon payment terms (Net 10, Net 15, Net 30, etc.).
- **Credit Limit**: Enter the approved credit limit for this customer.
- **Credit Status**: Select the current credit status (Good Standing, At Risk, On Hold, etc.).
- **Payment History**: Select the customer's payment behavior (Always Pays On Time, Usually Pays On Time, etc.).
- **Previous Delinquencies**: Enter the number of past due incidents.
- **Contact Attempts Made**: Enter the number of contact attempts made.
- **Last Contact Date**: Enter the date of the most recent contact.
- **Last Contact Method**: Select the method used to contact the customer (Phone Call, Email, Letter, etc.).
- **Customer Response**: Enter any additional comments or notes from the customer.
- **Payment Commitment**: Enter any payment commitments or promises made by the customer.
- **Promised Payment Date**: Enter the date promised by the customer to pay.
- **Promised Payment Amount**: Enter the amount promised to pay.
- **Dispute Status**: Select the status of any dispute (No Dispute, Dispute Under Review, etc.).
- **Dispute Reason**: Enter any reason for the dispute.
- **Collection Status**: Select the current collection status (Internal Collections, External Agency, etc.).
- **Action Plan**: Enter the next steps for collection.
- **Follow-Up Date**: Enter the date to follow up on the collection status.
- **Risk Level**: Select the collection risk level (Low Risk, Moderate Risk, etc.).
- **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional information or notes about the customer account.
