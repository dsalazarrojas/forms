# Holiday Gift Box Preorder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Gift Box Preorder Form is designed to collect information from customers who want to preorder a gift box for a loved one. Filling out this form accurately will help us process your order correctly and ensure timely delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form in a new tab or window for easy reference and to avoid losing your progress.
- Make sure to complete all required fields (marked as "Required" in the form).
- If you need help or have questions, you can contact our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, Optional): Enter the shipping address where you'd like the gift box to be sent. This is the address we will use to deliver the gift box.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the person receiving the gift box. This is the person who will be receiving the gift box and will help us personalize it accordingly.
* **Gift Box Description** (`gift_box_description`, text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the gift box, so we can tailor the gift accordingly.
* **Gift Box Price** (`gift_box_price`, number, Optional): Enter the price of the gift box if you'd like to specify it. This will help us process your order correctly.
