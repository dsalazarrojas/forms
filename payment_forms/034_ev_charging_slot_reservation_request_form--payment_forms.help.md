# ev_charging_slot_reservation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to reserve a charging slot for an electric vehicle. Please fill out the form with your details to ensure a successful reservation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Select a reservation date for your charging slot.
5. Indicate whether you want to reserve a time slot for charging.
6. Specify the model of your electric vehicle.
7. Select the type of vehicle you are driving.
8. Optionally, provide any additional notes about your reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name** (Text, required): Enter your first name for reference purposes.
* **email** (Email, required): Enter your email address for communication.
* **phone** (Text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **reservation_date** (Date, required): Select the date you would like to reserve the charging slot.
* **time_slot** (Select One, optional): Choose 'Yes' to reserve a specific time slot, or 'No' to charge freely without a specific time slot.
* **charging_point** (Select Multiple, optional): Select as many charging points as you need for your vehicle to be charged.
* **vehicle_model** (Text, optional): Enter your vehicle's model for reference purposes.
* **vehicle_type** (Select One, optional): Choose the type of vehicle you are driving.
* **notes** (Note, optional): Add any additional information about your reservation.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate information to ensure successful reservation.
* If you need to cancel or modify your reservation, contact us at [support email].
* Only one reservation can be made per vehicle per day.
* Time slots are subject to availability and might be subject to change.
* Multiple vehicle types may be allowed at the same charging point.
