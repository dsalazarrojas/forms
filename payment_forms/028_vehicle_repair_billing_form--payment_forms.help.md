# Vehicle Repair Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to process vehicle repair services and generate payments for clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide vehicle details, such as the vehicle make, model, and year.
2. Enter the repair details, including the repair order and repair date/time.
3. Specify the parts needed for the repair.
4. Enter the total labor hours and labor rate.
5. Calculate the subtotal.
6. Enter the tax rate and calculate the tax amount.
7. Enter the total amount due.
8. Select the payment method (e.g., Stripe, Cash, or Check).
9. Enter any additional payment notes.
10. Enter the payment date and time.
11. Enter the client's email and phone number.
