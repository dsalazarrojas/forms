# Deposit Schedule Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Deposit Schedule Agreement Form is used to establish a payment schedule between a client and a provider. This form collects essential information to ensure both parties are aware of payment terms, amounts, and frequencies.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Client Name** in the designated field. This is a required field and should match the name of the individual or organization entering into this agreement.
2. Select a valid **Deposit Date** from the calendar picker. This field is required and should reflect the date on which the deposit is scheduled to be made.
3. Enter the **Payment Timing** details in the text field. This field should include the time the payment is due, and any additional notes or conditions.
4. Choose either **Yes** or **No** for **Payment Frequency**. This field indicates whether the payment will be made on a recurring basis or as a one-time payment.
5. Enter the **Payment Amount** in the corresponding field. This field is required and should reflect the amount of the deposit.
6. Select either **Yes** or **No** for **Payment Method**. This field indicates the method by which the payment will be made (e.g., bank transfer, cash).
7. Enter the **Contract Length** in the numeric field. This field is required and should reflect the length of the contract in terms of days or months.
8. Record your **Signature Client** and **Signature Provider** as time-stamped digital signatures.
9. Select either **Yes** or **No** for **Agreement Review**. This field indicates whether a review of the agreement is required before it takes effect.
10. Enter any additional **Notes** you would like to include in the document. This field is optional.
11. Enter the **Client Email** address. This field is required and should match the email address associated with the client.
12. Enter the **Provider Phone** number. This field is required and should be a valid phone number for the provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the client.
* **Deposit Date** (`deposit_date`, date, required): Select the date on which the deposit is scheduled to be made.
* **Payment Timing** (`payment_timing`, text, required): Enter the payment timing details, including the time and any conditions.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose whether the payment is made on a recurring basis or as a one-time payment.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of the deposit.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose the method of payment (e.g., bank transfer, cash).
* **Contract Length** (`contract_length`, number, required): Enter the length of the contract in days or months.
* **Signature Client** (`signature_client`, time, required): Record your digital signature as time-stamped.
* **Signature Provider** (`signature_provider`, time, required): Record the digital signature of the provider as time-stamped.
* **Agreement Review** (`agreement_review`, select_one, required): Indicate whether a review of the agreement is required.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes related to the agreement.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter the email address associated with the client.
* **Provider Phone** (`provider_phone`, text, required): Enter a valid phone number for the provider.
