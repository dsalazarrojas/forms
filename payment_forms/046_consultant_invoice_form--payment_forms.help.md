# Consultant Invoice Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects consultant and billing information from clients, including their identification details, billing period, service line items, and payment instructions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Consultant Full Name or Legal Entity**: Enter the full name or legal entity name of the consultant or client.
2.  **Consultant Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the consultant or client.
3.  **Invoice Reference Number**: Enter the internal reference number for the invoice.
4.  **Client Organization Name**: Enter the name of the client organization.
5.  **Client Billing Email**: Enter the email address of the client's billing contact.
6.  **Project Reference/Contract ID**: Enter the reference or contract ID for the project.
7.  **Billing Period Start Date**: Select the start date of the billing period.
8.  **Billing Period End Date**: Select the end date of the billing period.
9.  **Primary Service Description**: Enter a brief description of the primary service provided.
10. **Total Hours Rendered**: Enter the total hours rendered for the service.
11. **Standard Hourly Rate**: Enter the standard hourly rate for the service.
12. **Subtotal Labor Amount**: This field is automatically calculated based on the total hours rendered and standard hourly rate.
13.  **Reimbursable Expenses Amount**: Enter any reimbursable expenses as per the contract terms.
14.  **Total Invoice Amount**: This field is automatically calculated based on the subtotal labor amount and reimbursable expenses amount.
15.  **Preferred Payment Method**: Select the preferred payment method for the invoice.
16.  **Payment Due Date**: Select the date by which payment is due.
17.  **Additional Billing Instructions or Notes**: Enter any additional billing instructions or notes.
18.  **Date of Invoice Submission**: Select the date the invoice was submitted.
