# Exhibitor Booth Upgrade Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to upgrade an exhibitor's booth at an event. It's meant for exhibitors who want to change their initial booth allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2.  Review your submission carefully to ensure accuracy.
3.  Click the "Submit" button to send your upgrade request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exhibitor Info** (`exhibitor_info`): Enter your exhibitor's contact information, including name, email, and any relevant contact details.
* **Upgrade Plan** (`upgrade_plan`): Describe your desired upgrade, including specific details about the new booth location, size, and any special requirements.
* **Add-ons** (`add_ons`): Choose any additional services or amenities you'd like to have included in your upgrade, such as extra storage or internet connectivity.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`): Provide payment details, including method, amount, and any relevant payment terms.
* **Confirm Upgrade** (`confirm_upgrade`): Confirm that you have read and understand the upgrade terms and conditions.
* **Review Submission** (`review_submission`): Review your submission for accuracy and completeness.
* **Submit Button** (`submit_button`): Click to submit your upgrade request.
* **Add-ons 1-5** (`add_ons_1-5`): Select any additional services or amenities you'd like to include with your upgrade.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`): Review and agree to the payment terms and conditions.
* **Review Submission 1-3** (`review_submission_1-3`): Review your submission for accuracy and completeness.
* **Submit Button 1-2** (`submit_button_1-2`): Click to submit your review and completion.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select the method of payment (e.g., bank transfer, credit card).
* **Payment Method 1** (`payment_method_1`): Select the method of payment (e.g., bank transfer, credit card).
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`): Review the status of your payment.

Note: While this form is designed to be user-friendly, if you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to our support team.
