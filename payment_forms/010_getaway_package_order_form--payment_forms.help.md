# Getaway Package Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow customers to order getaways packages by selecting the desired package options, providing payment details, and tracking the status of their packages.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Select the type of getaways you are interested in** (`getaways`, `Yes/No`): Select the type of getaways you would like to order from the options provided.
2. **Select the package options you would like to purchase** (`package_selection`, `Yes/No`): Choose the package options you would like to include in your order.
3. **Provide payment details** (`payments`, `Yes/No`): Enter your payment details, including the amount and method of payment.
4. **Enter package details** (`package_details`, `text`): Enter any additional information about the package you are ordering.
5. **Provide travel dates** (`travel_dates`, `date`): Enter the travel dates for your getaways package.
6. **Enter customer details** (`customer_details`, `text`): Enter your contact information and any other relevant customer details.
7. **Enter travel agency information** (`travel_agency`, `text`): If applicable, enter the name and contact information of your travel agency.
8. **Enter package price** (`package_price`, `number`): Enter the price of the package you are ordering.
9. **Enter total cost** (`total_cost`, `number`): This field is automatically calculated based on your selections.
10. **Track payment status** (`payment_status`, `Active/Inactive`): View the status of your payment.
11. **Track package status** (`package_status`, `Active/Inactive`): View the status of your package.
12. **Enter comments** (`comments`, `text`): Add any additional comments about your order.
13. **Enter order number** (`order_number`, `text`): This field will be populated automatically.
14. **Enter second payment method** (`payment_method_2`, `Yes/No`): Select whether you have a second method of payment.
15. **Enter second travel agency information** (`travel_agency_2`, `text`): If applicable, enter the name and contact information of your second travel agency.
16. **Enter second agency details** (`travel_agency_details`, `text`): Enter any additional details about your second travel agency.
17. **Enter second payment amount** (`payment_amount_2`, `number`): Enter the amount for your second payment method.
18. **Enter order date** (`order_date`, `date`): Enter the date for your order.
19. **Select package type** (`package_type`, `Yes/No`): Select the type of package you are ordering.
20. **Enter package type details** (`package_type_details`, `text`): Enter any additional information about the package type.
21. **Enter selection 2** (`package_selection_2`, `Yes/No`): Choose the package options for your second selection.
22. **Select multiple package selections** (`package_selections`, `Yes/No`): Select multiple packages for your order.
23. **Track second payment status** (`payment_status_2`, `Active/Inactive`): View the status of your second payment.
24. **Track second package status** (`package_status_2`, `Active/Inactive`): View the status of your second package.
