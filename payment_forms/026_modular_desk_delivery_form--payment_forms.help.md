# Modular Desk Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Modular Desk Delivery Form is used to collect information from customers or end-users for the delivery process of a modular desk. This form likely helps the delivery team to understand the order details, delivery information, shipping address, customer information, payment details, and any other relevant details for a smooth delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Go through each page of the form, answering the questions in the order they appear.
2. For each page, answer the questions according to the information you have about your order.
3. Be sure to fill in all the required fields (indicated by a *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Order Details
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): This field is used to enter any additional information about the order you would like to include. Please be brief and provide any relevant details about the order.
### Page 2: Delivery Information
* **Delivery Information** (`delivery_info`, text, optional): This field is used to provide information about how you would like to receive your modular desk, such as any special instructions or details.
### Page 3: Shipping Address
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter the shipping address where you would like to receive your modular desk. This includes the street address, city, state, and zip code.
### Page 4: Customer Info
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): This field is used to provide any additional information about yourself or the person receiving the modular desk.
### Page 5: Payment Info
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, optional): This field is used to enter any payment details or method you would like to use for the payment.
### Page 6: Order Total
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, select_multiple, required): Please select whether the order is for "Yes" or "No".
### Page 7: Delivery Date
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Enter the desired delivery date for your modular desk.
### Page 8: Delivery Time
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, optional): Enter the desired delivery time for your modular desk.
### Page 9: Customer Email
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you with any updates about your order.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will review your form submission and get back to you with any updates or instructions.
