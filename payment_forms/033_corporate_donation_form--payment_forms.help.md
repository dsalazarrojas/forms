# Corporate Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from corporate donors about their donation preferences, including frequency of donations, payment method, and other relevant details. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the preferred donation frequency from the options provided (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually). 
2. Enter the preferred payment method (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Cash). 
3. Enter the donation amount (in numbers). 
4. Enter the corporate name, address, and contact information (employee name, employee email, and employee phone number). 
5. Indicate whether the donation is tax-deductible or not. 
6. Enter any additional notes or comments. 
7. Select the preferred method of corporate donation (Online or In Person).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Amount** (donation_amount, number, optional): Enter the amount to be donated. 
* **Donation Frequency** (donation_frequency, select_one, optional): Select the frequency of the donation (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually). 
* **Frequency Description** (frequency_description, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the donation frequency. 
* **Frequency Unit** (frequency_unit, select_multiple, optional): Select the unit of the donation frequency (months, quarters, years). 
* **Donation Frequency Start Date** (donation_frequency_start_date, date, optional): Enter the date when the donation frequency starts. 
* **Payment Method** (payment_method, select_multiple, optional): Select the preferred payment method (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Cash). 
* **Corporate Name** (corporate_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the corporate entity making the donation. 
* **Corporate Address** (corporate_address, text, optional): Enter the address of the corporate entity making the donation. 
* **Employee Name** (employee_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee making the donation. 
* **Employee Email** (employee_email, email, optional): Enter the email of the employee making the donation. 
* **Employee Phone** (employee_phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the employee making the donation. 
* **Corporate Donation Amount** (corporate_donation_amount, number, optional): Enter the amount of the corporate donation. 
* **Tax Deductible** (tax_deductible, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether the donation is tax-deductible or not. 
* **Notes** (notes, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the donation. 
* **Corporate Donation Method** (corporate_donation_method, select_multiple, optional): Select the preferred method of corporate donation (Online or In Person). 
* **Corporate Donation Frequency Unit** (corporate_donation_frequency_unit, select_multiple, optional): Select the unit of the corporate donation frequency (months, quarters, years). 

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have the necessary authority and approval from the corporate management before filling out this form. 
* Double-check the information for accuracy before submitting the form. 
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact us for help.
