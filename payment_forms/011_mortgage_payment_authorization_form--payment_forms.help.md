# Mortgage Payment Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mortgage Payment Authorization Form is used to provide accurate and secure information for the payment process. It's essential to fill out the form accurately to avoid any issues with payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your borrower information: Enter your name and email address.
2. Set the authorization amount: Enter the amount to be authorized.
3. Choose the authorization method: Select one of the available methods (ACH, Wire Transfer, or Other).
4. Provide bank details: Enter your bank name, branch name, routing number, and account number.
5. Specify account type: Select the type of account (Checking, Savings, or Other).
6. (Optional) Add an authorization note: Provide any additional comments or notes about the authorization.
7. Review and submit: Review all the information before submitting the form.
