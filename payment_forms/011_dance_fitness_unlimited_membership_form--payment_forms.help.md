# Dance Fitness Unlimited Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dance Fitness Unlimited Membership Form is used to manage membership plans and payment information for the dance fitness program. This form helps users create, edit, or manage existing memberships, track payments, and update payment status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your plan options on the first page, which includes plan selection and plan type.
2. Provide your personal details, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. Add your signature, if required.
4. Confirm your online payment preference and select the payment method.
5. Choose your desired payment status.
6. Click the "Submit" button to complete your membership registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Plan selection** (`plan_selection`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the membership plan that suits your needs. Select one or more plans from the available options.
- **Plan** (`plan`, select_one, required: false): Confirm your selected plan type.
- **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name as you would like it to appear in our records.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Signature** (`signature`, note, required: false): If required, add your handwritten signature or scanned copy of your signature.
- **Online Payment** (`online_payment`, select_one, required: false): Confirm if you want to pay online.
- **Amount** (`amount`, number, required: false): Enter the membership amount you are willing to pay.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose your preferred payment method (e.g., credit card, bank transfer).
- **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required: false): Select your current payment status (Active or Inactive).
- **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required: false): Confirm your submission of the membership application.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to avoid any issues with your membership registration.
- Double-check your plan selection and preferences before submitting the form.
- If you encounter any issues with your payment, contact our support team for assistance.
