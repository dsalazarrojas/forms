# Child Support Payment Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Child Support Payment Declaration Form is a document used to declare that child support payments have been made. It helps track and record payment history for accurate financial records and to prevent miscommunication.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are completed before submitting.
- Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Parent Making Payment (Payer Name)** (`payer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the parent making the payment.
* **Name of Parent Receiving Payment (Payee Name)** (`payee_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the parent receiving the payment.
* **Total Amount Paid** (`amount_paid`, `number`, required): Enter the exact amount paid in a numeric currency value.
* **Date of Payment** (`payment_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the funds were sent.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method_select`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following payment methods: Cash Payment, Personal or Cashier Check, Bank Transfer (ACH), or Money Order.
* **Payment Period Start** (`period_start`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of the period covered by the payment.
* **Payment Period End** (`period_end`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of the period covered by the payment.
* **I Declare That This Payment Was Made In Full** (`payment_declaration_ack`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following: Yes - Confirmed or No - Partial Payment.
* **Additional Notes** (`payment_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional details regarding the transfer or receipts.
* **Date of Declaration** (`declaration_date`, `date`, required): Enter the current date.

Please submit the form once you have completed all required fields to ensure accurate and efficient declaration of child support payments.
