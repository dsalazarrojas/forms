# Smart Kitchen Appliance Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Kitchen Appliance Order Form is designed to collect user information and order specifics for a smart kitchen appliance order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Information section with your details.
2. Provide your order details in the Order Details section.
3. Summarize your order in the Order Summary section.
4. Enter your payment information in the Payment Information section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your name and contact information for the order.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe the specific smart kitchen appliance you are ordering, including model number if applicable.
* **Order Summary** (`order_summary`, text, required: false):
	+ Summarize your order, including any special requests or instructions.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your payment method and relevant payment details.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields, if any.
- Double-check your order details for accuracy.
- Review your summary for completeness.
- Verify your payment information for correctness.
