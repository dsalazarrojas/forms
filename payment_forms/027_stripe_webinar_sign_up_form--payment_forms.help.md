# Stripe Webinar Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stripe Webinar Sign Up Form is designed to collect user information for a Stripe Webinar, allowing us to manage the event effectively and follow-up with attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title to give your presentation a clear name.
2. Choose the category of the webinar from the options provided.
3. Provide a brief description of the webinar.
4. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
5. Enter your email address for communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title for the webinar, for example, "Stripe Webinar for Beginners".
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, select_one, required): Choose the category of the webinar from the options provided, such as "Payments" or "Web Development".
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the webinar, including its main topics and objectives.
* **Phone** (`form_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes, if you prefer to be reached by phone.
* **Email** (`form_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for communication and follow-up purposes.
* **Submit** (`form_submit`, text, required): Click the "Submit" button to complete the form and register for the webinar.
