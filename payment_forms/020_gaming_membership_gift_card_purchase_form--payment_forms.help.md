# gaming_membership_gift_card_purchase_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to purchase a gift card for a gaming membership. Please fill out the form with the required information to complete the purchase and receive your gift card.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the **Gift Card Name** in the text field.
2. Select the **Type** of gift card (Monthly Membership or One-Time Membership) from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter the **Membership Plan ID** if applicable.
4. Enter the **Price** of the gift card.
5. Enter the **Quantity** of gift cards to purchase.
6. Enter the **Email** address of the recipient.
7. Enter the **Phone** number of the recipient.
8. Enter a **Gift Card Message** for the recipient (optional).
9. Select the **Payment Method** (PayPal or Stripe) from the dropdown menu.
10. Enter the **Card Number**, **Exp Month**, and **Exp Year** for the payment method.
11. Enter the **CCV** and **CVV** for the payment method.
12. Confirm the **Payment Terms** by selecting "yes" from the dropdown menu.
13. Enter the **Delivery Details** in the text field (optional).
14. Select the **Gift Card Status** (active, inactive, or expired) from the dropdown menu.
15. Select the **Payment Status** (pending, paid, or failed) from the dropdown menu.
16. Enter the **Delivery Date** (optional).
17. Enter the **Delivery Address** in the text field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gift Card Name**: Enter the name of the gift card to be purchased.
* **Type**: Select the type of gift card to be purchased (Monthly Membership or One-Time Membership).
* **Membership Plan ID**: Enter the ID of the membership plan associated with the gift card (if applicable).
* **Price**: Enter the price of the gift card.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of gift cards to be purchased.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the recipient.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the recipient.
* **Gift Card Message**: Enter a personalized message for the gift card (optional).
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method to be used (PayPal or Stripe).
* **Card Number**: Enter the card number for the payment method.
* **Exp Month**: Select the expiration month of the payment method.
* **Exp Year**: Select the expiration year of the payment method.
* **CCV**: Enter the Card Security Code (CCV) for the payment method.
* **CVV**: Enter the Card Verification Value (CVV) for the payment method.
* **Payment Terms**: Confirm the payment terms by selecting "yes" from the dropdown menu.
* **Delivery Details**: Enter the delivery details (optional).
* **Gift Card Status**: Select the status of the gift card (active, inactive, or expired).
* **Payment Status**: Select the status of the payment (pending, paid, or failed).
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date of delivery (optional).
* **Delivery Address**: Enter the delivery address in the text field (optional).
