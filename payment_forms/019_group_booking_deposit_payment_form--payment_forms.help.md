# Group Booking Deposit Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect payment information from customers making a group booking deposit. This information will be used to confirm the payment details and update the booking status accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the payment amount due for the group booking.
2. Choose the payment method used (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, or cash).
3. Enter the date the payment was received.
4. Add any additional information regarding the payment in the "Additional Info" field.
5. Select the current payment status (e.g., paid, pending, or cancelled).
6. Provide the customer's email address.
7. Enter any additional details about the customer, such as their name and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Total Amount Due**: Enter the total amount the customer is required to pay for the group booking.
- **Payment Method**: Choose the method used to make the payment, such as credit card, bank transfer, or cash.
- **Date Received**: Enter the date on which the payment was received.
- **Additional Info**: Provide any additional details about the payment, such as the payment reference number or any other relevant information.
- **Payment Status**: Select the current status of the payment, such as paid, pending, or cancelled.
- **Customer E-mail**: Enter the customer's email address, which will be used for communication and updates.
- **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name.
- **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
- **Additional Info**: Enter any other details about the customer, such as their address or other relevant information.

## Tips
* Double-check all the fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
* Ensure that the customer is aware of any required documents or further information needed to complete their booking.
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure accurate and up-to-date payment details.
