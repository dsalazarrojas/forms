# Flower Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for flower delivery. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure your order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the "order_details" field with a brief description of your order.
2. Provide your flower preferences in the "flower_preferences" field.
3. Select a delivery date and time in the "delivery_date" and "delivery_time" fields, respectively.
4. Add a message in the "message" field if you wish to include a personal note.
5. Provide your contact details, including phone number and email address, in the "contact_details" and "email" fields.
6. Select a payment method from the "payment_method" dropdown list.
7. Enter the amount of your order in the "payment_amount" field.
8. Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions by selecting the corresponding option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details** (order_details, text, required): A brief description of your order, such as the type and quantity of flowers you'd like to order.
* **Flower Preferences** (flower_preferences, text, required): Any specific preferences you have for the flowers, such as color or type.
* **Payment Details** (payment_details, text, required): A description of the payment method and amount you'd like to use.
* **Delivery Date** (delivery_date, date, required): The date you'd like to receive your order.
* **Delivery Time** (delivery_time, time, required): The time you'd like to receive your order.
* **Message** (message, text, optional): A personal note or message for the delivery person.
* **Contact Details** (contact_details, text, required): Your phone number and any additional contact information.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Your email address.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required): Your phone number.
* **Payment Method** (payment_method, select_multiple, required): The method you'd like to use for payment (Cash, Credit card, or PayPal).
* **Payment Amount** (payment_amount, number, required): The amount you'd like to pay for your order.
* **Confirm** (confirm, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Agree to Terms and Conditions** (I do not agree to terms and conditions, select_one, optional): Confirm that you do not agree to the terms and conditions (this option is not recommended and may result in order cancellation).

## Tips

* Please double-check your order details and preferences for accuracy.
* Make sure to enter your contact details correctly.
* Choose a suitable payment method and enter the correct amount.
* Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions to ensure order processing.
