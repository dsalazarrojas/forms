# Square Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Square Product Order Form is a tool designed to facilitate the creation and management of orders within the Square platform. It is meant to be a versatile and customizable form that caters to various industries and businesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the **Product Details** section with information about the product being ordered, such as its name, description, and any other relevant details.
*   In the **Customer Info** section, provide essential information about the customer, including their name, contact details, and any specific instructions or requests.
*   The **Payment Details** section is used for adding payment-related information, such as payment method, due date, and any other specific details about the transaction.
*   **Order Details** provides an option to add extra comments or instructions related to the order.
*   You can provide additional **Order Notes** if needed for further context.
*   You can also add any relevant **Customer Notes** that the customer or your team might want to reference later.
*   Finally, select either **Save and Send to Square** or **Cancel** as your action.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **1. Product Details**: This field is meant for you to input the name, description, or any other relevant information about the product or service being ordered. It's a text input field.
*   **2. Customer Info**: In this field, provide the customer's name, contact details, or any other essential information that's required for the order or transaction. It's also a text input field.
*   **3. Payment Details**: This is where you can input payment specifics such as payment method, due date, or other payment-related details. It's a text input field.
*   **4. Order Details**: This field is for adding any extra comments or instructions related to the order. It's a text input field.
*   **5. Order Notes**: You can use this field for any additional information or context that you or your team might need for the order. It's a text input field.
*   **6. Customer Notes**: In this field, you can store any important notes from the customer or your team about the order. It's a text input field.
*   **7. Submit Form**: Here, you can choose to either save and send the form to Square or cancel the order creation process. It's a select multiple field with options to save and send or cancel.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled out before proceeding with the order.
- If you need to make changes to the form, consider using the "Save and Send to Square" option.
- Use customer notes and order notes fields to keep track of important details and references.

This form is designed to be easy to use and allows you to collect all necessary information for creating and managing orders through the Square platform. It's a helpful tool for any business that needs to create and manage orders efficiently.
