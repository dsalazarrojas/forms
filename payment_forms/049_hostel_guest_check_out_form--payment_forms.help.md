# Hostel Guest Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hostel Guest Check Out Form is a comprehensive document used to gather information about a guest's stay and payment details. This form is essential for ensuring accurate records and facilitating smooth check-out processes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by answering the "What did you think of our hostel?" section to provide feedback about your experience.
2. Next, fill in your "Name" and "Contact Number" for identification purposes.
3. For your stay details, enter the "Check In Date and Time" and "Room Type" of your accommodation.
4. If applicable, select the payment method you used, which can be "Cash," "Credit Card," or "Bank Transfer."
5. Enter the "Total Cost" of your stay and the "Amount Paid."
6. If necessary, indicate the "Refund Status" of your payment.
7. Finally, provide your "Comments" and submit the form.
