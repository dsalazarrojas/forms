# mental_health_therapy_reimbursement_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a claim for reimbursement related to mental health therapy. Please fill out this form with accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth and efficient claim processing process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully review each field below to ensure you understand what information is requested.
- Make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name (id: 1)** (`text`, required: false): Please enter your first name in the format "John" (e.g., not "John Doe").
* **Last name (id: 2)** (`text`, required: false): Please enter your last name in the format "Doe" (e.g., not "John Smith Doe").
* **Date of birth (id: 3)** (`date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY" (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Date of claim (id: 4)** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date you are submitting the claim in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Type of claim (id: 5)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select the type of claim that best describes your situation:
	+ Mental health claim
	+ Other claim
	+ Reimbursement claim
* **Claim amount (id: 6)** (`number`, required: false): Enter the total amount you are claiming for reimbursement.
* **Insurance provider name (id: 7)** (`text`, required: false): If you have an insurance provider, please enter their name.
* **Insurance provider address (id: 8)** (`text`, required: false): If applicable, please enter the address of your insurance provider.
* **Therapist name (id: 9)** (`text`, required: false): If applicable, please enter the name of your therapist.
* **Claim status (id: 10)** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the current status of your claim:
	+ Claim submitted
	+ Claim pending
	+ Claim denied
* **Notes (id: 11)** (`note`, required: false): Please enter any additional comments or notes related to your claim.
* **Email (id: 12)** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address so that we can contact you regarding your claim.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out, even if you are unsure of some information.
- Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
