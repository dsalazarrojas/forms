# Fair Trade Product Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information for a fair trade product order, including product specifics, customer details, and payment information. Fill out this form to help us process your order accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form sections sequentially, starting from **Order Details**.
2. Enter your **Customer Information** in the subsequent section.
3. Select your preferred **Payment Method** and enter the corresponding **Payment Amount**.
4. Optionally, provide additional **Customer Notes** for our team to take note of any special instructions or requests.
5. Finally, review your entire order and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details**:
  * **(Required)**: A brief description of the product or service ordered.
* **Customer Information**:
  * **Customer Info**: Enter your name, contact number, and any other details relevant to your order.
* **Customer Email**:
  * **(Optional)**: Enter your email address so we can communicate with you about your order.
* **Customer Phone**:
  * **(Optional)**: Enter your phone number for further contact or updates.
* **Customer Address**:
  * **(Optional)**: Enter your full address for our delivery and communication purposes.
* **Customer Notes**:
  * **(Optional)**: Add any special instructions or notes you'd like our team to be aware of while processing your order.
* **Total Cost**:
  * **(Optional)**: Enter the total cost of your order.
* **Payment Method**:
  * **(Optional)**: Choose between **Cash** and **Bank Transfer** for payment.
* **Payment Amount**:
  * **(Optional)**: Select the amount to be paid for your order.
* **Notes**:
  * **(Optional)**: Choose any additional notes you'd like us to consider while processing your order.

## Tips
- Ensure your contact information is accurate to receive updates and notifications on your order status.
- Please review your order details before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
