# Stem Toy Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stem Toy Pre Order Form is designed to collect information from customers who want to pre-order a toy. This form will help us understand their order requirements and provide a smooth experience for both the customer and our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Customer Details" section with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. In the "Shipping Details" section, provide your shipping address and any additional shipping instructions.
3. In the "Payment Method" section, choose one or more payment methods you are comfortable with (Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal).
4. If you have any specific product information or requests, enter it in the "Product Info" section.
5. Finally, if you have any additional notes about your order, enter it in the "Order Notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Details
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, `text`, required): This section is where you enter your contact information. Please enter your name, email address, and phone number so we can get in touch with you.

### Shipping Details
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, `text`, required): Enter your shipping address in this section. This will help us ensure that your toy is delivered to the right place.

### Payment Method
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose one or more payment methods you prefer. You can select Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal.

### Product Info
* **Product Info** (`product_info`, `text`, optional): If you have any specific information about the product you are ordering, such as a unique gift request or special instructions, enter it here.

### Order Notes
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional notes about your order, such as special requests or instructions, enter them here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to avoid any delays in processing your order.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We appreciate your patience and cooperation in filling out this form accurately and completely.
