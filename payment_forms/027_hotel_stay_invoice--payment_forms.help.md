# Hotel Stay Invoice - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hotel Stay Invoice form is used to collect billing information and payment details for a hotel stay, allowing hotel staff to generate a comprehensive invoice for customers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information before starting.
2. Fill out the form with the required details for the hotel stay.
3. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Billing Details**: Enter the billing address and contact information for the customer's stay. This includes the name, address, and any additional details required for billing purposes.
* **Bill To**: Enter the name and address of the person or company to bill the customer for the stay.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the stay. This will automatically be included in the invoice.
* **Total Amount**: Enter the total amount charged for the stay, including all applicable taxes and fees.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method used for the stay, such as Cash, Credit Card, or Pay Pal.
* **Services Rendered**: Enter any additional services provided during the stay, such as amenities or upgrades.
