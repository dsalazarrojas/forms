# Athletic Conference Gift Box Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Athletic Conference Gift Box Order Form is a tool for athletic conference organizers to order customized gift boxes for their events. This form helps you to select the type of gift box, contents, and other customizations to create a unique gift for your attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Customer Information**: Fill in your name, email, phone number, and organization or school name.
2. **Order Details**: Select the type of gift box, date of order, and event name.
3. **Gift Contents and Customizations**: Choose the items to include in the gift box and select any custom items or branding options.
4. **Shipping and Payment Information**: Select the shipping method, payment method, and provide any special instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Full Name**: Your name and surname.
* **Email Address**: Contact email for order confirmation.
* **Phone Number**: Contact phone number.
* **Organization or School**: Name of the organization or school (if applicable).
* **Order Date**: Date of the order.
* **Athletic Event or Conference**: Name of the event or conference.
* **Event Date**: Date of the event.
* **Gift Box Type**: Choose the type of gift box (Standard, Premium, Deluxe, or Custom).
* **Quantity**: Number of gift boxes to order.
* **Box Contents Selection**: Select which items to include in the gift box.
* **Custom Items Description**: Describe any custom items if selected.
* **Branding Options**: Select the branding preferences (Conference Logo, School Logo, Event Name, Custom Message, or No Branding).
* **Custom Message**: Text for the custom message.
* **Packaging Color**: Preferred color scheme for the packaging.
* **Ribbon Color**: Preferred ribbon color.
* **Gift Message Card**: Choose whether to include a gift message card.
* **Gift Message**: Message to include on the card (if applicable).
* **Price Per Box**: Price in USD for each gift box.
* **Subtotal**: Subtotal before tax and shipping.
* **Shipping Method**: Select the shipping method (Standard Shipping, Express Shipping, Overnight Shipping, Pickup at Event, or Local Delivery).
* **Shipping Cost**: Shipping fee in USD.
* **Delivery Address**: Address to ship the order to.
* **Delivery Instructions**: Special delivery instructions or notes.
* **Tax**: Sales tax if applicable.
* **Total Amount**: Total cost in USD.
* **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, Purchase Order, Check, or Wire Transfer).
* **Purchase Order Number**: Purchase order number if applicable.
* **Special Instructions**: Any special requests or instructions.
* **Rush Order**: If this is a rush order, select 'Yes - Additional Fees Apply'.

Note: All fields are required unless specified otherwise.
