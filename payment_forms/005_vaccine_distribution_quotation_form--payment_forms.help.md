# vaccine_distribution_quotation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vaccine distribution quotation form is designed to facilitate the process of creating and managing vaccine distribution agreements between customers and suppliers. It collects essential information for a smooth and efficient distribution process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Double-check that all fields are filled out correctly before submission.
3. Review the completed form for any errors or inconsistencies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer** (customer, text, false): Enter the customer's name and any other identifying information that is relevant to the agreement.
* **Contact** (contact, text, false): Provide the customer's contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Supplier** (supplier, text, false): Enter the supplier's name and any other relevant details.
* **Quote Date** (quote_date, text, false): Specify the date of the quote or agreement.
* **Quote Time** (quote_time, text, false): Enter the time of the quote or agreement.
* **Vaccine Order** (vaccine_order, text, false): Describe the vaccine order or details of the delivery.
* **Customer Signature** (customer_signature, text, false): Sign and date the agreement as the customer.
* **Supplier Signature** (supplier_signature, text, false): Sign and date the agreement as the supplier.
* **Quote Details** (quote_details, text, false): Provide any additional details or terms of the agreement.
* **Vaccine Info** (vaccine_info, text, false): Enter any relevant vaccine information, such as the type or dosage.
* **Payment Terms** (payment_terms, text, false): Describe the payment terms and conditions.
* **Delivery Info** (delivery_info, text, false): Enter any necessary shipping or delivery information.
* **Customer Notes** (customer_notes, text, false): Add any personal notes or comments from the customer.
* **Payment Methods** (payment_methods, text, false): Specify the payment methods accepted or used for the delivery.
* **Vaccine Dose** (vaccine_dose, text, false): Describe the details of the vaccine dose or delivery.
* **Delivery Method** (delivery_method, text, false): Enter the delivery method used for the vaccine.
* **Shipping Address** (shipping_address, text, false): Provide the customer's shipping or delivery address.
* **Shipping Date** (shipping_date, text, false): Specify the shipping or delivery date.
* **Shipping Time** (shipping_time, text, false): Enter the shipping or delivery time.
* **Shipping Status** (shipping_status, text, false): Track the shipping or delivery status.
* **Vaccine Status** (vaccine_status, text, false): Enter the current status of the vaccine.
* **Customer Phone** (customer_phone, text, false): Enter the customer's contact phone number.
* **Customer Email** (customer_email, text, false): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Vaccine Type** (vaccine_type, text, false): Describe the type of vaccine being delivered.
* **Vaccine Dose 2** (vaccine_dose_2, text, false): Provide any additional vaccine dose information, if applicable.

Note: Since all fields in this form are optional (set to "false" in the YAML file), ensure that you only fill out the fields that are necessary for your specific use case. This will keep the form concise and avoid unnecessary information.

## Tips

* Review each field carefully before submission to ensure accurate and complete information.
* Verify that all required fields are filled out correctly.
* Save a copy of the completed form for your records and for future reference.

By following these guidelines, you can efficiently and effectively use this form to manage vaccine distribution quotations and agreements.
