# Payment Gateway Pricing Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payment Gateway Pricing Inquiry Form is designed to collect information from businesses regarding their payment gateway pricing inquiry. Please fill out this form to provide us with the necessary details to assist you with your inquiry.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting the form, make sure you have all the required information ready. The form is divided into several fields, each with its own specific purpose. Follow the instructions below to complete the form:

1. Enter your business name in the "Business Name" field.
2. Select 'Yes' if you are a payment gateway, 'No' if you are not in the "Payment Gateway Type" field.
3. Enter your business email address in the "Business Email" field.
4. Enter your business phone number in the "Business Phone" field.
5. Enter your business address in the "Business Address" field.
6. Enter your business city in the "Business City" field.
7. Enter the state of your business address in the "Business State" field.
8. Enter the zip code of your business address in the "Business Zip" field.
9. Enter a brief message with your inquiry in the "Message" field.
10. Select your business hours of operation in the "Business Hours" field.
11. (Optional) You can add any additional information or details in the "Message" field.
