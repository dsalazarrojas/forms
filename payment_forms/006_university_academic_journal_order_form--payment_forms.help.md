# University Academic Journal Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Academic Journal Order Form is a tool used by university staff to collect information about journal orders. This form helps streamline the ordering process and ensures that all necessary details are captured.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred delivery options.
2. Enter the name of the journal you are ordering.
3. Provide any additional order details.
4. Enter billing information (if required).
5. Choose the order type.
6. Enter the order date and time (if applicable).
7. Select the order status.
8. Enter the name of the university.
9. Enter the contact person's name.
10. Enter the contact person's email.
11. Enter the contact person's phone number.
12. Enter the journal's address.
13. Enter the journal's city.
14. Enter the journal's state.
15. Enter the journal's postal code.
16. Enter the university's address.
17. Enter the university's city.
18. Enter the university's state.
19. Enter the university's postal code.
20. Enter any additional journal posting information.
21. Add any notes about the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **order_form (Text)** (`university_academic_journal_order_form`, text, required): Enter the journal order form details in this text field.
* **delivery_preferences (Select Multiple)** (`delivery_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred delivery options for the journal order.
* **journal_name (Text)** (`journal_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the journal you are ordering.
* **order_details (Text)** (`order_details`, text, required): Enter any additional details about the order.
* **staff_details (Text)** (`staff_details`, text, optional): Enter any details about the university staff associated with the order.
* **submission_preferences (Select Multiple)** (`submission_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select any submission preferences for the journal order.
* **billing_info (Select One)** (`billing_info`, select_one, optional): Select the billing information for the order.
* **order_type (Select One)** (`order_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the order type.
* **order_date (Date)** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the order date and time (if applicable).
* **order_time (Time)** (`order_time`, time, optional): Enter the order time (if applicable).
* **order_status (Select One)** (`order_status`, select_one, optional): Select the order status.
* **university_name (Text)** (`university_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the university.
* **contact_person (Text)** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **contact_email (Email)** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact person's email.
* **contact_phone (Text)** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **journal_address (Text)** (`journal_address`, text, optional): Enter the journal's address.
* **journal_city (Text)** (`journal_city`, text, optional): Enter the journal's city.
* **journal_state (Text)** (`journal_state`, text, optional): Enter the journal's state.
* **journal_postal (Text)** (`journal_postal`, text, optional): Enter the journal's postal code.
* **university_address (Text)** (`university_address`, text, optional): Enter the university's address.
* **university_city (Text)** (`university_city`, text, optional): Enter the university's city.
* **university_state (Text)** (`university_state`, text, optional): Enter the university's state.
* **university_postal (Text)** (`university_postal`, text, optional): Enter the university's postal code.
* **journal_posting (Text)** (`journal_posting`, text, optional): Enter any additional journal posting information.
* **order_note (Text)** (`order_note`, text, optional): Add any notes about the order.
