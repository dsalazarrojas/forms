# Comprehensive Menu Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Comprehensive Menu Selection form is used to collect information about a customer's order. This form is divided into several sections, including Order Details, Menu Selection, and Payment Information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Order ID, Order Date, and Customer Name.
2. Select your dining option (Dine In, Takeout, Delivery, or Catering Event).
3. Choose your Party Size and Reservation Date and Time.
4. Select your Table Preference.
5. Choose your Appetizer, Main Course, and Side Dish from the available options.
6. Select your Beverage options (if applicable).
7. List any dietary restrictions and allergies.
8. Enter any special requests or allergy information.
9. Choose your Sauce Preferences (if applicable).
10. Select the occasion for the order (if applicable).
11. Enter a Promotional Code (if applicable).
12. Select your Payment Method.
13. Choose a Tip Percentage (if applicable).
14. Enter the Total Amount for the order.
15. Select the Current Order Status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order ID** (1, text, required): Enter a unique order identifier.
* **Order Date** (2, date, required): Enter the date of the order.
* **Customer Name** (3, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (4, email, required): Enter your email address for order confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (5, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Dining Option** (6, select_one, required): Select how you will dine (Dine In, Takeout, Delivery, or Catering Event).
* **Party Size** (7, number, required): Enter the number of people in your party.
* **Reservation Date** (8, date, required): Enter the date for the reservation or pickup.
* **Reservation Time** (9, time, required): Enter your preferred time.
* **Table Preference** (10, select_one, optional): Choose your table preference (No Preference, Window Seating, Outdoor Patio, Private Room, Bar Seating, Quiet Area, or Not Applicable).
* **Appetizer Selection** (11, select_multiple, optional): Choose your appetizer options (Caesar Salad, Bruschetta, Calamari, Soup of the Day, Shrimp Cocktail, Cheese Plate, or None).
* **Main Course Selection** (12, select_one, required): Choose your main course (Grilled Salmon, Ribeye Steak, Chicken Parmesan, Vegetarian Pasta, Seafood Platter, Lamb Chops, Duck Confit, Mushroom Risotto, Chef Special).
* **Cooking Temperature** (13, select_one, optional): Select how you would like your meat cooked (Not Applicable, Rare, Medium Rare, Medium, Medium Well, Well Done).
* **Side Dish Selection** (14, select_one, required): Choose your side dish (Mashed Potatoes, Roasted Vegetables, French Fries, Rice Pilaf, Steamed Broccoli, Garden Salad, Coleslaw).
* **Dessert Selection** (15, select_one, optional): Choose your dessert (Tiramisu, Chocolate Cake, Cheesecake, Ice Cream, Fruit Tart, Creme Brulee, No Dessert).
* **Beverage Selection** (16, select_multiple, optional): Choose your beverage options (Water, Soft Drink, Fresh Juice, Coffee, Tea, Wine, Beer, Cocktail).
* **Dietary Restrictions** (17, select_multiple, required): Select any dietary restrictions (None, Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut Allergy, Shellfish Allergy, Kosher, Halal, Low Sodium).
* **Allergy Information** (18, text, optional): List any food allergies.
* **Special Requests** (19, text, optional): Enter any special requests for your meal.
* **Sauce Preferences** (20, text, optional): Enter your sauce preferences or specific requests.
* **Occasion** (21, select_one, optional): Select the occasion for the order (Regular Dining, Birthday Celebration, Anniversary, Business Dinner, Date Night, Family Gathering, Other).
* **Promotional Code** (22, text, optional): Enter a promo code if applicable.
* **Payment Method** (23, select_one, required): Select your payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, Cash, Gift Card, Mobile Payment).
* **Tip Percentage** (24, select_one, optional): Choose a tip amount (15 Percent, 18 Percent, 20 Percent, 22 Percent, Custom Amount).
* **Total Amount** (25, number, required): Enter the total order amount.
* **Order Status** (26, select_one, required): Select the current order status (Received, Confirming, Preparing, Ready, Served, Completed).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Select the correct dining option and table preference for your party.
* Choose your main course and side dish carefully to ensure the correct order is placed.
* List any dietary restrictions and allergies to avoid any issues with your order.
* Enter accurate and complete information to ensure your order is fulfilled correctly.
