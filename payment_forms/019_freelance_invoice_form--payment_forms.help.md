# Freelance Invoice Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Freelance Invoice Form is a tool designed to help freelancers manage their invoices. This form collects relevant information about the services rendered, payment details, and client contact information. The purpose of this form is to create a comprehensive and accurate invoice.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Invoice Date** field with the date you provided services to the client.
2. Enter the **Services Rendered** field with a brief description of the services you provided.
3. Enter the **Hourly Rate** field with your hourly rate of pay.
4. Enter the **Hours Worked** field with the number of hours you worked on the project.
5. Fill in the **Payment Details** field with any relevant payment information.
6. Enter the **Client Name** field with the client's name.
7. Enter the **Client E-mail** field with the client's email address.
8. The **Freelance Invoice Number** field will be automatically generated and is not editable.
9. Enter the **Subtotal** field with the calculated subtotal for the services rendered.
10. The **Total Amount** field will be automatically calculated based on the **Subtotal** and **Hourly Rate** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Invoice Date (1)**: Enter the date you provided services to the client.
* **Due Date (2)**: Enter the due date for the invoice.
* **Services Rendered (3)**: Describe the services you provided to the client.
* **Hourly Rate (4)**: Enter your hourly rate of pay.
* **Hours Worked (5)**: Enter the number of hours you worked on the project.
* **Payment Details (6)**: Enter any additional payment information, such as payment method or notes.
* **Client Name (7)**: Enter the client's name.
* **Client E-mail (8)**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Freelance Invoice Number (9)**: This field will be automatically generated and is not editable.
* **Subtotal (10)**: Enter the calculated subtotal for the services rendered.
* **Total Amount (11)**: This field will be automatically calculated based on the **Subtotal** and **Hourly Rate** fields.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate calculation of the total amount.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your administrator for assistance.
