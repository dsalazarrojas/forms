## Purpose

This form is used to capture information about a website's payment details. It is designed to collect relevant data about the website, including contact information, services provided, and other related details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the First Name field with your full name.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. If you have a company, fill in the Company Name field with your company's name.
4. If applicable, provide your website's URL in the Website field.
5. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
6. Fill in your Phone number in the Phone field.
7. Select the services you are interested in from the Services field.
8. Choose whether or not you have a package by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the Package field.
9. Add any additional comments about your project in the Additional Comments field.
10. If applicable, specify which tool is assigned to you in the Assigned Tool field.
11. Enter any relevant SEO audit data in the SEO Audit field.
12. Conduct keyword research and fill in the Keyword Research field.
13. Perform on-page optimization and fill in the On-Page Optimization field.
14. Conduct off-page optimization and fill in the Off-Page Optimization field.
15. Engage in link building and fill in the Link Building field.
16. Create content and fill in the Content Creation field.
17. Perform technical optimization and fill in the Technical Optimization field.
18. Fill in the Submitted field with the date you submitted the form.
19. Fill in the Assigned field with the date it was assigned to you.
20. Add any notes about your project in the Notes field.
21. Fill in the Created field with the date it was created.
22. Fill in the Created By field with the person who created it.
23. Fill in the Updated field with the date it was last updated.
24. Fill in the Updated By field with the person who updated it.
25. Fill in the Status field with the current status of your project.
