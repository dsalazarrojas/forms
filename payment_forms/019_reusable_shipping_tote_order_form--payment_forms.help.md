# Reusable Shipping Tote Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reusable Shipping Tote Order Form is designed for customers to provide their shipping tote order details, including their personal and shipping information, delivery preferences, and any additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Customer Details** section with your name, email, and phone number.
2. Choose your preferred **Reusable Tote Color** and **Tote Size** from the provided options.
3. Select your preferred **Delivery Preference** and **Return Preference** options.
4. Enter your **Return Address**, **Return City**, and **Return State** and **Return Zip** for the return shipping address.
5. Add any **Order Comments** or **Delivery Comments** if necessary.
6. Choose the **Delivery Status** of your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details (1)**: Enter your name, email, and phone number for order tracking and communication.
* **Reusable Tote Order (2)**: Enter a brief description of your order, including the tote type and quantity.
* **Delivery (3)**: Enter the delivery date and time you prefer.
* **Delivery Preference (4)**: Choose your preferred delivery method, such as in-person or shipping.
* **Return Preferences (5)**: Select how you prefer to receive returns, such as through email or phone.
* **Contact (6)**: Enter your contact information, including email and phone number, for order tracking and communication.
* **Shipping (7)**: Enter your shipping address details.
* **Delivery Date (8)**: Enter the date you prefer to receive your order.
* **Delivery Time (9)**: Enter the time you prefer to receive your order.
* **Payment (10)**: Enter your payment information, including payment method and amount.
* **Total (11)**: Enter the total cost of your order.
* **Order Notes (12)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Email (13)**: Enter your email address for order tracking and communication.
* **Phone (14)**: Enter your phone number for order tracking and communication.
* **Tote Quantity (15)**: Enter the number of totes you would like to order.
* **Tote Color (16)**: Choose the color of your reusable tote from the provided options.
* **Tote Size (17)**: Choose the size of your reusable tote from the provided options.
* **Return Address (18)**: Enter your return shipping address.
* **Return City (19)**: Enter the city of your return shipping address.
* **Return State (20)**: Enter the state of your return shipping address.
* **Return Zip (21)**: Enter the zip code of your return shipping address.
* **Return Country (22)**: Enter the country of your return shipping address.
* **Order Comments (23)**: Add any additional comments about your order.
* **Delivery Comments (24)**: Add any additional comments about your delivery.
* **Delivery Status (25)**: Choose the status of your delivery.

Please note that some fields may be optional or have specific requirements for completion.
