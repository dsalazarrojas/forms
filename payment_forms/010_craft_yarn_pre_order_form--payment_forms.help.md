# craft_yarn_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a pre-order form for customers to provide payment information for crafting yarn. It is used to gather necessary information for order processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your preferred payment method from the options provided.
2. Enter the amount you would like to order.
3. Enter your delivery address.
4. Choose your preferred delivery method.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Select the delivery date.
8. Enter any order comments or notes.
9. Enter shipping instructions.
10. Enter payment terms and any additional notes.
11. Enter delivery instructions.
12. Enter any customer information.
13. Select the payment status.
14. Select the delivery status.
15. Enter the order total.
16. Enter payment terms notes.
17. Enter delivery terms.
18. Enter payment notes.
19. Enter customer notes.
20. Enter order notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **payment_method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the method of payment you would like to use for your order. Options include: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4.
- **amount** (`amount`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the amount of yarn you would like to order.
- **delivery_address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your delivery address.
- **delivery_method** (`delivery_method`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your preferred delivery method. Options include: Delivery at pickup, Delivery to store, Delivery to home.
- **email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
- **phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
- **delivery_date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the delivery date.
- **order_comments** (`order_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any comments or notes about your order.
- **shipping_instructions** (`shipping_instructions`, `text`, required/optional): Enter shipping instructions.
- **payment_terms** (`payment_terms`, `text`, required/optional): Enter payment terms.
- **delivery_instructions** (`delivery_instructions`, `text`, required/optional): Enter delivery instructions.
- **customer_info** (`customer_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any customer information.
- **payment_status** (`payment_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the payment status. Options include: Paid, Pending.
- **delivery_status** (`delivery_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the delivery status. Options include: In stock, Out of stock, Unknown.
- **order_total** (`order_total`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the total order amount.
- **payment_terms_note** (`payment_terms_note`, `text`, required/optional): Enter payment terms notes.
- **delivery_terms** (`delivery_terms`, `text`, required/optional): Enter delivery terms.
- **payment_note** (`payment_note`, `text`, required/optional): Enter payment notes.
- **customer_notes** (`customer_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter customer notes.
- **order_notes** (`order_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter order notes.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check the entered information for accuracy.
* Use the correct payment and delivery methods to avoid any errors.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact us.
