# Home Backup Generator Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Backup Generator Order Form is designed to collect essential information from customers to process their requests for a home backup generator.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Customer Information** field with your name, address, and any other relevant details.
2. Select the desired backup generator from the **Backup Generator Selection** dropdown menu.
3. Enter your **Contact Information** in the provided text field.
4. Choose your preferred **Payment Method** from the dropdown menu.
5. Provide additional **Order Details** in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (`name: customer_info`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant details to help us process your request.
* **Backup Generator Selection** (`name: generator_selection`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select the backup generator you are interested in purchasing.
* **Contact Information** (`name: contact_info`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your email, phone number, or any other contact details to help us get in touch with you.
* **Payment Method** (`name: payment_method`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Choose between available payment options to complete your order.
* **Order Details** (`name: order_details`, `type: text`, required: false): Provide additional details about your order, such as special requests or comments.
