# Law School Scholarship Fund Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Law School Scholarship Fund Donation Form is designed to collect donations from individuals who wish to support the law school scholarship program. The form helps the scholarship committee to track donations and ensures that all necessary information is collected for processing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form details section to understand the purpose and goals of the scholarship fund.
2. Enter your donation amount in the "Donation Amount" field.
3. Select your preferred donation frequency from the list of options or provide a custom frequency in the "Frequency Other" field.
4. If you have a preferred payment method not listed, enter it in the "Payment Method Other" field.
5. Provide your contact information, including your first and last name, email, and phone number.
6. If you have any additional payment terms or notes, enter them in the "Payment Terms Other" and "Notes" fields.
7. Check the "Confirmation" field to confirm your submission.
8. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Details**: This section is a brief description of the scholarship fund and its purpose.
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount you wish to donate to the scholarship fund. This field accepts numeric values only.
* **Frequency**: Select your preferred donation frequency from the list of options (monthly, one-time, quarterly, semesterly, annually). If you have a custom frequency, select "Other" and enter it in the "Frequency Other" field.
* **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method (credit card, check/cash, bank transfer). If you have an other payment method, select "Other" and enter it in the "Payment Method Other" field.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name for contact purposes.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name for contact purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Billing Address**: Select whether you want to provide a billing address.
* **City**: Enter your city for billing purposes.
* **State**: Enter your state for billing purposes.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code for billing purposes.
* **Payment Terms**: This field is for any additional payment terms or conditions.
* **Notes**: This field is for any additional comments or notes related to your donation.
* **Confirm**: Confirm that you have reviewed and understand the terms of the donation.
* **Submit**: Complete the form by clicking the "Submit" button.
