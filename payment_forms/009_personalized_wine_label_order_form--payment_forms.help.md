# Personalized Wine Label Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personalized Wine Label Order Form is used to create a custom label for a wine bottle. This form helps customers design and order their label according to their preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Select whether you want a label design or not. If yes, you can choose from a variety of options.
2.  Choose the type of wine for your label (Red, White, or Blanc).
3.  Select the size of your label.
4.  Select the color of your label (Red, White, or Black).
5.  Choose the shape of your label (Round, Oval, or Square).
6.  Select the font type (Serif, Sans Serif, or Script).
7.  Enter the date of the event.
8.  Enter the time of the event.
9.  Enter the quantity of labels you need.
10. Enter your shipping address.
11.  Select a payment method (Credit Card, Paypal, or Bank Transfer).
12.  Enter the name of the event.
13.  Add any order comments or notes.
14.  Enter the price of the wine.
15.  Enter the price of the label.
16.  Enter the shipping cost.
17.  Enter the total cost of the order.
18.  Select the shipping method (Ground, Air, or Express).
19.  Select the status of your payment (Pending, Processing, or Paid).
20.  Select the status of your order (New, In Progress, or Delivered).
21.  Enter your name.
22.  Enter your email address.
23.  Enter your phone number.
24.  Enter your address.
25.  Add any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Label Design** (`label_design`, Select Multiple): Check if you want a label design. If yes, you can choose from a variety of options.
* **Wine Type** (`wine_type`, Select One): Choose the type of wine for your label (Red, White, or Blanc).
* **Label Size** (`label_size`, Text): Enter the size of your label.
* **Label Color** (`label_color`, Select One): Select the color of your label (Red, White, or Black).
* **Label Shape** (`label_shape`, Select One): Choose the shape of your label (Round, Oval, or Square).
* **Font Type** (`font_type`, Select One): Select the font type (Serif, Sans Serif, or Script).
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, Date): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, Time): Enter the time of the event.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, Number): Enter the quantity of labels you need.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, Note): Enter your shipping address.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, Select One): Select a payment method (Credit Card, Paypal, or Bank Transfer).
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, Text): Enter the name of the event.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, Note): Add any order comments or notes.
* **Wine Price** (`wine_price`, Number): Enter the price of the wine.
* **Label Price** (`label_price`, Number): Enter the price of the label.
* **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`, Number): Enter the shipping cost.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, Number): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, Select One): Select the shipping method (Ground, Air, or Express).
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, Select One): Select the status of your payment (Pending, Processing, or Paid).
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, Select One): Select the status of your order (New, In Progress, or Delivered).
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, Text): Enter your name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, Email): Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, Text): Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, Note): Enter your address.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, Note): Add any additional comments or notes.
