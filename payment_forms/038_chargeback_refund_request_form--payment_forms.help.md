# Chargeback Refund Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a chargeback refund for a disputed charge. Please fill out the form with accurate information to expedite the refund process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) below.
2. Ensure that all fields are filled out with accurate and complete information.
3. Review all fields carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Full Name* (1) - Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.**
* **Order or Transaction ID* (2) - Provide the original order or transaction ID associated with the dispute.**
* **Customer Contact Email* (4) - Enter your contact email for refund status updates and follow-up.**
* **Reason for Refund Request (6) - Briefly describe the reason for your refund request.**
* **Refund Amount Requested* (7) - Enter the total amount you are requesting as a refund.**
* **Original Purchase Date (8) - Enter the date of the original purchase.**
* **Customer Consent for Refund (11) - Select "I Consent and Agree" to confirm you consent to the refund process.**
* **Preferred Refund Method (16) - Select the most accurate description of your preferred refund method.**
* **Transaction Dispute Status (18) - Indicate if the transaction is currently being disputed.**
* **Refund Request Priority (19) - Select the most accurate description of your refund request priority.**
* **Date of Request Log (20) - Enter today's date for the request submission.**
* **Additional Refund Case Notes (21) - Provide any additional relevant information or context.**
* **Final Submission Confirmation Check (15) - Select "I Confirm and Submit" to confirm you have reviewed your request and are ready to submit.**
* **Final Approval Status Check (23) - Select "I Confirm and Approve" to confirm you have reviewed the request and are ready for processing.**
