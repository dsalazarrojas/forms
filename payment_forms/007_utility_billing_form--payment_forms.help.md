# Utility Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Utility Billing Form** is used to collect information about your utility billing details.

## How To Complete This Form
Follow the steps above to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user_input (Required: False)**: Enter your billing information, such as your name, address, or other relevant details.
* **billing_info (Required: False)**: Enter any additional billing information that may be relevant to your account.
* **payment_method (Required: False)**: Select the payment method you would like to use, such as 'Yes' for credit card or 'No' for other methods.
* **payment_amount (Required: False)**: Enter the amount you would like to pay.
* **payment_frequency (Required: False)**: Indicate how often you would like to make payments, such as 'Yes' for weekly or 'No' for monthly.
* **account_status (Required: False)**: Check your account status, such as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **note (Required: False)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your account.
