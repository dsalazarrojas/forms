# Rare Disease Research Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to support rare disease research. By filling out this form, you will be able to make a donation and provide a description of the research you want to support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields: **Donation Amount**, **Email**, and **Name**.
2. Select your preferred **Donation Method** from the options listed.
3. Enter your **Address** and optional **Research Description** and **Research Purpose**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount you would like to donate.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Donor Donation Method**: Choose from the available options: Cash, Check, Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Other.
	+ If you choose **Other**, please provide a brief description in the **Research Description** field.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Name**: Enter your name.
* **Research Description**: If you selected **Other** as your **Donor Donation Method**, provide a brief description of the research you want to support.
* **Research Purpose**: If you selected **Other**, provide a brief description of the research purpose you want to support.
