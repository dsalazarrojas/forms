# Workshop Facilitator Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Workshop Facilitator Payment Form" is a simple form designed for workshop attendees to submit their payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your User Email: This is the email address you used to register for the workshop. Enter it here.
2. Provide Your Name: This is your name as it appears on your registration.
3. Select your Payment Method: Choose how you'd like to pay for the workshop (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
4. Enter your Payment Amount: Type the amount you wish to pay for the workshop.
5. Add Payment Details (optional): If you have any specific payment details, you can enter them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, required=false): Enter your email address used for registration.
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required=false): Enter your name as it appears on your registration.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required=false): Choose your preferred payment method (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required=false): Enter the amount you wish to pay for the workshop.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, note, required=false): Enter any additional payment details (optional).

## Tips
- Please ensure that your email address is accurate to facilitate smooth payment processing.
- Double-check your payment method and amount to avoid any errors.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact the workshop facilitators for assistance.
