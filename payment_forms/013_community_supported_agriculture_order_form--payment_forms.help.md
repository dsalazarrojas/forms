# Community Supported Agriculture Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Supported Agriculture Order Form is a crucial tool for customers to provide their details and preferences for a regular delivery of fresh produce from a local farm or community garden. This form helps farmers and administrators efficiently process customer orders and ensure timely delivery of produce.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **Customer full name** in the "Customer full name" field. This is the name that will be used for order and delivery purposes.
2.  Enter your **Customer email** in the "Customer email" field. This email address will be used for order confirmation and any further communication.
3.  Provide your **Delivery address** in the "Delivery address" field. This is the address where the produce will be delivered.
4.  Choose your preferred **Subscription type** from the available options (Weekly, Biweekly, or Monthly box). Select the frequency that suits your needs.
5.  Select your preferred **Box size** from the available options (Small, Medium, or Large). Choose the size that best suits your needs.
6.  Specify the **Quantity** of boxes you wish to subscribe to, per selected frequency.
7.  Choose your preferred **Payment method** from the available options (Credit card, Bank transfer, or Cash on delivery). Select the method that best suits your financial preferences.
8.  If you have any specific **Delivery instructions**, such as gate codes or a preferred drop location, you can provide them in this field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer full name**: Your full name as it should be displayed for order and delivery purposes.
  * Type: text
  * Required: Yes
  * Hint: Name for order and delivery
- **Customer email**: The email address used for order confirmation and further communication.
  * Type: email
  * Required: Yes
  * Hint: Order confirmation will be sent here
- **Delivery address**: The address for produce delivery.
  * Type: text
  * Required: Yes
  * Hint: Address for produce delivery
- **Subscription type**: Choose your preferred subscription frequency (Weekly, Biweekly, or Monthly box).
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: Yes
  * Hint: Choose your CSA package
- **Box size**: Select your preferred box size (Small, Medium, or Large).
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: Yes
  * Hint: Select preferred box size
- **Quantity**: Specify the number of boxes you wish to subscribe to, per selected frequency.
  * Type: number
  * Required: Yes
  * Hint: Number of boxes per selected frequency
- **Payment method**: Choose your preferred payment method (Credit card, Bank transfer, or Cash on delivery).
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: Yes
  * Hint: Choose how you will pay
- **Delivery instructions**: Provide any specific delivery instructions, such as gate codes or a preferred drop location.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No
  * Hint: Gate codes or preferred drop location

This guide is designed to provide a clear and concise understanding of each field's importance and purpose, ensuring that customers complete the form efficiently and accurately.
