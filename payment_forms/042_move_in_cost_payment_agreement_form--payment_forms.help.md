# Move In Cost Payment Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Move In Cost Payment Agreement Form is used to document the financial obligations of a tenant for the first month's rent and any additional move-in costs. This form helps to ensure that both the tenant and landlord or property manager are on the same page and understand the terms of the payment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Review the Form:** Before filling out the form, please read the entire form carefully and make sure you understand the questions and fields presented.
2. **Fill Out Fields:**
   1. **Move In Date**: Enter the date you will move in.
   2. **Rent Amount**: Enter the total rent amount for the first month.
   3. **Security Deposit**: Enter the security deposit amount required.
   4. **Payment Method**: Select one of the available payment methods (Credit Card, Check, or Money Order).
   5. **Security Deposit Bond**: Check if a bond is required for the security deposit.
   6. **Move In Cost**: Enter any additional costs for move-in (e.g., utility deposits, fees).
   7. **Submit**: Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Move In Date** (move_in_cost_payment_agreement_form_move_in_date, date, required):
	+ The date the tenant will move in.
* **Rent Amount** (move_in_cost_payment_agreement_form_rent_amount, number, required):
	+ The total rent amount for the first month.
* **Security Deposit** (move_in_cost_payment_agreement_form_security_deposit, number, required):
	+ The security deposit amount required for move-in.
* **Payment Method** (move_in_cost_payment_agreement_form_payment_method, select_one, required):
	+ The method the tenant will use to pay rent (Credit Card, Check, or Money Order).
* **Security Deposit Bond** (move_in_cost_payment_agreement_form_bond_info, text, required):
	+ Is a bond required for the security deposit?
* **Move In Cost** (move_in_cost_payment_agreement_form_move_in_cost, number, required):
	+ Additional costs for move-in (e.g., utility deposits, fees).
* **Payment Method Note**: Please ensure you enter the correct payment method that you plan to use for rent payments. The landlord or property manager will use this information to set up the payment method for the rent.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult the lease agreement or contact your landlord or property manager.
* This form is a document to help you understand your financial obligations and payment methods for the first month's rent. It is not a contract, and this form is not a substitute for the lease agreement itself.
