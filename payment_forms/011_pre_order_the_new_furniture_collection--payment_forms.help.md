# Pre Order The New Furniture Collection - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential details from customers who want to pre-order the new furniture collection. By filling out this form, customers can reserve their spot in the queue, ensuring they receive the desired items before they are sold out.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required information:
   - **Home Address**: Enter your home address where the furniture will be delivered.
   - **Delivery Date**: Choose a date for delivery of the furniture.
   - **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method (Visa, Mastercard, American Express).
   - **Furniture Type**: Choose the type of furniture you want to pre-order (Sofa, Armchair, Desk).
   - **Quantity**: Enter the number of items you want to pre-order.
   - **Price**: Enter the total price for the pre-ordered items.
   - **Confirm Order**: Confirm that you would like to pre-order the items.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Address** (Required):
  * Enter your home address where the furniture will be delivered.
* **Delivery Date** (Required):
  * Choose a date for delivery of the furniture. Please note that delivery dates may vary based on production and availability of stock.
* **Contact Number** (Optional):
  * Enter your contact number for any further communications about your pre-order.
* **Payment Method** (Required):
  * Select your preferred payment method (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) to complete the payment process.
* **Furniture Type** (Required):
  * Choose the type of furniture you want to pre-order (Sofa, Armchair, Desk).
* **Quantity** (Required):
  * Enter the number of items you want to pre-order.
* **Price** (Required):
  * Enter the total price for the pre-ordered items.
* **Confirm Payment** (Required):
  * Confirm that you would like to pre-order the items and complete the payment process.
* **Confirm Delivery** (Required):
  * Confirm that you would like to receive the pre-ordered items.
* **Confirm Furniture** (Required):
  * Confirm that the pre-ordered items are correct.
* **Confirm Order** (Required):
  * Confirm that you would like to complete the pre-order process.
* **Order ID** (Required):
  * A unique ID for your pre-order will be generated automatically.
* **Form Data** (Optional):
  * Enter any additional information you would like to provide about your pre-order.
* **Form Signature** (Optional):
  * Sign the form to confirm your identity.
* **Form Comments** (Optional):
  * Enter any comments or questions about your pre-order.
* **Form Status** (Optional):
  * Track the status of your pre-order.
* **Form Submitted By** (Optional):
  * Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Form Submitted At** (Optional):
  * The date and time the form was submitted.
* **Form Last Updated At** (Optional):
  * The date and time the form was last updated.
* **Form Version** (Optional):
  * The current version of the form.
* **Form Created At** (Optional):
  * The date and time the form was created.

Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure smooth processing of your pre-order.
