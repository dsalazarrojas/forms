# Trail Maintenance Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trail Maintenance Donation Form is designed to collect donations towards the upkeep and maintenance of trails in the area. This form helps allocate funds to support trail maintenance activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Donation Amount field with the amount you would like to donate.
2. In the Donation Frequency field, select how often you would like your donation to be recurring (e.g., Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
3. In the Frequency Duration field, choose the duration of your selected frequency (e.g., Month, Quarter, or Year).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount you would like to donate towards trail maintenance. This field is not required to be filled out, but it's essential to enter a valid number if you're choosing a recurring donation.
* **Donation Frequency**: Select how often you would like your donation to recur. Options include Monthly, Quarterly, and Annually. This field is not required, but selecting an option will help allocate funds properly.
* **Frequency Duration**: Choose the duration of your selected frequency (Month, Quarter, or Year). This field is not required, but it's necessary to match your donation frequency selection.
