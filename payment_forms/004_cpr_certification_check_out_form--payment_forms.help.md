# CPR Certification Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to complete the process of obtaining CPR certification after attending a training session. It captures essential information about the participant, payment, and certification level.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Participant name** as it appears on your ID. This will help us confirm your identity.
2. Provide your **Email address**, where we will send the receipt of your payment.
3. Select the **Payment method** you used to pay for the training session. This can be "Credit card", "Bank transfer", or "Invoice".
4. Choose the **Certification level** you are requesting. This can be "Basic life support", "Advanced cardiac life support", or "Pediatric advanced life support".
5. Enter the **Total amount paid** for the training session. This should be a numeric value in your local currency.
6. If applicable, provide any **Special requirements or accessibility needs** you may have. This can include dietary or mobility needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name**: This field captures the name of the participant as it appears on their ID. Please ensure it matches your government-issued ID.
* **Email address**: This field is where you will receive the receipt of your payment. Please use a valid email address that you can access regularly.
* **Course date**: This field is used to record the date of the training session you attended. Please ensure it matches the date listed on your confirmation.
* **Payment method**: This field allows you to select how you paid for the training session. Please choose the option that best describes your payment method.
* **Certification level**: This field determines the level of certification you are requesting. Please choose from the available options based on your training requirements.
* **Total amount paid**: This field is used to capture the total amount you paid for the training session. Please enter a numeric value in your local currency.
* **Special requirements or accessibility needs**: This field is optional and used to provide any special needs you may have, such as dietary or mobility needs.
