# Documentary Screening Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to pre-order tickets for a documentary screening. Fill out this form to reserve your tickets with your desired payment method and details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your **First Name** in the "first name" field, which is a required field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "last name" field, which is also a required field.
3. Enter your valid **Email** address in the "email" field, which is a required field.
4. Choose the quantity of **Ticket Quantity** you wish to purchase, which is a required field.
5. Select the **Order Date** you would like your tickets for, which is a required field.
6. Choose the **Screening Time** you prefer, which is a required field.
7. Enter the **Ticket Price** you are willing to pay, which is a required field.
8. Calculate the **Total Cost** of your purchase, which is automatically calculated based on the ticket quantity and price.
9. Select your preferred **Payment Method** from the options: Visa, Mastercard, or PayPal, which is a required field.
10. If you'd like to provide more information about your payment, you can enter it in the "payment info" field, which is optional.
11. If you'd like to specify a **Payment Date** and **Payment Time**, you can do so in the "payment date" and "payment time" fields, respectively, which are both optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address here.
* **Ticket Quantity** (`ticket_quantity`, number, required): Choose the number of tickets you'd like to purchase.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Select the date for which you'd like to order tickets.
* **Screening Time** (`screening_time`, time, required): Choose the time slot for the screening.
* **Ticket Price** (`ticket_price`, number, required): Enter the price you're willing to pay per ticket.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, number, required): This field is automatically calculated based on the ticket quantity and price.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to pay for your tickets: Visa, Mastercard, or PayPal.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, optional): If you have any additional payment information, enter it here.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, optional): If you'd like to specify a different payment date, enter it here.
* **Payment Time** (`payment_time`, time, optional): If you'd like to specify a different payment time, enter it here.

This help guide is intended to be a comprehensive resource for users filling out the "Documentary Screening Pre Order Form".
