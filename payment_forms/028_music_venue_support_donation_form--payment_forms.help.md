# music_venue_support_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by music venues to collect payments and donations from their patrons.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form title. This will be the title of the form as it appears on the music venue's website or other online platforms.
2. Select a category for the donation from the list of available options (Payment Prophets, Payment Forms, Payment Tools, Payment Methods).
3. Add a description of the donation, if desired.
4. Enter the form ID, which is a unique identifier for the form.
5. Specify the output file, if applicable.
6. Choose the assigned tool from the list of available options (chatjimmy, chatbot, chatty).
7. Select the payment method from the list of available options (PayPal, Stripe, Bank Transfer, Credit Card).
8. Enter the donation amount.
9. Choose the payment type from the list of available options (One-time Donation, Recurring Donation, Subscription).
10. Specify the recurring frequency, if applicable (Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
11. Enter the email address of the patron.
12. Enter the phone number of the patron.
13. Add any additional note about the donation, if desired.
