# University Tuition Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture payment details and other relevant information from students who are making tuition payments to their university.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the required information. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own set of fields. You can navigate between sections by clicking on the "Next" or "Previous" buttons at the bottom of each page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: false): Choose how you'd like to pay your tuition fees. This could be by check, online, or other options listed.
* **Card Number** (`card_number`, number, required: false): Enter the card number you're using for payment.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required: false): Enter your university-issued student ID.
* **Tuition Fee** (`tuition_fee`, number, required: false): Enter the amount you're paying as tuition fee.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the total payment amount you're making.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required: false): Select the date you'd like to pay your tuition fees.
* **Payment Method 2** (`payment_method2`, select_one, required: false): Choose a secondary method of payment, if applicable.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required: false): Select whether your payment is active or inactive.
* **Payment Status 2** (`payment_status2`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether your second payment status option is active or inactive.
* **Student Level** (`student_level`, select_one, required: false): Choose the student level that best describes you (e.g., High, Low).
* **Institution Code** (`institution_code`, select_one, required: false): Select whether this payment is for an institution code that is 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **University Code** (`university_code`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether this payment is for a university code that is 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes related to your payment.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **University Name** (`university_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your university.
* **Institution Name** (`institution_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your institution.
* **Department Name** (`department_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your department.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as a student.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, required: false): Enter your student address.
* **University Address** (`university_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of your university.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, number, required: false): Enter your student grade level.
* **Payment Type** (`payment_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of payment you're making.
* **Note 2** (`note2`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes related to your payment.
