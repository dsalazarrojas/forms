# Stripe Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stripe Product Order Form is designed to collect information from customers to place an order for a product. This form helps in providing accurate and up-to-date information about the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Select the desired product from the options provided.
2. Enter the product price and currency of the order.
3. Indicate if shipping is required or not.
4. Choose the relevant tax options for the order.
5. Select any additional product options.
6. Enter the quantity of the product ordered.
7. Enter any order comments.
8. Provide customer information, including email and phone number (if applicable).
9. Enter the order total.
10. Choose the payment method for the order.
11. Select the order status (Active or Inactive).
12. Enter the order date and time (if applicable).
13. Enter any additional order notes.
14. Enter the customer email.
15. Enter the customer phone number.
16. Enter the customer address.
17. Enter the order ID.
18. Select an additional payment method (if applicable).
19. Enter the customer name.
20. Enter any customer notes.
21. Enter the product ID.
22. Enter any order comments.
23. Select an additional order status (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **product** (Select One): Select the product you wish to order from the provided options.
* **product price** (Number): Enter the price of the product.
* **currency** (Select One): Choose the currency for the order price.
* **shipping** (Select One): Indicate if shipping is required or not.
* **tax** (Select One): Choose the relevant tax options for the order.
* **product options** (Select Multiple): Select any additional product options.
* **quantity** (Text): Enter the quantity of the product ordered.
* **order comments** (Text): Enter any comments or notes about the order.
* **customer info** (Text): Enter the customer's information, including email and phone number.
* **order total** (Number): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **payment method** (Select One): Choose the payment method for the order.
* **order status** (Select Multiple): Select the status of the order (Active or Inactive).
* **order date** (Date): Enter the date of the order.
* **order time** (Time): Enter the time of the order.
* **order notes** (Note): Enter any additional order notes.
* **customer email** (Email): Enter the customer's email address.
* **customer phone** (Text): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **customer address** (Text): Enter the customer's address.
* **order id** (Number): Enter the order ID.
* **payment method 2** (Select One): Select an additional payment method (if applicable).
* **customer name** (Text): Enter the customer's name.
* **customer notes** (Note): Enter any notes about the customer.
* **product id** (Text): Enter the product ID.
* **order comments 2** (Text): Enter any additional comments about the order.
* **order status 2** (Select Multiple): Select an additional order status (if applicable).
