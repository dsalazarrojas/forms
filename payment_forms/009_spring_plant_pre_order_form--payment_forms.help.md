# Spring Plant Pre Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Spring Plant Pre Order Form is designed to collect information for the pre-ordering of Spring plants. This includes customer details, delivery information, and payment method selection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Spring plant pre-order details in the **Spring Plant Pre Order** field (id: 1).
2. Provide your **Customer Information** in the **customer_info** field (id: 2).
3. Input your **Delivery Info** in the **Delivery Info** field (id: 3).
4. Select a **Delivery Date** in the **Delivery Date** field (id: 4).
5. Choose a **Delivery Time** in the **Delivery Time** field (id: 5).
6. Select the type of **Plant Type** from the available options (id: 7).
7. Enter the desired **Quantity** of plants (id: 8).
8. Choose a **Delivery Option**: **Pickup** or **Delivery** (id: 9).
9. If applicable, select **Zip** option as 'Yes' or 'No' (id: 10).
10. Enter your preferred **Pickup Time** in the **Pickup Time** field (id: 11).
11. If necessary, add **Special Instructions** or **Order Comments** in the respective fields (id: 12 and 13).
12. Provide any **Customer Notes** (id: 14).
13. Select a **Payment Method** from the available options (id: 15).
14. Enter your **Customer Email** address (id: 16).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Spring Plant Pre Order** (id: 1): Enter your Spring plant pre-order details in this field.
* **customer_info** (id: 2): Provide your name and contact information to help us process your order efficiently.
* **Delivery Info** (id: 3): Enter your delivery address to ensure accurate shipping.
* **Delivery Date** (id: 4): Select the date you would like to receive your plants.
* **Delivery Time** (id: 5): Choose the time frame for your delivery.
* **Plant Type** (id: 7): Select from available plant types, such as Apple, Cherry, etc.
* **Quantity** (id: 8): Enter the number of plants you wish to order.
* **Delivery Option** (id: 9): Choose between **Pickup** and **Delivery** options.
* **Zip** (id: 10): If you have a delivery option, select 'Yes' or 'No' accordingly.
* **Pickup Time** (id: 11): Enter your preferred pickup time.
* **Special Instructions** (id: 12): Add any special instructions or requests for your order.
* **Order Comments** (id: 13): Enter any additional comments or messages for your order.
* **Customer Notes** (id: 14): Provide any further information about your order.
* **Payment Method** (id: 15): Select your preferred payment method, such as Credit, Debit, etc.
* **Customer Email** (id: 16): Enter your email address for communication and updates.
