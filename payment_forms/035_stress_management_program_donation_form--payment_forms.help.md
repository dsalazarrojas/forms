# Stress Management Program Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect donations for the Stress Management Program. It helps us understand how you would like to support this program by donating a certain amount, at a certain frequency, and through a specific method.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, as much as possible.
2. Select a donation amount that you are comfortable with.
3. Choose how often you would like to make the donation (e.g., Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
4. Select the method you would like to use for the donation (e.g., Credit Card, Check, Bank Transfer).
5. Fill out your name, email, and phone number for contact purposes.
6. If you have any additional comments or requests, please include them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you would like to donate to the Stress Management Program. You can enter a specific amount, and we will process it accordingly.
* **Frequency of Donation** (`frequency_of_donation`, select_one, optional): Choose how often you would like to make the donation. Options include Monthly, Quarterly, and Annually.
* **Donation Method** (`donation_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select the method you prefer for the donation. Options include Credit Card, Check, and Bank Transfer.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please provide your name for contact purposes. This is helpful for us to follow up with you regarding your donation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for us to contact you about your donation and any other updates about the program.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for us to reach you regarding your donation and the program.
* **Donation Date** (`donation_date`, date, optional): If you have a specific date in mind for the donation, please enter it here.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or requests, please include them here. This helps us understand your intentions and preferences better.
