# Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) Authorization Form is used to authorize and document electronic fund transfers. This form helps ensure accuracy and consistency in the transfer process, and is likely used by financial institutions or individuals to manage and track electronic funds transfers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of transfer you are making (e.g. ACH, Wire, or Other).
2. Enter the account number associated with the transfer.
3. Enter the name of the bank associated with the transfer.
4. Enter the routing number associated with the transfer.
5. Enter the name of the account holder associated with the transfer.
6. Enter the transfer amount.
7. Enter the transfer date.
8. Select the time zone for the transfer.
9. Enter a brief description of the transfer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Transfer Type** (Transfer Type, Select One, Required): Select the type of transfer you are making (e.g. ACH, Wire, or Other).
* **Account Number** (Account Number, Text, Required): Enter the account number associated with the transfer.
* **Bank Name** (Bank Name, Text, Required): Enter the name of the bank associated with the transfer.
* **Routing Number** (Routing Number, Text, Required): Enter the routing number associated with the transfer.
* **Account Name** (Account Name, Text, Required): Enter the name of the account holder associated with the transfer.
* **Transfer Amount** (Transfer Amount, Number, Required): Enter the amount of the transfer.
* **Transfer Date** (Transfer Date, Date, Required): Enter the date of the transfer.
* **Transfer Time** (Transfer Time, Time, Required): Select the time of the transfer in 24-hour format.
* **Transfer Timezone** (Transfer Timezone, Select Multiple, Required): Select the time zone of the transfer (e.g. EST, CST, MST).
* **Account Holder Name** (Account Holder Name, Text, Required): Enter the name of the account holder associated with the transfer.
* **Account Address** (Account Address, Text, Required): Enter the address of the account holder associated with the transfer.
* **Transfer Description** (Transfer Description, Text, Required): Enter a brief description of the transfer.
