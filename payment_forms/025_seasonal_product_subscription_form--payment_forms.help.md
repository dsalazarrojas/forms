# Seasonal Product Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect user information for seasonal product subscription purposes, allowing customers to express interest in seasonal products or services and providing necessary contact information for follow-up communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that apply to you and provide any necessary contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Selection** (`Product Selection`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the seasonal products or services you are interested in subscribing to. You can choose multiple options if applicable.
* **Subscription Frequency** (`Subscription Frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose how often you would like to receive the seasonal product or service, such as once a season or multiple times a year.
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, date, optional): If you've already decided on a specific start date for your subscription, you can enter it here. Otherwise, you can leave this field blank.
* **End Date** (`End Date`, date, optional): If you've already decided on a specific end date for your subscription, you can enter it here. Otherwise, you can leave this field blank.
* **Customer Name** (`Customer Name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it should be used for follow-up communication.
* **Customer Email** (`Customer Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address as it should be used for follow-up communication.
* **Customer Phone** (`Customer Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number as it should be used for follow-up communication.
* **Customer Note** (`Customer Note`, note, optional): Enter any additional information or notes about your subscription, such as special requests or preferences.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your selections and contact information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or need assistance with the form, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for support.
