# Insurance Add On Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Add On Order Form is designed to collect information from clients regarding their insurance add-ons. This form helps to process client requests for additional coverage or policy changes. It captures essential details such as policy numbers, coverage types, and payment methods, ensuring accurate and efficient processing of client requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have the necessary information and documentation ready, including policy numbers and client contact details.
2.  Enter the policy number and select the coverage type (Life, Auto, or Home) that applies to your add-on request.
3.  Provide the effective date for the add-on coverage.
4.  Specify the premium amount for the additional coverage.
5.  Choose the coverage period you require.
6.  Enter the start date for the coverage.
7.  Enter the end date for the coverage (if applicable).
8.  Select the payment method (Cash, Credit Card, or Wire Transfer).
9.  Choose the payment status (Pending, Paid, or Partially Refunded, or Fully Refunded).
10.  Add any additional notes from your conversation with the client.
11.  Enter the client's name.
12.  Enter the client's email address.
13.  Enter the client's phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Policy Number** (`Policy Number`, `text`, required): Enter the policy number associated with the client's current insurance policy. This is a unique identifier that helps us process your request accurately.
*  **Coverage Type** (`Coverage Type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of coverage you are requesting (Life, Auto, or Home).
*  **Effective Date** (`Effective Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when you want the add-on coverage to start. This ensures that your coverage starts on the desired date.
*  **Premium Amount** (`Premium Amount`, `number`, required): Enter the amount you are willing to pay for the additional coverage.
*  **Coverage Period** (`Coverage Period`, `number`, required): Choose how long you want the coverage to last.
*  **Coverage Start Date** (`Coverage Start Date`, `time`, required): Enter the start time for the coverage.
*  **Coverage End Date** (`Coverage End Date`, `date`, required): Enter the end time for the coverage (if applicable).
*  **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you want to make the payment (Cash, Credit Card, or Wire Transfer).
*  **Payment Status** (`Payment Status`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the current payment status (Pending, Paid, or Partially Refunded, or Fully Refunded).
*  **Agent Notes** (`Agent Notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the client's request.
*  **Client Name** (`Client Name`, `text`, required): Enter the client's name for reference.
*  **Client Email** (`Client Email`, `email`, required): Enter the client's email address for communication purposes.
*  **Client Phone** (`Client Phone`, `text`, required): Enter the client's phone number for future contact.

This guide will help you complete the Insurance Add On Order Form accurately and efficiently. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
