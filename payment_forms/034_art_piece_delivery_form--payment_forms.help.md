# Art Piece Delivery Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Art Piece Delivery Form is designed to collect information for delivering art pieces. This includes details about the sender, recipient, artwork, and delivery instructions. This form will help ensure that your art piece is delivered smoothly and on time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you're filling this form for the correct purpose.
2.  Fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
3.  Enter accurate and complete information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Delivery Date
*   **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you would like the art piece to be delivered. This will help us plan and coordinate the delivery.

### 2. Sender Information
*   **Sender Name** (`sender_name`, text, required): Enter your name or the name of the person or gallery that will be sending the art piece.
*   **Sender Email** (`sender_email`, email, required): Enter your email address or the email address of the person or gallery that will be sending the art piece.
*   **Sender Phone** (`sender_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number or the contact phone number of the person or gallery that will be sending the art piece.
*   **Sender Address** (`sender_address`, text, required): Enter the address where the art piece will be shipped from.

### 3. Recipient Information
*   **Recipient Name** (`recipient_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or institution receiving the art piece.
*   **Recipient Email** (`recipient_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person or institution receiving the art piece.
*   **Recipient Phone** (`recipient_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the person or institution receiving the art piece.
*   **Recipient Address** (`recipient_address`, text, required): Enter the address where the art piece will be delivered.

### 4. Artwork Specifications
*   **Artwork Title** (`artwork_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the art piece being delivered.
*   **Artist Name** (`artist_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the artist who created the art piece.
*   **Artwork Type** (`artwork_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of art piece (e.g., Painting, Sculpture, etc.).
*   **Dimensions** (`dimensions`, text, required): Enter the height x width x depth of the art piece (in inches or cm).
*   **Weight** (`weight`, number, optional): Enter the weight of the art piece in pounds.
*   **Framed** (`framed`, select_one, required): Indicate if the art piece is framed (True/False).
*   **Crating Required** (`crating_required`, select_one, required): Indicate if the art piece requires crating (Yes - Custom Crate, Yes - Standard Crate, False).
*   **Artwork Value** (`artwork_value`, number, required): Enter the declared value of the art piece.
*   **Insurance Required** (`insurance_required`, select_one, required): Indicate if insurance is needed (Yes - Full Value, Yes - Partial, False).
*   **Delivery Speed** (`delivery_speed`, select_one, required): Choose the delivery timeframe (Standard (5-7 days), Express (2-3 days), Overnight, Same Day, Scheduled Date).
*   **Preferred Delivery Date** (`preferred_delivery_date`, date, required): Enter the preferred date for delivery.
*   **Preferred Delivery Time** (`preferred_delivery_time`, select_one, optional): Choose the preferred time window (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Any Time).
*   **White Glove Service** (`white_glove_service`, select_one, required): Indicate if premium handling service is required (True/False).
*   **Installation Required** (`installation_required`, select_one, required): Indicate if installation is needed at the destination (True/False).
*   **Special Handling** (`special_handling`, text, optional): Enter any special handling requirements.
*   **Climate Controlled** (`climate_controlled`, select_one, required): Indicate if climate-controlled handling is required (True/False).
*   **Signature Required** (`signature_required`, select_one, required): Indicate if a signature is required on delivery (True/False).

## Tips
*   Please fill out all required fields accurately to ensure smooth delivery.
*   If you have any special requests or instructions, please include them in the "Special Handling" field.
*   If you're unsure about any field, please ask your gallery or the person handling delivery for clarification.
*   This form will help us plan and coordinate the delivery of your art piece effectively.
