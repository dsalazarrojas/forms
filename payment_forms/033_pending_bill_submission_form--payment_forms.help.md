# pending_bill_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pending_bill_submission_form is used to capture information about pending bills to be submitted to the billing team for processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Form Details section with the relevant information about the bill.
2. Select the department and project associated with the bill from the drop-down menus.
3. Enter the due date for the bill.
4. Add any additional description or notes about the bill in the Description field.
5. Enter the total amount of the bill.
6. Enter the date you would like to submit the bill.
7. Enter your name as the submitter.
8. Add any additional department or project notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Details** (Text field): Enter a brief description of the bill, such as the bill number or a description of the services provided.
- **Department** (Select One field): Select the department that the bill is associated with, such as Marketing or Sales.
- **Project** (Select One field): Select the project that the bill is associated with, such as Marketing Campaign or Product Launch.
- **Due Date** (Date field): Enter the date by which the bill is due.
- **Description** (Text field): Add any additional description or notes about the bill.
- **Total Amount** (Number field): Enter the total amount of the bill.
- **Submit Date** (Date field): Enter the date you would like to submit the bill.
- **Submitter** (Text field): Enter your name as the submitter of the bill.
- **Department Notes** (Text field): Add any additional notes about the department that the bill is associated with.
- **Project Notes** (Text field): Add any additional notes about the project that the bill is associated with.
