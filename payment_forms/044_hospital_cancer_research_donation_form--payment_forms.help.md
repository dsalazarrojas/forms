# Hospital Cancer Research Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to make a donation to support cancer research at the hospital.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit Form" button at the bottom of the page to begin.
2. Select the "Payment Method" field and choose one of the available options.
3. Choose the "Donation Status" field and select the relevant status for your donation.
4. Fill in the Donor Information fields with your details.
5. Fill in the Gift Amount and Gift Frequency fields if applicable.
6. You can also choose your recognition preferences in the "Recognition Preferences" field.
7. Finally, submit the form to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, required): Select the payment method you would like to use for your donation (e.g. credit card, bank transfer, etc.).
* **Donation Status** (`donation_status`, required): Choose the status that best describes your donation (e.g. recurring, one-time, etc.).
* **Donor Information** (`donor_information`, not required): Provide your name and contact information so we can keep in touch with you.
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, not required): Enter your name as you would like to be recognized.
* **Donor Email** (`donor_email`, not required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for updates.
* **Donor Phone** (`donor_phone`, not required): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Donor Address** (`donor_address`, not required): Enter your address for administrative purposes.
* **Recognition Preferences** (`recognition_preferences`, not required): Choose how you would like to be recognized for your donation (e.g. public, private, etc.).
* **Gift Amount** (`gift_amount`, not required): Enter the amount you would like to donate.
* **Gift Frequency** (`gift_frequency`, not required): Enter the frequency of your donations (e.g. monthly, yearly, etc.).
* **Gift Date** (`gift_date`, not required): Enter the date of your donation if it's a one-time gift.
* **Gift Time** (`gift_time`, not required): Enter the time of your donation if it's a one-time gift.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, not required): Re-enter the amount you want to donate (this is for administrative purposes).
* **Submit Form** (`submit_form`, not required): Click this button to submit your form and complete the process.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (Payment Method and Donation Status).
