# Hiking Boot Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for customers to pre-order hiking boots. It collects necessary information for payment and shipping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Enter your payment details, including whether you have a preferred payment method.
2. Provide any necessary payment information, such as the amount and currency of the order.
3. Enter your shipping address and country.
4. Provide your contact information, including your name and phone number.
5. Add any additional notes if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form 1**: Enter the first part of the form, which is a text field for general information.
* **Payment Method**: Select whether you have a preferred payment method (Yes/No).
* **Payment Details**: Select whether you need any payment details (Yes/No).
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount you want to pay.
* **Payment Currency**: Select the currency of the payment (Yes/No).
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your shipping address.
* **Shipping Country**: Select your shipping country.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Note**: Add any additional notes about your order.
