# Extension Bracket Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Extension Bracket Order Form is used to capture information for an extension bracket order. This form includes fields for user, shipping, payment, and customer details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Information section with your details.
2. Enter the shipping details, including shipping method and cost.
3. Specify the payment terms and method.
4. Enter customer details, including name, email, phone, and address.
5. Review and fill out any other fields as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_input`): Enter your name, email, and phone to be associated with this order.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`): Enter your shipping details, including shipping method and cost.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`): Specify whether the payment includes tax.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`): Enter your shipping address.
* **Billing Details** (`billing_details`): Enter your billing details, including any notes.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`): Select the shipping method for this order.
* **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`): Enter the shipping cost.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select the payment method for this order.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`): Specify whether the payment includes tax.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`): Enter the payment amount.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`): Enter the payment date.
* **Order Note** (`order_note`): Enter any notes about this order.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`): Select the payment status of this order.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`): Enter the customer's address.
* **Order Id** (`order_id`): Enter the order ID.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`): Enter the order date.
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`): Enter any notes about the customer.
* **Shipping Cost Total** (`shipping_cost_total`): Enter the total shipping cost.
* **Shipping Cost Tax** (`shipping_cost_tax`): Enter the shipping cost tax.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`): Enter the total cost.

**Tips**

* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team.
