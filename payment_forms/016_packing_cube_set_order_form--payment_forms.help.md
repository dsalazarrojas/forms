# Packing Cube Set Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect order details and payment information from customers for packing cube set orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Select the payment method you prefer (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
3. Provide your shipping address.
4. Enter your order total (if required).
5. Specify your payment status (Paid, Pending, or Unpaid).
6. Add any customer notes if needed.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Provide your phone number.
9. Enter your shipping name (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info**: This field is used to collect general information about the customer, such as their details for the order.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you prefer from the options provided (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the shipping address where the packing cube set will be delivered.
* **Order Total**: Enter the total amount of the order.
* **Payment Status**: Specify the current status of the payment (Paid, Pending, or Unpaid).
* **Customer Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the customer.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number for contact.
* **Shipping Name**: Enter the name of the person to be shipped to (if different from the customer's name).
