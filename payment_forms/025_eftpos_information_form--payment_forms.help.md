# eftpos_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The eftpos_information_form is used to collect and store information about merchants' accounts and terminals for efficient processing of EFTPOS transactions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the relevant sections with accurate and up-to-date information about the merchant's account and terminal.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out, even if some fields are not marked as required.
3. Use the provided hints and options for guidance when filling out specific fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Merchant Details**: This section is for merchant-related information. Please fill in the name, account type, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Info**: Enter the merchant's contact information, including phone and email.
* **Terminal Details**: Describe the terminal's type (online, offline, POS) and location.
* **Account Details**: Provide the account name and number.
* **Payment Info**: This field is for payment-related information; please fill in any relevant details.
* **Business Details**: Enter any relevant business details.
* **Bank Details**: Fill in the bank's details.
* **Review Info**: Enter the review date and time.
* **Merchant Address**: Enter the merchant's address.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the contact person's name and phone number.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number.
* **Contact EMail**: Enter the contact email.
* **Account Type**: Choose the type of account (merchant, bank, or third-party).
* **Terminal Type**: Select the terminal type (online, offline, POS).
* **Terminal Location**: Enter the terminal's location.
* **Terminal Address**: Enter the terminal's address.
* **Terminal City**: Enter the terminal's city.
* **Terminal State**: Enter the terminal's state.
* **Terminal Zip**: Enter the terminal's zip code.
* **Account Name**: Enter the account name.
* **Account Number**: Enter the account number.
* **Terminal ID**: Enter the terminal ID.
* **Account Status**: Enter the account status.
* **Review Date**: Enter the review date.
* **Review Time**: Enter the review time.

Note: Some fields are not required, but it's essential to fill them out if they're relevant to the merchant's account or terminal.
