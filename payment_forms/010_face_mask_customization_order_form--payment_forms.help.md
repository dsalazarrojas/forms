# Face Mask Customization Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for customers who want to customize their face masks with specific colors, sizes, and design patterns. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure that your order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Mask Colors** (`mask_colors`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the colors you want for your face mask. You can choose multiple colors by selecting more than one option from the dropdown list.
* **Choose Mask Size** (`mask_sizes`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the size of your face mask from the available options. This will ensure that the mask fits you properly.
* **Select Design Pattern** (`design_pattern`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Pick the design pattern you want for your face mask. You can choose multiple patterns by selecting more than one option from the dropdown list.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `note`, required: false): If you have any special requests or instructions for your face mask, please enter them in this field. This is a text area where you can type in any additional information.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `email`, required: false): Enter your contact information, including your email address, so we can get in touch with you about your order.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your order if needed.

## Tips

* Be sure to select accurate and valid information for each field to ensure that your order is processed correctly.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
