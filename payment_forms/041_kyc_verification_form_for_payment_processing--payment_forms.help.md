# KYC Verification Form For Payment Processing - Help Guide
## Purpose
The KYC Verification Form For Payment Processing is a form designed for merchants to submit their information for verification purposes. This form is used to verify merchants' identities and ensure that they are legitimate businesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your Merchant Details section with your basic information such as your name, business number, email, phone, tax ID number, business address, and business license.
2. Proceed to the Account Details section, where you will be asked to provide information about your account such as your account type, account holder, routing number, account number, document, and document type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Merchant Name**: Provide your business name.
- **Business Number**: Enter your business number.
- **Email**: Enter your email address.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
- **Tax ID Number**: Enter your tax ID number.
- **Business Address**: Enter your business address.
- **Business License**: Enter your business license number.
- **Account Type**: Choose 'Yes' if you have an account with our platform, 'No' if you don't.
- **Account Holder**: Enter your account holder's name.
- **Routing Number**: Enter your account's routing number.
- **Account Number**: Enter your account number.
- **Document**: Upload any relevant document to support your account information.
- **Document Type**: Choose 'Yes' if you have a document to upload, 'No' if you don't.
