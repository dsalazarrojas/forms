# Veterans' Home Facility Upgrade Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from donors interested in supporting the Veterans' Home Facility Upgrade program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the base information, providing general details about yourself and your connection to the donation.
2. Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
3. Share any additional details about your donation, including the amount you're willing to contribute and whether it's a recurring or one-time donation.
4. Specify the donation frequency and start and end dates if you're planning a recurring donation.
5. Add any additional comments or information you think is relevant to your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Base Information (1)**: Provide basic details about yourself, such as your name and title.
- **Contact Information (2)**: Enter a valid email address, phone number, and address where we can contact you.
- **Donation Information (3)**: Describe your donation, including the amount and any specific reasons why you're supporting the Veterans' Home Facility Upgrade program.
- **Donation Amount (5)**: Enter the exact amount you're willing to donate. Please note that this field is optional, but it helps us understand the extent of your contribution.
- **Recurring Donation (6)**: Choose 'Yes' if you plan on making recurring donations, which will allow us to plan and budget for the program more effectively.
- **Donation Frequency (7)**: Choose how often you're willing to make donations.
- **Recurring Donation Frequency Details (8)**: If you're making a recurring donation, provide any additional information about your donation schedule.
- **Start Date (9)**: Enter the start date for your recurring donation, if applicable.
- **End Date (10)**: Enter the end date for your recurring donation, if applicable.
- **Donor Information (11)**: Share any relevant details about yourself as a donor, such as your organization affiliation or role in the community.
- **Additional Donation Info (12)**: Leave any additional comments or information about your donation, if needed.
