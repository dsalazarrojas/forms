# Construction Material Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather payment information for construction materials from customers or businesses. It captures essential details such as customer and billing contact information, material and order details, payment and transaction information, and delivery verification. This form helps to accurately process payments and ensure that materials have been received correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by entering the required customer and billing contact information.
2.  Provide the invoice or order number and project reference for the payment.
3.  Check all relevant material categories and add a brief description of the materials purchased.
4.  Enter the subtotal amount before taxes and delivery fees.
5.  Calculate and enter the total payment amount by including taxes and delivery fees.
6.  Select the preferred payment method, and for business accounts, add the purchase order number.
7.  Confirm whether materials have been received or if it's a pre-payment for materials not yet delivered.
8.  Add any additional billing or delivery notes.
9.  Enter the date of submission.
10. Review and submit the form for accurate payment processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name or Business Name**: Enter the customer or business name as it appears on the invoice or order.
* **Billing Email Address**: Enter the email address for invoice and receipt delivery.
* **Billing Phone Number**: Enter the phone number for billing and communication purposes.
* **Billing Address**: Enter the address for the customer or business to ensure accurate delivery and billing.
* **Invoice or Order Number**: Enter the unique number for the order or invoice.
* **Project Reference (Project Name or ID)**: Enter the reference or name of the project.
* **Material Categories Purchased**: Check all categories of materials purchased (e.g., Concrete/Cement, Lumber/Wood Products).
* **Brief Summary of Materials**: Provide a brief description of the materials purchased (e.g., 500 Sheets of 1/2in Drywall).
* **Subtotal Amount ($)**: Enter the amount before taxes and delivery fees.
* **Taxes and Delivery Fees ($)**: Enter the total taxes and delivery fees for the order.
* **Total Amount to Pay ($)**: Calculate the sum of the subtotal and taxes and fees.
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Select the preferred method of payment (e.g., Credit/Debit Card, Bank Transfer (ACH), Check (Already Sent/Dropped off), Internal Business Account/PO).
* **Purchase Order (PO) Number**: Enter the PO number for business account payments.
* **Materials Have Been Received**: Confirm whether all materials have been delivered or if it's a pre-payment for materials not yet received.
* **Additional Billing or Delivery Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments for the payment or delivery.
* **Date of Submission**: Enter the date the form was submitted.

This form is designed to ensure accurate and efficient processing of construction material payments, and following this guide will help users complete it correctly.
