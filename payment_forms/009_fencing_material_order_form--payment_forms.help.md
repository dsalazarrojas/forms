# Fencing Material Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fencing Material Order Form is used to place orders for fencing materials. This form captures essential details about the customer, material type, and order requirements to facilitate accurate processing and delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer information, including email and phone number (fields 1-4).
2. Select the type of material you'd like to order (field 5).
3. Specify the quantity of material needed (field 6).
4. Enter the price for the order (field 7).
5. Add any additional comments about the order (field 8).
6. Enter your customer email address (field 9).
7. Enter your customer phone number (field 10).
8. Click the "Submit" button (field 11) to complete the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer_info**: This field is used to collect your name, address, and other relevant customer information. Please fill this section with your name, address, and contact information.
* **Material Type (material_type)**: Choose the type of material you'd like to order from the options provided (Lumber, Vinyl, Metal, or Wood).
* **Quantity (quantity)**: Enter the amount of material needed for your order.
* **Price (price)**: Enter the price for the order.
* **Order Comments (order_comments)**: Add any notes or comments about your order.
* **Customer Email (customer_email)**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your order.
* **Customer Phone (customer_phone)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Customer Address (customer_address)**: Enter your address to ensure accurate delivery of the order.
* **Customer Notes (customer_notes)**: Add any additional notes or comments about yourself or the order.
* **Submit (submit)**: Click this button to complete and submit your order for processing.
