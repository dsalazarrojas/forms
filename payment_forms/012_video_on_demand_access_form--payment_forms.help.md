# Video On Demand Access Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Video On Demand Access Form is used to request access to a video content for streaming. It captures essential information from the user to facilitate the process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Step 1: Enter Your First Name**
	* Enter your first name in the text field.
2. **Step 2: Enter Your Email Address**
	* Enter your email address in the email field.
3. **Step 3: Choose Your Video Title**
	* Select one or more video titles from the list of available options.
4. **Step 4: Choose Your Access Type**
	* Select the type of access you need for the video.
5. **Step 5: Add a Note (Optional)**
	* If you have any additional information or special requests, you can add a note in the text field. This is not required.
6. **Submit the Form**
	* Click the submit button to send your request for video on demand access.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`video_on_demand_access_form_page_1`, text, required)
	+ Enter your first name to identify yourself.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, required)
	+ Enter your email address to receive access instructions and any further communication.
* **User Phone** (`user_phone_number`, text, optional)
	+ Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication (optional).
* **Video Title** (`video_id`, select multiple, required)
	+ Select one or more video titles for which you need access.
* **Access Type** (`access_type`, select one, required)
	+ Choose the type of access you need, such as rental or purchase.
* **Request Reason** (`user_note`, note, optional)
	+ Add any additional information or special requests for the video access.
