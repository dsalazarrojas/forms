# Farm To Table Product Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Farm To Table Product Delivery Form is a tool for farmers to deliver their products directly to customers. This form helps in organizing and tracking the delivery process, ensuring timely and efficient transportation of fresh produce from farm to table.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a date for the delivery, making sure to choose the correct date when the delivery should take place.
2. Enter the name of the farm providing the product.
3. Enter the name of the customer receiving the delivery.
4. Enter the email address of the customer.
5. Provide the phone number of the customer.
6. Click "Submit" to send the delivery information to our system for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Choose the date when the delivery should take place. Please use the standard date format (dd/mm/yyyy) and select from the calendar options available.
* **Farm Name** (`farm_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the farm providing the product. This helps us to identify the farm of origin and ensure smooth coordination.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the customer receiving the delivery. This helps us to personalize the delivery process and provide better service.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the customer. This allows us to keep the customer informed about delivery updates and schedules.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the customer. This ensures timely communication and resolves any last-minute queries or concerns.
* **Tips**: Make sure to fill in the form accurately and completely to avoid any confusion in the delivery process.
