# Catering Service Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Catering Service Contract Form is a necessary document to be completed by clients and catering companies prior to catering services for an event. The form captures essential information regarding event details, services provided, pricing, and payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Section 1: Parties to the Contract**
   - This is the introductory section with two sub-sections: 
     - **Client Name, Client Email, and Client Phone**: Enter your personal contact information.
     - **Catering Company Name, Catering Contact, and Catering Company Address**: Enter the contact information for the catering company.

2. **Section 2: Event Details**
   - **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
   - **Event Time**: Enter the start time of the event.
   - **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event, including the full address.
   - **Expected Number of Guests**: Enter the expected number of guests attending the event.

3. **Section 3: Services Offered**
   - **Service Type**: Select the type of catering service to be provided (e.g., Buffet, Plated Service, etc.).
   - **Menu Description**: Describe the menu to be served.
   - **Setup Service Included**: Select whether setup services are included or not.
   - **Cleanup Service Included**: Select whether cleanup services are included or not.

4. **Section 4: Pricing & Payment**
   - **Cost Per Person**: Enter the cost per person for the catering service.
   - **Total Estimated Cost**: Calculate the total cost based on the expected number of guests.
   - **Deposit Amount**: Enter the deposit amount (usually 25-50% of the total cost).
   - **Deposit Due Date**: Enter the date by which the deposit is due.
   - **Final Payment Date**: Enter the date by which the final payment is due (usually 1-2 weeks before the event).

5. **Section 5: Special Requests & Terms**
   - **Special Dietary Needs or Restrictions**: If necessary, enter any special dietary needs or restrictions.
   - **Terms Acceptance**: Select whether you accept the terms and conditions of this contract.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Double-check the accuracy of the event and catering details.
- Ensure all payment terms and dates are clear and understood.
- Review the terms and conditions before accepting the contract.
