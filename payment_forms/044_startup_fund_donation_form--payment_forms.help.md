# Startup Fund Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for collecting information about startup fund donations, including donation details, payment methods, and reporting requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about the donation.
2. Select the correct options for select fields, such as "donation_type" and "payment_method".
3. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "donation_description" field.
4. Ensure that the form is complete before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Amount** (1): Enter the amount of the donation.
* **Donor Name** (2): Enter the name of the donor.
* **Donor Email** (3): Enter the email address of the donor.
* **Donor Phone** (4): Enter the phone number of the donor.
* **Donation Description** (5): Enter a brief description of the donation.
* **Donation Type** (6): Select the type of donation (General Donations or One-time Donations).
* **Donation Frequency** (7): Select the frequency of the donation (One-Time, Monthly, or Quarterly).
* **Payment Method** (8): Select the payment method (Credit Card or PayPal).
* **Payment Status** (9): Select the payment status (Pending or Completed).
* **Payment Amount** (10): Enter the amount of the payment.
* **Payment Currency** (11): Select the currency of the payment (USD or EUR).
* **Donor Notes** (12): Enter any additional notes from the donor.
* **Payment Notes** (13): Enter any additional notes about the payment.
* **Startup ID** (14): Enter the ID of the startup.
* **User ID** (15): Enter the ID of the user.
* **Payment Method Other** (16): If the payment method is not listed, enter the alternative method.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Be accurate and precise when filling in the form.
* If you have any questions, please reach out to the support team.
* Double-check the form before submitting.
* Ensure that the payment method is selected correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the help guide or contact the support team.
