# Private Marina Slip Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Private Marina Slip Booking Form is designed to collect essential information for customers to book a private marina slip. Please complete this form to ensure accurate and efficient communication between customers and marina operators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Select the relevant fields and enter the required information for the marina slip booking.
2.  Fill out the **Boat Length**, **Boat Type**, **Marina Fee**, **Payment Method**, and **Payment Status** fields as required.
3.  Enter the **Slip Status** to indicate the status of the slip.
4.  Enter the **Additional Comments** field for any further details or information that need to be shared with the marina operator.
5.  Click the "Submit" button to complete the form and send the booking request to the marina operator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Private Marina Slip Booking Form**: This is the title of the form.
*   **Slip Type**: This field is not used, please ignore it.
*   **Boat Length**: This is the length of the boat that the customer is looking to book. Please enter the boat's length in meters.
*   **Boat Type**: This is the type of boat the customer is looking to book. Please enter the type of boat (e.g., sailboat, motorboat, etc.).
*   **Boat Length Units**: This field is not used, please ignore it.
*   **Marina Fee**: This field is for the marina fee associated with the slip. Enter the fee as required.
*   **Slip Status**: This field is used to indicate the status of the slip, e.g., available, booked, or reserved. Enter the status as required.
*   **Comments**: This field is for any further comments or information that the customer wants to share with the marina operator.
*   **Payment Method**: This is the method of payment for the slip booking. Please enter the method as required.
*   **Payment Status**: This field is likely used to indicate the status of the payment, e.g., paid, pending, or cancelled. Enter the status as required.
*   **Private Marina Slip Booking Form Additional Comments 2**: This field is for additional comments or information that need to be shared with the marina operator.
*   **Payment Status Label**: This field is not used, please ignore it.
*   **Comments Label**: This field is not used, please ignore it.
*   **Private Marina Slip Booking Form Payment Status 2**: This field is not used, please ignore it.
*   **Confirmation**: This is a confirmation field for the user to verify their submission. Please enter "I understand that the slip booking is subject to marina operator confirmation."
