# Afterpay Furniture Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Afterpay Furniture Order Form is designed to capture essential information from buyers for the purpose of processing and fulfilling their orders accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your **Buyer name** in the text field. This is a required field and should be filled out with your full legal name.
2. Enter your **Email** in the text field. This is a required field and will be used for order updates.
3. Enter a brief description of the **Furniture description** in the text field, including the item name, model, and finish.
4. If applicable, enter the **Dimensions** of the item in the text field. This includes the width, depth, and height.
5. Select the preferred **Color or finish** of the item from the list provided.
6. Choose the **Delivery service** from the options provided. This will determine how you would like your item delivered.
7. Select whether you would like to **Use Afterpay** payment method. If you select "True", you will be redirected to the Afterpay payment page.
8. If the item requires professional setup, select **Installation required**. This will notify the delivery team to take extra care when delivering the item.
9. Finally, enter your **Delivery address** in the text field. This includes the street, city, and postal code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Buyer Name** (Buyer name, text, required): Enter your full legal name.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address to receive order updates.
* **Furniture Description** (Furniture description, text, required): Enter a brief description of the item, including the name, model, and finish.
* **Dimensions** (Dimensions, text, optional): Enter the item's dimensions if applicable.
* **Color or finish** (Color or finish, text, optional): Select the preferred color or finish from the list provided.
* **Delivery Service** (Delivery service, select_one, required): Choose the type of delivery service you would like (Standard, White glove, Curbside).
* **Use Afterpay** (Use Afterpay, select_one, required): Select whether you would like to use Afterpay payment method.
* **Installation Required** (Installation required, select_one, optional): Select if the item requires professional setup.
* **Delivery Address** (Delivery address, text, required): Enter your delivery address, including street, city, and postal code.
