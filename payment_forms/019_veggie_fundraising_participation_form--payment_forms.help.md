# veggie_fundraising_participation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect essential information from participants in a veggie fundraising event. It gathers details about the users, their veggie orders, and their contact preferences. This data will be used to efficiently manage the event and enhance the overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring you provide accurate and relevant information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you're entering the correct data.
3. If you're unsure about any field, contact the event organizers for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your basic contact information, such as your name, email, and phone number. This will help us get in touch with you regarding the event.
* **Veggie Order** (`veggie_order`, text, required/optional): Specify your ordered veggie product or package name. Please provide the correct name as it appears on the packaging or marketing materials.
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, text, required/optional): Tell us how you'd like to be contacted about the event, such as via phone, email, or text. This ensures we reach you effectively and promptly.

## Tips

- Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the event organizers.
- For any issues with the form itself, please contact form@gic.mx.
