# Lease Agreement Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lease Agreement Billing Form is designed to collect payment details from tenants for lease agreements. It's a crucial form for landlords and property managers to process payments accurately and on time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Choose the correct payment category (Rent, Utilities, or Late fees) from the options provided in the "payment_details" field.
2. Select the payment method (Bank Transfer, Online, or Cash) in the "payment_method" field.
3. Enter the rent amount in the "rent_amount" field.
4. Choose the date of payment in the "date_of_payment" field.
5. Optionally, add any additional notes in the "notes" field.
6. Sign and date the form as the tenant by filling out the "tenant_signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **lease_agreement_billing_form**: This is a heading to identify the form, and it's not a field to fill out.
* **agreement_form**: This field is not visible in the form, it seems to be a placeholder or a non-required field.
* **payment_details**: Select one or more payment categories (Rent, Utilities, or Late fees) that you're paying for. This is a crucial field to ensure accurate payment processing.
* **payment_method**: Choose how you'll be making the payment (Bank Transfer, Online, or Cash). This ensures that the payment is processed correctly.
* **rent_amount**: Enter the exact amount of rent or payment you're making. This field is required to process payments accurately.
* **date_of_payment**: Choose the date when you'll be making the payment. This is essential for scheduling payments.
* **notes**: This is an optional field for any additional comments or information about the payment.
* **tenant_signature**: Sign and date this field to confirm that the form is filled out by the tenant.

### Tips
* Make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly to avoid any payment issues.
* Double-check the "payment_details" field to select the correct payment category.
* Ensure that you've selected the correct "payment_method" and "rent_amount" fields to avoid payment processing errors.
* Date and sign the form to confirm that it's filled out by the tenant.
