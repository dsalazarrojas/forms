# Custom Cutting Board Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for customers to enter their details to place a custom cutting board order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
- Fill in the customer name field with your actual name.
- Enter a valid email address in the customer email field.
- Provide your phone number in the customer phone field.
- Choose a date for the order in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- Choose a time for the order in the format HH:MM:SS.
- Submit the form to place your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your actual name.
* **Customer Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address associated with your order.
* **Customer Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Choose a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY for your order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Choose a time in the format HH:MM:SS for your order.

## Tips
- Please make sure to enter accurate information to ensure timely and correct order processing. 
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at your provided email or phone number. 
- Orders placed through this form are subject to our company policies and available inventory.
