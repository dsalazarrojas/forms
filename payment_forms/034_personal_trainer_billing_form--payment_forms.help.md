# Personal Trainer Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects billing information for a personal trainer, ensuring that payment processing is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, follow these steps:
	* Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
	* Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
	* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
	* Enter your billing address in the "Billing Address" field.
	* Enter your city in the "City" field.
	* Select the state from the "State" field.
	* Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
	* Choose a payment method from the "Payment Method" field.
	* Enter your payment card account number in the "Account Number" field, if applicable.
	* Enter your CVV (card verification value) in the "CVV" field, if applicable.
	* Enter the expiration date of your payment card in the "Expiration Date" field.
	* Enter the expiration month and year of your payment card in the "Expiration Month" and "Expiration Year" fields.
	* Select the type of your payment card from the "Card Type" field.
	* If you selected "Other" as your card type, enter the type in the "Other Card Type" field.
	* If you selected a non-standard payment method, enter the type in the "Other Payment Method" field.
	* Enter the expiration time of your payment card in the "Expiration Time" field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Billing Address** (Text, Optional): Enter your billing address.
* **City** (Text, Optional): Enter the city of your billing address.
* **State** (Select One, Optional): Select your state (e.g. Yes, No).
* **Zip** (Text, Optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Payment Method** (Select One, Optional): Choose a payment method (e.g. Card, Bank Transfer).
* **Account Number** (Text, Optional): Enter your payment card account number, if applicable.
* **CVV** (Number, Optional): Enter your payment card CVV, if applicable.
* **Expiration Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the expiration date of your payment card.
* **Expiration Month** (Text, Optional): Enter the expiration month of your payment card.
* **Expiration Year** (Number, Optional): Enter the expiration year of your payment card.
* **Card Type** (Select One, Optional): Select the type of your payment card (e.g. Credit Card, Debit Card, Other).
* **Other Card Type** (Text, Optional): Enter the type of your payment card, if you selected "Other".
* **Other Payment Method** (Text, Optional): Enter the type of your non-standard payment method, if applicable.
* **Expiration Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the expiration time of your payment card, if applicable.
