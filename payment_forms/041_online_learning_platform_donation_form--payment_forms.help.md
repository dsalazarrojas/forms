# online_learning_platform_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect donations for the online learning platform. The form asks for your name, email address, donation amount, donation type, frequency of donation, any comments, preferred payment method, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the form with your name. 
2. Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you for follow-up.
3. Indicate the amount you'd like to donate.
4. Choose the type of donation you'd like to make.
5. Select your preferred donation frequency.
6. Add any comments you'd like to include with your donation.
7. Choose your preferred payment method.
8. Date and time of the donation are optional fields.
9. Finally, enter a unique form ID to help us process your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **donor_name** (donor_name, text, required/optional): Please enter your name as you prefer to be identified.
* **email** (email, email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address to ensure we can contact you.
* **amount** (amount, number, required/optional): Enter the amount you wish to donate.
* **donation_type** (donation_type, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the type of donation you'd like to make. Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
* **frequency** (frequency, select_one, required/optional): Select the frequency of your donation. Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
* **comments** (comments, note, required/optional): Add any comments you'd like to include with your donation.
* **payment_method** (payment_method, select_one, required/optional): Choose your preferred payment method. Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
* **date** (date, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your donation. This is optional.
* **time** (time, time, required/optional): Enter the time of your donation. This is optional.
* **form_id** (form_id, number, required/optional): Enter a unique form ID to help us process your donation.

## Tips

* Please be as accurate as possible when filling out the form to ensure your donation is processed correctly.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
* If you have any questions about the form or the online learning platform, contact our support team for assistance.
