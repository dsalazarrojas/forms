# Guide Dog Program Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users to support their donation to the Guide Dog Program. The purpose of this form is to collect contact information, donation details, and any additional messages the user would like to provide to the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your donation amount in the "Donations" field.
2. Provide your name, email, phone number, and address in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter your city and state where you reside.
4. Enter your zip code.
5. Select the frequency of your donation (e.g., one-time or recurring).
6. If you have a preferred method of communication, you can select it from the "Assigned Form" dropdown list.
7. If you have any additional information or comments, you can enter it in the "Message" field.
8. Finally, you can select the type of output file you would like to receive from the organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donations**: Enter the amount you would like to donate to the Guide Dog Program.
* **Name**: Enter your name as you would like to be referred to by the organization.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached by the organization.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached by the organization.
* **Address**: Enter your physical address where you can be sent any supporting materials.
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State**: Enter the state where you reside.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Message**: Enter any additional information or comments you would like to provide to the organization.
* **Donation Frequency**: Select how often you would like to make a donation (e.g., one-time or recurring).
* **Assigned Form**: Select how you would like to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, or mail).
* **Output File**: Select the type of output file you would like to receive from the organization.
