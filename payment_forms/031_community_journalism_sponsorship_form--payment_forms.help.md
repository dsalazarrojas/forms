# Community Journalism Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Journalism Sponsorship Form is a crucial tool for the newsroom to understand the commitment and preferences of potential sponsors. By filling out this form, sponsors can choose their desired level of commitment, specify their contribution amount, and select how they would like to be recognized. This form helps the newsroom ensure a clear and transparent partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Complete the required fields:
    *   Official Sponsor Name: Enter your business name or individual name for recognition purposes.
    *   Primary Contact Person: Enter the name of the primary contact person.
    *   Contact Email Address: Enter your email address for communication.
    *   Desired Sponsorship Level: Choose your level of commitment: Community Patron, News Sustainer, Reporting Guardian, Local Partner, or Friend of Journalism.
    *   Specific Contribution Amount: Enter your contribution amount.
    *   Date of Commitment: Choose a date for your commitment.
2.  Optional fields:
    *   How should we recognize your sponsorship? Choose how you would like to be recognized: Use individual full name, Use business or organization name, or Keep my sponsorship anonymous.
    *   Specific Journalism Initiative to Support: Choose the area you would like to support: General Local News Operations, Investigative and Long-form Reporting, Arts, Culture, and Community Events, Youth Journalism and Internships, or Environmental and Climate Coverage.
    *   Message to the Newsroom: Enter a message explaining why you're supporting local journalism (optional).
    *   Will you be providing a logo for digital recognition? Choose Yes, will email to newsroom, Yes, will upload to portal, or No logo needed.
    *   I agree to the terms of sponsorship and editorial independence: Choose I Agree and Understand.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Official Sponsor Name (text)**: Enter your business or individual name for recognition purposes. This field is required.
*   **Primary Contact Person (text)**: Enter the name of the primary contact person. This field is required.
*   **Contact Email Address (email)**: Enter your email address for communication. This field is required.
*   **Desired Sponsorship Level (select one)**: Choose your level of commitment: Community Patron, News Sustainer, Reporting Guardian, Local Partner, or Friend of Journalism. This field is required.
*   **Specific Contribution Amount (number)**: Enter your contribution amount. This field is required.
*   **Date of Commitment (date)**: Choose a date for your commitment. This field is required.
*   **How should we recognize your sponsorship? (select one)**: Choose how you would like to be recognized: Use individual full name, Use business or organization name, or Keep my sponsorship anonymous.
*   **Specific Journalism Initiative to Support (select one)**: Choose the area you would like to support: General Local News Operations, Investigative and Long-form Reporting, Arts, Culture, and Community Events, Youth Journalism and Internships, or Environmental and Climate Coverage.
*   **Message to the Newsroom (text)**: Enter a message explaining why you're supporting local journalism (optional).
*   **Will you be providing a logo for digital recognition? (select one)**: Choose Yes, will email to newsroom, Yes, will upload to portal, or No logo needed.
*   **I agree to the terms of sponsorship and editorial independence (select one)**: Choose I Agree and Understand. This field is required to confirm your understanding of the sponsorship terms.
*   **Specific Journalism Initiative to Support (select one)**: Choose the area you would like to support. This field is required.
*   **Logo Upload Indicator (select one)**: Choose how you would like to provide your logo: Yes, will email to newsroom, Yes, will upload to portal, or No logo needed. This field is required for certain levels of sponsorship.
*   **Message to the Newsroom (text)**: Enter a message explaining why you're supporting local journalism (optional). This field is not required.
*   **I agree to the terms of sponsorship and editorial independence (select one)**: Choose I Agree and Understand. This field is required to confirm your understanding of the sponsorship terms.
*   **Recognition Preference (select one)**: Choose how you would like to be recognized: Use individual full name, Use business or organization name, or Keep my sponsorship anonymous. This field is optional but recommended.
*   **Specific Contribution Amount (number)**: Enter your contribution amount. This field is required for the newsroom to accurately record your contribution.
