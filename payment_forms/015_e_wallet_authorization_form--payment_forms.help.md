# E Wallet Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Wallet Authorization Form is used to gather information about customers and their E-Wallets for payment or transaction purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Fill in your Customer ID.
2. Enter your Customer Name.
3. Provide your Customer Email.
4. Enter your Customer Phone number.
5. Enter your E-Wallet ID.
6. Select your E-Wallet Type (e.g., Apple Pay, Google Pay, or PayPal).
7. Enter the Authorization Amount (payment amount).
8. Enter the Authorization Date.
9. Enter the Submission ID (if applicable).
10. Provide any additional Submission Data (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, number, required: false): A unique identifier for the customer.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): The name of the customer.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): The customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): The customer's phone number.
* **E-Wallet ID** (`e_wallet_id`, text, required: false): A unique identifier for the customer's E-Wallet.
* **E-Wallet Type** (`e_wallet_type`, select_one, required: false): The type of E-Wallet (e.g., Apple Pay, Google Pay, or PayPal).
* **Authorization Amount** (`authorization_amount`, number, required: false): The amount to be authorized for payment or transaction.
* **Authorization Date** (`authorization_date`, date, required: false): The date of the authorization or transaction.
* **Submission ID** (`submission_id`, number, required: false): A unique identifier for the submission.
* **Submission Data** (`submission_data`, text, required: false): Any additional data to be submitted with the authorization or transaction.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in each field correctly to avoid errors or missed information.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to your internal documentation or seek assistance from a support team member.
* Double-check your E-Wallet details to ensure accuracy and prevent authorization issues.
