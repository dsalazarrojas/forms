# RV Loan Online Payment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect loan information and payment details for an RV loan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "RV Loan Application Form" section with your loan details.
2. Enter the required fields: "Loan Amount", "Loan Rate", and "Loan Term".
3. Click the "Submit" button to submit your loan application and payment information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **RV Loan Application Form** (text, required): Fill out this form with your loan details.
- **Customer Details** (text, optional): Enter your customer information, including name, email, and phone number.
- **Loan Terms** (text, optional): Select your loan terms.
- **Payment Info** (text, optional): Enter your payment information, including loan amount, rate, and terms.
- **Submit** (text, required): Click this button to submit your loan application and payment information.
- **Loan Amount** (number, required): Enter the amount you are borrowing.
- **Loan Rate** (number, required): Enter the rate of the loan.
- **Loan Term** (number, required): Select the term of the loan.
- **Payment Method** (select_one, optional): Select your payment method.
- **Loan Amount Paid** (number, optional): Enter the amount you paid.
- **Payment Status** (select_one, optional): Select the status of your payment.
- **Loan Status** (select_one, optional): Select the status of your loan.
- **Payment Method Other** (text, optional): Enter other payment method if selected in the "Payment Method" field.
- **Other Payment Method** (text, optional): Enter other payment method if selected in the "Payment Method" field.
- **Date** (date, optional): Select the date of payment.
- **Time** (time, optional): Select the time of payment.
- **Amount** (number, optional): Enter the amount of payment.
- **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Date 2** (date, optional): Select the date of payment.
- **Time 2** (time, optional): Select the time of payment.
- **Amount 2** (number, optional): Enter the amount of payment.
- **Email 2** (email, optional): Enter your email address.
- **Phone 2** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Note** (note, optional): Enter any additional comments or information.

## Tips
This form is designed to collect loan information and payment details for an RV loan. Please fill out all required fields and review your submission carefully before clicking the "Submit" button.
