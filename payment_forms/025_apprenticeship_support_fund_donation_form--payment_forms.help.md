# Apprenticeship Support Fund Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Apprenticeship Support Fund Donation Form is used to collect donations for the Apprenticeship Support Fund. This form gathers essential information from donors, including their contact details, donation amount and type, and preferred payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name and email address.
2. Provide a phone number (optional).
3. If applicable, enter the name of your organization.
4. Enter the donation amount in dollars.
5. Choose the type of donation: one-time, monthly recurring, or annual.
6. Select the program area of interest (optional).
7. Choose your preferred payment method: credit card, bank transfer, check, or wire transfer.
8. Decide if you want to request a tax receipt.
9. If applicable, choose how you would like to be recognized: specify a program or a specific apprentice.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name to be identified as a donor.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address to receive updates and confirmation of your donation.
* **Phone Number**: Provide your phone number for further contact (optional).
* **Organization Name (if applicable)**: If you represent an organization, enter its name.
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount you would like to donate in dollars. Make sure to enter the correct value for the selected donation type.
* **Type of Donation**: Choose between one-time, monthly recurring, or annual donations.
* **Program Area of Interest**: Select an optional field to indicate which program area you are supporting (optional).
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Select how you would like to pay for your donation: credit card, bank transfer, check, or wire transfer.
* **Request Tax Receipt**: Decide if you want to receive a tax receipt for your donation.
* **How Would You Like to Be Recognized**: If applicable, choose how you would like to be recognized for your donation (optional).
