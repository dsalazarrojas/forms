# drone_model_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a pre-order form for customers to reserve a drone model before it's available for sale. To ensure a smooth pre-order process, please fill out all required fields accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Drone Pre-Order Form" title to access the form.
2. Fill out all required fields, denoted by an asterisk (*).
3. Review each field explanation below for guidance on how to fill out each field accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Drone Pre-Order Form** (`pre_order_form`): This is the form title.
* **Name** (`name`): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification. 
* **Email** (`email`): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly. 
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your contact phone number in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX`. 
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your shipping address, including your street, city, and state. 
* **Delivery Info** (`delivery_info`): Select "Yes" if you want to receive delivery updates, or "No" if you prefer not to. 
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`): Enter the date you'd like to receive your drone. 
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`): Enter the time of day you'd like to receive your drone. 
* **Quantity** (`quantity`): Enter the number of drones you'd like to pre-order. 
* **Comments** (`comments`): This is an optional field for you to provide any additional information about your pre-order.
* **Retailer Info** (`retailer_info`): Enter the name of your retailer or seller. 
* **Product Info** (`product_info`): Provide any additional information about the drone model you're pre-ordering. 
* **Delivery Note** (`delivery_note`): If you have any special delivery instructions, enter them here. 
* **Total** (`total`): This field will display automatically once you've completed the form. 
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select how you'd like to pay for your pre-order, either by credit card or bank transfer. 
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`): Select "Paid" once you've completed payment, or "Pending" if payment is still in process. 
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`): Enter the date of payment. 
* **Payment Time** (`payment_time`): Enter the time of payment. 
* **Receipt** (`receipt`): Select "True" if you'd like to receive a receipt, or "False" if not. 
* **Store** (`store`): Enter the name of the store where you'd like to pick up your drone. 
* **Store Address** (`store_address`): Enter the store's address where you'd like to pick up your drone. 
* **Store Phone** (`store_phone`): Enter the store's phone number where you can contact them. 
* **Store Email** (`store_email`): Enter the store's email address where you can contact them. 
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`): Enter any additional information about yourself as a customer. 
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`): This is an optional field for you to provide any additional information about your pre-order.
