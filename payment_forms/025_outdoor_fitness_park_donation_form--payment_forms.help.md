# outdoor_fitness_park_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The outdoor fitness park donation form is designed to collect information and facilitate donations for the park's development and maintenance.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the required personal information fields:
	* First Name: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
	* Last Name: Enter your surname as it appears on your official documents.
	* Email: Enter a valid email address associated with your account.
	* Amount: Enter the amount you'd like to donate, in numerical format.
2. Select your preferred payment method:
	* Payment Method: Choose from the available options (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Payoneer).
3. Specify your donation frequency:
	* Frequency: Select from the available options (Daily, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
	* (Note: This field is optional, you can skip it if you don't want to provide this information.)
4. Leave any additional comments, if you'd like to share any specific thoughts, requests, or concerns.
5. Confirm your intention to donate:
	* Confirm Donation: Select the option that reflects your true intention to donate.
6. Review and agree to the terms and conditions:
	* Donor Information Agreement: Ensure you've read and agree to the terms and conditions by selecting the correct option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1): Your full first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (2): Your full last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (3): A valid email address associated with your account for communication purposes.
* **Amount** (4): The amount you'd like to donate to the outdoor fitness park in numerical format.
* **Payment Method** (5): Choose from the available payment methods to ensure safe and secure transactions.
* **Frequency** (6): Optionally select your preferred frequency of donation for the park's communication and offerings.
* **Comments** (7): Share any specific thoughts, requests, or concerns you'd like to discuss with the park.
* **Confirm Donation** (8): Select this option to confirm your intention to donate to the outdoor fitness park.
* **Donor Information Agreement** (9): Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the donation process to ensure clarity and trust.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check your selection for the payment method and frequency fields.
* Review the terms and conditions carefully before confirming your donation.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact the outdoor fitness park administration.
* This form is for donations only, not for general inquiries or suggestions. If you'd like to provide feedback, please use the park's contact form instead.
