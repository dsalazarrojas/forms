# Invoice Payment Proof Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Invoice Payment Proof Request Form is used to request proof of payment from clients to verify the payment status of an invoice. This form helps to ensure that payments are correctly received and processed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Client Information**: Please enter the client's information, including their name and any other relevant details.
2. **Proof Type**: Select the type of proof you are requesting (e.g., Invoice, Credit Note, Debit Note, etc.).
3. **Proof Date**: Enter the date of the payment proof.
4. **Proof Value**: Enter the total value of the payment proof.
5. **Proof Amount**: Enter the amount paid by the client.
6. **Proof Method**: Select the method used for payment (e.g., Bank Transfer, Cheque, Cash, etc.).
7. **Proof Number**: Enter any relevant proof number or reference number.
8. **Proof Image**: If required, attach a proof image or document supporting the payment proof.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (`client_info`, text, optional): Enter the client's name and any other relevant details.
* **Proof Type** (`proof_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of proof you are requesting (e.g., Invoice, Credit Note, Debit Note, etc.).
* **Proof Date** (`proof_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the payment proof.
* **Proof Value** (`proof_value`, number, optional): Enter the total value of the payment proof.
* **Proof Amount** (`proof_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount paid by the client.
* **Proof Method** (`proof_method`, select_one, optional): Select the method used for payment (e.g., Bank Transfer, Cheque, Cash, etc.).
* **Proof Number** (`proof_number`, text, optional): Enter any relevant proof number or reference number.
* **Proof Image** (`proof_image`, text, optional): If required, attach a proof image or document supporting the payment proof.
