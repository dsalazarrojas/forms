# Charity Badminton Marathon Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for the Charity Badminton Marathon Tournament. It collects essential information about participants, including their contact details, skill level, and medical conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Start by filling out the header section**, which serves as a brief introduction to the form.
2. **Enter your participant information**: Fill in your **participant name** (id: 2) and **team name** (id: 3) if you are part of a team or doubles. If you don't have a team, leave this field blank.
3. **Provide your contact details**: Enter your **email address** (id: 4) and **phone number** (id: 5) for emergency contact and updates.
4. **Select your category**: Choose your **category** (id: 6) as Singles, Doubles, or Mixed doubles.
5. **Assess your skill level**: Indicate your **skill level** (id: 7) as Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
6. **Confirm entry fee status**: Select your **entry fee paid** (id: 8) status as Paid, Pending, or Exempt.
7. **Disclose any medical conditions**: If you have any **medical conditions** (id: 9), please list them here.
8. **Agree to the waiver**: Confirm your acceptance of the **waiver agreement** (id: 10) by selecting "I agree" or "I do not agree".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant name** (id: 2, required, text): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **team name** (id: 3, optional, text): If you are part of a team or doubles, enter the name of your team or partner.
* **email** (id: 4, required, email): Enter your email address for updates and emergency contact.
* **phone** (id: 5, optional, text): Enter your phone number for emergency contact.
* **category** (id: 6, required, select_one): Choose your category as Singles, Doubles, or Mixed doubles.
* **skill level** (id: 7, required, select_one): Choose your skill level as Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **entry fee paid** (id: 8, required, select_one): Indicate your entry fee status as Paid, Pending, or Exempt.
* **medical conditions** (id: 9, optional, text): List any medical conditions the organizers should be aware of.
* **waiver agreement** (id: 10, required, select_one): Confirm your agreement to the event's waiver.

## Tips
* Ensure that you enter accurate and complete information for each field.
* Make sure to review your form before submission to avoid mistakes.
* If you need assistance with any part of this process, please contact the event organizers.
