# Flood Relief Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flood Relief Donation Form is used to collect donations for disaster relief efforts. This form allows individuals to contribute to support the victims of floods and aid in response and recovery efforts.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start by clicking on the "Donor Information" page.
2. In the "Donor Information" page, enter your name and any other relevant contact information in the "donor_info" field.
3. On the next page, enter the amount of the donation you wish to make in the "donation_amount" field. This field is optional, and you can leave it blank if you do not wish to specify a specific amount.
4. Select your donation frequency by choosing from the options "Monthly", "Quarterly", or "One-time" in the "donation_frequency" field.
5. If you have chosen to donate monthly or quarterly, select the corresponding frequency in the "donation_frequency_frequency" field. This will help us to better understand your donation schedule.
6. Choose your payment method from the options "Online", "In-person", or "Other (Specify below)" in the "payment_method" field.
7. If you have chosen "Other (Specify below)", enter your payment method in the "specify_other_payment_method" field.
8. Select whether you would like to make a recurring or one-time gift by choosing "True" or "False" in the "gift_option" field.
9. Enter any additional payment information in the "payment_info" field.
10. If you have chosen to make a recurring gift, enter a note about your gift in the "gift_note" field.
11. Finally, select the type of donation you wish to make from the options "General", "Emergency", or "Both" in the "donation_type" field.
12. Enter the amount of the donated goods or services in the "donation_amount_donated" field, if applicable.
13. Select the frequency of the donation from the options "Monthly", "Quarterly", "One-time" in the "donation_frequency_donated" field.
14. Choose the method of the donation from the options "Online", "In-person", or "Other (Specify below)" in the "donation_method_donated" field.
15. If you have chosen "Other (Specify below)", enter your donation method in the "specify_donation_method_donated" field.
16. Finally, select the type of donation from the options "General", "Emergency", or "Both" in the "donation_type_donated" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Information (donor_info)**: Enter your name and other relevant contact information.
* **Donation Amount (donation_amount)**: Enter the amount of the donation you wish to make.
* **Donation Frequency (donation_frequency)**: Choose from the options "Monthly", "Quarterly", or "One-time".
* **Frequency (donation_frequency_frequency)**: If you have chosen "Monthly" or "Quarterly", select the corresponding frequency.
* **Payment Method (payment_method)**: Choose from the options "Online", "In-person", or "Other (Specify below)".
* **Specify Other Payment Method (specify_other_payment_method)**: If you have chosen "Other (Specify below)", enter your payment method.
* **Gift Option (gift_option)**: Choose "True" for a recurring gift or "False" for a one-time gift.
* **Payment Information (payment_info)**: Enter any additional payment information.
* **Gift Note (gift_note)**: Enter a note about your gift, if applicable.
* **Donation Type (donation_type)**: Choose from the options "General", "Emergency", or "Both".
* **Donation Amount (donation_amount_donated)**: Enter the amount of the donated goods or services.
* **Donation Frequency (donation_frequency_donated)**: Choose from the options "Monthly", "Quarterly", or "One-time".
* **Donation Method (donation_method_donated)**: Choose from the options "Online", "In-person", or "Other (Specify below)".
* **Specify Donated Method (specify_donation_method_donated)**: If you have chosen "Other (Specify below)", enter your donation method.
* **Donation Type (donation_type_donated)**: Choose from the options "General", "Emergency", or "Both".
