# Service Partner Payment Policy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather feedback and insights from service partners regarding their experiences with payment-related issues, such as frequency and types of payment problems, communication on payment matters, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To fill out this form, select the most suitable answer to each question based on your current experiences with your service partner.
2.  Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly, as this information will help us understand your concerns and ideas.
3.  Please note that the questions are designed to be concise and clear, making it easier for you to provide accurate and detailed feedback.
4.  If you have any specific examples or examples of payment issues, feel free to describe them in the text box provided (Field 8).
5.  Your answers will contribute to improving the service partner payment policy, and thus improve your overall service experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What is your current payment policy?** (`service_partner_payment_policy_1`, Text, Optional)
    This question is used to understand the current payment policy that you use.
*   **How often do you have payment issues with your partner?** (`service_partner_payment_2`, Select One, Optional)
    This question helps us understand the frequency of payment issues you encounter with your partner.
*   **What type of payment issues do you usually encounter with your partner?** (`service_partner_payment_3`, Select Multiple, Optional)
    This question aims to identify the types of payment problems you typically face with your partner.
*   **Have you had any payment issues with your partner in the last 6 months?** (`service_partner_payment_4`, Select One, Optional)
    This question is used to determine if you have recently experienced payment problems with your partner.
*   **How would you rate your partner's communication about payment-related issues?** (`service_partner_payment_5`, Select One, Optional)
    This question helps us understand how well your partner communicates about payment-related matters.
*   **How would you rate your partner's communication about payment-related issues in the last 6 months?** (`service_partner_payment_6`, Select One, Optional)
    This question is used to assess the improvement in communication about payment issues over time.
*   **Do you have any suggestions for improving your partner's payment policy?** (`service_partner_payment_7`, Text, Optional)
    This question invites you to share any ideas or recommendations you have for enhancing the payment policy.

## Tips

*   Answer honestly and thoroughly to help improve the service partner payment policy.
*   If you are unsure about a question, choose the option that best describes your experience.
*   If you wish to provide more information on a question, you can do so in the text box provided (Field 8).
