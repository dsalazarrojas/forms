# Factory Floor Cleaning Device Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Floor Cleaning Device Order Form is used to collect order details from customers, including payment information, shipping addresses, contact details, and order notes. This form helps to process orders efficiently and ensure that all necessary information is captured accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the order details on pages 1-3.
2. Select the payment terms and method on pages 4 and 5.
3. Enter the shipping address, including the address lines, city, state, and zip code on pages 6-8.
4. Provide contact information, including phone number and email on pages 9-10.
5. Select the payment status and delivery status on pages 11-12.
6. Enter the payment date and time on pages 13-14.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details (Page 1)**: Enter your order details, including order notes.
* **Order Summary (Page 2)**: This page provides a summary of your order. Please review and ensure it is accurate.
* **Payment Terms (Page 3)**: Select whether you agree to the payment terms.
* **Payment Method (Page 4)**: Select your preferred payment method.
* **Payment Form Page 4 (Page 5)**: This page allows you to select multiple payment terms. Select all that apply.
* **Shipping Address (Page 6)**: Enter your shipping address, including the address lines, city, state, and zip code.
* **Address Line 1 (Page 7)**: Enter your address line 1.
* **City (Page 8)**: Enter your city.
* **State (Page 9)**: Enter your state.
* **Zip (Page 10)**: Enter your zip code.
* **Country (Page 11)**: Enter your country.
* **Phone (Page 12)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Payment Method (Page 13)**: Select your preferred payment method.
* **Payment Terms (Page 14)**: Select multiple payment terms. Select all that apply.
* **Shipping Method (Page 15)**: Select your preferred shipping method.
* **Email (Page 16)**: Enter your email address.
* **Contact Info (Page 17)**: Enter your contact information.
* **Order Notes (Page 18)**: Enter any additional order notes.
* **Order Total (Page 19)**: Enter the total cost of your order.
* **Payment Status (Page 20)**: Select the status of your payment.
* **Delivery Status (Page 21)**: Select the status of your delivery.
* **Payment Date (Page 22)**: Enter the date of your payment.
* **Payment Time (Page 23)**: Enter the time of your payment.

Note: All fields marked as "required" must be filled in for the form to be considered complete.
