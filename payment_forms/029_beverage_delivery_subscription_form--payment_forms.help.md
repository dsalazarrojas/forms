# Beverage Delivery Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beverage Delivery Subscription Form is designed to collect necessary customer information, payment details, and subscription preferences to provide a seamless delivery experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, **Phone Number**, and **Delivery Address** (pages 1-6).
2. Choose your preferred **Subscription Type**, **Beverage Package**, and **Delivery Quantity** (pages 7-9).
3. Select your **Preferred Delivery Day** and **Preferred Delivery Window** (pages 12-13).
4. Select your **Payment Method** and enter your **Card Number** (pages 15-16).
5. If different from your delivery address, fill in your **Billing Address** (page 19).
6. Optionally, you can **Pause Deliveries** when you are away (page 20).
7. Agree to the **Terms and Conditions** (page 21).
8. Provide **Additional Comments** about your subscription (page 22).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (page 1)**: Enter your full name for our records.
* **Email Address (page 2)**: We will use this to send subscription updates and notifications.
* **Phone Number (page 3)**: For delivery coordination and any questions or concerns.
* **Delivery Address (page 4)**: Your address where you want to receive your deliveries.
* **Company Name (page 4)**: If you're a business, enter your company name for our records.
* **Delivery Address (page 4)**: Your address for delivery.
* **City (page 5)**: Your city for delivery purposes.
* **State (page 5)**: Your state for delivery purposes.
* **ZIP Code (page 6)**: Your postal code for delivery.
* **Subscription Type (page 7)**: Choose your preferred subscription plan.
* **Beverage Package (page 8)**: Select your preferred package.
* **Delivery Quantity (page 9)**: Choose how many items per delivery.
* **Preferred Delivery Day (page 12)**: Select a day that works best for you.
* **Preferred Delivery Window (page 13)**: Choose a time that suits you best.
* **Payment Method (page 15)**: Select how you'd like to pay for your subscription.
* **Card Number (page 16)**: Enter your card number for payment.
* **Expiry Date (page 16)**: Enter your card's expiry date in MM/YY format.
* **CVV (page 16)**: Enter your card's 3-digit security code.
* **Billing Address (page 19)**: If different from your delivery address, enter your billing address.
* **Delivery Instructions (page 20)**: Provide any special instructions for the delivery team.
* **Would You Like the Option to Pause Deliveries (page 20)**: Choose if you want to pause deliveries when you are away.
* **I Accept the Terms and Conditions (page 21)**: Agree to the subscription terms and conditions.
* **Additional Comments (page 22)**: Provide any other information about your subscription.

## Tips

- Ensure you fill in all required fields marked with \*.
- Use correct formatting for address and date fields.
- Choose a subscription type and package that suits your needs.
- Select a preferred delivery day and time that works for you.
- Agree to the terms and conditions.
- Be detailed with your additional comments, if needed.
