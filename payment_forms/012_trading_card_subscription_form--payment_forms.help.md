# Trading Card Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information for a trading card subscription service. Fill out the form carefully to provide the necessary details for your subscription.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Follow the step-by-step instructions below to complete the form.
2. Please ensure you fill out all required fields to avoid any issues with your subscription.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (1, text, required): This field is used to collect your name and contact information.
* **Subscription Type** (2, select one, required): Select the type of subscription you wish to purchase. Please choose from the available options (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Shipping Information** (3, text, required): Enter your shipping address where the trading cards will be delivered.
* **Payment Information** (4, text, required): Enter your payment details to complete the subscription process.
* **Subscription Preferences** (5, text, required): Specify any additional preferences or notes about your subscription.
