# Zoo Animal Exhibit Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Zoo Animal Exhibit Donation Form is designed to allow visitors to make a donation to support their favorite animal exhibits and programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your desired donation amount in the **Donation Amount** field.
2. Select your preferred **Donation Frequency** from the provided options.
3. Choose the date for your donation from the calendar.
4. Enter your **Name**, **Email**, and **Phone** (optional) in the respective fields.
5. Select up to 3 **Conservation Program(s)** you'd like to support from the provided options.
6. Provide any additional **Donation Note** if you'd like to include a message with your donation.
7. Choose your preferred **Payment Method** from the provided options.
8. Select up to 3 **Program 1** you'd like to support from the provided options.
9. Select up to 3 **Program 2** you'd like to support from the provided options.
10. Select up to 3 **Program 3** you'd like to support from the provided options.
