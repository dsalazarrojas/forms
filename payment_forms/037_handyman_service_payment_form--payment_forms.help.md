# Handycraft payment form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Handycraft payment form is a supplementary tool for customers to submit payments for various services provided by Handycraft. It's designed to be flexible and adaptable to different types of services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of service you're paying for from the drop-down menu in the "Service Type" field.
2. Specify the amount you're paying, using the "Amount" field.
3. Choose a date and time for the payment or appointment, using the "Date" and "Time" fields. **Note:** These fields are for scheduling purposes, not for specifying the payment's due date.
4. If necessary, provide additional context or comments about the payment in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Type** (Select Multiple, Required: false): Select the type of service you're paying for from the provided drop-down menu.
* **Amount** (Number, Required: false): Specify the total or portion of the payment you're making.
* **Date** (Date, Required: false): Choose a date for the payment or appointment.
* **Time** (Time, Required: false): Select a time for the payment or appointment.
* **Notes** (Notes, Required: false): Provide any additional context or comments about the payment, if needed.

## Tips

* Ensure you have selected the correct service type from the drop-down menu.
* Double-check the amount and date/time fields for accuracy.
* Use the notes field to provide any additional information that may be helpful for processing the payment.
