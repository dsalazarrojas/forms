**Sports Event Ticket Portal Access Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This form is designed for event organizers to manage ticket sales, attendee information, and event details. It is a centralized platform for ticket access and management.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form in sections**: Divide the form into sections for event organizers and attendees.
2. **Provide accurate information**: Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information for each section.
3. **Submit the form**: Once you have filled out all the required sections, click the submit button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Organizer** (1): Enter your event title.
* **Attendee Information** (2): Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself as an attendee.
* **Event Date** (3): Enter the date of the event.
* **Ticket Price** (4): Enter the price of the ticket.
* **Event Location** (5): Enter the location of the event.
* **Ticket Type** (6): Select the type of ticket you want to purchase.
* **Contact Number** (7): Enter your contact number.
* **Attendee Email** (8): Enter your email address.
* **Event Details** (9): Enter any additional event details.
* **Payment Method** (10): Select the payment method you want to use (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Card Number** (11): Enter your credit card number.
* **Payment Card Expiration** (12): Enter your credit card expiration date.
* **Payment Card Security Code** (13): Enter your credit card security code.
* **Payment Bank Name** (14): Enter the name of your bank.
* **Attendee Info** (15): Enter any additional information about yourself as an attendee.
* **Ticket ID** (16): Enter your ticket ID number.
* **Ticket Number** (17): Enter your ticket number.
* **Event ID** (18): Enter the ID of the event.
* **Event Organizer Email** (19): Enter the event organizer's email address.
* **Event Organizer Phone** (20): Enter the event organizer's phone number.
* **Event Organizer Name** (21): Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Address** (22): Enter the address of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Notes** (23): Enter any additional notes from the event organizer.
* **Ticket Status** (24): Select the status of your ticket (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Submit** (25): Confirm that you have filled out the form correctly and want to submit it.

**Tips**

* Review all fields before submitting to ensure that you have entered accurate information.
* Ensure that you have filled out all required fields before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the event organizer for clarification.
