# rv parking spot reservation form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rv parking spot reservation form is designed to collect information from users for a reserved parking spot.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Reservation Details" tab.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Click "Next" to proceed to the next section.
4. Repeat the process for each section: "Additional Guest Details", "Parking Details", "Payment Details", and "Guest Information".
5. Once you've completed all sections, click "Submit" to finalize your reservation.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Reservation Details**: \* 
    - Enter the details of your reservation, including the dates and duration you wish to reserve the spot.
    - Select the spot size you require from the dropdown menu.
- **Additional Guest Details**: \* 
    - Enter any additional guest information if applicable.
- **Parking Details**: \* 
    - Enter any additional parking details if necessary.
- **Payment Details**: \* 
    - Select your payment method and amount from the dropdown menus.
    - Enter the total payment amount.
- **Guest Information**: 
    - Enter your guest's name.
    - Enter your guest's email address.
    - Enter your guest's phone number.
- **Guest Name**: \* 
    - Enter your name as the guest.
- **Guest E-mail**: \* 
    - Enter your email address as the guest.
- **Guest Phone**: \* 
    - Enter your phone number as the guest.
- **Arrival Date**: \* 
    - Enter the date you wish to arrive at the parking spot.
- **Departure Date**: \* 
    - Enter the date you wish to depart from the parking spot.
- **Parking Duration**: \* 
    - Select the duration of your parking spot from the dropdown menu.
- **Spot Size**: \* 
    - Select the size of the parking spot you require from the dropdown menu.
- **Guest Type**: \* 
    - Select the type of guest you are (Individual, Group, or Company).
- **Payment Type**: \* 
    - Select the payment method you wish to use (Credit Card or Bank Transfer).
- **Payment Total**: \* 
    - Select the total payment amount from the dropdown menu.
- **Notes**: \* 
    - Enter any additional notes or comments for your reservation.
