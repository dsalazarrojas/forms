# stripe_sponsor_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Stripe Sponsor Donation Form** is a form designed to collect information for making a one-time or recurring donation to a sponsor using the Stripe payment gateway.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Amount** field with the amount you wish to donate.
2. Select the **Frequency** of your donation from the options provided (Monthly, One-time, or Quarterly).
3. If you selected a frequency other than One-time, fill in the **Frequency Frequency** field with the frequency of the donation (e.g., Monthly).
4. Provide your **Email** address for communication and payment confirmation.
5. Enter your **Phone Number** for any additional contact or payment-related purposes.
6. Add any **Notes** regarding your donation, such as a message or specific details.
7. Select your preferred **Payment Method** from the options provided (Credit Card, Stripe, or PayPal).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Form**: This field is for entering the amount of your donation.
* **Amount**: Enter the amount you wish to donate.
* **Frequency**: Select the frequency of your donation: Monthly, One-time, or Quarterly.
* **Frequency Frequency**: If you selected a frequency other than One-time, enter the frequency of the donation (e.g., Monthly). This field is only required for frequencies other than One-time.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication and payment confirmation.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for any additional contact or payment-related purposes.
* **Notes**: Add any notes or messages regarding your donation.
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method: Credit Card, Stripe, or PayPal.

## Tips
* Please ensure you select the correct frequency and payment method for your donation.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or issues with the form, please contact the sponsor or our support team.
