# Credit Card Payment Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their credit card payment experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose whether or not you had a credit card payment.
2. Rate your payment experience as "Yes" or "No" for each of the following questions:
	* Did you have a card payment?
3. Rate your satisfaction level with your payment experience as "Yes" or "No" for each of the following questions:
	* Was your payment experience satisfactory?
4. Enter the amount you paid (optional).
5. Select how satisfied you were with your payment experience:
	* Satisfied
	* Dissatisfied
6. Add any additional comments about your experience.
7. Enter your email address (optional).
8. Enter your phone number (optional).
9. Provide any feedback about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Card Type**: 
	+ This field is a select-one field that asks if you had a credit card payment.
	+ Please choose "Yes" if you paid with a credit card, "No" otherwise.
* **Payment Experience**: 
	+ This field is a select-one field that asks if your payment experience was satisfactory.
	+ Please choose "Yes" if you're satisfied, "No" otherwise.
* **Transaction Amount**: 
	+ This field is optional and allows you to enter the amount you paid.
	+ If you're unsure about the amount, you can leave this field blank.
* **Customer Satisfaction**: 
	+ This field is a select-one field that asks about your satisfaction level.
	+ Please choose "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied" to rate your experience.
* **Comments**: 
	+ This field is a text field where you can add any additional comments about your experience.
	+ Please be as detailed as possible to help us understand your experience.
* **Email**: 
	+ This field is optional and allows you to enter your email address.
	+ Please enter your email address if you'd like to receive updates or follow-up about your feedback.
* **Phone**: 
	+ This field is optional and allows you to enter your phone number.
	+ Please enter your phone number if you'd like to receive follow-up calls about your feedback.
* **Customer Feedback**: 
	+ This field is a text field where you can provide any additional feedback about your experience.
	+ Please be as detailed as possible to help us understand your experience.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to help us understand your experience.
- If you have any issues or concerns, please let us know in the "Comments" field.
- If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, please enter your email or phone number.
