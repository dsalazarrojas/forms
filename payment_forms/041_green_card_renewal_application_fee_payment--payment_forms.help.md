# Green Card Renewal Application Fee Payment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect payment information for green card renewal applications. It is intended for customers or individuals who are renewing their green card and need to provide payment details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure that your payment information is collected correctly.
2. Select the payment method you wish to use (e.g., Cash, Check, Credit Card, PayPal, Eft).
3. Enter the date of payment.
4. Enter any comments or notes about your payment (optional).
5. Review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Green Card Renewal Application Fee Payment** (2, number, required): This is the payment amount for the green card renewal application.
* **Date of Payment** (3, date, required): Enter the date you made the payment. This is used to track the payment history.
* **Payment Method** (4, select_one, required): Select one of the available payment methods (Cash, Check, Credit Card, PayPal, Eft).
* **Comments** (5, text, optional): If you have any comments or notes about your payment, you can enter them here. This is not required but can be helpful for further reference.
* **Form Footer** (6, note, not required): This is not a required field and is intended for any additional information or notes that the user may want to add.
