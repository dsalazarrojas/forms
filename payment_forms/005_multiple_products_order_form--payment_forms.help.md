# Multiple Products Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Multiple Products Order Form is used to gather information for placing multiple product orders. This form is designed to be completed by customers who want to purchase multiple products from our store.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the products you would like to order by choosing from the list of options for each product field.
2. Provide the customer details, including email address, phone number, and address.
3. Review your order to ensure accuracy before submitting.
4. Click the "Submit Order" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order details**: This field is the main section of the form where you will select the products you want to order. Select the products you are interested in and review the options carefully.
* **Customer details**: This field is where you enter the customer's email address, phone number, and address. Make sure to enter the correct information to ensure accurate order processing.
* **Shipping details**: (Note: This field is not visible in the provided YAML and appears to be a non-existent field. It's possible that the field was not included in the YAML, or its options/label were not specified. To be conservative, it will be omitted from this help guide.)
* **Payment terms**: This field is not currently used in the form, as it does not have a corresponding "Payment terms" field in the YAML inputSchema. (Note: If a "Payment terms" field were to be present, it would likely be used to specify any payment conditions or requirements.)
* **Submit order**: This is the final step to complete the form. Once you have reviewed your order and entered all the necessary information, click this button to submit your order.
* **Order status**: This field is not visible in the provided YAML and appears to be a non-existent field. It is likely an error or omission in the inputSchema.
* **Order notes**: This field is a text field where you can enter any additional comments or notes about your order. It is not required but can be useful for providing further information to our staff.
* **Customer email**: Enter the customer's email address here. This is a required field for order processing.
* **Customer phone**: Enter the customer's phone number here. This is not a required field but entering it can be helpful for communication purposes.
* **Customer address**: Enter the customer's address here. This is a required field for order processing.
* **Product 1-15**: Select the products you would like to order from the list of options for each product field. Each product field is a required field and must be filled out for order processing to be successful.
