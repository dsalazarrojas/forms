# Food Relief Donation Form (Conflict Zones) - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who wish to donate to support food relief efforts in conflict zones around the world.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the conflict zones you wish to support by choosing from the options provided in the "Conflict Zones" field.
2. Enter the amount you wish to donate in the "Amount Donated" field. You can enter a numerical value.
3. Choose your preferred payment method from the options provided in the "Payment Method" field.
4. Enter the date and time of your donation in the "Date Donated" and "Time Donated" fields, respectively.
5. If you have any additional comments or notes about your donation, enter them in the "Donation Note" field.
6. Provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number, in the "Email" and "Phone" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Conflict Zones** (`Conflict Zones`, select multiple, required): Select the conflict zones you wish to support. You can choose one or multiple options.
* **Amount Donated** (`Amount Donated`, number, required): Enter the amount you wish to donate. You can enter a numerical value, e.g., 10, 20, 50.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select one, required): Choose your preferred payment method. Options include Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal.
* **Date Donated** (`Date Donated`, date, required): Enter the date you wish to make your donation. The format is mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time Donated** (`Time Donated`, time, required): Enter the time you wish to make your donation. The format is hh:mm AM/PM.
* **Donation Note** (`Donation Note`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or notes about your donation, enter them here. This is an optional field and can be left blank.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted about your donation or have further questions.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted about your donation or have further questions.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
