# Health Insurance Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect billing information for health insurance claims. It is intended for healthcare providers to input patient data to facilitate the submission of accurate and timely claims. The form is part of the billing process and helps ensure that all necessary details are included.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Enter the patient's ID number (page 1).
3. Provide the patient's full name (page 2).
4. Select the patient's insurance plan (page 5) from the available options.
5. Enter the billing address (page 6) if necessary.
6. Enter the primary care provider's name (page 7) if necessary.
7. Enter the patient's medical record number (page 8).
8. Enter the patient's phone number (page 9).
9. Enter the patient's email address (page 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Id** (page 1, number, required): Enter the patient's ID number as assigned by the healthcare provider or insurance company.
* **Name** (page 2, text, required): Enter the patient's full name, including first and last names.
* **Date Of Birth** (page 3, date, optional): Enter the patient's date of birth, including the month and year of birth. This information will help with demographic analysis.
* **Patient Id** (page 4, number, required): Enter the same patient's ID number as assigned by the healthcare provider or insurance company. Ensure it matches the ID entered in page 1.
* **Insurance Plan** (page 5, select_one, required): Select the patient's insurance plan from the available options: Medicare or Tricare.
* **Billing Address** (page 6, text, optional): Enter the address used for billing purposes, if different from the patient's home address.
* **Primary Care Provider** (page 7, text, optional): Enter the name of the primary care provider, if the patient has one.
* **Medical Record Number** (page 8, text, required): Enter the patient's medical record number as assigned by the healthcare provider or medical facility.
* **Phone** (page 9, text, required): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Email** (page 10, email, required): Enter the patient's email address.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Check for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting the form.
