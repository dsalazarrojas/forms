# Program Agreement And Financial Disclosure - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain participant acknowledgment and financial disclosure related to a program agreement. It is intended to ensure that participants understand their obligations and responsibilities under the program terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the **Participant Acknowledgments** field on page 1.
2.  Enter your acknowledgment and any other relevant comments or concerns.
3.  Proceed to the **Program Terms** field on page 1.
4.  Carefully read and review the terms of the program.
5.  Enter any necessary comments or questions regarding the terms.
6.  Proceed to the **Financial Disclosures** field on page 1.
7.  Enter any financial information or disclosures required by the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Acknowledgments**: This is a text field where you can acknowledge your understanding and acceptance of the program's terms and conditions. Enter your acknowledgment and any other relevant comments or concerns.
* **Program Terms**: This is a text field where you can review and understand the terms and conditions of the program. Enter any comments or questions you may have regarding the terms.
* **Financial Disclosures**: This is a text field where you can enter any financial information or disclosures required by the program.
