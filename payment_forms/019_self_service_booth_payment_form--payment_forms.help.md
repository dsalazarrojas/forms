# Self-service Booth Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The self-service booth payment form is a tool for customers to provide payment information for items they have already chosen to purchase. This form ensures that customers can complete their purchases with ease and securely provide necessary payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the self-service booth payment form, follow these steps:

1. Select the payment method (yes or no)
2. Provide your customer name
3. Enter your customer email address
4. Enter your customer phone number
5. Enter your customer street address
6. Enter your customer city
7. Enter your customer state
8. Enter your customer ZIP code
9. Description of the item(s) purchased
10. Amount of the purchase

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **payment_method** (select_one): Choose whether to pay "Yes" or "No" for this purchase.
- **customer name** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the customer.
- **customer email** (email, required: false): Enter the customer's email address for contact and payment verification.
- **customer phone** (text, required: false): Enter the customer's phone number.
- **customer street** (text, required: false): Enter the customer's street address.
- **customer city** (text, required: false): Enter the customer's city.
- **customer state** (text, required: false): Enter the customer's state.
- **customer zip** (text, required: false): Enter the customer's ZIP code.
- **item description** (text, required: false): Describe the item(s) purchased.
- **purchase amount** (number, required: false): Enter the total amount of the purchase. 

**Tips:**
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete payment information to avoid any issues with the payment processing.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for help or contact our customer support for assistance.
* If you have already filled out this form for the same purchase, please do not re-submit it. A new payment transaction will be processed each time you fill out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to our customer support team for assistance.
