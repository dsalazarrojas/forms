## Court Rental Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Court Rental Booking Form is designed to collect information from users who want to rent a court for a specific time period. The form helps the court staff to manage the booking of their facilities effectively and provide a smooth experience for their clients.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Please fill out the form with your information accurately**. Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure that your booking is processed correctly.
- **Review the form carefully before submitting**. Double-check your information for any mistakes or errors.
- **Submit the form to book the court**. Once you submit the form, the court staff will review your booking request and confirm your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Court Rental Booking Form** (`court_rental_booking_form`, Text, Optional): This is the main form title that helps identify the form's purpose.
* **User Information** (`user_info`, Text, Optional): Provide your name and contact details so that the court staff can contact you regarding your booking.
* **Court Information** (`court_info`, Text, Optional): Enter the court's name and other relevant details to help the court staff identify the correct court for your booking.
* **Reservation Time** (`reservation_time`, Time, Optional): Choose the time slot you want to book the court. Please note that this field is not required, so you can leave it blank if you're not sure of the exact time.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the payment method you'd like to use to pay for your court booking. You can choose from:
	+ Credit/Debit Card
	+ Bank Transfer
	+ Other (Please specify in the payment_gateway field)
* **Payment Gateway** (`payment_gateway`, Text, Optional): If you've selected "Other" in the payment method field, provide the name of your payment gateway or method here.
* **Payment Gateway** is not strictly necessary to fill out if you're paying through another method.
 
## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
