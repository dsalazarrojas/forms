# Monthly Fee Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to calculate and record monthly fees for different categories and purposes. It is crucial to fill in accurate and detailed information to ensure correct assessments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form title in the field labeled "form_title".
2. Select the category for the fee in the field labeled "form_category".
3. Provide a brief description of the fee in the field labeled "form_description".
4. Enter the tool assigned to the fee in the field labeled "form_assigned_tool".
5. Enter the month of the assessment in the format MM/DD (e.g., 01/2024).
6. Enter the year of the assessment.
7. For the "form_frequency" fields, enter the frequency of the payment (e.g., monthly, quarterly, annually). Please note that there are two "form_frequency" fields, but only the first one is required. You can fill in both if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_title** (Required, Text): Enter a brief title for the form.
* **form_category** (Required, Text): Select a category for the fee (e.g., membership, subscription).
* **form_description** (Required, Text): Provide a short description of the fee.
* **form_assigned_tool** (Required, Text): Enter the tool assigned to the fee.
* **form_month** (Required, Date): Enter the month of the assessment in the format MM/DD (e.g., 01/2024).
* **form_year** (Optional, Number): Enter the year of the assessment.
* **form_frequency** (Required, Text): Enter the frequency of the payment (e.g., monthly, quarterly, annually). Please note that this field is repeated, but only the first instance is required.
* **form_frequency** (Optional, Text): This field can be filled in if you need to specify additional frequency information.
