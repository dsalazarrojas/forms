# Film Special Edition Pre-Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Film Special Edition Pre-Order Form is designed to collect information from customers for pre-ordering a special edition film product. This form is intended for customers who are interested in pre-ordering a special edition film product and want to ensure that their order is processed accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your email address in the "User Email" field. This field is used to contact you if there are any issues with your order.
2.  Enter your order ID in the "Order ID" field. If you do not have an order ID, you can leave this field blank.
3.  Enter the price of the product you want to pre-order in the "Product Price" field.
4.  Enter the quantity of the product you want to pre-order in the "Quantity" field.
5.  Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu. You can choose from Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, or PayPal.
6.  Enter your card number in the "Card Number" field.
7.  Enter your billing address in the "Billing Address 1" and "Billing Address 2" fields.
8.  Select your city of residence from the "Billing City" dropdown menu. You can choose from New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, San Francisco, Austin, Dallas, Houston, or Seattle.
9.  If you have any additional notes or comments, you can enter them in the "Buyer Notes" field.
10.  Select the version of the film you want to pre-order from the "Film Version" dropdown menu. You can choose from 4K, 1080p, 3D, 2K, or HD.
11.  Select your preferred shipping type from the "Shipping Type" dropdown menu. You can choose from Standard, Priority, Rush, or None.
12.  Enter the shipping price in the "Shipping Price" field.
13.  Enter the tax rate in the "Tax Rate" field.
14.  Enter the total price in the "Total Price" field.
15.  Enter your phone number in the "Buyer Phone" field.
16.  Enter your name in the "Buyer Name" field.
17.  Select the payment status of your order from the "Payment Status" dropdown menu. You can choose from Paid, Partial, or Unpaid.
18.  Select the order status from the "Order Status" dropdown menu. You can choose from Confirmed, Unconfirmed, Cancelled, or In Progress.
