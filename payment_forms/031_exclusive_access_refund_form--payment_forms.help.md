# Exclusive Access Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers request a refund for an order that may not have been fulfilled as expected. 
Fill out this form to initiate the refund process, which will be reviewed and processed according to the store's refund policy.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name in the **Name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
3. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
4. Briefly explain the reason for your refund request in the **Reason For Refund** field.
5. Enter the refund amount you are requesting in the **Refund Amount** field.
6. Enter the date of your original payment in the **Payment Date** field.
7. Enter the order number associated with your refund request in the **Order Number** field.
8. Enter the date of your original order in the **Order Date** field.
9. If you have a document supporting your refund request, you can upload it in the **Upload Document** field (optional).
10. Optionally, you can provide any additional information or comments about your refund request in the **Review** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your account or in your order.
* **Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address associated with your order.
* **Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Reason For Refund** (`reason_for_refund`, text, required): Briefly explain why you are requesting a refund.
* **Refund Amount** (`refund_amount`, number, required): Enter the exact amount you are requesting as a refund.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your original payment.
* **Order Number** (`order_number`, text, required): Enter the number of your original order.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your original order.
* **Upload Document** (`upload_document`, text, optional): Upload any supporting documents related to your refund request.
* **Review** (`review`, note, optional): Provide any additional information or comments about your refund request.
