# Payment Processing Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool to help our team identify potential partners for our payment processing services. By providing us with essential details about your partners and their payment processing needs, we can assess their suitability and potential for collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your partner's name in the **Partner Name** field.
2. Provide the contact person's name in the **Contact Person** field.
3. Enter the partner's **Phone Number** and **Email** for our team to communicate with them.
4. Select the type of **Partner Type** that best describes your partner's business or organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the full name of your partner.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person we should contact for more information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your partner's phone number for us to call them back.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your partner's email address so we can contact them.
* **Partner Type** (`partner_type`, text, required/optional): Choose the category that best describes your partner's business or organization.
