# Pay What You Can Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register for a payment plan. Users who are interested in contributing financially can fill out this form to express their willingness and ability to pay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Choose whether you would like to register by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
2. If you have selected "Yes" in the previous step, enter the amount you are willing to pay in the "payment_amount" field.
3. Enter your name in the "Your Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. If necessary, enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Register** (`Register`, select_multiple, Required): Choose whether you would like to register for the payment plan by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **payment_amount** (`payment_amount`, number, Optional): If you have selected "Yes" in the "Register" field, enter the amount you are willing and able to pay.
* **Your Name** (`Your Name`, text, Optional): Enter your name so we can acknowledge your contribution.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, Optional): If you have a phone number, enter it so we can contact you.

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill out the form accurately, as the information provided will be used to determine the payment plan's success.
* If you have already registered, do not fill out this form again.
* If you are unable to pay the amount specified, please let us know so we can discuss alternatives.
