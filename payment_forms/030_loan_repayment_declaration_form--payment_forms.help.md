# loan_repayment_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The loan repayment declaration form is used to document the repayment terms and schedule of a loan that has already been approved and is currently being repaid. It is essential for both borrowers and lenders to have access to this form to ensure they understand their respective responsibilities and obligations towards each other.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the loan repayment declaration in the **loan_repayment_declaration** field.
2. Provide information about the lender in the **lender_info** field.
3. Enter the payment schedule in the **payment_schedule** field.
4. Specify the loan terms in the **loan_terms** field.
5. Enter the loan amount in the **loan_amount** field.
6. Enter the loan interest rate in the **loan_interest_rate** field.
7. Select the loan repayment plan in the **loan_repayment_plan** field.
8. Enter the date when the loan repayment will start in the **loan_repayment_start_date** field.
9. Enter the date when the loan repayment will end in the **loan_repayment_end_date** field.
10. Provide the lender's contact information in the **lender_contact_info** field.
11. Provide the borrower's contact information in the **borrower_contact_info** field.
12. Attach relevant loan documents in the **loan_documents** field.
13. Add any additional notes about the loan in the **loan_notes** field.
14. Enter the loan agreement details in the **loan_agreement** field.
15. Enter the date and time when the loan was signed in the **loan_sign_date** and **loan_sign_time** fields.
16. Enter the name of the person who signed the loan in the **loan_signed_by** field.
17. Enter the date and time when the loan was reviewed in the **loan_reviewed_by** field.
18. Enter the date and time when the loan was signed by the lender in the **loan_signed_by_date** and **loan_signed_by_time** fields.
19. Enter the lender's signature in the **lender_signature** field.
20. Enter the borrower's signature in the **borrower_signature** field.
21. Enter the date when the loan was signed in the **loan_signed_date** field.
22. Enter the name of the person who signed the loan in the **loan_signed_by** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **loan_repayment_declaration**: Enter a detailed description of the loan repayment terms and schedule.
* **lender_info**: Provide information about the lender, such as name, address, and contact details.
* **payment_schedule**: Enter the payment schedule for the loan, including the frequency and amount of payments.
* **loan_terms**: Describe the loan terms, including any conditions or restrictions.
* **loan_amount**: Enter the total amount of the loan.
* **loan_interest_rate**: Enter the interest rate of the loan.
* **loan_repayment_plan**: Select the loan repayment plan from the available options.
* **loan_repayment_start_date**: Enter the date when the loan repayment will start.
* **loan_repayment_end_date**: Enter the date when the loan repayment will end.
* **lender_contact_info**: Provide contact information for the lender, such as phone number and email.
* **borrower_contact_info**: Provide contact information for the borrower, such as phone number and email.
* **loan_documents**: Attach any relevant loan documents, such as contracts or agreements.
* **loan_notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the loan.
* **loan_agreement**: Enter the details of the loan agreement.
* **loan_sign_date**: Enter the date when the loan was signed.
* **loan_sign_time**: Enter the time when the loan was signed.
* **loan_signed_by**: Enter the name of the person who signed the loan.
* **loan_reviewed_by**: Enter the name of the person who reviewed the loan.
* **loan_signed_by_date**: Enter the date when the loan was signed by the lender.
* **loan_signed_by_time**: Enter the time when the loan was signed by the lender.
* **lender_signature**: Enter the lender's signature.
* **borrower_signature**: Enter the borrower's signature.
* **loan_signed_date**: Enter the date when the loan was signed (repeated field, please use the **loan_signed_by_date** field instead).
* **loan_signed_by**: Enter the name of the person who signed the loan (repeated field, please use the **loan_signed_by** field instead).

Note: The repeated fields (**loan_signed_date**, **loan_signed_by**) should not be used as they are duplicates of the **loan_sign_date** and **loan_signed_by** fields respectively. Please use the correct fields to avoid confusion.
