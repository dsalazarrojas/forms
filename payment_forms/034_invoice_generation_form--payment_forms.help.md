# Invoice Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Invoice Generation Form is used to create a comprehensive invoice for a customer or client, capturing their billing and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the billing address and contact person fields with the relevant details.
- Enter the customer's email address and phone number in the email and phone fields, respectively.
- Enter the total amount, tax rate, and tax amount fields with the corresponding values.
- Select the payment method (Bank Transfer, Check, PayPal, or Credit Card) and specify the payment date and time.
- Add any additional notes or comments to the invoice in the invoice note field.
- Finally, enter the invoice number and the billing company name and address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Billing Address** (billing_address): Enter the billing address of the customer or client.
* **Contact Person** (contact_person): Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the billing.
* **Email** (email): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone** (phone): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Total** (total): Enter the total amount of the invoice.
* **Tax Rate** (tax_rate): Enter the tax rate as a percentage (e.g., 10%).
* **Tax Amount** (tax_amount): Enter the tax amount calculated based on the total and tax rate.
* **Payment Method** (payment_method): Select the payment method (Bank Transfer, Check, PayPal, or Credit Card).
* **Payment Date** (payment_date): Enter the date of the payment.
* **Payment Time** (payment_time): Enter the time of the payment.
* **Invoice Note** (invoice_note): Add any additional notes or comments to the invoice.
* **Invoice Number** (invoice_number): Enter a unique invoice number.
* **Billing Company** (billing_company): Enter the name of the billing company.
* **Company Tax ID** (company_tax_id): Enter the tax ID of the billing company.
* **Company Address** (company_address): Enter the address of the billing company.
