# mental_health_billing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health billing form is used to gather and track information for mental health services and procedures, ensuring accurate and efficient billing and payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Select a Category**: Choose the relevant category for the mental health service or procedure.
2. **Provide a Description**: Enter a detailed description of the service or procedure provided.
3. **Identify Assigned Tool**: Select the tool or system used for the service or procedure.
4. **Specify Output File**: Enter the name of the output file related to the service or procedure.
5. **Enter Title**: Provide a title for the mental health service or procedure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Category**: A text field to select the category of the mental health service or procedure.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: No
  - Purpose: Select the relevant category for the service or procedure.
- **Description**: A text field for a detailed description of the service or procedure.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: No
  - Purpose: Enter a clear and detailed description of the service or procedure provided.
- **Assigned Tool**: A text field to select the tool or system used for the service or procedure.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: No
  - Purpose: Identify the tool or system used for the service or procedure.
- **Output File**: A text field to enter the name of the output file related to the service or procedure.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: No
  - Purpose: Specify the output file related to the service or procedure.
- **Title**: A text field to provide a title for the mental health service or procedure.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: No
  - Purpose: Enter a title for the service or procedure.
