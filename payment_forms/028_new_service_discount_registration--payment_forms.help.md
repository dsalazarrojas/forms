# new_service_discount_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register a new service discount. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields: 
    - **Service Name**: A unique name for the service discount.
    - **Customer Name**: The name of the customer or end-user of the service discount.
    - **Email**: A valid email address associated with the customer.
    - **Service Type**: The type of service associated with the discount.
    - **Discount Amount**: The amount of discount to be applied.
    - **Start Date**: The date when the discount starts to be applied.
    - **End Date**: The date when the discount ends to be applied.
2. Fill in the optional fields:
    - **Phone**: A phone number associated with the customer.
    - **Description**: A brief description of the service discount.
    - **Agreement**: A text field for any additional agreements or terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Name**: A text field for the name of the service.
* **Customer Name**: A text field for the name of the customer or end-user.
* **Phone**: A text field for the phone number associated with the customer.
* **Email**: A text field for a valid email address associated with the customer.
* **Service Type**: A text field for the type of service associated with the discount.
* **Discount Amount**: A numerical field for the amount of discount to be applied.
* **Start Date**: A date field for the date when the discount starts to be applied.
* **End Date**: A date field for the date when the discount ends to be applied.
* **Description**: A text field for a brief description of the service discount.
* **Agreement**: A text field for any additional agreements or terms.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete.
* This form will not affect existing service discounts.
* If you need help with this form or have any questions, please contact us.
