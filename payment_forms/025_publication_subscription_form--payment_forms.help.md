# Publication Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Publication Subscription Form is a tool for users to provide information about their subscription to a publication. This form is used for both internal and external purposes, such as managing subscriptions for users within an organization or for customers to sign up for a publication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Provide your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email Address.
4. Select your chosen Payment Plan.
5. Choose your preferred Payment Method.
6. Enter the amount you wish to pay.
7. Choose your preferred payment frequency.
8. Select the start and end dates of your subscription.
9. Add any additional notes about your subscription (optional).
10. Select the tool assigned to you for this subscription (optional).
11. Select the category for your subscription (optional).
12. Add a brief description of your subscription (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (`first_name_label`, Text, Not Required)
Enter your first name here. 

### Last Name (`last_name_label`, Text, Not Required)
Enter your last name here. 

### Email Address (`email_label`, Email, Not Required)
Enter your email address here, in the format `username@domain.com`.

### Payment Plan (`plan_selection_label`, Select One, Not Required)
Select the payment plan that suits your needs.

### Payment Method (`payment_method_label`, Select Multiple, Not Required)
Choose the payment method you prefer, such as bank transfer or credit card.

### Payment Amount (`payment_amount_label`, Number, Not Required)
Enter the amount you wish to pay.

### Payment Date (`payment_date_label`, Date, Not Required)
Select the date you wish to make the payment.

### Payment Time (`payment_time_label`, Time, Not Required)
Select the time you wish to make the payment.

### Payment Frequency (`payment_frequency_label`, Select One, Not Required)
Choose how often you want to make payments.

### Subscription Start Date (`subscription_start_date_label`, Date, Not Required)
Select the start date of your subscription.

### Subscription End Date (`subscription_end_date_label`, Date, Not Required)
Select the end date of your subscription.

### Notes (`notes_label`, Note, Not Required)
Add any additional information about your subscription here.

### Assigned Tool (`assigned_tool_label`, Text, Not Required)
Select the tool assigned to you for this subscription.

### Category (`category_label`, Select One, Not Required)
Choose the category for your subscription.

### Description (`description_label`, Text, Not Required)
Add a brief description of your subscription here.
