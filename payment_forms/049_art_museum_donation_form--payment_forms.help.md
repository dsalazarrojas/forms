# Art Museum Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from donors about their charitable donation to the Art Museum. It includes fields for donor contact information, donation details, and payment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Begin by filling out the donor's contact information, including their full name, email address, and phone number.
2.  Next, enter the donation details, such as the donation amount, frequency (one-time, monthly, or annual), and the specific fund to which the donation should be applied.
3.  Then, choose the preferred payment method and, if applicable, select the option to have the donation matched by the donor's employer.
4.  Finally, provide any additional information regarding the recognition preferences and the date of the donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Donor Full Name, text, required): Please enter your full name as it should appear in our recognition materials.
* **Email Address** (Donor Email, email, required): Enter your email address for your donation receipt.
* **Phone Number** (Donor Phone, text, optional): Your phone number may be required for tax-deductible receipts.
* **Mailing Address** (Donor Address, text, required): Enter your mailing address for tax-deductible receipts.
* **Donation Amount** (Donation Amount, select_one, required): Select one of the available options for your donation amount.
*   *If Other Amount is selected, please specify the amount in the text field below.*
* **Specify Other Amount** (Other Donation Amount, number, optional): Only required if selecting Other Amount above.
* **Donation Frequency** (Donation Frequency, select_one, required): Choose whether this is a one-time donation, monthly recurring, or annual recurring donation.
* **Where would you like your donation to go?** (Designation of Funds, select_one, required): Select the specific fund to which you would like your donation applied.
* **Name as it should appear in recognition materials** (Recognition Name, text, required): Enter the name as you would like it to appear in our recognition materials.
* **Is this donation in honor or memory of someone?** (In Honor or Memory of, text, optional): If this donation is in honor or memory of someone, please provide their name.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method, such as credit card or PayPal.
* **Will your employer match this donation?** (Company Match, select_one, optional): If your employer offers a matching donation program, select True.
* **Company Name for Matching** (Matching Company Name, text, optional): If your employer offers a matching donation program, provide their name.
* **Date of Donation** (Donation Date, date, required): Enter the date of the donation.
