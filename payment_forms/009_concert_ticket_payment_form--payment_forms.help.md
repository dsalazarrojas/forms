# Concert Ticket Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect payment details from customers for concert tickets. It ensures that you provide accurate information for a successful and secure payment transaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your **Payer full name** in the "Payer full name" field. This is the name that will be associated with the payment method.
2. Enter a valid **Email for receipt** in the "Email for receipt" field. This email will receive the payment confirmation.
3. Enter the **Order ID** from the ticket order in the "Order ID" field.
4. Enter the **Payment amount** in the "Payment amount" field. This is the total amount to be charged in USD.
5. Select the **Payment method** from the options provided, such as Credit card, Debit card, PayPal, or Bank transfer.
6. If required, enter your **Billing address** in the "Billing address" field for billing purposes.
7. If required, enter your **Contact phone** number in the "Contact phone" field for payment queries.
8. If applying, enter the **Promo code** in the "Promo code applied" field.
9. Read and acknowledge the **Fraud prevention notice** to ensure safe payments.
10. If necessary, provide **Additional notes** in the "Additional notes" field for any payment-related comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payer full name** (`payer_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name associated with the payment method.
* **Email for receipt** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email to receive payment confirmation.
* **Order ID** (`order_id`, `text`, required): Enter the reference from the ticket order.
* **Payment amount** (`amount`, `number`, required): Enter the total charged amount in USD.
* **Payment method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment method used, such as Credit card, Debit card, PayPal, or Bank transfer.
* **Billing address** (`billing_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the street address for billing purposes, if required.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number for payment queries, if required.
* **Promo code applied** (`promo_code`, `text`, optional): Enter the promotional code applied, if applying.
* **Fraud prevention notice** (`fraud_notice`, `note`, optional): Read and acknowledge the guidance for safe payments.
* **Additional notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional payment-related comments, if necessary.
