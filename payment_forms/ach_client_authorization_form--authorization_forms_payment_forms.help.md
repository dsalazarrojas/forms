# ACH Client Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ACH Client Authorization Form is designed for clients to authorize recurring payments from their bank accounts. This form outlines the terms and conditions of these payments, ensuring both the client and the payment processor are on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as it appears on your bank account.
2. Provide your email address, which will be used for sending payment confirmations.
3. Enter your phone number (including area code).
4. Provide your billing address (street address, city, state, and ZIP code).
5. Specify the amount to be debited from your account.
6. Choose the payment frequency (one-time, weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, quarterly, or annually).
7. Enter the first payment date.
8. If applicable, enter the end date for recurring payments.
9. Enter the name of your financial institution (bank).
10. Provide the 9-digit ABA routing number for your bank.
11. Enter your account number.
12. Select the type of account to debit (checking or savings).
13. Check the box to accept the terms and conditions.
14. Type your full name as an electronic signature.
15. Enter the current date.
16. Optionally, provide any additional comments or instructions for the payment processor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (Full name, text, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your bank account.
* **Email address** (Email address, email, required): We'll use this to send payment confirmations.
* **Phone number** (Phone number, phone, optional): Include area code.
* **Billing address** (Billing address, text, required): Street address, city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Amount to be charged** (Amount to be charged, decimal, required): Enter the total amount to be debited from your account.
* **Payment frequency** (Payment frequency, select_one, required): Choose how often this payment will occur (one-time, weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, quarterly, or annually).
* **First payment date** (First payment date, date, required): When should the first payment be processed?
* **End date for recurring payments (if applicable)** (End date for recurring payments (if applicable), date, optional): Leave blank if this is a one-time payment.
* **Bank name** (Bank name, text, required): The name of your financial institution.
* **Routing number** (Routing number, text, required): 9-digit ABA routing number for your bank.
* **Account number** (Account number, text, required): Your checking or savings account number.
* **Account type** (Account type, select_one, required): Select the type of account to debit (checking or savings).
* **Terms and authorization** (Terms and authorization, note, required): Check this box to authorize the payment.
* **Electronic signature** (Electronic signature, text, required): Type your full name as an electronic signature.
* **Date of signature** (Date of signature, date, required): Enter the current date.
* **Additional comments or special instructions** (Additional comments or special instructions, text, optional): Any additional information for the payment processor.
