# Airport Transfer Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Airport Transfer Quotation Form is designed to collect information from clients to generate a quote for airport transfer services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields as accurately as possible. Make sure to provide your email address and phone number to ensure smooth communication. The trip details section is optional but provides valuable information for the quotation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name* (text):** Please enter your full name as it appears on your passport or travel documents.
* **Company Name (Optional)**: Enter your company name if applicable.
* **Email Address* (email):** Enter your email address for sending the quote and any follow-up communication.
* **Phone Number* (text):** Enter your phone number for smooth communication.
* **Trip Details for Quote --**: Enter any relevant trip details in this section.
* **Pickup Address* (text):** Enter the address from which you will be picked up.
* **Drop-off Address* (text):** Enter the address to which you will be dropped off.
* **Frequency of Transfer (select_one):** Select one of the options:
	+ One-time
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
* **Preferred Vehicle Type (select_one):** Select one of the options:
	+ Executive Sedan
	+ Luxury SUV
	+ Premium Minivan
	+ Coach/Bus
* **Estimated Number of Passengers* (number):** Enter the estimated number of passengers.
* **Require Meet and Greet Service? (select_one):** Select one of the options:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Is this a recurring corporate requirement? (select_one):** Select one of the options:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Other Requirements or Special Needs (Optional)**: Enter any additional requirements or special needs.
* **How soon do you need the quote? (select_one):** Select one of the options:
	+ Within 1 hour
	+ Within 24 hours
	+ Not urgent
* **Preferred Currency for Quote (select_one):** Select one of the options:
	+ USD
	+ EUR
	+ GBP
	+ local currency
