# Stripe Cookie Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers to place a cookie order. Please fill out the form with your details to place your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form with your customer information, including name, email, and phone number.
* Select your preferred cookie type from the options provided.
* Enter the quantity of cookies you would like to order.
* Enter the total amount you would like to pay for the cookies.
* Choose your preferred payment method from the options provided.
* Confirm your payment method to ensure accuracy.
* Provide a confirmation of your order details for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name (`customer_name`):** Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Email (`customer_email`):** Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Customer Phone (`customer_phone`):** Enter your phone number for us to contact you, if needed.
* **Cookie Type (`cookie_type`):** Select the type of cookie you would like to order from the options provided.
* **Cookie Count (`cookie_count`):** Enter the number of cookies you would like to order.
* **Order Total (`order_total`):** Enter the total amount you would like to pay for the cookies.
* **Payment Method (`payment_method`):** Choose your preferred payment method from the options provided.
* **Payment Method Confirmation (`payment_method_confirmation`):** Confirm your selected payment method to ensure accuracy.
* **Order Confirmation (`order_confirmation`):** Confirm the details of your order for review.
* **Cookie Order:** Enter the type of cookie you would like to order in this text field.
