# Online Cookie Order Form WorldPayUS Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for cookies online. To complete the form, please follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the order information in the **Order Info** section.
2. Select your payment method in the **Payment Method** section.
3. Provide additional order details in the **Order Details** section.
4. Choose your shipping method in the **Shipping Method** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Info** (optional): Enter the order information, such as the type of cookies you'd like to order and any special requests.
* **Payment Method** (optional): Select your preferred payment method to complete the order. This may include options for credit card, PayPal, or other payment methods.
* **Order Details** (optional): Provide any additional information about your order, such as the number of cookies you'd like to order or any special instructions.
* **Shipping Method** (optional): Choose how you'd like your cookies shipped. This may include options like standard shipping or expedited shipping.
