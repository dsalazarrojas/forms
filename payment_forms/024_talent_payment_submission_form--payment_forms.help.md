# talent_payment_submission_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to submit new talent payments to the system. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure that all relevant details are captured correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the payment method from the dropdown list. This can be one of the three options provided: Payment 1, Payment 2, or Payment 3.
2. Enter the amount of the payment, if required.
3. Choose the currency of the payment from the dropdown list. Multiple options are available.
4. Enter the payment date, if required.
5. Select the payment time, if required.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about the payment.
7. Enter the name of the talent associated with the payment.
8. Select the status of the payment from the options provided: Pending, Paid, or Refunded.
9. Provide any additional details about the payment method, if necessary.
10. Enter the talent ID number, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select the method of payment, such as Payment 1, Payment 2, or Payment 3.
* **Amount** (`amount`, `number`, required): Enter the amount of the payment.
* **Currency** (`currency`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the currency of the payment from the list.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the payment, if required.
* **Payment Time** (`payment_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the payment, if required.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required): Add any additional comments or notes about the payment.
* **Talent Name** (`talent_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the talent associated with the payment.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the payment from the options provided: Pending, Paid, or Refunded.
* **Payment Method Details** (`payment_method_details`, `text`, required): Provide any additional details about the payment method, if necessary.
* **Talent ID** (`talent_id`, `number`, required): Enter the talent ID number.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are submitting payments for the correct talent.
* Double-check that the payment method, amount, and currency are accurate before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please consult the relevant department or contact support.
