# Ski Resort Parking Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ski Resort Parking Reservation Form is used to reserve a parking spot for guests arriving at the ski resort. It helps us to manage parking efficiently and ensure that guests can park their vehicles in a convenient location during their visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Parking Reservation" page.
2. Select the arrival time that suits your needs from the drop-down menu.
3. Choose the type of vehicle(s) you will be driving/parking.
4. Provide any additional information about your vehicle (if needed).
5. Select the payment method you would like to use (cash, credit card, or other).
6. Enter any relevant payment details (e.g., payment amount, payment method).
7. Confirm your parking reservation by submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parking Reservation** (Page 1: Parking Reservation):
	+ A text input field where you can type in any relevant information about your reservation.
* **Arrival Time** (Page 1: Parking Reservation):
	+ Select your expected arrival time in 24-hour format (e.g., 9:00 AM, 12:00 PM, etc.).
* **Reservation Slot** (Page 1: Parking Reservation):
	+ Choose a specific time slot for your parking reservation (9:00 AM - 12:00 PM, 12:00 PM - 3:00 PM, 3:00 PM - 6:00 PM).
* **Vehicle Type** (Page 1: Parking Reservation):
	+ Select one or multiple vehicle types you will be driving/parking (Car, Truck, Bus).
* **Additional Info (if needed)** (Page 1: Parking Reservation):
	+ Provide any additional information about your vehicle, if needed (e.g., size, special requirements).
* **Payment Method** (Page 1: Parking Reservation):
	+ Choose how you would like to pay for your parking (Cash, Credit Card, Other).
* **Payment Details** (Page 1: Parking Reservation):
	+ Enter any relevant payment details (e.g., payment amount, payment method).
* **Parking Confirmation** (Page 1: Parking Reservation):
	+ Confirm your parking reservation by submitting the form.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure efficient parking spot allocation.
* If you have any special requirements or requests, please provide them in the "Additional Info" field.
* Payment details are required for payment confirmation and processing.
