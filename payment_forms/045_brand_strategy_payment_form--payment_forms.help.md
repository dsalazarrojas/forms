# Brand Strategy Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Brand Strategy Payment Form is a tool designed to collect payment information from clients for services rendered. This form helps streamline the payment process, ensuring that clients can easily provide the necessary details for invoicing and payment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter the client's company name and invoice number in the designated fields.
3. Select the correct payment type from the dropdown options.
4. Enter the amount due in USD.
5. Select the payment method and card type (if applicable).
6. Enter the billing zip code.
7. Choose whether to receive a receipt via email.
8. Add any additional payment notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client / Company Name** (`client_company_name`, text, required): Enter the client's company name.
* **Invoice Number** (`invoice_no`, text, required): Enter the invoice number for this payment.
* **Today Date** (`pay_dt`, date, required): Enter the current date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Description of Brand Strategy Service** (`svc_desc`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the service provided to the client.
* **Payment For** (`pay_type_select`, select_one, required): Select the type of payment (e.g., Project Deposit, Monthly Retainer, Final Project Payment, or Workshop Fee).
* **Amount Due - USD** (`due_amt`, number, required): Enter the amount due in USD.
* **Billing Zip Code** (`bill_zip`, text, required): Enter the billing zip code for the payment.
* **Card Type** (`card_select`, select_one, required): Select the type of card used for payment (e.g., Visa, Mastercard, American Express, or Discover).
* **Would you like a receipt sent to your email?** (`receipt_req`, select_one, optional): Choose whether to receive a receipt via email.
* **Additional Payment Notes** (`pay_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional payment notes if necessary.
* **Thank you for your business!** (`pay_closing`, note, optional): A polite thank-you message after completing the form.

Note: Make sure to double-check your inputs for accuracy before submitting the form.
