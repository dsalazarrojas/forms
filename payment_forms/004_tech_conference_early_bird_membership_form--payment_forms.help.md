# tech_conference_early_bird_membership_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to determine whether a user qualifies for an early bird discount for a tech conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Read the form carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Fill in your payment details, including selecting whether you will pay and the payment method.
3. Provide your attendee details, including selecting whether you will attend and entering your name.
4. Enter your contact information (email and phone).
5. Choose the event date and time.
6. Enter any additional notes or comments.
7. If you are eligible, enter the early bird discount details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Details**
	+ `Payment Details` (select_one): This field asks whether you will pay for the conference.
	+ If you answer 'Yes', you will be asked about the payment method. 
* **Attendee Details**
	+ `Attendee Details` (select_multiple): This field asks whether you will attend the conference.
	+ If you answer 'Yes', you will be asked for your name. 
* **Attendee Name**
	+ `Attendee Name` (text): Enter your name as it should be listed on the conference materials.
* **Email**
	+ `Email` (email): Enter your email address so we can contact you about the conference.
* **Phone**
	+ `Phone` (text): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about the conference.
* **Payment Method**
	+ `Payment Method` (select_one): This field asks whether you will pay by a traditional method or another method. 
* **Other Payment Method**
	+ `Other Payment Method` (text): If you selected 'Other' as your payment method, enter a description of your payment method. 
* **Payment Method Details**
	+ `Payment Method Details` (text): Enter additional details about your payment method. 
* **Event Date**
	+ `Event Date` (date): Enter the date of the conference.
* **Event Time**
	+ `Event Time` (time): Enter the time of the conference.
* **Note**
	+ `Note` (note): Enter any additional comments or notes about your registration.
* **Early Bird Discount**
	+ `Early Bird Discount` (number): Enter the discount percentage you are eligible for.
* **Early Bird Discount Value**
	+ `Early Bird Discount Value` (number): Enter the amount of the discount you are eligible for.
* **Early Bird Discount Type**
	+ `Early Bird Discount Type` (select_one): This field asks whether you are eligible for the early bird discount.
* **Other Early Bird Discount**
	+ `Other Early Bird Discount` (text): If you selected 'Other' as your early bird discount type, enter a description of your early bird discount.
* **Early Bird Discount Details**
	+ `Early Bird Discount Details` (text): Enter additional details about your early bird discount. 

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information for all fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, leave it blank or contact the conference organizers for help.
* The early bird discount will be calculated based on the information provided.
