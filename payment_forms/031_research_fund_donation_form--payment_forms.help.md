# Research Fund Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect donations for research initiatives. You will be asked to provide information about your donation, including the amount, payment method, and any other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

* Fill in the required fields: **donation amount**, **supporter name**, **supporter email**, and **submit** fields.
* You can leave the other fields blank or fill them in as desired, but be aware that these are optional.
* For **payment method**, select the method you would like to use for your donation (e.g. Bank Transfer, Cash, Credit Card, or Other).
* For **payment date**, enter the date you plan to make the payment.
* For **research initiative**, choose the research initiative you are supporting with your donation.
* For **assigned tool**, choose the tool assigned to your donation.
* For **supporter note**, you can add any additional information or comments you'd like to share with us.
* Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the **submit** button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **donation amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you would like to donate.
* **supporter name** (`supporter_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the supporter of this donation.
* **supporter email** (`supporter_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **supporter phone** (`supporter_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **payment method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the method you would like to use for your donation (e.g. Bank Transfer, Cash, Credit Card, or Other).
* **payment date** (`payment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to make the payment (optional).
* **supporter note** (`supporter_note`, note, optional): Add any additional information or comments you'd like to share with us.
* **research initiative** (`research_initiative`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the research initiative you are supporting with your donation.
* **assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Choose the tool assigned to your donation.
* **submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to complete and submit your form.
