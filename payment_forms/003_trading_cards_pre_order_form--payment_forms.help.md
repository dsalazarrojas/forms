# Trading Cards Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer information and order details for a pre-order of trading cards.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the order details in the corresponding section.
2. Provide your customer details, including any required information.
3. Confirm the total cost of the order.
4. Fill out the shipping details, including any required information.
5. Select your preferred shipping option from the provided options.
6. Enter any additional delivery details.
7. Review your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): This field is used to enter information about the order, such as the order description or any special instructions.
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the customer's name, address, and contact information.
* **Total Cost** (`order_total`, number, optional): This field is used to confirm the total cost of the order.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, optional): This field is used to enter information about the shipping method and any additional shipping details.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, optional): This field is used to enter information about the payment method and any additional payment details.
* **Shipping Options** (`shipping_options`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select the preferred shipping option from USPS, UPS, FedEx, or DHL.
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, optional): This field is used to enter any additional delivery details, such as delivery date or time.

## Tips

- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out completely.
- Review your submission before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the support team.
