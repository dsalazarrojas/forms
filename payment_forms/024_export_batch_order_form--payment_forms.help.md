# Export Batch Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Export Batch Order Form is a tool used to collect customer information and order details for export orders. It is a crucial part of the export process, ensuring accurate and timely fulfillment of customer requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer details section with the customer's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. Provide product specifications, such as product names, quantities, and descriptions.
3. Enter order information, including the order date and time.
4. Add any additional comments or instructions for the export order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, required/optional): This section is for filling in the customer's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
- **Product Specs** (`product_specs`, text, required/optional): Enter the product names, quantities, and descriptions to help us understand what the customer is ordering.
- **Order Info** (`order_info`, time, required/optional): Please enter the order date and time to ensure accurate fulfillment of the order.
- **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or instructions for the export order, such as special requests or delivery instructions.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate processing of the order.
- Double-check the information entered to avoid errors.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
