# plumbing_services_billing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the billing process for plumbing services, allowing you to efficiently record and manage payments related to these services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the necessary information for the customer, including their name, service date, service time, billing address, and any other relevant details.
* Select the type of service provided (Plumber, Plumber's Assistant, or Plumber's Service).
* Enter the cost of the service and choose the payment method (Credit Card, Cash, or Bank Transfer).
* Update the payment status (Paid, Unpaid, or Pending) and enter any additional notes about the transaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the customer's name for identification purposes.
* **Service Date** (`service_date`, Date, Optional): Record the date on which the service was provided.
* **Service Time** (`service_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time of the service, if applicable.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, Text, Optional): If different from the service address, enter the billing address for the customer.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, Select One, Optional): Choose from the options: Plumber, Plumber's Assistant, or Plumber's Service).
* **Service Cost** (`service_cost`, Number, Optional): Enter the cost of the service provided.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the method used for payment, such as Credit Card, Cash, or Bank Transfer.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, Number, Optional): The total cost of the service, calculated based on the selected service type and cost.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, Select Multiple, Optional): Update the status of the payment, choosing from Paid, Unpaid, or Pending.
* **Notes** (`notes`, Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the transaction.

## Tips
- Double-check the accuracy of the information entered to prevent errors.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Review the form for any updates or changes to the service type, cost, or payment status.
- Use the form to track and manage payments and updates for multiple services.
