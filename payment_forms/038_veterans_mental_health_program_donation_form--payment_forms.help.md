# Veterans Mental Health Program Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterans Mental Health Program Donation Form is a tool designed to collect donations for the program, allowing individuals to contribute their support to the well-being of veterans. This form is intended to be simple and user-friendly, ensuring that donors can provide their information in a secure and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the donation amount you wish to contribute from the options provided.
* Choose the type of donation that best reflects your intention (e.g., General Donation, Tribute to a Veteran, or Monthly Subscription).
* If desired, include a personal message to express your dedication to the veteran(s) or a heartfelt thought for the program.
* Indicate whether or not you wish to remain anonymous as a donor.
* Provide your email address so the program can contact you if needed.
* Enter your full name.
* Enter your physical address.
* Include a message if you'd like to express your thoughts or gratitude.
* Select a payment method (PayPal or Credit Card) that suits your preference.
* Enter your phone number (optional, but useful for follow-up contact).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of your donation. This can be a one-time contribution or a recurring monthly donation.
* **Donation Type** (`donation_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of donation that best reflects your intention. Options include:
	+ General Donation: A straightforward contribution to support the program.
	+ Tribute to a Veteran: A donation in memory or in support of a specific veteran.
	+ Monthly Subscription: A recurring donation to contribute to the program on a regular basis.
* **Dedications** (`dedication_message`, text, optional): If you'd like to express a personal message or dedication to the veteran(s) you're supporting, enter it here.
* **Would you like to stay anonymous?** (`anonymous_donor`, select_one, optional): Decide whether or not you'd like to remain anonymous as a donor. This will ensure that your identity remains private.
* **Email** (`donor_email`, email, optional): Provide your email address so the program can contact you if necessary.
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as the donor.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your physical address for follow-up purposes or program recognition.
* **Message** (`donation_message`, text, optional): If you'd like to express your thoughts or gratitude, enter them here.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required): Select your preferred payment method:
	+ PayPal: A secure online payment platform.
	+ Credit Card: A traditional payment method that requires your credit card details.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up contact or for the program to reach you if needed.
