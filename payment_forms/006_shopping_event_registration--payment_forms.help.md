# Shopping Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Shopping Event Registration" form is designed to collect event-related information from users. This form is used to gather details necessary for event planning and registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the event details, such as the event title.
2. Provide attendee information, if applicable.
3. Select the payment method you wish to use.
4. Enter the total cost and any other relevant details.
5. Choose a payment status and terms.
6. Enter the event date, start and end times, and attendee count.
7. Describe the event location and details.
8. Finally, review your information and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Enter the title of the event.
* **Attendee Information** (`attendee_info`, text, optional): Provide any relevant information about the attendees, such as their names and contact details.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you will be paying for the event.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, number, optional): Enter the total cost of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Start Time** (`event_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event End Time** (`event_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the event.
* **Registration Fee** (`registration_fee`, number, optional): Enter the registration fee for the event.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of your payment.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select_multiple, required): Select the terms and conditions of your payment.
* **Attendee Count** (`attendee_count`, number, optional): Enter the number of attendees.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Describe the location of the event.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, optional): Describe the event in detail.

Note: Fields marked as "required" must be filled in for the form to be considered complete.
