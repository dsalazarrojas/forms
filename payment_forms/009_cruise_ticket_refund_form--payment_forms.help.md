# cruise_ticket_refund_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Cruise Ticket Refund Form is used to submit a refund request for a cruise ticket.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information ready for completion.
2. Review the form fields below and fill in the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Passenger Details** (`Passenger Details`, text, required): Enter the passenger's details, including name, date of birth, or any other identifying information required for refund processing.
* **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, text, required): Enter the passenger's contact number, which can be a phone number or email address.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter the passenger's email address.
* **Cruise Line** (`Cruise Line`, text, required): Enter the name of the cruise line associated with the ticket.
* **Ticket Number** (`Ticket Number`, number, required): Enter the ticket number issued for the cruise.
* **Ticket Date** (`Ticket Date`, date, required): Enter the date of the ticket.
* **Ticket Time** (`Ticket Time`, time, required): Enter the time of the ticket.
* **Reason for Refund** (`Reason for Refund`, text, required): Select the reason for the refund.
* **Refund Request** (`Refund Request`, number, required): Enter the refund amount.
* **Refund Method** (`Refund Method`, select multiple, required): Select the refund method (e.g., Cash, Credit).
* **Refund Amount** (`Refund Amount`, number, required): Enter the refund amount.
* **Confirm Refund** (`Confirm Refund`, select one, required): Confirm that the refund is correct.
* **Passenger Confirmation** (`Passenger Confirmation`, text, required): Enter the passenger's confirmation of the refund details.
* **Operator Confirmation** (`Operator Confirmation`, text, required): Enter the operator's confirmation of the refund details.
* **Refund Status** (`Refund Status`, text, required): Enter the current refund status.
* **Refund Date** (`Refund Date`, date, required): Enter the date of the refund.
* **Operator Notes** (`Operator Notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes from the operator.
* **Passenger Notes** (`Passenger Notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes from the passenger.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments.
* **Refund Currency** (`Refund Currency`, select one, required): Select the currency of the refund.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select one, required): Select the payment method.
* **Payment Amount** (`Payment Amount`, number, required): Enter the payment amount.
* **Passenger Signature** (`Passenger Signature`, note, required): Enter the passenger's signature.
* **Operator Signature** (`Operator Signature`, note, required): Enter the operator's signature.
* **Operator Name** (`Operator Name`, text, required): Enter the operator's name.

Please note that all fields marked as required must be filled out for the form to be submitted successfully.
