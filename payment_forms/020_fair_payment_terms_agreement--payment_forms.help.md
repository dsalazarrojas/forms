# fair_payment_terms_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fair_payment_terms_agreement form is used to collect information from customers or clients regarding payment terms, including names, payment details, and submission times. This form is designed to be completed by individuals or companies who need to agree on specific payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Company name in the Company field.
4. Select the Payment Term from the drop-down options.
5. Enter the Payment Amount in the Payment Amount field.
6. Choose your preferred Payment Method from the drop-down options.
7. Enter the Submission Date and Submission Time in the corresponding fields.
8. If you agree to the Terms and Conditions, select the "Agree" checkbox (this is optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (`First Name`, text, required)
Enter your first name in this field.

### Last Name (`Last Name`, text, required)
Enter your last name in this field.

### Company (Company, text, required)
Enter your company name in this field.

### Payment Term (Payment Term, number, required)
Select the payment term that best suits your needs.

### Payment Amount (Payment Amount, number, required)
Enter the amount you agree to pay.

### Payment Method (Payment Method, select_one, required)
Choose how you would like to make the payment. Options include:
* Credit card
* Bank transfer
* Check

### Submission Date (Submission Date, date, required)
Enter the date you plan to submit your payment.

### Submission Time (Submission Time, time, required)
Enter the time you plan to submit your payment.

### Terms and Conditions (Terms and Conditions, note, optional)
If you agree to the terms and conditions, select the "Agree" checkbox.

### Agreement (Agreement, note, optional)
If you agree to the terms and conditions, select the "Agree" checkbox.

Tips:

* Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Make sure to select the correct payment method and payment term that suits your needs.
* Double-check your payment amount and submission date and time before submitting the form.
