# paypal_payment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for processing transactions via PayPal. Please fill in the required fields accurately to ensure a secure and successful payment process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "User ID" with your unique identifier, which should match your PayPal account.
2. Enter the "Amount" you wish to pay, which will be used for the transaction amount.
3. Select a "Currency" type (if not choosing "Other Currency", enter the selected currency code).
4. If choosing "Other Currency", enter the "Currency" code in the format XXX.
5. Select the "Card Type" for your payment card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover, Diners, or Enrobi).
6. Enter the "Expiration Date" in MM/DD format.
7. Enter the "CVV" (Card Verification Value) for your card.
8. Select or enter the "Billing Address" (address1 to address5).
9. Select or enter the "Shipping Address" (address1 to address5).
10. Enter the "Shipping Address 2" (if required for shipping).
11. Select a "Payment Method" (PayPal or Stripe).
12. Enter any "Notes" regarding the transaction.
13. Enter your "Email" address.
14. Enter your "Phone" number.
15. Enter the "Card Number" of your payment card.
16. Select "Card Type 2" (if different from the first card type).
17. Enter the "Card Expiration Date" in MM/DD format.
18. Enter the "CVV 2" (Card Verification Value 2).
19. Select or enter "Billing Address 2", "Billing Address 3", and "Billing Address 4" as needed.
20. Select "Shipping Address 3" and "Shipping Address 6" as needed.
21. Enter "Shipping Address 7" if required for shipping.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User ID** (1): Enter your unique PayPal user ID.
* **Amount** (2): Enter the transaction amount.
* **Currency** (3): Select your currency type (USD, EUR, JPY, CNY, BRL, RUB, or enter 'Other Currency' for a custom code).
* **Other Currency** (4): Enter the custom currency code if not selecting 'Currency'.
* **Card Type** (5): Select your payment card type (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover, Diners, or Enrobi).
* **Expiration Date** (6): Enter the card expiration date in MM/DD format.
* **CVV** (7): Enter the Card Verification Value.
* **Billing Address** (8): Select your billing address options (address1 to address5).
* **Shipping Address** (9): Select your shipping address options (address1 to address5).
* **Shipping Address 2** (10): Enter your shipping address 2 information (if required).
* **Payment Method** (11): Select your payment method (PayPal or Stripe).
* **Notes** (12): Enter any notes regarding the transaction.
* **Email** (13): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (14): Enter your phone number.
* **Card Number** (15): Enter the card number of your payment card.
* **Card Type 2** (16): Select your card type 2 (if different from the first card type).
* **Card Expiration Date** (17): Enter the card expiration date in MM/DD format.
* **CVV 2** (18): Enter the Card Verification Value 2.
* **Billing Address 2** (19): Select or enter your billing address 2 information.
* **Billing Address 3** (20): Select or enter your billing address 3 information.
* **Billing Address 4** (21): Select or enter your billing address 4 information.
* **Billing Address 5** (22): Select or enter your billing address 5 information.
* **Shipping Address 3** (23): Select your shipping address 3 options.
* **Shipping Address 6** (24): Enter your shipping address 6 information.
* **Shipping Address 7** (25): Enter your shipping address 7 information.
