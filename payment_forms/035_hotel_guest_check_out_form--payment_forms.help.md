# Hotel Guest Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Guest Check Out Form is designed to collect information from hotel guests upon check-out. This includes personal details, contact information, and payment methods to facilitate smooth interactions and ensure accurate records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the "Guest Name" field.
2.  Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3.  Enter the room number you occupied during your stay.
4.  Select your departure date in the "Departure Date" field.
5.  Choose your departure time from the provided options.
6.  Select the correct room number from the options.
7.  Enter any comments or feedback in the "Comments" fields.
8.  Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" options.
9.  If applicable, enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Guest Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification or reservation.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
  * Purpose: To identify you with the hotel records.
- **Email**: Enter a valid email address to receive communications from the hotel.
  * Type: email
  * Required: false
  * Purpose: To enable hotel staff to contact you for any further information.
- **Room Number**: Enter the number of the room you occupied during your stay.
  * Type: number
  * Required: false
  * Purpose: To ensure accurate room assignments and records.
- **Departure Date**: Select your date of departure from the calendar options.
  * Type: date
  * Required: false
  * Purpose: To determine your stay duration and room occupancy dates.
- **Departure Time**: Select your estimated time of departure.
  * Type: time
  * Required: false
  * Purpose: To coordinate with hotel staff for smooth check-out processes.
- **Room Number 2**: Select the correct room number you occupied from the provided options.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: false
  * Purpose: To verify room assignments for accurate billing and services.
- **Comments**: Enter any comments, feedback, or special requests.
  * Type: note
  * Required: false
  * Purpose: To enable hotel staff to address any concerns or provide additional services.
- **Room Services 2, 3**: Select any additional services you require during your stay, such as room cleaning or extra linens.
  * Type: select_multiple
  * Required: false
  * Purpose: To provide hotel staff with information on services needed for smooth operations.
- **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for hotel staff to contact you with any further information.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
  * Purpose: To facilitate communication for any additional services or concerns.
- **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method, such as cash or credit card.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: false
  * Purpose: To determine your preferred payment method for check-out processes.
