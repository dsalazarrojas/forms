# Directed Payment Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for submitting applications for the Directed Payment Program. It collects necessary information from applicants, including contact details, payment information, and other supporting documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required and optional sections as needed.
2. Review each section carefully before proceeding.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and any other identifying information as requested by the program.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Provide your contact information, including phone number and email address.
- **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, required/optional): Enter payment information as requested by the program.
- **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, required/optional): Attach any additional documents that support your application, if required.
- **Approval** (`approval`, text, required/optional): This section may be used for internal approval purposes.
- **Organization Information** (`organization_info`, text, required/optional): Enter information about your organization, if applicable.
- **Review** (`review`, text, required/optional): Review your application before submission.
- **Upload File** (`upload_file`, text, required/optional): Upload any required or supporting files.
- **Confirm Payment** (`confirm_payment`, text, required/optional): Confirm your payment information.
- **Confirm Upload** (`confirm_upload`, text, required/optional): Confirm your uploaded file.
- **Confirm Approval** (`confirm_approval`, text, required/optional): Confirm your approval status.
- **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): Complete your application by submitting the form.

## Tips
- Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.
- Make sure to attach all required supporting documents.
- Contact the program administrators if you have questions or need assistance.
