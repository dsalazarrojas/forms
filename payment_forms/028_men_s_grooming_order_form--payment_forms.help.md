# Men’s Grooming Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place orders for men's grooming services, such as haircuts, shaves, and other treatments. It collects necessary information for order processing and ensures that customers receive the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Order Details** field with any relevant information about your order.
2. Select a payment method from the dropdown options provided.
3. Choose whether you want to pick up your order or have it delivered.
4. Enter your contact information, including **Name**, **Phone**, and **Email**.
5. Fill out any additional **Payment Details** and **Order Details** fields as requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details (id: 1)**: Enter any relevant information about your order, such as services requested or special requests.
* **Payment Method (id: 2)**: Select a payment method from the provided options. Please note that this field is not always required.
* **Pickup or Delivery (id: 3)**: Choose whether you want to pick up your order or have it delivered to you.
* **Pickup Address (id: 4)**: Enter the address where you would like to pick up your order (if you selected "Pickup").
* **Delivery Address (id: 5)**: Enter the address where you would like to have your order delivered (if you selected "Delivery").
* **Name (id: 6)**: Enter your name for our records.
* **Phone (id: 7)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Email (id: 8)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Comments (id: 9)**: Enter any additional comments or special requests you may have.
* **Total Cost (id: 10)**: This field is not required, but it will display the total cost of your order.
* **Payment Details (id: 11)**: Enter any additional payment information if required.
* **First Name (id: 12)**: Enter your first name for our records.
* **Last Name (id: 13)**: Enter your last name for our records.
* **Address 1 (id: 14)**: Enter the address of your location (if you selected "Pickup").
* **Address 2 (id: 15)**: Enter the address of your location (if you selected "Pickup").
* **City (id: 16)**: Enter the city of your location (if you selected "Pickup").
* **State (id: 17)**: Enter the state of your location (if you selected "Pickup").
* **Zip (id: 18)**: Enter the zip code of your location (if you selected "Pickup").
* **Payment Method 1 (id: 19)**: Enter an additional payment method if required.
* **Payment Method 2 (id: 20)**: Enter another additional payment method if required.
* **Payment Method 3 (id: 21)**: Enter a third additional payment method if required.
* **Order Details 1 (id: 22)**: Enter any additional order details if required.
* **Order Details 2 (id: 23)**: Enter any additional order details if required.
* **Order Details 3 (id: 24)**: Enter any additional order details if required.
* **Order Details 4 (id: 25)**: Enter any additional order details if required.

Please fill out this form thoroughly and accurately, and we will do our best to process your order efficiently and effectively.
