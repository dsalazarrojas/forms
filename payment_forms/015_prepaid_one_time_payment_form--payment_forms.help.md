# prepaid_one_time_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The prepaid_one_time_payment_form is used to collect data related to one-time payments for a pre-arranged purchase. Fill out the form to provide payment details and confirm payment status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field and determine which information you need to provide.
2. Fill out the form by entering your payment details and other required information.
3. Review your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **One-time-Payment** (1, number): Enter the total price of your purchase.
* **Product-Price** (2, number): Enter the price of the product being purchased.
* **Purchase-Amount** (3, select): Select the amount to purchase from the available options ($20.00, $15.00, $25.00, $30.00).
* **Payment-Method** (4, select): Select your preferred payment method (Bank Transfer, Credit/Debit Card, PayPal, or Other).
* **Confirm-Payment** (5, select): Confirm your payment status (True or False).
* **Email** (6, email): Enter your email address (optional).
* **Phone** (7, text): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Address** (8, text): Enter your address (optional).
* **City** (9, text): Enter the city where you live (optional).
* **Zip** (10, text): Enter your zip code (optional).

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct payment method and confirm your payment status accurately.
* If you're unsure about any field, review the form carefully before submitting.
* You may enter any other relevant details in the optional fields to provide additional context.
