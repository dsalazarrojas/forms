# Hardware Purchase Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hardware Purchase Payment Form is used to collect payment details for hardware purchases. This form is used to ensure accurate and up-to-date payment information is captured for all hardware transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Determine whether the hardware purchase is for "Yes" or "No".
2. Select the payment method used for the transaction (Visa or Amex).
3. Enter the date of the transaction.
4. Enter the time of the transaction.
5. Enter the total amount paid for the hardware.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Hardware details** (`hardware_purchase_payment_form_1`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate whether the hardware purchase is for a new or used item.
  * Please select "Yes" if the hardware purchase is for a new item, and "No" if it's for a used item.
- **Payment Method** (`hardware_purchase_payment_form_2`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the payment method used for the transaction.
  * Choose between "Visa" and "Amex" as the payment method.
- **Transaction Date** (`hardware_purchase_payment_form_3`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the transaction.
  * Format the date as MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 02/01/2022).
- **Transaction Time** (`hardware_purchase_payment_form_4`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the transaction.
  * Format the time as HH:MM AM/PM (e.g., 01:30 PM).
- **Amount** (`hardware_purchase_payment_form_5`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total amount paid for the hardware.
  * Enter the exact amount without any currency symbols or decimal points.

## Tips
- Double-check your responses to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or assistance.
- This form is for payment details only; for hardware purchase orders, please use the "Hardware Purchase Order Form".
