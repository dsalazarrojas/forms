# Episode Based Payment Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Episode Based Payment Program Application Form is used to gather information for creating or updating episode-based payments for patients. Please complete this form carefully to ensure accurate and efficient processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and ensure you're submitting a new application or update an existing one for the same episode.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and relevant information.
3. Check your email address and phone number for correct formatting (e.g., `username@example.com`).
4. Enter any additional notes or comments about the application in the "User Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input**: Please enter a brief description of the episode-based payment you're applying for.
* **Application Details**: Describe the specific application details, including any relevant dates or events related to the episode.
* **Contact Information**: Provide your name, title/position, and organization (if applicable).
* **Review**: Summarize your review or feedback about the episode-based payment, if any.
* **User Email**: Enter a valid email address associated with you or your organization. Please format as `username@example.com`.
* **User Phone**: Enter your phone number in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX` or `XXX.XXX.XXXX`.
* **User Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the application, if needed.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact your organization's support team for assistance.
* Ensure you're filling out the correct form for the intended episode.
