# Damaged Goods Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to process a refund for a damaged or defective product.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Customer Name** and **Customer Email** to ensure that we can contact you with any updates on your refund.
2. Describe the **Product Description** of the damaged or defective product.
3. Attach a **Product Image** of the damaged or defective product, if possible.
4. Enter your **Order ID** number to help us locate your order.
5. Explain the **Reason for Refund**, specifying the issue with your product.
6. Select a **Refund Method** that you would like to receive your refund (Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal).
7. Enter the **Refund Amount** you are requesting.
8. Enter the **Date of Refund** and **Time of Refund** you would like to receive your refund.
9. Add any additional **Notes** or comments about your refund.
10. Enter any additional information in the **Customer Message** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Description**: Enter a brief description of the product you received that is damaged or defective.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your order receipt or ID.
* **Product Image**: Attach an image of the damaged product, if possible. This can help us to visually inspect the issue.
* **Order ID**: Enter the number on your order receipt or confirmation email.
* **Reason for Refund**: Explain the reason why you are requesting a refund, such as a defect, damage during shipping, or a mistake with your order.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates on your refund.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Refund Amount**: Enter the amount you are requesting as a refund, based on the value of the product and any applicable sales tax.
* **Refund Method**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Cash: Receive a refund in cash.
	+ Credit Card: Receive a refund on your credit card.
	+ PayPal: Receive a refund through PayPal.
* **Date of Refund**: Enter the date you would like to receive your refund.
* **Time of Refund**: Enter the time you would like to receive your refund.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information or comments about your refund.
* **Customer Message**: Enter any additional information or comments about your refund.
