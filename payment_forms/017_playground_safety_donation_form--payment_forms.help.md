# playground_safety_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The playground safety donation form is used to report any incidents or accidents that occurred during a playground shift. This form should be filled out by staff or volunteers to provide important information about the incident, including the type of donation made and any relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the donation type (yes or no)
2. Enter the amount of the donation (if applicable)
3. Provide your email address
4. Enter your phone number (if applicable)
5. Add any additional notes or comments about the incident

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Type** (select_one, required): This field is used to indicate if a donation was made. Select 'Yes' if a donation was made, and 'No' if no donation was made.
* **Donation Amount** (number, optional): If a donation was made, enter the amount of the donation in this field.
* **Email Address** (email, optional): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you about the incident.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you about the incident.
* **Note** (note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the incident that you think are relevant.
* **Assigned Tool** (select_multiple, optional): If a specific tool was assigned to a child or staff member during the incident, select 'Yes'. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out as much information as possible to help us understand the incident better.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [your email address].
* This form is used to report incidents that occurred during your shift, so please be as detailed as possible when providing information.
