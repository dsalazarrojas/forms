# Family Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Donation Form is designed to collect information about a family's donation to the organization. This form is intended for families who wish to make a one-time gift or a recurring donation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your family's name in the **family_name** field.
2. Enter the amount of your donation in the **donation_amount** field.
3. Select your preferred **payment_method** from the dropdown list.
4. Provide any additional details about your donation in the **donation_details** field.
5. If you are paying by bank transfer, enter the details of the transfer in the **bank_transfer_details** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **family_name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the family making the donation.
* **donation_amount** (Number, Required): Enter the amount of the donation (e.g., 100.00).
* **payment_method** (Select One, Optional): Choose how you wish to make the donation, either by Credit Card or Bank Transfer.
* **bank_transfer_details** (Text, Optional): If you selected Bank Transfer as your payment method, enter the details of the transfer (e.g., account number, routing number).
* **donation_details** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional information about your donation (e.g., purpose, frequency).
