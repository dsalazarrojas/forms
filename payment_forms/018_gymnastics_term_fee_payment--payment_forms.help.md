# Gymnastics Term Fee Payment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect payment method and details for gym term fees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the payment method you wish to use.
2. Enter your email address for communication purposes.
3. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
4. Review your payment details before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Gym Term Fee** (`term_fee`, number, Required): Enter the amount of the term fee.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, Optional): This field allows you to select how you would like to pay for your term fee. Choose from the options of Visa, Mastercard, Amex, or Discover.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number. This will be used for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Ensure that you enter accurate and complete information for your payment details.
* If you have any issues with your payment, contact your gym management team.
* Review your details carefully before submitting the form.
