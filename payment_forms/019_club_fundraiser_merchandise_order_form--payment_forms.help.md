# Club Fundraiser Merchandise Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for ordering merchandise related to a fundraiser event. It captures the buyer's information, order details, and payment method for a smooth transaction process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter the **Order Date** in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
2. Provide your **Buyer Full Name** and **Buyer Email Address** to receive order confirmations.
3. Enter your **Buyer Phone Number** (optional).
4. Select the **Fundraiser Event Name** to associate the order with the correct event.
5. Choose the type of merchandise you wish to purchase by selecting one or more items from the **Merchandise Type** list.
6. If ordering a T-Shirt or Hoodie, select the corresponding **T-Shirt Size** or **Hoodie Size**.
7. If ordering a T-Shirt or Hoodie, select the desired **T-Shirt Color Choice**.
8. Enter the quantity of each item you wish to purchase.
9. Enter the **Order Subtotal** which will be automatically calculated based on the items selected.
10. Enter your **Delivery Address** for proper delivery of the order.
11. Select the **Payment Method** for processing the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Date (Required)**: Enter the date you are placing the order in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Buyer Full Name (Required)**: Enter your full name as it should be displayed on the order receipt.
* **Buyer Email Address (Required)**: Enter your email address to receive order confirmations and updates.
* **Buyer Phone Number (Optional)**: Enter your phone number for additional contact.
* **Fundraiser Event Name (Required)**: Select the event this order is for to associate with the correct event.
* **Merchandise Type**: Select one or more items from the list to purchase.
* **T-Shirt Size (Optional)**: If ordering a T-Shirt, select your desired size.
* **T-Shirt Color Choice (Optional)**: If ordering a T-Shirt, select your desired color.
* **T-Shirt Quantity (Optional)**: Enter the quantity of T-Shirts you wish to purchase.
* **Hoodie Size (Optional)**: If ordering a Hoodie, select your desired size.
* **Hoodie Quantity (Optional)**: Enter the quantity of Hoodies you wish to purchase.
* **Details for Other Items (Optional)**: If ordering any other items, provide a description of the item(s) and desired quantity.
* **Order Subtotal (Required)**: This will be automatically calculated based on your selections.
* **Delivery Address (Required)**: Enter your full address for delivery of the order.
* **Payment Method (Required)**: Select the payment method for processing the order.
