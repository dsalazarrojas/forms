# Global Humanitarian Relief Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and contact information from individuals and organizations wishing to make donations to global humanitarian relief efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your personal details and donation information.
2. Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`form_section_1`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, text, required): Enter the physical address where you can be contacted.
* **City** (`address_city`, text, required): Enter the city where you can be contacted.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, text, required): Enter the postal code of the city where you can be contacted.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required): Enter the country of your residence.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter a valid phone number where you can be contacted.

## Tips
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure your email address and phone number are valid and up-to-date.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the relief organization directly.
