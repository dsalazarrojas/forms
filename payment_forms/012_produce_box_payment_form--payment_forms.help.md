# Produce Box Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Produce Box Payment Form is designed to collect payment information from customers for their produce box orders. This form helps customers provide their details and payment information in a convenient and streamlined process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Customer Information" page to begin.
	* Fill in your name, email, and any other relevant contact details.
2. Move to the "Order Information" page.
	* Enter your order details, including the order number or any other relevant information.
3. Proceed to the "Payment Information" page.
	* Enter your payment method and details, such as card information or other payment options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information (Customer Information, text, required: false)**: Enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Order Information (Order Information, text, required: true)**: Enter your order number or any other relevant order details.
* **Payment Information (Payment Information, text, required: false)**: Enter your payment method and details, such as card information or other payment options.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact the form creator at form@gic.mx.
