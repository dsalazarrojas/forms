To create a user-facing help guide for this "Afterpay Concert Tickets Order Form", we'll break down the form purpose, how to complete it, and each field's explanation.

### Form Purpose
The "Afterpay Concert Tickets Order Form" is designed for customers to order concert tickets while opting for the "Afterpay" payment method, which allows them to buy tickets now and pay later in installments. This form collects necessary customer information, concert details, and payment options for a seamless ticket purchasing experience.

### How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
2. Enter your valid email address for ticket confirmation and updates.
3. Enter your phone number for important updates and communication.
4. Select the concert details, including the artist or band name, concert date, start time, venue name, and venue address.
5. Choose the type of ticket you want to purchase, including General Admission, VIP, Premium Seating, etc.
6. Specify the number of tickets you'd like to buy.
7. Indicate any seating preferences or special needs accommodations you may require.
8. Select how you'd like to receive your tickets, either digitally, via the mobile app, will call, or by mail.
9. Enter the delivery address (if mailing tickets).
10. Select your preferred payment method, including Afterpay, Credit Card, Debit Card, etc.
11. Agree to the event terms and Afterpay conditions.
12. Confirm you are 18 years or older.
