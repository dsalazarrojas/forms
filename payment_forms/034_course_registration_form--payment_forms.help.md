# Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Registration Form is designed for students to provide details about their course registration, including their course selection, personal information, and payment method. This form is essential for instructors or administrators to track student enrollment and process course payments.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To fill out the Course Registration Form, navigate through the form pages using the navigation buttons or click on the "Continue" button to proceed.
2.  On the "Course Details" page, enter the title of the course you wish to register for.
3.  On the "Student Info" page, provide your personal details as required.
4.  On the "Payment" page, select the payment method you prefer between "Pay with Card" or "Pay with PayPal".
5.  Click the "Submit" button to review your form entries and complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Details** (`course_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the title of the course you wish to register for. This will help the course instructor or administrator track your course selection.
* **Student Info** (`student_info`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide your personal details as required. This is crucial for the course instructor or administrator to contact or communicate with you about the course.
* **Payment** (`payment`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment method you prefer, which can be "Pay with Card" or "Pay with PayPal". This ensures that you can complete the payment process smoothly.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `note`, required): Click this button to review and complete your course registration process.
