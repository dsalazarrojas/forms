# Freelancer Payment Invoice - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create and send professional invoices to clients as a freelancer. It captures essential information such as client details, payment terms, and payment status for accurate and efficient financial management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Enter the client's name in the "Client name" field.
2. Enter the client's email address in the "Client email" field.
3. Select the invoice date from the calendar dropdown or enter the date manually in the "Invoice date" field.
4. Choose the payment terms from the "Payment terms" dropdown, which determines when the payment is due.
5. Select the payment method from the "Payment method" dropdown, which could be "Bank transfer" or "Online payment".
6. Enter the subtotal amount for the services rendered.
7. Enter the tax amount, if applicable.
8. Enter the total amount including tax (subtotal + tax).
9. Select the payment status from the "Payment status" dropdown, which could be "Paid", "Pending", or "Overdue".
10. Optionally, add a note for payment terms in the "Payment terms note" field.
11. Enter the invoice number, if desired.
12. Enter the client's address, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Freelancer Payment Invoice** (`title`, `text`, required: false): This is the title of the form, which is not editable.
* **Client name** (`client_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the client.
* **Client email** (`client_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the client.
* **Invoice date** (`invoice_date`, `date`, required: false): Select or enter the date of the invoice.
* **Payment terms** (`payment_terms`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the payment terms, which determines when the payment is due (Net 7 days, Net 14 days, or Net 30 days).
* **Payment method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the payment method, which could be "Bank transfer" or "Online payment".
* **Subtotal** (`subtotal`, `number`, required: false): Enter the subtotal amount for the services rendered.
* **Tax amount** (`tax_amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the tax amount, if applicable.
* **Total** (`total`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total amount including tax (subtotal + tax).
* **Payment status** (`payment_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the payment status, which could be "Paid", "Pending", or "Overdue".
* **Payment terms note** (`payment_terms_note`, `note`, required: false): Optionally add a note for payment terms.
* **Invoice number** (`invoice_number`, `number`, required: false): Enter the invoice number, if desired.
* **Client address** (`client_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's address, if required.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields, if any.
* Review the form carefully before sending it to the client.
* You can customize the payment terms and method to suit your business needs.
* Keep the client's address and payment status up-to-date to avoid confusion.
