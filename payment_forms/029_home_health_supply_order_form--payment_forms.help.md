# home_health_supply_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The home health supply order form is used to collect and track information related to home health supply orders. This form is designed to gather details such as patient information, payment information, and order specifics to facilitate efficient order processing and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields with the necessary information. Please ensure that you provide accurate and clear information to ensure smooth processing of your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **order_details** (`order_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your order details, such as your order request or the specific items you are ordering. 
* **patient_info** (`patient_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's information, including any relevant medical details. 
* **payment_info** (`payment_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter payment information related to your order, such as method of payment or payment amount. 
* **order_total** (`order_total`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the total amount of your order. 
* **payment_method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose whether your order will be paid using a payment method (e.g. 'Yes') or not ('No'). 
* **payment_status** (`payment_status`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the status of your payment, such as Active or Inactive.
* **patient_name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's name. 
* **address** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's address. 
* **order_date** (`order_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of your order. 
* **order_time** (`order_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of your order. 
* **order_notes** (`order_notes`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes related to your order. 
* **patient_email** (`patient_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the patient's email address. 
* **patient_phone** (`patient_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's phone number. 
* **medical_supplies** (`medical_supplies`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose whether you require medical supplies (e.g. 'Yes') or not ('No'). 

Note: Some fields may not be applicable or visible based on your order type or other factors. If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your healthcare professional or contact our support team for guidance.
