# digital_poster_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The digital poster order form is used to capture details for ordering digital posters. This form is used to collect order details such as specifications, quantity, delivery address, and payment method. It is also used to collect contact information like contact number and email address. The form is designed to be completed by customers to provide their delivery preferences and order comments.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the digital poster order form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your order details in the "Order Details" field.
2. Provide your specifications for the order in the "Specifications" field.
3. Enter the quantity of the digital poster you are ordering.
4. Provide your delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field.
5. Choose your delivery method (Yes/No) in the "Delivery Method" field.
6. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field (optional).
8. Choose your payment method (Yes/No) in the "Payment Method" field.
9. Enter any additional comments about your order in the "Order Comments" field (optional).
10. Sign your order in the "Signature" field.
11. Choose your delivery date in the "Delivery Date" field (optional).
12. Choose your delivery time in the "Delivery Time" field (optional).
13. Enter your order date in the "Order Date" field.
14. Enter any delivery notes in the "Delivery Note" field (optional).
15. Enter any customer comments in the "Customer Comments" field (optional).
16. Enter your customer signature in the "Customer Signature" field (optional).
17. Enter the order ID and order reference number in the "Order ID" and "Order Ref" fields (optional).
18. Enter your customer signature again in the "Customer Signature 1" field.
19. Choose your delivery method again in the "Delivery Method 1" field.
20. Enter any delivery notes in the "Delivery Note 1" field (optional).
21. Enter any customer comments in the "Customer Comments 1" field (optional).
22. Enter your order details again in the "Order Details 1" field.
23. Sign your order again in the "Signature 1" field.
