# livestock_support_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users who need support for their livestock-related needs. Please answer the following questions to provide your support request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose the answer to "Don't Have a Donkey?" from the provided options.
2. Enter a donation amount, if you wish to make a donation.
3. If you have an email address you would like to provide, enter it in the field labeled "Email Address".
4. Enter your Name of Donor in the text field.
5. If you have a Phone Number you would like to provide, enter it in the field labeled "Phone Number".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Don't Have a Donkey?**: 
    * Select 'Yes' if you own a donkey and need support.
    * Select 'No' if you do not own a donkey and do not need support.
* **Donation Amount**: 
    * Enter a numeric value representing the amount you would like to donate.
    * Leave blank if you do not wish to make a donation.
* **Email Address**: 
    * Enter a valid email address if you wish to provide contact information for yourself.
    * Leave blank if you do not wish to provide an email address.
* **Name of Donor**: 
    * Enter your name in this field.
    * This field is required for us to address you personally.
* **Phone Number**: 
    * Enter a valid phone number if you wish to provide contact information for yourself.
    * Leave blank if you do not wish to provide a phone number.
