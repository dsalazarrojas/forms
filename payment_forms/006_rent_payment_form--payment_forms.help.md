# Rent Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rent Payment Form is designed to facilitate secure and efficient rent payments between landlords and tenants. This form gathers necessary information for rent payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1.  Ensure all required fields are filled.
2.  Enter your name and contact information in the "Tenant Information" section.
3.  Provide payment details such as date and amount paid in the "Payment Details" section.
4.  Select the appropriate payment method (Cash, Check, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer).
5.  If applicable, indicate your signature and the landlord's verification in the "Tenant Signature" and "Landlord Verification" sections.
6.  Add any additional notes or comments if necessary.
7.  Enter your email and phone number for contact.
8.  Finally, enter your and landlord's IDs for identification purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Information (1)**: Enter your name and contact information to identify yourself as the tenant.
* **Payment Details (2)**: Enter the date and amount of the rent you paid.
* **Date of Payment (3)**: Select the date you made the payment.
* **Amount Paid (4)**: Enter the exact amount you paid.
* **Payment Method (5)**: Choose how you made the payment (Cash, Check, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer).
* **Tenant Signature (6)**: Check "Signed" if you physically signed the payment document.
* **Landlord Verification (7)**: Check "Verified" if the landlord has confirmed your payment.
* **Notes (8)**: Add any additional comments or information related to your rent payment.
* **Email (9)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (10)**: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Tenant ID (11)**: Enter your unique ID as the tenant.
* **Landlord ID (12)**: Enter the landlord's unique ID for verification.

This form is designed to be simple and easy to use, ensuring accurate and efficient rent payments between landlords and tenants.
