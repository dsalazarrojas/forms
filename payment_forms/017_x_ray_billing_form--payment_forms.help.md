# X Ray Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The X Ray Billing Form is a form designed to collect billing information for X-ray services. It is intended to be completed by individuals who need to submit their billing information for these services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide all required information (indicated by an asterisk \*).
3. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required: False): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required: False): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required: False): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (Text, Required: False): Enter your address.
* **Phone** (Text, Required: False): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (Email, Required: False): Enter your email address.
* **Insurance Info** (Text, Required: False): Enter any relevant insurance information.
* **X-Ray Service** (Text, Required: False): Enter the X-ray service you are requesting billing for.
* **Billing Address** (Text, Required: False): Enter your billing address.
* **Billing Phone** (Text, Required: False): Enter your billing phone number.
* **Payment Method** (Select One, Required: False): Select one of the following payment methods:
	+ Credit
	+ Check
	+ Online
	+ None

## Tips
* Please double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have already submitted this form, please do not resubmit it.
* This form is intended for one-time use. If you need to submit multiple X-ray billing requests, please submit one form per request.
