# hvac_system_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hvac_system_order_form is used for customers to place orders for hvac systems, including heating and cooling systems. This form allows customers to provide their personal details, order specifics, and payment information to ensure a smooth ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address and Phone number.
3. Select the Order Date for the desired delivery date.
4. Choose your preferred Heating Type, Cooling Type, and Payment Method.
5. Enter any additional Order Details if necessary.
6. Review and submit your order for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed on the order and communication with our company.
- **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed on the order and communication with our company.
- **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you for order updates and communication.
- **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your contact phone number in case we need to reach you for order clarification or updates.
- **Order Date** (Order Date, date, required): Select the date you would like to receive your hvac system.
- **Heating Type** (Heating Type, select_one, required): Choose between AC Unit, Furnace, or Heat Pump for your heating needs.
- **Cooling Type** (Cooling Type, select_one, required): Select a cooling method that suits your needs: AC Unit, Furnace, or Heat Pump.
- **Order Details** (Order Details, text, required): Add any additional details about your order if needed.
- **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, required): Choose between Cash, Credit, or Check for payment.
- **Total Cost** (Total Cost, number, required): You will be able to see this value after selecting the order details and payment method. It represents the total cost of your order.
- **Customer Comments** (Customer Comments, note, required): If you have any additional comments or requests, please add them here.
- **Assigned Tool** (Assigned Tool, text, required): Our system will automatically populate this field with the assigned technician's name. You do not need to fill this in.
- **Created By** (Created By, text, required): This field will be automatically populated with the name of the staff member who processed your order. You do not need to fill this in.
 
Note: Fields marked as required will need to be filled in for your order to be processed.
