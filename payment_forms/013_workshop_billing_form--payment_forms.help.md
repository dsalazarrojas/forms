# Workshop Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for workshops to collect billing information from their participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for your workshop billing purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Billing Form**: This is the title of the form, which will be displayed on the form.
* **Participant Details**: Enter the name, address, and other relevant details of the workshop participants here.
* **Payment Information**: This section is for entering the payment information of the workshop participants, such as the payment method and amount.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill in the required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check the information entered for any errors before submitting the form.
* If you need help with completing this form or have any questions, contact form@gic.mx.
