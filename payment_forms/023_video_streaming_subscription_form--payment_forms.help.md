# Video Streaming Subscription Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Video Streaming Subscription Form is used to collect information for the user's subscription to the video streaming service. It contains fields for user details, payment method, and billing/shipping addresses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Information field with your name and other relevant details.
2. Select your preferred payment method and subscription plan in the Payment Method and Subscription Plan fields, respectively.
3. If you have a billing address, fill out the Billing Address field. If you don't have a billing address, you can leave it blank.
4. If you have a shipping address, fill out the Shipping Address field. This address should match the billing address if you are using the same address.
5. Fill out the Payment Card field with your credit card number (if applicable).
6. If your payment method requires it, fill out the CVV field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (User Information, text, optional): Enter your name and other relevant details that we need to know to complete your subscription.
* **Subscription Plan** (Subscription Plan, select_one, required): Select your preferred subscription plan. This will determine which service plan you will be subscribed to.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_multiple, required): Select at least one payment method for your subscription.
* **Payment Card** (Payment Card, text, optional): Enter your credit card number if you are paying by card.
* **Billing Address** (Billing Address, text, optional): Enter your billing address if you are paying by card or using a different billing address. If you are using the same address as your shipping address, you can leave this blank.
* **Shipping Address** (Shipping Address, text, optional): Enter your shipping address if you are using a different address for shipping. This address should match your billing address if you are using the same address.
* **CVV** (CVV, text, optional): Enter your card verification value (CVV) if your payment method requires it for verification purposes.
