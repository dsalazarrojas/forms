# Responsive Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your order. Please fill it out as instructed, and our team will be able to process your order efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field according to the guidelines below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (order-details): Enter information about the product you are ordering (e.g., product name, description).
* **Customer Info** (customer-info): Provide your contact and demographic details (e.g., name, email, phone number).
* **Billing Address** (billing-address): Enter your billing address, including street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Shipping Address** (shipping-address): Enter your shipping address, including street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Contact Details** (contact-details): Provide any additional contact information or details for order tracking.
* **Payment** (payment): Enter your payment information (e.g., credit card number, expiration date).
