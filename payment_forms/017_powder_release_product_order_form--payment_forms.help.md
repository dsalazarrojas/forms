# Powder Release Product Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Powder Release Product Order Form is designed to collect information about orders and customers for the purpose of processing and fulfilling these orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Order Details** section with the relevant information about the order, such as order type, quantities, or any other specific details required for order processing.
2. Provide **Customer Details** to help the order fulfiller know who to contact or send the ordered product to.
3. Fill in the **Payment Details** section to ensure the order can be properly invoiced or settled.
4. In the **Order Items** field, list any additional items or products that are part of the order, along with their corresponding quantities or specifications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details (order_details)** (`<text>`, Optional): 
    This field is for entering specific details about the order, such as the type of powder, quantity, or any special requirements. Please enter the information relevant to the order you are trying to process.
* **Customer Details (customer_details)** (`<text>`, Optional): 
    Enter the details of the customer, which could include name, address, or any contact information. This helps ensure that the order is delivered correctly and the customer is informed about the order status.
* **Payment Details (payment_details)** (`<text>`, Optional): 
    Provide the payment method or any financial details related to the order. This could include payment method (e.g., credit card), date of payment, or other financial specifics. Make sure to include any necessary proof of payment or confirmation of order status.
* **Order Items (order_items)** (`<text>`, Optional): 
    List each item or product being ordered, along with their quantities or any specific details needed for fulfillment. This could include product codes, item descriptions, or special instructions for each item in the order.

## Tips

- Ensure you complete all required fields to avoid delays in processing your order.
- Double-check your information for accuracy to prevent any mistakes or miscommunications.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us for support.
