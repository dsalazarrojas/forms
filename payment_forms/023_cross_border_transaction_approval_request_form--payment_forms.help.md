# Cross Border Transaction Approval Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cross Border Transaction Approval Request Form is used to request approval for cross border transactions. This form is completed by the originator of the transaction and submitted to the approver for review and approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the transaction details in the "Transaction Details" field.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Originator" as per the company's standard practice.
3. Enter the date of the originator in the "Originator Date" field.
4. Enter the phone number of the originator in the "Originator Phone" field.
5. Enter the email of the originator in the "Originator Email" field.
6. Enter any additional details about the cross border transaction in the "Cross Border Transaction Details" field.
7. Enter the address of the originator in the "Originator Address" field.
8. Select the "Approval Date" for the transaction.
9. Enter the name of the approver in the "Approver" field.
10. Enter the phone number of the approver in the "Approver Phone" field.
11. Enter the email of the approver in the "Approver Email" field.
12. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Cross Border Transaction Approvals" as per the company's standard practice.
13. Select the "Approval Status" of the transaction.
14. Enter any comments about the approval process in the "Approval Comments" field.
15. Enter your name in the "Submitter" field.
16. Enter your phone number in the "Submitter Phone" field.
17. Enter your email in the "Submitter Email" field.
18. Enter any additional notes about the cross border transaction in the "Cross Border Transaction Notes" field.
19. Attach any relevant documents or files related to the transaction in the "Cross Border Transaction Attachment" field.
20. Enter the date and time when the form was submitted in the "Cross Border Transaction Approval Form Submitted On" field.
21. Enter the name of the user who submitted the form in the "Cross Border Transaction Approval Form Submitted By User" field.
22. Enter the role of the user who submitted the form in the "Cross Border Transaction Approval Form Submitted By User Role" field.
23. Enter the IP address of the user who submitted the form in the "Cross Border Transaction Approval Form Submitted By IP" field.
24. Enter the user agent of the user who submitted the form in the "Cross Border Transaction Approval Form Submitted By User Agent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Transaction Details** (`cross_border_transaction_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the cross border transaction.
* **Originator** (`originator_id`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you are the originator of the transaction.
* **Originator Date** (`originator_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the originator.
* **Originator Phone** (`originator_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the originator.
* **Originator Email** (`originator_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the originator.
* **Cross Border Transaction Details** (`cross_border_transaction_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the cross border transaction.
* **Originator Address** (`originator_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the originator.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, optional): Select the date for approval.
* **Approver** (`approver`, text, optional): Enter the name of the approver.
* **Approver Phone** (`approver_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the approver.
* **Approver Email** (`approver_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the approver.
* **Cross Border Transaction Approvals** (`cross_border_transaction_approvals`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if the transaction requires approval.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the approval.
* **Approval Comments** (`approval_comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments about the approval process.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, optional): Enter your email.
* **Cross Border Transaction Notes** (`cross_border_transaction_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the cross border transaction.
* **Cross Border Transaction Attachment** (`cross_border_transaction_attachment`, text, optional): Attach any relevant documents or files related to the transaction.
* **Cross Border Transaction Approval Form Submitted On** (`cross_border_transaction_approval_form_submitted_on`, date, optional): Enter the date and time when the form was submitted.
* **Cross Border Transaction Approval Form Submitted By User** (`cross_border_transaction_approval_form_submitted_by_user`, text, optional): Enter the name of the user who submitted the form.
* **Cross Border Transaction Approval Form Submitted By User Role** (`cross_border_transaction_approval_form_submitted_by_user_role`, text, optional): Enter the role of the user who submitted the form.
* **Cross Border Transaction Approval Form Submitted By IP** (`cross_border_transaction_approval_form_submitted_by_ip`, text, optional): Enter the IP address of the user who submitted the form.
* **Cross Border Transaction Approval Form Submitted By User Agent** (`cross_border_transaction_approval_form_submitted_by_user_agent`, text, optional): Enter the user agent of the user who submitted the form.
