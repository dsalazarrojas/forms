# Concert Ticket Billing Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Concert Ticket Billing Form is a tool to collect billing information from customers buying concert tickets. Fill out this form to provide necessary details for payment processing and receive a receipt.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field carefully and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out completely.
3. Double-check that your card type and last four digits match your actual card details.
4. Review and acknowledge the refund policy.
5. Add any additional instructions if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payer name**: Enter the name of the person or entity making the payment. This will appear on the billing statement.
* **Billing email**: Enter a valid email address for payment and receipt purposes. This will help us contact you regarding your purchase.
* **Billing address**: Provide the street address and postal code for the billing location.
* **City and state**: Enter the city and state where the payment is being made.
* **Country**: Choose your country from the available options.
* **Card type**: Select the type of credit or debit card used for payment.
* **Last four digits**: Enter the last four digits of your card for verification purposes. 
* **Invoice or order reference**: If you have an internal reference number for your purchase, enter it here.
* **Refund policy acknowledgement**: Review the refund policy summary and acknowledge that you have read it.
* **Additional instructions**: If you have any special billing instructions or requests, enter them here.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have all required fields filled out completely.
* Double-check your card type and last four digits for accuracy.
* Be sure to review the refund policy and acknowledge it.
* Use a valid email address for payment and receipt purposes.
* Enter your billing address and city, state, and country accurately.
