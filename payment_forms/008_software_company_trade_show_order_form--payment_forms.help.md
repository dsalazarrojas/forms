# Software Company Trade Show Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers who attended a trade show and want to place an order for our software products. It's meant to be used to ensure accurate and organized orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company name in the "Company" field.
2. Enter your contact email in the "contact_email" field. This is required for us to contact you.
3. Enter your first name in the "contact_first_name" field.
4. Enter your last name in the "contact_last_name" field.
5. Enter your job title in the "contact_job_title" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "contact_phone" field.
7. Select the software products you're interested in from the "Software1", "Software2", and "Trade Show" fields.
8. Select the date and time of the order in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
9. Enter any additional notes about your order in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name**: This field is used to store the name of the company that attended the trade show.
* **Contact Email**: This field is used to store the email address of the person who placed the order. It's required so we can contact you.
* **First Name**: This field is used to store the first name of the person who placed the order.
* **Last Name**: This field is used to store the last name of the person who placed the order.
* **Job Title**: This field is used to store the job title of the person who placed the order.
* **Contact Phone**: This field is used to store the phone number of the person who placed the order.
* **Software1**: This field is used to select one of the three software product options. Choose the one you're interested in.
* **Software2**: This field is used to select multiple software product options. Choose all the products you're interested in.
* **Trade Show**: This field is used to select the trade show you attended. Choose the correct option.
* **Date**: This field is used to select the date of the order. Choose a date that works for you.
* **Time**: This field is used to select the time of the order. Choose a time that works for you.
* **Notes**: This field is used to enter any additional notes or information about your order. Enter anything that you think we need to know to complete your order.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, especially "contact_email" and "Software1", "Software2", and "Trade Show", as they are required for us to process your order.
* Double-check your selections before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
