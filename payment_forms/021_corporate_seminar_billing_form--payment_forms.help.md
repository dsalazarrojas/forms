# Corporate Seminar Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Seminar Billing Form is used to collect billing details for a corporate seminar, including participant information, seminar details, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form sections and ensure you have all the necessary information before filling out the form.
2. Enter your seminar details, including date, start and end time, duration, and participant count, in the **Seminar Details** section.
3. In the **Participant Information** section, enter your name, email address, phone number, company name, job title, department, and address as required.
4. In the **Billing and Payment** section, select your preferred payment method, enter the billing amount, and choose the payment currency.
5. Provide any additional details as necessary in the **Additional Details** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seminar Details** (id 5, label: Seminar Date): Enter the date of the seminar you're enrolling in.
* **Seminar Start Time** (id 6, label: Seminar Start Time): Enter the start time of the seminar.
* **Seminar Duration** (id 8, label: Seminar Duration): Enter the duration of the seminar.
* **Participant Count** (id 9, label: Participant Count): Enter the number of participants attending the seminar.
* **Participant Name** (id 2, label: Participant Name): Enter your name as the participant.
* **Participant Email** (id 3, label: Participant Email): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (id 4, label: Participant Phone): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Company** (id 21, label: Participant Company): Enter your company name.
* **Participant Job Title** (id 22, label: Participant Job Title): Enter your job title.
* **Participant Department** (id 23, label: Participant Department): Enter your department.
* **Participant Address** (id 24, label: Participant Address): Enter your address.
* **Participant City** (id 25, label: Participant City): Enter your city.
* **Billing Details** (id 10, label: Billing Details): Enter any additional comments or details about your billing.
* **Payment Method** (id 11, label: Payment Method): Select your preferred payment method (Bank Transfer, Cheque, or Credit/Debit Card).
* **Billing Amount** (id 12, label: Billing Amount): Enter the amount to be billed.

* **Payment Currency** (id 18, label: Payment Currency): Select the payment currency (USD, CAD, or EUR).

* **Participant Details** (id 20, label: Participant Details): Enter any additional comments or details about your participant information.
