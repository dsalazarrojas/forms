# Community Class Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students registering for classes at our music school. The form helps us to understand the student's personal details, class preferences, and payment information, allowing us to provide tailored services and payment options.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the instructions and hints provided for each field
- Fill in the required fields with accurate information
- Select options for class preferences and payment details from the available choices

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`student_first_name`, text, required): Enter the student's first name as per their identification documents.
- **Last Name** (`student_last_name`, text, required): Enter the student's last name as per their identification documents.
- **Email Address** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for registration confirmation and future communications.
- **Phone Number** (`student_phone`, text, required): Enter the student's primary contact phone number.
- **Street Address** (`student_address`, text, required): Enter your home street address for contact and billing purposes.
- **City, State, ZIP** (`city_state_zip`, text, required): Enter your city, state, and ZIP code for contact and billing purposes.
- **Class Selection** (`class_selection`, select_one, required): Choose the class you wish to register for from the available options.
- **Preferred Class Schedule** (`class_schedule`, select_one, required): Select the class schedule you prefer for your lessons.
- **Current Skill Level** (`skill_level`, select_one, required): Choose your current skill level from the provided options.
- **Do You Own an Instrument** (`instrument_owned`, select_one, required): Let us know if you own an instrument or need to rent/borrow.
- **Lesson Type** (`lesson_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of lesson you prefer: individual or group.
- **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, select_one, required): Select the duration of your lessons in weeks.
- **Total Amount Due** (`total_amount`, number, required): This field will automatically calculate the total cost based on your selections.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method.
- **Card Number** (`card_number`, text, optional): Enter your credit card number if paying by card (only required for card payments).
- **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, text, optional): Enter the expiration date of your card if paying by card (only required for card payments).
- **CVV** (`cvv`, text, optional): Enter the 3-digit security code for card payments (only required for card payments).
- **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, optional): Enter the address associated with your payment method (only required for card payments).
- **Discount Code** (`discount_code`, text, optional): Enter any applicable discount code if available.
- **Apply for Scholarship** (`scholarship_application`, select_one, required): Let us know if you wish to apply for financial assistance.
- **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_agreement`, select_one, required): Agree to our cancellation and refund policy.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
- Double-check your selections and details before submitting the form.
- Our system will automatically calculate the total amount due based on your selections.
- For card payments, only enter the card number, expiration date, and CVV if applicable.
- Our staff will contact you for further instructions and details regarding your application.
