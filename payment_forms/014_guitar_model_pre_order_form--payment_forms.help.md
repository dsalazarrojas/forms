# Guitar Model Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guitar Model Pre Order Form is designed to collect information from customers to pre-order a guitar model. Please fill out this form to place your order and receive a pre-order confirmation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Select your preferred options in the customer information, model selection, and shipping address fields.
3. Enter your preferred delivery date and time.
4. Enter the deposit amount.
5. Add any additional notes about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer information (customer_info)**: Select "Yes" if you are the customer.
* **Model selection (model_selection)**: Choose the model you wish to pre-order.
* **Model description (model_description)**: Enter a brief description of the model you selected.
* **Price (price)**: Enter the price of the pre-order.
* **Quantity (quantity)**: Enter the quantity of the pre-order.
* **Delivery date (delivery_date)**: Enter the desired delivery date for your order.
* **Delivery time (delivery_time)**: Enter the desired delivery time for your order.
* **Deposit (deposit)**: Enter the deposit amount for your order.
* **Shipping address (shipping_address)**: Enter your shipping address.
* **Notes (notes)**: Add any additional notes about your order.

## Tips
- Please double-check your answers to ensure they are accurate.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for assistance.
- We will contact you with a pre-order confirmation and any further instructions after you submit this form.
