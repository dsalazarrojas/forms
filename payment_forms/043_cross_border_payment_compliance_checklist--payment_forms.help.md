# cross_border_payment_compliance_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to review and document payment transfers that may be considered high-risk or suspicious to prevent financial crimes such as money laundering and terrorist financing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a brief description of the transfer in the Transfer Details section.
2. Select the review date and time in the Review Date and Review Time sections.
3. Select the status of the review from the Review Status section.
4. Indicate whether the transfer is of the type that requires review in the Transfer Type section.
5. Select whether the reviewer has access to the customer information in the Reviewer section.
6. Enter any additional notes about the review in the Review Notes section.
7. Enter the email address of the customer or payer in the Email section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Transfer Details** (`transfer_details`, text, no): Enter a brief description of the transfer, such as "Transfer of funds to a foreign account".
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, no): Enter the date the transfer was made or is intended to be made.
* **Review Time** (`review_time`, time, no): Enter the time the transfer was made or is intended to be made.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, select_one, no): Select one of the following options: Pending, Approved, Rejected.
* **Transfer Type** (`transfer_type`, select_one, no): Select one of the following options: 'Yes', 'No', indicating whether the transfer requires review.
* **Reviewer** (`reviewer`, select_one, no): Select one of the following options: 'Yes', 'No', indicating whether the reviewer has access to the customer information.
* **Review Notes** (`review_notes`, note, no): Enter any additional notes about the review.
* **Email** (`email`, email, yes): Enter a valid email address of the customer or payer.

## Tips
- Make sure to thoroughly review each field before submitting the form.
- Double-check the transfer details to ensure accuracy.
- Use a valid email address for the Email field.
- If the transfer requires review, select 'Yes' in the Transfer Type field.
- If you have access to the customer information, select 'Yes' in the Reviewer field.
