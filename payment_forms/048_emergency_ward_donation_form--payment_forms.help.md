# Emergency Ward Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Emergency Ward Donation Form is designed to collect donations for the emergency ward, providing essential care to those in need. This form ensures that all necessary information is accurately recorded for efficient processing and follow-up procedures.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, start by choosing a donation amount from the provided fields. This will determine the total amount of the donation.
2.  Next, select the donor's information category: Individual, Organization, or Other. This will help the hospital staff understand the source of the donation.
3.  Enter the date of the donation, ensuring that it is formatted as a date (MM/DD/YYYY).
4.  Select the preferred payment method: Cash, Bank Transfer, or Credit Card. This will guide the donor to choose the most suitable option for their financial situation.
5.  Provide your name and email address, if applicable, for contact purposes.
6.  Enter your phone number, if you prefer to be contacted.
7.  Add any additional notes you may have about the donation, such as the reason for the donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter a valid numerical value representing the amount of the donation.
*   **Donor Info** (`donor_info`, select_one, Individual, Organization, or Other): Select the type of donor you are representing (Individual, Organization, or Other).
*   **Donation Date** (`donation_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the donation in MM/DD/YYYY format.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, Cash, Bank Transfer, or Credit Card): Choose the payment method you prefer (Cash, Bank Transfer, or Credit Card).
*   **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, text, optional): Enter your name, if you are the individual making the donation.
*   **Email** (`donor_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, if you have one.
*   **Phone** (`donor_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you prefer to be contacted.
*   **Notes** (`donor_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or reasons for your donation.
