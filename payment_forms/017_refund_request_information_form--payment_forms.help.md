# refund_request_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers provide necessary information for a refund request. It gathers details about the customer, their order, and the reason for the refund.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the customer details section, including their name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2.  Provide information about the order that requires a refund, including any order numbers or dates.
3.  Specify the reason for the refund, which will help our team understand your situation.
4.  Enter the refund amount, which should be a numerical value.
5.  If you have any additional comments or messages for our team, enter them in the customer message section.
6.  Ensure you enter a valid email address for our team to contact you about the refund.
7.  Finally, provide any business notes or comments for our team to consider during the refund process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer_details** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the customer's name, contact information, and any other relevant details about the customer.
* **order_details** (Text, Required/Optional): Provide information about the order that requires a refund, including any order numbers or dates.
* **refund_reason** (Text, Required/Optional): Specify the reason for the refund, which will help our team understand your situation.
* **refund_amount** (Number, Required/Optional): Enter the refund amount, which should be a numerical value.
* **customer_message** (Text, Required/Optional): If you have any additional comments or messages for our team, enter them in this section.
* **customer_contact** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter a contact method for our team to reach you about the refund.
* **customer_email** (Email, Required/Optional): Ensure you enter a valid email address for our team to contact you about the refund.
* **business_note** (Text, Required/Optional): Provide any business notes or comments for our team to consider during the refund process.
