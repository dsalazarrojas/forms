# Counselor Honorarium Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Counselor Honorarium Submission Form is a tool used to collect payment information from Counselors, ensuring that all necessary details are gathered for the payment process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please carefully read this guide before starting to fill out the form.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- If you are unsure about any field, you can ask for clarification or assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Input** (`User Input`, text, optional): This field is used to provide additional information about the payment. Please type any relevant details about the payment in this text field.
- **Payment Details** (`Payment Details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the payment, including any relevant details.
- **Honorarium Amount** (`Honorarium Amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the honorarium you are requesting.
- **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select_one, required): Select one of the available payment methods:
  • Bank Transfer
  • Check
  • Other
- **Other Payment Method** (`Other Payment Method`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" in the Payment Method field, please describe the other payment method you are using.
- **Honorarium Reason** (`Honorarium Reason`, text, optional): Enter a brief reason for the honorarium, if applicable.
- **Payment Date** (`Payment Date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the payment.
- **Payment Time** (`Payment Time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the payment.

## Tips
- Be accurate and thorough when filling out this form to ensure that all necessary details are captured.
- Review your submissions carefully before submitting the form to ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
