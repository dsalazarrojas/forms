# sales_tax_calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to calculate the sales tax of an item based on the provided purchase amount and sales tax rate. It's used to determine the total amount due, including tax. 

## How To Complete This Form
To calculate the sales tax, follow these steps:

1. Enter the purchase amount in the "Purchase Amount" field.
2. Enter the sales tax rate in the "Sales Tax Rate" field.
3. If you want to calculate the tax, click on the "Calculate Tax" button.
4. Review the "Total Amount" field to see the result.
5. You can also add a category and description for your calculation if needed.
6. Click on the "Output File" button to save the result as a file.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Purchase Amount**: Enter the amount of the purchase. This field is required.
* **Sales Tax Rate**: Enter the rate of sales tax. This field is required.
* **Calculate Tax**: Click to calculate the sales tax. This field is optional.
* **Total Amount**: The total amount including tax. This field will automatically update when you click on the "Calculate Tax" button. This field is required.
* **Category**: Enter a category for your calculation (optional).
* **Description**: Enter a description for your calculation (optional).
* **Output File**: Click to save the result as a file. This field is optional.
