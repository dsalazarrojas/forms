# custom_ice_cream_cup_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The custom ice cream cup order form is used to collect information about your ice cream preferences for a custom order.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions below, selecting the relevant options for each field. If you need help with any of the fields, please refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Flavors
* **Flavors** (`flavors`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the flavors you'd like to have in your ice cream cup.
*   This field allows you to choose one or multiple flavors for your ice cream.
*   If you select 'Yes' for multiple flavors, you will be given options to choose individual flavors.

### Toppings
* **Toppings** (`toppings`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the toppings you'd like to have in your ice cream cup.
*   This field allows you to choose one or multiple toppings for your ice cream.
*   If you select 'Yes' for multiple toppings, you will be given options to choose individual toppings.

### Size
* **Size** (`size`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the size of your ice cream cup.
*   Select 'ice cream cup size' for a standard ice cream cup size.

### Order Notes
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any other notes about your order.
*   This field is for any additional comments or instructions you have for your order.

### Flavor
* **Flavor** (`flavor`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select a flavor for your ice cream cup.
*   Choose a single flavor from the options provided.

### Quantity
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the quantity of the ice cream cups you'd like to order.
*   Enter the number of ice cream cups you'd like to order.

### Submit
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required/optional): Click 'Submit' to submit your order.
*   Click 'Submit' to send your completed order form for processing.

## Tips
*   Please select 'Yes' for multiple flavors or toppings to be given options to choose individual flavors or toppings.
*   Enter a valid quantity of 1 or more for 'Quantity'.
