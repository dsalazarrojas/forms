# Therapy Liability And Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information for payment of therapy sessions, ensuring clients understand and agree to the payment terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reviewing the form carefully, ensuring you have all necessary information.
2. Choose the payment method you wish to use (Visa, PayPal, or Mastercard).
3. Enter the payment details accurately, including amount.
4. Review and agree to the payment terms.
5. Provide your consent for the payment process.
6. Submit the form to complete the payment process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Details (1)**: Enter the amount you wish to pay for the therapy session.
* **Payment Method (2)**: Choose how you would like to pay (Visa, PayPal, or Mastercard).
* **Payment Terms (3)**: Review and agree to our payment terms and conditions. This is an essential step in the payment process, so take your time to read and understand what is required.
* **Client Consent (4)**: This is a non-editable field that indicates your agreement and consent to the payment terms and conditions.
* **Payment Amount (5)**: Enter the exact amount you wish to pay for the therapy session.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are completed accurately to avoid delays or errors in the payment process.
* Take your time to carefully review the payment terms before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant department for assistance.
