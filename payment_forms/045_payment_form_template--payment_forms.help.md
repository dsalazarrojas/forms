# Payment Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a general payment form used to collect payment information from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer's name in the "customer_name" field.
2. Specify the payment method in the "payment_method" field.
3. Enter the amount due in the "amount" field.
4. If you need to collect a card number (e.g. for a credit card), enter it in the "card_number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, text, optional): Specify the type of payment, e.g. cash, credit card, etc.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount due in the currency specified by your organization.
* **Card Number** (`card_number`, text, optional): Enter the card number if you are collecting a credit card payment.
