# Tech Expo Booth Sponsorship Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from potential Tech Expo booth sponsors. Please fill out this form to apply for sponsorship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the required fields with your general information.
2. Provide details about your logo.
3. Choose your preferred method of payment.
4. Enter your contact information.
5. Confirm your sponsorship request.
6. Review and agree to the terms and conditions.
7. Click the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info** (required): This field is for entering your general information such as name, title, and address.
* **Logo Info** (optional): Enter your logo details such as logo name and URL.
* **Payment Method** (optional): Select your preferred method of payment.
* **Contact Info** (optional): Enter your contact information such as name, email, and phone number.
* **Confirm Sponsorship** (optional): Confirm that you are requesting sponsorship.
* **Terms and Conditions** (optional): Review and agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Submit** (required): Click this button to submit your application.
