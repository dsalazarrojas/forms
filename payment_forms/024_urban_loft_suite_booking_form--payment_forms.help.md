# urban-loft-suite-booking-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect basic booking information and any special requests from guests who are interested in booking an urban loft suite.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your booking information in the `booking-info` field.
2. Provide your guest details in the `guest-details` field.
3. If you have any special requests, select them from the `special-requests` dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Booking Info** (`booking_info`, Text, Required/Optional): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant information about your booking request.
* **Guest Details** (`guest_details`, Text, Required/Optional): Provide any information about your guests, such as their names, ages, or mobility needs.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, Select Multiple, Required/Optional): If you have any special requests, such as extra towels or a specific room type, select them from the dropdown menu.
