# youth-sports-fund-donation-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from donors who wish to support the youth sports fund. This form is used to gather essential details about the donor, including their contact information, donation amount, and payment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your name and email address in the "Donor Name" and "Donor Email" fields, respectively. Please make sure to enter a valid email address.
2. Enter the amount you wish to donate in the "Donation Amount" field. This field requires a numerical value.
3. Select the frequency of your donation from the "Donation Frequency" dropdown menu. Choose from "monthly", "annually", or "one-time".
4. If you'd like to provide more information about your donation frequency, you can enter a brief note in the "Frequency Hint" field. This is optional.
5. Choose your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu. Select from "credit card", "bank account", or "other".
6. If you've selected "bank account" as your payment method, you can enter a specific date and time for the payment in the "Payment Date" and "Payment Time" fields, respectively. These fields are optional.
7. Finally, you can add any additional notes or comments about your donation in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Name (Donor Name)** (`text`, required): Enter your name as it should be displayed on the donation receipt.
* **Donor Email (Donor Email)** (`email`, required): Enter a valid email address to receive updates and notifications about your donation.
* **Donation Amount (Donation Amount)** (`number`, required): Enter the amount you wish to donate to the youth sports fund.
* **Donation Frequency (Donation Frequency)** (`select_one`, required): Choose how often you plan to make this donation. Options include "monthly", "annually", or "one-time".
* **How often do you plan to make this donation? (Frequency Hint)** (`text`, optional): Provide a brief note about your donation frequency.
* **Payment Method (Payment Method)** (`select_one`, required): Select your preferred payment method. Options include "credit card", "bank account", or "other".
* **Payment Date (Payment Date)** (`date`, optional): If you've selected "bank account" as your payment method, enter a specific date for the payment.
* **Payment Time (Payment Time)** (`time`, optional): If you've selected "bank account" as your payment method, enter a specific time for the payment.
* **Additional Notes (Additional Notes)** (`note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your donation.

## Tips
* Please review your information carefully before submitting the form.
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
