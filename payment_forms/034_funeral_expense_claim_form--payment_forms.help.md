# Funeral Expense Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Funeral Expense Claim Form is used to collect information for a funeral expense claim. This form is designed to gather details about the claimant, the funeral expenses, and any supporting documents related to the claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **Claimant Details** page (Page 1). Enter the claimant's name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
2. Next, move on to the **Funeral Expense Claim** page (Page 2) and enter details about the funeral expenses, including the amount spent and any receipts or invoices.
3. On the **Supporting Documents** page (Page 3), upload any supporting documents related to the claim, such as receipts or invoices.
4. The **Payment Details** page (Page 4) is used to enter payment information, including the payment method and amount.
5. Finally, the **Claimant Declaration** pages (Pages 5-21) are used to enter additional information and declarations related to the claim. These pages are intended to be used to provide further details about the claim, but are not mandatory.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Claimant Details**: 
  * (`claimant_details`, text, required) Enter the claimant's name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
- **Funeral Expense Claim**: 
  * (`funeral_expense_claim`, text, required) Enter details about the funeral expenses, including the amount spent and any receipts or invoices.
- **Supporting Documents**: 
  * (`supporting_documents`, text, required) Upload any supporting documents related to the claim, such as receipts or invoices.
- **Payment Details**: 
  * (`payment_details`, text, required) Enter payment information, including the payment method and amount.
- **Claimant Declaration**: 
  * (`claimant_declaration`-`claimant_declaration_21`, text, optional) Enter additional information and declarations related to the claim. (These fields are not mandatory and are intended to provide further details about the claim.)

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure the claim is processed correctly.
- Attach any supporting documents to the relevant fields.
- If you are unable to attach a document, please describe it in the text field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the relevant authorities for assistance.
