# Bank Transfer Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a single or recurring bank transfer between accounts. The form includes fields for account holder, transfer type, transfer amount and currency, recipient details, transfer purpose, and transfer fees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the account holder's information, including their full name, email address, phone number, and mailing address.
2. Select the type of account you are transferring from and the type of transfer you want to make.
3. Enter the amount and currency you want to transfer.
4. Provide the recipient's information, including their name, bank name, account number, routing number, and account type.
5. Select the transfer purpose and acknowledge your understanding of transfer fees.
6. If this is a recurring transfer, select the frequency and end date.
7. Sign and date the authorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Account Holder Full Name**: Enter the full name of the account holder.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the account holder.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the account holder.
* **Mailing Address**: Enter the mailing address of the account holder.
* **Source Account Number**: Enter the account number of the source account.
* **Source Account Type**: Select the type of source account (e.g., Checking, Savings).
* **Transfer Type**: Select the type of transfer (e.g., One-Time, Recurring).
* **Transfer Amount**: Enter the amount to transfer.
* **Currency**: Select the currency of the transfer (e.g., USD, EUR).
* **Transfer Date**: Enter the date to transfer the funds.
* **Recipient Full Name**: Enter the full name of the recipient.
* **Recipient Bank Name**: Enter the name of the recipient's bank.
* **Recipient Account Number**: Enter the account number of the recipient.
* **Recipient Routing Number**: Enter the ABA routing number of the recipient's bank.
* **Recipient Account Type**: Select the type of recipient's account (e.g., Checking, Savings).
* **Recipient Address**: Enter the address of the recipient (optional).
* **Transfer Purpose**: Select the reason for the transfer (e.g., Personal Transfer, Bill Payment).
* **Recurring Frequency**: If this is a recurring transfer, select how often you want to transfer (e.g., Weekly, Monthly).
* **Recurring End Date**: If this is a recurring transfer, enter the date to stop the transfer.
* **Transfer Fee Acknowledgment**: Acknowledge your understanding of transfer fees.
* **Exchange Rate Acknowledgment**: For international transfers, acknowledge your understanding of exchange rates.
* **Authorization**: Confirm that you authorize this transfer.
* **Signature Date**: Date and sign the authorization.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If this is a recurring transfer, ensure you select the correct recurring frequency and end date.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask for clarification.
* Review all information carefully before submitting the form.
