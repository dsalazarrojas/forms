# Assistive Technology Fund Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Assistive Technology Fund Donation Form allows individuals and organizations to contribute to the fund, providing support for people with disabilities through assistive technology.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. If applicable, enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Enter your street address in the "Street Address" field for tax documentation purposes.
5. Enter your city, state/province, and ZIP/postal code in the respective fields.
6. Select your donor type (individual or organization) in the "Donor Type" field.
7. If you are an organization, enter your name and tax ID or EIN (Employer Identification Number) in the "Organization Name" and "Tax ID or EIN" fields, respectively.
8. Enter the amount of your donation in the "Donation Amount" field.
9. Select the frequency of your donation (one-time, monthly, quarterly, or annually) in the "Donation Frequency" field.
10. Choose the purpose of your donation from the options provided in the "Purpose of Donation" field.
11. Select your preferred payment method (credit card, debit card, bank transfer, check, PayPal, or wire transfer) in the "Payment Method" field.
12. Optionally, enter the name of the person this donation is in honor or memory of in the "Donation In Honor Of" or "Donation In Memory Of" fields, respectively.
13. If you want to notify a family member or friend about this donation, select "Yes" in the "Notify Family Member" field.
14. Enter the email address of the person you want to notify in the "Email to Notify" field, if applicable.
15. Choose how you would like to be recognized for this donation (publicly or privately) in the "Donor Recognition Preference" field.
16. Select whether you would like to subscribe to our newsletter in the "Subscribe to Our Newsletter" field.
17. Enter any additional comments or instructions in the "Additional Comments" field, if needed.
18. Confirm that this donation is tax-deductible by selecting "Yes" in the "Confirm this donation is tax-deductible" field.
19. Finally, enter the date of this donation in the "Donation Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name, which is required for identification purposes.
* **Last Name**: Your last name, which is required for identification purposes.
* **Email Address**: Your email address, which will be used to notify you about this donation and for tax purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number, which is not required but can be helpful for communication purposes.
* **Street Address**: Your address for tax documentation purposes.
* **City**: The city of your residence, which is required for tax purposes.
* **State/Province**: The state or province of your residence, which is required for tax purposes.
* **ZIP/Postal Code**: Your postal code, which is required for tax purposes.
* **Donor Type**: Your type of donor (individual or organization), which is required for tax purposes.
* **Organization Name (if applicable)**: The name of your organization, which is not required but can be helpful for tax purposes.
* **Tax ID or EIN (if applicable)**: The tax ID or EIN of your organization, which is not required but can be helpful for tax purposes.
* **Donation Amount**: The amount you are willing to donate, which is required for tax purposes.
* **Donation Frequency**: The frequency of your donation (one-time, monthly, quarterly, or annually), which is required for tax purposes.
* **Purpose of Donation**: The purpose of your donation (wheelchairs, communication devices, vision aids, etc.), which is required for tax purposes.
* **Payment Method**: The method of payment (credit card, debit card, bank transfer, etc.), which is required for tax purposes.
* **Donation In Honor Of (optional)**: The name of the person this donation is in honor of, which is not required but can be a nice gesture.
* **Donation In Memory Of (optional)**: The name of the person this donation is in memory of, which is not required but can be a nice gesture.
* **Notify Family Member**: Whether you want to notify a family member or friend about this donation, which is not required but can be helpful.
* **Email to Notify**: The email address of the person you want to notify, which is not required but can be helpful.
* **Donor Recognition Preference**: How you want to be recognized for this donation (publicly or privately), which is required for tax purposes.
* **Subscribe to Our Newsletter**: Whether you want to subscribe to our newsletter for updates about the fund, which is not required but can be helpful.
* **Additional Comments**: Any additional comments or instructions about this donation, which is not required but can be helpful.
* **Confirm this donation is tax-deductible**: Whether this donation is tax-deductible, which is required for tax purposes.
* **Donation Date**: The date of this donation, which is required for tax purposes.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Review your donation details before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact our support team.
